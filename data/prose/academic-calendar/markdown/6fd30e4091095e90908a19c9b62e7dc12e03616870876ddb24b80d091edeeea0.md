Note that dates and deadlines outlined below are based on courses following a standard term\. Not all courses follow the standard term and formal examination period schedule\. A course not following a standard term \(including module\-based curriculum, graduate, and professional programs\) have different deadlines that reflect the duration of study\. Check course section details in Workday to verify specific dates for each course\.

All deadlines are 11:59:00 p\.m\. on the day stated\.
