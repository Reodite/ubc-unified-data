Students in this Option must take a minimum of 36 credits in 300\- or 400\-level kinesiology courses\. Students must select at least 6 credits from 3 of the 6 lists below\. Then, students must choose 3 credits from either of the remaining 3 lists for a total of 21 credits\.

-   Neuromechanics: KIN\_V 310, 311, 313, 316, 318, 411, 413, 415, 418, 419
-   Leadership Education for Pedagogy and Physical Activity: KIN\_V 342, 344, 345, 442, 444
-   Psychology of Movement: KIN\_V 351, 353, 355, 450, 453, 457, 459
-   Sociocultural Studies: KIN\_V 360, 363, 368, 460, 463, 464, 465
-   Systems Biology, Exercise &amp; Health: KIN\_V 320, 324, 330, 335, 421, 422, 423, 424, 432, 434, 435, 438
-   Indigenous Perspectives in Kinesiology: KIN\_V 368, 428, 469
