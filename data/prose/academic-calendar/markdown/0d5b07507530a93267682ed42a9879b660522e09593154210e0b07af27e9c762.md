Get an edge in British Columbia and Canada’s fast\-evolving workplaces with a balanced foundation of practical technical skills and transferrable career skills like critical thinking, problem\-solving and strategic implementation\. Our technology courses and programs are created and delivered by experts and innovators from UBC Faculties, industry and educational partners\.

-   [Artificial intelligence \(AI\)](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/study-topic/artificial-intelligence)  
    Our artificial intelligence courses and programs are designed to provide the in\-demand skills and expertise to plan, integrate and apply AI technologies within cloud environments and in industries that use digital media\. Courses range from introductory offerings for those without technical experience to specialized options developed specifically for IT professionals\. Through expert\-led, hands\-on learning, gain key competencies to make strategic decisions, assess ethical implications, and position yourself and your organization for success\.  
     
-   [Cloud computing](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/study-topic/cloud-computing)   
    Our cloud computing courses and programs are developed by experts and innovators to help you gain in\-demand cloud computing skills—a top\-five hard skill, according to industry surveys\. They cover a range of topics, such as AI cloud services, transformation and infrastructure strategy, and technology implementation\.  
     
-   [Cybersecurity](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/study-topic/cybersecurity)   
    Our cybersecurity courses and programs were developed in consultation with leading BC cybersecurity experts, consultants and academics to provide learners with the tools to understand, assess and create strategic cybersecurity policies so you can manage threats to your organization as a confident cybersecurity champion\.   
     
-   [Data science](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/study-topic/data-science)   
    Learn the principles of data science, improve your data literacy, get hands\-on experience using data analytics techniques and real\-world cases, and translate your findings into data\-driven solutions\. The immeasurable value of these specialized skills, along with the potential fulfilling career opportunities they offer, extends across all sectors\.   
     
-   [Game design and development](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/study-topic/extended-reality-xr-gaming)   
    The gaming industry is a fast\-growing sector, and as video games become an increasingly powerful medium for education, social connection and immersive experiences, the demand for skilled designers and developers continues to outpace supply\. We have partnered with Circuit Stream, an industry leader in game development education, to offer hands\-on, project\-based gaming bootcamps\.   
     
-   [Software development](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/study-topic/software-development)   
    Our programming courses are developed and taught by top\-rated partners, including the UBC Department of Computer Science faculty and Circuit Stream, and feature world\-class content aligned with the needs of employers and industry\. Whether you’re looking to stay up to date in your current role or take the first step toward a career in software development, our offerings equip you with the necessary programming knowledge and in\-demand skills\.  
     
-   [Technology courses for teens](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/study-topic/technology-courses-teens)   
    We have partnered with Circuit Stream to offer technology courses for Canadian and international teens ages 13 to 18\. Bridging the gap between academic learning and real\-world applications, courses are offered online in the fall, winter and summer, and on\-campus in the summer at UBC Vancouver\.
