The University welcomes applications from outstanding international students\. Well\-qualified applicants from recognized secondary schools, colleges, and universities will be considered, as well as students applying as transfer students from recognized universities and colleges\. Applications from short\-term visiting students from other recognized universities are also accepted\. See [Undergraduate Admissions](http://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc) to apply online\.

For more information or further assistance, international students may visit [Ask Me @ UBC](http://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/ask-me)\.
