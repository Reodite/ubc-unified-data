The [Interdisciplinary Oncology Program](https://www.iop.ca/) \(IOP\) is a long\-standing and highly regarded graduate program at UBC and is recognized as one of the leading interdisciplinary oncology training programs in Canada\. Over the years, it has built a strong national reputation for excellence in cancer research training\. The program is widely valued by research supervisors and provides an outstanding academic home for scientists focused on cancer research who hold appointments across diverse departments throughout UBC\. Its cross\-disciplinary structure fosters collaboration, innovation, and high\-impact research in oncology\.
