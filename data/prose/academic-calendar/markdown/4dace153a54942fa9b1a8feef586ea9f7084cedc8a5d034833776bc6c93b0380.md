### Recommendations

The Department makes the following recommendations:

1.  Mathematically able students are encouraged to take the honours stream MATH\_V 120, 121, 223, 226, and 227\.
2.  In second year, MATH\_V 221 should be taken in Term 1\. It is possible to take \(with appropriate prerequisites\) some of MATH\_V 307, 308, 312, 317, 340, and MATH\_V/STAT\_V 302 \(as well as MATH\_V 300\) in the second year\.
3.  Students interested in pursuing statistics to some depth should take STAT\_V 200 and MATH\_V/STAT\_V 302 in the second year\. This will prepare them for more advanced statistics courses such as STAT\_V 305, 306, and 404\.
4.  Major students should consider taking some of MATH\_V 300, 320, 322\.
5.  MATH\_V 302 and MATH\_V 307 are courses which are useful in many areas of mathematics\.
6.  Students interested in operations research should take MATH\_V 340, 441, 442, and 443\. They are also advised to take MATH\_V 303, STAT\_V 305, and 306, and some advanced computer science courses\.
7.  Students interested in teaching are advised to take MATH\_V 308, 309, 312, 313, 414, 446\.
8.  Students interested in becoming actuaries can make substantial progress toward this career goal while majoring in mathematics or statistics\. These students should consult the actuarial advisors in the Mathematics and/or Statistics Departments for detailed advice on course selection and advice on taking the Society of Actuaries' exams\.
9.  Students interested in the physical sciences should take MATH\_V 317 which is important for MATH\_V 300, 316\.
10.  Students interested in economics should consider taking ECON\_V 420 and 421, and should consult an advisor in the Vancouver School of Economics for other appropriate economics courses\. A joint Major in Mathematics and Economics is available\. See below\.
11.  In selecting electives, students should consider pursuing an area of application of mathematics in some depth\. They should also ensure that they fulfill all the graduation requirements of the Faculty of Science\.

### Mathematics Areas of Concentration  

Mathematics areas of concentration are designed for Mathematics Majors, including Combined Majors, who wish to specialize in one of five areas, each based on a core theme in mathematics research and practice\.  

Each area of concentration consists of a prescribed set of nine related MATH\_V courses numbered 300 or higher, totaling 27 credits\.   
  
Note that the Mathematics Major specialization requires students to take 24 credits of MATH\_V courses numbered 300 or higher\. Completing an area of concentration is therefore an opportunity for a Mathematics Major to fulfill their upper\-year degree requirements in a particularly focused way\. 

On completing an area of concentration, a student will have acquired: 

1.  a solid background in algebra and analysis, essential pillars of modern mathematics, and 
2.  advanced knowledge and skills in their focus area\. 

Special proficiency in an area of concentration is appealing to both graduate schools and employers\. 

#### **Requirements**

The five areas of concentration and their prescribed courses are as follows: 

1.  Differential Equations and Analysis   
    Courses: MATH\_V 300, MATH\_V 301, MATH\_V 316, MATH\_V 317, MATH\_V 319, MATH\_V 329, MATH\_V 345, MATH\_V 400, MATH\_V 405   
     
2.  Computational Mathematics    
    Courses: MATH\_V 307, MATH\_V 316, MATH\_V 319, MATH\_V 329, MATH\_V 340, MATH\_V 360, MATH\_V 405, MATH\_V 441, MATH\_V 461   
     
3.  Mathematics of Information   
    Courses: MATH\_V 302, MATH\_V 303, MATH\_V 307, MATH\_V 319, MATH\_V 329, MATH\_V 340, MATH\_V 344, MATH\_V 441, MATH\_V 442   
     
4.  Mathematical Modelling   
    Courses: MATH\_V 302, MATH\_V 303, MATH\_V 316, MATH\_V 319, MATH\_V 329, MATH\_V 344, MATH\_V 345, MATH\_V 360 and MATH\_V 461, or MATH\_V 361 and MATH\_V 462  
     
5.  Classical Mathematical Structures  
    Courses: MATH\_V 308, MATH\_V 309, MATH\_V 312, MATH\_V 313, MATH\_V 319, MATH\_V 329, MATH\_V 341, MATH\_V 342, MATH\_V 442 

#### **Admission**

Mathematics areas of concentration do not require pre\-approval or special admission\. All Math Majors are eligible\. Upon admission into the Math Major specialization, all Math Majors will be provided instructions on how to complete an area of concentration\. Prior to graduation, all Math Majors will be provided instructions on how to register a completed area of concentration for formal recognition\.

### Dual Degree Program in Mathematics and Education

The Dual Degree program in Mathematics and Education offers qualified students the opportunity to earn a B\.Sc\. degree in Mathematics \(Major\) and a B\.Ed\. degree in Secondary Education, in five winter sessions with some academic requirements in Term 1 of the Summer sessions\. After completing all the requirements, students are normally eligible for a British Columbia Professional Teaching Certificate\. Admission to the Dual Degree program requires application in the fall of second year and successful completion of a teaching practicum at the end of second year\. See the [Dual Degree Program in Science and Education](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/15389#18521) for more information on admission and continuation requirements\.
