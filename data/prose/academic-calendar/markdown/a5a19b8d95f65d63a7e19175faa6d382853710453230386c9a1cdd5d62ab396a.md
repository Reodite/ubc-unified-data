Admission to a Co\-op program is based on academic performance and employment suitability which is usually determined by an application process\. Students wishing to be admitted must meet all the requirements of their department and faculty\. Admission to the Co\-op Program is subject to the current state of the market as well as the resources available to support coaching and job development\. Students accepted into a Co\-op program are required to comply with the Terms &amp; Conditions of their Co\-op program in addition to those regulations listed in the UBC Academic Calendar\.

Refer to your Faculty’s Co\-op Program description in the Calendar for link to the Terms and Conditions\.
