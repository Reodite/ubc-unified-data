Student fees include fees established and authorized by the UBC Board of Governors and fees established and authorized by student societies\. Student fees are charged to all students for the period of September 1 to August 31\. Fees are calculated according to full\-time or part\-time status, academic session \(winter or summer\), and study level \(undergraduate or graduate\)\.
