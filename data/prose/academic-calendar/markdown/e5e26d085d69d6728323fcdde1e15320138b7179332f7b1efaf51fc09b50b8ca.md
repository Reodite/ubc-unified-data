Every student in the third year of the Bachelor of Commerce program is required to declare an option of study\.

Students who declare the Organizational Behaviour and Human Resources \(OBHR\) option are encouraged to register in the courses necessary for their specialization \(option\) on their assigned date of registration\.

Registration in the OBHR option does not guarantee enrolment in any specific course or section\. Note that space in required courses is limited\.

### Third and Fourth Year

The OBHR option requires that students complete 15 credits of COHR\_V courses\.   
  
Students may follow a track and take a minimum of nine course credits in the chosen track and then the remaining six credits of course work from either track\. The list of courses currently offered are available on the [Faculty website](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/options/organizational-behaviour-and-human-resources)\.

Students are permitted to retake a required option core course only once if they have failed it, without needing to submit an appeal\. Students who need to retake a core option course for a third time must appeal to the Director or designate within the Office of Student Academic Services for permission\. Should a student’s appeal be denied, or should they fail the required option core course for a third time, they will be required to switch to a different specialization \(option\)\.
