### Major in Environment and Sustainability

#### Lower\-level Requirements

Students must take the following courses:

-   9 credits GEOG\_V 121, GEOG\_V 211 or ENST\_V 211, GEOS\_V\[1\] 270
-   6 credits GEOS\_V\[1\] 102\[2\], 103\[2\]
-   3 credits from GEOS\_V\[1\] 200, 204, 206, 207, GEOG\_V 122, 202, 210, 220, GEOG\_V 250 or URST\_V 200\.

#### Upper\-level Requirements\[3\]

30 credits from courses numbered 300 and higher, as follows:

**Environmental Concentration**

-   6 credits of GEOG\_V 313 and GEOG\_V 314
-   6 credits from GEOG\_V 302, 310, 311, 312, 316, 318, 351 or ENST course equivalents
-   3 credits from GEOS\_V\[1\] 300, 303, 304, 305, 306, 307, 308, 309
-   3 credits from GEOS\_V\[1\] 400, 401, 402, 407; GEOG\_V 402, 410, 411, 412, 419, 423

**Research and Methods**

-   3 credits GEOG\_V 374
-   9 credits from GEOG\_V 315 \(or 379\), 319, 342, 345, 371, 395, 410, 411, 412, 419, 423, 429, 446, 447, 448, 491, 492; GEOS\_V\[1\] 309, 370, 372, 373, 400, 401, 402, 403, 405, 406, 407, 415, 472, 479

Although some courses pertain to more than one requirement, no course can be counted twice \(credits applied to more than one requirement\) within the Major\.

\[1\]As of 2021S, the course code GEOS\_V replaced the course code GEOB\_V\. There were no changes to course content\.  
\[2\] Students may obtain credit for only one of GEOS\_V 102 or EOSC\_V 112, and only one of GEOS\_V 103 or EOSC\_V 110\. See the [Science Credit Exclusion List](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-science/bachelor-science/credit-exclusion-lists)\.   
\[3\] The Department will accept an ENST\_V course in place of a GEOG\_V course equivalent when taken by the student\.  Credit will only be granted for one of the courses, not both\. See [GEOG\_V courses](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/course-descriptions/subject/geogv) for equivalency notes\. 

### Minor in Environment and Sustainability

#### Lower\-level Requirements

Students must take 6 credits of GEOG\_V 121, GEOG\_V 211 or ENST\_V 211 and 3 credits from GEOS\_V\[1\] 102\[2\] or 103\[2\]\.

#### Upper\-level Requirements\[3\]

21 credits of geography \(GEOG\_V/GEOS\_V\[1\]\) courses numbered 300 and higher, as follows:

-   6 credits of GEOG\_V 313, 314
-   15 credits from GEOB\_V/GEOS\_V\[1\] 303, 306, 307, GEOG\_V 302, 310, 311, 312, 316, 318, 319, 351, 395, 396, 410, 411, 412, 419, 423, 432, 446, 495, 491, 492, 497

\[1\]As of 2021S, the course code GEOS\_V replaced the course code GEOB\_V\. There were no changes to course content\.  
\[2\] Students may obtain credit for only one of GEOS\_V 102 or EOSC\_V 112, and only one of GEOS\_V 103 or EOSC\_V 110\. See the [Science Credit Exclusion List](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-science/bachelor-science/credit-exclusion-lists)\.   
\[3\] The Department will accept an ENST\_V course in place of a GEOG\_V course equivalent when taken by the student\. Credit will only be granted for one of the courses, not both\.
