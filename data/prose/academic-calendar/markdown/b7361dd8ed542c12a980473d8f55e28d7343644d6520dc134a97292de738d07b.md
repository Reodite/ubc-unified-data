#### Alma Mater Society

The Alma Mater Society of UBC Vancouver \(AMS\) is the undergraduate student society of UBC's Vancouver campus\.  
[ams\.ubc\.ca](http://ams.ubc.ca/)

#### Athletics and Recreation

At UBC Vancouver, the Thunderbirds represent UBC’s varsity and sport club teams\. With 26 varsity teams across 15 sport disciplines, there are often games on Friday evenings and weekends that are free for students to attend\. The Vancouver campus is also home to a variety of sport facilities \(ice rinks, tennis centre, aquatic centre, fitness centres, gymnasiums, and track\) offering recreation and physical activity programs\.  
[athleticsandrecreation\.ubc\.ca](http://athleticsandrecreation.ubc.ca/)

#### Bookstore &amp; Campus Mail

The Bookstore provides learning materials, trade books, stationary, and UBC and Thunderbird branded merchandise to the campus community\. Campus Mail delivers thousands of pieces of mail and parcels every day to locations across campus\.

#### Student Affairs \- Centre for Accessibility

Centre for Accessibility provides leadership on issues of accessibility for people with disabilities at UBC Vancouver, working in partnership with faculties to foster inclusive learning, living and working environments for students, faculty and staff\. Centre for Accessibility provides support and programming initiatives designed to remove barriers for students with disabilities and facilitates disability related accommodations for members of the UBC Vancouver community\.  
[students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/centre\-for\-accessibility](http://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility)

#### Student Health and Wellbeing \- Counselling Services

#### Student Health and Wellbeing \- Student Health Service

Student Health Service cares for registered UBC students year\-round\. Services are provided by family doctors, nurse practitioners, and registered nurses\.

#### Student Health and Wellbeing \- The Student Recovery Community

The Student Recovery Community is a safe, welcoming, and inclusive space for students who are in recovery or curious to explore their relationship with alcohol, drugs, and other addictive substances or behaviours\.  
[students\.ubc\.ca/health/ubc\-student\-recovery\-community](https://students.ubc.ca/health/ubc-student-recovery-community)

#### Student Health and Wellbeing \- Wellness Centre

The Wellness Centre is a peer\-hosted space for students to learn about health and wellness\-based skills and resources, as well as be a part of building healthier campus communities\. Through leadership on health promotion and education strategies, the Wellness Centre offers innovative and evidence\-based programs such as the distribution of harm reduction supplies, hosting the Student Recovery Community, and operating the Sexual Health Shop\.  
[students\.ubc\.ca/health/wellness\-centre](http://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre)
