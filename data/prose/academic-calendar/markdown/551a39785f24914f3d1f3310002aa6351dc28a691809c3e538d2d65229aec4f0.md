UBC Extended Learning offers UBC inclusive excellence beyond degree\-focused learning, with relevant, ever\-evolving content centered around students' life\-long quest to learn and grow – personally and professionally\.  
  
**You can go anywhere with knowledge behind you\.**  
  
Whether you want to advance your career, start a passion project, travel the world or simply gain new skills and experiences, our programs and courses are here to support your learning journey with top\-calibre content across numerous areas of study\. No matter which path you are on, you’ll come away with the knowledge to get you where you want to go\.  

Our courses, programs and credentials are offered across a wide variety of subjects and formats to accommodate your interests and your schedule\. Take an individual course to quickly gain in\-demand skills or explore an interest\. Deepen your knowledge and earn a non\-credit credential through our programs which consist of courses that complement and build upon one another\.
