Students in the Bachelor of Global Resource Systems program are permitted to take electives to satisfy degree requirements in compliance with the University’s Credit/D/Fail policy\.

Courses selected for Credit/D/Fail cannot be used to satisfy Regional or Resource specialization requirements\. See [Credit/D/Fail](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/grading-practices/creditdfail-grading-elective-undergraduate-courses "Credit/D/Fail Grading for Elective Undergraduate Courses")\.
