This certificate is a direct response to the climate emergency \(at UBC and beyond\)\. It is delivered as an interdisciplinary collaboration between the UBC’s Department of Geography \(Faculty of Arts\) and the Department of Earth, Ocean and Atmospheric Sciences \(Faculty of Science\), and offered on the traditional and unceded territory of the Musqueam First Nation\. The program is open to and designed for all undergraduate students across UBC, providing students with an opportunity to integrate an interdisciplinary climate change credential into their undergraduate program\.

Increasing capacity for climate action is the focus of the certificate\. Early classes cover climate science, the crises that led to declaring an “emergency” and the various strategies that already exist to address it\. Upper level classes take a deeper look at climate justice, the social, economic, political and cultural processes that underlie climate change and include an elective class for students to deepen knowledge in a specific area\. Community\-engaged learning, with a climate action focus, is embedded throughout the program, culminating in a capstone project\.

For more information see the certificate [website](http://geog.cms.arts.ubc.ca/undergraduate/climate-certificate)\.

### Admissions Information

To apply, complete the form on the certificate [website](http://geog.cms.arts.ubc.ca/undergraduate/climate-certificate)\.

Students from all undergraduate programs can apply\. Students are encouraged to apply at the end of their first year, but there is flexibility for students coming to the certificate late \(e\.g\. transfer students\) to enrol in their third or fourth year\.

### Program Requirements

To complete the certificate, students must complete 18 credits across 5 categories:

-   **Climate Science \(3\)\[1,2\]**
    -   One of EOSC\_V 112, EOSC\_V 340, EOSC\_V 345, EOSC\_V 440 or GEOS\_V 102
-   **Climate Emergency \(3\)\[1,2,3\]**
    -   One of GEOG\_V 202, GEOG\_V 312 or ENST\_V 312
-   **Climate Justice \(3\)**
    -   GEOG\_V 302
-   **Elective \(3\)\[2\]**
    -   One of BEST\_V 401, CONS\_V 310, CONS\_V 425, ECON\_V 473, ENST\_V 312, ENVR\_V 410, EOSC\_V 340, EOSC\_V 345, EOSC\_V 440, GEOG\_V 312, GEOS\_V 408, NURS\_V 290 or POLI\_V 351
-   **Climate Action \(6\)**

2 credits chosen from: 

-   ENVR\_V 201 \(1\) and ENVR\_V 301 \(1\) or
-   ENVR\_V 302 \(2\)

4 credits chosen from:

-   ENVR\_V 401 \(1\) and GEOG\_V 402 \(3\) or
-   ENVR\_V 401 and ENVR\_V 402\[4\] \(3\)

ENVR\_V 201, ENVR\_V 301, and ENVR\_V 401 are intended to be taken as three 1\-credit courses over three years\. However, students may complete these requirements over a shorter timeline depending on their year of entry into the certificate\. The following combinations are permitted:

-   ENVR\_V 201, ENVR\_V 301, and ENVR\_V 401 completed over three years:
-   ENVR\_V 302 and ENVR\_V 401 completed over two years;
-   ENVR\_V 201 completed in one year, followed by ENVR\_V 301 and ENVR\_V 401 taken concurrently in a subsequent year; or
-   ENVR\_V 302 and ENVR\_V 401 taken concurrently and completed within one year\.

\[1\] 300 and 400\-level options are included to accommodate upper\-level students that need to meet upper\-level credit degree requirements\.
