All work submitted by students \(including, without limitation, essays, dissertations, theses, examinations, tests, reports, presentations, problem sets, and tutorial assignments\) may be reviewed by the University for authenticity and originality\. Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, such review may include the use of software tools and third party services including Internet\-based services such as TurnItIn\.com\. By submitting work, students consent to their work undergoing such review and being retained in a database for comparison with other work submitted by students\. The results of such review may be used in any University investigation or disciplinary proceedings \(see [Student Discipline](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/15668)\)\.
