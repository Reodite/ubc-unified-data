The Vancouver School of Economics, in collaboration with the Faculty of Commerce and Business Administration \(also known as the Sauder School of Business\), offers a Bachelor of International Economics \(B\.I\.E\.\) degree\.

The B\.I\.E\. degree is a four\-year program featuring intensive study in economics with an emphasis on international trade, international finance, and international development\. Students may pursue a [Minor](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/15483#20089) as part of their degree program\. Part\-time study is not an option for the B\.I\.E\.
