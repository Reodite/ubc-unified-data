UBC Library's Instruction program provides training at all levels on getting the most from electronic and print information sources\. Classes cover topics such as subject\-specific research skills, using databases to find journal articles and specialized information, and finding relevant research materials beyond UBC's collections\. The program features hands\-on workshops, self\-paced online tutorials, and instructional sessions for credit courses, both during and outside class time\.  
  
Faculty can contact their [subject librarian](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/subjectlibrarians/) to enquire about embedding library instruction within their classes\. Additional library instructional sessions are available through [UBC Library's calendar of events](https://libcal.library.ubc.ca/calendar/vancouver?cid=7544&amp;t=g&amp;d=0000-00-00&amp;cal=7544&amp;inc=0)\.
