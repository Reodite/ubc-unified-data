Applicants apply to the Vantage One programs via an online application form\. The fundamental principles governing admission to the Vantage One programs are the mandate to diversify the international student population and the capacity of the student to matriculate into a UBC degree program\.

Academic Admission requirements are specified in [Admissions](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/15604)\.

Transfer and Advance Credit regulations are specified in [Admissions](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/15604)\.
