The following description of the University of British Columbia's institutional [Coat of Arms](http://www.library.ubc.ca/archives/coat.html) is from the Grant of Arms issued by the College of Arms on September 23, 1915:

"Argent three bars wavy Azure issuant from the base a demi\-sun in splendour proper, on a chief of the second an open book proper edged, strapped and buckled Or inscribed with the words TUUM EST Sable\."
