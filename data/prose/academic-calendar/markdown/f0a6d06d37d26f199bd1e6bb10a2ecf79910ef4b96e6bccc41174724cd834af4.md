The Faculty of Commerce and Business Administration, also known as the Sauder School of Business, offers an undergraduate program leading to the degree of Bachelor of Commerce\. Through the Real Estate Division, the Faculty offers a post\-diploma degree completion program that leads to the Bachelor of Business in Real Estate\. The Faculty also offers a Diploma in Accounting program\. At the graduate level, programs leading to the degrees of Master of Business Administration, Executive M\.B\.A\., International M\.B\.A\., Professional M\.B\.A\., Master of Business Analytics, Master of Management, Master of Science in Business Administration, and Doctor of Philosophy are offered\. For information on research\-oriented graduate programs \(M\.Sc\.B\. and Ph\.D\.\) see [Business Administration](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/15017), and for professional master's programs see [Professional Master's Degrees](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/14778)\.
