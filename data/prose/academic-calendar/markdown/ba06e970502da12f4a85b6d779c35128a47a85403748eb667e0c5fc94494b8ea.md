The possession of the Doctor of Medicine degree does not confer the right to practice medicine\. Each jurisdiction in Canada has a licensing body who has the final authority to grant a license to practice medicine within that jurisdiction\. To be eligible for independent practice in BC, graduates will need to meet the requirements of the College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC found here: [https://www\.cpsbc\.ca/registrants/current\-registrants/registration\-and\-licensing](https://www.cpsbc.ca/registrants/current-registrants/registration-and-licensing)\.
