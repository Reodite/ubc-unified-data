Unassigned credit may be granted where a course\-to\-course equivalence cannot be established\. This credit may be used as elective credit\. Elective credit may be either in a particular discipline \(e\.g\., "Economics \(3\) credits"\) or in a faculty \(e\.g\., "Arts \(6\) credits"\)\. Note: there may be specific faculty or school requirements, as well as specific program requirements, that unassigned credit may not satisfy\.
