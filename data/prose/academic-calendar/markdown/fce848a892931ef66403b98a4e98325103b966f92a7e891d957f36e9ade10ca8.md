#### AMS/GSS Health/Dental Plan \(StudentCare\)

The AMS/GSS provides a mandatory Extended Health and Dental Plan\. If you have equivalent coverage, you may opt out\.  
[studentcare\.ca](http://studentcare.ca/)

#### Medical Insurance

Application forms for medical insurance coverage are available at the [Student Health and Wellbeing](http://students.ubc.ca/livewell) and online at [Medical Services Plan of BC](http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health) \(MSP\)\.

The University reserves the right to require a medical examination if circumstances warrant\.

The faculties of Dentistry and Medicine have special medical requirements:

-   [Faculty of Dentistry](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/14807)
-   [Faculty of Medicine](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/15224)

#### Student Health and Wellbeing

Student Health and Wellbeing offers care for all registered UBC students year\-round\. Services are provided by family doctors and registered nurses\.  
[students\.ubc\.ca/livewell](http://students.ubc.ca/livewell)
