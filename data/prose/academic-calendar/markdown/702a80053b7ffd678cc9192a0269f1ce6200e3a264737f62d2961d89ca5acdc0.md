The [Department of French, Hispanic and Italian Studies](https://fhis.ubc.ca/) offers undergraduate programs of study in Italian that lead to the Bachelor of Arts\.

### Major in Italian

Currently the Department of French, Hispanic and Italian Studies does not offer a Major in Italian\.

### Minor in Italian

To graduate with a Minor in Italian, students must complete:

#### Lower\-Level Requirements

Up to 12 credits\*

-   6 credits of ITAL\_V 101 and 102 or 3 credits of ITAL\_V 103
-   6 credits of ITAL\_V 201 and 202 

\*Students with heritage knowledge of Italian may not need to take all, or even any, of ITAL\_V 101, 102, 103, 201 or 202; they should discuss their placement with the Student Programs Coordinator at [https://fhis\.ubc\.ca/undergraduate\-advising\-contact\-form/](https://fhis.ubc.ca/undergraduate-advising-contact-form/)\.

#### Upper\-Level Requirements

Students must complete 18 credits from any 300\-level or 400\-level courses in ITAL\_V or RMST\_V courses from Cluster B – Italian Studies \(RMST\_V 340\-355, RMST\_V 440\-459\)\.
