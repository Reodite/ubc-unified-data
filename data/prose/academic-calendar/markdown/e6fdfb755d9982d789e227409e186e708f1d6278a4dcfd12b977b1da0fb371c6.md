### UBC\-Langara Indigenous Transfer Partnership

To be eligible to transfer to UBC into the Faculty of Forestry and Environmental Stewardship through this [partnership](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/indigenous-admissions/indigenous-transfer-partnership), Indigenous students must meet the general requirements for admission as a [post\-secondary transfer student](http://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/university-college-transfer) as well as the following specific requirements:

-   Successful completion of at least 24 transferable credits\.
-   An academic average of 2\.0 or greater on the most recent 30 credits of transferable courses attempted, including failed and retaken courses\.
-   Completion of required high school academic prerequisites\. In some cases, university transferable coursework may satisfy these prerequisites\.
-   Successful completion of the Transition Plan offered by Langara in collaboration with UBC\.

Applicants who do not meet these requirements may be considered for admission as a general [transfer student](http://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/canadian-high-schools/#basic) and can be considered through [UBC's Indigenous Admissions Policy](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/15559)\.

For more information about the UBC\-Langara Partnership, please visit the [website](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/indigenous-admissions/indigenous-transfer-partnership)\.
