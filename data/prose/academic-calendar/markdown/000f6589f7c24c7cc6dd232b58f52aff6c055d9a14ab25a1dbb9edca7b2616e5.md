The School of Kinesiology is committed to supporting Bachelor of Kinesiology students by providing comprehensive academic advising resources\. While accurate registration within the program's parameters is the responsibility of each student, our academic advising service is easily accessible and serves as a valuable resource for navigating the complexities of your studies\. The [Undergraduate Advising Centre](https://kin.educ.ubc.ca/academic-advising/) offers personalized assistance, ensuring students receive the guidance needed for an exceptional academic experience\. We emphasize the importance of proactive engagement with academic advising for course selection, program requirements, and overall academic planning\. Our advising team is dedicated to creating a collaborative and supportive atmosphere, contributing to the holistic development and success of our Bachelor of Kinesiology students\.
