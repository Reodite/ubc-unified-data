Graduate and undergraduate students are eligible to complete a research placement at a recognized university or research institution\. With their UBC supervisor, students are responsible for arranging a minimum 4 week placement with a host supervisor\. For more information please visit the [Self\-Directed Research Abroad](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/research-abroad/self-directed-research-abroad) section of the Go Global website\.
