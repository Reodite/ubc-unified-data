There are over 400 workstations available in UBC library branches\. PC Workstations are equipped with MS Office software and several branches have Mac workstations with a suite of multimedia applications \(Adobe Creative Cloud\)\. UBC Pay for Print allows printing, copying and scanning across campus using a UBC Card and print jobs can be submitted online\. [Laptops and other equipment](http://services.library.ubc.ca/computers-technology/technology-borrowing/) such as chargers and projectors are also available for loan at certain branches\.
