Admission to the Bachelor of Design Honours Option is by application and requires meeting honours option entry requirements and completion of an independent senior design project \(DES\_V 403\) in year four in lieu of DES\_V 402\.

Entry to the Honours Option requires achievement of a sessional average of 80% or better in each of year two and three and a grade of 80% or better in each of DES\_V 201, 202, 301 and 302\.

Graduation from the Honours Option requires a grade of 80% or better in DES\_V 401 and 403\.

Successful completion of the Honours Option will be reflected on the academic transcript and degree parchment\.
