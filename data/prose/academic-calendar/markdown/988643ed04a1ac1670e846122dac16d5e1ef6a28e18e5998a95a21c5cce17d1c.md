Email is the preferred means for the College administration and faculty members to communicate important messages to students\. It is the responsibility of all program students to ensure that their current email address is accurately recorded on [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/) and to read email sent to that account on a regular basis\. [Free email accounts](http://id.ubc.ca/) are available for all UBC students, but any email account may be registered through [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\.
