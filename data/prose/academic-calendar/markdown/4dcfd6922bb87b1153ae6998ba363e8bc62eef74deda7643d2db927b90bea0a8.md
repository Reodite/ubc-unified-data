Effective September 1, 2022, the following requirements represent the core courses of the Bachelor of Commerce \(B\.Com\.\) program\. In addition, students in third year must select an option and complete the specific option program requirements set out below\. Students who were initially registered in the B\.Com\. program prior to September 1, 2022, should consult the Office of Student Academic Services regarding the requirements to complete the program\. Students completing the B\.Com\. with a Combined Major in Business and Computer Science should refer to the degree requirements listed under the Business and Computer Science Combined Major option\.

A student transferring from another university or college may be granted transfer credit for a maximum of 50% of degree requirements\. All upper\-level commerce core courses and option core courses must be completed while registered in the Bachelor of Commerce program\. Students planning a cross\-campus transfer to the Bachelor of Commerce program at the UBC Vancouver campus are encouraged to consult with the [Office of Student Academic Services](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/osas) to explore transfer pathways and program requirements\.

### Business Communications Requirements

To qualify for the B\.Com\., students must satisfy the Business Communications requirements of the Faculty\. To do this, students must obtain credit for COMM\_V 196 and COMM\_V 396 with a minimum grade of 60% in each of these two courses\.

### Prerequisites

The required 200\-level Commerce courses generally are prerequisite to 300\- and 400\-level courses, and in each option, it is assumed that the required 300\-level courses will be taken prior to the 400\-level courses\.

Any student not registering for a normal sequence of courses must consult an Academic Advisor in the Office of Student Academic Services\. Non\-Commerce students taking Commerce courses as a part of a program should contact the Office of Student Academic Services for information\.
