The Food, Nutrition, and Health \(FNH\) Major offers to students the flexibility to tailor the program to reflect specific interests in food, nutrition, and health without the specialization depth afforded by the other majors\. With suitable course selections, students will be prepared to enter professional programs such as medicine, dentistry, and a variety of other careers\.
