-   The undergraduate's gown is black in colour, of ankle length, and with long sleeves\.
-   The master's gown is the same as the undergraduate's\.
-   The Ph\.D\. gown is Cambridge style, of maroon silk material with front panels and sleeves of University blue with gold piping\.
-   The Ed\.D\. gown is similar in style to that of the Ph\.D\. but with black front panels and sleeves of University blue with gold piping\.
-   The D\.M\.A\. gown is similar to that of the Ph\.D\. but with black front panels and sleeves of University blue with gold piping\.
