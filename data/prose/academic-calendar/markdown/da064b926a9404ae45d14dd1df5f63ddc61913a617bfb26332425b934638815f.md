- **Row 43**

  - **Cell 1 (colspan 2):**
  
    \[7\] Students must complete their first year MATH\_V requirement before taking ECON\_V 301, 302 and 303, and must take ECON\_V 301 and other prerequisites before taking 400 level ECON\_V courses\.

- **Row 44**

  - **Cell 1 (colspan 2):**
  
    \[8\] Choose from FRE\_V 302, 340, 374, 420, 460, 474 and 490\. Recommended: FRE\_V 340, 460 and 474 \(Food Markets and Development\); FRE\_V 374, 420 and 474 \(Land, Resource\_Vs &amp; Environment\); and FRE\_V 302 and either FRE\_V 460 or FRE\_V 420 \(Food and Resource Management\)\.

- **Row 45**

  - **Cell 1 (colspan 2):**
  
    \[9\] There are three topic areas \(see note 7\)\. Students can specialize by choosing all supporting courses from one topic area or can achieve breadth by choosing supporting courses from multiple topic areas\. Acceptable courses are posted on the Faculty website: [https://www\.landfood\.ubc\.ca/current/undergraduate/degree\-planning/restricted\-elective/](https://www.landfood.ubc.ca/current/undergraduate/degree-planning/restricted-elective/)

- **Row 46**

  - **Cell 1 (colspan 2):**
  
    \[10\] Choose any eligible 300 or 400 level course in COMR\_V, ECON\_V and FRE\_V\. Students with a strong interest in research should take FRST\_V 399 and consider having a FRE faculty member supervise a directed studies course \(FRE\_V 497\) and/or a 6 credit thesis \(FRE\_V 499\)\.

- **Row 47**

  - **Cell 1 (colspan 2):**
  
    \[11\] If FRE\_V 497 \(Directed Studies in Food and Resource Economics\) is chosen, it must have a quantitative focus and the work plan must be approved by the program advisor\.

- **Row 48**

  - **Cell 1 (colspan 2):**
  
    \[12\] A minimum of 45 credits required for the Major must be from courses numbered 300 or higher\.
