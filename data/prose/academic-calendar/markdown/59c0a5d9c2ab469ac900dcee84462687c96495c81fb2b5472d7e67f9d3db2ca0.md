Admissions advising is recommended before entering the program\. To learn about the B\.S\.N\. Program, and how to access the resources and supports for your applications, please visit the [B\.S\.N\. Admissions Website](https://nursing.ubc.ca/admissions/undergraduate-admissions)\.  
  
An Academic Advisor is available for students currently enrolled in the B\.S\.N\. Program\. To learn more about UBC and B\.S\.N\. Program Supports, and how to access Academic Advising, or request an Academic Concession, please visit [“Support for B\.S\.N\. Students”](http://nursing.ubc.ca/undergraduate-learning/support-bsn-students) on the [School of Nursing Website](http://nursing.ubc.ca/)\. The School of Nursing reserves the right to request advising during the program while students’ progress to completion continues\.   
  
For Current Students \- To request an appointment with the Academic Advisor, please email [student\.advisor@nursing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:student.advisor@nursing.ubc.ca)   
  
For applicants to the B\.S\.N\. Program – please visit our [B\.S\.N\. Admissions Website \- Resources](https://nursing.ubc.ca/undergraduate/academic-resources-for-bsn-students) or contact [information@nursing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:information@nursing.ubc.ca)
