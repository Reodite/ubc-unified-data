Students with Associate Degrees of Arts or Science from a recognized college or university college in BC or Yukon, if admitted to the Faculty of Arts or the Faculty of Science, are guaranteed full transfer credit \(60 credits\) for the work done for their Associate Degree\. Students must still meet all 100\- and 200\-level course requirements for specific degree programs \(e\.g\., majors, honours, faculty\-level requirements\), which may require students to take more than 120 total credits to earn their degree\. Students admitted with Associate Degrees must meet the competitive admission average for the program to which they apply if this average is above the published minimum\.
