[Faculty of Applied Science](http://www.apsc.ubc.ca/)

On the Vancouver campus, the Faculty of Applied Science offers undergraduate and graduate programs in engineering, architecture and landscape architecture, community and regional planning, and nursing\. The Faculty offers engineering programs through the School of Biomedical Engineering; through the Departments of Chemical and Biological Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical and Computer Engineering, Materials Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Mining Engineering; through five boards of study: Engineering Physics, Environmental Engineering, Geological Engineering, and Integrated Engineering and Manufacturing Engineering; as well as through the [Joint UNBC/UBC Environmental Engineering program](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-applied-science/joint-unbcubc-program-environmental-engineering)\. The three non\-engineering schools in the Faculty offer programs in their respective disciplines, as described in [The School of Architecture and Landscape Architecture](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/14529), [The School of Community and Regional Planning](https://scarp.ubc.ca/), and [The School of Nursing](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/15316)\.

The Faculty offers graduate programs leading to the degrees of Master of Applied Science \(M\.A\.Sc\.\), [Master of Engineering](https://engineering.ubc.ca/programs-admissions/grad) \(M\.Eng\.\), [Master of Engineering Leadership](https://apscpp.ubc.ca/programs/mel/) \(M\.E\.L\.\), [Master of Health Leadership and Policy](https://apscpp.ubc.ca/programs/mhlp/) \(M\.H\.L\.P\.\), and Doctor of Philosophy \(Ph\.D\.\), and provides research facilities in many areas of engineering\. The Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies lists [requirements for admission](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-graduate-and-postdoctoral-studies/admission "Admission") to the M\.A\.Sc\. and Ph\.D\. programs, while the Faculty of Applied Science lists requirements for admission to the M\.Eng, M\.E\.L\., and M\.H\.L\.P\. programs\.
