### Hoods

-   The master's hood is the same as the bachelor's, lined with the distinctive colour\.
-   The M\.B\.A\. hood conforms similarly to that of the B\.Com\.
-   The M\.Sc\.\(Bus\.Admin\.\) hood is similar to that of M\.Sc\., with grey trim and black and white cord\.
-   The M\.Eng\. hood is the same as that of the M\.A\.Sc\., except that it is trimmed with a University blue cord\.
-   The hood for the honorary degree of LL\.D\. is of scarlet broadcloth lined with dark blue velvet\.
-   The hood for the honorary degree D\.Sc\. is the same as for the LL\.D\., with dark purple lining\.
-   The hood for the honorary D\.Litt\. is the same as for the LL\.D\. and D\.Sc\., with cream lining\.
-   The hood for the special honorary degree for Japanese Canadian students is a red shell, lined with royal satin with a gold satin chevron, old gold satin trim, and a white soutache along the shell\.
