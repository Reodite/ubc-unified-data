The University Act was rewritten in 1974 and has since been further revised\. The University currently operates under the authority of the British Columbia University Act, R\.S\.B\.C\. 1996, c\. 468\.

The full and unedited text of the University Act is available at [BC Laws](http://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/00_96468_01)\.
