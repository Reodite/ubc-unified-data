B\.M\.S\. students are expected to make regular progress in their program requirements each year, completing Core Requirements as scheduled\. They are further expected to register in designated sections of Core Courses each year, along with peers in their cohort\. See [Registration](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-arts/bachelor-media-studies/registration "Registration")\.

In Year One students will normally complete:

-   the "Media Studies" stream of the [Co\-ordinated Arts Program](http://cap.arts.ubc.ca/)\. This CAP program comprises ASTU\_V 100, CRWR\_V 213, FIST\_V / CINE\_V 100, JRNL\_V 100, VISA\_V 110\[1\]; and,
-   CPSC\_V 110\[1,2\] or CPSC\_V 103\[2\]; and,
-   electives\[3\]\.

In Year Two students will normally complete:

-   “Core” courses: ENGL\_V 232, FIPR\_V 133, FIST\_V / CINE\_V 240, INFO\_V 250, VISA\_V 241; and,
-   “Core Plus” required electives; and,
-   electives\[3\]\.

In Year Three students will normally complete:

-   “Core” courses: ARTH\_V 380, CRWR\_V 312, ENGL\_V 332, FIST\_V / CINE\_V 340, VISA\_V 210; and,
-   “Core Plus” required electives; and,
-   electives\.

In Year Four students will normally complete:

-   “Core” courses: ARTH\_V 480 or JRNL\_V 400, GERM\_V 412 / GMST\_V 475, INFO\_V 419, INFO\_V 456; CPSC\_V 344\[4\]; and,
-   “Core Plus” required electives; and,
-   electives\.

\[1\]Students interested in taking additional courses in Computer Science or in completing a Minor in Computer Science will normally select CPSC\_V 110\.

\[2\]Students are required to attempt either CPSC\_V 103 or 110 no later than Year 2 of the program\. Those who do not complete the requirement prior to entering Year 3 of the program may have their registration restricted until the requirement is completed\.

\[3\]Students are strongly encouraged to include at least 3 credits of 100\-level Art History \(ARTH\_V\) as electives in Year One or Two as preparation for advanced study in the discipline in Year Three’s ARTH\_V 380\.

\[4\]Students must successfully complete CPSC\_V 103 or CPSC\_V 110 before attempting CPSC\_V 344\. Students cannot attempt CPSC\_V 103 or CPSC\_V 110 concurrently with CPSC\_V 344\.
