Enrolment is limited in all courses and admission does not guarantee that space will be available in any course or section\. However no student in a graduating year may be excluded from a course necessary to meet degree program requirements because of lack of space \(this rule does not apply to elective courses or preferred sections of courses\)\. Any student in a graduating year who is confronted with such a situation must promptly consult the dean, director, department head, or faculty advisor, if resolution is desired\.
