**Mining law**  
  
In today’s mining industries, both in Canada and internationally, it’s important not only to have a solid understanding of sustainable mining practices, but also to be equipped with the knowledge to navigate complex governing legal and regulatory systems\.    
  
Our offering was developed in partnership with the Peter A\. Allard School of Law to provide a new framework for making sustainable mining and business decisions\. The content and instruction go beyond the status quo to consider Indigenous participation, climate change, human rights and anti\-corruption laws and regulations—all of which help to advance sustainable mining\.
