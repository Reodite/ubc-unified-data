### Costs Other than Sessional Fee

There are additional expenses for travel and clinical practice\. Students should be prepared for clinical practice outside the Vancouver area and should include travel and living costs for these experiences when estimating total expenses\. See [Midwifery](http://www.midwifery.ubc.ca/) for more information regarding additional costs\.
