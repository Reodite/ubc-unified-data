The Bachelor of Science in Food and Resource Economics \(B\.Sc\. \(F\.R\.E\.\)\) combines economics, data analysis, business, and physical sciences to provide students with a unique set of interdisciplinary skills which are used to explore economic policy impacts on international food markets, biodiversity conservation, and sustainable food systems in a changing climate\. Graduates of the program are thus trained to address real world food and environmental challenges with a comprehensive, data intensive inquiry\.
