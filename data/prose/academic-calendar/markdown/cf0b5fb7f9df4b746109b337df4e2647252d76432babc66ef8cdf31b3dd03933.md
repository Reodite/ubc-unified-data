UBC’s Student Safety Abroad Program supports safe experiences abroad\. The Program is based on the requirements outlined in [UBC’s Student Safety Abroad Policy](http://safetyabroad.ubc.ca/)\. Through the program students are:

-   asked to complete the Student Safety Abroad Registry prior to departure
-   offered advice for assessing and mitigating risks
-   provided information on how UBC can assist during a critical incident
-   offered a clear and simple process for adjudicating high\-risk travel requests\.
