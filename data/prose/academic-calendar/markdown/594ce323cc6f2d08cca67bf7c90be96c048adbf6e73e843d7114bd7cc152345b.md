#### Admission

Enrolment in this program is limited\. Students must have successfully completed one of MATH\_V 100, 102, 104, 180, or 184 and both of ECON\_V 101 and 102\. Meeting the stated requirements does not guarantee admission to the program\.

The Commerce Minor is intended to be completed over two years\.

#### Minor in Commerce Requirements:

The program consists of COMR\_V 329, 457, 465, 473, 493; and one of COMR\_V 398, or 458\.
