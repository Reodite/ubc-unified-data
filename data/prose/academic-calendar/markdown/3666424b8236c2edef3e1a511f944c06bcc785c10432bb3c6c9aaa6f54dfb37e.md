In the Business and Markets major students learn how to use economic theories of business and markets along with data analysis to answer policy\-relevant questions concerning global food security, natural resource conservation and environmental outcomes\. Students interested in agri\-business management can broaden their credentials by choosing their restricted electives from select courses offered by the Sauder School of Business \(e\.g\., marketing, finance and accounting\)\.
