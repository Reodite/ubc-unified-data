The undergraduate Bachelor of Kinesiology program at UBC's School of Kinesiology provides a world\-class education with a core curriculum covering active health, biomechanics, anatomy, physiology, motor learning, psychology, and sociology\. Students have an opportunity to specialize in either a Neuromechanical and Physiological Sciences, Social and Behavioural Sciences, or Multidisciplinary Science Option from the second year onwards, with each Option emphasizing interdisciplinary studies\. This approach ensures graduates have a comprehensive skill set, ready to navigate the diverse challenges in the dynamic field of kinesiology\.
