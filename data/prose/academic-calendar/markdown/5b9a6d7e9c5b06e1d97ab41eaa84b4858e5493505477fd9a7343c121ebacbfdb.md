#### Our Vision: To Transform Health for Everyone\.  
  
Our Values: Respect, Integrity, Compassion, Collaboration and Equity

The UBC Faculty of Medicine is one of the world’s top medical schools, leading bold innovations in education, training and research that are transforming health for communities in British Columbia, across Canada and around the world\.  
  
Together with its academic and health partners, the Faculty of Medicine delivers immersive training experiences in hospitals, clinics and primary care settings province\-wide — including rural, remote and Indigenous communities\. With more than 145 training sites across B\.C\., the Faculty of Medicine is preparing the next generation of doctors, health professionals and researchers to support the diverse healthcare needs of patients and communities and to meet the health challenges of tomorrow\.  
  
At the centre of Canada’s fastest\-growing life sciences sector, the Faculty of Medicine is a leading global hub for biomedical innovation and health research\. Home to 19 departments, three schools and multidisciplinary research centres and institutes throughout the province, the Faculty is accelerating the development of lifesaving treatments and creating pathways to better health for local and global communities\.  
  
For more information visit the Faculty of Medicine [website](https://www.med.ubc.ca/)\.
