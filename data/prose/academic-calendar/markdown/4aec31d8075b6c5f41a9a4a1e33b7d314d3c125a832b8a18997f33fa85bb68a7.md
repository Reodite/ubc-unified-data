Under section 27\.1 of the University Act, a student society has the authority to establish and approve student fees by referendum\. The University is required to collect these fees on behalf of the student society, but does not approve these fees or determine the amount of these fees\.

The following fees have been approved by the UBC Vancouver student societies, the Alma Mater Society \(AMS\) and the Graduate Student Society \(GSS\)\.

### General Student Fees Established and Approved by a Student Society

**Active Members**  
Active Members are students enrolled in in\-person or hybrid courses who have full access to on\-campus services, facilities, and student society\. These members are assessed the full range of AMS student fees\.

**Online\-only Active Members**  
Online\-only Active Members are students registered exclusively in courses delivered in an online format\. These members are assessed a reduced set of AMS student fees\.

**Incoming Exchange, Visiting Graduate, or UBC Okanagan Campus Students**  
The following members are assessed a reduced set of AMS student fees\.

-   Students participating in exchange programs from institutions abroad who are not assessed tuition at UBC Vancouver\[1\]\.
-   Students participating in the Western Dean program from other institutions, who are not assessed tuition fees by UBC Vancouver\.
-   Students from UBC Okanagan attending UBC Vancouver for one term or more while continuing to pay student fees at UBC Okanagan\.

### Student Society Fees

Tuition fees are reviewed annually by the Board of Governors\. In recent years, tuition increases have been 2% for continuing domestic students and between 2% and 5% for continuing international students\.
