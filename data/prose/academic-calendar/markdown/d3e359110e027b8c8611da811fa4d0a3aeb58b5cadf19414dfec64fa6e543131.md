The Graduate Certificate in Migration Studies is open to all graduate students enrolled in any master’s, doctoral, or professional graduate degree program at UBC, and will be assessed tuition in alignment with their primary program of study for which they are concurrently enrolled\.  
  
The Undergraduate Certificate in Earth, Ocean, and Atmospheric Sciences is open to undergraduate students from all Faculties across UBC's Vancouver Campus, except students currently enrolled in: EOAS degrees; the Bachelor of Science with a Combined Major in Science \(Environment and Earth Sciences option\); the Bachelor of Arts in Environment and Sustainability; or the Bachelor of Science in Geographical Sciences, and will be assessed tuition in alignment with their primary program of study for which they are concurrently enrolled\.

The Undergraduate Certificate in Geomatics for Environmental Management is open to undergraduate students from all Faculties across UBC's Vancouver Campus, and will be assessed tuition in alignment with their primary program of study for which they are concurrently enrolled\.

The Undergraduate Certificate in French Translation is open to undergraduate students from all Faculties across UBC's Vancouver Campus, and will be assessed tuition in alignment with their primary program of study for which they are concurrently enrolled\.
