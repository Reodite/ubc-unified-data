Undergraduate students can participate in structured research programs as part of a group of international students\. Placements can varying in length \(4 weeks to 4 months\) and take place between May and August\. Research placements are non\-credit bearing experiences and cost can vary depending on the program\. For more information please visit the [Research Abroad section](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/research-abroad/self-directed-research-abroad) of the Go Global website\.
