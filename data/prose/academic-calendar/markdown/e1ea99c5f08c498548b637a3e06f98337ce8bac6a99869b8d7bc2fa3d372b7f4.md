#### BC Studies: The British Columbian Quarterly

BC Studies is a multi\-disciplinary journal publishing a wide variety of original scholarship on British Columbia\.  
[bcstudies\.com](http://bcstudies.com/)

#### Canadian Literature

Canadian Literature features critical articles on Canada's literature, new poems by Canadian poets, and reviews of significant Canadian publications\.  
[canlit\.ca](http://canlit.ca/)

#### Pacific Affairs

Pacific Affairs is an international quarterly that features articles on the current political, economic, social, and diplomatic issues of Asia and the Pacific\.  
[pacificaffairs\.ubc\.ca](http://pacificaffairs.ubc.ca/)

#### PRISM international

PRISM international is a quarterly literary journal published by the Creative Writing Program\. It features poetry, fiction, drama, creative non\-fiction, and translation by emerging and established authors from Canada and around the world\.  
[prismmagazine\.ca](http://prismmagazine.ca/)

#### UBC Press

The University of British Columbia Press is Canada’s leading social sciences publisher and has an international reputation for publishing high\-quality works of original scholarship\.  
[www\.ubcpress\.ca/](http://www.ubcpress.ca/)
