Academic advising is available through the Office of Student Academic Services\. Faculty advisors can assist students with course planning, provide guidance on academic requirements and policies, as well as help navigate the regulations governing their program\. Advisors can evaluate and approve requests for course changes, course withdrawals, academic concessions, and letters of permission\. Students experiencing academic difficulty are encouraged to visit the [Faculty website](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/) and schedule an appointment with an academic advisor as soon as possible\.
