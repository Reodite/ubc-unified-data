Students, faculty, and staff with mobility or print disabilities may obtain Enhanced Services designation\. This designation is obtained by contacting a UBC Disability Diversity Advisor from UBC’s Centre for Accessibility\. People with this designation are entitled to book and journal retrieval, browsing assistance, catalogue assistance, and photocopying by library staff with a UBC or guest print/copy card provided by the patron\.

For information about services, contact [Centre for Accessibility](http://students.ubc.ca/about/access), 604\.822\.5844 \(voice\), or 604\.822\.9049 \(Telecommunications Device for the Deaf\)\.
