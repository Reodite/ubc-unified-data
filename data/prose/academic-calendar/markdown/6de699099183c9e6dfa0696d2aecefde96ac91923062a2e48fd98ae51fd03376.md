UBC is one of Canada's leading institutions for global learning and international growth\. Our commitment to strengthening ties with Latin America has fostered  a  long\-term partnership with Mexico's Instituto Tecnologico y de Estudios Superiores de Monterrey \(ITESM\), commonly known as  Tec de Monterrey \(Tec\)\. 

Through the Tec de Monterrey – UBC Joint Academic Program, Tec students have the opportunity to spend one or two semesters  at UBC\. where they can take undergraduate courses in the Faculties of Arts, Commerce, Land and Food Systems, or Applied Science\. Certificates of Specialty are also available for students who wish to focus on a specific area of study\. For more information, please visit [Tec de Monterrey\-UBC Joint Academic Program](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/tec-de-monterrey-ubc-joint-academic-program)\.

Similarly, UBC students are encouraged to participate in exchange programs at various Tec campuses available through Go Global: International Learning Programs\. For more information, please visit [Go Global](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/) and check “Programs for UBC students”\.
