GIS&amp;GC is a program administered through the Department of Geography\.

Students interested in [Environment and Sustainability](http://geog.ubc.ca/undergraduate/programs/environment-sustainability/) or [Human Geography](http://geog.ubc.ca/undergraduate/programs/human-geography/) should refer to [Geography](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/14643)\.

### Minor in Geographic Information Science and Geographical Computation \(GIS&amp;GC\)

The minor consists of 30 credits, of which 18 credits must be at the 300\-level or above\.

#### Lower\-level Requirements

12 credits, consisting of:

-   CPSC\_V 103 or CPSC\_V 110
-   3 credits from GEOB\_V/GEOS\_V\[1\] 102, 103, GEOG\_V 121, 122
-   GEOB\_V/GEOS\_V\[1\] 270
-   3 credits from GEOB\_V/GEOS\_V\[1\] 200, 206, 207, GEOG\_V 211, 220, 250, 290, URST\_V 200, SOCI\_V 260, CPSC\_V 107, DSCI\_V 100

#### Upper\-level Requirements

18 upper\-level credits, consisting of:

-   GEOS\_V 370
-   GEOS\_V 372
-   GEOG\_V 374\*
-   9 credits as: Minimum of 6 credits from GEOS\_V 373, 407, 472, 479, GEOG\_V 314, 450, URST\_V 450 Maximum of 3 credits from FNIS\_V 454, PHIL\_V 461, PLAN\_V 331, 341, 425, BIOL\_V 314

\*With permission of departmental advisor, students may substitute GEOG\_V 374 with similar courses listed under the [Science Credit Exclusion List](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/15408)\.

\[1\]As of 2021S, the course code GEOS\_V replaced the course code GEOB\_V\. There were no changes to course content\.

Some of these courses may have prerequisites; students should consult Departmental requirements before registering\.
