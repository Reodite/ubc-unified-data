UBC offers instruction in each of its faculties and schools\. Listed below are the bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degrees, as well as diplomas and certificates offered at UBC\. Doctoral and master's degrees are offered either by the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies or by a disciplinary faculty\. For complete information on graduate programs, see the respective sub\-chapter of the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies or the disciplinary faculty or school\. For a listing of graduate interdisciplinary programs offered, see Interdisciplinary Studies in the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies sub\-chapter\. For a listing of honorary degrees, see Calendar Chapter IV, Establishment and Constitution\.
