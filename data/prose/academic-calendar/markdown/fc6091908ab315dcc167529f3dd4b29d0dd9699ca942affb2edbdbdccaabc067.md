The David Lam Management Research Library offers services and collections for research in business management and administration, including: accounting, collective bargaining, finance, marketing, international trade, and urban land economics\. For further information, see the [Branch website](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/)\. 

In addition to its extensive online and print collection of business resources, the library is also a partner in the Canaccord Learning Commons, a facility that provides co\-curricular learning spaces, programs, and services to UBC Sauder School of Business students\. For further information, see the [Branch website](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/clc)\. 

The David Lam Library and Canaccord Learning Commons is located on the 2\[nd\] and 3\[rd\] floors of the David Lam Management Research Centre, 2033 Main Mall\.
