The Global Supply Chain and Logistics Management option is a specialized joint exchange program between the Faculty of Commerce and Business Administration \(also known as the Sauder School of Business\), Copenhagen Business School, and Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen\. Option core course requirements are unique to each partner institution and the program is cohort based\. Thus, option core course requirements must be completed as scheduled by and at the partner institutions\. Students will spend the first term of third year at Copenhagen Business School, the second term at the Sauder School of Business, and first term of their fourth year at Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen\. Students interested in this option must submit an application to the Undergraduate Office and must complete the prerequisite of COMM\_V 204 prior to starting the option\.

For information on the application process, visit the [Faculty website](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/options/global-supply-chain-and-logistics-management)\.  
  
The program sequence is as follows:

### Third Year

Term 1 at Copenhagen Business School:

-   COMM\_V 342\[1\], 343\[1\]

Term 2 at UBC Sauder School of Business:

-   COMM\_V 341\[2\], 414\[3\]

### Fourth Year

Term 1 at Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen:

-   COMM\_V 344\[1\], 345\[1\]

Students are encouraged to take COMM\_V 446\.

\[1\] Must be completed successfully while on exchange at partner institutions\. In the case of one failed option core course requirement at the exchange institutions, a faculty advisor may approve an appropriate substitute in consultation with the Division Chair of Operations and Logistics\. Two or more failed option core course requirements at the exchange institutions will result in required withdrawal from the option\.   
  
\[2\] Course may be used to fulfil the requirements toward a maximum of one of the following two options: Global Supply and Logistics Management or Operations and Logistics\.   
  
\[3\] Course may be used to fulfil the requirements toward a maximum of one of the following three options: Global Supply and Logistics Management, Marketing, or Operations and Logistics\.
