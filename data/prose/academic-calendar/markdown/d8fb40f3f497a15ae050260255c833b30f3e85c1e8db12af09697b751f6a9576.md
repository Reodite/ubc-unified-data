The [UBC Learning Exchange](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/) supports UBC to connect with Vancouver’s Downtown Eastside on pressing community\-identified priorities relevant to all of British Columbia\. It is a unique shared space for exploring ideas and opportunities, a learning hub where members of the community and the university exchange experience and expertise\. Activities happen year\-round, including:

[Community Members](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/community-based-programs/): Free learning programs  
  
[UBC Students](http://www.learningexchange.ubc.ca/ubc-student-learning/ubc-students/): Experiential learning opportunities for UBC students   
  
[UBC Faculty](http://www.learningexchange.ubc.ca/ubc-student-learning/ubc-faculty/): Support for faculty to extend and enrich coursework  
  
[Researchers](http://www.learningexchange.ubc.ca/community-based-research/): Support for community\-based research collaborations and results\-sharing  
  
[Community Organizations](http://www.learningexchange.ubc.ca/resources-for-community-organizations/): Partnerships with local organizations to address critical issues
