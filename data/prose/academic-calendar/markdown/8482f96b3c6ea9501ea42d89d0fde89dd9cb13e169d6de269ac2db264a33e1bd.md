### Minor in Journalism and Social Change

The Minor in Journalism and Social Change is a skills\-based program that provides students with foundational knowledge in journalism, media and digital literacy skills, community engagement, and the tools to understand and respond to social change\. Students will be able to complement their major field of study with practical skills, critical thinking, analytical news and digital literacy, with a focus on the role of journalism and social change\.

This program enriches students' primary field of study by cultivating advanced competencies in critical thinking, fact\-checking and investigative methods, community engagement, and digital storytelling \- all through the lens of journalism's role in social change\.

The Minor in Journalism and Social Change empowers students to develop their unique journalistic voice across digital and traditional platforms while honing their abilities as thoughtful media creators and consumers\.
