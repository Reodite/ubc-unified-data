Every student in the third year of the Bachelor of Commerce program is required to declare an option of study\.

Students who declare the General Business Management option are encouraged to register in the courses necessary for their specialization \(option\) on their assigned date of registration\.

Registration in the General Business Management option does not guarantee enrolment in any specific course or section\. Note that space in some courses is limited\.

### Third and Fourth Year

Students in this option must complete:

-   15 credits of 300/400 level COMM\_V/COHR\_V courses, of which 6 credits are at the 400 level\.\[1\]

\[1\] See [Faculty website](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/options/general-business-management) for eligible courses to fulfill this requirement\.
