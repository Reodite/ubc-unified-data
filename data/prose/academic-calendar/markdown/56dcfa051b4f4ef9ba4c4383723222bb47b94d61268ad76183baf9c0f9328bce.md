Students may gain admission directly from secondary school or transfer from a recognized university or college with a minimum of 24 credits, or as mature students\.

For admission to the Bachelor of Science in Food, Nutrition, and Health, students should consult the Faculty’s [Admission section](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-land-and-food-systems/admission "Admission")\.

Admission to the majors in [Dietetics](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/14416), [Food Science](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/14419), [Food and Nutritional Sciences Double Major](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/14418), and to the [Kinesiology](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-land-and-food-systems/dual-degree-and-minor-options "Dual Degree and Minor Options") and [Commerce](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-land-and-food-systems/dual-degree-and-minor-options "Dual Degree and Minor Options") Minors is competitive\. See those sections for details\.
