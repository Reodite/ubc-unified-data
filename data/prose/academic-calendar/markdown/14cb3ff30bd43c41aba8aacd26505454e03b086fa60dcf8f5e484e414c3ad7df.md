Koerner Library supports teaching, learning, and research in the humanities, social sciences and education\. Reference help and format specialists \(for maps, government publications, data\) are available for consultation on both a drop\-in and appointment basis\. Koerner houses an extensive print collection of books, journals, maps, microfilm and government publications\. In addition, it licenses a wide range of electronic journals, books, and databases relevant to the humanities and social sciences\. 

Koerner is home to the Library’s Research Commons, a constellation of services targeted to graduate students and faculty\. The [Research Commons](https://researchcommons.library.ubc.ca/) provides expertise on Digital Scholarship, Research Data Management, Data Analysis, Geographic Information Systems, Citation Management and more\. It also houses a specialized computer lab to support digital scholarship teaching and learning\. For further information, see the [Branch website](http://koerner.library.ubc.ca/)\.

The Walter C\. Koerner Library is located at 1958 Main Mall\.
