The Department of Theatre and Film offers the program leading to the B\.F\.A\. in Acting\. For details about current offerings, visit the [Department](http://theatrefilm.ubc.ca/)\.

#### Admission and Transfer

Admission to the B\.F\.A\. in Acting is limited, and is based on an evaluation of the preparation and capabilities of each applicant during a performance audition\. Meeting minimum admission requirements does not ensure admission to the B\.F\.A\. in Acting Program\. The number of places available in the program is limited\.

Applying for admission to the B\.F\.A\. in Acting is a two\-stage process requiring both an online application form, submitted directly to UBC Undergraduate Admissions, and a successful audition, held by the Department of Theatre and Film\. Program acceptance is contingent on students meeting the university’s entrance requirements\. Please see [BFA Admission and Transfer](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/14675) and [Undergraduate Admissions](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/15589) for details\.

The B\.F\.A\. in Acting normally consists of four years of study, and individuals may apply for admission to either Year 1 or Year 2 of the program\. Students who apply and audition prior to studying at a post\-secondary institution, if accepted, will enter into Year 1\. Students who apply and audition while studying at UBC or another post\-secondary institution, or after completing these studies, may enter into the second year of the program, if accepted\. Auditions are held in February\. Please see the [Acting Program website](http://theatrefilm.ubc.ca/undergraduate/acting/) for more information\.

All students enrolled in the B\.F\.A\. program will be reviewed annually upon completion of the Winter Session\. Please see [Continuation Requirements](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/14684)\.

### Program Requirements

Students in the B\.F\.A\. in Acting must complete the Writing Component of the Writing and Research Requirement \(please see the [B\.F\.A\. Degree Requirements](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/14678)\) within their first 54 credits or, for transfer students, in the first Winter Session of study\. Within the minimum of 120 total credits required for the program, students must also include the following courses:

#### Lower\-level Requirements

Normally completed in Year 1:

-   THTR\_V 130, FIPR\_V 131 and THFL\_V 100

Normally completed in Year 2:

-   THTR\_V 271, 272, 273, and 274
-   At least 3 credits from THTR\_V 210 and 211

#### Upper\-level Requirements:

60 credits comprising:

-   THTR\_V 371, 372, 373, 374, and 12 credits of THTR\_V 391
-   THTR\_V 471, 472, 473, 474, and 12 credits of THTR\_V 491
-   6 credits chosen from THTR\_V 320, 325, 420
-   6 credits of 300\- or 400\-level FIPR\_V in screen acting \(see Department\)
