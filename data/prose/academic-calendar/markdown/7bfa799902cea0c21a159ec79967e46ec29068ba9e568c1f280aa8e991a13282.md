Students intending to specialize in Gender, Race, Sexuality and Social Justice may do so by taking an interdisciplinary major or minor program\.

Please visit the [Institute for Gender, Race, Sexuality and Social Justice](https://grsj.arts.ubc.ca/) for detailed descriptions of the program, courses, and other information\.

### Major in Gender, Race, Sexuality and Social Justice

#### Lower\-level Requirements

Students must take 6 credits of 100\-level GRSJ\_V courses, and 6 credits from 200\-level GRSJ\_V courses or ANTH\_V 213\.

#### Upper\-level Requirements

Students must complete:

-   GRSJ\_V 325, 326, 327, and 328
-   GRSJ\_V 422
-   GRSJ\_V 480
-   At least 12 additional credits from GRSJ\_V or CSIS\_V courses numbered 300 or above\. Other courses may be approved; please consult the [department website](http://grsj.arts.ubc.ca/undergraduate/gender-race-and-social-justice/major-minor/)

### Minor in Gender, Race, Sexuality and Social Justice

#### Lower\-level Requirements

Students must take 6 credits of 100\-level GRSJ\_V courses, and 6 credits of 200\-level GRSJ\_V courses or ANTH\_V 213\.

#### Upper\-level Requirements

-   6 credits of GRSJ\_V 325, 326, 327 or 328; and
-   an additional 12 credits from GRSJ\_V or CSIS\_V courses numbered 300 and higher

Other courses may be approved\. Consult the [department website](http://grsj.arts.ubc.ca/undergraduate/gender-race-and-social-justice/major-minor/)\.
