### Master of Management Dual Degree Program Option

Interested students may apply for the Bachelor of Science \(Global Resource Systems\) – Master of Management Dual Degree Program Option\. For details regarding this Dual Degree Program Option and application see the [Faculty of Commerce and Business Administration](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/node/14792) section of the Academic Calendar\.
