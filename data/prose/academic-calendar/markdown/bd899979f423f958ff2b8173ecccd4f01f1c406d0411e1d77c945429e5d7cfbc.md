#### Carey Centre

Carey Centre provides a Christian residential community for undergraduate and graduate students studying at the University of British Columbia and other local college campuses\. In addition to student housing, Carey Centre provides year\-round accommodation for visitors to the Vancouver area for short\- and long\-term stays\.  
[carey\-edu\.ca](http://carey-edu.ca/)

#### Green College

Green College is a graduate residential college and open venue for interdisciplinary, co\-curricular, and cross\-sectoral inquiry at the University of British Columbia\.  
[greencollege\.ubc\.ca](http://greencollege.ubc.ca/)

#### St\. Andrew's Hall

St\. Andrew's Hall is a theological college of the Presbyterian Church in Canada and provides a multi\-disciplinary residential community located at the heart of the UBC campus\.  
[www\.standrews\.edu](http://www.standrews.edu/)

#### St\. John's College

St\. John’s College is UBC’s international residential college for graduate students, postdoctoral fellows, and visiting scholars from around the world\. Founded in 1997 by alumni of St\. John’s University, Shanghai, China, and named for their alma mater, the College seeks to inspire a global vision and foster a spirit of community among its residents and alumni through multicultural education, intercultural understanding, and international exchange\.  
[stjohns\.ubc\.ca](http://stjohns.ubc.ca/)
