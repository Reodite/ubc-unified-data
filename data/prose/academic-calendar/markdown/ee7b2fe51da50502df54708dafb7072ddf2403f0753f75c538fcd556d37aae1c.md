[Coaching](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/areas-study/coaching-leadership) provides a mutually beneficial learning methodology to unlock someone’s creative potential or facilitate an organization’s transformation, all while enhancing your own communication skills and leadership presence\.  

Whether your passion lies in helping individuals discover their strengths and find the job of their dreams or working with individuals, groups and teams in organizations to navigate change, enhance leadership and achieve strategic objectives, our courses and programs provide the necessary training to upskill or reskill as a credible coach practitioner\. 

Our programs and courses offer various specialized learning pathways, regardless of which you choose, learners will gain the knowledge and confidence to work with individuals, groups and teams toward achieving their goals\.  

[**UBC Certificate in Organizational Coaching**](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/programs-credentials/organizational-coaching-certificate)  
  
Develop practitioner\-level coaching skills to influence positive employee engagement, culture and performance in workplaces and organizations\. As an organizational coach, you can help individuals and teams in organizations to achieve strategic objectives, enhance leadership capacity and be empowered to create solutions for sustainable change\. This is an ICF Level 2 accredited coach training program\. 

[**Mentor Coaching Practitioner**](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/courses/ubc-mentor-coaching-practitioner/0460)    
  
Mentor coaching is an integral part of professional coach accreditation, essential for developing core coaching competencies in leader coaches and coach practitioners\. If you are an accredited coach looking to expand your skill set to include mentoring new and established coaches this course would be beneficial for you\. Earn 40 ICF Continuing Coach Education Units \(CCUEs\) after completion of this program\.     

[**Coaching Neurodiversity at Work**](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/courses/coaching-neurodiversity-work/0493)    
  
Learn how to support, elevate and co\-create with neurodivergent adults in the context of work\. Expand your mindset, language, and tools for coaching at the intersection of identity, systems and work\. Earn 40 ICF Continuing Coach Education Units \(CCUEs\) after completion of this program\.

[**Leader as Coach**](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/programs-credentials/leader-coach-icf-level-1)   
  
Expand your leadership range by integrating your lived experience with a coaching approach and skillset that promotes development, performance, autonomy and resilience for the leader and those they engage with\. This new program is an ICF level 1 accredited coaching training program\. 

[**Career Coaching Practitioner**](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/courses/career-coaching-practitioner/0120)  
  
The ability to have effective career coaching conversations with clients and the people you support can accelerate their personal and career development\. Increase your range and confidence in engaging in career development and personal growth conversations with the people you support\. Earn 20 ICF Continuing Coach Education Units \(CCUEs\) after completion of this course\.
