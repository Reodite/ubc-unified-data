## Living in Acadia Park

Student families live in Acadia Park, which also offers accommodation for eligible couples that include at least one UBC student\. This residence is child\-friendly and promotes healthy families through community activities, support programs, and nearby amenities, such as schools, shopping and green spaces\. Nearby [child care](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/resources-who-are-parents/child-care-school-registration/) is also available, but apply as soon as possible, since waitlists can be lengthy\.

# Rooms at Acadia Park

Acadia Park suites are various sizes and come with [non\-standard floor plans](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/acadia-park-sample-floor-plans/ "Acadia Park Sample Floor Plans")—from split\-level townhouses to high\-rise condos\. Part of the Acadia Park charm is that each suite is unique\.

-   [icon\-one\-bedroom\-suite](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon-one-bedroom-suite.png)  
    
    # One Bedroom
    
    One bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, living area and eating area\. Some suites also have a den\.
    
-   [icon\-two\-bedroom\-suite](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/icon-two-bedroom-suite.png)  
    
    # Two Bedroom
    
    Two bedrooms, bathroom, kitchen, living area and eating area\. Some suites have an additional bathroom\.
    
-   [icon\-three\-bedroom\-suite](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/icon-three-bedroom-suite.png)  
    
    # Three Bedroom
    
    Three bedrooms, two bathrooms, kitchen, living area and eating area\.
    
-   [icon\-four\-bedroom\-suite](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon-four-bedroom-suite.png)  
    
    # Four Bedroom

# Facilities at Acadia Park

-   [icon\_facilities\_frontdesk](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_commonsblock.png)  
    
    ## Commonsblock
    
    The hub for activities, programs and services designed to support education, family life and recreation\. There’s a fitness room, fireside lounge, and activity centre\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_outdoor](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_outdoor.png)  
    
    ## Activities
    
    Acadia Park residents get exclusive access to activities with a [UTown@UBC Community Services Card](http://utown.ubc.ca/discovering-utownubc/utownubc-community-programs/utownubc-community-services-card/apply-utownubc)\. Residence Life staff also organize a variety of events and programs throughout the year\.
    
-   [sorting station](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/icons_sorting_orange.png)  
    
    ## Sort It Out\!
    
    Sustainability is a shared value in residence\. Acadia Park has a large sorting area with collection bins for household recyclables, e\-waste, charitable donations, and trash\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_laundry](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_laundry.png)  
    
    ## Laundry Facilities
    
    Machines operate on a card system—no need to worry about coins\. Machines are available for suites in townhouses, Sopron House and Point Grey Apartments\.
    

-   [icon\_facilities\_parking](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_parking.png)  
    
    ## Parking &amp; Bicycle Storage
    
    [Parking](https://parking.ubc.ca/) is available for a fee\. Bicycle racks are available\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_frontdesk](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_frontdesk.png)  
    
    ## Support Staff
    
    From our Front Desk staff and Overnight Mobile Team to Residence Life professional and student staff, you are never alone\! [Meet the people](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/meet-the-team/) you can contact with questions, concerns or a request for support\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_health](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_health.png)  
    
    ## Health &amp; Safety
    
    Residents have access to a campus medical clinic and safety programs such as SafeWalk\. There are regular nightly patrols of residence properties\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_family](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/icon_facilities_family.png)  
    
    ## Family Friendly
    
    Playgrounds for children, family\-centric programs, and a close\-knit community\.
    

## Sustainability Features

UBC has been recognized internationally for its commitment to sustainable living\. All residences have waste sorting areas and residences and modern [sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/ "Sustainability Features") designed to reduce waste, energy consumption, and water use\.
