Your Residence Contract is the agreement you sign with [Student Housing and Community Services](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/contact-us/ "Contact Us") when accepting your room in residence\.

Contracts contain important information about the [rights and responsibilities](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/rights-and-responsibilities/) of those living in the residence community\. For students moving into Orchard Commons, Place Vanier or Totem Park, your contract also contains information about the required [Residence Meal Plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/residence-meal-plan-all-access-dining/ "Totem &amp; Vanier")\.

[Adobe Acrobat Reader](https://acrobat.adobe.com/ca/en/acrobat/pdf-reader.html) is required to view/download residence contracts\.
