Students in residence are strongly advised to carry residential insurance protection to cover their liability for loss or damage to the property of others \(including UBC’s property\), for personal injury to others, and for loss or damage to their own personal property\.

## How do I get renter’s insurance?

Many companies offer the option of extending a parent’s home insurance policy to cover your room in residence\. However, the best protection may be achieved through a standard tenant insurance policy\.

The company you purchase renter’s insurance from will need some information about your residence building\. You’ll find that information below\!

### Local insurance providers

Here are several local insurance providers you might consider:

-   [BCAA](https://www.bcaa.com/insurance/home)
-   [Westland](https://www.westlandinsurance.ca/)
-   [Apollo](https://info.apollocover.com/ubc)

Plus, visit the [Insurance Brokers Association of British Columbia](https://ibabc.org/) for more information and resources regarding renter’s and general insurance and to [find additional insurance brokers](https://ibabc.org/FindBroker)\.

*Please note that UBC does not endorse or verify the qualifications of any insurance providers or their products\. Renter’s insurance is a personal matter for students, and UBC does not involve itself in these decisions\.*
