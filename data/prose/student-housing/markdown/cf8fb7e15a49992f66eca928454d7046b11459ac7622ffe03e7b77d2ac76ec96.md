## Summary

Six bedroom suites are available for Winter Session, Year Round, and Summer Session\. Students seeking Summer Residence are placed in [Fairview Crescent](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/fairview-crescent/ "Fairview Crescent")\.

-   Furnished
-   Shared kitchen and bathrooms
-   [High\-speed internet access](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet)
-   [Bed linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens") not included
-   Photos/layouts are samples only—suites vary by location

### Furnishings

Twin XL \(extra long\) bed, wardrobe, dresser, desk, lamp, chairs, bookshelf, closet, couches, and tables\. Amenities vary by location\.

# Facilities

-   [icon\_facilities\_kitchen](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/icon_facilities_kitchen.png)  
    
    ## Kitchen
    
    Standard kitchen includes sink, stove, oven and fridge\. Some suites have a microwave\. Fairview Crescent six bedroom suites have a dishwasher\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_shared](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/icon_facilities_shared.png)  
    
    ## Bathroom
    
    Walter Gage suites have one large shared bathroom\. Fairview Crescent and Fraser Hall have multiple smaller bathrooms\. All bathrooms include toilet, sink, shower and storage cabinets\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_landline](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/icon_landline.png)  
    
    ## Telephone Landline
    
    Use of a residence landline is optional\. Additional charges apply\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_internet](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/icon_facilities_internet.png)  
    
    ## Internet
    
    [Wireless and/or hard\-wired high\-speed internet access](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet) \(ethernet cable not included\)\. Wireless\-only at Fraser Hall and Walter Gage\.
    

## Sustainability Features

UBC has been recognized internationally for its commitment to sustainable living\. Many residences and rooms have modern [sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/ "Sustainability Features") designed to reduce waste, energy consumption, and water use\.
