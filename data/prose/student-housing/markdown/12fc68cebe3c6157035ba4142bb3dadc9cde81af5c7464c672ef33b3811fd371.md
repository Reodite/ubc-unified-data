## **Doing laundry in residence is easy as 1, 2, 3\!**

### 1\. Purchase your laundry card

Buy your card \($5\) at the card reload centre in your residence commonsblock\.

If you’re moving into Place Vanier, Ritsumeikan\-UBC House, Totem Park or Walter Gage, you’ll receive your laundry card in your check\-in package\. Orchard Commons residents will use the Coinamatic app instead of a laundry card\.

### 2\. Add money to your card

Add money to your card in increments of $10, $20 or $50\.

Please note, this fall UBC Student Housing will transition to a new laundry service provider in residence\. **Until then, please only load small amounts onto your laundry card—just enough to cover what you expect to use between now and November\.**

### 3\. It’s laundry time\!

Use your card at the laundry machines located in your residence building\.

#### 2025 pricing

-   Wash:
    -   $1\.25 \(cold\)
    -   $1\.50 \(warm/hot\)
-   Dry:
    -   $1\.25 \(60 minutes\)

#### How much can I expect to spend on laundry total?

Costs will vary based on how many loads of laundry you need to do, what kind of clothing you’re washing/drying, if you choose cold water or hot, and if you will use the dryer or hang dry your clothing\.

-   **Weekly estimate:** budget $5–$10 per week \(two to four loads of laundry, including up to one hour of dryer time per load\)\.
-   **8\-month total:** based on the weekly estimate, expect to spend between $150 and $300 between September and April \(for 8\-month, Winter Session residents\)\.
-   **12\-month total:** based on the weekly estimate, expect to spend between $260 and $520 per year \(for 12\-month, Year Round residents\)\.

Pro Tip

Use cold water and liquid detergent for maximum washing benefits\.
