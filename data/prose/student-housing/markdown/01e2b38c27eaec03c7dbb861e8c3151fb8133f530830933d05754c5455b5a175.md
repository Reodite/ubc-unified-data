**UBC offers commuter students affordable overnight accommodation during Winter Session to support academic success and involvement in campus co\-curricular activities\.**

Commuter Student Hostel rooms, located in tə šxʷhəleləm̓s tə k̓ʷaƛ̓kʷəʔaʔɬ student residence, are available Sunday to Thursday from mid\-September to the end of April \(except during the winter holiday break\)\. During exam periods, hostel rooms are available seven days a week\.

## How to make a reservation

-   Reservations open in September 14, 2026
-   Please visit the [online reservation system](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/shib/sgw/ws_hostel.reservation?p_group_code=CSH25) to book your room\.

### Reservations FYI

-   Online reservations only; we are unable to make reservations by phone\.
-   You must book at least 24 hours in advance\.
-   You must present your UBC student ID card upon check in\. Your room access will be programmed to your UBC student ID card\.
-   Only one booking is permitted at a time per student\.
-   There is a maximum stay of two nights in a seven\-day period\.
-   For weekends with a stat holiday on the Monday, reservations are not available for the Sunday evenings—except during exam periods\.
-   Reservations are not available during [Midterm Breaks](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/dates-and-deadlines) or during the [Winter Break](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/winter-break-residence-closure/)\.

## Payment

-   Reserve your room online using a credit card\.
-   Pay for your stay with a credit card, debit card or cash at check\-in\.

## Parking

-   Limited overnight parking is available for an additional $20 per night, including taxes\.
-   Parking permits are valid from 4 pm to 11 am\.

## Check\-in / check out info

-   **Time:** Check in after 4 pm; check out by 11 am\.
-   **Location:** Check in and out at the Walter Gage Residence front desk \(open 24 hours per day for hostel and hostel guests\)\.

## Conduct and safety

Guests must adhere to the standards of conduct outlined in the Commuter Student Hostel \(CSH\) Agreement\.

## Cancellations

Cancellations must be made 48 hours in advance\. If you cancel less than 48 hours prior to your reservation, you will be charged for one night’s stay\.

## Summer bookings

From mid\-May to mid\-August each year, students can book a room at the [Pacific Spirit Hostel](https://pacificspirithostel.com/), located at Place Vanier residence\.
