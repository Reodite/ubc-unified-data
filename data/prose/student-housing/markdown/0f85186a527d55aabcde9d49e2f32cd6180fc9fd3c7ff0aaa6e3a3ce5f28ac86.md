Hi, we’re Stephanie, Sabina and Isabella, UBC’s Counsellors in Residence\. University can be a challenging time\. There are lots of [support resources](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/counsellor-in-residence/#resources) at UBC—and we’re here to provide additional support for students living in UBC Student Housing\.

### Access free resources\!

Get your copies of our free [activity and journaling workbooks](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/counsellor-in-residence/#resources)\.

## Top reasons to make an appointment

-   Sadness
-   Feeling overwhelmed
-   Stress
-   Anxiety
-   Relationship troubles
-   Loss and/or grief
-   Past experience of physical, sexual, or emotional violence
-   Other mental health challenges

## What to expect

You’ll come in for a 50\-minute session, where Sabina, Stephanie and Isabella will listen to your needs and help you meet your immediate personal goals\. Many students find that a single counselling session helps them to get on track and improve their well\-being\.

Sabina, Stephanie or Isabella may also recommend self\-directed resources, workshops and coaching, group therapy, or individual short\-term counselling\. If longer\-term counselling is needed, they will help connect you with resources and options in the local community\.

In addition to daytime appointments, the Counsellors in Residence are able to book appointments in the early evening and on weekends\.

## How to make an appointment

### Book online

Book your appointment today using the UBC Student Health and Wellbeing online booking system\.

[BOOK ONLINE](https://ubcshw.inputhealth.com/ebooking#new)

### Appointment FYI

-   Appointments become available two weeks from the appointment date\.
-   If all of the available appointments are booked, you may encounter an alert that there are no available appointments\. If this happens, please consider checking back frequently since new appointment times open daily, including when someone submits a cancellation\.
-   If it’s not an emergency, consider calling [Here2Talk](https://here2talk.ca/main) while you wait for an appointment\. Through this program, all students – domestic or international – currently registered in a B\.C\. post\-secondary institution have access to free, confidential counselling and community referral services, conveniently available 24/7 via app, phone and web\. Additionally, visit UBC’s central [Counselling Services](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services) website to learn more about drop\-in appointments, available Monday through Friday\.

## Questions?

If you have any questions about Counsellor in Residence, like what to expect in a session or whether you should make an appointment, send an email to [counsellor\.residence@ubc\.ca](mailto:counsellor.residence@ubc.ca)\!

## Confidentiality

Our Counsellors in Residence and all of UBC’s health professionals are committed to keeping your visit and concerns completely private and confidential\.

Information about you and your health can only be shared with your permission, or when safety concerns or legal requirements arise\.

If you have any questions about confidentiality, please ask during your appointment\.
