### All Applicants

-   $50 non\-refundable application fee required\.
-   All residence offers are sent by email and include an expiry date to respond and pay a deposit\.
-   Separate residence applications required for UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan\.
-   Submitting an application does not guarantee an offer will be made\.
-   Prospective undergraduates can submit a residence application after applying to UBC\.

### Winter Session Residence—September to April

[Fairview Crescent](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/fairview-crescent/ "Fairview Crescent") \| [Orchard Commons](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/orchard-commons/) \| [Place Vanier](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/place-vanier/ "Place Vanier") \| [Ritsumeikan\-UBC House](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/ritsumaiken-ubc-house/ "Ritsumeiken-UBC House") \| [Totem Park](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/totem-park/ "Totem Park") \| [Walter Gage](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/walter-gage/ "Walter Gage")

### Year Round Student Family Residence—ongoing

[Acadia Park](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/acadia-park/ "Acadia Park")

### Early Arrival

Early arrivals are not typically permitted\. [Eligible](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/eligibility/ "Eligibility") students must apply by the [required deadline](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/important-dates/ "Dates &amp; Deadlines")\.

-   There is an [early arrival fee](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/early-arrival/ "UBC Early Arrival Fees")\.
-   May be required to move to different accommodation on short notice \(less than 24 hours\)\.
