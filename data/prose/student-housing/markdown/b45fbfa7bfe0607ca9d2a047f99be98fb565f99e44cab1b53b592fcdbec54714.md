## Living in Place Vanier

Place Vanier is for students new to UBC who want space and time for studying, the comforts of home, and a relaxed communal atmosphere perfect for making new friends\. Students always have someone to walk to class with, talk to, or have dinner, all within a fun, safe, and supportive environment\.

## Quick Facts

-   Winter session \(September to April\)
-   First\-year undergraduate students
-   Most residents eligible for [First Year Guarantee](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/first-year-guarantee/ "First Year Guarantee")
-   [Residence dining plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/residence-meal-plan-all-access-dining/)
-   1,422 beds in 12 single\- and mixed\-gender houses; most floors single\-gender
-   Furnished rooms: 1,048 singles, 4 singles with private bathrooms, 179 shared rooms, 6 shared rooms with shared private bathrooms
-   [High\-speed wireless internet](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet)
-   [Eligibility requirements](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/eligibility/ "Eligibility")
-   [Fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/place-vanier/)
-   [Residence sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/)
-   Take a [virtual tour of Place Vanier](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-virtual-tours/#place-vanier)

# Facilities at Place Vanier

-   [icon\_facilities\_mealplan](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_mealplan.png)  
    
    ## Meal Plan
    
    Place Vanier residents don’t have to cook\. The mandatory [All Access Dining Plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/residence-meal-plan-all-access-dining/) is valid at more than 30 campus locations, including Gather—Place Vanier’s on\-site dining hall\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_commonsblock](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_commonsblock.png)  
    
    ## Commonsblock
    
    The hub for many residence activities\. Includes: Wi\-Fi, dining room, fitness room, music rooms, group and private study spaces, ballroom, games lounge, and after\-hours convenience store\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_frontdesk](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_frontdesk.png)  
    
    ## Support Staff
    
    From our Front Desk staff and Overnight Mobile Team to Residence Life professional and student staff, you are never alone\! [Meet the people](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/meet-the-team/) you can contact with questions, concerns or a request for support\.
    
-   [sorting station](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/icons_sorting_orange.png)  
    
    ## Sort It Out\!
    
    Sustainability is a shared value in residence\. Place Vanier has a large sorting area with collection bins for household recyclables, e\-waste, charitable donations, and trash\. The dining hall has an area to separate and compost food waste\.
    

-   [icon\_facilities\_laundry](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_laundry.png)  
    
    ## Laundry Facilities
    
    Machines operate on a convenient card system—no need to worry about coins\. Facilities are located within each residence block\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_parking](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_parking.png)  
    
    ## Parking &amp; Bicycle Storage
    
    Limited monthly [parking](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/parking/) is available for a fee\. Bicycle storage is available\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_outdoor](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_outdoor.png)  
    
    ## Outdoor Facilities
    
    Place Vanier and Totem Park have on\-site tennis and basketball courts and many acres of green space\. Nearby Pacific Spirit Regional Park has over 35 kilometres of multipurpose trails to explore\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_health](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_health.png)  
    
    ## Health &amp; Safety
    
    Residents can access the on\-campus medical clinic and safety programs such as SafeWalk\. There are regular nightly patrols of residence properties\.
    

## Sustainability Features

UBC has been recognized internationally for its commitment to sustainable living\. Many residences and rooms have modern [sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/ "Sustainability Features") designed to reduce waste, energy consumption, and water use\.
