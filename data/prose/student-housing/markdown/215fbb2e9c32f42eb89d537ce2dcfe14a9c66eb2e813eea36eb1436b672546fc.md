## Living in Orchard Commons

Orchard Commons is one of our newest residences\. It's ideal for students who are new to UBC and want space and time for studying, the comforts of home, and a relaxed communal atmosphere ideal for making new friends\. Students always have someone to walk to class with, talk to, or have dinner with, all within a fun and supportive environment\.

The building features stunning architecture and design, a LEED Gold certificate, dining facilities as well as interconnected floor lounges with snack kitchens and wonderful mountain and ocean views\. It is also home to Vantage College\.

# Facilities at Orchard Commons

-   [icon\_facilities\_mealplan](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_mealplan.png)  
    
    ## Meal Plan
    
    Orchard Commons residents don’t have to cook\. The mandatory [All Access Dining Plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/residence-meal-plan-all-access-dining/) is valid at more than 30 campus locations, including Open Kitchen—the on\-site dining hall\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_commonsblock](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_commonsblock.png)  
    
    ## Commonsblock
    
    The hub for many residence activities\. Includes: Wi\-Fi, Dining Room, fitness room, music rooms, group and private study spaces and games lounge
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_frontdesk](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_frontdesk.png)  
    
    ## Support Staff
    
    From our Front Desk staff and Overnight Mobile Team to Residence Life professional and student staff, you are never alone\! [Meet the people](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/meet-the-team/) you can contact with questions, concerns or a request for support\.
    
-   [sorting station](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/icons_sorting_orange.png)  
    
    ## Sort It Out\!
    
    Sustainability is a shared value in residence\. Orchard Commons has waste sorting stations with collection bins for household recyclables, e\-waste and charitable donations\. The dining hall has an area to separate and compost food waste\.
    

-   [icon\_facilities\_laundry](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_laundry.png)  
    
    ## Laundry Facilities
    
    Machines operate on a convenient card system—no need to worry about coins\. Facilities are located in each House\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_parking](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_parking.png)  
    
    ## Parking &amp; Bicycle Storage
    
    Monthly [parking](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/parking/) is available for a fee\. A bike hook is provided in your room\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_outdoor](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_outdoor.png)  
    
    ## Outdoor Facilities
    
    Orchard Commons features a large courtyard perfect for lounging or playing sports and it is adjacent to many campus recreation facilities\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_health](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_health.png)  
    
    ## Health &amp; Safety
    
    Residents have access to a full array of services including the campus medical clinic, couselling services, and personal safety programs\.
    

## Sustainability Features

UBC has been recognized internationally for its commitment to sustainable living\. Many residences and rooms have modern [sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/ "Sustainability Features") designed to reduce waste, energy consumption, and water use\. Read the design &amp; sustainability [case study](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Orchard-Commons-Design-Case-Study.pdf) for Orchard Commons\.
