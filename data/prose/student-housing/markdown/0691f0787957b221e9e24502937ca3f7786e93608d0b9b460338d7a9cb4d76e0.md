**In SHCS, we are grateful to have worked directly with the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm \(Musqueam\) Nation since 2011 on several student residence projects, for which they generously gifted hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓\-language names\.**

Learn more about and how to pronounce the gifted hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓\-language residence and house names, read about the history of  Indigenous names at Totem Park, and explore the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm plants located at many of our residence areas and across campus\.
