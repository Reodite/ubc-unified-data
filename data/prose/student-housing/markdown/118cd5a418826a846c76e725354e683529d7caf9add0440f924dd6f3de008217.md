Please forward the following documents to the [Student Housing Main Office](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/contact-us/ "SHHS Main Office") in support of your contract cancellation request:

#### Graduation

Please provide a letter from your Faculty Advising office confirming the date that you are expected to complete your degree requirements\.

#### Co\-op Work Term Placement

Please provide a letter from your Co\-op office confirming the dates that you will be on a Co\-op Work Placement\. Residents who are placed locally are welcome to remain in residence while they are on a work placement\.

#### Go Global Student Exchange Program

Please provide a letter from the Go Global office confirming the dates of your exchange program\.

#### Teaching Practicum

Please provide a letter from the Teacher Education Office confirming your practicum dates\.

#### Graduate Students

Please provide a copy of your Program Completion and Closure Date memo from the Faculty of Graduate Studies\.
