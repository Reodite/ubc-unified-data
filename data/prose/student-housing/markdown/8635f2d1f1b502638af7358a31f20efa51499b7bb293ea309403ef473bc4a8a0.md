# [Shared Room](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/rooms/shared-room/)

[Shared Room](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/Room_detail_shared_PV_1170x660.jpg)

Shared traditional rooms—double occupancy—are found in [Place Vanier](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/place-vanier/ "Place Vanier") and [Totem Park](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/totem-park/ "Totem Park") where on a standard floor 25–30 residents share communal bathrooms\. [Orchard Commons](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/orchard-commons/), [Place Vanier](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/place-vanier/ "Place Vanier") and [Totem Park](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/totem-park/ "Totem Park") also have shared rooms with a shared private bathroom\.

**First Year \| Winter Session \| Mandatory Meal Plan**

# [Connected Single Room](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/rooms/connected-single-room/)

[Connected Single Room](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/Room_detail_single_connected_TP_1170x660.jpg)

Connected single rooms are located in [Totem Park](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/totem-park/ "Totem Park") and [Orchard Commons](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/orchard-commons)\. Rooms connect by a bathroom that is shared between two residents \(single gender\)\. On a standard floor, 40 residents share a common area\.

**First Year \| Winter Session \| Mandatory Meal Plan**

**First Year \| Winter Session \| Mandatory Meal Plan**

# [Nano Suite](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/rooms/nano/)

[Nano Suite](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Exchange-Nano-Header.jpg)

Nano suites are a unique room type at UBC, designed for the student who wants a room to suit their minimalist lifestyle\. Nanos pack a convertible desk/bed, full private bathroom, kitchenette and closet into a small but streamlined space\.

**Upper Year \| Graduate Students \|** **Year Round**
