There are a few ways that students in residence can request to switch rooms\. We try our best to accommodate these requests, but cannot make guarantees\.

Students in Winter Session residence areas can apply for a room switch [before](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/switch/#beforemovein) or [after](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/switch/#aftermovein) moving in\. Students in Year Round residence areas can apply for a room switch [after moving in](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/switch/#aftermovein)\.

## Find out what room types you are eligible to switch to

Before looking for another student to switch with, make sure you are eligible to switch into their room type\. Eligibility differs depending on your residence contract\.

Please note that room switches may also be subject to age and gender requirements, and all switch requests require approval\.

### Winter Session Housing

**First year residence**

If you live in a first year residence, you are only eligible to switch with another student who lives in a first year residence\. This includes students living in first year suite\-style residences, such as Walter Gage or Ritsumeikan House, and students living in meal plan residences, such as Totem Park, Place Vanier or Orchard Commons\.

As Walter Gage has designated first year and upper year suite\-style units, please reach out to [information@housing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:information@housing.ubc.ca) if you have found a potential switch and want to confirm it is eligible\.

**Upper year residence**

If you live in an upper year Winter Session residence, you are only eligible to switch with a student in the same room type\. You may switch across residence areas provided the room type is the same\.

For example, students in suite\-style units may switch with students in other suite\-style units \(such as Fairview Crescent or Walter Gage\), and students in self\-contained units \(studios and one\-bedroom apartments\) may switch with students in other self\-contained units\. Switches between suite\-style and self\-contained unit types cannot be approved\.

### **Year Round Housing**

If you live in Year Round Housing, you are only eligible to switch with a student in the same room type\. The student may live in a different Year Round residence area, provided the room type is the same\.

For example, students in shared suite\-style units may switch with students in other shared suite\-style units, and students in self\-contained units \(studios and one\-bedroom apartments\) may switch with students in other self\-contained units\. Switches between suite\-style and self\-contained unit types cannot be approved\.

## Requesting a switch before move\-in

*Winter Session residents only\.*

**You can request a switch between the date you receive your room assignment \(mid\-August\) and the deadline of August 18, 2026\.**

Your room assignment is not available prior to the date it is released and we cannot process switch requests before then\. For switch requests after August 18, find out how to [request a switch after move\-in](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/switch/#aftermovein)\.

### 1\. Find another UBC student to switch with

First, find another UBC student to switch with\! To help you find another student to switch rooms with, you can use our [Room Switch forum](https://www.facebook.com/groups/262294609938407/) on Facebook\.

### 2\. Both students apply for the room switch

You *both* must [submit a request via the form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6XbvchLAZN5OQRw) below on the same day\. Please be prepared to provide the following information:

-   Your full name and student ID\#
-   Current room assignment \(room, house, residence\)
-   Full name of the person you agreed to switch with and their current room assignment

*The deadline to switch rooms before move\-in has passed\. Find out how to [request a switch after move\-in](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/switch/?preview_id=5274&amp;preview_nonce=8e0518bcac&amp;_thumbnail_id=12142&amp;preview=true#aftermovein)\.*

### FAQs

How long does it take to get a response?

About five business days\. We’ll contact you by email to confirm the status of your request\.

Is the request guaranteed if both students agree to it?

No\. You must both be [eligible](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/eligibility/ "Eligibility") to live in each other’s room\. There may be age and gender restrictions\.

What if I don’t find anyone to switch with—or if I prefer not to post on the room switch forum?

If you’re not able to find anyone to switch with, you’re expected to move into your original assignment, but you still have options\. See our information below about applying for a room switch or transfer [after moving in](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/switch/#aftermovein)\.

When is the deadline to request a room switch before moving in?

August 18, 2025\.

## Requesting a switch after move\-in

*Winter Session and Year Round residents\. For requests after August 18, 2026\.*

### Winter Session residents

If you would like to switch rooms after moving into residence and you [find another UBC student](https://www.facebook.com/groups/262294609938407/) who shares the same floor, house, or residence area with you and who wants to switch, please visit your residence Front Desk together to discuss your request\.

Please keep in mind that what unit type you are eligible to will depend on whether you are assigned to a first year or upper year unit type, please see the section at the top of the page to ensure your switch can be considered\.

Students who live in different residence areas can also request a switch\. If you are requesting a room switch with someone from a different residence area, both students must physically come into the [Student Housing and Community Services Main Office](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Student+Housing+and+Community+Services+Main+Office/@49.2614954,-123.2556301,18z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x548672cabe683e51:0xc2b571f29976c0da!8m2!3d49.2612501!4d-123.2555809) to request the switch\. If the room switch is approved, both students must complete the switch on that day\.

There is a $10 administration fee per person\.

Please keep in mind, Upper Year students in Winter Session Housing are only eligible to switch with a student in the same unit type as them\. Students in shared accommodation may only switch with another student in shared accommodation, and students in self\-contained units \(studios and one\-bedroom apartments\) may only switch with another student in a self\-contained unit\. Switches between shared and self\-contained unit types cannot be approved\.

### Year Round residents

You are welcome to [find another UBC student to switch with](https://www.facebook.com/groups/262294609938407/) before moving in, but you can only apply for the switch after moving in\. You are eligible to switch rooms with another UBC student in the same residence area and room type\.

To apply for the room switch, both students—after moving in—must visit their residence Front Desk together to make the request\.

Students who live in different residence areas but the same room type can also request a switch\. If you are requesting a room switch with someone from a different residence area, both students must physically come into the Student Housing and Community Services Main Office to request the switch\. If the room switch is approved, both students must complete the switch on that day\.

Students who live in different room types are not eligible to switch rooms\. Instead, you are eligible to [apply for a room transfer](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/)\.

There is a $10 administration fee per person\.

## **Room Transfers**

### Winter Session residents

If you would like to move rooms, but you are unable to find another student to switch with, beginning at the end of the first week of classes you can [apply online for a room transfer](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/)\. Room Transfers are offered based on availability and your [eligibility](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/eligibility/) to live in the proposed residence area and room\.

Before applying, we encourage you to speak with your [Residence Life Manager](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/contact-us/), who would be happy to discuss your concerns, help you find a solution and access resources\.

There is a $50 fee for room transfers\.

### Year Round residents

If you would like to move to a different room type or to a room in a different residence area, beginning at the end of the first week of classes you can [apply online for a room transfer](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/)\. Room Transfers are offered based on availability and your [eligibility](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/eligibility/) to live in the proposed residence area and room\.

Before applying, we encourage you to speak with your [Residence Life Manager](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/contact-us/), who would be happy to discuss your concerns, help you find a solution and access resources\.

There is a $50 fee for room transfers\.
