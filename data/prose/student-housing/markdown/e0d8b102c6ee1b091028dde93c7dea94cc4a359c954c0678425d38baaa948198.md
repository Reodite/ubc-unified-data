## Summary

Four bedroom suites have many different layouts and are available for Year Round and Winter Session\.

-   Furnished
-   Shared kitchen and bathroom\(s\)
-   [High\-speed internet access](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet)
-   [Bed linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens") not included
-   Photos/layouts are samples only—suites vary by location

### Furnishings

Twin XL \(extra long\) bed, wardrobe, dresser, desk, lamp, chairs, bookshelf, closet, couches, table, and chairs\. Amenities vary by location\.

# Facilities

-   [icon\_facilities\_kitchen](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/icon_facilities_kitchen.png)  
    
    ## Kitchen
    
    Standard kitchen includes sink, stove, oven and fridge\. Some suites have a dishwasher and microwave\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_shared](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/icon_facilities_shared.png)  
    
    ## Bathroom
    
    Bathrooms are shared and include toilet, sink, shower and storage cabinets\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_landline](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/icon_landline.png)  
    
    ## Telephone \(landline\)
    
    Use of a residence landline is optional, where available\. Additional charges apply\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_internet](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/icon_facilities_internet.png)  
    
    ## Internet
    
    [Wireless and/or hard\-wired high\-speed internet access](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet) \(ethernet cable not included\)\. Wireless\-only at Brock Commons, Exchange and tə šxʷhəleləm̓s tə k̓ʷaƛ̓kʷəʔaʔɬ\.
    

## Sustainability Features

UBC is recognized internationally for its commitment to sustainable living\. Many residences and rooms have contemporary [sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/ "Sustainability Features") designed to reduce waste, energy consumption, and water use\.
