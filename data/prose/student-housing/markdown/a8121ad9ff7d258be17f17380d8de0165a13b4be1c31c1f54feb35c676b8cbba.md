## Ready\!

### What are you applying for?

#### Winter Session Residence—September to April

Mainly for new\-to\-UBC students who qualify for the [First Year Guarantee](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/first-year-guarantee/ "Who Gets Priority")\.

#### Year Round Residence—typically May to April

For upper\-year students, graduate students and student families\.

#### Summer Residence—May to August

For UBC students who are studying or staying on\-campus for the summer—or for visiting research students\.

Are you [eligible to live in residence?](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/eligibility/ "Eligibility")

## Set\!

### Where would you like to live?

When applying, we’ll ask you to rank your room preferences\. [See what’s available](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/residences/ "At a Glance") and make a list\. Remember: we try our best to accommodate these requests but cannot make guarantees\.

Applying to live in Marine Drive or Ponderosa Commons?

Be aware that the Lower Mall Precinct project, a new 1,500\-bed student residence, is being built nearby\. There will be construction activity and noise in the area\. [Learn more](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/projects/lower-mall-precinct/construction/)\. 

Applying to live in Thunderbird Residence?

Be aware that building repairs at Thunderbird are taking place in summer 2026, and there will be construction activity and noise in the area\. [Learn more](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/thunderbird-repairs/)\.

### Application fee

Please note that we require a $50 non\-refundable application fee\.

### Do you need additional documents?

Some students will need to have additional documents ready\.

-   Students with disabilities or ongoing health conditions can apply for [accessible student housing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/accessibility/)\.
-   Applicants requesting accommodation at Green College and/or St\. John’s College must apply for membership to the colleges directly\.
-   Applicants to Student Family Residence must send documents for all occupants—birth certificates, visas, passports and/or medical documentation—to Student Housing and Community Services\.
