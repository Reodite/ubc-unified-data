**Information and resources for parents, guardians and families of students living in residence\.**

Residence is so much more than a place for your student to sleep and study\. Students and alumni tell us that living on campus helped them become better students and feel like part of a community\.

[Research shows](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/reasons-to-live-in-residence/) that students who live in residence tend to be more successful in their academic studies, have increased self\-confidence, and develop stronger life skills compared to students who commute\.

The UBC Student Housing team is here to help support your student’s residence experience and academic achievement, along with their personal growth and wellbeing\.

## Information privacy

Your student’s records and information at UBC are protected under BC’s [Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/subject-areas/access-and-privacy-general/) \(FIPPA\), which means that the information your student provides to UBC and information UBC has on file about their academic progress, conduct and wellbeing, or their student housing application and information is not available to anybody other than them—even you\. However, your student can authorize you to access certain information or data, if needed, by granting [third\-party authorization](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/third-party-authorization)\.

Learn more about [information privacy and student housing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/privacy/)\.

## Eating on campus

At UBC, it’s easy to enjoy delicious, high\-quality foods every day\.

-   **First year students** who live in Orchard Commons, Place Vanier and Totem Park, purchase a required [Residence Meal Plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/residence-meal-plan-all-access-dining/)\. Those assigned to a suite\-style residence have their own shared kitchen, but might also consider purchasing the [UBCcard Value Plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/ubccard-value-plan/) or an [All Access Dining Swipe Plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/residence-meal-plan-all-access-dining/#AADSwipePlan)\.
-   **Upper year students** who live in a suite\-style residence have a shared kitchen and can cook in their suite or [eat elsewhere on campus](https://food.ubc.ca/feedme/)—and can enjoy added convenience and savings with a [UBCcard Value Plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/ubccard-value-plan/)\.
-   **Dietary supports:** [UBC Food Services’ dietitians](https://food.ubc.ca/nutrition/) provide student residents with advice and support on healthy eating, and managing [food allergies](https://food.ubc.ca/nutrition/allergies/) while dining on campus\.

Send a gift

Parents and Guardians can send care packages, gift cards and load money onto their student’s UBCcard\. [Learn more](https://food.ubc.ca/gifts/)\.

## Health and wellbeing

Between academics, paid or volunteer work, clubs, sports, friends and family, student life is vibrant and full\. Staying healthy and taking care of personal wellbeing is also a critical part of student success\. We encourage parents to discuss these topics with their student before they move to campus and throughout their time at UBC\.

-   **Health insurance:** Learn about [basic and extended health insurance](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance) for UBC students\.
-   **Health and wellbeing:** Find key residence and UBC resources to support your student’s [health and wellbeing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/health-safety/health-wellness/), including our [Counsellor in Residence](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/counsellor-in-residence/) program\.
-   **Fitness facilities:** Our residence commonsblocks include fully\-equipped fitness facilities, and we offer [free fitness and yoga classes](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/fitness/) for residents\. Plus, there are plenty of additional [UBC Recreation fitness facilities and classes](https://recreation.ubc.ca/) open to all UBC students\.
-   **Substance use:** Learn more about [alcohol, cannabis and nicotine](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/alcohol-cannabis-nicotine) use on campus, along with information about [substance use and harm reduction](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/substance-use-harm-reduction)\.
-   **Sex and sexual health:** Learning more about [sex and sexual health](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/sex-sexual-health)—including information about [navigating sex and consent](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/sex-sexual-health#navigating-sex)—can help someone have safer, respectful, and enjoyable experiences\. See our Residence Life blog post, [Consent \(for parents and guardians\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/consent-for-parents-and-guardians/), as well\.
-   **Understanding stress:** Learn more about [stress and the stress response](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/understanding-stress)\.
-   **Sustainability in residence:** Find out more about [sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/) in our residence buildings and more\.
