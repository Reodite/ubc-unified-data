**Are you a student living in residence? Residence Life has a ton of programming just for you\!**

## Prof Chats

Did you know we have a [Professor in Residence](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/get-involved/professor-in-residence/)? In addition to offering weekly drop\-in hours, the Prof in Residence hosts various events in residence, eats meals in the dining hall, and is available to answer questions about your academic success\.

## Residence Life Presents

A series of free, in\-person workshops offered to residents\. Check out the [residence events calendar](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/events/) for a list of upcoming workshops and to register\.

Past offerings have included:

-   Substance use First Aid
-   Self\-defense workshops
-   Nutrition and healthy eating workshops

## Fitness in Residence

Free yoga and group fitness sessions are offered to all students who live in UBC Student Housing\. Visit [fitness in residence](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/fitness/) for details\.

## Counsellor in Residence programming

Our [Counsellors in Residence](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/counsellor-in-residence/) don’t just offer 1\-to\-1 appointments\. They also offer fun events and sessions throughout the term\! Everything from board game nights to exam virtual mindfulness sessions\. Check out the [residence events calendar](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/events/) for more info\!

## Grad Student Programs

Are you a grad student? Don’t miss out on all the Residence Life events and activities just for you\! It’s a great way to catch up with other grad student friends—and meet new ones\. Visit [grad student programs](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/grad-student-programs/) for details\.

## Additional Residence Programs

RAs also organize community, house and residence\-specific programs and events for residents\. Watch for info from your RA\!
