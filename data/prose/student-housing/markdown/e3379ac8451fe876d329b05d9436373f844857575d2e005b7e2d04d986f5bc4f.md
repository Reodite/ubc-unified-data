## Summary

Single rooms are found in [Totem Park](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/totem-park/ "Totem Park") and [Place Vanier](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/place-vanier/ "Place Vanier") \(first year residences\)\. On a standard floor, 25\-30 residents share communal bathrooms\. Winter Session only\.

-   Furnished private room, communal bathroom
-   Mandatory [Residence Meal Plan](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/dining/meal-plans/totem-vanier/ "Totem &amp; Vanier")
-   [In\-room access to high\-speed wireless internet \(Wi\-Fi\)](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet)
-   Floor lounges have TVs with basic cable
-   [Bed linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens") not included
-   Photos/layouts are samples only—suites vary by location

### Furnishings

Adjustable height twin XL \(extra long\) bed, desk, chair, desk lamp, wardrobe, bookshelf, and closet\. Room amenities can vary by location\.

    ## Common Spaces
    
    Each floor has a lounge with a snack kitchen and basic cable TV\. The Commonsblock has fitness facilities, music rooms, study spaces and a games lounge\.
    

## Sustainability Features

UBC has been recognized internationally for its commitment to sustainable living\. Many residences and rooms have modern [sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/ "Sustainability Features") designed to reduce waste, energy consumption, and water use\.
