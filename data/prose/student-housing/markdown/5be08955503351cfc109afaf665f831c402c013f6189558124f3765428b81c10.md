## Summary

Shared traditional rooms—double occupancy—are found in [Place Vanier](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/place-vanier/ "Place Vanier") and [Totem Park](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/totem-park/ "Totem Park") where on a standard floor 25–30 residents share communal bathrooms\. [Orchard Commons](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/orchard-commons/), [Place Vanier](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/place-vanier/ "Place Vanier") and [Totem Park](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/totem-park/ "Totem Park") also have shared rooms with a shared private bathroom\. Shared rooms are only available for Winter Session\.

-   Furnished room, communal bathroom \(Place Vanier and Totem Park\)
-   Furnished room, shared private bathroom \(Orchard Commons, Place Vanier and Totem Park\)
-   Single gender rooms
-   Mandatory Residence Meal Plan
-   [In\-room access to high\-speed wireless internet \(Wi\-Fi\)](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet)
-   Floor lounges have TVs with basic cable
-   [Bed linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens") not included
-   Photos/layouts are samples only—suites vary by location

### Furnishings

Adjustable height twin XL \(extra\-long\) bed\*, desk, chair, desk lamp, wardrobe, bookshelf, dresser\. Room amenities vary by location\.

\*In Compact Shared rooms the beds are bunk\-style twin XL \(extra\-long\)\.

## Sustainability Features

UBC has been recognized internationally for its commitment to sustainable living\. Many residences and rooms have many modern [sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/ "Sustainability Features") designed to reduce waste, energy consumption, and water use\.
