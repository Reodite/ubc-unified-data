## Summary

Townhouses are two\-bedroom year round units in [Exchange](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/exchange/)\. They are located at ground level along Wesbrook Mall and Student Union Boulevard\.

-   Exterior entrance
-   Furnished
-   Shared kitchen
-   Private bathrooms
-   Shared in\-suite washer/dryer
-   [High\-speed wireless internet \(Wi\-Fi\)](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet)
-   [Bed linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens") not included
-   One [designated roommate](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/designated-roommates/) \(i\.e\., additional occupant\) is permitted per room—in *Townhouse Type A* only\.
-   Photos/layouts are samples only

### Furnishings

Bed, dresser, desk, chairs, bookshelf, closet, couches and tables\.

## Sustainability Features

UBC has been recognized internationally for its commitment to sustainable living\. Many residences and rooms have modern [sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/ "Sustainability Features") designed to reduce waste, energy consumption and water use\.
