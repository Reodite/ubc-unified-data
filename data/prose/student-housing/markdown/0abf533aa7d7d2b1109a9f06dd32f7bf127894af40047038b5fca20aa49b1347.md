## Living at Exchange

Exchange is one of our newest residence communities at UBC\. Home to over 650 students, Exchange offers an active, urban energy and a uniquely connected location\. It is located alongside the UBC bus exchange and just steps from the UBC Aquatic Centre and Student Recreation Centre\. The AMS Nest, UBC Life Building and many other campus amenities are right nearby\.

## Quick Facts

-   Year Round
-   Mainly upper\-year undergraduate and graduate students
-   650 beds: 71 nano suites, 88 studios, 5 large/accessible studios, 77 one\-bedroom suites, 19 two\-bedroom suites, 84 four\-bedroom suites, 15 townhouses \(2\-bedroom\)
-   Furnished living areas and private bedrooms
-   Furnished/appliance\-equipped kitchens\. All units except Nano suites equipped with dishwashers
-   [High\-speed wireless internet](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet)

# Facilities at Exchange

-   [icon\_facilities\_frontdesk](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_commonsblock.png)  
    
    ## Commonsblock
    
    The hub for residence activities\. Includes: Wi\-Fi, activity and meeting rooms, group and private study spaces, lounge areas, exercise room and music practice rooms\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_frontdesk](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_frontdesk.png)  
    
    ## Support Staff
    
    From our Front Desk staff and Overnight Mobile Team to Residence Life professional and student staff, you are never alone\! [Meet the people](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/meet-the-team/) you can contact with questions, concerns or a request for support\.
    
-   [sorting station](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/icons_sorting_orange.png)  
    
    ## Sort It Out\!
    
    We strive to be a “Zero Waste” campus\. Exchange has a large sorting area with collection bins for compost, household trash, recyclables, e\-waste, and charitable donations\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_laundry](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_laundry.png)  
    
    ## Laundry Facilities
    
    Machines operate on a convenient card system—no need to worry about coins\. Facilities are located on the bottom floor\.
    

-   [icon\_facilities\_parking](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_parking.png)  
    
    ## Parking &amp; Bicycle Storage
    
    Limited monthly vehicle [parking](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/parking/) is available nearby for a fee\. Bicycle storage hooks are available in each suite, with the exception of Nano suites\. A bike storage room is located at the building entrance for those who do not have bicycle storage in their suite\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_outdoor](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_outdoor.png)  
    
    ## Outdoor Facilities
    
    Basketball, tennis courts, playing fields and beaches are easily accessible\. Nearby Pacific Spirit Regional Park has over 35 kilometres of multipurpose trails to explore\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_health](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_health.png)  
    
    ## Health &amp; Safety
    
    Residents have access to a campus medical clinic and safety programs such as SafeWalk\. There are regular nightly patrols of residence properties\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_family](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_mealplan.png)  
    
    ## Meal Plan
    
    Exchange suites come with kitchens\. Students can purchase an [optional meal plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/ubccard-value-plan/ "Food Services Meal Plans") for convenience—no need to cook or carry cash\.
    

## Sustainability Features

UBC has been recognized internationally for its commitment to sustainable living\. Many residences and rooms have modern [sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/ "Sustainability Features") designed to reduce waste, energy consumption, and water use\.
