The maps below define the property boundaries for each of UBC’s student residences, for purposes addressed in each [residence contract](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/residence-contracts/) related to activities involving cannabis consumption\.

*Relevant contract sections include Section 310A in the Year Round, Winter Session, Vantage College and Summer Session contracts, Section 35A in the Acadia Park Residential Agreement and Section 42A in the St\. John’s College and Green College contracts\.*
