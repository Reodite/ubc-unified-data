## Eligibility

### Who can sponsor

Sponsors must be a government agency, company, Indigenous band or other organization\. Individuals, parents or relatives cannot sponsor a student through this program\. If a sponsor or third\-party vendor reimburses fees directly to the student rather than paying UBC, this is not considered sponsorship\.

### How sponsorship works

Sponsors are invoiced each term for the costs they agree to cover and pay UBC directly for these fees\. All sponsors must submit an online Student Housing Sponsorship Application for each student—or one application for a group of students who fall under the same sponsorship criteria\.

### Currency and exchange rates

UBC accepts payments in Canadian or US funds only\. US funds will be converted to Canadian funds using UBC Finance’s daily exchange rate on the date we process the payment\. We cannot use exchange rates provided by sponsors\.

To avoid exchange rate discrepancies, send funds in Canadian dollars\. When setting a sponsorship contract dollar limit, you can only set limits in Canadian funds\.

#### Tax receipts

Following Canada Revenue Agency guidelines, sponsoring organizations must issue a T4A tax receipt to their sponsored students\. UBC does not provide this document\.

## How to apply

Complete the Student Housing Sponsorship Application for each student you intend to sponsor—or one application for each group of students who fall under the same sponsorship criteria—by the deadlines below\.

You can sponsor a student for as short as one term or for the entire calendar year\.

If you support a student for one term and later want to continue the sponsorship, submit a new application\. Contracts do not automatically renew\.

[SUBMIT A SPONSORSHIP APPLICATION](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_8q9foXnpnROykLA)

If you are unable to apply online, please email a completed [Student Housing sponsorship billing form \(PDF\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Student-Housing-Sponsorship-Application.pdf) to [information@housing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:information@housing.ubc.ca)\.

## Invoices and payments

### When you’ll receive invoices

We invoice sponsors at the end of each term for the costs they agree to pay\. If you’re covering multiple terms and require separate invoices in advance, indicate this request on your application form\.

### Making a payment

Paying by cheque is the preferred method for sponsorship funding\. Other payment options are also available\.

#### To pay by cheque:

1.  Make your cheque payable to “The University of British Columbia”
2.  Enter the following information in the memo field
    -   Residence / meal plan fees
    -   Student’s full name
    -   Student number
3.  Mail your cheque to:

SHCS MAIN OFFICE  
MARINE DRIVE BUILDING 6  
2205 LOWER MALL  
VANCOUVER BC  V6T 1Z4

***If funding was sent to the UBC Sponsorship Billing Office in error, please email [information@housing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:information@housing.ubc.ca)\.*** 

## Information privacy

By entering into a sponsorship agreement, students agree to allow UBC to share limited information from their student housing account with you for the duration of the sponsorship\.

Information that can be shared includes:

-   student name and student number
-   residence area and room type
-   room fees

Any further student information, including exact address, cannot be shared with a sponsor unless the student has authorized you as a third\-party\.

### Student academic information

If your sponsorship conditions include receiving updates on the student’s academic progress, transcripts or other documents, ask the student to send these directly to you\. UBC cannot provide this information to sponsors\.
