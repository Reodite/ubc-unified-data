> **“The biggest and best decision in my life was to live in residence\. You get the full university experience\.” – Totem Park resident**

First year undergraduate students, who meet the eligibility criteria and requirements detailed below, are guaranteed a room in residence at UBC, as part of our first year guarantee\. We assign rooms to these applicants first\.

## First Year guarantee

Criteria for students with Winter Session contracts starting in August/September 2026\.

### Criteria

*You must meet **all** requirements\.*

-   You are [eligible](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/eligibility/ "Eligibility") to live in residence based on your age and academic credit load\.
-   Your application to UBC and application for **Winter Session Residence** was received by May 1\.
    -   **Reminder:** you can open a residence application when you apply to UBC—do not wait to receive your UBC admission offer\.
-   You must receive and accept UBC’s offer of academic admission by June 1\.
-   You are entering UBC **directly from high school** or CEGEP\. For example:
    -   You graduated in June 2026\.
    -   You graduated no earlier than June 2025 and you have not completed any post\-secondary coursework since graduating \(i\.e\., you took a gap year\)\.
