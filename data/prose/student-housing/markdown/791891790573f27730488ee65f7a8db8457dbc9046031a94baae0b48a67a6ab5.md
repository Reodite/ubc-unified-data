## FAQs

### Do I need to book a timeslot?

Residents moving in on May 10, 2026 do not need to select a timeslot\. You can go directly to your residence front desk to check\-in\.

If you’re planning to arrive on our official Move In Day \(Sept\. 5, 2026\), you need to book a timeslot\. Watch for an email from us in August with info about how to book yours\!

### What if I miss my timeslot?

We understand that travel delays can occur\. If you end up arriving later than the move\-in timeslot you booked, but before 10:30 pm, follow the same arrival and check\-in instructions: park and check\-in as planned\.

### What if I arrive late?

If you’ll be arriving too late to check in on Move In Day, you’ll need to book a hotel and check\-in at the Acadia Park Commonsblock the next day \(7:30 am–10:30 pm\)\. On\-campus [hotel accommodation](https://suitesatubc.com/) is available\.

### Can I arrive after Move In Day?

Yes, you can arrive on a later date\. Simply check in at the Acadia Park Commonsblock, 7:30 am–10:30 pm \(a half hour after opening and a half hour before closing\) seven days a week\.

If you are using a vehicle and moving in on a date other than our two official Move In Days, you can park temporarily in a loading zone near your building while you briefly unload or park/pay at the [nearest parking lot or parkade](https://parking.ubc.ca/)\.

### Can I bring my pet?

While we love animals, pets are not permitted in residence\. Please leave your pet at home\.

### What if I forget to bring something?

UBC Bookstore also has plenty of [residence supplies](https://the.bookstore.ubc.ca/home--lifestyle/categories/residence-supplies) in stock, for purchase online or in\-store\.
