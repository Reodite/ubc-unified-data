## Transition to the new system

Starting in spring 2026, we’re transitioning to a new student housing management system using StarRez, a leading residence platform adopted widely in Canada and across North America\.

This change is part of a broad effort to modernize our systems and improve the online experience for UBC students\.

The system you [log in](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/your-account/#top) to depends on the type of housing you applied for or where you live\.

### Phases

The current plan for transitioning to the new system\. Schedule subject to change\. Precise transition dates will be provided to residents via email\.

-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/your-account/)**Spring 2026:** Student Family \(Acadia Park\) application opens in the new system\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/your-account/)**Summer 2026:** Student Family \(Acadia Park\) accounts move to the new system\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/your-account/)**By fall 2026:** Year\-round accounts move to the new system; Year\-Round application opens in the new system\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/your-account/)**Fall 2026:** Winter Session application \(2027/28\) opens in the new system\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/your-account/)**Winter 2027:** Summer Session residence application \(for 2027 Summer Session\) opens in the new system\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/your-account/)**Summer 2027:** Full transition to the new system\. Summer Session \(2027\) and Winter Session \(2027/28\) residents—who applied in the new system—continue to manage their accounts in the new system\.

### Your information

Your student housing history and records are safe\. StarRez is an encrypted platform that meets top information security standards\. This transition won’t affect your existing contracts or applications\.

## Questions?
