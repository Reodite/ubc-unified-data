## Additional info

### Do I need to register?

Nope\! All classes are drop\-in\.

*Please note that classes have a limited number of people per session, depending on the size of the room they’re being held in\. Arrive early to secure a spot\.* 

### Will I be the only newbie?

Don’t know how to downward dog? Don’t worry, our awesome instructors will be there to help you, so don’t feel lost\!

### What should I wear/bring?

-   Dress in comfortable workout clothes and athletic shoes\.
-   Bring a water bottle—fill up right from the tap\.
-   For yoga and pilates: bring your own mat and, if you have them, yoga blocks\.

### Which sessions can I attend?

Residents from any residence area operated by UBC Student Housing can attend any session\.
