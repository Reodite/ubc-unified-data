Student Housing and Community Services operates residences on UBC’s 400 hectare Vancouver campus\. This map is primarily intended to show residence areas and their proximity to main roadways and buildings\.

Visit UBC’s [official map](http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index.php "Wayfinding at UBC") to find more campus facilities and academic buildings\.

See our [Residences Overview](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/residences/ "At a Glance") page to take a closer look at each residence area\.
