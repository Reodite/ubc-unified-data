## Living in Ritsumeikan\-UBC House

Ritsumeikan\-UBC House was established as a symbol of UBC's academic partnership with Ritsumeikan University in Japan\. Relaxing green space around the property is ideal for studying, hanging out with friends or enjoying quiet time\. The nearby Commonsblock is shared with Orchard Commons, one of our first year residences\.

## Quick Facts

-   Winter session \(September to April\)
-   Mainly first\-year undergraduate students
-   [Optional meal plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/ubccard-value-plan/ "Suite-style Residences") available
-   205 beds: 50 four and five\-person units, 5 one\-bedroom units
-   Single gender roommate assignments
-   Furnished kitchens, living areas, and private and shared bedrooms
-   [Internet access \(ethernet port\) in each room](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet)
-   [Eligibility requirements](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/eligibility/ "Eligibility")

# Facilities at Ritsumeikan\-UBC House

-   [icon\_facilities\_frontdesk](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_commonsblock.png)  
    
    ## Commonsblock
    
    Ritsumeikan\-UBC residents share a Commonsblock with Orchard Commons\. Includes: Wi\-Fi, dining room, fitness room, music rooms, group and private study spaces, ballroom, games lounge, and after\-hours convenience store\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_frontdesk](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_frontdesk.png)  
    
    ## Support Staff
    
    From our Front Desk staff and Overnight Mobile Team to Residence Life professional and student staff, you are never alone\! [Meet the people](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/meet-the-team/) you can contact with questions, concerns or a request for support\.
    
-   [sorting station](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/icons_sorting_orange.png)  
    
    ## Sort It Out\!
    
    Sustainability is a shared value in residence\. Rits\-UBC has a large sorting area with collection bins for household recyclables, e\-waste, charitable donations, and trash\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_laundry](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_laundry.png)  
    
    ## Laundry Facilities
    
    Machines operate on a convenient card system—no need to worry about coins\. Facilities are located within each residence block\.
    

-   [icon\_facilities\_parking](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_parking.png)  
    
    ## Parking &amp; Bicycle Storage
    
    Limited monthly [parking](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/parking/) is available for a fee\. Outdoor bicycle storage is available\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_outdoor](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_outdoor.png)  
    
    ## Outdoor Facilities
    
    Basketball, tennis courts, playing fields and beaches are easily accessible\. Nearby Pacific Spirit Regional Park has over 35 kilometres of multipurpose trails to explore\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_health](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_health.png)  
    
    ## Health and Safety
    
    Residents have access to a campus medical clinic and safety programs such as SafeWalk\. There are regular nightly patrols of residence properties\.
    
-   [icon\_facilities\_family](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/icon_facilities_mealplan.png)  
    
    ## Meal Plan
    
    Ritsumeikan\-UBC suites come with kitchens\. Students can purchase an [optional meal plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/ubccard-value-plan/ "Food Services Meal Plans") for convenience—no need to cook or carry cash\.
    

## Sustainability Features

UBC has been recognized internationally for its commitment to sustainable living\. Many residences and rooms have modern [sustainability features](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/sustainability-features/ "Sustainability Features") designed to reduce waste, energy consumption, and water use\.
