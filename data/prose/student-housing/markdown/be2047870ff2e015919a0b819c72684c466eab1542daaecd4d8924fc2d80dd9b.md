## Summary

Nano suites are a unique room type at UBC, designed for the student who wants a room to suit their minimalist lifestyle\. Nanos pack a convertible desk/bed, full private bathroom, kitchenette and closet into a small but streamlined space\.

-   Furnished
-   [In\-room access to high\-speed wireless internet \(Wi\-Fi\)](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet)
-   [Bed linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens") not included
-   Photos/layouts are samples only

### Furnishings

Convertible bed/desk, desk chair, lamp, bookshelf, closet\.
