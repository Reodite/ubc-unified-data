In August 2024, SHCS, UBC and the Province of BC [announced a major partnership](https://news.ubc.ca/2024/08/government-funding-brings-more-affordable-ubc-campus-housing/) to build a new student residence complex in the Lower Mall Precinct, on the current site of St\. John’s College\.

The Lower Mall Precinct \(LMP\) project will address the growing demand for student housing by building approximately 1,500 graduate student focused beds \(1,325 new and 175 replacement beds\)\.

Designed by [Ryder Architecture](https://www.ryderarchitecture.com/) \+ [3XN](https://3xn.com/) and developed together with [UBC Properties Trust](https://ubcproperties.com/) and [UBC Campus \+ Community Planning](https://planning.ubc.ca/), the project reflects UBC and SHCS’s commitment to [Campus Vision 2050](https://planning.ubc.ca/vision), UBC’s [Housing Action Plan](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/housing-immigration-and-relocation-services/housing-programs/housing-action-plan) and our goals to enhance campus life and support students by providing exceptional, on\-campus student housing, food services and child care\.

## Key features

-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/projects/lower-mall-precinct/)**Student Housing room types:** Numerous room types will be available, including studios \(with kitchens\), studios \(without kitchens\), one bedroom, two bedroom and four bedroom units\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/projects/lower-mall-precinct/)**Dining hall:** This project will feature a dining hall for residents and the wider community\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/projects/lower-mall-precinct/)**Child care:** Like other recent student housing developments, this project will include a child care setting \(37 spaces\)\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/projects/lower-mall-precinct/)**Environmental sustainability and climate:** This project will incorporate many energy\-saving features to significantly reduce greenhouse gas emissions and will be built to LEED Gold standards\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/projects/lower-mall-precinct/)**Community:** By including amenities for student residents and the wider community, we’ll enhance this increasingly vibrant area of campus and create opportunities for community and connection\.

## Project status and details

### Project timeline

-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/projects/lower-mall-precinct/)**May 2026:** St\. John’s College Fellows move out or transfer to a unit in another residence on\-campus and site decommissioning begins\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/projects/lower-mall-precinct/)**Fall 2026:** Construction of new buildings begins\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/projects/lower-mall-precinct/)**2028–2029:** Students move in—in stages between 2028 and 2029 as buildings are completed\.

### Financial sustainability

The Government of British Columbia is contributing more than $300 million to this $560 million project in a combination of loan and grants\. The balance of the investment comes from UBC internal loans\. The financial support from the province means that we can ensure sustainable rents for future generations of UBC students, at this residence and at existing residences\.

## The 98\-year\-old that stopped traffic

On November 14, 2024, UBC’s historic Old Fire Hall, built in 1926, was [carefully lifted from its foundation and relocated](https://news.ubc.ca/2024/11/ubc-historic-fire-hall-moves/)—to preserve its heritage for future generations\. The building is currently parked at a temporary home on\-campus until its permanent relocation as part of the Lower Mall Precinct project\.
