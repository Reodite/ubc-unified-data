Demand to live in residence at UBC significantly exceeds the number of vacancies\. If you do not qualify for the [first year guarantee](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/first-year-guarantee/), we strongly recommend you apply for alternative accommodation, in addition to housing operated by UBC Student Housing\.

Explore these alternative housing options, all conveniently located on campus\.

## Properties developed in partnership with UBC

| Property | Details |
| --- | --- |
| [Carey Centre](https://carey-edu.ca/ubc-student-residence/) | Furnished rooms with private bathrooms and meal plans\. Studios and two to three\-bedroom units\. |
| [leləm̓](https://lelemliving.com/) | Contemporary rental homes connected to nature and community in Pacific Spirit Park\. |
| [MBA House](http://mbahouse.ca/) | Graduate student housing for singles and couples\. |
| [St\. Andrew’s Hall](http://www.standrews.edu/) | Rental units including studios, one bedrooms, four bedrooms and townhouses\. Students only\. |
| [Wesbrook Properties](https://www.wesbrookproperties.com/) | A wide range of rental units, from studios to four bedrooms, in residential buildings across campus\. |
