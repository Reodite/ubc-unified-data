While living in residence at UBC, there are many opportunities to live, grow and thrive\.

Participate in events and activities organized by your RA, join a residence committee, access resources or reach out to residence staff for support when you need it, and use our tips to expertly navigate roommate relationships\. Plus, learn about residence services, amenities, health, safety and more\.
