***The information on this page is for residents whose contracts are ending at 12 pm on April 26, 2026 and they are moving out before or on April 26\.***

Thanks so much for living with us in residence at UBC this year\! We hope that your final exams and assignments went well and that you’re looking forward to summer\.

Prepare for Move Out

[Plan your move and avoid last\-minute moving chaos](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/moving-out/) by ensuring that rooms and shared spaces are left as clean as you’d want them if you were moving in\.

## Last day in residence: April 26

While many residents will move out earlier in April, once their exams are complete, some residents stay until the final days in their residence contract\.

-   Last day in residence: **April 26, 2026**
-   Check out time: **no later than 12 pm \(noon\)**—please arrive by 11:30 am to avoid lineups and ensure that you can complete the check out process by noon\.

You must clean your unit, remove all of your items and move out BEFORE 12 pm on Sunday, April 26 unless you have an approved residence contract extension \(i\.e\., you are moving into Year Round residence\)\.

**Pro tip:** Check out before 10 am to avoid the lineups\!

## How to check\-out?

Follow these instructions to make your check out process as smooth and efficient as possible\.

### Your check\-out to\-do list

To complete the check out procedures, please ensure you do all of the following:

-   **Remove all your belongings from your room/unit**, including any bikes stored outside\!\*
-   **Dispose of all trash, food and recycling**—and anything you still need to donate at your [nearest donation zone](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/moving-out/)\!
-   **Clean your unit**\.
-   **Close and lock all windows and doors**\.
-   **Return your keys and check out**—at your residence front desk or, during limited hours, at a centrally\-located check\-out table near your front desk or commonsblock\.
-   **Set up letter mail forwarding in the [Online Service Centre](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/)**\. If you do not set up mail forwarding, any mail or packages that we receive after you check out will be returned to sender\.
    -   Fairview Crescent residents: [Contact Canada Post](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/home.page) to forward mail\. Note: This service is not available for packages\.

Remember to bring all your keys\! You’ll be charged a fee on your residence account for any keys you don’t return\.

\*Any bikes left behind at Winter Session residence areas are removed and donated to [The Bike Kitchen](https://www.thebikekitchen.ca/)\.

[Cleaning and departure checklists](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/moving-out/cleaning-departure/)

### When can I check out?

-   **April 25 and earlier:** check out during regular front desk hours, 7 am–11 pm\. Please arrive at the front desk by 10:30 pm to ensure that you can complete the check out process before the front desk closes\.
-   **April 26:** check out between 7 am and 12 pm \(noon\)\. Please arrive at the front desk by 11:30 am to ensure that you can complete the check out process by noon\.

Given the high volume of final check\-outs on April 26, please arrive early and expect some lineups\.

#### **Can I check out overnight?**

Please check out during front desk hours, 7 am–11 pm\.

If you plan to leave overnight, when the front desk is closed, please check out before 11 pm—and you are welcome to remain in the commonsblock until you leave campus\.

### Can I borrow a luggage cart?

You can borrow a luggage cart for maximum 20 minutes, between 7 am and 11 pm, when the front desk is open\.

To borrow a luggage cart:

-   Visit the cart check\-out table near your residence front desk\.
-   Leave one piece of Photo ID and your phone number\.

Please note that carts will be in high demand\.

Reminder to set up mail forwarding\!

Before you move out, make sure to set up mail forwarding in the [Online Service Centre](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/)\. \(Fairview Crescent residents: contact [Canada Post](http://www.canadapost.ca/) to forward mail\.\)  
If you do not set up mail forwarding, any mail or packages that we receive after you check out will be returned to sender\.

### Moving out with a vehicle?

If you will be using a vehicle to move out on April 26, follow the arrival instructions provided below to get to the correct loading zone for your residence area and/or building\.

On April 26, we’ll also have helpful signage and friendly staff in place to help you find your way\.

You will be able to temporarily park and load up close to the residence area \(max 20 minutes\), and then if you plan to stay on campus afterward, you will need to park at a nearby parkade, as indicated below\.

Please note that all parking rules and signage are in effect\. Parking in fire lanes or “authorized vehicles only” spaces and roads may result in a ticket if you are moving out any day other than April 26\.
