## FAQs

### Do I need to book a timeslot?

No, you do not need to reserve an arrival time\.

### **What if I’m** arriving **after my official move\-in date \(May 3, May 10 or June 7, 2026\)**?

Your unit is yours and will remain empty until you move in\. If you’re arriving after any of the above listed dates, you will check in at your residence front desk between 7 am and 10:45 pm on the date that you arrive\.

### Can a friend or family member check in for me?

No\. Your ID is used during check\-in and is used to confirm if you are the correct person so you can sign your resident card and get your keys\.

[Your belongings can be moved in/out if the official resident authorizes it](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/designated-movers/), but the family/friend will be escorted by a member of our front desk team – no keys are handed over\.

### **What if i’m moving in after January 4, 2026?**

If you’re arriving after the official Move In Day of January 4, please proceed directly to your residence front desk to check\-in\. The front desk is open between 7:00 am to 11:00 pm daily\. Please arrive by 10:30 pm at the latest to check\-in before the front desks close at 11 pm\.  

### What if I arrive late?

If you’ll be arriving too late to check in and move in, you’ll need to book a hotel and check\-in at your residence front desk the next day\. On\-campus [hotel accommodation](https://suitesatubc.com/) is available\.

### Can I bring my pet?

While we love animals, pets are not permitted in residence\. Please leave your pet at home\.

### What if I forget to bring something?

UBC Bookstore also has plenty of [residence supplies](https://the.bookstore.ubc.ca/home--lifestyle/categories/residence-supplies) in stock, for purchase online or in\-store\.
