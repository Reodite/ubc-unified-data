## Things to Do Before You Leave Home

-   Check out [what to bring and what not to bring](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/what-should-i-pack/) to UBC\.
-   Find out [what to expect on residence move in day](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/move-in-day/)\.
-   Buy or order [bed and bath linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens")\.
-   [Apply for a UBCcard](http://www.ubccard.ubc.ca/) if you don’t have one\. The option to have your UBCcard delivered to residence opens in mid\-July\. Please check the applicable box in your UBCcard application\.
-   Get [renter’s insurance](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/health-safety/renters-insurance/) to protect your belongings\. You may be covered under your family plan\.
-   Review important [Residence Standards](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/residence-standards/ "Residence Standards") and be prepared to live by them\.
-   Know what [services and amenities](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/ "Services &amp; Amenities") are available in residence and how to access them, especially [how to use waste sorting areas](http://sustain.ubc.ca/sortitout) that will help you live sustainably\.
-   Explore your options for [getting to, from and around campus](https://transportation.ubc.ca/), including bikeshare, carshare and public transit\.
-   [Contact your roommates online](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/) to coordinate shared items\.
-   Leave your [residence mailing address](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/contact-us/ "Contact Us") with family and friends\.
-   Know what time you’re supposed to arrive and other [things to expect when moving in](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/move-in-day/)\.
