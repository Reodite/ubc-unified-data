## Did you rank your preferences?

Your residence application is not complete until you [go online](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/) to rank preferred room types\. You are also welcome to update your choices if you change your mind\. We try our best to accommodate preferences but cannot make guarantees\.

## Do I need to send additional documents?

Some students need to provide additional documentation or take additional application steps\.

-   Students with disabilities or ongoing health conditions can apply for [accessible student housing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/accessibility/)\.
-   Students [applying for membership](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/graduate-colleges/) to Green College and/or St\. John’s College must apply to the colleges directly\.
-   Students applying for Student Family Residence at Acadia Park must provide documents for all occupants, as detailed below\.

### Additional documentation for Student Family Residence

Applicants to Student Family Residence must provide documentation for children and evidence of prior cohabitation\.

Please email copies of your documentations to the Student Housing Main Office\.

#### Documentation for children

Please provide a scanned/digital copy of one of the following:

-   Passport
-   Birth certificate
-   Medical card

#### Evidence of prior cohabitation

Students who are applying with a partner or spouse, please provide a scanned/digital copy of at least one of the following:

-   Joint bank account or credit card statements
-   Documentation of joint ownership of residential property
-   Joint residential leases
-   Joint rental receipts
-   Joint utility account statements \(i\.e\., electricity, gas internet, cable, etc\.\)
-   Documentation showing joint management of household expenditures
-   Evidence of joint purchases, especially for household items

## Request roommate groups

This is optional\. Applicants can go online to request to live together [before the deadline](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/important-dates/ "Important Dates")\. You will need proper name\(s\) and student ID number\(s\)\. We try our best to accommodate these requests but cannot make guarantees\.

## Monitor your email inbox

If you receive a residence offer, you will need to pay a deposit within seven days of the offer date to secure your room\. The deposit will be the combined total of your residence and meal plan first instalments\.

**March**—majority of Year Round Residence offers are sent\.  
**June**—offers sent to prospective students for Winter Session Residence\.

Don’t miss the offer\! Keep your email address and contact information current and keep us out of your junk/spam folder\. Add these email addresses to your contact list: [information@housing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:information@housing.ubc.ca); [summer@housing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:summer@housing.ubc.ca)\.

## Waitlist?

[Go online](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/) regularly to check your application status and position in the waitlist\. We send offers to students on the waitlist throughout the entire year to fill vacancies\.
