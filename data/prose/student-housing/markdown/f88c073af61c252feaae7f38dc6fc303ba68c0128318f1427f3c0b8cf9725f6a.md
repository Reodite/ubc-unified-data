**The global climate crisis requires strong collective action, including right here at UBC\.**

The university has set [ambitious targets](https://planning.ubc.ca/cap2030) to cut down our operational greenhouse gas \(GHG\) emissions and reduce the carbon footprint from our entire community\. To reach these goals, we need your help\.

Within UBC, Student Housing and Community Services \(SHCS\) [has been working hard to advance the university’s goals](https://shcs.ubc.ca/sustainability-climate-action/) in five key areas: buildings, food systems, waste, transportation and engagement\.

## Buildings

**Did you know?**

21% of greenhouse gas emissions worldwide are from the [building sector](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/chapter/chapter-9/)\.

97% of UBC’s emissions from operations are from operating buildings\.

## Food systems

**Did you know?**

About a third of all human\-caused greenhouse gas emissions is linked to [food](https://www.un.org/en/climatechange/science/climate-issues/food)\.

Here at UBC, our food systems—things like buying and preparing food, and dealing with food waste—account for about three quarters of the university’s emissions from operations\.

### What we’re doing

#### Climate\-friendly menus

We’re making it easier to choose low\-emission foods by offering plenty of plant\-based menu options and using climate\-friendly food labels on our menus\.

#### Sustainable ingredients

We source from local suppliers including the [UBC Farm](https://ubcfarm.ubc.ca/)\. All our seafood is [Ocean Wise](https://ocean.org/) certified, and the coffee and chocolate we serve is Fair Trade\.

#### Less food and packaging waste

We partner with [Vancouver Food Runners](https://www.vancouverfoodrunners.com/) to share surplus food with people in need, reducing waste and the emissions they cause\. All our outlets have [Sort It Out](https://sustain.ubc.ca/resources/campus-guides/sort-it-out) stations to help you properly sort food waste\.

## Zero waste

**Did you know?**

18% of methane emissions worldwide are from [landfills and wastewater](https://www.un.org/en/chronicle/article/ecology-recycling)\.

With nearly 14,000 students living in residence at UBC, we collectively produce a lot of waste\.

[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/WdYwx8nltJ0?feature=oembed&amp;iv_load_policy=3&amp;modestbranding=1&amp;rel=0&amp;autohide=1&amp;playsinline=0&amp;autoplay=0)

### What we’re doing

#### Convenient waste sorting stations

Sort It Out [composting and recycling stations](https://sustain.ubc.ca/resources/campus-guides/sort-it-out) are in every residence building and around campus\.

#### Circular economy

We run a [Mindful Market](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/mindful-market/) for students moving into residence, and a [Mindful Move Out](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/moving-out/) campaign to make it easier for students to donate and recycle items they no longer need\.

#### Reduce

The UBC Bookstore offers [reusable drinkware](https://the.bookstore.ubc.ca/Catalogue/ubc-bookstore/home--lifestyle/categories/drinkware) options to help students produce less waste\.

In 2025, we will be piloting a new reusable container system at our Hero Coffee Harvest Market location in Brock Commons\.

[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/get-involved/live-green/)

### What you can do

-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/get-involved/live-green/)[Use reusable containers](https://planning.ubc.ca/zero-waste-food-ware-strategy)—avoid single\-use ones\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/get-involved/live-green/)[Sort your waste](https://sustain.ubc.ca/resources/campus-guides/sort-it-out) thoroughly: food scraps, paper, containers and garbage\.

## Transportation

**Did you know?**

25% of greenhouse gas emissions globally are related to [transportation](https://www.un.org/sites/un2.un.org/files/media_gstc/FACT_SHEET_Climate_Change.pdf)\.

Every month, 175,000\+ cars are parked on campus\.

### What we’re doing

#### Building more on\-campus housing

By providing more housing on campus, we help reduce the need for commuting, cutting down on transportation emissions\.

#### Sustainable transportation

We encourage eco\-friendly ways to get [to, from and around campus](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation), such as walking, cycling, bike share, transit, car share, carpooling and more\.

#### EV charging stations

We’ve installed 75 electric vehicle \(EV\) charging stations in many parkades on campus\.

#### Clean energy

Solar energy is used to fuel electric vehicles and produce green hydrogen fuel\. Learn more about our innovative [Smart Hydrogen Energy District](https://news.ubc.ca/2024/06/ubc-smart-hydrogen-energy-district-launch/)\.

#### Zero\-emission vehicles

When we add or replace vehicles in our fleets, we aim for zero\-emission options, including electric and hydrogen vehicles\.

## Student engagement

**Did you know?**

Large organizations like UBC can have a positive impact on carbon emissions, and we are working hard to get to net zero\.

We can’t do it alone—getting every one of the roughly 80,000 students, faculty and staff at UBC Vancouver involved is critical to our success\.

#### Raising awareness

We’re educating and empowering our residents and community on how to take meaningful action\.

## How we measure success

We regularly measure the success of our sustainability and climate efforts, using UBC’s ambitious sustainability and CAP 2030 goals as our primary metric, and drawing on everything from real\-time building energy data and greenhouse gas emissions data to residence waste data and residence survey data\.

We do this in close collaboration with teams across SHCS and our partners at UBC Sustainability, Campus and Community Planning, and UBC Facilities\.
