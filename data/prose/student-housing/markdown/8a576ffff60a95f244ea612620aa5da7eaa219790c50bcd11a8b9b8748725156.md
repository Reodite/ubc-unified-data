## New\!

Campus Security is now patrolling outside the Fraternity Village\.

If you are a nearby resident and see or hear an unreasonable disturbance at the Fraternity Village, please call [Campus Security](https://security.ubc.ca/) at 604\-822\-2222\.

## What action will be taken?

A Campus Security patrol member will be dispatched to monitor the situation from the village perimeter, but they will not go into the Fraternity Village\. However, they will remain in the area to gather information and report on the incident\. Incident reports are written at the end of campus security shifts or following a complaint investigation\. Follow\-up actions may include misconduct notices or fines\.

## When to call the RCMP \(police\)

If you believe unlawful activity is taking place, please call 9\-1\-1 immediately\.
