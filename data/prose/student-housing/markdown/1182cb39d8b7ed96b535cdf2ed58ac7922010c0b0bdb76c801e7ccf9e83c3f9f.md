# Pre\-arrival checklist

Here are some to\-dos before moving into residence\.

-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/year-round-residence-guide/)First things first—did you accept your offer? Residence offers are sent by email and you will want to make sure you accept your offer and pay your first instalment of [residence fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/) by the deadline\. 
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/year-round-residence-guide/)[Apply for a UBCcard](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/obtaining-a-ubccard/students) if you don’t have one\.

[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/year-round-residence-guide/)

## Make the most out of life at UBC

Check out these [10 tips for making the most out of living in residence](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/new-here/)\.

-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/year-round-residence-guide/)Purchase a [UBCcard Value Plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/ubccard-value-plan/) to save 5% on food and non\-alcoholic beverages at a ton of locations on campus, including dining halls, cafes and restaurants\. It can also be used for printing and vending machines\. Once you get your UBCcard, start by loading $5 or more through the [SHCS online service centre](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/), then start saving at UBC Food Services locations—easy as pie\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/year-round-residence-guide/)Buy or order [bed and bath linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/bed-linens/) and check out [what to bring and what not to bring](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/what-should-i-pack/) when you move in\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/year-round-residence-guide/)Get [renter’s insurance](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/health-safety/renters-insurance/) to protect yourself and your belongings\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/year-round-residence-guide/)Become familiar with your [rights and responsibilities](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/rights-and-responsibilities/) as a resident within the residence community\. Review important [Residence Standards](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/residence-standards/)—including your [residence contract](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/residence-contracts/)—and read up on some tips for [living with roommates](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/living-with-roomates/)\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/year-round-residence-guide/)As part of your student fees, you get a U\-Pass, which gives you unlimited access to transit in the Lower Mainland\. If you still need parking, limited spaces are available so remember to apply for a [residence parking space](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/parking/)\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/year-round-residence-guide/)Check out the [important dates](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/important-dates/) and [fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/) pages for other key deadlines\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/year-round-residence-guide/)If you live in Year Round Housing, you can apply to [sublet your room](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/subletting/) to other eligible UBC students during the summer \(May–August\)\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/year-round-residence-guide/)Don’t forget to follow us on social\! [UBCResidence](https://www.instagram.com/ubcresidence/), [UBCFoodie](https://www.instagram.com/ubcfoodie/), [UBCBookstore](https://www.instagram.com/ubcbookstore/), [UBCLife](https://www.instagram.com/ubc_studentlife/), and [UBC\_Living](https://www.tiktok.com/@ubcliving) are your go\-to accounts for event info, residence life, contests and more\.
