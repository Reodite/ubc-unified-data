## Moving In

### **Arrival**

Occupancy begins at noon on the day your contract starts, unless other arrangements have been confirmed with Student Housing and Community Services\.

-   Move\-in period on the date your contract starts: 12 pm \(noon\) to 10 pm\.
-   Move\-in period after the date your contract starts: 8 am to 10 pm\.

You may park a moving truck outside your unit for a short period\. Please ensure minimal disruption to other residents\.

### **Checking in**

Visit the Front Desk to pick up the keys to your new home and sign your contract\.

### **Internet &amp; TV**

Shaw wireless high\-speed internet is included in the rent for all units\. Wireless service is available in the Commonsblock, Beanery Coffeehouse, and Acadia Highrise study rooms\.

Cable television is available at a bulk rate for Acadia Park residents\.\* For more information and to purchase this service, please contact the Student Housing main office at [family@housing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:family@housing.ubc.ca)\.

\*Basic cable television is included in the rent for Acadia Highrise units\.

### **Landline phone**

This is optional and not included in your rent\. To hook up a landline at an extra cost, contact Shaw or Telus\.

### **Cleaning/inspection**

Carpets are usually scheduled before you move in\. All units are inspected by housekeeping\. If you feel your unit is not acceptable, contact the Front Desk\.

## Moving Out

Acadia Park residents can move out any time of year\. Visit the Front Desk to complete a notice to vacate form and be sure to read our policy in your [residential agreement\.](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/residence-contracts/)

### **What happens after you give notice**

-   Within 24 hours we’ll schedule a first inspection of your unit\.
-   During this inspection you will get a detailed cleaning checklist\. Pay close attention\. You may be charged if any cleaning requirements are not completed by noon on the last day of the month\.
-   The cleaning checklist also includes a list of approximate costs for tenant\-related damages and failure to clean properly \(broken cupboard, hole in the wall, stained floors, dirty oven, etc\.\)\.
-   Staff will conduct a thorough inspection of the unit with you and discuss any potential costs for repairs/improper cleaning\. This inspection is meant to ensure you are fully aware of these costs and give us time to arrange labour for necessary repairs\.

### **What happens when you’re ready to depart**

-   Return residence keys to the front desk\. There is a fee for any unreturned keys\.
-   If a door lock replacement is required, you will be charged for each lock cylinder we have to replace\.
-   We will conduct a second and final inspection of the unit to determine if an assessment \(cleaning charge\) will be levied against your damage deposit\. Our policy is to document this inspection \(photo/video\), should you choose to dispute our findings\.
-   Leave your contact information with the front desk\.
-   Contact [Canada Post](http://www.canadapost.ca/) to forward your mail\.

### **Assessment Disputes**

Residents may dispute assessments \(cleaning/damage charges\) in writing to the Building Services Manager\.
