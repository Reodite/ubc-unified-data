Student Housing and Community Services \(SHCS\) administers accommodation for nearly 14,000 students at the University of British Columbia in Vancouver, BC\. Our goal is to provide an environment that helps students succeed both academically and socially\.

SHCS also oversees operations for UBC [Bookstore](https://bookstore.ubc.ca/), [Campus Mail](https://campusmail.ubc.ca/), [Child Care](http://www.childcare.ubc.ca/), [Conferences and Accommodation](http://www.ubcconferences.com/), [Food Services](http://www.food.ubc.ca/) and [Parking](https://parking.ubc.ca/)\.
