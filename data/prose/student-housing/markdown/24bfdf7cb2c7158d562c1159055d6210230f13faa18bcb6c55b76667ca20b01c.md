# Pre\-arrival checklist

Here are some to\-dos before moving into residence\.

-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/winter-session-residence-guide/)First things first—did you accept your offer? Residence offers are sent by email and you will want to make sure you accept your offer and pay your first instalment of [residence fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/) and [meal plan fees](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/residence-meal-plan-all-access-dining/) \(if applicable\) by the deadline\. 
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/winter-session-residence-guide/)Are you a first year student? Register for [Jump Start](https://you.ubc.ca/jump-start-vancouver)\! Jump Start gives you the opportunity to get to know campus and meet new friends before classes start\.

[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/winter-session-residence-guide/)

## Make the most of life at UBC

Check out these [10 tips for making the most of living in residence](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/new-here/)\.

-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/winter-session-residence-guide/)[Apply for a UBCcard](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/obtaining-a-ubccard/students) if you don’t have one\. The option to have your UBCcard delivered to residence typically opens in mid\-July\. Please check the applicable box in your UBCcard application\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/winter-session-residence-guide/)Buy or order [bed and bath linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/bed-linens/) for on\-time delivery to residence before Move In Day and check out [what to bring and what not to bring](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/what-should-i-pack/) when you move in\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/winter-session-residence-guide/)Get [renter’s insurance](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/health-safety/renters-insurance/) to protect your belongings\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/winter-session-residence-guide/)Review important [Residence Standards](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/residence-standards/)—including your [residence contract](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/residence-contracts/)—and become familiar with your [rights and responsibilities](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/rights-and-responsibilities/) as a resident within the residence community\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/winter-session-residence-guide/)As part of your student fees, you get a U\-Pass, which gives you unlimited access to transit in the Lower Mainland\. If you still need parking, limited spaces are available so remember to apply for a [residence parking space](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/parking/)\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/winter-session-residence-guide/)Check out the [important dates](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/important-dates/) and [fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/) pages for other key deadlines\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/winter-session-residence-guide/)We know it seems like a long way away, but we recommend you begin thinking about where you are going to live after this year\. Check out our guides to [other housing options at UBC](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/other-housing/more-campus-housing/) and [living off\-campus](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/other-housing/off-campus-housing/)—and don’t forget to [apply for residence](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/returning/) as a returning student\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/winter-session-residence-guide/)Don’t forget to follow us on social\! [UBCResidence](https://www.instagram.com/ubcresidence/), [UBCFoodie](https://www.instagram.com/ubcfoodie/), [UBCLife](https://www.instagram.com/ubc_studentlife/), [UBCBookstore](https://www.instagram.com/ubcbookstore/) and [UBC\_Living](https://www.tiktok.com/@ubcliving) are your go\-to accounts for event info, residence life, contests and more\.
