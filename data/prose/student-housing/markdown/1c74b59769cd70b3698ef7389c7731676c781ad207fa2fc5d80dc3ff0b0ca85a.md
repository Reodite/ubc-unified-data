## FAQs

### Can I move in before my Move In Day?

We are typically not able to permit move\-ins prior to your move in day \(i\.e\., Jump Start Move In Day or Labour Day Move In Day, as applicable\)\. If you are planning to arrive in Vancouver early, be sure to plan where you’ll be staying prior to your move in day\. Staying with friends and family or in a [short\-term accommodation](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/other-housing/off-campus-housing/#short-term-accommodation) are good options\.

### **What if i’m moving in after Sept\. 5**?

If you’re arriving after the official Move In Day of Sept\. 5, please proceed directly to your residence front desk to check\-in\. The front desk is open between 7:00 am to 11:00 pm daily\. Please arrive by 10:30 pm at the latest to check\-in before the front desks close at 11 pm\.

### Can a friend or family member check in for me?

No\. Your ID is used during check\-in and is used to confirm if you are the correct person so you can sign your resident card and get your keys\.

[Your belongings can be moved in/out if the official resident authorizes it](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/designated-movers/), but the family/friend will be escorted by a member of our front desk team– no keys are handed over\.

### Can I ship packages to residence before Move In Day?

No, please don’t ship anything to residence ahead of your move in\.
