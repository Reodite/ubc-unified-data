Each year we receive more applications to live in residence than we have rooms\. Applicants who do not qualify for a [guaranteed room in Winter Session Residence](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/first-year-guarantee/ "Waitlists &amp; Sublets") may be placed on a waitlist and assigned a waitlist number\.

**Monitor your number**  
Your waitlist number will change as vacancies are filled and applications cancelled\. You can track your number online\.

**What if a space becomes available?**  
Your application will remain active for the academic year\. If a spot opens up, we will email a residence offer with an expiry date to respond\.

**Cancelling your application  
**If you no longer require accommodation in residence, please cancel your application online\.
