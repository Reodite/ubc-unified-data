### Stay at UBC

From the stylishly appointed West Coast Suites with hotel amenities to our budget\-friendly Shared Apartments and the Pacific Spirit Hostel, UBC offers affordable accommodation for anyone visiting Vancouver—including short\-term housing rentals from mid\-May to late August each year\.

Visit [Stay at UBC](http://suitesatubc.com/) to find the accommodation that’s right for you\.
