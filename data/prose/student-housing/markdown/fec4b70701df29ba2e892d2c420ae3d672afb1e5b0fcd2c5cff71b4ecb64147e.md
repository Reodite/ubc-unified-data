**Are you a student moving into residence? Are you in search of kitchenware, mini fridges or other miscellaneous items? Visit UBC’s Mindful Market to find second hand items for cheap\!**

Not only will these items guarantee a steal, but you’ll also be advancing on\-campus sustainability and helping to build UBC’s circular economy—by putting items donated by last year’s residents [during move out](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/moving-out/) to use\!

## Event details

**When:** Saturday, September 5–Sunday, September 6, 2026

-   Saturday, September 5: 10 am–3 pm
-   Sunday, September 6: 10 am–1 pm \(while quantities last\)

#### **Location:** [Ponderosa Commons ballroom](https://maps.app.goo.gl/rmDjRSTiVStEA2Bx9)

**Payment:** CASH ONLY  
Pricing ranges from $\.50 to $35—with many low priced items available\! No refunds or exchanges after the market\.

**Items on sale:** 

-   Clothing
-   Kitchen supplies
-   Mini fridges
-   Fans
-   Household items
-   and more\!

We look forward to seeing you there\!

Mindful Market 2026: a big success\!

Thanks to everyone who came out to our Mindful Market on September 5 and 6\.

-   Hundreds of students visited the market\.
-   We sold over 800 items, including mini fridges, fans, jackets and kitchenware and more\.
-   We raised over $9,400, which will be deployed in support of campus food security\.
-   Remaining items will be distributed by the [UBC Free Store](https://secubc.com/ubc-free-store/), operated by the UBC Student Environment Centre\.

Many thanks to the student volunteers who helped make the market a success\!
