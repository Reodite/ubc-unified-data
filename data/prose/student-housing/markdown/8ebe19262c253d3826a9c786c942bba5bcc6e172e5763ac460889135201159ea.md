Some UBC students have a portion of their residence and/or meal plan fees paid by a sponsor, such as an Indigenous community, employer or government agency\.

Sponsors must meet the required criteria and apply by the deadline\. Both the student and sponsor should notify Student Housing if there are changes to the sponsoring agency or the coverage amount\.
