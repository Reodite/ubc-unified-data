## Move In Day overview

Here’s a brief overview of how the day will go\. More details below\!

-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/move-in-day/flag-pole-plaza-check-in/)**Arrive at UBC**—if you’re arriving by vehicle, park for free at the [Rose Garden Parkade](https://maps.app.goo.gl/aWHtyWo4UZjkT2an7)\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/move-in-day/flag-pole-plaza-check-in/)**Check in**—check in at [Flag Pole Plaza](https://maps.app.goo.gl/f7fPuqoaVsqqxDGc9), located above the Rose Garden Parkade\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/move-in-day/flag-pole-plaza-check-in/)**Proceed to your residence**—at check\-in we’ll give you a map with directions to your residence\. If you drove a vehicle, you’ll return to your vehicle then drive to your residence\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/move-in-day/flag-pole-plaza-check-in/)**Move in\!**—if you drove a vehicle, you’ll have 15 minutes to unload, then you’ll re\-park at a different parkade near your residence\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/move-in-day/flag-pole-plaza-check-in/)**Unpack and settle in**—if you drove, walk back to your room, unpack and settle in\! If you need a bite to eat, [find out what’s open on\-campus](https://food.ubc.ca/feedme/)\.

## FAQs

### Do I need to book a timeslot?

If you’re planning to arrive on one of the two official Move In Days, during the time when our central check\-ins are open, you need to book a timeslot\. Watch for an email from us in August with info about how to book yours\!

### What if I miss my timeslot?

We understand that travel delays can occur\. If you end up arriving later than the move\-in timeslot you booked, but while our central check\-ins are still open, follow the same arrival and check\-in instructions and head to your central check\-in location as planned\.

### What if I arrive late?

You’ll be able to check in at your residence commonsblock starting about 90 minutes after the Flag Pole Plaza check\-in closes: 3 pm closure on Sept\. 1; 2 pm closure on Sept\. 5\.

Please arrive no later than 10:30 pm to check in at your residence commonsblock, to allow time for check\-in before the residence front desk closes at 11 pm sharp\.

If you’re arriving later than 10:30 pm, you’ll need to book a hotel and check\-in the next day \(7:30 am–10:30 pm\)\. On\-campus [hotel accommodation](https://suitesatubc.com/) is available\.

#### Where can I park if I arrive late?

-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/move-in-day/flag-pole-plaza-check-in/)**Place Vanier:** park at the [Fraser River Parkade](https://maps.app.goo.gl/LoGVGGakpEzarpuNA)\.
-   [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/move-in-day/flag-pole-plaza-check-in/)**Walter Gage:** park in the [North Parkade](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Z4iMwgb8udJoCGZa7)\.

### Can I arrive after Move In Day?

Yes, you can arrive on a later date\. Simply check in at your residence front desk, 7:30 am–10:30 pm \(a half hour after opening and a half hour before closing\) seven days a week\.

If you are using a vehicle and moving in on a date other than our two official Move In Days, you can park temporarily in a loading zone near your building while you briefly unload or park/pay at the [nearest parking lot or parkade](https://parking.ubc.ca/)\.

### Can I bring my pet?

While we love animals, pets are not permitted in residence\. Please leave your pet at home\.

### What if I forget to bring something?

UBC Bookstore also has plenty of [residence supplies](https://the.bookstore.ubc.ca/home--lifestyle/categories/residence-supplies) in stock, for purchase online or in\-store\.
