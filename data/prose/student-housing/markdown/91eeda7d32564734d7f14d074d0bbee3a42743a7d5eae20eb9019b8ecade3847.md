**Winter Session Residence Dates—First Year Students**

## 2026

May 1

Application deadline for students who have not already applied for residence when applying to UBC\.

May 15

Deadline to update roommate group requests [online](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/)\.

June 1

Deadline to accept UBC’s offer of academic admission, in order to be considered for the First Year Guarantee\.

June–August

Residence offers are sent by email\. Applicants have seven days to accept the offer and pay first instalment of [residence/meal plan fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/)\.

Early August

Detailed room assignments sent by email\. Roommates can [contact each other online](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/)\.

August 10

Deadline to apply for [early arrival](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/eligibility/#early-arrival "Early Arrival Fees")\.

August 18

Deadline to apply for a [UBCcard](http://ubccard.ubc.ca/obtaining-a-ubccard/students) and receive it in your residence move\-in package for Jump Start Move In Day\.  
*The option to have your UBCcard delivered to residence typically opens in mid\-July\. Please check the applicable box in your UBCcard application\.* 

August 20

Deadline to order [bed/bath linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens") from Campus Linens of Canada for on\-time delivery to residence for Jump Start Move In Day\.

August 23

Standard deadline to apply for a  [UBCcard](http://ubccard.ubc.ca/obtaining-a-ubccard/students) and receive it in your residence move\-in package\.  
*The option to have your UBCcard delivered to residence typically opens in mid\-July\. Please check the applicable box in your UBCcard application\.*

August 26

Standard deadline to order [bed/bath linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens") from Campus Linens of Canada for on\-time delivery to residence\.

September 1

Students participating in UBC Jump Start move in\.

September 5

Students [move in](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/ "Moving In")\. First Residence Meal Plan meal at dinner\.

September 25

Second instalment of [residence/meal plan fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/ "fees") due\.

Mid\-December

Deadline to order [bed/bath linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens") from Campus Linens of Canada for on\-time delivery to residence \(Term 2\)\.

*Note: if you missed the deadline, you may still be able to receive linens by move\-in day\. Campus Linens can provide more specific delivery timelines during the order process\.*

December 23

Last Residence Meal Plan meal at breakfast\. First Year Residences close for the holidays\. Please check your contract for details\.

## 2026

February 1

Last day to apply for upper\-year [Winter Session residence](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/returning/#wintersession) \(for 2026/27\)\.

Mid\-February

A random residence allocation process determines application status\. There are two possibilities: receive a residence offer or be added to a [waitlist](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/waitlists-sublets/)\.

Mid\-February–early\-April

Deadline for students who receive an offer to accept and pay a deposit\. Please check your offer for your specific deadline\.

April–August

Applicants on the waitlist may receive offers by email as spaces become available\.

May 1

Deadline to withdraw and receive a full deposit refund\.

May 15

Deadline to update roommate group requests [online](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/)\.

June

Residence offers sent by email\. Students have seven days to accept and pay first instalment of [residence fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/)\.

Early August

Check your [online account](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/) for information about contacting roommate\(s\)\.

August 10

Deadline to apply for [early arrival](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/eligibility/#early-arrival "Eligibility")\.

August 23

Deadline to apply for a [UBCcard](http://ubccard.ubc.ca/obtaining-a-ubccard/students) and receive it in your residence move\-in package\.

*The option to have your UBCcard delivered to residence typically opens in mid\-July\. Please check the applicable box in your UBCcard application\.*

August 26

Deadline to order [bed/bath linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens") from Campus Linens of Canada for on\-time delivery to residence\.

September 5

Students [move in](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/moving-in/ "Moving In") for 2026/27 Winter Session residence\.

September 25

Second instalment of [residence fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/ "fees") due\.

Mid\-December

Deadline to order [bed/bath linens](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/bed-linens/ "Bed Linens") from Campus Linens of Canada for on\-time delivery to residence \(Term 2\)\.

*Note: if you missed the deadline, you may still be able to receive linens by move\-in day\. Campus Linens can provide more specific delivery timelines during the order process\.*

**2026 Summer Important Dates**

Summer Residence &amp; Summer Stay Through Dates

## 2026

Early\-April

Offers are sent by email\. First instalment of fees due on acceptance\.

Mid\-April

Unit/room assignments are sent by email for applications indicating a May start date\. Full fees are due upon acceptance for contracts that end on or prior to July 1\.

Late\-April–May 3

Move\-in period for Winter Session and Year Round residents moving into Summer Stay Through Residence\. We will extend your stay in your current residence until your room is ready for move in\.

May 10

Move\-in date for new Summer Housing residents that have not lived in Winter Session or Year Round housing during the term prior\.

May–June

Unit/room assignments are sent by email for applications indicating a June or July start date\. Full fees due upon acceptance for contracts that end on or prior to July 1\.

June 12

Second instalment is due for full summer contract\.

August 22

[Move out](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/moving-out/ "Moving Out") for students without a 2026/27 Winter Session Residence assignment\.

Late\-August to Early\-Sept\.

Move out for students transferring into 2026/27 Winter Session Residence\.

**Year Round Residence Dates**

Year Round Residence contracts run from the beginning of May to the end of April\.\*

[Applications](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-to-apply/) are accepted any time\. Find out [why you should apply early](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/ "How We Assign Rooms")\.
