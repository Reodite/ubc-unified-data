**Living and dining in residence is an important part of the first year experience\.**

Residence fees for [Orchard Commons](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/orchard-commons/) include a [required meal plan](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/residence-meal-plan-all-access-dining/), [internet \(ResNet\)](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/resnet) and utilities\. [Early Arrival and Extension Fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/early-arrival/) and [Parking fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residence-life/services-amenities/parking/ "Parking Fees") are not included\.

-   Learn more about [how and when to pay your fees](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/), plus [cancellations](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/cancellations-refunds/) and [refunds](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/refunds/)\.
