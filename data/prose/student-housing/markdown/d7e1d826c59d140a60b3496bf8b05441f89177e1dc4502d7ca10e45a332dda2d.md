All furnished rooms come with a mattress pad\. However, Student Housing and Community Services does not provide bedding or bath linens—you must bring your own\.

For extra convenience, [Campus Linens of Canada](https://campuslinenscanada.com/ "Campus Linens Canada") offers online sales of high quality bed and bath linens and is the only vendor approved to deliver products to UBC’s residences before students move in\.

## Delivery order deadline

### **Term 1 Move In \(2026/27\)**

**August 20, 2026:** Deadline to order bed/bath linens from [Campus Linens of Canada](https://campuslinenscanada.com/) for on\-time delivery to residence for Jump Start Move\-in Day \(Term 1\)\.  
**August 26, 2026:** Standard deadline to order bed/bath linens from [Campus Linens of Canada](https://campuslinenscanada.com/) for on\-time delivery to residence \(Term 1\)\.

*Note: if you missed the deadline, you may still be able to receive linens by move\-in day\. Campus Linens can provide more specific delivery timelines during the order process\.*

### First Year Students

-   The sheet size for the following first year residence areas is **Twin XL**: Orchard Commons, Totem Park, Place Vanier and Ritsumeikan\-UBC House\. For other primarily first year residence areas, such as Walter Gage, please see the table below\.
-   If you don’t yet know which residence you will be living in, you can still order your bed linens from Campus Linens of Canada\.
-   When you place your order, enter the address as your residence area \(i\.e\., Totem Park\)\. Campus Linens will verify your residence area with us before shipping, and your bed linens will be sent to you at the correct residence\.
