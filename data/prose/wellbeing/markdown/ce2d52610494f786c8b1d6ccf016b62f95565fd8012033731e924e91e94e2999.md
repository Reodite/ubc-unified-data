The [UBC Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre) offers a variety of workshops for student leaders\. Workshops will help you navigate conversations with peers in need of support, plan inclusive health and wellness events, learn to be a role\-model for wellbeing, and more\.

Visit the [Wellness Workshops page](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre/wellness-workshops) for more information on workshops for student leaders\. You can also request a workshop for a specific group\.
