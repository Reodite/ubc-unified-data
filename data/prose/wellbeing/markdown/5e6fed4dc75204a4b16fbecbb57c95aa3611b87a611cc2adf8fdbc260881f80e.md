The [UBC Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre) offers a variety of workshops for students\. Learn to balance your academic commitments with your physical and mental wellness, manage your time effectively, communicate about sex, sexual health and consent, and much more\. 

Visit the [Wellness Workshops page](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre/wellness-workshops) for a full list of workshops for students\. You can also request a workshop for a specific group\.
