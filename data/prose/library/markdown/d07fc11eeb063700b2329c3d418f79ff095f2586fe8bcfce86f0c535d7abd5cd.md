UBC Librarians create [Research Guides](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/menu) to help you locate resources and improve your research skills\. Find research guidance for subject areas or disciplines, broad topics, specific courses, research methods, and for finding and using different types of content and tools\.

You can also contact your [Subject Librarian](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/subjectlibrarians/) for assistance with finding and evaluating relevant sources of information\.
