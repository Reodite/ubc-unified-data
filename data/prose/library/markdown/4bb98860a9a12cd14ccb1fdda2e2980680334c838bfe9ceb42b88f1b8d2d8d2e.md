This is a general system error that may occur when authentication fails or a session expires\.

Try clearing your browser cache and signing back into OpenAthens via the [UBC Library homepage](https://www.library.ubc.ca/ "https://www.library.ubc.ca/")\. From the library homepage, click “Login” in the top\-right corner and select “OpenAthens Login” from the dropdown menu\.

You may also try:

1.  Disabling any VPNs or browser extensions that may interfere with login
2.  Using a different browser \(e\.g\., Chrome, Firefox, Edge\)
3.  Trying an incognito/private browsing window

If the issue persists, connect with our support team by completing an [Ask Us form](https://ask.library.ubc.ca/ask/)
