If you know the title of the article, you can begin by searching for it in [Summon](https://ubc.summon.serialssolutions.com/#!/search?pn=1&amp;ho=t&amp;include.ft.matches=f&amp;l=en&amp;q=)\. Enter the main title using quotation marks to search for those exact words\. If needed, select the Journal Articles filter listed under Content Type\. 

[Summon search of "The science of literature reviews" with Journal Articles selected under Content Type\.](https://d1qywhc7l90rsa.cloudfront.net/accounts/80416/images/FAQ_SummonArticleSearch.png)

If you have trouble finding the article or want to make sure we have a particular journal, search for a [journal by title](https://search.library.ubc.ca/#journals)\. Under journal details, select a holding \(via the hyperlink\) for the relevant dates\. Then navigate to the correct volume or issue\. This is also a good way to browse a journal's publications\. 

[Journal search showing the holdings of AlterNative : An International Journal of Indigenous Peoples](https://d1qywhc7l90rsa.cloudfront.net/accounts/80416/images/FAQ_JournalSearchExample.png)

If you are not looking for a specific journal article, but need support finding articles related to a specific research topic contact your [subject librarian](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/subjectlibrarians/)\.
