UBC Library provides an online payment system available to all students, staff and faculty, as well as community card members\. This system allows users to view their current library fines/fees, view their payment history, and pay library fines &amp; fees\. Visa, Mastercard and Interac Debit are accepted in the online payment system\. To pay using the online system visit: [https://paynow\.library\.ubc\.ca/](https://paynow.library.ubc.ca/)\. 

For users looking to pay by cash or by card in person at the Vancouver Campus, please visit the circulation desk at Koerner Library, Woodward Library or the Music Art and Architecture Library\. For users looking to pay in person by debit at the Okanagan Campus, please visit the Library Service Desk\. To review library hours and locations: [https://hours\.library\.ubc\.ca/](https://hours.library.ubc.ca/)\. 

You can also call us for support: Vancouver: 604\-822\-2406, Okanagan: 250\-807\-9107 

Visit the [Loan Policies &amp; Fines](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/loan-policies-and-fines/) website for related information\.
