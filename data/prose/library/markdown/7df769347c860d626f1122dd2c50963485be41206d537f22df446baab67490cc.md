UBC Library strives to support the diverse needs of library users\.

Visit [access guides by building](https://services.library.ubc.ca/facilities/disability-access-by-building/) for information on accessible spaces\.

Visit this [accessible library resources guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/accessiblelibraryresources) for information about finding and using accessible resources at UBC, including accessible text providers\.

Students at UBC Vancouver can register for [Enhanced Library Services](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/get-library-access/enhanced/) through the Centre for Accessibility\.

Students at UBC Okanagan can register for access to the Inclusive Technology Lab in the Okanagan Library and can view all [accessibility supports](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/accessibility-supports/) available\.
