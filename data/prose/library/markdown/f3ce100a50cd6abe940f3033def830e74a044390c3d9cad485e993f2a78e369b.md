UBC Library provides guidance on how to cite sources in multiple ways:

-   The [Library Skills Tutorial](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/library_tutorial/citation) provides an overview of citation topics
-   Select a [Citation Guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/citation), curated by UBC Librarians, to help you cite in a specific citation style, cite different types of sources including AI, or learn about citation software
-   [Chat](https://answers.library.ubc.ca/askus/) with a library employee live online
-   [Visit](https://hours.library.ubc.ca/) a library location and ask your question at a service desk
-   Attend a workshop on [citation management software](https://libcal.library.ubc.ca/calendar/vancouver?cid=-1&amp;t=g&amp;d=0000-00-00&amp;cal=-1&amp;ct=33741&amp;inc=0)
-   Contact your [subject librarian](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/subjectlibrarians/)
