If you are not a current student, staff, or faculty member, you can print and copy at UBC Vancouver by purchasing a UBC Guest card\. Guest cards are loaded with a minimum of $5\.

Visit the [Pay for Print for Visitors and Guests](https://services.library.ubc.ca/computers-technology/copy-print-scan/pay-for-print-visitors-guests/) page for more information about purchasing and using print cards\.

At the Okanagan Library, guests can inquire about printing options at the service desk\.
