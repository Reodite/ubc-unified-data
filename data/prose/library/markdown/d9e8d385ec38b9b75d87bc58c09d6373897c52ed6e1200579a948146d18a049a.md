Hospital staff cards are exclusively issued for one of the following UBC\-affiliated teaching hospitals:

-   BC Children’s Hospital and BC Women’s Hospital &amp; Health Centre
-   St\. Paul’s Hospital
-   Vancouver General Hospital
-   UBC Hospital

Visit the [Library Access for Staff](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/get-library-access/staff/) webpage for more information about this service, or connect with our support team at [library\.accounts@ubc\.ca](mailto:library.accounts@ubc.ca) to resolve the issue\.

For remote eResource access, users must be UBC appointed and have an active status in Workday\. To access additional online resources that have been licensed for use on UBC computers as a non\-UBC appointed hospital staff member, visit the [BMB Library](https://woodward.library.ubc.ca/services-at-hospitals/bmb/) \(or any other [UBC library location](https://hours.library.ubc.ca/)\) to register for a [Guest OpenAthens account](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/connect/openathens/guests)\.
