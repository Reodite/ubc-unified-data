You can request expedited access to items the [library catalogue \(OPAC\)](https://webcat1.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/searchBasic?) that have a status message of: In Process, On Order, and Pre\-Order Process\.   
  
Depending on the exact stage of receiving, processing and cataloguing that the item is in, we may or may not be able to increase the speed of delivery\.  

If we can rush the delivery of the item to you, it will be sent to the library location listed in the library catalogue record, and the contact information collected in this form will be used to notify you when the item is available\. Due to the variation in processing stages, we cannot estimate a delivery timeline\. 

Note: If a physical item in the catalogue has a call number displayed, it is already on its way to the library location and cannot be further rushed\.

Complete [this form to place rush item request](https://ask.library.ubc.ca/rushrequest/)
