UBC Faculty can authorize library access to people they are working with through a Faculty Authorization library account\. A Faculty Authorization library account provides full access to online resources as well as a card to borrow physical items\. Faculty Authorization accounts must be renewed yearly, and require the signature and contact information of the authorizing Faculty member\.

Visit the [Library Access for Faculty page](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/get-library-access/faculty/) and connect with the UBC Faculty member you are working with to apply\.
