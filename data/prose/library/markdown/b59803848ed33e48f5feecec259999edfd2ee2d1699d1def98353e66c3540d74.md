Refunds are issued if the quality of printing is poor or you were mistakenly charged\.

UBC Vancouver students: If you are printing at IKBLC or in any UBC Library, visit a UBC Library service desk at the nearest library branch to request a refund\. The local desk will review and can issue a refund directly\. If you are in residence, contact [Student Housing and Community Services](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/contact-us/ "https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/contact-us/")\.

UBC Okanagan students: Visit the [UBCO IT](https://ubc.service-now.com/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0019650) website for steps on requesting a refund\.
