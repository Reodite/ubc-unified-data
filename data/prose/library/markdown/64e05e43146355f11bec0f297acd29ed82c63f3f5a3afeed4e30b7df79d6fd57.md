The **General tab uses Summon**, a discovery tool that searches across many library resources at once\.

The other tabs \(Books &amp; Media, Indexes and Databases, Journal Titles\) do not use Summon\. Instead, they connect to separate library systems designed for specific purposes\.

-   Use the **Books &amp; Media** tab to search the UBC Library Catalogue for books and other catalogue materials\. 
-   Use the **Indexes &amp; Databases** tab to locate subject\-specific research databases\.
-   Use the **Journal Titles** tab to look up a specific journal by title\.

Because these tabs search different systems, results may vary depending on which tab you use\.

Visit [How to Search Summon](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/summonhelp) for more details
