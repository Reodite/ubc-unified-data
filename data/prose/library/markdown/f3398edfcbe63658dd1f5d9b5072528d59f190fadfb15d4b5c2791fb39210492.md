Yes, Library card holders can designate somebody to pick up library materials on their behalf\! In order to do so, please complete the UBC Proxy Patron Form on the [Library's Loan Policies and Fines page](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/loan-policies-and-fines/)\. 

Note that the Proxy Authorization form allows UBC library card holders to designate someone to borrow materials for a specified period of time, and requests longer than 1 year must be renewed\. The proxy must be 18 years or older and must provide one piece of government\-issued, photo ID during their registration\.
