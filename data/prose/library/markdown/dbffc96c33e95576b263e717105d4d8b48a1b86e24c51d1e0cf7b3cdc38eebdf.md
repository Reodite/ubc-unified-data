These instructions are for UBC students:

UBC Vancouver:

-   To pay online, login to [payforprint\.ubc\.ca](https://payforprint.ubc.ca/) and navigate to Add Money\- UBC Card
    -   Use the system to select a Campus Plan to add a minimum of $5\.00 CAD
    -   Please note that TD bank cards often do not work
-   To pay with cash visit the card vending machine on level 3 of the [Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](https://ikblc.ubc.ca/)
-   If you have a $0 balance showing for printing but have money on your meal plan, ensure your UBCCard or Residence Flex Meal plan is activated before trying to print, visit [UBC Student Housing and Community Services](https://broker.id.ubc.ca/auth/realms/idb0/protocol/saml?SAMLRequest=fZLNboMwEIRfBfkebJwAwQpINDk0UtqiQnvopTLgFqtgU6%2Fpz9sXQqOml5x39tuZ0W6Ad23P0sE26l68DwKs89W1CthxEKPBKKY5SGCKdwKYrVie3hwYdQnrjba60i1yUgBhrNRqqxUMnTC5MB%2ByEg%2F3hxg11vbAMAZRDUa4jR5Aqld3KCu34jhvZFnqVtjGBdB4glOc3eUFcnajG6n4xP2jlEa%2FCePK%2BgTgo3dsBG87wLIuCT7ZwlME5Ox3MXpecr%2BKSPkShdHK9ygh9dqPVlEYiHBNwnI5ygAGsVdgubIxooQGC%2BIvqFdQj9GALYMn5GS%2F5Cup6jHC5XbKWQTsuiiyxZzoURg4phkFKNlMBtnxsDlr%2FTKWn6pGycViN%2FiMPp%2Fq2e2I2%2B8y3crq20nbVn9ux%2BasiJGHcDKv%2FP%2BG5Ac%3D&amp;RelayState=ss%3Amem%3A3721c2e33d19d93980d04db99f2718d2f1253648496a8d248ed1e62e7e1c858e&amp;SigAlg=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.w3.org%2F2001%2F04%2Fxmldsig-more%23rsa-sha256&amp;Signature=INjm6bsmnIQodLTQqUGwN6UV5m3809DMrhW%2BSF1mT%2FYNxhs2PC7v3eDweJ7w9Mdo2vf8ActgAkhzYIIbokdMVwZJi9gwcrsxWZzgEhCviXEGY6wEN9laFHBs5yUdhO9Kd1c6nLyOjcb7W12SkI1XFkKKwncN%2Bo5Swvu9FEt6KM7WaKGbIOoK8sk5pfW9mYkggBDKWejBJZmGyXoTAGUFtTE5RS%2Bm8su9nMbA8vzPzjYu7bBXSPR%2Bu%2FccM%2FJc7cCKhECs1KJR6pFo%2FB5AEnkwx3cS5%2FcRF%2BJ%2B8tNka2E1SRB5GvJv7QIdsiGntdEY8NALpYvDszZpY5SkILr8tY5Rs09FmlJovfFnnhatDbgszP5K3Tk8wMUqCKPeVtjIinwkGdxwfRZ%2BSesVE4VgsnCWloWUV3u%2BQhpRS4uIymZRqxkY9Ry9g2KuBuU9KuajpvXMvrql6PsPzuHuUqMAp4ehVU8X3J41TDZvNsxmVZNBfspkQzq3ZTquYTfNEhpoG2rh) to activate your card
