UBC Library supports two curated repositories that meet most funder and publisher requirements for data deposit, increasingly mandated by funders like the Tri\-Agencies:

-   [UBC Dataverse Collection \(Borealis\)](https://researchdata.library.ubc.ca/deposit/dataverse): a national repository for more than 90 Canadian institutions, curated by UBC Library\. Best for small to medium\-sized datasets; supports DOI assignment, access controls, and FAIR principles\.
-   [Federated Research Data Repository \(FRDR\)](https://researchdata.library.ubc.ca/deposit/frdr/): a national platform curated by the Digital Research Alliance of Canada, designed for large\-scale datasets \(terabytes\)\.

UBC Library can also help identify discipline\-specific repositories\. Visit the [Deposit](https://researchdata.library.ubc.ca/deposit/) page or contact [research\.data@ubc\.ca](mailto:research.data@ubc.ca) to request a consultation\.
