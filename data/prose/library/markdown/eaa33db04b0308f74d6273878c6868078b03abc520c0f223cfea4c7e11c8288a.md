To book an appointment with your subject librarian for support with finding, evaluating and using all types of information sources, including literature reviews, consult the [subject librarian directory](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/subjectlibrarians/) and connect with your librarian\. 

For support with knowledge synthesis projects including systematic, scoping, and related reviews, visit the [Knowledge Synthesis service](https://knowledgesynthesis.library.ubc.ca/) page for more information\.

Visit the [Rare Books &amp; Special Collections](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/), [University Archives](https://archives.library.ubc.ca/), and [Okanagan Special Collections and Archives](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/special-collections-and-archives/) for details on how to plan a visit to engage with unique collections\.
