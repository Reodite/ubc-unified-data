UBC Library cardholders can request library materials via UBC's Document Delivery \(DocDel\) service \- this service retrieves library materials for you\. 

The most common use of this service is book and article delivery between UBC campuses \(eg\. UBC Point Grey, UBC Robson Square, UBC Okanagan, and UBC teaching hospitals: BMB and BCCW Learning Commons\)\. 

DocDel also serves UBC undergraduate students and faculty in the Island Medical Program or Northern Medical Program, [distance education students](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/get-library-access/registering-for-distance-learning-library-services/), and students who qualify for [Enhanced Library Services](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/get-library-access/enhanced/)\. 

For more information, including details on fees attached to some DocDel services and DocDel contacts, please review the Library's [DocDel guide](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/docdel/#WhatisUBCDocDel-0)\.
