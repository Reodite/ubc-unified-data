Users will need to log in with either their CWLs or OpenAthens accounts in order to view online journals and access databases\. To log in, navigate to the [library home page](https://www.library.ubc.ca/) and click "Login" and select the "OpenAthens Login" button on the right hand side of the page\.

[OpenAthens Login button on the Library home page Login menu](https://d1qywhc7l90rsa.cloudfront.net/accounts/156391/images/OA.png)

If you are a current student, staff or faculty member, log in with your CWL\. Other users should log in with their OpenAthens accounts\. Not all OpenAthens accounts provide access to online resources remotely; for more information, review the [Connect to Library Resources guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/connect/general)\.

Note: Alumni can log in with a CWL to access select resources\. Please see [our FAQ about Alumni resources](https://answers.library.ubc.ca/askus/faq/215678) for more information\. 

Users can also log in via the Summon search page by clicking "Log in" on the top right of the results page:  

[Log in button on the Summon search page](https://d1qywhc7l90rsa.cloudfront.net/accounts/156391/images/Summon.png)

Logging in will show all available results, and provide access to online resources\. 

For assistance [locating journal articles](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/JournalArticles) or [navigating databases](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/library_tutorial/searching#s-lg-box-16492432), review the linked guides or reach out to your [Subject Librarian](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/subjectlibrarians/)\. 

If you have followed these steps and are unable to access online resources, submit an [Ask Us form](https://ask.library.ubc.ca/ask/)\.
