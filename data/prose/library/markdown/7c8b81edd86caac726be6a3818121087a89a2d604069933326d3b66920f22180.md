You can cancel a study room booking at multiple points during the booking process\. Before completing the room booking process, you will have the opportunity to review your booking; if you do not wish to proceed with the location or time you have booked, you may cancel your specific booking by clicking "cancel" on the Booking Review page\. 

[Study Room cancellation button on the Booking Review page](https://d1qywhc7l90rsa.cloudfront.net/accounts/156391/images/Booking_Review.png)

If you have completed the room booking process, but wish to cancel, you can do so by viewing the email confirmation for your study room booking; at the bottom of the email confirmation is a link you can follow to cancel the booking\.

[Cancellation link included in the email confirmation for study rooms](https://d1qywhc7l90rsa.cloudfront.net/accounts/156391/images/Email.png)

When you successfully cancel a booking, you will receive an email confirmation\.
