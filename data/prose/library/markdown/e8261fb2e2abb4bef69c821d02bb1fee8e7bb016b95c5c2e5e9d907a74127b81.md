UBC cardholders can order books and articles from other institutions via our Interlibrary Loan \(ILL\) service\.

UBC cardholders can request and track orders from other institutions\. Please review [UBC's Interlibrary Loan guide](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/ill/) for more information on how to order, fee information for Community Cardholders and Alumni, and contact information for UBC ILL\.
