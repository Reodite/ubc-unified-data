Please search the Library's [Catalogue](http://webcat.library.ubc.ca/) to check if a copy is already in our collection before submitting a request\.

For course materials or reserves, please use [LOCR \(Library Online Course Reserves\)](https://courses.library.ubc.ca/)

What happens after a title is suggested?

1.  A librarian reviews your suggestion and may order the title if it meets our Collection Development Policies
2.  For non\-digital titles selected for purchase, if a library barcode is included in the form, you will be notified when the item is available for pick up

Note: Due to the high volume of suggestions we are unable to respond to all submissions

To suggest a book or other material, [complete this form](https://ask.library.ubc.ca/suggestbook/)\.
