UBC Library provides many services for researchers\. This list is not comprehensive, but intended to connect researchers with key contacts in the library:

-   [Subject librarians](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/subjectlibrarians/) provide individualized support with many aspects of the research lifecycle
-   [Borrowing services](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/get-library-access/faculty/) are available to all researchers and there are options for visiting scholar access
-   The [Scholarly Communications Office](https://scholcomm.ubc.ca/?login) at UBC Vancouver provides support for journal discounts, open publishing, copyright and related topics
-   The [Research Commons](https://researchcommons.library.ubc.ca/?login) is a multidisciplinary hub that provides access to services and expertise in digital scholarship, geospatial information systems \(GIS\), data services, research data management \(RDM\), and citation management
-   The [Centre for Scholarly Communication](https://csc.ok.ubc.ca/) at UBC Okanagan, provide support for journal discounts, open publishing, copyright and related topics, data services, research data management \(RDM\), and citation management
-   Visit the [Research Data Management](https://rdm.ubc.ca/) website for data management services
-   Visit the [Knowledge Synthesis Service](https://knowledgesynthesis.library.ubc.ca/?login) website for two tiers of service which support different types of reviews
-   Visit the [cIRcle](https://circle.ubc.ca/?login) website to review options for depositing research outputs at UBC Library
