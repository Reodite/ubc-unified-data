UBC Library provides [bookable study spaces](https://services.library.ubc.ca/facilities/bookable-study-spaces/) in many of our library locations\.

1.  Locate the space you want to book
2.  Select a start and end time
3.  Login with your CWL
4.  Agree to the terms and conditions, which vary by location
5.  Complete your booking
6.  You will receive an email confirmation of your booking, and you can cancel your booking from the link in your email
