UBC Information Technology, in partnership with Rogers Cable, offers cable television in some student residences and many administrative buildings on campus\. 

Students, faculty, and residents have the option of subscribing to Rogers Cable services, including HDTV, Pay Per View, and Video on Demand\. 

To access UBC/Rogers digital cable TV services, a Rogers digital terminal is required\.  

For more details please call Rogers Customer Service at 1\-844\-235\-7429 if you are UBC Faculty or Staff\. Studentsin Residence can contact their front desk\. Please note that you must quote your building's full street address \(not UBC building name\) in order to be recognized as one of Rogers' serviceable areas\. More information can also be found in the [Frequently Asked Questions](https://it.ubc.ca/node/2623) section\.
