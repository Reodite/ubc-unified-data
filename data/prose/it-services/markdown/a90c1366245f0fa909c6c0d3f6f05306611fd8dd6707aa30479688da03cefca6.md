Workday is UBC’s human resource \(HR\), finance, payroll operations and student information systems \(formerly SSC, SISC and FSC\)\. All UBC employees – faculty, staff and student employees – use Workday for personal tasks related to their position \(e\.g\. view payslip, submit vacation request\)\. Some also use Workday as part of their job at UBC \(e\.g\. run reports, post a job, pay an invoice, move a student off a waitlist\)\.

UBC students use Workday to manage their academic journey, from course registration to graduation\.

Workday is managed by the Integrated Service Centre \(ISC\)\.

-   [About Workday](https://isc.ubc.ca/about/workday)
-   [About ISC](https://isc.ubc.ca/about/isc)

[**Log in to Workday**](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/) \(CWL account required\)

**Note for Students**: If you don’t have a Student CWL, you should only create one *after* you have accepted your offer and received your student number\. You can [set up a Student CWL](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/how-create-account "How to Create an Account") through UBC IT\.

## Features and Benefits

Workday brings all of UBC’s core administration \(HR, Finance, Payroll, Student\) onto one platform\. Available 24/7 and accessible through any internet browser\.

## Get Help

**Faculty, Staff, and Student Employee Support**

Get Workday support from the Integrated Service Centre \(ISC\):

-   Submit a request through [Get Workday Support](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_category&amp;sys_id=48e309381bcb54908f672fc02a4bcb5a) in the UBC Self Service Portal
-   Contact the ISC by phone 604\-822\-8200 \(UBCV\) or 250\-807\-8163 \(UBCO\), Monday–Friday, 8:30 am–4:30 pm \(except statutory holidays\)
-   Faculty and staff who need help with Workday Student can also email [isc\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:isc.support@ubc.ca)\.

Learn more on the ISC [Workday Support](https://isc.ubc.ca/resources/workday-support#workday-student) page\.  
  
Note:  While many of UBC’s administrative and operational processes run in Workday, the ISC can only provide support with how to use Workday\. Inquiries about what to do in Workday should go to the [respective operational department](https://isc.ubc.ca/resources/workday-support#operational-support) \(e\.g\. Central HR\) or your Faculty/unit administrators\.
