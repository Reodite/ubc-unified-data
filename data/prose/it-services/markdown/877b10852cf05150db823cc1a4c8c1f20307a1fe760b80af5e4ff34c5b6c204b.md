The award\-winning UBC Studios produces professional quality media for the university\. Our studio space features advanced equipment and experienced staff dedicated to providing end\-to\-end creative production services with a focus on communication, recruitment, training and educational content\. Working together, we will help you determine the most effective approach for your unique audience and budget constraints\. Contact us today to kickstart the creative development process and find out how we can tailor our offerings to meet your unique needs\.

## Features and Benefits

UBC Studios provides full service media production, which can include:

**Concept Development &amp; Production Planning**

The team at UBC Studios will work with you to create innovative concepts specific to your individual communication, education, training, or recruitment needs\.

**Production Planning**

-   Studio &amp; On\-location Video Production
-   Animation and Motion Graphics
-   Immersive Media \(XR, VR, AR, 360 Video\)
-   Photography
-   Virtual Tours
