UBC is building a foundation to introduce a suite of Microsoft 365 \(M365\) apps into our technology environment\.

To learn more, visit the [M365 @ UBC](https://it.ubc.ca/microsoft-365 "Microsoft 365") portal site\.

Microsoft \(MS\) Teams is a collaboration tool for groups to create content and share the information they need to work together\. The tool is operated by Microsoft as part of their MS 365 suite\. MS Teams enables users across the University to connect via instant messaging, video conferencing, online meetings, and simultaneously work on a document in real\-time together\.

## Features and Benefits

-   Video conferencing
-   Persistent chat
-   Collaborate in real time
-   [MS Whiteboard integration](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/microsoft-whiteboard "Microsoft Whiteboard")

To learn about the differences between UBC collaboration tools that are available, [click here](https://it.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/UBC%20Collaboration%20Tools%20-%20Features%20Comparison%20Chart.pdf)

## Requirements and Eligibility

Active UBC Students, Faculty and Staff

**Faculty and Staff:**

-   An active employee status
-   An active Enterprise Active Directory \(EAD\) account
-   An active Faculty and Staff \(FASmail\) mailbox
-   A *[firstname\.lastname@ubc\.ca](mailto:firstname.lastname@ubc.ca)* email alias that is associated with their FASmail mailbox

**Students:**

-   An active student status
-   An active Enterprise Active Directory \(EAD\) account
-   An active UBC Student Email mailbox with a [cwl@student\.ubc\.ca](mailto:cwl@student.ubc.ca) email address

For information on eligibility requirements for Guests, please review our [FAQs](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/microsoft-teams/microsoft-teams-faqs "Microsoft Teams FAQs")\.

## **Report Content**

### **Students**

To report inappropriate/ unsolicited content on MS Teams or OneDrive, please take a screenshot of the content and report it to your [Student Advisor](https://ssc.adm.ubc.ca/sscportal/servlets/SSCMain.jsp?function=Finances-Summary) listed in your Workday or [Student Services](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/diversity-campus/discrimination-harassment) \(in case of discrimination and harassment\) or [Academic Advising \(Vancouver\)](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising)/ [Academic Advising \(Okanagan\)](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/academic-advising/)\.

If you would like the content to be removed, please request the Teams owner\.

### **Faculty or Staff**

To report inappropriate/ unsolicited content on MS Teams or OneDrive, please take a screenshot and discuss the content with your [HR and Faculty Relations](https://www.hr.ubc.ca/contact/) or [Equity and Inclusion Office](https://equity.ubc.ca/about/contact/) or follow the [bullying and harassment](https://bullyingandharassment.ubc.ca/) reporting process\.

### How do I sign in as faculty or staff?

#### **1\. Verify service eligibility**

-   Ensure your FASmail is associated with *[firstname\.lastname@ubc\.ca](mailto:firstname.lastname@ubc.ca)* email alias
-   If you have a FASmail account, but do not have *[firstname\.lastname@ubc\.ca](mailto:firstname.lastname@ubc.ca)* email alias, please contact your department directory admin\.
-   If you do not have FASmail account, please contact your local IT help desk or UBC IT help desk\.
-   Once you meet the service eligibility, you will automatically get access to MS Teams and OneDrive within 48 hrs

#### **2\. Sign in to portal\.office\.com**

-   Sign in using your *[firstname\.lastname@ubc\.ca](mailto:firstname.lastname@ubc.ca)* email and CWL password
-   You can either download MS Teams and OneDrive app on your device OR use MS Teams and OneDrive web version
-   [Log into Teams](https://portal.office.com/)
-   **UBC Owned Device**: If you have an UBC\-owned device, you can check if you have a Teams desktop application shortcut\. If you do not, please download Teams from the Software Centre\. Note: Please reboot your device for the Teams desktop shortcut to appear

#### **3\. Get started**

-   Click on Get started icon in MS Teams to access training resources

### How do I sign in as a student?

#### **1\. Sign up for UBC Student Email**

-   Go to [CWL MyAccount page](https://www.myaccount.ubc.ca/myAccount/login.xhtml)
-   \(Optional\) Rename your CWL ID
-   Click *Activate Student Email*
-   Read and accept Terms of Use
-   Your *[cwl@student\.ubc\.ca](mailto:cwl@student.ubc.ca)* email will be created within 2 hrs and;
-   you will also automatically get access to MS Teams and OneDrive within 48 hrs

#### **2\. Sign in to portal\.office\.com**

-   Sign in using your *[cwl@student\.ubc\.ca](mailto:cwl@student.ubc.ca)* email and CWL password
-   You can either download MS Teams and OneDrive app on your device OR you use MS Teams and OneDrive web version
-   [Log into Teams](https://portal.office.com/)

#### **3\. Get started**

-   Click on *Get started* icon in MS Teams to access training resources
