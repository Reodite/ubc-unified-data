The Campus Wide Login \(CWL\) system provides access to many of the University's online systems, such as the Student Workday and Canvas\.

This is the most important login account for Students, Staff, Faculty, Alumni, Guests, Extended Learners, and others accessing UBC resources\.

## Features and Benefits

-   Available 24/7
-   [Description](https://it.ubc.ca/node/999)

## Requirements and Eligibility

-   Create a CWL Account
    -   For a step\-by\-step guide, please refer to our [how\-to create an account page](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/how-create-account "How to Create an Account")\.
-   [Add multi\-factor authentication \(MFA\) protection](https://mfadevices.id.ubc.ca/) to your account
    -   For more information about MFA, visit [it\.ubc\.ca/mfa](https://it.ubc.ca/mfa)
-   [Sponsor a Guest](https://access.ubc.ca/)
    -   For a step\-by\-step guide, please refer to our [How\-to Sponsor a Guest page](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/how-sponsor-guest "How to Sponsor a Guest")\.
-   To manage your account and services, please go to [myAccount](https://www.myaccount.ubc.ca/myAccount/)
-   Administrators, [manage integration requests here](http://web.it.ubc.ca/forms/iam/)
