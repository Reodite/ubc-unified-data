The UBC Survey Tool is provided by Qualtrics\. It is an easy\-to\-use, top\-tier survey tool platform that offers a wide range of features\. It complies with the BC **Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act** \(FIPPA\) because the survey data is kept secure and is stored and backed up in Canada\.

**NOTE:** If you use the UBC Survey Tool, your name will be available to other users of the tool\. This feature is important to facilitate collaboration\.

The UBC Survey Tool is available to all UBC faculty, staff, actively enrolled students, and sponsored guests with an active CWL account\. Users can login at [https://ubc\.qualtrics\.com](https://ubc.qualtrics.com/) with their **CWL account** to access the tool immediately\.

Before accessing the UBC Survey Tool for the first time, it is important for you to [read the Terms of Use](https://it.ubc.ca/services/teaching-learning-tools/survey-tool/ubc-survey-tool-terms-use "UBC Survey Tool Terms of Use")\.

[**Login to Qualtrics**](https://ubc.qualtrics.com/)

## Features and Benefits

-   Over 100 question types to choose from
-   Advanced branching and customization
-   Extensive reporting and analytics
-   Multi\-channel distribution
-   Compliant with BC Privacy laws and legislations

## Requirements and Eligibility

The service is available to all UBC staff, faculty members, actively enrolled students and sponsored guests with an active CWL account\. To sponsor a guest please [visit the CWL site](https://access.ubc.ca/)\.

## Price

The Survey Tool is available for active CWL account holders at no cost\. Holders of basic CWL accounts are ineligible for this service\.

#### Getting Started

 **Important Note**: The time zone of your Qualtrics account is set to Mountain Time by default\. To switch to Pacific Time, navigate to *Account Settings* by clicking on the icon in the top right corner of your screen\. Select the desired time zone under *Change Time Zone*\. The new setting will be saved automatically, and will remain unless manually updated\.
