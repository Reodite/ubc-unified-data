> Canvas is UBC’s primary learning platform for delivering online course content\. UBC instructors and students can log in to Canvas with their UBC CWL \(Campus\-Wide Login\)\.

[**Log in to Canvas**](https://canvas.ubc.ca/)  

## Canvas for instructors

In Canvas, instructors can share materials, enable student collaboration and discussion, manage assignments and quizzes, and assign grades\. Canvas can also be used with many other UBC learning technologies\.

### Learn more:

[http://lthub\.ubc\.ca/guides/canvas\-instructor\-guide/](http://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/canvas-instructor-guide/)[Access UBC's instructor guide to Canvas by clicking here](https://about.canvas.ubc.ca/files/2021/12/canvas-instructor-guide.png)

## Canvas for students

Students can use Canvas to communicate, engage with course materials, and complete learning activities\. Additional features in Canvas can help students keep track of their work, collaborate with classmates, and preview grades\.
