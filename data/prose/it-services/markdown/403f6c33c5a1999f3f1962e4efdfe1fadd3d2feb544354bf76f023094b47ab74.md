Initial consultation for digital media projects are available at no cost through [UBC Studios](https://ubcstudios.ubc.ca/)\. During your professional media consultation, UBC Studios staff can assist with:

-   Defining requirements
-   Providing guidance for grant funding
-   Scoping and budgeting
-   Making stylistic recommendations

Learn about options available to you and decide whether a professional or do\-it\-yourself approach makes the most sense for your digital media project\.

## Features and Benefits

UBC Studios can assist you in applying for educational or research grants by offering cost estimates for the digital media aspect for your proposal\. Experienced staff can help you avoid pitfalls that might otherwise be overlooked\.

-   Learn what options are available including new technologies and innovative ideas
-   Decide on the best approach for you project \- Do\-It\-Yourself \(DIY\) vs\. Professional

## Price

Media consultations are free for UBC faculty and staff members creating UBC content\.
