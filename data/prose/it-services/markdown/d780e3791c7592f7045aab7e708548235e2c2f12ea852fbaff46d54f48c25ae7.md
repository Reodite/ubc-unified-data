#### New Self\-Service Option to Recover MFA Access

On August 27, 2025, UBC IT introduced a self\-service recovery option for MFA access through the existing [MFA Device Management Portal](https://mfadevices.id.ubc.ca/)\. This enables users to securely restore their MFA access if they’ve lost or replaced their phone, without contacting the IT Service Centre\.

This change is in direct response to feedback from students, faculty, and staff who asked for quicker, more modern ways to resolve urgent access issues\. While IT Service Centre support will remain available, the self\-service option gives users more flexibility and control to get back online faster\.

#### What’s changing?

When users need to restore access to their MFA credentials \(for instance, if they’ve lost or replaced their phone\), they will now be able to do so without contacting the IT Service Centre for support with using the new [MFA Device Management Portal](https://mfadevices.id.ubc.ca/)\. The MFA Device Management Portal provides a secure and convenient alternative to restore access, and is a part of UBC IT’s ongoing efforts to:

-   Allow users to manage their own account access when it’s convenient\.
-   Modernize UBC’s digital services so they’re faster and easier to use\.
-   Improve time to resolution

#### IT Service Centre Support Remains Available

This change is designed to increase options for restoring access, not replace live support\. For those that prefer speaking to a live agent over a self\-serve option, the IT Service Centre will continue to support MFA access recovery via their phone line at 604\.822\.2008\.

#### FAQs for the community

### 1\. What is the MFA self\-service recovery option?

The MFA self\-service recovery option enables UBC community members to securely regain access to their Multi\-Factor Authentication \(MFA\) credentials if their registered device is lost or replaced\. This initiative is designed to enhance convenience and expand self\-service options for recovering MFA access\.

### 2\. Why is UBC IT introducing this new service?

Currently, most MFA device recovery requests require a call to the IT Service Centre for manual support\. This can be time\-consuming and inconvenient, particularly for users who prefer to manage their accounts independently\.

The MFA self\-service recovery option has been developed to:

-   Enable faster, more convenient recovery of MFA access\.
-   Modernize core IT services without compromising security\.

### 3\. What verification methods are supported?

Currently, the portal provides self\-recovery options for employees and students who still have access to their old phones or have set up a hardware token for their account\. Additionally, employees can recover their MFA account via SMS if they have lost access to their phone\.

This functionality has been expanded to allow students to also recover their MFA account via SMS if they no longer have access to their old phone and have not set up a hardware token\. This enhancement enables students to restore access to their Duo account \(UBC’s current MFA provider\) without needing to contact IT Operations\.

### 4\. Who can access the MFA Self\-Service Recovery Option?

All UBC users with a Campus\-Wide Login \(CWL\) account may use the portal to self\-recover their MFA account\.

### 5\. Will the IT Service Centre still be available for support?

Yes\. The IT Service Centre will remain available for any user who prefers direct support or is unable to complete the self\-service process\. This change is intended to increase the options available to users, not to replace live support\.

### 6\. When will the MFA Self\-Service Recovery Option be available?

MFA self\-service recovery option using Duo SMS codes \(via mfadevices\.id\.ubc\.ca\) will launch on August 27, 2025\.
