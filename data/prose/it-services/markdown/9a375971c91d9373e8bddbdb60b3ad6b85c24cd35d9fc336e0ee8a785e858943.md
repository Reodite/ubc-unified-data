#### New\! An internet for your personal devices\.

UBC IoT Internet of Things wi\-fi network gives your personal devices their own private network — secure, encrypted, and invisible to everyone else on campus\. Your laptop, phone, gaming console, smart TV, speaker, and other personal devices see each other and work together, just like they do at home\.

UBC staff, faculty, and students can connect their devices to the network using their CWL account \(Multi\-factor Authentication required\)\.   
  
[**Learn More**](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/ubc-internet-things-iot-wi-fi "UBC Internet of Things (IoT) Wi-Fi")

[**Log in and add your devices**](https://iot.ubc.ca/accounts/ubc/device/) 

**Heads\-up**: *for now, log in once every 4 weeks to keep your devices active\. It’s a temporary security measure while we finish automating personal devices this fall — after that, you’ll only log in to add or remove a device\.* 

UBC hosts several wireless networks to support students, faculty and staff on campus and at partner institutions\. Visit the links below to find out which wireless network will best support your needs\.

#### ubcsecure

["ubcsecure"](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30771) uses WPA \(Wi\-Fi Protected Access\) Enterprise and 802\.1x for an encrypted and secure wireless connection\.

*This wireless network is available to everybody within the UBC community with a valid student, faculty, or staff CWL account\.*

#### UBC Internet of Things \(IoT\) Wi\-Fi

[UBC Internet of Things \(IoT\) wi\-fi](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/ubc-internet-things-iot-wi-fi) network gives your personal devices their own private network that is secure, encrypted, and invisible to everyone else on campus\. Your laptop, phone, gaming console, smart TV, speaker, and other personal devices will be able to see each other and work together, just like they do at home\.

#### eduroam

The ["eduroam" network](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30767) is a collaborative network that provides students, staff and faculty roaming wireless access at partner institutions\. Users authenticate using the same credentials as they would at their home institution\.

#### ubcprivate

["ubcprivate"](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30769) is the wireless extension of the [Virtual Networks service](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/virtual-networks "Virtual Networks") and allows authorized users to be placed directly into their department's own network and security domain\.

***Note:** that this wireless network is only available to UBC community members affiliated with departments that have their own* [*virtual network*](https://it.ubc.ca/node/41)*\.*

#### ubcvisitor

["ubcvisitor"](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30773) is a public wireless network service available for guests visiting on the UBC campus\. Some restrictions apply\. We encourage staff, faculty, and students to use ubcsecure as their default UBC network\.

#### RogersGuest

*RogersGuest* is the updated name for the former Rogers WiFi Hotspots \(previously known as *RogersOpen*\)\. It offers free guest WiFi access across many UBC locations, including the Point Grey, Robson Square, and Okanagan campuses, as well as select remote sites where UBC WiFi services are available\.

Coverage extends across both indoor and outdoor spaces where UBC wireless networks operate\.

The *RogersGuest* network will appear in your list of available WiFi networks alongside ["ubcsecure"](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30771), "[eduroam](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30767)", "[ubcvisitor"](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30773), and "[ubcprivate"](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30769)\.

When connecting, users will now be presented with a 1\-click guest access page, which provides **5 GB of data valid for 30 days**\. 

For technical assistance, please contact **Rogers Support** at **1\-855\-877\-WIFI \(9434\)** or [visit the Rogers website](https://www.rogers.com/internet/wifi/get-started) for more information\.

#### Wireless Device Registration System

The [Wireless Device Registration System](https://it.ubc.ca/node/189075) allows residential multimedia and gaming devices to be pre\-registered to connect to the ubcvisitor Wireless\.

For information about where wireless internet is available across UBC campuses and other locations, please refer to our [Wireless Coverage page](https://it.ubc.ca/node/2645)\.
