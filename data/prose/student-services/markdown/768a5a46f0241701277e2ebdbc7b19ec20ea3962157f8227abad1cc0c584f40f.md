## About Food Hub Market Volunteers[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/food\-hub\-market\-volunteers/\#about\-food\-hub\-market\-volunteers](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/food-hub-market-volunteers/#about-food-hub-market-volunteers)

The [Food Hub Market](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/food-hub-market/) is a student\-led, not\-for\-profit initiative committed to enhancing food security for the UBC student community\. 

Food Hub Market volunteers support our mission to provide affordable groceries while fostering a welcoming space that promotes food literacy, cultural vibrancy, and social connections at the UBC Vancouver campus\. There are 3 volunteer teams:

-   **Operations**  
    Support daily Food Hub Market operations, connect students with resources, and promote community well\-being\.
-   **Programming**  
    Support food skill\-building workshops and campus info booths\.
-   **Social Media**  
    Create content related to food, nutrition, and food security topics for the [Food Hub Market Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ubcfoodhubmarket/)\.

## Who can apply?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/food\-hub\-market\-volunteers/\#who\-can\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/food-hub-market-volunteers/#who-can-apply)

All UBC students who have interest in food, nutrition, and food security are welcome to apply\.

Each year, there are **over 25 positions** available across the 3 volunteer teams\. 

### If you require accommodations

If you require accommodations to apply and participate in this volunteer opportunity, please reach out via email to [community\.foodhub@ubc\.ca](mailto:community.foodhub@ubc.ca)\.

## Time commitment[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/food\-hub\-market\-volunteers/\#time\-commitment](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/food-hub-market-volunteers/#time-commitment)

Volunteers must commit to at least 1 full volunteer term\. You should be prepared to dedicate **2 to 3 hours per week** to your shifts during your volunteer term\. There will also be a volunteer appreciation event near the end of each term that will last approximately 1\.5 hours\. 

There are 3 volunteer terms throughout the academic year:

1.  **Winter Term 1:** September to last week of classes in December
2.  **Winter Term 2**: January to last week of classes in April
3.  **Summer Term 1**: May to last week of classes in June
