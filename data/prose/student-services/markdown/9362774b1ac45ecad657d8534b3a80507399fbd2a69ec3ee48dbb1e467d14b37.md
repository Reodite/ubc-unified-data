## About the award[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/3m\-national\-student\-fellowship/\#about\-the\-award](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/3m-national-student-fellowship/#about-the-award)

The [3M National Student Fellowship](https://www.stlhe.ca/awards/3m-national-student-fellowship/) honours up to 10 full\-time diploma and undergraduate students at Canadian post\-secondary institutions who have demonstrated outstanding leadership and innovation in their lives and at their college or university\. These students embrace a vision of quality education that enhances their academic experience and beyond\.  Awards are coordinated through the Society for Teaching and Learning in Higher Education \(STLHE\)\.

The award consists of:

-   An award of $2,000
-   Certificate and recognition at the annual Society for Teaching and Learning in Higher Education \(STLHE\) conference in June, to participate in a 3M National Student Fellows Conference Summit
-   Coverage of all costs for conference registration, travel, and accommodation to attend the annual STLHE conference
-   2\-year membership to the Society for Teaching and Learning in Higher Education
-   Letter from STLHE President and 3M Canada to their identified institutional leader and to the individual awarded
-   Recognition in STLHE and 3M Canada social media, and partnering media, such as Maclean’s and University Affairs

## Eligibility requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/3m\-national\-student\-fellowship/\#eligibility\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/3m-national-student-fellowship/#eligibility-requirements)

You must meet all of the following requirements before applying:

-   You’re enrolled full\-time in an undergraduate degree or college program at a post\-secondary Canadian institution, as defined by UBC\.
-   You’ve completed at least 1 full term of study at the time of nomination\.
-   You have not previously received a 3M student fellowship\.

In recognition of the systemic conditions of disadvantage in higher education and society, the Society for Teaching and Learning in Higher Education \(STLHE\) encourages nominations of members of groups that have been historically disadvantaged and marginalized, including First Nations, Métis, and Inuit peoples, Indigenous peoples of North America, racialized persons, persons with disabilities, and those who identify as women and/or 2SLGBTQI\.

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/3m\-national\-student\-fellowship/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/3m-national-student-fellowship/#how-to-apply)

To apply, you will need the following: 

-   Your completed application
-   A nomination letter and additional letters of support
-   A copy of your transcript
-   A statement from the UBC VP Students \(Vancouver students\) or
-   A statement from the Associate VP, Students, UBC Okanagan \(Okanagan students\)

Visit the [STLHE website](https://www.stlhe.ca/awards/3m-national-student-fellowship/) for more information and detailed instructions on applying for this award\.

Submit your completed application by email for review and to request your statement letter:

-   Okanagan students: [avps\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:avps.ok@ubc.ca)
-   Vancouver students: [vpsassist@mail\.ubc\.ca](mailto:vpsassist@mail.ubc.ca)
