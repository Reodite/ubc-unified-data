## Full\-time funding[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/loans\-summer\-sessions/\#full\-time\-funding](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/loans-summer-sessions/#full-time-funding)

Students enrolled in Summer Session courses at UBC are eligible for student loan funding if they meet the eligibility criteria for their degree type\. It is your responsibility to register in a loan\-eligible course load\.

If you currently receive loans, please contact your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to ensure registration changes will not impact your loan eligibility\.

### Undergraduate students

Unlike Winter Session courses, Summer Session courses start and end at a variety of times and can be as short as 3 weeks in length\. To be eligible for [BC full\-time loans](https://studentaidbc.ca/sabc-home-page) during the Summer Session, you must meet all of the following criteria:

-   Be registered in at least 9 credits in most programs \(audited, wait\-listed, and withdrawn courses do not count\)
-   Be registered in a study period that is at least 12 consecutive weeks in length
-   Have no breaks longer than 10 percent of the study period

Eligibility requirements can differ across provinces\. Please consult with an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) if you have questions\.

### Graduate students

Most graduate programs at UBC do not have a minimum course load requirement\.

In order to be eligible for student loans, UBC graduate students must meet both of the following requirements:

-   Pay full\-time fees in each term that they receive loan funding
-   Have an “In Progress” status in their program of study for each term that they receive loan funding

This does not apply to any program that requires credit registration\.

## Part\-time funding[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/loans\-summer\-sessions/\#part\-time\-funding](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/loans-summer-sessions/#part-time-funding)

If you are not taking courses that match the full\-time criteria, you may be eligible for a part\-time student loan\.

Learn more about part\-time funding for [BC students](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/part-time-funding-british-columbia/) or [out of province students](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/part-time-funding-out-province/)\. If you have any questions about the application, contact your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\.
