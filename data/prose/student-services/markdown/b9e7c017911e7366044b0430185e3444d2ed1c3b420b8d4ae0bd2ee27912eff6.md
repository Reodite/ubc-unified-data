## How having maintained status impacts your MSP coverage[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/health\-insurance/health\-insurance\-international/medical\-services\-plan\-msp\-international/extend\-bc\-medical\-services\-plan\-msp/\#how\-having\-maintained\-status\-impacts\-your\-msp\-coverage](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international/extend-bc-medical-services-plan-msp/#how-having-maintained-status-impacts-your-msp-coverage)

You will have “[maintained status](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/maintained-status/)” \(formerly called “implied status”\) if **all** of the following applies to you:

-   You applied for a new immigration document, such as a study or work permit, before your [study permit became invalid or expired](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/#maintaining-your-student-status); or your work permit/visitor status expired\.
-   You won’t receive a new immigration document before your old one expires\.
-   You remain in Canada beyond the expiry of your old document\.

Since your BC MSP will end on the expiry date of your current immigration document, you **must take action to get health insurance coverage before your MSP expires**\. Without valid health insurance, you will need to pay for any health services\. Check how to get [MSP temporary coverage](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international#how-having-maintained-status-impacts-your-msp-coverage)\.

**If you were approved for MSP temporary coverage**, apply to extend your MSP online with a copy of your new study or work permit\.

**If you were not approved for MSP temporary coverage**, depending on the length of the gap in MSP coverage, you will either be able to get an extension of your MSP, or you will need to reapply for MSP\. You must [contact MSP by phone](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/health-drug-coverage/msp/bc-residents-contact-us) to confirm your eligibility, as it will depend on your individual circumstances\.

If you will need to [reapply for MSP](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international/apply-bc-medical-services-plan-msp/) and complete the 3\-month MSP waiting period again, be sure to purchase temporary private health insurance to cover you during the 3\-month waiting period\. You can [contact GuardMe](https://sites.guard.me/ubc/contact), the provider of [iMED](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international/), to discuss your options for temporary health insurance, or you can find and purchase your own private insurance\.

If you can extend your MSP and you hold a study permit, you will continue to be charged the monthly health\-care coverage fee\.

## Tips for the process[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/health\-insurance/health\-insurance\-international/medical\-services\-plan\-msp\-international/extend\-bc\-medical\-services\-plan\-msp/\#tips\-for\-the\-process](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international/extend-bc-medical-services-plan-msp/#tips-for-the-process)

The easiest way to apply is to [submit an application online](http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/health-drug-coverage/msp/bc-residents/eligibility-and-enrolment/how-to-enrol)\.

If you decide to submit a [paper application](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/forms/203fil.pdf) by mail, consider using a secure mailing method such as Canada Post Xpresspost\. You should also include a cover letter along with other supporting documents if requesting [MSP temporary coverage](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international#how-having-maintained-status-impacts-your-msp-coverage)—check the [MSP Temporary Coverage Sample Letter](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/msp_temporary_coverage_sample_letter.pdf) \(pdf\)\.

### International students

Temporary BC residents \(such as international students and workers\) will typically be issued a [BC Services Card](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/government-id/bc-services-card) \(formerly known as “BC CareCard”\) without a photo\. Carry your card with you as well as a digital or paper copy of your valid study permit or work permit\.

You can also apply for a [BC driver’s license](https://www.icbc.com/driver-licensing/moving-bc/Pages/Default.aspx) and/or [BCID Card](https://www.icbc.com/driver-licensing/getting-licensed/Apply-for-a-bcid) \(which includes your photo\) by submitting a separate application to the [Insurance Corporation of BC](https://www.icbc.com/Pages/default.aspx) \(ICBC\)\.
