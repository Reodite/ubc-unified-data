## Get a new medical exam, if required[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/before\-you\-travel/\#get\-a\-new\-medical\-exam\-if\-required](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/before-you-travel/#get-a-new-medical-exam-if-required)

**

If you applied for a study permit or TRV outside Canada

**

[Immigration medical exam](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/medical-exams.html) \(IME\) results are valid for 12 months and your medical exam must be valid when you enter Canada\.

If you completed an immigration medical exam for your study permit application and you will enter Canada more than 12 months after you completed your medical exam, you must take another medical exam before travelling to Canada\. If you need to take a second medical exam, be sure to complete this well in advance of travelling to Canada and send a copy of your new E\-medical \(the document you receive from your panel physician with your photo\) to IRCC through a [webform](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/contact-ircc/web-form.html) as soon as you receive it\. Select “Add a document to your application” as the request category to submit your E\-medical\. When you travel to Canada bring both your previous and new E\-medical copies with you\.

If you get a request in your online IRCC account for a medical exam it is important to complete the medical exam or provide an explanation for why you cannot complete the medical exam by the deadline\. See our [medical exam FAQ](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-immigration-health-insurance-faq/#medical-exam) for details\.

If a medical exam was not required for your study permit application, you do not need to complete one unless your situation has changed since you applied and you now [require a medical exam](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/medical-exams/requirements-temporary-residents.html#needs)\. This could apply to you, if, for example, you lived in a [designated country](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/medical-exams/requirements-temporary-residents/country-requirements.html) for 6 months or longer consecutively in the year before you travel to Canada\. In this case, complete the medical exam with a panel physician well in advance of travelling, send a copy of your E\-medical to IRCC through a [webform](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/contact-ircc/web-form.html) as soon as you receive it\. Select “Add a document to your application” as the request category to submit your E\-medical\. Bring a copy of the E\-medical with you when you travel to Canada\.   

In addition, if you are a medical student, will work, or complete a Co\-op or student work placement in [jobs which require a medical exam](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/#if-you-work-with-children-or-seniors-in-healthcare-settings-or-in-agriculture), check that your study and/or work permit conditions allow you to work in such jobs\. You cannot work in jobs that require a medical exam until you have received a study or work permit with the appropriate conditions and are [eligible to work](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/)\.

**

If you are a continuing student with a valid study permit who has been outside Canada for more than 6 months

**

If you’ve been in a [designated country](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/medical-exams/requirements-temporary-residents/country-requirements.html) for 6 months or longer in the past 12 months, you will need to complete a medical exam with a [panel physician](https://secure.cic.gc.ca/pp-md/pp-list.aspx) before travelling to Canada\. You should take the medical exam well in advance before you plan to travel to Canada, send a copy of your new E\-medical to IRCC through a [webform](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/contact-ircc/web-form.html) as soon as you receive it\. Select “Other” as the request category to submit your E\-medical\. Bring a copy of the E\-medical with you when you travel to Canada\.

In addition, if you are a medical student, will work, or complete a Co\-op or student work placement in [jobs which require a medical exam](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/#if-you-work-with-children-or-seniors-in-healthcare-settings-or-in-agriculture), check that your study and/or work permit conditions allow you to work in jobs which require a medical exam\. If your study and/or work permit do not have conditions which allow you to work in jobs which require a medical exam, complete a medical exam and [apply to change conditions](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/extend-study-permit-within-canada/) on your study and/or work permit\. You cannot work in jobs that require a medical exam until you have received a study or work permit with the appropriate conditions and are [eligible to work](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/)\. If your study and/or work permit conditions are correct but you later lived in a designated country for 6 months or longer consecutively, please contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support\. 

## Confirm your health insurance coverage[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/before\-you\-travel/\#confirm\-your\-health\-insurance\-coverage](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/before-you-travel/#confirm-your-health-insurance-coverage)

### If you’re new to UBC

**

Students in programs longer than 6 months \(including degree\-seeking, two\-term exchange, and VIRS students studying more than 6 months\)

**

As a new international student who will be studying for more than 6 months, who has registered in one or more on\-campus courses, and is charged AMS/GSS student fees, you have temporary, private health insurance coverage for your first 3 months through [iMED](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international/)\.

iMED is designed to cover your 3\-month waiting period before you are eligible for the British Columbia \(BC\) government Medical Services Plan \(BC MSP\) – you must [apply for MSP](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international/apply-bc-medical-services-plan-msp/) as soon as you enter BC\. Learn more about [health insurance for international students](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/)\.

Your iMED coverage dates depend on when you start your program:

-   Winter Term 1 \(September to December\): coverage will start on August 10
-   Winter Term 2 \(January to April\): coverage will start on December 10
-   Summer Term 1 \(May to August\): your coverage will start on April 10

If you are a Go Global exchange student or a VIRS student, please connect directly with [Go Global](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/contact) if you need to change your coverage dates\. 

For all other students, [see what to do](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international/#coverage-dates) if you will arrive in Canada before your coverage dates, or in the calendar month after your iMED insurance starts\.

**

One\-term exchange and non\-degree\-seeking students studying less than 6 months

**

As a new international student who will be studying for less than 6 months, who is registered in one or more on\-campus courses, and who is charged AMS/GSS student fees, you have temporary health insurance coverage through iMED for the entire duration of your program of studies\.

**One\-term exchange students** have set iMED [coverage dates](https://sites.guard.me/ubc/department/go-global-_-virs) for 130 days \(approximately one academic term\) which cannot be changed\.

If you will arrive in Canada before your iMED coverage start date, contact [Go Global](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/contact) directly for support\.

If you plan to stay in Canada after your iMED coverage dates, contact [GuardMe](https://sites.guard.me/ubc/contact) or another private health insurance provider for options\. Ensure you always have sufficient health insurance during your stay in Canada\. You also need to ensure you have valid immigration status in Canada – contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support\.  

**VIRS students** studying for less than 6 months have iMED coverage dates based on their research period\. If you need to change your research period, learn about changing your iMED [coverage dates](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international#changing-your-imed-coverage-dates)\.

For other non\-degree seeking students such as unclassified, access studies and visiting students, please check the [iMED page](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international/) for details or [contact GuardMe](https://sites.guard.me/ubc/contact) to ask about your options\.

#### Getting your iMED card

Your welcome email from GuardMe will contain instructions for accessing your iMED card and account\. Check that the iMED coverage period on your iMED card is correct\.

If you have not received your welcome email by the start of the iMED coverage period, check your Workday student account to make sure you have been charged iMED fees\. If you do not see iMED fees in your student account, contact [International Student Advising](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0J8VRJgJQkuO2b4) for support\. Learn more about accessing your iMED card and account\.

Learn more about [accessing your iMED card and account](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international/#logging-into-your-guardme-account)\.

### Returning students

Before you travel, check whether you have valid [BC Medical Services Plan](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international/) \(MSP\) coverage\.

**

If you’ve been outside of Canada for six months or less

**

If you have been outside of Canada for **less than six months** and you did not cancel your MSP, ensure your MSP coverage has not expired\. You should be eligible to continue to be covered under MSP; however, you should [contact BC MSP](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/health-drug-coverage/msp/bc-residents-contact-us) to confirm that this is the case\.

**

If you’ve been outside of Canada for more than six months

**

If you have been outside of Canada for **more than six months** in a calendar year, [contact BC MSP](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/health-drug-coverage/msp/bc-residents-contact-us) to confirm your eligibility for MSP coverage\.

-   If you can still be covered under MSP, no further action is required until your MSP expires\.
-   If you are no longer covered under MSP, please check the instructions provided below\.

#### If you don’t have MSP

If you are not eligible to be covered under MSP, you will need to get private health insurance to cover you during the MSP 3\-month waiting period\. You can contact [GuardMe](https://sites.guard.me/ubc/contact), the provider of iMED, to discuss your options, or you may find and purchase your own private insurance\.

As soon as you re\-enter BC, [apply for the BC MSP online](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international/apply-bc-medical-services-plan-msp/)\.

## Complete the optional Advance Declaration[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/before\-you\-travel/\#complete\-the\-optional\-advance\-declaration](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/before-you-travel/#complete-the-optional-advance-declaration)

If you are flying to Canada and will first arrive in Vancouver, Toronto, Montreal, or other participating airports, you can use [Advance Declaration](https://www.canada.ca/en/border-services-agency/services/arrivecan.html) to submit your customs and immigration declaration in advance \(up to 72 hours before your scheduled arrival\) to save time at the airport\.
