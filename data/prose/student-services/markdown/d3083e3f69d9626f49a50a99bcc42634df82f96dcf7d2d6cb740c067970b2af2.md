Your deadlines and responsibilities as a returning student will vary depending on your return scenario and specific situation\. 

If you haven’t yet, follow the [online guide](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/readmission/) on returning to UBC and applying for readmission, and review the [common return scenarios](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/readmission/return-scenarios/) first\.

## First, connect with advising [https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/readmission/return\-checklist/\#first\-connect\-with\-advising](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/readmission/return-checklist/#first-connect-with-advising) 

If you haven’t already, meet with an [Academic Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) to discuss course selection and degree planning, including your remaining requirements for graduation\. If you cannot access Workday to view your academic record, you can access an [unofficial transcript](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-transcript/#unofficial-transcript) through the [UBC Official Documents tool](https://ubc.appiancloud.com/suite/sites/ubc-official-documents)\. You will be able to access [Workday](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/) again once you are readmitted to UBC\.

Then, contact an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to learn about admission requirements if you’re submitting a readmission application or returning as a mature student\. You can also receive support with creating a financial plan, course registration issues, or clearing enrolment holds or debts on your account\.

If you’re an international student, connect with [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) first to review your visa and study permit with an advisor to ensure that you are eligible to study in Canada\.

## Next, review additional steps for your situation[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/readmission/return\-checklist/\#next\-review\-additional\-steps\-for\-your\-situation](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/readmission/return-checklist/#next-review-additional-steps-for-your-situation)

Read the list below to learn what support is available for your situation and other steps you may need to follow to return to studies\. 

**

Indigenous students

**

Connect with the [Indigenous Student Support Team](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/financial-support-options/financial-support-indigenous/) if you would like help creating a financial plan and require guidance on planning for the costs associated with returning to studies at UBC\. [The First Nations House of Learning \(FNHL\)](https://fnhl.ubc.ca/students/) provides services and activities to support First Nations, Métis, and Inuit students’ academic success and positive social and cultural experiences\.

**

BC Former Youth in Care

**

Connect with [Joe Stevens](mailto:joseph.stevens@ubc.ca), a designated UBC Vancouver campus contact for [BC youth in and from care](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc-life/former-youth-in-care/), before you submit an admission application and prior to re\-enrolling\.

**

Students with enrolment holds or debts

**

All holds and/or debts must be resolved before you can register\. Connect with an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) for help\.

**

Students with government loans

**

If you had previous Canadian or US government student loans, you must notify your loan servicer if you are returning to part\-time or full\-time studies\. Speak to an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) for information about your eligibility for in\-school deferment or interest\-free status\.

**

Students who are sponsored

**

If a portion of your UBC tuition fees is paid by a sponsor, please ensure your sponsor submits a [Sponsorship Billing application](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_dg7icsLodwcOdeu) before the start of the term\. Learn more about your [responsibilities as a sponsored student](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsored-students/)\.

**

Students who are parents

**

There are several ways you can receive support as a student parent at UBC\. Learn more about [available resources](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/resources-who-are-parents/)\.

**

Students who plan to live on campus

**

If you are interested in living on campus, review the eligibility requirements and [apply for on\-campus housing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-to-apply/) as early as possible\. UBC Housing has a separate application process and deadlines\.

## Apply for readmission[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/readmission/return\-checklist/\#apply\-for\-readmission](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/readmission/return-checklist/#apply-for-readmission)

If you need to formally apply for readmission based on your return scenario, you can do so through the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) using your Campus Wide Login \(CWL\) and by paying a non\-refundable [application fee](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/application-and-administrative-fees)\.

Most programs require you to submit your readmission application by **January 15** for the upcoming Summer or Winter session, but deadlines may vary\. For specific dates, check the UBC Academic Calendar or contact [Enrolment Services](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\.

## Access other support and resources[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/readmission/return\-checklist/\#access\-other\-support\-and\-resources](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/readmission/return-checklist/#access-other-support-and-resources)

**[Get health support](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/)**

Taking care of your physical and mental health through self\-help resources or support from professional staff and peer communities is an important part of your return to studies at UBC\.

**[Contact an Accessibility Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/)**

The Centre for Accessibility can provide guidance and resources if you require support with medical or disability\-related accommodations\.

**[2SLGBTQIA\+ resources](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/2slgbtqia-resources/)**

Learn more about campus resources, involvement opportunities, and career guidance for Two Spirit, lesbian, gay, bisexual, trans, queer, intersex, and asexual \(2SLGBTQIA\+\) students\.

**[Learn about living off campus](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/other-housing/off-campus-housing/)**

If you don’t plan to stay in residence while attending UBC, access resources to help you find housing, avoid scams, and more\.
