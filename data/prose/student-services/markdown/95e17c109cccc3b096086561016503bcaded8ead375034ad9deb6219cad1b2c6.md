## Getting a loan[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/loans\-full\-time\-other\-provinces/\#getting\-a\-loan](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/loans-full-time-other-provinces/#getting-a-loan)

If you are from a Canadian province or territory other than BC, you can apply to receive government student loan assistance through your home province or territory\.

Your home province or territory is generally the province where you lived for 12 continuous months, excluding months of full\-time post\-secondary study before attending UBC\. The requirements may differ depending on whether you are a dependent or an independent student\.

To find out if your course load meets the full\-time requirements for your loan province, visit the [Course load guide](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/course-load-guide/)\.

## Automatic tuition deferral[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/loans\-full\-time\-other\-provinces/\#automatic\-tuition\-deferral](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/loans-full-time-other-provinces/#automatic-tuition-deferral)

Eligible graduate and undergraduate students will automatically have fees deferred for one month after the tuition fee deadline in September, January, or May\.

You are eligible for automatic tuition deferral if you meet both requirements: 

-   You are eligible for funding to pay your tuition and fees\.
-   You submit a complete full time loan application by the following dates:
    -   Jul 31 for a loan beginning in September \(Winter Term 1\)
    -   Nov 15 for a loan beginning in January \(Winter Term 2\)
    -   Apr 15 for a loan beginning in May \(Summer Session\)
