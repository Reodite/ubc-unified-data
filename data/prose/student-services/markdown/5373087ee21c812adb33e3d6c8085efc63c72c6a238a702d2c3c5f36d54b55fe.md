## Financial Wellness Workshops[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/financial\-wellness\-training\-education/\#financial\-wellness\-workshops](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/financial-wellness-training-education/#financial-wellness-workshops)

UBC student groups, staff, and faculty members are invited to book workshops organized by [Enrolment Services Advisors](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) \(ES Advisors\)\.

ES Advisors offer workshops to student groups by request\. They focus on financial planning, understanding financial and other resources at UBC, and student loan support\. ES Advisors are unable to provide tax or financial investment advice\.

Fill out the [workshop request form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_cH2hYUpno4pmguy) if you are interested in having an ES Advisor come to one of your meetings or events\.

## Learn at your own pace[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/financial\-wellness\-training\-education/\#learn\-at\-your\-own\-pace](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/financial-wellness-training-education/#learn-at-your-own-pace)

If you’re a UBC student, you can take self\-guided courses to learn about financial wellness\. Log into Canvas using your CWL to enrol\.

[Understanding Your Financial Picture](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/GGWED8)

Develop practical skills for managing your finances and explore financial wellness topics like budgeting\.

[Food Budgeting and Meal Planning](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/RA3A6C)

Learn how to budget for groceries realistically, plan meals that fit your schedule, and make the most of your food budget throughout the term\.

[Understanding Your Financial Credit Score](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/MKD89J)

Understand how credit works in Canada, what affects your credit score, and how to make informed decisions about credit cards and borrowing\.
