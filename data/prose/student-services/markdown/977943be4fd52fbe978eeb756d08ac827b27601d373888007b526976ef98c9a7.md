## About the fund [https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/funding\-project/william\-murrin\-fund/\#about\-the\-fund](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/funding-project/william-murrin-fund/#about-the-fund) 

The William G\. Murrin Fund was established through a legacy gift from the late Mr\. William G\. Murrin to support religious work at UBC\. 

The Fund provides up to $1,000 to campus\-based organizations that offer meaningful spiritual leadership and make valuable contributions to the religious lives of UBC students, staff, and faculty\. Funding is awarded in alignment with UBC policies and principles of equity and non\-discrimination\. 

## Eligibility requirements [https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/funding\-project/william\-murrin\-fund/\#eligibility\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/funding-project/william-murrin-fund/#eligibility-requirements) 

Any UBC organization or association, including campus religious or spiritual groups, can apply\.

Applications are encouraged for programs that will benefit a wide spectrum of the University community, particularly those led by individual religious clubs and groups\. 

Partnerships and proposals with matching funds are highly encouraged\. The committee will only accept one application per organization per application cycle\. 

## Proposal Review Process [https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/funding\-project/william\-murrin\-fund/\#proposal\-review\-process](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/funding-project/william-murrin-fund/#proposal-review-process) 

Proposals are reviewed by 4 Co\-Chairs of the President’s William G\. Murrin Fund Committee\.

### Selection criteria 

Proposals submitted to the William G\. Murrin Fund will be evaluated by the Committee based on the following criteria: 

-   Demonstrated contribution to the religious and spiritual lives of UBC students, staff, and faculty
-   The anticipated impact of the program or activity on the UBC community
-   Evidence of meaningful spiritual leadership, thoughtful program design, and clear objectives
-   Capacity of the organization to successfully deliver the proposal within the funding period
-   A clear, reasonable, and well‑justified budget that demonstrates responsible use of funds
-   Ability to provide a final report after the initiative has been completed

## Funding limits and requirements [https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/funding\-project/william\-murrin\-fund/\#funding\-limits\-and\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/funding-project/william-murrin-fund/#funding-limits-and-requirements) 

-   The maximum funding is $1,000 per project per year\. Funds must be used within a 12\-month period and cannot be carried forward without permission from the Committee\.
-   Funds that are unspent each year must be returned to the Office of the Vice\-President, Students\.
-   Organizations or groups that receive funding must provide a written report explaining their expenditure of funds received from the Murrin Committee and a summary of activities and their impact\. Future funding considerations will be contingent upon the timely receipt of this report\.
