To graduate, students must submit a formal application for graduation\. If you plan to graduate, you must apply, regardless of whether or not you intend to participate in the scheduled ceremonies\.

Before applying to graduate, make sure you’ll have completed all program requirements by the specified dates below\.

## If you’re graduating in November[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying\-graduate/\#if\-youre\-graduating\-in\-november](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying-graduate/#if-youre-graduating-in-november)

If you plan to graduate in Fall 2026, you must apply for graduation by **Aug 28, 2026**\. For important dates and deadlines, refer to the Fall graduation timeline on this page\.  
  
Your parchment will be printed with a conferral date of November 2026 and will be available at or after the Fall graduation ceremonies\. Your parchment refers to your diploma\. 

## Graduation timeline[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying\-graduate/\#graduation\-timeline](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying-graduate/#graduation-timeline)

There are 2 graduation periods, one in spring, and another in fall: 

-   **Spring graduation:** May 20 to 22, 2026 and May 25 to May 28, 2026
-   **Fall graduation:** November 25 to 27, 2026

You can find the graduation ceremony schedule on the [UBC Graduation website](https://graduation.ubc.ca/schedule/)\. Please review the important dates and information below to ensure you are eligible to graduate at your desired time\. 

You are required to apply for graduation, even if you don’t plan on attending the ceremony\.

[View detailed schedule](https://graduation.ubc.ca/schedule/)

## Eligibility to graduate[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying\-graduate/\#eligibility\-to\-graduate](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying-graduate/#eligibility-to-graduate)

-   If you are graduating in November, you must complete your program requirements by the end of October\.
-   If you’re a graduate student, there are [specific deadlines](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/deadlines) for you\.
-   If you are completing Term 1 courses in December, you are not eligible for November graduation and should wait to apply for May graduation\. If you have questions about your degree requirements, contact your [academic advising office](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/)\.
-   You will not receive a parchment or be able to order transcripts if you have an outstanding balance of fees\.

## How to apply to graduate[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying\-graduate/\#how\-to\-apply\-to\-graduate](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying-graduate/#how-to-apply-to-graduate)

### Before you apply

Make sure your financial account with UBC is settled\. You will not receive a parchment or be able to order transcripts with an outstanding balance of fees\. You will still be able to apply to graduate and attend the ceremony, but will not receive your parchment when you cross the stage\. If you have questions about your finances, contact your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\. If you don’t have an assigned advisor, they’re still available to help\.

If you are an international student, and you plan to invite family from abroad to attend your graduation, be sure to apply for the [necessary travel documents](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/inviting-friends-family-visit/) for your family in advance\.

If you are a student completing a double major, you will be assigned to the ceremony for the major that is listed first on your student record \(e\.g\., a double major in French and Economics will be assigned to French\)\. To check your student record, log into [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/) and select “Academic history” under *Academics*\. For any changes to your programs of study, or the major that is listed first, contact your faculty or departmental [advising office](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) before you apply to graduate\.

### Use of names on parchments and graduation ceremonies

While completing your application, check your legal name and your listed program of studies for accuracy\. Unless specified, the name on your official student record will be the name printed on your parchment\.

Find out what to do if you’d like to [change your name](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-parchment-diploma/) on your parchment\.

-   **May graduation:** You must submit your name change requests for your parchment by **Fri, Apr 17, 2026**\.
-   **November graduation:** You must submit your name change request for your parchment by **Fri, Oct 9, 2026**\.

### Applying for November 2026 graduation

You’ll need to complete two parts to your application: 

-   **Graduation application:** This is required to ensure you can attend your graduation ceremony and receive your parchment\. You’ll also find your ceremony details and procession number closer to the ceremony date\. Your procession number is your place in line and indicates the order of when you will cross the stage\.
-   **Program completion application**: You’ll need to review your academic record and enter your expected completion date\.

Follow [step\-by\-step instructions](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/degree-planning/applying-for-graduation-or-program-completion/) to submit your application for November 2026 graduation\.

### When you can expect an approval decision

Your faculty will assess and approve your graduation application status if you have met your program requirements\. You are welcome to check your application status on Workday\. Approval decisions will be updated by your faculty on Workday by mid\-May or mid\-November, and earned credentials will appear on Workday about a week before the ceremony\.

If you have concerns about your application status or whether you’ve met your degree requirements, please contact your [academic advising office](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/)\.

## Getting your parchment[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying\-graduate/\#getting\-your\-parchment](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying-graduate/#getting-your-parchment)

Parchments are not printed until just before the May and November ceremonies\. Parchments will only be available at the ceremony or by pick up or courier delivery in early June and December for all students\.

-   **If you’re attending the ceremony**  
    You can start planning using the Graduation checklist on the [Graduation at UBC website](http://graduation.ubc.ca/students/your-graduation-checklist/)\. You’ll receive your parchment on the day of your ceremony\.
-   **If you’re not attending the ceremony**  
    You can pick up your parchment or pay for your parchment to be couriered\.
-   **Changing to courier delivery**  
    To switch your selection on the grad application from pick\-up to courier, please edit your application by **Fri, Aug 28** or email [graduation@ubc\.ca](mailto:graduation@ubc.ca)\. Make sure to include your UBC student number\.

**

In\-person pick up

**

If you’re graduating in November 2026, you can pick up your parchment starting **Mon, Dec 7, 2026** from the Advising Centre in [Brock Hall](http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?locat1=112-1)\.

You may pick up your parchment or have it picked up by someone else on your behalf with a [third party pick up form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/third_party_document_authorization_form.pdf) \(pdf\)\.

Alternatively, if the person picking up your document is authorized on your Workday account, they must have your student number and authorization code in addition to their ID\.

Learn about [third\-party authorization](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/third-party-authorization/)\.

**

Courier delivery

**

Couriers cannot deliver to PO boxes\.

If you’ve selected the courier option on your graduation application, you’ll receive an email for when you can pay the courier fee\. You won’t be able to pay online until your degree has been conferred\.

Once graduation ceremonies end, parchments will be packaged and sent out via courier\.

## Changing your ceremony attendance[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying\-graduate/\#changing\-your\-ceremony\-attendance](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/graduation/applying-graduate/#changing-your-ceremony-attendance)

You can change your selection for the Fall graduation in your Graduation Application Tool until **Fri, Aug 28, 2026**\.

If you miss the deadline and would like to change your selection to attending, please email [graduation@ubc\.ca](mailto:graduation@ubc.ca)\. Please include your full name and UBC student number\.

### Important information about late requests to attend the ceremony

The Ceremonies Office cannot guarantee ticket availability\.

Your parchment may not be available at the ceremony, but will be available for pickup or courier in either December \(if you are graduating in November\) or June \(if you are graduating in May\)\.

### If you can’t make the ceremony

If you indicated that you were attending the ceremony on the application but can no longer make it, you can pick up your parchment at the Enrolment Advising Centre at [Brock Hall](http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?locat1=112-1) in early December for the Fall ceremony or early June for the Spring ceremony\. If you prefer courier delivery, email [graduation@ubc\.ca](mailto:graduation@ubc.ca)\.
