If you took courses outside of UBC or you are transferring to UBC from another institution, you may be able to request transfer credits for your previous coursework, depending on your scenario\.

You’ll need to submit your transcript to UBC to process any transfer credit\.

## Students applying to UBC[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer\-credits/requesting\-transfer\-credit/\#students\-applying\-to\-ubc](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer-credits/requesting-transfer-credit/#students-applying-to-ubc)

If you’re planning on [applying to UBC](https://you.ubc.ca/), please make sure you’ve updated your post\-secondary attendance record within the Academic History section of your [Education Planner BC](https://www.educationplannerbc.ca/) profile\.

UBC Admissions will review your attendance background and will ask you to submit your transcripts during the review process\. If you are admitted, you will be assigned any eligible [transfer credits in Workday](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/degree-planning/viewing-your-transfer-credit-or-high-school-and-ap-ib-credit/) after you accept your admission offer\. In many cases, transfer credit can be viewed on the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) before accepting an offer\.

## Current students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer\-credits/requesting\-transfer\-credit/\#current\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer-credits/requesting-transfer-credit/#current-students)

If you are a student in the Faculty of Arts or Faculty of Science and would like to request a re\-evaluation of any of your General Credits, please complete the [Transfer Credit Evaluation Request](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_bBGurnbl1TTkH54) form\.

For students in faculties other than Arts or Science, please contact an [Academic Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) in your faculty to request a re\-evaluation\.

Not all requests for specific credits are granted\. Course re\-evaluations may take 4 to 8 weeks or longer\. Plan your course registration accordingly\. Contact your faculty’s advising office prior to registering to learn which courses you need to stay on track for your degree\.

### Opportunities to study elsewhere

If you’re a current UBC student and would like to take a course at another institution, you can apply to the [Go Global program](https://global.ubc.ca/go-global) for exchange opportunities or [take courses at UBC Okanagan](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/cross-campus-registration/)\.

You will need a [Letter of Permission](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/letter-permission/) from your faculty to study at another institution\.

## Former UBC Okanagan Students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer\-credits/requesting\-transfer\-credit/\#former\-ubc\-okanagan\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer-credits/requesting-transfer-credit/#former-ubc-okanagan-students)

If you’re currently a UBC Vancouver student who was admitted from UBC Okanagan, your UBC Okanagan courses will remain as part of your UBC records\.

You can find out how your UBC Okanagan course translates to a UBC Vancouver course with the [https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/equivalency\-search](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/equivalency-search)[BC Transfer Guide](https://www.bctransferguide.ca/)\.

If you were granted UBC Okanagan transfer credits based on your previous studies at other post\-secondary institutions or through university\-level course work in high school \(IB, AP, or A\-levels\), the transfer credits will be re\-evaluated for UBC Vancouver and may result in changes\.

## Non\-Degree students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer\-credits/requesting\-transfer\-credit/\#non\-degree\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer-credits/requesting-transfer-credit/#non-degree-students)

Non\-Degree students, such as those in [visiting students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-visiting/), [unclassified students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/), or those in [Access Studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-access-studies/) do not receive transfer credit\.

## Second\-degree students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer\-credits/requesting\-transfer\-credit/\#second\-degree\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer-credits/requesting-transfer-credit/#second-degree-students)

Students pursuing a second degree at UBC do not receive transfer credits from their previous degree\. Please contact an [Academic Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) in your faculty to find out about second degree requirements and how to plan for registration\. 

## Summer courses[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer\-credits/requesting\-transfer\-credit/\#summer\-courses](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer-credits/requesting-transfer-credit/#summer-courses)

You may be eligible for transfer credit if you completed courses at a different university in the summer after you accepted your UBC admission offer\. Those credits will be evaluated in September or October once UBC receives your summer transcript with final grades\.

## Submitting your transcript for transfer credits[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer\-credits/requesting\-transfer\-credit/\#submitting\-your\-transcript\-for\-transfer\-credits](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer-credits/requesting-transfer-credit/#submitting-your-transcript-for-transfer-credits)

If you’ ve applied to UBC, please follow email instructions and timelines from the Undergraduate Admissions office to submit the transcripts required for your application\.

If you’ve been admitted, have accepted your offer of admission, and submitted all your transcript\(s\) required for admission, please follow instructions on the [document submission page](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/applied/submit-documents/) and arrange for your final official transcript to be sent to UBC as soon as possible\.
