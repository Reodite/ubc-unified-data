UBC is committed to providing a safe, supportive, and inclusive living and learning environment with resources and services to support all students\.

The University does not tolerate racism or bias and strives to create an inclusive campus community through resources, services, conversations with critical groups, and funding for program initiatives\.

## About the UBC Vancouver campus[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/equity\-diversity\-inclusion\-resources/\#about\-the\-ubc\-vancouver\-campus](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/#about-the-ubc-vancouver-campus)

The University of British Columbia is located on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm Musqueam people\. We are grateful to live, work, and study on a land full of rich history and community\. 

Learn about what it means to be on this land in the [introduction to Musqueam and UBC](https://indigenous.ubc.ca/indigenous-engagement/musqueam-and-ubc/)\.

## Browse UBC resources[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/equity\-diversity\-inclusion\-resources/\#browse\-ubc\-resources](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/#browse-ubc-resources)

[Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/)

Connect with an advisor if your disability or ongoing medical condition is impacting your learning and participation in UBC activities\.

[Gender Diversity Hub](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/gender-diversity/)

Get information on inclusive washrooms on campus, changing your gender marker, gender\-affirming healthcare, and more\.

[https://antiracism\.ubc\.ca/resources/](https://antiracism.ubc.ca/resources/)[Anti\-racism resources and support](https://antiracism.ubc.ca/resources/)

Find toolkits, funding, courses, and support to learn about and help advance anti\-racism efforts\.

[https://indigenous\.ubc\.ca/students/](https://indigenous.ubc.ca/students/)[UBC Indigenous Student Portal](https://fnhl.ubc.ca/students/student-resources/)

Access resources specific to Indigenous students, including mentorship, jobs, and tutoring\.

[UBC Women’s Centre](https://womenscentre.ams.ubc.ca/about/)

The Centre offers a safe space and access to snacks, feminist literature, safer sex products, pregnancy tests, and free menstrual products\.

[Students who are parents](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/resources-who-are-parents/)

Caring for a child during your studies can be challenging\. Find childcare options, food assistance programs, and more\.

[Scholarships and bursaries](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/)

Browse awards available to specific student groups, offered by UBC to meet financial needs and recognize academic or extracurricular achievements\.

[Strategic Equity &amp; Anti\-Racism Enhancement Fund](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/funding-opportunities/strategic-equity-anti-racism-enhancement-fund-student-stream/)

Review funding for projects and initiatives that advance racial, disability, gender, and 2SLGBTQIA\+ equity\.

[2SLGBTQIA\+ resources](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/2slgbtqia-resources/)

UBC offers inclusive resources, support, and community for students exploring or affirming their 2SLGBTQIA\+ identities\.

[Black Student Space](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/black-student-space/)

The Black Student Space is a dedicated place for Black students to study, connect, recharge, and build community\.
