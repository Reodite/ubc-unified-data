## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/environmental\-sciences/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/environmental-sciences/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Environmental Sciences](https://www.eoas.ubc.ca/academics/ugrad/specializations-programs/environmentalsciences), you’re learning to use interdisciplinary skills to analyze issues impacting the earth\. You’ll develop important scientific skills and perspectives to consider how people can live in a more sustainable way\.

These skills may include:

-   Gain a systematic understanding of environmental issues
-   Field sampling
-   Data analysis and modeling
-   Analysis of contaminants and pollutants
-   Observation and interpretation of data experimental design
-   Environmental impact assessments and audits
-   Technical report writing
-   Technical knowledge using geographic information systems \(GIS\), statistics, and computer programming

## Make the most of your specialization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/environmental\-sciences/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-specialization](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/environmental-sciences/#make-the-most-of-your-specialization)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[Earth, Ocean, and Atmospheric Science clubs](https://www.eoas.ubc.ca/undergrads/student-life-events)**  
    Meet other UBC students with similar interests through events and programs\.
-   **[UBC Sustainability programs and clubs](http://www.sustain.ubc.ca/get-involved)**  
    Browse on\-campus paid and volunteer opportunities related to sustainability\.
-   **[UBC Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/ensc)**  
    Gain work experience in environmental sciences between study terms\.
-   **[Register as a Professional Geoscientist or Biologist](https://www.egbc.ca/how-to-apply/register-as-a-geoscientist/professional-geoscientist/apply-to-be-a-professional-geoscientist-in-bc)**  
    Understand the courses you’ll need to become certified\.
-   **[Departmental research opportunities](https://www.eoas.ubc.ca/research/areas)**  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   **[UBC Centre for Sustainable Food Systems](http://ubcfarm.ubc.ca/contact-us/upcoming-events/)**  
    Check out educational and community\-building events at the UBC Farm\.
-   **[Pacific Institute for Climate Solutions internships](http://www.sustain.ubc.ca/research/featured-research-groups/pacific-institute-climate-solutions)**  
    Apply your classroom knowledge to real\-world settings through climate change mitigation or adaptation projects\.
