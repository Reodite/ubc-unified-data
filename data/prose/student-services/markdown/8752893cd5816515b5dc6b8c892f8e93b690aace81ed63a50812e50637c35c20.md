## About the lab[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/community\-experiences/social\-impact\-lab/\#about\-the\-lab](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/social-impact-lab/#about-the-lab)

The Social Impact Lab \(SIL\) brings students and community partners together to tackle complex social and environmental challenges\. Through collaboration, critical analysis, and hands\-on projects, students work alongside community experts to explore root causes, generate innovative ideas, and support community\-led solutions\.

Social Impact Lab challenges students to think beyond traditional approaches, question existing systems, and take meaningful action on issues affecting communities across British Columbia\. By connecting classroom learning with real\-world experience, students contribute directly to projects that address community\-identified priorities\.

By participating in Social Impact Labs, you will:

-   Build meaningful connections with community organizations, community members, and students from diverse disciplines\.
-   Develop systems\-thinking skills to better understand complex social and environmental issues\.
-   Create innovative solutions to real\-world challenges while strengthening your creativity and problem\-solving abilities\.
-   Gain hands\-on experience on how to make strategies for local impact actionable in local contexts\.
-   Learn from diverse perspectives and discover how collective action can drive social change\.

## Past opportunities[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/community\-experiences/social\-impact\-lab/\#past\-opportunities](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/social-impact-lab/#past-opportunities)

**

Reframing Marpole

**

Working with Marpole Neighbourhood House, students, residents, and community organizations came together to identify strengths, challenges, and opportunities in an under\-resourced Vancouver neighbourhood\. Through collaborative workshops, participants generated community\-driven recommendations for the City of Vancouver’s Marpole Neighbourhood plan to ensure greater equity, access, and community self\-determination\.

**

Cool ‘Hood Champs: Community Climate Action

**

Students joined community members, local organizations, and cross\-campus partners  to explore the impacts of climate change in neighbourhoods across Metro Vancouver and identify practical solutions\. Through community climate walks and collaborative workshops, participants developed strategies to expand green spaces, increase tree planting, and advocate for sustainable home energy solutions within their communities\.

**

Collaboratory: Vancouver Plan

**

The City of Vancouver sought new ways to engage young adults in shaping the Vancouver Plan 2050\. Working alongside city staff and non\-profits, UBC students designed innovative engagement strategies to better reach youth in the Vancouver Plan consultations and helped launch the City’s first virtual public consultation during the COVID\-19 pandemic\.

**

Disability Justice and Civic Engagement

**

Ahead of the 2020 federal election, students partnered with disability organizations to explore barriers that prevent people with disabilities from voting\. Together, they developed practical solutions including accessible voting resources, an Accessible Democracy podcast, and transportation support to help disabled voters reach polling stations\.
