## Travelling as an international student[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/check\-eligible/\#travelling\-as\-an\-international\-student](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/check-eligible/#travelling-as-an-international-student)

Before making travel plans, check that you have the right documents and understand when to travel\.

COVID\-19 emergency border measures ended on October 1, 2022\.

Travel restrictions may be announced without notice\. Be sure to check the [latest updates](https://travel.gc.ca/travel-covid)\.

## Immigration documents[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/check\-eligible/\#immigration\-documents](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/check-eligible/#immigration-documents)

To travel to Canada for studies, you must have two types of documents:

1.  Valid documents to study in Canada
2.  Valid documents to travel to Canada

### Have valid documents to study in Canada

First, you must fall into one of the following categories:

-   You have an original, [valid study permit](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/study-permits#maintaining-your-student-status)\.
-   You have a valid [Letter of Introduction](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/apply-initial-study-permit/#get-your-study-permit-approval) \(study permit approval letter\)\.
-   You will study in a [short\-term program](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/study-permits#about-study-permits) for less than 6 months as a visitor, and can study without a study permit\.
-   You are a US citizen or lawful US permanent resident, or a resident of Greenland or St\. Pierre and Miquelon, and will [apply for a study permit on arrival](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/study-permits#important-considerations) in Canada\.

If you will study in Canada for more than 6 months, **do not travel to Canada** until you have received your Letter of Introduction, even if you have an eTA or TRV\.

### Have valid documents to travel to Canada

Second, you must fall into one of the following categories:

-   You have a valid [Temporary Resident Visa \(TRV\)](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/temporary-resident-visa-trv/) \(a sticker in your passport\)
-   You have a valid [Electronic Travel Authorization \(eTA\)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta.html)  
-   You are a US citizen or [lawful US permanent resident](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/entry-requirements-country.html#lawful-pr-us) who holds valid status in the US \(you do not require a TRV or an eTA\)\.

Find [entry requirements](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/entry-requirements-country.html) by country or territory\.

### About your Temporary Resident Visa \(TRV\)

If you travel from Canada to the US and back without travelling to a third country, you could [re\-enter Canada without a TRV](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/travelling-outside-canada#visiting-the-united-states) if you have a valid study or work permit, or are returning before your initial visitor status \(e\.g\., usually 6 months starting on the day you first enter Canada\) ends\. You may print out section 190\(3\)\(f\) of [Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227/page-25.html#docCont:~:text=%28f%29%C2%A0to%20re%2Denter%20Canada%20following%20a%20visit%20solely%20to%20the%20United%20States%20or%20St.%20Pierre%20and%20Miquelon%2C%20if%20they) to carry with you in case the border officer has questions about your re\-entry to Canada\.

### About your Electronic Travel Authorization \(eTA\)

If you are from an [eTA expansion country](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta/eligibility/eta-x.html), your eTA can be used to board flights to Canada, however you would need to have a TRV to enter Canada by land \(car, bus or train\) or sea \(boat or cruise ship\)\. For example, if you travel from your home country to the US, then drive from the US to Canada, a TRV would be required to enter Canada by car\. If you travel from Canada to the US and back without travelling to a third country, you could re\-enter Canada without a TRV as per above\. For students from Mexico, please see the [Feb 29, 2024, policy changes](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/eta-work-visa-mexico.html)\.

## When you can travel[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/check\-eligible/\#when\-you\-can\-travel](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/check-eligible/#when-you-can-travel)

There is no specific earliest date you can travel\. However, an officer may have concerns if you are travelling several months before you begin your studies\. Arriving a month before you begin or resume studies should be considered reasonable\. You cannot work in Canada until you have begun full\-time studies and [meet the requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/)\. 

If you must travel earlier, prepare a detailed explanation and supporting documents, if possible, in case the airline or border officer asks\. The Canada Border Services Agency \(CBSA\) officer you meet at the airport/border crossing will make the final decision on whether or not to admit you to Canada\.

If you took a [gap in studies](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/taking-time-away-school-international/) and are not currently enrolled, you should avoid travelling to Canada more than a month before you resume studies\. Contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support\.

Do not travel or purchase non\-refundable flight tickets or make non\-refundable accommodation deposits until you have received the right documents to study and travel to Canada \([see above](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/check-eligible/#:~:text=Have%20valid%20documents%20to%20travel%20to%20Canada)\)\.

### Booking your flight\(s\)

Be aware of local rules if you are transiting airports in other countries en route to Canada\. Check the transit country’s immigration department website and see the [International Air Transport Association \(IATA\) page](https://www.iatatravelcentre.com/) for guidance on international visa and passport requirements\.

You will need to get your study permit at the **first** airport you arrive to in Canada\. For example, if you arrive in Toronto and then take another flight to Vancouver, you must get your study permit in Toronto\. Consider booking flights which have a long layover to allow time for immigration\.

#### Getting to UBC or your final destination

UBC does not offer airport pick\-up services\. For transportation options from YVR airport, check [YVR’s transportation information](https://www.yvr.ca/en/passengers/transportation)\. You can also plan your trip via public transit by using [Translink’s trip planner](https://www.translink.ca/trip-planner) or [Google maps](https://maps.google.com/)\. 

To get to UBC by public transit, consider taking the Canada Line train from the airport to the Oakridge\-41st Avenue station then take the R4 express bus to UBC, or get off at the Broadway\-City Hall station, then take the 99 B\-line express bus to UBC\.

### Driving across the border

The largest border crossing near UBC is the [Douglas \(Peace Arch\) border crossing](https://do-rb.cbsa-asfc.cloud-nuage.canada.ca/?lang=en_CA&amp;id=405), which is open 24 hours\. See the list of [border crossings and hours](https://do-rb.cbsa-asfc.cloud-nuage.canada.ca/?lang=en_CA)\.

Also, see considerations when [temporarily importing a vehicle](https://tc.canada.ca/en/road-transportation/importing-vehicle/temporarily-importing-vehicles)\.

## Get advice on whether you should travel[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/check\-eligible/\#get\-advice\-on\-whether\-you\-should\-travel](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/check-eligible/#get-advice-on-whether-you-should-travel)

If you have unusual circumstances, to receive advice on documents for travel or timing of travelling, you may email or call the [Canada Border Services Agency](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/contact/bis-sif-eng.html) \(CBSA\)\. If you are calling, please ask for and take note of the agent’s badge number, the date and time you speak with the agent, and record notes of your conversation in case you need to refer to this in the future\. Please note the CBSA officer you meet with when you arrive in Canada will make the final decision on whether you can enter Canada\.

You may also consider contacting your airline to confirm what documentation they will request from you when boarding your flight\.
