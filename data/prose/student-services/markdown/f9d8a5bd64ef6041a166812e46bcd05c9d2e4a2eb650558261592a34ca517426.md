The AMS Prayer Space is located on the lower level of the [AMS Student Nest](https://maps.ubc.ca/?show=y,n,y,y,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=n&amp;locat1=795&amp;locat2=) in Room 2303\.

The prayer room is open by drop\-in to all students of any faith for prayer, quiet contemplation, and reflection\.

## Before using the room[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/ams\-prayer\-space/\#before\-using\-the\-room](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/ams-prayer-space/#before-using-the-room)

Please review the [guidelines for use](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/#prayer-room-rules-and-responsibilities) and follow all prayer room rules and responsibilities\.

If you’re hosting an event that serves the faith\-based or spiritual student community at UBC, please book the [Brock Commons South Community Space](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/brock-commons-south-community-space/)\.

## Prayer room hours[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/ams\-prayer\-space/\#prayer\-room\-hours](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/ams-prayer-space/#prayer-room-hours)

The space is open for drop\-in prayer, quiet contemplation, and reflection during the AMS Student Nest [opening hours](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/your-building/)\.

## Room capacity[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/ams\-prayer\-space/\#room\-capacity](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/ams-prayer-space/#room-capacity)

The room has a capacity of 6 people\.

## Reporting a problem about the facilities[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/ams\-prayer\-space/\#reporting\-a\-problem\-about\-the\-facilities](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/ams-prayer-space/#reporting-a-problem-about-the-facilities)

If you encounter any issues with the prayer room or its use, please report it as soon as possible to the Office of the Vice\-President, Students at [vpsassist@mail\.ubc\.ca](mailto:vpsassist@mail.ubc.ca) or the Office of the AMS Vice\-President, Administration at [avpadmin@ams\.ubc\.ca](mailto:avpadmin@ams.ubc.ca)\.

Concerns may include cleanliness, damage, lighting, misuse of these facilities, or complaints about other users\.

### Urgent concerns

In case of fire or emergency evacuation, follow the procedures posted in the rooms\. In the event of an incident or concern that requires attention, contact [UBC Security](https://security.ubc.ca/) by calling [604\.822\.2222](tel:6048222222)\. For emergencies that require an immediate response, call 911\.
