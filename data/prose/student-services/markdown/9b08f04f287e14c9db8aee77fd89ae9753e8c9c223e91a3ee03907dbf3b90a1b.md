## About the role[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/vantage\-peer\-mentors/\#about\-the\-role](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/vantage-peer-mentors/#about-the-role)

The Vantage Peer Mentors are a group of Vantage College alumni who are passionate about fostering social connections and providing peer support to Vantage One students\. Their responsibilities include event\-planning, assisting international students with first\-year transition, leading communications campaigns, and working closely with Vantage staff and faculty members on student engagement initiatives\. The Vantage Peer Mentors also share their UBC experience and connect students with resources and support to help them navigate campus life\.

## Who can apply?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/vantage\-peer\-mentors/\#who\-can\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/vantage-peer-mentors/#who-can-apply)

You must be a UBC undergraduate student who has completed the Vantage One Program, or you are scheduled for completion of the program in July 2026\.

There are **10 positions** **available**\. View the [Vantage Peer Mentor Program](https://vantagecollege.ubc.ca/vantage-peer-mentor-program) for more information\.

### If you require accommodations

The Peer Programs Program Advisors would like to recognize that being able to participate in student leadership opportunities is a privilege\. The weekly commitment for each Peer Program varies in length but may not be possible for some students who need accommodations based on chronic health conditions, disabilities, mental health, and/or other lived experiences\.

If you have specific accommodations for your ability to participate and contribute to the program, please connect with the [Program Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/vantage-peer-mentors/) to discuss what accommodations can be made for your particular situation\.
