## About the group[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/art\-therapy\-for\-anxiety\-group/\#about\-the\-group](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/art-therapy-for-anxiety-group/#about-the-group)

A 6\-session therapeutic group that blends Cognitive Behavioral Therapy \(CBT\) with Art Therapy to help students explore and manage anxiety through creative expression\.

The primary areas of focus are:

-   Foster insight, emotional regulation, and resilience\.
-   Use visual art to explore and shift anxious thought patterns\.
-   Provide a supportive space for healing\.

## Eligibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/art\-therapy\-for\-anxiety\-group/\#eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/art-therapy-for-anxiety-group/#eligibility)

You do not need artistic experience\. Session facilitators will provide all the supplies you need to create\.

The group sessions are held in person on the UBC Vancouver campus\. UBC Counselling Services recognizes the unique needs of each student and will provide alternate options if group programming is not an appropriate referral option or fit\.
