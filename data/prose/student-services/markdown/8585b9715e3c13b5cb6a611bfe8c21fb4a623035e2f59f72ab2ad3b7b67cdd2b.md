If you’re a student with children, finding activities that work for both school and family life can make a big difference\. 

Browse community centres and family\-friendly activities in the UBC neighbourhood and beyond\.

[University Neighbourhoods Association Community Centres](https://www.myuna.ca/)

Drop into family\-friendly facilities and recreational programs\.

[UBC Inspired Community Programming for Families](https://planning.ubc.ca/community/ubc-inspired)

Connect with the UBC community through programs and fun events for all ages\.

[Culture Club at the Museum of Anthropology](https://moa.ubc.ca/events/)

Participate in monthly art\-based and hands\-on learning events\.

[Kits House](http://www.kitshouse.org/)

Access services like childcare, parent groups, or programs like English language conversation circles\.

[West Point Grey Community Centre](http://www.westpointgrey.org/)

Visit the community centre for recreation, sports, and arts programming for all ages\.

[YWCA Single Moms Group](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=RAoxFornOUuzJrEeMDkS79nJluSao1xAjPIsyQJTIC1UM0dUU0NJQ0VPQlVPTTlBMU1JNlBSUjJWOS4u)

Join facilitated sessions each month to connect with other mothers in the community\.
