## About the scholarship[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/mccall\-macbain\-scholarship/\#about\-the\-scholarship](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/mccall-macbain-scholarship/#about-the-scholarship)

The McCall MacBain Scholarship is Canada’s first comprehensive, leadership\-driven scholarship to support graduate students enrolled in a master’s program at McGill University\. Current undergraduate students are invited to apply\.

The scholarship will cover your tuition and fees for a [full\-time master’s program](https://www.mcgill.ca/gradapplicants/programs) or a second\-entry professional undergraduate program such as dentistry, law, or medicine at McGill University\. You will also receive a living stipend of $2,300 per month during academic terms\.

## Eligibility requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/mccall\-macbain\-scholarship/\#eligibility\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/mccall-macbain-scholarship/#eligibility-requirements)

You must meet all the following requirements to apply:

-   You are in good academic standing and have good overall conduct\.
-   You are enrolled in an undergraduate degree or are on track to complete your undergraduate degree by August 2027\.
-   After applying for the scholarship, you must apply for full\-time enrolment in an eligible master’s or professional program at McGill for Summer or Fall 2027\.

## Before you apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/mccall\-macbain\-scholarship/\#before\-you\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/mccall-macbain-scholarship/#before-you-apply)

You can find detailed instructions on the application form\. Before you start, make sure you complete the following:

1.  Attend an online information session to learn more about the scholarship\.
2.  Prepare the following items for your online application:
    -   Two reference letters
    -   Official transcript
    -   Personal statement
    -   Short essays reflecting on your community and goals, a significant leadership experience, a significant volunteer contribution, and a “course correction”
    -   A list of your community and work experiences, as well as your extracurricular activities

After the application period, successful candidates will be invited to a regional interview, followed by a final interview in Montréal\. Your travel for the final interviews is funded\.

### Current students and alumni

If you are a current student at UBC or you graduated in 2021 or later, you must earn the endorsement of your institution\.

At minimum, a university representative will review your application once it is submitted through the [McCall MacBain Scholarship website](https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/) and verify your academic standing and overall conduct\.
