## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/engineering/chemical\-engineering/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/engineering/chemical-engineering/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Chemical Engineering](http://www.chbe.ubc.ca/academics/undergraduate/programs/), you’re learning to convert raw materials into value\-added products with consideration to safety, cost, and sustainability\. You’re building important skills to develop and implement energy\-efficient and eco\-friendly industrial processes\.

These skills may include:

-   Applying principles related to thermodynamics, transport phenomena, reactor design, and unit operations to manufacture materials and renewable energy
-   Designing, developing, and evaluating processes, techniques, and equipment to separate complex mixtures
-   Formulation of product specifications, test protocols, and measurement capabilities
-   Developing a continuous improvement mindset to the creation of new chemical processes, reactions, and materials for cleaner production
-   Developing quality control, safety protocols and environmental prevention, and control technologies to reduce solid, liquid, and gaseous wastes
-   Conducting economic and technical feasibility studies for resource\-based processing industries
-   Using process simulation software, advanced instruments, machines, and lab equipment across chemistry, biology, and medicine

## Make the most of your program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/engineering/chemical\-engineering/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/engineering/chemical-engineering/#make-the-most-of-your-program)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

    Apply your knowledge and work on a real\-world project from energy research to automated beer brewing\.
-   [UBC Chem\-E\-Car](https://www.ubcenvision.com/chemecar/)  
    Collaborate with peers to design and build a car powered only by chemical reactions, and compete in the annual competition\.
-   [CHBE Distinguished Speaker Seminar Series](https://www.chbe.ubc.ca/news-events/speaker-series/)  
    Learn about innovation and advancement in the field from various scholars from around the globe\.
-   [Canadian Institute of Mining, Metallurgy and Petroleum, UBC Student Chapter](https://www.cim.org/)  
    Network with industry professionals and attend guest lectures to learn about the mining field and potential career paths\.
-   [GreenChem UBC](https://green.chem.ubc.ca/)  
    Meet like\-minded individuals who are interested in sustainable chemical research\. 
-   [UBC Oil and Gas Initiative](https://www.facebook.com/theoilandgasinitiative/)  
    Attend panel discussions and networking events pertaining to the energy industry\.
-   [Common Energy UBC](https://commonenergyubc.com/)  
    Join one of 6 action teams to implement sustainability initiatives and practices into all aspects of the UBC community\.
-   [Departmental research opportunities](http://www.chbe.ubc.ca/research/)  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   [UBC Engineering Co\-op](https://coop.apsc.ubc.ca/employers/disciplines/chemical-biological-engineering)  
    Gain work experience in chemical engineering between study terms\.
-   [Innovation OnBoard](https://www.innovationboard.ca/)

-   [American Institute of Chemical Engineers Pacific Northwest Student Regional Conference](https://www.aiche.org/conferences-events)

-   [Canadian Chemical Engineering Conference and Exhibition](https://www.cheminst.ca/conference/ccec2021/)
