## Masters practicum program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/counselling\-practicum\-residency\-program/\#masters\-practicum\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/counselling-practicum-residency-program/#masters-practicum-program)

UBC Student Health and Wellbeing offers practicum placements to students in graduate counsellor training programs\. There are 4 new practicum positions for the upcoming training year\. The practicum is a high\-quality, personalized training experience in an open, collegial atmosphere that promotes self\-exploration and growth\.

Read the [practicum brochure](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/counselling_practicum_brochure.pdf) \(pdf\) for more information\.

### Eligibility requirements

Practicum applicants must meet all of the following requirements:

-   You must be enrolled in a master’s counsellor training program\.
-   You must have completed your counselling theory and counselling skills training courses\. Previous clinic or practicum experience is preferred\.
-   You must have approval from your program supervisor to apply\.

### How to apply

Prepare and submit all required application materials\. Review the [practicum brochure](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/counselling_practicum_brochure.pdf) \(pdf\) for the list of application materials required and detailed application instructions\.

You can submit your application materials by email to the Practicum Coordinator at Student Health and Wellbeing, Dr\. Karen Flood, at [karen\.flood@ubc\.ca](mailto:karen.flood@ubc.ca)\.

### Application timeline

Apply by **Sun, Jan 15, 2027**\.

All applications will be reviewed by the Practicum Selection Committee\. Selected candidates will be invited for a virtual interview\. The selection process will end once all 4 practicum positions are filled\.

## Pre\-doctoral residencies[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/counselling\-practicum\-residency\-program/\#pre\-doctoral\-residencies](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/counselling-practicum-residency-program/#pre-doctoral-residencies)

UBC Student Health and Wellbeing offers 4 residency positions to doctoral students registered in Counselling Psychology and/or Clinical Psychology programs\. This residency provides advanced clinical training and supervision within a comprehensive, multidisciplinary university setting\.

You can review the following for more information:

-   [Pre\-doctoral internship brochure](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/pre-doctoral_internship_brochure.pdf) \(pdf\)
-   [Public disclosure table](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/counselling_services_public_disclosure_table.pdf) \(pdf\)

### Residency responsibilities and training

Residents work with a diverse student population, addressing a broad spectrum of mental health and wellbeing concerns in a stepped\-care model\.

The residency program is designed to support the development of competent, ethical, and reflective psychologists\. The residency is primarily experiential, combining direct clinical service with structured supervision and didactic training\. Core activities include assessment, individual and group psychotherapy, crisis intervention, treatment planning, and providing practicum supervision within the universities collaborative stepped care model\. Residents receive 4 hours per week of individual supervision by registered psychologists\. 

Over the course of the training year, residents complete a minimum of 1,800 hours of supervised work experience\. Residents receive training and clinical experiences in providing telehealth appointments\. There is also an opportunity to work from home on Thursdays\. Opportunities to work with student athletes are available for applicants with training in sport psychology\.

The pre\-doctoral residency program is accredited by the [Canadian Psychological Association](http://www.cpa.ca/accreditation) for the Accreditation Term 2022/23 to 2029/30\.
