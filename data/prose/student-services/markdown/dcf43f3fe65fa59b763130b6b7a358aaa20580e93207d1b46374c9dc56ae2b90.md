## Making changes to your courses[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/course\-change\-dates/\#making\-changes\-to\-your\-courses](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates/#making-changes-to-your-courses)

### Before the add/drop deadline

Each term, before the add/drop deadline, also known as the course change deadline, you may add, drop, change, or request [Credit/D/Fail](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/creditdfail-grading/) or [auditing status](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/auditing-courses/) for your courses without having the record of registration appear on your academic record\. 

If you drop a course before the course change deadline, you will be eligible for a full [tuition refund](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/tuition-refunds/)\. 

Make sure you are dropping the course from your registered courses and not from your saved schedule\. Removing courses from your saved schedule is not considered dropping those courses\. You can confirm that you have successfully dropped the course by reviewing your timetable\.

You must drop your course in [Workday](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/) by 11:59 pm PT on the course change deadline\.

View step\-by\-step instructions on [adding](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/registering-for-individual-course-sections/), [dropp](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/dropping-a-course/)[i](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/dropping-a-course/)[ng](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/dropping-a-course/), or [swapping](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/swapping-a-course/) a course in Workday\.

### After the add/drop deadline

-   To add a course, you’ll need permission from your faculty\. You’ll also be required to submit the [Change of Registration \(Add/Drop\) Form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/registration_adddrop_form_2024_fall.pdf) \(pdf\)\.
-   You will not be able to switch between [Credit/D/Fail](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/creditdfail-grading/) and percentage grading\. 
-   You will receive a “W” standing on your academic record if you choose to drop a course after the deadline\. Talk to your [academic advising office](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) if you are facing extenuating circumstances that require you to do so\.
-   Depending on your withdrawal date, you may be eligible for a [partial refund](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/tuition-refunds/)\.
-   View step\-by\-step instructions on [withdrawing from a course](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/withdrawing-from-a-course/) in Workday\.

### What is a W standing? 

A “**W**” standing is recorded on a student’s transcript when they withdraw from a course **after** the add/drop deadline\. While it is not used for computing averages, it may impact applications for future studies\.

### Considerations before dropping or withdrawing from courses

Your credit load directly impacts your eligibility for [awards and student loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/maintaining-awards-loan-eligibility/), [UBC bursary](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-bursary-program/), [UBC student housing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/eligibility/#academic), [immigration](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/immigration-impacts-part-time-studies-withdrawing-courses/), and [payment\-free/interest–free status](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/student-loan-interest/) for past government loans\. 

Review the course load requirements for these programs before dropping or withdrawing from your courses\.
