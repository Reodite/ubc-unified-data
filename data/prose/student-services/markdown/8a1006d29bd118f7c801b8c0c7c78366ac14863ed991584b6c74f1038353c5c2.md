## About the bursary[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/john\-r\-scarfo\-wellness\-bursary/\#about\-the\-bursary](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/john-r-scarfo-wellness-bursary/#about-the-bursary)

The John R\. Scarfo Wellness bursary has been made available through an endowment established by Dr\. John R\. Scarfo for students who have demonstrated financial need and are committed to a healthy lifestyle\. Successful applicants will demonstrate a commitment to health as described in the eligibility requirements below\.

Dr\. Scarfo came from a family with 12 children and would not have been able to receive his MD at UBC without the generosity of Dean Walter Gage\. Having been the recipient of the generosity of others, Dr\. Scarfo wishes to support UBC students and honour those who had supported him\. These bursaries are adjudicated by Enrolment Services\.

## Eligibility requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/john\-r\-scarfo\-wellness\-bursary/\#eligibility\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/john-r-scarfo-wellness-bursary/#eligibility-requirements)

You must meet all the following requirements to apply:

-   You must be a Canadian citizen or permanent resident\.
-   You must be considered a [full\-time student](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/course-load-guide/)\.
-   You must meet the terms for awards eligibility set out in [UBC’s Regulations Governing University Awards](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/university-awards)\.
-   You must demonstrate unmet financial need based on [UBC’s Common Assessment Mechanism](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-bursary-program/)\.
-   You must meet the eligibility requirements and submit an application to the [UBC Bursary Program](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-bursary-program/) by Sept 15, 2026\.
-   You must prepare a statement \(max 150 words\) demonstrating your commitment to health and wellness in your daily life\. Examples may include, but are not limited to:
    -   Leading a balanced and healthy lifestyle and communicating a clear vision for what this means\.
    -   Demonstrating one or more of the multiple aspects of healthy living, including social, environmental, mental, emotional, physical, and spiritual\.
    -   Identifying the damaging effects of the use of tobacco or illicit drugs on an individual’s or the general public’s health and wellbeing\.
    -   Providing leadership in wellbeing promotion\.

## Before you apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/john\-r\-scarfo\-wellness\-bursary/\#before\-you\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/john-r-scarfo-wellness-bursary/#before-you-apply)

Before you start your application, make sure you complete the following:

1.  Review the eligibility requirements for the John R\. Scarfo Wellness Bursary outlined above\.
2.  Prepare a 150\-word \(max\) Personal Statement explaining your commitment to health and wellness to the selection committee\.
    -   Your Personal Statement should define what health and wellness means to you and how you strive to lead a personally healthy lifestyle or promote healthy living in your community\. Health can be related to your social, environmental, emotional, physical, or spiritual wellbeing\. 
    -   **Your Statement should identify your single most meaningful commitment or contribution to health and wellness** which could can include personal commitments, leadership roles, promotion activities, club participations, academic projects and/or research, sports and recreation/active engagement, volunteer and/or work experiences\., as well as your goals for the future\.
    -   Your statement should articulate how you work towards integrating your belief system in your own life and the lives of others\.

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/john\-r\-scarfo\-wellness\-bursary/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/john-r-scarfo-wellness-bursary/#how-to-apply)

The John R\. Scarfo Wellness Bursary application is part of the General Bursary program\. Apply and check your eligibility for the [UBC General Bursary](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-bursary-program/) during the application period between **Aug 14, 2026** and **Sep 15, 2026**\.

In the section, “Personal Interests and Community Participation” in the General Bursary application, clearly write “SCARFO” followed by your Personal Statement \(maximum 150 words\)\.

If you have already submitted your General Bursary application, you can log back in and edit your application to add your statement and re\-submit it during the application period by **Tues, Sep 15, 2026**\.
