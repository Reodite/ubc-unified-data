## Financial holds[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/late\-payment\-fees/\#financial\-holds](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/late-payment-fees/#financial-holds)

A student may be placed on financial hold as a result of outstanding fees, including tuition and student fees, parking and library fines, and Student Housing and Community Services fees\. The hold will be removed once the outstanding balance, including penalties, is paid in full\.

If you are put on financial hold, you will be blocked from the following registration activities:

-   Registering in new courses
-   Enroling in course waitlists
-   Switching course sections

### What happens during a financial hold

If you are placed on hold, you will not be dropped from class automatically and your professors will not be notified\. However, the following will happen:

-   Your registration will be blocked\. You can still drop or withdraw from courses before the [course change dates](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates/)\.
-   A $35\.00 processing fee will be assessed\.
-   You will be assessed [financial interest](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/late-payment-fees/#financial-interest) near the end of every month\.
-   You will not be able to order transcripts and parchments\.
-   You will not be able to download a program completion letter\.
-   UBC Student Housing and Community Services may refuse your admission to residences, withdraw your residence and dining privileges, and/or require you to vacate the premises\.
-   UBC Parking may withdraw your parking privileges and tow your parked vehicles\.
-   The Library may withdraw your borrowing privileges and electronic access\.
-   Accounts that are still outstanding after the session end date are subject to be sent to a collection agency\.

### Removing a financial hold

The financial hold will be removed when the outstanding balance is paid in full\. You can make partial payments until the entire balance has been paid\. Try to pay as soon as possible to avoid being charged further interest\.

Payment processing times vary depending on your [payment method](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/paying-tuition/)\.

## Financial interest[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/late\-payment\-fees/\#financial\-interest](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/late-payment-fees/#financial-interest)

Interest will be charged at a [rate of prime](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/daily-digest/) plus 6% per annum\. Interest is calculated near the end of each month and charged on all outstanding amounts that are past due\.

Anything else in this section notwithstanding and any amounts charged as penalties for the late payment of fees and accounts \(including the Interest Penalty and the processing fee\) will not exceed 60% per annum\.

### How to calculate interest rates charged

Follow the steps below to find the interest charged at the end of each month:

1.  Find the [rate of prime](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/daily-digest/)
2.  Add 6 percent to the rate of prime to get your interest per year
3.  Divide your interest per year by 12 months

The total will be the interest charged on all outstanding amounts past due\.

If you’re facing an unexpected [financial emergency](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/financial-support-options/financial-emergencies/), or have had a significant change to your financial situation, contact an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to discuss your options and learn how UBC can help\.
