## About academic leave[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/taking\-break\-courses/\#about\-academic\-leave](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/taking-break-courses/#about-academic-leave)

In accordance with the [Academic Calendar](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?tree=3,289,0,0), for most programs, students in “Good Standing” may take an Academic Leave \(time away from UBC studies\) of up to one full year or 12 months\. 

Students in some programs are required to seek permission for an academic leave before leaving\. All students should discuss their plans with an academic advisor in their faculty or school before requesting a leave\.

For example, a student in “Good Standing” after completing the 2025/26 Winter Session will automatically be eligible to register in the same program for the 2026 Summer, 2026/27 Winter, 2027 Summer and 2027/28 Winter Sessions\.

### Consequences of not being in good standing

If you’re placed on academic probation, you may be restricted to register in a maximum number of credits in the following term\. Please see your faculty’s academic regulations or contact your faculty’s [advising office](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) for more information\.

### Programs requiring approval for Academic Leave

If you are in a cohort\-based or a limited enrolment program, you are required to seek permission for an academic leave before taking time away due to the limited ability to accommodate leaves in such programs\.

However, for all other programs, you are highly encouraged to notify your faculty to ensure you understand the implications and next steps\. Please [contact your faculty or school](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) before taking an academic leave\.

## Requesting an Academic Leave[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/taking\-break\-courses/\#requesting\-an\-academic\-leave](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/taking-break-courses/#requesting-an-academic-leave)

### Undergraduate students

If you are an undergraduate student and want to take time off, complete the steps below:

1.  Review UBC’s policy on [academic leave](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?tree=3,289,0,0)\.
2.  Talk to an [academic advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) in your program or faculty for program\-specific requirements\.
3.  Check with an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to see if taking a break will affect your financial assistance or awards\.

### International students

If you’re an international student, there are special considerations around taking time off\. Read the [important steps to take](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/taking-time-away-school-international/) for requesting time away from your studies and contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) if you are planning to take a break\.

### Graduate students

If you’re a graduate student, you’ll have to apply for a leave of absence from [Graduate and Post\-doctoral Studies](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/)\.

## Returning from academic leave[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/taking\-break\-courses/\#returning\-from\-academic\-leave](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/taking-break-courses/#returning-from-academic-leave)

If you have been away for one year, there are specific instructions for returning to your studies depending on your situation\.

**

You did not attend school elsewhere

**

You will automatically receive a registration appointment and be sent an email with your registration date and time\. If you do not register by Term 2 of the Winter session in which you are expected to return, your record will be discontinued\.

**

You would like to return to a new program

**

1.  Log into your [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/)\.
2.  Click the blue “Re\-Admission/Change Program” button on the right\.
3.  Under “First Program Selection”, choose the program you would like to apply to using the drop\-down\.

## Extending an academic leave[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/taking\-break\-courses/\#extending\-an\-academic\-leave](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/taking-break-courses/#extending-an-academic-leave)

If you decide to extend your academic leave beyond one year, discuss your plans with a [Faculty Academic Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) and your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\. If you’re an international student, connect with [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) first\.

You will need to submit an application for readmission to resume your studies when you are ready through the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/)\. Each faculty has different readmission deadlines, and there may be financial implications if you have awards, scholarships or government student loans\.

If you cannot log into [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/), you will regain access once you are readmitted to UBC\. If you need to download your [unofficial transcript](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-transcript/#unofficial-transcript) or order an [official transcript](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-transcript/#pdf-transcript), you can complete this request through the [UBC Official Documents tool](https://ubc.appiancloud.com/suite/sites/ubc-official-documents)\.
