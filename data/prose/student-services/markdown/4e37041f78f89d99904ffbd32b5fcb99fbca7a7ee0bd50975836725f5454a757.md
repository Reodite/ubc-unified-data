## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/linguistics/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/linguistics/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Linguistics](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/undergrad/prospective-students/linguistics-program-description/), you’re learning about one of the most fundamental things that makes us human: our language\. You’ll develop important skills within a highly interdisciplinary field that combines research methods from the humanities, social, natural, and mathematical sciences\.  
  
These skills may include:

-   Applying logical thinking to analyze how language has evolved through history and can be taught and preserved
-   Analyzing the structural properties of language and how it is processed
-   Researching, collecting, synthesizing, and presenting information across various subjects
-   Critical thinking that demonstrates an awareness of cultural, historical, and social contexts 
-   Carrying out fieldwork and analyzing sounds, words, sentences, and meanings in real\-world language
-   Collaborating in team situations with people from diverse professional, academic, and cultural backgrounds
-   Clear and concise written communication drawing upon a deep understanding of language
-   Public speaking and debating abilities that demonstrate persuasive, carefully reasoned arguments and comprehensive knowledge of linguistic theories and ideas

Linguistics is a particularly varied discipline, with possibilities to continue on in social, humanitarian, and scientific directions\. Career opportunities vary widely across a range of fields including interpretation or translation, language teaching, communications, publishing, government, health services, speech recognition, artificial intelligence, and others\.

## Make the most of your program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/linguistics/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/linguistics/#make-the-most-of-your-program)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities, develop your skillset, and help you clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[UBC Speech and Linguistics Student Association](https://ubcsalsa.wordpress.com/)**  
    Meet other UBC students through events, peer tutoring, and volunteer opportunities\.
-   **[Departmental events](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/events/event/?date=upcoming-events&amp;eventTag=&amp;topic=&amp;type=)**  
    Network with Linguistics students and faculty members at upcoming colloquia, seminars, and conferences\.
-   **[Linguistics research labs](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/research/labs-groups/)**  
    Check out current UBC research in the field of linguistics and contact these groups to see if there are opportunities to get involved\.
-   **[Language Sciences Undergraduate Research Conference](https://blogs.ubc.ca/lsurc/)**  
    Share your work, meet peers with similar interests, and learn about language\-related research in Term 2\.
-   [UBC Language Sciences](https://languagesciences.ubc.ca/)
-   Find out about interdisciplinary work in the language sciences happening across campus\.
-   **[Centre for Artificial Intelligence Decision\-making and Action](https://caida.ubc.ca/)**  
    Volunteer in a research role focused on the development, analysis, and application of AI systems\.
-   [Explore: French immersion program](https://englishfrench.ca/explore/)  
    Travel to a francophone community in Canada, live with a host family or with other students, and practice your French while experiencing francophone culture\.
-   [Teach English in Japan](https://jetprogramme.ca/)Engage in foreign language education and international exchange
-   [LSA Summer Institute](https://www.lsadc.org/content.asp?contentid=138)Attend a month\-long program of courses, lectures, workshops, and social events with faculty and staff from other Linguistics programs\.
-   [North American Summer School for Logic, Language, and Information](https://nasslli25.shane.st/)Join students and researchers from many disciplines for a series of courses and workshops on topics in the interdisciplinary study of logic, language, and information\.
-   [European Summer School for Logic, Language, and Information](https://2025.esslli.eu/)Attend courses and workshops are offered in logic, linguistics and computer science\.
