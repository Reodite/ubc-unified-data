## Documents all students need[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/extend\-study\-permit\-within\-canada/gather\-required\-documents\-study\-permit\-extension/\#documents\-all\-students\-need](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/extend-study-permit-within-canada/gather-required-documents-study-permit-extension/#documents-all-students-need)

All students extending their study permit will need the following documents\. 

When possible, documents should be issued within 30 days of submitting your application to be considered recent\. If you must provide several documents in a section, combine them into one file \(such as a PDF\) before uploading\. The size limit is 4MB per file; you may need to [reduce the file size](http://www.cic.gc.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1213&amp;top=23)\. 

If your documents are not in English or French, they must be accompanied by both of the following:

-   English or French official translation
-   An [affidavit](http://www.cic.gc.ca/english/helpcentre/glossary.asp#affidavit) from the person who completed the translation

Below are Certified Translators in British Columbia:

-   [Society of Interpreters and Translators of British Columbia](https://www.stibc.org/)
-   [MOSAIC](https://www.mosaicbc-lsp.org/)

**

Letter of Enrolment \(listed as ‘Letter of Acceptance’ on your MyCIC checklist\)

**

Your Letter of Enrolment shows your current registration at UBC\. This letter is required to explain details about your studies, show current and future enrolment, and help an officer determine the remaining time you need to complete your program\. You can only obtain one after registration has opened and you are registered\. Review [registration dates](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/registration-dates/) for the term\.

Make sure to get a recently issued Letter of Enrollment issued within the past 30 days\. **Do not use** the Letter of Acceptance you received when you were first accepted to UBC \(unless you have been recently admitted to start a new program\)\. Usually, your study permit will be issued to match the expected program completion date indicated on your Letter of Enrollment, plus 90 days\. Therefore, it is important to make sure your Letter of Enrollment has the correct information, otherwise you may need to extend your study permit again\.

**Undergraduate Students**

Learn how to [get your Letter of Enrolment](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/student-documents/downloading-your-confirmation-of-enrolment-letter/), known as a “Confirmation of Enrolment Letter” in Workday\. Be sure to select the most recent academic session \(Winter or Summer Session\)\. If the letter does not indicate you are enrolled [full\-time for immigration purposes](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/ubc-definitions-immigration-purposes/#full-time), contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support\.

Your Class Standing \(previously known as your “year level” in the Student Service Centre\) indicates your remaining program length on your Letter of Enrolment\. For example, if you issue your Letter of Enrolment in December 2026, and your letter states you have Class Standing 3, this indicates you would complete your program in April 2028\. Your study permit should normally be issued to match that period \(April 2028\) plus 90 days \(July 2028\)\.

Contact an [Academic Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) from your faculty to request a Customized Enrolment Letter if:

-   You need additional time to complete your program than what your Workday Letter of Enrolment indicates\. For example, you are in a co\-op program, you transferred credits to UBC or your letter indicates you are in Class Standing 4 but you will need additional terms to finish your program requirements based on your anticipated course load\.
-   You are applying at the end of Winter Session Term 2 \(Jan\-Apr\) or during the Summer Session \(May\-Aug\) before [registration opens](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/registration-dates/) for the following Winter Session \(Sep\-Apr\)\.
-   You are on [authorized leave](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/taking-time-away-school-international/) and need to extend your study permit\. Contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support with your application\.

Your Customized Enrolment Letter should include your program, your current status as a student, and your expected completion date\.

**Important:** Submit both a Customized Enrolment Letter and Letter of Enrolment ​​\(if required\)\.

**Graduate Students**

Learn how to [get your Letter of Enrolment](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/student-documents/downloading-your-confirmation-of-enrolment-letter/), known as a “Confirmation of Enrolment Letter” in Workday\. Be sure to select the most recent academic session \(Winter or Summer Session\)\. If the letter does not indicate you are enrolled [full\-time for immigration purposes](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/ubc-definitions-immigration-purposes/#full-time), contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support\.

Your Letter of Enrolment indicates your remaining program length\. For example, if you issue your Letter of Enrolment in October 2026, and your letter states you are enrolled until April 2027, this indicates you are completing your program in April 2027\. Your study permit should normally be issued to match that period \(April 2027\) plus 90 days \(July 2027\)\.

Contact your [graduate program staff](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/supervision/program-advising) to request a Customized Enrolment Letter if:

-   You need additional time to complete your program than your Workday Letter of Enrolment indicates\. For example, your letter indicates you are enrolled until the end of the current Winter Session ending in April, but you need additional terms to finish your program requirements\.
-   You are applying at the end of the Winter Session Term 2 \(Jan\-Apr\) or during the Summer Session \(May\-Aug\) before [registration opens](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/registration-dates) for the following Winter Session \(Sep\-Apr\)\.
-   You receive funding \(such as a teaching assistantship or research assistantship\) as part of your program\. This letter could also be uploaded in your proof of financial support documents\)\.
-   You are on a [leave of absence](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/taking-time-away-school-international/) and need to extend your study permit\. Contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support with your application\.

Your Customized Enrolment Letter should include your program, your current status as a student, and your expected completion date\.

UBCO graduate students must request their customized enrolment letters from the [College of Graduate Studies](https://gradstudies.ok.ubc.ca/resources/forms/graduate-student-letter-request-form/)\.

**Important:** Submit both a Customized Enrolment Letter and a Letter of Enrolment ​​\(if required\)\.

**Students coming from another Canadian School or applying for your first study permit**

If you are extending your study permit based on your admission to UBC, such as you completed high school in Canada or you are transferring from another post\-secondary institution, or you are a new student who is [eligible](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/extend-study-permit/) to apply for a study permit from inside Canada, you will need to show an official **Letter of Acceptance \(LOA\)** from UBC\. Find out how to get your [Letter of Acceptance](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-documents-required-study-permit/#if-you-have-questions)\. You can also include a Letter of Enrolment if you have already registered in courses — see above\.​​​​​

Proof of means of financial support

**

The proof of financial support must confirm that you have enough funds to support your studies and living expenses for a year\.

As of September 1, 2025, the minimum cost\-of\-living financial requirement is $22,895 for a year\.

In your study permit application, you should show proof of funds which would be sufficient to cover:

-   **One year of living expenses**  
    The current cost\-of\-living amount is $22,895 for a single person\. The cost\-of\-living amount will be adjusted each year based on Statistics Canada’s low\-income cut\-off\. If you have dependents \(spouse, common\-law partner, and/or children\), you’ll need to show additional proof of funds based on the number of [family members](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/financial-support.html)\. If you have less than a year remaining to finish your program, you can prorate this amount to $1,907\.91 per month\.
-   **One year of tuition fees**  
    You only need to show proof of funds to cover one year of tuition fees\. If you have less than a year remaining to finish your program, you could show proof of funds for the remaining tuition fees\.

You may provide a combination of financial documents and sources, as long as the total exceeds the minimum cost\-of\-living financial requirement for yourself and any dependents plus one year of tuition fees, such as:

1.  An official bank statement issued in the past 30 days that has been stamped or signed by your Canadian bank showing the balance\.
2.  A letter from your parent\(s\) stating their relationship to you and that they will financially support you along with their financial documents\. The letter should be signed by your parent\(s\)\. You must also provide an official bank statement\(s\) of your parent\(s\), issued by their bank and that has been signed or stamped\. It should be in English or French \(or accompanied by an official translation\)\. To write a letter of financial support, they can [download our Word template](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/letter_financial_support_international_students.docx) \(docx\)\.
3.  A letter from your sponsor \(e\.g\., sponsorship organization, scholarship\(s\), loan\(s\)\) or employer \(e\.g\., teaching assistantships, research assistantships\) stating their relationship to you and the amount that they will provide you\.
4.  If you have already paid tuition fees for a future term, get proof of payment from Workday\. Learn how to get your [account statement](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/finances/downloading-your-proof-of-tuition-payment-account-statement/)\.

See IRCC’s [proof of financial support](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/financial-support.html) page for further examples\. 

While you only need to show proof of funds for one year, the officer must be satisfied that the possibility of funding for additional years of studies exists, such as your parents are employed or that your scholarship is for more than 1 year\.   

If providing a combination of financial documents and sources, include a summary or an index page of all the documents you are submitting for this section\. This will also allow you to clarify or explain the connection between documents, if applicable\. You can download a [template for the summary](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/ubc_summary_proof_funds_template.docx) \(docx\)\.

If you are having challenges with providing proof of funds, contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support\.

**Important:** If the balance\(s\) in any of the documentation above is in foreign currency, provide a screenshot of the [Bank of Canada currency converter](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/currency-converter/) result \(if your currency is not listed, use [xe\.com](https://www.xe.com/)\)\.

Passport

**

The scanned copy of your passport pages provides information about your travel history and the expiry date of your passport\.

You **must** provide a copy of the bio\-data page \(the page with your name, date of birth, passport number, expiry date, etc\.\) and a copy of every page in your passport that has stamps, visas, or markings from either Canada or other countries\.

Make sure you merge all the pages into one PDF document and upload it\.

**Important:** Your study permit will not be issued past your passport’s expiry date\. If your passport will expire soon, extend it before you apply for your study permit extension\.

Need help scanning your documents? Go to the [Irving K\. Barber Chapman Learning Commons](http://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tech-support/print-copy-scan/) or [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/)\.

## Documents you might need[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/extend\-study\-permit\-within\-canada/gather\-required\-documents\-study\-permit\-extension/\#documents\-you\-might\-need](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/extend-study-permit-within-canada/gather-required-documents-study-permit-extension/#documents-you-might-need)

Depending on your specific circumstances, you may need one or more of the following documents\.

**

Letter of Explanation

**

A letter of explanation is a good way to describe additional factors or include additional documents\. It could include:

-   The purpose of your application
-   Issues you would like to clarify or that the officer might find useful
-   Supporting documents, screenshots or any other proof to support your explanation
-   Additional employment history if you have run out of space in your application

Upload this document under ‘*Client Information’* in the ‘*Optional Documents’* section of your online checklist\.

Download the [Letter of Explanation](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/ubc_letter_explanation_template_word.docx) Word document template\.

**

Provincial Attestation Letter \(PAL\)

**

Most new UBC students who apply for a study permit \(initial or extension\) inside Canada need to include a PAL with their  application\. 

Whether your application requires a PAL will depend on the [level of study](https://gazette.gc.ca/rp-pr/p1/2025/2025-01-18/html/notice-avis-eng.html) for your program\. You can find out more about your level of study and whether a PAL will be required by reviewing our [Provincial Attestation Letter](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/) page\.

**Master’s and Doctoral Degree students**

As of Jan 1, 2026, you do not need a PAL if you are applying for a study permit for a degree\-granting graduate program at the Master’s or Doctoral level\. This applies to students who are new to UBC, or are extending their study permit for studies at this level\. 

On your document checklist, you will be asked to upload proof of your exemption\. Download the [PAL exemption template](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sp_pal_exemption_template.docx) \(docx\) and personalize it with your own information\. Combine this letter with your letter of enrolment \(for continuing students\) or letter of admission \(if you haven’t started your program yet\) into one file, then upload this file in your document checklist where they request a PAL or proof of exemption\. This means you will be uploading your letter enrolment or admission in two different places on the document checklist\.

**New to UBC students**

If you are a new to UBC student, such as you are coming to UBC from a Canadian high school or another post\-secondary institution, you must include a PAL in your application, unless you will be enrolled in a Master’s or Doctoral degree program\. Find out [how to get your PAL](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/#how-and-when-to-get-your-pal)\. If you are coming to UBC from another post\-secondary institution, you must receive your study permit before you can begin studies at UBC\. If you are coming to UBC from a Canadian high school, you can start your studies at UBC while you are waiting for a decision on your new study permit application\.

**Current UBC students in the same level of studies**

If you applied for your study permit with a UBC Letter of Admission, and you are continuing studies at the same level of studies, you do not need a PAL\. For example, if you are extending your study permit to continue your undergraduate degree \(even if you changed programs or majors\), you do not need a PAL\. Apply for your study permit extension from inside Canada before your study permit expires\. If you have a request for a PAL letter or proof of exemption from the PAL in your document checklist, you will need to write a letter to explain why you are exempt from the PAL and provide supporting documents\. If you previously transferred to UBC from a Canadian high school or post\-secondary institution, or you will have [more than 150 days](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/taking-time-away-school-international/) between UBC programs, contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support\. 

Download the [PAL exemption template](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/spe_pal_exemption_template.docx) \(docx\)\. For the suggested supporting documents, in addition to your current study permit and current Letter of Enrolment, we recommend providing proof of your previous level of studies and institution, which could be one of the following:  

-   The UBC Letter of Acceptance you submitted in your most recent study permit application
-   The UBC Letter of Enrollment you submitted in your most recent study permit extension application
-   A current [UBC transcript](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-transcript/) \(official or unofficial\) from the past 30 days \(a PDF version is ok\)
-   A current academic record from Workday from the past 30 days – login to [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/), go to the “Academics” tab, select “View my academic record”, then save the page as a PDF\. 

Combine this letter with the suggested supporting documents into one file, then upload this file in your document checklist where they request a PAL\. If you previously transferred to UBC from a Canadian high school or post\-secondary institution, studied at another institution then returned to UBC \(other than on a UBC Go Global exchange\), took time away from studies or you will have [more than 150 days](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/taking-time-away-school-international/) between UBC programs, contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support\.

**Current UBC students changing level of studies**

If you are transitioning to a Master’s or Doctoral degree program, you are exempt from the PAL requirement, even if you are changing levels of study\. However, there are some cases where a PAL may be required if you are changing levels of study\. A PAL would be required if you are changing from a Master’s or Doctoral degree program to a program at the undergraduate level when you extend your study permit\. For example, if you are enrolled in a Master’s program, and you are changing to a Graduate certificate at UBC, you can continue to study at UBC with the same study permit, so long as it’s still [valid](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/#maintaining-your-student-status)\. However, if you need to extend your study permit, you will need a PAL for your study permit extension application\. Find out [how and when to get your PAL](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/#how-and-when-to-get-your-pal)\. Contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) if you will have [more than 150 days](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/taking-time-away-school-international/) between programs\. 

**If you transferred to UBC before Nov 8, 2024**

If you transferred to UBC from a Canadian high school or another post\-secondary institution in Canada before Nov 8, 2024, send International Student Advising a [webform](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0J8VRJgJQkuO2b4) with details about your situation before applying to extend your study permit\. An assessment must be made to determine if a PAL will be required as part of your application\. 

If you have a [gap of more than 150 days](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/taking-time-away-school-international/) between programs or institutions, or you are applying for [restoration](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/restoration-status/) of your student status, contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support\.

Medical exam

**

If you have been studying in Canada for the past 12 months, you do not need proof of a medical exam unless:

-   You have lived in a designated country or territory for more than 6 months in the past 12 months\. See [this page](http://www.cic.gc.ca/english/information/medical/dcl.asp) for a clear definition of what a designated country or territory is, or
-   You would like to work in a job that will bring you into close contact with patients, children, the elderly, etc\. This includes public health, health sciences or agriculture\-related fields \(e\.g\., health care workers, primary and secondary school teachers\)\. See [this page](http://www.cic.gc.ca/english/information/medical/medexams-temp.asp) to find out if you require a medical exam\.

If the above applies to you, you will need a medical exam conducted by an [IRCC\-approved Panel Physician](http://www.cic.gc.ca/pp-md/pp-list.aspx)\. Your medical exam report \(referred to as your ‘e\-medical’\) can be submitted with your study permit application\.

There is a [temporary policy](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/notices/2022-foreign-exempt-ime.html) allowing some students to use a medical exam completed in the past 5 years which has been extended to October 5, 2029\. Learn more about [medical exams and work](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/#if-you-work-with-children-or-seniors-in-healthcare-settings-or-in-agriculture)\.

**Important:** If you answered “yes” to any question in the “Background information” section other than 2\(c\), please speak to an [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/)\.

Biometrics

**

If you have not previously provided biometrics \(fingerprints and a photo\) or your biometrics have expired, you must submit them in\-person at a [designated Service Canada location](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/biometrics/where-to-give.html)\. 

The processing time for your study permit extension does not include the time it takes to submit your biometrics\. Your application will not be processed until biometrics are received\. You only need to give biometrics once every 10 years\.

U\.S\. citizens and people who have provided biometrics in the past 10 years and [certain other individuals](http://www.cic.gc.ca/english/visit/biometrics.asp) are exempt from providing biometrics\. 

1.  Pay the biometrics fee at the same time you submit your study permit application for faster processing\.
2.  Receive your biometrics instruction letter\. If you applied online, you will receive an electronic biometrics instruction letter within 24 hours\.  
3.  [Book an appointment](https://apps.canada.ca/login?redirect=/reservation?utm_campaign=not-applicable&amp;utm_medium=vanity-url&amp;utm_source=canada-ca_service-canada-appointment) as soon as possible at a designated Service Canada location\.
4.  Submit your biometrics within 30 days of receiving your biometrics instruction letter\.
