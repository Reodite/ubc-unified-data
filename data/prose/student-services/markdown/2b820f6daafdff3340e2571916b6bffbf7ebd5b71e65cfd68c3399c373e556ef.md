## Immigration document support[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/\#immigration\-document\-support](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/#immigration-document-support)

UBC offers helpful resources to support you as you prepare your immigration documents\.

### Step\-by\-step tutorials

Browse the complete list of step\-by\-step instructions on how to prepare applications for your study permit, work permit, BC Medical Services Plan, and more\.

[View the list](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/tutorials/)

### Application review courses

Before applying, enrol in a Canvas application review course to go over your initial study permit, study permit extension, Temporary Resident Visa, or Post\-Graduation Work Permit application\.

[View Canvas links](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/tutorials/#online-application-review-courses)

### Get immigration reminders

Add your document expiry dates to the confidential UBC Reminder System and get notifications for when you need to extend your documents\. 

[Learn more](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/immigration-documents-reminder-system/)

## If you still have questions[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/\#if\-you\-still\-have\-questions](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/#if-you-still-have-questions)

Throughout your degree, there are many in\-person and online resources to support you\.

### International Student Advising

International Student Advisors are Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants \(RCICs\) or Regulated International Student Immigration Advisors \(RISIAs\) who can help support you in your experience as a UBC international student\.

[Connect with an advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/)

### Other UBC support and resources

UBC staff are here for you throughout your studies\. See [resources and services](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/) for help with academics, money, health, and more\.
