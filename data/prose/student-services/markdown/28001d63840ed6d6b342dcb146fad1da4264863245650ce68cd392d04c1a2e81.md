Taking a self\-assessment can be a useful way to gather and organize  information about your values and preferences in order to make informed decisions about your career\. These resources can’t predict your ideal career path, but they can help you identify options and overcome feeling stuck or unsure about your career\.

## Self\-guided tools[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/career\-resources/self\-assessment\-tools\-resources/\#self\-guided\-tools](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/self-assessment-tools-resources/#self-guided-tools)

Your values and interests are 2 of the most significant predictors of career satisfaction\. If you are interested in your work and able to live out your values, you are more likely to feel confident about your career choices\. Clarifying your interests and values to identify career options that could be a good fit for you\.

-   Download the [values inventory worksheet](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/values_inventory_worksheet.pdf) \(pdf\) to help you use this approach in your career decision\-making\.
-   Use the [skills inventory worksheet](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/skills_inventory_worksheet.pdf) \(pdf\) to identify how your skills compare to the skills employers are looking for\.
-   Complete this [free 30\-minute quiz](https://www.careerexplorer.com/), to gain insights into your personality and career preferences\.

### Paid online assessment tools

There are a wide variety of online tools out there that you can access for a fee\. While it is not necessary for you to pay to identify your interests or abilities, some people find it to be worthwhile to use them\. Explore some helpful assessment tools below:

-   [SkillScan](https://www.skillscan.com/)  
    Complete a short quiz and receive a concrete and practical profile of your skills and preferences matched to a list of relevant career options\.
-   [CliftonStrengths](https://commerce.gallup.com/category/cliftonstrengths/0ZGPa00000007eDOAQ)  
    After completing a timed quiz, you will receive a report that outlines your top 5 strengths\. Each strength can give you ideas for your personal and professional goals\. Knowing your top 5 strengths helps you understand and articulate the value you offer to potential employers\. Use your [UBC student email address](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/ubc-student-email-service?upn=) to get an Educator Price on this well\-regarded self\-discovery tool\.

## Talk to a Career Educator[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/career\-resources/self\-assessment\-tools\-resources/\#talk\-to\-a\-career\-educator](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/self-assessment-tools-resources/#talk-to-a-career-educator)

Book an appointment with a Career Educator at the [Career Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-career-centre/) to:

-   Reflect on your self\-assessment results and explore future possibilities
-   Apply your sense of self to your career decision\-making
-   Create action plans to help you take steps towards your goals

Before scheduling an appointment, complete any of the assessments mentioned above\. Make sure you bring your results to the appointment\. When you are ready, [book an appointment](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/index.php/pid414375?) through CareersOnline\.
