If you’ve been impacted by a significant event or unanticipated circumstances, there are services and resources to help you\.

## Courses, assignments, and exams[https://students\.ubc\.ca/support\-unanticipated\-circumstances/\#courses\-assignments\-and\-exams](https://students.ubc.ca/support-unanticipated-circumstances/#courses-assignments-and-exams)

Find the academic support options available to you\.

-   Check your course syllabus to see what options you have\.
-   Apply for an [academic concession](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-concessions/)\.
    -   Undergraduate students can contact their [academic advising](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) office\.
    -   Graduate students can speak to their program [graduate advisor](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/supervision-advising/program-advising) or supervisor\.
    -   Indigenous students can find a list of [program advisors](https://fnhl.ubc.ca/students/student-resources/program-advisors/)\.

## Mental and physical health[https://students\.ubc\.ca/support\-unanticipated\-circumstances/\#mental\-and\-physical\-health](https://students.ubc.ca/support-unanticipated-circumstances/#mental-and-physical-health)

Access resources for mental and physical health\.

-   Call or chat with a [crisis responder](https://crisiscentre.bc.ca/) 24/7\.
-   Access 24/7 mental health counselling using [Here2Talk](https://here2talk.ca/main)\.
-   Book an appointment with a [mental health](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/) or [medical provider](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/) on campus\.
-   Connect with a spirituality or faith group through a [chaplain at UBC](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/chaplains/)**\.**

## Financial support[https://students\.ubc\.ca/support\-unanticipated\-circumstances/\#financial\-support](https://students.ubc.ca/support-unanticipated-circumstances/#financial-support)

Get help if you’re experiencing financial difficulties\.

-   See if you’re eligible for [emergency funding](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/financial-support-options/emergency-funding/)\.
-   Talk to an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) about your financial support options\.
-   Indigenous students can reach out to the [Indigenous Student Support Team](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/financial-support-options/financial-support-indigenous/)\.

## Sexual assault support[https://students\.ubc\.ca/support\-unanticipated\-circumstances/\#sexual\-assault\-support](https://students.ubc.ca/support-unanticipated-circumstances/#sexual-assault-support)

Help is available if you have experienced sexual assault or sexual violence\. We believe you\.

-   Book a consultation or appointment with a [Support Specialist](https://svpro.ubc.ca/)\.
-   Access [Sexual Assault Support Centre](https://amssasc.ca/) for support and educational resources\.

## Visas and study or work permits[https://students\.ubc\.ca/support\-unanticipated\-circumstances/\#visas\-and\-study\-or\-work\-permits](https://students.ubc.ca/support-unanticipated-circumstances/#visas-and-study-or-work-permits)

Get support if you’re an international student\.

-   Check out the [International Student Guide](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/)\.
-   Connect with an [International Student Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/)\.
