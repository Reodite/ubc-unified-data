## About the event[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/events\-workshops/spring\-career\-fair/\#about\-the\-event](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/spring-career-fair/#about-the-event)

UBC’s Spring Career Fair will be a 2 day event that features more than 80 employers and schools eager to engage with students and share career opportunities and insights\. Register on [CareersOnline](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/app/career-fairs/602b13933d6ac1bc2a27b1d879873f42/overview) to attend the Career Fair\.

### Event details

-   **Date**: Wed, Mar 4 to Thurs, Mar 5, 2026
-   **Time**: 10:30 am to 3:30 pm
-   **Location**: [Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre](https://maps.ubc.ca/?code=ALUM)

## Additional workshops[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/events\-workshops/spring\-career\-fair/\#additional\-workshops](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/spring-career-fair/#additional-workshops)

A series of workshops will be available to help you prepare for your job search and career development\. Whether you’re practicing how to answer tough interview questions, refining your resume, building your online presence, or getting ready to meet employers, these sessions are designed to give you practical tools and build your confidence\.

[Interview Preparation Strategies](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/index.php?s=event&amp;ss=ws&amp;mode=form&amp;id=ec8d0a7e5ede33a0366b44f3100a8346)

-   **Date:** Thurs, Feb 26, 2026
-   **Time:** 12:30 to 1:30 pm
-   **Session format:** Virtual

Practice answering tough questions, asking interviewers good questions, and get tips for how to prepare your stories in advance\.

[How to Prepare for a Career Fair](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/?s=event&amp;ss=ws&amp;mode=form&amp;id=af3e084fa06a7a48a4a60cab1ccd10fb)

-   **Date:** Mon, Mar 2, 2026
-   **Time:** 12:00 to 1:00 pm
-   **Session format:** Virtual

Make the most of each conversation with employers by learning how to introduce yourself, ask good questions, and research employers before attending the next UBC Spring Career Fair\.

[Ask Me Anything: How to Follow\-up After a Career Fair?](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/?s=event&amp;ss=ws&amp;mode=form&amp;id=123d0bd628443eb5ffb44b231da267b9)

-   **Date:** Thurs, Mar 12, 2026
-   **Time:** 12:30 to 1:30 pm
-   **Session format:** Virtual

Bring your questions about what to do after the career fair, whether you got to connect with a recruiter and are wondering how to follow up, or you left feeling a little unsure of your next steps\.

[Linkedin](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/?s=event&amp;ss=ws&amp;mode=form&amp;id=753c7aa155f299ca55f7eb5176ff40e7)

-   **Date:** Tues, Mar 17, 2026
-   **Time:** 10:00 to 11:00 am
-   **Session format:** Virtual

Create an authentic profile, practice finding potential contacts within UBC’s alumni network, and learn how to research career ideas using alumni and employer profiles\.

## Event accessibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/events\-workshops/spring\-career\-fair/\#event\-accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/spring-career-fair/#event-accessibility)

To make the UBC Spring Career Fair more accessible for those who experience barriers related to crowds and bright lights, doors will open at 10:30 am, which is 30 minutes before the official start time\. During this 30\-minute period, lighting will be dimmed, and staff will not be collecting student information for entry\.

If you do not have sensitivity to crowds or bright lights, please wait until 11:00 am to attend the event\. Email event staff at [spring\.fair@ubc\.ca](mailto:spring.fair@ubc.ca) if you have any questions or require any accommodations\.
