## About BC student loans[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/loans\-full\-time\-british\-columbia/\#about\-bc\-student\-loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/loans-full-time-british-columbia/#about-bc-student-loans)

If you are a resident of British Columbia enrolled in full or part\-time undergraduate or graduate studies at UBC, you may be eligible for government student loan assistance\. StudentAid BC funding exists to supplement, not replace, funds available to students through individual and family financial resources, savings, and income\.

A full\-time loan application evaluates a student’s income and other financial resources against the costs of post\-secondary study, including educational costs and typical living costs associated with a moderate standard of living\. 

Since StudentAid BC funding is intended to supplement other available financial resources, federal and provincial student financial assistance programs have weekly and lifetime [in\-study payment\-free](https://studentaidbc.ca/maintain/when-payments-are-not-required) funding maximums\. Not all students receive the [maximum amount](https://studentaidbc.ca/help-centre/applying-loans/maximum-funding-limits)\. Refer to [StudentAid BC](https://studentaidbc.ca/) for more information\. 

The province of British Columbia offers Canada\-BC integrated loans to students studying full\-time\. An integrated student loan has two lenders, the governments of Canada and British Columbia\. However, it is administered as a single loan, which streamlines service delivery as students sign a joint federal\-provincial loan agreement and have a single point of contact for managing their loans\. Residents of BC apply for a student loan through StudentAid BC\.

If you’re a part\-time student living in BC, please review your specific [eligibility requirements and assessments\.](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/part-time-funding-british-columbia/#part-time-funding-coverage)

### Grant funding

Some students who apply for student loans will be eligible for grant funding, which does not have to be repaid\. Your eligibility is based on your application\.  
  
Refer to StudentAid BC to view a [list of available programs](https://studentaidbc.ca/explore/grants-scholarships) to support students with the cost of post\-secondary education\.

### Dependent and Independent Status Residency requirement

Students are classified as dependent or independent to determine residency and financial need\. Please refer to [Student Aid BC’s residency requirements](https://studentaidbc.ca/apply/eligibility#residency) to determine your eligibility\.

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/loans\-full\-time\-british\-columbia/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/loans-full-time-british-columbia/#how-to-apply)

Apply for a loan at least 2 months before classes start to receive your funding at the beginning of the term, or up to 6 weeks before the end of your study period\. 

To apply for BC full\-time funding, please follow the steps below:

1.  Set up your [BC Services Card](https://id.gov.bc.ca/account/) account and app if you have not already done so, and log into the [StudentAid BC](https://studentaidbc.ca/sabc-home-page) website for your online application\.
2.  Determine your [course load](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/course-load-guide/) and loan\-eligible study dates\. If you’re unsure, speak to your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\.  
      
    If you are a student with a Permanent Disability \(PD\) or Persistent or Prolonged Disability \(PPD\), you must establish PD or PPD status with StudentAid BC to be considered full\-time for government student loans with at least 40% of a full\-time course load\.
3.  Find your program and study dates from the application through [StudentAid BC](https://studentaidbc.ca/sabc-home-page)\.  
      
    If you cannot locate your program or study dates, check the box indicating that your program or study dates are not listed, and proceed until you submit your application\. This will trigger a Program Information Request \(PIR\) to UBC to confirm your program details before your application can be further processed after your submission\.
4.  Submit your application\. You will be asked to provide your consent to the Canada Revenue Agency \(CRA\) and complete the StudentAid BC Declaration\. This allows StudentAid BC to verify the information in your application\.
5.  Monitor your online application for next steps\.   
      
    If applicable, you will need to have your parent\(s\) and/or spouse provide information to StudentAid BC\. This may require them to set up their BC Services Card account and app\.
6.  ​​ Review your Notice of Assessment \(NOA\) on your [StudentAid BC dashboard](https://studentaidbc.ca/dashboard/login) when available\. The NOA will describe:
    -   The type of funding you are eligible to receive
    -   The amount of funding approved
    -   When you will receive the funding
7.  Submit the online [Master Student Financial Assistance Agreement](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/msfaa.html) \(MSFAA\)\.  
      
    You are required to complete an online MSFAA if you are applying for a full\-time student loan for the first time or have taken a leave from your studies for 2 or more years\. 
8.  **Accept your Notice of Assessment** in a timely manner\. This is required before your enrolment can be confirmed\.
9.  UBC will be prompted to confirm your enrolment by StudentAid BC\. UBC will contact you by email if your enrolment cannot be confirmed\.    
      
    Continue to monitor your application status and inbox for notifications from StudentAid BC and UBC\. You are strongly encouraged to log into your [StudentAid BC account](https://studentaidbc.ca/dashboard/login) to view updates, changes to your assessment if applicable, and communications from the provincial lender\. 

If you are receiving a full\-time loan, you are also recommended to apply for the [UBC Bursary](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-bursary-program/)\.

## Maintain your eligibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/loans\-full\-time\-british\-columbia/\#maintain\-your\-eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/loans-full-time-british-columbia/#maintain-your-eligibility)

To continue being eligible for student loans, you must meet all of the following requirements:

-   You are enrolled in an eligible program of study\. 
-   You are making progress in your studies and are in good academic standing in each period of post\-secondary study\.
-   You are maintaining full\-time student status\. This means you are enrolled in and attending a minimum percentage of a full course load\.
    -   Undergraduate students: Consult the [Course Load Guide](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/course-load-guide/) for more details\.
    -   Graduate students: You must be registered and paying full\-time fees in each term that you receive loan funding
    -   Program exceptions: LLMT, LLMCL and MFA in Creative Writing\. Consult the [Course Load Guide](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/course-load-guide/) for more details\.

## Student loan interest[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/loans\-full\-time\-british\-columbia/\#student\-loan\-interest](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/loans-full-time-british-columbia/#student-loan-interest)

There is no interest assessed on the provincial portion of your student loan\. Effective Apr 1, 2023, the Government of Canada has permanently eliminated the accumulation of interest on the federal portion currently being repaid\. You continue to be responsible for paying any interest that may have accrued on your loan before Apr 1, 2023, except during the period of April 2021 to March 2023, when federal interest was waived\.

You are not required to make payments until you’ve been out of school for 6 months\.

If you return to school with a full\-time student loan at a designated post\-secondary institution before this 6\-month grace period ends, the interest \(if any\) that has accumulated since your last study end date will be waived and you will be in [payment\-free status](https://studentaidbc.ca/maintain/when-payments-are-not-required) for your new study term\.

If you return full\-time to school at a designated post\-secondary institution but do not plan on applying for full\-time loans, you will need to apply for in\-study payment\-free status through your loan provider for your previous loans to be in payment\-free status\.

Being in [payment\-free status](https://studentaidbc.ca/maintain/when-payments-are-not-required) means you are in a “funded term” whether you have new loans or not\. If you withdraw or have an unsuccessful term, it may affect your eligibility for further assistance\.

## Automatic tuition deferral[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/loans\-full\-time\-british\-columbia/\#automatic\-tuition\-deferral](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/loans-full-time-british-columbia/#automatic-tuition-deferral)

Eligible graduate and undergraduate students will have fees deferred for one month after the tuition fee deadline in September, January, or May\.

You are eligible for automatic tuition deferral if you meet both requirements: 

-   You are eligible for funding to pay your tuition and fees\.
-   You submit a complete full time loan application by the following dates:
    -   Jul 31 for a loan beginning in September \(Winter session Term 1\)
    -   Nov 15 for a loan beginning in January \(Winter session Term 2\)
    -   Apr 15 for a loan beginning in May \(Summer session\)
