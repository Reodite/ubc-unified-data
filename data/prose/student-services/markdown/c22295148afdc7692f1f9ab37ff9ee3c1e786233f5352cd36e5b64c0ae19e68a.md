The Student Directed Seminars program provides upper\-year undergraduate students \(in third year or later\) the opportunity to propose, coordinate, and lead their own 3\-credit seminar class with a small group of peers on a topic not currently offered at UBC\-Vancouver\. Student Directed Seminars run from January to April in Winter Term 2\.

Each seminar brings together a group of highly\-motivated students to explore and investigate a topic through learning activities including group discussions, research papers, presentations, guest lectures, applied problem\-solving, and Community Service Learning\. 

If you’re a student from UBC Okanagan, please review the [Okanagan Student Directed Seminars guide](https://fass.ok.ubc.ca/student-resources/undergrad/student-directed-seminars)\.

## Get involved[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/student\-directed\-seminars/\#get\-involved](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/student-directed-seminars/#get-involved)

### Become a student coordinator

Student Directed Seminars are an expansion of the directed studies option offered by most departments and are a great way to enhance your own learning experience while gaining facilitation and leadership skills\. You will also have the unique opportunity to work closely with a faculty member to develop the course proposal and syllabus\.

[Find out more](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/student-directed-seminars/creating-student-directed-seminar/)

### Be a student participant

Student participants have a role in shaping many aspects of the course\. From helping to edit the course syllabus and choose readings, to peer marking, facilitating learning activities, and leading individual classes, each student builds their own learning experience and contributes to the learning outcomes for their peers\.

You must be a third or fourth\-year undergraduate student to register in a Student Directed Seminar\.

Registration for [Student Directed Seminars](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/student-directed-seminars/student-directed-seminar-courses/) is now open\. Review the [program guide](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/student_directed_seminar_program_guide.pdf) \(pdf\) for all available seminars\.

## Advisory committee[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/student\-directed\-seminars/\#advisory\-committee](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/student-directed-seminars/#advisory-committee)

The Student Directed Seminars Advisory Committee is made up of faculty members from various departments, staff from the Centre for Community Engaged Learning, the Alma Mater Society \(AMS\), and former Student Director Seminars Student Coordinators\.

The committee reviews all course proposals thoroughly for academic rigor, quality of course plans, appropriate marking schemes and assignments, and the overall qualifications and suitability of the student coordinator\(s\)\.
