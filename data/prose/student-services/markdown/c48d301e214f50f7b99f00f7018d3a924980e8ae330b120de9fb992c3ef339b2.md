## About the space[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/black\-student\-space/\#about\-the\-space](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/black-student-space/#about-the-space)

The Black Student Space is an identity\-affirming space available only for Black undergraduate and graduate students currently enrolled at the UBC Vancouver campus\. Students can use the space to  connect with peers , recharge, host events , and build community\. They can also access networks, programming, and resources that are specific, relevant, and representative of Black experiences, intersectional identities, and cultures\. 

The Black Student Space recognizes that anti\-Black racism exists in all systems, including higher education, and provides a safe space and dedicated support that contributes to Black students’ academic success, personal growth, and wellbeing\. All staff and faculty are welcome to organize programming and resources in the space that support the needs and interests of Black UBC Students\.

### History of the Black Student Space

The Black Student Space was opened in fall 2023 by the [Office of the Vice\-President Students](https://vpstudents.ubc.ca/) and the [Centre for Student Development and Leadership](https://students.ubc.ca/centre-for-student-development-and-leadership/), in consultation and co\-creation with the UBC Black student community on the Vancouver campus\. It is an intentional step in response to the [Student Strategic Plan](https://studentplan.ubc.ca/) \(strategies 4 and 9\), as well as the [Anti\-Racism and Inclusive Excellence Recommendations Taskforce Report](https://antiracism.ubc.ca/) \(recommendations 3 and 41\)\. 

## Location and layout[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/black\-student\-space/\#location\-and\-layout](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/black-student-space/#location-and-layout)

The space is located in the Exchange Building \([5960 Student Union Boulevard](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Exchange+Student+Residence/@49.2685506,-123.2476201,15z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x548673f2f3ddf7db:0x400bae9c95148b3!8m2!3d49.2685506!4d-123.2476201!16s%2Fg%2F11hzhg7zh1?entry=ttu)\) and consists of an Upper and Lower area that cater to different activities: 

-   The Upper Space, located on the second level, is a dedicated quiet area for studying, individual contemplation, meditation, and other quiet activities\. See how it looks with the [interactive tool](https://my.matterport.com/show/?m=WxrQp1BgNWN)\.
-   The Lower Space includes a lounge space for napping and resting, as well as a large open area for events and group activities\. Students, staff, and faculty can book the Lower Space\. Find out how it looks with the [interactive tool](https://my.matterport.com/show/?m=GZX91RC2KRd)\.

## Space amenities and features[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/black\-student\-space/\#space\-amenities\-and\-features](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/black-student-space/#space-amenities-and-features)

To ensure a welcoming space, the Black Student Space offers multi\-purpose activities from baking, hosting movie nights, reflecting and writing, to hosting events\.

Review the lists below to learn more about the amenities available in each space\.

**

Upper space for quiet study and reflection

**

-   Quiet study and contemplation area
-   Beanbag chairs
-   Ample charging outlets
-   Library
-   Meeting room with capacity for 6 to 8 people
-   Whiteboards and bulletin boards

**

Lower space for lounging and events

**

-   Moveable furniture for study, group activities, and events
-   Lounge and napping area with couches and fireplace
-   Television and projector
-   Board games

**

Shared facilities in both spaces

**

-   Kitchens with oven, microwaves, refrigerators, small appliances, dishes, and cutlery
-   Showers and lockers
-   Printer and scanner
-   Refreshments 

**

Accessibility supports

**

-   Ramp access at the main building entrance
-   Wheelchair accessible gender\-neutral bathrooms and shower facilities
-   Wheelchair accessible automated doors
-   Soft lighting and warm paint colours
-   Elevator access between Upper and Lower Spaces

## How to access the Black Student Space[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/black\-student\-space/\#how\-to\-access\-the\-black\-student\-space](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/black-student-space/#how-to-access-the-black-student-space)

The space is open to all currently enrolled UBC Vancouver students who identify as Black, including, but not limited to, those of Black African descent, African\-American, African\-Canadian, Afro\-Caribbean, Afro\-Latinx, and Afro\-Indigenous heritage\.

To access the Black Student Space, please complete the sign up form below\. Requests are processed bi\-weekly\. You will receive a confirmation email once your access is approved\.

For urgent access, email [bss\.info@ubc\.ca](mailto:bss.info@ubc.ca)\.
