## About the group[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/international\-support\-group/\#about\-the\-group](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/international-support-group/#about-the-group)

Join Global Connections, UBC’s in\-person international student support group, to meet other international students in a safe and relaxing space, connect and build a community of support on campus, and learn ways to navigate the unique challenges often experienced by international students\.

Participants are encouraged to bring a variety of relevant topics to the group meetings\. Past group topics have included:

-   Adjusting to Canadian culture
-   Language concerns and challenges
-   Making connections and new friendships
-   Coping with academic stress and demands
-   Facing uncertainty and unknowns in a new country
-   Building resilience and self\-compassion

## Eligibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/international\-support\-group/\#eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/international-support-group/#eligibility)
