## About the awards[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/undergraduate\-student\-research\-awards\-nserc\-cihr\-sshrc/\#about\-the\-awards](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/#about-the-awards)

The information on this page applies to both UBC Okanagan and Vancouver students\.

Undergraduate Student Research Awards \(USRA\) are opportunities for undergraduate students to consider graduate studies and/or a research career by providing research work experience that complements their studies in an academic setting\. Through these awards, eligible professors receive a subsidy to hire students to work on interesting research\-related jobs and projects\.  
  
These awards are administered by Canada’s 3 research granting agencies:

1.  Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada \(NSERC\)
2.  Canadian Institutes of Health Research \(CIHR\)
3.  Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada \(SSHRC\)

All UBC Vancouver and Okanagan undergraduate students who are Canadian Citizens or Permanent Residents can apply to the NSERC awards\. CIHR and SSHRC USRA are exclusively offered to Canadian Black students only through the [Black student researchers pathway](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/black-student-researchers-pathway-usra/)\.

Learn more about the research areas for each of the above agencies on the [Government of Canada website](https://science.gc.ca/site/science/en/interagency-research-funding/policies-and-guidelines/selecting-appropriate-federal-granting-agency)\. Be sure your research proposal follows the [application guidelines](https://science.gc.ca/site/science/en/interagency-research-funding/policies-and-guidelines/selecting-appropriate-federal-granting-agency#General) of the agency you are applying to\.

### Where to hold your award

An Undergraduate Student Research Award can be held at any eligible Canadian university and can allow you to expand your background training at other universities\. You must apply online at the institution where you would like to hold the USRA and must comply with the internal selection criteria of that institution and be selected by that university to hold one of their quotas of awards\. Once NSERC has approved your award for tenure at one particular institution, you may not transfer to another institution\.

Selected applications are submitted to NSERC by institutions through NSERC’s online system\. You cannot submit your USRA application directly to the agencies\. Currently, CIHR and SSHRC are exclusively for Black student researchers\.

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/undergraduate\-student\-research\-awards\-nserc\-cihr\-sshrc/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/#how-to-apply)

1.  Review the [Information for Students Handout](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/sites/facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/NSERC_USRA_2026-27_Information_for_Students_11.27.2025_AVP.pdf) \(pdf\) to understand the application process\.
2.  The [NSERC USRA program](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/UG-PC/USRA-BRPC_eng.asp) is administered and coordinated through the Faculty/School USRA Coordinators\. Each faculty establishes procedures for identifying and ranking applicants and setting deadlines\. Make sure you are aware of your [faculty’s deadlines](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/usra-faculty-school-application-details/) before applying\.
3.  Read through the [online instructions](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/OnlineServices-ServicesEnLigne/instructions/202/USRAExperience-BRPCExperience_eng.asp) for how to complete the forms\. The forms can be found on the [NSERC USRA website](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/UG-PC/USRA-BRPC_eng.asp)\. View additional resources:
    -   [Online Application Portal for Faculty Supervisors and Student Applicants](https://ebiz.nserc.ca/nserc_web/nserc_login_e.htm)
    -   [NSERC USRA Instructions for Completing Form 202](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/undergraduate-student-research-awards)
4.  Once you have found an eligible professor, you must submit Part 1 of the NSERC USRA application Form 202 in the [online application system](https://ebiz.nserc.ca/nserc_web/nserc_login_e.htm)\.
    -   If you’re a first\-time user, [register an account](https://ebiz.nserc.ca/nserc_web/nserc_register_e.htm)\.
    -   Forward your reference number to your supervisor so they can include it in their part of the application\.
5.  Your supervisor must submit Part II in the [online application system](https://ebiz.nserc.ca/nserc_web/nserc_login_e.htm) with your reference number\.
6.  You must also upload a copy of your [official transcript](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-transcript/) with the back page legend as part of your application\. 
7.  Once you submit your application, you must submit a copy of both your Part I and Part II forms, as well as your transcript, to your [Faculty, School, or Department USRA Coordinator](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/usra-faculty-school-application-details/) for review\.
    -   Your Faculty, School, or Department USRA Coordinator will be in touch with your proposed project supervisor about the outcome of the application\.
    -   NSERC will not accept hardcopy forms so the official application must be completed and submitted electronically\. The copies sent to the Faculty, School, or Department USRA Coordinator are only necessary for the internal review process\.

If you experience any technical issues with the NSERC online portal, please [contact NSERC](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/ContactUs-ContactezNous/index_eng.asp)\.

## How to contact professors[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/undergraduate\-student\-research\-awards\-nserc\-cihr\-sshrc/\#how\-to\-contact\-professors](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/#how-to-contact-professors)

You are encouraged to create your own research opportunity by contacting eligible professors you would like to work with and tell them about this program\. Many professors will be happy to talk to you about the opportunity to hire you at a subsidized wage\. If you find an interested professor, ask them to contact their USRA Coordinator for more information\.

Below are some tips for contacting professors:

-   Keep your emails short and to\-the\-point\. Read [helpful tips](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/sites/facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/NSERC_Approaching_Professors_to_Ask_about_Research_Opportunities_12.18.2025_AVP.pdf) \(pdf\) on approaching a professor, and browse the list of faculty supervisors and research areas\.
-   Know what you are going to say\. Be prepared to talk about your interests, knowledge areas, and lab or volunteer experience\. Familiarize yourself with the professor’s research subject so you can ask informed questions about it\. This will show that you are genuinely interested in helping with the research\.
-   Bring a copy of your resume\.
-   Read the [NSERC program rules](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-development-services/nserc-undergraduate-student-research-awards) for supervisors\. Make sure you understand these rules and application procedures\.
-   Regardless of whether you get the position or not, thank the professor for their time with a thank\-you email\.
