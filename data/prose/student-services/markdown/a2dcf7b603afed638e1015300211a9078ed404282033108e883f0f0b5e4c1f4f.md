## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/creative\-writing/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/creative-writing/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Creative Writing](https://creativewriting.ubc.ca/), you’ll learn the craft and process of writing through workshops led by experienced, working writers that make up the faculty\. You’ll develop important skills while writing in multiple genres\.

These skills may include:

-   Creating original works such as fiction, non\-fiction, poetry, plays, screenplays, lyric and graphic forms, and translations
-   Using effective planning, time management, problem solving, and leadership skills on various creative projects
-   Working knowledge and application of the professional standards and expectations for your chosen genres
-   Researching, understanding, and assessing various types of creative works
-   Analyzing artistic works clearly and concisely
-   Adapting content to a variety of forms or mediums
-   Providing and receiving constructive feedback on opinions and works
-   Crafting and telling compelling and creative stories

## Make the most of your program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/creative\-writing/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/creative-writing/#make-the-most-of-your-program)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities, develop your skillset, and help you clarify your values and interests\.

-   [BC Alliance for Arts \+ Culture job board](https://www.allianceforarts.com/job-board)  
    Search for part\-time and full\-time opportunities in BC across a variety of fields in the arts\.
-   [Brave New Play Rites Festival](https://www.bravenewplayrites.com/)  
    Attend the festival in Term 2 or collaborate with theatre students for an opportunity to produce your short play\.
-   [Creative Writing BFA group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/ubc.crwr.bfas/)  
    Join fellow creative writers for various events and make new friends\.
-   [Creative Writing co\-op success stories](https://artscoop.ubc.ca/success-stories/?title=&amp;industry=&amp;areas-of-study=creative-writing&amp;types-of-roles=&amp;locations=)  
    Learn about the experiences of past and present co\-op students from your program and others\.
-   [Departmental research opportunities](https://creativewriting.ubc.ca/people/)  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   [Preparation for a Career in Writing](https://creativewriting.ubc.ca/courses/)  
    Check out UBC courses on how to build a career in writing, such as CRWR 430 or CRWR 530\.
-   [PRISM international](http://prismmagazine.ca/)  
    Inquire about volunteer opportunities to support an online magazine focused on modern and contemporary literature\.
-   [Resources for writers](https://www.vpl.ca/guide/resources-writers)  
    Find local writing groups, contests, festivals and other resources for writers\.
-   [The Garden Statuary](http://www.thegardenstatuary.com/submissions)  
    Showcase your essays, poetry, photographs, and other work in UBC’s English Undergraduate Journal\.
-   [The Ubyssey](https://ubyssey.ca/pages/volunteer/)  
    Volunteer to write articles for this weekly student paper\. No previous experience required\.
-   [The Whole Cloth](https://wholecloth.substack.com/)  
    Join this experiment in deep listening where a poet reads an entire book of poems from start to finish\.
-   [UBC Creative Writing Program events](https://creativewriting.ubc.ca/events/event/)  
    Meet other students at readings, info sessions, and other programs\.
-   [Vancouver Writers Fest](https://writersfest.bc.ca/)  
    Check out the works of local and international writers or volunteer at a literary event\.
-   [Writers’ Exchange](https://vancouverwe.com/)  
    Volunteer to support literacy programs for kids in Vancouver\.
-   [Scripted Media Career Accelerator](mailto:crwr.scripted@ubc.ca?subject=Subscribe%20to%20Career%20Accelerator)  
    Email to subscribe to their mailing list to access curated career insights from a creative writing lens\.
-   [Young Adulting](https://youngadulting.ca/about/)  
    Read or contribute your own analyses of books for young readers\.
