## International Community Achievement Awards[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/awards\-international/\#international\-community\-achievement\-awards](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-international/#international-community-achievement-awards)

The International Community Achievement Award recognizes international students who contribute to our campus and communities while maintaining strong academic standing\.

Awards up to $10,000 each are available to continuing international undergraduate students who have demonstrated leadership in one or more of the following areas:

-   Community service
-   International engagement
-   Intercultural understanding
-   Promotion of diversity
-   Intellectual, artistic, or athletic pursuits
-   Other campus activities

## Eligibility requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/awards\-international/\#eligibility\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-international/#eligibility-requirements)

You must meet all the following requirements to apply:

-   You must be a continuing international student in a four\-year undergraduate degree program at UBC’s Vancouver campus\.
-   You must be studying on a Canadian study permit and paying international undergraduate tuition and fees\.
-   You must have been registered at the Vancouver campus for the previous Winter Session\.
-   You must be registered for the current Winter Session\. Students registered in a Go Global Program or Co\-operative Education are eligible to apply\.

To retain eligibility for international student awards, students should be continuing as an international student\. Change in citizenship status or tuition fee rate will affect award eligibility and may result in recovery of award funding\.

## Recommended awards[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/awards\-international/\#recommended\-awards](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-international/#recommended-awards)

Thanks to the generosity of external donors and UBC faculties, UBC offers many awards to undergraduate students after their first year of study\. These awards may be in the form of scholarships, prizes, fellowships, and more\.

Individual faculties are responsible for recommending their students for these awards\. Their recommendations are based on a range of factors, such as academic achievement, leadership, UBC involvement, and participation on varsity athletic teams\.

International students must pay the international rate for tuition in order to be considered for any award, scholarship, or funding opportunities for international students\.

[Get details](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/faculty-recommended-awards/)

## Tax information[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/awards\-international/\#tax\-information](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-international/#tax-information)

If you receive an award, you’re required to submit a T4A form at tax time\.

Make sure your [Social Insurance Number](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/taxes/social-insurance-number-sin/) \(SIN\) or your [Individual Tax Number](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/taxes/individual-tax-number-itn/) \(ITN\) is updated on Workday as soon as possible so UBC can process your T4A form\. 

If you haven’t applied for your SIN or ITN, do so as early as possible\. It can take 2 months to process your ITN\.
