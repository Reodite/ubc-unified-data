## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/mathematics/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/mathematics/#skills-youll-develop)

By studying [Mathematics](https://www.math.ubc.ca/undergraduate), you’ll learn how to take a complex problem and frame it in a logical way\. You’ll develop habits of rigorous, logical thinking and formal proof, as well as a toolkit of technical and computational skills\. A degree in Mathematics helps you describe and model all kinds of phenomena, from financial to physical, from biological to computational\.

These skills may include:

-   Data analysis and technical writing
-   Organizational interpretation
-   Deductive reasoning
-   Logical, systematic thought processes
-   Computer literacy
-   Advanced quantitative skills
-   Numerical computation
-   Technical skills in mathematical concepts, logic, and specialized software

Career opportunities vary widely across a range of fields including financial services, insurance, hardware and software design, health and pharmaceuticals, manufacturing, research, consulting, and education\.

Most importantly, a solid grounding in mathematics is evidence to employers that you can *think* rigorously, numerately, and creatively\.

## Make the most of your program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/mathematics/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/mathematics/#make-the-most-of-your-program)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities, develop your skillset, and help you clarify your values and interests\.

A slate of research, employment and outreach opportunities is outlined on the [Math Department webpage](https://www.math.ubc.ca/undergraduate)\.

-   [UBC Undergraduate Math Society](https://ums-ubc.com/)  
    Meet other Mathematics students through events and programs\.
-   [UBC Undergraduate Math Colloquium](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~fsl/UMC.html)  
    Attend talks about research in mathematics\. 
-   [Departmental research opportunities](https://www.math.ubc.ca/research/research-topics)  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   [UBC Mathematics outreach initiatives](http://outreach.math.ubc.ca/)  
    Interact with elementary and high school students through workshops and sessions\.
-   [Putnam math competition](http://www.math.ubc.ca/Links/Putnam/)  
    Test your mathematical understanding in the prestigious undergraduate math exam\. 
-   [Mitacs funding](https://www.mitacs.ca/en/programs)  
    Apply for up to $15,000 to fund a four\-month internship in your specific area of study\.
-   [Math job board](https://www.mathjobs.org/jobs/jobs)  
    Check out employment opportunities related to mathematics\.
