## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/central\-eastern\-northern\-european\-studies/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/central-eastern-northern-european-studies/#skills-youll-develop)

In the [Department of Central, Eastern and Northern European Studies](https://cenes.ubc.ca/), you’ll learn in a multicultural and multilingual environment with experts in media, literature, film, gender and queer studies, environmental humanities, linguistics, and translation\. You’ll explore Europe’s history and current challenges, and think about how you may help shape its future and its relationship with Canada\. You will also have the opportunity to study abroad for more hands\-on experience in the region\. Through this program, you will develop a range of critical communication skills to support your future career\.

These skills may include:

-   Spoken and written competency, listening and reading comprehension in Danish, German, Polish, Russian, Swedish, Ukrainian, and/or Yiddish language\(s\)
-   Research practices used to examine and assess issues relevant to the various regions, cultures, and histories of Central, Eastern, and Northern Europe
-   Theoretical approaches and methodologies related to Modern European Studies as well as German, Nordic, and Slavic and Eastern European Studies
-   Critical thinking and analysis of concepts while demonstrating intercultural awareness 
-   Written and verbal communication skills for different research platforms and formats 
-   Translation of original texts from and to the region’s languages
-   Public speaking and reasoning skills that acknowledge different cultural perspectives and traditions

## Make the most of your program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/central\-eastern\-northern\-european\-studies/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/central-eastern-northern-european-studies/#make-the-most-of-your-program)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities, develop your skillset, and help you clarify your values and interests\.

-   [Departmental events](https://cenes.ubc.ca/events/event/?date=upcoming-events&amp;topic=&amp;type=)  
    Network with Central, Eastern, and Northern European Studies \(CENES\) students and faculty members at a variety of events, both social and academic, throughout the year\.
-   [Ziegler Lecture Series](https://cenes.ubc.ca/news-events/ziegler-lecture-series/)  
    Annual lecture series featuring scholarly talks on current and historical topics related to German Studies\.
-   [Sawchen Lecture Series](https://cenes.ubc.ca/news-events/sawchen-lecture-series/)  
    Annual lecture series featuring scholarly talks on current and historical topics related to Slavic Studies\.
-   [CENES Involvement Opportunities](https://cenes.ubc.ca/undergraduate/opportunities/cenes-involvement-opportunities/)  
    Events such as German *Kaffeestunde* or *Plaudertreff*, Nordic *Fika*, and Slavic Tea are informal opportunities for language practice and engagement\.
-   [Departmental research opportunities](https://cenes.ubc.ca/research/)  
    Explore research opportunities for CENES students\.
-   [Research with faculty](https://cenes.ubc.ca/people/)  
    To work with faculty on their research, reach out directly to faculty members to inquire about potential openings\.
-   [UBC Augenblick](https://www.ubcaugenblick.com/)Develop and submit your research paper to the CENES undergraduate journal in German Studies\.
-   [CENES Student Advisory Council](https://cenes.ubc.ca/undergraduate/opportunities/cenes-involvement-opportunities/)  
    Submit your application to serve on the department’s Undergraduate Student Advisory Council to plan CENES’s student\-facing programming and professional development activities\.
-   [CENES Student Associations &amp; Clubs](https://cenes.ubc.ca/undergraduate/opportunities/student-associations-and-clubs/)  
    Get involved in social gatherings, conversations, and other exciting events led by student peers\.
-   [Go Global](https://cenes.ubc.ca/undergraduate/study-abroad/)  
    Find opportunities to study the languages and cultures of Central, Eastern, and Northern Europe abroad through Global Seminars, Go Global Exchange and Study Abroad programs\.
-   [Non\-UBC Affiliated Study Abroad Programs](https://cenes.ubc.ca/undergraduate/opportunities/experiential-learning/)  
    Check out a list of recommended exchange destinations, as well as study and work opportunities, in Central, Eastern, and Northern Europe\.
