## Eligibility requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/changing\-degree\-program\-or\-campus/\#eligibility\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/changing-degree-program-or-campus/#eligibility-requirements)

You must meet all of the following requirements to change your degree program at the same campus or switch to another campus:

-   Minimum [admission criteria](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/university-college-transfer/#section-1) for transfer students
-   [Specific requirements](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/university-college-transfer/#section-1) for the program you are planning to transfer to
-   Competitive academic performance described in [Post\-Secondary UBC Admission Requirements](http://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/university-college-transfer/)

## If you’re changing programs[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/changing\-degree\-program\-or\-campus/\#if\-youre\-changing\-programs](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/changing-degree-program-or-campus/#if-youre-changing-programs)

-   Make sure you submit all the documents required for your new program by the application deadline\. Some degree programs may require a portfolio, supplemental application, or Personal Profile\.
-   At least 50% of your required credits must be completed under your new program for you to graduate\.
-   Research the degree requirements for your intended program of study\. Degree program requirements are unique to each program, regardless of campus\.

### Course equivalencies between campuses

UBC courses may be eligible for credit towards a UBC degree program on either campus\. This means credits from a different campus will not be converted to transfer credit, but may be equivalent to credits for a similar course, also known as course equivalency\.

-   Courses that are determined to be equivalent between campuses may be used to satisfy course\-specific degree requirements\.
-   Courses that are not equivalent may be applied to degree requirements as discipline\-specific or general elective credit\.

Use the [https://www\.bctransferguide\.ca/](https://www.bctransferguide.ca/)[BC Transfer Guide](https://www.bctransferguide.ca/) to check if a UBC course is equivalent between campuses\.

If a course does not appear on the BC Transfer Guide, then it does not have an established equivalency and may be applied towards degree requirements as discipline\-specific or general elective credits\. Please speak to an Academic Advisor to find out how your credits may be applied\.

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/changing\-degree\-program\-or\-campus/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/changing-degree-program-or-campus/#how-to-apply)

January 15 is the general deadline to apply for a change of degree or campus for the upcoming Winter Session\. Be sure to check if your program has any additional degree\-specific deadlines\.

Visit the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) and log in with your CWL\. You can then select the “Re\-admission/Change Program” option and will be asked to follow next steps\.

Applying to change your degree program doesn’t mean giving up your seat in your current program\. You do not give up your current spot until you accept your offer to a new program\.

## If you receive a program or campus change offer[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/changing\-degree\-program\-or\-campus/\#if\-you\-receive\-a\-program\-or\-campus\-change\-offer](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/changing-degree-program-or-campus/#if-you-receive-a-program-or-campus-change-offer)

When you receive your offer on the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/), you will be provided with a specific deadline to accept your offer by\. You’ll be required to pay an acceptance deposit to accept your offer\. This deposit will be applied towards future tuition\.

For any degree planning questions in your new program, you can connect with [academic advising](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/)\.
