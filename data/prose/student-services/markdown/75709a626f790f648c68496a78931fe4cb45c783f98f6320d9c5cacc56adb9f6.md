## About sponsorship billing[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/sponsorship\-billing/information\-sponsored\-students/\#about\-sponsorship\-billing](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsored-students/#about-sponsorship-billing)

Some UBC students have a portion of their UBC fees paid by a sponsor, such as an Indigenous community, employer, or government agency\. This is called sponsorship billing\.

To have an organization sponsor your studies at UBC, they must meet the required criteria and apply before the start of the term\. Please notify the Sponsorship Billing Office immediately if there are changes to your sponsoring agency or the coverage amount\.

### Housing sponsorship

Enrolment Services does not coordinate sponsorship billing for UBC\-managed residences\. If your sponsor has indicated they can support you with your on\-campus residence fees, please email the Vancouver Student Residence office at [information@housing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:information@housing.ubc.ca) or the Okanagan Student Residence office at [information@housing\.okanagan\.ubc\.ca](mailto:information@housing.okanagan.ubc.ca)\.

Find out more about [student housing sponsorships](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/student-housing-sponsorship/) at UBC\.

## Sponsorship invoicing[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/sponsorship\-billing/information\-sponsored\-students/\#sponsorship\-invoicing](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsored-students/#sponsorship-invoicing)

We will invoice your sponsor only for the tuition and fees they agree to pay, up to the coverage limit specified for the academic session or term they selected\.

Your sponsor\(s\) will receive an invoice after each Winter Term’s [course change date](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates/), also known as the add/drop deadline\. That way, an accurate invoice will be generated after you finalize your course schedule\. 

If you are taking summer courses, UBC will only issue one invoice in mid\-July for both Summer Session terms\. If you are registered for Summer Term 1 courses, you will not be placed on financial hold as long as an active sponsorship contract is in place by the term’s tuition deadline\.

### Invoice delivery times

-   **Winter Session Term 1**: late September
-   **Winter Session Term 2**: late January
-   **Summer Session**: mid\-July

### Books and supplies

If your sponsor has indicated that they will cover books and supplies, the UBC Bookstore will generate an invoice for that amount\. Bookstore sponsorship is not available for international sponsors\. Email the Sponsorship Billing Office at [ubc\.sponsorship@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.sponsorship@ubc.ca) or the UBC Bookstore at [ar\.bookstore@ubc\.ca](mailto:ar.bookstore@ubc.ca) if you have any questions\.

## Your responsibilities as a sponsored student[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/sponsorship\-billing/information\-sponsored\-students/\#your\-responsibilities\-as\-a\-sponsored\-student](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsored-students/#your-responsibilities-as-a-sponsored-student)

### Communicate with your sponsor

As a sponsored student, you must contact your sponsor to negotiate the terms and length of your sponsorship agreement\. UBC will not do this on your behalf\.

Please also be mindful that you will be responsible for informing your sponsor\(s\) of any changes to your situation which may affect your level of sponsorship \(e\.g\., awards\) and any fees that your sponsor does not cover, including but not limited to, interest charges, late fees, U\-Pass, and the AMS/GSS or SUO Health and Dental plan\.

### Pay other required fees

You are responsible for paying any charges and student fees not covered by your sponsor, such as your [student health and dental coverage](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/support-services/health-dental/)[,](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/support-services/health-dental/) by the published tuition due dates in Workday\.

Any late payment charges you accumulate before your sponsorship contract is authorized will not be reversed\. Sponsors typically do not cover late fees, unless the fees result from their own delayed submission, in which case they may agree to cover the penalties\. Learn more about [paying tuition and student fees](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/paying-tuition/)\.

If your sponsor doesn’t pay the invoice by the deadline, or if there is an outstanding balance on the amount you are responsible for, your student account will be placed on [financial hold](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/policies-fees#17924)\. You won’t be able to register for new courses, view grades, audit a course, or order transcripts while on hold\. You’ll also be charged [late payment fees](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/late-payment-fees/) to your account\.

Please contact an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) if you’re unsure about the fees that you owe\.

### Review your sponsorship contract

You are strongly encouraged to review the terms and coverage limits set out by your sponsor so you understand what your sponsor is covering and what you owe if your sponsor isn’t covering all charges\. You may also request this information from your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/), as it will not be available in Workday\.

### Academic information

If your sponsor requires your academic information, such as transcripts, you are responsible for providing it\. Learn how to [order an official transcript](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-transcript/) for a small fee, or order an unofficial transcript free of charge by logging into the UBC Official Documents tool \(ODMT\)\.

### Submit your student declaration

You must complete the [Sponsor Student Declaration](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_9G3ZP9bQHIjpNAO) as soon as possible to confirm the sponsorship contract\. The declaration only needs to be completed once per program\.

## Granting third\-party authorization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/sponsorship\-billing/information\-sponsored\-students/\#granting\-third\-party\-authorization](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsored-students/#granting-third-party-authorization)

UBC complies with the British Columbia [Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96165_00) \(the “FIPPA”\)\. As a result, UBC requires your permission to disclose details of your financial account to your sponsor\.

### Add your sponsor\(s\) information to Workday

1.  Log in to [Workday Student](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/) and click on the icon on the top right of the screen\. 
2.  Click on “View Profile”, then “Personal”, then “Additional Data”\. 
3.  Complete the details on the “Third\-Party Authorization Form” to authorize your sponsor to receive applicable information about you\.
4.  Select “Yes” for the statement “I acknowledge that I have read, understand and agree to the Terms of Use and Agreement for Third Party Authorization\.”
5.  Type your sponsor organization into the “Name of the person you are authorizing” section\.
6.  From the dropdown options, select “Sponsorship Billing” in the “Person’s relationship to me”\.
7.  Select “Yes” to the statement, “I consent to the release of information pertaining to my financial assistance and award status, including but not limited to student loans, bursaries, scholarships and financial need\. As related to this status, registration, grades, fees and enrolment status may also be released\.” 
8.  Click “Save\.”

By entering into a sponsorship agreement and setting up third\-party authorization, you agree to UBC sharing information from your student account directly with your sponsor\. Information that can be shared with your sponsor includes, but is not limited to, your registered courses, program information, tuition and fee assessments, awards, bursaries or scholarships, grade information, and any other items related to your studies\. 

## Opting out of the AMS Health and Dental Plan[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/sponsorship\-billing/information\-sponsored\-students/\#opting\-out\-of\-the\-ams\-health\-and\-dental\-plan](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsored-students/#opting-out-of-the-ams-health-and-dental-plan)

If you have extended health and dental coverage elsewhere, you can opt out of the UBC Vancouver [AMS/GSS Health and Dental plan](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/about-us/finances/opt-out-opt-in/) or the UBC Okanagan [SUO Health and Dental plan](https://www.studentcare.ca/rte/en/IHaveAPlan_UBCOkanaganUniversityofBritishColumbiaStudentsUnionOkanaganUBCSUO_ChangeofCoverage_OptOuts)\. If you do not opt out before the deadline, you will be responsible for paying these fees by the deadline\.

## Awards[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/sponsorship\-billing/information\-sponsored\-students/\#awards](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsored-students/#awards)

Domestic Indigenous students sponsored by their Indigenous community who receive a monetary award through UBC are eligible to keep their full award funding\. The student is responsible for notifying their sponsor or education coordinator of any awards they may receive\.

Sponsored graduate students are not eligible for the following UBC awards:

-   UBC International Tuition Award
-   UBC International Doctoral Fellowship
-   Four\-Year Doctoral Tuition Award
-   Four\-Year Fellowship Tuition Award 
-   Faculty of Science PhD Tuition Award 
-   Chemistry PhD Tuition Award 
-   Graduate Covid Program Delay Tuition Award 
-   Doctoral Recruitment Fellowship Tuition Award

UBC Vancouver graduate students should contact the [Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/contact-us) for more information about awards\. UBC Okanagan students can contact the [College of Graduate Studies](https://gradstudies.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/)\.
