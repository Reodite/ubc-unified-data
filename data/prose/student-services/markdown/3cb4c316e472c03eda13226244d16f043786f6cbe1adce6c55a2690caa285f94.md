## How Academic Advisors can help[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/academic\-learning\-resources/academic\-advising/\#how\-academic\-advisors\-can\-help](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/#how-academic-advisors-can-help)

Talk to an Academic Advisor for help with the following:

-   [Course planning](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/register-courses/#plan-your-courses) and degree requirements
-   Exploring academic and learning resources
-   Understanding [academic concessions](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-concessions/)
-   Confirming how [transfer credits](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer-credits/) can be applied towards your degree
-   Checking graduation requirements

## Program advising for specific students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/academic\-learning\-resources/academic\-advising/\#program\-advising\-for\-specific\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/#program-advising-for-specific-students)

Students in certain groups are encouraged to connect with their program advising:

-   If you’re a graduate student, reach out to your [graduate program advisor](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/program-advising)\. 
-   Students in Non\-Degree studies can contact [Enrolment Services](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\.
-   Indigenous students can find a list of program advisors on the [Indigenous Portal](https://fnhl.ubc.ca/students/student-resources/program-advisors/)\.
