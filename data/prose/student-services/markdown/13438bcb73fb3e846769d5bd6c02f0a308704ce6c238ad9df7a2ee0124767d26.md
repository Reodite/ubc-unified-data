## About the scholarship[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/rhodes\-scholarship/\#about\-the\-scholarship](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/rhodes-scholarship/#about-the-scholarship)

The [Rhodes Scholarships](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/scholarships/the-rhodes-scholarship/) were established in 1902 to bring outstanding students from across the world to study at the University of Oxford in the interests of promoting international understanding and public service\. 11 Canadians are selected each year to join a class of over 100 Scholars from across the world\.

The Scholarships cover all approved fees and living expenses for 2 years in a second bachelor’s degree or a graduate degree\. For a list of programs covered under the Rhodes Scholarship, please refer to the [Conditions of Tenure](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/media/mcpnrffh/conditions-of-tenure.pdf)\.

## Before you apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/rhodes\-scholarship/\#before\-you\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/rhodes-scholarship/#before-you-apply)

Eligible candidates for the Rhodes Scholarships are strongly encouraged to review the [selection criteria](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/scholarships/selection-criteria/)\. Successful Rhodes Scholars demonstrate outstanding intellect, character, leadership and commitment to service\. 

Please research the academic programs offered at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate) before starting your application\. A competitive applicant is able to express how their chosen program of study fits into their academic ambitions and trajectory\.

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/rhodes\-scholarship/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/rhodes-scholarship/#how-to-apply)

If you meet the eligibility requirements defined by the [Rhodes Scholarship Trust](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/scholarships/applications/) and the UBC Rhodes Scholarship Selection Committee, you will be invited to attend an information session in June\.

To apply to the Rhodes Scholarship competition, eligible candidates must seek support from their university in the form of an institutional certification\. To request an institutional certification, all applicants must submit an online application to the UBC Rhodes Scholarship Selection Committee\. The online application will consist of a personal statement, an academic statement of study, and a curriculum vitae\. All applicants will be emailed regardless of the outcome\.

The UBC Selection Committee will shortlist the strongest candidates for an interview\. Only successful candidates from the interviews will receive an institutional certification to apply to the one of the Rhodes Scholarship Regional Committees in Canada\. Each Regional Committee will select their Rhodes Finalist\(s\)\.

Please do not contact the President’s Office for an endorsement letter or an institutional certification as all arrangements are made through the UBC Rhodes Scholarship Selection committee\.

This application process is for eligible candidates applying to the Rhodes Scholarships for Canada\. If you are applying to another Rhodes constituency, please email [rhodes\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:rhodes.inquiry@ubc.ca) as the application procedures and deadlines may be different\.
