## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/sociology/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/sociology/#skills-youll-develop)

While [studying Sociology](https://sociology.ubc.ca/), you’re learning to investigate the social ties between private and public, agency and structure, or individual and society\. You’ll develop important skills to critically apply a sociological perspective to your own life and to the social worlds in which you live\.

These skills may include:

-   Analytical skills applied to understanding the constructed nature of social life
-   Qualitative and quantitative research, data collection and interviewing, statistical interpretation, synthesis of facts, and presentation of findings
-   Collaboration and leadership skills gained through hands\-on fieldwork experiences
-   Time management of projects throughout planning, organization, and implementation stages
-   Problem solving and presentation of alternative solutions to contemporary social issues
-   Clear and concise written communication that incorporates an alertness of how power operates across different levels of society 
-   Critical thinking and public speaking skills that demonstrate an ability to see issues from a variety of perspectives

## Make the most of your program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/sociology/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/sociology/#make-the-most-of-your-program)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities, develop your skillset, and help you clarify your values and interests\.

-   [Departmental events](https://sociology.ubc.ca/events/event/)  
    Network with Sociology students and faculty members at a variety of events\.
-   [Departmental research opportunities](https://sociology.ubc.ca/people/)  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   [Sojourners: Undergraduate Journal of Sociology](http://blogs.ubc.ca/sojourners/)  
    Showcase your research by submitting papers to be published in a peer and faculty\-reviewed journal\.
-   [UBC Sociology Students’ Association](https://blogs.ubc.ca/ubcssa/)  
    Meet other Sociology students through events and programs\.
-   [UBC Sociology student support](https://sociology.ubc.ca/undergraduate/opportunities/mentoring-and-career-support/)  
    Attend various programs and events related to mentorship, peer support, and applying to graduate and professional programs\.
-   [Sociology co\-op success stories](https://artscoop.ubc.ca/success-stories/?title=&amp;industry=&amp;areas-of-study=sociology&amp;types-of-roles=&amp;locations=)  
    Learn about the experiences of past and present co\-op students from your program and others\.
-   [Experiential opportunities](https://sociology.ubc.ca/undergraduate/opportunities/experiential-learning/)  
    Check out field school as well as local and international study opportunities for Sociology students\.
-   [Teaching assistantships](https://sociology.ubc.ca/about/job-opportunities/)  
    Apply for an undergraduate teaching assistant opportunity with the Department of Sociology\.
-   [Distinguished Speaker Series](https://sociology.ubc.ca/news-events/speaker-series/)  
    Meet with fellow Sociology students and faculty to learn about topics from a variety of guest speakers\.
-   [International fieldwork opportunities](https://www.archaeological.org/programs/professionals/fieldwork/afob/)  
    Find volunteer and field school opportunities around the world\.
-   [Volunteer and internship opportunities](https://sociology.ubc.ca/undergraduate/opportunities/)  
    Browse various resources to gain local and international experience\.
