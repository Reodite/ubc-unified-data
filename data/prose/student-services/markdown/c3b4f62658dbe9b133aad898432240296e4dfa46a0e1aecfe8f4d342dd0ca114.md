## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/integrated\-sciences/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/integrated-sciences/#skills-youll-develop)

During your [Integrated Sciences](http://www.intsci.ubc.ca/) degree, you’re envisioning and designing an interdisciplinary area of study according to your goals\. You’ll develop a unique set of skills through faculty and peer mentorship, self\-directed learning, and field experiences\.

These skills may include:

-   Persuasive and evidence\-based proposal writing
-   Faculty and peer mentor collaboration
-   Design, development, and adjustment of multi\-year degree plans
-   Communication across diverse disciplines
-   Self\-management and initiative
-   Application of systems\-thinking to sustainability issues and other complex global problems
-   Collaborative research skills with multidisciplinary teams

## Make the most of your specialization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/integrated\-sciences/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-specialization](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/integrated-sciences/#make-the-most-of-your-specialization)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[Integrated Sciences Student Association](http://ubc-issa.weebly.com/)**  
    Meet other UBC students with similar interests through events and programs\.
-   **[UBC Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/isci)**  
    Gain work experience between study terms\.
-   **[Peer Mentoring Program](https://intsci.ubc.ca/advising/peer-mentoring-program)**  
    Find or become a mentor for other UBC Integrated Sciences students\.
-   **[UBC Life Sciences Institute](http://lsi.ubc.ca/news-events/events/)**  
    Discover research opportunities, events, programs, and student competitions in life sciences\.
-   **[Faculty of Medicine summer research](https://www.med.ubc.ca/current-learners/summer-student-research-program/)**  
    Get experience in undergraduate medical research at UBC\.
-   **[UBC Pathology and Laboratory Medicine summer program](https://pathology.ubc.ca/education-resource/summer-student-fellowship-program/)**  
    Apply for funding and propose your research project to gain clinical experience in laboratory medicine\.
-   **[BC Children’s Hospital research](https://bcchr.ca/ssrp)**  
    Participate in an undergraduate research project related to child and family health\.
-   **[Online tutorials and training in ethics](https://ethics.research.ubc.ca/education-training/online-tutorials-training)**  
    Develop your understanding of research ethics and integrity through online training and tutorials\.
-   [UBC Medical Journal](https://ubcmj.med.ubc.ca/submissions/)  
    Submit your academic research, case reports, reviews, commentaries, and news or letters for publication\.
-   **[Student Biotechnology Network](https://www.facebook.com/thesbn/)**  
    Attend events, meet professionals, and receive mentorship from innovative industry leaders\.
