Part of your success at UBC involves proactive financial planning\. Review the resources below and connect with an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) if you would like guidance on how to start\.

## Understand your costs[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/\#understand\-your\-costs](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/#understand-your-costs)

### Tuition and student fees

The first step is to understand how much a school year will cost you and what the related expenses are\. Knowing your costs will give you the foundation to build your financial plan\. Start by understanding the costs for tuition, student fees, and books:

-   [Undergraduate student tuition and fees](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/undergraduate-tuition-fees/)
-   [Graduate student tuition and fees](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/tuition-fees-cost-living/graduate-tuition-fees)

### Living costs

Next, consider how much it will cost to live in Vancouver\.

-   If you are living on campus, you’ll need to cover [residence fees and a meal plan](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/)\.
-   If you’re off\-campus, you’ll want to consider rent, groceries, and utilities\.

Be sure to account for your personal expenses, such as your phone bill, subscriptions, and social expenses\. If it’s in your financial plan, you won’t be caught off\-guard\. Use the [cost calculator](http://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/cost#cost-calculator) to help you calculate your first year finances\.

If you’re a graduate student, use the [graduate cost calculator](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/tuition-fees-cost-living/cost-living) to develop your financial plan\.

## Know your resources[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/\#know\-your\-resources](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/#know-your-resources)

After determining your educational costs, you’ll need to find resources to cover those costs\.

Domestic and US students may be eligible for [government student loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/)\.

International students must ensure they have sufficient financial resources for their studies at UBC\. Unless you have received a scholarship, plan to cover the full cost of your degree\. 

Learn more about [financial assistance programs](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/financial-support-options/) for students studying at UBC\.

## Map your timeline[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/\#map\-your\-timeline](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/#map-your-timeline)

Enrolment Services is committed to helping you, but it is your responsibility to meet important deadlines\. Review key [dates and deadlines](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/dates-deadlines/)\.

You’re strongly recommended to apply for your [student loan](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/) by mid\-July\.

## Build a financial plan[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/\#build\-a\-financial\-plan](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/#build-a-financial-plan)

Every student should have a financial plan\. Once you’ve gathered the information above, you’ll be ready to make one and find what works best for you\.

These tools and tips can help you get started on your financial plan:

-   [How to budget](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/budget/)
-   [Spending Plan Template](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/financial_planning_spending_plan_template.xlsx) \(Excel\)
-   [Budget\-Planning Worksheet](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/ubc_budget_planning_worksheet.pdf) \(pdf\)
-   [Sticking to the Plan](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sticking_to_the_plan.pdf) \(pdf\)
-   [Student Loans and Graduation](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/financial_planning_repayment_graduation.pdf) \(pdf\)

## Maintaining your financial plan[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/\#maintaining\-your\-financial\-plan](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/#maintaining-your-financial-plan)

Once you have a plan, it’s important to use it\. Sometimes this is easier said than done\. If it’s not working, making adjustments as needed is okay\. Stay on top of your loan obligations and be prepared for your [loan repayment](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/repaying-my-student-loans/)\.

If you’re facing an unexpected [financial emergency](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/financial-support-options/financial-emergencies/) or experiencing a significant change in your financial situation, contact an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to discuss your options\.

## Financial Wellness workshops[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/\#financial\-wellness\-workshops](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/#financial-wellness-workshops)

During the summer months, Enrolment Services Advisors host free Financial Wellness workshops for students who are new to UBC\. Other workshops open to all students are available throughout the year\. Browse upcoming sessions and register through the [event schedule](https://events.ubc.ca/organizer/enrolment-services/?eventDisplay=past)\.
