## About the Provincial Attestation Letter[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/ubc\-provincial\-attestation\-letter/\#about\-the\-provincial\-attestation\-letter](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/#about-the-provincial-attestation-letter)

Many new\-to\-UBC students need a Provincial Attestation Letter \(PAL\) before applying for a study permit\.

A [Provincial Attestation Letter](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/provincial-attestation-letter.html) \(PAL\) is a document which confirms an international student has a spot in the provincial allocation for study permits and is included in the yearly federal cap to submit a study permit application\. 

UBC expects to receive sufficient PALs for all newly admitted undergraduate degree\-seeking students\.

### Levels of study

Whether your application requires a PAL will depend on the correct [level of study](https://gazette.gc.ca/rp-pr/p1/2025/2025-01-18/html/notice-avis-eng.html) for your program\. There are 2 levels of study in relation to the PAL requirement:

1.  **Graduate level**: Master’s degree and Doctoral degree students are exempt from the PAL requirement\.
2.  **Undergraduate level**: All other programs, including bachelor’s degrees, undergraduate certificates and diplomas, graduate certificates and diplomas, Visiting International Research students, and unclassified studies are considered to be at the Undergraduate level and will require a PAL in most cases\.

### Additional required documents

In addition to the Provincial Attestation Letter, you will need [further documents](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-documents-required-study-permit/) from UBC for your study permit application: a Letter of Admission and/or a Letter of Enrolment\.

## Who needs a PAL?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/ubc\-provincial\-attestation\-letter/\#who\-needs\-a\-pal](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/#who-needs-a-pal)

As of Jan 22, 2025, all international students at the Undergraduate level outside Canada \(except [Go Global exchange](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange) students\) and most new\-to\-UBC students submitting a study permit application from inside Canada must include a valid Provincial Attestation Letter \(PAL\) with their study permit application\.

The following groups of students now require a PAL: 

-   Students changing institutions, such as from high school to UBC or from other post\-secondary institutions in Canada to UBC\. Check the [latest updates](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/#latest-updates) on “changing institutions” for additional information\. 
-   Students who have a parent holding a study or work permit 
-   Students who are diplomats or who are a dependent of a diplomat 
-   Visiting students, including [Visiting International Research Students](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/visiting-international-research-students)
-   Work permit holders
-   Students whose study permit applications were refused
-   Students who are applying to restore their study permit

## Who does not need a PAL?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/ubc\-provincial\-attestation\-letter/\#who\-does\-not\-need\-a\-pal](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/#who-does-not-need-a-pal)

You will not need a PAL for your study permit application if you meet **one** of the following criteria:

-   As of Jan 1, 2026, you do not need a PAL if you are applying for a study permit for a degree\-granting graduate program at the Master’s or Doctoral level\.
    -   This exemption does not apply if you are applying for a study permit as a Visiting International Research Student at the graduate level\.
-   You are applying to extend your study permit to continue in the same [level of study](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/#level-of-study) at UBC\. Examples include:
    -   You applied for your study permit with a UBC Letter of Admission for an undergraduate degree, and you are extending your study permit to continue working towards your undergraduate degree at UBC\.
    -   If you took time away from studies at UBC and/or studied at another institution \(other than on a UBC Go Global exchange\), contact [International Student Advising](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0J8VRJgJQkuO2b4) for support\. 
-   Exchange students coming to UBC through [Go Global](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange)\.
-   You are eligible for a [temporary public policy](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/mandate/policies-operational-instructions-agreements/public-policies.html) for citizens of certain countries and submit your study permit application before the policy expires\.

Check IRCC’s page on who is [exempt from the PAL](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/provincial-attestation-letter.html) for the complete list\.

## Who may need a PAL in the future[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/ubc\-provincial\-attestation\-letter/\#who\-may\-need\-a\-pal\-in\-the\-future](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/#who-may-need-a-pal-in-the-future)

The following groups can begin their program without applying for a study permit and needing a PAL, but may require a PAL in their study permit application in the future\.

### Changing levels of study at UBC

If you applied for your study permit with a UBC Letter of Admission, you could begin your next program at a different [level of study](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/#level-of-study) with the same study permit, so long as it’s still [valid](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/#maintaining-your-student-status)\.

For example, you are a UBC undergraduate student who will begin a Master’s degree at UBC [within 150 days](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/taking-time-away-school-international/) of completing your first program\. 

Master’s or Doctoral degree students changing to Undergraduate, Graduate Certificate, Graduate Diploma, or unclassified studies will require a PAL\.

If you took time away from studies at UBC and/or studied at another institution \(other than on a UBC Go Global exchange\), contact [International Student Advising](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0J8VRJgJQkuO2b4) for support\.

### Work permit holders without a study permit

If you are a work permit holder who is eligible to study without a study permit under the [temporary policy for workers](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/permit/temporary/workers-study-without-study-permit.html), you do not need a PAL at this time to continue your studies with your work permit\.

However, if you need more time to finish your program beyond your eligibility for the temporary policy, you must [apply for your study permit](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/extend-study-permit/) as soon as possible\. Unless you are studying in a degree program at the Master’s or Doctoral level, you will require a PAL for your application\.

Once you are no longer eligible for the temporary policy, you must receive your study permit before you can resume your studies\.

## If you need a PAL[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/ubc\-provincial\-attestation\-letter/\#if\-you\-need\-a\-pal](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/#if-you-need-a-pal)

### Confirm your personal details as soon as possible

Please ensure your personal details \(name, date of birth, and address\) in UBC’s systems are correct\. If needed, you must request a change before you accept your offer, since this information will be included on your PAL\. Once issued, the details on your PAL cannot be changed and the PAL cannot be reissued\.

If your personal details on your PAL are not correct, you will need to address this in a letter of explanation as part of your study permit application\. Contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support\.

**

Your address

**

When you are considering whether to use a current address at the time of application or your permanent address, you should be consistent and use the same address which you will have continued access to\. The address on your Letter of Admission, PAL and study permit application should be the same\.  

Not all address details you provide to UBC will appear on the PAL\. The information from address line 2, the postal code, and the province/state will not be included on the PAL\. This will not impact your application\.

**

Your name

**

Make sure the name that you provided in your UBC profile matches your full legal first name and last name as they appear on your passport\. Your name on your UBC Letter of Admission, PAL and study permit application form should be the same\.  

If your passport has a separate field for middle name\(s\) and you enter your middle name separately in your UBC profile, it would show up on your UBC Letter of Admission, but not on your PAL since PALs do not include middle names\. This will not impact your application\.

#### How to check and update your personal details

-   **Undergraduate degree students**: Log into the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/), click *Edit Profile,* then select “Contact Summary” to confirm your personal details\. You can update your address in the system if needed\. To update your name or date of birth, email [ubc\.pal@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.pal@ubc.ca)\.
-   **All other students**: find your situation under “[How and when to get your PAL](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/#how-and-when-to-get-your-pal)”\.

### PAL Validity

Your PAL only needs to be valid at the time you submit your study permit application and does not need to be valid when you travel to Canada or when you begin your program\.

PALs issued in 2025 expired on **Dec 31, 2025**, at **11:59 pm** Eastern Time \(ET\)\. PALs issued in 2026 will expire on **Dec, 31, 2026** at **11:59 pm** Eastern Time \(ET\)\.

Study permit applications are based on Coordinated Universal Time \(UTC\), not local time zones, so be sure to apply at least one day before your PAL expires\.

### Changing UBC programs or campuses

If you applied to multiple programs at UBC or to both UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan campuses, and then you decide to change programs or campuses after you accept your offer, you do **not** need a new PAL, since UBC has one Designated Learning Institution \(DLI\) number\.

New non\-degree seeking students studying 6 months or less

**

This section applies to one\-term exchange students through [Go Global](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange), one\-term [UBC\-TEC de Monterrey Joint Academic Program](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/tec-de-monterrey-ubc-joint-academic-program) students, [VIRS](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/visiting-international-research-students), access studies, [visiting students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-visiting/), and [unclassified students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/) studying for 6 months or less\.

If you will study in Canada for 6 months or less, you do not need to apply for a study permit and do not need a PAL, but you’ll likely need to apply for a travel document to come to Canada as a visitor\.

Most visitors are allowed to stay in Canada for 6 months from the day they enter Canada\. You can study in Canada without a study permit as a visitor if **both** of the following applies to you:

-   Your entire program of studies will be completed within 6 months or less\.
-   Your program will be completed within your first 6\-month visitor period\.

Therefore, if you will enter and leave Canada within 6 months and complete your studies within your first 6\-month visitor period, a study permit and a PAL are not required\. 

If you would like to stay longer beyond the 6 months, you can apply for a [Visitor Record](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/extend-stay.html) after arriving in Canada to extend your stay as a visitor, however, your studies must be completed within your first 6\-month period\.

You must apply for and receive a travel document before you can travel to Canada\. You should **apply as soon as you receive your Letter of Acceptance** from Go Global or Enrolment Services\. Depending on [your citizenship](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/entry-requirements-country.html), you need one of the following to travel to Canada as a visitor:

-   [Electronic Travel Authorization \(eTA\)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta.html)
-   [Temporary Resident Visa \(TRV\)](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/temporary-resident-visa-trv/)

Contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) if you need support with your TRV\.

US citizens and US lawful permanent residents do not need an eTA or TRV\.

Check the [Travelling to Canada guide](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/) for recommended documents to bring and other considerations\.

New non\-degree seeking students studying for more than 6 months

This section applies to [unclassified](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/), [access studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-access-studies/), [visiting students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-visiting/), and two\-term [UBC TEC de Monterrey Joint Academic Program](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/tec-de-monterrey-ubc-joint-academic-program) students\.

If your program of studies is 6 months or less, you do not require a study permit—read the section above for “non\-degree seeking students”\.

If your program of studies is more than 6 months, you will need both your Letter of Admission and your undergraduate level Provincial Attestation Letter \(PAL\) before applying for your study permit\.

**Unclassified, access studies, and visiting students**

1.  **Confirm your personal information in your UBC student record**\.  
    Log into the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/), click *Edit Profile* and select “*Contact Summary”* to confirm that your name matches the name on your passport, and that your home address and date of birth are correct\. You can update your address in the system, if needed\. To update your name or date of birth, email [ubc\.pal@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.pal@ubc.ca)\.
2.  **Accept your offer of admission\.  
    **Learn how to [accept your offer](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/admitted/your-offer/) and pay your deposit
3.  **Request your Provincial Attestation Letter  
    **Contact [ubc\.pal@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.pal@ubc.ca) to discuss next steps\. PALs are limited in supply\. Each applicant’s request will be reviewed on a case\-by\-case basis\.
4.  **Download your Letter of Admission from UBC\.  
    **Log into the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/), click *Admissions*, then select ”*Letter of Admission”*\.
5.  **Apply for your study permit\.  
    **Review our [initial study permit tutorial](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/apply-initial-study-permit/) to prepare your application\.

**Two\-term UBC\-TEC de Monterrey Joint Academic Program students**

1.  **Confirm your personal information in your UBC student record**\.  
    Check that your name matches the name on your passport and that your home address and date of birth are correct in your profile in the [Go Global Student Application Portal](https://goglobal.students.ubc.ca/ggapplications/s/login/) and on your Letter of Acceptance \(LOA\)\. If you need to correct any information, you **must** notify Go Global within **5 business days** of receiving the email notification about your admission and **before** accepting your offer of admission\.
2.  **Download your UBC Letter of Admission\.**  
    You can find the letter in the [Go Global Student Application Portal\.](https://goglobal.students.ubc.ca/ggapplications)
3.  **Accept your offer of admission**\.  
    Log into the [Applicant Service Centre](https://broker.id.ubc.ca/auth/realms/slid/protocol/openid-connect/auth?scope=openid&amp;state=69cZnqo2wOkddmhRNc-bIHD76djeW_oGa-gKVk2jLPo.CJpXeKwiNh0.8gtT8ngjR0qqHlXcsPYsUg&amp;response_type=code&amp;client_id=client_oidc_idb0_prd&amp;redirect_uri=https%3A%2F%2Fbroker.id.ubc.ca%2Fauth%2Frealms%2Fidb0%2Fbroker%2FSLID%2Fendpoint&amp;nonce=PMiQ9HFZ9FSPBcg_nKLeCg) and accept your offer before the deadline\.
4.  **Get your Provincial Attestation Letter**\.  
    If you will study in Canada as a two\-term TEC\-UBC Joint Academic Program student, Go Global will contact you regarding your PAL about 2 weeks after you accept your offer of admission\.
5.  **Apply for your study permit**\.  
    Review our [initial study permit tutorial](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/about-initial-study-permits/tutorial-apply-initial-study-permit-overview/) to prepare your application\.

**

Current and returning UBC students

**

If you cannot return to Canada before your current study permit expires, you will need to apply for a new study permit from outside Canada and wait for your application to be processed before returning to Canada\. In this case, you will need a PAL in addition to a current Letter of Enrolment\. You are exempt from this requirement if you are enrolled in a Master’s or Doctoral degree program\.

1.  **Confirm your personal information in your UBC student record**\.  
    Check that your name matches the name on your passport, and that your home address and date of birth are correct in [Workday](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/)\. If you need to update any information, check how to [change your personal information](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/)\.
2.  **Request your UBC Letter of Enrolment\.  
    **Contact an [Academic Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) in your faculty to request a Letter of Enrolment and make sure the letter indicates when you will resume studies, as well as your expected program completion date\. Use a Letter of Enrolment issued within the past 30 days – not your Letter of Admission from when you were first accepted to UBC\.
3.  **Contact International Student Advising\.  
    **Send a [webform](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0J8VRJgJQkuO2b4) to share your document expiry dates, when you will resume studies, if you transferred to UBC from a Canadian high school/post\-secondary institution, and your travel plans\.
4.  **Wait for your Provincial Attestation Letter\.  
    **If a PAL is required, International Student Advising will request it for you and, upon approval by the PAL team, it will be emailed to you directly from the UBC PAL Team\.
5.  **Apply for your study permit**\.  
    Review our [initial study permit tutorial](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/apply-initial-study-permit/) to prepare your application\. Be sure to include your recently issued Letter of Enrolment and UBC PDF [transcript](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-transcript/) in your application\.

## If you are inside Canada[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/ubc\-provincial\-attestation\-letter/\#if\-you\-are\-inside\-canada](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/ubc-provincial-attestation-letter/#if-you-are-inside-canada)

**

Current UBC students in all programs

**

Plan ahead if your documents expire soon and you have [travel plans](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/travelling-outside-canada/)\. In general, it’s best to extend your study permit and [Temporary Resident Visa](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/temporary-resident-visa-trv/) \(if you need one\) inside Canada before you travel\. It’s also possible to return and [extend your study permit](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/extend-study-permit/) from inside Canada before your study permit expires\. Contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support with planning your document extensions and travel plans\.

You will need a **current UBC Letter of Enrolment** for your study permit extension, not your initial Letter of Admission to UBC\. Learn how to apply for your study permit extension and how to get your Letter of Enrolment by reviewing the [study permit extension tutorial](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/extend-study-permit/)\.Whether you need a Provincial Attestation Letter \(PAL\) in your study permit extension application depends on your situation—check below\.

**Current UBC students in the same level of studies**

If you applied for your study permit with a UBC Letter of Admission, and you are continuing studies at the same level of studies, you do not need a PAL\. For example, if you are extending your study permit to continue your undergraduate degree \(even if you changed programs or majors\), you do not need a PAL\. Apply for your study permit extension from inside Canada before your study permit expires\.

**If you transferred to UBC before Nov 8, 2024**: If you transferred to UBC from a Canadian high school or another post\-secondary institution in Canada before Nov 8, 2024, send International Student Advising an [online form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0J8VRJgJQkuO2b4) with details about your situation before applying to extend your study permit\. An assessment must be made to determine if a PAL will be required as part of your application\.

If you transferred to UBC after Nov 8, 2024, you need a PAL\. Check the next section\.

**Current UBC students changing levels of studies at UBC**

If you applied for your study permit with a UBC Letter of Admission, and you are changing levels of studies at UBC, you can continue studies with your current valid study permit\. 

For example, if you are an undergraduate student who completed your bachelor’s degree and have been accepted to a master’s degree at UBC, you can continue to study at UBC with the same study permit, so long as it’s still [valid](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/#maintaining-your-student-status)\. If you will have a [gap of more than 150 days](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/taking-time-away-school-international/) between programs, contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/)\. 

If you are changing levels of study from a Doctoral or Master’s degree to certificate, diploma, undergraduate, or unclassified studies, you will need a PAL for your study permit extension application\. Send International Student Advising an [online form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0J8VRJgJQkuO2b4) about your situation for support\.

**Current UBC students restoring status**

If your study permit extension was refused, or your study permit has expired and you need to apply for [restoration](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/restoration-status/), contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for assistance\.

**

Students coming to UBC from a Canadian high school or Canadian post\-secondary institution with a study permit

**

You will need both your Letter of Admission and your Provincial Attestation Letter \(PAL\) before applying for your study permit\.

As of Nov 8, 2024, you must be enrolled at the Designated Learning Institution \(DLI\) named on your study permit\. If you [change institutions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/change-schools.html), you must apply for a study permit extension with your UBC Letter of Admission and most students need to receive the new study permit before beginning studies at UBC\. There are special rules for students transferring from a Canadian high school\. Check [important considerations](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/#important-considerations) for details and what to do if you have travel plans\. Be sure to apply for a study permit with your UBC Letter of Admission and PAL as soon as you receive them\.

If you are a new undergraduate bachelor’s degree student:

1.  **Confirm your personal information in your UBC student record**\.  
    Log into the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/), click *Edit Profile,* then select “Contact Summary” to confirm that your name matches the name on your passport, and that your home address and date of birth are correct\. You can update your address in the system if needed\. To update your name or date of birth, email [ubc\.pal@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.pal@ubc.ca)\.
2.  **Accept your offer of admission and pay your deposit**\.  
    Learn how to [accept your UBC offer](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/admitted/your-offer/) and pay the acceptance deposit\.
3.  **Wait for your Provincial Attestation Letter\.  
    **UBC will automatically send you your Provincial Attestation Letter by email within about 3 business days after you accept your offer\. Check the email account that you provided when you applied to UBC\. If you do not get your PAL after 3 business days, contact [ubc\.pal@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.pal@ubc.ca)\.
4.  **Download your Letter of Admission from UBC\.  
    **Log into the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/), click *Admissions*, and select “Letter of Admission”\.
5.  **Apply for your study permit\.  
    **If you will apply from inside Canada, review our [study permit extension tutorial](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/extend-study-permit/) to prepare your application\. If you will apply from outside Canada, review our [initial study permit tutorial](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/apply-initial-study-permit/)\.

**

New students in Canada as a visitor, worker, or asylum seeker

**

Only some people can apply for a study permit through the [process inside Canada](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/extend-study-permit/) and be [exempt from the PAL](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/provincial-attestation-letter.html)\.

Contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for support\.
