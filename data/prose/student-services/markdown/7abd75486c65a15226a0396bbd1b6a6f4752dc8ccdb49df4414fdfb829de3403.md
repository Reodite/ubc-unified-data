If you’re an international student planning to come to UBC, we are here to help\.

There are important steps to take before you plan your trip\. It’s important for you to carefully review the guide and understand the process and requirements before you travel\.

## Check before you make travel plans[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/\#check\-before\-you\-make\-travel\-plans](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/#check-before-you-make-travel-plans)

Before making travel arrangements, there are a few things you will need to consider:

-   Understand travel documents you need\.
-   Consider when to travel\.
-   Get advice on whether to travel\.

[Start the guide](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/check-eligible/)

## Before you travel to Canada[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/\#before\-you\-travel\-to\-canada](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/#before-you-travel-to-canada)

Before you travel to Canada, you must make sure you prepare the right documents\.

-   Get a new immigration medical exam, if required\.
-   Confirm your health insurance coverage\.
-   Complete the optional Advance Declaration\.

[Read requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/before-you-travel/)

## Know what to bring[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/\#know\-what\-to\-bring](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/#know-what-to-bring)

Prepare the documents you should bring and learn about restrictions\.

-   Learn what to pack\.
-   See documentation to bring\.

[Get the checklist](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/what-bring-canada/)

## While you travel to Canada[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/\#while\-you\-travel\-to\-canada](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/#while-you-travel-to-canada)

Know what to expect at the airport or border, follow arrival steps, and ensure your study permit has conditions to work\.

[Read details](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/while-you-travel/)

## After arriving in Canada[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/travelling\-to\-canada/\#after\-arriving\-in\-canada](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/#after-arriving-in-canada)

There are a few things you need to do as soon as you arrive in Canada\.

-   Apply for MSP and check that you have iMED\.
-   Complete the after arrival checklist\.
-   Learn about life in Canada\.
-   Find helpful resources to get settled\.

[Find resources](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/travelling-to-canada/after-arriving/)
