UBC aims to create a safe and inclusive campus environment where all genders and sexual identities are welcome\.

Whether you’re learning about your identity or supporting a friend, there are campus resources, involvement opportunities, and career guidance for Two Spirit, lesbian, gay, bisexual, trans, queer, intersex, and asexual \(2SLGBTQIA\+\) students\.

[Pride flag of rainbow colours waving in the breeze](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/resources_lgbtq_flag.jpg)

## Find resources[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/equity\-diversity\-inclusion\-resources/2slgbtqia\-resources/\#find\-resources](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/2slgbtqia-resources/#find-resources)

[Gender neutral bathrooms](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/gender-diversity/inclusive-washrooms-changerooms/)

Locate gender\-inclusive washrooms and change rooms across campus\.

[Insurance coverage](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/gender-diversity/transgender-health/) [for gender\-affirming care](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/gender-diversity/transgender-health/)

Find out which gender\-affirming services are covered by your MSP or AMS Health Plan\.

[Changing your personal information](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/)

Make sure your student information is up\-to\-date, including your legal and/or preferred name\.

[Updating your student record](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/gender-diversity/gender-markers-at-ubc/)

Find out how to change your UBC pronouns and gender identity on your official student documents\.

## Get support[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/equity\-diversity\-inclusion\-resources/2slgbtqia\-resources/\#get\-support](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/2slgbtqia-resources/#get-support)

[If you experience or witness discrimination](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/responding-discrimination/)

UBC doesn’t tolerate discrimination in any form\. Learn what to do if you’ve been treated unfairly due to your identity\.

[Sexual Assault Support Centre \(SASC\)](https://www.amssasc.ca/)

SASC supports people of all identities who have been affected by sexualized violence\.

[Sexual Violence Prevention and Response Office \(SVPRO\)](https://svpro.ubc.ca/)

SVPRO is a confidential space for those who have experienced, or have been affected by, sexual or gender\-based violence or harassment\.

## Learn more and get involved[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/equity\-diversity\-inclusion\-resources/2slgbtqia\-resources/\#learn\-more\-and\-get\-involved](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/2slgbtqia-resources/#learn-more-and-get-involved)

[Student groups, clubs, and committees](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/student-life/clubs/)

Find your community and connect with other students who share your interests and passions\.

[The Pride Collective](https://www.prideubc.com/)

Attend Pride events, join a discussion group, and shop at the Gender Empowerment Store\.

[Positive Space: Foundations online course](https://wpl.ubc.ca/browse/equity-and-inclusion/courses/wpl-eio-psfnd)

Take a self\-paced Canvas course covering topics such as 2SLGBTQIA\+ terms, inclusive practices, and the concepts of gender, sex, and sexuality\.

[Strategic Equity &amp; Anti\-Racism Enhancement Fund](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/inclusive-initiatives-fund/)

Funding is available for student\-led projects that can positively impact the student experience and create change on campus\.

[Trans, Two\-Spirit, and Gender Diversity Task Force](https://equity.ubc.ca/trans-two-spirit-and-gender-diversity-task-force/)

Propose recommendations to UBC’s senior leadership for a more inclusive campus\.
