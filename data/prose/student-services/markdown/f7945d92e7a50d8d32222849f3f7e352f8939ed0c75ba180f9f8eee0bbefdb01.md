## About the application form[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/apply\-initial\-study\-permit/tutorial\-apply\-initial\-study\-permit\-fill\-application/\#about\-the\-application\-form](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/apply-initial-study-permit/tutorial-apply-initial-study-permit-fill-application/#about-the-application-form)

Your application form is an important part of your study permit application\. Missing or incorrect information can negatively impact the outcome of your application\. Below are 4 steps to help create a comprehensive application\.

Note that some people are “[inadmissible](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/inadmissibility.html)” to Canada, which means they are not allowed to enter or remain in Canada for reasons such as having committed a crime, [medical reasons](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/inadmissibility/reasons/medical-inadmissibility.html), or for [misrepresentation](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/protect-fraud/consequences-fraud.html) \(not being honest in an application\)\. It is very important to carefully read and accurately answer all questions on the application form\. Contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) if you have concerns about inadmissibility\.

Below are 4 steps to help create a comprehensive application\.

## First, download the newest form[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/apply\-initial\-study\-permit/tutorial\-apply\-initial\-study\-permit\-fill\-application/\#first\-download\-the\-newest\-form](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/apply-initial-study-permit/tutorial-apply-initial-study-permit-fill-application/#first-download-the-newest-form)

Use the “Application for a Study Permit Made Outside of Canada \[IMM 1294\]” form\.

IRCC makes periodic updates to their application forms\. Always make sure you are using the most current application form by downloading the application form from your Document Checklist on your IRCC online account\.

[Screenshot of the application form to download in your IRCC online account](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/study_permit_download_form_screenshot.png)

If you see the “Please wait…” message when downloading the form, click on the download arrow at the top right to save the file onto your computer, such as in your documents folder or desktop, and then open it from where you saved it on your computer with Adobe Reader\. You will not be able to open it from a browser\. If you do not see the arrow, move your cursor to the top of the screen and it will appear\.

## Get help with commonly asked questions[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/apply\-initial\-study\-permit/tutorial\-apply\-initial\-study\-permit\-fill\-application/\#get\-help\-with\-commonly\-asked\-questions](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/apply-initial-study-permit/tutorial-apply-initial-study-permit-fill-application/#get-help-with-commonly-asked-questions)

**

What is a UCI?

**

UCI stands for “Unique Client Identifier,” also known as “Client ID\.” It is an eight\-digit or ten\-digit number\. **If you do not have a UCI, leave this section blank\.**

[Screenshot of the "UCI" section on your initial study permit application](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/study_permit_uci_screenshot.png)

**

What is UBC’s Designated Learning Institution \(DLI\) number?

**

UBC’s DLI is **O19330231062**\. It starts with the letter “O” and not zero\.

[Screenshot of the "DLI" section on your initial study permit application](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/study_permit_dli_number.png)

**

How do I answer “Duration of expected study”?

**

Your study permit length will usually match your program duration plus 90 days, unless your passport expires sooner\.

-   **In the “From” field**: use the “Program Start Date” shown on your Letter of Acceptance\.
-   **In the “To” field**: Add 90 days after your anticipated program completion date\.

[Screenshot of how to fill out "Duration of study" in IRCC form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/study_permit_duration_study.png)

**

How do I fill out the “the cost of my studies” section?

**

Provide information on your expenses including your first\-year tuition fees, rent and food, travel expenses, and other estimated costs for one year\. 

For “tuition”, check your Letter of Admission for tuition costs for one year\. You should provide proof of funds which exceed the minimum of the tuition fee range, if one was provided\. 

For “room and board”, add the minimum for cost\-of\-living expenses for one year\. You should indicate at least $22,895 for the [cost\-of\-living financial requirement](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/financial-support.html#) as of 2025\. The cost\-of\-living amount will be adjusted each year based on Statistics Canada’s low\-income cut\-off\. The amount required for [additional family members](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/financial-support.html) depends on how many dependents you have\.

You can use these two cost calculators to help you estimate your costs:

-   [Undergraduate students](http://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/cost/)
-   [Graduate students](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/tuition-fees-cost-living/cost-living)

[Screenshot of the "Cost of studies" section on your initial study permit application](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/study_permit_cost_studies.png)

**Note**: exchange students coming through [Go Global](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange) should indicate ‘$0’ in the ‘tuition’ field as you will not pay tuition fees to UBC, you will pay your home university\. Include a letter of explanation to address your tuition fees as an inbound exchange student and provide supporting documents, if available\. One\-term exchange students may provide estimated costs based on one term only and prorate the cost\-of\-living amount\.

**

How do I fill out the “funds available for my stay” section?

**

The amount you indicate in “*Funds available for my stay \(CAD\)*” should match the total amount of funds you are providing in your application from all sources\.

For example, if you have a combination of $20,502 in your bank account, $10,000 from your scholarship, and $63,843 from your parents, you would indicate $94,345\.

Remember, you **must** show that you have enough funds to cover your first year tuition fees, travel expenses, and the minimum for cost\-of\-living expenses\. So the amount provided under box 5 “funds available for my stay \(CAD\)” must be greater than the total amounts for tuition, room and board and other under box 4 “the cost of my studies”\.​​​​​

[Screenshot of how to fill out "Funds available" section on initial study permit application](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/study_permit_funds_available_screenshot.png)

**

How do I fill out the “Employment” section?

**

The information you provide in this section helps IRCC have a fuller picture of your activities within the last ten years:

-   Include all part\-time and full\-time jobs as well as any internships and practicum placements, even if they were unpaid\.
-   Under “*current activity*”, if you are not currently working/employed, but you are a student, write “student” as activity and your school name as “employer”\. Your ‘to’ date can be the month and year of your study permit application; for example, if you are applying in April 2026, indicate this in your ‘to’ field\. 
-   If you are working at the time of application, write the month and year of your application submission as your ‘To’ date\.
-   Fill out the form in reverse chronological order, with the most recent job/activity first\.
-   If you need more space, provide additional information in a Letter of Explanation\.

**

How do I answer “Background” questions?

**

It is very important to carefully read these questions and answer truthfully and honestly\.

Please contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) if you answered “yes” to any of the questions so that you are aware of possible implications\.

**Important:** If you have previously applied for any Canadian immigration application, including a Canadian study permit, a Temporary Resident Visa \(TRV\), a work permit, or permanent residence, whether it is approved or rejected, you must report it to IRCC by checking the box “Yes” on 2\(c\) and writing down what you applied for and what year you applied for each type of document in the box in addition to any details\.

**

How can I sign the form if I apply online?

**

You do not need to sign your name in the signature field on the bottom of page 5, but you will need to type in the date of your application if applying online\. Upload the form **without** printing or signing\.

-   After uploading all your documents, you will be asked to electronically sign your application by typing your name\.
-   **Do not** print and sign the form because you will not be able to upload it to the site\.

## Validate your form[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/apply\-initial\-study\-permit/tutorial\-apply\-initial\-study\-permit\-fill\-application/\#validate\-your\-form](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/apply-initial-study-permit/tutorial-apply-initial-study-permit-fill-application/#validate-your-form)

When you finish filling out your application form, click the blue “Validate” button \(found on the first or last page\)\. This will highlight any missing information that is required and create an additional page with barcodes\.

-   You can make changes after the application is successfully validated – just remember to validate again\.
-   You can edit and validate as many times as you need\.

Once your application has been successfully validated, it will show the following:

[Screenshot of the barcode required to validate your TRV application](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/validate_form_screenshot.png)

If you don’t see any barcodes when you validate your application, please visit the [CIC help page](http://www.cic.gc.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=767&amp;top=23)\.

## If applying online, take screenshots as you apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/study\-permits/apply\-initial\-study\-permit/tutorial\-apply\-initial\-study\-permit\-fill\-application/\#if\-applying\-online\-take\-screenshots\-as\-you\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/apply-initial-study-permit/tutorial-apply-initial-study-permit-fill-application/#if-applying-online-take-screenshots-as-you-apply)

In case there are issues with your application submission, take screenshots of these screens:

1.  The “Your document checklist” page after all your documents have been successfully uploaded to your IRCC online account\.
2.  The “Acknowledgment of receipt” page after the application has been submitted\.
3.  After [uploading or submitting](http://www.cic.gc.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1310&amp;top=23) any ‘after\-the\-fact’ documents that an IRCC officer has requested after your original application has been submitted\.

Save your screenshots to a safe place where you can access them easily\.

Learn how to take screenshots if you’re [using a PC](http://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/13776/windows-use-snipping-tool-to-capture-screenshots#1TC=windows-7) or [on your Mac](http://support.apple.com/en-ca/HT201361)\.
