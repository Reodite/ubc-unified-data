Non\-Degree Studies provide study options for individuals wishing to take UBC credit\-based courses without pursuing a UBC degree, diploma, or certificate\.

Non\-Degree Studies are an option for you if you meet one of the following:

-   You want to take courses for general interest\.
-   You want to take a course for professional development, upgrading or academic advancement\.
-   You are completing an undergraduate degree at another institution\.
-   You have already completed a degree\.

If you would like to take courses at the UBC Okanagan campus, please contact [UBC Okanagan Non\-Degree Studies](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/non-degree-studies/)\.

## Types of Non\-Degree Studies[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/\#types\-of\-non\-degree\-studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/#types-of-non-degree-studies)

[Unclassified](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/)

This option is for students who have already completed an undergraduate degree\.

[Visiting](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-visiting/)

This option is for students who are currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s degree program at another institution\.

[Access studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-access-studies/)

This option is for students who have not completed an undergraduate degree and are interested in taking courses for general interest, not towards a UBC degree\.

## Enrolling in Non\-Degree Studies[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/\#enrolling\-in\-non\-degree\-studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/#enrolling-in-non-degree-studies)

The admission process differs based on your academic background\. However, you can follow these steps for all 3 types of Non\-Degree Studies\.

1.  Review the program requirements and submit an application for the type of Non\-Degree Studies category you want to enroll in:
    -   [Unclassified students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/)
    -   [Visiting students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-visiting/)
    -   [Access Studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-access-studies/)
2.  Pay the application fee\.
3.  Wait for a confirmation email including next steps\.

## Registering for courses[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/\#registering\-for\-courses](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/#registering-for-courses)

Once you are admitted, you will need to [register for courses](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/register-courses/)\. Even if you’re enrolled in Non\-Degree Studies, you are not guaranteed a spot in any particular course\.

## UBC’s inclusive post\-secondary education initiative[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/\#ubcs\-inclusive\-post\-secondary\-education\-initiative](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/#ubcs-inclusive-post-secondary-education-initiative)

If you have developmental or intellectual disabilities and are interested in attending post\-secondary education in a way that supports your strengths and learning style, consider applying for UBC’s inclusive post\-secondary education initiative with the [STEPS Forward program](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/how-to-apply/disability/#component-3)\.
