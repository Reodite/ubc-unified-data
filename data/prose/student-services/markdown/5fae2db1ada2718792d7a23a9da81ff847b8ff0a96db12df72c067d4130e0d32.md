## Funding amounts[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/us\-loans\-faq/\#funding\-amounts](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-loans-faq/#funding-amounts)

**

How much US Direct Loan funding am I eligible for?

**

You are eligible to borrow up to the full amount of your Cost of Attendance\. Additional funding from other resources, such as scholarships and loans you may be receiving will be subtracted from the total amount you can borrow\. The Winter Session Cost of Attendance, including living costs, typically ranges from $70,000 to $85,000 for undergraduate international students at UBC\. Your maximum funding also depends on your degree and year level\. UBC can not certify you for funding that exceeds either your Cost of Attendance or the [loan maximums](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/subsidized-unsubsidized#how-much-can-i-borrow) set by the US Department of Education\.

To learn how your funding is calculated, read about [US Direct Loan funding amounts](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-direct-loan-funding-amounts/)\.

Use the UBC [cost calculator](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/cost/) to plan your first\-year costs\.

**

Do Direct Loan maximums apply to each term or each year?

**

The [loan maximums](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/subsidized-unsubsidized#how-much-can-i-borrow) set by the US Department of Education apply to the academic year, which is September to August of the following year\.

If you use the maximum amount in Direct Subsidized and Direct Unsubsidized Loan funding for the Winter Session \(September to April period\) and then decide to take summer courses, you are not eligible for any further Direct Subsidized and Direct Unsubsidized loan funding for the Summer Session \(May to August period\)\.

If you do not use the entire annual loan limit during the Winter Session, you can still be considered for the remainder of the amount for the rest of the academic year or Summer Session\.

## Funding eligibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/us\-loans\-faq/\#funding\-eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-loans-faq/#funding-eligibility)

**

What type of funding can I apply for?

**

You can apply for Direct Loans, which may include Direct Subsidized and Direct Unsubsidized Loans, and Grad PLUS or Parent PLUS funding, depending on your personal eligibility\. Undergraduate students may be eligible for Direct Subsidized funding, while graduate students are only eligible for Direct Unsubsidized funding\.

If you are not eligible for Direct Loans, you have the option of applying for private loans through [Sallie Mae](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/sallie-mae-private-loans/)\.

**

Can I get Pell Grants or Perkins Loans?

**

UBC is considered a foreign school and US students attending foreign institutions are not eligible to receive these types of Federal aid\.

**

Can I receive loan funding for Distance Education?

**

Starting in 2023 Summer Session, students participating in any ineligible courses, such as [Online, Hybrid, Multi\-Access](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/course-modes-delivery/), or any type of course or part of a course which is taught online, will lose Direct Loan eligibility for the remainder of their degree\.

Under the temporary COVID\-19 Relief Bill, US students were previously allowed to take online courses or courses with online components until the end of 2022/23 Winter Session Term 2 \(Sep 2022 to Apr 2023\)\. This was applicable to courses completed since Sun, Mar 1, 2020 without it negatively impacting students’ eligibility for Direct Loan funding\.

**

Am I eligible for a US Loan while I’m on exchange?

**

If you’re thinking about going on exchange and are eligible for US Direct Loans, contact an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to understand your financial options before you apply for exchange\.

Check the US [Federal School Code Lists](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/resource-type/Federal%20School%20Code%20Lists) \(under “Foreign Country”\) to confirm that your exchange university is also eligible to provide Title IV funds\.

If you choose to do an exchange at a school in the US, you could lose your eligibility for Direct Loans for the rest of your program\. If you require federal loans, it’s not recommended to go on exchange to a US school\.

If your partner university is Title IV and **not located in the US**, UBC can assess you for Direct Loans while you’re on exchange\.

If your partner university is **not Title IV**, you will not be eligible to receive Direct Loans for the period of your exchange\. You may want to consider private loans like [Sallie Mae](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/sallie-mae-private-loans/) in this case\.

**

Can I receive funding for Jump Start?

**

Yes, if you are participating in Jumpstart and would like to include the fee in your US loan assessment, please email your request to [usloans\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:usloans.inquiry@ubc.ca) with your name and UBC student number\. The registration fee will then be included when your Cost of Attendance is calculated\.

**

Am I eligible for a US Loan while I’m on Co\-op?

**

If you’re thinking about doing your Co\-op in the US, please connect with an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) first to fully understand the financial implications for your future Direct Loan eligibility\. Co\-op placements in the US may make you ineligible for future Direct Loan funding\.

If you’re a graduate student, please check with an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to confirm if your Co\-op is loan\-eligible\.

## Application and funding timelines[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/us\-loans\-faq/\#application\-and\-funding\-timelines](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-loans-faq/#application-and-funding-timelines)

**

When should I apply for my Direct Loan?

**

You’re recommended to apply for your Direct Loan at least 6 weeks before the start of the term to make sure your funding is available by the first week of classes\.

Follow [step\-by\-step instructions](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/#applying-for-a-us-direct-loan) and avoid delays to your application\.

**

When will I receive my Direct Loan \(Subsidized, Unsubsidized or Parent/Grad PLUS\) funds?

**

Your Direct Loan will pay towards your tuition and student fees on the [disbursement date](https://studentaid.gov/help-center/answers/article/what-is-disbursement-date) listed on your assessment letter from UBC\. If there is any money leftover from your loan after your fees are paid, you can receive this money through [direct deposit](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/finances/setting-up-your-direct-deposit/)\.

Please ensure your direct deposit details are up\-to\-date to receive your refund in your bank account\. This funding will be available a few business days after the disbursement date\.

**

When will I receive my US Private Loans?

**

UBC releases your loan funding at the start of the term\. It takes up to 6 weeks to process a US Private Loan, such as the Sallie Mae private loan\. You’re recommended to apply at least 6 weeks before the start of term to ensure you receive your funding for the start of the session\.

**

Can I receive my loan funding before school starts?

**

If you’ve been approved for a Direct Loan, the earliest time your loan funding will be available is the first week of classes\.

You are expected to have some savings to cover housing deposits and other initial costs related to your studies\. If you apply for your Direct Loans at least 6 weeks before the start of the term, you will receive your funding before any late fees are assessed\.

Use the [UBC cost calculator](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/cost/) to plan your first\-year costs\.

## After receiving a Direct Loan[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/us\-loans\-faq/\#after\-receiving\-a\-direct\-loan](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-loans-faq/#after-receiving-a-direct-loan)

**

Can I use audited courses or courses not for credit towards my registration requirement?

**

Any [audited courses](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/auditing-courses/) do not count towards the 6\-credit requirement\.

Direct Subsidized Loans are only available to undergraduate students registered in at least 6 credits per term\. 

Direct Unsubsidized Loans are available for undergraduate and graduate students registered in at least 6 credits per term\.

**

Can I cancel my Direct Loan?

**

You can cancel or reduce your Direct Loan within 30 days from the [disbursement date](https://studentaid.gov/help-center/answers/article/what-is-disbursement-date) by emailing [usloans\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:usloans.inquiry@ubc.ca)\.

After 30 days, you must contact your lender to make arrangements to return all or a portion of your loan\.

**

How do I file my taxes in the US while I’m studying in Canada?

**

As a Canadian institution, UBC does not issue American tax documents \(e\.g\., Form 1098\-T\)\. However, UBC does issue a form called the T2022 that [can be downloaded](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/finances/downloading-your-tax-forms/) through the UBC Official Documents tool on Workday\. You can use this form or provide it to your accountant for the purposes of filing your taxes in the US\. It is recommended that you file taxes to Canada while you are studying at UBC\.

Learn more about [filing your taxes in Canada](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/taxes/filing-taxes-canada/)\.

**

Where do I go to get my In\-School Deferment or Continuation of Interest\-Free Status form filled out?

**

If you have an assigned Enrolment Services Advisor, they can help you complete your In\-School Deferment or Continuation of Interest\-Free Status form\. Find your advisor by [logging into Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/) and selecting the “Support” tab in the Academics app\.

If you don’t have an assigned advisor, you can contact one by calling [604 822 9836](tel:6048229836) or submit your question through the [online form](https://www.askme.ubc.ca/form/s/)\.
