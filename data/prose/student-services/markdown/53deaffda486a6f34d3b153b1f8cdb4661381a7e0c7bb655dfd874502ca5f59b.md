The Cost of Attendance \(COA\) for US students is an estimate of your educational expenses for a period of study\. The expenses included in the estimate are determined by US federal law\. Your COA determines the maximum financial aid that you may receive for each study period\.

## What is included[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/cost\-of\-attendance\-for\-us\-students/\#what\-is\-included](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/cost-of-attendance-for-us-students/#what-is-included)

Cost of Attendance is determined by UBC and consists of reasonable amounts calculated yearly for the average student in each program\. 

The COA includes:

-   Tuition and fees based on a full course load\. Per\-credit fees are published in the [Academic Calendar](https://calendar.ubc.ca/)
-   Books, course materials, supplies, and equipment determined by your program
-   Transportation based on average travel costs from US to UBC and local transportation in Vancouver
-   Miscellaneous personal expenses, which are based on the [Moderate Standard of Living](https://studentaidbc.ca/help-centre/applying-loans/your-financial-need) ‘Miscellaneous’ category for a single student away from home, as determined by BC’s student loan provider
-   Living expenses \(food and housing\), based on your situation:
    -   On campus with a UBC Housing meal plan
    -   On campus without a UBC Housing meal plan
    -   On campus with dependent\(s\)
    -   Off campus
    -   Off campus with dependent\(s\)
    -   Dependent student residing at home with parent\(s\)
-   Loan fees, which reflect your exact loan fees for Direct Loans

## Determining funding amounts[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/cost\-of\-attendance\-for\-us\-students/\#determining\-funding\-amounts](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/cost-of-attendance-for-us-students/#determining-funding-amounts)

Direct Loans and US private loan funding amounts are based on the Cost of Attendance \(COA\)\. 

### Need\-based funding

Direct Subsidized Loans are available to undergraduate students with financial need\. Your financial need is determined by the [Student Aid Index](https://studentaid.gov/complete-aid-process/how-calculated) \(SAI\) generated by the answers provided in your [Free Application for Federal Student Aid](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa) \(FAFSA\)\.

Direct Subsidized Loan eligibility is calculated through the following equation:

Cost of Attendance \(COA\) – Student Aid Index \(SAI\) – Other Financial Assistance \(OFA\) \= Financial Need

### Non need\-based funding

Direct Unsubsidized Loans, PLUS Loans, and US private loans are not based on financial need\. Eligibility is calculated with the following equation:

Cost of Attendance \(COA\) – Other Financial Assistance \(OFA\) \= Eligible Funding

### Other Financial Assistance \(OFA\)

Other Financial Assistance \(OFA\) refers to eligible funding you can reasonably anticipate to receive during the study period, whether the assistance is awarded by the school or by an individual or organization outside the school\. This includes any awards, scholarships, and other loan funding you are receiving\. It does not include any income you have earned\.

It’s important to report all of your scholarships and awards to avoid having your Direct Loans audited, reduced or returned\.
