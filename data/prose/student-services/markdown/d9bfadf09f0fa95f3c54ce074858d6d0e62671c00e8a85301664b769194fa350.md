## UBC Awards for Indigenous Students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/awards\-indigenous/\#ubc\-awards\-for\-indigenous\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-indigenous/#ubc-awards-for-indigenous-students)

The UBC Awards for Indigenous Students include scholarships and bursaries\. When a student applies for these awards, they are considered for both merit and need\-based awards\.

-   Scholarships are awards that recognize academic, community, or athletic achievement\.
-   Bursaries are based on financial need\. Student awards based solely on financial need are not included on your official transcript\.

You can find a full list of how UBC defines different types of awards in the [Academic Calendar](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/university-awards)\. There are also external scholarships and bursaries available for Indigenous students outlined on the page below\.

[Read UBC Indigenous award descriptions](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-indigenous/descriptions-awards-indigenous/)

## Eligibility requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/awards\-indigenous/\#eligibility\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-indigenous/#eligibility-requirements)

To be considered for UBC Indigenous awards, you must meet all the following requirements:

-   You are a Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or have refugee status\.
-   You are an Indigenous student and have [self\-identified in Workday](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/updating-your-indigenous-self-identification/)\. 
-   You will submit the application for intended Indigenous awards by the award program deadline\.
-   You are enrolled in a full\-time [course load](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/course-load-guide/) studying on the Vancouver campus\.
-   You meet the eligibility requirements as outlined in [UBC’s Regulations Governing University Awards](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/university-awards)\.
-   You demonstrate financial need to be considered for bursaries\. 

Unclassified or qualifying students are not eligible for these awards\. Read through the [Academic Calendar information](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/admissions/classification-students) on the classification of students\.

UBC uses the term “Indigenous” in place of “Aboriginal” as held in the spirit of its use in section 35\(2\) of the Canadian Constitution, to refer inclusively to members of First Nations, status and non\-status, treaty and non\-treaty Indians, Métis, and Inuit peoples in Canada, recognizing that many people prefer the terms that are specific and traditional to their communities\.

### Additional requirements

Some awards have additional requirements, which are listed in the application\. Please read the [award descriptions](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-indigenous/descriptions-awards-indigenous/) before you start your application, to better target the awards you qualify for\.

If you are eligible for bursaries, submit an application to the [UBC Bursary Program](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-bursary-program/) by the deadline to be considered for UBC General Bursary at the same time\. Application to the general bursary program at UBC is helpful in determining your financial need\. An application to the UBC Bursary Program **is not required** for your application to UBC Awards for Indigenous Students\.

## Before you apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/awards\-indigenous/\#before\-you\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-indigenous/#before-you-apply)

There are detailed instructions on the application form\. Follow these steps before you apply:

1.  Make sure you meet all [general and specific eligibility requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-indigenous/descriptions-awards-indigenous/) for applicable awards\.
2.  Prepare written statements describing your personal situation, financial need, and connection to any Indigenous UBC campus communities\. Include any additional information for specific awards as requested\.
3.  If you are applying for bursaries, consider applying for the [UBC Bursary Program](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-bursary-program/) before the deadline\. Application to this program helps establish your financial need\. but is **not required** to apply for UBC Indigenous Awards\.

## If you need help[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/awards\-indigenous/\#if\-you\-need\-help](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-indigenous/#if-you-need-help)

From the beginning of your UBC journey until graduation, Enrolment Services Advisors are here to provide support with your finances, including:

-   Creating a financial plan
-   Helping you understand the different funding options
-   Developing a scholarship or award application

All undergraduate students have an assigned [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) for personalized advising\. If you do not have an assigned advisor, you can email the Indigenous Advising Team at [es\.indigenous@ubc\.ca](mailto:es.indigenous@ubc.ca) for help\.

-   If you are a prospective, undergraduate, graduate, or post\-baccalaureate Indigenous student you can ask questions or book a one\-on\-one advising appointment by emailing [es\.indigenous@ubc\.ca](mailto:es.indigenous@ubc.ca)\.
-   During the academic year \(September to April\), visit the [UBC First Nations Longhouse](https://fnhl.ubc.ca/students) on Tuesdays from 10:00 a\.m\. to 3:00 p\.m\. to speak with an Enrolment Services Advisor and a member of the Indigenous Student Support Team\. Advisors are available in the Sty\-Wet\-Tan Great Hall during the lunch hour, or assistance is available at the front desk\.
-   During the Summer Session \(May to August\), advising is offered online on Tuesdays from 12:00 p\.m\. to 3:00 p\.m\. Visit the [UBC Events](https://events.ubc.ca/series/es-indigenous-student-support-virtual-drop-in-advising/) page for the Zoom link\.
-   If you’re a UBC Okanagan student, please contact the [UBC Okanagan Indigenous Student Advising Team](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/academic-advising/aboriginal-student-advising)\.

The Indigenous Student Support and Advising Team in Enrolment Services works closely with other teams on campus, including the following:

-   [First Nations House of Learning](https://fnhl.ubc.ca/)
-   [Indigenous Student Academic Advising Team](https://fnhl.ubc.ca/students/advising-counselling/)
-   [Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/)
