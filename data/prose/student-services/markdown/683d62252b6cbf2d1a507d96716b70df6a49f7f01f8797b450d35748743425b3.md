The Bachelor of Education \(BEd\) is an intensive program with significant costs\. Understanding your costs is essential to building a good financial plan and a successful year of study\.

## Understanding your costs[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/financial\-planning\-post\-baccalaureate/funding\-bachelor\-education/\#understanding\-your\-costs](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/financial-planning-post-baccalaureate/funding-bachelor-education/#understanding-your-costs)

Start by using the [cost calculator](http://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/cost/#cost-calculator) to estimate your costs for the year, including tuition, fees, and living expenses\. Next, consider the resources available to you\. You are expected to prepare for the costs of the Bachelor of Education program by accessing all resources available, including but not limited to:

-   Parent and family support
-   Summer earnings
-   Personal savings
-   Government student loans
-   Bursaries
-   Part\-time work

## Awards[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/financial\-planning\-post\-baccalaureate/funding\-bachelor\-education/\#awards](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/financial-planning-post-baccalaureate/funding-bachelor-education/#awards)

There are a limited number of awards available that you may be eligible for\. These include [Faculty of Education awards](http://teach.educ.ubc.ca/students/awards/) for both new and continuing Education students, as well as other [general awards for UBC students](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/)\.

## Bursaries[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/financial\-planning\-post\-baccalaureate/funding\-bachelor\-education/\#bursaries](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/financial-planning-post-baccalaureate/funding-bachelor-education/#bursaries)

You are encouraged to apply for the [UBC Bursary Program](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-bursary-program/) as another type of financial support\.

To be eligible for the UBC Bursary Program, you must apply for your full\-time government student loan by Jul 31, and apply for the bursary through [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/) between Aug 15 and Sep 15\.

-   The Bachelor of Education \(BEd\) program is eligible for a limited number of bursaries and therefore your full unmet need may not be filled\.
-   By applying for the Winter Bursary Program between Aug 15 and Sep 15, you will be considered for the full length of the 11\-month program\. You will not be eligible for more assistance in the Summer Bursary program, as your unmet need will have already been considered for the full year\.

## Student loans[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/financial\-planning\-post\-baccalaureate/funding\-bachelor\-education/\#student\-loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/financial-planning-post-baccalaureate/funding-bachelor-education/#student-loans)

Government student loans are available to help supplement your financial resources\. It is important to note that these loans will not cover all of your costs for the Bachelor of Education \(BEd\) program\. Read more about [types of loans and eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/)\.

-   Your student loan will be disbursed in 2 unequal installments\.
-   Check your loan assessment for exact amounts and disbursement dates\.
-   By applying for your loan by Jul 31, your tuition will be deferred until after your loan disbursement dates in early September and mid\-February\.
-   Please ensure you have met the minimum credit load requirements to maintain your loan\. As this is an intensive program, the minimum credit loads are different between terms and are higher than the standard 9\-credit minimums\. Refer to the email you will receive in early July titled “Important student loans and bursary information for Education Students”\.

## Student line of credit[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/financial\-planning\-post\-baccalaureate/funding\-bachelor\-education/\#student\-line\-of\-credit](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/financial-planning-post-baccalaureate/funding-bachelor-education/#student-line-of-credit)

A student line of credit may be an option for you to make sure you have enough money to cover your costs\.

You can apply for a student line of credit at any private bank\. Bring your letter of acceptance or a proof of enrolment letter to the bank when you apply\.

Learn how to [download your proof of enrolment letter](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/student-documents/downloading-your-confirmation-of-enrolment-letter/) in Workday\.​

## Work opportunities[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/financial\-planning\-post\-baccalaureate/funding\-bachelor\-education/\#work\-opportunities](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/financial-planning-post-baccalaureate/funding-bachelor-education/#work-opportunities)

The Bachelor of Education \(BEd\) program involves an intense course load so finding time to work may be difficult\.

A [Work Learn position](https://students.ubc.ca/career/work-learn-program/) may be your best option if you choose to work during your studies\. It’s a great way to earn extra money part\-time during the academic year\. There are also other [part\-time jobs on campus](https://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-jobs/) that you can explore\.
