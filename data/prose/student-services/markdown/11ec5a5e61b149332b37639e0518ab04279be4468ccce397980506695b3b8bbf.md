## About CCEL Grants[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/community\-experiences/grants\-community\-projects/\#about\-ccel\-grants](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/grants-community-projects/#about-ccel-grants)

At the [Centre for Community Engaged Learning](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-community-engaged-learning/) \(CCEL\), we empower students to drive social change through funding for meaningful projects in partnership with BC\-based community organizations\. 

Our grants support students in co\-designing and implementing impactful initiatives that directly address community needs\. We encourage bold, creative ideas and offer hands\-on experiences in tackling real\-world challenges\. Dive in, explore new possibilities, and gain experience working to address real\-world issues\.

Applications for the 2026 cycle are now closed\. Applications for 2027 projects open from **Nov 1, 2026** to **Jan, 31, 2027**\.

## Connect to Community Grant[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/community\-experiences/grants\-community\-projects/\#connect\-to\-community\-grant](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/grants-community-projects/#connect-to-community-grant)

Connect to Community Grants \(C2C\) are designed for students embarking on the initial phases of their changemaking journey, intended for those taking their first strides\. They offer UBC students the opportunity to create and carry out introductory, trial, and smaller\-scale projects that support community priorities\. 

Designed as an introduction to the grants experience, students are challenged to learn from the application process through to project completion\.

Successful applicants can request between $500 and $2,000 per project\. Projects must be completed within 6 months of the grant being awarded\. Review the [application toolkit](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/connect_to_community_application_toolkit.pdf) \(pdf\) for more details and download the [application form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/connect_to_community_grant_application.pdf) \(pdf\)\.

## Global Fund[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/community\-experiences/grants\-community\-projects/\#global\-fund](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/grants-community-projects/#global-fund)

Global Fund Grants are funded in partnership with the [UBC Alma Mater Society](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/), and are intended for students who have some experience working alongside communities towards social change, to further empower them in expanding their learning and capacity to work in partnership with the community\. They offer UBC students the opportunity to create and execute a more robust project to address a community priority\. 

The Global Fund seeks to uplift projects that thoughtfully engage with any of the following themes\.

Intercultural Collaboration

-   Creates space for intercultural learning and exchange
-   Builds relationships across cultural communities through dialogue, shared practice, or co\-creation
-   Addresses barriers to understanding, inclusion, or equitable participation across cultures
-   Fosters and celebrates cultural heritage, and deepens cultural understanding through locally grounded, lived experiences
-   Elevates underrepresented or historically marginalized voices within a community

Successful applicants can request between $2,000 and $10,000 per project\. Projects must be completed within 6 months of the grant being awarded\. Review the [application toolkit](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/global_fund_application_toolkit.pdf) \(pdf\) for more details, and download the [application form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/global_fund_application.pdf) \(pdf\)\.

## Chapman and Innovation Grants[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/community\-experiences/grants\-community\-projects/\#chapman\-and\-innovation\-grants](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/grants-community-projects/#chapman-and-innovation-grants)

Chapman and Innovation Grants \(C&amp;I\) are designed for students advanced in their path toward making a difference and well\-positioned to make meaningful contributions to the community\. They offer UBC students the opportunity to work on projects that promote the self\-empowerment of communities through meaningful collaboration\. 

Strong projects will have an innovative or creative approach to addressing social issues\.

Successful applicants can request between $5,000 and $10,000 CAD per project\. Projects must be completed within 6 months of the grant being awarded\. Review the [application toolkit](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/chapman_and_innovation_application_toolkit.pdf) \(pdf\) for more details, and download the [application form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/chapman_and_innovation_grant_application.pdf) \(pdf\)\.

## Eligibility requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/community\-experiences/grants\-community\-projects/\#eligibility\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/grants-community-projects/#eligibility-requirements)

-   You must be enrolled in an undergraduate program or Master’s degree\. PhD students are not eligible for CCEL grants\.
-   Domestic and international students are welcome to apply\.
-   Each application must include at least one UBC student and one community organization listed on the application\.

Read the [Grant Eligibility Guidelines](https://ccel.ubc.ca/students/ccel-grants/) for more information about student, community partner, and project eligibility\.

## Year\-round advising[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/community\-experiences/grants\-community\-projects/\#year\-round\-advising](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/grants-community-projects/#year-round-advising)

UBC CCEL Grant Advising is available to support students throughout the grant application process\. Examples of discussion topics include:

-   Connecting with community partner organizations
-   Exploring, scoping out, and evaluating project ideas and their feasibility
-   Answering questions about the application process
-   Determining eligibility to apply to grants
-   Preparing grant applications, including offering resources for grant writing
-   Creating and revising project budgets

## Reporting requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/community\-experiences/grants\-community\-projects/\#reporting\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/grants-community-projects/#reporting-requirements)

-   A final project report detailing the project outcomes
-   An expense report of how the fund was used
