## Eligibility for Access Studies[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-access\-studies/\#eligibility\-for\-access\-studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-access-studies/#eligibility-for-access-studies)

If you haven’t completed a degree but you’d like to take courses for general interest at UBC outside of a degree program, you can apply as an Access Studies student\.

To be eligible, you must meet all the following requirements:

-   You fulfill the [English language requirement](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/english-language-competency/)\.
-   You meet UBC’s [criteria for Access Studies](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/admissions/classification-students) students\.
-   You do not hold a bachelor’s degree from a recognized institution\.

If you’re a senior citizen or a current high school student, there are additional steps you’ll need to follow\. Review the information in the sections below\.

## Considerations for Access Studies students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-access\-studies/\#considerations\-for\-access\-studies\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-access-studies/#considerations-for-access-studies-students)

-   Enrolment for Access Studies students is limited to 6 credits per term and a lifetime maximum of 24 credits\.
-   Being admitted to UBC as an Access Studies student does not guarantee enrolment in requested courses or admission to a degree program at UBC\.
-   You may be able to transfer your course credits to a future UBC degree program at the discretion of the faculty you apply to\.
-   To register, you’ll need to meet course prerequisites through previous study, relevant experience, or instructor permission\.

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-access\-studies/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-access-studies/#how-to-apply)

### Select your courses

1.  Use the [Course Search](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/course-search) to find eligible courses in Arts, Forestry, Land and Food Systems, or Science\.
2.  Select “Course Search” and enter the subject area or keywords for your area of interest, making sure that you meet the stated course restrictions and prerequisites\.

### Apply online

All new and previously registered students must complete the steps below:

1.  Create an [Education Planner BC account](https://apply.educationplannerbc.ca/) online\. 
2.  Fill in your personal information and academic history\.  
3.  Click *Start Applying* and select “University of British Columbia” as the institution\.
4.  Select the appropriate Admissions Category based on your academic history\.
5.  Select “Access Student” under *First Program Choice*\. You do not need to select a second program choice\.
6.  When asked, make sure to include the list of course\(s\) you’re interested in taking or the specific program under which you would like to take courses\.
7.  UBC Admissions will email you with a confirmation of your application and next steps\.
8.  After completing your application, email any additional and required documents to [non\.degree@ubc\.ca](mailto:non.degree@ubc.ca)\.

## Senior citizens[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-access\-studies/\#senior\-citizens](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-access-studies/#senior-citizens)

If you’re a Canadian citizen or permanent resident that is 65 years of age or older, you are eligible to apply through Access Studies to take courses for general interest if you meet the following requirements:

-   You are a British Columbia resident\.
-   You do not hold a bachelor’s degree from a recognized institution\. If you completed a bachelor’s degree, you need to apply as an [unclassified student](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/)\.

### Tuition costs for senior citizens

Normally, senior citizens do not pay [tuition or student fees](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/policies-fees#18205), except for programs within Dentistry, Law, Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, or any faculty or school where existing facilities and resources are limited\.

If you wish to opt in to the full suite of student fees, please email [non\.degree@ubc\.ca](mailto:non.degree@ubc.ca)\.

Expect to pay about $100 to $150 per course for course materials, which are available through the [UBC Bookstore](http://www.bookstore.ubc.ca/)\.

### How to apply as a senior citizen

Follow the same application instructions as mentioned above, then email [non\.degree@ubc\.ca](mailto:non.degree@ubc.ca) with proof of your date of birth as well as British Columbia \(BC\) residency, such as a photocopy of your BC driver’s license\. Include your UBC reference or student number\.

## Concurrent high school students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-access\-studies/\#concurrent\-high\-school\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-access-studies/#concurrent-high-school-students)

To be considered for concurrent studies at UBC as an Access Studies student, you must meet all of the following requirements:

-   You are in good academic standing and have a [superior academic record](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/admissions/applicants-following-bcyukon-secondary-school-curriculum/concurrent-enrolment-policy)\.
-   You are enrolled in a British Columbia secondary school in a program that meets [regular UBC entry requirements](http://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/canadian-highschools)\.
-   You are 16 years of age or older at the start of studies\.
-   You can provide a written recommendation from your high school principal\.
-   You have the written consent of your parent or legal guardian\.

### How to apply as a high school student

Submit your application and required materials by the deadline, and UBC Admissions will submit them to your chosen faculty for evaluation\.

Once you have been approved by the faculty for enrolment in courses at UBC, you will receive an email with confirmation and registration instructions\. Submit supporting documentation to [non\.degree@ubc\.ca](mailto:non.degree@ubc.ca) with your full name and UBC reference \(student\) number\.

Access studies is not intended for recent high school graduates who have already applied to an undergraduate degree program at UBC or wish to pursue one in the near future\.
