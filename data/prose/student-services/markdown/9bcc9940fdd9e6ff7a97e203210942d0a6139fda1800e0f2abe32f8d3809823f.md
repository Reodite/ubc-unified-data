## About UBC Collegia[https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/ubc\-collegia/\#about\-ubc\-collegia](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/ubc-collegia/#about-ubc-collegia)

Are you an incoming first\-year commuter student or Indigenous student?

Collegia offers welcoming, lounge\-like spaces located across the UBC Vancouver campus, perfect for studying, relaxing, or connecting with your peers between classes\. It’s also a community where you can ask questions, get guidance from upper\-year students, and build friendships that can support you throughout your time at UBC\.

[View map of locations](https://www.google.com/maps/d/viewer?mid=1nduxHQ3D_Y83GJf8h6eUPdchoYUiTZ-a&amp;femb=1&amp;ll=49.26642999663815%2C-123.25320005&amp;z=16)

### Collegia programs

There are 2 Collegia programs available at the UBC Vancouver campus:

-   **First Year UBC Collegia**  
    Available to first\-year undergraduate students who don’t live in a UBC residence and have graduated high school within the last 2 years\.
-   **Šxʷta:təχʷəm**  
    For self\-identifying Indigenous graduate and undergraduate students\. By registering, you’ll gain access to both the first\-year Collegia community, as well as the [šxʷta:təχʷəm](https://fnhl.ubc.ca/students/sx%ca%b7tat%c9%99%cf%87%ca%b7%c9%99m/) community\.

## Reasons to join Collegia[https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/ubc\-collegia/\#reasons\-to\-join\-collegia](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/ubc-collegia/#reasons-to-join-collegia)

-   **Find community**  
    Join regular small group gatherings to build friendships, ask questions to your peers and upper\-year students, and have fun together at social events\.
-   **Get support from peers**  
    Learn from the experiences of upper\-level student leaders and hear how they’ve navigated their university journey so far\.
-   **Discover resources and opportunities**  
    Explore your interests through involvement opportunities and student resources, all while building your community at university\.

## About the space[https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/ubc\-collegia/\#about\-the\-space](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/ubc-collegia/#about-the-space)

Your UBC Collegium is a place where you can take a break between classes to socialize, use a kitchen, or relax in a comfortable, friendly, and welcoming environment\.

Amenities in each space include:

-   Bright, open space with floor\-to\-ceiling windows 
-   Kitchen facilities with an oven, microwaves, refrigerators, small appliances, dishes, and cutlery
-   Study spaces with desks, lamps, and shelves
-   Comfortable lounge chairs, sofas, and fireplace
-   Bathroom and shower facilities with lockers
-   Secure Wifi and plenty of outlets to charge your devices
-   A social area with board games, cards, and activities

## How to join First Year UBC Collegia[https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/ubc\-collegia/\#how\-to\-join\-first\-year\-ubc\-collegia](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/ubc-collegia/#how-to-join-first-year-ubc-collegia)

To be eligible for the UBC Collegia program, you must meet the requirements below:

-   You’re in first\-year standing of a direct\-entry undergraduate program at UBC\.
-   You don’t live in UBC residence\.
-   You’ve graduated from high school within the past 2 years\.

If you’re registered for Jump Start 2026, you’ll be automatically enrolled in the year\-long UBC Collegia program\.

While Indigenous students of all years are welcome at [šxʷta:təχʷəm](https://fnhl.ubc.ca/students/sx%ca%b7tat%c9%99%cf%87%ca%b7%c9%99m/) without requiring registration, first\-year Indigenous students who commute to campus are especially encouraged to register for the First Year UBC Collegia program\.

## UBC Collegia hours[https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/ubc\-collegia/\#ubc\-collegia\-hours](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/ubc-collegia/#ubc-collegia-hours)

### First Year Collegia

First Year Collegia is open **Monday to Friday** from 9:00 am to 7:00 pm during the Winter Session \(September to April\)\. These hours may change during exam periods\. First Year Collegia is closed on all [statutory holidays](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/statutory-holidays) observed by UBC\.

### šxʷta:təχʷəm

šxʷta:təχʷəm is open on weekdays during the Winter Session \(September to April\) except on all [statutory holidays](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/statutory-holidays) observed by UBC\. The hours below may change during exam periods: 

-   **Mon to Thurs**: 9:30 am to 7:00 pm
-   **Fri**: 9:30 am to 6:00 pm
