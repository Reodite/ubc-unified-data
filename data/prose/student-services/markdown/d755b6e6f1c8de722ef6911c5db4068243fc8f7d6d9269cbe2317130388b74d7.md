Some UBC students have a portion of their UBC fees paid by a sponsor such as an Indigenous community, employer, or government agency\. This is called sponsorship billing\.

For an organization to sponsor your studies at UBC, it must meet the required criteria and apply before the start of the term\. Both the student and sponsor should notify the Sponsorship Billing Office at [ubc\.sponsorship@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.sponsorship@ubc.ca) right away if there are changes to the sponsoring agency or the coverage amount\.
