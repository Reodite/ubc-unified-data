## Academic accommodations[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/academic\-learning\-resources/academic\-accommodations\-disabilities/types\-academic\-accommodations/\#academic\-accommodations](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-accommodations-disabilities/types-academic-accommodations/#academic-accommodations)

Academic accommodations are intended to provide students with a disability or ongoing medical condition access to the academic environment\. They do not apply to temporary injuries or acute medical conditions that are not expected to persist for longer than one term\.

While this page describes the common types of academic accommodations at UBC, the specific accommodations you’re eligible for will be determined by your Accessibility Advisor\. You must register with the [Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/) to request an academic accommodation\.

View the [Accommodation Inventory](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/cfa_accommodation_inventory.pdf) \(pdf\) for a comprehensive list of accommodation examples and information on the accommodation decision\-making process\.

## Alternate format materials[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/academic\-learning\-resources/academic\-accommodations\-disabilities/types\-academic\-accommodations/\#alternate\-format\-materials](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-accommodations-disabilities/types-academic-accommodations/#alternate-format-materials)

Alternate format materials allow students with print disabilities to access academic materials in other formats such as digital audio, accessible PDF, and electronic text\.

If you are eligible for this accommodation, submit your [Alternate Format Request Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0Tbtus5o0IoZpDU) following the instructions provided in your alternate format welcome letter\.

Production times for materials vary depending on the format\. If possible, please send all requests **4 to 6 weeks** before the start of the course to avoid delays\. Materials will be sent to the email address you have on file, so make sure your contact information is up to date\.

## Assistive technology[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/academic\-learning\-resources/academic\-accommodations\-disabilities/types\-academic\-accommodations/\#assistive\-technology](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-accommodations-disabilities/types-academic-accommodations/#assistive-technology)

The Centre for Accessibility facilitates access to a range of assistive technology\. If you require software or other specialized equipment, please contact your Accessibility Advisor to explore your options\. You may be eligible for funding support\.

## Captioning and ASL interpreting[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/academic\-learning\-resources/academic\-accommodations\-disabilities/types\-academic\-accommodations/\#captioning\-and\-asl\-interpreting](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-accommodations-disabilities/types-academic-accommodations/#captioning-and-asl-interpreting)

ASL interpreting, real\-time captioning, and captioning media are available for eligible Deaf and hard of hearing students\.

### If you are eligible for this accommodation

If you require this accommodation, you must register with the Centre for Accessibility as early as possible or as soon as you have been offered admission and before the start of your program\.

You’ll be required to submit a [Request for Classroom Captioning or Interpreting Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3PigwE9Ian5OBOm) if you are eligible\. 

## Requesting Note\-taking support[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/academic\-learning\-resources/academic\-accommodations\-disabilities/types\-academic\-accommodations/\#requesting\-note\-taking\-support](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-accommodations-disabilities/types-academic-accommodations/#requesting-note-taking-support)

Your eligibility to request Note\-taking support is determined by your Accessibility Advisor\. It does not replace your attendance requirements\.

Most students will be provided with a free subscription to Genio note\-taking software\. This software supports autonomous note\-taking through customizable features, including audio recording, transcription, and integration of course notes\. Genio Notes allow quick access to your class notes immediately after class while supporting active engagement with course materials during class\. You will be required to sign an agreement to use the software\. 

If Genio Notes does not meet your accessibility needs, please contact your Accessibility Advisor to explore alternate options\.

Once you are assigned Genio Notes as your note\-taking support, please follow the steps below:

1.  Access the [ClockWork student portal](https://clockwork.as.it.ubc.ca/ClockWork/custom/misc/home.aspx) and navigate to “Online Forms”\.
2.  Carefully review and complete all sections of the “Student Agreement for using Genio Notes” and submit it\. You will receive a confirmation email while your submission is under review\.
3.  Once we have received and approved the Agreement form, you will receive an activation email from Genio with instructions on how to access Genio and set up your account\.

Your Genio account will be linked to your UBC student number and the email you have listed in Workday\.

If you have previously signed up for a Genio demo account, this can be linked to your licensed account\. Please contact [access\.assistants@ubc\.ca](mailto:access.assistants@ubc.ca) for support\.

If you no longer need Genio Notes, please email access\.assistants@ubc\.ca as soon as possible\.

## How to request an academic accommodation[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/academic\-learning\-resources/academic\-accommodations\-disabilities/types\-academic\-accommodations/\#how\-to\-request\-an\-academic\-accommodation](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-accommodations-disabilities/types-academic-accommodations/#how-to-request-an-academic-accommodation)

The Centre for Accessibility typically works with students to arrange an academic accommodation for permanent, chronic, or long\-term conditions\. To request an academic accommodation, you must register with the [Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/)\. An Accessibility Advisor will determine the accommodations you are eligible for\. 

If you are experiencing a temporary injury or acute medical condition that is not expected to persist for longer than one term, please connect with [Academic Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/)\.
