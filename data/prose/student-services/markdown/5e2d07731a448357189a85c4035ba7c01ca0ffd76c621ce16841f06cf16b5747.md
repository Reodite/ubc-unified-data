## About the space[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/brock\-commons\-south\-community\-space/\#about\-the\-space](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/brock-commons-south-community-space/#about-the-space)

The Brock Commons South Community Space is a multi\-purpose room available to undergraduate and graduate students currently enrolled at UBC Vancouver\. It’s a space to pray, reflect, worship, meditate, contemplate, and build community\.

The Brock Commons South Community Space is located in Room 4104 of [Brock Commons South](https://www.maps.ubc.ca/?code=BRCS) \(6180 Walter Gage Road\)\. 

### Amenities

To ensure the space can accomodate a variety of events and activities, the following amenities are available:

-   Accessible building entrances, room entrance, and elevators
-   All\-gender washroom located nearby
-   Kitchen facilities, including a microwave, refrigerator, and dishwasher
-   36 chairs for community gathering
-   7 moveable tables and 1 stationary table
-   Screen and audiovisual \(AV\) system for presentations
-   An adjacent [Prayer and Quiet Contemplation Room](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/brock-commons-south-multifaith-prayer-and-quiet-contemplation-room/)

## Booking the space[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/brock\-commons\-south\-community\-space/\#booking\-the\-space](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/brock-commons-south-community-space/#booking-the-space)

The Brock Commons South Community Space can be booked for community programming that serves the faith\-based and spiritual student community at UBC\. Bookings are available Monday to Friday from 7:00 am to 10:00 pm, excluding [statutory holidays](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/statutory-holidays) observed by UBC\. 

To book the space, you must be one of the following:

-   Student from AMS and GSS faith\-based and spiritual clubs at UBC Vancouver
-   The Office of the Vice\-President, Students \(VPSO\) staff member
-   Chaplain at UBC

### How to book the space

If you are interested in booking the space, please follow these steps:

1.  Make sure your programming complies with the [booking guidelines](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/brock_commons_south_community_space_booking_guidelines.pdf) \(pdf\)\.
2.  Check the space availability on [Calendly](https://calendly.com/bcs-communityspace-ubc/event-booking)\. Please do not book on Calendly, as registration happens separately\.
3.  Submit a [booking request form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_eb9Xb1CkQ3OolKe)\.
