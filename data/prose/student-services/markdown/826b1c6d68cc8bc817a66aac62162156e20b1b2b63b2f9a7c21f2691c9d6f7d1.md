## Legal name changes[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/changing\-name\-student\-record/\#legal\-name\-changes](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-student-record/#legal-name-changes)

If your legal name is incorrect on UBC’s official records, such as your transcript, parchment, or student card, and you wish to be known by your correct legal name, you can request a name change\.

To have your name on your parchment changed after graduation, you will need to [order a replacement parchment](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-parchment-diploma/)\.

## Documents that require your legal name at UBC[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/changing\-name\-student\-record/\#documents\-that\-require\-your\-legal\-name\-at\-ubc](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-student-record/#documents-that-require-your-legal-name-at-ubc)

You will need your legal name listed for the documents below:

-   Official letters \(including but not limited to admission, enrolment, award letters, and student loans\)
-   Tax forms
-   Transcripts
-   Parchments
-   Residence contracts

## How to update your legal name[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/changing\-name\-student\-record/\#how\-to\-update\-your\-legal\-name](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-student-record/#how-to-update-your-legal-name)

You can request a legal name change on your UBC record in person or online\.

You must follow a separate process to [request a name change](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-parchment-diploma/) for your parchment\. There is a deadline to submit name change requests before graduation\.

**

Submit your documents in person

**

Bring your legal documents and UBCcard \(student ID\) to the [Enrolment Services Advising Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) in Brock Hall\.

**

Upload your documents online

**

Contact an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to change your legal name on your UBC record\. They will follow up with a secure link for you to upload your legal documents online\.

Please do not email copies of your documents directly to Enrolment Services\.

## Preferred or chosen name changes[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/changing\-name\-student\-record/\#preferred\-or\-chosen\-name\-changes](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-student-record/#preferred-or-chosen-name-changes)

### About preferred or chosen names

A preferred or chosen name is a name that you commonly use that is different from your legal name\. It is what you want to be called\.

While we use the term “preferred name”, for many people, this is not just a preference\. It is the only name they use and is an essential part of their identity\. It is especially important for transgender and non\-binary students whose university experience and wellbeing can be negatively impacted when their preferred or chosen name is not used\. Read more about [names and their impact](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/gender-diversity/names/?login) at UBC\.

### Updating your preferred or chosen name

Inappropriate use of a preferred or chosen name such as attempts to avoid a legal obligation via misrepresentation, or the use of inappropriate language, will result in the denial and/or reversal of the request and can be disciplined through [Academic or Non\-Academic Misconduct](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-non-academic-misconduct-student-code-conduct#19702)\.

The earlier you update your preferred or chosen name, the more likely you will experience the consistent use of your preferred or chosen first name by UBC faculty, instructors, and staff\.

Learn how to [update your preferred name](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/updating-your-preferred-name/) in Workday\.

You should do this even if you have provided your preferred or chosen name when applying to UBC\.

### Use of a preferred or chosen first name

Below are places where UBC uses a student’s preferred name:

-   Class lists
-   UBCcard \(if requested\)
-   Canvas
-   Workday
-   Student Housing Online Service Centre
-   UBC Recreation
-   Counselling Services
-   Student Health Services \(except in places where MSP requires use of your legal name\)
-   Graduation Application
-   Graduation ceremony booklet \(if requested\)
-   Graduation ceremony \(read aloud as you cross the stage, if requested\)

Find [more information](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-parchment-diploma/) on requesting your preferred name to be printed in the graduation ceremony booklet and read aloud as you cross the stage\.

Students may still see their legal name in some online systems and communications due to the complexity of UBC’s information systems and limitations in data sharing\. UBC is working to create a more unified experience that would only display legal given names when legally required\.

#### Possible impact of using a preferred or chosen name

While UBC allows students to indicate their preferred or chosen name without changing their legal name, it’s important to think about the possible impact of this practice:

-   Official documents are often used to verify one’s identity when applying for work or additional education\. Some employers, licensing bodies, or other educational institutions may question the use of a preferred or chosen name in daily or informal correspondence\. This discrepancy happens when institutions rely on the consistent use of legal names\.
-   If someone is using a preferred or chosen name consistently, this may cause some confusion in situations where official documents have to be provided\. For example, employers might be confused about a UBC student portfolio that uses a preferred or chosen name while the transcripts use a legal name\.

#### Update your UBCcard

Your preferred or chosen name can be displayed instead of your legal name on your UBCcard\.

1.  If needed, [update your preferred name](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/updating-your-preferred-name/) in Workday\. Please wait 48 hours for the change to appear on the system\.
2.  Contact the [UBC Carding Office](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/contact-us) in the UBC bookstore and request a card renewal with your preferred or chosen name\.

There is no charge to get a new UBCcard with your preferred or chosen name on the card\. Find more information about [UBCcard renewals](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/policies) by selecting “Name and status changes” under *Policies*\.

## Updating your citizenship status[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/changing\-name\-student\-record/\#updating\-your\-citizenship\-status](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-student-record/#updating-your-citizenship-status)

If you become a Permanent Resident or Canadian Citizen during your time at UBC, you can update your status in person at the [Enrolment Services Advising Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) in Brock Hall\.

You must bring one of the following pieces of identification:

-   Confirmation of Permanent Residence \(COPR\) and a government\-issued photo ID 
-   Your Permanent Resident card

### Impacts of updating your citizenship status

Read more information in the [UBC Academic Calendar](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/policies-fees#17962) about how your tuition may be impacted when you update your citizenship status\.

Changes to your citizenship status may also impact your [iMED health coverage](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international/)\. Please contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) if you have questions related to your health insurance\.

Changes to your citizenship status will also impact awards offered to you as an international student\.

## Indigenous self\-identification[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/changing\-name\-student\-record/\#indigenous\-self\-identification](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-student-record/#indigenous-self-identification)

UBC has a number of unique programs and services for [Indigenous students](https://indigenous.ubc.ca/students/), including [scholarships and bursaries](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-indigenous/)\. Indigenous self\-identification helps UBC offer and manage these programs and services\. 

As an Indigenous student, you can self\-identify when you apply to UBC, update your Workday student profile, or submit your graduation application\. Learn how to [add your Indigenous Self\-Identification](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/updating-your-indigenous-self-identification/) in Workday\.
