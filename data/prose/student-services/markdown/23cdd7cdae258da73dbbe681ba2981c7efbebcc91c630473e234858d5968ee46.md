## About the role[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/lfs\-academic\-career\-engagement\-ace\-team/\#about\-the\-role](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/lfs-academic-career-engagement-ace-team/#about-the-role)

The LFS Academic and Career Engagement Team \(ACE Team\) focuses on planning and implementing programming related to academic success, wellness and career development\.

Team members develop project management and communication skills, increase their knowledge of student resources, and expand their network and professional relationships\.

The team is divided into the following committees:

-   **Communications Committee**  
    Preparing and developing communications content for LFS ACE events and workshops, LFS Student Services on platforms including the [LFS Student Services Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/lfsnewslettuce/)\.
-   **Events Committee**  
    Planning and implementing LFS ACE events, such as Let’s Talk About Research and LFS: Career Stories with LFS Alumni\.
-   **Workshops Committee**  
    Preparing, coordinating, and facilitating academic, wellness, and career\-related workshops for LFS undergraduate students including, but not limited to, Bounce Back in Term 2 and Mapping Your Major\.

## Who can apply?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/lfs\-academic\-career\-engagement\-ace\-team/\#who\-can\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/lfs-academic-career-engagement-ace-team/#who-can-apply)

LFS undergraduate students in second year and above in good academic standing can apply for the role\.

Each year, there are typically **18 to 30 roles available**, with 6 being Team Lead positions\. Team Lead positions are hired before LFS ACE student leader committee members\. Learn about the [Team Lead applications](http://www.landfood.ubc.ca/undergraduate/lfs-academic-and-career-engagement-team/)\.

### If you require accommodations

The Peer Programs Program Advisors would like to recognize that being able to participate in student leadership opportunities is a privilege\. The weekly commitment for each Peer Program varies in length but may not be possible for some students who need accommodations based on chronic health conditions, disabilities, mental health, and/or other lived experiences\.

If you have specific accommodations for your ability to participate and contribute to the program, please connect with Thilini Leitan, LFS Student Engagement Officer to discuss what accommodations can be made for your particular situation for participation in the LFS ACE Team\.

### Additional application questions

You will be required to list your committee preference and respond to 3 additional short answer questions

1.  Why do you want to be involved as a student leader in the LFS ACE Team? 
2.  What knowledge and skills do you hope to learn through the role? 
3.  Please identify 2 to 3 strengths and/or qualities that you will bring to the LFS ACE Team\. Be sure to refer to your past experiences that have helped you develop them\.
