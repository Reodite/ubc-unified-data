## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/biology/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/biology/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying Biology, you will take lecture, laboratory, or field courses\. You’ll develop an appreciation for the diversity, adaptability, and complexity of life\. You can also gain an understanding of all the fundamental aspects of biology such as cell and molecular biology, genetics, ecology, physiology, and organismal diversity\.

The skills you will develop may include:

-   Experimental design, data collection, analysis, and presentation
-   Laboratory and/or field work techniques and safety protocols
-   Statistical analysis and quantitative reasoning
-   Collaboration and communication with peers
-   Critical reading and evaluation of relevant scientific literature

## Make the most of your specialization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/biology/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-specialization](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/biology/#make-the-most-of-your-specialization)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[UBC Biological Sciences Club](https://www.facebook.com/UBCBIOSOC/)**  
    Attend an annual Biology student career night in term 2 or other events throughout the year\.
-   **[UBC Sustainability programs and clubs](http://www.sustain.ubc.ca/get-involved)**  
    Browse on\-campus paid and volunteer opportunities related to sustainability\.
-   **[UBC Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/biology)**  
    Gain work experience in biology between study terms\.
-   **[UBC Life Sciences Institute](http://lsi.ubc.ca/news-events/events/)**  
    Find research opportunities, events, programs, and student competitions in life sciences\.
-   **[Bamfield Marine Sciences Centre](http://www.bamfieldmsc.com/education/prospective-students)**  
    Take selected field courses in coastal and marine sciences for university credit\.
-   **[Zoology research opportunities](https://www.zoology.ubc.ca/research)**  
    For research positions related to zoology, contact a faculty member directly\.
-   **[Faculty of Medicine Summer Research](https://www.med.ubc.ca/current-learners/summer-student-research-program/)**  
    Gain experience in undergraduate medical research at UBC\.
-   [UBC Pathology and Laboratory Medicine summer program](https://pathology.ubc.ca/education-resource/summer-student-fellowship-program/)  
    Apply for funding and propose your research project to gain clinical experience in laboratory medicine\.
-   **[BC Children’s Hospital research program](https://bcchr.ca/ssrp)**  
    Participate in an undergraduate research project related to child and family health\.
-   **[Online tutorials and training in ethics](https://ethics.research.ubc.ca/education-training/online-tutorials-training)**  
    Develop your understanding of research ethics and integrity through online training and tutorials\.
-   **[UBC Medical Journal](https://ubcmj.med.ubc.ca/submissions/)**  
    Submit your academic research, case reports, reviews, commentaries, and news or letters for publication\.
-   **[Let’s Talk Science](http://outreach.letstalkscience.ca/ubc/get-involved.html)**  
    Check out volunteer opportunities to engage children and youth in STEM learning\.
-   **[UBC Gearing Up summer camps](https://www.geeringup.apsc.ubc.ca/staff/volunteering/)**  
    Volunteer as a camp instructor or counselor for high school and elementary school students\.
-   **[Beaty Biodiversity Museum](https://beatymuseum.ubc.ca/get-involved/join-the-team/)**  
    Learn through exhibits and volunteer opportunities at the on\-campus museum\.
