## About the funding[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/funding\-former\-youth\-care/\#about\-the\-funding](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/funding-former-youth-care/#about-the-funding)

As a former youth in care, you may be eligible for the following awards:

-   BC Provincial Tuition Waiver Program
-   UBC Tuition Waiver Program
-   Learning for Future Grant
-   SAJE \(Strengthening abilities and journeys of empowerment\)

When seeking tuition waiver funding, please complete and return applications to both the BC Provincial Tuition Waiver and the UBC Post Care Tuition Waiver at least 4 weeks before the end of your study period\. If you are eligible for both programs, you will only receive funding through the BC Provincial Tuition Waiver Program\. Both waiver programs fund your first undergraduate degree program at UBC\.

## BC Provincial Tuition Waiver Program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/funding\-former\-youth\-care/\#bc\-provincial\-tuition\-waiver\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/funding-former-youth-care/#bc-provincial-tuition-waiver-program)

The BC Provincial Tuition Waiver Program funds eligible students with experience in care in British Columbia, and covers the cost of a first undergraduate degree\.

You must meet the [BC government criteria](http://studentaidbc.ca/explore/grants-scholarships/provincial-tuition-waiver-program#Am_I_eligible) to receive this funding\.

### How to apply

Submit your application at least 4 weeks before the end of your study period\.

1.  Apply for admission to UBC\.
2.  Download and complete the [Provincial Tuition Waiver Program Application Form](https://studentaidbc.ca/sites/all/files/form-library/provincial-tuition-waiver-program-application.pdf) \(pdf\)\.
3.  When you’re admitted to UBC, email your completed application to [joseph\.stevens@ubc\.ca](mailto:joseph.stevens@ubc.ca)\.

## UBC Tuition Waiver Program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/funding\-former\-youth\-care/\#ubc\-tuition\-waiver\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/funding-former-youth-care/#ubc-tuition-waiver-program)

The UBC Post Care Tuition Waiver is for eligible students who have lived as youth in care in British Columbia and covers the cost of the tuition fees of a first undergraduate degree\.

### Eligibility requirements

You must first be enrolled at UBC for your first undergraduate degree\. You must also have lived as a youth in care in British Columbia under one of the following conditions for a period of at least 12 months \(consecutive or accumulated\) and/or at the time you turned 19 years old:

-   Temporary Custody Order
-   Continuing Custody Order
-   Youth Agreement
-   Adoption
-   Extended Family Plan
-   Interim Custody Order
-   Interim Custody Order \(to a person other than a parent\)
-   Permanent Transfer of Custody \(to a person other than a parent\)
-   Temporary Transfer of Custody Order
-   Under Guardianship of Director \(Adoptions Act\)
-   Under Guardianship of Director \(Infants Act\)
-   Voluntary Care Agreement
-   Special Needs Agreement
-   Child in Home with a Relative Program \(CiHR\)

### How to apply

Submit your application at least 4 weeks before the end of your study period\.

1.  Apply for admission to UBC\.
2.  Download the [UBC Post Care Tuition Waiver application](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/fffyic_post_care_tuition_waiver_application.pdf) \(pdf\)\.
3.  When you’re admitted to UBC, email your completed form to [joseph\.stevens@ubc\.ca](mailto:joseph.stevens@ubc.ca)\.

## Learning for Future Grant[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/funding\-former\-youth\-care/\#learning\-for\-future\-grant](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/funding-former-youth-care/#learning-for-future-grant)

The Learning for Future Grant is available to full\-time or part\-time students who meet the eligibility criteria requirements for the Provincial Tuition Waiver Program and are enrolled in a program or course that leads, or will lead, to a credential at the undergraduate level\.

Find out more at [StudentAidBC](https://studentaidbc.ca/explore/grants-scholarships/learning-future-grant)\.

## SAJE \(Strengthening abilities and journeys of empowerment\)[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/funding\-former\-youth\-care/\#saje\-strengthening\-abilities\-and\-journeys\-of\-empowerment](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/funding-former-youth-care/#saje-strengthening-abilities-and-journeys-of-empowerment)

The [SAJE Program](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/youth-and-family-services/youth-transitions) is for young people with care experience\. This program provides support and services until the age of 27\.

The SAJE program supports eligible youth in areas such as:  

-   Housing 
-   Life skills and Training  
-   Counselling  
-   Mental Wellness and Cultural connections 

You can be eligible for SAJE if you meet one of the following: 

-   You reached your 19th birthday while on a Youth Agreement, Continuing Custody Order or were under the personal guardianship of a director
-   You achieved permanency between the ages of 12 to 19 \(i\.e\., adoption after being in care, or under section 54\.1 or 54\.01\)
-   You have 24 months of cumulative time in care between the ages of 12 to 19, in a range of in\- and out\-of\-care statuses 
-   You were in a comparable arrangement under Indigenous law

Check to see if you’re eligible for the program using the [SAJE Eligibility Check](https://submit.digital.gov.bc.ca/app/form/submit?f=2ebff64f-5b17-4029-996e-f4f65aa12af3)\.

Contact the SAJE program at [866 623 3001](tel:18666233001) or by email at [MCF\.SAJE@gov\.bc\.ca](mailto:MCF.SAJE@gov.bc.ca)\.

## Additional resources[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/funding\-former\-youth\-care/\#additional\-resources](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/funding-former-youth-care/#additional-resources)

### Aged Out

The [Aged Out online resource](https://agedout.com/) was created by and for youth who have been in care or are currently in care\. The interactive website includes topics such as health and wellness, housing, and ID needs\.

### UBC Centennial Scholars Entrance Awards

The [UBC Centennial Scholars Entrance Award](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-canadian-students/entrance-award/) program is designed for students transitioning to UBC from secondary school or another post\-secondary institution\. The program financially supports academically qualified students who show an interest in joining and contributing to the UBC community, but would not be able to attend without significant financial assistance\.

Through the program, new students can access a variety of one\-time and renewable awards of up to $40,000, payable over four years\. If you’re studying at UBC Vancouver, there are opportunities to receive full\-ride awards that cover the cost of your degree and living expenses\.
