## 1098\-T tax form[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/information\-for\-us\-tax\-returns/\#1098\-t\-tax\-form](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/information-for-us-tax-returns/#1098-t-tax-form)

If you are filing taxes in the United States, you may be asked to produce [Form 1098\-T, Tuition Statement](https://www.irs.gov/uac/form-1098-t-tuition-statement), which is used for qualified tuition and related expenses paid during the tax year\. 

UBC is an eligible educational institution because of its participation in the US Direct Loan Program\. However, UBC does not issue the Form 1098\-T, Tuition Statement\. As a Canadian institution, UBC only provides Canadian federal tax receipts, such as the [T2202](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/taxes/#t2202-tax-form)\.

Claiming tuition and related expenses without a 1098\-T

For the most up\-to\-date information on claiming tuition and related expenses without a 1098\-T, learn more about [Publication 970, Tax Benefits for Education](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-publication-970)\.

## Codes for your tax return[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/information\-for\-us\-tax\-returns/\#codes\-for\-your\-tax\-return](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/information-for-us-tax-returns/#codes-for-your-tax-return)

American students can use their US IRS Employer Identification Number to claim education tax credits on their income tax\. Parents filling out the [education tax credits form 8863](http://www.irs.gov/uac/Form-8863,-Education-Credits-%28American-Opportunity-and-Lifetime-Learning-Credits%29) may be eligible for a tax credit\. Visit [American Opportunity Credit](http://www.irs.gov/publications/p970/ch02.html) for details\.

Keep the following numbers handy for filing your tax return\.
