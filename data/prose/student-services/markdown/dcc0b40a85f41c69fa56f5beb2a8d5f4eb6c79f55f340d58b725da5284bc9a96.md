## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/math/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/math/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Mathematics](https://science.ubc.ca/students/programs/mathematics), you’re learning how to take a complex problem and frame it in a logical way\. You’ll develop important skills and master proofs as a way to show others your thinking and reasoning\.

These skills may include:

-   Statistical and data analysis
-   Organizational interpretation
-   Deductive reasoning
-   Logical, systematic thought processes
-   Computer literacy
-   Advanced quantitative skills
-   Numerical computation
-   Technical skills in mathematical concepts, logic, and specialized software

## Make the most of your specialization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/math/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-specialization](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/math/#make-the-most-of-your-specialization)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[UBC Math Club](https://ubc-math-club.github.io/)**  
    Meet other UBC mathematics students through events and programs\.
-   **[UBC Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/students)**  
    Gain work experience in mathematics between study terms\.
-   **[Putnam Math Competition](https://www.maa.org/math-competitions/putnam-competition)**   
    Test your mathematical understanding in the prestigious undergraduate math exam\.
-   **[Mitacs funding](https://www.mitacs.ca/en/programs)**  
    Apply for up to $15,000 to fund a four\-month internship in your specific area of study\.
-   **[UBC Math Colloquium](https://www.math.ubc.ca/~yhkim/colloquium/colloquium.html)**  
    Attend talks about research in mathematics\.
-   **[Departmental research opportunities](https://www.math.ubc.ca/)**  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\. 
-   **[UBC Mathematics outreach initiatives](http://outreach.math.ubc.ca/)**  
    Interact with elementary and high school students through workshops and sessions\.
-   **[Math job board](https://www.mathjobs.org/jobs/jobs)**   
    Check out employment opportunities related to mathematics\.
