## About the roles[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/ubc\-orientation\-transition\-leadership/jump\-start\-collegia\-leader/\#about\-the\-roles](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/jump-start-collegia-leader/#about-the-roles)

As a Jump Start Leader or Collegia Advisor, you’ll support new students as they transition into university life, helping to build connection, confidence, and community\. You’ll work closely with the [Centre for Student Development and Leadership](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-student-development-leadership/) staff and other student leaders to create welcoming, engaging experiences where students connect with others and learn what to expect in their first year\. These roles will support programs including [Jump Start](https://you.ubc.ca/jump-start-vancouver/), [Collegia](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/ubc-collegia/), and [Imagine UBC](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/orientations/imagine-ubc/)\.

### Jump Start roles 

Jump Start Senior Orientation Leaders, Jump Start Orientation Leaders, and Jump Start Events Crew focus on the delivery of the Jump Start program for a one\-week period leading up to the beginning of classes in September\. 

### Collegia roles

Collegia Advisors and Senior Collegia Advisors focus on year\-long community\-building and student support, beginning with Jump Start and continuing throughout the Winter Term\.

## Skills and qualifications[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/ubc\-orientation\-transition\-leadership/jump\-start\-collegia\-leader/\#skills\-and\-qualifications](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/jump-start-collegia-leader/#skills-and-qualifications)

To be eligible for the roles, you must demonstrate the following:

-   Strong leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills
-   Excellent organization and time management
-   Ability to lead and facilitate programs, activities, or group experiences with students
-   Ability to adapt to new experiences and challenges with a positive mindset
-   Comfort working independently and collaboratively with peers and staff
-   Ability to build welcoming, inclusive communities for students from diverse backgrounds and lived experiences
-   An interest to engage in ongoing personal and professional development opportunities
-   The ability to represent UBC and its student leadership programs in a positive and professional manner

## What will you get out of the role?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/ubc\-orientation\-transition\-leadership/jump\-start\-collegia\-leader/\#what\-will\-you\-get\-out\-of\-the\-role](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/jump-start-collegia-leader/#what-will-you-get-out-of-the-role)

Jump Start Leaders and Collegia Advisors build hands\-on leadership skills and help shape the first\-year student experience at UBC\.

As a leader or advisor, you will:

-   Build confidence in your leadership, facilitation, and teamwork skills
-   Strengthen your ability to connect with and support diverse student communities
-   Gain insight into how orientation and transition programs shape the first\-year experience
-   Make a tangible, positive impact on incoming students while growing as a leader yourself
