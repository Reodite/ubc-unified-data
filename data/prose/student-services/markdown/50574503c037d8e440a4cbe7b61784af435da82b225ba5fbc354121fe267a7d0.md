Your parchment refers to your diploma\.

The name on your official student record will be the name printed on your parchment, but there are certain acceptable name changes you can request\.

If you’re a UBC Okanagan student, review [information and instructions](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/graduation/changing-the-name-on-your-diploma/) specific to your campus\.

## Updating your legal name[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/changing\-name\-parchment\-diploma/\#updating\-your\-legal\-name](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-parchment-diploma/#updating-your-legal-name)

If you have recently changed your legal name, or previously did not include your middle name and want it to be added to your parchment, you must notify UBC by [updating your name](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-parchment-diploma/) on your official student record\. The legal name on your student record will be the name printed on your parchment\.

The deadline to update your legal name on your student record and on your [graduation application](https://ubc.appiancloud.com/suite/sites/graduation-management-application) for your parchment for Spring graduation is **Fri, Apr 17, 2026**\. After that date, you will be required to order a [replacement parchment](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-parchment-diploma/)\.

### If you’ve already submitted your graduation application

Please email [graduation@ubc\.ca](mailto:graduation@ubc.ca) after you have [updated your legal name](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-student-record/#how-to-update-your-legal-name) with Enrolment Services to ensure your graduation application and parchment are also updated\.

Updating your official student record will not automatically update your graduation application\.

## Using your preferred name for the upcoming graduation ceremony[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/changing\-name\-parchment\-diploma/\#using\-your\-preferred\-name\-for\-the\-upcoming\-graduation\-ceremony](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-parchment-diploma/#using-your-preferred-name-for-the-upcoming-graduation-ceremony)

If you would like to have your [preferred or chosen name](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-student-record/#preferred-or-chosen-name-changes) read as you cross the stage for the Spring 2026 graduation ceremony and included in the ceremony program, submit a request on your [graduation application](https://ubc.appiancloud.com/suite/sites/graduation-management-application) by **Fri, April 17, 2026**\. This request does not change the name printed on your parchment\.

You do **not** need to submit a request if you want your legal name to be used, since it’s recorded in Workday automatically\.

## Other acceptable changes to your parchment \(diploma\)[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/changing\-name\-parchment\-diploma/\#other\-acceptable\-changes\-to\-your\-parchment\-diploma](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-parchment-diploma/#other-acceptable-changes-to-your-parchment-diploma)

-   An unused first name may be abbreviated to initials, such as “J\. Mary Smith”\.
-   Middle names may be abbreviated to initials or omitted from the parchment\.
-   If you are attending UBC under a married name, your birth name can be printed on your parchment upon presentation of appropriate ID\. Your birth name can be added to your records for future reference if necessary\.
-   Changing the order of your legal name \(eg\. first name, last name\) is not permitted\.
