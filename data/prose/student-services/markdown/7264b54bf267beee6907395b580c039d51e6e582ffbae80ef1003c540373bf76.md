## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/combined\-major\-in\-science/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/combined-major-in-science/#skills-youll-develop)

During your [Combined Major in Science](http://www.cms.science.ubc.ca/) degree, you’re studying the world of scientific concepts, theories, and principles across multiple disciplines\. You’ll develop important skills in data interpretation, modelling, and communication to apply in the fields you choose to study\.

These skills may include:

-   Mental agility to draw connections and identify relationships across disciplinary boundaries
-   Collaborative research in diverse teams
-   Experimental design and execution
-   Laboratory techniques specific to the disciplines you combine
-   Computing skills and data manipulation
-   Quantitative interpretation of data
-   Reporting on experimental results and statistical modeling
-   Critical evaluation and communication of scientific findings

## Make the most of your specialization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/combined\-major\-in\-science/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-specialization](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/combined-major-in-science/#make-the-most-of-your-specialization)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[Combined Major in Science Student Association](https://www.facebook.com/ubccmssa/)**  
    Meet other UBC students with similar interests through events and programs\.
-   **[UBC Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/gsci)**  
    Gain work experience between study terms\.
-   **[UBC undergraduate research in Chemistry](https://www.chem.ubc.ca/undergraduate-research)**  
    Get involved in summer opportunities or take classes that incorporate lab experience in chemistry\.
-   **[UBC Physics research](https://www.phas.ubc.ca/research-areas)**  
    Work in a research role to support faculty on physics research projects\.
-   **[UBC Life Sciences Institute](http://lsi.ubc.ca/news-events/events/)**  
    Discover research opportunities, events, programs, and student competitions in life sciences\.
-   **[Bamfield Marine Sciences Centre](http://www.bamfieldmsc.com/education/prospective-students)**  
    Take selected field courses in coastal and marine sciences for university credit\.
-   **[UBC Mathematics outreach initiatives](http://outreach.math.ubc.ca/)**  
    Interact with elementary and high school students through workshops and sessions\.
-   **[Mitacs Globalink Research Award](https://www.mitacs.ca/en/programs/globalink/globalink-research-award)**  
    Apply to participate in 12 to 24\-week research projects overseas\.
-   **[UBC Medical Journal](https://ubcmj.med.ubc.ca/submissions/)**  
    Submit your academic research, case reports, and reviews for publication\.
