[Arts Peer Academic Coaches](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/arts-peer-academic-coaches-apac/)

[LFS Academic and Career Engagement Team](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/lfs-academic-career-engagement-ace-team/)

Join a team of LFS students who create programming to support the academic and career experiences of their peers\.

[SCI Team](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/sci-team/)

Join a team of dedicated Science students to create personal and professional development resources, videos and events for their peers\.

[Vantage Peer Mentors](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/vantage-peer-mentors/)

Support first\-year international students in their transition to campus life and plan events to enhance their university experience\.

[Science Peer Academic Coaches](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/science-peer-academic-coaches-spac/)

Join a team of dedicated Science students who provide academic coaching support to their peers\.

[Kinesiology Peer Academic Coaches](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/kinesiology-peer-academic-coaches-kpac/)

Join a team of dedicated upper\-year Kinesiology students who coach their peers on academic skills and tasks\.

## Interest\-specific peer programs[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/\#interest\-specific\-peer\-programs](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/#interest-specific-peer-programs)

[UBtheChange Community Activators](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/ubthechange-community-activators/)

Partner with local community organizations to co\-design short\-term projects and mentor fellow UBC students\.

[UBtheChange Student Activators](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/ubthechange-student-activators/)

Ignite pro\-social action at UBC by creating accessible events and campaigns that empower students to engage with social issues\.

[Peer Health Educators](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/peer-health-educators/)

Educate your fellow peers all about physical and mental health and student wellbeing\.

[Associate Career Advisors](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/associate-career-advisors/)

Apply to support your peer UBC students with their career and professional development through one\-on\-one coaching conversations\.

[Food Hub Market Volunteers](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/food-hub-market-volunteers/)

Help run the at\-cost grocery store on campus, and support your peers in building food skills and awareness of food security resources\.

[Chapman Learning Commons Peer Advisors](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/about/chapman-learning-commons-assistants/)

Join student assistants to facilitate student\-led support in academics, study skills, and personal growth\.

[MoveU Crew](https://recreation.ubc.ca/get-moving/move-u-crew/)

Apply to be a movement specialist to encourage and enhance campus wellbeing\.
