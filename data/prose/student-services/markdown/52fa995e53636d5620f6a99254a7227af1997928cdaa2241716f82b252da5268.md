## Who you are matters[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/forestry/\#who\-you\-are\-matters](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/forestry/#who-you-are-matters)

Your Forestry degree is just one part of what you have to offer\. You also bring skills and knowledge from experiences you’ve had, places you’ve been, and the values you share with family, friends, and community\.

Reflecting on your skills and interests can help you make life and career decisions\.

-   [Identify your values](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/identifying_your_values.pdf) \(pdf\)  
    Clarifying your interests and values can help you determine career possibilities that are a good fit for you\.
-   [Know your strengths](https://store.gallup.com/p/en-ca/10265/cliftonstrengths-for-students-top-5)  
    Purchase the CliftonStrengths assessment tool \($28 CAD\) using your [student email address](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/ubc-student-email-service?upn=) to identify and describe your talents\.

## Share your compelling story[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/forestry/\#share\-your\-compelling\-story](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/forestry/#share-your-compelling-story)

Throughout your Forestry degree, you’ll develop specific skills that employers are looking for when hiring graduates, including:

-   Application of natural sciences and social science fundamentals to consider complex issues relating to natural resources and the environment
-   Leadership and project management, including resource planning and risk assessment
-   Creative and strategic problem solving for local and global challenges using an innovative approach, while considering environmental and social impact
-   Ability to understand, examine, and synthesize concepts while considering multiple perspectives
-   Effective written and oral communication skills to present ideas to a range of audiences
-   Data collection, analysis, and interpretation through research within field and lab environments
-   Familiarity with specialized tools, equipment, and modern technologies
-   Collaboration with others through projects and assignments
-   Critical thinking and analysis

## Get experience[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/forestry/\#get\-experience](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/forestry/#get-experience)

UBC offers many opportunities for Forestry students to build their careers\.

### Workplace experience

-   [Work Learn](https://students.ubc.ca/career/work-learn-program/)  
    Build work experience through a part\-time, on\-campus job\.
-   [UBC Forestry Co\-op Program](https://forestry.ubc.ca/students/co-op/)  
    Work in your area of study while completing your degree\.
-   [On\-campus jobs](https://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-jobs/)  
    Find student jobs available on campus\.
-   [Careers Online](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/index.php)  
    Browse work or volunteer positions on UBC’s online career resources platform\.
-   [UBC Forestry Get Hired series](https://forestry.ubc.ca/students/co-op/about-co-op/events/employer-information-sessions-get-hired/)  
    Attend Info Sessions hosted by different employers and organizations looking to hire Forestry students\.
-   [Forestry Digest job postings](https://forestry.ubc.ca/students/undergraduate-student-portal/forestry-digest/)  
    Browse and apply for work opportunities posted through Forestry’s weekly email newsletter\.

### Research

From volunteering in a lab to getting a research award, there are many possibilities to build your research experience and prepare for graduate school or a career in research\.

[Find opportunities](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-research/)

### Involvement and leadership

-   [Forestry Undergraduate Society \(FUS\)](https://www.ubcfus.com/)  
    Join your student council to plan events and advocate on behalf of Forestry undergraduate students\.
-   [Centre for Community Engaged Learning](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-community-engaged-learning/)  
    Get involved at a local school or non\-profit organization\.
-   [Student Directed Seminars](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/student-directed-seminars/)  
    Propose, coordinate, and lead your own seminar class for credit\.
-   [Student leadership opportunities](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/)  
    Take on a student leadership role with Residence Life, a Peer Program, Orientations, Collegia, and many more campus groups\.
-   [Volunteer opportunities for Forestry students](https://forestry.ubc.ca/students/undergraduate-student-portal/forestry-digest/)  
    Find opportunities on\- and off\-campus in the Faculty of Forestry’s weekly email newsletter\.

### International experience

Living, studying, and working abroad builds traits employers look for when hiring\. Show employers you are adaptable, can work independently, and have global experience\.

[Browse options](https://students.ubc.ca/career/international-experiences/)
