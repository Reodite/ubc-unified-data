## How to change your contact information[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/changing\-contact\-information/\#how\-to\-change\-your\-contact\-information](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-contact-information/#how-to-change-your-contact-information)

Learn how to add or update your [contact information](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/updating-your-contact-information/) in Workday\.

To authorize another person to access your UBC information, such as medical information, awards and scholarships, or housing details, you will need to grant them [third\-party authorization](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/third-party-authorization/)\. Third\-party\-authorized individuals are not able to update information on your file\.

### Update your emergency contact information

Make sure you and your emergency contact can receive emergency and urgent information from UBC\.

Log into [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/) each year and review your emergency contact details\.

Learn how to [add your emergency contacts](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-your-emergency-contacts/) in Workday\.

## If you need help[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/changing\-contact\-information/\#if\-you\-need\-help](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-contact-information/#if-you-need-help)

If you’re having difficulty updating your information online, you can reach out to an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) for support\.
