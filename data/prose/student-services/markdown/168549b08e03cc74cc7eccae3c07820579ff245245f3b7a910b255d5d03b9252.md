Tuition fees are reviewed annually by the Board of Governors\. In recent years, tuition increases have been 2% for continuing domestic students and 3% for continuing international students\.

The 2026/27 fees have been approved by the UBC Board of Governors on Dec 4, 2025 and are effective on May 1, 2026\.

For international undergraduate students starting their degree programs in the 2026/27 academic year, tuition will increase by no more than 3% each year for the subsequent 4 years or until graduation from their current degree program, whichever comes first\.

The tuition amounts below are for students starting in 2026 Summer Session and 2026/27 Winter Session\. All fees are in Canadian dollars\.

### How UBC determines your tuition and fees

At UBC, your citizenship status typically determines whether you’ll pay domestic or international tuition fees, but there may be an exception\. If you’re an international student, find out whether you meet the [eligibility requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/financial-support-options/financial-support-indigenous/international-tuition-exemption/) to be assessed domestic student fees\.

Report any changes to your citizenship as soon as possible to [Enrolment Services](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/changing-name-student-record/#updating-your-citizenship-status)\.
