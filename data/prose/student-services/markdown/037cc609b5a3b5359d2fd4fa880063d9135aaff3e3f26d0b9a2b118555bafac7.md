## Who you are matters[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/engineering/\#who\-you\-are\-matters](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/engineering/#who-you-are-matters)

Your Engineering degree is one part of what you have to offer\. You also bring knowledge and skills from experiences you’ve had, places you’ve been, and values you share with others\. 

Analyzing your skills and assessing your interests can help you make strategic life and career decisions\.

-   [Identify your values](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/identifying_your_values.pdf) \(pdf\)  
    Clarifying your interests and values can help you determine career possibilities\.
-   [Know your strengths](https://store.gallup.com/p/en-ca/10265/cliftonstrengths-for-students-top-5)  
    Purchase the CliftonStrengths assessment tool \($28 CAD\) using your [student email address](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/ubc-student-email-service?upn=) to identify and describe your talents\. Engineering Co\-op students can contact their Co\-op Coordinator to learn how to access the resource for free\.

## Find your competitive edge[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/engineering/\#find\-your\-competitive\-edge](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/engineering/#find-your-competitive-edge)

Throughout your Engineering degree, you’ll develop specific skills that employers are looking for, including:

-   Application of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering fundamentals to design solutions that reflect professionalism and protect the public
-   Familiarity with specialized tools, equipment, and modern technologies
-   In\-depth computational analyses and statistical techniques to understand and interpret complex data and processes
-   Identification of project requirements through research, economic analyses, and feasibility tests
-   Collaboration in multi\-disciplinary team environments
-   Leadership and project management, including resource planning, risk assessment, implementation, monitoring, and attention to ethics, accountability, and equity
-   Oral and written communication of technical design concepts to different audiences
-   Investigation and analysis of problems, demonstrating a commitment to life\-long learning
-   Problem solving for complex issues using an innovative and big\-thinking approach, while considering the impact of engineering on society and the environment

### Workplace experience

-   [Applied Science Co\-op](https://coop.engineering.ubc.ca/)  
    Work in your area of study while completing your degree\.
-   [Work Learn](https://students.ubc.ca/career/work-learn-program/)  
    Build work experience through a part\-time, on\-campus job\.
-   [On\-campus jobs](https://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-jobs/)  
    Find student jobs available on campus\.
-   [CareersOnline](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/index.php/pid389509?)  
    Browse and apply for work or volunteer positions using UBC’s online career resources platform\.

### Research

From volunteering with a faculty member to getting a research award, there are many ways to gain undergraduate research experience\.

-   [Mitacs](https://www.mitacs.ca/en/programs#student)  
    Apply for up to $15,000 to fund a 4\-month internship or participate in a research project abroad\.
-   [UBC research opportunities](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-research/)  
    Check out student research opportunities, including conferences and awards\.

### Involvement and leadership

-   [Engineering Design Teams](https://teams.engineering.ubc.ca/)  
    Join a team to develop hands\-on technical experience\.
-   [Design and Innovation Day](https://design-innovation.apsc.ubc.ca/)  
    Showcase your capstone or research project to faculty and industry guests\.
-   [Women in Engineering](https://wie.engineering.ubc.ca/)  
    Participate in professional development workshops and networking opportunities\.
-   [Community opportunities](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-community-engaged-learning/)  
    Get involved at a local school or non\-profit organization\.
-   [Student Directed Seminars](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/student-directed-seminars/student-directed-seminar-courses/)  
    Propose, coordinate, and lead your own seminar class for credit\.
-   [Engineers for a Sustainable World](https://www.eswglobal.org/)Work in a multi\-disciplinary team and apply your technical knowledge to solve various sustainability challenges\.
-   [Engineers Without Borders](https://www.ewb.ca/en/)Join an interdisciplinary team to tackle complex social issues locally or globally\.
-   [UBC Gears and Queers](https://www.instagram.com/gears.and.queers)  
    Help cultivate a welcoming community and foster a safe space for queer and trans engineering students\.
-   [UBC Geering Up](https://geeringup.apsc.ubc.ca/)  
    Develop your leadership skills and share your passion for STEM with the children and youth of BC\.
-   [AMS student clubs](https://amsclubs.ca/)  
    Join a club based on your interests and meet new friends\.
