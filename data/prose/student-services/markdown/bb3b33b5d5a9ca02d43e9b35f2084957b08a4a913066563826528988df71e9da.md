UBC is a Title IV institution, which means eligible students and parents can borrow directly from the US Department of Education through the US Federal Direct Loan Program\. 

At UBC, the available types of Direct Loans include: 

-   [Direct Subsidized Loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-direct-loans/#direct-subsidized)
-   [Direct Unsubsidized Loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-direct-loans/#direct-unsubsidized)
-   [Parent PLUS Loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-direct-loans/#parent-plus-loans)
-   [Grad PLUS Loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-direct-loans/#grad-plus-loans) \(available only to legacy borrowers as of July 1, 2026\)

Other Title IV funding programs such as Pell Grants, Federal Supplemental Educational Opportunity Grants, and the Federal Work\-Study Program are not available at UBC\.

UBC can provide Direct Loan funding to students who meet the [basic eligibility requirements](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/eligibility/requirements?custom2=a9ce5bba-1bfd-4de0-a985-3bce21885f4a&amp;og1=a9ce5bba-1bfd-4de0-a985-3bce21885f4a&amp;tg1=opg_fafsa&amp;tg5=%7Btg5%7D&amp;tg7=%7Btg7%7D&amp;tg8=%7Btg8%7D&amp;tg9=%7Btg9%7D&amp;utm_source=government-assistance.org&amp;utm_medium=crm-guide_crm-1pe&amp;utm_campaign=%7Btg4%7D&amp;utm_content=%7Btg6%7D&amp;utm_term=%7Btg10%7D)\.

Maximum funding amounts depend on whether you’re classified as a dependent or an independent student by the answers you provided on your [Free Application for Federal Student Aid](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa) \(FAFSA\)\. Please read the [Federal StudentAid guide](https://studentaid.gov/sites/default/files/fafsa-dependency.pdf) \(pdf\) to determine if you are a dependent or independent student\.

If you have questions about your funding eligibility or assessment, please contact an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\.

## Direct Subsidized Loans

Direct Subsidized Loans are offered to eligible undergraduate students with demonstrated financial need\. Financial need is determined by the [Student Aid Index \(SAI\)](https://studentaid.gov/help-center/answers/article/what-is-sai) from your Free Application for Federal Student Aid \(FAFSA\)\. 

The US Department of Education pays the interest on a Direct Subsidized Loan during the following periods:

-   While you’re enrolled in school at least [half\-time](https://studentaid.gov/help-center/answers/article/half-time-enrollment)
-   During the first 6 months after you leave school \(referred to as a grace period\)
-   During a period of deferment \(a temporary postponement of loan payments\)

## Direct Unsubsidized Loans

[Direct Unsubsidized Loans](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/subsidized-unsubsidized) are another type of federal student loans offered by the U\.S\. Department of Education, available to eligible undergraduate and graduate students\. There is no requirement to demonstrate financial need, and interest accumulates during all periods\.

## Direct Parent PLUS Loans

[Parent Plus Loans](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/plus/parent) can help parents pay for the educational expenses of dependent children studying at the undergraduate level\. As of July 1, 2026, Parent Plus Loans are capped at $20,000 USD per student per year, with a $65,000 USD lifetime limit per student\. Interest is charged during all periods\. 

Continuing undergraduate students with previous Direct Loans may be eligible for an exception to the new limits for up to 3 years\. Contact [usloans\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:usloans.inquiry@ubc.ca) for information about your eligibility\.

## Direct Grad PLUS Loans

As of July 1, 2026, the [Grad PLUS Loan program](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/plus/grad) has ended\. 

Continuing graduate students may be eligible to continue borrowing Grad PLUS Loans for up to 3 years\. Contact [usloans\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:usloans.inquiry@ubc.ca) for information about your eligibility\.

## Eligibility for the Direct Loan program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/us\-direct\-loans/\#eligibility\-for\-the\-direct\-loan\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-direct-loans/#eligibility-for-the-direct-loan-program)

To maintain your Direct Loan eligibility, you must not enroll in any online courses during your degree, even during periods in which you are not receiving Direct Loan funding\.

### Additional eligibility requirements

**

Undergraduate student requirements

**

All undergraduate student applicants must meet all the following requirements:

1.  You must be registered in and maintain at least 6 credits for each term for which you’re applying for funding:
    -   You must be registered only for in\-person and on\-campus courses\.
    -   Registered courses must be for credit\. Audited and waitlisted courses do not meet this requirement\.
    -   For Summer Session eligibility, you must be registered in courses across both terms from May to August\.
2.  You must maintain Satisfactory Academic Progress throughout your studies\. Refer to the [policy guidelines](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/ubc_satisfactory_academic_progress_undergrads.pdf) \(pdf\) for more information\.This includes maintaining a cumulative average of 60% and completing at least 67% of cumulative credits attempted at the end of each term\.

**

Graduate student requirements

**

All graduate student applicants must meet the following requirements:

1.  You must be considered full\- time and be enrolled in at least one course for each term in which you’re applying for funding:
    -   You must be registered only for in\-person and on\-campus courses\.
    -   Registered courses must be for credit\. Audited and waitlisted courses do not meet this requirement\.
    -   For Summer Session eligibility, you must be registered in courses across both terms from May to August\.
2.  You must maintain [Satisfactory Academic Progress](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/ubc_satisfactory_academic_progress_grads.pdf) throughout your studies\. Refer to the [policy guidelines](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/ubc_satisfactory_academic_progress_undergrads.pdf) \(pdf\) for more information\. This includes maintaining a cumulative average of 68% and completing your program of study in a timely manner\.

**

If you are a Canadian citizen or Permanent Resident of Canada

**

You may be eligible for [Canadian Student Loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/) through your home province\. 

If you have any residency affiliation**,** through you or your parents, to the province of Ontario, you may be an exception\. Please contact your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) for more information\.

**

Co\-op terms

**

Co\-op is a great way to gain work experience as you study\. Most co\-op placements arranged through an official [UBC Co\-op](https://coop.ubc.ca/) program are eligible for Direct Loan funding as long as they are not located in the United States\.

If you’re considering doing your Co\-op in the US, please connect with an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to fully understand the implications to your future Direct Loan eligibility\. Co\-op placements in the US might make you ineligible for future Direct Loan funding\.

If you’re a graduate student, please check with your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to confirm if your Co\-op is eligible for loans\.

**

Exchange programs

**

Many opportunities are offered by [Go Global](https://global.ubc.ca/go-global/international-experiences) for students to spend a term or year studying abroad\.

If you’re thinking about going on exchange and you’re eligible for US Direct Loans, contact an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to understand your financial options before you apply for exchange\.

In addition, check the US [Federal School Code Lists](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/resource-type/Federal%20School%20Code%20Lists) to confirm that your partner university is also eligible for Title IV funds\.

If you choose to do an exchange at a school in the US, you could lose your eligibility for Direct Loans for the rest of your program\. We do not recommend going on exchange to a US school if you require federal loans\.

If your partner university is Title IV and **not located in the US**, UBC can assess you for Direct Loans while you’re on exchange\.

If your partner university is not Title IV, you will not be eligible to receive Direct Loans for the period of your exchange\. You may want to consider [private loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-private-student-loans/) instead\. 

## Applying for a US Direct Loan[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/us\-direct\-loans/\#applying\-for\-a\-us\-direct\-loan](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-direct-loans/#applying-for-a-us-direct-loan)

The process of applying for a US Direct Loan at UBC is different from the process for students who attend a school in the US\.

When you complete your [Free Application for Federal Student Aid](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa) \(FAFSA\), the Department of Education will notify UBC of your application\.

### How to apply

1.  Submit your [Free Application for Federal Student Aid](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa) \(FAFSA\)\. Make sure to select UBC from the school list\. UBC’s school code is G08369\.
2.  Submit a UBC [US Direct Loan Request Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_77cbe5dqz3TGKKa)\.
    -   As a foreign school, UBC requires additional information and cannot assess you for Direct Loans until you have submitted this form\.
    -   If you make changes to your FAFSA after submitting your request form, please email [usloans\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:usloans.inquiry@ubc.ca) so UBC can determine if the updates will affect your assessment\.
    -   Read the Privacy Notice: Your personal information is collected under the authority of section 26\(c\) of the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act \(FIPPA\)\. This information will be used for the purpose of evaluating your US Direct Loan application while attending UBC\. In order to participate in the Title IV Direct Loan program, UBC is required to share your personal information with the US Department of Education, and their agents and contractors\. Questions about the collection of this information may be directed to [usloans\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:usloans.inquiry@ubc.ca)\.
3.  If you are going to apply for PLUS Loans, [complete your application](https://studentaid.gov/plus-app/) on the Federal Student Aid website\.
4.  Fill out a [Master Promissory Note](https://studentaid.gov/mpn/) \(MPN\) for each type of loan you wish to receive\. There are separate MPNs for Direct Subsidized and Direct Unsubsidized Loans, as well as Parent and Grad PLUS Loans\. New MPNs are required each year at foreign schools\.
5.  If this is your first Direct Loans application at UBC, complete the [Loan Entrance Counselling](https://studentaid.gov/entrance-counseling/) requirement\.
6.  Verify your email\. Log into your [Workday account](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/) and make sure UBC has your current email address\. Learn how to [update your personal information](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-changing-your-contact-information/) in Workday\. All US Direct Loan updates and information will be emailed to you at this email address\.

### Application timeline

You’re recommended to apply for Direct Loans at least **4 weeks** before the start of the term\. This will ensure that you receive your funding when the term begins\. 

However, you can apply for a Direct Loan and submit a [US Loan Request Form](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/#applying-for-a-us-direct-loan) up to 2 weeks before the end of your study period\.

Any [late payments](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/late-payment-fees/) may result in a financial hold applied to your tuition account\.

If you have a Canadian bank account, you’re required to set up [direct deposit](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/finances/setting-up-your-direct-deposit/)\. Direct deposit allows the Direct Loan amount exceeding your fees to be deposited directly into your bank account\.

UBC uses the yearly loan limits and lifetime \(or aggregate\) limits listed on the [Federal Student Aid website](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/subsidized-unsubsidized), as set by the US Department of Education, to determine your eligibility for Direct Subsidized and Direct Unsubsidized Loans\.
