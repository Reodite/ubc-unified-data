## About the Centre for Community Engaged Learning

The [Centre for Community Engaged Learning](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-community-engaged-learning/) collaborates with students, staff, faculty, and community partners to work through complex community\-based issues, both locally and internationally\. Their programs connect students with community \(non\-profits, community initiatives, and K to Grade 12 schools\) either as a required part of an academic course or through voluntary co\-curricular opportunities like UBtheChange and the Social Impact Lab for a hands\-on, immersive experience\.
