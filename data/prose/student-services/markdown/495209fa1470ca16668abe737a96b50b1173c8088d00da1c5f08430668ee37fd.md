## Getting a job

Find tips and suggestions for job\-searching or preparing a job application\.

[Job search](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/job-search/)

Whether you’re looking for a part\-time or full\-time role, learn how to use targeted strategies to improve your chance of success\.

[Resumes and cover letters](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/resumes-cover-letters/)

Learn how to identify and articulate employable skills in ways that make your application stand out\.

[Curricula Vitae](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/curricula-vitae/)

Know how to highlight your education and accomplishments on the academically\-focused curricula vitae\.

[Interviews](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/interviews/)

Identify what employers are looking for in interviews and strengthen your approach to speaking about your experience, skills, and interests\.

[Job offer negotiations](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/job-offer-negotiations/)

When you start a full\-time position, there may be room to negotiate your salary, benefits, vacation time, and more\. Get tips to build your case and anticipate their response\.

## Networking and meeting professionals

Gain expert insight from people working in your field of interest\.

[Making the most of LinkedIn](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/make-most-linkedin/)

Build your personal brand and strengthen your employability on the most popular networking platform in North America\.

[Networking](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/networking/)

Develop relationships to get advice, referrals, and inside information from experts in the field\.

[Informational interviews](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/informational-interviews/)

Talk to an industry professional to get a better understanding of the job or field\.

[What to do before attending career events](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/what-do-before-attending-career-events/)

Prepare to meet alumni and employers and ask questions that matter to you\. 

[What to do after attending career events](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/what-do-after-attending-career-events/)

Take next steps with the people you met and the opportunities you learned about\. 

## Exploring future career possibilities

Many tools and resources exist to help you reflect on your career goals and interests\.

[Self\-assessment tools and resources](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/self-assessment-tools-resources/)

Trying out an analytical tool may give you insights about who you are, what matters to you, and what you’re good at\. This can help you make informed decisions about your career goals\.

[Design your career](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/design-career/)

If you want to be very intentional in your decision\-making, the design process and mindset will help you clarify your goals, prototype options and make data\-informed career decisions\.

[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/career\-resources/career\-resources\-historically\-marginalized\-students/](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/career-resources-historically-marginalized-students/)[Career resources for students from specific communities](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/career-resources-for-students-from-specific-communities/)

Find resources tailored for students from diverse and intersecting communities, including 2SLGBTQIA\+ students, students with disabilities, and graduate students\.
