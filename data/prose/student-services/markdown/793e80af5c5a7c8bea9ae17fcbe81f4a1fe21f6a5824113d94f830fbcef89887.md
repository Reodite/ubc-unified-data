Most exams are automatically scheduled on the Vancouver campus\. Review the table below for standard final exam periods and when the exam schedule is normally available for each term\. 

Some exams require an application and may follow a different exam schedule, including [Standing Deferred and Supplemental exams](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/exams/standing-deferred-supplemental-exams/)\.

All exams follow [UBC policies and regulations](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/exams/exam-clashes-hardships-cancellations/)\. Make sure you understand your responsibilities before you write your exam\. 

## Final exam schedule[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/exams/\#final\-exam\-schedule](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/exams/#final-exam-schedule)

View the [UBC exam schedule](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/exam-schedule/) to find final exam times, dates and locations when they are released\. You can also view your [personalized exam schedule](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/degree-planning/viewing-your-exam-schedule/) in Workday\.
