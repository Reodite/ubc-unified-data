## What is an ITN?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/taxes/individual\-tax\-number\-itn/\#what\-is\-an\-itn](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/taxes/individual-tax-number-itn/#what-is-an-itn)

An Individual Tax Number \(ITN\) is a unique number that Canada Revenue Agency \(CRA\) uses to identify you for tax purposes if you aren’t eligible for a [Social Insurance Number \(SIN\)](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/taxes/social-insurance-number-sin/)\.

If you are an international student who is not eligible for a SIN, you will need an ITN if:

-   **You received a scholarship or award from UBC\.**  
    As an incoming student, you can accept scholarships or awards without a SIN or ITN\. You must enter a SIN or ITN into Workday by January so UBC can issue your [T4A tax forms](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/taxes#t4a-tax-form)\. Learn how to [upload your SIN or ITN](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-your-social-insurance-number-sin-or-international-tax-number-itn/) in Workday\.
-   **You want to file a tax return\.**  
    As an international student now living in Canada, you may need to [file a tax return](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/taxes/filing-taxes-canada/) depending on your situation\.

## Applying for an ITN[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/taxes/individual\-tax\-number\-itn/\#applying\-for\-an\-itn](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/taxes/individual-tax-number-itn/#applying-for-an-itn)

If you are not eligible for a SIN, follow [step\-by\-step instructions](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/taxes/individual-tax-number-itn-apply-individual-tax-number-itn/) to apply for your ITN\.

Make sure you **apply early,** as it can take up to 8 weeks to process your application\. If you receive an award, or are planning to file Canadian taxes, you should apply as soon as you arrive in Canada\. If you have not received a reply within 8 weeks, call the CRA for an update on the status of your application\. You can check to [confirm the processing times](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/international-non-residents-tax/in-outside-canada/apply-individual-tax-number.html#how) for your application\.

## If you have questions[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/taxes/individual\-tax\-number\-itn/\#if\-you\-have\-questions](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/taxes/individual-tax-number-itn/#if-you-have-questions)

For questions about the ITN, call the [Sudbury Tax Centre](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/international-non-residents-tax/in-outside-canada/apply-individual-tax-number.html#contact)\.

Contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/) for questions related to immigration, health insurance, and life as an international student in Canada\.
