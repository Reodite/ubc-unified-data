The Marine Drive Residence Prayer Room is located in the Hong Kong Ballroom of the [Marine Drive Residence](https://www.maps.ubc.ca/?locat1=377-3)\.

The prayer room is open to the Muslim Student Association and the Thaqalayn Muslim Association only for Friday congregational prayers\.

## Before using the room[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/marine\-drive\-residence\-prayer\-room/\#before\-using\-the\-room](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/marine-drive-residence-prayer-room/#before-using-the-room)

Please review the [guidelines for use](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/#prayer-room-rules-and-responsibilities) and follow all prayer room rules and responsibilities\.

If you’re hosting an event that serves the faith\-based or spiritual student community at UBC, please book the [Brock Commons South Community Space](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/brock-commons-south-community-space/)\.

## Prayer room hours[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/marine\-drive\-residence\-prayer\-room/\#prayer\-room\-hours](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/marine-drive-residence-prayer-room/#prayer-room-hours)

The prayer room is available on **Fridays** from **11:30 am to 3:00 pm**\.

## Reporting a problem about the facilities[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/marine\-drive\-residence\-prayer\-room/\#reporting\-a\-problem\-about\-the\-facilities](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/marine-drive-residence-prayer-room/#reporting-a-problem-about-the-facilities)

If you encounter any issues with the prayer room or its use, please report it as soon as possible to the Office of the Vice\-President, Students at [vpsassist@mail\.ubc\.ca](mailto:vpsassist@mail.ubc.ca) or the Marine Drive Front Desk at [marine@housing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:marine@housing.ubc.ca)\.

Concerns may include cleanliness, damage, lighting, misuse of these facilities, or complaints about other users\.

### Urgent concerns

In case of fire or emergency evacuation, follow the procedures posted in the rooms\. In the event of an incident or concern that requires attention, contact [UBC Security](https://security.ubc.ca/) by calling [604\.822\.2222](tel:6048222222)\. For emergencies that require an immediate response, call 911\.
