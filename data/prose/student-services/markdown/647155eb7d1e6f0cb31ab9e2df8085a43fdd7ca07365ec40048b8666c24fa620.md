UBC students can receive medical care from a range of healthcare providers, including family physicians, nurse practitioners, specialists, and primary care nurses\. Appointments are offered in person or by phone or video, depending on your needs and preferences\.

If you have a medical provider you already see within your community, we encourage you to connect with them before booking an appointment with Student Health and Wellbeing\. This can support your continuity of care throughout your time at UBC and after you graduate\.

Located on Level 3 of the Gateway Health building at [5955 University Boulevard](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DyvFtS5BbVfefndE9), Student Health and Wellbeing medical providers can support you with:

-   General health concerns
-   Preventive care, including vaccinations and immunization reviews
-   Sexual and reproductive health, such as STI testing, contraception, and pap tests
-   [Mental health care](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/), including diagnosis, treatment, and prescription support
-   Sports Medicine concerns
-   [Allergy shots](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/allergy-shots/)
-   Injuries and wound care
-   Naloxone kit distribution and training
-   Referrals to specialists and other services
-   Completion of third\-party medical forms

## Emergency situations[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus/\#emergency\-situations](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/#emergency-situations)

If you are injured, in danger, or in urgent need of medical care or crisis support, get immediate support using one of the options below:

-   Call [911](tel:911) or visit your [nearest 24/7 emergency department](https://www.vch.ca/your-care/emergency-care) for after\-hours support\.
-   Go to an [Urgent Primary Care Centre](https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/primary-care/service-type/urgent-and-primary-care-centres)\.

If you or someone you know is feeling hopeless or at risk of harm, call or text [988](tel:988) toll\-free at any time to reach the [Suicide Crisis Helpline](https://988.ca/)\.

## What to expect for appointments[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus/\#what\-to\-expect\-for\-appointments](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/#what-to-expect-for-appointments)

### In\-person appointments

Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled time\. You may be asked to fill out some forms\. Then, you’ll meet with a doctor or nurse practitioner to discuss your health concerns\.

### Phone appointments

If your appointment is by phone, the call may come from an unlisted number or a number without caller ID\.

### Changing or cancelling your appointment

If you need to cancel your appointment, please provide us with at least 1 to 3 days advance notice\.  
  
You can cancel or change your appointment by logging into the [online booking platform](https://ubcshw.inputhealth.com/portal)\. You can also use the platform to read and respond to clinic messages and update your contact information\.  
  
Appointments cancelled without sufficient notice may have a [cancellation fee](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/student-health-wellbeing-clinic-fees/) applied\.

### Fees

Most appointments are covered by your provincial health plan \(such as MSP\), so there is usually no cost for your visit\. Some forms and non\-insured services have fees\. Review the complete [list of fees](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/student-health-wellbeing-clinic-fees/)\.

### Specialist referrals

In British Columbia, a referral from a doctor or nurse practitioner is needed in order for you to see a specialist physician\. If needed, your doctor or nurse practitioner may refer you to a specialist\. 

Student Health and Wellbeing specialists include allergists, dermatologists, gynecologists, psychiatrists, sports medicine specialists, and respirologists\. Depending on your needs, we may also refer to a specialist physician in the community\.

## Confidentiality notice[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus/\#confidentiality\-notice](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/#confidentiality-notice)

Information you share with Student Health and Wellbeing is confidential\. Your personal health information is protected and is not shared with your faculty or your Workday Student account\.

Aggregate information may be used within Student Health and Wellbeing and shared more broadly at UBC for service planning, quality improvement, and reporting\. This means your information is combined with other students’ information, without names, to improve programs and services\.

Personal information about you and your health can only be shared outside UBC with your permission, or when safety concerns or legal requirements arise\.

If you have any questions about confidentiality, or anything else, please ask during your appointment\. If you wish to release your medical record information to another individual or medical care office, call [604 822 3811](tel:6048223811)\.
