## Eligibility for Unclassified Studies[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-unclassified/\#eligibility\-for\-unclassified\-studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/#eligibility-for-unclassified-studies)

As an unclassified student, you can continue to take courses at UBC even after completing a recognized 4\-year bachelor’s degree program\.

To be eligible, you must meet all of the following requirements:

-   You meet the [English language requirement](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/english-language-competency/)\.
-   You have completed a bachelor’s degree from a recognized institution\.
-   You can provide a final official transcript showing your degree conferral\.

If you’re a senior citizen, there are additional steps you’ll need to follow, which you can review in [the section below](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/#senior-citizens)\.

If you plan to take Faculty of Education courses only as an unclassified student, apply directly to the [Faculty of Education](http://teach.educ.ubc.ca/admissions/visiting-and-unclassified-students/)\.

## Considerations for unclassified students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-unclassified/\#considerations\-for\-unclassified\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/#considerations-for-unclassified-students)

-   Depending on the time of year, it may take several weeks for your documents to reach UBC Admissions and be processed\.
-   Being admitted as an unclassified student does not guarantee enrolment in requested courses or admission to a degree program at UBC\.
-   Credits from your courses may be transferable to a future UBC degree program at the discretion of the faculty you apply to\.
-   Non\-Degree students are not eligible for [Credit/D/Fail grading](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/creditdfail-grading/)\. However, you may consider [auditing courses](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/auditing-courses/)\.

## If you’ve graduated from UBC[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-unclassified/\#if\-youve\-graduated\-from\-ubc](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/#if-youve-graduated-from-ubc)

UBC graduates, including alumni of undergraduate or graduate degrees and limited diploma programs, do not need to submit an application or pay an application fee to become an unclassified student\. It is granted upon request\. The above application deadlines do not apply\.

Please email [non\.degree@ubc\.ca](mailto:non.degree@ubc.ca) to confirm your eligibility for the Vancouver campus\. For the Okanagan campus, please email [nondegree\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:nondegree.ok@ubc.ca) with your details\.

Make sure you include the following information:

-   UBC student number
-   Date of birth
-   Dates you attended UBC
-   Intended start date
-   Campus preference: Vancouver or Okanagan
-   Citizenship status \(Canadian citizen, permanent resident, study permit\)

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-unclassified/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/#how-to-apply)

Complete the steps below even if you’ve previously studied at UBC:

1.  Create an [Education Planner BC account](https://apply.educationplannerbc.ca/) online\.
2.  Fill in your personal information and academic history\.
3.  Choose “Start Applying” and select University of British Columbia as the institution\.
4.  Select the appropriate Admissions Category based on your academic history\.
5.  Select “Unclassified Student” under *First Program Choice*\. You do not need to select a second program choice\.
6.  UBC Admissions will email you with a confirmation of your application and next steps\.
7.  After completing your application, email any additional and required documents to [non\.degree@ubc\.ca](mailto:non.degree@ubc.ca)\.

## Senior citizens[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-unclassified/\#senior\-citizens](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-unclassified/#senior-citizens)

If you are a Canadian citizen or permanent resident that is 65 years of age or older, you’re eligible to apply through Unclassified Studies to take courses for general interest if you meet all the requirements below:

-   You are a British Columbia resident\.
-   You hold a bachelor’s degree from a recognized institution\.

### Tuition costs for senior citizens

Normally, senior citizens do not pay [tuition or student fees](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/policies-fees#18205), except for programs within Dentistry, Law, Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, or any faculty or school where existing facilities and resources are limited\.

If you wish to opt into the full suite of student fees, please email [non\.degree@ubc\.ca](mailto:non.degree@ubc.ca)\.

Expect to pay about $100 to $150 per course for course materials, which are available through the [UBC Bookstore](http://www.bookstore.ubc.ca/)\.

### How to apply as a senior citizen

Follow the same application instructions as mentioned above, then email [non\.degree@ubc\.ca](mailto:non.degree@ubc.ca) with proof of your date of birth as well as British Columbia \(BC\) residency, such as a photocopy of your BC driver’s license\. Include your UBC reference or student number\.
