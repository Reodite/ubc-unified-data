## Keep your student loan payment\-free and interest\-free[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/student\-loan\-interest/\#keep\-your\-student\-loan\-payment\-free\-and\-interest\-free](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/student-loan-interest/#keep-your-student-loan-payment-free-and-interest-free)

If you had a previous loan, you must notify your lender that you have returned to your studies to keep your student loan interest\-free and prevent your loan from entering repayment\.

If you do not notify your lenders, your loans may begin to build interest and you will [enter repayment\.](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/repaying-my-student-loans/)

### Notifying your lenders

Notify your lenders as soon as possible for each new eligible study period\.

To find out if you are a part\-time or full\-time student and if you are registered in an eligible course load for your loans, visit the [Course Load Guide](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/course-load-guide/)\.

Your lenders will automatically be notified of your return to study only if one of the following happens: 

-   You submit a payment\-free or interest\-free request to the same province, lender, or loan program as last year\.
-   You apply for a new student loan and receive funding before you enter repayment from the same province, lender, or loan program as last year\.

## Students with full\-time Canadian government funding[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/student\-loan\-interest/\#students\-with\-full\-time\-canadian\-government\-funding](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/student-loan-interest/#students-with-full-time-canadian-government-funding)

Complete the following steps for your province or territory to notify your lenders\.

**

Students from BC

**

Submit a request for interest\-free status through the [National Student Loans Service Centre](https://protege-secure.csnpe-nslsc.canada.ca/en/public/sign-in-method)\.

**

Students from Alberta

**

If you have both Alberta and Canada student loans, or only Canada student loans including previously issued part\-time Canada loans, visit [National Student Loans Service Centre’s \(NSLSC\) online services](https://protege-secure.csnpe-nslsc.canada.ca/en/public/sign-in-method)\.

If you only have Alberta student loans, complete the [Confirmation of Registration \(Form B\)](https://formsmgmt.gov.ab.ca/Public/ASA12750.xdp)\.

**

Students from Ontario

**

Students must apply for interest\-free status through the [Ontario Student Assistance Program \(OSAP\)](https://www.ontario.ca/page/osap-ontario-student-assistance-program)\.

To apply for Continuation of Interest\-Free Status, follow these steps:

1.  Log into your Account on your OSAP portal\.
2.  Select “Apply for Continuation of Interest\-Free Status \(CIFS\)”\.
3.  Complete the online form\.

**

Students from Quebec

**

Email your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to complete a Form 2006 for an interest\-free request\. You can [find your advisor](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/ubc-advisor-contacts/finding-your-enrolment-services-advisors-contact-information/) in Workday\.

If you do not have an advisor**,** submit your question through the [online form](https://www.askme.ubc.ca/form/s/)\.

**

Students from other provinces

**

Submit a request for interest\-free status through the [National Student Loans Service Centre](https://protege-secure.csnpe-nslsc.canada.ca/en/public/sign-in-method)\.

## Students with part\-time Canadian government funding[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/student\-loan\-interest/\#students\-with\-part\-time\-canadian\-government\-funding](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/student-loan-interest/#students-with-part-time-canadian-government-funding)

### How to notify your lenders

There are specific instructions to notify your lender depending on the type of your part\-time loan\.

-   **If you are not taking out a new part\-time loan**  
    Register in an eligible [course load](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/course-load-guide/) for your part\-time studies and notify your lenders by submitting a request for interest\-free status through the [National Student Loans Service Centre](https://protege-secure.csnpe-nslsc.canada.ca/en/public/sign-in-method)\.
-   **If you are taking out a new part\-time loan**  
    Register in an eligible [course load](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/course-load-guide/) for your part\-time studies, apply for a new loan, and receive funding before you [enter repayment](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/repaying-my-student-loans/)\.

Part\-time studies cannot be used to put previous full\-time loans into interest\-free or payment\-free status\.

## Students with US loans[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/student\-loan\-interest/\#students\-with\-us\-loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/student-loan-interest/#students-with-us-loans)

If you applied for a previous loan, you must notify your lender that you have returned to part\-time or full\-time study\.

If you do not notify your lenders, your loans will begin to accrue interest and you will [enter repayment\.](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/repaying-my-student-loans/)

UBC can inform your lender\(s\) that you’ve returned to school through the [National Student Loan Data System \(NSLDS\)](https://nsldsfap.ed.gov/login)\.

You’ll need to email [usloans\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:usloans.inquiry@ubc.ca) to request that Enrolment Services submit your current enrolment information to your US lenders\.

Your SSN is needed to add you to the NSLDS roster\. Please provide your SSN to [Enrolment Services](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) by phone\.

Once this update is submitted, you will be on a report that UBC completes once every 60 days for the NSLDS\. You will not need to manually request an enrolment update again, unless you cease to be a full\-time student at UBC for a semester or more\.

### In\-school deferment eligibility

If you are registered in at least 6 credits per term, you will be eligible for [In\-School Deferment](https://studentaid.gov/help-center/answers/topic/glossary/article/deferment) for your current study period\.

Depending on the [types of US loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/) you have, you may still build interest on your loans even if you are eligible for deferment\.
