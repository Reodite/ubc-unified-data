## About the scholarships[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/affiliation\-scholarships/\#about\-the\-scholarships](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/affiliation-scholarships/#about-the-scholarships)

Affiliation scholarships are based on merit such as academic achievement, community involvement, or leadership, and require that students be connected to a specific group, such as:

-   Clubs
-   Trade unions
-   Companies
-   Geographic regions
-   Demographic groups

Some awards are open to students at other British Columbia universities and colleges\.

If you are applying as a **non\-UBC student,** please read the award descriptions carefully to determine your eligibility and follow the application process below\.

Apply only for awards you are eligible for\. Applying for scholarships you do not qualify for may delay the review of your application\.

[Read award descriptions](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/affiliation-scholarships/descriptions-affiliation-scholarships/)

## Eligibility requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/affiliation\-scholarships/\#eligibility\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/affiliation-scholarships/#eligibility-requirements)

You must meet all the following requirements to apply:

### UBC Vancouver Campus students

-   You must have been registered in **at least** 24 percentage\-graded credits in the prior Winter Session\.
-   You must be registered in **at least** 24 percentage\-graded credits in the upcoming Winter Session\.
-   You must have an average of **at least** 75% with no failed or incomplete courses\.
-   You must meet the terms for awards eligibility set out in [UBC’s Regulations Governing University Awards](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/university-awards)\.
-   Incoming first\-year Post\-secondary students will be reviewed based on their high school transcript submitted to UBC Undergraduate Admissions\.

Courses in Pass/Fail or Credit/D/Fail format are not taken into account, except Doctor of Dental Medicine \(DMD\) or Doctor of Medicine \(MD\) courses\.

### UBC Okanagan Campus students

-   You must have been registered in **at least** 24 percentage\-graded credits in the prior Winter Session\.
-   You must be registered in **at least** 24 percentage\-graded credits in the upcoming Winter Session\.
-   You must have an average of **at least** 75% with no failed or incomplete courses\.
-   You must meet the terms for awards eligibility set out in UBC Okanagan Campus Minimum Scholarship [Course Load Requirements](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/finances/financial-support/awards-scholarships-and-prizes/minimum-course-load-requirements/)\.
-   Incoming first\-year Post\-secondary students will be reviewed based on their high school transcript submitted to UBC Undergraduate Admissions\.

Courses in Pass/Fail or Credit/D/Fail format are not considered, except Doctor of Dental Medicine \(DMD\) or Doctor of Medicine \(MD\) courses\.

### Non\-UBC Post\-secondary students

-   You must have been registered in **at least** 24 percentage\-graded credits for your program during the previous academic year between September and April\.
-   You must be registered in **at least** 24 percentage\-graded credits at your institution during the upcoming academic year between September and April\.
-   You must have an average of **at least** 75% with no failed or incomplete courses\.
-   You must provide an official transcript showing your academic records for the 2025/26 academic year as well as proof of registration for the upcoming 2026/27 academic year\.
-   First\-year post\-secondary students must also upload their official high school record showing proof of high school graduation and final grades for Grade 11 and 12\.

Courses in Pass/Fail or Credit/D/Fail format are not considered\.

### Additional requirements

Some awards have additional eligibility requirements or are only open to students attending a specific campus or post\-secondary institution\.

Please review each [award description](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/affiliation-scholarships/descriptions-affiliation-scholarships/) carefully to ensure you meet the eligibility criteria and can obtain supporting documentation\.

## Before you apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/affiliation\-scholarships/\#before\-you\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/affiliation-scholarships/#before-you-apply)

Detailed instructions are provided on the application form, but before you start your application, make sure you complete the following:

1.  Meet all general and specific eligibility requirements for the scholarship you are applying to\.
2.  Read each [award description](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/affiliation-scholarships/descriptions-affiliation-scholarships/) carefully because they all require different supporting documentation before you are able to submit your completed application\.
3.  UBC students do not need to provide transcripts; however, non\-UBC students must provide official transcripts\.
