## About the flu[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus/flu\-colds/\#about\-the\-flu](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/flu-colds/#about-the-flu)

The flu, also known as Influenza, is an infection of your nose and throat, or upper airway, caused by an influenza virus\. The flu can cause serious illness and health complications, especially for young children, pregnant people, the elderly, or people with certain medical conditions\. A cold is caused by a viral infection of the upper respiratory tract\. Most viral infections are self\-limiting and resolve on their own\.

## What to do if you’re sick[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus/flu\-colds/\#what\-to\-do\-if\-youre\-sick](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/flu-colds/#what-to-do-if-youre-sick)

### Managing your symptoms

Many cold and flu symptoms such as fever, muscle pain, and sore throat can be managed at home with over\-the\-counter medications such as Tylenol or Advil\. Read about the [flu symptoms](https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/healthlinkbc-files/facts-about-influenza-flu) you can manage at home on your own, and the ones that require medical attention\.

If you experience severe symptoms, like difficulty breathing, confusion, persistent fever, or chest pain, you should visit your nearest emergency department or urgent care\.

Visit [Student Health and Wellbeing](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/) if you do not start to feel better in a few days or your symptoms worsen\.

Call HealthlinkBC at [811](tel:811) for free health information and advice from a registered nurse, dietician, pharmacist, or a qualified exercise professional\.

### If you have to miss class or an exam

If you’re feeling unwell and cannot attend class, follow the instructions in your syllabus\. Your course instructor should not need you to present a sick note\. They may simply ask you to email them about your situation and make up the work you missed\.

You may want to consider an [academic concession](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-concessions/) if a medical circumstance is preventing you from completing graded work or exams\. Contact your [academic advising office](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) if you have to miss class or coursework for an extended time due to illness\.

If your medical condition is ongoing and affecting your studies for more than one term, you can request an [academic accommodation](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-accommodations-disabilities/)\.

### If you’re unable to work

UBC offers [emergency funding](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/financial-support-options/emergency-funding/) for students who encounter an unexpected situation that impacts their finances\. An [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) can work with you to review your personal finances and connect you to helpful resources\.

## Protecting yourself and others from getting sick[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus/flu\-colds/\#protecting\-yourself\-and\-others\-from\-getting\-sick](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/flu-colds/#protecting-yourself-and-others-from-getting-sick)

Colds and flus spread easily through coughing, sneezing, or having face\-to\-face contact\. The risk of getting sick can be higher in spaces with large numbers of people, such as lecture halls, indoor campus spots, and on the bus\.

Follow the tips below to protect yourself and others from getting sick:

-   Cover your mouth when you cough or sneeze\.
-   Wash your hands frequently\.
-   Stay home if you are unwell\.
-   If you’re sick, wear a mask when around others\.
-   Avoid touching your eyes, mouth, or nose\.
-   Get your flu shot at a local pharmacy or clinic\.

## Get your flu shot[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus/flu\-colds/\#get\-your\-flu\-shot](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/flu-colds/#get-your-flu-shot)

The flu shot, also known as [flu vaccine](https://immunizebc.ca/influenza), is available during late October or early November\. In British Columbia, the flu shot is recommended and free for everyone aged 6 months or older\. If you’re registered with the [Get Vaccinated](https://www.getvaccinated.gov.bc.ca/s/) system, you’ll receive a notification when it’s time to book your next flu vaccine\.

You can also call and book an appointment for your flu vaccine at [UBC Student Health and Wellbeing](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/)\.

### COVID\-19 Vaccine

Several pharmacies and clinics also offer the COVID\-19 vaccine with the flu shot during the same appointment\. Please call ahead to confirm availability if you decide to walk in\.

If you’re registered with the [Get Vaccinated](https://www.getvaccinated.gov.bc.ca/s/) system, you’ll receive a notification when it’s time to book both of these vaccines\. If you don’t have MSP or a Personal Health Number \(PHN\), you can call [1 833 838 2323](tel:18338382323) \(toll free\) to register for the system\.
