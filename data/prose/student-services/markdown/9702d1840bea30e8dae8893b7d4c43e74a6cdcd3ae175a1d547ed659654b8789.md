## About The United Church of Canada[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/chaplains/united\-church\-canada\-chaplain/\#about\-the\-united\-church\-of\-canada](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/chaplains/united-church-canada-chaplain/#about-the-united-church-of-canada)

The United Church of Canada is the largest Protestant Christian denomination in Canada\. We minister close to 3 million people in over 3500 congregations across the country\. The United Church was formed in 1925 as a union of Methodists, Presbyterians and Congregationalist Christians\. We continue to enjoy a diversity of faith expressions while communing as a single body\.  
   
The United Church strives to be open and welcoming as Jesus was, regardless of age, race, class, gender, or physical ability\. We view each person as a unique, loved creation of God and welcome all people to the full life of the Christian community\. The Bible is the shared standard for our faith, but members are not required to adhere to a particular creed or formulation of doctrine\. The United Church views the religious practices of all people of goodwill with respect and gratitude\. We believe the Spirit of God is at work in many different faith communities\.

## About Aaron Miller[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/chaplains/united\-church\-canada\-chaplain/\#about\-aaron\-miller](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/chaplains/united-church-canada-chaplain/#about-aaron-miller)

[Profile of Aaron Miller from United Chaplain](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/united_church_chaplain_aaron_miller.jpg)

Aaron Miller is an ordained minister in the United Church of Canada, and the Campus Minister for the [Pacific Mountain Regional Council](https://pacificmountain.ca/)\. He has been serving in full\-time ministry since 2009 in Ontario and at UBC with [University Hill \(U Hill\)](https://www.uhill.net/) Congregation\. Aaron completed his undergraduate degree at Brock University in English Language and Literature\. He received his Masters of Divinity from Emmanuel College in Toronto\.
