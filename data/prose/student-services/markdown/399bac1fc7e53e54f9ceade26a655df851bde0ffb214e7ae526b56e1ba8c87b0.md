## Book a career appointment

Book a one\-on\-one discussion with a Career Educator to explore future career possibilities or to walk through your job application\.

Appointments are held virtually at this time and can be scheduled for 15, 30, or 45 minutes\. Current students and recent graduates up to 12 months after graduation can book an appointment\.

1.  Log into [CareersOnline](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/index.php) with your CWL\. 
2.  Select “Appointments” in the top menu\. 
3.  Choose “Book New Appointment”\.
4.  If you have a specific date, time, advising topic, or advisor you’d like to meet, fill out the fields\. Otherwise, leave the fields empty and click “Check availability”\.
5.  Select your preferred appointment time and complete the information requested\.

Once you’ve booked an appointment, you will no longer be able to see the times to book another appointment\.

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please call [604 822 4011](tel:6048224011) or email [career\.centre@ubc\.ca](mailto:career.centre@ubc.ca)\.
