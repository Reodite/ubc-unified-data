## Develop your Skillset [https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/what\-can\-i\-do\-with\-my\-kinesiology\-degree/\#develop\-your\-skillset](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/what-can-i-do-with-my-kinesiology-degree/#develop-your-skillset) 

Throughout your Kinesiology degree, you’ll develop specific skills that employers are looking for when hiring graduates, including: 

-   Knowledge of safe, effective movement techniques in a wide variety of physical activities 
-   Creative and strategic problem solving 
-   Collaboration with peers, faculty, and community members through group projects and assignments 
-   Critical thinking and analysis 
-   Familiarity with specialized tools, equipment, and modern technologies connected to exercise physiology, motor performance, and more  
-   Data collection, analysis, and interpretation through research within lab and occasional field environments 
-   Effective written and oral communication skills to present ideas to a range of audiences 
-   Ability to understand, examine, and synthesize concepts while considering multiple perspectives 
-   Developing inclusive teaching practices in clinical and educational environments 

## Get experience [https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/what\-can\-i\-do\-with\-my\-kinesiology\-degree/\#get\-experience](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/what-can-i-do-with-my-kinesiology-degree/#get-experience) 

UBC offers many opportunities for Kinesiology students to build their careers\. 

### Workplace experience 

-   [Work Learn](https://students.ubc.ca/career/work-learn-program/)  
    Build work experience through a part\-time, on\-campus job\. 
-   [UBC Kinesiology Work\-Integrated Learning](https://kin.educ.ubc.ca/undergraduate/bkin-degree-requirements/upper-level-requirements/work-integrated-learning/)  
    Develop work skills through experience\-based learning in for\-credit courses\. 
-   [Kinesiology Outreach Programs](https://kin.educ.ubc.ca/outreach/)  
    Apply your class learnings through experiential paid or volunteer opportunities 
-   [On\-campus jobs](https://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-jobs/)  
    Find student jobs available on campus\. 
-   [Careers Online](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/index.php)  
    Browse work or volunteer positions on UBC’s online career resources platform\. 
-   [Canada Summer Jobs Program](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/youth)  
    Check out summer jobs for full\-time students\. 
-   [Federal Student Work Experience Program](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-service-commission/jobs/services/recruitment/students/federal-student-work-program.html)  
    Apply for government positions across Canada through the student recruitment initiative\. 

### Research 

From volunteering in a lab to getting a research award, there are many possibilities to build your undergrad research experience and prepare you for graduate school or careers in research: 

-   [Faculty research opportunities](https://kin.educ.ubc.ca/research/)
-   [Undergraduate Research Awards](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/)
-   [Directed Studies \(KIN 490\)](https://kin.educ.ubc.ca/undergraduate/bkin-degree-requirements/upper-level-requirements/directed-studies-kin490/) 
-   [Undergraduate Research Experience \(KIN 492\)](https://kin.educ.ubc.ca/undergraduate/bkin-degree-requirements/upper-level-requirements/undergraduate-research-experience-kin492/) 

### Involvement and leadership 

Connect your academic learning to experiences outside of the classroom\. Take initiative to engage in different communities to strengthen your communication and collaboration skills\. 

-   [Kinesiology Undergraduate Society \(KUS\)](https://www.ubckus.ca/)  
    Join your student council to plan events and advocate on behalf of Kinesiology undergraduates\. 
-   [Centre for Community Engaged Learning](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-community-engaged-learning/)  
    Get involved at a local school or non\-profit organization\. 
-   [Student Directed Seminars](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/student-directed-seminars/)  
    Propose, coordinate, and lead your own seminar class for credit\. 
-   [Student leadership opportunities](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/)  
    Take on a student leadership role with Residence Life, a Peer Program, Orientations, Collegia, and many more campus groups\. 

### International experience 

Living, studying, and working abroad build valuable traits\. Show employers you are adaptable, can work independently, and have global experience\. 

-   [Go Global](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/)  
    Go on exchange, attend a global seminar, or conduct research abroad\. 
-   [Office of Regional and International Community Engagement \(ORICE\)](https://orice.ubc.ca/programs/)  
    Make meaningful connections between the wider community and academia around common interests in complex global issues\.
