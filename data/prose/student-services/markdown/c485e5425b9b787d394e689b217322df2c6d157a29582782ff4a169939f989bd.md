## About Arts Peer Academic Coaches[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/arts\-peer\-academic\-coaches\-apac/\#about\-arts\-peer\-academic\-coaches](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/arts-peer-academic-coaches-apac/#about-arts-peer-academic-coaches)

Arts Peer Academic Coaches are friendly, inclusive, and knowledgeable upper\-year students who know what it’s like to learn and study in UBC Arts\. They share a common goal of seeing other students succeed and are trained to support academic growth through 1:1 coaching conversations\.

## Who can apply?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/arts\-peer\-academic\-coaches\-apac/\#who\-can\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/arts-peer-academic-coaches-apac/#who-can-apply)

Upper\-year undergraduate Arts students who are in good academic standing are invited to apply for the role\. 

There are **18 to 20 positions** **available** each year\.

### If you require accommodations

The Peer Programs Program Advisors would like to recognize that being able to participate in student leadership opportunities is a privilege\. The weekly commitment for each Peer Program varies in length but may not be possible for some students who need accommodations based on chronic health conditions, disabilities, mental health, and/or other lived experiences\.

If you have specific accommodations for your ability to participate and contribute to the Arts Peer Academic Coaches program, please email the Program Advisor, Cecilia Langill, at [cecilia\.langill@ubc\.ca](mailto:cecilia.langill@ubc.ca) to discuss what accommodations can be made for your particular situation\.

## Time commitment[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/arts\-peer\-academic\-coaches\-apac/\#time\-commitment](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/arts-peer-academic-coaches-apac/#time-commitment)

The time commitment for the role is **3\.5 to 5** **hours per week**\. For detailed time requirements, please review the position description\.
