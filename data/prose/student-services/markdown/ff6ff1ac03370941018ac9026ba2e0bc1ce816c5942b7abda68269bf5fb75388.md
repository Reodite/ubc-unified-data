Students can book general teaching spaces on campus if they are part of an AMS\-constituted club or if they are a booking representative for an authorized student group\. All students must read and follow UBC’s [Respectful Environment Statement](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/respectful-environment/files/UBC-Statement-on-Respectful-Environment-2014.pdf) \(pdf\) and [Booking Guidelines for General Teaching Space](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/media-uploads/files/GTS%20Booking%20Guidelines%20w%20Rental%20Rates%20-%20011725.pdf) \(pdf\)\.

Use the [Learning Spaces search tool](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/find-space) to find the perfect classroom with the right amenities for your event, workshop, or activity\. Check that building hours align with your event schedule\. Bookings are subject to availability\.

## Required forms[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/organizing\-campus\-events/booking\-general\-teaching\-space\-campus/\#required\-forms](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/organizing-campus-events/booking-general-teaching-space-campus/#required-forms)

Submit the [UBC Vancouver Student Booking Representative Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_b1R19lL5qq1SKQS) **before** submitting room booking requests\. You must update your forms each academic year\.

Students who are affiliated with the AMS must complete and upload the [AMS booking representatives form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/ams_booking_representatives_form.pdf) \(pdf\)\.

Students who are not affiliated with the AMS but would like to establish a student organization must first fill out the [authorization form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/student_organization_authorization_form.pdf) \(pdf\)\. To operate, you will need the permission of the department head\.

After you have been authorized by the department head, complete the [student booking representatives form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/student_organization_booking_representatives_form.pdf) \(pdf\)\. Both forms must be completed and uploaded\.

## Rules and policies[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/organizing\-campus\-events/booking\-general\-teaching\-space\-campus/\#rules\-and\-policies](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/organizing-campus-events/booking-general-teaching-space-campus/#rules-and-policies)

-   Room bookings are scheduled to begin and end on the hour or half\-hour\. The activity should end 10 minutes prior to the scheduled end time to allow for transition to the next activity\. 
-   Food, alcohol, non\-alcoholic drinks are not permitted in general teaching spaces \(classrooms\)\. 
-   If a room is not returned to its original condition, Custodial Services may charge fees to restore it\.
-   Limit your requests for specific buildings, layouts, and features when booking a space\.
-   All bookings must adhere to the following policies:
    -   [Policy \#UP9 Booking and Rental of UBC Space](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/space-rental-policy/)
    -   [Policy \#UP5 Ownership and Use of UBC Equipment and UBC Support Services](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/equipmentservices-use-policy)
    -   [Policy \#UP8 Posting of Notices, Posters, and Signs](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/signage-policy/)
    -   [UBC’s Respectful Environment Statement](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/respectful-environment/files/UBC-Statement-on-Respectful-Environment-2014.pdf)

### Collection week

Booking requests for winter and summer sessions will be collected for a one\-week period called collection week \(e\.g\., Wednesday to Tuesday\) for each respective session\. Requests received within the one\-week collection period will be booked in accordance with their respective priority\. Booking requests received after the one\-week periods will be processed on a first come, first serve basis\.

For example, if January 25 is the release date, then January 25 to 31 is the collection week\. Anything submitted on February 1 onwards will be processed on a first come, first serve basis\.

## Making a room booking request[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/organizing\-campus\-events/booking\-general\-teaching\-space\-campus/\#making\-a\-room\-booking\-request](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/organizing-campus-events/booking-general-teaching-space-campus/#making-a-room-booking-request)

1.  Read the [policies and procedures](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/sites/facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/GTS%20Booking%20Guidelines%20w%20Rental%20Rates%20-%20011725.pdf) \(pdf\) in place for general teaching space booking requests carefully\. If you have any questions, contact [room\.bookings@ubc\.ca](mailto:room.bookings@ubc.ca)\.
2.  Complete and submit your [booking authorization form](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/organizing-campus-events/booking-general-teaching-space-campus/#required-forms) to [room\.bookings@ubc\.ca](mailto:room.bookings@ubc.ca)\.
3.  Complete the [Room Booking Request Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3vHf0CNAatJGRRc) at least **4 weeks** before your requested booking date\.
4.  Scheduling Services will process the request and email you about your room booking confirmation within 15 business days except between January and March, and September and October, where response times may be longer\.

Please **do not** advertise your event until you have received a final booking confirmation\.
