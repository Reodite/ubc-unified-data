## About the fees[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus/student\-health\-wellbeing\-clinic\-fees/\#about\-the\-fees](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/student-health-wellbeing-clinic-fees/#about-the-fees)

Student Health and Wellbeing charges fees for certain tests, appointments, forms, and equipment\. If you miss or cancel your appointment without advance notice, you may also be charged a cancellation fee\. All amounts are listed in Canadian dollars and are subject to change without notice\. Please call [604 822 3811](tel:6048223811) if you have any questions about the fees\.

## Missed or cancelled appointment fees[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus/student\-health\-wellbeing\-clinic\-fees/\#missed\-or\-cancelled\-appointment\-fees](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/student-health-wellbeing-clinic-fees/#missed-or-cancelled-appointment-fees)

Log into the [Patient Portal](https://ubcshw.inputhealth.com/ebooking#new) to cancel or reschedule your doctor or nursing appointment\. To cancel or reschedule a psychiatry appointment, please call [604 822 3811](tel:6048223811)\.

You will be charged a cancellation fee if you do not cancel your specialist or psychiatry appointment ahead of time\.

Cancellation fees and the amount of cancellation notice required varies by appointment type\. Please see below for more details\.

### Specialist appointments

Monday appointments must be cancelled on the previous Friday, within business hours\.

| **Appointment type** | **Fees for cancelling less than 48 business hours in advance** |
| --- | --- |
| First consultation for dermatology, or allergy and immunology | Up to $150\.00 |
| Follow\-up consultation for dermatology, or allergy and immunology | Up to $100\.00 |
| IUD Clinic or Gynecology | $100\.00 |

### General Internal Medicine or Respirology appointments

Tuesday appointments must be cancelled before 12:00 pm on the previous Thursday\.

| **Appointment type** | **Fees for cancelling later than 12:00 pm 3 business days in advance** |
| --- | --- |
| First consultation | $240\.00 |
| Follow\-up visit | $95\.00 |

### Psychiatry appointments

Monday appointments must be cancelled on the previous Friday, within business hours\.

| **Appointment type** | **Fees for cancelling less than 24 business hours in advance** |
| --- | --- |
| First consultation | $303\.31 to $410\.62, depending on length of appointment |
| Follow\-up visit | $132\.44 to $264\.89, depending on length of appointment |

## Fees for students without health insurance from Canada[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus/student\-health\-wellbeing\-clinic\-fees/\#fees\-for\-students\-without\-health\-insurance\-from\-canada](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/student-health-wellbeing-clinic-fees/#fees-for-students-without-health-insurance-from-canada)

| **Service** | **Fee** |
| --- | --- |
| General clinic visit | $97\.20 |
| Extended clinic visit | $174\.00 |
| Complete physical exam and detailed history | $209\.00 |
| Injection | $30\.15 |

## Specialist fees for students without health insurance[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus/student\-health\-wellbeing\-clinic\-fees/\#specialist\-fees\-for\-students\-without\-health\-insurance](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus/student-health-wellbeing-clinic-fees/#specialist-fees-for-students-without-health-insurance)
