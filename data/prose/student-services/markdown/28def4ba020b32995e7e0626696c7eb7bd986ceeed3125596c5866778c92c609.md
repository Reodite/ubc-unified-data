## About the pathway[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/undergraduate\-student\-research\-awards\-nserc\-cihr\-sshrc/indigenous\-student\-researchers\-pathway\-nserc\-usra/\#about\-the\-pathway](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/indigenous-student-researchers-pathway-nserc-usra/#about-the-pathway)

The Federal Ministry of Innovation, Science and Industry offers an undergraduate research award pathway for Indigenous Student Researchers through the [NSERC USRA](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/UG-PC/USRA-BRPC_eng.asp)\. If you self\-identify as Indigenous, apply to the NSERC USRA program\.

## Application requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/undergraduate\-student\-research\-awards\-nserc\-cihr\-sshrc/indigenous\-student\-researchers\-pathway\-nserc\-usra/\#application\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/indigenous-student-researchers-pathway-nserc-usra/#application-requirements)

To apply through this pathway, you will be required to:

-   Indicate that you self\-identify as Indigenous Student on your application Form 202 Part 1 under ‘Personal Profile’ on the [NSERC online portal](https://ebiz.nserc.ca/nserc_web/nserc_login_e.htm)\.
-   Follow the [application instructions](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/indigenous-student-researchers-pathway-nserc-usra/) of the faculty, school, or department you’re applying to, and submit your application by their [specified deadline](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/usra-faculty-school-application-details/)\.

## Application deadlines[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/undergraduate\-student\-research\-awards\-nserc\-cihr\-sshrc/indigenous\-student\-researchers\-pathway\-nserc\-usra/\#application\-deadlines](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/indigenous-student-researchers-pathway-nserc-usra/#application-deadlines)

Please check the [Faculty and school application details](https://students.ubc.ca/career/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/usra-faculty-school-application-details/) for deadlines\.
