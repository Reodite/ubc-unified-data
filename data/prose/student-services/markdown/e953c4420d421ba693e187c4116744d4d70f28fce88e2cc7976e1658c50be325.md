## Important deadlines[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/dates\-deadlines/\#important\-deadlines](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/dates-deadlines/#important-deadlines)

Search your courses in the [Course Schedule](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/) for important deadlines, such as:

-   Last day to drop **without** a [W standing](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates#w-standing)
-   Last day to select [Credit/D/Fail grading](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/creditdfail-grading/) for eligible courses
-   Last day for withdrawal **with** a W standing

You can also find [add and drop deadlines](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates/) for your courses\.
