The Wesbrook Building Prayer Room is located on the first floor of the [Wesbrook Building](https://maps.ubc.ca/?code=WESB) in rooms 116 and 118\.

The two prayer rooms are open by drop\-in to all students of any faith for prayer, quiet contemplation, and reflection\. Room 116 is open to female\-identifying students, and Room 118 is open to male\-identifying students\.

Single\-occupancy ablution stations are available on the same level and opposite the prayer space in rooms 132A and 132B\.

## Before using the room[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/wesbrook\-building\-prayer\-room/\#before\-using\-the\-room](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/wesbrook-building-prayer-room/#before-using-the-room)

Please review the [guidelines for use](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/#prayer-room-rules-and-responsibilities) and follow all prayer room rules and responsibilities\.

If you’re hosting an event that serves the faith\-based or spiritual student community at UBC, please book the [Brock Commons South Community Space](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/brock-commons-south-community-space/)\.

## Reporting a problem about the facilities[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/wesbrook\-building\-prayer\-room/\#reporting\-a\-problem\-about\-the\-facilities](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/wesbrook-building-prayer-room/#reporting-a-problem-about-the-facilities)

If you encounter any issues with the prayer room or its use, please report it as soon as possible to the Office of the Vice\-President, Students at [vpsassist@mail\.ubc\.ca](mailto:vpsassist@mail.ubc.ca)\.

Concerns may include cleanliness, damage, lighting, misuse of these facilities, or complaints about other users\.

### Urgent concerns

In case of fire or emergency evacuation, follow the procedures posted in the rooms\. In the event of an incident or concern that requires attention, contact [UBC Security](https://security.ubc.ca/) by calling [604\.822\.2222](tel:6048222222)\. For emergencies that require an immediate response, call 911\.
