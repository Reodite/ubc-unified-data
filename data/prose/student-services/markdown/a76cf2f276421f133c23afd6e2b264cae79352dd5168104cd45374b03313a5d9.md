## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/caps/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/caps/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Cellular and Physiological Sciences](https://cps.med.ubc.ca/), you’re inquiring into the basis of life\. You’ll develop important skills and ways of thinking that can have a significant impact on communities of people and the world\.

These skills may include:

-   Application of an in\-depth understanding of the human body, spanning from a histological level to gross anatomy and physiology
-   Interdisciplinary approach to observing and studying life and health sciences
-   Development and execution of research proposals and experimental design
-   Research and laboratory techniques with consistent application of safety protocols 
-   Laboratory report writing and presentation
-   Mock clinical testing in physiology labs, including usage of technology and tests for electrocardiograms, spirometry, and oral glucose tolerance
-   Technical biochemistry lab techniques using PCR, gel electrophoresis, UV spectrophotometry, and protein assays

## Make the most of your specialization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/caps/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-specialization](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/caps/#make-the-most-of-your-specialization)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[UBC Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/physiology)**  
    Gain work experience in cellular and physiological sciences between study terms\.
-   **[UBC Life Sciences Institute](http://lsi.ubc.ca/news-events/events/)**  
    Browse research opportunities, events, programs and student competitions in life sciences\.
-   **[Departmental research opportunities](https://cps.med.ubc.ca/research/)**  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   **[Faculty of Medicine summer research](https://www.med.ubc.ca/current-learners/summer-student-research-program/)**  
    Gain experience in undergraduate medical research at UBC\.
-   **[UBC Pathology and Laboratory Medicine summer program](https://pathology.ubc.ca/education-resource/summer-student-fellowship-program/)**  
    Apply for funding and propose your research project to gain clinical experience in laboratory medicine\.
-   **[BC Children’s Hospital research program](https://bcchr.ca/ssrp)**  
    Participate in an undergraduate research project related to child and family health\.
-   **[Online tutorials and training in ethics](https://ethics.research.ubc.ca/education-training/online-tutorials-training)**  
    Develop your understanding of research ethics and integrity through online training and tutorials\.
-   **[UBC Medical Journal](https://ubcmj.med.ubc.ca/submissions/)**  
    Submit your academic research, case reports, and reviews for publication\.
-   **[Canadian Journal of Undergraduate Research](https://cjur.ca/)**  
    Get your research papers published and gain experience with the peer review process\.
-   **[UBC Undergraduate Research Opportunities](https://www.uroubc.com/)**  
    Find a mentor, learn how to get into research, or receive help on publishing your manuscript\.
-   **[Student Biotechnology Network](https://www.facebook.com/pg/thesbn/events/?ref=page_internal)**  
    Attend events, meet professionals, and receive mentorship from innovative industry leaders\.
