## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/english/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/english/#skills-youll-develop)

While [studying English](https://english.ubc.ca/), you’re engaging with literature and language in a global context through a range of genres, historical perspectives, cultural lenses and theoretical approaches\. You’re developing important skills while learning how literature and language construct and comment on the human experience\.

These skills may include:

-   Critical understanding of the structure, function, and use of language across a broad cross\-section of media
-   Advanced research, writing, and rhetorical skills
-   Researching, collecting, synthesizing, and analyzing large amounts of text and data
-   Applying critical and theoretical methods to understand and communicate how print, visual, and digital forms of narrative mediate our perceptions of the world
-   Organizing content with careful consideration of audience, purpose, and form
-   Attention to detail in editing for content, style, and grammar
-   Persuasive public speaking and debating skills that demonstrate critical thinking and carefully reasoned arguments

## Make the most of your program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/english/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/english/#make-the-most-of-your-program)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities, develop your skillset, and help you clarify your values and interests\.

-   [Arts Co\-op Program](https://artscoop.ubc.ca/)  
    Gain paid work experience in your area of study as part of your degree program\.
-   [Create Your ePortfolio](https://ubcarts.ca/)  
    From projects and papers to travels and volunteer work, Arts ePortfolios help you collect, curate, and share your learning\.
-   [Departmental events](https://english.ubc.ca/community/events/events-calendar/)  
    Network with English students and faculty members at a variety of events\.
-   [Departmental research opportunities](https://english.ubc.ca/research/)  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   [English co\-op success stories](https://artscoop.ubc.ca/success-stories/?alumni-profile-program=english)  
    Learn about the experiences of past and present co\-op students from your program and others\.
-   [JET Programme](https://jetprogramme.ca/)  
    Get experience working abroad after graduation as a language teacher or international relations coordinator\.
-   [PRISM International](https://prismmagazine.ca/submit/)  
    Submit your contemporary writing or participate in a fiction, poetry, short form, or creative non\-fiction contest\.
-   [Resources for writers](https://www.vpl.ca/guide/resources-writers)  
    Find local writing groups, contests, festivals and other resources for writers\.
-   [The Garden Statuary](http://www.thegardenstatuary.com/submissions)  
    Showcase your essays, poetry, photographs and other work in UBC’s English Undergraduate Journal\.
-   [The Ubyssey](https://ubyssey.ca/pages/volunteer2/)  
    Volunteer to write articles for this weekly online student paper\. No previous experience is required\.
-   [UBC English Students’ Association](https://www.facebook.com/ubcenglishstudents/)  
    Meet other UBC students through events and programs\.
-   [Vancouver Writers Fest](https://writersfest.bc.ca/)  
    Check out the works of local and international writers or volunteer at a literary event\.
-   [Writers’ Exchange](https://vancouverwe.com/)  
    Volunteer to support literacy programs for kids in Vancouver\.
