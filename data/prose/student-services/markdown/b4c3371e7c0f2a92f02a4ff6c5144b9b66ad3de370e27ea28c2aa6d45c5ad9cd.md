The Brock Commons South Multifaith Prayer and Quiet Contemplation Room is located on the fourth floor of the [Brock Commons South building](https://maps.ubc.ca/?code=BRCS) in rooms 4110A and 4001B\.

The two prayer rooms are open by drop\-in to all students for prayer, quiet contemplation, and reflection\. Separate ablution facilities are available in both spaces\. Universal washrooms are across the hall from the prayer space\.

## Before using the room[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/brock\-commons\-south\-multifaith\-prayer\-and\-quiet\-contemplation\-room/\#before\-using\-the\-room](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/brock-commons-south-multifaith-prayer-and-quiet-contemplation-room/#before-using-the-room)

Please review the [guidelines for use](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/#prayer-room-rules-and-responsibilities) and follow all prayer room rules and responsibilities\.

If you’re hosting an event that serves the faith\-based or spiritual student community at UBC, please book the [Brock Commons South Community Space](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/brock-commons-south-community-space/)\.

## Prayer room hours[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/brock\-commons\-south\-multifaith\-prayer\-and\-quiet\-contemplation\-room/\#prayer\-room\-hours](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/brock-commons-south-multifaith-prayer-and-quiet-contemplation-room/#prayer-room-hours)

The prayer rooms are available from Monday to Friday at the following times:

-   Rooms 4110A and 4110B are open to all genders from 7:00 am to 1:00 pm\. 
-   Room 4110A is open to self\-identifying male students from 1:00 pm to 8:30 pm\.
-   Room 4110B is open to self\-identifying female students from 1:00 pm to 8:30 pm\.

## Room capacity[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/brock\-commons\-south\-multifaith\-prayer\-and\-quiet\-contemplation\-room/\#room\-capacity](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/brock-commons-south-multifaith-prayer-and-quiet-contemplation-room/#room-capacity)

-   Room 4110A has a capacity of 40 to 45 people\.
-   Room 4110B has a capacity of 20 to 25 people\.

## How to access the room[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/brock\-commons\-south\-multifaith\-prayer\-and\-quiet\-contemplation\-room/\#how\-to\-access\-the\-room](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/brock-commons-south-multifaith-prayer-and-quiet-contemplation-room/#how-to-access-the-room)

You can enter and use the prayer rooms without a UBC card during the building hours from 7:00 am to 8:30 pm\.

## Reporting a problem about the facilities[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/ubc\-multifaith\-prayer\-rooms/brock\-commons\-south\-multifaith\-prayer\-and\-quiet\-contemplation\-room/\#reporting\-a\-problem\-about\-the\-facilities](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms/brock-commons-south-multifaith-prayer-and-quiet-contemplation-room/#reporting-a-problem-about-the-facilities)

If you encounter any issues with the prayer room or its use, please report it as soon as possible to the Office of the Vice\-President, Students at [vpsassist@mail\.ubc\.ca](mailto:vpsassist@mail.ubc.ca)\.

Concerns may include cleanliness, damage, lighting, misuse of these facilities, or complaints about other users\.

### Urgent concerns

In case of fire or emergency evacuation, follow the procedures posted in the rooms\. In the event of an incident or concern that requires attention, contact [UBC Security](https://security.ubc.ca/) by calling [604\.822\.2222](tel:6048222222)\. For emergencies that require an immediate response, call 911\.
