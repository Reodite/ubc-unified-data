## About Imagine UBC[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/ubc\-orientation\-transition\-leadership/imagine\-ubc\-senior\-orientation\-leaders/\#about\-imagine\-ubc](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/imagine-ubc-senior-orientation-leaders/#about-imagine-ubc)

[Imagine UBC](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/orientations/imagine-ubc/) is the university’s biggest back\-to\-school event, welcoming thousands of new students to the UBC community\. The day is full of exciting opportunities to meet others and learn about UBC\. New students will get to know the campus, find out more about their faculty or school, meet a professor, connect with a small group of students from their faculty or school, and more\.

## About the role[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/ubc\-orientation\-transition\-leadership/imagine\-ubc\-senior\-orientation\-leaders/\#about\-the\-role](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/imagine-ubc-senior-orientation-leaders/#about-the-role)

The Senior Orientation Leader team plays an important role in Imagine UBC by supporting Imagine UBC Orientation Leaders\. Senior Orientation Leaders help select Orientation Leaders and contribute to their training, team building, and development\.

Time commitment, responsibilities, and position requirements vary depending on your faculty, school, or program\. Check back in February for 2027 position descriptions\.

## What you’ll get out of the role[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/ubc\-orientation\-transition\-leadership/imagine\-ubc\-senior\-orientation\-leaders/\#what\-youll\-get\-out\-of\-the\-role](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/imagine-ubc-senior-orientation-leaders/#what-youll-get-out-of-the-role)

-   Learn how to communicate with a diverse group of students and peers\. 
-   Build leadership and teamwork skills working in large groups\.
-   Understand how to facilitate groups, including engaging students in team games and activities \(online and/or in person\)\.
-   Meet many other student leaders in your faculty or school\.
-   Develop your time management, organization, and problem\-solving skills\.
