## Crisis support 24/7[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/\#crisis\-support\-24\-7](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/#crisis-support-24-7)

Get help if you or someone else is in immediate danger, or at risk of harming yourself or others\. Call [911](tel:911) or visit your [nearest emergency room](https://www.vch.ca/en/health-topics/emergency-care)\.

If you or someone you know is feeling hopeless or at risk of harm, call or text [988](tel:988) toll\-free at any time to reach the [Suicide Crisis Helpline](https://988.ca/)\.
