## About your parchment[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/ordering\-parchment\-diploma/\#about\-your\-parchment](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-parchment-diploma/#about-your-parchment)

Your parchment refers to your diploma\.

If you’re a UBC Okanagan student, please [read information](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/graduation/ordering-diplomas/) specific to your campus\.

You will receive your parchment at your in\-person graduation ceremony\. If you do not attend your ceremony, you can request to pick up your parchment afterwards at the [Enrolment Services Advising Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) on the first floor of [Brock Hall](http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?locat1=112-1) during opening hours, or have it sent by courier\. If you’d like it couriered, contact [graduation@ubc\.ca](mailto:graduation@ubc.ca) and they can further assist you\. Please include your full name and student number in the email\.

The information on this page is for students who have already graduated and need a replacement or certified copy of their parchment\.

If you have already graduated, and have not yet collected your parchment, please contact [graduation@ubc\.ca](mailto:graduation@ubc.ca) to arrange for courier delivery, or collect it in person at the [Enrolment Services Advising Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\.

If you need a Program Completion Letter, [download the letter](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/student-documents/downloading-your-program-completion-letter/) in Workday\.

Alumni who aren’t able to log into Workday can log into the [UBC Official Documents tool \(ODMT\)](https://ubc.appiancloud.com/suite/sites/ubc-official-documents) to access a Program Completion Letter\.  

## Replacement parchments[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/ordering\-parchment\-diploma/\#replacement\-parchments](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-parchment-diploma/#replacement-parchments)

A replacement parchment is an official reprint of your original UBC parchment\. It will look almost exactly like an original parchment, with a notation that says “Replacement Parchment month/year” in small print along the front and bottom\.

Rush orders are not available during May and November due to graduation, and in December over the holiday break\. Rush orders can be placed online and in person\.

| Order type | Estimated processing time | Cost in Canadian dollars |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 2 to 4 weeks, plus [additional delivery time](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-parchment-diploma/#pick-up-and-delivery-options) | $75\.54 including tax per parchment \(courier cost is additional\) |
| Rush | 3 to 5 business days, plus [additional delivery time](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-parchment-diploma/#pick-up-and-delivery-options) | $151\.62 including tax per parchment \(courier cost is additional\) |

## Certified copies of your parchment[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/ordering\-parchment\-diploma/\#certified\-copies\-of\-your\-parchment](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-parchment-diploma/#certified-copies-of-your-parchment)

A certified copy is a photocopy of the original or replacement parchment with a stamp to certify that it is a true copy of the original document\.

Your original parchment is required to produce a certified copy\. If you do not have your original parchment, you must order a replacement parchment to request a certified copy\. A replacement parchment takes 2 to 4 weeks to process\.

For same\-day orders, you must place the order in person at the [Enrolment Services Advising Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) in Brock Hall\. You’ll receive your certified copy and original parchment immediately\.

| Order type | Estimated processing time | Cost in Canadian dollars |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular | 3 to 5 business days | $7\.81 including tax |
| Same\-day | 1 to 2 hours | $15\.84 including tax |

## How to order[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/ordering\-parchment\-diploma/\#how\-to\-order](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-parchment-diploma/#how-to-order)

### Ordering online

Learn how to [order a replacement parchment](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/student-documents/ordering-a-parchment/) and certified copies in Workday\.

Alumni who aren’t able to log into Workday can log into the [UBC Official Documents tool](https://ubc.appiancloud.com/suite/sites/ubc-official-documents) to order a parchment and certified copy instead\.

If you choose to order a certified copy online, you’ll need to order and pay for a replacement parchment as well\. This will increase the processing time by 2 to 4 weeks\.

### Ordering in person

You can order your replacement parchment and certified copies at the [Enrolment Services Advising Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) in Brock Hall during opening hours\. If you wish to order only your certified copy, make sure to bring your original parchment to the [Enrolment Services Advising Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\. You must present a photo ID when picking up your order and pay by debit card, VISA, or MasterCard\.

## Pick up and delivery options[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/records/ordering\-parchment\-diploma/\#pick\-up\-and\-delivery\-options](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-parchment-diploma/#pick-up-and-delivery-options)

### Courier delivery

If you choose courier delivery, replacement parchments or certified copies will be sent via FedEx as soon as they are processed\. Couriers cannot deliver to PO boxes\. You will be notified when your order has been completed\.

| Destination | Cost in Canadian dollars | Estimated delivery time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Within Canada | $23\.20, plus replacement or certified copy cost | 1 to 2 business days, depending on destination |
| United States | $45\.20, plus replacement or certified copy cost | 1 to 3 business days, depending on destination |
| International locations | $45\.20, plus replacement or certified copy cost | 3 to 5 business days, depending on destination |

### Picking up in person

You’ll receive an email when your document is ready for pick up at the [Enrolment Services Advising Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) in Brock Hall\. Check the [Enrolment Services Advising Centre webpage](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) for opening hours and seasonal hour changes\.

You must bring the following:

-   Proof of your name change if you’ve requested a name change on your parchment
-   A piece of photo ID with a name matching the name on the parchment for pick\-up

### If someone is picking up your documents for you

You must order your replacement parchment or certified copy yourself, even if you authorize a third party to pick it up\. The person picking up your document should be authorized on your Workday account\. They must have your student number and authorization code in addition to their identification\. Learn about [third\-party authorization](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/third-party-authorization/)\.

Alternatively, if you are unable to provide authorization through Workday, the person picking up your document must provide the following:

-   A completed [Third\-Party Pick\-up Form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/third_party_document_authorization_form.pdf) \(pdf\)\.
-   The required identification noted in the pick\-up form instructions, for both the third party and the document owner\.
