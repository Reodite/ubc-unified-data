## Working on campus[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/campus\-jobs/\#working\-on\-campus](https://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-jobs/#working-on-campus)

Earn extra money during your school year and build valuable work skills through a part\-time student job on campus\.

Many places on campus hire student staff regularly\. You can apply for school\-year positions in August or summer jobs in March\.

International students can work at UBC if they meet the conditions to [work in Canada](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/)\.

## CareersOnline opportunities board[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/campus\-jobs/\#careersonline\-opportunities\-board](https://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-jobs/#careersonline-opportunities-board)

Browse [CareersOnline](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/index.php/pid389509?), the opportunities board for UBC students and alumni for on\- or off\-campus jobs and opportunities\. Positions are posted regularly and year round\.

You may also want to check out the following:

-   [Work Learn program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/work-learn-program/)  
    Develop your professional skills in a work environment\. Learn from a mentor, expand your professional network, and gain work experience through a part\-time UBC job while still taking classes\.
-   [UBC Co\-op](https://coop.ubc.ca/)  
    Alternate between study and paid work terms and get a head start on building your career\.
