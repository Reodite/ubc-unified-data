## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/geographical\-sciences/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/geographical-sciences/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Geographical Sciences](https://www.geog.ubc.ca/undergraduate/programs/biogeosciences/), you’re gaining a hands\-on understanding of how earth and atmospheric systems can have broad and significant impacts\. You’ll develop important skills to explore interactions between life and the Earth’s atmosphere, hydrosphere, and geosphere\.

These skills may include:

-   Application of knowledge about physical environments and spatial relations
-   Geo\-spatial data analysis
-   Designing and creating maps/graphs
-   Designing, planning and managing projects
-   Surveying and field sampling
-   Evidence\-based approach to researching, consulting and advising
-   Technical report writing and accurate maintenance of field notebooks
-   Technical knowledge in using geographic information systems \(GIS\), cartography, statistics, remote sensing, MATLAB, Python, R, Jupyter Notebooks, and computer coding

## Make the most of your specialization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/geographical\-sciences/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-specialization](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/geographical-sciences/#make-the-most-of-your-specialization)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[Geography Students’ Association](http://www.ubcgsa.ca/)**  
    Meet other UBC students with similar interests through events and programs\.
-   **[Earth, Ocean, and Atmospheric Science clubs](https://www.eoas.ubc.ca/undergrads/student-life-events)**  
    Check out related student groups and events\.
-   **[UBC Sustainability programs and clubs](http://www.sustain.ubc.ca/get-involved)**  
    Browse on\-campus paid and volunteer opportunities related to sustainability\.
-   **[UBC Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/geob)**  
    Gain work experience in geographical sciences between study terms\.
-   **[Departmental research opportunities](https://www.eoas.ubc.ca/research/areas)**  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   **[Bamfield Marine Sciences Centre](http://www.bamfieldmsc.com/education/prospective-students)**  
    Take selected field courses in coastal and marine sciences for university credit\.
-   **[Register as a Professional Geoscientist](https://geoscientistscanada.ca/)**  
    Learn what courses you need to become certified for this role\.
-   **[Pacific Institute for Climate Solutions internships](http://www.sustain.ubc.ca/research/featured-research-groups/pacific-institute-climate-solutions)**  
    Apply your classroom knowledge to real\-world settings through climate change mitigation or adaptation projects\.
-   **[BCIT Advanced Diploma in GIS](https://www.bcit.ca/study/programs/9100fadvdip)**  
    Develop your technical skills for a career in Geographic Information Systems through this diploma for university grads\.
-   **[Cartographers’ Guild](https://www.cartographersguild.com/content.php?s=591e200959f6553e198a41033f494e70)**  
    Practice your cartography skills in fictional lands for novels and games\.
