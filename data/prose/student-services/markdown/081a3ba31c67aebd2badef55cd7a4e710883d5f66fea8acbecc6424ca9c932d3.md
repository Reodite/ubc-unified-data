## Application fee[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-tuition\-fees/\#application\-fee](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-tuition-fees/#application-fee)

Students enrolled in [Non\-Degree Studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/) are required to pay an [application fee](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/application-and-administrative-fees)\.

## Tuition fees[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-tuition\-fees/\#tuition\-fees](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-tuition-fees/#tuition-fees)

Your [tuition fees](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/tuition-fees/undergraduate#17997) are assessed on a per\-credit basis and depend on the level of the course\.

Most undergraduate courses are 3 or 6 credits, with some exceptions\. You can check the [Course Search](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/course-search) for specific course information, including number of credits, when and where the course is offered, and who the course instructor is\.

Like degree\-seeking students, you are assessed [student fees](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/student-fees)\. While some student fees are prorated based on the number of credits you are registered in, most are assessed on a one\-time basis\. There are some student fees that you can choose to [opt out](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/student-fees/refunds-and-opting-out-student-fees) of\.

Non\-Degree Studies students are held to the same policies surrounding registration and tuition as degree seeking students at UBC\. It is the [student’s responsibility](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-declaration-and-responsibility) to periodically check their student account for any changes and to ensure that their registration and finances are up to date\.

## Course materials[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-tuition\-fees/\#course\-materials](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-tuition-fees/#course-materials)

Expect to pay about $100 to $150 per course for course materials, such as textbooks or online educational software, which are available through the [UBC Bookstore](http://bookstore.ubc.ca/home)\.

## Exceptions for tuition and fees[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/non\-degree\-studies/non\-degree\-studies\-tuition\-fees/\#exceptions\-for\-tuition\-and\-fees](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-tuition-fees/#exceptions-for-tuition-and-fees)

### Senior citizens

BC residents 65 years of age and older who are Canadian citizens or permanent residents normally do not pay tuition, application, or student fees\.

However, fees may be assessed for programs within Dentistry, Law, Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, or any faculty or school where existing facilities and resources are limited\.

If you wish to opt into the full suite of student fees, please email [non\.degree@ubc\.ca](mailto:non.degree@ubc.ca)\.

### UBC faculty and staff members

UBC’s faculty and staff [tuition waiver](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/benefit-plan-details/tuition-waivers) covers tuition fees **only**\. Other fees that may be associated with each course, like assessed [student fees](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/student-fees), are not covered by the waiver\.

The tuition waiver is not managed by Non\- Degree Studies\. Please connect with UBC Human Resources to facilitate the tuition waiver process once you are registered in courses\.
