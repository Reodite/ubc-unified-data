[UBC Library services](https://www.library.ubc.ca/)

Access online journals, printing and scanning services, or connect with a librarian for help finding research material and citations\.

[Learning toolkits](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/)

Improve your study techniques, presentation skills, group work, critical thinking, and more with online videos and written advice\.

[Academic Integrity Hub](https://academicintegrity.ubc.ca/student-start/)

Learn about academic integrity, avoiding academic misconduct, and the use of GenAI at UBC through Canvas courses, FAQs, and more\.

[Learning Technology Hub: Student Support](https://lthub.ubc.ca/student-support/)

Find out more about learning technologies and how to prepare for online studies\.

## Get one\-on\-one support[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/academic\-learning\-resources/\#get\-one\-on\-one\-support](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/#get-one-on-one-support)

[Academic advising](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/)

Connect with your Academic Advisor for course planning, degree requirements, academic progress, or graduation eligibility\.

[Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication](https://writing.library.ubc.ca/)

Book a writing consultation or access online writing resources for research papers\.

[Peer coaches or tutors](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tutoring-and-coaching/)

Meet with a trained student or staff member to identify strategies in improving your studying, learning, writing, and more\.

## If you have a situation impacting your studies[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/academic\-learning\-resources/\#if\-you\-have\-a\-situation\-impacting\-your\-studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/#if-you-have-a-situation-impacting-your-studies)

[Disability or ongoing medical conditions](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-accommodations-disabilities/)

Request an academic accommodation if you have an ongoing or persistent health condition affecting your studies\.

[Health concern, conflicting responsibility, or family emergency](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-concessions/)

Apply for an academic concession if a health concern, conflicting responsibility, or family emergency is affecting your studies and wellbeing\.

[Academic disputes and other conflicts](https://ombudsoffice.ubc.ca/)

Visit the UBC Ombuds office for confidential guidance and conflict resolution regarding academic disputes\.
