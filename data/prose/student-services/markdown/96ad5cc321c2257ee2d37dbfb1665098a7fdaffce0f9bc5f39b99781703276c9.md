UBC is committed to creating an inclusive campus where all students feel supported and welcome\. We recognize that identities intersect, and you may connect with more than one community\. The ways you identify or what you need may also change over time—and that’s completely okay\. 

Our resources are designed to support these layered and evolving realities\. To help you navigate them, we’ve listed identities below in alphabetized order\.
