## What the Centre can help with[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/centre\-for\-accessibility/\#what\-the\-centre\-can\-help\-with](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/#what-the-centre-can-help-with)

The Centre for Accessibility facilitates disability\-related accommodations designed to remove barriers for students with disabilities and ongoing medical conditions in all aspects of university life\. The Centre works with students to explore their accommodation needs, and to connect them with appropriate support and resources\. 

## How UBC accommodates students with disabilities[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/centre\-for\-accessibility/\#how\-ubc\-accommodates\-students\-with\-disabilities](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/#how-ubc-accommodates-students-with-disabilities)

[Disability Accommodation Policy LR7](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/disability-accommodation-policy/) provides guidance for students, faculty, and staff to understand the process through which accommodations for students with disabilities are made at the University\. It defines responsibilities and key terms regarding accommodation\. Read through [frequently asked questions](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/frequently-asked-questions-about-policy-lr7-accommodation-disabilities/) about the policy\.

## Requesting an accommodation[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/centre\-for\-accessibility/\#requesting\-an\-accommodation](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/#requesting-an-accommodation)

At UBC, [academic accommodations](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-accommodations-disabilities/) are intended to provide students with a disability or ongoing medical condition access to the academic environment\. They do not apply to temporary injuries or acute medical conditions that are not expected to persist for longer than one term\.

Before requesting an accommodation, you must [register with the Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/registering-centre-accessibility/)\. Registration with the Centre is a multi\-step process and can take some time\. We encourage you to start the process as early in the term as possible\. Please reach out to the Centre directly if you have any questions about the process\.

## If you’re already registered with the Centre[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/centre\-for\-accessibility/\#if\-youre\-already\-registered\-with\-the\-centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/#if-youre-already-registered-with-the-centre)

Please log into your [Clockwork account](https://clockwork.as.it.ubc.ca/ClockWork/custom/misc/home.aspx) and submit an accommodation letter and/or exam reservation request\. The Centre for Accessibility online services portal, Clockwork, is for UBC Vancouver students only\.

If eligible, submit your peer notetaking request as well\. Review important [notetaking request guidelines](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-accommodations-disabilities/types-academic-accommodations/)\.

## Information for faculty[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/centre\-for\-accessibility/\#information\-for\-faculty](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/#information-for-faculty)

If you are a faculty member, please log in to view student accommodation letters and upcoming exams requested by your students registered with the Centre\. The list of Accommodation Letters does not represent the students who have booked an exam\.

Exam information, including duration, format, and exam allowances must be received at least 2 business days prior to the exam date, otherwise the Centre is unable to host the exams\. UBC’s obligation to provide the exam accommodations will fall to the instructor\. You will be responsible for implementing the exam accommodations, as listed on the Letter of Accommodation\.

Review the [online guide](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/centre_for_accessibility_canvas_guide.pdf) \(pdf\) for how to adjust for student exam accommodations\.

View [Faculty and Staff resources](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-affairs/centre-accessibility) for more information and answers to frequently asked questions about Genio Notes\.
