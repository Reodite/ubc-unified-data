## Student logins[https://students\.ubc\.ca/\#student\-logins](https://students.ubc.ca/#student-logins)

To access UBC’s online platforms, you’ll need your [Student Campus\-wide Login](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl) \(CWL\)\. You may also need to enable [multi\-factor authentication](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/multi-factor-authentication-mfa#getting-started) for added security\.

-   [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/)
-   [Canvas](https://canvas.ubc.ca/)
