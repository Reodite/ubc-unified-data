UBC’s Simon K\. Y\. Lee Global Lounge, located at the Marine Drive Residence, is an inclusive space dedicated to supporting the unique needs and experiences of international students\. The lounge provides a welcoming environment to help international students navigate the challenges of living and studying in Canada while fostering a strong sense of community and connection among students from diverse backgrounds\.

## Eligibility to book the Simon K\. Y\. Lee Global Lounge[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/simon\-k\-y\-lee\-global\-lounge\-resource\-centre/booking\-global\-lounge/\#eligibility\-to\-book\-the\-simon\-k\-y\-lee\-global\-lounge](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/simon-k-y-lee-global-lounge-resource-centre/booking-global-lounge/#eligibility-to-book-the-simon-k-y-lee-global-lounge)

The Simon K\. Y\. Lee Global Lounge spaces are available year round to UBC students, staff, and faculty, subject to availability\. All bookings must meet **at least one** of the following program criteria to align with the lounge’s mission:

-   Focus on supporting the needs and experiences of International Students\. 
-   Fostering a sense of connection, community, and belonging for international students\.
-   Focus on helping international students navigate the challenges related to studying, working and living in Canada\. 

### UBC students

UBC students must have a valid student card and be enrolled in the current term to book space\. To ensure fair access, any student groups may book up to **3 times** per academic term\.

## Booking the Simon K\. Y\. Lee Global Lounge[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/simon\-k\-y\-lee\-global\-lounge\-resource\-centre/booking\-global\-lounge/\#booking\-the\-simon\-k\-y\-lee\-global\-lounge](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/simon-k-y-lee-global-lounge-resource-centre/booking-global-lounge/#booking-the-simon-k-y-lee-global-lounge)

The Simon K\. Y\. Lee Global Lounge has 2 rooms—the Media Centre and the Resource Centre—that can be booked throughout the week\. The lounge is **not available** for bookings in August\.

### Media Centre

[Image](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/global_lounge_media_centre-1024x744.jpeg)

**Room capacity**: 40 people

Room features:

-   More than 40 movable tables and chairs
-   Podium and lectern 
-   Natural light 
-   Can be separated by a glass wall from the Resource Centre and Lounge 
-   AV system: 60\-inch TV, DVD player, and ability to connect laptops for presentations 

### Resource Centre and Lounge

[Image](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/global_lounge_resource_centre-1024x683.jpeg)

The space includes tables and chairs that open into a cozy area with couches\.

**Room capacity**: 15 to 30 people

Room features:

-   Working area 
-   Lounge area and couches 
-   Movable tables and chairs \(3 tables and 12 chairs\)  
-   Podium and lectern 
-   Natural light 
-   Multimedia projector
