## About the group[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/dialectical\-behavior\-therapy\-skills\-group/\#about\-the\-group](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/dialectical-behavior-therapy-skills-group/#about-the-group)

When emotions feel too intense, or your reactions create more problems than solutions, Dialectical Behavior Therapy \(DBT\) can offer a way forward\.

DBT is an evidence\-based approach that teaches practical, step\-by\-step skills to manage emotions, tolerate distress, and navigate relationships more effectively\. It’s built on the idea that two things can be true at once – you can accept yourself as you are and still work toward meaningful change to build your better life\.

Structured like an interactive class, this 8\-week, in\-person group is where you can learn and practice strategies\. Alongside the group, you would take part in regular individual check\-ins with your health professional to support your progress\. 

This group is designed for students who want to:

-   Respond to emotions without feeling overwhelmed
-   Cope better with crisis moments
-   Build stronger, healthier relationships
-   Stay grounded and present, even under stress
