## About the group[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/rooted\-resilient\-group\-counselling/\#about\-the\-group](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/rooted-resilient-group-counselling/#about-the-group)

This 8\-week group is a supportive, practical space designed to help you build mental and emotional resiliency and handle academic stress without burning out\. Instead of pushing tough emotions away, the group will focus on strengthening your ability to navigate common challenges that university students face\. 

Each session will help you understand your emotional experiences, learn to pause and respond rather than react, and navigate discomfort using real, everyday strategies\. This is also a space to connect with other students and build a lasting social support network with one another beyond the group\.

This group is designed for students who are: 

-   Wanting to build resiliency and gain practical tools to cope with school stress, anxiety, and overwhelm
-   Hoping to connect with other students and build a community of mutual support
-   Interested in learning how to navigate their emotions but have little to no previous counselling experience
-   Experiencing mild signs of stress, like mild worry, frequent frustration, or feeling easily overwhelmed
-   Looking for a skills\-based group environment rather than individual therapy

## Eligibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/rooted\-resilient\-group\-counselling/\#eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/rooted-resilient-group-counselling/#eligibility)

This session is open to all UBC Vancouver students\. The sessions are held in person on the Vancouver campus\.

UBC Student Health and Wellbeing recognizes the unique needs of each student and will provide alternate options if group programming is not an appropriate referral option or fit\.
