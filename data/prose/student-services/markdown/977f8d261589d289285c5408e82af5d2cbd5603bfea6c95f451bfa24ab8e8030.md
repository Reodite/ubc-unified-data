## About the group[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/social\-skills\-group\-counselling/\#about\-the\-group](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/social-skills-group-counselling/#about-the-group)

This is a 7‑session therapeutic group that uses Dungeons and &amp; Dragons to support students with neurodiversity or social anxiety in developing social interaction and personal growth within a structured, supportive setting\.

The primary areas of focus are:

-   Foster insight, emotional awareness, regulation, and resilience in social situations
-   Practice and explore everyday social skills, such as communication, collaboration, and boundary‑setting, in a predictable group environment
-   Provide a supportive and inclusive space for personal growth, confidence‑building, and connection

## Eligibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/social\-skills\-group\-counselling/\#eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/social-skills-group-counselling/#eligibility)

You do not need to have previous experience playing Dungeons and Dragons to join the group\. The facilitators will  provide all game materials needed to enjoy the experience\.

All group sessions are held in person on the UBC Vancouver campus\. 

UBC Student Health and Wellbeing recognizes the unique needs of each student and will provide alternate options if group programming is not an appropriate referral option or fit\.
