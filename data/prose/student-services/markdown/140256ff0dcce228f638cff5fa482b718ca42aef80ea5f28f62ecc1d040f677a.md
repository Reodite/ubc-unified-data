## What ES Advisors can help with[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/enrolment\-services\-advisors/\#what\-es\-advisors\-can\-help\-with](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/#what-es-advisors-can-help-with)

If you are an undergraduate student in a degree program, you’re assigned a dedicated Enrolment Services Advisor \(ES Advisor\) to help you navigate registration, finances, and more at UBC\. The majority of newly admitted students are assigned an ES Advisor by the end of June\. If you don’t have an assigned advisor, you can still connect with other ES Advisors for help\.

For questions about course planning or program requirements, please connect directly with your [academic advising office](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/)\.

Reach out early for support, especially during registration periods and the beginning of school terms\. Enrolment Services will offer extended hours of phone support during the registration periods in June and July\.

### Top reasons to connect

-   Get help with managing your finances or creating a [financial plan](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/)\.
-   Talk to someone about [scholarships, bursaries, awards](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/), and [student loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/)\.
-   Walk through the ways you can pay your [tuition and fees](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/paying-tuition/)\.
-   Understand UBC regulations and processes\.
-   Find support for [course registration](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/) issues\.
-   Access and update your [student records](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/)\.
-   Request letters, complete forms, or gather signatures required from the Registrar’s office\.

## Contact us[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/enrolment\-services\-advisors/\#contact\-us](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/#contact-us)

Enrolment Services is closed on all [statutory holidays](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/statutory-holidays) observed by UBC\.

### Connect online

-   **If you’re an undergraduate student,** learn how to [find your ES Advisor’s email](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/ubc-advisor-contacts/finding-your-enrolment-services-advisors-contact-information/) in Workday\. Your ES Advisor is your main point of contact in Enrolment Services\. Please contact them directly with questions\.
-   **If you’re a graduate, postgraduate, other type of student, or an alumni,** submit your question through the [online contact form](https://www.askme.ubc.ca/form/s/)\.
