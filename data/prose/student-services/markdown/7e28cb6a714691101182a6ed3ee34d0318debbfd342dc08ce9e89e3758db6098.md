## About the group[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/acceptance\-commitment\-therapy\-anxiety\-group/\#about\-the\-group](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/acceptance-commitment-therapy-anxiety-group/#about-the-group)

Anxiety may show up in many forms, such as perfectionism, exam anxiety, excessive worry about social interactions, or panic\. This in\-person, interactive group is for anyone who feels like they are in a constant struggle with anxiety and worry\.  

Based on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy \(ACT\), the group offers a safe and non\-judgmental space to develop strategies to take a step back from the cognitive struggle, become more accepting of yourself and emotions, and to reconnect with a value\-driven life\.  

## Eligibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/acceptance\-commitment\-therapy\-anxiety\-group/\#eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/acceptance-commitment-therapy-anxiety-group/#eligibility)

This session is open to all UBC Vancouver students\. Group sessions are held in person\.

UBC Student Health and Wellbeing recognizes the unique needs of each student and will provide alternate options if group programming is not an appropriate referral option or fit\.
