Many different forms of emergencies can occur\. Get options for immediate help if you or someone else is in danger, or at risk of harming yourself or others\.

## 24/7 support options[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/crisis\-support\-services/\#24\-7\-support\-options](https://students.ubc.ca/health/crisis-support-services/#24-7-support-options)

[British Columbia Emergency Care](https://www.vch.ca/en/health-topics/emergency-care)

Call [911](tel:911) or visit the nearest [hospital emergency department](https://www.vch.ca/en/health-topics/emergency-care) if you or a loved one requires immediate medical attention for illness, injury, self\-harm, or overdose\.

[9\-8\-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline](https://988.ca/get-help/help-right-now)

If you are thinking about suicide, or you’re worried about someone else, call or text [988](tel:988) in Canada, toll\-free, any time\.

[Here2Talk](https://here2talk.ca/main)

Call, chat online, or use the mobile app to get free, immediate, 24/7 mental health counselling, available in various languages for post\-secondary students in British Columbia\. UBC students can reach out as often as needed, anytime, from anywhere in the world\.

[First Nations and Inuit Hope for Wellness Helpline](https://www.hopeforwellness.ca/)

If you are an Indigenous student in Canada, get immediate mental health counselling or crisis intervention by calling [1 855 242 3310](tel:18552423310) or by chatting online any time\.

[Community Safety](https://security.ubc.ca/)

Community Safety is available 24/7\. Call [604 822 2222](tel:604%20822%202222) if you need help, feel threatened, or notice suspicious activity\.

## Help a friend[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/crisis\-support\-services/\#help\-a\-friend](https://students.ubc.ca/health/crisis-support-services/#help-a-friend)

If a friend is at risk of harming themselves:

-   Call [911](tel:911) or contact your [nearest emergency department](https://www.vch.ca/en/health-topics/emergency-care)\.
-   Access the [9\-8\-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline](https://988.ca/get-help/help-right-now)\. Call or text [988](tel:988) in Canada, toll\-free, any time\. A crisis responder can talk you through your concerns and offer advice and support\.

Learn more about how you can [support your friend](https://students.ubc.ca/health/crisis-support-services/helping-friend/) if they’re experiencing suicidal thoughts or struggling with their mental health\.

If you think a friend may be experiencing a drug poisoning or overdose, call [911](tel:911) immediately and follow guidance on [responding to an overdose](https://students.ubc.ca/health/substance-use-health-harm-reduction/)\.

## Responding to an overdose or drug poisoning[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/crisis\-support\-services/\#responding\-to\-an\-overdose\-or\-drug\-poisoning](https://students.ubc.ca/health/crisis-support-services/#responding-to-an-overdose-or-drug-poisoning)

If you or someone else may be experiencing an overdose:

1.  Call [911](tel:911) right away\.
2.  Stay with the person until help arrives\.
3.  If available, give [naloxone](https://towardtheheart.com/naloxone-training) and follow the emergency responder’s instructions\.

Learn more about recognizing the signs of overdose and how to respond on UBC’s [Substance use health and harm reduction](https://students.ubc.ca/health/substance-use-health-harm-reduction/) page\. Visit [Toward the Heart](https://towardtheheart.com/naloxone-training) for more training resources\.

## Help for those who have experienced sexual assault[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/crisis\-support\-services/\#help\-for\-those\-who\-have\-experienced\-sexual\-assault](https://students.ubc.ca/health/crisis-support-services/#help-for-those-who-have-experienced-sexual-assault)

If you or someone you know have been sexually assaulted, the first steps you should take include ensuring safety, getting to a safe space, and seeking medical attention if needed\. UBC’s [Sexual Violence Prevention and Response Office](https://svpro.ubc.ca/) \(SVPRO\) is here to help and listen\.

## Support for those who have experienced gender\-based violence[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/crisis\-support\-services/\#support\-for\-those\-who\-have\-experienced\-gender\-based\-violence](https://students.ubc.ca/health/crisis-support-services/#support-for-those-who-have-experienced-gender-based-violence)

If you or someone you know has experienced gender\-based violence in any form, please take steps to prioritize your safety and seek medical attention if needed\. UBC’s [Sexual Violence Prevention and Response Office](https://svpro.ubc.ca/) \(SVPRO\) is here to help and listen\.
