## About the event[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/events\-workshops/launch\-career\-canada/\#about\-the\-event](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/launch-career-canada/#about-the-event)

Launch Your Career in Canada is UBC’s annual career development intensive for international undergraduate and graduate students\. The event provides a space to explore career possibilities with your UBC degree, test your assumptions about the job market with industry professionals, and learn from UBC alumni who have been where you are now\. This is your opportunity to practice job search skills in a low\-stakes environment with people who are interested in your success and growth\. Registration opens on **Mon, Jan 26, 2026** on [CareersOnline](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/app/events)\.

### Event details

-   **Date**: Mon, Feb 23 to Thurs, Feb 26, 2026
-   **Location**: Virtual and in\-person

## 2026 Event schedule[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/events\-workshops/launch\-career\-canada/\#2026\-event\-schedule](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/launch-career-canada/#2026-event-schedule)

### Path to Professional Success: Your First Canadian Work Experience 

-   **Date:** Mon, Feb 23, 2026
-   **Time:** 1:00 to 2:00 pm
-   **Session format:** Virtual

Gain valuable job search strategies that can help you pave the way to securing your first Canadian job\. Learn about common career challenges as international students, the norms of the Canadian labour market, and how to explore on\-campus experiences to enhance your professional skills before you enter the job market\. This workshop will address issues that are important to both undergraduate and graduate students\.

### Chart Your Path: Making Career Decisions in Uncertain Times

-   **Date:** Tue, Feb 24, 2026
-   **Time:** 12:00 to 1:00 pm
-   **Session format:** Virtual

This workshop provides a clear roadmap, practical strategies, and tailored resources to help expand or clarify your possibilities and guide your decision\-making process\. Learn how to navigate uncertainty with confidence and make choices that set you up for success in launching your career in Canada\.

### Pathways to Post\-Graduation Work Permit \(PGWP\)

-   **Date:** Wed, Feb 25, 2026
-   **Time:** 1:00 to 2:30 pm
-   **Session format:** Virtual

Get up\-to\-date information about the recently announced changes to the Post\-Graduation Work Permit \(PGWP\) Program, eligibility criteria, application process, work eligibility during the transition to a PGWP, and important considerations when travelling after completing your studies\. The presentation will be followed by a Q&amp;A session with Regulated International Student Immigration Advisors \(RISIAs\) from [International Student Advising\.](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/)

### Ask Hiring Managers and Alumni How to Get Recruited

-   **Date:** Thurs, Feb 26, 2026
-   **Time:** 6:00 to 8:00 pm
-   **Session format:** In\-person

Attend an in\-person event featuring insightful discussions and networking opportunities with hiring managers and UBC alumni\. You’ll have the chance to hear from hiring managers about what they look for in candidates, what unique strengths international students bring to organizations, and connect with alumni who were international students and have successfully established their careers in Canada\.
