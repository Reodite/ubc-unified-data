## About Associate Career Advisors[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/associate\-career\-advisors/\#about\-associate\-career\-advisors](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/associate-career-advisors/#about-associate-career-advisors)

Associate Career Advisors \(ACAs\) work with the [UBC Career Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-career-centre/) to help UBC students from all disciplines become career\-ready graduates\. ACAs facilitate career learning for undergraduate and graduate students through one\-to\-one advising, workshops, and more\.

## Who can apply?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/associate\-career\-advisors/\#who\-can\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/associate-career-advisors/#who-can-apply)

To apply, students must be:

-   Current upper\-year undergraduate, Master’s, or PhD students
-   Eligible for Work Learn positions
-   Can commit to a one\-year term from May to the following April

There are **4 to 8 positions** available each year\.

### If you require accommodations

The Career Center would like to recognize that being able to participate in student leadership development opportunities such as this is a privilege\. We will ensure that individuals with disabilities are provided reasonable accommodation to participate in the job application or interview process, perform essential job functions, and receive other benefits and privileges of employment\.

If you have specific accommodations for your ability to participate and contribute to the program, please email the Team Lead, Gelsey, at [gelsey\.guan@ubc\.ca](mailto:gelsey.guan@ubc.ca) to discuss what accommodations can be made for your particular situation\.

## Time commitment[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/associate\-career\-advisors/\#time\-commitment](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/associate-career-advisors/#time-commitment)

The time commitment is 20 hours per week during the Summer Term \(May to August\), and 10 hours per week during the Winter Term \(September to April\)\. For detailed time requirements, please review the position description\.

## How to Apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/associate\-career\-advisors/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/associate-career-advisors/#how-to-apply)

This position is listed as a Summer Work Learn position in [CareersOnline](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/app/home)\.

You can apply in March during the Work Learn application period\. Check the Work Learn website for specific timelines and how to submit your application through CareersOnline\.
