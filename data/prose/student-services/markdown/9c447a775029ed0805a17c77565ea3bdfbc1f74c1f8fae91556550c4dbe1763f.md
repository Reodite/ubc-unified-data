## About the awards[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/awards\-women\-part\-time\-studies/\#about\-the\-awards](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-women-part-time-studies/#about-the-awards)

Scholarships are available for female\-identifying students on the Vancouver campus enrolled in part\-time studies for both the previous and upcoming Winter Sessions\. The Winter Session is from September to April\.

[Read award descriptions](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-women-part-time-studies/description-awards-women-part-time-studies/)

## Eligibility requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/awards\-women\-part\-time\-studies/\#eligibility\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-women-part-time-studies/#eligibility-requirements)

You must meet all of the following requirements to apply:

### Undergraduate students

-   You must be registered in 23 credits or less in the upcoming Winter Session\.
-   You must have been studying part\-time during the past academic year\.

### Graduate students

-   You must be paying [part\-time fees](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/tuition-fees-cost-living/graduate-tuition-fees) in the upcoming Winter Session\.
-   You must have been studying part\-time during the past academic year\.

### Exceptions

-   Your courses cannot predominantly use [Credit/D/Fail grading](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/creditdfail-grading/)\.
-   Eligibility for [Non\-Degree Studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/) students is considered on a case\-by\-case basis\.

## Before you apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/awards\-women\-part\-time\-studies/\#before\-you\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-women-part-time-studies/#before-you-apply)

Detailed instructions are provided on the application form, but before you start your application, make sure you complete the following:

1.  Read through the [award descriptions](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-women-part-time-studies/description-awards-women-part-time-studies/) to view eligibility criteria\.
2.  If you were not a UBC student in the 2025/26 Winter Session, you will be required to provide a copy of your official transcript for the 2025/26 academic year\. The transcript can be uploaded with your application\.
3.  You will also need to prepare a letter detailing your educational goals, objectives, and financial need\.
