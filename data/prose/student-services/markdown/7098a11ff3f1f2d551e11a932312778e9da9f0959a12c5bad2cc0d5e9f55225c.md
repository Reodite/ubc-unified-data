## About the group[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/adhd\-skills\-support\-group/\#about\-the\-group](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/adhd-skills-support-group/#about-the-group)

If you have ADHD or think you might have ADHD, this in\-person skills\-based support group may be what you are looking for\. Attend a non\-clinical, non\-diagnostic group with other students who are struggling with ADHD and ADHD\-related concerns\. An ADHD diagnosis is not required to join this group\.

Topics will include:

-   Understanding ADHD
-   Strategies to sharpen memory
-   Coping with procrastination and distraction
-   Choices and getting unstuck
-   ADHD\-related strengths

Group sessions are standalone\. You can join at any time, and you are encouraged to attend as many sessions as you find helpful\.

## Eligibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/adhd\-skills\-support\-group/\#eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/adhd-skills-support-group/#eligibility)
