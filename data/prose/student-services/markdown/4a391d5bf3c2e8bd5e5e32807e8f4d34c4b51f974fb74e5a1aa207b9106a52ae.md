UBC is a Title IV institution, which means eligible students can access United States \(US\) federal financial aid programs\.

Various sources of funding are available at UBC to US citizens and eligible non\-citizens, including Direct Loans, 529 Plans, private loans, and more\.

Eligibility varies for each type of funding and is based on many factors\. Please review the individual requirements for each funding source\.

## Direct Loans[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/\#direct\-loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/#direct-loans)

Eligible students and parents can borrow directly from the US Department of Education through the US Federal Direct Loan Program\. 

At UBC, the available types of Direct Loans include 

-   Direct Subsidized Loans
-   Direct Unsubsidized Loans
-   Parent and Grad PLUS Loans

[Get details](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-federal-direct-loan-program/)

## Alternative source of funding[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/\#alternative\-source\-of\-funding](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/#alternative-source-of-funding)

Eligible students may use [alternative funding sources](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/alternative-sources-funding-us/) such as 529 plans, AmeriCorps, and Veterans Affairs education benefits\.

If you’re a dual Canadian citizen, you may also be eligible to apply for [Canadian Student Loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/)\.

## Private loans[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/\#private\-loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/#private-loans)

If you are not eligible for Direct Loans, or need additional funding, [private US student loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/us-private-student-loans/) are available at UBC through various lenders\.

If you have questions about your funding eligibility or assessment, please contact an Enrolment Services Advisor\.
