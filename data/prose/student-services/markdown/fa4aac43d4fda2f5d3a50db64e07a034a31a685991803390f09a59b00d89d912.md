Here’s to new beginnings and the start of your UBC experience\.

## Welcome to your first year\! [https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/\#welcome\-to\-your\-first\-year](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/#welcome-to-your-first-year) 

Congratulations on joining a diverse community of scholars\. We can’t wait for you to arrive on the beautiful UBC Vancouver campus, situated on the [traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory](https://indigenous.ubc.ca/indigenous-engagement/musqueam-and-ubc/) of the Musqueam people\.

UBC is committed to providing a safe, supportive, and inclusive living and learning environment, with [resources and services](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services) to support all students\. Whether you’re interested in meeting new people, pursuing a passion, gaining work experience, or more, the opportunities are endless\.

## Before you start at UBC[https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/\#before\-you\-start\-at\-ubc](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/#before-you-start-at-ubc)

### Your student checklist

1.  Apply online for your [UBCcard](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/obtaining-a-ubccard/students) and get your monthly [U\-Pass transit pass](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/transit/u-pass-compass-card)\.
2.  Find out what [UBC classes and grading](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/introduction-ubc-classes) are like\.
3.  Understand your [student health insurance details](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance)\.
4.  Get a [Social Insurance Number \(SIN\)](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/taxes/social-insurance-number-sin) if you plan to work or if you receive an award\.
5.  Kick off your school year with [UBC orientations](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/orientations)\.
