## What does auditing mean?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/auditing\-courses/\#what\-does\-auditing\-mean](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/auditing-courses/#what-does-auditing-mean)

Auditing means taking a course without seeking a grade or credit\. Students usually audit courses for self\-interest and academic exploration\.

When you audit a course, your participation in the course is set by the instructor\. In general, if you’re auditing a course, you’ll be expected to complete all course requirements except the final exam, and your transcript will show a statement of audit for the course\.

If you choose to audit a course, you will still be assessed [regular tuition and mandatory student fees](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/undergraduate-tuition-fees/)\. Read more about the Audit \(AUD\) course standing in the [Academic Calendar](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/grading-practices/standings)\.

## Registering to audit a course[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/auditing\-courses/\#registering\-to\-audit\-a\-course](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/auditing-courses/#registering-to-audit-a-course)

The following instructions are for undergraduate students\. Graduate students should refer to the [Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/managing-your-program/audit) website for information\. 

1.  Download the [Registration or Change of Registration \(Add/Drop\) Form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/registration_adddrop_form_2024_fall.pdf) \(pdf\)\. You can also pick up a paper copy from your [faculty advising office](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) or the [Enrolment Services Advising Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) in Brock Hall\.
2.  Fill out the “Course Section Add” section of the form and check off “Audit” in the left\-hand column\.
3.  Ask your course instructor to sign the form\.
4.  Follow the instructions on the form to submit it to the appropriate office\.

## Application deadlines[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/auditing\-courses/\#application\-deadlines](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/auditing-courses/#application-deadlines)

Submit your form by the [add/drop deadline](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates/) to update your status to “Auditing”\.

For course\-specific add/drop deadlines, [view your course information](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/viewing-the-course-schedule/) in Workday\.

| Course type | Deadline for changing auditing status |
| --- | --- |
| 2026/27 Winter Term 1 courses | Mon, Sep 21, 2026 |
| 2026/27 Winter Term 1 and 2 courses | Mon, Sep 28, 2026 |
| 2026/27 Winter Term 2 courses | Mon, Jan 18, 2027 |

## Additional signatures you may need[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/courses/auditing\-courses/\#additional\-signatures\-you\-may\-need](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/auditing-courses/#additional-signatures-you-may-need)

Follow the form instructions for the required signatures\. Depending on your program, you’ll need to connect with either your [faculty advising office](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) or the [Enrolment Services Advising Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\.

If you’re auditing a graduate\-level course, you’ll need to receive a form and signature from [Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/managing-your-program/audit)\.
