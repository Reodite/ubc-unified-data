## About the Global Lounge[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/simon\-k\-y\-lee\-global\-lounge\-resource\-centre/\#about\-the\-global\-lounge](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/simon-k-y-lee-global-lounge-resource-centre/#about-the-global-lounge)

The Global Lounge is a welcoming space designed for international students at UBC\. Whether you’re looking to make friends, connect with peers, or learn how to navigate life at UBC and in Canada, the Global Lounge offers programs, events, and peer support to help you feel at home\. 

### Global Lounge space bookings

If you’re a student group, faculty, or campus unit interested in hosting events for international students, you can [book a room](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/simon-k-y-lee-global-lounge-resource-centre/booking-global-lounge/) in the Global Lounge space for events\.
