## About the event series[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/events\-workshops/ubc\-purpose\-driven/\#about\-the\-event\-series](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/ubc-purpose-driven/#about-the-event-series)

UBC Purpose\-Driven is a career building and networking experience organized by the [UBC Career Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-career-centre/), in partnership with Alumni UBC\.

Attend these events to learn about the latest research, current trends, challenges, opportunities, and more in various industries\. You’ll get to hear from amazing alumni and industry professionals from different backgrounds who are tackling global matters with creative solutions\.

## Careers Powering Sport and Human Performance[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/events\-workshops/ubc\-purpose\-driven/\#careers\-powering\-sport\-and\-human\-performance](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/ubc-purpose-driven/#careers-powering-sport-and-human-performance)

Curious about how you can turn your passion for sports into a career in the industry, no matter your degree? Explore diverse career journeys you didn’t know existed and connect with industry professionals, experts, and alumni that work in the Sports and Human Performance field\.

This session is open to students from all faculties\.
