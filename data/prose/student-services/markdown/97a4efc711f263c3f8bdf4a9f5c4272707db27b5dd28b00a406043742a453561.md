## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/your\-degree\-in\-neuroscience/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/your-degree-in-neuroscience/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Neuroscience](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc_programs/neuroscience/), you’ll gain a deep understanding of the biological basis of behaviour and explore the structure and function of the brain and nervous system\. You’ll develop important skills and learn advanced research methods to gain a multidisciplinary understanding of behavior and cognition as they relate to nervous system function\.

These skills may include:

-   Critical observation and evaluation of nervous system dysfunction, ranging from psychiatric to neurological conditions
-   Design and execution of neuroscience experiments with human participants or animal models
-   Research and critical evaluation of biomedical literature
-   Application of specialized knowledge related to neurological diseases and disorders
-   Application of ethical considerations in neuroscience research, decision making, and consideration of societal implications
-   Verbal and written communication of technical and scientific data aimed at neuroscientific and non\-neuroscientific audiences
-   Technical usage of computational modeling, neuroimaging, and statistics
