## About the role[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/ubthechange\-student\-activators/\#about\-the\-role](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/ubthechange-student-activators/#about-the-role)

UBtheChange Student Activators create fun, low\-barrier ways for UBC students to get involved with social and community issues, regardless of their background or experience\.

Through events, peer advising, social media campaigns, and pop\-up activities, UBtheChange Student Activators spark curiosity, build awareness, foster connections, and highlight pathways to engage in social and climate justice on and off campus\.

## Who can apply?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/ubthechange\-student\-activators/\#who\-can\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/ubthechange-student-activators/#who-can-apply)

Students from all disciplines and years can apply, with preference given to students with experience in community engagement\.

-   Passionate about social and environmental issues
-   Enjoys collaborating with diverse peers, campus, and community partners
-   Excited to build facilitation, mentorship, and leadership skills
-   Desire to learn through hands\-on experiences
-   Interest in about systemic injustice, power, and community strengths
-   Respectful of diverse perspectives and lived experiences
-   Reflective, open to feedback, and committed to growth
-   Organized, self\-motivated, and able to manage tasks and deadlines

## Time commitment[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/peer\-programs/ubthechange\-student\-activators/\#time\-commitment](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/ubthechange-student-activators/#time-commitment)
