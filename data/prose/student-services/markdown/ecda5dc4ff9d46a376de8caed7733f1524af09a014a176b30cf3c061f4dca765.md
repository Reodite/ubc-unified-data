## If you’re injured[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/campus\-safety/\#if\-youre\-injured](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety/#if-youre-injured)

**For medical emergencies**, please call [911](tel:911)\. 

If you’re injured, you can visit [UBC Student Health and Wellbeing](https://students.ubc.ca/health/student-health-service/) on campus, or go to [Urgent Care](http://www.vch.ca/Locations-Services/result?res_id=991) at the UBC Hospital\.

If you need help getting to a class or another location on campus due to mobility issues, book a ride on the [Accessibility Shuttle](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/ubc-accessibility-shuttle/)\.

## Contact Community Safety anytime[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/campus\-safety/\#contact\-community\-safety\-anytime](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety/#contact-community-safety-anytime)

Community Safety is available 24/7\. They provide first aid support, can accompany you across campus anytime, and are here to help if you need assistance, feel unsafe, or notice suspicious activity\. Call [604 822 2222](tel:6048222222)\. For emergencies, call [911](tel:911)\.

You can also reach them immediately on a [Blue Phone](https://security.ubc.ca/home/safety-prevention-resources/emergency-blue-phones/), one of more than 85 blue\-coloured poles on campus that have the word “Assistance” printed on them\.

## Know the signs of an accidental drug poisoning[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/campus\-safety/\#know\-the\-signs\-of\-an\-accidental\-drug\-poisoning](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety/#know-the-signs-of-an-accidental-drug-poisoning)

Many illegal substances in Canada can have unknown mixes of drugs in them, like fentanyl or benzodiazepines\. This has resulted in many deaths in British Columbia and across the country\. If you plan to use substances, [make a plan to reduce harm](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/substance-use-health-harm-reduction/) and know how to [respond to accidental drug poisoning](https://towardtheheart.com/opioid-od-awareness)\.

You can get a free and confidential naloxone kit from [Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre/), at [AMS SASC](https://www.amssasc.ca/), or at any [participating BC pharmacy](http://www.naloxonetraining.com/training)\. There are also [fentanyl test strips](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/substance-use-health-harm-reduction/) available on campus\.

## Keeping your stuff safe[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/campus\-safety/\#keeping\-your\-stuff\-safe](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety/#keeping-your-stuff-safe)

Don’t leave valuables like your phone or computer unattended in libraries and other public spaces\. It can only take a few seconds for them to disappear\.

As a preventative measure, upload free device location software like [Find My iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/explore/find-my-iphone-ipad-mac-watch) to track your phone or computer if it should ever be lost or stolen\.

If you bike to campus, investing in a high\-quality lock goes a long way to preventing bike theft\. Check out [helpful tips](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/cycling/cycling-tips-and-resources) on keeping your bike safe and cycling to campus\. 

If you lose something on campus, you can search the [Lost and Found Database](https://lostandfound.ubc.ca/all-items) to check if your item has been turned in\. Most lost items will typically be brought to the on\-campus [Lost and Found desk](https://lostandfound.ubc.ca/)\.

## Walking at night on campus[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/campus\-safety/\#walking\-at\-night\-on\-campus](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety/#walking-at-night-on-campus)

If you don’t feel comfortable walking around campus by yourself, particularly in the dark, plan to walk with friends, or contact [AMS Safewalk](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/support-services/student-services/safewalk/) for a co\-ed team to walk with you or drive you to your destination\.

AMS Safewalk is available until **2:00 am**\. [UBC Campus Security](http://security.ubc.ca/) is available 24/7 and will gladly provide the same service\.

## Staying safe online[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/campus\-safety/\#staying\-safe\-online](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety/#staying-safe-online)

Watch out for emails that do not seem legitimate as they can be [phishing emails](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/phishing-emails), which try to steal your personal info by inviting you to click on links\.

Remember that UBC will **never** send you an email asking for your Campus\-Wide Login or password\. 

Visit [Privacy Matters @ UBC](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/) to learn more about how to stay safe online, including how to encrypt your computer and mobile devices\.​​

## Frauds and scams[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/campus\-safety/\#frauds\-and\-scams](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety/#frauds-and-scams)

Unfortunately, there are many different frauds and scams out there, such as those impersonating government departments like the [Canada Revenue Agency](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency.html)\.

Visit the [RCMP website](http://bc.rcmp-grc.gc.ca/ViewPage.action?siteNodeId=2081&amp;languageId=1&amp;contentId=-1) for valuable tips on how to protect your money and remember that if something sounds too good to be true, it probably is\.

If you suspect a phone call, email, rental, job offer, or anything else may be a scam, or if you have been the victim of a scam, the best thing to do is call the police at 911\.

## Reach out for sexual assault support[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/campus\-safety/\#reach\-out\-for\-sexual\-assault\-support](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety/#reach-out-for-sexual-assault-support)

If you have experienced sexual violence or if you know someone who has, call [604 822 1588](tel:6048221588) to contact the [Sexual Violence Prevention and Response Office](http://svpro.ubc.ca/) \(SVPRO\) for support\.

## Get safety alerts[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/campus\-safety/\#get\-safety\-alerts](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety/#get-safety-alerts)

Sign up for the [UBC Alert system](https://srs.ubc.ca/emergency/get-notified/) to receive safety news and information from the university through the UBC Safe Vancouver app\. [Alertable](https://metrovancouver.org/services/emergency-management/emergency-notification-system) is a free app used by emergency management agencies across BC to issue regional alerts like wildfires, severe weather, and more\. Use this to stay informed about events that may impact your commute or region\.

## Preparing for emergencies[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/campus\-safety/\#preparing\-for\-emergencies](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety/#preparing-for-emergencies)

Emergencies like earthquakes, fires, or severe weather can happen when you least expect them\. Knowing what to do ahead of time can make a big difference\.

Visit the [UBC Safety &amp; Risk Services](https://srs.ubc.ca/) website to learn how to stay safe on campus in a variety of situations\. You’ll find simple checklists, emergency kit guides, evacuation tips, and more\.
