The Centre’s programs provide you with community\-based opportunities to learn, participate, and collaborate on projects on and off campus\. You can engage with passionate students and community partners through a [UBtheChange](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/ubthechange/) event or Community Action Day project, present your research skills at the [Multidisciplinary Undergraduate Research Conference](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/multidisciplinary-undergraduate-research-conference/), lead a project by writing a [community grant](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/grants-community-projects/), and learn how strategize for action on complex social issues through the [Social Impact Lab](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/social-impact-lab/)\.

The Centre also provides resources and support to instructors, departments, and faculties to enhance teaching and learning processes and connect university resources to the community in ways that support lasting relationships\.

### Reasons to connect with the Centre

-   Connect with community organizations to lead or collaborate on projects\.
-   Learn how to affect positive social change in your community\.
-   Grow your network by connecting with like\-minded students and community\.
-   Channel your values into personal growth\.
-   Explore something you are passionate about\.

### Programs offered

In person and virtual programming are being offered\. Please check out the programs below\.

-   [CCEL workshops](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/ccel-workshops/)  
    Learn how to get started in community\-based projects and learning experiences\.
-   [Social Impact Lab](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/social-impact-lab/)  
    Develop ideas and solutions through actionable projects that tackle social and environmental issues\.
-   [UBtheChange](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/ubthechange/)  
    Work towards becoming a changemaker through on\-campus engagements and short\-term community projects\.
-   [Community grants](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/grants-community-projects/)  
    Apply to fund your in\-person or virtual projects\.
-   [Multidisciplinary Undergraduate Research Conference](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/multidisciplinary-undergraduate-research-conference/)  
    Present your research with fellow UBC students, family, and friends\.
-   [Student Directed Seminars](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/student-directed-seminars/)  
    Coordinate your own 3\-credit seminar class with a small group of peers\.
