## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/canadian\-studies/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/canadian-studies/#skills-youll-develop)

During your [Canadian Studies](http://canadianstudies.ubc.ca/program/) degree, you’ll critically engage the country’s past and present and seek to understand Canada’s place in the world\. You’ll develop important skills through an interdisciplinary program that examines culture and society, language and the arts, geography, history, economics, and politics\.

These skills may include:

-   Thinking critically and analyzing concepts related to Canadian culture and history from multiple perspectives
-   Applying problem solving to the examination of Canadian issues
-   Researching, collecting data, synthesizing, and analyzing large amounts of data
-   Being resourceful in finding crucial information and using context to understand its meaning and importance
-   Persuasive public speaking and reasoning abilities that are evidence\-based and acknowledge the influence different perspectives can have on facts
-   Written and verbal communication skills used to identify and examine contemporary issues
-   Reflecting on and analyzing one’s own identity, influences, assumptions, and values

## Make the most of your program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/canadian\-studies/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/canadian-studies/#make-the-most-of-your-program)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities, develop your skillset, and help you clarify your values and interests\.

-   [UBC Canadian Studies Students’ Association](https://canadianstudies.ubc.ca/student-opportunities/the-canadian-studies-student-association/)  
    Meet other UBC students through events and programs\. 
-   [Departmental research opportunities](http://canadianstudies.ubc.ca/the-chair-of-the-program-in-canadian-studies/ubc-faculty-with-canadian-research-interests/)  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   [Young Canada Works in Heritage Organizations](https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/services/funding/young-canada-works/students-graduates/heritage-organizations-students.html)  
    Develop new skills and gain practical knowledge in a heritage field over the summer\.
-   [Parliamentary Internship Programme](https://pip-psp.org/)  
    Apply for an opportunity to shadow a Member of Parliament over 10 months \(for new grads\)\.
-   [BC Legislative Internship Program](https://www.leg.bc.ca/learn/bclip)  
    Assist a Member of the Legislative Assembly and observe the daily workings of the BC Legislature \(for new grads\)\.
-   [Ontario Legislature Internship Programme](https://www.olipinterns.ca/the-internship)  
    Gain practical experience with the daily workings of the Ontario Legislature \(for new grads\)\.
-   [Canada Summer Jobs Program](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/youth)  
    Check out summer jobs for full\-time students\.
-   [Federal Student Work Experience Program](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-service-commission/jobs/services/recruitment/students/federal-student-work-program.html)  
    Apply for government positions across Canada through this student recruitment initiative\.
-   [Government jobs](https://www.govjobs.ca/)  
    Access federal, provincial and municipal government job postings\.
-   [Government volunteer and internship positions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/culture/cultural-youth-programs/jobs-internships.html)  
    Discover local and international opportunities to prepare you for future employment\.
-   [Volunteer and internship opportunities](https://ir.arts.ubc.ca/programs/opportunities/)  
    Check out various resources to gain local and international experience\.
