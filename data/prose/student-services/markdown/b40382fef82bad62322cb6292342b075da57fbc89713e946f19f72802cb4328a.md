## Your first stop: UBC Career Centre[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/\#your\-first\-stop\-ubc\-career\-centre](https://students.ubc.ca/career/#your-first-stop-ubc-career-centre)

Whether you’re just starting at UBC or planning your next steps after graduation, the UBC Career Centre is your go\-to service for career exploration and guidance\. Meet with a Career Educator, explore future possibilities, get support on your job applications, discuss grad school options, and more\.

We’ll help you connect your strengths to future opportunities, no matter your area of study or where you are in your journey\.
