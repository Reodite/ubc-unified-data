If you’re an international student, please familiarize yourself with the Canadian laws about the [rights and responsibilities of parents](https://family.legalaid.bc.ca/children/parenting-guardianship/guardianship/guardianship-parenting-time-and-parental) in British Columbia\. ## About the British Columbia school system[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/equity\-diversity\-inclusion\-resources/resources\-who\-are\-parents/child\-care\-school\-registration/\#about\-the\-british\-columbia\-school\-system](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/resources-who-are-parents/child-care-school-registration/#about-the-british-columbia-school-system)

In Canada, the school year runs from September to June\. There is typically a two\-week break in December and a two\-week break in March for elementary and secondary schools\. Children can start kindergarten at age 4\.

The [Vancouver School Board](http://www.vsb.bc.ca/) operates the public elementary and secondary schools in Vancouver\. Suburbs of Vancouver have their own school boards\.

### International student information

If your study permit is valid for at least one year, and you are enrolled at a publicly funded institution such as UBC, your minor children aged 18 years or younger are eligible to attend elementary and secondary school as if they were Canadian residents\. They will not be required to pay international student fees\. 

### English classes for children

Many schools offer ESL classes for their students as part of the regular curriculum\.

For information about these programs, contact the [Immigrant Services Society of BC](http://www.issbc.org/) by phone at [604 684 2561](tel:6046842561) or through email at [iss@issbc\.org](mailto:iss@issbc.org)\.

## Registering your children in school[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/equity\-diversity\-inclusion\-resources/resources\-who\-are\-parents/child\-care\-school\-registration/\#registering\-your\-children\-in\-school](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/resources-who-are-parents/child-care-school-registration/#registering-your-children-in-school)

You should enroll your children in school as soon as you’ve accepted your offer to study at UBC\. If you’ll be living on\-campus or in Vancouver, visit the Vancouver School Board’s [Newcomer Welcome Centre](https://www.vsb.bc.ca/nwc) online for more information\.

### **For children born outside of Canada**

If you meet all of the following requirements, please review the [Vancouver School Board resources](https://www.vsb.bc.ca/nwc/page/11332/registration) to register your child for school:

-   Your child was born outside of Canada
-   Your primary language at home is not English
-   You and your child are currently living in Vancouver and have a confirmed address in Vancouver

### **For children born in Canada**

If your child was born in Canada and English is their first language, please [register online](https://vsbapplynow.vsb.bc.ca/) with the Vancouver School Board\.
