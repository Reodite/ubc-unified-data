## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/pharmacology/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/pharmacology/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Pharmacology](https://apt.med.ubc.ca/education/undergraduate-program/), you’re learning how biomedical advances have practical applications to human health\. You’ll develop important skills in research and understand the effects of drugs on cells, organs and systems\.

These skills may include:

-   Comprehension and identification of connections across complex processes and systems
-   Analytical method development or validation \(QA/QC\)
-   Quantitative and qualitative research and data synthesis
-   Application of logical and systematic thought processes to understand the biochemical or physiological effects of drugs
-   Collaboration with cross\-discipline laboratory teams
-   Written and verbal presentation of scientific data to non\-technical audiences
-   Laboratory techniques in compliance with quality control and safety regulations
-   Usage of ELISA, PCR, gel\-electrophoresis, protein assays, and western blot techniques

## Make the most of your specialization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/pharmacology/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-specialization](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/pharmacology/#make-the-most-of-your-specialization)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[UBC Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/pharmacology)**  
    Gain work experience in pharmacology between study terms\.
-   **[Departmental research opportunities](https://apt.med.ubc.ca/pharmacology/faculty-and-staff/)**  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   **[Faculty of Medicine summer research](https://www.med.ubc.ca/current-learners/summer-student-research-program/)**  
    Get experience in undergraduate medical research at UBC\.
-   **[UBC Pathology and Laboratory Medicine summer program](https://pathology.ubc.ca/education-resource/summer-student-fellowship-program/)**  
    Apply for funding and propose your research project to gain clinical experience in laboratory medicine\.
-   **[BC Children’s Hospital research](https://bcchr.ca/ssrp)**  
    Participate in an undergraduate research project related to child and family health\.
-   **[Online tutorials and training in ethics](https://ethics.research.ubc.ca/education-training/online-tutorials-training)**  
    Develop your understanding of research ethics and integrity through online training and tutorials\.
-   **[UBC Life Sciences Institute](http://lsi.ubc.ca/news-events/events/)**  
    Find research opportunities, events, programs, and student competitions in life sciences\.
-   **[UBC Medical Journal](https://ubcmj.med.ubc.ca/submissions/)**  
    Submit your academic research, case reports, reviews, commentaries, and news or letters for publication\.
