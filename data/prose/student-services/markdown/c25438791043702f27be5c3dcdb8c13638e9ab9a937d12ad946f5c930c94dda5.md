## About the program[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/financial\-planning\-post\-baccalaureate/\#about\-the\-program](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/financial-planning-post-baccalaureate/#about-the-program)

Post\-baccalaureate programs include Law, Dentistry, Medicine and Education\. These programs involve significant cost and time commitments\. As a post\-baccalaureate student, it’s important to make sure you are financially prepared\.

## Student loans and bursaries[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/financial\-planning\-post\-baccalaureate/\#student\-loans\-and\-bursaries](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/financial-planning-post-baccalaureate/#student-loans-and-bursaries)

Government student loans and bursaries may be available to help supplement your financial resources\. It is important to note that these resources will not cover all of your costs for your program\. Read more about [types of loans and eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/)\.

-   Check your loan assessment for exact amounts and disbursement dates\.
-   Ensure you have met the minimum [credit load requirements](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/course-load-guide/) to maintain your loan\. 
-   Apply for your student loan by **Fri, Jul 31, 2026** to be eligible for a one\-month tuition deferral

Learn more about the [UBC bursary program](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-bursary-program/) and how to apply\.

## Scholarships and awards[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/financial\-planning\-post\-baccalaureate/\#scholarships\-and\-awards](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/financial-planning-post-baccalaureate/#scholarships-and-awards)

Scholarships and awards recognize outstanding student achievement\. You do not need to apply for most scholarships and awards, since your faculty directly recommends students\.

As a post\-baccalaureate student, you may also apply for:

-   [Affiliation scholarships](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/affiliation-scholarships/)
-   [Affiliation bursaries](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/affiliation-bursaries/)
-   [Awards for Indigenous students](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-indigenous/)
-   [Awards for students with disabilities](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/awards-disabilities/)
-   [Premier Undergraduate and Wesbrook scholarships](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/premier-undergraduate-wesbrook-scholarships/)

## Professional line of credit[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/funding\-studies/financial\-planning/financial\-planning\-post\-baccalaureate/\#professional\-line\-of\-credit](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/funding-studies/financial-planning/financial-planning-post-baccalaureate/#professional-line-of-credit)

You may want to consider a professional student line of credit to fulfil any gaps in funding\. Many financial institutions offer lines of credit and have packages specifically designed for students in professional programs\.
