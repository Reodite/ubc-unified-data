## About International Student Advising[https://students\.ubc\.ca/international\-student\-guide/international\-student\-advising/\#about\-international\-student\-advising](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/international-student-advising/#about-international-student-advising)

If you have questions about life as an international student, International Student Advising is here to help\. International Student Advisors are Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants \(RCICs\) or Regulated International Student Immigration Advisors \(RISIAs\) who can support you in your experience as a UBC international student\.

### Top reasons to connect

-   Get help with your [visa](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/temporary-resident-visa-trv/), [study permit](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/), and [work permit](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/) application\.
-   Ask about part\-time or full\-time [work requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/)\.
-   Learn about your [health insurance coverage](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/)\.
-   Talk to someone about transitioning to life in Canada\.

Advisors are not able to fully review immigration applications\. Before applying for your initial study permit, study permit extension, Temporary Resident Visa, or Post\-Graduation Work Permit, sign up for the [Canvas application review courses](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/tutorials/#online-application-review-courses) to go over your application and avoid common mistakes\.

### In\-person advising

Visit the International Student Advising office in the UBC Life Building at room 1200 of [6138 Student Union Boulevard](https://goo.gl/maps/sLxUUvBedGh5uA8eA), located on the main floor across from Starbucks\.

Before arriving at the office, you can sign in to the [virtual line](https://vue.comm100.com/joinqueue/80000265/location?locationId=0bd862af924b401d8f888a4afdb195ac) to save your spot\. You can also join the virtual line at the office by using a QR code\.

### Zoom advising

International Student Advising is available to meet virtually on Zoom\. Join the [virtual line](https://vue.comm100.com/joinqueue/80000265/location?locationId=0bd862af924b401d8f888a4afdb195ac) with your email address and Canadian or US phone number, if you have one, and you’ll receive the Zoom link when it’s your turn in line\. You will get updates about your place in the line by email and text message, if providing a Canadian or US phone number\. 

If you do not have a Canadian or US phone number, you can still join the virtual line, but you will not receive text messages with updates\.

While waiting to connect with our office, you can check your place in line, push your appointment back, leave the line, or get an update when it’s almost your turn\.

Find [step\-by\-step instructions](https://support.zoom.us/hc/en-us/articles/201362193-Joining-a-Meeting) on how to download and use Zoom\.

Zoom advising hours are as follows\. Advising is closed on statutory holidays\.

-   **Monday**: 9:30 to 11:30 am, 2:00 to 4:00 pm
-   **Tuesday**: 9:30 to 11:30 am, 2:00 to 4:00 pm
-   **Wednesday**: 2:00 to 4:00 pm
-   **Thursday**: 9:30 to 11:30 am, 2:00 to 4:00 pm
-   **Friday**: 9:30 to 11:30 am, 2:00 to 4:00 pm

While we strive to support as many students as possible, when there is high volume, we may not be able to connect with you\. If you are in the queue and there are a large number of students in front of you before Zoom advising hours end, you will be removed from the line\. We apologize for not being able to assist you at that time\. Please rejoin the queue when we re\-open or send us a [webform](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0J8VRJgJQkuO2b4)\.
