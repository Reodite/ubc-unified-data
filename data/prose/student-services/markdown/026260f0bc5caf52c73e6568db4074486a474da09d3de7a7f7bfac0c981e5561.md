## About sponsorship billing[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/sponsorship\-billing/information\-sponsors/\#about\-sponsorship\-billing](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsors/#about-sponsorship-billing)

Some UBC students have a portion of their UBC fees paid by a sponsor, such as an Indigenous community, employer, or government agency\. This is called sponsorship billing\. Sponsors must meet the required criteria and apply before the start of the term\. The sponsor should notify the Sponsorship Billing Office of any changes to the sponsoring agency, the coverage amount, or the sponsor’s contact information\.

## Eligibility criteria for sponsors[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/sponsorship\-billing/information\-sponsors/\#eligibility\-criteria\-for\-sponsors](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsors/#eligibility-criteria-for-sponsors)

You cannot sponsor a student as an individual, parent, or relative\. You must be a government agency, company, Indigenous band, or other organization\. If a sponsor or any third\-party vendor reimburses tuition and fees directly to the student, this is not sponsorship\.

Sponsors are invoiced each term for the costs they agree to and pay UBC directly for these fees\. All sponsors must submit an online [Sponsorship Billing Application](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_dg7icsLodwcOdeu) for each student they wish to sponsor or a group of students who fall under the same sponsorship criteria\. If you are unable to apply online, please fax a completed [Sponsorship Third Party Billing Form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sponsorship_third_party_billing_form_2024.pdf) \(pdf\) to the Sponsorship Billing Office\.

UBC has the right to refuse sponsorship or sponsor payments\. Please be aware that contracts or payments from a person, company, or government department can be refused due to rules and regulations enforced by the Canadian government\.

### Accepted currency

UBC can only accept payments in Canadian or US funds\. No other foreign currency is accepted\. All US funds received by UBC will be converted to Canadian funds using the daily exchange rate provided by the UBC Finance Office on the date UBC processes the payment\. UBC cannot use the exchange rate provided by the sponsor\.

Sponsors who want to avoid exchange rate discrepancies should only send funds in Canadian dollars\. UBC cannot control fluctuations in the US and Canadian currency exchange rates, which can change within the same day\.

If setting a sponsorship contract dollar limit, the sponsor can only set dollar limits in Canadian funds\. Any foreign currency limits will be converted to Canadian funds, as UBC can only assess tuition and fees in Canadian funds\. Returns can only be issued to sponsors as a cheque in Canadian funds\.

Please contact the Sponsorship Billing Office if you have any questions about foreign currencies and exchange rates before making payment\.

### Tax receipts

In keeping with Canada Revenue Agency guidelines, the sponsoring organization must issue a T4A tax receipt to their sponsored students\. UBC does not provide this document\.

## How the application process works[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/sponsorship\-billing/information\-sponsors/\#how\-the\-application\-process\-works](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsors/#how-the-application-process-works)

-   Sponsors must complete the [online application form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_dg7icsLodwcOdeu) before the start of the term\. Sponsors who are unable to complete the online application can submit a paper [Sponsorship Third Party Billing Form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sponsorship_third_party_billing_form_2024.pdf) \(pdf\) and return it by fax to [604 822 5816](tel:6048225816)\. 
-   Sponsors can apply to support a student for as short as one term or over multiple years\. The duration of the sponsorship must be specified on the application form\. Sponsors with a multi\-year contract do not need to submit a new application every term or session\.
-   Sponsors who support a student for only one term only but later determine they can continue the sponsorship are asked to submit a new application\. Contracts do not automatically renew\. 
-   If you wish to sponsor a student for books and supplies in addition to tuition and student fees, UBC can help you to arrange this\. You can indicate this on the sponsorship application form, which will be forwarded to the appropriate department on your behalf\.
-   Once the Sponsorship Billing Office receives your form, they will update your account and let you know your next steps\.

### Application deadlines

Sponsors should submit their sponsorship billing applications by the dates below\. If you’re unable to apply before the deadlines, please contact the Sponsorship Billing Office\.

| Academic Session | Dates | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summer Session | May to August | April 1 |
| Winter Session Term 1 | September to December | August 1 |
| Winter Session Term 2 | January to April | November 1 |

## After you submit your sponsorship billing request[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/sponsorship\-billing/information\-sponsors/\#after\-you\-submit\-your\-sponsorship\-billing\-request](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsors/#after-you-submit-your-sponsorship-billing-request)

Once the Sponsorship Billing Office receives your application, they will update the student’s account within 2 to 3 business days and send a confirmation email to the student when the sponsorship is added\.

The office will also send you a copy of the invoice by email after the [add/drop deadline](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates/) of each term:

-   **Winter Session Term 1**: late September
-   **Winter Session Term 2**: late January
-   **Summer Session**: mid\-July

UBC will only issue invoice\(s\) for charges you have agreed to cover\. If you wish to make a change to the terms of your sponsor agreement for one or more students, please email [ubc\.sponsorship@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.sponsorship@ubc.ca) immediately\.

## Communication with students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/tuition\-fees/sponsorship\-billing/information\-sponsors/\#communication\-with\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/sponsorship-billing/information-sponsors/#communication-with-students)

As a sponsor, you will only receive an email if your application is refused or if the Sponsorship Billing Office has questions about the sponsorship details\.

If the conditions of your sponsorship include being updated of the student’s progress, or transcripts and other documents, you will need to ask the student to send them directly to you\.
