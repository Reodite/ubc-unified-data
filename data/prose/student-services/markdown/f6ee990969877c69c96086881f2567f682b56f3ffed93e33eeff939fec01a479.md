## Learning and support[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/wellness\-resources/\#learning\-and\-support](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-resources/#learning-and-support)

[Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre/)

Connect to wellbeing resources, get answers to your questions, and pick up free wellness supplies\.

[Nurse on Campus](https://students.ubc.ca/health/student-health-service/nurse-campus/)

Visit a registered nurse during drop\-in hours and get confidential support for your questions\.

[Wellbeing workshops and training](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre/wellbeing-training-education/)

Browse self\-paced courses, training, and workshops to develop skills to support yourself and others\.

## Mental health programs or counselling[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/wellness\-resources/\#mental\-health\-programs\-or\-counselling](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-resources/#mental-health-programs-or-counselling)

[Here2Talk](https://here2talk.ca/)

Call for free, immediate, mental health counselling, available 24/7 in various languages\.

[Foundry BC counselling app](https://foundrybc.ca/)

Schedule virtual counselling appointments, find peer support, or join a youth group\.

[Hope for Wellness Helpline](https://www.hopeforwellness.ca/)

If you’re an Indigenous student, call for immediate mental health counselling or crisis support\.

[MindHealth BC](http://www.mindhealthbc.ca/)

Take an online assessment to find resources to support your mental health\.
