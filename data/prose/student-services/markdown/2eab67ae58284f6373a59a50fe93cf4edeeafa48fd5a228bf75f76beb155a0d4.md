## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/physics/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/physics/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Physics](http://www.phas.ubc.ca/undergrad-students), you are learning about the universe, from the smallest particles to the largest structures, and the properties that control the behaviour of matter\. You will develop the skills of taking basic principles and applying them to new problems or novel situations to observe what will happen\.

These skills may include:

-   Application of theoretical concepts and scientific principles to unexplained or novel situations
-   Observation and interpretation of relationships between factors
-   Usage of computer programming to create complex models or solutions
-   Application of logic, imagination, judgment, and abstract thinking to solve real\-world problems
-   Quantification of complex calculations and statistical analysis
-   Communication of complex ideas through advanced technical writing
-   Usage of a wide range of advanced instruments, machines, and lab equipment

## Make the most of your specialization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/physics/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-specialization](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/physics/#make-the-most-of-your-specialization)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[UBC Physics Society](https://www.facebook.com/ubcphyssoc/)**  
    Attend student events, seek mentorship, and meet other UBC Physics students\.
-   **[UBC Physics Olympics](https://physoly.phas.ubc.ca/)**  
    Volunteer to support local high school teams competing in a series of physics events\.
-   [UBC Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/physics)  
    Gain work experience in physics between study terms\.
-   **[Departmental research opportunities](https://www.phas.ubc.ca/researchers)**  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   **[Metro Vancouver Physics Circle](https://outreach.phas.ubc.ca/events/metro-vancouver-physics-circle/)**  
    Provide guidance to advanced high school students in physics through biweekly sessions\.
-   **[Saturday Morning Lectures](https://www.triumf.ca/saturday-lectures)**  
    Attend lectures at TRIUMF to learn about the frontiers of modern physics\.
-   **[CAP University Prize Exam](https://www.cap.ca/programs/medals-and-awards/prizes-students/university-prize-exam/)**  
    Compete against other undergraduates in the national Physics competition and win prizes in Term 2\.
-   **[UBC Physics outreach initiatives](https://www.phas.ubc.ca/outreach)**  
    Interact with elementary and high school students through workshops and sessions\.
-   **[UBC Department of Physics events](https://www.phas.ubc.ca/events)**  
    Check out departmental events, including seminars, colloquia, and lectures\.
-   **[UBC Physics resources for students](https://www.phas.ubc.ca/undergrad-students)**  
    Attend workshops and browse resources in the Physics department\.
-   **[UBC Young Women for STEM](https://ubcywfs.wordpress.com/about/)**  
    Speak at different high schools to inspire young female students to pursue a career in Physics\.
