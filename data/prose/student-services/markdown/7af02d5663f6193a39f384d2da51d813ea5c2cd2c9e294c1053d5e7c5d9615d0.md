## About the UBC Life Building[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/ubc\-life\-building/\#about\-the\-ubc\-life\-building](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-life-building/#about-the-ubc-life-building)

Located next to the AMS Nest, the UBC Life Building is a space for students to grab food, eat lunch, work out, study, or hang out with friends\. You can also access many student services and programs, or book a space for an event\.

## Hours and location[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/ubc\-life\-building/\#hours\-and\-location](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-life-building/#hours-and-location)

The UBC Life Building is located at [6138 Student Union Boulevard](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ooCYeEZxC8G1uaVz6)\.

### Opening hours

The UBC Life building is closed on all [statutory holidays](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/statutory-holidays) observed by UBC\.

-   **Monday**: 7:00 am to 10:00 pm
-   **Tuesday**: 7:00 am to 10:00 pm
-   **Wednesday**: 7:00 am to 10:00 pm
-   **Thursday**: 7:00 am to 10:00 pm
-   **Friday**: 7:00 am to 10:00 pm
-   **Saturday:** 8:00 am to 8:00 pm
-   **Sunday:** 8:00 am to 8:00 pm
