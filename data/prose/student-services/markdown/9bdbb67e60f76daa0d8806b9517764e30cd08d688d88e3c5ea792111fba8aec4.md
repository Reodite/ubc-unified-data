## About the Pentecostal Church[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/chaplains/pentecostal\-chaplain/\#about\-the\-pentecostal\-church](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/chaplains/pentecostal-chaplain/#about-the-pentecostal-church)

The Pentecostal Church is found within the mainstream of orthodox evangelical protestant faith\. As a movement, the church is just over 100 years old\. While Pentecostals emphasize the physical expression of God’s inner dwelling through spiritual and supernatural gifts, we are also known for our ministry to the fringes of society, cultural diversity, and passion for talking about God to others\.

### University Christian Ministries at UBC

At UBC, the Pentecostal ministry is made up of more than just Pentecostals\. Virtually every Christian denomination has been represented in our community at one time or another\. Through the Pentecostal Chaplaincy, we sponsor an interdenominational Christian student group on campus\. We are a community who desires to walk alongside students through the contours of university life, and we hope to be a place where students can discover what it means to integrate what you learn in the classroom with the Christian faith\.  
   
[University Christian Ministries](http://www.ucmatubc.com/) \(UCM\) at UBC is a student community passionate about Christ, excited about His transforming power at work in our lives, and open to God’s life\-changing presence through the Holy Spirit\.

## About Paul Engels[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/chaplains/pentecostal\-chaplain/\#about\-paul\-engels](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/chaplains/pentecostal-chaplain/#about-paul-engels)

[Image of Paul Engels from the Pentecostal chaplain](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/pentecostal_chaplain_paul_engels.jpg)

Paul’s life was transformed after he was in a car accident and experienced a severe traumatic brain injury when he was 18 years old\. This left him comatose for a month and a half\. His miraculous healing came through his reception of God, so now Paul lives to share the same life\-giving power of love with others\. Paul serves as campus director University Christian Ministries at UBC, by regularly providing spiritual care for students, multiplying groups which seek to grow in faith, and mobilizing missional initiatives to create peace and reconciliation on campus\.

You can contact Paul by emailing him at [paul\.engels@paoc\.org](mailto:paul.engels@paoc.org), or by texting him at [604 679 3520](tel:604%20679%203520)\.
