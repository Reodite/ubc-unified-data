If you’re a UBC Vancouver undergraduate, you can take up to 15 credits worth of courses at the Okanagan campus\. Registration and enrolment are reviewed on a case\-by\-case basis\.

UBC Vancouver graduate students can also apply for cross\-campus registration through the [Authorization and Course Registration Form](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/forms/authorization-course-registration-ubc-ubco)\. For more information, graduate students can visit the [UBC Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies’ website](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/), or contact their program staff or Graduate Advisor\.

If you’re transferring to the UBC Okanagan campus and switching to an Okanagan degree program, apply for a [change in campus or faculty](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/changing-degree-program-or-campus/) through the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) \(ASC\)\.

## Check your eligibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/cross\-campus\-registration/\#check\-your\-eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/cross-campus-registration/#check-your-eligibility)

The information below applies to **undergraduate** programs and courses\. 

To be considered for registration at the Okanagan campus, you must meet the following requirements:

-   You are registered as a UBC Vancouver undergraduate student\.
-   You have completed a minimum of **24 credits** with percentage grades\.
-   You have not failed a course in the most recent 24 credits\.
-   You have a minimum overall average of 68% on your most recent 24 credits\.
-   You have satisfied all the prerequisites for the course you’re requesting to take at UBC Okanagan\.

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration/cross\-campus\-registration/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/cross-campus-registration/#how-to-apply)

You will not be able to register for cross\-campus courses by yourself\. An Enrolment Services staff member at the Okanagan campus will help you complete your registration\.

Faculties reserve the right to decline a request if the course is in high demand, or if the equivalent course is available at the UBC Vancouver campus in either the Summer or Winter Session\.

Enrolment may be limited in some or all courses due to high demand from UBC Okanagan students\. If registration has already started, check the course schedule to see how many seats remain in the courses you are requesting\. Courses that are full or very close to full are less likely to be approved for cross campus registration\.

To apply for a cross\-campus course, follow these steps:

1.  Download and complete the [UBC Vancouver cross\-campus request form](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/cross_campus_registration_request_form.pdf) \(pdf\)\. If you are a **graduate** student, please complete the [Authorization and Course Registration Form](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/forms/authorization-course-registration-ubc-ubco) instead\.
2.  Submit the request form to your [academic advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) for approval\.
3.  Your academic advisor will then send the form to Enrolment Services staff at the UBC Okanagan campus, and it will be forwarded to the appropriate faculty at the Okanagan campus for review\.
4.  You will receive an email notifying you if your request has been approved\. Cross\-campus registration is typically scheduled on the Visiting students registration date for **Summer and Winter**, respectively\. You will receive a registration token and an email notifying you that the token has been granted\. You can then register in the approved course on Workday, if there is space\.
5.  You will receive an email with your registration outcome for your requested course\(s\)\.

If you are unable to download and submit the form by email, you can print and complete it by hand and bring it to your [academic advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) in person\.
