The [UBC Life Building](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-life-building/) is a central and vibrant hub to study, work out, grab a bite to eat, and access student services and programs\. Additionally, there are many informal spaces for students to spend time catching up with friends\. 

Students and staff can also book several areas on the **main floor** for social events and student\-directed programming\.

## Booking eligibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/ubc\-life\-building/ubc\-life\-building\-bookings/\#booking\-eligibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-life-building/ubc-life-building-bookings/#booking-eligibility)

Bookings are only available to UBC students with an active student card or UBC staff and faculty members hosting student\-facing programming\.

The UBC Life Building Operations team reserves the right to approve booking requests based on what best balances and supports the vision and goals of the building\.

To meet the UBC Life Building programming requirements, your programming or event must fulfill one of the requirements below:

-   Helps students connect, build community, and contribute to a vibrant, inclusive campus culture  
    
-   Supports student wellbeing and resilience through learning and engagement  
    
-   Creates a welcoming and engaging space for both students and visitors  
    
-   Makes good use of the spaces to reach as many students as possible, in areas of interests such as wellness, academics, physical activity, arts, and social events\.

## Booking policies[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/ubc\-life\-building/ubc\-life\-building\-bookings/\#booking\-policies](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-life-building/ubc-life-building-bookings/#booking-policies)

Policies and fees are different depending on the booking group\. Review your group’s policies below\.

**

UBC students

**

-   Open area spaces are bookable from Monday to Thursday during [building hours](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-life-building/#hours-and-location)\. 
-   Each group may make a maximum of 5 bookings per term\.
-   Events booked in the meeting room are free of charge\.
-   Events of over 50 people in the Conversation Pit and Concourse are subject to cleaning fees\.

**

UBC staff and faculty

**

-   Open area spaces can be booked from Monday to Friday during [building hours](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-life-building/#hours-and-location)\.
-   Events booked in the meeting room are free of charge but may be subject to cleaning fees for damage to the room and/or multi\-day events\.
-   Events of over 50 people in the Conversation Pit and Concourse are subject to cleaning fees\.

## How to book[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/ubc\-life\-building/ubc\-life\-building\-bookings/\#how\-to\-book](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-life-building/ubc-life-building-bookings/#how-to-book)

Before you start, make sure you meet the [booking timeline](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-life-building/ubc-life-building-bookings/#booking-timelines) for the space you’d like to book\. 

1.  **Start the booking**  
    Email [csdl\.bookings@ubc\.ca](mailto:csdl.bookings@ubc.ca) to check the space availability for your date\(s\)\.
2.  **Fill out the booking survey**  
    You’ll receive a survey asking for your event date and time, preferred space, and additional event details\.
3.  **Monitor your email for a follow\-up**  
    You may be contacted to provide more information about your program or event\. Once your information is accepted, you’ll receive an email to confirm or decline your booking\.
