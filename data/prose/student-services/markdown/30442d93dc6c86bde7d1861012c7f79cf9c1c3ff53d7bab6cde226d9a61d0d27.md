UBC is committed to fostering a respectful environment for students, staff, and faculty\. Campus is also a space for inclusive events, dialogue, respectful debate, and engagement on important issues regarding our community and the world\.

If you’re organizing an event that’s affiliated with UBC, you must follow important policies and guidelines\.
