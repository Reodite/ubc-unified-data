Each year, UBC welcomes thousands of new students to campus\. Become a student leader and guide them through orientation programs\. These programs allow students to meet friends, learn about their academic experience, and connect with professors from their faculty\.

## What you can expect[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/ubc\-orientation\-transition\-leadership/\#what\-you\-can\-expect](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/#what-you-can-expect)

-   Learn about building respectful and inclusive communities\.
-   Develop leadership, facilitation, and teamwork skills\.
-   Meet other students in your faculty or school\.
-   Find out about involvement opportunities and resources in the UBC community\.

## Apply for a position[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/ubc\-orientation\-transition\-leadership/\#apply\-for\-a\-position](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/#apply-for-a-position)

Get involved in leadership positions and support students in their transition to UBC\.

**[Imagine UBC Orientation Leaders](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/imagine-ubc-orientation-leaders/)**

Welcome new students on Imagine Day and help them feel supported in their first year experience\.

**[Imagine UBC Senior Orientation Leaders](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/imagine-ubc-senior-orientation-leaders/)**

Lead a team of Orientation Leaders and build community on the first day of school and beyond\.

[Jump Start and Collegia Leader opportunities](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/jump-start-collegia-leader/)

Apply to be a student leader in the first\-year experience programs, Jump Start and Collegia\.
