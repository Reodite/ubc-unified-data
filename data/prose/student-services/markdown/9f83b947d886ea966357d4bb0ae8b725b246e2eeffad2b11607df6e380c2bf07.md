Find health insurance options and explore healthcare, mental health, and family wellbeing resources available to UBC students and their dependents in British Columbia\.

## Health Insurance for dependents[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/equity\-diversity\-inclusion\-resources/resources\-who\-are\-parents/family\-health\-for\-students\-with\-children/\#health\-insurance\-for\-dependents](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/resources-who-are-parents/family-health-for-students-with-children/#health-insurance-for-dependents)

If you are a UBC student with children, you may be able to add them to your health insurance coverage\. Learn how to enrol family members in UBC student health plans and BC Medical Services Plan \(MSP\) coverage\.

[Overview of health insurance](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/)

Learn about the main health insurance programs in British Columbia and at UBC\.

[AMS/GSS Health and Dental Plan](https://www.studentcare.ca/rte/en/UniversityofBritishColumbiaAMSGSS_ChangeofCoverage_CoupleFamilyEnrolments)

UBC students can enrol eligible dependents on their plan for an additional fee\.

[BC Medical Services Plan \(MSP\)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/health-drug-coverage/msp/bc-residents/managing-your-msp-account/adding-and-removing-dependents)

Learn about eligibility requirements and how to add dependent family members to your plan\.

[iMED coverage](https://imed.david-cummings.com/enrolment/add-family-members/) 

International students can purchase iMED coverage for eligible dependent family members\.

[Vancouver Coastal Health parenting resources](https://www.vch.ca/en/parenting-resources)

Explore resources related to pregnancy, childbirth and new parenthood\.

[Medimap](https://medimap.ca/)

Search for a clinic off campus and book with a health or wellness practitioner\.

[Foundry](https://foundrybc.ca/)

Access free services for youth aged 15 to 24, including STI testing, birth control, and wellness counselling\.

[Health Link BC](https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/)

Receive free health information and advice\. Call [811](tel:811) for personalized assistance from trained providers\.

[https://www\.healthlinkbc\.ca/](https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/)[MindMapBC](https://www.mindmapbc.ca/)

## Vaccinations or immunizations[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/equity\-diversity\-inclusion\-resources/resources\-who\-are\-parents/family\-health\-for\-students\-with\-children/\#vaccinations\-or\-immunizations](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/resources-who-are-parents/family-health-for-students-with-children/#vaccinations-or-immunizations)

Vaccinations protect people of all ages from harmful bacteria and viruses\. Doctors, pharmacists and nurses provide vaccinations at local public and private health facilities\. 

Be sure to keep complete records of immunizations for you and your family\. An immunization record is usually requested when your child is registered to enter day care, preschool, kindergarten, school and certain programs in post\-secondary institutions\. 

Learn more about [immunizations](https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/health-library/immunizations) in British Columbia and how to access your records\.

## Mental health and counselling[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/equity\-diversity\-inclusion\-resources/resources\-who\-are\-parents/family\-health\-for\-students\-with\-children/\#mental\-health\-and\-counselling](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/resources-who-are-parents/family-health-for-students-with-children/#mental-health-and-counselling)

On\-campus programs, including [Counselling Services](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/) and the [Indigenous Mental Health and Wellbeing program](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/indigenous-mental-health-wellbeing-program/) are available and free for UBC students\.

Beyond UBC services, UBC students and their dependents registered in the AMS/GSS Health and Dental Plan are eligible for coverage for appointments with a private mental health practitioner, including those that specialize in relationships or family therapy\. Learn how to [find a private counsellor](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/finding-private-counsellor/)\.

You can also connect with a healthcare provider for off\-campus resources\.
