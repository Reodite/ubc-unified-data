## Information for first year students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/orientations/imagine\-ubc/\#information\-for\-first\-year\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/orientations/imagine-ubc/#information-for-first-year-students)

Imagine Day is on **Tues, Sep 8, 2026**\.

For most students, Imagine UBC replaces the first day of classes\. If you’re a first year undergraduate student, you will have the chance to meet great people on campus, including your Dean, some of your future professors, and other students\. It will be an opportunity to learn about your new academic environment and the specific expectations of your faculty\. You’ll also tour campus in a small group led by student leaders\.

You will receive your personalized Imagine UBC schedule by email in late August\. Make the most of this day and find out what awaits you at UBC\. Be sure to check if your email address is up to date in [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/), and [update your contact information](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/updating-your-contact-information/) if needed\.

## Transfer students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/orientations/imagine\-ubc/\#transfer\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/orientations/imagine-ubc/#transfer-students)

If you are coming to UBC from another university or college, Imagine UBC is also for you\!

By participating in Imagine, you’ll be able to make little connections all day long that will help kick start your UBC experience\.  
  
You will receive your personalized Imagine UBC schedule by email in late August\. Make the most of this day and find out what awaits you at UBC\. Be sure to check if your email address is up to date in [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/), and [update your contact information](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/updating-your-contact-information/) if needed\.

## Returning students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/orientations/imagine\-ubc/\#returning\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/orientations/imagine-ubc/#returning-students)

Imagine UBC is an event that most UBC students don’t forget\. All students are invited to participate in the day’s activities\. 

By attending exciting events, you’ll be able to reconnect with the community, show your school spirit, and find opportunities to get involved\.

Details about how you can participate in Imagine UBC will be emailed to you by late August\. Check if your email address is up to date in [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/), and [update your contact information](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/updating-your-contact-information/) if needed\.

## If you’re a graduate student[https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/orientations/imagine\-ubc/\#if\-youre\-a\-graduate\-student](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/orientations/imagine-ubc/#if-youre-a-graduate-student)

You’re invited to the Campus Life Expo on Main Mall during Imagine Day\! It’s a great opportunity to discover campus resources, connect with services, and learn about the support available to you as a graduate student\.

Check out upcoming [Graduate Student Orientation events](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/events/)\.

## Notice about accessibility[https://students\.ubc\.ca/new\-to\-ubc/orientations/imagine\-ubc/\#notice\-about\-accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/orientations/imagine-ubc/#notice-about-accessibility)

If you have a disability or medical condition that may affect your participation in Imagine Day, please check out the [Imagine Day Accessibility FAQ](https://orientations.students.ubc.ca/accessibility-faq)\. If you have any questions after looking through the FAQ, please reach out to us at [orientations\.info@ubc\.ca](mailto:orientations.info@ubc.ca) as soon as possible so that arrangements can be made to ensure full participation\.
