## Part\-time funding coverage[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/part\-time\-funding\-british\-columbia/\#part\-time\-funding\-coverage](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/part-time-funding-british-columbia/#part-time-funding-coverage)

The part\-time student loan program covers:

-   Tuition
-   Books
-   Supplies
-   Transportation
-   Child care \(up to the funding maximums\)

Part\-time funding does not cover living costs\. If you need additional financial support for living costs, please contact your [Enrolment Services Advisors](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to discuss your financial plan\.

## Eligibility to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/part\-time\-funding\-british\-columbia/\#eligibility\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/part-time-funding-british-columbia/#eligibility-to-apply)

Loans are available for students studying part\-time at UBC\. To be considered a part\-time student, you must be registered in 20% to 59% of the full\-time load listed for your program, which you can check on the [Course Load Guide](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/course-load-guide/)\.

Not all programs are eligible for part\-time funding\. You must be in a program approved for full\-time student loans\. Contact your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to check if your program is approved\. If you don’t have an assigned advisor, they’re still available to help\.

To apply for part\-time student financial assistance from StudentAid BC, you must be a resident of British Columbia \(BC\)\. 

You are considered a resident of BC if you meet one of the following requirements:

-   You lived in BC for 12 continuous months before starting your studies, excluding any time spent in full\-time post\-secondary education\.  
     
-   You are an independent permanent resident or designated as a protected person living in BC at the time of your application, you will spend your study period in BC, and you have not lived in any province for 12 continuous months before your study start date\.  
      
    For example, if you are an independent student who arrived in Canada as a permanent resident less than 12 months before the start date of classes and intend to study in BC, you would be considered a resident of BC for student financial assistance purposes\.

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/part\-time\-funding\-british\-columbia/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/part-time-funding-british-columbia/#how-to-apply)

You should apply for a loan at least 2 months before classes start to receive your funding at the beginning of the term, or up to 6 weeks before the end of your study period\. 

To apply for BC part\-time funding, follow the steps below:

1.  Set up your [B\.C\. Services Card](https://id.gov.bc.ca/account/) account and app if you have not already done so, and log into [StudentAid BC](https://studentaidbc.ca/sabc-home-page) for your online application\.
2.  Determine your [course load](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/course-load-guide/) and loan\-eligible study dates\. If you’re unsure, speak to your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\.  
      
    If you are a student with a Permanent Disability \(PD\) or Persistent or Prolonged Disability \(PPD\) and you are taking a 40% course load or higher, you are encouraged to apply for a [full\-time loan](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/loans-full-time-british-columbia/), as a part\-time loan does not cover living costs\.
3.  Find your program and study dates from the application through [StudentAid BC](https://studentaidbc.ca/sabc-home-page)\.  
      
    If you cannot locate your program or study dates, check the box indicating that your program or study dates are not listed, and proceed until you submit your application\. This will trigger a Program Information Request \(PIR\) to UBC to confirm your program details before your application can be further processed after your submission\.
4.  Submit your application\. You will be asked to provide your consent to the Canada Revenue Agency \(CRA\) and complete the StudentAid BC Declaration\. This allows StudentAid BC to verify the information in your application\.
5.  Monitor your online application for next steps\. Once your application has been assessed, you will be able to review your Notice of Assessment, which describes:
    -   The amount of funding approved
    -   When you will receive the funding
6.  Submit the [Master Student Financial Assistance Agreement](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/msfaa.html) \(MSFAA\) and accept your Notice of Assessment **as soon as possible**\. Both are required before your enrolment can be confirmed\.  
      
    You are required to complete an online MSFAA if you are applying for a part\-time student loan for the first time or have taken a leave from your studies for 2 or more years\.
7.  UBC will be prompted to confirm your enrolment by StudentAid BC\.
8.  Continue to monitor your application status and inbox for notifications from StudentAid BC and UBC\. You are strongly encouraged to log into your [StudentAid BC](https://studentaidbc.ca/dashboard/login) account to view updates, changes to your assessment if applicable, and communications from the provincial lender\.  
      
    UBC will contact you by email if your enrolment cannot be confirmed\.
