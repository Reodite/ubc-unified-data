## About group counselling[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/\#about\-group\-counselling](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/#about-group-counselling)

If you are dealing with concerns like depression, anxiety, or loneliness, a healthcare professional may recommend group counselling\. UBC Student Health and Wellbeing offers groups throughout the year for specific concerns\.

Get more information about Student Health and Wellbeing and the [range of options](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/) to support your mental health\.

[Several groups of students meeting on the benches in front of the AMS Nest\.](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/group_counselling_programs.jpeg)

### Benefits of group counselling

Group counselling is as effective as individual counselling for concerns such as stress, anxiety, depression, and relationship concerns\. It can also benefit you in different ways\.

-   **Connect with other students**  
    You are not alone\. The group experience can help you connect with other students experiencing similar challenges\. The group environment seeks to be encouraging, supportive, and safe\.
-   **Learn new skills**  
    Groups offer opportunities for you to realize your strengths and gather new skills to try out\. Most groups are offered once a week\.
-   **Get “unstuck”**  
    Group therapy can offer a different perspective on your situation and a sense of accountability which can help motivate you to try new strategies\.
-   **Build social confidence**  
    It is common for students to feel nervous before coming to their first group\. The groups are clear about the purpose of the group and what will happen during the session\. You can share as much or as little as you like in the group\. The supportive environment can provide an opportunity to practice new skills to help reduce anxiety in a social setting\.  
-   **Easy access**  
    Groups are offered on various days and times throughout the academic year\. Online registration is available for drop\-in groups\.

Group counselling programs are available throughout the year for UBC Vancouver students\. Sessions are staff\-facilitated, confidential, and offer an opportunity to connect with peers who may be facing similar challenges\.

Student Health and Wellbeing recognizes the unique individual needs of each student\. For programs that require a referral, your clinician will discuss alternative options if group programming is not an appropriate referral or fit\.

## Other UBC support groups[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc/group\-counselling\-programs/\#other\-ubc\-support\-groups](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc/group-counselling-programs/#other-ubc-support-groups)

There are a variety of groups and meetings on campus which offer a sense of community and support for mental health and wellness related concerns\.

-   **[Kaleidoscope](https://the-kaleidoscope.com/)**  
    The Kaleidoscope offers weekly peer led support groups for undergraduate and graduate students living with the experience of mental health concerns\.
-   **[UBC Student Recovery Community \(SRC\)](https://students.ubc.ca/health/substance-use-health-harm-reduction/ubc-student-recovery-community/)**  
    For students who are in recovery, or curious to explore their relationship with alcohol, drugs, and/or addictive behaviours such as disordered eating, the SRC offers peer led support meetings\.
-   **[UBC Sexual Assault Support Centre \(SASC\)](https://www.amssasc.ca/support/support-services-available/)**  
    SASC Support and Advocacy staff facilitate support groups for people of all genders and identities who have experienced any form of sexualized violence\. These groups are open to all members of the UBC community\.
