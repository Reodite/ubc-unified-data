## About the bursary[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/ubc\-vancouver\-technology\-bursary/\#about\-the\-bursary](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-vancouver-technology-bursary/#about-the-bursary)

The UBC Vancouver Technology Bursary is a one\-time needs\-based bursary\. It is intended for students who find themselves in financial distress and cannot meet their basic technology requirements, such as headphones, web cameras, specialized accessibility technology, or Internet access\.

## Eligibility requirements[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/ubc\-vancouver\-technology\-bursary/\#eligibility\-requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-vancouver-technology-bursary/#eligibility-requirements)

-   You must be enrolled and registered in an undergraduate, graduate, or post\-baccalaureate degree program at the UBC Vancouver campus\.
-   You must demonstrate why other publicly available technology resources on the UBC Vancouver campus \(e\.g\., library, computer lab\) are insufficient\.
-   You must demonstrate you have financial need due to unforeseen circumstances, and that you do not have access to any alternative funding to cover the costs requested through this bursary\.
-   You must outline how the technology is needed for you to engage in and complete UBC coursework\.
-   You must confirm how you otherwise planned to cover the cost of the item\(s\) being requested through this bursary\.

## What’s not included in the funding[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/ubc\-vancouver\-technology\-bursary/\#whats\-not\-included\-in\-the\-funding](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-vancouver-technology-bursary/#whats-not-included-in-the-funding)

This funding is not intended for the following:

-   Improvements to physical workspaces such as ergonomic keyboards, desks, or chairs
-   Upgrades to older technology
-   Supplemental technological needs for employment

Employers should provide necessary resources for staff to perform their work\. Students are also expected to provide the technology required for their program of study, such as a laptop or special software\. Learn more about [technology requirements](https://lthub.ubc.ca/student-support/tech-requirements/) that students should plan for\.

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/ubc\-vancouver\-technology\-bursary/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-vancouver-technology-bursary/#how-to-apply)

There are 2 ways to apply for the technology bursary\. Choose the option that works best for you\.

### Option 1: Contact an Enrolment Services Advisor

If you’re an undergraduate student, contact your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to discuss your situation\.

If you’re a graduate or post\-baccalaureate degree student, you can call 604 822 9836, visit [Brock Hall](http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?locat1=112-1), or submit an [online form](https://www.askme.ubc.ca/form/s/)\.

If you’re an Indigenous student, please contact your assigned ES Advisor or reach out to the Indigenous Student Support team by emailing es\.indigenous@ubc\.ca\.

### Option 2: Submit an online application

You can also [complete an online application](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2lwwp4hKZ09IPrL) for funding\.

Your application will be reviewed by an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) and you may be contacted by email or phone if more information is required\. If you are an undergraduate student, please note that your application will be reviewed by your assigned Enrolment Services Advisor\.

## Bursary verifications[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/awards\-scholarships\-bursaries/ubc\-vancouver\-technology\-bursary/\#bursary\-verifications](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/ubc-vancouver-technology-bursary/#bursary-verifications)

All UBC bursary support is subject to financial review and verification\. Please keep all your relevant receipts and documents for 18 months after applying\. 

If you fail to provide the requested documents in a timely manner, your funding may be delayed, cancelled, or reassessed\.
