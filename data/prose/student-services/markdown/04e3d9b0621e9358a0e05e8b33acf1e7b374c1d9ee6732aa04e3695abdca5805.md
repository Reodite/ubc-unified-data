## About Every Nation[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/chaplains/every\-nation\-chaplain/\#about\-every\-nation](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/chaplains/every-nation-chaplain/#about-every-nation)

Every Nation is a global family of churches that exist to honour God by establishing Christ\-centered, Spirit\-empowered, socially responsible churches and campus ministries in every nation\. It lands within the orthodox evangelical protestant faith tradition\. The foundational values are lordship, evangelism, discipleship, leadership, and family\.

## Every Nation Campus UBC[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/chaplains/every\-nation\-chaplain/\#every\-nation\-campus\-ubc](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/chaplains/every-nation-chaplain/#every-nation-campus-ubc)

Every Nation Campus \(ENC\) UBC is a church\-based campus ministry passionate about helping students grow in their relationships with God, one another, and the world\. They do this by meeting in large group settings to build a sense of community, and in small groups to pursue truth, love, and a deeper connection with God and each other, together\. As a group, they do their best to help students connect with their community on campus, get connected to a local church family, and create spaces where students can navigate the big questions of life from a Christian perspective, such as:

-   What is the meaning of life?
-   Does God exist?
-   Do all religions lead to God?
-   Why is there evil?
-   Is there life after death?

## About Tyler Mitchell[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/chaplains/every\-nation\-chaplain/\#about\-tyler\-mitchell](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/chaplains/every-nation-chaplain/#about-tyler-mitchell)

[Profile of Tyler Mitchell from Every Nation Chaplain](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/every_nation_chaplain_tyler_mitchell.png)

During his undergrad, Tyler came face to face with some big existential questions, such as who am I? Who is God? How can I be loved? What is my purpose? It was a critical time for his relationship  with God and Christian maturity\. It was during those years he decided to take his faith seriously and was discipled by older Christian mentors\. His university years were formative, which inspired him to create spaces for others to do the hard but fulfilling work of spiritual formation\. He considers it his greatest privilege to walk alongside students on their journeys of faith\.

As an Every Nation Chaplain, Tyler walks with students who are wanting to investigate existential and faith\-centered questions, eager to learn how to read the Bible contextually, and curious to discover what apprenticeship under the person of Jesus looks like\. 

Additionally, he is passionate about creating spaces for students to engage with each other as they pursue spiritual formation together\.

Born and raised in Vancouver, Tyler studied at Langara College and the University of British Columbia\. He now holds a Bachelor of Arts majoring in Psychology from UBC\. He is currently serving as a full\-time campus minister with Every Nation Campus and gratefully spends a majority of his time at UBC\.

You can contact Tyler by emailing [tyler\.mitchell@envan\.ca](mailto:tyler.mitchell@envan.ca)\.

## Services and events[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/religion\-spirituality/chaplains/every\-nation\-chaplain/\#services\-and\-events](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/chaplains/every-nation-chaplain/#services-and-events)

-   **Campus hangouts**  
    Join the Every Nation Campus community in the Life Building to hang out, meet students, and play games together\. Regardless of where you’re at on your faith journey, this is a space to simply connect\. Come meet some lovely people\!
-   **Engage groups**  
    Our Engage groups are places for students to explore deeper questions of faith and life in a small group with other students\. We get to know each other as friends and build deeper relationships, and we open up the Bible together to see how it can help us know God and grow as loving people in all aspects of life\. Come get connected\!
-   **Monthly ENC events**  
    Join the Every Nation Campus community on the last Friday of each month for a special event\. These are spaces for students to gather to enjoy good times and build connections\. There’s often food, games or some fun activity, and always great people\. You don’t wanna miss it\! 
-   **Sunday Gathering with Every Nation Church Vancouver**  
    Every Nation Vancouver \(ENV\) rents their space from Langara College\. Visit Room A130 at 11 am for their weekly Sunday Service\.
