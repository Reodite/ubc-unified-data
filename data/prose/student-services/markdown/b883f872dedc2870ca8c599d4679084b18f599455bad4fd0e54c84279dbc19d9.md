## About the shop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/sexual\-health/wellness\-centre\-sexual\-health\-shop/\#about\-the\-shop](https://students.ubc.ca/health/sexual-health/wellness-centre-sexual-health-shop/#about-the-shop)

Since 2006, the Wellness Centre Sexual Health Shop \(or the “Shop”\) has aimed to be a sex\-positive, inclusive, welcoming, and safer space for all members of the UBC community\. The Shop exists to provide sexual health information for all UBC students and increase access to sexual health and pleasure products\.

Drop by the [Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-resources/wellness-centre/) to browse the Shop and make a purchase in person or [place an order online](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_9no9nGaL5UheBlY)\. Peer Health Educators; trained UBC student volunteers, or other trained Wellness Centre student staff, will be available to answer questions and to share resources\.

## Available products[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/sexual\-health/wellness\-centre\-sexual\-health\-shop/\#available\-products](https://students.ubc.ca/health/sexual-health/wellness-centre-sexual-health-shop/#available-products)

The Shop offers products made of body\-safe materials, and all sexual health and sex toys are compatible with water\-based lubricant\. Products are specifically chosen to be gender inclusive, shame\-free, pleasure\-focused, and to celebrate the diversity of sexual experiences and identities at UBC\.

Prices are listed in Canadian dollars\. All purchases are **cash only** and returns are not accepted\.

The Shop currently offers sexual health products, such as external condoms, lubricants, menstrual products, and pregnancy tests, as well as a variety sex toys, including vibrators, masturbation sleeves and eggs, c\-rings, and anal plugs and beads\.

Check out the [cleaning guides](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sexual_health_shop_cleaning_guide.pdf) \(pdf\) for tips on how to safely use, clean, and store products\.

[Browse all products \(pdf\)](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sexual_health_shop_products.pdf)

## How to place an order[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/sexual\-health/wellness\-centre\-sexual\-health\-shop/\#how\-to\-place\-an\-order](https://students.ubc.ca/health/sexual-health/wellness-centre-sexual-health-shop/#how-to-place-an-order)

There are 2 ways you can purchase products from the Shop: 

-   Visit the Shop at the [UBC Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-resources/wellness-centre/) during open hours\. 
-   Place an order [through the online form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_9no9nGaL5UheBlY) and pick up in person at the [UBC Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-resources/wellness-centre/)\. You will receive an email notification for pick up in 2 business days\.
