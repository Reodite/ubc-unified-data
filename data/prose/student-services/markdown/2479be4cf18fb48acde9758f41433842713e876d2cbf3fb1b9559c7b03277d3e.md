This page is for Bachelor of Science students specializing in Cognitive Systems\.  If you are a Bachelor of Arts student, visit [Your degree in Cognitive Systems](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/cognitive-systems/)\.

## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/cognitive\-systems/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/cognitive-systems/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Cognitive Systems](https://cogsys.ubc.ca/) programs, you’re learning the principles and approaches used by both natural and artificial intelligence systems\. You’ll develop important skills and investigate issues related to the study of the mind and the interfaces between different forms of intelligent agents\.

These skills may include:

-   Application of principles and techniques used by natural and artificial intelligent systems
-   Research, collection, synthesis, and presentation of information across diverse subjects
-   Evaluation of the connections between computer science, linguistics, philosophy, and psychology, and relating them to real\-world applications
-   Observation of issues through diverse perspectives grounded in theories
-   Critical thinking and ability to debate different sides of an argument while considering philosophical and ethical implications
-   Collaboration and teamwork with individuals from different backgrounds
-   Design of new intelligent systems or interfaces, through digital or machine touchpoints

## Make the most of your specialization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/cognitive\-systems/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-specialization](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/cognitive-systems/#make-the-most-of-your-specialization)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[UBC Cognitive Systems Society](http://www.ubccogssoc.ca/)**  
    Meet students interested in neuroscience, artificial intelligence, human\-computer interfaces, and more\.
-   **[UBC Neuroscience Club](https://ubcneuroscienceclub.wixsite.com/uncweb)**  
    Learn about neuroscience research through weekly journal club meetings, student\-led seminars, and an annual conference\.
-   **[UBC Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/cogs)**  
    Gain work experience in cognitive systems between study terms\.
-   **[Departmental research opportunities](https://psych.ubc.ca/people/?type=faculty)**  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   **[UBC Engineering Design Team](http://teams.engineering.ubc.ca/the-teams/robotics-teams/)**  
    Get hands\-on experience in designing robots or automation and artificial intelligence systems\.
-   **[https://innovation\.ubc\.ca/](https://innovation.ubc.ca/)[Innovation UBC](https://innovation.ubc.ca/)**  
    Get the resources and mentorship support you need to take your startup idea to the next level\.
-   **[UBC Turing Club](https://www.instagram.com/ubcturing/)**  
    Learn how to understand and work with artificial intelligence and machine learning – no coding experience required\.
-   **[Centre for Artificial Intelligence Decision\-making and Action](https://caida.ubc.ca/)**  
    Volunteer your time in a research role focused on the development, analysis, and application of AI systems\.
-   **[\#BCTECHSummit](https://www.bctechsummit.ca/)**  
    Attend the conference, do some industry networking, or join an innovation challenge in term 2\.
-   **[UBC Life Sciences Institute](https://lsi.ubc.ca/)**  
    Browse research opportunities, events, programs and student competitions in life sciences\.
-   **[UBC Undergraduate Research Opportunities club](https://www.uroubc.com/)**  
    Get a mentor, learn how to get into research, or receive help on publishing your manuscript\.
-   **[UBC Computer Science Student Society](https://ubccsss.org/)**  
    Find volunteer opportunities, socialize with your peers, and network with industry members at events\.
-   **[UBC Speech and Linguistics Student Association](https://ubcsalsa.wordpress.com/)**  
    Access peer tutoring services, attend career panel talks or social events, and learn about volunteer opportunities\.
-   **[UBC Philosophy Students’ Association](https://psa.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about/)**  
    Attend guest lectures, department dinners, and the annual undergraduate conference\.
