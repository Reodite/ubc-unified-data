## Funding options for US students[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/alternative\-sources\-funding\-us/\#funding\-options\-for\-us\-students](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/alternative-sources-funding-us/#funding-options-for-us-students)

Beyond Direct Loans, UBC offers a range of funding options for US students, including: 

-   Americorps
-   State funding
-   Qualified Tuition Plans \(529 Plans\)
-   Veterans Affairs

## Americorps[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/alternative\-sources\-funding\-us/\#americorps](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/alternative-sources-funding-us/#americorps)

Students who have completed voluntary activities for AmeriCorps may be eligible for an Education Award\. UBC is a qualified institution for certifying Americorps Education Awards\.

To determine your eligibility for educational benefits or request disbursements \(payments\) from your education awards, please log into your [My Americorps account](https://my.americorps.gov/) or call Americorps directly at [1 800 942 2677](tel:18009422677)\.

### Using your benefits at UBC

Once you have applied for your AmeriCorps benefits through your [My AmeriCorps account](https://my.americorps.gov/mp/login.do), UBC will receive an email from AmeriCorps to complete a Cost of Attendance for you and confirm your enrolment\.

AmeriCorps funds are sent directly to UBC and are applied to your tuition account\. If you have a Canadian bank account, consider setting up [direct deposit](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/finances/setting-up-your-direct-deposit/)\. Direct deposit allows any funds remaining after tuition and student fees are paid to be deposited directly into your bank account\.

## State Funding[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/alternative\-sources\-funding\-us/\#state\-funding](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/alternative-sources-funding-us/#state-funding)

UBC can provide you with a Cost of Attendance letter and proof of enrolment for a number of state\-funded plans, including:

-   Alaska Education Loans
-   Rhode Island Higher Education Assistance Authority \(RIHEAA\)
-   Vermont Student Assistance Corporation \(VSAC\)

For RIHEAA and VSAC state funding, email [usloans\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:usloans.inquiry@ubc.ca) to confirm that your electronic loan or enrolment request has been received\. Be sure to provide your name and UBC student number\.

For Alaska Education Loans, visit [Enrolment Services](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to get your paperwork signed\. 

If you’re eligible for student loans or funding from a state not listed above, email [usloans\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:usloans.inquiry@ubc.ca) with your name and UBC student number\.

## Qualified Tuition Plans \(529 Plans\)[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/alternative\-sources\-funding\-us/\#qualified\-tuition\-plans\-529\-plans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/alternative-sources-funding-us/#qualified-tuition-plans-529-plans)

UBC is an eligible institution for the use of 529 Plans\. Contact your 529 Plan provider for information on how to withdraw and use funds\. 

Funds are usually provided via a USD cheque\. Depending on the provider, you may choose to have funds sent to you, or directly to UBC\. If possible, request that your plan provider send your funds to you\.

 If your provider requires a Cost of Attendance letter, please contact [usloans\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:usloans.inquiry@ubc.ca)\. 

Please ask your plan provider to include your full name and UBC student number on the cheque**\.**

## Veterans Affairs[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/funding\-us/alternative\-sources\-funding\-us/\#veterans\-affairs](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/funding-us/alternative-sources-funding-us/#veterans-affairs)

The Veterans Affairs program is under review until further notice\. If you were previously approved under Veterans Affairs and are actively registered at UBC, your benefits will be maintained\.

If you are a new applicant, please email [usloans\.inquiry@ubc\.ca](mailto:usloans.inquiry@ubc.ca) with your Certificate of Eligibility and intended program of study, and we will review your application\.
