## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/arts/health\-and\-society/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/arts/health-and-society/#skills-youll-develop)

The [Health and Society \(HESO\) Minor](https://heso.arts.ubc.ca/) offers a multidisciplinary exploration of issues related to health, illness, and society\. As a HESO student, you will tailor your minor to your interests and goals by choosing from approximately 60 courses in social sciences, humanities, arts, population and public health, and more\. Given this flexibility, you will develop a unique course list and set of skills\.

These skills may include:

-   Applying theory to understand sociocultural determinants of health
-   Understanding the ways in which representations of health and disease have changed over time
-   Describing the ways in which cultural representations of health and illness reflect or frame societal understandings of disease and health care
-   Creating interdisciplinary connections and gaining broader perspectives on specific aspects of health, such as historical, social, and psychological perspectives of drug use, and more
-   Understanding how the structure of health care systems impact health in Canada and beyond
-   Describing factors influencing health status and health behaviours in Canada
