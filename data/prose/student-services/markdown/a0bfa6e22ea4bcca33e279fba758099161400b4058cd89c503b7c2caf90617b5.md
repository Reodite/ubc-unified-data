Getting involved is a great way to build connections and make the most of your university experience\. 

Explore opportunities to develop new skills, learn more about your interests and career goals, and see the positive impact you can make in the community\.

## Community involvement[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/\#community\-involvement](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/#community-involvement)

[AMS Clubs](https://amscampusbase.ubc.ca/login_only?redirect=https%3a%2f%2famscampusbase.ubc.ca%2fgroups)

Discover over 350 student clubs available to students, help plan events, and meet new friends\.

**[Apply for a project grant](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/funding-project/)**

Find funding opportunities to transform your ideas into action\.

**[Community experiences](https://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences/)**

Get involved in both on\- and off\-campus opportunities to learn about and create social change\. 

## Leadership and peer support[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/\#leadership\-and\-peer\-support](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/#leadership-and-peer-support)

[AMS Leadership](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/about-us/leadership/)

Run for AMS leadership positions to support UBC students throughout their university experience\.

**[First Year Experience and Orientations](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/ubc-orientation-transition-leadership/)**

Support new students in their transition to UBC at the start of the school year\.

[Peer programs](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/peer-programs/)

Strengthen your leadership and teamwork skills by acting as a role model and resource to your fellow students\.

[UBC Residence Life positions](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/jobs/)

Support other students’ residence experience while working on a team and organizing meaningful events and programs\.

**[https://students\.ubc\.ca/campus\-life/involved/funding\-project/](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/involved/funding-project/)[Student Directed Seminars](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/student-directed-seminars/)**

If you’re an upper\-year student, lead a dynamic, academic seminar on a topic of your choice in a collaborative learning environment\.
