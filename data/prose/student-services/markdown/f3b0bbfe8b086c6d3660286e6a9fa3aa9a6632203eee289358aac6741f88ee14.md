The UBC Accessibility Shuttle is a free, shared ride service for people with conditions that impact their mobility\. It travels along a designated route, making stops throughout the central pedestrian areas of campus\. The shuttle has a limited\-storage trunk that can transport mobility aids\. It does not provide door\-to\-door service, and does not operate outside of central pedestrian areas\.

The Accessibility Shuttle serves areas with limited vehicle access and parking options for people with disabilities\. For locations outside the shuttle’s service area, you are able to use the UBC Community Shuttle or transit, which provides accessible service\.

## Who can use the shuttle?[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/centre\-for\-accessibility/ubc\-accessibility\-shuttle/\#who\-can\-use\-the\-shuttle](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/ubc-accessibility-shuttle/#who-can-use-the-shuttle)

The UBC Accessibility Shuttle is available for all students, staff, faculty, residents, and visitors on the UBC Point Grey campus who have temporary or permanent conditions that impact their mobility\.

Only designated attendants and service animals may accompany the passenger in the shuttle\. If you’re travelling with a designated attendant, please tell the dispatcher in advance when you book your ride\.

Failure to respect the [Accessibility Shuttle policies](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/ubc-accessibility-shuttle/#policies) and staff may lead to the suspension of privileges\.

## Ride booking and pick\-up[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/centre\-for\-accessibility/ubc\-accessibility\-shuttle/\#ride\-booking\-and\-pick\-up](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/ubc-accessibility-shuttle/#ride-booking-and-pick-up)

### Step 1: Book a ride

Shuttle availability is limited, and bookings must be made in advance\. Please call [604 822 9929](tel:6048229929) to speak with a dispatcher or leave a voice message\. When you book your ride, please be prepared with your [pick\-up and drop\-off locations](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/ubc-accessibility-shuttle/#stop-locations), dates, and times\.

Bookings are made on a first\-come, first\-served basis and are subject to availability\. Wait times can vary depending on demand\. It is the passenger’s responsibility to schedule their pick\-up early enough to reach their destination on time\. If you no longer require the shuttle, please call [604 822 9929](tel:6048229929) as soon as possible to cancel your booking\.

There is no limit to how far in advance you can book your ride\. Therefore, it’s recommended that you book ahead to ensure a more timely and reliable service\. If you leave a voice message to book your trip, you will receive a call\-back to confirm your ride within one business day\.

If you need the shuttle on a predictable, day\-to\-day basis, recurring bookings may be arranged on a term\-by\-term basis\.

### Step 2: Catch the shuttle

Please be at your shuttle stop on time\. Once the shuttle arrives at your stop, it will wait for a maximum of five minutes, depending on passenger demand\.

Look for shuttle designs at your stop location, like [this example](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Accessibility_Shuttle_sign.pdf) \(pdf\)\. This is where the shuttle will meet you\. View the [shuttle map](https://portal.gis.ubc.ca/arcgis/apps/instant/media/index.html?appid=4337f91b2eb24d388c36a8dd86ee05d9) for more details on pick\-up\-locations\.

Travel times vary depending on road conditions, weather, and other rides\. Please allow for up to 30 minutes from when you are picked up to when you are dropped off at the designated stop\.

## Hours of operation[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/centre\-for\-accessibility/ubc\-accessibility\-shuttle/\#hours\-of\-operation](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/ubc-accessibility-shuttle/#hours-of-operation)

The Accessibility Shuttle operates **Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 4:30 pm\.** The last ride of the day may be booked for 4:10 pm\. 

After 4:10 pm, please contact [AMS Safewalk](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/student-services/safewalk/) if you feel unsafe travelling alone across campus\. Safewalk operates from 9:00 pm to 2:00 am daily\.

During the formal final exam periods in April and December, the shuttle operates with extended hours, and on Saturdays and Sundays when exams are scheduled\.

## UBC Accessibility Shuttle policies

### Shuttle pick\-up and drop off

-   The shuttle stops only at designated stops\. Please check the [shuttle service map](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/ubc-accessibility-shuttle/#areas-served-by-the-accessibility-shuttle) for a list of campus stops\.
-   Passengers who do not arrive on time or fail to cancel their booking before their pick\-up will be flagged\. Repeated missed rides may result in the suspension of riding privileges\.
-   Accessibility Shuttle passengers are limited to 3 rides per day\.

#### Situations where service may be denied

Employees reserve the right to deny service if:

-   The passenger is intoxicated and shuttle staff determine they are unable to care for themselves or provide informed consent\.
-   The shuttle staff, passenger, or property is at risk of harm\.
-   The passenger fails to comply with program policies or treat staff respectfully\.

### Food and drink on the shuttle

Open food or beverages are not permitted on the shuttle\. You are allowed to have a beverage in a closed container\.
