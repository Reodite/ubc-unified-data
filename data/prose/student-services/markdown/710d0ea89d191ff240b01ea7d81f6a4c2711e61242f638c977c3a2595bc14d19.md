## Skills you’ll develop[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/chemistry/\#skills\-youll\-develop](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/chemistry/#skills-youll-develop)

While studying [Chemistry](http://www.chem.ubc.ca/current-undergraduate), you’re learning how to work with chemical compounds that can be used across industries like energy, water, medicine and materials development\. You’ll develop important skills while creating and validating processes to test or formulate compounds\.

These skills may include:

-   Analytical method development or validation
-   Focused observation
-   Laboratory report writing and presentation of data to scientific and non\-scientific audiences
-   Advanced qualitative and quantitative analysis
-   Application of logical, systematic thought processes to understand cause and effect
-   Identification of chemical hazards and facilitation of proper chemical waste disposal
-   Techniques in isolation, purification, analysis, synthesis, and usage of specialized laboratory instruments

## Make the most of your specialization[https://students\.ubc\.ca/career/your\-degree/science/chemistry/\#make\-the\-most\-of\-your\-specialization](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science/chemistry/#make-the-most-of-your-specialization)

Your experiences will open doors to new opportunities and help clarify your values and interests\.

-   **[Undergraduate Chemistry Society](https://ucs.chem.ubc.ca/)**  
    Meet other UBC Chemistry students and interact with faculty, through events and programs\.
-   **[UBC Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/chem)**  
    Gain work experience in chemistry between study terms\.
-   **[UBC Chemistry research and awards](https://www.chem.ubc.ca/undergraduate-research)**  
    Get involved in summer opportunities or take classes that incorporate laboratory experience in chemistry\.
-   **[Departmental research opportunities](https://www.chem.ubc.ca/research-areas)**  
    Reach out directly to faculty members to ask about potential research positions\.
-   **[Collaborative Research and Training Experience program](https://css.chem.ubc.ca/)**  
    Receive training in project management and patent development while getting involved in catalysis research\.
-   **[Mentorship Program for Chemistry Students](http://ucs.chem.ubc.ca/mentorship-program/)**  
    Chat with an upper\-level UBC Chemistry student to learn about helpful resources and opportunities\.
-   **[Reactive Intermediates Student Exchange](http://www.risecanada.ca/)**  
    During summer, gain hands\-on experience in experimental techniques for the study of chemical or biochemical reaction mechanisms\.
-   **[Canadian Chemistry Conference and Exhibition](https://www.cheminst.ca/conferences/)**  
    Attend national or regional conferences in the summer and connect with chemists across industries and academia\.
