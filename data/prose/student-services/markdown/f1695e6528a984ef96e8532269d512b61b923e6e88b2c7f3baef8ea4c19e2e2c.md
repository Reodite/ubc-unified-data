## Part\-time funding coverage[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/part\-time\-funding\-out\-province/\#part\-time\-funding\-coverage](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/part-time-funding-out-province/#part-time-funding-coverage)

The part\-time student loan program covers:

-   Tuition
-   Books
-   Supplies
-   Transportation
-   Child care \(up to the funding maximums\)

Part\-time funding does not cover living costs\. If you need additional financial support for living costs, please contact your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to discuss your financial plan\.

## Eligibility to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/part\-time\-funding\-out\-province/\#eligibility\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/part-time-funding-out-province/#eligibility-to-apply)

Loans are available for students studying part\-time at UBC\. To be considered a part\-time student, you must be registered in 20% to 59% of the full\-time load listed for your program, which you can check on the [Course load guide](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/course-load-guide/)\.

Not all programs are eligible for part\-time funding\. You must be in a program approved for full\-time student loans\. Contact your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) to check if your program is approved\. If you don’t have an assigned advisor, they’re still available to help\.

If you are a student with an established disability status with your loan province and you are taking a 40% course load or higher, you are encouraged to apply for [a full\-time loan](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/loans-full-time-other-provinces/), as a part\-time loan does not cover living costs\.

## Where you should apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/part\-time\-funding\-out\-province/\#where\-you\-should\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/part-time-funding-out-province/#where-you-should-apply)

Your province or territory of residence is typically where you lived for 12 continuous months excluding months of full\-time post\-secondary study before attending UBC, depending on whether you are a dependent or an independent student\.

## How to apply[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/part\-time\-funding\-out\-province/\#how\-to\-apply](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/part-time-funding-out-province/#how-to-apply)

1.  Download the application form based on your province or territory of residence:  
    -   [Alberta Student Aid](http://studentaid.alberta.ca/resources/applications-and-forms/)
    -   [Manitoba Student Aid](https://www.edu.gov.mb.ca/msa/pt-application-status.html)
    -   [Newfoundland &amp; Labrador Student Aid](https://www.gov.nl.ca/education/studentaid/parttime/)
    -   [Nova Scotia Student Assistance](https://novascotia.ca/studentassistance/Apply/New/PartTimeStudies.asp)   
    -   [Ontario Student Assistance Program](https://www.ontario.ca/page/osap-ontario-student-assistance-program) 
    -   [Prince Edward Island Student Financial Services](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/en/information/workforce-and-advanced-learning/canada-student-grants-and-loans-for-part-time-students)
    -   [Saskatchewan Student Aid](https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/education-and-learning/saskatchewan-student-aid/student-loans-and-grants/apply-for-student-aid)
    -   All other provinces and territories must apply through [National Student Loans](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/education/student-loan/forms.html#h2.7)\.             
         
2.  Fill out your application form and contact [Enrolment Services](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) for instructions on submitting your part\-time student loan application\. You will receive an assessment from the loan provider detailing what kind of funding you’ll receive and when you’ll receive it\.

Your funding will be sent to you through direct deposit by your loan provider after your enrolment has been confirmed by UBC\.

## Students with previous part\-time loans[https://students\.ubc\.ca/finances/student\-loans/part\-time\-funding\-out\-province/\#students\-with\-previous\-part\-time\-loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/part-time-funding-out-province/#students-with-previous-part-time-loans)

If you have a previous part\-time government student loan and are returning to part\-time or full\-time study, you do not need to begin repayment\. However, you must inform your lender that you are returning to study\. 

Please review the information for [students with previous loans](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/student-loans/student-loan-interest/)\.
