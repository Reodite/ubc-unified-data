**Construction Manager:** The United Group  
**Project Coordinator:** UBC Project Services – Victor Wong

### Project Summary

The existing Reboot Cafe was renovated to transform it into a Pacific Poke outlet\. The finishes and lighting were replaced to enhance the space and comply with the franchise look\.  The existing millwork was also modified to fit in the new equipment that needed to be installed\.

The renovation was completed on a very tight timeframe during the 2017 summer, so that the outlet could be ready to receive the UBC community during the first week of classes in September 2017\.
