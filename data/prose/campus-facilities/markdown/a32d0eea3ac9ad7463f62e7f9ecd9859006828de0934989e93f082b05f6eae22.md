The Records Retrieval System is our primary means of providing access to the records\. Major record groups include:

-   Buildings \(landscaping, architectural, structural, mechanical, plumbing, and electrical categories\)
-   Building specifications, and operating &amp; maintenance manuals
-   Underground services \(gas, water, storm, sanitary, etc\.\)
-   British Columbia Safety Authority \(BCSA\) operating permits for boiler pressure vessels

Access the RRS is available to UBC staff and authorized consultants\.
