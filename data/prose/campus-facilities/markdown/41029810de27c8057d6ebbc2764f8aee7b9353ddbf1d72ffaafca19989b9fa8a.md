### Project snapshot

**Project Size:** 2,751 square metres  
**Budget:** $25\.5 million estimate  
**Status:** In progress  
**Occupancy:** December 2027  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services

### Project Summary

The existing four\-storey Douglas T\. Kenny Building, originally constructed in 1983, will undergo construction work to complete seismic upgrades beginning July 2026\. The project is designed to reduce the building’s seismic risk through targeted structural improvements, creating a safer environment for occupants while maintaining the building’s operational integrity during and following seismic events\. 

The project includes upgrades to exterior and interior shear walls, along with modifications to structurally vulnerable interior elements, including bridge connections and floor slab structures\. Work will involve strengthening the building’s structure through the addition of concrete, installation of soil anchors, and the integration of structural drag struts throughout the building\. 

Areas impacted by the structural work, as well as any mechanical and electrical services affected during construction, will be carefully documented by the project team\. These areas will be restored to their existing condition, or better, following completion of the seismic upgrades\. 

This project supports UBC’s strategic priorities by enhancing campus safety, strengthening risk management, and supporting operational continuity\. It also advances sustainability goals by extending the useful life of the existing building and minimizing embodied carbon emissions where possible through the reuse of existing structural elements and the incorporation of low\-carbon concrete in new construction\.
