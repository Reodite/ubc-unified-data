### Project Snapshot

**Project Sponsor:** UBC Library  
**Project Size:** 24,542 ft2  
**Budget:** $9\.940 million  
**Occupancy:** Provisional occupancy August 2015\. Final occupancy October 2015\.  
**Project Manager:** UBC Properties Trust  
**Architect:** DGBK Architects  
**Construction Manager:** Wales McLelland  
**Sustainability:** LEED Gold

### Project Summary

A high\-density, archival storage facility to preserve and protect UBC research collections, formerly known as the “BC Integrated Research Library”, this facility will have a modular design to allow for future expansion of UBC collections storage needs and potential inclusion of other partner institutions\.
