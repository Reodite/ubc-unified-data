### Project Snapshot

**Project Sponsor:** Provost &amp; Vice President Academic  
**Project Size:** 2140 GSF Renovation  
**Budget:** $4\.1 million  
**Occupancy:** June 2017  
**Project Manager:** UBC Properties Trust / UBC Project Services – Ryan Huffman  
**Architect:** Stantec Architecture  
**Construction Manager:** Holaco

### Project Summary

The new Northwest Coast Masterworks Gallery transformed the existing Ames Theatre which will be relocated to a new space in the Museum\. At 2,235 ft2 the Gallery will preserve and celebrate masterworks of the Northwest Coast and enhance MOA’s capacity to present and interpret First Nations art and culture to wider audiences\.
