**Occupancy:** February 2015  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services – Jay Jethwa  
**Project Coordinator:** UBC Project Services – David Kosub

### Project Summary

To modernize and renovate the lobby area with the provision of a new registration area with support offices, the existing kiosk was removed and a new structure that matched the building’s architecture was constructed\. This doubled the size of the office area, therefore creating a much more student\-accessible counter\.
