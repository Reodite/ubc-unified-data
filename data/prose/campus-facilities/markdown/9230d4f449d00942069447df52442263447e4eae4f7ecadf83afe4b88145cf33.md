### Project Snapshot

**Budget:** $140,000  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services – Darren Wong

### Project Summary

Additional and clear signage was added around campus to indicate the direction and walking distances to buildings, facilities and attractions\. Existing information kiosks will also be upgraded over the coming months\.

Wayfinding blades were installed at 27 campus locations, on main pedestrian routes and at major intersections\.  The project duration for both the wayfinding blades and the kiosk restoration project is approximately 12 months from design to completion\.
