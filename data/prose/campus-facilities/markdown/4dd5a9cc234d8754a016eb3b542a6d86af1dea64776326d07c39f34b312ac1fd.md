### Project Snapshot

**Project Size:** 2,409 gross square metres  
**Budget:** $33 million estimate  
**Status:** TBD

### Project Summary

UBCO is assessing options to purchase and renovate space in an existing commercial building to support the Masters of Physical Therapy \(MPT\) and the Masters of Occupational Therapy programs in the UBC Faculty of Medicine\.

The facility is anticipated to require specific infrastructure and architectural, mechanical, electrical, and acoustic requirements to enable full audio visual and information technology functionality between sites\. This approach is similar to that developed for the Physical Therapy &amp; Occupational Therapy \(Surrey Expansion\)\.
