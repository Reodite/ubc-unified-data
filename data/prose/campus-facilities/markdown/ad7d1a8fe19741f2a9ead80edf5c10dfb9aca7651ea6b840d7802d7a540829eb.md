### Project Summary

The previous compartmentalized laboratories were demolished to create an open\-plan flexible laboratory system for Food Biology and Clinical Nutrition research, supported by a Mass Spectrometer and sterilization room\. Included were renovations in the corridors to accommodate graduate student workstations\.
