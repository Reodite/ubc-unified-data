### Project Snapshot

**Project Sponsor:** Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences  
**Project Size:** 246,182 ft2  
**Budget:** $155\.230 million  
**Occupancy:** September 2012  
**Project Manager:** UBC Properties Trust  
**Architect:** Saucier \+ Perrotte with Hughes Condon Marler Architects  
**Construction Manager:** Ledcor  
**Sustainability:** LEED Gold  
**Funding:** Province of BC $86\.4 million; UBC Endowment &amp; Fundraising

### Project Summary

This state\-of\-the\-art new facility will allow the expansion of entry\-to\-practice and graduate programs to address the acute shortage of pharmacists in British Columbia and will bridge the gap between academic discoveries and commercialization\.

The building was chosen by University Affairs as one of 8 iconic buildings within Canadian Universities: [read the full article here](https://www.universityaffairs.ca/features/feature-article/8-iconic-canadian-university-buildings/)\.
