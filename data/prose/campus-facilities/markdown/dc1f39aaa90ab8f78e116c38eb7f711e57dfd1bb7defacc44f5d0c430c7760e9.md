### Project Snapshot

**Budget:** $181,000  
**Occupancy:** December 2013  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services – Ryan Huffman  
**Project Coordinator:** UBC Project Services – David Kosub

### Project Summary

To create an open and collaborative office space for the Student Communications team and a state of the art teleconferencing facility in the Media Room for the International Student Initiative\.

**Room 2305:** Originally housed a kitchen and eight cubicles\. This was demolished and transformed into the media room and supplied with all international telecoferencing resources and accompanying technological capabilities\.

**Room 2303/A:** The existing partitions and cubicles were upgraded to open up the space with new office furniture, lockers, work spaces, carpet, and wall paint\.

**Room 2303E/F:** These two rooms were opened up into one space, and the same theme that was used in 2303 was applied here: new office furniture, carpet and paint\.
