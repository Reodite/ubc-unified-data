### Project Snapshot

**Budget:** $280,000  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services

### Project Summary

“A moving camera obscura image in the interior of a darkened itinerant, 19th\-century horse\-drawn carriage would have constituted a prefiguration of the cinema, had such a thing existed\. To realize this “philosophical toy” in a post\-cinema age is to fabricate a kind of time machine in which the spectators, looking forward, may see backwards and upside\-down, that which is forever receding behind them,” Rodney Graham\.

A building was erected for displaying and protecting the Landau Carriage interactive Mobile Camera Obscura art sculpture by Canadian Artist Rodney Graham\.  The project took approximately 4 months to complete\.
