### Inclusive Washroom Guidelines

Inclusive washrooms are washrooms that can be used safely, accessibly, and comfortably by all members of the UBC community and visitors to the UBC Vancouver \(Point Grey\) and Okanagan campuses\.

[Read More](https://facilities.ubc.ca/policies-guidelines/inclusive-washroom-guidelines/)
