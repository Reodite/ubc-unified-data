**Budget:** $39\.9 million  
**Occupancy:** December 2016  
**Project Manager:** UBC Properties Trust  
**Architect:** Acton Ostry Architects \+ MacLennan Jaunkain Miller Architects  
**Construction Manager:** Heatherbrae  
**Sustainability:** LEED Gold  
**Program:**  
1 – 52m competition pool  
1 – 25m recreational lap pool  
1 – leisure pool with hot tub

### Project Summary

The new UBC Aquatic Centre is one of the few aquatic facilities in Canada to target LEED Gold certification and to have a high\-performance and recreational pool under the same roof\. The Centre includes a 50m competition pool, a 25m recreational pool, a leisure pool, spectator seating, a hot tub, sauna and steam room, universal change rooms, male and female change rooms, a wet classroom, and meeting rooms\.
