### Project Snapshot

**Budget:** $ 75,000  
**Occupancy:** December 2016  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services – André Harmann  
**Project Coordinator:** UBC Project Services – David Kosub

### Project Summary

Project purpose: to provide privacy, sound attenuation and security to offices\.

The project enclosed four offices open to a corridor with glazing panels and Card Reader Access to match the style of the rest of the corridor\.

#### Sustainable Elements

Reuse of some glazing panels and hardware\. Modified desks rather than replaced them\.
