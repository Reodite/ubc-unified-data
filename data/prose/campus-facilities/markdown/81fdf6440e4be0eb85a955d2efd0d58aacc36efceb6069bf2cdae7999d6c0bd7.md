### Project Snapshot

**Project Sponsor:** Provost &amp; VP Academic  
**Project Size:** No change to existing building area  
**Budget:** $4\.162 million  
**Occupancy:** January 2017  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services  
**Architect:** Acton Ostry  
**Construction Manager:** Heatherbrae

### Project Summary

Structural upgrades to address very high seismic risk in a high use office tower occupied by the Sauder School of Business\. This project was prioritized for upgrade as part of the UBC Seismic Mitigation Plan based on the impact evaluation and the relatively straightforward nature of the upgrade work required\.
