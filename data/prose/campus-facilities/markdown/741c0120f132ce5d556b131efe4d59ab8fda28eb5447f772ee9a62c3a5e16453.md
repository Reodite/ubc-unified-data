### Project Snapshot

**Budget:** $77,300  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services

### Project Summary

The Asian Centre currently houses an auditorium, Asian Library, Tea Gallery, Music Studio, Swann Collection of Rare Asian Books along with the Department of Asian Studies and the Office of the AVP International\.

In order to allow for wheelchair access into the building, Project Services was asked to install an interior ramp and power door making the building completely wheelchair accessible\.
