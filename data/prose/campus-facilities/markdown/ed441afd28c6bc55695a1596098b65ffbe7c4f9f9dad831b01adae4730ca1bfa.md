### Project Summary

This core infrastructure project involves the addition of 12 MW of boiler capacity to the existing [Bioenergy Facility](https://sustain.ubc.ca/research/research-collections/bioenergy-research-demonstration-facility) to allow the campus hot water heating system to keep up with projected increased demand over the coming years from academic and student residence buildings\.

The expanded use of biomass as an energy source will provide additional heat at a lower cost than natural gas and will help UBC diversify its fuel mix, allowing more effective management of the fuel cost risk as well as to help reduce the University’s carbon tax through lowered GHG emissions\.

The scope of work includes civil and structural modifications to the existing facility, removal of redundant equipment, significant mechanical and electrical upgrades, seismic modifications to reflect the altered layout of the building, the addition of a significantly upgraded biofuel material handling system, and the supply and installation of a new boiler\.
