### Project Snapshot

**Project Sponsor:** Engineering Undergraduate Society / Faculty of Applied Science  
**Project Size:** 10,058 ft\[2\]  
**Budget:** $5\.220 million  
**Occupancy:** Provisional occupancy September 2015\. Final occupancy November 2015\.  
**Project Manager:** UBC Properties Trust  
**Architect:** Urban Arts  
**Construction Manager:** Syncra  
**Sustainability:** LEED Gold  
**Funding:** EUS Student Fees $2\.56 million; APSC Fundraising $1\.91 million; APSC Contribution $0\.50 million; UBC Informal Learning Space Fund $0\.25 million

### Project Summary

The Engineering Student Centre \(ESC\) is being developed by the Faculty of Applied Science \(APSC\) and the Engineering Undergraduate Society \(EUS\) propose to provide a hub for undergraduate engineering students\. The ESC will replace the current EUS facility, the “Cheeze Factory”\.
