### Project Summary

The goal of the ESB is to consolidate Earth &amp; Ocean Sciences and allied units into a single, state\-of\-the\-art complex that addresses the pressing need for space for research and learning in Earth Sciences and to enhance opportunities for shared learning and collaboration\.
