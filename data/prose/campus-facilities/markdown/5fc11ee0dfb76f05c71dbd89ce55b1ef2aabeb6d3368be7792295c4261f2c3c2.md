### Project Snapshot

**Budget:** $360,000  
**Occupancy:** July 2013  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services – Jay Jethwa

### Project Summary

The existing studio and offices in the University Services Building required modernization and were transformed into a state\-of\-the\-art studio space\.

The 4500 sq ft of studio space underwent extensive renovations and modernization\. The project area comprised:

-   a reception and waiting area
-   a green room
-   multiple offices and meeting rooms
-   an editing suite
-   audio visual suites, and
-   the main studio\.

The modernization involved high end finishes, furniture and furnishings with bold and vibrant colours, particularly in the waiting area and main meeting room\.
