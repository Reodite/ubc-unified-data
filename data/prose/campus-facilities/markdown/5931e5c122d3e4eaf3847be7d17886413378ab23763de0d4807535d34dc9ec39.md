### Project Snapshot

**Project Sponsor:** Alma Mater Society/UBC  
**Project Size:** 253,750 ft\[2\]  
**Budget:** $106\.760 million  
**Occupancy:** Provisional occupancy May 2015\. Final occupancy September 2015\.  
**Project Manager:** UBC Properties Trust  
**Architect:** Dialog  
**Construction Manager:** Bird Construction  
**Sustainability:** LEED Platinum  
**Funding:** AMS Student Levy $81\.76 million, UBC $25 million

### Project Summary

The heart of student life on campus, the new SUB will house AMS offices, social and meeting spaces, club rooms, assembly space, various food outlets and AMS commercial ventures\.
