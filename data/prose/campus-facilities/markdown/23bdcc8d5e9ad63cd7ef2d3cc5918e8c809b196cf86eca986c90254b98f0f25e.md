### Project Snapshot

**Occupancy:** June 2017

**Architect:** Public Architecture  
**Room Capacity:** 294

### Project Summary

A new state\-of\-the\-art research facility for the Stewart Blusson Quantum Matter Institute \(QMI\) expands the globally significant quantum materials research currently underway at UBC\. This facility is an addition to the Brimacombe building, which houses the related Advanced Materials and Process Engineering Laboratory \(AMPEL\)\.
