The UBC Facilities’ Custodial Services Quality Assurance Dashboard serves as a comprehensive tool for monitoring the overall cleanliness standards across the campus\. The target reflects a campus\-wide quality level consistent with APPA Level 3, which represents the level of care provided\. The dashboard displays results derived from our returned inspections, providing a holistic view of the custodial services quality across the University\. It is important to note that these results represent the collective performance across all inspected areas, and may not directly correspond to the conditions of each individual building\. Further trending of results is available in the breakdown per space type\. For more details, please contact your [Facilities Manager](https://www3.buildingoperations.ubc.ca/facility-manager-search/)\.
