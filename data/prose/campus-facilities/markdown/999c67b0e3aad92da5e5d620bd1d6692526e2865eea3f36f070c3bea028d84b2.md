When extreme weather comes to UBC Vancouver, Facilities is responsible for managing snow and ice within the Vancouver campus\. The roads around UBC \(Marine Drive, University Boulevard from Wesbrook Mall to Blanca Street, and West 16th Avenue from Blanca Street to Marine Drive\) are maintained by The [The Mainroad Group](https://mainroad.ca/) under contract with the [Provincial Government of BC](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home)\.

The weather at UBC can be unpredictable and variable, as UBC has its own micro climate which can be quite different from lower lying areas\. Custom weather feeds are monitored to get accurate predictions of what’s coming and how we need to respond in order to make the campus as safe as possible, as soon as possible\.

> ### Updates during extreme weather
> 
> **Check ubc\.ca:** In extreme weather situations, updates on campus conditions, classes or exams are posted on [ubc\.ca](https://www.ubc.ca/)\.
> 
> **Download the UBC Safe App:** We recommend UBC students, faculty, staff and campus visitors download the [UBC Safe Vancouver app](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/ubc-safe-vancouver-app/) and turn on your push notifications to receive immediate information \(e\.g\. weather advisories, class cancellations, etc\.\) and updates that are impacting the entire campus\.

## Snow removal and ice prevention

Facilities’ Municipal Services and Custodial Services teams provide ice prevention and snow removal services with the full complement of staff during regular working hours\. To ensure a safe campus, our teams also works outside of regular working hours, with a reduced crew and scope of work\.

Our fleet of snow removal and deicing vehicles start early in the morning to plow and treat as many roads, sidewalks and pathways as possible before the first students, faculty, and staff arrive\. Weather is unpredictable and crews do their best to make sure the campus is ready for regular operating hours\.

We can only plow when there is at least 4 cm of snow\. We work in priority order to ensure the highest risk areas of campus are accessible first—places like the bus loop, hospital, day\-cares, and arterial roads\. We then move onto academic and student building access\. Main and accessible entranceways to campus buildings are prioritized\. So please use main entranceways during extreme weather and be careful on side paths\.

Salt and brine are applied to ensure ice can be managed appropriately to minimize our environmental impact\. To ensure effectiveness, we typically brine roads, sidewalks and pathways during dry cold weather as a preventative measure\.

When weather is extreme, we provide information to the campus administration group to inform key decision making and minimize disruptions\. Please be safe around our equipment and teams when we are working as equipment may be noisy and conditions are challenging\.

## Roads approaching UBC and neighbourhood areas

The roads approaching UBC are managed by [The Mainroad Group](https://mainroad.ca/) under contract with the [Provincial Government of BC](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home)\. These roads include:

-   Marine Drive from W 41\[st\] Avenue to East Mall
-   W 16\[th\] Avenue from Southwest Marine Drive to Blanca Street
-   University Boulevard from Wesbrook Mall to Blanca Street
-   Chancellor Blvd from East Mall to Blanca Street

Facilities provides support to the [University Neighbourhood Association \(UNA\)](https://www.myuna.ca/) when resources are available\. For more information on the UNA snow removal plan, please visit the [UNA website](https://www.myuna.ca/una-snow-removal-plan-2/)\.

For information on roads managed by the [University Endowment Lands \(UEL\)](http://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/) please visit the [University Endowment Lands website](http://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/UEL_Snow_Removal_Policy.pdf)\.
