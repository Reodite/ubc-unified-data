Enabling Greatness: Our story behind UBC Vancouver’s buildings and grounds

In 2017, we participated in the [APPA FMEP](http://www.appa.org/FMEP/) evaluation process\. The book *Enabling Greatness: Our Story Behind UBC Vancouver’s Buildings and Grounds* served as both a formal [APPA FMEP](http://www.appa.org/FMEP/) self\-evaluation as well as a comprehensive overview of Building Operations, its activities, current performance, and future plans\.

In addition to this self\-evaluation, an onsite review was conducted by a team of senior facilities executive who produced  [a final companion report \[PDF\]](https://vpfo-facilities.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/02/UBC-FMEP-Report.pdf)
