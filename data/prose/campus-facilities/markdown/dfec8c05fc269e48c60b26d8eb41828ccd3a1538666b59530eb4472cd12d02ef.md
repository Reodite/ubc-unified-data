> ### For immediate assistance, call the [Facilities Service Centre](https://facilities.ubc.ca/services/submit-a-service-request/) at 604\-822\-2173

UBC Facilities delivers services to plan, design, construct, operate and maintain UBC’s buildings, public realm and utilities infrastructure to ensure a safe, functional and sustainable environment for the UBC Vancouver Point Grey campus community\.

From core maintenance to minor construction to custom\-built designs and prompt response to facilities issues, Facilities is here to help\. Our skilled tradespeople and professional staff are dedicated to keeping UBC’s grounds and facilities operating smoothly\. 

To set up service contracts for your department’s equipment, such as A/C units, furnaces, ice makers, fleet vehicles and other specialized equipment, please contact your [Facilities Manager](https://www3.buildingoperations.ubc.ca/facility-manager-search/) for details\. 

[Learn more about our service delivery](https://facilities.ubc.ca/about-us/service-delivery/)
