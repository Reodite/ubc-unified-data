### Project Snapshot

**Occupancy:** March 2016  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services – Jay Jethwa  
**Project Coordinator:** UBC Project Services – Luc De Beir

### Project Summary

Project purpose: to renovate, renew &amp; replace 2 existing greenhouses to provide up to date research facilities for the Department of Botany at UBC\.

Two existing greenhouses were demolished and replaced with two new greenhouses\. Greenhouse \#1 is 6900 sq ft and is a Research Greenhouse Facility with environmentally controlled chambers and support services\. Greenhouse \#2 is 5830 sq ft and is a Shade/Production Facility with more basic services\.
