UBC Facilities’ Stores Tool Crib has a wide range of power tools and equipment available for rental to on\-campus customers\. Our knowledgeable staff can provide advice on the right tool for your needs\.

Example rental tools include:

-   power saws and drills
-   pipe cutters
-   grinders
-   power washers
-   portable pumps
-   shop vacuums
-   generators
-   air compressors
-   ladders
-   portable lights

Daily, weekly, and monthly rental rates are available\. Rental transactions may be arranged through a standard Facilities’ customer work request in Planon\. A standing work request can be set up if regular rentals are expected\.

View our Planon [Tool Rental Catalogue](https://vpfo-facilities.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/02/Tool-Rental-Catalogue.pdf) for a complete list of available equipment\.

## Hardware and safety supplies for the UBC community

UBC Facilities’ Stores is open to all UBC academic or ancillary departments and carries all manner of hardware and safety supplies including:

-   building materials
-   flashlights and batteries
-   safety vests
-   coveralls and gloves
-   electrical supplies
-   plumbing supplies
-   fasteners
-   adhesives

Special order items can be obtained upon request\.

Stores purchases may be arranged through a standard Facilities’ customer work order\. A standing work order can be set up if regular purchases are expected\.
