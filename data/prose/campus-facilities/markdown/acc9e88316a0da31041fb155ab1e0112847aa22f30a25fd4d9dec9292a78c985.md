### Project Snapshot

**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services – Anita Ryder

### Project Summary

Project purpose: to upgrade the audiovisual equipment at 17 lecture theatres across campus\.

AV upgrades were completed at lecture theatres in various buildings, including the Civil and Mechanical Engineering Building, Friedman Building, Macleod Building, and the Robert F\. Osborne Centre\. These upgrades involved the installation of new:

-   digital displays
-   projectors
-   lecture capture units, and
-   lecterns with document cameras\.
