### Project Snapshot

**Project Sponsor:** Provost &amp; Vice President Academic  
**Project Size:** 5200 GSF  
**Budget:** $5\.5 million  
**Occupancy:** June 2017  
**Project Manager:** UBC Properties Trust  
**Architect:** Formline Architecture  
**Construction Manager:** Bird Construction

### Project Summary

This striking new facility located in the heart of campus between the Koerner Library and IK Barber Learning Centre provides:

-   A central and visible location for re\-thinking the relationship between Aboriginal peoples and Canadian society\.
-   An on\-campus meeting place for community members and scholars and a powerful recognition of Aboriginal culture and history in the heart of campus\.
-   A place for knowledge, records and the preservation of memory\.
