### Project Snapshot

**Project Sponsor:** UBC Student Housing &amp; Hospitality Services; Faculty of Arts; Faculty of Education  
**Project Size:** 604,436 ft2  
**Budget:** $165\.5 million  
**Occupancy:** Phase 1 Sep\-Dec 2013; Phase 2 Sep 2015\-June 2016  
**Project Manager:** UBC Properties Trust  
**Architect:** Kuwabara Payne McKenna &amp; Blumberg with Hughes Condon Marler Architects  
**Construction Manager:** Ledcor \(Phase 1\); Urban One \(Phase 2\)  
**Sustainability:** LEED Gold  
**Number of Beds:** 1,158  
**Funding:** Loan from Student Housing Financing Endowment paid back through rental revenue\. Faculty and UBC fundraising and cash contributions\.

### Project Summary

One of five proposed commons or “hubs” designed to increase on\-campus student housing and create vibrant social and academic centres, bringing life to the core of campus and connecting students who live on campus, commuter students, faculty and staff\.
