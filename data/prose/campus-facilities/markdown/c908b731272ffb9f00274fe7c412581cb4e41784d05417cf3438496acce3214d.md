In 2017, we participated in the APPA FMEP evaluation process\. The book Enabling Greatness: Our Story Behind UBC Vancouver’s Buildings and Grounds served as both a formal APPA FMEP self\-evaluation and a comprehensive overview of UBC Facilities\.
