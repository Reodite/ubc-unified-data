### Project Snapshot

**Budget:** $100,000  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services

### Project Summary

The Frederic Lasserre building currently houses the School of Community and Regional Planning, School of Architecture and Landscape Architecture and the School of Art History and Visual Art along with various classroom facilities\.

We intensified the use of the entry lobby by adding a “Seating Box,” the built expression of a student architectural competition\.  This project took three months to complete construction\.
