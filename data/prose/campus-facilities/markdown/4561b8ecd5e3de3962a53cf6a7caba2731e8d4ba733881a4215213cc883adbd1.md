This support page is for Planon users, whether you want to learn more on how to perform your everyday, routine activities in Planon more efficiently, or if you’re looking for a deeper dive into the more nuanced processes performed in Planon, these resources will help you navigate Planon in your day\-to\-day work\.

### Planon Training Aids

Find condensed [Training Aids](https://wpl.ubc.ca/browse/planon/courses/planon-job-aids-for-building-ops-ews-locksmiths) to quickly assist you with the most common Planon tasks\.

### Planon Video Library

Find our most popular video tutorial series and many other instructional videos providing in\-depth topic overviews and step\-by\-step demonstrations of numerous Planon processes in the [Video Library](https://wpl.ubc.ca/browse/planon/courses/planon-training-recordings)\.

### How to Get Planon Help

For Planon support, please submit a ticket through the [ServiceNow Self Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)\. Support includes new staff requests for access, changes to your existing access, questions about Planon functionality or processes and requests for new features and/or enhancements to existing functionality\.
