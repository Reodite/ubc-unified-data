### Project Summary

To accommodate changing requirements in partnership with the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences for the 3rd and 4th floors at Friedman Building at UBC, architectural, electrical and mechanical upgrades were completed to accommodate accessibility, audio visual upgrades and modernization\.
