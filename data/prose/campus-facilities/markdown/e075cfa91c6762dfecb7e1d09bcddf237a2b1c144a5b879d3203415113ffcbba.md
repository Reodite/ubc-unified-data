### Project Snapshot

**Budget:** $126,000  
**Project Manager:** UBC Project Services

### Project Summary

The Forest Sciences Centre has a variety of occupants, including the Faculty of Forestry, Forest Sciences, Wood Science, Forest Resource Management, Centre for Advanced Wood Processing &amp; Centre for Applied Conservation Biology\.

Updating an underused lounge space with signage, art, comfortable furniture, lighting and finishes gives forestry students a place of their own\.  The project took about 4 months to complete\.
