UBC Facilities manages the construction of new buildings, modernization of existing spaces, and  renewals of capital assets on the Vancouver and Okanagan campuses\.

For detailed information about a project, please contact your [Facilities Manager](http://buildingoperations.ubc.ca/business-units/building-services/facilities-management/facility-manager-search/) or the Project Manager\.

For emergencies, please contact the [Services Centre](https://vpfo-facilities.sites.olt.ubc.ca/services/) or call 604\-822\-2173

## Planning &amp; consultation

UBC Facilities partners with [Campus &amp; Community Planning](https://planning.ubc.ca/) in the development and planning projects across campus in academic and administrative spaces, neighbourhoods and outdoor spaces\. Planning projects and projects under application may be open for public consultation\.

[Explore planning projects and consultations](https://planning.ubc.ca/planning-development/projects-and-consultations)

## Road closures

The UBC campus is always growing and changing so it’s common to encounter construction and road closures\. [Campus &amp; Community Planning](https://planning.ubc.ca/) planning provides updates on road closures and transits updates to help you locate existing construction areas and road closures at UBC Vancouver\. It is updated regularly to make navigating campus easier\.

[View road closures and transit updates \(updated bi\-monthly\)](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/construction-and-transit-updates)
