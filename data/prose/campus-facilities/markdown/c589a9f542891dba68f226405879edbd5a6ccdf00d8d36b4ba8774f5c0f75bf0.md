### Project Snapshot

**Project Sponsor:** UBC Athletics &amp; Recreation  
**Budget:** $8\.9 million  
**Occupancy:** June 2011  
**Project Manager:** UBC Properties Trust  
**Architect:** Shape Architecture  
**Construction Manager:** Heatherbrae

### Project Summary

A new change room and clubhouse facility to support UBC and community rugby\. Located in Thunderbird Park adjacent to the grass rugby fields\. Developed with funding contributions from UBC Rugby alumni including Gerald McGavin and Peter Bull\.
