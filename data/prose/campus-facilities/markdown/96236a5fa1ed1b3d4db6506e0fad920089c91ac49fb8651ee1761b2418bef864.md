### Project Snapshot

**Project Sponsor:** UBC Athletics &amp; Recreation / Vancouver Whitecaps Football Club  
**Project Size:** 37,795 GSF  
**Budget:** $23\.019 million  
**Occupancy:** June 2017  
**Project Manager:** UBC Properties Trust  
**Construction Manager:** Syncra  
**Sustainability:** LEED Gold  
**Funding:** Province of British Columbia $11\.006 million

### Project Summary

A joint venture between UBC Athletics &amp; Recreation and the Vancouver Whitecaps Football Club, the National Soccer Development Centre is a state of the art high performance training centre for the Whitecaps and an important recreational and high\-performance facility for the UBC community\. The Centre includes a new field house, two grass playing fields, one artificial turf field and a relocated varsity turf field\.
