Please refer to the “Design Review and Construction Coordination Information Package” found with UBC’s Technical Guidelines [Division 33 specifications](https://www.technicalguidelines.ubc.ca/technical/divisional_specs.html#Div33), which outlines the development processes related to UBC’s utilities infrastructure\. The package includes a checklist of UBC Energy and Water Services \(EWS\) consultant and contractor service requirements\.

All the UBC standards that builders are expected to adhere to can be found in the [UBC Technical Guidelines](http://technicalguidelines.ubc.ca/)\. Most underground utilities\-specific information is found in the [Division 33 \(mechanical\)](http://www.technicalguidelines.ubc.ca/technical/divisional_specs.html) and [Division 26 \(electrical\)](http://www.technicalguidelines.ubc.ca/Division_26/2019_Division_26_Tech_Guidelines.pdf) guideline documents\. Please see below for additional work procedures and forms related to project design and construction coordination requirements\.

> ### UBC is part of [BC One Call’s](http://www.bconecall.bc.ca/) call before you dig program
> 
> By submitting a ticket to them, we’ll be able to assist you in locating underground utilities, provided that UBC [Campus \+ Community Planning](https://planning.ubc.ca/planning-development/permits-and-business-licenses) has issued you a permit\.

All Safety and Project Administration policies and procedures can be found [here](https://facilities.ubc.ca/policies-guidelines/)\.
