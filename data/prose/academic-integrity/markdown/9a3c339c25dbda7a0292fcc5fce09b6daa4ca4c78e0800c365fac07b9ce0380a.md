## Key Terms

**Warning letter**

A Warning Letter may be issued by a Dean’s Office to a student as a response to an allegation of academic misconduct\. A Warning Letter may impact how any subsequent incident of misconduct is responded to \(Discipline for Academic Misconduct, 5\.6; [Vancouver](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/5-review-allegations-dean#:~:text=of%20the%20review.-,5.6,-For%20the%20purposes) and [Okanagan](https://okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/5-review-allegations-dean#:~:text=of%20the%20review.-,5.6,-For%20the%20purposes)\)\.

**Diversionary Process**

The [**Diversionary Process**](https://academicintegrity.ubc.ca/diversionary-process/) is a pathway available to Dean’s Offices to respond to academic misconduct when a student has admitted to the misconduct allegation and has no previous record of academic misconduct at UBC*\.* It is designed to be a flexible and educational process that allows Faculties to tailor responses to misconduct through a voluntary agreement with the student \(an “Integrity Plan”\) \(Discipline for Academic Misconduct, 6; [Vancouver](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/6-integrity-plans-diversionary-option) and [Okanagan](https://okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/6-integrity-plans-diversionary-option)\)\.

**Integrity Plan**

An Integrity Plan is a voluntary agreement between student and Dean’s Office that includes a description of the academic misconduct incident and agreed\-upon outcomes to respond to the academic misconduct\. Examples of outcomes that may be included in an Integrity Plan are listed in the Academic Calendars \([Vancouver](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/6-integrity-plans-diversionary-option) and [Okanagan](https://okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/6-integrity-plans-diversionary-option)\)\. An Integrity Plan is part of a student’s record of academic misconduct and may impact how any subsequent incident of misconduct is responded to \(Discipline for Academic Misconduct, 5\.6; [Vancouver](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/5-review-allegations-dean#:~:text=of%20the%20review.-,5.6,-For%20the%20purposes) and [Okanagan](https://okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/5-review-allegations-dean#:~:text=of%20the%20review.-,5.6,-For%20the%20purposes)\)\.

**PACSD**

The **President’s Advisory Committee on Student Discipline \(sometimes referred to as the “President’s Committee”\)** advises the President on matters of academic discipline through a formal hearing process \(Academic Calendar: [**Vancouver**](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/7-presidents-committee) and [**Okanagan**](https://okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/7-presidents-committee)\)\. The PACSD has a committee for each UBC campus\. Allegations of academic misconduct referred to the PACSD that have yet to be determined, and findings of academic misconduct by the PACSD and the President’s decision letter as to the appropriate discipline \(if any\) may impact how any subsequent incident of misconduct is responded to \(Discipline for Academic Misconduct, 5\.6; [Vancouver](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/5-review-allegations-dean#:~:text=of%20the%20review.-,5.6,-For%20the%20purposes) and [Okanagan](https://okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-conduct-and-discipline/discipline-academic-misconduct/5-review-allegations-dean#:~:text=of%20the%20review.-,5.6,-For%20the%20purposes)\)\.
