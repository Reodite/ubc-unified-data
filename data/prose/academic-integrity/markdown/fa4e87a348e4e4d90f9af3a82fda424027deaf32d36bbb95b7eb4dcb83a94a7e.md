Ever wonder what ‘counts’ as academic misconduct or what else you should know about academic integrity at UBC? Tune in to Academic Integrity Roundup, a video podcast series hosted by the Academic Integrity Hub\. In each episode, we unpack real scenarios and questions asked by UBC students, and offer practical tips to support your academic success\.

**What you’ll get out of each episode:** 

-   **Real scenarios:** Dive into real questions and situations faced by UBC students\. 

-   **Myth\-busting:** Learn to identify myths and misconceptions about academic integrity\. 

-   **Level up your understanding:** Gain familiarity with UBC policies and academic integrity concepts\. 

-   **Practical tips**: Find helpful tips to learn with integrity and avoid academic misconduct\. 

-   **Find support:** Discover UBC resources available to support your academic success\.
