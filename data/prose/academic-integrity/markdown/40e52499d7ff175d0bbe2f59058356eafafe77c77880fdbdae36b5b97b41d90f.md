In the February edition of *[Edubytes](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/2022/02/24/edubytes-academic-integrity/)*, we dive into concepts around academic integrity, with a spotlight on new developments and resources at UBC\. Our guest editors are **Ainsley Rouse, PhD**, Senior Manager Academic Integrity, Office of the Provost and Vice\-President Academic; and **Jaclyn J Stewart, PhD**, Associate Dean Academic, Faculty of Science and Co\-Chair, Academic Integrity Working Group\.
