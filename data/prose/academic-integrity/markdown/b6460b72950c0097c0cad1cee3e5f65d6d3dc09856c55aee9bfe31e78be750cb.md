**Learning Modules**

# Check out our academic integrity learning modules**\.**

Access our selection of academic integrity learning modules\. These modules can be accessed and used by instructors to help promote academic integrity in their courses or can be accessed directly by students to learn about the various aspects of academic integrity in courses and beyond\.

### Vancouver Modules

[https://canvas\.ubc\.ca/enroll/KGMND4](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/KGMND4)[Image](https://academic-integrity-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/foundations-1.jpg)

### [Academic Integrity Foundations](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/KGMND4)

**A Canvas\-based course designed by the Academic Integrity Hub\.**

This online course introduces academic integrity, why it is important within the UBC community, how students can put it into practice, what pitfalls to watch out for, and where to go for help\.

[ENROLL HERE](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/AN7RPL)
