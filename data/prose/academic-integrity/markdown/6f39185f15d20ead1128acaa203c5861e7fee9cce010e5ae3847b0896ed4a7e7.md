**Education and Outreach**

# Fostering awareness at UBC\.

Explore our materials for promoting academic integrity and use them in your classes or units\. Reach out the academic integrity staff who are also available to deliver many of these materials on your behalf\.

### Academic Integrity Workshops

At UBC Vancouver, the Academic Integrity Hub delivers workshops to students, instructors, and staff\. We offer standard workshops and can customize a session to the needs of your team\. Our workshops can cover academic integrity concepts and resources tailored to the specific context, for example instructors in a particular Faculty, or graduate students in a particular program\.

If you are interested in having the Academic Integrity Hub deliver a workshop for your team or unit, please complete our [workshop request form below](https://academicintegrity.ubc.ca/awareness/#request)\.

At UBC Okanagan, workshops can be directly requested at [aim\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:aim.ok@ubc.ca) or the [Student Learning Hub](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/learning-hub/faculty-resources/academic-integrity/)\.

### In\-class Presentation Materials

[Announcement icon](https://sandbox-academic-integrity-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/07/awareness-on-white.png)

**Want to promote academic integrity in your class? We can help\!**

*Take Five for Academic Integrity* is an in\-class awareness campaign that provides materials for instructors to use for a 10\-15 minute presentation and discussion with their students\. Instructors are invited to take a few minutes to check in on these concepts with their students and how they are applied in their course\. Discussions about academic integrity are not only for first\-year students or the first class of the term – checking in with students regularly can support their understanding\.

**Instructor presentation**

1.  Download the Take Five PowerPoint slides and accompanying notes below\. You may also wish to share the video with your students\.
2.  Tailor the slides to fit your class which might include adding examples and points that are relevant in your specific class and discipline\.
3.  Present it to your class\!

**Academic integrity staff presentation**

1.  Fill out the form below to request an in\-class visit\.
2.  Include any specific questions that you may have\.
3.  Academic integrity staff will visit your class and deliver the presentation for you\.
