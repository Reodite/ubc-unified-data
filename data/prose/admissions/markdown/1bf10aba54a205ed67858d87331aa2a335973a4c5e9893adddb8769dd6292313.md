Look for an email from UBC with the subject line “Your UBC Application – Next Steps” that outlines which documents you are required to submit, how you can submit them, and when you’re required to submit them\. Once you have applied, you can check for these instructions in the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/)\. Please do not submit documents prior to completing your UBC application\.

If your deadline falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or Canadian statutory holiday, your documents will be due the next business day\. Documents received after your deadline will be accepted as long as your school has requested them before that date\. If you applied to a program that closed after January 15, your deadline might be later than those listed below\. Please check your “Your UBC Application – Next Steps” email for details\.

## Canadian high school students

#### British Columbia and Yukon

If you applied directly from high school, you’ll need to authorize UBC to receive your grades from the BC Ministry of Education\. To do so, you must [create a BCeID account](https://www.bceid.ca/), and then follow the instructions at the [student transcripts website](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/transcripts-and-certificates)\.

When you order your transcript, select the option to “send my transcript now and allow this post\-secondary institution to request transcript updates\.” Choose the default date \(one year from the date of your order\) so that UBC receives your grades throughout the year\.

Do not send a paper copy of your transcript or report card\.

## Where to submit your documents

Where you submit your documents depends on whether you are asking to submit an unofficial document or an official document\. An official document is one sent directly from your school or a designated third\-party verification service\. An unofficial document comes directly from a student\.

Refer to the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) regularly to check which types of documents you need to submit\.

**If you are asked to submit an unofficial document you can:**

-   upload it into the Applicant Service Centre
-   send it by [email](mailto:final.grades@ubc.ca) to UBC

**If you are asked to submit an official document you can ask your school to:**

-   send it by [email](mailto:final.grades@ubc.ca)
-   use an approved third\-party delivery platform such as Clearinghouse, MyCreds, Scoir, or Parchment\. \(Please ensure that you have your documents directed to Undergraduate Admissions\)\.
-   send it by mail\. Documents for undergraduate applications to the Okanagan and Vancouver campuses may be sent to:

The University of British Columbia  
Undergraduate Admissions  
2016 – 1874 East Mall  
Vancouver, BC, Canada V6T 1Z1

Note that official documents need to be sent directly by your school to UBC \(electronically or in an official envelope\)\. Students cannot submit an official document\.

UBC will only review requested documents\. If you send documents that were not requested, it will delay our evaluation of your application\.

## Wondering if UBC has received your documents?

It will take 4 to 6 weeks for us to process your documents and indicate that we have received them in the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/)\. Once you have submitted your documents, know we will honour the date it was submitted\. Thank you for your patience\!

**UBC encourages the submission of transcripts and documents electronically wherever possible\.**

## Irreplaceable documents

Documents submitted in support of an application become the property of UBC and will not be returned unless they are irreplaceable \(e\.g\. graduation certificates\)\. If you are submitting an irreplaceable document, please [contact us](https://you.ubc.ca/contact-us/) to make arrangements for delivery to UBC, and complete and submit the form below with your documents so we can return them once UBC’s evaluation of your application is complete\.
