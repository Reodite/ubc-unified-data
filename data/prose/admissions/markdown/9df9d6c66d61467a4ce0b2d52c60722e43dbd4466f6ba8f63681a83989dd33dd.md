## Life on UBC’s Vancouver Campus

With popular beaches, international food, and a lively social scene, the campus is so easy to live on that some students say they hardly need to leave\.

UBC’s Vancouver campus is a vibrant community with almost 32,000 undergraduate students\. It feels like its own little city but it’s within easy reach of beaches, downtown, and mountains\. You can go from exploring a rainforest trail in [Pacific Spirit Regional Park](http://www.vancouvertrails.com/trails/pacific-spirit-regional-park/), to relaxing on Jericho Beach, to shopping on [Robson Street](http://robsonstreet.ca/) — all in one day\.

[Explore Vancouver Campus](http://you.ubc.ca/ubc-life/vancouver/)

## Life on UBC’s Okanagan campus

UBC’s Okanagan campus is an active, young community nestled among orchards, mountains, and beautiful lakes\. It’s an amazing place to live, learn, play, and grow\.

The Okanagan campus is a close\-knit community with over 7,000 undergraduate [students](http://essaylab.org/admission-essay/)\. That means you’ll see plenty of familiar faces in and around our new state\-of\-the\-art facilities\. And with all of the recreational and cultural opportunities nearby, there’s never a shortage of great things to do\.

[Explore Okanagan Campus](http://you.ubc.ca/ubc-life/okanagan/)
