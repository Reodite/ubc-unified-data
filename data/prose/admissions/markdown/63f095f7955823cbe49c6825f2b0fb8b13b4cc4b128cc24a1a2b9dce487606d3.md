### Health and Wellness

When you live well, you learn well\. Your mental and physical well\-being is key to your academic success and UBC is committed to supporting campus initiatives and resources that promote student well\-being\. Peer\-led workshops, campaigns, and online resources help build health awareness and skill development, while campus\-wide programs and services allow for early identification of difficulties and connection to support when help is needed\. UBC also provides easily accessible health services to assist students with mental and physical health concerns\.

All students have convenient access to health clinics on campus\. International students are required to have basic medical insurance coverage for the duration of their studies\.

Health care and health insurance at UBC: [Vancouver](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?tree=14,298,0,0) \| [Okanagan](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/okanagan/index.cfm?tree=6,307,907,0)

### Counselling Services

Confidential counselling can help you deal with concerns or problem that are creating problems for you and interfering with your ability to be successful at university\. Trained cousellors will help you find new ways of dealing with your concerns and work with you to create achievable goals\.

### Safety

Among North American cities, Vancouver and Kelowna are some of the safest, with a very high quality of life\. The University strives to make sure that students can live, study, and play on campus safely through programs such as Safewalk and the blue phones\.

### Religion

As a public university, UBC is a secular institution, however students are welcome to practice a range of faiths\. Opportunities for religious observance are available through the UBC Chaplains, multi\-faith spaces, and student\-led groups\.

Vancouver: [UBC Chaplains](http://chaplains.students.ubc.ca/)
