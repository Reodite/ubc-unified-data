## On\-campus services

### Health services

As a UBC student, you will have access to a wide range of health and wellness support services at [UBC Okanagan](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/health-wellness/) and [UBC Vancouver](https://students.ubc.ca/health) should you need medical attention, mental health support, or health information and advice\.

### Disability support

If you have a disability or medical condition, including mental illness, that could affect your full participation at UBC, reach out to the [Disability Resource Centre](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/disability-resources/) \(UBC Okanagan\) or [Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility) \(UBC Vancouver\) before you arrive\. You may be eligible for accommodations and initiatives designed to remove barriers to your academic success\.

## Health insurance

Your health coverage plan includes three main components:

-   **The BC Medical Services Plan \(MSP\)**\. MSP covers medically required services provided by MSP\-enrolled physicians, maternity care, and dental and oral surgery\. It is run by the BC government and enrollment is **not** automatic\.  
-   **iMed temporary insurance for international students\.** iMed covers emergency hospitalization and medical services, including visits to the doctor for sickness or injury during your first 3 months in BC while you wait to be eligible for MSP\. It is run by a private health insurance company and enrollment is automatic\.
-   **Extended health and dental plan \(UBCSUO or AMS/GSS plan\)\.** The extended health and dental plan provides extended health and dental coverage, beyond what’s provided by MSP\. It is run by student unions at UBC Okanagan or UBC Vancouver and enrollment is automatic\.

### All students

You will be automatically enrolled in the extended health and dental plan offered by the [UBC Students’ Union Okanagan \(UBCSUO\)](https://www.ubcsuo.ca/health-dental) at UBC Okanagan or the [Alma Mater Society/Graduate Student Society \(AMS/GSS\)](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/student-services/health-dental/) at UBC Vancouver when you register for courses – unless you’re an exchange student, in which case you must opt in\. Review the healthcare fees listed in your [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) account to determine if you are covered\.

You must always have basic insurance, such as MSP or iMED, in addition to the extended health and dental plan\. 

If you’re already covered by an extended health and dental plan, you may be able to opt out of the UBCSUO or AMS/GSS plan until the third week of classes\. For more information, contact [service@studentcare\.net](mailto:service@studentcare.net)\.

### Canadian students from outside BC

If you’re already covered by another Canadian provincial health plan, you do not need to apply for MSP\.  

If you do not have a Canadian provincial health plan, such as you are coming to Canada from abroad, you must [**apply for MSP**](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-details-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international/apply-bc-medical-services-plan-msp) as soon as you arrive in BC\. You will need temporary insurance to cover your first 3 months in BC while you wait to become eligible for MSP, such as the [**Global Campus Health Plan**](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/global-campus-health-plan/)\.  

Learn more about [health insurance for domestic students](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-domestic/)\. 

### International students who are new to UBC

MSP is legally required for anyone who is planning to live in BC for longer than six months\. Be sure to [**apply for MSP**](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-details-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international/apply-bc-medical-services-plan-msp) as soon as you arrive in BC\. During the three\-month period before your MSP coverage takes effect, you’ll be covered by [**iMED**](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international/) temporary insurance\. You will be automatically enrolled in iMED when you register for courses at UBC\.  

Learn more about [health insurance for international students](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/)\.  

### One\-term international exchange students

As a one\-term international exchange student, you are not required to apply for MSP\. When you register for courses at UBC, you will be automatically enrolled in [iMED](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international/), which will cover you for the duration of your studies at UBC\.
