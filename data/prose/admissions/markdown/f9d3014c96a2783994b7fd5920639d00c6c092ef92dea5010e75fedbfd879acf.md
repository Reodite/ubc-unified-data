Transitioning from high school to university can seem daunting at first\. To make the move as easy as possible, UBC offers a selection of first\-year study options designed to help you build a community, take the stress out of registering for classes, and offer greater access to instructors\.

First\-year study options are available in a number of faculties, and each has its own requirements and curricula\. You can choose a first\-year study option in place of creating your own custom timetable\.

## Options for Arts students

After you’ve been accepted into the Bachelor of Arts degree at UBC Vancouver, you’ll have to decide which classes to take when registration opens in **June**\. If you choose to join a first\-year study option, you’ll enjoy a predesigned course schedule and you’ll take nearly all of your classes with the same people\.

There are two to choose from\. Both offer an integrated curriculum that covers many different disciplines, such as English, Philosophy, and Political Science\.

### Arts One

[Arts One](https://artsone.arts.ubc.ca/) is a single, integrated course led by five instructors who work together to create the assignments and a reading list based on the year’s theme\. You’ll read and discuss classical and contemporary texts and their influence on culture and society, including novels, philosophical and political works, films, drama, graphic memoirs, and more\.

-   Attend lectures led by one of your professors once a week\.
-   Participate in twice\-weekly seminar discussions \(20 students\) led by one instructor\.
-   Enjoy once\-weekly tutorials, where you and three classmates will read and evaluate each other’s essays\.

#### **Coordinated Arts Program**

In the [Coordinated Arts Program](https://cap.arts.ubc.ca/) \(CAP\), you’ll get to choose from one of [five streams](https://cap.arts.ubc.ca/our-streams/), each offering a different combination of courses from across the Faculty of Arts\. The stream topics for 2021/2022 were Globalization, Power and Society; Individual and Society; Law and Society; Politics, Philosophy, and Economics; and Media Studies, and allow students and faculty to discuss ideas from various perspectives\.

-   CAP courses are separate, but faculty work together to connect the concepts and issues you’ll study and to make sure your deadlines won’t overlap\.
-   CAP classes are smaller than if you choose to create your own custom timetable, ranging from 25 students in the writing course to around 100 to 125 students in a lecture\.

**How to register**

Registration for both Arts One and CAP is first\-come, first\-served\. Both programs simplify the process by offering easy\-to\-use forms on their websites, or you can register directly in Workday when first\-year registration opens in June\. Find out more about [registering for Arts One](https://artsone.arts.ubc.ca/program/registration/ "https://artsone.arts.ubc.ca/program/registration/") and [registering for CAP](https://cap.arts.ubc.ca/program/registration/ "https://cap.arts.ubc.ca/program/registration/") on their websites\.

**Looking for more information?** Read what it’s like to be an [Arts One student](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/news/choosing-arts-one/ "https://www.arts.ubc.ca/news/choosing-arts-one/") and a [Coordinated Arts Program student](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/news/choosing-the-coordinated-arts-program-cap/ "https://www.arts.ubc.ca/news/choosing-the-coordinated-arts-program-cap/") and why students chose each program\.

## Options for Science students

Science students at UBC Vancouver can choose between creating their own timetable, requesting a [Course\-Bundle Early Registration](https://science.ubc.ca/students/registration/bundles), or [Science One](https://scienceone.ubc.ca/) which offers courses integrating Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics\. Science One provides small class sizes, dedicated study spaces, and lower student\-to\-instructor ratios\.

Science One will allow you to build a strong foundation in science, be part of a tight\-knit community with your classmates, and take some of the stress out of registering for classes\.

[medical laboratory science, ubc](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/Medical-Laboratory-Science-768x511.jpg.webp)

### Course Bundle Early Registration

[Course Bundle Early Registration](https://science.ubc.ca/students/registration/bundles) is a registration option that allows first\-year science students to request to be registered into a bundle of first\-year courses before regular registration opens\. With Course Bundle Early Registration, you will be registered to take a number of first\-year science courses together with the same group of other Science students who have requested the same courses\.

By requesting a course bundle, you simplify your registration process, and your course bundle will make it easier for you to connect with new friends in multiple courses\.

#### **Science One**

[Science One](https://scienceone.ubc.ca/) will allow you to build a strong foundation in science, be part of a tight\-knit community with your classmates, and take some of the stress out of registering for classes\.

Science One can be challenging\. It has a competitive application process, an intense schedule, and teaches first\-year science at one of the highest levels at UBC to a tight\-knit group of students\.

-   You’ll be one of just 75 individuals, and will be supported by eight to nine instructors\.
-   All Science One teachers attend each other’s classes to dynamically shape your curriculum
-   Share your own work and study space with your instructors\.
-   Take part in weekly tutorials, learn from guest lecturers, and have extra instruction in science literacy and computer programming\.
-   Be mentored on two major research projects – the results of which can be published in undergraduate journals\.
-   Attend student conferences and enjoy two field trips\.

Science One is also a social experience\. You’ll help elect student representatives to sit in on the Science One student council and the Science Undergraduate Society \(SUS\), and take part in alumni\-led mentorship and social activities such as the Winter Formal and Pie Day events\.

##### How to apply

If you want to join Science One, you’ll need to [submit an application](https://scienceone.ubc.ca/apply) at the same time or shortly after you [apply to UBC](https://ssc.adm.ubc.ca/sscportal/apply.xhtml)’s Bachelor of Science degree, as registration opens on **November 1**\. Applications received after **April 30** may be considered, but not with the same priority as those received before that date\.

## Options for Forestry, Applied Biology, and Food, Nutrition, and Health students

If you have been accepted into the [Forestry](https://forestry.ubc.ca/) or [Land and Food Systems](https://www.landfood.ubc.ca/) faculties, you are eligible to apply for the [Land One](https://landone.ubc.ca/) first\-year study option\.

[Image](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/770x350-Corn-and-sqaush-Mayan-Garden-768x349.jpg.webp)

### Land One

Land One includes a selection of core courses in Biology, Economics, Math, and English, as well as an integrative seminar\. You’ll take all of these key classes with the 50 to 60 students who are admitted to the program\.

Land One’s seminar course covers a range of issues related to land use, climate change, food security, and sustainability\.

-   Discuss and address how to solve these problems
-   Visit the UBC Farm to learn more about sustainable food systems, and count the Museum of Anthropology as one of your learning spaces
-   Participate in a two\-day field trip to UBC’s Malcolm Knapp Research Forest, where you’ll experience the coastal old\-growth rainforest first\-hand\.

#### How to apply

You will need to be accepted by your Forestry or Land and Food Systems degree program, and then [submit an online application](https://landone.ubc.ca/apply-now/) through Land One’s website\. Applications open in the spring, and the deadline is **May 31\.** Enrolment in Land One is limited by size, so apply early\!

## Options for English language instruction \(Vantage One\)

UBC’s [Vantage One programs](https://vantagecollege.ubc.ca/) are designed to allow international students who do not yet meet the [English language requirements](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/english-language-competency/) to enter directly into a UBC faculty\. Vantage One courses last 11 months, and add English language instruction to first\-year degree courses\. You can choose from [Arts](https://vantagecollege.ubc.ca/arts), [Engineering](https://vantagecollege.ubc.ca/engineering), or [Science](https://vantagecollege.ubc.ca/science), and you’ll be taught by award\-winning UBC faculty members\.

[vantage one, science, ubc](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/Vantage-One-Science-768x432.jpg.webp)

### Arts, Science, or Engineering Vantage One

The Arts and Science Vantage One programs take place at UBC Vancouver, while Engineering involves studying at both [UBC Vancouver](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc-life/vancouver/) and [UBC Okanagan](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc-life/okanagan/)\. When you successfully complete your Vantage One program, you’ll progress into your second year at UBC, where you can study the following degree programs:

-   [Bachelor of Arts](https://you.ubc.ca/programs/#mode=by-topic&amp;viewMode=grid&amp;filters%5Bsearch%5D=Bachelor%20of%20Arts) \(at UBC Vancouver\)
-   [Bachelor of Science](https://you.ubc.ca/programs/#mode=by-topic&amp;viewMode=grid&amp;filters%5Bsearch%5D=Bachelor%20of%20Science) \(at UBC Vancouver\)
-   [Bachelor of Applied Science](https://you.ubc.ca/programs/#mode=by-topic&amp;viewMode=grid&amp;filters%5Bsearch%5D=Bachelor%20of%20Applied%20Science%20%28Engineering%29) \(at either UBC Okanagan or UBC Vancouver\)

Vantage One accepts students with a minimum score of 70 on the TOEFL and 5\.5 on the IELTS\. Certain sub\-scores apply for each test, so be sure to review the [full English language requirements](https://vantagecollege.ubc.ca/admission-requirements) on the Vantage College website\.

#### How to apply

[Applications open](https://vantagecollege.ubc.ca/how-apply) in **early September**, and the deadline is **January 15**\. If you wish to be considered for the [Vantage One Excellence Award](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-international-students/international-scholars/), you must submit your application before **November 15**\.
