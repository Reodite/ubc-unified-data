If you are an Indigenous person \(a member of First Nations, Métis, and/or Inuit peoples in Canada\), you’ll have the chance to join us at a number of events that will introduce you to UBC and its Indigenous community\. Take part in fun workshops led by our Indigenous recruiters, get first\-hand tips on how to create a strong application, and learn more about what it’s like to be an Indigenous student at UBC\.

These events will help you navigate the application process and learn more about our two campuses, and will be hosted either in\-person or virtually via Zoom\.

## Your fall events

We offer a number of events and workshops to introduce you to UBC and its Indigenous community\.

### Indigenous Campus Tours

Meet an Indigenous student ambassador who will highlight their favourite locations on either of our two campuses, show you Indigenous\-specific spaces, and help you navigate programs and resources for Indigenous students\. After the tour, an Indigenous Recruiter Advisor will be available to answer your questions\.

### All About UBC for Indigenous Students

Learn about UBC’s two campuses, the academic programs they each offer, and what life on campus looks like\. You’ll discover when and how to apply to UBC, get help on financial planning, and find out about the resources and services available to Indigenous students\.

### Indigenous UBC Application Workshop

Our Indigenous Recruiter Advisors will walk you through a demonstration of UBC’s online application, and provide tips for writing a strong personal profile\. Your parents or guardians are welcome to attend\.

### Indigenous Finances at UBC Workshop

Learn about UBC’s Indigenous\-specific awards and deadlines, and get tips for submitting your awards applications\. Your parents or guardians are welcome to attend\.

## How to register for an event

[Image](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/UBC_20210903_PJ_9493-768x511.jpg)

1.  Visit our [tours and events page](https://account.you.ubc.ca/s/events)
2.  Click “[create account](https://account.you.ubc.ca/s/create)” or [log in](https://account.you.ubc.ca/s/login/) using the buttons in the top right hand corner\. When you create an account, make sure you self\-identify as Indigenous\.
3.  Based on your profile, we’ll show you a list of all the events available to you – just click the category you’d like to browse or search “Indigenous” to see events specifically for Indigenous students\.
4.  To register, simply click the event card and follow the instructions to reserve your spot\.

After you’ve created your account, you can find all of the events you’re registered for any time on the [Your account](https://account.you.ubc.ca/s/) page\.

**Please note:** it may take up to 24 hours after creating your account for the system to allow you to register for events\. If, after 24 hours, you’re still having issues with registering for events, please reach out to [indigenous\.recruitment@ubc\.ca](mailto:indigenous.recruitment@ubc.ca)\.

To register for an Indigenous campus tour, please email [indigenous\.tours@ubc\.ca](mailto:indigenous.tours@ubc.ca)\.
