## How to accept your offer

1

Log in to the Applicant Service Centre\. Confirm that your address is correct by clicking **Edit Profile** and selecting **Contact Summary***\.*

2

Next, click **Admissions**, then select **Accept or** **Decline Offer**\.

3

Select **Yes, I accept this offer**\.

4

Follow the steps to pay your deposit\. The fee is $500 for Canadian citizens and permanent residents and $3,000 for students on a study permit\. You must pay your deposit with a credit card\.

[Accept your offer](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/)

## Offer information

### Conditional offers

Your spot at UBC is subject to the conditions outlined in your letter of admission\. It’s important that you maintain your grades and submit your final documents to confirm your offer\. Any significant changes in your academic standing could lead to [your offer being reassessed](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/admitted/keep-offer/)\.

### If you applied to two degrees

If you’re still waiting to hear back about your first choice, check the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) regularly for status updates\. If you receive offers to both your first\- and second\-choice degrees, you can choose either one\.

### Deferring your offer

Under certain circumstances, UBC will consider deferring your offer of admission by one year \(or two years for mandatory military service\) if you’re joining UBC as a first\-year undergraduate\. For more information, review our [deferral eligibility requirements and deadlines](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/admitted/deferring-admission/)\.

Have more questions? Your campus advisor is here to help\.

### Your Enrolment Services Advisor at UBC Okanagan

As a student at UBC Okanagan, you’ll have access to Enrolment Services Advisors\. These advisors are there to help you decipher university policy, connect you with resources, and help you plan your finances\. Contact Enrolment Services to get help with all things related to your finances: student loans, tuition, &amp; creating a spending plan\.

 [https://students\.ok\.ubc\.ca/courses\-money\-enrolment/advising/](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/advising/)

### Your Enrolment Services Advisor at UBC Vancouver

As a student at UBC Vancouver, you’ll be paired with an Enrolment Services Advisor shortly after you accept your offer\. Find your Enrolment Services Advisor in Workday by navigating to the **Student** menu, opening the **Academics** app, then clicking the **Support** tab\. Ask them for help with planning your finances, registering for courses, and finding resources online and on campus\.

 [https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/enrolment\-services\-advisors/](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)

### Academic Advisors

Our campuses have Academic Advisors who can help with course planning, degree requirements, and transfer credits\.

Connect with an Academic Advisor on the [Okanagan campus](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/advising-options/academic-advising/) or the [Vancouver campus\.](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/)
