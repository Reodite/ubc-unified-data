Sign up to be considered for research studies to help improve you\.ubc\.ca\.

We regularly run research studies to improve the experience for those who visit you\.ubc\.ca\. You can sign up to express interest in participating in these research studies\. Participants will help shape you\.ubc\.ca\.

We’re looking for students who are between the ages of 16\-21 who do not attend UBC to help us do short research activities\.

[Take the survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_cwQ92eetGxDU5Rs) to express interest in participating\.

Research participants are asked to participate in activities like:

-   Surveys
-   Completing tasks online while talking through your approach

Responses and recordings are kept confidential as they are for research purposes only\. In general the participant spends about 30 minutes on the research activity \(but time varies depending on the study\)\.

Once you complete a website study, UBC will thank you with **a gift card** \(in Canadian dollars\) for each session you participate in\.

Participating in website research studies will not impact your UBC application\.
