## Getting to and from campus

UBC is a 20\-minute bus ride from downtown Vancouver\. The campus is served by 13 bus routes with connections to destinations throughout the Greater Vancouver area\. Every full\-time UBC Vancouver student receives a reasonably priced [U\-Pass](http://www.upass.ubc.ca/upass/upassindex.html) that provides unlimited travel on bus, SkyTrain, and SeaBus\. The cost of the pass is included in your University fees\.

Check out UBC’s [Trek program](http://www.trek.ubc.ca/) for information on transportation options, including cycling and carpooling\.

## Off\-campus housing resources

If you are looking for off\-campus housing near UBC, there are many resources on the [UBC Student Housing and Hospitality Services website](http://www.housing.ubc.ca/off-campus-housing "UBC Off-campus Housing") that can help you in your search\.

## On\-campus hostel

As a commuter, you may find that there are times when it would be easier to spend the night on campus rather than head home\. Perhaps you have a late evening club meeting, or maybe you have an exam early the next morning and you want to stay late to study in the UBC Library\. For those occasions, the [Commuter Student Hostel](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/other-housing/commuter-student-hostel/) offers rooms for just $30 a night\. You’ll be able to spend the night on campus and wake up ready for whatever the next day holds\.

## Orientation and transition

Whether you live on\- or off\-campus, when you become a student at UBC, you join a community\. On your first day at UBC, all new students take part in Imagine UBC, an exciting day\-long event where you’ll meet new friends and get connected with campus services and resources\.

Before and beyond your first day, we’ll work hard to keep you in the loop about how to be an active part of the UBC community\. All new students receive [New to UBC](http://blog.students.ubc.ca/newtoubc/), a bi\-weekly e\-newsletter with information about upcoming events and opportunities on campus\.

[Connections magazine](http://www.students.ubc.ca/newtoubc/download/commuter_student_connections.pdf) is published in the fall to help commuter students adjust to UBC\.

Your studies at UBC will also be a transition for your family\. [Commuter Student Parent Orientation](http://www.students.ubc.ca/parents/orientation.cfm?page=parent) is a great way for family members to get acquainted with UBC and to prepare them for the changes you’ll be experiencing\.
