## Application changes or transfers

### Changing your application

If you have already submitted an application to UBC for the 2026/27 academic year and want to make changes to your application, please contact using this [online form](https://account.you.ubc.ca/s/ask-ubc)\. Do not submit a new application\.

### Current UBC students transferring to a different campus, faculty, or degree

If you are currently enrolled in a program at UBC and want to transfer to a different program/faculty, or if you’ve been away from UBC and now want to apply for re\-admission, you must do so on the [UBC Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/)\.

### Returning to UBC

If you have previously completed courses at UBC and are applying to any degree **except** the Bachelor of Education at UBC Vancouver **or** the Bachelor of Dental Hygiene – Degree Completion streams, please apply through the [UBC Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/)\. If you are applying to the Bachelor of Education at UBC Vancouver, please apply at [EducationPlannerBC](https://apply.educationplannerbc.ca/)\.

## After you’ve applied

After you apply to UBC, you are able to check your application status within a few days in our [UBC Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) or visit our [Undergraduate Programs and Admissions website](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/applied/) for more information on your next steps\.
