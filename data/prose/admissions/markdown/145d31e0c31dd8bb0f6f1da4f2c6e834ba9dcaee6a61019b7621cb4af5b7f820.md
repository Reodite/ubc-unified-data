## Do you need to work with an agent to apply to UBC?

No\. Students are encouraged to apply directly to UBC and use the information provided in our [Applying to UBC](http://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/) section\.

You – not the agent – are responsible for submitting your application to UBC on time, paying your fees, and ensuring you [meet the requirements](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/)\. You are also responsible for completing all the other necessary steps to be considered for admission\.

## What an agent can do for you

-   An agent can give you information about various schools and programs that will suit your academic goals\.
-   An agent can guide you through the application process \(i\.e\. where to set up an account, where to find information, key deadlines, etc\.\)\.
-   An agent can assist you with applying for your visa and study permit \(if they are certified by [ICCRC](https://college-ic.ca/)\)\.
-   An agent can assist you with transportation\.
-   An agent can give you information and tips on living and studying in Vancouver, Kelowna, and Canada\.
-   An agent can give you information on study costs and any available funding or scholarships\.

## What an agent cannot do for you

-   Working with an agent does not increase your chances of admission or of obtaining a scholarship at UBC\. All students are assessed on the merit of their grades and overall application\.
-   An agent cannot write or assist in writing your personal profile or any part of your application\.
-   An agent cannot submit false records or documents\.
-   An agent cannot access your UBC records without your consent\. You must complete a Third\-Party Authorization for your agent to have access to your UBC information in the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/)\.
-   An agent cannot submit payments for any UBC\-related fees on your behalf\.

## Other points to consider when working with an agent

-   Look for evidence that the agent specializes in Canadian higher education\.
-   Does your agent give you information on a broad enough variety of institutions?
-   Is your agent suggesting institutions and programs that fit your academic goals?
-   Does your agent have the qualifications to assist you with your Canadian visa and study permit?

**Please note:** working with an agent is your decision and it will not affect your status as an applicant to UBC\.
