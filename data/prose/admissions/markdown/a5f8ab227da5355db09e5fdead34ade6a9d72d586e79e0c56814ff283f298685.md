## Your admission requirements

Admission to UBC is competitive, and your requirements change depending on your degree and campus of choice\. All applicants must meet UBC’s general admission requirements and English Language Admission Standard\.

[Canadian high school applicant requirements](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/canadian-high-schools)  
[University or college student applicant requirements](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/university-college-transfer/)[Mature student applicant requirements](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/mature-students/)

## Scholarships and awards for Canadian students

The following awards are available to Canadian students in their first year:

-   [Awards for Indigenous students](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-canadian-students/indigenous-students/ "Awards for Aboriginal Students")

-   [Loran Awards](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-canadian-students/loran-awards/)
-   [Presidential Scholars Awards](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-canadian-students/presidential-scholars/)
