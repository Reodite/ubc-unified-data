## Veterans Education and Training Benefit

The Government of Canada’s [Veterans Education and Training Benefit](http://www.veterans.gc.ca/eng/services/transition/education-training-benefit) is designed to help Canadian veterans achieve their education and career goals\. If you’re a Canadian veteran, you may be eligible to receive up to $80,000 in funding toward a UBC degree\.

### Are you eligible?

To be eligible for the Veterans Education and Training Benefit:

-   You must have been honourably released from the Canadian Armed Forces \(Regular Forces or Reserves\) on or after April 1, 2006\.
-   You must have served for a minimum of six years\.

The value of the benefit will vary depending on your length of service: $40,000 for at least six years of service and $80,000 for at least 12 years of service\.

### How do you apply?

Choose a degree, review your admission requirements, and [apply to UBC](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/how-to-apply/) by **January 15**\. Your admission requirements will vary, depending on whether you’re a [mature student](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/mature-students/) or a [transfer student](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/university-college-transfer/) from another university or college\.

Once you receive an offer of admission from UBC, [submit a Veterans Education and Training Benefit application](http://www.veterans.gc.ca/eng/services/transition/education-training-benefit#04) to Veteran Affairs Canada\.

### Questions?

For more information on eligibility requirements, how the program works, and when payments are disbursed, please visit the [Veteran Affairs Canada website](http://www.veterans.gc.ca/eng/services/transition/education-training-benefit)\.

If you have further questions or need assistance, contact an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors) \(Vancouver campus\) or email [sis\.ubco@ubc\.ca](mailto:sis.ubco@ubc.ca) \(Okanagan campus\)\.
