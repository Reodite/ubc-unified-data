Whether you are a first\-year student, a student coming here from the other side of the globe, an Aboriginal student, or a transfer student, we provide an [orientation program](http://students.ubc.ca/campus/orient-yourself-campus-life/orientations/undergraduate-students "UBC - Orientation on Vancouver Campus") to get your UBC experience off to a great start\.

## All UBC students

New student orientations will help you make new friends, meet with student leaders and professors, get to know more about your faculty, and learn all about the services we offer to support you, as well as the many ways you can get involved on campus\. Learn more about each campus’s unique orientations\.

**Vancouver Campus: Imagine UBC**

[Imagine UBC](http://www.students.ubc.ca/newtoubc/orientations.cfm?page=imagine) is the main orientation program at UBC’s Vancouver campus, is one of the largest in Canada and is run by students for students\. This campus\-wide orientation program is held every year on the Tuesday after Labour Day\. It replaces the first day of classes\. All UBC students – new, returning, and transfer students – attend Imagine UBC\.  And Imagine UBC doesn’t end after just one day\. Events and email newsletters throughout the year will help you get the most out of your first two terms at UBC\.

**Okanagan Campus: Create UBC** 

[Create UBC](http://web.ubc.ca/okanagan/students/newtoubc/orientations/create.html) is the new student orientation program at UBC’s Okanagan campus and is held on the first day of classes\. It’s a day\-long series of events that will introduce you to the wide range of programs and services available to ensure your success\. You’ll make new friends, meet with student leaders and professors, and learn about how you can get involved on campus\.

## International students

### Vancouver Campus

There are two main programs at UBC’s Vancouver campus provided to help international students transition to life in Canada\.

**Jump Start**  
[Jump Start](http://students.ubc.ca/campus/get-oriented/attend-orientation/undergrads/jumpstart/register) is a first\-year transition program for new undergraduate international students\. It begins with a two\-week immersion program in August that will introduce you to the university, academic life and, most importantly, your faculty\. It continues from September through to April to guide you through your first year of studies, providing ongoing academic coaching and peer\-to\-peer support\.

International students who are not able to arrive in Vancouver in time to participate in the two\-week immersion program will still be integrated into the year\-long academic programming once they arrive at UBC\.

**International Welcome**  
[International Welcome](http://students.ubc.ca/campus/orient-yourself-campus-life/orientations/undergraduate-students/international-undergraduates) is held in September, January, and May for new international exchange, visiting, and transfer students\. In attending International Welcome, you’ll learn everything you need to know about study permits, entry visas, health insurance, employment, and the services and resources offered on campus\.

### Okanagan Campus

Each year we run two programs to help prepare international students for life at UBC’s Okanagan campus\.

**Jumpstart**  
[Jumpstart](http://web.ubc.ca/okanagan/students/newtoubc/orientations/jumpstart.html) will provide you with the chance to settle in before the start of classes\. You’ll enjoy preparation sessions focused on Canadian academic culture, plus the opportunity to build your social and academic support network to help you succeed in your first year\. There are also exciting adventures in and around Kelowna\.

**International Student Information Session**  
The [International Student Information Session](http://students.ok.ubc.ca/newtoubc/orientations/international.html) covers practical matters such as medical insurance and study permits, and includes information on various services for international students\.

## Parents

At UBC, we recognize the critical role parents play in helping their children make the right post\-secondary choice and then supporting them as they pursue their dreams\. Parents with students living in residence should pick up a Rez Parent Guide when their student moves in\. There are also useful [resources for parents of residents](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/ "UBC - Housing ") online\.
