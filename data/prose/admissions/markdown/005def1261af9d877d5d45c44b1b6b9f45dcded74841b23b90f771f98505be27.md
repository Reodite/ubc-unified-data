### Job description

Student Ambassadors lead prospective students and their families on tours of the campus\. If you believe you’d be great at representing the positive, inclusive, and dynamic learning and living environment at UBC, take a look at the job description to learn more about the role\.

### How to apply

**Applications are now closed\.**

If you are interested in being notified of our next round of hiring, you may join the prospective Student Ambassador email list\. In order to do so:

1.  Send an email to [listserv@lists\.ubc\.ca](mailto:listserv@lists.ubc.ca)
2.  Include the following text in the body of the email: subscribe prospective\-ambassadors

You can also connect with our team on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ubc-vancouver-campus-tours/) and follow for future updates\.
