Going to university is not just about studying, it’s also a social adventure that, for many students, begins in “rez\.”

Each year, UBC houses almost 9,400 students in 12 residence complexes that circle the Vancouver campus\. You’ll find everything, from traditional\-style rooms and apartments to townhouses and condominium units, all designed to help you adjust to university and make connections on campus\.

And the location couldn’t be better: You’re surrounded by gardens, there’s a regional park next door, and the Pacific Ocean is within walking distance\.

## Residence options

Most first\-year students live in Winter Session Residence from September to April\. Rooms and suites come with basic furnishings and a high\-speed internet connection\. Most first\-year students live in a shared room, although you may be assigned a single room\. Residence areas include [Totem Park,](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/totem-park/) [Place Vanier](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/place-vanier/), [Walter Gage](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/walter-gage/), [Fairview Crescent](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/fairview-crescent/), and [Ritsumeikan\-UBC House](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences/ritsumeikan-ubc-house/)\.

## Residence support

No matter which residence you live in, you will be part of a supportive environment that’s designed to help you adjust to university and enjoy your time as a student\. Residence Advisors, current UBC students who have experience in residences and lots to offer younger students, live in your area and are plugged in to the resources and support that are available at UBC\.

When you hit a snag or need to vent, Residence Advisors can help\. They organize events that will connect you with fellow residents and campus opportunities, and help ensure you live in an environment that supports your academic success\. All residences also operate a front desk that is ready to help when the need arises\.

## Meal plans

Our campus [meal plans](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/dining/meal-plans/ "UBC - Residence Meal Plans") offer flexibility and tasty, nutritious options\. If you live in Totem Park or Place Vanier, it is mandatory to purchase a meal plan\. Students in other residences can purchase an optional campus dining plan for added savings and convenience at more than [30 Food Services outlets](http://www.food.ubc.ca/) around campus\.

Food Services can accommodate a variety of nutritional needs and health conditions that require a specific eating plan\. Please email them in advance if you have special dietary needs at [meal\.plan@ubc\.ca](mailto:meal.plan@ubc.ca)\.

## Residence costs

Your [costs will vary](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/) depending on what type of room you live in and what size of meal plan you choose\. A typical cost for a room and [meal plan](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/dining/meal-plans/) is between $6,500 and $8,500 for the eight\-month school year\. Payments are spread over the school year\.

## Applying for residence

Most first\-year students will be eligible for a [guaranteed room in residence](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/first-year-guarantee/)\. Every student coming to UBC’s Vancouver campus who will graduate from high school in 2015 can now live on campus and benefit from the exceptional academic and social support offered through our residence program\.

It’s best to start your residence application as soon as you apply for admission to UBC\. You can start your residence application when you [apply to UBC](https://account.you.ubc.ca/ubc/apply/apply_now.ezc), and then visit the [Student Housing and Hospitality Services \(SHHS\) Online Service Centre](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/ "UBC Student Housing and Hospitality Services (SHHS) Online Service Centre") to finish your residence application\. Or, you can apply for residence directly from the Student Housing and Hospitality Services website, if you didn’t start the process during your application for admission to UBC\.

If you are considering both the Vancouver and Okanagan campuses for your studies, apply to both residences at the same time and you will only pay the application fee once\.

The Winter Session Residence application deadline is **May 1**\. If you wait until you are given an offer of admission to UBC to apply for residence, you may not get a space\.

## After your first year

Residence is not guaranteed after your first year\. [Returning students](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/returning/) who want to live in residence are encouraged to apply for a space in Year Round Residence, which runs from May to April\.
