Why is UBC such a great place for students to learn, grow, and develop? The answer is simple: UBC combines a solid reputation for academic excellence with a flexible, student\-centred learning environment\. And our location doesn’t hurt either\.

Find out below why we’re one of the [world’s top 40 research universities](http://ubc.ca/global/index.html)\.

## Undergraduate Research

As a UBC student, you’ll have the opportunity to participate in hands\-on research and interdisciplinary collaborations\. Work side by side with faculty members who are experts in their fields, contribute to research projects with global significance, and pursue your own unique interests – no matter how diverse\.

Whether you’re actively looking for research opportunities, or want to learn more before you dive in, UBC has a variety of resources, groups, and awards to support you in your ambitions\.

-   Attend the [Multidisciplinary Undergraduate Research Conference](http://murc.ubc.ca/)
-   Apply for a [Research Award](http://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-experiences/undergraduate-research)
-   Join the [Undergraduate Research Opportunities Club](http://www.uroubc.ca/)
-   Apply for a paid [Work Learn](http://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-experiences/work-learn) opportunity if you’re studying in Vancouver, or a [Work Study](http://students.ok.ubc.ca/careers/services/jobs/workstudy.html) opportunity if you’re a student in the Okanagan\.

## Learning Support

Learning extends further than textbooks and lectures, which is why UBC offers a wide range of supports to help you achieve your best\. From advising and tutoring to new educational technology and facilities, you have access to the people,  tools, and spaces that will help you succeed\.

### Advising and tutoring

Whether you want advice on picking courses or need help understanding challenging course materials, get the support you need on a drop\-in or by\-appointment basis\. Meet with an academic advisor to discuss your academics, join a peer\-assisted study session to hone your studying skills, or attend a tutoring session led by a senior student in your field\. Learn more about advising and tutoring services offered at the [Vancouver](http://students.ubc.ca/) and [Okanagan](http://students.ok.ubc.ca/welcome.html) campuses\.

### Learning spaces

As any student who has spent long hours researching or studying can tell you, finding space that’s conducive to learning is crucial\. As a UBC student, you’ll have access to state\-of\-the\-art classrooms, libraries, facilities, and technologies\.

#### Libraries

The [UBC Library](http://www.library.ubc.ca/), one of the largest in Canada, is home to extensive collections and electronic resources, as well as helpful services like research tutorials and real\-time chat with librarians\. With over 21 branches located in Vancouver and the [Okanagan](http://www.library.ubc.ca/ubco/), you have free and unlimited access to world\-class educational materials wherever you’re studying\.

#### Learning &amp; Study Facilities

Both the Vancouver and Okanagan offer innovative learning spaces that combine information resources and services, and the latest in technology\. As a UBC student, you’ll have access to study spaces, group study rooms, wireless access, laptop lending programs, and classroom and lecture spaces at the [Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](http://www.ikebarberlearningcentre.ubc.ca/) in Vancouver and the UBC Okanagan [Information Commons](http://www.library.ubc.ca/ubco/InfoCommons.html)\.

## Co\-op Education Programs

UBC’s popular [co\-op program](http://www.coop.ubc.ca/) lets you combine your studies with full\-time, paid work experience\. After being accepted to the program, you could find yourself learning forest conservation in northern British Columbia, editing books in Toronto, or designing software in California\. Learn more about [UBC co\-op programs and availability](http://www.coop.ubc.ca/)\.

## Community Learning Initiatives

At UBC, you have an opportunity to put down your books and venture into the community to learn, connect with new people, and make positive social change\. With a variety of options in Vancouver, the Okanagan, and abroad, it’s easy to find a community experience that fits your academic and personal interests\.

Learn more about [Vancouver](http://students.ubc.ca/career/community-experiences) and [Okanagan](http://students.ok.ubc.ca/cslprogram/welcome.html) community opportunities\.
