## British\-patterned education

If you studied [British\-patterned education](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/international-high-schools/#united-kingdom) and got a final A\-Level grade of A or B in most academic science, math, and arts subjects, UBC will grant you first\-year credit\. If you got an A or B on an A\-Level\-examined subject not listed in the table below, UBC will determine your first\-year credit on a case\-by\-case basis\.
