## You’re preparing to apply to UBC

- **Row 1**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### Early October 2026
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Application opens
    
    -   Online application opens for Winter Session \(September 2027 to April 2028\) and Summer Session \(May to August 2027\)\. [Apply here\!](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/apply)

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### November 15, 2026
    
    \(11:59 p\.m\. PST\)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Scholarships and awards
    
    -   International applicants: Deadline to [apply to UBC](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/apply) and submit an award application if you’re an international student and you wish to be considered for the [UBC International Scholars Program awards](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-international-students/international-scholars "https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-international-students/international-scholars?openModal=2")\.

- **Row 3**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### December 1, 2026
    
    \(11:59 p\.m\. PST\)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Scholarships and awards
    
    -   Canadian citizens and permanent residents: Deadline to [apply to UBC](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/apply) if you wish to be considered for the [Presidential Scholars Awards](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-canadian-students/presidential-scholars/), [UBC Centennial Scholars Entrance Awards](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-canadian-students/entrance-award/), or the [Beyond Tomorrow Scholars Award\.](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-canadian-students/beyond-tomorrow/)

- **Row 4**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### January 15, 2027
    
    \(11:59 p\.m\. PST\)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Applications due
    
    -   Deadline to apply for Winter Session \(September 2027 to April 2028\)\.
    -   Deadline to apply for Summer Session \(May to August 2027\), unless you’re a [visiting, unclassified, or Access Studies student](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/#section-5)\.

## You’ve applied to UBC

- **Row 1**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### November 2026 through to June 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Admissions decisions
    
    -   Admissions decisions are made for high school applicants on a continuous basis\.

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### January through to July 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Admissions decisions
    
    -   Canadian post\-secondary applicants: Admissions decisions are made\.

- **Row 3**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### January 31, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### International Scholars Program applicants
    
    -   Meet [UBC’s English Language Admission Standard for direct\-entry](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/english-language-competency/), or the [English language requirement for UBC Vantage College](https://vantagecollege.ubc.ca/admission-requirements) if you have applied to a Vantage One program\.
    
    -   [Submit any documents](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/applied/submit-documents/) required for your admission to UBC\. Please submit by this deadline, not the date indicated in your “next steps” email\.

- **Row 4**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### February 15, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Interim transcripts
    
    -   Canadian post\-secondary applicants currently attending another institution: Deadline to [submit your official interim transcript](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/applied/submit-documents/#section-4)\. Interim transcripts are those that include final grades for courses completed to December, and that list any courses in\-progress from January – April\.

- **Row 5**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### February 15, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### ELAS
    
    -   Deadline to submit documents showing you meet the [English Language Admission Standard](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/english-language-competency/) \(ELAS\)\.

- **Row 6**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### March through to April 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Scholarship announcements
    
    -   [Presidential Scholars Award](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-canadian-students/presidential-scholars/)
    -   [Centennial Scholars Entrance Award](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-canadian-students/entrance-award/)
    -   [Beyond Tomorrow Scholars Award](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-canadian-students/beyond-tomorrow/)
    -   [International Major Entrance Scholarships](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-international-students)
    -   [Outstanding International Student Award](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-international-students)

- **Row 7**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### March 1 to March 15, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Grade submissions
    
    -   Canadian high school applicants outside of BC and Ontario: Upload your Spring transcript of grades including grades for distributed learning courses that are 50% complete to the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) for your UBC academic assessment\.

- **Row 8**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### March 15, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Grade submissions
    
    -   Students who graduated from a Canadian high school before applying to UBC: Deadline to submit your final grades\. All courses required for admission to UBC, including distributed learning courses, must be completed and appear with a final grade on an official Ministry of Education transcript by this date\. Upgraders are encouraged to submit confirmation of completed courses as soon as possible\.

- **Row 9**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### March 15, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Out\-of\-country documents
    
    -   Post\-secondary applicants from the United States: Deadline to [submit required documents](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/applied/submit-documents/#section-5) to UBC\.
    -   High school applicants from outside Canada: Deadline to [submit required documents](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/applied/submit-documents/#section-3) to UBC\.

- **Row 10**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### April 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Scholarship announcements
    
    -   [International Scholars Program award](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-international-students/international-scholars)

- **Row 11**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### May 31, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### International documents
    
    -   Out\-of\-country post\-secondary applicants \(except US applicants\): Deadline to submit all required documents\.

- **Row 12**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### June 30, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Course completion
    
    -   Canadian high school applicants: Deadline to complete all high school courses, including distance and distributed\-learning courses to meet the conditions of your offer of admission\.

- **Row 13**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### September 15, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Bursary applications
    
    -   Canadian citizens and permanent residents: Deadline to submit general bursary applications\.

## You’ve been admitted to UBC

- **Row 1**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### May 1, 2027 or June 1, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Accept your offer\!
    
    -   Deadline for most students to accept UBC’s offer of admission and pay the acceptance deposit\. Please refer to your letter of admission or the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) to find your assigned offer deadline date\.

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### As soon as you accept your offer of admission
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Study permits
    
    -   International applicants: Getting a study permit can take several months, so be sure to [apply for a study permit](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/admitted/study-permit/) as soon as you accept your offer of admission\.

- **Row 3**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### May 1, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Housing applications
    
    -   Application deadline for first\-year housing guarantee \(see [Vancouver housing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/first-year-guarantee/) or [Okanagan housing\)](https://okanagan.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/who-gets-priority/)\. If you’re considering a degree on both campuses, you must complete a separate residence application for each campus\.

- **Row 4**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### May 15, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Final transcripts
    
    -   Canadian post\-secondary applicants: Submit your final transcripts electronically or by mail to UBC’s Undergraduate Admissions Office\. \(Final transcripts are required to finalize conditional offers of admission\. Please review your application status in the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) for more information\.\)

- **Row 5**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### Early June 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Housing offers
    
    -   If you’ve qualified for the first\-year guarantee \(see [Vancouver housing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/first-year-guarantee/) or [Okanagan housing\)](https://okanagan.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/who-gets-priority/) you’ll receive a housing offer\.

- **Row 6**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### Late June 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Course registration
    
    -   [Course registration](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/admitted/register/) opens for first\-year students\.

- **Row 7**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### June 30, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Final transcripts
    
    -   US applicants: Deadline to submit final transcripts to UBC to confirm your offer of admission\.
    
    ##### Loan applications
    
    -   US applicants: Recommended deadline to submit UBC’s [US Loan Request form](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/student-loans/funding-us) to ensure loan funding for September\.

- **Row 8**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### Late June 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Jump Start Vancouver orientation
    
    -   Deadline to register for [Jump Start orientation](https://you.ubc.ca/jump-start-vancouver/) on the Vancouver campus\.

- **Row 9**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### July 1, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Deferred admission
    
    -   Deadline to apply for [deferred admission](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/admitted/deferring-admission/) for the Vancouver campus\.

- **Row 10**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### July 25, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Loan applications
    
    -   Canadian citizens and permanent residents: Recommended deadline to apply for Canadian and provincial loans to ensure funds for September and to qualify for the [UBC bursary program](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/loans-bursaries/bursaries/)\.
    
    ##### Out\-of\-province and out\-of\-country high school transcripts
    
    -   Out\-of\-province applicants: Deadline to have your final official transcript mailed to UBC to confirm your offer of admission\.
    -   Out\-of\-country applicants \(except US and A\-Level applicants\): Deadline to submit your final transcript to confirm your offer of admission\. Please check your letter of admission for curriculum\-specific deadlines\.

- **Row 11**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### August 1, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Jump Start Okanagan orientation
    
    -   Deadline to register for [Jump Start orientation](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/orientations/jump-start/) on the Okanagan campus\.

- **Row 12**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### Early August, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Early arrival to residence
    
    -   Deadline to apply for early arrival to residence\. \(If you register for Jump Start, you’ll automatically qualify to move in early\.\)

- **Row 13**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### August 15, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Deferred admission
    
    -   Deadline to apply for [deferred admission](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/admitted/deferring-admission/) for the Okanagan campus\.

- **Row 14**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### August 20, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### A\-Level results
    
    -   Applicants from the British\-patterned system: Deadline to submit final A\-Level results to UBC to confirm your offer of admission\.

- **Row 15**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### Early September 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Jump Start
    
    -   Jump Start Okanagan for all new\-to\-UBC students\.
    -   Jump Start Vancouver orientation for all first\-year students coming to UBC from high school\.

- **Row 16**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### Early September, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Residence move\-in day
    
    -   Move\-in day\.

- **Row 17**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### Early September, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### First day of classes
    
    -   First day of classes\. [Imagine UBC orientation](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/orientations/imagine-ubc) on the Vancouver campus\.

- **Row 18**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### Early September, 2027
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    ##### Tuition payment
    
    -   Deadline to pay your first instalment of Winter Session tuition\.
