## Campus tours

A great way to get a feel for UBC before you arrive is to take a campus tour, which are led by current UBC students\. They’ll take you to some of their favourite spots to study, work up a sweat, and relax between classes, and show you the best parts of the campus before your first day\.

## Campus community

Foster a sense of a home away from home\. Participate in clubs, join an athletic team, or seek out one of our many on\-campus support centres\.

[Image](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Clubs-and-societies-2-768x512.jpg)

### Clubs and societies

Are you a budding stand\-up comedian or avid environmentalist? Do you want to play Quidditch or try dragon boating? UBC Vancouver has more than 350 clubs and course unions for you to get involved with\. Take a look at some of the clubs on offer before arriving on campus\.

[Learn more](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc-life/getting-involved/clubs/)  

[Sports and recreation at UBC Vancouver](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Sports-and-recreation-2-768x432.jpg)

### Sports and recreation

You don’t have to be a star athlete to stay active at UBC Vancouver\. Join an intramural team or a recreational league, or take advantage of the state\-of\-the\-art facilities and year\-round programming at locations like the [UBC Aquatic Centre](https://recreation.ubc.ca/aquatics/), [BirdCoop Fitness Centre](https://recreation.ubc.ca/fitness-classes/), or [Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Arena](https://sportfacilities.ubc.ca/dmc/)\.

[Learn more](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc-life/sports-recreation/)  

[Aboriginal community at UBC Vancouver](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Aboriginal-Community-1-768x512.jpg)

### Indigenous community

If you’re a First Nations, Métis, or Inuit student, you can visit the [First Nations Longhouse](https://aboriginal.ubc.ca/longhouse/) to meet other Indigenous students, or access campus services like tutoring, financial advising, or wellness programming\. For Indigenous scholarship, you can go to the [X̱wi7x̱wa Library](https://aboriginal.ubc.ca/longhouse/xwi7xwa-library/), or stop by the [xʷc̓ic̓əsəm Garden](http://lfs-iherg.sites.olt.ubc.ca/the-garden/), which supports land\-based teaching and community engagement\.

[Learn more](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc-life/indigenous-students-ubc/)  

[International community at UBC Vancouver](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/International-community-1.jpg)

### International community

If you’re an international student, or just interested in exploring other cultures, there are places on campus where you can gather to exchange ideas\. Visit the [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/international-student-advising) building have your questions about living in Canada answered, or check out the [Global Lounge](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/simon-k-y-lee-global-lounge-resource-centre) to socialize with other students from overseas\.

[Learn more](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/simon-k-y-lee-global-lounge-resource-centre/)
