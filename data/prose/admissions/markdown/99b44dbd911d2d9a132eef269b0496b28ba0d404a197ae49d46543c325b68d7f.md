## Explore your program options

The curriculum emphasizes interdisciplinary, project\-based learning\. Our world\-class faculty and researchers are committed to an instructional approach that is varied, experiential, and engaging\. Our close\-knit, student\-centred program affords high levels of student\-professor interaction and collaborations\.
