## What to expect

[Image](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/49747797377_8e19212060_k-e1787267216311-768x424.jpg)

-   Chat live from anywhere in the world – even the comfort of your own home\.
-   Watch an informative live presentation, given by UBC advisors and current students\.
-   Meet and chat online with other students from around the world who are applying to UBC just like you\.
-   Get candid advice from current students who share stories about their experience applying to UBC\.
-   Receive immediate answers from advisors, UBC experts, professors, and current students\.
-   Take part in interactive polls and contests\.
