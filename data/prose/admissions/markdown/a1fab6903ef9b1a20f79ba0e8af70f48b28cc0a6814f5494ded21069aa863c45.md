Get a financial picture of the year ahead and take note of key dates, including when your first tuition payment is due, when to pay your housing fees, and more\.

  - **Header cell 1:**
  
    Date
  
  - **Header cell 2:**
  
    Event
  
  - **Header cell 3:**
  
    Amount

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    May 1 or June 1, 2021
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Deadline to accept your offer of admission and pay the tuition deposit online through the [Student Service Centre](http://www.students.ubc.ca/ssc)\. Please refer to your letter of admission or Student Service Centre account to find your assigned date\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    -   $500 for Canadian citizens and permanent residents
    -   $1,000 for students on a study permit

- **Row 3**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    June 10, 2021 onward
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Check your email regularly for your residence offer\. Once Student Housing sends your offer, you will be given a designated time frame to accept the offer and pay your first residence/meal plan instalment through the [UBC Student Housing and Community Services online service centre](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/)\. Residence fees for most first\-year residences include a mandatory meal plan\. View an overview of residence fees on [UBC’s Okanagan campus](http://okanagan.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/) and [UBC’s Vancouver campus](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/ "UBC Housing - Residence Fees") online\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    -   UBC’s Okanagan campus: $1,850
    -   UBC’s Vancouver campus: $1,858
    
    Your first payment includes a combination of residence and meal plan fees\.

- **Row 4**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    June 1, 2021 onward
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    If you are an international student, your temporary [iMED medical insurance fee](https://students.ubc.ca/health-wellness/health-insurance/health-insurance-details-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international) will be charged to your account when you register for courses\. This insurance will cover you until your [BC Medical Services Plan \(MSP\)](https://students.ubc.ca/health-wellness/health-insurance/health-insurance-details-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international/apply-bc-medical-services-plan-msp) coverage begins \(likely in November\)\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    $225 \(or $315 if you are a one\-term exchange student\), plus a $9\.74 direct billing fee

- **Row 8**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    November 1, 2021 onward
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    If you do not have other medical coverage, you are required by BC law to register for the [BC Medical Services Plan \(MSP\)](https://students.ubc.ca/health-wellness/health-insurance/health-insurance-details-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international/apply-bc-medical-services-plan-msp)\. You’ll need to wait for three months before you are eligible for the plan\. If you arrived in August, your waiting period will likely be over in November\.
