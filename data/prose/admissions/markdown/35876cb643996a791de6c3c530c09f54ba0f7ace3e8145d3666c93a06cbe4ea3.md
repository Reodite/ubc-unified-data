For many students, working part\-time while studying is a great way to earn some extra cash, meet new people, and get a break from studying\. UBC has programs that create well\-paid, on\-campus jobs for Canadian and international students\. Our on\-campus employers are flexible when it comes to working around exam and study schedules\.

## Work Learn and Work Study

With the popular [Work Learn](http://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-experiences/work-learn) and [Work Study](http://students.ok.ubc.ca/careers/services/jobs/workstudy.html) programs, you have the opportunity to develop professional skills and learn in a work environment\. To be eligible, you must be registered in courses at UBC and have a valid UBC student number\. Students can work in only one Work Learn position at any given time\.

The programs run from September to April during the Winter Session, and from May to August in the Summer Session\. For more information, including additional eligibility requirements for international students, visit the links below:

Vancouver Campus: [Work Learn](http://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-experiences/work-learn) \| Okanagan Campus: [Work Study](http://students.ok.ubc.ca/careers/services/jobs/workstudy.html)

## Co\-Op Education Program

Get valuable work experience in the field you’re studying with four, eight, twelve, and sixteen\-month paid co\-op terms\. With past employers such as the Canadian Space Agency, Microsoft, PepsiCo, and Adidas, co\-op experiences give you the foundation for a successful career after you graduate\.

Learn more about [Co\-Op Education Programs at UBC](http://www.coop.ubc.ca/)

## Undergraduate Research

You don’t have to be a graduate student to have your research taken seriously at UBC\. Apply for an undergraduate research award for a chance to work directly with professors, gain valuable academic work experience, and discover new research passions of your own\.

Learn about [Undergraduate Research Awards](http://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-experiences/undergraduate-research/NSERC-USRA)
