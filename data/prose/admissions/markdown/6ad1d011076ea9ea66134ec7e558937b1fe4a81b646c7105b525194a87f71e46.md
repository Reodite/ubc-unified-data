UBC has historically been a pioneer in teaching across disciplines, and today we have more integrated first\-year programs than any other Canadian university\. We passionately believe that this style of teaching is one of the most effective ways for you to become a critical, independent thinker\.

Here are some examples of programs across UBC’s Vancouver and Okanagan campuses that embrace inter\- and multi\-disciplinary thinking:

## Applied Science

[Integrated Engineering](http://www.igen.ubc.ca/) – Rather than earning a degree in one specific area of engineering, gain background in several core disciplines through the Integrated Engineering Program\.

[Engineering One](http://web.ubc.ca/okanagan/engineering/undergraduate.html) –  This introduction to the engineering profession integrates math, chemistry, biology, and physics to show you, in a meaningful way, the connections between these fields and how the sum is greater than the parts, particularly when it comes to engineering challenges on everything from bridge construction to snowboard design\. Offered in the Okanagan\.

[Cognitive Science](http://web.ubc.ca/okanagan/ikbarberschool/programs/undergradprograms/general-ba.html) – This program brings philosophy, psychology, linguistics, and computer science together in the study of intelligence\. Part of the General Studies Program, this multidisciplinary option allows students to study cognition from a variety of approaches\.

## Arts

[Arts One](http://arts1.arts.ubc.ca/) – This program takes an innovative approach to the study of the humanities and focuses on English, History, and Philosophy\. Instead of attending several separate lectures, you’ll attend one weekly lecture taught by a dynamic team of instructors from a variety of academic disciplines\. The core of Arts One is its extensive reading list: you will read, discuss, and write essays about great works of literature, history, and philosophy, both ancient and modern\.

[Coordinated Arts Program](http://www.cap.arts.ubc.ca/) – This introduction to core arts disciplines is taught in small, 25\-person study groups\. Choose from four streams: Civil and Sustainable Societies; Individual in Society; Global Citizens; and PPE: Political Science, Philosophy &amp; Economics\.

[Interdisciplinary Studies BA Program](http://www.interdisciplinary.arts.ubc.ca/) – This option provides an alternative to the traditional, department\-based major\. Design your own cross\-disciplinary course of study in consultation with an advisor\.

[International Relations](http://web.ubc.ca/okanagan/ir/welcome.html) –  Develop a solid background in the related fields of political science, history, sociology, anthropology, economics, and modern languages\.

[Philosophy, Politics &amp; Economics](http://web.ubc.ca/okanagan/ppe/welcome.html) This program gives you an exceptional opportunity to view our world through three different lenses: philosophy teaches logic, ethics, and how to question critically; politics determine the rules by which we are governed; economics encompasses how we make decisions as individuals and groups in our increasingly global world market\. Placed into context with each other, these three fields allow you to form critical and informed opinions about the world in which you live\.

## Forestry

[Natural Resources Conservation](http://www.forestry.ubc.ca/students/undergraduate/prospective/degree-programs/natural-resources-conservation/) – Immerse yourself in the multidisciplinary world of conservation science and get prepared for a range of career options: from resource conservation to managing protected areas to landscape planning\.

## Land &amp; Food Systems

[Global Resources Systems](http://www.landfood.ubc.ca/programs/grs_ug.htm) – This award\-winning program lets you combine language, travel, and science as your studies focus on a specific region of the world\.

## Science

[Science One](http://www.scienceone.ubc.ca/)  – This team\-taught program blends biology, chemistry, mathematics, and physics into a challenging format allowing you to hone your critical thinking, analytical skills, and independent thought through lectures, tutorials, laboratories, and field trips\.

[Coordinated Science Program](https://csp.science.ubc.ca/) – This popular program allows students to explore the connections between all scientific disciplines – biology, chemistry, physics, computer science, and math – in an intimate learning environment\.

[Environmental Sciences Program](http://www.ensc.ubc.ca/) – Interested in understanding the major environmental issues facing human societies? This program offers a integrative, cross\-disciplinary approach to the study of these important issues\.

[Earth &amp; Environmental Sciences](http://web.ubc.ca/okanagan/eesc/welcome.html) is a multidisciplinary Bachelor of Science program that emphasizes the interactions between humans and the environment\. The program is extremely flexible; you can focus on the environment or the solid earth in your studies as well as enhance your program with related courses in biochemistry, biology, chemistry, geography, plus mathematics and statistics\.

[Integrated Sciences Program](http://intsci.ubc.ca/) – An alternative to the traditional, department\-based major\. Design your own cross\-disciplinary course of study in consultation with an advisor\.
