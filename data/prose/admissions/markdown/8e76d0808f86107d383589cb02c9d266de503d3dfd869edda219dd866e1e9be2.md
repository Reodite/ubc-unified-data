## Arts programs

-   Vancouver
    
    [creative writing, ubc vancouver](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/Creative-Writing-BFA-Vancouver-768x511.jpg)
    
    ### Creative Writing
    
    This two\-year studio program offers workshops in the following genres: fiction, poetry, creative non\-fiction, writing for children, translation, stage play, radio, television, song lyric and libretto, screenwriting, new media, and writing for graphic\.\.\.
    
    [Learn more](https://you.ubc.ca/programs/creative-writing-vancouver/ "Learn more about Creative Writing")
    
-   Vancouver
    
    [Image](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/Mathematics-BA-Vancouver-768x432.jpg)
    
    ### Mathematics \(BA\)
    
    Mathematics majors apply math, probability, statistics, and the principles of business and finance to analyze statistical data and assess business risks\. Mathematics at UBC provides an excellent balance of pure and applied mathematics and is useful,\.\.\.
    
    [Learn more](https://you.ubc.ca/programs/mathematics-vancouver-ba/ "Learn more about Mathematics (BA)")
    
-   Vancouver
    
    [UBC Psychology](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/UBC-Vancouver-Psychology-768x432.jpg)
    
    ### Psychology
    
    Study human and animal behaviour using scientific methods and from a variety of perspectives including behavioural neuroscience, cognition, clinical, health, developmental, social and personality psychology, and quantitative methods\.
    
    [Learn more](https://you.ubc.ca/programs/psychology-vancouver-ba/ "Learn more about Psychology")
    
-   Vancouver
    
    [first nations and indigenous studies, ubc](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/First-Nations-Indigenous-Studies-768x508.jpg)
    
    ### First Nations and Indigenous Studies
    
    Gain an understanding of the histories, contemporary realities, and political concerns of Indigenous peoples in Canada and beyond\. This interdisciplinary program reflects the belief that deep understanding and ethical engagement are central to the we\.\.\.
    
    [Learn more](https://you.ubc.ca/programs/first-nations-indigenous-studies/ "Learn more about First Nations and Indigenous Studies")
    
-   Vancouver
    
    [film studies, ubc](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/Film-Studies-768x432.jpg)
    
    ### Cinema Studies
    
    Learn about the diversity of World Cinema and cinematic aesthetic practices\. As a Cinema Studies student, you will explore the cultural roles of films and how they impact our political and social perceptions\.
    
    [Learn more](https://you.ubc.ca/programs/cinema-studies/ "Learn more about Cinema Studies")
    
-   Vancouver
    
    [economics, ubc vancouver](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/Economics-Vancouver-768x432.jpg)
    
    ### Economics
    
    Gain a deep understanding of the economy and examine the social, cross\-cultural, and ethical dimensions of economic decision\-making\. You’ll learn how to choose and apply appropriate quantitative methods to a range of economic issues, and effectively\.\.\.
    
    [Learn more](https://you.ubc.ca/programs/economics-vancouver/ "Learn more about Economics")
    
-   Vancouver
    
    [computer science, ubc vancouver](https://you.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/Computer-Science-Vancouver-BA-768x511.jpg)
    
    ### Computer Science \(BA\)
    
    Learn how to use and improve computers while you explore topics such as databases and operating systems, software engineering, security, web development, and numerical methodology\. Computer Science gives you a thorough grounding in computer software\.\.\.
    
    [Learn more](https://you.ubc.ca/programs/computer-science-vancouver-ba/ "Learn more about Computer Science (BA)")
