UBC is home to more Loran Scholars than any other university in Canada\. Recognizing that academic performance is not the only indicator of value, the Loran Scholars Foundation looks beyond transcripts to find the next generation of leaders\. Loran Awards of up to $100,000 are awarded to students who demonstrate integrity, courage, grit, and personal autonomy\.

For information on the application process and deadlines, please visit the [Loran Scholars Foundation](http://www.loranscholar.ca/) website\.
