### By social media

Connect with current students and advisors by joining the conversation on our social media channels\. Catch the latest trend on [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@ubcadmissions), watch a student takeover on [Instagram](https://instagram.com/ubcadmissions), and ask a UBC advisor a question on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/ubcadmissions)\.

Please do not send private information through social media channels\. If private information is required please use one of our other communication channels to contact us\.

### By mail

UBC encourages the [submission of transcripts and documents electronically](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/applied/submit-documents/) wherever possible\. If you need to mail your undergraduate application transcripts and documents, please mail your document to the Vancouver campus\. This includes if your application is for the Okanagan campus\.
