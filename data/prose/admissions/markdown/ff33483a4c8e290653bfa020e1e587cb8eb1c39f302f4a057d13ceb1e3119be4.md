Joining a student\-run club is a terrific way to try something new, meet others with interests similar to yours, gain marketable skills like event organization, and have fun\.

## Student Government

UBC’s two campuses each have their own student governments that administer student\-run clubs, resource centres, and services\. There are lots of ways to get involved with your student government – apply for a paid position, volunteer, or run for a student leadership position in the annual elections\. Learn more getting involved with student government: Student Government: [Alma Mater Society](http://www.ams.ubc.ca/) \(AMS\) – Vancouver \| [University of British Columbia Students’ Union Okanagan](http://www.ubcsuo.ca/) \(UBCSUO\) – Okanagan

## Clubs

If you want to meet other like\-minded students, why not join a student\-run club? With over 400 clubs at UBC’s two campuses, there’s a good chance you’ll find one that matches your interests\. If you don’t see the club you’re looking for, start a new one\!

Here are some examples of student\-run clubs you can join:

-   Groups based on an academic major, such as the award\-winning International Relations Students Association or the Pre\-Med Club
-   Groups based on cultural interests, such as the Le Club Français or the Chinese Varsity Club
-   Clubs based on hobbies or sports, such as the Ski and Board Club and the Mountain Riders
-   Politically affiliated clubs, such as the Federal Young Liberals of UBC
-   Groups based around a religion, such as the the University Christian Ministry and the Muslim Students Association
-   And many, many more clubs

Clubs at UBC: [Vancouver](http://www.ams.ubc.ca/clubs/) \| [Okanagan](http://www.ubcsuo.ca/get-involved/student-associations/clubs/)
