## Your admission requirements

Admission to UBC is competitive, and your requirements change depending on your degree and campus of choice\. All applicants must meet UBC’s general admission requirements and English Language Admission Standard\.

[International high school applicant requirements](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/international-high-schools)  
[International Baccalaureate applicant requirements](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/international-baccalaureate/)  
[University or college student applicant requirements](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/university-college-transfer/)

## Scholarships and awards for International students

UBC recognizes the academic achievement of outstanding students from around the world by devoting more than $35 million annually to awards, scholarships, and other forms of financial support for international undergraduate students\. [Find out more about awards for International students](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-international-students/)\.

## English Language Admissions Standard

English is the primary language of instruction at UBC\. All prospective students are required to demonstrate a minimum level of English before they’re admitted\. [Find out how to meet UBC’s English Language Admission Standard\.](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/english-language-competency/)

## How to apply for a study permit

International students need a valid study permit to attend UBC\. Getting a permit can take several months, so be sure to begin the process as soon as you can\. [Find out how to apply\.](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/admitted/study-permit/)

## International student supports

### Enrolment Services Advisors

Get advice and support from Enrolment Services Advisors on the [Okanagan](https://ok.ubc.ca/faculty-and-staff/enrolment-services/) or [Vancouver](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/) campus

### Study permit concierge

To help students apply for a study permit and navigate Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada \(IRCC\), UBC offers complimentary [study permit concierge services\.](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/admitted/study-permit/study-permit-concierge/) These services are designed to provide you with resources and real\-time guidance as you work through your study permit application process\.

### Global Pathways \(Okanagan\)

Global Pathways is for international students on the Okanagan campus to assist with planning their career and immigration success\.

[Find out more about Global Pathways](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/global-pathways/)
