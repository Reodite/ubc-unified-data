## Meals

We will provide your meals during your stay on campus\. Please let us know about your dietary restrictions \(if you haven’t done so already\)\. Email us at [destination\.ubc@ubc\.ca](mailto:destination.ubc@ubc.ca)\. **Note:** parents and/or other guests will not be provided with meals\.

## What to pack

In addition to any personal items and clothes you bring with you, consider bringing:

-   -   Any medications you require for the duration of your stay
    -   Comfortable shoes walking and running
    -   A small backpack or bag to carry your things

-   Outdoor clothing in case of rain, including a raincoat and umbrella
-   Cell phone and charger
-   Personal toiletries, including a towel and a hair dryer
-   Personal items such as musical instruments, a yoga mat, sunglasses, etc\.
-   Spending money for the UBC Bookstore and snacks
-   A reusable water bottle

The weather can be a bit unpredictable at this time of year, it’s a good idea to check the forecast when you pack\!
