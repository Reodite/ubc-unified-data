From Olympic champions to professional superstars, UBC connects you to a tradition of sporting excellence\. Thanks to state of the art facilities and exceptional programs, the University has produced 231 Olympians, won 24 national swimming titles in the last 13 years, and claimed multiple championships in basketball, golf, soccer, volleyball, rowing, and field hockey over the past decade\.

What does this mean for you? Whether you’re an elite athlete or a casual competitor, UBC’s campuses offer you the opportunities and spaces necessary for an active lifestyle\. Take a look at the teams, classes, and varsity sports you can participate in\. Which will you choose?

### Varsity Teams and Opportunities

Go UBC, go\! The UBC Thunderbirds in Vancouver and UBC Heat in the Okanagan are powerful contenders in university athletics: our 37 varsity teams have claimed over 101 national championships, the highest of any Canadian university\. Considering that success, it’s no surprise that UBC has produced:

-   214 Olympic participants
-   Medal holders in Olympic and World competition
-   Major League Baseball draft picks
-   A world\-record holder in swimming \(Brian Johns\)

#### Our Teams

UBC Thunderbirds \(Vancouver campus\): Born in 1934, the UBC Thunderbirds represent a tradition of excellence in athletics and sportsmanship\. The Thunderbirds compete in 13 sports in two leagues – the [Canadian Interuniversity Sport](http://www.universitysport.ca/e/index.cfm) \(CIS\) league and the US\-based [National Association of Intercollegiate Athletics](http://naia.cstv.com/) \(NAIA\)\. Visit the [UBC Thunderbirds website](http://www.gothunderbirds.ca/) to see the full list of teams and sports represented\.

UBC Heat \(Okanagan campus\): The UBC Heat fields teams in basketball \(men’s and women’s\), soccer \(men’s and women’s\), and volleyball \(men’s and women’s\) in the [Canada West Universities Athletic Association](http://www.canadawest.org/landing/index) \(CWUAA or Canada West\)\. Canada West is one of four conferences in the CIS \([Canadian Interuniversity Sport](http://english.cis-sic.ca/landing/index)\), the governing body for the highest level of amateur sport in Canada\. The Heat men’s and women’s golf team, as well as the men’s and women’s cross country team, competes in the CCAA \([Canadian Collegiate Athletic Association](http://www.ccaa.ca/?language=en)\)\.

Interested in joining your campus’s varsity team? Contact the Head Coach of the sport you’re interested in to learn more about programs, scholarship opportunities, and open try\-outs at the beginning of the season\.

Team Pages: [UBC Thunderbirds](http://www.gothunderbirds.ca/) \| [UBC Heat](http://athletics.ok.ubc.ca/welcome.html)

### Intramurals and Recreation

Not every sports enthusiast is a high\-level varsity athlete\. At UBC, that’s not a problem because we have one of the largest intramural sports programs of any university in Canada\. Offering literally hundreds of choices, [UBC REC](http://www.rec.ubc.ca/) in Vancouver and [UBC Campus Recreation](http://ok.ubc.ca/athrec.html) in the Okanagan have something for everyone, whatever their skill level\. From 10\-person voyageur canoe races to the world’s largest indoor triathlon, UBC’s campuses hold more than 30 annual special events, intramural leagues in eight sports, and over 50 fitness, dance, and martial arts classes\. Whatever your chosen activity, intramurals and recreation at UBC offer you a great way to meet friends, get some exercise, and have fun\!

Campus recreation: [Vancouver](http://www.rec.ubc.ca/) \| [Okanagan](http://www.ubc.ca/okanagan/campusrec/welcome.html)

### Fitness Facilities and Venues

Whichever campus you call home, both UBC’s campuses offer state of the art fitness facilities and venues to keep you on your game\. We have everything from an Olympic winter sports centre and football stadium, to an outdoor turf field and multi\-court recreation centre\.

#### UBC Vancouver Campus Facilities

Take your pick of activities at the centrally located [Student Recreation Centre](http://www.rec.ubc.ca/src/index.cfm), which holds three full size gyms, a fitness centre, studio, and martial arts dojo\. If you’re more water inclined, head to the Aquatic Centre’s 50\-metre indoor pool complex or the John MS Leakey Boathouse for rowing on the Fraser River\. You can also visit the state of the art [Thunderbird Winter Sports Centre](http://www.recreation.ubc.ca/ice/), a 2010 Winter Olympics venue, for figure skating drop\-ins or a game of hockey\.

The Vancouver campus is located in a temperate climate, you can play outside year\-round on natural and artificial turf fields, a public golf course, indoor and outdoor tennis courts, or explore Pacific Spirit Park’s many hiking, running, and biking trails\. Venture off campus and you’ll discover the natural attractions Vancouver is known for: mountain hiking trails, beaches, parks, three local ski hills, and the world\-class Whistler Blackcomb Ski Resort\.

#### UBC Okanagan Campus Facilities

The [Recreation Facility](http://camprec.ok.ubc.ca/welcome.html) on the UBC Okanagan campus is one of the finest in British Columbia and has hosted many local, national, and international events\. Inside, the facility includes gymnasiums, weight room, cardio equipment, and sports medicine and massage therapy clinics\. Step outside and you’ll find tennis courts, an international size soccer pitch, beach volleyball courts, and a lit multi\-sport court\. UBC Okanagan is growing quickly and plans are in the works to construct a new fitness centre, additional gymnasium space, and artificial fields\.

Kelowna and the beautiful Okanagan Valley also provide students with plenty of outdoor recreational opportunities, including skiing, golfing, biking, rock climbing, sailing, and much more\.
