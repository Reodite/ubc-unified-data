Ask any current UBC student – one of the smartest things you can do while at UBC is to get involved on campus\. In fact, studies show that students who participate in student life tend to do better in their academics as well\. Between UBC programs, student government, and the hundreds of student\-run clubs available, you’ll be sure to find others on campus with interests similar to your own\.

What makes a great leader? How can you be part of the solution to local, international, and environmental challenges? UBC has created leadership programs that give you opportunities to challenge yourself, make a difference, and learn new skills\.

## Learn to Lead

Prime Ministers\. Nobel Laureates\. CEOs\. Olympians\. UBC has been educating leaders for almost a century\. Our [leadership programs](http://students.ubc.ca/about/go-global/go-global-leadership-programs) can help you become one\.

-   Student Leadership Conferences – The largest of their kind in Canada, these events are held annually in Vancouver each January, and in the Okanagan each November\. With over 1,200 potential and current student leaders in attendance, the Student Leadership Conferences are an exceptional opportunity to network, gain new skills, and learn from other like\-minded peers\. These are not events to be missed\!

## Give Back

Volunteering is a great way to develop skills you can put to use both at UBC and after you graduate\.

-   [Centre for Community Engaged Learning](http://students.ubc.ca/about/centre-community-engaged-learning#student-programs "UBC - Centre for Community Engaged Learning") – Offers programs that place students in community settings \(non\-profits and inner city schools\) either as a required part of an academic course, or through voluntary co\-curricular placements like Trek, Reading Week and International Service Learning for a hands\-on, immersive experience\.
-   International Volunteering – Through [Go Volunteer: International Community\-Based Learning](http://www.students.ubc.ca/global/learning-abroad/international-service-learning/), part of UBC’s [Go Global: International Learning Programs](http://www.students.ubc.ca/global), you can participate in international community projects around the world\. Projects range from microfinance in South Africa and protecting endangered sea life in Greece to working at rural schools in Chile and caring for orphans in Madagascar\. Get hands\-on experience while you learn about international development issues and the role you can play in the global community\. Some placements may contribute to UBC academic credit\. The Global Service Learning Endowment supports the efforts of UBC students who help communities in need around the world\.
-   [AMS Connect](http://www.ams.ubc.ca/vex) – AMS Connect can match your skills and goals with volunteer and internship positions\.

## Keep it Green

Surrounded by forest, mountains, and ocean, it’s no surprise that UBC is a world leader in the [campus sustainability](http://www.sustain.ubc.ca/) movement\. You can do your part by taking the [Campus Sustainability Pledge](http://www.sustain.ubc.ca/sustainable_u/)\.
