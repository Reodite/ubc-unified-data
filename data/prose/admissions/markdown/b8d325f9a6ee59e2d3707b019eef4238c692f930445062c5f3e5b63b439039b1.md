We recognize that even though you have the motivation and potential to be a UBC student, scholarships, awards, and family contributions may not be enough to cover the cost of your degree\. Our policy on financial support declares that no eligible Canadian student will be prevented from attending UBC for financial reasons alone\.

Depending on your situation, you may be eligible for some of the following loans, bursaries, or funding supports:

-   [Loans for Canadian and US students](http://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/loans-bursaries/loans-canadian-us-students/)
-   [Bursaries for Canadian students](http://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/loans-bursaries/bursaries/)
-   [Awards for Canadian students with disabilities](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/loans-bursaries/awards-students-disabilities/)
-   [Funding for former youth in care in BC](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/loans-bursaries/youth-in-care-funding/ "Youth in care tuition waiver")
-   [Funding for Canadian veterans](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/loans-bursaries/funding-for-veterans/)
-   [Emergency funding](http://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/loans-bursaries/emergency-funding/)
-   [Work programs](http://you.ubc.ca/ubc-life/getting-involved/work-programs/)
