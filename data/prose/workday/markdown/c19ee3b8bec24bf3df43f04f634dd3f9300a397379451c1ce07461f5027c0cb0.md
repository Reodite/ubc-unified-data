## About award payments

Once you [accept a UBC award](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/finances/reviewing-your-ubc-award-offers/#accept-offer) in the Learner Financial Support Management \(LFSM\) application, it will be displayed in Workday as an “Anticipated Payment”\. 

After UBC has deposited your award amount towards your outstanding tuition or student fees, it will show up in Workday as an “Applied Payment”\.

## How to view your award payments

### Go to your Finances app

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  Select “Student” in the navigation bar on the left\. 
3.  Select “Finances”\.

### Go to your account activity

Click the “View Account Activity” link in the “Tuition and Fees” menu to review: 

-   Anticipated Payments 
-   Applied Payments

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/07/Viewing-award-payments_go-to-account-activity-940x270.jpg)

#### Anticipated Payments

“Anticipated Payments” are funds that you expect to receive but have not been deposited in your account yet, like sponsorship or award payments\. 

1.  On the “View Account Activity” page, click the “Due Now Details” tab above the “Transaction Summary” table to display a summary of your charges and payments\. Learn more about your [charges and payments](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/finances/viewing-your-financial-account-activity-tuition-amount-and-payments/)\.
2.  Click the blue dollar amount link in the “Anticipated Payments” field to learn more about your upcoming payments\. A pop\-up window listing all anticipated payments will appear\.

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/07/Viewing-award-payments_anticipated-payments.jpg)

3.  Your anticipated payments will reflect the award amounts you have accepted\. 

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/07/Viewing-award-payments_anticipated-payments-table-940x350.jpg)

#### Applied Payments

“Applied Payments” are award payments that have been deposited to your account\. 

When you open the “View Account Activity” page, your Transaction Summary will be displayed by default\. If you have just reviewed your Due Now Details, click back to the “Transaction Summary” tab to view your award payments\. 

Your transaction summary will be displayed in a table\. Your most recent transactions will be listed at the top\. 

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/07/Viewing-award-payments_applied-payments-940x440.jpg)
