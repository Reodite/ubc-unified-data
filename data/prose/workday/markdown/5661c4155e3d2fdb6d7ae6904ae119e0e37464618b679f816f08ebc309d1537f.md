## About Credit/D/Fail courses

In some direct\-entry undergraduate programs of study, students can take eligible elective courses for Credit, D, or Fail standing instead of percentage grades\. Learn more about [Credit/D/Fail grading](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/creditdfail-grading)\. 

You will not be able to change your course to Credit/D/Fail on your own in Workday\. You must contact your [Academic Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising) to request the change in grading basis\.  

## How to Find Credit/D/Fail Courses 

### First, find a course section 

Follow the steps to [view the course schedule](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/viewing-the-course-schedule/)\.

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Finding-course-section-940x390.jpg)

2.  Once you find a course section you want to check, click the course title to go to a page titled “View Course Section”\. 

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Finding-CRdF_click-course-title-940x414.jpg)

3.  On the left side of the “View Course Section” page, review the “Grading Basis” section\. “Credit/D/Fail” will be listed in this section if you are able to take the course with this grading basis\.

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Finding-CRdF_check-grading-basis-940x447.jpg)

## Next steps

To request to change your course to Credit/D/Fail, contact your [Academic Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising)\.
