## About declaring or changing your major

You will only be able to self\-declare your major in Workday if you meet the program entry requirements\.  

You cannot use the steps below to change your degree program\. For example, you cannot change from a Bachelor of Science to a Bachelor of Arts, or from a Bachelor of Arts in Vancouver to a Bachelor of Arts in the Okanagan\. To change your degree program, speak to an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors)\.

### When to use this process: 

-   To declare your primary major, honours, or specialization in your program\.
-   To change your primary major, honours, or specialization to another one\. For example, changing a Bachelor of Arts Major in Psychology to a Bachelor of Arts Major in Sociology\.

## How to declare your primary major

### First, go to your Profile in Workday

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  On the Workday home screen, click your user icon in the top right corner\.
3.  Click the “View Profile” button in the pop\-up menu\.

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/home_view-profile.png)

###  Next, go to your academics overview

1.  Click the “Academics” tab in the left menu\.
2.  By default, you’ll be viewing the information under the “Overview” tab in the top menu\.

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Declaring-your-major_go-to-academic-overview.jpg)

### Change your program of study

1.  You should see a table with your program of study details\. Click on the white “Request” button in the row of your current primary program of study\.  
    
    [Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Declaring-your-major_change-program-of-study_request-1-940x242.jpg)
    
2.  In the pop\-up window, remove your current program of study from the “New Program of Study” box by clicking the ‘x’ next to the program name\. This way, you will not need to re\-enter your expected completion date and other details\.  
    
    [Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Declaring-your-major_change-your-program_remove-current-program.jpg)
    

3.  Search for the program you want to declare as your major or specialization in the “New Program of Study” box\. Make sure you include your degree \(for example, B\.A\.\) in the name of the new primary program of study\. The degree’s initials must also include a period after the letters\. For example, if you are searching for a Bachelor of Arts, type “B\.A\.” not “BA”\.  
    
    [Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Declaring-your-major_change-your-program_declare-new-major.jpg)
    
4.  Select the program you want from the drop\-down menu\.
5.  If you have bundled programs on your record, such as second majors or minors, you will need to remove your current program of study from the “Bundled With” box by clicking the ‘x’ next to the program name\. Then, enter your new primary program of study in the “Bundled With” field on the same line as your secondary program\.  
    
    [Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Declaring-your-major_change-your-program_bundled-with-major-1-940x157.jpg)
    
6.  After selecting your primary program of study, click the checkbox in the “Is Primary” column\.
7.  Enter your Expected Completion Date in the right column if it is not automatically populated\. Use the same Expected Completion Date you see on your current primary program of study\. Your Faculty can change this date later if necessary\. You are **not required** to graduate by this date\.
8.  **Do not** change the “Declare Date”, as it will show your current date\.
9.  Click the “Submit” button to save your changes\.   
    
    [Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Declaring-your-major_change-your-program_submit-new-program-940x230.jpg)
    

### Confirm your changes

Follow the [previous instructions above](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/degree-planning/declaring-or-changing-your-primary-major-honours-or-specialization/#academic-overview) to go to your academics overview\. 

After declaring your new program, it will show up under your academics overview\. The status will show “In Progress”\. 

Your previous primary program of study will also be listed in your academics overview\. The status for this program will show “Discontinued”\.

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Declaring-your-major_view-new-major-940x223.jpg)

## Errors and Alerts 

You may come across errors and alert messages when declaring your major: 

-   You may not meet the program entry requirements or you aren’t allowed to self\-declare your selected program\. Contact your faculty or department\.
-   You may not have filled out all required fields\. For example, if you do not enter an “Expected Completion Date”, you will not be able to declare your major\. 

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Declaring-your-major_errors-940x222.jpg)
