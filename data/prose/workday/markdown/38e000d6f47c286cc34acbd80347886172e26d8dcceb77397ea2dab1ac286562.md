If you are both a student and an employee at UBC, Workday allows you to view both your Student and Worker profiles and choose which profile appears by default when you log in\.

## Go to your Profile Summary in Workday

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  Click the user icon in the top right corner 
3.  Next: 
    -   **To view your Worker and Student information:** Click the “View Profile” button\. 
    -   **To** **chang****e** **your default profile view:** Click “My Account”\. 

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Profile_box.png)

3\. Depending on your selection:

-   **View Profile:** The Summary page will display your Worker and Student information\. Select your name under “Worker” to view your employee profile\. 
-   **My Account:** Select “Change Preferences” and scroll to “Preferred Default Profile View” under the “Account Preferences” section\. Here you can choose your default profile view to be “Student” or “Worker”\. 

## Maintaining information on your profiles

If you have a Student and a Worker profile in Workday, any updates that are made to your **personal information** \(legal name, preferred names, citizenship, etc\.\) and **home contact information** will update on both your Student and Worker profiles at the same time\. 

**Emergency Contact information is not shared between profiles\.**  You will need to add an emergency contact to your [Worker profile](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article_view&amp;sysparm_article=KB0016822) and to your [Student profile](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-your-emergency-contacts/)\.[https://students\.ok\.ubc\.ca/ws\-tutorial/adding\-your\-emergency\-contacts/](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/ws-tutorial/adding-your-emergency-contacts/) These do not need to be the same emergency contact person\. Ensure you have emergency contact information entered on both profiles\.
