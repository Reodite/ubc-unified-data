## About Enrolment Services Advisors

If you are an undergraduate student in a degree program on the UBC Vancouver campus, you’re assigned a dedicated [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors) \(ES Advisor\) to help you navigate finances, registration, and more at UBC\. If you don’t have an assigned advisor, they’re still available to help\.

If you are a graduate student, please contact your [program advisor](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/program-advising)\.

## How to find your assigned ES Advisor

### Go to your Academics app

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  Select “Student” in the navigation bar on the left\. 
3.  Select “Academics”\. 

### Find your ES Advisor

Click the “Support” tab in the Academics App to view your ES Advisor and their contact information\.
