## About Workday

Log into Workday to complete essential tasks like registering for courses, updating your personal information, paying your tuition, and downloading important documents\.

[Log into Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/)

[UBC student uses Workday on her laptop in a study space with other students in the background](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/home_workday_intro.jpg)

### [Navigating Workday](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/navigating-workday/)

Find out how to access your personal, financial, and academic records and documents in Workday and the UBC Official Documents tool \(ODMT\)\.

### [Personal information](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/ "Go to tutorials")

Get details on how to update your personal information in your account, including citizenship status, third\-party authorization, and emergency contacts\.

### [Course registration](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/ "Go to tutorials")

Learn how to search courses, create a saved schedule, add or drop course sections, and find your registration appointment\.

### [Degree planning](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/degree-planning/ "Go to tutorials")

Follow instructions to declare or change your program of study in Workday and review your transfer credits and grades\.

### [Finances](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/finances/)

Learn how to pay your tuition and student fees\.

### [Student documents](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/student-documents/)

Download your Confirmation of Enrolment Letter and more\.

### [UBC advisor contacts](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/ubc-advisor-contacts/ "Go to tutorials")

Find your Enrolment Services or Accessibility Advisor in Workday\.
