## About Accessibility Advising

Accessibility Advisors facilitate disability\-related accommodations and programming initiatives designed to remove barriers for students with disabilities and ongoing medical conditions\. Learn how to [register with the Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility)\.

## How to find your Accessibility Advisor

### Go to your Academics app

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  Select “Student” in the navigation bar on the left\. 
3.  Select “Academics”\. 

### Find your Accessibility Advisor

Click the “Support” tab in the Academics App to view your Accessibility Advisor and their contact information\.
