## Important information about emergency contacts

-   All UBC students must add emergency contact information in Workday\. 
-   If you are a student who works for UBC,  you will need to add emergency contacts in both your student and employee Workday profiles\.
-   The “Third party user” record on the “Friends and Family” tab is not used for third\-party authorization for UBC business processes\. Find out how to [add a third\-party authorization](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-your-third-party-authorization/)\.

## How to add or change an emergency contact

### First, go to your Profile in Workday

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  On the Workday home screen, click your user icon in the top right corner\.
3.  Click the “View Profile” button in the pop\-up menu\.

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/home_view-profile.png)

### Next, navigate to your contact information

1.  In the Profile, click the “Contact” tab on the left menu\.
2.  Click the “Friends and Family” tab in the top menu\. 

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/Personal-Information_adding-your-emergency-contact_navigate-to-contact-info.jpg)

### Add your emergency contact information

1.  Click the white “Add” button\.
2.  In the pop\-up menu, choose ‘Emergency Contact’ as the “Relationship Type”\.  
      
    Do not select a relationship type other than ‘Emergency Contact’, as they will not be allowed for UBC purposes\.

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/Personal-Information_adding-emergency-contact_add-your-friends-and-family.jpg)

3.  Do not check the “Is Third Party User” box\. In Workday, this does not mean third\-party authorization and will not be considered as an authorized third party for UBC business\. Find out how to [add third\-party authorization](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-your-third-party-authorization/)\.

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/Personal-Information_adding-emergency-contact_third-party-authorization.jpg)

4.  Enter the name of your emergency contact in the name fields\.
5.  Click the “Contact Information” tab next to “Name” and add their contact information in the fields\.

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Adding-emergency-contact_contact-information-tab.jpeg)

6.  Click the orange “OK” button to save your emergency contact information in Workday\.
