Your Account Statement shows your balance and all transactions, like payments and charges, for one academic period and is available for downloading while you are a current UBC student\. Your Account Statement can be used as your proof of tuition payment\.

## How to download your Account Statement

### Go to your Finances App in Workday

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\.
2.  Select “Student in the navigation bar on the left\.
3.  Select “Finances”\.

### Next, go to your Account Statement

1.  In the “Tuition and Fees” menu to the right, click the “View Statement” link\.
2.  A pop\-up window will appear\. From the drop\-down list, select the academic period you want to generate the Account Statement for\.
3.  Click the “OK” button\.

### Download your Account Statement

1.  On the “View Statement” page, click the “Generate PDF” button\.
2.  It may take a moment for the PDF to be generated\. Once ready, click the download icon in the upper right corner of the statement\.
3.  Click “Download”\.
