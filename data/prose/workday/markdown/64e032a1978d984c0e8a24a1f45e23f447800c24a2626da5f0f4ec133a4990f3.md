This information is applicable to UBC Vancouver students\.

Learn more about the [U\-Pass BC program](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/transit/u-pass-compass-card), including exemption criteria\.

## How to apply for a U\-Pass exemption

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\.
2.  Type “Create Request” in the search bar\.
3.  Select the “Create Request” task\.
4.  Enter ” U\-Pass” into the “Request Type” field on the pop\-up window\.
5.  Follow the instructions in the “U\-Pass Exemption Request” window to complete the request form\.
6.  Submit the exemption request\.
