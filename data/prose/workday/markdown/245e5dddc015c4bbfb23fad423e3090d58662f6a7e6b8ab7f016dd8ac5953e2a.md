UBC students use [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/) and the [UBC Official Documents tool \(ODMT\)](https://ubc.appiancloud.com/suite/sites/ubc-official-documents) to complete essential tasks like: 

-   Viewing the course schedule 
-   Registering for courses 
-   Accessing your final grades 
-   Updating your personal information 
-   Paying your tuition 
-   Downloading important documents such as transcripts

## How to log into Workday

Log into Workday at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/) with your Campus\-wide Login \(CWL\)\. 

-   If you have forgotten your login information, you can go to your [CWL myAccount portal](https://www.myaccount.ubc.ca/myAccount) to manage your Campus\-Wide Login\. [Learn more](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl)\. 
-   If you are a new undergraduate student and have accepted your offer, but have not created a **Student CWL**, [follow the instructions](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/how-create-account) under “**Student Accounts \(CWL Account Upgrader\)**”\.

### Sidebar navigation menu

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/UX-Whole-Page-1_Dashboard-768x475.png)

The sidebar navigation menu located on the left of the homepage contains links to important apps and features within Workday\. 

### Student

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/UX-Focused-View-1_Student-768x514.png)

Select the **“Student”** menu category to find the two Workday apps that are related to: 

-   **Academics:** Where you can view the course schedule, create a saved schedule, register for courses, and more\. 
-   **Finances:** Where you can view your tuition and fees, download your account statement, access TouchNet to make a payment, setup your direct deposit, and more\. 

### Personal

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/UX-Focused-View-2_Personal-768x514.png)

Select the **“Personal”** menu category, then select **“Personal Information”** to view and edit your home contact information, personal information, preferred name, and more\. 

If you are a student who is also employed by UBC, you may see additional apps that are related to your employment\. 

### Your profile

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/Workday_Profile-768x432.png)

In your profile you can view and edit select information\. To access your profile: 

1.  On the Workday homepage, click your user icon in the top right corner\. 
2.  Click the “View Profile” button in the pop\-up menu\. 
3.  View: 
    -   **Summary:** Contains your student ID and class standing\. 
    -   **Personal:** Add your [preferred name](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/updating-your-preferred-name/), [pronouns](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/updating-your-pronouns-trans-experience-and-gender-identity/), [SIN, ITN](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-your-social-insurance-number-sin-or-international-tax-number-itn/), [Third Party Authorization](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-your-third-party-authorization/), and [Indigenous self\-identification](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/updating-your-indigenous-self-identification/)\. You can also view your [legal name](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/viewing-your-legal-name/) and [date of birth](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/viewing-your-date-of-birth/)\. 
    -   **Contact:** Add personal [contact information](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-changing-your-contact-information/) and [emergency contacts](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-your-emergency-contacts/)\. 

### Tasks and notifications

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/Workday_notifications-768x433.png)

Select the **“My Tasks”** **inbox** icon in the top right or an item in the **“Awaiting Your Action” section** to view your tasks\. Examples of tasks include completing registration for a waitlisted course and Workday onboarding\. 

Select the **“Notifications”** icon in the top right to view your notifications which may include updates to program change requests, waitlist status, and changes to your residency status\. 

## Downloading UBC documents

Current and former UBC students can use the [UBC Official Documents tool \(ODMT\)](https://ubc.appiancloud.com/suite/sites/ubc-official-documents) to download and order documents this includes: 

-   Official and unofficial transcripts 
-   Parchments \(diplomas\) 
-   Program Completion Letters 
-   Tax forms \(T2202 and T4A\) 
-   Confirmation of Enrolment letters \(current students only\) 

Current students can also log into ODMT through Workday in the Academics app\. 

View step\-by\-step tutorials on [downloading documents in ODMT](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/student-documents/)\.
