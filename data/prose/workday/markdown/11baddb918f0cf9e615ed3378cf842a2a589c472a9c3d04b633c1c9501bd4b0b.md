## About Workday

UBC students use the Workday student information system to access student records, including the following:

-   Course schedule and registration
-   Grades
-   Awards
-   Tuition summary and payment
-   Exam schedule
-   Transcripts
-   Transfer credits
-   Your personal and contact information

## UBC applicants

If you’re an undergraduate applicant who has just applied to UBC, please log into the [Applicant Service Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) to access your application status and information\.

## Former students and alumni

Your access to Workday will expire approximately 18 months after you become an inactive student \(e\.g\. after graduation or last registration activity\)\. To order a transcript or replacement document, or download a tax form, log in to the [UBC Online Document tool](https://ubc.appiancloud.com/suite/sites/ubc-official-documents) \(ODMT\) with your CWL\.

## How to login to Workday

Log in to Workday at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/) with your Campus\-wide Login \(CWL\)\. 

If you don’t have a Student CWL, you can create one after accepting your offer and receiving a student number\. You can [set up a Student CWL](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/how-create-account) through UBC IT\. If you do not have Multi\-factor Authentication set up, [follow instructions](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/multi-factor-authentication-mfa#getting-started) online, then log into Workday\.

## How to change or reset your CWL password

You can change your CWL password after logging in to your [Campus\-Wide Login \(CWL\) myAccount](https://www.myaccount.ubc.ca/myAccount/login.xhtml)\. You can also [reset your CWL password](https://www.myaccount.ubc.ca/myAccount/forgot/forgotPassword.xhtml) if needed through the website\.

For more information, read the [UBC IT article](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/problems-your-cwl) for instructions and troubleshooting support\.

## If you forgot your student number or CWL username

Your UBCcard has your student number printed on it, which is an 8\-digit number\. If you’ve lost your UBCcard, you can [request a new card](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/card-renewals-lost-stolen-cards/loststolen-cards?login)\.

If you forgot your CWL username, you can start the [account recovery process](https://www.myaccount.ubc.ca/myAccount/forgot/forgotLogin.xhtml) or contact [UBC IT](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/problems-your-cwl)\.
