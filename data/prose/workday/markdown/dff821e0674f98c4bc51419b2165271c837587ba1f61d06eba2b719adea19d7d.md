## Access the UBC Official Documents Tool \(ODMT\)

Current and former UBC students can download their tax forms from the [UBC Official Documents tool](https://ubc.appiancloud.com/suite/sites/ubc-official-documents) \(ODMT\)\. Current students can also log into the UBC Official Documents tool through Workday: 

-   Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
-   Select “Student” in the navigation bar on the left\. 
-   Select “Finances”\. 
-   Select “Tax Forms” \(T2202 and T4A\) from the top menu\. 
-   Click the “UBC Official Documents” link to go to the UBC Official Documents tool \(ODMT\), under “Student Tax Forms \(T2202 and T4A\)\. 

## How to download your tax forms

1.  Select “Tax Form T2202” or “Tax Form T4A” under “Order Options”\. 
    -    Note: The T2202 replaced the T2202A beginning with the 2019 calendar year\. To download a historical T2202A \(PDF\) from the UBC Official Documents tool, select “Tax Form T2202”\. 
2.  Select the tax year that you want to download using the drop\-down menu\. 
3.  Review your address under the “Tax Form Information” box to ensure it is correct\. 
    -   Note: If you update your address in the ODMT, it will not be reflected in Workday\. Learn how to [update your address](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-changing-your-contact-information/) in Workday\. 
4.  Select “Request Tax Form T2202 \(PDF\)” or “Request Tax Form T4A”\. 
5.  Wait a few seconds before selecting the “Generate T2202 Tax Form \(PDF\)” or “Generate T4A Tax Form \(PDF\)”’\. There might be a short delay while the tax form is being generated\. 
6.  Select the “PDF” icon to download\.
