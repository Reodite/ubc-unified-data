## How to view the course schedule

### Go to your Academics app

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  Select “Student” in the navigation bar on the left\. 
3.  Select “Academics”\. 

### Find course sections

1.  Select the “Registration &amp; Courses” tab in the top menu\.  
2.  Click the “Find Course Sections” link in the “Registration” menu to the right\. 

#### Select the academic term or session

In the pop\-up menu, select the Term or Session you want to find course sections for:  

1.  Click the “Start Date within” field\.  
2.  If you are exploring courses for the upcoming year, select “Future Periods”\. 
    -   Make sure you choose the correct campus \(i\.e\., UBC\-O for the Okanagan campus, UBC\-V for the Vancouver campus\)\. 
3.  For Winter registration, each Term must be selected individually\. 
    -   Select one or both Winter Term 1 and/or Winter Term 2 \.
    -   **Do not** choose the option that combines Winter Term 1 and Winter Term 2 as it will not display the courses\. 

[find course sections](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/view-course-schedule-768x510.png)

4\. For Summer registration, select the applicable Academic Session\.

#### Select your academic level

1.  Click the “Academic Level” field\. 
2.  Select ‘Undergraduate’ \(e\.g\. bachelor’s degree programs\) or ‘Graduate’ \(e\.g\. master’s degree and PhD programs\) from the list\.   
    -   **Do not** select “Academic Level Not Applicable”\. This will result in an error\. 
3.  Click the “OK” button\. 

#### **Filter your search results**

1.  To narrow your results, select the filters on the left menu\. You can filter by campus, course level, course subject, number of credits, delivery mode, and instructional format\.   
2.  Click the box next to the filter you want to apply\. Your search results will be automatically updated\.

#### View course details and drop and withdrawal deadlines

Click the arrow beside the course section to view course section details and **drop and withdrawal deadlines**\. You can view details of all course sections by clicking “Expand All”\. Learn more about the [add/drop deadline](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates/) and [tuition refund dates](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/tuition-refunds/)\.
