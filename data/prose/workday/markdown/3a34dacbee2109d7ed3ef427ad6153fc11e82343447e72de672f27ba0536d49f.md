There are two primary ways you can register for courses in Workday\. You can [register for individual courses](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/registering-for-individual-course-sections/) one at a time, or you can create a saved schedule and register for **all your courses at once**, which is covered in the tutorial below\. 

---

**Before proceeding:**

-   **Creating a saved schedule does not register you in courses\.** You must follow separate instructions to:
    -   [register from a saved schedule](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/creating-viewing-and-registering-from-a-saved-schedule/#register)\.
    -   [register in a waitlisted course](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/joining-viewing-and-registering-from-course-waitlists/)
-   For the Winter Session, you must make a saved schedule for each term\. We recommend starting with Term 1\. 
-   When creating a saved schedule, you can only add courses that are offered in the same academic period \(Winter Term 1, Winter Term 2, Summer Session\)\. Courses that span Term 1 and Term 2 will only appear in the Term 1 saved schedule\. If you register in a course that spans both terms, be mindful of course conflicts in Term 2\. Learn more about [resolving course conflicts](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/troubleshooting-your-course-registration/)\.  
-   When you are building **your Term 2 saved schedule**, you may see an alert that you do not meet the prerequisites for some courses\. This may be because you have the **prerequisites on your Term 1 saved schedule**\. Check your Term 1 saved schedules to make sure you have the required courses to meet your Term 2 prerequisites\.
    -   If you complete the prerequisite that triggered the alert, or register in the Term 1 prerequisite, the alert will no longer appear\. As long as there are no other registration issues you will be able to proceed with registration\.
-   You will be able to create your saved schedule with courses from any academic level, but you may not be able to register if you do not meet the eligibility requirements\. 
-   If you have more than one academic record \(e\.g\. concurrent Master’s degree and PhD students\), you need to make a separate saved schedule for each academic record\.

## How to create a saved schedule

1.  On the Workday homepage, click the “View My Saved Schedules” button\. 
2.  Click the “Create Saved Schedules” button 
3.  Enter a **name** for the saved schedule and select an **Academic Period**\. 
    -   If you have more than one academic record \(e\.g\., concurrent  Master’s degree and PhD students\), select the academic record you are creating the schedule for\.  
    -   **Tip:** Since you can create as many saved schedules as you wish, it can be helpful to identify the academic year and term in the name to avoid confusion \(e\.g\., “2026W Term 1 First Choice”\)\.  
    -   **Reminder:** You must make a separate saved schedule for each Academic Term\. Courses that span Term 1 and Term 2 will only appear in the Term 1 saved schedule\. 
4.  Select “Ok”\. 

### Select courses to add to your saved schedule

1.  Search for a course using the search box and/or; 
2.  Use the filters to narrow down your search by Academic Level, Delivery Mode, Meeting Days, etc\. 
3.  Click on the down arrow beside a course name to expand it\. You can view the required course activities \(e\.g\. lecture, lab, discussion, etc\.\), grouped by activity type\. 
4.  Click on the: 
    -   “i” icon to view **course section details**\. Please note that add/drop deadlines are not included here\. View the course schedule to find the add/drop date for course sections\. 
    -    “\+” icon to **add** a course to your saved schedule\. 
    -   trash can icon to remove a course from your saved schedule\.

> Reminder: You can save schedule conflicts \(i\.e\. courses with overlapping meeting times\) but will need to resolve the conflict before registering\.

### Errors and alerts

If there are any errors or alerts in your saved schedule, they will appear in the top right corner, in an orange or a red box\. Select the box to view the descriptions for the errors and/or alerts\. 

-   **Red error:** You must resolve this error before you are able to save your saved schedule\. 
-   **Orange alerts:** You can save your saved schedule, but you will need to resolve this alert before registering\. 

[Example error and alerts](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/errors.png)

To view your alerts after exiting your saved schedule:

-   On the Workday homepage, select the “View My Saved Schedules” button\. 
-   If there are any alerts, they will appear under the “Alerts” column for the applicable saved schedule\. 
-   View the Resolving saved schedules alerts tutorial to find out how to manage and resolve alerts prior to registration\. 

## How to edit a saved schedule

1.  On the Workday homepage, click the “View My Saved Schedules” button\. 
2.  In the actions column for the saved schedule you want to change, select “Edit”\. **Do not** click “Remove”\. This will delete your entire saved schedule\. 
3.  Delete courses by clicking on the course section you want to remove and then select the trash can icon\. Remember to remove all associated activities for the course \(e\.g\. lab, discussion, etc\.\)\. 
4.  Add any new course sections\. 
5.  Select the “Save” button once you have finished making edits\. 

## How to register in courses from a saved schedule

### Before registering in courses

If you choose to register all your courses using a saved schedule, make sure to review and manage your saved schedule to resolve [alerts](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/managing-a-saved-schedule/), [conflicts](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/troubleshooting-your-course-registration/), and [holds on your record](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/resolving-a-hold-on-your-record/) before registration opens\. This can help prevent issues during your registration process\. 

### Navigate to a saved schedule

On the Workday homepage, select the “View My Saved Schedules” button\. 

### Register a saved schedule

**Reminder:** If registering for two terms, you must register for Term 1 before registering for Term 2\. 

1.  Click the “Register” button\.
    -   The button will only appear when registration opens for you\. 
2.  Make any last\-minute changes or resolve course conflicts before registering\. If you have unresolved course conflicts, you **will not** be registered in the conflicting courses\.  
3.  Once you are ready, click the “Register” button in the lower left corner\. Your registration will be processed in Workday\. 

### Review registration

After you complete your registration, a registration summary will appear on the screen in a table format\. The summary is divided into three sections: 

1.  **Unsuccessful registrations**:
    -   You were not registered in these courses\. Review the “Reason” column to find out why your registration was unsuccessful\. 
2.  **Waitlisted registrations**:
    -   You were added to the waitlist of these courses\. You may be offered a spot in the course if a seat becomes available\. Make sure your email address is correct in Workday to receive these messages\. 
    -   Learn how to [register in a course from the waitlist](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/registering-in-a-course-from-the-waitlist/)\.
3.  **Successful registrations**:
    -   You were successfully registered in these course sections\. Click the white “View Registered Courses” button in the lower left corner under the table summary for a complete list of your registered courses\. 

#### Register for Term 2

Once you have completed your registration for Term 1, [repeat the steps above](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/creating-viewing-and-registering-from-a-saved-schedule/#saved-schedule) to register for Term 2\.
