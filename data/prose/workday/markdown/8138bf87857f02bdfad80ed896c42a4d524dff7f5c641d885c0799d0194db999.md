## How to view your registration appointment

### Go to your Academics app

1.   Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  Select “Student” in the navigation bar on the left\. 
3.  Select “Academics”\. 

### Go to your registration appointment

1.  Click the “Registration &amp; Courses” tab in the top menu\.
2.  Your registration appointment date and time will be listed on the right side of the table\. 

> Please note:  
> – Your Workday account may show both a registration appointment **start time and end time**\. While the start time indicates the start of your personal registration period, the end time simply refers to the period when general enrolment begins\. It has no relevance to your registration timeline\.  
> – If you are enrolled in more than one program, you will be assigned two registration appointments\. For example, an undergraduate dual degree student would register in courses for **both** programs on the earliest listed registration appointment date\.  
> – **Your registration date and time may change**, so please check your registration appointment in Workday regularly for updates\. 

## Next steps

To prepare for your course registration, you can [view the course schedule](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/viewing-the-course-schedule/) and [create a saved schedule](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/creating-a-saved-schedule/)\. 

When your registration appointment opens, you can [register from a saved schedule](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/registering-in-courses-from-a-saved-schedule/) or [register for individual course sections](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/registering-for-individual-course-sections/)\.
