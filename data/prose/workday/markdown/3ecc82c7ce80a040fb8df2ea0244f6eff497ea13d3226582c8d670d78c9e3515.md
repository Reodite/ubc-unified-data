If you see an alert on your saved schedule, you may need to complete an action to avoid registration issues\.  

-   **Red errors:** You will not be able to proceed with your registration\. You must resolve the alert prior to registering for courses\. 
-   **Orange alerts:** You will be able to proceed with registration, but you should read the alert to ensure you meet the eligibility requirements\.

> **Before proceeding:**  
> When you are building **your Term 2 saved schedule**, you may see an alert that you do not meet the pre\-requisites for some courses\. This may be because you have the **pre\-requisites on your Term 1 saved schedule**\. Check your Term 1 saved schedules to make sure you have the required courses to meet your Term 2 pre\-requisites\.  
>   
> **This alert will be resolved after you register for your Term 1 courses\.** 

## View and resolve saved schedule alerts

### **Navigate to a saved schedule**

-   On the Workday homepage, select “View My Saved Schedules” button\. 
-   Click on the name of the saved schedule that you would like to view and resolve alerts for\. 
-   If your saved schedule has any issues, click the orange “Errors” box in the top right corner\. 
-   Review your alerts and take action to resolve them before you attempt registration\. 

The following table lists commonly encountered alerts and error messages, what they mean, and how you can resolve them\. 

- **Row 1**

  - **Header cell 1:**
  
    **Alert or Error Message**
  
  - **Header cell 2:**
  
    Issue
  
  - **Header cell 3:**
  
    Resolution

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    “Your saved schedule includes course sections with time conflicts\.”
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The time for a course in your saved schedule overlaps with another course\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    Remove one of the courses that has an overlapping schedule\.

- **Row 3**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    “Your saved schedule includes course sections that you’re not eligible for\.”
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    You do not meet eligibility requirements for a course in your saved schedule\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    Remove the course\. If you have coursework from a prior college or university that may meet the eligibility rules, please contact an [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors) to discuss

- **Row 4**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    “Your saved schedule includes course sections you are not eligible for\.”
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    You do not meet the eligibility requirements for a course in your saved schedule\. You have not added a prerequisite course to your saved schedule\. A prerequisite course is a course you must take before the selected course\. If you do not add the prerequisite course in a term before your selected course, you will not meet the eligibility requirements set in Workday\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    Add the selected course in Term 2 and the prerequisite course in Term 1\.   
      
    When you register for your courses, you must register for Term 1 before Term 2 to meet the eligibility requirement\.

- **Row 5**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    “Your saved schedule includes course sections you are not eligible for\.”
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    You have not added a corequisite course to your saved schedule\. This may be due to a missing corequisite\. A corequisite is a course that you must take before or at the same time with the selected course\. If you do not add a corequisite, you will not meet the eligibility requirements set in Workday\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    If you are missing a corequisite \(found within the course eligibility rules\), add the corequisite course to your saved schedule\.

- **Row 6**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    “You’ve already received academic credit for \[Course\]\. Retaking it won’t count towards your overall credit requirements\.”
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    You have already received academic credit for the course section or an equivalent/cross\-listed course\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    You can still register for the course, but you will not receive credit for it\.

- **Row 7**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    “\[Instructional Format\] is required for \[Course\]\. Please select an \[Instructional Format\]”Example \| Discussion is required for ENGL\_V 112\. Please select a discussion\.
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    You are missing a required activity for the course\. For example, you have selected a lecture but not a discussion section\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    Go to the course on your saved schedule and select a section for each of the required instructional formats showing on the screen \(e\.g\., Lecture, Laboratory, Discussion, Seminar\)

- **Row 8**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    “You can’t register for a course section that overlaps with and belongs to the same course as another course section you’re already enrolled in\.”
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    You have already registered for that course for the academic period\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    Change or drop the course section you are currently registered in\. Note that you will lose your seat in the course section if you drop it\.

- **Row 9**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    “There is already a \[Instructional Fromat\] section for \[Course\] under this saved schedule\. If you would like to change the section, remove the other section from your saved schedule\.”
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    This course activity has already been added to your saved schedule\. You now have multiple instances of the same course activity\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    Remove the course section from the saved schedule before adding a new course section\.
