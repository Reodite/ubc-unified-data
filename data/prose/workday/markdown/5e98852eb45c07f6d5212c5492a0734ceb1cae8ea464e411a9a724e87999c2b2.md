Notes may be added to your profile in Workday by UBC faculty or staff\. For example, you may have a note on your profile if you have submitted a prerequisite waiver request\. 

## How to view your profile notes in Workday

### Go to your history information in Workday

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  Click your user icon in the top right corner, then click the “View Profile” button\. 

[Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Profile_box.png)

3\. In your profile, click the “History” tab\.   
4\. Click the “Note History” tab in the top menu\.
