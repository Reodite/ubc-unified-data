A waitlist may be set up in [Workday](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/) when a course section is full\. If you try to register in a course that is full **and** has a waitlist, you will be added to the waitlist until a seat becomes available for you to register\. 

Learn more about course [waitlists and what to do if a course is full](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/register-courses/#get-ready-for-registration)\.

Before proceeding:

-   You can [add yourself to a waitlist for individual courses](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/adding-yourself-to-the-waitlist-for-an-individual-course-section/) or [add a course waitlist to your saved schedule](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/viewing-your-waitlisted-courses/)\. 
-   If a course has multiple course activities \(e\.g\., a lecture and a lab\), the waitlist generally applies only to the **graded course activity** \(typically the lecture\)\. 
-   When a waitlist is active, **non\-graded activities** like labs will show zero available seats\. You can only register in a non\-graded course activity after you are offered a seat in the graded course activity\. 
-   Adding yourself to a waitlist **does not guarantee** that you will be able to register in the course\. You will only be able to register in the course if you receive an invitation to register once a seat opens\. 
-   To join a waitlist, you must meet any course registration requirements that are [viewable in the course section details](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/viewing-the-course-schedule/), including any prerequisites and corequisites\. Learn more about prerequisite and corequisite courses in the [Academic Calendar](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/)\. 

## How to check if there a course section has a waitlist

### Navigate to the course schedule  

Follow the instructions to view the [course schedule\.](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/course-registration/viewing-the-course-schedule/)

### Review the section status

Once you’ve found the course section you want, check the section status in the line below the section title\. 

-   If the section status is **“Waitlist”**, then a waitlist has been opened for that course section\. 
-   If the section status is **“Open”**, you can register directly into the course if you are eligible\. 
-   If the section status is **“Closed”**, then the course is full\. 

### View the waitlist capacity

Courses with a waitlist will also show a “Waitlisted/Waitlist Capacity” below the section title\. 

“Waitlisted/Waitlist Capacity” refers to the number of students currently on the waitlist and the maximum number of students who can be on the waitlist\. For example, a “Waitlisted/Waitlist Capacity” of 17/500 means that there are currently 17 students on the waitlist, and the waitlist can hold a maximum of 500 students\.
