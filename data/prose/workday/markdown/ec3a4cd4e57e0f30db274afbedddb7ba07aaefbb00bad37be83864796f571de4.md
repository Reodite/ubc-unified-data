### Access the Official Documents tool \(ODMT\)  

Current and former UBC students can order official parchment \(diploma\) or certified copy from the [UBC Official Documents tool](https://ubc.appiancloud.com/suite/sites/ubc-official-documents) \(ODMT\)\.

Current students can also log into the Official Documents tool through Workday:

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  Select “Student” in the navigation bar on the left\.
3.  Select “Academics”\.
4.  In the “Transcripts and Enrolment/Completion Letters” menu, click the “UBC Official Documents” link to go to the UBC Official Documents tool \(ODMT\)\.

### Request a replacement parchment \(diploma\)

1.  Select “Replacement Parchment” under “Order Options”\.
2.  Complete the mandatory fields:
    -   Requestor Email
    -   Credential
    -   Name on Diploma
3.  The “Recipient” fields will appear after the “Name on Diploma” is selected\. Enter the recipient details:
    -   The “Delivery Type” will default to Print\.
    -   If you choose “Pick up” as a delivery option, the address will default to the “Enrolment Services” address\.
4.  **Optional**: Add certified copies to the order\. If certified copies are requested, a replacement parchment must also be ordered\.
5.  Click “Proceed to Summary”\.

### Review the order summary

Confirm that the details of the order summary are correct and click through to proceed to the payment screens\.

### Enter payment details

1.  Review the confirmation details\.
2.  On the “Payment Success” page, you can click the “ID” link to view order details\.
