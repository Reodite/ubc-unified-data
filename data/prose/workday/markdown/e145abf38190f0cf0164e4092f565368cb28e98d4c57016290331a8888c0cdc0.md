## How to view your final exam schedule

### Go to your Academics app

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  Select “Student” in the navigation bar on the left\. 
3.  Select “Academics”\.

### View your final exam schedule

1.  Select the “Registration &amp; Courses” tab in the top menu\. 
2.  Click the “Final Exam Schedule” link in the “Exams” menu to the right\. 
3.  Select “Student” then “Final Exams” from the top menu bar\. 
4.  Select the exam period under the “Scheduled Assessments” header\.
