If you want to give someone permission to access your academic or financial record, or to pick\-up documents on your behalf, you can grant them third\-party authorization\. 

Learn more about [third\-party authorization](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/third-party-authorization/)\. 

> – You may enter up to three authorized third parties\.  
> – Authorized third parties can contact UBC for your information but cannot act on your behalf \(e\.g\. order transcripts, request refunds\)\.  
> – You must complete every field in the third\-party authorization form\. You can choose which permissions to grant to your third party

## How to add or change third\-party authorizations

### Go to your personal information in Workday

1.  Log into your Workday account at [myworkday\.ubc\.ca](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. 
2.  Click your user icon in the top right corner, then click the “View Profile” button\.   
    [Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Profile_box.png)
3.  In your profile, click the “Personal” menu item under “Summary”\. **Do** **not** select “Personal” in the left sidebar\.  
    [Image](https://student-workday-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Personal_box.png)
4.   Click the “Additional Data” tab in the top menu\.   
    

### Add or update your third\-party authorizations

1.  Scroll down to find the “Third Party Authorization” section\. 
2.  Click the “Edit” button\. 
3.  Read the instructions and terms of use for third\-party authorizations\. 
4.  Fill out all fields in this section\. You do not have to grant your third\-party all permissions, but you do need to input a response in each field\. 
    -   The “Authorization code” is a code you create and share with your third\-party contact so UBC can verify their identity\. This can be a word, a number, or any combination of letters, numbers or symbols\. 
5.  Click the “OK” button to save your third\-party authorization information\.
