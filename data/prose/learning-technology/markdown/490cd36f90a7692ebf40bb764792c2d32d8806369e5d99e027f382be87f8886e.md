**iClicker Cloud is an online student response system that allows you to respond to polls and quizzes during a class session, using your computer or mobile device**\. When you answer questions, your instructor receives your responses instantly and may share the results and the correct answers during the class session or afterward\.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Access** | *Immediate* | You can [set up an iClicker student account](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-student-guide/#set-up-account), then use the same account to [log in to iClicker](https://student.iclicker.com/#/login)\. |
| **Bandwidth** | *Yes* | Low demand on internet connections\. |
| **Privacy** | *Yes* | Complies with UBC’s privacy requirements\. |

### What will I use it for?

Your instructor may have you use iClicker Cloud for a variety of activities:

-   Test your understanding or share your opinions at different points during a class session
-   Participate in peer instruction, where you answer a question, discuss it in small groups, and answer again
-   Record your attendance at a class session
-   Study outside of class sessions

This tool guide was last reviewed in November 2025 with version 7\.2\.0 of iClicker Cloud\.

### Set up an iClicker Cloud account

Note that if you have already made an iClicker Cloud account for another course, you can use that same account to log in and [manually add another iClicker course to your account](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-student-guide/#add-course)\.

1.  If you have not already made an iClicker account for another course, go to the [iClicker Student account creation page](https://app.reef-education.com/#/account/create)\.
2.  Type "University of British Columbia" into the "Find Your Institution" field, choose **University of British Columbia** from the search results, and click **Next**\.
3.  Enter the rest of your information\.
    -   For iClicker and Canvas to sync, your iClicker account email must match your default email in your Canvas account\. You can check and change which email you have listed as default in Canvas by following the [instructions under “Tips” section of the “Manage Canvas notifications” accordion](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/canvas-student-guide/#manage-notifications)\.
    -   Make sure to enter your UBC student number into the "Student ID" field\. This number will link your Canvas account to your iClicker account, so please enter it accurately and only use it for one iClicker account\.
4.  Once you are ready, agree to the **Privacy Notice / Terms of Use** and the **treatment of personal information**, then click **Create Account**\.
    -   If you are not comfortable agreeing to these items with your regular email address, you can set up an anonymized email address to use instead\. Please note that your instructor may see this email address in the iClicker activity reports\.
5.  You now have an account and are ready to add a course following the instructions in the accordion directly below\.

#### Tips

-   **You only need to complete these steps once**\. After you have created your UBC iClicker account the first time, you should use the same account to access any subsequent UBC courses that have iClicker activities\.

[&gt; Add an iClicker Cloud course to your account](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-student-guide/#add-course)

### Add an iClicker Cloud course to your account

Most instructors will sync iClicker with Canvas\. As long as you have added your UBC student number to your iClicker account, you will automatically be enrolled in your course\(s\) with no extra steps needed\.

However, if you do not see your course in iClicker, your instructor may have set up your iClicker course to allow students to self\-enroll\. In this case, you can enroll yourself manually:

1.  [Log in to iClicker](https://student.iclicker.com/) using your iClicker account email and password\.
2.  Click the **plus sign** in the top\-right corner of the screen, choose **University of British Columbia** from the available options, and type in the name of the iClicker course that your instructor has provided\.
3.  Click the **course** you would like to join, and click **Add this course**\.
4.  You will now see and be able to access this course on your Courses page\.

[&gt; Join a class session in iClicker Cloud](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-student-guide/#join-class-session)

### Join a class session in iClicker Cloud

After you add a course to your account, you will join that course in iClicker each time there is a class session to attend\.

1.  [Log in to iClicker](https://student.iclicker.com/#/login) using your iClicker account email and password\.
    -   If you do not have an account, follow the [steps to set up an iClicker account](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-student-guide/#set-up-account)\.
2.  Click the **course name**\.
3.  Click **Join** when it appears on your screen \(it may take a minute to show up\)\. Note that this button only displays once your instructor has manually started the class on their instructor iClicker account\. The option to join the course will not be available if you log in early\.
4.  You will see a confirmation message, and your instructor will see from their iClicker instructor account that you have joined the class\.

#### Tips

-   **Joining a class session enables you to participate in the iClicker activities during class\.** These activities include answering polls or quizzes\.
-   **The time when you join a class session is recorded and visible to your instructor\.** To ensure you receive full participation credit, it is best to join on time\.
-   **Not all instructors track attendance with iClicker Cloud\.** You can ask your instructor if you are not sure\.

[&gt; Participate in iClicker Cloud activities](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-student-guide/#participate-in-activities)

### Participate in iClicker Cloud activities

When your instructor initiates an iClicker activity during the class session, you will see the options for responding to it in the iClicker interface\.

-   For polls, you may see a screenshot of the question to answer alongside the response options\.
-   For quizzes, you will only see the response options\.
-   For both activity types, your instructor will share the questions with you in the live class, though the format will vary based on whether your class is in person, online, or hybrid\.

Please note that some instructors may choose to show questions only for those students in attendance, so you may have to be present in class in real time \(whether it is in person or online\) to see the questions\. Instructors may also limit attendance\-taking only to those students whose devices are in the physical classroom\. You can ask if you are not sure whether these features will be used\.

1.  Once you have [logged in to iClicker](https://student.iclicker.com/#/login) and joined the class session, wait for your instructor to initiate an activity\.
2.  When an activity starts, you will see a question number and your options for responding on your screen:
    -   For [polls](https://mhe.my.site.com/iclicker/s/article/How-to-Participate-in-a-Poll-with-the-iClicker-Student-App), you may be able to see a screenshot of the question to answer\. You will respond to a single question in one of several ways, depending on how your instructor has set it up: by choosing one or more multiple\-choice items, writing a short answer \(up to 140 characters\), typing a numeric or formula\-based answer, or clicking part of an image\.
    -   For [quizzes](https://mhe.my.site.com/iclicker/s/article/How-to-Participate-in-a-Quiz-with-the-iClicker-Student-App), you will answer a set of questions by choosing a multiple\-choice item for each, then reviewing and submitting all your answers together\.
3.  Follow the prompts on screen to submit each answer\. When completing a poll, you will see an “Answer Received” for each question\. For a quiz, you will see a "Quiz Submitted" once you have finished the set of questions and submitted your entire response\.
4.  When an activity ends, your instructor may choose to share the correct poll response and/or class results\. Quiz results may also be available after the class session ends\. Viewing the iClicker poll and/or quiz results is dependent on whether or not your instructor chooses to share the results\. Check with your instructor if you are not sure\.

#### Tips

-   **You may change your answer as many times as you like, up until the point your instructor closes the answering period**\. Instructors cannot re\-open polls or quizzes, so make sure to review your answers before submitting your responses\.
-   **If you see an anonymous icon for a question, your answer will not be tied to your identity**\. However, your response to an anonymous question may still count for participation points\.
-   **Re\-join the session if you accidentally close or get bumped out of iClicker during a lecture \(e\.g\., you lose your internet connection\)**\. If you miss any iClicker activities during a class session, let your instructor know, as they may be willing to adjust your participation for that session\.
-   **You can review iClicker polls outside of class to help you study, as every question asked is recorded as part of the course**\. Click the “Class History” tab when you first log in, and read about the available [iClicker features that can help you study](https://mhe.my.site.com/iclicker/s/article/How-to-Use-iClicker-s-Student-Study-Tools)\.
-   **Make sure your iClicker work honestly reflects your learning and not anybody else's**\. Letting someone else answer your questions with your iClicker account counts as [academic misconduct](https://academicintegrity.ubc.ca/regulation-process/academic-misconduct/)\.

## iClicker Cloud FAQ

Find UBC\-specific answers to frequently asked questions by clicking any accordion bar below\.

[&gt; What can I do if I cannot see my course in iClicker?](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-student-guide/#course-access)

If you do not see the course in your list of courses, try to [manually add an iClicker course to your account](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-student-guide/#add-course)\.

If you still cannot see your course:

1.  Make sure your UBC student number has been added correctly to your iClicker account\.
    -   To verify, [log in to iClicker](https://student.iclicker.com/#/login), click the **three lines** at the top\-left corner, and select **Profile**\.
    -   Ensure that you entered your UBC student number—not your UBC CWL—in the "Student ID" field\.
2.  If you have added your UBC student number to your iClicker account correctly, it is possible that you have multiple iClicker accounts with the same student number\. iClicker will not add duplicate student numbers to the same iClicker course\.
    -   If you think this might be the case, [log in to iClicker](https://student.iclicker.com/#/login) using your duplicate account\(s\) and remove your UBC student number from the “Student ID” field\. Please ensure that your student number remains linked to the account you wish to continue using\.
    -   Log in to the iClicker account you wish to continue using\. Your courses should now appear under Courses\.
    -   If you still do not see your courses, please contact your instructor and have them re\-sync the course roster\.
3.  Make sure that your default email in Canvas matches the email that you used to make an iClicker account\. This matching will ensure that the right iClicker account is added to your iClicker course\.
    -   You can check and change which email you have listed in Canvas by following the [instructions under “Tips” section of the “Manage Canvas notifications” accordion](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/canvas-student-guide/#manage-notifications)\.
4.  If you are still experiencing issues with seeing your course, please [contact us](https://lthub.ubc.ca/support/lt-hub/#contact-info) at the LT Hub for further assistance\.

[&gt; What can I do if I cannot see my iClicker grades in Canvas?](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-student-guide/#grade-access)

If you have properly linked your iClicker account with Canvas \(i\.e\., by ensuring both your iClicker and Canvas emails match and your UBC student number is correctly entered in “Student ID”\), and you cannot see your grades in Canvas, it is possible that your instructor has not set up iClicker to sync grades with Canvas\. Not all instructors plan to release iClicker grades in Canvas\. Please ask your instructor or teaching assistants if you are not sure\.

If your instructor has set up grade syncing between iClicker and Canvas and you are still having trouble seeing your grades, please [contact us](https://lthub.ubc.ca/support/lt-hub/#contact-info) at the LT Hub for assistance\.

[&gt; What can I do if I have trouble connecting during a class session?](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-student-guide/#connection-issues)

You can try to troubleshoot some connection issues yourself using the [iClicker troubleshooting tips](https://mhe.my.site.com/iclicker/s/article/How-to-Troubleshoot-Your-Connection-to-the-iClicker-Student-App)\.

You can also reach out to your instructors or teaching assistants, as they may be able to provide real\-time assistance in helping you resolve issues\. If not, they will at least know that you were trying to participate but could not connect during the class session\.
