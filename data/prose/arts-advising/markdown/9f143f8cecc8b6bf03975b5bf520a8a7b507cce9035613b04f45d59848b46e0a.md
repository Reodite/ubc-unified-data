## First year resources

Attending an event is a great way to connect, but support doesn’t stop there\. These online resources are here for you anytime\.

New to UBC

The New To UBC Guide is a great place to start when trying to navigate all the resources available to you at UBC\.

**Explore the guide**

[Source link](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/)

Arts Peer Academic Coaches

Chat with an upper\-year student to set goals, refine study habits, explore strategies, and build a personalized academic action plan\.

**Find out more**

[Source link](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/student-support/academic-support/peer-academic-support/)

First Year Bucket List

The Faculty of Arts has a lot to offer outside of the classroom\. Check out events and participate to win great prizes through the Arts First Year Bucket List\.

**Learn more**

[Source link](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/news/arts-first-year-bucket-list/)

AMS Clubs

Looking to get involved on campus? Check out AMS Clubs\.

**Learn more**

[Source link](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/student-life/clubs/campusbase/)
