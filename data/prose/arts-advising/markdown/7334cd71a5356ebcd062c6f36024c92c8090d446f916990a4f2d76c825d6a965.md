## International students

As a UBC Arts student, you will be part of a diverse community that is home to some of the brightest minds from over 120 countries around the world, including over 5,000 international students\.

If your English language proficiency does not yet meet the requirements for direct entry into UBC Arts, the [UBC Conditional Admission Program](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/english-language-competency/conditional-admission-program/) offers specially\-designed pathway programs for international students that support the transition from high school to your first year at UBC\.
