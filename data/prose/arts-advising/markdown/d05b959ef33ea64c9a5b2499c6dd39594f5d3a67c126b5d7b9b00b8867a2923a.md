> Entrepreneurship is about taking chances to turn ideas into real projects, products, or cool new solutions that actually make a difference\.

It’s all about being creative, curious, and okay with a little risk\. As an Arts student, you bring a diverse perspective and an interdisciplinary way of thinking that sets you up with a solid foundation to make real change\. Entrepreneurship teaches you how to take initiative and solve problems, skills that come in handy no matter where your journey leads\.
