**What is your best study strategy/tip?**

Time management\! I really make a point to begin studying for exams in advance, so rather than spending 2\-3 days studying right before the test, I like to start a week before the exam and study 2\-3 hours a day\. This helps consolidate the information over the week, so there’s less blanking out during exams, and allows you to have more time for social activities/other opportunities\!

**If you could travel back in time to your first year at UBC, what is one thing you would do differently?**

I would push myself more to seek out social and career\-oriented opportunities\. I was very focused on academics, so I spent most of my time in my room studying\. Looking back, I could have studied much more efficiently to make time for other opportunities, and I would have encouraged myself to be braver in approaching new roles/situations\.
