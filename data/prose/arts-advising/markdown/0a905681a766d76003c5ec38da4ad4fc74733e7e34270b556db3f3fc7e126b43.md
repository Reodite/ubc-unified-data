> The Faculty of Arts is thrilled to announce that nine of its members have been awarded the prestigious UBC Killam Research Fellowship and Prizes\. The Office of the VP, Research and Innovation will be hosting a reception on June 5, 2025 to acknowledge the achievements of our researchers\. Congratulations to all recipients\!

---

### **UBC Killam Research Fellowships**

The UBC Killam Research Fellowships provide faculty members with support to pursue full\-time research during a recognized study leave\. These prestigious fellowships enable recipients to make significant advancements in their fields, contributing to UBC’s reputation for research excellence\.

-   [Dr\. Alisabeth Ayars](https://philosophy.ubc.ca/profile/alisabeth-ayars/), Department of Philosophy
-   [Dr\. Megan Daniels](https://amne.ubc.ca/profile/megan-daniels/), Department of Ancient Mediterranean and Near Eastern Studies
-   [Dr\. Naomi Schwartz](https://geog.ubc.ca/profile/naomi-schwartz/), Department of Geography
-   [Dr\. Mila Zuo](https://theatrefilm.ubc.ca/profile/mila-zuo/), Department of Theatre and Film  
    

---

### **UBC Killam Research Prizes**

The UBC Killam Research Prizes recognize outstanding research and scholarly contributions by faculty members across various disciplines\. Awarded annually, these prestigious prizes celebrate exceptional achievements that advance knowledge and reflect UBC’s commitment to research excellence\.

**Established Category:** 

-   [Dr\. Jonathan Ichikawa](https://philosophy.ubc.ca/profile/jonathan-ichikawa/), Department of Philosophy
-   [Dr\. Vin Nardizzi](https://english.ubc.ca/profile/vin-nardizzi/), Department of English Language and Literatures
-   [Dr\. Victoria Savalei](https://psych.ubc.ca/profile/victoria-savalei/), Department of Psychology

**Early Category:**

-   [Dr\. Alfred Hermida](https://jwam.ubc.ca/profile/alfred-hermida/), School of Journalism, Writing, and Media
-   [Dr\. Daniela Palombo](https://psych.ubc.ca/profile/daniela-palombo/), Department of Psychology

---

The Office of Research Prizes and Awards provides guidance and support for external awards and oversees the university’s internal faculty research awards program\.
