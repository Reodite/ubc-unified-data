## Building your Arts foundation

**How do I plan my UBC Arts degree?**  
UBC Arts offers over [90 program options](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/programs/program-options)\. Use the [Ways of Knowing Breadth Explorer](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/ways-of-knowing-breadth-explorer/) and [Workday Tutorials](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/) to map your courses, track your requirements, and plan a degree that reflects your interests and goals\.  

Find planning tools

*arrow\_right\_alt*

[Source link](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/degree-planning)

**What are university academics like, and how are they different from high school?**  
University gives you more freedom and more responsibility at the same time\. Lectures are larger, deadlines are yours to track, and research goes deeper\. Arts Academic Advisors and Arts Peer Academic Coaches are here to help you make that shift\.  

Find academic support

*arrow\_right\_alt*

[Source link](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/student-support/academic-support/)

**What are my degree requirements?**  
To earn your degree, you’ll be required to fulfill a set of Faculty\-wide requirements and program\-specific requirements\. The Writing Component of the Writing and Research Requirement is the only requirement that must be completed in your first year for the Bachelor of Arts degree\.  

Review your degree requirements

*arrow\_right\_alt*

[Source link](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/degree-planning/degree-requirements)
