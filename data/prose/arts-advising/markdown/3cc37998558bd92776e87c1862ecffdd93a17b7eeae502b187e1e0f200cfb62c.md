### **Department of Geography**

**Apartments can become sweltering in summer\. Why heat bylaws could be coming to a city near you

**How to live in a restless world  
**Dr\. Michele Koppes said accepting uncertainty about climate change can help people cope with grief and anxiety, noting that “control is a fallacy” and resilience comes from adapting to ongoing change\.  
[The Narwhal](https://www.thespec.com/news/canada/how-to-live-in-a-restless-world/article_80ac8d0f-96e4-52b2-9016-a02c133e834b.html) \(paywall\)

**How Taiwan is balancing between American and Chinese visions of energy dominance  
**Dr\. Philippe Le Billon argues that U\.S\. “energy dominance” is proving limited, as global energy control remains fragmented while China advances in clean energy and import\-dependent states like Taiwan face growing security risks\.  
[The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/how-taiwan-is-balancing-between-american-and-chinese-visions-of-energy-dominance-284701)

**Ex\-member of top climate body says Alberta pipeline deal is not compatible with net\-zero  
**Dr\. Simon Donner, a former co\-chair of Canada’s Net\-Zero Advisory Body, said the federal pipeline deal with Alberta is not compatible with Canada’s 2050 net\-zero target, arguing that governments should be transparent with Canadians about the emissions implications of approving such projects\.  
[CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/net-zero-advisory-body-9.7229357)
