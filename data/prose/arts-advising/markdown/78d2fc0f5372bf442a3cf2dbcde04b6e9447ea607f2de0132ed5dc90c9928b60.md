> From March 4 to 8, the annual Arts Multilingual Week was celebrated at the Arts Student Centre in collaboration with various units from the Faculty and beyond\. The event provided an opportunity to experience different cultures and languages from around the world\.

The week featured a plethora of activities designed to engage with the diverse range of languages spoken at UBC\. Students, scholars, and community members showcased their talents reflecting on multilingualism as a practice, discussing career opportunities for multilingual graduates and performing multilingualism through artistic means, including poetry slams and art competitions\.
