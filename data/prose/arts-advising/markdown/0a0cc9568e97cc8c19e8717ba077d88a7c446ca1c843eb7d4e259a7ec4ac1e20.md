## Featured opportunities

Faculty of Arts Bursary

The Arts Bursary Fund provides outstanding students with the opportunity to reach their potential, regardless of their financial situation\.

**Learn more**

[Source link](https://give.ubc.ca/projects/faculty-of-arts-bursary-fund/)

Arts Career Design Studio

The Arts Career Design Studio provides students with practical career\-building skills and guided visits to workplaces across sectors to design meaningful careers\.

**Learn more**

[Source link](https://give.ubc.ca/arts-career-design-studio/)

Humanities 101 Community Programme

Humanities 101 offers free university\-level courses to people living on low incomes in Vancouver’s Downtown Eastside and surrounding community\.

**Learn more**

[Source link](https://give.ubc.ca/projects/humanities-101-community-programme-hum/)
