**What is your best study strategy/tip?**

One evidence\-based study strategy I’ve come across that is tried and true is retrieval practice \(also known as active recall\)\. This technique actively attempts to retrieve information from your memory, reinforcing memory traces and improving retention significantly compared to passively re\-reading notes or re\-watching lectures\. Another study strategy I prioritise is explaining concepts in my own words\. This process helps identify gaps in understanding and integrates new information to existing knowledge, which strengthens encoding and creates deeper retrieval pathways\. Lastly, a fantastic technique I’ve recently learned in my cognitive science class is dual\-coding\. It works by combining verbal and visual elements \(such as words with diagrams, pictures, or charts\), which is a powerful way to engage multiple cognitive systems\. This is also particularly useful because dual\-coding theory suggests that the mind processes information along two separate streams: verbal and nonverbal\. It’s so incredibly easy to get overwhelmed by the number of studying strategies there are, but remember there’s no right way of studying\! So experiment with different strategies as you go and find what works best for your needs and goals\.
