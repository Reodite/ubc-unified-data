##### BRAINS BEHIND THE WORK

### Tell us about the team you work with\. What do you admire about them?

I am lucky enough to work with an incredibly creative team of staff and faculty\. Finding different ways to do the impossible is a regular daily task for us and I am constantly impressed by our team’s agility as they tackle the myriad of odd requests that come our way\.

---

##### LIFE OUTSIDE OF WORK

### What is a secret talent you are really proud of?

Although I stepped away from acting professionally a few years ago, I still enjoy getting back in front of the camera or on the stage a few times a year\. Each year, a few of us perform in a fundraiser for a student scholarship that focuses on the plays of Samuel Beckett, and I still work with film directors I met from my university days because it is so much fun and not ‘work’ at all when we get together\.

My other secret talent is making slow cars just a little bit faster\. I started racing in autocross over 20 years ago, and while no one is signing me up for Formula 1 – it is more fun than a round of golf and has led to some precision driving in a couple of car commercials filmed in town\. I recently had a chance to autocross a fully electric EV and can attest that the future is here quickly\!

---

##### WALK DOWN MEMORY LANE

### What is your fondest memory at UBC?

Many years ago, as a student, I met my future wife in an acting class\. Many years after that we were married on the stage of the Frederic Wood Theatre\. It’s still the best show I have ever been involved in and we continue to extend the ‘run’ some 25 years later…

---

### Is there anything else you would like to share?

I can’t stress enough how just a little laughter can lift the darkest days\. I try to find some humour every day and share that with the people around me\.
