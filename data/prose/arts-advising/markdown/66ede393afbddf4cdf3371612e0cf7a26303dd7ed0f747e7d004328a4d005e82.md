[Considerations](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/degree-planning/degree-requirements/bachelor-of-international-economics/#considerations-2)

BIE students may complete any minor program available to BA students, including those outside of the Faculty of Arts, with the exception of the minor in Economics and minor in Commerce\.

If the desired program is not available as a program option under the BIE on Workday and you are in the correct year level to declare the program, contact [Arts Academic Advising](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/student-support/academic-support/academic-advising) to have the program declared or to inquire about admission\.
