**What is your best study strategy/tip?**

Be flexible and adapt to the current circumstances\. Different courses will require different studying strategies, so starting to study a few days ahead of the final gives you the space to adjust your approach as needed\. Getting good sleep leading up to the exam and isolating core course concepts to focus on will also help with almost every subject\.
