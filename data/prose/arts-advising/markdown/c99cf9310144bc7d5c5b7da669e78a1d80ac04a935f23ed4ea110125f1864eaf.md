> You choose your direction\. We’re here to help you navigate\.

The Compass connects Arts students, staff, faculty, and alumni through a supportive community, events, and tailored resources shaped by what it’s really like to be a UBC Arts student—supporting you through your degree and helping you explore what’s possible beyond it\.

Each pathway below offers tools, events, and support to help you navigate your degree, find community, and prepare for your future\.
