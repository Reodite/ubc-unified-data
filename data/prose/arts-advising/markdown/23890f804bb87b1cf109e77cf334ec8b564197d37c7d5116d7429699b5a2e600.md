The [SSHRC](https://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca/home-accueil-eng.aspx) \(Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council\) has announced that 23 UBC Faculty of Arts researchers were awarded funding as part of Partnership Grants, Insight Grants, Partnership Engage Grants, and Aid to Scholarly Journals Grants\.
