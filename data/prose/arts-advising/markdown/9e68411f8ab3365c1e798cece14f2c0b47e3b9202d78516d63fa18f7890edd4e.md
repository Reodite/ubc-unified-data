Dean of the Faculty of Arts at UBC, world\- renowned Haitian scholar, and now, recent Grammy nominee— there is not much that Dean Gage Averill cannot seem to do\!

Listen to Gage Averill speak on Prof Talk \(CITR 101\.9FM\) and hear his thoughts on his new role at UBC , his experiences in Haiti and his recent Grammy nomination for his project, Alan Lomaz in Haiti: Recordings for the Library of Congress, 1936\-1957\.
