> UBC Faculty of Arts researchers are advancing 58 projects supported through the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council \(SSHRC\)’s Insight, Insight Development, and Partnership Development Grant competitions\.

The funded projects span a wide range of research areas, including artificial intelligence and digital literacies, climate change and sustainability, cultural histories, Indigenous\-led methodologies, labour inequalities, and social change\. Explore the Arts recipients and their research projects below\.

### **Insight Development Grants**

[Insight Development Grants](https://sshrc-crsh.canada.ca/en/funding/opportunities/insight-development-grants/2024/competition.aspx) support research in its initial stages\. The grants enable the development of new research questions, as well as experimentation with new methods, theoretical approaches and/or ideas\. Funding is provided for short\-term research development projects of up to two years that are proposed by individuals or teams\.
