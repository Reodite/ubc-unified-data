### Slide 3: Land Acknowledgement 

The UBC Vancouver\-Point Grey campus is located on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓\-speaking Musqueam peoples\.  

Unceded means that these lands were never sold, traded, or given up through treaty or other agreement\. The University’s location and history requires that we critically reflect on the ongoing effects of settler colonialism and structural racism and question what it means to teach, learn, and conduct research on these lands\.

### Slide 8: Strategic Planning Process 

*Ongoing validation and consultation throughout the process\.* 

**Phase 1: Planning \(November 2023 to February 2024\)** 

Establish the “team”, agree on approach and process\. 

**Phase 2: Listening \(February to July 2024\)** 

Facilitate cross\-faculty conversations, develop and validate strategic plan framework \(includes mission, vision, values, and strategic directions\)\. 

**Phase 3: Development \(August to December 2024\)** 

Facilitate strategic direction\-specific discussion, develop and validate goals and actions\. 

**Phase 4: Drafting \(January to March 2025\)** 

Develop a compelling narrative, agree on implementation approach and roadmap\. 

**Phase 5: Launch \(April to May 2025\)** 

Finalize and launch plan\. 

**Phase 6: Implementation \(May 2025 onward\)** 

Implement and sustain\.

### Slide 14: Vision 

Lead\. Empower\. Thrive\.  

The Faculty of Arts, grounded in our connection to Musqueam land and community, will lead in inclusive academic excellence to empower critical thinking, creativity, and collaboration, and to build a thriving future that is more just, sustainable, and full of possibility for all\.
