**What is your best study strategy/tip?**

Make “cheat sheets” while you’re studying for an exam for every reading, chapter, or section you’re working with\! Doing this recall and working with the material in a refreshed way often helps me realize anything I’ve missed\! These cheat sheets then are PERFECT for reviewing through exam prep\.

We’re all in this together, and there are so many wonderful people who will work to understand and stand with you through difficult times\. We’re stronger together\!
