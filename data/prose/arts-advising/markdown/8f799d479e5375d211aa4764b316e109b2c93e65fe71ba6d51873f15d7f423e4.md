> UBC Faculty of Arts thrives in research excellence as demonstrated by the multitude of research grants won by our scholars\.

Arts researchers obtain funding from all three major federal granting agencies – SSHRC, NSERC, &amp; CIHR\. They develop cross\-disciplinary partnerships, engage in knowledge exchange, and apply their research locally and globally\. Last year, Arts researchers received $34\.6 million in funding for more than 900 projects\.
