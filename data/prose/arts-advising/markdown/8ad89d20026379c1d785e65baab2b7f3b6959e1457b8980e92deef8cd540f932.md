### **Department of Psychology**

[A B\.C\. study gave 50 homeless people $7,500 each\. Here’s what they spent it on\.](https://www.ctvnews.ca/vancouver/article/a-bc-study-gave-50-homeless-people-7500-each-heres-what-they-spent-it-on/) \| Dr\. Jiaying Zhao led a study which found that homeless people are more likely to spend money given to them on housing\.

[The instinct to share our good fortune](https://www.wsj.com/science/the-instinct-to-share-our-good-fortune-51720ad3) \| Media mentioned a study co\-authored by Dr\. Elizabeth Dunn that gave 200 people $10,000 to spend to investigate if they would be selfish or generous with the money\.

[What do we really know about happiness? Probably less than we think](https://www.smh.com.au/lifestyle/health-and-wellness/what-do-we-really-know-about-happiness-probably-less-than-we-think-20230815-p5dwki.html) \| Dr\. Elizabeth Dunn and graduate student Dunigan Folk conducted a study which found that some activities people associate with happiness lack scientific evidence to prove they actually boost your mood\.

[Here’s what’s actually happening when you have trouble remembering a familiar word](https://www.wellandgood.com/tip-of-the-tongue-phenomenon/) \| Dr\. Todd Handy commented on tip\-of\-the\-tongue phenomenon, which is when you have a specific piece of information stored in your memory, but you can’t quite recall it\.
