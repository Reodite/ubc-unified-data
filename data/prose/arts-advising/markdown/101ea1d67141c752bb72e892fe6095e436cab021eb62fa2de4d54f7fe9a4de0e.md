> On May 26, 2026, the Faculty of Arts gathered for the annual Dean’s Awards Recognition Reception, celebrating faculty members recognized for excellence in teaching, research, mentorship, and creative practice\.

Dean Clare Haru Crowston opened by welcoming Leona Sparrow of the Musqueam Indian Band, recognizing the Canada Foundation for Innovation funding awarded to the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm Musqueam Archaeology Centre, co\-led by Leona Sparrow and Dr\. Andrew Martindale \(Anthropology\)\.

Dean Crowston also paid tribute to Dr\. Kirby Manià, an Assistant Professor of Teaching in the School of Journalism, Writing, and Media and the Coordinated Arts Program, who passed away this year\. Dr\. Manià was honoured posthumously with a Dean of Arts Educational Leadership and Innovation Award and was remembered as a deeply respected educator, mentor, and community\-builder\. 

Congratulations to all of this year’s honourees, whose work strengthens the Faculty of Arts and makes a lasting difference for students, colleagues, and communities\.

### **Dean of Arts Award**

This year, two Dean of Arts Awards were presented, recognizing faculty whose contributions to teaching, research, leadership, and community engagement have proven transformative to the Faculty of Arts\.

-   [**Dr\. Geoffrey Hall**](https://psych.ubc.ca/profile/geoffrey-hall/) \(Professor, Psychology\) was recognized for his internationally acclaimed contributions to developmental psychology, his decade of leadership as Head of the Department of Psychology, and a career defined by patience, diligence, and exceptional care for students and colleagues\. Dr\. Hall named Psychology Professor Emeritus [Dr\. Richard Tees](https://psych.ubc.ca/profile/richard-tees/) as his co\-recipient\.
-   [**Dr\. Sharalyn Orbaugh**](https://asia.ubc.ca/profile/sharalyn-orbaugh/) \(Professor and Department Head, Asian Studies\) was honoured for nearly three decades of teaching across Japanese literature, visual culture, and gender studies, and for her leadership as Head of Asian Studies since 2020\. Dr\. Orbaugh named Asian Studies Professor Emerita [Dr\. Tineke Hellwig](https://asia.ubc.ca/profile/tineke-hellwig/) as her co\-recipient\.

### **Killam Teaching Prize**

Six faculty members were recognized for teaching excellence this year:

-   [**Dr\. Alifa Bandali**](https://grsj.arts.ubc.ca/profile/alifa-bandali/) \(Assistant Professor of Teaching, Institute for Gender, Race, Sexuality and Social Justice\) — for care\-focused pedagogy and leadership in curriculum development centring historically marginalized communities
-   [**Dr\. Amanda Cheong**](https://sociology.ubc.ca/profile/amanda-cheong/) \(Assistant Professor, Sociology\) — for community\-based learning with Filipino Canadian, Chinatown, and Downtown Eastside partners, and for transforming T\.A\. training in her department
-   [**Dr\. Priti Narayan**](https://geog.ubc.ca/profile/priti-narayan/) \(Assistant Professor, Geography\) — for creating intellectually vibrant classrooms where students move from hesitancy to scholarly confidence
-   [**Dr\. John Roosa**](https://history.ubc.ca/profile/john-roosa/) \(Professor, History\) — for his critical pedagogy of care and his “dramaturgy of history,” teaching dressed as historical figures to embody history with vulnerability
-   [**Dr\. David King**](https://psych.ubc.ca/profile/david-king/) \(Lecturer, Psychology\) — for connecting course content to lived experience and founding PrideMind, a community of care for 2SLGBTQIA\+ students, staff, and faculty in Psychology
-   [**Dr\. Patrick Pennefather**](https://theatrefilm.ubc.ca/profile/patrick-pennefather/) \(Associate Professor, Theatre &amp; Film\) — for pedagogy centered on “learning through failure,” empowering students across disciplines to experiment and take intellectual risks
