> Is life just a dream and the world a stage? What’s really lost when something gets translated? Who didn’t fit the Roman mold?

Each year, the Faculty of Arts adds new courses to the mix, and this year’s lineup is asking some genuinely big questions\. We’ve done the digging, so you don’t have to\. Whether you’re hunting for the perfect elective or just want to take something completely different\.

We’ve grouped the courses into three categories for each term:

📘 **Open to All:** Whether you’re exploring a new interest or fulfilling elective credits, you’re welcome here\. No prerequisites required\.

🎨 **Interdisciplinary &amp; Hands\-on:** Courses that connect ideas and concepts across multiple fields, across Arts departments\.

🎓 **Upper\-Year Focus:** Courses designed for third\- and fourth\-year students that may have prerequisites\.

Each course also comes with emoji tags so you can spot the themes at a glance\. Check out the full [emoji guide](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/news/new-courses-ubc-faculty-of-arts-2026-winter-session/#emoji-guide) below for all the details\.

Here’s what’s new this term ⬇️

**Special Topics in Hispanic Studies: Afro\-Latinidad 👥 ⚖️ 🕰️**  
*RMST\_V 470A OR LAST\_V 425 \| Mon/Wed 12:30–2:00 PM*

What does it mean to be Black in Latin America? Who gets counted, who gets erased, and why? This course traces the African diaspora to the Americas across history, literature, politics, and cultural expression, from the brutal architecture of slavery to the geopolitical shockwaves of the Haitian Revolution\. *Taught in English\. Open to students with 2nd year standing or above, from all programs\.*
