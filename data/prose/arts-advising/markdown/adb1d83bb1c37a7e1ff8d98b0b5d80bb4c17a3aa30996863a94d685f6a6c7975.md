## Academic and cultural support

As part of Arts Academic Advising, Arts Indigenous Student Advising is here to provide you with academic and cultural support to help you meet your educational and personal goals at UBC\.

#### Advising staff are available year\-round to help with:

-   Course planning
-   Degree requirements
-   Academic concession
-   Graduation checks
-   Finding your on\-campus community
