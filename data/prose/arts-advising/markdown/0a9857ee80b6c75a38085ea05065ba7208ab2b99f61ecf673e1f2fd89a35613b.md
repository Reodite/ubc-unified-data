> The five strategic directions in the Faculty of Arts’ Strategic Plan reflect UBC’s core areas while addressing Arts’ distinct focus\.

The plan contains 22 goals and over 80 associated actions related to:

-   Excelling in research and creative practice
-   Advancing transformative education
-   Engaging communities for greater impact
-   Generating sustainable futures
-   Integrating digital innovation

Explore the goals and actions, as well as the latest stories, for our 2025\-2030 strategic directions\.

---
