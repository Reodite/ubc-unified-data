> Career planning isn’t always straightforward, especially when there’s no single path through an Arts degree \(or afterward\)\. That’s why career prep and networking events are here to help you explore your options, connect with Arts alumni who’ve turned their degrees into meaningful careers, and take steps toward a future that feels right for you\.

You’ll find lots of ways to get started, and Arts Career Conversations and the Career Design Studio are just the beginning—you’ll also find one\-on\-one support and plenty of other ways to explore what’s next for you\.

---

💬 **Arts Career Conversations  
**Panels and networking events where Arts alumni share how they got started, what they’ve learned along the way, and how an Arts degree can open doors in surprising places\.

💼 **Career Design Studio  
**A two\-day, cohort\-based intensive to help you explore your strengths, connect with employers, and take steps toward a career that fits you\.
