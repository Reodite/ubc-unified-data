**Year and program: 4th year, Economics and Psychology double major, Honours**

**Where is home for you?** South Korea

**What three adjectives best describe you?**

Observant, Empathetic, Independent

**What extracurricular activities have you been involved with \(UBC related or not\)?**

I am part of the executive team of the Economics Student Association \(ESA\), and have volunteered with the AMS Food Bank and the Korean Speaking Practice Forum\. I am also involved with several research labs within the Psychology Department, working as either a volunteer research assistant or an honours thesis student\.

**What is your best study strategy/tip?**

Don’t overthink it\! Of course, you should think carefully about what you’re studying, but I find that spending too much time thinking about how much you have to get done can make you feel overwhelmed and slow you down\. Instead, I suggest just getting started \(even without a detailed plan\) for 30 minutes and seeing how it goes\.

**If you could travel back in time to your first year at UBC, what is one thing you would do differently?**

I would make studying abroad a priority and plan for a full\-term exchange\. While I did participate in a Summer Abroad program in 2025, and loved the time there, it made me wish even more that I had spent a full term studying abroad\. I also wasn’t very aware of opportunities such as research abroad programs and global seminars, so if I could go back, I would be more proactive about seeking out experiences that are unique to university life – this includes studying abroad, but also the various events, programs, and opportunities available on campus\!
