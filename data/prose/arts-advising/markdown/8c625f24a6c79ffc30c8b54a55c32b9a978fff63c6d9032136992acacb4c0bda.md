### The Arsonists

**January 29 – February 8 \| 7:30–9:00 PM \| Telus Studio Theatre, Chan Centre for the Performing Arts  
Tickets: $13–$27**

In a town plagued by arson, businessman Biedermann remains oblivious as he welcomes two strangers into his home, even helping them store gasoline in his attic\. Max Frisch’s *The Arsonists* is a dark satire on the dangers of complacency, presented by UBC’s [Department of Theatre](http://theatrefilm.ubc.ca/)\.
