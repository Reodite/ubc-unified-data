**Year and program: 2nd year, intended Creative Writing major**

**Where is home for you?** Bogota, Colombia

**What three adjectives best describe you?**

Resourceful, Diligent, Caring

**What extracurricular activities have you been involved with \(UBC related or not\)?**

Since arriving at UBC, I’ve met friends and had new experiences in all sorts of places\. I joined Latin Dance Club \(LDC\) and K\-Wave because I enjoy dancing\. During the first semester, I joined the play my housing community was creating\. During the summer, as a Media Coordinated Arts Program \(CAP\) student, I joined the editing process for the CAP Magazine, known as ‘The Capsule’\.

**What is your best study strategy/tip?**

Studying is all about what works for the specific student\. What works for my study style is a combination of time management \(I prefer the Pomodoro style\) and listening to lo\-fi\. Having scheduled breaks encourages me to stay motivated for longer while working and gives me an excuse to reward my studies with a sweet treat between time blocks 🙂

**If you could travel back in time to your first year at UBC, what is one thing you would do differently?**

If I could travel back in time to first year, I’m honestly not sure what I would do differently \(it’s only been a few months, so it’s still pretty fresh in my mind\), but if I could talk to my past self or send her a letter of advice, I would write six words: ‘you’re going to figure it out’\. So I could free my past self sooner from the fear about classes, workload, and making friends, because by the end of the year it all fell into place\.
