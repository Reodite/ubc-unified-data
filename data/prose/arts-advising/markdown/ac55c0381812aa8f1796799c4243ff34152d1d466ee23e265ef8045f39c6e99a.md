## Shaping your UBC Arts degree

Everything you need to declare your program, meet your requirements, and plan your path to graduation\.

**How do I plan my UBC Arts degree?**  
UBC Arts offers over [90 program options](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/programs/program-options)\. Use the [Ways of Knowing Breadth Explorer](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/ways-of-knowing-breadth-explorer/) and [Workday Tutorials](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/) to map your courses, track your requirements, and plan a degree that reflects your interests and goals\.  

Find planning tools

*arrow\_right\_alt*

[Source link](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/degree-planning)

**When do I declare a major at UBC Arts?**  
Most students declare or apply for a major, honours, or minor before their third year\. Review your program options to find out whether you need to apply to your program or simply declare it on Workday\.  

Add a major or minor

*arrow\_right\_alt*

[Source link](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/degree-planning/year-by-year-planning/add-major-minor)

Questions about your major/minor? [Contact Arts Advising](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/degree-planning/year-by-year-planning/middle-years/)\.

**What are the degree requirements for UBC Arts?**  
To earn your degree, you’ll be required to fulfill a set of Faculty\-wide requirements and program\-specific requirements\. If you’re a BA student, make sure you’re on your way to fulfilling the Ways of Knowing Breadth Requirement in your second and third years\. Explore and create different course combinations using the Ways of Knowing Breadth Explorer\.  

Review your degree requirements

*arrow\_right\_alt*

[Source link](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/degree-planning/degree-requirements)
