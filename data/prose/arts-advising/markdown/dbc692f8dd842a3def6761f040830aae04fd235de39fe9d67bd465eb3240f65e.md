### Chart Your Degree: Degree Requirements

In this informative and interactive session, you will learn the details of Faculty\-wide Arts requirements and how your program requirements for your specialization\(s\) fit into your degree\.

-   [Watch a recording of the Chart Your Degree: Degree Requirements workshop\.](https://youtu.be/mlaoKcGI004) \(2022\)

### Chart Your Degree: Degree Navigator

Learn how to access Degree Navigator and use it for course planning\. Through a case study, you will understand how to interpret a Degree Navigator report and use it to make informed decisions\.

*If you are new to UBC, we recommend you take the "Degree Requirements" workshop before attending "Degree Navigator\."*

-   [Watch a recording of the Chart Your Degree: Degree Navigator workshop\.](https://youtu.be/Fk7tqVB6w5c) \(2022\)

### Choosing Your Major

The Faculty of Arts has a plethora of undergraduate programs to choose from, and opportunities abound no matter what your program of study is\!

This workshop featured a panel of staff from Arts Advising who shared their personal experiences in exploring and deciding on their program combinations, as well as what they learned from their undergraduate studies\.
