**What is your best study strategy/tip?**

I find being able to vocalize and explain your argument is a helpful tool\. I always imagine I’m presenting my ideas to a board of discerning professionals and find ways to defend my argument against their scrutiny and self\-critical argument\. If I am confident in my pitch, then I know that I am prepared\.

**If you could travel back in time to your first year at UBC, what is one thing you would do differently?**

I think everyone’s journey is special and significant because of their own choices\. So I would tell myself to trust my instinct and go with the flow\. Be unapologetic and get involved in any ways I am happy with\. Joining a club, participate in a society, do a research paper, talk to that professor, etc\.
