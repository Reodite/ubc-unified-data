#### **International Student Scholarship**

-   **Eligibility:** International Arts students in a BA, BFA, BMS, BMUS, or BSW degree program who have completed a previous academic year in Arts\. Please note that BIE students, and students with Long\-term Work Permit Dependent status are not eligible to apply for this award\.
-   **Amount:** $2,500 – $5,000
-   **Application deadline:** 11:59 pm November 5, 2026

[Application Requirements](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/student-support/funding/#application-requirements-29)

The International Student Scholarship recognizes international Arts students who demonstrate strong academic achievement, engagement in the Faculty, and the potential to make a scholarly contribution within their chosen field of study\. Preference is given to upper year students\. Preference may be given to students who have not received this award previously\.

**Eligibility**

-   Candidates must be International Students in the Faculty of Arts at the UBC Vancouver Campus for both the previous and current winter session\.
-   Candidates must be registered in a minimum of 24 percentage\-graded credits in the current academic year, or sufficient to graduate, in addition to having been registered in a minimum of 24 percentage\-graded credits in the previous academic year with no failures or missing grades\.
-   Minimum GPA of 75%\.
-   Students with University\-approved reduced course loads are also eligible\. Students who were on Exchange, or completed Co\-op may also be eligible\. Please refer to [Senate’s eligibility criteria](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?tree=3,299,0,0) for full eligibility information\.

\*Courses completed during summer terms do not count towards your GPA, or credit load for award eligibility

\*\*Courses taken as Cr/D/F are not considered percentage\-graded credits

    -   Engagement could be through a club, a peer group, a service organization, a sports team, an academic competition, a research position, published research or writing or another type of activity that you feel would fall under the umbrella of “engagement”\.
    -   Engagement in the broader UBC community is also acceptable\. \(Maximum 500 words\)
-   **Resume:** Which includes all activities relating to community involvement with dates \(Maximum two pages\)
    -   Both documents should be combined into one PDF saved as “Surname, Preferred name then Award title” for submission\.

Please ask your reference to watch for this e\-mail which will come from our [arts\.awards@ubc\.ca](mailto:arts.awards@ubc.ca "mailto:arts.awards@ubc.ca") address\.

#### **Premier Undergraduate and Wesbrook Scholarships**

-   **Eligibility:** Students who are eligible to apply for this award are determined by Enrolment Services, and contacted directly in late August or early September\.
-   **Amount:** Awards range from 20 Wesbrook Scholar designations \($1,000 each\) to renewable scholarships of $20,000 over two years \(Premier Undergraduate Scholarships\)\.
-   **Application deadline: The Faculty of Arts follows a UNIQUE application process with deadlines that differ from those advertised by Enrolment services**\. For the current Academic Year, the deadlines that invited Faculty of Arts students MUST follow are:
    -   8:00 a\.m\. on Monday, September 21, 2026: Deadline to complete “Stage 1: Students Request to Apply”\.
    -   Tuesday, October 13, 2026: Deadline to complete "Stage 2: Submission of Completed Application Package"\. Deadline for receipt of all application materials, including reference letters to Enrolment Services\. Only applies to students who are approved to apply to Stage 2\.
    -   Mid\-November: The Office of the Dean submits 20 nominated applications to Enrolment Services\.

[Application Requirements](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/student-support/funding/#application-requirements-31)

The Premier Undergraduate Scholarships and Wesbrook Scholars are the University’s most prestigious designations given to senior students with outstanding academic performance, as well as leadership and involvement in student and community activities\.

There are strict eligibility requirements which can be found on the [PUS/Wesbrook website](http://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/awards/premier-wesbrook)\.

Students who are eligible to apply for this award will receive personal e\-mail communication from the Faculty of Arts informing them of their eligibility\. If you do not receive this personal invitation and, if after reviewing the [PUS/Wesbrook website](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/premier-undergraduate-wesbrook-scholarships) you believe that you meet the award’s eligibility criteria, please contact your Enrolment Services Advisor to confirm if you are eligible\.

**Stage 1: Students Request to Apply**  
**Deadline: 8:00 a\.m\. on Monday, September 21, 2026**

The Faculty of Arts has hundreds of students who are invited by ES as eligible to apply for this award\. In an effort to select the 20 most exceptional candidates for nomination to the university wide competition, we conduct a pre\-screening process \(Stage 1\)\. At this stage we ask that you submit your application for review, without the inclusion of reference letters\. In reviewing your submission, we are looking to identify students with outstanding leadership experience, and strong contributions to their community\. Please follow the formatting institutions on the “student instructions \(pdf\)” found [here](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/premier-undergraduate-wesbrook-scholarships/)\.

**Personal Statement \(Max 500 words\)** – An important part of this application is letting UBC know who you are and what makes you extraordinary\. In your personal statement, you should speak to your most important leadership\-related accomplishments and experiences during your undergraduate studies at UBC\. Focusing on your leadership roles, activities, clubs, academic projects and/or research, sports, volunteer and/or work experiences, as well as your goals for the future, tell us what makes you a strong candidate for this award\. Your statement should be written in the first person, maximum 500 words\.

**Statement of Activities \(Max 1 page\)** – Your statement of activities should focus on your accomplishments over the last three to four years\. Your statement of activities must be no more than one page, Please follow the formatting institutions on the “student instructions \(pdf\)” found [here](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/premier-undergraduate-wesbrook-scholarships/)\.

**Application Submission** – Both documents \(Personal Statement, and Statement of Activities\) should be combined into one PDF for submission\. \.

**References** – Please DO NOT include any recommendation/reference letters at this point, only identify who you are planning to ask, should you proceed to the application stage \(Stage 2\)\.

One of your references must be from a UBC faculty member\. A faculty reference should be someone who you have worked closely with during your time at UBC and holds the title of Instructor, Assistant, Associate or full Professor, adjunct Professor, or Emeritus Professor\.

\*A Teaching Assistant \(TA\), or PhD candidate cannot be accepted as a faculty reference\.

\*Only students who are invited to apply in Stage 2 by the Faculty of Arts will need to seek reference letters\.

All students who request to apply in Stage One will be informed if they moved onto the next step – Stage 2: Submission of a Completed Application Package by the last week of September\.

**Stage 2: Submission of Completed Application Package to Enrolment Services**  
**Deadline: Tuesday, October 13, 2026**

Students who are invited to submit a full application will receive additional information on how to do so\. Application items will include an application form, personal statement, statement of activities and reference letters submitted by your referees directly to Enrolment Services\.

All students who submitted a completed application will be informed if they moved onto the next step — the university\-wide stage — by mid\-November\.

#### Russ Patrick Arts Undergraduate Research Writing Award

-   **Eligibility:** The award is made on the recommendation of the Faculty of Arts Awards Committee\.
-   **Amount:** $500 to $1,000
-   **Application deadline:** May 1, 2026

[Application Requirements](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/student-support/funding/#application-requirements-32)

The Russ Patrick Arts Undergraduate Student Research Award was endowed in 2009 by Russ Patrick to recognize the outstanding achievement in research writing by an Arts undergraduate student\.

The award is made on the recommendation of the Faculty of Arts Awards Committee\. Submissions are judged by professional standards and assessed on their ability to communicate the research and its significance to a non\-specialist audience\.

-   Submissions should be structured in a research paper format with citations and formal language, exactly as would be produced for a UBC course, and should not exceed 2,000 words\.
-   All submissions should include a nomination/statement of support from a faculty instructor and/or Undergraduate Journal editorial team\.
-   Submissions, including nominations should be submitted in PDF format to arts\.awards@ubc\.ca\.

If you have questions, please contact [arts\.awards@ubc\.ca](mailto:arts.awards@ubc.ca)\.

## Other funding options

Explore the financial support options available to you by considering a student loan, applying for a scholarship or bursary, or browsing through job opportunities\.

UBC provides financial support through scholarships, bursaries, and other assistance programs for both undergraduate and graduate students\. Scholarships are usually awarded based on merit, while bursaries are typically awarded based on financial need\. There are also awards specifically for different types of students, as well as external award opportunities\. UBC’s policy on financial support declares that no eligible Canadian student will be prevented from attending UBC for financial reasons alone\.

In UBC Arts, graduate students have access to a range of internal and external awards as well as employment opportunities as research and teaching assistants\.
