The UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs program is proud to celebrate the achievements of our student\-athletes at our annual Awards Ceremony\.

To read more about the 2025\-2026 TSC Award winners, check out the blog [here](https://recreation.ubc.ca/2026/04/10/2025-2026-tsc-awards/)\.

#### **TSC Legacy Award**

This award is presented to the outstanding graduating athlete who has left an indelible legacy on their club\. This award considers career athletic achievement, leadership and contribution to their club, and overall impact on the TSC program\.

**Eligibility requirements:**

-   Minimum three years in Thunderbirds Sport Clubs
-   Must have represented UBC at a competition sanctioned by their sport’s governing body
-   Preference to those who have served as an executive for their sport club for a minimum of one whole season
