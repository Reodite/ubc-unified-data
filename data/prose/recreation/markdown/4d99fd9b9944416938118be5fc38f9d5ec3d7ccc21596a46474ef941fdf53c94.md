Simon

Simon is a NASM Certified Personal Trainer and a fifth\-year Kinesiology student at the University of British Columbia with over three years of coaching experience\. At UBC Recreation, he has worked with a wide range of clients including beginners, students, older adults, athletes, and individuals managing health conditions\.

Simon’s training approach focuses on building strength, improving movement quality, and developing resilience\. He tailors programs to each client’s goals, abilities, and experience level while emphasizing a strong foundation for long\-term health and performance\.

His coaching style is supportive and educational, helping clients understand their training so they can feel confident, capable, and eventually train independently\.

*Fun fact: Simon can juggle with one hand\.*
