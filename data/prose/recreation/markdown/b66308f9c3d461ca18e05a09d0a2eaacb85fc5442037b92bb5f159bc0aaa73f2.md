### UBC Student Memberships

Student fitness memberships are valid within the term of purchase\.

-   Fall \| September – December

-   Winter \| January – April

-   Spring &amp; Summer \| May – August

To [qualify for the UBC Student membership](https://recreation.ubc.ca/2022/05/16/student-rate-eligibility/), a UBC Student must be enrolled in courses and paying/paid the Athletics &amp; Recreation student fee\.

## How to Purchase a Membership

Follow these steps to purchase a membership for the Fitness Centres and group fitness classes:

**Memberships are available for purchase online**\. UBC Recreation is using a new registration system; an online login will be required for all activities\. If you have not recently set up a new login, please follow the link below to connect your CWL or create a UBC Recreation online login\.

[**Create a UBC Recreation Login**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/home/new-reg/)

*For membership purchases using debit or cash, please visit the Student Recreation Centre or ARC\.*

## Using Your Memberships for Group Fitness Reservations

Reservations are not required for fitness centres and the BirdCoop Bouldering Cave\.

For group fitness classes, online reservations will be available 24 hours prior to the start of each activity\.

Members are limited to one reservation per 24 hour period\. If you are unable to attend a session, please ensure you [cancel online\.](https://recreation.ubc.ca/2022/01/21/fitness-cancellation-steps/)

[**Book a Group Fitness Class**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/drop-in/fitness-classes/)
