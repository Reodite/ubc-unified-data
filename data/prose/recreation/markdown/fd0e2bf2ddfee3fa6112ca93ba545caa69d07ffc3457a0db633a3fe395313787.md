### Thank you for your interest in joining the UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs Program \(TSC\)\!

Applications to join the TSC Program are accepted during specific intake windows, typically held every \~2 years\.

The most recent TSC Sport Intake Process was conducted in Fall 2025, with Squash and Wrestling accepted to join the TSC Program for the 2026\-27 competitive season\. The timeframe for the next intake process has not been confirmed\.

If you would like to be added to the list to receive updates about a future application process please email [sport\.clubs@ubc\.ca](mailto:sport.clubs@ubc.ca)\. Please include your contact details and a short description of your club\.

---

### Sport Club Eligibility Requirements

-   The purpose of the sport club is competitive and athletic in nature,
-   Does not compete in a sport currently offered within the TSC or Varsity program,
-   Competes in a recognized inter\-collegiate or club league affiliated with a provincial, national or inter\-collegiate sport governing body,
-   Club must be comprised of full\-time UBC students only,
-   Club must be fully led and operated by student\-athletes\.
-   Club must have pre\-existing, ongoing access to the facilities it requires for training and competition\. Access to UBC Athletics and Recreation facilities is not automatically provided for sports in the Thunderbirds Sport Clubs program, and rental availability cannot be guaranteed\.

### Why be a Thunderbirds Sport Club?

Being a Thunderbirds Sport Club allows you to represent UBC as Thunderbirds in competition\. You will also receive administrative support from the TSC Office and the department of Athletics &amp; Recreation\.

  

[**Thunderbirds Sport Club Program Highlights**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/home/tsc-application/#thunderbirds-sport-club-progra-143)

-   Official status as a UBC Thunderbirds team
-   Comprehensive executive and student leadership training
-   Dedicated administrative support from the Thunderbirds Sport Clubs Office
-   Access to UBC online fundraising platforms and support through the Development and Alumni Engagement team
-   Communications and Marketing support including access to an official UBC email, a Club webpage hosted by UBC Recreation, and story amplification via UBC Recreation channels
-   Access to letters requesting Academic Concession for travel &amp; injury

### Evaluation Process

Applications will be evaluated using this [matrix](https://recreation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/4.-TSCIntake-Evaluation.xlsx), which includes the following categories:

-   Club Presence at UBC
-   Membership
-   Student Leadership
-   Financial
-   Coaching
-   Competition Structure,
-   Facilities, Equipment &amp; Training
-   Risk Management
-   Alignment with UBC Athletics &amp; Recreation
