JJ Mahony

With over 35 years of coaching experience, JJ joined the UBC Tennis Centre in 2011 after serving as Tennis Director at the North Shore Winter Club from 2003 to 2011\. Since arriving at UBC, he has led the program as Tennis Director for more than 15 years, continuing to share his passion for the game with players of all ages and levels\. A highly accomplished competitor, JJ played in junior provincial, national, and international events, and proudly represented Canada at the ITF World Championships in the 35\+ division in New Zealand\. In his current role, JJ oversees all operations at the UBC Tennis Centre and remains dedicated to growing and promoting tennis as a lifelong sport within the community\.

Pete Grimsey

Pete Grimsey is the High Performance Director at the UBC Tennis Centre, where he has led one of British Columbia’s top programs since January 2020\. With more than 20 years of coaching experience at public and private clubs in North America, Pete has developed numerous junior athletes from provincial to national levels and helped guide many players to successful NCAA collegiate careers\.

From 2013 to 2019, Pete served as a Provincial Coach with Tennis BC, leading and assisting with training camps as well as travelling with British Columbia’s highest\-ranked junior players to several Canadian National Championships\. He has also travelled with players to the prestigious Little Mo International tournaments in California \(2018\) and Florida \(2019\)\. He has also done contract work for Tennis Canada including a U12 National Training Camp in Montreal and coached Canada’s three top U18 female prospects at the Vancouver ITF J60 tournament in 2019\.

Pete worked as part of Rebecca Marino’s coaching team from 2021 to 2022\. In this role, he assisted with her off\-season training in preparation for 2022 and travelled with her to several tournaments including the 2022 Australian Open where she successfully qualified for the main draw\.

Recognized for his contributions to player development and high\-performance coaching, Pete was awarded the Tennis BC High Performance Coach of the Year in 2016\. He combines an accomplished playing background with extensive coaching experience, bringing a wealth of knowledge and expertise to the UBC Tennis Centre’s High Performance Program\. Pete is passionate about developing athletes both on and off the court and helping them achieve their goals—whether competing at the national level, earning opportunities to play collegiate tennis, or pursuing a professional career\.

Thiago Barrios

Thiago Barros is a Tennis Professional at the UBC Tennis Centre with over 25 years of coaching experience across private clubs, tennis academies, and recreational programs in both Brazil and Canada\. His coaching philosophy emphasizes strong technical foundations, fitness integration, and inclusive instruction for players of all ages and levels\.

Since moving to Vancouver, Thiago has contributed to the coaching teams at the Jericho Tennis Club and worked under Gord Haukas Tennis, developing and leading lessons focused on skill development, fitness, and enjoyment of the sport\. Prior to that, he coached extensively in Brazil at Top Tennis, Chácara do Abaeté, and Niterói Racket Club, where he coordinated youth and adult training programs and organized community tournaments\.

Thiago brings a unique blend of technical tennis instruction and physical education training, with a background in physical therapy and multiple certifications from both Tennis Canada and the Brazilian Tennis Federation\. His coaching style is energetic, student\-centered, and rooted in long\-term athlete development principles\.
