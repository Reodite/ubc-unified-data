### Rules &amp; Etiquette

-   Track traffic moves in a counter clockwise direction\.

-   Slower traffic should use the outside lanes and allow others to pass on the left or inner lanes\.

-   No stopping while on track – please use the corners and warm\-up/stretching zones to rest\.

-   No spiked footwear is permitted on track\.
