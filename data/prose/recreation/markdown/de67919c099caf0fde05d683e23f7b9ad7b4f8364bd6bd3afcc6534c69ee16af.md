#### Long Term Locker Rental Terms &amp; Conditions

*Only debit or credit card payment is accepted for long term locker rentals\.*

-   Only one \(1\) total locker rental is permitted across the Rec North, ARC, SRC, and UBC Aquatic Centre facilities\.
-   Deposit must be paid by credit card or debit card
-   I agree that I will not damage or alter the locker
-   I agree that I can only access the locker while I am using the facilities
-   I agree that I will renew or clear by locker by 12 pm \(noon\) on the agreement end date and return my lock to the front desk in order to get my deposit back\.
-   I agree that the time on the agreement end date may change subject to facility needs and I am aware that I will be advised by staff if it does change\.
-   I agree that if I fail to abide by the above conditions my items will be removed, bagged and left at the front desk for **1 week\.**
-   UBC Recreation is not responsible for lost or damaged property or contents of the locker
-   Locker rentals are non\-refundable and non\-transferable
-   UBC Recreation reserves the right to access the locker at any time, without notice, for reasonable cause; including but not limited to, suspicion of possession of illegal property, complaints, inappropriate use of locker, or facility matters\.

### Short\-Term Locker Rentals

#### Rec North &amp; ARC

-   Bring your own lock to use with the available labelled, short\-term lockers
-   Maximum 2 hours while using the facility
-   All locks and content remaining are removed at the end of the day\.Any items left in specific Short\-Term lockers after the facility has closed will be removed and stored on site\. There is a subsequent fee of $25 for locker cleaning and storage that must be paid prior to obtaining belongings\.   
    *Any locks cut in this process will not be reimbursed*
-   In the event an individuals items remain in a Short\-Term locker beyond the allocated time \(2 hours\) a $25 fee will be charged for the removal of the items and cleaning of the locker\.   
    *Any locks cut in this process will not be reimbursed* 
-   UBC Recreation is not liable for any lost, stolen, misplaced, or broken items through the use of Short\-Term lockers at Rec North or the ARC 

#### UBC Aquatic Centre

-   Swim session lockers are available for $0\.25 or $1\.00 depending on the size of the locker\.
-   Maximum 3 hours while using the facility
-   Lockers are located in the Universal, Women’s and Men’s changing spaces,

### Hourly Locker Rentals

#### Student Recreation Centre \(SRC\)

-   Exchange UBC Card or government ID at the front desk for locker keys to be used with free lockers
-   Maximum 2 hours while using the facility
-   Passports, Nexus Cards, and SIN cards, are not accepted
-   Locker keys that are lost and/or returned after the time limit will result in a $15\.00 charge\.
