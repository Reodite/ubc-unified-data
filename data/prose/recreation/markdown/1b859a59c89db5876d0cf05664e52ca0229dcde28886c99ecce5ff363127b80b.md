### Court Etiquette

-   When entering or leaving the courts, please respect any matches in progress and wait for a break in play before moving through another court\.
-   Before retrieving a ball from another court please wait for a break in play\. 
-   A maximum of 4 people is allowed on a court at any given time\. 
-   Please be courteous to those who are booked on the court before and after you\. Please leave the court in a timely manner at the end of your booking and do not enter your court sooner than your booked court time\.  
-   Please put your phone on silent during your court booking, and keep your voice down so as not to disrupt players on adjacent courts\. 
-   No profanity\. 
-   UBC allows an 8\-ball maximum per court at any one time\. 
-   Please report to the front desk if you wish to extend your court time – availability may vary\. 

### Coaching Policy

-   Only UBC Tennis Centre coaches are authorized to provide instruction on court\.
-   Patrons who appear to be coaching will be issued a warning\. Repeat instances could result in suspension from the UBC Tennis Centre\.
-   Coaching behaviour includes but is not limited to:  
    -   Providing verbal, visual, and physical instruction  
    -   Setting up and executing drills \(e\.g\., feeding balls to players\) 
    -   Holding multiple bookings a day to provide lessons for various patrons  

### Clothing &amp; Footwear

-   Proper tennis footwear must be worn at all times – non tennis footwear can damage the court surface and increases the risk of injury\. 
-   Proper sportswear is encouraged\. A shirt must be worn at all times\. 

### Ball Machine

-   Before your booking, please ask our operations desk for the ball machine remote\. 
-   When you are finished using the machine, please be sure to pick up all the balls, return the machine to its original location, and return the remote 
-   If you are using the ball machine for the first time, please feel free to ask our operations staff for guidance\. 

### Food &amp; Drink

-   Food is not permitted on court\.
-   All drinks must be contained and sealed to prevent spillage – No pop or coffee\.
-   No chewing gum &amp; no spitting\.

### Garbage

-   Please ensure that all garbage is removed when you have completed your court time\.
