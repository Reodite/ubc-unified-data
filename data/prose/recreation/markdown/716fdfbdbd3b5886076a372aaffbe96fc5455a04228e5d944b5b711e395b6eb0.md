# Welcome to Move UBC

**Move UBC is a university\-wide annual initiative to increase physical activity and reduce the time students, staff, faculty and the UBC community spend sitting**\. Small changes can add up to big impacts—moving more and sitting less can improve both mental and physical health, impact academic and professional success, and contribute to wellbeing in meaningful ways\. This February, make movement apart of your day by getting involved with Move UBC\.

### Move UBC Keynote: The Power of Movement for Resilience with Olympian Clara Hughes 

#### February 24 \| Free In\-Person Event

As part of Move UBC’s 10\-year anniversary, join Olympian and mental health advocate Clara Hughes on Tuesday, February 24, 1:30\-3 pm at the UBC Vancouver Telus Studio Theatre, Chan Centre and a livestreamed UBC Okanagan watch party in the SUO Theatre \(UNC106\)\. Clara will share her experiences as a high\-performance athlete and explore how movement can support resilience, mental health, and overall wellbeing both on campus and beyond\. 

UBC students, faculty, and staff are invited to get their complimentary ticket below\.

[***Get Your Tickets Here\!***](https://tickets.ubc.ca/online/seatSelect.asp?createBO::WSmap=1&amp;BOparam::WSmap::loadBestAvailable::performance_ids=747C4B9C-1742-43E4-900C-CF5124599D50)

### [Image](https://recreation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Chan-Centre_Clara-Hughes_1280x940-768x564.jpg)

[**Presenter Information**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/get-moving/move-ubc-events/#presenter-information-19)

This year's Keynote speaker is Clara Hughes\. Clara is a multi\-sport Olympian, one of the few to compete in both the Winter and Summer Olympics\. Hughes earned two bronze medals in the 1996 Summer Olympics and four medals over the course of three Winter Olympics\. Her decorated past has led her to be recognized in Canada's Sport Hall of Fame\. 

Clara's story aligns closely with UBC's commitment to advancing wellbeing through teaching, learning, research, and campus culture\. Her voice spotlights physical activity not only for physical health but as a key contributor to mental wellbeing\.
