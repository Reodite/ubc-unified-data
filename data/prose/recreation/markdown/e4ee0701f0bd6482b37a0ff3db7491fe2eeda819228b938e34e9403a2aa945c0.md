**Gymnasium drop\-in sports** include regular sessions of volleyball, basketball, badminton, futsal and more\. These activities are free for UBC students, faculty, and staff\. CSC and UNA residents as well as the public are welcome to participate with a fee\.

When attending a drop\-in sport session, please visit the front desk to sign\-in and receive a wristband\. The wristband must be worn throughout the duration of a drop\-in sports session\.

[Rules](https://recreation.ubc.ca/drop-in/gym-sports/#rules-6)

-   All participants are required to check in at the front desk and wear a wristband during the entirety of the drop\-in sports activity\.
-   Participants should remain in the sport/gym that they signed into\.
-   Clean indoor shoes are required\.
-   No food or drink permitted in the SRC gymnasiums with the exception of water\.

[Equipment Rentals](https://recreation.ubc.ca/drop-in/gym-sports/#equipment-rentals-7)

-   Participants must bring their own equipment, or provide a valid ID at the front desk to exchange for equipment\.  
-   Current available equipment: badminton racquets, basketballs, volleyballs, futsal balls, table tennis racquets, and table tennis balls\.
-   Badminton shuttlecocks are available for purchase\.
-   Borrowed equipment must be returned, and any lost or damaged items will result in a fee being charged\.
