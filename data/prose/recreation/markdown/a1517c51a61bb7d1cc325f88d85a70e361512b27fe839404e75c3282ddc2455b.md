> ### Spring/Summer 2026 Schedule
> 
> The Women \(Trans Women Welcome\) and Two\-Spirit, Trans, Non\-Binary Fitness Hours for UBC’s 2026 spring/summer term will run from:
> 
> -   May 11th – September 3rd
>     
> -   Exclusive dates: May 18, July 1, Aug 3

With the opening of Recreation Centre North, Women \(Trans Women Welcome\) &amp; Two\-Spirit, Trans, Non\-Binary \(2STNB\) fitness hours is expanding\. The program will feature longer hours in a dedicated space at the ARC Fitness Centre and will be supported by staff who identify as a woman or 2STNB\. Feel empowered and gain the confidence you need to make physical activity part of your everyday routine\.

This program is open to anyone with an active [Fitness Centre Membership\.](https://recreation.ubc.ca/fitness-classes/memberships/) We encourage guests to participate in sessions you feel most comfortable attending\.

**Women \(Trans Women Welcome\)**

-   For cis and trans women who are comfortable in a space that centres the experiences of women\.
-   Mondays \| 7:00 – 9:00 AM
-   Wednesdays \| 5:00 \-7:00 PM

**Two\-Spirit, Trans, Non\-Binary \(2STNB\)**

-   For those who identify as Two\-Spirit, trans, and non\-binary\.
-   Thursdays \| 5:00 – 7:00 PM

*Please contact [rec\.operations@ubc\.ca](mailto:rec.operations@ubc.ca) with questions or support\.*
