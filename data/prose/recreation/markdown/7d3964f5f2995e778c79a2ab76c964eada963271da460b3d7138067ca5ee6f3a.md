## Check Out Our Wellbeing Walks on Campus\!

Looking for an easy way to get moving on campus? Twice a month, the UBC [Move U Crew](https://recreation.ubc.ca/get-moving/move-u-crew/) host a 45\-minute themed lunch walk and roll around campus\. These walks are a great way to meet new people, explore the campus, have some fun, and get moving\! 

-   July 8 – Tower Beach Walk
-   July 22 – Ice Cream Walk
-   August 5 – Pacific Spirit Walk
-   August 19 – UBC Art Walk

[Walk With Us This Summer](https://recreation.ubc.ca/get-moving/walking/)
