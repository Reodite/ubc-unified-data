### Online Reservations &amp; Drop\-In Access

All activities require reservations using the online registration system\. These sessions help ensure entry to visitors during peak periods\.

-   **REGISTER – Online registration is required\.** If capacity permits walk\-in guests, they will be asked to complete their booking using the online registration\. Reservations are available up to 48 hours prior to each session start time\. Please do not arrive more than 10 minutes before the scheduled time\.
-   **DROP\-IN** – Entry is first\-come, first\-serve and subject to venue capacity\.

### Activities

| Activity | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| [**Family Hockey**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/ice/drop-in-ice/#family-hockey) | These sessions require full hockey equipment and may include skaters as young as 6\. Games, scrimmages, and practice are not permitted\. |
| [**Figure Skating**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/ice/drop-in-ice/#figure-skating) | Figure Skating sessions are for skaters who want to work on their jumps, spins, and on\-ice routines\. Figure skaters may practice jumps and spins in the centre area of the ice but no full ice routines\. |
| [**Hockey**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/ice/drop-in-ice/#hockey) | Sessions are intended for full\-sheet, informal, and unofficiated games of hockey\.  Line changes on the fly and pinnies provided\. Full hockey gear is required\. **Goalie registration is free \(call 604\.827\.4324 on session date to register\)\.** |
| [**Public Skating**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/ice/drop-in-ice/#public-skating) | Public skating sessions are for skaters of all abilities and for lap skating only\. Wheelchairs and strollers permitted\. |
| [**Stick &amp; Puck**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/ice/drop-in-ice/#stick-puck) | Stick &amp; Puck sessions are intended for individual work on skating, stickhandling, shooting, and passing skills\. Scrimmages, games, and team training are not permitted\. |

*Additional rules apply for each activity\. Please review rules and etiquette for each activity prior to your next visit\.*

### Rules

-   All participants are required to check in at the Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre Pro Shop and wear a wristband \(or pinny\) during the entirety of the drop\-in activity\.
    -   All skaters 17 years of age and younger must have a parent or legal guardian sign a waiver at check\-in\.
-   All skaters 12 years of age and younger must be accompanied by an adult on the ice\.
-   Helmets
    -   CSA\-approved hockey helmets are required for all skaters 12 years of age and younger\.
    -   CSA\-approved hockey helmets are strongly recommended for all skaters\.
    -   Bicycle helmets are not permitted\.
-   Skaters must wait until the Zamboni has left the ice surface and rink doors are closed before entering the ice surface\.
-   Unauthorized coaching and lessons not permitted\.
-   Anyone perceived under the influence of drugs and alcohol will be refused entry
-   All participants skate at their own risk

Family Hockey Rules

-   All skaters are required to wear CSA\-approved hockey helmets\.
-   Skaters 16 years of age and younger are required to wear full hockey equipment\.
-   The following activities and items are not permitted:
    -   Goalies, games, drills, or scrimmages\.
    -   Training devices \(including cones\)\.
    -   Aggressive shooting or slap shots\.
    -   Body checking or unnecessary contact\.
-   A maximum of 30 skaters are permitted on ice at any given time\.

Figure Skating Rules

-   All skaters are required to wear figure skates\.
-   The following activities and items are not permitted:
    -   Games, sticks, pucks, or balls\.
    -   Training devices \(including cones\)\.

Hockey Rules

-   All skaters are required to wear full equipment, including a CSA\-approved hockey helmet\.
-   All skaters must provide government issued, photo ID at check\-in\.
-   The following activities and items are not permitted:
    -   Body checking or unnecessary contact\.
-   A maximum of 26 skaters and 2 goalies are permitted for each session\.

Public Skating Rules

-   All persons on ice are required to wear skates\.
-   The following activities and items are not permitted:
    -   Carrying of children on ice – strollers are permitted\.
    -   Speed skates, jumps, spins, or digging of skates into the ice\.
    -   Dangerous activities such as snowballs, throwing of objects, racing, fast skating, weaving through other skaters, etc\.
    -   Games, sticks, pucks, or balls\.
    -   Food or beverages on ice\.

Stick &amp; Puck Rules

-   All skaters are required to wear CSA\-approved hockey helmets\.
-   Skaters 16 years of age and younger are required to wear full hockey equipment\.
-   The following activities and items are not permitted:
    -   Goalies, games, drills, or scrimmages\.
    -   Training devices \(including cones\)\.
    -   Aggressive shooting or slap shots\.
    -   Body checking or unnecessary contact\.
-   A maximum of 30 skaters are permitted on ice at any given time\.
