## Inclusive Feedback Form

We know that we have lots of work to do when it comes to making our facilities, programs, and staff entirely inclusive\. Please fill out the form below for any questions, comments, or feedback about inclusivity at UBC Recreation\. Participant feedback is an integral part of growing our inclusive culture\.

UBC Recreation meets privacy principles and requirements with respect to personal information under the British Columbia Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act \(FIPPA\)\. The information disclosed on this form will only be shared with appropriate individuals associated with UBC Recreation \(employees, volunteers and other interested parties\) who will be directly involved in assisting with the request\. To learn more about this policy, visit [here\.](https://recreation.ubc.ca/home/policies/privacy/)
