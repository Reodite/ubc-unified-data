### Who We Are

For over 50 years, Intramural staff have passionately volunteered their time and delivered some of UBC’s most iconic events, leagues, and marketing initiatives\. Supported by professional staff with experience in sport management and student development, all of our student staff are challenged to grow as individuals and innovate within their roles as student leaders while being a part of one of the most immersive student experiences on campus and recognizable Intramural staff programs in the country\.

From the thousands of staff who have spent time in this program, many have called this experience pivotal for both their time at UBC, and in their lives\. If you are looking for a work hard, play hard community on campus, Intramurals could be the place for you\.

---

### What We Do

With over a dozen departments and 100 student staff, there are many opportunities for staff to gain experience in diverse areas of programming\. The Intramural staff program consists of three involvement areas – Leagues, Events, Media, and Digital Content\. Within these areas, there are multiple teams of student staff made up of Assistant Directors \(ADs\) led by one Director\. These teams work to create a positive participant and student experience across campus\.

From organizing tournaments, races, and leagues, to engaging students through digital content and storytelling, there is something for everyone\! Keep reading below to find out more details:

**INTRAMURAL LEAGUES

[***Hiring Open \| Intramural League Staff***](https://recreation.ubc.ca/home/employment/intramural-staff/leagues-staff/)

**INTRAMURAL** **EVENTS**  
[***Hiring Open \| Intramural Volunteer Event Staff***](https://recreation.ubc.ca/home/employment/intramural-staff/events-staff/)   
Building on a reputation for excellence that is many decades in the making, the Events team runs some of UBC's most iconic events\. Staff are challenged and empowered in this professional event planning environment and will gain skills in large\-scale event management, event production &amp; atmosphere, logistics efficiency, race management, and more\.

**INTRAMURAL MEDIA &amp; DIGITAL CONTENT  
**  
[**Hiring Open \| Intramural Volunteer Media &amp; Digital Content Staff**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/home/employment/intramural-staff/media-digital-content-staff/)  
Media&amp; Digital Content \(Events &amp; Leagues\) is made up of 3 teams who are responsible for creating and maintaining the Intramural brand story \- promoting participation, engagement, and recognition in all Intramural programs, using a variety of innovative strategies\. These staff help to define the role of Intramurals within the UBC community\. Media is the most unique, and technically elevated team in the Intramural program\. From videography, photography, creative content &amp; story\-telling, to internal community building through the lens, Media sees and does it all\.

[**Hear From Previous** **Digital Content Staff**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/home/employment/intramural-staff/#hear-from-previous-digital-con-31)

These teams are focused on driving awareness and registration for all Intramural programming through the creation of innovative content for a variety of professional digital channels\. These staff will gain experience in digital strategy, creative content production, video editing, business accounts, and more\.
