### Activities

The space is designated for individual dance, individual martial arts practice, or functional use, and limited equipment will be accessible within the space\. Any group exercise and/or routines can only be done in pairs, and martial arts can only be practiced solo\.

### Rules

-   Check\-in at the front desk for a wrist band prior to entering a studio\. 
-   **Footwear**
    -   Only non\-marking indoor, rubber\-soled shoes or bare feet are permitted\.
    -   High heels \(i\.e\. ballroom or Latin dance shoes\) are not permitted
-   No food or drink is permitted in studios except for water in sealed, non\-glass containers\.
-   **Martial arts**
    -   No sparring, and no group/partner martial art practice\. 
-   **Dance**
    -   Groups cannot exceed 6 persons\. Groups with more than 6 people must [rent the studio](https://sportfacilities.ubc.ca/src/) as a practice space\.
    -   No head spins\.

### Equipment Rentals

-   Participants must bring their own equipment, or provide a valid ID at the SRC or ARC front desk to exchange for equipment\.
-   Current available equipment: table tennis racquets and balls, skipping ropes, free weights, punching bags, and speed bags\.
-   Borrowed equipment must be returned, and any lost or damaged items will result in a fee being charged\.
