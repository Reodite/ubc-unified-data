## UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs

UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs \(TSC\) within the Department of Athletics and Recreation, is a competitive sport clubs program that features 13 sports, 15 teams, and over 330 student\-athletes\. Launched in 2015, these student\-led sport clubs represent UBC in competitions across Canada and the United States\.

As a competitive program, all teams participate in club or intercollegiate leagues at a local, provincial, national, or international level\. Each Sport Club must be financially self\-sustaining, supporting their operations through fundraising, sponsorship, athlete dues, and funding from the Department of Athletics and Recreation for coaching, National Championship travel, and competitive enhancement\.

In the pursuit of becoming the leading competitive sport clubs program in Canada, UBC takes pride in supporting the development of its employees, student executives, and athletes\. The Sport Clubs Office provides comprehensive student leadership training to equip executives with the skills, knowledge, and resources to successfully operate their clubs\.

## About Thunderbirds Sport Clubs

In 2013\-14, UBC conducted a Sport Review to ensure the long term sustainability of competitive athletics at UBC\. As a result of this review, 26 sports were designated as Varsity programs, while a new stream of competitive sport was added to provide additional opportunities for student athletes to represent UBC as Thunderbirds\. This new stream officially launched in 2015\-16 as the Thunderbirds Sport Clubs Program \(TSC\)\.

Since the inception of the program, UBC Sport Clubs have achieved considerable competitive success, including numerous national championships \(Artistic Swimming, 2022, 2023, 2024; Men's Tennis, 2022, 2024; Triathlon, 2024, 2025; Women's Ultimate, 2025\), multiple podium finishes at national championships, and numerous athletes representing their country at the international level\.

## Frequently Asked Questions

[**How do Sport Clubs compare to Varsity teams?**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/home/about/#how-do-sport-clubs-compare-to--236)

Although Sport Clubs and Varsity student\-athletes may have similar health and competitive sport goals, UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs are completely *student\-driven* and compete in sports not already offered within the varsity program at UBC\. Thunderbirds Sport Clubs may have professional coaches and receive administrative and financial support from UBC Athletics and Recreation, however, each club is fully operated by its student leaders\. These students are tasked with administrative, financial, and social responsibilities for their respective teams, which include scheduling games and tournaments, handling practices and facility rentals, fundraising, organizing competition travel, and hiring coaches \- just to name a few\. Sport Club executives and athletes are presented with an excellent opportunity to build leadership, teamwork, and professional skills that can launch their careers after their time at UBC\.

[**How do Sport Clubs compare to AMS Clubs?**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/home/about/#how-do-sport-clubs-compare-to--237)

While Thunderbirds Sport Clubs and AMS Clubs focus on student leadership and development, UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs strives for *competitive excellence*\. With weekly practices, Sport Clubs travel locally, nationally, and internationally to compete against other universities, representing UBC at a highly competitive level\. Sport Clubs athletes are challenged with athletic and self\-development, enriching their experiences at the University\.

[**How do Sport Clubs compare to Intramurals?**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/home/about/#how-do-sport-clubs-compare-to--238)

With student leadership driving the Sport Clubs and Intramurals programs, UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs allows students to compete at an inter\-collegiate level\. Sport Clubs encourage athletes to be competitive and successful within their athletic domains, while Intramurals allow athletes to try out new sports at the recreational level\. For athletes looking for a balance between competition and recreation, Sport Clubs provide the perfect in\-between\.

[**My sport is not listed, can I still get involved?**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/home/about/#my-sport-is-not-listed-can-i-s-239)

UBC Athletics and Recreation will review current sport club programs and will accept applications for the TSC program approximately every two years\.

**If an AMS Club for your sport exists**, we suggest you get involved in the competitive component of their club or the creation of their competitive component\.

**If an AMS Club for your sport does *not* exist**, we highly suggest you create an AMS Club to prepare for your TSC application\.

If you run a highly competitive team on campus and would like to take this next step, please email your inquiries to [sport\.clubs@ubc\.ca](mailto:sport.clubs@ubc.ca)\.

[**Am I eligible to be a TSC Athlete?**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/home/about/#am-i-eligible-to-be-a-tsc-athl-240)

To be a Sport Clubs athlete, UBC requires each athlete to be registered in 9 credits per term or registered in a UBC\-affiliated co\-op term\. \(A player can be registered in fewer than 9 credits if they have fulfilled all their academic requirements and are expecting to graduate in that term\.\) In addition, they must maintain a cumulative average of 60% in order to remain eligible for athlete status\.

[**I am a UBC Okanagan Student, how can I participate?**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/home/about/#i-am-a-ubc-okanagan-student-ho-241)

While the above teams are only available to UBC Vancouver Students, there are Sport Clubs available at UBC Okanagan\. Head to their home page for more information: [UBC Okanagan Recreational Sport Clubs](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/sports/sport-clubs/)

[**How do I get more information about a club or TSC in general?**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/home/about/#how-do-i-get-more-information--242)

If you would like more information on getting involved with UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs, either on an existing team or for a new sport, please contact the team directly through their team email or email [sport\.clubs@ubc\.ca](mailto:sport.clubs@ubc.ca) for general inquiries\.
