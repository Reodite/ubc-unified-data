Before you register your team, please make sure you have the following:

-   Team Name
-   Individual or Team
    -   Individual registrants will be placed on teams with other individuals to form a team
-   Just\-for\-Fun or Competitive category
    -   Just\-for\-Fun – results will not be tallied and scored in the overall event standings
    -   Competitive – results are tallied and scored for the event standings

Additionally, you will need the following information for all the participants \(4\-6 people\) on your team:

-   Name \(First &amp; last name\)
-   UBC employee number

-   Email address
-   Campus

-   Department or unit
-   Exceptionalities

## Steps to Register

Please note that registration will take part in two steps:

1.  Team Registration
    -   One team member \(designated as *Team Captain*\) will need to register your team\.
    -   To complete your team registration, the *Team Captain* will require a UBC Recreation login\. If you do not already have a login, [please follow these steps to set one up](https://recreation.ubc.ca/new-reg-cwl)\.
    -   Once the online registration has been received, the *Team Captain* will receive an email in 2\-3 business days with additional information about the next step\.
        -   Please note that if you are registering prior to April 8, email instructions for adding members to your roster and links to the online waiver will not be sent until after this date\.
2.  Roster submission &amp; online waiver – **Available after April 7**
    -   *Team Captains* will use the information provided in the email to submit a team roster
    -   Once individual team members are added to a roster, they will receive a notification to complete an online waiver\. All team members will need a [UBC Recreation login](https://recreation.ubc.ca/new-reg-cwl) to complete their online waiver\.

## Registration FAQs

[Who can participate?](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/register/#who-can-participate-103)

Current UBC staff and faculty from both UBC Vancouver campuses are all encouraged to partake in Sports Day\. All individuals with a current UBC employee ID number are eligible\!

[How many people are on a team?](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/register/#how-many-people-are-on-a-team-104)

The minimum number of participants on a team is 4 with a maximum of 6\.

[How much does it cost to participate?](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/register/#how-much-does-it-cost-to-parti-105)

UBC Human Resources and UBC Recreation are excited to offer this event for FREE\.

[What's the difference between Competitive Teams and Just for Fun Teams?](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/register/#whats-the-difference-between-c-106)

**Competitive Team:** This stream is designed for those who want to come out to win\! For competitive teams, participants must complete as many challenges as possible during their registered time slot\. Competitive teams are eligible to win the Winners Trophy\.

**Just for Fun Team:** This type of team is for people who just want to have fun\! These teams are not scored, and participants are welcome to participate in as many activities as they want\. These teams are not eligible for the Winners Trophy; however they are eligible for the Spirit Trophy\.

[One of our team members needs some accommodations due to a disability, health condition or other concern\. Can they participate?](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/register/#one-of-our-team-members-needs--107)

Yes, please provide this information in the registration form and contact us right away so that we can have enough time to make sure we can adapt the Sports Day experience to ensure your team has as much fun as possible\! We would never want a team member left out and there are a lot of different activities\. Even if your concern is a last\-minute request, please do contact us so that we can do as much as possible to make it the best experience\. You can reach us at [sports\.day@ubc\.ca](mailto:sports.day@ubc.ca)\.
