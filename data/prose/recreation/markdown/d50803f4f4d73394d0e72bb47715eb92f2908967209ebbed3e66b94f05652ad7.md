### Monday, September 14 – Sunday, September 20, 2026

Welcome to UBC Recreation’s Free Week – a week when you can try out a variety of fitness classes for free\! Open to UBC Students, UBC Faculty &amp; Staff, and the public, programs are available across several recreation facilities at UBC Vancouver’s Point Grey campus\. No experience is required for any of the classes and all abilities are welcome\! Please note Free Week is only available to to registered fitness classes and group fitness\.

### How to Participate:

-   **A FREE reservation is required\.**
-   Reservations are available 24 hours prior to the scheduled start time of each class\.
-   Use your online [UBC Recreation Login](https://recreation.ubc.ca/new-reg "https://recreation.ubc.ca/new-reg") to sign\-up\.
-   Please arrive at the class location 5 – 15 minutes before the start time to check\-in and claim your spot\.
    -   Note: 5 minutes prior to class start time any unclaimed spots will be given to walk\-ins who are on the waitlist\.

**If the class you want to attend is full, please sign up for the waitlist and stop by the class as any unclaimed spots will become available 5 minutes prior to the class start time\.**

### Program Types:

**After Free Week,** these programs are available through registration, memberships, or drop\-in:

-   **Registration Based Classes:** [Martial Arts](https://recreation.ubc.ca/fitness-classes/martial-arts/) and [Fundamentals of Fitness](https://recreation.ubc.ca/fitness-classes/cardio-conditioning-strength/) activities are offered as term\-long programs that require registration\.
-   **Group Fitness Classes:** [Group Fitness, Yoga &amp; Pilates, and Spin classes](https://recreation.ubc.ca/drop-in/fitness-classes/) are included in select [Fitness Centre Memberships,](https://recreation.ubc.ca/fitness-classes/memberships/) and operate on a drop\-in basis\.
-   **\*NEW\* Dance Pass Classes**: All of the dance classes are available through paid drop\-in or as part of our all access pass for the term\.
