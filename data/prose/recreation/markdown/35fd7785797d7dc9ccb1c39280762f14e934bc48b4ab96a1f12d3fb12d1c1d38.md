## Welcome to UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs

The UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs \(UBC TSC\) is a competitive sport program for UBC student\-athletes\. UBC TSC is home to fifteen sports and seventeen teams which include:

-   Alpine Skiing
-   Artistic Swimming
-   Curling
-   Cycling
-   Equestrian
-   Field Lacrosse
-   Nordic Skiing
-   Quadball
-   Sailing

-   Squash
-   Table Tennis
-   Men’s Tennis
-   Women’s Tennis
-   Triathlon
-   Men’s Ultimate
-   Women’s Ultimate
-   Wrestling

TSC student athletes get to experience representing UBC in competition locally, across Canada and in the United States\. Following a pay\-to\-play model, Sport Clubs provide the opportunity for students to be part of a team, build community, develop leadership skills and train for the sport they love\!
