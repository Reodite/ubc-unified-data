  
  

## A New Home for Recreation

Recreation Centre North \(Rec North\) is a 101,000 sq\. ft\. activity space supporting fitness, physical activity, and recreation\. With a four\-floor fitness centre, an elevated 200m track, three gymnasiums, wood and rubber\-floored studios, Rec North is UBC’s new home for recreation\.

The fitness centre features cardio, strength, and functional training equipment spread across four floors\. Vibrantly lit by natural light through glass windows, the centre offers a variety of zones for different workouts\.

### Indoor Track

An elevated, three\-lane track sits above the gymnasiums on the eastern portion of the building\. The 200m track is perfect for warm\-ups, walking meetings, and training\.
