## About the Team

UBC TSC Quadball is a competitive team with a year\-long season\. Based on the sport of Quidditch from the “Harry Potter” series, Quadball blends elements of rugby, dodgeball, handball, and even flag football, for an intense and exciting experience\. Quadball is the only sport that is fully co\-ed as well as being full contact\. We compete with other teams from all over Canada as well as the northwestern US\.

Quadball is an incredible and worthwhile sport on its own merits, regardless of its origins in Harry Potter\. What makes it really stand apart from other sports is the community and culture\. While everyone in our club takes the sport seriously and takes our training and performance seriously, off the pitch it is an amazingly different environment from what most people experience in traditional sports\. UBC Thunderbirds Quadball Sport Club attracts players who create a culture that is inclusive and accepting\. We’re looking for a unique mix of athleticism, academics, open, and social individuals; and those who don’t mind tackling others every now and then\.

[How to Join](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/quidditch-sc/#how-to-join-184)

Quadball is a fun way to get in shape, make new friends, travel and compete against other universities\. We also have many social events such as the Yule Ball and Rookie Night\! We welcome all students who would love to join our Thunderbirds Sport Club team and compete as an official UBC student athlete\. For more information please feel free to contact an executive member at [quidditch\.sc@ubc\.ca](mailto:quidditch.sc@ubc.ca)\.

We post information about our tryouts and open practices where you can try out the sport on our Instagram [@ubcquadball](https://www.instagram.com/ubcquadball/) or Facebook [@ubctscquadball\.](https://www.facebook.com/ubctscquadball)

**Eligibility Requirements:**

-   Attend all team practices, games and gym sessions
-   Notify Coach of all conflicts and absences 2 weeks in advance
-   Must pay team and tournament fees
-   Must be a full\-time UBC Student \(typically this means 9 credits a term, however, co\-op and exchange students are also eligible\!\)
-   Must be in good academic standing\. Must maintain an average of over 60% in order to compete or travel, but lower can still train with the team\.

**Tryouts:** 

Interested in learning more about quadball? Come check out our booth on Imagine Day \(September 3rd\) or come to our fun try\-quadball nights in September to try out for the team\.

-   **Try\-Quadball Nights** – Open for all and will include fun games that build skills for quadball and a minigame to get a feel for the game\.
    -   These are very informal and we do not expect players to already know how to play\! Just bring a positive attitude and eagerness to learn\.
    -   To our Try\-it nights please bring shoes you can run in, a water bottle, and comfortable clothing\. We provide the rest 🙂

**Team Fees:**

Team fees will be around $250 CAD per semester \(subject to change, hopefully will be lower\)\. These fees will cover your uniform, field rentals, equipment usage, travel insurance, travel costs, tournament fees and social events\.

We expect every member of our team to help with fundraising efforts, any excess funds we have will be reimbursed to members\.

**Practice Schedule:**

-   We will have 2 practices a week in Term 1 and 2 and optional team gym hours for TSC athletes once a week\.
    -   We will have one indoor practice and one outdoor practice per week\.
    -   Our outdoor practices will be on Mondays from 7\-9pm on MacInnes Field\.
-   We will have weekly TSC gym hours in Term 1 and 2, on Sunday Nights from 7\-8:30pm at Birdcoop Gym\.
-   We will also have social events throughout the term, so there will be no shortage of activities\!

**Competition Schedule:**

-   We aim to attend or host one local tournament a month\. Some tournaments for the following year include:
    -   UBC October Octopus Open Tournament
    -   Western Divisionals \(at UBC\)
    -   Canadian Nationals
    -   UBC Hosted Tournament
    -   Vancouver Quadball Cup

[History](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/quidditch-sc/#history-187)

The UBC Quadball Club was founded in 2010 as an AMS Club\. UBC Quadball plays in the Canadian league \(Quidditch Canada\) and is a UBC Thunderbirds Sport Club\.

Perennially, UBC Quadball is often one of the strongest teams in BC and most of its surrounding area, with victories against teams all over BC and Washington\. We have been battling to be the best team in Western Canada against the Alberta Clippers, SFU Quidditch, and Vancouver Vanguard Quidditch for over 12 years\.

In January 2023, Quidditch Canada changed the sport’s name to Quadball and our sport club followed suit\.
