### How to Get Here

The UBC Aquatic Centre is located next to the Student Recreation Centre and the War Memorial Gym\. The quickest ways to get here are walking, cycling, and public transit\. You can [download a map](https://recreation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Aquatic-Centre-Wayfinding-Map.pdf) to print\-off prior to your visit\. ### Drop\-Off &amp; Parking

*Ongoing construction in the North Parkade and Student Union Boulevard areas throughout the summer will result in delays and detours\. Visitors using North Parkade or pick\-up and drop\-off zones along Student Union Boulevard should plan ahead\. [Read more](https://recreation.ubc.ca/2024/06/27/north-parkade-delays/)\.*

-   **Drop\-off only:** Pick\-up and drop\-off can be facilitated via the following\.
    -   -   West end of the Student Union Blvd turn\-around \(note: do not park or leave your vehicle unattached in this area\)
        -   [University Blvd Lot](https://parking.ubc.ca/university-centre-lot) \(do not leave your vehicle unattended in this area without parking\)

-   **For short\-term parking \(up to 2 hours\):**
    -   -   University Blvd Lot has 100\+ short\-term parking spots within very close proximity to the UBC Aquatic Centre\.
        -   North Parkade has 60 spots signed and available for visitors\.
        -   Thunderbird Parking provides overflow parking\.

-   **Visiting us for longer than 2 hours?**
    -   -   Day parking is available at North Parkade\. For information about rates and hours, please see [UBC Parking](https://parking.ubc.ca/)\.

**Note: Due to construction in area, we encourage visitors to allow for extra time for their trips\.**
