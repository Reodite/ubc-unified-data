## About the Team

The UBC Thunderbirds Curling Sport Club is a competitive curling team that competes provincially and nationally with tournaments under Canada West, U Sports, Curl BC and Curling Canada\. The season consists of BC Curling Tour Events, local competitions and weekly practice, as well as off\-season training and team activities\. Our season culminates in the Canada West Curling Championships, where we hope to qualify for the U Sports national championship\.

UBC’s Thunderbirds Curling SC is looking for passionate, determined and hard\-working individuals interested in refining their technical curling skills and representing their university at a high level\. Our roster consists of a Thunderbird and Gold Team, with 2 for male and female\. The Thunderbird Team will be representing UBC at the Canada West Championship, while the Gold team will be more focused on skill development\.

If you are interested in learning more, please email our team at [curling\.sc@ubc\.ca](mailto:curling.sc@ubc.ca) or check out our Instagram @ubccurling or Facebook page at UBC Curling\.

#### Team Structure

There will be two teams per gender \(male and female\) with four to five players on each and they will be designated as Thunderbird and Gold\. The description of each team is as follows:

**Thunderbird:**

-   These teams will represent UBC at the Canada West Championship with the goal of reaching the U Sports National Championships\. The Thunderbird Teams will be competing in the following tournaments: Kamloops Crown of Curling, Abbotsford Curling Bonspiel, Canada West Curling Championships, U Sports National Championships\* \(\*pending qualification\)\. Other competitions may be added to the schedule so be on the lookout\!

**Gold:**

-   These teams will be focused more on skills development during the year with an emphasis on bridging the gap between junior and men/women curling\. Competitions have not been confirmed but will be announced soon\!

Men’s Team Schedule:

-   Program Wide Practice – Sunday @10am\-12pm and Wednesday @9pm\-11pm \(Wednesday to be confirmed\)

Women’s Team Schedule:

-   Program Wide Practice – Sunday @10am\-12pm and Wednesday @9pm\-11pm at Richmond Curling Club \(Wednesday to be confirmed\)

[How to Join](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/curling-sc/#how-to-join-106)

UBC Curling will be holding tryouts for the Thunderbird and Gold teams in September\. In order to showcase interest please fill out our expression of interest form [here\.](https://forms.gle/6yFJ2hBgSbQW77Lw6) You will also be expected to attend any scheduled training camps and team practices, as agreed upon with your coach, and to volunteer at any fundraising events we organize\.

Additionally, team members are required to maintain good academic standing – minimum 60% average or passing standards as identified by their faculty – and must not be on academic probation\.

Team members must be students at the University of British Columbia who are registered in an undergraduate or graduate 9\-credit course load per term in the current session, or fulfilling the requirements for a PhD/Masters, or Co\-op Program\.

*Students registered in diploma programs, certificate programs, or affiliated colleges/ programs with the University of British Columbia including: Carey Theological College, Regent College, St\. Mark’s College, and the Vancouver School of Theology, are not eligible for active membership in a UBC Thunderbirds Sport Club\.*

[Additional Information](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/curling-sc/#additional-information-111)

In addition to its competitive interests, the UBC Thunderbirds Curling Sport Club believes in fostering interest and passion for curling in the UBC community\. We plan to execute “Learn to Curl” clinics every winter term, to teach people about the beauty of the wonderful winter sport\. Curling is a pivotal sport in Canada’s history, as our country has consistently performed well on the world stage and we are looking to continue that legacy with our club and competitive program at UBC\.
