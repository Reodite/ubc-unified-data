The DMTSC is excited to offer a wide\-range of private lessons for hockey, figure skating and skating both on and off the ice\!  We offer private and semi\-private training for youth and adults with our main purpose to work on your desired developmental needs\. Each lesson is one hour in length\.

> **COVID\-19 Change:** During the initial reopening phase of the facility, Off\-Ice Private Lessons on a one\-on\-one instruction will only be permitted\. Groups of up to 4 will be permitted where the participants are from the same household\.

### How to Book a Private Lesson

1.  Persons interested in private lessons will be required to register online using their new UBC Recreation login\. If you have not previously registered in a program this year or in a 2020 spring break or summer camp, you will need to set up a new online login\. 

[**Create a UBC Recreation Login**](https://recreation.ubc.ca/home/new-reg/) 

2.  Purchase a 5\-lesson package\. You will use this package to register in each individual private lesson\. Please note that you will not be able to register in a lesson without purchasing this package\. 

[**Purchase a Private Lesson Package**](https://ubc.perfectmind.com/24063/Client/MembershipWidget/List?widgetId=de4a6e96-19a4-4104-9941-f612570b7513) 

3.  Using your package, register in any of the available private lesson times\.

[**Book a Private Lesson**](https://ubc.perfectmind.com/24063/Clients/BookMe4BookingPages/Classes?calendarId=a7fc390c-736c-44a2-a1ce-e58af5f9b404&amp;widgetId=15f6af07-39c5-473e-b053-96653f77a406&amp;embed=False) 

-   -   An Instructor will be in touch directly to confirm the lesson\. If you have any questions prior to the lesson, please contact: [hockey\.programs@ubc\.ca](mailto:hockey.programs@ubc.ca)\. 
    -   We understand it is likely you will build a rapport with your instructor\. After a membership is purchased and a few lessons are booked online, you may connect directly with them to book time that may not be listed online\. You can connect with our Instructors through email\.

##### Please Note:

-   Lessons must be booked three days in advance\.
-   Once you have completed 5 private lessons, you will be required to purchase another 5\-pass membership if you wish to continue\.
-   Off\-Ice Hockey Lessons will take place on our Off\-Ice shooting range and dry\-floor space\. Lessons will be tailored to your individual needs\.
