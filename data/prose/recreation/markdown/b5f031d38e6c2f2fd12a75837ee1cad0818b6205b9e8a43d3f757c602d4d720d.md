> ### BirdCoop Bouldering Cave Closing July 17
> 
> To allow for construction on the new bouldering facility, the existing bouldering cave will be closed for the remainder of summer 2026\. It will re\-open as part of a new bouldering experience in the fall of 2026\.

*A new bouldering experience will open at UBC in the fall of 2026\.*

*The space will feature 207 linear feet of climbing terrain designed both to challenge experienced boulderers and introduce the sport to new people\. The wall angles range from \+4 \(slab\) to \-61 \(roof\) including a sizable amount of steep overhang to optimize the climbing height\. Three new feature areas will complement UBC’s original and beloved bouldering cave which will be integrated into the new facility\.*
