## About the Team

The UBC Thunderbirds Cycling Sport Club is a competitive road cycling team competing in the local Vancouver race series and the USA Cycling Northwest Collegiate Cycling Conference \(NWCCC\)\.

We aim to develop riders into competitive, safe, and smart racers to form a high\-ranking team capable of winning elite races\. The collegiate season extends from April to June, although team members compete in races throughout the summer and fall\.

The essence of the UBC Thunderbirds Cycling Sport Club is fueled by team members’ shared passion for cycling and the community around this spectacular sport\. We provide an introduction to safe riding, developing key technical skills, and learning how to win races\.

[How to Join](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/cycling-sc/#how-to-join-211)

The UBC Thunderbirds Cycling Sport Club provides an opportunity for athletes to compete in high\-level sport while granting new riders a comprehensive immersion into the sport of cycling\. 

#### **Eligibility Requirements:**

No past racing experience is required\. We are looking for hard\-working riders with good attitudes\. Please ensure that you have the right equipment \(drop bar road bike with skinny tires and working gears\)\.

Members are required to fulfill the following:

-   Full\-time student \(minimum 9 credits per semester\)
-   Weekly Saturday rides during the Winter session at 9 AM at Thunderbird Stadium\. \(NOTE: Athletes must be rostered after tryouts to attend group rides\.\)
-   Additional weekly training sessions
-   Members will need a valid passport and UCI Race License in order to compete in the USA\. \(Note: Do not purchase a new race license until after January 1st for racing in 2025\.\)

#### **Tryouts:** 

The 2025/2026 team will be capped at around 26 athletes\. Tryouts will be based on an athlete’s level of commitment to the team and general athleticism\. We expect all members to ride safely and to follow the rules of the road\. Attendance at weekly Saturday rides and completion of the required training sessions will be key factors in making the team\.

Although we look for cycling ability, we also focus on prospective development and contribution to a positive team environment\. We are scoping for athletes who are eager and capable of competing come race season\.

2025/2026 season try out dates:

-   September 13th \(Open ride, not part of try outs, but we encourage you to attend\!\)
-   September 20th \(Try outs 1, open to all\)
-   September 27th \(Try outs 2, only if invited\)

Feel free to contact us at cycling\.sc@ubc\.ca if you have any questions\.

#### **Team Fees:**

The 2025/2026 Team Fees are subject to change\. If these fees pose a barrier to you joining our team, please get in touch with us as soon as possible\.

-   TSC Athlete Membership fee: $100 \(One time fee\)
-   Team Fee: $100 per term \(Tehe funding from team fees goes directly back into athlete’s racing\)
-   Cost of team kit: TBD \(It will be at a reduced team rate\)

#### **Training Schedule:**

Weekly Saturday morning team rides at 9 AM leaving from Thunderbird Stadium from September through April\. The ride usually lasts 3 to 5 hours\. Additional indoor and outdoor training sessions will be optional during the week\. There are no mandatory group rides over stat holidays, reading week or exam period\.

#### **Competition Schedule:**

Our competition schedule is comprised of both local and collegiate racing\. Local racing begins in March and occurs almost every weekend till the end of June\. The Vancouver racing scene is competitive and growing, making for tight and fun racing\!

The collegiate series will take place from April to June\. Races are around 1 to 13 hours driving distance from Vancouver, depending on the host\. For out\-of\-town races, the team will usually depart from Vancouver on Fridays \(often later in the day if possible, to allow for members to attend their classes or work\), and drive back after races finish on Sunday morning\. A collegiate weekend includes a road race and team time trial on Saturday and a criterium on Sunday morning\.

Only races held during the school year will be mandatory to attend, although summer racing is strongly encouraged\!  

#### **Academic Requirements:**

Team members must maintain good academic standing – minimum 60% average or passing standards as identified by their faculty – and must not be on academic probation\.

Team members must be students at the University of British Columbia who are registered in an Undergraduate or Graduate 9\-credit course load per term in the current winter session, or fulfilling the requirements for a PhD/Masters, or Co\-op Program\. \(Students registered in diploma programs, certificate programs, or affiliated colleges/ programs with the University of British Columbia including Carey Theological College, Regent College, St\. Mark’s College, and the Vancouver School of Theology, are not eligible for active membership in a UBC Thunderbirds Sport Club\.\)

[History](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs/cycling-sc/#history-214)

As a part of the UBC AMS Bike Club, our team dates back to the 1990’s but was revived after many years of dormancy\. Following the return of the competitive side of the club in 2014, it has gone from just a handful of riders to more than twenty racers since 2015\. 

The 2016\-17 season marked our inaugural year as a competitive sport club with UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs\. The club has seen better overall standings since joining TSC\. We have moved from 6th to 3rd, to 4th place in the end\-of\-season team standings in the NWCCC over the past years\. The 2017\-2018 season had several notable accomplishments including holding 2nd place overall in the team omnium for several weeks and winning the Men’s B and Women’s C omniums\. 

Former Club Lead and alumnus Margot Clyne transitioned from competing for UBC Cycling to riding with a professional women’s race team\. She was pivotal in our establishment as a Thunderbird Sport Club and brought together our strong women’s team\.

Throughout the last seven years, the team has continued to grow into one of the key development cycling teams in the North\-West, preparing athletes to continue on to elite racing teams\. In 2025, the team won the mixed\-team category at the Whistler Grand Fondo to round off a year of many successes in all disciplines and categories\.
