Move More, Learn More is an interactive health education program designed for women \(trans women welcome\) to learn more about health and fitness and find ways to engage with physical activity that suits their lifestyle\. From yoga, to weight training, dancing and more – come find what moves you\! Plus, participants can earn a variety of prizing for attendance\!

## Attend More, Win More\!

Move More, Learn More has an attendance based prizing initiative in which you could win the following\!

-   **Attend 7 classes and win a lululemon yoga mat\!**
-   **Attend 11 classes and win a Fitbit or lululemon belt bag\!**
