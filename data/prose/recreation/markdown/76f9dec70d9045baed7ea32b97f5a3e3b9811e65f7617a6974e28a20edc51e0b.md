  

# Welcome to Yoga and Pilates\!

Discover a peace of mind and connection with the body through the practice of yoga or Pilates\. Each of these classes is open to all individuals – absolutely no experience required – and aims to increase flexibility, mobility, and muscle endurance\. Perfect for those looking for a quick escape from work or school, the newest Yoga and Pilates membership allows you to attend as many as 13 yoga and Pilates classes per week\! Come in, relax, and gain a sense of rejuvenation to tackle the rest of your day\.
