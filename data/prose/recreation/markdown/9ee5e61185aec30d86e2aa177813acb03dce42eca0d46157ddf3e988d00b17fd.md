UBC Rec’s fitness centres and classes include options for teens ages 13 to 18 with specific requirements depending on age\. Please note that a signed copy of our Parental Consent form is required for all unaccompanied teenage participation\.

### Ages 12 and Under

-   Access to fitness centres &amp; group fitness classes is not permitted 

### Ages 13\-15

-   Unaccompanied access to fitness centres &amp; group fitness classes requires:
    -   Completion of a Teen Fitness &amp; Facility Orientation  
    -   Completion of a Parent/Guardian Consent Form prior to the first visit 

### Ages 16\-18

-   Unaccompanied access to fitness centres &amp; group fitness classes requires:
    -   Completion of a Parent/Guardian Consent Form prior to the first visit 
-   Completion of a Teen Fitness &amp; Facility Orientation is not required, but strongly recommended 

## How to Access the Fitness Centres &amp; Group Fitness

### 1\. Download, complete, and return by email the Parent/Guardian Consent Form

-   [Ages 13\-15 – Parent/Guardian Consent Form with Par\-Q](https://recreation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/UBC-Age-13-15-Fitness-Parent-Consent-Par-Q-Form.pdf)
-   [Ages 16\-18 – Parent/Guardian Consent Form](https://recreation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/UBC-Age-16-18-Fitness-Parent-Consent-1.pdf)

Please ensure the Parent/Guardian Consent Form is submitted online prior to registering for an orientation session or your first visit to the fitness centres or attending a fitness class\.

### 2\. Set up a UBC Rec Login

A UBC Rec Login will be required to register for an orientation session and to purchase a membership\.

[Learn more about setting up a UBC Rec login](https://recreation.ubc.ca/home/new-reg/)

### 3\. Register for &amp; attend a Teen Fitness &amp; Facility Orientation

****Facility orientations are a requirement for individuals aged 13\-15*\.** *This step is optional for individuals aged 16\-18\.**

Orientations sessions are offered weekly as a 60 minute workshop and designed to help youth learn to safely use and move throughout the fitness centres\. Topics will include:

-   **Facility:**  Facility tour, fitness centre navigation, how to access changerooms, locker use and more\.
-   **Safety First:** How to set up and use cardio and strength equipment correctly 
-   **Gym Etiquette:** Respecting equipment, space, and other members 
-   **Form &amp; Technique:** Proper technique and movement patterns to prevent injury 
-   **Goal Setting:** How to create realistic, age\-appropriate fitness goals 

[Register for a Teen Fitness Orientation](https://ubc.perfectmind.com/24063/Clients/BookMe4BookingPages/BookingCoursesPage?calendarId=590b37eb-0fbd-46bb-9dd2-7e65ab536d57&amp;widgetId=15f6af07-39c5-473e-b053-96653f77a406&amp;embed=False&amp;sessionsMode=True)

For the orientation session:

-   Please wear your workout gear\.
-   Bring your own lock if there is anything you would like to lock up during the session\.
-   No large bags permitted in the fitness spaces\. Ideally, leave valuables at home\.
-   The session will focus on showing you the facility and you may get to try some of the equipment
-   Meet at the main lobby in Rec North and inform the staff at the main desk that you have arrived for the orientation session\.

### 4\. Purchase a fitness membership

Fitness memberships for teens are only available for purchase in\-person\. In\-person purchase of a fitness membership can be paid for using a debit card, cash, or a credit card on file\.
