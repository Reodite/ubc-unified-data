> ### Fall 2026 Tennis Registration Update
> 
> **Due to unforeseen circumstances, registration for all Fall 2026 adult tennis programs will be delayed until Monday, August 17 at 1:00pm\.** We recognize the immense interest in our tennis programs and this decision to delay our registration ensures that we can offer the best experience for all\.

[Info Icon](https://recreation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/Icon-_Info.png)

**If you’re unsure where to begin,** we recommend checking out our [Adult Program Pathway\.](https://recreation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/2024_Tennis-Adult-Pathways_Letter.pdf) This resource outlines our [“Learn, Practice, and Play” philosophy](https://recreation.ubc.ca/2024/10/28/winter-2025-learn-practice-and-play/) and shows how to progress through our offerings based on your skill level\. We utilize the North American and Tennis Canada [rating scale](https://recreation.ubc.ca/tennis/adult/level-of-play/), where players rate themselves from 1\.0 \(beginner\) to 7\.0 \(advanced\)\.

**Before registering,** please identify the rating that best describes your level of play during matches\. For all programs 2\.5 and above \(this includes UBC Faculty &amp; Staff \| Intermediate, UBC Student \| Beginner, Cardio Tennis and Adult Performance programs\) [a skill assessment is required](https://recreation.ubc.ca/2024/06/26/new-fall-2024-adult-assessments-for-2-5-programs/) to ensure appropriate placement\. If you’re unsure of your level, we encourage you to [consult the self\-rating guide](https://recreation.ubc.ca/tennis/adult/level-of-play), speak with your tennis coach, or [contact our reception desk](https://recreation.ubc.ca/tennis/contact) before registering\.
