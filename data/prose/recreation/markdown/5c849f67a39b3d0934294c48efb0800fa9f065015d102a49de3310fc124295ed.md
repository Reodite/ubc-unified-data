# Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre Rules

-   All participants and facility users must follow directions of Doug Mitchel Thunderbird Sports Centre staff\.
-   No Spitting in the facility, including bench floors, hallways, washrooms and change room \(Spitting is only permitted onto ice surfaces\)
-   Only authorized personal allowed on the ice or user groups with allocated times\.
-   No Alcohol to be consumed in building \(unless it is a sanctioned event\)
-   No Tobacco or Drugs allowed in the Building\.
-   No Dogs or other pets \(unless it is a guide dog\)
-   All Children under the age of 12 must be accompanied by an adult\.
-   Must follow all rules for the drop in programs\.
-   Immediately report all incidents and injuries to Doug Mitchel Thunderbird Sports Centre staff\.
-   Patrons must actively supervise children for whom they are responsible\.
-   The use of Rollerblades, scooters, Segway’s or skateboards are not permitted at the DMTSC\.
-   No running, fighting or engaging in other conduct likely to cause an injury\.
-   Photography is not permitted without prior consent from aquatic staff
-   Use equipment safely and properly\. Do not use equipment for purposes other than what they were designed for\.
-   Refrain from hanging or swinging on or from facility fixtures, equipment or amenities\.
-   Persons under the influence of drugs or alcohol are not permitted at the Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Centre\.
-   The Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre is not responsible for lost or stolen items\.
