Use the Player &amp; Team Finder Directory to search for individuals or teams to participate in Intramural League Sports\.

-   Use the filters to find players or teams interested in the same activity, skill levels, and competition options\.

-   Listings highlighted in navy represent teams looking for additional players

-   The directory is only for the purposes of connecting individuals and teams\. It does not guarantee a roster spot or registration in any of UBC Recreation’s Intramural Leagues\. All communication is up to the discretion of individuals\.

To list yourself or your team in this directory, please use the [Find a Team/Player form](https://recreation.ubc.ca/intramurals/free-agent-single-participants/free-agent-form/)\.

*Please note listings older than 30 days are automatically expired\. If you wish to remove your entry from the directory, please use the link in the email confirmation sent when your listing was approved or email [intramurals\.info@ubc\.ca\.](mailto:intramurals.info@ubc.ca)*
