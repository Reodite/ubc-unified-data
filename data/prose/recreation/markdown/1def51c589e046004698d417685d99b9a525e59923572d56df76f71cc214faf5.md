#  **Welcome to UBC Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day**

*Sports Day is brought to you by UBC Human Resources in partnership with UBC Recreation\.* 

### Sunshine &amp; Smiles at Sports Day

Posted on May 19, 2026

On a Friday afternoon filled with sunshine and smiling faces, 108 teams took part in the 16th annual Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day\. This year’s event included long\-time, favourite activities at the UBC Aquatic Centre, SRC, War Memorial Gym, MacInnes Family Field, as well as debuting brand new events at Rec North\.

Over 610 faculty and staff participated with many of their teams dressing up in costumes and showing incredible spirit throughout the two time slots of the event\.

The 2026 Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day event was made possible through the support of UBC Human Resources and the efforts of more than 60 event volunteers,

### Know Before You Go: Sports Day

Posted on May 4, 2026

Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day is almost here\. Read on to find out about what’s new, what’s changed, and helpful tips for you and your team to have the best possible experience\.

-   [Preparing for Event Day](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/#prep)
-   [Event Day Check\-in](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/#checkin)
-   [Event Day Information](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/#info)
-   [FAQs](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/#faq)

## Preparing for Event Day

-   **North Campus** – Sports Day is happening at UBC’s north campus\. Use the [course map](https://recreation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Sports-Day-Area-Map_FINAL.pdf) to familiarize yourself with key locations or check out the individual facilities for additional details\.
-   **Attire** – The forecast for Sports Day is promising but with some activities occurring outdoors and travel required between buildings, please consider the weather conditions\.
    -   Layers of clothing that can be added or removed
    -   Sunscreen &amp; costumes are also highly recommended
-   **Travel light** – A bag check is available after your event day check\-in, so we encourage teams to combine their belongings\. During the event, we recommend carrying:
    -   **At least one phone or mobile device per team**
    -   **Re\-usable water bottle\. We are going cup\-less –** In an effort to reduce the use of single use materials, this sports day will be cup\-less\. Water stations are located throughout the event course\.
    -   **NOTE: UBC Card or other photo ID is required for event day check\-in, but not during the activity portion of Sports Day**
-   **Make a plan** – Review this year’s [list of activities](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/activities/) – be sure to check out the various locations and activity levels\. Use this information to plan out an adventure that will work best for you and your teammates\.

**Activity adaptation &amp; modifications** – In addition to varying levels of physical activity for each station, activity leaders are trained to provide adaptations and/or modifications so that an activity is fun, empowering, and comfortable for everyone\. If you or a teammate does not wish to participate in an activity, there is no requirement to take part\.

## Event Day Check\-in

*Friday, May 1 \| UBC Life Building*

-   **UBC Card or other photo ID is required for each team member to complete check\-in**
-   Check\-in is available up to one hour prior to the scheduled start time of each session:
    -   Time slot \#1: 12:00\-1:30pm – Check\-in available starting at 11:00am
    -   Time slot \#2: 2:00\-3:30pm – Check\-in available starting at 1:00pm
-   Please plan to arrive for check\-in at least 30 minutes prior to your desired start time\. Allot additional time if:
    -   A member of your team has not completed their online waiver
    -   Adding a new member to your team
-   At check\-in, each team will receive the following items:
    -   Wristbands for each team member – please ensure these are worn until you’ve reached the après portion of the event
    -   Activity passport – track your progress both for competition and the best spirited teams
-   After check\-in, teams should proceed to the start area on MacInnes Family Field and be prepared to start at least five minutes before their time slot begins\.

If you or a member of your team is feeling unwell, please do not attend Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day\. Teams may replace a team member or activities can be adapted to accommodate persons unable to participate due to illness\.

If during the event you or a teammate experience an injury or feel unwell, please let an activity volunteer know and first\-aid will be provided\.

## FAQs

### How do I add someone to our team or replace a member of our team?

*Additional team members or replacements can be added during the event day check\-in\. New team members must provide a UBC employee ID and complete the waiver in order to participate\. Please arrive early if you require any roster adjustments\.*

*No further action is required if a team member is no longer participating\.*

### My team is no longer able to participate, what should I do?

*Please email [sports\.day@ubc\.ca](mailto:sports.day@ubc.ca) to remove your team from the schedule\.*

### What do I do if I or my team shows up late?

*Please ensure that you visit the event check\-in – our staff will get you out on course as quickly as possible, but we do require that all individuals check\-in prior to participating\. Please note that late arrival will affect the available time for activities as the course is only open for 1\.5 hours\.*

### Are accommodations available?

*Accommodations and adaptations can be made for most aspects of the Sports Day program\. When completing the online waiver, there is a prompt to share additional information that will aid staff in providing an inclusive experience including activity modifications, dietary adjustments, and accessibility measures\. Activity leaders and staff are trained to provide adaptations or modifications based on these requests\. If you have additional requests or concerns, please email [sports\.day@ubc\.ca](mailto:sports.day@ubc.ca)\.*
