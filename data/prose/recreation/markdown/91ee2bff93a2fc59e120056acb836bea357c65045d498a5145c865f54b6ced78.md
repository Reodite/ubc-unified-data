For the enjoyment and safety of all patrons inside UBC Rec’s Fitness Centres, please adhere to the following rules and etiquette:

#### Fitness Centres Rules

-   UBC Card or UBC Recreation Card is required for entry and must be given to staff to scan\.
    -   **Only physical IDs are accepted\.** In the instance of lost UBC Card, a Government Issued ID will be temporarily accepted for entry, however confirmation must be presented that a new UBC Card has been ordered via [UBCcard Online Services\.](https://services.ubccard.ubc.ca/#:~:text=UBCcard%20is%20the%20official%20identification,Library%20and%20UBC%20Food%20Services)
    -   Please note **memberships are non transferable**, and the **use of someone else’s membership/UBC ID to access the fitness facilities is prohibited**\.
-   No food, gum, or drinks except water are permitted\. 
    -   Please use water bottles and containers with a lid\.
-   Backpacks, bags, umbrellas, skateboards, etc\. are not permitted in fitness centres\. 

#### Apparel Rules

-   No jeans or jean shorts allowed\. 
-   Proper athletic shoes and workout clothes must be worn when in the fitness centres\. 
    -   Closed toe shoes must be worn **at all times and in every area** of the fitness centres\.

#### Equipment Rules

-   Do not move equipment between floors or remove equipment from the fitness centres\.
-   Small equipment can be requested from the fitness centre desks by asking a staff member for use in exchange for an ID, and then must be returned after use\. 
-   Only use equipment on which you have been given proper instruction on\. 
-   Please report any equipment malfunctions to the staff immediately to ensure the safety of all patrons\. 

#### Proper Etiquette

-   Ask staff for assistance if you are unsure how to use a machine or piece of equipment\.
-   Use bar collars/clips on all bars at all times – please ask floor staff for them\.
-   Use of personal chalk is allowed at the Olympic platforms\.
-   Do not drop weights except on the Olympic platforms\.
-   Only bumper plates are allowed to be used on the Olympic platforms\.
-   Put away all your weights and plates after use\.
-   **Wipe down all equipment and benches before and after use to be respectful of the next person to use that piece of equipment\.**
-   Keep **your** workout area clear\.
-   Please respect time limits listed for specific areas of the fitness centres such as the free weights, stretching, and squat racks and platform areas\.
-   Do not work out between people using benches\.
-   Please limit your cardio use to 30 minutes \(20 minutes at peak times\)\.
-   If you feel faint or dizzy, stop, sit down and ask for assistance\.

*Failure to comply with these rules may result in loss of fitness centres privileges\.*

## Group Fitness Rules &amp; Etiquette

### Online Reservations

Reservations open 24 hours before class time and must be made in advance\.

-   **Members** with Group Fitness access can **reserve a spot for free**\.
-   **Reservations with a paid drop\-in fee are available** if you do not have an applicable membership\.
-   If you cannot make a class, **please cancel your reservation online\.** 

### \*NEW\* Class Check\-In Procedures

To help classes run smoothly and safely,

  
✔️ **Arrive on time** for the best experience\.   
✔️ **Check in directly at the class location 5–10 minutes before class begins\.**  
❌ Once a class has started, **entry is not guaranteed\. After 5 minutes**, late arrivals will not be permitted\.
