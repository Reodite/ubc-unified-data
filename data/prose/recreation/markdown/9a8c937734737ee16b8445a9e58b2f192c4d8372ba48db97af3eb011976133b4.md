### Private &amp; Semi\-Private Lessons

Private instruction is one of the best ways to improve your game quickly\. All UBC Tennis coaches are certified and trained to take your game to the next level\. Private and Semi\-Private lessons are taken based on the coaches’ availability so often clients will be placed on a waiting list until a suitable coach and time is available\.
