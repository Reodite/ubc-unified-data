### Facility Features

##### UBC Tennis Centre

-   7175 sq\. m facility housing 8 indoor courts
-   Surface – premier court / plexipave standard – Australian and US Open blue – pace is medium/slow
-   1 outdoor court on the west side of the building \(only available during the Spring &amp; Summer\)
-   Fully equipped with full amenities: men’s, women’s &amp; single stall universal change rooms, a meeting room, and multipurpose rooms

##### UBC Tennis Bubble

-   4 indoor courts
-   Surface – plexipave standard – pacific blue – pace is medium/slow

### Location

The UBC Tennis Centre is located immediately west of the Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre at 6160 Thunderbird Boulevard\. The Tennis Bubble is located on the south\-east corner of Thunderbird Boulevard and East Mall\. For the UBC Tennis Centre and the Tennis Bubble, patrons must check\-in with the operations desk inside the lobby of the UBC Tennis Centre\.

### Court Bookings

As a public facility, there is no membership fee required to book courts\. All courts must be booked through our online booking system or through the Operations Desk at the UBC Tennis Centre\. Anyone can book courts by visiting the [Court Bookings page](https://recreation.ubc.ca/tennis/court-booking/) or calling the facility at 604\-822\-2505\.
