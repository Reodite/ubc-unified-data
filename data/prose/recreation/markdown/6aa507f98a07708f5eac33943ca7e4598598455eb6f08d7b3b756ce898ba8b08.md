Use this form to add yourself or your team to the [Intramural Team &amp; Player Finder Directory](https://recreation.ubc.ca/intramurals/free-agent-single-participants/free-agent-directory/)\.

Your email address and last name are never shared and all correspondence is via anonymized email\. Once you’re comfortable there’s a match, you can communicate additional details with the other party to join their team or add them to your roster\.

*Please note that all submissions are subject to approval and may take 1\-2 business days before showing in the directory\.*

> This is a developing service\. If you have feedback or suggestions on how this can be improved, please send an email to [intramurals\.info@ubc\.ca](mailto:intramurals.info@ubc.ca)\.
