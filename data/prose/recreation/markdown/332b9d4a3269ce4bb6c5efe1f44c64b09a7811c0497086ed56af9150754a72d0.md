UBC Recreation Intramurals provides team uniform orders as an inexpensive way to kit your team in t\-shirts or hockey jerseys that meet the requirements for its league sports programs\. All uniforms feature player numbers on the back and the choice of a custom or UBC Thunderbirds logo on the front\.

The ordering process is comprised of a few key steps:

-   [Uniform Design](https://recreation.ubc.ca/intramurals/#design)
-   [Online Payment &amp; Ordering](https://recreation.ubc.ca/intramurals/#order)
-   [Confirm Uniform Details](https://recreation.ubc.ca/intramurals/#confirm)
-   [Pick\-up of Uniforms](https://recreation.ubc.ca/intramurals/#pick-up)

## Design Your Team’s Uniform

Before ordering your team’s uniforms, please take some time to review the available options and complete any design work required\. This will ensure that your order is processed without delay and delivered quickly\.

### Uniform Logo

On the front of each set of team uniforms, you can choose from one of three options:

-   [Custom logo](https://recreation.ubc.ca/intramurals/#custom-logo) – single colour print up to 8″ x 10″
-   Thunderbirds logo – single colour print, 8″ x 10″ on centre of chest
-   No logo

## Confirm Order Details

Within 2 business days of placing your order you will receive an email program that will include a link to a questionnaire to confirm the details of your team’s uniforms\. The responses you provide will help put the finishing touches on your order\.

Ensure that you have the following information before completing the questionnaire:

-   League uniform colour
-   Sizing and associated player number for each uniform \(e\.g\. 3 larges with numbers 22, 23, and 16\)
-   [Artwork file in \.eps format for custom logos – must be single colour and single layer](https://recreation.ubc.ca/intramurals/#custom-logo)
-   CO\# from your UBC Recreation Login – this information is also available on the receipt from your online order

Find your CO\# within the details of your UBC Recreation profile\.

Uniforms are often delayed by incomplete information, incorrect file uploads or adjustments needed due inappropriate design or team names\.

*If you do not receive an email receipt or have other questions, please contact [intramurals\.info@ubc\.ca](mailto:intramurals.info@ubc.ca)\.*

## Uniform Pick\-up

All team uniforms are ordered and shipped together to minimize costs associated with production and shipping – these savings are passed along to you\! **While team uniforms are scheduled to arrive by October 14, please wait until you are contacted directly to confirm pick\-up before claiming your order\.**

##### Custom Logo Requirements

**  
×  
**

-   Maximum size of 8″ x 10″
-   Only single colour designs are permitted
    -   Bright colour logos show up best on dark coloured uniforms
    -   Dark colour logos show up best on light coloured uniforms
-   All logo artwork must be submitted in \(\.EPS\) file format \(Encapsulated PostScript\)
-   File must be in black and white and created in a 1:1 scale of the final artwork
