Welcome to UBC Science\! Let's dive in and start looking at your first year, whether its your first year of university or if you're transferring from another institution\.

### Things to Think About in Your First Year

-   Keep your doors open when planning courses—don't limit your program specialization options
-   Be proactive—talk to people and get info on program specializations you might be interested in
-   Attend [Beyond First Year](https://science.ubc.ca/students/events/beyond-first-year) and helpful [Science events](https://science.ubc.ca/students/sciteam)
-   Explore [what you can do with the many specializations offered in a BSc](https://students.ubc.ca/career/your-degree/science)
-   Physics/Computer Science students: apply for [Science Co\-op](http://www.sciencecoop.ubc.ca/) if interested
-   End of first year: [choose and apply](https://science.ubc.ca/students/specialization-introduction) for a program specialization
-   [Read advice](https://science.ubc.ca/students/blog/career-year-one) for planning your first year and beyond\.

### Your Orientation to UBC Science

**Jumpstart** is UBC’s immersive, multi\-day orientation is designed to introduce you to the University, your faculty, and new friends\. Can’t attend Jump Start? Don’t worry \- your UBC orientation will begin with **Imagine UBC**\. You’ll be paired with a Science Imagine Orientation Leader and connect with the Science community at UBC\.

-   [Jumpstart Multi\-day Orientation for First Year Students](https://you.ubc.ca/jump-start-vancouver/)
-   [Imagine UBC Orientation for First Year Students](https://science.ubc.ca/students/imagine-ubc-orientations)
