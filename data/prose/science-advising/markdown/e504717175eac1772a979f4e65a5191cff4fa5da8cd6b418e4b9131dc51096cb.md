## If you're graduating soon

Transition anxiety, sometimes called change anxiety, refers to the feelings of unease, nervousness, or apprehension that arise when facing major life changes, such as graduation\. This [mental health guide designed specifically for new BSc graduates](https://science.ubc.ca/media/1048) shares strategies that can help you navigate and embrace change\.

## Tools and apps

You can use these online tools to learn more about common stressors, find mental health resources, or to add to your study routine for focus\.

-   [MindHealth BC](http://www.mindhealthbc.ca/): Complete an online assessment and access personalized resources to help support your mental health journey\.
-   [Forest](https://www.forestapp.cc/): Use a timer as you study and earn digital coins to grow real trees\.
-   [Flow](https://www.flow.app/): Track your productivity and study time\.
-   [Finch](https://finchcare.com/): Track your self\-care and mental health activities\.

These apps offer helpful mindfulness and relaxation exercises to support your mental wellbeing\. Some features may require a paid plan\.

-   [Headspace](https://www.headspace.com/)
-   [Calm](https://www.calm.com/)
-   [Insight Timer](https://insighttimer.com/)
-   [Breethe](https://breethe.com/)
