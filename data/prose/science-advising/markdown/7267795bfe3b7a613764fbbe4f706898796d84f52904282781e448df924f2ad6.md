Learn tips to lessen the stress and anxiety you feel during the final exam period\.

#### **1\. It's Natural to Feel Some Anxiety Around Exams**

No matter how much you study or how prepared you are for an exam, you may still feel stress during the exam period\. Schedule some time around your studying to take a deep breath and relax\. Stress is a normal reaction \- understanding how you experience stress can help you to manage it more effectively\. [Tips and more](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/understanding-stress/)\.

#### **2\. Study Smarter, Not Harder**

-   Make sure you know what will be covered on the exam
-   Review the syllabus and use the course learning objectives as a guide to what might be tested
-   [Go to office hours and review sessions](https://science.ubc.ca/students/blog/secret-key-academic-success-office-hours)
-   [Create an effective study plan](http://science.ubc.ca/students/blog/making-final-exam-study-plan-step-step)
-   [Find a productive study space on campus](http://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tutoring-studying/study-spaces/)
-   [Form a study group with classmates](http://science.ubc.ca/students/blog/study-groups)
-   [Learn strategies to study for Science courses](http://science-student.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/03/Stress-Less-for-Exam-Success-Package.pdf)
-   Drop in to [Exam Success in 15 Min or Less](https://science.ubc.ca/students/events/exam-success) during the last few weeks of class
-   Read more study tips from the "[Didn't Do So Hot On Your Midterm?](https://science.ubc.ca/students/blog/didnt-do-so-hot-on-your-midterm)" blog post

#### **3\. Take Regular Breaks**

Make sure you take small breaks in between to stretch and physically move \- self\-care is important\! Avoid surfing the web or checking social media as a break, since your eyes need a break from close focusing too\. [Find wellness resources and tools to support your wellbeing\.](https://science.ubc.ca/students/wellbeing)

#### **4\. Give Yourself Time to Get to an Exam**

Many unexpected things can happen on your way to an exam\. Plan to arrive at your exam location at least 20\-30 minutes early\. A flat tire, a blocked route, a late bus – these can all delay you and increase your last\-minute jitters\. On Saturdays, buses may also not be as frequent\. Remember, students will not be permitted entrance to the exam room 30 minutes after the exam start time\.

#### **5\. Write the Exam at the Scheduled Time**

You may think you would do better on the exam if only you had *more time*\. That’s rarely the case – very few students do better writing the exam at a later date than they would have writing it at the scheduled time, especially if you have been struggling in the course\. Studies show deferred exams are not to the student’s advantage\.

#### **6\. Take Action Immediately for Exam Issues**

If you are **truly unable** to write the exam, contact [Science Advising](http://www.science.ubc.ca/students/advising) as soon as possible\. Get a medical note if you are sick, as documentation is expected if you need to apply for a deferred exam\. An [exam hardship](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/academic-assessment/examination-hardships-and-clashes) happens when 3 final exams are scheduled within a 24\-hour period\. If that happens, notify your instructor immediately\. [Learn more\.](http://science.ubc.ca/students/advising/exams)

### Resources for Final Exams

#### Commuter Student Hostel

Planning to stay late to study but live off\-campus? Make a reservation to stay overnight on campus\. [Learn more](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/other-housing/commuter-student-hostel/)

#### Study Spaces: More Than Just Irving and Koerner

The main libraries at UBC are great places for studying, but they can get packed during exam period\. Find your perfect study spot\. [Learn more](http://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tutoring-studying/study-spaces/)
