Imagine UBC is the university’s biggest back\-to\-school event, welcoming many new students into the Faculty\. It's full of exciting opportunities to meet others and learn about UBC Science\. New students will get to know the campus, find out more about the Faculty of Science, meet a professor, connect with a group of students from our faculty, and more\.

[Image](https://science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Senior%20OL%20banner_original.jpg)

#### Key Position Details

Senior Orientation Leaders play an important role in Imagine UBC by supporting Orientation Leaders and contribute to their training, team building, and development\.

-   Facilitate delivery of Imagine UBC for first year and transfer students
-   Support academic skills programming for first year students in the fall
-   Role model outstanding student leadership on campus
-   Maintain regular contact with Orientation Leaders and the Science Imagine UBC Orientations team

#### [Position Description](https://science.ubc.ca/students/imagine-senior-orientation-leaders/position-description)

For more details, including detailed information about the role, important dates and commitments, and answers to other questions you may have, review the position description\.

#### What you’ll get out of the role

-   Contribute to building a friendly and welcoming community in the Faculty of Science
-   Learn how to communicate with a diverse group of students and peers
-   Build leadership and teamwork skills working in large groups
-   Understand how to facilitate groups, including engaging students in team games and activities
-   Develop your time management, organization, and problem\-solving skills
-   Build connections with peers and university staff and faculty
