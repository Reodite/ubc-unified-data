**ALERT** \- Education services companies are falsely claiming that they can help students find UBC research projects or positions on campus for a fee\. [Read more](https://science.ubc.ca/students/blog/research-alert)\.

The Science Undergraduate Research Experience \(SURE\) Awards program is funded by the Faculty of Science and subsidizes faculty supervisors to hire Science students to work on full\-time research projects during the 2026 Summer Session\. It is a great opportunity to gain research experience through a project of 8 consecutive weeks or longer\.  

The SURE Awards are available to domestic and international BSc or BCS students on the Vancouver Campus\. Graduating students may be eligible as well\. Research work may take place on campus or remotely, but you must reside within BC during the tenure of the award\. For international students, it is your responsibility to ensure that you have the proper immigration documentation to be eligible to work in Canada\. 

These awards have a value of up to $3,500\. The minimum pay for your project must meet or exceed the BC minimum wage standards \($17\.85/hour as of June 1, 2025\)\. Your supervisor will receive the funds after the end of your appointment usually in October/November\.  

## **How to Apply**  

Apply through one of the Science Departments or BSc programs listed below\. Please note that in most Departments or programs, there is no formal application form for the SURE Awards\. Make sure to thoroughly read any additional requirements from the relevant Department or program\. 

If you have applied for the [NSERC USRA](https://science.ubc.ca/students/undergrad-research/nserc) or [Work Learn International Undergraduate Research Awards \(WLIURA\)](https://science.ubc.ca/students/undergrad-research/wliura) through one of the Science Departments, but were not internally recommended for an award, the Department may automatically consider you for a SURE Award, provided you have met the SURE eligibility requirements\. Please note that you may hold only one award from NSERC USRA, WLIURA, EnSURE or SURE Awards per cycle, if you receive multiple offers\. Additionally, if you have received funding from the Work Learn program, you are not eligible to receive additional funding from the aforementioned research awards programs\.

For Department\-specific questions, please refer to the information online if available, or contact the relevant USRA coordinator below\. For general or other Faculty\-level inquiries, contact the Dean’s Office via [email](mailto:ugradawards@science.ubc.ca)\.

## **Other Funding Programs and Research Resources:** 

-   [**NSERC Undergraduate Student Research Awards**](https://science.ubc.ca/students/undergrad-research/nserc) – full\-time research experience in the Summer Session, or either Term of the Winter Session for domestic students\. 

-   [**Work Learn International Undergraduate Research Awards**](https://science.ubc.ca/students/undergrad-research/wliura) – summer research experience for international students\.

-   [**Launchpad Program**](https://launchpad.phas.ubc.ca/) – an opportunity for Indigenous students to join a top\-tier research group in the Department of Physics and Astronomy over the summer\.

-   [**Canadian Institutes of Health Research \(CIHR\) and Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada \(SSHRC\) USRA**](https://students.ubc.ca/career/ubc-experiences/undergraduate-student-research-awards-nserc-cihr-sshrc/black-student-researchers-pathway-usra/) – research awards currently exclusively for Canadian Black student researchers\. 

-   [**BRC Undergraduate Diversity Research Grants Program**](https://biodiversity.ubc.ca/training-and-award-opportunities/brc-undergraduate-diversity-research-grants-program)– mentorship and research opportunities for undergrads who self\-identify as IBPOC and a wide variety of undergrad researchers\. 

-   [**Data Science for Social Good \(DSSG\) Program**](https://dsi.ubc.ca/data-science-social-good) – interdisciplinary summer research program focusing on data\-intensive projects\. 

-   [**Faculty of Medicine Summer Student Research Program \(FoM SSRP\)**](https://www.med.ubc.ca/current-learners/summer-student-research-program/) – undertaking a summer project focusing on health research, supervised by a Faculty of Medicine researcher\. 

-   [**Work Learn Program**](https://students.ubc.ca/career/ubc-experiences/work-learn-program/)– gaining part\-time research or non\-research work experience\. 

-   [**Multidisciplinary Undergraduate Research Conference \(MURC\)**](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/multidisciplinary-undergraduate-research-conference/) – presenting your research at the annual conference along with your peers, or volunteering to help make the conference a success\.
