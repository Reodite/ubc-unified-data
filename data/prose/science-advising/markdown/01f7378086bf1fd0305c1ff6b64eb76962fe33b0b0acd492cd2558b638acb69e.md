> I took a course on exoplanets and astrobiology, which was taught by Dr\. Jaymie Matthews\. I loved the course so much that when I applied for an NSERC Undergraduate Student Research Award last summer, I listed Dr\. Matthews as my top choice for a research supervisor and “exoplanets” as my main research interest\. I’d love to work with NASA or just do research for a while, but I’d also like to do academia eventually\.
