Ranked as one of the world’s leading public universities, UBC is a global centre for top\-tier research in the life, physical, earth and computational sciences\. Our discipline\-based expertise is broadly integrated with pan\-faculty computational and data sciences units, and complemented by strong interdisciplinary hubs in the life and environmental sciences\.

#### UBC Science researchers have created more than 75 spinoff companies\.

#### The Faculty is home to more than 40 Canada Research Chairs and two CERCs\.

#### UBC research is top\-ranked in Canada in a range of natural and life science areas\.
