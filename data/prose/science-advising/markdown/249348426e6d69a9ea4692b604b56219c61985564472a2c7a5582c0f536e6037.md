UBC President Stephen J\. Toope officially opened the Abdul Ladha Science Student Centre on October 4th, 2007\. Funded through a generous donation from Abdul and Hanifa Ladha and by contributions from all Science undergraduate students and the university, the centre provides a welcoming space for our 6,600 Science students to gather, exchange ideas and engage in scientific discussions\.  
  
"It fosters interactions among science students from different fields and stages of their academic career\. Whether you’re talking about the environment, physics or medicine, science is the precursor," says UBC alumnus Ladha\.
