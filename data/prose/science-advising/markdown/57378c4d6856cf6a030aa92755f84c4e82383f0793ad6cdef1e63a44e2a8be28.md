## Before dropping or withdrawing from a course

-   Make sure you’re aware of the [add/drop deadline](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates/)\.
-   If you withdraw after the deadline, review the [impacts of a “W” on your transcript](https://science.ubc.ca/students/blog/impact-of-a-w)\.
-   International students should consider the [immigration impacts](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/immigration-impacts-part-time-studies-withdrawing-courses/) of withdrawing from courses\.

## How to drop or withdraw from a course in Workday

If you’re dropping a course **before** the add/drop deadline, you can find [step\-by\-step instructions](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/dropping-a-course/) on how to do so in Workday\. If you’re withdrawing from a course **after** the add/drop deadline, follow [these instructions instead](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/withdrawing-from-a-course/)\.

## How to swap courses in Workday

Workday allows you to swap courses or sections in *most* courses\. This means you can switch a course or section you’re already registered in with a different one while keeping your current spot until a seat in the new course or section is confirmed\.

This may be a convenient alternative to dropping a course\. If you drop a course, your seat is immediately available to other students\. You can follow step\-by\-step [instructions on swapping a course](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/swapping-a-course/)\.

## Find support

If you are having technical difficulties in Workday with dropping a course, contact your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\.

If you would like to discuss your course load or the impacts of withdrawing from courses, [drop into Science Advising](https://science.ubc.ca/students/advising)\.

If you would like advice on specific courses for your specialization, contact your department advisor\. Each department’s contact information can be found on their [department website](https://science.ubc.ca/about/departments)\.
