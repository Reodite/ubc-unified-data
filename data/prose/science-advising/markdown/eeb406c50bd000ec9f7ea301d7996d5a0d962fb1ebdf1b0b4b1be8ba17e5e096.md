The Faculty of Science is always looking for students to step up to the challenge and get involved with our community\. 

Whether you're looking to gain professional skills, work with motivated peers, or just have fun, look below, and plan ahead for upcoming opportunities\.

### Science Imagine UBC Orientation Leaders

[Image](https://science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/New%20Orientation%20Leader%20banner_crop-min.JPG)

Support the transition of around 2,500 first year and transfer students at Imagine UBC and beyond\. From facilitating social connections to providing direct peer guidance, help orient incoming Science students to student life\. 

Gain skills and experience in communication, teamwork, and leadership\.

[How to Become a Science Imagine UBC Orientation Leader](https://science.ubc.ca/students/imagine-orientation-leaders)  
[Learn about Rosha's experience as an Orientation Leader](https://science.ubc.ca/students/blog/science-student-leader-rosha)  
[Learn about Mina's experience as a Transfer Orientation Leader](https://science.ubc.ca/students/blog/science-student-leader-mina)  
[Learn about Mishena's experience as an Orientation Leader](https://science.ubc.ca/students/blog/science-student-leader-Mishena)

Questions? Contact [Science Student Engagement](mailto:sdassist@science.ubc.ca)\.

### Science Peer Academic Coaches

[Image](https://science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/SPAC%20banner_crop_1880x783-min-nologo.jpg)

Support fellow Science students to adapt their academic skills like studying and time management to facilitate their ability to meet their goals at UBC Science\. 

Through this team, you'll gain skills in one\-on\-one coaching that you'll carry forward in your personal life and future career\!

[How to Become a Science Peer Academic Coach](https://science.ubc.ca/students/spac/join)   
[Learn more about the Science Peer Academic Coaches](https://science.ubc.ca/students/spac)

Questions? Contact [Marcus Jung](mailto:marcus.jung@science.ubc.ca), the Science Peer Academic Coaches advisor\.

### Science Imagine UBC Senior Orientation Leaders

[Image](https://science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Orientation%20Leader%20banner_1491x653-min.jpg)

Help build campus community and welcome new students by coordinating a team of Orientation Leaders\. This senior student leader role takes charge to facilitate Imagine UBC and other community\-building events for incoming Science students\. 

Gain skills in team development, logistical coordination and more\!

[How to Become a Science Imagine UBC Senior Orientation Leader](https://science.ubc.ca/students/imagine-senior-orientation-leaders)

Questions? Contact [Marcus Jung](mailto:marcus.jung@science.ubc.ca), the Senior Orientation Leaders advisor\.
