Wondering how to tackle the transition to university? The Academic Skills Certificate is a series of events and resources designed to help you recalibrate your study skills and techniques to thrive in university\. 

Connect with like\-minded students and peer leaders to get a better understanding of which study methods and strategies work best for you\. Different science subjects necessitate different approaches for success\!

To earn the Academic Skills Certificate, participate in the following:

-   Engage in 2 [Study Halls](https://science.ubc.ca/students/events/study-hall)
-   Meet with a member of the [Science Peer Academic Coaches](https://science.ubc.ca/students/spac) by November 20 for a coaching conversation
-   Take part in [Start Off Strong](https://science.ubc.ca/students/events/start-off-strong) at the end of September
-   Participate in [Exam Success in 15 Minutes or Less](https://science.ubc.ca/students/events/exam-success) in late November
-   Submit a brief reflection at the end of Term 1 \- we'll email instructions to you in December
