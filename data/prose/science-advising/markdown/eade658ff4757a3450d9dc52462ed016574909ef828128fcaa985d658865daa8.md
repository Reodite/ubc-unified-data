Do you have a passion for supporting academic and career\-related experiences for our science community?

Are you interested in building your own skills and experience in areas like event planning, resource development, and public speaking?

#### Key Position Details

-   Members of SCI Team are Science students with their own diverse lived experiences\. This experience, paired with training for the role, enables you to actively contribute to dialogues and events that will benefit fellow students both during and after their degree\.
-   The time commitment is 4 – 7 hours per week, which may include team meetings, events and committee work\.
-   This is a volunteer position\.
-   SCI Team is a senior student leadership team, which is an official leadership program in the Faculty of Science and is supported by a staff advisor\.
-   [Learn more](https://science.ubc.ca/students/sciteam) about what SCI Team offers to the Science community\.

#### [Position Description](https://science.ubc.ca/students/sciteam/join/position-description)

This includes detailed information about the role, important dates and commitments, and other questions you may have\. 

#### Professional and Leadership Development

As a member of this team, it is typical to gain professional development — through training and experience — in the following areas:

-   Communication
-   Adaptability
-   Event and project planning
-   Organization
-   Time management
-   Teamwork and team building
-   Resourcefulness
-   Analysis and problem solving
-   Intercultural understanding
