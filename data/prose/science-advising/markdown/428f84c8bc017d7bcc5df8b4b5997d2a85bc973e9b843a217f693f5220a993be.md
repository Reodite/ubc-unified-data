## Share your scientific superpowers

*Make a difference in just one conversation*

UBC Science is building a network of alumni volunteers who are interested in sharing their experiences, insights, and career journeys with current students\. Whether you have an hour to spare or would like to get involved more regularly, there are flexible opportunities to fit your interests and availability\.

Every UBC Science graduate has a story \- and that story could help a student discover what's possible\. Whether you've pursued research, advanced healthcare, launched a business, developed new technologies, protected the environment, or followed an unexpected career path, your perspective can inspire and guide the next generation\.

[Volunteer with us\!](mailto:alumni@science.ubc.ca)

## Ways to Get Involved

### Career Conversations &amp; Networking Events

Connect with students through career nights, networking events, and speed mentoring sessions\. Share your experiences, answer questions, and help students explore potential career paths, including as a volunteer mentor/advisor at the annual **UBC Science Alumni Networking Night** each January\.

### Alumni Profiles

Be featured in a [UBC Science alumni story](https://science.ubc.ca/alumni/profiles)\. Through a brief interview, you'll share your journey and reflections, helping students learn about the many opportunities a Science degree can create\. 

[Also featured in *UBC Science Connect*](https://science.ubc.ca/alumni/connect)

### Mentoring Opportunities

Support students through short\-term or longer\-term mentoring programs offered by various departments and campus partners\.

[UBC Computer Science Tri\-mentoring Program](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/alumni/tri-mentoring-program)

[UBC Integrated Sciences Tri\-Mentoring Program](https://intsci.ubc.ca/integrated-sciences-mentoring)

[Online Mentoring](https://alumni.ubc.ca/volunteer-and-donate/volunteer/online-career-mentor/)

### Other Opportunities

From science Olympiads and fairs to math or biotech challenges, from discovery workshops to museum tours into the plant and animal worlds, to programs that bring science to Vancouver's downtown East Side, there's science for everyone\. 

[Outreach and Community](https://science.ubc.ca/community/outreach)

### Convocation &amp; Special Initiatives

Celebrate the next generation of graduates by volunteering at Convocation or participating in other opportunities at UBC throughout the year\.

##### Be part of the UBC Science Team

Email us at [alumni@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:alumni@science.ubc.ca) to volunteer as a UBC Science Representative in the Chancellor’s Procession \(the platform party\), and congratulate our graduates as they cross the stage\.

##### Volunteer with UBC Ceremonies

Each year, over 150 UBC faculty, staff, emeriti, and students from every corner of the campus [volunteer with UBC Ceremonies](https://graduation.ubc.ca/volunteer/) to bring Congregation to life\.

## **Why Volunteer?**

-   Inspire and support UBC Science students
-   Share your expertise and real\-world experience
-   Give back to the UBC Science community
-   Connect with fellow alumni
-   Stay engaged with the Faculty and campus

You don't need to be a CEO, researcher, or industry leader to make an impact\. Students value authentic stories, diverse experiences, and honest advice\.

## **Join Our Alumni Volunteer List**

Interested in future opportunities? Contact [**alumni@science\.ubc\.ca**](mailto:alumni@science.ubc.ca) to join our volunteer list and we'll be in touch when opportunities arise that match your interests and availability\.

*Your experience and perspective is unique and valuable\. Help students discover what's possible\.*
