If a medical or personal issue is affecting your academic life, see us as soon as possible\!   
  
Don't wait for exams\. Our advisors can connect you with resources to help you cope with extenuating circumstances\. We can also help you with general inquiries like applying for a minor, taking a term off, Faculty level requirements, taking summer courses, graduation eligibility, and more\.

## **What types of questions can and can't we answer?**

Your **departmental advisor** is the best contact for discussing requirements for your specific specialization \(e\.g\., major\)\. They can also help you choose courses once you’re in the specialization\. They are who can confirm that you’re meeting *specialization\-level* requirements for your degree and eventual graduation\. 

If you need help with course registration waitlists, you need to contact the [course’s department advisor](https://science.ubc.ca/node/1025)\. For example, if you need help with a Chemistry course, reach out to Chemistry Advising\.

At **Science Advising,** we can support your planning for the [BSc Specialization Application](https://science.ubc.ca/students/specialization-introduction) and chat about your options based on your interests; however, when it comes to specifics of the specializations, departmental advisors are the experts\. You can also attend program information sessions, workshops \(such as [Beyond First Year](https://science.ubc.ca/students/events/beyond-first-year)\), and career fairs to gather more insight to inform your specialization decision\.

We can also provide guidance throughout your academic journey, support you during personal challenges, discuss academic concessions, connect you to resources and services, review *Faculty\-level* degree requirements and your degree progression, check *Faculty\-level* graduation eligibility and refer you to your departmental advisor to check your *specialization\-level* requirements for graduation, and more\. 

The **Academic Calendar** is the rule book for your time at UBC\. Academic Advisors can help clarify and confirm the rules, regulations and expectations set out in the Calendar, and help you interpret the policies as they apply to your situation\. However, advisors cannot give information beyond what is established in the Calendar\.

## **Talk to an Advisor**

**Drop\-in Advising** is the fastest way to get your questions answered, in three simple steps:

1.  Join the [Science Advising line](https://vue.comm100.com/joinqueue/80000180/location?locationId=90864154a3fb4566884b2e1833d3951d) \(you need to do this for both in\-person and virtual drop\-in\)\.
2.  Review the final confirmation screen carefully\.
3.  When it's your turn, you'll get a text with instructions\.

*For in\-person advising, you can find us on the ground floor of the Chemistry Physics Building, Room A150 at 6221 University Boulevard\.*

### Alternate Contact

We strongly recommend contacting us through drop\-in advising \- it allows us to provide better, more personalized advising and is more efficient\. If it truly is not an option, you can [submit a contact form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0dpIObVUbXoYNRb)\. Please note that our response to these messages will be significantly delayed \(peak periods: 10 \- 15 business days\) and the fastest way to connect with us is through drop\-in advising\.
