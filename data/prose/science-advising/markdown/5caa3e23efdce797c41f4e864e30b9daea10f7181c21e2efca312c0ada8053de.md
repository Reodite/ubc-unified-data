### UBC's first\-year housing guarantee 

Students from more than 140 countries live in housing on UBC’s Vancouver campus\. Overlooking the ocean and surrounded by forests, the stunning campus is a 30\-minute bus ride away from downtown Vancouver\. Many prospective students qualify for UBC’s first\-year guarantee in winter session residence\. And we're committed to providing an inclusive and welcoming environment for all\.

## A student\-centred science community

[A male student looks up from his table at a professor who is teaching offscreen\.](https://science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/53727256057_83357cb89d_k.jpg)

Your guaranteed first\-year, small\-class experience

In UBC's [First\-Year Seminar in Science](https://science.ubc.ca/students/SCIE113) you’ll explore the nature of science and its impact on society in an interactive, discussion\-based course\. In a class of 25 students led by a professor, you’ll sharpen your critical thinking and communication skills\. A required part of your BSc program, the class helps you build a strong network from day one\.

Easy and early registration into science courses

Most UBC Science students design their own course schedule in first year, depending on the program they want to enter in second year\.  With our [course bundle early registration](https://science.ubc.ca/students/registration/bundles), you have the option to be placed in first\-year science courses before registration opens\. By seeing some of the same people in multiple courses, it will be easier to make new friends and support each other during your transition to university\.

Support from dedicated science peer programs

Our [peer academic coaches](https://science.ubc.ca/students/spac) are science students with diverse experiences and special training to help you with time management, goal setting, and study strategies\. If you need a non\-academic boost, [SCI Team](https://science.ubc.ca/students/sciteam) is here to support your personal and professional development\. UBC also has over [400 students clubs](https://sus.ubc.ca/clubs/), more than 50 specific to science\.
