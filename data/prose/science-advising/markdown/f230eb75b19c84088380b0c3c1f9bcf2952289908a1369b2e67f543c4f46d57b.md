Enter for your chance to win a professional portrait session with [Simon Rochfort Photography](https://www.simonrochfortphoto.com/) in downtown Vancouver\. 

Looking to get ahead in a competitive job market? A flattering headshot will portray you as a confident professional\. Investing in a career image is great for your social media platforms, resumes and post\-UBC networking world\! 

The winner will receive a 90 minute session and three retouched images with the option to independently purchase additional images\.

**Bonus:** All entrants will be sent a 20% discount code for a Simon Rochfort Photography session\* when the contest closes\. \(\*One\-time discount applicable against your booking fee only\.\)

Answer this skill testing question for a chance to win\!

## **Step 1**

What is the home department of our [Dean Pro\-Tem Mark MacLachlan](https://science.ubc.ca/about/welcome)?

## **Step 2**

Email your answers to [alumni@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:alumni@science.ubc.ca) by December 10, 2024\.

The winner\* will be drawn by December 12, 2024 and notified via email with further instructions\.  
  
\**Sessions are non\-transferable\. Must be a UBC Science alum \(UBC Science Fall 2024\) to be eligible\.*
