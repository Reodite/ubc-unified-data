Prepare for next steps after your BSc\. Strengthen your résumé, explore job search skills, and more\.

Designed for UBC Science students\. Participate in a series of workshops designed to help you take the first steps in transitioning from the lecture hall to the workplace\.

These workshops will help you dig deeper into the experience gained through your BSc\. You’ll get the chance to identify what’s transferable to the workplace, brush up your résumé &amp; cover letter, practice interview skills, and more\.

### Flexible

Attend all the virtual workshops, or just register for a few\.

### Free

All virtual workshops are free to attend for UBC Science undergrads\.

### For everyone

Register for any of the workshops beneficial to your career and personal development\.

### Workshops and Events

Beyond the BSc includes both virtual and in\-person workshops and an in\-person event\. Sign up for one or two, or work towards a Certificate of Completion\. See more details about the certificate below\.

The workshops below are from our 2026 series\. Check back in December for information about the 2027 series\!

[Image](https://science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Writing%20Stories.JPG)

### Using Stories to Explore Career Plans

**Saturday Jan 31, 2026** \| **10 \- 11:15 AM** \(virtual workshop\)  
Reflect on your experiences and stories in an organized way to clarify your strengths, desires, interests, personal qualities, and influences\. Explore your potential as you consider possible career options\.  
\(Please note this workshop was originally scheduled for Jan 17\)

[Image](https://science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Alum%20%281%29.jpg)

### Building Industry Connections to Understand your Career Potential

**Saturday Jan 24, 2026** \| **10 \- 11:15 AM** \(virtual workshop\)  
What do you want to do and what exists out there in the world of work? Your specialization \(e\.g\., major\) can play a role in your future career, and it doesn’t always lead to a specific group of career titles\. Learn a different way of identifying potential career pathways and what steps you can take to get a range of information from people, websites, and employment data\.

[Image](https://science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Alumni%20Networking.jpg)

### Alumni Networking Night

**Wednesday Jan 28, 2026** \| **5:30 \- 7:30 PM** \(in\-person event\)  
Network with BSc alumni from a variety of career fields to find inspiration for careers you might not have considered before\. [Learn more](https://science.ubc.ca/students/events/alumni-networking-night) about this event\.

[Image](https://science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Research.jpg)

### Job Searching within Industry and Research Realms and How to Connect with Academics

**Saturday Feb 7, 2026** \| **10 \- 11:15 AM** \(virtual workshop\)  
Dive into the world of job searching and research opportunities\. Explore funding options like NSERC, WLIURA, and the Black Student Research Stream\. We'll also guide you on how to effectively find opportunities that may not be posted online by networking with professors and teaching assistants\.

[Image](https://science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/AI%20Gen.jpg)

### Using GenAI to Get Started with your Job Search

**Saturday Feb 28, 2026** \| **10 \- 11:15 AM** \(virtual workshop\)  
Learn how to use powerful tools like generative AI to create structures and support for parts of your job search\. Discover appropriate ways to leverage GenAI with tasks like resume writing, job search, networking, and brainstorming without losing the necessary judgment and insight to effectively highlight your individuality and abilities\.

### Beyond the BSc Certificate

The Beyond the BSc Certificate of Completion is awarded to those who:

-   Participate in \(at least\) three workshops
-   Connect with alumni at the Alumni Networking Night event
-   Complete the reflection assignments

You can use this certificate as an example to demonstrate your motivation and follow through to employers\.

Keep an eye on your *Distillation* email for details about registration for the Beyond the BSc certificate\.
