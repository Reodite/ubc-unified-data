## Undergraduate students

### UBC Student Health and Wellbeing

You can access physical and mental health care at UBC Student Health and Wellbeing\.

[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health](https://students.ubc.ca/health)

#### Mental health care at UBC

The first point of contact at UBC for all mental health concerns, whether you’d like to see a doctor, a counsellor, or get more information\.

[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/mental\-health\-care\-ubc](https://students.ubc.ca/health/mental-health-care-ubc)

#### Medical care on campus

UBC students can receive medical care from a range of healthcare providers, in\-person or online as\-needed\.

[https://students\.ubc\.ca/health/medical\-care\-on\-campus](https://students.ubc.ca/health/medical-care-on-campus)

### Here 2 Talk

Here2Talk offers 24/7 confidential, single\-session counselling services by app, phone or online chat\.

[https://here2talk\.ca/main](https://here2talk.ca/main) 

### UBC Student Services

Additional health and wellbeing resources are available through Student Services, including group counselling\.

[https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services)

### Science Advising

Community, health and wellness resources via UBC Science Advising, along with resources to help you cope with extenuating circumstances impacting your academic performance\.

[https://science\.ubc\.ca/students/resources](https://science.ubc.ca/students/resources) 

## Faculty and staff

Services, programs, and resources available to support your mental health and your workplace wellbeing\.
