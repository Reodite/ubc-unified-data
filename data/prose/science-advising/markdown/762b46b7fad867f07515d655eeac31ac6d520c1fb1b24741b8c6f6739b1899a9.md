Three UBC Science staff members have been recognized with President’s Staff Awards for their outstanding achievement and contributions to the university\.

**Derek Tan**, Digital Media Specialist at the Beaty Biodiversity Museum is the recipient of a President’s Staff Award for Creativity &amp; Innovation\.

**Patrick Tamkee**, Aquatics Technician, Zoology, is the recipient of a President’s Staff Award for Enhancing the UBC Experience\.

**Kali Wilson**, formerly Senior Student Development Coordinator, received a President’s Staff Award for Emerging Leadership\.

There are six categories of President’s Staff Awards\. In 2016, a total of 12 individuals were honored with Staff Awards\.
