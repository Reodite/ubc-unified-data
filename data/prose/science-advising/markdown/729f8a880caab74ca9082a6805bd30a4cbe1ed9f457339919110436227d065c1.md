## Nobel Laureates

Eight Nobel Laureates are associated with UBC\. Four have connections to UBC Science—most famously Dr\. Michael Smith who won the prize in 1993 while at the University\.

-   **Michael Smith \(1993, Chemistry\)**   
    Won for groundbreaking work in reprogramming DNA segments\. Founder of UBC’s Michael Smith Laboratories\.  
     
-   **Carl Wieman \(2001, Physics\)**   
    Joined the University in 2007 to lead UBC Science’s efforts to transform undergraduate science education\.
-   **Bertram N Brockhouse \(1994, Physics\)**   
    Graduated from UBC with BA in physics and math in 1947, before impressive career at McMaster University\.
-   **Hans G Dehmelt \(1989, Physics\)**  
    Conducted work at the Volkoff laboratory at UBC Physics and Astronomy, prior to a move to University of Washington\.
