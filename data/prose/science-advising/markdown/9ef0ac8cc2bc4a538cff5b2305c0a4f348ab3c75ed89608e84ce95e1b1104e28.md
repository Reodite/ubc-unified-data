UBC Science is dedicated to ensuring equity, diversity, inclusion, and Indigeneity are embedded throughout the Faculty's academic and work life\. We are a leader in taking a data\-centric approach to advancing inclusivity\.

## Equity and Inclusion at our Departments and Institutes
