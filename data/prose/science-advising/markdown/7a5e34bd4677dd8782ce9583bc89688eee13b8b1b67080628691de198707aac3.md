## Registration Support

If you encounter technical issues in Workday, contact your [Enrolment Services Advisor](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/)\.

Contact your [departmental advisor](https://science.ubc.ca/about/departments) to discuss *specialization* \(e\.g\., major\) requirements\. They can help you choose courses once you’re in the specialization and manage waitlists\. You can drop into [Science Advising](https://science.ubc.ca/students/advising?t=1737411528) for help with planning for the [BSc Specialization Application](https://science.ubc.ca/students/specialization-introduction), such as discussing options based on your interests, but we cannot help you choose your specialization\. We can help you review *faculty* requirements and your degree progression\.
