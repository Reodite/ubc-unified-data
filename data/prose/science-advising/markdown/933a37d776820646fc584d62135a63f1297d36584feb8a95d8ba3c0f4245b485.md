## Report an issue

Have you experienced an equity\-related issue and aren't sure where to start? The AMS Ombuds can help you out\.

[Contact the Office of the Ombudsperson for Students](https://ombudsoffice.ubc.ca/contact-us/)

### Mental Health Support

-   [**UBC Counselling Services**](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services/)**:** Faculty of Science students can contact Counselling Services at 604\-822\-3811\. Based on your needs, you’ll promptly be offered an appointment with the Science Embedded Counsellor, a drop\-in session, or wellness advising\.
    
-   [**AMS/GSS Health and Dental Plan**](https://studentcare.ca/rte/en/UniversityofBritishColumbiaAMSGSS_Health_HealthCoverage_HealthPractitioners_Psychologists)**:** Students with the [AMS/GSS Health and Dental Plan](https://studentcare.ca/rte/en/UniversityofBritishColumbiaAMSGSS_Health_HealthCoverage_HealthPractitioners_Psychologists) may receive up to 100% coverage for psychotherapy services from licensed professionals\. For more details, visit [Finding a Private Counsellor](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services/finding-private-counsellor/)\.
    

#### 24/7 Support Options

-   [**Here2Talk**](https://here2talk.ca/): Free, 24/7 counselling via phone, chat, or app, available to all post\-secondary students in BC, including UBC students\.
    
-   [**Crisis Centre BC**](https://www.crisiscentre.bc.ca/): Provides immediate support via phone or chat for students at risk of harm\.
