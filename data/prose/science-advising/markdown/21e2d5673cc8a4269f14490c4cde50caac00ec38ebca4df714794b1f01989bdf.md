Other than Co\-op, there are numerous on\-campus job positions for UBC students to build work experience\.

### [UBC Work Learn Program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/ubc-experiences/work-learn-program/)

**Apply for winter positions in August\. Apply for summer positions in March\.**

-   Work 10 hours/week during school year; 20 hours/week in the summer
-   Student jobs on campus, including research positions

### [UBC Residence Life](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/jobs/)

**Apply in November**

-   Residence advisor and coordinator positions available
-   Assist students to succeed socially and academically through fun, engaging events
-   Build leadership skills and other personal and professional skills

### [UBC Alma Mater Society \(AMS\) Jobs](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/get-involved/jobs/)

**Positions are available year\-round with more opportunities at the start and end of the school year**

-   Alma Mater Society \(AMS\) is the official student society representing all UBC undergrads
-   Opportunities in commercial, retail, student services, restaurants and eateries

### [UBC CareersOnline](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/)

-   Regularly updated database of volunteer/job opportunities
-   Notetaker jobs \- get paid to take notes in class\!
-   Global opportunities available

### [UBC Recreation](https://recreation.ubc.ca/home/employment/)

**Positions available year\-round**

-   Opportunities to work in fitness, aquatics, sports, and event support\.
-   Student\-friendly and flexible hours

### [UBC Food Services](http://www.food.ubc.ca/jobs/)

-   Work at a UBC Food Service restaurant or café
-   Maximum 14 hours/week
-   Shifts are generally 2\-3 hours

### [UBC Library](https://about.library.ubc.ca/work-with-us/)

-   Assist with reference questions, compiling statistical data, and helping with collection projects
-   Work in an environment that encourages growth, community, and creativity 

### [Geering Up \| UBC Engineering and Science for Kids](http://www.geeringup.apsc.ubc.ca/staff/)

**Apply in late\-August and January**

-   Engage BC youth in science, engineering, and technology
-   Hiring instructors, administrators, coordinators, and more

### Additional resources

You can find additional [on\-campus jobs](https://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-jobs/) and check out [more career resources](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/) such as preparing for job applications and meeting professionals in your field of interest\.
