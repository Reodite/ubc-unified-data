Researchers in the departments of Physics and Astronomy and Computer Science stood out in this year's Killam research award series—one of UBC's most prestigious internal competitions\.  
  
Fellowships are designed to assist promising faculty members who wish to devote full time to research, and are worth up to $15,000\. Research prizes in the amount of $5,000 each are awarded annually to full\-time faculty members in recognition of outstanding research and scholarly contributions\.  
  
Select honourees will be profiled at UBC's Celebrate Research Gala on March 12, 2009\.  
 

### Killam Faculty Research Junior Fellows

Colin Brauner, Zoology Andrea Damascelli, Physics and Astronomy Steven Plotkin, Physics and Astronomy Alla Sheffer, Computer Science  
 

### Killam Faculty Research Senior Fellows

Dominique Weis, Earth and Ocean Sciences  
 

### Killam Research Prize, Junior Science Category

Ruth Signorell, Chemistry  
 

### Killam Research Prizes, Senior Science and Arts Categories

David Lowe, Computer Science Ron Rensink, Psychology, Computer Science  
  
The awards are given annually from the Killam Endowment Fund to faculty nominated by students, colleagues and alumni\. All fields of research are included\.
