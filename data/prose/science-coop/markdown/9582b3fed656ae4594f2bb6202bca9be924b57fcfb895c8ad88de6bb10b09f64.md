The Co\-op Office provides dedicated support to both employers and students throughout the placement offering guidance, coaching and intervention whenever needed\. Formal structures like learning objectives, mid\-term check\-ins and end\-of\-term evaluations, combined with a dedicated Co\-op Coordinator for each student, help ensure a smooth and successful placement from start to finish\.
