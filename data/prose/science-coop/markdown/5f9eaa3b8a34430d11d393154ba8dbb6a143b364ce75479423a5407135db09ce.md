## Gain industry work experience

Learn new skills and cultivate professional work experience, while building an impressive resume\.

## Build your career network

Gather valuable employment contacts advantageous to future permanent employment opportunities\.

## Earn money while you build skills

Help pay for your education, or that backpacking trip you've always wanted to take\.

## Apply your classroom studies to real\-life projects

Integrate learning in the classroom with related work experience and put yourself one step ahead of your peers\.

## Explore your career options

Become more aware of specializations in your area, and explore different career options through your co\-op placements\.

## Build life\-long friendships

Make lasting connections with like\-minded, talented people through Co\-op work placements\.
