##### Step 1: Connect With Us

  
Reach out to our team to discuss your talent needs or share a job description if you're ready to post\. Email us at [info@sciencecoop\.ubc\.ca](mailto:info@sciencecoop.ubc.ca) to get started\.

####   
Step 2: Post the Job 

  
We'll post your position on our co\-op job board, visible only to registered UBC Science Co\-op students\. Roles must be full\-time \(minimum 35 hours/week\) and paid for a minimum 4\-month work term\.

####   
Step 3: Receive Your Applicant Package 

  
We compile student applications including a student summary profile, resumes, cover letters, and transcripts into one convenient PDF bundle\. Log into [SCOPE](https://scope.sciencecoop.ubc.ca/home.htm), our online recruitment platform, to review\. You may also direct students to your own online portal, if preferred\.

####   
Step 4: Interview Top Candidates

  
We can coordinate interviews on campus, virtually or at your location, making the scheduling process for you\.   If preferred, you may also schedule interviews directly with students\.

####   
Step 5: Make an Offer 

  
Once you've found your ideal candidate, simply let us know and we'll present the offer on your behalf\. Students have 2 business days to make a decision\.

####   
Step 6: Finalize the Details 

  
We'll guide you through any remaining paperwork including offer letters, verification documents and any screening requirements, to ensure a smooth start to the work term\.
