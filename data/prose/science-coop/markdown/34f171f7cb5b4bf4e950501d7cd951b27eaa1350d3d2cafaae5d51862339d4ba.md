### Stories from the Science Co\-op Community

Listen to stories from the Science Co\-op community – featuring employers, faculty and students – via our Co\-op Catalysts podcast or read Co\-op Chronicles, our interview series that focuses on student experiences and highlights why they applied, how they overcame challenges and advice that they would pass along\. Gain insight into various career development topics and the values that the Science Co\-op program provides to both employers and students\.

-   [Read Our Student Focused Co\-op Chronicles](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/community/chronicles)
-   [Listen to our podcast on Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ca/podcast/co-op-catalysts-inspiring-career-stories-from-ubc-science/id1747589008)
-   [Listen to our podcast on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/3LDz4YF742dPIFJ1orjmtD)
