Students who complete **one work term during the Winter Session** of their third year may notice that their class standing remains at Year 3 in Workday and that they are not automatically promoted to Year 4\. This situation affects students in Schedule A within the following Co\-op programs: Biology, Chemistry, Earth &amp; Ocean Sciences, Geographical Sciences, Geological Sciences, Geophysics, Microbiology, and Oceanography\. These students may encounter difficulties when registering for fourth\-year level courses in the subsequent Winter Session\. To address this issue, affected students may be eligible for a manual promotion to Year 4 class standing, provided they meet **ALL** of the following requirements:

1.  Successful completion of 72 or more credits
2.  Successful completion of at least **12** upper\-level \(300 or 400 level\) credits
3.  Successful completion of at least 50 science credits, of which **9** must be upper level \(300 or 400 level\) science credits
4.  Completed the [Laboratory Science Requirement](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-science/bachelor-science/lower-level-requirements)
5.  Completed the [Communication Requirement](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-science/bachelor-science/communication-requirements)

The above Rules \# 2 and 3 are modified from the [regular BSc Y4 promotion requirements](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-science/bachelor-science/promotion-requirements-and-degree-progression) to account for their Co\-op participation\.  The review will be conducted in February and the Year 4 status will be used to determine registration priority and eligibility in time for Summer session\. Students who do not meet the above will *not* be promoted\.

If you have any questions, feel free to contact your Co\-op Coordinator or the [Science Advising team](https://science.ubc.ca/students/advising?t=1769018573)\.

Co\-operative education course credits cannot be used in lieu of or to complement academic course credits required by the specialization\.
