Instagram Contest Runs from Wednesday, July 29th to Monday, August 24th 2026 \(9:00 AM PT\)

We’re thrilled by the impactful work that you’re doing in STEM and so curious about your Co\-op experiences\. Bring us along on one of your work days, and you might be the winner of the **three $100 Uber Eats gift cards**\!

To enter, email us at [instagram@sciencecoop\.ubc\.ca](mailto:instagram@sciencecoop.ubc.ca) with a series of **carousel\-style photos/videos** or a **reel** that walk us through a “Day in the Life” of your current Co\-op work term \(remember to include your full name and student number\!\)\. Show us how you’re combining your academic learning with on\-the\-job experience, how your team collaborates, and everything in between – like the favourite iced matcha spot near your office, self care practices you developed to maintain work\-life balance, or the emotional support pet at the office \(especially the emotional support pet at the office\!\)\.

Consider capturing:

-   What’s your favourite part of the job?
-   What tasks or type of work do you usually perform \(with permission from your employer if there is sensitive information\)?
-   How do you spend your lunch breaks?
-   How do you connect with others in your workplace?
-   What makes your co\-op experience memorable? 

By entering your photos in the UBC Science Co\-op Day in the Life photo contest, you are providing consent for UBC Science Co\-op to use your photos for marketing purposes\. When submitting photos, please ensure to respect the privacy of your employer and do not submit images/audio/video containing confidential or sensitive content unless you have received consent to do so\. If you are submitting media content of/with individuals other than yourself, please ensure that you have received their permission to do so\.
