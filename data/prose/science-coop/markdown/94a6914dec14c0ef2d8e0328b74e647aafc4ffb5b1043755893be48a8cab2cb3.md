UBC offers a variety of different programs to help student improve skills that many employers are looking for today\. Please take advantage of the opportunities below: 

## Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication

The Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication \(CWSC\) offers year\-round writing consultations, workshops and resources to students\.  Writing consultations provides in\-depth feedback on specific written document and at any stage of the writing process\.

Reach out to their office for more support\.

[https://writing\.library\.ubc\.ca/undergraduates/](https://writing.library.ubc.ca/undergraduates/)

## Presentation Skills

Visit the Chapman Learning Commons for presentation skills support and additional resources:  [https://learningcommons\.ubc\.ca/presentation\-skills/](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/presentation-skills/)

**Toastmasters club** in Vancouver also offers public speaking practice opportunities\.

 [Vancouver Toastmasters](https://vancouversbestplaces.com/lower-mainland-clubs/toastmasters/)

## Chapman Learning Commons

The Chapman Learning Commons also offers peer tutoring, advice, space and equipment rental services\.  Follow the link to view available resources available to students free of charge\!

[https://learningcommons\.ubc\.ca/tutoring\-advice/](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tutoring-advice/)

-   [Here2Talk](https://here2talk.ca/home) \- free, confidential counselling and community referral services, conveniently available 24/7
-   [Empower Me](http://www.studentcare.ca/rte/en/UniversityofBritishColumbiaAMSGSS_Home) at 1\-844\-741\-6389 \(toll free, 24 hours, multilingual, free counselling sessions\)
-   [UBC Student Services](https://students.ubc.ca/covid19/mental-health-during-covid-19-outbreak) \- see Health &amp; Wellbeing
