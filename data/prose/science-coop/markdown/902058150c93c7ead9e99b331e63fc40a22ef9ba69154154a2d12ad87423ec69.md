**Co\-op students must be paid at least the minimum wage of the applicable work location\.**  
As an employer, you set the salary\. The salary varies based on your organization, the student's field of study and experience level\.   
Here's are what you need to know:  
•    All placements must be **paid and meet minimum wage requirements based on the region**  
•    Co\-op students **are your employees** and **may be compensated \(hourly or salary\)**  
•    BC minimum wage is **$18\.25/hour as of June 1, 2026**  
•    All postings must include a pay range per the [BC Pay Transparency Act](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/about-the-bc-government/gender-equity/pay-transparency-in-bc)
