# Explore Your Career Options

Co\-operative Education \(Co\-op\) integrates students' academic education \(classroom\-based learning\) with relevant, supervised and paid work experience \(work\-based learning\)\. UBC Science Co\-op placements are typically held in three annual work terms: Fall \(Sept – Dec\), Winter \(Jan – Apr\) and Summer \(May – Aug\)\. UBC Science Co\-op works closely with local, national and international employers to develop challenging, career\-related positions for our students\.

## Combined majors students

Apply to the Co\-op program of your current major\. Combined majors students only need to submit ONE application to the program of their choice\.

## Don’t know your major yet?

No worries\! There's always time\. If you're still in the process of deciding, explore our Co\-op programs, and if needed, talk to people from various programs to get a better feel for your options\. Make sure you still apply to your tentative area of interest so that one of our coordinators can take a look at your application and advise you\.

## Accessibility

Students enrolled with the Centre for Accessibility are encouraged to contact the Co\-op office should they have questions about the admission requirements\. If you are not able to meet the admission requirements for reasons related to the impact of a disability, please contact us at [science\.coop@ubc\.ca](mailto:science.coop@ubc.ca) to learn about special consideration that may be available to you\. Details of the nature of the disability will not be required\.
