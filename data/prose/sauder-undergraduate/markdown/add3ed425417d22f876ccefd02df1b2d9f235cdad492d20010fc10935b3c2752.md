## Glossary

-   APR: Academic Progress Report
-   OAP: Overall Academic Progress
-   In excess: Extra Courses
-   Override: Manual update completed by OSAS
-   Didn’t Match: Course falls outside of academic requirements for your program
-   Didn’t Satisfy: Failed or repeated courses, or course falls outside of academic requirements for your program
-   In Progress: Course in progress \(grade not yet available\)
-   Credits Defined: Total number of credits needed for your degree
-   Requirements effective date: The date you were admitted to the program or declared your option
-   Class Standing: Year level

## What is Academic Progress Report \(APR\)?

Your personalized APR in Workday Student is a tool to keep track of your degree requirements and credits\. UBC’s degree audit tool displays your academic requirements as listed on the corresponding UBC Academic Calendar entries for your program and your progression in the program\.

### Attention: APR Limitations

The APR has limitations and should be used in combination with our [Course Planning Worksheets](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/bcom-program-planning-tool) and [Elective Checklist](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/elective-requirements) for degree planning\. Refer to the important considerations listed below for additional information related to the APR, and for issues you may find with your APR\.

Be sure to check the [Elective Checklist](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/elective-requirements) for electives not for credit in the BCom program\.

## Reading Your Academic Progress Report

### Academic Progress Wheel

The Academic Progress Wheel shows you an estimate of the percentage of academic requirements you have completed for your program\. An academic requirement may be a single course or a group of courses\. There is one wheel for each program of study\. A program of study can be an option, co\-op, or, in some cases, a minor\. You will only see one wheel for your primary option and a wheel for co\-op if you are completing co\-op\.

The Academic Progress Wheel only provides a partial estimate of your progress toward degree completion\. **Because of this,** OSAS will not use this wheel to assess your graduation requirements, as it is not a reliable indicator of your full progression toward degree completion\.

**Academic Progress Wheel and CO\-OP**

The wheel for co\-op will only show "Satisfied" after all co\-op requirements have been completed\. Until then, the wheel will show "0 Satisfied\." BUCS CPSC co\-op courses are currently not applying correctly on the APR\. These courses will be found in Unused Registrations\.

### Important Considerations 

**Transfer Credit**

  
If you received transfer credits, the credits awarded can be viewed in Workday Student under Academics &gt; Transfer Credits\.  
Transfer credits that have been given a specific UBC course subject and code \(e\.g\., MATH\_V 100\) which matches an APR requirement will automatically apply in the APR\.

COMM\_V unassigned transfer credits \(e\.g\., COMM 2nd\) must be reviewed by the UGO to determine if they can be used in the program\. An override may be needed to exclude or apply the credit correctly in the APR\.

Transfer credit assigned NC \(e\.g\., MATH 1NC, STAT 2NC, COMM 1NC\) is typically not for credit in the BCom program\. An exception is year 2 and 3 transfer students with COMM 1NC credit for IB Business Management HL or A\-Level Business Studies may use the credit toward electives\. UBCO transfer students may use only one of MGMT 100 and COMM 1NC\.

**Transfer Students**

  
Transfer students often require multiple overrides in the APR\. Overrides should be completed by the Term 1 deadline to add or drop a course without a W\. More information on transfer credits and how to submit a transfer credit assessment can be found [here\.](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment-program-requirements/general-program-requirements/transfer-students)  

**Viewing an Override**

  
Students will know an override has occurred if "Override Assigned" appears next to the requirement in your APR\. By clicking on "Override Assigned," you can view the comments associated with the override\. If an override has occurred in your OAP, you will see an Override Assigned column and can view the override by clicking on the magnifying glass symbol\.  

**COMM\_V 101 Override for Transfer Students**

  
COMM\_V 101 is not required for transfer students\. In lieu, transfer students will complete 3 additional credits of Commerce or Non\-Commerce courses at any level\. In your APR, you will see the additional elective has been applied to COMM\_V 101 as an override\.  

**APR Refresh after Registration Change**

  
It may take up to 24 hours after adding or dropping a course for your APR to refresh and for the change to display in your APR\.  

  

**Issues with “One of” Requirement**

  
If your option includes a requirement of completing one or two courses from a list of possible courses, they may be incorrectly applied to the option requirement and to the elective requirement\. In other words, the same course\(s\) will appear in two areas on your APR\. You cannot double count the same 3\-credit course toward your option and your elective requirements\.  

**Electives**

  
Your electives will not show Satisfied until the entire requirement is complete\. A status of In Progress indicates the requirement is in progress but isn’t necessarily fulfilled\. If the Status is In Progress and the Remaining column indicates Minimum Combination Required, the requirement is not being fulfilled\.  

**Courses Not Displayed in APR**

If a requirement has been met, excess courses fulfilling the requirement may not be displayed in the APR or in unused registration\.

**In\-Progress Excess Issue**

  
There may be some "In Progress" courses that appear in the "Unused Registrations" of your APR, even though they are intended to count towards a specific requirement\. Once the course is completed, the system should apply the course to the requirement\.  

**Residency Rule Edits**

  
Per the [UBC Calendar](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?tree=3,40,0,0) at least 50% of the credits required for the program must be completed while registered in the program\. As the BCom program consists of 121 credits \(including one 1\-credit core course: COMM\_V 202\), 61 credits must be completed while in the BCom program and up to 60 credits completed prior to admission to the BCom program may apply\. In the case of BUCS, 64 credits must be completed while in the BCom program\. Go Global exchange credits completed after admission to the BCom program are eligible toward the minimum\. To ensure the appropriate number of credits are completed in the program, the APR for transfer students following the residency rule will be edited to exclude excess transfer credit\.  

**Exchange Transfer Credit**

  
Overrides for transfer credit earned on exchange may be necessary to split credit between elective requirements or apply transfer credit to specific requirements\.  

The APR is programmed to allow a maximum of 60 transfer credits\. If you are a transfer student and the transfer credit earned prior to admission to the program combined with your exchange transfer credit exceeds 60 credits, overrides may be necessary to allow the excess exchange transfer credit to apply\.

  

**Special Topics Courses**

  
Special topics courses that fulfill specific requirements, such as option courses \(e\.g\., COMM\_V 486T can be used toward the Finance option\), will need to be manually updated on the APR\. These courses will appear in electives until the override is made\.  

**Six Credit Courses Fulfilling Two 3\-Credit requirements**

  
A limitation of the APR is that it is unable to automatically split 6\-credit courses to fulfill two separate 3\-credit requirements\. An override can be made to 6\-credit courses taken at UBC to apply 3 credits to two requirements \(e\.g\., COMM\_V 466\)\.  
For students following the old BCom program requirements \(including those who entered the program directly to Year 1 in September 2021 or earlier, transferred as a Year 2 student in September 2022 or earlier, or transferred as a Year 3 student in September 2023 or earlier\), and if you have 6 credits of ENGL\_V 1st being used to fulfill the ENGL\_V 112 requirement, it is not possible to split transfer credit to use towards two separate requirements\. As a workaround, your APR will be updated so that all 6 credits of ENGL\_V 1st will apply to ENGL\_V 112, and your electives will be reduced by 3 credits\.  

**Requirements Effective Date**

  
Depending on what year you are enrolled in the program, your academic requirements will vary\. Your program requirements are based on the program effective date on the APR\. Your requirements effective date is usually the date you were admitted to the program or declared your option\. If your effective date appears to be incorrect, please submit an APR feedback form so that it can be corrected\.

## APR &amp; Your Year Level

Please select your year level below for further information

### Year 1 Students

Overrides for students with high school transfer credit should be completed by the Term 1 deadline to add or drop a course without a W\. After that time the year 1 requirements outlined in your APR should be accurate\. The APR has limitations and should be used in combination with our [Course Planning Worksheets](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/bcom-program-planning-tool) and [Elective Checklist](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/elective-requirements) for degree planning\.

### Year 2 Students

Overrides for transfer students and continuing students should be completed by the Term 1 deadline to add or drop a course without a W\. After that time the year 1 and 2 requirements outlined in your APR should be accurate\.The APR has limitations and should be used in combination with our [Course Planning Worksheets](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/bcom-program-planning-tool) and [Elective Checklist](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/elective-requirements) for degree planning\.

### Year 3 Students

Overrides for transfer students and continuing students should be completed by the Term 1 deadline to add or drop a course without a W\. If registration changes occur further overrides may be necessary and should be completed by the Term 2 deadline to add or drop a course without a W\. The APR has limitations and should be used in combination with our [Course Planning Worksheets](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/bcom-program-planning-tool) and [Elective Checklist](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/elective-requirements) for degree planning\.

### Year 4 Students

Any remaining overrides should be completed by the Term 1 deadline to add or drop a course without a W\. After that time your APR should correctly reflect your academic progression, and we recommend that you take a moment to review it\. Be sure to check whether your elective status is showing Satisfied or In Progress\. If the Remaining column indicates Minimum Combination Required your electives are not being satisfied\. If registration changes occur further overrides may be necessary and should be completed by the Term 2 deadline to add or drop a course without a W\. Please read the information on our website prior to viewing your APR\. The APR has limitations and **must** be used in combination with our Course Planning Worksheets and Elective Checklist\.

## Academic Progress Report \(APR\) FAQs 

**Why can’t I register in a course even though the APR says I have the pre\-requisite?**

Workday looks for the exact course code on your record to match with the pre\- or co\-requisite\. So, if your transfer credits are not an exact match to a UBC course—but an override was assigned so that your transfer credit is applying towards a UBC course—then you can register for the course, but you will need to contact the department who owns the course \(e\.g\., Math Department, OSAS\) to request a registration waiver to enable you to register yourself in the course\.

**Why is my co\-op APR wheel not moving although I have completed some of the requirements?**

The wheel will only show Satisfied after all requirements have been completed\. Until then, the wheel will show 0 Satisfied\.
