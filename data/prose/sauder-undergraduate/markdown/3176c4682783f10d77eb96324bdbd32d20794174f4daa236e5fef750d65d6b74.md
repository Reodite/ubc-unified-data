Scholarships have been made available annually through a gift from the International Council of Shopping Centers Foundation Canada, Inc \(ICSC\) for outstanding domestic Bachelor of Commerce students in the Real Estate option\. Ordinarily, each scholarship will be valued at $2,500\. The ICSC Foundation Canada is the non\-profit arm of the International Council of Shopping Centers, the international trade association for the retail real estate industry\. The ICSC Foundation Canada aims to create a strong retail real estate industry by supporting the next generation of individuals to drive the field forward\.
