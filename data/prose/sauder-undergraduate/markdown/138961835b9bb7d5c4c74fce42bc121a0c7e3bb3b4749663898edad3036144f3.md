## Course Description

Introduction to decision models in business, including basic optimization, linear programming, probability, decision analysis, random variables, simulation, and solving decision problems using spreadsheet tools\.

### Responsible Business Content

This course incorporates responsible business perspectives through applications of quantitative decision\-making in areas such as sustainable resource allocation, healthcare operations, and public\-sector planning \(Environmental Sustainability, Health and Well\-Being, Responsible Business and Ethics\)\.

Strongly recommended as corequisite: One of MATH\_V 100, MATH\_V 102, MATH\_V 104, MATH\_V 110, MATH\_V 120, MATH\_V 180, MATH\_V 184\.
