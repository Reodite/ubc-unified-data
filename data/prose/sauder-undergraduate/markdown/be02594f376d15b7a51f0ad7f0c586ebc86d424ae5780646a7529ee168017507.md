### About the group

**Feeling overwhelemd? You're not the only one\.** Join other Sauder undergraduate students in a supportive five\-session group to learn practical tools for balancing university life, managing stress, building confidence, and finding a sense of belonging\. Connect with peers who are on this journey with you\. 

This is a drop\-in group so students are free to attend as many or as few sessions as they may need\. 

Group session topics include:

-   Finding your place: Navigating Expectations and Finding Belonging
-   Managing Academic Expectations &amp; Workload
-   Work\-life Balance: Juggling it all
-   Caring for your Mental Health &amp; Wellbeing
-   Dealing with Self\-Doubt &amp; Comparison

### Eligibility

To participate, you must be an undergraduate student of the Sauder School of Business and have availability to attend our group sessions\.

-   **Times:** 12:00 to 1:00 PM
