Located in CLC 222 in the David Lam Library and Canaccord Learning Commons, the Leith Wheeler Investment Research Lab is part of the UBC Sauder School of Business computer lab system and provides students and faculty access to industry databases and financial analysis systems\.

### Lab Policies

Priority use on database\-specific terminals is given to users of Bloomberg, S&amp;P Capital IQ, and Morningstar Direct\. One Bloomberg terminal and the S&amp;P Capital IQ terminal can be used by non\-UBC Sauder students\. A staff member must sign on non\-UBC Sauder students to the terminals\. If you have any questions, please reach out to Fei Song, Investment Labs Coordinator \([fei\.song@sauder\.ubc\.ca](mailto:fei.song@sauder.ubc.ca)\)\.

##### Ethical/Appropriate Use of Data

-   All data should be accessed, used, and shared in accordance with UBC policies and legal  
    regulations\.
    
-   Users must adhere to strict ethical standards when using data and software\.
    
-   Unauthorized manipulation, distribution, or misuse of data and software is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action\.
    

##### No Food/Drink

-   Absolutely no food is allowed in the lab\.
-   Only sealed water bottles for drinks are permitted\. Cups with straws or holes are not accepted\.

##### Responsible Use

-   Do not unplug any cords or alter the equipment setup in any way\.

-   Priority use is given to patrons who need access to the computer's specialized financial software\.

-   Do not remove or rearrange the furniture in the lab\.

##### Conduct

-   Conversations are allowed, keep a reasonable volume to maintain a conducive learning environment\.
    
-   Maintain a respectful demeanor towards all lab users and staff\.
    
-   Refrain from any disruptive behavior that could disturb others\.
    
-   Clean up your workspace before leaving\. Dispose/recycle of any trash properly\.
    
-   Respect others' privacy and work\. Do not look at or interfere with other users' screens or materials
    

##### Consequences

-   Failure to comply with these policies may result in being asked to leave the lab\.
-   This policy is intended to supplement other UBC policies\. Inappropriate use of the lab space or financial software may result in more serious consequences as outlined in other policies\.
    

### Financial Database Access for Non\-Sauder Users

For non\-Sauder users, terminals are available on a first come, first serve basis\. There is no pre\-booking system to reserve a specific terminal; if the terminal that you would like to log into is in use, please try again later\. 

UBC Sauder students please visit the [Wayne Deans Investment Analysis Centre page](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/specialized-learning-spaces/wayne-deans-investment-analysis-centre) for instructions on database access, as well as the information about workshops and office hours\.

**Bloomberg**

1.  Once you are signed onto the machine, launch Bloomberg on the desktop
2.  If you have a Bloomberg account, login to your account
3.  If you do not have an existing account, click on “Create New Login” under the username and password

-   Use your cell phone number as the work phone number
-   Use your *student\.ubc\.ca* email address as your work email address\. You cannot use any non\-UBC email address\. Using a generic ubc\.ca email address that does not belong to you constitutes non\-academic misconduct\.

-   Your personal Bloomberg account is associated with the Bloomberg terminal that you logged in last time, and thus will not work on other Bloomberg terminals\. 
    

**S&amp;P Capital IQ**

1.  Log in to the computers with the "Capital IQ" label using CWL\.stu
2.  Launch Capital IQ on the desktop – no login required

UBC students can set up a personal Capital IQ account during the fall term, [instructions here](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/51980/pages/capital-iq)\.

**Morningstar Direct**

Please request login assistance at the circulation desk at the David Lam Library &amp; Canaccord Learning Commons\. After you have logged in to the terminals using your CWL account, the staff will help you login to the Morningstar Direct account\.

### About Leith Wheeler

Murray Leith \(BCom ‘53\) was one of three alumni who founded UBC Sauder’s prestigious [Portfolio Management Foundation \(PMF\) program](http://www.sauder.ubc.ca/pmf)\. Leith Wheeler Investment Counsel Ltd\., the company he founded in 1982 has grown significantly and the PMF program has become a training ground for investment professionals across the globe\. Murray is remembered for the strong emphasis he placed on ethics, integrity, and mentorship\.

Honouring this longstanding association, Leith Wheeler donated $200,000 towards a much needed increase in Bloomberg terminals for the school’s finance students\. Leith Wheeler has a longstanding history of supporting initiatives that benefit UBC Sauder students, with a number of alumni holding key positions at the firm\.
