## What is Activate?

ACTIVATE is a series of student leadership workshops hosted by OSAS\.

Designed for emerging leaders, ACTIVATE equips you with foundational and transformative leadership skills, focusing on inclusivity, teamwork, and real\-world applications to help you thrive in your studies, community, and future opportunities\. Through engaging workshops, thought\-provoking panel discussions, and valuable networking opportunities, you’ll learn how to build resilience, lead teams effectively, and approach leadership with confidence and impact\.

Whether you’re driven to create positive change, excel in team environments, or prepare for internships and co\-ops, ACTIVATE will help you lead with purpose, adaptability, and the skills to grow as a leader\.

#### Visha Thamboo 

Visha Thamboo is the Associate Director of Student Engagement and Development at the Office of Student Academic Services at UBC Sauder\.

Term 1 Workshop – Public Speaking Tool Kit – Speak with Confidence \| Date and Time: Thurs, Oct 23, 5:00pm – 6:15pm \| Location: HA 291

Public speaking is one of the most valuable — and most feared — leadership skills\. Whether you’re leading a team meeting, pitching a idea, or welcoming new students, your ability to speak with clarity and confidence can set you apart as a leader\.

In this hands\-on 75\-minute workshop, you’ll learn simple, practical tools to organize your thoughts, engage an audience, and manage nerves\. Through fun and interactive exercises, you’ll practice impromptu speaking in a supportive environment, receive positive feedback, and leave with strategies you can apply immediately in your leadership journey\.

Term 2 Workshop – Bouncing Back from Rejection – Building Your Resilience Toolkit \| Date and Time: Tues, Feb 10, 5:00pm – 6:00pm

The opportunities presented to us are often unequitable, and we are bound to face a closed door, sooner or later\. What we do after the door is closed on us, can change your success trajectory\. Can you create opportunities for yourself and build identity resilience? Using growth mindset, elements of planned happenstance and some internal reflection, this workshop will equip you with the tools you’ll need to open your next door \(no matter your circumstance\)\.
