### Employment outlook and opportunities

**[Business Technology Management](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/node/1798) \(BTM\)** is an information technology\-related option for tech\-savvy students with creative problem\-solving and analytical skills wanting to combine their business and technology interests\.

Students in this option will learn about **various IT management systems** and how these technologies **contribute to a company’s competitive advantage**\. This option gives you an overview of the information systems area complemented with hands\-on lab experience\.  
  
Organizations value individuals who have

-   a solid understanding of technology and its applications[https://mybcom\.sauder\.ubc\.ca/file/289/download](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/file/289/download)
-   and the ability to communicate effectively and professionally with internal and external clients

As a BTM graduate, you could be working in a number of industries, as the majority of businesses need information technology to support their business needs\.

### Build a career in BTM

#### Some potential job titles and future career paths include:

- **Row 1**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    -   Business Analyst
    -   Computer/Information Systems Manager
    -   Database Manager
    -   Information Systems Auditor
    -   IT/IS Manager
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    -   Project Manager
    -   Senior IT Executive
    -   Systems Analyst
    -   Technology Consultant/Consulting Analyst

Additional education or certification may be required for some fields and occupations \(e\.g\., completion of association registration, a professional program, or specialized studies\)\.

Within the field, students can pursue a number of certifications, such as Certified Information Systems Auditor \(CISA\) and Certified Information Security Manager \(CISM\)\. These certifications are globally recognized\.
