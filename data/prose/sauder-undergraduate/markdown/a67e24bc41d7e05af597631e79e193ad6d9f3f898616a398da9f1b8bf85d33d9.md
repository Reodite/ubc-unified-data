### Funding your studies

Student loans, scholarships, bursaries, and employment are all ways for you to finance your studies\. Take time to understand the full range of financial support options available to you so that you can spend less time worrying about money and more time focusing on your coursework\.

### Maintain your eligibility for student loans

Government loans, grants, and interest\-free status all require students to be enrolled in at least 60% \(80% for Newfoundland and Labrador loans\) of a full\-time course load in each term\. Given the credit structure of the BCom program, the minimum number of credits you must be enrolled in varies at different year levels\. Consult the course load guide at students\.ubc\.ca to ensure that you meet the requirements to maintain your funding\. Note that eligibility to receive scholarships and to live in UBC residence is determined by separate registration criteria\.

[Consult the course load guide](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/course-load-guide)

## Scholarships and awards

In addition to entrance scholarships, there are many other scholarships and awards available to you as a student in the BCom program\. At the start of the academic year, typically over 50 academic merit awards are assigned to continuing students based on academic achievement in the previous academic year\.

### By application awards

-   In addition to merit awards, many more awards are available by application to recognize achievement in your previous year of studies\. Academic achievement may be a factor, but non\-academic factors such as leadership, community involvement, or financial need may also be included in individual award criteria\.
-   The application deadline for 2026W awards is **September 20, 2026 \(11:59 PM PT\)**\. Applications are evaluated sequentially by award\. Recipients will be notified as decisions are made, from early October until late November\.

The list of available awards by application for 2026W will be posted at the end of August\.

Eligibility requirements

There are limited awards available to new BCom students\. Awards for Indigenous students are available to both new and continuing students\. All other awards are only available to continuing students who were registered in the BCom program the previous academic year\.

#### Academic Merit awards

Award eligibility is based on the academic year \(September\-April\) and does not include summer registration\. Candidates must be registered in the BCom program in a minimum of 24 percentage\-graded credits in the previous academic year with no failures or missing grades and a minimum of 24 credits in the current academic year\. A minimum 75% in the previous academic year is required\.

##### Exceptions to minimum credit rules can be found [here](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/awards-scholarships-bursaries/special-circumstances-receiving-awards/)\.

#### Non\-Academic Merit awards

Candidates must be registered in the BCom program in the previous academic year and in the current academic year\. Applicants are expected to be in good academic standing \(obtained minimum 60% in the previous academic year\)\.

Refer to the list of available awards by application to determine if an award is an academic merit award or a non\-academic merit award\.

Application instructions

In your online application, be sure to:

-   provide all biographic details as applicable
-   upload your one\-page resume
-   provide an accompanying statement \(350\-word maximum\) for each award to which you wish to apply
-   provide details of your estimated budget for the 2026/27 Winter Session if you are applying to any awards that list financial need as one of their criteria \(refer to the downloadable budget planner for guidance\)

Once you start your online application you CANNOT save a draft to return to at a later time\. Please be prepared to complete the entirety of your application in one sitting\. We recommend that you prepare your responses in advance and then paste them into your application when you are ready to submit it\. Submit only one complete application\. If duplicates are submitted, only the most recent submission will be reviewed\. Incomplete or late applications will not be reviewed\.
