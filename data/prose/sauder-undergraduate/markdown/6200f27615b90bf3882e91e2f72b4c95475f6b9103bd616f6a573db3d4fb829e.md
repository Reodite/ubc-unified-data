An award is given at graduation for the student earning the top marks and standing at the head of the graduating class in the Bachelor of Commerce degree\. The award is given to recognize Daniel F\. Muzyka who served as Professor and Dean of the Sauder School of Business from 1999\-2012\. The award celebrates his contributions to the Sauder School, the University and the business community and the affection and esteem in which Dean Muzyka is held by his students, colleagues and friends\. The award is made on the recommendation of the Sauder School of Business\.
