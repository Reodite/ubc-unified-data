### Discover ways to develop skills &amp; make connections

[Students in meeting](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/general/level_1_-_gain_experience-2.jpg?itok=ODhVJb2Z&amp;c=7ae45214af527a8247a4afa02dd269fe)

Work experience comes in all shapes and sizes, and **every little bit counts**\. Whether you:

-   Dive into **internships;**
-   **Work part\-time** during the school year;
-   **Volunteer** for causes you care about; or
-   **Participate in clubs**, there are many ways to build your resume as you complete your BCom degree\.

The Hari B\. Varshney Business Career Centre \(BCC\) provides BComs free access to a wide range of resources to help you build your experience portfolio\.

Seizing different opportunities will not only help make your resume stand out to future employers, but it will also help you:

-   Test\-drive different careers to figure out what you like and don’t like
-   Gain transferrable workplace skills such as teamwork, communication, and problem\-solving
-   Meet peers, professionals, alumni, and mentors who will become part of your expanded network
-   Make career decisions
