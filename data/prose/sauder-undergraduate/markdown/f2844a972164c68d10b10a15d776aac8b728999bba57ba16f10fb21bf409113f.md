## What is SEHUB?

The Student Engagement Hub \(SEHUB\) is an in\-person, staffed space that offers student services, peer support, and community\-building opportunities for UBC Sauder BCom, DAP, B\+MM and graduate students\. It’s not a lounge or study area, but a space where students can access helpful resources, engage with student staff, and take part in small\-scale, peer\-led activities\. The Student Engagement Hub exists to help you feel connected, supported, and informed throughout your time at UBC Sauder\.

**Location:** HA 338

**Hours:** Monday–Thursday, 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM \(drop\-ins welcome\)

**This space brings:**

-   Craft and community activities that support belonging and engagement
    
-   Friendly student staff who can answer questions and connect you to UBC Sauder resources
    
-   Wellbeing resources including snacks and sensory items\!
    
-   A lending system for select materials and supplies to support student\-led initiatives and programming
    
-   Flexible use of the space for student programming, workshops, and meetings \(by request\)
    
-   CLC workshops are offered at scheduled times, including PASS and English Speaking Circle sessions, and other skill\-building workshops**\.**
