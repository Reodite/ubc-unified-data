### Boost your finance career with FMIPP\!

Are you serious about landing an internship in finance? 

Whether you're aiming for investment banking, private equity, or other buy\-side and sell\-side finance fields, the UBC Sauder **Financial Modelling &amp; Interview Prep Program \(FMIPP\)** is here to equip you with the essential skills and knowledge to ace the hiring process\.

### Program details

The FMIPP, offered by the Hari B\. Varshney Business Career Centre \(BCC\) in collaboration with the Commerce Undergraduate Society \(CUS\) and UBC Sauder Finance Faculty, is a self\-paced online training program designed to equip BCom students with comprehensive skills in financial modelling and interview preparation\.

By registering, you’ll gain lifetime access to 14 courses from [Wall Street Oasis](https://www.wallstreetoasis.com/), which, if purchased outside of FMIPP, would cost you over $2,000 CAD\. Courses include: 

-   **Accounting Foundations**: 60\+ Video Lessons
-   **Excel Modelling**: 120\+ Video Lessons
-   **PowerPoint for Finance**: 60\+ Video Lessons
-   **Financial Statement Modelling**: 60\+ Video Lessons
-   **Valuation Modelling**: 170\+ Video Lessons
-   **DCF Modelling**: 75\+ Video Lessons
-   **LBO Modelling**: 120\+ Video Lessons
-   **M&amp;A Modelling**: 120\+ Video Lessons
-   **Real Estate Modelling**: 110\+ Video Lessons
-   **Venture Capita**l: 160\+ Video Lessons
-   **Investment Banking Interview Package**: 30\+ Hours
-   **Private Equity Interview Package**: 9\+ Hours
-   **Hedge Fund Interview Package**: 5\+ Hours
-   **Consulting Interview Package**: 5\+ Hours

Plus, you can get course certifications from Wall Street Oasis upon completing the courses\. You’ll also receive a 70% discount on other Wall Street Oasis courses\.

### Why participate in FMIPP?

**Preparation for Competitive Internships**: Stand out in the competitive finance job market and boost your chances of landing an internship by developing polished interview skills and technical expertise that employers seek\.

**Diverse Course Offerings**: Gain a deep understanding of key concepts and practical skills across a wide range of finance topics, ensuring you’re ready for industry demands\.

**Practical Application**: Apply theoretical knowledge to real\-world scenarios through practical exercises and case studies, preparing you to tackle the challenges finance professionals face\.

**Self\-Paced Learning**: Study at your own pace and convenience with our flexible online format\.
