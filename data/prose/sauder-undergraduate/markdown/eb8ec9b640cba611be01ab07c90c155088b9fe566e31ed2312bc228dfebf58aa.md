### Gain clarity on your career interests and aspirations

[UBC Sauder student at lap top on campus](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/level_1-_career_personal_exploration.jpg?itok=FCu5W7Xw)

Curious about **which professions might be the right fit for you**? Want to dig deep and figure out what truly matters to you as you navigate career decisions?

**Exploring** your personal qualities and professional aspirations can help you **understand who you are** and **what you want** and set meaningful goals for your future\.

This essential groundwork can **boost your confidence** in your decisions and **empower you** as you progress through your career journey\.

The Hari B\. Varshney Business Career Centre \(BCC\) provides BComs free access to a wide range of career and personal development resources that can help you:

-   Get to **know yourself**
-   **Explore** a variety of exciting **career paths**
-   Draw inspiration from **UBC Sauder hiring data** via UBC Sauder Career Hub's **[Job IQ Explorer](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/job-analytics/post-graduation)** **[https://sauder\-ubc\.12twenty\.com/](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/)**
-   And much more\!
