The YMCA of Greater Vancouver awards a scholarship to a female student planning to pursue a career in Marketing and Advertising\. The award is in memory of Ellen Bell, a talented and creative individual who worked in the field of marketing and advertising and was a committed community volunteer\. 

For additional information including eligibility, award criteria, application details, and deadline, please visit [www\.gv\.ymca\.ca/Ellen\-Bell\-Memorial\-Scholarship](https://www.gv.ymca.ca/Ellen-Bell-Memorial-Scholarship)[https://gv\.ymca\.ca/Opportunities\-to\-Give/Endowment\-Fund\-of\-the\-YMCA\-of\-Greater\-Vancouver/ellen\-bell\-memorial\-scholarship](https://gv.ymca.ca/Opportunities-to-Give/Endowment-Fund-of-the-YMCA-of-Greater-Vancouver/ellen-bell-memorial-scholarship)
