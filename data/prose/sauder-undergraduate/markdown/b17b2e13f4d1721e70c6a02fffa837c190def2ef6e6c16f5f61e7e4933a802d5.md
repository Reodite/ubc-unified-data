### Learn from the pros

[Students sitting outside with computer](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/level_1_-_mentorship_specialty_programs.jpg?itok=7Z8RWxMR&amp;c=02bad87a92440e326398dc296887127c)

There’s nothing like learning directly from people with business experience\. **Build your experience** and **develop new skills** with the help of industry professionals through our mentorship and specialty training programs\.

Taking part in one \(or more\!\) of these programs is a great way to understand **what it’s actually like to work in specific industries** and **gain insider knowledge** on hiring, and different career paths\.

The style and format of our programs vary\. Some are geared towards getting you recruitment\-ready, while others are skills\-focused\. But, all will help you **advance your career**\. As a UBC Sauder BCom student, you have plenty to choose from\.

### [Brand Management Mentorship Program](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/brand-management-mentorship-program)

Learn how to land coveted brand management positions with leading companies, facilitated by Professor Tim Silk and led by alumni marketing professionals\.

[Learn More](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/brand-management-mentorship-program)

### [Executive Mentorship Program](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/executive-mentorship-program)

Foster genuine and lasting relationships with executive mentors over a series of one\-to\-one meetings and events\.

[Learn More](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/executive-mentorship-program)

### [Finance Mentorship Program](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/finance-mentorship-program)

Explore career opportunities in the finance sector and prepare for summer recruitment, paired with an industry professional\.

[Learn More](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/finance-mentorship-program)

### [Financial Modelling &amp; Interview Prep Program](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/capital-markets-training-program)

A self\-paced online training program designed to equip BCom students with comprehensive skills in financial modelling and interview preparation

[Learn More](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/capital-markets-training-program)

### [Investment Banking Access Pathway \(IB\-AP\)](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience-gain-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/investment-banking-access-pathway)

The Investment Banking Access Pathway \(IB\-AP\) is a program designed to help 2nd\-year students stand out in the competitive investment banking internship recruitment process\.

[Learn more](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience-gain-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/investment-banking-access-pathway)

### [Montalbano Mentorship Program](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/thought-leadership/research-outreach-centres/montalbano-centre-responsible-leadership-development/mentorship)

A one\-on\-one mentorship program where high\-potential BCom leaders in years 3 and higher are matched with accomplished UBC Sauder alumni for an 8\-month period

[Learn more](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/thought-leadership/research-outreach-centres/montalbano-centre-responsible-leadership-development/mentorship)

### [Portfolio Management Foundation](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/thought-leadership/divisions/finance/pmf-program)

The UBC Portfolio Management Foundation \(PMF\) provides a small group of select BCom students with an experience that will provide world\-class training in the global capital markets\.

[Learn More](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/thought-leadership/divisions/finance/pmf-program)

### [Real Estate Internship &amp; Mentorship Program](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/real-estate-mentorship-program)

Experience a four\-month summer internship with a top real estate employer in Vancouver\.

[Learn More](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/mentorship-specialty-programs/real-estate-mentorship-program)

### [UBC SWIFT: UBC Sauder Women\+ in Finance Training](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/swift)

An extra\-curricular program for BCom students\. Through connections between academics and practitioners in finance, students reconcile practical portfolio management with rigorous academic theory\.

[Learn More](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/swift)
