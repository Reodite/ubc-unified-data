The CPAEF Education Foundation has established a bursary award to honour Mr\. David Sale FCPA FCGA, a former board chair of the legacy CGA\-BC Association and co\-chair of the CPABC Joint Venture, in memory of his tremendous contribution to academia and the accounting profession\.

The David Sale FCPA FCGA Memorial Bursary assists accounting students attending a BC post\-secondary institution\. Selection for these awards is based on community involvement, personal circumstances, and financial need\. Applications are reviewed twice a year and must be submitted by the deadlines of May 15 and November 15, respectively\.
