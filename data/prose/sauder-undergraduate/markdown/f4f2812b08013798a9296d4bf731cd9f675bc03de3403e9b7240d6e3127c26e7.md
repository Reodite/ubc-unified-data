### The Hari B\. Varshney Business Career Centre \(BCC\) at the UBC Sauder School of Business

The BCC is **your home for career development services** at UBC Sauder\. Our services and resources will help you gain career momentum to help you secure a bright future for yourself\.

We can also help you understand **what matters to you** and **how to find a career that fits** **you**\.
