A $3500 award has been endowed by the Commerce Undergraduate Society and the Sauder School of Business to honour Dr\. Stanley Hamilton for his over 25 year of outstanding service to UBC’s Sauder School of Business and to the Vancouver community\.  The award is offered to a 4th Year student in a Bachelor of Commerce program who is graduating and who has made a significant contribution to the Sauder School of Business and/or the larger community\.  The award is made by nomination of a selection committee, at least one member of which is appointed by the Commerce Undergraduate Society, and the Sauder School of Business\.

To apply, please provide:  
  
**Reflection of Your Top 3 Involvements at UBC Sauder \(video or text format\) –** An important part of this application is letting us know who you are and what makes you extraordinary\. In this section, tell us about your 3 most impactful or memorable leadership, involvement, and/or contributions to UBC Sauder\. Speak about your most important accomplishments and experiences during your undergraduate degree\.

-   If you choose to submit a video, please make sure the video is no longer than 3 minutes\.
-   If you choose to submit written text, please make sure your answer is 1000 words maximum and written in first person\.

**Statement of Activities/Resume** – Your statement of activities should focus on your accomplishments during your undergraduate career\. It will follow a similar format to a resume and be a maximum of two pages, 12 point font, with ¾ inch margins\. Please make sure you include ALL leadership/community involvement at UBC/Sauder\.

Your statement should be formatted as follows:

-   Personal Information \(name, email address, LinkedIn URL\)
-   University or community Activities \(Details / Impact / Dates for each\)
-   Each activity should include title/role, description of activity, and impact\. Work experience may be included in your activities\. Dates should include the month, year and hours per week \(e\.g\. Sep 2023 to Apr 2024; 3 hours per week\)\.
