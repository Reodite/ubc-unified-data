An award has been made available through an endowment established by Peter Brunold for an undergraduate student enrolled in the Bachelor of Commerce degree program in the UBC Sauder School of Business\. The award is for a student who has demonstrated academic excellence, is on the Dean's List, and who would not be able to pursue a degree program without financial support\. Preference will be given to a student with demonstrated leadership ability and involvement in the community\. To be considered, candidates must be Canadian Citizens or Permanent Residents\.
