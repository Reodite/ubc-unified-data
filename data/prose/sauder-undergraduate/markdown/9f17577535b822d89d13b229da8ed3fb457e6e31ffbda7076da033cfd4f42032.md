## Transfer Credits

Transfer credit is awarded at the time of admission by the university\. However, faculties determine how the credits will apply to their programs\. 

 If you have been admitted to UBC and have accepted your offer of admission, you may be able to use transfer credits that you have been awarded by UBC to fulfill some of the requirements in the [Bachelor of Commerce \(BCom\) degree](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-and-enrolment/program-requirements)\. Transfer Credits apply only to transfer students who completed the equivalent course prior to entering the BCom program\. Upon entering the program, BCom students are expected to satisfy the BCOM course requirements \(not the equivalent credits\) at UBC\. 

All students\-\-including transfer students\-\-must complete the following courses: [COMM 105, COMM 196 and COMM 202\.](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment-program-requirements/general-program-requirements/transfer-students) If you did not receive transfer credit for these courses, you should plan on registering in them as soon as possible\. Not completing these courses will delay your [degree progression\.](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-and-enrolment/exams-and-grades/academic-standing)Transfer credits awarded at another institution or at UBC\-O will be reassessed for the UBC\-V BCom program\.

Per the [UBC Calendar](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?tree=3,40,0,0) at least 50% of the credits required for the program must be completed while registered in the program\. As the BCom program consists of 121 credits \(including one 1\-credit core course: COMM\_V 202\), 61 credits must be completed while in the BCom program and up to 60 credits completed prior to admission to the BCom program may apply\. In the case of BUCS, 64 credits must be completed while in the BCom program\.  

If you completed coursework in another UBC faculty prior to transferring from a post\-secondary institution into year 2 or 3 of the BCom program, you must notify the Office of Student Academic Services \(OSAS\) if you completed additional coursework at a post\-secondary institution in the summer prior to beginning your studies in the BCom program\. Consider the following example scenario\. A student completed one year in UBC Faculty of Arts \(Sept\-Apr\), one year at a post\-secondary institution \(Sept\-Apr\), and completed additional coursework at a post\-secondary institution in the summer \(May\-Aug\)\. The student must notify OSAS they earned additional credit\. Failure to notify OSAS of additional credit earned may lead to the inability to graduate due to insufficient credits completed in the program\.

NOTE: *all* upper\-level \(300\- and 400\-level\) commerce courses, including core, option core and commerce electives must be completed while registered in the BCom program\. It is not possible to apply transfer credit toward these upper\-level requirements\. [See Requirements to Receive a Degree or Diploma\.](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/requirements-receive-degree-or-diploma) 

### Where to View Transfer Credits

You can view your credits on your Workday Student account by following the pathway Workday Student account &gt; Academics &gt; View Academic Record\. The exception to this is transfer students admitted with a prior degree\. Students holding a prior degree from another institution will receive a separate assessment from UBC Sauder\.

### Requesting Transfer Credit Assessments

Transfer students who received an unassigned COMM credit \(e\.g\., COMM 1st, COMM 2nd\) may request a re\-evaluation to determine if the credit fulfills a requirement in the program \(e\.g\., COMM 293\)\. Only requests for first\- or second\-year courses will be considered, as all upper\-level Commerce requirements must be completed while registered in the BCom program\.

Before submitting a request

-   Review the credits in the [BC Transfer Guide](https://www.bctransferguide.ca/) and/or [UBC’s Transfer Credit Search](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/transfer-credits/search-tool) \(and [UBC’s first\-year credit](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/applied/first-year-credit/) for AP, IB or A\-Level courses\)\. Some courses may already include a notation “not for credit in the BCOM program”\.
-   Check the [program requirements](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/program-requirements/bcom-requirements) and the course description to see if there is a substantial overlap in the content with the course you have completed\.

Requesting an assessment

-   Ensure you include a comprehensive outline of the course\(s\) you seek an assessment from the academic year when you had taken the course\. The outline should include:
    -   institution’s name
    -   course name and code
    -   credit value
    -   course completion year
    -   course duration
    -   name of the instructor
    -   textbook and materials used
    -   breakdown of the classes per week or day
    -   grade assessment
    -   course description/learning outcomes
    -   Complete the [Transfer Credit Evaluation Request Form](https://sauder.jotform.com/team/ugo/transfer-credit-evaluation)
    -   An assessment can take upwards to 4 weeks to complete from the time the request is submitted\.
    -   An assessment may results in one of the following:  
        -   Credit changing to a specific course
        -   No change and the unassigned credit may be considered toward a COMM toward elective requirements
        -   No credit in the program
            
            **NOTE**: *all* upper\-level \(300\- and 400\-level\) core requirements must be completed while enrolled in the BCom program\. It is not possible to apply transfer credit toward these upper\-level core requirements\. [See Requirements to Receive a Degree or Diploma\.](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/requirements-receive-degree-or-diploma)
            

After an assessment

-   You are not permitted to forfeit post\-secondary transfer credits that have been granted\.
-   Courses that are assessed to a direct equivalent COMM course will be updated on your UBC Workday Student\. No grade information is listed\. The grades you received for the course will not be reflected or used in the calculation of your UBC grades\. However, the Undergraduate Office will refer to the grade received from the original institution to determine if you have fulfill the [course minima required for declaring an option in Accounting or Finance](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/options)\.
-   You may decide to reduce your course load\. Note that reducing your course load may affect your eligibility to receive and retain scholarships, to reside in UBC residence, or to apply to a post\-graduation work permit\. Carefully review any conditions relevant to your personal circumstances to ensure your continued eligibility\.
