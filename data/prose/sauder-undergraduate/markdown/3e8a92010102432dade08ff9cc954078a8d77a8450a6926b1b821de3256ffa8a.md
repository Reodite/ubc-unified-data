A scholarship in memory of Dr\. Harry L\. Purdy, recognizes his leadership, interest and contributions in business, teaching and community affairs and has been endowed by Westar Timber Ltd\. The award is made to a student entering the final or graduating year in the undergraduate program of the Sauder School of Business who has been able to combine academic excellence with outstanding contributions in student or community affairs\.

> 
> community affairs/engagement
