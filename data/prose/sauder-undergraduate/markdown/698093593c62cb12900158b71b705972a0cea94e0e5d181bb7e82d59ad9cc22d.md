One\-on\-one \(or small group if you attend with a friend\) appointments with our Peer Math &amp; Economics Coaches can help with improving your comprehension of difficult concepts presented in:

-   MATH\_V 100
-   MATH\_V 101
-   MATH\_V 180
-   ECON\_V 101
-   ECON\_V 102

Attendees should come to appointments with questions regarding what they need assistance with\. There are also Exam Review sessions offered on for applicable classes\. Math &amp; Economics Coaching is offered by the Canaccord Learning Commons \(CLC\)\.

More information about the types of appointments available can be found in the 'CLC: Online' canvas course\. You may book an appointment on the **[CLC sign\-up page on Jane](https://canaccordlearningcommons.janeapp.com/)\.**
