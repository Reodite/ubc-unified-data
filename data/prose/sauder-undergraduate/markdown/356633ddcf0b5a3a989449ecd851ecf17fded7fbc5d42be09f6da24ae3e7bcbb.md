Awards are offered annually to students enrolled at the UBC Sauder School of Business in any year of study who are also members of the Varsity Thunderbird Swimming team\. This award may be renewable provided the award\-winning student maintains participation in the swimming team\. The award is made on the recommendation of the Sauder School of Business in consultation with UBC Swimming\.

> **Scholarship Type**
> 
> Sauder\-specific scholarships
> 
> **Basis of Selection**
> 
> Assigned by merit
> 
> **Criteria**
> 
> athletics
