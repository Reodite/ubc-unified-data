A scholarship and a one year membership in the Association has been made available by the Human Resources Management Association of British Columbia\. The award is made to a student entering the fourth year in the Organizational Behaviour and Human Resources option of the Sauder School of Business\. While the award is based mainly on scholastic achievement, participation in university and community activities may also be considered in the event of a tie\. In addition, HRMA will provide a free registration package to the annual conference\.
