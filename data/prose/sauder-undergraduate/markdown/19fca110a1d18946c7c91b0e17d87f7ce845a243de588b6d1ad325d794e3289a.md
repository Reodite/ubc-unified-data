Procrastination

It's easy to fall off track and get stuck in a pattern of procrastination\. To avoid the stress and anxiety that follows, here are some strategies to help get you started and trick yourself into taking action\! Below are some of the best practices you can try out:

-   Pomodoro technique: do uninterrupted work for 25 minutes, take a 5 minute break, then repeat 3 times and take a longer 20 minute break
-   5 minutes: You can do almost anything for 5 minutes\. Start an assignment, start reading a chapter, review your lecture notes, etc\. You’ll find that after 5 minutes, you either want to keep plugging away at it, you’re almost done whatever you set out to do, or you’re really not into it\. But at least you took action for those 5 minutes\.
-   Sounds silly, but put your phone somewhere you can’t get to it: give it to your neighbour in residence, charge it across the room, or turn it off so it’s more challenging to check it\. At least keep it out of arm’s reach\.

[View strategies to help with procrastination](https://academicservices.engineering.ubc.ca/2021/03/11/procrastination-why-do-we-do-it-and-what-can-we-do-to-stop/)
