The purpose of this course is to provide an intensive overview of union\-management relations\. The course builds upon COMM 392 by looking more closely at the nature of the actors, the legal framework, the substantive issues and the interactions of the actors\. The course will conclude with a consideration of industrial relations in international perspective\.

Note: COHR 310 will not be offered in 2026W\.

Prerequisite: [COMM 203](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses/comm-203)\.
