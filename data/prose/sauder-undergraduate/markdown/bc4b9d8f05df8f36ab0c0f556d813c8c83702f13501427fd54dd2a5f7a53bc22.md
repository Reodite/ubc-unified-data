### Build your network and take your career to the next level\!

[BCC Networking Event](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/level_2_-_networking_resources.jpg?itok=T9B-fjcc)

You might wonder, "What's the big deal with networking?"

Well, it’s **one of the most important things you can do for your career**\. Not only will it help you **make meaningful connections** with peers, alumni, and professionals, i**t can** **help you:**

-   **Explore your possibilities**: Get a better understanding of what it’s like to work in different careers
-   **Practice the art of communication**: sharpen your communication skills to express your unique talents and career interests
-   **Get face\-to\-face with employers**: chat with employers hiring for roles you’re interested in

Networking isn’t just for extroverts\. We get it\. Networking can be intimidating, but you can do it almost anywhere – from large\-scale events to 1:1 coffee chats, LinkedIn, **[UBC student club](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/student-life/clubs/)** events or while volunteering \(check out **[Go Volunteer](https://govolunteer.ca/)** to find opportunities\)\.
