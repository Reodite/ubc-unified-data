An award is offered annually by the John M\. S\. Lecky Foundation for students in their 2nd year in the Sauder School of Business who have achieved a minimum percentage of 72% and are the best qualified in terms of academic merit and financial need\. The award is made on the recommendation of the Sauder School of Business\.

> **Scholarship Type**
> 
> Sauder\-specific scholarships
> 
> **Basis of Selection**
> 
> Assigned by merit
> 
> **Criteria**
> 
> academic achievement/standing
> 
> financial need
