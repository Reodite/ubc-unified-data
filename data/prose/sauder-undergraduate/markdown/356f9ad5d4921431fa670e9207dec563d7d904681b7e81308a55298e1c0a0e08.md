HSBC Bank Canada offers an award that has been endowed through donations by employees who are Sauder Alumni\. The award is available for an undergraduate student entering first year of the Bachelor of Commerce Program\. The award is based on a combination of financial need and community service\.

> **Scholarship Type**
> 
> Sauder\-specific scholarships
> 
> **Basis of Selection**
> 
> Awarded through application process
> 
> **Criteria**
> 
> community affairs/engagement
> 
> financial need
