## Student Academic Services at UBC Sauder

Whether you're looking for academic guidance, need support navigating university life, or would like help planning your next steps, the Office of Student Academic Services \(OSAS\) is here for you\.

### Reasons to Visit OSAS

**Academic Planning &amp; Course Support**

-   Get help planning your courses\.
-   Ask what it could mean if you increase or decrease your course load\.
-   Learn how dropping a core COMM or COHR course might affect promotion or graduation\.
-   Understand how taking an elective as Credit/D/Fail impacts GPA, scholarships, or academic honours\.
-   Ask about promotion requirements, academic policies, or regulations\.
-   Learn more about the different specializations\.
-   Plan for exchange\.

**Graduation &amp; Registration**

-   Confirm if you're on track to meet graduation requirements\.
-   Ask questions about course registration\.

**Support &amp; Resources**

-   Connect with us if you are experiencing circumstances that are affecting your academic performance and you need guidance or referrals to campus supports\.
-   Academic concessions: the website has a lot of information for you if have you missed classes or an exam due to illness or emergency\. You are also welcome to connect with an advisor to understand what to do\.
-   Come talk to us if you are not sure where to start when looking for campus resources\!
-   Connect with the Student Engagement team if you want to get involved at UBC Sauder or UBC and aren’t sure where to begin\.

## Online Request Forms

Academic concession

Sometimes unanticipated events or circumstances can affect your ability to complete your academic responsibilities\. You may be eligible to submit a petition for an academic concession if you meet the certain eligibility criteria, and in some circumstances, provide supporting documentation\.  

An academic concession is a form of support that allows for deferment of marked work so that you can complete a course's requirements\. Carefully review UBC's academic concession policy to understand the different types of concessions as well as how to submit a petition\.

[Request academic concession](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/academic-concession)  

Credit/D/Fail grading

If you would like to switch a course to [Credit/D/Fail grading](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-and-enrolment/exams-and-grades/grades) you can submit the request form once you have registered for all of your courses; you won’t be able to perform this function yourself in Workday\. OSAS will only process requests as defined in the form’s instructions\. Academic advisors will direct all requests they receive to this form\. Please allow at least five business days for processing\. The deadline to switch between percentage and Credit/D/Fail grading is the same date to which you are still eligible to drop the course without a W standing\.

[Request Credit/D/Fail grading switch](https://sauder.jotform.com/team/ugo/switching-course-to-cdf)  

Proof of enrolment and confirmation of program completion letters

Your UBC transcript and diploma are official documents issued by the university to affirm your academic record and completion of degree program, respectively\.

On occasion, you may be asked by a third\-party to provide additional documentation regarding your studies at UBC, such as when applying to extend your study permit or proving your credentials to a potential employer\. This is typically provided in the form of a letter from the school which may, for example, confirm:

-   your status as a registered, full\-time student
-   your expected program completion date
-   your completion of degree requirements
-   conferral of your degree 

You can download some of these letters on demand from the Student Service Centre \(SSC\), but others must be requested from the UGO\.

[Request a letter](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/letters)  

Registration assistance

All registration requests will be reviewed to determine if they meet one of the valid reasons for registration assistance\. Requests made for invalid reasons will be dismissed\. Office of Student Academic Services \(OSAS\) will attempt to accommodate your registration request based on seat availability in sections \(not necessarily section preference\), and in some cases seat availability in alternate courses that will fulfill the same requirement\.

Please review the following webpage to determine if your reason for registration assistance is valid before submitting your request\. Do not submit requests until you have an open registration\.

[Registration Assistance Webpage](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses-and-registration/registration-assistance)

Credit limit increase

Balancing a full course load is challenging, so a credit increase is not usually recommended, particularly if you work or have other responsibilities\. If you are approved for a credit increase \(3\-6 credits max\), you will not be eligible for academic concession for any issues related to your increased workload\.

In Workday, the credits for two\-term, six credit courses are not evenly split across academic periods, but instead apply to the term in which the course begins\. In this instance, you can request an increase to your Term 1 limit to offset the imbalance – your Term 2 credit limit will be reduced accordingly\.

Credit increases to take an increased course load are only permitted after all students have had a chance to register\. Year 1 students are not eligible for credit limit increases\.

The credit limit increase request form below is available for students to request rebalancing of credit limits to allow for registration in two\-term, six credit courses\.

**Requests for credit limit increases to take additional courses in the Winter Session will be accepted starting August 1 once all students have had an opportunity to register\.**

#### [Request a credit limit increase](https://sauder.jotform.com/team/ugo/credit-limit-increase)
