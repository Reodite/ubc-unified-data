## UBC Sauder's Embedded Counselling Services

### Nafiza Ali \(she, her, hers\), MA

Registered Clinical Counsellor for the UBC Sauder School of Business

[Nafiza](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/custom_small_200x200_/public/2022-07/NafizaBioPicture_portrait.jpg?itok=4IBOFORJ)

Nafiza Ali \(she/her\), MA, is the registered Clinical Counsellor for the UBC Sauder School of Business\. Nafiza offers drop\-in and short\-term mental health and wellbeing counselling to undergraduate and graduate students at UBC Sauder\. She also facilitates workshops and provides outreach support tailored to the concerns and needs of students, staff, and faculty\.

Students can book an appointment with Nafiza to discuss any personal, professional, or academic concerns impacting their lives\.

To learn more and book an appointment with UBC Sauder's Embedded Counselling Services:

### Take care of your mind, body, and spirit

Being mentally and physically healthy includes taking care of yourself every day, understanding that challenges are normal, and knowing what to do when you don't feel well\. 

Some of the top wellness concerns affecting students include: 

Food and nutrition

Learn how to eat well on a budget, get started on cooking, and meal prep\. Food access supports can also be found on our ['student support and resources'](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/support) page\.

[Learn more](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/food-nutrition)  

Physical activity

Being physically active can reduce stress and help you feel more energized and alert, making it a lot easier to focus on studying, writing, completing assignments or lab work, and preparing for exams\.

[Learn more](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/physical-activity/)  

Sleep

Getting enough quality sleep is crucial for performing your best\. Lack of sleep has a significant impact on brain function, health, safety, and longevity\. Sleep itself can actually improve mental function is vital for learning and memory\. 

[Learn more](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/sleep-success)   

Understanding stress

Stress is a normal part of the human experience\. However, everyone experiences stress differently and stressors are unique to each person\. Below you will find information on recognizing signs of stress, managing the stress response, and understanding different types of stressors\.

[Learn more](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/understanding-stress/)  

Sex &amp; sexual health

Learning about sex and sexual health can help you have safe, respectful, and enjoyable experiences\.

[Learn more](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/sex-sexual-health/)  

Alcohol and other drugs

Understand UBC substance use policies and learn more about substance misuse\.

[Learn more](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/alcohol-cannabis-nicotine/)  

### Safety on campus

Everyone plays a role in making UBC’s Vancouver campus a safe and welcoming place\. The best way to keep our campus safe is to look out for each other and to reach out when we need help ourselves\.

[Learn more](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety/)
