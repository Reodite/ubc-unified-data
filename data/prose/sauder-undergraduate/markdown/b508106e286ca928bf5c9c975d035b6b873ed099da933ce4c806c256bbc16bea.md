Awards have been made available annually through a gift from JLL for domestic Bachelor of Commerce students who identify as Black, have achieved good academic standing and demonstrated community involvement\. Preference will be given to students with an interest in real estate\. JLL is a global professional services firm specializing in real estate\. They created this award to support Black students in the UBC Sauder School of Business, and to encourage those with an interest in real estate to consider entering the industry\.
