The intention of this course is to provide students with a solid grasp of the essential practices and tools of employee recruitment and selection within a comprehensive human resource management strategy\.

### Learning objectives

Specifically, the course has the following learning objectives:

-   develop students’ understanding of how staffing strategies and decisions are developed and executed\.
-   ensure that students fully grasp the legal and ethical constraints and obligations associated with staffing\.
-   develop students’ grasp of the technical process of job analysis as the basis for staffing decisions\.
-   establish students’ understanding of the various modes and means of employee recruitment\.
-   develop students’ grasp of the various tools and techniques used in selecting employees from among job applicants\.
-   enable the students to understand some of the key principles related to retention management\.  
      
    

Prerequisite: [COMM 203](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses/comm-203)\.
