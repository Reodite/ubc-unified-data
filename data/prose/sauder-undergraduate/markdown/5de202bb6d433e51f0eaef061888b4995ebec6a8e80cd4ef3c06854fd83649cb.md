This scholarship is awarded annually to the student, entering Year 2, who best exemplifies the spirit and practice of bilingualism in the Canadian communications and public relations profession\. It is inspired by the career of Armand Torchia, who cofounded The Houston Group with Stan Houston, and Torchia Communications with his son Jean\-Claude\. The Scholarship adheres to the values espoused by Torchia Communications in providing quality services in both of Canada’s official languages\. 

For additional information including eligibility, award criteria, application details, and deadline, please visit [cprs\.ca](https://www.cprsfoundation.ca/en/impact)\.
