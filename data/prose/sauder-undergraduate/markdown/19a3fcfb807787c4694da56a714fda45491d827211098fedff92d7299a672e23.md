Scholarships have been made available annually through a gift from Greater Vancouver REALTORS, in memory of Alan G\. Creer for Bachelor of Commerce students in the Real Estate Option entering their fourth year who have high academic standing and are deserving of assistance to further their education in the profession of real estate\. The awards are made on the recommendation of the UBC Sauder School of Business\.

> **Scholarship Type**
> 
> Sauder\-specific scholarships
> 
> **Basis of Selection**
> 
> Assigned by merit
> 
> **Criteria**
> 
> academic achievement/standing
> 
> financial need
> 
> option
>
