Three bursaries have been endowed by the Commerce Alumni Division for students entering the second, third and final year of the BCom program\. The awards are based on a combination of financial need and contributions to the University and/or community\. Candidates will normally be expected to have at least a second\- class average \(65%\) in the previous academic year\.

> **Scholarship Type**
> 
> Sauder\-specific scholarships
> 
> **Basis of Selection**
> 
> Awarded through application process
> 
> **Criteria**
> 
> academic achievement/standing
> 
> community affairs/engagement
> 
> extra\-curricular involvement
> 
> financial need
