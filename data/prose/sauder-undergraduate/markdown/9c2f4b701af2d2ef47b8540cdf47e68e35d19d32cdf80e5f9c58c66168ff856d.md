### Personal experience

If you are claiming funding to offset expenses for a personal experience such as volunteer/service learning or attending a competition or conference\.

**After your experience:**

**Receipts:**

-   Collect all of the receipt\(s\) for the expenses you are claiming\. Compile into a single PDF document and save with the naming convention **firstname\_lastname\_receipts**
-   Screenshots are not accepted\. Please make sure to submit a complete confirmation of payment document \(PDF or email confirmation\)\.
-   Please make sure receipts have an itemized breakdown \(showing tax amount and how much each item cost\)
-   All receipts will need to show detailed payment information, such as credit card used and your full name\. If this is not possible, students should additionally submit a credit card statement\.
-   What if someone else on the team made the purchase for me? Or I only paid for a portion of the costs?
-   Make sure you submit a screenshot or confirmation of the etransfer to show that you have paid for your portion or someone paid you back for their portion

**Letter of Impact:**

-   Prepare a letter of impact to the OSAS, explaining the impact this experience and funding has had on your personal, professional or academic experience\. Save as a single PDF document with the naming convention **firstname\_lastname\_letter**
-   If you have taken photos during your experience, please upload them as well

**Once you have those things ready:**

-   Complete the online [**Student Experience Grant Acceptance Form**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_dmR0Fp61wpUux4q "https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/sv_87b6cnddxdpvmhn") and upload your receipts and letter by the deadline indicated in the email\.
-   Ensure your mailing address and direct deposit information is up to date on [Workday Student\.](https://myworkday.ubc.ca/ "https://myworkday.ubc.ca/")
    -   [Instructions for updating mailing address](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-changing-your-contact-information/ "https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-changing-your-contact-information/")
    -   [Instructions for updating direct deposit](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/finances/setting-up-your-direct-deposit/ "https://workday.students.ubc.ca/finances/setting-up-your-direct-deposit/")

Please note, completion of steps are required for students to receive funding through the Student Experience Grant\.
