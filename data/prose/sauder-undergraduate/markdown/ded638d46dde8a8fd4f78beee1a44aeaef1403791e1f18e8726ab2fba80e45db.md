At UBC Sauder, **you don't have to navigate your career alone**\.

The Hari B\. Varshney Business Career Centre \(BCC\) offers a range of coaching and advising options, whether you're feeling stuck and unsure, or know exactly what you want to do\.

### What is career coaching &amp; advising?

Career coaching &amp; advising involves talking with Career Strategists and/or trained Peer Career Advisors about different aspects of your career journey\.

You’re in charge\! These conversations are for and about you and can dive into any career\-related topic you want support with, including:

-   Job application reviews
-   Career exploration
-   Job search strategies
-   Feeling stuck \(see a Career Strategist for these conversations\)

### Who can you meet with?

In 1st year, meet with Peer Career Advisors at Drop\-in or book 1:1 appointments through [**UBC Sauder Career Hub**](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/appointments)\. Beginning in 2nd year, you can also book appointments directly with Career Strategists\.

Registered Co\-op students have access to a team of knowledgeable Co\-op Career Strategists to help guide them throughout their Co\-op experience\.

### What topics do students usually talk about?

BComs most commonly book appointments for

-   **Job Application Review**: get guidance on your resume and cover letter
-   **Career Exploration/Navigation**: learn tactics to discover jobs, industries, and employers that align with your interests and goals
-   **Job Search Strategies**: find advice on how you get started and find meaningful work experience opportunities
-   **I’m Feeling Stuck**: share what’s making you feel stuck, and we’ll help you create a path forward
-   **Interview Prep &amp; Strategies**: practice answering interview questions and get tips for success

Find a complete list of coaching &amp; advising appointment types on [**UBC Sauder Career Hub**](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/appointments)\.

### How long are coaching &amp; advising appointments?

You can book 15, 30, and 45\-minute appointments—depending on your program and your topic\.

### Where are appointments held?

**Virtual**: Zoom—you will be emailed a Zoom link ahead of your appointment

**In\-Person with BCom Career Strategists/Co\-op Career Strategist**: In the BCC \(HA 155\)

**In\-Person with Peer Career Advisors**: They will be back in September 2026\.

### How to book an appointment?

1.  Log in to the UBC Sauder Career Hub using your CWL 
2.  Select **Appointments** from the left navigation menu 
3.  Filters appointments by **location, Career Advisor\***, or **appointment type** 
4.  Select an available time under a Career Advisor's name, then choose **View Available Slots** 
5.  Enter your appointment details and select either **virtual** or **in\-person** location type 
6.  Select **Book Appointment** 

\*Career Strategists are labelled Career Advisors in the Career Hub\.

[Log into UBC Sauder Career Hub](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/appointments)
