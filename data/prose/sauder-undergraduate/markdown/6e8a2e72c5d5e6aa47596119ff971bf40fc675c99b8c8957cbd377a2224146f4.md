### Student support

In addition to in\-person services, we continue to provide student support through email and online platforms during business hours from Monday to Friday\. 

You'll find many of the answers to your questions about your program—as well as resources and services available to support you—on **[this site](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/)** and at **[students\.ubc\.ca](https://students.ubc.ca/)**\. We encourage you to start by reviewing our **[Frequently Asked Questions page](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/ugo/faqs)**\. If you still have questions or concerns, please visit our **[virtual office](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/ugo/#virtualoffice)** during business hours to ask a quick question in the chat or schedule an appointment with an academic advisor\.

##### OSAS

Please use our self\-serve booking system to check for available times and schedule your online appointment with an academic advisor\.

[Book an online advising appointment](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/ugo#bookadvising)  

Visit our virtual office during business hours to ask a quick question in the chat\. 

[Virtual office](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/ugo#virtualoffice)  

Visit our virtual office in Zoom by clicking the button above\. We encourage you to edit your display name in Zoom to your first name only before joining the virtual office waiting room \(i\.e\., our “lobby”\) to protect your privacy\. Please do not include your last name or student number in your display name or the group chat as this private information will be viewable by everyone in the virtual office\.

-   If you have a time\-sensitive inquiry or you would like to book an advising appointment, please wait to be connected with one of our team members\.

-   If you are joining the virtual office to attend your scheduled advising appointment, please wait for your advisor to move you to their private breakout room \(“office”\) for your meeting\.

##### BCC

##### **Career Coaching &amp; Advising**

Monday\-Friday: 8:30am to 4:30pm  
Saturday\-Sunday: closed

To make a 1:1 coaching &amp; advising appointment with a BCom or Co\-op Career Strategist, take the following steps:

1.  Log in to the UBC Sauder Career Hub using your CWL 
2.  Select Appointments from the left navigation menu 
3.  Filter appointments by location, Career Advisor\*, or appointment type 
4.  Select an available time under a Career Advisor's name, then choose View Available Slots 
5.  Enter your appointment details and select either virtual or in\-person location type 
6.  Select Book Appointment \*Career Strategists are labelled Career Advisors in the Career Hub\. 

[Log in to Career Hub](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/appointments/)
