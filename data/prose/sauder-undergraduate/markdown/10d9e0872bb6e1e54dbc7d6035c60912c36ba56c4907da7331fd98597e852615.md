As a student enrolled at an Affiliated University and in an affiliated degree program you can apply for a CFA Program Student Scholarship\.

As a participant in the University Affiliation Program, UBC Sauder is allotted a number of Student Scholarships that are made available to BCom and MBA students\. The current allotment is six scholarships, awarded two times a year\.

Students who apply for a scholarship should not register for a CFA Program exam until a decision on the scholarship application has been issued\.

If you are selected for an award, the Student Scholarship waives the one\-time program enrollment fee, and discounts the exam registration fee to $600, which includes access to the curriculum and all available study tools\. This discounted price \($600\) will be reflected during the checkout process\.

The scholarship is valid for one year from the date an award decision is made\. The scholarship recipient can register for any available exam offering they qualify for during that time\.

Students not selected will be eligible to register at the early registration pricing for up to one year from the date the award decision is made\.

All applicants will be notified immediately when the award decision is made, and the award decision is final\.

To apply for a CFA Program Student Scholarship, you must:

-   Not be currently registered for a CFA Program exam
-   Satisfy the enrollment requirements detailed here: [https://www\.cfainstitute\.org/en/programs/cfa/charter](https://www.cfainstitute.org/en/programs/cfa/charter)
-   Be a current BCom student in year 3 or 4 at the time of the scholarship application deadline
-   Your selected exam window for Level 1 must be 23 months or fewer before your graduation month for your bachelor’s degree or equivalent program\. You must be within 11 months of graduation to sit for your Level II exam and you must have completed your bachelor’s degree or accrued 4,000 hours of professional work experience to sit for your Level III exam\.  
      
    
-   **Application and selection:**

Complete the Student Scholarship application on the CFA website:  
[https://www\.cfainstitute\.org/en/programs/cfa/scholarships/student](https://www.cfainstitute.org/en/programs/cfa/scholarships/student)

In the attachments section of the application, upload a one\-page motivation letter explaining how the CFA Program fits with your career plans\. Please make reference to your registered name at UBC in your letter and include the last four digits of your UBC student number\.

Selection is based on academic merit\.

#### **Deadlines:**

Scholarship applications will be reviewed each year on a biannual basis:

-   November 1
    
-   April 1
    

Applications will not be reviewed until shortly after each deadline\. Please note, if there are unused scholarships available after the April deadline, an additional application period will be added with a deadline of June 29\.
