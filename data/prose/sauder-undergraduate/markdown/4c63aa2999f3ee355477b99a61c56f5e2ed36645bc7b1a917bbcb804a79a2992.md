### Gain experience &amp; build your professional skills

[Students working at a whiteboard](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/level_2_-_work_and_volunteer_experience.jpg?itok=LzMGbyu9)

When talking about gaining experience for your resume, we’re **not just referring to *paid* work experience**, especially early on in your career journey\. To build your experience and skills, you can take part in a wide variety of activities, including:

-   Internships
-   Co\-op work terms
-   Paid work \(part\-time, full\-time, and contracts\)
-   Mentorship programs
-   Case competitions
-   Starting your own business
-   Volunteering
-   Team &amp; club participation
-   Group projects in the classroom

These experiences will help you gain transferrable skills, make connections, build your story for future job applications, and clarify your career goals\.

- **Row 3**

- **Row 4**

- **Row 5**

Build on your experience working with the community and get creative with what you can do to address community needs\.
