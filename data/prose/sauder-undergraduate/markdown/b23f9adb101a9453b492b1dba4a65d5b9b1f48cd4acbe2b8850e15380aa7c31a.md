The Harold A\. Jones Memorial Scholarship is donated by Seaspan ULC \(an organization integrating Vancouver Tug Boat Co\. Ltd\. and Island Tug &amp; Barge Limited, both pioneer companies in marine transportation on the B\.C\. coast\) as a memorial to Harold A\. Jones, who was President of Vancouver Tug Boat Co\. Ltd\. from 1924 to 1956\. This scholarship serves not only to pay tribute to Mr\. Jones' contribution to the company, but also to give recognition to his interest and participation, both public and personal, in all matters pertaining to coast\-wise shipping\. The scholarship is to be awarded to an outstanding student entering the final year in the Bachelor of Commerce Program and majoring in the field of transportation in the Sauder School of Business\.
