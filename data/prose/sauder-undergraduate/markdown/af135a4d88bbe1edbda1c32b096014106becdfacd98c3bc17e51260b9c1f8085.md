An investigation and report on a topic to be agreed upon by a member of the faculty and a senior student\. Directed Studies allow you to receive course credit for an independent study/project completed under the supervision of a Faculty member\. Once you have found a professor who has agreed to supervise your project, download the Directed Studies form at [mybcom\.sauder\.ubc\.ca/files/directed\-studies\-form](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/comm_490_directed_studies_application_form.pdf)\.

[View form](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/comm_490_directed_studies_application_form.pdf)  

With the form, you will be required to provide a course outline describing your project and a summary of how it will be graded\. Please submit the completed form and course outline to the UBC Sauder Office of Student Academic Services for approval and registration\.
