## The Canaccord Learning Commons &amp; David Lam Management Research Library

**The Canaccord Learning Commons \(CLC\) and David Lam Management Research Library is a hub for UBC Sauder students\.**

The Canaccord Learning Commons and [David Lam Library](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/) provides support for all UBC Sauder School of Business students – including BCom’s, MBA’s, MBAN’s, MM’s, B\+MM’s, PhD’s, DAP, and all other degree programs – with learning support services, resources, and opportunities to hone the skills that will make them successful in the classroom and in the working world\.

Sauder students, check out our announcements, and access additional information, on the [CLC: Online](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/51980)\. You can use our [self\-serve online booking system](https://canaccordlearningcommons.janeapp.com/) to sign up\.

### Reasons to visit the CLC and Library

-   Access research help from business librarians, writing consultations, and subject specific support
-   Find and borrow print books and course reserves
-   Borrow equipment, including laptops, cameras, projectors, and chargers
-   Find group and silent study spaces or reserve a bookable group study room
-   Access ebooks and business databases
-   Browse the "Great Reads" collection of popular business books
-   Use specialized software in the Digital Media Lab, the Leith Wheeler Investment Research Lab, and the Wayne Deans Investment Analysis Centre
-   Use printers, photocopiers, scanners, desktop workstations, and dual display stations

### Jane Sign\-Up FAQ's 

**How can I sign up for a session?**

 You can book directly through the **[CLC Booking Page](https://canaccordlearningcommons.janeapp.com/)**\. 

-   Click on the session you want to book\. 
-   Choose the available timeslot on the “Book an Appointment” page\. 
-   When the confirmation pop\-up appears, click “I’m Done” or “Book More”\. 
-   You’ll be taken to the “Thank You for Booking” page — you’re all set\! 

**How can I join a waitlist for a certain session?**

 If a session is fully booked, you can join the waitlist\. 

-   Complete the **[CLC Booking Waitlist Form](https://canaccordlearningcommons.janeapp.com/wait_list/new?discipline_id=1&amp;treatment_id=1)** \(also accessible through the CLC Booking Page\)\. 

-   Ensure Staff or Discipline matches the session\. 
-   Select the dates and times you wish to be notified about\. 
-   Choose SMS or Email notifications\. 

-   You’ll be redirected to **[“Your Account” page](https://canaccordlearningcommons.janeapp.com/account)**, where your waitlist request will appear\. 

⚠️ **Please Note:** When a spot opens, everyone on the waitlist is notified\. Appointments are filled on a first\-come, first\-served basis\. 

**How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?**

You can manage your appointments from **[“Your Account” page](https://canaccordlearningcommons.janeapp.com/account)**  

-   Access it via the link at the bottom of your confirmation email, or  
-   Click on the “My Account” button in the top\-right corner of the booking page after signing in\. 
    

Under Upcoming Appointments, select the appointment you want to cancel or reschedule\. 

⚠️ **Please Note:** We ask that you cancel at least 48 hours in advance\. When you cancel, waitlisted students are automatically notified\. 

**How do I find the location of my session?**

Locations are listed at the top of the session description in the **[CLC Booking Page](https://canaccordlearningcommons.janeapp.com/)**, and in your booking confirmation/reminder emails \(denoted with ‼️\)\.

⚠️ **Please Note:** Be sure to note the specific room number or Zoom link, not the general CLC address shown on the booking site or calendar event\. 

**How do I add appointments to my preferred calendar software?**

  
Appointments can be synced automatically to your calendar

-   Go to **[“Your Account” page](https://canaccordlearningcommons.janeapp.com/account)**:

-   Access it via the link at the bottom of your confirmation email, or
-   Click on the "My Account" button in the top\-right corner of the booking page after signing in\.

-   Click "Subscribe to your calendar"\.
-   You will be redirected to a new page "Calendar Subscriptions"\. Choose an option or manually embed the \.ics subscription link into your preferred calendar app\.

**How do I manage email and text reminders?**

  
By default, you'll receive reminder notifications for:

-   Changes to your booked appointments\.
-   Waitlist openings \(only for sessions that you've joined the waitlist\)\.
-   A reminder 48 hours before your session\.

To update your preferences:

-   Go to **[“Your Account” page](https://canaccordlearningcommons.janeapp.com/account)** 

-   Access it via the link at the bottom of your confirmation email, or 
-   Click on the "My Account" button in the top\-right corner of the booking page after signing in\.

-   On the side bar, click "Notifications &amp; Reminders"\.
-   Adjust your notification settings\.
-   Click "Save Preferences"\.

**Who do I contact if I have questions or need help?**

  
Please reach out to [clchelp@sauder\.ubc\.ca](mailto:clchelp@sauder.ubc.ca)\. We will get you connected right away during regular business hours\.
