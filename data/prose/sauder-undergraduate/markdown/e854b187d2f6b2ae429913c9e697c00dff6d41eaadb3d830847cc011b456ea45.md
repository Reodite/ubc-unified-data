A scholarship has been endowed by the friends of the late Granville Mayall with the assistance of the British Columbia Trucking Association\. The scholarship is offered to graduate or undergraduate students in Commerce and Business Administration\. It is awarded on the basis of academic standing, personal qualities and interest in the field of transportation\. It is expected that the student will undertake a paper in the area of transportation\.
