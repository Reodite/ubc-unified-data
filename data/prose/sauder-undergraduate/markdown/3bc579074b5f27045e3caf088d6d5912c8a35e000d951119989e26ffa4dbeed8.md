## What's the plan? 

During the afternoon of Saturday, January 27, 2024, students will attend workshops designed and led by UBC Sauder Alumni\. These sessions are meant to be 2\-way conversations where students can discuss their goals, curiosities, and challenges when it comes to preparing for life after UBC Sauder\. Alumni will share intimate and personal stories about the first 3\-5 years after graduation and guide students through activities with the intention of sharing insights they wish they knew when they graduated\.    

End the day with an optional social to meet and chat with a multitude of UBC Sauder Alumni\.

## Why should you attend:  

-   Discover some of the most important lessons in business straight from the minds of UBC Sauder’s Leadership Team\.
