Awards have been made available annually through a gift from RSM Canada LLP for outstanding third\- or fourth\-year Bachelor of Commerce students majoring in Accounting who demonstrate leadership qualities, involvement in their community through volunteering, and a desire to work in accounting and consulting\. RSM Canada LLP is a consultancy firm that specializes in auditing, tax and consulting for middle market organizations\. The Canadian affiliate of the RSM Global network was formed in 2017 and opened its Vancouver, BC office in the summer of 2021\.
