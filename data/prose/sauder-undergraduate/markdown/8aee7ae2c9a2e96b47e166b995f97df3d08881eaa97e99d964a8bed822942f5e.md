### Vault Career Guides &amp; Rankings

[Vault logo](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/vault_new-01.png?itok=qnSmXeDI&amp;c=0e89631cf728c28dde4932f49cc40efa)

**Find in\-depth intelligence on what it's really like to work in different industries, companies, or professions**\. With Vault, you get unlimited access to:

-   **Career Guides &amp; Advice**: Access guides on a wide range of industries and roles, along with interviewing and networking tips
-   **Company Rankings &amp; Profiles**: Find insider information on where people love to work and what might make an employer the right fit for you
-   **Jobs &amp; Internship**s: Find opportunities and get all the details on top internship programs
-   **Exclusive Events**: Discover career fairs where you can meet and interview with employers

#### **How to access Vault**

The BCC provides all BCom students **free access to Vault** to support their career exploration\. Follow the below steps:

1.  Log in to **UBC Sauder Career Hub** using your CWL
2.  Click on ***Resource Library*** in the left navigation
3.  Search **"Vault"** in the top search bar
4.  Click on the Vault resource you want to use
5.  Once on the Vault page, select ***Get Started*** &gt; ***Register now***
6.  Complete the registration form using your preferred email address
7.  Create your profile to tailor the content you want to see and more

[Access Vault on Career Hub](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/resource-library/)
