Awards have been made available annually through a gift from the Commerce Undergraduate Society for Bachelor of Commerce students who identify as Indigenous, Black or a person of colour and are in good academic standing\. Financial need may be considered\. The Commerce Undergraduate Society is one of the largest business student organizations in Canada, and is committed to enhancing the personal, professional and academic experiences of Bachelor of Commerce students at UBC\.
