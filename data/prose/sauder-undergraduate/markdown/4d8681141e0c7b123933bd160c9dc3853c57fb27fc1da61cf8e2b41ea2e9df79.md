## Elective Requirements

##### Elective requirements differ slightly depending on which option you choose to complete\. Effective 2023W for transfer students, non\-Commerce electives must include at least 3 credits in the humanities, and at least 3 credits in science or social science\. These elective credits may be completed during any year of your program\. Language courses are not eligible for the humanities requirement, but literature courses are\. BUCS students are exempt and do not have to fulfil this requirement\. See Elective Requirements for a list acceptable humanities, and science or social science courses\.  

#### **Accounting, Entrepreneurship, Finance, General Business Management, Organizational Behaviour and Human Resources, Business Technology Management, Marketing, Real Estate** 

-   **45 credits in total** 
    

-   27 credits non\-Commerce: must include minimum 12 credits at the 300 or 400\-level and 15 credits at 100, 200, 300 or 400\-level 
    

-   15 credits Commerce or non\-Commerce: must include minimum 3 credits at the 300 or 400\-level and 12 credits at 100, 200, 300 or 400\-level 
    

-   3 credits Commerce or non\-Commerce in lieu of COMM\_V 101 and can be 100, 200, 300 or 400\-level 
    

#### **Operations and Logistics** 

-   **48 credits in total** 
    

-   27 credits non\-Commerce: must include minimum 12 credits at the 300 or 400\-level and 15 credits at the 100, 200, 300 or 400\-level 
    

-   18 credits Commerce or non\-Commerce: must include minimum 6 credits at the 300 or 400\-level and 12 credits at the 100, 200, 300 or 400\-level 
    

-   3 credits Commerce or non\-Commerce in lieu of COMM\_V 101 and can be 100, 200, 300 or 400\-level  
    

#### **Global Supply Chain and Logistics Management** 

-   **42 credits in total** 
    

-   27 credits non\-Commerce: must include minimum 12 credits at the 300 or 400\-level and 15 credits at the 100, 200, 300 or 400\-level 
    

-   12 credits Commerce or non\-Commerce at any level 
    

-   3 credits Commerce or non\-Commerce in lieu of COMM\_V 101 and can be 100, 200, 300 or 400\-level 
    

## Residency Requirement

Per the [UBC Calendar](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?tree=3,40,0,0) at least 50% of the credits required for the program must be completed while registered in the program\. As the BCom program consists of 121 credits \(including one 1\-credit core course: COMM\_V 202\), 61 credits must be completed while in the BCom program and up to 60 credits completed prior to admission to the BCom program may apply\. In the case of BUCS, 64 credits must be completed while in the BCom program\.    

## Added Credit Summer Session

If you completed coursework in another UBC faculty prior to transferring from a post\-secondary institution into year 2 or 3 of the BCom program, you must notify the Office of Student Academic Services \(OSAS\) if you completed additional coursework at a post\-secondary institution in the summer prior to beginning your studies in the BCom program\. Consider the following example scenario\. A student completed one year in UBC Faculty of Arts \(Sept\-Apr\), one year at a post\-secondary institution \(Sept\-Apr\), and completed additional coursework at a post\-secondary institution in the summer \(May\-Aug\)\. The student must notify OSAS they earned additional credit\. Failure to notify OSAS of additional credit earned may lead to the inability to graduate due to insufficient credits completed in the program\.
