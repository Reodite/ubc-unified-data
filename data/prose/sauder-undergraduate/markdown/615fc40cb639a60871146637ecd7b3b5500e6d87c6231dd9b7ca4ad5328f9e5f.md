An award is offered by KPMG in memory of David McShane, office managing partner for the greater Vancouver practice, to an undergraduate student completing their second year in the Sauder School of Business\. In adjudicating the award, academic achievement, community involvement, and the ability to serve, work and lead others are considered\. The award is made on the recommendation of the Sauder School of Business\.
