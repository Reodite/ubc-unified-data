### Thinking about studying at the UBC Sauder School of Business on exchange? We welcome exchange students from around the world\. 

### The UBC Sauder advantage

##### A rich student life

The UBC BCom program offers you a wealth of opportunities to make new friends, develop personally and professionally, and get involved\. See what [student life at UBC](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life) is like\.

##### Our dedicated Business Career Centre

Exchange students have access to the full scope of services at UBC Sauder's [Business Career Centre \(BCC\)](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/know-sauder/business-career-centre) including career coaching, recruitment events, and job postings\.

##### Your Commerce Undergraduate Society

The [Commerce Undergraduate Society \(CUS\)](http://cus.ca/) is the largest student\-run business school organization in Canada, both in terms of its over $1 million annual operating budget and its thousands of active members\. Get involved in the UBC Sauder student community by participating in CUS activities\.

### Eligibility

Check our list of business partner schools to find out if your university has a business school exchange with us\. If not, you can still apply to come on exchange with the business school as long as your university has an exchange agreement with UBC\.

Explore this website and find out what our program has to offer\. Then review the UBC Go Global website for everything you need to know to make your exchange a reality\.

[UBC Go Global](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/eligibility)

### Frequently asked questions about coming to the UBC Sauder on exchange

How do I apply for exchange at UBC?

Exchange at the UBC Sauder School of Business is administered through UBC Go Global\. The UBC Go Global [website](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc) includes all the information you need about coming to UBC\.

Do I have the appropriate academic background to apply to the business school?

To apply, you need to be at the academic level required for third and fourth year BCom courses\. See [studying commerce at UBC on exchange](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange/step-1-planning-eligibility/studying-commerce-ubc-exchange) for details\.

Where can I find the course schedule?

Check the Vancouver campus [course schedule](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/) for courses offered in the semester you plan to come on exchange\. Business courses are labeled COMM\_V, COHR\_V and COMR\_V\.

What courses can I take?

Exchange students can take business courses along with a wide variety of courses offered in other disciplines at UBC\. It is your responsibility to check for prerequisites to ensure you have the appropriate academic background to be successful in your courses\.

Are there any business courses I can’t take?

See [studying commerce at UBC on exchange](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange/step-1-planning-eligibility/studying-commerce-ubc-exchange) for a list of courses not available\.

Are course outlines online?

You can find brief course descriptions in the [UBC Calendar](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/courses.cfm) and more detailed descriptions on our [website](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-and-enrolment/courses-and-registration/registration-assistancehttps:/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-and-enrolment/courses-and-registration/registration-assistance)\. Course syllabi will be available at the start of the semester via the course website, Canvas\. If you need a syllabus in advance of the course start for transfer credit purposes you can request a recent copy by emailing [bcomquestions@sauder\.ubc\.ca](mailto:bcomquestions@sauder.ubc.ca)\.

How many courses can I take?

A typical course load per semester is 12 to 15 credits, or 4 to 5 courses\. Consider combining business courses with courses in other disciplines as space in business courses may be limited\. Students on exchange with business will have priority in our courses\. Students on exchange through another program may request to take business courses\.

Can I take courses if I’m not on exchange with the business school?

Students on exchange through another program may request to take business courses\. While other courses may be available, COMR\_V 100, COMR\_V 329, COMR 398\_V, COMR 458\_V, COMR 465\_V, COMR\_V 473, and COMR\_V 493 are typically taken by students in non\-business majors\.

How can I make changes to my registered courses?

To request changes to your registration including course add, course switch and course section switch please submit a registration assistance form [here](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-and-enrolment/courses-and-registration/registration-assistancehttps:/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-and-enrolment/courses-and-registration/registration-assistance)\.  You can drop courses yourself via [Workday Student](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/dropping-a-course/)\.
