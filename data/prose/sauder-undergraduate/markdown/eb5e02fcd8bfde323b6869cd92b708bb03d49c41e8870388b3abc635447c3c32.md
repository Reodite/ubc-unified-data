This course examines advanced financial accounting including inter\-corporate investments, transactions and balances in foreign currencies, and accounting principles applicable to not\-for\-profit organizations\. The course will prepare the student for the professional practice of accounting\.

### Learning objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

-   Prepare journal entries for investments in equity securities with varying levels of influence
-   Prepare consolidated financial statements for Canadian parent companies and their domestic and foreign subsidiaries
-   Account for transactions denominated in foreign currencies including hedged transactions
-   Preparation journal entries and financial statements for not\-for\-profit organizations\.

Prerequisite: All of [COMM 353](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses/comm-353), [COMM 450](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses/comm-450)\.
