An award is offered by Deans Knight Capital Management Ltd\. for a student entering third year in the Faculty of Commerce and Business Administration in the Portfolio Management Foundation program who has demonstrated leadership ability\. The award is made on the recommendation of the Faculty in consultation with the UBC Portfolio Management Foundation and is renewable for a second year\.
