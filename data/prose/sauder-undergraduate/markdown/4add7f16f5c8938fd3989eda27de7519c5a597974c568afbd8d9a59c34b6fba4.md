### Office of Student Academic Services \(OSAS\) Virtual Office

-   Please use your CWL to login to the Office of Student Academic Services \(OSAS\) Virtual Office\.
-   Please let one of our staff know if you have a time\-sensitive or urgent inquiry and do not have an appointment\.
-   If you are joining the Virtual Office for a scheduled advising appointment, please wait for the Academic Advisor  to move you to their private breakout room \(“office”\) for your meeting\.

Ensure that the name on your Zoom ID matches your name as it appears on Workday Student\. Do NOT put your student number on your Zoom ID\.

***AI tools which record, transcribe or take notes during meetings are not compliant with privacy laws at this time and are therefore NOT permitted during advising sessions\.***

[Access virtual office](https://site.sauder.ubc.ca/osas-virtual-office/)  

If you do not have a CWL and need to connect with the Office of Student Academic Services \(OSAS\), please contact us in person at HA 165, by phone at 604\.822\.8333 or by email at [bcomquestions@sauder\.ubc\.ca](mailto:bcomquestions@sauder.ubc.ca)\.

#### Prospective Students

If you are a prospective student—whether you are currently in high school, studying at a different post\-secondary institution, or in a different faculty at UBC—you are welcome to connect with our Recruitment &amp; Admissions team:

-   **Email us** at [bcom@sauder\.ubc\.ca](mailto:bcom@sauder.ubc.ca)\.
-   **Book an appointment** or **register for an event** [here](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/programs/bachelors-degrees/bachelor-commerce/need-more-info)\.
-   To learn more about BCom program admissions, visit the [UBC Sauder Program Admissions webpage](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/programs/bachelors-degrees/bachelor-commerce/program-admission)\.

If you are on campus \(either as a student or visiting for the day\), you are welcome to drop by the Henry Angus Building \(Room HA 137\) for drop\-in admissions advising every **Thursday from 12 \- 2 pm\.**  
*Please note that drop\-in advising check\-in occurs with the Robert H\. Lee Graduate School and not the Office of Student Academic Services\.*
