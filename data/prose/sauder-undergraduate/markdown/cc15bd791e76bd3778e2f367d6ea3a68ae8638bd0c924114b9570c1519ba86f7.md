An award is offered by Laura Mackenrot to an undergraduate student at the Sauder School of Business who has a mobility impairment or physical disability, and/or is experiencing chronic pain\. Preference will be given to a student who has demonstrated leadership and service in the UBC campus community and/or the wider community\. This award is made on the recommendation of the Sauder School of Business\.
