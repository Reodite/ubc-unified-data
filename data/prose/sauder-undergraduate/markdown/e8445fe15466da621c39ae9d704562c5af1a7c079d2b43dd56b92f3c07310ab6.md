Do you want to help welcome new students to the UBC Sauder community? We're hiring students for Imagine Day and The Spark 2026\!

### We're looking for individuals who:

-   embody the UBC Sauder Spirit
-   are passionate about making sure every new student feels welcome in the UBC Sauder community
-   exemplify UBC Sauder’s core values of rigour, respect, and responsibility
-   know how to have responsible fun with a good sense of judgement
-   love being a UBC Sauder student\!

### What orientation programs are happening?

We are planning to deliver a fun orientation experience\. Help welcome 600\+ new\-to\-Sauder students this orientations through Imagine Day and The Spark\!

#### [Jump Start \(September 2 \- 4, 2026\)](https://you.ubc.ca/jump-start-vancouver)

-   More information can be found on: [you\.ubc\.ca/jump\-start\-vancouver](https://you.ubc.ca/jump-start-vancouver/)
-   For all newly admitted first\-year students
-   Students will participate in group components to help them make friends and feel prepared to start their academic journey at UBC

#### [Imagine UBC \(Tuesday, September 8, 2026\)](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/orientations/imagine-ubc)

-   For all newly admitted first\-year and transfer undergraduate students
-   Students will meet a faculty member and other new UBC Sauder students, be officially welcomed by the Dean, participate in a pep rally, and learn about involvement opportunities across campus
-   Applications have closed for Senior Orientations Leaders  
    
-   Hiring open for Orientation Leaders\! Applications Close May 3

#### [The Spark \(Friday, September 11, 2026 and Saturday, September 12, 2026\)](https://www.ubcsauderspark.com/)

-   For all new\-to\-UBC students which includes students admitted to Year 1 and transfer students
-   We are planning for an in\-person experience in which students will be in groups with others in their cohort, participating in fun games and small group activities\.
-   Check out [The Spark 2025 Recap Video](https://youtu.be/aeb_HzxrZlU?si=WHqX50jv28OFNF0y)
-   The Spark Hiring Timeline 

-   More hiring information about The Spark can be found [here](https://www.ubcsauderspark.com/) 
-   Applications have closed for Spark Exec Team[https://www\.ubcsauderspark\.com/spark\-exec\-hiring\-2026](https://www.ubcsauderspark.com/spark-exec-hiring-2026)
-   Hiring open for Spirit Leaders\! Applications Close May 3
-   [Hiring open for Spark Volunteers\!](https://www.ubcsauderspark.com/volunteers) Applications Close in August

Do I need to be an extrovert to be an OL or Spark Volunteer?

Absolutely not\. We understand that orientations can seem like a loud, outgoing event, and admittedly, there is cheering\. That being said, we have many different types of students coming to UBC Sauder, and many types of Orientation Leaders\. We want orientation to be welcoming to all\! We are looking to hire a team that is balanced and representative of the UBC Sauder community, with all energy levels represented\.

How are leader hiring decisions made?

Applications will be reviewed against the job description, the goals of Imagine UBC, and UBC Sauder values\. Participants will be invited to a group interview, where our team will assess candidates around scenarios and general fit for the role

What if I'm already a Jumpstart Leader?

Starting 2025, Jumpstart Leaders can also be UBC Sauder Orientation Leaders\!
