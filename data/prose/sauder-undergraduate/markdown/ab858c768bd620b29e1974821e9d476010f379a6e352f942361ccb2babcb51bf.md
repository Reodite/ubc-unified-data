Awards have been made available annually through a gift from OUTtv for Bachelor of Commerce students who identify as 2SLGBTQIA\+ and have demonstrated an interest in community service and leadership qualities\. OUTtv is the world’s first 2SLGBTQIA\+ television network\. They are headquartered in Vancouver, BC, and since launching in Canada in 2001 now distribute their programming in the United States, the United Kingdom, Ireland, South Africa, New Zealand, and Australia\.
