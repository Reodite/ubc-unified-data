## Registration dates

[Registration for the summer session](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/registration-dates#summer-session-registration) will open in March\.

You will receive an email from Enrolment Services in mid\-February directing you to check the Workday Student for the exact date and time when your registration will open \(i\.e\., your registration appointment\)\.

[Refund of tuition fees](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/tuition-fees/refund-tuition-fees)  

## Plan your courses wisely

If you're considering enrolling in summer courses, be mindful that with the shorter summer terms—typically six weeks followed by a condensed final exam period—keeping up with your coursework and managing your study time effectively will be essential to your academic success\.

Taking two summer courses during a single term requires the same amount of time and effort as taking a full five\-course load during a regular winter term to achieve comparable results\. Therefore, we strongly recommend that you limit your summer registration to a maximum of two courses per term to ensure your continued success in the program\.

### Term 2 courses with Term 1 prerequisites 

Workday cannot detect that you are taking a prerequisite in Summer Term 1 for a Term 2 course\. If you plan to take a Term Commerce 2 course that requires a Term 1 prerequisite, complete the [Registration Assistance](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses-and-registration/registration-assistance "https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/dates-deadlines/") form before your registration appointment\. We will grant an override allowing you to register for the Term 2 course \(subject to seat availability\)\. Note that we audit registration; if you do not register for the Term 1 prerequisite or subsequently drop it, you will be removed from the Term 2 course without notification\.  

It is your responsibility to confirm your enrolment in Workday, pay all necessary fees, and make any changes to your registration with awareness of [deadlines](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/dates-deadlines/ "https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/dates-deadlines/") to: 

-   elect Credit/D/Fail grading for eligible courses 
-   withdraw from courses, with or without ‘W’ standing 

## Frequently asked questions about taking summer courses

I'm in first year\. Will I be able to register in second year Commerce courses?

Yes\! For summer registration, seats are reserved with the expectation that students will have achieved the appropriate class standing for eligibility after completing their Term 2 winter session courses in May\.

What should I do if I can't register in a course that I want?

Waitlist for the course if there is a waitlist and there are still seats available on it\.

Commerce courses do not have waitlists\. Continue to monitor enrolment so that you can register yourself should a seat become available before the add/drop deadline\. There is often a lot of movement in course enrolment at the start of term\.

If you are graduating in November and need assistance to register in a required Commerce course, please submit a request for registration assistance\.

Do grades in summer courses matter?

Yes\! While grades for summer courses are not included in the calculation of your winter session average, your summer grades will be included in your cumulative average which is used to determine your registration appointment for subsequent sessions\.

Additionally, any senior\-level Commerce courses \(300 or 400\-level\)—and Computer Science courses if you are in the BUCS program—will be included in the calculation of your graduating average to determine eligibility for Honours standing\.

Furthermore, if your future plans include additional studies, some graduate programs, depending on the institution, may consider all undergraduate grades as the basis of admission\.

I'm taking summer courses to finish requirements in my current year\. Will I be assessed for promotion once my grades have been posted?

Yes, your year level \(i\.e\., class standing\) will be updated automatically as soon as you have completed all of the necessary requirements for promotion\. 

Eligibility for the [U\-Pass](http://www.upass.ubc.ca/) is determined by your continued registration\. During the summer session, you will be charged the U\-Pass fee on a monthly basis\. For example, if you take a Term 2 course scheduled from July to August, you will be eligible and subsequently charged for a July and August U\-Pass\.

### Making travel plans

Your exam schedule will be available midway through the term of study\. If you are making advance travel plans before the exam schedule is published, assume that you may be writing your last exam on the last possible date and time during the exam week\. The Office of Student Academic Services will not grant concessions due to conflicting travel arrangements\.
