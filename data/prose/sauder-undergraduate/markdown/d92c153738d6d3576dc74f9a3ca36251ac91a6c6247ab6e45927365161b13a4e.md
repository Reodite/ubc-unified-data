This course takes an empirical approach to economics, using data to analyze real estate markets and urban issues\. Students develop end\-to\-end data analysis skills — acquiring and cleaning large datasets such as Census microdata and Vancouver housing transactions, transforming data, building visualizations, and producing spatial maps — through hands\-on application of statistical methods, R programming, and AI tools\.

The curriculum combines structured training with individual and group research projects, guiding students from raw data to polished, professional presentations of empirical findings\. As R is the primary tool for analysis and visualization, exams will emphasize R coding proficiency\.

Prerequisite: One of [COMM 295](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses/comm-295), ECON 201, ECON 301\.
