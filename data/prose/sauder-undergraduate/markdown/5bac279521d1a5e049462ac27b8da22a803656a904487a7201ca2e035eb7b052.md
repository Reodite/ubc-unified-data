An award has been made available annually through a gift from the Association of Women in Finance for an outstanding woman entering the Bachelor of Commerce program\. Preference will be given to a student who demonstrates an interest in finance, community involvement and/or barriers to entering post\-secondary education\. Led by a group of professionals, the Association of Women in Finance is a non\-profit organization devoted to supporting the advancement of women in finance\.
