Awards have been made available through an endowment established by Azzam Jivraj, in memory of his grandfathers Sultan Lalji and Haiderali Jivraj, for outstanding Bachelor of Commerce students who demonstrate financial need\. Preference will be given to students who demonstrate an interest in entrepreneurship\. Sultan and Haiderali built businesses that created a lasting impact across East Africa, inspiring future generations to follow in their footsteps\. Alongside their pursuit of business excellence, their generosity and enduring commitment to giving back inspired the creation of this award\. It is the hope of the donor that recipients of this award will carry forward that same spirit of entrepreneurship, excellence and generosity\.
