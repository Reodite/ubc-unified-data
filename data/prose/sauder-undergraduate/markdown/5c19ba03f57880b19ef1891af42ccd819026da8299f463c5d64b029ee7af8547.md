A scholarship is offered by Aaron Carter for a student entering the third or fourth year of the Bachelor of Commerce Program at the Sauder School of Business in the Portfolio Management Foundation program\. This scholarship is made on the recommendation of the Sauder School of Business in consultation with the UBC Portfolio Management Foundation\. Preference will be given to a student with demonstrated financial need\.

> **Scholarship Type**
> 
> Sauder\-specific scholarships
> 
> **Basis of Selection**
> 
> Assigned by merit
> 
> **Criteria**
> 
> financial need
> 
> portfolio management foundation
