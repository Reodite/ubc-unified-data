### What's it like to work in finance?

The field of **[Finance](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/options/finance)** incompasses a broad set of activities, including the following:

-   Capital markets and investment management
-   Corporate finance
-   Banking and credit
-   Private equity and venture capital
-   Financial advisory
-   Compliance and risk management
-   International finance
-   FinTech, crypto, and DeFi
-   Climate, impact, and socially responsible investing

Finance professionals participate in investing, borrowing, lending, budgeting, saving, and forecasting\. Finance also offers opportunities to practice managerial and development skills with the oversight, creation, and study of money, banking, credit, investments, assets, and liabilities that make up financial systems\.

### CFA University Affiliation Program Partner

The UBC Sauder School of Business is a **Chartered Financial Analyst \(CFA\) University Affiliation Program Partner**, an internationally recognized designation certifying that the UBC Sauder BCom meets the CFA Program Candidate Body of Knowledge\.

As a CFA Program Partner, UBC Sauder gives its students the edge they need in pursuing this professional designation and careers in positions as financial analysts, portfolio managers, and investment advisors\. To learn more and for questions, visit the **[CFA Institute](https://www.cfainstitute.org/en/programs/cfa/university-students)**\.

### UBC SWIFT: Sauder Women in Finance Training

**[SWIFT](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/swift)** is an extra\-curricular program for students pursuing a BCom degree at UBC Sauder\. The objective of the program is to provide top\-quality training in finance to a diverse pipeline of talent\. We will supply excellence, equity, diversity, and inclusion to employers and students who share our vision for responsible leadership\. 

Through connections between academics and practitioners in finance, students reconcile practical portfolio management with rigorous academic theory\. 

### Portfolio Management Foundation

The **[Portfolio Management Foundation \(PMF\)](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/thought-leadership/divisions/finance/pmf-program)** provides a small group of select BCom students with an experience that will provide world\-class training in the **global capital markets**\.

Our students learn from the best investment professionals within and outside of Canada to prepare for careers in investment banking, research, sales and trading, and money management\.  PMF graduates stay actively involved in the program as mentors and supporters\.
