### Am I the right fit for this option?

Completing the Finance Option will provide you with the professional competencies required to enter financial management positions in business enterprises, investment management and services organizations, banking, financial institutions, or government agencies concerned with private finance\.

Key Areas of Study and Career Opportunities in Finance:

-   Money Management
-   Investment Banking
-   Commercial Banking
-   Corporate Finance
-   Central Banking
-   Venture Capital
-   Private Equity
-   Private Debt
-   FinTech
-   Big Data / Asset Digitalization / AI Integration
-   Climate Finance / Impact Investing / Sustainable Finance
-   Research and Regulation
-   Financial Forensics and Compliance

Because the program is competitive, you should be able to demonstrate the ability to do well in your quantitative core courses\. To self\-declare the Finance Option, you must achieve a minimum grade of 67% in each of COMM\_V 190, COMM\_V 191, COMM\_V 293, COMM\_V 294, COMM\_V 295, and COMM\_V 298\.

### Early Start to the Finance Option

The Early Start to the Finance option allows students interested in a career in finance to take finance courses earlier\. This is designed to better prepare students for competitive finance internships\.
