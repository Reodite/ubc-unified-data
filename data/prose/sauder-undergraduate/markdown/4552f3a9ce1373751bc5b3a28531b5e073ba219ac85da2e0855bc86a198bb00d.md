Two prizes are awarded to the top students competing in the public speaking contest conducted by the Sauder School of Business\. The contest is open to BCom students who have completed the business communications course offered by the Faculty in the previous 12 months\. The prizes are made on the recommendation of the Sauder School of Business\.
