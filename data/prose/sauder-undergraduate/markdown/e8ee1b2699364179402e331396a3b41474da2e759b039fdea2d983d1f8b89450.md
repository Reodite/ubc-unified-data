Awards have been made available annually through a gift from Clio, for outstanding first\-year Bachelor of Commerce students who are First Nations, Inuit or Métis of Canada, and/or who identify as Black and/or as a Person of Colour\. Students must demonstrate leadership in their community and involvement in social purpose, impact, and/or access to justice community efforts\. Financial need may be considered\. Clio is a legal technology company headquartered in Burnaby, BC\. The company is powering the future of legal services for lawyers and their clients through a suite of cloud\-based solutions, including legal practice management, client intake and CRM, online payments, and client\-attorney communication and collaboration software\.
