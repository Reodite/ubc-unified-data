### Your home for career support at UBC Sauder\!

The **UBC Sauder Career Hub** is your exclusive career platform from the Business Career Centre \(BCC\)\. Explore opportunities, build your skills, and access support and resources to help you navigate your career journey, all in one place\. 

[Access Career Hub](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/) 

\(Log in with your *CWL\)*

[Career Hub Homepage Image](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/ubc_sauder_career_hub_home_page.jpg?itok=IbW17NGI)

### What you can do:

-   **Find jobs:** Browse and apply for internships and full\-time opportunities  
-   **Book career coaching &amp; advising appointments:** Meet one\-on\-one Career Strategists and Peer Career Advisors  
-   **Register for events:** Sign up for employer recruiting sessions and career workshops  
-   **Access career resources:**  Explore Resume and Cover Letter Toolkits, industry guides, Management Consulted, Career Masterclass and more\!  
    
-   **Explore hiring data:** Use Job IQ Explorer to find UBC Sauder hiring data and trends 

### Get started 

1.  Complete your profile to personalize your experience 
2.  Download the **Career Hub** **Guide** for tips on using the system

[Access Career Hub Guide](https://bit.ly/careerhubguide) 

### Book a coaching and advising appointment  

1.  Log in to the **UBC Sauder Career Hub** 
2.  Select **Appointments** from the left navigation menu 
3.  Use the filters to narrow appointments by location, Career Adviser\*, or appointment type 
4.  Select an available time under a Career Adviser's name, then **choose **View** Available Slots** 
5.  Enter and review your appointment details and select **Book Appointment** 

\*Career Strategists are labelled Career Advisors in the Career Hub 

[Book an appointment](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/appointments/)  

##### *\(CWL login required\)*
