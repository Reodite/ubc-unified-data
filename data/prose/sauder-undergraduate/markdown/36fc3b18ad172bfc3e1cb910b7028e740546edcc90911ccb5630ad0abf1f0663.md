Recognizing that starting up a new venture can be financially challenging, Crystal Hung, PREC, B\.Com\. ’09, offers an award to support a student in the Sauder School of Business with entrepreneurial aspirations\. The successful student will either be enrolled in the entrepreneurship specialization of the B\.Com\., New Venture Design course or have participated in entrepreneurship@UBC programming\. Preference will be give to a student who demonstrates financial need\.
