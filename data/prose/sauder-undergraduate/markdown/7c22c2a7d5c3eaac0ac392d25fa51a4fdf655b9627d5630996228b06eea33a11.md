Awards are offered to support the participation of Sauder School of Business undergraduate students at case competitions\. The awards are given on the recommendation of the Accounting Division at the Sauder School of Business to undergraduate students who have been selected to participate in case competitions\. The award is given in memory of Professor Emeritus Arthur “Dick” Beedle, FCA and his over 25 years as an inspiring colleague and teacher at the Sauder School of Business\.

> 
> Sauder\-specific scholarships
> 
> **Basis of Selection**
>
