Prizes have been made available annually through a gift from Greater Vancouver REALTORS, in honour of Professor Davis' many contributions to the Faculty of Commerce and Business Administration and to the real estate industry of this Province \. The prizes are awarded to fourth year students in the Real Estate Option in the Bachelor of Commerce program with the highest standing in Commerce 434 \(Land Law\)\. The award is made on the recommendation of the UBC Sauder School of Business\.
