Scholarships have been made available annually through a gift from Greater Vancouver REALTORS, in memory of Douglas P\. Woodley and as a tribute to his warm personal qualities and outstanding public service in the field of real estate for outstanding students entering their third year of the Bachelor of Commerce program who demonstrate financial need\. Preference will be given to students indicating an interest in specializing in Real Estate\. The awards are made on the recommendation of the UBC Sauder School of Business\.
