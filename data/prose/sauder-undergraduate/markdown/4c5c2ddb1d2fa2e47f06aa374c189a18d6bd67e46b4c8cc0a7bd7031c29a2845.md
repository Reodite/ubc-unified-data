Awards have been made available annually through a gift from Oakwyn Realty for women in the Bachelor of Commerce program who demonstrate an interest in real estate and leadership through community service, volunteerism and/or extra\-curricular activities\. Ordinarily, each award will be valued at $1,000\. This award marks 10 years of Oakwyn Realty's Women of Oakwyn \(WOO\) program, which celebrates the impact that women have made throughout the organization\.
