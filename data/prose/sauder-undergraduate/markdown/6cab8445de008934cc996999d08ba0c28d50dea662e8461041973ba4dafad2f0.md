A prize is offered by the Vancouver Chapter of Lambda Alpha International \(an honorary land economics society\) to a student who excels in Urban and Real Estate Economics\. The prize alternates between being given to a student in the Sauder School of Business in even numbered years and the School of Community and Regional Planning in odd numbered years\.
