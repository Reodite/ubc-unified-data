### Preparation is key\! Get interview\-ready 

[Students preparing for interviews](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/level_2_-_interview_prep_resources.jpg?itok=VodlrY3O)

Job interviews can be both exciting and nerve\-wracking\. **Practicing and preparing is the best way to boost your confidence** and land the job you want\.

Interviews are your chance to shine and to tell your unique story in more detail\. Do this by clearly showcasing your

-   Skills
-   Interests
-   What you can bring to the role and organization

Doing your homework before every interview to understand the role, organization, and industry will help you feel more confident, stand out, and land the position you want\.
