## **Co\-op Resources**

How does the Co\-op program support you throughout your work terms?

While the Co\-op program doesn't match you with an employer, it provides valuable resources to help you in your journey\.

[Image](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/screenshot_2024-04-09_094432.png?itok=PGPqh48T)

[Personalized Coaching](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/52469824552_be2a1fbadc_k.jpg?itok=rBr78k1E&amp;c=7934185c0cc68cf336a5c2361327f52a)

#### As a Co\-op student, you will have ongoing access to book 1:1 coaching appointments with your [Co\-op Manager](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/career-coaching/co-op-managers)\.

#### These sessions are tailored to your specific needs, whether you're looking for support you with job search preparation, decision making, or support in maximizing the learning and growth opportunities during your work terms\.
