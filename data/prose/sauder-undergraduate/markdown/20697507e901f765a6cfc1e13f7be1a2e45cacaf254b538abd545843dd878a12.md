### Learn how to make resumes that will impress employers 

[Student working on resume on computer](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/level_2_-_resume_resources.png?itok=ufq_gA9Y)

Did you know recruiters spend an **average of 6 seconds** looking at a resume? This is why it’s essential to create a professional, easy\-to\-read, well\-formatted resume that gives you a chance to make a solid first impression\.

Also, increase your chances of standing out from the crowd by ensuring that **EVERY** **resume you submit is tailored** to the:

-   Role
-   Industry
-   Organization

How do you put your best resume forward? **The Hari B\. Varshney Business Career Centre \(BCC\) has you covered**\. Check out the below resources to help you tackle resumes\.
