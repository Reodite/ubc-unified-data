### The Hari B\. Varshney Business Career Centre \(BCC\) is here to support every step of your career journey

[3 students in front of the BCC](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/about-bcc.jpg?itok=jwSAQX6S)

The Business Career Centre \(BCC\) plays a crucial role in your UBC Sauder experience\. We aim to equip you with the tools and knowledge to **gain work experience** and **find fulfilling careers after graduation**\.

Through one\-on\-one coaching with our experienced **[BCom Career Strategists](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/career-coaching/bcom-career-strategists)**, **[Co\-op Career Strategists](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/career-coaching/co-op-managers)**, and **[Peer Career Advisors](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/career-coaching/career-peer-advisors)**, along with tailored events and programs focused on your career development, you have opportunities to **explore, discover, and realize your career aspirations**\.

Plus, we leverage UBC Sauder's extensive network to connect you with the business community\. To help you make connections we facilitate:

-   **[Industry events &amp; workshops](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/industry-events-%26-workshops)**: Company recruiting sessions, hiring events, networking events, and more
-   Information interviews and mock interviews: check out our **[interview prep resources](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/job-interviews/interview-prep-resources)**  
    [https://mybcom\.sauder\.ubc\.ca/file/1467/download](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/file/1467/download)
-   **[Mentorship &amp; specialty programs](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/mentorship-specialty-programs)**: Brand Management, Finance Mentorship, and Capital Markets Training, to name a few[https://mybcom\.sauder\.ubc\.ca/file/2663/download](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/file/2663/download)

Additionally, each year, we post thousands of internship and full\-time job opportunities on the **[UBC Sauder Career Hub](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/career-resources/cool-career-platform)** that span diverse industries and roles\.

While we provide these opportunities, it's up to you to take action\. The BCC's career team is here to support you every step of the way\.

### Career services designed for UBC Sauder students

We provide services and resources to help you clarify career goals, explore career options, develop job search skills, and find job opportunities through the following:

- **Row 1**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **Career development resources**: self\-serve resources available 24/7 on **[UBC Sauder Career Hub](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/resource-library/)** to help you with resumes, cover letters, career research, interviews, and more

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    In\-person and virtual one\-on\-one **[career coaching &amp; advising](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/career-coaching)** appointments \(book on **[UBC Sauder Career Hub](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/appointments)**\)

- **Row 3**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    Drop\-in **[Peer Career Advising](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/career-coaching/career-peer-advisors)**  
    
    -   **When**: 11:00 am to 2:00 pm \(Monday\-Friday\), during the academic term
    -   **Where**: CPA Hall near the BCC \(HA 155\)

- **Row 4**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **[UBC Sauder Co\-op program](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/co-op-program)**: opportunities to apply your classroom learning with three 4\-month work terms in your chosen field

- **Row 5**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **[COMM 202 \- Career Fundamentals](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/comm202)**: learn about professional cover letter and resume writing, professional employment, networking, and information interviews

- **Row 6**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **[Industry events &amp; workshops](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/industry-events-%26-workshops)**: Company recruiting sessions \(CRS\), Beyond Vancouver Panel Series, Spring Hiring Fair, and more\. Register on **[UBC Sauder Career Hub](https://sauder-ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/app/events)**\.

- **Row 7**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **[Mentorship &amp; Specialty programs](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/mentorship-specialty-programs)**: learn directly from people with business experience

Learn more about [**UBC Sauder Career Hub**](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/appointments) and what it can do for you **[here](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/career-resources/cool-career-platform)**\.

### BCom Career Strategists &amp; Co\-op Career Strategists

The BCC offers a variety of coaching and advising appointments you can book throughout the year \(even in the summer\!\) to get personalized, 1:1 support\. Your **[BCom Career Strategists](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/career-coaching/bcom-career-strategists)** and **[Co\-op Career Strategists](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/career-coaching/co-op-managers)** \(for those enrolled in the Co\-op program\) will work alongside you throughout your degree to help you take action and thrive in your personal and professional life\.

Not sure what coaching &amp; advising is? Learn more **[here](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-experience/career-coaching)**\.

### Connections with Business Communities

We also help students **connect with business communities**, **explore job markets**, and **find job opportunities** in Vancouver and worldwide\.

Our eight\-person **team of industry\-focused Business Development professionals** actively bridges the gap between our students and the business world\. We connect students with hundreds of potential employers, from global industry leaders to local growing startups\.
