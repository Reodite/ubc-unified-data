### Ace your case interviews

[Two person interview](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/photo.jpg?itok=eVxaOviY&amp;c=8179543497e14b31ef9feb6c2955af54)

Case interviews test a candidate's **analytical abilities** and **human skills** within **real\-life business scenarios**\.

This interview format is used in a variety of industries, including:

-   Consulting
-   Healthcare
-   Technology
-   Retail consumer products
-   Banking
-   Finance, to name a few

### Ways to get started 

#### Management Consulted 

[Image](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/2024_mc_icon_logo_1.png?itok=hzKzbVNw)

**[Management Consulted](https://managementconsulted.com/)** is the world’s leading resource for consulting and case interview prep, built on 15\+ years of consulting recruitment experience\. Designed by former consultants and recruiters, it offers a structured, step\-by\-step approach and equips you with the tools you need to: 

1.  Land a consulting interview 
2.  Prepare for a case interview  
3.  Sharpen your skills to thrive in a consulting role in any industry  
4.  Build your knowledge of consulting and the industry's recruitment process 

**As a UBC Sauder Student, you get FREE\* access to:** 

**Pre\-Interview Support:**  

-   Industry\-tailored resume and cover letter templates 
-   12 industry overviews &amp; company insights  
-   Networking strategies for consulting roles 

**Case Interview Prep:**  

-   600\+ practice cases with solutions &amp; 27 chatbot\-style cases 
-   Mock interview tool with scoring rubric to practice with peers 
-   Live case workshops on case structures, math, industry insights and more  

**Skill Building:** 

-   9 video courses on case interviews, mental math, behavioural interviews, Excel, PowerPoint \(PPT\) &amp; consulting skills
-   25K\+ interactive case drills to sharpen your skills with math, logic, market sizing, exhibits, and frameworks
-   Case theory training with expert\-led video &amp; structured prep

[Access here](https://managementconsulted.com/ubc-student-login/) 

\(CWL login required\) 

**\*Please note****:** Management Consulted also offers premium, paid services to individual clients\. As a UBC Sauder student, you already have full access to practice cases, drills, industry drills, and other services\. Extra services, such as individual coaching, are completely optional and would be an additional cost to you if you choose to use them\.
