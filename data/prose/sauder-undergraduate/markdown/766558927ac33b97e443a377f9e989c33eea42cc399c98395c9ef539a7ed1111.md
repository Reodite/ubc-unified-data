A scholarship has been endowed by family and friends of Ken Young, who joined the University as Assistant Registrar in 1965\. He was appointed as Registrar and Secretary to Senate in 1980, and held these positions until he passed away in 1987 at 48 years of age\. He obtained his B\.A\. and B\.Com\. degrees from the University of Alberta\. The scholarship is awarded to a student in the Sauder School of Business, on the recommendation of the school\.
