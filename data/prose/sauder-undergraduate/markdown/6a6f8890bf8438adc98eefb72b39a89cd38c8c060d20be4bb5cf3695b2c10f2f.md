This course focuses on logistics services management \- that is, the broad constellation of activities that are involved in delivering goods to customers\.

### Learning objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will have the knowledge and skills to:

-   describe the core decisions which apply to logistics services management\.
-   apply tools and principles that will help you make good decisions\.
-   describe key challenges that logistics services providers fact, both in the day\-to\-day and longer term time horizons\.
-   take away at least one new idea that you will be able to use in your future career\.
-   understand the socio\-economic\-environmental aspects of B\.C\.

Corequisite: One of [COMM 204](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses/comm-204), [COMM 296](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses/comm-296)\.
