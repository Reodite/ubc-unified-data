An award is offered annually by the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors \(RICS\) to a third year student in the Real Estate option of the Bachelor of Commerce program\. Preference is given to students who have demonstrated a passion for excellence in the real estate profession\. It is also a requirement that the recipient be a student member of RICS\.
