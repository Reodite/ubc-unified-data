Managing and working in a family business presents unique challenges: family issues can conflict with business issues\. This course examines governance structures and tools to manage the interface between the family and the business\.

### Learning objectives

This course has four basic objectives:

-   to strengthen your awareness of the importance and competitive strengths of family businesses\.
-   to develop your understanding of the unique and complex challenges that both family businesses and business families’ face\.
-   to enhance your appreciation of the different priorities and perspectives of various stakeholders associated with family businesses\.
-   to build your knowledge of the managerial and advisory practices that can help improve the financial and relational prospects for family businesses\.

Prerequisite: [COMM 203](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses/comm-203)\.
