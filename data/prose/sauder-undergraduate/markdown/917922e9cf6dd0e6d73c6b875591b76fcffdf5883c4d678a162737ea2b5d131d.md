### Get peer support around COMM 196 presentations

The Canaccord Learning Commons \(CLC\) and COMM 196 have partnered to offer coaching to help you with COMM 196 presentation projects\. Peer coaches will listen to your presentation, look over slides, and discuss content\. You can sign up for presentation coaching for COMM 196 through the **[CLC sign\-up page on Jane](https://canaccordlearningcommons.janeapp.com/)**\.
