### Leverage LinkedIn to grow your network &amp; connect with opportunities

[LinkedIn](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/linkedin_photo.jpg?itok=74m54Qmn)

Did you know that **90% of Fortune 100 companies use LinkedIn** to find future hires?

LinkedIn is the world's largest professional network, with hundreds of millions of members\. Creating and developing a LinkedIn profile is valuable as it will enable you to:

-   **Expand your professional network** and become part of a supportive online community
-   Gain **industry insights**
-   **Research** companies and explore different career paths
-   Get connected with **career opportunities** that interest you
