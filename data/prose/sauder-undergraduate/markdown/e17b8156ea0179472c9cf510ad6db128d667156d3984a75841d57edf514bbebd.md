> ### UBC Student Alumni Council \(SAC\)
> 
> Every year, current students from each faculty are invited to apply to join the UBC Student Alumni Council, which aims to connect students with alumni and foster community pride across campus\. Members of the SAC act as student representatives that develop programs, collaborate with UBC's partners, and promote the alumni network on campus\. They work with and are supported by President Ono, as well as UBC's alumni association\.
> 
> [Learn more about SAC](https://alumni.ubc.ca/about/student-alumni-council/)
