PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP is offering a scholarship in memory of Donald McLeod Anderson\. This scholarship is awarded to a student who has completed, with high academic standing, his or her second year of study in the Sauder School of Business, and is entering the final year of the Accounting or Business Technology Management option\. The successful applicant will be one who, in the opinion of the school, most clearly shows consistent general academic achievement throughout his or her two years, but with special emphasis in accounting\.
