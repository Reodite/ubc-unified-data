Awards have been made available through an endowment established by the QuadReal Property Group, in honour of Susan MacLaurin, for second\-year Bachelor of Commerce students who, in their previous year of study, have demonstrated an exceptional commitment to inclusivity, collaboration and team spirit\. Susan joined QuadReal in 2016 as one of the company’s founding leaders\. As Chief Communications Officer, Susan has made a significant and lasting contribution to QuadReal’s culture of collaboration, teamwork and inclusivity\. She has been a role model and source of inspiration for QuadReal employees globally\.
