Scholarships totalling $10,000 have been made available annually through a gift from Gentai Financial Group for outstanding Bachelor of Commerce students in the Finance option in the UBC Sauder School of Business\. Each scholarship should ideally not be less than $2,000\. Gentai Financial Group is the umbrella company for Genesis Mortgage Investment Corporation, Gentai Capital Corporation, and Gentai Asset Management Corporation\. Gentai invests in mortgages across Canada with a long term focus to provide investors with capital protection and income generation\. The company is excited to build a meaningful relationship with the University by providing financial support for students who wish to pursue higher education\. The scholarships are made on the recommendation of the UBC Sauder School of Business\.
