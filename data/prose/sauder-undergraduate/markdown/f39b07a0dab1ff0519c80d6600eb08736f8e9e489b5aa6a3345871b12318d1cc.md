### Make connections and get career\-ready\!

[UBC Sauder student connecting at a networking event](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/sites/mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/media/level_1_-_networking_events.jpg?itok=OhKStWJa)

Throughout the year \(and even during the summer\!\), the Hari B\. Varshney Business Career Centre \(BCC\) organizes a range of **industry events**, **alumni panels**, and **networking events** to empower you to:

-   **Explore** potential **career paths**
-   **Learn directly from organizations** in various industries and locations
-   Sharpen your **professional** and **interpersonal skills**
-   **Make meaningful connections** and **gain invaluable insights** from peers, alumni, and the business community

To help you get the most out of these events and your time at UBC Sauder, the BCC provides all BComs free access to various tools to guide you on the **best practices for making and maintaining connections** throughout your career journey\. 

Check out our resources and put your learnings to practice at an industry or networking event\.
