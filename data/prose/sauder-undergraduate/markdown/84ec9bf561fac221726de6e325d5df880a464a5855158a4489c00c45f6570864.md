Scholarships are offered by Peter Lee to students entering the third or fourth year of the Finance option in the Bachelor of Commerce Program at the Sauder School of Business\. The awards are made on the recommendation of the school\.

Applications are now being accepted for one of four awards of $5,000 each under the Peter Lee Scholarships in Finance\. The recipients will be individuals with a strong interest in finance and a high degree of motivation for a career in the capital markets\.

Qualifications:             

-   3\[rd\] or 4\[th\]\-year Sauder School of Business undergraduate student in the Finance Option
-   Outstanding academic credentials
-   Evidence of leadership skills and the ability to work well in groups
-   Evidence of a desire for a career in the capital markets

Application Process: Applicants must submit one pdf file with three items in the order shown:

1.  A 1\-page cover letter stating why they deserve the scholarship
2.  A 1\-page resume
3.  A list of all university \(UBC or elsewhere\) grades \(this can be an o[fficial or an unofficial](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/student-documents/ordering-a-transcript/), but accurate, transcript\)

**Application Deadline: Noon, Friday, 18 October 2024** 

Submit applications by email to: Professor Rob Heinkel \| [Rob\.heinkel@sauder\.ubc\.ca](mailto:Rob.heinkel@sauder.ubc.ca)

Additional Qualifications: The Peter Lee Scholarship is a UBC\-recognized scholarship\. As such, it will appear on the scholarship recipients’ transcripts\.
