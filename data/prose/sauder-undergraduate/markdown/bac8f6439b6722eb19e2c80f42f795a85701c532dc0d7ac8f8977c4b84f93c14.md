### What's next? We're here to help you find the answers

Through the Hari B\. Varshney Business Career Centre \(BCC\), all UBC Sauder BCom students have access to a team of **dedicated and knowledgeable Career Strategists**\.

This team offers a variety of coaching and advising appointments you can book to **get personalized, 1:1 support**\.

Your Career Strategists will work alongside you throughout your degree to help you take action and thrive in both your personal and professional life\.

### Talk to a Career Strategist About:

-   **Job Application Review**: get guidance on your resume and cover letter, including tips on how to tailor your resume for specific roles and how to craft accomplishment statements
-   **Career Exploration/Navigation**: get support and learn tactics to find out the types of jobs, industries, and employers that align with your interests and goals
-   **Job Search Strategies**: find advice on where to get started and how to find meaningful work experience opportunities
-   **I'm Feeling Stuck**: share what's making you feel stuck, and we'll help you create a path forward
-   **Interviewing Prep &amp; Strategies**: practice answering questions and get tips for succes

Find a complete list of coaching &amp; advising appointment types on **[UBC Sauder Career Hub](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/appointments)**\.

### How do you book an appointment with a BCom Career Strategist?

To make a 1:1 appointment with a BCom Career Strategist, take the following steps:

1.  Log in to the UBC Sauder Career Hub using your CWL 
2.  Select **Appointments** from the left navigation menu 
3.  Filters appointments by **location, Career Advisor\*****,** or **appointment type** 
4.  Select an available time under a Career Advisor's name, then choose **View Available Slots** 
5.  Enter your appointment details and select either **virtual** or **in\-person** location type 
6.  Select Book Appointment \*Career Strategists are labelled Career Advisors in the Career Hub\. 

[Log in to Career Hub](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/appointments)
