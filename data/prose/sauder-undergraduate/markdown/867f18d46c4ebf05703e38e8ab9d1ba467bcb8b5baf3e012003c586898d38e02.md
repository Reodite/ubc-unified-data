Two awards have been made available annually through a gift from Concert Properties for First Nations, Inuit, or Métis students of Canada who are enrolled in the Bachelor of Commerce program\. Financial need may be considered\. Preference will be given to students who demonstrate involvement in their community\. Concert Properties is a real estate company that aims to improve the lives of people who live and work in their communities by building resilient, inclusive and sustainable communities across Canada\. Concert is proud to establish this award to support the next generation of Indigenous students in commerce\.
