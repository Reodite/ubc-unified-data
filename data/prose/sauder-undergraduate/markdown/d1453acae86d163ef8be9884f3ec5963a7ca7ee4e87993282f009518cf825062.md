First launched in 2012, KPMG’s Indigenous Student Awards program is administered by Indspire’s Building Brighter Futures: Bursaries, Scholarships and Awards program that is available to Indigenous post\-secondary students in the pursuit of university or college education across Canada\.

For additional information including eligibility, award criteria, application details, and deadline, please visit [https://kpmg\.com/ca/en/home/careers/students/student\-programs/indigenous\-student\-programs\.html\.](https://kpmg.com/ca/en/home/careers/students/student-programs/indigenous-student-programs.html)
