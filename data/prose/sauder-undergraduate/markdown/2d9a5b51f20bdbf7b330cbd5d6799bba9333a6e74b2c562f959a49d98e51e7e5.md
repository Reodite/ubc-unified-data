*Note: Only available in odd years \(i\.e\., 27W, 29W\)*

Scholarships have been endowed by friends, family and colleagues in memory of Dr\. Peter Frost, a devoted teacher, distinguished researcher, and inspirational leader who taught at UBC from 1975 to 2004\. The awards are made on the recommendation of the Sauder School of Business to undergraduate or graduate students studying Organizational Behaviour with an interest in organizational leadership and compassion\.
