A $5000 award is available to an international student who will be continuing their studies in the Bachelor of Commerce program at the UBC Sauder School of Business in the upcoming winter session\. Students graduating in May or November 2026 are not eligible\. To be considered, candidates must be able to demonstrate leadership and outstanding contributions in student or community affairs\. The recipient of this award will be honoured at Commerce Night\.

### To apply, please provide:

**Reflection of Your Top 1\-3 Involvements at UBC Sauder \(video or text format\) –** An important part of this application is letting us know who you are and what makes you extraordinary\. In this section, tell us about your 3 most impactful or memorable leadership, involvement, and/or contributions to UBC Sauder\. Speak about your most important accomplishments and experiences during your undergraduate degree\.

-   If you choose to submit a video, please make sure the video is no longer than 3 minutes\.
-   If you choose to submit written text, please make sure your answer is 1000 words maximum and written in first person\.

  
**Statement of Activities/Resume** – Your statement of activities should focus on your accomplishments during your undergraduate career\. It will follow a similar format to a resume and be a maximum of two page, 12 point font, with ¾ inch margins\. Please make sure you include ALL leadership/community involvement at UBC/Sauder\.

Your statement should be formatted as follows:

-   Personal Information \(name, email address, LinkedIn URL\)
-   University or community Activities \(Details / Impact / Dates for each\)
-   Each activity should include title/role, description of activity, and impact\. Work experience may be included in your activities\. Dates should include the month, year and hours per week \(e\.g\. Sep 2023 to Apr 2024; 3 hours per week\)\.

Please apply through this [application form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_b2ubczWoIwYiXXM) by **11:59pm PT on Monday, February 16, 2026**
