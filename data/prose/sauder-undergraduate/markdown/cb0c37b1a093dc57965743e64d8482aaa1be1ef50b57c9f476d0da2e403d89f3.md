### Support services available to UBC students

The list of supports and resources included below is not a comprehensive list\. Visit **[students\.ubc\.ca/support](https://students.ubc.ca/support)** to explore student resources and support options for academics, money, immigration, health, career, sexual assault and more\.

[Browse supports and resources](https://students.ubc.ca/support)  

### Useful links for student supports

For health and wellbeing resources available based on specific needs and location visit **[students\.ubc\.ca/health](https://students.ubc.ca/health)**

#### **UBC Counselling Services**

If you're feeling persistently stressed, anxious, or sad, you can book a virtual, phone or in\-person initial or follow\-up appointment with Counselling Services\.

-   [UBC Sauder counselling services](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/mental-health-and-wellbeing)
-   [UBC campus\-wide counselling services](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services)

#### **Food Access Supports**

If you are struggling with food security as a student, the following supports offer free perishable and non\-perishable foods, budgeting tips, and other resources on campus and in/around Vancouver\.

-   [AMS Food Bank \- List of supports](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/support-services/student-services/food-bank/resources/)
-   [UBC Student Services \- Food and nutrition \(resources under 'Food security'\)](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/food-nutrition)
-   [The Foodhub \- Food &amp; Financial Resources](https://foodhub.ubc.ca/resources/?campus=5&amp;support-resource-type=7)

#### [Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion resources and support](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/edi-support)

Mistreatment towards anyone in our UBC Sauder community is not acceptable\. Have you witnessed or been subject to mistreatment? Find out how to get support or discuss an issue related to discrimination, bullying, harassment, or sexual misconduct\.

#### [Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre)

Find out more about mental health resources by dropping into the Wellness Centre inside the UBC Life building or attending a virtual Zoom drop\-in session\. If you identify as Indigenous, Black, or as a Persons of Colour, receive wellness support and peer mentorship from [trained IBPOC students](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre/ibpoc-wellness-mentors)\. 

#### [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/international-student-advising)

Connect with International Student Advising for guidance on life as an international UBC student, from study permits and health insurance, to adjusting to life in Canada\.

#### [Enrolment Services Advising Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors)

All undergraduate students in a degree program have a dedicated Enrolment Services Advisor to help you navigate UBC, from making a budget or applying for loans to understanding UBC regulations and processes\. Enrolment Services Advisors may also assist with supporting students experiencing financial emergencies\.

#### [Sexual Violence Prevention and Response Office \(SVPRO\)](https://svpro.ubc.ca/)

UBC Sexual Violence Response Specialists are available to support you if you want to talk, explore your options, or get help with next steps\.

#### [Sexual Assault Support Centre \(SASC\)](https://www.amssasc.ca/)

The SASC offers education, support, and resources for people of all genders who are survivors of sexualized violence, as well as their friends and family\. 

#### [Student Health Service](https://students.ubc.ca/health/student-health-service)

Student Health Service offers a wide range of health assessments and treatments provided by doctors, nurse practitioners, and nurses\.  
  

#### [CALM: ADHD Assessment and Support \(External Resource\)](https://mycalm.ca/)

UBC Student Health and Wellbeing has partnered with CALM to provide students with a more accessible pathway to ADHD assessments and skills\-based support for executive functioning and stress management\. Students can explore services and coverage details directly through CALM: [https://mycalm\.ca/](https://mycalm.ca/)

*Note: This is an external resource\. Students are encouraged to review eligibility, coverage \(e\.g\., MSP or AMS StudentCare\), and service details on the provider’s website\.*

#### [Nurse on Campus](https://students.ubc.ca/health/student-health-service/nurse-campus)

Registered nurses from UBC Student Health Service can give you personalized tips, resources, and health advice\. It's confidential, free and there is no appointment needed\.

#### [Student Recovery Community](https://students.ubc.ca/health/ubc-student-recovery-community)

Connect with other students in a welcoming and inclusive space to learn about recovery from addiction to alcohol, drugs, disordered eating, and/or addictive behaviours\.

#### [Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility)

The Centre for Accessibility facilitates disability\-related accommodations and programming initiatives designed to remove barriers for students with disabilities and ongoing medical conditions in all aspects of university life\.

#### [Centre for Community Engaged Learning](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-community-engaged-learning)

The Centre’s programs provide you with community\-based opportunities to learn, participate, and collaborate on projects in the community\. You can learn from amazing community partners at an event, participate in an immersive community experience like Reading Week, lead a project by writing a grant, or by joining a Collaboratory project\.

#### [Chaplains at UBC](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/chaplains/#:~:text=The%20University%20Multifaith%20Chaplains%20Association%20office%20is%20in%20UBC%20Life,found%20on%20their%20individual%20pages.)

Opportunities for religious observance are available under the guidance of Chaplains representing a variety of faiths\. Chaplains are an important spiritual resource for students, but they are not UBC employees and are completely separate and independent from UBC\. They are accountable to and financially supported by their own religious constituencies, and they do not act under the direction or authority of UBC\.
