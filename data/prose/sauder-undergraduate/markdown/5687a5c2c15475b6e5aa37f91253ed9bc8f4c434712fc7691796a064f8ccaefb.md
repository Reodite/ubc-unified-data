This course is designed to give Commerce students an overview of land law principles as they apply in business situations\. The course will be of relevance beyond the field of actual real estate development, as land, in some form or other, is the subject of or security for, most commercial ventures\. While the course has been structured as part of the urban land program, it will be of interest and importance to many students not in urban land who are seeking a broader base of business\-related knowledge\.

Prerequisite: [COMM 393](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses/comm-393)\.

Course credits:

3
