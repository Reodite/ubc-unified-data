### Evaluate and cite information sources

Reputable business information comes from a variety of sources\. You may need to find information from library databases and/or freely available internet sources\. It's important for you to evaluate your sources to ensure that you are using reputable and appropriate information\. Consider:

-   Author/organization credentials
-   Accuracy
-   Currency
-   Bias
-   Purpose

[Evaluating Information Sources guide](http://help.library.ubc.ca/evaluating-and-citing-sources/evaluating-information-sources/)   [UBC Library How to Cite guide](http://help.library.ubc.ca/evaluating-and-citing-sources/how-to-cite/)  

### Top research topics

Companies, industries or markets

Identify and research a company, the industry in which it operates or its target market using credible information sources\. 

[Company research](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/company)   [Industry &amp; market research](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/industry)  

Case competitions prep

Learn how and where to research companies and industries for case competitions\.

[Case competitions guide](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/businesscases/competitions)  

Startups and entrepreneurship

Find out what it takes to start your own business using a variety of sources to understand your target industry and market, costs and funding opportunities\. 

[Entrepreneurship research guide](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/entrepreneurship)   [Small business accelerator](https://sba.ubc.ca/)  

International business

Find information sources on the business and economic climate and business culture for different countries\. 

[International business guide](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/international_business)  

Finance and investment

Data sources for popular finance and investment topics\.

[Finance and investment guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/finance)
