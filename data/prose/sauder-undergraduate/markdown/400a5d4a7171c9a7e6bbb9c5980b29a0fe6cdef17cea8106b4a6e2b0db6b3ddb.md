### Have a quick career question? Peer Career Advisors are here to help\! 

We look forward to seeing you\!

Peer Career Advisors are a team of **senior BCom students** from different options who can offer a unique viewpoint on campus involvement and work experiences\.

They are trained by BCom Career Strategists on many aspects of the **job search and application** **process**\. As senior students who have been in your position before, Peer Career Advisors can point you in the right direction\. If you’re feeling stuck, **they’re here to help\!**

### Talk to a Peer Career Advisor about:

-   Getting involved on campus
-   Option choice
-   Club, Co\-op, &amp; job applications
-   Interview Prep

**Visit the Drop\-in Booth**—no appointment needed\. Drop\-ins are first\-come, first\-served\.

-   **When:** Monday–Friday \| 11 am–2 pm \(for the Fall term\) 
-   **Location:** **CPA Hall** \(in front of HA 155\)

They also provided resume, cover letter, and interview tips to help me become a more competitive applicant\." \- Jennifer T\., Marketing Student
