Awards have been made available annually through a gift from the Dennis and Phyllis Washington Foundation for First Nations, Inuit, or Métis students of Canada who are enrolled in the Bachelor of Commerce program\. Recipients must demonstrate financial need, evidence of co\-curricular and community activities\. Preference will be given to students who graduated from a high school in Metro Vancouver or the Greater Victoria area\. Ordinarily, each award will be valued at $5,000\. The Dennis and Phyllis Washington Foundation aims to improve the quality of people's lives by providing them with the tools they need to succeed\.
