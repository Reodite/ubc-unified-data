Awards have been made available annually through a gift from JLL for First Nations, Inuit, or Métis students of Canada in the Bachelor of Commerce program who have achieved good academic standing and demonstrated community involvement\. Preference will be given to students with an interest in real estate\. JLL is a global professional services firm specializing in real estate\. They created this award to support Indigenous students in the UBC Sauder School of Business, and to encourage those with an interest in real estate to consider entering the industry\.
