KPMG is offering an award in memory of Larry James, a leader in the area of the Canadian Tax practice for KPMG from 1995 through 2003 and a UBC graduate\. The award is made to an undergraduate student completing their third year at the Sauder School of Business\. In adjudicating the award, academic achievement, community involvement, and the ability to serve, work and lead others are considered\. The award is made on the recommendation of the Sauder School of Business\.
