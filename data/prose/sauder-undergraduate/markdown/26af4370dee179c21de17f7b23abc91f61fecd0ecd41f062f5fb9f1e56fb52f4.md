**BCom grads, what are your plans?**

We’re thrilled to welcome you to the distinguished UBC Sauder alumni community\! Give back to future BComs and support the school by sharing what you’re doing after graduation on the Post\-Sauder Questionnaire\. It only takes a few minutes\!

### How to share?

-   **Complete the** **questionnaire** in less than 5 minutes\!
-   Let us know if you’ve accepted a job or if you're job\-searching, continuing your education, or doing something else\.
-   Retake the questionnaire if your plans change—keep us in the loop as you progress\!

Submission Deadlines: 

-   **Spring 2024 Grads:** Dec 11, 2024
-   **Fall 2024 Grads:** May 29, 2024

***Your privacy is protected\. Everything you share is only reported in aggregate and anonymous form*** ****on the** [Sauder Employment Analytics Tool \(SEAT\)](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/)\.**

[Share now \(November grads\)](https://ubcsauder.biz/postsauderfall)

[Share now \(May grads\)](https://ubcsauder.biz/postsauderspring)

### Why Share?

Your response will help UBC Sauder improve its career resources and services to support future students and alumni like you\. We value every response, wherever you are in your career journey\!

### How can we support you?

As a UBC Sauder alum, you have lifetime access to a range of career services:

[UBC Sauder Alumni Career Support](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/alumni-career-support?sess=7z7whlkrf0j): Access resources to continue developing your professional skills at any stage of your career journey\.

[COOL](https://sauder-ubc-csm.symplicity.com/)[https://sauder\-ubc\-csm\.symplicity\.com/](https://sauder-ubc-csm.symplicity.com/): Continue accessing exclusive job postings and complimentary career development resources with no expiry date\.

[Alumni Community Connections](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/alumni/alumni-support): Stay in touch and connect with your fellow UBC Sauder alumni worldwide\!

### Questions?

Learn more about the Sauder Employment Analytics Tool \(SEAT\), where data from the Post\-Sauder Questionnaire is saved, on our [UBC Sauder Alumni Job Data](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/ubc-sauder-alumni-job-data) page, or reach out to us at [bccmktg@sauder\.ubc\.ca](mailto:bccmktg@sauder.ubc.ca)\.

Your response helps shape the future of UBC Sauder—thank you for being part of our community\!
