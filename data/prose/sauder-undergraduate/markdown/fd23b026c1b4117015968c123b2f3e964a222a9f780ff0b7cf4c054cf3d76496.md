A scholarship has been endowed by The University of British Columbia to honour the memory of W\. Maurice Young\. The award is offered to a student in second, third, or fourth year of the Bachelor of Commerce program, with preference given to First Nations students, and is made on the recommendation of the Faculty of Commerce and Business Administration\.
