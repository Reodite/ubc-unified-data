The results of unsustainable human development seem evident worldwide\. Society needs more effective ways of using its skills and resources to create positive change\. This course considers whole\-systems views and trans\-disciplinary approaches to the challenges and complexities of real estate in sustainable development, including:

-   a survey of the concepts of sustainability related to real estate investment, real estate operations and real estate development of sustainable communities\.
-   strategies for implementing sustainable development in the primary activities of real estate\.
-   identifying the key interrelationships in creating asset value and sustainable real estate development\.
-   examining the outlook and trends in sustainable real estate best practices\.
