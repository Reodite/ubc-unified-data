This course examines the statutory provisions and case law that govern the creation and governance of sole proprietorships, partnerships, corporations, joint ventures and franchises in British Columbia\. The fiduciary duties of partners, directors and officers are also examined\.

Prerequisite: [COMM 393](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/courses/comm-393)\.

Note: Formerly titled *Law of Business Associations*\.

Course credits:

3
