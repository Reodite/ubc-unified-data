A scholarship has been endowed by Gertrude Jeanne Elliot\. The award is made to a student entering the penultimate or final year in the Sauder School of Business\. Preference is given to a mature student with a demonstrated interest in business and community affairs\.

*Note: ‘mature’ student typically refers to someone who may have returned to or started university later than usual due to employment or personal reasons\.*
