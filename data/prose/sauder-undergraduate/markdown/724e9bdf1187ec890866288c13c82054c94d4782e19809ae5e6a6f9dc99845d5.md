Scholarships are offered to continuing international students in the Sauder School of Business\. Students selected for these scholarships must have completed 30 credits towards their undergraduate degree or be entering the second year of their program\. Students must demonstrate strong academic achievement, engagement in their faculty, and the potential to make a scholarly contribution within their chosen field of study\.

> 
> Sauder\-specific scholarships
> 
> **Basis of Selection**
> 
> Awarded through application process
> 
> **Criteria**
> 
> academic achievement/standing
> 
> community affairs/engagement
