## Connect with employers

### Company Recruiting Sessions \(CRS\)

Exploring career paths, internships, or full\-time roles for after graduation? CRS events connect you with employers who are hiring now\. 

The BCC brings in companies from a wide variety of industries, including:

- **Row 1**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    -   Marketing
    -   Finance
    -   Accounting
    -   Technology
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    -   Consumer Packaged Goods \(CPG\)
    -   Consulting
    -   Real Estate
    -   Supply Chain

**Why attend?**

-   Employers come to meet students like you
-   Make a strong first impression
-   Get insider insights from recruiters, hiring managers, and UBC Sauder alumni

**Pro tip**: Sessions fill fast—check **[UBC Sauder Career Hub](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/events)** often so you don't miss out\.

### UBC Sauder Recruit Night

Meet employers who are actively recruiting and connect with business professionals and alumni at this annual UBC Sauder\-exclusive event\.

This event is designed for **4th\-year and above BComs and BCom Co\-ops seeking full\-time roles**\.

Before the event, we encourage students to:

-   Do your homework: research attending companies in advance
-   Come prepared with questions to make a strong impression

-   **Date:** February 24th, 2026
-   **Registration**: Sign up on [Career Hub](https://sauder-ubc.12twenty.com/events)

The Business Career Centre thanks the **[Commerce Undergraduate Society](https://www.cus.ca/)** for supporting UBC Sauder Recruit Night\.

### UBC\-wide career events &amp; workshops

Along with events hosted by the BCC, you can also check out events hosted by the **[UBC Career Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-career-centre)**\. Make the most of their career events, typically held every year:

-   **[Career Days](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/ubc-career-days)**: Visit the 2\-day in\-person career fair that features over 90 employers from various industries, eager to engage with students and alumni to share career opportunities and insights\.
-   [**West Coast Virtual Fairs**](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/west-coast-virtual-fairs): Find work, volunteer, and academic opportunities available after your UBC degree at this 2\-day, multi\-school virtual fair\.
-   [**Spring Career Fair**](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/spring-career-fair): Take part in UBC's 2\-day event and meet around 90 employers and schools for potential career opportunities\.
-   [**Multidisciplinary Undergraduate Research Conference**](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/multidisciplinary-undergraduate-research-conference): Attend the MURC to present your research to other UBC students, friends, and family\.
-   [**Graduate and Professional Schools Fair**](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/graduate-professional-schools-fair): Meet recruiters from graduate and professional programs to learn about further educational opportunities at UBC and beyond\.

Find out more about UBC Career Centre events and workshops **[here](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops)**\.
