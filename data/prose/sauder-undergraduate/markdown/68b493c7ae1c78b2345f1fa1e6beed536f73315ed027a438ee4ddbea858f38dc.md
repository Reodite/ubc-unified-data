Located just outside the Canaccord Learning Commons in room HA 238, the Wayne Deans Investment Analysis Centre \(WDIAC\) offers Sauder students and faculty access to industry databases and financial analysis systems\. Non\-Sauder users please refer to the information on the [Leith Wheeler Investment Research Lab](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/specialized-learning-spaces/leith-wheeler-investment-research-lab)\. 

UBC Sauder students can sign up for workshops, access database guides, resources, as well as detailed access instructions through logging into CLC: Online, and reviewing the [Finance Lab Spaces page](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/51980/pages/finance-lab-spaces-databases-bloomberg-capital-iq-morningstar-direct-and-applications?module_item_id=7042308)\. 

### Lab Policies

The Wayne Deans Centre is only able to be used by current UBC Sauder students\. Room access and computer log\-in is granted through your student card and CWL credentials respectively\. If you have any questions, please reach out to Fei Song, Investment Labs Coordinator \([fei\.song@sauder\.ubc\.ca](mailto:fei.song@sauder.ubc.ca)\)\.

##### Ethical/Appropriate Use of Data

-   All data should be accessed, used, and shared in accordance with UBC policies and legal  
    regulations\.
    
-   Users must adhere to strict ethical standards when using data and software\.
    
-   Unauthorized manipulation, distribution, or misuse of data and software is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action\.
    

##### No Food/Drink

-   Absolutely no food is allowed in the lab\.
-   Only sealed water bottles for drinks are permitted\. Cups with straws or holes are not accepted\.

##### Responsible Use

-   Do not unplug any cords or alter the equipment setup in any way\.

-   Priority use is given to patrons who need access to the computer's specialized financial software\.

-   Do not remove or rearrange the furniture in the lab\.

##### Conduct

-   Conversations are allowed, keep a reasonable volume to maintain a conducive learning environment\.
    
-   Maintain a respectful demeanor towards all lab users and staff\.
    
-   Refrain from any disruptive behavior that could disturb others\.
    
-   Clean up your workspace before leaving\. Dispose/recycle of any trash properly\.
    
-   Respect others' privacy and work\. Do not look at or interfere with other users' screens or materials
    

##### Consequences

-   Failure to comply with these policies may result in being asked to leave the lab\.
-   This policy is intended to supplement other UBC policies\. Inappropriate use of the lab space or financial software may result in more serious consequences as outlined in other policies\.
    

### Financial Database Access for UBC Sauder Users

Non\-Sauder users, please visit the [Leith Wheeler Investment Research Lab](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/specialized-learning-spaces/leith-wheeler-investment-research-lab) for instructions on database access and hours\.

**Bloomberg**

1.  Log in to the computers with the "Bloomberg" label using CWL\.stu
2.  Launch Bloomberg on the desktop
3.  If you have a Bloomberg account, login to your account
4.  If you do not have an existing account, click on “Create New Login” under the username and password

-   Use your cell phone number as the work phone number
-   Use your *student\.ubc\.ca* email address as your work email address\. You cannot use any non\-UBC email address\. Using a generic ubc\.ca email address that does not belong to you constitutes non\-academic misconduct\.

-   Your personal Bloomberg account is associated with the Bloomberg terminal that you logged in last time, and thus will not work on other Bloomberg terminals\. 
    

If you are currently enrolled in a UBC Sauder course for which completing Bloomberg certification is an assignment, please refer to course instructions shared on your course Canvas\.

**S&amp;P Capital IQ**

1.  Log in to the computers with the "Capital IQ" label using CWL\.stu
2.  Launch Capital IQ on the desktop – no login required

UBC students can set up a personal Capital IQ account during the fall term, [instructions here](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/51980/pages/capital-iq)\.

**Morningstar Direct**

1.  Log in to the computers with the "Morningstar Direct" label using CWL\.stu
2.  Launch Morningstar Direct on the desktop
3.  Login credentials are available [on CLC: Online](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/51980/pages/morningstar-direct)

Morningstar Direct terminals are only accessible in\-person to PhD students enrolled at UBC Sauder\. UBC Sauder students can access Morningstar Direct through their own Windows devices, or via remote access as well\. [Instructions here](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/51980/pages/morningstar-direct)\.

### About Wayne Deans 

##### Wayne Deans has been actively involved with UBC’s BCom program for the past 25 years since helping to establish UBC Sauder’s prestigious Portfolio Management Foundation \(PMF\) program\. Wanting to give students a tool that would benefit them in their studies and their careers after graduation, he provided a $2\.2 million gift that provides students dramatically increased access to financial tools and technology used by leading investment banking professionals\.
