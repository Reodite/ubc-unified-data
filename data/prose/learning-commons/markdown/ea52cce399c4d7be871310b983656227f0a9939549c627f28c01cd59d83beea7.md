## Event Highlights

[Generations Lost: Healing the Legacy of Residential Schools](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-october/#generations-lost-healing-the-l-71)

The Faculty of Education is hosting two groundbreaking exhibits on the residential school system\. The exhibits are presented in partnership with UBC Library and will be on display in the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre\. Read more about this and similar events on the [Faculty of Education website](https://educ.ubc.ca/exhibits-generations-lost-healing-the-legacy-of-residential-schools/), which provides a brief summary of the legacy of Indian Residential Schools and the Sixities Scoop\.

To continue to educate oneself and critically reflect on our own positionality, one can visit the LibGuide found here: [Indian Residential School System in Canada by Xwi7xwa Library](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/c.php?g=699914)

[Drop\-in financial advising with the Indigenous Student Support Team](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-october/#drop-in-financial-advising-wit-72)

The UBC First Nations Longhouse is enthusiastic to be re\-opening “in\-person” advising for Indigenous students every Wednesday in October from 12:30\-2:30pm\. Questions regarding scholarships, bursaries, student tuition, student loans, among a variety of financial topics are welcome\. Find out more [here](https://www.calendar.events.ubc.ca/cal/event/showEventMore.rdo)\!

[West Coast Virtual Fairs: Career, Volunteer, and Graduate Schools](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-october/#west-coast-virtual-fairs-caree-73)

Want to learn more about career opportunities and make connections with graduate school representatives from various industries? Check out this first\-ever virtual event, open to all current students and alumni, happening from October 5th \- October 7th\. Choose what day or time works best for you and pop\-in and out of sessions to develop networking strategies and even see what types of co\-op employment is available in the work\-force\! [Register here](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/?s=event&amp;ss=is&amp;mode=form&amp;id=104c273c8423b4c54d6a92b0f779d298)\.  
For more information on the event, check out [this website](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/west-coast-virtual-fairs)\!

  
 

## Resources for New and Returning Students

As we get back into the swing of things and round the corner into October, here are some academic resources for students who are looking to get a head start on all things research papers\!

-   Check out a **[Step\-by Step Guide for student research](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/a-step-by-step-guide-for-student-research/)** or drop by the [**Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication**](https://writing.library.ubc.ca/undergraduates/writing-consultations/) for a free academic writing consultation\.
-   Come see us at the Chapman Learning Commons on Level 3 of IKBLC if you have any questions, want to know about workshops, or want to use one of our many equipment lending services that are available to support you \(equipment borrowing is free to students and faculty\)\!
-   Check out our current and upcoming workshop offerings on [**our website**](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/events-workshops/)\.
-   Find out about our equipment services and what we can lend you – yes, we can even help if you forgot your laptop charger at home\! Find out all the details on [**our equipment list\!**](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/our-space-equipment/equipment-lending/)
