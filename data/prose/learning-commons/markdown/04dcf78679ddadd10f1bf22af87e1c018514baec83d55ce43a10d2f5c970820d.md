## Summer Recharge Tips

We have asked our fellow Chapman Learning Commons students for some summer recharge tips, and below are some suggestions for you to relax, enjoy, and maximize your summer\.

#### 1\. Exercise

-   Summer is a great time to start a new habit\. Check out [this blog](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/how-to-stay-hungry-and-foolish-over-the-summer/) to see a former Chapman Learning Commons Assistant \(CLCA\) Flint’s journey and thoughts about fitness\.
-   [UBC Recreation is open this summer with new programs](https://recreation.ubc.ca/2021/02/23/spring-summer-programs-now-live-registration-opens-on-march-1/) for you to try out \(fitness, aquatics, tennis to name a few\)\. Our IKBLC Assistant \(IKBLCA\) Sam has a [blog post](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/enhance-daily-productivity-and-motivation-with-excercise/) that talks about how exercise can help your productivity\.

#### 2\. Self\-Reflection and Self\-Care

-   As a school year just passed, it is important to self\-reflect and slow down from your fast\-paced lifestyle, to take care of yourself\.
-   What were the things you did that make you feel proud this school year? Celebrate it, write it down, and compliment yourself\.
-   What are the things you still want to work on? What are your plans to work towards it?
-   [CLCA Alina wrote a blog post](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/a-positive-approach-to-facing-university/) on her approach to university, where she discussed the importance of self\-awareness and self\-acceptance\. This blog might help aid you in your process of self\-reflecting\.
-   The [Keep Learning website](https://keeplearning.ubc.ca/self-care/) is also a great resource for self\-care tips\.

#### 3\. Learn Something New\!

-   Summer is a slower time for you to reflect and learn new skills that you want to be able to develop further for your career development\. Beside the wide\-range of summer courses you can register for at UBC, the university also offers a lot of other resources\.
-   Want to learn a new language? Check out [UBC Tandem Language Learning Progra](https://tandem.ubc.ca/)m to get match with a language partner\.
-   Want to learn a new sport? Check out [new programs](https://recreation.ubc.ca/2021/02/23/spring-summer-programs-now-live-registration-opens-on-march-1/) that UBC Recreation offers\.
