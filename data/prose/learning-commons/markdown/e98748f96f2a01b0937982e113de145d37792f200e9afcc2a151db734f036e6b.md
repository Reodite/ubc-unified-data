Our friendly Chapman Learning Commons Assistants provide help in person on the [3rd floor of the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](https://hours.library.ubc.ca/#view-chapman), on the phone \(604 827 3909\), and over email\.We help with:  

-   Questions about learning or academic support at UBC
  
  
-   Learning technology support \(such as Canvas, Collaborate Ultra, or Zoom\)
  
  
-   Guiding you to helpful UBC resources for your situation
