## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### Articles &amp; Websites

[UBC Library: Evaluating and Citing Resources](http://help.library.ubc.ca/evaluating-and-citing-sources/how-to-cite/?login) \(Webpage, UBC Library\)

[Purdue General Writing Resources](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/index.html) \(Webpage, Purdue University\)

[Academic Writing Tips](https://libguides.usask.ca/c.php?g=742287&amp;p=5362789) \(Website, University of Saskatchewan\)

A comprehensive academic writing guide including advice on grammar, punctuation, citations, writing styles, and AI\.

#### Videos

[Do You Actually Need to Read Your Textbooks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bMaqk-ope1M) \(YouTube, College Info Geek\)

This informational video covers the importance of reading assignments, how to balance readings from multiple courses, and how to evaluate reading sources\.

[How to Read an Academic Paper](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SKxm2HF_-k0) \(YouTube, UBC iSchool\)

The UBC iSchool describes the difference in reading an academic paper compared to a textbook and the best strategy to absorb the most information from a paper in this video\.

[Academic Writing](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLJSPTc0K-PlTaEBbDi5e15O6a4AMQlqwg) \(YouTube, University of Melbourne\)

The videos in this series introduce you to the features of academic writing, including essay structure, analysing your task, writing cohesive paragraphs, effective sentences, and APA referencing\.

### Chapman Learning Commons Resources

#### Related Toolkits

[Taking Notes](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/taking-notes/)

Notes are another inescapable part of getting a university education\. We might not be able to make them fun, but we can offer tips on how to make them useful\.

[Managing Your Time](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/managing-your-time/)

Wasting a lot of time online? Pulling too many all\-nighters? Getting better at managing your time is easier than you think\!

[Thinking Critically](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/thinking-critically/)

You can’t get the most out of university without learning to think critically\. It isn’t easy, but with our handy guide, we’ll have you thinking up a storm in no time\!

[Academic Integrity and Plagiarism](http://help.library.ubc.ca/planning-your-research/academic-integrity-plagiarism/)

Make sure you are appropriately citing your sources in your writing and giving credit to the work you used\.
