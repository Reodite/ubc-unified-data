## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### UBC Resources

[Career Goals Toolkit](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/career-goals-toolkit) \(Website, UBC Sauder\)

This toolkit from the Sauder School of Business introduces different strategies to tackle big goals\.

[UBC Career Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/ubc-career-centre) \(Website, UBC\)

UBC Career Centre offers a variety of services and resources geared to supporting you and your career goals\.

[UBC Academic Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising) \(Website, UBC\)

The Open University is a collection of open articles, activities, videos, and courses on subjects ranging from business to history\.

#### External Resources

[Goal\-Setting Techniques and Strategies You Can Use Now For Future Success](https://www.nsls.org/goal-setting-techniques) \(Website, NSLS\)

This webpage from the National Society of Leadership and Success \(NSLS\) introduces a variety of goal\-setting strategies and techniques\.

[How to Make SMART Goals](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-set-smart-goals-help-you-succeed-bernard-marr/) \(Website, LinkedIn\)

This post on LinkedIn goes over how and why to make SMART \(Specific, Measureable, Achievable, Relevant, Time\-Bound\) goals for achieving success, and offers some examples\.

[Mastering The Art of Goal Setting](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbescoachescouncil/2022/11/18/mastering-the-art-of-goal-setting/?sh=25ad40c36158) \(Website, Forbes\)

This article from Forbes discusses the importance of goal setting and tips on making meaningful and impactful goals\. 

### CLC Resources

#### Related Tools

 [Managing Your Time](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/managing-your-time/)

Struggling with managing your time or wanting to develop a more efficient schedule? Check out our toolkit to learn how to create a schedule that works for you\.

[Motivation](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/motivation/)

Staying motivated is a key aspect of achieving your goals\. Learn more about motivation and other students’ tips through this resource guide\.

[Growing Grit Toolkit](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/growing-grit/)

There may be ups and downs while trying to achieve your goals\. Learn how to be perseverant and resilient in making your goals a reality\.

#### Related Resources

[I Think I Can](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/34517-2/) by Appu \(Blogpost, CLC\)

This blog introduces and discusses how “self\-efficacy” \(the internal belief that one has the ability to achieve their goal\) can impact your approach to goals\.

[Pick Your Brain: The Neuroscience of Motivation](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/pick-your-brain-the-neuroscience-of-motivation/) by Sanjana \(Blogpost, CLC\)

Read this blog post to learn about the brain chemistry behind motivation and how it plays a role in our daily lives\.
