-   Adapters available: Mini DisplayPort to VGA, Mini DisplayPort to HDMI, Mini HDMI to VGA, Micro HDMI to VGA, USB\-C Digital AV Multiport Adapter, Lightning to VGA Adapter, Lightning to HDMI, 30PIN to VGA Adapter, USB\-C to USB, Mini DisplayPort to VGA, USB\-C to VGA, USB\-C to HDMI, USB\-C to AUX, 3\.5mm to 2\-Male RCA, Sony Camcorder RCA Cable\.
