## Frequently Asked Questions

### What can I use the kit for?

You can borrow the kit for 1 day \(24 hours\)\. It contains tools you can use to stim, find visual calm, manage your time, and study with\. Our recommendation is to take the kit to a study room or a table at the library, and experiment with the different options available\. It’s perfect for individual study or group study\.

### Can I borrow just one of the items?

The NeuroTool Kit for students must be borrowed as an entire kit\. It’s designed to help you experiment with different tools and help make student study experiences at UBC Library adjustable for students’ specific needs\.

### Where can I purchase one of these items for my own use?

Email clc\.assistants@ubc\.ca to find out more details about each item\. We’re happy to share more information so that you can purchase these items for your personal use\.

### Can I book the Student Study Kit in advance?

As of April 2024, all CLC equipment is available on a first come, first serve basis, meaning it cannot be booked in advance\.
