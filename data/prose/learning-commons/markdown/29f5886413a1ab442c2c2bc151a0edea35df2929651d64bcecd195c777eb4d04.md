UBC PayforPrint allows everyone at on the University campus to pay for printing, copying and scanning using a unified system of payment – the **UBCcard** or **UBC Guest Card**\. Printed items can be submitted from labs, public access computers as well as online at [payforprint\.ubc\.ca](https://payforprint.ubc.ca/)\.

## Print from Personal Device

1.  Login to [payforprint\.ubc\.ca](http://payforprint.ubc.ca/)\. 
    -   *Students*: sign in with your **username\.stu** and **CWL password**\. 
    -   *Faculty and staff*: sign in with your regular CWL credentials\. 
    -   *Guests*: Sign in with your UBC Guest Card account number and password\. 
2.  On the left sidebar, select **Web Print**\. 
3.  Click **Submit a Job**\.
4.  Scroll and choose a ‘**Central**’ printer option below and specify what kind of print job you are doing\. 
    -   Single \(single\-sided\) or Duplex \(double\-sided\) 
    -   LTR \(standard letter size; 8\.5×11\), LGL \(legal; 8\.5×14\), or TBLD \(tabloid; 11×17\)

5.  Click **Print Options and Account Selection**\. 
6.  Input the number of copies you wish to print and click **Upload Documents**\. 
7.  Upload the files you wish to print and click **Upload &amp; Complete\.** 
8.  When the status says ‘**Held in Queue**’, your print job is ready to be released\. Please note this can take a few minutes during busy times\.
9.  Go to the printer with your UBCcard or UBC Guest Card and swipe your card through the scanner to release your print job\. Make sure that it is the black bar being inserted through the scanner\.

## Computer Scanning

Computer scanning is a great option:

-   If you want to scan to email\. 
-   If you want to scan multiple pages easily and free of charge\. 
-   When you don’t have a USB stick to store your scans from a printer\. 

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/08/0d47c10c-4a51-4d28-85ad-b12abbb64419.gif)

### How to use a computer scanner \(on Mac\):

1.  Put your document face down on the scanner glass\. Double check the arrow marks on the machine to make sure your document is aligned to the correct corner\. 
2.  On the desktop, click on the **Image Capture** app\. 
3.  The app will show you an overview of your document\. Expand or minimize the dotted box to capture the specific area you want to scan\. 
4.  Adjust scan settings, such as colour, resolution, size, and file format, to achieve your desired scan quality\. The standard resolution for printing and scanning documents is **300 DPI**\.
5.  Specify which folder you want it to save to by selecting the dropdown box next to **Scan To:**\. For convenience, we recommend saving it to the Desktop\. 
6.  Click **Scan** to scan your document\. 
7.  Open the scanned document to view your document and do a quality check\. 
8.  Each document will have to be scanned individually and will save as a separate file\. To merge all pages into one PDF, select all of them in Finder, right click, and choose Quick Actions, then Create PDF\. 
9.  Send the scan/s to yourself via email or save it on your own personal drive before logging out of the computer\. Library workstations will reset the computer and wipe existing files upon logging off\.
10.  Don’t forget to collect the originals from the scanners\. 

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/08/fvssfbv-fd-1.gif)

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/08/wwverf.gif)

### How to use a computer scanner \(on Windows\):

1.  Put your document face down on the scanner glass\. Double check the arrow marks on the machine to make sure your document is aligned to the correct corner\. 
2.  On the desktop, click on the **EPSON Scan** app\. 
3.  Click on the **Scan** button located on the bottom right\. 
4.  A window titled **File Save Settings** will come up\. Under settings for **Type**, choose **PDF** if you want your multiple scans to be compiled into one file\. 
5.  The program will scan and prompt you to scan the next image\. If you have more pages you want to scan, choose **Add Page**\. Then, place the next page you want to scan face down on the scanner glass\. Click **Scan** once ready\. 
6.  When your scan is done and there are no more additional pages, click **Save File**\. By default, the scan will appear in the **Documents** folder\. 
7.  Send the scan/s to yourself via email or save it on your own personal drive before logging out of the computer\. Library workstations will reset the computer and wipe existing files upon logging off\.
8.  Don’t forget to collect the originals from the scanners\.

## Scan to USB

Please note that to scan to a USB using the printers:

-   You will need \(1\) a personal USB stick and \(2\) a PayforPrint account\. 
-   The cost to scan per side is 2 cents\. 

### How to scan \(on a printer\): 

1.  Connect USB to the printer \(left side of the display\)\. 
2.  Swipe your UBCcard or UBC Guest card through the card scanner\. Make sure that it is the black bar being inserted through the scanner\. 
3.  Navigate to **Device Functions** and click **Use Device Functions**\. 
4.  Choose **Print/Scan \(Memory Storage Device\)**, then **Scan to Storage Device**, then **USB\.** 
5.  Place document/s face up in the tray on the top of the printer\.
6.  At this stage, you can choose a folder on your USB stick to save your scan into\.
7.  Click **Scan Settings** to review your scanning preferences, like B&amp;W or colour, resolution, etc\.
    -   You can click on **File Type**, and then **Others** to choose your file format\.
        -   Choose ‘Single\-page’ for separate scans or ‘multi\-page’ to combine scans\. 
        -   The printers can create either TIFF, JPEG, or PDF files\.
8.  Tick **Preview** on the top right of the display to view your scanned files once the scanning is done\. 
9.  Select **Start** to scan\. 
10.  Once done scanning all the documents, if you ticked **Preview**, you should see the scanned file\. Check the scan\. 
11.  If everything looks good, click **Save**\. You will also see the scanned file in your selected folder\. 
12.  Remove your USB stick safely by clicking the eject button in the bottom left of the display\. 
13.  Collect your original copies from the printer\.
14.  On a separate personal device or workstation, check that your scan has been saved on the USB and is correctly formatted\.

## Sustainability

The Chapman Learning Commons has a commitment to sustainability\. Conserve your use of paper by: 

-   Reusing paper from old assignments and syllabi 
-   When printing:
    -   Print double\-sided to cut your paper use in half\. Print your cover page separately if you need one\. 
    -   Print multiple slides at the same time\.
        -   Powerpoint: Click on “Full Page Slides” in the print settings\. Choose from layouts like “4 slides horizontal” or “6 slides horizontal\.” If printing from your own device, save your new layout as a PDF before sending it to the printer\. 
        -   Keynote: Choose the “grid” layout and select the number of slides per page\. Save as PDF using the bottom left corner button before submitting to PayforPrint\. 
-   When scanning:
    -   Scan to PDF instead of on paper\.
