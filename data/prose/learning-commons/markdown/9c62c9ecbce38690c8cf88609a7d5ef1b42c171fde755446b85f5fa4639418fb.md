## Research Tips

#### Library Services Update: Goodbye EZproxy, Hello OpenAthens\!

UBC Library is excited to announce that on June 2, 2021, we will launch a new way to log into electronic library resources including databases, journals, articles, and ebooks\. OpenAthens, an identity\-based authentication system, will provide access to the Library’s licensed online resources using single sign\-on \(SSO\) technology, both on and off\-campus\. [Learn more about what this means, here\!](https://about.library.ubc.ca/2021/04/07/introducing-openathens-ubc-librarys-new-secure-way-to-access-online-resources-using-cwl/)
