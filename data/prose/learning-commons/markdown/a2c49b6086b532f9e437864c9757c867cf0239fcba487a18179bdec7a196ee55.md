## **Tips for Learning from Home**

-   [**Keep Learning**](https://keeplearning.ubc.ca/) is a great resource for setting up, online learning tips, technologies and self\-care\.
-   Take the [**Online Learning Readiness Questionnaire**](http://plato.algonquincollege.com/ac-library/lp/learning-online/story.html) to determine if you are prepared for success in your online courses\.
-   Online or not, time management is always a valuable skill for students\. Access the **[Managing Your Time toolkit](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/student-toolkits/managing-your-time/)** to establish short and long\-term goals\.
-   The **[Online Learners toolkit](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/resource-guides/online-learners/)** lets you know about library services, transitioning to learning online, and setting up your space at home\.

### **Time Management When Learning Online**

With online learning, some of us are taking classes that have both asynchronous \(on our own time\) and synchronous \(“live”\) learning elements\. In this session you will learn how to separate your “work space” from your “home space” when you can’t physically come and go from class\. You will also get tips on time management as a remote learner to set you up for success when taking classes online\.
