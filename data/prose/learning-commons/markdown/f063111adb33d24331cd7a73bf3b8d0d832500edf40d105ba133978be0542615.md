## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### UBC Resources

[Presentation Skills](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Presentation_Skills) \(Guide, UBC Wiki\)

This UBC Wiki page goes over various aspects of providing an effective presentation and provides plenty of links giving advice for online presentations\.

[Learning Skills Workshop on Online Group Presentations: Key Advice &amp; Resources Handout](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/07/Group-Presentations-Online-Top-Tips.pdf) \(PDF, CLC\)

A handout that covers all of the key challenges that students face during online presentations and tips for how to overcome them\.

#### UBC Clubs

[UBC Public Speaking Club](https://www.mbasociety.ca/public-speaking-club) \(Website, UBC Club\)

The MBA Public Speaking Club is designed to help students develop confidence and become compelling communicators\. The club emphasizes practical skill development through interactive workshops, guest speakers from the theatre and performance community, and exposure to high\-quality speaking events

[UBC Debate Society](https://www.ubcdebatesociety.com/) \(Website, UBC Club\)

Whether you are a master debater or a novice, join this club to build public speaking skills and compete in debate competitions\.

#### Videos

[How To Give a Great Oral Presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=swIWHuIpubI) \(YouTube, Microbiology Society\)

Get tips on how to give the best presentation possible in this video that covers the entire process from planning to delivery\.

[Be A More Confident Public Speaker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tShavGuo0_E) \(YouTube, Watchwellcast\)

This useful video also features a handout to help you perfect your public speaking skills\.
