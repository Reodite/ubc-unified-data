## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### Articles and Website

[19 Top Interview Questions with Sample Answers \(Website, Indeed\)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/interviewing/top-interview-questions-and-answers)

This article by Indeed highlights some of the commonly asked interview questions and tips to answer them\.

[How to Prepare for an Interview \(Website, UBC Student Services\)](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/interviews)

This webpage gives an overview of the steps to take in preparing for different types of interviews\. It also includes some general tips to consider in preparing\.

[On\-Campus Jobs \(UBC\)](https://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-jobs/)

Learns about job opportunities available to you on campus, when to apply, and what your options are including Co\-Op and Work Learn\.

#### Videos

[Hiring and Interviewing Training Courses \(Videos, LinkedIn Learning\)](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/topics/hiring-and-interviewing)

LinkedIn Learning has a few course videos to help you boost your interview confidence\. LinkedIn Learning is [free for UBC students](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-resources/make-most-linkedin/linkedin-learning)\.

### Chapman Learning Commons Resources

#### Related Blogs

[Tips &amp; Tricks for First\-Time Job Hunters](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tips-tricks-for-first-time-job-hunters/)

Is this your first time applying for a job? You are not alone\! Learn some tips from a student staff’s job search reflections\.

[How to Recover from an Interview Failure](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/how-to-recover-from-an-interview-failure/)

Do you have an experience of an interview that did not go well? Learn how to transform that into success in your next interview from this blog post\.

#### Related Toolkits

[Communicating Effectively](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/writing/)

Communication is one of the most important skills that help you shine in an interview\. Check out this toolkit to learn how to articulate your thoughts for your interview\.
