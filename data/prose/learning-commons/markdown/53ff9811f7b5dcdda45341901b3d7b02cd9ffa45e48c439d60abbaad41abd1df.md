## How to Book a Space at IKBLC

Want to study at the library this exam season? The Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre \(IKBLC\) building at UBC Vancouver is open from 8am\-10pm on Monday\-Sunday, from Sunday, April 18 to Thursday, April 29\.

Check out [this link](https://ikblc.ubc.ca/spaces/book-a-study-space/) to book a study space at IKBLC\.

## Final Exam Preparation Resources

**A\) Stress Management**  
Exam season can be stressful\. Drop by the [UBC Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre)’s virtual office to talk to a nurse or a Wellness Peer about stress management\.

**B\) Technology Guides**

-   Familiarize yourself with the software you are using for exams\. Sign up for our [Online Exam Canvas course](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/online-exams/) to test out your software and avoid technical difficulties during your exams\.
-   The Chapman Learning Commons provides support with academics and technology \(Canvas, Zoom etc\.\) over [email](mailto:clc.assistants@ubc.ca) and video chat up to 1 hour before your exam \(chat or video call from 9am to 4pm PST, Monday to Friday\)\. Check out our [Online Exams support page](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/online-exams/) for information about key exam technologies and software\.
-   For tech troubleshooting during your exam, contact your instructors or the [Learning Technology Hub](https://lthub.ubc.ca/support/lt-hub/) to receive support\.
-   Self\-enroll in our [CLC Exam Canvas Course](http://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/C7TG94/) to practice setting up for your online exams using software including Proctorio, Lockdown Browser, and more\.
-   Keep Learning also has a page dedicated for [Learning Technologies FAQs here](https://keeplearning.ubc.ca/faq/?login)\.

**C\) Study Tips**

-   Time management is really important when it comes to planning your exam review schedule\. Check out this [Managing Your Time toolkit](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/student-toolkits/managing-your-time/) to establish short and long\-term goals when studying for your exams\.
-   Chapman Learning Commons Assistant Tam wrote [a very detailed blog post](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/your-first-online-university-exam-in-2020/) to help you prepare for an online exam here\. She has shared tips on how to prepare your environment for an online exam and troubleshoot technical difficulties\.
