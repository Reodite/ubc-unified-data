Try engaging in a conversation with your fellow UBC students on our 2022 Fall Board of Thoughts\! We have a brand\-new set of topics for you to share your experiences and reflections on\!  

The Board of Thought is a community board – a place to share ideas and comments\! It is found online [here](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tutoring-and-advice/board-of-thoughts/) and joining the conversation is as simple as clicking the “\+” sign\. Once your comment is approved, it will appear as a new block within the column of the prompt that you chose to reply to\. 

Visit our physical Board of Thoughts location – it’s next to the Chapman Learning Commons Help Desk in IKBLC, beside the entrance to the MAA Library\. Please feel to comment online, in person, or both\! 

If you would like to share your experience interacting with the Board of Thoughts, please let us know in the comment section – we would love to hear from you\!
