### How to renew an item \(e\.g\. request to keep items for longer than the initial loan time\)  

1.  Visit the library home page: [library\.ubc\.ca](http://www.library.ubc.ca/) 
2.  Click on the ‘Log In’ button on the top right corner of the screen

3.  Select ‘My Library Account Login’\.

4.  Log in using one of two methods:
    -   **CWL Login:** Press the Blue ‘CWL Login’ Button and log in with your CWL username and password\. You may need to use Duo Multi\-Factor Authentication\. 
    -   **Standard Library Login:** Enter your library barcode \(found on the back of your UBC card\) and your pin\. If you forgot your pin, you can reset it using your library barcode and your email address\.

### Renewal Troubleshooting: Why is my renewal not working? Why am I being charged late fees?

If your item failed to renew, please make sure:

-   That the item is eligible for renewal\. Short loan items such as chargers, headphones, and adapters are not eligible for renewal\.
-   After checking out your item, you must wait to renew until the day after the item was checked out\. If you try to renew on the same day that you checked out the item, your renewal will fail\.
-   If you try to do multiple renewals on the same you will loose those renewal options\.
-   That you have not reached the maximum number of renewals allowed\. You can check the maximum number of renewals allowed on the [Borrowing Equipment page](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/borrow-equipment/)\.

If you fail to renew by the initial due date, you will be charged a late fee as per our borrowing policy\. You will still be able to renew your item \(provided it is eligible for renewal\) even if you are charged a late fee \(until you reach the maximum late fee amount\)\.

All renewal and loan time information can be found on the [Borrowing Equipment page\.](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/borrow-equipment/) 

Need help? Visit the CLC Help Desk on Level 3 of IKBLC, or phone/email us at 604\-827\-3909 or clc\.assistants@ubc\.ca\.

### **Why can’t I book equipment in advance?**

The Chapman Learning Commons has a first come, first served model for equipment\. This keeps access to equipment equitable for our many users\. Removing advance bookings reduced the inconvenience many of our users experienced when they arrived to pick up a booking and the item is not physically at the library\.
