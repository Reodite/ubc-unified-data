## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### Websites

[Academic Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising/) \(Website, UBC\)

Learn how academic advisors can help as well as how to receive program\-specific advising\.

[Plan your Degree](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/admitted/degree-planning/#campus=Vancouver) \(Website, UBC\)

Find out which courses you should take in first year, when to declare a major, and more depending on your degree program\.

[Changing your Degree Program or Campus](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/changing-degree-program-or-campus/) \(Website, UBC\)

Review necessary eligibility requirements and steps to applying if you are seeking to change your degree or campus\.

[What Happens If I Don’t Get The Degree I Want?](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/news/what-happens-if-i-dont-get-into-the-major-i-want/) \(Webpage, Faculty of Arts\)

Read advice from an Arts Advisor on how to parallel plan and the different alternatives you can take when aiming for a competitive major\.

#### Workday Tutorials

[Degree Planning](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/degree-planning/?login) \(Website, Workday UBC\)

Follow these tutorials to navigate program or degree changes in Workday\.

[Academic Progress Report](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/degree-planning/year-by-year-planning/academic-progress-report/) \(Webpage, Faculty of Arts\)

A guide on how to use the APR tool on Workday to keep track of degree requirements and credits\.

#### Worksheets

[College Degree Course Tracker](https://studentaidbc.ca/sites/all/files/form-library/budget_worksheet.pdf) \(Webpage, Notion Marketplace\)

A Notion template to give students a single place to keep track of coursework and ensure they meet degree requirements\.

[First Year Planning Guide](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/PTNWA8) \(Canvas Course, UBC\)

A useful guide for new Arts first year students planning their degree\.

#### Videos

[How I Create a Four\-Year Plan](https://itools-ioutils.fcac-acfc.gc.ca/BP-PB/budget-planner) \(YouTube, University of North Carolina\)

Watch this video to see an alternate version of a four\-year course plan that outlines courses a student needs to meet their goals throughout their university career\.

### Chapman Learning Commons Resources

#### Related Toolkits

[New to UBC – Learn from Peers](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc-learn-from-peers/)

This guide offers helpful advice from previous UBC students on topics such as choosing your program, academic success, and making friends\.

#### Related Tools

[Approach to Learning Quiz](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/approach-to-learning-quiz/)

This quiz may help you gain a little more insight into your typical approach to learning and which strategies may work well for you in maximizing your strengths\.

[Course Registration Quiz](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/course-registration-quiz/) 

What do you look for the most when registering for courses? Topic, academic objectives, instructor, course format, or time? Take this quiz to figure out what’s most important for you\. 

#### Related Blogposts

[A Student Guide to Picking Classes by Richard](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/a-student-guide-to-picking-classes/) \(Blogpost, CLC\)

Review everything you need to know to keep scheduling classes hassle\-free with helpful advice from a previous UBC student\.

[How to Maximize Academic Advising Appointments by India](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/chart-your-degree-better-than-degree-navigator-how-to-maximize-academic-advising-appointments/) \(Blogpost, CLC\)

Learn some helpful tips on how to make the most of academic advising appointments\.

[Five Things This Fifth Year Wishes She Knew in First Yea](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/five-things-this-fifth-year-wishes-she-knew-in-first-year/)r by Nicole \(Blogpost, CLC\)

Get some advice as you plan out your degree from an upper\-year student\.

[Picking Your Courses](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/picking-your-courses/) by Nuriya \(Blogpost, CLC\)

Don’t know which electives to take? Struggling between multiple sections? Get personalized advice from an upper year student on how to pick your courses\.

[Understanding Electives](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/news/understanding-electives/) \(Blogpost, Faculty of Arts\)

Read more to understand what electives are, when you should take them, and how you can use them to design your degree\.
