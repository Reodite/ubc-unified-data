## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### Worksheets &amp; Time Management Tools

[Weekly Planning Worksheet](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/08/blank-weekly-timetable.doc) \(Word Document, UBC\)

Use this UBC\-created downloadable template to plan out your time during the week and create a schedule that fits your needs\.

[13 Powerful Ways To Visualize Your Data](https://www.sisense.com/blog/10-useful-ways-visualize-data-examples/) \(Blog Post, Sisense Team\)

If you’re making your presentation and you’re trying to figure out the best way to visualize your data, check out this article about the different resources you could use to present different types of data\.

[Your Energy Cycle](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/02/your-energy-cycle.doc) \(Word Document, UBC\)

Use this downloadable document by UBC to track your energy levels throughout the day in order to visualize when you are most active and create a schedule that fits around your energy levels\.

[Planning Assignments Using GenAI](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/GenAI/assignmentplanning) \(Website, UBC Library\)

This guide is intended to help you plan your assignments using Generative AI tools\. It provides tips on how to write effective prompts to generate detailed outlines and schedules to help you plan your time and understand how to approach assignments\.

[Pomodoro Technique](https://www.asundergrad.pitt.edu/study-lab/study-skills-tools-resources/pomodoro-technique) \(Website, University of Pittsburgh\)

The Pomodoro technique is a structured series of shorter periods of focus that builds in time for your brain to take a break\. This website goes over the general structure and lists some common apps used to implement it\.

#### Videos

[Time Management](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uc86kqIC5WU) \(YouTube, UBC Leap\)

UBC Leap’s Time Management video focuses on creating a work and life balance and how you can create free time for yourself while also managing your class schedule and school work\.

[Study Exam Skills](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z5Ru8sx5d1c) \(YouTube, UBC Leap\)

UBC Leap’s Study Exam Skills video focuses on different elements that you should keep in mind during the exam season, such as how to create a study schedule that best fits your workload\.

[Three Design Hacks for Better Visual Presentations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RJCp-ttFpd4) \(YouTube, BrightCarbon\)

Check out this video by BrightCarbon for three easy design hacks on how to make your presentations the best that they could be\.

[Inside the Mind of a Master Procrastinator](https://www.ted.com/talks/tim_urban_inside_the_mind_of_a_master_procrastinator) \(TedTalk, Tim Urban\)

In Tim Urban’s TedTalk, he encourages viewers to explore the reasons why they might be procrastinating in the first place in order to uncover what they are really trying to avoid and face the problem head\-on\.
