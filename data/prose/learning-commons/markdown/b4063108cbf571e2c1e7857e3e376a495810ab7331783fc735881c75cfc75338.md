This past midterm season, I faced something I had never experienced before: three exams on three consecutive days\. When I first saw my schedule, a terrible wave of panic washed over me\. I wasn’t sure how I was supposed to balance studying three totally different subjects over such a short period of time without burning out\. Now that I’ve made it through, I wanted to share what helped me stay organized during one of the busiest weeks of my term\. 

## **Build a Detailed Study Schedule** 

The very first thing I did to prepare was open up my calendar and map out everything\. I listed my exam days, broke down the time I had before each one, and slotted in study blocks for every course\. Seeing everything in one place made the whole situation feel a lot less chaotic\. Instead of bouncing between random topics, I had a clear plan to follow each day\. ## **Alternate Subjects to Stay Focused** 

During my blocks of studying, I made sure not to stick with just one subject for hours on end to prevent feeling mentally drained\. I rotated subjects every hour or two, which helped my brain stay fresh and focused\. Switching gears regularly also made it easier to jump into each exam without feeling stuck in a certain subject for too long\. 

## **Put More Time Into Weak Areas** 

With so little time between exams, I knew I couldn’t pull off re\-studying everything from scratch\. Knowing this, I focused mainly on the concepts that were still confusing for me\. I went through my slides and notes, made a list of everything I didn’t understand, and gave those sections the most attention\. Skipping the material I already understood saved me a lot of time and effort\. 

## **Take a Break After Each Exam** 

One thing that I have learned over the years is how important it is to give myself a small break after every exam\. Even 30 minutes made a difference to my mental health\. After walking out of each exam room, I’d grab some food, take a short walk, and chat with some friends before diving back into study mode\. That little pause helped me reset and stopped me from burning out halfway through midterm season\. 

---

Looking back, juggling three back\-to\-back exams wasn’t easy, but these strategies helped me get through the week feeling more prepared and a lot less overwhelmed\. Now that I’ve survived this exam marathon and gathered my tips, I feel way more confident facing whatever future exam seasons throw at me, and hopefully you do too\!
