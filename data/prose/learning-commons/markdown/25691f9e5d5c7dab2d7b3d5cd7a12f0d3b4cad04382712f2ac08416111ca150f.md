I have gone through all 4 years of my degree telling myself that THIS semester is when I am going to get organized and do everything I can to be the best version of myself\. Eat right, get 9 hours of sleep, study, workout, and have a social life\. Trying to figure this all out myself has proven to be difficult but I finally decided that I would use AI to help me optimize my life\. And here is how it went\.

## **The Prompt**

I started off with trying to figure out what it was that I wanted to allocate my time to effectively\. As someone who loves going to the gym, eating clean, and getting adequate amounts of sleep, I wanted to highlight the importance of having time for all of these things needed to be part of my regular schedule\. This helped me develop this prompt, which I plugged into ChatGPT so that it could create a schedule for me:

> *So, I am a 22 year old university student in my last year of university\. I value community, hard work, rest and having a few moments of self care in my day\. I like to eat whole foods which means dedicating some time in my week to meal prep and rest and sleep is very important to me\. I want to have at least 8 to 9 hours of sleep at night\. I value my time in the gym as well and I want to do a good job in school with my classes\. I want you to create a schedule for me based on my life so that I can achieve these goals\. I have class from Monday to Thursday at these times \(insert the times of your classes here\) and I work on these days at these times \(insert your work hours here\)\.*

I need time to study and do assignments\. I like to go to the gym in the morning and can be there for around 1 and a half to 2 hours\. Please make my schedule\.

## **My Experience**

When it finally gave me a schedule that I felt like I could work with, I tried it for a week\. I took note of the things I liked and didn’t find realistic for myself\. For example, it recommended that I do a debrief of my classes after every single class\. While this might be a good idea for some\. I didn’t find that it would be a great idea for me since I prefer dedicating large blocks of time towards my studying\.

## **Would I Recommend this to Someone Else?**

Now if anyone asks me if I think that this is something they should do, my answer is yes\! I found using AI to organize my life has given me a way to holistically account for the things that I value and ensure that I have enough time allocated to each thing\. I’ve found that using a planner or a calendar was just too rigid and did not allow for the flexibility that AI can account for\. For example, I can tell ChatGPT to adjust my schedule if I have an upcoming exam that I want more study time for, even telling it to break down what days I should start studying for each chapter\.

## **My Tips and Tricks**

For anyone wanting to use AI to schedule their lives, I would say:

-   Clearly outline any big goals or priorities that you have in your prompt\.
-   Don’t be afraid to tweak anything you don’t find realistic or feasible in the schedule it provides you\.
-   You can tweak the schedule again and again until it works for you\!
