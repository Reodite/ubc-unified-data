Before I moved into my first\-year dorm, I went on a full Pinterest\-fueled shopping spree\. I had a list, a colour scheme, and a vision and I was convinced I needed every single item the “dorm essentials” videos told me to buy\. But now as a fourth year, I am ashamed to admit that a lot of the stuff I bought is now collecting dust under my bed, while a few cheap, “un\-pretty” items have genuinely made my life easier\.

Here is what I actually use almost every day, and what I really wish I had not bought\.

## **What I actually use\!**

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/image-edited-1.png)

[Image from Sterilite](https://www.sterilite.com/product/6-qt-stacking-drawer/)

### Storage bins under the bed

Dorms are tiny, and the space under your bed is real estate you should absolutely be using\. I keep extra bedding, out\-of\-season clothes, and snacks in flat storage bins, and it keeps my room from feeling cluttered when there is nowhere else to put my stuff\. The bins were one of the cheapest things I bought and probably one of the most useful\.

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/image-1-edited.png)

[Kit](https://unsplash.com/photos/white-and-black-power-strip-YrdSQ_1gYRE) Unsplash

### A good power strip

There are never enough outlets in a dorm room\. A solid power strip with a few USB ports built in saved me from constantly fighting my roommate for the one accessible plug near my bed\. Spend a little extra on one with surge protection, because your laptop will thank you\!

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/image-2-940x627.png)

[Neakasa](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-woman-is-holding-a-dryer-and-ironing-clothes-ELnyS2wG3ss) Unsplash

### A steamer \(or pro tip: wrinkle release spray\)

I brought a full iron and ironing board and used them maybe twice\. A small handheld steamer is so much faster, takes up way less space, and works for basically any fabric\. And honestly, if you do not even want to deal with that, wrinkle release spray is a game changer\. You spray it on, smooth out the fabric with your hands, and walk out the door\.

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/image-3-940x627.png)

[Bluewater Sweden](https://unsplash.com/photos/black-and-white-plastic-containers-on-brown-sand-tF9iUErZXWA) Unsplash

### A reusable water bottle that keeps water hot or cold

I know everyone says to bring a water bottle, but I want to be a little specific here\. Get one that is properly insulated and that you actually like, because that is what determines whether you carry it around or not\. I went through three random ones before I found one that kept my water cold for hours and survived being thrown into my backpack\. It also doubles as a tea or coffee container\.

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/image-4-edited.png)

[Chandan Chaurasia](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-pink-fan-sitting-on-top-of-a-table-ZBa8Jp411kY) Unsplash

### A small fan

Even if your residence has air conditioning, dorm rooms get stuffy real fast, especially with two people and zero ventilation\. I bought a small clip\-on fan and it has been a lifesaver during warmer months and stuffy late\-night study sessions\.

## **What I didn’t really need\!**

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/image-5-940x627.png)

[Joonas Sild](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-pink-fan-sitting-on-top-of-a-table-ZBa8Jp411kY) Unsplash

### A printer

I was so sure I needed one but I used it like maybe three times all year? The Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre has printers you can use, and most assignments are submitted online anyway\. If you ever need help figuring out how the printers work, the CLC student staff are always around and happy to walk you through it\.

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/Untitled-design-edited.png)

[Pixelshot](https://www.canva.com/photos/MAG58GcRt7w-young-woman-cleaning-carpet-with-vacuum-cleaner-in-living-room/) Canva

### A vacuum cleaner

If you are in UBC housing, they actually lend out vacuums for free, so there is no reason to bring or buy your own\. Just check with your residence front desk\!

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/image-6-940x627.png)

[Clay Banks](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-pink-fan-sitting-on-top-of-a-table-ZBa8Jp411kY) Unsplash

### A monitor

Unless you are big into gaming or you genuinely study better at home all the time, you do not need to bring a monitor\. UBC libraries have monitors ranging from Mac to PC that you can use whenever you want, which saves you the hassle of fitting one onto your already\-tiny dorm desk\!

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/image-7-edited.png)

[Humphrey M](https://unsplash.com/photos/open-black-compact-refrigerator-filled-with-soda-bottles-3h7jbWXhpgM) Unsplash

### A mini fridge

This one really depends on your situation, but most UBC housing comes with a fridge after first year, and even in first year, you usually have access to a communal fridge on your floor\. A mini fridge takes up so much space and uses more power than you would think\. Try the communal one first, and then make a decision\!

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/image-9-940x529.png)

[Debby Hudson](https://unsplash.com/photos/three-assorted-decorative-bowls-in-white-surface-RXKERUSGN8U) Unsplash

### Cute food containers that are not microwave or dishwasher safe

Please check the bottom of your containers before buying\. I bought a whole set of cute ones that I could not microwave, which made meal prepping pointless when I had to transfer everything onto a plate first\. Get the boring, durable, microwave and dishwasher safe ones\.
