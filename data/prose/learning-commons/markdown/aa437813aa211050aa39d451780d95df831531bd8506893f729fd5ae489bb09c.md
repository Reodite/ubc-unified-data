## About the Zine

As the summer session comes to an end, we are proud to release our fifth edition of the Chapman Learning Commons Zine, a collection of art pieces, written works, and creative design, created and curated by our Student Staff\.

The end of summer brings a new cohort of fresh students to UBC, ready to experience this university that is so special to us for the first\-time\. Being a first\-year is not easy, whether it’s adjusting to university\-level classes, living away from home for the first time, or even just making friends\. That’s why we decided to make our First Year Survival Guide\.

Even if you are not a first\-year and are returning to UBC after the summer, this zine is sure to share some new information with you or even tell you a trick about campus you may not have known\. After all, UBC is full of surprises\!

We wish all UBC students the best of luck, as we all embark on a new academic year together\. It is not easy, but the people, this campus, and the community that we have at UBC makes even the lowest of lows feel doable\. So, as the weather starts to become slightly cooler and the days shorter and shorter with every passing night, it’s important to welcome the new academic year that is upon us with open arms, however hard it may feel in the moment\. To the first\-years, we know this can be a daunting time, but we hope you find what you are looking for in this zine\. And to everybody else, we hope you not only “survive” but thrive this year\.

Have a great year,

*Your Summer 2026 CLC Assistants*

## What Your First\-Year Residence Says About You\!

*Written by Manavi*

### Orchard Commons \(OC\)

If you live in OC, you’re an independent person who appreciates the finer things in life\. You have an eye for the newest technology and can spend hours people\-watching in Open\-Kitchen \(while enjoying the delicious egg tarts\!\)\. You love your alone time and a gorgeous sunset from the top\-floor lounge\.

### Place Vanier

If you live in Place Vanier, you’re an old soul that loves making friends and appreciates the more rustic things in life\. You’re a foodie that always tries to convince your friends to grab lunch at Vanier\. You enjoy long walks \(especially to visit your friends in OC and Totem\) and are rarely late to class, because everything is so nearby\!

### **Totem Park**

If you live in Totem Park, you’re an ambivert that enjoys spending time with yourself but also with others\. You have a calm and grounding energy that your friends appreciate, especially when you give them advice over the Honey Chicken Sesame Salad at Feast during lunch\. You’ve decorated your room, especially your accent wall, with posters of niche albums you love\!

### Ritsumeikan House

If you are one of the handful of first\-years living at Rits, you’re extremely mature and put\-together\. You value your own space and enjoy cooking your own meals instead of relying on the Dining Hall\. You really enjoy spending time with your roomates and often host movie nights\.

## Dorm Food Recipes – No Kitchen Needed\!

*Written by Selena*

### 🌮 Single\-Serve Nachos

**Ingredients**:

-   15 tortilla chips
-   1/4 cup shredded cheese
-   1/4 avocado cubed
-   jalapeno slices, as desired
-   sliced green onions, as desired
-   cilantro roughly chopped
-   2 tbsp salsa
-   2 tbsp sour cream

**Instructions:**

1.  Arrange chips into an even layer on a microwave\-safe plate\. Layer the cheese, jalapeno slices, and green onion evenly over the chips\.
2.  Microwave until the cheese is melted \(around 1 minute\)\.
3.  Top with avocado cubes, salsa, sour cream, and cilantro\.

### 🥙 Tuna Salad Wrap

**Ingredients**:

-   2\.5 oz\. can water\-packed tuna \(drained\)
-   2 tbsp mayo
-   1 tbsp finely diced onions
-   0\.25 cup finely sliced lettuce
-   1/4 tsp salt
-   1 standard whole grain tortilla

**Instructions:**

1.  Mix the tuna, mayo, onions, and salt in a bowl until well combined\.
2.  Lay out the tortilla and put down an even layer of lettuce\. Spread the tuna salad over that\.
3.  Roll up the wrap tightly and cut in half before serving\.

### 🫐 Blueberry French Toast in a Mug

**Ingredients**:

-   1 slice of bread
-   1 egg
-   2 tbsp milk
-   ¼ tsp cinnamon
-   ½ tsp vanilla extract
-   blueberries, to taste
-   maple syrup

**Instructions:**

1.  In a bowl, mix egg, milk, cinnamon, and vanilla\.
2.  Slice the bread into cubes and add to a microwave\-safe mug with blueberries\.
3.  Pour the egg mixture into the mug\.
4.  Microwave until egg is fully cooked \(around 1\.5 mins\)\.
5.  Top with maple syrup\.

A thousand paths, a whole new city,

A thousand hearts, all feeling brave\.

Standing with my luggage, feeling heavy,

Watching the crowd move like a wave\.

Ribs starts playing through my head,

A soundtrack to the ground I tread\.

A feeling takes its hold

I’ve never felt so deeply home,

Wrapped in the blue and gold\.

From my first steps onto campus

To the Chan Centre’s final stage,

I’ll cherish every single day
