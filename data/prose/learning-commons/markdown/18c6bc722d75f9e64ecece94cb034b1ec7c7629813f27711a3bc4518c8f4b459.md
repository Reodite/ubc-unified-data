## Event Highlights

[UBC Thrive Month](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-november/#ubc-thrive-month-74)

It’s [Thrive Month](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/wellbeing-campaigns-and-initiatives/thrive)\! For those new and returning to UBC, the focus of Thrive Month is to learn, talk, and explore ways to support mental health, such as through healthy living and social connection\. See the resource section below for some of the many events that will promote mental health goals and support a balanced wellbeing for long\-term success\.

For more information on Thrive Month, check out the Thrive Welcome Booth on Nov 1st from 1\-3pm in UBC’s Life Building\.

[Literature Review Workshop](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-november/#literature-review-workshop-75)

Discussing all things ‘Literature Review’ for Engineering, Forestry, Health Science, Land Food Systems, and general Sciences\! If you have a Literature Review upcoming or simply want to learn about search strategies for databases and finding the right database, the UBC library has got you covered\.

Be sure to register for this event on Nov 4: [Literature Reviews for Engineering, Forestry, Health Science, Land &amp; Food Systems, and Science \- LibCal \- University of British Columbia \(ubc\.ca\)](https://libcal.library.ubc.ca/event/3640316)

[Using RefWorks for Citation Management](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-november/#using-refworks-for-citation-ma-76)

Having trouble with citations? This workshop presented by UBC Library will teach you how to use RefWorks for generating citations and bibliographies in APA, MLA, and other formats as well as how to organize references using tags and folders\. Due to UBC Library providing UBC students, faculty, staff and alumni with a continued site license, RefWorks is freely available\.

Find out how to use RefWorks for efficient citation creation and management on Nov 25 by registering [here](https://libcal.library.ubc.ca/event/3636307)\.

## Resources for New and Returning Students

Here are some ways to get involved as well as support Thrive Month\!

The Board of Thoughts project was created to build social connections and promote thought\-provoking conversations at the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre \(IKBLC\)\. Each week in November, find yours and your peers’ answers on the board next to the walking and bike stations on level 3 of the IKBLC\. [Answer questions here to contribute to the Board\!](https://bit.ly/boardofthoughts)

Are you a grad student who has trouble clearly defining your assertiveness or communicating your goals, needs, and ideas with confidence? Check out this workshop presented through Thrive Month entitled, “Assertiveness: Speaking Up for Yourself in Difficult Conversations\.” [Registration for this event on Nov 24th can be found here\.](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/about-us/events/21118-assertiveness-speaking-yourself-difficult-conversation)
