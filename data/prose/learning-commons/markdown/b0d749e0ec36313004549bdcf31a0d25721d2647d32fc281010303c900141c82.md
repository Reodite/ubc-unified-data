## Common Questions:

[Can I borrow equipment?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-july/#can-i-borrow-equipment-34)

Yes\! Our equipment lending service has returned\! Check out our [equipment lending page](https://lc2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/our-space-equipment/equipment-lending/) to book your item\.

[How can I learn more about course registration?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-july/#how-can-i-learn-more-about-cou-35)

[Take our quiz to understand your course selection priorities for the fall](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tutoring-and-advice/course-registration-self-assessment/) and be connected with resources for making informed choices about your classes this upcoming school year\.

[When should I apply for my student loans?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-july/#when-should-i-apply-for-my-stu-36)

UBC recommends that you apply for your student loans by July 31st in order to allow your application to be processed and approved in time for the school year and tuition deadlines\. Learn more about student loan applications in regards to UBC [here](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/student-loans)\.

[What is Jump Start?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-july/#what-is-jump-start-37)

[Jump Start](https://you.ubc.ca/jump-start-vancouver) is a program specifically developed for incoming first year students that allows them to arrive and live on campus two weeks early\. This allows for the students to get to know their classes, their peers, and to build friend groups before the school year begins to allow for an easier transition into university on\-campus life\. If you are interested in beyond beginning your social life at UBC and want to know how you would fit into different courses check out our self\-assessment tool [here](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tutoring-and-advice/course-registration-self-assessment/)\.

[Can I still come to visit IKBLC?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-july/#can-i-still-come-to-visit-ikbl-38)
