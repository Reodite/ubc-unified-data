## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### Websites

[AMS Peer Support](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/support-services/student-services/peer-support/) \(Website, UBC\) 

Make use of free, confidential, one\-on\-one peer support available for any challenges you may be facing\. 

[UBC Counselling Services](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services) \(Website, UBC\) 

On\-campus counselling services can serve as a first point of contact for a variety of mental health concerns\. 

[Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre) \(Website, UBC\) 

The Wellness Center is a peer\-hosted space on campus, offering many different wellbeing programs for students\. Drop by in\-person or submit a question online\! 

#### Articles

[Natural Light and Productivity](https://go.exlibris.link/HPWyQ2nk) \(Article, UBC Library\) 

Learn about the link between natural light and its effects on productivity\. 

[Food for Thought](https://go.exlibris.link/pWb0HmH2) \(Article, UBC Library\) 

Find out how the amount and type of dietary fat you’re consuming plays a role in focus\. 

[Mental Health Benefits of Nature Based Recreation](https://go.exlibris.link/zZys0VNv) \(Article, UBC Library\) 

Learn about the cognitive benefits of nature\-based recreation and how it may lead to positive mental health outcomes\. 

[Music\-Dependent Memory in Immediate and Delayed Word Recall](https://go.exlibris.link/l2l4wh8d) \(Article, UBC Library\) 

Understand how the music you listen to while studying could affect your recall during an exam\. 

#### Books and Videos

[Deep work: rules for focused success in a distracted world](https://webcat.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/holdingsInfo?bibId=8578100)  by Cal Newport \(Book, UBC Library\) 

Explore the benefits of intense focus and concentration in an age of constant distractions, with strategies to cultivate profound productivity and achieve meaningful results\. 

[The shallows: what the Internet is doing to our brains](https://webcat.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/holdingsInfo?bibId=4602103) by Nicholas Carr \(Book, UBC Library\) 

Investigate the impact of the internet on our cognitive abilities, and whether constant online engagement is reshaping our brains and changing the way we think\. 

[How to Get Your Brain to Focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hu4Yvq-g7_Y) \(YouTube, TED Talk\) 

See how your ability to focus can play a role in your ability to harness your attention and enhance creativity in this Ted Talk by Chris Bailey\. Learn how to harness your focus and minimize distractions to live a more meaningful life\. 

### Chapman Learning Commons Resources

#### Related Blogposts  

[Cultivating the Ability to Focus](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/cultivating-the-ability-to-focus/) \(Blogpost, CLC\) 

Learn tips like silencing notifications and scheduling blocks of time for different activities\.  

[Focusing Flexibly: Tips for Staying Zoned\-In](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/focusing-flexibly-tips-for-staying-zoned-in/) \(Blogpost, CLC\) 

Get a student perspective on how to stay in the zone, such as making your work fun and taking lots of breaks\. 

[Does listening to music make you a better learner?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/does-listening-to-music-make-you-a-better-learner/) \(Blogpost, CLC\) 

See the effects of different music on your learning experience, and how things such as perception play a role\. 

[How to Prevent the Afternoon Slump](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/how-to-prevent-the-afternoon-slump/) \(Blogpost, CLC\) 

See how you can prevent the feeling of burnout and lack of energy and stay focused throughout the day\.  

[What is a food coma and how to avoid it](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-is-a-food-coma-and-how-to-avoid-it/) \(Blogpost, CLC\) 

Learn how what you eat may make you feel tired and drained throughout the day\. 

#### Related Tools

[StayFocused](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/stayfocusd-block-distract/laankejkbhbdhmipfmgcngdelahlfoji?pli=1) \(Website Blocker, Chrome\) 

This is one of a few Chrome extensions that can help regulate how much time you’re spending online each day\.   
  
[Aesthetic Pomodoro Timer](https://studywithme.io/aesthetic-pomodoro-timer/) \(Website, Gridfiti\)

If you want to try out studying with a Pomodoro timer, this is a great website to have in a separate tab in the background\.

#### Related Toolkits

[Managing Your Time](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/managing-your-time/) 

Looking to learn new ways to improve your time management skills? It’s easier than you’d think\.

Explore how sleep habits can affect your focus levels and academic success\.
