## Irving K Barber Learning Centre: Booking No Longer Required\!

It’s true\! **You are no longer required to register for a space** at IKB to enter; just arrive at the door labelled ‘Enter Here’ on East Mall\! **The only thing you need to do is complete your [daily self\-assessment](https://bc.thrive.health/covid19/en)**\. Opening times are 10am to 8pm Mon\-Thurs, 10am to 6pm Fri\-Sat \(closed Sundays and holidays\)\. Please note that Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre will be closed for BC Day on August 2nd\.

Individual study rooms on level 2 are still available for booking\. You can book these at any time they are available \(no longer only 24 hours in advance\)\! [Just go to this link and click ‘Book Study Rooms\.’](https://ikblc.ubc.ca/spaces/book-a-study-space/)

## Student FAQs

[What is Work Learn?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-august/#what-is-work-learn-43)

UBC’s Work Learn Program is a great opportunity to gain some experience and professional skills \(and money\) while working part\-time on UBC’s campus\. [Find out more about Work Learn, here\!](https://students.ubc.ca/career/ubc-experiences/work-learn-program)

[What is Jump Start?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-august/#what-is-jump-start-44)

[Jump Start](https://you.ubc.ca/jump-start-vancouver) is a program for incoming first year students, allowing them to arrive and live on campus, or commute to campus, two weeks early\. Get to know your classes, peers, and build friend groups before the school year begins for an easier transition into university on\-campus life\.  
If you are interested in beginning your social life at UBC and want to know how you would fit into different courses [check out our self\-assessment tool here](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tutoring-and-advice/course-registration-self-assessment/)\.

[What is Collegia?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-august/#what-is-collegia-45)

The Collegia are physical sites on campus designed for first\-year students and Indigenous students who commute, intended to offer an interdisciplinary “home\-away\-from\-home” for them on campus\. Designed to complement programming for first\-year students in residence, students can access resources to help navigate their transition to university, make friends, and get advice form senior peer mentors and faculty members\.

To check your eligibility and to register, [visit their website](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/ubc-collegia)\. The deadline to register is Tuesday, August 31, 2021\.

[What is Imagine Day?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-august/#what-is-imagine-day-46)

[Imagine Day](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/imagine-ubc) is a chance to meet some great people including your Dean, some of your future professors, and other students\. The experience may be fully virtual or a combination of in person and virtual, and will be confirmed with you during the summer\. Either way, it will include a group meet up, faculty welcome, pep rally, and main event with hundreds of clubs and organizations\! Best of all, all students are invited to participate in the day's activities\!

[Where can I get help studying for finals?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-august/#where-can-i-get-help-studying--47)

For help preparing, check out [this toolkit](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/student-toolkits/preparing-for-exams/) and [this guide](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/online-exams/) prepared by the Chapman Learning Commons Assistants\! They are also happy to assist with academic support and resources over email and Zoom\! During your exams, you can get technical support from the Learning Technology Hub here\.

[Can I borrow equipment from the Chapman Learning Commons?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-august/#can-i-borrow-equipment-from-th-48)

Yes\! Our equipment lending service has returned\! [Check out our equipment lending page](https://lc2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/our-space-equipment/equipment-lending/) to book your item\.

[When do classes start in the fall? What is the latest date to add/drop classes?](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-you-need-to-know-in-august/#when-do-classes-start-in-the-f-49)

September 7th is the start of the Winter Session for most faculties, while the add/drop deadline varies depending on course length and type\. You can [find out more about term dates here](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/dates-deadlines), and [course change \(add/drop\) deadlines here](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates)\!
