Want to share your thoughts or advice? Look no further than the Chapman Learning Commons Board of Thoughts: an online community that fosters conversation between primarily UBC students\. The Board of Thoughts is found at this [link](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tutoring-and-advice/board-of-thoughts/), and joining in on the conversation is as simple as clicking the **“\+”** sign\. Once your comment is approved, it will appear as a new block within the column of the prompt that you chose to reply to\.

You might remember that we also have a physical location for the Board of Thoughts: next to the [Chapman Learning Commons Help Desk](https://ikblc.ubc.ca/?login) in IKBLC, beside the entrance to the MAA Library\. Due to the semester starting online and wanting to provide the same ease of accessibility and resources, an online version has been created\!

If you would like to share your experience interacting with the Board of Thoughts, please let us know in the comment section\. We would love to hear from you\!
