## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### Toolkit Handouts

[Infographic Handout](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/01/wellness-toolkit-sleep-infographic-OFFICIAL-aug-20.pdf) \(PDF, CLC\)

Check out this visual summary of all the toolkit content\!

[Sleep and Academic Success: Workshop Handout \(PDF, CLC\)](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/04/Sleep-and-Academics-Handout-1.pdf)

This useful document outlines questions to consider when thinking about sleep and how to address the sleep related challenges that students face\.

#### Websites &amp; Books

[Sleep for Success](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/sleep-success) – UBC Student Services \(Website, UBC\)

UBC Student Services provides a thorough overview of the importance of sleep and resources that can help you improve your sleep hygiene\.

[UBC Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre) \(Website, UBC\)

Learn how to access the UBC Wellness Centre’s services, events, and programs\.

[UBC Wellness Centre Online](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/PCNEN4) \(Canvas Course, UBC\)

Enroll in this Canvas course to get access to wellness resources and learn about a wide variety of topics including sleep and mental health\.

*[Why We Sleep](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=8863888)* by Matthew Walker \(Book, UBC Library\)

Explore sleep on a deeper level in this book that answers many important questions about sleep and its importance in our lives\.

*[Sleep Solution](https://vpl.bibliocommons.com/v2/record/S38C5311244)* by Chris Winter \(Book, VPL\)

If you’re having sleep difficulties, read this book that outlines why certain sleep disorders occur and how to help solve them\.

#### Videos

[What Would Happen If You Didn’t Sleep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dqONk48l5vY) \(YouTube, Claudia Aguirre\)

Watch this quick video to learn about the effects of sleep deprivation on your brain, body, and cognition\.

[Why Sleep Matters](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LmwgGkJ64CM) \(YouTube, Harvard Medical School\)

This video lecture covers the importance of sleep and how to sleep better and longer\.

[Master Your Sleep &amp; Be More Alert When Awake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lIo9FcrljDk) \(YouTube, Andrew Huberman\)

This video explains the science behind sleep and the various factors that affect it, such as light exposure\. Use this video to understand how to set yourself up for high quality sleep\.
