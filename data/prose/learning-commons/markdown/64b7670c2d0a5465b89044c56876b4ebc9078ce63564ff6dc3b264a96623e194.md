## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### Websites &amp; Worksheets

[Lab Notebooks, Techniques &amp; Protocols](https://libguides.wpi.edu/ch1010/lab_notebooks)

\(Website, Worcester Polytechnic Institute\)

This guide explains how to organize and maintain your lab notebook with an annotated template\.

[Organizing Your Lab Notebook](https://guides.lib.purdue.edu/c.php?g=352816&amp;p=2377935)

\(Website, Purdue University\)

This resource provides tips on organizing your lab notebook with supporting videos, formatting data, as well as finding safety protocols and scientific references for lab work\.

#### Videos

[Keeping a Laboratory Notebook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?si=cFMe4WNKDqiGoWo7&amp;v=QrUiUGrBwIc&amp;feature=youtu.be)

\(YouTube, Wake Forest Graduate Student\)

This informative video shares a grad student’s perspective on the importance of keeping a lab notebook and maintaining it in research\.

[Ten Tips for Keeping a Better Lab Notebook](https://youtu.be/O3isoitK-Ac?si=3kNpotUErvXz6qrN)

\(YouTube, Gold Biotechnology, Inc\.\)

This video breaks down ten strategies to keep an effective lab notebook in terms of clarity and reliability\.
