## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### Articles &amp; Websites

[Bloom’s Taxonomy](https://www.eoas.ubc.ca/research/cwsei/resources/Blooms-verbs-stems.htm) \(Website, UBC EOAS\)

Check out this UBC webpage to learn about the six levels of the cognitive domain from knowledge to evaluation\.

[Foundation for Critical Thinking](http://www.criticalthinking.org/) \(Website, The Foundation for Critical Thinking\)

A website dedicated to cultivating critical thinking skills that features various resources and events\.

#### Videos

[Critical Thinking 101](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9G5xooMN2_c) \(YouTube, UBC LEAP\)

A video that provides an introduction to the spectrum of authority and how to create a compelling argument on a topic\.
