“2023 is almost over? No way\!” is what I keep thinking as December comes to a close\. But, after a term full of \(what seems like\) endless midterms and assignments, we could all definitely use the time to kick back and relax\. Winter break is perfect to do just that, so we’ve put together a list of cozy activities to make the best out of your holiday\!

### Books, shows, and films, oh my\!

[Image](https://lc2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/12/pavan-trikutam-avJ9uz9Qhcw-unsplash-940x627.jpg)

Photo by [Pavan Trikutam](https://unsplash.com/@ptrikutam?utm_content=creditCopyText&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_source=unsplash) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/white-book-near-mug-avJ9uz9Qhcw?utm_content=creditCopyText&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_source=unsplash)

Not sure where to get your entertainment fix? Have no fear\! UBC gives access to thousands of films and books; all you need is your Campus\-Wide Login \(CWL\)\!

-   Get your “watch” on by accessing the [Audio Cine Films Digital Campus Collection](https://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?details=audio-cine-films&amp;id=2525), a database featuring films from Disney, Pixar, Universal Studios, and more\!
-   For all the book worms out there, [Novelist Plus Database](https://web.s.ebscohost.com/novelist/search/novbasic?vid=0&amp;sid=418a016d-01a9-468b-98f7-e3e366905a20%40redis) is a literary treasure trove\. Want to find your next read? This is the place to go\.
-   Want to go back in time? [Women Writers Online](https://wwo-wwp-northeastern-edu.eu1.proxy.openathens.net/WWO/search?browse-all=yes) features early writing from women, dating all the way back to the 16th century\! How’s that for a history lesson?

### Looking for some comfort and entertainment this break? Read these blog posts for some ideas:

-   Take a look at this [Winter Movie List](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/holiday-movie-list/) of some holiday favourites to find your next watch\!
-   Prefer curling up with a warm blanket and a book? We’ve got you covered; check out our student staff’s [Must reads for Spring 2023](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/must-reads-for-reading-week-2023-spring/) and these [4 fiction books by UBC Faculty](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/4-fiction-books-by-ubc-faculty-for-your-next-library-trip/)\!
-   What’s a holiday break without good food? Get cozy with Oishi’s [Recipes to Soothe your Soul](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/cooking-for-comfort-recipes-to-soothe-your-soul/) and fight those winter blues\!

### New year, new me\! Prepare to enter beast mode next year:

-   Looking to prep early for the next term? Check out Angela’s [Tips to Prepare for Courses Effectively \(Part 1\)](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tips-to-prepare-for-courses-effectively-part-1/)\! 
-   Got a New Year’s resolution to reduce screen time? Check out Yanya’s [Guide to “Digital Detoxing”](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/disconnect-to-reconnect-a-guide-to-digital-detoxing/)\!
-   Whether you’re on vacation on a tropical beach, or staying in the comfort of your home, breaks are a great time to pick up a new hobby\. Read Mika’s blog post on [Journaling for a Week](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/i-tried-it-journaling-for-a-week/) for some inspiration\!

Moral of the story: take the time to recharge and be kind to yourself\! Juggling academics with work, friends, and other life stress can be draining\. Getting through this term–whether as a first year or a seasoned student–is a huge achievement and a step towards greater things\. Now, go get that well\-deserved rest and let us know if you tried any of our suggestions in the comments\!

### Have an amazing Winter Break\!
