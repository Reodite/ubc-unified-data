## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### Websites &amp; Worksheets

[Imposter Syndrome Worksheet](https://www.carepatron.com/files/imposter-syndrome-worksheet.pdf) \(PDF, Carepatron\) 

This worksheet is designed to help you identify and challenge thoughts related to imposter syndrome\.

[I Am](https://theiam.app/) \(Mobile app\)

Building self\-love and self\-confidence is key in mitigating feelings of being an imposter\. Self\-affirmation apps such as these can be a good way to introduce these daily reminders into your routine\.

#### Videos

[The Imposter Syndrome Paradox](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u2zbcZBI0Do)

\(TEDx, YouTube video\)

Lisa and Richard Orbé\-Austin, licensed psychologists and founders of Dynamic Transitions Psychological Consulting, offer tips for recognizing triggers and dealing with the imposter syndrome\.

[Imposter Syndrome: Why You Have It &amp; How to Overcome It](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VHkfEoc-3mk)

\(struthless, YouTube video\) 

Struthless, a YouTuber, content creator, illustrator, and animator, speaks on his experience with imposter syndrome and how it has affected his mental health personally\.

### Chapman Learning Commons Resources

#### Related Toolkits

[Reframing Learning Challenges](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/reframing-learning-challenges/)

Academic challenges are fairly common experiences, and it may lead to feelings of being out of place\. Learn how to reframe your mindset around these\.

[Growing Grit](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/growing-grit/)

Learn how to develop perseverance, define your goals, and embrace a growth mindset\.

[Personal Wellbeing](http://learningcommons.ubc.ca/personal-wellbeing/)

Managing a healthy and balanced lifestyle as a student can be tough\. This toolkit covers a few of our personal tips on cultivating wellness\.

#### Related Blogposts

[How to have Self\-Compassion in Academics](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/how-to-have-self-compassion-in-academics/) by Alex

Explore resources and approaches for building the skill of treating yourself kindly and compassionately while being a student\.

[Mental Health and Balancing Student Work Life](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/mental-health-and-balancing-student-work-life/) by Kartik

Learn some tips for maintaining your mental health and wellness while having a busy academic workload\.

[What I Would Do Differently If I Could Do University All Over Again](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-i-would-do-differently-if-i-could-do-university-all-over-again/) by Lia

Read some reflections and advice from a previous upper\-year student on life during university\.

[I Think I Can: Determining Your Self\-Efficacy](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/34517-2/) by Appu 

Many students often encounter a sudden lack of confidence in their abilities during university; thus, determining one’s self\-efficacy is important\.
