## Explore

### Websites &amp; Videos

#### Websites and Articles

[Motivation &amp; Procrastination](https://forum.tfs.ca/LearningGuide/Motivation-Procrastination) \(Website, Canada’s International School\)

Learn tips on finding motivation, tackling work habits and challenging your mindset through Canada’s International School’s Learning Forums\.

[UBC Wellbeing – Student Learning and Motivation](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/educational-strategies/student-learning-and-motivation) \(Website, UBC Wellbeing\)

Find articles and workshops on Student Learning and Motivation with tools from the UBC’s Wellbeing department\.

#### Videos

[Preparing for Exams](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XFtGSIV0TmI)  \(Youtube, CLC\)

This Chapman Learning Commons video focuses on preparing for exams and is a great way to learn how to apply discipline to your studying\.

[How to Do Well in an Online Course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BnhW-ETSmrw) \(Youtube, CLC\)

Watch this quick video to learn about ways in which motivation and discipline can help you for online courses\. 

### Chapman Learning Commons Resources

#### Related Blogposts  

[Enhance Daily Productivity and Motivation with Exercise](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/enhance-daily-productivity-and-motivation-with-excercise/) \(Blogpost, CLC\)

Check out this blog post to learn about the connection between exercise and motivation\.

[Pick Your Brain: The Neuroscience of Motivation](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/pick-your-brain-the-neuroscience-of-motivation/)  \(Blogpost, CLC\)

Gain a better understanding of the neuroscience behind motivation and how you can incorporate it into your daily goals\.

[Why Keeping a To\-Do List is a Good Idea](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/why-keeping-a-to-do-list-is-a-good-idea/)  \(Blogpost, CLC\)

Learn about the importance of a to\-do list and how to build your own to stay self\-motivated\.

[Cultivating the Ability to Focus](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/cultivating-the-ability-to-focus/) \(Blogpost, CLC\)

Explore useful techniques that can strengthen your focus in order to study, absorb material, and perform well in school\. 

#### Related Tools

[Time Management when Learning Online](https://lc2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/07/Time-Management-when-Learning-Online-Handout.pdf) \(Workshop Handout, CLC\)

Learn more about time management tools, and ways to prioritize your objectives and goals\.

[How to Focus](https://lc2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/07/HowToFocus_Handout_July2021.pdf)  \(Workshop Handout, CLC\)

Check out this key resource for techniques to improve your ability to focus and how students can tackle these challenges\.

#### Related Toolkits

[Managing Your Time](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/managing-your-time) 

Gain helpful skills on time awareness, time management and long term versus short term planning\.
