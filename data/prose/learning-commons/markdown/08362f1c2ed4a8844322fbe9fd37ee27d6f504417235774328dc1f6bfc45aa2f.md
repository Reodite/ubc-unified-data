Are you a new UBC student who wants to prepare for the fast\-approaching school year? Or have you been on campus for a while but want to find more places and resources to connect to? Check out our [Learn from Peers: New to UBC guide\!](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc-learn-from-peers/)

Our helpful guide acts as a hub for all the resources that can help you transition into UBC student life\. Learn how your UBC peers chose their majors and navigated their way through university in the guide’s videos\. It also features advice from UBC students on important aspects of student life like housing, budgeting, and more\.

If you’re looking to feel more connected on campus, the [New to UBC guide](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc) is here to help\. Let us know in the comments below if you’ve checked out the guide\.
