As the cold seeps into Vancouver’s streets and the rain starts its daily performance, it’s officially the season of fuzzy socks, hot drinks, and indoor game nights that last way too long\. Whether you’re hosting a cozy night in or procrastinating on that one assignment \(we’ve all been there\), here are some of my favourite games to play with friends when it’s too chilly to step outside\. 

## **Charades**

Yes, it’s a classic, but the *movie edition*? Instead of acting out random words, you only act out *movie titles*, and the more dramatic, bizarre, or obscure they are, the funnier it gets\. My go\-tos? “Come Forth and Bear Witness to My Unscrupulous Wang,” and “A Lizard in a Woman’s Skin\.” Watching your friends act these out is priceless, and the confused faces trying to guess? Even better\. Bonus: you might just discover your next movie\-night pick\.

## **Guess Who: Personal Edition**

Okay, I’m claiming this one, I *think* I invented it \(please don’t ruin my dream 😅\)\. The rules? Simple\. One person secretly picks someone you all know: could be your first RA, a random cafe barista, or that one friend\-of\-a\-friend everyone met once\. Then everyone fires off yes\-or\-no questions until someone cracks the case\. It sounds harmless… until you’re three rounds deep arguing over who counts as “mutual\.”

## **Cards**

### 1\. Playing Cards

You can *never* go wrong with classic old cards\. I once played Bluff until 6 AM \(partly because I was winning, but who’s counting? …clearly not me\)\. Whether it’s Bluff, Spoons, or just an excuse to sip hot chocolate under a blanket, it’s the perfect weekend ritual\. You can find rules for these games and many more such games on this [website](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play)\! 

### 2\. Uno

The game that destroys friendships and builds character\. Everyone has “their” UNO rules \(and apparently none of them match\)\. Between “UNO: No Mercy” and everyone pretending *their* house rules are the real ones, it’s the one game where my competitive side instantly comes alive\.

### 3\. Monodeal

Fifteen minutes of chaos and strategy\. One card can flip the entire game\. You’ll be a real estate mogul one second and completely broke the next… unless, of course, you’ve got a “Just Say No\.” If you know, you *know\.* If you don’t, it’s time to find out\.

Games like these are exactly why indoor nights hit different\. One minute I’m convinced I’ve mastered the rules, the next I’m begging my friends for mercy and wondering how the game turned on me so fast\. It’s that mix of competitiveness, ridiculousness, and the kind of laughter that warms me from the inside out\. Add blankets, warm drinks, fuzzy socks, and the rain humming in the background, and suddenly the whole night feels soft, cozy, and exactly where I truly want to be\.

So here’s to warm rooms, late\-night giggles, and inside jokes born from pure game\-night madness\. If you try any of these out, tell us which one had you laughing the hardest or share your go\-to game that always steals the show in the comments below\!
