## Who can go?

-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements:
    -   [**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/eligibility "Eligibility")
-   Only students currently in the Faculty of Forestry

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(University of the Basque Country Autumn semester\): September to January
-   UBC Term 2 \(University of the Basque Country Spring semester\): January to June
-   UBC Full Year \(University of the Basque Country Autumn \+ Spring semester\): September to June

### Areas of study available:

-   Forestry

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Courses at the University of the Basque Country are mainly taught in Spanish and Basque, but there is a wide range of courses available in English as well\.
    -   More than 440 undergraduate courses are taught in English:
        -   Undergraduate Courses: [https://www\.ehu\.eus/en/web/nazioarteko\-harremanak/en\-courses\-taught\-in\-english\-for\-bachelor\-students](https://www.ehu.eus/en/web/nazioarteko-harremanak/en-courses-taught-in-english-for-bachelor-students)
        -   Master's Courses: [https://www\.ehu\.eus/en/web/nazioarteko\-harremanak/en\-master\-courses\-taught\-in\-english](https://www.ehu.eus/en/web/nazioarteko-harremanak/en-master-courses-taught-in-english)
-   More than 460 undergraduate courses are “English Friendly courses” \(where the main instruction is in Spanish, but the course syllabus, tutoring, and exams are also available in English\),
    -   This would be an interesting option for students who have a basic level of Spanish and want to improve it\.
    -   [https://www\.ehu\.eus/documents/d/vitoria\-gasteizko\-ingeniaritza\-eskola/english\_friendly\_courses\_20252026\_ingenieria](https://www.ehu.eus/documents/d/vitoria-gasteizko-ingeniaritza-eskola/english_friendly_courses_20252026_ingenieria) 

[Partner Course Information](https://www.ehu.eus/en/web/vitoria-gasteizko-ingeniaritza-eskola/incoming-students)

### Course equivalency Formula

ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits 

### Course load Information

-   Maximum: 30 ECTS per semester

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

## Finances and funding

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.

-   15 credits or equivalent full\-time program tuition per term, paid to UBC
