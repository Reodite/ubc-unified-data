##### Where can I get a copy of a course syllabus?

If you require UBC course syllabi, please contact the respective instructor via email\. After you’ve been admitted and have Workday access, you can find course instructor contact info in the course schedule in [Workday](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/) when you click into a section of the course\. Please keep in mind that some courses may not have instructors assigned yet, or some instructors are away during summer and may take longer to respond\. It’s also not uncommon for instructors to finalize their course syllabi in the weeks leading up to September\. You can also visit the departmental website, as some department upload course syllabi on their syllabi\. As course syllabi are considered intellectual property at UBC, **Go Global does not keep copies of course syllabi\.**
