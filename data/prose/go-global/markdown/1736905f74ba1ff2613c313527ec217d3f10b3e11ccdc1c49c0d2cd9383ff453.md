### **About the program**

The Camp will bring together outstanding students from our partner universities worldwide, offering them a unique opportunity to delve into Macao’s rich history, explore its captivating world heritage sites, and gain insights into its vibrant economy and blend of Eastern and Western cultures\. The five\-day program will be conducted in English, and students will have the opportunity to engage in seminars, site visits, and a wide range of cultural activities\.

The Summer Camp is fee\-waived; UM will cover on\-campus accommodation \(in shared rooms\), local transportation, meals and activities expenses included in the program, while ***students are responsible for their round\-trip traveling expenses, insurance and other personal expenses incurred outside the programme\.***

Full\-time undergraduate students in any disciplines of study are eligible for the program\. 

Go Global will not charge any fees for this program\. This program does not offer academic credit\. 

Unfortunately, there are no Go Global awards available for students participating in this program\.
