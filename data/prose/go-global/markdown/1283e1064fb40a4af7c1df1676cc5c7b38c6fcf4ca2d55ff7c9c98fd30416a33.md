## Who can go?

-   This partnership is only open to **Health &amp; Exercise Science students on the Okanagan Campus**
-   Students must have a cumulative average of at least 73% to attend this partnership
-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of undergraduate study

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Other opportunities at this partner

**British Universities Transnational Exchange Association \(BUTEX\)**  
Each year, BUTEX awards a number of £600 scholarships to students studying abroad for a single semester or full academic year\. The Sustainability Abroad Scholarship is a unique opportunity that merges the critical themes of sustainability with the enriching experience of international study\. In a world where travel is increasingly accessible, this scholarship emphasises the importance of sustainable practices and environmental awareness alongside cultural exchange and education\.

The University of Stirling is a University Member of BUTEX, so incoming study abroad students applying to Stirling can submit a scholarship application\.

[Find out more and apply for the BUTEX scholarship](https://www.butex.ac.uk/scholarships/)\.
