##### Do I need a Meal Plan?

Residence Meal Plans are only required if you are living in Orchard Commons, Totem Park or Place Vanier\. If you have been placed in a residence other than the three mentioned above, you are not required to purchase a meal plan as you will have a kitchen in your unit\.

For more information about meal plans at UBC, please see [this website](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/residence-meal-plan-all-access-dining/)\. Contact [Student Housing and Hospitality Services](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/contact-us) for any questions\.
