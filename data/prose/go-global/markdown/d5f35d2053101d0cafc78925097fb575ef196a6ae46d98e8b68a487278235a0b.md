## Who can go?

-   Only COMM \(UBCV\) students

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(WHU Fall semester\): late August \- late December
-   UBC Term 2 \(WHU Spring semester\): early January \- late April

### Areas of study available:

-   Commerce \(UBCV\)

### Course availability and restrictions

-   May be provided with a  “Schein," for each course completed \- please be sure to retain this in order to give to Go Global upon your return

[Partner Course Information](https://www.whu.edu/en/programs/exchange-programs/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Wide range of courses in English at the undergraduate and Graduate evel
-   WHU offers intensive tuition\-free [German language courses](https://www.whu.edu/en/campus-vallendar/language-learning/#:~:text=WHU%20offers%20German%20courses%20at,the%20end%20of%20the%20course.) for exchange students worth 5 ECTS credits
-   Students must have at least a B1 level to study in German \(To determine your level, please self\-assess with: [Common European Framework of Reference for Languages \(CEFR\)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)\)
