## Who can go?

-   **This partner is available to UBC Okanagan students only**
-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study
-   Students must have a minimum 70% cumulative average of above to attend this partnership

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(UL semester 1\): Mid September to Late January
-   UBC Term 2 \(UL semester 2\): Late January to Early June

***\*Note that Term 1 students can return to UBC for Term 2 and write their UL exams on their home campus\.*** 

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Engineering
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Ensure you have approximate pre\-requisites to what is listed for your preferred courses
-   Liverpool strongly encourages students to choose modules from a maximum of two departments and, where possible, within the same school, as this will minimise timetable clashes
-   To avoid further timetable clashes, Liverpool suggests picking all modules from the same level
-   Students are limited to two classes per semester from the [Management School](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/management/), unless you're majoring in Management/Commerce

[Partner Course Information](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/global-opportunities/visiting-students/study-in-liverpool/what-can-i-study/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   Liverpool credits divided by 4 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   Normal/Maximum Courseload: 60 credits per term / 120 credit per year
-   Minimum Courseload: 45 credits per term / 90 credits per year

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)
