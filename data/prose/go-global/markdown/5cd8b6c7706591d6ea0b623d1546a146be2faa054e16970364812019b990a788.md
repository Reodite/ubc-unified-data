## Global Seminars

Take a focused and structured UBC course led by a faculty member with a small cohort\. Immerse yourself in a specific topic and experience course content brought to life\.

[View seminars](https://global.ubc.ca/go-global/international-experiences/global-seminars "View seminars")

## Coordinated International Exchange \(CIE\)

Take a term of undergraduate engineering and environmental design courses that are pre\-approved by the Faculty of Applied Science at another university\.

[Read program details](https://cie.apsc.ubc.ca/ "Read program details")
