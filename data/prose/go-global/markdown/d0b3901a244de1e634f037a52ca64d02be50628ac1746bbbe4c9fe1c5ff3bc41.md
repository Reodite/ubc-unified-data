## Who can go?

-   Only Forestry and LFS students are eligible to apply to this partner university

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(SLU Semester 1\): Late August to mid January
-   UBC Term 1 \(SLU Semester 2\): Mid January to early June

**\*Students attending during UBC Term 1 take courses from block 1 and 2, unlikely to be able to return for Term 2 at UBC**

### Areas of study available:

-   Forestry
-   Land and Food Systems

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Forestry and LFS students may also be eligible to take courses in Economics and Business Administration, Environmental Sciences, Food Science and Agricultural Science, Landscape Architecture, Life Sciences, Horticulture, Animal Science and Biology

[Partner Course Information](http://www.slu.se/en/education/courses/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   There are limited course offerings available in English
