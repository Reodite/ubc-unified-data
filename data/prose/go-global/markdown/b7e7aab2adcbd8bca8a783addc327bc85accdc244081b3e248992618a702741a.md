## About the program

Fudan University's International Summer Sessions provides students the opportunity to learn about China, experience Shanghai, and connect with Fudan University students to be part of the Fudan community\. Take courses taught by outstanding academics over a 4\-week period during the summer term\. The program is intended to "help you achieve multicultural understanding, establish a global vision, and get connected with peers from around the world\."

More specific and finalized program details can be found on the partner institution's website\. However, you must apply through Go Global to be considered for this program and ensure you follow the appropriate transfer credit process\.

## Who can go?

**This program is available via** [**summer exchange**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad#summerabroadfaq) **and** [**study abroad**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad#summerabroadfaq) **for students from all faculties\. Spaces for both pathways are very limited\. Generally, the number of applicants exceeds the number of spaces\.** 

***Students who apply to this program for 2026 summer and who are eligible will be matched on a lottery basis***

**All matched students should be prepared to be nominated to either pathways \(**[**summer exchange**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad#summerabroadfaq) **or** [**study abroad**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad#summerabroadfaq)**\), despite their indicated preference\. Switches post\-nomination will not be permitted\.**

-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements
    -   [**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad/eligibility "Eligibility")
    -   [**UBC Okanagan students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-short-term-programs/summer-abroad/)
-   You must be above 18 years of age to participate in this program

## Academic planning

### Areas of study available:

### Course equivalency formula

1 FISS credit \= 1 UBC credit\*

**\*A review of all FISS summer courses was conducted after the summer of 2025 and a new credit equivalency formula is now in place to accurately reflect the number of UBC credits a student will receive\.** 

### Course load information

-   Take up to 6 credits' worth of content courses \(apart from PE and Chinese language courses\)
-   The Chinese language courses \(for foreign passport holders only\) and PE courses are optional\.

### Transfer credit

**The maximum number of UBC credits a student can receive for participation in this program is 6 UBC credits\.** 

-   One FISS course worth 2 FISS credits \= 2 UBC credits
-   Two FISS courses totalling 4 FISS credits \= 4 UBC credits
-   Three FISS courses totalling 6 FISS credits \= 6 UBC credits

**The Vancouver School of Economics will not award Econ credits for any courses taken at Fudan University International Summer School\. UBCV students taking Econ courses will be awarded general Arts credits\. Please consult your Academic Advisor if you are planning to take or transfer Econ courses from your summer experience\.** 

Check out the courses that previous students have transferred back to UBC in the last 5 years using our [Course Search Tool](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)\. If you don't see a particular course you listed, it simply means you need to submit it as a new course request through the [Transfer Credit Portal](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/) while on your Go Global experience\. For more information, please visit the [Go Global Transfer Credit webpage](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global")\.

**Not all courses listed are offered through the summer program at this partner institution\.** The tool is for reference only–research the partner's website for actual course offerings\.

[Search for previously approved courses](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global")

Note: Transfer credits are only granted for courses with academic content that meet the academic standard for UBC\. Courses that do not contain academic coursework may not be eligible\. Please visit [Go Global Transfer Credit webpage](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") to learn more\.
