## Who can go?

-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Unibo Semester 1\): mid September \- mid February
-   UBC Term 2 \(Unibo Semester 2\): mid February \- late July

***\*Students attending term 1 will not be able to return to UBC to attend term 2***

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Education
-   Engineering
-   Forestry
-   Health Sciences
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Science

[Partner Course Information](http://www.unibo.it/en/teaching/degree-programmes)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   30 ECTS per semester

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Most lessons taught in Italian \- students strongly advised to have at least basic knowledge of Italian language \(level A2\)
-   Only some programmes taught entirely in English, and are generally at the graduate level \(please see the [international programme catalogue](http://www.unibo.it/en/teaching/degree-programmes)\)
-   [Italian language courses](https://www.unibo.it/en/international/incoming-exchange-students/exchange-students-unibo-learning-italian) offered for exchange students  \(limited number of places\)
-   Students with B2 level may take Italian\-taught courses \(To determine your level, self\-assess with the [Common European Framework of Reference for Languages \(CEFR\)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)\)

## Finances and funding

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.

-   15 credits or equivalent full\-time program tuition per term, paid to UBC
