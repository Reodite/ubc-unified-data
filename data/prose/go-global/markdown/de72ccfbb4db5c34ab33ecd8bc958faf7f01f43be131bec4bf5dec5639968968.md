## Who can go?

-   Students must have a minimum 73% cumulative average to attend this partnership 

## Academic planning

### Term dates

UBC Term 1 \(St\. Andrews Semester 1\): September to December  
UBC Term 2 \(St\. Andrews Semester 2\): Late January to May

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Students can take a variety of courses within the Faculty of Arts and Science\. Courses taken at the Divinity College may not be available for transfer back to UBC\.
-   St\. Andrews has a suite of [Study Abroad Themes](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/study-abroad/at-st-andrews/studies/what-will-i-study/interdisciplinary/) for exchange students on the topics of Scotland, Sustainability, Travel and Exploration, Science for Arts Majors, and Unique to St Andrews\.
-   Some modules in International Relations can be highly sought after, so it can be wise to have alternative options in mind for module selections\. 

[Partner Course Information](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/subjects/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   St\. Andrews credits divided by 4 \= UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

English is the primary language of instruction

### Other opportunities at this partner

-   The University of St Andrews Global Office is delighted to offer a range of scholarships to inbound students\. The scholarships include several Inbound Study Abroad Scholarships of up to £4,000, which must be used towards either university accommodation or programme fees\. These scholarships are available to exchange students\. For more information and details on how to apply, [please visit their website](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/study-abroad/at-st-andrews/before/fees/)\. 
-   St Andrews offers the opportunity for exchange students to apply for their [Vertical Integrated Projects \(VIPs\)](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/education/vip/)\. VIPs offer students a unique opportunity to engage in meaningful research while abroad and to receive credit\.
