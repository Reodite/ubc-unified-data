## Who can go?

-   Recommended for students in Asian Studies
-   Students with Chinese Citizenship are not eligible 

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Zhejiang Autumn and Winter Semester\): Mid September to Mid January
-   UBC Term 2 \(Zhejiang Spring and Summer Semesters\): Late February to Early July
-   UBC Full Year \(Zhejiang Autumn, Winter, Spring \+ Summer Semester\): September \- July

***\*Please note that if you go on a term 1 exchange to Zhejiang, you will not be able to return to UBC in time for Term 2 classes***

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Engineering
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

Zhejiang University does not publish available courses online\. For a list of courses, please email contact [Go Global](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/contact-us "Contact us ") and we will supply you with a spreadsheet of course listings\. 

[Partner Course Information](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/19598/list.htm)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   20 Zhejiang Credits \= 15 UBC credits 

### Course load Information

-   20 credits per semester \= full time course load

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   There are a limited range of undergraduate and graduate courses taught in English
-   Chinese proficiency required if taking regular classes
-   Students who are fluent in Mandarin may enrol in courses taught in Mandarin

## Finances and funding

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
