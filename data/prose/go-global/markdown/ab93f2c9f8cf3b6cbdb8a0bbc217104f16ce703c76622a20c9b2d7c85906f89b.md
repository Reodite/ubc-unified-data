[Apply](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/3146)

## Who can go?

-   Students must have minimum 72% cumulative average
-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   Law students: Select 'University of Sydney\-LAW'
-   CIE students: Select 'University of Sydney\-CIE’
-   All other students: Select 'University of Sydney\-Uniwide' 

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(USyd Semester 2\): July to November
-   UBC Term 2 \(USyd Semester 1\): February to June
-   UBC Full Year \(USyd Semester 2\+1\): July to June
-   UBC Split Year \(USyd Semester 1\+2\): February to November

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Engineering
-   Health Sciences
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Law
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Exchange Students cannot enroll in postgraduate units of study at the Sydney Law School \(with the exception of Juris Doctor units of study\), or postgraduate units of study offered by Medicine, Pharmacy, Psychology, Physiotherapy or Veterinary Science, or any Dentistry programs\.

[Partner Course Information](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/corporate/documents/study/study-abroad/sydney-abroad-units.pdf)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   Sydney credit points x 0\.625 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   Normal study load per semester 24 credits \(4 units of study\)
-   18 credits minimum per semester \(3 units of study: student visa condition\)
-   30 credits maximum  per semester \(5 units of study\)

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
