## About the program

NTU offers 5 different study tracks during the summer\. Students can take courses from multiple tracks if the timetables work\. The 5 tracks are:

**Language &amp; Cultural Studies**  
Learn Malay and Chinese language

**Entrepreneurship &amp; Innovation**  
Develop fundamental entrepreneurship competencies, business acumen and stamina to build and grow businesses\.

**Creative Design &amp; Media**  
Learn various aspects of visual arts, including watercolor painting, digital media, drawing, communication design, international art perspectives, and contemporary global art concerns\.

**Success in the Globalised Marketplace**  
Offers a diverse range of courses from different disciplines all designed to support students to gain all\-round insights into the global skillsets required for today’s competitive market place\.

**New Technologies, New World**  
Learn about new technologies such as bioprinting and their processes and applications to today's world\.

## Who can go?

**This program is available via** [**summer exchange**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad#summerabroadfaq) **and** [**study abroad**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad#summerabroadfaq) **for students from all faculties\. All students matched to this program should be prepared to be matched to either pathways**

-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements
    -   [**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad/eligibility "Eligibility")
    -   [**UBC Okanagan students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-short-term-programs/summer-abroad/)
-   You must have a minimum cumulative average of 2\.0/4\.0
-   Students of Singapore Citizenship/Singapore Permanent Residency may not be eligible to apply, unless they are given special permission by NTU\. Please contact Go Global if you require assistance with this matter

### Transfer credit

If you don't see a particular course you listed, it simply means you need to submit it as a new course request through the [Transfer Credit Portal](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/) while on your Go Global experience\. For more information, please visit the [Go Global Transfer Credit webpage](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global")\.

**Not all courses listed are offered through the summer program at this partner institution\.** The tool is for reference only–research the partner's website for actual course offerings\.
