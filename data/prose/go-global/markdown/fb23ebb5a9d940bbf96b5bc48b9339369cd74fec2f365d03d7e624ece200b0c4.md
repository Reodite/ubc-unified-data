#### After your time at UBC 

- **Row 1**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **Outstanding fees**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Pay any outstanding fees \(iMED insurance, U\-Pass and any other administrative fees\) so there is no financial hold placed on your account\. We are unable to process your transcript if you have a hold on your account\.

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **Transcripts &amp; credits**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Go Global will send your transcript to your home university about eight weeks after your exchange ends\.   
      
    If you need your transcript earlier, [order it directly from Enrolment Services](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-transcript#:~:text=You%20can%20pick%20up%20your,card%2C%20VISA%2C%20or%20Mastercard.)\. \(You will need to pay a small fee if you choose to order a transcript directly from Enrolment Services\.\)    
      
    If you need your transcripts as part of an application to graduate school or other program, [order a copy through Enrolment Services](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-transcript)\.   
      
    It is up to your home university to determine how the credits you earned at UBC will count towards your degree progression in your program\.

- **Row 3**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **Certificates**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    For students having successfully completed a certificate program, Go Global will send a PDF copy of your certificate along with your transcripts to your home university\. Should you need a copy of your certificate, put in a request with your home institution\.

- **Row 4**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **Tax returns**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Students who have received an award from UBC might need to [file a tax return](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/taxes/filing-taxes-canada) depending on the situation\. [Find out more\.](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/taxes)  
    Info on:   
    [Individual Tax Number \(ITN\)](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/taxes/individual-tax-number-itn) 
    
    [Social Insurance Number \(SIN\)](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/taxes/social-insurance-number-sin)

- **Row 5**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **Summer terms**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Go Global does not support exchange during the summer terms\. If you are interested in extending your stay at UBC, consider [the UBC Vancouver Summer Program](https://vancouversummerprogram.ubc.ca/) or [the Visiting International Research Students \(VIRS\) program](https://global.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/visiting-international-research-students-virs)\.

- **Row 6**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **Research**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    If you are interested in taking on a research project with one of your professors, consider [the Visiting International Research Students \(VIRS\) program](https://global.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/visiting-international-research-students-virs)\. The VIRS program is ideal for students who want to engage in full\-time research studies at UBC under the supervision of a UBC supervisor\.

- **Row 7**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **Faculty of Graduate Study**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    If you want to pursue graduate studies at UBC, please check out opportunities with [the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/faculties/graduate-postdoctoral-studies)\.
