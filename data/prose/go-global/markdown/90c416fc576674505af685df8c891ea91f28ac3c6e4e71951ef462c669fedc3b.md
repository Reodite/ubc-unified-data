##### When can I request graduate courses?

Students \(Undergraduate and Graduate\) wishing to take graduate \(500\+ level\) courses are required to download and complete a separate PDF form\. Refer to [this page](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange/step-4-course-registration/graduate-studies-restrictions) for instructions on how to obtain approval for request into graduate courses\. Go Global cannot register students into graduate level courses until we receive the completed form with **all required signatures** for each graduate course\. You are welcome to begin reaching out to instructors now; however, some instructors may be on leave until closer to the beginning of the Academic Term\. If you don’t receive a response, please follow up again closer to the term start or once you arrive and can visit the department in person\.
