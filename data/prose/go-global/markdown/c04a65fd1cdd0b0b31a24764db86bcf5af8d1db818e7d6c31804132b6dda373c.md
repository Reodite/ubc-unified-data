**Tuition payable to:** UBC

[Apply](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/3146)

## Who can go?

-   You need a minimum cumulative average of 72% to attend this partnership

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Monash semester 2\): Late July to Late November
-   UBC Term 2 \(Monash semester 1\): Late February to Late June
-   UBC Full year \(Monash semesters 2 \+ 1\): July to June
-   UBC Split year \(Monash semesters 1 \+ 2\): February to November

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Engineering
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Avoid Capstone units
-   If you are an undergraduate student, choose Level 1,2, or 3 units\. This is indicated in the unit code\. For e\.g\. MKW 1002 is a Level 1 unit, indicated by the first digit\. You should not choose units that are Level 5 and above\.
-   If you are a postgraduate student, you may choose Level 5 units and above\.

[Partner Course Information](https://handbook.monash.edu/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   8 Monash Points \= 5 UBC Credits

### Course load Information

-   Average load: 24 credit points \(four units/subjects\)
-   18 credit points \(three units/subjects\) is permitted
-   Maximum load: 24 credit points per semester

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   English is the primary language of instruction
