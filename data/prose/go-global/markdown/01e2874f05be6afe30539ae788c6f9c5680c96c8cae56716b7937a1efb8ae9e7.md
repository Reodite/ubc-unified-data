## Who can go?

-   Engineering and Science students

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(RWTH Winter semester\): October \- late March
-   UBC Term 2 \(RWTH Summer semester\): April \- late September

***\*Students attending Winter Semester will not be able to return for Term 2 at UBC***  
***\*Students attending Summer Semester will not be able to return for Term 1 UBC***

### Areas of study available:

-   Engineering
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Students will receive a "Schein" upon completion of each course \(typical of German universities\) \- be sure to keep this for your records as you will be required to provide a copy to Go Global upon your return

[Partner Course Information](http://www.rwth-aachen.de/go/id/yev/lidx/1)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Majority of courses offered are taught in German
-   Students with at least B1 level may take German\-taught classes \(To determine your level, please self\-assess with the [Common European Framework of Reference for Languages \(CEFR\)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)\)

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
