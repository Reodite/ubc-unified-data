#### Programs with Restrictions

#### Engineering courses

To be registered in Engineering courses, you must be applying to be specifically admitted to the Faculty of Applied Science or you must be pursuing a degree in Engineering at your home university\. There is a limited space for exchange students in the Faculty of Applied Science  and registration in Engineering courses is not guaranteed\.

While UBC will make every effort to register Engineering students in their requested courses, registration in requested courses is not guaranteed\. Applicants are encouraged to have a back\-up plan in the event that they are not accepted into their requested courses\.

Engineering courses that are normally restricted to inbound exchange students may be available to students coming to UBC from universities that have a [Coordinated International Experience \(CIE\)](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange/step-1-planning-eligibility/studying-engineering-ubc-exchange-cie "Studying engineering at UBC on exchange (CIE)") student mobility agreement with UBC's Faculty of Applied Science\.

BMEG courses are normally not available\. CIE students may be allowed to take BMEG courses upon request \(not guaranteed\), but they will be evaluated on a case\-by\-case basis\. All requests should be made through Go Global\.

#### Architecture, Landscape Architecture &amp; Design courses

Students wishing to take studio courses with the School of Architecture and Landscape Architecture \(SALA\) must be nominated during the Round 1 \(Jan 15\-Mar 15\) nomination period\. 

Students should check [the additional required documents](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange/step-2-application-process "Application and admission process") and submit them to register for courses in the School of Architecture and Landscape Architecture\. 

##### **Architecture \(ARCH\)**

###### Architecture

Graduate\-level courses open to students in the final year of an undergraduate Architecture degree or to students enrolled in another Master of Architecture program\. Course and studio registration is limited and subject to application review and approval\.

##### Landscape Architecture \(LARC\)

Graduate\-level courses open to students in the final year of an undergraduate degree in a related design discipline or to students enrolled in another Master of Landscape Architecture program\. Course and studio registration is limited and subject to application review and approval\.

##### Design \(DES\)

Students must be enrolled in the third or fourth year of a comparable undergraduate program in a design discipline\. DES 110, 130, 200, 220, and 230 are open to exchange students\. Registration in other courses, including studio courses, is limited and will be reviewed on a case\-by\-case basis\.
