Visiting International Research Students \(VIRS\) are not charged UBC tuition, but are required to pay two separate fees:  

1.  VIRS program fee
2.  iMED health insurance 

##### VIRS program fee

All applicants are required to pay a nonrefundable $440\.00\* program fee while submitting the application\. This fee covers administrative costs and grants access to a limited range of student support services upon acceptance\*\*\. If you choose to extend your research period beyond one year from your original start date, a second $440\.00 fee will apply\. 

To pay the fee, follow the instructions outlined in "Ready to apply" on the [VIRS Application for Admission](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/2627)\.

Host faculty supervisors sometimes choose to pay this fee for the student\. If this is the case, ask your UBC research supervisor to contact Go Global to arrange payment\. 

\*Note: The VIRS program fee is subject to change\.

\*\*Note: Your iMED fee is additional and not part of the VIRS program fee\.  

##### iMED health insurance

**iMED is mandatory for all VIRS students\.** After acceptance, all VIRS students will be registered with a VIRS course code which automatically enrolls you in the mandatory iMED insurance program adjusted to the duration of your program\.   

[iMED](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international#how-do-i-use-my-imed-insurance) is a temporary basic health insurance plan required for new international students and gives you health insurance coverage during the three\-month waiting period for BC's Medical Services Plan \(MSP\) or the duration of your study period if you are not eligible for MSP\. It covers doctors' visits, most emergencies, some medications and other services\.

###### iMED insurance fees 

The iMED fee depends on the duration of your research period\. 

**If your research period is one to six months,** you will be assessed $68\.10 per month*​\*​​*, in addition to a $11\.15\* direct billing fee\. For example, students studying for five months pay $351\.65 

**If your research period is longer than six months,** you must apply for a BC Medical Services Plan \(MSP\) for coverage\. However, there is a three\-month waiting period before you receive MSP coverage\. So during this three\-month waiting period, you are insured by iMED\. Your costs are $204\.3\* for the first three months, in addition to $11\.15\* for the direct billing fee for your iMED and the monthly premium for MSP for the following months\.  

You are charged for iMED in advance of your arrival, but this does not mean you are paying for the months you are not in Canada\. Read "Coverage Dates" \(below\) ​​​to understand how your fees align with your research dates\.   
  
*\*This rate applies to students arriving at UBC from late August 2026 onwards\. Subject to change from year to year*

###### Paying iMED insurance fees 

iMED fees will be charged to your tuition account at UBC\. Your tuition account can be found under "Financial Summary" in your [UBC Workday Student](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/finances/viewing-your-financial-account-activity-tuition-amount-and-payments/) account\. Please use your Campus Wide Login \(CWL\) to login and pay iMED by the listed deadline\. Options for payment can be found [here](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/paying-tuition/)\.

**It is your responsibility to pay your fees by the deadline\.** UBC will notify you if you miss the deadline about "overdue fees\." This could result in:

-   Accruing interest charges on outstanding balances
-   Placing you on “financial hold”  

If you cannot make these payments on time, contact us at [go\.global@ubc\.ca](mailto:go.global@ubc.ca)\. Students who need to withdraw should also contact Go Global asap\. Failure to inform us in a timely manner will result in you owing iMED fees, regardless of whether you have arrived in Canada or not\.

###### Coverage dates 

*Applicable to all VIRS students arriving at UBC from late August 2026 onwards:*

IMED coverage for VIRS students are defaulted to the below start dates to better align with UBC's academic terms\. 

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | Research duration of 3 months or &gt;6 months | Research duration of 1,2,4,5, or 6 months |
| Arrival by Term 1 \(Sept\. – Dec\.\) | August 10 | September 1 |
| Arrival by Term 2 \(Jan\. \- April\) | December 10 | January 1 |
| Arrival by Summer \(May – August\) | April 10 | May 1 |

**However, most VIRS students' research dates do not align with UBC's academic terms\. Most likely you will need to request to have your** [**coverage dates updated**](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international#changing-your-imed-coverage-dates)\. Once you have been enrolled in the insurance plan, you will be notified by the insurance provider via email\. Login to [your account](https://myaccount.guard.me/login.php) to double check your coverage dates\. If they do not align with your official research start date, request for an update by using the 'Date Change Request' function \([navigate to 'Go Global &gt; Date Change Request'](https://sites.guard.me/ubc/page/go-global/date-change-request)\)\. The standard submission deadline is at least 3 weeks before your arrival\.  
  
**Failure to submit your date change request in a timely manner will result in coverage that does not align with your research dates, which can mean added costs or not being properly covered during your visit\.**

###### Receiving your policy details and Healthcare Access Card

Once enrolled, VIRS students will receive a 'Welcome Message' from the insurance provider via email\. This happens at different times throughout the year, depending on your arrival date\. The 'Welcome Message' will provide details on accessing your policy and Healthcare Access Card\. 

If you have paid your iMED fees and you have not received this information before arriving in Canada: 

1.  Search "GuardMe Enhanced@UBC iMed \- Healthcare Access Card" in your email\.
2.  Check your email spam or junk folder\.
3.  If you cannot find this email, contact [go\.global@ubc\.ca](mailto:go.global@ubc.ca)\. Include your name and UBC student number\. We will check your status\. 

###### Opt\-out options 

Typically, having coverage under a different private health insurance plan or a government health plan from your home country does not make you eligible to opt\-out of iMED\. There are very limited circumstances in which a VIRS student may opt\-out of iMED\. Please feel welcome to contact us if you have questions about this\.

##### Budgeting

British Columbia and Vancouver have a high cost of living\. VIRS student are not eligible for UBC housing\. Plan your budget and consider costs related to off\-campus housing, food, travel, tourism, personal expenses and money to cover contingencies\. 

Include in your budget: 

-   Return flight costs
-   Visa and study permit application costs
-   Clothing for each season in British Columbia
-   Costs of your accommodation, which can vary depending on where you live, whether you'll live with roommates \(flat\-mates\) or live on your own, and the quality of the housing
-   Food\. Will you prepare your own meals or dine out?
-   What about sightseeing and entertainment? Will you be visiting many places in the area? What will your social life be like?
-   Your transportation fees\. A monthly [1\-zone transit pass](https://www.translink.ca/transit-fares/pricing-and-fare-zones) costs a minimum of $104\.90/month\.
-   Cell phone fees\. Canada has some of the most expensive mobile phone plans in the world\. Plan for at least $50\-60/month\. 

Plan on **at least $2,200 CAD** per month to live in Vancouver\.  

Financial requirements vary depending on individual goals, the cost of living in Vancouver and personal preferences\. To get a clear financial picture of your studies at UBC, create a budget with both university and living costs\. Use this [sample budget worksheet \(PDF\)](https://global.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/Exchange%20Student%20Budget%20Worksheet.pdf) to estimate your monthly expenses for exchange\. Fill in the table with realistic amounts for each category that applies to you\.
