#### Before arriving at UBC

1.  Make sure you've completed the [admission and acceptance](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange/step-2-application-process "Application and admission process") procedures and have copies of your Letter of Acceptance available\.
2.  Ensure you have the **required immigration documents** to enter into and study in Canada\. We recommend preparing ahead of time so that you are ready to submit your applications as soon as you receive your admission because some may take months to process:
    1.  [Find out what documents you need to study in Canada](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration) and check [IRCC processing times](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html)
    2.  [Read the Travelling to Canada Guide for international students before making travel plans](https://students.ubc.ca/new-to-ubc/travelling-to-canada)
    3.  Enroll in and complete the [iPrep Canvas course for "Exchange and non\-degree seeking students"](http://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/NCK6XM), an online pre\-arrival course specifically designed for new exchange and visiting students to learn tips about their immigration applications and health insurance
3.  Make sure you have your **iMED health insurance card** from [GuardMe](https://sites.guard.me/ubc/department/go-global)\. You will receive your iMED card via email, within two weeks before your study term begins\. Check your junk mail folder periodically to make sure you didn't miss it\. **Keep a copy of the iMED card with you** at all times or download the [GuardMe mobile app](https://news.guard.me/GuardMe_Mobile_App.html)\. [Learn more about how to use your iMED insurance](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international#how-do-i-use-my-imed-insurance)\.
4.  **Register** with the [**UBC Centre for Accessibility**](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/registering-centre-accessibility) if you anticipate needing academic or exam accommodations during your exchange\.
5.  **Attend pre\-arrival sessions** and **sign up for a campus tour\.** We will provide more information closer to your exchange term\.
6.  Review our [Inbound Student Welcome Booklet](https://canva.link/ms80fll4v2m6i4t) as well as the [Inbound Canvas Modules](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/120619) for tips, guides, resources and more\!
7.  [Contact Go Global](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/3122) if you are arriving late for your study at UBC\. Check the term and other key dates [here](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/2129)\. 

#### After arriving at UBC

1.  Get your [**UBC Student Card**](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/) and transit **Compass Card** at the UBC Bookstore\. **Link your Compass Card** to [U\-pass BC](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/transit/u-pass-compass-card) every month\.
2.  **If you arrive in the calendar month after your iMED coverage begins, submit an** [**iMED Date Change Request**](https://sites.guard.me/ubc/page/go-global/date-change-request) directly through GuardMe\.
3.  You are legally required to enroll in MSP if you plan to live in the British Columbia \(BC\) province for six months or longer\. **Apply for MSP as soon as** you arrive in BC\. Read [how to apply for BC MSP](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international/apply-bc-medical-services-plan-msp)\.
4.  **Attend Go Global’s orientation sessions** and a **campus tour\.** We will share more information closer to your exchange term\.
5.  [**Pay your student fees**](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/paying-tuition/) before the UBC [deadline](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/dates-deadlines/)\. A student will be put on [**financial hold**](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/tuition-fees/late-payment-fees/) if there are outstanding fees\.
6.  **Check with your home university** to ensure you meet program/scholarship/loan requirements at home\. **Finalize your courses** before [the course add/drop deadline](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/dates-deadlines)\.
7.  If living in a student residence, **review your housing contract** and **pay your housing fees** [by the deadline](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/)\.
8.  Sign up to receive the [*International Community Update* newsletter](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/pages/e1707daf47fe5d0aec7695d91e4ad59a)\.
