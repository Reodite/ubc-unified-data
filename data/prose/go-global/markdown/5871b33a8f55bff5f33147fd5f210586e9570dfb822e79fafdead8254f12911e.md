[Apply](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/3146)

## Who can go?

-   Students from the Faculty of Arts

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Sciences Po Fall semester\): mid\-September \- mid\-January
-   UBC Term 2 \(Sciences Po Spring semester\): mid\-January \- late\-May
-   UBC Full Year \(Sciences Po Fall \+ Spring semester\): September \- May

***\*Term 1 exchange students taking the Certificate of French and European Studies finish exams in December and can return to UBC for Term 2\.***

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Courses are offered in English through the [Diploma of French and European Studies](https://en.sciencespo-lyon.fr/students/study-opportunities/programmes-for-international-students/)
-   Regular courses in French are the [standard Sciences Po Lyon curriculum](https://www.sciencespo-lyon.fr/formations/cursus-en-5-ans/1ere-2eme-et-3eme-annee)\.

[Partner Course Information](https://en.sciencespo-lyon.fr/students/study-opportunities/programmes-for-international-students/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   2 ECTS \= 1 UBC credit

### Course load Information

-   30 ECTS per term

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Courses taught in English are available in political science and history
-   Certain courses are offered in English through the [Diploma of French and European Studies](https://www.sciencespo-lyon.fr/sites/default/files/article/documents/2022/brochure_dfes_22-23_0.pdf)
-   Students who wish to take French\-taught academic courses \(other than French language study\) must have a minimum language level of B2 \(self\-assess with: [Common European Framework of Reference for Languages \(CEFR\)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)\)

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
