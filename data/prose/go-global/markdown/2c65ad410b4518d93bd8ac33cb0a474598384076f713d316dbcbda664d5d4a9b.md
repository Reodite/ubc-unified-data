##### What is iMED?

[iMED](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international/) is a temporary basic health insurance that is automatically triggered by course registration for international students and provided by [GuardMe](https://sites.guard.me/ubc)\.  

To ensure the health and safety of international students, iMED was implemented years ago, after a number of instances in which international or exchange students incurred large medical costs in Canada due to not having sufficient insurance coverage\. It is mandatory for all incoming exchange and visiting students who are not covered by a Canadian provincial government health care plan\. International students will be covered by iMED in addition to whatever other insurance they may have\. Go Global students with Canadian citizenship may choose to opt\-in through [GuardMe](https://sites.guard.me/ubc) if they do not have other similar basic health insurance coverage\.

With your iMED card, you won’t have to pay for your visit if you see a doctor at the Student Health Service at UBC or the University Village Medical and Dental Clinic in the Village on campus\. Also, iMED covers emergency hospitalization and medical services \(including outpatient treatment at a doctor's clinic\) for an unexpected sickness or injury\. See this [page](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international) for more information\.
