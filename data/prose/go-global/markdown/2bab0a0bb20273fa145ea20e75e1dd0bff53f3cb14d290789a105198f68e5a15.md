## Who can go?

-   Only students within the Faculty of Land and Food Systems are eligible to apply to the Agro3 Consortium
-   Students may select from:
    -   [ISA Junia](http://www.junia.com/en/junia/international/academics-exchanges/), located in Lille, France
    -   [École d’Ingenieurs ISARA \(ISARA Lyon\)](https://isara.fr/en/study-at-isara/), located in Lyon, France
    -   [École d’Ingenieurs de Purpan \(EI Purpan\)](http://www.purpan.fr/en/) located in Toulouse, France
-   When applying to Go Global: please apply to the specific institution location within the consortium that you wish to attend:
    -   FA3 Lille\-LFS\-STUDY
    -   FA3 ISARA Lyon\-LFS\-STUDY
    -   FA3 Toulouse\-LFS\-STUDY

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Agro3 Fall semester\): September to Mid January
-   UBC Term 2 \(Agro3 Spring semester\): January to Mid June

### Areas of study available:

-   Forestry
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Refer to the website below for course information\. Sort by campus, language, and topic\.  
-   **ISA Junia**
    -   [Courses](https://www.junia.com/en/academics/) in Agricultural Science, Food Science, Marketing Management, and Environmental Science
-   **ISARA**
    -   All courses in the [Agroecology](https://isaranet.fr/eduprog/DownloadFile?idtemplate=303&amp;up=S9_AGROECOS&amp;lang=en&amp;form=ING) and [Sustainable Food Systems](https://isaranet.fr/eduprog/DownloadFile?idtemplate=303&amp;up=S9-SDFI&amp;lang=en&amp;form=ING) program are taught in English
-   **PURPAN\***
    -   [IBC \- International Bachelor Course](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/media/190) focuses on Winemaking, Wine Business, Food Science and Innovation and is taught in English\.
    -   [Global Semester](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/media/188) focuses on a wide range of issues including climate change, gender inequality, and food security and is taught in English\.
    -   [Ingenieur](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/media/189) courses are taught in French\. French language proficiency required to take courses taught in French\.
    -   \*Please note, the course lists are based on the 2024\-5 offerings and are subject to change from year to year\.

[Course options for exchange](https://www.franceagro3.org/en/find-a-course/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

30 ECTS \= 15 UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)
