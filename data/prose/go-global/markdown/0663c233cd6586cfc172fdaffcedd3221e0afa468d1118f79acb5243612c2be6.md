##### I have not received my iMED Card\.

Students who have been registered into at least one course in the coming term will receive their iMED welcome message with details about accessing their GuardMe portal account and iMED Healthcare Access Card via email prior to arrival in Canada\. Please check your email “junk” or “spam” folders to see if the email was filtered there\. If you do not find it and will be arriving in Canada within a week, please request to have the welcome email resent to you by contacting GuardMe, the iMED insurance provider, at [customercare@guard\.me](mailto:customercare@guard.me)\. Please include your name, UBC student number, date of arrival and put "missing iMED card" in the subject field\.
