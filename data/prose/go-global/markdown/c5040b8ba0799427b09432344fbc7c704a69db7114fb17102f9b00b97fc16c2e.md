## Who can go?

-   Exchange students can take coursework in many areas of study

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Tsukuba Fall semester\): Early October to Late March
-   UBC Term 2 \(Tsukuba Spring semester\): Early April \- Late September
    -   *classes end in August, students are able to return to UBC for the following term*

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Engineering
-   Forestry
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   UBC students will be participating in the Junior Year \(Third Year\) at University of Tsukuba, Faculty of Arts programs
-   [Information on Japanese Language Courses for international students](https://www.tsukuba.ac.jp/en/academics/international-jp-courses/)
-   When selecting courses, ensure that they are available in the semester for which you are applying

[Partner Course Information](http://www.tsukuba.ac.jp/en/study-tsukuba/under-graduate/ug-courses)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   Same as UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search/external?external_id=231&amp;title=&amp;subject_code=&amp;course_number=)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   For undergraduate level short\-term exchange students, Tsukuba offers classes in English through the Junior Year at Tsukuba \(JTP\) Program \(not available to graduate students\)
-   JTP offers over 60 classes available in areas such as international relations, education, psychology, law, comparative cultures, Japanese language and culture, biology, and engineering
-   For more information regarding coursework in English please see: [Courses in English](http://www.tsukuba.ac.jp/en/study-tsukuba/under-graduate/ug-courses)
-   Japanese language courses are also available throughout the semester \- see: [International Student Center](https://www.tsukuba.ac.jp/en/academics/international-jp-courses/)
-   Students take courses in Japanese and English in the following disciplines: international relations, education, psychology, law, comparative cultures, Japanese language and culture, biology, and engineering

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
