## Who can go?

-   Only COMM \(UBCV\) are eligible to apply to this partner university

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(ESSEC semester 1\): Early September \- late December
-   UBC Term 2 \(ESSEC semester 2\): Late February \- mid June
-   UBC Full Year \(ESSEC semester 1 \+2\): September \- June

As of Spring 2020, the ESSEC Singapore campus is open to exchange students\. The ESSEC Singapore campus is only available for Term 2 exchanges\.

As of Fall 2026, the Rabat, Morocco ESSC campus is open to exchange students\. The Morocco campus is available for Term 1 and/or 2 exchanges\. 

### Areas of study available:

-   Commerce \(UBCV\)

### Course availability and restrictions

-   As of Spring 2020, the ESSEC Singapore campus is open to exchange students\. Please read the information [available here\.](https://www.essec.edu/en/essec-asia-pacific-en/)
-   As of Fall 2026, the Rabat, Morocco ESSC campus is open to exchange students\. Please see more information [available here\.](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cHJYJ7Mk2ZiRS5UADsTAkG2CnAVZ3KFV/view) 

[Partner Course Information](https://sites.google.com/a/essec.edu/bba-incoming-international-exchange/academic-info)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   Minimum: 15 ECTS / semester
-   Maximum: 30 ECTS / semester
-   Full time course load for regular students: 30 ECTS per semester

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Incoming exchange students work with 3rd and 4th year undergraduates and have a choice of management classes taught in French and/or English
-   It is possible to take a study program taught entirely in English
-   Students must have at least a B1 or B2 level to study in French \(please self\-asses with: [Common European Framework of Reference for Languages \(CEFR\)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)\)
