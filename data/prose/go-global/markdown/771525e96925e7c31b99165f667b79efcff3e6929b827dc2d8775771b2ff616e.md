## Who can go?

-   Only Commerce students \(Vancouver\) may study at the KU Leuven [Faculty of Economics and Business](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/admissions/exchange)
-   Arts, LFS and Forestry students may study in the [Faculty of Arts](https://www.arts.kuleuven.be/english/education/leuven) and [Faculty of Bioscience](https://onderwijsaanbod.kuleuven.be/2024/opleidingen/e/SC_51315093.htm#activetab=voorwaarden)

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(KU Leuven Semester 1\): mid\-September to late January \(exams in January can sometimes be written by distance\)
-   UBC Term 2 \(KU Leuven Semester 2\): Early February to early July  
     
-   Students on exchange in term 1 may be able to write some exams in December
    
-   Exams not available in December may sometimes be written in January at UBC
    
-   Coordination of invigilation and any related costs are the responsibility of the student \(please contact Go Global at least 4 weeks before the exam\)
    

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Forestry
-   Land and Food Systems

### Course availability and restrictions

-   ARTS students may participate in [PECS Program](http://www.arts.kuleuven.be/pecs/), and may also enroll in non\-PECS courses, and choose from any of the 3 campuses
-   LFS and Forestry students may study in the [Faculty of Bioscience Engineering](https://www.biw.kuleuven.be/en) and Arts
    -   The Faculty of Bioscience Engineering has 7 MSc programmes taught in English\. Exchange students are able to pursue these programs given that they have sufficient academic background\.
    -   See the list of [Courses for Exchange Students in the Faculty of Bioscience Engineering \(Leuven\) \- Programme structure \| KU Leuven](https://onderwijsaanbod.kuleuven.be/opleidingen/e/SC_51315093.htm)
    -   Exchange students pursuing studies in the Faculty of Bioscience Engineering are required to take 50% of their coursework in the Faculty of Bioscience Engineering\. The other 50% of coursework may be taken in other faculties of KU Leuven\.
-   Study in the [Faculty of Economics &amp; Business \(FEB\)](https://feb.kuleuven.be/eng/international/comingtofebonexchange/comingtofebonexchange) is open to COMM students only
-   COMM students may also choose from the main campus \(Leuven\), Antwerp or Brussels:[https://feb\.kuleuven\.be/eng/international/information\-package/courses](https://feb.kuleuven.be/eng/international/information-package/courses)
    -   **Leuven**: Courses in economics and finance tend to be very analytical \(bachelor students should be well versed in mathematics/statistics\)
    -   **Antwerp and Brussels**: Courses have a more hands\-on approach, with slightly less focus on quantitative skills and slightly more on management skills
        -   Antwerp: Undergraduate and Graduate \(Master of Science in Business Administration\) programs available for incoming exchange students
        -   Brussels: English\-taught Bachelor and Master of Science in Business Administration, an English\-taught Master of Science in Business Engineering as well as a MSc in International Business Economics and Management

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   Minimum: 27 ECTS per semester
-   Maximum: 33 ECTS per semester

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Other opportunities at this partner

**\*NEW\* Erasmus\+ Scholarship \(KA171\)**

-   The [Erasmus\+ Programme](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities) provides funding for student, faculty and staff mobility between institutions in the European Union and their international partners\.
-   Two scholarships are provided by the Erasmus\+ Programme of the European Union
    -   One will be awarded to a student nominated to a one\-term exchange at KU Leuven during the 2027/8 academic year within the Faculty of Arts 
    -   One will be awarded to a student nominated to a one\-term exchange at KU Leuven during the 2027/8 academic year within the Faculty of Economics and Business
-   Scholarship Details:
    -   Monthly grant: 900 Euro/month
    -   One\-off travel grant: 1188 Euro
-   Additional funding is possible based on the factors outlined [here](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/E%2B%20Top%20Up_Wageningen%20University_1.pdf): 250 Euro/month
-   Students who are matched to KU Leuven will be contacted by Go Global with further details on how to be considered for this award
