## Who can go?

-   This partner university is open to most fields of study
-   You must have a 75% cumulative average to attend this institution
-   You must have at least 3rd year standing at the time of your exchange to be considered for this institution

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(UTokyo Winter Semester\): early October \- late February
-   UBC Term 2 \(UTokyo Summer Semester\): early April \- late July 

### Areas of study available:

-   Architecture and Landscape Architecture
-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Engineering
-   Forestry
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Students may enroll in an undergraduate course either as a regular student or a research student
-   For more information on the USTEP program, [click here](http://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/academics/ustep.html)

[Partner Course Information](http://catalog.he.u-tokyo.ac.jp/ug-index?usep=true)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   2 U of T Credit \= 1\.5 UBC credit

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search/external?external_id=229&amp;title=&amp;subject_code=&amp;course_number=)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Large variety of English taught course work as a part of the USTEP program\.
