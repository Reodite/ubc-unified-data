**Location:** Warsaw, Poland  
**Language of instruction:** English, Polish  
**Tuition payable to:** UBC  
**Program website:** [Warsaw School of Economics Exchange Website](http://administracja.sgh.waw.pl/en/dpm/international_exchange/incoming_students/exchange/Pages/default.aspx)  
**Terms available:** Term 1, Term 2, Full year Term 1 \+ 2 \(UBC Term 1 start\), Split year Term 2 \+1 \(UBC Term 2 start\)

[Apply](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/3146)

## Who can go?

-   Students from the Sauder School of Business

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(SGH Fall Semester\): End of September to Early February
-   UBC Term 2 \(SGH Spring Semester\): Mid\-February to End of June

**\*UBC students attending the Fall Semester may be able to write exams in December, permission must be secured from professors at SGH**

### Areas of study available:

-   Commerce \(UBCV\)

[Course Information Website](http://administracja.sgh.waw.pl/en/dpm/international_exchange/incoming_students/exchange/Pages/Courses_at_SGH.aspx)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   6 ECTS \= 3 UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   60 ECTS per academic year, or 30 ECTS per term

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)
