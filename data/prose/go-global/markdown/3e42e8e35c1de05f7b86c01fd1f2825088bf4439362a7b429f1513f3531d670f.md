##### The courses I need for a certificate are restricted\. Does this mean this certificate is no longer available? Or, how do I get access or permission to these courses?

In general, Go Global is not able to guarantee registration in any specific course, even if it is a requirement\. Although there are typically spaces for TEC\-JAP\-Tec de Monterrey Joint Academic Program students who need to meet certificate requirements\. Be sure to include restricted courses in your final course requests\.
