##### Do students coming to UBC for a Split Year exchange need to request courses for both Term 2 and the following Term 1 on the survey?

No\. Split Year students should request courses for Term 2 only on the course survey\. The following Winter Term 1 is the beginning of a different academic year at UBC \(2027\-28\), so courses are not yet available\. Go Global will reach out with another course survey in early summer for your Term 1 course requests\.
