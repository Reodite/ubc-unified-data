Living abroad is a life\-changing experience, but it can also be daunting\. Ease the transition for students venturing overseas by volunteering as a Global Ambassador\. Share your experience, hone new skills, and participate in fun events to promote an intercultural community on campus\!

##### About Global Ambassadors

Global Ambassadors are past participants of Go Global programs—such as Exchange, Global Seminars, Summer Abroad, and Research Abroad—who are passionate about international learning\. They play a vital role in supporting and inspiring fellow UBC students by sharing their experiences, offering guidance, and helping others feel confident and prepared for their own global journeys\.

###### Responsibilities &amp; duties

### **Role Summary**

As a Global Ambassador, you’ll help inspire and prepare fellow UBC students to embark on their own international learning journeys\. You'll represent the Go Global program, share your experience, and connect with students through events, social media, and peer support\. This is a unique opportunity to grow as a leader, communicator, and intercultural connector on campus\.

 [Image](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/IMG_2183.JPEG)

**Key Responsibilities**

-   Mentor prospective Go Global students by sharing your personal international learning experience\.
-   Raise campus awareness of Go Global through booths, information sessions and various events\.
-   Participate in Pre\-Departure sessions and serve on peer panels\.
-   Provide support during application drop\-in sessions\.
-   Co\-facilitate Go Global info sessions alongside staff or fellow ambassadors\.
-   Write a [**Global Exchange Insider**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/student-experiences/student-stories) post highlighting your exchange experience for Go Global’s website and social media\.
-   Engage with students on the Go Global UBC Pre\-Departure Canvas Modules by answering questions and sharing insights\.
-   Lead campus tours for visiting partner institutions when needed\.

###### Who can apply? 

-   Current **UBC Vancouver students** who will be enrolled during the 2026\-27 academic year
-   Students who have participated in a Go Global program \(e\.g\., Exchange, Summer Abroad, Research Abroad, Global Seminars\)
-   Applicants who have completed the [Go Global Student Report](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/student-experiences) will receive priority consideration in the selection process\. Completion of the report demonstrates engagement with the exchange experience and a commitment to supporting future students\. Please note that the content of your Student Report will not be evaluated as part of the Global Ambassador selection process\. We value honesty and reflection in the report itself, and applicants are not expected to tailor or revise their report for this role\.

##### **Resources for UBC Faculty, Staff &amp; Community**

**How can I include Global Ambassadors in my event?**  
To request the volunteer support of UBC’s Go Global Ambassador team, UBC faculty, staff, and community members are welcome to email **go\.global@ubc\.ca**\. Please note that requests are considered based on Ambassador availability, the timing of the opportunity, and how well it aligns with the goals of the Ambassador role\.

**Recommend a student to become a Go Global Ambassador**  
If you know a current UBC student who would be a great fit for the Go Global Ambassador Program, please send an email to [go\.global@ubc\.ca](mailto:go.global@ubc.ca) with their name, contact information, and a short explanation of why you believe they would make a strong ambassador\.
