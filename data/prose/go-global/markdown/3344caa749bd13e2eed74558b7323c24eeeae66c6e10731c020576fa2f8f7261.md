## About the program

DIS Summer gives you an opportunity to take academically challenging courses while experiencing life in Copenhagen or Stockholm \(or both\!\) over the summer months\. Sessions of study include:

-   Session 1 \(3\-weeks\)
-   Session 2 \(3\-weeks\)
-   Session 3 \(3\-weeks\)
-   Architecture and Design Session \(6\-weeks\)
-   Furniture Design Session \(6\-weeks\)
-   [Arctic Experiential Studies: Greenland](https://disabroad.org/arctic-summer-session/) \(6\-weeks\)

Each session has a diverse selection of courses\. Students can stay for one session and take one course, or combine sessions and take up to 3 courses\. You can even mix and match sessions between DIS Copenhagen and DIS Stockholm in order to experience two Scandinavian capitals in one summer\. Depending on the session and course, you may also take part in week\-long study tours in different parts of Europe, which are integrated into coursework\.

**Students interested in the Labs, Research &amp; Practicums Session should apply via the** [**Research Abroad Program**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/research-abroad "Research Abroad")\.

More specific and finalized program details can be found on the partner institution's website\. However, you must apply through Go Global to be considered for this program and ensure you follow the appropriate transfer credit process\.

**This program is available via** [**study abroad**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad#summerabroadfaq) **for students from all faculties\. Space for study abroad may be limited depending on the number of applicants**

-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements
    -   [**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad/eligibility "Eligibility")
    -   [**UBC Okanagan Students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-short-term-programs/summer-abroad/)
-   You must have a minimum cumulative average of 3\.0/4\.0 \(73%\) to participate in this program

## Finances and funding

**Tuition and Fees**

You will pay tuition directly to the partner institution\. All prices quoted on the partner's website are in USD\. 

UBC students will receive a $600 USD discount per 3\-credit session and $1200 USD discount per 6 credit session\. There is also an additional discount for combining multiple summer sessions of the program\. For details, please contact Go Global\.

The partner institution may charge other fees\. **Please check the program website for details of program costs\.**
