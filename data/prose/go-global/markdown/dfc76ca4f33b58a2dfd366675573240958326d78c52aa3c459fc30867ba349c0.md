## Who can go?

-   Students must have a minimum 72% cumulative academic average and be entering 3rd year or above to be eligible
-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements
    -   [**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/eligibility "Eligibility")
    -   [**UBC Okanagan students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-exchange-program/eligibility/)

*Caution:*

-   *There is limited space for exchange \(pay tuition to UBC\) at UPF, students have the option to attend on a Study Abroad basis, paying tuition to UPF \(*[*fees*](http://www.upf.edu/web/studyabroad/fees) *are outlined on their website\)*
-   *Commerce students may not select the study abroad option*

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   Commerce students: select 'Universitat Pompeu Fabra\-Commerce\-STUDY'  application\.
-   Exchange \(pay full courseload tuition to UBC\): select 'Universitat Pompeu Fabra\-UniWide\-STUDY'
-   Study Abroad \(pay [fees](https://www.upf.edu/web/studyabroad/fees) directly to UPF\): select "Universitat Pompeu Fabra\-Study Abroad\-STUDY'

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(UPF Fall term\): Mid September to Mid December
-   UBC Term 2 \(UPF Winter and Spring terms\): Early January to July

***\*Students matched to UPF may shorten term 2 exchange to only the Winter term \(January \- March\), please contact Go Global advisor to select the Winter term only option prior to September\.*** 

See the UPF Calendar [here](https://www.upf.edu/web/incoming/exchange-students-academic-information?rld=f) \(click "Academic Calendar"\)\.

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Students with a fluent level of Spanish can take regular courses offered by the university
-   For more information about accessing regular courses taught in Spanish or Catalan, click [here](https://www.upf.edu/web/incoming/exchange-students-academic-information)
-   Please note the ECTS credit load for regular Spanish courses courses is different than the ECTS credit load for the HESP program
-   Courses in Communication, Audiovisual Communication, Journalism and Economics are also offered to Exchange students at this institution

[Partner Course Information](https://www.upf.edu/web/studyabroad/academic-programs)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Classes can also be taken in Catalan
-   Students should be able to take notes and participate in classroom activities on a par with Spanish nationals \(To determine your level, please self\-assess with: [Common European Framework of Reference for Languages \(CEFR\)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)\)
-   UPF offers some regular academic courses in English and a Spanish Language and Culture Program \(HESP\)
-   No previous knowledge of Spanish is required for these options

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.   

-   15 credits or equivalent full\-time program tuition per term, paid to UBC \(exchange\) or fees paid directly to UPF \(study abroad\)
