## Who can go?

-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study\. 

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(PUC Semester 2\): Early August to Mid December
-   UBC Term 2 \(PUC Semester 1\): Early March to Mid July
-   UBC Full Year \(PUC Semester 2 \+1\): August \- July
-   UBC Split Year \(PUC semester 1 \+ 2\): March \- December

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Fine Arts
-   Forestry
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Exchange students cannot take courses in specific subject areas \(for more information on restricted programs of study, see [here](http://relacionesinternacionales.uc.cl/en/international-students/exchange-and-mobility-programs/undergraduate-exchange-program#restricted-programs-of-study)\)

[Partner Course Information](https://admision.uc.cl/carreras/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   PUC 3\.33 \= UBC 1\.0 credits
-   Language Courses \- 6 credits \(PUCC\) \= 3 UBC Credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Mainly Spanish and limited English courses
-   Spanish Language Proficiency Assessment is required
-   PUC requires that four semesters worth of Spanish be previously taken at UBC or a language proficiency form signed by an instructor of Spanish from the Department of French, Hispanic, and Italian Studies
-   One optional intermediate level Spanish Course for is offered in March to exchange students free of charge \(recommended for those who do not pass the diagnostic Spanish test\)
-   Advanced level Spanish Course is also offered for a cost of US$350

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
