UBC alum Saanvi Bhambhani never expected her academic path to span continents—but curiosity has always been her compass\. Growing up in New Delhi, India, she was drawn to new ideas, unfamiliar places and the promise of perspectives beyond her own\. That curiosity eventually led her to UBC, thousands of miles from home, and from there to Amsterdam, making her journey to the graduating class of 2025 with a degree in political science a truly international voyage\.
