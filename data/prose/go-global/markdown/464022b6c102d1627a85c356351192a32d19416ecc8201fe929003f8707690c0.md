##### Course X is “closed” in the Course Schedule, can I request it?

If a course is “closed” on the Course Schedule or Workday, it is not currently available for registration\. We don’t know when or if closed courses may open, so we advise not to request closed courses on the Course Survey for now\. If you’d like to enquire about whether a particular course will be offered, you can reach out to the department directly\. If the course does open later, you will be able to request to switch into it later during our course add/drop/switch period after the initial registration is complete\.
