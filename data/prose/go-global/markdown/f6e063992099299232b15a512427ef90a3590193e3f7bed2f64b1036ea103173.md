## Who can go?

-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study
    
-   Students must have completed 1 year of continuous study at their home institution before participating in the exchange
    
-   Students must have a minimum 70% cumulative average to attend this partnership
    

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   Commerce students \(UBC\-V\): Select 'Cardiff University\-COMM'
-   All other students: Select 'Cardiff University\-Uniwide' 

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Cardiff Semester 1\): Late September to Late January
-   UBC Term 2 \(Cardiff Semester 2\): Late January to Mid June

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Health Sciences
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Exchange students are restricted from the following Academic Schools at Cardiff:
    -   Dentistry, Healthcare Sciences, Medicine, Optometry, Pharmacy, Architecture
    -   Psychology, Politics, Journalism \- Very limited module availability\.
    -   Biosciences \- year\-long only
    -   Chemistry \- mainly year\-long study\.
-   When selecting courses, ensure that they are available in the semester for which you are applying
-   Please research applicable course restrictions on the University of Cardiff's exchange website

[Partner Course Information](https://www.cardiff.ac.uk/study/international/study-abroad-in-cardiff/modules)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   Cardiff credits divided by 4 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   A full load of modules is 120 credits per academic year, or 60 credits per semester
-   Students are required to take 60 credits while on exchange, and are requested to select 80 credits on their application to Cardiff

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)
