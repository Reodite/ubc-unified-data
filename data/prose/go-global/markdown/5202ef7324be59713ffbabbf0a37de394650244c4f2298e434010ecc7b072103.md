## Who can go?

-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study
-   Law students can take course work in the faculty of law
-   **Student must have a cumulative average of 70%**
-   Please note that this school is extremely competitive\. As such, **students may only apply for 1 term** of exchange at Yonsei \(university\-wide agreement\)

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   Commerce students \(UBCV\): select 'Yonsei University\-COMM'
-   All other students: select 'Yonsei University\-Uniwide' 

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1: Late August to Late December
-   UBC Term 2: Late February to Late June

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Engineering
-   Fine Arts
-   Forestry
-   Health Sciences
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Students take courses through the Division of International Education and Exchange
-   The faculty of nursing is also open for students to take courses
-   When selecting courses, ensure that they are available in the semester for which you are applying

[Partner Course Information](https://oia.yonsei.ac.kr/intstd/exCourse.asp)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   Same as UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   Undergraduate: 9 to 18 credits \(for exchange students taking Korean Language course: maximum 12 credits\)
-   Graduate: 9 to 15 credits \(may vary by school\)

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
