[Apply](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/3146)

## Who can go?

-   Students in Arts, Science, LFS and Forestry are eligible
    

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   Students applying for the Arctic Semester in Svalbard: Select 'Norwegian University of Life Sciences \(NMBU\) \- Svalbard Arctic Semester\-Study'
    
-   All other students: Select 'Norwegian University of Life Sciences \(NMBU\)\-Uniwide'
    

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(NMBU Autumn semester\): Mid August to Late December
-   UBC Term 2 \(NMBU Spring semester\): Early January to Late June

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Forestry
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Through NMBU, students can participate in a semester of Arctic studies at the [University Centre in Svalbard\.](https://www.unis.no/studies/) See more information below\.

[Partner Course Information](http://www.nmbu.no/courses?text_3=All&amp;text_4=All&amp;text_5=All&amp;text=&amp;emnekode=)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   30 ECTS per semester

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://global.ubc.ca/go-global/international-experiences/exchange/transfer-credits-go-global) takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search/external?external_id=96&amp;title=&amp;subject_code=&amp;course_number=)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   40% of the courses at NMBU are given in English
-   View NMBU's [English study options](http://www.nmbu.no/en/studies/study-options)

### Other opportunities at this partner

**Arctic studies at the University Centre in Svalbard \(UNIS\)**:

-   If you are wanting to study in Svalbard you must speak with a Go Global Advisor ***before applying***
    
-   UNIS is a collaboration between a number of institutions, students must be accepted to both NMBU and UNIS
    
-   Note that acceptance to Svalbard is not guaranteed as the limited spots available are competitive, students not accepted to UNIS can still attend NMBU
