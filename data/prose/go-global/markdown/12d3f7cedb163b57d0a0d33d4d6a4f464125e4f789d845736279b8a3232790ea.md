##### Whom do I contact to get ahold of my transcripts and/or certificate after I’ve finished my studies at UBC?

Go Global will send your transcripts and certificates to Tec’s exchange office\. We aim to send out all these documents four to five weeks after your exam period\. Email [outbound@servicios\.itesm\.mx](mailto:outbound@servicios.itesm.mx) if you are missing any documents\.
