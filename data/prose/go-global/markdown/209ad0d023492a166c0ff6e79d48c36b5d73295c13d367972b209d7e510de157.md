##### Can we make changes to our course registration after the survey?

You will have the opportunity to modify your course registration through an add/switch survey that will be sent by our team around August until the [Add/Drop deadline](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates/)\.

You can [drop courses](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/course-registration/dropping-a-course/) on your own through Workday until the [Add/Drop deadline](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration/course-change-dates/)\. Check the UBC Academic Calendar for the precise date\.
