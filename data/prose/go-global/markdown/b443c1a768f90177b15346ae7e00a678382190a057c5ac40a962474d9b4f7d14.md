##### What are these fees I see on Workday? Am I paying tuition? Do I have to pay them?

Exchange students are not charged tuition fees\. They will have the mandatory [International Health Insurance \(IMED\)](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international), [U\-Pass Program](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/transit/u-pass-compass-card) and other [AMS student fees](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/student-fees/student-fees-established-and-approved-student-society) assessed to their student account\. Some students may also have course\-based activity fees \(e\.g\. studio fees, lab and course material fees, etc\.\) depending on the courses they've enrolled in\.

You may view and pay these fees on UBC’s [Workday](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/) by logging in with your [Campus Wide Login \(CWL\)](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl) Information\. See this page for more information on [living expenses](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange/step-1-planning-eligibility "Planning &amp; eligibility ") to be aware of during your study term\.
