As a visiting exchange student, you can: 

-   Study at UBC between one to two winter session terms \(single term or full/split year\)\.
-   Start your studies in Term 1 \(September \- December\) or Term 2 \(January \- April\), depending on your exchange duration\.
-   Take undergraduate or graduate courses available to exchange students\.
-   Earn credit for courses taken at UBC towards your degree at your home university\.
-   Study at UBC’s Vancouver or [Okanagan campus](http://students.ok.ubc.ca/involvement-activities/go-global/study-abroad-at-ubc-okanagan/)

##### Term Dates and Application Timelines

Check out our [Key Dates page](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange/key-dates-applying-ubc-exchange "Key dates for applying to UBC on exchange") for details about timelines and deadlines\.

##### Eligibility

##### Academic Requirement

UBC Go Global welcomes students with a minimum GPA of 70% on average \(or B or 3\.0/4\.0 or equivalent\) who are nominated by a UBC partner university\. Please check with your home university's international exchange office for application processes, requirements and timelines\.

Academic standing must be established at the nominating partner institution at time of application\.

**English Language Proficiency**

Students must also meet our minimum [English language requirements](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/2128) to be eligible for exchange\.

##### Not from a university partner?

If you want to take UBC credit\-based courses, but your home university does not have a Student Mobility Agreement with UBC, contact [UBC Non\-Degree Studies](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies)\. 

##### Financial planning

Estimated living expenses are $6,900\-$11,000 CAD per term\. But financial requirements vary depending on individual goals, the cost of living in Vancouver and personal preferences\. To get a clear financial picture of your studies at UBC, create a budget with both university and living costs\.

See this [sample budget worksheet \(PDF\)](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/Exchange%20Student%20Budget%20Worksheet_0.pdf) to estimate your monthly expenses\. Fill in the table with realistic amounts for each category that applies\.

Here is a breakdown of some key costs: 

##### Accommodation 

##### On\-campus housing: $4,050 \- $6,800 CAD per term

Housing costs depend on the location, size and type\. There are apartment and townhouse\-style accommodations available \(e\.g\., Walter Gage, Fairview Crescent, Ritsumeikan\-UBC House, Marine Drive\)\. For detailed costs for each residence, visit [UBC Vancouver Housing](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/residences-rooms/residences/) to check winter session residence fees\.  

##### Off\-campus housing options: $4,800\-$8,000 per term

Rents vary greatly depending on apartment location, age and condition\. For example, it is generally more expensive to rent in a condominium building than an apartment building, and lodging close to UBC is typically more expensive than elsewhere in the city\. Keep in mind these are only general guidelines for cost\. See more info on [living off campus](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/other-housing/off-campus-housing/#costs)\.

-   Shared room: $4,800\+/term
-   Private room \(shared unit\) : $8,000\+/term 

##### Meal / grocery costs

Approximately $2,000/term

UBC students with an active UBCcard can get a 5% discount at UBC Food Services\. For details, see [UBCcard Value Plan – FOOD AT UBC VANCOUVER](https://food.ubc.ca/meal-plans/ubccard-value-plan/)\. 

##### Local transportation: mandatory student public transit pass \(U\-Pass\)

##### U\-Pass fee: $191\.40 for the winter academic term \(subject to change\) and mandatory for all exchange students attending UBC\. 

Only students registered in a minimum of three credits are eligible for the U\-Pass, a universal transportation pass with unlimited access to all TransLink buses, the SkyTrain and the SeaBus during the winter session\. 

##### Medical insurance costs \(iMED and BC MSP\)

**Mandatory iMED insurance for international students**

The iMED program is a temporary basic health insurance plan required as a condition of registering as an international student new to UBC\. Enrolment is automatic upon registration of your courses\. See more information on iMED, including coverage dates\.

iMED is the basic [health insurance](http://www.david-cummings.com/imed/) required for all new international exchange students\. Enrolment is automatic upon registration in your courses\. Find [more information](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international#:~:text=About%20iMED&amp;text=iMED%20provides%20you%20with%20basic,student%20or%20a%20VIRS%20student.) on iMED, including coverage dates\.

-   **Single\-term exchange:** The cost is $295\.10 CAD per term, plus a $11\.15 direct billing fee for iMED\. If you are a single\-term exchange student, iMED will cover you for the duration of your stay \(approximately four months and one week\)\.   
     
-   **Full\-year exchange:** The cost is $204\.30 CAD plus a $11\.15 direct billing fee for iMED\. The cost covers the three\-month waiting period for the [British Columbia Medical Services Plan \(MSP\)](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international)\. **Students on exchange for six months or longer are required to apply for MSP\.** The MSP plan costs about $75 CAD per month\.

##### Educational expenses

For academic expenses such as books and supplies: $500\-$700 CAD per term\. 

Tuition: In most cases, students on exchange at UBC pay tuition directly to their home institution\. Certain courses \(e\.g\. studio courses, etc\.\) may also charge a fee for materials, field trips, etc\. separate from tuition\. Any specific course fees will be available under "Finances" in Workday upon registration\.

Student fees: Incoming exchange students are responsible for some student fees charged by the UBC Alma Mater Society \(UBC's student society\)\. For details, please refer to the Academic Calendar [here](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/student-fees/student-fees-established-and-approved-student-society)\. Questions about these fees can be directed to the [UBC Alma Mater Society](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/contact-us/)\.
