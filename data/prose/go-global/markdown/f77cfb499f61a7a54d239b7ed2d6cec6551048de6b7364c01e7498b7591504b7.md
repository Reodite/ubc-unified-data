##### Can I request a course with reserved seats?

Exchange students may be able to request reserved seats where they feel they meet the restrictions, but we cannot guarantee registration as each department has different enrolment policies\.

In general, you can check the course restrictions for exchange students on our website here: [https://global\.ubc\.ca/go\-global/coming\-ubc/coming\-ubc\-exchange/step\-4\-course\-registration](https://global.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange/step-4-course-registration)\. If a reserved course **is not** listed as unavailable to exchange students here, please feel free to request it on your Course Survey, provided you meet the eligibility requirements for the course\.  

If you do request a course with reserved seats on your Course Survey, be aware that we may not be able to register you in it, or you may be placed on a waitlist\. Exchange students may also be able to gain access to a restricted course by obtaining approval after arrival if needed\.
