##### Do I need to have the course prerequisites/corequisites for the course I want to take?

Yes, for courses that list prerequisites/corequisites, you must have completed the equivalent prior to enrolment\.  All exchange students are responsible for ensuring they meet the course prerequisite requirements, and academic departments may ask to see your transcripts and previous course syllabi as proof of requirements met\. Look up the individual prerequisites in the course schedule and use your best judgment to determine whether you’ve completed equivalent coursework at your home university\.
