##### I have a question about Study Permits, Visas, or eTAs

For any questions regarding study permits or Visa/Electronic Travel authorization applications, or travelling to Canada, please review the [UBC International Student Guide](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide)\. For step\-by\-step instructions on how to apply for your student permit, please review UBC’s online tutorial: [How to apply for an initial study permit](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/study-permits/about-initial-study-permits/tutorial-apply-initial-study-permit-overview)\.

If you have further immigration questions, you must connect with our International Student Advisors who are Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants/Regulated International Student Immigration Advisors\. You can connect with them [here](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/international-student-advising)\. If you would like to contact them for an appointment, please review the instructions on their page\. Ensure to let them know you are an incoming UBC exchange student and share your UBC Student ID number\.
