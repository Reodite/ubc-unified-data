## Who can go?

-   Only engineering and commerce students may apply to this institution

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   Term 1: Late August \- Late December
-   Term 2: Early January \- Mid May

### Areas of study available:

-   Engineering

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Some courses with capacity limit do not accept exchange students
-   For pre\-requisite required courses, students must have taken courses considered equivalent to the pre\-requisite
-   Applied Science Students: Exchange students attached to College of Science and Engineering \(CSE\) are required to take a minimum of 9 credit units from courses offered by departments of CS
-   Commerce Students: Aside from business courses, students will also have access to Mandarin and Cantonese courses
-   Commerce Students: Minimum 50% of course load must be business courses from the college of business 

[Partner Course Information](http://www.cb.cityu.edu.hk/exchange/international_student/information)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   Same as UBC Credits

### Course load Information

-   12 credits minimum

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)
