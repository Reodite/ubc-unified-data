## Who can go?

-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(UU Semester 1\): Late August to Mid January
-   UBC Term 2 \(UU Semester 2\): Mid January to Mid June 

***\*Depending on course selection and scheduling, students on exchange in Term 1 may be able to return to UBC for Term 2***

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Education
-   Engineering
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Exchange students can be admitted to masters courses if they meet the prerequisites based on the policy of each department

[Partner Course Information](http://www.uu.se/en/admissions/exchange/course/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits 

### Course load Information

-   Full time workload: 30 ECTS
-   Minimum: 22,5 ECTS
-   Maximum: 37,5 ECTS

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Opportunity to take an intensive beginners Swedish class before the beginning of term
-   Depending on course selection and scheduling at Uppsala, students on exchange in Term 1 may be able to complete their exchange in December and return for Term 2\. This is up to the student to plan through scheduling or arrangement with their professors\. More information on course schedulding available [here](https://www.uu.se/en/study/exchange-studies/course-mapping#anchor-282507)\.

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
