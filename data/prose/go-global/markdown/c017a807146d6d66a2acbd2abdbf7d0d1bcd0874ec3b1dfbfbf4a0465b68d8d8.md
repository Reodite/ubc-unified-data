## Who can go?

-   Most areas of study open to students
-   Due to limited seats available at Fudan, for the University\-wide exchange program, only single term exchanges are available
-   Limited number of English taught courses each semester \(80 \- 100\)
-   Chinese language courses
-   Note for Commerce students \- Fudan has requested that students not holding Chinese passports be given priority for space at Fudan\. If you do hold Chinese citizenship, you will still be considered for Fudan, but will not be given priority\.
-   **Starting Spring 2027, Fudan has announced that it will no longer accept students with Chinese citizenship at the university\-wide level** \(including Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macao\)

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   Commerce students: Select 'Fudan\-COMMERCE'
-   All other students: Select 'Fudan\-Uniwide'

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Fudan Semester 1\): Early September to end of January
-   UBC Term 2 \(Fudan Semester 2\): Early March to Mid\-July

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   **Language students:**  will be enrolled in the language school and after the LPT \(language Positioning Test\), the student will have a list of courses in intensive Chinese learning Language, students cannot choose other courses in undergraduate level
-   **General Advanced Student** \(undergraduate level\): specify major, courses are taught in Foreign languages, student will register courses in the Course Registration System
-   First Phase of course registration is in your Fudan major or program
-   Second Phase: all courses are open to students
-   There are no mandatory courses for exchange students \- however, you will find that courses in your major can be registered earlier than all other courses so you should choose your major carefully
-   When students get their admission package they can refer to the website for course info

[Partner Course Information](http://www.fao.fudan.edu.cn/1619/list.htm)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   Fudan 6 credits \= UBC 7\.5 credits
-   Fudan 4 credits \= UBC 5 credits
-   Fudan 3 credits \= UBC 3\.75 credits
-   Fudan 2 credits \= UBC 2\.5 credits
-   Fudan 1 credit \= UBC 1\.25 credits
-   For Language courses: Fudan 1 credit \= \.75 Ubc credit

### Course load Information

-   6\-15 credits at Fudan for one semester \(2\-6 courses\)
-   Average 10 credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)
