## Who can go?

-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study
-   Students pursuing courses at the School of Architecture at Lunds University require 3 years of studies in the subject, prior to their term\(s\) abroad\.

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   Engineering students: select 'Lunds\-Engineering'
    
-   Architecture students: select 'Lunds \- Architecture'
    
-   All other students: select 'Lunds \- Uniwide'
    

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Lunds Semester 1\): Late August to Mid January
-   UBC Term 2 \(Lunds Semester 2\): Late January to Early June

**\*Students attending Semester 1 taking courses in Swedish will not be able to return to UBC to attend Term 2, most English\-taught courses end in December**

### Areas of study available:

-   Architecture and Landscape Architecture
-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Education
-   Engineering
-   Fine Arts
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

[Search for courses at this institution](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/international-admissions/exchange-study-abroad/exchange-studies/find-exchange-courses)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

## Finances and funding

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.   

-   15 credits or equivalent full\-time program tuition per term, paid to UBC
