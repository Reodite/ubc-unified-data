## Who can go?

-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study
-   Students need a minimum cumulative average of 70% to attend this university

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   Commerce students \(UBC\-V\): Select 'University of Otago\-COMM'
-   Law students: Select 'University of Otago\-LAW'
-   [Tūrangawaewae, Pōkai Whenua students](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/indigenous-global-connections): Select 'University of Otago\-Turangawaewae, Pokai Whenua’
-   All other students: Select 'University of New South Wales\-Uniwide' 

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Otago Semester 2\): July to November
-   UBC Term 2 \(Otago Semester 1\): February to June
-   UBC Full year \(Otago Semester 2 then 1\): July to June
-   UBC Split year \(Otago Semester 1 then 2\): February to June &amp; July to November

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Health Sciences
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Law
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Medicine, Dentistry, Psychiatry, Physiotherapy, Pharmacology, Pharmacy or any other clinical course are not open to exchange students
-   Each 18 point paper will require approximately 18 hours study a week made up of a combination of lectures, tutorials, laboratories, assignments and reading

[Partner Course Information](http://www.otago.ac.nz/courses/subjects/index.html)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   Otago points divided by 4 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   International students are required to maintain a full time student status while at Otago
-   Minimum Course Load\= 54 Points per semester \(3 papers/courses\)
-   Recommended Course Load\= 54\-72 Points per semester \(3 or 4 papers/courses\)
-   Only in exceptional circumstances will an Otago Academic Advisor approve a programme of 5 papers a semester

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Other opportunities at this partner

[**Tūrangawaewae, Pōkai Whenua**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/indigenous-global-connections "Indigenous Global Connections ")  
Tūrangawaewae, Pōkai Whenua \(A place to stand, a world to explore\) is a unique student exchange built on kanohi\-ki\-te\-kanohi \(face\-to\-face\) relationships established between mana whenua and Indigenous representatives of universities throughout the world\. Indigenous students participate in an Exchange and are welcomed at Indigenous events, conferences, sports teams and social outings in Dunedin\. The program adds new dimension to traditional exchange, introducing students to contemporary life and culture within local Indigenous communities and offering students deeper transnational engagement to provide new perspectives on local and global issues\.
