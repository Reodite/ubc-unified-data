## Who can go?

-   You need a minimum cumulative average of 72% to attend this partnership  
     
-   Students who wish to attend Monash on exchange are also given the option of attending their campus in [Malaysia](http://global.ubc.ca/career/international-experiences/exchange/monash-university-malaysia) \(all applications are administered through Monash Australia\)
    -   Students planning to go on exchange for two semesters can complete one semester at both campuses\.
    -   It is recommended to study in Australia first, and then Malaysia \- students will apply to Australia, and Monash's Australia office will help you apply to Malaysia for the second semester\.

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   CIE students: Select 'Monash University\-CIE’
-   All other students: Select 'Monash University\-Uniwide' 

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Monash semester 2\): Mid July to Late November
-   UBC Term 2 \(Monash semester 1\): Mid February to Late June
-   UBC Full year \(Monash semesters 2 \+ 1\): July to June
-   UBC Split year \(Monash semesters 1 \+ 2\): February to November

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Engineering
-   Health Sciences
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Please refer to [academic considerations](http://monash.edu/study-abroad/inbound/study-options/academic-considerations/) for more information on course selection

[Partner Course Information](https://www.monash.edu/study-abroad/inbound/study-options)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   8 Monash Points \= 5 UBC Credits

### Course load Information

-   Average load: 24 credit points \(four units/subjects\)
-   18 credit points \(three units/subjects\) is permitted
-   Maximum load: 24 credit points per semester

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)
