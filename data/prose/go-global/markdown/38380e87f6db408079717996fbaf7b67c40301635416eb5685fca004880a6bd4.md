##### I received my iMED card but the dates on it are wrong\. What should I do?

If you will arrive in Canada in the calendar month after your iMED coverage start date \(for example, your iMED coverage starts August 10, but you will arrive in Canada in September\), you'll need to complete and submit an [iMED Date Change Request](https://sites.guard.me/ubc/page/go-global/date-change-request) to GuardMe so that your coverage dates can be adjusted to align with your study or research period\.

For students in programs longer than 6 months, your default iMED coverage dates depend on when you start your program:

-   Winter Session Term 1 \(September to December\): coverage starts on August 10
-   Winter Session Term 2 \(January to April\): coverage starts on December 10
-   Summer Session \(May to August\): coverage starts on April 10

For one\-term Go Global exchange or visiting students, your default iMED coverage dates are based on the term you're enrolled in:

-   Winter Session Term 1 \(September to December\): August 26 to January 2
-   Winter Session Term 2 \(January to April\): December 23 to May 1

Most students' research dates are not aligned with UBC's academic terms, therefore most students will need to request to have their coverage dates updated\.
