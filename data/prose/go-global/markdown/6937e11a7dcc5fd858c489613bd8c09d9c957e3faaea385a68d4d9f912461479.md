## Who can go?

**Travel to Israel is not currently permitted, the current advisory level is level 4: “Avoid all travel\.” UBC uses** [**the Government of Canada’s**](https://travel.gc.ca/) **travel advisories to assess the risk level of a destination\.  As per UBC’s** [**Student Safety Abroad**](https://safetyabroad.ubc.ca/) **policy, students are not permitted to travel to Level 3 and 4 destinations unless exceptional circumstances exist\.**

-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study
-   Exchange students are eligible to take [Hebrew classes](https://international.tau.ac.il/Intensive_Hebrew_Ulpan/?id=term-3) and programs at an additional cost\.

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Limited courses taught in English
-   The majority of courses will be taught in Hebrew
