#### Important

If you are an eligible student, get started and submit your VIRS application as early as possible: **at least 15 weeks before the planned start date of your research\.** It will take us 1\-2 months for us to review VIRS applications and for you to receive your acceptance letter\. After receiving your letter, you will then need to apply for relevant immigration documents: Canadian visa and/or study permit\. 

Instructions on this page are intended for students doing research at UBC Vancouver Campus\. If you are researching at UBC Okanagan campus, please see instructions [here](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/visiting-international-research-student-at-ubc-okanagan/)

##### Before applying

1.  Make sure VIRS is the [appropriate program](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/2626) for you\.
2.  Ensure you meet the [eligibility requirements](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/visiting-international-research-students/eligibility-requirements "Eligibility requirements")\.
3.  Check that your research start date is realistic, especially if you need to obtain immigration documents for travel\. **\*Students who are planning a research study period of 6 months or more are advised to check the notice on the availability of Provincial Attestation Letter \(PALs\) \-\- a document required to apply for a study permit \-\- on** [**this page**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/visiting-international-research-students/applying-your-initial-study-permit) **before submitting a VIRS application\.** 

***How do I know if my start date is realistic?*** 

*Check* [*processing times*](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html) *for immigration documents based on your country of citizenship, then factor processing times into your start date\. \* For example, if you are applying in July for a October start and the listed processing time is 15 weeks, your research start date is not realistic\.* 

***\*Please note that there is currently a delay in processing times across the world and it is very likely you need to give yourself even more time than what is posted\.*** 

4.  Download and complete the required application documents\. Collect all required signatures\.
    -   Attach a VIRS Approval Form to your application\. \(Scanned copies are OK\.\) Save the Approval Form in PDF or JPEG format\.
        -   VIRS students can only be admitted for up to one year maximum in the initial application\. For extension beyond a year, see guidelines in "Withdrawals and Extensions" on [this page](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/visiting-international-research-students/info-current-accepted-students)\.
    -   Are you interested in the [Joint Doctoral Research Student \(JDRS\) designation](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/visiting-international-research-students/prospective-students "Prospective students") and eligible? Complete the [Joint Doctoral Research Scholar \(JDRS\) Request Form \(PDF\)](https://global.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/JDRS_form_Dec-6-22.pdf) to apply\. Save the Approval Form in PDF or JPEG format\.

##### Ready to apply? 

Since it could take up to two months for application review, submit as soon as possible\! The VIRS application processing time will depend on the overall number of applications Go Global receives\. **We are not able to rush or expedite letters of acceptance based on your individual situation\.** Thank you for your patience in this process\.

###### How to apply

1.  Create an account through the [VIRS Student Application Portal](https://goglobal.students.ubc.ca/ggapplications/s/create-account)\. **Use an email address you check regularly and plan to use throughout the application process\.** We will send email you important updates about your VIRS application through this email address\. Make sure @ubc\.ca emails are on your safe list\.
2.  After you create an account, check your email for instructions on how to begin the application\. 
3.  Fill out the application and upload the necessary documents\. Please use the same email address in application that you used to create your account\.
    1.  the [VIRS Approval Form](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/VIRS%20Approval%20Form%20Sept%202026.pdf)
    2.  the [JDRS Request Form](http://global.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/JDRS_form_Dec-6-22.pdf) \(if applicable\)\.
    3.  Identity Document
    4.  Proof of enrolment from your home institution
        1.  Please upload a letter **written in English** showing your enrollment as a student at your home unversity\. **The letter is only sufficient if it can prove that you will maintain enrolment at your home university for the entire duration of the research period indicated on your VIRS application\.** 
        2.  Please ensure that the letter is issued on official letterhead and dated within the last six months
        3.  Note: Copies of academic transcripts or a student card are not acceptable
4.  Submit your application\. 

###### After you’ve submitted   

1.  Check your email again for your new UBC student number\.
2.  Pay your VIRS program fee \($440\.00\)\* [online](https://secure.touchnet.net/C20435_ustores/web/store_main.jsp?STOREID=35&amp;SINGLESTORE=true)\.    
    a\. Select “VIRS Program Fee” \(CAD440\.00\)\* &gt; select “Add to cart” &gt; provide your name, email \(the same one you used to submit your VIRS application\) and your UBC student number &gt; select “Check out” &gt; enter your email address again &gt; provide your payment details\.
3.  We aim to have your UBC acceptance letter ready within 1\-2 months of receiving your VIRS program fee, depending on the volume of applications\.

\*Note: The VIRS Program Fee is subject to change year to year\. 

##### Next: I have my letter of acceptance\. Now what?

1.  **Verify your personal information** \(for example, legal name, date of birth, permanent address, etc\.\) is correct on your student application in the Go Global Student Application Portal and on the VIRS Letter of Acceptance\. If the information needs to be updated, you MUST notify Go Global within five business days of the email notification about your admission and prior to accepting your offer of admission\.
2.  **Accept your UBC admission offer in the Applicant Service Centre\.** Your acceptance to UBC is only confirmed after you accept your admission offer\. You have three weeks from the date of your acceptance letter to accept your offer\. **Ensure you are accepting offer to the correct UBC campus\.**
3.  **Create your Campus Wide Login \(CWL\)** account using your UBC student number\.
4.  **Apply for the appropriate immigration document using your letter of acceptance\. Do not delay this step** if you need immigration documents\. Documents can include a study permit and/or Temporary Resident Visa, or Electronic Travel Authorization, if applicable\.
    1.  Students engaged in research for **six \(6\) months or less** can complete their research under visitor status with a Temporary Resident Visa or Electronic Travel Authorization, depending on citizenship\.
    2.  Students engaged in research for **longer than six \(6\) months** require a study permit\.   
        Please note there have been changes made to the study permit application process\. To see the most recent updates and to determine what immigration document to obtain, please see [this page](https://global.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/visiting-international-research-students/applying-your-initial-study-permit)\. Check estimated [processing times](http://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html) for various immigration document applications on the Government of Canada website\.
5.  **Pay your iMED fees** through [Workday](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/logging-into-workday/)\. Log into your account using your CWL \(see step 3 above\)\.  

Students admitted through VIRS will be registered as full\-time students at UBC for the duration of their research period\. Admitted students will have permission to engage in research education, but cannot register for academic courses\.  

##### Application troubleshooting

### I am having trouble creating an account\.

Please try the following and if you are still experiencing difficulties, please contact [go\.global@ubc\.ca](mailto:go.global@ubc.ca):

1.  Clear the cache and browsing history on your web browser;
2.  Try creating your account using the incognito mode on your web browser;
3.  Try creating your account on another web browser\.

### I have created an account, but I can't log back\-in\. 

1.  Try logging in directly to the [VIRS application](http://goglobal.students.ubc.ca/ggapplications) using your existing email address \(the same email address you used to create your account\) for your username\.
2.  If you are unable to log in, click “Forgot your password?” to create a new password\.
3.  If you are still experiencing difficulties, contact us\.

If you need to change the start or end dates of your research, complete this [Delayed Arrival Online Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_ba5VaYUUclYNwSq) to ensure your acceptance letter is accurate and that you have appropriate insurance coverage\. 

If you are not sure when your new research start and end dates will be, please select: "I am not sure when my new arrival date will be" in the form above\.
