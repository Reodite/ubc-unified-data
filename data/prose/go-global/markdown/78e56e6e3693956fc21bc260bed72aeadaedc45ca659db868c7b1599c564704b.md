## About the program

The Globex Julmester program offered by the College of Engineering at Peking University welcomes international students from all over the world to study at PKU together for an intensive 3\-week period during the summer\. **Topics of study will be focused on science and engineering; there is limited coursework in Chinese studies\.**

More specific and finalized program details can be found on the partner institution's website\. However, you must apply through Go Global to be considered for this program and ensure you follow the appropriate transfer credit process\.

## Who can go?

**This program is available via** [**summer exchange**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad#summerabroadfaq) **and** [**study abroad**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad#summerabroadfaq)**\. Spaces for both pathways are very limited\. Generally, the number of applicants exceeds the number of spaces\.** 

***Students who apply to this program for 2026 summer and who are eligible will be matched on a lottery basis\****

**\*Faculty of Applied Science \(APSC\) students will be prioritized for matching to this program and may be nominated to summer exchange or study abroad, while students from all other faculties will be nominated to study abroad if selected by lottery**

-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements
    -   [**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad/eligibility "Eligibility")
    -   [**UBC Okanagan Students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-short-term-programs/summer-abroad/)
-   Available to students with Chinese citizenship \(this summer program does not have the same citizenship restrictions as winter session term\-based exchange at Peking University\)

### Transfer credit

Check out the courses that previous students have transferred back to UBC in the last 5 years using our [Course Search Tool](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)\. For more information, please visit the [Go Global Transfer Credit webpage](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global")\.
