Before you get started, make sure you are eligible for a Go Global program\. For Exchange and Study Abroad, you must meet all requirements to participate\.

#### Check all requirements:

##### 1\. Go Global

-   You must have a 70% [cumulative average](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/degree-planning/viewing-your-grades-and-academic-record/) on UBC course work
-   You must be a UBC student [good standing](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/academic-standing)
-   You must be studying [full\-time](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/course-load-guide/) in the Winter session prior to exchange\. UBC's definition of full\-time for undergraduate students can be found [here](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/definition-full-time-status-undergraduate-students)\.
-   Students may participate in one full\-year or two single\-term \(winter session\) exchange experiences during their degree

###### How your grades will be evaluated

-   **Students applying in December \(round 1\):** Go Global will review your cumulative average\. Final grades will be reviewed in the Spring\. *If your exchange begins in July we will only review grades up to and including term 1*
-   **Students applying in May \(round 2\):** Go Global will review your cumulative grades\.

**NOTE:** These criteria are set to ensure that you are prepared to thrive academically while on exchange and meet the admissions criteria of partner institutions\. However, we understand that there are cases when your average may drop below this standard but you are still able to demonstrate academic preparedness to participate\. To discuss possible appeals or exceptions to the UBC requirements outlined above, please connect with a [Go Global advisor](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/3122)\.

*If you require academic or other accommodations abroad, please speak to a Go Global advisor six weeks before applying\.*

###### Taking your final term abroad before graduating

Students **cannot graduate** before all transfer credit is processed and degree requirements are complete\. Students should return to UBC for at least one term after their Go Global program before graduation in order to allow for the [transfer credit](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/node/2122) process to be complete\.

For example, due to normal transfer credit processing times, it is not possible to graduate in May if you finish your degree requirements while abroad in Term 2\.

###### Requirements for taking your final term abroad

Going on exchange or study abroad for your final term requires approval from Go Global and your faculty advisor\. 

1.  Meet with a Go Global advisor\. In this meeting, you will discuss the program, transfer credit timelines, and possible risks or consequences with your plan\.
2.  Meet with your faculty advisor to discuss your degree requirements and eligibility for graduation\. It is also recommended that you meet with your program or departmental advisor\.

As transfer credit equivalencies are not always determined before your exchange or study program ends, there is also a risk that your transfer credits may not meet your remaining degree requirements, and you may need to return to UBC for additional courses\.​​​​​​

##### 2\. Your UBC faculty 

###### Undergraduate students

For all undergraduate students, you must have completed year two prior to the Go Global experience\.

If you require academic or other accommodations abroad, please connect with a Go Global Advisor six weeks before applying\.

###### Arts students

-   Writing requirement must be completed or in progress at time of application\. 
-   Student must have 2nd year standing at time of application\.

If you require academic or other accommodations abroad, please connect with a Go Global Advisor six weeks before applying\.

###### Bachelor of International Economics students

-   BIE students will need to obtain approval from BIE Advising if their exchange will break BIE cohort, please consult with the BIE program if you are planning to go on exchange\. 
-   note: BIE students may apply to [Summer Abroad](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad "Summer Abroad") programs in year 1

If you require academic or other accommodations abroad, please connect with a Go Global Advisor six weeks before applying\.

###### Commerce students

-   Going on exchange or study abroad for your final term requires approval\. To qualify for a final winter term exchange, you must have 9 to 15 required credits remaining to complete abroad\. Summer abroad requires a minimum 3 credits remaining\. 

If you require academic or other accommodations abroad, please connect with a Go Global Advisor six weeks before applying\.

###### **Forestry and Environmental Stewardship** **students**

-   FES students must complete all core 1\[st\] and 2\[nd\] year courses within their degree prior to going abroad\.  
-   Transfer students need to spend one full year at UBC before going abroad\.
-   Students must consult with an academic advisor in FES regarding transferability of credits\. 

If you require academic or other accommodations abroad, please connect with a Go Global Advisor six weeks before applying\.

###### **Kinesiology** **students**

-   All KIN students must complete WRDS 150B \(or equivalent\), plus all 1st and 2nd year KIN core courses by the time they go on exchange\.
-   Please be sure you meet with a KIN advisor prior to going on exchange to make sure the courses you plan on taking work within your degree\.

If you require academic or other accommodations abroad, please connect with a Go Global Advisor six weeks before applying\.

###### Law students

Law students must have at least a 70% average in the term they apply, and a 70% cumulative average overall\. A student who is approved for exchange based on term 1 marks must maintain a sessional average of at least 70%\. Law students must have second or third\-year standing during your Go Global period\.

**Transfer students:** You can only go abroad for one term\.

If you require academic or other accommodations abroad, please connect with a Go Global Advisor six weeks before applying\.

###### Science students

-   You must have your [Foundational Requirement](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-science/bachelor-science/lower-level-requirements) completed
-   You must have at least 3 credits of the [Communication Requirement](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-colleges-and-schools/faculty-science/bachelor-science/communication-requirement) completed
-   You must be established in your Science specialization \(i\.e\., have completed at least one term of required specialization courses\)
-   You cannot have attempted more than 100 credits by the end of the Winter Term 2 prior to the exchange term start
-   You must have no pending Standing Deferred examinations from terms preceding the term in which you are applying for exchange
-   You must complete all pending Standing Deferred examinations from the session in which you are applying for exchange, prior to departure on exchange 

###### **If you are in fourth year class standing at the time of application:**

-   You will only be eligible for Summer Exchange, and
-   If you went on summer exchange, you will not be eligible to apply for November Graduation, and
-   If you are intending to complete your degree requirements in Term 1 of the Winter Session, please be aware that transfer credit will not be assigned until after the add/drop deadline, and that likely Term 2 courses will be required\. Courses should be planned accordingly\.

###### **Transfer students:** 

-   You must spend a full year at UBC before going abroad\.
-   Prior degree holders and students in the Bachelor of Computer Science program are not eligible to apply\.

Please visit [https://science\.ubc\.ca/students/exchange](https://science.ubc.ca/students/exchange) for the full list of eligibility requirements, FAQ and complete details on going on exchange as a student in the Faculty of Science\.

If you require academic or other accommodations abroad, please connect with a Go Global Advisor six weeks before applying\.

###### Bachelor of Design in Architecture, Landscape Architecture and Urbanism students

Students in the Bachelor of Design in Architecture, Landscape Architecture and Urbanism program are recommended to participate in exchange in term 2 in their third year\.

Students must have at least 70% overall average and have achieved 80% or higher average on the most recent term of full\-time studies to participate in [CIE](https://experience.apsc.ubc.ca/cie/plan-your-exchange/before-you-apply) exchange opportunities\.

If you require academic or other accommodations abroad, please connect with a Go Global Advisor six weeks before applying\.

###### Graduate students

You must have finished at least one year of full\-time studies at UBC before going abroad and be a student in good standing with the support of your supervisor\. 

Once accepted and matched to your partner institution, to ensure courses will transfer, you will need to get approval of your [study plan](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/how-apply) and approval from the [Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/contact-us) and your program supervisor before you go on exchange\.

The faculty may have additional requirements for the number of credits you can transfer and the minimum grades you must obtain per course\. Please follow up with the faculty for further details\.

If you require academic or other accommodations abroad, please connect with a Go Global Advisor six weeks before applying\.
