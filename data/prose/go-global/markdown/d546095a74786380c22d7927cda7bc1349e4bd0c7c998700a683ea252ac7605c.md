## Who can go?

-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements
    -   [**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/eligibility "Eligibility")
    -   [**UBC Okanagan students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-exchange-program/eligibility/)

There are two study tracks available within the Contemporary Japan Program \(CJP\)\. Students can choose one of the following study tracks:

-   Japanese Language Track, for students who wish to focus on their Japanese language studies
    -   Students must at least be able to fluently read, write, understand and pronounce the Japanese Hiragana and Katakana phonetic scripts by the time they arrive in Japan
-   Modern Japan Track, for students who wish mainly to learn about Japan via English\-taught courses through a variety of subjects\.

## Academic planning

### Term dates

UBC term 1 \(KGU Fall semester\): early September \- mid January  
UBC term 2 \(KGU Spring Semester\): mid March \- late July

[Contemporary Japan Program courses](https://ciec.kwansei.ac.jp/study/exchange/outline/#h3-004)

### Course load Information

Exchange students must register and attend more than 10 hours of classes per week\. To fulfill this requirement, registering and attending at least 7 periods every week is necessary\.

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)
