### About the course                                                                                                               

MIDW 370 Global Clinical Placement is open to Midwifery and non\-midwifery students\. In this course, we undergo experiential learning about remote health systems in Nepal, with a special focus on maternal and fetal health\. We explore innovative public health strategies to reduce maternal and infant mortality and morbidity, the impact of skilled birth attendants on maternal and newborn health, and ethical considerations related to global clinical placements and medical tourism including decolonization of global health\.

**Final selection will be done by the lead faculty program directors\.** Min 3 Maximum 8 participants\. In the event that interests exceeds capacity, preference will be given to midwifery students\.  

Students who wish to participate in the summer after graduating may be eligible on a case\-by\-case basis\.

### Experience and conditions in the Field

This is a rural/remote focused experience\. It includes visits to various the Midwifery Society of Nepal, Ministry of health, Paropakar Maternity and Women’s Hosiptal and Tribuvan University Mangala Devi Birthing Centre in Kathmandu\. Then students will travel to Phaplu Referral Hospital, Sotang Hospital, and Gudel\. Students may travel by foot to remote health centres, Maternal Waiting Homes, meet with Dhamis, community midwives and community health volunteers\. 

Water and food borne diseases are common in this region\. Students are advised to bring water treatment tablets, only drink purified water, take Dukoral vaccine and use hand sanitizer before food consumption\.
