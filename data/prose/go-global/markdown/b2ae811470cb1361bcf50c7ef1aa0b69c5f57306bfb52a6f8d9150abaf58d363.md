## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(UGA semester 1\): September \- mid January
-   UBC Term 2 \(UGA semester 2\): Mid January \- mid June

***\*Students who wish to attend Term 1 only, will not be back to UBC in time for the January term***

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Engineering
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Science

[Partner Course Information](https://formations.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/fr/index/acces-directs/offre-de-cours-pour-etudiants-en-echange.html)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Some coursework is offered in English
-   Students must have at least a B2 level to study in French \(To determine your level, please self\-assess with: [Common European Framework of Reference for Languages \(CEFR\)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)\)

## Finances and funding

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.

-   15 credits or equivalent full\-time program tuition per term, paid to UBC
