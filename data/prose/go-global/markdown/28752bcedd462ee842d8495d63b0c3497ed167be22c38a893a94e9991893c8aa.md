## Who can go?

-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements
    -   [**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/eligibility "Eligibility")
    -   [**UBC Okanagan students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-exchange-program/eligibility/)
-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study
-   Only COMM \(UBCV\) students are eligible to apply to the Bachelor in Business Administration program
-   Exchanges are available at the Segovia or Madrid campus

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(IE Fall semester\): Mid September to end of December
-   UBC Term 2 \(IE Spring semester\): Mid January to mid June

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   The following IEU Bachelor Programs are available to UBC exchange students\*:
    -   [**BBA \(Bachelor in Business Administration\)**](http://www.ie.edu/university/studies/academic-programs/bachelor-business-administration/courses/) \- Only open to COMM \(UBCV\) \- COMM \(UBCV\) students **must** attend IE University through the BBA stream if applying through the IE University\-Commerce pathway\.
        -   Madrid campus course offerings: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th year &amp; electives
        -   Segovia campus course offerings: 1st and 2nd year
    -   **BIE \(Bachelor in Economics\)**
        -   Madrid campus course offerings: 3rd, 4th year &amp; electives
        -   Segovia campus course offerings: 1st and 2nd year
    -   **BCDM \(Bachelor in Communication and Digital Media**\)
        -   Madrid campus course offerings: 2nd, 3rd, 4th year &amp; electives
        -   Segovia campus course offerings: Not available
    -   **BBSS \(Bachelor in Behavior and Social Sciences\)**
        -   Madrid campus course offerings: 2nd, 3rd, 4th year &amp; electives
        -   Segovia campus course offerings: Not available
    -   **BIR \(Bachelor in International Relations\)**
        -   Madrid campus course offerings: 1st, 2nd, 3rd year &amp; electives
        -   Segovia campus course offerings: 1st and 2nd year
    -   **BCSAI \(Bachelor in Computer Science and AI\)**
        -   Madrid campus course offerings: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th year &amp; electives
        -   Segovia campus course offerings: 1st year
    -   **BDBA \(Bachelor in Data and Business Analytics\)**
        -   Madrid campus course offerings: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th year &amp; electives
        -   Segovia campus course offerings: 1st year
    -   **PPLE \(Bachelor in Philosophy, Politics, Laws and Economics\)**
        -   Madrid campus course offerings: 1st, 2nd, 3rd year &amp; electives
        -   Segovia campus course offerings: 1st and 2nd year

\* Note: course offerings at each campus are subject to change year to year\.

-   Students are required to register for at least 60% of credits in their study area/major in one of the above programs, and be able to take up to 40% of their credits from a different study area \(depending on availability and overlaps\)\.
-   Determine whether the Madrid or Segovia campus aligns best with your area of study, based on your year of study during your exchange and the course offering on each campus, noted above\.

[Partner Course Information](https://www.ie.edu/university/ieu-experience/international-experience/exchange-students-ieu/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
