#### Prepare &amp; plan

Studying at one of our [**partner schools**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/partner-universities "Partner universities") in another country immersed in a different culture far from home can be a perspective\-broadening, life\-altering and meaningful experience\. Is it right for you? Before you do anything else, make sure you are first eligible for a Go Global exchange\. Scan program details and deadlines, look up transfer credit information and consider how to finance your experience abroad\.
