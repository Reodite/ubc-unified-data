## Who can go?

-   Students must be in at least their third year while on the exchange to be eligible
-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements
    -   [**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/eligibility "Eligibility")
    -   [**UBC Okanagan students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-exchange-program/eligibility/) 
-   COMM \(UBCV\) students are eligible to apply to study at the business school
    
-   Arts students in Political Science, Economics and International Relations can take courses in ESADE's new Bachelor of Global Governance, Economics and Legal Order program
    
-   Exchange students are required to take the seminar "Espana y Catalunya" as a brief introduction to their host country and region
    
-   Courses include case studies, discussions, management
    

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(ESADE Semester 1\): Mid September to Late December
-   UBC Term 2 \(ESADE Semester 2\): Late January to Early June

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Course offerings and exchange information is housed on ESADE's intranet \(non\-public\)
-   Once admitted to the University, exchange students will be given a login to access this information
-   General info on the BBA can be found [here](http://www.esade.edu/grados/eng/grado-direccion-empresas)
-   For information on the Bachelor of Global Governance, Economics and Legal Order program see: [https://www\.esade\.edu/en/programmes/undergraduate/our\-programmes/bachelor\-global\-governance/academics/curriculum\#/third\_year](https://www.esade.edu/en/programmes/undergraduate/our-programmes/bachelor-global-governance/academics/curriculum#/third_year)

[Partner Course Information](http://www.esade.edu/web/eng/alumni-and-network/prospective-exchange-students)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   The full\-time workload for a semester is 30 ECTS 
-   Exchange students can take between 20\-30 ECTS per semester

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Business courses primarily taught in English
-   Bachelor of Global Governance, Economics and Legal Order is taught in English
-   Students must have at least a B2 level to study in Spanish \(please self\-assess with the: [Common European Framework of Reference for Languages \(CEFR\)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)\)
-   ESADE offers exchange students a free one\-month Spanish language course
