## Who can go?

Students in the Faculty of Arts and Sauder School of Business \(UBC\-V\), and The Faculties of Management and Arts &amp; Sciences \(UBC\-O\)

## Academic planning

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Students MUST enrol 50% of their credits from their host faculty\. The rest can be taken from other faculties \(not affiliated schools\), but it is not guaranteed\.
-   As a general rule, it is recommended that students have a minimum language level of B1 in the language of instruction of the courses\. However, some faculties have specific language requirements:
    -   **Faculty of Economics and Business:** Minimum level of B2 of the language of instruction of the courses selected by the student\. A list of valid certificates is available [here](https://www.uab.cat/web/international-exchange/pathways/uab-incoming-exchange-students/application-form-and-enrolment-process-1345699494272.html)\.
    -   **Faculty of Political Science and Sociology**: Minimum level of B2 in the language of instruction of the courses selected by the student *\(students must provide a language certificate as proof\)*\. Keep in mind that there is a restriction on the number of courses you can attend in English and Spanish \-\- a maximum of 2 subjects in English and 1 subject in Spanish per semester\. See more information on the [faculty website](https://www.uab.cat/web/mobilitat-internacional/itineraris/incoming-exchange-students/subject-offer-requirements-1345842938419.html)\.
    -   **Faculty of Communication:** Minimum level of B1 of the language of instruction of the courses selected by the student\.
    -   **Faculty of Psychology:** Minimum level of B1 in Spanish to enroll in courses in Spanish and in courses in English as well\. **Keep in mind that the faculty’s offer of courses in English is 2 subjects per semester and the demand to follow these courses is bigger than the room available, so all students should be prepared to take courses in Spanish**\. See more information on the [faculty website](https://www.uab.cat/web/mobilitat-internacional/itineraris/incoming-exchange-students/subject-list-incoming-students-1345857540029.html)\.
        -   Psychology majors **must** have a high proficiency in Spanish to study at UAB, *regardless of their intended areas of study while at UAB*\.

### Course equivalency Formula

ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

While Catalan is the primary language of instruction at UAB, a wide range of subjects are offered in Spanish and English\. Different faculties offer a wide range of undergraduate degree courses in English \( Arts and Humanities, Health Sciences, Social Sciences, etc\)

### Other opportunities at this partner

**\*NEW\* Autonomous University of Barcelona Erasmus\+ Scholarship \(KA171\)**

-   The [Erasmus\+ Programme](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities) provides funding for student, faculty and staff mobility between institutions in the European Union and their international partners\.
-   One scholarship is provided by the Erasmus\+ Programme of the European Union for a one\-term exchange at UAB during the 2027/8 academic year 
-   Scholarship Details:
    -   Monthly grant \(up to a max of 5 months\): 850 Euro 
    -   One\-off travel grant: Up to 1735 Euro
    -   Additional funding is possible based on the factors outlined [here](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/E%2B%20Top%20Up_Wageningen%20University_1.pdf): 250 Euro/month
-   Students who are matched to UAB will be contacted by Go Global with further details on how to be considered for this award\.
