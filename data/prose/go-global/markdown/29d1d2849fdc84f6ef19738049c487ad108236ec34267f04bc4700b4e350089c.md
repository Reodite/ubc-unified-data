## About the program

Join international and local students in July and August sessions of ISS to take courses in a diverse academic streams, while participating in Chinese culture immersion activites\. Courses will be taught by prominent academics from CUHK and other renowned universities\.

More specific and finalized program details can be found on the partner institution's website\. However, you must apply through Go Global to be considered for this program and ensure you follow the appropriate transfer credit process\.

## Who can go?

**This program is available via** [**summer exchange**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad#summerabroadfaq) **and** [**study abroad**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad#summerabroadfaq) **for students from all faculties\. Students matched to this program should be prepared to be nominated to either pathways\.**

-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements
    -   [**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/summer-abroad/eligibility "Eligibility")
    -   [**UBC Okanagan students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-short-term-programs/summer-abroad/)

## Academic planning

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Intensive Language Program
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course equivalency formula

-   1 CUHK credit \= 1 UBC credit

### Course load information

-   Take up to two CUHK courses per session\. Each course is worth 3 CUHK credits each unless otherwise specified

### Transfer credit

Check out the courses that previous students have transferred back to UBC in the last 5 years using our [Course Search Tool](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)\. If you don't see a particular course you listed, it simply means you need to submit it as a new course request through the [Transfer Credit Portal](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/) while on your Go Global experience\. For more information, please visit the [Go Global Transfer Credit webpage](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global")\.

**Not all courses listed are offered through the summer program at this partner institution\.** The tool is for reference only–research the partner's website for actual course offerings\.

[Search for previously approved courses](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global")

Note: Transfer credits are only granted for courses with academic content that meet the academic standard for UBC\. Courses that do not contain academic coursework may not be eligible\. Please visit [Go Global Transfer Credit webpage](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") to learn more\. Please speak to a departmental advisor if you have any questions\.
