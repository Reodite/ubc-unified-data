## Who can go?

-   Only COMM \(UBCV\) students are eligible to apply to this partner university
-   Third year students study in the Bachelor program, Fourth year students study in the Masters program

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1: Early September to Late December
    -   *EUR Bachelor program Trimester 1*
    -   *EUR Masters program Block 1 and 2*
-   UBC Term 2: Early January to late June
    -   *EUR Bachelor program Trimester 2 and 3*
    -   *EUR Masters program Block 3, 4, and 5*

### Areas of study available:

-   Commerce \(UBCV\)

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Students will be sent a course registration form where they can find the courses offered to them
-   Students in the Bachelor program may take one course \(5 ECTS\) outside of the business school in another faculty\. Students in the Masters program must take all courses in the business school\. 

[Partner Course Information](https://courses.eur.nl/#/#RSM)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   Minimum: 15 ECTS
-   Maximum: 30 ECTS

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
