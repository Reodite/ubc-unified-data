## Who can go?

-   Only Engineering, Science and Arts \(Social Science\) students may apply
-   Priority will be given to applicants interested in the [IREP Program](https://www.tu-darmstadt.de/studieren/studieninteressierte/internationale_studieninteressierte/kurzzeitprogramme_und_sommeruniversitaet/irep_1/index.en.jsp)

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Darmstadt Winter semester\): Mid\-October \- Mid\-February
-   UBC Term 2 \(Darmstadt Summer semester\): Mid\-April to Mid\-June

***\*Term 1 studies with a departure in December are generally possible upon approval by individual course instructors \(early examination or additional remote work to complete courses\)***

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Engineering
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Science

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   Average course load: 30 ECTS/semester
-   Minimum: 18 \- 20 ECTS/semester
-   Maximum: 30 ECTS/semester

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Students with A2 level may take German\-taught courses \(To determine your level, please self\-assess using the [Common European Framework of Reference for Languages \(CEFR\)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)\)
-   4\-week Intensive German language course offered in September and March at 3 levels of difficulty, prior to classes beginning \(free of charge\)
-   Some [coursework](https://www.tucan.tu-darmstadt.de/scripts/mgrqispi.dll?APPNAME=CampusNet&amp;PRGNAME=ACTION&amp;ARGUMENTS=-Aw-M5aUFJIhA7uh~MwtXwqbB9Kmh7ksnUbjXMTGCCNfZyQZa3Qovh6kUeg4EQ8Cgk7LwSRmvNByRTO5aHIFRV3OWlKS4GYhcFWonOklwFQZb2hMr3I2lXq95PkcQFT8I3tR0w7NCKgY9O1yvgVNDEorJpq8lZsVu1D0T5ahW5ykPNmUeHEmtsbH8SnCWD3fzI-5eTDZrSLBASbvY_) is available in English

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
