#### Programs with restrictions

If you are interested in exploring education and you have completed two years of studies, you may take Education courses \(ADHE, CNPS, ECED, EDCP, EPSE, EDST, EDUC, LLED\) at the 300\-400 level **except for** those listed below\. If you have any questions, please consult with a [Program Coordinator](https://teach.educ.ubc.ca/students/advising/) from the Faculty of Education\.

Courses that are **not available**:

-   100/200 level courses
-   Graduate level Counselling Psychology \(CNPS 500\+\)
-   EDCP 301, 302, 340, 342, 358, 371, 372, 373, 374, 375, 377, 378, 391, and 468
-   EDUC 440
-   EPSE 308, 310, 311, 316, 317, 348, 403, 406, and 449
-   EDST 401, 403 and 404
-   LLED 315A, 325, 336, 350, 351, 352, 353, 360, 361, 366, 371A, and 377

Students who are enrolled in a similar teacher education program at their home institution should contact a [Program Coordinator](https://teach.educ.ubc.ca/students/advising/) about taking a limited number of Bachelor of Education degree program courses\.
