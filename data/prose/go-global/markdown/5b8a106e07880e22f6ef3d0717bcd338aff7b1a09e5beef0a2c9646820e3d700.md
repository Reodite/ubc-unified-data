## Who can go?

-   UBC Vancouver Commerce \(BCOM\) students can study in the [school of business](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/academics/coming-to-mannheim/exchange-students/courses/course-catalog/business-school-bachelors-level-all/) at Mannheim
-   Other UBC students can study in Mannheim's Faculty of [Social Sciences](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/academics/coming-to-mannheim/exchange-students/courses/course-catalog/social-sciences-bachelors-level-all/), [Humanities](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/academics/coming-to-mannheim/exchange-students/courses/course-catalog/humanities-all/), or [Economics](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/academics/coming-to-mannheim/exchange-students/courses/course-catalog/economics-all/)

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Mannheim Fall semester\): late August \- late December
-   UBC Term 2 \(Mannheim Spring semester\): early February \-  mid\-June

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Students must take 50% of their credits in one faculty
-   UBC\- Vancouver Commerce \(BCOM\) students can study in the [school of business](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/academics/coming-to-mannheim/exchange-students/courses/course-catalog/business-school-bachelors-level-all/) at Mannheim
-   Other UBC students can study in Mannheim's Faculty of [Social Sciences](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/academics/coming-to-mannheim/exchange-students/courses/course-catalog/social-sciences-bachelors-level-all/), [Humanities](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/academics/coming-to-mannheim/exchange-students/courses/course-catalog/humanities-all/), or [Economics](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/academics/coming-to-mannheim/exchange-students/courses/course-catalog/economics-all/)

[Mannheim Course Listing](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/academics/coming-to-mannheim/exchange-students/courses/course-catalog/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   2 ECTS \= 1 UBC credit

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Wide range of courses offered in English at the undergraduate and graduate levels

## Finances and funding

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.

-   15 credits or equivalent full\-time program tuition per term, paid to UBC
