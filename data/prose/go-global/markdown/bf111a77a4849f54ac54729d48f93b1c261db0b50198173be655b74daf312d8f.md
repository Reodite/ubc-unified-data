## Who can go?

-   UBC students in the Peter A\. Allard School of Law\.
-   Students must have a minimum 70% cumulative average to study at the partnership\.

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(QUB Autumn semester\): Late September \- Early February
-   UBC Term 2 \(QUB Spring semester\): Late January \- Mid June

***\*Special arrangements for assessment can be made for Fall Semester students to complete exams and return to UBC for term 2***

### Areas of study available:

-   Law

### Course availability and restrictions

UBC Students in the Faculty of Law will have access to coursework at the School of Law at Queen's University Belfast\.

[Courses at QUB](http://www.qub.ac.uk/courses/)

### Course equivalency Formula

Queen's University Belfast Credits divided by 4 \= UBC Credits

### Course load Information

60 credits per semester 120 for the full\-year\.

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)
