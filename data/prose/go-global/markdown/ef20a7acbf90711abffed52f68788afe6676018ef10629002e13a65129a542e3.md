##### **What does an Imagine Orientation Leader do?**

As one of around 45 amazing volunteers, you’ll be part of the first impression that new Exchange and Visiting students get of UBC and Go Global\. You’ll help them get connected, feel at home, and start their UBC journey strong—while building your own leadership and communication skills in the process\! 

##### **Key Dates \+ Time Commitment**

🖥️ **2\-3 hours** of online training: **July &amp; August**  
🧠 **1 day** in\-person training: Tuesday, **August 26, 2026** 

##### **Why Should You Apply?**

-   Grow your **leadership**, **mentorship** &amp; **public speaking** skills
-   **Boost your résumé** &amp; prepare for UBC jobs or senior volunteer roles
-   **Strengthen your scholarship applications** with volunteer experience
-   **Represent** your program and **build your legacy**
-   Make amazing **friends** from around the **world**
-   Have a ton of **fun**\!
