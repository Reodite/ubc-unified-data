##### \[Students\] I am unable to arrive on my original start date of my VIRS stay\. What should I do?

Sometimes hurdles get in the way of arriving on your originally scheduled start date: study permit or visa delays, travel disruptions and so on\. If this happens, fill out the [Delayed arrival online form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_ba5VaYUUclYNwSq) so we can update your letter of acceptance and ensure you have appropriate insurance coverage\. 

If this delay is due to a visa and/or study permit processing times, you can find useful information in the [International Student Guide](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide) or submit a [web form](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/international-student-advising) to talk with an immigration advisor\.

In addition, you will need to update your iMED coverage dates by submitting a 'Date Change Request' inside your GuardMe account\. You must do this as soon as your new research dates are confirmed and before your policy expires\.
