## About the program

The NCCU International Summer School is a six\-week academic and cultural program held at National Chengchi University in Taipei, Taiwan\. It offers a diverse range of English\-taught academic courses and Mandarin Chinese language classes, allowing participants to earn up to 7\.5 NCCU credits\. The academic courses provide a panoramic view of multiple facets of East Asian and Taiwanese societies, covering topics such as politics, culture, technology, indigenous communities, and sustainability\. These courses offer valuable insights into the region’s unique position within the international community, fostering a deeper understanding of Taiwan’s global relevance\. Participants also enjoy cultural immersion through guided excursions in Taipei and central Taiwan\. 

More specific and finalized program details can be found on the partner institution's website\. However, you must apply through Go Global to be considered for this program and ensure you follow the appropriate transfer credit process\.
