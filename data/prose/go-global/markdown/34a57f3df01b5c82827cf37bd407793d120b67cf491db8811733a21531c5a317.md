#### Go Global fee

The Go Global Fee \($440\)  is an administrative fee required for all applicants to all Go Global programs\. The fee will be posted to your Workday account following the application deadline\. You will be notified of the due date by email once the fee is posted\.

Students participating in [Research Abroad](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/research-abroad "Research Abroad") and [Global Seminar](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/global-seminars/programs "Global Seminar Programs") programs are subject to a different fee policy\.

#### Paying your tuition and fees

Students participating in exchange pay tuition directly to UBC and not the partner institution in most cases\. For term\-based exchange, students are required to pay for 15 UBC credits \(per term\), regardless of the number of actual credits they take/earn abroad\. For summer exchange, students will pay for the number of UBC\-equivalent credits they are completing\.

Please see the [list of options](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/paying-tuition) you have for paying tuition\.

Students participating in study abroad pay tuition directly to the partner university\.

#### Student loans

Students going on exchange may have a different timeline for paying your loans and different eligibility requirements\. Find [more information](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/youve-been-matched "You've been matched") on applying and receiving your loans\.

#### Awards and scholarships

Go Global offers many [International Learning Awards](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/awards-students-participating-go-global "Awards for students participating in Go Global") to help make your experience more affordable\.

Go Global students remain registered at UBC – you can still qualify for scholarships and awards\. This includes affiliation scholarships, and faculty\- or department\-specific awards\.

#### Budgeting for your experience

Your financial requirements will vary depending on your individual goals, the cost of living in your host country, and your personal preferences\. Go Global is not able to provide you with a precise dollar amount\. Only you can determine how much money you will need for your experience\.

###### Tips for planning your budget

-   See [student reports](https://goglobal.students.ubc.ca/ggoutboundapplications) to learn about other students' experience
-   Search travel guides and partner university websites for local budgeting information
-   Research the cost of living in your potential host country
-   Consider your spending habits, your lifestyle preferences, your current expenses and how you like to travel for your lifestyle abroad

###### Prepare your budget

Use this sample budget worksheet to estimate your expenses and compare them to your income\. You might find it easier to estimate the monthly expenses for some categories and multiply it by the number of months you'll be away\.

#### Go Global fee adjustment policy

The Go Global Fee is an administrative fee required for all applicants to Go Global programs\. **The fee is non\-refundable\.** **However, it may be adjusted under some circumstances\.** To withdraw from your Go Global exchange, research or summer abroad program, you must contact [Go Global](mailto:go.global@ubc.ca) by email to request withdrawal by the following deadlines: 

-   March 30: For summer and research programs
-   June 30: For UBC Winter Term 1 and Full\-Year exchange
-   September 30: For UBC Winter Term 2 and Split\-Year exchange

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reason for withdrawal** | **Refund Amount \(2026\-2027\)** | **Refund Amount \(2025\-2026\)** |
| You have applied to Go Global but do not meet our minimum eligibility requirements\. | $340 \(Go Global fee will be adjusted from $440 to $100\) | $335\.00 \(Go Global fee will be adjusted from $431\.50 to $96\.50\) |
| Go Global cannot match you to a partner university\. | $340 \(Go Global fee will be adjusted from $440 to $100\) | $335\.00 \(Go Global fee will be adjusted from $431\.50 to $96\.50\) |
| The partner university does not accept your application and Go Global cannot find another academically suitable partner\. | $340 \(Go Global fee will be adjusted from $440 to $100\) | $335\.00 \(Go Global fee will be adjusted from $431\.50 to $96\.50\) |
| You withdraw from Go Global for reasons other than those stated above\. | $100 \(Go Global fee will be adjusted from $440 to $340\) | $96\.50 \(Go Global fee will be adjusted from $431\.50 to $335\.00\) |

*\*This fee adjustment does not apply to Global Seminars*
