### Course availability and restrictions

Students can enroll in subjects of the undergraduate course and Graduate course, as well as the courses at Global Law Program \(part of our Master Degree Program\), as long as they are offered in that semester, and there is no conflict in schedule\. Acceptance will also depend on availability, and prior approval from the responsible professor of the course\. Places in the undergraduate and graduate courses \(in Portuguese\) are subject to availability\.

The only mandatory class for all Exchange students will be the “Introduction to the Brazilian Legal System”\. They all exchange students to take at least 3 courses from the Global Law Program\.

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

FGV DIREITO SP does not have a language test requirement\.  
We believe that our partners will select students able to attend our classes in Portuguese, or will only attend classes that are being offered in English\.  
For the classes in Portuguese we ask the students to have at least an intermediate level of comprehension of the language\.  
For the classes in English, for those students that English is not their mother tongue, we ask at least an intermediate to advanced comprehension level of the language or equivalent to the TOEFL exam \- 95 iBt\.

## Student experience

**Housing**  
FGV DIREITO SP does not offer on\-campus accommodation\.  
However, they recommend web sites that offer apartments or rooms that the students can rent for a short period\. They also put the students in contact with some property owners that hosted prior exchange students\.  
  
**Accessibility**  
Some destinations may be more accessible than others for students with disability\-related accommodations\. If you identify with having a disability or pre\-existing health condition which could impact your participation, or if you require academic accommodation, please contact a Go Global advisor before applying to explore options that are best for you\.
