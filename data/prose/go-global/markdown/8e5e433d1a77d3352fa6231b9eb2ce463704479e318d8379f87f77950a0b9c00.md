#### **Am I eligible?**

First, double\-check that you meet the Go Global and UBC faculty requirements\. You must meet all requirements to participate\. 

[**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/eligibility "Eligibility")  
[**UBC Okanagan students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-exchange-program/eligibility/)

#### Yes, I’m eligible\. Now how do I apply? 

##### 1\. Check the deadline

Refer to [this page](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/go-global-deadlines "Go Global deadlines") for program deadlines\. 

##### 2\. Select your top 3 universities

Look up the partner university requirements on each individual [partner institution page](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/partner-universities "Partner universities") under the "Who can go?" section and make sure you meet them\. 

##### 3\. Apply

You will be applying through Gateway, Go Global's online application portal\. If you've already started your application, you can resume from where you left off at any time\.

-   Complete and submit your Go Global study plan, budget worksheet and any relevant [supporting documents](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/how-apply#supportingdocs) \(within Gateway\) by the application deadline\. See below for further details
-   The [Go Global fee](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/budgeting-exchange-research-and-study) will be posted to your tuition account after the application deadline has passed\. You will be notified of the due date by email once the fee is posted\.

###### Accessibility

Some destinations may be more accessible than others for students with disability\-related accommodations\. If you are considering applying for a Go Global program and identify with having a disability or pre\-existing health condition \(mental or physical\) which could impact your participation, or if you require academic accommodation, please contact a Go Global advisor before applying\. Go Global and your Accessibility Advisor will work together with you to explore the supports available at our partner universities\.  

-   [The Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility) \(Vancouver\)  

-   [Disability Resource Centre](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/drc/welcome.html) \(Okanagan\) 

##### 4\. Pay the Go Global fee

The [Go Global Fee](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/budgeting-exchange-research-and-study "Budgeting for Exchange, Research and Study Abroad") is an administrative fee required for all applicants to all Go Global programs\. The fee will be posted to your Workday account 1\-3 weeks following the application deadline, and will be due approximately 2 weeks later\. You will be notified of the due date by email once the fee is posted\.

Please see the [list of options](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/paying-tuition) you have for paying tuition\.

Students participating in [Research Abroad](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/research-abroad "Research Abroad") and [Global Seminar](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/global-seminars/programs "Global Seminar Programs") programs are subject to a different fee policy\.

If your circumstances have changed, or you are not matched to a partner you may be eligible for a partial [reimbursement of the Go Global fee](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/budgeting-exchange-research-and-study "Budgeting for Exchange, Research and Study Abroad")\. To withdraw from your Go Global exchange, research or summer abroad program, you must contact Go Global by email to request withdrawal by the following deadlines: 

-   March 30: For summer and research programs
-   June 30: For UBC Winter Term 1 and Full\-Year exchange
-   September 30: For UBC Winter Term 2 and Split\-Year exchange

##### 5\. After Go Global acceptance

We’ll send you an email about 8 weeks after the application deadline\. We’ll share your university match and the next steps\.

##### 6\. Final steps

After you are matched Go Global will provide instructions to [apply to your host university](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/youve-been-matched "You've been matched")\.

Then, you’ll want to:

-   Make a budget
-   See which host university courses will count towards UBC course credits: see the Courses \+ Transfer Credits section of your [partner university page](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/partner-universities "Partner universities")
-   Talk to your faculty advisor about your degree requirements
-   Map out your courses needed to graduate, including exchange and study abroad, with the Go Global Study plan\.
