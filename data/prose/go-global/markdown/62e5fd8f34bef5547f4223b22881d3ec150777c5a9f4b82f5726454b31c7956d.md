## Who can go?

-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study
-   Students must have a minimum cumulative 70% GPA to attend this partnership

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   Commerce students \(UBCV\): select 'University of Warwick\-COMM'
-   All other students: select ''University of Warwick\-Uniwide' 

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(UW Semester 1\): September \- late December
-   UBC Term 2 \(UW Semester 2 and 3\): January \- July \(The Summer Term \(UW 3\) is mainly reserved for revision and examinations; although there are some subject areas which continue teaching modules\)\. 

***\*Arrangements can be made for Term 1 returning to UBC for term 2***

-   **Commerce students may attend:** UW Semester 1 only, OR UW Semester 1 &amp; 2 only, OR UW Semester 2 &amp; 3 only, OR UW Semester 1, 2 &amp; 3
-   **Engineering students may attend:** UW Semester 1, 2, 3 OR UW Semester 2 &amp; 3

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Engineering
-   Health Sciences
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Please research applicable course restrictions on the University of Warwick's exchange website
    -   See: [School of Engineering](http://www2.warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/eng)
    -   See: [Warwick Business School](https://www.wbs.ac.uk/)
-   Most of the modules you take at Warwick need to be within the Faculty that you will be accepted into \(generally, you can only take one module outside of the admitting department\)
-   Please note that Politics is not available for the Spring and Spring/Summer Semester\(s\)

[Partner Course Information](https://warwick.ac.uk/services/studentopportunity/studentmobility/studyatwarwick/moduleinformation/partyearstudents-examinationmethods/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   CATS divided by 4 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

The University of Warwick expects Study Abroad students to undertake the following amount of work:

-   120 CATS points \(60 ECTS\) for a full academic year
-   48 CATS \(24 ECTS\) for Autumn or Spring only
-   96 CATS \(48 ECTS\) for Autumn &amp; Spring
-   72 CATS \(36 ECTS\) Spring &amp; Summer

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)
