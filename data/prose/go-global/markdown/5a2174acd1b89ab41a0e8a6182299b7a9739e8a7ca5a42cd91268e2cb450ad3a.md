## Who can go?

-   Only Bachelor of Commerce and MBA \(UBCV\) students are eligible to apply to this partner university

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(KEDGE Fall semester\): mid\-September to December
-   UBC Term 2 \(KEDGE Spring semester\): January to mid\-June
-   UBC Full Year \(KEDGE Fall \+ Spring semester\): September \- June  
     
-   The academic year runs from mid\-September to mid\-June and is divided into 10 sequences\. One sequence is 3 weeks time \= 45 teaching contact hours \= 6 ECTS

### Areas of study available:

-   Commerce \(UBCV\)

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Review programs available through the KEDGE program [search](https://student.kedge.edu/programmes) tool \- make sure to check off "Bordeaux" or "Marseille" \(or both\) under the "Place" filter when searching
-   Note: Only 4th year UBC students are eligible for Masters Advanced courses

[Partner Course Information](https://student.kedge.edu/exchange-programmes/academic-information/academic-overview)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   The entire exchange program can be completed in English
-   Students must have at least a B1/B2 level to study in French \(self\-assess with: [Common European Framework of Reference for Languages \(CEFR\)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)\)
