## Who can go?

-   This partner is open to Commerce \(UBCV\) students only
-   Chulalongkorn offers Accounting and International Business Management courses for Commerce students
-   Students may take 1 course outside of the business program while at Chulalongkorn

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Chulalongkorn Fall term\): Early August to Mid December
-   UBC Term 2 \(Chulalongkorn Spring term\): Early January to Mid May

### Areas of study available:

-   Commerce \(UBCV\)

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Students must attend 80% of classes, and provide a letter to the BBA Office when they need to be absent
-   Students must wear a proper Chula uniform both inside and outside classroom or when they enter the library, computer room or contact the BBA Office
-   Composition of [courses](https://bba.acc.chula.ac.th/index.php/2014-06-27-06-11-06/exchange-incoming) Tentative Course Schedule
-   Chulalongkorn offers Accounting and International Business Management courses for Commerce students
-   Students can choose any course offered in each semester as long as they have appropriate prerequisite background and no time conflicts
-   Students may take 1 course outside of the business program while at Chulalongkorn
-   When selecting courses, ensure that they are available in the semester for which you are applying

[Partner Course Information](http://bba.acc.chula.ac.th/index.php/2014-06-27-06-11-06/exchange-incoming)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   Same as UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)
