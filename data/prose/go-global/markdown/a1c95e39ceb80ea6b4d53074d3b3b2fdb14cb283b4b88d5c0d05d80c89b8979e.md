## Who can go?

Students from all faculties\.

Most courses offered at Wageningen are relevant to students studying:

-   Food, Nutrition and Health
-   Forestry
-   Animal Biology
-   Earth Science
-   Environmental Science
-   Biology
-   Global Resource Systems
-   Geography and Urban Studies
-   Landscape Architecture
-   Related fields

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(WUR Autumn Term: September \- January
    -   Period 1 \(Sept to Oct\), Period 2 \(Nov to Dec\), Period 3 \(Jan\)
    -   can study Period 1 and 2 \(to earn a maximum of 12 UBC credits\) OR Period 1, 2, and 3 \(to earn a maximum of 15 UBC credits\)
-   UBC Term 2 \(WUR Spring Term\): February \- July
    -   Period 4 \(Feb to Mar\), Period 5 \(Mar to May\), Period 6 \(May to July\)

**\*Students who study at Wageningen in Term 1 and stay for Period 3 will be unable to return to UBC for Term 2**

### Areas of study available:

-   Architecture and Landscape Architecture
-   Arts
-   Forestry
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   The academic year is divided into 6 periods
-   Refer to [course information](https://www.wur.nl/en/education-programmes/study-abroad-and-exchange-students/incoming-students-to-wageningen-university/courses.htm)

[Partner Course Information](https://ssc.wur.nl/Handbook/Department)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   60 ECTS per academic year:
    -   Period 1, 2, 5, 6\- Maximum of 12 ECTS per period
    -   Period 3 and 4\- Maximum of 6 ECTS per period

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)
