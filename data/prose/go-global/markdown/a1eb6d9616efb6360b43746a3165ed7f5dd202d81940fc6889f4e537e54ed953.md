##### Why can’t I register myself on Workday?

UBC students have their degree progression, program requirements, and prerequisites/corequisites, etc\. recorded in their student profiles\. However, an exchange student’s academic history is not documented in the same way\. This means that if you try to register yourself on Workday, the action will be blocked by system restrictions set in place, or students may be removed from courses by the Department\. This is why Go Global reviews course requests and assists with the course registration process\.
