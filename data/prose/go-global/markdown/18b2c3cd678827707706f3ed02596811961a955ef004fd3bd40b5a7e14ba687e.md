## Who can go?

-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study

**Information for Law students:**

Law students have the option of doing either the SELF, DEUF programs or one semester of the International [LLM program](https://llm.univ-lyon3.fr/en/)\. The International and European Business Law is the recommended option for Law students\. The full LLM program is not available to UBC Law students\.

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   Commerce students:  Select 'Lyon III\-COMM'
-   Law students: Select 'Lyon III\-LAW'
-   All other students: Select 'Lyon III\-Uniwide

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Lyon 3 Fall semester\): Early September to Mid January \(regular/DEUF program\) OR to Mid December \(SELF program\)
-   UBC Term 2 \(Lyon 2 Spring semester\): Mid January to End May \(regular/DEUF program\) OR to End April \(SELF program\)

***\*SELF program ends in late December allowing term 1 students to return to UBC for term 2***  
***\*DEUF Program has Term 1 exams that end in mid\-January depending on the chosen stream within DEUF \(some end in December\)***

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Law
-   Management \(UBCO\)

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Please research applicable course restrictions on the Lyon III exchange website
-   SELF Program \- Compulsory courses \(Only students with A2 and below French can choose this pathway\):
    -   French as foreign languages: 50 hours 5 credits
    -   Introduction to French Culture and Civilization: 20 hours 2 credits
    -   Optional courses: 4 courses to be chosen from around 20 courses in business and arts
-   DEUF Program \- Compulsory courses \(Students with French B1 level and above **must** choose this pathway\):
    -   French as a foreign language: 20 hours per semester: 5 credits per semester
    -   Introduction to French Culture and Civilization: 10 hours per semester: 3 credits per semester
    -   Choice of courses in all faculties: 36 credits including 21 credits in French language
-   Commerce students who want to study through the **Bachelor of Business Management** program at Lyon III are **exempt** from any French\-language level considerations as the programs are taught entirely in English\.
    -   If you want to take elective coursework while at Lyon, please refer to the SELF and DEUF information above\.

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Limited course selection in English along with French language instruction through SELF program
-   Students must have at least an B1 level in French for the DEUF program \(To determine your level, please self\-assess with: [Common European Framework of Reference for Languages \(CEFR\)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-2-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-self-assessment-grid)\)
    -   SELF program is appropriate for French beginners and intermediate \(A2 and below\)
    -   DEUF program is appropriate for students with intermediate to advanced French \(B1 and above\)
-   The LLM program for Law students is in English
