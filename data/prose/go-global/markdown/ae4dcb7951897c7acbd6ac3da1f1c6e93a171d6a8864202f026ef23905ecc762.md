##### How much does iMED cost?

-   $204\.30 for 3 months of coverage during the waiting period for [the Medical Services Plan \(MSP\)](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international) \(provincial government health insurance mandatory for those residing in Canada for more than 6 months\), **or**
-   $295\.10 for one\-term exchange students; **and**
-   $11\.15 for a one\-time direct billing fee \(for Vancouver campus students only\)

The fees are charged to your [Workday](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/) account\. Fees are subject to change\.  
See [more information on iMED](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international) and [MSP](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international)\.
