The how behind a plan is as important as the strategy behind it\. During the consultation process, we identified four pathways to guide both the university’s collective action and implementation of *In Service* to ensure maximum impact\.

#### 4 PATHWAYS \- TO REACHING OUR GOALS 

1.  Partnerships for fundamental and use\-inspired research, creative endeavours, learning and capacity development
2.  Transformative global educational experiences for students
3.  Infrastructure \(people, data and processes\) to encourage cross\-disciplinary engagement on issues of global relevance
4.  Strategic communication to tell the story of our global endeavours, our partners and our collective impact

#### 4 ACTION AREAS \- FOR IMPLEMENTATION 

1.  Partnerships Canvas: an assessment framework to identify opportunities for enhanced collaboration and business model to guide our approach towards expanding partner types
2.  Global Education Experiences I: Global Citizenship through Virtual Learning
3.  Global Education Experiences II: Global Citizenship on Campus
4.  Communications Strategy
