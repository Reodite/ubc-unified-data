## Who can go?

-   This partnership is only available to management students on the Okanagan campus
-   MGMT 490 is not available  
     
-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements
    -   [**UBC Okanagan students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-exchange-program/eligibility/)

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(UB semester 1\): September \- January
-   Term 2 \(UB semester 2\): February \- June

**\*Students wishing to only study in Term 1 have the option of writing their final exams in December**

### Areas of study available:

-   Management \(UBCO\)

[Partner Course Information](https://www.ub.edu/economiaempresa-internacional/web/en/academic-information/)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits; full course load is 60 ECTS per year

### Course load Information

-   60 ECTS per academic year

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Additional note for language of instruction

-   Business and Economics courses are offered in English
-   Please refer to their [website](http://www.ub.edu/web/ub/en/sites/llengues/estudiants_de_mobilitat/estudiants_arribada/arribada/requeriments_linguistics.html) for further information on the language of instruction

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.
