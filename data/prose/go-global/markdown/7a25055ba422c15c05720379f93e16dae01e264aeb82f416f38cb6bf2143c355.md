## About the program

Learn Russian at Tallin University's Summer School, located in the capital city of Estonia\.

Through this three\-week summer school, students can:

-   Learn Russian at an accelerated pace in small class settings
-   Take Russian language courses at all levels for various interests and needs
-   Gain conversational practice for social exchanges and understanding native speakers in various contexts

While studying, students can experience Estonian culture, history, and the sights of the Estonian capital through cultural activities and weekend excursions\.

Note: Tallin Summer School offers other courses/programs; these are not available through Go Global

More specific and finalized program details can be found on the partner institution's website\. However, you must apply through Go Global to be considered for this program and ensure you follow the appropriate transfer credit process\.

## Academic planning

### Areas of study available:

-   Intensive Language Program

### Course equivalency formula

-   2 ECTS credits \= 1 UBC Credit

### Course load information

-   You will be enrolled in a 3\-week program, worth 5 ECTS\. A language placement test is given upon arrival and students are placed in the appropriate course level\. For this reason, course and year level obtained cannot be guaranteed in advance\.
