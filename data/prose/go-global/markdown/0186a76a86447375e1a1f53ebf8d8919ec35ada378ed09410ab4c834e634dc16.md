##### Am I eligible for X course?

If you believe that you meet the prerequisites and the course is not [restricted](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange/step-4-course-registration) for exchange students, feel free to request it on the Course Survey\. However, please be aware that Go Global cannot guarantee registration in every course you request on the survey\.  

Check restricted courses for exchange students here: [https://global\.ubc\.ca/go\-global/coming\-ubc/coming\-ubc\-exchange/step\-4\-course\-registration](https://global.ubc.ca/go-global/coming-ubc/coming-ubc-exchange/step-4-course-registration)\. Check if there are any prerequisites by clicking on a course schedule on Workday or the public Course Schedule\.  

Please use your best judgment about whether you meet the prerequisites based on the coursework you've previously completed at your home university\. If you need further guidance, you can reach out to the instructor of the course closer to the start of the semester\. The instructor may ask to see your transcript or other course materials to assess your eligibility\.
