## Who can go?

-   All applicants must meet Go Global eligibility requirements
    
    -   [**UBC Vancouver students**](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/exchange/eligibility "Eligibility")
    -   [**UBC Okanagan students**](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/global-engagement-office/go-global/outbound-exchange-program/eligibility/)
    
-   Exchange students can take coursework in most areas of study
-   Please note, the following areas are restricted: Department of Political Science, Department of Law \(within the School of Business and Social Sciences at Aarhus University\), and the Faculty of Health

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Aarhus Autumn term\): late August to late January \(exam period from mid\-December to late January\)
-   UBC Term 2 \(Aarhus Spring term\): late January to late June \(exam period from mid\-May to late June\)
-   UBC Full Year \(Aarhus Autumn \+ Spring term\): August \- June

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)
-   Engineering
-   Land and Food Systems
-   Management \(UBCO\)
-   Science

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Please note that students can select courses from one faculty only, and most faculties allow students to choose courses from one department only\.
-   For more information about course selection and to see details about course restrictions please see [here\.](https://international.au.dk/education/admissions/exchange/admission-to-aarhus-university/course-information-restrictions)

[Partner Course Information](https://kursuskatalog.au.dk/en/?year=2023-2024)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   ECTS credits divided by 2 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

-   60 ECTS per academic year

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

## Finances and funding

While you’re learning abroad, you remain a UBC student and are eligible for UBC awards, scholarships, and financial assistance\.

-   15 credits or equivalent full\-time program tuition per term, paid to UBC
