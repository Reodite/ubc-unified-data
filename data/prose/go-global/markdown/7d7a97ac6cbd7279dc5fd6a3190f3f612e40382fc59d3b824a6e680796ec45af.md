## Who can go?

-   Only Commerce students \(UBCV\) are eligible to apply to this partner
-   Students must have a minimum 70% cumulative average of above to attend this partnership
-   **Important Information for UBC Students Studying at Leeds in Their Final Year\-**If you plan to study at the University of Leeds during your final year at UBC, please note the following: official transcripts from Leeds are not issued until late June\-July\. Students attending Leeds for UBC Term 1 only \(Leeds Semester 1, from mid\-September to late January\) will receive a provisional transcript in March or April, and the official transcript will follow in July or August\. It is an official University of Leeds document, but the grades are not official\. Marks are provisional until moderated by external examiners after exams finish in June\. This may affect your ability to graduate on time or begin employment after your exchange\.
    -   **Vancouver Students:** Please discuss how this timeline may impact you with a Go Global Advisor and your Faculty Designate before deciding to proceed\. Some UBC faculties may accept an interim transcript to meet graduation requirements, but it is your responsibility to confirm this with your Faculty Designate before you go on exchange\.
    -   **Okanagan Students:** Okanagan students are not eligible to study at this partner institution\. 

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Leeds Semester 1\): Late September to Mid January
-   UBC Term 2 \(Leeds Semester 2\): Late January to Mid June

***\* Term 1 students who plan to return to UBC for term 2 can apply to do their exams at UBC\. Students will also need the approval of their home university and will also need to check the assessment for each of their modules, which can be taken remotely by checking with the relevant teaching schools\. There is a £50 administration fee \(total fee, not per exam\) paid to Leeds for this service\. Further information on how and when to apply to take January examinations overseas can be found on the*** [***Taking Assessments Abroad***](https://students.leeds.ac.uk/personalisedexamarrangements) ***webpage\.***

### Course equivalency Formula

-   4 credits \= 1 UBC credit

### Course load Information

-   60 credits per semester/120 credits for the full academic year
-   Students may take 50 credits per semester and still be considered to be a full\-time student, but must have this agreed with their home university, and be sure that this does not affect their visa status

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)

[Search for previously approved courses](https://tcp.goglobal.ubc.ca/search)

### Other opportunities at this partner

#### **British Universities Transnational Exchange Association \(BUTEX\)**

Each year BUTEX awards a number of £500 scholarships to students studying abroad for a single semester or full academic year\. The University of Leeds is a BUTEX member so incoming study abroad students applying to Leeds can submit a scholarship application\.

BUTEX scholarships are awarded based on a calendar year and not an academic year\. This means that students who are completing a full year of exchange that takes place across two calendar years will have two opportunities to apply, but each student can only win a BUTEX scholarship once\.

[Find out more and apply for the BUTEX scholarship](https://www.butex.ac.uk/scholarships/)\.

## Student experience

**Housing**   
[Information on accommodation](https://accommodation.leeds.ac.uk/)

University of Leeds accommodation is guaranteed to all new international students who apply by the application deadline \(1st July for September starters and 1st December for January starters\)\. Wi\-Fi, utility bills, laundry facilities, contents insurance, and sports membership at their gym, The Edge, are all included in the accommodation rent\.     
   
**Accessibility**    
Some destinations may be more accessible than others for students with disability\-related accommodations\. If you identify with having a disability or pre\-existing health condition which could impact your participation, or if you require academic accommodation, please contact a Go Global advisor before applying to explore options that are best for you\.
