## Who can go?

-   Students must have a cumulative average of at least 70% to attend this partnership

**When applying to Go Global:**

-   Students wanting to study Psychology: Select 'University of Exeter\-Psychology\-STUDY'
-   All other students: Select 'University of Exeter\-Uniwide'
-   The Penryn campus is well\-suited for UBCO's Bachelor of Sustainability students, particularly those in the Environmental Humanities concentration

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(Exeter Semester 1\): Late September \- Mid January
-   UBC Term 2 \(Exeter Semester 2\): Mid January \- Mid June

###### ***\* Alternative assessments may be offered to term 1 students returning to UBC for term 2\. Alternative assesments not available for psychology students***

### Course availability and restrictions

Students are not able to complete studies at Exeter in the following:

-   Students cannot combine modules taught at the [Penryn](https://www.exeter.ac.uk/study/studyabroadprogrammes/moduleselection/penryncampus/) and [Exeter\-based](https://www.exeter.ac.uk/study/studyabroadprogrammes/moduleselection/exetercampuses/) campuses; students must choose their modules from only one of these campus options\. 
-   Graduate School of Education, Biomedical Sciences, Drama and Liberal Arts are not available to UBC exchange students
-   Modules in some departments can be competitive; general information is provided on the module guidance link [here](http://www.exeter.ac.uk/studyabroad/inbound/studying/moduleselection/)\.
    -   While Psychology is normally limited, UBC students may complete some studies in this subject\. Students who want to take the majority of their courses in Psychology at Exeter must select the Exeter\-Psychology\-STUDY pathway when submitting their Go Global application\. Please speak to your Go Global Advisor for more information\.

[Partner Course Information](https://www.exeter.ac.uk/study/studyabroadprogrammes/moduleselection/)

### Course equivalency Formula

Exeter credits divided by 4 \= UBC credits

### Course load Information

60 Exeter credits per semester  
120 Exeter credits per academic year

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)
