##### When will I receive my iMED Card?

Shortly after course registration, exchange students are automatically enrolled in iMED, and the fees should appear on [Workday](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/)\. Students will also receive a welcome email from [GuardMe](https://sites.guard.me/ubc) approximately one month prior to the start of each academic term, with details about logging into the GuardMe portal and accessing the iMED Healthcare Access Card included\. Please watch for the email in your inbox and junk mail folders\.

Print your iMED card and keep it with you at all times\. The IMED and other students fees will be due during the first week of classes at UBC\. Details about fee payment deadlines can be found [here](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/dates-deadlines/)\.
