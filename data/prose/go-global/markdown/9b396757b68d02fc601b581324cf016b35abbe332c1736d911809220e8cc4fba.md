## Who can go?

-   Only Commerce \(UBCV\) and Arts students are eligible to apply to this partner university
-   Commerce \(UBCV\) students may only select courses from the Bayes Business School
-   Arts students will be admitted to one of the following schools and may only select courses offered by that faculty: The School of Communication &amp; Creativity, the School of Policy &amp; Global Affairs, and the School of Health &amp; Psychological Sciences \(SHPS\) Department of Psychology
    -   Find the courses available [here](https://www.city.ac.uk/prospective-students/courses?meta_school_sand=school+of+arts+and+social+sciences&amp;meta_level_sand=undergraduate+degree) for Arts Students
-   **Important Information for UBC Students Studying at City St George’s, University of London in Their Final Year\-**If you plan to study at the University of City St George’s, University of London during your final year at UBC, please note the following: official transcripts from City St\. George's University London are not issued until late July\-August\. Students attending for only UBC Term 1 will receive an interim transcript in March or April, and the official transcript will follow in July or August\. No official transcripts can be issued earlier than late July under any circumstances\. This may affect your ability to graduate on time or begin employment after your exchange\.
    -   **Vancouver Students:** Please discuss how this timeline may impact you with a Go Global Advisor and your Faculty Designate before deciding to proceed\. Some UBC faculties may accept an interim transcript to meet graduation requirements, but it is your responsibility to confirm this with your Faculty Designate before you go on exchange\.
    -   **Okanagan Students:** Please discuss how this timeline may impact you with your Go Global Advisor\.

## Academic planning

### Term dates

-   UBC Term 1 \(City Autumn term\): Late September to late\-January
-   UBC Term 2 \(City Spring term\): early January \- to mid\-June

***\*Term 1 students can usually complete assessments in December***  
***\*Commerce students: Ask the Bayes Student Exchange Office for more details at [bayes\-ug\-incoming@city\.ac\.uk](mailto:bayes-ug-incoming@city.ac.uk)*** 

### Areas of study available:

-   Arts
-   Commerce \(UBCV\)

### Course availability and restrictions

-   Exchange students will need to make a preliminary module selection on their application form and final module registration will take place during the first week of the term
-   Exchange Students are also eligible to take our 2 cultural elective modules \(further details of these classes will be provided during orientation\)
-   When selecting courses, ensure that they are available in the semester for which you are applying
-   Arts Students Students will need to provisionally select their modules before arrival\. Applicants will receive a list of available  
    modules upon receipt of the application\.
-   Arts students will register for courses in the [School of Communication &amp; Creativity](https://www.city.ac.uk/about/schools/communication-creativity), the [School of Policy &amp; Global Affairs](https://www.city.ac.uk/about/schools/policy-global-affairs) or the [School of Health &amp; Psychological Sciences \(SHPS\) Department of Psychology](https://www.city.ac.uk/prospective-students/courses/undergraduate/psychology)
-   Commerce students will register for courses in the [Bayes Business School](https://www.bayes.citystgeorges.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/placements-and-study-abroad/student-exchange-programme) 

[Bayes Business School Partner Course Information](https://studenthub.citystgeorges.ac.uk/new-students/registration-and-enrolment/student-administration/bayes-business-school-undergraduate-modules)

### Course equivalency Formula

-   4 City U of London credits \= 1 UBC credit
-   15 City credits \= 7\.5 ECTS

### Course load Information

-   It is recommended that students take 4 \-5 modules per term for a full course load

### Transfer credit

The [transfer credit process](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/new-programs-ubc-students/new-student-resources/transfer-credits-go-global "Transfer credits for Go Global") takes approximately 16 weeks following each submission deadline\. You should allow sufficient time to meet important deadlines that may affect you \(e\.g\. application for graduation, registration in courses that require prerequisites that you took on exchange, entry to a major/honours program, etc\.\)
