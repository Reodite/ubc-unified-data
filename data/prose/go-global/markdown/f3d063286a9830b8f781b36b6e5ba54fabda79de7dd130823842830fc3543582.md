#### Paying the Go Global Fee

The Go Global Fee \(CAD $440\) is an administrative fee required for all applicants to all Go Global programs\. The fee will be posted to your Workday account 1\-3 weeks following the application deadline, and will be due approximately 2 weeks later\. You will be notified of the due date by email once the fee is posted\.

Please see the [list of options](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/paying-tuition) you have for paying tuition\.

Students participating in [Research Abroad](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/research-abroad "Research Abroad") and [Global Seminar](https://goglobal.ubc.ca/go-global/programs-ubc-students/global-seminars "Global Seminars ") programs are subject to a different fee policy\.
