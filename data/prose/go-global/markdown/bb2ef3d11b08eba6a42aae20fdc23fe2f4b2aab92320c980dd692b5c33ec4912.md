##### What is the BC Medical Services Plan \(MSP\) and why do I need it?

The [Medical Services Plan \(MSP\)](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-insurance/health-insurance-international/medical-services-plan-msp-international) is a mandatory health insurance plan, for everyone who lives in British Columbia \(B\.C\. province\) for 6 months or longer\. Medical fees in BC are very costly, so to protect yourself against surprisingly big bills that can go up to $150 just to see a doctor\. With MSP, you will not pay when you seek basic medical care, and the fees for emergency care will be reduced to much less\.
