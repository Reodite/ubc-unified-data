##### What does being on a course waitlist mean \(am I in the course or not\)? What is the likelihood of getting in?

Waitlists are not available for all courses, and being on the waitlist does not guarantee registration in the course\. Students on a waitlist should check their registration in [Workday](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/) to see if they have been officially registered into the course\.

1.  If there is a waitlist for the course, register for the waitlist and contact [go\.global@ubc\.ca](mailto:go.global@ubc.ca) to ensure you have a token to be able to get off the waitlist into full registration when a seat becomes available
2.  Check the departmental website to review their waitlist procedures
3.  Keep your eye on the course via Workday
4.  Check the course on your Workday after the first class
5.  Attend your waitlisted course\(s\) where possible, and have a backup plan in place
