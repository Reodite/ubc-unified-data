##### \[Students\] Can I work while I am a VIRS student?

VIRS students may not have an employment relationship with UBC related to their research activity\.

As a VIRS student, you are considered [full\-time](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/immigration/ubc-definitions-immigration-purposes#full-time) for immigration purposes\.

You are eligible to work on\-campus in jobs not related to your research if you are full\-time \(see above\) and have a valid study permit\. You are only eligible to work during the dates you are a VIRS student\.

You are not eligible to work off\-campus as you are not enrolled in a program that leads to a degree, diploma or certificate at UBC\.

Make sure you meet the requirements to [work in Canada](https://students.ubc.ca/career/working-canada) and be sure to apply for a [Social Insurance Number](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/taxes/social-insurance-number-sin) before working\.
