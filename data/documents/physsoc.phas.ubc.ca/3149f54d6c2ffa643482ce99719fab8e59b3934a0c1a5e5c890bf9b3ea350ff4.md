The UBC Physics Society holds a number of academic events over the year\. These include review sessions for first\-year physics classes, Python and LaTex help sessions, and the occasional lecture event\! Please stay tuned to our [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/ubcphyssoc/) page for more updates and upcoming events\! Also be sure to join our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/xXKCS46) to keep in easy contact with classmates in Physics courses\!

**Upcoming 2023 events:**

**LaTeX Review Session January 25th at 4pm in HENN307**

Have you ever wondered what your math professors use to make those super nice\-looking equations on your assignments and exams? Does any of your homework involve having to use LaTeX? Or perhaps you’re looking to format a fancy research paper?

Physsoc will be hosting a LaTeX Basics Workshop on Wednesday January 25th at 4pm in HENN307\. We’ll be going over the ins and outs of the LaTeX typesetting language, from how to set up your document, to text and math formatting, to more advanced usage like figures and citations\. This is an interactive workshop with live demos and exercises, so bring your laptop to follow along\. Admission is free for Physsoc members, and $5 for non\-members \(so you might as well become a member\!\)\. Hope to see you there\!
