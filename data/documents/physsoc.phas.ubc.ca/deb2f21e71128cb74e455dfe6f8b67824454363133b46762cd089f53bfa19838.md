### PHYSSOC Membership \+ Building Access Form 2026/2027

The Physics Undergraduate Society \(PHYSSOC\) runs both social and academic events, manages a PHAS discord, and maintains the PHYSSOC lounge, HENN 307\. By becoming a member of PHYSSOC, you get access to member only events, discounts on exam packs, and may request Hennings building access\. Please purchase the membership via: https://www\.showpass\.com/ubc\-physics\-society\-membership\-20262027\-term\-1/ \. You will need to create an account to buy our membership\. After purchasing, fill out the form to confirm and request Hennings building access\.
