#### [UBC Physics Society](https://physsoc.phas.ubc.ca/2016/07/13/front-page-physsoc-slider/)

The UBC Physics Society \(Physsoc\) is a club for people who study physics, who enjoy physics, or who just want a cool place to spend their time with good company\. We have tutoring and sell exam packages for first and second year physics classes, run a physics lecture series and mentorship program for undergrads, hold review sessions for courses, Python, and LaTeX, and maintain a [private tutor list](https://physsoc.phas.ubc.ca/academic/private-tutors/)\. We put together various social events for people to wind down and mingle with other students, faculty and staff in the Physics &amp; Astronomy department\. We also run our very own [Discord server](https://discord.gg/xXKCS46), open to everyone, started by the [2020 council](https://physsoc.phas.ubc.ca/about/2020-2021-council/)\. You can find us in Hennings 307, where we have a study lounge, a silent study space, a microwave, a fridge, and a foosball table\.

For more updates, check out our [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ubcphysicssociety/)\.

For a list of upcoming events, check out the [News page](https://physsoc.phas.ubc.ca/news/)\.

We would like to acknowledge that Physsoc is located on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the Musqueam People\.
