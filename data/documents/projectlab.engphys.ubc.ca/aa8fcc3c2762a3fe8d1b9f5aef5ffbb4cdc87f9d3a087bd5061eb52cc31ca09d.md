The Engineering Physics Project Lab is unique in that it is the only capstone lab on the UBC campus with multiple staff fully dedicated to supporting student projects\. Combined, our staff have more than three decades of engineering experience in high technology industries and are committed to helping students learn practical, hands\-on engineering, communication, and management skills they can employ in their co\-op work terms, in graduate school, in their first jobs, or in their own entrepreneurial ventures\.
