## Waterjet Cutter

-   We have an OMAX Waterjet 2652 \(2foot x 4foot workspace\) located in Henning 219\.
-   [OMAX Layout and Make software tools](http://www.omax.com/software.php)
-   “OMAX Layout” and “OMAX Make” software is installed on the Waterjet PC\.
-   Ask Projectlab staff if you want to install the waterjet software on your own PC \(WIN only\)\.
-   Fabrication costs:  $1\.50 per minute of cutting time\.
-   Click [HERE](http://phaswaterjet.pbworks.com/w/page/16291919/UBC-PHYSICS-AND-ASTRONOMY-WATERJET-WIKI) for the waterjet wiki\.

##  Laser Cutter

-   [Lasercutter Operations](https://projectlab.engphys.ubc.ca/lasercutter-operations/) – see who is planning to use it, and when\.
-   We have the Versa Laser VLS 4\.60\. 24″ x 18″ workspace, 60W CO2 laser\.   Located in the Fabratory \(ChemPhys A040\)\.
-   [Instructions \[PDF\]](https://projectlab2019.phas.ubc.ca/projectlab.engphys.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/laser-cutter-instructions-Nov2017.pdf) on how to use the EngPhys laser cutter
-   Check out these [videos for laser cutter beginners](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLfCQFn-5zQeZwizYKJ4SH1w-P3TkE5dvL), especially \#5 and \#6
-   [Short video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-mDdCI6pQKc) showing the machine in action
-   [list of materials](http://www.pololu.com/docs/0J24/3) that can be cut  \(plastics that do not contain chlorine, wood, paper, cardboard\)  \(no metal\)
-   Click [HERE](http://phaswaterjet.pbworks.com/w/page/37058570/UBC%20Engineering%20Physics%20Project%20Lab%20Laser%20Cutter) for the laser cutter wiki\.

## 3D Printers

-   We have [Monoprice Select Mini Pro](https://www.monoprice.com/product?p_id=33012) Printers that use [PLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polylactic_acid) type filament for printing
-   Video about how to use the [Monoprice Select Mini 3D](https://youtu.be/6YZD6K7osco?t=219) Printers
-   Small objects \(\~1 cubic inch\) print in 1\-2 hours, larger ones can easily take 6 or 8 hours\.
-   Printing cost: $0\.10 per gram\. Free printing for capstone projects, pay when printing personal projects\.
-   Can’t do it yourself right now? See below, “outsourcing fabrication”
