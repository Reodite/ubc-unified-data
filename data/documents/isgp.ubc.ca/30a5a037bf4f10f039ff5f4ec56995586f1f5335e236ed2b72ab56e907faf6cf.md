**Mantle** is the journal of the Interdisciplinary Graduate Studies Program \(ISGP\) at the University of British Columbia\. The ISGP facilitates individualized graduate programs for its students and draws upon the resources of 35 university departments, 10 research centres and institutes as well as nine faculties\.

We are grateful to the Dean of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies and the Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(Office of the Provost and Vice\-President Academic\) for their ongoing support\. **Mantle** also owes its existence and success to the unswerving commitment and support of ISGP Administration\.
