## Getting Started

Congratulations\! Now that you have been admitted into the ISGP program, one of the first things to do is to create a Campus\-Wide\-Login \(CWL\) account so that you can access your records on the Student Service Centre \(SSC\) account\. For info on how to do so, please visit [here](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/how-create-account)\.

Once you set up your CWL, use it to log into your [SSC account](https://ssc.adm.ubc.ca/sscportal/) to access your records\.

## Student Profiles

Check out the [current student profiles](https://isgp.ubc.ca/student-profiles/) and learn more about your peers and cohort members\. Reach out to the ISGP admin to add your details to the profile page\.

## Academic Progress

The [Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies \(G\+PS\) web site](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/) is a great resource for any information you are looking for in regards to your program needs \(e\.g\. Auditing a course; Request for Leave; Extensions; Change in Registration; Applying for awards…etc\.\)

Here is a timeline and sequence of steps of your program \(from being admitted to graduation\)\. For more information on each step, please clink on the link:

-   [Admitted](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/newly-admitted)
-   [Subscribe to ISGP Mailing list](https://lists.ubc.ca/scripts/wa.exe?SUBED1=ISGP-STUDENTS&amp;A=1)
-   Maintain [continuous registration](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/managing-your-program/course-registration)
-   Develop [Program of Study](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/managing-your-program/establishing-your-program-study) and [Coursework](http://www.students.ubc.ca/calendar/index.cfm?tree=3,45,98,0)
-   Form [Supervisory Committee](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/supervision-advising/supervisory-committee)
-   Submit [ISGP Annual Progress Report](https://isgp2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/2023-2024-ISGP-ANNUAL-PROGRESS-REPORT.docx) \(June 30th every year\)
-   \(*PhD*\) [Comprehensive Exam](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/managing-your-program/comprehensive-examination) \(24 months – after finishing ALL course work\) – [**ISGP Comprehensive Exam Guidelines &amp; Checklist**](https://isgp2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/05/ISGP-Comprehensive-Exam-Guidelines-Checklist.docx) \(**MUST be submitted for approval prior to comprehensive exam**\)
    -   It is highly recommended that you complete your comps by the 24 month mark, leaving you with one year to prepare and successfully defend your thesis proposal\.
-   \(*PhD*\) Defend thesis proposal
-   \(*PhD*\) [Advancement to Candidacy](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/managing-your-program/advancement-candidacy) \(36 months – completed ALL course work; passed comprehensive exam; thesis proposal approved\)
-   Write [thesis/dissertation](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/dissertation-thesis-preparation)
-   [Submit thesis/dissertation](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/final-doctoral-exam/exam-overview) for revision/review
-   \(*PhD*\) G\+PS sends thesis/dissertation to [External Examiner](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/final-doctoral-exam/external-examiner)
-   \(*PhD*\) Final [Doctoral Oral Defence](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/final-doctoral-exam/doctoral-examination-final-oral-defence)
-   \(*PhD*\) Submit Doctoral thesis/dissertation [Approval Form](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/final-doctoral-exam/doctoral-dissertation-approval-form)
-   [Submit thesis/dissertation](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/final-dissertation-thesis-submission) online to [cIRcle](https://circle.ubc.ca/)
-   Apply for [Graduation](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/graduation)
-   Unsubscribe from ISGP Mailing list \([students](https://lists.ubc.ca/scripts/wa.exe?SUBED1=ISGP-STUDENTS&amp;A=1) or [supervisors](https://lists.ubc.ca/scripts/wa.exe?SUBED1=ISGP-SUPERVISORS&amp;A=1)\)

### Academic Interruption

If you feel that your academic progress is being hindered by extenuating circumstances, please contact the ISGP office immediately to discuss the possibility of a [leave of absence](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/forms/request-leave-absence)\. Simply send our office a brief email letting us know that you are experiencing difficulties\.  An email of this nature can later be used to support your request for a leave of absence\. 

**Please note that G\+PS** ***discourages retroactive requests of any nature**\.* The sooner you contact the office, the easier it is for us to help you find a solution\.

## **Supervisors and Supervisory Committee**

You are highly encouraged to consult the Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies [Handbook of Graduate Supervision](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/handbook-graduate-supervision)\.

If your supervisors have changed since you entered the ISGP, please notify the [ISGP Office](mailto:enid.ho@ubc.ca) and ask your new co\-supervisor to fill out the [Student Supervisor Agreement](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/forms/student-supervisor-agreements)\.

The composition of your co\-supervisors and supervisory committee must be at least 50% UBC members\.

To recommend a non\-UBC **supervisor**, please fill out the form [here](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/forms/recommendation-supervision-graduate-student-non-gps-member) and submit all documents to the ISGP for further approval\.

To recommend a non\-UBC **supervisory committee member**, please fill out the form [here](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/forms/recommendation-non-gps-member-join-supervisory-committee) and submit all documents to the ISGP for further approval\.

*\***NOTE**:* *Indication \(from the graduate program\) of the particular qualifications that make the nominee suitable **must** be provided by student’s supervisor as a rationale\.*

*Only completed forms and required documents will be accepted and processed\.*

For more information on supervisors and supervisory committees, please visit the [Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies website](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/supervision-advising)\.

## **Mailing Lists**

### STUDENTS Email Announcement List

All current ISGP students **must** subscribe to the **ISGP\-STUDENTS** mailing list in order to receive important announcements \(awards, deadlines, reminders…etc\.\)

To subscribe to **ISGP\-STUDENTS**, please sign up [here](https://lists.ubc.ca/scripts/wa.exe?SUBED1=ISGP-STUDENTS&amp;A=1)\.

You will then receive an email asking you to confirm your subscription to the **ISGP\-STUDENTS** list\.

### SUPERVISORS Email Announcement List

All faculty members supervising ISGP students **must** subscribe to the **ISGP\-SUPERVISORS** mailing list in order to receive important graduate program\-related announcements \(awards, deadlines, reminders…etc\.\)

To subscribe to **ISGP\-SUPERVISORS**, please sign up [here](https://lists.ubc.ca/scripts/wa.exe?SUBED1=ISGP-SUPERVISORS&amp;A=1)\.

You will then receive an email asking you to confirm your subscription to the **ISGP\-SUPERVISORS** list\.

### Unsubscribing

If you have graduated from the ISGP or you no longer supervise students at the ISGP, and wish to unsubscribe from the ISGP mailing list, you may do so [here](https://lists.ubc.ca/scripts/wa.exe?SUBED1=ISGP-STUDENTS&amp;A=1) \(for students\), and [here](https://lists.ubc.ca/scripts/wa.exe?SUBED1=ISGP-SUPERVISORS&amp;A=1) \(for supervisors\)\.
