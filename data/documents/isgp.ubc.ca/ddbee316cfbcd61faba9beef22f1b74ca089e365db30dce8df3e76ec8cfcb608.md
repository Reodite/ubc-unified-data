### ***Deadline:** **Tuesday September 15, 2020 @ 12pm \(PDT\)***

The objective of the [Canada Graduate Scholarships\-Doctoral \(CGS D\) Program](http://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/PG-CS/CGSD-BESCD_eng.asp) is to promote continued excellence in Canadian research by rewarding and retaining high\-calibre doctoral students at Canadian institutions\. By providing support for a high\-quality research training experience to awardees, the CGS D program strives to foster impacts within and beyond the research environment\.

**Note:** In addition to the CGS D, each agency has its own doctoral awards\. Refer to the appropriate literature for [CIHR](http://www.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/50513.html#details-panel2), [NSERC](http://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/PG-CS/BellandPostgrad-BelletSuperieures_eng.asp) and [SSHRC](http://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca/funding-financement/programs-programmes/fellowships/doctoral-doctorat-eng.aspx) for further details\.  Meritorious CGS D applicants may also be eligible for agency\-specific doctoral awards\. Applicants complete and submit one application to be considered for both a CGS D scholarship and an agency doctoral award \(exception: [CIHR DFSA](https://www.researchnet-recherchenet.ca/rnr16/vwOpprtntyDtls.do?prog=3352&amp;view=currentOpps&amp;org=CIHR&amp;type=EXACT&amp;resultCount=25&amp;sort=program&amp;next=1&amp;all=1&amp;masterList=true)\)\. CGS D scholarships are offered to top\-ranked eligible applicants\.  Some eligibility requirements for CGS D differ from those of the [agency\-specific doctoral awards](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/PG-CS/CGSD-BESCD_eng.asp#a3)\.

Please visit the [Awards and Scholarships page](https://isgp.ubc.ca/current-students/awards-scholarships/) for more info\.
