**–** **Due to the ongoing strike at Canada Post, please choose electronic submission of transcripts \(whenever possible\)**

### PROCEDURE

Please refer to our “[Application Information](https://isgp2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?p=1364)” page for details\.

**FINDING RESEARCH SUPERVISORS**

It is a major commitment for faculty members to co\-supervise your program of study; therefore we encourage you to start this process early\. For tips, please click [here](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/application-admission/finding-supervisor)\. You may also consult the [UBC Experts Guide](http://experts.news.ubc.ca/)\. Please note that it is the applicants’ responsibilities to identify supervisors\. When approaching potential supervisors, it is sometimes helpful to send the following:

-   A [Statement of Proposed Research Topic \(SPRT\) Form](https://isgp2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/ISGP-SPRT-Form.docx)
-   A rationale for why they should be your potential research supervisor
-   A statement indicating why you are applying to ISGP instead of a disciplinary department

For more information and tips on finding supervisors, please visit the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies [website](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/application-admission/finding-supervisor)\.
