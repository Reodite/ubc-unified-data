[https://isgp2\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2011/06/IMG\_85141\.jpg](https://isgp2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/06/IMG_85141.jpg)On April 21st 2011, Dr\. Amartya Sen visited UBC for the celebration of ISGP’s 40th Anniversary as a program\.  Dr\. Sen delivered a lecture on Interdisciplinarity as the keynote speaker in ISGP’s 40th Anniversary lecture series and received an honorary degree from the University\.

Dr\. Sen’s lecture and photos from the ceremony  can be found below:
