> We love to hear from former VSP students to hear about their experiences\. Learn about Evelyn’s favourite memories and top tips for future students\!

---

***What was your favourite event/activity at VSP 2023? Why?***

My favourite activity was the trip to Lonsdale Quay organized by the volunteers at VSP\. We got to explore a different area in Vancouver\. There was a craft fair the day we went which was really cool to explore with the volunteers\. We also got to travel on the yellow school buses\.

***What was your personal highlight of the program?*** 

My personal highlight was going to Koerner’s pub for trivia, I really enjoyed getting to know other people and trying to win the quiz they host every Tuesday\.

***If you were to recommend VSP to other students, what would you say?***

I would say to other students to go in with an open mind\. It is a great confidence\-building experience\.

***If there’s one word you could use to describe your experience, what would it be? Why?*** 

Adventurous\- it was a lot of new experiences over the month which was very exciting and fast paced\.

***What advice would you give to future VSP students?*** 

I would say to future VSP students be ready to be changed whether through being changed by the people you meet or the places you visit

***What is one cool thing you learned during class?***

That the Turkish language has no he or she pronouns\.
