Health and Exercise Sciences students focus on the interdisciplinary nature of human health, including psychological, physiological, neuromechanical and socio\-cultural aspects of movement\.

Before students begin their first work term, they will have completed a two\-year core program\. They start into their co\-op term as motivated, focused individuals with demonstrated leadership and teamwork skills to enhance the health and quality of life for the community\-at\-large\.

You can find our graduates working in health promotion organizations, community recreation centres, rehabilitation clinics, professional sport teams, and sport marketing agencies around the world\.

##### Students: Apply to Co\-op

To learn more about how and when to apply to co\-op in Human Kinetics, visit the co\-op program page [UBC Okanagan](https://coop.ok.ubc.ca/current-ubc-students/)\.
