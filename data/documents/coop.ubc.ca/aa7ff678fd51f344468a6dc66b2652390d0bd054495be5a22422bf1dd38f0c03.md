As the largest school of its type in Canada, the UBC Faculty of Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship is recognized both nationally and internationally for its degree programs and the quality of its research\.

Earn a Bachelor of Science or a Bachelor of Urban Forestry degree through 1 of 6 programs offered by the Faculty of Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship\. Degree programs included are Urban Forestry, Conservation, Forestry, Forest Sciences, Wood Products and Bioeconomy\.

Participating in co\-op allows students to integrate their studies with valuable employment experience by alternating their eight academic terms with five co\-op work terms over a five year period\. Upon successful completion of the program, students receive a co\-op designation on their degree\.

Forestry co\-op students are available for 4, 8 or 12 month work terms and bring to employers a solid technical foundation, including field and lab experience, complemented by enthusiasm, a strong desire to learn and critical thinking\.

##### Students: Apply to Co\-op

To learn more about how and when to apply to co\-op in Forestry, visit the co\-op program page [here](https://forestry.ubc.ca/students/co-op/students/)\.
