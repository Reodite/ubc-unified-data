## edX Registration Tutorial

You only need to do this process when registering for the first time\. After doing so, you can simply login with your CWL account\.

## **1**

Visit the main page: [https://thesismodules\.edst\.educ\.ubc\.ca/](https://thesismodules.edst.educ.ubc.ca/)

---

## **2**

Choose the module

[Image](https://thesismodules-edst-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/Module-Library-Thesis-Modules.jpg)

---

## **3**

If you **have not registered**: Go ahead and Register

[Image](https://thesismodules-edst-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/Screenshot_2020-08-10-Educational-Policy-Analysis-Conceptual-and-Qualitative-Approaches-Thesis-Modules.png)

---

## **4**

Log in to your UBC CWL

[Image](https://thesismodules-edst-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/Screenshot_2020-08-10-CWL-Authentication-940x288.png)

##   

---

## **5**

Fill in your details, create account

[Image](https://thesismodules-edst-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/Screenshot_2020-02-13-Sign-in-or-Register-edX.png)

If you have registered but is logged out: click the “logged in” button
