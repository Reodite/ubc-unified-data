[UBC Manufacturing Engineering Class of 2025](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2025-06/img_6809-1.jpg.webp?itok=gFOKB7AM)

June 3, 2025

On May 28, 2025, the UBC Manufacturing Engineering program proudly celebrated the accomplishments of this year’s graduating class with a heartfelt reception held at Cominco Library\. The event brought together students, faculty, staff, and proud loved ones to honour the achievements and journeys of our newest alumni\.

Laughter, reflections, and well\-wishes filled the room as graduates shared memories with their mentors and peers—marking not just the end of their academic chapter, but the beginning of an exciting future\. 

On behalf of the entire MANU community, we extend our warmest congratulations to the Class of 2025\. You have inspired us with your determination, creativity, and commitment to excellence\. As you step into the next stage of your careers and lives, we encourage you to carry forward the spirit of UBC’s motto, *Tuum Est*—“It is yours\.” The future is indeed yours to shape\.
