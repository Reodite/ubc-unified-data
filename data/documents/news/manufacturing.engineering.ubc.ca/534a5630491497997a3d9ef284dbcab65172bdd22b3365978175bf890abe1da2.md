May 31, 2023

On June 1st, 2023, the class of 2023 celebrated their graduation from the manufacturing engineering program, following in the footsteps of the pioneering class of 2022\.

This accomplishment not only demonstrates their hard work and dedication but also represents a significant milestone for the program\.

The graduates are now equipped to apply their knowledge and contribute to the ever\-changing manufacturing industry\. They are the torchbearers for future graduates, entrusted with upholding the program’s best practices and UBC engineering values\.

Congratulations to the class of 2023\!
