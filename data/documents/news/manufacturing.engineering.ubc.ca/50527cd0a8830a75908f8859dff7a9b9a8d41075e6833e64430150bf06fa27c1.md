September 19, 2024

UBC Manufacturing Engineering Associate Director Dr\. Adam Clare has been elected Fellow of the prestigious Royal Academy of Engineering in the United Kingdom\. Recognized for his exceptional contributions to the field, Dr\. Clare joins an elite group of global engineering leaders\.

With a strong research focus on sustainable manufacturing technologies, Dr\. Clare's work spans additive manufacturing, aerospace, biomedical applications, and more\. He will be formally inducted during a ceremony in London on November 27, 2024\.

Congratulations to Dr\. Clare on this remarkable achievement\!
