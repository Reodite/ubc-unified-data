March 17, 2025

On March 14, 2025, first\-year engineering students engaged with senior Manufacturing Engineering students, explored the MANU Maker space, and gained valuable insights into the opportunities within the program during First\-Year Pizza Night\.  

The event provided a space for first year students to connect with aspiring Manufacturing Engineering peers and potential mentors, learn the benefits of the program, and explore the potential career paths in the field\.  

Through these events, the Manufacturing Engineering program continues to foster a community of learning and engagement\.
