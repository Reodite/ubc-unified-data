April 11, 2025

On April 4, 2025, MANU 330 students gathered in front of the Frank Forward building for MANU Race Day, an exciting showcase of engineering skill and creativity\. Students designed, built, and raced their own remote\-controlled cars, transforming classroom concepts into real, functional prototypes\.    
  
Each team demonstrated problem\-solving, manufacturing expertise, and strategic thinking through their unique designs\.    
  
The event was a celebration of innovation and practical application of engineering principles in a fun, high\-energy competition\.
