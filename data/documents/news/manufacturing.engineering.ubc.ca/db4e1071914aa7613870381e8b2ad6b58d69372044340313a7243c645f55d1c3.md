    ## [Celebrating the Class of 2025](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/news/2025/celebrating-class-of-2025)
    
    On May 28, 2025, the UBC Manufacturing Engineering program proudly celebrated the accomplishments of this year’s graduating class with a heartfelt reception held at Cominco Library\. The event brought together students, faculty, staff, and proud loved ones to honour the achievements and journeys of our newest alumni\.Laughter, reflections, and well\-wishes filled the room as graduates shared memories with their mentors and peers—marking not just the end of their academic chapter, but the beginning of an exciting future\.

### [UBC Manufacturing Engineering Receives Full 6\-Year Accreditation from CEAB](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/news/2025/ubc-manufacturing-engineering-receives-full-6-year-accreditation-ceab)

We are proud to share that the UBC Manufacturing Engineering program has been granted a full six\-year accreditation by the Canadian Engineering Accreditation Board \(CEAB\)—the maximum possible term—extending through to June 30, 2031\.
