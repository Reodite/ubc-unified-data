March 10, 2025

On March 7, 2025, the Manufacturing Engineering program took part in the APSC First Year Program Fair in the Kaiser Atrium\. This event provided first\-year engineering students with the opportunity to ask questions to MANU students and faculty, gaining insider knowledge about the program\. Conversation topics ranged from career prospects, curriculum details, hands\-on learning experiences, and advice\. Students also had the chance to learn about design teams and explore the Manufacturing industry\.    
  
The Manufacturing Engineering program strives to provide valuable networking opportunities for both current MANU students and those interested in the program\!
