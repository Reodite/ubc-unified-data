April 7, 2025

On April 3, 2025, senior Manufacturing Engineering students showcased their year\-long capstone projects at Design and Innovation Day, held in the Life Sciences Centre’s East Atrium\. A total of 9 innovative projects were showcased\. Working in teams of up to 6, MANU students demonstrated their manufacturing expertise, technical creativity, and collaborative problem\-solving through interactive exhibits and hands\-on demonstrations\.    
  
These projects offered innovative solutions to real\-world challenges, ranging from robotic imaging platforms to alternative materials for mountain bike components\. They highlight MANU students’ readiness to contribute meaningfully to the future of advanced manufacturing and product development\.
