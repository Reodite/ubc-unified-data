[Manufacturing Engineering Board of Study 2025](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2025-03/IMG_6238%20%281%29_11zon.jpg.webp?itok=jnmnOATy)

January 31, 2025

On January 29, 2025, the Manufacturing Engineering program held its annual Board of Study meeting to continue previous discussions and strategic planning\. This gathering provided an opportunity to assess past recommendations and plan for the future development of the program\.

The two main areas of discussion were Generative AI in Manufacturing Engineering, which explored how AI\-driven advancements could be integrated into the curriculum, and Amplifying the Capstone Impact, which focused on enhancing capstone projects to better align with industry needs\.

The MANU Board of Study plays a crucial role in guiding the program’s ongoing improvement, advancement and success\.
