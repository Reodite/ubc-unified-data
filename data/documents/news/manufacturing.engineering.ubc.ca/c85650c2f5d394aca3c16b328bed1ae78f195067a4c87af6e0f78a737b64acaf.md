October 3, 2024

From September 20\-22, the UBC Manufacturing Undergraduate Club organized its annual retreat, bringing together UBC Manufacturing Engineering students from both the Vancouver and Okanagan campus to visit Manning Park\.    
  
This retreat provided an opportunity for MANU students to connect with fellow peers, immerse themselves in the beauty of British Columbia’s outdoors, enjoy delicious meals, and participate in a range of engaging activities\.    
  
We hope everyone had an incredible time, building new friendships and creating unforgettable memories\!
