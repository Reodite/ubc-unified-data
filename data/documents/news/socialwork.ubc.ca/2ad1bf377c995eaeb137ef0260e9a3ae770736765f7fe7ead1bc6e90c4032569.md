The School of Social Work \(UBC\-Vancouver\) is seeking qualified social workers with MSW or PhD who are primarily engaged in social work practice as part\-time Adjunct Professor for the following course in Summer Term 2, July 3 – Aug 10, 2018 of the academic year 2017\-18\.

**SOWK553C\.001 Quantitative Research** – 3 credits, Summer Term 2, Tues &amp; Thurs 5:00pm – 8:00pm

Appointment at the adjunct rank does not imply that the appointee has the qualifications necessary for appointment to one of the regular academic ranks\. The adjunct professor will be responsible for teaching, holding regular office hours, marking assignments and exams and reporting grades\. Remuneration is commensurate with experience\. Summer 2017 course outline can be found on our [website](http://socialwork.ubc.ca/current-students/course-outlines/)\.

Letter expressing interest and CV will be accepted for the above courses until **Feb\. 22, 2018**\. Contact: [shirley\.yue@ubc\.ca](mailto:shirley.yue@ubc.ca)

UBC hires on the basis of merit and is committed to employment equity and diversity within its community\. We especially welcome applications from members of visible minority groups, women, Aboriginal persons, persons with disabilities, persons of minority sexual orientations and gender identities, and others with the skills and knowledge to engage productively with diverse communities\. We encourage all qualified persons to apply; however, Canadian citizens and permanent residents of Canada will be given priority\.
