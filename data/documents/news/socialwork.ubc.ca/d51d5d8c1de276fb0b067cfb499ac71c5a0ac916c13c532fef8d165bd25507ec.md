Nanivara staff presented their project at ArcticNet, and shared Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit with researchers in western science\.  Nanivara connects traditional knowledge with modern methods of documentation, and ArcticNet is a conference that brings together the scientific community from around the world to share research related to the Arctic\.
