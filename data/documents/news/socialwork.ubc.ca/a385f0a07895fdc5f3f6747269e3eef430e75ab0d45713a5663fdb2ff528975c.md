### Celebrating Social Work Week

Social Work Week in British Columbia celebrates the accomplishments of social workers\. The provincial government declared **March 13\-19, 2022**, Social Work Week in British Columbia\. Social workers throughout BC plan activities that highlight their essential contribution to the health and well\-being of British Columbians\. The theme for 2022 is In Critical Demand – Social Work is Essential\. BCASW wishes you a Happy Social Work Week\!

### Days in the Lives of Social Workers

In the run up to BC Social Work Week \(March 13\-19, 2022\) the BCASW will host four online sessions via Zoom, each featuring a different panel of professional social workers sharing their experiences of working in their chosen field\. They will discuss the job they do and the challenges and rewards they face, describe a typical day, and respond to questions\. The event, Days in the Lives of Social Workers, runs March 7\-10 at 6pm\. This is a free event provided for BC social work students, particularly those about to graduate and launch their career\.
