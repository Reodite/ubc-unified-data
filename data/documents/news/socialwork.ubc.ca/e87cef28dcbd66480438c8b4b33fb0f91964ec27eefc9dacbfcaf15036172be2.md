The School of Social Work is now accepting applications for the position of **Sessional Lecturer** for the SOWK 554C\.002 undergraduate course: Research – Program Evaluation, in Term 1 &amp; 2 of the academic year 2016\-17\.

**The deadline for applications is May 5, 2016\.** 

Please see the [full job posting](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2013/05/external-Sessional-posting-SOWK554C.002-2016-17.pdf) for more details and how to apply\.
