The School of Social Work \(UBC\-Vancouver\) is seeking qualified social workers with MSW or PhD who are primarily engaged in social work practice as part\-time Adjunct Professor for the following course in Term 1, September 5 – December 1, 2017 of the academic year 2017\-18\.

SOWK440B\.001 **Addictions** – 3 credits, Term 1, Thurs, 2:00pm – 5:00pm

Appointment at the adjunct rank does not imply that the appointee has the qualifications necessary for appointment to one of the regular academic ranks\. The adjunct professor will be responsible for teaching, holding regular office hours, marking assignments and exams and reporting grades\. Remuneration is commensurate with experience\. 2016\-17 course outlines can be found on our website: [https://socialwork\.ubc\.ca/current\-students/course\-outlines](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/current-students/course-outlines)

Letter expressing interest and CV will be accepted for the above courses until **June 27, 2017**\. Contact: [sowk\.recruitment@ubc\.ca](mailto:sowk.recruitment@ubc.ca)\.

View the [full job posting](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2017/06/external-posting-adjunct-position-available-2017-18.pdf)\.
