Read here the newly released report: “[Sexual Assault at the University of British Columbia: Prevention, Response, and Accountability](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2016/09/USAP_Report.pdf)\.”

Natalie Clark, Assistant Professor with the School of Social Work until July 2015, was one of the six panel members who worked in this important report\. The report provides recommendations for an action plan that addresses the presence of sexual and gendered violence at UBC\.
