Posted: January 12, 2016

The School of Social Work invites applications for the position of Director, with an anticipated start date of July 1, 2016\.

We expect that the successful candidate will be appointed at the rank of full Professor; however, exceptional candidates at the senior Associate Professor level also will be considered\. The successful candidate will hold an MSW and Ph\.D\. in Social Work or a related field and bring to the position a distinguished record of research, excellence in teaching at the graduate and undergraduate levels, and strong communication skills across diverse partnering groups\. A demonstrated ability and commitment to play a significant leadership role within the School, the University and the wider community – both local and international – is also expected\.

**Please see the [job posting](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2016/01/Director-School-of-Social-Work-Jan-2016.pdf) \(PDF\) for more details\.**

*Updated March 4, 2016: This posting is now closed\. Thank you for your interest\.*
