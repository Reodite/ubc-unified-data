Stephanie Wahab

The UBC School of Social Work hosted our 90th Anniversary Fellow Keynote Lecture on March 9, 2020\. We welcomed Stephanie Wahab, a professor at [Portland State University’s School of Social Work](https://www.pdx.edu/ssw/home), who presented Teaching Social Justice in Dangerous Times: Practices of Hope\.

View the lecture [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=630bpBrSUWY&amp;feature=youtu.be)\.
