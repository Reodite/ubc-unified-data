Welcome to [***The Knowledge Exchange: A Partnership\-in\-Action Podcast***](https://blogs.ubc.ca/swkx/), founded and hosted by Dr\. Barbara Lee, a [Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization \(KxM\) Scholar](https://kx.ubc.ca/ubc-kxm-scholars) \(2023\-2025\) in the University of British Columbia \(UBC\), School of Social Work\.

Each episode will feature a discussion between UBC School of Social Work faculty and their community partners about their  projects together and the impact emerging from their knowledge exchange and mobilization practices\.

**Episode 1**

We’re thrilled to announce the release of our very [first podcast episode](https://blogs.ubc.ca/swkx/episodes/episode-1/) featuring **Dr\. Richard Vedan**, Associate Professor Emeritus, Elder Scholar at the UBC School of Social Work\.

ImageIn this powerful and reflective episode titled **Rediscovering Roots: An Indigenous Scholar’s Story of Connection and Reclamation**, Dr\. Vedan shares his journey from military service to academia, his work addressing intergenerational trauma, and his lifelong advocacy for Indigenous communities\. He offers insights into identity, resilience, and equity; while highlighting the role of social work in fostering systemic change and reconciliation\.

As we celebrate Dr\. Vedan’s remarkable contributions—most recently recognized with the **2024 Inspiring Social Worker of the Year Award**—this episode is a must\-listen for anyone passionate about social justice, Indigenous education, and transformative leadership\.
