Jack York, UBC School of Social Work’s CECP Program Coordinator \(also Instructor and Clinician\), joins *The Early Edition* host, Stephen Quinn, on CBC Listen to talk about our new Micro\-certificate in Mental Health and Substance Use; a new training program aimed at equipping frontline workers with practical tools to handle mental health and substance use emergencies safely and compassionately\.

The new UBC Micro\-certificate in Mental Health and Substance Use is a part\-time, asynchronous, online program designed to provide learners with the foundational knowledge and core skills they need to effectively and ethically engage in frontline service provision to individuals experiencing mental health and substance use challenges in multiple service contexts\.
