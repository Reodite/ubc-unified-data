As one way to carry out our commitment to remember the historical and current injustices faced by Indigenous Peoples, to transform social work education and practice to end these injustices, to uphold Indigenous rights, and to work towards healing and reconciliation the ***Indigenous Resurgence Project*** published a website for social work educators on Orange Shirt Day, September 30, 2024\.

The website can be found here: **[indigenous\-resurgence\.arts\.ubc\.ca/](http://indigenous-resurgence.arts.ubc.ca/)**

For information about the website or the *Indigenous Resurgence Project* feel free to contact Marie Nightbird, [marie\.nightbird@ubc\.ca](mailto:marie.nightbird@ubc.ca) or Kelly Allison, [Kelly\.allison@ubc\.ca\.](mailto:Kelly.allison@ubc.ca)
