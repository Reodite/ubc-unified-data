On Sep\. 8, the School of Social Work community gathered at the Musqueam Cultural Centre to start the new academic year together\. The event saw BSW, MSW, and PhD students gathering with Social Work faculty and staff for a full day of reflection and celebration\.

The day’s program opened with a traditional welcome from the Musqueam Elder Dr\. [Larry Grant](https://cis.arts.ubc.ca/profile/elder-larry-grant/), followed by greetings from Elder Scholar Dr\. [Richard Vedan](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/richard-vedan/), who offered welcoming words to students, faculty, staff, alumni, and community members at the start of the new academic year\. Faculty and staff introductions provided attendees with an opportunity to meet the people who support the School’s teaching, research, and community engagement\.

Following a coffee break, attendees participated in round table discussions about their unique pathways to the School of Social Work, which created space for meaningful dialogue and connection\.

Lunch catering inspired by traditional West Coast cuisine was provided by Salishan Catering, and attendees had the opportunity to share lunch and connect at the beautiful Fraser River estuary just outside of the venue\.

The afternoon program featured remarks from the [BC Association of Social Workers \(BCASW\)](https://www.bcasw.org/), followed by the Paul Cheng Memorial Lecture, delivered by [Kaitlyn Nohr,](https://www.instagram.com/wildchrs/) recipient of the 2026 Inspiring Social Worker of the Year Award\. Her lecture reflected on her career in harm reduction and the realities of social work in remote communities in B\.C\., and inspired thoughtful discussion among attendees\.

The day concluded with a Coast Salish music and dance performance by the [Coastal Wolf Pack](http://tsatsustalqayu.ca/) and closing remarks from Acting Director [Silvia Bartolic](https://sociology.ubc.ca/profile/silvia-bartolic/)\.

Bringing together students, alumni, faculty, staff, and community partners, the annual Welcome Back event offers a meaningful opportunity to begin the academic year in community, celebrate shared commitments to social justice, and strengthen the connections that sustain the School’s work throughout the academic year\.
