Our MSW students from the Qualitative Research classes will be showcasing their research on **April 8, 2016**, from **8:45 am to 1:00 pm** in the Jack Bell Building \(2080 West Mall\)\. This year’s research presentations will be in a new format, dedicating an entire morning to a mini\-conference that will feature three panel sessions of students presenting their work and a poster session\.

All students, faculty, staff, research participants, friends and family are invited\. Please join us\!

Please click here to view the [event program](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2016/04/MSW-Conference-Program.pdf)\.
