1.  University of Benin \(Nigeria\)
2.  York University \(Canada\)
3.  University of Windsor \(Canada\)

**Brief Description:** This fiver\-year project is funded by CIDA/AUCC to enhance the social work capacity of Nigeria through strengthening social work training programs at the University of Benin and social work personnel of the government and non\-government organizations particularly in the Edo State of Nigeria\. Women empowerment and gender equality are the two key focus of this project\.
