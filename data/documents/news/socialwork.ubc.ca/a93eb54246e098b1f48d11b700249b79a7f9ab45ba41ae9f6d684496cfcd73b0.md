UBC students are invited to participate in a contest that not only beautifies the Jack Bell Building but also reflects our shared commitment to social justice and an ethic of care\.

Introducing an artwork contest where you can express in a creative way what equity and social justice mean to you\.

All eligible contest entries will be showcased in a public forum as well as judged by committee\. The winning submissions will have their work reproduced on vinyl, displayed on the five pillars that greet you as you enter the Jack Bell Building\. Winner will also receive an ipad and the opportunity to knit the School together in images that reflect our shared values\.

### Submissions

While we welcome all forms of visual art in the form of paintings, photography, graffiti, street art, digital art, comics and collages, if the artwork is not created digitally it **must** be properly photographed in high resolution in order for it to be reproduced onto vinyl\.

Artwork must be original and royalty\-free\.

Please include brief description of the artwork and its intended message\.

More than one submission is allowed per student or group\.

Depending on the number of winning submissions, artwork may be displayed over multiple pillars\. It may be necessary to crop some of the artwork and/or zoom in to a specific part of the artwork in order to fit the vinyl specifications\. We recommend that submissions take into account the vertical and narrow columns\. Columns are 30" x 91" or 76\.5cm x 231cm\. Please note the vinyl will only appear on the front\-facing portion of the column \(it will not wrap around\)\.

Once submitted, the School has the right to reproduce the artwork both physically and online on the website and in social media\.

### Application Process

To apply, please email sowk\.program@ubc\.ca before 11:59pm on Friday, October 8, 2021\.

Please include the following information:

1.  First Name
2.  Last Name
3.  Program
4.  Year

You may be required to submit your file via OneDrive if it is too large\.

Please retain receipts for your materials\. You can be reimbursed up to $50 per submission *\(some restrictions may apply\)*\.

### Technical Specs

-   Accepted file formats:  \.jpg, \.tif, \.psd, \.pdf, \.ai, \.indd, \.eps
-   In order to be reproduced onto vinyl, submission MUST be at least 100 dpi\.
-   All files must include ½ inch to 1\-inch bleed around border \(we can add this in the production phase if you have trouble creating bleed\)\.
-   For digital files, please set colour to CYMK\.
-   File size must not exceed 20 MB\.
-   Every element of the artistic composition must be original and royalty\-free\.

### Participants

Participation can be individual or by group\.

The contest is only open to current UBC students\.

### Prizes

Up to 5 x 32GB ipads will be awarded\.

### Judging

All entries will be evaluated by a board of adjudicators representing both the School and the UBC Curator of Outdoor Art\. Winners will be announced via email and on the website by Monday, October 25, 2021\.

Criteria: Artwork will be judged on the following:

-   Creativity
-   Is the art visually engaging/appealing?
-   Does the artwork clearly represent UBC School of Social Work’s values: \(a\) Human dignity, \(b\) Social equity, \(c\) Social justice, and \(d\) Scholarship?

-   Does the artwork support the University Campus Plan’s fundamental strategies: \(a\) Create a sustainable campus, \(b\) Provide a campus for world class teaching, learning and research, \(c\) Nurture a more vibrant and interesting campus for UBC’s community of scholars, \(d\) Rediscover UBC’s sense of place on traditional, ancestral, unceded Musqueam territory and \(e\) Ensure a well\-connected and accessible campus
