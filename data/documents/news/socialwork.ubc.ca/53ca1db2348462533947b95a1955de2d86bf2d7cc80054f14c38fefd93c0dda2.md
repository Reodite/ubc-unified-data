UBC’s Liu Institute for Global Issues is hosting a book launch exploring food rights, civil society, and public policy; featuring Food Bank Nations’ Graham Riches, Public Policies for Food Sovereignty’s Annette Desmarais, and Big Hunger’s Andy Fisher\.

It will be in 120\-1855 West Mall, on October 2, 2018, 2:00\-3:00\. [Read more or RSVP](https://liu.arts.ubc.ca/event/big-food-feeding-the-hungry-poor-economic-democracy-food-justice-and-human-rights/)\.
