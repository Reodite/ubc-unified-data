[https://healthresearchbc\.ca/news\_article/new\-cohort\-of\-researchers\-leading\-the\-way\-to\-better\-health\-outcomes/](https://healthresearchbc.ca/news_article/new-cohort-of-researchers-leading-the-way-to-better-health-outcomes/)ImageMichael Smith Health Research BC has announced funding for 23 new Scholars and 14 Health Professional\-Investigators for 2024\. Established in 2001 in honor of BC’s first Nobel Laureate, Dr\. Michael Smith, the organization aims to support early\-career health researchers and advance world\-class health research in the province\.

The Scholar Program is a flagship initiative that has granted over 560 awards since its inception, helping to develop and retain top talent in BC’s health research community\.

We congratulate **[Dr\. Mohamed Ibrahim](https://healthresearchbc.ca/award_researcher/mohamed-ibrahim/)** from the UBC School of Social Work, one of this year’s Michael Smith Scholar awardees\.

To learn more about his project visit [Strengthening mental health in Horn of Africa \(IGAD region\) and Somalia \(MH Care\-Somalia\)](https://healthresearchbc.ca/award/strengthening-mental-health-in-horn-of-africa-igad-region-and-somalia-mh-care-somalia/)\.
