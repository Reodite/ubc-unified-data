The Community Care Research Fund has been made possible by a generous donation made by the B\.C\. Association for Community Care\. This fund is for **graduate students only**\. To apply, fill out this [application form](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2016/02/Community-Care-Fund-Application-Form-10-Feb-2016.doc), and email the form with supplemental documents to Professor Deb O’Connor at [deborah\.oconnor@ubc\.ca](mailto:deborah.oconnor@ubc.ca?Subject=Community%20Care%20Research%20Fund%20application)\.

The fund will support graduate student and faculty research related to aging, family care, community care, continuing care, and service delivery on behalf of people needing additional supports to sustain their life in the community\.

Priority for funding student research will be given to students whose proposals give clear indication that the research is community based and focuses on developing knowledge and application of knowledge about long\-term care issues\.
