Professor Tim Stainton was invited to present to the Quebec National Assembly’s select committee on the evolution of the law regarding the end of life\. He presented on the concerns of the disability community around the expansion of the [MAiD legislation](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/medical-assistance-dying.html) and potential risks to people with Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities\.
