Congratulations to our faculty members Dr\. [Donna Baines](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/donna-baines/) and Dr\. [Grant Charles](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/grant-charles/), whose research projects were awarded funding through the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council \(SSHRC\)’s Insight and Insight Development Grant competitions\.

Dr\. Baines won a 6\-year, $449,858 Insight Grant for her research project “Vulnerable Workers, Vulnerable Services and Non\-Profits: Working in the Shadow of the New Authoritarianism and Intolerance\.”

Dr\. Charles was a co\-applicant for a 2\-year, $92,424 Insight Development Grant for a project entitled, “Parenting Following Childhood Family Adversity: How Yesterday’s Children Become Today’s Parents\.”

Read more about [SSHRC funding for UBC Arts researchers](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/news/ubc-arts-researchers-awarded-sshrc-funding-to-advance-social-cultural-and-economic-research/)\.
