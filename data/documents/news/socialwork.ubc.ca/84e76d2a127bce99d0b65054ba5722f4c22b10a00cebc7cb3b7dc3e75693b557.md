Professor Miu Chung Yan has been appointed as Director of School of Social Work from July 1, 2016 to June 30, 2019\.

Thank you to the members of the School for participating in the search process by attending Dr\. Yan’s presentation and by providing their feedback for the Committee’s consideration\. Special thanks to members of the Search Committee for their guidance and input throughout this important process\.

Learn more about Professor Yan’s experience and research [here](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/persons/miu-chung-yan/)\.
