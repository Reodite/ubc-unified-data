The School of Social Work extends its warmest congratulations to Dr\. [Nancy Lin](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/nancy-lin/), who successfully defended her PhD in Social Work this month\.

Supervised by [Dr\. Christiana Bratiotis](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/christiana-bratiotis/), Dr\. Lin’s research focuses on advancing health inclusion for people with \(dis\)abilities, with a particular emphasis on improving mental health accessibility for people with acquired brain injury\. Her doctoral research explores cross\-disciplinary approaches to adapting psychosocial supports to better accommodate brain injury\-related \(dis\)ability, helping to make mental health care more accessible and inclusive\.

[Embedded content: Nancy Lin—Accommodating Disability after Acquired Brain Injury in Mental Health Treatment](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/news/nancy-lin-doctoral-defense/)

As a healthcare social worker, Dr\. Lin’s research interests also include evidence\-based practice and epistemological pluralism\. She was awarded a SSHRC Canada Graduate Scholarship in support of her doctoral research and is affiliated with the [UBC Centre for Collaborative Research on Hoarding](https://hoarding.psych.ubc.ca/) and the [Coping with Neurological Symptoms Lab](https://neuro.psych.ubc.ca/)\.

Congratulations, Dr\. Lin\!
