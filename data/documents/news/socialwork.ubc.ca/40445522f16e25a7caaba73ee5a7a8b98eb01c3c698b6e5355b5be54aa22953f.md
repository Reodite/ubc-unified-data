Photo of Miu Chung YanProfessor [Miu Chung Yan](https://schoolofsocialwork.sites.olt.ubc.ca/persons/miu-chung-yan/) has recently published a chapter entitled, “Multiple positionality and intersectionality: Towards a dialogical social work approach” in the book, *[Diversity and Social Work in Canada](http://www.oupcanada.com/catalog/9780199007066.html)*, edited by Alean Al\-Krenawi, John R\. Graham and Nazim Habibov\.

In addition, Professor Yan’s co\-authored article with Jenny Francis, “[Bridging the gaps: Access to formal support services among young African immigrants and refugees in Metro Vancouver](https://muse.jhu.edu/journals/canadian_ethnic_studies/v048/48.1.francis.html)” can be read from the recent issue of *Canadian Ethnic Studies*\.
