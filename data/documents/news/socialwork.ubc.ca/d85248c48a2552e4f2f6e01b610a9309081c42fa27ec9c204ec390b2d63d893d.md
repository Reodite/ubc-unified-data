The UBC School of Social Work and the [BCASW](https://www.bcasw.org/) welcomes nominations for the Inspiring Social Worker of the Year Award\. It has been a challenging year for all and in many ways, we all deserve to be applauded and recognized for our hard work and commitment to social justice and equity under conditions not of our choosing\. However, we are pleased to recognize the work of those who made outstanding contributions this year and invite your nominations\.

The deadline for nominations is **June 30, 2021**\.
