David Young, a UBC School of Social Work alumnus, has been recognized with a 2016 BC Community Achievement Award\. The BC Community Achievement Awards celebrate and highlight the work of British Columbians who have made significant contributions to their community\.

Young is the CEO of [Sources](http://www.sourcesbc.ca/), a non\-profit agency providing support programs to families, children, youth, and seniors in the communities of White Rock, Surrey, Langley, Delta and Prince George\.

Learn more about the [BC Community Achievement Awards](https://www.bcachievement.com/)\.
