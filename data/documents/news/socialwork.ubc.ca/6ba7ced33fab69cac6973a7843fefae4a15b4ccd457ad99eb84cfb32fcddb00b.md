Each year, National Social Work Month \(NSWM\) is celebrated in March to recognize the contributions of social workers to society\. In BC, Social Work Week will take place March 13\-19, 2016\.

Find out more about NSWM and read other social workers’ stories on the [Canadian Association of Social Workers website](http://www.casw-acts.ca/en/events-and-conferences/canadian/national-social-workers-month)\.

[Download free posters](http://www.bcasw.org/resources-publications/social-work-posters/) on National Social Work Week, courtesy of the BC Association of Social Workers\.
