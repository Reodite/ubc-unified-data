**Working with Immigrants and Refugees:** Issues, Theories, and Approaches for Social Work and Human Service Practice

Image

Miu Chung Yan \(Professor and Director, UBC School of Social Work\) has a new edited book, co\-edited with Uzo Anucha \(Associate Professor, School of Social Work, York University\)\.

For more information, see the book at the [Oxford University Press website](http://www.oupcanada.com/catalog/9780199019250.html)\.
