Frank Tester \(Professor Emeritus, UBC School of Social Work\) is one of the authors of “**Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit: What Inuit Have Always Known to Be True**”\.

The book launch for Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit will be on Monday, October 30, 2017, in Ottawa, Ontario at 25OneCommunity\.

For more details, please see the [Book Launch event page on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/events/116206652418667/)\.
