Lea Caragata on CBC Vancouver News

Director and Associate Professor, Dr\. Lea Caragata co\-authored a research report with the Centre of Family Equity published in March 2024, detailing the significant impact of the COVID\-19 pandemic on low\-income lone mothers\.

On May 12, 2024, Dr\. Caragata spoke with CBC News on the findings of this report\.
