**Distinguished Indigenous Speaker Series**

Social Work and the Sixties Scoop: Plus ça change, plus c’est la même chose  
with Dr\. Raven Sinclair  
Register [here](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/events/event/dr-raven-sinclair-and-the-sixties-scoop/)\.

**Indigenous Cultural Sharing Recess  
**March 26, 2021, 12\-1pm \(Vancouver time\)  
The Indigenous Health team from the [BC Women’s](http://www.bcwomens.ca/) and [Children’s Hospitals](http://www.bcchildrens.ca/) will talk about the work they do to support Indigenous women, children, and families accessing services at the hospitals\. Learn about Indigenous Patient Liaisons, Indigenous complex care service, and Elder cultural supports\.

The team from [Native Courtworkers and Counselling](https://nccabc.ca/) will discuss services they provide in the areas of addictions and the criminal justice system\. They will also discuss BC’s First Nations Courts\.

Zoom link will be provided closer to the date\.
