Congratulations to Assistant Professor [Hannah Kia](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/hannah-kia/), a co\-Principal Investigator on the study *Interdisciplinary LGBTQ/2S\+ HEALTH Hub: Healing through Education and Awareness in stigma reduction and Lifecourse Training in Health\.*

Hannah is part of a $2\.4 million team grant from [CIHR \(Canadian Institutes of Health Research\)](https://cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/193.html) to develop a national training platform intended to equip researchers, students and community stakeholders to contribute to research on stigma reduction and mental wellness across the life course among LGBTQ/2S\+ populations\.

This grant will span six years and, as co\-PI, Hannah will play a role in key aspects of curriculum development and knowledge exchange processes supported\.
