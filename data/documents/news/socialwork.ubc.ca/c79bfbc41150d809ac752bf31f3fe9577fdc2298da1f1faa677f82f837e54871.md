To mark the 90\[th\] Anniversary of the School of Social Work we held a Symposium on Social Justice and an Ethic of Care – Feb 24 &amp; 25\. The Symposium opened with a keynote address from Prof\. Christine Morley, Queensland University of Technology, Australia \(details below\)\. The Symposium continued the next day with research papers and panels from 9:00 am to 5 pm at Jack Bell School of Social Work, 2080 West Mall, UBC\. Agenda and Abstracts can be found [here](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/events/event/90th-anniversary-symposium/)\. The event was co\-sponsored with the Department of Applied Social Sciences, Hong Kong Polytechnic\.

Keynote and Keynote Registration: Professor Christine Morley, Queensland University of Technology, spoke on “Reactivating Social Work Practice as an Emancipatory Project: The Role of Critical Theory, Pedagogy and Reflection”\. The keynote was from 5:30 to 7 pm on Feb\. 24 \(Room: The Place of Many Trees, Liu Institute for Global Issues, UBC\) and was open to the public\. [Please click here for more info\.](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2019/10/90thAnniversary_KeyNoteEvent_20200224_Flyer-002.pdf)

Registration for the event has been closed\.
