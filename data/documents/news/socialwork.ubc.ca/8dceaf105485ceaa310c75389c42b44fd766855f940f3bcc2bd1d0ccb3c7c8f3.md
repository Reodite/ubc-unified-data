The Centre for the Study of Services to Children and Families \(CSSCF\) was founded in UBC\-Okanagan in 2011 and subsequently extended to UBC\-Vancouver in 2022\. CSSCF is dedicated to identifying and supporting the development of evidence\-informed policies, programs, and services aimed at enhancing the well\-being of children, youth, and families from various social backgrounds\. We accomplish this through collaborative university\-community partnerships, conducting research, training future researchers, and the mobilization of research knowledge\.
