Dean of Arts Dr\. Clare Haru Crowston and Dr\. Donna Baines

In May, the Faculty of Arts Dean’s Office hosted a reception to honour the 2024/25 Dean of Arts Faculty Research Award recipients\. Among those recognized for her outstanding contributions to research was [**Dr\. Donna Baines**](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/donna-baines/), Professor at the School of Social Work\. The annual awards celebrate faculty who demonstrate excellence in teaching, research and community engagement across UBC\.

Dr\. Baines is also the co\-editor of the recently published book [International Handbook of Feminisms in Social Work](https://www.routledge.com/The-Routledge-International-Handbook-of-Feminisms-in-Social-Work/Noble-Rasool-Harms-Smith-Munoz-Arce-Baines/p/book/9781032327600?srsltid=AfmBOoq9vp5gu9bSpuu8RaGLucsbPpnZ7GBXlLtL0C76F0PzHnuywK2k), a global collection of feminist social work perspectives which brings together 52 chapters and feminist\-engaged, equity and social justice aspirations of 108 authors from all corners of the world\.
