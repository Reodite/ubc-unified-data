### **The Knowledge Exchange Podcast**

ImageFounded and hosted by Dr\. Barbara Lee, a [Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization \(KxM\) Scholar](https://kx.ubc.ca/ubc-kxm-scholars) \(2023\-2025\) in the University of British Columbia \(UBC\), School of Social Work, this [podcast](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/news/the-knowledge-exchange-podcast-release-announcement/) highlights inspiring community\-engaged partnerships\.

Each episode will feature a discussion between faculty and their community partners about their projects together and the impact emerging from their knowledge exchange and mobilization practices\.
