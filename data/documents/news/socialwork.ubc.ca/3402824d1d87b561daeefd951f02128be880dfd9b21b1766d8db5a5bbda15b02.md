We are happy to announce that [**Dr\. Barbara Lee**](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/barbara-lee/), Assistant Professor at UBC School of Social Work, was one of the recipients of the [2025 Dean of Arts Mentorship Award\.](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/news/meet-the-recipients-of-the-2025-dean-of-arts-mentorship-award/?utm_medium=email&amp;utm_campaign=2025_0522_Faculty_InsideArts&amp;utm_source=Envoke-2025_0522_Faculty_InsideArts&amp;utm_term=Inside-Arts-%7C-Highlights-from-the-Arts-Strategic-Plan-Launch)

Dr\. Lee is a social work scholar and community leader with a strong commitment to advancing child welfare research and practice\. She was recognized with this award for her personalized and empowering mentorship\. Dr\. Lee is known for her accessibility, collaborative spirit, and support to her mentees throughout their journey, dedicated to ensuring that they succeed\.

The 2025 Dean of Arts Mentorship Award dinner took place yesterday, May 27\. Here are some photos of Dr\. Barbara Lee with her mentees from last night’s event\.
