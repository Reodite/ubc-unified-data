Congratulations to [**Dr\. Barbara Lee**](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/barbara-lee/), who was selected as a recipient of the 2023/24 Dean of Arts Faculty Research award\. This award allows research faculty to devote their time to research for one semester by reducing teaching or administrative duties\.

**Research project**: Exploring the Barriers and Facilitators for Effective Child Welfare Intervention: The Experiences of Asian Children and Families involved in the Child Welfare System in Canada
