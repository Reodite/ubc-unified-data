The 4th edition of *Doing Anti\-Oppressive Social Work: Rethinking Theory and Practice* – edited by [Professor Donna Baines](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/donna-baines/) – was just released by [Fernwood Publishing](https://fernwoodpublishing.ca/)\.

It contains updated and revised chapters, as well as a rethink of anti\-oppressive practice in light of emerging issues including decolonization and the overlapping crises facing humanity\.

This 4th edition features a new editorial team of Donna Baines, Natalie Clark and Bindi Bennett, as well as new authors from the School of Social Work, including [Lea Caragata](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/lea-caragata/) and Jaclyn Sauer\.
