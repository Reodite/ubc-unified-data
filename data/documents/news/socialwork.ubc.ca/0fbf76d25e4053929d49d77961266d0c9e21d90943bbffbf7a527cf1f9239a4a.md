For Orange Shirt Day \| National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, September 30, the School of Social Work encourages everyone to participate in the Intergenerational March and the Sacred Fire Ceremony taking place at UBC\. These are some of the many ways you can honour survivors and those who did not make it home and join together in learning the truth, taking action and working towards reconciliation on this important day\.
