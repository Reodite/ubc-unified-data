**Announcing the Inspiring Social Worker of the Year \(2025\)**

We are proud to announce that Brandon Wong has been selected as this year’s *Inspiring Social Worker of the Year*\.

Brandon, specializing in mental health and substance use, has demonstrated remarkable leadership and compassion in Vancouver’s Downtown Eastside\. His trauma\-informed and client\-centered practice reflects resilience, justice, and hope—values that inspire both his colleagues and the communities he serves\. Brandon’s dedication has improved access to care, strengthened interdisciplinary collaboration, and exemplified the very best of the social work profession\.
