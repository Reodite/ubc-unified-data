Congratulations to [Assistant Professor Barbara Lee](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/barbara-lee/), who was recently announced as a member of the first cohort of [Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization \(KxM\) Scholars](https://kx.ubc.ca/news/november-11-2022/ubc-knowledge-exchange-and-mobilization-scholar-program "The UBC Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization Scholar Program")\.

As part of this cohort, Barbara will lead community of practices, implement and evaluate KxM training activities, and provide peer\-mentorship at the School of Social Work\.
