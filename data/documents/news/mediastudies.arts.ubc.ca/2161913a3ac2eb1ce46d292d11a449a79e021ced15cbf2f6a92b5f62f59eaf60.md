UBC Bachelor of Media Studies admissions are open for the 2022/2023 Academic Year\!

The Bachelor of Media Studies program attracts top students from around the world who are ambitious and curious about media studies, and looking to thrive in a cohort environment designed to further enhance their studies at UBC\.

ImageImage

**Program Overview**

The UBC Bachelor of Media Studies \(BMS\) program on the Vancouver campus is an interdisciplinary, direct\-entry program that combines artistic, technical and analytical skills to help students understand the impact of media in our society\.

Courses are from eight diverse disciplines: Art History &amp; Visual Arts, Film Studies &amp; Production, English Language &amp; Literatures, German Media Theory, Computer Science, Creative Writing, Journalism and Information Studies\.

**ImageApplication Deadlines**

Apply by December 1, 2021, to be considered for entrance scholarships and awards\.

The final deadline to apply for the Bachelor of Media Studies program is January 15, 2022\.
