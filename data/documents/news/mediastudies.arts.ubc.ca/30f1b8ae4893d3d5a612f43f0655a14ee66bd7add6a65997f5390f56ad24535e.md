BMS faculty member Jaleh Mansoor has just published *[Universal Prostitution and Modernist Abstraction: A Counterhistory \(Duke University Press, 2025\)](https://www.dukeupress.edu/universal-prostitution-and-modernist-abstraction)*, a work that offers a fresh take on modernist art history by bringing it into conversation with Marxist theory\. Drawing on Marx’s notion of prostitution as a metaphor for modern labour, Mansoor examines how avant\-garde artists understood and resisted the economic forces shaping their world and time\. Already receiving strong praise from leading scholars worldwide, Dr\. Mansoor’s work is a great example of the rigorous, boundary\-crossing research happening in the BMS faculty\. For students curious about the intersections of art, identity and critical theory, this is well worth exploring\.
