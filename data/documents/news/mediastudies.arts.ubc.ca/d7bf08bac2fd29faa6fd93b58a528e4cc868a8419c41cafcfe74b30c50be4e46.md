Do you have a passion for photography and want to build your portfolio while contributing to the UBC and Bachelor of Media Studies community?

The BMS program is currently looking for a student photographer for a 1\-2 hour scheduled photoshoot around the UBC campus, including post production, capturing student models in typical BMS activities\! For a reference to the types of images, please visit the UBC Flickr account here: [https://www\.flickr\.com/photos/134760388@N08/](https://www.flickr.com/photos/134760388@N08/), the BMS Program website here: [https://mediastudies\.arts\.ubc\.ca/](https://mediastudies.arts.ubc.ca/), and the UBC Brand and photography guidelines: [https://brand\.ubc\.ca/guidelines/photography/?login/](https://brand.ubc.ca/guidelines/photography/?login/)\.

This paid position is open to BMS students in all years of the program\. If you are interested in applying, please email [arts\.mediastudies@ubc\.ca](mailto:arts.mediastudies@ubc.ca) no later than **Friday, October 25\[th\] at 12pm** with your:

-   Name
-   Year
-   Photography Portfolio

We thank all applicants in advance for their interest, and please let us know if you have any questions\!
