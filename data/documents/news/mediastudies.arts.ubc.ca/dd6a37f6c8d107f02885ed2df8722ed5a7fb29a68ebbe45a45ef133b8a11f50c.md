Congratulations to our UBC Bachelor of Media Studies and [UBC School of Information professor](https://ischool.ubc.ca/), [Dr\. Richard Arias Hernandez](https://mediastudies.arts.ubc.ca/profile/richard-arias-hernandez/), who has been awarded the Dean’s Award for Educational Leadership projects this year\!⁣ ⁣

Dr\. Richard Arias\-Hernandez’s project focuses on developing an educational leadership methodology and instruments to work with faculty to conduct transformational audits of Indigenization &amp; Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion of their course offerings\. ⁣ ⁣

Read more about it in the Faculty of Arts [website](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/news/deans-award-for-educational-leadership-supports-eight-new-pilot-projects-in-arts/)\.
