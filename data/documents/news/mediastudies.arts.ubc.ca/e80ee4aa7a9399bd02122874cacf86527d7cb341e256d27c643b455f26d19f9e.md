We are so excited to announce the creation of **Beacon: UBC Journal of Media Studies\!**

Beacon will be the first student\-led undergraduate, peer &amp; faculty reviewed online multimedia journal in Canada about Media Studies\. It aims to showcase the most ambitious &amp; outstanding works of current UBC Media Studies students and push boundaries in new media and communications studies\!

An inclusive platform, Beacon will celebrate the creativity of students with exuberant &amp; quieter voices who see Media as a beacon to illuminate the unique stories within them &amp; illuminate the stories that need to be told – shedding light on new perspectives\.

Beacon provides visibility to undergraduate creative and research work in the field of media studies, and establishes dialogues between media studies students at various levels\.

Beacon sees the light in you, and can’t wait to showcase your work to a greater audience\!

To contact Beacon, please email the journal at ubc\.beaconjournal@gmail\.com or follow them on Instagram at @ubcjms\!
