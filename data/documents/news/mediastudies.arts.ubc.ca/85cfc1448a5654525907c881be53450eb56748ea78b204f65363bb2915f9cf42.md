Recently, faculty, staff, students, and families came together at the Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre to celebrate the BMS Class of 2026 at our annual Graduation Reception\.  
The evening was everything a celebration should be: warm, joyful, and full of good conversation\. Program Chair Christine D’Onofrio and faculty members Richard Arias\-Hernandez, Richard Cavell, Patrick Parra, and Jodie Leung joined graduates and their families for a night of connection and well\-deserved recognition\.  
A heartfelt congratulations to every member of the Class of 2026\. The future is yours\! Stay curious, stay bold, and keep making your mark on the world of media\. And please remember to come say hi from time to time\!
