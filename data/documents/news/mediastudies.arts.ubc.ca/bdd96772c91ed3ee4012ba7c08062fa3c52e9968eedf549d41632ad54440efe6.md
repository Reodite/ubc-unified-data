Recently, faculty, staff, and families came together at our Graduation Reception to celebrate the successful graduation of the BMS Class of 2023\. 

From reminiscing memories to envisioning the future, the afternoon was filled with joy, love, and a deep sense of pride\. At the event, Program Chair Christine D’Onofrio and BMS Professor Richard Arias\-Hernandez shared some words with the graduates at the podium\. BMS alum and former program assistant Catherine Zhu, though unable to make it to the event, had some words shared on her behalf\.

A big final congratulations to all the graduates\! The future is yours, and we can’t wait to witness the amazing things you’ll accomplish\. Whatever path you choose, remember to stay curious, stay bold, and continue to enjoy and impact the dynamic world of media\.
