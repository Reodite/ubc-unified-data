## SceneEats Trailer \(2019\)

[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://mediastudies.arts.ubc.ca/news/student-spotight-danny-arafa/)

Danny approached the [M04 Network](https://themo4network.net/), a media agency based in Cairo, with an idea to create [SceneEats](https://sceneeats.com/), a digital magazine cataloguing all the amazing food options in Egypt, to address the media agencies overwhelming number of food\-based clients\. Together with M04’s team of videographers and editors, Danny helped launch SceneEats in the spring of 2019, to create”a love letter to food like you’ve never seen before”\. To see all the amazing recipes, food blogs, and restaurant reviews on Scene Eats, follow them on instagram @sceneeats, and visit their [website\!](https://sceneeats.com/)

## *The Dace That Flew To Space \(2017\)*

Image

What originally started as a passion for watercolours and creative rhyming quickly grew into a children’s book that Danny illustrated and wrote himself\. After completing *The Dace that Flew To Space* in 2017, Danny travelled to different elementary schools in his community to share the message of reading and learning to make our world a better place\. To learn more about Danny’s first illustrated book, visit his website [https://lostadventuring\.wordpress\.com/](https://lostadventuring.wordpress.com/)\.
