A total of 58 new projects led by UBC researchers have been awarded funding of $13 million through the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council’s Partnership Grants, Partnership Development Grants and Insight Grants programs\.

Among these awarded UBC\-led projects is the Archives 4\.0: Artificial Intelligence for Trust in Records and Archives project, which our UBC Bachelor of Media Studies and UBC School of Information professor Dr\. Richard Arias\-Hernandez was co\-applicant for\.

The project received the SSHRC Partnership Grant, which provides funding for four to seven years of advance research, research training and/or knowledge mobilization in the social sciences and humanities through mutual co\-operation and sharing of intellectual leadership\.

Congratulations, Dr\. Richard Arias\-Hernandez\!
