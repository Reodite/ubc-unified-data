BMS students are encouraged to take electives which allow them to specialize in an area that will compliment their focus on media studies\. In addition to the 12 [Core Plus Credits](https://mediastudies.arts.ubc.ca/core-courses/core-plus-credits/), BMS students can tailor their electives to specific areas of study within Arts\.

See the list below for a more thorough breakdown of preferred topics for specialization that can coincide with a theoretical media studies degree\.

---

**Political Activism \(Politics and Social Justice\)**

-   Foundational work in Political Science \([POLI 100](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=3&amp;dept=POLI&amp;course=100), [POLI 110](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=3&amp;dept=POLI&amp;course=110)\)
-   Foundational work in History which might include [HIST 236](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=3&amp;dept=HIST&amp;course=236): Public History in Canada: Memory, Representation, and Interpretation
-   foundational work within Social Justice \([GRSJ 101/102](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=1&amp;dept=GRSJ)\) leading to upper level courses

---

**Public Information on Climate Change, and Sustainability**

-   [GEOG 121](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=3&amp;dept=GEOG&amp;course=121): Geography, Modernity and Globalization along with [HIST 106](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=3&amp;dept=HIST&amp;course=106): Global Environmental History
-   Foundational work in climate change/sustainability \([GEOB 102](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main;jsessionid=7F159F22DDBFADFC8F12EC20A463D619?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=3&amp;dept=GEOB&amp;course=102) or [GEOB 103](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main;jsessionid=7F159F22DDBFADFC8F12EC20A463D619?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=3&amp;dept=GEOB&amp;course=103)\)

---

**Science, Technology, and Communications**

-   [HIST 260](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=1&amp;dept=HIST): Science and Society in the Contemporary World
-   [PHIL 150](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=1&amp;dept=PHIL): Minds and Machines
-   Foundational Psychology courses \([PSYC 101/102](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=1&amp;dept=PSYC)\) leading to upper level courses

---

**Digital and Cultural Sectors in Music**

-   [MUSC 119](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=3&amp;dept=MUSC&amp;course=119): Introduction to Music Technology

---

**Preserving and protecting the world’s languages**

-   [FNEL 180](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=3&amp;dept=FNEL&amp;course=180): Introduction to Endangered Language Documentation and Revitalization
-   [LING 101](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?sessyr=2015&amp;sesscd=W): Languages of the World

---

**Moral, economic and legal issues**

-   [LASO 204](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=3&amp;dept=LASO&amp;course=204): Introduction to Law and Society
-   [PHIL 230A](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=1&amp;dept=PHIL): Introduction to Moral Theory
-   [ECON 101/102](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=1&amp;dept=ECON) – May lead to upper level ECON courses

---

**Visual Arts’ Interaction with the World**

-   [ARTH 101](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=0): Ways of Seeing: Introduction to Visual Studies
-   [ARTH 102](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=0): Crisis and Contradiction in Art and the Built Environment

---

**The Role of Media in the Historical and Modern Aboriginal Experience**

-   [FNIS 100](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=1&amp;dept=FNIS)
-   [FNIS 120](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subjareas&amp;req=1&amp;dept=FNIS)

---

You’ll notice that we have not mentioned foundational course work within Film Studies, Film Production, and Visual Art\. Through the CAP Year one Media Studies stream, you will already be building a strong foundation in these areas\.

If your particular area of interest was not included in the examples listed above, please email the [arts\.mediastudies@ubc\.ca](mailto:arts.mediastudies@ubc.ca)\.  We will work with you to develop an individualized program plan focused on developing the necessary expertise to match your future career goals\.
