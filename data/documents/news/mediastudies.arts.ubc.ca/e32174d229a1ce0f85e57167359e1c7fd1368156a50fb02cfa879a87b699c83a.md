Beacon: UBC Journal of Media Studies is seeking submissions for academic papers, prose, artwork and new media to be published in their annual online journal\. Students do not have to be enrolled in the Bachelor of Media Studies to submit, as long as the work submitted is related to the field of media studies, including:

-   Media Theories
-   Media analysis \(film analysis, new media analysis, art critiques, information policy/visualization research and journalism research\)
-   Creative works \(art, prose, photography, video/audio works and new media/blended\-media\)

Further details and submission instructions can be found at https://beaconjournal\.arts\.ubc\.ca/submit/\. Contact ubc\.beaconjournal@gmail\.com for any questions\.

Deadline: January 7, 2022, at 11:59 pm \(PST\)\.
