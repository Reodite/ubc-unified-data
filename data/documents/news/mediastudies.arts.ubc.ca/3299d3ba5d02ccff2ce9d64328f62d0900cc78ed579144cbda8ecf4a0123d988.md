We’re looking for BMS students \(must be 19\+\) to participate in an on\-campus photoshoot\! Have fun, meet other students, and showcase life in the BMS program\.

This volunteer opportunity is open to BMS students in all years of the program who are available for 1\-1\.5 hours for indoor and outdoor shots, and are willing to be featured in BMS marketing and recruitment material\. There will be light refreshments available at the end of the shoot\.

If you are interested in participating, please complete the contact form by **Tuesday, October 29\[th\]** at 12pm, which you can find on the BMS website here: [https://mediastudies\.arts\.ubc\.ca/call\-for\-bms\-photoshoot\-participants/](https://mediastudies.arts.ubc.ca/call-for-bms-photoshoot-participants/)\.
