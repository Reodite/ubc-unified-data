As part of CiTR’s radio documentary series “Keep it Local”, Rachel Lau has produced and released [“The Young and The Resistant”](http://www.citr.ca/radio/citr-documentaries/episode/20180504/),  a radio documentary on a personal and very relevant topic in the Vancouver community; the youth resurgence movement in downtown Chinatown\. Rachel’s cultural background as a Canadian Born Chinese \(CBC\), lead them to Chinatown’s youth resurgence for the amazing impact and relationship that youth are creating in the area, including a number of youth activists currently operating on Chinatown’s biggest issues\. Rachel touches on the historical background of Chinese advocacy in Vancouver, as well as the power of community radio\.
