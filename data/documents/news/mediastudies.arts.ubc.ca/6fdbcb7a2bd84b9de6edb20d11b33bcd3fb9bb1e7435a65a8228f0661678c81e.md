From ideas of representation in the media, the importance of digital media literacy education, having a critical/analytical eye when viewing media, to all the ways that media is intertwined in our lives, our five BMS Speech Contestant Finalists this year each uniquely responded with their ‘M\-DEAS’ \(ideas about media\) at the first M\-DEA Talk: UBC Bachelor of Media Studies Public Speaking Competition on April 6th to the topic – ***What should Media mean to us?***

Their speeches promoted thought\-provoking perspectives and discussions through incorporating a combination of scholarly research works, media theory, and lived experiences\.

After two years of virtual studies, it was also wonderful to bring together students together in\-person and celebrate our learning for this academic year\!

We want to congratulate all the contestants for their amazing speeches, and thank Dr\. Biz Nijdam, Dr\. Richard Arias\-Hernandez, and Tim Flanagan \(Judging Panel\), BMS Peer &amp; Academic Mentors, and everyone who contributed to the success of this inaugural event\!  
\_

### 2021W M\-DEA Talk \| Results

-   **First Place:** Gabrielle Bonifacio
-   **Second Place:** Cameron Redding
-   **Third Place:** Miwa Kuwada Ramos
