4th Year BMS student Angelica Poversky, AKA ANGELIQUOI? will be releasing their “real” debut poetry book “TINDER LOVING EMPIRE” with a one hour spectacle at [CBC Vancouver](https://www.facebook.com/CBCVancouver/) Studio 700 as part of [BC Culture Days \| Fête de la culture](https://www.facebook.com/BCCultureDays/) and [Culture Days\-Fête de la culture](https://apps.facebook.com/culturedays/)\.

Image

ANGELIQUOI? will be performing Tinder Loving Empire which is a one hour show based on the book, Tinder Loving Empire, to celebrate its release\. The show, like the book, focuses on themes of relationships, self\-forgiveness, sexual consent, sexuality, rape culture, gender, and the journey of self\-love\.

To attend, RSVP [here\.](https://www.facebook.com/events/2560999167512576/)
