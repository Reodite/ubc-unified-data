Hi BMS Students,

We are so excited to welcome you all to the new academic year\!

To our returning students, we look forward to welcoming you back, and to our First\-Year students, we look forward to having you in the program – you are all part of a unique, interdisciplinary program with peers that have diverse talents and interests in Media\!

**To welcome you all, we have organized two virtual orientation sessions “Welcome Back, BMS\!” via Zoom where you will have the opportunity to:** 

-   Meet the BMS Program team\.
-   Meet BMS student\-led initiatives to learn how to be involved in the UBC BMS community\.
-   We will have a “Q&amp;A” segment where you can ask questions about the new school year\.
    -   You can ask questions during the segment and/or submit your questions anonymously beforehand by Friday, September\. 3 via the form linked [here](https://bms.air.arts.ubc.ca/bms-orientation-question-submission/)\.

**RSVP**  
Please RSVP for your orientation below by Monday, Sept\. 6:

**Welcome Back, BMS\! Orientation for BMS Year 1 and Year 2 \| Wednesday, Sept\. 8 \(3 – 4pm PST\)  
**For BMS students in Year 1 and Year 2, please RSVP for your orientation [here](https://bms.air.arts.ubc.ca/rsvp-welcome-back-bms-year-1-year-2/) by Monday, September\. 6 to retrieve your Zoom link to attend\.

**Welcome Back, BMS\! Orientation for BMS Year 3, Year 4, Year 4\+ \| Monday, Sept\. 13 \(2pm\-3pm PST\)  
**For BMS students in Year 3, Year 4, and Year 4\+, please RSVP for your orientation [here](https://bms.air.arts.ubc.ca/welcome-back-bms-year-3-year-4-year-4/) by Monday, September\. 6 to retrieve your Zoom link to attend\.

We look forward to seeing you all there\! Don’t miss out on this valuable opportunity to kick off your bright year ahead with your fellow BMS peers by chatting with and learning more about the program, and how to maximize all the opportunities that you have as a BMS student\. 😊
