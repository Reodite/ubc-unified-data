We are so excited to announce the creation of Beacon: UBC Journal of Media Studies\!

Beacon will be the first student\-led undergraduate, peer &amp; faculty reviewed online multimedia journal in Canada about Media Studies\. It aims to showcase the most ambitious &amp; outstanding works of current UBC Media Studies students and push boundaries in new media and communications studies\! An inclusive platform, Beacon will celebrate the creativity of students with exuberant &amp; quieter voices who see Media as a beacon to illuminate the unique stories within them &amp; illuminate the stories that need to be told – shedding light on new perspectives\.

Beacon provides visibility to undergraduate creative and research work in the field of media studies, and establishes dialogues between media studies students at various levels\.

**The journal is currently hiring student editors, web design coordinator, and communications coordinator to join the inaugural student\-led Beacon journal team\! Please check out the [Hiring Package](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Et39fZj7QwPEsuPKUzTs4EIImSTyUo43wKyrAvR8m_Y/edit?fbclid=IwAR0mf-G4AKonGGjrhZVvOfPzHvZOSvNmxmhhwnkVUSlmmHUcxv9iWRfs-H4#slide=id.g35f391192_00) for the deadline and details on how to apply\!** 

The journal will also be making a Call for YOUR Submissions very soon so please stay tuned for more announcements\!

If you have any questions in the meantime, please email the journal at [ubc\.beaconjournal@gmail\.com](mailto:ubc.beaconjournal@gmail.com) or follow them on Instagram at [@ubcjms](https://www.instagram.com/ubcjms/)\!
