## GERM 412: German Media Studies \(in English\) Final Project

Image

Image

Image

For Tamara’s final project in the BMS course German Media Studies, she designed a series of graphics and illustrations to demonstrate concepts and theories learned in class\. Tamara specifically reflected on a few theorists who expressed beliefs on the notion of totalitarianism that arises from the consumption of media\.
