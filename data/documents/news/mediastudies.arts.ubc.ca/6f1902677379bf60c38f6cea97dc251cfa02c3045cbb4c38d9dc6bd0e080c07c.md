For BMS 4th\-Year students/BMS students who will be in Year 4 in 21S interested in taking online summer courses in 2021S that fulfill your Core Plus requirement, the Centre for Digital Media is offering three online Digital Media courses exclusive to BMS students\!

-   DMED 540 001: User Experience
-   DMED 540 002: Finding Product Market Fit
-   DMED 540 004: Building an E\-Commerce Ecosystem

For more information, visit the [course schedule](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/courseschedule?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subj-course&amp;dept=DMED&amp;course=540) on SSC and email [arts\.mediastudies@ubc\.ca](mailto:arts.mediastudies@ubc.ca) for more details on how to register\.
