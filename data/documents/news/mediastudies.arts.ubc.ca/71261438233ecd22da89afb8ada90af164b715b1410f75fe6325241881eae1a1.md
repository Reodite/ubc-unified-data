We’re very excited to announce a great remote job opportunity for 3\[rd\], 4\[th\] or 4\[th\] plus\-year BMS students who reside in Canada\. Please see below for the detailed Job Description and Application instructions\.

We are hiring a multimodal content developer of educational/training materials \(i\.e\. interactive website or app, photographs, and video\) for two training modules in social media content development\. The audience of this training material is Community Partners that work with UBC in Community Engaged Projects and who are interested in developing social media content generation skills involving professional\-quality photography and video\.

This remote student work term will be from October 15, 2020 – March 30, 2021\. The transferable skills you will gain through this role include: website development, education videos development, project management, professional communication, and training skills on professional photography and video\-making for social media\.

**Deadline to apply: October 12\[th\], 2020\. If you are interested in this job opportunity, please state your interest on the position, send a link to your media portfolio showcasing your photography and video\-making skills, and in 1\-2 paragraphs write a pitch on why you think you can develop this training content for community partners, and email your responses to the supervisor – Professor Richard Arias\-Hernandez at  richard\.arias@ubc\.ca as soon as possible by October 12\[th\], 2020\.**

 Don’t miss out on this great opportunity, we look forward to reading your responses\!
