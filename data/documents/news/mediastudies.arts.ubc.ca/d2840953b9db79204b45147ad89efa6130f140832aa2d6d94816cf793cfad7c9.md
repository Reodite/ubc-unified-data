The Department of Art History, Visual Art &amp; Theory is calling for both paper and art submissions for their 43rd annual Graduate symposium\. This year’s theme is **Decadence: Flows of Abundance and Decay\.** If you have a piece related to this year’s theme, please take a look at the submissions guidelines for paper and artist submissions below\.

[AHVA SYMPOSIUM CALL FOR ARTISTS](https://mediastudies.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/12/AHVA-SYMPOSIUM-CALL-FOR-ARTISTS-REVISED.pdf)

[AHVA SYMPOSIUM CALL FOR PAPERS](https://mediastudies.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/12/AHVA-SYMPOSIUM-CALL-FOR-PAPERS-REVISED.pdf) 

The AHVA Graduate Symposium organizing committee encourages students from various humanities fields whose research engages this year’s theme to apply\.The deadline to submit your work is December 27th, 2019\. All selected works will be included in a concurrent exhibition running from March 5th\-6th, 2020 on the UBC campus\.
