The University for Development Studies \(UDS\), in partnership with the University of British Columbia and supported by the Peter Gilgan Foundation, has launched a two‑day workshop focused on strengthening the shea value chain and promoting sustainable production in Northern Ghana\. The event, organised by the Faculty of Natural Resources and Environment, brought together researchers, industry actors, development practitioners, and community stakeholders\.

Speakers highlighted the central role of women in the shea sector, the need to integrate scientific and indigenous knowledge, and the importance of collaboration across institutions\. Prof\. Terry Sunderland of UBC emphasised the value of international research partnerships in advancing sustainable shea production and improving livelihoods\. Sessions covered market dynamics, governance, ecological management, gender issues, and shea propagation techniques\. The workshop continues with a field visit to explore nursery practices and shea parkland management\.
