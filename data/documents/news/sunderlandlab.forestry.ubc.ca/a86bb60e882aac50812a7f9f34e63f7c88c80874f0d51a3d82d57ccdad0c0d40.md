Alida just won the [SSHRC Canada Graduate scholarship\-Doctoral Program](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/students-etudiants/pg-cs/cgsd-bescd_eng.asp) for 2021\! This scholarship will support her doctoral research assessing the implementation of integrated landscape approaches in Ghana and Zambia as a part of the [COLANDS initiative](https://www2.cifor.org/colands/)\.
