What does decolonization look like in the context of academia? How do we tackle inequality in international forestry and associated research? How does gender intersect with all of this? Our evening with Carol Colfer \(interviewed by Terry Sunderland\) covered a lot of ground, informed by her many decades in the field, from Indonesia to Oman, and even living with loggers in the Pacific Northwest\!
