A new article titled ‘**Out of Africa**‘ by Terry Sunderland has been published on ‘[Bamboo and Rattan Update](https://www.inbar.int/bru-magazine/)‘\. Bamboo and Rattan Update is a magazine published by the International Bamboo and Rattan Organisation \(INBAR\) that is dedicated to sharing the latest high\-quality research and news about the bamboo and rattan sector\. In his article, Terry discusses how African rattan can become an important source for international markets\.
