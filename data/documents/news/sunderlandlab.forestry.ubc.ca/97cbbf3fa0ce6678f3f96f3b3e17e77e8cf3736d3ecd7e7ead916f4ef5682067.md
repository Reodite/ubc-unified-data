Terry Sunderland and the team were featured on UBC Forestry News recently for their contribution on the international collaborative book [*Operationalizing landscape approaches in the tropics*](https://www.cifor.org/knowledge/publication/7800), recently launched at the international Global Landscapes Forum \(GLF\) Biodiversity Digital Conference\.

Upwards of 600 researchers from around the world attended the GLF Oct\. 29, 2020 event that addressed everything from monitoring and evaluation strategies for both social and biodiversity impacts to effectively engaging multiple collaborators\.
