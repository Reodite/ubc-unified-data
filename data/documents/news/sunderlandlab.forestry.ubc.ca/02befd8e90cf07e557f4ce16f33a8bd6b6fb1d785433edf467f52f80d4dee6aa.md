Professor **Terry Sunderland** and Lecturer **Peter Wood**, director and coordinator of UBC’s **Master of International Forestry \(MIF\)** program respectively, are making a significant global impact as lead authors of the United Nations’ **Global Forest Goals Report 2026**\. Their leadership in shaping this landmark assessment—evaluating worldwide progress toward the UN Strategic Plan for Forests—demonstrates the program’s deep engagement with real\-world forest governance and international policy processes\.

Their contributions highlight the MIF program’s unique strength: students learn directly from practitioners who are actively influencing global forest policy\. This connection between classroom learning and international decision‑making underscores why the MIF continues to prepare graduates for impactful careers at the forefront of sustainable forest management and global environmental stewardship\.
