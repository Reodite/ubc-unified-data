Terry recently published an article titled “**‘Fortress’ conservation policies threaten the food security of rural populations**” in The Conversation\.

The article finds that “Conservation, especially when modelled on notions of “pristine nature” — environments untouched by human influence — can create obstacles by limiting access to important food sources\. We must shift from strict fortress conservation to more integrated, [sustainable use of rural landscapes](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.landusepol.2020.104822) if we are to achieve both biodiversity conservation and dietary outcomes\.”

Read the article [here](https://theconversation.com/fortress-conservation-policies-threaten-the-food-security-of-rural-populations-219363)\.
