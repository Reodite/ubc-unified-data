Diling Liang, a masters student at Sunderland lab, gave an update on the [Tree inventory project at UBC Farm](https://ubcfarm.ubc.ca/tree-inventory-of-ubc-farm/) 2021 Research Planning Session on Friday November 20th\. Under this project, Sunderland lab is measuring and tagging all trees at the UBC farm forest\. Diling’s talk highlighted the progress so far, preliminary results from the 2000 trees already recorded and future plans\. This project will lead to the development of UBC Farm’s first 100% tree assessment, acting as a baseline for future projects and research, as well as acting as a teaching opportunity for research project members\.
