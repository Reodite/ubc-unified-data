Samuel Adeyanju recently spoke at the [20th Commonwealth Forestry Conference](https://cfc2021.ubc.ca/program/) on ‘Learning from Community\-Based Natural Resource Management \(CBNRM\) in Ghana and Zambia: Lessons for integrated landscape approaches’\. He presented the successes and challenges of the CBNRM initiatives in the two countries and the potential of  the integrated landscape approaches in addressing the challenges to ensure sustainable landscape management\.
