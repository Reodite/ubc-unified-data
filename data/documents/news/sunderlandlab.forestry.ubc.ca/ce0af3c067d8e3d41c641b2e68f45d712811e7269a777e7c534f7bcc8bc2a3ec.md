[Carbon for Good project](https://www.cifor-icraf.org/project/Carbon4Good/)‘s policy dialogue in Hanoi\! 🌍

🇻🇳📌 Hosted by VNU–UEB in collaboration with [The University of British Columbia](https://www.linkedin.com/company/universityofbc/), [CIFOR\-ICRAF](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cifor-icraf/), [Flinders University](https://www.linkedin.com/company/flinders-university/), [CARE Canada](https://www.linkedin.com/company/care-canada/), and other global partners, the event held on Nov 25–26 brought together policymakers, researchers, and industry to shape next‑generation carbon markets in post‑COP30 Vietnam\.

Vietnam is taking a firm stand to protect the integrity of global carbon markets\. In a recent Hanoi Times piece, UBC Forestry’s Dr\. [Terry Sunderland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/terry-sunderland-3b5a93149/) highlights why high\-quality, verifiable credits are essential for meaningful climate action — and why “cheap” credits come at a cost\.

Strong MRV systems and transparent governance will be key to ensuring forest communities benefit\.
