We are excited to announce ‘Sunderland Lab Guest Lecture Series 2021’ that is primarily to meet diverse researchers and practitioners across the world, and to learn about their ongoing research and issues related to food, livelihood and biodiversity conservation\. This is currently open to Sunderland lab members but the presentation and recording of the talks will be made available [here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Y1fqEMgklu33RQYGcuq29AnF4qONDxfp)\.

The first talk of this series was delivered by [Ronju Ahammad](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ronju-ahammad/?originalSubdomain=au) on ‘Forest and livelihood relationships of rural communities in Bangladesh: Ecosystem services perspective’ on 25th Jan, 2021\. Please see [here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Ci5qRjlNrLpH2OicHgFSMgevLviujSPi) for more details\.
