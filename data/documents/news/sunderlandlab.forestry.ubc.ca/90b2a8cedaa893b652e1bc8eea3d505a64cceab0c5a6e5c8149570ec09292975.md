Sunderland lab had the pleasure to host [Laura Vang Rasmussen](http://thelandlab.net/), Associate Professor, University of Copenhagen and [Bowy den Braber](http://thelandlab.net/bowy-den-braber/) a postdoctoral fellow working with Laura on 25th February\. Bowy presented his work on effects of trees on dietary diversity in the Sahel\. We look forward to hearing more updates from the exciting research\.
