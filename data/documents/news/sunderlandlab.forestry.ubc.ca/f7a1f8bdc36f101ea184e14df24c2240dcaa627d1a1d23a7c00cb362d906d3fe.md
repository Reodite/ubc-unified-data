Debbie Pierce, our PhD candidate at the Sunderland Lab, has released a new paper titled *“Using landscape approaches to understand peacebuilding policy coherence: a case study of Caquetá, Colombia,”* published in *Ecology &amp; Society*\. The study examines how landscape approaches can help identify gaps and opportunities in Colombia’s peacebuilding policies, particularly within the Integrated Rural Reform and its territorial development programs\.

Working alongside co‑authors from the Alliance of Bioversity International and colleagues from the Sunderland Lab, Debbie explores how environmental governance, rural development, and peacebuilding intersect in post‑conflict regions\.

The paper highlights:

-   How landscape approaches can reveal inconsistencies between policy objectives, instruments, and local implementation\.
-   Why participation, rights, and cross‑sectoral coordination are essential for effective peacebuilding\.
-   What Colombia’s Caquetá region can teach us about governing complex, fragile landscapes where environment, food systems, and conflict dynamics are deeply intertwined\.

The authors show that landscape approaches offer a powerful lens for understanding policy coherence — and for strengthening the alignment needed to scale sustainable and peaceful development\.
