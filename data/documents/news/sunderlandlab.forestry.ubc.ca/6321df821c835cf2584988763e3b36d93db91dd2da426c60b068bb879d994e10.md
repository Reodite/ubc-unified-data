We’re thrilled to celebrate Dr\. [Alida O’Connor](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alida-a-oconnor/), who successfully defended her PhD thesis on October 16th, 2025\. Her dissertation, titled “Understanding Collaborative Landscape Governance to Inform Integrated Landscape Approaches: Evidence from Ghana and Zambia,” offers valuable insights into inclusive governance and integrated landscape management in complex socio\-ecological systems\.

Alida is the latest PhD graduate from a cohort of six doctoral researchers under the [COLANDS](https://www.linkedin.com/company/colands/) initiative—a collaborative effort involving [The University of British Columbia](https://www.linkedin.com/company/universityofbc/), [CIFOR\-ICRAF](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cifor-icraf/), [University of Amsterdam](https://www.linkedin.com/company/university-of-amsterdam/), and several local partners\.

This achievement is especially meaningful, as Alida is the first PhD graduate from the [Sunderland Lab at UBC Faculty of Forestry](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sunderlandlab/) Lab\. We couldn’t be prouder of her dedication, impact, and the trail she’s blazing for future scholars\.

Please join us in congratulating Dr\. O’Connor on this incredible accomplishment\! 👏
