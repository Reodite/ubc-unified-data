We are proud to announce that the 2000 BC Lions team has been inducted into the BC Sports Hall of Fame, recognizing their outstanding achievements and contribution to the history of Canadian football\. This prestigious honor celebrates the team’s remarkable performance, including their triumphant 2000 Grey Cup victory, and their enduring legacy in the sport\. We extend our heartfelt congratulations to Dr\. Navin Prasad, a valued member of the team’s medical staff, whose expertise and dedication played a vital role in their success\.
