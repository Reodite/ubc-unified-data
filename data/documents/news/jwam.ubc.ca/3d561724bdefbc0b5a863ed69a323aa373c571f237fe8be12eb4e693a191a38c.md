**Adjunct Professor**

Dan McKinney

Course: [Visual Journalism](https://journalism.ubc.ca/academic_program/elective-course-descriptions/)

Email: dpmckinney@gmail\.com

Dan McKinney is an Emmy Award\-winning documentarian who has worked as a director of photography and producer for over a decade\. His work for *PBS*, *History Channel* and *Discovery Channel* has taken him around the world\.

McKinney has spent the last six years field\-producing and teaching the [International Reporting Program](http://www.internationalreporting.org/) at the School of Journalism\. In 2011 he led a group of students to the [Ukraine to investigate access to pain medication](http://www.aljazeera.com/programmes/peopleandpower/2011/07/2011720113555645271.html) and he went to China in 2013 to look at the [effects of pollution on children in Beijing\.](http://internationalreporting.org/greenchina/) 

McKinney has recently branched off into the non\-profit world creating web documentaries for eco\-friendly companies and institutions\. Watch his short doc about the secret life of trees [here\.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-8SORM4dYG8)

McKinney graduated from UC Berkeley’s school of journalism in 2002 where he specialized in documentary filmmaking\.
