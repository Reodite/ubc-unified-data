[image](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/crowdsourcing-panel-with-prof-hermida-and-guests/)Join UBC journalism professor Alfred Hermida on Oct\. 6 for a panel discussion at W2 in Vancouver on one of the major trends in media\-making, crowdsourcing\.

The event, “Harnessing The Crowd: The Evolving Crowdsourcing/Collaboration/OpenEverything Continuum,” is part of the [Fresh Media ReMixology series of talks](http://www.facebook.com/event.php?eid=153162971382884&amp;ref=ts "Fresh Media ReMixology series of talks")\. The events are designed to bring people together to sample and share ideas in discussions\.

Together with Prof Hermida will be [Leigh Christie](http://ca.linkedin.com/pub/leigh-christie/9/6a7/527 "Leigh Christie"), executive director and co\-founder of the [eatART foundation](http://www.eatart.org/index.html "eatART foundation"), and [David Ascher](http://www.mozillamessaging.com/en-US/about/staff/ "David Ascher"), CEO of Mozilla Messaging\.

The discussion takes place on Wednesday Oct\. 6 at 6:30pm @ W2 Storyeum Salon, 151 West Cordova, Vancouver\.

[Tickets are available online](http://remixology2.eventbrite.com/ "Tickets are available online"), priced at $5 tickets or $10 for a “champion\.”

ReMixology is a series of monthly events bringing together media makers, artists, new media guru’s and citizens to re\-imagine media and celebrate innovation\.
