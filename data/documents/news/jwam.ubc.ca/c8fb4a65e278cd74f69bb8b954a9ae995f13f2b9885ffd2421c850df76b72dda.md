> UBC Journalism students have won four 2025 [Canadian Online Publishing Awards](https://canadianonlinepublishingawards.com/2025/winners) \(COPAs\) for their visual journalism projects\.

The awards, announced on February 12th, recognize the country’s best in digital media, from legacy newsrooms to emerging student talent\.

The students won four silvers in the academic category\. Two other UBC Journalism projects were finalists in the academic and the consumer media categories\.

Cooking pork

The School’s success was driven by innovative [multimedia projects](https://ubcjournalism.shorthandstories.com/) produced as part of its second\-year [Visual Journalism courses](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-520-v/) taught by professors Alfred Hermida and Uytae Lee in fall 2024 and winter 2025\.

One of the winning projects, Pyar is Pyar by Chaimae Chouiekh, has already been recognized with a Radio Television Digital News Association \(RTDNA\) Canada [Best Canadian Local News Award](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/ubc-journalism-rtdna_canada_win/) and a [Canada West Region award](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/ubc-journalism-celebrates-double-rtdna-canada-wins/)\. The multimedia piece explored the intersection of faith, love, and LGBTQ\+ identity in Surrey’s South Asian community

In 2024, [students won four COPAs awards](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/ubc-journalism-students-triumph-in-canadian-online-publishing-awards/), while students and faculty [took home five](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/ubc-journalism-scoops-seven-copa-nominations-copy/) in 2023\.

The COPAs recognize excellence in digital journalism\. The winners for 2025 were announced on February 12 in Toronto\.

### **COPA winners**

**Academic Division**

*Category: Best Feature/Cover Story \(silver\)*  
[Pyar is Pyar](https://ubcjournalism.shorthandstories.com/pyar-is-pyar/index.html) by Chaimae Chouiekh

*Category: Best Photo Journalism \(silver\)*  
[Food without borders](https://ubcjournalism.shorthandstories.com/food-without-borders/index.html) by Bismah Mughal

*Category: Best Multicultural Story \(silver\)*  
[She Speaks in Power](https://ubcjournalism.shorthandstories.com/she-speaks-in-power/index.html) by Katrianna Skulsky

*Best Patriotic Story \(silver\)*  
[A Taste of Home](https://ubcjournalism.shorthandstories.com/a-taste-of-home/index.html) by Jasmine Ng

### **2025 COPA finalists**

**Academic division**

*Category: Best Feel Good Story*  
[Vancouver’s Food Rescue Service](https://ubcjournalism.shorthandstories.com/vancouvers-food-rescue-service/index.html) by Tia Sacks

**Consumer division**

*Category: Best Video Content*  
[Tobi Akinkunmi: Surging Forward](https://ubcjournalism.shorthandstories.com/tobi-akinkunmi-surging-forward/index.html) by Ashrit Vaidya &amp; Ian French
