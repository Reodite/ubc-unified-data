The W\. Maurice Young Centre for Applied Ethics is pleased to present a talk by Stephen J\. Ward on the topic of “The Challenges of Science Journalists in a Media\-saturated Universe\.”

The talk will take place on Thursday, April 10, from 2:30 to 4:00 in the Forest Sciences Building, Room 1221, 2424 Main Mall\.  Professor Ward will discuss science journalism research and the future of quality science journalism and public communication of science\.
