The Journalists for Human Rights are celebrating the launch of their first, nationally distributed, human rights publication *Speak Magazine* from 4:00 to 5:00 on March 23rd in MASS \(Meekison Arts Student Space\) Buchanan D\.  They are holding a speaker\-panel on the relationship between media and human rights\.  Panelists are:

Stephen Toope, President and Vice Chancellor, University of British Columbia  
Peter Klein, Emmy award\-winning journalist and filmmaker for *CBC* and *60 Minutes*, Professor at the UBC School of Journalism  
Darren Fleet, former Journalists for Human Rights Media Trainer in Zambia; graduate student at UBC School of Journalism

Wine and beer will be served after the panel\.

For more information, please contact Kate O’Neill at info@jhr\-ubc\.ca
