Director Stephen J\. Ward’s award\-winning book *The Invention of Journalism Ethics: The Path to Objectivity and Beyond* was translated recently into Korean, and published by the Korean National Open University Press for use in South Korea\.

The book won the 2006 Harold Innis Prize for the best English\-language book in the social sciences in Canada, from the Federation for the Humanities and Social Sciences\.
