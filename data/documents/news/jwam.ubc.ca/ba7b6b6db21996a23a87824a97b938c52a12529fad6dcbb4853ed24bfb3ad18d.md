Allison GrinerUBC Journalism graduate [Allison Griner](http://allisongriner.com/) has won an Online Journalism Award as part of the [News21](http://news21.com/) team that produced [Gun Wars](http://gunwars.news21.com/#overview), an in\-depth analysis of the debate over gun control in the U\.S\.

The award was announced at the [Online Journalism Awards](http://journalists.org/2015/09/27/the-wall-street-journal-reported-ly-baltimore-sun-and-bbc-news-take-home-2015-online-journalism-awards/) dinner held September 26 in Los Angeles\.

The awards recognize excellence in digital journalism across the world\. The News21 Gun Wars project won in the category of Student Projects, Pro\-Am\.

UBC Journalism director, Alfred Hermida, [was nominated](http://journalists.org/2015/08/11/2015-online-journalism-awards-finalists-announced/) in the category of online commentary \(small\)\. The award went to Marcell Shehwaro, a Syrian blogger who documented [life in the city of Aleppo](https://globalvoices.org/2014/06/27/we-have-all-become-killers-in-syria/) for Global Voices\.

The awards are the climax of the annual [Online News Association conference](http://ona15.journalists.org/), an annual gathering of journalists, media executives, technologists and academics interested in emerging practices in digital news, technology trends and industry developments\.

**Canadian university first**

News21 brings together reporters, videographers and photojournalists from universities to collaborate on an investigative project\.

Allison was the first recipient of the prestigious [News21 fellowship](http://journalism.ubc.ca/soj-student-receives-prestigious-news21-fellowship/?login) from a Canadian university when she was selected as a fellow in 2014\.

The following year, another of our students, Emi Sasagawa, was named a News21 fellow\. She worked on a News21 project on [marijuana legalization in the U\.S\.](http://weedrush.news21.com/)

News21 is based at the Walter Cronkite School of Journalism and Mass Communication at Arizona State University\.
