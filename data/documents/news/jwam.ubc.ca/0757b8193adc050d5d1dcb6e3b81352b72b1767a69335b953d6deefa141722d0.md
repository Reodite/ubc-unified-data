The School of Journalism’s student\-led news service, [TheThunderbird\.ca](http://thethunderbird.ca/ "TheThunderbird.ca"), is the only journalism school news website nominated for The Canadian Online Publishing Awards\.  The Thunderbird\.ca was chosen as a finalist in the category of best online\-only article/series for a series on immigration\.  The new Canadian Publishing Awards, produced by Masthead, received more than 250 entrants\.

The articles are:

[Premier woos Punjabis](http://thethunderbird.ca/2009/04/20/premier-woos-punjabis-with-overseas-immigration-office/ "Premier woos Punjabis") by student Kate Allen

[Afghan pop singer stands up for women](http://thethunderbird.ca/2009/04/14/afghan-popstar-stands-up-for-women/ "Afghan pop singer stands up for women") by student Erin Empey

[Canada failing foreign nannies](http://thethunderbird.ca/2009/04/08/canada-failing-foreign-nannies/ "Canada failing foreign nannies") by student Rebecca teBrake

The articles originated from assignments in the school’s Integrated Journalism program in Spring 2009\. They are up against entries from fellow\-nominees, The Tyee, Canadian Living and Granville Online\. Winners will be announced Oct\. 26 in Toronto\. The Canadian Online Publishing Awards recognizes excellence in online editorial and innovation by Canadian magazine and website publishers\.
