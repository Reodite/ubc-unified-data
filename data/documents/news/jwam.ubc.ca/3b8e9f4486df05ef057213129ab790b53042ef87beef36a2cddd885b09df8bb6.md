Allison Cross, who graduated from the UBC Graduate School of Journalism in 2009, is currently on board the HMCS Fredericton in the Gulf of Aden\.

[https://soj\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2010/03/Allison\_photo\_for\_web\.jpg](https://soj.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2010/03/Allison_photo_for_web.jpg)Allison Cross

Allison Cross

Allison works for Canwest News Service in Ottawa\.

She was sent to the Fredericton to report on the Canadian Navy’s counter\-terrorism mission in the region\.

The region, which includes countries like Yemen and Somalia, has been patrolled by different international navies since 2001\.
