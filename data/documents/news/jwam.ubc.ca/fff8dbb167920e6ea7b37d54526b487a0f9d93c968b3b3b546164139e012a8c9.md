The School of Journalism, Writing, and Media in the Faculty of Arts at the University of British Columbia, Vancouver, invites UBC students to apply to join the ASRW Program as an Undergrad Teaching Assistant for Term 1 of 2020W\.

Students in all faculties who have taken WRDS 150 are encouraged to apply because of their previous involvement in the course\.

Review of applications will begin on Thursday August 20th 2020 and will continue until the positions are filled\. Click [**here**](https://asrw.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/2020WT1-UTAII-job-description-1.pdf) for more information\. **Click [here](https://jrnl.air.arts.ubc.ca/asrw-undergrad-ta-2-2020w/) to apply\.**
