The UBC Graduate School of Journalism was featured in a report by CBC National on Thursday, September 25th\.

CBC National reported on comments by NDP candidate Michael Byers calling for the tar sands to be shut down, during a debate between Vancouver candidates at the UBC Graduate School of Journalism

The debate between the four candidates competing for the Vancouver\-Centre riding — Hedy Fry \(Liberal incumbent\), Lorne Mayencourt \(Conservative\), Adriane Carr \(Green Party\) and Michael Byers \(NDP\) — was a class assignment for first\-year journalism students\.

The debate was filmed by CBC National and [live blogged](http://thethunderbird.ca/2008/09/25/live-blog-j-school-hosts-vancouver-election-debate/ "live blogged") by journalism student Erin Empey\.

The full debate is available as [audio on TheThunderbird\.ca](http://thethunderbird.ca/2008/09/25/ndp-candidate-michael-byers-calls-for-end-to-tar-sands/ "audio on TheThunderbird.ca"), as well as a podcast from the [UBC Graduate School of Journalism on iTunes U](http://deimos.apple.com/WebObjects/Core.woa/Browse/ubc.ca.1624074662.01624074664 "UBC Graduate School of Journalism on iTunes U")\.
