> UBC students have secured six 2025  [Canadian Online Publishing Awards](https://canadianonlinepublishingawards.com/2025/winners) \(COPAs\) nominations for their visual journalism projects\.

Five of the projects are finalists in the academic category for student projects, and one is a finalist in the consumer category, which caters to consumer niches such as consumer magazines and radio/TV stations\.

Market Stall

[The projects](https://ubcjournalism.shorthandstories.com/) were produced for the [Visual Journalism courses](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-520-v/) taught by professors Alfred Hermida and Uytae Lee in fall 2024 and winter 2025\.

One of the nominated projects, Pyar is Pyar by Chaimae Chouiekh, has already been recognized with a Radio Television Digital News Association \(RTDNA\) Canada [Best Canadian Local News Award](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/ubc-journalism-rtdna_canada_win/) and a [Canada West Region award](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/ubc-journalism-celebrates-double-rtdna-canada-wins/)

In 2024, [students won four COPAs awards](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/ubc-journalism-students-triumph-in-canadian-online-publishing-awards/), while students and faculty [took home five](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/ubc-journalism-scoops-seven-copa-nominations-copy/) in 2023\.

The COPAs recognize excellence in digital journalism\. The winners for 2025 will be announced on February 12 in Toronto at Dim Sum King and broadcast live on Zoom\.
