UBC journalism school student Amanda Ash has been featured in a [CBC Radio 3 show](http://radio3.cbc.ca/blogs/2009/05/Live-Girlsor-A-Radio-Host-and-an-Intern-Talk-about-Women-in-Music "CBC Radio 3 show") discussing the place of women in music\.

[https://soj\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2009/05/images\.jpg](https://soj.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2009/05/images.jpg)Amanda Ash

Amanda Ash

Amanda was interning at Radio 3 in Vancouver for two weeks as part of her Masters of Journalism program\.

During her time at the station, she produced a radio documentary about women in Canadian indie music\.

Amanda describes herself as a music junkie, freelancing for the Vancouver Sun, the Edmonton Journal, Exclaim\! Magazine, NIGHTLIFE Magazine and SPINearth\.tv\.

She is also a juror for the Polaris Music Prize\.

She blogs about indie music at [The Indie Files](http://theindiefiles.wordpress.com/ "The Indie Files")\.

Amanda will also be interning at the Victoria [Times Colonist](http://www.timescolonist.com/ "Times Colonist") over the summer\.
