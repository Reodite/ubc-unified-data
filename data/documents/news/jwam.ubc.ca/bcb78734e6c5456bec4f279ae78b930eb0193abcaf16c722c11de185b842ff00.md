Documentary “To The Tar Sands” by Jodie Martinson \(‘10\) will be shown on Jan\. 20\. The film, which was recently featured at the Calgary International Film Festival, tells the story of a group of young environmentalists who traveled Alberta by bike in 2007 and offers an under\-represented perspective on the Alberta oil sands and the environmental impact they are having on Canada and the world\. The screening will be followed by a question period with four of the cyclists from the documentary\.

The event is sponsored by the Student Environment Centre, is free and open to all\.

The screening will be held:

Date: Jan\. 20  
Time: 5:30\-7:30PM  
Place: Hennings 202 \(corner of Agricultural Rd and East Mall\)
