A panel will give short presentations, followed by lengthy discussion\.

The panelists are:

– Simon Donner, Assistant Professor of Climatology in UBC’s Department of Geography\. Simon leads a program of teaching and research at the interface of climate science, marine science, ecology and public policy\. He is actively involved in public outreach on climate change through a variety of initiatives including the Aldo Leopold Leadership Program\.

– Candis Callison, Assistant Professor at UBC’s School of Journalism\.  Candis received her PhD and MSc from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, and previously worked as a journalist in Canada and the US\.  Her research foci includes environment and science journalism, public engagement, media change and democracy\.

– Nicola Jones, Science Journalist in Residence at UBC’s School of Journalism, Commissioning editor for the ‘comment’ section of Nature, and freelance reporter\. Nicola has been a science journalist for more than 10 years in England and Canada, focusing on the earth sciences\.
