University of British Columbia Graduate School of Journalism students, Jes Abeita ‘11 and Shannon Dooling ‘12, have been selected as a finalists in [Photographer’s Forum Magazine’s](http://pfmagazine.com/ "Photographer's Forum Magazine's") 31st Annual College Photography Contest\.[https://soj\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2011/03/Checking\_it\_twice\.jpg](https://soj.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/03/Checking_it_twice.jpg)Checking\_it\_twice

Abeita, a second\-year student in the Master’s program, has been taking photos since she was five years old\.

[https://soj\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2011/03/dooling\.jpg](https://soj.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/03/dooling.jpg)dooling“The program at UBC has really given me the chance to see my photography as another tool in my reporter’s toolkit, not just a hobby,” she said\.

Over 13,000 images were submitted by college students from around the world, of that group 1,570 images were selected as finalists\.
