Nicola Jones, Science Journalist in Residence, UBC Graduate School of Journalism  
Commissioning Editor, Opinion section, Nature

Feb\. 7th, 12:30 p\.m\. – 2 p\.m\. School of Journalism main lecture room

Find out how Nature commissions, reviews, edits and publishes everything from the research papers through the opinion section to the news\. Get a guided tour of how Nature is adapting to new media\. Hear about Nature’s role in dealing with hot science papers and scandals, from faked stem cell lines through to ‘climategate’ and more\. There will be time for discussion and questions\.
