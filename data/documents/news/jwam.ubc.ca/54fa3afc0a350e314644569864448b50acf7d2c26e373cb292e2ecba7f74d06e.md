Kim Ives, the founding editor of the Haitian weekly newspaper *[Haiti Liberté,](http://www.haiti-liberte.com/archives/volume4-50/U.S.%20Embassy%20Cables.asp)* the host of a weekly Haiti show on WBAI\-FM New York, and a filmmaker who has helped produce several [Image](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/kim-ives-to-give-public-talk-on-wikileaks-shedding-light-on-u-s-policy-in-haiti/)documentaries about Haiti, will be giving a public presentation titled “Wikileaks: Shedding Light on U\.S\. Policy in Haiti” on Tuesday, January 31st, 2012 from 12:30\-2:00 p\.m\. at the UBC Graduate School of Journalism\.

He was lead writer and editor of a [series of articles](http://canadahaitiaction.ca/wikileaks) published in 2011 by *Haiti Liberté* and [*The Nation*](http://www.thenation.com/article/161009/wikihaiti-nation-partners-haiti-liberte-release-secret-haiti-cables) magazine about U\.S\. government policy in Haiti, which were based on U\.S\. diplomatic cables entrusted to the two publications by the Wikileaks organization\.

Where: UBC Graduate School of Journalism, 6388 Crescent Road, Room 104

When: Tuesday, January 31st, 2012, 12:30\-2:00 p\.m\.
