PhD student Nikki Stuart has published new research in the journal *Psychosomatic Medicine* \([**https://doi\.org/10\.1097/psy\.0000000000001338**](https://doi.org/10.1097/psy.0000000000001338)\)\. Her paper examines the associations between stressors and subjective cognitive function in everyday life, and whether physical activity buffers against stressor\-related cognitive effects\.
