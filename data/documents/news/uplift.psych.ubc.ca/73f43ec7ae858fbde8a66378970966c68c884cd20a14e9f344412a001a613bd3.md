Nancy Sin was recently featured in a Q &amp; A in the [UBC Psychology News](https://psych.ubc.ca/news/qa-the-american-psychosomatic-society-antiracism-task-force-implementation-activities-and-lessons-learned/)\. The task force published a paper to guide other organizations on practices to promote equity, diversity, and inclusion\.
