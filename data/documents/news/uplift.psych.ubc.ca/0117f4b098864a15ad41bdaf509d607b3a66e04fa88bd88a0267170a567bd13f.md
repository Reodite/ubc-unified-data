Undergraduate students Taima and Kyra showcased their research at this year’s Psychology Undergraduate Research Conference\. Taima presented a poster on the relationship between age\-based unfair treatment and depressive symptoms, and Kyra shared a poster examining the role of loneliness in working memory function\.
