A message from Sharmila Anandasabapathy, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, UBC\.

---

I am pleased to announce that Dr\. Kishore Mulpuri has been reappointed as Head, Department of Orthopaedics for a second five\-year term effective to June 30, 2031\.

Dr\. Mulpuri is a professor in the Department of Orthopaedics and a pediatric orthopaedic surgeon at BC Children’s Hospital\. He is also the principal investigator of the Global Hip Dysplasia Registry and the Slipped Capital Femoral Epiphysis Longitudinal International Prospective Registry, two global research collaborations advancing the care of pediatric hip conditions\. His clinical practice and research are dedicated to improving care for children and adolescents with hip conditions stemming from cerebral palsy, developmental conditions and traumatic injuries\.

[Dr\. Kishore Mulpuri](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/KishoreMulpuri_400x400.jpg)

In his first term as Head, Department of Orthopaedics, Dr\. Mulpuri led the Department in developing and launching its strategic plan, creating a shared vision for orthopaedic education, research and care in alignment with the Faculty of Medicine’s strategic priorities\. Dr\. Mulpuri further fostered a culture of academic excellence within the Department through a variety of initiatives, including the establishment of a new award for early\-career faculty, while investing in the next generation of academic leaders through new faculty development and mentorship opportunities\. Dr\. Mulpuri also strengthened the Department’s connections with orthopaedic leaders across British Columbia and with international partners, including extending the Department’s global impact through UBC Global Orthopaedics, aimed at improving orthopaedic care, research and training in underserved regions around the world\.

In his second term, Dr\. Mulpuri will continue strengthening the Department through strategic investment and recruitment, including the recruitment and future appointment of a new Tier 2 Canada Research Chair\. He will also advance the Department’s education and research programs through seed funding and new grants supporting clinical quality improvement and innovation, while continuing to celebrate excellence across the Department’s community of faculty, staff and learners\.

Please join me in congratulating Dr\. Mulpuri on his reappointment\.
