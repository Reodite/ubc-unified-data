A message from Ravi Sidhu, Vice Dean, Education\.

---

I am pleased to announce the appointment of Dr\. Rita Shah as the Associate Dean, Undergraduate Medical Education \(UGME\), effective September 21, 2026 to September 21, 2031\.

Dr\. Shah is a clinical associate professor in the Department of Family Practice\. She has held a number of leadership roles within the Faculty, most recently serving as Assistant Dean, Vancouver Fraser Medical Program since 2022\. Alongside her academic leadership, she practises as a family physician at the Reach Community Health Centre, a community\-based clinic serving a population that includes Indigenous, newcomer and lower\-income communities\.

[Dr\. Rita Shah](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/RitaShah_400x400.jpg)

Dr\. Shah represents UBC on the board of the Canadian Association for Medical Education and serves on the Association of Faculties of Medicine of Canada’s Disability Inclusion and Accessibility Network\. She is also a Fellow of the College of Family Physicians of Canada\. Dr\. Shah’s background in medical education, curriculum, assessment and program evaluation will support the MD Undergraduate Program \(MDUP\)’s continued focus on educational quality and innovation\.

As Associate Dean, UGME, Dr\. Shah will provide strategic leadership for undergraduate medical education within the Faculty of Medicine, collaborating closely with the Regional Associate Deans to ensure continuity and advancement of UBC’s undergraduate medical education across British Columbia\. Building on the program’s strong foundation, she will sustain momentum on key strategic priorities and ongoing initiatives, fostering collaboration across regions and the broader Faculty to support a cohesive, innovative and forward\-looking approach to undergraduate medical education at UBC\.

Dr\. Shah’s appointment follows the leadership of Dr\. Andrea Townson\. We extend our deepest gratitude to Dr\. Townson for her dedicated service as Associate Dean pro tem, UGME during this period\. Over the past six months, she has provided thoughtful and steady leadership, supporting the continued delivery and advancement of high\-quality and inclusive educational experiences for learners and trainees across the MDUP\. We are grateful for her commitment, care for our learners and the lasting contributions she has made to our community\.

Please join me in congratulating Dr\. Shah on her appointment\.
