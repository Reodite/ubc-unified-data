Check out WildCo’s new addition – “Effects of scent lure on camera trap detections vary across mammalian predator and prey species” [published in PlosOne this week](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0229055)\. It shows that using of scent lure can attract predators to your cameras, but its effects are highly species\-specific\. The lead author, Dacyn Holinda, was an undergraduate in the lab from 2017\-2018… great work team\!
