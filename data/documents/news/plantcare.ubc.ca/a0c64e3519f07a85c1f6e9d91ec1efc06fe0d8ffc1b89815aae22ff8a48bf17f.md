November 27, 2019

Plant Care Services is pleased to announce that the heating upgrade at the South Campus greenhouse has been completed in time for the cold season\.

The upgrade allows for a backup heat system and provides overall more heat output than the old boiler\. The four new Viessmann boilers operate at great efficiency\.

Thanks to the VPRI Office, Provost Office and Project Services for supporting this project\.
