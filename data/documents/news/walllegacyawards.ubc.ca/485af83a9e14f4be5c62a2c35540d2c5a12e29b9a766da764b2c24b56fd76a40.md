UBC is pleased to announce the recipients of the 2024 Wall Fellowships and Wall Research Awards\. These recipients represent a wide array of scholarly programs that will generate meaningful solutions to major sustainability challenges in BC and around the world\.

The Wall Legacy Awards program is funded by the $100M\+ Peter Wall Endowment and supports $4M in research annually for UBC faculty and graduate students\. The program includes two Wall Fellowships valued at $1M each, and this year includes 19 graduate student awards and a total of eight faculty research awards for individual and team projects\.

This suite of awards is one of the largest internal award programs at any university in North America\. The outcomes of this research will elevate UBC’s global reputation, inform policy and directly impact the future of our province\.

All projects align with three key areas of focus: urban sustainability, environmental protection of coastal areas, and sustainable approaches to resource\-intensive industries, with a particular emphasis on the province of B\.C\. This year’s award recipients are leading a diverse range of research projects on issues in the province, from climate change to biodiversity loss, sustainable transportation, energy storage, urban water systems, and biodiversity conservation\.

We are extremely grateful to Dr\. Wall for his generous donations, which represent a sustained investment in UBC’s research and scholarly community\.
