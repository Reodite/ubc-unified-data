### UBC Tribute to Peter Wall

UBC gratefully honours the life of our friend Peter Wall, one of Vancouver’s leading land developers and philanthropists\. The word “visionary” is often used to describe successful leaders\. In the case of Peter, “prescient” might be an even more accurate descriptor\. His willingness to invest in the future and to see the potential in people and what they can achieve is part of his legacy to the city, the province and UBC\.  
  
*March 6, 2025
