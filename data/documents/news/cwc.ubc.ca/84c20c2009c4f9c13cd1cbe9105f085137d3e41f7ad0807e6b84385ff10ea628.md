UBC’s new *Push Forward* campaign highlights wildfire coexistence research at UBC, emphasizing that wildfires are a year\-round challenge requiring proactive, collaborative solutions\. Through cutting\-edge data, predictive technologies, and community partnerships, UBC students and researchers are shifting the focus from reactive wildfire response to long\-term preparedness and resilience – helping communities learn to live with fire while shaping a safer, more sustainable future\.
