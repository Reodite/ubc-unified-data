May 26, 2025

On Thursday, May 8, 2025, the UBC community gathered to celebrate a landmark moment for the Department of Mining Engineering — the establishment of the **George Poling Professorship in Critical Minerals Processing and Innovation**\.

This special event honoured **Dr\. George Poling**, whose generous support and outstanding legacy have helped shape generations of mineral processing professionals\. His contributions continue to inspire innovation and excellence in a field critical to Canada’s resource future\.

The reception brought together faculty, alumni, industry partners, and friends to reflect on Dr\. Poling’s enduring impact and to mark the beginning of an exciting new chapter for the department\.

Image

[Marek Pawlik, Benoit\-Antoine Bacon \(UBC President\), George Poling, and James Olson \(Dean of APSC\)](https://mining.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/original_image/public/2025-07/marek_pawlik_benoit-antoine_bacon_ubc_president_george_poling_and_james_olson_dean_of_apsc_optimized_1000_3_optimized_250-2.png.webp?itok=zATRuDRh)

We are pleased to announce that recruitment for the **George Poling Professor** will begin this summer\. This new role will strengthen UBC’s leadership in critical minerals research, education, and innovation — helping to build a more sustainable and secure future\.

Thank you to everyone who joined us to celebrate\.
