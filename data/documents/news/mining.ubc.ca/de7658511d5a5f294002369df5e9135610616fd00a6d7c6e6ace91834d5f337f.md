This year, UBC secured a back\-to\-back victory after winning the 35th Annual Canadian Mining Games held at Université Laval\!

[2025’s winning team](https://mining.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2025-05/galacmg25-11388.jpg.webp?itok=HHwynha-)

March 25, 2025

**2025’s winning team members:**  
Back \(left to right\): Tom Terblanche, Malcolm Salamon, Balrammit Singh Bhullar, Jackson Thomas, Gage Paskiewich, Markus Frigaard, Ethan Stroud, Ian Platzer, Dylan McIntosh  
Front \(left to right\): Dawson Chung, Gabriel Demers, Matthew Chee, Jun Yao Wang \(Captain\), Dylan Conway \(Captain\), Ruby Pribadi, Dies Haditsa Putra Nursadih

The team of 16 was thrown into 27 different mining\-related challenges that simulate problems which occur in actual work environments\. Despite the tight competition and time\-constrained environment, team members efficiently delivered quality mine designs, process flowsheets, and economic analysis as well as demonstrating benchmark standards in mine rescue and equipment handling\. The team has shown incredible dedication and is proud to have achieved for the department and defend UBC's championship title across the top mining schools in Canada\.
