## [Be part of Homecoming, Saturday, September 17th\!](https://broadcastemail.ubc.ca/2016/09/16/be-part-of-homecoming-saturday-september-17th/)

September 16, 2016

You’ll want to be one of 10,000 fans at Thunderbird Stadium for the biggest event of the year \#UBCHomecoming 2016\! This UBC tradition starts on Saturday, September 17th at the Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre where it kicks off with the Great Thunderbird Trek and carries on to a high\-energy pre\-game street party at Thunderbird Stadium\. Wear your ‘Blue and Gold’ and come cheer on the 2016 Vanier Cup Champion UBC Thunderbirds football team as they take on Regina\. Tickets are available now, get yours early to avoid lineups at the stadium\. \#gobirdsgo
