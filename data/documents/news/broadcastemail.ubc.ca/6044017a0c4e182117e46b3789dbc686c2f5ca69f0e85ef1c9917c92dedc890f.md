To: Faculty and staff in Vancouver and the Okanagan

As announced yesterday, the Board of Governors and the Senates of UBC have agreed to the Terms of Reference for a presidential search committee\. As part of that process, faculty members, management &amp; professional staff, and unionized staff from both campuses of UBC are to elect members to the search committee\.

For more information or to make a nomination for the committee, please visit [http://facultystaff\.students\.ubc\.ca/psc](http://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/psc) or contact [christopher\.eaton@ubc\.ca](mailto:christopher.eaton@ubc.ca)\. For more information on the search process generally, please visit [http://bog\.ubc\.ca/?page\_id\=10201](http://bog.ubc.ca/?page_id=10201)\.

Please note that all nominations for members of the search committee are due by 4 pm on 5 October 2015\.

**Christopher Eaton**  
Director, Office of the Senate
