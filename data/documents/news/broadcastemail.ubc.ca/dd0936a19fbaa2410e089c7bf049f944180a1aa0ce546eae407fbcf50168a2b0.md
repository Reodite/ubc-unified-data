## [External review of Enrolment Services](https://broadcastemail.ubc.ca/2017/10/12/external-review-of-enrolment-services/)

October 12, 2017

An external review of Enrolment Services is being carried out to assist the university as it considers the reappointment of Dr\. Kate Ross\. Dr\. Ross’ current term as Associate Vice\-President, Enrolment Services and Registrar comes to an end on 31 October, 2018\.
