There have now been two calls for nominations for representatives of the Joint Faculties \(faculty members\-at\-large\) on the Vancouver Senate\.

At this stage in the process, we would like to inform you that elections to fill the remaining positions are now underway and you are invited to vote for your Joint Faculties representatives on the Senate\. Candidate information and voting instructions are available [online](https://ubc.simplyvoting.com/)\.

The deadline for voting is 4pm on Thursday, January 22\.

Please contact [elections\.information@ubc\.ca](mailto:elections.information@ubc.ca) if you have any questions\.

**Rella Ng**  
Associate Vice\-President, Enrolment Services and Registrar
