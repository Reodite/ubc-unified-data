To: Faculty, staff and students in Vancouver and the Okanagan

As we enter the final stages of UBC’s strategic planning process, I would like to remind you of the different ways to have your say on the emerging plan\.

**Online survey \(November 23 – December 8\)**  
I invite you to take the time to share your thoughts on the emerging plan, and your ideas for how we can bring the plan to life, by completing this online survey before December 8 [https://openminds\.ubc\.ca/c/r/Phase5\_Consultation](https://openminds.ubc.ca/c/r/Phase5_Consultation)

**Open houses \(November 24 – 30\)**  
There is also an opportunity to provide your input in person at one of the following open house events:

**UBC Vancouver**  
Friday November 24, 2017  
12 pm to 4 pm  
Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre  
Golden Jubilee Room \(4th floor\)

Wednesday November 29, 2017  
4 pm to 7 pm  
Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre  
Golden Jubilee Room \(4th floor\)

**UBC Okanagan**  
Thursday November 30, 2017  
11 am to 2 pm  
Charles E\. Fipke Centre for Innovative Research \(foyer\)

To learn more about the strategic plan and the process so far, visit [www\.ubc\.ca/strategicplan](http://www.ubc.ca/strategicplan)\.

Whatever your role at UBC, I sincerely hope you will take the time to participate in this dialogue\.

**Professor Santa J\. Ono**  
President and Vice\-Chancellor
