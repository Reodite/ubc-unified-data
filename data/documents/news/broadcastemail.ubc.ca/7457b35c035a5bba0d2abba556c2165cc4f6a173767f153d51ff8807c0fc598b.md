To: Faculty and staff in Vancouver and the Okanagan

The results are in\! Together with our survey partner, TalentMap, Human Resources will present the survey results at open sessions in Vancouver and the Okanagan\.

All faculty and staff are welcome to attend and learn what colleagues said about their experience in areas such as:

-   Professional growth
-   Faculty support and academic excellence
-   Collaboration and communications
-   Health &amp; wellbeing and work/life integration
-   And more

## Vancouver Results Open Session

Wednesday, February 7, 2018  
10:00 am – 11:30 am  
Jack Poole Hall — South, Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre  
To attend the event, please register: [http://ubc\.ca/wes](https://broadcast.ubc.ca/sendy/l/D0dECfgn892UqAH4q8YAAh763A/EvNwKeBN53x3GXRR7632KALQ/Vge77wKzflM46Knbx8RPDQ)

We wish to accommodate as many participants as possible\. With a limited number of seats available, we are also providing a livestream option\. To watch the session live, please visit: [http://ubc\.ca/wes](https://broadcast.ubc.ca/sendy/l/D0dECfgn892UqAH4q8YAAh763A/EvNwKeBN53x3GXRR7632KALQ/Vge77wKzflM46Knbx8RPDQ)

## Okanagan Results Open Session

Thursday, February 8, 2018  
1:30 pm – 3:00 pm  
University Centre, Ballroom, UNC 200

After the open sessions, overall reports for both Vancouver and the Okanagan will be available online for all UBC faculty and staff\. Before the end of February, all units with ten or more survey respondents will also receive unit\-level reports\.

For more information, visit [http://ubc\.ca/wes](https://broadcast.ubc.ca/sendy/l/D0dECfgn892UqAH4q8YAAh763A/EvNwKeBN53x3GXRR7632KALQ/Vge77wKzflM46Knbx8RPDQ) or contact [workplace\.surveys@ubc\.ca](mailto:workplace.surveys@ubc.ca)\.

Thank you to all who have participated in the survey and helped to create a clearer picture of our workplace experience\.
