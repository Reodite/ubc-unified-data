**Vancouver Campus  
**Date/Time: June 13, 2016 10am  
Location:  
Franklin Lew Forum  
Allard Hall  
1822 East Mall, Vancouver

**Okanagan Campus  
**Date/Time: June 13, 2016 2pm  
Location:  
EME Richard S\. Hallisey Atrium
