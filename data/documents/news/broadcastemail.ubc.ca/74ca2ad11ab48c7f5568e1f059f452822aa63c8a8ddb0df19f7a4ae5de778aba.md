To: Faculty in Vancouver

Elections are now open to fill the vacancy for an elected Vancouver faculty member on the Board of Governors for the remainder of the 2017\-2020 triennium\.

Polls are open on the university’s WebVote system today, and will close at **4pm on Tuesday, June 5th, 2018**\.

For voting instructions and candidate information please visit: [https://facultystaff\.students\.ubc\.ca/2018\-board\-governors\-election\-vancouver\-faculty\-member](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/2018-board-governors-election-vancouver-faculty-member)\.

If you have any questions, please contact [elections\.information@ubc\.ca](mailto:elections.information@ubc.ca)\.

**Kate Ross**  
Associate Vice\-President, Enrolment Services and Registrar
