As part of the 2026\-2029 triennial election process, we would like to invite nominations for two representatives from each Faculty to serve on the Okanagan and Vancouver Senates\.

Nomination forms and information about eligibility are posted [online](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/enrolment-services/senate-curriculum-services/elections-and-search-committees/2025-2026-triennial-elections/faculty-and-professional-librarian-representatives-okanagan-and-vancouver-senates)\.

Nominations close at 4pm on February 19\.

For general questions, please contact UBC Elections at [elections\.information@ubc\.ca](mailto:elections.information@ubc.ca)\.

**Rella Ng**  
Associate Vice\-President, Enrolment Services and Registrar
