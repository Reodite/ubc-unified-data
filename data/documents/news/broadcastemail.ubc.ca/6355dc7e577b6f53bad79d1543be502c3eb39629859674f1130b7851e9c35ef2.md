To: Faculty and staff in Vancouver and the Okanagan

I wish to announce that UBC has embarked on a global search for a new Vice\-President, External Relations\.

The Vice\-President, External Relations reports to me and provides strategic leadership to the External Relations portfolio\. As a member of the UBC Executive, the role has responsibility for providing strategic leadership for various communications functions, broad\-based engagement with external communities and campus planning functions\.

This position also supports the university in accomplishing the vision, commitments, goals, and actions as set out in the Strategic Plan: *Shaping UBC’s Next Century\.*

In accordance with UBC Policy \#34, I have established a committee to support this search process and prepare a recommendation of a suitable candidate for this position\. The committee consists of:

-   Santa Ono, President and Vice\-Chancellor \(Chair\)
-   Chaslynn Gillanders, Board of Governors
-   Charles Menzies, Board of Governors
-   Kevin Smith, Board of Governors
-   Deborah Buszard, Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal, UBC Okanagan
-   Rickey Yada, Dean, Faculty of Land and Food Systems
-   Peter Arthur, Professor, Faculty of Education
-   Merje Kuus, Professor, Faculty of Arts
-   Max Holmes, Vice\-President Academic AMS
-   Shahd Shaker, Vice\-President Internal UBCSUO
-   Kate Hunter, Director, Internal Communications
-   Terry Lavender, Manager, Communications, President’s Office
-   Michael White, Associate Vice\-President, Campus and Community Planning
-   Adriaan de Jager, Associate Vice\-President, Government Relations and Community Engagement

A member of the President’s Office staff will act as non\-voting secretary to the committee\. We are supported in this search by Caldwell search consultants\.

I wish to take this opportunity to once again thank our outgoing Vice\-President, External Relations, Philip Steenkamp, who recently announced that he was resigning from UBC to take up the post of President and Vice\-Chancellor at Royal Roads University\.

I want to acknowledge and thank Philip for his command of the role and wish him the best, as a new president\.

**Professor Santa J\. Ono**  
President and Vice\-Chancellor
