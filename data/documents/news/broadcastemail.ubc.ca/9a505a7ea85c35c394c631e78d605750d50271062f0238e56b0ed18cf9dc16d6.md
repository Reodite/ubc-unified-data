*If you are a manager of staff whose work is not computer\-based, please print this email and display it in a common work area for them to review\.*

To: Staff in Vancouver

I am delighted to announce the launch of the Staff Rent\-Geared\-to\-Income pilot program\.

This pilot program will be phased in over 2\-3 years to provide rental housing on campus for up to 100 lower income staff households\. Rent will be geared to 30% of household income, rather than a set percentage discount from market rates\.

Program highlights include:

-   50 spaces approved this year;
-   1 to 3 bedroom units available, depending on number of children in the household;
-   Selection will be by lottery for the first round pilot assignments and then by waitlist;
-   Subject to eligibility, applicants may remain in the program up to 10 years;

Applicants must:

-   Be full\-time staff in good standing on the Vancouver campus with 1 year or more of work experience at UBC \(or term employees with 3 or more years of continuous service\);
-   Fall under maximum gross household income and asset thresholds\.

For further information and application details please visit: [http://www\.hr\.ubc\.ca/housing\-relocation/housing/rental/rgi/](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/housing-relocation/housing/rental/rgi/)\. Enquiries may be directed to: [info\.facultyhousing@ubc\.ca](mailto:info.facultyhousing@ubc.ca)\.

The deadline for this year’s applications is **March 22, 2018**\.

I would like to take this opportunity to thank the Board of Governors for their support in this matter\. I believe that launching this program will deliver important and tangible improvements for many valued staff members at UBC\.

**Professor Santa J\. Ono**  
President and Vice\-Chancellor
