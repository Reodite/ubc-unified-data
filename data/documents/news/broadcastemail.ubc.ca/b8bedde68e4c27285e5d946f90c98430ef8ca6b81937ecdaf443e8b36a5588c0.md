To: UBC faculty and staff in Vancouver and the Okanagan

*If you are a manager of staff whose work is not computer\-based, please print this email and display it in a common work area for them to review\.*

Please be advised that an election is occurring for faculty \(Okanagan and Vancouver\) and staff \(Vancouver unionized and all Management &amp; Professional\) representatives on the Presidential Search Committee\. The Okanagan unionized position has been filled by acclamation\.

Voting is now available and closes at 4 pm on Friday 23 October 2015\.

To more information and to vote, please visit [http://facultystaff\.students\.ubc\.ca/psc](http://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/psc)\.

Thank you,

**Christopher Eaton**
