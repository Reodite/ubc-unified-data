## [UBC community advised to be aware of sophisticated phishing campaign](https://broadcastemail.ubc.ca/2025/10/29/ubc-community-advised-to-be-aware-of-sophisticated-phishing-campaign/)

October 29, 2025

Faculty, staff and students are being encouraged to be on alert for phishing emails impersonating Human Resources, payroll and/or university administration\. UBC’s cybersecurity team has identified highly convincing emails that include links to fraudulent login pages and multi\-factor authentication \(MFA\) prompts designed to compromise payroll systems\.
