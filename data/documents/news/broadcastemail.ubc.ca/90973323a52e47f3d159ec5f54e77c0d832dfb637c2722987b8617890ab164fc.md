To: Faculty, staff and students in Vancouver and the Okanagan

You are cordially invited to the formal installation of Professor Santa J\. Ono as the 15th President and Vice\-Chancellor of the University of British Columbia on Tuesday, November 22, 2016\. There are a number of events scheduled throughout the day, including some that will be live webcast\.

### Installation ceremony

*10:00–11:30 a\.m\. — The Chan Centre for the Performing Arts, Vancouver campus*

The installation ceremony will be a lively and entertaining event to welcome Professor Ono\. The ceremony will be live webcast on [president\.ubc\.ca/installation](http://president.ubc.ca/installation) and on [facebook\.com/universityofbc/](http://facebook.com/universityofbc/)

### Celebrating discovery: Four visionary thinkers – a forum hosted by Professor Santa J\. Ono

*3:00–5:00 p\.m\. — Old Auditorium, Vancouver campus*

Join Professor Ono as he interacts with thought leaders in the areas of brain health \(Dr\. Lara Boyd\), robotics \(Dr\. Elizabeth Croft\), music \(Hussein Janmohamed\) and mathematics \(Dr\. Ken Ono\)\. The forum will be live webcast on [president\.ubc\.ca/installation](http://president.ubc.ca/installation) and on [facebook\.com/universityofbc/](http://facebook.com/universityofbc/)

### Celebration concert

*7:30–9:00 p\.m\. — The Chan Centre for the Performing Arts, Vancouver campus*

Attend a celebration concert featuring students and faculty members from the UBC School of Music, and special musical guests\.

Register to attend each of these events, and learn more at [president\.ubc\.ca/installation/](http://president.ubc.ca/installation/)

Whether you are able to attend in person or plan to watch one of the webcasts, we look forward to celebrating this important occasion\.

**Philip Steenkamp**  
Vice\-President, External Relations
