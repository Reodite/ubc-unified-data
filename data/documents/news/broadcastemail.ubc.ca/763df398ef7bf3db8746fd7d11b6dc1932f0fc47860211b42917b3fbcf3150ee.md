To: Faculty and staff in Vancouver and the Okanagan

Today, UBC is announcing our new Blue &amp; Gold Campaign for Students, with the goal of raising $100 million in student support over three years – the largest fundraising campaign for students in UBC history\! The awards will support students in need, students from under\-represented communities, leadership\-based awards and combinations of these\.

To get thing started, UBC will provide up to $5 million in matching funds for new endowed gifts to select Blue &amp; Gold Campaign priorities, including the Blue &amp; Gold Bursaries, where every dollar will provide talented students with the tools they need to create lasting and meaningful change\.

Join me today – or even tomorrow on Giving Tuesday – to support UBC’s new Blue &amp; Gold Campaign for Students at: [https://support\.ubc\.ca/projects/ubc\-blueandgold\-campaign/](https://support.ubc.ca/projects/ubc-blueandgold-campaign/)

*Change their world, so they can change ours*\.

**Professor Santa J\. Ono****,**
