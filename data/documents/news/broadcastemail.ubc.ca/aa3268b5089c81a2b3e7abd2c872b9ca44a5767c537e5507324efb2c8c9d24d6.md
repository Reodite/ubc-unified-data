## [January 28 is Data Privacy Day\. Help keep your data secure](https://broadcastemail.ubc.ca/2017/01/25/january-28-is-data-privacy-day-help-keep-your-data-secure/)

January 25, 2017

As the use of mobile devices such as laptops, tablets, and smartphones increases, the need to be aware of the security risks to your data is also heightened\. January 28 is Data Privacy Day, an annual and worldwide effort to empower people to protect their privacy, both online and off\. In light of this, I ask you to take a moment to ensure your personal information stays private\.

## [January is Sexual Assault Awareness Month at UBC](https://broadcastemail.ubc.ca/2017/01/12/january-is-sexual-assault-awareness-month-at-ubc-2/)

January 12, 2017

Sexual Assault Awareness Month \(SAAM\) is held each year at UBC in January, and aims to raise awareness and understanding about the issues surrounding sexual assault\. Several events have been planned for the month, including **Denim Day on Wednesday, January 18**\. On this day, you can choose to wear denim or a Denim Day sticker to show your support for ending sexual assault on campus\.
