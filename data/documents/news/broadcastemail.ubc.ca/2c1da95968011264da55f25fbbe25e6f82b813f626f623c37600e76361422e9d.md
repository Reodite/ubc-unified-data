To: Students in Vancouver and the Okanagan

Later this week you will receive a link to a new survey, inviting you to share your experiences with diversity and inclusion on campus\. By sharing your opinions through the **Campus Climate Survey**, you’ll help us make decisions that will improve life for every student at UBC\.

Each one of you is unique, and together we make up one exceptional, diverse community\. That’s why we need input from every student on campus\. In the survey, we will ask you about your community, sense of belonging, culture, and campus experiences\.

When you complete the survey, you’ll also have the chance to enter a draw for one of three $100 Visa gift cards, and the grand prize draw for a $500 Visa gift card\.

Creating inclusive and positive spaces is everyone’s responsibility\. I am committed to campus initiatives supporting diversity – I encourage you to read more about this on my blog: [https://president\.ubc\.ca/blog/2018/03/12/campus\-climate\-survey\-2018/](https://president.ubc.ca/blog/2018/03/12/campus-climate-survey-2018/)\.

Help inspire a more welcoming UBC — because tomorrow is shaped by what we hear today\.

**Professor Santa J\. Ono**  
President and Vice\-Chancellor
