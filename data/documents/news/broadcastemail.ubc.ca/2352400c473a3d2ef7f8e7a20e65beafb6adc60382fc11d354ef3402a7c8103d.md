## [January 28 is Data Privacy Day\. Help keep your data secure](https://broadcastemail.ubc.ca/2017/01/25/january-28-is-data-privacy-day-help-keep-your-data-secure/)

January 25, 2017

As the use of mobile devices such as laptops, tablets, and smartphones increases, the need to be aware of the security risks to your data is also heightened\. In light of this, I ask you to take a moment to ensure your personal information stays private\.
