## [UBC’s Centennial](https://broadcastemail.ubc.ca/2015/05/14/ubcs-centennial/)

May 14, 2015

Over the coming days, as we do each year at this time, we will begin celebrating UBC’s newest graduating class with their families and loved ones\. At the same time, a new group of students and their families will arrive for our annual ‘Destination UBC’ orientation\. These events are always special, but this year is even more significant because it marks the last graduating class of UBC’s first 100 years and the first incoming class of UBC’s next 100 years\.
