I am pleased to share the University of British Columbia’s *Connected by Commitment* 2015\-2016 Annual Report\. The report communicates our progress each year through a mix of storytelling and metrics that highlight the achievements of our students, faculty, staff, and alumni as they support the nine commitments of the university’s strategic plan, *Place and Promise*\.

Shared stories connect us as a community, serve as inspiration, and act as a catalyst to ignite change\. As we look forward to the upcoming year please take some time to read the stories that marked UBC’s Centennial milestone\.

I wish to express special thanks to the members of the President’s Office, the Metrics Committee, and Communications &amp; Marketing who put this report together\.  
For the full Annual Report visit: [http://www\.annualreport\.ubc\.ca](http://message.broadcast.ubc.ca/email/Redirect.aspx?u=1736261&amp;lm=81326826&amp;r=821222&amp;qz=c3b358819143c87a163a29778ccc8c99)
