Flipping Stigma Action Group launches a line of products online to support further research\.

[Three t\-shirts with logos](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2023-11/Flipping%20Stigma%20Launch.jpg.webp?itok=60s91RbN)

November 20, 2023

British Columbia’s Flipping Stigma Action Group, the creators behind the internationally renowned Flipping Stigma Toolkit, launched its online store in November 2023 with a brand\-new line of products designed to challenge stereotypical thoughts and beliefs about “dementia\.”

The printed hoodies and t\-shirts celebrating life’s positives were co\-designed by people with lived experience to inform and inspire others\. The best part? All sales will go to funding research for improving the lives of people with dementia\.

[Hoodie with Tookit logo](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/unisex-premium-hoodie-white-front-64b9d8129a483.png)

### Support Dementia Research

Make a purchase at the online store or visit the Flipping Stigma on its Ear website\.
