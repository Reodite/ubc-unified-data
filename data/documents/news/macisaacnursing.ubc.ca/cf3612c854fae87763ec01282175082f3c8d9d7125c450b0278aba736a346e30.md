Congratulations to [Emily Chan](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/emily-chan "Emily Chan"), awarded the 2024 Dean's Staff Award for Excellence, Equity, Diversity, Inclusion and Indigeneity, and [Dr\. Bernie Garrett](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/bernie-garrett "Bernie Garrett"), awarded the Dean's Faculty Award for Service\. Dr\. James Olson, Dean of Applied Science, presented in total, nine awards to nine outstanding Applied Science Faculty and Staff members at the APSC Staff and Faculty Barbecue on June 18, 2024\.
