March 9, 2021

Congratulations to UBC School of Nursing assistant professors, [Dr\. Saima Hirani](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/saima-hirani) and [Dr\. Lillian Hung](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/lillian-hung), for their successful grants in the 2020 CIHR Fall competition:

**Short and Long term impact of COVID\-19 on mental health and well\-being of health workers in Pakistan**  
CIHR Project Grant  
UBC co\-PI: Hirani, Saima \(Nursing\)   
CIHR Funding: $302,175  
As COVID\-19 continues to spread, there is growing concern about its impact in low\-and middle\-income countries like Pakistan where the impact could be extreme given its fragile economy, limited healthcare resources, widespread poverty, gender inequality, and poor health outcomes\.

**'What Matters' \- A digital solution to support person\-centered care for people with dementia in care settings**   
Priority Announcement: Patient\-Oriented Research: Early\-Career Investigator   
UBC PI: Hung, Lillian \(Nursing\)   
CIHR Funding: $100,000  
The Priority Announcement funding for early career researcher\-led Patient\-Oriented Research will help launch development of a mobile app to reduce social isolation, support person\-centred care, and continuity of care when moving between hospitals and care homes\.
