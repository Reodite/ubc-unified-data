Congratulations to Dr\. Jillian Harding on earning her PhD in Nursing\! 

[Dr\. Jillian Harding](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2024-06/Harding%2C%20Jillian%20UBC_20210914_PJ_6338.jpg.webp?itok=E87evVuk)

July 1, 2024

Jillian's focus of study is to learn from Indigenous peoples about how cultural safety is enacted in health care\. Her ultimate goal is to support Nurse Practitioners in providing culturally safe care in specific health care situations\. Jillian continues to work in direct clinical practice as an NP in primary care, and is transitioning from Lecturer to Assistant Professor of Teaching in the summer of 2024\. 

We look forward to seeing all the incredible contributions Dr\. Harding will continue to make in nursing and healthcare education\.
