November 10, 2022

Kerry Marshall is pursuing a Doctor of Philosophy in Nursing under the guidance of research supervisors [Vicky Bungay](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/vicky-bungay) and [Lydia Wytenbroek](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/lydia-wytenbroek)\. Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies has highlighted Kerry in their "Meet Our Students" webpage\.

> The nursing program at UBC is highly regarded\. Many of the UBC faculty members had written articles that helped to shape my practice, research, and ways of the world\. It is an amazing privilege to work alongside people that have inspired me throughout the journey\.
