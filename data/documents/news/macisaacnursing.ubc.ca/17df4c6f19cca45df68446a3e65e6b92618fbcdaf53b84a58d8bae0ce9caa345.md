The Faculty of Applied Science hosted a research showcase in February for Dr\. Benoit\-Antoine Bacon, the President of UBC\.

[Kristen Haase takes selfie with President Bacon, Dean Olson and Bonnie Leung\.](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2025-03/APSC%20President%27s%20Day%202025%2002%2027.jpg.webp?itok=uOR8sgD3)

Kristen Haase takes a selfie with President Bacon, Dean Olson and PhD Student Bonnie Leung\.

March 18, 2025

This showcase demonstrated how engineers, planners, architects, and nurses are using specialized skills to create change that benefits people, places, and our planet\. Dr\. Elizabeth Saewyc attended with Ash Scott and Dr\. Kristen Haase and their  research teams\.

[Showcase attendees](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Research%20Showcase%20for%20UBCPrez.jpg)

Attendees at the 2025 APSC President's Day Showcase

[Ashley Scott](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/ashley-scott "Ashley Scott"), Associate Professor of Teaching, offered this information about his showcase:

> I showcased my research on the development and evaluation of an AI\-driven, virtual reality simulation designed to enhance nurse practitioner training\. The project focuses on usability, student perceptions, and its impact on learning, particularly in preparing students for clinical practice and OSCEs\.
> 
> The simulation integrates AI\-driven natural language processing to create realistic patient interactions, and provides students with dynamic feedback on their clinical decision\-making and consultation skills\. My work aims to explore how emerging technology can improve competency\-based training in nursing education\.

[Ash Scott discusses his project with the Dean and President](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/2025-02-26_PresidentVisit_SalmaAbuHijleh_restricted_178.jpeg)

Ashley Scott  explains his research to UBC President Bacon and Dean Olson

Dr\. [Kristen Haase](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/kristen-haase "Kristen Haase") and [Bonnie Leung](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/community/news/2022/bonnie-leung-cihr-hsi-phd-fellow "Bonnie Leung - CIHR HSI PhD Fellow") \(NP, PhD student, BC Cancer\) presented on the work that the [ACCESS Research Lab](https://accesslab.nursing.ubc.ca/) is doing on geriatric oncology services in Canada—specifically, the lack of clinics specializing in the care of older adults with cancer in Western Canada\. This is despite statistics showing that cancer is more common among older adults and that they are more likely to experience cognitive and functional challenges\.

**Top care priority:** Quality cancer care should not only occur during treatments – quality care should begin from diagnosis and continue throughout survivorship\.  
**Top Research priority:** Explore the role, potential benefit, and feasibility of geriatric assessment and co\-management by a healthcare team \(including nurses, physiotherapist, occupational therapist, pharmacist to name a few\)\.

**Knowledge to Action** –   
**Geriatric Oncology Clinic**: Bridging care gap by increasing access to geriatric medical services  
**Nurse Navigator:** Pilot project exploring the value of a nurse navigator dedicated to support older adults in cancer clinical trials  
**Nurse\-led Geriatric Screening and Management**: Pilot project on the integration of geriatric screening and management as part of routine oncology practice  
**Clinical Pharmacist\-Initiated Medication Deprescribing**: Pilot project on reducing the negative consequences of polypharmacy among older adults with cancer
