July 6, 2020

Krisztina Laszlo  MAS, Archivist at Rare Books and Special Collections \(RBSC\) at the UBC Library, recently blogged in celebration of nurses and health care professionals at UBC\.

In addition, Ms\. Laszlo details in the blog, the transfer of the extensive archives from the BC History of Nursing to the RBSC which is now being undertaken, although the process has been interrupted for a time\. Take a little walk through history and celebrate nursing\!
