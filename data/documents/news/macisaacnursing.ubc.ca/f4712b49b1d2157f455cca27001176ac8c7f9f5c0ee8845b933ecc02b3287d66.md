Natasha Prodan\-Bhalla is now a Fellow of the class of 2021 American Association of Nurse Practitioners\.

[Headshot of Natasha Prodan\-Bhalla](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/field/image/Prodan-Bhalla%20Natasha%20Twitter.png.webp?itok=WlaG-StB)

March 1, 2021

Please join us in congratulating Dr\. Natasha Prodan\-Bhalla, Adjunct Professor, who has been selected as a 2021 Fellow in the American Association of Nurse Practitioners \(AANP\)\. She will be inducted at the National Conference in June\. According to Minna Miller, Family Nurse Practitioner, Adjunct Professor, and Fellow of the American Association of Nurse Practitioners, Dr\. Prodan\-Bhalla is only the third Canadian NP to receive this recognition/honour\.

The AANP is the largest professional organization of NPs in the world with a membership in excess of 118,000, of whom only 874 have been inducted as Fellows\. The Fellows of the AANP impacts national and global health by engaging recognized nurse practitioner leaders who make outstanding contributions to clinical practice, research, education, or policy to enhance the AANP mission to advance the NP profession locally and globally\.

Congratulations Dr\. Prodan\-Bhalla\!
