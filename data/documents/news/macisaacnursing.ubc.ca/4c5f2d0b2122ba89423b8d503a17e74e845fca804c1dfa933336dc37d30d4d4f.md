[Dr\. Jennifer Baumbusch](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/jennifer-baumbusch "Jennifer Baumbusch") and [Dr\. Leah Lambert](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/leah-lambert "Leah Lambert") have been highlighted in Michael Smith Health Research BC's 2024\-25 Impact Report

[Cover art for MSHRBC Impact Report for 2024\-25](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2026-01/mshrbc-impact-report.png.webp?itok=saSHU_n0)

January 9, 2026

We're proud to share that Dr\. Baumbusch appears on page 4 and Dr\. Lambert appears on page 13 of the most recent MSHR BC Impact report, Research Matters\. Their inclusion alongside leading researchers underscores the significant influence UBC School of Nursing continues to have across British Columbia’s healthcare landscape\.

[MSHR BC Impact Report](https://healthresearchbc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Research-Matters-Health-Research-BC-Impact-Report-2024-25.pdf)
