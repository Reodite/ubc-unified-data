Congratulations to [Dr\. Lillian Hung](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/lillian-hung "Lillian Hung") who has been promoted to Associate Professor at the UBC Vancouver School of Nursing as of July 1, 2024\.

[Associate Professor Lillian Hung](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2024-04/Hung%2C-Lillian-cherry-blossoms.jpg.webp?itok=aXkbzjnC)

Associate Professor Lillian Hung

July 1, 2024

Lillian holds a Canada Research Chair in Senior Care and a Clinician Scientist at the Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute\. She is also the driving force behind the [IDEALab](https://idea.nursing.ubc.ca/), an inter\-generational community with shared values where a diverse community can create innovative solutions towards improving dementia and older people care\.

When the promotion announcement was made earlier this spring, Lillian posted on social media:

> Thrilled to celebrate my promotion to Associate Professor
> 
> Immense gratitude to my patient partners, students, mentors, and colleagues for their support\. Here’s to continuing our journey in advancing care for older adults\! 

We add our warm congratulations to those expressed by this caring community for this well\-deserved promotion\!
