Congratulations to [Dr\. Fuchsia Howard](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/fuchsia-howard), Associate Professor and [Dr\. Lillian Hung](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/lillian-hung), Assistant Professor, on receiving Michael Smith Health Research BC Collaborating and Convening awards\.
