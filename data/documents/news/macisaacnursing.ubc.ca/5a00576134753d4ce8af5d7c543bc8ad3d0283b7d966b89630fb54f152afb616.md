We extend our heartfelt congratulations to [Natalie Chambers](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/natalie-chambers "Natalie Chambers"), the School's manager of the Clinical Practice Placement Unit, who recently earned her Master of Business Administration while working full time and raising a young family\. 

[Natalie Chambers Headshot by Paul Joseph](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2025-07/1-natalie-chambers-by-paul-joseph-2024-0424.jpg.webp?itok=-M0akY8R)

Natalie Chambers, MSN, MBA

July 28, 2025

As if this achievement wasn't extraordinary enough, she was also selected as valedictorian of her class\. This honour is a testament to her perseverance and leadership\-\-as well as her extraordinary organisation skills\! She is an inspiration and we are proud to celebrate this milestone with her\.

Congratulations, Natalie\!
