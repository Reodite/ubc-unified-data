January 8, 2020

We are delighted to share the splendid news that one of our former star faculty will become the next president of SFU\! We are so proud that such a fabulous nurse leader and scholar will take the helm at one of BC’s research universities, bringing nursing’s values and her longstanding commitments to equity, diversity, inclusion and Indigenous truth and reconciliation to the broader academic community there\.

Please join us in congratulating Joy on this momentous next stage of her distinguished career\!
