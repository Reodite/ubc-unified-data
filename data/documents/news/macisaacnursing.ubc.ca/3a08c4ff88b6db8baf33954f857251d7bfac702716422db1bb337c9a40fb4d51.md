## Faculty of Applied Science Highlights three Rising Stars in Nursing

Joining [Leah Shin](https://apsc.ubc.ca/spotlight/student/leah-shin), who graduated with her BSN in the spring, the following November graduates reveal why they have been highlighted as this year's APSC Rising Stars in Nursing\.

[Dr\. Abdul\-Fatawu Abdulai](https://apsc.ubc.ca/spotlight/student/trauma-informed-design-changing-narrative-in-health-technology-development-0) \(PhD 2022\) \| Trauma\-informed design: Changing the Narrative in Health technology development

[Anais Merat\-Fournier](https://apsc.ubc.ca/spotlight/student/expert-registered-nurse-to-novice-nurse-practitioner-0) \(MN\-NP\-2022\) \| From expert Registered Nurse to novice Nurse Practitioner

[Olivia Maracle](https://apsc.ubc.ca/spotlight/student/be-brave-be-relevant-stay-relevant-0) \(MSN 2022\) \| BE Brave, BE Relevant, STAY Relevant
