March 18, 2021

Congratulations to **Dr\. Manon Ranger** on two top\-ranked BCCHRI Health Starts Catalyst grants:

-   Ranked \#1: The role of neuroinflammation in early life pain/sucrose\-induced neurodevelopment impairments will be awarded a Catalyst Grant of $40,000 CAD\.
-   Ranked \#2: This project also received $40k funding and is titled: Brain\-to\-brain synchrony in mother\-child and father\-child dyads: a novel biomarker of brain development\.  
    PI is Dr\. Guy Dumont from Engineering and Co\-PI Dr\. Naznin Virji\-Babul from Rehab\.
