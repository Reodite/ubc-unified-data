November 2, 2022

Congratulations to [Dr\. Saima Hirani](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/saima-hirani), Assistant Professor at UBC Nursing and 2023 winner of the Outstanding Woman Researcher in Mental Health Promotion from **Venus International Women Awards**\.

Each year since 2020, this award is conferred to a remarkable Woman Researcher/ Faculty Member for exceptional contribution to fundamental discoveries, new theories, or insights\.
