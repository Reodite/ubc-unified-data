Observing the methodology of a/r/tography, Kyra Philbert developed a drag\-burlesque performance as a component of her MSN thesis

[Kyra Philbert in performance as Nurse Angelique](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2024-06/Pointing.jpg.webp?itok=cCg8PBk0)

June 11, 2024

In [Nurse Angélique: revisioning French Catholic nursing history as an ethical intervention in contemporary Canadian nursing practice](http://click.nursing.svc.ubc.ca/click/chdx-2tnwko-drjys0-725vida2/) Kyra interrogates nursing history through a Black feminist intersectional lens with sexuality as a key vector of analysis\. She posits that the future of nursing benefits from being imagined beyond the current limits of what is considered “good” and “moral” nursing\. Kyra was co\-supervised by [Dr\. Lydia Wytenbroek](http://click.nursing.svc.ubc.ca/click/chdx-2tnwko-drjys1-725vida3/) and [Dr\. John Oliffe](http://click.nursing.svc.ubc.ca/click/chdx-2tnwko-drjys2-725vida4/)\.
