March 18, 2021

**UBC SUSTAINABILITY INITIATIVE GRANT FOR CLIMATE EDUCATION**

Congratulations to UBC Nursing Lecturer, **Raluca Radu**, for securing a UBC Sustainability Initiative Grant for $4,956 to support a Climate Education\-related project, Enriched learning through an interactive case\-based online module: Nursing 290 Health Impacts of Climate Change\.

Her co\-applicants are **Dr\. Fuchsia Howard** and **Dr\. Sally Thorne\.**
