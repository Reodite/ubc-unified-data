September 8, 2020

[Dr\. Fuchsia Howard](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/fuchsia-howard) has been awarded the prestigious 5\-year Michael Smith Foundation for Health Research Scholar Award*\.* Dr\. Howard's research program is in *Critical Illness Survivorship: Evidence to Design Patient\-Centred Interventions*, which aims to identify patient\-and family\-perspectives for designing healthcare services that meet the needs of the high percentage of ICU patients who are readmitted within a year with complex medical conditions\.

#### Two post\-doctoral fellows in the UBC School of Nursing received 3\-year MSFHR Research Trainee awards:

**Dr\. Alison Ezzat** \(supervisor [Dr\. Sabrina Wong](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/sabrina-wong)\) for *Using electronic medical record data and patient reported outcome measures to improve the early identification and management of knee osteoarthritis in Canadian primary care*

**Dr\. Sandra Marquis** \(supervisor [Dr\. Jennifer Baumbusch](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/jennifer-baumbusch)\) for *Using population\-level administrative data to study the health of youth with developmental disabilities transitioning to adult services*
