October 17, 2022

Congratulations\! On October 14, 2022, Dr\. Jennifer Baumbusch and Dr\. Wendy Hall were named among the third class of Fellows of the Canadian Academy of Nursing, joining the ranks of [other faculty](https://nursing.ubc.ca/about/our-people/faculty) who have been inducted into the Academy\.

"Established in 2019, the Academy is the first pan\-Canadian organization dedicated to identifying, educating, supporting, and celebrating nursing leaders across all the regulated categories and domains of practice\. Fellows are among the most accomplished nurses in our country and their successful nominations reflect careers of sustained impact across nursing and health systems\."
