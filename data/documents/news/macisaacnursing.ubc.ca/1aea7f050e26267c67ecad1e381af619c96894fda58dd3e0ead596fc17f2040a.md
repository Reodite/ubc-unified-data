The following nurse researchers are proud recipients of grants from the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada \(SSHRC\) announced July 21, 2026\.

[Headshots of John Oliffe, Lara Hudspith, Angela Russolillo, Kristen Haase, Scott Ramsay and Tonje Molyneux](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2026-07/2026-07-21-announcement-sshrc-grants.jpg.webp?itok=jKRFhz6_)

July 23, 2026

## Insight Grants

*Insight Grants support research excellence in the social sciences and humanities\. Funding is available to both emerging scholars and established scholars for research initiatives of two to five years\.*

[**John Oliffe**](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/john-oliffe "John Oliffe") **$328,814**   
***Understanding older men's spousal caregiving: A photovoice study***  
Co\-applicants: [Baumbusch, Jennifer L\.](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/jennifer-baumbusch "Jennifer Baumbusch") \(UBC\) and [Hung, Lillian L\.H\.](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/lillian-hung "Lillian Hung") \(UBC\)  
Collaborators: Chow, Janice; BC Ministry of Health; Dawes, Stacey; Family Caregivers of BC; Dawson, Lisa

---

## Insight Development Grants

*Insight Development Grants support research in its initial stages\. The grants enable the development of new research questions, as well as experimentation with new methods, theoretical approaches and/or ideas\. Funding is provided for short\-term research development projects of up to two years that are proposed by individuals or teams\.*

[**Lara Hudspith**](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/lara-hudspith "Lara Hudspith") **$84,427**  
***Advancing Wellbeing and Justice Outcomes for Victim\-Survivors of Sexual Assault: The Role of the Medical Forensic Exam***  
Co\-applicants: [Bungay, Vicky](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/vicky-bungay "Vicky Bungay") \(UBC\); Kassam, Shahin \(UBC\); Carter, Grace; Coventry University   
Collaborators: Early, Sheila; Hughes, Ellie\. 

[**Angela Russolillo**](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/angela-russolillo "Angela Russolillo") **$80,000**  
***Understanding Public Views on Drug Diversion and Drug Policy Acceptability in Canada***  
Co\-applicant: Kealy, David

[**Kristen Haase**](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/kristen-haase "Kristen Haase") **$95,860**  
***Understanding the social impact of knowledge mobilization among community\-dwelling older adults***

[**Scott Ramsay**](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/scott-ramsay "Scott Ramsay") **$78,000**  
***Unseen, Unpaid, Unending: An Institutional Ethnography of Family Labour in Pediatric Acquired Brain Injury***  
Co\-applicants: Phelan, Shanon; Dalhousie University; Schmidt, Julia \(UBC\)  
Collaborators: MacRae, Whitney and O'Connor, Dana

[**Tonje Molyneux**](https://wellstream.ca/about/team/) **$76,493**  
***Developing and Validating a Measure of Coaching Practices that Foster School Belonging Among Secondary School Student Athletes***  
Co\-applicants: Bredin, Shannon; Faulkner, Guy; Gadermann, Anne; [Jenkins, Emily](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/emily-jenkins "Emily Jenkins") \(all UBC\)  
Collaborators: Abney, Jordan; Smith, Annie
