[Dr\. Helen Brown](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/helen-brown "Helen Brown") and PhD student [Kelsey Timler](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/kelsey-timler "Kelsey Timler") were invited to witness a historical event on Wednesday, December 8, 2021\. They were present among the invited guests when President Santa Ono signed a Memorandum of Understanding between the University of British Columbia and the Tŝilhqot’in National Government\.

December 10, 2021

Representing the Tsilhqot’in Nation in the signing of the memorandum were:

Nits’ilʔin Joe Alphonse \- Tribal Chairman \(Tŝilhqot’in\)   
Nits’ilʔin Otis Guichon – Vice Chairman \(Tŝideldel\)  
Nits’ilʔin Francis Laceese – \(Tl’esqox\)  
Nits’ilʔin Troy Baptiste – \(ʔEsdilagh\)  
Nits’ilʔin Jimmy Lulua – \(Xeni Gwet’in\) and  
Nits’ilʔin Lennon Solomon \(Yuneŝit’in\)

"Because of our work with the Tsilhqot’in Nation," said Dr\. Brown, "Kelsey Timler and I were invited to witness the signing ceremony\. It was quite a powerful experience of decolonization and reconciliation within the research context\!" Please read more about Dr\. Brown's important work here:
