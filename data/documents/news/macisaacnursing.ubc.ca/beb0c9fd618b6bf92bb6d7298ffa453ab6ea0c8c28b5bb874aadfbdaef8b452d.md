We are sorry to say goodbye to [Amazing Alumni](https://nursing-alumni.sites.olt.ubc.ca/home-page/amazing-alumni-1950s/marilynn-j-wood-bsn-59/) Marnie Wood who died on September 5, 2025\. 

[Marnie Wood, Headshot](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2025-09/marilynnwood.jpg.webp?itok=YEG7eFCG)

September 10, 2025

A member of the Class of 1959, Marilynn "Marnie" Wood went on to have a high profile career, including Dean at the University of Alberta\. She was honoured with our Alumni Award of Distinction at our 2021 Gala\.
