MSN graduate \(2021\) Neda Hamzavi's thesis publication has achieved status among the top\-ten most cited papers published by *Nursing Inquiry* in 2023\. 

[Neda Hamzavi standing in front of a body of water](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2025-03/Neda%20Image.jpg.webp?itok=kOLaxrpu)

Neda Hamzavi as an MSN student in 2020\.

March 19, 2025

[Top Cited Article certification](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Top%20Cited%20Wily%20Article.png)

Top Cited Article Certification

Neda Hamzavi's paper, which she published with supervisor Dr\. [Helen Brown](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/helen-brown "Helen Brown"), is entitled ["Who has been here that looks like me?": A narrative inquiry into Black, Indigenous, and People of Color graduate nursing students' experiences of white academic spaces](http://click.email2.wiley.com/?qs=4eac67ee4b8c6d3a03344adac652d3b5df253cf5c4d5ef84f29df47a01898ddf3fc846501680535205903cc710cb3a8d8b44b586d3a06c0f45afe4c10ba83f09)\. 

In 2020, Neda's work was featured in an article for Touchpoints, linked below\.

We are thrilled that her thesis has achieved this recognition\. It is, as her supervisor has noted, "Quite an accomplishment\!"

[Touchpoints 2020 Article](https://blogs.ubc.ca/touchpoints/our-hopes-for-the-future-neda-hamzavi-racism-and-whiteness-in-nursing/)
