## Nursing Leader's Legacy Lives On

[Dr\. Tracy Truant](https://nursing-alumni.sites.olt.ubc.ca/home-page/amazing-alumni-1980s/tracy-truant-bsn88-msn98-phd18/) is featured in the [Penticton Herald](https://www.pentictonherald.ca/news/article_6d89ccca-b4fb-11ec-bdc5-c7bd5f459321.html?utm_medium=social&amp;amp;utm_source=twitter&amp;amp;utm_campaign=user-share), timed to coincide with Oncology Nurses Day in Canada\. See attached PDF proclaiming April 5, 2022 to be "Oncology Nurses Day" in BC\.

The Penticton Herald could not have chosen a better representative of the field than Tracy, whose tremendous work continues\. Tracy died of cancer on April 8, 2021\.
