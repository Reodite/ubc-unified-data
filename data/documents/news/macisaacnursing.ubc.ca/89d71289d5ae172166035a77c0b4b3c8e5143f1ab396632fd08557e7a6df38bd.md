## UBC Nursing Grad Student Research features in BC Cancer Research Report

Three nursing students, two of them UBC Graduate Students, are highlighted in the BC Cancer Research Report for 2021 \- see pages 20\-23\. These BC Cancer fellowships are among several other awards these amazing nurses have received and we are so proud they were selected for such a prominent feature in the Research Institute Report\.

Congratulations Jagbir Kaur \(PhD\), Heather Kilgore \(MSN\) and Amber Bourgeois \(UVic PhD co\-supervised by Leah Lambert\)\!
