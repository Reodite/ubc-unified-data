[Dr\. Rachel Ollivier](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/rachel-ollivier "Rachel Ollivier"), Adjunct Professor, has been named among the top 100 of Canada's Most Powerful Women, according to [the Women's Executive Network](https://wxnetwork.com/) \(WXN\)\.

[Rachel Ollivier headshot](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2024-10/ALP_Rachel_Ollivier_Final_3186.jpeg.webp?itok=or2QyNDm)

October 30, 2024

As a Nurse Practitioner at BC Women's, Dr\. Ollivier practices clinically in the areas of complex gynecology and women's cardiovascular health in addition to being a Full Member of the Women's Health Research Institute\. She is also a member of the core team responsible for developing the new Menopause &amp; Midlife Health Program at the BC Women's Hospital &amp; Health Centre and recently became a Menopause Society Certified Practitioner \(MSCP\)\. Dr\. Ollivier is passionate about bridging research and clinical practice in women’s health\. 

In addition to all of this as well as her volunteer work \- or likely because of it \- WXN has recognized her as one of five "RBC Future Launch Future Leaders" in 2024:

> Dr\. Rachel Ollivier is an accomplished clinician scientist, community leader and advocate for women’s and maternal health\. Her various activities in education, research, policy and program development, community service and clinical practice have made a profound and tangible impact on health outcomes in Canada and abroad\. She is also a dedicated volunteer, serving on several boards and committees in addition to volunteering as a workshop speaker, helping to bridge the gap in sexual and reproductive health\.
