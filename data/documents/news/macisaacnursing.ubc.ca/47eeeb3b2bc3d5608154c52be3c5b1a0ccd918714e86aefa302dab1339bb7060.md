The Canadian Institutes of Health Research has released its Spring 2023 list of Project Grants\.

[Elder Roberta and Fuchsia headshots against maple leaves](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2023-07/CIHR%20Project%20Grants%20Fuchsia%20and%20Elder%20Roberta_0.jpg.webp?itok=4rREn5tp)

Dr\. Elder Roberta Price and Dr\. A\. Fuchsia Howard

July 26, 2023

Among the 2023 Project Grants funded by the Canadian Institutes of Health Research \(CIHR\), the following projects feature colleagues from the School of Nursing\. Dr\. Elder Roberta Price co\-leads a project to elevate the stories of Indigenous women, and two projects include teams featuring the co\-leadership of Dr\. Fuchsia Howard: one with co\-investigators Leanne Currie and John Oliffe among others, and the other in partnership with the Department of Surgery\.
