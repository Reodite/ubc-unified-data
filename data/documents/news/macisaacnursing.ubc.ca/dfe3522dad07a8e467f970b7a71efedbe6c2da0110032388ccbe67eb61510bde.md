Dr\. Sandra Lauck inducted as a Fellow of the American Academy of Nursing\!

[Headshot of Sandra Lauck by Paul Joseph](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2023-10/Lauck%2C%20Sandra%20UBC_20210921_PJ_0786%20APSC%20back.jpg.webp?itok=qtuNiGsQ)

October 6, 2023

Congratulations to [Dr\. Sandra Lauck](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/sandra-lauck "Sandra Lauck") who on Saturday, October 7, will be inducted into the American Academy of Nursing's 2023 class of Fellows in this, the Academy's 50th Anniversary Year\. Sandra is recognized for her "substantial, sustained, and outstanding impact on health and health care\." Kenneth R\. White, PhD, RN, AGACNP, ACHPN, FACHE, FAAN, announced the honour noting that the Academy began with 36 Charter Fellows in 1973\. This year is the Academy's largest class representing " 40 states, the District of Columbia, and 13 countries\." 

Sandra's colleagues heartily congratulate her on this achievement\!
