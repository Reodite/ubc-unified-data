UBC Knowledge Exchange &amp; Mobilization Scholars Announced

February 21, 2023

The UBC Knowledge Exchange Unit is excited to announce its first cohort of [Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization \(KxM\) Scholars](https://kx.ubc.ca/news/november-11-2022/ubc-knowledge-exchange-and-mobilization-scholar-program): Drs\. Sandra Lauck \(UBC School of Nursing\); Barbara Lee \(UBC School of Social Work\); Jasmin Ma \(UBC School of Kinesiology\) and Michelle Stack \(UBC Learning Exchange\) **recognized** for their knowledge of KxM strategies and commitment to promoting KxM culture in their respective faculties\.
