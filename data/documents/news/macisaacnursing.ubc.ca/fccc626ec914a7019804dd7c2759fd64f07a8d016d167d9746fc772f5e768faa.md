Congratulations to Karen Wong, recent awardee of a CIHR Health System Impact Fellowship\.

[Karen Wong Award Announcement \(CIHR HSI Fellowship\)](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2024-12/K%20Wong%20Michael%20Smith%20Announcement.png.webp?itok=YOtGWYXf)

December 9, 2024

# Karen Wong

[Karen Lok Yi Wong](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/profile/karen-wong/) is a PhD candidate and sessional lecturer at [UBC School of Social Work](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/)\. Her fellowship is co\-supervised by UBC Nursing associate professor [Dr\. Lillian Hung](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/lillian-hung) and VCH Senior Learning Health Systems Lead Krisztina Vasarhelyi\. The award is co\-funded by the Canadian Institutes of Health Research \(CIHR\), Michael Smith Health Research BC \(MSHRBC\), and Vancouver Coast Health\. She is also a registered social worker in elder and dementia care and is currently practising with [Providence Healthcare](https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en)\. She received the Inspiring Social Worker of the Year in 2021 from the [BC Association of Social Workers](https://www.bcasw.org/) and the Distinguished Service Award in 2024 from the [Canadian Association of Social Workers](https://www.casw-acts.ca/en), acknowledging her contribution to clinical practice and policy advocacy in social work in aging\. She is affiliated with the [Innovation in Dementia and Aging \(IDEA\) Lab](https://idea.nursing.ubc.ca/), the [Centre for Research on Personhood in Dementia](https://www.crpid.ubc.ca/), UBC, and the [Science and Technology for Aging Research \(STAR\) Institute](https://www.sfu.ca/starinstitute.html), SFU\. Her research interests include healthcare and social service systems in aging, aging and diversity, and aging and technology\. Karen’s PhD study is funded by the [Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council \(SSHRC\)](https://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca/home-accueil-eng.aspx) doctoral award and [AGE\-WELL](https://agewell-nce.ca/) Michael F\. Harcourt Policy Fellowship\.
