UBC's Public Scholars Initiative \(PSI\) has announced 43 new doctoral students for the 2023/24 academic year, including nursing PhD student Sabina Staempfli\.

[Sabina Staempfli Headshot](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2023-09/Staempfli%2CSabina%202023%20feature.jpg.webp?itok=y-jOuPJM)

September 28, 2023

Congratulations to [Sabina Staempfli](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/meet-our-students/staempfli-sabina), PhD student at the School of Nursing, for being named among the Public Scholars Initiative's \(PSI\) ninth cohort of scholars\. Sabina's research topic is: "Long\-term care reform in action: A realist review and evaluation of care delivery to improve quality of life for Canadian long\-term care residents\."
