Congratulations to Dr\. Alison Phinney, Dr\. Angela Russolillo, and Dr\. Farinaz \(Naz\) Havaei, recipients of the Michael Smith Health Research BC Engage Award\!

[Headshots of Drs Phinney, Russolillo and Havaei](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_480w/public/announcement-images/2026-08/2026-08-05-announcement-phinney-russolillo-havaei.jpg.webp?itok=tHE1CjEi)

July 31, 2026

Among the 50 teams across BC receiving the Impact Pathways Program's Engage award, 19 are based at UBC, and three of those are led by School of Nursing faculty members\. The award is introduced this year under the Impact Pathways Program, a new initiative supporting collaborative, community\-connected health research across BC\.
