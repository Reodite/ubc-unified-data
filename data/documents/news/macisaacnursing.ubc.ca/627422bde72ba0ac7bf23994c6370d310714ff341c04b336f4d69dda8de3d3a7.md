November 9, 2022

UBC\-Vancouver School of Nursing is piloting a project to support up to five Indigenous RNs in BC who want to experience a graduate course in nursing as a step to applying to a graduate program\.

Indigenous RNs who participate in the pilot will take ONE ONLINE course in Nursing Leadership \(January 2023\-April 2023\)\. Tuition will be covered, and an Indigenous nursing tutor/mentor will provide weekly support for the course\.

If you are interested in applying, please attend the information session via Zoom on Monday, Nov 14th at 4 pm and complete this survey by Monday, Nov 21, 2022\.
