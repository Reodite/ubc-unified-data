Ife Adebara \(left\) and Nicole Ebbutt

Two students have been honored with the Edna Dharmaratne Award: Nicole Ebbutt, an undergrad in speech sciences, and Ife Adebara, a Ph\.D\. student in linguistics\. Congratulations to both\! The award honors students with a commitment to volunteerism\. Two awards were granted this year, exceptionally, in light of the especially deserving candidate pool and the difficulties COVID\-19 has imposed\.

The [Dharmaratne Award](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/undergraduate/funding/the-edna-dharmaratne-award-in-linguistics/) is named after the linguistics department’s former head administrator, who served in that capacity from 1997 to 2017\. [Donations to the fund](https://support.ubc.ca/projects/edna-dharmaratne-award-linguistics/) are always appreciated and especially welcome this year\.
