Join UBC SALSA this summer in their “Fun with Language” camps\! The camp is aimed for children age 5\-10\. Each day will have a different focus with language\-specific instruction and game\-play situations making for a stimulating and challenging learning environment, as well as promoting the use of particular aspects of language, such as pronunciation, comprehension, and social use\. For more information, see [http://camps\.ubc\.ca/summer\-camps/fun\-with\-language/](http://camps.ubc.ca/summer-camps/fun-with-language/)\.
