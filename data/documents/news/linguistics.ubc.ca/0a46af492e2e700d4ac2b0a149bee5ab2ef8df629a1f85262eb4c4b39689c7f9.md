Congratulations are due to Linguistics PhD student [Zoe Wai\-Man Lam](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/persons/zoe-lam/ "Zoe Lam"), who won both first place AND the people’s choice award at the UBC\-wide “Three\-Minute Thesis” competition for her dissertation, “Perception of Lexical Tones by Heritage Speakers of Cantonese\.” Her co\-supervisors are [Dr\. Douglas Pulleyblank](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/persons/douglas-pulleyblank/ "Douglas Pulleyblank") and [Dr\. Kathleen Currie Hall](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/persons/kathleen-hall/ "Kathleen Hall")\. Zoe was in fact the only Humanities student in the final round\! You can see her [winning performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2LzBE9IxJk) here\. She will be going on to compete at the regional level in April; details are here: [http://3mt\.grad\.ubc\.ca/news/final\-results\-ubc\-3mt\-2015/](http://3mt.grad.ubc.ca/news/final-results-ubc-3mt-2015/)\. Congratulations and good luck\!
