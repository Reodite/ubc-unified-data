We sadly mourn the passing of Joanne Francis\. Joanne was our Field Methods consultant for ʔayʔaǰuθəm \(Comox\-Sliammon\) in 2016\-17 and has contributed greatly to our work on the language since then \(many of our students have worked with her extensively\)\. Joanne will be dearly missed\.
