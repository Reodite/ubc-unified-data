On Monday April 28th, the Department threw a surprise retirement party for Doug Pulleyblank, who will be retiring in June 2025 after 34 years as a UBC Linguistics faculty member\.

Five linguists and family smiling

Anne\-Marie, Kathleen, Doug, Diana and Samuel

In addition to surprise guest appearances including Diane Archangeli, and many friends, former students, colleagues and family members, it also served as the official reveal of a surprise festschrift and website for Doug, affectionately titled [**Dougschrift**](https://arts-pulleyblank-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/)\! The site includes [20\+ squibs, puzzles and studies](https://arts-pulleyblank-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/dougschrift/) in Doug’s honour, as well as a slew of [Appreciations](https://arts-pulleyblank-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/appreciations/) with reminiscences and congratulations from linguists, friends and family world\-wide, and a [biographical sketch](https://arts-pulleyblank-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/a-biography-of-doug-pulleyblank/) of Doug’s remarkable life and career\.

Fancy white sheetcake with Yoruba icing text

The caption, in Yoruba, refers to one who knows languages better than the speakers themselves\.

Thanks to the whole team who built the website, arranged the party, co\-wrote the biography, and miraculously *kept the surprise a secret* over months of planning – including Kathleen Currie Hall, Gunnar Hansson, Anne\-Mare Comte, Diana Archangeli, Larry Hyman, Carla Hudson Kam and Anne\-Michelle Tessier\.

*Félicitations,* Doug\! We know we have not seen the last of you\.
