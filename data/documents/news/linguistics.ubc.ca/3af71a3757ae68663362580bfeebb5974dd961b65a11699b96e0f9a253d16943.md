Starting in the 2026/2027 academic year, the UBC Linguistics department will be granting a new annual award, the **International Mother Language Day Award in Linguistics\.** This yearly award of $2000, made possible through a gift from [Mother Language Lovers of the World Society](https://motherlanguagelovers.com/ "https://motherlanguagelovers.com/"), will be awarded to graduate students in the Department of Linguistics who have demonstrated an interest in mother languages and community involvement\.
