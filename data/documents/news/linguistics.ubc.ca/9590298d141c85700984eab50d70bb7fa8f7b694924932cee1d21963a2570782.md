Doctoral candidate [Kate Zhou](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/profile/yangshuying-zhou/) has received a Public Scholars Initiative grant, and doctoral candidate [Ana Arrieta\-Zamudio](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/profile/ana-laura-arrieta-zamudio/) has received a renewal of her PSI grant\. The [Public Scholars Initiative](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/psi) is intended “to enable doctoral students to make purposeful contributions to the public good through collaborative, action\-oriented, and/or creative forms of scholarship\.” Ana’s project received [an extended public write\-up](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/meet-our-students/arrieta-zamudio-ana-laura) on the PSI site\.

Congratulations, both\!
