UBC’s new director of the FNLG program, Mark Turin, will be hosting a two\-part BBC Radio 4 series, “On Language Location,” starting next week\. The episodes cover the languages of Bhutan \(Monday 20 October\) and Burma/Myanmar \(Monday 27 October\)\.  
  
The podcasts will be available online after they are broadcast through the series website:
