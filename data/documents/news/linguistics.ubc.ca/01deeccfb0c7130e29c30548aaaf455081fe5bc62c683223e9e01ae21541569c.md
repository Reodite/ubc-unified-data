Congratulations to Dr\. Patrick Littell, whose 2016 dissertation “Focus, predication, and polarity in Kwak’wala” \(supervised by [Dr\. Hotze Rullmann](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/person/hotze-rullmann/)\) has been chosen by UBC as a finalist for the national competition for the Canadian Association of Graduate Studies’ Distinguished Dissertation Award\.
