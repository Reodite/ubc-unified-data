The [2019 Language Sciences Undergraduate Research Conference](https://blogs.ubc.ca/lsurc/) \(LSURC\) was a resounding success\! We offer a big THANK YOU to the organizers, led by conference co\-chairs Ashley Chand &amp; Paris Gappmayr; to the presenters and their mentors; to the judges; and to the audience members\. Special congratulations go to the winners of the Best Oral and Poster Presentation Awards:

Best Oral Presentation Award:

Yurika Aonuki, *Phonology of Adjective Intensification in American Sign Language*

Best Poster Presentation Award:

Gracellia Purnomo, *Biomechanical Constraints on Signed Languages: Repetitive Motion in Two\-Handed Alternating Signs*
