Recent PhD graduate Heather Bliss has been making headlines in November\. She was honoured in Ottawa for being [a finalist for the SSHRC Talent Awards](http://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca/news_room-salle_de_presse/latest_news-nouvelles_recentes/impact_awards_2014_shortlists-prix_impacts_2014_finalistes-eng.aspx) and is featured in the UBC news for [her work on saving endangered languages](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/about-us/news/new-ubc-phd-helps-save-endangered-language)\. Congratulations to Heather and to her supervisor, Dr\. [Martina Wiltschko](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/persons/martina-wiltschko/ "Martina Wiltschko")\!
