The prime minister recently raised eyebrows by referring to ‘speaking moistly’ during a coronavirus press briefing\. [Bryan Gick](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/person/bryan-gick/) discussed what this might mean from a scientific perspective on CBC radio, and participated in [this brief Q&amp;A](https://news.ubc.ca/2020/04/17/why-speaking-moistly-needs-to-be-taken-seriously/) on the topic\. It’s not at all dry, in the best possible sense—and, among other things, raises the interesting question of whether languages might vary in their relative ‘moistness’\.
