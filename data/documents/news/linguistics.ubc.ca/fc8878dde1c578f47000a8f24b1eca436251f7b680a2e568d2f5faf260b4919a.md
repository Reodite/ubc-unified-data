Three grad students have been recognized as UBC Public Scholars\. [The Public Scholars Initiative](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/psi) supports doctoral students “make purposeful contributions to the public good through collaborative, action\-oriented, and/or creative forms of scholarship\.” They are Anne Bertrand, Roger Yu\-Hsiang Lo, and Michael Schwan\.

Anne’s project is called ‘The grammar of scalarity in Ktunaxa’ and is supervised by Martina Wiltschko\. Roger’s is “From annotation to assessment: Tools for sign language phonetic and phonological research” and is supervised by Kathleen Currie Hall\. Michael’s is “Gitksan Neologism Formation: The Future of Gitksan With Roots in the Past” and is supervised by Henry Davis\.

Congratulations all around\!
