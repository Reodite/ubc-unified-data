The UBC Occasional Papers in Linguistics is very pleased to announce the publication of Sqwéqwel’ Múta7 Sptakwlh, a volume of 18 narratives by Carl Alexander in St’át’imcets \(Lillooet Salish\), with accompanying English translation and interlinear gloss\. The volume is now available for purchase and download: the \.pdf and accompanying audio files may be downloaded without cost, or you may order a print volume for $27 CAD plus shipping\.

The Sqwéqwel’ Múta7 Sptakwlh volume is hosted on our newly\-launched website, [https://lingpapers\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/alexander\-stories\-collection/](https://lingpapers.sites.olt.ubc.ca/alexander-stories-collection/)\. Please also visit our new website to find archives of the ICSNL and WSCLA proceedings – remember to update your bookmarks to the new site\!
