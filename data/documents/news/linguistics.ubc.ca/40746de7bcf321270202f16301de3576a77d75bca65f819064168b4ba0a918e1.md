Kevin Yan, a high school student who participated in UBC [NACLO](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/news-events/recurring-events/north-american-computational-linguistics-open-competition/) in February 2022 has won a bronze medal in the International Linguistics Olympiad\.

Congratulations, Kevin on this great achievement\!

Also, thanks to Kaili Vesik and and Yadong Liu who took the lead as this year’s local organizers of the first round\.
