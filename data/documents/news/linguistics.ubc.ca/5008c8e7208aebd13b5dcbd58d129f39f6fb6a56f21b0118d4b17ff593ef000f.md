Congratulations to Michel Assis Navarro, who has been awarded the **UBC Dissertation Prize** in the category of Fine Arts, Humanities, and Social Sciences for his doctoral dissertation, *“Quantization and Quantification: Countability and Scope of Bare Nouns in Kaingang and Beyond\.”* This prestigious award recognizes outstanding graduate research at UBC\.

As the recipient of this prize, Michel has also been nominated by UBC for the **national CAGS/ProQuest Distinguished Dissertation Award**\.

More information about these awards is available on the [UBC Dissertation Prize](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/ubc-dissertation-prize) and [CAGS/ProQuest Award](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/cagsproquest-distinguished-dissertation-awards) pages\.

Michel’s award\-winning dissertation can be accessed [here](https://open.library.ubc.ca/soa/cIRcle/collections/ubctheses/24/items/1.0445214?o=0)\.
