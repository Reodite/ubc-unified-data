The *2015 Annual Meeting on Phonology* \(AMP 2015\) will in be held in Vancouver on October 9–11, 2015, jointly organized by the [University of British Columbia](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/) and [Simon Fraser University](http://www.sfu.ca/linguistics.html)\. The conference will take place at the [UBC Robson Square](http://robsonsquare.ubc.ca/) facility in downtown Vancouver\.

**Invited speakers:**  
[Ryan Bennett](http://ling.yale.edu/people/ryan-bennett) \(Yale University\)  
[Ellen Kaisse](https://linguistics.washington.edu/people/ellen-kaisse) \(University of Washington\)  
[Kie Zuraw](http://www.linguistics.ucla.edu/people/zuraw/) \(University of California, Los Angeles\)

For more information, please see [http://blogs\.ubc\.ca/amp2015/](http://blogs.ubc.ca/amp2015/)\.
