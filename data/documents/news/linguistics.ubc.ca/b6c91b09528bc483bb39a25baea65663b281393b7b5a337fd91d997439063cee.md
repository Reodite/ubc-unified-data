The Department of Linguistics warmly congratulates **Starr Sandoval**, who successfully defended her PhD dissertation on **August 14, 2026**\.

Her dissertation is titled:

> *“The Semantics of Adjective Dimensions: Quality adjectives as a window onto subsectivity, kinds, as phrases, and how questions”*

Dr\. Sandoval’s research was supervised by **Marcin Morzycki**, with committee members **Ryan Bochnak** and **Hotze Rullmann**\. Special thanks also go to **Lisa Matthewson** for her support and involvement during the defence\.

Congratulations, Dr\. Sandoval, on this remarkable milestone\! We wish you all the best in your future research and career\.
