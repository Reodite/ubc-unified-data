[Gloria Mellesmeon](https://www.mellesmoen.ca/ "https://www.mellesmoen.ca/") \(Ph\.D, 2025\) has accepted a three\-year position as Lecturer in Historical Linguistics at Yale University\. It starts in the fall\. She will also hold a secondary appointment as an Associate Research Scientist\.

Congratulations, Gloria\!
