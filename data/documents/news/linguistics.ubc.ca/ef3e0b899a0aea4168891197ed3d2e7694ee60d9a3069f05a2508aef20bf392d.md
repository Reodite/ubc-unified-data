In June 2025, the MDS Computational Linguistics program is saying goodbye to our lecturer Dr\. Jungyeul Park\. Jungyeul joined the MDS\-CL program in 2021 with a Ph\.D\. in Linguistics from the Université Paris Diderot \(Paris VII\)\. Over the past four years Jungyeul has played many roles: he taught LING 242: “Computational Tools for Linguistic Analysis”, as well as all of the program’s course labs; he developed computational linguistics curriculum; he has also published multiple papers on Korean morphology\. In addition, Jungyeul has been a popular mentor for the program’s final Capstone projects\.

As part of his farewell, the MDS\-CL program presented Jungyeul with a book of thanks and appreciation from present and past MDS\-CL members\. The book’s contributions especially highlighted Jungyuel’s engaging teaching style, supportiveness, and the joy he brought to each class\.

UBC Linguistics and the MDS\-CL program wish Jungyeul all the very best\!
