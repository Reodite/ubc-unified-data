As a way to boost morale during isolation, the LING 314 \(Instrumental Phonetics\) class decided to have an online potluck party, but, as they couldn’t actually share food, they decided to share recipes instead\. The result was [this coronavirus cookbook](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/27/2020/04/Covid-Cookbook-final.pdf) which everyone is welcome to enjoy\. You can even print it off \(in booklet format\) and fold it to make a physical cookbook\!

NB: Coronavirus is not required to make the dishes\.
