Doctoral program alumnus John Lyon has accepted an assistant professor position at UBC Okanagan in Indigenous Studies in the [Department of Community, Culture and Global Studies](https://ccgs.ok.ubc.ca/undergraduate/indigenous-studies/)\. His [2013 dissertation](https://open.library.ubc.ca/cIRcle/collections/ubctheses/24/items/1.0165734) was on the syntax and semantics of Okanagan Salish and was supervised by Henry Davis \(chair\), Lisa Matthewson, and Hotze Rullmann\. This means John will be well\-located to continue working on Salish\. Henry describes this as a significant development for Salish linguistics more broadly, so congratulations are due to John, to his committee, and to Salish linguistics\.
