The Linguistics Department is offering a Swahili language course in Winter 2020 Term 2\. The course code is ASTU 204T\.

The course is an introduction to the essentials of Swahili sound systems, vocabulary, expressions, grammar and cultural life\. There will be a focus on listening, reading, speaking and writing\. There are no prerequisites for this course, though it is not available for students with any prior knowledge of the language\.
