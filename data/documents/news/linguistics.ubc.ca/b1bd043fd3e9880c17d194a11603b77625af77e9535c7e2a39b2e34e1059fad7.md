We’re excited to announce that the festschrift in honour of [Henry Davis](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/person/henry-davis/)‘ 60th birthday has been released\! “Wa7 xweysás i nqwal’úttensa i ucwalmícwa: He loves the people’s languages” is a collection of essays inspired by the research and mentorship of Henry Davis, edited by [Lisa Matthewson](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/person/lisa-matthewson/), [Erin Guntly](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/person/erin-guntly/), [Marianne Huijsmans](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/person/marianne-huijsmans/), and [Michael Rochemont](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/person/michael-rochemont/)\. Much of the research is based on original fieldwork with many contributions focused on endangered languages of North America\. The scope of the book is wide\-ranging, covering topics from many subfields of linguistics, including syntax, morphology, phonology, phonetics, historical linguistics, acquisition, and narratives from the Pacific Northwest\. To learn more and / or to order a copy, please see [https://lingpapers\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/opl\-volumes/wa7\-xweysas\-i\-nqwaluttensa\-i\-ucwalmicwa\-he\-loves\-the\-peoples\-languages/](https://lingpapers.sites.olt.ubc.ca/opl-volumes/wa7-xweysas-i-nqwaluttensa-i-ucwalmicwa-he-loves-the-peoples-languages/)
