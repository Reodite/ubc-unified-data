We have big news\!

One of the 2021 MDS\-CL cohort student capstone projects \(completed in May\-June 2022\) was recently featured in an article by NASA\! Check out the main story here: [https://appliedsciences\.nasa\.gov/our\-impact/story/university\-students\-use\-reddit\-help\-nasa\-find\-landslides](https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/our-impact/story/university-students-use-reddit-help-nasa-find-landslides)

Image

Image credit: [NASA](https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/our-impact/story/university-students-use-reddit-help-nasa-find-landslides)

In case you are curious, the MDS\-CL program is an intensive 10\-month professional program, and the last course of the program is a 2\-month \(6 credit\) project in which students work with an industry partner on a language related problem they would like to solve\. One of the 2021 cohort groups, who worked on and completed their capstone projects in May\-June 2022, worked on a project titled “Social Landslides: Improve NASA Global Landslide Catalog with Automated Social Media Mining” \. This project was working with BGC Engineering Inc\., an earth science consulting company with a strong interest in data science\-driven engineering practices, and in collaboration with NASA data\.
