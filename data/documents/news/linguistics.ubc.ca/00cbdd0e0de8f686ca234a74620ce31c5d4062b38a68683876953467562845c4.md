The Department of Linguistics will be co\-hosting the International Conference on Salish and Neighbouring Languages \(ICSNL\) with the Simon Fraser University First Nations Language Centre from August 5\-7, 2015\. It is the 50th anniversary of the conference, and we already have an outstanding line\-up of invited speakers who have all made important contributions to the field and to past conferences\. More info at: [http://icsnl\.org/](http://icsnl.org/)
