Students in the Masters of Data Science in Computational Linguistics program presented their capstone projects on June 25\. Each group wrote a blog post describing their research:

-   [Cancer Rehab](https://medium.com/@komalpbrar123/taking-on-the-challenge-what-we-learned-building-a-chatbot-for-people-with-cancer-5cc4d4a6778e?postPublishedType=repub) \(motivational chatbots to encourage recovery during Cancer rehabilitation\)
-   [CGI](https://medium.com/@kanfreya/what-our-first-real-data-project-taught-us-about-messy-data-and-honest-tools-aa46dbbbb154) \(workload and expertise\-informed personnel management\)
-   [Ednoda](https://medium.com/@shiaoligreen/curriculum-aligned-esl-sentence-recommendations-dc076665c520) \(discovery of language\-learning examples that match learner expertise\)
-   [Jeppesen](https://medium.com/@yickvincent/the-radio-never-stops-how-ai-assistance-can-help-with-air-traffic-control-atc-communication-6ce70635edb7) \(knowledge distillation from myriad sources into easy\-to\-read, accurate summaries\)
-   [SUEDE](https://medium.com/@christinajenmccallum/s-u-e-sexuality-understanding-education-a-chatbot-s-u-e-table-for-all-ages-c647dc859ed4) \(educational assistant for age\-appropriate, expert\-vetted sexual health education\)[https://medium\.com/@christinajenmccallum/s\-u\-e\-sexuality\-understanding\-education\-a\-chatbot\-s\-u\-e\-table\-for\-all\-ages\-c647dc859ed4](https://medium.com/@christinajenmccallum/s-u-e-sexuality-understanding-education-a-chatbot-s-u-e-table-for-all-ages-c647dc859ed4)
-   [UBC Psych](https://medium.com/@vencywang091/what-if-you-could-just-call-an-ai-for-help-debb15b1982e) \(fast, accurate AI for texting and mental health\)
