We are delighted to share that Kaili Vesik and Kathleen Hall have published a paper titled **“Improved Student Learning through Active Retrieval Practice and Random\-Sampled Exams”** in the *Canadian Journal of Linguistics / Revue canadienne de linguistique*\.

The paper explores the benefits of active, effortful retrieval practice in effective learning\. The authors:

-   Provide a framework for fostering active study habits through practice and assessment\.
-   Share resources to implement this framework in linguistics classrooms\.
-   Evaluate its success in an introductory phonetics/phonology course\.

Access the open\-access article here: [https://doi\.org/10\.1017/cnj\.2024\.24](https://doi.org/10.1017/cnj.2024.24)\.

Congratulations to Kaili and Kathleen on this impactful contribution\!
