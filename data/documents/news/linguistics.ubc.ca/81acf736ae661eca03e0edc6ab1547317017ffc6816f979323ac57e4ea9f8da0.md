Congratulations to our current department head, [Dr\. Bryan Gick](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/person/bryan-gick/), on being selected as a [2017 Guggenheim Fellow](http://www.gf.org/fellows/all-fellows/bryan-gick/)\! “As a Guggenheim Fellow, Dr\. Gick will work with top interdisciplinary experts developing and extending his body\-based approach to speech, with the goal of deepening links between biomechanics, the nervous system, the digestive system and the evolution of speech communication\.”
