In keeping with the Faculty of Art’s lead and guidelines, staff in the Linguistics Department are now working remotely, but they are still available to assist you\. Please do not hesitate to contact them\.

Please see [**ubc\.ca**](https://www.ubc.ca/) for the most up\-to\-date information about UBC’s response to COVID\-19, and keep an eye out for emails as we explore and assess new ways to stay connected during this challenging time\.

In the meantime, here are some useful links of support resources:

-   [Tips for When the News Stresses You Out](https://foundrybc.ca/stories/tips-for-when-the-news-stresses-you-out/)
