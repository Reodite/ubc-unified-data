Congratulatons to Dr\. Molly Babel on her promotion to the rank of Associate Professor, with tenure, effective July 1, 2015\.

Molly began her appointment at UBC as an Assistant Professor in July, 2009\. She came to us with an M\.A\. and Ph\.D\. in Linguistics from the University of California, Berkeley \(2006 and 2009 respectively\), and a B\.A\. in Linguistics, Anthropology and Spanish Studies from the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities \(2004\)\. We all know Molly as a dynamic contributor to the Department, a dedicated teacher, and already a prominent member of the field\.
