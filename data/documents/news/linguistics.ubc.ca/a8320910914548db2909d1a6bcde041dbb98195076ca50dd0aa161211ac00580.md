Paris Gappmayr, linguistics undergrad extraordinaire, has advanced to the Top 10 Finals of the [Undergraduate 3 Minute Thesis Competition](https://ubc.3mt.ca/)\. This is a new competition funded by the [PURE award](https://research.ubc.ca/about-vpri/program-undergraduate-research-experience-call-proposals/pure-funding-recipients#9) that falls under UBC’s Strategic Plan\. Congratulations, Paris\!

You can watch her [successful presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLmg4D8W3MI&amp;feature=youtu.be) on YouTube\. Better still, you can and should attend the final round of the competition on [Feb\. 15](https://ubc.3mt.ca/) \(a Saturday\) to cheer her on\.
