SSHRC has just [released the names of its top three finalists for the Impact Talent Award](http://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca/news_room-salle_de_presse/latest_news-nouvelles_recentes/impact_awards_2014_shortlists-prix_impacts_2014_finalistes-eng.aspx), and recent UBC Linguistics alum [Heather Bliss](https://linguistics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/persons/heather-bliss/ "Heather Bliss") is one of them\! This is an outstanding achievement — congratulations, Heather\! “The annual Impact Awards recognize the highest achievements from outstanding researchers, students and research partners in social sciences and humanities research, research training, knowledge mobilization and scholarship funded partially or completely by SSHRC (Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council)\.“
