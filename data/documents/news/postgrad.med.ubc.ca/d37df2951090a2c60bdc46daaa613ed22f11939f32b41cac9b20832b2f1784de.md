### **Transition Into Practice**

The [Transition into Practice \(TIP\) program](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/transition-into-practice/ "https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/transition-into-practice/") prepares all residents for their transition into practicing independently\. We host our [Transition Tuesday Evening Seminar Series](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/transition-into-practice/events/ "https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/transition-into-practice/events/") regularly at 6:00 PM to support self\-directed learning on topics such as financial literacy, debt management, tax planning, and more\.

**[Understanding the Agreements and Contractual Terms Relevant for Newly Practicing Physicians](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0qXXPEN6azi4Ebs)  
**August 12, 2025 \| 6:00\-7:00PM PST

The session will provide a general introduction to contracts and contractual terms relevant to new physicians\. The focus of this session will be on the terms of contracts using the examples of different types of locum contracts and other contractual agreements\.

**[The LFP Payment Model 101 – How to get Started](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_8qe3yhAk9BbYpzU)  
**August 19, 2025 \| 6:00\-7:00 PM PST

This session focuses on learning about the LFP Payment Model as a compensation option for longitudinal family physicians and locums\.

**[Show Me The Money: The Business Behind Medical Clinics](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_4MFPs2GKDxijAV0)  
**August 26, 2025 \| 6:00\-7:00 PM PST

This session explores how community clinics operate\. It aims to give physicians insight into behind\-the\-scenes operations at their future workplaces to enhance transparency and understanding\.

### [Residents as Teachers](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/LOGO-ResidentsAsTeachers@2x-480x214-1-300x134.png)Residents as Teachers

Elevate your ability to teach, inspire, and lead\. Residents are invited to attend [Residents as Teachers \(RaT\)](https://facdev.med.ubc.ca/events/#rat) workshops that prepare you to enhance your teaching moments\. All residents are welcome to attend\.

**[Patient Education](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0PtGocJRhzU6JRY)  
**September 22 \| 12:00\-1:30pm PST \| Facilitators: Drs\. Adrian Yee and Kim Papp

In this session, participants will learn to highlight the role of patient education in care and teaching, communicate clearly, engage empathetically, and use the Teach Back method to ensure understanding and support treatment plans\.

**[Designing presentations for learning](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_88QHHNa93QwjPam)  
**October 21 \| 3:30\-5:00pm PST \| Facilitators: Dave Sanders, and Drs\. Michelle Schneeweiss, and Tony Fang

Participants will learn to design interactive presentations using backward design, apply multimedia learning principles to reduce cognitive overload, and create inclusive, supportive learning environments through thoughtful language and visuals\.

**[Teaching clinical reasoning with collaborative questioning](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6zLLncGc58nZolU)  
**November 19 \| 12:00\- 1:30pm PST \| Facilitators: Drs\. Kyla Hildebrand, Tony Fang, and Shandel Riedlinger

This session will equip participants to map the diagnostic reasoning process, identify clinical reasoning types, and implement the “Collect\-Generate\-Prune” method\.

### Continuing Professional Development

[UBC Continuing Professional Development](https://ubccpd.ca/) \(UBC CPD\) designs, delivers, accredits, researches and evaluates lifelong learning opportunities for health professionals in British Columbia and beyond\. They have many [events suitable for residents](https://ubccpd.ca/learn/learning-activities) on their website\.

[**Surgical First Assist in Obstetrics**](https://ubccpd.ca/learn/learning-activities/course?eventtemplate=1009-surgical-first-assist-in-obstetrics-vancouver)   
October 25 \| Vancouver, BC

This course was developed to provide training in a Canadian context for entry level skills for the surgical assist with a focus on obstetrics\. It is appropriate for Midwives, Family Physicians, Physician Assistants and Residents\.

[**Practice Foundations: Achieving Success in Your First Years of Practice**](https://ubccpd.ca/learn/learning-activities/course?eventtemplate=931-practice-foundations-achieving-success-in-your-first-years-of-practice)  
November 29 \| UBC Robson Square \| Vancouver, BC \|In\-person Conference  
Audience: family physicians, specialty physicians, nurse practitioners, international medical graduates, residents and students\.

Join this dynamic, in\-person conference packed with practical insights from experienced professionals currently in the field\. Explore a wide range of timely topics including job opportunities, work–life balance, practice management, college complaints, billing strategies, navigating Pathways, and more\. Don’t miss the chance to connect with colleagues and potential employers at our networking reception and job fair\.
