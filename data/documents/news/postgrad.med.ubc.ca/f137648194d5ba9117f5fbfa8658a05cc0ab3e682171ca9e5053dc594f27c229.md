**13th Annual UBC Resident Talent Night – Sept 17, 2020**

We are excited to invite you to attend the [https://www\.youtube\.com/watch?v\=AokqnSm2NlU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AokqnSm2NlU)[13th Annual Resident Talent Night](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AokqnSm2NlU) on **Thursday, September 17th, 2020\!**

[Resident Talent Night](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/resident-talent-night/) celebrates the resident community’s many talents beyond medicine\. The event started off as an informal gathering among internal medicine residents in 2007 and has grown year\-after\-year to now boast a diverse set of performers across many residency programs, including; musicians, spoken word poets, singer\-songwriters, dancers, martial artists, magicians, acrobats, comedians, and more\. In addition to a wide range of performances, Resident Talent Night also features a physical and digital art exhibition, showcasing photography, painting, and drawing submissions from residents across the province\. 

Resident Talent Night is going virtual this year – t his means more people can attend, and more r esidents from across the province can show off their mad skills\! As performances and submissions will be pre\-recorded in advance, it also means that all of you talented folks out there can record yourselves whenever it suits you – when you have time from work, when the lighting is just right in your apartment, when that epic high note is going to sound perfect, or when you know you’ll land that backflip\.

**Event Details** 

**Thursday Sept 17th, 2020 at 7pm**

**Location:** Resident Talent Night is going virtual\! F ellow residents will host this fun evening as live emcees, while all performances and art will be pre\-recorded, submitted in advance, and shown throughout the evening\.  A Zoom link will be provided closer to the date\.
