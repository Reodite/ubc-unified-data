[Image](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/IMG_2337-300x225.jpg)The Resident Wellness Office is excited to share the dates for our next session of the Aim, Focus, Release Support Group for Residents on Leave\!

The [Support Group](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/list-of-services/aim-focus-release-a-support-group-for-residents-on-leave/) uses the evidence\-based approach of Mindful Self\-Compassion to develop healthy coping skills to challenging situations\.

**Dates: April 29th, April 26th, May 3rd, May 11th**

Facilitated via Zoom with [RWO Counsellors Rebecca and Nish](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/about-us/)

Please email [resident\.wellness@ubc\.ca](mailto:resident.wellness@ubc.ca?subject=) to register\!
