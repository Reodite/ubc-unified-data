The Resident Wellness Office recognizes how the COVID\-19 pandemic may be affecting your residency training in many ways\.

Our office has created a [COVID\-19 Resource Page](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/covid-19-resident-wellness-resources/), updated regularly, to provide support to residents at this time\.

Additionally, we recognize that this time is particularly stressful for many residents, and that group support at this time can be incredibly valuable\. In response, we are launching a digital drop\-in support group via Zoom for all residents surrounding anxiety and stress related to COVID\-19\.

The support group will run Monday and Thursday evenings starting Thursday April 2nd from 7:30\-9pm \(Mondays\) and 7\-8:30pm \(Thursdays\)\. Online booking will be available for booking one week in advance\. There is a limit of 10 resident participants per group session\. Please email [resident\.wellness@ubc\.ca](mailto:resident.wellness@ubc.ca) or visit our [RWO COVID\-19 Resource Page](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/covid-19-resident-wellness-resources/) for further information\. To register, please visit our [online booking site](https://ubcresidentwellness.janeapp.com/)\.
