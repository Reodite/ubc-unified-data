Back in Fall 2019, the Resident Wellness Office launched a [support group for residents currently on medical leave](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/list-of-services/aim-focus-release-a-support-group-for-residents-on-leave/), where residents learn healthy coping skills through the evidence\-based approach of mindful self\-compassion\. The launch of this program was met with success, including positive responses from participants: 

“*This group has been very helpful to me because it made me feel like I am not alone in the struggle and that others are also feeling similar things as I am*\.”

“*It was nice to see humanity in residents\. Thank you so much for this group*“\. 

The Resident Wellness Office will be running a new session of our Support Group for residents on medical leave **starting November 3rd**\. The group will **run weekly on Tuesdays from 9:30\-11:30am via Zoom**\. 

You can register or request further information by contacting [resident\.wellness@ubc\.ca](mailto:resident.wellness@ubc.ca)\.
