Megan Roche, M\.D\., and David Roche host a podcast called [Some Work, All Play](https://open.spotify.com/show/3AaJYZngimocFf8aztKTcO), focusing on a number of topics from the standpoint of self\-compassion and unconditional love while offering evidence\-based research to offer insight into different discussions\. In their most recent episode, the Roche’s discuss the challenges, complexities, and beauty of relationships, particularly in challenging times such as in a pandemic\. Other recent topics include harnessing the superpower of kindness to navigate change and uncertainty, and how to look for hope in challenging situations\. Have a listen and enjoy\!
