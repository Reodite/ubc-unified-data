[Image](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/rwo-nish-1-scaled.jpg)The Resident Wellness Office is excited to welcome Nish Laszczuk to the Resident Wellness Team\! Nish joined the RWO in April 2022\. She is a Registered Social Worker with the BC College of Social Workers as well as the Ontario College of Social Workers\. Nish will currently be providing Individual Counselling support via Zoom and she specializes in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, as well as training in Mindfulness, Dialectical Behavioral Therapy, and uses a trauma informed lens\.

We are happy to welcome Nish Laszczuk to our team\! Nish will be working Monday\-Friday from 7am\-1pm for virtual sessions only at this time\. You can reach Nish at: [nish\.laszczuk@ubc\.ca](mailto:nish.laszczuk@ubc.ca)

If you would like to request an appointment with Nish, please email [resident\.wellness@ubc\.ca](mailto:resident.wellness@ubc.ca) or complete our new Appointment Request Form here: [https://ubc\.ca1\.qualtrics\.com/jfe/form/SV\_cGZyUhYBmSI4ww6](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_cGZyUhYBmSI4ww6)\.
