**[Image](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Screen-Shot-2020-12-16-at-11.55.47-AM-300x129.png)Wellness Rounds** is a confidential, resident\-only discussion that allows learners to connect, offer one another support, learn some helpful coping tools, and share in the successes and challenges experienced in residency\.

Starting **January 13th**, the Resident Wellness Office is launching monthly drop\-in Wellness Rounds for all residents **every second Wednesday of every month from 6\-7pm via Zoom\.** 

The sessions will be facilitated by RWO Counsellors Asmae or Rebecca\.

Maintaining psychological safety and confidentiality during Wellness Rounds is very important to us\. You are welcome to share as much or as little about your name, residency program, and other identifying details as you wish\. You are welcome to keep your camera turned on or off based on comfort level\.

Please email [resident\.wellness@ubc\.ca](mailto:resident.wellness@ubc.ca) for further information and for the Zoom link\.
