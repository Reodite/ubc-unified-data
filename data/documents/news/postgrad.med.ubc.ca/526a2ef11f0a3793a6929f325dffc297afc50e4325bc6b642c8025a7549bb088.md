We excited to partner with the PHSA Alumni QI team to launch the **QIPS Resident Workshop &amp; Mentorship Pilot Program\!** This initiative provides residents with practical tools and guided mentorship to develop skills in Quality Improvement and Patient Safety, supporting safer, higher\-quality patient care\. **[Sign up by January 9\!](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_5tf5iaEyOQEmd6u)**

[https://postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca/wp\-content/uploads/2025/12/2026\-02\-QIPS\-Resident\-Workshop\-and\-Mentorship\.pdf](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2026-02-QIPS-Resident-Workshop-and-Mentorship.pdf)[QIPS Resident Workshop and Mentorship Poster](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/QIPS-Poster-231x300.png)What’s included:

-   A virtual workshop on **Tuesday, February 10, 2026 from 3:30–5:00 PM**
-   Completion of a **Patient Safety Event \(PSE\) analysis** by **February 17, 2026**
-   A **1\-hour virtual discussion with a QIPS mentor** by **March 6, 2026**
-   **Total Estimated Time: 5\.5 hours –** Workshop \(1\.5 hours\) / PSE Analysis \(3 hours\) / Mentorship \(1 hour\)

[Register by January 9](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_5tf5iaEyOQEmd6u)

**Space is limited\.**
