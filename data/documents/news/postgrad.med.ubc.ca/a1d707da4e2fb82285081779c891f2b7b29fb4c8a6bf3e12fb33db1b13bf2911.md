Congratulations to the 2026 PGME Program Award recipients\! Each year PGME recognizes the excellence of faculty and staff involved in our programs for their impact on education and learning at UBC\. To honour the passing of Dr\. David Dix we have renamed the Outstanding Contribution Award to the **Dr\. David Dix Outstanding Contribution Award**\. Dr\. Dix was also the recipient of this award last year\. Recipients for PGME Awards are selected for their incredible program leadership, support and other facets of residency education\. See the list of recipients below\.

### **Program / Site Director Award**

**Dr\. Zoe Brown**, *Program Director, Anesthesiology*  
**Dr\. Chelsea Monell**, *Site Director, Family Medicine – Indigenous Site*

### **Faculty Award**

**Dr\. Kirk Lawlor**, *Vascular Surgery, Vancouver General Hospital  
***Dr\. Stephanie Mokrycke**, *Family Practice – Emergency Medicine, St\. Paul’s Hospital &amp; Mount St\. Joseph’s Hospital

### **Dr\. David Dix Outstanding Contribution Award**
