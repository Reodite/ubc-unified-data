Self\-talk is the stream of unspoken thoughts that we constantly have in our head\. Taking control of this self\-talk to frame negative thoughts into constructive thoughts can be [beneficial in reducing stress](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/stress-management/in-depth/positive-thinking/art-20043950) and enhancing your performance as you work towards your goals\. Ultrarunner and certified Mental Performance Consultant with the Association for Applied Sport Psychology Addie Bracy shares strategies of how to [control your self\-talk within a performance context](https://trailrunnermag.com/training/take-control-of-your-self-talk.html)\.
