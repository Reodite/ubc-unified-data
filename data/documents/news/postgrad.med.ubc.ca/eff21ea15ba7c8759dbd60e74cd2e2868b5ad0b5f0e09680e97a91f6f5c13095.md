Congratulations to the 2025 PGME Program Award recipients for all their hard work\! Each year PGME recognizes the excellence of many of the faculty and staff involved in our programs for their impact on education and learning at UBC\. Award recipients are selected for their incredible program leadership, support of the program and other facets of residency education\. See the list of award recipients below\.

### Program / Site Director Award

**Dr\. Dean Brown** *Site Director, Family Medicine \-Coastal*

**Dr\. Irfan Khanbhai** *Program Director, Psychiatry*

### Faculty Award

**Dr\. Lindsey Forest** *Family Practice, Cowichan*

**Dr\. Nadia Branco** *Obstetrics &amp; Gynaecology, BC Women’s*

### Program Administrator Award

**Ana Palomino** *Program Coordinator, Adult Critical Care Medicine*

**Liana Polsinelli** *Senior Program Assistant, Thoracic Surgery and Otolaryngology \-Head and Neck Surgery*

**Lindsay Gowland** *Program Manager, Family Practice, Enhanced Skills*

**Kristen Hatch** *Site Coordinator, Family Medicine \-Okanagan South*

### Outstanding Contribution Award

**Pamela Blake** *Site Coordinator, Family Medicine \-Strathcona*

**Dr\. David Dix** *Clinical Professor, Dept of Paediatrics/ PGME Faculty Lead, Accreditation*
