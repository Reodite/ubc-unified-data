[Image](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/PHP-BC-Peer-Sessions-Facebook-and-Twitter-300x169.png)The BC Physician Health Program \(PHP\) is offering free drop\-in Physician Peer Support Sessions every 2\[nd\] and 4\[th\] Wednesday of each month at 12\-1pm PST via Zoom\.

Join other physicians and residents, Psychiatrist Dr Jennifer Russel, and PHP’s Manager of Clinical Services, Roxanne Joyce, to talk over challenges and issues you are facing as a health care professional generally or more specific to the COVID\-19 pandemic\. Sessions are drop\-in, no commitment necessary, focusing on peer support not psychiatric care\. You are able to join anonymously using a pseudonym or to listen only\.

Email peersupport@physicianhealth\.com for the link to join by phone or video\. Learn more [here](https://www.doctorsofbc.ca/news/covid-19-physician-peer-support-sessions)\.
