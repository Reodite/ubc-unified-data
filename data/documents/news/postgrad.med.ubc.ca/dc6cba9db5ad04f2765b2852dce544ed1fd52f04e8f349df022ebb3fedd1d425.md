[Image](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/IMG_6813-300x225.jpg)Back in Fall 2019, the Resident Wellness Office launched a [support group for residents currently on medical leave](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/list-of-services/aim-focus-release-a-support-group-for-residents-on-leave/), where residents learn healthy coping skills through the evidence\-based approach of mindful self\-compassion\. The launch of this program was met with success, including positive responses from participants:

“*This group has been very helpful to me because it made me feel like I am not alone in the struggle and that others are also feeling similar things as I am*\.”

“*It was nice to see humanity in residents\. Thank you so much for this group*“\.

The Resident Wellness Office will be running a new session of our Support Group for residents on medical leave **starting January 12th 2021**\. The group will **run Tuesdays January 12th, 19th, 26th, and February 6th from 12:30\-2:30pm\.** 

Questions and registration requests can be directed to Aileen, Resident Wellness Program Coordinator at: [resident\.wellness@ubc\.ca](mailto:resident.wellness@ubc.ca)
