This event will feature resident\-led oral presentations in research, quality improvement \(QI\), and other scholarly activities, including community\-based research projects\.

## **Want to attend?**

**Date:** Wednesday, October 14, 2026  
**Time:** 7:30 AM – 1:00 PM \(TBC\)  
**Location:** In person at Diamond Health Care Centre \(DHCC\) or Virtual\.

Register to attend PGME Scholar Day and watch the presentations either in person or virtually\. Open to residents, fellows, medical students, program and site directors, program administrators, research staff and more\.
