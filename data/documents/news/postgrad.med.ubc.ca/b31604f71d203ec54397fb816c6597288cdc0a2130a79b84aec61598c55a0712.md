[PGME Scholar Day call for abstracts](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/News-Post-1.jpg)

Residents are encouraged to submit their abstracts by July 31  for the chance to present at the first PGME Scholar Day on October 16, 2025\!

This new event gives you the opportunity to showcase your research, quality improvement, and other scholarly work and connect with peers and faculty to receive valuable feedback\.

**[https://postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca/wp\-content/uploads/2025/06/PGMEScholarDay\.png](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/PGMEScholarDay.png)[Image](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/PGMEScholarDay-232x300.png)To apply:**

1.  [Download the abstract template](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/CP/File.php?F=F_dnhDgJ0t6jQsSDY)
2.  [Submit your abstract](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_ahFeAJACY9kTWK2)

*Please make sure your abstract is ready before submitting the abstract form\.*

All abstract submissions will be reviewed, and selected residents will be notified by the end of September\. The detailed agenda and additional information will be shared in the fall\.  If you have any questions, please email us at pgme\.research@ubc\.ca\. [Learn more about Research at PGME](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/research/)\.

We look forward to your participation in this exciting new event\!
