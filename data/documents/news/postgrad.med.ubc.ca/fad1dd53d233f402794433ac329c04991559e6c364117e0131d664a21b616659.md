Multi\-sport adventures fuel happiness; RWO Coordinator Aileen explores deep in the Seymour Valley, at the base of Paton Peak\. North Vancouver, BC

Learning about different types of emotions and events that may trigger a particular emotion within us can be helpful in learning we respond to particular emotions and how this influences our actions in different situations\. [This Atlas of Emotions](http://atlasofemotions.org/#introduction/), developed by the Dalai Lama, breaks down the process of processing emotions in a clear and skills\-based approach\.
