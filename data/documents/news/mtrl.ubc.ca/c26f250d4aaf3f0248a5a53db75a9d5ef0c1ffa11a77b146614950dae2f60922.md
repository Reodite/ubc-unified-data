Congratulations to two of our faculty members for receiving the 2024/25 Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \([TLEF](https://tlef.ubc.ca/announcements/announcing-the-2024-25-teaching-and-learning-enhancement-fund-projects/)\)\! The TLEF supports UBC’s strategic plan by advancing transformative learning at the university\. This year, $2\.2 million of TLEF will be supporting 62 new and returning TLEF projects across campus\.  
  

**Integration of Simulation Software Teaching Modules into Materials and Manufacturing Engineering Curricula to Enhance Practical Learning**  
[Dr\. Sergey Kravchenko](https://mtrl.ubc.ca/sergey-kravchenko/)

**Development of a New Case Study Incorporating Themes of Indigenous Engagement and Consultation in a Core First Year Engineering Course**  
[Dr\. Jon Nakane](https://mtrl.ubc.ca/jon-nakane/)
