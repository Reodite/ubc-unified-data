This is a platform for MTRL faculty, staff, and students to submit departmental news and events\. News will be published on the MTRL News section and may also be featured on the [MTRL LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ubc-mtrl-eng/) page\. Events will be published on the MTRL Events section\. Kindly adhere to the form instructions and place your submission\.

**Example of news to be posted:**

-   Awards announcements, such as grants or funding secured by faculty members, student accolades, etc\.
-   Updates or reflections on recent MTRL events, such as alumni events, research day, lab tours, etc\.
-   Spotlight and stories\. ***Note:*** ***If you would like to nominate a MTRL member i\.e\., student/ alumni/ faculty/ PDF, please select this option\.***

**Example of events to be posted:**

-   Seminars/ lectures \(host or co\-host\)
-   Lab tours
-   Orientation/ info sessions/ departmental social events
-   Student clubs activities, events, competition and others\.

Submissions will be reviewed and published on the website once requirements are fulfilled\.
