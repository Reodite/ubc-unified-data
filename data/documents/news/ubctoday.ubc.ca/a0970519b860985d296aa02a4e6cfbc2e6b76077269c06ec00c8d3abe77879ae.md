### Name:

Sharon Lim

### Title:

Administration &amp; Operations Coordinator

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology &amp; Learning Technology Innovation Centre

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2017

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

Driving operational excellence through process optimization, organizational effectiveness, and service\.

---

### What is the best thing about your work/role at UBC?

The best thing about my role at UBC is the opportunity for continuous professional growth\. Over the past eight years, my position has evolved alongside my own professional development, as well as changing departmental priorities and organizational needs\. This has allowed me to continually learn, take on new challenges, and expand my skills and responsibilities\.

---

### What three words would others use to describe you?

The three words that most people would use to describe me are: Reliable, Analytical, and Resourceful\.

---

### What are your hobbies and interests outside of work?

Outside of work, I'm a devoted hockey fan and rarely miss a Canucks game\.  
I also have a strong passion for the arts\. I enjoy drawing, watercolour painting, digital illustration, graphic design, and crafts\. My favourite subject is portraiture, and I especially enjoy creating black\-and\-white portraits that focus on capturing a person's likeness and character\.
