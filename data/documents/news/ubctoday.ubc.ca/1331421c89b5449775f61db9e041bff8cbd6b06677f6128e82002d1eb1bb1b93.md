October 16, 2023

UBC Faculty of Medicine professor, Dr\. Pieter Cullis, is among six health leaders selected as 2024 Canadian Medical Hall of Fame inductees\.

Dr\. Cullis, a professor in the department of biochemistry and molecular biology, has been recognized for his work in developing the lipid nanoparticle delivery technology that enabled effective injection of the COVID\-19 mRNA vaccines\.

“The lipid nanoparticle technology enabling the COVID\-19 mRNA vaccines reflect the efforts of literally hundreds of people\. I have been extremely fortunate to receive credit for what was really a huge collective effort,” said Dr\. Cullis\.
