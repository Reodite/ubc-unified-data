May 12, 2025

More than 850 faculty and staff enjoyed this year's sports day which was held on May 2 at both campuses\. The sun shone and many teams dressed up to add to the fun atmosphere\. The event was made possible with the support of a dedicated group of staff and 90 volunteers who made this amazing day possible\.

Check out the photos from both campuses\.
