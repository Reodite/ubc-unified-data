January 13, 2022

Don't forget to use available [professional development \(PD\) funding](https://hr.ubc.ca/career-development/professional-development-funding) and submit claims in Workday by **Mar\. 4**\. PD funds are intended to support learning activities that will enhance your workplace knowledge or skills and support your career growth at the university\. Claims will be accepted in Workday on a first\-come, first\-served basis, until the annual funding pool is exhausted or by Mar\. 4, whichever comes first\.
