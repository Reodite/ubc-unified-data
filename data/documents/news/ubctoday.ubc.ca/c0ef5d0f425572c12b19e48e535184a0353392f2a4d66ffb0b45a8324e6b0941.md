UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers, and senior professional leaders in Vancouver and the Okanagan\.

September 6, 2022

I wish to announce that Karamjeet Heer will be leaving UBC to take up a new role at Simon Fraser University \(SFU\)\. Karamjeet joined the university in 2019 as Comptroller and was more recently appointed as interim Vice\-President, Finance\. Her last day at UBC will be October 31, 2022\.   
  
Please join me in wishing Karamjeet well in her new role as Associate Vice\-President, Finance, at SFU\. This is an exciting opportunity and we wish her every success\.  
  
As of November 1, 2022, Yale Loh will take on the role of interim Vice\-President, Finance\. Yale is currently UBC Treasurer, and is responsible for supporting strategic treasury initiatives for all UBC campuses and locations\. I am grateful to Yale for taking on this interim Vice\-President role and am confident he will provide strong leadership, alongside John Metras, who is interim Vice\-President, Operations\.  
  
With regard to the search for a new Vice\-President, Finance and Operations, the process is ongoing and we hope to have an update in October\.

**Santa J\. Ono**  
President and Vice\-Chancellor
