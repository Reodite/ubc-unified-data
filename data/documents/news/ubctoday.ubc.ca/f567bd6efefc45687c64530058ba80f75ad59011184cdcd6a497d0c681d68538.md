March 11, 2024

Seven projects, led by faculty and staff in Vancouver and the Okanagan, which embed sustainable health and wellbeing strategic initiatives within their plan have been awarded grant funding through the teaching, learning and research environments stream of the 2023/24 [UBC Wellbeing Strategic Initiative Fund](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/ubc-wellbeing-strategic-initiative-fund)\.

The successful projects range from working with Syilx Elders and Knowledge Keepers on Okanagan campus to explore Indigenous assessment and possibilities in education, improving mental health and social connection through dog therapy on Vancouver campus, and the development of Brief Guides to Grief to support students and faculty\. 

[Learn more](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/examples-and-research/announcing-funded-teaching-learning-research-wellbeing-projects-ubc-vancouver)
