August 17, 2026

### Name:

Joanna Guan

### My Pronouns:

They/Them &amp; She/Her

### Title:

Residence Advisor

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

Student Housing &amp; Community Services

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2024

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

Help residents survive and thrive in their home away from home\.

---

### What is the best thing about your work/role at UBC?

1.  Life on campus\. Working in universities proves to me that work can never be boring with students around\. As a student staff, I get to see both the creative, and operational side of UBC\. The UBC Ecosystem fascinates me\.  
     
2.  Being immersed in the residence community has sparked my passion for designing liveable cities that support healthy living\.

---

### What trait do you most admire in others?

Unique life experience and honesty\.

---

### What do you do regularly to invest in your mental and/or physical health?

I love regularly cooking, visiting the beach, and biking \(shoutout to community planning\)\.

A big portion of healthy living actually revolves around the environment and infrastructure\. It’s one of the reasons why I think living and working at UBC is so exciting\. I am excited to see where that interest may grow\.
