UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers in Vancouver and the Okanagan\.

May 22, 2020

On behalf of the Faculty Research Awards Committee, I am happy to advise that nominations are now open for the UBC Faculty Research Awards\.

These awards acknowledge research excellence across all disciplines at UBC\. Recognition at the university level can also lead to increased opportunity at the national and international level\. We strongly encourage you to consider equity, diversity and inclusion principles in your nomination strategies\.

Updated application process for 2020:

-   Submit a Notice of Intent by **July 31, 2020**\.
-   Final submissions for all awards must be made online by **October 15, 2020**\.

For full details, please visit [https://research\.ubc\.ca/support\-resources/awards\-applications\-nominations/internal\-faculty\-awards\-deadlines](https://research.ubc.ca/support-resources/awards-applications-nominations/internal-faculty-awards-deadlines)\.

If you have questions about the nomination process, contact the Office of Research Prizes and Awards \(research\.prizes@ubc\.ca\) or call 604 827 4057\.

**Gail Murphy**  
Vice\-President, Research &amp; Innovation
