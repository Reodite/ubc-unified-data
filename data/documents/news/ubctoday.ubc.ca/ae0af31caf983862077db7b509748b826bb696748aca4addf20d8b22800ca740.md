November 28, 2024

*Move UBC* grants are now available for faculty and staff to initiate projects or initiatives that support the wellbeing of our community by moving more\.

There are two funding streams available: 

-   Small grants: Projects requesting quick\-start funds under $200
-   Large grants: Projects requesting funds between $200–$1,500

Applications are open year round until funds are fully allocated\. 

[Learn more](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/moveubcgrants)
