UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers in Vancouver\.

January 9, 2023

UBC is embarking on a search for a new Vice\-Provost and Associate Vice\-President, Faculty Planning\.  
  
The Vice\-Provost and Associate Vice\-President, Faculty Planning, oversees domestic and international enrolment, participates in the university budgeting process and provides advice to the Provost\. This role also liaises with the Operations team on the development of strategic capital projects, coordinates the use of space and facilities, and works with the university emergency management team to ensure the safety of the UBC community\.  
  
This position is internal to the university and is a full\-time five\-year\-term appointment, eligible for a second term\.  
  
If you know of any faculty in your area of the university who you think would be a good fit for this role, please share this opportunity and encourage them to apply\. The call for applications will remain open until Feb\. 3, 2023\.  
  
Full details of the position and how to apply can be found [here](https://academic.ubc.ca/vice-provost-avp-faculty-planning)\.

**Gage Averill**  
Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver
