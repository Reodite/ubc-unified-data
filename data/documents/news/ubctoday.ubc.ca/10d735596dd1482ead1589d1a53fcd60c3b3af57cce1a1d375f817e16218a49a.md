UBC experts share meditative practices for mitigating your climate\-related stress\.

Find out about faculty and staff recommendations for tangible, calming, and restorative methods to process climate concerns, including: 

-   experimenting with climate writing, 
-   going on nature walks while contributing to citizen science, and 
-   volunteering at the UBC Farm\.
