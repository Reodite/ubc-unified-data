November 24, 2025

UBC Campus Security is preparing to transition into UBC Community Safety in early 2026, refreshing its identity to better reflect their caring and proactive approach to campus safety\. 

As part of this transformation, UBC’s Slow Fashion Research Cluster, in partnership with UBC Community Safety, is inviting the UBC Vancouver community to help reimagine the next generation of UBC Community Safety uniforms through a campus\-wide Uniform Design Challenge\.

If you have any ideas on the design of the clothes that Community Safety staff wear on\-duty, submit a drawing or notes about your concept by Dec\. 9 for a chance to see your vision come to life as part of UBC’s Slow Fashion Season 2026\.

[Learn more](https://security.ubc.ca/ubc-community-safety-uniform-design-challenge/)
