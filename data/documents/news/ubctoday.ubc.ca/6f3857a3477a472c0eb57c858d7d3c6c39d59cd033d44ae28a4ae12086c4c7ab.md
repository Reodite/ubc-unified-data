September 17, 2021

Faculty and staff are invited to an open forum to learn about changes to the Student Experience of Instruction end\-of\-course student surveys \(formerly Student Evaluation of Teaching\) that are being implemented in 2021/22 Winter Session, Term 1\.

During the open forum, we will provide updates about the implementation of the recommendations endorsed by both UBC Senates in May 2020, focusing in particular on revisions to the University Module Questions that were endorsed by Senate committees at UBC Okanagan and UBC Vancouver in August 2021\.

Faculty and staff are invited to join our group of panelists from UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan to learn about the changes to the questions, the metrics used in reporting results, and to ask questions about what the changes in the survey questions will mean for you\. We will also have information on how the implementation of the new survey questions will take place this fall\.

**Student Experience of Instruction open forum**

**Date:** Tuesday, September 28, 2021  
**Time:** 12:00–1:30pm
