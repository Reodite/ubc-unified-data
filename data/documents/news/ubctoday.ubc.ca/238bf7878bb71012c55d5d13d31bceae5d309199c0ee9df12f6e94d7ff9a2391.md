March 23, 2026

The year's annual Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day returns on Friday, May 1, 2026\. 

Sign up for an afternoon of competitive or just‑for‑fun physical activities—for bragging rights, for movement, and for the simple joy of connecting with colleagues\. Activities are designed for a wide range of experience, fitness and ability levels\. This event is all about reducing barriers, building community, and supporting your wellbeing\. Whether you participate as an office, department, group of colleagues or as an individual, there’s something for everyone\. 

There are also opportunities to volunteer\. 

All UBC faculty and staff on both campuses are welcome to join\.

[Find out more and register your team](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/news/march-12-2026/registration-now-open-2026-faculty-staff-sports-day)
