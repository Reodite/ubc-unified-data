May 11, 2021

The expiry date for unused funds from the 2016/17 Faculty Professional Development \(PD\) Fund is **Wednesday, June 30, 2021**\. 

To ensure expiring 2016/17 funds are used, submit your claims in Workday **by Friday, June 11, 2021**\. Any claims submitted after this deadline will be reimbursed through the next available balance \(i\.e\., PD Fund years after 2016/17\)\.

If you are a Department Head or manager \(or equivalent\), please review and approve these claims **by Tuesday, June 22, 2021**\.
