February 18, 2025

### Name:

Sam Gannaway

### My Pronouns:

he/him

### Title:

Copyright and Permissions Assistant

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

Digital Initiatives, UBC Library

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2021

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

License course materials and make them available on the Library Online Course Reserves\.

---

### What's the best advice you ever received?

Everyone's an idiot at some point\. Give yourself an 'idiot tax' of $500 a year so when you inevitably lose your keys, break your phone, or drive into a wall – it's OK\!

---

### What three words would others use to describe you?

Mischievous \| Gracious \| Self\-effacing

---

### What's one personal experience you would encourage others to do/try?

Move to a different country\. Experience a new culture and widen your horizons by changing your environment\.

---

### What was your job before you joined UBC?

I was a creative TV producer in the UK, working on primetime comedy and entertainment shows for the BBC, Sky, Channel 4 and many others\. I wrote scripts, interviewed celebrity guests and wrote research packs\.
