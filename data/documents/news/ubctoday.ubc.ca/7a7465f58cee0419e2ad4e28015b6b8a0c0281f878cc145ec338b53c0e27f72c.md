March 14, 2023

Celebrate Learning Week is a forum for the teaching and learning community to collaborate, network and engage in knowledge exchange\. The week\-long initiative will be hosted on May 2\-9, and the theme is the *Power of Place in Teaching and Learning*\. 

Faculty and staff are encouraged to participate by coordinating and/or attending events that reflect on the power of place in teaching and learning experiences\.

If you are interested in hosting a lecture, poster session, or workshop to showcase teaching and learning at UBC, submit your ideas by **Mar\. 31**\. 

[Find out more](https://celebratelearning.ubc.ca/submission-form/)
