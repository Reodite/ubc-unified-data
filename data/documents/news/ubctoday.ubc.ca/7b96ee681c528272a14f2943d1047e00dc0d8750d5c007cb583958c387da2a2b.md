April 18, 2023

For the 12th consecutive year, UBC is recognized as one of Canada’s Greenest Employers in 2023\. This special award recognizes Canadian employers that lead the nation in creating a culture of environmental awareness, developing exceptional sustainability initiatives, and are attracting employees because of their environmental leadership\.
