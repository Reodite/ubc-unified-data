October 1, 2024

### Name:

Angela Wagner

### My Pronouns:

she/her

### Title:

Education Manager

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

Faculty of Medicine, Rural Continuing Professional Development

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2015

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

I am one of the managers within our supportive relationships programs for rural healthcare\.

---

### Name another staff or faculty member you admire and why

Dr\. Joana Gil\-Mohapel is the Director of Curriculum, UBC Medical Undergraduate Program and Faculty Lecturer in the Island Medical Program and Division of Neurology\. Joana is purposeful, a system thinker, a medical education influencer, and responsive\. She’s approachable, knowledgeable, friendly, and an effective communicator with the ethos that it's always worth seeking equity in organizing, resourcing, supporting and delivering meaningful medical education\. Her superpower is relationship\-building, though she has many superpowers since she can balance and juggle it all\!

---

### What trait do you most admire in others?

Integrity\. A good sense of humour is a close second\!

---

### What are your hobbies and interests outside of work?

I am honoured to serve as a volunteer member of the Squamish Search and Rescue team, lending a hand to lost or injured persons and I love spending time with friends and family\. It nourishes and sustains my spirit\!
