January 25, 2021

If you are building a science innovation, disruptive technology, social venture or climate solution, entrepreneurship @ UBC helps UBC founders execute on their ideas by focusing on customer discovery, competitive analysis, problem validation and product\-market fit\. 

entrepreneurship @ UBC incubates over 100 ventures annually, producing skilled entrepreneurs with validated business models who are ready to take their venture to the next level\. 

Faculty and staff may apply for venture building programs by February 25\.

[Learn more](https://www.start.entrepreneurship.ubc.ca/venturebuilding)
