February 9, 2023

The UBC community is invited to comment on the [Statements on Responsible Investing](https://finance.ubc.ca/responsible_investment) in preparation for submission to the Board of Governors this spring\.

The Statements on Responsible Investment has been drafted to refine the responsible investing approach to reflect current environmental, social and governance findings into our policy\. View the draft [here](https://finance.ubc.ca/sites/finserv.ubc.ca/files/DRAFT_Statements%20on%20Responsible%20Investing_20230207.pdf)\.

The deadline to share your feedback is Feb\. 17\.

[Find out more](https://finance.ubc.ca/responsible_investment)
