October 5, 2022

The Research Facility Support Grants \(RFSG\) competition provides funding for the operation, maintenance, replacement, repair or upgrade of equipment used in UBC facilities to support research\. 

The RFSGs are one\-year grants of up to $50,000 intended to enhance UBC research, training, and equipment sustainability\.  

Researchers located on the UBC Vancouver campus and affiliated hospital sites are eligible to apply by **October 24, 2022**\.

[Learn more](https://research.ubc.ca/vpri-competitions-initiatives/research-facility-support-grants)
