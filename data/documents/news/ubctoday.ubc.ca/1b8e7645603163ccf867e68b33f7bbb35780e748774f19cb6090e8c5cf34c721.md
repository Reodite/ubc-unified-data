May 5, 2021

Celebrate Learning Week \(CLW\) is a week\-long initiative celebrating teaching and learning opportunities at UBC\. Now in its twelfth year, CLW will be hosted online from May 17 – 23, 2021\.

The theme of this year’s showcase is shaping our future\. For the first time, the Provost and Vice\-President Academic from UBCV and UBCO will collaboratively present this week\-long initiative that showcases teaching and learning opportunities at UBC through open lectures, poster sessions, workshops and more\.

Faculty and staff are encouraged to participate in Celebrate Learning Week by coordinating and/or attending events\.

[Learn more](https://celebratelearning.ubc.ca/)
