August 27, 2025

Dr\. Mieke Koehoorn has been appointed as Dean *pro tem,* Faculty of Medicine, and interim Vice\-President, Health, effective September 1, 2025, to October 31, 2025\. 

Dr\. Koehoorn is currently Vice Dean, Academic Affairs, in the Faculty of Medicine, where she provides strategic leadership for a variety of Faculty\-related matters, ranging from academic standards and faculty recruitment to the administration of policies and procedures that support the Faculty’s success and academic mission\.
