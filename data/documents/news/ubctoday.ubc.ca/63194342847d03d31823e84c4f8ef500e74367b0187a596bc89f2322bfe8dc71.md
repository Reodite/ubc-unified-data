March 23, 2026

The Healthy Workplace Initiatives Fund Program offers up to $3,000 each year to UBC departments and units to support grassroots activities that promote wellbeing at work\. The fund is intended to help teams get new ideas off the ground and support initiatives that contribute to healthier, more connected workplaces over time\.

Funding can be used for a range of activities, including mental health training, healthy eating and nutrition programs, art initiatives, community gardens, team challenges, and fitness classes\. Projects should be inclusive, sustainable, and designed to have a lasting impact within teams and units\. 

The deadline to apply is Apr\. 23 at 4 pm\.

[Learn more](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/workplace-wellbeing-programs/healthy-workplace-initiatives-fund-program)
