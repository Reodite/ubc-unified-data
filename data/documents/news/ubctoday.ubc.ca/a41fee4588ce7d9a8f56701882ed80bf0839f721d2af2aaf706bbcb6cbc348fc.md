    ## [2021 Celebrate Learning Week](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/march-03-2021/2021-celebrate-learning-week)
    
    UBC’s 12th annual Celebrate Learning Week will take place online from May 17\-23\. This year’s theme is Shaping Our Future, and we’d like to encourage all to take part by attending and/or hosting an event\. Celebrate Learning Week is an opportunity to recognize all the hard work, talent, and teaching and learning achievements at UBC\. Throughout the week, UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan will be working together to host a series of online events, including open lectures, information sessions, student advising activities, poster sessions and workshops\.
