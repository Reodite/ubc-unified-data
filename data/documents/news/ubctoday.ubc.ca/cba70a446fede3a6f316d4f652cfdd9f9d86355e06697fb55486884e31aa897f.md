October 23, 2023

Thank you to everyone who has participated in Campus Vision 2050 so far\.

The next step in the process is a legislatively required public hearing on proposed amendments to the Land Use Plan for UBC Vancouver, which will be held on **Nov\. 7** online and in person\. This is an opportunity for the university community to share comments, which will be considered by UBC's Board of Governors\. 

Ahead of the public hearing, you can also attend an information session on **Nov\. 1** to view the proposed changes to the Land Use Plan and learn more\.

[Register and find out more](https://campusvision2050.ubc.ca/lup)
