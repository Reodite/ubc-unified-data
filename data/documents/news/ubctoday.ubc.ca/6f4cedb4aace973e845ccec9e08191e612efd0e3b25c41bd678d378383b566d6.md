July 15, 2024

As music lovers flock to outdoor festivals and concerts this summer, many may not realize the noise levels could be high enough to cause hearing damage\.

Dr\. Navid Shahnaz, an associate professor at the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences, discusses the impact loud music can have on our hearing, and how we can protect ourselves while enjoying live performances\.
