March 24, 2025

The Faculty of Applied Science has interviewed faculty, staff and students with disabilities in order to raise awareness of disability and neurodiversity at the university\.

Read candid accounts from members of the UBC community who describe the challenges they have encountered around accessibility, discuss the need to eliminate stigma and showcase how their disability enhances the way they work\.

[Learn more](https://apsc.ubc.ca/celebrating-disability-and-neurodiversity)
