October 30, 2023

UBC’s annual [United Way campaign](https://unitedway.ubc.ca/about/) is currently underway until December, providing an opportunity for the UBC community to come together and raise funds for a great cause\.

United Way British Columbia is strengthening vital connections that support people in need in our local communities, across BC\. All money raised will make a difference locally\.

There are a number of ways you can get involved in this year’s campaign:

-   [Donate](https://unitedway.ubc.ca/howtogive/) to United Way British Columbia and help support the development of healthy, caring and inclusive communities\. You can choose to donate by payroll or a one\-time gift\.
-   Join this year’s [Coffee campaign](https://unitedway.ubc.ca/freecoffee/) \(Vancouver only\)\. Anyone who pledges $5 per paycheque \(or a $120 one\-time gift\) by November 2 will be mailed a coffee card to use at participating UBC Food Services locations in the month of November\.
-   Attend an upcoming United Way Pit Stop at UBC Okanagan: [Thrive United Way Pit Stop](https://events.ok.ubc.ca/event/thrive-united-way-pit-stop-2/): November 15, 9:30am – 11:00am, Gymnasium, upper level
-   Become a [Campaign Champion](https://unitedway.ubc.ca/be-a-2022-campaign-champion/) and lead United Way activities in your area of the university\. 
-   Learn more about the [real\-world impact](https://unitedway.ubc.ca/impact-of-your-gift/) United Way activities are having in our communities\.
