February 18, 2020

### Name:

Gregory Garrard

### Title:

Associate Dean of Graduate Studies and Research

### Department/Unit:

English and Cultural Studies

### Location:

Okanagan

### Year I started working at UBC:

2013

### 10 words or less to describe what I do at UBC:

Associate Dean of Research, environmental and animal literature expert\.

---

### The best thing about my work/role at UBC:

Incredible opportunities for collaboration and support for interdisciplinary research\.

---

### My favourite place to spend my lunch hour:

At home in the garden with my Sheltie\. Second place: outside the fish taco van, when it's on campus\.

---

### My top UBC tip for my colleagues, or for someone beginning their UBC journey:

The Okanagan campus, like the city of Kelowna, hasn't yet decided what it's going to be about\. That can be disappointing initially to arrivals from elsewhere, but it's also a tremendous opportunity\.
