This message was sent to all faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan on behalf of the Office of the Vice\-Principal, Research and Innovation\.

November 29, 2021

***A message from Phil Barker, Vice\-Principal and Associate Vice\-President, Research and Innovation, UBC Okanagan***

Dear friends and colleagues,

I'm excited to be back on campus and hosting my first in\-person Research and Innovation Town Hall since 2019\. I have many exciting updates to share and I look forward to hearing what’s on your mind too during the audience Q&amp;A\.

**Town Hall details**

**Date:** Wednesday, December 8, 2021  
**Time:** 1:30 to 3:30 p\.m\.  
**Location:** UNC 200 \(Ballroom\)  
**[RSVP](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6YajikfIsQtr22q) to attend in person\***  
**[Register](https://ubc.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_C6G2pmu2TMGy--XRLlEH_A) to join online**

\*Seating is limited due to COVID\-19 event protocols, so RSVP early if you are planning to attend in person\.
