July 28, 2025

### Name:

Stacey Nguyen Clarke

### My Pronouns:

She/her

### Title:

Technical Delivery Manager

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

UBC IT / Enterprise Automation and Solution Delivery / Technical Delivery

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2022

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

Actively delivering solutions in identity, data integration and automation space\.

---

### What work achievement are you most proud of?

I am proud of the accomplishment we have made in the delivery of IRP Student and our path towards supporting the UBC Community \(People\), Automation, Cybersecurity and our journey in AI Innovation\. I am most proud to be part of a high performing team, lead by supportive senior leadership who has inspired us to do our best, paving the way towards a bright future for us and our children\.

---

### What trait do you most admire in others?

Leaders who brings out the best in their people\. Those who inspire and mentor, seek to understand, and listen empathetically\.

---

### What's one personal experience you would encourage others to do/try?

I would encourage others to test their mental and physical limits by training for and signing up to run a marathon\.
