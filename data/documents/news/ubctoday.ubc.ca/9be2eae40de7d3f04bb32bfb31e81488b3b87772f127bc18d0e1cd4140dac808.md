May 30, 2022

Does your lab have refrigerators, freezers, or cold rooms for sample storage? 

If so, join the International Laboratory Freezer Challenge\. Compete with labs to improve sample accessibility and integrity, reduce costs, and improve energy efficiency for cold storage in your lab\. 

By participating in the challenge, you will help reduce energy consumption, improve sample accessibility and sample integrity and learn freezer maintenance techniques\. Individual labs earn one point for each action taken per refrigeration unit—including points earned, kWh saved, points earned per cold storage unit, and kWh saved per cold storage unit\. Labs participating in the challenge will need to submit an online score sheet at the end of the competition\. 

Register today and submit your score sheet by **July 1**\.

[Learn more](https://sustain.ubc.ca/international-freezer-challenge)
