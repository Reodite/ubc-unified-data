This message was sent to all faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan on behalf of the Office of the Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, Okanagan\.

November 19, 2020

***A message from Ananya Mukherjee Reed, Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Okanagan*** 

Dear colleagues,

Professor Gordon Binsted, Dean of the Faculty of Health and Social Development \(FHSD\), will conclude his term as Associate Provost, Academic Health Initiatives on November 30, 2020\. Professor Binsted took up the position on January 1, 2018, when the Okanagan campus was beginning to define its objectives in the area of Health\. In this role, Professor Binsted has represented the Okanagan campus and made important contributions to many health\-related developments across both campuses, as well as in collaboration with Interior Health and Kelowna General Hospital\. Professor Binsted will continue on committees and collaborations in his capacity as the Dean of FHSD\.

I thank Professor Binsted for his support and development of the Academic Health Initiatives portfolio, and look forward to working closely with him as we move forward with our health\-related initiatives\.
