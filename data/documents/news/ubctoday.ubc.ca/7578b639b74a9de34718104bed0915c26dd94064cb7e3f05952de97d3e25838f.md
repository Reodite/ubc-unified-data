Do you have a data challenge you need help solving? Partner with a team of UBC Master of Data Science \(MDS\) students to turn your data into insights, tools or solutions\.

During the final two months of the program each May and June, teams of MDS students work with Capstone Project Partners to apply their skills in data analysis, programming, statistics and machine learning to help address real\-world data challenges\.  
  
Join the Sept\. 18 information session to learn more about:

-   What makes a strong MDS Capstone Project
-   What types of projects are suitable for student teams
-   What to expect when working with MDS students
-   How to prepare and submit a project proposal

[Attend the virtual information session on Sept\. 18](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/employers/capstone-project?utm_campaign=mds+r2027+capstone+info+session&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_source=ubc+today&amp;utm_content=&amp;utm_term=#virtual_information_session)
