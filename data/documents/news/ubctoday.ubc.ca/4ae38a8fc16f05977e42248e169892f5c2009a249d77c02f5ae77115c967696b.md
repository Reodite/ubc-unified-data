    ## [Metro Vancouver transit strike \- confirmed](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/january-01-2024/metro-vancouver-transit-strike-confirmed)
    
    CUPE Local 4500, a union representing 180 Coast Mountain Bus Company Transit Supervisors, have confirmed they are escalating labour action to shut down bus service across Metro Vancouver, starting today \(January 22\)\. This has resulted in a full\-scale shut down of bus and SeaBus services for at least 48 hours\. At this time, it is not expected to affect SkyTrain, Canada Line, HandyDART, West Coast Express and other contracted services, which should continue to operate\.
