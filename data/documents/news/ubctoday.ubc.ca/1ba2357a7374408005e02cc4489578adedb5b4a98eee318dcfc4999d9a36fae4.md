July 7, 2026

As summer schedules ramp up, now is a great time to add this fall's staff and faculty BBQs to your calendar\. September may feel far away, but before we know it, we'll be back in the midst of the back\-to\-school rush and a new academic year\. Reconnect with colleagues, enjoy live entertainment and celebrate the start of a new academic year together\.

The *UBC Okanagan Welcome Back BBQ and Info Fair* will be held on Sept\. 1, and the *UBC Vancouver Staff and Faculty BBQ* will take place on Sept\. 9\.

[Learn more about what's happening at UBC Vancouver\.](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/staffandfacultybbq/) 

[Learn more about what's happening at UBC Okanagan](https://news.ok.ubc.ca/2026/08/18/welcome-back-fall-town-hall-bbq-and-info-fair/)\.
