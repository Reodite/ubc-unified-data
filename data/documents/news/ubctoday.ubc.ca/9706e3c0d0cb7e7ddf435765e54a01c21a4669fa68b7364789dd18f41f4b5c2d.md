June 3, 2024

We are pleased to announce the launch of ‘Staff in focus’ – a new series highlighting our staff members and their work at the university\.    

Over the years, you have told us that you would like to learn more about your colleagues at UBC\. We currently feature teaching faculty through ‘Teaching in focus’ and researchers through ‘Research in focus’ – and now, 'Staff in focus' is a dedicated platform to showcase the diverse work of our staff community\.  

Whether you're new to UBC or have been here for a while, ‘Staff in focus’ is an opportunity to share who you are, what you do at UBC, and what insights or hidden talents you’d like to share with the faculty and staff community\. 

[Submit your Staff in focus](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/submit-your-staff-focus)

*If you are a faculty member, we welcome you to submit a* [*Research in focus*](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/submit-your-research-focus) *or* [*Teaching in focus*](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/submit-your-teaching-focus) *profile\.*
