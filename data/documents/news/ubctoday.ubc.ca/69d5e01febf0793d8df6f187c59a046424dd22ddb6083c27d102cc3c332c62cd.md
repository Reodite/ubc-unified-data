October 16, 2023

Congratulations to the recipients of UBC Okanagan's 2023 Staff Awards of Excellence\. 

This year, six individuals were recognized for their unwavering commitment and dedication across the following categories: Advancing Anti\-Racism and Inclusive Excellence, Enhancing the UBC Experience, Global Citizenship, Leadership, Sustainability, and Wellbeing\.
