October 18, 2023

This summer, Partnership Recognition and Exploration \(PRE\) funding was awarded to 20 collaborative community\-university initiatives, showcasing the diverse ways in which communities are partnering with UBC students, faculty and staff on reciprocal initiatives\. 

There is still an opportunity for interested faculty and staff to apply for up to $1,500 in funding until December 31, 2023, or until available funds are exhausted\. 

[Learn more](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/news/2023-pre-fund-summer-recipients/)
