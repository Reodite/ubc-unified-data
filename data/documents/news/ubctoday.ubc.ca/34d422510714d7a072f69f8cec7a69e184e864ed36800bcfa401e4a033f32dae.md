    ## [Enrolling in benefits](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/enrolment-enrolment-changes/enrolling-benefits)

### Building your network

-   UBCV
    
    ## [Faculty Women’s Club at UBC Vancouver](http://facultywomensclub.ubc.ca/)
    
    A club for all women affiliated with the university
    
-   UBCV
    
    ## [Hot lunch for staff at UBC Vancouver](https://stjohns.ubc.ca/hot-lunch/)
    
    A regular cross\-campus staff networking event, featuring a different speaker from UBC’s leadership team\.
    
-   UBCV
    
    ## [Indigenous portal](https://indigenous.ubc.ca/)
    
    Based in the First Nations Longhouse, the FNHL is here to make UBC's resources more accessible to Indigenous people and to improve the university's ability to meet their needs
    
-   UBCV &amp; UBCO
    
    ## [Queer Faculty and Staff Collective](https://equity.ubc.ca/queer-faculty-and-staff-collective/)
    
    For UBC faculty and staff who identify as members of 2SLGBTQIA\+ communities to come together, make connections and build community\.
    
-   UBCV &amp; UBCO
    
    ## [Staff in focus](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/submit-your-staff-focus)
    
    Share a little bit about your work and get to know your colleagues at the university\. If you submit a 'Staff in focus' profile, it will be published on the UBC Today website for faculty and staff to read\.
    
-   UBCV &amp; UBCO
    
    ## [UBC Directory](https://www.directory.ubc.ca/index.cfm)
    
    To search the directory of faculty and staff and find their contact details
