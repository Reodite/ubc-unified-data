    ## [Apply for the 2025 Partnership Recognition and Exploration Fund](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/may-05-2025/apply-2025-partnership-recognition-and-exploration-fund)
    
    Applications are now open for the 2025 Partnership Recognition and Exploration \(PRE\) Fund\. The PRE Fund awards up to $1,500 in funding to support collaboration and help build reciprocal relationships between community partners and UBC students, faculty, and staff\. This year, there is a total of $50,000 to fund community\-university projects\.Faculty and staff are invited to apply for funding until December 31, 2025, or until available funds are exhausted\. Visit the Community Engagement website for more information on how to apply and useful FAQs\.
