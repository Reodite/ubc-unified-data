October 5, 2021

UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan are offering free, drop\-in COVID\-19 vaccination to students, faculty and staff this fall\.

**UBC Vancouver**

A COVID\-19 vaccine clinic is available from **October 5 to 29**, Tuesdays to Fridays, from 9:00am to 4:00pm at the Ponderosa North Building Ballroom\. [Learn more about vaccine requirements](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/campus-safety#ubc-vaccine-clinic)\.

**UBC Okanagan**

A COVID\-19 vaccine clinic is available on **October 15** from 8:00am to 4:00pm at the ADM Foyer\. To expedite the process on\-site, [attendees are encouraged to pre\-register](https://www.getvaccinated.gov.bc.ca/s/) and receive a confirmation number\. [Find out more](https://ok.ubc.ca/covid19/vaccines/)\.
