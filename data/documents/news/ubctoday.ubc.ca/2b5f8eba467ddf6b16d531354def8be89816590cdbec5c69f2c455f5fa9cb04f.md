    ## [Dr\. Sara\-Jane Finlay re\-appointed Associate Vice\-President, Equity &amp; Inclusion](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/july-07-2020/dr-sara-jane-finlay-re-appointed-associate-vice-president-equity-inclusion)
    
    We are pleased to announce the Board of Governors has approved the re\-appointment of Dr\. Sara\-Jane Finlay as Associate Vice\-President \(AVP\), Equity &amp; Inclusion for an ongoing term\. The re\-appointment process was guided by an Advisory Committee comprising faculty, staff and student representatives\. The committee reviewed feedback from a UBC community consultation, and considered a recent external review of the Equity &amp; Inclusion Office \(EIO\)\.
