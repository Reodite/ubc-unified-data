March 23, 2026

A total of $2\.4 million has been awarded to Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(TLEF\) projects in 2026/27\. Sixty new and returning projects aimed at advancing innovation in teaching at UBC have received funding\. This year’s funded projects include [18 Large TLEF Transformation projects](https://tlef.ubc.ca/funded-proposals/?filter_43=2026&amp;filter_42=&amp;filter_15=Large+TLEF&amp;filter_34=&amp;gv_search=&amp;mode=all#gv-view-3295-1) and [42 Small TLEF Innovation projects](https://tlef.ubc.ca/funded-proposals/?filter_43=2026&amp;filter_42=&amp;filter_15=Small+TLEF&amp;filter_34=&amp;gv_search=&amp;mode=all#gv-view-3295-1)\. 

For a complete picture of the 2026/27 TLEF funding round, as well as enrolment reach from projects funded in 2025/26, please visit the [2026/27 TLEF Snapshots page](https://tlef.ubc.ca/previously-funded-proposals/snapshots/2026-27-snapshot/) on the TLEF website\. 

An additional $120,000 was awarded to support [12 project teams](https://tlef.ubc.ca/application/special-call/universal-design-for-learning-fellows-program/2026-universal-design-for-learning-fellows-cohort/) as part of the special call for the [Universal Design for Learning Follows Program](https://udlhub.ubc.ca/)\. These projects were nominated by Faculties at UBC Vancouver to participate in the 4th UDL Fellows Program cohort that will launch in spring 2026\.
