October 21, 2021

As influenza \(flu\) season approaches, protect yourself by taking the flu vaccine\. UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan are offering free, appointment\-based flu immunization clinics for faculty and staff this fall\. 

**UBC Vancouver**

**Dates:** Oct\. 25\-29, Nov\. 1\-5 and Nov\. 8\-10  
**Venue:** Ponderosa North Building Ballroom

[Book an appointment](https://ubcfluclinic.janeapp.com/#discipline/1/treatment/1) \| [Find out more](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/occupational-hygiene/occupational-hazards/flu/?web=1&amp;wdLOR=c773AD081-C311-D14C-A337-B3AAD1551AED)

*For COVID\-19 vaccination, drop\-in is available at this venue on Oct\. 28 and 29\.*

**UBC Okanagan**

**Dates:** Oct\. 28\-30 and Nov\. 1\-3   
**Venue:** Nechako 221 gathering room

[Book an appointment](https://ubc-booking.appointy.com/hse/bookings/appointment?service=srv_01FANBY46N3HKZAZ1HWDTZ46N6) \| [Find out more](https://hse.ok.ubc.ca/health/discont/flu/)

*Free vaccination is also available to immediate family members of faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan\.*
