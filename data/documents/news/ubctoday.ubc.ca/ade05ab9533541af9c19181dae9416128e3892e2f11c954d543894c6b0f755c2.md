May 8, 2026

The Bike Buddy program, offered by the UBC Bike Kitchen, is a commute mentorship initiative designed to help you overcome barriers to cycling to and from UBC Vancouver\. 

Whether you’re new to cycling or an experienced rider, you’ll be matched with a partner to complete 3–5 commutes during the month of June\.

Register as a novice or mentor by May 20\.

[Learn more](https://www.thebikekitchen.ca/bikebuddy)

Explore more cycling resources, discounts and programs at [UBC Vancouver](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/cycling) and [UBC Okanagan](https://campusplanning.ok.ubc.ca/transportation/travel-options/cycling/)\.
