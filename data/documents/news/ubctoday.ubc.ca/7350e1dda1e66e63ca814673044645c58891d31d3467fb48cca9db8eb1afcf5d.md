August 11, 2022

The [Teaching Development Program](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/faculty-programs/new-to-ubc/teaching-development-program-for-new-faculty/) for new faculty is a 10\-month long faculty development program offered by the [Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/)\.

The program runs from Aug\. 2022 to Apr\. 2023 and will help newly hired faculty \(research\-stream, educational leadership stream, sessional faculty, lecturers\) build a foundation for their teaching careers by providing a network of support around teaching and learning\. 

Accessible via CWL, applications will be accepted on a rolling basis until **Aug\. 23**\.

[Learn more](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/2022-teaching-development-program-for-new-faculty-preliminary-application/)
