UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers, and senior professional leaders in Vancouver\.

September 23, 2026

I am pleased to announce that the UBC Board of Governors has approved the extension of the appointment of Dr\. Robert Kozak as Dean of the Faculty of Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship for a second five\-year term\.  
  
During his first term as Dean, Dr\. Kozak successfully led the Faculty through a significant evolution, expanding its name to the Faculty of Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship\. The new name reflects more completely the present breadth and depth of the Faculty’s research, teaching, education programs and community outreach, including efforts in identifying solutions to pressing global environmental challenges\. He has also led the Faculty in its expansion of global engagements, Indigenous programming, microcredentials, and graduate and undergraduate programming\.  
  
Read the full announcement on the [VP Academic website](https://academic.ubc.ca/academic-community/appointments-searches/extension-of-appointment-dr-robert-kozak-dean-faculty-forestry-environmental-stewardship)\.  
  
I would like to thank the President's Advisory Committee and the faculty, staff and students who participated in the consultation process regarding of the extension of Dr\. Kozak’s appointment\.  
  
I am grateful to Dr\. Kozak for his visionary work to date as Dean and look forward to continuing to work with him and the Faculty of Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship’s leadership team in the coming years\.  
  
***Gage Averill***  
*Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver*
