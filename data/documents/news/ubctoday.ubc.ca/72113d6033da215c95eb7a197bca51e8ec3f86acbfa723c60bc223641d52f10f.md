April 15, 2024

In recent years, we’ve asked faculty and staff to share your snaps of nature\-inspired moments\.  

This spring, we’ll be putting out another call for photos – this time focused on the birds and wildlife you encounter in your neighbourhood or out on hikes across the province\. 

This is a heads up to get your camera ready so you can unleash your photography skills and share your photos via *UBC Today* later this spring\. Whether it’s a bear you spot while hiking on a trail, or a hummingbird in your garden, we want you to capture those moments\. 

If you’re looking for inspiration, take a look at the various photos shared in previous years:  

[View nature\-inspired snapshots in 2023](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/stories/july-5-2023/view-complete-gallery-nature-inspired-snapshots) 

[See snapshots of spring blossoms by faculty and staff in 2022](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/stories/april-19-2022/see-snapshots-spring-blossoms-faculty-and-staff) 

[See faculty and staff fall\-inspired photo submissions in 2021](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/stories/october-13-2021/see-faculty-and-staff-fall-inspired-photo-submissions) 

Watch this space for the call for submissions\. We’ll be announcing it through the *UBC Today* weekly update in June\.
