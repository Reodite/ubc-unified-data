December 9, 2025

Faculty and staff are invited to nominate UBC community members in recognition of their professional and community contributions\. There are eight categories to recognize UBC alumni, faculty members, or community members committed to building an exceptional UBC\.

These awards recognize excellence, leadership, and innovation in achievements and contributions to the social, cultural, and economic well\-being of society, which positively reflect on the university\.

Find out about the awards, eligibility, criteria, and process and submit your nomination by February 13\.

[Learn more](https://alumni.ubc.ca/events/awards/)
