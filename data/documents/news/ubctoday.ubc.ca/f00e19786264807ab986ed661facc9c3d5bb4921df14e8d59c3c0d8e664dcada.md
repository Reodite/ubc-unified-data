August 11, 2025

As the new academic year gets underway, staff and faculty on both campuses are invited to the annual complementary BBQ lunch, where they can enjoy live entertainment and build connections with colleagues and community partners\.

UBC Okanagan Welcome Back BBQ and Info Fair will be held on Aug\. 26, and the UBC Vancouver Staff and Faculty BBQ will take place on Sept\. 10\.

Find out more: [UBC Vancouver](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/staffandfacultybbq/) \| [UBC Okanagan](https://hr.ok.ubc.ca/2025/06/27/celebrate-the-start-of-a-new-academic-year-at-the-2025-faculty-and-staff-welcome-back-bbq-info-fair-in-ubcos-20th-year/)
