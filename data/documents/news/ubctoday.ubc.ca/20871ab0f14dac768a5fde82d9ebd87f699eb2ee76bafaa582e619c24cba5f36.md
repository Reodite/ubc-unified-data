April 28, 2025

As a faculty or staff member, you can apply for a UBC Wellbeing Strategic Initiative Fund before June 4\. These funds are granted to departments and units in Vancouver and the Okanagan to support the university's commitment to the [*Okanagan Charter*](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/okanagan-charter) to become a health and wellbeing\-promoting institution, as outlined in UBC's [*Wellbeing Strategic Framework\.*](http://www.wellbeing.ubc.ca/framework)

**This year, $150,000 in funding is available to advance Wellbeing Strategic Framework priority areas, including two special focus areas:**

-   Supporting wellbeing in classroom environments
-   Advancing community and planetary wellbeing through health equity  

[Learn more](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/ubc-wellbeing-strategic-initiative-fund#:~:text=UBC%20Wellbeing%20Strategic%20Initiative%20Funds,in%20UBC&amp;apos;s%20Wellbeing%20Strategic%20Framework)
