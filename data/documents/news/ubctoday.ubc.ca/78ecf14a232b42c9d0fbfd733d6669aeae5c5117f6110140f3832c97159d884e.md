September 29, 2025

Help recognize outstanding colleagues who significantly impact teaching and learning at UBC Okanagan\. Nominations are now open for 2025/26 UBC Okanagan teaching awards\.

**Provost’s Award for Teaching Excellence and Innovation**  
  
This award recognizes up to two awards for faculty members with continuing appointments and one award for instructors holding a full or part time limited\-term appointment\. Nominations for this award are accepted from faculty members, deans, department heads, staff, students, and alumni\.

**Killam Teaching Prize**  
  
UBC Okanagan awards up to one Killam Teaching Prize each year to a faculty member with a continuing appointment who has distinguished themselves as an exemplary teacher with a demonstrated commitment to teaching excellence and innovation throughout their career\. Department heads or equivalents are invited to submit nominations for this award\.

Learn more about these prestigious awards which honour exemplary role models who embody teaching excellence in our community\.

The deadline to nominate teaching faculty members is November 7\. 

[Learn more](https://provost.ok.ubc.ca/awards-funding/teaching-awards/)
