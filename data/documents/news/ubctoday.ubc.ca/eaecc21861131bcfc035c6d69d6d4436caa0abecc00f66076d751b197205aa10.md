December 15, 2025

The Work Study program has $400,000 available in subsidies to enable faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan to create outstanding student employment opportunities\.

If you have a work opportunity to help students develop their professional skills and contribute to a work environment, submit a proposal to the Work Study program by **January 20, 2025**\. 

[Learn more](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/hire-a-student/work-study/)

Information about the Work Learn program at UBC Vancouver is available on the [Work Learn website](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-affairs/ubc-career-centre/work-learn)\.
