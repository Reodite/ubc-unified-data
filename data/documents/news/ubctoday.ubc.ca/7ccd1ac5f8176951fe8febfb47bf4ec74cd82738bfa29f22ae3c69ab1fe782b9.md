November 2, 2020

UBC’s Campus as a Living Lab Fund competition provides seed funding of up to $50,000 to support innovation projects on the Vancouver campus that address strategic sustainability priorities and embody the principles of a living lab\.

Submit your full proposal by **11:59pm on November 15, 2020**\.

[Learn more](https://sustain.ubc.ca/research/cll-fund-competition)
