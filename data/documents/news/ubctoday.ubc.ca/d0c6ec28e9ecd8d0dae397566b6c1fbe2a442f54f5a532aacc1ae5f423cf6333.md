January 4, 2023

47 community partners have received funding from UBC’s 2022 Partnership Recognition and Exploration fund\. This fund aims to support community\-university collaboration and relationship building and empower community partners to build reciprocal relationships with UBC students, faculty, and staff\.   

Receiving up to $1,500 each, this year’s projects are examples of how communities and UBC are working together to advance equity, diversity, and inclusion; Indigenous engagement and reconciliation; health innovation and community wellbeing; sustainability and climate action; and teaching, learning, and research excellence\.  

[Learn more about the recipients](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/home-page/2022-partnership-recognition-and-exploration-fund-recipients/)
