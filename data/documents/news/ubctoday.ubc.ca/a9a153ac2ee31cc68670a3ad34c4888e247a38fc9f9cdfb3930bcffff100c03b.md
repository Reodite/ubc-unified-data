October 14, 2020

You are invited to comment on a proposal to amend the Registrar/Librarians Appointment Policy \(AP11\), Academic Administrators Appointment Policy \(AP12\), and their associated Procedures\.

Please submit feedback to the Office of the University Counsel at [university\.counsel@ubc\.ca](mailto:university.counsel@ubc.ca) by **November 3, 2020**

[Learn more about the proposed policy changes](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/board-of-governors-policies-procedures-rules-and-guidelines/open-policy-consultations/proposed-amendments-ap11-and-ap12/)
