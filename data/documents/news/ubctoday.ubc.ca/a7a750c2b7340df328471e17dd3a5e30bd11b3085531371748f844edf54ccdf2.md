September 18, 2025

The ALT\-2040 Fund supports transformative change in teaching and learning to deliver UBC Okanagan's academic vision\.

Faculty members at UBC Okanagan are invited to submit proposals for program development, curriculum transformation, student experience enhancement and educational resource development projects on our campus\. The deadline to submit a proposal is December 15\.

Attend an [information session on Sept\. 24](https://events.ok.ubc.ca/event/2025-alt-2040-fund-information-session/), and find out more about the ALT\-2040 Fund priority focus areas, funding streams, how to apply, and more\.

[Learn more](https://alt-2040.ok.ubc.ca/)
