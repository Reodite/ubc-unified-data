April 25, 2022

Thanks to all those faculty and staff who submitted their [photos of spring blossoms](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/stories/april-19-2022/see-snapshots-spring-blossoms-faculty-and-staff)\. With fresh new blooms currently happening in BC’s interior, our colleagues in the Okanagan have asked for more opportunities to share their snapshots\. In response to their request we are doing a special additional call for photos\. Okanagan faculty and staff \(and anyone who didn’t get a chance to submit a photo\) are invited to share their spring\-inspired photos by May 4\.

A quick refresher of what do you need to do\.\.\.

-   Complete [this form](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/share-snapshot-spring-blossoms) and submit a photo that captures what the spring season means to you \(*max\. 2 photos per submitter*\)
-   Ensure your image is high resolution\.
-   Share relevant image credits\.
-   Write a caption that describes the photo\.

[Share your photos by Wednesday May 4](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/share-snapshot-spring-blossoms) 

[See the first round of spring blossom snapshots by faculty and staff](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/stories/april-19-2022/see-snapshots-spring-blossoms-faculty-and-staff)
