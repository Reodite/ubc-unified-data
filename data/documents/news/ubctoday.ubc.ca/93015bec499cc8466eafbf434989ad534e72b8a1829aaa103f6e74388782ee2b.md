January 3, 2023

If you are a faculty or staff member at UBC Vancouver, you can submit a proposal to hire temporary student employees through the summer 2023 \(May 1 – August 31, 2023\) Work Learn program\. The deadline is **January 23, 2023**\.

The Work Learn program funds approximately 3,500 part\-time hourly appointments each year, subsidizing meaningful work experiences for current UBC Vancouver students\.

Information and Resources

1.  View the Work Learn [Submission Guide](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/sites/facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/WL%20Submission%20Guide_0.pdf) for writing your proposal and the [step\-by\-step guide](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/sites/facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/WL%20Proposal%20Submission%20Guide.pdf)
2.  Bring your questions to Work Learn proposal office hours \| January 11, 11:00am \([zoom details](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5YsceqrrD8jE9ZyhJpev1lEbszpNaNm7Uuu)\), January 16, 12:00pm \([zoom details](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Mrd-mvpjoiHdI7hMJ-_AZSef70S8Bq3ACP)\) OR January 19, 1:00pm \([zoom details](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5UtcOqhrTssHdx07vVRgBHII1zfOd3OtTWT)\)
3.  Attend a webinar on writing a Work Learn proposal \| January 10, 11:00am \([zoom details](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u50pcu-hqTkiHtx30cUuJ8JTiu0Pqwdk2yjU)\) OR January 12, 1:00PM \([zoom details](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Isceitqj0oHt3CfhMtgmTexIU58Uv5EGqt)\)
