November 9, 2021

Elect two Faculty Pension Plan members to serve on the Board from Jan\. 1, 2022 to Dec\. 31, 2025\. 

**Vote online or by paper ballot by Nov\. 22**\. Members with a Campus\-Wide Login \(CWL\) username and password can vote online through the WebVote system\. Those who do not have a CWL are asked to submit a paper ballot\.
