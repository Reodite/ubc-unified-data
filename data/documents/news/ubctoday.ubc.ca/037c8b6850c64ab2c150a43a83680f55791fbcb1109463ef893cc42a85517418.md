October 11, 2022

If you know an individual or team who demonstrates excellence in mentorship, interprofessional collaboration, or health education, nominate them for a 2022 UBC Health award **by November 10\.**

The UBC Health awards recognize the academic and research excellence of faculty and staff from various health disciplines at the university, as well as the achievements of health educators, professionals, and community partners across BC\. 

Find more information on individual awards, scholarships, and nomination and application processes\. 

[Learn more](https://health.ubc.ca/awards-scholarships/all-awards-and-scholarships)
