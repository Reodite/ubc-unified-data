October 12, 2022

This November, UBC will be welcoming nearly 7,000 alumni from the classes of 2020 and 2021 on the Vancouver campus back to experience the in\-person graduation they missed during the pandemic, on top of celebrating the achievements of over 2,000 students during fall congregation\.

At UBC Okanagan, make\-up ceremonies will be held for graduates from the classes of Spring 2020 and 2021\.

A number of volunteering opportunities are available for faculty and staff\. Find out what you can do to show your support\.
