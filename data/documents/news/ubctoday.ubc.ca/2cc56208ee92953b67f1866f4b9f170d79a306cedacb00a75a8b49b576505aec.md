### Name:

Kimberly Gelera

### My Pronouns:

she/her

### Title:

Events Coordinator

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

Sauder School of Business

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2024

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

I build meaningful connections and bring communities together through events\.

---

### What work achievement are you most proud of?

I'm most proud of the systems I've built that help me manage multiple projects simultaneously while creating a seamless and effortless experience for the UBC Sauder community\. We've recently started using Asana, a project management software, where I've helped to create workflows that help our coordinators juggle multiple concurrent events without worrying about missing any tasks or deadlines\. I also stay on top of Workday updates to make sure our finance tracking stays current, and I am proactive in leading the documentation of our service standards and SOPs\. It's behind\-the\-scenes work, but it keeps our operations running smoothly and makes life easier for the whole team\.

---

### What three words would others use to describe you?

Creative: Always asking the questions that others may miss\.   
Efficient: A chaos manager handling multiple competing priorities\.   
Adaptable: A problem\-solver who always has the answers or finds them fast\.

---

### What are your hobbies and interests outside of work?

I'm passionate about dance, cultural experiences, and travel\. Bringing these interests together, I've co\-created an online community with over 100K followers\!
