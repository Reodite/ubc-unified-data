This message was sent to faculty and staff in the Okanagan\.

May 16, 2022

***A message from Rehan Sadiq, Provost and Vice\-President, Academic pro tem, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues,

The Aspire\-2040 Learning Transformations \(ALT\-2040\) Fund supports program development, curriculum transformations, student experience enhancement and educational resource development that accelerates the curricular vision in UBC’s strategic planning documents *Outlook 2040* and UBC’s Strategic Plan, *Shaping UBC’s Next Century*\.

I am pleased to announce the 2022 ALT\-2040 fund award recipients\. Projects are listed here with lead applicants\. We recognize, however, that many projects involve incredible teams of collaborators\. I encourage you to visit the [ALT\-2040 website](https://alt-2040.ok.ubc.ca/funded-projects/?utm_medium=email&amp;utm_campaign=UBC-Okanagan-Global-Message-%7C-&amp;utm_source=Envoke-UBCO-Global-and-supplem&amp;utm_term=UBC-Okanagan-Global-Message-%7C-#fundedprojects) to learn more about these innovative projects and the exceptional faculty and staff involved\.
