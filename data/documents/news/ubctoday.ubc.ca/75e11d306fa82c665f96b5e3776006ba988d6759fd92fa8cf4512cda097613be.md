    ## [How ‘parks for bugs’ boost pollinators in Vancouver](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/april-04-2025/how-parks-bugs-boost-pollinators-vancouver)
    
    As the days get longer and gardeners plan their spring planting, research from UBC offers some good news—small, simple changes to urban green spaces can make a big difference for pollinators\. The study found that reducing lawn mowing and creating pollinator meadows—think of them as ‘parks for bugs’—significantly boosts pollinator diversity, creating healthier and more resilient ecosystems\.Read more

    ## [UBC recognized as one of Canada's greenest employers in 2025](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/april-04-2025/ubc-recognized-one-canadas-greenest-employers-2025)
    
    For the 14th consecutive year, UBC has been named one of Canada’s Greenest Employers\. This special award recognizes Canadian employers that lead in creating a culture of environmental awareness, developing exceptional sustainability initiatives and attracting employees because of their environmental leadership\. Three reasons why UBC was recognized this year include environmental initiatives across both campuses, its Green Building Action Plan and the Sustainability Coordinator Program\.

    ## [Meet the 2025 recipients of UBC’s Community\-University Engagement Support Fund](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/april-04-2025/meet-2025-recipients-ubcs-community-university-engagement-support-fund)
