August 25, 2025

*Teaching in focus* highlights our talented teaching faculty, their unique teaching style, and their incredible work at UBC\.

If you are an educator, this is an opportunity to share what inspired your approach to teaching, acknowledge your collaborators, and the impact you hope to have on students\. *Teaching in focus* is quick and easy to complete, and may inspire and connect you to future educators in your field\.
