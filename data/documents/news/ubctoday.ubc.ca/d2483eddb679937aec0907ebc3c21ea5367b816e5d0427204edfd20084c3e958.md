February 9, 2022

***A message from Dr\. Rehan Sadiq, Provost and Vice\-President, Academic pro tem, UBC Okanagan; and Kate Ross, Associate Vice\-President, Enrolment Services and Registrar, UBC***

Dear colleagues,

We are pleased to announce that Bert Annear has been appointed Deputy Registrar for UBC Okanagan\. Bert brings extensive experience to the role, having worked within Enrolment Services at three universities in British Columbia, most recently as Registrar and Secretary to Senate at the University of Northern British Columbia \(UNBC\)\.

Over the course of his career, Bert has taken on increasingly more challenging roles, focused on building high\-performing teams to support the needs of the campus\. He is seen as a student advocate, a partner in establishing effective strategic enrolment management and a process improver—making the best use of technology to enhance the student experience\.
