    ## [New gravitational wave signal helps fill the ‘mass gap’ between neutron stars and black holes](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/april-04-2024/new-gravitational-wave-signal-helps-fill-mass-gap-between-neutron-stars-and)
    
    A collaboration of researchers including UBC scientists have observed gravitational waves from the collision of what is most likely a neutron star and an object likely to be a light black hole, 650 million light\-years from Earth\.The mass of the black hole is 2\.5 to 4\.5 times the mass of Earth’s sun, meaning it falls in the so\-called ‘mass gap’: heavier than heaviest known and theorized neutron stars but lighter than the lightest black holes in our galaxy\.

    ## [Open Education Resources Excellence and Impact Award recipients announced](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/march-03-2024/open-education-resources-excellence-and-impact-award-recipients-announced)
    
    The OER Excellence and Impact Awards recognize outstanding work by faculty in Vancouver and the Okanagan who materially advance the use and impact of open educational resources \(OER\) in credit courses at UBC\.The recipients were selected based on their overall excellence in creating, revising or using OER in teaching and learning; the impact of their OER work on students, including addressing the affordability of educational materials; and their contribution to the greater open education community at UBC\.
