UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

September 24, 2026

I am pleased to announce that UBC has embarked on a search for a new Vice\-President, Students\.  
  
The Vice\-President, Students, plays a central role in shaping the student experience at UBC, providing leadership across a broad range of services and programs that support students throughout their time at the university\. The portfolio serves more than 73,000 undergraduate and graduate students across both campuses, with responsibilities spanning student affairs, housing and community services, health and wellbeing, athletics and recreation, and career development\.  
  
Reporting to the President, and part of the President’s Executive Team, the next Vice\-President, Students will work closely with leaders across both UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan to strengthen the student experience and implement the new [Student Strategic Plan](https://usend.ubc.ca/sendy/l/wjhSJ763Owyy27Pp69EIUlLA/RN82ytQh6jCa1AOfpcSaRg/Axp40tRpDzrQEw6kyx3JQQ)\. We are seeking a collaborative and experienced leader who can bring a university\-wide perspective to decisions that affect students across both campuses to enhance the campus experience, lead revenue generation through housing, conferences, events and parking, and support the health and wellbeing of our students\. Further information on this wide\-ranging portfolio can be found [online](https://usend.ubc.ca/sendy/l/wjhSJ763Owyy27Pp69EIUlLA/1ErtHO5Ilwq3ujEmeQp763hw/Axp40tRpDzrQEw6kyx3JQQ)\.  
  
In accordance with Board Policy AP14, an Advisory Committee has been established to support the search process\. The committee consists of:

-   Benoit\-Antoine Bacon, President and Vice\-Chancellor \(Chair\)
-   Philipp Reichert, UBC Board of Governors
-   Adam Charania, Vice\-President, Human Resources
-   Marie Tarrant, Provost and Vice\-President, Academic *pro tem*, UBC Okanagan
-   Lalitha Raman\-Wilms, Dean, Pharmaceutical Sciences
-   Hsingchi von Bergmann, UBC Vancouver Senate
-   Julien Picault, UBC Okanagan Senate
-   Jaiya Panchi, UBC Vancouver Student
-   Olivia Lai, UBC Okanagan Student
-   Janice Stewart, Deputy Provost and Associate Vice\-Provost, Academic, UBC Vancouver
-   Shannon Dunn, Associate Vice\-President, Student Housing and Community Services
-   Noorjean Hassam, Associate Vice\-President, Student Health and Wellbeing
-   Brian Heathcote, Finance Director, VP Students
-   Kirsten Nicholson, Executive Director, President’s Office \(Secretary\)

We are supported in this search by Griffith Group and we welcome your perspectives on the priorities and qualities that should inform this appointment\. You can share comments confidentially with Griffith Group at: Jane Griffith \([jane@griffithgroup\.ca](mailto:jane@griffithgroup.ca)\) and/or Caroline McLean \([caroline@griffithgroup\.ca](mailto:caroline@griffithgroup.ca)\) until October 31, 2026\.  
  
***Benoit\-Antoine Bacon***  
*President and Vice\-Chancellor*
