June 3, 2024

As a faculty or staff member, you can apply for a UBC Wellbeing Strategic Initiative Fund before July 9\. These funds are granted to departments and units in Vancouver and the Okanagan to support the university's commitment to the [*Okanagan Charter*](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/okanagan-charter) to become a health and wellbeing\-promoting institution, as outlined in UBC's [*Wellbeing Strategic Framework\.*](http://www.wellbeing.ubc.ca/framework) 

**This year, $120,000 in funding is available to advance three funding streams:** 

-   Supporting Wellbeing in Classroom Environments
-   Advancing Community and Planetary Wellbeing through Health Equity
-   Celebrating 10 Years of UBC Wellbeing 

[Find out more](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/ubc-wellbeing-strategic-initiative-fund#)
