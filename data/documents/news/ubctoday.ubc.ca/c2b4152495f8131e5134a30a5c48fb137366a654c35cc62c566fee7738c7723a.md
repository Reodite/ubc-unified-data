    ## [Minutes matter most when exercising to control blood sugar](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/april-04-2026/minutes-matter-most-when-exercising-control-blood-sugar)
    
    A recent study from UBC Okanagan suggests that results depend less on how you exercise and more on how long you keep moving—especially for people newly diagnosed with Type 2 diabetes \(T2D\)Researchers found that longer exercise sessions—especially early in an exercise program—were the strongest predictor of improvements in blood sugar control among inactive adults who were newly diagnosed with T2D\.
