January 20, 2025

Don't forget to use available [professional development](https://hr.ubc.ca/career-development/professional-development-funding) \(PD\) funding and submit claims in Workday by **Feb\. 28**\. 

PD funds are intended to support learning activities that will enhance your workplace knowledge or skills and support your career growth at the university\. Claims will be accepted in Workday on a first\-come, first\-served basis, until the annual funding pool is exhausted or by Feb\. 28, whichever comes first\.
