October 27, 2025

### Name:

Khushbu Parikh

### My Pronouns:

She/her

### Title:

Senior Programmer Analyst

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

UBC IT/Enterprise Automation and Solution Delivery

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2022

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

I bridge the gap between developers and management, driving technical direction and successful deliveries\.

---

### What is the best thing about your work/role at UBC?

The best part of my role is solving technical problems with team leadership\. I get the opportunity to design systems, guide technical decisions, as well as help the team grow while delivering high\-impact solutions\. It’s rewarding to see a well\-coordinated team solve complex challenges and bring ideas to life\.

---

### What is your favourite quote and why?

"Great vision without great people is irrelevant\." — by Jim Collins, author of *Good to Great*  
  
Because having the best ideas, strategies, or goals, but without the right people — aligned, motivated, skilled — cannot bring that vision to life\. The focus shouldn't be just about setting direction, it's about building and empowering the right team to get there\.

---

### What are your hobbies and interests outside of work?

In my spare time, I love painting and working on DIY crafting projects\. Recently, I’ve also taken up Taekwondo, which has been an exciting new challenge to push my physical limits\.
