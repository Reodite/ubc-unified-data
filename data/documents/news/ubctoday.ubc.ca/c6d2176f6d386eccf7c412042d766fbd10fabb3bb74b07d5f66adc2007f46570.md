November 24, 2021

The [Work Study program](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/hire-a-student/work-study/) offers $400,000 in minimum wage subsidies, to help faculty and staff create outstanding student employment opportunities at UBC Okanagan\. Funding is awarded to eligible employers via a competitive application and adjudication process\.

If you have a work opportunity to help students develop their professional skills and contribute to a work environment, submit a proposal to the Work Study program by **January 17, 2022**\. 

[Learn more](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/hire-a-student/work-study/)
