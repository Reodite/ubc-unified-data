January 7, 2026

In another significant step towards building a more inclusive community, 41 new equity and anti\-racism projects have been funded across the Vancouver and Okanagan campuses through the fall 2025 cycle of the Strategic Equity &amp; Anti\-Racism \(StEAR\) Enhancement Fund\.

These initiatives, led by students, faculty, and staff, focus on dismantling systemic barriers and fostering a sense of belonging through learning, research, and campus life\. The projects cover a broad range of topics, including:

-   Disability equity
-   Racial equity
-   Gender and 2SLGBTQIA\+ equity
-   Intersectional approaches

While we celebrate these new projects, we are also looking ahead\. 

Faculty and staff can now apply for the next round of the StEAR Fund until Feb\. 8\. This is a chance to secure funding for departmental or unit\-level initiatives that advance equity and anti\-racism goals\.
