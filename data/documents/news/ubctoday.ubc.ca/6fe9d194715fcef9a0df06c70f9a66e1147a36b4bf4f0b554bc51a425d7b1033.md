April 20, 2021

***A message from Lesley Cormack, Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues,

I am pleased to invite you to our virtual Spring Town Hall on Thursday, May 13, from 10 to 11:30 a\.m\. This town hall provides us with an opportunity to come together and share in UBC Okanagan’s many remarkable achievements and notable developments over the past year, as well as provide an update on where we’re heading as a university\.  

I know there are many questions surrounding resumption of in\-person classes and our return to campus this fall\. At this town hall, I will provide an update on current administrative plans for resumption, recognizing that we are still in the planning stages with guidance from the Provincial Health Officer and Interior Health\. I am also pleased to announce that the Office of the Provost will be holding additional town hall events in May and June that will share survey data regarding student preferences and concerns about the return to in\-person learning as well as preliminary information about returning to campus and in\-person teaching and learning in Fall 2021\.
