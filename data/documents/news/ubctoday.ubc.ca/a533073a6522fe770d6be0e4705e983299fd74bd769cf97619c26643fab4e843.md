This message was sent to deans, directors, managers or heads of academic or administrative units, or as members of the Okanagan Campus Communicators Network\.

November 5, 2020

***A message from Shelley Kayfish, Director, Campus Operations and Risk Management, Co\-chair, UBC Okanagan Resumption Planning and Coordination Committee; Michael Burgess, Associate Provost, Strategy, UBC Okanagan, Co\-chair, UBC Okanagan Resumption Planning and Coordination Committee\.***

On behalf of the Resumption Planning and Coordination Committee and its working group, thank you for your continued support of UBC Okanagan’s COVID\-19 safety planning process—your commitment is truly appreciated\. 

We want to provide a brief update on topics of particular interest to leaders in faculties and administrative units at UBCO\. There is a great deal of online information about UBC’s COVID\-19 response, and the following is intended to raise additional awareness of a few key items\.
