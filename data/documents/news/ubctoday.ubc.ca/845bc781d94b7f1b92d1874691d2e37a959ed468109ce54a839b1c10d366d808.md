August 27, 2021

In partnership with UBC Okanagan, Interior Health is offering free, drop\-in COVID\-19 vaccination on campus on the following dates:

**August 30:** 11:30 a\.m\. to 3:30 p\.m\.  
**September 8:** 11:30 a\.m\. to 3:30 p\.m\.  
**September 17:** 11:30 a\.m\. to 3:30 p\.m\.

Until September 7, students can also access express\-line service at the off\-campus Trinity Hall immunization clinic in Kelowna\.

[Find out more](https://ok.ubc.ca/covid19/vaccines)
