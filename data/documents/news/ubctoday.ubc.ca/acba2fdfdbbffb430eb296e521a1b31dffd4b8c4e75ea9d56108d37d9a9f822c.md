November 6, 2023

The Developing Leaders Program – which has evolved from the Managing at UBC Program – is now taking applications from eligible managers\.

The program is aimed at equipping early\-career managers in Vancouver and Okanagan campuses with the skills and knowledge required to lead with more confidence and effectiveness\.

The deadline for applications is noon on Nov\. 24 and the program will begin on Jan\. 25, 2024\.
