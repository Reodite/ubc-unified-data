    ## [Nitobe Memorial Garden: A garden that bridges worlds](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/october-10-2025/nitobe-memorial-garden-garden-bridges-worlds)
    
    At first light, the Nitobe Memorial Garden breathes in silence\. Dew settles on stone lanterns and soft moss\. The pond lies still, a mirror for the pale sky\. Among the maples, a figure moves slowly, checking branches, brushing leaves from a path and pausing to listen\. “That’s my favourite moment,” says Ryo Sugiyama, the garden curator\. “It’s so quiet when it’s just me and the garden before anyone comes in\. I can see the work I’ve done and the work I should do\.”
