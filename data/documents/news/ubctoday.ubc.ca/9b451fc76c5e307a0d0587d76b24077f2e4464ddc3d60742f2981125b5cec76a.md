September 23, 2025

Join us for an Intergenerational March in UBC Vancouver to commemorate Orange Shirt Day on **Tuesday, Sept\. 30**\. 

Members of the UBC community, families and those in solidarity are welcome to participate\. The march aims to be educational and supportive to the Indigenous community on campus and beyond\.

For information on how you can get involved at UBC Okanagan, [click here](https://ok.ubc.ca/about/indigenous-engagement/orange-shirt-day/)\.
