UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers in Vancouver\.

April 12, 2023

UBC is embarking on a search for a new Vice\-Provost and Associate Vice\-President, Teaching and Learning\.  
  
This position oversees several administrative units, including the Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology \(CTLT\), UBC Extended Learning \(ExL\), the Academic Director of the Learning Exchange, the Academic Integrity Hub, and other initiatives related to enhancing teaching and learning at UBC\.  
  
The Vice\-Provost and Associate Vice\-President, Teaching and Learning, will advance experiential learning programs, work closely with students, faculty and staff to develop and implement innovative approaches to teaching and learning, and focus on enhancing student learning outcomes\. The individual in this position oversees Continuing Education, with responsibilities across both campuses\.  
  
Full details of the position and how to apply can be found [here](https://academic.ubc.ca/vice-provost-avp-teaching-learning)\.  
  
If you know of any faculty in your area of the university who you think would be a good fit for this role, please share this opportunity and encourage them to apply\. The call for applications will remain open until May 2, 2023\.

**Gage Averill**  
Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver
