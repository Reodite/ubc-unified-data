Humanity is set to make a return to the Moon with the Artemis program, in what NASA says is a first step to Mars\. So, who gets first dibs?

Dr\. Aaron Boley, a professor in UBC’s Department of Physics &amp; Astronomy, discusses the mission’s plans and why we need to sort out access and resource rights before we return to the lunar surface\.
