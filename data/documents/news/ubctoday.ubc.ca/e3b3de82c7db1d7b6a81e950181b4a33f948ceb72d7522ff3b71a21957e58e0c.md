June 16, 2025

The 30th United Nations Climate Change Conference \(COP30\) will take place in Belém, Brazil, from Nov\. 10–21\. The Sustainability Hub is assembling a virtual delegation to witness official negotiations and take part in this year's event\. 

Submit your application by July 8\. 

[Learn more](https://sustain.ubc.ca/cop30)
