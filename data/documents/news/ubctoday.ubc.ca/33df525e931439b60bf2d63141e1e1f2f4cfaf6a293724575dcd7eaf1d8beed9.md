February 10, 2026

This designation is awarded through [BC’s Top Employers](https://www.canadastop100.com/bc/), an annual competition organized by the editors of [Canada’s Top 100](https://www.canadastop100.com/) Employers, recognizing organizations that lead their industries as exceptional places to work\. 2026 marks the 21st year of the competition and the 19th year UBC has been selected as a BC’s Top Employer\.
