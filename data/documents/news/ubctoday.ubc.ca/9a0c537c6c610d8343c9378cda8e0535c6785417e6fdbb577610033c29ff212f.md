April 29, 2026

Are you a UBC Vancouver or Okanagan faculty member interested in integrating climate change and wellbeing content into your existing undergraduate or graduate course?

The Climate Change and Wellbeing Education Grant program aims to improve the depth and delivery of content at the intersection of climate change, health, and wellbeing\. Up to $6,000 in funding is now available through the Climate Change and Wellbeing Education Grants to help you develop new course materials, host consultation workshops, and create new teaching resources that focus on the connections between climate change and human health and wellbeing\.

Browse past projects, learn about eligibility, and find out about the application timeline\. 

[Apply by June 5](https://sustain.ubc.ca/education/climate-change-and-wellbeing-education-grants)

Funding is offered via a partnership between the Office of Wellbeing Strategy and the Sustainability Hub\.
