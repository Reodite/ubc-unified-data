October 19, 2020

As a reminder, currently funded [Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund](https://tlef.ubc.ca/) \(TLEF\) project teams are invited to submit renewal applications for second and third\-year funding for their projects, by **3pm on Thursday, November 12, 2020**\.

Learn more about the [TLEF program](https://tlef.ubc.ca/) or contact [tlef\.admin@ubc\.ca](mailto:tlef.admin@ubc.ca)\.
