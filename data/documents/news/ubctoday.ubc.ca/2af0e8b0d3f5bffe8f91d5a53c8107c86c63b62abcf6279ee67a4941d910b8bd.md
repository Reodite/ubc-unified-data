November 12, 2025

If you bike to the UBC Vancouver campus, there's still time left to avail these offers:

-   [Try an e\-bike for free for two weeks](https://planning.ubc.ca/e-bike)\. The program runs until end\-November\.
-   You could receive a $400 rebate \(while supplies last\) with the purchase of an e\-bike for commuting to and from the Vancouver campus\. This pilot program is open until Dec\. 31\. [Learn more](https://planning.ubc.ca/e-bike-subsidy)

If you are based in UBC Okanagan, explore [e\-bike and e\-scooter rental discounts](https://campusplanning.ok.ubc.ca/transportation/travel-options/cycling/bike-share/) for faculty and staff\.
