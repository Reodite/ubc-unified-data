October 21, 2024

Are you seeking certification as a professional coach and want to apply your new coaching skills and knowledge to working with fellow UBC staff and faculty?

The Coaching Internship program gives faculty and staff valuable experience in coaching their peers in addition to professional development, mentorship and $1,500 towards the tuition cost of the accredited coach training program of your choice\. Applications are due Dec\. 2\.  

Please note, participants in this internship must be enrolled or planning to enrol in an International Coaching Federation accredited Level 2 Program\.

You can join an information session to learn about coaching as well as the internship program details and timelines, followed by a Q&amp;A\. 

**Information session:** Nov\. 1, 2024: [Register now](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Uudu-opjgoGdbCz220YM5O8PFZ6kcj98OX#/registration) 

[Find out more](https://hr.ubc.ca/career-development/coaching-staff-and-faculty/coaching-internship-program)
