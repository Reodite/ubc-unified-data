April 8, 2024

UBC’s Spring 2024 graduation ceremonies will take place on the Vancouver campus from May 22\-30 and on the Okanagan campus from June 6\-7 with more than 12,000 students celebrating this important milestone\.

Get involved by volunteering to support the event\. As a volunteer, you will play an instrumental role in making the experience memorable for everyone\.

A number of volunteering opportunities are available for faculty and staff during the graduation ceremonies on both campuses and at the Alumni Welcome Receptions at the Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre\.

Find out what you can do to show your support at the various locations\.

[Vancouver ceremonies](https://graduation.ubc.ca/volunteer/)

[Okanagan ceremonies](https://graduation.ok.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/#volunteers)

[Vancouver Alumni Centre welcome receptions](https://alumni.ubc.ca/2024/featured-volunteer-opportunity/volunteer-at-graduation/)
