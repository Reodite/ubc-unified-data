UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers, and senior professional leaders in Vancouver and the Okanagan\.

August 19, 2024

I am pleased to announce that UBC has embarked on a search for a new Vice\-President, Human Resources\.  
  
Adam Charania is currently Interim Vice\-President, Human Resources\. He was appointed to the role in April of this year, following the departure of the former Vice\-President, Marcia Buchholz\.  
  
The Vice\-President, Human Resources, reports to the President and has responsibility for providing strategic leadership for the Human Resources \(HR\) portfolio as well as the broader human resource professional community of the university\.  
  
The HR portfolio includes people and workplace culture; organizational development and workplace learning; faculty and staff relations; compensation and pensions; recruitment and equity and inclusion; as well as financial and administrative management\.  
  
In accordance with Board Policy AP14, an advisory committee has been established to support the search process\. The committee consists of:

-   Benoit\-Antoine Bacon, President \(Chair\)
-   Natalie Chan, UBC Board of Governors
-   Frank Laezza, Vice\-President, Finance and Operations
-   Mark Maclachlan, Dean *pro tem*, Faculty of Science
-   Joanne Fox, UBC Vancouver Senate
-   Shirley Chau, UBC Okanagan Senate
-   Danial Asif, UBC Students' Union Okanagan
-   Christian Kyle, Alma Mater Society, UBC Vancouver
-   Michael Vizsolyi, Interim Associate Vice\-President, Strategic Advisory Partnerships and Support Services
-   Mark Trowell, Executive Director, Faculty Relations &amp; ALDP
-   Alexandra Bayne, Managing Director, Human Resources, UBC Okanagan
-   Janice Stewart, Deputy Provost, UBC Vancouver

A member of the President’s Office will act as non\-voting secretary\. We are also supported in this search by PFM Executive Search\. If you have any feedback for this search, please send your comments to the confidential email [president\.staff@ubc\.ca](mailto:president.staff@ubc.ca)\.

**Benoit\-Antoine Bacon**  
President and Vice\-Chancellor
