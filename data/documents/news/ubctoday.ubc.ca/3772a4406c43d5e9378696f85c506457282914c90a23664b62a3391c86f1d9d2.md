    ## [Call for applications at UBC Vancouver: Campus as a Living Lab Fund competition](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/august-08-2025/call-applications-ubc-vancouver-campus-living-lab-fund-competition)
    
    The Campus as a Living Lab Fund Competition offers up to $500,000 in total seed funding to support innovative, interdisciplinary projects led by UBC Vancouver faculty and staff\. Projects should advance sustainability through academic–operational collaboration and use the campus as a testbed for real\-world impact\.Each project must have two co\-leads—one faculty and one staff—from different departments, disciplines or units, who will co\-develop and co\-direct the project\.Attend an upcoming information session by the Sustainability Hub and submit a Notice of Intent by Sept\. 30\.
