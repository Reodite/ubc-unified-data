This message was sent to all faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan on behalf of the Office of the Provost and Vice\-President, Academic\.

February 25, 2022

***A message from Dr\. Rehan Sadiq, Provost and Vice\-President Academic pro tem, UBC Okanagan; and Michael Burgess, Associate Provost, Strategy, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues,

From September 2021 to January 2022 we engaged in a strategic review of the campus’ health programming and activities\. The intention of the review was to determine an overall approach to developing a sustainable health strategy for our campus that will inform diverse outcomes, such as faculty recruitment, program development, integrating health\-related research and teaching, and others\.

The timing of this review was ideal, particularly given UBC Health’s release of its [strategic plan](https://health.ubc.ca/strategicplan) in 2021, alongside tremendous expansion of health\-related activities and programming across all Faculties at UBC Okanagan since the campus’ inception\.

We are pleased to share with you the report on the [External Review of Health Related Research, Teaching and Engagement](https://provost.ok.ubc.ca/initiatives/quality-assurance-and-enhancement/strategic-reviews/)*,* prepared by three external reviewers\.
