### HR support

### Recruiting

-   UBCV
    
    ## [Hiring Solutions at UBC Vancouver](https://hr.ubc.ca/managers-admins/recruiting-and-hiring/ubc-hiring-solutions)
    
    For temporary staffing and managed recruitment services
    
-   UBCO
    
    ## [Hiring student employees at UBC Okanagan](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/hire-a-student/)
    
    For more on the different types of student employment opportunities
    
-   UBCV
    
    ## [Hiring student employees at UBC Vancouver](https://hr.ubc.ca/managers-admins/recruiting-and-hiring/student-employees/hiring-student-employee)
    
    For more on the different types of student employment opportunities
    
-   UBCV &amp; UBCO
    
    ## [Inclusive recruiting with Textio](https://hr.ubc.ca/managers-admins/recruiting-and-hiring/inclusive-recruiting)
    
    Learn how to write inclusive, bias\-free job descriptions and postings with Textio\. Log in the Textio with your CWL to find out how you can enhance your recruitment materials\.
    
-   UBCV &amp; UBCO
    
    ## [Onboarding new faculty and staff](https://hr.ubc.ca/managers-admins/onboarding-new-employees)
    
    For information to help you onboard new faculty and staff, including everything from criminal record checks to relocation funding
    
-   UBCV &amp; UBCO
    
    ## [Promoting faculty, graduate student, and postdoctoral positions](https://faculty-staff.grad.ubc.ca/recruitment/job-ads)
    
    Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies \(G\+PS\) offers a service that helps promote your posting on a number of external career portals, for free\.
    
-   UBCO
    
    ## [Recruiting and hiring staff at UBC Okanagan](https://hr.ok.ubc.ca/working-at-ubc/manager-administrator-resources/hiring-review-process/)
    
    Information about the hiring review process
    
-   UBCV
    
    ## [Recruiting and hiring staff at UBC Vancouver](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/administrators/recruiting/)
    
    For information to support you in the hiring process
    
-   UBCV &amp; UBCO
    
    ## [Recruiting faculty](https://academic.ubc.ca/faculty-life/recruiting-supporting-top-faculty-students)
    
    For resources to support you when recruiting a new faculty member
    

### Managing

-   UBCV &amp; UBCO
    
    ## [Accommodation\-relevant observances](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/days-of-significance-calendar/)
    
    Plan ahead and consult a list of observances that may give rise to accommodation requests from students, faculty and staff
    
-   UBCV &amp; UBCO
    
    ## [Managing staff](https://www.hr.ubc.ca/administrators/managing-staff/)
    
    For tools and resources to support you with the most common questions you may face as a manager of staff
    
-   UBCV
    
    ## [Managing student employees at UBC Vancouver](https://hr.ubc.ca/managers-admins/recruiting-and-hiring/student-employees/managing-student-employees)
    
    For information to support you when managing student employees
    
-   UBCV &amp; UBCO
    
    ## [Mandatory training for new faculty and staff](https://srs.ubc.ca/training/required-training-for-all-ubc-workers/)
    
    For an overview of the courses that are mandatory for faculty and staff
    
-   UBCV &amp; UBCO
    
    ## [Team and professional development](https://hr.ubc.ca/managers-admins/team-and-professional-development)
    
    For opportunities to support your team’s development, as well as individual opportunities for faculty and staff
