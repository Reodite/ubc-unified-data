This message was sent to all faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan on behalf of the Office of the Vice\-Principal, Research and Innovation\.

November 8, 2021

***A message from Phil Barker, Vice\-Principal and Associate Vice\-President, Research and Innovation***

Each year, UBC Okanagan recognizes significant contributions to research and/or creative scholarly activity\. 

If you know of an outstanding faculty member or graduate student who has made a significant contribution to research during their time on our campus, I invite you to nominate them for a Researcher of the Year Award\.
