UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers, and senior professional leaders in Vancouver and the Okanagan\.

March 29, 2021

UBC’s 12th annual Celebrate Learning Week will take place online from May 17\-23\. This year’s theme is *Shaping Our Future*, and we’d like to encourage all to take part by attending and/or hosting an event\.   
  
Celebrate Learning Week is an opportunity to recognize all the hard work, talent, and teaching and learning achievements at UBC\. Throughout the week, UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan will be working together to host a series of online events, including open lectures, information sessions, student advising activities, poster sessions and workshops\.   
  
**Call for contributions**  
If you are interested in hosting an event, we’re inviting contributions that demonstrate innovation in online, blended or other teaching practices; student experiences; equity, diversity and inclusion; open educational resources; and wellbeing\. Please use the link below to submit your event before noon on **Friday, April 16**: [http://celebratelearning\.ubc\.ca/events/submission\-form/](http://celebratelearning.ubc.ca/events/submission-form/)\.  
  
We look forward to your participation in the 2021 Celebrate Learning Week as we highlight UBC’s innovative and diverse learning opportunities\. For more information, visit [https://celebratelearning\.ubc\.ca/](https://celebratelearning.ubc.ca/)\.  
 

**Andrew Szeri**  
Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver  
  
**Ananya Mukherjee Reed**  
Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Okanagan
