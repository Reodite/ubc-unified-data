UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers, and senior professional leaders in Vancouver and the Okanagan\.

March 12, 2025

The UBC community is invited to comment on the draft of the new joint policy of the Okanagan and Vancouver Senates, *Integrated Evaluation of Teaching*, by April 4, 2025\.  
  
The policy is applicable to all course instructors and is designed to ensure a broadly consistent use of an integrated approach to the evaluation of teaching, while also supporting flexibility where needed\. The policy stipulates that the evaluation of teaching will include data from at least three sources: peer review, student feedback, and self\-reflection\.  
  
For more information and to share your feedback on this policy, visit one of the sites below:  
[**UBC Okanagan**](https://senate.ubc.ca/okanagan/consultations-surveys/consultation-policy-on-integrated-evaluation-of-teaching/)  
[**UBC Vancouver**](https://senate.ubc.ca/vancouver/consultations/consultation-policy-on-integrated-evaluation-of-teaching/)  
 

**Rella Ng**  
Associate Vice\-President, Enrolment Services and Registrar
