May 1, 2023

If your role involves student administrative functions, participate in Integrated Renewal Program \- Student’s second Institutional Readiness Assessment\. 

This 10\-minute survey will help UBC assess how ready the community is for the adoption of Workday Student\.

The survey completion deadline is May 19\.

[Complete the survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6fKwdE6noVQQILA)

[Learn more about the Integrated Renewal Program \- Student](https://irp.ubc.ca/)
