    ## [Dr\. Mary Jung appointed Scientific Director of CIHR Institute of Nutrition, Metabolism and Diabetes](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/november-11-2025/dr-mary-jung-appointed-scientific-director-cihr-institute-nutrition)
    
    UBC Okanagan researcher Dr\. Mary Jung is taking over leadership of one of the 13 institutes that make up the Canadian Institutes of Health Research \(CIHR\), enabling her—and UBCO—to play a key role in advancing health research across the country\. Dr\. Jung, a Professor in the School of Health and Exercise Sciences, was appointed Scientific Director of the CIHR Institute of Nutrition, Metabolism and Diabetes \(INMD\) for a four\-year term, beginning January 1, 2026\.
