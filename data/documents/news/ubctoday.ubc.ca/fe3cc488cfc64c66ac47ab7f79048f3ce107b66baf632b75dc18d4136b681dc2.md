    ## [Tips on how to digitally declutter this December](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/december-12-2020/tips-how-digitally-declutter-december)
    
    With many faculty and staff transitioning to remote working and teaching in 2020, there has been an increase in emails exchanged and files shared\. Privacy Matters @ UBC and the University Records Management Office offer tips and strategies to help you and your team declutter digital files during December\.  During this month, work together as a team to clean up transitory email records, purge and organize shared files to increase efficiency and reduce storage costs\.
