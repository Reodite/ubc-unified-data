March 20, 2023

Recognize the outstanding achievements of your colleagues in Vancouver who have demonstrated their commitment to reducing climate change, reversing biodiversity loss, transforming to circular economies and creating more resilient communities\. The deadline to submit your nomination is April 3\.

All submissions will be reviewed against the following criteria:

-   Innovation
-   Initiative
-   Leadership
-   Inclusive communities

Award recipients will be recognized at the [Sustainability Celebration](https://sustain.ubc.ca/events/sustainability-celebration) on April 21\.
