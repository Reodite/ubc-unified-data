May 30, 2022

The Work Learn program funds approximately 3,500 part\-time hourly appointments each year, subsidizing meaningful work experiences for current UBC Vancouver students\. If you're looking to build capacity in your team to advance departmental priorities or are in need of additional support, submit a proposal to hire temporary student employees through UBC Vancouver’s Work Learn program\. Proposals for winter 2022 \(September 1, 2022 – April 30, 2023\) will be accepted until **4pm on June 20**\.

Information and resources are available to support you:

-   View the Work Learn [Submission Guide](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/sites/facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/WL%20W21%20Submission%20Guide.pdf) for writing your proposal and the [step\-by\-step guide](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/sites/facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/WL%20Proposal%20Submission%20Guide.pdf)\.
-   Register for Work Learn Proposal Office Hours:
    -   [June 6](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5EvcuutpjwrHtwTtTaFcE8TBqQW4UBrSYue), 1–2pm 
    -   [June 15](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Uof-mrrz4rGdYaGza0D2XSos-DC1qLmFUu), 10–11am 
    -   [June 16](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5YrcOurrDMrE9NODSPE28-y28Bg0gHMEaBi), 1–2pm 
-   Attend a webinar on writing a Work Learn proposal:
    -   [June 8](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5UtdO6qrjIrGdN1C9H-U7f4UwPvOX2fW-QH), 11am–noon
    -   [June 13](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Asdu6qqzkqGtXCCrLnE1fBTmWRpAExM79f), 1–2pm

More information about the Work Learn program is available on the [Work Learn website](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-engagement/centre-student-involvement-careers/work-learn)\.

Information about the Work Study program at UBC Okanagan, such as program goals and key dates, are available on the [Work Study website](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/hire-a-student/work-study/)\.
