November 12, 2020

The Aspire\-2040 Learning Transformations \(ALT\-2040\) Fund's main goal is to transform student learning at UBC Okanagan\.

ALT\-2040 supports curriculum change, innovative teaching practices and learning environment enhancement\. The fund enables faculty members to make strategic changes within existing, or implement new, undergraduate and graduate academic programs that are more flexible, interdisciplinary, experiential and/or collaborative\. 

Faculty members are invited to submit proposals by **January 15, 2021\.**

[Learn more](https://alt-2040.ok.ubc.ca/alt-2040-fund-call-2021/)
