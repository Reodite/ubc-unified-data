July 8, 2024

In partnership with UBC, Samsung R&amp;D Institute Canada invites proposals for research projects delivering innovative outcomes in the areas of:

-   Preventative health solutions
-   Data security
-   Wearable devices

Letters of intent due are Aug\. 1\.

A CWL is required to access full details and the application process\.
