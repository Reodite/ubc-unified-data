September 15, 2025

Grants worth up to $500 are available for faculty and staff in Vancouver and the Okanagan who wish to host events that promote mental health during November, which is [Thrive](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/thrive) month at UBC\. 

During the annual, month\-long mental health initiative, the UBC community is encouraged to learn about, talk about, and explore ways to support our mental health\.

Applications for Thrive grants are open until Oct\. 1\. Funds will be allocated on a rolling basis until the total available funds are exhausted\.

[Read more](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/getinvolvedthrive)
