The *UBC Today* website is the home for the latest updates, news and announcements for faculty and staff, and stories about the people who make up our community\.

If you have something timely and relevant to share with UBC’s working community, please submit it to us for consideration\. We welcome content submissions from all faculty and staff from across UBC’s campuses and learning sites\.

Please use this [UBC Today checklist for faculty and staff](https://comm-mar3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/04/UBC-Today-checklist-for-faculty-and-staff.pdf) to learn more about our content submission guidelines\.

## Share your content

If you have any of the following to share with faculty and/or staff:

-   A timely update
-   A notable achievement
-   A notable appointment
-   An opportunity for staff and/or faculty to provide feedback
-   A story about the interesting work a faculty and/or staff member is doing
-   A useful resource
-   An important event

[Submit your content](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/suggest-content "Suggest content")

## Talk to us about a potential story idea

Let us know if you or someone you know is doing something inside or outside of work that you feel would be relevant and/or of interest to the broader faculty and staff community\.
