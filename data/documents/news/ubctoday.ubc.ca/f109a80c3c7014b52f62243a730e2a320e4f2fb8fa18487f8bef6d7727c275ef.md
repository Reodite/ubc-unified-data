June 14, 2021

In May 2021, the BC government amended the Employment Standards Act to provide three days of COVID\-19 paid sick leave\. This new COVID\-19 paid sick leave provision applies to UBC employees in certain non\-unionized roles, as well as employees who do not accrue paid sick leave\. 

COVID\-19 paid sick leave covers absences for COVID\-19 testing, self\-isolation at the direction of a health authority, as well as absences due to COVID\-19 symptoms\. Eligible employees will need to record their COVID\-19 paid sick leave absences in Workday\. COVID\-19 paid sick leave is in place until December 31, 2021\.
