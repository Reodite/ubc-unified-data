July 21, 2025

The [2025 Progress Report](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/policies-reports/strategic-equity-and-anti-racism-stear-progress-report-2025/) on the [Strategic Equity and Anti\-Racism \(StEAR\) Framework](https://equity.ubc.ca/stear-framework-and-roadmap-for-change/) tells the story of the ongoing engagement and progress on implementing UBC's equity and anti\-racism priorities\.

In this Q&amp;A, Dr\. Arig al Shaibah, Associate Vice\-President, Equity and Inclusion, reflects on key milestones, areas of learning, and what’s ahead in the next phase of the StEAR Framework\.

She reminds faculty and staff that "equity work doesn’t always have to be a big, visible initiative\. Sometimes, it starts with small everyday decisions: how you run a meeting, how you listen, how you advocate for someone’s inclusion\."
