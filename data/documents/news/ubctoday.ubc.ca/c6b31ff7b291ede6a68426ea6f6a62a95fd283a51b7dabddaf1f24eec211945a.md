April 15, 2024

### Name:

Violet Addams

### My Pronouns:

they/them

### Title:

Arts Admin Partner

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

Faculty of Arts, Arts Service Centre – Human Resources

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

1998

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

Solve problems, find longer\-term solutions, make Administrators' lives better\.

---

### What is the best thing about your work/role at UBC?

My answer has always been the people I'm surrounded by\. While I love my job for many reasons, what has kept me at UBC \(and brought me back after a 3 year break\) is the wonderful people I work with, from my amazing teammates to the incredible Administrators group I'm a part of and support, and the succession of great managers who have helped me become a better person over all these years\.

---

### What do you think is the greatest question ever asked?

"Can I help?" When said – it is a gift, a connection, an expression of caring, an opportunity to make real change in someone's life and thereby the world\. When heard – it is a gift, a connection, a recognition and being seen, an offer of care and support, a hug in just three words\.

---

### What's one personal experience you would encourage others to do/try?

Twice I've done a different kind of New Year's resolution and both years I stuck to it for the whole year\! I abandoned the idea of what I 'should' do and embraced things I wanted more of in life\. The first year was "I will go to one live event per month" and saw my first opera, a parade, Evil Dead the Musical \(splash zone\) and more; the second year was make one new recipe a month \(mini lasagnas\!\)\.
