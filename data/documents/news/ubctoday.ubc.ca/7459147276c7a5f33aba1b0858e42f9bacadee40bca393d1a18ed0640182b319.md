February 27, 2023

You are invited to share your opinion of internal communications at UBC – ranging from how you access information in your Faculty and department, to the work of the central Internal Communications team\.

Since the Internal Communications team was founded in 2014, we have been working to improve the way the university communicates with faculty and staff\.

The survey will take 10\-15 minutes to complete and will require your Campus\-Wide Login\. Please complete the survey and encourage your faculty and staff colleagues to do the same\.

[Complete the survey by Mar\. 17](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2mIahGv4zQSFUgu)
