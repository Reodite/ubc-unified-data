    ## [Participate in the new Canadian Campus Wellbeing Survey](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/may-05-2021/participate-new-canadian-campus-wellbeing-survey)
    
    You are invited to participate in the new Canadian Campus Wellbeing Survey \(CCWS\) for employees\. The CCWS is a comprehensive, voluntary and confidential survey that assesses the health and wellbeing status of staff and faculty in the post\-secondary sector\. The survey takes under 15 minutes to complete and responses will be anonymous and confidential\. Results of the survey will be reported in statistically aggregated form only, and it is not possible for UBC or CCWS to identify individual employees\. The deadline is May 31, 2021\.
