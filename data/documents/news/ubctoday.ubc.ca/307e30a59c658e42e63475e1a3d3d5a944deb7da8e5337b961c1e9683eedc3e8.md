September 13, 2021

As part of keeping our campus safe and secure, UBC is standardizing building hours for 2021/2022 Winter Session, Term 1\. Buildings will be open to the public Monday to Friday from 7:30 a\.m\. to 5:00 p\.m\. These standard hours will be assigned to approximately 70 buildings on the Vancouver campus, that fall into the categories of academic, research, or administrative buildings that service the operations of the University\. 

You will need your UBC Card to access these buildings in the evenings and over the weekend\.

[Find out more](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/features/news/aligning-building-access-hours-pilot-project)
