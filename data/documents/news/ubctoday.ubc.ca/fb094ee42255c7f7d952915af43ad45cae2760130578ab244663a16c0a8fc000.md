October 14, 2020

### UBC Vancouver

Faculty at UBC Vancouver are invited to apply for the Open Educational Resources \(OER\) grants\. The OER fund supports affordable and inclusive access to learning materials through the adaption, adoption, development, and integration of open educational resources within UBC credit courses\. The OER fund consists of two grant pathways:

**OER Rapid Innovation Grants**

Grants of up to $2,000 are available to the UBC Vancouver community for innovative activities that increase open educational resources development, awareness and capacity building\. **Applications are accepted on a rolling basis until the year’s funds are exhausted\.**

**OER Implementation Grants**

Grants of up to $25,000 for faculty at UBC Vancouver who wish to incorporate open educational resources as the primary materials into their UBCV credit courses\. The application deadline for this grant is **3pm on Friday, November 20**\.

Attend an [information session Wednesday, October 14](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/oer-fund-online-general-information-session-and-faculty-panel-october-14-2020/) to learn more about open education at UBC, and the grant criteria and process\.

[Learn more and apply at the OER fund website](https://open.ubc.ca/call-for-applications-for-open-educational-resource-grants/?login)

---

### UBC Okanagan

Faculty at UBC Okanagan are invited to apply for the Open Educational Resources grant through a pilot program, designed to fund and support you to adapt or create open educational resources\. Grants of up to $5,000 are available\.

Information sessions are available for applicants looking to develop their Open Educational Resources Grant proposals on [October 8 and 23, and November 6](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6D67uGrSFlgf1fD)\.

The application deadline for proposals is **Friday November 27**\. 

[Learn more](https://provost.ok.ubc.ca/awards-funding/oer/)
