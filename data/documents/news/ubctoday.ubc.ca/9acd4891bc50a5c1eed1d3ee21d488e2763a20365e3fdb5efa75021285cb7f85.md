This message was sent to faculty and staff in Vancouver and the Okanagan\.

May 6, 2024

***A message from Adam Charania, Interim Vice\-President, Human Resources and Alexandra Bayne, Managing Director, Human Resources, UBC Okanagan\.***

Faculty and staff are invited to participate in the [Canadian Campus Wellbeing Survey](https://pair.ubc.ca/surveys/ccws-staff-faculty/) \(CCWS\) 2024 to help us understand your lived experience at UBC, as we prepare to refresh our existing wellbeing strategy\. Your insights will have a direct impact on how our community members work, learn, and enjoy our campuses for years to come\.  
  
The CCWS is a comprehensive, voluntary, and confidential online survey that assesses the health and wellbeing status of students, faculty, and staff in the post\-secondary sector\. The survey consists of validated measures used in existing Canadian health surveys and it will take 15\-20 minutes to complete\. All responses will be anonymous and confidential\. Results of the survey will be reported in statistically aggregated form only; it is not possible for UBC or CCWS to identify individual faculty and staff\.  
  
The survey deadline is **May 17**\.
