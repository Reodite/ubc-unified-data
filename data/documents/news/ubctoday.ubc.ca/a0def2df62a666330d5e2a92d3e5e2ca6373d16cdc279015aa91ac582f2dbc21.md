March 18, 2024

The Pick Your Peak Stair Challenge is an initiative that encourages faculty and staff to incorporate movement, social connection, and physical activity throughout the day\.

You can register a team of 4\-6 people or sign\-up as a solo climber \(**by** **4pm on April 15**\), select your stair climbing goal, and start tracking your progress\. 

Weekly participation prizes will be awarded randomly\. After the challenge, prizes will be awarded to the highest individual climber and the team that has the highest average points per participant\.

[Learn more](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/physical-wellbeing/pick-your-peak-stair-challenge)
