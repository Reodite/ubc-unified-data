January 16, 2025

*“Challenges in Partnered Research” is a Q&amp;A series by* [*Partnering in Research*](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/our-work/partnering-in-research/) *that highlights individuals transforming policies, practices, and communities through collaborative research\.* 

In this conversation, Adriaan de Jager, UBC’s Associate Vice\-President of Government Relations and Community Engagement, takes on the role of interviewer and, together, they reflect on the evolving role of universities in society\. Professor Moura Quayle, the Associate Provost at UBC Vancouver, shares advice for academics on navigating traditional structures, calls for boldness and innovation in UBC’s strategic planning\. She also discusses the importance of knowledge exchange between universities and the wider community\.
