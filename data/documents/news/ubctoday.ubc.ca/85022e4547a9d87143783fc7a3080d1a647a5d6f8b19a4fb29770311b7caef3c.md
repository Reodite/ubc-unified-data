September 3, 2025

An air quality warning is in effect for Metro Vancouver, including UBC\.

For information on how to reduce the effects of smoke exposure, visit the [Safety and Risk Services website](https://srs.ubc.ca/emergency/types-of-emergencies/fire/)\. 

Learn more about current conditions [here](https://metrovancouver.org/services/air-quality-climate-action/air-quality-data-and-advisories)\.
