    ## [Dr\. Rehan Sadiq appointed Provost and Vice\-President Academic pro tem, UBC Okanagan](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/january-01-2022/dr-rehan-sadiq-appointed-provost-and-vice-president-academic-pro-tem-ubc)
    
    I am pleased to announce that, effective February 1, 2022, Dr\. Rehan Sadiq will serve as Provost and Vice\-President, Academic pro tem, for UBC Okanagan\. Dr\. Sadiq is a professor of Civil Engineering and a Distinguished University Scholar, and currently holds the position of Executive Associate Dean in the School of Engineering at UBC Okanagan\. He is also a co\-lead of the Life Cycle Management Laboratory at UBC\.
