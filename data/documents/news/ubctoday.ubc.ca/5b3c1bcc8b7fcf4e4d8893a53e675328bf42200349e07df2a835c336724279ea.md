This message was sent to all faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan on behalf of the Office of the Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal\.

February 17, 2022

***A message from Lesley Cormack, Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal, UBC Okanagan***

way’ UBC Okanagan campus community,

In 2019, UBC Okanagan pledged to support Indigenous students, culture and scholarship through a public declaration of five commitments in response to the [Truth and Reconciliation Commission’s Calls to Action](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/british-columbians-our-governments/indigenous-people/aboriginal-peoples-documents/calls_to_action_english2.pdf)\.

These commitments were made to support and implement the recommendations of the Indigenous Advisory Committee to the Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal to advance truth and reconciliation\. The commitments can be seen at [ok\.ubc\.ca/about/indigenous\-engagement](https://ok.ubc.ca/about/indigenous-engagement/)\.

I am honoured to invite you to participate in a virtual session to discuss UBC Okanagan’s continuing progress toward fulfilling our TRC commitments\.
