March 10, 2021

On March 11, 2021, UBC will lower the following flags in recognition of the [National Day of Observance](https://pm.gc.ca/en/news/statements/2021/03/08/statement-prime-minister-announcing-national-day-observance-covid-19), which commemorates the people who lost their lives and the significant impacts we have all felt because of COVID\-19:

-   At UBC Vancouver, the Canada flag at Flagpole Plaza and the BC, Musqueam and UBC flags at the North Plaza of the Life Building
-   At UBC Okanagan, the Canada, BC and Okanagan Nation Alliance flags

For more information, please visit the Ceremonies and Events website for [UBC Vancouver](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/flag-lowering/) and [UBC Okanagan](https://ceremonies.ok.ubc.ca/campus-flags/)\.
