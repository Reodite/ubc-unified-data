March 27, 2023

UBC Vancouver Campus Security is taking a look at its services and organizational model to better understand how it can meet the needs of a rapidly changing community\.

One part of this review will be an engagement process with the UBC community\. As part of this process, you will be able to take an online survey, book a one\-on\-one interview, send in a formal email submission, and attend dialogue sessions with representatives of groups with a particular interest in campus security\.

The engagement process will run until **Apr\. 18**\.
