October 27, 2025

The annual Thrive mental health campaign is dedicated to making the UBC community aware of the resources and initiatives available year\-round to support mental health and wellbeing\. This November, take time to explore ways to support your mental health\. 

From Nov\. 1 – Nov\. 30\., attend events, apply for grants, and explore resources, such as the Mental Health Continuum \(a n ew mental health self\-assessment tool for faculty and staff\)\. 

[Learn more](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/thrive)
