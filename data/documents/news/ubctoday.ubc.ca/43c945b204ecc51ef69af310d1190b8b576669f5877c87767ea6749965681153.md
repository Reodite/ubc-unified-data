May 14, 2025

With only ten fellows selected annually, we are pleased to announce that three UBC faculty members have been recognized for their exceptional contributions to teaching and learning at Canadian universities, and have been named as 3M National Teaching Fellows in 2025\. In addition, this year is the first time a faculty member from UBC Okanagan has received this honour\. 

We congratulate the three UBC recipients of the 3M National Teaching Fellowship in 2025:

-   [Dr\. Ahmed Hieawy, Associate Professor, Faculty of Dentistry, Division of Endodontics, University of British Columbia](https://www.dentistry.ubc.ca/dr-ahmed-hieawy-named-a-2025-3m-national-teaching-fellow/)
-   [Dr\. W\. Stephen McNeil,  Associate Professor, Department of Chemistry, University of British Columbia, Okanagan Campus](https://news.ok.ubc.ca/2025/05/13/ubcos-stephen-mcneil-earns-national-recognition/)
-   [Dr\. Gabriel Potvin, Associate Professor of Teaching, Department of Chemical and Biological Engineering, University of British Columbia](https://apsc.ubc.ca/news/2025/dr-gabriel-potvin-recognized-with-3m-national-teaching-fellowship)

The next call for nominations will be issued in fall 2025\. 

[Learn more about the 3M National Teaching Fellowship Awards](https://academic.ubc.ca/awards/3m-national-teaching-fellowship-awards)
