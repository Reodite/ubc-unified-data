February 25, 2025

Dr\. Emily Jenkins, Associate Professor in the School of Nursing, has led a national survey which reveals that school principals across Canada are spending a substantial amount of time managing student substance use but lack the resources to respond effectively\.

The study recommends a pan\-Canadian strategy to prevent substance use harms through K\-12 schools in addition to a coordinated system of support to reduce substance use harms\.
