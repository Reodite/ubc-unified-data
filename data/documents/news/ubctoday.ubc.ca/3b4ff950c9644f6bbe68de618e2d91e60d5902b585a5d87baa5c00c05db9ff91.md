February 3, 2026

Whether it’s an earthquake, wildfire smoke, extreme heat, or flooding, having the right information and resources can make all the difference\. We want to hear from you: How prepared are you for emergencies?

By sharing your experience in a [15\-minute anonymous survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_8qYyfgXTliwNiXc), help shape UBC’s emergency management planning that’s tailored to community needs\. The survey results will guide improvements to emergency communication, resource accessibility, and resilience infrastructure\.  

[Learn more](https://srs.ubc.ca/2026/01/30/help-shape-emergency-preparedness-at-ubc/)
