    ## [COVID–19 — Temporary closures of library facilities at UBC](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/march-03-2020/covid-19-temporary-closures-library-facilities-ubc)
    
    As you are already aware, UBC continues to navigate its response to COVID\-19 and support social distancing protocols across our campuses and other learning sites\. In light of this, we have made the decision to close all UBC Library branches and cease loaning physical books on both the Vancouver and Okanagan campuses effective Friday, March 20 at 5:00pm\. Closures will remain in effect until April 6, at which time the situation will be reassessed\.
