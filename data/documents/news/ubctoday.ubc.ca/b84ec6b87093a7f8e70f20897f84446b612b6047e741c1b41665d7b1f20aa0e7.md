    ## [Submit an application to the Scholarly Publication Fund](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/september-09-2022/submit-application-scholarly-publication-fund)
    
    If you are a faculty member looking to publish scholarly works, the Scholarly Publication Fund can provide support with publication costs, including the use of images, copyrighted materials, or special production elements\. Faculty members from UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan are invited to apply for funding until available funds are exhausted\. Approximately $75,000 is available annually, and applications will be reviewed three times each year\. Submit an application by 4pm on October 15, February 15, or June 15\.
