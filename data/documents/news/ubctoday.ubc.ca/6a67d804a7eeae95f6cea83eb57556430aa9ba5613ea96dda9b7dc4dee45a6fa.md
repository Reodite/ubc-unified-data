October 7, 2024

You are invited to complete the latest version of the Employment Equity Survey\. This confidential online survey helps inform the university's understanding of the demographic composition of faculty and staff and efforts to ensure equity and fairness in employment practices\. New this year, the survey includes an additional response option among disaggregated ethno\-racial population groups and an additional question on disability which supports UBC's compliance under the Federal Contractor's Program\.  

If you previously completed the survey, please update your responses [here](https://equity.ubc.ca/news-and-stories/complete-revised-employment-equity-survey/) \(CWL required\)\. 

Join over 84 per cent of UBC employees who have participated to date\. It takes three minutes, and your information will remain confidential and secure\. 

The deadline is **Oct\. 31**\.

[Find out more](https://equity.ubc.ca/news-and-stories/complete-revised-employment-equity-survey/)
