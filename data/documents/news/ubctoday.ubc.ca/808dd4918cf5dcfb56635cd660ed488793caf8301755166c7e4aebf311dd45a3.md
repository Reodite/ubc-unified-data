July 3, 2024

The 2024 Olympics takes place in Paris from July 26 to August 11\. Along with the 10,500 athletes, a team of 45,000 volunteers is needed to make the world’s biggest sporting event a success\. 

Among the volunteers heading to Paris are two faculty members and a staff member from UBC, each playing a vital role in the success and well\-being of the athletes\. 

Hear about the previous experiences and hopes for this year’s event from Bruce Forster, Professor of Radiology, Faculty of Medicine; Samantha Pritchard, Senior Manager of Sport Science and Sport Medicine, Athletics and Recreation; and Michael Wilkinson, Clinical Assistant Professor, Sport and Exercise Medicine, Faculty of Medicine\.
