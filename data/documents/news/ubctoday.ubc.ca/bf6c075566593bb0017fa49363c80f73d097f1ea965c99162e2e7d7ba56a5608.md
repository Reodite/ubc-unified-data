June 30, 2025

### Name:

Doris Liu

### My Pronouns:

she/her

### Title:

Course Representative

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

Real Estate Division

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2019

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

Advise on the Real Estate Licensing and Registration programs\.

---

### What is the best thing about your work/role at UBC?

The best part of my work must be the group of amazing people I work with\. I believe the quality of your relationships determines the quality of your life\. Having people I can lean on at work makes the workplace so much more rewarding\. I appreciate the inclusive and diverse work environment that UBC offers, which enriches my life experiences and broadens my perspective on life\.

---

### What trait do you most admire in others?

I have a lot of respect for people who have been through tough times yet maintain a positive outlook on life\. Being able to find the good in every situation and treat each obstacle as a valuable lesson are qualities I admire—and the kind of people I strive to surround myself with\.

---

### What do you do regularly to invest in your mental and/or physical health?

I like to start my day with a nutritious smoothie that contains more than ten healthy ingredients\. I also take my daily multivitamins and probiotics\. I check in with myself regularly to ensure I’m not overly stressed or exhausted\. If I am, I slow down and give myself time to recharge by spending time in nature\. To me, a good cry can also help release built\-up emotions and bring a sense of relief\.

---

### What is one of the most meaningful lessons you have learned?

Very often, when you don’t rush through life, you end up going further\.
