June 15, 2022

At UBC, more than 17,000 faculty and staff work across two campuses and many learning sites\. We want to hear from you, and celebrate the unique contributions that you bring to our community\. 

If you are a current faculty or staff member, the **30\-second bio** is a way to share what your team does, work achievements you're proud of, or what you admire about your colleagues\. **[Read previously published 30\-second bio](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/ubc-today-archive?campus=All&amp;audience=All&amp;created=&amp;topic=All&amp;type=67) [s](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/ubc-today-archive?campus=All&amp;audience=All&amp;created=&amp;topic=All&amp;type=67)**

If you are a current faculty researcher, the **Research in focus** is an opportunity to share what motivates you to conduct your research, acknowledge your collaborators, and highlight what you hope will change as a result of your work\. **[Read previously published Research in focus](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/ubc-today-archive?campus=All&amp;audience=All&amp;created=&amp;topic=All&amp;type=223)**

Introduce who you are and what you do
