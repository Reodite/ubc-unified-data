    ## [Applications open to host Indigenous youth in summer research program](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/march-03-2025/applications-open-host-indigenous-youth-summer-research-program)
    
    Faculty members in science, technology, engineering, or math \(STEM\) fields are invited to submit a seed2STEM proposal to host Indigenous youth in a summer research program\.The seed2STEM program provides Indigenous high school students with paid six\-week internships from July 7 to August 15, offering hands\-on experience to inspire their interest in STEM fields\. Find out how you can host a student this summer—whether in a wet lab, conducting literature reviews, or with knowledge translation\.Learn more and submit a proposal by Apr\. 15\.
