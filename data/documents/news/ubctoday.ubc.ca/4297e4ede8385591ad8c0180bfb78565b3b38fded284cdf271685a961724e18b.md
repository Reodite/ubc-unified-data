November 30, 2020

Thank you for your ongoing support and patience following the launch of Workday on November 2\. As many of you know, the Integrated Service Centre \(ISC\) is now your main point of contact for Workday Finance and HR related queries, issues, and requests\.

To help support you, the ISC has created a resource called [*Workday this Week*](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article&amp;sysparm_article=KB0017265), which includes all the latest updates, recent changes and timely information about Workday\.

*Workday this Week* is updated regularly based on frequently asked questions from the UBC community, submitted tickets, upcoming deadlines, and Workday system updates\. It includes “Top Topics” to help you find answers to common questions, including key changes and resources like submitting expense reports, vacation requests, and payroll reminders by upcoming deadlines\.

*Workday this Week* also includes:

-   Known issues
-   Recently resolved issues
-   Upcoming scheduled maintenance
-   Tips and helpful hints about using Workday

You can read *Workday this Week* by clicking on the link in the green bar at the top of the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)\.

[Workday this Week](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_970x970/public/2020-11/Screen%20Shot%202020-11-27%20at%2014.51.10.png?itok=DxtkGgAa)

Please consider using the resources available on *Workday this Week* before you submit a ticket\. This will help the ISC team focus on complex questions with dedicated resources\.

Please check *Workday this Week* regularly as it’s constantly being updated as soon as new information is available\.
