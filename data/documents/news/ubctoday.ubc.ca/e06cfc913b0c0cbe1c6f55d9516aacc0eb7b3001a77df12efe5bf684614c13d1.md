    ## [UBC community members among new Order of Canada appointees](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/january-01-2024/ubc-community-members-among-new-order-canada-appointees)
    
    On December 28, 2023, Her Excellency the Right Honourable Mary Simon, Governor General of Canada, announced 78 new appointments to the Order of Canada\.The Order of Canada is one of Canada’s highest honours\. It was created in 1967 to honour people whose service shapes our society, whose innovations ignite our imaginations, and whose compassion unites our communities\. Its Companions, Officers and Members take to heart the motto of the Order: DESIDERANTES MELIOREM PATRIAM \(“They desire a better country”\)\.
