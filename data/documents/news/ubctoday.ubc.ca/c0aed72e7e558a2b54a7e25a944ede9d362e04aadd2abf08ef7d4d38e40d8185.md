March 11, 2024

Workday Student is now ready to be used by faculty and staff currently using the Student Information System, based on your individual security role\.

The transition to Workday Student will provide UBC with the foundation for a modern student experience that is adaptable and can evolve to meet the university's needs in the future\.

Information and a range of resources are available to support this transition\.

[Find out more](https://irp.ubc.ca/workdaystudent_march11)
