May 12, 2025

UBC Okanagan's prestigious Researcher of the Year awards recognize individuals who are making the world a better place through excellence in research and scholarly activity\.

Three outstanding faculty members have been honoured: Dr\. Mohammad Arjmand in the Natural Sciences and Engineering category, Dr\. Brodie Sakakibara in the Health category and Michael V\. Smith in the Social Sciences and Humanities category\.

Find out more about how they are addressing the most pressing issues of our time\.
