UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers, and senior professional leaders in Vancouver\.

June 28, 2024

I am pleased to announce that Dr\. Lalitha Raman\-Wilms has been appointed as Dean of the Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences for a five\-year term, commencing October 1, 2024\.

Dr\. Raman\-Wilms is currently serving as Dean of the College of Pharmacy, Rady Faculty of Health Sciences at the University of Manitoba, where she has been appointed since 2017\. Prior to her time at the University of Manitoba, she served in various leadership roles at the University of Toronto’s Leslie Dan Faculty of Pharmacy, including as Associate Dean \(Education\), Associate Dean \(Professional Programs\), and Director of Division of Pharmacy Practice\.

Dr\. Raman\-Wilms has demonstrated a strong commitment to education, research, practice and community engagement\. She continuously supports efforts in equity, diversity, inclusion, anti\-racism and reconciliation, most recently as a member of the University of Manitoba’s President’s Task Force on EDI\. She strives to be inclusive, consultative and transparent, and is committed to bringing people together through positive and collaborative leadership\.

For the full announcement, visit: [https://academic\.ubc\.ca/dr\-lalitha\-raman\-wilms\-dean\-faculty\-of\-pharmaceutical\-sciences](https://academic.ubc.ca/dr-lalitha-raman-wilms-dean-faculty-of-pharmaceutical-sciences)  
  
I would like to thank the students, faculty and staff who participated in this process and provided valuable feedback to the President’s Advisory Committee\. I would also like to thank Dr\. Larry Lynd for his generous work and commitment as Dean *pro tem* for the Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences over the last year\.

Please join me in congratulating Dr\. Raman\-Wilms on her appointment\.

**Gage Averill**  
Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver
