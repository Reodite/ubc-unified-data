    ## [UBC\-led partnership tackles cattle methane with cost\-saving tools for farmers](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/january-01-2026/ubc-led-partnership-tackles-cattle-methane-cost-saving-tools-farmers)
    
    When Dr\. Leluo Guan peers inside a cow’s stomach, she sees more than microbes—she sees an opportunity to cut methane emissions from cattle and improve profits for Canadian beef and dairy farmers\. The industries contribute more than $50 billion annually to Canada’s GDP and support thousands of jobs from British Columbia to Ontario and Quebec\. But cattle account for more than half of Canada’s agricultural methane emissions—a potent driver of global warming\.

    ## [Advance your research while supporting student workplace learning](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/january-01-2026/advance-your-research-while-supporting-student-workplace-learning)
    
    Each year, the Undergraduate Student Research Awards \(USRA\) program funds approximately 250 full\-time appointments for undergraduate students, subsidizing research work experience that complements their studies in an academic setting\. UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan faculty members can receive $6,000 in wage subsidies to hire Canadian undergraduate students full\-time during summer 2026, fall 2026 or winter 2027 by submitting a proposal through the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada \(NSERC\) USRA program\.
