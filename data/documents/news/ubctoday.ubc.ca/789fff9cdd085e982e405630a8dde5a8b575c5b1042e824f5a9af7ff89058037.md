August 5, 2026

Faculty members planning their courses for the 2026/27 Winter Session can now access the Open Resource Centre for Assessments \(ORCA\), UBC Vancouver's new service for secure, flexible, and equitable digital assessments\.

The centre is currently accepting requests from instructors teaching 2026 Winter Term 1 undergraduate courses with exams delivered through platforms such as Canvas, PrairieLearn, Brightspace, and Webwork\.

ORCA builds on the services previously offered through the Computer\-Based Testing Facility \(CBTF\) and aligns with UBC's Strategic Direction, *Create Flexibility for Learners*, by expanding assessment options for instructors and supporting accessible, student\-centred assessment practices\. It also provides Faculties with an additional resource to support student success and academic planning\.
