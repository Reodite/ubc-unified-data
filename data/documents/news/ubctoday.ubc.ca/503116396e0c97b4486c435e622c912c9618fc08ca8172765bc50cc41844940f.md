October 13, 2020

Each year BC takes part in the annual ShakeOut earthquake drill\.

This year, UBC's Safety &amp; Risk Services’ Emergency Management team will be hosting a virtual ShakeOut drill, so you can learn how to get your home ready, and know what to do in the event of an earthquake\.

UBC's virtual ShakeOut drill will take place on **October 15 between 10:00am and 10:20am\.** [Find more information, including the Zoom webinar details](https://ready.ubc.ca/events/ubc-virtual-shakeout-drill/)\.
