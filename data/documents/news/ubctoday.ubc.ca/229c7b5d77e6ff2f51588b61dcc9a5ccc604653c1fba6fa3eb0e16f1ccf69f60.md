    ## [Some screen time better than none during children’s concussion recovery](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/october-10-2022/some-screen-time-better-none-during-childrens-concussion-recovery)
    
    Too much screen time can slow children’s recovery from concussions, but new research from UBC and the University of Calgary suggests that banning screen time is not the answer\. The researchers looked for links between the self\-reported screen time of more than 700 children aged 8\-16 in the first 7\-10 days following an injury, and symptoms reported by them and their caregivers over the following six months\.
