August 2, 2022

*Managing at UBC* is a leadership development program designed to equip new managers with the foundational skills, knowledge and tools to succeed and grow in leadership roles at UBC\.

The program will take place online from Oct\. 2022 to May 2023\. During this time, leaders from across the university will come together to share experiences and learn best practices on topics related to leading self, leading teams, and leading within the organization\.

Applications are open until **Aug\. 12**\.

[Find out more](https://hr.ubc.ca/career-development/leadership-programs/managing-ubc)
