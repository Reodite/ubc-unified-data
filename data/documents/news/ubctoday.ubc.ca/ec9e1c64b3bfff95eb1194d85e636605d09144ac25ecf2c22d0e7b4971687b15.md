October 8, 2021

There are plenty of ways for you to get involved with this year's *UBC United Way Campaign*\. From making a donation to taking part in this year's coffee card offer, or participating in events in your local community, now is the time to show your support\.

**Make a financial donation to your local community**  
More information about making a donation and the impact of your gift is available on the UBC United Way Campaign website\. [Find out more](https://unitedway.ubc.ca/howtogive/)  
  
**Join a UBC United Way event \(*****UBC Vancouver only*****\)**  
Learn more about the [activities taking place Oct 12 \- Oct 15](https://unitedway.ubc.ca/events/)\. Plus, attend a virtual kick\-off event on Thursday, October 14\. [Find out more](https://unitedway.ubc.ca/events/)

**Take part in the UW coffee campaign \(*****UBC Vancouver only*****\)**  
When you pledge $5 or more, per pay, to the UBC United Way campaign through payroll \(or a one\-time gift of $120 or more\) by October 31, you will earn free coffee for the month of November\. [Learn more about the UW coffee campaign](https://unitedway.ubc.ca/events/event/coffee-campaign/)

**Follow along on social**  
This year's goal is to inspire 1,100 donors during the campaign\. Check out UBC United Way on Twitter or Instagram for updates on the campaign's progress\.

Follow along on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ubcunitedway/)  
Follow along on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/ubcunitedway)

If you have any questions about this year’s campaign, please reach out to UBC's United Way Campaign Manager at [united\.way@ubc\.ca](mailto:united.way@ubc.ca)\.
