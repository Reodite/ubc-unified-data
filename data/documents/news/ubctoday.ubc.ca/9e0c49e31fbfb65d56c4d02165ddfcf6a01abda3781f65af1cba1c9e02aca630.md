September 28, 2020

What’s happening?

You are invited to provide feedback on a proposed site amendment to the Vancouver Campus Plan \(2010\) that will allow for a future academic building at the corner of Agronomy Road and Wesbrook Mall\. 

How you can participate

1\. [Watch the video and read the proposal](https://planning.ubc.ca/vancouver-campus-plan-amendment-proposed-site-use-change)

2\. [Attend a virtual open house on September 30, 4 – 5:30pm](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_1N6QOLLeWoLA4rb)

3\. [Complete an online survey by October 9](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3mapBO8oEm0s7uR)
