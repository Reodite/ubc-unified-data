February 22, 2021

### Name:

Jeetender Amritsar

### Title:

Facility Manager

### Department/Unit:

Building Operations

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2020

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

Lead daily operations of buildings and maintain user contacts\.

---

### What is the best thing about your work/role at UBC?

I am the primary contact for building maintenance services \(for the Teal Zone\), which include cleaning, preventative maintenance, repair and minor renovations at the UBC Vancouver campus\. I am involved in planning, hiring, training, directing, and managing tradespeople who provide these services\. This gives me overall satisfaction in my work\.

---

### What trait do you most admire in others?

Transparency\.

---

### What’s one personal experience you would encourage others to do/try?

Walk in the lovely trails on a rainy day\. It is a beautiful experience\.

---

### How do you challenge yourself in life on a regular basis?

Keep upgrading yourself with degrees and certifications and implement that knowledge in your workplace\.
