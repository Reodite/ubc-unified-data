This message was sent to faculty and staff in the Okanagan\.

April 20, 2022

***A message from Lesley Cormack, Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal, UBC Okanagan; Adam Charania; Associate Vice\-President, Human Resources***

Dear colleagues,

We are delighted to announce the appointment of Alexandra Bayne as Managing Director, Human Resources at UBC’s Okanagan campus\. Already a member of the UBC Human Resources leadership team, Alex will be beginning her new role leading the Okanagan campus HR team on June 1, 2022\.

Alex brings with her over 20 years of HR experience at UBC\. Fittingly, earlier in her career, one of her major assignments was as part of the team which opened the Okanagan campus, where Alex led the workforce integration strategy and implementation as part of the transition of Okanagan University College’s North Kelowna campus to UBC Okanagan\. Since then, Alex has taken on multiple roles within UBC HR based at the Vancouver campus, including as managing director since 2017, with responsibility for strategic workplace initiatives, learning and engagement\.
