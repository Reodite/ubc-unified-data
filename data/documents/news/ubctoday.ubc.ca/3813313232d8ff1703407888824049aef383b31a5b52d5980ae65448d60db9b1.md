September 27, 2021

Next week, a total of 9 0 staff members from UBC Vancouver, including 5 0 new inductees, will be honoured for their more than 25 years of service to UBC, at the annual *25 Year Club* celebration\.

This year's celebration will again be held virtually and is scheduled for **October 7**\.

The *25 Year Club* was established in 1971 by President Walter Gage to recognize the contributions of staff\. In addition to inducting staff who have reached this major 25\-year milestone, the event also honours those with 35 and 40 years of service, and as of 2020 — for the first time ever and in celebration of the 50\[th\] anniversary of the *25 Year Club* — 50 years of service as well\. 

See a full list of 2021 Honourees:

-   [Staff with 25 years or more of service](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/honours-recognition/25-year-club/25-year-club-new-members-2021/)
-   [Staff with 35 years of more of service](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/honours-recognition/25-year-club/35-year-club-members-2021/)
-   [Staff with 40 years or more of service](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/honours-recognition/25-year-club/40-year-honourees-2021/)

Congratulations to all of those inducted and recognized this year\!

For additional details on the event or to request an invitation, please contact [ceremonies\.rsvp@ubc\.ca](mailto:ceremonies.rsvp@ubc.ca)\.

[Find out more](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/honours-recognition/25-year-club/)
