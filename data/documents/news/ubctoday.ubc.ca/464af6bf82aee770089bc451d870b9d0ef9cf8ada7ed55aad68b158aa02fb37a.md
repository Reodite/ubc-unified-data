April 4, 2022

On March 28, [we invited you to share a photo](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/march-03-2022/share-snapshot-spring-blossoms-apr-11) of spring blossoms in celebration of the changing seasons\.

Thank you to those who have already shared photos with us\. In case you haven't already, there's still time to submit a photo of how spring inspires you\. The deadline is **Monday, April 11\.**

What do you need to do?

-   Complete [this form](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/share-snapshot-spring-blossoms) and submit a photo that captures what the spring season means to you\. 
-   Ensure your image is high resolution\.
-   Share relevant image credits\.
-   Write a caption that describes the photo\.
-   Submit your photo, image credits and caption via [this form](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/share-snapshot-spring-blossoms) by **Monday, April 11**\.

**All photo submissions will be published on UBC Today in the coming weeks\.**
