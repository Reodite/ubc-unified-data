April 21, 2026

For the 15th consecutive year, UBC has been named one of Canada’s Greenest Employers\. 

This special award recognizes Canadian employers that lead in creating a culture of environmental awareness, developing exceptional sustainability initiatives and attracting employees because of their environmental leadership\. Find out why the university is recognized nationally year over year for its climate action and sustainability leadership***\.***
