April 29, 2024

On March 27, the Board of Governors approved amendments to UBC’s Conflict of Interest \(COI\) Policy \(SC3\) and its associated Procedures, effective as of April 2\. The policy was amended to fully reflect the annual COI reporting requirements for UBC staff members, as well as address various best practices that have emerged since the last revision\.

A copy of the approved Conflict of Interest Policy and its associated Procedures is available on the University Counsel website\.
