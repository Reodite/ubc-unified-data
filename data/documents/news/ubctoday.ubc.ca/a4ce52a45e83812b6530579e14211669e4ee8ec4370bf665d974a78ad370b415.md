June 1, 2021

The UBC Board of Governors is pleased to announce that two new members have been appointed to serve on the board by the BC Provincial Government, effective May 17, 2021\. The first appointee is Natalie Chan, President and Research Director of Be the Change Group, a Vancouver\-based public and population health consulting and creative firm\. Natalie is also a family physician, providing primary care and addiction medicine services to Vancouver’s key inner city populations, including people living with mental illness and substance use disorders, sex workers, and at\-risk youth\. The second appointee is Anthonia Ogundele, Founder and Executive Director of Ethos Lab Educational Society\. Ethos Lab is a non\-profit STEM and culture\-focused Innovation Academy for youth ages 13\-18 that provides access to emerging technologies, culture, and a community of innovators\. Anthonia also has a background in urban planning and sustainability and continues to consult with the World Bank as a resilience professional\.

The Board of Governors is pleased to welcome Natalie Chan and Anthonia Ogundele to these new roles\. Their first board meeting will be June 24\.

Learn more: [Natalie Chan](https://bog.ubc.ca/board-members/board-members/appointed/natalie-chan/) \| [Anthonia Ogundele](https://bog.ubc.ca/board-members/board-members/appointed/anthonia-ogundele/)
