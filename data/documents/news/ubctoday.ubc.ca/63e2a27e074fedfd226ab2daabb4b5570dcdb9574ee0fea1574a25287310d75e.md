September 4, 2020

The Vancouver and Okanagan Senates welcome the nomination of worthy individuals for honorary degrees before **September 30**\.

Honorary degrees are intended to recognize individuals who have made substantial contributions to society at the local, national and global level\. The committee encourages nominations which reflect the cultural diversity of Canada and the international character and diversity of UBC itself\.

To find out more, including how to submit your nomination, visit the relevant website below:

[Vancouver Senate](https://senate.ubc.ca/vancouver/honorary)

[Okanagan Senate](https://senate.ubc.ca/okanagan/honorary)
