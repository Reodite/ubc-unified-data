    ## [Equity Enhancement Fund – call for proposals](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/november-11-2021/equity-enhancement-fund-call-proposals)
    
    As part of UBC’s commitment to advancing equity and inclusion across its campuses, faculty and staff are invited to submit a proposal to the Equity Enhancement Fund for projects that are responsive to community needs and advance one or more of the actions outlined in UBC’s Inclusion Action Plan\. Please encourage faculty and staff in your area to submit a proposal by November 29\. Up to $15,000 in funding per project is available in Vancouver, and up to $10,000 is available in the Okanagan\.

    2021 marks the 71st year that UBC has hosted a Remembrance Day ceremony\. Following a virtual ceremony in 2020, UBC’s 2021 ceremony returns to War Memorial Gym at UBC Vancouver on Nov\. 11\.
