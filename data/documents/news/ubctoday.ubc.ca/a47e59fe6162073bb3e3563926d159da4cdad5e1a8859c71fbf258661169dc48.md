March 2, 2026

### Name:

Paz Pino

### My Pronouns:

she/her

### Title:

Conflict of Interest Administrator

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

University Counsel

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2017

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

Assist the UBC community in understanding and managing conflicts of interest\.

---

### What is the best thing about your work/role at UBC?

Getting to build relationships with Department Heads and Deans, and assisting them in understanding conflict of interest and conflict of commitment disclosures\.

---

### What is your favourite quote and why?

"Trees have a secret life that is only revealed to those who are willing to climb them" by Reinaldo Arenas\. This quote has always reminded me to appreciate nature and to take risks\.

---

### What are your hobbies and interests outside of work?

I love pet sitting for friends and family and I love all animals\. I also love learning afrobeat dancing and I'm teaching myself how to sew\.
