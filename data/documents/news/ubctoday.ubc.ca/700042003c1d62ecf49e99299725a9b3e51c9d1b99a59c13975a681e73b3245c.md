    ## [Leadership transition in the Peter A\. Allard School of Law](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/july-07-2020/leadership-transition-peter-allard-school-law)
    
    After five years of outstanding service as Dean of the Peter A\. Allard School of Law, Professor Catherine Dauvergne is stepping down from her role to join Simon Fraser University as their Vice\-President, Academic and Provost\. Professor Dauvergne’s achievements have been numerous during her term as Dean\. She has implemented an experiential learning curriculum, developed a post\-graduation debt\-relief pilot program — the first of its kind among law schools in western Canada — and led the implementation of Truth and Reconciliation Commission calls to action within the school\.
