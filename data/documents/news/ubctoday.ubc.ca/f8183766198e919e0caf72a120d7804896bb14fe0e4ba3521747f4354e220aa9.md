### Name:

Alvin Nganga

### My Pronouns:

he/him

### Title:

Residence Administration Assistant

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

Student Housing and Community Services

### Location:

Okanagan

### Year I started working at UBC:

2023

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

I facilitate residence communications and process requests, transactions and reports\.

---

### What's the best advice you ever received?

The best advice I've ever received is to be grateful for everything I have in life and to take full advantage of opportunities in the moment\.

---

### What trait do you most admire in others?

I admire kindness and openness in people because it is telling of their humility and ability to contribute to and thrive within community\.

---

### What are your hobbies and interests outside of work?

I love basketball, art \(different mediums: sculpting, printing, drawing, painting\), music and film\.
