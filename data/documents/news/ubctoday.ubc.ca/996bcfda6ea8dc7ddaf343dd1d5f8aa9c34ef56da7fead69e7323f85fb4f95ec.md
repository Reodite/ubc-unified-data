November 23, 2020

By order and direction of the Provincial Health Officer \(PHO\), all individuals, places of work and businesses in BC must significantly reduce their level of social interactions and travel\. The order is in effect until December 7, 2020 at midnight\.

[Learn more on the Government of BC website](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/safety/emergency-preparedness-response-recovery/covid-19-provincial-support/restrictions?bcgovtm=20200506_GCPE_AM_COVID_9_NOTIFICATION_BCGOV_BCGOV_EN_BC__NOTIFICATION)
