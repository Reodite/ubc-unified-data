October 28, 2022

The *25 Year Club* was established in 1971 by President Walter Gage to recognize the contributions of staff\. The annual celebration recognizes staff who have reached this major 25\-year milestone, and also honours those with 35, 40 and 50 years of service\.

On October 20, a total of 128 staff members from UBC Vancouver, including 92 new inductees, were honoured for their more than 25 years of service to UBC\.

See a full list of 2022 Honourees:

-   [Staff with 50 years or more of service](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/honours-recognition/25-year-club/50-year-honourees-2022/)

Congratulations to all of those inducted and recognized this year\!
