June 1, 2026

### Name:

Sukhbir \(Sunny\) Goraya

### My Pronouns:

he/him

### Title:

Delivery Manager

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

Enterprise Automation &amp; Solution Delivery, UBC IT

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2021

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

Lead enterprise integration delivery and sustainment across UBC applications\.

---

### What is the best thing about your work/role at UBC?

The best part of my role at UBC is the people and the sense of purpose behind the work we do\. I enjoy bringing people together, solving meaningful challenges, and knowing that our work helps support students, faculty, and staff in ways that truly make a difference\. No two days are ever the same, and I appreciate the opportunity to continuously learn, grow, and build connections across the university community\.

---

### What is your favourite quote and why?

“Be the reason someone feels seen, heard, and capable of more\.”   
  
I like this quote because life moves so fast that we sometimes forget how much simple kindness and understanding can mean to someone\. I’ve learned that being present for people, listening genuinely, and appreciating the small moments in life often matter more than we realize\. Whether it’s family, friendships, or everyday interactions, I think the way we treat people leaves a lasting impact\.

---

### Do you have any hidden talents?

I’d say my hidden talent is bringing calm and clarity to complex situations\. I love connecting people, finding common ground and turning uncertainty into shared direction\. 

---

### What’s one personal experience you would encourage others to do/try?

I’d encourage people to intentionally develop emotional intelligence—the ability to listen deeply, stay grounded during uncertainty, and lead with empathy and self\-awareness\. Over time, I’ve realized that some of the most impactful moments in leadership and life come not from having all the answers, but from understanding people, building trust, and creating space where others feel heard and supported\. In a world moving faster every day, I believe human connection and emotional awareness are becoming more important than ever\.  
  
I also write reflections called [*The Sunny Perspective*](https://www.linkedin.com/newsletters/the-sunny-perspective-7361485725147582464/), where I translate everyday moments into lessons on leadership, purpose, and human connection\.
