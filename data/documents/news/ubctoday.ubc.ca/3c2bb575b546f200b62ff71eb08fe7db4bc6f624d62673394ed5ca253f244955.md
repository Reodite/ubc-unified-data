UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

February 13, 2025

UBC Vancouver faculty members are invited to submit letters of intent \(LOI\) for Continuous Learning Advancement Fund \(CLAF\) projects for 2025 funding\.  
  
CLAF supports the creation, renewal and expansion of non\-degree learning opportunities at UBC Vancouver, to help increase the university’s capacity to serve continuous learners in British Columbia\.  
  
The CLAF fund accepts proposals for non\-credit offerings, with preference given to proposals for fully online, micro\-credential, non\-credit programs serving continuous learners seeking to change \(reskill\) or advance \(upskill\) their careers in high\-demand sectors\.  
  
A total of $250,000 is available in funding for the 2025/26 CLAF cycle\.  
  
Key dates:

-   Letter of intent submissions due May 8, 2025
-   Full project proposals due July 24, 2025
-   Funding will be released to successful proposals by the end of November 2025

For details about the fund, including the application process, evaluation criteria and key dates, visit the [UBC Extended Learning website](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/about/continuous-learning-advancement-fund)\.  
  
If you have any questions, please email: [claf\.admin@ubc\.ca](mailto:claf.admin@ubc.ca)\.

**Simon Bates**  
Vice\-Provost and Associate Vice\-President, Teaching and Learning
