October 18, 2023

The Distinguished University Scholar awards recognize exceptional members of faculty who have distinguished themselves as scholars in research and/or teaching and learning\. The award is conferred by the President on the recommendation of an Adjudication Panel\.

Here are the 10 new Distinguished University Scholars at UBC in 2023:

-   Dr\. Purang Abolmaesumi \- Electrical and Computer Engineering, Faculty of Applied Science
-   Prof\. Joel Bakan \- Allard School of Law
-   Dr\. Allan Bertram \- Chemistry, Faculty of Science
-   Dr\. Lara Boyd \- Physical Therapy, Faculty of Medicine
-   Dr\. Scott Hinch \- Forest &amp; Conservation Sciences, Faculty of Forestry
-   Dr\. Catherine Johnson \- Earth, Ocean &amp; Atmospheric Sciences, Faculty of Science
-   Dr\. Daniel Justice \- English and Critical Indigenous Studies, Faculty of Arts
-   Dr\. Ara Norenzayan \- Psychology, Faculty of Arts
-   Dr\. Mary O’Connor \- Zoology, Faculty of Science
-   Dr\. Chris Overall \- Faculty of Dentistry
-   Dr\. Sunera Thobani \- Asian Studies, Faculty of Arts

The next call for nominations will be issued in early 2025\. 

[Learn more about the Distinguished University Scholar Program](https://academic.ubc.ca/awards-funding/funding-opportunities/distinguished-university-scholar-program)
