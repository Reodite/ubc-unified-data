August 18, 2025

The Teaching Development Program for new faculty supports early career instructors who teach credit courses at UBC Vancouver or UBC Okanagan\. 

Offered by the Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology, this flexible program can be completed in one or two semester\(s\), and includes online and in\-person elements\. It provides multiple pathways to completion, in order to accommodate early career instructors\.

 The deadline to register is August 25\.

[Learn more](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/faculty-programs/new-to-ubc/teaching-development-program-for-new-faculty/)
