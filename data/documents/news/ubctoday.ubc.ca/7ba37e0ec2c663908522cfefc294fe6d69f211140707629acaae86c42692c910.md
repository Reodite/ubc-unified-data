September 2, 2025

September 15–19 is Safety Awareness Week\. Stop by Campus Security’s information booths across the Vancouver campus to learn about theft prevention and helpful safety and security tips and resources at UBC\. It’s a chance to refresh your knowledge, ask questions, and stay informed\.

Don’t forget to download the [UBC Safe Vancouver app](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/ubc-safe-vancouver-app/?login#push_iphone) or the [UBC Safe Okanagan app](https://operations.ok.ubc.ca/security-and-emergencies/ubc-safeapp-and-ubc-alert/) and turn on push notifications to receive timely updates and urgent notifications\. The UBC Safe App is free and recommended for UBC students, faculty, staff and campus visitors\.

[Learn more](https://security.ubc.ca/2025/08/26/safety-awareness-week-is-september-15-19-2025/)
