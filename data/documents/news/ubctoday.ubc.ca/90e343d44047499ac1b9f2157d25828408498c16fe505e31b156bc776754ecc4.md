UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers, and senior professional leaders in Vancouver\.

September 27, 2023

We are pleased to announce the 2024/25 Grants for Catalyzing Research Clusters \(GCRC\) Competition\.  
  
The competition is open to new and returning clusters of research excellence in all disciplines on the Vancouver campus\. Applicants may select a budget term of one or two years\. Cluster leads must [submit a notice of intent](https://apply.research.ubc.ca/) by November 1, 2023\.  
  
This year, a number of changes have been incorporated into the program following external review and consultation, and are detailed [here](https://research.ubc.ca/vpri-competitions-initiatives/research-excellence-clusters/gcrc/revisions)\.  
  
More information about this funding competition, the application process and upcoming information sessions on October 16 and 17 is available [here](https://research.ubc.ca/vpri-competitions-initiatives/research-excellence-clusters/grants-catalyzing-research-clusters)\.  
  
Grants are enabled by the UBC Vancouver Academic Excellence Funds in support of Research Excellence Strategy 6 \(Collaborative Clusters\) in UBC’s strategic plan\.  
  
GCRC competitions are open to clusters led by researchers at UBC Vancouver\. Clusters led by researchers at UBC Okanagan are supported through the [Eminence program](https://research.ok.ubc.ca/funding-support/internal-funding/)\.

**Gail C\. Murphy**  
Vice\-President, Research &amp; Innovation  
  
**Gage Averill**  
Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver
