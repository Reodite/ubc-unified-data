    ## [Leadership change within the Vice\-President, Finance and Operations, portfolio](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/september-09-2022/leadership-change-within-vice-president-finance-and-operations-portfolio)
    
    I wish to announce that Karamjeet Heer will be leaving UBC to take up a new role at Simon Fraser University \(SFU\)\. Karamjeet joined the university in 2019 as Comptroller and was more recently appointed as interim Vice\-President, Finance\. Her last day at UBC will be October 31, 2022\.  Please join me in wishing Karamjeet well in her new role as Associate Vice\-President, Finance, at SFU\. This is an exciting opportunity and we wish her every success\.
