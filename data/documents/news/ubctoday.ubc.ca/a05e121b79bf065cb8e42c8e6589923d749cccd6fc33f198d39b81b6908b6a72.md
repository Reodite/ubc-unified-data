June 23, 2023

On June 28, join UBC’s Mass Care Open House &amp; Preparedness Fair to learn what the university is doing to prepare for major regional emergencies and how you can be better prepared\.  

You will have the opportunity to learn more about emergency preparedness and purchase emergency supplies for [your emergency kit](https://ready.ubc.ca/get-prepared/make-a-kit/)\.
