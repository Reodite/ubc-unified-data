On September 21, we invited you to [share a photo of what you're grateful for this season](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/september-09-2021/fall-reflections-snapshot-gratitude)\.

Thank you to those of you who have shared your photos with us\. In case you haven't already, here's your chance to share a photo that captures what the fall season means to you\. The deadline is **Monday, October 4**\.

**What do you need to do?**

-   Complete [this form](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/share-snapshot-your-fall-reflections) and submit a photo in high resolution by October 4\.
-   Share relevant image credits\.
-   Write a caption that describes your photo\.

All photo submissions will be published on UBC Today\.
