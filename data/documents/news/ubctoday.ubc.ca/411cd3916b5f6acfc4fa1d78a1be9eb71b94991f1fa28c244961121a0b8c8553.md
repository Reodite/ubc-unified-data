April 8, 2021

We are not choosing to work, teach, and learn from home, notes the introduction to the first high\-level principle in the [Guiding Principles for Online Course Adaptations](https://keepteaching.ubc.ca/?utm_source=UBCToday&amp;utm_medium=web&amp;utm_campaign=UBCToday_KTSGuidingPrinciples_April2021)\. When students, faculty and staff at UBC had to transition from life on campus to a mostly remote, online university, we found ourselves in uncharted territories\. Instructors had to re\-imagine how to share their knowledge differently, while students had to cope with a new definition of what life at a university looked like\. The truth is: nobody knew what to expect\. 

Yet, it did not stop instructors and program supervisors from doing what they love — helping students reach their goals — by relying on two important human values: compassion and care\.
