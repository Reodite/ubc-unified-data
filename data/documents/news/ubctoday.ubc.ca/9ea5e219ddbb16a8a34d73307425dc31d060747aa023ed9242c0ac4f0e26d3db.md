Last month, UBC hosted activities and events for Orange Shirt Day \(Sept\. 30\) to recognize and raise awareness about the history and consequences of the residential school system in Canada, and saw strong participation across the community\.

Take a moment to see highlights from the day, as well as view photos from this year’s intergenerational march in Vancouver\.
