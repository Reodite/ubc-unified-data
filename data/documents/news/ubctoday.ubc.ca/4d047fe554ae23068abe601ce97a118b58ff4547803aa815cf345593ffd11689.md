June 10, 2024

15 UBC\-led projects have been awarded a combined total of $9\.6 million through two competitions to conduct research that has the potential to have game\-changing impacts\.

Of these 15 projects, four projects are focused on climate and mitigation have been awarded a combined $6\.86 million\. The remaining 11 projects have been awarded a combined $2\.75 million as part of the NFRF \(New Frontiers in Research Fund\) Exploration Stream\.
