This message was sent to all faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan on behalf of the Office of the Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal\.

January 7, 2021

***A message from Lesley Cormack, Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues,

As many are already aware, planning is underway for UBC Okanagan’s downtown Kelowna site with a number of community\-accessible facilities being considered, including a new public gallery, creative innovation spaces and a public engagement and learning suite\.

To help share plans and better understand the needs of the community, we will be hosting a virtual open house for the public and our partners on January 19\. Participants will be invited to learn more about the building envisioned at 550 Doyle Avenue and to discuss how new and future spaces may serve the shared interests of the region and the university\.

While this event is designed primarily for the wider community, I’d also like to invite our faculty and staff to participate and to share this event with anyone that may be interested\.

Downtown presence open house

January 19 \| 7:00pm \- 8:00pm

[Learn more and register](https://ok.ubc.ca/about/community-engagement/downtown-presence-open-house/)
