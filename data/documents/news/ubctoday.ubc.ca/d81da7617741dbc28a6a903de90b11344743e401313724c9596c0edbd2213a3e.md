July 21, 2023

Effective July 24, a new Hybrid Work Agreement will be available in Workday that consolidates important elements of the original Hybrid Work Request and Work Area Safety Assessment into one simplified request process, and previous agreements will need to be replaced\.

All eligible staff who participate in UBC’s hybrid work program need to complete the new Hybrid Work Agreement in Workday, including individuals who already have a previous agreement in place\.
