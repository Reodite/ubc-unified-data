This message was sent to deans, directors, managers, heads of academic or administrative units, and members of the Okanagan Campus Communicators Network\.

July 15, 2021

***A message from Philip Barker, Vice\-Principal and Associate Vice\-President, Research &amp; Innovation, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues,

To support the identification, development, planning and implementation of new shared research facilities at UBC Okanagan, we are launching a call for proposals to help identify core infrastructure that could be housed within the new [Interdisciplinary Collaboration and Innovation \(ICI\) building](https://infrastructuredevelopment.ubc.ca/projects/interdisciplinary-collaboration-and-innovation-ici-building-ubc-okanagan/)\. Facilities that may be appropriate elsewhere on campus will also be considered\.
