July 13, 2021

[Safety &amp; Risk Services](https://srs.ubc.ca/contact-us/about-srs/) has created a series of online campus resources to support a safe and healthy work environment in preparation for increased on\-campus activity\. The online series will launch during the week of July 26\-30 with various live sessions, information sessions, and workshops\.

The series is intended for faculty and staff who have been working on campus throughout the pandemic, as well as those returning to work on campus later this year\. 

[Learn more](https://srs.ubc.ca/training/welcome-back-being-aware-and-taking-care/)
