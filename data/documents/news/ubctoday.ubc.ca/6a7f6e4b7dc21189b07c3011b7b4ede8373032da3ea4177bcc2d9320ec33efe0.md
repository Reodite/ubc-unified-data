May 31, 2021

If you're a faculty member interested in running a Global Seminar, [Go Global](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/go-global) is accepting proposals until June 30, 2021\.

[UBC Global Seminars](https://students.ubc.ca/career/international-experiences/global-seminars) are accredited courses, taught off\-campus, either as a full course or part of a course\. Global Seminars are two to six weeks during the summer term, and can be taught in the field, a combination of both on\- and off\-campus, or virtual\. Go Global provides customized, full\-service support, including planning, student services and financial management\.
