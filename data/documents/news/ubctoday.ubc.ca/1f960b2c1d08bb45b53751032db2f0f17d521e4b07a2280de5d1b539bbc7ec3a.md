May 19, 2026

The Wellbeing Fellows Program awards grants of up to $20,000 per year over two years to support faculty in advancing innovative approaches to wellbeing in teaching, learning, and academic workplace environments across UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan\.

Guided by UBC’s *Wellbeing Strategic Framework* and commitment to systems\-level change, this year’s funded projects explore topics including social connection, digital wellbeing, inclusive pedagogy, workload planning, mental health supports, and restorative classroom practices\.

This year, eight interdisciplinary projects representing six faculties across both campuses received funding to develop scalable initiatives that strengthen wellbeing, belonging, and resilience across the UBC community\.

1.  **Leveraging Insights into Teaching Assistant \(TA\) Wellbeing to Enhance TA\-Student Interactions Across Learning Environments**  
    Gail Hammond, Faculty of Land and Food Systems    
     
2.  **Enabling Collective Course Planning to Impact Students’ Academic Workload and Wellbeing**  
    Jaclyn Stewart, Faculty of Science  
     
3.  **Wellbeing from the Ground Up**  
    Laura Yvonne Bulk and Tal Jarus, Faculty of Medicine   
     
4.  **“Nature Snacks” Toolkit for Wellbeing Breaks in the Classroom**  
    Sara Baron, Faculty of Forestry   
     
5.  **Embedding Digital Wellbeing in Teaching and Learning: An Open\-Access Resilience Toolkit for Post\-secondary Classrooms**  
    Johanna Sam and Leyton Schnellert, Faculty of Education   
     
6.  **Connected in the Classroom: The Effects of Speed\-Friending on Classroom Community, Engagement, and Wellbeing**  
    Lauren Human and Tareq Yousef, Faculty of Arts  
     
7.  **Mind in Motion: Adapting an Exercise\-Based Program to Support Staff and Faculty Mental Health**  
    Guy Faulkner, Faculty of Education  
     
8.  **Trauma\-Informed Disability Justice Pedagogy: A Wellbeing Toolkit for Teacher Education**  
    Carly Christensen and Leyton Schnellert, Faculty of Education
