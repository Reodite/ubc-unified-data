May 27, 2025

Inspiring Community Grants provide up to $500 of funding to faculty, staff and students who wish to foster togetherness and connection on campus and in the Musqueam community – from art exhibits to cultural celebrations, the possibilities are endless\. Since 2016, this grant has funded over 200 projects led the UNC community and local residents of all ages\.

Applications for funding are now being accepted and will be reviewed on a rolling basis\. 

Details about this initiative, general guidelines and project eligibility are available on the UBC Inspired website\. 

[Learn more](https://inspired.ubc.ca/communitygrants/)
