March 10, 2022

Have you created openly licensed teaching resources that you want to share with the rest of the UBC community?

**The UBC OER Collection Challenge is a university\-wide competition to encourage faculty, staff and students to contribute open educational resources \(OER\) to a vital new open database, during March 7 to April 30\.**

Each year, the UBC community produces an abundance of open educational resources, from textbooks to course projects, workshops and tools\. The new [UBC OER Collection](https://oer.open.ubc.ca/) showcases the university's open educational resources in a searchable interface to help educators incorporate open educational resources and practices into curriculum\. 

Participate in the OER Collection Challenge with your Faculty or department and contribute open educational resources by April 30\.

[Learn more about the OER Collection Challenge](https://open.ubc.ca/the-ubc-oer-collection-challenge/)

[Read about the new curated database of openly accessible teaching and learning materials](https://about.library.ubc.ca/2022/03/07/open-ubc-launches-new-searchable-online-collection-to-highlight-ubc-open-educational-resources/)
