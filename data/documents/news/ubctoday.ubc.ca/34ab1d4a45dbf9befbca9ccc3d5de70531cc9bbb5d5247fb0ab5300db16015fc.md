August 22, 2023

In response to the evolving situation regarding the wildfires across the Central Okanagan region over the last few days, a number of UBCO Global messages have been sent to the community, with important information about how to access and provide support:

-   **Aug\. 18:** [Support during the Central Okanagan wildfire situation](https://news.ok.ubc.ca/2023/08/18/support-during-the-central-okanagan-wildfire-situation/)
-   **Aug\. 20:** [Support for students during the Kelowna wildfires](https://news.ok.ubc.ca/2023/08/20/support-for-students-during-the-kelowna-wildfires/)
-   **Aug\. 20:** [Kelowna wildfires: guidance for faculty and staff](https://news.ok.ubc.ca/2023/08/20/kelowna-wildfires-guidance-for-faculty-and-staff/)

Thank you to all the first responders and wildfire personnel on the front lines, as well as members of the UBC community who have remained united and supportive during these challenging times to ensure everyone's safety, security and wellbeing\.

For more information, read these [frequently asked questions](https://ok.ubc.ca/wildfire-response/)\. 

For the latest updates, follow [ok\.ubc\.ca](https://ok.ubc.ca/)\.
