November 18, 2020

The 2020 President’s Leadership Forum featured an afternoon of conversation around systemic racism and UBC’s commitment to inclusion\. The discussion was led by Professor Malinda S\. Smith, recently appointed Vice\-Provost, Equity, Diversity &amp; Inclusion \(University of Calgary\), along with Dr\. Ninan Abraham \(UBC\), Dr\. Frances Henry \(York University\), Dr\. Sheryl Lightfoot \(UBC\), Dr\. Minelle Mahtani \(UBC\), and Dr\. Handel Wright \(UBC\)\.
