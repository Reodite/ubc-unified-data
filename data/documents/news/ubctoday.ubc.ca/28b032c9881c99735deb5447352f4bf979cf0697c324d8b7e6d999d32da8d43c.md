December 2, 2024

Dr\. Mark MacLachlan, Professor of Chemistry and Canada Research Chair in Supramolecular Materials, has served as Dean *pro tem* for the Faculty of Science since January 1, 2024\. This extension is for the period January 1, 2025, until December 31, 2025, or the appointment of the new Dean\.
