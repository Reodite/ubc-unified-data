June 13, 2024

Join UBC’s delegation at COP29 to engage with the international climate negotiation process, share UBC’s leading research and climate actions, and build new partnerships with people from around the globe\.

The 29th United Nations Climate Change Conference of the Parties \(COP29\) summit is taking place in Baku, Azerbaijan, from Nov\. 11 to 22\. 

Each year UBC receives a limited number of observer badges for the negotiation space, known as the Blue Zone\. UBC is aiming to receive at least five badges for each week of COP29 \(Nov\. 11\-16 and Nov\. 17\-22\)\. 

Applications for the delegation at COP29 are open until **July 1**\.
