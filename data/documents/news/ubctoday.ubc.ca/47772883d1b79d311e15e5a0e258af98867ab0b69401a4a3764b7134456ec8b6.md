October 15, 2021

***A message from Ananya Mukherjee Reed, Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Okanagan***

As you are aware, Dr\. Gordon Binsted’s appointment as dean, Faculty of Health and Social Development, concludes on June 30, 2022\. 

Dr\. Binsted has recently been appointed as deputy provost, Markham, at York University and will conclude his term as dean, Faculty of Health and Social Development, on December 31, 2021\.

Gord has served in a leadership capacity since his initial appointment as acting dean, Faculty of Health and Social Development, in 2011 and 2012, followed by two five\-year appointments as dean in June 2012 and January 2018\.
