September 2, 2025

Faculty and staff play a crucial role in safeguarding personal information, and protecting it from unwarranted access, disclosure, or loss\. As such, UBC has a number of policies in place to ensure the safeguarding of personal data, and mandatory privacy and information security [training](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/training) is required for faculty and staff\.

In keeping with privacy and security due diligence, you will now be periodically required to agree to ‘Acceptable Use’ terms when you access Workday\.

To learn more about privacy and information, visit [Privacy Matters at UBC](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/)\.
