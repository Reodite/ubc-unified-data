April 21, 2026

UBC has announced this year's Killam Teaching Prize recipients\. Twenty\-eight faculty from UBCV and UBCO have been honoured for their contributions\. 

The [Killam Teaching Prize](https://academic.ubc.ca/awards/killam-awards-fellowships/killam-teaching-prize) is awarded annually to faculty nominated by students, colleagues and alumni in recognition of excellence in teaching\.

[Learn more](https://academic.ubc.ca/awards/killam-awards-fellowships/killam-teaching-prize)
