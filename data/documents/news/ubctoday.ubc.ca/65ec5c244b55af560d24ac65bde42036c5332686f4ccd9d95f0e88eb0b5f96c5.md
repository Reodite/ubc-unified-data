February 9, 2026

UBC Geering Up offers hands\-on summer camps for youth in Grades 1–12, designed to spark curiosity and build creativity in science, technology, engineering and math \(STEM\)\.

Did you know? As part of the UBC community, faculty and staff are eligible for discounted registration during select weeks\.

If you’re planning to register your child for a camp in Vancouver or Kelowna this summer, faculty and staff receive 24 hours of priority access starting Feb\. 17 at 9 am, before registration opens to the public on Feb\. 18\.

Learn more about the programs and email [geering\.up@ubc\.ca](mailto:geering.up@ubc.ca) for more information\.

[Camps in Vancouver](https://geeringup.apsc.ubc.ca/)

[Camps in the Okanagan](https://geeringupokanagan.ca/)
