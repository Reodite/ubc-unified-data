August 15, 2024

“Challenges in Partnered Research” is a new Q&amp;A series by [Partnering in Research](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/our-work/partnering-in-research/) that highlights individuals transforming policies, practices, and communities through collaborative research\.

In this conversation, Drs\. Henry Yu and Vicky Bungay share strategies to navigate the financial challenges of engaged research, as well as other critical obstacles such as ethical considerations, maintaining trust and reciprocity in partnerships, and ensuring the relevance and impact of their work\.
