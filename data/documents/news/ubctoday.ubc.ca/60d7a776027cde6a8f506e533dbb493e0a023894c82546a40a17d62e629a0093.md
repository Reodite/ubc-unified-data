August 31, 2020

#### UBC Vancouver

Translink will be suspending the following transit routes, effective September 7:

-   32 Dunbar / Downtown
-   44 UBC / Downtown
-   258 UBC / West Vancouver
-   480 UBC / Bridgeport Station

For more information, including alternative routes, visit the [Campus \+ Community Planning website](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/transit/translink-updates)\. 

---

#### UBC Okanagan

Kelowna Regional Transit is reducing the frequency of service to UBCO and suspending some routes temporarily until travel patterns resume\. Changes include:

-   The \#6 Glenmore route offering express bus service from downtown Kelowna, via Glenmore, and across John Hindle Drive accompanied by extended service hours and frequency
-   Re\-routing the \#4 Pandosy/UBCO Express from Highway 97 to Academy Way
-   Extending \#23 Lake Country service hours and frequency
-   Suspending the \#13 Quail Ridge route

For more information, visit the [Transportation website](https://transportation.ok.ubc.ca/transit/)\.
