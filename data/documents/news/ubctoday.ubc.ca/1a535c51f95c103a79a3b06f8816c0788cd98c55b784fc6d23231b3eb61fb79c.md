August 14, 2023

The Welcome Back BBQ is returning to Vancouver this year and is back again in the Okanagan\. A UBC tradition, the BBQ is a chance to enjoy a complimentary lunch, live entertainment and information booths that are interactive, fun and support your wellbeing, while mingling in\-person with your colleagues\.

The event will be held on Aug\. 31 for faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan, and on Sept\. 13 for staff at UBC Vancouver\.

*Since this was published, the Okanagan faculty and staff BBQ has been postponed and will be expanded to a campus\-wide event, including students \(date to be confirmed\)\.*

Learn more: [UBC Vancouver](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/staffbbq/) \| [UBC Okanagan](https://hr.ok.ubc.ca/learning/welcomebackfair-2023/)
