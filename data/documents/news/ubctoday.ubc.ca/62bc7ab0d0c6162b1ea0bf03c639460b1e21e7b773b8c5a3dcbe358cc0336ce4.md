November 17, 2025

UBC has launched *Push Forward*, the next evolution of the university’s brand campaign and further aligning with *FORWARD*, the university's fundraising and alumni engagement campaign\. The campaign highlights UBC’s role in driving positive change, while celebrating the work of faculty, staff, researchers, and students across the institution\. 

Featuring new illustration, animation, and video, the campaign introduces a refreshed approach to sharing UBC stories\. The first ads—spotlighting UBC’s economic impact and breakthrough research on removing microplastics from water—set an inspiring foundation for what’s ahead\. In the new year, additional stories will highlight work in wildfire resilience and accelerating healthcare through AI\.

*Push Forward* supports UBC’s strategic directions, including *Discover and Innovate for Impact* and *Partner for Purpose*, by harnessing creative expression to bring greater visibility to research and initiatives shaping positive change\.
