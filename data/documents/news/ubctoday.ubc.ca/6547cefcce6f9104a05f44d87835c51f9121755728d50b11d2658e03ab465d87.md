    ## [Participate in UBC's Remembrance Day ceremony](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/november-11-2025/participate-ubcs-remembrance-day-ceremony)
    
    Since the opening of the War Memorial Gym in 1951, The University of British Columbia has held a ceremony commemorating November 11\. This year, 2025 will mark seventy\-five years that The University of British Columbia has hosted a Remembrance Day ceremony\. This special ceremony is an opportunity for faculty, staff, students and members of the on and off\-campus community to honour and remember all those who served in times of war, military conflict and peace\.
