June 30, 2025

Before summer sets in, we’re inviting faculty across UBC to share a snapshot of their work through one of two short profiles:

Help us discover your [Research in focus](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/submit-your-research-focus)

Invite us into your [Teaching in focus](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/submit-your-teaching-focus)

These quick submissions help us highlight the range of research and teaching happening across the university\. It gives your colleages a sense of what’s being explored, tested, taught, and reimagined\. We feature selected submissions on UBC Today throughout the year to help connect faculty across disciplines and bring more visibility to the work happening in classrooms, labs, and communities\.

Whether you're deep into a long\-term project or just tried something new in your course this year, we'd love to hear about it\.
