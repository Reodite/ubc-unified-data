May 13, 2022

As part of a UBC Psychology experiment, pictures of a turtle and other marine animals were placed above recycling bins to see if they could influence people to reduce their plastic waste in offices\.

The research indicates that visualizing marine consequences helped reduce plastic waste by 17%\.
