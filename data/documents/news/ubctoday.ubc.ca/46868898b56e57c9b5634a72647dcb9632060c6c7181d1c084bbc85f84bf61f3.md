August 26, 2024

### Name:

Ash Raymond

### Title:

Registered Veterinary Technician 4

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

Animal Care Services

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2019

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

Veterinary health care with a focus on advanced anesthesia\.

---

### What work achievement are you most proud of?

I am most proud of recently becoming one of the only certified surgical research anesthetists \(SRA\) in Canada\.

---

### What is your favourite quote and why?

"I can do hard things\." This mantra has seen me through many challenging days\.

---

### What do you do regularly to invest in your mental and/or physical health?

Regular therapy sessions for the last four years, hiking, road/trail running, kick boxing, rock climbing and relaxing with my cat Ernie, diamond art or by reading mountaineering biographies and non\-fiction epidemiology novels\.
