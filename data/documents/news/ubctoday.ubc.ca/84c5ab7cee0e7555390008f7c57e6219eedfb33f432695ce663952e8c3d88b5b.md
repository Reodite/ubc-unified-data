August 17, 2021

The annual UN Climate Change Conference of the Parties \(COP\) brings together countries, civil society, companies and people on the frontline of climate change to accelerate action on the [UN Framework Convention on Climate Change](https://unfccc.int/process-and-meetings/the-convention/what-is-the-united-nations-framework-convention-on-climate-change)\.

Each year UBC receives a limited number of [observer badges](https://unfccc.int/process-and-meetings/parties-non-party-stakeholders/non-party-stakeholders/information-by-category-of-observer/admitted-ngos) for the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change Conference of the Parties \(UNFCCC COP\) negotiation space, known as the Blue Zone\. Members of the UBC community have attended COP22, COP23, COP24, and COP25 as official observers\. For COP26, UBC has received four badges for each week of the event \(October 31 \- November 6 and November 7 \- 12\)\. 

Faculty and staff are invited apply to receive one of UBC’s badges\. Submit an application by **Friday, September 10 at 12pm \(noon\)\.**
