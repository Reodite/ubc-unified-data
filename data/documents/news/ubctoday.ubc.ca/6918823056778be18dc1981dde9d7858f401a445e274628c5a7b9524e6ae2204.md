September 21, 2020

The Office of the Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver, invites currently funded [Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund](https://tlef.ubc.ca/) \(TLEF\) project teams to submit renewal applications for second\- and third\-year funding for their projects\. The application deadline is **3 pm on Thursday, November 12, 2020**\.

New TLEF project proposals are not being accepted during [this year’s call for proposals](https://tlef.ubc.ca/announcements/update-on-2021-22-teaching-and-learning-enhancement-fund/)\. As part of UBC’s response to COVID\-19, the Provost’s office redeployed $2 million dollars from the TLEF fund to assist UBC Vancouver Faculties with the transition to online instruction in the fall\.  
   
The results of this TLEF funding renewal round will be announced in February 2021\. 

If you have any questions, please contact [tlef\.admin@ubc\.ca](mailto:tlef.admin@ubc.ca)\.

[Learn more about the TLEF program](https://tlef.ubc.ca/)
