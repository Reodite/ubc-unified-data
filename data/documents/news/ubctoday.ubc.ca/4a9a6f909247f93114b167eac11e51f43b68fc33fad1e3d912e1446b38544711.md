May 12, 2022

***A message from Lesley Cormack, Principal and Deputy Vice\-Chancellor, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues,

It’s been a tough two years—but the normal things in life are starting to resume\. And opportunities to connect on campus are resuming, too\. I’m pleased to highlight the return of two meaningful opportunities to connect with our community on campus in the coming weeks:

1.  Volunteer at graduation
2.  Principal’s Town Hall
