October 28, 2024

Over 3,000 UBC Vancouver students will graduate this Fall\. Volunteers are needed to ensure smooth running of the 10 congregation ceremonies due to take place at the Chan Centre for the Performing Arts from Nov\. 27 to 29\. 

Volunteer roles are available for the [congregation ceremonies](https://graduation.ubc.ca/volunteer/?utm_source=Cyberimpact&amp;utm_medium=email&amp;utm_campaign=HR-Network-News--October-18-2024) and at the at the Alumni UBC [welcome receptions](https://alumni.ubc.ca/2024/featured-volunteer-opportunity/volunteer-at-graduation/) at the Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre\.

Find out what you can do to show your support\.

[Volunteer at a congregation ceremony](https://graduation.ubc.ca/generalvolunteersignup/)

[Volunteer at an Alumni UBC welcome reception](https://alumni.ubc.ca/2024/featured-volunteer-opportunity/volunteer-at-graduation/)
