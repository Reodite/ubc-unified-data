December 1, 2025

The Distinguished University Scholar awards recognize exceptional members of faculty who have distinguished themselves as scholars in research and/or teaching and learning\. The award is conferred by the President on the recommendation of an Adjudication Panel\.

This year’s recipients are:

-   [Mark Beauchamp](https://kin.educ.ubc.ca/beauchamp-mark/), Kinesiology, Faculty of Education
-   [Peter Black](https://urology.med.ubc.ca/dr-peter-black-appointed-head-department-of-urologic-sciences/), Urologic Sciences, Faculty of Medicine
-   [Lori Brotto](https://brottolab.med.ubc.ca/about/dr-lori-brotto/), Obstetrics &amp; Gynaecology, Faculty of Medicine
-   [Shawn Mansfield](https://forestry.ubc.ca/faculty-profile/shawn-mansfield/), Wood Science, Faculty of Forestry
-   [Takamasa Momose](https://www.chem.ubc.ca/takamasa-momose), Chemistry, Faculty of Science
-   [Barbara Pesut](https://gradstudies.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/barbara-pesut/), Nursing, Faculty of Health &amp; Social Development
-   [Terrance Snutch](https://psychiatry.ubc.ca/terrance-snutch/), Psychiatry, Faculty of Medicine
-   [Kiran Soma](https://psych.ubc.ca/profile/kiran-soma/), Psychology, Faculty of Arts
-   [Evan Thompson](https://philosophy.ubc.ca/profile/evan-thompson/), Philosophy, Faculty of Arts
-   [Vincent Wong](https://ece.ubc.ca/vincent-wong/), Electrical &amp; Computer Engineering, Faculty of Applied Science  
     

[Learn more about the Distinguished University Scholar Program](https://academic.ubc.ca/awards/distinguished-university-scholar-program)
