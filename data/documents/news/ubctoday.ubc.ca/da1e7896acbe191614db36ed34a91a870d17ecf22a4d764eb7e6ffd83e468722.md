December 7, 2022

With the end of the year just weeks away, there's still time left to the most your benefits and submit your extended health, dental and Health Spending Account claims before the year\-end deadlines:

-   **[Extended health claims](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/claims-and-forms/making-claim):** Deadline Dec\. 31
-   **[Dental claims](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/claims-and-forms/making-claim)** 
-   [**Health Spending Account claims**](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/benefit-plan-details/health-spending-account): For eligible employee groups

This is also a good time to review all your benefits information and make sure it is up to date\.
