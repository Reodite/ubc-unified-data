##### Name:

Jacqueline Ashby

##### My preferred pronouns:

she/her

### Title:

Program Coach &amp; Enhanced Skills Curriculum Advisor

### Faculty of Medicine, Department of Family Practice

UBC Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2015

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

Advise our team on faculty development, assessment/evaluation, and curriculum\.

---

### Name another staff or faculty member you admire and why

Ann Douglas, our Abbotsford\-Mission Family Practice Residency Program Site Coordinator\! She's brilliant, funny, and extremely efficient\. We're fortunate to have her navigating our ship\.

---

### What do you think is the greatest question ever asked?

Are we inventing math or discovering it?

---

### What’s one personal experience you would encourage others to do/try?

I try to learn something new every year that involves incorporating a different language and way of being\.
