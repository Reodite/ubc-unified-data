February 16, 2021

The Interdisciplinary Education Grant program supports UBC Vancouver faculty members in working together to build or refine curriculum on sustainability issues using interdisciplinary approaches\.

To apply, submit a proposal with another faculty member from a different Faculty, department or discipline by May 3, 2021\.

[Learn more](https://sustain.ubc.ca/teaching-applied-learning/interdisciplinary-education-grants)
