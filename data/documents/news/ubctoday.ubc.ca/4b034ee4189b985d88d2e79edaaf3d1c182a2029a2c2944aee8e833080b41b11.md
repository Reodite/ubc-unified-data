November 26, 2025

Adam Charania, UBC’s Vice\-President, Human Resources, has been named among the top global HR leaders\. 

The [2025 HR Global 100 report](https://www.hcamag.com/asia/best-in-hr/top-hr-executives-and-leaders-in-the-world-global-100/556819#winnersListSection) celebrates HR leaders who have shown exceptional dedication to their organizations and teams, navigating rapid change, safeguarding employee wellbeing, and supporting leaders through an evolving landscape\. 

Adam’s leadership reflects UBC’s commitment to fostering a workplace where innovation and empathy go hand in hand\. From working with leaders in advancing wellbeing initiatives to leveraging technology for better employee experiences, he continues to support a thriving, inclusive culture at UBC\.
