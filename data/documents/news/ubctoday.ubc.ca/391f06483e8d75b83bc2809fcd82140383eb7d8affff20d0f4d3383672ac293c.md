April 14, 2025

A total of $2\.44 million has been awarded to Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(TLEF\) projects this year\. A total of 66 projects aimed at advancing innovation in teaching at UBC were awarded funding\. This year’s funded projects include [13 Large TLEF Transformation projects](https://tlef.ubc.ca/funded-proposals/?filter_43=2025&amp;filter_42=&amp;filter_15=Large+TLEF&amp;filter_34=&amp;gv_search=&amp;mode=all#gv-view-3295-1) and [53 small TLEF Innovation projects](https://tlef.ubc.ca/funded-proposals/?filter_43=2025&amp;filter_42=&amp;filter_15=Small+TLEF&amp;filter_34=&amp;gv_search=&amp;mode=all#gv-view-3295-1)\. 

The 2025/26 funding round for Large TLEF Transformation projects included a special call for Generative AI \(GenAI\) Collaborative Cluster Grants to support UBC Vancouver project teams interested in experimenting with GenAI in teaching and learning\. A total of $1\.3 million has been allocated to a range of Gen AI initiatives that aim to tackle issues such as preparing students for an AI\-integrated workforce and responding to the evolving challenges GenAI presents to the role of writing in the Arts\.
