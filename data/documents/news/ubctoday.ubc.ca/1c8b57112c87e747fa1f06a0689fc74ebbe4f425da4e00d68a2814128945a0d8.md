May 25, 2026

The UBC Career Centre Work Learn program funds approximately 3,500 part\-time hourly appointments each year, subsidizing meaningful work experiences for current UBC Vancouver students\.

If you have a work opportunity to help students develop their professional skills and contribute to a work environment, submit a proposal to the Work Learn program for winter 2026 employment opportunities \(Sept\. 1, 2026–Apr\. 30, 2027\)\.

Submit a proposal by June 15\.

[Find out more](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-affairs/ubc-career-centre/work-learn)

Information about the Work Study program at UBC Okanagan, such as program goals and key dates, is available on the [Work Study website](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/hire-a-student/work-study/)\.
