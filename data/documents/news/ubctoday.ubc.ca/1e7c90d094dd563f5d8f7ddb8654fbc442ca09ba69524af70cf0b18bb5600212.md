March 16, 2026

UBC Community Engagement, in collaboration with the Office of the Vice\-President, Research and Innovation, announces the 2025–26 recipients of the Community–University Engagement Support \(CUES\) Fund\.

This year, $735,000 was awarded to support 30 projects, with grants of up to $25,000 each\. Through the CUES Fund, UBC supports faculty in building and sustaining reciprocal partnerships that connect research and teaching with community priorities across the province\. The 2026 projects reflect UBC’s Partner for Purpose commitment by expanding opportunities for faculty to engage in meaningful, community\-informed scholarship that responds to the diverse and evolving needs of communities across BC\.

Across BC, UBC faculty, students, and staff are working with community partners to tackle urgent issues, including protecting clean drinking water, strengthening food security, revitalizing Indigenous languages and knowledge systems, advancing culturally safe health care, improving newcomer health literacy, and fostering youth and intergenerational learning\.
