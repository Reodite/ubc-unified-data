May 12, 2025

### Name:

Colleen Kennedy

### My Pronouns:

she/her

### Title:

Cognitive Systems Program Coordinator

### Faculty/Department/Unit:

Cognitive Systems

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2023

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

COGS advising/admissions; hiring TAs/sessional instructors; course scheduling; budgeting; alumni relations/Tri\-Mentoring

---

### What is the best thing about your work/role at UBC?

Working with so many people who are passionate about cultivating a healthy learning environment for students\! That, and being regularly inspired by the creativity, ambition, and growth demonstrated by students in the COGS program\.

---

### What trait do you most admire in others?

I admire people who are able to address complex situations head\-on, acknowledging the reality of difficult truths with a calm, warm\-hearted demeanor\.

---

### What do you do regularly to invest in your mental and/or physical health?

To me, mental/physical health are one and the same\! Neglecting one impacts the other\. I try to exercise a few times a week \(yoga, jogging, hiking\), eat healthy foods \(incorporating treats as\-needed\), spend time with loved ones, and ensure I'm taking advantage of personal downtime to unwind/relax \(e\.g\. getting a massage, playing music, taking photos, reading\)\.
