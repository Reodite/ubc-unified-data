This message was sent to faculty in Vancouver and Okanagan\.

March 11, 2026

In recent months, we invited nominations for representatives for the UBC Board of Governors, as well as both Senates\. Thank you to everyone who submitted a nomination\.  
  
At this stage in the 2026\-2029 triennial election process, we are putting out an additional call for nominations for faculty representatives to serve on the Okanagan and Vancouver Senates\.  
  
Nomination forms and information about remaining positions and eligibility are posted [online](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/elections-and-search-committees/2025-2026-triennial-elections/faculty-and-professional-librarian-representatives-okanagan-and-vancouver-senates-2025-2026)\.  
  
Nominations close at 4pm on March 25\.  
  
For general questions, please contact UBC Elections at [elections\.information@ubc\.ca](mailto:elections.information@ubc.ca)\.  
  
**Rella Ng**  
Associate Vice\-President, Enrolment Services and Registrar
