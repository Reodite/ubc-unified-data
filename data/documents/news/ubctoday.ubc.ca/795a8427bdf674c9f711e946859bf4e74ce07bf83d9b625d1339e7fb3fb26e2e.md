July 26, 2021

Areas of British Columbia  are experiencing wildfires and associated air quality impacts due to smoke\. Wildfire smoke can be an issue during the summer and into the fall\. 

More information on air quality in both regions is available here: [Metro Vancouver](http://www.metrovancouver.org/services/air-quality/current-air-quality/airmap/Pages/default.aspx) \| [Okanagan](https://www.regionaldistrict.com/your-services/air-quality-program/current-air-quality.aspx)

 Provincial link: [https://www\.env\.gov\.bc\.ca/epd/bcairquality/data/aqhi\-table\.html](https://www.env.gov.bc.ca/epd/bcairquality/data/aqhi-table.html) 

 For tips on reducing your exposure to wildfire smoke, visit [https://ready\.ubc\.ca/take\-action/fire/](https://ready.ubc.ca/take-action/fire/)\.

 For UBC workplace health and safety information, please go [here](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/occupational-hygiene/wildfire-smoke-health-safety/)\.
