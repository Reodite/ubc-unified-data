December 14, 2020

With many faculty and staff transitioning to remote working and teaching in 2020, there has been an increase in emails exchanged and files shared\. Privacy Matters @ UBC and the University Records Management Office offer tips and strategies to help you and your team declutter digital files during December\. 

During this month, work together as a team to clean up transitory email records, purge and organize shared files to increase efficiency and reduce storage costs\.

[Learn more](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/digital-declutter)
