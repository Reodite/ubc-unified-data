November 18, 2024

The UBC Okanagan community is invited to give their feedback regarding an update to the existing UBC Okanagan campus plan\. 

The campus plan, which was created in 2015, is being updated to inform how UBC Okanagan will use its land over the next 20 years\. The first round of engagement took place in spring 2024\.

The second round of engagement is currently underway\. Faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan can provide feedback in an [online survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6PbFNzMKYU0azK6) \(deadline Nov\. 29\) or by visiting various on\-campus information events between Nov\. 22 and 27\.

[Find out more](https://campusplanning.ok.ubc.ca/planning-and-development/projects-and-engagement/campus-plan-update/)
