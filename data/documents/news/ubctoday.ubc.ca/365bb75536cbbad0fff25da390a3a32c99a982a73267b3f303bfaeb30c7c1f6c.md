March 16, 2026

Eight projects strengthening UBC’s sustainability leadership have received $500,000 in funding through the 2025 Campus as a Living Lab \(CLL\) Fund Competition\.​

Faculty and staff from more than 28 academic departments and operational units across UBC Vancouver submitted proposals blending creativity and research excellence to tackle pressing sustainability challenges\. The selected projects span power management, biodiversity conservation, AI in education, virome research, urban agriculture, fibre gardens, green lab sustainability, and native plant chemical mapping\. 

The bi\-annual CLL Fund Competition will open again for project proposals in September 2027\.​
