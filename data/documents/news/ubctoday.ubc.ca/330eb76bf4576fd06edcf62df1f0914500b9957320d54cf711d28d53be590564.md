This message was sent to faculty and staff in the Okanagan\.

April 13, 2022

***A message from Rehan Sadiq, Provost and Vice\-President Academic, pro tem, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues,

I am pleased to announce that Dr\. Greg Garrard will serve as Dean *pro tem* in the Faculty of Creative and Critical Studies from July 1, 2022, to January 31, 2023, during Dean Bryce Traister’s administrative leave\.

Dr\. Garrard is the Associate Dean, Research and Graduate Studies and a Professor of Environmental Humanities in the Faculty of Creative and Critical Studies\. Dr\. Garrard joined UBC Okanagan in 2013 and co\-designed the Interdisciplinary Graduate Studies Sustainability Theme and the Bachelor of Sustainability degree\.
