April 2, 2024

The [Indian Residential School History and Dialogue Centre](https://irshdc.ubc.ca/) \(IRSHDC\) is opening its doors to the UBC Vancouver community on April 15 to highlight its important role as a shared space that encourages dialogue about the Residential School system and the on\-going impacts of colonialism in Canada\. 

Ahead of the [Open House event](https://irshdc.ubc.ca/2024/03/07/join-us-at-our-open-house/), hear from Executive Director, [Kristin Kozar](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/november-11-2023/kristin-kozar-appointed-executive-director-indian-residential-school-history) about how the IRSHDC staff support Residential School Survivors by providing access to records and her plans to expand the centre’s role in supporting teaching and learning at UBC\.

[Learn more about the IRSHDC](https://irshdc.ubc.ca/2024/03/28/executive-director-kristin-kozar-discusses-the-work-of-the-irshdc-and-its-forthcoming-open-house/)
