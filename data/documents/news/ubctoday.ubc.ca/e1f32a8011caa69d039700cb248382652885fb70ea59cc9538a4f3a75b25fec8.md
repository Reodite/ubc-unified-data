This message was sent to faculty and staff in the Okanagan\.

August 25, 2023

*A message from the Office of the President and Office of the Principal and Deputy Vice\-Chancellor*

Dear colleagues,

There’s no doubt that these past weeks have been trying for all of us\. That said, I’m incredibly encouraged by the real progress being made in the community now that many evacuation orders have been lifted\.

While campus remains on evacuation alert, staff are now returning to regular operations and the work to welcome our community for the fall term continues at full pace\.
