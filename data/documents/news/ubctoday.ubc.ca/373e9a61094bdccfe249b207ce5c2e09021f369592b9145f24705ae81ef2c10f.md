September 22, 2026

Orange Shirt Day is an opportunity to learn and stand in solidarity with the Indigenous communities on campus and beyond\. It is also a time to recognize the ongoing work of truth and reconciliation and consider how each of us can contribute to this work\.

Join members of the UBC community for the [Intergenerational March at UBC Vancouver on Sept\. 30](https://forestry.ubc.ca/events/2026-intergenerational-march-to-commemorate-orange-shirt-day/) to connect with the original inhabitants of the land and begin conversations at home about a difficult era in Canadian history\.

Explore resources and events at your campus:  
[UBC Vancouver](https://irshdc.ubc.ca/orange-shirt-day/) \| [UBC Okanagan](https://ok.ubc.ca/about/indigenous-engagement/orange-shirt-day/)
