November 28, 2022

The Scholarship of Teaching and Learning Seed Program provides graduate research assistants, collegial collaborations, and complementary project funding for faculty interested in exploring and understanding the impact of their pedagogical choices\.

UBC Vancouver faculty members are invited to apply by **January 4, 2023**\. Priority is given to faculty members who teach at the undergraduate level, and who have not had an active Seed project in the last 12 months\. 

[Learn more](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-seed-program/?login)
