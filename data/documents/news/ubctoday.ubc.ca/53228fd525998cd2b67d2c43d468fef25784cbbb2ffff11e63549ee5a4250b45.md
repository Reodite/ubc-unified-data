November 6, 2023

The Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\) Seed Program helps you explore and understand the impact of pedagogical choices by providing graduate research specialists, collegial collaborations, and complementary project funding\.

Previously, the SoTL Seed Program was only open to faculty members at UBC Vancouver\. This year, the program has been expanded to include all faculty and staff at UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan\. 

Applications are open until Nov\. 20\.

[Learn more](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-seed-program/)
