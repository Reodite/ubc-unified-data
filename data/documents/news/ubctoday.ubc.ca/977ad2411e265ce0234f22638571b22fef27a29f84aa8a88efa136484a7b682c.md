October 5, 2020

British Columbians will elect a new provincial government on Saturday, October 24, 2020\. If you're planning to vote in this year's election, there are a number of voting options available to you, including by mail, during advance voting days and at a polling station on Election Day\. 

On his website, Santa Ono provides detailed information on who is eligible to vote, how you can vote, and what Elections BC is doing to make voting safe during the pandemic\.
