January 19, 2026

Faculty in Vancouver and the Okanagan are invited to apply to the Wellbeing Fellows Program\. Receive up to $20,000 per year for two years to bring a wellbeing project to life, with ongoing support and connection through monthly cohort meetings\.

As a Wellbeing Fellow, you will:  

-   Support instructor and student wellbeing in the classroom and academic workplace settings  

-   Enhance your knowledge of the health and wellbeing landscape at UBC and beyond 

-   Build your network among like\-minded faculty and staff across different departments and disciplines and leverage their expertise to advance shared goals 

[Learn more](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/wellbeing-fellows-program)
