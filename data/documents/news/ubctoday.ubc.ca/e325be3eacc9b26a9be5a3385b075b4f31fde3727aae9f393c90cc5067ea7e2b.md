November 14, 2023

Are you seeking certification as a professional coach and want to apply your new coaching skills and knowledge to working with fellow UBC staff and faculty?

Through the Coaching Internship program, you will gain valuable experience coaching faculty and staff in addition to professional development, mentorship and $1,500 towards the tuition cost of the accredited coach training program of your choice\. Applications are due Dec\. 10\.  

You can join an information session to learn about coaching as well as the internship program details and timelines, followed by a Q&amp;A\.   
Nov\. 16, 2023: [Register now](https://wpl.ubc.ca/browse/coaching/courses/wpl-cp-ficip)  
Dec\. 1, 2023: [Register now](https://wpl.ubc.ca/browse/coaching/courses/wpl-cp-ficipd)

[Find out more](https://hr.ubc.ca/career-development/coaching-staff-and-faculty/coaching-internship-program#)
