May 30, 2024

Nine UBC postdoctoral researchers have received Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships\.

The Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships is a program that provides federal government funding to the best national and international postdoctoral applicants who will positively contribute to Canada’s economic, social, and research\-based growth\. Fellowships are awarded to post\-doctoral researchers who are considered are part of Canada’s next generation of research leaders\.

UBC's new Banting scholars are:

-   Dr\. Anne\-Sophie Archambault: Faculty of Medicine, Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine
-   Dr\. Jonah Garde: Faculty of Arts, Department of Central, Eastern, and Northern European Studies
-   Dr\. Zhenwei Ma: Faculty of Medicine, Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine
-   Dr\. April Martinig: Irving K\. Barber Faculty of Science, Department of Biology, UBC Okanagan
-   Dr\. Jamie McDevitt–Irwin: Faculty of Science, Department of Zoology
-   Dr\. Codie Primeau: Faculty of Medicine, Department of Physical Therapy
-   Dr\. Christina Prokopenko: Irving K\. Barber Faculty of Science, Department of Biology, UBC Okanagan
-   Dr\. Minghan Xu: Faculty of Applied Science, Norman B\. Keevil Institute of Mining Engineering
-   Dr\. Marco Zenone: Faculty of Arts, School of Public Policy &amp; Global Affairs
