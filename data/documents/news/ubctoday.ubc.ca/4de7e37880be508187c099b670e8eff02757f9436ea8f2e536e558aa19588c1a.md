This message was sent to all faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan on behalf of the Office of the Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal, UBC Okanagan\.

August 11, 2021

***A message from Lesley Cormack, Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues,

Please join me for a virtual Fall Town Hall on Thursday, September 2, starting at 9 a\.m\. as we kick off the 2021/22 academic year\. At this event, I will share the plans for the upcoming year as we aim to realize the vision of our 2040 plan, followed by a Q&amp;A panel with our senior leadership team\.

We will also recognize many important contributions with the 2021 [Staff Awards of Excellence](https://hr.ok.ubc.ca/learning/awards-and-recognition/staffexcellence/)\. This year, we have more awards than ever before, reflecting the extraordinary contributions made in the unprecedented COVID year and the many diverse talents and skills that make UBC Okanagan such a great place to work, learn and teach\.

Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal’s Fall Town Hall and Staff Awards of Excellence  

**Thursday, September 2, 2021** 

9 to 9:45am – Update and Q&amp;A Panel  
9:45 to 10am – Staff Awards of Excellence
