This message was sent to all faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan on behalf of the Office of the Provost and Vice\-President, Academic\.

November 25, 2021

***A message from Ananya Mukherjee Reed, Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Okanagan; and Brad Wuetherick, Associate Provost, Academic Programs, Teaching and Learning, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues, 

As part of UBC’s standard practice under the Board of Governors [Policy AP8](https://universitycounsel-2015.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/10/Deans-Extension-Policy_AP8.pdf) and the Senate Policy on [Reviews of Administrative Units](https://senate.ubc.ca/vancouver/policies/reviews-administrative-units), we are arranging for an external review of the UBC Okanagan Faculty of Creative and Critical Studies\. 

The terms of reference guiding the review include the following objectives: 

-   to determine the effectiveness of the Faculty in delivering high\-quality undergraduate and graduate training; 
-   to evaluate the Faculty’s leadership and administration; 
-   to assist the university in its consideration for the re\-appointment of the dean of the Faculty; 
-   to assess the Faculty’s standing nationally and internationally; and 
-   to advise on the future development of the Faculty\.
