We want to hear from faculty and staff who are leading the way in generating positive change at the university\. 

This fall, we want to uncover and celebrate the individuals making changes within the university who haven’t yet received the spotlight\. If you are shaking up how things are done or thinking outside the box, we want to hear from you\.  

The deadline to submit your story idea is **5pm on June 14**\.

*For anyone who is interested in sharing their story idea, please note that our team will be conducting interviews and pursuing photography \(if necessary\) in June and July 2024\.*
