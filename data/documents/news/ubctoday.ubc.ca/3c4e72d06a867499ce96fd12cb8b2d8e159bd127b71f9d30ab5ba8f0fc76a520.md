January 22, 2024

The UBC Okanagan community is invited to participate in a [community engagement survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_bJ9sbQ7WOF05bRs) regarding an update to the existing UBC Okanagan campus plan\. 

The campus plan, which was created in 2015, is being updated to inform how UBC Okanagan will use its land over the next 20 years\. 

Community engagement will take place from Jan\. 22 to Feb\. 9, including: [a community engagement survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_bJ9sbQ7WOF05bRs), an open house in the ADM foyer on Jan\. 23 and pop\-up information booths throughout the Okanagan campus\. 

The updated Okanagan campus plan will reflect the evolving needs of the campus and the new priorities which have emerged against the backdrop of a growing region\. These include a need for more housing and better transportation, accommodating hybrid learning and working, deepening UBC’s relationship with the Syilx\* Okanagan Nation, and adapting to a changing climate\.  

[Learn more](https://campusplanning.ok.ubc.ca/projects/cpu/)
