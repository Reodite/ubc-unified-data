UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers, and senior professional leaders in Vancouver and the Okanagan\.

January 23, 2025

We are pleased to announce that UBC has embarked on a search for a new Dean, Faculty of Medicine, and Vice\-President, Health\. The current Dean and Vice\-President, Dr\. Dermot Kelleher, has served in the role since 2015, with his second term ending in summer 2025\.  
  
Reporting to the Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, and with reporting lines to the President, this role provides strategic and academic leadership across the Faculty and the university, driving excellence in education, research, and clinical service\. The Dean and Vice\-President will foster a collaborative environment, ensuring that UBC and the Faculty of Medicine continue to enhance our reputation for innovation, interdisciplinary research, and commitment to improving the health of individuals and communities locally, nationally, and globally\.   
  
In accordance with Board Policies AP5 and AP12, an advisory committee has been established to support the search process\. The committee consists of:

-   Gage Averill, Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver \(Chair\)
-   Nabil Ali\-Mohamad, undergraduate student
-   Aslam Anis, Professor and Director, School of Population and Public Health
-   Lara Boyd, Professor, Department of Physical Therapy
-   Natalie Chan, UBC Board of Governors
-   Darren Dahl, Dean, Sauder School of Business
-   Susan Forwell, Professor, Department of Occupational Science &amp; Occupational Therapy
-   Leonard Foster, Professor, Department of Biochemistry &amp; Molecular Biology and Michael Smith Laboratories
-   Grant Guo, UBC Vancouver Senate
-   Sandy Hilton, UBC Board of Governors
-   Shanda Jordan Gaetz, Managing Director, Faculty of Medicine
-   Faisal Khosa, Associate Professor, Department of Radiology
-   Anjana Rajendran, graduate student
-   Manuela Reekie, UBC Okanagan Senate
-   Eugenia Socías, Associate Professor, Department of Medicine
-   Victoria Wood, Managing Director, UBC Health

A member of the Provost's Office will act as non\-voting secretary\. We are also supported in this search by Boyden\. If you have any questions about this search, please contact Kate Carr at [kate\.carr@ubc\.ca](mailto:kate.carr@ubc.ca)\.  
  
Learn more about this role [here](https://academic.ubc.ca/academic-community/searches/dean-faculty-medicine-and-vice-president-health)\.  
 

**Benoit\-Antoine Bacon**  
President and Vice\-Chancellor  
  
**Gage Averill**  
Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver
