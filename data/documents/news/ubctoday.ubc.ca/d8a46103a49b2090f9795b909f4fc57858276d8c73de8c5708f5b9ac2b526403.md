May 4, 2026

UBC has achieved Platinum certification from Excellence Canada, recognizing its ongoing leadership in psychological health and safety, and wellbeing, supported by system\-wide frameworks, partnerships, and everyday workplace practices\.

The standard and framework align closely with the National Standard of Canada for Psychological Health and Safety\. 

*“Achieving Platinum certification is an important milestone for UBC\. It confirms our collective progress in creating a university where psychological health and safety, as well as wellbeing are embedded in how we work, lead, and support one another,*” said Benoit\-Antoine Bacon, UBC’s President and Vice\-Chancellor\. “*This recognition reinforces our responsibility to continue advancing this work as a core part of our commitment to nurturing a strong and vibrant UBC community\.”*
