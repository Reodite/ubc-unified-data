October 8, 2025

While [William Shakespeare’s First Folio](https://about.library.ubc.ca/2022/01/12/william-shakespeares-first-folio-published-in-1623-gifted-to-ubc-library/) was much celebrated when it arrived at UBC in 2021, did you know that UBC Library also has a Second Folio of Shakespeare’s works? 

The Second Folio, digitized by UBC Library’s Digitization Centre, is now publicly accessible through [Open Collections](https://open.library.ubc.ca/collections/shake2nd)\.
