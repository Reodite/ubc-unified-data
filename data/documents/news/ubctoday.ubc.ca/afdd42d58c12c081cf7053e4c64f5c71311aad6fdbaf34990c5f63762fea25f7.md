October 30, 2025

As the weather changes, protect yourself and others by getting your flu vaccine \(flu shot\)\. 

At UBC Vancouver, flu clinics will run on Nov\. 5 and Nov\. 12\. You can book an advance appointment through the provincial government’s [Get Vaccinated system](https://www.getvaccinated.gov.bc.ca/)\.

At UBC Okanagan, you can obtain a flu shot at a public clinic off campus or through a community pharmacy\.

Find out more: [UBC Vancouver](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/safety-programs/communicable-disease-prevention-framework/flu/) \| [UBC Okanagan](https://operations.ok.ubc.ca/health-and-safety/communicable-disease/vaccination-clinics/)
