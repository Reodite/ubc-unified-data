February 20, 2024

The Sustainability Coordinator Program provides faculty and staff with opportunities and resources to promote and implement sustainable practices at work at UBC Vancouver\.

It allows interested faculty and staff to work up to four paid hours per month \(subject to supervisor approval\)\. The program is aimed at enhancing sustainability awareness within your Faculty/unit, fostering relationships, and promoting sustainable practices across UBC through peer\-to\-peer learning and knowledge sharing\. Applications are accepted on an ongoing basis\.

[Learn more](https://sustain.ubc.ca/get-involved/sustainability-coordinator-program)
