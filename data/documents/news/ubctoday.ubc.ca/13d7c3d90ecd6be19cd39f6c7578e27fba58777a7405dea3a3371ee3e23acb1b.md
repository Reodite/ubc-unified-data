February 17, 2026

As UBC faculty members, you are invited to share how you are using Generative AI \(GenAI\) in your teaching, including any opportunities or concerns these tools raise for you\. This short, anonymous survey welcomes your perspective, whether you are experimenting with the technology, approaching it cautiously, or still forming your view\. Your input will help shape UBC’s approach to GenAI in ways that reflect your needs and teaching realities\. 

The survey is open from **February 17 \- March 9, 2026**\.

[Share your perspective on GenAI](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_bfGcchvB9xUfPO6)
