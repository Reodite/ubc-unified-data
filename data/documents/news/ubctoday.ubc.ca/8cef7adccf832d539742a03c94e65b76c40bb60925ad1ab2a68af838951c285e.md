    ## [Cancer drug shows potential as treatment for muscular dystrophy](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/june-06-2022/cancer-drug-shows-potential-treatment-muscular-dystrophy)
    
    Researchers at UBC’s School of Biomedical Engineering have discovered that an existing cancer drug could have potential as a treatment for muscular dystrophy\. The researchers found that the drug — known as a colony\-stimulating factor 1 receptor \(CSF1R\) inhibitor — helped slow the progress of Duchenne muscular dystrophy in mice by increasing the resiliency of muscle fibres\.

    ## [UBC experts call for proactive approach to prepare for more extreme wildfires](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/june-06-2022/ubc-experts-call-proactive-approach-prepare-more-extreme-wildfires)
    
    Wildfires have grown in frequency, intensity and overall burned area due to climate change\. This year, communities in B\.C\. and Canada are bracing for this pattern to continue\. UBC forestry researchers Dr\. Lori Daniels, Dr\. Kelsey Copes\-Gerbitz and Dr\. Kira Hoffman are wildfire experts whose research focuses on how natural disturbances such as wildfires and droughts, human factors and climate interact to affect how easily—or not—forests burn\.
