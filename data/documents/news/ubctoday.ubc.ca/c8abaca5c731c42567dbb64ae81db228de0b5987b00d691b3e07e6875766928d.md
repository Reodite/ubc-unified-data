May 20, 2026

More than 850 faculty and staff came together for the 16th annual Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day, hosted by Human Resources and Recreation\. Under sunny skies, teams engaged in friendly competition, while dressed in creative costumes\. The event was made possible by a dedicated organizing team and more than 100 volunteers, whose enthusiasm and hard work helped create a memorable celebration of community and connection\.

[View the UBC Vancouver photos](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/)

[Find out about the awards and results at UBC Okanagan](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/events/faculty-staff-sports-day/)
