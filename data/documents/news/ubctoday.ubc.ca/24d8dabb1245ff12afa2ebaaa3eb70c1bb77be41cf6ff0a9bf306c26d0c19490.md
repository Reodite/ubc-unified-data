    ## [Update on the Integrated Renewal Program — Student](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/june-06-2021/update-integrated-renewal-program-student-0)
    
    Over the past year, the university has completed major renewal projects related to our Human Resources and Finance systems under the Integrated Renewal Program \(IRP\)\. This resulted in the launch of Workday last November, which represented a significant step forward in modernizing our learning, research, and working environments at UBC\. Thank you to all those who supported the launch of Workday last fall, as well as to those who continue to support the ongoing adoption of our new systems and processes\.
