February 8, 2022

***A message from Lesley Cormack, Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues,

I am pleased to inform you that the search for UBC Okanagan’s next Provost and Vice\-President, Academic has commenced\.

The executive search firm Perrett Laver has been retained to support the search\. In the coming weeks, Perrett Laver will be consulting with the UBC community to familiarize themselves with key opportunities and challenges for the role, as well as ideal traits and competencies of a successful candidate\. The community is invited to submit confidential feedback on this topic in writing to [barbara\.siqueira@perrettlaver\.com](mailto:barbara.siqueira@perrettlaver.com) by February 28, 2022\.
