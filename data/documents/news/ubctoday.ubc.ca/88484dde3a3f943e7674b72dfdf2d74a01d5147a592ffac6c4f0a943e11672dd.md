    ## [Share your perspective on Generative AI in teaching](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/february-02-2026/share-your-perspective-generative-ai-teaching)
    
    As UBC faculty members, you are invited to share how you are using Generative AI \(GenAI\) in your teaching, including any opportunities or concerns these tools raise for you\. This short, anonymous survey welcomes your perspective, whether you are experimenting with the technology, approaching it cautiously, or still forming your view\. Your input will help shape UBC’s approach to GenAI in ways that reflect your needs and teaching realities\. The survey is open from February 17 \- March 9, 2026\.

    ## [Early access to registration for Geering Up STEM summer camps](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/february-02-2026/early-access-registration-geering-stem-summer-camps)
    
    UBC Geering Up offers hands\-on summer camps for youth in Grades 1–12, designed to spark curiosity and build creativity in science, technology, engineering and math \(STEM\)\.Did you know? As part of the UBC community, faculty and staff are eligible for discounted registration during select weeks\.If you’re planning to register your child for a camp in Vancouver or Kelowna this summer, faculty and staff receive 24 hours of priority access starting Feb\. 17 at 9 am, before registration opens to the public on Feb\. 18\.
