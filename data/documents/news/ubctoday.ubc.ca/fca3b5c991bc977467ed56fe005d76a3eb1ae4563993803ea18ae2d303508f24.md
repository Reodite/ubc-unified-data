UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers, and senior professional leaders in Vancouver\.

March 20, 2023

External reviews are conducted periodically to ensure that UBC’s Faculties and Administrative Units are supporting the university’s academic mission\. In April, the Faculty of Science will undergo an external review as part of this process\.  
  
The external review provides the opportunity for faculty and staff to submit comments, and includes interviews with key stakeholders conducted by visiting reviewers\. You can access the terms of reference [here](https://academic.ubc.ca/sites/vpa.ubc.ca/files/documents/External-Review-Science-ToR-2023.pdf)\.  
  
The reviewers will meet with stakeholders on April 26\-28, 2023\. They are:

-   **Dr\. Lisa Graumlich**, Professor and Dean Emerita, College of the Environment, University of Washington
-   **Dr\. Paul Chaikin**, Professor, Department of Physics, College of Arts and Science, New York University
-   **Dr\. Mark Giesbrecht**, Dean, Faculty of Mathematics, University of Waterloo

UBC faculty and staff are also invited to provide written comments relevant to the matters under review to Dr\. Herbert Rosengarten, the review coordinator, at [hjr@exchange\.ubc\.ca](mailto:hjr@exchange.ubc.ca), by April 23, 2023\. Please include your name and affiliation to UBC\.  
  
Your comments will be held in strict confidence and will be forwarded to the reviewers\.  
  
Thank you in advance for engaging in this process\.

**Gage Averill**   
Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver
