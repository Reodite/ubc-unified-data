September 13, 2021

### Name:

Treslyn Sundher

### Title:

Executive Assistant

### Department/Unit:

Office of the Vice\-President, Students

### Location:

Vancouver

### Year I started working at UBC:

2007

---

### Describe what you do at UBC in 10 words or less:

I work in the Office of the Vice\-President, Students and provide support to the Vice\-President of Students and the Executive Director\.

---

### What’s the best advice you ever received?

The best advice I ever received and still follow to this day is to live in the moment and never stress or sweat the small stuff especially, when they are beyond your control\.  Life is too short, so enjoy it as much as you can\.

---

### What three words would others use to describe you?

Calm, quiet and patient\.

---

### What do you do regularly to invest in your mental and/or physical health?

Taking time out for yourself and exercising is the best thing for your mental and physical health\. Always take care of yourself\.
