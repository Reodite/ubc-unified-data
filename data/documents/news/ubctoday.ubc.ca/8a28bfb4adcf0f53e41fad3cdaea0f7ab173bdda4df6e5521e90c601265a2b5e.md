September 14, 2020

Later this week, a total of 104 staff members from UBC Vancouver, including 63 new inductees, will be honoured for their more than 25 years of service to UBC, at the annual *25 Year Club* celebration\.

Due to COVID\-19, this year's celebration will be held virtually on **September 18**\.

The *25 Year Club* was established in 1971 by President Walter Gage to recognize the contributions of staff\. In addition to inducting staff who have reached this major 25\-year milestone, the event also honours those with 35 and 40 years of service, and \- for the first time ever and in celebration of the 50\[th\] anniversary of the *25 Year Club* \- 50 years of service as well\. 

See a full list of club members:

-   [Staff with 25 years or more of service](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/honours-recognition/25-year-club/25-year-club-new-members-2020/)
-   [Staff with 35 years of more of service](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/honours-recognition/25-year-club/35-year-club-members-2020/)
-   [Staff with 40 years or more of service](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/honours-recognition/25-year-club/40-year-honourees-2020/)
-   [Staff with 50 years or more of service](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/honours-recognition/25-year-club/50-year-honourees-2020/)

Congratulations to all of those inducted and honoured this year\!

UBC Okanagan will be recognizing staff the 2020 Length of Service honourees in October\. 

[Learn more](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/honours-recognition/25-year-club/)
