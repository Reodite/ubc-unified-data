Help your colleagues learn more about you and your work at UBC

A **Staff in focus** is a short, informative update about you and your work at UBC, that will be shared with all faculty and staff on the [UBC Today website](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/) and in the weekly newsletter\. 

If you’d like to share more information about you and your work on the UBC Today website, please complete the information below\.

Once you’ve sent your information, a member of the Internal Communications team will reach out and let you know when it will be published on the UBC Today website\.

**Please note: Staff in focus is only open to current staff members\.**
