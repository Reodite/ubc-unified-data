April 13, 2022

This spring, UBC Vancouver will be celebrating the achievements of nearly 10,000 students over 28 ceremonies on May 25, 26, 27, 30, 31, June 1 and 2\. In total, over 25,000 people participate in the spring graduation ceremonies: from graduating students, faculty members, dignitaries and guests\.

Volunteers are essential to help welcome and lead participants, ensuring a smooth and seamless experience for graduates, faculty, and guests\. Get involved by volunteering to support the event\. As a volunteer, you will play an instrumental role in making the experience memorable for everyone\.

A number of volunteering opportunities are available for faculty and staff\. Detailed instructions for each of the key roles needed for the graduation ceremonies are available on the registration page\. Find out what you can do to show your support\.

[Get involved](https://graduation.ubc.ca/volunteer/)
