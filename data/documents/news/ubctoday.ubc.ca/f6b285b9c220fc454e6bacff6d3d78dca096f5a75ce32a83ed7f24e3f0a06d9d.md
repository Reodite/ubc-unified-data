March 10, 2025

The Healthy Workplace Initiatives Fund Program is an annual fund of up to $3,000 available to UBC departments and units to support grassroots activities that promote wellbeing in the workplace\.

The program provides start\-up funds and support for health\-related, sustainable initiatives\. You can apply for funding for programs such as mental health training; healthy eating and nutrition; art initiatives; community gardens; team challenges; and fitness classes, to name a few\.

The deadline to apply for funding is **Apr\. 7**

[Learn more](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/workplace-wellbeing-programs/healthy-workplace-initiatives-fund-program)
