August 20, 2023

***A message from Alex Bayne, Managing Director, Human Resources, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues,

Let me begin by recognizing the resilience, resolve and professionalism of students, faculty and staff during these distressing past few days\. I am very grateful that we have a community that remains united and supportive despite the challenges we face\.

UBC’s Okanagan campus remains under evacuation order; this is a dynamic and evolving situation and we will continue to provide updates as matters evolve\. Your wellbeing, safety and security is our top priority\.
