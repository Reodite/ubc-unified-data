December 2, 2024

Faculty and staff who drive or take transit to the Vancouver campus should plan for extra travel time between Dec\. 10 and 13 as Wesbrook Mall will be closed between University Boulevard and Student Union Boulevard for 24 hours per day during that entire period due to ongoing construction\.
