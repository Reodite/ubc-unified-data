November 3, 2020

UBC is hosting a virtual ceremony for Remembrance Day

Since the opening of the War Memorial Gym in 1951, UBC has held a ceremony commemorating November 11\. 2020 marks the 69th year that UBC has hosted a Remembrance Day ceremony\. This year, due to COVID\-19, the Remembrance Day ceremony will be a virtual event, featuring a live broadcast from War Memorial Gym, with short readings, remarks from special guests and musical performances by the UBC’s School of Music\.

All UBC faculty, staff, students and community members are invited to watch—to honour and remember all those who served in times of war, military conflict and peace\.

**UBC Remembers  
November 11, 2020**

**10:45am**

Registration not required
