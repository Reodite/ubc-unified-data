May 27, 2024

The UBC Career Centre Work Learn program funds approximately 3,500 part\-time hourly appointments each year, subsidizing meaningful work experiences for current UBC Vancouver students\.

If you have a work opportunity to help students develop their professional skills and contribute to a work environment, submit a proposal to the Work Learn program for winter 2024 employment opportunities \(September 1, 2024 – April 30, 2025\) by June 17\.

Information and resources are available to support you:

1.  View the Work Learn [Submission Guide](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/sites/facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/WL%20Submission%20Guide_0.pdf) for writing your proposal and the [step\-by\-step guide](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/sites/facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/WL%20Submitting%20Work%20Learn%20Proposals_Step%20by%20step.pdf)
2.  If you need support developing or updating a Work Learn proposal:
    -   Attend a webinar on Writing a Work Learn Proposal
        -   May 29, 11:00AM \([zoom details](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/63905768270?pwd=UWdnR09mYkRId3dzVXZCWGw2Sk1JQT09#success)\)
        -   If you are unable to attend the webinar, [you can watch a pre\-recorded version here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q_roESL_O7Q&amp;list=PLtqusylF2444JR_Xj781IC8h7_mX41Ny5&amp;index=4)
    -   Bring your questions to Work Learn Proposal Office Hours
        -   June 4, 11:00AM \([zoom details](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/65605109643?pwd=YWJIZnNiVjV5cUxTSms4TmRqWmlIUT09#success)\)
        -   June 13, 1:00PM \([zoom details](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/64792654047?pwd=RXJkdVI3c0VldHNIVnFoK0NqTmUvZz09#success)\)

[Learn more](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-affairs/ubc-career-centre/work-learn)

Information about the Work Study program at UBC Okanagan, such as program goals and key dates, are available on the [Work Study website](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/hire-a-student/work-study/)\.
