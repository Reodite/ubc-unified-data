September 22, 2025

How you get to campus shapes daily life at UBC Okanagan\. By sharing your experiences in the 2025 Transportation Survey, you’ll help guide decisions that can make commuting easier, more affordable, and more sustainable for all faculty and staff\. 

Share your input and complete the survey by October 10\.

[Find out more](https://campusplanning.ok.ubc.ca/planning-and-development/plans-and-policies/transportation-planning/transportation-reports-studies/)
