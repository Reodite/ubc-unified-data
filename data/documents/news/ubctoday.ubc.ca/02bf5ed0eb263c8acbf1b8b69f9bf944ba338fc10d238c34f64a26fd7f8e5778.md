UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers, and senior professional leaders in Vancouver\.

December 11, 2023

I would like to announce that Dr\. Mark MacLachlan has been appointed Dean *pro tem* of the Faculty of Science effective January 1, 2024, replacing Dr\. Meigan Aronson at the completion of her term\.  
  
Dr\. MacLachlan is a professor of chemistry and has served as Associate Dean, Research and Graduate Studies, in the Faculty since 2016\. Over his tenure as Associate Dean, he launched STAIR, a collaborative, interdisciplinary research grant program within the Faculty, as well as new support for visiting early career scholars from underrepresented groups\. He has held a Canada Research Chair and is a Fellow of the Royal Society of Canada\.  
  
Dr\. Aronson’s term as the current Dean concludes on December 31, 2023\. On behalf of the university, I would like to thank Dr\. Aronson for her steady leadership and for the key role she has played in the many achievements of the Faculty of Science over the past five years\.  
  
During Dr\. Aronson’s term as Dean, more than 100 new faculty have joined the Faculty of Science, substantially increasing diversity\. The Faculty of Science advanced three interdisciplinary hiring clusters in strategically key areas \(Biodiversity, AI, and Pandemic Preparedness\) using the President’s Academic Enhancement Initiative \(PAEI\) funds, as well a Black Faculty Hiring Cluster Hire in Environmental Computation and another in Food Sustainability\. Dr\. Aronson also led the development of a new strategic plan \(Improving Our World through Science\) for the Faculty and provided strong leadership through the COVID\-19 pandemic\.  
  
I am grateful to Dr\. Aronson for her leadership and many contributions to the university\. The search for a new Dean for the Faculty of Science will launch in 2024\.

**Gage Averill**  
Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver
