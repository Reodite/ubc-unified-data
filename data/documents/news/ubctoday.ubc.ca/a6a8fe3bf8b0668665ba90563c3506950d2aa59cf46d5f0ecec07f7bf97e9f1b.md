This message was sent to all faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan on behalf of the Office of the Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Okanagan\.

October 18, 2021

***A message from Ananya Mukherjee Reed, Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Okanagan***

Dear colleagues,

As you know, Dr\. Roger Sugden will conclude his final term as dean, Faculty of Management on June 30, 2022\. During the last 18 months, a number of processes have been disrupted or delayed as many resources have been redeployed to support our students, faculty and staff through the COVID\-19 pandemic\.

As such, in order to have additional time to give full and proper consideration to the Faculty of Management’s search for its next leader, I have requested Dr\. Sugden to serve as dean *pro tem* for up to 12 months, effective July 1, 2022\. In accordance with Board policy AP8, I have also sought and received approval for the same from President Ono\.

I would like to take this opportunity to thank Dr\. Sugden for his willingness to continue to lead the Faculty through this unprecedented period\.
