January 17, 2022

Do you have a creative idea to make your lab or research activity more sustainable?

The Green Labs Fund is available to all faculty, staff and students at UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan\. Apply to 2022 Green Labs Fund for the opportunity to receive up to $4,000 for your innovative plan\.

To be eligible, proposals should:

-   Strengthen sustainability at UBC through reduction of environmental impacts of research activities
-   Benefit the UBC scientific community
-   Be within the range of $250 to $4,000
-   Have an immediate or short\-term benefit and practical applicability

Submit your project summary, supporting materials, and supervisor approval to [green\.labs@ubc\.ca](mailto:green.labs@ubc.ca)\. The deadline to apply is **Sunday, March 20, 2022**\.

[Find out more](https://sustain.ubc.ca/get-involved/green-labs-program/green-labs-fund)
