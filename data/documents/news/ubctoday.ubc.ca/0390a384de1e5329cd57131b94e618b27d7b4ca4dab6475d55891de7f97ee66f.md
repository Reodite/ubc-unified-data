January 19, 2026

Each year, the Undergraduate Student Research Awards \(USRA\) program funds approximately 250 full\-time appointments for undergraduate students, subsidizing research work experience that complements their studies in an academic setting\. 

UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan faculty members can receive $6,000 in wage subsidies to hire Canadian undergraduate students full\-time during summer 2026, fall 2026 or winter 2027 by submitting a proposal through the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada \(NSERC\) USRA program\. 

Faculty members are required to hold an active NSERC grant from the [research grant list](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/sites/facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/NSERC_USRA_2026-27_Proposed_Eligible_Research_Grant_List_11.27.2025.pdf)\. Faculties/Schools have the ability to endorse non\-NSERC grant holders to apply for the award, on the approval of the Associate Dean or an appropriate designate\.

Application deadlines vary by faculty and are open until March 20, 2026\.

[Learn more](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-affairs/ubc-career-centre/undergraduate-student-research-awards)
