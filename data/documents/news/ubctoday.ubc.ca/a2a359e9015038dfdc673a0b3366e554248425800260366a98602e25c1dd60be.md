UBCBulletin Updates for leadership

This message was sent to executive, deans and other senior academic leaders, academic heads, directors and managers, and senior professional leaders in Vancouver\.

November 6, 2025

As weather patterns begin to shift towards extreme winter weather, we would like to remind you of information available to help you be prepared\.  
  
If UBC is subject to extreme winter weather conditions, the administration may decide to curtail in\-person learning activities and other non\-essential services\. In the event of extreme weather, you are advised to regularly check the [UBC homepage](https://www.ubc.ca/) for campus status updates\.  
  
**Preparing faculty and staff in your department/unit for extreme weather:**  
  
We recommend you determine which staff in your department/unit have responsibilities that are of an essential nature, such that they would be required to attend campus during extreme weather, and ensure they are aware of their responsibilities\. For questions or help in determining what work is of an essential nature, contact your [HR Advisor](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/administrators/contact/)\.   
  
You are requested to review your department/unit’s extreme weather procedures, make any necessary updates, and share it with all faculty and staff in your department/unit\.  
  
Please ensure that those in your department/unit know what to do if extreme weather prevents them from getting to campus, and that campus remains operational\. For questions regarding policy in this scenario, contact your [HR Advisor](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/administrators/contact/)\.   
  
Please review your approved [hybrid work agreements](https://hr.ubc.ca/hybrid-work-ubc) and extreme weather procedures within your department/units to support those who can work remotely when extreme weather prevents them from getting to campus\.   
  
For more information regarding extreme weather safety and protocol, visit the [HR website](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/faculty-staff-resources/extreme-weather-conditions/)\. 

We also recommend UBC students, faculty, staff and campus visitors download the [UBC Safe Vancouver app](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/ubc-safe-vancouver-app/) and turn on push notifications to receive immediate information \(e\.g\. weather advisories\) and updates that are impacting the entire campus\. Learn more about the UBC Safe Vancouver app on the [Safety &amp; Risk Services website](https://srs.ubc.ca/ubcsafe)\.   
  
**Campus safety during extreme weather:**   
  
Find out about road clearing priorities and ice prevention work on the UBC Vancouver campus during snow events, and how to report slippery conditions [on the Facilities website](https://facilities.ubc.ca/services/ice-prevention-and-snow-removal/)\. 

**Supporting students impacted by extreme weather:**   
  
Faculty are encouraged to consider alternative arrangements to accommodate the needs of students who may be unable to travel safely to campus to attend class, write tests or turn in assignments, due to extreme weather conditions\.   
  
Thank you for your diligence in helping us prepare for extreme weather at UBC\. With your support, we hope to keep disruptions to a minimum\. 

**Gage Averill**  
Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, UBC Vancouver
