December 12, 2023

Launched in Jan\. 2023, UBC’s [Strategic Equity and Anti\-Racism \(StEAR\) Framework](https://equity3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/stear-framework-and-roadmap-for-change/) set out the university’s integrated approach to advancing equity and anti\-racism commitments and priorities drawn from across a number of institutional plans\. Now, almost a year since its launch, a number of efforts have moved the implementation of this work forward\.
