January 20, 2021

It's been ten years since the launch of the UBC Sustainability Initiative – Ten years of connecting, sharing knowledge, and working in partnership to advance sustainability teaching, learning, research, operations and infrastructure, and community development\. 

If you’re interested in finding opportunities to collaborate and advance sustainability, the 2019\-20 Annual Sustainability Report outlines the plans, policies, programs, and projects where UBC is actively working towards sustainability goals\.
