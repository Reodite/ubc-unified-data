July 8, 2024

Due to ongoing work to transition UBC’s student information system to Workday Student, from July 31, faculty and staff will be unable to submit a [tuition waiver](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/benefit-plan-details/tuition-waivers) via Workday for 2024 summer credit courses \(i\.e\. courses that started on or before July 3, 2024\)\.

Tuition waivers being submitted after July 31 must be done via a [general Workday support request](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=755a0f911b0eb050b8c7ff3ecc4bcb27)\.

Faculty and staff who wish to submit a tuition waiver for a 2024 winter session credit course \(i\.e\. courses starting on Sept\. 3, 2024 and beyond\) will be able to do so from Aug\. 1, 2024\.

[Find out more](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/news/july-2-2024/submit-your-tuition-waiver-2024-summer-session-credit-courses-july-31)
