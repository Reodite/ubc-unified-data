Gone are the days of crossing students’ names off lengthy lists to check them into exams\. With LTIC’s new UBC Exam Card Scanner mobile app, instructors and TAs can scan UBC Student cards to quickly and securely check\-in students and manage flagged issues  
through Canvas\.

[The app is ready to be enabled in Faculty of Science courses now\!](https://ltic.ubc.ca/ubc-exam-card-scanner-digitally-check-in-students-during-exams/#enabling-ubc-exam-card-scanner-for-your-course)

[Illustration of a UBC Student Card \(with the text 'UBC card'\)\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/adaptive-icon-300x300.png)

## As it stands currently

To confirm whether a student is present as themselves during an exam, instructors and TAs must manually check student cards and obtain their signatures on packets of paper\. As course sizes range anywhere from 20 – 4,000 students with exams spread across multiple classrooms, this process can be long, slow and error\-prone\.  

## Introducing the UBC Exam Card Scanner app 

UBC Exam Card Scanner is a mobile application synced to Canvas that invigilators can use to scan a UBC Student card, obtain an image of the student, mark their presence or flag an issue on the app itself\. Flags and comments can then be reviewed and resolved by the instructor through Canvas\. In large courses, the app can enable instructors and TAs to minimize time, labour and errors in verifying student identities and absences, even in exams spread across multiple locations\.

### Key features include:

-   **Photo verification via scan**: Easily verify the student’s identity against a big, clear photo of the student pulled securely from the UBC Student card database\. 
-   **Real\-time flag management system**: View and resolve flags and comments from invigilators across exam locations through the Canvas course shell\. 
-   **Digital record of attendance**: Track how many students have been checked\-in and how many remain\. 

By eliminating the need for bulky paper packets and manual counting, the UBC Exam Card Scanner app enables speedy and secure verification of students’ attendance in large exams using just a smartphone\.

The UBC Exam Scanner addresses a long\-standing area of improvement in exam invigilation by providing teaching staff with a tool that facilitates accuracy, efficiency and coordination in the high\-stakes environment of examinations\.  

## Enabling UBC Exam Card Scanner for your course

Instructors in the Faculty of Science can start using UBC Exam Card Scanner this term\! [Contact the LT Hub](https://lthub.ubc.ca/support/lt-hub) by March 20, 2026 to enable the app for your course\. 

The UBC Exam Card Scanner will be made available to the broader UBC community following the BETA testing phase\. An LTIC announcement will follow\.
