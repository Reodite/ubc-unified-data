Please note that we will be closed for the winter holidays starting from Wednesday, December 23\.

-   **Dec 18 Friday Noon:** Cut\-off time to submit air photo orders for regular turnaround time\. After cut\-off time only RUSH orders \(\+$40\) will be processed before the winter holidays
-   **Dec 22 Tuesday:** Last business day of the year\. Last day photo orders will be shipped out from our office\. Please do not return photo packages until after winter break unless they will be delivered by Dec 22\.
-   **Dec 23 Wednesday:** GIC closes
-   **Jan 4 Monday:** GIC reopens

As the GIC remains closed to the public until further notice due to Covid\-19 some library services are still available\. Please contact us by e\-mail or phone for inquiries\. Our business hours are: **Monday to Friday 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM**\.

We wish everyone a safe and happy holidays\!
