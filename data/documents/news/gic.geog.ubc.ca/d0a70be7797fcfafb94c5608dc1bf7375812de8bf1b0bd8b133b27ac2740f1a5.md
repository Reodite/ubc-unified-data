The GIC will be closed from **Wednesday, December 24** to **Sunday, January 4\.**

Key dates:

-   **December 18, Thursday Noon:** Final cut\-off time to submit orders for regular turnaround time\. After cut\-off time only RUSH orders \(\+$50\) will be processed before the winter holidays\.
-   **December 22, Monday:** Last day orders will be shipped out from our office\. ***Please do not return photo packages until after winter break unless they will be delivered by December 22*\.**
-   **December 24, Wednesday:** GIC closes  
    **January 5, Monday:** GIC reopens
