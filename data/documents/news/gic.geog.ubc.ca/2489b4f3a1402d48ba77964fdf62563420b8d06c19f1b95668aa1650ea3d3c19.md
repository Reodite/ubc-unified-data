The GIC will be closed from **Friday, December 24 to Monday, January 3**\.

Please note the follow key dates for aerial photo orders:

-   **Dec 20 Monday Noon:** Final cut\-off time to submit orders for regular turnaround time

After cut\-off time only RUSH orders \(\+$40\) will be processed before the winter holidays

-   **Dec 22 Wednesday:** Last day orders will be shipped out from our office\.  
    Please do not return photo packages until after winter break unless they will be delivered by Dec 22\.
-   **Dec 24 Friday:** GIC closes
-   **Jan 4 Tuesday:** GIC reopens

We have everyone a safe and wonderful holidays\!
