The GIC will be closed from **Friday, December 22 to Tuesday, January 2\.**

Key dates:

-   **Dec 18 Monday Noon:** Final cut\-off time to submit orders for regular turnaround time\. After cut\-off time only RUSH orders \(\+$40\) will be processed before the winter holidays\.
-   **Dec 20 Wednesday:** Last day orders will be shipped out from our office\. Please do not return photo packages until after winter break unless they will be delivered by Dec 21\.
-   **Dec 22 Friday:** GIC closes  
    **Jan 2 Tuesday:** GIC reopens
