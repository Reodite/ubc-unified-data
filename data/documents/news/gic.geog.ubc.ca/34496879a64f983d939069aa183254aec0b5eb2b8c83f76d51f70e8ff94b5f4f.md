The GIC will be closed from **Friday, December 23 to Monday, January 2**\.

Please note the following key dates for aerial photo orders:

-   -   **Dec 19 Monday Noon:** Final cut\-off time to submit orders for regular turnaround time\. After cut\-off time only RUSH orders \(\+$40\) will be processed before the winter holidays
    -   **Dec 21 Wednesday:** Last day orders will be shipped out from our office\. Please do not return photo packages after winter break unless they will be delivered by Dec 22\.
    -   **Dec 23 Friday:** GIC closes
    -   **Jan 3 Tuesday:** GIC reopens

We wish everyone a safe and wonderful holidays\!
