**UBC Library Open Education Impact &amp; Activity Report**

**2025/2026**

UBC Library’s [Open Education services](https://scholcomm.ubc.ca/open-scholarship/open-education/) seek to support the UBC community in finding, adapting, and creating high\-quality open educational resources, as well as planning and implementing innovative Open Education projects and Open Practices\.

UBC Library’s Open Education services play an active role in moving forward [UBC Vancouver Library’s Strategic Framework](https://about.library.ubc.ca/about-us/strategic-framework/) vision of,

**…leading and collaborating to advance open scholarship…**

Additionally, the supports and services for open education reinforce UBC Okanagan Library’s [commitment](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/strategic-framework/) to,

**… support\(ing\) transformative learning experiences\.**

The following report was developed by [Erin Fields, Open Education &amp; Scholarly Communications Librarian](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/people/view/798) \(Vancouver Campus\), [Donna Langille, Open Education Librarian](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/donna-langille/) \(Okanagan Campus\) and [Leila Malkin, Scholarly Communications Assistant](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/people/view/1266) \(Vancouver Campus\) to highlight UBC Library’s combined impact on open educational practices at UBC for 2025/2026\.

## **UBC OER Collection** 

Developed in partnership with the UBC Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology \([CTLT](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/)\), [UBC Library](http://library.ubc.ca/) [and UBC Okanagan Library, the UBC OER Collection](https://oer.open.ubc.ca/) is a curated database full of openly accessible teaching and learning materials\. [The UBC OER Collection](https://oer.open.ubc.ca/) showcases UBC open educational resources \(OER\) in a searchable interface to support both UBC faculty and the general community in incorporating open educational resources and practices into their curriculum\. 

The collection was launched March 7, 2022 and currently houses **94 OER** developed by UBC faculty, students, and staff\.

## OER in cIRcle

A team across UBCO and UBCV Libraries has been working to improve the discoverability of open educational resources in [cIRcle](https://circle.ubc.ca/about/), UBC’s institutional repository\. The work focuses on including more OER to the repository and adding enhanced metadata in record abstracts using [LRMI standards](https://www.dublincore.org/about/lrmi/)\.

**New to cIRcle:**

-   [Integral Calculus with Applications to Life Sciences](http://hdl.handle.net/2429/92278) is an open textbook that integrates calculus, linear algebra, probability, and statistics, using clear explanations and life sciences applications to support accessible, skills\-reinforcing learning\.
-   [Queer Geography Dictionary](http://hdl.handle.net/2429/90611) is a student\-created open resource from UBC Okanagan that defines key terms in queer human geography, with reading lists that foreground queer, feminist, and anti\-colonial perspectives\.

## Zines as Open Pedagogical Practice

UBC Library supported zine\-based open pedagogy across both campuses, helping instructors and students use zines as flexible, low\-cost formats for creative and public\-facing coursework\. At UBC Vancouver, librarians collaborated with faculty on classroom zine projects, providing support for research, ethics, copyright, and intellectual property\. Training materials developed from this work, including slides, activities, rubrics, and sample zines, are openly available through [UBC Open Collections](https://open.library.ubc.ca/cIRcle/collections/ubclibraryandarchives/67246/items/1.0396341?o=3)\. At UBC Okanagan, Library staff continued to build community around student zine creation through the annual [Student Zine Fair](https://www.instagram.com/p/DH_YlW0pZGJ/?hl=en&amp;img_index=1) and the [UBCO Library Zine\!](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2024/12/Final-Zine-2024-for-web-optimized2.pdf), highlighting zines as a creative approach to teaching, learning, and sharing student work beyond the classroom\.

## OER Excellence and Impact Awards

[Image](https://open-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/07/GJxNcEvWUAACqTS-940x526.jpg)

The **OER Excellence and Impact Awards** are a collaboration between the [Office of the Provost and Vice\-President, Academic](https://provost.ok.ubc.ca/), [UBC AMS](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/), [SUO Okanagan](https://www.suo.ca/), UBC Vancouver and Okanagan Library, are teaching and learning awards that recognize outstanding work done by faculty at both UBC campuses who materially advance the use and impact of open educational resources in credit courses at UBC\. The award program consists of one individual and one team\-based award of $5,000 each year per campus\.
