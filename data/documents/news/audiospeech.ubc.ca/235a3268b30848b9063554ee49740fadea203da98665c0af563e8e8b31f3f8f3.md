Audiology student Lareina Abbott is the recipient of a Paetzgold Fellowship\. These fellowships are endowed by Cordula Paetzgold and the University of British Columbia for full full\-time master’s or doctoral students, with preference given to graduate students with disabilities, First Nations graduate students, and women students pursuing graduate study in medicine, neuroscience, genetics, health care and epidemiology, pharmaceutical sciences, music, theatre and film, forestry and architecture\.

Congratulations, Lareina, for the award of such a strongly competitive fellowship\!
