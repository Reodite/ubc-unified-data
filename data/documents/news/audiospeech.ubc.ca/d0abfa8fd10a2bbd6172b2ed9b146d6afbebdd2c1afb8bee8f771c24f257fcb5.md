A celebratory reception was held following the UBC graduation ceremony\. Below are some photos from the event, including the presentation of the following 2024 Student Awards:

Canadian Academy of Audiology Student Award, **Jennifer Lam**

Alan B\. Clemons Award for Distinction in the Field of Speech\-Language Pathology, **Michelle Foo**

Heil\-Christie Service Award in Speech\-Language Pathology, **Annika Fong**

Speech and Hearing BC Award for Outstanding Final Year Student in Audiology, **Bing Wu**

Speech and Hearing BC Award for Outstanding Final Year Student in Speech\-Language Pathology, **Robyn Jones**

The Tavistock Trust for Aphasia University Student Prize, **Holly Davies**
