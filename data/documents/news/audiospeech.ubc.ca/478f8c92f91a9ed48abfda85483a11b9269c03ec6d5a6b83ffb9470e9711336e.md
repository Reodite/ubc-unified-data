The School of Audiology and Speech Sciences would like to express our heartfelt congratulations to our partners at the Western Institute for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing for receiving $1 million funding from the Ministry of Health for a new state\-of\-the\-art centre of excellence in Vancouver\.

Read all about the project on [WIDHH’s website](http://www.widhh.com/news/2018/09/28/ministry-health-supports-widhhs-new-centre-excellence/)\. The School looks forward to continuing our work together at the New Centre of Excellence\.
