The School of Audiology and Speech Sciences would like to congratulate our recent graduate, Martine Schlagintweit, for receiving the SAC National Clinical Certification Exam Award\! Martine was awarded the prize for attaining the highest score on the 2018 Audiology clinical certification exam\.

For more information about the award and to hear about other deserving winners, visit the [SAC website](https://www.sac-oac.ca/about-sac/award-winners#martine-schlagintweit)\.
