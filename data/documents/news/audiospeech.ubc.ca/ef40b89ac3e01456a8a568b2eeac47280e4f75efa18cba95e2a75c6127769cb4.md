May 1, 2025

The School of Audiology &amp; Speech Sciences would like to congratulate **Sophia Cook**, **Olivia Côté**, **Yu Cen Gao**, **Winston Lee**, and **Kevin Tyson**, recipients of 2025\-2026 Speech\-Language &amp; Audiology Canada \(SAC\) scholarships:

Sophia Cook \(Audiology, 2nd year\), Audiology Leadership Scholarship

Olivia Côté \(Speech\-Language Pathology, 2nd year\), Isabel Richard Scholarship *and* Virginia Martin Memorial Scholarship

Yu Cen Gao \(Speech\-Language Pathology, doctoral\), Student Achievement Award

Winston Lee \(Audiology, 1st year\), Elks &amp; Royal Purple Fund for Children, Susan Lane Scholarship

Kevin Tyson \(Speech\-Language Pathology, 2nd year\), Student Achievement Award

These prestigious awards recognize students who exemplify resilience, dedication to their program, and outstanding academic achievement\.

Please join us in celebrating their success\. Congratulations to each of you on this well deserved honour\!

For more information, please visit the [Speech\-Language &amp; Audiology Canada website](https://www.sac-oac.ca/joining-the-profession/scholarships/)\.
