It is our pleasure to announce the well\-deserved retirement of Darlene Hicks from her Vancouver Coastal Health role of Audiology Program Leader\- Richmond Public Health, and our School’s Clinical Teaching and Coordination Team, effective June 30, 2021\.  Darlene has been a valued member of our School since 2004, and words are insufficient to describe her impact on our School’s culture and the learning of our past and present Audiology and Speech\-Language Pathology students\. Congratulations and thank you Darlene for your contributions as a Clinician, Educator, and Colleague\.  We wish you joy and ease at this milestone\.
