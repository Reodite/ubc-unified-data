The School of Audiology &amp; Speech Sciences would like to congratulate **Emma Hancock** and **Kylie Brajcich**, recipients of 2024\-2025 Speech\-Language &amp; Audiology Canada \(SAC\) scholarships:

**Emma Hancock** \(Speech\-Language Pathology, 2nd year\) was awarded the Elks and Royal Purple Fund for Children’s Gordon Leslie Memorial Scholarship\.

**Kylie Brajcich** \(Speech\-Language Pathology, 2nd year\) was awarded the SAC’s Virginia Martin Memorial Scholarship\.

Scholarships awarded by SAC recognize outstanding students who have demonstrated resilience, dedication to their program, and academic excellence\.

Please accept our most sincere congratulations\!

For more information on these scholarships, please visit the [**SAC website**](https://www.sac-oac.ca/joining-the-profession/scholarships/)\.
