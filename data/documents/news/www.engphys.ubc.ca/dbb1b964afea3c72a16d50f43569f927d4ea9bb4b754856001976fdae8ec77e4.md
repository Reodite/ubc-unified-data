## [Open House for 1st year Science and Engineering on November 24 in CHEM D200](https://www.engphys.ubc.ca/2016/11/02/open-house-for-1st-year-science-and-engineering-on-november-24-in-chem-d200/)

An information session will be held for 1st year students in Science and APSC on Thursday, November 24 at 12:30 in CHEM D200\. Students in the program and the program director, Andre Marziali, will be answering your questions and providing information about the Engineering Physics Program\. Tours of the facilities will follow\.
