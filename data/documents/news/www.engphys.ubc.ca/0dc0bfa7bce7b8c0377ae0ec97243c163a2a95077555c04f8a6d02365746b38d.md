In 2018 Program alumnus Scott Phillips set an ambitious goal to raise $2M in support of the Engineering Physics Project Lab\. Phillips got the ball rolling with a contribution of $250K\.

Matching contributions were made by tech\. entrepreneurs Andrew Booth, Carl Hansen, and James Taylor\.

5 years later, that $2M goal is within reach, with less than $K400 to go\.

More information on this good news story and to donate to the Boye Ahlborn Endowment for Engineering Physics Project Lab [here](https://apsc.ubc.ca/spotlight/giving/projecting-new-generation-tech-success)\.
