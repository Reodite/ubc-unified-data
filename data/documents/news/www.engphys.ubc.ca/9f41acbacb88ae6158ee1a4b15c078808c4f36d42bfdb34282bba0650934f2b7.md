Pictured below L to R:  Lauren Halloway, Nika Martinussen, and Chloe Benko\-Prieur, testing their prototype of a large custom\-made bellows that will pump water into and out of cushions on a robotic “chair,” simulating the breathing of the other user\.

[Image](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/02/vr-breathing-2-940x705.jpg)

This project, which began in ENPH 459 and completed the following year in ENPH 479, is sponsored by the SFU iSpace Lab, which has developed a shared VR experience in which users are rewarded for breathing in sync\.
