Practice networking skills and connect with other Fizzers\.  This program is open to Engineering Physics students, staff and alumni\.

**How it works**:

Each week during the school year, you’ll receive an email asking if you’d like to sign up for a chat for the following week\. If you opt in, you’ll be matched with someone within a few days, Fizz Beans will send you an email about it, and you can schedule a chat with your match directly\.

More information and registration is [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScNCOFnopFmE9u2DfmLmoNJVowBUqSHCpQoyca7Za1-qzTASA/viewform)\.

Questions?  Email:  [beans@ubcfizz\.com](mailto:beans@ubcfizz.com)
