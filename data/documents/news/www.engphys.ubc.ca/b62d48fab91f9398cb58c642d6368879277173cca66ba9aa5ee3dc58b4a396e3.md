Chat with some new people in Fizz and learn about our networking program\. A lunch will be provided \(gluten free, vegan, and vegetarian options all available\) at our kick off event\.

[Image](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/10/Ace-Coffee_Roasters-slide2_1440x6401-e1601490121505-768x268.jpg)

Fizz Beans is a weekly opt\-in program that matches you up with someone else in Fizz for a coffee chat\.

Meet other people in Fizz and practice networking skills\!

[Image](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/10/coffee_promo_image_alt_43fbo1-360x200.jpg)

The program is open to current Fizz students and staff, as well as alumni\.

**Register [here](https://forms.gle/TF9vpZXM7VSFN8179):  
**

Each week during the school year, you’ll receive an email asking if you’d like to sign up for a chat for the following week\. If you opt in, you’ll be matched with someone within a few days\.

Fizz Beans will send you an email and you can schedule a chat with your match directly\.

[Image](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/10/coffee_unsplash1-360x255.jpg)

Wish you knew more people in Fizz? Now’s your chance\!

You can choose to opt in for whichever week/s you want after signing up\. Level of participation is up to you\.

Questions?  Email:  fizzbeans@ubcfizz\.com
