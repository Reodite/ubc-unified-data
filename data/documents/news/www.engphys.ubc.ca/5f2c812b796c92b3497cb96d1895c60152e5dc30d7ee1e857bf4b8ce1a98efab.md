Congratulations Morten Kals and Erik MacLennan, winners of the Faculty Award at the Faculty of Applied Science Design and Innovation Day\!

[Image](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/04/Pico-Hydro-Power-Faculty-Award-940x705.jpg)

Especially notable was the comprehensive execution of both projects, all the way down to the excellent video showcasing of the generator\.  The level of quality and attention to detail was evident in all meetings and interactions as the projects developed\.

The Industry Award was presented to Justin Myles, Declan Johnston, and Aaron Janz\.

[Image](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/04/Hydraulic-Ram-Wave-Energy-Converter-Faculty-Award-940x705.jpg)
