Congratulations to Lucas Cahill, named Student of the Year by Science Co\-op\!

[https://engphys3\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2015/04/Lucas\-Cahill\-2014\.jpg](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/04/Lucas-Cahill-2014.jpg)[Lucas Cahill 2014](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/04/Lucas-Cahill-2014-192x300.jpg)

“Throughout my time in the Science Co\-op program, I have been fortunate enough to work both internationally, at Laser Zentrum in Germany, as well as nationally at the Brain Research Centre, UBC and at BC Cancer Research Centre\. Co\-op has allowed me to explore different jobs, companies and styles of work which has been crucial in helping me choose a career path and in my decision to pursue graduate school\. The technical knowledge that I have gained from co\-op experiences, the interpersonal communication skills that I have developed, and the important contacts that I have established in the field will be major contributors to my future career\.”
