***This event is open to anyone who identifies with an underrepresented gender in our fields\!***

March 27, 2025, 6:30 – 8 p\.m\. in Hebb B112

A panel discussion and networking event featuring accomplished Engineering Physics alumni from diverse fields, including space, software, educational technology, clean energy, research, and entrepreneurship\.

This is a great opportunity to be inspired by their career paths and ask questions about professional life after EngPhys\!
