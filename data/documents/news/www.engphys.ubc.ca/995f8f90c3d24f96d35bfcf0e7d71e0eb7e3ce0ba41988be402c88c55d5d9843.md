## [Engineering Physics students Tyler Lum and Cailin Ringstrom chosen as Wesbrook Scholars, most prestigious designation at UBC](https://www.engphys.ubc.ca/2022/02/15/engineering-physics-students-tyler-lum-and-cailin-ringstrom-chosen-as-wesbrook-scholars-most-prestigious-designation-at-ubc/)

Congratulations Tyler Lum and Cailin Ringstrom chosen as Wesbrook Scholars for 2021\-2022\! 2 of the 20 UBC students selected this year are in Engineering Physics\.  The honour recognizes outstanding academic performance, leadership, involvement in student and community activities, and may include participation in sports\.
