After 6 years of work by 25 capstone course students, Puck Pilot has reached a major milestone: amateur humans can no longer beat it \(or even score a goal\), and the time has come to test it against a truly skilled human\.  

To that end, 10\-time air hockey world champion Tim Weissman and his son Jacob, also a world champion, travelled from Houston, Texas to the Hennings Building Project Lab\.

**Project Lab Director Dylan Gunn**:

“The robot fell to Jacob Wiessman 7\-5, but it beat Tim Weissman 2\-7\.  So, not a conclusive result, but we captured some pro\-level data and identified a number of things we need to fix\.  The table drifted out of alignment with the camera by a huge \~10 mm from repeated \(accidental\) pushing by the humans, and we discovered just before the competition that one of the 3D\-printed bearing housings was cracked through\.

And… we made the front page of the Vancouver Sun ***two days in a row\!*** 

All credit to the 25 incredibly hardworking and super\-smart students who made this happen over the past 6 years, and also my colleagues in the lab, Miti Isbasescu and Greg Reid, who are just incredible engineers and instructors\.”
