Congratulations Eng\. Phys\. 2021 graduate David Black, 2022 [NSERC Vanier Scholarship](https://vanier.gc.ca/en/scholar_search-chercheur_recherche_2022.html) recipient\!

The news release is [here](https://www.canada.ca/en/innovation-science-economic-development/news/2022/11/government-of-canada-invests-in-the-next-generation-of-scientists-and-researchers.html)\.

Selection for a scholarship is based equally on 3 criteria:  Academic excellence, research potential, and leadership \(potential and demonstrated ability\)\.

David Black was ranked \#3 out of 172 nominations reviewed by the NSERC Vanier CGS Competition Selection Committee\.

Link to the [UBC announcement](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/meet-our-students/black-david) by the Faculty of Graduate Studies\.
