Instructor Miti Isbasescu presents the winners of the Roenitz Project Award L – R: Seth Hinz, Davis Johnson, Sean Lan, and Emily Love for developing the Combobulator 5000, a smartphone app that helps order chaotic workspaces \(child’s toy room, your workshop or the ENPH student project lab\)\. The app identifies an item using the phone camera and then using augmented reality, guides the user to the storage location of the item\.
