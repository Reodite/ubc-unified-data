Congratulations Engineering Physics student Casey Krombein for an internship with her top choice company, the Canadian Space Mining Corporation\!

Casey Krombein is one of ten Zenith Fellow Interns across Canada in the Class of 2024 and will be contributing to novel research in the space sector while working on several incredible projects over the next 8 months\.

All the Fellows are placed with a Mentor through the fellowship\. Casey’s mentor, Kelsey Doerksen, is a PhD candidate at Oxford researching Autonomous Intelligent Machines and she has also worked with NASA, ESA, and UNICEF\.
