The Engineering Physics Program held an information session for first year students on Thursday, November 20, 2014\.

The BASC degree in Engineering Physics offers a perfect combination of fundamentals and applied knowledge to prepare graduates for technological changes\.

About half of our graduates choose to begin work as a licensed professional engineer \(P\.Eng\.\) and the other half continue their education in an extremely wide range of disciplines such as:

-   Working in research &amp; development
-   Entrepreneurship / starting a company
-   Graduate studies in Applied Sciences, Math, or Physics
-   Professional schooling in areas such as law, architecture, business, and medicine

Are you looking for a career applying new technologies to your innovative ideas?  If you enjoy and do well in math and physics, this program may be for you\!
