Congratulations Afshin Haidari\!

5th year Engineering Physics student, Afshin Haidari, has been honoured with one of the five *Electronic Arts* scholarships available to interns and co\-op students worldwide\.

Afshin was chosen for his strong potential for success in the interactive entertainment industry\.

> “I have been in Engineering Physics from 2010, and have been searching for my niche\.  Since working at *EA* in Cinematics and Animation, I rapidly grew, both professionally and intellectually, as I started to apply engineering principles in a way that meant something to me\.  *EA* is a company that gives back what the employees put in, and I am humbled that they chose to recognize my work\.  I plan to return to *EA* after graduation and start my professional life with this fantastic help I have been given\.”

August 2014
