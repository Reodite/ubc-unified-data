It is our pleasure to invite all UBC Engineering Physics alumni to the 19th annual UBC Engineering Physics Alumni Social Night\!

Come learn about the latest Eng Phys updates—for instance, did you know that Hebb was recently renovated and the Fizz clubhouse now takes up parts of both the 5th and 6th floors?—and connect with classmates and current students\. Catering and drinks will be provided\. We will also be handing out door prizes\!

Please RSVP [here](http://www.tinyurl.com/engfizznight) by Sunday, January 26, 2020\.

**Event Details**

Date:  Wednesday, January 29th, 2020

Time:  7pm – 10pm

Venue:  Koerner’s Pub, 1758 West Mall, Vancouver, BC V6T 1Z2

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to [vpevents@ubcfizz\.ca](mailto:vpevents@ubcfizz.ca)\.

We hope to see you there\!

**UBC Engineering Physics Student Association**
