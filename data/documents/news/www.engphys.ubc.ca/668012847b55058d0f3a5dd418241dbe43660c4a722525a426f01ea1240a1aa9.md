Congratulations Ryan, Daniel, Brendan and Jonah for a first place finish in the 2016 Engineering Physics Robot Competition for winning entry Jett Pace\!

[L \- R: Ryan Cotsakis, Daniel Chawla \(with Jett Pace\), Brendan Pousette, and Jonah Killam](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/08/Uber-Bots-Comp-EngPhys_ClareKiernan-652-940x627.jpg)

L – R: Ryan Cotsakis, Daniel Chawla \(with Jett Pace\), Brendan Pousette, and Jonah Killam

Students in ENPH 253 competed in teams, creating this year’s entries in *UBER Bots*: self\-driving cars that picked up passengers in a table top city, then dropped them off at their destination, without any crashes\!

[The City](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/08/The-City-940x626.jpg)

Teams had 2 minutes to complete the trip\.
