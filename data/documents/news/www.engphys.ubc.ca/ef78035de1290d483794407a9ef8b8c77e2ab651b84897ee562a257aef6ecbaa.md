The UBC Engineering Physics Project Fair was held Thursday, March 5th, 2015,  5pm\-9pm at the Irving Barber Centre, Victoria Theatre\.

In addition to the senior project posters, our two guest speakers talked about the technology and business opportunities in satellites and high\-quality imaging from space\.   [MDA’s RADARSAT Constellation](http://www.ctvnews.ca/business/mda-signs-706m-radarsat-constellation-deal-1.1107461) project involves end\-to\-end design, fabrication, launch and operation of satellites covering the entire country \(and ties with SpaceX for their 2018 launch\),  while [3vGeomatics](http://www.3vgeomatics.com/) focuses on processing and generating high\-quality images with sub\-meter accuracy for applications across all industries\.
