2\[nd\] Year Engineering Physics students worked through the summer to build autonomous robots capable of saving pets from a collapsing building\. Each robot was required to detect obstacles, following a route of black tape, and pick up stuffed animals around the course\. The annual competition, this year “Pet Rescue,” is the final exam for ENPH 253\.
