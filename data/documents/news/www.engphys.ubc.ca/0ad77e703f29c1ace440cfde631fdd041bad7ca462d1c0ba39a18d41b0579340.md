June 19, 2026

From Engineering Physics Project Lab Director Dylan Gunn:

Congratulations to current Engineering Physics student Ebrahim Hussain, who is bringing his startup, [Architect Labs](http://architectlabs.com/), out of stealth mode with a $24 million seed funding led by Kindred Ventures\! Architect is building AI systems to design and verify chips for the most challenging applications\. It was just over a year ago that we were reading his team’s 4th\-year capstone report on neuromorphic computing\.
