Congratulations to [Jeremy Witmer](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/2014/02/24/jeremy-witmer-on-being-awarded-the-wesbrook-and-premier-undergraduate-scholarship/ "Jeremy Witmer on being awarded the Wesbrook and Premier Undergraduate Scholarship"), who received the Harry Logan Memorial Scholarship and Harold B\. and Nellie Boyes Memorial Scholarship; and [Simon Ferguson](https://engphys3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/2014/02/26/simon-ferguson-on-being-awarded-the-wesbrook-and-premier-undergraduate-scholarship/ "Simon Ferguson on being awarded the Wesbrook and Premier Undergraduate Scholarship"), who received the HSBC Emerging Leader Scholarship\!

*The Premier Undergraduate and Wesbrook Scholarships are the University’s most prestigious designations, given to senior students with outstanding academic performance, participation in sports \(for some awards\), leadership, and involvement in student and community activities\.  
*

The UBC Physics and Astronomy announcement is [here](http://www.phas.ubc.ca/phas-students-receive-premier-undergrad-wesbrook-scholarships "Li-Wen Liang, Jeremy Witmer and Simon Ferguson")\.
