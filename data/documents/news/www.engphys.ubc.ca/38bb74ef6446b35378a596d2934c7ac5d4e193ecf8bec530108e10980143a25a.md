**From Rylan Stutters**:

UBC Uncrewed Aircraft Systems attended the national [SUAS](https://suas-competition.org/) drone competition in Maryland from Jun 24\-26, where teams from around the world travelled to compete in autonomous payload delivery and mapping objectives\. Despite suffering overheating issues in the 40\-degree weather, UBC UAS attained an overall placement of 28\[th\] out of the 81 registered teams, earning them 10\[th\] place out of all North American schools and achieving the highest score of all Canadian teams\!
