On the occasion of the 40th Anniversary of the Eng Phys Project Lab, and the reunion of the Eng Phys class of ’78, we are proud to announce the creation of the Boye Ahlborn Endowment for the Engineering Physics Project Laboratory,  seeded and created by a generous initial donation from Scott Phillips \(ENPH’89\), Founder and CEO of Starfish Medical\. Thank you to Scott for his generosity and leadership in support of Eng Phys, and thanks to Boye and the class of 1978 for creating the Project Laboratory\! We will leverage this funding to greatly advance entrepreneurial opportunities and applied training for our students\.

Please contact Program Director Andre Marziali to contribute to this transformative endowment\. [andre@phas\.ubc\.ca](mailto:andre@phas.ubc.ca)
