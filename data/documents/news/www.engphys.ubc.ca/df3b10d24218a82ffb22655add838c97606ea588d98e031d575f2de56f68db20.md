**Dragoneer and CPPIB back Canalyst’s US$70\-million venture funding** 

[The Globe and Mail](https://info.canalyst.com/hubfs/Globe_and_Mail_Series_C.pdf) covered the Vancouver capital markets data company’s Series C funding last week, in an article that describes Canalyst as a fast growing challenger to Bloomberg and outlines their big plans for growth\. Eng phys alum and Canalyst co\-founder/CEO Damir Hot says Canalyst has plans to double their team in the next year alone\. Read the [full press release](https://canalyst.com/news/canalyst-raises-70m-series-c-led-by-dragoneer/), and check out the open roles across all teams in this fast growing local fintech [here](https://canalyst.com/careers/)\.
