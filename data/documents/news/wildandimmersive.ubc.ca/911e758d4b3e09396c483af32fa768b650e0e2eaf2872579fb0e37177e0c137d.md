#### \|

#### Maple Ridge

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/20240317_223735-1-rotated.jpg)

### Presented by the [Royal Astronomical Society of Canada\- Vancouver](https://rasc-vancouver.com/)

Discover the wonders and beauty of the night sky at the RASC\-Vancouver Observatory located within the Malcolm Knapp Research Forest\. This event features guided observations of the Summer Constellations, dark sky views of the Milky Way, visible meteor showers, and a tour of the Observatory displaying celestial objects with a perspective into astro\-imaging processes\. You’ll also have the opportunity to peer through telescopes provided by RASC members to observe planets, star clusters, galaxies, and nebulae up close\.

This is a family friendly event and open to anyone with an interest in astronomy\.
