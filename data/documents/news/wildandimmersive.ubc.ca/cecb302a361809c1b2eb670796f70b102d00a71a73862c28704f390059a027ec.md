Special Events

#### February 28, 2025

#### \|

#### Williams Lake

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/aperture-vintage-GFKx0wazemc-unsplash-1.jpg)

Join us for an enchanting evening under the stars at our Family Astronomy Night\! This event is open to all families and designed to spark curiosity and wonder about the universe\. Experience the thrill of seeing distant planets through powerful telescopes\. Our knowledgeable astronomers will guide you as you explore the wonders of the night sky\. Discover the beauty of planets such as Saturn with its majestic rings and Mars with its distinctive red hue\. Learn how to identify some of the most iconic constellations visible in the night sky\. Our experts will share fascinating stories and myths behind these celestial patterns, helping you connect with the stars in a new way\. Whether you’re a seasoned star\-gazer or new to the world of astronomy, there’s something for everyone to enjoy\.
