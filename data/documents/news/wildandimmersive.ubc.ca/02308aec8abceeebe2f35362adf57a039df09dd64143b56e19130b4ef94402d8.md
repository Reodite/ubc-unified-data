#### December 21, 2024

#### \|

#### Williams Lake

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Candles.png)

Get into the holiday spirit with an evening of festive creativity\! Craft your own custom beeswax candles and holiday centerpiece using materials naturally sourced from our forest\.

Don’t miss this cozy and fun\-filled night of crafting and celebration\!
