Special Events

**This event has now passed\.**

#### July 5 &amp; 6, 2025

#### \|

#### Maple Ridge

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Buprestis-aurulenta-scaled.jpg)

[The Entomological Society of British Columbia](https://entsocbc.ca/) and UBC’s Malcolm Knapp Research Forest invite you to participate in an **Insect BioBlitz** event at **Malcolm Knapp Research Forest** on Saturday **July 5th** and Sunday **July 6th, 2025**\. The objective of a BioBlitz is to document as much biological diversity as possible during a short interval of time — in this case, documenting as many insect \(and other arthropod\!\) species as possible during the two\-day event\. 

Malcolm Knapp Research Forest is located in beautiful Maple Ridge, British Columbia, and is the site of many exciting past and ongoing UBC research projects, making it an ideal venue for an Insect BioBlitz\. An official invitation containing event details, a map, and a link to an iNaturalist project page for uploading insect observations is forthcoming and will be shared as the event approaches\. Observers may participate for any amount of time during the event hours \(9AM–6PM\) on either or both days\. Insect observations can include photographs and sound recordings uploaded to the designated iNaturalist project page, as well as physical specimens depending upon individual knowledge and capacity for arthropod collection\. Note that dogs are not permitted in the Research Forest\.

Members of the Entomological Society of British Columbia \(ESBC\) will be volunteering at the outreach table from 9AM–3PM Saturday and Sunday to provide help with iNaturalist and insect identifications, hand out maps, and sell fun merchandise\.

Synergy Semiochemicals will also be attending the BioBlitz with insect traps and a display of pinned insects collected from Malcolm Knapp Research Forest\! Come see how Synergy’s insect traps work \(set\-up Saturday morning\) and see what interesting insects they catch \(catches will be greatest on Sunday\)\. Synergy is also planning to hold a contest \(t\-shirt prizes\!\) for those who can track down specific sets of insects\. Come see us at the ESBC or Synergy outreach table for contest details\!

#### How to Join

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Bioblitz-area-1.png)

Participants join the Malcolm Knapp Research Forest Insect BioBlitz iNaturalist project [here](https://www.inaturalist.org/projects/malcolm-knapp-research-forest-insect-bioblitz-2025)\. Then on July 5\-6, head into the BioBlitz are of the research forest and begin uploading observations of insects and other arthropods you find\. Observations can include photographs and sound recordings\.

For those unfamiliar with iNaturalist, more information can be found [here](https://help.inaturalist.org/en/support/solutions/folders/151000552105) and a tutorial video [here](https://help.inaturalist.org/en/support/solutions/articles/151000170777-how-to-make-an-observation-with-the-inaturalist-mobile-app-video-tutorial)\.

Please direct any inquiries about this event to [Debra Wertman](mailto:debra.wertman@ubc.ca)\. We look forward to seeing you at the BioBlitz\!
