#### Meet the Facilitator\- Birch Bark Bakery

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sourdough-workshop-1-03.png)

Nestled by the tranquil shores of Beaver Lake, Shannon O’Donovan runs a charming micro\-bakery from her homestead in Beaver Valley called Birch Bark Bakery\. Shannon has a passion for sourdough that began growing up in the Yukon where she was raised on her Mom’s sourdough bread\. Once she had children of her own, she began a personal quest seeking wholesome, homemade bread\. Shannon has transformed her kitchen into a hub of sourdough creativity and community\.  
  
In addition to baking, Shannon loves sharing her knowledge with others\. She offers hands\-on workshops, where participants learn the art of sourdough from starter to finished loaf\. With a focus on sustainability and local sourcing, Shannon’s teachings emphasize the importance of connecting with nature through the food we create\.
