Special Events

**This event has now passed\.**

#### November 29, 2025

#### \|

#### Maple Ridge

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/IMG_6355.jpg)

Join us on Saturday, November 29th for a wreath making workshop in the research forest\! Enjoy a nice campfire while building yourself a festive wreath made from materials collected from the forest\. All needed materials will be provided\. If you wish to bring your own unique materials to add onto your wreath, you are more than welcome to\. Warm beverages and treats will be provided\. You can also bring your own snacks to roast over the fire\!

This event will be outdoors under covered space\. Please dress warmly and accordingly for the weather\.

We will be accepting cash and non\-perishable donations to give to the **Friends In Need Food Bank** in Maple Ridge\.

Examples of donations:

-   Canned fruits and vegetables
-   Formula and baby food
-   High\-protein foods \(Canned meats, beans, chili, nut\-butters, peanut butter, etc\.\)
-   Meal replacements \(Boost, Ensure, etc\.\)
-   Pasta and Sauce
-   Canned soups
-   Hygiene products

Limit of 15 spaces available\.
