#### Sunday May 10, 2026

#### \|

#### Maple Ridge

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Lake_13-scaled.jpg)

Celebrate Mother’s Day surrounded by the beauty and calm of Loon Lake with a restorative, nature\-inspired experience designed to nourish both the body and soul\.

**This unique Mother’s Day Event includes:**

-   A premium lakeside sauna session
-   Optional cold plunging in the lake
-   Exploring scenic forest trails
-   Canoeing across the lake
-   A tea service featuring treats and teas, including blends made with foraged forest ingredients\.

Whether you’re celebrating a mother figure or carving out time for yourself, this special day at Loon Lake offers a meaningful and memorable way to honour the mothers and caregivers in your life\.

**2 sessions available:** 11:00am – 2:00pm or 1:00pm – 4:00pm\. *\*tea is served from 1pm\-2pm for both sessions\.*

10 spaces available per session\.
