#### June 22\-23, 2024

#### \|

#### Williams Lake

The 1st Annual Cariboo Women in Wood Retreat\! A chance to relax and meet other women working in the Cariboo woods\.

Let’s get together for some socializing, light hiking, canoeing and networking in the sauna\. Come for the afternoon, or stay overnight\.

Taking place at Gavin Lake Camp, the Gavin Lake Forest Education Centre offers FREE outdoor education programs to local schools and school groups\. Amenities include cabins, a cookhouse, canoes, an archery range, a sauna, and an indoor bouldering cave\.

Admission covers facility rental, which directly supports the society to maintain the facility for their programming\.

Bring your own food, sleeping bag, towel and beverages\.
