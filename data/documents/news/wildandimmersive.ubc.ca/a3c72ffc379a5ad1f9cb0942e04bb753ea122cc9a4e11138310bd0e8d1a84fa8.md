**Explore the Hidden Side of the Forest**

Since 1949, the Malcolm Knapp Research Forest has hosted over 1,000 research projects covering a variety of topics and studies\. Now’s your chance to go behind the scenes and discover what researchers have learned over the decades\. Join us for a guided van tour through rarely\-seen areas of the forest and explore a rotating selection of research sites and studies each year\.

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/20230512_132150-3-768x1024.jpg)

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/52938215887_29b5ddd82f_k-1024x640.jpg)

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/52939265833_6358309ace_k-683x1024.jpg)

This tour is led by Hélène Marcoux, Registered Professional Forester and Manager of MKRF\. Participants will meet at the front gate, and then be taken on a tour through the forest in the research forest van\. This is a unique opportunity to access parts of the forest not usually open to the public—don’t miss it\!
