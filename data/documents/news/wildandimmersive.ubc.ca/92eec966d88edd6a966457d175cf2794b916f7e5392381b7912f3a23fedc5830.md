Join us for a hands\-on floral workshop led by the talented team at [Lo’s Florist\!](https://www.losflorist.com/) Whether you’re a budding enthusiast or simply love fresh blooms, you’ll enjoy a fun and interactive session filled with creativity\. Get step\-by\-step guidance from expert florists, creating your own custom floral arrangement to take home\! All materials are provided\. Unleash your inner florist and bring your creativity to life\.
