#### Williams Lake

[Alex Fraser Research Forest](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/alex-fraser-research-forest.jpg)

This 4\-day program will help prepare you for an exciting career in forestry by building your confidence in being outdoors\. During this all\-inclusive retreat in the Cariboo, you will learn the basics of hiking, orienteering, tree measurement, fire\-building, open\-flame cooking, and more\! Included in this program is return transportation from the Lower Mainland to Williams Lake, food, shared accommodation, and program activities\.
