Special Events

#### Sunday February 18, 2024

#### \|

#### Maple Ridge

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/stellar.png)

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/IMG_20190113_140820.jpg)

[Group of young kids under forest shelter at forest school](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/kids-in-shelter-1024x683.jpg)

Join us for a day of family adventure and heartfelt giving at the Family Day Fundraising Event hosted by Wild &amp; Immersive and [Stellar Play](https://stellarplay.ca/)\! This unique event promises an array of activities that are fun for the whole family, while raising funds for a deserving Maple Ridge family who are facing challenging times\. Dive into a day filled with unforgettable experience\.

**Battle Archery with Stellar Play:** Immerse yourself in the excitement of Battle Archery, an electrifying fusion of archery and dodgeball\! Take aim with foam\-tipped arrows as you strategize and dodge incoming shots\. It’s an adrenaline\-pumping activity that combines skill and fun for participants of all ages\.

**Survival with Wild &amp; Immersive:** Discover your inner adventurer with Wild &amp; Immersive’s Survival experience\. Learn essential outdoor survival skills, from packing wisely to building shelter, collecting freshwater, and mastering the art of starting a fire\. Do you have what it takes to thrive in the great outdoors?

100% of the registration fees go directly to supporting a local Maple Ridge family in need\. Your participation in this event ensures that we can make a positive impact on their lives and contribute to the well\-being of our community\.
