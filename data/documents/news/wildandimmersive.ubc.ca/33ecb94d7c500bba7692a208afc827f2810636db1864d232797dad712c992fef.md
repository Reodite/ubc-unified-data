Special Events

**This event has now passed\.**

#### February 16, 2026

#### \|

#### Maple Ridge

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/IMG_7901.jpg)

Get ready for a family day of fun, learning, and adventure in the great outdoors\! Bring the whole family to experience the thrill of survival skills in a hands\-on environment\. Together, you’ll:

**Build a Shelter** – Learn how to construct a shelter using natural materials\.  
**Master Fire\-Making** – Practice starting a fire safely, then enjoy roasting marshmallows and other treats over the flames\.  
**Bond Around the Campfire** – Share stories, snacks, and laughter while soaking in the warmth of your hard\-earned fire\.

This event is perfect for families who love the outdoors and want to learn practical skills in a fun, engaging way\. No prior experience needed—just bring your sense of adventure and we’ll provide the rest\!
