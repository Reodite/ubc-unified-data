Special Events

**This event has now passed\.**

#### January 2, 3, 4 &amp; 5, 2024

#### \|

#### Williams Lake

[kids learning to identify animal tracks in the snow](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/animal-track-identification-winter.jpg)

Join us in the new year for Snow Break, an outdoor winter day camp\. Dress for the weather to enjoy shelter building, snow tracking, crafts, games, and stories around the fire\. Select which days you’d like to attend and get ready for a day of fun and exploration before heading back to school\. Programming will be designed around weather and snow conditions, but children should expect to be outside for a majority of the day\.
