Special Events

#### February 10, 2024

#### \|

#### Maple Ridge

[Group of kids making wilderness shelter](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/group-in-wilderness.jpg)

Through the City of Maple Ridge, Wild &amp; Immersive will be leading a special event at the Albion Community Centre\!

Survival skills are crucial for anyone heading into the wilderness\. We have created a special Saturday Survival School to empower youth with the survival skills and safety knowledge they need to properly explore the great outdoors\. This program will require some off site walking\.
