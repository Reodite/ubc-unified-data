Special Events

#### April 14, 2024

#### \|

#### Williams Lake

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/barn-swallow.jpeg)

Come join the excitement at Wild &amp; Immersive for an enjoyable afternoon crafting your own barn swallow house\! This is the perfect time to set up your house for nesting season and watch the mosquitos and wasps disappear from your backyard\. We’ll supply the guidance, materials, tools, and a delightful warm beverage to assist you in completing the project\. All you need to bring is your can\-do attitude\!
