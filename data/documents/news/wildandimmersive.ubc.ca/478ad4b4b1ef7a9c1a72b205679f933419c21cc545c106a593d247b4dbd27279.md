Special Events

#### October 5, 2024

#### \|

#### Maple Ridge

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/WI-82-2.jpg)

Have you ever wondered about the many different tree species within the Malcolm Knapp Arboretum? Join us on a guided journey through the MKRF Arboretum, where you will explore tree species from Europe, China, North America, Korea and the majestic Caucasus Mountains, all amidst the backdrop of the Pacific Northwest\. As you explore, you’ll gain insights into identifying native trees while uncovering the intriguing contrasts between native, exotic, and invasive species\. Discover how the unique characteristics of each tree play a vital role in maintaining the health and balance of our ecosystem\.
