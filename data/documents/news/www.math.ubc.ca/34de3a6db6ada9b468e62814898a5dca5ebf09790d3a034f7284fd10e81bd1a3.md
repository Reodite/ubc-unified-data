## Michael Lindstrom and Vasu Tewari: Graduate Research Awards \(2014\)

[Michael Lindstrom](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~mlrtlm/) \(Applied Mathematics\) and [Vasu Tewari](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~vasu/) \(Pure Mathematics\) are the winners of the 2014 Graduate Research Awards\.   
Please join us in congratulating Mike and Vasu on their excellent research conducted during their time at UBC\.  
As part of their award, they will each give a [Departmental Colloquium](https://www.math.ubc.ca/news-events/events), where you can learn more about their work\. **Mike** will speak on November 28th, 2014, and Vasu will speak on March 20th, 2015\.
