## 13 UBC Mathematics Faculty Members Receive NSERC Award

We are thrilled to announce that thirteen UBC Mathematics faculty members have received NSERC Discovery Grants\. Congratulations **Sven Bachmann**, **Michael Bennett**, **Albert Chau**, **Dragos Ghioca**, **Stephen Gustafson**, **Izabella Laba**, **Mathav Murugan**, **Vinayak Vatsal**, **Liam Watson**, **Jun\-Cheng Wei**, **Brian Wetton**, **Alexia Yavicoli** and **Joshua Zahl**\.

The NSERC Discovery Grant Program supports basic discovery research at Canadian universities in the natural sciences and engineering\.
