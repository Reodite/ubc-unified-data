## Josh Zahl Awarded an ICBS Frontiers in Science Award

UBC Math Associate Prof\. Josh Zahl has been awarded a 2024 ICBS Frontiers in Science Award\.

Josh received this award, along with co\-author Nets Katz \(Cal Tech\), for their 2019 Journal of the American Mathematical Society paper titled "An improved bound on the Hausdorff dimension of Besicovitch sets in ℝ^3"\.
