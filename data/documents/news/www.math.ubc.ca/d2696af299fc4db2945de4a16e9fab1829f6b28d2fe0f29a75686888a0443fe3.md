## May 14, 2018: Tom Hutchcroft

Tom Hutchcroft has won the 2018 Canadian Mathematical Society Doctoral Prize\! The Doctoral Prize recognizes outstanding performance by a doctoral student\. Tom will receive his award and present a prize lecture during the CMS Winter Meeting here in Vancouver, December 7\-10, 2018\. Details on our [awards page](https://wdp.math.ubc.ca/news-events/awards)\.
