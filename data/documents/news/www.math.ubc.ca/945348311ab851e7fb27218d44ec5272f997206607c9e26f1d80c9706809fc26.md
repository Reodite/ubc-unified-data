## Dr\. Anotida Madzvamuse's Research on Single Cell Dynamics

Dr\. Anotida Madzvamuse was recently featured in a UBC Faculty of Science video where he discusses his research which focuses on understanding single cell dynamics and how they migrate through their environments\. His interdisciplinary work spans physics, biophysics, cellular biology, developmental biology, biomedical engineering and cancer research\.
