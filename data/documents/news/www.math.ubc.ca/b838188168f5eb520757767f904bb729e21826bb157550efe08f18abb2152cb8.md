## Ritesh Goenka has won the UBC Mathematics World Cup Pool

This prize is awarded infrequently to the person who submits the most accurate prediction of results at a major sporting event\. Ritesh was the clear winner of the 2022 World Cup edition with 72 points\. Natalia Accomazzo Scotti was a close second place with 59 points\. Ritesh is awarded the title of UBC Mathematics Chief Prognosticator until the next contest\. He will also receive a commemorative hat\. Ritesh, please collect your winnings from Theresa\. Congratulations to Ritesh\!
