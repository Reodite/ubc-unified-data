## Nike Vatsal: Ribenhoim Prize \(2006\)

[Nike Vatsal](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~vatsal/) has won the 2006 Ribenhoim Prize awarded by the Canadian Number Theory Association\. This prize is awarded every 4 years to a young number theorist \(as defined as having received their doctorate less than 12 years ago\) who is Canadian or has connections with Canadian mathematics\. For further details, see

[http://www\.pims\.math\.ca/science/2006/06cnta/prize\.html](http://www.pims.math.ca/science/2006/06cnta/prize.html)
