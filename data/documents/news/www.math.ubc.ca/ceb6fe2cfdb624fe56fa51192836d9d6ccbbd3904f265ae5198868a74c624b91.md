## Benjamin Young: Doctoral Prize \(2010\)

Benjamin Young is the recipient of the 2010 Doctoral Prize \([http://cms\.math\.ca/Prizes/info/dp/](http://cms.math.ca/Prizes/info/dp/)\)\. The CMS Doctoral Prize recognizes outstanding performance by a doctoral student\. Young received his Ph\.D\. in mathematics at the University of British Columbia, working under the joint supervision of Jim Bryan and Richard Kenyon\.
