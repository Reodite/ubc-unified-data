## Joel Friedman: Killam Research Fellowship \(2005\)

[Joel Friedman](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~jf/) has been awarded a [2005 Killam Research Fellowship](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/scholarships-awards-funding/awards-faculty-members)\. More information can be obtained in this [PDF document](http://www.orsil.ubc.ca/eforms/KRP-Guidelines-04.pdf)\.
