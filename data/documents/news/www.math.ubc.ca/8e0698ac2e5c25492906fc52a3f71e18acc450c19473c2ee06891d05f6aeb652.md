## Malabika Pramanik is now a Fellow of the American Mathematical Society \(2021\)

Her citation reads: "For contributions to complex and harmonic analysis and mentoring and support for the participation of \-represented groups in mathematics\.” So far as I can tell, we now have eleven colleagues in this group \(not counting emeritus professors\)\.
