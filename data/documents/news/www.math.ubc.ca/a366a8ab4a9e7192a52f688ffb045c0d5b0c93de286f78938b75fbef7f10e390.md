## Martin Barlow: Canadian Mathematical Society Jeffery\-Williams Prize \(2007\)

[Martin Barlow](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~barlow/) has won the Canadian Mathematical Society 2008 Jeffery\-Williams Prize that recognizes mathematicians who have made outstanding contributions to mathematical research\.

For more details, please see [the press release from the CMS](http://www.math.ubc.ca/Dept/Awards/CMS-JWP2008.txt)\. More information about the prize itself can be found at the [CMS web site](http://www.math.ca/)\.
