## Hong Zhe Chen: Governor General's Silver Medal \(2018\)

Hong Zhe Chen, in Combined Honours Physics and Mathematics, has been awarded a Governor General's Silver Medal as head of the graduating class in Science\.  
Details regarding nomination procedures are posted on the [Faculty of Graduate Studies website](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/governor-generals-gold-medal)\.
