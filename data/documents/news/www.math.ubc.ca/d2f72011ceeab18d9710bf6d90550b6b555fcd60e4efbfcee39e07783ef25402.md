## Zinovy Reichstein: Jeffery\-Williams Prize \(2013\)

[Zinovy Reichstein](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~reichst/) was the recipient of the 2013 Jeffery\-Williams Prize\. The prize was inaugurated in 1968 to recognize established mathematicians who have made outstanding contributions to mathematical research\.

The complete announcement has been posted here:
