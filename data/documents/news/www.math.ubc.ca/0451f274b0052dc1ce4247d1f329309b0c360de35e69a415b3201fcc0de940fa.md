## Nassif Ghoussoub: Honorary Degree: Doctorat Honoris Causa \(2005\)

[Nassif Ghoussoub](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~nassif/) was awarded a "Doctorat Honoris Causa" from the University of Paris, June, 2004\. This is in recognition of Nassif's research accomplishments in mathematics and for his many contributions to the discipline worldwide, through his role in the founding of PIMS, MITACS and BIRS\.
