## UBC Math Alum Chi Hoi \(Kyle\) Yip Awarded the 2025 CMS Blair Spearman Doctoral Prize

Dr\. Chi Hoi \(Kyle\) Yip, a distinguished alumnus of our UBC Mathematics PhD program, has been awarded the prestigious 2025 CMS Blair Spearman Doctoral Prize\. This national award, presented by the Canadian Mathematical Society \(CMS\), recognizes outstanding performance by a doctoral student graduating from a Canadian university\.
