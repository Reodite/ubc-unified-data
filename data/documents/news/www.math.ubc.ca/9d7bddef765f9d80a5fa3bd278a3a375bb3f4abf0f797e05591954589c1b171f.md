## Tom Hutchcroft and Stilianos Louca: Graduate Research Awards \(2015\)

[Tom Hutchcroft](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~thutch/) \(Pure Mathematics\) and [Stilianos Louca](http://www.zoology.ubc.ca/~louca/) \(Applied Mathematics\) are the winners of the 2015 Graduate Research Awards\.   
Please join us in congratulating Tom and Stilianos on their excellent research conducted during their time at UBC\.  
As part of their award, they will each give a [Departmental Colloquium](http://www.math.ubc.ca/Dept/Events/), where you can learn more about their work\.
