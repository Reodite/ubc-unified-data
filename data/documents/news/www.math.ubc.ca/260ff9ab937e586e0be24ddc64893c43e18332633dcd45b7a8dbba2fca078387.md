## Alejandro Adem and Dong Li: Canadian Mathematical Society's Research Prizes \(2015\)

[Alejandro Adem](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~adem/) and [Dong Li](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~dli/) have been announced as this year's winners of the Canadian Mathematical Society's major research prizes, the Jeffery\-Williams Prize \(for a senior researcher\) and the Coxeter\-James prize \(for a junior researcher\), respectively\. The press releases are

-   [https://cms\.math\.ca/MediaReleases/2015/jw\-award](https://cms.math.ca/MediaReleases/2015/jw-award)
