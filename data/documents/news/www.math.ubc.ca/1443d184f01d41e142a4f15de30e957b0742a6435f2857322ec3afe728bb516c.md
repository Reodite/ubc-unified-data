## Mathematics Department's Graduate Research Awards for 2021

Every year the Department of Mathematics celebrates excellence by presenting Graduate Research Awards to deserving Doctoral students for research done while at UBC\.

The winners of the Mathematics Department's Graduate Research Awards for 2021 have now been selected\. Please join us in congratulating them\!

-   [Nathan Lawrence](https://www.math.ubc.ca/user/2578)
-   [Sophie Macdonald](https://www.math.ubc.ca/user/2912)
-   [Emmanuel Michta](https://www.math.ubc.ca/user/3015)
-   [Ethan White](https://www.math.ubc.ca/user/2606)

Each award winner will give a Departmental Colloquium, where you can learn more about their work\.
