## Liam Watson Awarded a Killam Accelerator Research Fellowship

Dr\. Liam Watson, Associate Professor of Mathematics at UBC, is the recipient of one of six Killam Accelerator Research Fellowships\.

These annual fellowships support exceptional early career researchers across various disciplines\.
