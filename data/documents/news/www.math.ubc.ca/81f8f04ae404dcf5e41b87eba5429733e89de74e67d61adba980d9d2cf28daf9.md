## Rodolfo Brandão Joins UBC Mathematics

Image

UBC Mathematics is pleased to welcome Rodolfo Brandão as an Assistant Professor\.

Reflecting on his appointment, Rodolfo shares, “I’ve followed the work of people at UBC throughout my career, so it has been a great pleasure to become part of the community\.”

Welcome to UBC Mathematics, Rodolfo\!
