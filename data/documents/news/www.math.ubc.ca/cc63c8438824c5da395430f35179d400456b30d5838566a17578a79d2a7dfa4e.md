## Faculty Research Awards \(2016\)

UBC's 2016 Faculty Research Awards were announced:

-   [Dragos Ghioca](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~dghioca/) won a Killam Research Fellowship to support him while on sabbatical\.
-   [Christoph Hauert](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~hauert/) won a Killam Research Prize, in recognition of his outstanding research and scholarly contributions\.
-   [Dong Li](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~dli/) won the Charles A McDowell Award for Excellence in Research, recognizing demonstrated excellence in pure or applied scientific research by a young faculty member
