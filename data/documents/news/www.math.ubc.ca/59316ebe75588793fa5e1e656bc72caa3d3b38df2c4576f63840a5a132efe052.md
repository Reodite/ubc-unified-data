### Nakanishi

  
Global dynamics of the Zakharov system

September 11, 2026

ESB 2012 and Zoom

The Zakharov system is a coupled system of the Schrodinger equation and the acoustic wave equation to describe the Langmuir turbulence in plasma\. The goal in this talk is to classify the solutions depending on the initial data, in particular to distinguish dispersive waves, solitons and blow\-up\.\.\. [Read more](https://www.math.ubc.ca/events/oct-23-2025-global-dynamics-zakharov-system)
