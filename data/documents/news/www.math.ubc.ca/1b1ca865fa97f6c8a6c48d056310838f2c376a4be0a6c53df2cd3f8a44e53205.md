## Department Teaching Awards \(2012\)

The Department Teaching Awards were awarded to one postdoctoral fellow, [Deniz Karli](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~deniz.karli/), and to three graduate students, [Rebecca Hiller](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~rhiller/), [Ed Kroc](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~ekroc/index.htm) and Ali Vakil\. These awards recognize outstanding teaching performances from our postdoctoral fellows and graduate students\.
