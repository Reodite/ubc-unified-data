## Ailana Fraser: Canadian Math Society's 2012 Krieger\-Nelson Prize \(2012\)

[Ailana Fraser](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~afraser/) has been awarded the Canadian Math Society's 2012 Krieger\-Nelson Prize\. The prize was inaugurated in 1995 to recognize female mathematicians who have made outstanding contributions to mathematical research\.

Information about the Kriege\-Nelson Prize: [http://cms\.math\.ca/Prizes/info/kn\.html](http://cms.math.ca/Prizes/info/kn.html)
