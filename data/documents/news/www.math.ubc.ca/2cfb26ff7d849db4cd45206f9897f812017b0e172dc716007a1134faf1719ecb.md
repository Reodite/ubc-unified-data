## James J\. Feng: CAIMS Research Prize \(2017\)

[James J\. Feng](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~jfeng/) is the winer of the CAIMS Research Prize 2017\. It recognizes Feng's influential contributions to the study of complex fluids, through a combination of challenging computations, novel theoretical insights, and physical experiments\.
