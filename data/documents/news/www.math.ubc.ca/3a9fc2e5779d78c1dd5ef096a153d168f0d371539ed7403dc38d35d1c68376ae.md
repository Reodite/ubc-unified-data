## Colin Clark: Fellow of the International Institute of Fisheries Economics &amp; Trade \(2015\)

[Colin Clark](http://www.math.ubc.ca/Research/MathBio/clark.php) has been named 2016 Fellow of the International Institude of Fisheries Economics &amp; Trade \(IIFET\)\. See [IIFET Latest News](http://www.iifet-2016.org/news/) and [Press Release \(PDF\)](http://www.math.ubc.ca/Dept/Awards/IIFET_2016_Fellow_Announcement_Clark.pdf)\.
