## Ethan White: Killam\-Donald N\. Byers Memorial Prize \(2019\)

Ethan White, PhD Student in Discrete Mathematics, has been selected to receive the prestigious Killam\-Donald N\. Byers Memorial Prize for the 2019W session\. “In 2002, UBC created an endowed prize in memory of former Killam Trustee, Donald N\. Byers\. The prize is awarded to the highest\-ranking Killam Doctoral Fellow in the annual Affiliated Fellowships competition\. The award is made on the recommendation of the Faculty of Graduate Studies in consultation with the Affiliated Fellowships adjudication committee\. ” Read more about the award here: [https://www\.grad\.ubc\.ca/scholarships\-awards\-funding/killam\-awards\-fellow\.\.\.](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/scholarships-awards-funding/killam-awards-fellowships)
