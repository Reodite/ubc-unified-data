## Yoichiro Mori: Leslie Fox Prize in Numerical Analysis \(2007\)

[Yoichiro Mori](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~mori), a Post\-Doctoral Fellow, has won the Leslie Fox Prize in Numerical Analysis at Oxford University\. This award was based on a paper he wrote on a convergence proof for the immersed boundary method\.

More information about the prize can be found here:

-   [Leslie Fox Prize in Numerical Analysis](http://users.comlab.ox.ac.uk/endre.suli/fox/)
-   [Photos of this year's winners](http://users.comlab.ox.ac.uk/endre.suli/fox/2007Win.htm)
