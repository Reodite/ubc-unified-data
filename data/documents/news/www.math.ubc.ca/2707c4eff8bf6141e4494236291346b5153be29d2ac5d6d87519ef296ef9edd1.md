## Dr\. Priscilla E\. \(Cindy\) Greenwood Awarded SSC Gold Medal

UBC mathematics Professor Emerita Priscilla E\. \(Cindy\) Greenwood on being awarded the Statistical Society of Canada's Gold Medal\! This medal is awarded to a person who has made outstanding contributions to statistics or probability, either to mathematical developments or in applied work\.
