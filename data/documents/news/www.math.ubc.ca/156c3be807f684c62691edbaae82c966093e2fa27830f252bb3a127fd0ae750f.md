## Amir Moradifam: Killam Predoctoral Fellowship \(2008\)

Amir Moradifam won the prestigious [Killam Predoctoral Fellowship](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/killam-doctoral-scholarships)

The following students won [NSERC Fellowships](http://www.nserc.gc.ca/sf_e.asp?nav=sfnav&amp;lbi=2a):  
Michael LeBlanc \(Alexander Graham Bell CGS D\), Andrew Brown \(PGS M\), Keira Gunn \(PGS M\),Chan Ian \(PGS M\), Tiffany Chao \(PGS M\), Sitar Scott \(PGS D\), Rose Simon \(PGS D\), Cindy Blois\(extended PGSM\), Shane Cernele \(CGS M\), Tyler Helmuth \(PGS M\), Jerome Lefebvre \(PGS M\),Michael Lindstrom \(PGS M\), Carol Ross \(PGS M\), Vincent Sippola \(PGS M\), William Thompson\(PGS M\)

The following students won [University Graduate Fellowships](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/affiliated-fellowships):  
Omer Dushek, James Clarkson, Adam Clay \(UGF/[Pacific Century Graduate Scholarship](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/scholarships-awards-funding?menu=001,001,000,000)\), Vishaal Kapoor \(partial UGF\), Erez Louidor, Ryan Lukeman \(UGF/[Pacific Century Graduate Scholarship](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/scholarships-awards-funding?menu=001,001,000,000)\),Aurel Meyer \(partial UGF\), Andrew Morrison \(UGF/ [Josephine T\. Berthier Fellowship](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/affiliated-fellowships)\), Liang Zhu

In addition, Omer Dushek, Ryan Lukeman, and Jennifer Morrison won new [IGTC awards](http://www.pims.math.ca/scientific/igtc/mathematical-biology/application-process)\.
