## Jozsef Solymosi: Sloan Research Fellowship \(2006\)

[Jozsef Solymosi](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~solymosi/) has won a [2006 Sloan Research Fellowship](http://http//www.sloan.org/programs/fellowship_brochure.shtml) which is awarded annually to approximately 20 outstanding young mathematicians who have received their doctorate degree from a college or university in the US or Canada less than 6 years ago\. A list of recipients for this award \(including one of ours from last year\) can be found here:

[http://www\.sloan\.org/programs/fellowshiplist\.shtml](http://www.sloan.org/programs/fellowshiplist.shtml)
