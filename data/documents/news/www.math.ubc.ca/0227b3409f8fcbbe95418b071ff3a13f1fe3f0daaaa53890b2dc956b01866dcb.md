## Dr\. Ailana Fraser featured in Scientific American Magazine

Image

UBC Mathematics Professor Dr\. Ailana Fraser is featured in a ***Scientific American*** magazine article titled "These Are the Most Beautiful Equations, According to Mathematicians\." In the piece, Dr\. Fraser elaborates on the remarkable and surprising qualities of the Gauss\-Bonnett Equation\.
