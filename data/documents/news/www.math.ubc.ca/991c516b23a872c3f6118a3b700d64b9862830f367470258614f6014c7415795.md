## Philip Loewen and Andrew Rechnitzer: Faculty of Science Killam Prizes \(2009\)

[Philip Loewen](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~loew/) and [Andrew Rechnitzer](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~andrewr/) have won the Faculty of Science Killam Prizes for Excellence in Teaching\. For more details on this prize, please see  
[http://www\.science\.ubc\.ca/faculty/awards/killam](http://www.science.ubc.ca/faculty/awards/killam)
