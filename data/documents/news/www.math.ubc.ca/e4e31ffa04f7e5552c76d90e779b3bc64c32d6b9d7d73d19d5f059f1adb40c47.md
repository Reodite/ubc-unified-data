## Joel Feldman: CRM\-Fields\-PIMS Prize \(2007\)

[Joel Feldman](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~feldman/) has been awarded the CRM\-Fields\-PIMS prize for 2007\. This prize is awared jointly by the Centre de Recherches Mathématiques \(CRM\) of l'Université de Montré'al, the Fields Institute, and the Pacific Institute for the Mathematical Sciences\. An announcement of this prize has been posted on the PIMS and CRM web sites:

-   [2007 CRM\-Fields\-PIMS Award Announcement](http://www.pims.math.ca/The_News/Latest_News/Dr._Joel_Feldman%3A_Recipient_of_the_2007_CRM-Fields-PIMS_Prize/)
-   English: [http://www\.crm\.umontreal\.ca/prix/prix\_an\.shtml](http://www.crm.umontreal.ca/prix/prix_an.shtml)
