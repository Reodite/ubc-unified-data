## Katya Yurasovskaya: UBC Graduate Teaching Assistant Teaching Award \(2007\)

[Katya Yurasovskaya](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~yurasoe/) is one of 5 winners in the Faculty of Science of a [UBC Graduate Teaching Assistant Teaching Award](http://www.vpacademic.ubc.ca/awards/gradta/)\. The award includes a prize of $1000, and was awarded for Katya's work organizing problem\-solving workshops for MATH 180 and 184 students\.
