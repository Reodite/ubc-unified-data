## Professor Emeritus David Brydges awarded the Henri Poincaré Prize

 Professor Emeritus of Mathematics, Dr\. David Brydges, has been awarded the Henri Poincaré Prize\.

The Henri Poincare Prize recognizes outstanding contributions in mathematical physics and is awarded every three years at the International Mathematical Physics Congress\.
