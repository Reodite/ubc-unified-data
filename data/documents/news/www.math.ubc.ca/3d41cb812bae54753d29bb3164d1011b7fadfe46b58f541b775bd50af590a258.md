## Dr\. Joshua Zahl Named Among 2025’s Biggest Math Breakthroughs

Dr\. Josh Zahl and his collaborator Hong Wang of the NYU Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences resolved the Kakeya conjecture—a problem that has challenged researchers for decades\. Their work was featured in *Quanta Magazine’s* **The Biggest Breakthroughs in Mathematics: 2025**\.
