## Sabin Cautis: NSERC Discovery Accelerator Supplements \(2014\)

[Sabin Cautis](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~cautis/) has been awarded NSERC Discovery Accelerator Supplements\.

-   Sabin Cautis, for "Representation theoretic methods in geometry and topology"\.

More information about the program is available at:  
[http://www\.nserc\-crsng\.gc\.ca/Professors\-Professeurs/Grants\-Subs/DGAS\-SGS\.\.\.](http://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Professors-Professeurs/Grants-Subs/DGAS-SGSA_eng.asp)
