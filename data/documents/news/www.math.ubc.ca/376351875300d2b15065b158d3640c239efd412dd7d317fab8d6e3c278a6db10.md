## Nahid Walji Promoted to Associate Professor of Teaching

Image

UBC Mathematics is happy to announce that Dr\. Nahid Walji has been promoted to Associate Professor of Teaching\.

Prior to his time at UBC, Dr\. Walji served as an assitant professor at the American University of Paris\. 

[Visit Nahid Walji's Website](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~nwalji/index.html)
