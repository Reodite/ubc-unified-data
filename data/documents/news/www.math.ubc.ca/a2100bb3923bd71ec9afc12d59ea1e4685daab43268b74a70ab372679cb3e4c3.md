## Martin Barlow: Fellow of the Royal Society \(London\) \(2005\)

[Martin Barlow](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~barlow/) has been elected as a Fellow of the Royal Society \(London\)\. Fellows are elected from citizens or residents of a Commonwealth nation, and who have made "a substantial contribution to the improvement of natural knowledge, including mathematics, engineering science and medical science\."

More information about the Royal Society and the Fellowship can be found here:

-   [Royal Society Home Page](http://www.royalsoc.ac.uk/)
