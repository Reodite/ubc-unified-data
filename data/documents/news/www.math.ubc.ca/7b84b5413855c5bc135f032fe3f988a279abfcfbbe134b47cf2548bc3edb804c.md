## Joshua Zahl has won the UBC/PIMS mathematical sciences early career award

This prize was created by two founding donors, **Anton Kuipers** and **Darrell Duffie**, to recognize UBC researchers for their leading edge work in mathematics or its applications in the sciences\. Josh will receive a "monetary award” and deliver a PIMS/UBC Distinguished Colloquium lecture\. Congratulations to Josh\! Previous winners are here: [https://staging\.pims\.math\.ca/programs/prizes/pimsubc\-mathematical\-sciences\-early\-career\-award](https://staging.pims.math.ca/programs/prizes/pimsubc-mathematical-sciences-early-career-award)
