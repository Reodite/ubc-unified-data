## Colin Macdonald and Anthony Wachs: NSERC Discovery Accelerator Supplements \(2016\)

Colin Macdonald and [Anthony Wachs](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~wachs/) have been awarded NSERC Discovery Accelerator Supplements\.

-   Colin Macdonald, for "Numerical Computing on Evolving Domains"
-   Anthony wachs, for "Multi\-scale modelling of reactive particulate flows"

More information about the program is available at:  
[http://www\.nserc\-crsng\.gc\.ca/Professors\-Professeurs/Grants\-Subs/DGAS\-SGS\.\.\.](http://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Professors-Professeurs/Grants-Subs/DGAS-SGSA_eng.asp)
