## MATHEMATICS DEPARTMENT'S GRADUATE RESEARCH AWARDS FOR 2023

Every year the Department of Mathematics celebrates excellence by presenting Graduate Research Awards to deserving Doctoral students for research done while at UBC\.

The winners of the Mathematics Department's Graduate Research Awards for 2023 have now been named\. Please join us in congratulating them:

-   [Fanze Kong](https://www.math.ubc.ca/user/3050)
-   [Chi Hoi \(Kyle\) Yip](https://www.math.ubc.ca/user/2631)

Each award winner receives a cash prize and will give a Departmental Colloquium, where you can learn more about their work\. We hope that you will attend\!
