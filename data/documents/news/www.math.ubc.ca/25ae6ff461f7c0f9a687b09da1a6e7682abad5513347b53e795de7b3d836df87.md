## Malabika Pramanik: UBC Killam Teaching Prize \(2011\)

[Malabika Pramanik](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~malakika/) has been awarded a 2010\-11 UBC Killam Teaching Prize\. These prizes recognize outstanding achievement as a teacher, reflecting enthusiasm for teaching and commitment to students\.

This is the sixth UBC Killam Teaching Prize awarded in Math over the last five years\.
