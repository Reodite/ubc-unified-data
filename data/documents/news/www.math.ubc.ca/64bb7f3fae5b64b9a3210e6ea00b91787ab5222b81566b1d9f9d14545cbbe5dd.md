## Patrick Walls Awarded 2019 UBC Science Excellence in Service Award

The UBC Science Excellence in Service Award recognizes staff, students and faculty whose service contributions have had a significant positive impact in achieving UBC Science's mission\. In honour of his contributions, Associate Professor Walls was awarded $2,000\.
