## Jim Bryan and Michael Doebeli: Killam Faculty Research Fellowship \(2007\)

[Jim Bryan](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~jbryan/) and [Michael Doebeli](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~doebeli/) both awarded a Killam Faculty Research Fellowship for next year\.

The purpose of the award, based on special distinction of intellect, is to assist promising faculty members, who wish to devote full time to research and study in their field during a recognized study leave\.

Jim has also been awarded a Visiting Research Professorship at the Miller Institute for Basic Science at UC Berkeley\. The purpose of this Professorship is to bring promising or eminent scientists to the Berkeley campus on a short\-term basis for collaborative research interactions\. More info at: [http://millerinstitute\.berkeley\.edu/page\.php?nav\=24](http://millerinstitute.berkeley.edu/page.php?nav=24)
