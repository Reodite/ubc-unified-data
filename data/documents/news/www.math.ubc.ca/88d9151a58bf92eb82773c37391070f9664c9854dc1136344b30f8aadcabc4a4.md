## Oren Rippel: Wesbrook Scholarship \(2010\)

Oren Rippel, an outstanding Combined Honours Math and Physics student, who has been awarded a Wesbrook Scholarship\.  
The Wesbrook Scholarship recognizes demonstrated excellence in academics and service, encouraging students who balance high academic achievement with outstanding contributions to society through a range of extracurricular activities\. Information about the scholarships is available at:[http://www\.students\.ubc\.ca/finance/awards\.cfm?page\=scholarships&amp;view\=pre\.\.\.](http://www.students.ubc.ca/finance/awards.cfm?page=scholarships&amp;view=premier)
