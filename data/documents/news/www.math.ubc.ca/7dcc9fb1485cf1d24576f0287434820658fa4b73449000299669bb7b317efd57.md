## Mathav Murugan Awarded the 2025 PIMS/UBC Mathematical Sciences Early Career Award

UBC Mathematics Associate Professor Mathav Murugan has been awarded the 2025 PIMS/UBC Mathematical Sciences Early Career Award, which recognizes UBC researchers for their leading\-edge work in mathematics or its applications in the sciences\. 

Professor Murugan received his PhD from Cornell University and is a Canada Research Chair in Probability \(Tier 2\)\.
