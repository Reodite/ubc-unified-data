## Gordon Slade: CRM\-Fields\-PIMS Prize \(2009\)

[Gordon Slade](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~slade) has been awarded the 2010 CRM\-Fields\-PIMS prize\. The CRM\-Fields\-PIMS prize is the premier prize for research awarded jointly by the three Canadian mathematics institutes\. It recognizes exceptional achievement in the mathematical sciences for research conducted primarily in Canada or in affiliation with a Canadian university\.
