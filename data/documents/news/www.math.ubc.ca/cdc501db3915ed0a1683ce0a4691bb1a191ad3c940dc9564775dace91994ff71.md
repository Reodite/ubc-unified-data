## Ian Frigaard: President of the CAIMS\*SCAIM \(2011\)

[Ian Frigaard](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~frigaard/) has been elected President of the [CAIMS\*SCAIM](http://www.caims.ca/Society/index.html)\(Canadian Applied and Industrial Mathematics Society\) Board\. Ian's term will be 2011\-2013\.
