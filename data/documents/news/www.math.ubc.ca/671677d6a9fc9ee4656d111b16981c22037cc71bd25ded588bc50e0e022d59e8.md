## Dr\. Hossein Rahmani Awarded a 2024 \- 2025 Banting Postdoctoral Fellowship

UBC Mathematics Postdoctoral Fellow Hossein Rahmani is the recipient of a 2024 \- 2025 Banting Postdoctoral Fellowship\. The Banting Postdoctoral Fellowship program provides funding for postdoctoral fellows who positively contribute to Canada's economic, social, and research\-based projects\. Dr\. Rahmani was awarded this prize in response to his project titled "Combined AI and Network Modeling of Non\-Newtonian Flows in Porous Media"\.

Dr\. Rahmani recieved his PhD from Université Laval in 2023\. His area of research is civil and mechanical engineering \(fluid mechanics\)\.
