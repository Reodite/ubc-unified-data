## Nassif Ghoussoub: Order of Canada \(2015\)

[Nassif Ghoussoub](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~nassif/) was named an officer of the Order of Canada for his scientific contributions to the study of differential equations and for advancing mathematics research and education in Canada
