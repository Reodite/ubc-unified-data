## Kai Behrend and Brian Marcus: Fells of the American Mathematical Society \(2017\)

[Kai Behrend](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~behrend/) and [Brian Marcus](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~marcus/) had been named in the 2018 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](http://www.ams.org/home/page)\.

Kai Behrend  
"For contributions to algebraic geometry\."

Brian Marcus  
"For contributions to dynamical systems, symbolic dynamics and applications to data storage problems, and service to the profession\."

Details here: [http://www\.ams\.org/profession/ams\-fellows/new\-fellows](http://www.ams.org/profession/ams-fellows/new-fellows)
