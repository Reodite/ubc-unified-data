## Elina Robeva and Geoffrey Schiebinger awarded 2026 Killam Research Fellowship

Associate professors Dr\. Elina Robeva and Dr\. Geoffrey Schiebinger have been awarded a 2026 UBC Killam Research Fellowship\. 

These awards honour the outstanding research and scholarly accomplishments of Dr\. Robeva and Dr\. Schiebinger, and will support their ability to devote themselves fully to research during an approved study leave\.
