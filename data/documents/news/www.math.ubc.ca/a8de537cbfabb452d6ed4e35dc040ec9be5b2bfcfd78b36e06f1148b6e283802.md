## 2019 Putnam Competition Results

The 2019 Putnam Competition exam was given on Saturday December 7th 2019\.

4,229 students from 570 universities and colleges from North America took place in this competition\. We had 22 students from UBC taking part in the Putnam competition and 5 of them placed in the top 436; they are \(in alphabetical order\)

Gurkeerat Chhina  
Kim Dinh  
Sina Saleh  
Victor Wang  
Brandon Zhang

Our top scorer was Kim Dinh who placed in the top 250 students in all North America\. UBC Putnam team ranked 38th out of 500 universities\.

Congratulations to all of our competitors\!
