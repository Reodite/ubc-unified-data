### [FUSE, the FoS x SUS UBC Science Exchange](https://www.math.ubc.ca/events/sep-3-2026-fuse-fos-x-sus-ubc-science-exchange)

September 22, 2026

Abdul Ladha Science Student Centre

FUSE \(FoS x SUS UBC Science Exchange\) is a research showcase celebrating the diverse research taking place across the UBC Faculty of Science\. Undergraduate students who conducted research over the summer with the Faculty of Science are invited to present their work through a research poster or e\.\.\.
