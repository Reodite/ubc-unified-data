## Twelve UBC Mathematics Faculty Members Awarded NSERC Discovery Grants

UBC Mathematics is pleased to announce our faculty members who have been awarded a 2026 NSERC Discovery Grant\. These grants are designed to support long\-term research objectives that have the greatest potential to advance natural sciences and engineering\. They also recognize the creativity and innovation that are at the heart of all research advances\. 

Congratulations to the following recipients:

**Omer Angel**  
*Applications of discrete probability*

**Rodolfo Brandão**  
*Mathematical Modelling of Liquid Sheets and Filaments*

**Eric Cytrynbaum**  
*From Cytoskeleton to Tissue: Mechanochemical Models of Developmental Organization*

**Leah Edelstein\-Keshet**  
*Modeling and simulating the regulation of cell motility and cell\-cell interactions: from single cells to collective behaviour*

**Ailana Fraser**  
*Minimal submanifolds in Riemannian geometry*

**Kasia Jankiewicz**  
*Covers and fiberings of cell complexes*

**Deanna Needell**  
*Towards foundational machine learning*

**Chadi Saad\-Roy**  
*Theoretical models for adaptation and response of pathogens to new and changing host environments*

**Nicolle Gonzalez**  
*Combinatorics and representation theory in link homology*

**Pablo Shmerkin**  
*Deeper interactions between fractal geometry, harmonic analysis and ergodic theory*

**Lior Silberman**  
*Projects in pure and applied mathematics*

**Christoph Ortner**  
*Scientific machine learning for particle systems*
