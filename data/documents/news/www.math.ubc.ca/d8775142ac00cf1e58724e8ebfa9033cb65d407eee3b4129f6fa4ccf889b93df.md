## Sujatha Ramdorai Appointed as a Fields Institute Fellow

UBC Mathematics Professor Dr\. Sujatha Ramdorai has been appointed as a Fields Institute Fellow in recognition of outstanding contributions to activities at the Fields Institute and within the Canadian mathematical community\.
