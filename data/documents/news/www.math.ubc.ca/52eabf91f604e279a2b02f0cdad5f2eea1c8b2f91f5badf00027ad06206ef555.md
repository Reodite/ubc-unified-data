## Feb 23, 2018: Gordon Slade

[Gordon Slade](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~slade/) \(FRSC, FRS\) is the winner of the CMS Jeffery\-Williams Prize for 2018\! 

The Prize is awarded by the Canadian Mathematical Society to recognize mathematicians who have made outstanding contributions to mathematical research\. Details on our [awards page](https://wdp.math.ubc.ca/news-events/awards)\.
