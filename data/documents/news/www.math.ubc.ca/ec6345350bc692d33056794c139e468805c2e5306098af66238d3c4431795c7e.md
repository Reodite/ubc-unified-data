## Rachel Kuske: AWM Service Award \(2013\)

[Rachel Kuske](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~rachel/) has been selected by the Association for Women in Mathematics \(AWM\) as one of ten recipients of their inaugural AWM Service Award\. The AWM was founded in 1971 and now has over 3,000 student, individual and institutional members\. The AWM Service Award recognizes individuals for helping to promote and support women in mathematics through exceptional voluntary service to the Association for Women in Mathematics\.
