## Steph van Willigenburg has been awarded the Mathematical Association of America Robbins Prize for 2023

**[Steph van Willigenburg](https://www.math.ubc.ca/user/2696)** has been awarded the Mathematical Association of America Robbins Prize for 2023\! This prize is awarded every three years and honours the authors of a paper reporting on novel research in algebra, combinatorics, or discrete mathematics\. Papers are judged on quality of research, clarity of exposition, and accessibility to undergraduates\. Steph’s paper "Resolving Stanley’s e\-positivity of claw\-contractible\-free graphs” was written jointly with Samantha Dahlberg \(who you may remember as a postdoc here\) and her collaborator Angèle Foley from Wilfrid Laurier University\. Congratulations to Steph and see the attached photo from the prize awarding, below\!
