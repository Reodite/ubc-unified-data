## Melania Alvarez: UBC Envisioning Equality Award \(2022\)

**Melania Alvarez** has been announced as a recipient of the UBC Envisioning Equality award on International Women’s Day \(today\)\. This is the first award of its kind initiated to highlight and celebrate exceptional women and gender\-diverse faculty and staff at UBC\.
