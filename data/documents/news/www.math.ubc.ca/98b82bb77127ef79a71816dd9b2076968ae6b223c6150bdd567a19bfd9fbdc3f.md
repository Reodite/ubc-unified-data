## Omer Angel: Sloan Research Fellowship \(2010\)

[Omer Angel](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~angel/) has been awarded a 2010 Sloan Research Fellowship\. These prestigious two year fellowships seek to stimulate fundamental research by early\-career scientists and scholars of outstanding promise\. The awards recognize distinguished performance and a unique potential to make substantial contributions to their field\.

More information is available at: [http://www\.sloan\.org/pressroom/item/458/2010\-sloan\-research\-fellowships%C2%A0and](http://www.sloan.org/pressroom/item/458/2010-sloan-research-fellowships%C2%A0and) more on UBC awardees at: [http://science\.ubc\.ca/news/355](http://science.ubc.ca/news/355)
