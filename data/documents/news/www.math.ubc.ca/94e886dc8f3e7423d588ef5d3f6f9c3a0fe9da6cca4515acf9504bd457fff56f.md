## Gordon Slade: Killam Research Prize \(2005\)

[Gordon Slade](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~slade/) has been awarded a [2005 Killam Research Prize](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/scholarships-awards-funding/awards-faculty-members)\.
