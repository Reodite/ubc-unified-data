## Dragos Ghioca: Ribenboim Prize \(2012\)

[Dragos Ghioca](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~dghioca/) has been named the winner of the 2012 Ribenboim Prize\. This prize is awarded every two years by the Canadian Number Theory Association for distinguished research in Number Theory\. See [http://www\.cs\.uleth\.ca/%7Ecnta2012/CNTA12\-RibenboimAnnounce\.html](http://www.cs.uleth.ca/%7Ecnta2012/CNTA12-RibenboimAnnounce.html)
