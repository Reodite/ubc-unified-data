## Malabika Pramanik: CMS Krieger\-Nelson Prize \(2016\)

[Malabika Pramanik](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~malabika/) was the recipient of the 2016 CMS Krieger\-Nelson prize for her outstanding research contributions\. Details of Malabika's contributions are presented with the media release, at   
[http://cms\.math\.ca/MediaReleases/2016/kn\-award\.html](http://cms.math.ca/MediaReleases/2016/kn-award.html)

As a winner, Malabika joins an impressive list of Canadian mathematical leaders, including our own Ailana Fraser, Rachel Kuske,Izabella Laba, Leah Keshet, and Cindy Greenwood\.  
[https://cms\.math\.ca/Prizes/info/kn\.html](https://cms.math.ca/Prizes/info/kn.html)
