## Pardis Semnani Awarded Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship

Pardis Semnani has been awarded a Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship \(Vanier CGS\)\.

The Vanier CGS program awards up to $50,000 per year for three years, for students working in social sciences and humanities, natural sciences, engineering and health\. Across Canada, 166 Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships were announced\.
