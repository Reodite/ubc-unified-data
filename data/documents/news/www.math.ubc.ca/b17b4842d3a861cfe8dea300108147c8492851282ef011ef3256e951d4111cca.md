Quanta Magazine talked to UBC Mathematics Professor Dr\. Jim Bryan about 2026 Fields Medal recipient Dr\. John Pardon\. Dr\. Bryan discussed Dr\. Pardon's work, explaining why a proof he posted in 2023 is considered "the biggest result in enumerative algebraic geometry in the last 20 years\."
