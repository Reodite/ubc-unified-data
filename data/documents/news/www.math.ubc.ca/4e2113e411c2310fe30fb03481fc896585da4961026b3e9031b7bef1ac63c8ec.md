## Martin Barlow, Michael Doebeli and Leah Keshet: Distinguished Scholars in Residence \(2009\)

[Martin Barlow](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~barlow/), [Michael Doebeli](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~doebeli/) and [Leah Keshet](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~keshet/) have been appointed as Distinguished Scholars in Residence for 2009 at UBC's Peter Wall Institute for Advance Study\. The Distinguished Scholars in Residence program, targeted at senior Associate and Full Professors, was developed to bring to the Institute outstanding, UBC faculty members with distinguished research records and commitment to interdisciplinarity\.

More information on the PWIAS Distinguished Scholars in Residence program can be found at: [http://www\.pwias\.ubc\.ca/programs/sir\.php](http://www.pwias.ubc.ca/programs/sir.php)
