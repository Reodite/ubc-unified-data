## UBC Mathematics Welcomes Chadi Saad\-Roy as an Assistant Professor

Image

This fall, Dr\. Chadi Saad\-Roy joins UBC Mathematics as an Assistant Professor, jointly appointed in the Department of Microbiology and Immunology\!

Dr\. Saad\-Roy comes to UBC from the Miller Institute for Basic Research in Science at UC Berkeley, where he was a Miller Research Fellow\. His research focuses on infectious disease dynamics, using mathematical models to study immuno\-epidemiology, behavioral\-epidemiological dynamics, and pathogen eco\-evolutionary dynamics\.

Dr\. Saad\-Roy received his Ph\.D\. in Quantitative and Computational Biology from Princeton University in 2022\. He is also no stranger to British Columbia—he earned his B\.Sc\. in Mathematics and Statistics with a minor in Biology from the University of Victoria, where he was awarded the Jubilee Medal for Science\.  
 

Read more about Chadi's research [**HERE**](https://mbim.ubc.ca/news/welcome-chadi-saad-roy)\.
