## Dale Rolfsen: Docteur Honoris Causa \(2007\)

[Dale Rolfsen](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~rolfsen/) will receive an honorary doctorate \(Docteur Honoris Causa\) from the University of Caen, France\. The award will take place in Caen in November, where Dale will be giving a course of lectures and finishing a new edition of his book "Why are braids orderable?"
