## David Brydges and Zinovy Reichstein: Speakers \(2009\)

[David Brydges](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~db5d/) and [Zinovy Reichstein](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~reichst/) have both accepted invitations to speak at the next International Congress of Mathematicians in 2010 in Hyderabad, India\. This is an extremely prestigious honour \-\- only a handful of mathematicians receive these invitation are invited\. The ICM meets only once every four years\.
