## 2024 CANADA GRADUATE SCHOLARSHIP WINNERS

On March 13, 2024, the Government of Canada announced the award recipients of the NSERC Graduate and Postgraduate scholarships\. Two recipents are from the UBC Mathematics Department:

-   Chi Hoi \(Kyle\) Yip \- NSERC Canada Graduate Scholarships \- Doctoral
-   Nicholas Richardson \- NSERC Postgraduate Scholarships \- Doctoral

These awards recognize and support the next generation of research at UBC and across Canada\.
