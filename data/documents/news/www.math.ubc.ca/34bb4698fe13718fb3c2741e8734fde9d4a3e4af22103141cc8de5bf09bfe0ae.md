## Tai\-Peng Tsai: Andre\-Aisenstadt Prize \(2005\)

[Tai\-Peng Tsai](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~ttsai/) has been awarded the Andre\-Aisenstadt Prize\. This award is given by the Centre de Recherches Mathématique \(CRM\) at the Univeristy of Montreal to a young talented Canadian researchers\. More information about this prize can be found here:

English: [http://www\.crm\.umontreal\.ca/prix/prix\_an\.shtml](http://www.crm.umontreal.ca/prix/prix_an.shtml)  
French: [http://www\.crm\.umontreal\.ca/prix/prix\_fr\.shtml](http://www.crm.umontreal.ca/prix/prix_fr.shtml)
