## James Feng, Ian Frigaard, Mark Martinez: NSERC Strategic Grant \(2005\)

[James Feng](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~jfeng/), [Ian Frigaard](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~frigaard) and Mark Martinez \(Chemical Engineering\) have been awarded a NSERC strategic grant to research smart spacers for extended reach horizontal well cementing\.
