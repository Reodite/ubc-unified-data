## Inaugural Fellows of the American Mathematical Society \(2012\)

[Alejandro Adem](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~adem/), [Martin Barlow](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~barlow/), [David Boyd](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~boyd/boyd.html), [Jim Carrell](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~carrell/), [Bill Casselman](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~cass/), [Ailana Fraser](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~afraser/), N[assif Ghoussoub](http://www.birs.ca/~nassif/), [Izabella Laba](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~ilaba/), [Zinovy Reichstein](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~reichst/), Maurice Sion and [Gordon Slade](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~slade/) were announced as Inaugural Fellows of the American Mathematical Society\.  
See [http://www\.ams\.org/profession/ams\-fellows/ams\-fellows](http://www.ams.org/profession/ams-fellows/ams-fellows)
