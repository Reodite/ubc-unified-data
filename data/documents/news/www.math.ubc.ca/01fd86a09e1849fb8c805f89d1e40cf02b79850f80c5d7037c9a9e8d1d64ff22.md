## Kai Behrend: CRM\-Fileds\-PIMS Prize \(2014\)

[Kai Behrend](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~behrend/) has won the 2015 CRM\-Fileds\-PIMS prize\. This is the top award for a research mathematician in Canada\. More information about the prize and Kai's work is detailed in their [CMS Email announcement](http://www.math.ubc.ca/Dept/Awards/CRM-Fields-PIMS_2014.txt)\.
