## Dec 10, 2018: Martin Barlow, Michael Bennett, George Bluman, David Boyd, Ailana Fraser, Nassif Ghoussoub and Malabika Pramanik

Martin Barlow, Michael Bennett, George Bluman, David Boyd, Ailana Fraser, Nassif Ghoussoub and Malabika Pramanik were annouced as the [Canadian Mathematical Society](https://cms.math.ca/) Inaugural Class of Fellows\. Details on our [awards page](https://www.math.ubc.ca/news-events/awards/dec-10-2018-martin-barlow-michael-bennett-george-bluman-david-boyd-ailana-fraser)\.
