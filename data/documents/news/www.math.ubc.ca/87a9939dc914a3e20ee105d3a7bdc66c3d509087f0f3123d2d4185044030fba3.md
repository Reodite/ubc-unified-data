## Kasia Jankiewicz Promoted to Associate Professor

Image

UBC Mathematics is pleased to announce that Kasia Jankiewicz has been promoted to Associate Professor, with tenure\. 

Dr\. Jankiewicz, whose research focuses on geometry and topology, joined UBC Mathematics as an assistant professor in 2025\. Prior to UBC, she spent four years as an assistant professor at the University of California, Santa Cruz\.
