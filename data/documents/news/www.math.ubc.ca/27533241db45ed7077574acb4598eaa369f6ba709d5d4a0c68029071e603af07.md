## Matthew Coles and Vanessa Radzimski: Killam Prizes \(2016\)

[Matthew Coles](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~colesmp/) and Vanessa Radzimski are the winners of 2015/2016 Killam Prizes for outstanding work as a Graduate Teaching Assistant in Science\.
