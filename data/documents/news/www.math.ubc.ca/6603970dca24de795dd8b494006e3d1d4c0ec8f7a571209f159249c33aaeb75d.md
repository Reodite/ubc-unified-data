## Lindsey Daniels, Assistant Professor of Teaching, Speaks to CBC's Gloria Macarenko

Worried about your child's math skills slipping this summer? You're not alone\! Lindsey Daniels, UBC Mathematics Assistant Professor of Teaching, recently chatted with CBC's Gloria Macarenko on "On the Coast" about beating the dreaded "summer slide" when it comes to math\.

Dr\. Daniels offered some fantastic tips to help keep kids curious and confident with numbers throughout their break\. She highlighted how everyday activities can become engaging math moments, making learning fun and preventing that summer learning loss\.
