## Myrto Mavraki: UBC's Master's Governor General's Gold Medal \(2015\)

[Myrto Mavraki](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~myrtomav/) has been selected to receive UBC's Master's Governor General's Gold Medal, the prize given to the Master's graduate who achieved the most outstanding academic record in their graduating year\.  
Details regarding nomination procedures are posted on [the Faculty of Graduate Studies website](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/governor-generals-gold-medal)\.
