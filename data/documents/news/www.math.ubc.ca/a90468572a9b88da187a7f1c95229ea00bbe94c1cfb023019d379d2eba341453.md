## MIRANDA HOLMES\-CERFON RECEIVES EXPLORATION GRANT

[Embedded content: YouTube video player](https://www.youtube.com/embed/IEYnRPoIlmc?si=dlebD-1xYKqsPGZh)

Miranda Holmes\-Cerfon has received an Exploration grant from the New Frontiers in Research Fund for her project *Disordered Lattices: Mathematical Pathways to Engineering New Materials*\.

Exploration grants support research that push boundaries into exciting new areas\. Researchers are encouraged to think “outside of the box” and undertake research that would defy current paradigms; brings disciplines together in unexpected ways and from bold, innovative perspectives; and has the potential to be disruptive or deliver game\-changing impacts\.
