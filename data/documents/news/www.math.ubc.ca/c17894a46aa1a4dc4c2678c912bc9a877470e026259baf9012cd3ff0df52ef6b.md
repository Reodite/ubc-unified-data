## Patrick Brosnan: Coxeter\-James Prize \(2009\)

[Patrick Brosnan](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~brosnan/) has been awarded the 2009 Coxeter\-James prize for research by the Canadian Mathematical Society for his outstanding contributions to mathematical research\. The full citation can be found at [http://www\.cms\.math\.ca/MediaReleases/2009/res\-prizes](http://www.cms.math.ca/MediaReleases/2009/res-prizes)
