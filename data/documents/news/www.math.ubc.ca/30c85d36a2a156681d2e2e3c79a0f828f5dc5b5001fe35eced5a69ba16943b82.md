## Fok\-Shuen Leung wins Killam Teaching Prize \(2021/2022\)

The Killam teaching prize is awarded every year to an elite group across the university\. It recognizes outstanding achievements as a teacher and reflects enthusiasm and commitment to our students\.

More information about the prize is available at: [https://academic\.ubc\.ca/awards\-funding/award\-winners/killam\-teaching\-service\-winners](https://academic.ubc.ca/awards-funding/award-winners/killam-teaching-service-winners)
