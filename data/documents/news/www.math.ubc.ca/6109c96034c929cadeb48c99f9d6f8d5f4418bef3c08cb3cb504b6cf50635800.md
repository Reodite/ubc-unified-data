## Robert Miura: Leroy P\. Steele Prize \(2006\)

[Robert Miura](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~miura/) has won the 2006 Leroy P\. Steele Prize with C\.S\. Gardner, J\.M\. Greene, and M\.D\. Kruskal for the seminal paper Korteweg\-de Vries equation and generalization\. VI\. Methods for exact solution\. Comm\. Pure Appl\. Math\. 27\(1974\), 97\-\-133\. The Steele Prize is a prestiguous award given by the American Mathematical Society\. More information about this prize can be found at

[MS Prize \- Leroy P\. Steele Prize web site](http://www.ams.org/prizes/steele-prize.html)
