## Leah Edelstein\-Keshet Prize \(2016\)

[Leah Edelstein\-Keshet](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~keshet/) In February the Officers and Elected Directors of the [Society for Mathematical Biology](http://www.smb.org/prizes/index.shtml) unanimously approved the Leah Edelstein\-Keshet Prize to recognize the exceptional scientific contributions made by a woman in mathematical biology\.   
In the annoucement online at [http://www\.smb\.org/publications/SMBnet/digest/v16/v16i08\.html](http://www.smb.org/publications/SMBnet/digest/v16/v16i08.html), SMB President Schnell outlines UBC Mathematics Professor [Edelstein\-Keshet's](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~keshet/) outstanding scientific contributions and service\.
