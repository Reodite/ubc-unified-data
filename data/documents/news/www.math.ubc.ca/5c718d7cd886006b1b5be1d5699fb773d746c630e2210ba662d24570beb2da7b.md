A \(dis\)continuous percolation phase transition on the hierarchical lattice

October 23, 2024

For long\-range percolation on $\\mathbb\{Z\}$ with translation\-invariant edge kernel $J$, it is a classical theorem of Aizenman and Newman \(1986\) that the phase transition is discontinuous when $J\(x\-y\)$ is of order $\|x\-y\|^\{\-2\}$ and that there is no phase transition at all when $J\(x\-y\)\=o\(\|x\-y\|^\{\-2\.\.\.
