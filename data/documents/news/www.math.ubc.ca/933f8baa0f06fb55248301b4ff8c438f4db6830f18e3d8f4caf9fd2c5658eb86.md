## Nike Vatsal: Canadian Mathematical Society Coxeter\-James Prize \(2007\)

[Nike Vatsal](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~vatsal/) has won the Canadian Mathematical Society 2007 Coxeter\-James Prize\. This prize recognizes young mathematicians have made outstanding contributions to mathematical research\.

For more details, please see the [press release from the CMS](http://www.math.ubc.ca/Dept/Awards/CMS-CJP2007.txt)\. More information about the prize itself can be found at the [CMS web site](http://www.math.ca/)\.
