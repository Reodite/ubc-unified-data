## Gordon Slade: Fellowship in the Institute of Mathematical Statistics \(2011\)

[Gordon Slade](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~slade/) has been elected to Fellowship in the Institute of Mathematical Statistics\.

Fellowship in the IMS honors the outstanding research and professional contributions of its members, recognizing distinction in probability or statistics research and leadership\.

More information on IMS Fellows can be found at:  
[http://www\.imstat\.org/awards/honored\_fellows\.htm](http://www.imstat.org/awards/honored_fellows.htm)
