## Elina Robeva is the 2022 recipient of the CAIMS/PIMS Early Career Researcher Award \(2022\)

This award recognizes exceptional research in any branch of applied mathematics where the recipient is less than ten years past the date of Ph\.D\. at the time of nomination\.

More information is available at: [https://caims\.ca/award/caims\-pims\-early\-career\-award/](https://caims.ca/award/caims-pims-early-career-award/)
