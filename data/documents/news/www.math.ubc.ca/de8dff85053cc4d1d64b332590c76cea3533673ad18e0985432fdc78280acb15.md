## James J\. Feng: Fellow of the American Physical Society \(2013\)

[James J\. Feng](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~jfeng/) has been elected as a Fellow of the American Physical Society\.   
Citation:

> For pioneering studies of solid\-liquid two\-phase flows, interfacial dynamics of complex fluids, and phase\-field modeling of the moving contact line\.
