## Omer Angel: Rollo Davidson Prize \(2016\)

[Omer Angel](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~angel/) has been awarded the Rollo Davidson Prize from the Statistical Laboratory at the University of Cambridge\.  
The full citation is available at: [http://www\.statslab\.cam\.ac\.uk/Rollo/](http://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/Rollo/)
