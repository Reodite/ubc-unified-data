## Alejandro Adem: Corresponding Member \(2017\)

[Alejandro Ádem](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~adem/) has been named as a Corresponding Member of the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](http://amc.edu.mx/amc/)\. His installation will occur at the Mathematical Institute of the UNAM on 22 April 2017, where he will lecture on "Navegando entre grupos, representaciones y topologÍa"\. The promotional is at [http://amc\.edu\.mx/amc/carteles/CartelAADEMAMC\.pdf](http://amc.edu.mx/amc/carteles/CartelAADEMAMC.pdf)
