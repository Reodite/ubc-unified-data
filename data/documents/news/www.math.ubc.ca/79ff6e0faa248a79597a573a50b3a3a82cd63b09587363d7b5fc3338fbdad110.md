## Gordon Slade: Prix de L'Institut Henri Poincaré \(2003\)

[Gordon Slade](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~slade) is the co\-winner of the [Prix de L'Institut Henri Poincaré \(IHP\) 2003](http://www1.elsevier.com/homepage/sac/mathweb/prixihp_2003.html) with Remco van der Hofstad of the Stieljes Institue for Mathematics, Delft University, for the paper

###### Convergence of Critical Oriented Percolation to Super\-Brownian Motion Above 4\+1 Dimensions

Remco van der Hofstad and Gordon Slade Annales de l'Institut Henri Poincaré \(B\) Probabilités et Statistiques, Volume 39, Issue 3\.
