## May 07, 2018: Tom Hutchcroft

Tom Hutchcroft has been awarded a Governor General's Gold Medal\. This recognizes Tom as the UBC doctoral graduate with the most outstanding academic record\. Dr\. Hutchcroft made a strong impression during his time here, with a paper in the Inventiones in 2016, two productive summers as an intern at Microsoft Research, and the Math Department's Graduate Research Prize in 2015\. Details on our [awards page](https://wdp.math.ubc.ca/news-events/awards)\.
