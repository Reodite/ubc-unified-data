## Izabella Laba: Canadian Mathematical Society Krieger\-Nelson Prize \(2007\)

[Izabella Laba](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~ilaba/) has won the Canadian Mathematical Society 2008 Krieger\-Nelson Prize, which recognizes outstanding research by a female\.

For more details, please see [the press release from the CMS](http://www.math.ubc.ca/Dept/Awards/CMS-KNP2008.txt)\. More information about the prize itself can be found at the [CMS web site](http://www.math.ca/)\.
