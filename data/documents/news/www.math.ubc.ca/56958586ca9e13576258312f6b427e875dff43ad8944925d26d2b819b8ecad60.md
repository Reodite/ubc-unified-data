### Riku Anttila

University of Jyväskylä  
General Sobolev\-\-Poincaré type Inequalities and applications to heat kernel estimates

January 28, 2026

In this talk, I discuss how a Poincaré inequality with respect to a fixed doubling measure can be used to establish Sobolev\-\-Poincaré type inequalities for general Radon measures\. This approach provides tools to analyze general energies on metric spaces, including non\-linear or non\-local\.\.\. [Read more](https://www.math.ubc.ca/events/jan-21-2026-general-sobolev-poincar%C3%A9-type-inequalities-and-applications-heat-kernel-estimates)
