## Julia Gordon and Rachel Kuske: NSERC Discovery Accelerator Supplements \(2015\)

[Julia Gordon](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~gor/) and [Rachel Kuske](http://www.math.ubc.ca/~rachel/) have been awarded NSERC Discovery Accelerator Supplements\.

-   **Julia Gordon**, for "Uniform estimates and asymptotics for p\-adic orbital integrals and characters"
-   **Rachel Kuske**, for "Stochastic nonlinear dynamics in the environment and biology"

More information about the program is available at:  
[http://www\.nserc\-crsng\.gc\.ca/Professors\-Professeurs/Grants\-Subs/DGAS\-SGS\.\.\.](http://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Professors-Professeurs/Grants-Subs/DGAS-SGSA_eng.asp)
