**Vancouver, BC, November 17th, 2025**

[Image](https://kpark.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/11/Screen-Shot-2025-11-06-at-2.31.41-PM-1024x574.png)

Please join us in congratulating [Everly Jazi](https://park.forestry.ubc.ca/people/) on successfully defending her thesis on October 1st\! Her thesis, entitled *Natural space and psychological wellbeing: who benefits?*, investigates links between natural space and psychological wellbeing for marginalized community members\. Using three complementary studies, Everly examined if natural space access and use could bring about health equity by leveling up wellbeing\. We can’t wait to see the next chapter of Dr\. Jazi’s research on nature and health\!  
  
For more information on her research, please visit the [Projects](https://park.forestry.ubc.ca/projects/) page\.
