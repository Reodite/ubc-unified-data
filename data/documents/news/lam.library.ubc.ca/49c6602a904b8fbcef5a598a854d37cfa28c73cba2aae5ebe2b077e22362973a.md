October is Disability Employment Awareness Month \(DEAM\)\! The David Lam Management Research Library invites you to explore our thematic book display about Disability Employment Awareness, as we celebrate the tremendous contributions persons experiencing disabilities make to our workplaces and communities\.

From inclusive hiring practices to accessible design, disability is reshaping how organizations think about talent, customer experience, and social responsibility\. Our curated selection highlights key works that examine disability through the lens of entrepreneurship, workplace culture, policy, and advocacy\. Whether you’re interested in case studies, memoirs, or management strategies, these titles offer fresh perspectives on building a more inclusive economy\.

Stop by the display in the library to browse featured books \(links below\) or explore the full list online through our catalog\. Let these reads challenge assumptions, spark ideas, and inspire action\.

-   [Against technoableism : rethinking who needs improvement by Shew, Ashley](https://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=12860011)
-   [Disability management : theory, strategy &amp; industry practice by Dyck, Dianne E\. G\.](https://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=7247096)
-   [Human resources guide to managing disability in the workplace : a Canadian HR reporter special report by Gowan, Nancy J\.](https://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=8539238)
-   [Seven steps to college success : a pathway for students with disabilities by Hamblet, Elizabeth C\.](https://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=13627298)
-   [The canary code : a guide to neurodiversity, dignity, and intersectional belonging at work by Praslova, Ludmila N\.](https://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=13543311)
-   [Spectrum of success : how embracing neurodiversity can revolutionize your business by Bell, Thomas Duncan](https://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=13630729) 
-   [Neurodiversity at work : drive innovation, performance and productivity with a neurodiverse workforce by Smith, Theo \(Theo A\.\)](https://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=11914438)
-   [Improving workplace quality : new perspectives and challenges for worker well\-being](https://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=9358774) 
-   [Managing diversity : toward a globally inclusive workplace by Mor\-Barak, Michàlle E\.](https://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=6684463)
