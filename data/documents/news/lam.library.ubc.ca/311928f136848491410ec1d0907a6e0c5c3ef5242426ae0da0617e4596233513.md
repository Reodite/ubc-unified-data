February is a month to celebrate our diversity\. In addition to being designated as [Black History Month](http://diversity.ubc.ca/black-history-month/), there have been many other diversity events happening on campus such as [OUTweek](http://diversity.ubc.ca/outweek-2016/) throughout this month\. We encourage you to take a look at other [Diversity and Intercultural Events](http://diversity.ubc.ca/) at UBC\.

In celebration of diversity month, David Lam Library has put on a book display on diversity\.  We encourage you to come to the library and browse through our diverse book collection\.
