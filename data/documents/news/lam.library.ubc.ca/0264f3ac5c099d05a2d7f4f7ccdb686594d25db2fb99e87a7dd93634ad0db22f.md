UBC Library is pleased to announce that we now have a temporary access to RepRisk data thanks to funding from UBC Sauder’s Finance Division and the Peter Lusztig Award\.  RepRisk provides access to [ESG Risk data](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20151019006252/en/RepRisk-risk-data-now-available-to-academic-institutions-across-the-world)

Access will be for one year\. Patrons will need to have a [WRDS](https://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?details=wrds&amp;id=408) account in order to access the data\. Current UBC graduate students, faculty, librarians, staff  who wish to use WRDS can request an account\.
