UBC Library is streamlining its website, introducing more user\-friendly tools and design features\. On Monday, August 15th,  the library launched its new *[Search Collections portal](http://search.library.ubc.ca/),* available via the navigational bar at the top of every library page\.

This portal enables users to perform a basic search of the library catalogue, online and print journals, indexes and databases, library research guides, course materials, as well as UBC research and digital collections, using a common interface\.  Click [here](http://blogs.ubc.ca/eresourcestatus/2011/08/16/the-new-search-collections-portal-wheres-the-ejournals/) for more details on using *Search Collections* to find UBC Library’s ejournals\.

Click [here](http://search.library.ubc.ca/) to explore the new Search Collections portal\.
