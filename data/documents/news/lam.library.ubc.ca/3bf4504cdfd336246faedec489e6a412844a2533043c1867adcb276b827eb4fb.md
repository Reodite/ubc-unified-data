As part of our renewal of the [IBISWorld database](https://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?details=ibisworld-industry-reports&amp;id=961), UBC will have access to a new set of industry reports: the US NAICS Specialized Collection\. An additional module has been obtained for the next year \(until June 10, 2020\)\. This is the Ontario Provincial Industry Reports\.

The US Specialized Industry Database is a collection of industry reports that focuses on niche and emerging US industries\.  With more than 600 reports – ranging from Advisory Services to Technology – IBISWorld will help UBC faculty and students keep up to date on several specialized sectors\.

• United States Specialized Industry: Life Sciences  
• United States Specialized Industry: Technology  
• United States Specialized Industry: Goods &amp; Services  
• United States Specialized Industry: Financial Advisory  
• United States Specialized Industry: Specialist Engineering  
• United States Specialized Industry: Industrial Machinery  
• United States Specialized Industry: Online Retailing  
• United States Specialized Industry: Business Franchises  
• United States Specialized Industry: Retail Market

In addition to the US Specialized Collection, UBC will now have access to the IBISWorld Industry Provincial Reports for Ontario for the next year only\. These reports cover the largest industries and key economic drivers in the province of Ontario, covering the same level of detail as our collection of IBIS Canada reports, all at the 5\-digit level of the NAICS\. Some of the Ontario sectors covered include Manufacturing, Construction, Retail Trade, Professional Services, and Agriculture\.

Be sure to check out our specialized reports before next summer\. Whether you are interested in sunglasses stores, massage franchises, sushi restaurants, or Organic Crop Farming, IBIS has a report for you\.
