UBC’s 17th annual **Celebrate Learning Week** returns from May 4 – 8, 2026, bringing together faculty, staff, and students from both the Vancouver and Okanagan campuses to explore innovation and transformation in teaching and learning\.

Over the course of the week, more than 25 online and in\-person sessions will highlight how members of the UBC community are navigating transformation, whether through small, everyday shifts or bold, large\-scale initiatives\.

This week\-long event is an opportunity to showcase teaching and learning and to engage with peers, share ideas, and celebrate creativity and progress across disciplines\. Whether you’re interested in classroom innovation, digital learning, or institutional transformation, there’s something for everyone\.

Explore [all sessions](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/2026-celebrate-learning-week/) and [download the PDF Schedule](https://celebratelearning-new.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/CLW-2026-Schedule.pdf) to plan your week\.
