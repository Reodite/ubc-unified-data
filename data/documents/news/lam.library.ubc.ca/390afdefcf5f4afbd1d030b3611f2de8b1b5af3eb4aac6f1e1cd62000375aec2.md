## [Koerner Fiction collection on display at Lam library](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/2016/09/08/koerner-fiction-collection-on-display-at-lam-library/)

By [irena trebic](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/author/irena-trebic/ "Posts by irena trebic") on September 8, 2016

David Lam Library started a small fiction collection this summer and we have just added several new titles such as Cloud Atlas and Tigana\. We also wanted to take this opportunity to expose our students to the wider UBC collections\.
