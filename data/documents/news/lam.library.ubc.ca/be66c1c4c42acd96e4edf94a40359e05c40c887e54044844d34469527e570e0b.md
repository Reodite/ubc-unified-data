David Lam Library is hosting an information session on copyright guidelines for Sauder faculty, instructors, researchers and staff on **Tuesday, September 27\[th\] from 9:30 a\.m\. – 11:00 a\.m\.** in the Canaccord and Peter M\. Brown Amphitheatre \(DL 125\)\.

[Joy Kirchner](http://toby.library.ubc.ca/libstaff/showperson.cfm?PID=163), UBC Library’s Scholarly Communications Coordinator, is the guest speaker and has presented several workshops on the current copyright environment at UBC for the Library, for CTLT, and for individual faculties\. Her aim in this session is to provide participants with:

-   an overview of copyright and fair dealing provisions
-   an understanding of use permissions for copyrighted materials and licensed material for classroom, research and instructional use
-   an understanding of author’s scholarly rights and publisher agreements

To register for this session, please email [christina\.sylka@ubc\.ca](mailto:christina.sylka@ubc.ca)\.

For further information on the copyright environment at UBC, please visit [http://copyright\.ubc\.ca/](http://copyright.ubc.ca/)\.  If you have an individual questions about copyright, consult the copyright FAQ on this website\.

For questions not answered by the FAQ, e\-mail your question to [ubc\-copyright@interchange\.ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc-copyright@interchange.ubc.ca)\.
