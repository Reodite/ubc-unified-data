[Thrive UBC](http://thrive.ubc.ca/) is a weeklong series of events focused on helping everyone at UBC explore their path to mental health\. This year’s Thrive Week is happening October 29 to November 2\. Visit [Thrive UBC](http://thrive.ubc.ca/) to learn more about how you can attend a Thrive event, as well as other resources available to you\.

We also encourage you to check out our Thrive book display at David Lam Library\. Some of our relevant titles include:
