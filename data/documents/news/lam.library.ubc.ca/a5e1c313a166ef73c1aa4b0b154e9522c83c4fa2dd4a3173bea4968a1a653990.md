## [Remote library support during COVID\-19](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/2020/03/24/remote-library-support-during-covid-19/)

By [irena trebic](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/author/irena-trebic/ "Posts by irena trebic") on March 24, 2020

Due to the quickly evolving situation with COVID\-19, all UBC Library branches including David Lam Library and Canaccord Learning Commons will be closed from 5pm Friday, March 20th until Monday, April 6th\.  During this time librarians and staff will be working remotely to continue to support the UBC Sauder School of Business\.
