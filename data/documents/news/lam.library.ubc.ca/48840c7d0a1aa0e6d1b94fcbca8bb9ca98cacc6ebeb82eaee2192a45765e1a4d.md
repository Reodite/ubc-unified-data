[Open Education Week](https://open.ubc.ca/join-us-open-education-week-2026/?login) \(OE Week\) returns this March, bringing together educators, students, creators, and community partners for a global celebration of openness in teaching and learning\. It is an opportunity for actively sharing and learning about the latest achievements in Open Education\.

Running from Monday, March 2 to Wednesday, March 11, this year’s program offers a rich lineup of online workshops and interactive sessions designed to spark new ideas to remove barriers to learning, including using zines in open pedagogy, co\-creating open resources, creating accessible resources and more\. It’s all part of a [worldwide](https://oeweek.oeglobal.org/) celebration\.

For more details and to register please visit [Open Education Week 2026 \| Open UBC](https://open.ubc.ca/join-us-open-education-week-2026/?login)
