[eCorne](http://ecorner.stanford.edu/index.html)r, the *Stanford Technology Ventures Program \(STVP\) Entrepreneurship Corner*, is a  free archive of entrepreneurship resources, providing access to over 3,000 videos and podcasts featuring entrepreneurship and innovation thought leaders\.

The most popular videos currently on the site include:

-   The Art of Teaching Entrepreneurship and Innovation / Tina Seelig
-   The Power of Curiosity and Inspiration /Jack Dorsey
-   Creating Enchantment / Guy Kawasaki
-   The Future of Microsoft, The Future of Technology / Steve Ballmer
-   Tools for Business Model Generation  / Alexander Osterwalder
-   Five Biggest Mistakes That Entrepreneurs Make / Jerry Kaplan

[eCorner](http://ecorner.stanford.edu/index.html)‘s mission is to support and encourage the teaching and learning of entrepreneurship so videos and podcasts can be used for instructional and educational purposes\. [eCorner](http://ecorner.stanford.edu/index.html) uses a [Creative Commons](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/us/)  license and provides a detailed [Terms of Use](http://ecorner.stanford.edu/termsOfUse.html) statement\. Videos and podcasts can be viewed online\. Registering for a free personal account allows users to create playlists, receive updates and download content which can then be embedded in PowerPoint presentations or on web pages\.

Explore [eCorner](http://ecorner.stanford.edu/index.html) to search the collection, to browse [by topic](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/2014/04/16/new-in-the-lam-library-free-portal-of-entrepreneurship-videos-and-podcasts/ecorner.stanford.edu/browse.html) or to view a [list of speakers](http://ecorner.stanford.edu/speakersAuthors.html)\.
