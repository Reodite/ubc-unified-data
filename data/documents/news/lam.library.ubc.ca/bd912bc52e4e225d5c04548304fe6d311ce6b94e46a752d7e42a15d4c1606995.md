If you have a great idea to help make UBC a better campus but lack funding, the [UBC Equity Enhancement Fund](http://ubc.us2.list-manage.com/track/click?u=e16ca49d875b94fc549d40fa0&amp;id=997cc8564e&amp;e=d3947d001d) can assist\. This Fund supports community\-based initiatives that enhance equity, diversity, inclusion and intercultural understanding at UBC\. The 2016 call for proposals is open and applications are due by **April 1**\.

Proposals should demonstrate benefits to the representation or experiences of historically disadvantaged groups within the UBC community\. All academic or administrative units are invited to apply for funding\. [Learn more](http://ubc.us2.list-manage1.com/track/click?u=e16ca49d875b94fc549d40fa0&amp;id=4055bf2408&amp;e=d3947d001d) about the program including the diverse projects currently underway at the Vancouver and Okanagan campuses\!

If you are a student, be sure to attend a [student\-only](http://equity.ubc.ca/register-for-student-proposal-writing-session/) proposal writing session on March 2 or 3, 2016\.
