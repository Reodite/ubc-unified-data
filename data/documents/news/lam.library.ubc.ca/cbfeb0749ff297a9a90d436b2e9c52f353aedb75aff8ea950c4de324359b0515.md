February is Black History Month\. While Black history and excellence deserves recognition every day, this month offers a moment for focused, intentional and elevated reflection and recognition of Black history and achievement, and the many historical and contemporary contributions of Black Canadians, Black British Columbians and Black students, faculty and staff at UBC\.

We invite everyone to visit the branch and explore our latest selection of books celebrating Black history\. This year’s collection includes works spanning a wide range of genres and themes, from African Americans: Segregation and History to powerful personal memoirs that illuminate lived experiences, resilience, and resistance\.

You’ll find stories of courage, entrepreneurship, women’s empowerment, business, the history of racism, and more\.

Stop by the library display to browse the featured titles \(links below\)\.
