Did you know that October is Small Business Month? In order to support local small businesses, the Small Business Accelerator is launching its [Celebrating YOU\!](http://www.sba-bc.ca/blog/second-year-celebrating-you-campaign-kickoff-small-business-month-2015) Campaign to feature small business owners on its blog\.
