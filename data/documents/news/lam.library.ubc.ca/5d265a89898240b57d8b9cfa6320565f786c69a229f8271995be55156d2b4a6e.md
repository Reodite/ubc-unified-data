All UBC Library branches on the UBC Vancouver campus will reopen September 7, 2021\. This includes Sauder’s [David Lam Library](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/)\. The Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre remains open throughout August to students, faculty, staff and community members\. For the most up to date information of hours for different branches, please check out [Hours and Locations \| UBC Library Hours and Locations](https://hours.library.ubc.ca/)\.

We encourage Sauder students to check out **CLC:Online** on Canvas as well as our Facebook page, **UBC Sauder Canaccord Learning Commons and David Lam Library,** for more information about our services and collections\.

The Materials Pick\-Up Service will continue to provide access to the physical collection until September 2\.
