David Lam Library and the Canaccord Learning Commons will be closed for the holidays from Saturday, December 22, 2018 to Tuesday, January 1, 2019\.

The space will reopen at 8:30 am on Wednesday, January 2\. In the meantime, please visit library\.ubc\.ca to make alternative plans\.

Librarians including Irena and Kimberly will be available for online research consultations next week\. We can be reached at lam\.library@ubc\.ca
