UBC students, faculty and staff have trial access to Standard &amp; Poor’s Capital IQ database from January 11 until February 11, 2011\.

Capital IQ provides detailed coverage of public and private companies,  private investment firms, and executives\. It also provides data on capital transactions\.  Capital IQ’s software functionality includes sophisticated retrieval and personalization tools which enable users to screen the database and to create reports\.

To obtain a trial account, please visit the [Information Page](http://resources.library.ubc.ca/1696)   and click on [request an individual account](mailto:scott_hossfeld@capitaliq.com)\.  In the request email, please provide your affiliation \(UBC student, faculty or staff member\) and your UBC email address \(e\.g\. [your\.name@ubc\.ca](mailto:your.name@ubc.ca); [your\.name@sauder\.ubc\.ca](mailto:your.name@sauder.ubc.ca)\)\.  Once you have your account, please return to the information page to access the Capital IQ database\.

Thank you to UBC Library’s Kat McGrath and Capital IQ’s Scott Hossfeld for setting up this trial\.
