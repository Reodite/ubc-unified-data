New full\-text, downloadable, academic, business books are being added each month to [Palgrave Connect’s 2011 Business &amp; Management Collection](http://resources.library.ubc.ca/1671)\. Palgrave employs permissive *Digital Rights Management*, which allows authorized users to download ebook content without any physical restrictions\. Books in this collection can also be downloaded in [EPUB format](http://www.palgraveconnect.com/pc/info/latest_news.html#june2011), which is compatible with most reading devices, enabling readers to resize pages, adjust margins, set text size, search within the book, highlight text and add bookmarks\.

Click [here](http://www.palgraveconnect.com/pc/browse/advancedsearchresults?collection=busman2011&amp;order_by=publish-date) to view a complete list of the 2011 titles\. Some recent additions include:

-   The Future of Leadership Development : Corporate Needs and the Role of Business Schools\. Edited by Jordi Canals\.
-   Sport as a Business : International, Professional and Commercial Aspects\. Edited by Harald Dolles and Sten Söderman\.
-   Corporate Psychopaths : Organizational Destroyers\. By Clive Boddy\.
-   Women at the Top: Challenges, Choices and Change\. By Marianne Coleman\.

Palgrave MacMillan also publishes scholarly works for a variety of disciplines\. Explore recent publications in the areas of history, linguistics, literature, political and international studies, and social and cultural studies by connecting to the [Palgrave Connect](http://resources.library.ubc.ca/1687) collection\.
