May is Asian Heritage Month\. The theme for this year’s Asian Heritage Month is, “Continuing a legacy of greatness”\. This month is a reminder for all Canadians to come together to combat anti\-Asian racism and discrimination in all its forms\.

UBC is hosting several events in honor of Asian Heritage Month\. Some of them include:

-   **May 4, 2022 \| 4:00 p\.m\.:** **[Re\-Imagining Asian Canadian Futures](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/events/event/acre-launch-event/)**
-   **May 19, 2022 \| 5:00 p\.m\.:** **[Webinar: The Hong Kong and Taiwanese Diaspora in the Literary Imagination](https://hksi.ubc.ca/events/event/webinar-literary-imagination/)**
-   **May 2022:** **[explorASIAN Festival 2022](https://explorasian.org/explorasian-2022/)**

For more information check out:
