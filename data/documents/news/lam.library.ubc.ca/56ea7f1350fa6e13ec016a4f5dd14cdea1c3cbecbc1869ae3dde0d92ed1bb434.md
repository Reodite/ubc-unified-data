[The Leith Wheeler Investment Research Lab \(LWIRL\)](https://mybcom.sauder.ubc.ca/specialized-learning-spaces/leith-wheeler-investment-research-lab) at UBC Sauder School of Business is now equipped with upgraded audio\-visual systems and state\-of\-the\-art computer equipment\. LWIRL offers UBC students and faculty access to cutting\-edge financial analysis software and real\-time international financial data, enriching their learning and career prospects\.

This upgrade is part of a broader initiative to improve spaces at David Lam Management Research Library and Canaccord Learning Commons\.

Previous upgrades include:

September 2022 – [Wayne Deans Investment Analysis Centre](https://about.library.ubc.ca/2022/09/21/wayne-deans-investment-analysis-centre-upgrades-provide-opportunities-for-hybrid-learning/) installed new screens and other upgrades

April 2023 – [Digital Media Lab and Sound Recording Room](https://about.library.ubc.ca/2023/07/10/digital-media-lam-at-david-lam-library-completes-technology-upgrades/) completed several audio\-visual equipment upgrades

To access these labs, UBC Sauder students can book online through [CLC Online Canvas course](https://booking.sauder.ubc.ca/), while non\-Sauder students can utilize the Leith Wheeler Lab and Digital Media Lab on a drop\-in basis with assistance available at the Front Service Desk\.
