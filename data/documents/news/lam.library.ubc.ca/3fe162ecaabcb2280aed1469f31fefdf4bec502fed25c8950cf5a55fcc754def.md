Join in the Small Business Month festivities with the [Small Business Accelerator \(SBA\)](https://sba.ubc.ca/) at UBC\!

SBA \([@sba\_bc](https://twitter.com/sba_bc)\) is your go\-to resource for launching or enhancing your business strategy\. They specialize in sourcing reliable secondary market research and providing tailored support for entrepreneurs and small business owners in British Columbia, focusing on Canadian data related to BC’s unique business landscape, challenges, and opportunities\.

David Lam Management Research Library partnered with SBA to select print books for celebration of small businesses\.
