## [Trial access to Capital IQ](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/2011/01/12/trial-access-to-capital-iq/)

By [janwallace](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/author/janwallace/ "Posts by janwallace") on January 12, 2011

UBC students, faculty and staff have trial access to Standard &amp; Poor’s Capital IQ database from January 11 until February 11, 2011\. Capital IQ provides detailed coverage of public and private companies,  private investment firms, and executives\. It also provides data on capital transactions\.  Capital IQ’s software functionality includes sophisticated retrieval and personalization tools which enable users to screen the database \[…\]
