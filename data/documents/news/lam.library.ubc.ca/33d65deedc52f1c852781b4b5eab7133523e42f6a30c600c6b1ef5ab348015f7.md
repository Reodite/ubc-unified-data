[Celebrate Learning Week](https://ubc.us9.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f4dbe910b1a4d8dda48d2ac87&amp;id=4e912038dc&amp;e=11838aa87d) \(CLW\) is a week\-long initiative celebrating teaching and learning opportunities at UBC\. It will take place April 29 – May 5, 2019\. Events include open lectures, information sessions, student advising activities, [poster sessions](https://celebratelearning.ubc.ca/tlef-showcase/), workshops and more\.

This year we have several events focused on inclusive teaching\. There are two panel discussions on equity and inclusion on teaching and learning, one with [student perspectives](https://ubc.us9.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f4dbe910b1a4d8dda48d2ac87&amp;id=3e20237dbb&amp;e=11838aa87d) and one with [faculty perspectives](https://ubc.us9.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f4dbe910b1a4d8dda48d2ac87&amp;id=8f751429ce&amp;e=11838aa87d)\.

Other events include things such as UBC Studios tour, Rare Books and Special Collections \(RBSC\) tour, Indigenous Fisheries Practices, learning software such as Lynda\.com, [2019 TLEF Showcase](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/2019-tlef-showcase/) and others\.

Be sure to come out to the keynote by Kevin Lamoureux from the University of Winnipeg, “[Healing the Wound with the Weapon: Post\-Secondary Education and Decolonization](https://ubc.us9.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f4dbe910b1a4d8dda48d2ac87&amp;id=7f10b0cf45&amp;e=11838aa87d)\.” which will take place on May 2\.
