This summer the David Lam Library joined *UBC Library’s Great Reads*, a program which highlights the Library’s collections of popular and leisure reading materials\.

Lam’s *Great Reads* includes popular and recommended business books\.

Look for the green *Great Reads* sign in the David Lam Library/Canaccord Learning Commons to browse and sign out these books in person\.  Visit the *Great Reads* website \([http://greatreads\.library\.ubc\.ca/](http://greatreads.library.ubc.ca/)\) to see the *Great Reads* books located at Lam and elsewhere on campus\.

*Great Reads* collections are also available on the 3\[rd\] floor or Koerner library \(next to the fireplace\), the 2nd floor of [Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](http://www.ikebarberlearningcentre.ubc.ca/ "Irving K. Barber Learning Centre (IKBLC)") and the main floor of [Woodward Library](http://www.library.ubc.ca/woodward/)\.
