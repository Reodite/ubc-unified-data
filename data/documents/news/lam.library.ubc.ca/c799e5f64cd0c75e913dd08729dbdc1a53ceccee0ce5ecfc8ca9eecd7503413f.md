May is the [Asian Heritage Month](https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/campaigns/asian-heritage-month.html), an opportunity for Canadians to learn more about the many achievements and contributions of Canadians of Asian descent\.

UBC’s Rare Books and Special Collections \(RBSC\) has long been dedicated to preserving materials that document the history of Canada and its diverse communities\. A notable example of this work is the [Asian Canadian History and Archives guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/asian-canadian-archives), which brings together over 60 collections highlighting the experiences and contributions of Asian Canadians\. This curated resource offers valuable insight into the integral role Asian Canadians have played in shaping the nation’s historical and cultural landscape\.
