As the school year is starting, it is not uncommon for students to experience some back\-to\-school IT challenges\. The following blog lists the most common student IT questions\. Topics covered include:

– I can’t see my course in Canvas  
– I’m getting an error when logging in to my Student Email account  
– I’m getting a “username or password is incorrect” error when I log into my CWL  
– I receive an error that my email is invalid when opening the Zoom integration in Canvas  
– How do I sign up for Teams and OneDrive?  
– My Office 365 software stopped working

To obtain answers to these IT questions, check out [https://ubc\.service\-now\.com/kb\_view\.do?sysparm\_article\=KB0016964](https://ubc.service-now.com/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0016964)
