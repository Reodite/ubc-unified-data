The University of British Columbia’s Sustainability Scholars Program offers unique opportunity for full\-time UBC graduate students, regardless of their program, to contribute to sustainability impact\.

This opportunity is perfect for MBA students that are part of UBC Sauder’s new [MBA Climate Career Track](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/climate) or other MBA students that are interested to apply research skills to make impact on sustainability\. You will also gain valuable professional experience and earn while you learn, receive mentorship from experts in the field, and build a powerful professional network to boost your career prospects\.

Fall opportunities **close on Sept 17\[th\]** and more opportunities will be added throughout the year\.

See current opportunities [here](https://sustain.ubc.ca/teaching-applied-learning/sustainability-scholars-program/apply)\.

For those interested in past projects, you can explore [The Scholars Project Library](https://sustain.ubc.ca/teaching-applied-learning/sustainability-scholars-program/project-library)\. This houses hundreds of reports, charts, tool\-kits, and more, offering a valuable resource for further research in sustainability\.

More information can also be found on [this page](https://sustain.ubc.ca/teaching-applied-learning/ubc-sustainability-scholars-program)\.
