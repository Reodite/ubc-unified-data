Due to student demand, David Lam business librarians decided to add one more day of Zoom research help\. Our updated research help schedule consists of remote **Zoom drop\-ins** on Monday and Tuesday afternoons, from 1 to 4 pm\.

For Zoom Mondays and Tuesdays, join via browser in 4 easy steps:  
1\. Go to [zoom\.us/join](https://zoom.us/join)  
2\. Enter Meeting ID: 604\-822\-9400  
3\. Passcode: 164492  
4\. Click Join

Or you can just click on this [Link](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/6048229400?pwd=V3NVbVNobC9NSHltUk1ueTlvOFRhUT09) to access our Zoom drop in Research Help on Monday and Tuesday afternoons\.

**In\-person** drop\-in research help is still available on Wednesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays, in room CLC 220\. The hours remain unchanged, 1 to 4 pm\. This mixed approach will ensure that we offer afternoon research help using two different modes in order to reach students who have different schedules or who are taking some remote classes\.

Please feel free to drop in any time without an appointment during the stated hours\. We can help you with market research, company research, consumer research, citations, and more\. Drop by and speak with one of our friendly business librarians\.
