The Pink Shirt Day campaign recognizes the importance of unity, inclusivity, and diversity of all people\. For more information on the story of raising funds to support anti\-bullying programs, see the [official website](https://www.pinkshirtday.ca/about)\.

The Pink Shirt Day is on February 28, 2024 this year and everyone is encouraged to wear a pink shirt to support practice of kindness and stand against bullying in all its forms\. For those interested, [UBC Bookstore is selling pink shirt](https://the.bookstore.ubc.ca/Catalogue/ubc-bookstore/shop-new/pink-shirt-day-t-shirt) designed by Native Northwest Artist Francis Horne, Sr\., Coast Salish with some proceeds going to Native Northwest Reconciliation Fund\.

David Lam Library has mini poster displays of [**relevant eBooks**](https://lam-library-13mar2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/02/Pink-Shirt-Day-Display-E-Books-Posters.pdf) available inside the library, please check out posters and these three print books from the library:

Backstabbers and bullies : how to cope with the dark side of people at work by Adrian Furnham

The shield of silence : how power perpetuates a culture of harassment and bullying in the workplace by Lauren Stiller Rikleen

The asshole survival guide : how to deal with people who treat you like dirt by Robert I\. Sutton
