When people think of April, spring cherry blossoms and blooming tulips usually come to mind and… tax season\. Accounting is about so much more than just filing returns\. It’s the language of business, the foundation of personal finance, and occasionally, the center of some of history’s most gripping scandals\.

This month, we’re balancing the scales with our April Accounting Display\. Whether you’re a business student or just someone trying to make sense of your bank statement, we’ve got something for you\.

We invite everyone to visit the branch to explore our latest selection of featured titles, accounting resources, and financial management books at the library display \(links below\)\.
