Starting Tuesday, October 1, the loan period for UBC library DVD, VHS, and BluRay collections is being extended from 3 days to 7 days\. Please note that  the loan period for DVD, VHS, and BluRay collections in storage \(ASRS and PARC\) will remain at the current 14 days\.

Faculty members who use these formats in their courses can use the [media booking process](https://ubc.us2.list-manage.com/track/click?u=e16ca49d875b94fc549d40fa0&amp;id=093a4bc12f&amp;e=d3947d001d) to ensure items are available for specific classes or they may wish to place items on reserve to provide access for a class\.
