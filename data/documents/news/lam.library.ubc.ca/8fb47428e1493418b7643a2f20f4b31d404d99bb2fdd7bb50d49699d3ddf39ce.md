We have a new wall display called The Business of Pets\!

Send a photo of your pet and their name to us at **CLChelp@sauder\.ubc\.ca** by October 15 to be entered for a chance to win a gift card\.

Your pet picture will also be on display inside the David Lam Library, come visit us to see other pet pictures\.
