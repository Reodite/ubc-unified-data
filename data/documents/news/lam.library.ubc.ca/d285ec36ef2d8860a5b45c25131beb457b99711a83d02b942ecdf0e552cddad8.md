We invite you to join us for an international celebration of data during **Love Data Week**\.  UBC Library joins the celebration of [\#LoveDataWeek](https://twitter.com/hashtag/lovedataweek) along with many other universities across Canada and throughout the world\. Join us for a new annual tradition dedicated to spreading the awareness and importance of research data through a series of workshops held online between February 1st and 12th, 2021\. Registration is free and open to everyone\.

Some of the topics covered include designing dashboards with Tableau, as well as introduction to tools such as Voyant \(a free suite of visual tools for text analysis\), software such as R and RStudio, Github, NVivo, and more\.

Please check out the following Research Commons [link](https://researchcommons.library.ubc.ca/2021/01/18/love-data-week-2021/?login) in order to register for the workshops\.
