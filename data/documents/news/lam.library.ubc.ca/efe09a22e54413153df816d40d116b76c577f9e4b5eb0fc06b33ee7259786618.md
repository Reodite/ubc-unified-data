UBC Library is offering information sessions on copyright guidelines for UBC faculty, instructors and researchers\.  The aim of these sessions is to provide participants with:

-   an overview of copyright and fair dealing provisions
-   an understanding of use permissions for copyrighted materials and licensed material for classroom, research and instructional use
-   an understanding of author’s scholarly rights and publisher agreements

Interested faculty, instructors and researchers can register for one of the following sessions:

-   Wednesday, August 24th, 2011 at 2:30PM – 4:00PM: [Irving K Barber Learning Centre – Fraser River Room 2\.27](http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?show=y,n,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=n&amp;locat1=516), Registration via CTLT: [http://www\.events\.ctlt\.ubc\.ca/events/view/1334](http://www.events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/view/1334) \(this workshop is part of CTLT’s *New Faculty Orientation*\)
-   Wednesday, September 14th, 2011 at 11:00AM – 12:30PM RM 302\-Dodson Room:  Registration: [http://elred\.library\.ubc\.ca/libs/dashboard/view/2363](http://elred.library.ubc.ca/libs/dashboard/view/2363)
-   Friday, September 16th, 2011 at 10:00AM – 11:30AM: RM 302\-Dodson Room, IKBLC:  Registration: [http://elred\.library\.ubc\.ca/libs/dashboard/view/2362](http://elred.library.ubc.ca/libs/dashboard/view/2362)

For more information on copyright and UBC, please visit the University’s [copyright website](http://copyright.ubc.ca/?action=login&amp;dm=b6eb8c9d941339a1ea180d53b7bb73ed&amp;k=341b6bb520756e09d18f2528f92e5a56&amp;t=1802741587)\.
