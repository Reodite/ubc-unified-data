To celebrate [Thrive Week](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/news/2015/10/29/thrive-week-events/), David Lam Library is putting on a display of books about work\-life balance, dealing with stress, and building positive mental health\.

Drop by and grab some Thrive swag such as stress balls or bracelets from the CLC front desk\. Be sure to also check out campus [events](http://thrive.ubc.ca/thrive-events/?login) during Thrive Week, from November 2–6, 2015\.
