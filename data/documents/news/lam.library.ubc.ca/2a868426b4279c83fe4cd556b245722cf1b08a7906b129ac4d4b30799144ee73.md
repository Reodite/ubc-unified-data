UBC Library has recently subscribed to several new resources\. They include:

[Sage Skills: Business](https://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?details=sage-skills&amp;id=2697): Similar to LinkedIn Learning, this interactive digital resource features both academic and practitioner experts in a library of flexible learning tracks across five modules: Data Analytics, Entrepreneurship, Leadership, Organizational Communication, and Professionalism\. This resource empowers students to develop and practice real\-world skills needed to successfully transition from campus to the modern workplace\.

[Vancouver Sun newspaper digital archives](https://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?details=vancouver-sun&amp;id=2598): UBC faculty, staff and students can now access the digital archives of the *Vancouver Sun* newspaper from 1912 to 2010\. The *Vancouver Sun* digital archives are available remotely to all UBC Library users with a CWL through [Index and Databases](https://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?details=vancouver-sun&amp;id=2598) and the [library catalogue](https://search.library.ubc.ca/)\. This is the second notable addition in the last 12 months to UBC Library’s collection of current and [historical British Columbian newspapers](https://open.library.ubc.ca/collections/bcnewspapers)\. In June 2021, the library acquired access to [*The Province* newspaper digital archives](https://about.library.ubc.ca/2021/06/08/ubc-library-has-acquired-access-to-the-digital-archives-of-the-province-newspaper/) \(1894 to 2010\), a valuable new source for scholarship and teaching\. These papers are part of the [ProQuest Historical Newspapers](https://go.proquest.com/BCHistoricalNewspapers) collection\.
