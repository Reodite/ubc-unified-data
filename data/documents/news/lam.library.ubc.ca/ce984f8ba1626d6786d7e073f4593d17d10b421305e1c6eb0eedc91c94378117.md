As part of the Harry Potter exhibit, the Canaccord Learning Commons staff asked Sauder students to identify major themes from Harry Potter books\. The David Lam Library will continue to create displays over the coming weeks based on the themes identified\.  We have just created a new display on the theme of LEADERSHIP\.
