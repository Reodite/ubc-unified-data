We often get asked about Alumni Access to UBC Library Electronic Resources\. Whether you are a UBC alum or a current student wondering about your future library resources access, here is some useful information:

UBC Alumni with an authorized [Campus\-Wide Login](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl) \(CWL\) and UBC Alumni A\-Card can get remote access to many of UBC Library’s electronic resources\.  You can sign up for a UBC Alumni A\-Card [in\-person](https://www.alumni.ubc.ca/services/savings/#alumnicard) or [online](https://www.alumni.ubc.ca/services/savings/a-card-signup/)\.

For UBC Sauder alumni, two of the most helpful resources they can access remotely include [EBSCO Academic Source and Business Source alumni editions](https://www.alumni.ubc.ca/journals/) which have more than 4,150 full\-text journals from many of the top academic and business publishers worldwide\. For the ever\-growing list of other library electronic resources you can access from off campus, check out this [page](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/alumniresources?utm_source=Library+Update+List&amp;utm_campaign=b86315bcc4-LU_May16&amp;utm_medium=email&amp;utm_term=0_e262695394-b86315bcc4-335993717)\.

Due to licensing terms, UBC Library is not able to provide alumni with ***remote*** *access* to all the online resources available to currently enrolled students\.  Alumni can access these resources physically on campus at a public computer at David Lam Library or any UBC [library branch](http://hours.library.ubc.ca/)\.

The Library offers a [range of services](https://services.library.ubc.ca/user-guides/alumni/) to UBC Alumni, including access to print and video collections, attractive spaces and assistance using our resources\. We encourage you to visit us often and stay connected\.
