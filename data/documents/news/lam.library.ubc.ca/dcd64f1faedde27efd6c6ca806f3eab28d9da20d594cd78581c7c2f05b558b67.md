October 19 to 25, 2020 is Open Access week at UBC\. Join UBC Vancouver Library, UBC Okanagan Library, the Centre for Teaching, Learning, and Technology \(Vancouver\), and the Centre for Teaching and Learning \(Okanagan\) for a week of sessions on various topics related to open access to research and education\.

Learn about topics such as copyright, creative commons, finding, using and adopting open resources, open research data and more\.

For more information, explore Open Access week [events calendar](https://ubc.us5.list-manage.com/track/click?u=e1971ea25e9795a7f96774ae7&amp;id=4cc867a9b0&amp;e=c68a63570b)
