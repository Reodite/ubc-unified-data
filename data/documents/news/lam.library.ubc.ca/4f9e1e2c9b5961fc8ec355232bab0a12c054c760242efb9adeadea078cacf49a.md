The Social Science Research Network \(SSRN\.com\) disseminates scholarly papers in the social sciences and provides an abstracting service which sends out announcements of new papers, typically long before they are published\. SSRN\.com is a premier venue for business faculty research, and contains many working papers published at the Sauder School of Business\.

One database subscribed to at UBC – Proquest’s [ABI/Inform](http://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?details=abiinform&amp;id=2) – provides detailed indexing of working papers from SSRN\.com, permitting browsing and precise searching by business school, topic, subject term and keyword\.
