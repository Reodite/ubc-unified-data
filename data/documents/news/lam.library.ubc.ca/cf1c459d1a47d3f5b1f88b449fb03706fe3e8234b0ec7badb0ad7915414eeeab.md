Are you looking to do a deep dive into the fascinating world of Accounting? Check out some of the books we have on display this month\. Some of them were written by Sauder’s famed Professor Emeritus, [Richard Mattessich](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/people/richard-mattessich-memoriam)\.

Some of our titles include:

\-Two hundred years of accounting research : an international survey of personalities, ideas and publications \(from the beginning of the nineteenth century to the beginning of the twenty\-first century\)  
\-Reality and accounting : ontological explorations in the economic and social sciences  
\-The beginnings of accounting and accounting thought : accounting practicein the Middle East \(8000 B\.C\. to 2000 B\.C\.\) and accounting thought in India\(300 B\.C\. and the Middle Ages\)  
\-Called to account : fourteen financial frauds that shaped the American Accounting profession  
\-Understanding accounting ethics  
\-Chief value officer : accountants can save the planet  
\-Information systems : for accountants
