This year, Small Business Week is October 20\-26, 2024\. In honour of this event, David Lam Management Research Library has put together a book display focused on entrepreneurship and small business\. Some of our titles on display include:

• **Small business : issues, programs and investments** / John D\. Mijovic, editor  
o https://resolve\.library\.ubc\.ca/cgi\-bin/catsearch?bid\=10078994  
• **Company of one : why staying small is the next big thing for business** / Paul Jarvis  
o https://resolve\.library\.ubc\.ca/cgi\-bin/catsearch?bid\=11902709  
• **Shark tank : jump start your business : how to grow a business from concept to cash** / Michael Parrish DuDell ; with contributions by Mark Cuban, Barbara Corcoran, Lori Greiner, Robert Herjavec, Daymond John, and Kevin O’Leary  
o https://resolve\.library\.ubc\.ca/cgi\-bin/catsearch?bid\=7050044  
• **International handbook of research on Indigenous Entrepreneurship** / edited by Léo\-Paul Dana, Robert B\. Anderson\.  
o https://resolve\.library\.ubc\.ca/cgi\-bin/catsearch?bid\=3801903  
• **Innovation in small family businesses** / Sylvie Laforet  
o https://resolve\.library\.ubc\.ca/cgi\-bin/catsearch?bid\=6415845  
• **Indigenomics: taking a seat at the economic table** / Carol Anne Hilton, MBA  
o https://resolve\.library\.ubc\.ca/cgi\-bin/catsearch?bid\=11752318

For more information about Small Business Week, check out [BDC Small Business Week – October 20 to 26, 2024 \| BDC\.ca](https://www.bdc.ca/en/small-business-week/) or [GC Business Insights \| Get Ready for Small Business Week 2024 \(canada\.ca\)](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/newsletter-gc-business-insights/gc-business-insights-get-ready-small-business-week-2024)
