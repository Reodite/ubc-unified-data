October is Small Business Month\. Celebrate [\#SmallBusinessMonth](https://twitter.com/hashtag/SmallBusinessMonth?src=hashtag_click) with DLAM and the [@sba\_bc](https://twitter.com/sba_bc)  Discover recent developments in Indigenous and Black entrepreneurship and programs aspiring entrepreneurs in these communities by visiting the SBA blog, and check out the book displays at UBC Library\. [http://bit\.ly/SME\-2022](https://t.co/X0u3TNkHjY)

David Lam Library is hosting a book display focused on celebrating entrepreneurial diversity until October 26, 2022\. Come check out our display and borrow a book to learn more about diversity in entrepreneurship\. Some titles in our display include: Underdog Entrepreneurs: A Framework of Success for Marginalized and Minority Innovators, Business Ownership Among Persons with Disabilities in Canada, International Handbook of Research on Indigenous Entrepreneurship, and Black Women in Leadership: Their Historical and Contemporary Contributions\.
