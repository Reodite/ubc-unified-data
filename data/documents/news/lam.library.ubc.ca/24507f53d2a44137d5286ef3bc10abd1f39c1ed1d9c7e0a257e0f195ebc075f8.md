Join us for our second annual Academic Integrity Week, October 17\-21, as we work to promote academic integrity at UBC and beyond\. Academic Integrity Week will feature a range of events such as workshops, presentations, and an AMS information booth aimed at sparking conversation and raising awareness of the importance of academic integrity\. Academic Integrity Week coincides with the annual [International Day of Action Against Contract Cheating](https://academicintegrity.org/events-conferences/idoa-international-day-of-action-against-contract-cheating) on October 19 organized by the International Center for Academic Integrity\.

Some of the events include: Indigenous Academic Integrity: Paradigms into Practice, Alternative Approaches to Academic Integrity Responses Panel, Writing with Integrity: Citing Like an Insider, and Teaching with Integrity: methods and strategies for fostering academic integrity in your classroom or laboratory\. Events are offered either in person or online\. Check out the schedule of events [here](https://academicintegrity.ubc.ca/academic-integrity-week/?login) and be sure to register in advance\.
