## [UBC Community Leadership Program: Spotlight on Sunny Zhang](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/2016/03/16/ubc-community-leadership-program-spotlight-on-sunny-zhang/)

By [irena trebic](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/author/irena-trebic/ "Posts by irena trebic") on March 16, 2016

Sunny Zhang, the Wayne Deans Investment Analysis Centre Co\-ordinator participated in UBC Community Leadership Program \(CLP\) this year\.  CLP is UBC’s leadership development opportunity for aspiring staff leaders\. By participating in CLP, UBC employees have the opportunity to explore leadership concepts by leading a group of UBC undergraduate students through a three\-day community\-service learning project \[…\]
