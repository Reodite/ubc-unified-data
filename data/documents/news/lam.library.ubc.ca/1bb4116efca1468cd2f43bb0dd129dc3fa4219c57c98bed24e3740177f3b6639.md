[UBC Library](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/ill/#InterLibraryLoanRequests-0) is transitioning from Relais to Tipasa, an upgraded version of the WorldShare interlibrary loan \(ILL\) system\. Relais is being retired by the vendor, and we will move to Tipasa to improve [ILL](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/ill/) and [DocDel](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/ill/) workflows\. 

Tipasa will provide a more seamless experience for faculty, staff, students and community users\. We expect most UBC users to begin using Tipasa in June 2026, with additional configurations coming by the Fall\. Relais will be decommissioned by the vendor at a mutually agreed upon date\.

More information and updates, including upcoming training opportunities, will be shared soon\.

If you have immediate inquiries, please feel free to reach out to [Arielle Lomness](mailto:arielle.lomness@ubc.ca)\.
