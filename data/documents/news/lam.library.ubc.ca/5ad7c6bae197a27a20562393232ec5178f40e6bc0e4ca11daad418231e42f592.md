Three new transportation\-related research guides have been added to the [David Lam Library website](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/)\. David Lam librarians designed these guides to provide researchers and students with credible starting points for researching transportation related topics\.

The guides identify relevant books and media, article sources, databases, industry associations, government agencies, and scholarly resources\.  They also include tips on effective search strategies for transportation topics, by using appropriate search terms and subject headings\.

-   [Transportation: Air](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/air)
-   [Transportation: Land](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/transportation_by_land)
-   [Transportation: Marine](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/transportation_marine)

Click on individual guides for access, or visit the David Lam Library homepage \([http://www\.library\.ubc\.ca/lam](http://www.library.ubc.ca/lam)\) and find them by clicking on the Research Guides link\.

If you have questions or feedback about the guide, please contact the [David Lam Library\.](http://www.library.ubc.ca/lam/contactus.htm)
