February is Black History Month\. While we should celebrate Black history and excellence every day, this is an opportunity for a focused, intentional and elevated reflection and recognition of Black history and achievement\.

There are a lot of events at UBC this month celebrating Black History Month\. Learn about Black Lives Matter in Italy and Germany, join a heritage walking tour, or attend Kalabanté: Afrique en Cirque at the Chan Centre, and more\.

UBC has many community groups such as UBC Black Students Union, UBC Black Caucus, UBCO African Caribbean Student Club, and others\.
