We are pleased to announce that the December book display is now ready\. We warmly invite everyone to visit the branch and explore the latest selection of titles\.

This display was curated with a creative approach – judging by the cover, but rest assured, the content is equally compelling\. The collection spans a wide range of topics, from data culture to successful cannabis startup, political history of cigarettes, aviation, media, queer politics, and more\.
