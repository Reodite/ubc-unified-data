Librarians at the [David Lam Management Research Library](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/) have developed and published a new [Research Guide on the Arts, Media and Entertainment Industries](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/arts_media__entertainment)\.  This guide is of interest to researchers conducting business or market planning in the cultural industries, as well as those exploring this sector for career research\.
