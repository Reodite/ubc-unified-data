UBC Library is hosting a Harry Potter themed **Hallowe’en party** on October 30th in the Dodson Room of the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre\. Dress up as your favorite Potter character\. There will be a costume contest, games, crafts and light refreshments\.

The party is free, but tickets are required\. Beginning on October 15th you can pick up your ticket at Rare Books and Special Collections \(Level One, IKBLC\)\. Tickets will be available on a first\-come, first\-served basis at RBSC …Monday to Friday between 10 a\.m\. and 4 p\.m\.

Hallowe’en at Hogwarts West  
October 30 \| 1 – 3 p\.m\.  
Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre \(IKBLC\), 1961 East Mall  
Rm 302 \(Dodson Room\)
