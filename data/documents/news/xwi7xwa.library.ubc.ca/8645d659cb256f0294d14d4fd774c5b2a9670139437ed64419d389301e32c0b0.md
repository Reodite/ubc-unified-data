## **Access Xwi7xwa Resources during closure: updated Feb\. 7**

Looking for access to our print collections? Although we are closed during construction you can still order our resources from our branch using the Document Delivery system\.

**Step 1\.** Locate the item you would like to take out on loan:

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/08/Step1-300x211.png)

**Step 2\.** Under the “Action” column \(right side\) select the option “Order via Document Delivery”:

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/08/Step2-300x211.png)

**Step 3\.** Sign in using your [Campus Wide Login \(CWL\)](https://activate.id.ubc.ca/iamweb/) or Library Card, authenticate using [Duo Push account](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/learn-about-enhancedcwl) \(if these are new to you please click the hyperlinks\), sign in as either student/employee\.

**Step 4\.** Select the holding and “Create Loan Request”:

\(*NOTE: vertical files &amp; special collections do not circulate; see Special Collections below to access these resources*\)

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/08/Screen-Shot-2021-10-23-at-9.44.31-AM-300x227.png)

**Step 5\.** Enter your name, contact information, and under Location select desired UBC Library location for pick\-up, and click Submit\.

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/08/Screen-Shot-2021-10-23-at-9.55.48-AM-300x168.png)

Help, questions, concerns contact: [access\.xwi@ubc\.ca](mailto:access.xwi@ubc.ca)

## **Special Collections \(SP\) &amp; Vertical Files \(VF\) items:**

SP &amp; VF items cannot circulate but you can view them in the Elder’s Lounge of **[1985 West Mall, First Nations House of Learning Longhouse](https://www.google.com/maps/place/First+Nations+Longhouse/@49.2654542,-123.2565407,15z/data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x0:0xb2df99c77da02ca5?sa=X&amp;ved=2ahUKEwjWzfffgOHzAhX5IjQIHYOGB14Q_BJ6BAgaEAU)** Monday\-Friday 11AM\-3PM\. If these hours do not work please contact us [access\.xwi@ubc\.ca](mailto:access.xwi@ubc.ca) \.

While the branch is closed patrons can access resources by:

**Step 1\.** Locate the item you are interested in, collect as much information about the item as possible \(title, author, publication date\) OR simply collect the item’s permalink:

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/08/Screen-Shot-2021-10-23-at-10.05.50-AM-300x165.png)

1.  the item’s information or permalink
2.  your name, student number, or library barcode

**Step 2\.** Email [access\.xwi@ubc\.ca](mailto:access.xwi@ubc.ca) with the above information\.

Once we have received your request we will send a confirmation email and when the item will be available to you\.

Help, questions, concerns contact: [access\.xwi@ubc\.ca](mailto:access.xwi@ubc.ca)

October 14, 2020 – Update

**Materials Pick\-Up Service, a first\-hand experience with Librarian Karleen\!** 

Need access to print resources? So do we, even the librarians are using UBC Library’s pick\-up service\! To place an order you will need to know your [Campus Wide Login \(CWL\)](https://activate.id.ubc.ca/iamweb/) and then a [Duo Push account](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/learn-about-enhancedcwl) \(if these are new to you please click the hyperlinks\), then follow these steps to [place an order\.](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/get-it/)

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/10/Mask-225x300.jpg)

Once you’ve placed an order online you’ll receive a confirmation email with:  a request number \(we recommend writing this down in case there’s an issue with your order\) and the dates your order is available for pick up\!

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/10/Koerner-300x225.jpg)

**Remember to bring:** 

-   a mask, they’re mandatory at UBC campuses
-   your student/staff/faculty ID

**Location:** Resource pick\-up is at the [Koerner Library\!](https://www.google.com/maps?q=koerner+library&amp;rlz=1C1CHBD_enCA824CA824&amp;um=1&amp;ie=UTF-8&amp;sa=X&amp;ved=2ahUKEwi62r-F_LTsAhXWup4KHb-QB0AQ_AUoAXoECAgQAw)

**Hours:** See [Koerner’s hours here](https://hours.library.ubc.ca/#view-koerner)\.

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/10/Procedure-225x300.jpg)

**Procedure:**

1.  When you arrive at Koerner use the blue circles on the ground to line up 2 metres apart
2.  At the front door there’s a table \(ring the bell if no one is there\), allow the patron in front of you time to leave the table and the staff time to clean it
3.  Wait for the staff to move back, put your ID on the table, step back
4.  The staff will go get your order and place it on the table
5.  Grab your resources and you are good to go
