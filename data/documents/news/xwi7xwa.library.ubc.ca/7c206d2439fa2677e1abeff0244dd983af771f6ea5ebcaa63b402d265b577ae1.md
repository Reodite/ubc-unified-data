From October 14 to November 4 Xwi7xwa Library \(Karleen Delaurier\-Lyle\) will be hosting a series of presentations and workshops offered both virtually and in person \(FNHL Longhouse Boardroom – [1985 West Mall](https://www.google.com/maps/place/First+Nations+Longhouse/@49.2654542,-123.2565407,15z/data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x0:0xb2df99c77da02ca5?sa=X&amp;ved=2ahUKEwja196Gptn6AhUjPH0KHY8eAw0Q_BJ6BQiHARAF)\)\.

-   October 14 \(1\-2:30PM\): Exploring Resources
    -   Does your assignment require **peer\-reviewed sources** but you aren’t sure what that means? Are you unsure of the **differences between primary &amp; secondary sources**? This session covers the basics to answer these questions and the essential differences between books, journal articles, and more\! [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/j/61989143279?pwd\=VHRySlZmQzJLSDFoZXBmNC95aHN0dz09](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/61989143279?pwd=VHRySlZmQzJLSDFoZXBmNC95aHN0dz09) 
-   October 21 \(1\-2:30PM\): Finding Resources
    -   Learn how to search Summon, UBC Library catalogue, Xwi7xwa specific resources, and how to find and search relevant databases\.
-   November 4 \(3\-4:30PM\): Indigenous Research Methods
    -   How to find Indigenous Research Methods &amp; create your own facilitated with student librarian Taya Jardine using Kathleen Absolon’s flower methodology \(see: [http://resolve\.library\.ubc\.ca/cgi\-bin/catsearch?bid\=5729072](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=5729072)\)
-   RESCHEDULED: November 10 \(1\-2:30PM\): Citation
    -   Citation basics \(MLA, APA, Chicago\) &amp; citation management tools available to you through UBC Library\.
