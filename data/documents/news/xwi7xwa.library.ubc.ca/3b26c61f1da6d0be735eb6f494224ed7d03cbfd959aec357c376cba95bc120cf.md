## Head of X̱wi7x̱wa Library \(1996\-2017\)

### [Ann Doyle](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/04/Ann-Doyle.jpg)

Ann Doyle grew X̱wi7x̱wa Library from one Librarian with student support, to three Librarians and two Library Technicians\. She oversaw the process of X̱wi7x̱wa becoming a UBC Branch Library in 2005\. She also supported the FNHL – UBC Library Agreement ratified in 2009 and developed the ICIP Report which looked at Indigenous Cultural and Intellectual Property rights here at UBC\.
