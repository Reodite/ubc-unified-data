## Roots for Indigenous Research Book Club

Introducing the Roots Book Club\! Join the Roots team members, Sophie and Aria, for a two\-session virtual book club as they explore the themes of history, identity, and relationship in Patty Krawec’s [*Becoming Kin: An Indigenous Call to Unforgetting the Past and Reimagining Our Future*](https://go.exlibris.link/k95x18Vk)\. Whether you’re new to the book or eager to revisit it with others, this is a welcoming space for meaningful conversation and shared learning\.

**Relation Building Lunch:** Wednesday, January 21

-   When: 12:00 pm – 2:30 pm
-   Where: First Nations Longhouse: East Wing space
-   [More info and register here\!](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/roots-relation-building-lunch-tickets-1977673943001?aff=oddtdtcreator)

**Roots Book Club – *Becoming Kin* by Patty Krawec:** **Thursday, February 19 &amp; Thursday, March 19**

-   **When:** 1:00 pm to 2:30 pm, both sessions
-   **Where:** Free online via Zoom
-   [More info and register here\!](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/roots-cluster-becoming-kin-book-club-tickets-1977518475995?aff=oddtdtcreator)

Roots is a decolonized space, rooted in ceremony, that builds capacity at UBC for researchers to engage in meaningful, appropriate, and relational partnerships with Indigenous communities, collectives, and organizations\.

Learn more about Roots by visiting their website: [https://roots\.ubc\.ca/](https://roots.ubc.ca/)

For questions about this event or how to get involved, email: [aria\.viveiros@ubc\.ca](mailto:aria.viveiros@ubc.ca) or [sophie\.carriere@ubc\.ca](mailto:sophie.carriere@ubc.ca)
