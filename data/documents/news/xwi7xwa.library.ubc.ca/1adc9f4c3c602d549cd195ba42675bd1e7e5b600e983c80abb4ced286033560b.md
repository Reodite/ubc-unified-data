**About Science Literacy Week**

Led by the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada \(NSERC\), Science Literacy Week showcases the many ways kids and families can explore and enjoy the diversity of Canadian science\. From September 19 to 25, 2022, libraries, museums, science centres, schools and not\-for\-profits are coming together to celebrate this year’s theme, *Mathematics*\. They are highlighting the books, movies, podcasts and virtual and in\-person events that share exciting stories of the science, discoveries and ingenuity shaping our lives\. Feed your curiosity and explore science from across the country and within your region\.

[**Science Literacy at UBC Libraries\!**](https://about.library.ubc.ca/2022/09/16/science-literacy-week-2022/)

Here’s a selection of what’s on offer from UBC Library:

-   Math Reads, booklists curated by various UBC and UBCO librarians
-   Film recommendations
-   Math activities including paper geometry games
-   Math support resources at UBC, and more\!

For more details about all the upcoming activities, please visit: [UBC Library Guide to Science Literacy Week](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/scilit22)\.

Consult the [**full list**](http://www.scienceliteracy.ca/events/) of Science Literacy Week activities\.

Xwi7xwa Library participates in this annual event alongside other branches of UBC Library\! To see selected resources from our branches about this year’s theme: Mathematics see our [guide here](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/c.php?g=731386&amp;p=5253161)\.
