## Rebuilding Relations Gathering

We are pleased to share that the applications are now open for the Rebuilding Relations Gathering\.

Rebuilding Relations is a novel, art and storytelling\-based gathering that aims to invite 15\-20 Indigenous participants to learn about the concepts of Indigenous data sovereignty and governance and rebuild relationships with Indigenous data housed at X̱wi7x̱wa Library\.

The Gathering is open to Indigenous participants – students, faculty and community members – who are curious about Indigenous data sovereignty and willing to contribute their time and energy to create a supportive learning environment\.

The format is conversational and participant driven\. The gathering will take place within a talking circle, led by an Indigenous facilitator\. The focus will be on discussion and shared experience within the circle rather than on formal presentations\.

The Gathering will also include the co\-creation of artwork and stories, developed through participants’ contributions over the course of the day\.

**Rebuilding Relations Gathering:** Saturday, 17 October 2026

-   When: 9:30 AM – 5:00 PM \(lunch and tea/coffee will be provided\)\.
-   Where: TBD, on UBC Vancouver campus\.
-   A modest monetary gift of appreciation will be offered for participants time\.
-   [More info and participant application here\!](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_ekRC5C0ZWtcj9oq)

The Gathering is guided by Dr\. Warren Cardinal\-McTeague \(Assistant Professor, Department of Forest and Conservation Sciences\), Kayla Lar\-Son \(Head of X̱wi7x̱wa Library\) and Daphne Boyer \(Visual artist and plant scientist; [https://www\.daphneboyer\.com/](https://www.daphneboyer.com/)\)\.

For questions about the Gathering, email: [kayla\.lar\-son@ubc\.ca](mailto:kayla.lar-son@ubc.ca), [warren\.cardinal\-mcteague@ubc\.ca](mailto:warren.cardinal-mcteague@ubc.ca) or [caethier@student\.ubc\.ca](mailto:caethier@student.ubc.ca)\.
