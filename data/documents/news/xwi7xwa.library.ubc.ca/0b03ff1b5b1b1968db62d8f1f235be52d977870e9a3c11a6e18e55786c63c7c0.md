**Did you know that you can watch X̱wi7x̱wa’s playlist of videos online?**

UBC Library uses a platform called McIntyre Media to stream videos we’ve subscribed to\.

1.  Search the phrase “Mcintyre media” in [simple search](http://webcat2.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/searchBasic) and filter the results to *Online* OR click this [link](http://webcat2.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/search?searchArg=%22Mcintyre+media%22&amp;searchCode=CMD&amp;setLimit=15&amp;recCount=100&amp;searchType=1&amp;page.search.search.button=Search)\.
2.  Select any of the titles on the list to see their record\.
3.  Under *Actions* on the right hand side click *Online* to gain access to 165 titles

Not sure what to watch? Check out these selected titles\!

1.  *Indigo* by Amanda Strong

[http://www\.vtape\.org/video?vi\=8296&amp;\_wpnonce\=dd4c1f0ed7](http://www.vtape.org/video?vi=8296&amp;_wpnonce=dd4c1f0ed7)[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/04/Indigo-300x169.jpg)

*Indigo* tells the story of a woman who confronts her internal war with the help of grandmother spider and faces the many layers of herself and life, to revitalize her spirit\. *Indigo e*xamines the implications of the decline of the imagination concurrent with the rise of rationality and the cyclical war these two archetypes engage in\.

2\. *Butterfly Monument* by Jules Koostachin and Rick Miller

[http://www\.vtape\.org/video?vi\=8754&amp;\_wpnonce\=dd4c1f0ed7](http://www.vtape.org/video?vi=8754&amp;_wpnonce=dd4c1f0ed7)[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/04/Butterfly-300x181.jpg)

*Butterfly Monument* documents the creation a public memorial dedicated to the late Shannen Koostachin, a young Cree education advocate from Attawapiskat First Nation, Moskekowok territory\. Through personal stories shared by Shannen’s immediate family we learn about who Shannen was and what motivated her passionate crusade for equitable access to education for Indigenous children and youth\.

3\. *Crossed Mask* by Christiana Latham

[http://www\.vtape\.org/video?vi\=6786&amp;\_wpnonce\=dd4c1f0ed7](http://www.vtape.org/video?vi=6786&amp;_wpnonce=dd4c1f0ed7)[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/04/Crossed-300x214.jpg)

This work is based on questions about nicknames, what they were and why they were\. It is an analysis of the labels and nicknames used within and in relation to my family\.

4\. *He Who Dreams* by Dana Claxton

[http://www\.vtape\.org/video?vi\=8001&amp;\_wpnonce\=dd4c1f0ed7](http://www.vtape.org/video?vi=8001&amp;_wpnonce=dd4c1f0ed7)[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/04/Dream-300x157.jpg)

A supernatural spiritual art film about a man who is in the dreamworld and the real world\. The dreamworld and real worlds can be very similar\.

5\. *The Oldest Tree in the World* by Cara Mumford and Leanne Simpson

[http://www\.vtape\.org/video?vi\=8727&amp;\_wpnonce\=dd4c1f0ed7](http://www.vtape.org/video?vi=8727&amp;_wpnonce=dd4c1f0ed7)[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/04/OldestTree-300x225.jpg)

*The Oldest Tree in the World* is a love song to the oldest sugar maple in the region, living just outside of Peterborough in Mark S\. Burnham Provincial Park\. This grandmother tree, one of our oldest living relations, has witnessed over 500 years of history\.

6\. *Stolen* by Kawennáhere Devery Jacobs and Prospector Films

[http://www\.vtape\.org/video?vi\=8639&amp;\_wpnonce\=dd4c1f0ed7](http://www.vtape.org/video?vi=8639&amp;_wpnonce=dd4c1f0ed7)[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/04/Stolen-300x200.jpg)

14 year old Shayna runs away from her group\-home and into the truck of a dangerous man\. As a result, she becomes one of Canada’s 1200\+ Missing and Murdered Indigenous women\.

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/02/UBCED-300x300.jpg)
