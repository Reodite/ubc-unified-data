# What is the Winter Solstice?

The Winter Solstice signals the start of winter and is the year’s shortest day and longest night\. It recognizes seasonal cycles and is a time of celebration for many Indigenous Peoples\. Traditionally, time is taken to slow down, reflect, practice spiritual care, and prepare for the longer days ahead\. This is because activities like harvesting and gathering are over for the season\. Celebrations often include community gatherings, ceremonies, sharing food harvested over the year, and telling stories\!

# Why Storytelling?

During this longer period of darkness and rest, the Winter Solstice provides a time for people to gather and tell stories\. Traditionally, certain stories are exclusively shared during the winter months\. Cultural knowledge and teachings are shared through the practice of storytelling and are passed from one generation to the next\. Storytelling is also a great entertainment when staying indoors longer during the winter months\!
