## [Indigenous Land Based Activism Research Guide](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/2020/05/26/land-based-activism/)

By [Bronte Burnette](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/author/bronte-burnette/ "Posts by Bronte Burnette") on May 26, 2020

This research guide is intended for students, faculty, and researchers to use and locate resources to help their understanding of the complexities surrounding Indigenous spatial and land based activism\. It focuses specifically on strategies for researching contemporary Indigenous struggles over spatial justice\.
