Xwi7xwa Library is undergoing space improvements as of Thursday, June 4, 2026\. The branch is closed to the public during construction\.

Library users can continue to access Xwi7xwa Library collections during this time by ordering items through [UBC Library Document Delivery \(UBC DocDel\)](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/docdel/?login), free of charge\. For detailed instructions on how to use UBC DocDel, [visit our website](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/docdel/#PlaceaUBCDocDelorder-3)\.

For updates about when Xwi7xwa Library fully reopens see [regular branch hours](https://hours.library.ubc.ca/#view-xwi7xwa)\.

Please refer to the [Library Hours and Locations](https://hours.library.ubc.ca/) to find additional study spaces across campus\.
