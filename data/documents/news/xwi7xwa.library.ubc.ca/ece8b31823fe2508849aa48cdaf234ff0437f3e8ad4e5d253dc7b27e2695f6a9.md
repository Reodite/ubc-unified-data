Xwi7xwa Library has a wide range of [carefully curated library research guides](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/xwi7xwaresearchguide) \(also known as libguides\), covering everything from Aboriginal Filmmakers to Indigenous Education K\-12\. These guides are particularly helpful places to start when looking for information about a specific disciple or subject area\. Visit the portal to see all Xwi7xwa Library authored libguides\.
