### What’s Going on? Indigenous Strategic Plan 2020

Get caught up with the [Centre for Teaching and Learning Technology’s Overview of the ISP](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/2020/11/24/edubytes-the-indigenous-strategic-plan-overview/?login)\!

On Monday, September 14  Xwi7xwa’s team attended the celebration and launch of UBC’s Indigenous Strategic Plan \(ISP\); find out more about this virtual event [here](https://indigenous.ubc.ca/indigenous-engagement/indigenous-strategic-plan/isp-celebration-event/)\.

###### [Image](https://aboriginal-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/39931813854_f8ddb22c88_c-360x270.jpg)

###### Reconciliation Pole 7idansuu \(Edenshaw\) James Hart, Haida UBC Vancouver

The following resources are for people interested in learning more about the ISP, relevant contextual information, and connections between both Vancouver/Okanagan campuses regarding the ISP:

1.  [Indigenous Portal’s ISP home page](https://indigenous.ubc.ca/indigenous-engagement/indigenous-strategic-plan/):
    -   For background information, timelines, FAQ related to ISP process\.
2.  [ISP History](https://indigenous.ubc.ca/indigenous-engagement/indigenous-strategic-plan/aboriginal-strategic-plan/history/)
    -   Helpful for understanding what’s happened from 2007\-2020\.
3.  [UBC Okanagan’s Commitments to TRC Calls to Action](https://bog3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/06/6.2_2019.05_UBCO-TRC-Calls-to-Action-Response.pdf):
    -   This is a major component of the ISP because it builds on UBCO’s Commitments to the TRC Calls to Action\.
4.  [Signing of UBCO’s Commitments  to TRC Calls to Action](https://news.ok.ubc.ca/2019/09/25/the-time-to-act-is-now/):
    -   UBCO article on signing of UBCO’s commitments toward TRC\.
    -   Elders, chiefs and community members from throughout the Syilx Okanagan Nation joined with students, faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan to witness this event\.

The ISP will cover both UBC campuses:

-   UBC\-Okanagan is on the unceded, traditional, and ancestral territory of the [Syilx](https://www.syilx.org/about-us/syilx-nation/) People\.
-   UBC\-Vancouver is on the unceded, traditional, and anestral territory of the [xʷməθkʷəy̓əm](https://www.musqueam.bc.ca/) People\.
-   Each community has signed either an MOU or MOA, you can find more information [here](https://indigenous.ubc.ca/indigenous-engagement/affiliations/)\.

If you want to know more and the above resources haven’t covered it you can always [contact us\!](mailto:xwi7xwa.library@ubc.ca)
