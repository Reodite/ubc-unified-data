## The week of November 16 th \(November 10\-16\) X wi7 x wa Library is celebrating Métis Week 2024\!

We decorated the branch and pulled some resources for you to learn more about Métis and the contributions of Honourable Louis Riel\. We even have some colouring pages and word searches you can take home with you 😊
