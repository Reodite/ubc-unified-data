[This new guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/c.php?g=710755&amp;p=5064361&amp;) help in locating resources about two\-spirit &amp; Indigenous queer studies\. Here you will find links to books, e\-books, journals, articles, theses, videos, websites &amp; more about this topic\. We’ll have many of these materials at the Pride Event at UBC on August 6th from 5pm\-9pm\. Two\-spirit &amp; Indigenous queer studies is an interdisciplinary field grounded in the languages, histories, geographies, and contemporary experiences of Indigenous peoples\.

To view all two\-spirit &amp; Indigenous queer resources we hold at the library, please view our book list of [Two Spirit Books at Xwi7xwa Library\.](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/09/Two-Spirit-Books-at-Xwi7xwa-Library-521.pdf)
