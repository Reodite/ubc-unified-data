Orange Shirt Day was inspired by [Phyllis \(Jack\) Webstad](https://orangeshirtday.org/phyllis-story/), Northern Secwpemc \(Shuswap\) from the Stswecem’c Xgat’tem First Nation \(Canoe Creek Indian Band\)\. You can learn more about the history of Orange Shirt Day [here](https://orangeshirtday.org/orange-shirt-day/)\. September 30 is federally recognized as the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, learn more [here](https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/campaigns/national-day-truth-reconciliation.html)\.
