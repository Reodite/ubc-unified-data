Our LibGuides look a little different\! We’re making some changes to our LibGuides, here’s what to expect:

1.  Our guides will now [follow best practices](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/guidelines/overview) from UBC Library, this will make usability much easier for researchers\!
2.  Updated information: we’re working through broken links and finding more current information\.
3.  Reorganizing and consolidating information, this will make it much easier to find the information you’re looking for\.

We apologize for any disruptions you encounter at this time, please email us if you can’t find what you’re looking for: xwi7xwa\.library@ubc\.ca
