The UBC Department of Urologic Sciences is excited to announce that two of its research projects have been awarded CIHR Project Grants for Spring 2026, totalling over $3 million\. This funding will support initiatives focused on expanding treatment options for patients with advanced cancers and enhancing prostate cancer monitoring through the use of artificial intelligence\.

[Dr\. Yuzhuo Wang](https://www.prostatecentre.com/about-us/people/dr-yuzhuo-wang) \(Professor, UBC Urologic Sciences\) is leading a project to develop a promising new cancer treatment for people with advanced cancers that no longer respond to existing therapies\.

[Dr\. Miles Mannas](https://www.prostatecentre.com/about-us/people/dr-miles-mannas) \(Assistant Professor, UBC Urologic Sciences\) and [Dr\. Purang Abolmaesumi](https://ece.ubc.ca/purang-abolmaesumi) \(Associate Member, UBC Urologic Sciences and Professor, UBC Electrical and Computer Engineering\) are collaborating with Queen’s Computing Science to develop an AI tool that could help men with prostate cancer avoid unnecessary biopsies while ensuring signs of cancer progression are detected early\.

Together, these projects highlight two complementary approaches to improving cancer care: developing new treatments and enhancing clinical decision\-making with artificial intelligence\.  

## Creating new treatment options for patients with advanced cancers

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/Yuzhuo-Wang_800sq.png)

**Dr\. Yuzhuo Wang**

Professor, UBC Department of Urologic Sciences  
Senior Research Scientist, [Vancouver Prostate Centre](https://www.prostatecentre.com/) at the [M\.H\. Mohseni Institute of Urologic Sciences](https://mius.ca/)\.

**Project Title:** [Derisking the Development of VPC\-25959 Derivatives as Novel MCT4 Inhibitors for Clinical Trials in Patients with Advanced Glycolytic Cancers](https://webapps.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/decisions/p/project_details.html?applId=565068&amp;lang=en)

**Principal Investigator:** [Yuzhuo Wang](https://www.prostatecentre.com/about-us/people/dr-yuzhuo-wang)

**Co\-investigators:** Colin Collins, Mark Dawidek, Martin Gleave, Christopher Ong, Robert Young \(Simon Fraser Department of Chemistry\)

**Term:** 5 years

**Amount:** $1,912,500

Many advanced cancers consume large amounts of glucose and produce excessive lactic acid, creating conditions that help tumours grow, spread, resist treatment, and evade the immune system—a phenomenon originally hypothesized by the research team\. MCT4 is a key protein that cancer cells use to release lactic acid into their surrounding environment\.

The team has developed a promising new class of small\-molecule MCT4 inhibitors that have shown strong anticancer activity in preclinical studies\.

This project aims to identify the best candidate for clinical development, evaluate its safety and pharmacological properties, develop biomarkers to identify patients who are most likely to benefit from the treatment, and finalize the design of a first\-in\-human clinical trial\. The ultimate goal is to offer a new treatment option to patients with advanced, treatment\-resistant cancers\.

> “This award represents an important milestone after many years of research and collaboration\. It will allow us to move our MCT4 inhibitor program much closer to clinical testing, with the goal of developing a new treatment for patients with advanced cancers who currently have limited therapeutic options\.”
> 
> *– Dr\. Yuzhuo Wang*

## Using AI to help men with prostate cancer avoid unnecessary biopsies

**Dr\. Purang Abolmaesumi**

Professor, UBC Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering

**Project Title:** [PROPEL\-AS: Prostate personalized surveillance using pathology\-aligned multi\-modal imaging](https://webapps.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/decisions/p/project_details.html?applId=565015&amp;lang=en)

**Principal Investigators:** Parvin Mousavi \(Queen’s University, School of Computing\), [Purang Abolmaesumi](https://ece.ubc.ca/purang-abolmaesumi/), [Miles Mannas](https://www.prostatecentre.com/about-us/people/dr-miles-mannas)

**Co\-investigators:** Peter Black, Silvia Chang \(UBC Department of Radiology\), Gabor Fichtinger \(Queen’s School of Computing\), Robert Siemens \(Queen’s Department of Urology\)

**Term:** 5 years

**Amount:** $1,151,326

Prostate cancer is one of the most common cancers in Canadian men, with many low\-risk cases managed through active surveillance to avoid unnecessary treatment\. However, this approach often involves repeated biopsies, which can be burdensome for patients\.

The PROPEL\-AS team is developing an AI decision\-support system that combines MRI, micro\-ultrasound, PSA density, and previous biopsy data to enhance prostate cancer monitoring\. A key innovation is using pathology\-aligned imaging, enabling the AI to recognize patterns that align with tissue samples\.

The goal is to identify men who can be safely monitored with fewer biopsies while ensuring accurate detection of cancer progression\. This could reduce unnecessary procedures and support more personalized care, improving quality of life and outcomes for men with prostate cancer\.

> “Active surveillance can be an effective way to manage low\-risk prostate cancer, but it often requires repeated biopsies that can be stressful and uncomfortable for patients\. By combining AI with advanced imaging and pathology data, we hope to make monitoring more precise, reduce unnecessary procedures, and help patients receive more personalized care\.”
> 
> *– Dr\. Miles Mannas*

These CIHR\-funded projects underscore UBC’s commitment to advancing research that improves the lives of people affected by cancer\.
