The Department of Urologic Sciences is pleased to congratulate [**Dr\. Gillian Vandekerkhove**](https://www.prostatecentre.com/node/602) on receiving a **2026 Canadian Cancer Society Emerging Scholar Research Grant**\.

[Dr\. Gillian Vandekerkhove](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/dr-vandekerkhove.jpg-300x300.webp)

Dr\. Gillian Vandekerkhove

The five\-year grant, totalling **$550,000 CAD**, will support Dr\. Vandekerkhove’s research project titled:

**“Urinary DNA for enhanced detection and characterization of localized urothelial cancer\.”**

The Canadian Cancer Society Emerging Scholar Research Grants support promising early\-career investigators in establishing and advancing independent cancer research programs in Canada\. This year, the program is supporting 19 investigators through 17 projects across five provinces and the four pillars of health research\.

Dr\. Vandekerkhove is an Assistant Professor in the UBC Department of Urologic Sciences, a Senior Research Scientist at the Vancouver Prostate Centre and the M\.H\. Mohseni Institute of Urologic Sciences, and an Associate Scientist at the BC Cancer Research Institute\.

Congratulations to Dr\. Vandekerkhove on this outstanding achievement\!

Learn more about the [2026 Canadian Cancer Society Emerging Scholar Research Grants](https://cancer.ca/en/research/for-researchers/funding-results/2026-esrg)\.
