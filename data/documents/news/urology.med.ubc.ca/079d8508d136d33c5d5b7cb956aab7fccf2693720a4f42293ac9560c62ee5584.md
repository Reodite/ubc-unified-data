[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/KillianDaly_2026-500.png)

### Killian Daly

**[Uro\-Oncology Fellowship](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/education/fellowship/uro-oncology-fellowship-description/)**  
**2026**–**2027**

Killian Daly is from Dublin, Ireland and graduated from Trinity College Dublin in 2016\. After completing his internship at St\. James’s Hospital, Dublin, in 2017, he undertook Basic and Higher Specialist Training in Urology through the Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland, completing his training in 2026\. He was awarded the FRCS Urology in July 2025\.

Killian has a longstanding subspecialist interest in uro\-oncology\. Following meetings with Dr\. Gleave and Dr\. Black at a conference in Dublin, he visited the UBC Department of Urologic Sciences in 2024 with a view to pursuing a Uro\-Oncology Fellowship\.

Killian began his Uro\-Oncology Fellowship at UBC in July 2026 and is looking forward to an exciting year of clinical and academic development\. His fellowship will provide broad exposure to complex uro\-oncological practice, as well as opportunities to further develop his academic and research interests\.

He has relocated to Vancouver with his wife, who is also undertaking fellowship training in uro\-oncology, and their one\-year\-old son\.

[Olwyn Lynch](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/OlwynLynch_2026-500.png)

### Olwyn Lynch

**[Uro\-Oncology Fellowship](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/education/fellowship/uro-oncology-fellowship-description/)**  
**2026**–**2027**

Olwyn Lynch graduated from Trinity College Dublin, Ireland, in 2016\. Following completion of her internship in Ireland, she spent a year working in Perth, Western Australia, before returning to Ireland to undertake Higher Surgical Training in Urology through the Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland\. She completed her FRCS \(Urology\) in May 2024\.

Olwyn first met Dr\. Martin Gleave at an Irish urology meeting and was inspired to learn more about the program\. She subsequently arranged a visit to the department in 2024, where she was impressed by the fellowship, the team, and the city of Vancouver\. This experience ultimately inspired her to pursue a Uro\-Oncology Fellowship at Vancouver General Hospital\.

She completed her Irish urology training programme in June 2026 and began her Uro\-Oncology Fellowship at Vancouver General Hospital on July 1, 2026\. She is excited to develop her surgical and academic expertise while working alongside world\-renowned surgeon\-scientists in uro\-oncology\.

She has relocated to Vancouver with her husband, who is also undertaking a Uro\-Oncology Fellowship, and their one\-year\-old son, Ellis\.

[Abdullah Fouda Neel](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/AbdullahFoudaNeel_2026-500-2.png)

### Abdullah Fouda Neel

[**Pediatric Urology Fellowship**](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/education/fellowship/pediatric-urology-fellowship-description/)  
**2026**–**2028**

Abdullah Fouda Neel is originally from Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, where he has spent most of his life\. He lived in Ottawa for the first two years of his life while his father was working there\. Abdullah completed his medical degree at Alfaisal University and his urology residency at King Saud University Medical City\. Before joining UBC, he worked for a year and a half as a Senior Registrar in Pediatric Urology\. He is also a lecturer and a faculty member in the Department of Surgery at King Saud University\.

Abdullah was drawn to pediatric urology because of the opportunity to care for children across a broad spectrum of conditions, collaborate within multidisciplinary teams, and develop expertise across a wide range of surgical approaches\. He is particularly excited to join UBC because of its advanced clinical environment, diverse expertise, excellent case volume, and strong opportunities for research and learning\.

Outside medicine, Abdullah enjoys travelling, weightlifting, golf, reading, and playing guitar\. He has visited more than 20 countries and reads approximately 24 books a year\. He recently got married just before moving to Vancouver and is excited to explore Vancouver and British Columbia with his wife—almost like an extension of his honeymoon\!

### Andrey Ramírez González

**[Uro\-Oncology Fellowship](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/education/fellowship/uro-oncology-fellowship-description/)**  
**2026**–**2028**

Andrey Ramírez González is a urologist from Mexico City, Mexico\. He studied medicine at Universidad La Salle and completed two years of general surgery training before pursuing his urology residency at Hospital General Dr\. Manuel Gea González, affiliated with the National Autonomous University of Mexico \(UNAM\)\. He subsequently completed fellowship training in urologic oncology at the National Cancer Institute in Mexico City\.

Andrey was drawn to urologic oncology by its combination of challenging surgery, multidisciplinary teamwork, long\-term patient care, and meaningful clinical research\. His academic interests include prostate and bladder cancer, particularly early detection, risk assessment, and research that can support clinical decision\-making and improve patient outcomes\.

He is excited to join UBC Urologic Sciences, where he hopes to learn as much as possible while contributing to patient care and clinical research\. Outside medicine, he enjoys travelling, exploring new cities and restaurants, watching films, and spending time with friends and family\.

Justin Chan is originally from Burnaby, British Columbia, and is excited to return home to begin his fellowship at the University of British Columbia\. He completed his medical degree at UBC before pursuing residency training in Urology at the University of Toronto\.

As a Reproductive and Sexual Medicine Fel, he is particularly looking forward to joining UBC Urologic Sciences because of its many opportunities to contribute to both clinical care and wet\-lab research\.

Outside of medicine, Justin enjoys sailing during the summer and curling in the winter\.

### Nasser Alhazzani

**[Endo\-Urology Fellowship](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/education/fellowship/endo-urology-fellowship-description/)**  
**2026**–**2028**

Originally from Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, Nasser Alhazzani completed medical school at King Saud bin Abdulaziz University for Health Sciences and a Urology residency program at King Abdulaziz Hospital – MNGHA\.

Nasser was drawn to endo\-urology by a passion for minimally invasive technologies, including complex stone disease, prostate surgery, and laser treatments, as well as advanced laparoscopy and robotic surgery\. He is highly excited to join UBC’s pioneering clinical side and clinical research teams\.

Outside the operating room, he has long been a sports enthusiast who enjoys playing and watching soccer, padel, and tennis\. He also has a long\-standing passion for hiking and socializing with friends, hobbies that he looks forward to continuing during his time in Vancouver\.

## Residents

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/AparnaKuchibhatla_headshot2-940x940.jpg)

### Aparna Kuchibhatla

Aparna Kuchibhatla was born in Ottawa, ON, before moving to the United States, where she primarily grew up in Seattle, WA\. She returned to Canada to complete both her undergraduate and medical school education at Queen’s University\.

During clerkship, she unexpectedly discovered her passion for urology through a community family medicine rotation, where she was drawn to the variety of clinical presentations, procedural skills, and longitudinal patient care\.

Outside of medicine, Aparna enjoys playing badminton, exploring new coffee shops, and spending time with friends and family\. She’s excited to be back on the West Coast \(AKA the best coast\)\!

[Corey Fletcher](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/CoreyFletcher_headshot.jpg)

### Corey Fletcher

Corey Fletcher was raised primarily in Toronto and Brampton, Ontario\. He started his academic journey at Western University, where he completed a Bachelor of Medical Sciences in Physiology\. Following this, he completed his law degree \(JD\) at Osgoode Hall Law School in Toronto and has worked in corporate and healthcare law\.

Although a licensed lawyer, Corey continued to have a love for medicine and healthcare and subsequently attended medical school at Queen’s University in Kingston, Ontario\. There, he was introduced to urology and fell in love with the specialty’s mix of medicine and surgery, as well as its focus on improving the quality of life for patients\.

He is extremely excited to continue his training in beautiful British Columbia and to serve diverse patients\. Outside of medicine and being at the hospital, Corey enjoys karaoke, hitting the gym, biking, spending time outdoors, and exploring new places\.

[Justin Hampole](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/JustinHampole_headshot-940x940.jpg)

### Justin Hampole

Justin Hampole was born and raised in Prince George, where he developed a strong connection to British Columbia’s outdoors and community\. He began his post\-secondary training at the University of Northern British Columbia, completing three years of a biomedical studies degree before earning his medical degree through UBC’s Island Medical Program in Victoria\. During medical school, he was drawn to urology for its blend of technical innovation, variety, and meaningful impact on quality of life\. He is grateful for the outstanding mentorship he received from the urologists in Victoria while exploring the specialty\.

Before pursuing medicine, Justin competed as a figure skater at the national level, becoming a Canada Winter Games medalist and five\-time national competitor\. His passion for the sport continues to this day, and he enjoys staying involved in skating whenever possible\.

As he joins UBC Urology, Justin is excited to explore the Lower Mainland and take full advantage of what it has to offer, from hiking local trails to finding his go\-to coffee shops and exploring Vancouver’s world\-class food scene\.

[Sareen Singh](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/SareenSingh_headshot-940x940.jpg)

### Sareen Singh  

Sareen Singh was born and raised in Nova Scotia before moving to Montreal to complete a Bachelor of Science at McGill University\. She eventually returned to the East Coast to pursue a master’s degree in Epidemiology and Applied Health Research, followed by medical school, at Dalhousie University\.

Sareen discovered urology by chance on her second day of medical school and never looked back\. She was drawn to the immense variety and innovation within the specialty, the opportunities for both medical and surgical management, the “quiet wins” of caring for patients through sensitive issues, and the close\-knit urology community\. She is especially excited to learn from the world\-renowned urologists and researchers at UBC Urology while experiencing life on the West Coast for the first time\.

Outside of the hospital, Sareen enjoys golfing, travelling, exploring new neighbourhoods, trying local cafes and restaurants, and spending time with family and friends\.
