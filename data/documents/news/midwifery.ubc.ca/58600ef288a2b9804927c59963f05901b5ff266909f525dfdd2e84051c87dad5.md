*Update \(July 20, 2026\): This post has been updated to identify the presenter who delivered one of the conference abstracts on behalf of the lead researcher\.*

Held every three years, the International Confederation of Midwives \(ICM\) Congress brings together midwives, educators, researchers and advocates from around the world to share knowledge, strengthen partnerships and advance midwifery care globally\. 

This year marked UBC Midwifery’s second consecutive appearance with an exhibitor booth, providing an opportunity to connect with colleagues, showcase the program and celebrate the global midwifery community\. 

A delegation of faculty and staff attended the congress in Lisbon, Portugal, including the program’s leadership team, student services staff and faculty members\. The event provided opportunities to share research, exchange ideas and strengthen international partnerships that support excellence in midwifery education, research and practice\. 

The congress also provided meaningful opportunities to reconnect with alumni from both the undergraduate and Internationally Educated Midwives Bridging Program \(IEMBP\), celebrate their accomplishments and strengthen relationships across the global midwifery community\. 

## Faculty Presentations

[Image](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/Jevitt-ICM-presentation-2026-940x529.jpg)

**UBC Midwifery faculty contributed to the scientific program through presentations addressing evidence\-informed care, respectful maternity care, health equity and global midwifery education\.**

Interim Director **[Dr\. Cecilia Jevitt](https://midwifery.ubc.ca/cecilia-m-jevitt/)** presented the latest evidence on maintaining midwifery care and supporting healthy outcomes for individuals living in larger bodies\. **[Professor Emeritus Cathy Ellis](https://midwifery.ubc.ca/cathy-ellis/)** shared lessons from an international supportive supervision pilot that strengthened midwifery education in Somalia and Somaliland\. **Research led by** [**Professor Dr\. Saraswathi Vedam**](https://midwifery.ubc.ca/saraswathi-vedam/) , presented by Missy Cheyney, examined experiences of mistreatment during perinatal care in Canada and introduced the Undisturbed Labour and Birth \(ULaB\) Facility Index, a quality improvement tool that supports respectful, physiologic birth and greater accountability across maternity care settings\.
