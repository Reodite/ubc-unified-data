Under clear skies and warm sunshine, UBC Midwifery celebrated the graduation of the Class of 2026, the largest graduating class in the program’s history\. 

This year, 27 students crossed the stage at spring graduation, with one additional student expected to complete the program in the fall, bringing the Class of 2026 to 28 graduates\. Together, they represent the next generation of midwives who will serve families and communities across British Columbia and beyond\. 

Graduation marked the culmination of years of academic study, clinical learning and dedicated service\. Throughout their training, students developed the knowledge, skills and compassion needed to provide safe, evidence\-informed, person\-centred care during pregnancy, birth and the postpartum period\.

The celebrations extended beyond Convocation, with the Midwifery Student Association \(MSA\) hosting a graduation dinner that brought together students, faculty, staff, preceptors, family members and friends\. The evening featured heartfelt reflections, words of encouragement and inspiring messages for graduates as they prepare to begin the next chapter of their professional journeys\. 

A particularly meaningful moment was the blanket ceremony led by [Dr\. and Elder Roberta Price](https://nursing.ubc.ca/community/news/2021/dr-elder-roberta-price-honouring-event) , a Coast Salish matriarch and Knowledge Keeper from the Snuneymuxw and Cowichan Nations, and [Elder Jean Wasegijig](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/rcaps/meet-the-team/) , an Odawa First Nation Elder from the Wikwemikong Unceded Territory in Ontario\. Rooted in Indigenous traditions of honouring and recognition, the ceremony celebrated the graduates’ accomplishments, their commitment to serving families and communities and the responsibilities they carry forward as health\-care professionals\. It offered an opportunity for reflection, gratitude and celebration as students transitioned from learners to practising midwives\. 

Adding to the occasion’s significance was the presence of faculty members from UBC Midwifery’s first graduating class\. Their participation reflected the program’s growth over the past two decades and the lasting impact its graduates continue to have on midwifery education and care throughout the province\.

The celebration also provided an opportunity to recognize [Dr\. Cecilia Jevitt](https://midwifery.ubc.ca/cecilia-m-jevitt/) for her leadership and dedication to the program\. The Class of 2026 will be the final graduating class celebrated during her tenure as director before her upcoming sabbatical and retirement\. Students, faculty and staff expressed their gratitude for her years of service, mentorship and commitment to advancing midwifery education at UBC\. 

During the evening, Elder Roberta Price also honoured Dr\. Jevitt with a brushing ceremony using an eagle feather\. Guests were called upon as witnesses, recognizing her contributions to the program and the relationships she has fostered throughout her years of leadership\. 

The day was marked by celebration, reflection and gratitude for the students, faculty, preceptors, families and communities who supported the graduates throughout their journey\.

Feel the energy in the birth room and use that energy to recharge your own midwifery batteries so that you fulfill your obligation to the health of the citizens of British Columbia\. ”*

## Celebrating student achievement 

Several members of the graduating class were recognized for their outstanding contributions to the program, their peers and the families they served throughout their training\. Among them was **Mpho Holele**, who was recently featured in the Faculty of Medicine’s [*Tuum Est* graduation profile series](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/tuum-est-spring-graduation-2026/) , highlighting the experiences and achievements of members of the Class of 2026\. 

-   **UBC Midwifery Award for Academic Excellence**: Jenny Allan — $500 

-   **UBC Midwifery Award for Distinguished Service to Families**: Mpho Holele — $250 

-   **UBC Midwifery Award for Citizenship and Fellowship \(Peer Award\)**: Anya Olson — $100 

-   **UBC Midwifery Program Service Award**: Charlotte Mackenzie — $100 

-   **UBC Midwifery Solidarity Award**: Jen Attayoub — $200 

These awards recognize excellence in academic achievement, compassionate care, leadership, community building and service — qualities that reflect the values of the UBC Midwifery program and the profession as a whole\. 

As the Class of 2026 embarks on careers that will take them to urban, rural, remote and Indigenous communities throughout British Columbia and beyond, they join a growing network of UBC Midwifery graduates committed to strengthening maternity care and supporting families across the province\. 

Congratulations to the UBC Midwifery Class of 2026\. We look forward to seeing the many ways you will contribute to your communities and to the future of midwifery care\.
