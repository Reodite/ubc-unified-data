The Midwifery Messenger is the quarterly newsletter for the UBC Midwifery stakeholders and community\. The UBC Midwifery community is that of the Midwifery Undergrad Program, IEMBP, Birth Place Lab, Perinatal Wellbeing Research Lab, and CPD Midwifery for which the collected news and highlights will reflect\. Each issue is a review of the quarter with concise and clear points with forward\-looking statements for the next quarter\.

UBC Midwifery also maintains a Clinical Faculty ‘Preceptor’ email distribution list\. This announcement\-only list is separate from the Midwifery Messenger\.
