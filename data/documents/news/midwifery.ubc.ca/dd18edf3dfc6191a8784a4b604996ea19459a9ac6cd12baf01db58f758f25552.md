UBC Midwifery will welcome a new leadership team on September 1, 2026, as Dr\. Cecilia Jevitt begins a long\-deferred sabbatical\. Allison Campbell will be stepping in as Interim Program Director, with Dr\. Luba Butska taking on the roles of Associate Director and Undergraduate Program Lead previously held by Allison\. 

Dr\. Jevitt first joined UBC Midwifery as Program Director in July 2018 and has provided dedicated leadership to the program through a period of significant growth and change\. After retiring from the Director role in 2023, she returned to provide leadership when the program needed her, while also taking on the role of co\-heading of department within UBC’s Department of Family Practice\. 

Her planned sabbatical was put on hold during this period, making this transition both a well\-earned opportunity for Cecilia to pursue that work **once again** and a moment to recognize her considerable commitment to UBC Midwifery\. 

Following her sabbatical, Dr\. Jevitt will retire from UBC, marking an important transition in her longstanding leadership and service to the Midwifery Program\. We look forward to her continued connection with the program and community\. 

## *Statement from Dr\. Cecilia Jevitt*

```
I recently stumbled over a short interview that Communications did when I arrived at UBC in 2018. I said that I saw my role as helping to grow midwifery leadership in Canada. That has been my work for 8 years, showing the faculty how to handle the administrivia of academic day to day, making decisions that are as fair to all involved as possible, keeping the budget balanced…I start a year long study leave on September 1st where I can finally answer some small research questions that have been parked on my desk. At the end of that year, I’ll retire, hopefully into an active emeritus position where I can appear periodically and pontificate. Working with you all has made this the best midwifery work of my life. Thank you! You have sage support to carry midwifery forward. Allison Campbell assumes the post of Interim Program Director, and Luba Butska will be the Associate Director and Lead for the Undergraduate Program. I’m confident that with your help, Allison and Luba will continue to make midwifery the best health profession in BC. Cecilia, Jevitt
```

Allison Campbell, RM, MA, will become Interim Program Director on September 1\. A longtime member of the UBC Midwifery community, Allison brings a unique perspective to the role, with connections to the program that extend from her early experience as a graduate of UBC Midwifery’s inaugural cohort to more than a decade as an educator and academic leader\. 

Since joining the faculty in 2012, Allison has served in several leadership roles, including Undergraduate Program Lead, Interim Program Director \(2017\-2018\) and, most recently, Associate Director\. Her work has helped shape curriculum, expand access to midwifery education across British Columbia and advance the program’s commitments to social justice and health equity\. A recipient of the UBC Killam Teaching Prize, Allison is also pursuing doctoral research focused on reproductive justice and reproductive health in prison settings, supported by the [Public Scholars Initiative](https://grsj.arts.ubc.ca/profile/allison-campbell/) \.

She will be joined by Dr\. Luba Butska, RM, PhD, who will assume the role of Associate Director upon her return from sabbatical\. 

Blake Dobie, Senior Administrative Business Manager, will continue in his current role as part of the leadership team\. 

Luba is a longtime UBC Midwifery faculty member and Associate Professor of Teaching whose work spans midwifery education, research, clinical practice and global health\. She returns to the program following a year\-long study leave that continued her longstanding humanitarian and [global health work in Ukraine](https://news.ubc.ca/2023/08/ubc-health-care-workers-in-ukraine/) , where she has contributed her clinical and educational expertise to initiatives supporting health\-care delivery and midwifery services\. 

Blake brings more than 15 years of experience with UBC Midwifery to the leadership team\. Since joining the program in 2010, he has supported its growth through several stages of expansion and the development of new educational programs, progressing from educational and student services roles to Senior Administrative Business Manager\. 

Together, Allison, Luba and Blake bring decades of experience with UBC Midwifery and deep knowledge of its history, people and evolution\. Their longstanding relationships with students, faculty, staff, clinical faculty, partners and the broader midwifery community provide important continuity as the program moves forward\. 

## *Statement from Allison Campbell*

[Image](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/UBC-Midwifery-Leadership-Transition-2026-3-Allison-Campbell-UBC-Blue-Duotone.png)

```
I am very grateful to be asked to take on the role of Interim Director and am looking forward to supporting the Program in this way. In the past 7 years as Undergraduate Program Lead, and under Cele’s Directorship, we have seen the program expand from a student cohort of 80 students to 128; from training students in 2 locations to 3, plus an online option for first year students, making the program far more accessible for our many learners living outside the Lower Mainland. Internally, we have refined almost every aspect of the program from administrative functions to policies, from curriculum to pedagogical approach.  This coming year we will continue to heed the ICM’s call for “one million more midwives” in our own corner of this complex world. In addition to continuing to prepare new midwives for practice, we will be bringing focus to developing and supporting existing midwives to support students’ learning in the clinical setting.  As we move into the new academic year, I look forward to welcoming 32 more new students into UBC Midwifery, and welcoming back returning students; wishing students, staff, faculty and clinical faculty a smooth year ahead.    As always, please be in touch if there is anything I can do to strengthen our connections with you. AllisonAllison.Campbell@ubc.ca
```
