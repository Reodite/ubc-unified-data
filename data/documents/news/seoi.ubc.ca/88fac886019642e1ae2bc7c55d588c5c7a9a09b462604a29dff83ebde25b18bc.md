PAIR, which administers the SEI surveys, has updated the web addresses used by students and instructors to access the UBC Blue Course Survey tool directly\. This adjustment was made to provide direct access independent of Canvas integrations\.

When accessing the survey site, users will now be prompted to log in directly using their Campus\-Wide Login\. Once authenticated, they will be securely signed in to Blue to access surveys and reports\.

**This change does not impact students’ access to active surveys, nor does it impact instructors’ access to their reports\.**

-   Student survey access: [https://seoi\.ubc\.ca/surveys](https://seoi.ubc.ca/surveys)
-   Instructor reports access: [https://seoi\.ubc\.ca/reports](https://seoi.ubc.ca/reports)

Survey operations and access to reporting remain available through the updated process\.

If you have any questions, please contact us at [sei\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:sei.support@ubc.ca)\.

2026 Summer Term 1 Student Experience of Instruction \(SEI\) surveys are available from **June 13 – 21, 2026** for most classes\.

---

You are invited to provide feedback for the instructors and the courses in which you are enrolled\. The University and the Faculty offering the course value your comments as they do make a difference in the planning and delivery of curriculum\.

Results of Student Experience of Instruction \(SEI\) surveys are:

-   reviewed by your instructors and teaching assistants to help them improve their teaching \(instructors receive their SEI reports after they have submitted their final grades\),
-   beneficial for instructors and teaching assistants to reflect on the past term and plan for future offerings, and
-   reviewed by the Dean and Department Heads as input into academic decisions\.

Thank you for your participation\!

If you have any questions, please contact us at [sei\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:sei.support@ubc.ca)\.

Following the open forum on Student Experience of Instruction \(SEI\) in March we are holding another open forum on Sept\. 28, 2021, 12:00\-1:30 p\.m\. During this event we will provide updates about work being done to implement [recommendations about SEI endorsed by both UBC Senates in May of 2020](https://seoi.ubc.ca/upcoming-changes/senate-recommendations/), focusing in particular on revisions to the University Module Questions that were endorsed by Senate committees at both UBCO and UBCV in August\. These revised questions are being implemented on student end\-of\-course surveys starting in Winter Term 1, Fall 2021, so this open forum provides a useful opportunity to discuss the rationale behind the changes and answer questions\.

As a reminder, at the open forum in March we discussed changes to the end\-of\-course student surveys, including a change in the name of the surveys from “Student Evaluations of Teaching” to “Student Experience of Instruction\.”

At the open forum on September 28 we will begin with updates on implementing the recommended changes to SEI, and we will also open the floor to questions from participants\. We invite you to submit questions ahead of the open forum through the registration process\.
