### **A redesigned high and low ropes course is coming in 2027**

We’re excited to share that Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre is undertaking a full redesign and rebuild of its high and low ropes cours**e**, with a new course anticipated to open in Spring 2027\.

The new ropes course is part of our ongoing commitment to providing safe, sustainable, and high\-quality experiences for our guests\. The redesigned course will create new opportunities for challenge, collaboration, learning, and outdoor recreation, while supporting group programming and youth experiences for years to come\.

### **Designed and Built with Challenges Unlimited Inc\.**

To bring the new ropes course to life, Loon Lake has partnered with [**Challenges Unlimited**](https://challengesunlimited.com/), an experienced leader in the design and construction of adventure and challenge course environments\.

Together, we are developing a new ropes course that is thoughtfully designed for the Loon Lake setting and the diverse groups who visit the site\. The project will incorporate modern course design and construction practices while creating an engaging outdoor experience that connects participants with the surrounding forest\.

### **Next Steps**

The existing ropes course will close for construction effective **September 1, 2026**\. It will remain unavailable to guests while the new course is being designed, constructed, and installed\.

We recognize that the ropes course has been an important part of many group programs at Loon Lake, and we are sharing this information well in advance to help groups plan their upcoming visits\.

### **Key Dates**

**September 1, 2026  
**Existing high and low ropes course closes for guest use\.

**Fall 2026  
**Construction of the new ropes course\.

**Winter/Spring 2027  
**New high and low ropes course anticipated to open\.

### **Alternative Ways to Get Outside and Stay Active**

While the ropes course is unavailable during construction, there are still many ways for groups to connect, explore, and learn at Loon Lake\.

Our programming partners — [**Pinnacle Pursuits**](https://pinnaclepursuits.com/)**,** [**Stellar Play**](https://stellarplay.ca/)**, and** [**Wild &amp; Immersive**](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/) — offer a variety of outdoor recreation, team building, youth programming, and environmental education experiences\. Groups can work with our programming partners to explore activities that fit their goals and the needs of their participants\.

From getting out on the water and exploring the forest to participating in team\-building and environmental education activities, there are plenty of opportunities to make the most of your time at Loon Lake while the new ropes course takes shape\.

### **Partner With Us**

**Help Bring Outdoor Adventure to Life at Loon Lake**

With the new ropes course on the horizon the University of British Columbia’s Faculty of Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship is currently looking for a qualified outdoor recreation programming partner to operate the ropes course and deliver outdoor recreational programming at Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre\.

This opportunity is intended for an experienced organization that can provide engaging, high\-quality outdoor recreational, educational, leadership, and team\-building programming to guests staying at Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre\. The program partner will align with Loon Lake’s mission and vision by providing inclusive, high\-quality programming that is accessible to a wide range of group types, interests, and budgets, while contributing to increased year\-round site utilization and revenue generation\.

We welcome proposals from qualified organizations interested in becoming a long\-term programming partner at Loon Lake\.

[**VIEW THE REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL**](https://mkrf-forestry.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/UBC-Loon-Lake-Recreation-Request-For-Proposal-Aug-2026.pdf)

### **Looking Ahead to 2027**

We look forward to welcoming groups to a brand\-new ropes course in 2027\. The redesigned course will provide a renewed setting for challenge, collaboration, learning, and connection in the forest\.

**Planning a group stay at Loon Lake?** If you have an upcoming booking and would like to discuss how the ropes course closure may affect your plans, please [contact our team](mailto:loon.lake@ubc.ca)\. We’re happy to help you explore alternative programming options during the construction period\.
