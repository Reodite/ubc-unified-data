Picture your team gathered around a crackling bonfire, underneath a starlit sky\. The ambient sound of Loon Lake's waters sloshing against the shore is all that’s heard beneath the hum of excited conversations\. The day’s work has been productive, the setting inspirational, and the connections formed, invaluable\. Welcome to the mesmerizing world of corporate retreats, Loon Lake style\!

Loon Lake is where the Vancouver’s business community meets the enchanting embrace of nature\. It’s a place where innovative ideas are as abundant as the pine needles beneath your feet\. Whether it’s a fireside brainstorming session or a canoe meeting at dawn, Loon Lake brings a [breath of fresh air to corporate meetings](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/blog/a-healthy-approach-to-meetings)\.

In this piece, we unveil the 8 compelling reasons why Loon Lake should be top of your list for an invigorating and unforgettable corporate retreat\. Buckle up as we journey through emerald forests and azure waters, where business goals align with personal enrichment\.  
  

1.  ## Natural Beauty
    

### **Picturesque Landscapes**

Loon Lake is surrounded by a tapestry of breathtaking landscapes, where imposing mountains kiss the sky and dense forests meet the shimmering lake\. Your team will be in awe as they unwind amidst these captivating sights\.

### **Rich Biodiversity**

The rich biodiversity of Loon Lake offers a unique chance to encounter wildlife in its natural habitat\. Watch the morning sun shimmer through the swaying treetops, and listen to the haunting call of loons echo across the waters at night\.

### **The Healing Effects of Nature**

Being close to nature is proven to reduce stress and enhance creativity\. The tranquil environment of Loon Lake offers your team a rejuvenating break from their usual pace, making it a perfect setting to brainstorm new ideas\!   

##  2\. World\-Class Accommodation Options

[Image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/569ebff4c647ad650f36d23c/8c143df0-4531-4ab8-8fb6-69592d2e0b84/image2.jpg)

Loon Lake caters to corporate needs by offering customizable options, including room layouts and special amenities, ensuring a seamless and memorable stay for your team\. 

### **Accommodations Infused with Rustic Elegance**

**Walker C\. Koerner Forestry Centre \(10,193 sq\. ft\)** \- This location offers full amenities and comfortable accommodations for 16\-40 guests, making it an ideal spot for corporate retreats and lakeside learning\. It includes a conference room, WIFI, AV equipment, private diving, and fireside lounging\. As well as meal\-inclusive rates ranging from CAD$208 / person per night\.

**Johnson Yew and Arbutus Cabins \(8,471 sq\. ft\.\)** \- Featuring two adjoining modern log cabins that sleep 10\-32 guests, this location features lounge areas, fireplaces, WIFI, large multi\-purpose rooms with heated floors, and 8 tranquil bedrooms\. Meal\-inclusive rates start at CAD 133 / per adult and CAD 104 / per night for youth\.

**The Pan Abode \(2,411 sq\. ft\.\)** \- Ideal for small business gatherings or wellness retreats, this venue sleeps up to 20 guests and comes equipped with a kitchenette\. Located close to the Bentley Family Dining Hall, meal\-inclusive rates start at CAD 133/ a day per adult and CAD 104/ a day per youth\.

**Student Centre &amp; Trevor Linden Gymnasium \(11,248 sq\. ft\.\)** \- Suitable for larger groups, especially youth, this facility has seven large bedrooms equipped with bunk beds and lakeside views, along with a gymnasium for physical activities\. It sleeps 15\-63 guests, with meal\-inclusive rates starting at CAD 118/ a day per adult, and CAD 93 a day per youth\.

**Friends &amp; Alumni Cabin \(1,001 sq\. ft\.\)** \- A quaint cabin with a scenic view of the lake, sleeping up to 8 guests\. Perfect as an overflow accommodation for larger groups, it has a cozy upstairs loft, main floor bedrooms, and a kitchenette\. Meal\-inclusive rates start at CAD 118/ a day per adult and CAD 93/ per youth\.

**Thunder Inn \(1,603 sq\. ft\.\)** \- Centrally located and budget\-friendly, this accommodation is great for program leaders or small groups\. It sleeps up to 8 guests with meal\-inclusive prices starting at CAD 118/ a day per adult and CAD 93/ per youth\.

**Bentley Family Dining Hall** \- Often considered the heart of the Loon Lake experience, this hall can host up to 160 adults with additional seating outdoors and is ideal for mealtime gatherings\. The hall’s kitchen is staffed with professionals who serve buffet\-style meals with options for customization\.

With an array of [accommodation options](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/facilities-rates) that blend comfort with rustic charm, Loon Lake is the ideal setting for corporate retreats and team\-building getaways\.

***Note:*** The Dining Hall may be shared with other groups, and all meals are buffet\-style, including beverages and desserts\.

    ## 5\. Stellar Play for Fun and Excitement
    

Embrace the lighter side of life with Stellar Play at Loon Lake\! Offering an assortment of engaging and unique activities, Stellar Play creates unforgettable half\-day and full\-day experiences for kids, youth, and adults\. These activities are not only incredibly fun but are also designed to get everyone active, laughing, and trying something new\.

### **Activities Offered**

**Bubble Ball**: Get ready to roll in laughter with Bubble Ball\! Participants wear inflatable bubbles and can bump into each other, roll around, and engage in hilarious games like Bubble Soccer, British BullDog, and King/Queen of the Ring\. It’s a high\-energy activity that guarantees a good time for all\. 

**Battle Archery**: Merge archery with dodgeball and you get Battle Archery\! Before the games begin, groups will learn how to properly use a bow and arrow\. Geared up with bows, face masks, and arm guards, participants run around shooting giant foam\-tipped arrows at the opposing team\. 

**Geocaching Adventure**: Unleash the explorer within and embark on a Geocaching Adventure around the beautiful surroundings of Loon Lake\. Tailored for each group, participants use technology to find coordinates and search for hidden caches\. Race against time or take it slow \- the choice is yours\! 

**Forest Laser Tag**: Immerse yourself in an adrenaline\-pumping game of Laser Tag set in the forest around Loon Lake\. Equipped with top\-of\-the\-line tactical taggers, experience the thrill of navigating through the woods while engaging in strategic gameplay\.

Team meals are an opportunity for bonding, sharing stories, and creating memories\. At Loon Lake, culinary delights await to make your corporate retreat a deliciously unforgettable experience\!

At Loon Lake, our dining is served buffet\-style, which means you’ll have a variety of choices at your disposal\. From local salmon entrées to vibrant Mexican\-Fusion cuisine, our dishes are reminiscent of comfort food but served with an upscale flair\. 

### **In\-House Culinary Expertise**

Our in\-house culinary team is our pride, with specialists in fresh baking and homemade desserts\. We take great care in working with our guests to create custom menus that accommodate any special dietary requirements or food allergies, including vegan, halal, kosher, and gluten\-free diets\.

### **Breakfast at Loon Lake**

At Loon Lake, breakfast begins with an assortment of fresh fruit, cereal, yogurt, and muffins\. Groups seeking a heartier start to their day can add a hot breakfast menu to their stay including pancakes, waffles, bacon, sausages, and eggs\.

### **Lunch at Loon Lake**

As lunchtime rolls around, enjoy an array of fresh salads, mouth\-watering hamburgers, succulent fish tacos, and some of the best cookies and dessert squares you’ve ever tasted\! Lunch at Loon Lake is a midday treat that both energizes and satisfies\. 

### **Dinner at Loon Lake**

When the dinner bell rings, it’s time to gather for a grand feast\. Savour freshly baked bread and salads accompanied by a selection of vegetables and proteins that will impress even the most discerning palates\. To round off your day, indulge in our fantastic homemade desserts, ensuring your day ends on the sweetest note possible\. 

### **Catering to Special Diets**

We understand the importance of catering to various dietary preferences and restrictions\. Vegetarian and vegan dishes are available upon request, and our kitchen is more than capable of accommodating food allergies with advanced notice\.

 At Loon Lake, every meal is an experience, ensuring that the culinary aspect of your corporate retreat is as memorable and fulfilling as the rest of the activities\.

## 8\. Relaxation and Wellness 

[Image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/569ebff4c647ad650f36d23c/748b53b3-b84c-45ff-b066-44d7aa02d2dc/image3.jpg)

Immerse yourself in the tranquil environment of Loon Lake and revitalize your mind and body with [yoga and fitness](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/blog/yoga-retreat-in-bc)\. Experience the benefits of yoga and meditation, as you attain inner calm and physical wellbeing\.

### **Experience a Diverse Range of Yoga Practices**

**Hatha Yoga**: Strengthen your core, improve flexibility, and test your balance through physical postures and deep breathing techniques that reduce stress and promote mind\-body awareness\.

**Gentle Hatha Restorative Yoga**: Unwind with a restorative practice using props to help soothe the mind and body\.

**Yin Yoga**: Engage and relax your muscles in a slow and soft practice with a variety of poses held for 3\-5 minutes at a time\.

**Restorative Yoga**: Experience slow movement, stillness, and deep breathing through supported poses that promote relaxation and healing\.

**Meditation**: Explore different techniques that assist in building concentration, mental clarity, and inner tranquility\.

**Vinyasa Yoga**: Flow through a series of seamless movements paired with synchronized breathing to build internal awareness and balance\. 

**Mat Pilates:** Partake in a series of conscious full\-body movements designed to improve coordination, strength, and flexibility\. 

With options for all levels, [Epic Yoga &amp; Fitness](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/wellness) at Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre is the perfect place to discover inner peace and build physical strength\.

### **Relax in a Finnish Sauna**

In partnership with [The Finnish Sauna](https://www.thefinnishsauna.ca/), Loon Lake Lodge offers a restoring wood\-fired sauna experience in the heart of the wilderness\. Ideal for groups of up to 18 people, enjoy a 3\-hour sauna rental and experience hot and cold cycles that provide both relaxation and a variety of health benefits: 

-   Reduced inflammation
    
-   Promotes blood flow
    
-   Body detoxification
    
-   Prevention of cardiovascular disease
    
-   Improved mental health and sleep
    

Renting a Finnish sauna is a great way to help your team bond, reconnect with nature, and promote health and well\-being\. Immerse yourself in this revitalizing experience, sure to leave your mind, body, and team feeling refreshed and rejuvenated\.
