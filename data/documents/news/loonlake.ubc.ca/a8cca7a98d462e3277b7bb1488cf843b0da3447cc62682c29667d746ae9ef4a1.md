What better way to fully relax, recharge, and rejuvenate your body and mind than a yoga retreat? After all, it's during our time away from the hustle of everyday life that we can truly start to let go of stressors and any negative feelings\. ##  **Reason to Consider a Yoga Retreat**

[Image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/569ebff4c647ad650f36d23c/d62a2079-1e1b-43f0-81b0-33fb97b9cfef/woman_practicing_yoga.jpg)

Before we give you reasons to plan a yoga retreat, we should tell you what makes yoga a popular rejuvenation practice\. 

Yoga, through meditation, works to achieve a state of bliss by helping the mind work in sync with the body\. Stress, a negative influence on our physical and emotional health, can be controlled and decreased through meditation\. It can also help achieve an emotional balance through recognition and letting go\. 

The resulting inner calm and positivity are very beneficial for physical health\. The ultimate goal of the ancient yogis, who practiced the interconnectedness of physical yoga poses and meditation, was self\-realization or enlightenment, discovering inner peace and outer strength: reducing stress and increasing energy and movement\.

Here are a few [reasons for you to consider a yoga retreat](https://www.forbes.com/sites/bryanrobinson/2020/08/16/new-study-shows-surprising-benefits-of-yoga-to-deal-with-anxiety-and-stress/?sh=6925fc38a71b):

### You'll Learn New Skills 

A yoga retreat is also an excellent place for those who want to learn new skills and hone their existing craft\. You’ll have access to experienced instructors who can provide guidance in postures, breathing techniques, and meditation methods that can help deepen your practice\. In addition to learning about physical postures during your stay at a yoga retreat centre, you will also gain knowledge about other aspects of yoga such as philosophy or nutrition\. With this newfound information in hand, you’ll be able to bring what you learn back with you into everyday life\. 

### Experience Community Support

Yoga retreats are also great ways for yogis of all levels—from beginner through advanced—to come together in one place surrounded by like\-minded individuals who share similar values and interests\. You’ll have the chance to make connections with people who understand what it means to practice yoga not just as a physical activity but as a lifestyle choice as well\. And since many yoga retreat centres offer communal meals or even communal living spaces such as dormitories or cabins, there will be plenty of opportunities for meaningful conversations throughout your stay\!  

### A Chance to Unplug and Recharge 

By practicing yoga and meditation at a yoga retreat, one can focus on restorative practices such as gentle stretching and breathing exercises, and combine them with refreshing [outdoor activities](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/stellar-play) like hiking to reconnect with nature\. Away from all of the distractions of daily life, you’ll have time to focus on being present in the moment without any outside pressures or obligations\.

## **Why Staff Retreats are Important**

There are multiple [benefits of planning a corporate retreat](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/blog/benefits-planning-corporate-retreat) :

-   A retreat centre can be the perfect venue for fresh starts, re\-alignment, and team building\.
    

-   Employee burnout is real and it is a significant concern of corporations when their employees are overworked and feel physically and mentally drained of energy\. Staff retreats can help combat this overwhelming feeling by promoting rest and relaxation\. 
    

-   Planned and regular staff retreats give employees something to look forward to and work toward as they go about their daily routines\. 
    

-   When it comes to morale boosts, very few things can be as beneficial and effective as a team retreat\! It allows the company to have a physical expression of gratitude for their hardworking employees\. This eventually leads to a positive work environment\. 
    

A staff retreat at a reputable lodge and retreating centre can ensure that the experience provided is tailored around their unique interests and needs\.
