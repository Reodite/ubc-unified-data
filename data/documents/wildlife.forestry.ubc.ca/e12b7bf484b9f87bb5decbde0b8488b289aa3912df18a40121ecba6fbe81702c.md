This week brings not one but two publications fresh out of the WildCo lab:

-   A paper exploring the co\-occurrence patterns of predators along seismic lines in northern Alberta in [Ecology and Evolution](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/ece3.6028)\. The paper represents the second chapter published from Erin Tattersall’s MSc thesis\. Congratulations Erin *et al\.*
-   A paper addressing the trade\-offs between timber extraction and woodland caribou habitat conservation across their range in the [Canadian Journal of Forest Research](https://www.nrcresearchpress.com/doi/full/10.1139/cjfr-2019-0234#.XjOB8yOIZhE)\. Lead by Denys Yemshanov, this work represents a collaboration between Cole Burton at UBC and colleagues in the Canadian Forest Service, USDA Forest Service and Lakehead University\.

At this rate 2020 looks set to be a very productive year for the lab\!
