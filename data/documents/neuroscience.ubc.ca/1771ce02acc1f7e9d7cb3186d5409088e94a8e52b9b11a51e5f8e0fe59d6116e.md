**Welcome, International Students\!**

We are thrilled to welcome you to the Graduate Program in Neuroscience \(GPN\) at UBC\.

For questions about life as an international student, UBC’s **[International Student Advising Office](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/international-student-advising)** is an excellent resource\. Their team of Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants \(RCICs\) and Regulated International Student Immigration Advisors \(RISIAs\) is here to assist with immigration\-related inquiries and offer advice to enhance your overall UBC experience\.

We are here to support you every step of the way and look forward to seeing you flourish in our vibrant international community\!
