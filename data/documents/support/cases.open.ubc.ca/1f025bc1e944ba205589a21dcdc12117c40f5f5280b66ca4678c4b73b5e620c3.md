Thank you for your interest in the Open Case Studies Project, there are several ways to [get involved](https://cases.open.ubc.ca/get-involved/): use a case study in your course, comment on the case studies, create a new open case study or spread the word\! If you have any questions or would like to suggest another way to contribute, you can get in touch with the project using the form below\.

Please let us know if you’re using the case studies in a course, as we are trying to keep track of how the case studies are used\. If you would like to create a new case study to be included in the collection and used in your and others’ courses, we are always looking for more people to join us\!
