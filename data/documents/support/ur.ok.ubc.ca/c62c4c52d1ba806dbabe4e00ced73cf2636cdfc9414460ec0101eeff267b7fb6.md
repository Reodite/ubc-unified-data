Digital signage provides a way to promote short, simple messages about services, events, contests and other updates to campus audiences\.

## Service overview

University Relations uses Yodeck, UBC Okanagan’s primary digital signage platform, to manage content across public\-facing screens in major campus buildings\.

### Benefits

Digital signage provides a highly visible way to reach the UBCO community by:

-   capturing attention with engaging visuals,
-   sharing timely information quickly, and
-   increasing awareness of campus programs, events and initiatives\.

### Audiences

Current UBCO students are the primary audience for digital signage, with faculty and staff as secondary audiences\.

Submitted content must be relevant to one or more of these audiences\.

### Content display

Submitted content will normally be displayed for a maximum of two weeks\.

University Relations can also support geo\-targeted content on screens in specific buildings through unit\-developed Yodeck playlists\.

For support creating location\-based digital signage, [contact University Relations](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact-us/)\.

### Custom digital signage

Most digital signage is developed as part of branded campaigns for UBCO units and departments\.

For support creating custom digital signage, contact your unit communicator or Harrison Crerar at [harrison\.crerar@ubc\.ca](mailto:harrison.crerar@ubc.ca)\.

## Content submission guidelines

Submitted content is reviewed and launched on an ongoing basis\. **Please submit content at least three days before the proposed display date\.**

### Content specifications

Submitted content must be 1920 × 1080 pixels in landscape orientation, and use one of the following accepted formats:

-   \.pptx
-   \.jpg
-   \.png
-   video

**Note:** Video submissions must be no longer than 30 seconds and must not include audio\.

### Self\-serve templates

Self\-serve templates can be used for short\-notice events and notifications on UBCO’s digital signage\.

The templates incorporate required UBC Brand elements, fonts and colours in an easy\-to\-use, text\-based format\.

Please do not add images, alter content sections or otherwise modify the template structure\.

[Send your submissions now](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_9n6l8wv77gIP9bM)

[Download a self\-serve template](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/sites/13/2026/09/Digital-Screen-UBCO-Arial-2021.pptx)

### Permitted content

Only unit\-approved content will be considered for display and is subject to editorial review by University Relations\. Content type considered for display includes:

### 

Events

Upcoming UBCO events and activities may be promoted on digital screens\.

### 

Program or course promotion

Each faculty or school may promote one new course and one low\-enrolment course on digital screens at any given time\.

### 

Promotional campaigns and contests

Campaigns and contests that encourage students, faculty and staff to participate in UBCO initiatives can be promoted on digital screens\.

### 

Announcements and campus updates

University Relations coordinates campus\-wide announcements and updates displayed on digital screens\.

**Note:** Third\-party advertising is not permitted on the UBC Okanagan digital screens\. For more information, review [Board of Governors policy UP10](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/advertising-policy/)\.

#### QR codes

QR codes are permitted on digital screens and must be accompanied by a vanity URL\.

**Note**: Free, self\-serve QR code generators may expire\.

#### Vanity URLs

A vanity URL is a short, memorable web address created for marketing purposes \(e\.g\. [ok\.ubc\.ca/campusmap](https://ok.ubc.ca/campusmap)\)\.

To create a vanity URL, contact your unit administrator\.

## Specifications and best practices

Effective digital signage communicates quickly, often to audiences viewing screens from a distance or while moving through campus spaces\. Clear content and consistent design help messages stand out and remain easy to understand\.

### UBC Brand standards

All UBCO digital signage must:

-   adhere to the [Editorial style guide for UBC communicators](https://assets.brand.ubc.ca/downloads/ubc_editorial_guide.pdf) and [UBC visual identity guide](https://brand.ubc.ca/guidelines/visual-identity/);
-   use official UBC fonts and colours; and
-   apply UBC logos and brand signatures in accordance with brand guidelines\.

### Copy and readability

Unlike print or web, digital signage is viewed for a limited time and should be easy to read at a glance\.

-   Keep messages short and clear\.
-   Limit the amount of text on each slide\.
-   Use short, memorable URLs where appropriate\.

## Requesting digital signage equipment

Departments and units considering dedicated digital signage for their area\(s\) may request a consultation with University Relations about Yodeck and digital signage requirements\.

### Approval and installation

Digital signage installation requests require approval from University Relations, in partnership with UBC Okanagan IT and Infrastructure Development\.

Location, setup and costs are determined as part of the process\.

Once approved, UBCO IT will provide training on using Yodeck\.

### Location considerations

Unit\-specific installations may be permitted in a unit’s primary location or lobby and must use UBCO IT\-approved, enterprise\-grade displays designed for continuous operation\.

Common building spaces, including foyers and high\-traffic corridors, are reserved for University Relations\-managed screens\.
