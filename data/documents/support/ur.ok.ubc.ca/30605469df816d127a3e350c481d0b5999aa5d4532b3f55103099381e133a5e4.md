The Design Services team provides creative communications and strategic design expertise to strengthen UBC Okanagan’s brand and campus positioning\.

Communications Designers collaborate with Communications Managers, Strategists and Specialists in University Relations, as well as communicators within faculties, departments and administrative units, to deliver brand\-aligned design solutions\.

## Design expertise

Strategic, brand\-aligned design helps campaigns and initiatives communicate clearly, build recognition and create a consistent experience across channels\.

### Digital design

Creating engaging, user\-focused materials for digital platforms, including:

-   online advertising,
-   social media,
-   campus digital signage,
-   websites, and
-   email marketing\.

### Print design

Print design can complement digital communications and extend the awareness and reach of campaigns and initiatives\.

The Design team can support a range of print materials, including posters, brochures and postcards\.

### Temporary signage

Providing support for approved temporary signage, including:

-   design,
-   guidance on appropriate locations, and
-   coordination with approved suppliers for production, installation and removal\.

[Learn more about temporary interior signage](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/marketing-and-creative/design-services/temporary-interior-signage/)\.

### Visual identities

Developing visual identities for campaigns and initiatives that provide a distinctive look and feel while remaining aligned with UBC Brand\.

### Services not provided

The following services are outside the scope of Design Services:

-   Support for designs not created by University Relations, including printing support
-   Faculty\- and staff\-only requests
-   Permanent interior or exterior signage
-   Picture framing

#### Services requiring approval

The following services are available only with approval from the AVP, University Relations:

-   Reports
-   Photo shoots
-   Custom illustrations
-   Promotional items and swag, including stickers
-   Custom short\-term interior and exterior signage
-   Social media assets for channels other than @ubcokanagan
