The Digital Marketing team manages UBC Okanagan’s central social media channels and provides guidance to units across campus on their social media activities\.

Looking for resources to support your social channel? **[See our social media guidelines](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/marketing-and-creative/social-media/social-media-guidelines/)\.**

## UBC Okanagan's digital presence

The Digital Marketing team supports an engaging and consistent UBC Okanagan social media presence that aligns with the university’s brand\.

### Approach

Content is developed in collaboration with campus partners to share UBC Okanagan stories and connect with audiences across multiple platforms\.

Content shared on UBC Okanagan’s central social media channels must be relevant to broad audiences across campus and beyond\. Specialized or highly targeted messages may be better suited to other communication channels\.

The Digital Marketing team can provide guidance on alternative approaches and platforms for content intended for specific audiences\.

#### Social media support

The Digital Marketing team provides social media support to faculties and departments across campus, including:

-   planning and publishing content on central UBC Okanagan social media channels;
-   collaborating on storytelling, videos and campus features;
-   monitoring online conversations and responding to inquiries; and
-   providing guidance and best practices for units managing their own social media channels\.
