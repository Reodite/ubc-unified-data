The Digital Marketing team plans and manages digital advertising campaigns that help UBC Okanagan faculties and departments reach target audiences, build awareness and support key campus initiatives\.

## Digital advertising approach

Digital advertising campaigns are developed in collaboration with campus partners to identify campaign goals, audiences and appropriate digital channels\.

Advertising channels may include search engines, social media, online video and display advertising\.

The Digital Marketing team manages campaign:

-   setup;
-   monitoring; and
-   optimization\.

### Undergraduate program promotion

Undergraduate program promotion is managed separately by Recruitment, Marketing and Prospective Student Engagement \(RMPSE\)\.

For support promoting undergraduate programs, contact Diana Lowry, Associate Director of RMPSE, at [diana\.lowry@ubc\.ca](mailto:diana.lowry@ubc.ca)\.

## Digital advertising services

Digital advertising support is available to faculties and departments for:

-   promoting graduate programs and micro\-credentials;
-   supporting major events and strategic initiatives;
-   strengthening brand awareness; and
-   providing recommendations and guidance on advertising options\.

Want to learn more about digital campaigns? **[Connect with the Digital Marketing team](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact-us/)**\.
