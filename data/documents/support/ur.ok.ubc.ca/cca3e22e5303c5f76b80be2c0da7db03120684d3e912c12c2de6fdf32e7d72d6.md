Content marketing shares stories about the people, places and work that reflect UBC Okanagan’s unique location, strengths and impact\.

[Explore stories at news\.ok\.ubc\.ca](https://news.ok.ubc.ca/)

## Story formats

Through storytelling, University Relations supports year\-round promotional efforts across digital marketing, organic social media and web channels\.

### [All In Stories](http://ok.ubc.ca/all-in)

Designed to raise awareness of UBC Okanagan in select domestic and international markets by sharing stories about the people, research and initiatives creating opportunities and driving change at UBCO\.
