-   Accessibility and usability

-   August 20, 2026

Writing for a website differs from other channels in that it is being used by multiple users—people and crawlers \(search engines, AI\)\.

People often arrive to a website with a specific question/intention and scan the page for what they need; they rarely read through the content from top to bottom\.

Crawlers are using the structure of a webpage to identify pertinent pieces of information\. These tools often prioritize headings and the paragraph of content immediately after\.

When adapting existing content for the web, focus on making it easy for users to find, understand and action\.

## What is the user’s intention

-   What brought the user to this page?
-   What information do they need?
-   What action should they complete?
-   What content does not support that purpose?

Organize the page around the user’s needs rather than the structure or order of the original document\.

### Lead with the primary intention

Lead with the information or action users are most likely looking for\. Keep this initial bucket of information to the essentials, you can provide background and supporting details afterward\.

Avoid lengthy introductions that delay the information promised by the page title\.

## Make the page easy to scan

Break long content into meaningful sections with clear headings\. This allows users to quickly identify what is relevant to them\.

Use:

-   Descriptive headings
-   Short, focused paragraphs
-   Bulleted lists for related information
-   Numbered lists for sequential steps
-   Bold text sparingly for important words or phrases

### Use clear links and actions

Link text and button labels should describe where users are going or what they are doing\.

-   Use: Review admission requirements
-   Avoid: ‘click here’ or ‘learn more’

Link to the authoritative source when information is maintained elsewhere rather than copying it onto your page\.

### Remove document formatting

Content copied from Word, email or another website may carry formatting that conflicts with UBCO website styles\.

When moving existing content to Elementor:

-   Paste content as plain text when possible
    -   To paste text without formatting on a Windows, press Control \+ Shift \+ V
    -   To paste text without formatting on a Mac, press Command \+ Option \+ Shift \+ V
-   Alternatively, you can use the “paste as text” option in the Text Editor menu
-   Recreate headings, lists and links
-   Remove unnecessary fonts, colours and spacing
-   Check for manually typed bullets or numbers
