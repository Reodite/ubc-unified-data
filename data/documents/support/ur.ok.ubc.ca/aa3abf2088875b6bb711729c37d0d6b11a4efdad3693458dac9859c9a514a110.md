-   February 11, 2026

The Canada, BC, Okanagan Nation Alliance and UBC flags at UBC Okanagan, and the Canada flag \(at Flag Pole Plaza\) and the BC, Musqueam and UBC flags \(temporarily relocated to the East Mall Entrance and Plaza at the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre\) at UBC Vancouver are lowered until February 17, 2026, to mark the tragic deaths that occurred in Tumbler Ridge, BC on February 10, 2026\.

An [online book of condolences](https://submit.digital.gov.bc.ca/app/form/submit?f=5aadf492-bbc0-4685-b9ec-c7fdca617f97) is available for signing until end of day on Friday, February 20\.

For university community members impacted by this event and who are seeking support, please visit this site for a list of resources: [ubc\.ca/community\-supports](https://www.ubc.ca/community-supports/)\.
