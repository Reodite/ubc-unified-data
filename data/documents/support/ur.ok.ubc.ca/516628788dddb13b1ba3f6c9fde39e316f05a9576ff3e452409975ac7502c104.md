-   September 23, 2026

Flags at UBC Okanagan will be lowered on Tuesday, October 6, 2026, in memory of [Garth Evans](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/2026/08/18/in-memoriam-garth-evans/), a valued member of the UBC IT Okanagan team from January 2012 until his passing on August 3, 2026\. Our sincere condolences go to his family, friends and colleagues\.

A gathering in Garth’s memory will be held on October 6 at 2 pm in the Visualization and Emerging Media Studio, located in COM 207 in The Commons\. All are welcome to attend\.

UBC IT Okanagan has also created a [memorial page](https://rememberinggarth.ca/) where friends and colleagues can learn more about Garth and share their memories\.
