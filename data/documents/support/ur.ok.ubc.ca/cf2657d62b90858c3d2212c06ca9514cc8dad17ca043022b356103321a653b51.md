-   SEO \(search engine optimization\)

-   August 28, 2026

AI platforms crawl our web content to surface in their responses\. That means that our websites are a primary citation for questions related to UBC Okanagan\.

The same practices that help search engines discover and understand a webpage also help AI systems effectively reference our content\.

## How SEO supports AI\-powered search

Established search engine optimization \(SEO\) practices can help by making content:

-   Discoverable through clear site structure and internal links\.
-   Understandable through descriptive titles, headings and page content\.
-   Relevant through focused pages that answer a clear question or task\.
-   Reliable through accurate information, dates and authoritative sources\.
-   Accessible as meaningful page text rather than information contained only in images or files\.

**Please note**: Good SEO improves the conditions for visibility, but does not guarantee that an AI will use or cite a particular page\.

### Make information easy to understand and retrieve

Content that clearly answers a user’s question is easier for both people and AI\-powered searches to interpret\.

Where appropriate:

-   State important answers or information before lengthy background\.
-   Use specific page titles and descriptive headings\.
-   Identify relevant programs, units, locations or audiences clearly\.
-   Spell out UBC specific terms and acronyms on first use\.
-   Include dates, deadlines and eligibility details when they affect the answer\.
-   Link to the authoritative UBC source rather than duplicating information\.

Avoid vague introductory or promotional copy that delays useful information\.

### Keep authoritative information clear

AI\-generated responses may combine information from several sources\. Make it clear when UBC Okanagan is the authoritative source for information on your page\.

Identify the responsible program, service or unit when it provides useful context\. Link to policies, research or other primary sources when they support important information\.

Avoid maintaining different versions of the same information across several pages\. Keep one authoritative source where practical and link to it from related content\.

## Do not optimize specifically for AI

Terms such as answer engine optimization \(AEO\) and generative engine optimization \(GEO\) are sometimes used to describe efforts to increase visibility in AI\-generated answers\. These practices are evolving from—and do not replace—foundational SEO\.

Do not:

-   Repeat keywords or create content variations solely for AI\.
-   Add hidden text or prompts\.
-   Create unnecessary FAQ sections to target questions\.
-   Publish large amounts of lightly reviewed AI\-generated content\.
-   Add special AI files, structured data or custom code without approval\.
