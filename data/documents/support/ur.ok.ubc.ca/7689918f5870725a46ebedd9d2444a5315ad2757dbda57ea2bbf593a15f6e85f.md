-   Accessibility and usability \| SEO \(search engine optimization\)

-   August 20, 2026

People may discover UBC Okanagan information through websites, search results, AI\-generated summaries and chatbots\. UBCO websites are the primary source, so information should be accurate, current and easy to understand\.

Clear writing, logical structure, accessible content and descriptive links help people find and understand information while also helping search engines and AI systems interpret it\.

## Write for people

Search engines and AI recognize when a human is not writing content\. If they detect usage of AI they will not recognize a page as having authority—devaluing it’s usage in results\.

-   Build your website to give content a clear structure, not aesthetics
-   Use language your audience is likely to recognize
-   Pair important answers, deadlines, requirements or actions with clear headings
-   Provide additional context after the essential information
-   Avoid being overly descriptive or “salesy” in your content

Organize the page around what users need rather than what your unit wants to say about itself\.

## Structure content for readability

Clear headings, well\-structured lists and plain language help people scan and understand web content\. They also provide useful context and structure for search and AI systems interpreting a page\.

Use headings to organize content into meaningful sections, lists to present related or sequential information and clear, direct language throughout the page\.

### Give each page a clear focus

Use a specific page title that accurately describes the content\.

Keep related information together when users would reasonably expect to find it on the same page\. Consider a separate page when the topic, audience or task is substantially different\.

Avoid duplicating content across multiple pages\. When information belongs in one authoritative location, link to that page rather than copying the content elsewhere\.

### Use descriptive links and buttons

Link text should describe the destination or action\.

For example:

-   Use: Review application deadlines
-   Avoid: ‘Click here’ or ‘Learn more’
-   Keep button labels concise and specific\.

### Keep important information visible

Essential information should appear as text on the page rather than only within an image, graphic, animation, slider or other interactive element\.

Use accordions and tabs for supporting information when they make longer content easier to navigate, but do not hide the page’s primary answer or action inside them\.

### Do not add unapproved AI or SEO code

You do not need hidden keywords, special AI markup or custom code to make an Elementor page understandable to search or AI systems\. Contact your web team when technical SEO/GEO changes are required\.
