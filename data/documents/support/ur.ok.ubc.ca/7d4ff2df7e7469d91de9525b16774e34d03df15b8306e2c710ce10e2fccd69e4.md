The Exchange is UBC Okanagan’s faculty and staff newsletter and the source for the latest campus news, updates, opportunities and events\.

## Distribution schedule

The Exchange is distributed weekly by University Relations to UBCO faculty and staff every Wednesday\.

### Submission deadline

For inclusion in Wednesday’s newsletter, content must be submitted **by 12 noon on the preceding Friday**\.

[Send submission now](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_43pj9uR22WJOBee)

#### Remember to include:

-   A clear and appealing headline \(12 words maximum\)\.
-   A short description of your topic \(30 words maximum\)\.
-   A clear call to action \(e\.g\., “Learn more” or “Register now”\)\.
-   Optional: A high\-quality image \(\.jpg or \.png\) at 1000 × 600 pixels in landscape orientation\.
    -   **Note**: Keep added text to a minimum and do not include logos or branding elements\. The newsletter already includes the required UBC branding\.

**Late submissions will be included in the following week’s newsletter\.**

## Content best practices

### Headlines

Write action\-oriented headlines that are accessible and engaging \(12 words maximum\)\.

Headlines should stand on their own without supporting copy\. Clearly communicate the key message and how the audience will benefit\.

### Write for your audience

Write content from the perspective of faculty and staff who receive The Exchange\.

Highlight the aspects of an event or topic that are most valuable and relevant to the audience\.

### Body copy

Keep submissions short and use descriptive verbs\.

Summarize the message in 30 words or less, similar to a social media post\.

### Avoid duplication

If an update is worth sharing again, give it a fresh approach by highlighting a different perspective or new information\.

Repeated content generally receives fewer click\-throughs, so consider how the message can offer something new to the audience before resubmitting it\.

### Optimize your communications

The Exchange is one way to share information with UBC Okanagan faculty and staff\.

Many campus partners also have newsletters and other channels that can provide more targeted ways to reach specific audiences\. Other communication channels, including social media and digital signage, may also be appropriate\.

For guidance on selecting the appropriate internal communications channel, contact Chris Guenard at [chris\.guenard@ubc\.ca](mailto:chris.guenard@ubc.ca)\.
