-   March 25, 2026

The transgender flag will be flown in place of the UBC flag at both UBC Okanagan and UBC Vancouver on Tuesday, March 31, 2026, in observance of International Transgender Day of Visibility\.

Visit [events\.ubc\.ca/tdov](https://events.ubc.ca/tdov) for more information, upcoming events and relevant resources\. To learn more about the transgender flag and other Pride flags, please visit [equity\.ubc\.ca/pride\-flags](https://equity.ubc.ca/pride-flags/)\.

For additional details, please email [equity\.ubco@ubc\.ca](mailto:equity.ubco@ubc.ca) at UBCO and [info@equity\.ubc\.ca](mailto:info@equity.ubc.ca) at UBCV\.
