Temporary interior signage provides highly visible messaging for on\-campus audiences and may be installed for a **maximum of six weeks**\.

University Relations provides design and coordination support for approved temporary interior signage\.

**Signage installed for more than six weeks** is considered [permanent interior signage\.](https://operations.ok.ubc.ca/permanent-interior-signage)

## Requesting temporary interior signage

The Design team provides design support, guidance on appropriate locations and coordination with approved suppliers for production, installation and removal\.

Temporary interior signage requests follow an approval and planning process to support consistent, appropriate signage across the UBC Okanagan campus\.

### Custom design requests

Submit a [project brief](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/sites/13/2026/09/Custom-Design-Requests_Project-Brief.docx) to [Wiesje Stoneson](mailto:wiesje.stoneson@ubc.ca), Design Manager, to request custom temporary interior signage\.

The project brief **must include:**

-   photos of the preferred location\(s\),
-   signage measurements,
-   special instructions,
-   preferred installation date, and
-   removal date\.

#### Signage plan

Once a request is reviewed and approved, the Design team will develop a signage plan that includes:

-   a final design mockup,
-   location photos,
-   a campus map with identified locations,
-   special instructions,
-   installation date, and
-   removal date\.

[View an example signage plan](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/sites/13/2026/09/Example_Signage-Decal-Plan.pdf)

## Installing temporary interior signage

Temporary interior signage must be professionally installed and removed within six weeks by a Facilities Planning\-approved supplier or Facilities Management\.

### Installation requirements

#### Location

Signage may only be installed on surfaces where removal will not cause damage, including appropriate floors, windows, mirrors and stairs\.

Temporary signage must not be installed on painted walls, wood, tables or other surfaces that may be damaged during removal\.

#### Surface

Adhesive signage requires a hard, smooth surface to adhere and cure properly\.

For exterior\-facing windows, decal installation is not recommended between November and April because the surface temperature must be at least 5°C for the adhesive to cure\.

#### Material

Signage must use materials designed for easy removal, such as short\-term vinyl\.

Applicants are responsible for checking installed signage weekly to ensure it remains securely adhered and in good condition\.

#### Removal

The Design Services team coordinates signage removal according to the approved project timeline\.

Facilities Management can provide removal support at no additional charge\.
