Global and Admin News emails are used to distribute messages from UBC Okanagan’s senior leadership to designated campus audiences\.

[Read the latest Global and Admin News messages](https://news.ok.ubc.ca/global-and-admin-messages/)

## Global emails

Global emails are sent to UBCO faculty, staff and student employees to communicate time\-sensitive, campus\-wide information\.

Recognizing delegated authority from the Principal, Global emails must originate at the request of members of the Okanagan campus executive\.

### Protocols, requirements and guidelines

### 

Ownership, approvals and requirements

#### Ownership and approval

Global emails are owned by the Okanagan campus executive, with University Relations coordinating the channel and advising on message content and timing\.

-   **Requests must be submitted at least five days before the distribution date and approved by a member of the Okanagan campus executive\.**
-   Signatories must be members of the executive, except for messages related to electoral announcements, campus safety updates, emergency planning or construction disruptions\.

#### Content requirements

-   Global emails should use a conversational style and tone and follow the [Editorial Guide for UBC Communicators](http://assets.brand.ubc.ca/downloads/ubc_editorial_guide.pdf), [Indigenous Peoples Language Guidelines](https://assets.brand.ubc.ca/downloads/UBC_Indigenous_Peoples_Language_Guide_2024.pdf) and [UBC Voice and Tone Guide](https://assets.brand.ubc.ca/downloads/ubc_voice_and_tone_guide.pdf)\.
-   If images are included, consult the [UBC Visual Identity Rules](https://assets.brand.ubc.ca/downloads/ubc_brand_identity_rules.pdf)\.

#### Repeat messages and timing

Requestors are encouraged to consider all available communication tools before requesting more than one Global email on the same topic, event or development\.

-   In extenuating circumstances, a second Global email may be permitted, but the content must include a significant update\.
-   Related communications, including items in The Exchange, must be scheduled at least five working days apart\.

### 

Distribution schedule

Global emails are normally issued Monday to Thursday, with a maximum of one email permitted each  day\.

### 

Criteria

#### Timeliness

The information must be timely and must have significant importance to UBC Okanagan faculty, staff or students, and/or the operations of the campus\.

#### Approval

The message must have approval from, and be attributed to:

-   the Principal and Deputy Vice\-Chancellor,
-   a Vice\-President \(VP\), Vice\-Principal or Associate Vice\-President \(AVP\), or
-   the Executive Director of Human Resources\.

#### Relevance

Global emails must communicate time\-sensitive, high profile or campus\-wide topics\.

#### Adherence to existing policies

All existing UBC policies governing communication shall be applicable to Global emails\. See in particular:

-   [Policy \#96: Communications](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/communications-policy/) 
-   [Policy \#104: Acceptable Use and Security of UBC Electronic Information and Systems](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/information-systems-policy/)\.

The text of these policies is available on the [University Counsel website](http://www.universitycounsel.ubc.ca/)\.

### 

Audiences

Global emails are distributed to the UBC FASmail addresses listed in Workday for all UBC Okanagan faculty, staff and student employees\.

### 

Topics

Global emails are reserved for significant, campus\-wide topics, including:

-   Large\-scale announcements, such as UBCO’s downtown Kelowna presence
-   High\-profile or campus\-wide topics, such as COVID\-19, TRC commitments or systemic racism policies
-   Executive appointments or changes at the Vice\-President level
-   Senior\-level appointments or changes, including Deans, Associate Vice\-Presidents, Executive Directors or Senior Advisors to the Principal
-   Organizational changes affecting significant numbers of students, faculty or staff
-   Urgent campus safety or health updates, emergency planning or campus disruptions
-   Messages from the Principal or President
-   Large\-scale consultations and initiatives championed by a member of the Okanagan campus executive

### 

Limitations

Because not all UBC faculty and staff regularly check the email address listed in Workday, Global emails cannot guarantee reach across the entire UBC Okanagan community\.

### 

Emergency messages

An emergency Global email is a message that is of immediate, critical importance to the health and safety or wellbeing of the campus community\. 

This can include campus\-wide alerts regarding snow closures, water quality, wide\-area power/systems outages, or natural disasters\.

Global emails are only used as a supplementary channel to support [UBC ALERT](https://operations.ok.ubc.ca/security-and-emergencies/ubc-safeapp-and-ubc-alert/) in the event of an emergency\.

## Admin News emails

Admin News emails are sent to supervisors within academic and administrative units and members of the Okanagan Campus Communicators Network\.

These emails are used to engage and inform supervisors across the Okanagan campus\.

### Protocols, requirements and guidelines

### 

Ownership, approvals and requirements

Admin News emails are owned by the email signatory or the head of the department identified\. University Relations acts as the steward/facilitator of the channel\.

**All messages must be requested at least five working days before the distribution date, and be approved by a member of the Okanagan campus executive, a dean or director\.**

University Relations adopts the role of advisor around message content and timing\.

### 

Distribution schedule

Admin News emails are normally issued Monday to Thursday, with a maximum of one email permitted each  day\.

However, an Admin News email can be issued on the same day as a Global email\.

### 

Criteria

#### Timeliness

The information must be timely and must have significant importance for the majority of individuals within the intended audience and/or the operations of the campus\.

#### Approval

The message must have approval from a member of the Okanagan campus executive, dean, director, manager or head of an academic or administrative unit\.

#### Relevance

Admin News emails must communicate time\-sensitive information that engages broader leadership on campus\.

#### Adherence to existing policies

All existing UBC policies governing communication shall be applicable to Admin News emails\. See in particular:

-   [Policy \#96: Communications](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/communications-policy/)  
-   [Policy \#104: Acceptable Use and Security of UBC Electronic Information and Systems](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/board-of-governors-policies-procedures-rules-and-guidelines/policies/)\.

The text of these policies is available on the [University Counsel website](http://www.universitycounsel.ubc.ca/)\.

### 

Audiences

Admin News emails are sent to supervisors within an academic or administrative unit, and members of the Okanagan Campus Communicators Network\.

### 

Topics

Admin News emails are reserved for:

-   Advance notice of university\-wide initiatives or organizational changes that will be communicated to the broader UBC Okanagan campus community\.
-   Updates on previous institutional messaging/invitations for feedback\.
-   Large\-scale project updates/developments\.
-   Large\-scale consultations and updates\.
-   New tools or services for departments or individual faculty and staff\.
-   Unplanned or short\-notice service outages or interruptions\.

### Contact

Questions about Global and Admin News emails can be directed to **Nathan Skolski**, Director of Public Affairs, at [nathan\.skolski@ubc\.ca](mailto:nathan.skolski@ubc.ca)\.

## Other internal communication tools

A range of internal communication tools is available for sharing university\-related information with the UBC Okanagan community\.

Non\-emergency information can be shared through channels including:

-   [The Exchange](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-marketing/exchange/): Faculty and staff newsletter\.
-   [Digital signage](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-marketing/digital-signage/): Campus information displayed on screens in buildings across UBCO\.
-   [Events\.ok\.ubc\.ca](https://events.ok.ubc.ca/): Online calendar for UBCO events\.

Other internal communication channels include departmental newsletters, posters and targeted emails\.
