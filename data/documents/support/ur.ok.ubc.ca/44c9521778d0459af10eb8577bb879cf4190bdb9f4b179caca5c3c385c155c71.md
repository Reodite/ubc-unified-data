-   April 24, 2026

The Canada, BC, Okanagan Nation Alliance and UBC flags at UBC Okanagan, and the Canada flag \(at Flag Pole Plaza\) and the BC, Musqueam and UBC flags \(at the East Mall Entrance and Plaza at the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre\) at UBC Vancouver will be lowered on Tuesday, April 28, 2026, in recognition of the [National Day of Mourning](https://www.ccohs.ca/events/mourning/), to remember workers who have died, were injured or become ill from their job; and commit to protecting workers and preventing further workplace tragedies\.
