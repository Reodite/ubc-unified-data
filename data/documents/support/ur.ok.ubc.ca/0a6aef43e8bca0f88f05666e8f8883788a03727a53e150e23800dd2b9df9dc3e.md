-   July 2, 2026

Flags at UBC Okanagan will be lowered on Friday, July 3, 2026, in memory of [Dr\. Tom Siddon, LL\.D\.](https://www.castanet.net/obituaries/SIDDON%2C_Tom/37385/) who passed away on June 28, in Kelowna, BC\.

Dr\. Siddon received [an honorary degree from the Okanagan campus in 2007](https://news.ok.ubc.ca/2007/04/24/distinguished-former-federal-minister-to-receive-honorary-degree-at-ubc-okanagan-congregation/)\. Our sincere condolences to Dr\. Siddon’s family, friends and colleagues\.
