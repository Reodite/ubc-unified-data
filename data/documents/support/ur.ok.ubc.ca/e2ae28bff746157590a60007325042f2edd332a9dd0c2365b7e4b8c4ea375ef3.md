Campus banner poles provide highly visible outdoor spaces for communicating UBC messages to the campus community and visitors\.

## **Protocol**

UBC Okanagan’s banner pole protocol guides the use of banner poles due to their:

-   high visibility,
-   limited availability, and
-   role in animating the university’s public spaces\.

[UBC banners hanging along an tree\-lined campus street](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/sites/13/2026/07/ur-home-page-mobile-300x300.jpg)

### **Scheduled campaign periods**

Banner poles are scheduled for campaigns during the following periods:

-   First two weeks of June
-   Between September 1 and November 15
-   Between December 1 and March 1

**  
Note**: these dates may change and are weather\-dependent\.

#### Questions

Contact [University Relations](mailto:university.relations@ubc.ca) if you have questions regarding pole banners\.
