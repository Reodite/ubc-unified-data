-   Using images and media

-   August 19, 2026

Choosing the correct image sizes helps them remain clear, load quickly and display consistently across components and devices\.

When preparing images for UBC Okanagan websites, use appropriately sized and optimized images to improve website performance, accessibility and visual quality\.

## Recommended dimensions

1000 × 600 pixels \(5:3\)

*The recommended size for most images throughout the website\.*  
Example uses: Image and Image Carousel elements and images displayed alongside text\.

1000 × 1000 pixels \(1:1\)

Example uses: Story Tile container, Flip Card \(Image\) container and other elements designed for square images\.

1920 × 780 pixels \(32:13\)

Use for full\-width feature areas and container backgrounds\. Keep important content away from the edges, as images may be cropped on different screen sizes\.

780 × 780 pixels \(1:1\)

Use as a separate mobile image when a wide feature image does not display well on smaller screens\. A separate mobile image is not always required\.

1000 × 1250 pixels \(4:5\)

Example uses: Profile photos, contact pages and other portrait layouts\.

### Before uploading an image

-   Export image as the intended pixels, not just the ratio \(large images reduce page load times\)
-   Ensure image is a \.jpg or \.png
-   Do not enlarge small images, as this reduces image quality
-   Avoid placing important text inside images

### Tools for cropping an image

-   Adobe Photoshop
-   Adobe Express
-   Canva
-   Windows Photos

### Where to find images

-   [UBC Okanagan Mediavalet library](https://ubc.mediavalet.com/v5/browse/categories/most_viewed)
-   [UBC Okanagan Heat Flickr library](https://www.flickr.com/photos/ubcoheat/)
-   [UBC Brand photography library](https://brand.ubc.ca/guidelines/downloads/photography-library/)
-   [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/)

Before using an external stock image, review its licence and ensure it is appropriate for your intended use\.
