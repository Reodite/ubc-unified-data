-   Accessibility and usability \| Navigation and menus

-   August 20, 2026

A clear page title helps users quickly understand what a page is about and decide whether they are in the right place\. It also helps users recognize pages in navigation, browser tabs and search results\.

Write page titles for the user rather than the unit that owns the content\. Use familiar, specific language that describes the page’s topic or purpose\.

## Be concise but descriptive

A page title should tell users what information they will find or what they can do on the page in a few words\. If it is difficult to describe the page clearly in a title, consider whether the page is trying to serve too many purposes\.

**Use a topic\-based title for informational content\.**

-   Use: Tuition and student fees
-   Avoid: Important information

**Use an action\-oriented title when the page helps users complete a task\.**

-   Use: Apply for graduate studies
-   Avoid: Apply now

### Use familiar language

Choose words that users are likely to recognize and search for\. Avoid internal terminology, unexplained acronyms and broad labels that could describe many types of content\.

Use official program, unit or service names when users need them to identify the content\. Add plain\-language context when an official name may not be familiar to the intended audience\.

-   Use: Financial aid resources
-   Avoid: Resources

## Make each title distinct

Each page should have a title that distinguishes it from other pages on the website\. Do not add a unit or department name automatically\. Include it when it helps users distinguish the page from similar content\.

-   Use: Contact graduate admissions
-   Avoid: Contact us

### Keep related titles consistent

A webpage may have a page title, visible main heading, browser or search title and a shorter navigation label\. These do not always need to be identical, but they should clearly refer to the same content\.

For example:

-   Page title: Undergraduate admission requirements
-   Navigation label: Admission requirements
