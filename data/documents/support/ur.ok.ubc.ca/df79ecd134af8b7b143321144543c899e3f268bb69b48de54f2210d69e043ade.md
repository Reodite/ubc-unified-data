The Duke and Duchess of Cambridge visited UBC Okanagan on Tuesday, September 27 as part of their 2016 tour of Canada\.

[Duke and Duchess of Cambridge visiting UBC Okanagan](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/sites/13/2026/09/2016-Royals-Visit-1000.jpg)

## Visit highlights

### Public ceremony

The visit included a public ceremony in the Central Courtyard dedicating a new Aboriginal art installation commemorating the University of British Columbia’s centennial and the 10th anniversary of the Okanagan campus\.

### Meeting UBC students 

The Royal couple also met with students in the gymnasium, where they joined approximately 1,500 UBC Okanagan students to watch an inter\-squad game with the UBC Okanagan Heat women’s volleyball team\.
