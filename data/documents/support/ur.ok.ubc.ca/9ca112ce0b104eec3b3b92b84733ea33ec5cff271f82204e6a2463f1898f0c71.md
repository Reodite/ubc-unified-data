No\. Generally speaking, you should not need to update your website during an emergency\.

During an emergency, [UBC Okanagan’s official emergency communications](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-engagement/crisis-communications/) should be the single, authoritative source for information\.

## Follow official emergency communications

University Relations works with Campus Operations and Risk Management to distribute critical communications during campus emergencies\.

**Official information will be shared through:**

-   the UBC Safe app,
-   the UBC Alert notifications,
-   the Campus Alerts webpage, and
-   UBC Okanagan’s Instagram account\.

Do not use your website to independently report, interpret, reiterate or expand on emergency messaging\.
