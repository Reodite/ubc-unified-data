University Relations manages and supports a range of internal communication channels to share timely, relevant information across UBC Okanagan\.

### [The Exchange](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-engagement/internal-communications/the-exchange/)

Sharing campus news, updates and events with UBCO faculty and staff\.

### [Digital Signage](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-engagement/internal-communications/digital-signage/)

Promoting visual messages on public\-facing screens across campus\.

### [Campus Banner Poles](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-engagement/internal-communications/campus-banner-poles/)

Displaying UBCO messages on highly visible outdoor banners\.

### [Global and Admin News](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-engagement/internal-communications/global-and-admin-news-emails/)

Distributing communications from UBCO’s senior leadership\.
