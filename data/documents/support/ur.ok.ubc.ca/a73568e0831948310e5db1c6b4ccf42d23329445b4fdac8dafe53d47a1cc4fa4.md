Social channels provide an opportunity to advance UBC’s mandate to disseminate knowledge, engage with communities and support dialogue and academic freedom\. Social media activity can also affect individual, professional and institutional reputations\.

## Social Media Platforms

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube are among the social media platforms UBC employees may use to connect with audiences\.

### Creating a social media account

Before creating a new UBC\-affiliated social media account, consider the time and resources required to maintain the account and build an engaged audience\.

Where appropriate, collaborating with established UBC Okanagan social media channels may provide a more effective way to reach the intended audience\.

For comprehensive social media guidance for UBC employees, review the [UBC Brand website](https://brand.ubc.ca/guidelines/social-media/)\.

#### Inappropriate use of social media

Inappropriate use of social media, in either an official or personal capacity, may result in disciplinary action\.

Employees who are unsure whether a communication is appropriate should consult their manager, head or director and review [UBC’s Key Considerations for Social Media](https://brand.ubc.ca/guidelines/social-media/) before posting\.
