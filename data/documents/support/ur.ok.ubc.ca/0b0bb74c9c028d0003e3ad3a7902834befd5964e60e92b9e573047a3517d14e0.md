-   May 25, 2026

In observance of Pride Month, UBC will fly the Intersex\-Inclusive Pride flag in place of the UBC flag at both UBC Okanagan and UBC Vancouver from June 1 to June 30, 2026\. On the Vancouver campus, the BC, Musqueam and Pride flags are temporarily relocated to the [East Mall Entrance and Plaza at the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Irving+K+Barber+Learning+Centre,+1961+E+Mall,+Vancouver,+BC+V6T+1Z1/@49.2677672,-123.2521322,3a,75y,248.04h,90t/data=!3m7!1e1!3m5!1su41VhDJEtxhQUCbOotEmzw!2e0!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fpanoid%3Du41VhDJEtxhQUCbOotEmzw%26cb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile.gps%26w%3D203%26h%3D100%26yaw%3D248.03864%26pitch%3D0%26thumbfov%3D100!7i13312!8i6656!4m7!3m6!1s0x548672b6c27e6061:0x3441137d713f82af!8m2!3d49.2675321!4d-123.2526827!14m1!1BCgIgARICCAI)\.
