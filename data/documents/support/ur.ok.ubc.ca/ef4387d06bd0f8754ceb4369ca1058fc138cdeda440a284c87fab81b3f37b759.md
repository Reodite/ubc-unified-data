Resources are available to support event planning at UBC Okanagan, including leadership participation, event space and planning guidance\.

### [Ceremonial &amp; High\-Profile Events](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-engagement/event-guidance-and-resources/ceremonial-and-high-profile-events/)

Access guidance and resources for planning formal, university\-wide or high\-profile events\.

### President participation

Requests for the UBC President to speak at or participate in an event can be submitted through the [Office of the President](https://president.ubc.ca/contact/)\.

### DVC participation

Requests for the Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal to speak at or participate in an event can be submitted through the [DVC Participation Request Form](https://principal.ok.ubc.ca/about-the-office/office-staff-contact/)\.

### Event space

Event policies, procedures and resources for finding and booking space at UBC Okanagan are available through [bookspace\.ok\.ubc\.ca](https://bookspace.ok.ubc.ca/)\.

### Event planning checklist

The [event planning checklist](http://ur-new.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/219/2025/12/UR-EventsForm-OnlineFillable-FINAL.pdf) provides guidance to help identify and plan key event details\.
