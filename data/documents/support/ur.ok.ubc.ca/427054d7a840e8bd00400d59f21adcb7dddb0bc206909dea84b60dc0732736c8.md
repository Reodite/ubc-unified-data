Media relations connects journalists and the public with UBC Okanagan’s research and expertise to help explain what is happening, why it matters and how it informs public conversations\.

[Explore stories for media at news\.ok\.ubc\.ca](https://news.ok.ubc.ca/about/stories-for-media/)

## Resources for media

The Public Affairs team connects journalists with evidence\-based research, clear information and UBC experts who can provide context on current and emerging issues\.

### Media support 

Support is available to local, national and international media to help facilitate accurate reporting on UBCO research, priorities and impact\.

Media support can include:

-   background briefings,
-   interviews,
-   data and research,
-   expert commentary, and
-   context on current and emerging issues\.

### Find UBC experts

The [UBC Experts Guide](https://news.ubc.ca/ubc-experts-guide/) is a searchable database that helps journalists find UBC experts available to provide commentary and context on a range of topics\.

**Note:** Not all UBC experts are listed in the database\.

For assistance finding an expert, including experts at the Okanagan campus, [contact the Public Affairs team](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact-us/)\.

#### Stay informed

-   For UBC Okanagan news, contact [mediarelations\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:mediarelations.ok@ubc.ca)\.
-   For UBC news, subscribe to [daily and weekly digests from UBC](https://news.ubc.ca/subscribe-and-receive-ubc-news-by-e-mail/)\.

## Services for faculty and researchers

The Public Affairs team works with faculty communicators and researchers to bring academic expertise into public conversations and identify opportunities to share research with the media\.

### Research impact and visibility

Media coverage can increase awareness of research, strengthen researcher profiles and demonstrate the value of academic work to partners, donors, funding agencies and the public\.

It can also help broader audiences understand how research contributes to addressing important issues\.

#### Media guidance

Faculty and researchers can [contact the Public Affairs team](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact-us/) for guidance on:

-   identifying the news potential of research or projects,
-   communicating research findings to general audiences,
-   identifying timely or relevant story angles,
-   preparing for media interviews, and
-   sharing research with local, national and international media\.
