-   Copywriting \| SEO \(search engine optimization\)

-   August 28, 2026

Keywords are the words and phrases people use when searching for information\. They can help identify how users describe a topic, service or task and inform the language used on a webpage\.

In today’s SEO and AI environment, matching keywords or phrases is not the key to success\. These tools have evolved to the point where they can understand the intention behind a search\. This means we can use keywords to inform our content while still allowing for natural language and meeting users with terms they understand\.

## How to find keywords

Free online resources can help you understand terms used by your audience

-   [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/)
-   [AlsoAsked](https://trends.google.com/)

You can also use Google Search Console to see what terms are currently leading users to certain pages\. [Connect with UR](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/contact/) to learn more\.

## Use the language your audience uses

Use natural language that accurately describes UBC Okanagan content and services\.

Useful sources of audience language may include:

-   Questions received by service teams\.
-   Terminology used in forms, emails and support requests\.
-   Site search data, when available\.
-   Authoritative UBC pages and program materials\.
-   User feedback and research\.

Search data can identify useful wording, but it should not override accuracy, [UBC editorial guidance](https://brand.ubc.ca/guidelines/) or respectful and inclusive language\.

### Use important terms where they add meaning

Search engines and AI platforms crawl websites for content\. They prioritize pages titles, headings and the paragraph immediately following either of these elements\. This makes those areas key for detailing a user’s intention with keywords\.

Use the main topic and related terms naturally in the content, including:

-   page titles,
-   introductions,
-   section headings,
-   body content, and
-   links and buttons\.

**Note**: Links, buttons and alternative text have specific purposes\. Write them for users and accessibility rather than trying to add search terms\.

### Avoid keyword stuffing

Keyword stuffing is the unnatural repetition of words, phrases or variations in an attempt to influence search results\.

Watch for:

-   The same phrase repeated several times in a short section\.
-   Headings that repeat the page title without adding meaning\.
-   Lists of locations, programs or services added without useful context\.
-   Awkward sentences created to include a particular phrase\.
