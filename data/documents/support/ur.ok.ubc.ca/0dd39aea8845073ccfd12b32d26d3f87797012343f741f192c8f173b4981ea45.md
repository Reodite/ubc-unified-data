University Relations helps to ensure UBC Okanagan’s voice is clear, consistent and heard across all audiences\.

### [Internal Communications](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-engagement/internal-communications/)

Connecting faculty and staff with campus news and updates through internal communication channels\.

### [Crisis Communications](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-engagement/crisis-communications/)

Coordinating critical communications during emergencies and incidents affecting the UBCO community\.

### [Media Relations](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-engagement/media-relations/)

Connecting UBCO expertise, research and stories with local, national and international media\.

### [Event Guidance and Resources](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-engagement/event-guidance-and-resources/)

Providing guidance and resources to support effective event planning at UBCO\.
