Protocol, Ceremonies and Events advances UBC Okanagan’s strategic goals for engagement by producing key university ceremonial and high\-profile events including convocation, VIP, recognition and strategic events\.

The team also provides protocol guidance for ceremonial flags, order of precedence, speaking order and seating order\.

### Convocation

Protocol, Ceremonies and Events leads the planning for the annual spring convocation ceremonies with the support of partner units and volunteers\.

These ceremonies include the conferring of academic and honorary degrees, and feature the procession of graduands, faculty and dignitaries\. Learn more about [UBC Okanagan Graduation](https://graduation.ok.ubc.ca/)\.

## High\-profile events

Visits by high\-ranking officials to UBC Okanagan are typically coordinated with University Relations through the Office of the President, the Office of the Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal or the BC Protocol Office\.

### Invitation and planning

Faculties and departments may wish to invite dignitaries, VIPs or other high\-profile guests to UBC Okanagan\.

**Before extending an invitation**, [notify Protocol, Ceremonies and Events](mailto:okanagan.events@ubc.ca) for guidance on invitations, event arrangements, university protocol and the appropriate offices to involve in planning\.

UBC Okanagan protocols help ensure VIP visits are planned and delivered professionally, respectfully and safely\.

## Faculty and staff recognition

In collaboration with partner units, Protocol, Ceremonies and Events organizes a suite of annual events to celebrate the achievements of faculty and staff through awards and recognition programs and to foster community spirit and connection with colleagues\.

The event line\-up includes:

-   Provost’s Teaching Excellence and Innovation Awards
-   UBC Okanagan’s Welcome Back Faculty &amp; Staff Social
-   Length of Service Recognition and Principal’s Staff Awards
-   UBC Okanagan’s Faculty and Staff Holiday Open House

Learn more about UBC Okanagan’s [Faculty and Staff Recognition programs](https://hr.ok.ubc.ca/learning/awards-and-recognition/)\.

## Strategic events

In support of UBC Okanagan’s priority of transformative engagement, Protocol, Ceremonies and Events supports two strategic events designed to deepen community connections, foster dialogue on timely and critical topics, and cultivate meaningful cultural experiences:

-   [UBC Okanagan Debates Series \(Spring and Fall\)](https://ok.ubc.ca/ubc-okanagan-debates/)
-   Opera Under the Stars hosted by Opera Kelowna \(June/July\)

**Questions?** Contact [okanagan\.events@ubc\.ca](mailto:okanagan.events@ubc.ca)\.
