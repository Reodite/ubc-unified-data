## Resources

The UBC Women's Centre strives to provide a space for community\-building, discussion, sharing, and resistance\. We offer a safe space on campus with couches, brochures \(with information about resources\), event posters, menstrual products, safer sex products, pregnancy tests, a library of feminist literature, microwave, food, a kettle, and more\.
