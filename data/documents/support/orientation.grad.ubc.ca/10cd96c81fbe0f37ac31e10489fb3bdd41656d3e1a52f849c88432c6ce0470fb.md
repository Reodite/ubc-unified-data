### Are the pre\-arrival webinar events recorded? Where can I find them?

International student info session pre\-arrival webinars are not recorded due how frequently information changes throughout the year\. However, they are offered frequently, take a look at our [events page](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/events/) to sign up for the next available session\. If you are unable to attend any of the sessions, all the information is available in the [iPrep course](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/71186)\. Please note, you must first be [enrolled in iPrep](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/77NDCK) to access the resources\.

All other pre\-arrival webinars are recorded and available on the [resources page](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/resources/), under Preparing for Arrival/pre\-arrival webinar resources &amp; recording\.

### Do I need to register for orientation events?

Many GradStart Welcome events, whether in\-person or online/livestreamed, require registration for planning and event access purposes\. 

Please see the [schedule of events](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/events/) for event\-specific information, including how to register\.

### Can family members or guests attend orientation?

Most orientation events are for incoming graduate students only and space is limited at events\. We ask only graduate students participate in GradStart events\.  

One exception is graduate students with infants or young children for Main Welcome Day events\. There is a child\-friendly space where events will be livestreamed and you can meet other student parents\.  For questions, please email [graduate\.orientation@ubc\.ca](mailto:graduate.orientation@ubc.ca)

There is also a family day event each September which is specific to families with children\.

### Where can I find support and information for students who are parents

There are many [resources](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/equity-diversity-inclusion-resources/resources-who-are-parents/) for students who are parents\.  

To meet other students who are parents, you could also post to the [community forums](https://community.grad.ubc.ca/forum) for families or on making friends\.

### I have questions related to UBC on\-campus housing\. Who should I contact?

The [Student Housing website](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/contact-us/) provides further details and contact information\. If you have applied for graduate student housing with [St\. John’s College](https://stjohns.ubc.ca/about-us/contact/?login) or [Green College](https://greencollege.ubc.ca/contact-us), please follow up with the college directly\. Learn more about our [residential graduate colleges](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/residential-graduate-colleges)\.

The online [Student Housing and Community Services](https://secure.housing.ubc.ca/) may also be a helpful resource to check the status of your application\.

There is additional information about [life in Vancouver](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/life-vancouver) or our [UBC Vancouver campus](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/vancouver-campus) on our website\. We host several Living in Vancouver pre\-arrival webinars throughout the year that go over information about how to find housing, what to look out for, and more\. Check our [events listing](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/events/) to find the next session\.

### I have questions related to off\-campus housing or market rentals\. Who should I contact?

There is information about [life in Vancouver](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/life-vancouver) or our [UBC Vancouver campus](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/vancouver-campus) on our website\. UBC housing also provides information on how to find [off\-campus housing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/other-housing/off-campus-housing/)\.

We host several Living in Vancouver pre\-arrival webinars throughout the year that go over information about how to find housing, what to look out for, and more\. There is also a session by the [Tenant Resource &amp; Advisory Centre](https://tenants.bc.ca/your-tenancy/finding-rental-housing/)\. Check our [events listing](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/events/) to find the next sessions\.  Session recordings and handouts are available on the [resources page](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/resources/), under Preparing for Arrival/pre\-arrival webinar resources &amp; recording\.

### I have housing starting Sept 1st but would like to move in early to get settled in Vancouver and attend orientation events\. Who do I contact?

Early arrival is typically not permitted for UBC on\-campus housing\.  For more information about early arrival options check out [UBC housing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/early-arrival/) \.

For off campus rentals, please email your landlord to inquire about potential for early move in\.  Another option is finding [short\-term accommodation](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/other-housing/off-campus-housing/#short-term-accommodation)\.

### I have been admitted to UBC and have to submit my official transcript to clear my admission condition\. Who do I contact?

Please submit an enquiry to [Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/contact-us)\.

### I have questions about needing, applying for, and using a UBCcard

For all questions related to UBCcard, please visit their [FAQ page](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/about-the-ubccard/faq)\.

### Who do I contact for questions about tuition, deposits, and funding?

You can view tuition information on our [degree program listing page](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/graduate-degree-programs)\. For information regarding payment, payment scheduling, cost breakdown, etc\. contact [Enrolment Services](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors)\. The [scholarships, awards, and funding website](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/scholarships-awards-funding) may also be a helpful resource\.

Deposits are managed by your program, you can contact your program office by looking for your program on the [degree program listing](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/graduate-degree-programs)\.

For questions about needs\-based funding \(including loans, government loans, etc\.\), check the [students loans page](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/student-loans) or contact [Enrolment Services](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors)\.

For questions about merit\-based funding \(including scholarships, fellowships, awards, etc\.\) check the [awards page](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/scholarships-awards-funding) or contact the [Graduate Awards team](mailto:Graduate.awards@ubc.ca)\.

We host several webinars that will walk you through information about your fellowship or scholarship applications, and other funding needs\. View our [events listing](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/events/) to find the appropriate session for you\.

### I'm an international student and I have questions about visas/ study permits/ work permits\. Who do I contact?

Please visit the [International Student Guide](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide) for information on immigration resources, immigration document support, and International student advising\.  

We host several international student info sessions throughout the year\. Take a look at our [events page](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/events/) to sign up for the next available session\. If you are unable to attend any of the sessions, all the information is available in the [iPrep course](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/71186)\. Please note, you must first be [enrolled in the iPrep](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/77NDCK) to access the resources\.

If you have any further questions, please contact [International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/international-student-advising)\.
