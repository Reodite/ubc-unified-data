#### [Welcome to UBC](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/2022/welcome-to-ubc/)

We are excited to welcome new graduate students to our campus and are enthusiastic about you becoming part of our community\.

### Starting your program in September 2026?

Mark your calendar for GradStart Welcome on Thursday, Sept 3, 2026\. Pre\-arrival webinars begin in March and include Living in Vancouver with Q&amp;A with current grad students, sessions for international students, and more\. Visit the [Events page](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/events/) for details\. A full schedule of events for September will be posted by end of July\.

### **Connect with Fellow Graduate Students**

Visit the [Graduate Student Community](https://community.grad.ubc.ca/) [\(community\.grad\.ubc\.ca\)](https://community.grad.ubc.ca/) to connect with fellow graduate students\.

Graduate student orientation welcome events take place each year in January and in August/September\. The largest Orientation event happens every year just prior to the Labour Day weekend and is a week full of activities and events welcoming you to UBC and Vancouver\. These events are brought to you by [Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/), the [Graduate Student Society](http://gss.ubc.ca/) and [International Student Development](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/international-student-development)\. Stay tuned for details on our next event\!
