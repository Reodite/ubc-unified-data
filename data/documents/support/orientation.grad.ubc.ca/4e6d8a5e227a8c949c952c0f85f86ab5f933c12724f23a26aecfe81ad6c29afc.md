### New Student Checklist

New to UBC and want to make sure you’re set up for success? Use our interactive [New student checklist](https://community.grad.ubc.ca/checklists/new-student-checklist) on our [Graduate student community](https://community.grad.ubc.ca/) site to keep track of everything you need to feel confident about starting out your journey in grad studies\.

### Finding Accommodation

Check out our comprehensive [guide](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/life-vancouver/housing) to finding both on and off\-campus housing, including costs, neighbourhoods, and more\.
