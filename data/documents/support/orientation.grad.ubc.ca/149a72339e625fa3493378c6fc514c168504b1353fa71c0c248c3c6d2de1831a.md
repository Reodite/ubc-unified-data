#### Event Details

-   **Start:** 21 September 2026 12:00 pm
-   **End:** 21 September 2026 2:00 pm
-   **Categories:** [Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies](https://orientation.grad.ubc.ca/events/category/graduate-and-postdoctoral-studies/)

---

Join us for a welcome lunch to connect with fellow Indigenous graduate students from across UBC, build community, and kick off the academic year together\. Whether you’re new to UBC or returning, this is a chance to meet other Indigenous scholars, enjoy a catered lunch, and learn about opportunities to get involved throughout the year\. For questions or concerns regarding the event, please contact G\+PS Indigenous Specialist, [Anik Chartrand](mailto:graduate.indigenous@ubc.ca)\.

Monday, September 21  
12:00–2:00 p\.m\.  
UBC First Nations Longhouse – Great Hall  
Catered lunch provided

**Please [RSVP](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_7agq5NUSZrPrfdc) by Monday, September 7\.**
