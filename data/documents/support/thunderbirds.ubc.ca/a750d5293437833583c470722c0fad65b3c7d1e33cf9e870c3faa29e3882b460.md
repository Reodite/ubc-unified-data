### Athletics and Recreation Driver Authorization Form

This form captures information for all approved and authorized drivers for the vehicles within the UBC Department of Athletics and Recreation\. Please ensure that you have read and are familiar with the A&amp;R Vehicle Usage Policy and Procedures Manual located [here](https://athletics.share.ubc.ca/recreation/facilities/Facilities%20Documents/UBC%20Athletics%20and%20Recreation%20-%20Vehicle%20Policies%20and%20Procedures%20-%20August%202024.pdf?d=w410b641549ae4093ba927a9778433584)\.
