## UBC Thunderbirds Varsity

The UBC Thunderbirds varsity program consists of 26 teams in 14 sports, with affiliations in two inter\-university leagues\. It is one of Canada’s most decorated programs with over 125 U SPORTS national championships and 20 plus banners in the U\.S\.\-based National Association of Intercollegiate Athletics \(NAIA\)\.

[Learn more](https://gothunderbirds.ca/)

## Competitive Sport Clubs Led by Student\-Athletes

The UBC Thunderbirds Sport Clubs \(UBC TSC\) is a competitive sport program for UBC student\-athletes\. UBC TSC is home to fifteen sports and seventeen teams representing UBC across Canada and the United States\.

[Learn more](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sport-clubs)
