## Resources:

Looking for assistance? Athletics and Recreation staff should direct all their IT questions and requests to UBC IT**, except permission role group questions \(see below\)\.**  UBC IT can be contacted by:

-   calling the UBC IT Service Centre at **827\.4357 \(7\.HELP**\) or
-   submitting a ticket using the **IT self\-service portal  [www\.it\.ubc\.ca/selfservice](http://www.it.ubc.ca/selfservice)**

[UBC Athletics and Recreation Employee IT Orientation Manual](https://athletics.share.ubc.ca/Shared%20Documents/Policies/New+Departing%20Employee%20Checklists/IT-orientation.docx?Web=1)

-   Off\-Campus access to Orientation Manual – UBC Virtual Private Network \(VPN\) is required for users accessing the above link from off\-campus\. You must be logged into VPN prior to signing on\.Don’t have VPN? [Go here to download it](http://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/myvpn/setup-documents)

***Permission Role Groups:*** 

Access to printers, network shared drives, sharepoint and generic emails, is all done through ***Permission Role Groups***\.

Each A&amp;R position has a distinct set of specific permissions \(ie\. student roles – through to each specific FT role\)\.  To create, grant, change or revoke permissions, or if you are planning to add new printers, network shared drives, sharepoint sub\-sites or generic emails to A&amp;R assets, please talk to Heather Q, Mike T or Taira J first\.
