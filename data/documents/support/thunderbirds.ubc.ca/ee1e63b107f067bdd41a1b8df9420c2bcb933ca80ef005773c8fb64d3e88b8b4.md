This form is collecting information to give you card access to the facility or facilities where you work\.

IMPORTANT: To obtain the "\* 6 digits" as requested below from your staff or student card: flip the card over and across the back of the card along the bottom there are a series of numbers slightly lighter font than the rest of the writing\. Record the first 6 digits immediately following the "\*" or "\+" symbol from that number series into the form below\. \(ex\. card reads: \+568933 10110110111\-3, enter 568933 in the box below\)
