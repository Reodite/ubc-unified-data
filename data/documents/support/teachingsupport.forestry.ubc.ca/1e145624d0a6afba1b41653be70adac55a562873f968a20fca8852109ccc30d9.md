                        **Congratulations to Dr\. Lori Daniels\!**
                        
                        For receiving the alumni UBC Achievement Award: Faculty Community Service Award\. This award recognizes a professor who has rendered significant community service in areas other than teaching and research\.
                        
                        **TA Hour Allocation Form**
                        
                        All TAs and instructors must complete the Schedule E form at the start of the term\. This form breaks down how the TA’s hours will be allocated across tasks like teaching, grading, meetings, finals support, and more\. Filling the [form](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2025/01/Allocation-of-Hours-Form-UBC-Forestry.pdf) out ensures both the TA and instructor are aligned and protected\.

                        **TLEF and OER Fund Support Sessions**
                        
                        Educational development, instructional design, and media design support are available to applicants who are developing TLEF or OER Fund proposals\. Full event details can be found on this [website](https://eventsreg.ctlt.ubc.ca/event-category/tlef/)\.
                        
                        **Campus as a Living Lab Fund Competition**
                        
                        This competition offers up to $500,000 in total seed funding to support innovative, interdisciplinary projects led by UBC Vancouver faculty and staff\. An information session will be held on September 16th, and more details and RSVP information can be found [here](https://livinglabs.ubc.ca/programs-and-initiatives/cll-fund-competition)\.

                        **Resources Available to you as an Instructor**
                        
                        Take a look at the [Teaching Primer](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/faculty-resources/) that the TLS team has prepared you for important information, resources and support available to you as an instructor\.
                        
                        **Learn how to view the [2025 August Exam Schedule](http://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?p=10226)\.**
                        
                        **Using External Learning Technologies in Teaching**
                        
                        A friendly reminder that external learning technologies need to pass UBC’s [Privacy Impact Assessment \(PIA\)](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/pia-stra) before implementation in teaching\. If you need to use an external learning technology for your courses, feel free to contact the TLS team \(forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca\) for support and consultation\.
                        
                        **Turnitin “New” Interface**
                        
                        A new upgrade has made changes to all Turnitin assignments, and lost the ability to access Turnitin Gradebook and PeerMark, among other features\. Instructors have the choice between the classic and new interface for Turnitin assignments\. Please contact LT\.Hub@ubc\.ca for any questions or changing the default settings\.

                        **CTLT Summer Institute**
                        
                        August 18 – 22
                        
                        [Featuring events](https://institute2-ctlt.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/07/CTLT-Summer-Institute-Schedule-1.pdf) on generative AI in education, Canvas competencies, and sharing essential resources to help you prepare for the academic year\. Events are taking place both in\-person and online, and intended to be taken individually based on interest and availability\.
                        
                        **2025 Teaching Development Program for New Faculty**
                        
                        Apply by August 25th
                        
                        The TDP is designed for new UBC instructors who teach credit courses\. This flexible program, which can be completed in 1 or 2 semester\(s\), includes online and in\-person elements\. More details can be found [here](https://eventsreg.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/2025-tdp-for-new-faculty-application-form/)\.
                        
                        **2025 New Faculty Teaching Orientation**
                        
                        August 18 \(9:45am – 1:30pm\) \| In\-Person
                        
                        UBC’s [New Faculty Teaching Orientation](https://eventsreg.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/2025-new-faculty-teaching-orientation/?) is designed to welcome and support new faculty members as they navigate their roles and responsibilities at the institution\.
