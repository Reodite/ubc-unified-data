                | **Classroom Live Streaming and Recording** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    This fall, there could be a variety of COVID\-related emergency requests as our instructors, TAs, and students may be ill\. If you need flexible options in terms of **hybrid  
                    and/or online teaching**, please contact [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca) for discussion, demo, and support\.
                    
                    To schedule the classroom live streaming and recording, please submit your request **at least 2 business days** before your class to Central AV via this [link](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_d0CchruSKrBVigS)\. For more information, please check out this [website](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/recording-and-streaming-classrooms)\. You can follow the steps in this [tip\-sheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip_Sheet_Requesting_Classroom_Recording_and_Streaming.pdf) to fill out the reservation  
                    form\.  
                      
                    **Canvas Video Uploads Reminder**
                    
                    If you are planning to upload videos to Canvas, please do it via **Kaltura** and embed them into Canvas pages as needed\. The file system in each Canvas course only has 4\.2 GB of storage, while Kaltura has unlimited space\. This will prevent your Canvas course from running out of storage space\. Please check out pages 7\-16 in [this  
                    tip\-sheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tipsheet-Sharing-Class-Recordings-with-Students-.pdf) for using Kaltura on Canvas\.

                | **Call for Proposals for Small TLEF Fund** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Small Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(TLEF\) aims to support the experimentation of pedagogical approaches that will improve innovation in teaching and learning\. Small TLEF Innovation projects can request up to $50,000 over the life of the project, and can run from 1 to 3 years\. The deadline for proposals is **3 pm on Thursday, November 17, 2022**\. More information can be found [here](https://tlef.ubc.ca/announcements/call-for-proposals-for-small-teaching-and-learning-enhancement-fund-innovation-projects/)\. The workshop of preparing for TLEF applications is [here](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/news-events/how-to-put-together-a-winning-teaching-and-learning-enhancement-fund-tlef-application/)\.
                    
                    **TLS Drop\-in Sessions**  
                    The Teaching and Learning Support Team organizes weekly drop\-in sessions to answer teaching\-related questions \(e\.g\.,  
                    Canvas, in\-class live streaming and recording, etc\.\)\. Both instructors and TAs are welcome to join\.
                    
                    Time: **Fridays 13:00\-14:00**Join the [Zoom meeting](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/62814050960?pwd=OXFlZ3FTcDZOWWp4T3lvTlNpNk9sZz09) 
                    
                    **Canvas Workshop: Assignments, Quizzes, and SpeedGrader**  
                    The TLS team will be hosting a Canvas workshop on assignments, quizzes, and SpeedGrader on **October 7** **\(Friday, 1\-2 pm\)** during the weekly drop\-in session\. Instructors and TAs who are not familiar with Canvas grading are welcome to join\.

                | **IT Support** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    For general IT needs, please contact [forestry\.ithelp@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.ithelp@ubc.ca)\. To request  
                    **remote UG lab access**, please email [forestry\.ithelp@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.ithelp@ubc.ca) and cc [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\.
                    
                      
                    **Adobe Use in UG Labs**  
                    For Photoshop and Illustrator access in the UG labs, as well as Course Based Masters’ laptops, please refer to the [attached PDF](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/09/Adobe_SDL_Poster-1-Page-How-to-for-User-Login-Steps.pdf) for instructions on gaining access\.
                    
                    **ArcGIS Pro 3\.0 Now Available  
                    **ArcGIS Pro 3\.0 is now available\. Users should plan for their labs to *all upgrade at the same time*\. If teaching with ArcGIS Pro, do not upgrade, stay with ArcGIS Pro 2\.9  
                    until summer 2023\.
                    
                    ArcGIS 3\.0 saved projects and project templates *cannot* be opened in 2\.x\. It only works if using ArcGIS 3\.0 to save the projects as the 2\.9 version, and ArcGIS 3\.0 created project packages can be opened as a project in 2\.x\. When asking for ArcGIS Pro 3\.0, please consider how you share your files before proceeding ahead with the upgrade\. Email [forestry\.ithelp@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.ithelp@ubc.ca) for  
                    installation help with ArcGIS pro 3\.0\.
