                | **Access to Collaborate Ultra Recordings to be Turned Off** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    A reminder that UBC’s access to Collaborate Ultra and all stored recordings will officially end on July 31, 2021\.
                    
                    Should you need time in July to complete the transfer of the recordings, please [contact the LT Hub](https://lthub.ubc.ca/support/lt-hub/) to re\-enable access to Collaborate Ultra on a course\-by\-course basis until July 30\.
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | **Winter 2021 Reading Lists for UBC Library Course Reserve** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    The UBC Library course reserve is now accepting Winter 2021 reading lists, so long as your courses appear in the registrars\.
                    
                    Your list of readings may also be sent directly to the syllabus service \([syllabus\.service@ubc\.ca](mailto:syllabus.service@ubc.ca)\), who will input your request into Library Online Course Reserves \(LOCR\)\. To view the full message from the Woodward Library Course Reserve Unit, please [click here](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/news/6685/)\.
