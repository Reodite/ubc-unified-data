                | **Kaltura Cloud Migration is Complete** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Thanks for your cooperation and patience as 180,000 Kaltura videos were migrated in April\.
                    
                    What does this mean for me?
                    
                    \(1\) You can access and manage your Kaltura videos through the new Teaching &amp; Learning Media Portal at [learning\.media\.ubc\.ca](https://learning.media.ubc.ca/) or via Canvas\.
                    
                    \(2\) Kaltura embedded links in Canvas will be automatically updated to reflect their new home in the cloud\.
                    
                    \(3\) Links to Kaltura  
                    [videos embedded **outside** of Canvas must be **manually updated**](https://lthub.ubc.ca/initiatives/evp-project/kaltura-cloud-migration/#embedded-videos-change)  
                    by Fall, 2023\.
                    
                    If you have questions, feel free to contact  
                    [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\. Additionally, you can  
                    [register for a Kaltura workshop](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/tag/kaltura/), held at noon on variable dates each month\.
                    
                    These one\-hour introductory sessions will delve into the features available in the Kaltura cloud\.

                | **Forestry Teaching &amp; Learning Seminar – Building Classroom Community: Online Tools to Facilitate Dialogue between Class Sessions** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | Time: Tuesday, May 16, \(12 – 1 pm\) Dr\. Sara Barron will demonstrate two learning tools – Padlet and Perusal – inherently discussion\-based online platforms that can extend and enrich classroom conversations\. The talk will reflect on challenges and successes in adopting these tools across a range of learning environments and suggest their potential in building classroom community\. This event is free and will be held virtually and in person\. [Register here](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3pxyYrOShlqUgqW)\. |

                | **Sustainability Education Grants** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | UBC [Sustainability Education Grants](https://sustain.ubc.ca/teaching-applied-learning/sustainability-education-grants) is now open\. The program provides up to **$20,000 in funding, over two years,** to Vancouver faculty members looking to advance and diversify sustainability education opportunities for students in all disciplines\. Funding supports curriculum development centred on transformative learning that incorporates experiential, applied and interdisciplinary sustainability education principles\. **Applications close on May 14\.** |
