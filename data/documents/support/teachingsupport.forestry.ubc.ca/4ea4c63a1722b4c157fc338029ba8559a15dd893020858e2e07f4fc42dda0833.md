                | **Teaching Support** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    For questions about Canvas, hybrid teaching, course development and redesign, and AV support, feel free to book a one\-on\-one meeting with us via [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\.
                    
                    This fall, there could be a variety of COVID\-related emergency requests as our instructors, TAs, and students may be ill\. If you need flexible options in terms of **hybrid and/or online teaching**, please contact [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca) for discussion, demo, and support\.  
                    **Classroom Live Streaming and Recording**
                    
                    To schedule the classroom live streaming and recording, please submit your request **at least 2 business days** before your class to Central AV via this [link](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_d0CchruSKrBVigS)\. For more information, please check out this [website](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/recording-and-streaming-classrooms)\. You can follow the steps in this [tip\-sheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip_Sheet_Requesting_Classroom_Recording_and_Streaming.pdf) to fill out the reservation form\.
                    
                    Here are the tip\-sheets for using AV systems in FSC classrooms\.  
                    \(1\) Learning spaces with built\-in live streaming and recording systems: [FSC 1001/1003/1005/1221](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip_Sheet_Teaching_with_In-room_Recording_and_Streaming-1.pdf)  
                    \(2\) [Mobile AV cart in FSC 1001](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip_Sheet_Teaching_with_Mobile_AV_Cart_Updated.pdf)  
                    \(3\) [General learning spaces without built\-in live streaming or recording systems](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip_Sheet_Teaching_in_General_Learning_Spaces.pdf)  
                    \(4\) [Alternative recording options for in\-person classes](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tipsheet-Alternative-Recording-Options-for-In-person-Classes.pdf)  
                    \(5\) [Sharing class recordings with students](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tipsheet-Sharing-Class-Recordings-with-Students-.pdf)

                | **TLS Drop\-in Sessions** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    The Teaching and Learning Support Team organizes weekly drop\-in sessions to answer teaching\-related questions \(e\.g\., Canvas, in\-class live streaming and recording, etc\.\)\. Both instructors and TAs are welcome to join\.
                    
                    Time: **Fridays 13:00 – 14:00**Join the [Zoom meeting](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/62814050960?pwd=OXFlZ3FTcDZOWWp4T3lvTlNpNk9sZz09) 
                    
                    **Teaching Assistant Workshops: EDI in TAing**  
                    In these workshops, you will learn about power and privilege within the learning environment, the policies and guidelines  
                    that frame your work, and how best to support students as you navigate ethical dilemmas and potential conflicts\. This  
                    online synchronous session \(offered through Zoom\) is foundational to supporting an equitable and inclusive learning  
                    environment while enhancing department and program\-specific TA training\. Please  
                    [register](https://equity.ubc.ca/how-we-can-help/training-and-education/teaching-assistant-workshops-equity-diversity-and-inclusion/) to attend the section that best fits your academic discipline and schedule\.
                    
                    STEM Disciplines: Wednesday, Sept\. 14 from 4\-6 pm
                    
                    Creative Arts Disciplines: Wednesday, Sept\. 21 from 4\-6 pm
                    
                    Social Sciences &amp; Humanities: Wednesday, Sept\. 28 from 4\-6 pm

                |  |
                | --- |
                | Zoom has released a new feature allowing users to add preferred pronouns to their Zoom profile\. UBC enabled this feature for UBC Zoom accounts on August 5\.
