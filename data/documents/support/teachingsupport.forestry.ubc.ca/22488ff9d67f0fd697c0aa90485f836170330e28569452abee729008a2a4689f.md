                | **UBC Needs Assessment: Zoom for Teaching** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    The university is reviewing its core teaching and learning technical infrastructure\. Within that, the use of Zoom for teaching and learning is being assessed\.
                    
                    We would like to invite you to complete [this short survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_80vIsoktXJQawia)\.Your answers will help us better understand your needs, and how you are currently using Zoom\. While you might use Zoom for other activities, the survey is specifically focused on your teaching and learning uses\.
                    
                    If you have questions about using Zoom for teaching in Forestry, please contact [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\.

                | **Exam Planning &amp; Preparation Tips** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    There are various exam formats that teaching teams can choose from, such as in\-person paper, in\-person computer\-based, or online via Canvas\. For help with exam planning, feel free to check out the following tip\-sheets\.  
                    – [Navigating the Choice: Online or In\-Person Assessments\.](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Navigating-the-Choice-Online-or-In-Person-Assessments-V3.pdf)  
                    – [UBC Exam Policies, Procedures, and Reminders\.](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip-Sheet-Exam-Policy-V6.pdf)  
                    – [Technical Tips for Exam Logistics\.](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip-Sheet-Technical-Tips-for-Exam-Logistics-V6.pdf)
                    
                    For further questions or exam support, don’t hesitate to book a consultation or email us at [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\.

                | **Exam Seating System Pilot** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Having challenges in managing seating for in\-person exams? UBC exam seating system can be a potential solution\. The system randomly assigns seats to students when they enter the classroom, and auto\-tracks attendance to help verify student info \(see the picture below\)\.
                    
                    If you’d like to try it, please email [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca) at your earliest convenience so we can set it up in advance\.
                    
                    [Image](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/Seatr.png)
