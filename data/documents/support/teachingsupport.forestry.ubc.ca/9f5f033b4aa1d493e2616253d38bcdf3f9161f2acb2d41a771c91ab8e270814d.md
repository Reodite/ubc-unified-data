                        **Bulk Uploading Quiz Questions**
                        
                        Refer to [this tipsheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Bulk-creation-of-Canvas-Question-Bank.pdf) for step\-by\-step instructions on how to bulk\-upload quiz questions into a Canvas course question bank\.
                        
                        **Including Excel Sheet Question** **Sets in Lockdown Browser**
                        
                        Lockdown Browser is now compatible with MS Excel, allowing students to open linked spreadsheets during a Classic Canvas Quiz\. For full instructions on how to set up this feature, please see [this helpful tipsheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Using-Excel-in-LockDown-Browser.pdf)\.
                        
                        **Differentiation Tags**
                        
                        [This tipsheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tipsheet-_-Differentiation-Tags.pdf) walks through the new feature, which allow instructors to discreetly organize students into defined sets\. This may be particularly useful for assigning
                        
                        alternative deadlines or tagging students with CFA\.
                        
                        **Freeform Rubric Comments**
                        
                        The teaching team can now [https://community\.instructure\.com/en/kb/articles/661175\-how\-do\-i\-use\-free\-form\-comments\-instead\-of\-ratings\-in\-a\-rubric\-in\-speedgrader](https://community.instructure.com/en/kb/articles/661175-how-do-i-use-free-form-comments-instead-of-ratings-in-a-rubric-in-speedgrader)[save and reuse comments directly within specific criteria of the rubric](https://community.instructure.com/en/kb/articles/661175-how-do-i-use-free-form-comments-instead-of-ratings-in-a-rubric-in-speedgrader)\. This feature can help better explain the reasoning behind the breakdown of a given score\.

                        **Mid\-term Check\-in Survey**
                        
                        Deadline: Sunday March 15
                        
                        A check\-in survey for the students has been created by TLS\. To use the survey, instructors can post [this link](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6E7afQ3AsHLKkxo) to Canvas\.
                        
                        **Student Voices in Course Design**
                        
                        March 11 \(10:00am – 11:30am\) \| In\-Person
                        
                        An interactive workshop for faculty and staff exploring [practical approaches to meaningful student\-faculty collaboration](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/student-voices-in-course-design-2/?) where students are active partners in designing course materials, activities, and assessments\.
                        
                        **Best Practices in Survey Design**
                        
                        March 10 \(10:00am – 11:30am\) \| Online
                        
                        Looking at when surveys serve as an [appropriate evaluation tool](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/best-practices-in-survey-design/?), the most common types of questions, and best practices around inclusivity issues\.
                        
                        **Understanding and Using Student Feedback**
                        
                        March 19 \(1:00pm 2:00pm\) \| Online
                        
                        This session walks you through how to [interpret SEI data results in a constructive way,](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/making-the-most-of-student-experience-of-instruction-seoi-understanding-and-using-student-feedback/) identify patterns, and respond to feedback with confidence\.
