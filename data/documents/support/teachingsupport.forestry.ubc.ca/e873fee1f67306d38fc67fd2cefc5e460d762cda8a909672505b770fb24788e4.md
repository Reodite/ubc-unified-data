                | **Implementation of Universal Design for Learning in a “Hands\-On” Forestry Course: Experiences and Reflection** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    June 18 \(12 – 1 pm\) \| Online &amp; In\-person  
                    In this seminar, two Forestry representatives from the UBC UDL Fellows Program, Lee Salmon and Frederick Qi, will discuss UDL’s application in FRST 239\.
                    
                    Join us to learn about the barriers that were addressed, the solutions that were deployed, as well as how these solutions could be incorporated into other Forestry courses\. [Register here\.](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Yucu2opj0sHdZEHFgY5jg5VTAKcaw7nv2S#/registration)

                | **Large TLEF Special Call for Generative AI Collaborative Cluster Grants** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    The 2025/26 Large TLEF Transformation Projects funding round is a special call to support UBC Vancouver project teams interested in experimenting as part of collaborative clusters with the integration of Generative AI in teaching and learning\.
                    
                    Applicants need to submit Letters of Intent by **3 pm on** **July 25, 2024**\. Click [here for more info](https://tlef.ubc.ca/application/special-call/large-tlef-special-call-for-gen-ai-collaborative-cluster-grants/)\.
