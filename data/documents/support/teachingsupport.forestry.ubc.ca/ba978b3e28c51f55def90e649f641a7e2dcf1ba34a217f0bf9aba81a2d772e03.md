                    In the mean time, if you have any questions, comments, or concerns regarding learning and teaching technologies or course design, please contact the TLS team\. You can contact Michelle Zeng \(Senior Manager, Education Strategies\) at [michelle\.zeng@ubc\.ca](mailto:michelle.zeng@ubc.ca)\.  
                    Michelle leads Forestry’s Teaching and Learning Support \(TLS\) team, which specializes in supporting faculty and students as they navigate both online and offline teaching and learning\.                 | **Name Changes in Canvas and Other Services** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    LT hub is updating how learning technology  
                    systems at UBC determine what name to display for you\. These changes will be implemented on **July 27, 2022**\.  
                    
                      
                    If you are a faculty or staff member working for UBC, you can [set your preferred first and last name in Workday](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article&amp;sys_id=5830be9c1b089d509068c992604bcb55&amp;table=kb_knowledge)\. Canvas  
                    and other technologies will use this name\.
                    
                      
                    Learn more about the update [here](https://lthub.ubc.ca/2022/07/04/potential-change-to-your-name/?login)\.
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | **RefWorks Subscription is Ending** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    UBC Library has made the decision to end the subscription to the RefWorks citation management software as of **September 30, 2022**\.
                    
                      
                    There is support in place to make the transition away from RefWorks to other  
                    supported services \(such as Zotero  
                    and Mendeley\), which you can find [here](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/refworks/migration)\.
                    
                      
                    If you need any assistance with migrating to a new tool, please contact [research\.commons@ubc\.ca](mailto:research.commons@ubc.ca)\.
