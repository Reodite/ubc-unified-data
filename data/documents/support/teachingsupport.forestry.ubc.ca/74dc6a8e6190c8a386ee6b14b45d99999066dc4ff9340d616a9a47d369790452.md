**Topic:** Faculty of Forestry Learning Analytics tools in Canvas and other tools Workshop 2021

**Facilitators:** TLS Team

**Time:** Aug 11, 2021 11:00am

Register for workshop [HERE](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Ysdeuvrz8iGtR9K90HkYhMfHtnlTUZ8nTH)

**Description:**

This is a workshop for anyone in the Faculty who want to familiarize themselves more with Learning Analytic tools in Canvas\. We will dive into the OnTask tool and discuss how to send students personalized messages about their performance\. We will also look at the Canvas Course Analytics and Quiz Statistics feature\. This will help to understand how to create a thoughtful assessment structure that will ensure students receive formative feedback early in the course\. Faculty, staff, TAs and Academic Assistants are all welcome\.

**Key topics:**

-   Canvas integrated OnTask tool
-   Canvas Course Analytics and Quiz Statistics
-   Thoughtful assessment structure that will ensure students receive formative feedback early in the course
