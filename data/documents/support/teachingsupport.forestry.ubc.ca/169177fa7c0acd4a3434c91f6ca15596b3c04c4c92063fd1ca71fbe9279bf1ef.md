                    | Upcoming Term Reminders |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        **Midterm Check\-In Survey**
                        
                        The Forestry TLS Team has developed a check\-in survey for students\. Instructors can share the [survey link](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6E7afQ3AsHLKkxo) in their Canvas courses\. After the survey feedback is collected, instructors will receive course\-specific results via email\.
                        
                        The deadline for students to complete the survey is **Sunday October 26**\.
                        
                        **LockDown Browser Technical Issues**
                        
                        Issues have been reported for students that are accessing assessments using Chromebook Laptops and the LockDown Browser \(LDB\) app on an iPad\.
                        
                        The advanced LDB settings integrated in Canvas does not support the use of iPads and Chromebook\. As a result, these users will encounter technical challenges with LDB and the assessments may not function as expected\.
                        
                        The teaching teams are recommend to advise students to use laptops with LDB just to be safe\. If a student doesn’t have access to a laptop, they can [borrow one from the UBC Library](https://services.library.ubc.ca/computers-technology/technology-borrowing/) in advance, or make alternative arrangements\.
                        
                        It is highly recommended to arrange a practice assessment or session so students can test the LDB’s setup\. You can also share the [LockDown Browser Student Guide](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/lockdown-browser-instructor-guide/) as an additional resource\.
                        
                        If you have any questions or need exam support, please contact the TLS team\.

                    | GenAI Resources |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        Feeling lost on how to navigate the ever\-changing landscape of AI? Here a small list of some resources that UBC provides to Staff and Faculty to help stay ahead of the curve\.
                        
                        UBC CTLT \([Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/)\) and LITC \([Learning Technology Innovation Centre](https://ltic.ubc.ca/)\) both have resources available to help educators move from concern, through understanding, to seeing the opportunities [generative AI](https://genai.ubc.ca/) presents\.
                        
                        Are you planning to use GenAI in your teaching this term? Make sure to check [UBC’s PIA\-approved GenAI tools for 2025](https://ai.ctlt.ubc.ca/privacy-impact-assessments-for-generativeai-instructional-use-at-ubc/)\. Please note that without a PIA, instructors cannot require students to use the tool or service, without providing alternatives that do not require the use of student private information\.
                        
                        Whether you’re a beginner looking to grasp the basics or an experienced researcher diving into advanced techniques, UBC provides variety of [training programs](https://genai.ubc.ca/training-hub/) to provide accessible learning opportunities\.
                        
                        Additionally, use the CTLT’s website for their full list of events and workshops, and how to register\. If you missed an event you were interested in, check out CLTL’s [session and studio replays](https://ai.ctlt.ubc.ca/replays/)\.

                    | Events and Workshops |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        **Forestry Teaching &amp; Learning Bi\-Weekly Drop\-in Sessions**
                        
                        September 24 11:30am\-12:30pm \| \(Online\)
                        
                        The TLS team will host bi\-weekly drop\-in sessions to answer teaching\-related questions\. Instructors and TAs are welcome to join the [Zoom meeting](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/65722056764?pwd=SUE1X4njLVxxanzqzH1lFMaqY6ipup.1)\. If you are unable to join feel free to stop by FSC Room 2625 or 2718 for support\.
                        
                        **Forestry Research Seminar with Tom Booker \| Evolutionary Genomics of Conifers – Genome Size, Inbreeding and Adaptation**
                        
                        September 25 from 11:45am\-12:45pm CAWP case room 2916 \| \(In\-Person\)
                        
                        Conifers, which dominate Canada’s boreal ecosystem, have genomes that are typically five times larger than the human genome\. [This seminar](https://forestry.ubc.ca/events/evolutionary-genomics-of-conifers-genome-size-inbreeding-and-adaptation/) will describe recent work on conifer genomes and how their large genomes are related to important aspects of their evolution\.
                        
                        **Advancing Urban Sustainability: A conference on urban sustainability research**
                        
                        Sept 24th from 12pm\-3pm \(In\-person\) &amp; Sept 25th from 1pm\-4:pm \(Online\)
                        
                        Two afternoons of engaged discussions on a diverse range of applied sustainability projects produced by UBC graduate students in collaboration with partner organizations\. Additional information and RSVP details can be found on [here](https://sustain.ubc.ca/events/research-action-advancing-urban-sustainability-2025)\.
                        
                        **EDI for Teaching Assistants – UBC Equity &amp; Inclusion Office**
                        
                        October 1 at 1:00pm \| \(Online\)
                        
                        As a TA you can help build a more equitable learning environment in how you set expectations, manage classroom interactions and how you approach evaluating student work\. [This workshop](https://equity.ubc.ca/what-we-do/training-and-education/teaching-assistant-workshops-equity-diversity-and-inclusion/?utm_source=inclusive-ubc&amp;utm_medium=email) will teach you about power and privilege within the learning environment, the policies and guidelines that frame your work, and how best to support students as you navigate ethical dilemmas and potential conflicts\.
                        
                        **Dr\. Sara Brownell \| How Active Learning Can Present Challenges for Students with Marginalized Identities**
                        
                        October 1 from 10am – 11am \(In\-Person\)
                        
                        Dr\. Sara Brownell will explore how research has uncovered unintended consequences of active learning for students with marginalized and minoritized identities\. Dr\. Brownell’s examines how this work does not call for a return to traditional lecture, but showcases the need to be thoughtful in how we implement active learning in ways that are inclusive across a variety of identifies\. To RSVP, fill out this [survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6h9mfQIQVz0hgVw)\.
                        
                        **Exploring the Changing Landscape of GenAI Tools and Models in Teaching and Learning**
                        
                        October 2 from 12:00pm\-1:00pm \| \(Online\)
                        
                        Keeping up with the recent changes in AI tools and development can be difficult, but understanding them is increasingly important\. [This workshop](http://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/exploring-the-changing-landscape-of-genai-tools-and-models-in-teaching-and-learning/) will explore tools related to deep research, video creation, and reasoning and discuss what these developments mean for your academic practice\.
