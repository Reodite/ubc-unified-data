In the interest of community\-building during these challenging times, we have created a Digital Learning Media Repository, essentially a shared directory that is accessible by all\.  If you have developed some really cool digital learning materials and you don’t mind sharing, please consider loading them onto the directory for others to use – see attached instructions for how to do so\.  On occasion, check back to see if there are any digital materials that you can incorporate into your classes\.
