#### Event Details

-   **Start:** 16 June 2022 11:00 am
-   **End:** 16 June 2022 12:30 pm

---

**Date &amp; time:** May 19, 2022, from 11 AM to 12:30 PM PST  
**Zoom Registration:** [Here](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5YldOugqDMpEtZW000rbbtRMrHq0ou-tLE-)

Jeff Miller, TLEF Program Director &amp; Senior Associate Director, CTLT, and three Forestry speakers, Dr\. Shannon Hagerman, Dr\. Peter Marshall, Dr\. Simon Ellis, who have profound knowledge and experience in the Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(TLEF\) will share their insights in this workshop to help our Forestry instructors better understand the scope and criteria of TLEF grants as well as identify synergies across the faculty to enhance our chances for success\.

Workshop structure:

-   What is the TLEF program
-   Successful examples from Forestry
-   Tips for preparing a winning proposal \(panel discussion\)
-   Open discussion and brainstorming for ideas and synergies within Forestry
