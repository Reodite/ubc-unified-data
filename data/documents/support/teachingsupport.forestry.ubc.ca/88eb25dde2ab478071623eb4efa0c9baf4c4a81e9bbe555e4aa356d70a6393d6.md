                - **Row 1**

                    Our TLS newsletter is introducing a Teacher’s Corner, where **instructors are invited to share their knowledge** with the rest of the faculty\! As frontline personnel interacting with students, you **know best how to support student learning**\.
                    
                    Whether it be a Canvas quiz format, a discovery of what engages students most effectively, or shortcuts in technology navigation, we would love to hear from you\!
                    
                    Please contact us at [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca) if you’re interested\.

                                **Kaltura Enhancements**
                                
                                **Accessibility:** Tab\-based editing pages to simplify navigation and extended audio descriptions\.
                                
                                **My Media:** Up to three\-level folder hierarchies to organize media with bulk management\. Now available in Canvas global navigation\.
                                
                                **Playlists &amp; Channels:** Drag\-and\-drop reordering and bulk content additions\. Further details found [here](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/4ff550651d951a9627e6a204fbe629bc/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/4ff550651d951a9627e6a204fbe629bc/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d")\.
                                
                                **Genio Notes**
                                
                                Specifically adopted by UBC, Genio **gives a limited number of students** with disabilities **equitable access** to their education by capturing, organizing, and reviewing recordings\.
                                
                                This is **NOT a campus\-wide initiative** for all students\. This tool is for individual [students with demonstrated disabilities who are eligible for notetaking support through the Centre for Accessibility](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/077949d89ce6e223ee56a204fbe6a1a0/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/077949d89ce6e223ee56a204fbe6a1a0/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d") as part of UBC’s commitment to equitable access\.
