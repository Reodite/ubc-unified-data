                        **Oral Exams on Canvas**
                        
                        Classes can conduct oral exams through Canvas without requiring hundreds of live one\-on\-one sessions\. This approach assesses student ability to explain, justify, or interpret applied scenarios\.
                        
                        More information can be found on this [tipsheet](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/5ac8373e51eae41d11c6944334e2fa03/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d?eid=6431051f3859ec2e0a4d8234cfc1f852)\.

                    | Teaching Team Best Practices |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        Have you checked in with your teaching team yet? **Check out our short checklist:**
                        
                        -   Meet and clarify expectations and responsibilities of TAs\.
                        -   Complete the TA hour allocation form
                        -   Refer to [UBC Forestry TA Handbook](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/UBC-Forestry-TA-Handbook-Draft-Version-2.2-April-2026.pdf) if needed
                        
                        **TA Mentor Sessions**
                        
                        Friendly advisors are at the TA POD from 12\-1pm the first Monday of every month \(Jan 26, Feb 23, Mar 30\) to support your TA experience\! Bring questions, concerns, or thoughts – or just to chat\!
                        
                        The first TA Mentor Session will be **January 26, 12pm\-1pm @ the TA POD\!** It will be STA focused\.
                        
                        **TA POD \(2500A\)**
                        
                        The TA POD is a dedicated space for TAs to hold office hours and complete course related responsibilities\. To book the Pod, please [follow this guide](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/2500-TA-Pods-Booking-procedure.pdf)\.

                        **Exam Planning &amp; Preparation Tips**
                        
                        There are various exam formats that teaching teams can choose from, such as in\-person paper, in\-person computer\-based, or online via Canvas\. For help with exam planning, feel free to check out the following tip\-sheets\.
                        
                        -   [Navigating the Choice: Online or In\-Person Assessments\.](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Navigating-the-Choice-Online-or-In-Person-Assessments-V3.pdf)
                        -   [UBC Exam Policies, Procedures, and Reminders\.](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip-Sheet-Exam-Policy-V6.pdf)
                        -   [Technical Tips for Exam Logistics](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip-Sheet-Technical-Tips-for-Exam-Logistics-V6.pdf)
                        -   [Tips for Canvas Quizzes for Large Classes](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2023/08/Tip-sheet-Canvas-Quizzes-for-Large-Classes.pdf)
                        
                        **LockDown Browser**
                        
                        LockDown Browser is a secure browser
                        
                        that restricts what students can do during an assessment in Canvas\. When active, students will not be able to copy questions or visit other websites\. For further information, please refer to the [Tips for Using LDB\.](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2025/10/Tips-for-using-LDB-Tipsheet.pdf)
