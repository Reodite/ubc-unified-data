                | **Forestry Field Equipment Procedure** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Submit all equipment requests to: [field\.equip@ubc\.ca](mailto:field.equip@ubc.ca)\. The teaching equipment requests should be made by faculty members only\. TAs can be delegated to pick up and return the equipment if specifically mentioned and named in the request email\. Trimble lab equipment requests should be made by faculty and research staff only\.
                    
                    Please provide at least 1\-week notice \(2 weeks for major requests\), and promptly return all gear as scheduled, without any delay or reminder\. Make sure to avoid extended bookings; share equipment with fellow instructors\. UBC card is required for equipment sign\-out\.

                | **Exam Formats &amp; Planning** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | Instructors are encouraged to consider proactive rather than reactive/punitive ways to prevent and discourage inappropriate use of online web resources or AI in the classrooms\. There are different exam formats \(in\-person paper, in\-person computer, online Canvas, etc\.\) that instructors can consider\. Please refer to the following tip\-sheets: – [Navigating the Choice: Online or In\-Person Assessments](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Navigating-the-Choice-Online-or-In-Person-Assessments-V3.pdf)\. – [UBC Exam Policies, Procedures, and Reminders](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/12/Tipsheet-Exam-Policy.pdf)\. – [Technical Tips for Exam Logistics](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/12/Tipsheet-Technical-Tips-for-Exam-Logistics.pdf)\. |

                | **UBC Zoom &amp; Teams Video Retention Policy** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | Starting in October 2023 and March 2024 respectively, UBC will begin removing video recordings from Microsoft Teams and Zoom automatically, one year after each video is recorded\. More information about alternative storage, recovery of deleted recordings, previous lecture recordings, and frequently asked questions can be found in [this document](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/06/Zoom_and_Teams_Video_Retention.pdf)\.                 | **Forestry Trimble Technology Lab Resource Guide** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    [This guide](http://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/07/2022-University-of-British-Columbia-UBC-Trimble-Technology-Lab-Product-Resource-Guide.pdf) offers resources to support the delivery of forestry courses with practical applications\. It includes a range of software tools for 3D modelling and geospatial data processing, and hardware solutions like GNSS positioning devices\.
                    
                    To subscribe to the software, please email [forestry\.licensing@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.licensing@ubc.ca)\. To access the hardware, please reach out to [ht\.li@ubc\.ca](mailto:ht.li@ubc.ca)\. For teaching support, contact [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\.
