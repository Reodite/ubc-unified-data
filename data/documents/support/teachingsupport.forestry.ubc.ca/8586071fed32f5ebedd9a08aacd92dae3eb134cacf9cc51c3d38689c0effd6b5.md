                        **Final Exam Resources for Instructors**
                        
                        The TLS team has created an extensive collection of tipsheets to support teaching\. All tipsheets are organized by the associated learning tools on the [TLS website](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/learning-technologies/)\.
                        
                        Some **relevant tipsheets** include:
                        
                        -   [Technical Tips for Exam Logistics](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip-Sheet-Technical-Tips-for-Exam-Logistics-V6.pdf)
                        -   [UBC Exam Policies, Procedures, and Reminders](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip-Sheet-Exam-Policy-V6.pdf)
                        -   [Exam Invigilation Guidelines](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/exam_InvigilationGuidelines.pdf)
                        -   [Tips for Canvas Quizzes with Large Classes \(&gt;200\)](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2023/08/Tip-sheet-Canvas-Quizzes-for-Large-Classes.pdf)
                        -   [Giving Extra Attempts/Time to Canvas Quizzes](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tips-for-Giving-Extra-Attempts-and-Time-in-Canvas-Quizzes-updated.pdf)
                        
                        **CfA Final Exam Accommodations**
                        
                        Please be reminded instructors are to **submit exam information**, including duration, format, and allowances to the [Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/) **at least 2 business days prior** **to the exam date by 9:00am**, otherwise the Centre will cancel the booking\.
                        
                        The instructor is also responsible for adjusting Canvas exams \(e\.g\. length\) for students, unless they’ve made prior arrangements with CfA or the TLS team\. Two useful [Guide to Adjust Exams for Students](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/centre_for_accessibility_canvas_guide.pdf) and a [Guide on Submitting Exam Information](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/UBC-Centre-for-Accessibility-Guide-on-Submitting-Exam-Information-Sept-2023.pdf) for reference\.
                        
                        It is recommended to **remind registered students** to [book their exam accommodations](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/) with the CfA **before** **Tuesday, April 7th 11:59PM\.**
                        
                        For more information regarding UBC’s policies on exam clashes, hardships, and cancellations, check out the following [https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/exams/exam\-clashes\-hardships\-cancellations/](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/exams/exam-clashes-hardships-cancellations/)[page](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/exams/exam-clashes-hardships-cancellations/)[\.](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/exams/exam-clashes-hardships-cancellations/)

                        The TLS Team has created this tipsheet to help Instructors [Navigate the Choice between Online or In\-Person Assessments](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/Navigating-the-Choice-Online-or-In-Person-Assessments-V3.pdf)\.
                        
                        **LockDown Browser \(LDB\)**
                        
                        [LDB](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/lockdown-browser-instructor-guide/) prevents students from accessing external websites or applications during Canvas exams\. It is highly recommended to arrange a [https://lthub\.ubc\.ca/guides/lockdown\-browser\-student\-guide/](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/lockdown-browser-student-guide/)[practice session so students can test the LDB](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/lockdown-browser-student-guide/) setup before the exam\.
                        
                        **Zoom with LDB**
                        
                        LDB can also be opened while having live zoom sessions with students\. You can do so with either one device or two separate devices\. To set this up, please refer to [https://teachingsupport\.forestry\.ubc\.ca/files/2026/02/Tip\-Sheet\-Invigilating\-an\-Exam\-with\-Zoom\-and\-Lockdown\-Browser\-Two\-Options\.pdf](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip-Sheet-Invigilating-an-Exam-with-Zoom-and-Lockdown-Browser-Two-Options.pdf)[this tipsheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip-Sheet-Invigilating-an-Exam-with-Zoom-and-Lockdown-Browser-Two-Options.pdf)\.
                        
                        **Excel Files with LDB**
                        
                        Students can open linked MS Excel spreadsheets during a Classic Canvas Quiz\. For full instructions on how to set up this feature, please see [https://teachingsupport\.forestry\.ubc\.ca/files/2026/02/Using\-Excel\-in\-LockDown\-Browser\.pdf](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Using-Excel-in-LockDown-Browser.pdf)[this helpful tipsheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Using-Excel-in-LockDown-Browser.pdf)\.
                        
                        **To accommodate students unable to install LDB, consider these options:**
                        
                        -   Canvas Quiz \(without LDB\) with in\-person monitoring\.
                        -   In\-person paper exams
                        -   Canvas Oral Exams
                        
                        **Oral Exams on Canvas**
                        
                        Instructors can run oral exams through Canvas, without requiring hundreds of live one\-on\-one sessions\. To create such a quiz, detailed steps are provided in [https://teachingsupport\.forestry\.ubc\.ca/files/2026/02/Tips\-on\-Creating\-Audio\-and\-Video\-Submission\-Quizzes\-on\-Canvas\-with\-LDB\-V2\-1\.pdf](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tips-on-Creating-Audio-and-Video-Submission-Quizzes-on-Canvas-with-LDB-V2-1.pdf)[this tipsheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tips-on-Creating-Audio-and-Video-Submission-Quizzes-on-Canvas-with-LDB-V2-1.pdf)\.
                        
                        If you have questions or need support, please contact us at forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca\.
