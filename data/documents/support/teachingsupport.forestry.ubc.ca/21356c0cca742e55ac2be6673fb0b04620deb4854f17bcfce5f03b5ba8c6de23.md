                | **Mid\-term Check\-in Survey** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    An automated mid\-term check\-in survey has been launched for the students\. To use the survey, instructors can post [this link](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_ddu57vEgAVUcqAm) to Canvas or email it to the class\. The survey results for your particular course will be emailed to you during the week of Oct 17\.
                    
                    **Academic Integrity and Plagiarism  
                    **If you have concerns about plagiarism or academic integrity with a specific student, ADS Scott Hinch \(scott\.hinch@ubc\.ca\) is your first point of contact\. Additionally, UBC is in the process of building and beta\-testing an academic integrity hub which provides non\-punitive ways to turn an incident into more of a teachable moment\. See [Academic Integrity at UBC](https://academicintegrity.ubc.ca/)\.
                    
                    **New TA Meeting Pod**  
                    A new TA meeting pod is now available in the FSC atrium\. TAs can book a spot at one of the three tables in the pod to meet with students\. Instructions for reserving the space can be found [here](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/2500-TA-Pods-Booking-procedure.pdf)\.
                    
                    **Inclusive Teaching**  
                    UBC Centre for Teaching, Learning, and Technology organized resources \(e\.g\., inclusive syllabus, microaggressions in the classroom, etc\.\) for inclusive teaching\. Feel free to check them out [here](https://inclusiveteaching.ctlt.ubc.ca/resources/resources-for-faculty/)\.

                | **Canvas Workshop Recap** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    If you missed the Canvas workshop on Oct 7, a recording is now available\. In this workshop, the Teaching and Learning Support Team introduced the settings of assignments, quizzes, and tips for using the gradebook on Canvas\. The recording can be found [here](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/news-events/canvas-workshop-assignments-quizzes-and-gradebook/)\.  
                    **TLS Drop\-in Sessions**  
                    We organize weekly drop\-in sessions on **Fridays \(1\-2 pm\)** to answer teaching\-related questions \(e\.g\., Canvas, in\-class live streaming and recording, etc\.\)\. Both instructors and TAs are welcome to  
                    join the [Zoom meeting](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/62814050960?pwd=OXFlZ3FTcDZOWWp4T3lvTlNpNk9sZz09)\.
                    
                    **Classroom Live Streaming and Recording  
                    **To schedule the classroom live streaming and recording, please submit you request **at least 2 business days** before  
                    your class to Central AV via this [link](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_d0CchruSKrBVigS)\. For more information, please check out this [website](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/recording-and-streaming-classrooms)\. You can follow the steps in this [tip\-sheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip_Sheet_Requesting_Classroom_Recording_and_Streaming.pdf) to fill out the reservation form\. Feel free to contact [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.ithelp@ubc.ca) to discuss the flexible options for in\-person/online teaching\.

                | **Ongoing Construction Activity Near Classrooms** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Project teams are working diligently to minimize construction impact when possible on the UBC Vancouver campus\. Some construction work is continuing into the fall and impacting students, faculty, and staff\. You may notice a higher\-than\-normal volume of construction projects underway on campus\.
                    
                    **Noise Impacts during Construction  
                    **Project teams are trying to schedule noisier work and planned shutdowns outside of scheduled exams and specified events\. They are unable to move all classes that are near the construction areas, due to limited classroom availability\.
                    
                    Project teams are doing their best to maintain service and minimize impact in your area\. For specific questions or concerns, please contact the Project Manager, Project Coordinator, or your [Facilities Manager](http://www3.buildingoperations.ubc.ca/facility-manager-search/)\.
