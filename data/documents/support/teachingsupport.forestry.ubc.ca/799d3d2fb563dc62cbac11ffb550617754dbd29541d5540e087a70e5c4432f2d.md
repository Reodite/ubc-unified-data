                | **Classroom AV &amp; Lecture Capturing** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    To book an AV demo in the Faculty of Forestry, please contact [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\.  
                    If your assigned classrooms are outside of our faculty, we would be happy to help you try the AV system in a Forestry classroom with similar setups\.  
                    
                    To schedule the classroom live streaming and recording, please submit your request **at least 2 business days** before your  
                    class to Central AV via this [link](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_d0CchruSKrBVigS)\.  
                    For more information, please check out this [website](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/recording-and-streaming-classrooms)\. You can follow the steps in this [tip\-sheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip_Sheet_Requesting_Classroom_Recording_and_Streaming.pdf) to fill out the reservation form\.  
                    
                    Here are the tip\-sheets for using AV systems in Forestry classrooms\.  
                    
                      
                    \(1\) Learning spaces with built\-in live streaming and recording systems:  
                    [FSC 1001/1003/1005/1221](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip_Sheet_Teaching_with_In-room_Recording_and_Streaming-1.pdf)  
                    
                      
                    \(2\) [Mobile AV cart in FSC 1001](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip_Sheet_Teaching_with_Mobile_AV_Cart_Updated.pdf)  
                    
                      
                    \(3\) [General learning spaces without built\-in live streaming or recording systems](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip_Sheet_Teaching_in_General_Learning_Spaces.pdf)  
                    
                      
                    \(4\) [Alternative recording options for in\-person classes](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tipsheet-Alternative-Recording-Options-for-In-person-Classes.pdf)  
                    
                      
                    \(5\) [Sharing class recordings with students](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tipsheet-Sharing-Class-Recordings-with-Students-.pdf)

                | **Zoom &amp; Teams Video Retention Policy** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                      
                    Starting in October 2023, UBC will begin removing video recordings from Zoom and Microsoft Teams automatically, one year  
                    after each video is recorded\.  
                    This new video retention policy will affect all faculty, staff, and student employees who use UBC accounts to record  
                    with these tools\. Read more in UBC LT Hub’s latest news post [here](https://lthub.ubc.ca/2022/10/28/zoom-and-microsoft-teams-video-retention-policy/)\.
                    
                      
                    Feel free to contact  
                    [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca) if you have any questions\.
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | **Teams Chat Retention Policy** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | Since April 30, 2021, UBC has adopted a schedule of removing personal chat messages in Microsoft Teams after 90 days\. Messages in Teams channels will not be affected by this policy\. Please check out [this tip\-sheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/UBC-MS-Teams-Chats-Retention-Policy.pdf) for more information\. |
