The one\-button studio is stored in Forest Science Centre \(FSC\) 1502\. It allows you to create a video within a few minutes\. The unit is portable, and, with proper notification to the Faculty, can be taken to high head / wet lab spaces or other classrooms for demonstrations\. Please contact Derek Dong to book the studio, and please note that you may need a separate booking for the room in which you plan to use the studio\. Instructions for operation are attached below\.
