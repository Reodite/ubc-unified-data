                | **Final Exam Support** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    As the final exam season approaches \(see the [UBC Exam Dates and Deadlines](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/enrolment-services/scheduling-records-systems-management/scheduling-services/exam-scheduling/academic-final-exams/exam-dates-and-deadlines)\), we organized the following tip\-sheets to help teaching teams plan and deliver exams\.
                    
                    To understand the intricacies of UBC exam policies, academic accommodation and concession, invigilation and student conduct during exams, viewing marked exams, and more, feel free to check out [this tip\-sheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2023/12/Tipsheet-Exam-Policy.pdf)\.
                    
                    In addition, about the technical tips on the logistics for various exam options \(e\.g\., in\-person paper exam, in\-person computer exam, and online Canvas exam\), please refer to [this tip\-sheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2023/12/Tipsheet-Technical-Tips-for-Exam-Logistics.pdf)\. 
                    
                    A gentle reminder for all instructors to submit final student grades to the Faculty Service Centre \(FSC\) after posting the grades on Canvas:
                    
                    \(1\) 7 business days following the examination date, ***OR***
                    
                    \(2\) 5 days following examination dates written on the last 2 days of the examination period\.
                    
                    Before uploading final course grades, make sure the required data columns are visible for grades entry and upload, and your selected file format allows the ability to upload grades\. For the full detailed instructions on uploading grades, go to the  
                    [FSC User Guide](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/FSCUserGuide.pdf) \(see page 16 onwards\)\.
                    
                      
                    For questions or assistance, feel free to contact the Forestry Teaching &amp; Learning Support Team [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)  
                    in advance for exam support\.

                | **New Forestry Field Equipment Procedure** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    There is a new email address to submit all equipment requests: [field\.equip@ubc\.ca](mailto:field.equip@ubc.ca)\.  
                    The teaching equipment requests should be made by faculty members only\. TAs can be delegated to pick up and return the equipment if specifically mentioned and named in the request email\. Trimble lab equipment requests should be made by faculty and research staff only\.
                    
                    Please provide at least 1\-week notice, and promptly return all gear as scheduled\. UBC card is required for equipment sign\-out\.  
                    **Please send your requests for next term by mid\-December\.**

                | **UBC’s Student Mental Health Provider** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | [Here2Talk](https://here2talk.ca/) is the new student mental health assistance provider\. It allows UBC students to have immediate access to counselling and community referral services across phone, online chat, and mobile app\. Its services are free and available 24/7, in multiple languages\. |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | **Embedded Counselling** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | Students within the Faculty of Forestry are able to book a 1:1 session with our embedded mental health counsellor\. If students are interested in booking a session, please direct them to call Counselling Services at 604\-822\-3811 and indicate that they are a Forestry student\. [Click here](https://forestry.ubc.ca/students/mental-health-wellbeing/?login) for more details\. |
