                | **Grades Submission Deadline** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | Grades for all courses this term must be submitted to the Faculty Service Centre \(FSC\): \(1\) **within 7 working days** following the final exam date \(grades on Canvas do not count as submitted\) \(2\) by **April 26** for courses without final exams\. \(3\) by **May 3** if the final exam is written on the last 2 days of the exam period\. \(4\) by **May 3** if the final grades or grades\-to\-date are for graduating students\. Delays will result in students unable to graduate\. More details about how to submit grades to FSC can be found [here](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/FSCUserGuide.pdf)\. |

                | **Student Mental Health Resources** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    [Here2Talk](https://here2talk.ca/main) is the student mental health assistance provider\. It provides 24/7 access to counselling and community referral services across phone, online chat, and mobile app for UBC students\.
                    
                    Additionally, students within the Faculty of Forestry are able to book a 1:1 session with our [embedded counsellor](https://forestry.ubc.ca/students/mental-health-wellbeing/), Nicole\. Please direct them to call Counselling Services at 604\-822\-3811 and indicate that they are a Forestry student\.

                | **Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\) Programs** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    **– SoTL Seed Program**  
                    This program provides those interested in exploring the impact of their pedagogical choices with graduate research specialists, collegial collaborations, and limited funding\. All UBC faculty and staff can apply\. The deadline for applications is **May 27**\. Click [here for more info\.](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-seed-program/)
                    
                    **– SoTL Linkage Grants**  
                    These grants support the formation of interdisciplinary teams of SoTL researchers that seek to investigate fundamental teaching questions in multiple pedagogical contexts\. Notices of Intent will need to be submitted by **May 29**\. Click [here for more info\.](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-linkage-grants/)
