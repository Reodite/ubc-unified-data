                | **In\-Person and Online Canvas Exams** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Instructors may use [LockDown Browser](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/lockdown-browser-instructor-guide/) to prevent students from accessing external websites or applications during Canvas exams\. It is highly recommended to arrange a practice session [so students can test the LockDown Browser setup before the exam](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/lockdown-browser-student-guide/)\.
                    
                    To accommodate students unable to install LockDown Browser, consider these options:  
                    – Canvas Quiz \(without LockDown Browser\) with in\-person monitoring\.  
                    – Paper exams with in\-person monitoring\.
                    
                    Invigilators should use *Moderate This Quiz* to track student progress and refer to the *Quiz Log* to investigate potential issues \(e\.g\., unsaved answers or connectivity problems\)\.
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | **Exam Accommodations for Students with Accessibility Needs** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    When students register for exam accommodations, The Centre for Accessibility \(CfA\) will contact instructors to submit exam details for any required adjustments\. You can refer to this [Guide on Submitting Exam Information](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/UBC-Centre-for-Accessibility-Guide-on-Submitting-Exam-Information-Sept-2023.pdf)\.
                    
                    If desired, you can choose to **OPT OUT** of Canvas adjustments through CfA’s online portal\.  
                    – Selecting “Yes” to OPT OUT means that CfA will provide a list of students requiring accommodations, and either the teaching team or Forestry’s Teaching &amp; Learning Support team can adjust Canvas exam settings\.  
                    – This approach ensures smoother coordination and reduces confusion about exam durations, allowances, or other settings\.
                    
                    For questions or support, please contact [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\.

                | Grade Submission Reminders |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    A gentle reminder for all instructors to submit final student grades to Workday:
                    
                    \(1\) 7 business days following the examination date, ***OR***
                    
                    \(2\) 5 days following examination dates written on the last 2 days of the examination period, with the **final deadline on Dec 31, 2024**\.
                    
                    \(3\) For courses without final exams, grades are due no later than two weeks after the last day of classes\.
                    
                    **Note:**  
                    Please do not release provisional or final grades on Canvas before the end of the exam period as per the dates above\.
                    
                    – For undergraduate courses, UBC keeps grades suppressed until the exam period is over so that students do not become distressed over a poor grade in one class while they are still preparing for exams in other classes\.  
                    – For the system to operate effectively, instructors should mirror this practice and suppress the release of grades on Canvas\.
                    
                    The [Final Grade Submission Tool](https://confluence.it.ubc.ca/display/IRPTCM/Training+Hub+for+Instructors#:~:text=How%20to%20Upload%20Grades%20into%20the%20Final%20Grade%20Submission%20Tool%20via%20Class%20Lists%C2%A0) in Workday is available to instructors\. Step\-by\-step guide can be found on the [Faculty/Instructor Training Hub](https://confluence.it.ubc.ca/display/IRPTCM/Training+Hub+for+Instructors)\.
                    
                    In addition, Enrolment Services is offering:  
                    1\. **Q&amp;A Zoom Sessions** to guide instructors through grade submission processes and troubleshoot issues\.  
                    2\. **Live Troubleshooting Support** via email from Dec 9\-23\.
                    
                    Additional info is available on the [Resources and Support for UBC faculty and instructors webpage](https://irp.ubc.ca/ongoing-instructor-support)\.
