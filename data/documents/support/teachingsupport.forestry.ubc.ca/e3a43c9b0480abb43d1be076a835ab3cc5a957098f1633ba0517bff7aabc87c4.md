                    **Alternative Recording Options for In\-person Classes**  
                    The TLS team has completed a [tip\-sheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tipsheet-Alternative-Recording-Options-for-In-person-Classes.pdf?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail) to explore different recording options that you can consider if there is no in\-class recording system available in your classroom or if you would like to learn alternative recording options to add another dimension to your teaching\.
                    
                      
                    
                    **Other Tip\-sheets for Classroom Livestreaming and Recording**  
                    View [tip\-sheets](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/learning-technologies/recording-and-live-streaming/?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail) for using in\-room live streaming and recording systems, touch panels, and more\.
                    
                      
                      
                    ****Sharing Class Recordings with Students  
                    ****Here is the [tip\-sheet](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tipsheet-Sharing-Class-Recordings-with-Students-.pdf?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail) for different options to share class recordings with students\.

                    **TLS Drop\-in Session  
                    **Time: Friday \(March 4\) 13:00 – 14:00  
                    Open discussion\. [Join the Zoom meeting](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/65575469757?pwd=M2tMNndhM1VUVk1KT29BSzU0ZkFwZz09&amp;utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail)\.
                    
                      
                      
                    **Workshop: Student Peer Review Tools  
                    **Time: Friday \(March 11\) 13:00 – 14:00  
                    This workshop is for anyone in the faculty who wants to learn more about how they can incorporate student peer review activities into their classrooms using the various learning tools available at UBC\. Dr\. Lindsay Cuff and Dr\. Duffy Roberts will be joining us to share their experiences using PeerScholar and ComPAIR in their courses\. Together, the TLS team will present a comparative view of other student peer evaluation tools more commonly used in Forestry, including iPeer and Canvas Peer Review\. [Join the Zoom meeting](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/65575469757?pwd=M2tMNndhM1VUVk1KT29BSzU0ZkFwZz09&amp;utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail)\.
