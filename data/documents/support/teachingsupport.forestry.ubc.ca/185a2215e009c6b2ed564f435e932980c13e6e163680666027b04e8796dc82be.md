**Topic:** Faculty of Forestry Peer Assessment Workshop 2021

**Facilitators:** TLS Team

**Time:** Aug 18, 2021 11:00AM

**Description:**

This is a workshop for anyone in the Faculty who want to familiarize themselves more with Peer assessment tools\. We will dive into different peer assessment tools that can be integrated into the course to increase engagement within students\. Some tools we will touch on are: Canvas Peer Review, iPeer, Collaborative Learning Annotation System \(CLAS\) and ComPAIR\. Faculty, staff, TAs and Academic Assistants are all welcome\.

**Key topics:**

-   Canvas Peer Review
-   iPeer
-   Collaborative Learning Annotation System \(CLAS\)
-   ComPAIR
