#### Event Details

-   **Start:** 25 February 2021 12:00 pm
-   **End:** 25 February 2021 1:30 pm

---

#### Facilitator:

**Stephanie Savage, Lucas Wright, Michelle Zeng  
**

#### **Description**:

Join Stephanie Savage, Scholarly Communications and Copyright Services Librarian, and Lucas Wright, Senior Educational Consultant, for a conversation about copyright considerations for teaching in an online environment and the ways UBC\-supported learning technologies and media can be used to facilitate the sharing and dissemination of materials in a copyright compliant manner\. A brief presentation will be followed up with ample time for questions\.

**Where**: Zoom
