                | **Exam Scheduling** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    For summer courses, instructors must submit **Term 1** final exam requests to the department exam contact by **4:30 pm, May 14**\. Note that the exam period is [June 24\-28](https://trk.cp20.com/click/gfbl-9iane-ddl91-375fn89/)\. Information and support for examination scheduling can be accessed via the [exam website](https://trk.cp20.com/click/gfbl-9iane-ddl92-375fn80/)\.
                    
                    There are different exam formats \(in\-person paper, in\-person computer, online Canvas, etc\.\) that instructors can consider\. Refer to the following tip\-sheets prepared by the Teaching &amp; Learning Support team:  
                    – [Navigating the Choice: Online or In\-Person Assessments](https://trk.cp20.com/click/gfbl-9iane-ddl93-375fn81/)\.  
                    – [UBC Exam Policies, Procedures, and Reminders](https://trk.cp20.com/click/gfbl-9iane-ddl94-375fn82/)\.  
                    – [Technical Tips for Exam Logistics](https://trk.cp20.com/click/gfbl-9iane-ddl95-375fn83/)\.

                | **Generative AI Resources for Teaching &amp; Learning** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | UBC’s generative AI in teaching and learning advisory committee has been working on the guidelines for using generative AI tools in teaching and learning\. More information will be released after their consultations are concluded\. Meanwhile, you can check out the latest advice on [CTLT AI website](https://trk.cp20.com/click/gfbl-9iane-ddl9a-375fn87/)\. If you’d like to discuss any aspects of AI in your teaching in Forestry, please reach out to [sarah\.gergel@ubc\.ca](mailto:sarah.gergel@ubc.ca) or [michelle\.zeng@ubc\.ca](mailto:michelle.zeng@ubc.ca)\. |

                | **Turnitin with Canvas Integration** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | Turnitin is now available within Canvas, seamlessly integrating plagiarism checking within Canvas assignments, SpeedGrader, and Gradebook\. Teaching teams can check out UBC’s updated [Turnitin instructor guide](https://trk.cp20.com/click/gfbl-9iane-ddl9b-375fn88/)\. If you need support or have questions, please contact us at [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\. |
