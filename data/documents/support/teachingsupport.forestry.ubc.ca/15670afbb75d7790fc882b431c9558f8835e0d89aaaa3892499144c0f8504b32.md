                | **Changes Coming in W2 to Publisher Content and Tools in Canvas** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Willo Labs has been used to include publisher content and tools in UBC Canvas courses\. Examples of this type of content/tool include Achieve, WileyPlus, Connect, InQuizitive, MindTap, and Mastering\. These tools, often bundled with textbooks, requrie instructors to coordinate setup directly with the publishers\.  
                    Recently, Willo Labs was abruptly discontinued\. A new way is being explored to include publisher content and tools in Canvas courses\.
                    
                    **If your Canvas has publisher content and tools mentioned above, note that:**   
                    – You will not experience any impact on course content this term\.  
                    – Students will not lose access to paid course content this term\.  
                    – For multi\-term courses, students will need to contact the LT Hub in W2 to regain access to paid course content\.  
                    – You will not need to update existing orders for W2 course content\.  
                    – You may need to take extra steps to update your publisher\-based course materials in Canvas to prepare them for W2\.
                    
                    For more information, refer to this [webpage](https://lthub.ubc.ca/2024/10/31/changes-coming-to-how-publisher-content-and-tools-are-included-in-canvas/)\. For questions or support, feel free to contact [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\.

                | **Using External Learning Technologies in Teaching** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    A friendly reminder that external learning technologies need to pass UBC’s [Privacy Impact Assessment \(PIA\)](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/pia-stra) before implementation in teaching\.
                    
                    If you need to use an external learning technology for your courses, feel free to contact the TLS team \([forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\) for support and consultation\. Note that [all tools supported by UBC LT Hub](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/) undergo a PIA to receive central technical and pedagogical support\.
