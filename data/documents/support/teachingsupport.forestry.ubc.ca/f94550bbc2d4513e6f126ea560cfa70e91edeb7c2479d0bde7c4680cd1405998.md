                    | LockDown Browser Ban |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        Students may receive a LockDown Browser **ban message** when Respondus detects software [activity that may conflict with the secure testing environment](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/8b534d00f277aa62e9769e9437a3fc84/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/8b534d00f277aa62e9769e9437a3fc84/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e")\. Please note this technical safeguard is **not an academic misconduct determination\.**
                        
                        Once banned, the **student account will be unable to open** any assessments in LDB, even in other courses, or devices\.
                        
                        To regain access, the student must provide the reference code \(displayed in the ban message\) for the [instructor to contact the TLS team](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/cb80bf67c18e5851cfb69e9437a474ed/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/cb80bf67c18e5851cfb69e9437a474ed/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e") \(forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca\) **for further action\.**
