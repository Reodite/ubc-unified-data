                        **Don’t know where to find TLS team? We’ve moved\!**
                        
                        The TLS Hub is now located in FSC Room 2625\. Feel free to stop by to get learning and teaching support\! Michelle Zeng, is still located in room 2718\.
                        
                        **Message from Associate Dean, Academic:** ***AI Office Hours for Faculty***
                        
                        With recent advances in generative AI and AI in the past year, we encourage you to re\-examine a few AI tools, your syllabus statement and consider elaborating AI usage policies in your upcoming courses\. If you are struggling with how to deal with AI in your classroom please join Sarah and Michelle on Friday Aug 29th from 11am\-2pm in FSC 3006 or via [Zoom](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/7402260482?pwd=THFGRlhFdzZpVDY3WTcra3RrQ2d0UT09&amp;omn=66853339485)\.
                        
                        Meeting ID: 740 226 0482  
                        Passcode: 123123

                        **Teaching Support &amp; Canvas Resources**
                        
                        Take a look at the [Teaching Primer](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/faculty-resources/) that the TLS team has prepared you for important information, resources and support available to you as an instructor, whether you are new or returning\.
                        
                        In addition, we have organized the [Canvas &amp; EdTech Resources Site](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/8ALMP8) to compile key resources for setting up Canvas courses for instructors, teaching assistants, and staff in Forestry\.
                        
                        **Submitting Course Materials**
                        
                        The UBC library has key information about submitting your course materials through the UBC Library Syllabus Service and the [Library Online Course Reserves \(LOCR\) system](https://trk.cp20.com/click/gfbl-bt5f7-tcabbo-33oe8b8/)
                        
                        1\. Syllabus Service
                        
                        2\. Self\-Service
                        
                        3\. Merging Canvas Course Sections
                        
                        4\. Accessing Course Materials
                        
                        Full details can be found on this [website](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/ubc-library-course-reserve-materials-fall-2025-winter-2026/), or any questions can be directed to syllabus\.service@ubc\.ca, koerner\.reserve@ubc\.ca, or reach out to your [library branch](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/using-course-reserves/#Contacts-3)\.

                        **Campus as a Living Lab Fund Competition**
                        
                        This competition offers up to $500,000 in total seed funding to support innovative, interdisciplinary projects led by UBC Vancouver faculty and staff\. An information session will be held on September 16th, and more details and RSVP information can be found [here](https://livinglabs.ubc.ca/programs-and-initiatives/cll-fund-competition)\.
                        
                        **TLEF and OER Fund Support Sessions**
                        
                        Educational development, instructional design, and media design support are available to applicants who are developing TLEF or OER Fund proposals\. Full event details can be found on this [website](https://eventsreg.ctlt.ubc.ca/event-category/tlef/)\.

                        **Forestry TA Workshop**
                        
                        Time: September 5th from 11:am\-3:00pm \(In\-Person\)
