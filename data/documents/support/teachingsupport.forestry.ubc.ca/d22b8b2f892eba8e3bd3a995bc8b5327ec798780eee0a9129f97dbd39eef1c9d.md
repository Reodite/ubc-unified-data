The August Final Exam schedule  with locations is now available  in Workday and the information will be posted publicly [online](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/exams/exam-schedule) later this afternoon\.

You may download the exam schedule in [Workday](https://wd10.myworkday.com/ubc/d/home.htmld)\. To do so, please follow the steps below:

1.  Log in to [Workday](https://wd10.myworkday.com/ubc/d/home.htmld)
2.  Enter “Final Exam Schedules – Central“ into the Search bar
3.  Select Academic Period &gt; Current Periods &gt; 2024\-25 UBC\-V Academic Year &gt;   2025 Summer Session \(UBC\-V\)
    1.  In the “Exam Date From \(On or After\)” field enter 2025\-08\-12
    2.  In the “Exam Date To \(On or After\)” field enter 2025\-08\-16
4.  Click OK
5.  Choose Export to Excel or Worksheets from the top right of the report

Further Information on viewing the final exam schedule on Workday:

1.  **Instructors** – [Training Hub for Instructors](https://confluence.it.ubc.ca/display/IRPTCM/Training+Hub+for+Instructors) under “How to View the Final Exam Schedule”\. Visit this link for specific [training materials](https://confluence.it.ubc.ca/display/IRPTCM/Training+Hub+for+Instructors?preview=/345442855/364435223/How%20to%20View%20the%20Final%20Exam%20Schedule.pdf)\.
2.  **Students** – A [step\-by\-step guide](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/degree-planning/viewing-your-exam-schedule/) is available\.

Not familiar with your exam location? More details about the general teaching space can be found on the [Learning Spaces Website](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/find-space)\.  Floorplans showing washroom locations are being added to the [website](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/find-a-space/)\. They can be found on each building page, under Resources &gt; Floor Plan\. The floorplans indicate whether the washrooms are gendered or all\-gender, as well as whether the washroom is accessible\.

Senate Exam Rules related to [student conduct](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/academic-assessment/student-conduct-during-examinations) are available on the [academic calendar](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/academic-assessment/student-conduct-during-examinations)\.

Please be familiar with the [Exam Invigilation Guidelines](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/enrolment-services/scheduling-records-systems-management/scheduling-services/exam-scheduling/exam-invigilation/exam-invigilation-guidelines) prior to your exam\.

If you need to reach out for support, requests can be submitted with this [**ServiceNow** form](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=540d0aa61b3eb0901b75c995604bcba7#_blank)\.
