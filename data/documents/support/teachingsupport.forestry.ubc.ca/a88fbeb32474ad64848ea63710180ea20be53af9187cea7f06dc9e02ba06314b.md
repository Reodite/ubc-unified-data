                    **Zoom &amp; Teams Video Retention Policy  
                    **Starting in October 2023, UBC will begin removing video recordings from Zoom and Microsoft Teams automatically, one year after each video is recorded\.
                    
                    This new video retention policy will affect all faculty, staff, and student employees who use UBC accounts to record with these tools\. Read more in UBC LT Hub’s latest news post [here](https://lthub.ubc.ca/2022/10/28/zoom-and-microsoft-teams-video-retention-policy)\.
                    
                    Feel free to contact [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.ithelp@ubc.ca) if you have any questions\.
                    
                    **Using the “forestry\.ubc\.ca” Domain  
                    **At UBC, everyone is accountable for complying with information security standards and policy, including hosting a website under the forestry\.ubc\.ca domain\.
                    
                    If you have a website hosted outside of UBC and under a non\-forestry\.ubc\.ca domain, we strongly encourage you to make a change and migrate it back under the forestry\.ubc\.ca domain, and back within UBC systems\.
                    
                    Need help migrating? Please contact [forestry\.ithelp@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.ithelp@ubc.ca)\.

                    ****Forestry Teaching and Learning Drop\-in Sessions  
                    ****Time: Fridays \(1 – 2 pm\)We organize weekly drop\-in sessions to answer teaching related questions\. Instructors and TAs are welcome to join the [Zoom meeting](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/62814050960?pwd=OXFlZ3FTcDZOWWp4T3lvTlNpNk9sZz09)\.
                    
                    Note: there will be no session this Friday as it is Remembrance Day\.
                    
                    Evaluating Open Educational Resources \(OER\) Projects  
                    Time: Nov 10 \(11 am – 12:30 pm\)  
                    This workshop introduces evaluation frameworks to those working on an OER Fund project or incorporating OER into their teaching and learning\. More info [here](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/evaluating-oer-projects-november-10-2022/)\.
                    
                    **Considerations for Evaluation of Indigenous\-focused Projects  
                    **Time: Nov 21 \(10 – 11:30 am\)  
                    This workshop introduces project evaluations in relation to projects with an Indigenous focus or those that seek to engage Indigenous partners and communities\. More info [here](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/considerations-for-evaluation-of-indigenous-focused-projects/)\.
                    
                    **Anti\-Racism and Solidarity in University Settings  
                    **Time: Nov 30 \(1 – 3 pm\)  
                    This workshop explores anti\-racist teaching and learning practices that support students, faculty, and staff\. More info [here](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/anti-racism-and-solidarity-in-university-settings-nov-30-2022/)\.
