In addition to the one\-button studio housed in FSC 3003, we have one more addition to our online teaching and learning ‘bag of tricks’ that may be of interest to you\.

We have set up a portable AV cart in FSC 1001\.  This is a full‐featured and video conferencing cart that uses Zoom Room software to allow you to conduct live classes and/or record asynchronous classes in a classroom setting\.
