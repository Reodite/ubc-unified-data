                        **Classroom Recording Guidelines**
                        
                        Recording classes is a matter of instructor choice, which should be **indicated on the syllabus**\. Please see the [UBC guidelines and policies for recording classroom activities](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/3e13b054ca1a090b62f6aa193d96b6b3/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/3e13b054ca1a090b62f6aa193d96b6b3/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d")\.
                        
                        **Syllabus Reminders**
                        
                        Please be reminded that **instructors** **are required to provide a syllabus** to students registered in their course section within the first week of class\.
                        
                        Under UBC policy, the **syllabus must include:**
                        
                        -   [An AI Statement](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/50e1d5f2a95e1472eaa6aa193d96c040/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/50e1d5f2a95e1472eaa6aa193d96c040/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d")\.
                        -   Details of when and by what
                        -   means students may contact the instructor\.
                        -   A proposed course schedule\.
                        -   A list of required learning materials and an estimate of associated costs\.
                        -   A description of the learning activities and assessment methods\.
                        
                        UBC CTLT has provided [suggestions and resources for GenAI syllabus language](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/698a70dc4061b12939e6aa193d96c86c/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/698a70dc4061b12939e6aa193d96c86c/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d") around student use on their website\. See the [SENATE POLICY: V\-130](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/7ea3e237229c3f97e7b6aa193d96d00e/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/7ea3e237229c3f97e7b6aa193d96d00e/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d") for the full Syllabus Guidelines\. UBC CTLT has also prepared a [syllabus template](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/fe99a9c6f5fa790b6f86aa193d96dab4/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/fe99a9c6f5fa790b6f86aa193d96dab4/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d") available for download as a Word Doc\.

                        **LockDown Browser**
                        
                        LockDown Browser is a secure browser
                        
                        that restricts copying and pasting questions or visits to unauthorized websites\.
                        
                        ***What is ORCA?***
                        
                        ORCA provides secure, in\-person digital assessment services\. This accompanying [TLS tipsheet](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/1c59ee3fcabc383b18e6aa193d973cf8/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/1c59ee3fcabc383b18e6aa193d973cf8/7e844029a3259789ce0e37179a660c5d") provides an overview of ORCA and its benefits for instructors and students\. Those interested in using ORCA for 2026W2 are invited to contact FES TLS team at [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca "mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca")

                        **TA Hours Allocation Form &amp; Safety Plan**
                        
                        The TA Hours Allocation form is now a digital Qualtrics form\! If you have not received one to fill out yet, kindly contact your department administrator for further assistance\.
                        
                        Instructors are responsible for completing the TA Hours Allocation Form &amp; Safety Plan\. Once submitted, the form should automatically be sent to the email used in the hours allocation form\. Then the TAs will have a chance to carefully review and acknowledge the assigned hours and duties\. It can be helpful for teaching teams to meet and clarify expectations at the start of term\.
