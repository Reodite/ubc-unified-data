###### Intro to Lockdown Browser

Lockdown Browser can be used to invigilate online exams\. Please note that Lockdown Browser does not work in the UG Computing Labs as Lockdown Browser is designed for personal machines only\. Click [here](https://support.respondus.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409607170459-What-are-the-computer-requirements-for-installations-of-Respondus-LockDown-Browser-) for the minimum computer requirements to use LockDown browser
