                        **Technical Tips for Exam Logistics**
                        
                        Please see this [tip sheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tip-Sheet-Technical-Tips-for-Exam-Logistics-V6.pdf) for recommendations on planning and delivering exams across a range of formats\.
                        
                        For further assessment strategies, please refer to this [TLS webpage](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/assessment-strategies/)\. Points of interest may include the *UBC Exam Policies, Procedures, and Reminders* and *Navigating the Choice of Online and In\-Person Assessments\.*
                        
                        **Exam Accommodations for Students with Accessibility Needs**  
                        When students register for exam accommodations, The Centre for Accessibility \(CfA\) will contact instructors to submit exam details for any required adjustments\. You can refer to this [Guide on Submitting Exam Information](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/UBC-Centre-for-Accessibility-Guide-on-Submitting-Exam-Information-Sept-2023.pdf)\.
                        
                        If desired, you can choose to **OPT OUT** of Canvas adjustments through CfA’s online portal\.  
                        – Selecting “Yes” to OPT OUT means that CfA will provide a list of students requiring accommodations, and either the teaching team or Forestry’s Teaching &amp; Learning Support team can adjust Canvas exam settings\.  
                        – This approach ensures smoother coordination and reduces confusion about exam durations, allowances, or other settings\.

                        **In\-Person and Online Canvas Exams**  
                        Instructors may use [LockDown Browser](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/lockdown-browser-instructor-guide/) to prevent students from accessing external websites or applications during Canvas exams\. It is highly recommended to arrange a [practice session so students can test the LockDown Browser](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/lockdown-browser-student-guide/) setup before the exam\.
                        
                        **Some examples of using LockDown Brower include:**
                        
                        • In\-person Canvas Quiz with LockDown Browser
                        
                        • Online Canvas Quiz with LockDown Browser
                        
                        • Online Canvas Quiz with LockDown Brower for video or audio submission via Kaltura\. Detailed instructions can be found [here](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/Tips-on-Creating-Audio-and-Video-Submission-Quizzes-on-Canvas-with-LDB.pdfhttps://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/Tips-on-Creating-Audio-and-Video-Submission-Quizzes-on-Canvas-with-LDB.pdf)\.
                        
                        • Note that Lock Down Browser is installed in Forestry UG labs\.
                        
                        **To accommodate students unable to install LockDown Browser, consider these options:**  
                        – Canvas Quiz \(without LockDown Browser\) with in\-person monitoring\.  
                        – Paper exams with in\-person monitoring\.
                        
                        Invigilators should use *Moderate This Quiz* to track student progress and refer to the *Quiz Log* to investigate potential issues \(e\.g\., unsaved answers or connectivity problems\)\.

                        **New Features on Canvas**
                        
                        Smart Search
                        
                        Allows students to quickly find specific content across the course canvas page using keyword search\.
                        
                        Assignment Enhancements – Student
                        
                        Provides a Progress Tracker and Assignment Summary at the top of the Assignment page for better workflow\.
                        
                        To enable the new features:
                        
                        1\. Go to **Settings** in the left navigation menu in Canvas\.
                        
                        2\. Choose the **Feature Options** tab at the top of the page
                        
                        3\. Scroll down to **Smart Search/Assignment Enhancements – Student**
                        
                        4\. Click on the **red “x”** on the right, and choose “**Enable**“

                        **2025 CTLT Winter Institute**
                        
                        December 8\-11 \| In\-Person &amp; Online
