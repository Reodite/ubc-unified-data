### Step\-by\-Step Instructions on Self\-Booking FSC Meeting Rooms \(per FES IT\)

**How to reserve a room in Outlook:** [PDF Instructions](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/FSC-Occupants-Room-Booking-Procedure-2026.pdf) and [Youtube Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/j5EnpWK566Y)\.

Managing Calendars: [FASmail – Usage / Training Guides \| UBC IT](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/ubc-faculty-staff-email-fasmail/fasmail-usagetraining-guides)

When creating your booking request, please indicate a **brief descriptor for the booking purpose**\. In addition, please **include an alternate contact/organizer that we can reach out to for any questions**\.

In the event of booking conflicts, high\-level faculty events – including Dean’s Office functions \(e\.g\. Senior Management Team meetings, Faculty Accreditation meetings, Graduate student defenses, and comprehensive exams, etc\.\) will be prioritized – Individuals or groups holding existing bookings may be required to use alternate meeting space to accommodate the faculty event\(s\)\.
