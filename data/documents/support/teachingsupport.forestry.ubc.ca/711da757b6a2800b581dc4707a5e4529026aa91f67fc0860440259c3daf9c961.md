There are different options for recording and streaming lectures\.  These options are explained in the tipsheets below, and more information, including quick start guides and available support, can also be found on the [Learning Spaces website](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/recording-and-streaming-classrooms)\.

Please note that to use the in–room recording system in FSC 1001/1003/1005/1221, please make reservations from [here](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_d0CchruSKrBVigS) at least 48 hours before the session\.

**NEW UPDATE:**  
The Zoom AV Cart in FSC 1001 has been updated with an all new look\. It is decked out with a new camera, speaker, and microphone\. If you would like to use it, please contact the TLS team\.

[New AV Cart](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/06/new_av_cart-scaled.jpg)
