                        **Featuring: Salmon, Lee**
                        
                        **\[Lecturer, Forest Operations\]**
                        
                        ---
                        
                        “Helping students achieve mastery is our passion as instructors, though each of us takes a different approach\. The Center for Teaching, Learning and Technology emphasizes scaffolding, a concept many instructors use intuitively but that is worth reflecting on\. Scaffolded teaching builds mastery through progressive steps\. Unlike the linear model of laddering, scaffolding supports multiple topics at different stages of learning and encourages diverse forms of engagement\. In my practice, I find scaffolding allows for lateral and forward/backward movement on topics, which is necessary to meet a wider range of learners\. Sometimes getting to the next topic requires more than linear progression, just like getting to the next limb when climbing a tree\.”
                        
                        ---
                        
                        More information on applications of scaffolded teaching can be found on the [CTLT website under “Swim: Application”](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/94c55fc098a1e2d3f2b6a4e828a37abe/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/94c55fc098a1e2d3f2b6a4e828a37abe/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e")\.
                        
                        If you’d like to chat about scaffolded teaching with Lee, he can be reached by email at: [lee\.salmon@ubc\.ca](mailto:lee.salmon@ubc.ca "mailto:lee.salmon@ubc.ca")\.

                    | Computer\-Based Testing Facility |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        The TLS team has been exploring the use of UBC’s Computer\-Based Testing Facility \(CBTF\) and its potential for Forestry courses\. CBTF operates 4 computer labs currently and provides a secure, centrally supported assessment environment\. The facility combines network filtering, lockdown browser, centralized invigilation, and standardized testing procedures to support a more secure examination process\.
                        
                        With CBTF, students have the flexibility to book exams within a restricted period set by instructors, accommodating diverse schedules and needs\. CFA compatible sessions are also available with increased time and reduced distraction seating\.
                        
                        Further details can be found on our [CBTF Tip sheet](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/e15c163bda3bcc96c286a4e828a40457/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/e15c163bda3bcc96c286a4e828a40457/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e")\.
