                    | MATCHA Writer Pilot |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        **Seeking Pilot Program Participants**
                        
                        MATCHA Writer is a **specialized platform** designed to help **prevent AI misuse** during assessments, with features similar to LockDown Browser\.
                        
                        This tool may be **suitable for written assignments**, that benefit from safeguards such as **blocking copy\-and\-paste** from external sources, **disabling auto\-typing software**, and **detecting unusually fast typing**\.
                        
                        For a detailed overview of the **advantages and disadvantages** of MATCHA Writer, please refer to [this document](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/60c0b93afab0a85ce2a6a736cc59dca3/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/60c0b93afab0a85ce2a6a736cc59dca3/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e") prepared by the TLS team\.
                        
                        For this PIA approved Pilot, we plan to:
                        
                        -   **Limit participation** to a small number of volunteers
                        -   **Minimize data collection** by using test accounts when possible
                        -   **Make AI features optional**
                        -   **Limit access** and enable audit logging for sensitive records
                        -   **Delete pilot data** once it is no longer required
                        -   **Prevent secondary use** of student data by the platform
                        
                        Please contact the [TLS team](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca "mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca") for more information and if you are interested in piloting this program in your course\.

                        **Syllabus Reminders**
                        
                        Please be reminded that **instructors** **are required to provide a syllabus** to students registered in their course section within the first week of class\.
                        
                        Under UBC policy, the **syllabus must include:**
                        
                        -   Details of when and by what
                        -   means students may contact the instructor\.
                        -   A proposed course schedule\.
                        -   A list of required learning materials and an estimate of associated costs\.
                        -   A description of the learning activities and assessment methods\.
                        
                        Please see [SENATE POLICY: V\-130](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/f40a1c895227f3b2eaa6a736cc5c6681/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/f40a1c895227f3b2eaa6a736cc5c6681/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e") for the full Syllabus Guidelines\. UBC CTLT has also prepared a [syllabus template](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/e44c20f03cf551a315e6a736cc5d159c/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/e44c20f03cf551a315e6a736cc5d159c/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e")\.

                    | UBC Launches ORCA |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        The [Open Resource Centre for Assessments](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/12b947cd0cad51b10736a736cc5dc6eb/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/12b947cd0cad51b10736a736cc5dc6eb/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e") \(ORCA\) is currently **accepting requests from instructors teaching 2026W1 undergraduate courses** that have online exams on Canvas, PrairieLearn, Brightspace, and Webwork\.
                        
                        **Formerly known as the CBTF**, ORCA expands upon the Department of Computer Science’s Computer\-Based Testing Facility, providing secure, in\-person digital assessment services\.
                        
                        This accompanying [TLS tipsheet](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/e1ede394ef0e780e33a6a736cc5e93ca/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e "https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/cfc73c9c9ead67d55626871643345746/e1ede394ef0e780e33a6a736cc5e93ca/ea9c674d07a1973d6bb8b3e926215f9e") provides an **overview of ORCA and its benefits** for instructors and students\. Those interested are invited to contact FES TLS team at [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca "mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca")\.
