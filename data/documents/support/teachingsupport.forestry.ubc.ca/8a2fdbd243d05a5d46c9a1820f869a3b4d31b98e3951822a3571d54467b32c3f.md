                | **Call for Letters of Intent for Large TLEF Transformation Projects** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Faculty and staff at UBC Vancouver are invited to submit letters of intent for Large Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(TLEF\) Transformation projects by 3:00 pm on **July 15, 2021**\.
                    
                    Funding for Small TLEF Innovation Projects are also available, and proposals must be submitted by 3:00 PM on **November 18, 2021\.** [Le](https://tlef.ubc.ca/application/application-process/) [arn](https://tlef.ubc.ca/application/application-process/) [more\.](https://tlef.ubc.ca/application/application-process/) If you would like to request support in developing your project proposal, please contact Michelle Zeng \(michelle\.zeng@ubc\.ca\) or the TLS team \(forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca\)\.
