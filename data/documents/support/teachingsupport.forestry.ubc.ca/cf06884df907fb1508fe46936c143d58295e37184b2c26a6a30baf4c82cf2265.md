                        **Learning Technology Innovation Centre Wants to Hear From You\!**
                        
                        LTIC is a hub dedicated to elevated and transformative learning technology, committed to ensuring equitable access to learning technologies for all members of the UBC academic community\. LTIC encompasses the existing LT Hub, which will continue to provide support to all users of learning technology at UBC\. More information can be found on their [website](https://ltic.ubc.ca/)\.
                        
                        LTIC is charting its strategy for learning technology\. Lend your voice to the conversation\! Is learning technology meeting your needs? Tell them why or why not in this very [short survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_5gNg0tCVeBi8XP0)\.

                        **Climate Emergency Week**
                        
                        November 17\-21
                        
                        UBC Sustainability Hub has organized a [week full of events](https://climateemergency.ubc.ca/climate-emergency-week/), workshops, and activities to convene and energize communities of climate action\.
                        
                        **Celebrate GIS Day 2025 with Us\!**
                        
                        November 19 \(9:00am – 4:00pm\) \| Dodson Room, Koerner Library
                        
                        Annual worldwide [celebration](https://gis.ubc.ca/event/gis-day-2025/) of GIS technology and community, dedicated to promoting geospatial literacy, education, and awareness with demos, workshops and more\. If you’re interested in giving a GIS presentation or lightning/research talk, register [here](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_eUKcjZPPzWebreK) by Nov 12\.

                        **Updated Winter Term 1 Final Exam Schedule Now Available in Workday**
                        
                        The 2025 Winter Term 1 Final Exam Schedule has been updated with locations and posted publicly online now\.
                        
                        To download the exam schedule in Workday:
                        
                        1\. Log into [Workday](https://wd10.myworkday.com/ubc/d/home.htmld)
                        
                        2\. Search for “**Final Exam Schedule – Central**”
                        
                        3\. Select Academic Period \-&gt; Current Periods \-&gt; 2025\-26 UBC\-V Academic Year \-&gt; 2025\-26 Winter Term 1 \(UBC\-V\)
                        
                        4\. Click “**OK**”
                        
                        5\. Choose “**Export to Excel**” or “**Worksheets**” \(top right of the report\)
