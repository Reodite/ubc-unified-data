Announcing a Forestry specific Wi\-Fi connection for when one is on campus \(off campus, use the VPN\)\.

**Summary**: On May 2\[nd\], Forestry users should start using **UBCprivate** wi\-fi where your CWL username must be formatted as ***jdoe\.frsc***\. **UBCprivate** is for laptops, not smartphones, iPads nor other similar devices, these devices should continue to use **UBCsecure**\.

**What?**

-   Starting May 2\[nd\], Forestry’s printers and many of the Forestry license managers will be restricted to **UBCprivate**\. **UBCsecure** will no longer be able to access these Forestry resources\. Note that access to the Faculty shared drive and UBC’s resources \(OneDrive/Teams/Canvas/email\) are not impacted\.

**What do I need to do?**

-   -   **Faculty/staff** \= most, if not all, fac/staff have been bulk loaded in to UBCprivate space\. ***Just tag “\.frsc” to the end of your CWL username, e\.g\., jdoe\.frsc\.***
    -   **Grads** \= grads can register for access by emailing [forestry\.ithelp@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.ithelp@ubc.ca), cc’ing their supervisor and including their CWL username\.
    -   **Forestry visitors** \= visitors can register for access by emailing [forestry\.ithelp@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.ithelp@ubc.ca), cc’ing their faculty sponsor, with the following information: CWL username and expected end date\. Both pieces of information are required or no access will be provided\.

**What can I add to** **UBCprivate**

-   Just laptops, ***do not add smartphones or iPads***\. We can see this and will remove your access without notice\. Smartphones and iPads should just use **UBCsecure**\. Personal laptops should have UBC AMP anti\-virus installed \(available here: [https://it\.ubc\.ca/services/desktop\-print\-services/software\-licensing/software\-downloads](https://it.ubc.ca/services/desktop-print-services/software-licensing/software-downloads)\) before using **UBCprivate**\. This is a requirement for any device accessing UBC resources and data\.

This information is derived from UBC Forestry IT\.
