                        **Organizing and Submitting Final Grades**
                        
                        Grades in Canvas do not automatically transfer to Workday\. Instructors must manually submit grades using the Final Grade Submission Tool in Workday\. Please refer to this [tipsheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tips-for-Organizing-Final-Grades-for-Submission.pdf) for detailed guidance and helpful tips for completing the process\. Note that your final grades should only be posted on Workday and not on Canvas\.
                        
                        Final grades must be submitted either:
                        
                        -   7 business days following the exam date
                        -   5 days following the exam date on the last 2 days of the exam period\.
                        -   Please refer to [UBC Exam Dates and Deadlines](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/enrolment-services/scheduling-records-systems-management/scheduling-services/exam-scheduling/academic-final-exams/exam-dates-and-deadlines)
                        
                        Before uploading final course grades:
                        
                        -   Make sure the required data columns are visible for grades entry and upload, and your selected file format allows the ability to upload grades\.
                        -   For the full detailed instructions on uploading grades, go to the [FSC User Guide](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/FSCUserGuide.pdf)

                    | Oral Exams on Canvas |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        **Implementing Oral Exams Using Canvas**
                        
                        Large and small classes can run oral exams through Canvas, without requiring hundreds of live one\-on\-one sessions\. This approach works especially well for large Forestry courses to assess student ability to explain ecological processes, justify management decisions, or interpret applied scenarios, while keeping the workload manageable for the teaching team\. Some advantages include:
                        
                        -   Eliminating the need for individual live oral exam appointments
                        -   Maintaining the core benefit of oral assessments: to assess deeper thinking and understanding of student *in the moment*
                        -   Promoting academic integrity through oral exams, which is naturally resistant to AI\-assisted cheating
                        
                        More information can be found on the two options in the [Implementation of Oral Exams on Canvas tipsheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tips-on-Creating-Audio-and-Video-Submission-Quizzes-on-Canvas-with-LDB-V2-1.pdf)\.
                        
                        Additional resources can also be found from this [Oral Exam article by The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/education/2025/12/12/ai-artificial-intelligence-college-oral-exam/) and [How Oral Exams Improve Engineering Student Performance, Motivation from UC San Diego](https://today.ucsd.edu/story/oral-exams-improve-engineering-student-performance-motivation)\.
