                | **Call for Proposals: Emerging Media Lab Projects** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Faculty members at UBC Vancouver are invited to [submit a proposal](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/27db04d5450a58d851060e8c2c483d07/58b984a9a22242e0b7467ab9a726ef60/2ff3a7b04c8ec9b6e61612aed3c3adee) to work with the Emerging Media Lab \(EML\) team on incorporating technologies \(e\.g\., virtual reality, augmented reality, artificial intelligence, etc\.\) into teaching, learning or research projects\.                 | **Supporting Muslim UBC Students during Ramadan** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Many Muslim students have already begun observing Ramadan, a holy time for intention\-setting, fasting, reflection, and community connection\.
                    
                    Like previous years, a UBC [website](https://events.ubc.ca/ramadan/) has been updated to share events and opportunities for Muslim students\. A [student guide to accommodations during Ramadan](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/student_ramadan_accommodations_guide.pdf) has been published this month\.
                    
                    Supporting all students with spiritual commitments is integral to their experience at UBC\. For more information, see this [overarching student guide to religious and spiritual accommodations](https://students.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/student_religious_spiritual_accommodations_guide.pdf) \. See also the 2025 Course Instructor guides for [religious/spiritual accommodations](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/FINAL_Faculty-Staff-Religious-Spiritual-Accommodations-Guide_2025.pdf) and [Ramadan](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/FINAL_Faculty-Staff-Ramadan-Guide_2025.pdf) \.

                | **Equity, Diversity, Inclusion \+ Indigeneity Workshop: Lab Accessibility Training** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    May 28 \(9:30\-11:00\) \| In\-person or Online \(organized by Applied Science, Forestry, and LFS\)  
                    Do you make decisions about teaching labs? If you are involved in decisions about equipment, storage, layout, furnishings, safety, lab instruction, and more, this training is for you\!
                    
                    Come and learn more about accessibility in lab spaces\. Gain insight into how to make lab spaces usable by and safe for lab users with physical accessibility needs\. The focus of this training is on undergraduate teaching labs, however those involved in decisions about research lab spaces may also benefit\.                 | **H5P for Interactive Learning** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    H5P \(HTML5 Package\) is an open\-source tool available at UBC that allows instructors to create engaging, interactive content\. With 40\+ different content types \(e\.g\., interactive maps, virtual tours, etc\.\), H5P supports a variety of learning activities\.
                    
                    Feel free to check out these Forestry examples on [this webpage](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/h5p/)\.
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | **Forestry Trimble Technology Lab Resource Guide** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    [This guide](http://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/07/2022-University-of-British-Columbia-UBC-Trimble-Technology-Lab-Product-Resource-Guide.pdf) offers a wealth of resources to support the delivery of forestry courses with practical applications\. It includes a range of software tools for 3D modelling, structural analysis, and geospatial data processing\.
                    
                    It also features hardware solutions like integrated smartphone data collectors, mixed reality viewers, and high accuracy GNSS positioning devices\.
                    
                    To subscribe to the software, please email [forestry\.licensing@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.licensing@ubc.ca)\.

                | **Changes to MyLA &amp; OnTask in Canvas** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    UBC support for the learning analytics tools: My Learning Analytics \(MyLA\) and OnTask will end on Monday, April 7, 2025\. After this date, MyLA and OnTask will no longer be available to use in Canvas\. Learn more about this decision [here](https://lthub.ubc.ca/2025/03/10/central-support-for-myla-and-ontask-ending/) \.
                    
                    For questions or support, please contact [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca) \.

                | **Updates on Publisher Content and Tools in Canvas** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    A new interim process is now in place to include publisher content and tools in Canvas, such as Macmillan Achieve, McGraw Hill Connect, Norton Learning Tools, Cengage MindTap, Pearson MyLab/Mastering, and WileyPlus\.
                    
                    If your courses involve these publisher content and tools:  
                    – **You can continue to request publisher content and tools from the UBC Bookstore as usual**\. You will see a student privacy notice added to any Canvas course with publisher content/tools
                    
                    – **You will see a student privacy notice added to any Canvas course with publisher content/tools**\. To meet the information security requirements, please do not remove this notice from your course\.
                    
                    For more info, refer to [this webpage](https://lthub.ubc.ca/2025/01/28/new-interim-process-in-place-for-how-publisher-content-and-tools-are-included-in-canvas/)\.
