                    **Data Residency Restrictions Removed From FIPPA**   
                    The Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act \(FIPPA\) has recently been amended to eliminate the restriction on disclosure of personal information outside Canada\. In place of this restriction, the minister responsible for FIPPA has issued regulations that require UBC to undertake a risk assessment whenever it plans to store sensitive personal information, especially if outside Canada\. This is a must, not a recommendation or an option\. UBC conducts such risk assessments through the Privacy Impact Assessment \(PIA\) process\. Any initiative that involves the collection, use or disclosure of personal information must go through a PIA, no matter where the information will be stored\. 
                    
                    More information about this amendment, along with general instructions and reminders about the PIA process, can be found [here](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/bill22?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail)\.
                    
                    **Feedback for the Records Retention Schedules**   
                    The Records Management Office \(RMO\) is looking to update the records retention schedules and would like to welcome you to review and provide any feedback on them\. Two comment periods will be available and posted to the [Records Management Website](https://recordsmanagement.ubc.ca/schedules/?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail) on \(1\) March 15 to April 8, 2022 and \(2\) May 16 to June 9, 2022\. For additional information, please refer to [this @Issue document](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/03/@Issue-New-Records-Retention-Schedules.pdf?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail)\.

                    **Ending Refworks Subscription**  
                    UBC Library has decided to end the subscription to the RefWorks citation management software as of September 30, 2022\. All users of the Legacy or ProQuest RefWorks will have to transfer their references to another citation management tool before that date\. Support resources have been put in place to make this transition as smooth as possible\. This [RefWorks Transition guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/refworks/migration?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail) provides instructions for migrating references to Zotero and Mendeley, and answers to questions you may have\. UBC Library Research Commons also offers [workshops](https://libcal.library.ubc.ca/calendar/vancouver?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail) and [consultation bookings](https://researchcommons.library.ubc.ca/consultation-requests/?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail) for one\-on\-one support\.
                    
                    For assistance with migrating to a new tool, please contact [research\.commons@ubc\.ca](mailto:research.commons@ubc.ca)\.
                    
                    For questions about the decision, please contact [refworks\.transition@ubc\.ca](mailto:refworks.transition@ubc.ca)\.

                    Past Recent Workshop  
                    **Student Peer Review Tools Workshop**   
                    Adding peer\-to\-peer learning and reviewing to your class is one way to foster greater student engagement in your in\-person, virtual, or asynchronous classrooms\. This workshop is for anyone who wants to learn more about how they can incorporate student peer review activities into their classrooms using the various learning tools available at UBC\. Dr\. Lindsay Cuff and Dr\. Duffy Roberts joined us to share their experiences using PeerScholar and ComPAIR in their courses\. Together, the Teaching and Learning Support team presented a comparative view of other student peer evaluation tools more commonly used in Forestry, including iPeer and Canvas Peer Review\. Check out the recording, slides and summary notes [here](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/news-events/student-peer-review-tools-workshop/?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail)\.
