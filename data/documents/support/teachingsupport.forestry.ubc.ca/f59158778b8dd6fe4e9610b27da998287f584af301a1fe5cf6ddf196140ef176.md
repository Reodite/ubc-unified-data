                | **Academic Integrity and Plagiarism** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | If you have concerns about plagiarism or academic integrity with a specific student, ADS Scott Hinch \([scott\.hinch@ubc\.ca](mailto:scott.hinch@ubc.ca)\) is your first point of contact\. Additionally, UBC is in the process of building and beta\-testing an academic integrity hub which provides non\-punitive ways to turn an incident into more of a teachable moment\. See [Academic Integrity at UBC](https://academicintegrity.ubc.ca/)\. |
