                | **Resources for Students** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | This final push to the end of term can be challenging for students’ health, especially their mental health\. Some of you may come across students in distress and this email serves to reorient you to the resources available to you and to students who may need support\. |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    **The Student Assistance Program** is a free, confidential, 24/7 wellness resource with access to confidential counselling\. For more information, check [here](https://students.ubc.ca/health/ubc-student-assistance-program-sap)\.
                    
                    **UBC Health and Wellness** offer a variety of workshops, resources and information to support your overall health and wellbeing, which you can find [here](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics)\. The [Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre) is open for all students to drop\-in, and is a friendly, peer\-hosted space that offers many wellbeing programs for students\.
                    
                      
                    **Embedded Counselling:**  
                    Students within the faculty of Forestry are able to book a 1:1 session with Nicole, embedded mental health counsellor\.  
                    If students are interested in booking a session, please direct them to call Counselling Services at 604\-822\-3811 and  
                    indicate that they are a Forestry student to book an appointment\.

                - **Row 1**

                | **Panel Session: Educative Approaches to Academic Integrity** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Time: Dec 12 \(11 am – 12 pm\)  
                    
                    This panel discussion touches on a range of topics including course design and inclusive teaching, student wellbeing,  
                    and responding to misconduct\. Register[here](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/panel-educative-approaches-to-academic-integrity/)\.

                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**

                    This workshop introduces new SEI metrics and discusses how to interpret the summary statistics in instructor reports\.
