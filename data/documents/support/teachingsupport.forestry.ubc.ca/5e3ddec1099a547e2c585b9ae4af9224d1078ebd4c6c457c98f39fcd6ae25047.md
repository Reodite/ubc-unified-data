                | **TA Training Program Coordinators** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    The Faculty of Forestry is looking for 2 motivated graduate students to improve TA orientation and safety training\.  
                    These positions are paid and flexible, spanning from August 2023 to June 2024\.
                    
                    The [job advert](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/07/JD_TA_Training_Program_Coordinators_field_and_Indigenous.pdf) details the two positions:  
                    \(1\) TA Training Program Coordinator, Field Courses and Safety  
                    \(2\) TA Training Program Coordinator, Indigenous Engagement
                    
                    Please share this with any strong candidates you would like to recommend\.  
                    Applications can be sent directly to  
                    [michelle\.zeng@ubc\.ca](mailto:michelle.zeng@ubc.ca) by **July 31, 2023**\.

                | **Trimble Technology Lab Resource Guide** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    [This guide](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/07/2022-University-of-British-Columbia-UBC-Trimble-Technology-Lab-Product-Resource-Guide.pdf) offers a wealth of resources to support the teaching of practical applications in forestry\. It includes a range of software tools for geospatial data processing, and features hardware solutions, such as integrated smartphone data collectors and mixed reality viewers\.
                    
                    To use the software, please email  
                    [forestry\.licensing@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.licensing@ubc.ca)\. To access the hardware, reach out to Haitao Li at [ht\.li@ubc\.ca](mailto:ht.li@ubc.ca)\. For teaching support and consultation, contact [forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca)\.
