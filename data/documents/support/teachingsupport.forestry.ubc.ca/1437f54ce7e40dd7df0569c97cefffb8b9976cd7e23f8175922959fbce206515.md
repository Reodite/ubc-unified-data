                    Advice from the Associate Dean, Academic, Prof\. Sarah Gergel:
                    
                      
                    **\(1\) Vaccine status declarations**  
                    As part of UBC’s plan to keep everyone safe, the University is requiring all faculty, staff, and students to complete a declaration regarding their COVID\-19 vaccination status\. This policy may cause apprehension amongst students leading to questions and debate with students in non\-compliance\. Any faculty receiving such queries or complaints may use and adapt this [Response to Student Inquiries on the Vaccine Status Declaration template](https://secure.campaigner.com/media/76/766353/Response%20to%20Student%20Inquiries%20on%20the%20Vaccine%20S.pdf)
                    
                    **\(2\) Absenteeism**  
                    Due to the current situation, there is potential for high levels of absenteeism in your classes\. Please refer to [the Absenteeism tip\-sheet](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/01/PFFAC-Tip-Sheet-Absenteeism-Advice-2021W2.pdf) compiled by PFFAC to consider how you can adapt your online \(or eventual in\-person\) classes during this pandemic\.
                    
                    **\(3\) GAA Funding**  
                    We are in preparation to offer new GAA funding to support your transitions among teaching modes\. Each course is eligible for a GAA up to a total of 50 hours, with exception to faculty who has large courses, who are pre\-tenure/new to UBC or is offering courses online for the first time – If this applies to you, we are happy to entertain requests up to 100 GAA hours\. If online learning is extended again \(beyond February 7\), all 50\-hour positions will be upgraded to 75 hours\. If you would like to request GAA support, please fill out [this form](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/01/GAA-Hiring-Form.pdf) and send it to [jill\.yu@ubc\.ca](mailto:jill.yu@ubc.ca)\.
                    
                    **\(4\) Additional Support**  
                    If you need help with online teaching, learning technologies, and Canvas, please email Michelle at [michelle\.zeng@ubc\.ca](mailto:michelle.zeng@ubc.ca)\. If you need to link lab sections from SSC to Canvas, please also contact Michellle\.
                    
                    If you need help with access to the Undergradaute Computer Labs, please email [forestry\.ithelp@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.ithelp@ubc.ca)\.

Visit [SaP’s website](https://sap.ubc.ca/?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail) for more details\.
                    
                      
                    All applications should be submitted to SaP’s [application portal](https://apply.academic.ubc.ca/prog/sap-fund-cdg/?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail) and are due by **Tuesday, February 1, 2022, at 3:00 PM\.**
                    
                    CTLT has provided [an information session](https://ubc.zoom.us/rec/share/km46zro_9nO7NyBelABxQzrfRSPGMoFxVmHMEqRBQh0ZY8MDD7ZI_vh729kGcVWB.8cIaCwFxSe2FzXkO?utm_source=Email_marketing&amp;utm_campaign=Tuesday_November_16_2021&amp;cmp=1&amp;utm_medium=HTMLEmail) \(Passcode: 3HuRthV^\) specifically for the Faculty of Forestry\.
