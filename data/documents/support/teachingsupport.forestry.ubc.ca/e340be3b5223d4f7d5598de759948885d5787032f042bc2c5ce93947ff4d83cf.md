                        **Mid\-term Check\-in Survey  
                        **A mid\-term check\-in survey for the students has been created by the Forestry Teaching &amp; Learning Support Team\.                         **Tools for Designing Interactive Courses  
                        **February 11 \(10:00am – 11:30am\) \| IKB Seminar Room 2\.22  
                        A [90\-minute workshop](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/tools-for-designing-interactive-courses-2/) that focuses on practical applications of Canvas and H5P, to help professors enhance their teaching approaches and explore different active learning techniques that foster student engagement\.
                        
                        **Practical Strategies for Inclusive and Engaged Classrooms  
                        **February 12 \(1:00pm – 2:00pm\) \| Online  
                        This [hands\-on discussion](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/collaborative-learning-in-action-practical-strategies-for-inclusive-and-engaged-classrooms/) will explore strategies to design group activities that are meaningful and aligned with your course outcomes\. There will be tips for preventing common pitfalls such as uneven participation, interpersonal conflict, and mismatched expectations\.
