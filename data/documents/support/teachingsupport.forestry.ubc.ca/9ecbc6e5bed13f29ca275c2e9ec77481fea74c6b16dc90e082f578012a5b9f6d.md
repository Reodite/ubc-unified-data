                        **Resources Available to you as an Instructor**
                        
                        The TLS team has organized a short overview of the learning technology tools and resources available to instructors and teaching assistants in Forestry\. A full list and links to guides can be found on this [page](http://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/an-overview-of-online-learning-technologies-tools/)\.
                        
                        **Tip\-sheet for Designing Assessments**
                        
                        To aid in your decision\-making process for course assessments, we present a comprehensive guide that delves into the key aspects you should consider\.
                        
                        -   [Navigating the Choice: Online or In\-Person Assessments](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/Navigating-the-Choice-Online-or-In-Person-Assessments-V3.pdf)
                        
                        **Tip\-Sheet Kaltura Video Submissions in LockDown Browser**
                        
                        [Detailed instructions](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/Tips-on-Creating-Audio-and-Video-Submission-Quizzes-on-Canvas-with-LDB.pdf) on how to create an online Canvas quiz with LockDown Browser Quiz that requires audio/video submission via Kaltura\.
                        
                        **Guide for Using All Screens Mode in Zoom**
                        
                        A feature in Zoom that requires all participants to share their screen, for a more streamlined proctoring experience\. This feature provides instructors an alternative option to accommodate students who are unable to install LockDown Browser for an online assessment that requires invigilation tools\. More details can be found on this [website](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&amp;sysparm_article=KB0074632)\.

                        **Interactive Course Content with H5P**
                        
                        September 16 \(12:30pm\-1:30pm\)
                        
                        This 60\-minute workshop focuses on practical and interactive H5P elements that can be used to enhance student engagement with course materials\. More details [here](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/course-design-studio-interactive-course-content-with-h5p/)\.
                        
                        **Creating Teaching Materials with Generative AI**
                        
                        September 23 \(12:00pm\-1:30pm\)
                        
                        Learn how generative AI can streamline the creation of case studies, rubrics, discussion prompts and lesson plants, while maintaining quality, interactivity and pedagogical alignment\. Learn more [here](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/genai-maker-session-creating-teaching-materials-with-generative-ai/)\.
                        
                        **Forestry Teaching &amp; Learning Bi\-Weekly Drop\-in Sessions\.**
                        
                        September 24 11:30am\-12:30pm \| Online
                        
                        The TLS team will host bi\-weekly drop\-in sessions to answer teaching\-related questions\. Instructors and TAs are welcome to join the [Zoom meeting](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/65722056764?pwd=SUE1X4njLVxxanzqzH1lFMaqY6ipup.1)\. If you are unable to join feel free to stop by FSC Room 2625 or 2718 for support\.
                        
                        **Inclusive Teaching: What is it and Why does it Matter?**
                        
                        September 23 – October 6th \(Online \| Canvas\)
                        
                        Asynchronous course for instructors looking for an introduction to inclusive teaching practices\. This Cohort will run for approximately 2 weeks\. More details on enrollment can be found [here](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/inclusive-teaching-what-is-it-and-why-does-it-matter/)\.
