#### Important Information for TA Application

The Teaching Assistant application portal for the **2026** Summer Term will open on **March 15, 2026** with a deadline to apply of **March 31, 2026**\. Following this, positions for the **2026/27** Winter Terms 1 and 2 will open on **March 31, 2026** with a deadline to apply of **April 30, 2026**\. The portal will remain open for applications in the event that an adequate candidate is not found for a specific course, and those particular postings will be updated after this deadline\. To apply for a TA position, complete and submit an online application\. The maximum hours given to TAs per term is: **192 hours \(12 hours a week\)\.** If you accept more than one offer in any department, you *must* notify the office immediately\. Please note that positions posted are available subject to course enrollments, and provision of funds\.

You will only be contacted if you have been selected to be a TA\. If you are selected as a successful candidate, you will receive an email with the details of your TA position\. We encourage qualified students applicants to apply\. Conditions of employment are governed by the collective agreement between CUPE 2278 and the University of British Columbia\.

**Note**: To apply to be a TA for the Faculty of Forestry, you must be a student or recent graduate at UBC Vancouver\. Recent graduates are those who have completed their degree requirements within eight \(8\) months of the term they are being hired for\. If you are an international student, you need to have a valid study permit and social insurance number \(SIN\)\. The Teaching Assistant application portal is open to both graduate and undergraduate students\.

**IMPORTANT**: Teaching Assistant work must be done in British Columbia\.

##### Types of Position

-   ##### Senior Teaching Assistant \(STA\)
    
    Graduate student in a Master’s or Doctoral program at the University of British Columbia and is required by the University to perform lead hand duties as a Teaching Assistant\. These duties typically include coordinating other Teaching Assistants and providing guidance, technical knowledge, and subject matter expertise to other Teaching Assistants, and/or developing curriculum under the guidance of a faculty member\.
    
-   ##### Graduate Teaching Assistant I \(GTA 1\)
    
    Graduate student who has completed two \(2\) years’ service as a graduate student teaching assistant\.
    
-   ##### Graduate Teaching Assistant II \(GTA 2\)
    
    Graduate student with less than two \(2\) years’ service as a graduate student teaching assistant\.
    
-   ##### Undergraduate Teaching Assistant I \(UTA 1\)
    
    Undergraduate student who is registered in a bachelor’s degree program at the University of British Columbia who is required to perform a broad range Teaching Assistant Duties\.
    
-   ##### Undergraduate Teaching Assistant II \(UTA 2\)
    
    Undergraduate student who is registered in a bachelor’s degree program at the University of British Columbia who performs Teaching Assistant Duties that do not require knowledge of the discipline, or student contact beyond an incidental or transactional basis \(such as handling or returning exams or assignments\.\)

#### Connecting An Employee ID to Your CWL Account for Canvas Course Access

If this is your **first time working at UBC**, you may be contacted by UBC IT to link your Employee ID to your CWL\. Any CWL Account holder can add their Employee ID to their CWL account\.

To do so, go to the CWL myAccount page [here](https://www.myaccount.ubc.ca/myAccount/) and log in with your **existing CWL account**\. Navigate to the side bar on the left and look for the section named **“Add Affiliation”**\. From here you should be able to add your employee number by selecting the employee number option from the dropdown menu, typing your employee number into the given field, and clicking add\.

You may have to wait while the UBC system refreshes overnight for your linking to be effective\. It is important that you maintain **your existing CWL and do not create a new one**, as this may result in access issues\. If you have any issues, please contact the UBC IT Help Desk [here](https://it.ubc.ca/got-question-about-it-products-and-support)\.
