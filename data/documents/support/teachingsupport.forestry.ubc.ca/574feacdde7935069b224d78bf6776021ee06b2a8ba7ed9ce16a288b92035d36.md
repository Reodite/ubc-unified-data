                        **Nominate a Forestry Instructor for the Killam Teaching Prize**
                        
                        Have a great instructor in Forestry? Nominate them for the Killam Teaching Prize\! The Killam Teaching Prize recognizes outstanding teaching and learning contributions by instructors in the Faculty of Forestry\. We want to hear your thoughts on who has contributed to your academic success\!
                        
                        The deadline to submit a nomination is **November 7th, 2025**\. Fill out this [survey](https://bit.ly/42AHgxn) to find more instructions and complete your nomination\.
                        
                        **Sustainability Education Fellows Program**
                        
                        Supports faculty with up to $20,000 to develop new curriculum or significantly enhance existing courses in sustainability across all disciplines\. Applications open January 2026\. More details found [here\.](https://sustain.ubc.ca/programs/sustainability-education-fellows-program?mc_cid=47f501ac1c&amp;mc_eid=288f627890)
                        
                        You can also attend the [i](http://www.eventbrite.ca/e/info-session-sustainability-education-fellows-program-tickets-1549796114899?aff=odeimcmailchimp&amp;mc_cid=47f501ac1c&amp;mc_eid=288f627890)[nformation session](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/info-session-sustainability-education-fellows-program-tickets-1549796114899?aff=odeimcmailchimp&amp;mc_cid=47f501ac1c&amp;mc_eid=288f627890) that will be held on November 26\.
                        
                        **Climate Teaching Connector: Now Open for Instructor Requests**
                        
                        Provides an [opportunity](https://sustain.ubc.ca/programs/climate-teaching-connector) to integrate climate\-related content into undergraduate courses by connecting with graduate students from diverse disciplines to deliver guest presentations grounded in their research, lived experiences, and unique climate perspectives\.

                        **Special Guest Speaker Dr\. Andrew Wardell, CIFOR** \| **Globalization in a Nutshell: Tracing the 700\-Year History of the Shea Nut Trade in West Africa**
                        
                        October 30 \(1:30pm – 2:30pm\) \| Online
                        
                        This [talk](http://ubc.zoom.us/j/64950405412?pwd=PYOqaz9DBGDTZnhwSryypYpu9DkgG4.1) explores how globalization has shaped the production, trade, and value chains of non\-timber forest products\. Dr\. Wardell examines women’s roles in the trade, the rise of transnational corporations, and the ongoing challenges of local value addition and finance in the shea sector\.
                        
                        **GenAI in Teaching and Learning: A One\-Week Online Intensive Course**
                        
                        November 3\-10 \(Self\-paced, 12 hours\) \| Asynchronous Canvas Course
                        
                        [Discover](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/genai-in-teaching-and-learning-a-one-week-online-intensive-course/) how generative AI is transforming higher education and learn how to thoughtfully integrate these tools into your teaching, in a one\-week, fully online course\.
                        
                        **Creating Accessible OER and Course Materials**
                        
                        November 4 \(10:00am – 11:30am\) \| Online
                        
                        Dives into how to incorporate open educational resources into your course design so that they are more accessible and inclusive for all learners through practical examples, design strategies, and planning tips\. Registration details [here\.](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/creating-accessible-oer-and-course-materials/)
                        
                        To learn more about accessible OER funding, please visit the [OER fund page](https://oerfund.open.ubc.ca/2026-call-for-proposals-for-oer-grants/), or attend one of their reoccurring [consultation sessions](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/oer-fund-proposal-consulting-session/)\.
