                | **TLS Drop\-in Sessions** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    The Teaching and Learning Support Team organizes weekly drop\-in sessions to answer teaching\-related questions \(e\.g\., Canvas, in\-class live streaming and recording, etc\.\)\. Both instructors and TAs are welcome to join\.
                    
                    Time: **Fridays 13:00 – 14:00**Join the [Zoom meeting](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/62814050960?pwd=OXFlZ3FTcDZOWWp4T3lvTlNpNk9sZz09) 
                    
                    **Teaching Assistant Workshops: EDI in TAing**  
                    In these workshops you will learn about power and privilege within the learning environment, the policies and guidelines  
                    that frame your work, and how best to support students as you navigate ethical dilemmas and potential conflicts\. This online synchronous session \(offered through Zoom\) is foundational to supporting an equitable and inclusive learning environment, while enhancing department and program\-specific TA training\. Please  
                    [register](https://equity.ubc.ca/how-we-can-help/training-and-education/teaching-assistant-workshops-equity-diversity-and-inclusion/) to attend the section that best fits your academic discipline and schedule\.
                    
                    STEM Disciplines: Wednesday, Sept\. 14 from 4\-6pm
                    
                    Creative Arts Disciplines: Wednesday, Sept\. 21 from 4\-6pm
                    
                    Social Sciences &amp; Humanities: Wednesday, Sept\. 28 from 4\-6pm

                | **Adding and Sharing Pronouns on Zoom** |
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | Zoom has released a new feature allowing users to add preferred pronouns to their Zoom profile\. UBC has enabled this feature for UBC Zoom accounts on August 5\. Sharing personal pronouns helps to build inclusive virtual spaces at UBC\. You are not required to share your pronouns, if you choose to, instructions on how to do so can be found [here](https://support.zoom.us/hc/en-us/articles/4402698027533-Adding-and-sharing-yourpronouns)\. |
