                | **Exam Tip Sheets** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    As finals season approaches, explore the new tip sheets designed for teaching teams, offering insights into key  
                    aspects of the exam process\. Equip yourself for a smoother exam season with these resources\.
                    
                    For information on understanding the intricacies of exam accommodations, disruptions, and submitting final grades, check out:  
                    – [UBC Exam Polices, Procedures, and Reminders](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/12/Tip-Sheet-Exam-Policy-V5.pdf)
                    
                    For information on setting up and delivering exams based on different exam platforms, check out:  
                    – [Exam Formats, Technical Setups, and Reminders](https://learningsupport.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/12/Tip-Sheet-Technical-Tips-for-Exam-Logistics-V5.pdf)
                    
                    For questions or assistance, feel free to contact the Forestry Teaching &amp; Learning Support Team [\(forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca\)](mailto:forestry.tls@ubc.ca?subject=Forestry%20Teaching%20and%20Learning) in  
                    advance for exam support\.

                | **UBC’s Student Mental Health Provider** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    [Here2Talk](http://here2talk.ca/) is the new student mental health assistance provider\. It allows UBC students to have immediate access to counselling and community referral services across phone, online chat, and mobile app\.
                    
                    Its services are free and available 24/7, in multiple languages\.

                | **OER Awards** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    Starting this year, UBC is launching a new [Open Educational Resources award](https://open.ubc.ca/oer-award/?login) program, in alignment with one of the recommendations from [the Student Affordability Task Force report](https://bog3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/03/2.1i_2022.03_Student-Affordability-Task-Force.pdf) in March, 2022\. Lead nominees must be faculty members with a current appointment, though folks in other roles can be part of a group award\.
                    
                    Please see the [OER Award web page](https://open.ubc.ca/oer-award/) for criteria and details\. The deadline for nominations is **Nov 30, 2023\.**
                
                |  |
                | --- |
                | **West Coast Teaching Excellence Awards** |
                
                - **Row 1**
                
                  - **Cell 1:**
                  
                    The West Coast Teaching Excellence Awards, are regional awards hosted by the [BC Teaching and Learning Council](https://bctlc.ca/)\. Nominees can either be individual faculty  
                    members or teaching teams, and the award focuses on excellent teaching at the undergraduate level\. Please see [the award web page](https://bctlc.ca/west-coast-teaching-excellence-awards/) for criteria and details\.
                    
                    Please see the [West Coast Teaching Excellence Awards page](https://academic.ubc.ca/awards-funding/award-opportunities/teaching-awards/west-coast-teaching-excellence-awards) for details about the internal selection process for UBC Vancouver nominees\. The deadline for applications is **Nov 30, 2023\.**
