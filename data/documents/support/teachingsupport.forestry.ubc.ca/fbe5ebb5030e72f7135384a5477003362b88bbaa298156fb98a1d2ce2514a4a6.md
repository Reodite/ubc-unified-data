                    | Privacy Impact Assessment Reminder |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        Please be reminded that under BC’s FIPPA, **[UBC must conduct a PIA](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/pia-stra)** for any learning tools that **may collect student information and data\.**
                        
                        For example, while the use of tools like **Google Docs** and **Slack** may be convenient, there is no university wide PIA for teaching\.
                        
                        Please see more information on UBC’s website for [Guidelines &amp; Tools](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/pia-stra/pia-guidelines-tools), [FAQs](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/pia-stra/frequently-asked-PIA-questions), and [Requesting a PIA Process](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/pia-stra/pia-process-overview) or contact our team here\.

                    | New Canvas Features |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        **New: [Canvas Portfolio](https://community.instructure.com/en/kb/articles/662744-what-is-canvas-portfolio?utm_medium=email&amp;utm_campaign=LDDI-Newsletter-March-2026&amp;utm_source=Envoke-LDDI-Newsletter&amp;utm_term=LDDI-Newsletter-March-2026) – 2 Types**
                        
                        Evaluation Portfolios: enable instructors to implement structured assessment and monitor learner progress over time\.
                        
                        Showcase Portfolios: empower learners to tell their own stories and demonstrate growth beyond the classroom\.
                        
                        Please note the **legacy ePortfolio** feature will be **retired on June 30, 2026**\.
