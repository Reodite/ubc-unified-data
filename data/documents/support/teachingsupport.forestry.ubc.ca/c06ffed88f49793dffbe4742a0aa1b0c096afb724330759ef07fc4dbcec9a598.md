                    | Final Exam Reminders |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        The CfA has provided a memorandum for [Accommodated Exam Details &amp; Deadlines](https://ubcca-my.sharepoint.com/:b:/g/personal/heidi_chang_ubc_ca1/IQD6EMy4YsfMQ5zgWtX9gLkfAeexXpEQpXZqFU6tKGV5cnM?e=eHrt9Q)\. Instructors are recommended to review and reinforce the procedures outlined in the document\.
                        
                        In case of **locked doors and missing exam booklets** **on the exam day,** contact the exam emergency telephone number: 604\-827\-1981\.
                        
                        [UBC Enrollment Services](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver/exam-invigilation) has provided **procedures to help instructors navigate exam invigilation:**
                        
                        -   Helpful steps [Before](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver/exam-invigilation/guidelines#before), [During](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver/exam-invigilation/guidelines#during), and [After](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver/exam-invigilation/guidelines#end) the Exam\.
                        -   Options if you [observe/catch cheating](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver/exam-invigilation/guidelines#cheating)\.
                        -   Contact information [in case of emergencies](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver/exam-invigilation/guidelines#emergency)\.
                        -   [Fire Alarm Procedures](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver/exam-invigilation/fire-alarm-procedures) &amp; [Bomb Threat Procedures](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver/exam-invigilation/bomb-threat-procedures)\.
                        -   In case of [Inclement Weather](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver/exam-invigilation/inclement-weather-procedures) procedures\.
                        
                        Invigilation [requires one head invigilator](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver/exam-invigilation#duties) \(usually the instructor\) to be in charge of setup/problems\. **Instructors are to be available for the entire examination period** **and are expected to invigilate their own exam\.**
                        
                        Note GA/TAs are [not permitted to invigilate exams 24 hours before their own exams](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver/exam-invigilation#assigning)\.

                    | Grade Submission Reminders |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        The **final exam period is scheduled from April 14 to 25**\.
                        
                        A gentle reminder for all instructors to **submit final student grades to Workday\.** Please note UBC uses a [percentage scale \(100%\) and equivalent letter grades](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/grading-practices/introduction) for grading purposes\.
                        
                        Final grades must be submitted either:
                        
                        -   7 business days following the exam date\.
                        -   5 days following the exam date on the last 2 days of the exam period\.
                        -   Please refer to [UBC Exam Dates and Deadlines](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/exam-scheduling-vancouver?check_logged_in=1)\.
                        
                        Please refer to this [tipsheet](https://teachingsupport.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Tips-for-Organizing-Final-Grades-for-Submission.pdf) for detailed guidance and helpful tips for **navigating the Final Grade Submission Tool in Workday**\.
                        
                        **Before uploading final course grades:**
                        
                        -   Make sure the required data columns are visible for grades entry and upload, and your selected file format allows the ability to upload grades\.
                        -   For the full detailed instructions on uploading grades, go to the [FSC User Guide](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/files/FSCUserGuide.pdf)\.
                        -   Note that your final grades should only be posted on Workday and not on Canvas\.

                    | UBC Library Course Reserves |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        The [UBC Library Course Reserves](https://courses.library.ubc.ca/) \(LOCR\) provides instructors a centralized space to **access course materials** \(articles, eBooks, book chapters, media resources, etc\.\) while ensuring compliance with Canadian Copyright Law and UBC’s licensing agreements\.
                        
                        **Instructors can either:**
                        
                        1\. Use UBC Library’s [Syllabus Service](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/using-course-reserves/#fragment-8e20cd8d626b762e4eeeb4589a8ba7c4-1) to simply submit the course’s reading list\.
                        
                        2\. [Manually submit requests](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/using-course-reserves/#fragment-8e20cd8d626b762e4eeeb4589a8ba7c4-2) with specific bibliographic data\.
                        
                        If you plan on Merging Canvas Course Sections, please contact forestry\.tls@ubc\.ca **before submitting** LOCR requests\.
                        
                        Requests should be **submitted at least one month before the start of term** to ensure materials are available on time\.

                    | New Zoom Feature |
                    
                    - **Row 1**
                    
                      - **Cell 1:**
                      
                        Faculty and staff using UBC’s Zoom service can now enable [Zoom Meeting Summary](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&amp;sysparm_article=KB0058013), part of the Zoom AI Companion suite\.
                        
                        This feature can **improve the accessibility of meetings by generating a high\-level summary**, capturing key discussion points, action items, and next steps for the host to edit and share with participants\.
                        
                        Please note this feature is turned **off by default for each meeting** and the host must manually turn it on\.
                        
                        When using Meeting Summary, please [follow UBC’s privacy and information security requirements when using AI features](https://it-genai-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/Guidelines-GenAI_TL.pdf#page=13) &amp; **do not use in meetings where high\-risk or sensitive personal information will be discussed\.**
