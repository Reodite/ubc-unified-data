Individual Development Plans \(IDPs\) are tools that support graduate students in actively preparing for the career of their choice by:

-   exploring career paths
-   establishing career and academic goals
-   identifying skill gaps
-   creating an action plan to identify learning opportunities and achieve the goals above
-   monitoring and celebrating progress

Suggested IDP formats:

-   CIHR IDP: [https://cihr\-irsc\.gc\.ca/e/50516\.html](https://cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/50516.html)
-   MyIDP: [https://myidp\.sciencecareers\.org/](https://myidp.sciencecareers.org/)
