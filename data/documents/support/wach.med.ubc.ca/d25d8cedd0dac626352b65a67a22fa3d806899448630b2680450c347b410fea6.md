Competency Passport Framework \(ComPass\) The ComPass is adapted with permission from the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada\.

The WACH program encourages professional development for our students in the **Graduate Student Enhanced Scholar Program,** which is composed of:

[ComPASS Competency Passport](https://wach.med.ubc.ca/current-students/enhanced-scholars-program/compass-competency-passport/)

[Individual Development Plan](https://wach.med.ubc.ca/current-students/enhanced-scholars-program/individual-development-plan/)

[Peer Mentorship](https://wach.med.ubc.ca/current-students/enhanced-scholars-program/peer-mentorship/)

The Enhanced Scholar program aims to support students in identifying learning goals and gaps, and building competencies key to success in a changing employment market\.
