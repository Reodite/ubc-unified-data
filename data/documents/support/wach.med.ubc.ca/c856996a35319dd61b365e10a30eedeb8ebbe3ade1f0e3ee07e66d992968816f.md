### Women\+ and Children’s Health Sciences Student Organization

The WACH SO is a student\-run organization that coordinates social events, wellness initiatives, and more\!

Follow **ubcwachso** on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ubcwachso/)  [Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/03/Instagram-Icon-150x150.png)

You can reach the WACH SO at **[wach\.social@ubc\.ca](mailto:wach.social@ubc.ca)\.**  
If you have applied to the WACH program, you can also contact this organization with questions about the student life aspects of WACH\.
