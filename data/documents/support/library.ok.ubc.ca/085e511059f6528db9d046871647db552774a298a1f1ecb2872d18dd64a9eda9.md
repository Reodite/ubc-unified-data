### Research Guides

Use UBC Library research guides to begin research on a particular topic\. Browse by course, subject, type or alphabetically\.

[Research guides](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/?b=s)

### Indigenous Research Guides

These guides include search strategies for finding resources that are relevant to the multidisciplinary study of Indigenous topics and materials written from Indigenous perspectives, including a [Okanagan Syilx Research Guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/OkanaganSyilxResearchGuide)\.

[Indigenous Research Guides](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/xwi7xwaresearchguide)
