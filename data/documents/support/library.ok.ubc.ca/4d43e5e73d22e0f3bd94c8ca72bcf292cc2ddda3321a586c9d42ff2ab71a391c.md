The UBC Okanagan Library strives to provide a high\-level of service to our students, faculty, staff, and visitors year\-round\. We invite you to explore all of our available services and if you cannot find what you are looking for, please be sure to visit our service desk or contact a librarian\.

### Research &amp; Citation Help

Our [Library Service Desk](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/service-desk/) is a central point of contact and support for students, faculty, staff, and visitors\.

[Research &amp; Citation Help](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/researchandcitation/)

### Subject Librarians

A subject librarian is an invaluable resource at any stage of your academic career\. Find your [subject librarian](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/help-contact/subject-librarians/)\.

[Ask Your Librarian](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/help-contact/ask-your-librarian/)

### Student Learning Hub

Your one\-stop\-shop for math, science, writing, and language support\. The Student Learning Hub will help you build the skills, strategies, and behaviours that are essential to become a confident and independent learner\.

[Student Learning Hub](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/learning-hub/)
