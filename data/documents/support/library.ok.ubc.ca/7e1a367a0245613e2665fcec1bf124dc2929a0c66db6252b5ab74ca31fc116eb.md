UBC Okanagan Library has designed a BC Heritage Walk as part of [BC Heritage Week](https://heritagebc.ca/events-activities/heritage-week/all-ways/) and [Move UBC month](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/wellbeing-campaigns-and-initiatives/move-ubc)\. 11 stops are located between the Library and The Commons to help you learn more about Okanagan history and explore archival materials\. Participate in this activity and enter for a chance to win prizes from Theytus Books at the final stop\.

The first stop is located on the main floor of the Library near the service desk\.

[Image](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2023/02/service-desk-front-2-300x200.jpg)

Interested in learning more about local history? Visit [British Columbia Regional Digitized History](https://bcrdh.ca/) or [UBC Okanagan Special Collections and Archives](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/special-collections-and-archives/) for more information\.
