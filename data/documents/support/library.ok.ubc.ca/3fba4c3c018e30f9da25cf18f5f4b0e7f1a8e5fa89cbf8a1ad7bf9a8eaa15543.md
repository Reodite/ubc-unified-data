The UBC Okanagan Library is located in the heart of the UBC Okanagan campus situated on the territory of the Syilx Okanagan Nation\. The UBC Okanagan Library has a wide variety of spaces to support teaching, learning, collaboration and quiet study\.

### UBC Okanagan Library Hours

Monday to Friday \| **7:30 am – 10 pm**  
Saturday and Sunday \| **10 am – 10 pm**

*Closed most statutory holidays\.* 

Please visit the [Library Hours Portal](https://hours.library.ubc.ca/#view-okanagan) for details and for a visual format of both the Library and Commons building hours\.
