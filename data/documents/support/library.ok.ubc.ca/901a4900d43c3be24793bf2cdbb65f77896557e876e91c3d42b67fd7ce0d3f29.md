UBC Okanagan Special Collections and Archives serves as a dynamic access point for archival, primary source, and special collections reflecting the history and character of the Okanagan and neighbouring areas\.

## About Okanagan Special Collections and Archives

Okanagan Special Collections and Archives manages and stewards numerous distinct repositories with a combined aim of documenting the campus, the Okanagan area, and the broader context of southern interior of BC, including the Shuswap, Similkameen, Boundary, Kootenay, Columbia, Thompson, and Nicola regions\.

Our collections include archival records, photographs, audio\-visual, and ephemera as well as published works of and about our geography of focus\.

Themes of subject coverage include UBC Okanagan campus history, environment, agriculture, arts and culture, the BC wine industry, and Indigenous knowledge\.

### Planning your visit

Materials in Okanagan Special Collections and Archives are rare, unique, and must be handled with care\. Our space has enhanced security and a dedicated reading room for researcher convenience\.

See our [Welcome Guide](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2026/08/VisitingOSC_slides_20260731_Rev0.pdf) for details on how to prepare and what to expect\.

When possible, we ask that researchers come prepared with a list of materials they wish to consult\. To start, take a look at our [Research Guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/osc), published [archival descriptions,](https://rbscarchives.library.ubc.ca/university-of-british-columbia-library-okanagan-special-collections) and [library catalogue](https://webcat.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/searchBasic) for keywords relevant to your search\.

### Reproductions

Images of resources in copyright may be captured on a personal device under the [fair dealing exception](https://copyright.ubc.ca/copyright-guidelines/#fair-dealing) of the Copyright Act \(R\.S\.C\., 1985, c\. C\-42\), please capture OSC’s usage tag in the image frame\.

Okanagan Special Collections and Archives offers digital reproductions of images and/or materials within our collections\. To request a reproduction, please fill out a [Service Request Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0B9hbhzm3OMnbPE)\.

### Okanagan Special Collections and Archives

Okanagan Special Collections and Archives is comprised of personal papers, business records, photographs, and audio\-visual materials donated to the University from individuals and families with a connection to the Okanagan and surrounding areas\. These materials are often unique in context and unpublished\. Descriptions of processed archives are available through [Explore Archives at UBC](https://rbscarchives.library.ubc.ca/university-of-british-columbia-library-okanagan-special-collections)\.

### Baird Bowering Collection

Consisting of over 250 items, the [Baird Bowering Collection](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/special-collections-and-archives/baird-bowering-collection/) is made special by its particular provenance, and its inclusion of rare literary ephemera, compelling marginalia, and a number of intimate inserts including notes and photographs\. 

## British Columbia Regional Digitized History \(BCRDH\)

[Image](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2023/05/BCRDH_logo_square_20200108-300x300.png)

UBC Okanagan Library prioritizes cultivating spaces and collections in the context of its position within the campus, and the broader community\.

In keeping with these values, Okanagan Special Collections and Archives has partnered with regional memory institutions across BC’s southern interior to provide enhanced access to historical re sources including photographs, oral histories, videos, maps, newspapers, and other primary sources through the British Columbia Regional Digitized History \(BCRDH\) [online portal](https://bcrdh.ca/)\.
