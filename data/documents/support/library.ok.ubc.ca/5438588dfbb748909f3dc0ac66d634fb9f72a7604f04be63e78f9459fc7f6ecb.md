To ensure a welcoming and inclusive environment at the library the following guidelines have been developed to set expectations for all\.

### Read the full UBC Library Code of Conduct 

[UBC Library Code of Conduct](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2026/03/Library-Code-of-Conduct-2026.pdf)

[Person using wheelchair and reading](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2026/03/6-300x300.png)

**Keep our library accessible\.**  Don’t block aisles or pathways and prioritize accessible seating for those who require it\.

[Quiet and considerate\. Owl](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2026/03/5-300x300.png)

**Be kind and considerate\.**  Keep noise levels low and adhere to rules in our silent study spaces\.

[Worm in a book](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2026/03/4-300x300.png)

**Leave no trace\.**  Help us protect our books from pests and show respect for others by cleaning up after yourself\.

[Raccoon running with laptop](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2026/03/3-300x300.png)

**Take your items with you\.**  
Don’t leave your belongings unattended, especially laptops and valuables\.

[Eat only where permitted\.](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2026/03/2-300x300.png)

**Eat only where permitted\.** Food is allowed in designated spaces on the first floor, but only covered beverages are allowed in the reading rooms and on the second floor\.

[Icon librarian in stacks with owl](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2026/03/1-300x300.png)

**Respect our staff and signs\.**  Library staff are here to help\! They’re also here to take care of library spaces and resources\. Please follow all signs and directions from library staff\.

### UBC Okanagan Library General Policies

#### **Food and Drink**  

-   Only covered beverages are permitted on the second floor of the library\. 
-   Food and beverages are allowed in the main area on the first floor\. Please clean up after yourself\.   

[Library Food and Drink Policy](https://about.library.ubc.ca/policies/food-and-drink-policy/)

#### **Other Information** 

**Washrooms and water refill stations** 

-   Two gender\-neutral, accessible washrooms are available behind the fireplace on the main floor of the library\. 
-   Men’s and women’s washrooms are in the foyer of the library\. 
-   A water refill station is located in the foyer of the library\.   

### Policies, Procedures, and Guidelines 

### Library Conduct

UBC Library and its employees value belonging\.  We work to create and sustain an inclusive environment where everyone is respected and welcome\. We acknowledge, encourage, and celebrate diverse experiences and perspectives, and are working to identify and address systemic barriers to inclusion\. The following policies address the comfort and protection of all persons who study, research and work at UBC Library, both physically and virtually, as well the protection of library materials and property\. 

-   [Library Code of Conduct](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2026/03/Library-Code-of-Conduct-2026.pdf) 
-   [Library Food and Drink Policy](https://about.library.ubc.ca/policies/food-and-drink-policy/) 
-   [UBC Policy SC14 – Acceptable Use and Security of UBC Electronic Information and Systems](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/information-systems-policy/) 

### Copyright

Copyrighted materials are everywhere – you produce them, and you use them, everyday\. What you can and can’t do under copyright law is essential knowledge for all UBC faculty, staff and students – both as responsible global citizens, and as responsible teachers, researchers and students\. Infringing copyright is a serious matter, but the Library is here to help\.  

Find information about copyright law, UBC’s requirements and contact information for the team of professionals who can assist you: [copyright\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:copyright.ok@ubc.ca)\. 

-   [Copyright Resources](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/guides-and-tools/copyright-information/) 
-   [Copyright Requirements for UBC Faculty and Staff](https://copyright.ubc.ca/requirements/copyright-requirements/) \(university\-wide policy\) 
-   [Fair Dealing Requirements for UBC Faculty and Staff](https://copyright.ubc.ca/requirements/fair-dealing/) \(university\-wide policy\)
