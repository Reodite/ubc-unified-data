### Bookable spaces

Bookable spaces are available in the Library, Commons, and EME buildings for all UBC Okanagan students, faculty, and staff\. Before you book a space, please be aware of the following policies as they relate to all room bookings\.

Study Rooms are booked through LibCal\. If your booking is approved, you will receive an email confirmation\. Please note that your confirmed bookings will not appear in your profile\.

If you’re experiencing technical issues, please use the [Report a Tech Support Issue](https://ask.library.ubc.ca/ubc-library-booking-assistance/ "https://ask.library.ubc.ca/ubc-library-booking-assistance/") link included at the bottom of the booking system page\.

For maintenance and cleaning issues or other questions, please contact [library\.admin@ubc\.ca](mailto:library.admin@ubc.ca "mailto:library.admin@ubc.ca")\.

**General Policies:**

-   The [Library Code of Conduct](http://about.library.ubc.ca/policies/) applies to all study rooms
-   Bookings can only be made by groups of two or more UBC students, staff, or faculty with the exception of COM 309 and 310C, which are reserved for graduate students
-   Bookings can be made for up to two hours per day, for a maximum of three bookings per week, per person
-   Bookings can be made up to 14 days in advance
-   Groups who are more than 10 minutes late forfeit their booking time
-   Bookings may be cancelled due to unexpected circumstances and notification emails will be sent if this occurs

[UBC Okanagan Library](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/library/) has two study rooms and a [bookable recording studio](https://ubcstudios.ok.ubc.ca/studio123/)\. [The Commons](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/the-commons/) has 31 study rooms\.

Only graduate students can book Graduate Study Rooms COM309 &amp; COM310C on Floor 3 of The Commons\. [Request access](https://gradstudies.ok.ubc.ca/student-life/graduate-student-spaces/ "https://gradstudies.ok.ubc.ca/student-life/graduate-student-spaces/") by following the instructions\.

### Informal learning spaces \(not bookable\)

There are many open and informal learning spaces at UBC Okanagan, including spaces in the Library, Commons, EME, and Arts buildings\.

**Library:** Including the [Lindsay Gordon Reading Room](https://learningspaces.ok.ubc.ca/classrooms/lib-lib-115) \(LIB 115\) and [Field Reading Room](https://learningspaces.ok.ubc.ca/classrooms/lib-lib-102) \(LIB 102\)

**The Commons:** Including the [D\. Ross Fitzpatrick Great Hall](https://learningspaces.ok.ubc.ca/classrooms/com-com-003) \(upper and lower\), [Galleria](https://learningspaces.ok.ubc.ca/classrooms/com-com-211z), [Lois and Cliff Serwa Reading Room](https://learningspaces.ok.ubc.ca/classrooms/com-com-105-0), and Graduate Commons\.

**Engineering, Management, and Education \(EME\):** Including the lower level, mezzanine, and foyers\.

**Arts:** Including the main foyer, atrium, and corridors\.

[More Informal Learning Spaces](https://learningspaces.ok.ubc.ca/informal-learning-spaces) [Explore the Commons](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/the-commons/)
