[Pride Programming](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/campus-life/pride/) is a peer\-lead program for Queer\-identifying students and allies at UBC Okanagan\. Pride Programming focuses on building community, hosting events, and creating safe spaces on campus\.

UBCO Library has curated a book display this month that features books from diverse authors with a focus on amplifying 2SLGBTQIA\+ identities, experiences, and perspectives\.
