Library staff and librarians can help you discover appropriate resources for your research and can answer your citation questions\.

## Help getting started

Employees at our Library Service Desk are prepared to answer your general research and citation questions or refer you to an appropriate resource\.

**Get in touch:** 

-   Visit the [Library Service Desk](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/service-desk/)
-   Email [libquestions\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:libquestions.ok@ubc.ca)
-   Call 250\-807\-9107
-   Instant response through [AskAway Chat](https://www.library.ubc.ca/help/ask-colorbox.html)

## Specialized support

Subject Librarians are specialists who can address your unique and in\-depth research questions\. Subject Librarians are available to support you remotely through email and one\-on\-one consultations\. Use the link below to contact your subject librarian or book an appointment\.

[get librarian support](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/help-contact/ask-your-librarian/)

Not sure who your Subject Librarian is? Find out using the link below\.

[Find Your Subject Librarian](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/help-contact/subject-librarians/)

The Knowledge Synthesis Service is available for students and faculty seeking support with knowledge synthesis projects including systematic, scoping, and related reviews\.

[Knowledge Synthesis Service](https://knowledgesynthesis.library.ubc.ca/)

## **Self\-guided**

These curated style guides are designed to address the most common questions with solid examples and can assist you with formatting in\-text citations, formatting a reference list, and structuring your paper for course work\.

[CITATION STYLE GUIDES](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/guides-and-tools/citation-style-guides/)

Librarians have created self\-paced modules to help you learn foundational research and citation skills and how to find credible sources in the Library\.

[LIBRARY MODULES](https://proxy.qualtrics.com/proxy/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcanvas.ubc.ca%2Fenroll%2F3DEXYE&amp;token=3FkSrLkDk5lXCpMvNl%2Fie2jSp2Ivfmnmu%2Fb%2FBhXnbPU%3D)

Research Guides allow you to explore and locate key resources and databases for each subject\.

[Research Guides](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/?b=s) 

## Centre For Scholarly Communication

The CSC brings together the expertise of partners from Advanced Research Computing and Research Services with the UBC Okanagan Library to provide an information hub for research supports and services at UBC Okanagan\.

[CENTRE FOR SCHOLARLY COMMUNICATION](http://csc.ok.ubc.ca/)

#### **  
THE CSC PROVIDES:**

-   One\-on\-one [consultation support](https://csc.ok.ubc.ca/consultations/) for writing, presenting, copyright, research data management, research computing, open access, and more\.
-   A searchable database of [research tools and services](https://csc.ok.ubc.ca/search-tools-and-services/)\.
-   [Workshops](https://csc.ok.ubc.ca/workshops/) on a wide range of research projects and processes and Do\-It\-Yourself materials\.

### Writing consultations and tutoring

The Student Learning Hub offers centralized undergraduate services designed to enhance learning and empower students in all disciplines and at all undergraduate levels\.

#### THE HUB PROVIDES:

-   Free peer\-tutoring in writing, language, math, and science\.
-   One\-on\-one support to develop study skills and learning strategies\.
-   Writing workshops and learning seminars\.
-   Support programs such as Academic Integrity Matters and Supplemental Learning\.

[Student Learning Hub](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/learning-hub/)
