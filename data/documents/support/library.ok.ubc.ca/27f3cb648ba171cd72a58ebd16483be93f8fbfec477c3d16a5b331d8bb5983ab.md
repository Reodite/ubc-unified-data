**Need Sources?**

Your CWL gives you access to online articles and ebooks in the UBC Library collection\. In addition, you can use the [Library Access Browser Extension](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/connect/ll) to access materials anytime, anywhere\.

**Have Citation or Search Questions?**

Get search and citation help by emailing [libquestions\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:libquestions.ok@ubc.ca) or connect instantly through [AskAway chat](https://www.library.ubc.ca/help/ask-colorbox.html)\. You can also find answers to your citation questions in our [Citation Style Guides](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/guides-and-tools/citation-style-guides/)\.

**Need more in\-depth research support?**

Contact your Subject Librarian any time through the [Ask your Librarian form](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/help-contact/ask-your-librarian/)\.
