Your one\-stop\-shop for free and flexible learning supports and resources\.

[Group of students listening to instructor](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2019/08/flexspace-students-5-1000x666.jpg)

The Student Learning Hub will help you build the skills, strategies, and behaviors that are essential to become a confident and independent learner\.

#### Location

The Student Learning Hub is located in LIB 237 on the second floor of the Library building and accessible through the side \(West\) entrance\.

[Student Learning Hub](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/learning-support/)

## Learn More

The Student Learning Hub offers centralized undergraduate services designed to enhance learning and empower students in all disciplines and at all undergraduate levels\.

#### The Hub provides:

-   Free peer\-tutoring in writing, language, math, and science\.
-   One\-on\-one support to develop study skills and learning strategies\.
-   Writing workshops and learning seminars\.
-   Support programs such as Academic Integrity Matters and Supplemental Learning\.
