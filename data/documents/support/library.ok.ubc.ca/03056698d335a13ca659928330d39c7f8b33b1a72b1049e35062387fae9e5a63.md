The Library Service Desk offers borrowing services, assistance getting started with research and citation, peer technology help, and general library support\. These services are available during all library hours\.

## Services

### Research &amp; Citation Help

Ask your questions at the Library Service Desk to find answers or be connected with the expertise you need\.

[RESEARCH &amp; CITATION HELP](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/researchandcitation/)

### Borrowing

Borrow books, course reserve materials, media, laptops, and more\!  

[Borrowing](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/borrowing/)

### Accessibility Supports

Engage with library staff to ensure the access you need\.

[Accessibility Supports](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/accessibility-supports/)

The Inclusive Technology Lab includes a computer lab, group study space, distraction reduced study room, and a collection of technology to borrow\.

[Inclusive Technology Lab](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/inclusive-technology-lab/) 

### Student Technology Assistance

Get support with campus computing, learning technologies, programs, and software\. 

**Contact the Student Support Analyst**

-   In\-person with at the Library Service Desk
-   Email **students\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca** for assistance or to schedule an appointment
-   Enter an IT ticket:

[UBC IT Helpdesk Form](https://web.it.ubc.ca/forms/isf/)

-   Find answers to common questions, how\-to articles, user guides, and more:

[STUDENT TECH SUPPORT](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/student-tech-support/)

## Library Code of Conduct

To ensure a welcoming and inclusive environment at the library the following documents are used to set expectations for all\.
