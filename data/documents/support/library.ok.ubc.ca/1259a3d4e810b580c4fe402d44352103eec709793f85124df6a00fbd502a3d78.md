### Launch Pad is where ideas take off\.

Want to share your academic or personal passion? Launch Pad provides funding to UBC Okanagan students who want to hold an event, offer an opportunity, or showcase an idea through the media, technology, and prototyping opportunities available through [Constellation](https://constellation.ok.ubc.ca/)\.  
  
Constellation is a suite of emerging media and technology learning spaces that help push the boundaries of innovation and learning\. 

[Learn more about Constellation](https://constellation.ok.ubc.ca/)

### **Application details**

### Have an idea for an event, activity, or presentation in a Constellation space? Apply to receive up to $500 to support your event through Launch Pad\. 

**Application deadline:** the 15 th of each month\. Applicants must apply at least six weeks before the proposed event, activity, or presentation\.

**Amount:** Applicants can apply for up to $500 worth of funding\.

**Eligibility:** UBC Okanagan undergraduate, graduate, and PhD students are all welcome to apply\.

Questions about Launch Pad or Constellation? Contact: **constellation\.ok@ubc\.ca**

[Apply Now](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_88QGSNyUEy8DOE6)
