As one of the busiest branches in the UBC Library system, the UBC Okanagan Library is a nexus for scholarly engagement on the UBC Okanagan campus\. UBC Library holds more than 7 million items, making collections – both physical and digital – an essential support for teaching, learning, &amp; research at UBC\.

## **About the collections**

UBC Okanagan Library holds approximately 200 thousand physical items, including books, films, journals, maps, and sound recordings, as well as special collections and archival materials relevant to the Okanagan and neighbouring regions of British Columbia\.

## Featured collections

Explore the unique physical and digital library collections available at the UBC Okanagan Library\. Other featured collections are also available on the Vancouver campus and digitally\.

[Image](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2019/05/av-collection-740x340.jpg)

### **Audiovisual Collection**

Our Okanagan Library holds over 5,200 audiovisual titles in DVD and VHS format\. Highlights of this collection include a robust assortment of Hollywood feature films, as well as foreign language titles from countries around the world\.

Located on the second floor of the Library, all of these titles are available to borrow\.

[Image](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2019/05/digital-collection-740x340.jpg)

### **Digital Collections**

UBC Library offers access to searchable online collections through the [Open Collections](https://open.library.ubc.ca/) portal and the institutional repository [cIRcle](https://circle.ubc.ca/)\. UBC Okanagan Library offers access to digitized collections from [Okanagan Special Collections &amp; Archives](https://bcrdh.ca/islandora/object/ubco:osc) and community memory institutions through the [BCRDH](https://bcrdh.ca/) portal\.

Okanagan highlights include:

-   [Doug and Joyce Cox Research Collection](https://bcrdh.ca/islandora/object/ubco:cox)
-   [Okanagan Historical Society \(OHS\) Annual Reports](https://bcrdh.ca/islandora/object/ohs:root)
-   [Historical Newspapers of the Okanagan and Kootenays](https://bcrdh.ca/islandora/object/news:root)

[Image](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2019/05/map-740x340.jpg)

### **Maps**

Located on the second floor of the Okanagan Library, our topographic map collection focuses on areas of British Columbia and Alberta\. Other Okanagan maps are also available in our [Okanagan Special Collections](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/special-collections-and-archives/)\.

Additional support for locating maps:

-   [Maps &amp; Atlases – UBC Library](http://koerner.library.ubc.ca/collections/maps-atlases/?login)
-   [Historical Map Collections](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/historicalmaps)
-   [Aboriginal Maps and Mapping](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/aboriginalmaps)

[Image](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2019/05/special-collections-740x340.jpg)

### **Okanagan Special Collections &amp; Archives**

Special Collections and Archives houses our special resources related to the Okanagan region and is located in room COM004 in the Commons building\. Looking to do a bit of research on the area? Come and visit us\!

[Visit Special Collections and Archives](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/special-collections-and-archives/)
