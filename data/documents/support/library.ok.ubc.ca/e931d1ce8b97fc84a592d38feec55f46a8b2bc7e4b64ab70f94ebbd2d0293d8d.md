October 4, 2022 (Tuesday, October 4th, 2022, 1:08 pm)

Do you have a favourite 2SLGBTQIA\+ book or film? We want to hear from you\! For Queer Orientation, UBC Okanagan Library is asking UBCO students, staff, and faculty to recommend their favourite 2SLGBTQIA\+ literature, films, and more as we expand our collection\.
