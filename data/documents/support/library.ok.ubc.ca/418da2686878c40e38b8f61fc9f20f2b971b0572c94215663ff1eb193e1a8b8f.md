## [Queer Zine Fair collaborators awarded UBC Partnership Recognition and Exploration Fund](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/2025/07/24/queer-zine-fair-collaborators-awarded-ubc-partnership-recognition-and-exploration-fund/)

July 24, 2025 (Thursday, July 24th, 2025, 3:19 pm)

Congratulations to Donna Langille, Open Education Librarian at UBC Okanagan, and Erik Beardmore from Kelowna Museums for receiving the UBC Partnership Recognition and Exploration Fund for their project Queer Zine Fair\.
