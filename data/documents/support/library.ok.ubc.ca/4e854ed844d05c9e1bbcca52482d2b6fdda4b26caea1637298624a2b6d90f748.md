Subject Librarians provide individual research consultations to help you find and evaluate quality information sources for course assignments and research\. Subject Librarians also offer library instruction and workshops\.

Use our online booking system to [Make an Appointment](https://libcal.library.ubc.ca/appointments/ubco#s-lc-public-pt) with your subject librarian\.

Use the [Subject Librarian Directory](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/contact/subject-librarians/) to find your subject librarian\.
