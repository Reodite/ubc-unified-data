As research activity resumes on campus, the Library is ready to help\! Here are 7 ways the Library can support your research:

**1\. Subject Librarians**

Did you know that almost every subject has a designated librarian? [Subject librarians](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/help-contact/subject-librarians/) are available for remote one\-one one consultations to help with your research projects\.

**2\. Electronic Resources**

We’re doing everything we can to make sure you have the materials you need\. Search for electronic resources like [ejournals](http://search.library.ubc.ca/#journals), [databases](http://search.library.ubc.ca/#databases), and [ebooks and media](http://search.library.ubc.ca/#catalogue)\.

**3\. Physical Collections**

No contact? No problem\! We [can pull library material and make them available for pick\-up](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/covid-19/accessing-print-materials/)\. We can also scan and email journal articles and chapters \(fine print \= subject to copyright restrictions\)\.

**4\. Center for Scholarly Communications \(CSC\)**

The [CSC](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/research/csc/) provides one\-on\-one consultations about all aspects of scholarly communication, including writing support for journal articles, grant proposals, theses, dissertations, and conference presentations as well as copyright, open access, and author rights\.

**5\. Copyright**

Do you have a question about copyright or open access? Please reach out to [Marjorie](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/marjorie-mitchell/) our Copyright, Scholarly Communications, and Research Data Management Librarian\.

**6\. Workshops**

Check out our [online](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/#workshops) workshops\! If you don’t see one that you are looking for [contact](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/help-contact/) us and we will try to run one that meets your needs\.

**7\. Lean Library**

Try the new [Library Access browser extension](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/connect/ll)\. Search the Internet and the Library Access extension will let you know when you’re on a website that the library has access to\. With the Library’s [new browser extension](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/connect/ll) you can search the web and get direct access to scholarly information from the library’s collection\. Install it today\!
