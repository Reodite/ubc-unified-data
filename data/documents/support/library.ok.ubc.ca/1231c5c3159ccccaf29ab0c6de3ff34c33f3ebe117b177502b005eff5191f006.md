Browse, borrow, and check out holds from the Okanagan Regional Library’s collection at UBC Okanagan\.

The Okanagan Regional Library operates a branch on the first floor of the UBC Okanagan Library\. Anyone with an ORL library card can take out books, access online streaming services, and put holds on books for pick\-up\. An ORL staff member will be in the UBC Okanagan branch on Tuesdays from 9:30 to 10:30 a\.m\. to issue ORL library cards and answer questions\.

Having a public library card gives you access to the [ORL collection](https://orl.bc.ca/), including thousands of ebooks and audio books\! To get a library card you will need two pieces of ID, one with a local address and/or your ID with a bill or receipt that includes your local address\.    

You can easily pick up your holds in the ORL space and return ORL books to the UBC Okanagan Service Desk\.

[Browse ORL Collection &amp; Services](http://www.orl.bc.ca/)
