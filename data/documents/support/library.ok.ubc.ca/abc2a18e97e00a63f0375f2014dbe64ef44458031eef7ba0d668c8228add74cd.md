Whether you are looking to showcase your project using cutting\-edge technology, receive funding for a community engagement initiative, or have your exceptional research paper or project recognized, there is an opportunity for you\! 

**Sharron Simpson Family Community Engagement Endowment Fund**

Follow your interests while you work with community partners and university mentors and gain experience with community research, project management, and knowledge dissemination\.

[Learn more](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/opportunities/sharron-simpson-family-community-engagement-endowment-fund/)

**UBCO Library Student Advisory Committee**

UBCO LSAC members provide insight into current student needs, priorities, and challenges so that UBCO Library can plan services that are relevant, inclusive, and responsive\. 

[Learn more](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/opportunities/lsac/)

**Launch Pad \|** **where ideas take off**

Want to share your interests? Receive funding to hold an event, offer an opportunity, or showcase an idea through the media, technology, and prototyping opportunities available through Constellation\.

[Learn more](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/opportunities/launch-pad/)

**UBC Undergraduate Prize in Library Research**

Showcase your effective and innovative use of library services, information experts, and resources provided by the UBC Library\.

[Learn more](https://about.library.ubc.ca/undergraduate-prize-in-library-research/)

**new Array\(\) Exhibition**

A pop\-up exhibition of innovative digital scholarship and digital art by students, faculty, and staff showcased in high\-traffic areas on both UBC campuses\.

[new array](https://researchcommons.library.ubc.ca/new-array/)

**Employment opportunities**

Looking for employment opportunities? See our Work With Us page\.

[Work With Us](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/work-at-the-library/)
