[Taste of Home Food Drive for UBCO Students](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2021/11/TasteofHome_Newsletter.png)

The Student Union, the Global Engagement Office, and UBC Okanagan Library are collaborating on this annual food drive to help stock the shelves of [The Pantry student food bank\.](https://www.suo.ca/pantry/) Taste of Home Food Drive encourages UBCO staff, faculty, and students to donate unique and nutritious items for our diverse campus community\.

Help give UBCO Students a Taste of Home\. **Donations will be collected at UBC Okanagan Library \(1st floor\) and at Picnic \(UNC 1st floor\) from November 1 until November 30\.**
