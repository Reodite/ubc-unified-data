The UBC Okanagan Library is a nexus of scholarly engagement for students, faculty, staff and visitors at the UBC Okanagan campus\. The Library is a vibrant space full of services, varied study spaces, technology and library collections\.

### Explore the Library

This tour of the library uses 360\-degree photography to provide our community with an opportunity to virtually visit our spaces, including the two main floors of the library and the [Student Learning Hub](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/learning-hub/)\.

For more information about the accessibility supports available to people in the library, visit [Accessibility Supports](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/accessibility-supports/)\.

### **Library Study Spaces**

The first and second floors of the library have a variety of individual and collaborative study spaces with diverse seating options and moveable whiteboards\. Food is allowed on the first floor, but only covered beverages are allowed in the reading rooms and on the second floor of the library\.

[**The Field Reading Room**](https://learningspaces.ok.ubc.ca/find-a-space-informal/?classroom=lib-102-field-reading-room&amp;building=&amp;filters=%257B%2522audioVisualFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%252C%2522accessibilityFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%252C%2522buildingFilter%2522%253A%255B%257B%2522label%2522%253A%2522LIB%2520-%2520Library%2522%252C%2522value%2522%253A%255B%2522LIB%2522%255D%257D%255D%252C%2522furnitureFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%252C%2522layoutFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%252C%2522capacityFilter%2522%253Anull%252C%2522otherRoomFeaturesFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%252C%2522ISAmenitiesFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%257D) \(LIB 102\) and [**The Lindsay Gordon Reading Room**](https://learningspaces.ok.ubc.ca/find-a-space-informal/?classroom=lib-115-gordon-reading-room&amp;building=&amp;filters=%257B%2522audioVisualFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%252C%2522accessibilityFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%252C%2522buildingFilter%2522%253A%255B%257B%2522label%2522%253A%2522LIB%2520-%2520Library%2522%252C%2522value%2522%253A%255B%2522LIB%2522%255D%257D%255D%252C%2522furnitureFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%252C%2522layoutFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%252C%2522capacityFilter%2522%253Anull%252C%2522otherRoomFeaturesFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%252C%2522ISAmenitiesFilter%2522%253A%255B%255D%257D) \(LIB 115\) and **The Corbishley Reading Room** \(COM 004\) in [Okanagan Special Collections](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/special-collections-and-archives/) provide quiet spaces for individual study\.

### **Library Service Desk**

The [Library Service Desk](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/service-desk/) is a central feature of the main floor and is the primary location for:

-   -   [borrowing services](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/borrowing/)
    -   [research and citation help](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/researchandcitation/)
    -   [peer technology help](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/library/service-desk/#peertechnology)

All services are available during [library hours](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/hours-and-locations/)\.

### **Library Collections**

The UBC Library is a leading research library and holds more than 7 million items\.

Book stacks are found on the second floor of the Library and are organized according to the Library of Congress classification system\. View our [guide to finding books](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/c.php?g=707940&amp;p=5040073)\.

Our [collection](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/collections/) also includes curriculum materials, print journals, media resources and maps\. A print reference collection is located by the fireplace on the first floor\.

[Okanagan Special Collections and Archives](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/special-collections-and-archives/) forms part of the Library, and is located in The Commons\.

### Library Instruction Space

LIB 111 is a flexible teaching space for [library instruction](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/library-instruction/), equipped with technology to facilitate collaborative learning\.

### [Image](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2021/09/ITL1000x666.jpg)

### Inclusive Technology Lab

The [Inclusive Technology Lab](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/inclusive-technology-lab/) \(LIB 101\) provides students with assistive technology and supportive study spaces\.

### Okanagan Regional Library

The [Okanagan Regional Library](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/okanagan-regional-library/) operates a branch on the first floor of the UBC Okanagan Library in LIB 125\.

### Studio 123

UBC Studios supports [Studio123](https://ubcstudios.ok.ubc.ca/studio_spaces/studio123/), located in LIB 123 on the first floor of the UBC Okanagan Library\. 

#### Additional Features on the First Floor

**Exercise while you study\!** Two active workstations, a bike desk, and a treadmill desk are located near the main Service Desk\.

**The Flex\-space** \(LIB 106E\) a\.k\.a “the fish bowl” is a collaborative study space, a workshop location, and where you will find [B\.A\.R\.K\.2GO](https://bark.ok.ubc.ca/programs-session-dates/bark2go/) in the Library\.

### Centre for Scholarly Communication

The [Centre for Scholarly Communication](https://csc.ok.ubc.ca/) seeks to enhance research productivity by connecting researchers with supports and tools at every stage of the research life cycle\.

Located on the second floor, and accessible through the main entrance\.

### [Student Learning Hub 1000x666](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2021/09/SLH_Library_1000x666-1.png)

### Student Learning Hub

The [Student Learning Hub](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/learning-hub/) is a one\-stop\-shop for free, flexible, and online student learning supports and resources\.

Located in LIB 237 on the second floor and accessible through the West entrance\.

#### Additional Library Features

**Looking for a class located in LIB?** Classrooms are located on the third floor of the Library building and are accessible through the West entrance only\.

**Artwork** in UBC Okanagan Library is part of the [Public Art Collection](https://gallery.ok.ubc.ca/public-art-collection/)\.

**Gender neutral washrooms** are located on the main floor and third floor of the Library building\. Additional washrooms are located near the main entrance and on the third floor\.
