[The Moose Hide Campaign](https://moosehidecampaign.ca/) is a grassroots movement co\-founded by Raven Lacerte and her father Paul Lacerte to engage all Canadians, especially men and boys, in ending violence towards Indigenous women, girls, and Two\-Spirit People\. This year, Moose Hide Campaign Day is on May 15\.  

### Moose Hide Campaign at UBC Okanagan: May 15  

**Livestream May 15 \| 8:30 to 11 am in ART 114**  

Join Indigenous Programs and Services, the Sexual Violence Prevention &amp; Response Office, the Centre for Teaching and Learning, and the UBCO Library on May 15 for a livestream of the 2025 general plenary and learn how we can all work together to end gender\-based violence in Canada\.  

The plenary will feature keynote speakers [Elaine Alec](https://www.elainealec.com/) and [Michael Kaufman](https://michaelkaufman.com/), as well as messages from the [co\-founders Raven and Paul Lacerte](https://moosehidecampaign.ca/team/)\. 

There will be coffee and refreshments available\. No registration necessary\.    

[Moose Hide Campaign Day Livestream](https://events.ok.ubc.ca/event/moose-hide-campaign-day/)

**Other events on campus:**

-   **May 12 and 14:** Stop by the Sawchuk Family Theatre from 10 am to 1 pm to learn more about the Moose Hide Campaign, [pick up your Moose Hide pin](https://events.ok.ubc.ca/series/moose-hide-campaign/), and make a pledge to end colonial and gender\-based violence\.
-   **May 13:** [Attend an information session](https://events.ok.ubc.ca/series/moose-hide-campaign/) from 11:30 am to 12:30 pm in LIB 111 to learn more about the Moose Hide Campaign\.  

To find out more about the Moose Hide Campaign Day and livestream schedule, click on the link below\.

[Moose hide campaign Day events](https://events.ok.ubc.ca/series/moose-hide-campaign/)
