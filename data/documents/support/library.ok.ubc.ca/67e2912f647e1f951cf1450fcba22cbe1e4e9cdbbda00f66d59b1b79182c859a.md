## A Queer Century, 1869\-1969

[Image](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2019/11/QueerCent-233x300.png) “A Queer Century” tells stories from the history of sexuality, progressing from the emergence of homosexuality as a named concept in 1869 to the announced decriminalization of homosexual activity in Canada in 1969, and highlighting cultural events in the changing perceptions of gender and sexuality\. The exhibition features books, ephemera, and archival materials in English, German, and French from RBSC’s collections, as well as original correspondence held in UBC’s University Archives, and materials generously loaned by local private collectors\.
