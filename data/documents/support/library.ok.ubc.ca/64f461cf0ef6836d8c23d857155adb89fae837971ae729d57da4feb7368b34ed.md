UBC Okanagan Library is committed to finding ways to connect each student with the resources and support needed to fully engage with library collections and services\.

[Woman using lamp, headphones, and screen reader on a computer](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2021/08/assisitive-tech-web-740x340-1.png)

## Accessibility &amp; Inclusion

As reflected in our [Strategic Framework](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/strategic-framework/) and mission statement, UBC Okanagan Library values inclusivity and accessibility\.

We work closely with the [Disability Resource Centre](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/disability-resources/) to support students and yet we do not require or collect any medical or personal information in order to offer assistive supports\. The following list contains frequently requested support:

-   Assistance with retrieving physical books and journals
-   Assistance with navigating accessibility features in different library databases
-   Modified access to print collections, loan periods, and return procedures
-   Individual tours of the Library and Commons buildings with a focus on specific features

[Strategic Framework](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/strategic-framework/) [Disability Resource Centre](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/disability-resources/)

## **Inclusive Technology Lab** 

Located on the first floor in the Library, the [Inclusive Technology Lab](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/inclusive-technology-lab/) \(ITL\) is a supportive environment designed to facilitate learning and research with assistive technologies\. The ITL includes a computer lab, group study space, distraction reduced study room, and a collection of technology to borrow\. 

[Learn about the ITL](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/inclusive-technology-lab/) [ITL Technology Collection](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/inclusive-technology-lab/itl-technology-for-borrowing/)

## Library Instruction

Librarians provide [instruction](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/library-instruction/) on research skills through individual consultations, in classes, and with content provided through Canvas\. In all cases, we strive to create [Accessible Library Instruction](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/accessibleinstruction)\.

Connect directly with your [Subject Librarian](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/help-contact/subject-librarians/) to learn how to make the most of the accessibility features in the tools that you use for library research\.

[Library Instruction](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/library-instruction/) [Accessible Instruction](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/accessibleinstruction) [Subject Librarians](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/help-contact/subject-librarians/)
