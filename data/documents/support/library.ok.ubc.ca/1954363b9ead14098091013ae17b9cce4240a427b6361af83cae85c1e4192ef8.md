**The Records Storage program, located within Okanagan Special Collections on the bottom floor of the Commons, is operational\.** Staff are regularly on campus and can accommodate any box transfers or access requests you have, though they will be subject to some special safety considerations due to [Covid](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2020/09/ORMO_GUI-6_COVID19-pickup_20200714_Rev0.pdf)\. They have also officiated the new records management database O’Neil for more efficient tracking of your box transactions\.

Don’t hesitate to [get in touch](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/records-management-services/#contact) if you want to process a box transfer or access your records in storage\! See the updated website for all forms, instructions and safety protocols\.  
[Records Storage Program](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/records-management-services/records-storage-program/)
