## [Two Lives: a historical podcast researched through Okanagan Special Collections &amp; Archives](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/2025/09/25/two-lives-a-historical-podcast-researched-through-okanagan-special-collections-archives/)

September 25, 2025 (Thursday, September 25th, 2025, 10:09 am)

Renowned local author Alix Hawley has produced a new podcast about the history of the Okanagan in the 1800s called Two Lives and credits UBCO Library’s resources and supports with helping her to create and produce her podcast\.
