UBC Library collections include over 7 million items, including digital and physical formats\. In addition to books, the library lends videos, laptops, assistive technology and other items to support your studies and research\. All borrowing services are free for UBC students, staff and faculty\.

## Account Information

### UBC Card

Your [UBC Card](https://ok.ubc.ca/about/campus-services/ubccard/) is your library card\. Both printed and digital cards are welcome\. For most users, such as students, faculty, and staff, all you need to borrow from the UBC Library is your UBCcard\. [See exceptions for other types of users](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/library-cards/)\.

## Borrowing Physical Collections

Books and videos are found on the second floor of the UBC Okanagan Library\. Bring your UBC ID and your items to the [Library Service Desk](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/service-desk/) to sign them out\. Return your books in the book drop at the main entrance\.

**Recall** is a function to request a book within the UBC Library system that is currently on loan to someone else\. The person with the recalled item will receive a notification, without any personal detail\. Recall is an important mechanism for shared access in a library system and items must be returned within the specified timeline to avoid [fines](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/loan-policies-and-fines/#Fines-1)\.

## Delivery Services

Materials ordered through these services will be available for pickup on the bookshelves adjacent to the [Library Service Desk](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/service-desk/) and must be signed out to your library account\. These services are free for UBC students, faculty, and staff\.

### Borrowing between UBC campuses

[Document Delivery](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/docdel/#WhatisUBCDocDel-0) is a retrieval service for items within the UBC Library system\. Document Delivery is how people located at the UBC Okanagan campus can request items from UBC Vancouver\. Most items take between two to five business days to be delivered\. 

### Borrowing from other institutions

[Interlibrary Loan](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/ill/) \(ILL\) is a service to obtain materials from other libraries that are not available in the UBC Library collection\. 

If you have any questions, or need help with your request, please contact us at **[ill\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:ill.ok@ubc.ca)**

## Course Reserves

Physical [Course Reserves](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/course-reserves/) are kept at the [Library Service Desk](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/service-desk/) and require a UBC ID to borrow\. The loan periods are shortened to allow access for everyone in a course\. Items must be returned to the Library Service Desk\.

[Course Reserves](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/course-reserves/)

## Technology Loans

Technology loans are available at the [Library Service Desk](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/service-desk/)\. Available items include [adapters](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=3959140), [laptops](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=7414318), the [Inclusive Technology Lab borrowing collection](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/spaces/inclusive-technology-lab/itl-technology-for-borrowing/), and calculators\. A Technology Borrowing Agreement must be completed annually before borrowing laptops and ITL technology\. Items must be returned at the Library Service Desk\.

## Fidget Kits and Neurodiversity Tools

[Fidget kit](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2026/03/Untitled-design-73-2-768x960.png)

UBC Okanagan Library has added a collection of supports that can help students stay regulated during the demands of their school day\.

These items include loanable fidget kits, a wiggle seat, “Happy” lights to help combat Seasonal Affective Disorder, visual timers, reading strips, a multi\-position chair, and more\.
