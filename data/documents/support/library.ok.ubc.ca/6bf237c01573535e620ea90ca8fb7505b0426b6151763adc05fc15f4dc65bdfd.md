**How does it work?**

From **February 1 to 23,** tag [@UBCOLibrary](https://www.instagram.com/ubcolibrary/) in an Instagram post \(story or feed\) about your favourite book on movement or sports\. You can also provide your recommendation by commenting on our posts\. Along with tagging @UBCOLibrary, you can join the wider conversation by including [\#ubcolibraryrecs](https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/ubcolibraryrecs/) and [\#moveUBC](https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/moveubc/) in your posts\. Enter as many times as you like\. The more recommendations \= more chances to win\!

This initiative is in partnership with [UBCO Recreation](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/), with funding from [MoveUBC](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/wellbeing-campaigns-and-initiatives/move-ubc)
