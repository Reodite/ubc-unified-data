Instructors can make both physical and electronic learning materials available to students through the Course Reserves service\. They may include electronic articles, scanned chapters from books, ebooks, DVDs, and even websites\.

[Student using a tablet to access digital materials](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2019/05/digital-collection-740x340.jpg)

#### Students

Login with your CWL to find your course reserve readings:

[Login with your CWL](https://courses.library.ubc.ca/login.form)

#### Instructors

UBC Okanagan offers two ways to enter readings into course reserves\.

1.  Let the Library take care of it for you by logging in with your CWL and upload your reading list through the Add Syllabus button\. If you have questions about the service, [email the UBC Okanagan Course Reserves Team](mailto:okanagan.reserve@ubc.ca?subject=Mediated%20Course%20Reserves)\.
2.  Set up your course reserves yourself by logging in and [viewing the user guide](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/using-course-reserves/?login) for help\.

[Login with your CWL](https://courses.library.ubc.ca/login.form)
