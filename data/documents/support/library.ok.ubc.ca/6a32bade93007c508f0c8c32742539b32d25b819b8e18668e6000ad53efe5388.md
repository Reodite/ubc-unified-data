[Students in the UBC Innovation Library](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2019/07/spaces-innovationlib-1000x1000-e1566283746640.jpg)

Students, faculty, staff and community members alike, can access a multipurpose space through the Innovation Library located within the Okanagan Regional Library \(1380 Ellis Street\) in downtown Kelowna\. Uses may include, but are not limited to: meetings, research collaborations, focus groups, small seminar classes, or book clubs\.

### Booking

Access to the Innovation Library is available by appointment only\. Contact Library Services Manager, [Michelle Tinling](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/michelle-tinling/) at michelle\.tinling@ubc\.ca to book this space\.

### Research Assistance for Community Members

For research inquiries, please email [libquestions\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:libquestions.ok@ubc.ca)\. We invite you to explore UBC Library’s Open Collection, which contains over 240,000 unique digital objects\.
