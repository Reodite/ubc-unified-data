## Centre for Scholarly Communication

Through a partnership with Advanced Research Computing, the Office of Research Services, and the UBC Okanagan Library, the Centre for Scholarly Communication functions as an information hub for research support services at UBC Okanagan\.

[Centre for Scholarly Communication](https://csc.ok.ubc.ca/)

#### Location

The Centre for Scholarly Communication is located on the second floor of the UBC Okanagan Library building\. Turn right when exiting the elevator or turn completely around after ascending the stairs to the second floor and you will see the Centre for Scholarly Communication decals on the East side of the building\.

Email [csc\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:csc.ok@ubc.ca) for assistance\.

## Learn more

#### **THE CSC PROVIDES:**

-   One\-on\-one [consultation support](https://csc.ok.ubc.ca/consultations/) for writing, presenting, copyright, research data management, research computing, open access, and more\.
-   A searchable database of [research tools and services](https://csc.ok.ubc.ca/search-tools-and-services/)\.
-   [Workshops](https://csc.ok.ubc.ca/workshops/) on a wide range of research projects and processes and Do\-It\-Yourself materials\.
