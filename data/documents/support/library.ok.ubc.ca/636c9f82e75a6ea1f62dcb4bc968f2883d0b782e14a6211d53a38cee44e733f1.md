As creators, researchers, and distributors of intellectual property, students, staff, and faculty must comply with copyright law and the terms of the UBC Copyright Requirements\.

---

## Resources

Please visit [Copyright at UBC](https://copyright.ubc.ca/) and [Open Course &amp; Educational Resource Guidelines](https://copyright.ubc.ca/open-course-educational-resource-guidelines/) for  answers to common copyright\-related questions from faculty, staff, and students, including:

-   Uploading copyrighted content to Canvas courses
-   Reproducing text and images in a graduate thesis
-   Submitting original academic work to UBC’s digital respository [cIRcle](https://circle.ubc.ca/)
-   Using student work
