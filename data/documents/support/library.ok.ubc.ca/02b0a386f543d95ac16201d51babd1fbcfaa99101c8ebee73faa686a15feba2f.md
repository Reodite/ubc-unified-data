UBCO Library is hiring a Project Records Manager to work alongside Records Manager and Archivist Paige Hohmann\. One of their responsibilities will be the oversight of a portion of UBCO’s [digital records governance projects](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/services/records-management-services/digital-readiness-projects/)\. The objective of this program is to reorganize a campus unit’s shared drive to bring it in line with electronic records management best practices\. This program has proven to be so popular that the demand warrants expanding it\! We hope that the Project Records Manager role will increase records management capacity this summer\. If your unit is considering a digital governance project, get in touch with [Stephanie\.plumb@ubc\.ca](mailto:Stephanie.plumb@ubc.ca) for more details\!
