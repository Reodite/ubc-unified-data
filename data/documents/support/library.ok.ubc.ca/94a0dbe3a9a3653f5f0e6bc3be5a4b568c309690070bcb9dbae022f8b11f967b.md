[Library Management Platform Upgrade](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/116/2026/08/LMP-2026-WebBanner8.jpg)

UBC Library has launched a major project to upgrade the library management and discovery systems to the modern, integrated cloud\-based platforms, Alma and Primo\. The project will provide a more stable, supported and future\-ready foundation for how library resources are managed, discovered and delivered across both UBC campuses\.

Learn more about this migration and [access FAQs](https://about.library.ubc.ca/news/library-management-platform/faq/) by clicking the button below\.
