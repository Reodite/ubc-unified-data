Dashboards include retention and graduation rates, as well as information about the number and type of credentials awarded\.

*Note: the following interactive dashboards are accessible at UBC only \(or via VPN\)\.*

### [Retention and Graduation](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/performance/retention-and-graduation/)

Retention to second\-year, and graduation within six years, for first\-time first\-year full\-time students \(i\.e\. those that meet the CSRDE standard\)\. Filter by campus, faculty, and degree program\.

### [Credentials Awarded](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/performance/credentials-awarded/)

Number of credentials awarded, by student level and program type\. Filter by campus, faculty, and degree program\.
