The **National Survey of Student Engagement \(NSSE\)**, is a standardized survey conducted by the NSSE Institute at Indiana University\. Post\-secondary institutions participate in NSSE on a regular basis\. UBC participates in NSSE every three years \(2026, 2023, 2020, 2017, 2014, etc\)\. It is administered on first year and fourth year undergraduate students\.

**[Learn more about NSSE 2026](https://pair.ubc.ca/nsse/)**
