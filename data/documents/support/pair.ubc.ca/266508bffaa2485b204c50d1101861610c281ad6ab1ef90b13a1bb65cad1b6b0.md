The SEI Dashboards presents survey results starting 2018 Winter Term 1\. The dashboards are updated at the end of each survey period and after 80% of grades are submitted campus wide\.

In order to access the dashboards, the following conditions must be met:

1.  You must be connected to one of UBC’s campuses or online via VPN\.
2.  You must have permission to see SEI Dashboards\.

\*\* *Due to data security and licensing, access will be regularly reviewed\. Accounts with no activity may be disabled\.*

***Instructor Dashboard*:**

Access is automatically provisioned for Instructors who have SEI results within the last 2 academic years\.

If you don’t have access, please submit request to [sei\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:sei.support@ubc.ca) and allow 2 business days for a response\.

***Academic Unit Dashboard:***

If you require access to the Academic Unit Dashboard, please complete the [SEI Administrative Access Form](https://ap-seoi-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/09/SEI-Administrative-Access-Form-September-2024-include-Dashboard.pdf) and contact [sei\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:sei.support@ubc.ca)\.
