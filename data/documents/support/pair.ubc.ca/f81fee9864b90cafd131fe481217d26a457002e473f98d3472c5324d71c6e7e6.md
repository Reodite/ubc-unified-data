### [U15 Data Exchange \(U15DE\)](https://u15.ca/)

Under the leadership of the Academic Affairs Committee, the Data Exchange group helps develop and enhance the distinctive mission of the U15 and its advocacy objectives, through the formal and informal exchange of data sets and information\. The Data Exchange helps develop and enhance the distinctive mission of the U15 and its advocacy objectives, through the formal and informal exchange of data sets and information\. The Data Exchange creates and maintains the resources necessary to construct inter\-institutional and inter\-jurisdictional comparisons to support local and collective, and internal and external policy decisions\. Only authorized users can view the data\. [Contact PAIR](mailto:pair.team@ubc.ca) for more information\.
