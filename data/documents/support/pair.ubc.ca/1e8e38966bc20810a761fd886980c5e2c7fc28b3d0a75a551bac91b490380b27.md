The **Canadian Campus Wellbeing Survey \(CCWS\)** is a new standardized survey led by Dr\. Guy Faulkner, a faculty member at UBC, and a group of academic researchers across Canada\. It is meant as an alternative to the NCHA, or the National College Health Assessment\.
