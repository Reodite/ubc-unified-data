The PAIR Office provides a core institutional service that facilitates higher education research, integrated planning, policy development, and decision\-making at UBC\. Our mission is to make sense of data to support the future of UBC, together\.

### **What is Institutional Research?**

Institutional Research includes a range of objective and systematic research activities that provide information about an institution and its activities, including its students, faculty, staff, programs, management, and operations\.

The findings of institutional research are used by leaders in both academic and administrative roles to inform campus decision making and planning\.
