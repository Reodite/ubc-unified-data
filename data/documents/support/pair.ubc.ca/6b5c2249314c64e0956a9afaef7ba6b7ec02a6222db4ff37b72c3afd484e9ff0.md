## [Decommissioning of CoursEval](https://pair.ubc.ca/2025/12/03/decommissioning-of-courseval/)

December 3, 2025 (Wednesday, December 3rd, 2025, 3:24 pm)

The former survey system CoursEval, which housed SEI surveys conducted prior to Winter 2018, will be decommissioned on December 19, 2025\. After the end of day December 19, the system URL and login will no longer be accessible\. Historical reports and data have been fully archived by the SEI Support Team\. Read more\.
