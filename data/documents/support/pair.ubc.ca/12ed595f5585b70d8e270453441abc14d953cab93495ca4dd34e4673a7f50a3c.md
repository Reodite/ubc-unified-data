**The Planning, Analytics, &amp; Institutional Research Office \(PAIR\)** provides leadership and direction to enhance the coordination of institutional data and analysis across the UBC system, providing optimal support to Faculties, UBC Executive and to external partners\.

The PAIR team leverages data and analytics to guide university\-wide planning and decision\-making, to support integrated university planning, to strengthen student learning and experiences, to support faculty bargaining, and to promote a deeper understanding of the nature and quality of education at UBC\.

The PAIR portfolio encompasses two primary project streams: Integrated Reporting &amp; Accountability and Strategic Intelligence &amp; A dvanced Analytics\.

The PAIR office supports academic and administrative planning and decision\-making for UBC, including faculty collective bargaining\. Data and analytic support are provided for enrolment planning and academic program reviews through online dashboards\.

We research and report information about the university and its environment in support of key initiatives at UBC\. The PAIR office is responsible for internal and external student surveys\. We produce annual reports for the university community and accountability reporting to government\.

### Data Governance

The PAIR office adheres to the policies, procedures, and guidelines of data governance as outlined by the Office of the Chief Information Officer \(OCIO*\)* and the Chief Data Officer \(CDO\)\.

[Learn more](https://cio.ubc.ca/data-governance/policies-procedures-and-guidelines)
