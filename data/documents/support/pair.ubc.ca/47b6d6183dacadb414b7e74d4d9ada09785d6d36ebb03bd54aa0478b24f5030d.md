Dashboards feature information about our student population, including results from the Student Diversity Census and administrative sources\.

*Note: the following interactive dashboards are accessible at UBC only \(or via VPN\)\.*

### [Student Diversity Census](https://pair.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/demographics/student-diversity-census/)

The findings from the Student Diversity Census provide a comprehensive view of UBC’S diverse and intersectional student identities, offering valuable insights into demographics\.

### [Demographics Overview](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/demographics/demographics-overview/)

Demographic data including gender, age, and country of citizenship\.
