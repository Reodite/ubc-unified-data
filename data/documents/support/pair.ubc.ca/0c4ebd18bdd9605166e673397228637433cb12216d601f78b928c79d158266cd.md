In addition to administering our own surveys, we are able support your efforts to engage the UBC community with surveys or focus groups\.

PAIR can provide guidance on several aspects of your survey including:

-   Methodology
-   Population identification and sampling
-   Design and/or review of survey questions
-   Design and/or review of communications
-   Survey training and programming in Qualtrics
-   Coordinated deployment with IT

These services are not necessarily guaranteed at all times of the year\. Please check with us to ensure we have the capacity to support you: [pair\.team@ubc\.ca](mailto:pair.team@ubc.ca)
