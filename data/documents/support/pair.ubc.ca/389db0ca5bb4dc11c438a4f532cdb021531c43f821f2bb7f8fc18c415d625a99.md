These interactive dashboards include information for all registered students at UBC\. Students who are taking non\-credit continuing studies courses or programs are not included\.

*Note: the following interactive dashboards are accessible only at UBC \(or via VPN\)\.*

### [Annual Enrolment Reports](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/enrolment/annual-enrolment-reports/)

The UBC Annual Enrolment Reports provide detailed information about incoming and enrolled students  
system\-wide and at the Okanagan and Vancouver campuses\.

### [Program Enrolments](https://pair.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/enrolment/program-enrolments/)

Enrolment by campus, faculty, program, subject area \(headcounts and FTEs\), filter by domestic/ international, or new to UBC\. Views include data as of November 1\[st\] or March 1\[st\] \(for Winter\) and August 1\[st\] \(for Summer\)\.

### [Course Enrolments](https://pair.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/enrolment/course-enrolments/)

Course enrolments \(registrations or FTE\) by campus, faculty, department, and student program, filter by domestic/international, or gender\.  Views include data as of November 1\[st\] or March 1\[st\] \(for Winter\), and August 1\[st\] \(for Summer\)\.

### [Course Section Schedule Heatmaps](https://pair.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/enrolment/course-section-schedule-heatmaps/)

Course Section Schedule Heatmaps display course section counts, total enrolment, and course section capacity for each campus and session, broken down in 30 minute increments by day of week and time of day\.
