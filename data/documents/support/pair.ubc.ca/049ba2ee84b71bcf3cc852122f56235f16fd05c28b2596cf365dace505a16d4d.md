View the Department Profiles specific to each campus\. The profiles can only be viewed by those who have specifically been granted access, and are accessible at UBC only \(or via VPN\)\.

### [Vancouver Faculty/Department Profiles](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/ubc-data-analytics/department-profiles/vancouver-faculty-department-profiles/)

Contains key performance indicator data for Vancouver campus Faculties and Departments \(where available\)\. **Access is limited to the Deans or their designates\.**

### Okanagan Faculty/Department Profiles \(in progress\)

Contains data for Faculties for all years on the Okanagan campus, and where available specific departments can be selected\.

### [Vancouver Faculty of Arts Profiles](https://pair.ubc.ca/ubc-data-analytics/department-profiles/faculty-of-arts-profiles/)

Contains key performance indicator data for Vancouver Faculty of Arts\. **Access is limited to the Faculty of Arts Dean and department heads, or their designates\.**
