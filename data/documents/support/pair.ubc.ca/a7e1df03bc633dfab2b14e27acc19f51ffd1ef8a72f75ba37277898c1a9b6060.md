Explore institutional data and analytics for UBC: fact sheets, faculty and department profiles, and more\.

### [UBC at a Glance](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/ubc-data-analytics/ubc-at-a-glance/)

Explore UBC by the numbers, including headcount by campus, faculty and school, program, and international students\.

### [Fact Sheets](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/ubc-data-analytics/fact-sheets/)

The Fact Sheets contain an overview of student enrolment, demographics, and faculty and staff data\.

### [Faculty &amp; Staff Data](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/ubc-data-analytics/faculty-staff-data/)

The Faculty &amp; Staff dashboard includes data about paid and unpaid, full and part\-time faulty and staff positions, as well as gender and age distribution\.

### [Department Profiles](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/ubc-data-analytics/department-profiles/)

Contains key performance indicator data by Faculty \(and Department where available\)\. **Access is limited to Deans or their designates\.**

### [Comparative Metrics](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/ubc-data-analytics/comparative-metrics/)

Relevant resources including: BCHEADset, Student Transitions Project \(STP\), and more\.
