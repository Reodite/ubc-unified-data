UBC wants to hear from you\! The **Undergraduate Experience Survey** is a quick 5\-minute check\-in to ask about your year at UBC and your plans for next year\.   

Student feedback has led to real change at the university, including:  

-   Initiatives to address affordability, 

-   Enhanced academic programming and services, 

-   Additional experiential education and research opportunities, and  

-   Extended career development programming  

The survey is open **March 12 – March 28**\. Complete the survey to be entered in a draw to win one of ten $100 gift cards\!  

 Check your inbox for the survey invitation\.
