The Planning, Analytics, &amp; Institutional Research Office \(PAIR\) leverages data and analytics to guide university\-wide planning and decision\-making, to support integrated university planning, to strengthen student learning and experiences, to support faculty bargaining, and to promote a deeper understanding of the nature and quality of education at UBC\.

## About PAIR

PAIR is a university\-wide unit that reports into the Provost and Vice\-President, Academic on each campus\. We provide leadership and direction to enhance the coordination of institutional data and analysis across the UBC system, providing optimal support to UBC executive, Faculties, staff, government and to external partners\.

[Learn more](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/about/)
