View student admissions, demographics, enrolment, and performance data and analytics\.

### [Admissions &amp; Enrolment Planning](https://pair.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/admissions-enrolment-planning/)

Contains functional data to inform the enrolment planning meetings and the admissions process\. **Access is limited to UBC employees working directly on admissions and on enrolment planning\.**

### [Enrolment](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/enrolment/)

Includes information about students in programs, students in courses, annual enrolment reports, and more\.

### [Demographics](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/demographics/)

Features information about our student population, including results from the Student Diversity Census and administrative sources\.

### [Performance](https://pair.ok.ubc.ca/student-data-analytics/performance/)

Includes retention and graduation rates, as well as information about the number and type of credentials awarded\.
