## Advancing UBC\-Wide EDID Efforts

The Robert Quartermain Centre for SOGI\-Inclusive Excellence in Education seeks to advance University\-wide efforts regarding Equity, Diversity, Inclusion, and Decolonization, through a wide range of strategies, including improving teacher education practices, public engagement, broad outreach to schools, professional capacity building, coupled with a strong virtual presence and promotion of crucial health and well\-being messaging\.
