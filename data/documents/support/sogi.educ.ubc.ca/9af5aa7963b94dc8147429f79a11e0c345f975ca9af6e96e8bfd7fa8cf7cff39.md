## Looking to Connect with Other Queer &amp; Trans Teacher Candidates?

The Robert Quartermain Centre for SOGI\-Inclusive Excellence in Education is hosting weekly pizza hangouts for 2SLGBTQIA\+ Elementary and Secondary Teacher Candidates\!

Come eat, connect, and work together, every Monday from 12:00 pm – 1:00 pm in **SCRF 310**\.

The last Monday of every month, Prideful Pedagogy is open to allies\!
