## Overview of our Office

The UBC Investigations Office \(IO\) responds to formal complaints involving UBC students, faculty and staff under the following policies: 

-   [Sexual Misconduct Policy \(SC17\)](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/sexual-misconduct-policy/) 
-   [Discrimination Policy \(SC7\)](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/discrimination-policy/) 
-   [Public Interest Disclosure Policy \(SC19\)](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/pida-policy/)

When retaliation allegations arise out of complaints under these policies, the IO will address them under the [Retaliation Policy \(SC18\)](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/retaliation-policy/)\. If a complaint under one of these policies contains other forms of alleged misconduct, the IO may conduct a consolidated investigation under the [Investigations Policy \(SC8\)](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/investigations-policy/)\.  

We are committed to delivering processes that are fair, accessible, and trauma\-informed\.
