We’d love to connect with you further about the United Way campaign\. Our general email is [unitedway\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:unitedway.ok@ubc.ca)\.

### United Way Campaign Committee

Each year the United Way Campaign is guided by a volunteer committee made up of members from the UBC Okanagan Community\. The committee helps to set campaign goals and build campaign momentum\.

#### Join the 2026 committee

We are looking for additional members to join the 2026 campaign committee\. If you’re interested, please email [unitedway\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:unitedway.ok@ubc.ca) and we’ll send you more information\.

We are looking for faculty and staff from all areas of campus\!
