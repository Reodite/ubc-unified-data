## Looking for joint pain relief?

Hyaluronic acid \(HA\) injections are a non\-surgical treatment option commonly used to manage mild to moderate osteoarthritis of the knee, hip, shoulder, and ankle\. These injections can improve joint lubrication, reduce pain, and help restore mobility, so you can get back to doing what you love\. 

### **How Do Hyaluronic Acid Injections Work?** 

Hyaluronic acid is a substance that mimics the natural synovial fluid found in healthy joints\. It helps to: 

-   Lubricate the joint 

-   Reduce inflammation 

-   Improve joint shock absorption 

HA injections help restore joint function and are also used in treating patellofemoral pain syndrome\. 

### **When Are HA Injections Helpful?** 

Hyaluronic acid injections may be recommended when: 

-   You want to delay or avoid joint surgery 

-   You need an alternative to corticosteroid injections or NSAIDs 

-   Conservative treatments \(like physical therapy or oral medications\) haven’t worked
