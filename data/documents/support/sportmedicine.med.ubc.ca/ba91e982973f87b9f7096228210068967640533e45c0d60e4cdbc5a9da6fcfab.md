The UBC Sport and Exercise Medicine Clinic supports clinical teaching of residents, students and visiting clinicians\.

Family Practice residents may apply for a four\-week sport medicine rotation during their R2 year\. For more information about the program or application process, please contact Julianna Shipanoff at [julianna\.shipanoff@ubc\.ca](mailto:julianna.shipanoff@ubc.ca)\.

We also make every effort to accommodate requests from residents and fellows from emergency medicine, physical medicine, rheumatology, and, space permitting, visiting clinicians\.
