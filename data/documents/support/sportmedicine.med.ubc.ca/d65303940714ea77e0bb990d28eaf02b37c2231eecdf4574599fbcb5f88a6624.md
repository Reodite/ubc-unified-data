We aim to improve your quality of life whether you have been injured and are looking to return to sport, or if you are looking for that extra edge to improve how your body is moving\. At UBC SEM, anyone can be an athlete and we welcome all people to come as they are regardless of age or athletic ability\. We support a wide range of individuals with their sport and exercise needs\.

To receive services, call us or get a referral\. Our [interdisciplinary care team](https://med-fom-sportsmedicine.sites.olt.ubc.ca/team/) includes insured physician services and paramedical services\. Everyone is welcome—you define what sport medicine looks like for yourself\.
