**About the study**

This study aims to develop a questionnaire designed to assess psychological readiness for returning to sport following a concussion\.

Who can take the survey?  
\-Athletes \(16 \+ years\) in competitive contact sports  no prior concussion required  
\-Coaches of competitive contact sports  
\-Healthcare providers with experience treating athletes  
   
What’s involved?  
Complete an online survey \(≈ 45 min\) All answers are anonymous and voluntary\.

About The Study

Canadian Sport Institute Pacific has launched a new research study titled, ‘Bone Stress Injury Recovery and Return to Sport – A Natural History Study’ and is actively recruiting participants based in Victoria and Vancouver\. This project aims to enroll runners or field sports\-based athletes with suspected or confirmed bone stress injury \(aka stress fractures\) of the metatarsal, posterior tibia, femoral neck or sacrum\. Upon meeting the eligibility criteria and providing consent to be research participants, athletes receive a diagnostic MRI \(if required\), and expert developed “return to run” monitored program, with progress MRIs at week 4 and 8\. Check out the poster for more details and contact information\!

Healthy volunteers needed, ages 60–80  
Researchers at the Centre for Heart Lung Innovation \(UBC and St\. Paul’s Hospital\) are recruiting healthy adults for a study on leg muscle function and breathing\.  
You will serve as a healthy control for a study evaluating the function of the leg muscles in patients with interstitial lung disease and determining how leg muscle function affects shortness of breath and exercise capacity\.  
As a participant, you will receive your lung function and VO2max results\.  
For additional questions and to participate in this study, please scan the QR code on the poster or contact Olivia Ferguson at [Olivia\.Ferguson@hli\.ubc\.ca](mailto:Olivia.Ferguson@hli.ubc.ca) or 604\-806\-8835\.
