[UBC Athletics](https://gothunderbirds.ca/) has partnered with us as their main medical care provider\. Through our partnership, UBC varsity athletes are able to receive exceptional and timely care\. The practitioners in the SEM clinic work closely with varsity practitioners to ensure that athletes receive comprehensive treatment plans and can return to sport as quickly as possible\.

-   2024 Canada West 15’s Champions \(5\[th\] consecutive title\)
-   2024 U SPORTS Champions \(1\[st\] in program history\)

**Women’s Volleyball**

-   Won second straight U SPORTS National Championship title

**Women’s Soccer**
