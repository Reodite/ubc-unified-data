We are proud to have formed an industry alliance with MD Biologix, a trusted leader in regenerative medicine\. Their commitment to advancing patient care through innovative technologies, clinical expertise, and education allows us to remain at the forefront of emerging treatment options, helping patients improve their quality of life and live pain\-free\.

At UBC Sport and Exercise Medicine, one of these treatment options includes Synolis, a hyaluronic acid \(HA\) product offered through MD Biologix\. Synolis represents an evidence\-informed approach to joint health and recovery\. Our clinicians incorporate Synolis within a comprehensive care model that includes assessment, rehabilitation, and exercise\-based strategies\.

Synolis is one of several HA injection options available through our clinic and is recommended based on each patient’s unique needs and clinical assessment\.

For more information on how to receive an injection at our clinic, please visit our [Services](https://sportmedicine.med.ubc.ca/injection-services/) page\.  
For details about Synolis, you can visit the company’s website [here](https://www.mdbiologix.com/synolis-va)\.
