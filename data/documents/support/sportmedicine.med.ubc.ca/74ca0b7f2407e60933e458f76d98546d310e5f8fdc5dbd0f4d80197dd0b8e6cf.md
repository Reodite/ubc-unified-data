Sport and exercise medicine is the backbone of the clinical services provided at UBC SEM\. We focus on the diagnosis and management of exercise\-related injury or illness\. This includes the all\-important history, physical examination and appropriate investigations in order to arrive at a working diagnosis\. Prudent management entails activity modification, rehabilitation exercises, optimizing equipment/footwear, medications \(as required\), braces/orthotics and injections \(as needed\)\.

Our primary care physicians have all participated in a [sport and exercise medicine fellowship](https://med-fom-sportsmedicine.sites.olt.ubc.ca/education/fellowships/) program, and most have additional accreditation such as the [Canadian Academy of Sport &amp; Exercise Medicine](http://casem-acmse.org/) \(CASEM\) exam or the SEM accreditation from the Canadian Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons\. Many of our physicians manage the care of athletes on professional or elite national/provincial teams and several have worked at multiple major games including the Olympics, Commonwealth Games, BC Summer Games and Canada games\. A core group are involved in the day\-to\-day care of the UBC Thunderbirds, our varsity athletic teams\.

A [referral](https://med-fom-sport-medicine.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/08/Referral-8.8.2023-js.pdf) from a physician is required to receive an appointment with a sport and exercise medicine physician\.

**Requests for forms or notes from the doctor are subject to a fee and must be paid in full before the document is released to the patient\.** We kindly ask for your understanding in this matter\.
