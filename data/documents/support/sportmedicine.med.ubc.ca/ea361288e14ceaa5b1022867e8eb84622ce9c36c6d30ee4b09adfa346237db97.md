The UBC Sport and Exercise Medicine Clinic offers different types of injection therapies that can effectively promote healing, control inflammation, and manage chronic pain\. These treatments are minimally invasive non\-surgical options that can significantly improve your quality of life\.

Our injection services include the following options\. Use the navigation menu to the left \(top of the page on your phone\) to learn more about each option\.

-   Barbotage Injections
-   Cortisone Injections for Joint Pain Relief
-   Hyaluronic Acid Injections for Osteoarthritis
-   Hydrodilatation \(or Hydrodistension\) Therapy
-   Platelet\-Rich Plasma \(PRP\) Therapy
-   Prolotherapy

All patients receiving injections are required to sign a [**Patient Consent Form**](https://med-fom-sportsmedicine.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/SEM-Patient-informed-consent.pdf)\.

**Please review the patient information form [here](https://med-fom-sportsmedicine.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/Injection_Preparation_Information_Sheet_FINAL-PDF.pdf)\.** It contains detailed information regarding various injection procedures, and what to do before, during, and after your injection appointment\.

For service pricing please review the webpage [here\.](https://sportmedicine.med.ubc.ca/patient-resources/procedure-pricing-and-appointment/)
