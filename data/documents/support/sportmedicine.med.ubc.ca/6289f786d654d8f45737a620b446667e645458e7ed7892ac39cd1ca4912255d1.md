## Fully Body Composition DEXA Scans

DEXA is the gold standard for body composition analysis\. Unlike weight or BMI alone, it provides a detailed breakdown of fat, muscle, and bone, giving you and your care team the clarity to make informed decisions about nutrition, training, and long\-term health\.

#### No referral required \| 30\-60 minutes \(depends on the addition of nutrition services\) \| Includes weight &amp; height measurement \(max weight 300 lbs, max height 6’5\) \| Low radiation, non – invasive \| Results reviewed immediately\.

### What the scan measures

1.  **Body Fat and Lean Mass** : Fast vs muscle across your whole body, detailed by each individual body region\.
2.  **Visceral Fat**: Metabolically active fat around internal organs\.
3.  **Fat Distribution**: Android / gynoid ratio, waist vs hip and thigh storage\.
4.  **Bone Mineral Content:** estimated bone mass and structural health
5.  **Resting Metabolic Rate:** estimated daily calorie burn at rest
6.  **Muscle Symmetry**: Lean mass balance between limbs and sides

This assessment is commonly used for reasons including: fat loss \| muscle gain \| athletic performance \| Metabolic health \| healthy aging &amp; longevity \| sarcopenia risk \| baseline measurements before a program \| progress tracking\.

### Radiation Safety

The radiation dose from a DEXA scan \(&lt;10 µSv\) is extremely low \(comparable to background radiation over a normal day in North America, and less than a flight from Vancouver to Toronto\)\. A conventional chest X\-ray, by comparison, delivers around 60 µSv\. DEXA is very safe but not recommended for pregnant women\.

**Your responsibility:** *Please inform your practitioner if you are pregnant or may be pregnant, have recently had a barium exam, or have received contrast material for a CT or radioisotope scan\.*

### Important Note

DEXA: This scan includes an estimate of overall bone mass but is **not** a diagnostic assessment for osteoporosis or osteopenia\. If you have specific concerns about bone health or fracture risk, consider a Bone Density DEXA Scan \(Spine \+ Dual Femur\)\. Both scans can be completed in a single appointment\.

# Bone Density DEXA Scan : Spine \+ Dual Femur

This scan measures bone density at the lumbar spine and both hips/femur, the primary sites assessed when evaluating bone health and fracture risk\. It is available as a private, non\-referral assessment for individuals seeking proactive insight outside standard referral pathways\.

### Important Note

This is not a hospital\-based radiology service and scans are not formally interpreted by a radiologist\. It is intended for educational, wellness, screening, and monitoring purposes, not medical diagnosis\. If results suggest significant concerns, follow\-up with your physician or a formal medical imaging provider may be recommended\.

## Why Bone Health Matters

Bone loss can occur gradually over many years without obvious symptoms\. Osteoporosis is often called a “silent disease” many individuals don’t realize bone density has declined until a fracture occurs\. A bone density scan can help identify early changes, monitor trends over time, and inform strategies around exercise, resistance training, nutrition, calcium, vitamin D, and healthy aging\.

In Canada, bone density scans are available through physician referral pathways, particularly for adults over 65 or those with elevated risk factors\. Our clinic offers the same assessment as a private service for those seeking earlier or more proactive access\.

## Who May Benefit

Indiviudals with:

A family history of osteoporosis \| Previous fractures \| Post\-menopausal bone health concerns \| Low body weight or low muscle mass \| Low hormone levels \| Long\-term medication use affecting bone \| Proactive aging &amp; baseline monitoring \| Concerns about osteoporosis or osteopenia

### Why Clients Choose Private Testing

Faster access with no referral wait \| Objective baseline measurements \| Proactive, on your own timeline \| Ongoing monitoring over time

# Which Assessment Is Right for You?

  
A Full Body Composition DEXA Scan may be most appropriate for individuals focused on fat loss, muscle gain, performance, metabolic health, or tracking body composition changes over time\.  

A Bone Density DEXA Scan may be more appropriate for individuals wanting to assess bone health, monitor osteoporosis or osteopenia risk, evaluate fracture risk, or track changes in bone density over time\.  

Some clients choose to complete both assessments during the same appointment for a more comprehensive understanding of body composition, muscle health, and bone health\.

# Preparing For Your Visit

#### 24 Hours Before

-   No heavy exercise within 24 hours; no exercise within 12 hours
-   Eat and hydrate normally
-   No alcohol or creatine supplementation
-   Avoid excessive sweating \(sauna, prolonged sun

#### Scan Time

##### Morning Scans

-   Fast overnight
-   No food or fluids from the time you wake up
-   You may eat and drink immediately after

##### Afternoon Scans

-   Fast for at least 4 hours beforehand
-   Record all food and fluids consumed that morning
-   Maintain normal hydration

#### Arriving

-   Wear comfortable clothing with no metal \(zippers, clasps, buckles\)
-   Remove all metal jewellery before the scan
-   Inform staff of any implanted metal
-   No nuclear or dye\-based scans within 2 weeks

#### Follow\-up scans

-   Maintain your usual routine in the 24 hours prior
-   Eat, exercise, and hydrate at the same time as your last scan
-   Book at a similar time of day for consistency
