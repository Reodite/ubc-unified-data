## Meeting with Your Advisor

-   In the first week you will sit down with Sean and he will walk you through this document
-   He will introduce you to the lab’s tools and resources including Google Drive, the Website, and Smartsheet
-   You will talk about semester and overall program objectives, and a funding plan
-   After the initial meeting you will send an email with your expectations of him as an advisor and he will  send you one with his expectations of you as a graduate student
-   You will set a weekly meeting time
-   In your second meeting you will discuss expectation from both sides, identify any issues and revise the email if necessary
-   You will sign the Policy 85 form

## Nuts and Bolts

-   Its good to review the [LFS graduate student handbook](https://blogs.ubc.ca/lfsgrads/lfs-grad-student-handbook "LFS Grad Student Handbook") 
-   Make sure you have access to:
    -   The SAL Lab Google Drive and Google Calendar
    -   Lab computers
    -   Get a desk assignment from Shelly for room 124
    -   [Request](https://secure.landfood.ubc.ca/keydesk/ "LFS Key Request") keys to room 124 and 144
