#### INITIATIVES

**[Carl Wieman Science Education Initiative \(CWSEI\)](http://www.cwsei.ubc.ca/index.html):** CWSEI currently provides support across the Faculty of Science by funding Science Teaching and Learning Fellows \(STLFs\) within departments\.  An STLF is an expert in their discipline and a growing expert in pedagogy who bridges the CWSEI and their department to facilitate the implementation of more effective, research\-based methods of instruction\.  STLFs design and carry out evidence\-based research in the process of improving pedagogy in their department\.
