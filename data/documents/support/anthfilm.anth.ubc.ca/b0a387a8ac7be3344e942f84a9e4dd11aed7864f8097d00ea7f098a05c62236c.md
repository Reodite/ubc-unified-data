The Department of Anthropology offers an ethnographic film methods course \(ANTH 478\) as part of the Department’s regular curriculum\. Students interested in this course should consult with the Department of Anthropology as to the scheudling of the course\. Further details about the course can be found [here](https://anthfilm-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/courses/)\.

The Film Unit has a production lab in the Anthropology\-Sociology Building, more details of which can be found [here](https://anthfilm-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/services-and-facilities/facilities/)\.
