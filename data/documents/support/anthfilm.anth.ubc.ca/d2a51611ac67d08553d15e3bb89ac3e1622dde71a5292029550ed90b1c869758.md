To order videos please print and complete this [pdf order form](https://anthfilm-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/05/order_form.pdf) and mail it, along with payment, to the Ethnographic Film Unit\.

PRICE LIST  
For education use in the classroom $75\.00  DVD format\.  
For private home, non\-commercial use $25\.00  DVD format\.

Payment is by cheque or money order, payable to The Ethnographic Film Unit\. You can also use PayPal\.

[Weather the Storm](https://anthfilm-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?p=22599) can be ordered from [Bullfrog Films\.](http://www.bullfrogfilms.com/catalog/wstorm.html) For Canadian and French distribution please contact the film unit directly\. All other requests should be directed to BullFrog Films\.

Streaming versions of the videos are available for previewing [here](https://vimeo.com/charlesmenzies)\. We do not send preview DVDs\. Some project documentaries are also available on loan from community and school libraries in Prince Rupert in VHS format\.

Copyrights are held by Charles Menzies and, where noted, by Tsimshian communities\. Please do not duplicate DVDs without permission\.
