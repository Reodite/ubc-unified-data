The Film Unit has a production lab in the Anthropology\-Sociology Building\. The lab is dedicated to core productions of the the Film Unit and the teaching of [ANTH 478](https://anthfilm-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/services-and-facilities/courses/) \(when offered\)\.  
Film Lab Hours: The lab is not staffed and thus access is by appointment only\. The normal hours of access are, by prior arrangement, M\-F 08:00 – 18:00\. Department members \(faculty and graduate students\) who wish to use the film lab should contact [Charles Menzies](mailto:charles.menzies@ubc.ca) to arrange access\.  
In certain situations students working on Film Unit projects will be provided with a key to the lab and will agree to the following conditions: \(1\) Use is contingent upon participation in film unit projects\. \(2\) Use of the lab is to be scheduled at least 24 hours in advance of planned use\. \(3\) Normal hours of the lab are 8am\- 6pm – use after these hours requires explicit approval from the Film Unit Director\. \(4\) Guests are not permitted without prior approval from the Film Unit Director\.  
The Faculty of Arts provides a full range of editing stations and software that all students can access\.

#### Equipment Booking

The Film Unit has HD and SD digital tape camera kits that members of the film unit and associated members of the University may use\. Priority of use: \(1\) Core Film Unit projects \(2\) ANTH 478 projects \(3\) Film Unit member projects \(4\) other projects\.  
For a listing of equipment booking fees [click here](https://anthfilm-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/05/equipment-booking-fees.pdf)\.
