As you enter the Music, Art and Architecture library, we welcome you to “Profess Your Love” through this interactive work currently installed on the bulletin board near our library’s main entrance\. Be inspired by the timeless quotes about love and write what is in your heart, be it a bold proclamation or sound advice to passers by\! Visit the site often to see how the work evolves over time\.
