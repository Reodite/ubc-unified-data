The Music, Art and Architecture \(MAA\) Library has dedicated, secure study space for graduate researchers that require **extensive** use of the MAA collection\. The Graduate Research Rooms \(GRRs\) include storage space for library materials\. Interested users may apply for access to the Graduate Research Rooms for the Fall/Winter term or the Summer terms; applications are renewable, depending upon demand\.  

Priority is given to graduate students from the departments and schools directly served by the MAA Library:

-   [School of Music](https://music.ubc.ca/)
-   [Department of Art History, Visual Art &amp; Theory](https://ahva.ubc.ca/)
-   [School of Architecture \+ Landscape Architecture](https://sala.ubc.ca/)
-   [School of Community &amp; Regional Planning](https://scarp.ubc.ca/)

  
**Before** submitting an application please review the rooms’ [**Terms of Use Agreement**](https://maa.library.ubc.ca/files/2019/07/GRR_TOR.pdf)\.

Current GRR users are encouraged to complete an [**Evaluation Form**](https://maa.library.ubc.ca/facilities-technology/graduate-research-room/graduate-research-rooms-evaluation-form/)\.

[**Please click here to submit a Graduate Research Room submission**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2c2rLfxMoKwwxZs)
