Come to the Dodson Room in the Irving K Barber Learning Centre on November 3rd, from 9am to 12:15pm, and enjoy a lively discussion from the RACE\-ing QUEER MUSIC SCHOLARSHIP symposium\. The RACE\-ing QUEER MUSIC SCHOLARSIP symposium, happening November 2 \-November4, will feature a concert, keynotes and multiple panels of academic papers, to race queer music scholarship by critically engaging a richer, more intersectional approach to thinking about music in its relationship with queer bodies\.
