For BC’s Family Day, Monday, February 13th, the Music, Art and Architecture library’s Research Help Desk will be closed\.  
Please direct any questions you have either to the Research Help Desk’s email, [maa\.library@ubc\.ca](mailto:maa.library@ubc.ca), or leave a message with the Research Help Desk’s voice mail, 604\-822\-3943  
Hours for other Research Help Desks on campus for Monday, Feb\. 13th can be found [here\.](http://hours.library.ubc.ca/)
