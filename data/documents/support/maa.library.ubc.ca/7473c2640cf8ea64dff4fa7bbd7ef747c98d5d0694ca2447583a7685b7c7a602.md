UBC Library is happy to receive book suggestions from UBC faculty, students and staff at any time of the year\.  Please be sure to check the [Library Catalogue](http://webcat.library.ubc.ca/) to make sure we don’t already have the book in our collection before submitting a request\.  
To make a suggestion, please use our online [suggest a book form](http://about.library.ubc.ca/contact-us/suggest-a-book/) or contact the appropriate [subject librarian](http://directory.library.ubc.ca/people/subjectlibrarians/)\.
