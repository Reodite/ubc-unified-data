Come visit our current exhibit: *The Heat Is On: Creative responses to climate action in Music, Art, and Architecture*, located in the entrance foyer of the Music, Art &amp; Architecture \(MAA\) Library on Level 3 of the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre \(IKBLC\)\.

This exhibit highlights a selection of material from the MAA Library’s collections which provide critical reflections on pressing contemporary ecological issues, from local and international contexts, and from across the intersecting fields of music, art history and visual art, and architecture and planning\.

Keep an eye out for additional UBC Library displays and programming during Climate Action Week, November 2\-8 2024\.
