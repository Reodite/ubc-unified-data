Having trouble finding high quality images?  
Not sure which images you can use and how to cite them?  
Join us for an introduction to ARTstor digital library, which contains over 1 million images from many disciplines, and Oxford Art Online, a scholarly art encyclopedia covering a wide range of subjects\.  
The workshop will be held on Thursday, February 21st, 2013 at 10:30AM – 12:00PM, in [Room 318](http://parsnip.library.ubc.ca/ikblc/findme/index.php?level=level3) of the IKBLC\.
