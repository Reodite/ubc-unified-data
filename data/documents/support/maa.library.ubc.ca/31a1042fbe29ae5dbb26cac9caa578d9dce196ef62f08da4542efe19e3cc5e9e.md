The Music, Art and Architecture library will begin winter holiday hours as of December 19, 2018\.  We will be closed from December 25 through until January 2, 2019, when we will reopen and resume Winter Term session hours\. Please visit [hours\.library\.ubc\.ca](http://hours.library.ubc.ca/#view-library) for complete details\.  
The MAA Library Research Help Desk hours at the MAA Library will be following during the winter holiday period:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Mon\-Fri | 11am – 3pm |
| Saturday &amp; Sunday | Closed |

If you are physically unable to visit the library and the Research Help Desk, you can email your research questions to [maa\.library@ubc\.ca](mailto:maa.library@ubc.ca) or phone 604\-822\-3943\.  
Wishing everyone a safe and happy holiday\!
