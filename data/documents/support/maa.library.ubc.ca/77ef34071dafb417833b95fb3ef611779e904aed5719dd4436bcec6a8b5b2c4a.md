You are invited to attend a brief 30 minute tour of the physical resources and services attached to the UBC Music Collection in the Music, Art &amp; Architecture Library \(MAA Library\), Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre \(IKBLC\)\.  
**Dates:** Monday, September 21st – Thursday, September 24th  
**Time:** 12:15 to 12:45 on each day  
**Location:** meet at the entrance of the MAA Library, 3\[rd\] floor, IKBLC  
No need to register\. I am happy to work with whoever shows up\!  
Best, Kev Madill \(UBC Music Librarian\)
