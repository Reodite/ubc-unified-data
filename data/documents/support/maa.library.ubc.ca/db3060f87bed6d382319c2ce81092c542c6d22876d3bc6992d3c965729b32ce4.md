The Music, Art and Architecture library will be closed on Monday, August 01, for the BC Day Civic holiday\. We will be open again, and resuming our summer hours on Tuesday, August 02\.  
To check the MAA library hours, please go the [hours\.library\.ubc\.ca\.](http://hours.library.ubc.ca/#view-library)  
If you have a research question, you can get in contact with the Research Help Desk team either by [email](mailto:maa.library@ubc.ca), or by phoning and leaving a detailed voice message, 604\-822\-3943  
   
We wish everyone a safe and happy holiday\!
