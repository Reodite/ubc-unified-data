Plan on visiting the MAA library during Reading Week? Need research help while you’re here? The Music, Art and Architecture library’s **Research Help Desk will be open Monday, Feb\. 15 to Friday, Feb\. 19 from 11am to 3pm\.**  
If you have a research question, and we are unable to assist you at that time, you can email the Music, Art and Architecture library at [maa\.library@ubc\.ca](mailto:maa.library@ubc.ca) or call the Research Help Desk and leave a message, 604\-822\-3943
