```text
     Diana E.M. Cooper
        1943 - 2004



Diana Elizabeth Malcolm Cooper was born and raised

in Vancouver, BC.


Diana's passion for the fine arts and teaching was clearly

demonstrated by her education and career path. Diana

graduated from UBC with a Fine Arts Degree in 1964, and

went on to obtain her Bachelor of Library Science in 1965.



As a newly graduated librarian, Diana started her

professional career at UBC Library. From 1965 to 2004, she

worked as a Fine Arts Reference Librarian. During this time,

she facilitated numerous partnerships to build UBC's fine arts

collection, dedicating her boundless energy to new projects,

exhibits, writing and teaching. Through this process, she built

lasting relationships with faculty, staff and students.
```
