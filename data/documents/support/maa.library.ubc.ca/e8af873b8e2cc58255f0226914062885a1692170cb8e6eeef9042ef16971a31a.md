The Music, Art and Architecture Library has reduced reference hours during the December exam period\.  
**Research Help Desk Hours:**

-   Dec\. 4 – 8: 11 am – 3 pm
-   Dec\. 11 – 20:  12 pm – 2 pm

The Research Help Desk will be **closed as of Thursday, December 22**, and will reopen again on January 3, 2018\.  
Please be sure to also check the [MAA library hours page](http://hours.library.ubc.ca/#view-library) if you are planning on working in the library this holiday season\.  
On behalf of the MAA library, we wish you all a safe and happy holiday\!
