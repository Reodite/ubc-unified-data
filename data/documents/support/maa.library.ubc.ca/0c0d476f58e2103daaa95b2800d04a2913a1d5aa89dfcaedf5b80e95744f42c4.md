On June 24th the Music Library’s collections and services joined the Art \+ Architecture \+ Planning Division of UBC Library in the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre and became the Music, Art and Architecture Library\.  
For more information on the relocation of the Music Library [click here](http://about.library.ubc.ca/2013/06/14/music-library-relocation/) or contact [Vanessa Kam](http://directory.library.ubc.ca/people/view/600), Acting Head of the Music, Art and Architecture Library\.
