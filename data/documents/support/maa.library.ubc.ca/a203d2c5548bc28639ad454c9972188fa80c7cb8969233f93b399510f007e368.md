Attention Graduate Students\!  
Would you like a quiet and secure space to store library materials and conduct graduate\-level research in the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre \(IKBLC\)?  
Our Graduate Research Room:

-   has secure storage for library materials checked out to you so that you won’t have to schlep large, heavy, back\-breaking monographs to and from UBC?
-   provides you with a locked room, reserved for 20 Graduate Students \.
-   provides you with easy access to the IKBLC  library collections \(which include art, architecture and planning materials\)\.
-   provides you with easy access to the art, architecture and planning librarians\.

Apply online today\!  
[http://services\.library\.ubc\.ca/library\-facilities/research\-space/](http://www.library.ubc.ca/home/graduate-research-room/)  
Space is limited to twenty students\.  First come, first served\.  
Questions? Contact Paula Farrar, Acting Head Librarian, Art \+ Architecture \+ Planning, UBC Library: [paula\.farra@ubc\.ca](http://directory.library.ubc.ca/people/email/103)
