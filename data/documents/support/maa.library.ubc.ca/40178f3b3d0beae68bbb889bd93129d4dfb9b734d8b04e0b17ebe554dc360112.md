[https://aarp\-library\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2011/12/barcodesuggestbook\.jpg](https://aarp-library.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/12/barcodesuggestbook.jpg)[barcodesuggestbook](https://aarp-library.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/12/barcodesuggestbook.jpg)AArP staff members are ecstatic to receive book suggestions from UBC faculty, students and staff at any time of the year\.  Please be sure to check the Library’s [Catalogue](http://webcat.library.ubc.ca/) to make sure we don’t already have the book in our collection before submitting a request\.  
   
   
Please provide us with the following: a\) title; b\) author; c\) year of publication; and d\) the ISBN\.  Feel free to tell us why you think the title would make a great addition to our collection\.  Send your requests to [aarp\.library@ubc\.ca](mailto:aarp.library@ubc.ca)\. Please provide contact information if you would like  to be notified when the book arrives\.
