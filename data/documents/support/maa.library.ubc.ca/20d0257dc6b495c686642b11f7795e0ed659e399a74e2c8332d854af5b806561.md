Having trouble navigating your way through the complex world of information available to you through UBC Library? Our hands\-on workshops may help\.  
The Library offers a number of workshops throughout the year, ranging from library orientations to database\-specific tutorials to in\-depth workshops including Literature Reviews, Citation Management, Current Awareness, and much more\.  
Everyone is welcome to attend public workshops listed on the [Library’s Public Events Calendar](https://libcal.library.ubc.ca/calendar/vancouver)\. Register \(for free\), from the workshop description in the calendar\.
