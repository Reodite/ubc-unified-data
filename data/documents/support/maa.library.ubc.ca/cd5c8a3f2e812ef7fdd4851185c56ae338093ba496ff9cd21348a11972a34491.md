The Music, Art and Architecture library will be closed on Friday, July 01 for Canada Day\. We will also be closed on Saturday, July 02, and Sunday, July 03\. We will open on Monday, July 04 at 9am, resuming our summer alternate hours\.  
The MAA library Research Help Desk will also be closed from July 01\-03, opening again on Monday, July 04 at 11am\.  
To review our hours, including the Research Help Desk hours, please check [the library hours and locations webpage](http://hours.library.ubc.ca/#view-library)  
Have a safe and happy Canada Day\!
