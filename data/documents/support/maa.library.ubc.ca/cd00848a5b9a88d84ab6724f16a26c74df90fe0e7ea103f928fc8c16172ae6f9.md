The generosity of Library friends is crucial for UBC Library to keep pace with the information resources and education needs of today’s and tomorrow’s students, researchers and lifelong learners\. Donors come in all shapes and sizes and each one is integral in helping UBC Library reach its goal of being a leader in information resources and services\.  
There are many ways that you can [donate to the Music, Art and Architecture Library](http://support.library.ubc.ca/giving/)\. We accept donations of materials \(books, journals, memorabilia, etc\.\) as well as contributions to endowment funds like the [Diana Cooper Endowment](https://maa.library.ubc.ca/collections/donate/cooper-endowment/)\.  
Please note: due to limitations in space and staff resources, we are only able to accept unique items that meet our current collection needs\.  If you have items that you would like to donate to the Music, Art and Architecture Library collection, the first step is to contact the Music, Art and Architecture Library via email at [maa\.library@ubc\.ca](mailto:maa.library@ubc.ca) or via phone at \(604\) 822\-3943\.  Thank you\!
