A [facsimile of the Book of Kells](https://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=798509), first acquired by UBC Library’s Rare Books &amp; Special Collections \(RBSC\) in 1990, is currently on display in the Music, Art &amp; Architecture \(MAA\) Library’s Ridington Room\.

The original Book of Kells is a 9th century illustrated manuscript and Celtic Gospel book written in Latin\. Its lavishly decorated pages are considered a masterwork of Western calligraphy and Insular art\. The name originates from the Abbey of Kells, where it was kept for centuries before moving to Trinity College Library in Dublin, Ireland where it is on permanent display\.

The pages of the facsimile are identical to the original manuscript in scale, layout, and colour\. Only 1480 facsimile editions were produced by Swiss publisher Faksimile\-Verlag Luzern, of which UBC Library’s copy is no\. 349\. It is accompanied by a presentation book from Vancouver’s Book of Kells Committee that includes the names of all donors, with calligraphy by Aidan Meehan\. The Committee raised $20,000 from 340 donors to purchase the book and commission an intricately carved display case\. Plaques on the case, located in the Ridington Room, acknowledge these gifts\.

This contribution to UBC Library by Irish Canadians and Friends of the University is being highlighted during the month of March which, since 2022, is officially recognized as Irish Heritage Month in Canada\.

An additional selection of materials from MAA Library collections explores some of the many different ways that Irish cultural heritage has engaged with the arts \(including music, visual art, book arts, and literature\), design, and the built environment – historically and in contemporary capacities\.
