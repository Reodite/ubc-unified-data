UBC Library is currently running trials of the following two databases until November 30, 2018:

-   **[Bloomsbury Architecture Library](https://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?id=2437)**  
    A carefully curated collection of fully\-searchable text and image content\. Incorporating the new edition of Sir Banister Fletcher’s Global History of Architecture\.

-   **[Pidgeon Digital](https://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?id=2439)**  
    A collection of historical and recent talks given by some of the world’s most important architectural professionals\.

To help the Library assess the merit of these resources please fill out the online feedback forms or send comments to Paula Farrar \([paula\.farrar@ubc\.ca](mailto:paula.farrar@ubc.ca)\) before December 15, 2018\.
