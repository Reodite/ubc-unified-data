The Music, Art &amp;Architecture Library at the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre supports the study, research and teaching needs of the faculty, students and staff in the [School of](https://music.ubc.ca/) [Music](https://music.ubc.ca/), the Department of [Art History, Visual Art &amp; Theory](https://ahva.ubc.ca/), the [School of Architecture \+ Landscape Architecture](https://sala.ubc.ca/), the [School of Community &amp; Regional Planning](https://scarp.ubc.ca/)\.  In addition to these subject areas the collection contains mathematics, statistics, computer science, and library and information studies and older humanities &amp; social sciences materials\.  It is also the home of the [ASRS](http://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/asrs/), UBC Library’s Automated Storage and Retrieval System\.
