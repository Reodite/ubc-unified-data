For a limited time, UBC library is offering access to a new database, [Routledge Encyclopedia of Modernism](https://www.rem.routledge.com/)\.  
The Routledge Encyclopedia of Modernism is a comprehensive, regularly\-updated resource with over 1,000 articles of multi\-disciplinary content about the Modernist period\. It can be browsed by subject, movement, or place with new content always added\.  
There are over 1,000 articles covering eight key subject areas: literature, architecture, visual arts, music, dance, theatre, film and intellectual currents\.  
Please enjoy this trial of the Routledge Encyclopedia of Modernism, and [tell us what you think of this resource\!](http://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?details=routledge-encyclopedia-of-modernism&amp;id=2303)
