The Research Help Desk in the Music, Art and Architecture library will go to reduced hours for the summer months\. The hours for research help will be:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Monday to Friday |  11:00 am to 3:00pm |
| Saturday &amp; Sunday |  CLOSED |

If you have a research question and we are not at the desk to help you, you can email the Music, Art and Architecture library’s email at [maa\.library@ubc\.ca,](mailto:maa.library@ubc.ca) or call and leave a voicemail message at 604\-822\-3943\.  
Have a safe and enjoyable summer\!
