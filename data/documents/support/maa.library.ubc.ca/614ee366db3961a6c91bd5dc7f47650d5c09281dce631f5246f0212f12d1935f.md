Come visit our current exhibit: *The Old Auditorium at 100,* located in the entrance foyer of the Music, Art &amp; Architecture \(MAA\) Library on Level 3 of the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre \(IKBLC\)\.  
  
This exhibit focuses on the history of the Auditorium, one of the first buildings built on UBC’s Point Grey Campus, and touches on both physical aspects of the building, and the Old Aud’s role as a cultural hub at the heart of UBC\. The exhibition highlights materials from University Archives and Rare Books &amp; Special Collections\.

  
The exhibit will be on display throughout Winter Term I \(October 2025\-January 2026\)\.
