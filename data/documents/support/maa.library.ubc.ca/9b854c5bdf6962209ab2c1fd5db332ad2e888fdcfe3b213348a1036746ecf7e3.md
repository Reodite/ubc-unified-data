The MAA Library supports the academic listening needs of the UBC School of Music by offering 1 CD player, 1 cassette player, and 1 LP player as well as the bookable acoustically buffered [Digital Media Rooms](https://maa.library.ubc.ca/facilities-technology/dmr)\. These items are located in the alcove area behind the digital media rooms\. Head phones are available to be signed out at the Circulation Desk\.
