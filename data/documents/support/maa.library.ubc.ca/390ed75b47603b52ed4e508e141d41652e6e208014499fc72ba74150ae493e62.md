| Have you ever been frustrated trying to find the perfect image for your research paper?  Have you found the perfect image, but been unable to use it due to insufficient information?  Or not sure if you can use it due to copyright restrictions?  **[ARTstor](http://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?details=artstor&amp;id=620)** is a digital image library for the arts and sciences with over 1\.6 million images licensed for educational use here at UBC\.  In this one hour workshop you will learn how to effectively search the ARTstor database as well as interact with its many features such as saving and downloading images and using the offline presentation software Interested in learning more?  **[Register here](http://elred.library.ubc.ca/libs/dashboard/view/5386)** for the The Music, Art &amp; Architecture Library’s ***Finding &amp; Using Images workshop*** on Wednesday, December 3, from 11am \-12pm in room 318\. |
