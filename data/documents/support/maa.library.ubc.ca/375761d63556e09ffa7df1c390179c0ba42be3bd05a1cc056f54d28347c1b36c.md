### The Music, Art and Architecture library is hosting an ARTstor workshop

**Date:** Wednesday, January 27th \| 1:00\-2:00PM  
**Location:**  IK Barber Learning Centre – Library Instructional Lab, Room 318  
**Register**: Click [here](https://events.library.ubc.ca/dashboard/view/6082) to register\.  
ARTstor is a digital library of over 1\.9 million high quality images licensed for educational use at UBC, covering the areas of art, architecture, the humanities, and social sciences\. ARTstor will also be of interest to anyone in disciplines where images play a significant role in knowledge building and discovery\. In this workshop, you will learn how to effectively search the ARTstor database as well as interact with its many features such as saving and downloading images and using the offline presentation software\. All students, faculty, and staff are welcome\!
