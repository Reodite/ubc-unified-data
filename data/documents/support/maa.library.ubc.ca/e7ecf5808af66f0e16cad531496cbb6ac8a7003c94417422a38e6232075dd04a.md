The Dodson Music Series schedule for 2011/2012 [is now posted](http://www.ikebarberlearningcentre.ubc.ca/dodson_music)\! Performances are held in the Dodson Room of the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre\. These free concerts are organized and performed by students of the [UBC School of Music](http://www.music.ubc.ca/)\. Leah Giselle Field is the Artistic Director, and Kirsten Walsh is the Library liaison\.  
Read about the history of the Dodson Music Series [here](http://www.ikebarberlearningcentre.ubc.ca/dodson_music)\.
