Diana E\. M\. Cooper worked as a Fine Arts Reference Librarian at UBC Library from 1965 to 2004\. During this long career, Diana cultivated her passion for the fine arts by creating a body of colourful sketches and watercolours, many with a library theme\. These images have adorned many a Library website over the years and are now brought together here for everyone’s enjoyment\.
