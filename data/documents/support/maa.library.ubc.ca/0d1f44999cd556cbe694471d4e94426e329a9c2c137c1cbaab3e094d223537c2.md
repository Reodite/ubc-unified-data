Based in Berlin and New York, Sternberg Press publishes titles on art criticism, theory, fiction, and artists’ books\.  
From their website: “Dedicated to an expanded notion of writing on art, Sternberg Press has created a formidable platform in which practitioners from the fields of art and culture \(architecture, design, film, politics, literature, and philosophy\) can engage in a critical discourse\. Each book is a special object celebrating creative publishing at its best, based on both meticulous editorial decisions and distinctive design\. Through both commissioned and translated works, Sternberg Press seeks out the blind spots within contemporary discourse and offers a timely response to the related debates\.” [Visit the Sternberg Press website](http://www.sternberg-press.com/)\.
