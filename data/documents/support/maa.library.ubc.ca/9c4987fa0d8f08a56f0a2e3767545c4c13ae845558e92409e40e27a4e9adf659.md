#### In person

The Research Help counter, located at the Information Desk on Level 3 of the Music, Art and Architecture Library, is open from Monday to Friday during Winter Terms 1 and 2\. Please see the “Today’s Hours” box at left for our current hours\.

UBC students, faculty, and staff can also book reference consultations with the appropriate [subject librarian](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/subjectlibrarians/People.division_id:53)\.  
Librarians’ contact information can also be found on the subject\-specific [Research Guides](https://maa.library.ubc.ca/?login#fancyboxID-1)\.

#### By phone

Call the Research Help desk at \(604\) 822\-3943\.

#### By email

Email us at [maa\.library@ubc\.ca](mailto:maa.library@ubc.ca)\.

#### **Specialized help**

Our [subject librarians](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/subjectlibrarians/People.division_id:53) can provide personalized help with your research\. Please reach out by email or phone to arrange a reference consultation\.
