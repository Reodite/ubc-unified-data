Opera enthusiasts will be delighted to know that we have access to [Opera in Video](http://resources.library.ubc.ca/1713/) \(Alexander Street Press\)\. This resource currently contains 260 videos, including important opera performances, captured on video through staged productions, interviews, and documentaries\.
