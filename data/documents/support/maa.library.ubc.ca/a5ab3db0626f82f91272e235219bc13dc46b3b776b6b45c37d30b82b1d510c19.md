UBC Library is now subscribing to EBSCO’s [**Garden, Landscape &amp; Horticulture Index\.**](http://resources.library.ubc.ca/page.php?id=2102)

Garden, Landscape &amp; Horticulture Index is “designed for gardening enthusiasts, professionals, and students of horticulture and of garden and landscape design &amp; history\.  \[It\] covers a wide range of serial titles including general gardening titles of national, international, and regional interest and titles devoted to specialty gardens and plant groups\. Indexed and abstracted are more than 500 core titles, the majority of which are published in English with a strong focus on environmentally sustainable horticultural and design practices”\. \(*description from [website](http://www.ebscohost.com/academic/garden-landscape-horticulture-index)*\)
