The Music, Art and Architecture library’s Research Help Desk will begin winter session hours, starting Tuesday, September 6th\. Stop by the Research Help Desk, Monday to Friday from 11am to 5pm for research help\.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Monday – Friday |       11:00 – 5:00 |
| Saturday &amp; Sunday |          CLOSED |

If you are physically unable to visit the library and the Research Help Desk, you can email your research questions to [maa\.library@ubc\.ca](mailto:maa.library@ubc.ca) or phone 604\-822\-3943\.
