We offer two field locations for your research needs\.

[totem\.jpg](https://plantcare.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_325x325/public/images/totem.jpg?itok=7CbHTbeC)

### Totem Field

2613 West Mall, V6T 1Z2

-   Located on the main Vancouver UBC campus
-   Field preparation, seeding or planting, fertilizing, weed control, harvesting
-   Plant tissue drying and processing
-   Short term or long term projects
-   Soil information available for each plot of land
-   Diverse soil types range from frich and loamy to dry sandy soils
-   Adaptive irrigation capacity from drip line to overhead on some plots
-   Support for data collection or teaching purposes

For consultation, quote or reserving space, please [contact us](https://plantcare.ubc.ca/node/764)\.

### Fields adjacent to South Campus Greenhouse

3929 Wesbrook Mall, V6T 1W5 

Please note that weed control may be necessary to maintain clean plant growing conditions in fields and greenhouses\.

-   Close to South Campus greenhouse facility
-   Field preparation, seeding or planting, fertilizing, weed control
-   Support for data collection or teaching purposes
-   Adaptive irrigation capacity from drip line to overhead available

For consulation, quote or reserving space, please [contact us](https://plantcare.ubc.ca/node/764)\.
