We offer two greenhouses for your research needs\.

[hgh600\.jpg](https://plantcare.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/max_325x325/public/images/hgh600.jpg?itok=8vufEG_R)

### Main Campus Greenhouse

6394 Stores Road, V6T 1Z4 \(on East Mall to the north of Orchard Commons\)

-   Programmable temperature, LED and HPS lights, CO2 regimes and fertilizer regimes
-   Six different compartments with either flood tables or hydroponics
-   Outside patio
-   Support for experimental design
-   Support for data collection or teaching purposes

For consultation, quote or reserving space please [contact us](https://plantcare.ubc.ca/node/764)\.

### South Campus Greenhouse

3929 Wesbrook Mall V6T 1W5 \(adjacent to Botanical Garden nursery\)​

-   Programmable temperature, LED lights and CO2 regimes
-   Eight different compartments with flood tables
-   Support for experimental design
-   Support for data collection or teaching purposes

For consultation, quote or reserving space please [contact us](https://plantcare.ubc.ca/node/764)\.
