Plant Care Services at UBC Vancouver: a shared research platform for greenhouse and field scientific experiments\.

-   Coordinated access and services at two research greenhouses and two field locations on UBC's Vancouver campus
    
-   Expert advice for experimental design, plant cultivation, integrated pest management, nutrition and hydroponics
    
-   Academic and industry projects
    
-   Training and guidance
    

Plant Care Services \(PCS\) is a shared research platform funded as a partnership between the Faculty of Science, Dept\. of Botany, Faculty of Forestry, Faculty of Land and Food Systems and the Office of the Vice\-President, Research &amp; Innovation\.

#### [OUR TEAM](https://plantcare.ubc.ca/about-us "About Us")

**The PCS Steering Committee** is in charge of ensuring the sustainability of the platform\. It is led by Amy Angert, Associate Professor and Canada Research Chair in Conservation Ecology who is the Scientific Advisor of PCS\. Other members of the Steering Committee include faculty members, representatives of VPRI and stakeholder UBC Farm\.

**The staff team**, led by Melina Biron, includes three technicians who offer research services and training and are waiting to meet you\!
