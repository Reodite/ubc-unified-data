## Who We Are

[Stephanie Ewan](https://afrf-forestry.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/11/Stephanie-Ewan.jpg)

**[Stephanie Ewen](mailto:stephanie.ewen@ubc.ca)**, MF, RPF  
Director 

Worked as planner and silviculture forester at AFRF for 2\.5 years prior to becoming manager in 2018\. Trained in forest management and analysis in British Columbia and Quebec, and has over 10 years in experience as a forestry consultant in BC\. Offers a diverse suite of knowledge and experience in forestry, tourism, and academia\.

[Read an interview with Stephanie on the UBC Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship website](https://forestry-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/news/inside-the-forest-stephanie-ewen-on-managing-the-alex-fraser-research-forest/)

[Noel Mckinnon](https://afrf-forestry.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/12/Noel-McKinnon-web.jpg)

[**Noel Mckinnon**](mailto:noel.mckinnon@ubc.ca)  
Maintenance &amp; Infrastructure Development Technician

Decades of experience in the Cariboo in field work, namely running his own fencing business, led Noel to join the AFRF team in 2023\. Between building his own home, ranching, and raising his two daughters, Noel’s passion for the outdoors keeps him active and engaged in a variety of hobbies\.

[Mustafa Onder Ersin](https://afrf-forestry.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/Mustafa-Onder-Ersin-web.jpg)

**[Mustafa Onder Ersin](mailto:mustafaonder.ersin@ubc.ca)**  
Project Manager, Community Bioenergy Systems \(CHP Academy\)

An ecologist with 15\+ years’ experience across government, academia, and non\-profits in Europe and Canada\. Specializing in forestry, wildlife ecology, and community initiatives, he leads UBC bioenergy and forestry projects focused on Indigenous partnerships, training, and sustainable land use\.
