-   Assistive Listening System \- Advance Bookable
-   Automatic Door Opener
-   Wheelchair Lift \- Incline
-   Wheelchair Accessible \- Student Seating Area

The accessible route to the front of A104 requires users to take a wheelchair lift in the corridor A130A \(beside BUCH A102\) and then cross through the front of both BUCH A102 and BUCH A103\.
