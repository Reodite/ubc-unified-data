The Office of the Provost and VP Academic has created an [Advisory Committee for UBC Learning Spaces](https://academic.ubc.ca/academic-community/committees/vpa-committees)\. The Learning Spaces Team and the Advisory Committee are committed to supporting UBC’s ability to be adaptable to the evolving needs of teaching and learning\.

The committee is composed of the Associate Deans of each of the faculties and senior staff from administrative units\. It provides a framework for optimizing UBC’s learning spaces to support [flexible learning](https://web.archive.org/web/20241220021358/http://flexible.learning.ubc.ca/) practices and deliver the best possible student learning experience\.

View the current [Learning Spaces A](https://vpfo-learningspaces-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/01/LSAC-Contact-List-Jan-2022.pdf) [dvisory Committee members \(PDF\)\.](https://web.archive.org/web/20241220021358/https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/sites/learningspaces.ubc.ca/files/LSAC%20Contact%20List%20Jan%202022.pdf)
