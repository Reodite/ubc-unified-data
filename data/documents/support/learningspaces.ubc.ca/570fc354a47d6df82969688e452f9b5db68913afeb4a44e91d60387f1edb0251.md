## Not all learning takes place in traditional classrooms

Finding informal learning spaces that meet your needs is easy\. Whether you are looking for space for conversation and collaboration or a quiet corner to study, you can find a variety of facilities across campus\.

In addition to these informal learning spaces, UBC Library has a variety of [study and learning spaces](http://services.library.ubc.ca/facilities/group-silent-study-space/)\. Links to their hours, locations, and further information can be found [here](http://hours.library.ubc.ca/)\.
