### Have questions for us or just want to let us know about something?

Get in touch and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible\.

-   If you need Audio Visual support, contact the **AV Help Desk** at 604\.822\.7956 or [av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:av.helpdesk@ubc.ca)
-   If you are locked out of your classroom, contact **UBC Security** at 604\.822\.2222\. 
-   If you would like to book a general teaching space or have questions about your room booking, contact **UBC Scheduling Services** at [room\.bookings@ubc\.ca](mailto:room.bookings@ubc.ca)
-   You may be able to find an answer in our [Help and support section](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/useful-resources/help-support/)\.
-   We can also be reached by email at [learning\.spaces@ubc\.ca](mailto:learning.spaces@ubc.ca)
