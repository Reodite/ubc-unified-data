## Address

6363 Agronomy Road, Vancouver, BC V6T 1Z4

## Hours

Winter session hours M\-Sun: 7am \- 10:45pm\. Hours for summer session vary\.

## Building Notes

The entrance to the classroom block of Orchard Commons is the Agronomy Road entrance\. Orchard Commons is typically open limited hours \(Mon\-Fri, 7:30am\-5pm\) in the summer when the residence is closed\.
