## Find answers about furniture, policies, Audio Visual \(AV\) and other equipment

## Furniture and equipment

-   Audio Visual Equipment: 604\-822\-7956 or [av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:av.helpdesk@ubc.ca)
-   Furniture: [learning\.spaces@ubc\.ca](mailto:learning.spaces@ubc.ca)

If there is permanent marker on a whiteboard, use a dry\-erase marker to draw over the permanent marker, and then erase\. If the mess is more than a quick fix, please let UBC Facilities know by emailing [servicecentre\.facilities@ubc\.ca](mailto:servicecentre.facilities@ubc.ca)\.

The Learning Spaces team has an annual summer upgrade program\. However, we welcome suggestions for room improvements at any time\. We have an ongoing maintenance program and can often get to small fixes quickly\. Please [contact us](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/contact-us/) with a room improvement need\.

Contact [learning\.spaces@ubc\.ca](mailto:learning.spaces@ubc.ca) to review room capacities\. Room capacity depends on many variables, including safety and building requirements, type of furniture, and student comfort\. Most of our classrooms are already optimized for the right number of seats, meaning it is rare for capacities to increase without impacting room functionality\.

We consult with users about writing surfaces when creating new learning spaces, or when replacing boards in existing rooms\. While the majority of classrooms have whiteboards, some users prefer to use chalkboards\.

All new classrooms have clocks installed when they are built as per our Learning Space Design Guidelines\. These clocks are synced across campus using Wi\-Fi to provide the reliability that an institution like UBC needs\. If your classroom doesn’t have a clock, please [contact the Learning Spaces Team](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/contact-us/)\.

We select the most appropriate furniture in consultation with instructors, students, program administrators, and other key classroom stakeholders\. We aim to provide furniture that:

-   offers student choice
-   supports a variety of activities and learning modes
-   meets universal design, health and wellbeing standards
-   is sourced from sustainable and socially responsible vendors

## Audio Visual \(AV\) and technology

Technicians are available for user support, orientation, or advanced system repairs\. Please contact the AV Help Desk at 604\-822\-7956 or [av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:av.helpdesk@ubc.ca)\. In\-person and phone support available during the regular business hours Mon – Fri, 7:45 AM – 4:00 PM

For more support agreement options \(project\-based, short term or long term\) please contact the AV Help Desk for more details\.

For your convenience we have listed below several local AV providers who have worked with UBC in the past, please contact them directly to arrange support for your event\.

**Full\-service AV Providers:**

[Focus AV](https://focus-av.com/)

[Proshow Audio Visual](https://www.proshow.com/audiovisual-contact/)

[Showmax Events](https://showmaxevents.com/)

[SW Audio Visual](https://www.sw-online.com/)

**Livestreaming:**

[Collide Entertainment](https://collideentertainment.com/)

[SLV Live](https://slvlive.ca/)

**Audio Equipment Rentals:** [Long &amp; McQuade](https://www.long-mcquade.com/rentals/main/)

**Computer/Tablet Rentals:** [Vernon Technology](https://www.vernontechnology.com/)

**Interpretation Equipment / Push\-to\-talk Rentals:** [LMB Systems](https://www.lmbsystems.com/)

**2\-Way Radios:** [BC Communications](https://bccommunications.ca/)

When initiating any new project, a thorough needs analysis is completed with users of the space to identify any specific needs\. In addition to specific needs identified by users, the UBC IT Audio Visual design team utilizes a standards\-based approach based on a set of design guidelines\. These standards and guidelines provide benchmarks for technology based on factors such as seat count, viewing distance, sightlines, etc\. Standardized models of equipment such as projectors, document cameras, and touch panels are used for the purpose of creating a common look and feel in all classrooms which fosters familiarity for users moving from one classroom to the next\. It also streamlines support of classroom spaces, which reduces costly downtime and system outages\.

AV systems are typically refreshed every five to seven years in general teaching spaces\. If you would like to suggest an upgrade to one of these rooms before an AV system refresh is scheduled, please contact the AV Help Desk at 604\-822\-7956 or [av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:av.helpdesk@ubc.ca) for assistance\.

UBC IT Audio Visual’s digital signage team offers hardware infrastructure and software solutions for you to quickly and efficiently display multimedia content on flat panel displays in your space\. Even if you don’t own signage, you can still provide content for the UBC digital signage network\. For more information, visit the [UBC IT Audio Visual Digital Signage](https://it.ubc.ca/services/audio-visual-creative-services/digital-signage) webpage\.

Depending on the classroom, power plugs are located in floorboxes, on tabletops, or under the seats\. If you are in a room with floorboxes, please close the box after you are finished so you don’t create a tripping hazard\.

## Accessibility

We are helping to make the campus more accessible by installing automatic door openers \(push\-buttons\) into lecture theatre doors\. Wheelchair lifts have been installed to aid movement in some rooms, and require no keys to operate\. 98% of our classrooms are accessible – the few inaccessible classrooms are in old buildings that do not have an elevator; this will be noted on the individual classroom pages\.

Individual wireless headphone receivers are available upon request in most large classrooms/theatres with 100 or more seats\. Please contact the AV Help Desk at 604\-822\-7956 or [av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:av.helpdesk@ubc.ca) for assistance\.

## Policies

Use the [Find a Classroom](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/find-a-space/) page to check the hours for any particular building\. If you are booking an event that takes place outside of the building hours, the main doors to the building will be unlocked for the time of the event\. If additional doors are required to be unlocked, please contact [acms\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:acms.support@ubc.ca) and detail why this exception is required \(charges for this service will apply\)\. Classrooms on secure access \(electronic doors/card reader\) are programmed to be unlocked from 7:30am to 11pm; all other classrooms are left unlocked and can be used until 11pm\. \(Note the building likely locks before 11pm\)\.

Catering in classrooms **is not permitted\.**

Drinks in spill\-proof containers and snacks are acceptable for students to consume during academic course sections\. It is your responsibility to take your waste to the nearest [Sort It Out](https://sustain.ubc.ca/get-involved/campaigns/sort-it-out) station\. 

In the event of a power outage or other event that affects the learning environment, Scheduling Services will attempt to re\-locate academic course sections\. If a class needs to be temporarily re\-located, we will contact the relevant department and advise them of new location\.

To help maintain a safe and inclusive learning environment, animals are generally not allowed in classrooms\. However, recognized Service Animals and Service Animals in Training are welcome and have the same access rights as their handlers\. No prior approval is needed to bring a Service Animal into a classroom\.

Please note that Emotional Support Animals \(ESAs\) are not considered Service Animals under current legislation and are typically not permitted in classroom settings\. If you have any questions or need further clarification about Service Animals or Emotional Support Animals, please contact the Centre for Accessibility: [students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/centre\-for\-accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility)

Posters are not permitted in any General Teaching Space classroom\. For other spaces \(hallways, etc\), please contact the building administrator for permission to post signage or posters\. For more information, refer to [UBC’s Signage Policy – UP8](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/signage-policy/)\.

## Scheduling and booking

Scheduling Services, Enrolment Services works closely with 160\+ Timetable Representatives in 22 Faculties and Schools, and stewards the production of the UBC Course Schedule\. Further details can be found [here](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/course-scheduling-vancouver) \(CWL Login required\.\) Scheduling Services is the primary contact for administering the bookings of General Teaching Spaces, defined as shared space used for the teaching needs of Faculties, Departments, and Schools\.

Please contact your department’s Timetable Representative or Exam Representative\. They will contact Scheduling Services on your behalf\.

The [Find a Classroom](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/find-a-space/) search on the Learning Spaces website only displays General Teaching Spaces\. The room you are looking for may be a restricted departmental classroom or a teaching lab, both of which are not included in our inventory\. Please contact the department in charge of the building you’re looking for to book the room you need\.

Please visit the Scheduling Services [Room Booking page](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/external-room-bookings) for more information or to submit a booking request\.

Per [Policy 107](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/space-rental-policy/), Scheduling Services establishes rules for General Teaching Spaces for “Academic/Educational Activities” and “Other Activities”\. For full details about booking priorities view our [booking guidelines](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/media-uploads/files/GTS%20Booking%20Guidelines%20w%20Rental%20Rates%20-%20011725.pdf)\.

Please visit the [Enrolment Services website](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/) or email:

-   [schedulingservices@students\.ubc\.ca](mailto:schedulingservices@students.ubc.ca) for Course Scheduling inquires
-   [finalexams\.administrator@ubc\.ca](mailto:finalexams.administrator@ubc.ca) for Final Exams
-   [room\.bookings@ubc\.ca](mailto:room.bookings@ubc.ca) for all Events/Ad\-hoc bookings

## Lost and found / Wayfinding

Please visit [UBC’s Central Lost and Found](https://lostandfound.ubc.ca/) at the UBC Bookstore to check whether your item has been dropped off\. You may also check the [online database](https://lostandfound.ubc.ca/all-items) for information on newly found items\. 

Usually, it is best to leave items of lower value like water bottles and umbrellas where you find them\. Valuable items like phones should be taken to the [Central Lost and Found](https://lostandfound.ubc.ca/)\. 

Google Maps does not always work well with UBC’s building short codes\. As an alternative while on campus, [Wayfinding UBC](https://maps.ubc.ca/) provides accurate wayfinding to UBC buildings and is searchable using building short codes\. To access Wayfinding UBC, please visit [maps\.ubc\.ca](https://maps.ubc.ca/) or through the [UBC Safe app](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/ubc-safe-vancouver-app/) under the “Campus Maps” icon\.
