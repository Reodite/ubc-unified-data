## There are many places to study, teach, or hold an event on campus

Find the classroom that best suits your needs, and then visit the [Enrolment Services website](https://enrolmentservices.ubc.ca/external-room-bookings) to make a room booking request\. To see room schedules and availability, visit the [UBC Online Timetable](https://sws-van.as.it.ubc.ca/) \([CWL](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl) required\)\. Need a room in a particular area? Use the [Wayfinding at UBC website](https://maps.ubc.ca/) to look for buildings on campus\.
