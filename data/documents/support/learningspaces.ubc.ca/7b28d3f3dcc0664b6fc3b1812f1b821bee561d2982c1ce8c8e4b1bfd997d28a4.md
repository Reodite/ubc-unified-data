Room offers power outlets to students\. Percentage of power outlets to seats can range from 50% to 100%\.

Learning spaces specifically designed to support student\-centered, flexible, and interactive learning\. Active Learning Classrooms contain furniture designed for collaboration, and both peer\-to\-peer learning and instructor\-student interaction\. High\-tech Active Learning Classrooms may feature technology that allows both instructors and students to share their work with the class\.

Provides individual wireless headphone receivers for those with hearing disabilities \(available upon request in most theatres/tiered rooms with 100 or more seats\)\.

Provides an intuitive interface in the form of a touchscreen or button panel\. The system allows the user to control all elements of the AV system from a single location, rather than controlling each device independently with multiple remote controls\.

Involves some combination of in\-person and online learning, with the goals of increasing flexibility in terms of when and where students learn, and taking advantage of in\-person and online spaces and learning technologies to enhance student learning\. Three common components of this learning style are online learning materials and activities, in\-person activities in the classroom guided by instructors, and structured independent study for students\.

Plays both Blu\-Ray Hi\-Definition discs and NTSC digital video discs \(DVDs\) \(HDCP compliant\)\.

Writing surfaces available in room can be either whiteboards, chalkboards or glassboards\.

Either Mac or Windows PC computer system available in room\.

 A Mac computer system is available at the instructor station\. Comes installed with Microsoft PowerPoint, Word, and Excel\. Internet access requires a UBC campus\-wide login \(CWL\)\. For more information please visit [http://www\.cwl\.ubc\.ca](http://www.cwl.ubc.ca/)\.

 A Windows PC computer system is available at the instructor station\. Comes installed with Microsoft PowerPoint, Word, and Excel\. Internet access requires a UBC campus\-wide login \(CWL\)\. For more information please visit [http://www\.cwl\.ubc\.ca](http://www.cwl.ubc.ca/)\.
