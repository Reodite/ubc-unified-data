### Accessibility

-   Assistive Listening System \- Advance Bookable
-   Automatic Door Opener
-   Instructor Area/Front of Room Not Wheelchair Accessible
-   Wheelchair Accessible \- Student Seating Area

This is a tiered room with wheelchair access available to the back of the room only\. The front of the room, including the instructor area, is not wheelchair accessible\. The entrance to the back of the room is equipped with an automatic door opener\. 

[Contact the Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility)
