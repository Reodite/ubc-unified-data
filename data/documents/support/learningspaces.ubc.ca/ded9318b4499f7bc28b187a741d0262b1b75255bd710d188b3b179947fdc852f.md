Posted on: October 7, 2025

A number of general teaching spaces will be unavailable for all or part of the 2026 summer session due to various renovation and AV projects\. 

*Last updated: Feb 20, 2026*

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rooms** | **Dates** | **Description** |
| ALRD 112, 113 | April 27 – September 4 | AV system renewal |
| ANGU 037, 039, 334 | April 27 – July 3 | AV system renewal |
| ANGU 335, 343, 345, 347 | July 6 – September 4 | AV system renewal |
| AUDX 142, 157 | April 27 – September 4 | Maintenance |
| BUCH B208, B218, B302, B304, B306, B308, B310, B316, B318, D204, D214, D216, D218, D222, D228, D304, D306, D312, D314, D322 | April 27 – May 8 | Installation of solar window film\. Some classrooms will also receive new furniture\. |
| BUCH D201, D204, D205, D207, D209, D213, D214, D216, D221, D228, D229 | April 27 – June 26 | AV system renewal\. Some classrooms will also receive new furniture\. |
| BUCH D217, D218, D219, D222 | July 6 – August 31 | AV system renewal\. Some classrooms will also receive new furniture\. |
| FRDM 153 | April 27, 2026 – January 3, 2027 | Lecture theatre renovation |
| FSC 1001, 1002, 1003 | April 27 – May 15 | Installation of automatic door openers and access controls |
| MCML 256, 260 | April 27 – September 4 | Renovation \(AV system, ceiling, lights, floor, paint, etc\) |

In addition to the above, all MCML classrooms will be unavailable for the summer due to noise generated from the MacMillan Seismic Upgrade\.

LASR 5C will receive new tables and IONA 633 will receive new chairs over the summer\.
