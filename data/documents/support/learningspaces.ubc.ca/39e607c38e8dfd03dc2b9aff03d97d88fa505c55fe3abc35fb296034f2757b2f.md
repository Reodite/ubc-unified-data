Posted on: January 13, 2025

The newly redeveloped Find a Space tool on the Learning Spaces website is now live and ready for use\!  

## **Improved search** 

This upgraded tool was created using your feedback\. It will provide advanced search options for you to find buildings, classrooms, and other informal spaces related to teaching and learning\.  

[Image](https://vpfo-learningspaces-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/01/Screenshot-new-LS-website-940x457.png)

Pictured: screenshot of the new Find a Space tool on the Learning Spaces, UBC website

**Filters include:** 

-   Capacity 
-   Furniture
-   Layout \(seminar, rows, case\-style, groups\) 
-   Accessibility \(wheelchair accessible, assistive listening, automatic door\) 
-   Building 
-   Audio visual equipment 
-   Other room features \(whiteboard, natural light, AC power to seats\) 

## **Another fun feature that came highly requested—the back button\!** 

That’s right, you will be able to go back to your search after clicking into a classroom page\. No more time wasted redoing your filter or having to open classrooms in a new tab\. 

[Try the new Find a Space tool](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/find-a-space/)
