Bioimaging North America \(BINA\) is open to accepting subscriptions of light microscopy images for the image contest 2023\!  
The top 3 images will receive a monetary prize and be featured in their newsletter and on social media\.  
Annika Lea Schulz \(Jean lab\) was one of the top winners last year\!  
More information can be found in the link below\.  
[https://www\.bioimagingnorthamerica\.org/events/bina\-image\-contest\-2023/](https://www.bioimagingnorthamerica.org/events/bina-image-contest-2023/)
