A scanning electron micrograph of *Leptopilina japonica*, an Asian parasitoid wasp that was found on the UBC Farm\. Image courtesy of Jordan Tam \(Co\-op student\) in Dr\. Julli Carrillo lab \(UBC Applied Biology, Centre for Sustainable Food Systems\)\. They are known to parasitize Spotted Wing Drosophila \(*D\. suzuki*\), a type of fruit fly that is a major pest in North America and Europe\. Hitachi S2600 SEM was used to acquire an image\.
