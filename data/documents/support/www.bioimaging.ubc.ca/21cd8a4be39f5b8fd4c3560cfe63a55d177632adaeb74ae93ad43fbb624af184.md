The Zeiss Crossbeam 350 \(XB350\) has capabilities of Field\-emission SEM, Focused\-ion beam milling, 3D FIB\-SEM, CryoSEM, and CryoFIB\-SEM\. Dr\. Miki Fujita \(BIF Research manager\), Lucas Andrew \(Graduate student in MacLachlan lab, UBC Chemistry\) and Dr\. Sam Livingston \(Post\-doc researcher in Keeling lab, UBC Botany\) gave a talk for the FOSI seminar in February\. If you would like to access the recorded seminar, please contact Miki \(bif\.manager@ubc\.ca\)\.
