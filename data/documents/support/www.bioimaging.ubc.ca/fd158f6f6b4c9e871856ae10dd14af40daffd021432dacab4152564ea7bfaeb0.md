Focused On Scientific Imaging \(FOSI\) seminar is back in February, and will take place on February 21st \(Wednesday\) at 12:30 pm in Michael Smith Laboratory \(MSL\) Auditorium 102 or Zoom \(Hybrid\)\. Prof\. Claire Brown, Director of the Advanced Bioimaging Facility at McGill University, will give a talk titled “The Power of Networks to Accelerate Scientific Discovery”, covering the Canadian BioImaging Networks, Courses, and Funding Opportunities\.

Please see the FOSI website for a Brief Bio\!

[https://fosiseminar\.wixsite\.com/fosiseminarseries/copy\-2\-of\-next\-seminar](https://fosiseminar.wixsite.com/fosiseminarseries/copy-2-of-next-seminar "https://fosiseminar.wixsite.com/fosiseminarseries/copy-2-of-next-seminar")

Before the talk \(12:15\-12:30 pm\), Snacks and Coffee will be provided for those attending in person outside the MSL auditorium\. Please arrive before the seminar starts as food and drinks are not allowed in the auditorium\.

For those who don’t know about the FOSI seminar, the FOSI seminar series was created to provide a space for everyone to exchange ideas about imaging techniques and to connect with the people who use the imaging techniques that you might need for your research\.

We hope that you can join us at this month’s FOSI seminar\! For more information, see our home page at [http://fosiseminar\.wixsite\.com/fosiseminarseries](http://fosiseminar.wixsite.com/fosiseminarseries)\.
