    **Undergraduate Research Assistant**  
      
    Azuki is currently a fourth\-year Biology student at the University of British Columbia and will be joining the team as an Undergraduate Research Assistant\. Azuki is particularly interested in physiology and cell biology and has conducted research on IRE1 yeast mutant cells by testing multiple pathways under different level of endoplasmic stress\. Outside of academics, Azuki enjoys skiing and hiking, as well as discovering new local cafés and restaurants\. She is looking forward to acquiring new skills and hands\-on experience with different microscopes, while exploring the important role of microscopy in scientific research\.
