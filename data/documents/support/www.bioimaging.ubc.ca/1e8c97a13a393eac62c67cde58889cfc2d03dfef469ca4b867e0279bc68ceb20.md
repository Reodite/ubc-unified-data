### Instructions:

1.  Open up the “CyberDuck” Application
2.  Click on “Open Connection”\.
3.  Select “FTP\-SSL \(Explicit AUTH TLS\)”\.
4.  For”Server”: ftp\.bioimaging\.ubc\.ca and your “Username” and “Password”\. Click “Connect”\.
5.  To download, right click on the folder/file in the window, and click “Download”\.

### Warnings:

1.  Users will not be able to upload onto the server\.
2.  Data purging occurs twice a year which will wipe out all user data older than 180 days\. It is important to keep your local files updated\.
