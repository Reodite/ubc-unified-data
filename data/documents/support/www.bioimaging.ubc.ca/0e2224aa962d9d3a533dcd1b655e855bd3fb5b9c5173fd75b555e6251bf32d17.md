## **Capabilities**:

– [Variable pressure SEM imaging on non\-coated samples](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/sem-techniques/)

– Energy Dispersive X\-ray analysis \(EDX\)

## **Cost**:

Academic users: $40/h, Industry users: $95/h

## **Specifications**:

Electron Source: Thermionic tungsten gun

Accelerating voltage: 0\.5 – 30 KV

Magnification: 40 – 300,000x

Variable Pressure: 1 – 270 Pa

Maximum Resolution: 5 nm

Secondary Electrons \(SEs\) and Back\-Scattered Electron \(BSE\) detectors are available
