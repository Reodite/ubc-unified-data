Transmission electron microscopy of *Arabidopsis thaliana* stem cross sections show distorted cell shapes \(left image\) and cytoplasmic inclusions \(right image\) identified in cuticle deficient mutant *abcg11\-3* \(Bird et al\., 2007\. Plant J\. 52 \(3\) 485\-497\)\. Images taken with the Tecnai Spirit 120kV TEM by undergraduate researcher Valerie Pringle in collaboration with PhD student Jessica Hancock, a member of the Samuels lab \(Dept of Botany\)\.
