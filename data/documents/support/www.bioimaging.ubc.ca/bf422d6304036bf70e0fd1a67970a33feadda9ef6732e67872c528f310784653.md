The Bioimaging Facility provides imaging services for industry clients\. We work with industry clients on SEM, TEM, FIB\-SEM, Cryo\-SEM, Cryo\-TEM, 3D electron tomography TEM and optical microscopy projects ranging from biological samples to materials\.

Some previous projects include Cryo\-TEM imaging of liposomes for pharmaceutical companies, SEM and TEM imaging of nanomaterials for Biotech and Biomedical industries, and 3D electron tomography imaging of fuel cell samples for clean energy industries\.

Our staff has extensive EM experience and can assist our clients in acquiring the images they need\. For clients who frequently use our microscopes, our staff will also provide training on the operation of the microscope\.

If you are interested in working with us, please visit UBC Imaging Labs, [www\.imaginglabs\.ubc\.ca](https://imaginglabs.ubc.ca/?login), to learn more about how we work with industry clients or contact our Research Manager, Miki Fujita \(bif\.manager@ubc\.ca\)\.

UBC Imaging Labs is a joint initiative of UBC imaging facilities located at the Vancouver campus\.
