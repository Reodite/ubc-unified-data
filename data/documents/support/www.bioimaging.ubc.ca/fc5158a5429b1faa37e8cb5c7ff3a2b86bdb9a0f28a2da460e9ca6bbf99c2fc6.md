The Characterization@UBC Fall Training School will take place from October 20\-22 at UBC Vancouver \(Fred Kaiser 2020/30\)\. We are incorporating your suggestions from last year into this year’s program to make it more streamlined and structured for our community\!

What’s changed this year?

Dedicated daily themes –

Day 1 – Fundamentals in Characterization Techniques \(e\.g\., S/TEM, optical, X\-ray based, microanalysis\) in 60\-min lecture format;

Day 2 – Analysis &amp; Applications highlighting applied, experimental &amp; advanced methods in characterization;

Day 3 – New and Emerging Characterization Techniques\.

Shorter and engaging talks;

Trainee oral presentations/ short talks;

A pre\-event student mixer/ poster session for peers to set up poster, mingle and network \(tentatively on Oct 19\)\.

If your research involves characterization technique, such as S\(T\)EM, optical, microanalysis, X\-ray based, quantitative, and other imaging methods, consider submitting your abstract for the upcoming Characterization@UBC Fall Training School \(Oct 20\-22\)\! Guidelines and submission can be found in attached flyer\.

[Image](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/2026-Fall-School-Flyer_V.png)Deadline to submit abstract is Sep 8, 2026\.
