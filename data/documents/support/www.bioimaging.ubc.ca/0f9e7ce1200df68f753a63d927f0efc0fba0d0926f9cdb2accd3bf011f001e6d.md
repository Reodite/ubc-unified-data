[https://bioimaging\-2018\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2016/07/Ultracut\-T\.jpg](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/07/Ultracut-T.jpg)[Ultracut T](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/07/Ultracut-T-150x150.jpg)Ultramicrotome for cutting ultra\-thin \(&lt;100nm\) sections of resin embedded samples for transmission electron microscopy\. When couple with the Leica FCS the Ultracut T can cut ultra\-thin cryosections, used mainly for the Tokuyasu technique for immunolabeling\. Is also used to section semi\-thick \(0\.5 – 1um\) sections for light microscopy\.
