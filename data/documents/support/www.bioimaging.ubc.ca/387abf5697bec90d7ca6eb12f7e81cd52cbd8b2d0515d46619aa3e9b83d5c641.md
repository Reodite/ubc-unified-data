A new confocal microscope, Leica Stellaris 8 Dive, has been installed at the BIF\!  
The Leica Stellaris 8 confocal system is capable of multi\-color, 3D imaging, tiling and stitching, multi\-point imaging, Fluorescence Recovery After Photobleaching \(FRAP\) with 4 tunable HyD detectors and 4 solid laser lines \(405, 488, 561 and 638 nm\)\.  
The upright microscope system is equipped with the Spectra Physics Insight X3 dual pulsed\-lasers, allowing us to perform multi\-color multi\-photon imaging, Fluorescence Life Time Imaging Microscopy \(FLIM\), FLIM\-FRET \(forster resonance energy transfer\), Second Harmonic Generation \(SHG\) and Third Harmonic Generation \(THG\) imaging\.

If you want to test this microscope or get training, please submit an enquiry form\.
