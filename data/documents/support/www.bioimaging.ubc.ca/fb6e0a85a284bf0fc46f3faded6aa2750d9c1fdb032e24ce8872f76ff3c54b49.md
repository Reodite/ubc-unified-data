## **Capabilities:**

– A combination of laser scanning confocal microscopy and multiphoton microscopy

– *Upright* Leica DM6 CFI microscope with AFC \(Adaptive Focus Control\) and XY scanning stage

– Confocal Imaging with 4 visible laser lines and 4 tunable HyD detectors \(410\-850nm\)

– Multiphoton \(MP\), Second Harmonic Generation \(SHG\), and Third Harmonic Generation \(THG\) imaging using pulsed invisible dual IR lasers

– Spectra Physics IR laser Insight X3 Dual \(tunable 700\-1300nm &amp; fixed 1045nm beam\), dual beam\-routing with VBE

– MP imaging with 3 spectrally tunable 4Tune NDD channels \(380\-800 nm\) for simultaneous spectral separation of up to 3 fluorescent signals and sequential acquisition of unlimited signals

– Extra\-large field of view, focus adapting tile scanning with Navigator

– Super\-resolution imaging \(120 nm XY, &lt;300nm Z\) using LIGHTNING adaptive deconvolution

– Fluorescence lifetime \(FLIM\) imaging using a pulsed IR laser, with analysis options including average photon arrival time \(TauSense\) and TCSPC\-based FLIM via FALCON

– FALCON for comprehensive FLIM analysis : Exponential fitting, FLIM phasor analysis, pattern fitting and FLIM\-FRET analysis

– Transmitted light detector for bight\-field imaging

– Resonant scanner for high\-speed imaging

– FRAP/photostimulation

– FLIM\-FRET

## **Specifications:**

[Image](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/06/Stellaris8-DIVE-whole-image.png)

#### **Microscope:**

– DM6 CFI *upright* fixed stage microscope

– Conventional fluorescence filters and LED light sources for eyepieces observation \(DAPI, GFP, mCherry\)

– Maerzhaeuser motorized platform; Motorized xy positioning and manual height adjustment to accommodate a variety of specimens

– Exchangeable nosepiece, including a single objective holder and a motorized 6\-position nosepiece

#### **Scanners:**

– FOV scanner \(10 frames/sec at 512 x 512\)

– Resonant Scanner \(8kHz, 28 frames/sec at 512 x512\)

#### **Excitation Lasers:**

***1P visible Lasers for confocal imaging***

405nm \(50mW\) for DAPI  
488nm \(20mW\) for GFP, Alexa488  
561nm \(20mW\) for mCherry, Alexa 546  
638nm \(30mW\) for Cy5, Alexa 633 &amp; 647

***2P invisible pulsed laser for multiphoton, SHG, and FLIM imaging***  
Dual IR laser Insight X3  
Tunable 700\-1300nm  
Fixed 1045nm

#### **Objectives**

***Confocal***  
5x/0\.15 HC PL FLUOTAR, dry, WD 13\.7mm  
10x/0\.40 HC PL APO CS2, dry, WD 2\.56 mm  
20x/0\.75 HC PL APO CS2, dry, WD 0\.62mm  
40x/0\.95 HC PL APO CORR, dry, WD 0\.17 mm  
63x/1\.40 Oil HC PL APO CS2, Oil immersion, WD 0\.14 mm

***Multiphoton***  
16x/0\.6 HC FLUOTAR L 16x/0\.6, IMM CORR VISIR, multi\-immersion, WD 2\.5 mm  
25x/0\.95 Water HC FLUOTAR L 25x/0\.95 VISIR, water immersion, WD 2\.5 mm  
40x/1\.10 Water HC PL IRAPO motCORR \(Motorised correction collar\), water immersion, WD 0\.63 mm

#### **Detectors:**

***Confocal***  
4 of spectral HyD detectors with fully tunable at 1nm resolution  
\(410 – 850nm detection window\)

***Multiphoton, SHG &amp; FLIM***  
3 of 4TUNE tunable NDD HyD\-X detectors  
\(380 – 800nm detection window\)

***Transmitted light detector***

#### **Software:**

LAS X with Navigator, 3D Visualization, Lightning Expert, and FALCON in Windows 11  
Offline Software \(rm0135\) – LAS X Offline with 3D Visualization, 3D Analysis, Lightning, FLIM analysis
