## **Capabilities**:

– [Standard SEM imaging with a field emission gun](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/sem-techniques/) 

## **Cost**:

Standard FESEM &amp;Variable Pressure \(VP\)\-SEM, EDX: Academic users: $66/h, Industry users: $120/h

## **Specifications**:

Electron Source: Schottky field emission gun Accelerating Voltage: 0\.1 – 30 KV

Magnification: 20 – 2,000,000x

Maximum 0\.6 nm resolution at 15 kV, 3\.0 mm Working Distance \(WD\)/ Maximum 0\.8 nm resolution at 1 kV, 1\.5 mm WD

5 to 300 Pa VP pressure range

Specimen stage tilts from \-5° – 360°

InLens detectors: UD \(SE\) and MD \(BSE\) detectors

Inchamber detectors: LD \(SE\+BSE\) and PD\-BSE \(BSE\) detectors

For VP\-mode, UVD\-SE \(SE\) detector

STEM detector

Oxford Energy Dispersive X\-ray Microanalysis system: AZtecLive Advanced Microanalysis System with UltimMax Infinity 100 Analytical Silicon Drift Detector
