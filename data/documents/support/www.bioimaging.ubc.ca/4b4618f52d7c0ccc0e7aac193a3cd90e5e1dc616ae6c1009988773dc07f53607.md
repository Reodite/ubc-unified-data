Epicuticular wax crystals of *Oemleria cerasiformis*, Image courtesy of Chris Cote, MSc student in Rosado lab \(Dept of Botany\)\. Surface view of epicuticular wax structures on the stomatous fruit of *Oemleria cerasiformis*, showing a mixture of tubules \(left\) and platelets \(right\)\. Sample was coated with 2 nm of Au/Pd and imaged using a Zeiss Crossbeam 350 FIB\-SEM at 2500x magnification\.
