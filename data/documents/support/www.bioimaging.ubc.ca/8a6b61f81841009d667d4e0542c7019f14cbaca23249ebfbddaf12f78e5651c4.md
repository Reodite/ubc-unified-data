Zeiss will give us “Lunch &amp; Learn: Zeiss Next Generation Confocal and Live Cell Super Resolution Imaging” on January 24th \(Wednesday\) from 11:30\-1pm in BioSci Rm 3157\.

They will talk about how the Zeiss LSM980 with Airyscan2 &amp; Dynamics Profiler could meet and expand your research needs\.

If you are interested in attending this event, please respond to Eun \([eunkyoung\.lee@botany\.ubc\.ca](mailto:eunkyoung.lee@botany.ubc.ca)\) by Friday, the 19th at 12 pm\.

If you would like to try their microscope, please contact Eun \([eunkyoung\.lee@botany\.ubc\.ca](mailto:eunkyoung.lee@botany.ubc.ca)\) to schedule a demo\. Demo is available till January 31st\.

You can bring your samples for the demo \(live, fixed, biological and materials samples\) to try out their system \(scan up to 10 fluorescent probes at 90 nm resolution, ultrafast imaging, high signal\-to\-noise ratio, reveal molecular dynamics, such as concentration, diffusion and flow in living cells using the new Dynamics Profile, FRAP and more\)\. BIF staff will be there to discuss your experiments and find out your imaging needs\.
