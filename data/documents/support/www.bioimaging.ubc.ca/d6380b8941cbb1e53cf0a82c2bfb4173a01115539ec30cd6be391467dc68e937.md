Focused On Scientific Imaging \(FOSI\) seminar is back for the month of March, and will take place on Monday, March 28th at 1pm in LSC 3 or Zoom \(Hybrid\)\. Guang Gao from LSI imaging centre will be giving a talk titled “Full\-scale imaging techniques at LSI imaging Core Facility: Subcellular structures examined with super\-resolution imaging”\.  
Guang will be speaking about super resolution microscopy technique\.

Prior to the talk \(12:45\-1pm\), Snacks and Coffee will be provided for those attending in person at LSC 3 \(LSC1003\) in Life Sciences Institute Building, main floor\.

To join the FOSI seminar, please click a Zoom link below\.  
https://ubc\.zoom\.us/j/64682599472?pwd\=S2RkME1rZk9GUTdEaSt1aGp5NGNZUT09  
Meeting ID: 646 8259 9472

For those who don’t know the FOSI seminar, FOSI seminar series was created to provide a space for everyone to exchange ideas about imaging techniques and to connect with the people that use the imaging techniques that you might need for your own research\.  
We hope that you can join us at this month’s FOSI seminar\! For more information, see our home page at http://fosiseminar\.wixsite\.com/fosiseminarseries\.

FOSI organizers: Ania Bogoslowski, Evan McKenzie, Sean Ritter
