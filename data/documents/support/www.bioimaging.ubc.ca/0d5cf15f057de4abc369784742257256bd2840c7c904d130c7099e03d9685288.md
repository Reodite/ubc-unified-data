Image editing workshop will be held on July 21st \(Wednesday\) 1\-4:30pm\.

Presenters: EunKyoung Lee &amp; Miki Fujita \(UBC Bioimaging Facility\), Jeffrey LeDue \(UBC NeuroImaging and NeuroComputation Centre\), Keegan Flanagan \(UBC Dynamic Brain Circuits cluster\)

Free/Registration required \(please submit a form in the link below, registration is closed on July 16th, 2021\)

[Registration form](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?p=3112)

Scope: This workshop is suitable for those who are starting to learn digital image editing and processing\. The workshop covers guidelines for the appropriate use and manipulation of scientific digital images, Introduction to ImageJ/Fiji, what is an image, histogram, brightness and contrast adjustment, look up tables\. region of interest\. Macro writing in ImageJ/Fiji\.

Prerequisite: Prior to the workshop, please download and install ImageJ/Fiji\.
