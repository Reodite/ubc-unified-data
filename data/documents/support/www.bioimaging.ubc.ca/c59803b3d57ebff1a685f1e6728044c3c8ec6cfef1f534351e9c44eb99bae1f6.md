FOSI returns for the month of November\! On November 28th at 3:30 pm in the LSI room 1416, Dr\. Kurt Haas from the Djavad Mowafaghian Centre for Brain Health will present a seminar, “Ultra\-fast 3D Two Photon Microscopy \(for in vivo imaging of the whole\-neuron activity in the awake brain\.”

This presentation will feature the AOD\-based two photon microscope designed and constructed by Dr\. Haas’ group, as well as the SLAP2 microscope that will soon be constructed in his lab\.

Hope to see you there for snacks and interesting science\!
