For more information about our confocal microscopes \(Leica Stellaris 8 Multi\-Photon with FALCON or Evident SpinSR Spinning Disk\) or basic light microscopes, please select from the list below\.

- **Row 1**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [**Leica Stellaris 8 DIVE Multiphoton Confocal System**](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?page_id=4730&amp;preview=true)  
      
    \- Confocal Imaging with 4 visible laser lines and 4 tunable HyD detectors \(410 \- 850 nm\)  
    \- Multiphoton , Second Harmonic Generation \(SHG\), and Third Harmonic Generation \(THG\) imaging using pulsed invisible dual IR lasers \(tunable 700 \- 1300 nm &amp; fixed 1045 nm beam\) and 3 spectrally tunable 4Tune NDD channels \(380 \- 800 nm\)  
    \- Super\-resolution imaging \(120 nm XY, &lt; 300 nm Z\) using LIGHTNING adaptive deconvolution  
    \- Fluorescence lifetime microscopy \(FLIM\) imaging, including average photon arrival time \(TauSense\) and Time Correlated Single Photon Counting \(TCSPC\)\-based FLIM via FALCON \(FAst Lifetime CONtrast\)  
    \- Resonant scanner for high\-speed imaging  
    \- Fluorescence recovery after photobleaching \(FRAP\)  
    \- FLIM \- Förster Resonance Energy Transfer \(FRET\)

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [**Evident IXplore SpinSR Spinning Disk Confocal Microscope**](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/equipment/light-microscopes/evident-ixplore-spinsr-spinning-disk-confocal-system/)  
      
    \- High\-resolution and fast spinning disk confocal system for fixed and live sample imaging  
    \- Bright\-field, DIC, wide\-field fluorescence, and confocal imaging  
    \- 5 laser lines and 2 sCMOS cameras \(Hamamatsu Fusion BT\) for simultaneous two\-channel, high\-speed imaging  
    \- Yokogawa CSU\-W1 \(2 discs: 50 μm pinhole disk for high resolution confocal imaging, SoRa disk for super resolution imaging\)  
    \- Z drift correction function \(ZDC, autofocus\) to maintain long\-term time\-lapse focus stability  
    \- High speed FRAP/photostimulation system  
    \- Fully automated objectives, stage, and filter turrets  
    \- High\-speed piezo stage for capturing fast\-moving, high\-resolution images  
    \- Stage\-top live cell incubation system \(30\-40°C, CO2\) for piezo stage  
    \- High\-speed image processing, powered by TruSight deconvolution and OSR \(Olympus Super Resolution\) algorithms, achieving 120 nm XY resolution
