### Instructions:

1.  Open up the “WinSCP” Application
2.  Enter “File protocol”: FTP ; “Encryption”: “TLS/SSL Explicit Encryption”
3.  Enter “Hostname”: ftp\.bioimaging\.ubc\.ca and your “Username” and “Password”\. Click “Login”\.
4.  To download, right click on the folder/file in the right window, and click “Download”\.

### Warnings:

1.  Users will not be able to upload onto the server\.
2.  Data purging occurs twice a year which will wipe out all user data older than 180 days\. It is important to keep your local files updated\.
