## **Capabilities**:

– [Standard SEM imaging with a field emission gun](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/sem-techniques/) 

– [Cryo\-SEM](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/sem-techniques/)

## **Cost**:

Standard SEM: BIF members: $39\.60/h, Academic users: $66/h, Industry users: $120/h

Cryo\-SEM: BIF members: $330/day, Academic users: $550/day, Industry users: $960/day

## **Specifications**:

Electron Source: Cold field emission gun producing high brightness \(2 x 10\[9\] A/cm\[2\]/sr\) with little energy spread \(0\.2 – 0\.3 eV\)

Accelerating Voltage: 0\.5 – 30 KV

Magnification: 30 – 500,000x

Maximum 1\.5 nm resolution at 15 kV, 12 mm Working Distance \(WD\)/ Maximum 2\.5 nm resolution at 1 kV, 2\.5 mm WD

Specimen stage tilts from \-5° – 360°

Two Secondary Electrons \(SEs\) and one Back\-Scattered Electrons \(BSEs\) detectors are available
