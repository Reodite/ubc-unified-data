Ultramicrotomy is a method for cutting specimens into extremely thin slices, called ultra\-thin sections \(usually &gt;100nm thick\), that can be studied and documented in a transmission electron microscope \(TEM\)\. It is used mostly for biological specimens, but sections of plastics and soft metals can also be prepared\.
