## [\(Untitled\)](https://www.bioimaging.ubc.ca/2019/08/29/2561/)

A spheroid of breast cancer cells imaged by Soroush Nasseri \(PhD student\) in Dr\. Karen Cheung lab \(Dept of Electrical and Computer Engineering\) using multi\-photon confocal microscopy \(Olympus FV1000 with Maitai laser\)\. He used cell tracker blue for visualizing all cells and FUCCHI marker proteins for examining cell growth phase\.
