Microscopy Society of Canada is having its 50th anniversary this year\.

As part of this year’s Annual Meeting \(June 4\-7, Ottawa\), the Microscopical Society of Canada invites all undergraduate and graduate students, post\-docs and research technicians across Canada to the microscopy imaging contest\. You could showcase your research and submit your best image based on optical, probe or electron microscopy\!

The submission deadline is May 31st, 2024\.

For more information, please visit the link below\.

[https://event\.fourwaves\.com/msc2024/pages/8dab7e70\-d57e\-4c99\-8ece\-c8de9f40459b](https://event.fourwaves.com/msc2024/pages/8dab7e70-d57e-4c99-8ece-c8de9f40459b)

There is an opening for a BC student chapter for the Microscopy Society of Canada, supporting activities like image competitions and student seminars\. If you are interested, please visit the link below\.

[https://msc\-smc\.org/en/about\-msc\-smc\-student\-chapters\-218\.aspx](https://msc-smc.org/en/about-msc-smc-student-chapters-218.aspx)
