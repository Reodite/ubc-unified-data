Dr\. Eunji Park, a postdoctoral researcher in the Leander lab \(Zoology\), was able to visualize the different local microenvironment within a marine organism using the Leica Stellaris 8 FALCON\. DNA was stained with a fluorescent dye\. The left image shows a fluorescence image based on the intensity; the right image shows a color\-coded fluorescence lifetime imaging of the sample\. Red color shows a short lifetime, blue shows a long lifetime\.
