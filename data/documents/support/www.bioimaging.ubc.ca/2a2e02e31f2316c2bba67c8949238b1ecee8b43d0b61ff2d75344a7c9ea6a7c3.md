Focused On Scientific Imaging \(FOSI\) seminar is back for the month of April, and will take place on April 24th \(Monday\) at 1 pm in Michael Smith Laboratory \(MSL\) Auditorium 102 or Zoom \(Hybrid\)\. Farid Jalali, Senior advanced microscopy systems specialist, will be giving a talk titled “Image processing using deconvolution: a primer on why you should use it”\.

Prior to the talk \(12:45\-1 pm\), Snacks and Coffee will be provided for those attending in person outside the MSL auditorium\. Please arrive before the seminar starts as food and drinks are not allowed in the auditorium\.

To join the FOSI seminar, please click a Zoom link below\.

For those who don’t know the FOSI seminar, FOSI seminar series was created to provide a space for everyone to exchange ideas about imaging techniques and to connect with the people that use the imaging techniques that you might need for your own research\.  
We hope that you can join us at this month’s FOSI seminar\! For more information, see our home page at [http://fosiseminar\.wixsite\.com/fosiseminarseries\.](http://fosiseminar.wixsite.com/fosiseminarseries.)
