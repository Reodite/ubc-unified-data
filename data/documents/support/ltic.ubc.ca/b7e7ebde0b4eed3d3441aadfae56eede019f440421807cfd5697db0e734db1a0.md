UBC’s Centre for Teaching and Learning and the Learning Technology Hub are committed to catalysing and incubating innovations central to pedagogical inquiry\.  The incubator houses development within the Learning Technology team\. The faculty for each project work closely with the team, and guide the strategy of the project by acting as product owners\. 

Read more here: [Learning Tech Innovation Incubator](https://elearning5.sites.olt.ubc.ca/initiatives/learning-technology-incubator/)

The GenAI Sandbox provides a platform for development of tooling designed to investigate and explore teaching and learning with Generative AI\. It has two layers: the web front end that serves as a load balancer, and a model layer that allows client applications to select models\. We devised a component language to help non\-technical faculty express their incubation needs\. 

Read more here: [GenAI Sandbox](https://tlef.ubc.ca/uncategorized/genai-sandbox-architecture/)
