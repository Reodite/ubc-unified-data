## [Recent Zoom upgrade may require re\-adding Zoom links to Canvas courses](https://ltic.ubc.ca/recent-zoom-upgrade-may-require-re-adding-zoom-links-to-canvas/)

On August 19, 2025, 10:30 a\.m\. PST, we upgraded the Zoom integration in Canvas to a new version to improve stability and performance\. The upgrade completed successfully; however, certain Zoom settings were reset in Canvas and may require re\-adding Zoom to areas of your Canvas course\.

[Read more »](https://ltic.ubc.ca/2025/08/25/recent-zoom-upgrade-may-require-re-adding-zoom-links-to-canvas/)

## [Zoom video retention policy postponed and recordings to be stored in Kaltura](https://ltic.ubc.ca/zoom-video-retention-policy-postponed-recordings-to-be-stored-in-kaltura/)

You were previously informed that the new Zoom video retention policy would come into effect on March 31, 2024\. That is no longer the case, as the policy rollout date is being postponed\. Zoom videos that are a year older or more will not be deleted on March 31 and links to Zoom videos will continue to work past that date\.

To ensure a smooth transition, the postponed policy rollout will also happen with a change in how we store Zoom recordings\. Rather than store these videos in the Zoom cloud, videos will instead be stored in Kaltura\. Kaltura is the media platform that is built into Canvas and is also accessible through the UBC Teaching &amp; Learning Media Portal\.

[Read more »](https://ltic.ubc.ca/2024/03/28/zoom-video-retention-policy-postponed-recordings-to-be-stored-in-kaltura/)

## [Zoom and Microsoft Teams video retention policy going into effect soon](https://ltic.ubc.ca/zoom-and-microsoft-teams-video-retention-policy-going-into-effect/)
