# [Interview](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/ltic-stories/#interviews) – “Haocheng describes his summer activities” \(from “LT Incubator Project – CREATE: Generating Interactive Learning”\)

[Haocheng Fan\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/featured-image-interview-watch-haocheng-discusses-the-technical-hurdles-in-making-create-from-tlef-project-create-generating-interactive-learning-ltic.jpg)

LTIC interviewed Haocheng Fan \(a Student Developer from [the Incubator Team at the Learning Technology Innovation Centre](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/learning-technology-incubator/)\) as part of [our “LT Incubator Project – CREATE: Generating Interactive Learning” story](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/lt-incubator-project-create-generating-interactive-learning/)\.

In this clip, Haocheng’s describes some of the activities he worked on in the summer as a student developer on [the CREATE project](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/lt-incubator-project-create-generating-interactive-learning/)\.

## Transcript

“During the summer – under the guidance \[of\] [Richard Tape](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/lt-incubator-project-create-generating-interactive-learning/#meet-the-incubator-team-at-the-learning-technology-innovation-centre), our supervisor – me and [Deepak](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/lt-incubator-project-create-generating-interactive-learning/#meet-the-incubator-team-at-the-learning-technology-innovation-centre), we work\[ed\] on [this project](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/lt-incubator-project-create-generating-interactive-learning/) together\.

“We start\[ed\] \[by\] reading \[the\] project proposal first, and then we draft\[ed\] a PRD and a PSD, which \[are\] the Project Requirement Documen\[t\] and \[the\] Project Specification Documen\[t\]\. These two documents explained what \[…\] \[this\] project\[‘s\] main functionalities \[are\]\. And \[they\] help\[ed\] us understand how this project works and also help\[ed\] [Roger](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/lt-incubator-project-create-generating-interactive-learning/#meet-the-create-team) know that our project aligns with his goal\.

“And then after that \(after we creat\[ed\] the PRD\), we start\[ed\] building up the wireframes, which is the simple UI \(but \[we did\] not focus on UI; we mainly focus\[ed\] on the functionality created by [Figma](https://www.figma.com/)\)\. Then we started coding\. We start\[ed\] from \[the\] front\-end first, and then start\[ed\] \[the\] back\-end\. \[Those\] were our summer activities\.”
