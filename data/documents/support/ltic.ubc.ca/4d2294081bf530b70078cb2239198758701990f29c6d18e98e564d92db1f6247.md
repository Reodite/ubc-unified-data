## Tools for Teaching

### Blogs

A private website for your course\. You can upload your Canvas materials \(except Quizzes or the Gradebook\)\.

**Automatic Student enrolment** **is working**: Automatically refreshes every 6 hours\.

-   [Watch the Blogs workshop recording to learn how to get set up\!](https://learning.media.ubc.ca/media/teaching-with-ubcs-blogs.mp4/0_h0j1b58k)
-   Read the [Teaching With UBC Blogs FAQ](https://piazza.com/class/moxconx6oqc4f6/post/21) on the instructor forum

[Sign up for Blogs](http://blogs.ubc.ca/)

### Moodle

Moodle is a full scale Learning Management System\. It has quizzes, a forum, a grade book, and more\.

**Request your Moodle course now:** Submit a ticket to [lt\.hub@ubc\.ca](mailto:lt.hub@ubc.ca) to request a Moodle course\. You can ask for it to be populated with your Canvas materials\.

[Read “Getting Started with Moodle \(Instructor Guide\)” \(PDF\)](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/getting-started-with-moodle-instructor-guide-pdf/)

[View the workshops \(Moodle\)](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/event-category/learning-technology/)

### &amp; More \(“Tools for Teaching”\)

**[UBC Wiki](https://wiki.ubc.ca/)** lets any user logged in with CWL edit pages

**[LOCR](https://courses.library.ubc.ca/):** Instructors and students can access to LOCR directly\.

[Piazza\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/image-8.png)

### Piazza

Piazza is a discussion forum where you can also host resources and post announcements\.

You provide a sign\-up link and your students self\-enrol\. **Set an access code to give students access without a UBC email address**, and send it to them through the communication tool\.

[Download Piazza Transition Guide \(PDF\) \(365 KB\)](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/Piazza-Guide-Resources-for-moving-from-Canvas.pdf)

[Sign up for Piazza](https://piazza.com/ubc.ca)

[Zoom\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/image-7-940x688.png)

### Zoom

Use Zoom as you always have for delivering online lectures\. There are other features in Zoom that can be used for student communication and sharing, such as Whiteboards\.

## Assessment\-Related Tools

[Gradescope\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/image-12.png)

Free for summer term 1\!

You can grade scanned exams or uploaded PDFs\. Students can still submit assignments directly to the platform\.

Sign in with your existing account \(the same one you used with Canvas\), or sign up for a new account using UBC as the school\.

[Sign up here \(Gradescope\)](https://www.gradescope.ca/)

## Media Tools

[Camtasia\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/image-13.png)

### Camtasia

**Camtasia is a screen recording and video editing software\.** It lets you record from your computer screen and webcam, as well as add audio or other media to create videos such as lectures, presentations, and video assignments\.

[Read More \(Camtasia\)](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/camtasia-guide/)

[Kaltura\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/image-14.png)

### **Kaltura**

Kaltura lets you record and edit media, create shorter clips from existing media, and add interactive video quizzes\.

[Access Kaltura Here](https://learning.media.ubc.ca/)

[H5P\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/image-15-940x940.png)

### **H5P**

Content hosted on the **[UBC Open H5P Hub](https://h5p.open.ubc.ca/)** or created via the **[Lunii desktop app](https://lunii.com/en-ca/application-lunii)** are available\. You will need to move Canvas\-embedded content for it to be visible outside Canvas \(such as on UBC Blogs\)\.

Contact [lt\.hub@ubc\.ca](mailto:lt.hub@ubc.ca) for support with this\.

## Specialized Learning Environments

[GitHub\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/image-16-940x940.png)

### GitHub

**UBC GitHub is a Git repository\-hosting service available for academic use at UBC\.** GitHub is commonly used with Git to manage code or other projects while maintaining a clear record of additions, removals, and edits\.

Students use CWL to join\.

[Go to GitHub](https://github.ubc.ca/login)

[JupyterHub\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/image-17.png)

### JupyterHub

**JupyterHub** is a web\-based, high\-performance computational workspace offering “notebooks” where students, instructors, and researchers can collaborate on shared data and develop related resources, visualizations, and computer\-generated figures\.

[Go to JupyterHub](https://open.jupyter.ubc.ca/)

[Qualtrics\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/image-18-937x960.png)

### Qualtrics

A survey tool that can be used for multiple choice quizzes with scoring, or for assignment submissions\.

You can add authentication at the start of the survey to collect identity, and restrict access to those with a CWL\.

[Get started with Qualtrics](https://ubc.qualtrics.com/)

## Unavailable without the primary LMS

-   **LockDown Browser \(LDB\):** Requires a Canvas Quiz to “lock onto”
-   **Canvas Quizzes:** All native assessments and Question Banks are inaccessible\.
-   **Integrated Publisher Tools**: WileyPlus, Cengage MindTap, Norton Inquizitive, McGraw\-Hill Connect, VHLCentral\.
-   **Kaltura &amp; Panopto \(Embedded\):** While the videos themselves are safe on the servers, the “My Media” and “Course Content” galleries inside Canvas will not load\.
