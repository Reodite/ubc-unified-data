# [Interview](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/ltic-stories/#interviews) –  “Haocheng discusses the technical hurdles in making CREATE” \(from “LT Incubator Project – CREATE: Generating Interactive Learning”\)

[Haocheng Fan\.](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/featured-image-interview-watch-haocheng-discusses-the-technical-hurdles-in-making-create-from-tlef-project-create-generating-interactive-learning-ltic.jpg)

LTIC interviewed Haocheng Fan \(a Student Developer from [the Incubator Team at the Learning Technology Innovation Centre](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/learning-technology-incubator/)\) as part of [our “LT Incubator Project – CREATE: Generating Interactive Learning” story](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/tlef-project-create-generating-interactive-learning/)\.

In this video, Haocheng dives deeper on some of the creative solutions he utilized to solve some of the technical challenges he encountered in [the CREATE project](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/tlef-project-create-generating-interactive-learning/), and talks about the inspiration \(and motivation\) behind CREATE’s “Learning Objectives” feature\.

## Transcript

“During the development of [the \[CREATE project\]](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/tlef-project-create-generating-interactive-learning/), I think one of the most challenging problem\[s\] \[was\] ‘how can I export a quiz into [H5P](https://h5p.org/) forma\[t\]?’ And the process of tackl\[ing\] this question \[was\] that I found \[an\] example online, and I analyzed it backwards\.

“First, I turned the H5P file into a ZIP file, then unzipped \[the\] file\. And in that folder, I \[could\] see the full structure of the H5P format\. It includes many libraries\. And I just cop\[ied\] all these libraries into a single folder\. So, every time that \[I\] export\[ed\] a quiz, I \[would\] include all of these libraries, and I also analyze\[d\] the syntax H5P used\. The structure is basically a [JSON](https://www.json.org/json-en.html) format, and it has some fixed parameters, so I need\[ed\] to learn how it works together\. And that’s one of the problem\[s\] I faced\.

“And for ‘Learning Objective\[s\]’ – this functionality, it comes from the process of my personal learning \(for example, in my previous class, CPSC 310 – \[which\] is teaching software engineer\[ing\]\)\. And when I was learning this course, I would literally download the materials \(like, the slides we have\)\. And, in the slides, you have some summaries\. Basically, they are learning objectives\. And what I did is that I copy\-paste\[d\] all these learning objectives and copy\-paste\[d\] into ChatGPT\. And also the slides\. And I asked ChatGPT to generate questions based on these learning objectives\. And that’s the inspiration \[for the\] ‘Learning Objective\[s\]’ functionality in \[the\] project\.”
