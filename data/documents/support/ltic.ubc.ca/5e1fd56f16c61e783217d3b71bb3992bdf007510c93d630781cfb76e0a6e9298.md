We welcome requests from UBCV or UBCO for learning technology changes\. The kind of changes we may be able to help with include managing a shared license for commonly used technologies, centralizing support for commonly used technologies, enabling additional functionality for UBC\-supported technologies, and investigating new technology ideas that have broad appeal\.
