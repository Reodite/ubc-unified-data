In addition to the tips you can find in the UBC [tool guides](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/guides/) on this website, we also offer resources to help you teach with technology and support your students in their learning\. Key areas for further exploration are listed below\.

## Tips for Teaching with Technology

### Accessibility &amp; Inclusivity Considerations

This resource explains key considerations to help your online teaching—synchronous or asynchronous—be accessible and inclusive\. The information is not meant to be an exhaustive or fixed list of things you must do, but rather provides UBC\-specific guidance to help you design and deliver courses so that all students can engage\.

[View Page – “Accessibility &amp; Inclusivity Considerations”](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/technology-tips/accessibility-and-inclusivity/)

### Email Security

Keeping your email secure is a critical part of protecting both your personal information and the broader campus community\. This resource provides information on important aspects of email security to consider\.

[View Page – “Email Security”](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/technology-tips/email-security/)

### How to Regenerate a Personal API Token in Canvas

Personal API tokens are like passwords\. They grant access to your account and should be treated with the same care\. To keep your integrations secure, it’s best practice to regenerate them periodically\. This guide walks you through how to do that\.

[View Page – “How to Regenerate a Personal API Token in Canvas”](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/technology-tips/how-to-regenerate-a-personal-api-token-in-canvas/)

### Internet Connection Tips

Being prepared to deal with internet connection issues can help alleviate stress during your online exams\. This resource summarizes ways that you and your teaching team can prepare for and manage potential internet connection issues\.

[View Page – “Internet Connection Tips”](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/technology-tips/internet-connection/)

### Online Assessment Tips

Staff from Instructional Support Units across different UBC faculties compiled the common lessons they learned from supporting online exams during the 2020 period of fully remote teaching\. This resource summarizes approaches that can help make any online assessment run more smoothly\.

[View Page – “Online Assessment Tips”](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/technology-tips/online-assessment/)
