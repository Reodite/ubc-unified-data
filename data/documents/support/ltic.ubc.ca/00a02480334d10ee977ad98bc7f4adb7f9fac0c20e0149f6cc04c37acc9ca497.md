The LT Hub suspended the Threadz and Student Timezones tools on May 5, 2026\. After this date, Threadz and Student Timezones were no longer available to use in Canvas\.

## Why are Threadz and Student Timezones being suspended?

As the LT Hub continues to look into ways to support the collection and analysis of learning data for faculty and students, our learning analytics team has developed a new tool, Course Insights, that will replace both Threadz and Student Timezones\.

Our team will focus on deploying Course Insights this summer\.

## Where can I get more support with transitioning?

For questions or requests for help in transitioning from Threadz and Student Timezones, including requests to extract data from these tools, [contact us](https://lthub.ubc.ca/support/lt-hub/#contact-info) at the LT Hub
