LTIC is beta\-testing a locally running instance of [Open WebUI](https://openwebui.com/) for faculty to use in any academic work, privately and securely

[Image](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/opebwebui-940x666.jpg)

Our UBC AI Platform offers many of the same features as commercial tools, such as GPT creation, thematic analysis, and agentic applications, but runs completely locally at UBC\. You can safely workshop learning materials, course design, or upload qualitative or quantitative data for assistance with analysis and data visualisation\.

[Limited spots in the pilot, so sign up now\!](http://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/ai-signup)

If you’re looking for a secure student\-facing AI tool, check out the [Web Chatbot Plugin](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/web-chatbot-plugin/) pilot\.
