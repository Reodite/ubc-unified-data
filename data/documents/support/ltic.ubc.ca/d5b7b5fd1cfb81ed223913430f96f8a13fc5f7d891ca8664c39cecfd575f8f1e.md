**The UBC Exam Card Scanner is a tool for supporting in\-person exams\.** The mobile application allows teaching teams to move around an exam room to check in students using their UBCcards and flag any student whose identity needs extra visual confirmation\. The interface in Canvas allows you to oversee and manage this check\-in process\.

## What can I use it for?

You can use UBC Exam Card Scanner for supporting in\-person exams:

-   Decreasing the time and paper it takes to check in students for an exam
-   Increasing the focus teaching teams can give to invigilating and answering student questions during an exam
-   Improving collaboration and accuracy around verifying student identities during an exam
-   Creating a clear record of exam attendance that is tied to a Canvas course

### A note about UBC Exam Card Scanner

**Currently, the tool is only available for Faculty of Science courses for BETA testing\.** It will be made available to the broader UBC community following the testing phase\. An LTIC announcement will follow\.

## What do I need to use UBC Exam Card Scanner?

### Mobile devices for your teaching assistants to use

The mobile application supports iOS \(iPhones running iOS 16\+ or iPads running iPadOS 16\+\) and Android \(any mobile device running Android 15\+\)\.

### The UBC Exam Card Scanner mobile application

Once UBC Exam Card Scanner is added to your course, teaching assistants will be given instructions how to install the mobile applications on their devices\.

### Non\-expired UBCcards from students

To scan UBCcards using the mobile application, the UBCcards must be less than five years old\.

### A supported web browser

The UBC Exam Card Scanner instructor interface works in Canvas\. Canvas runs in your web browser and supports using Chrome, Edge, and Firefox\. To learn more about the browser versions that work with Canvas, check the [Canvas supported browser page](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Basics-Guide/What-are-the-browser-and-computer-requirements-for-Instructure/ta-p/66)\. Canvas will not work with Internet Explorer\.

### Tips

-   **Fast and stable Internet connection is necessary for optimal functioning of the app\.** If the app loses connection and/or closes, you can log back in and continue scanning once Internet connection is reestablished\. No progress is lost\.

## How do I use UBC Exam Card Scanner?

You will first need to contact us in the LT Hub to set up the UBC Exam Card Scanner in your Canvas course and receive instructions for installing the mobile application\.

Click any bar below for instructions and tips for using UBC Exam Card Scanner\.

### Request access to UBC Exam Card Scanner

1.  Access our LT Hub contact form\.
2.  Under “Support Requested”, select Other from the drop\-down\. Type in “UBC Exam Card Scanner”\.
3.  In the “How can we help you?” section, list the following details:
    -   the name of your course
    -   UBC email IDs of your Teaching Assistants
    -   Whether you and your TAs have Android or iOS devices, or both
4.  Fill out the rest of the required fields and click Submit\.
5.  An LT Hub team member will reach out to you and your teaching team with instructions about UBC Exam Card Scanner setup and use\.

### Installing UBC Exam Card Scanner: Android

You will first need to change a setting on your Android device to allow for installing applications that do not come from the official app store\. To do this, navigate to “Settings” on your phone\.

1.  Search for “unknown” and select **Install unknown apps** from the list of results\.
    -   If you see multiple options for this, that is okay\. You can choose any one to access the setting\.
2.  Turn on the **My Files toggle** \(so it turns blue\)\.
3.  If you haven’t done so already, email the LT Hub as per the “Request access to UBC Exam Card Scanner” section\.
4.  On your phone, download the zipped folder from the link provided by the LT Hub\.
    -   If prompted, log in with your UBC CWL to access the folder\.
5.  Navigate to “My Files” on your phone, select the downloaded zipped folder, and click **Extract**\.
6.  In the pop\-up, click **Extract** again and a new unzipped folder will be added to your files\.
7.  Select the unzipped folder, select the \.apk file inside, and click **Install**\.
8.  When installation completes, click **Open** to launch the application\.
    -   Once installed, UBC Exam Card Scanner can be closed and re\-opened from your home screen\.
9.  Before scanning UBCcards in the UBC Exam Card Scanner, you will need to scan a QR code that will be provided for the exam\.
    -   This QR code can also be accessed in Canvas, once you see that the LT Hub has added “Exam Card Scanner” to the Course Navigation\.

#### Note

-   **Once the BETA testing phase has concluded, you will be able to install the UBC Exam Card Scanner app from your device’s app store without the additional pre\-installation steps\.**

### Installing UBC Exam Card Scanner: iOS

Install the TestFlight application\. Developed by Apple, TestFlight lets you use and provide feedback on beta versions of applications that are not yet ready for release in the Apple App Store\.

1.  Request UBC Exam Card Scanner as outlined in the “Request access to UBC Exam Card Scanner” section\.
2.  On the iPhone or iPad that you will use during the exam, open a browser and go to the UBC Exam Card Scanner Join Page link provided by the LT Hub\.
3.  Under “Step 1”, click **View in App Store**\.
4.  When the App Store opens, click **Get**, then click **Install** when it appears\.
    -   If prompted, sign in to your Apple account\.
5.  When installation completes, click **Open** to launch TestFlight\.
    -   If prompted, enter your Apple account password again\.
6.  Click **Continue** and agree to the terms and conditions\. TestFlight will then be ready to install the UBC Exam Card Scanner app\.
    -   You do not need to click the “Redeem” button; instead, continue to the next set of instructions when ready\.

Now, you will be able to install UBC Exam Card Scanner\.

1.  Using the iPhone or iPad where you installed TestFlight, open a browser and go to the UBC Exam Card Scanner Join Page link again\.
2.  Under “Step 2”, click **View in TestFlight** to access the installation in TestFlight\.
3.  Click **Install** in the pop\-up for UBC Exam Card Scanner\.
4.  When installation completes, click **Open** to launch the application\.
    -   Once installed, UBC Exam Card Scanner can be closed and re\-opened from your home screen or from the “Currently Testing” apps list in TestFlight\.
5.  In UBC Exam Card Scanner, click **Next**, then click **Start Testing**\. Before scanning UBCcards, you will need a QR code that will be provided for the exam\.
    -   This QR code can also be accessed in Canvas, once you see that the LT Hub has added “Exam Card Scanner” to the Course Navigation\.

#### Note

-   **Once the BETA testing phase has concluded, you will be able to install the UBC Exam Card Scanner app from the App Store without the additional pre\-installation steps\.**

## UBC Exam Card Scanner FAQ

Find UBC\-specific answers to frequently asked questions by clicking any bar below\.

### Does the app store information about students’ UBCcards on the TA’s device?

No, the app does not store any information about the student cards that it scans\. When a UBC student card is scanned, the app securely pulls a photo of the student from the central UBC student card database, that the TA can use to verify the student’s identity\. Once the student is marked as present or flagged for review, this information is recorded in the Canvas course; the TA’s device does not retain any details about the student\.

### Does the app verify the student’s identity automatically when the UBCcard is scanned?

No, the app does not automatically verify a student’s identity\. The teaching team determines whether to mark a student’s presence or flag an issue, as per the [general UBC exam invigilation guidelines](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/enrolment-services/scheduling-records-systems-management/scheduling-services/exam-scheduling/exam-invigilation/exam-invigilation-guidelines)\.
