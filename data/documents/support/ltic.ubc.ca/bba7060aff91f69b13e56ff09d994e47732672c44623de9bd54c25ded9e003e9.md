## Faculty projects we made happen\!

### LT Incubator Projects

[The Learning Technology \(LT\) Incubator](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/learning-technology-incubator/) supports funded UBC learning technology projects by housing their software development within the LT Hub\. The instructor\(s\) for each project work closely with the LT Incubator development team, and guide the strategy of the project by acting as product owners\.

The following Stories highlight some of the [LT Incubator projects](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/enabling-ai-in-inquiry/#selected-incubator-projects) we’ve helped make happen for faculty:

-   [https://ltic\.ubc\.ca/lt\-incubator\-project\-create\-generating\-interactive\-learning/](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-incubator-project-create-generating-interactive-learning/)[CREATE: Generating Interactive Learning](https://ltic.ubc.ca/files/2025/12/featured-image-create.jpg)

    The LT Incubator has been busy\! Take a peak into the development of CREATE – a GenAI tool for generating interactive elements for Canvas \(created by researchers at UBC in partnership with LTIC\)\.
    
    [Read More &gt;&gt;](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-incubator-project-create-generating-interactive-learning/)
    
-   [https://ltic\.ubc\.ca/lt\-incubator\-project\-biocbot\-a\-genai\-study\-buddy\-tool/](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-incubator-project-biocbot-a-genai-study-buddy-tool/)[BiocBot: a GenAI Study\-Buddy Tool](https://ltic.ubc.ca/files/2025/10/BiocBot-2.png)

### Learning Analytics Faculty Partnership Projects

[The Learning Analytics Faculty Partnership Program](https://lthub.ubc.ca/support/learning-analytics/la-faculty-partnership-program/) is intended to catalyze evidence\-based teaching and learning inquiry through learning analytics data\.

The following Stories highlight some of the Learning Analytics Faculty Partnership projects we’ve helped make happen for faculty:

-   [https://ltic\.ubc\.ca/lafpp\-project\-student\-learning\-and\-the\-use\-of\-lecture\-recordings/](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lafpp-project-student-learning-and-the-use-of-lecture-recordings/)[Student Learning and the Use of Lecture Recordings](https://ltic.ubc.ca/files/2025/12/jonathan-graves.jpg)
    
    #### [Student Learning and the Use of Lecture Recordings](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lafpp-project-student-learning-and-the-use-of-lecture-recordings/)
    
    Lecture recordings are a common resource in university courses today\. But what do we actually know about how lecture recordings impact student learning? UBC’s Vancouver School of Economics’ Jonathan Graves is investigating just that, as part of the Learning Analytics Faculty Partnership Program\.
    
    [Read More &gt;&gt;](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lafpp-project-student-learning-and-the-use-of-lecture-recordings/)
