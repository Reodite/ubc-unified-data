LTIC is developing an AI\-powered chat assistant for your UBC WordPress course website or blog

[A screenshot of a web page with an example course syllabus on one half and in the other half is a chat interface allowing students to chat with the syllabus](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/wp-rag-chat-syllabus-demo-940x665.png)

This Chatbot Plugin easily attaches to any UBC WordPress site, and runs entirely on UBC infrastructure – student interactions and web content never leave UBC’s servers\. Student access to participating sites are private and CWL\-protected\. If you don’t have a WordPress site yet, we can help get you set up, including in\-person support and self\-serve guides\. Just let us know and we’ll point you in the right direction\!

[Limited spots in the pilot, so sign up now\!](http://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/ai-signup)

## Answer student questions about your course materials

Students can ask questions directly about the content on your WordPress course site – readings, lecture notes, slides – and get answers drawn from your materials, any time of day\.

## Guide your students toward key topics about your course materials

Suggest questions to guide students focus on what matters most, or to spark engagement with tricky concepts\.

## Customize by page 

Set up a different chat assistant for each page of your site – so students get responses tailored to exactly what they’re reading\.

If you’re looking for a private, secure AI tool to support your academic work, check out the [UBC\-hosted AI Platform pilot](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?page_id=8484&amp;preview=1&amp;_ppp=2a2012932e)\.
