UBC faculty members are invited to join us on Tuesday, December 3 for an introduction to learning analytics\. This hands\-on workshop will provide an overview of the emerging field of learning analytics and introduce learning analytics dashboards and tools for instructors that are designed to help you improve teaching and learning activities in your courses\.

During this workshop, you’ll learn how to use [**Canvas Analytics**](https://learninganalytics.ubc.ca/get-started/access-canvas-analytics-quiz-statistics/) to observe, analyze, and reflect on activity in your courses\. This workshop will also provide an overview of current learning analytics research and of UBC’s [**learning analytics pilot projects**](https://learninganalytics.ubc.ca/about-the-project/tool-pilots/)\.

Come learn more about how learning analytics can support you in improving teaching, learning, and engagement in your courses\.

**[Sign up for this workshop](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/introduction-to-learning-analytics-for-instructors-2/)\.**

**Tuesday, December 3 \| 10:00 – 11:30 a\.m\. \| IKBLC Seminar Room 2\.22 \|  UBC Point Grey Campus**
