  - **Cell 2:**
  
     [ComPair logo](https://ltic-sandbox.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/icon-compair.png)
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    **ComPAIR** is UBC’s homegrown peer review tool that adds comparing to the peer review process\. In ComPAIR, students see pairs of their peers’ assignments presented side\-by\-side for criteria\-guided comparison and feedback\. Students learn by compring and identifying strengths or weaknesses that may not be as evident in an isolated assignment\. The process encourages productive peer feedback and critical thinking\.
  
  - **Cell 4:**
