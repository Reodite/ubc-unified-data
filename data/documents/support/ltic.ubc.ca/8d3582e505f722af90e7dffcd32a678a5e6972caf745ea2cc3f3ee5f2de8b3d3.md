## What Is Learning Data?

Learning data is any data that is created or collected during the running of a class\. This includes:

-   student assessment submissions
-   grades and feedback
-   discussion posts
-   responses to in\-course surveys
-   logs of student activity in learning tools

A Data Access Request \(DAR\) is required for any access to data for purposes other than Teaching and Quality Assurance related to one’s own course or teaching practice, including use for a UBC Administrative Function, UBC Project, or UBC Initiative\.

## Instructor Use of Learning Data

Instructors can use and access their own learning data for reflection on their own teaching practice, or for enhancement of the pedagogy of their courses\.

**Learning data includes high\-risk student data and student intellectual property\.** These data must not be submitted to any AI tool or application that has not been assessed through a UBC Privacy Impact Assessment \(PIA\)\.

For help accessing your learning data, or determining allowable tools for use in analysis, email us at [lt\.hub@ubc\.ca](mailto:lt.hub@ubc.ca)\.

## Research Use of Learning Data

Instructors who intend to use their learning data for research must first obtain *conditional approval* of this use through the [Behavioural Ethics Review Board \(BREB\)](https://ethics.research.ubc.ca/about-human-research-ethics/ethics-boards), and then submit a [Data Access Request](https://cio.ubc.ca/data-governance/data-governance-services/access-ubc-data)\.

[Read More – “Research Use of Learning Data”](https://researchethics.ubc.ca/about-behavioural-research)

## Requesting Additional Data

Instructors who wish to access data beyond their own courses, including data from Workday or data from a course belonging to another instructor, must submit a [Data Access Request](https://cio.ubc.ca/data-governance/data-governance-services/access-ubc-data)\.

[Read More – “Requesting Additional Data”](https://cio.ubc.ca/data-governance/data-governance-services/access-ubc-data)
