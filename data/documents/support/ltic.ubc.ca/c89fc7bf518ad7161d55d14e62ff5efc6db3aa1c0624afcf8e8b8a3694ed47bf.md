## Virtual Drop\-In Support

We offer virtual drop\-in support during our regular hours \(excluding UBC statutory holidays\)\.

### Monday – Friday \| 9:00 a\.m\. – 4:30 p\.m\.

[Enter the virtual LT Hub room](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/61010970208?pwd=QVVRNEtDNWwxcUFFblg1UlJDUGs3UT09)

### What You Can Expect

When you access virtual drop\-in support during our regular hours, you will be taken to a Zoom waiting room\. Our LT Hub support staff will be notified of your arrival\. As soon as a staff member is available to assist you, you will join the virtual room for live support\.

#### Alternative support options

You can also receive our support in other ways:

-   Email [LT\.hub@ubc\.ca](mailto:LT.hub@ubc.ca) or [use our request form](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/support/lt-hub/contact/)\.
-   Call 604 827 4775\.
-   Read our [tool guides](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/guides) to find instructions and tips for using specific learning technology in your course\.
-   Use our [tool finder](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/tool-finder) to find the learning technology best suited to your course and teaching goals\.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I copy my Canvas course?

If you will be teaching the same course in a future semester, you can [copy existing course content into a new course\.](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Instructor-Guide/How-do-I-copy-content-from-another-Canvas-course-using-the/ta-p/1012) To ensure that the links in your course are up to date, you can use the [Canvas link validation tool](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Instructor-Guide/How-do-I-validate-links-in-a-course/ta-p/1001) and modify links as necessary\.

### How do I add additional people into my Canvas course?

If you wish to add another person in a traditional role \(e\.g\., as an Instructor, TA, or Student\) or if anyone on your teaching team does not have access to a course when they should, please contact your faculty or department directly, or reach out to your faculty’s [Instructional Support Unit](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/support/instructional-units/)\.

If you wish to add other people in support roles \(e\.g\., as an Observer, Designer, or Test Student\) or modify their Canvas access/permissions, then you can do so by [contacting us](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/support/lt-hub/)\. Please be sure to describe what you need the additional people to do, so that the appropriate role can be assigned in a timely manner\.

### How do I use Zoom in Canvas?

1.  You will first need to request a UBC Zoom account by contacting the appropriate helpdesk:
    -   UBC Vancouver outside the Faculty of Medicine: Email the UBC IT Audio/Visual \(A/V\) Helpdesk at [av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:av.helpdesk@ubc.ca) with your UBC email and your faculty, school, or department\.
    -   UBC Vancouver Faculty of Medicine: [Complete the UBC Zoom account request form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_eLLYLgIgXph7MQl)\.
    -   UBC Okanagan: [Complete the general IT service request form](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=17a3df611b9129105a0999b4bd4bcbdb), selecting “UBC – Okanagan” as the campus, and writing “UBC Zoom Account Request” in the description\.
2.  You will then need to enable Zoom in your Canvas course:
    -   Log in to your Canvas course with your UBC CWL and click **Settings** in the Course Navigation\.
    -   Click the **Navigation** tab\.
    -   Find the Zoom menu item, click the **options menu** \(the 3 vertical dots\), and choose **Enable**\.
    -   Click **Save**\.
3.  The Zoom menu item will now be available for you and your students\. You can click **Zoom** where it appears in the Course Navigation to open the Zoom application in Canvas\.

### Why can’t students access my Canvas course?

Please note that Canvas courses may not appear for students until the first day of classes, and access will typically end eight weeks after the last day of class\.

If classes have started and a student still does not have access to your Canvas course, check that you have followed the [steps to set up and publish your Canvas course](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/guides/canvas-instructor-guide/#set-up-course)\. Unpublished courses will not be visible to students in Canvas\.

You can also make sure the student is viewing all courses by having the student log in to Canvas, click **Courses** in the Global navigation, then click **All Courses** at the top\.

Students continuing to have trouble may need to confirm that they are registered in the course as expected by logging in to Workday and contacting Enrolment Services to help resolve any registration issues\.

### Why can’t students access my iClicker Cloud course?

Several issues can prevent students from accessing an iClicker Cloud course, including an incorrect student number, duplicate iClicker accounts, or a mismatched email address in Canvas\.

You can refer students to UBC’s iClicker Cloud student guide FAQ for [ways students can troubleshoot course access issues with iClicker Cloud](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/guides/iclicker-cloud-student-guide/#course-access)\.

### How do I get an iPeer account?

For instructors, you can request an iPeer account from the LT Hub:

1.  Access our [LT Hub contact form](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/support/lt-hub/contact/)\.
2.  Under “Support Requested”, select **iPeer** from the drop\-down\.
3.  Fill out the rest of the required fields and click **Submit**\.
4.  Once your iPeer account is created, you will receive a notification from us through email\. Then you can [log in to iPeer](http://ipeer.elearning.ubc.ca/) to begin setting up your course\.

For students and other teaching staff \(e\.g\. TAs\), iPeer accounts will automatically be created when the instructor creates an iPeer course\.
