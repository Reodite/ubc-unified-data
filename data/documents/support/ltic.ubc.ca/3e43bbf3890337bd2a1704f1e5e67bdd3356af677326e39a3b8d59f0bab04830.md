## [Studiosity 2024/25 W2 pilot concluded](https://ltic.ubc.ca/studiosity-pilot-concluded/)

During 2024/25 W2 term, the LT Hub piloted Studiosity, a study tool that students can use to improve their writing with the help of AI\. Now that the pilot has concluded, Studiosity is no longer available to use at UBC\. The LT Hub has elected to continue exploring the AI space rather than commit to a single tool at this time\.

[Read more »](https://ltic.ubc.ca/2025/08/20/studiosity-pilot-concluded/)

## [New AI\-driven tool for improving student writing available to pilot in 2024/25 W2](https://ltic.ubc.ca/new-ai-driven-tool-for-improving-student-writing-available-to-pilot/)

We are seeking participants to pilot Studiosity, a study tool that students can use to improve their writing with the help of AI\.

The pilot will run in the 2024/25 W2 term, beginning roughly the end of January and concluding at the end of May 2025\. During the pilot period, you can request Studiosity student accounts for yourself or for TAs and students in your courses\.

## [Annotation tool to receive support for use outside of Canvas](https://ltic.ubc.ca/annotation-tool-to-receive-support-outside-canvas/)

During the past year, the LT Hub has been piloting the annotation tool Hypothesis\. Hypothesis lets teaching teams and students annotate and tag online readings, for their own use or to share their notes with each other\. Now that the pilot has concluded, LT Hub Leadership has decided to centrally support use of Hypothesis—outside of Canvas\.

[Read more »](https://ltic.ubc.ca/2023/12/14/annotation-tool-to-receive-support-outside-canvas)

## [Pause on central support for Ally starting in 2024](https://ltic.ubc.ca/pause-on-central-support-for-ally/)

Ally is a content\-checking tool that works in Canvas to help teaching teams improve the accessibility of course content\.
