The Kaltura LTI integration in Canvas will be upgraded to use LTI 1\.3\. Originally, we had planned for this to happen on January 19, 2026, however, this upgrade is now delayed until the 2026 Summer Term \(May to August 2026\)\.n

## What do you need to know about this change?

nNo downtime is expected, but you may notice a brief “blip” during the transition\.  nnAfter the upgrade, the location of My Media will move from the course navigation menu to the Canvas global navigation menu\. nnAdditionally, you will need to re\-authorize Kaltura when you first access My Media or the Media Gallery\. This is a legitimate one\-time request that will need to be accepted to continue using Kaltura with Canvas\.  nnThank you for your patience as we improve your experience\! n

## Where can I get further help?

Please [contact us](https://ltic.ubc.ca/support/lt-hub/#contact-info) at the LT Hub if you have questions about this change\.
