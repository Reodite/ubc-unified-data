This process guides the evaluation and intake of ideas from faculty, staff, and students\.

Last Updated:

August 20, 2026

## Phase 1: Initial Submission \(Day 0\)

**An idea is submitted via [intake form](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/i-have-an-idea/) on LTIC website form with basic information:**

-   Name, association with university
-   Brief description of the idea \(2\-3 sentences\)
-   Is this for an existing tool or new tool creation?
-   Do you have funding? \(Yes/No\)
-   Contact details

**Automatic response sent informing user of timeline for next steps**\.

## Phase 2: Initial Review &amp; Triage \(Days 1\-3\)

**Team conducts initial assessment:**

-   Is the idea technically feasible with available resources?
-   Does it align with university mission/priorities?
-   Is there potential for broad impact?
-   Are there obvious red flags or conflicts?

**Outcomes:**

-   **Clear rejection:** Move to rejection process
-   **Needs clarification:** Move to Phase 3
-   **Promising:** Move to Phase 3

## Phase 3: Detailed Discovery \(Days 4\-10\)

**Schedule [brief meeting/call with requestor](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/new-application-discovery-meeting/) to gather:**

### Project Scope &amp; Requirements

-   Who are the intended users? \(How many? Which groups?\)
-   What specific problem does this solve?
-   What would success look like?
-   Are there existing alternatives they’ve considered?
-   Any technical requirements or constraints?

### Timeline &amp; Resources

-   Is there a preferred or required timeline?
-   Level of involvement expected from requestor during development
-   Ongoing maintenance expectations

### Compliance &amp; Approval Check

-   Does this involve student data, grades, or personal information?
-   Are there accessibility requirements?
-   Any other university approvals needed?
-   Integration with existing university systems?

### Budget &amp; Funding

-   If funded: What’s the budget range? Funding source/timeline?
-   If unfunded: Would they be open to seeking funding if we recommended sources?
-   There is a possibility that LTIC can *help* fund the initial proof\-of\-concept/small trial stage of your app, such that, if successful, you can more easily secure funding\. Details on request\.
-   For project requirements that reach up to $10,000, with verified department matching indication, LTIC requires a minimum of 2 months notice\.
-   For projects requirements that exceed $10,000, again with verified department matching indication, LTIC requires at least 6 months notice\.

## Phase 4: Internal Assessment \(Days 11\-13\)

**Team evaluates and documents:**

### Feasibility Analysis

-   Development time estimate \(hours/weeks\)
-   Required skills/resources
-   Technical complexity rating \(Simple/Medium/Complex\)
-   Cost estimate \(if no funding provided\)

### Impact Assessment

-   Number of potential users
-   Alignment with university strategic priorities
-   Potential for reuse/scaling
-   Educational/operational value

### Resource Planning

-   Can we do this with current team?
-   Would we need to hire? And who? FTE/Students?
-   Timeline realistic with other commitments?
-   Ongoing support requirements

### Recommendation

-   **Accept:** Move forward with project
-   **Accept with modifications:** Suggest alternative scope/approach
-   **Defer:** Good idea but not feasible with current resources
-   **Redirect**: If an alternative pathway at the university makes more sense
-   **Reject:** Not aligned with mission/capacity

## Phase 5: Decision &amp; Communication \(Day 14\)

**Academic Director reviews recommendation and makes final decision**

**Response to requestor includes:**

-   Decision \(Accept/Accept with modifications/Defer/Redirect/Reject\)
-   Brief rationale
-   If accepted: Next steps and timeline
-   If modified: Explanation of suggested changes
-   If deferred: Potential future timing or funding suggestions
-   If redirected: Soft hand\-off to the redirection target
-   If rejected: Reference to rejection process for detailed feedback

## Process Notes

### Quality Control

-   Track response times and adjust process if missing targets
-   Monthly team review of accepted/rejected projects and outcomes
-   Quarterly process review with Academic Director

### Handoff Points

-   **Accepted projects:** Transition to project management process
-   **Rejected projects:** Transition to rejection communication process
-   **Funding needs:** Connect with university funding resource contacts

-   Response time \(target: within 2 weeks\)

-   Quality of information gathered for decision\-making
