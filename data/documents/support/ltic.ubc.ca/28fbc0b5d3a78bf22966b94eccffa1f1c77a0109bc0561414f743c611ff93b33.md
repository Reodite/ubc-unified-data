## Transcript

“The [BiocBot](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/tlef-project-biocbot-a-genai-study-buddy-tool/) is not just an AI tutor\. It’s not just telling you something – which is how we usually use GenAI\. What we want to do is create a system that encourages learning\-by\-teaching for students who are unable to participate in the in\-person study\-buddy program otherwise\. Everyone, despite barriers, wants to do better in their studies – so we are enabling them to do so with the BiocBot\.”
