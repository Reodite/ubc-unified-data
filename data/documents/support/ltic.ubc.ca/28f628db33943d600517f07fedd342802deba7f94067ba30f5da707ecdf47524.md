Keeping your email secure is a critical part of protecting both your personal information and the broader campus community\. This resource provides information on important aspects of email security to consider\.

-   **Use MFA:** Always use multi\-factor authentication for institutional logins\.
-   **Spot Spear\-Phishing:** Be skeptical of unexpected emails that reference your specific course codes or ask you to click links to “verify grades”\.
-   **Check Email Rules:** Review your inbox settings at the start of each term to ensure no unauthorized forwarding rules have been created\.
-   **Report, Don’t Delete:** Forward suspicious messages to [security@ubc\.ca](mailto:security@ubc.ca) so they can block the sender and protect the rest of the campus community\.

For more information on keeping your email secure, you can visit:

-   [Identifying &amp; Reporting Suspicious Emails](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/news/identifying-reporting-suspicious-emails)
-   [Tips for Faculty &amp; Staff: Wherever You Work](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/resources/wherever-you-work)
