UBC is an Institutional Member of the [Society for Learning Analytics Research](https://solaresearch.org/) \(SoLAR\)\. Members of the UBC Community can join SoLAR free of charge\.

## About SoLAR

[Image](https://elearning5.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/05/SoLAR_InstitutionalMember-e1525211833208.png)

SoLAR is an inter\-disciplinary network of leading international researchers who are exploring the role and impact of analytics on teaching, learning, training, and development\. SoLAR’s work includes supporting collaborative and open research around learning analytics and promoting the publication and dissemination of learning analytics research\.

## SoLAR Membership

Because UBC is an Institutional Member of SoLAR, all current students, instructors, and staff are eligible for a SoLAR membership, free of charge\. Membership provides benefits such as discounted rates for the [Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference](https://solaresearch.org/events/lak/), the opportunity to attend the [Learning Analytics Summer Institute](https://solaresearch.org/events/lasi/), and the opportunity to nominate for and serve on SoLAR’s Executive Committee\.

To join SoLAR, please [contact the Learning Technology Hub](mailto:lt.hub@ubc.ca) and request UBC’s SoLAR membership access code\.
