## About LT Hub

UBC’s Learning Technology \(LT\) Hub is the support team within the Learning Technology Innovation Centre ensuring that faculty, staff, and students never feel alone with their learning technology challenges\. We provide personalized help for teaching and learning with technology—whether you are in person, online, hybrid, or multi\-access\.

We also develop and maintain technology, offer tech\-related training, and share technical documentation\. In supporting our mission, we focus first on pedagogy and strive to make learning technology approachable and accessible for everyone\. We look forward to hearing from you\!

## Join training and initiatives

Interested in the training available or initiatives underway related to learning technology at UBC? Here are a few key ways that you can get involved\.

### Training and Professional Development

Learn more about pedagogical practices, get familiar with available technologies, and explore effective ways of integrating technology into your teaching\.

[Learn More – “Training and Professional Development”](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/support/training-and-professional-development/)

### Accessibility and Inclusivity

Find UBC\-specific guidance to help you design and deliver courses so that all students can engage\.

[Learn More – “Accessibility and Inclusivity”](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/technology-tips/accessibility-and-inclusivity/)

### Learning Technology Decisions

See what learning technologies are currently under consideration or have been adopted or declined; you can also submit a request of your own\.

[Learn More – “Learning Technology Decisions”](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/initiatives/learning-technology-decisions/)

### Faculty Working Groups

Join a working group to compare similar tools and provide recommendations on which technologies should receive central funding and support\.

[Learn More – “Faculty Working Groups”](https://ltic.ubc.ca/lt-hub/initiatives/faculty-working-groups/)
