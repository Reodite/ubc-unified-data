On December 30, 2026, the Canvas ePortfolios feature, currently listed as “ePortfolios \(Legacy\)” in the Global Navigation, will be discontinued and replaced by the new [Canvas Portfolio](https://community.instructure.com/en/discussion/660793/introducing-canvas-portfolio-now-part-of-the-core-canvas-experience) feature\. Canvas recommended exporting or migrating any important content before this date\. The new Canvas Portfolio is fully integrated within Canvas and provides a more user\-friendly portfolio experience\.

## What do I need to know about this new feature?

-   **There is no automatic migration from ePortfolio to Canvas Portfolio\. You will need to manually migrate from ePortfolio to Canvas Portfolio\.** The Canvas Portfolio feature is currently available in Canvas for students to begin transferring their ePortfolios\. For instructions, please refer to the LT Hub [guide for students](https://elearning5.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/How-to-Migrate-from-ePortfolio-to-Canvas-Portfolio.pdf)\.

## Where can I get further help?

If you have other questions about this change or about Canvas, feel free to contact us at the LT Hub\.

-   To learn more about how to migrate to Canvas Portfolios, refer to our LT Hub [guide for students](https://elearning5.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/How-to-Migrate-from-ePortfolio-to-Canvas-Portfolio.pdf)\.
-   You can also refer to the [FAQ page](https://community.instructure.com/en/kb/articles/661342-how-do-i-view-portfolios-as-a-student) set up by Canvas on this new feature\.
