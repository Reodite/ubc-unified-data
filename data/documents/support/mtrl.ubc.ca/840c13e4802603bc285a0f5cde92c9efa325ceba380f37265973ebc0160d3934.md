MTRL Stores provides department services including ordering, receiving, and shipping of items in support of MTRL Research and Teaching\.

**Stores hours are typically:**

Monday to Friday  
Morning: 8:30 am to 12:15 pm  
Afternoon: 1:00 pm to 4:30 pm

  
**An integrated [webform](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3z7nUz6l94axEfc) has been developed to place orders, receive, and ship packages\.**

**Notes:**

\(1\) When requesting stock items from MTRL Stores, please search through MTRL Stores Inventory [Spreadsheet](https://apsc-mtrl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/MTRL-inventory-listing-May-2021.xlsx) to check what items are carried by Stores and the naming convention for the item\(s\)\.  
  
\(2\) If you expect to make frequent purchases, you may want to fill out a [Blanket Purchase Authorization Form](https://apsc-mtrl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/Purchase-Authorization-Form-2.docx)\.
