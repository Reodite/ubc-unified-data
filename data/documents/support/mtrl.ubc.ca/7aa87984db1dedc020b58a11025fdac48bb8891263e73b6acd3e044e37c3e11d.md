Before proceeding to the Departmental Safety Orientation Courses \(**Step 3**\), please ensure you have completed all mandatory safety training required for UBC workers \(**Step 2**\)\. The list of required training modules is provided below in the accordion and is also available on the [UBC SRS](https://srs.ubc.ca/training/required-training-for-all-ubc-workers/) website\. **You will be required to upload your training completion certificates before gaining access to the Departmental Safety Orientation Courses\.**

**Mandatory safety training required for all UBC Workers are:**

-   New Worker Safety Orientation
-   Preventing &amp; Addressing Workplace Bullying and Harassment Training
-   Preventing &amp; Responding to Sexual Misconduct
-   Privacy &amp; Information Security Fundamentals Training Part 1 &amp; 2
-   Workplace Hazardous Material Information Systems \(WHMIS\)
-   Workplace Violence Prevention Training

**Note: If there is any discrepancy between the following list and the one provided on the [SRS website](https://srs.ubc.ca/training/required-training-for-all-ubc-workers/), the latter takes precedence\.**

## Additional Safety Orientations

Depending on the nature and scope of your work, you may be required to complete additional safety orientations and review relevant resources – please discuss with your supervisor\(s\) about this\. Some commonly accessed orientations/ resources are:

-   [Biosafety Training](https://srs.ubc.ca/training/research-safety-training-courses/biological-safety-training/)
-   [Chemical Safety Resources](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/research-safety/research-safety-resources-documents/chemical-safety-resources/) – Acid/ Base, Cryo, Peroxides, Perchloric Acids, Risk Assessment for Chemical Hazards
-   [Chemical Safety Training](https://srs.ubc.ca/training/research-safety-training-courses/chemical-safety-training/)
-   [Emergency Preparedness Training](https://srs.ubc.ca/training/emergency-preparedness-training/)
-   [Environmental Training](https://srs.ubc.ca/training/environmental-training/) – Hazardous Waste Management; Autoclave Training
-   [Laser Safety Training](https://srs.ubc.ca/training/research-safety-training-courses/laser-safety-courses/)
-   [SRS Working Alone Risk Assessment](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/safety-programs/personal-safety/workingalone/)
