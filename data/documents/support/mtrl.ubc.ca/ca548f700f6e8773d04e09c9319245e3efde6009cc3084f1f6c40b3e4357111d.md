## Resources for Prospective Students

Plan your journey with us\! We provide comprehensive resources to help you make an informed decision\.

-   **Academic Advising**: Contact our admissions team for personalized guidance\. Faculty graduate advisors are available to help on any aspect of graduate studies within the department – both professional and personal\. **To seek support from an advisor please send an email to** [**graduate@mtrl\.ubc\.ca**](mailto:graduate@mtrl.ubc.ca)\. Please do not email advisors at their personal email addresses\.
    -   Graduate Advisors: Dr\. Ben Britton, Dr\. Matthias Militzer
    -   Program Assistants: Lydia Lyu, Becky Silverman
-   **Graduate School Fair**: Each year, the Department participates in the [Canadian Graduate Engineering Consortium](https://www.engineeringgradstudies.com/), usually take place in the Fall\.

-   **Financial Planning:** More information about budgeting and financial planning tips for living in Vancouver, including a cost of living estimator can be found on the [G\+PS website](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-commmunity/blog/easy-tips-budgeting-saving-money-vancouver)\.
-   **UBC Housing Support:** [G\+PS Housing Portal](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/housing), [UBC Graduate Student Housing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/graduate-students/)[, GSS Housing Aids](https://gss.ubc.ca/housing-aids/)

-   [**Admission requirements**](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/application-admission): Graduate admission process is administered by the UBC Graduate \+ Postdoctoral Studies \(G\+PS\)\. Please visit their website to confirm detailed admission requirements and eligibility to apply\.
-   **Program Overview**: [One\-page poster](https://apsc-mtrl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/10/MTRL_CEGC_2023_09_29.pdf?_gl=1*1bsulxt*_gcl_au*MTYwNTU4OTg4My4xNzM1MDgzODQ4*_ga*Mjk2NTAyODY0LjE3MjcxMzIzNzY.*_ga_4HEE092K89*MTczODY0MzQwMy43My4xLjE3Mzg2NDYyMDUuNTkuMC4w), [Programs slide deck](https://apsc-mtrl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/07/MTRL_GradOpportunities_web.pdf?_gl=1*1bsulxt*_gcl_au*MTYwNTU4OTg4My4xNzM1MDgzODQ4*_ga*Mjk2NTAyODY0LjE3MjcxMzIzNzY.*_ga_4HEE092K89*MTczODY0MzQwMy43My4xLjE3Mzg2NDYyMDUuNTkuMC4w)
-   **MTRL PhD Journey**: [Detailed information about doing a PhD at UBC MTRL](https://apsc-mtrl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/MTRL-PhD-Journey_7Mar2025.pdf)

## Resources for Current Students

Find information about scheduling requests, graduate forms submission, academic calendar, and many more\.

-   [**G\+PS important dates and deadlines**](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/deadlines): Dates and deadlines about graduation, thesis submission, etc\.
-   [**Exam schedules**](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/dates-and-deadlines): Official exam schedules published on the UBC academic calendar\.

Faculty should request the scheduling of the following on behalf of their students using the [MTRL Scheduling Requests](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_4M94eSyhwVYhDtI) survey\. 

-   Supervisory Committee Meetings
-   Master’s Final Oral Exam
-   PhD Comprehensive Exam
-   PhD Proposal
-   PhD Internal Exam

  
Faculty or students should submit graduate forms to Student Services using the [Graduate Forms Submission](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6ooLg2tOhhLi9QG) survey\. Forms you can find and submit through this survey are: 

-   Request for Extension
-   Request for Leave of Absence
-   Changes to Supervisory Committee
-   PhD Candidacy
-   Voluntary Withdrawal
