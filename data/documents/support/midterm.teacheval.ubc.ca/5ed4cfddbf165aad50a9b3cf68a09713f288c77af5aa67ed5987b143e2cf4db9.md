The office of the AMS VP Academic and University Affairs wrote a report in Summer 2014 that describes the results of efforts by the AMS and CTLT to encourage instructors to conduct MEoTs during the 2013/2014 academic year\. It also provides the findings of a survey that was sent to Faculty participants in Term 2 of that year, as well as recommended actions moving forward with the project\. You can [download this report here](https://teaching-eval.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/08/MEoT-Update-for-Summer-2014.pdf), or you can find it and past reports on the MEoT project and assessment on our [About](https://teaching-eval.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about/ "About") page\.
