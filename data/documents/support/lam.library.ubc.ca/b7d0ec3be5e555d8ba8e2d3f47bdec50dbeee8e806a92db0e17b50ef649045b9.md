The following guides and tutorials to help you get started on your own\. The guides will help you:

-   Apply critical thinking skills to the secondary research process
-   Polish your database search skill
-   Create citations

[David Lam Library – Jump\-Start Your Research](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/jumpstart-your-research/)  
A step by step guide to planning your secondary research that you can e\-mail to yourself and/or a business librarian\.

[Basic Library Skills Tutorial – UBC Vancouver](http://help.library.ubc.ca/planning-your-research/tutorials/basic-library-skills-tutorial/?login) An interactive set of tutorials, introducing the core knowledge needed to complete quality research using UBC Library resources\.

[Research Tips](https://lambranch.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/03/Research_Tips_DLAM_Jan2021.pdf)Tips for searching databases effectively and for realistic research\.

[APA Citation Examples](https://lambranch.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/02/Business_Citation_Examples_Feb2021.pdf)[https://lambranch\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2013/01/Business\_Citation\_Examples\_Jan2013\.doc](https://lambranch.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/01/Business_Citation_Examples_Jan2013.doc)Tips and examples of citations from business databases

[How to Cite Sources](http://learningcommons.ubc.ca/what-we-offer/citing-sources/)[https://lambranch\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2013/01/Business\_Citation\_Examples\_Jan2013\.doc](https://lambranch.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/01/Business_Citation_Examples_Jan2013.doc)\(Chapman Learning Commons\) Guide to citing sources in APA, MLA and other citation styles\.

[RefWorks at UBC Library](http://help.library.ubc.ca/evaluating-and-citing-sources/refworks-write-n-cite/)  
Citation management software that allows you to create your own database of citations and generate citations and bibliographies in a variety of citation styles\.

[Jump Start Your Debate Research](https://lambranch.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/02/Jump_Start_Your_Debate_Research_2011.pdf)  
A step by step guide to help you start researching secondary sources for debates\.
