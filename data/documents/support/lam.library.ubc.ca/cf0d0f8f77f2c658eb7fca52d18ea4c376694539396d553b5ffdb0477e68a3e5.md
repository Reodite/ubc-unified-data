Many of your class assignments will require you to find high quality information on an industry, company, consumer group, location or other business topic\. 

Drop by **CLC 220** anytime from **1pm to 4pm** on **Mondays to Fridays** to get in\-person research help from a professional business librarian\. No appointment necessary\! 

[Image](https://lam-library-13mar2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/03/noun_Email_1841941-e1584464896664.png)Staffing these drop\-in hours is dependent on librarian availability and could change without notice\. If no one is available during the regular drop\-in hours, please submit your request via email: [lam\.ref@ubc\.ca](mailto:lam.ref@ubc.ca) or click [here](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/ask-us/) 

You can also check out the following guides and tutorials to help you get started on your own\.  

[David Lam Library – Jump\-Start Your Research](https://lam.library.ubc.ca/jumpstart-your-research/) 

A step by step guide to planning your secondary research that you can e\-mail to yourself and/or a business librarian\. 

[Research Tips](https://lam-library-13mar2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/03/Research_Tips_DLAM_Jan2021.pdf)

Tips for searching databases effectively and for realistic research\.
