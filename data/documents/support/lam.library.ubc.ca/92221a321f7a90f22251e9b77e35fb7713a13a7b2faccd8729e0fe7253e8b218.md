## CLC Writing Coach

One\-on\-one writing appointments with our professional Writing Coach can help with planning, idea development, tone, stylistic improvement, and error recognition\. Any genre of writing is suitable; however, this is not an editing service and attendees are expected to participate actively in the conversation\.

Attendees must bring a **printed copy** of their work to review during their appointment\. Possible writing samples include a draft of an academic assignment, a cover letter, a professional email, etc\.

Open to all UBC Sauder students\. Appointments are 30 minutes long and will take place in\-person in CLC 219\.

To register for an appointment, visit your Canvas Calendar, click “Find Appointments,” select “CLC: Online,” and hit submit\. Click on each appointment to learn more and register\.

## **Graduate Writing Consultations**

The UBC Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication \(CWSC\) offers writing consultations for UBC Sauder graduate students at the CLC/DLam, Room 205, Tuesday\-Thursday\. The CWSC doctoral writing consultants provide feedback on a wide range of writing projects including course assignments, manuscripts for publication, and research papers\.

Reserve your spot [**here**](https://ubcca.mywconline.com/index.php)\! Select the Sauder Graduate In\-Person Schedule\.

## UBC Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication Writing Consultations

The Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication \(CWSC\) supports a diverse community of writers at UBC, at various levels of proficiency, across many types of undergraduate and graduate writing situations: academic, creative, professional, and technical\.

The CWSC offers 2 types of consultations—In\-Person Writing Consultations and Online Written Feedback Writing Consultations\. For more information, please click [**here**](https://writing.library.ubc.ca/)\.

**Online Written Feedback Writing Consultation:**

1\. Proceed to [**WC Online**](https://ubcca.mywconline.com/)\.

2\. Select the schedule “Written Feedback Writing Consultation” and choose an appointment slot\.

3\. Prior to the appointment start time, choose “Edit Your Appointment” and upload your document\. If possible, upload the assignment description and/or rubric as well\.

4\. The Writing Consultant will provide feedback and re\-upload the document, at which time you’ll receive an email and you’ll be able to retrieve the document with the Consultant’s comments\.

5\. If you have trouble retrieving your document, try right\-clicking “Download Attached File” and opening in a new tab or new window\.
