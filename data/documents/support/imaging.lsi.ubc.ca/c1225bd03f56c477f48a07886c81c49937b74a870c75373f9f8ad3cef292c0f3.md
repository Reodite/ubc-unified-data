Minimum fees are being collected to cover the service contracts and do system upgrades\.

We send the invoices to UBC every 3 months for billing:

-   March
-   June
-   September
-   December

Please enter your speed chart or worktag\# when requesting training or booking microscopes or workstations

---

### Training fees:

-   $100 for Leica SP5 confocal
-   $200 for STED, Leica Thunder Imager, and Leica Stellaris 5 confocal
-   $5/hr for workstation \(Imaris and Huygens Pro\) bookings

### Usage fees:

#### For confocal and wide field:

-   **Independent Use** \| Sun\-Sat 12:00 AM – 12:00 AM
    -   Internal: $15\.00/hr or $25\.00/hr for Leica Stellaris confocal or $35\.00/hr for Leica Thunder microscope
    -   External: $25\.00/hr
    -   Corporate: $50\.00/hr
-   **Assisted Use** \| Mon\-Fri 09:00 AM – 05:00 PM
    -   Internal: $50\.00/hr
    -   External: $60\.00/hr
    -   Corporate: $120\.00/hr
-   **Starter Use** \| Mon\-Fri 09:00 AM – 05:00 PM
    -   Internal: $15\.00/hr
    -   External: $25\.00/hr
    -   Corporate: $50\.00/hr

-   **Starter Use** \| Mon\-Fri 09:00 AM – 05:00 PM \(All Customers\) \(needs approval\)
    -   Internal: $25\.00/hr
    -   External: $25\.00/hr
    -   Corporate: $50\.00/hr
-   **Independent Use** \| Sun\-Sat 12:00 AM – 12:00 AM \(Trained\)
