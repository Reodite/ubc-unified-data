**Features about VS2000**

-   The VS200 is a six\-slide scanning system that supports brightfield, darkfield, fluorescence, polarized light, and Speckle Illumination fluorescence imaging\.

-   The system includes dual cameras: a high\-resolution colour camera and a Hamamatsu ORCA Fusion camera for high\-sensitivity fluorescence imaging\.

-   Available objectives include 2x, 4x, 10x, 20x, 40x, and 60x oil, with automated oil dispensing\.

-   The system can batch\-scan up to six slides at a time and supports fluorescence excitation for DAPI, AF488/GFP, Cy3, mCherry/AF568, AF594/Texas Red, AF647/Cy5, and AF750/Cy7\.
