We are pleased to announce that the ZEISS Lattice SIM 3 microscope has been installed and commissioned on the 3rd floor of the Life Sciences Institute\. This next\-generation structured illumination microscopy \(SIM\) system will be available for user projects starting in the new year\. *What the ZEISS Lattice SIM 3 offers:*

·         High\-speed super\-resolution imaging – Achieve lateral resolution down to \~140 nm, providing sub\-diffraction detail beyond conventional widefield microscopy\.

·         Large field of view with fine detail – Seamlessly move from broad overviews \(e\.g\., tissue sections or multicellular structures\) to high\-resolution subcellular imaging within the same dataset\.

·         Fast and gentle acquisition – Advanced SIM Apotome and Lattice SIM modes enable rapid imaging with reduced phototoxicity, ideal for live or sensitive samples\.

·         Live and fixed sample compatibility – Well\-suited for 3D model organisms, embryos, organoids, tissue sections, and subcellular structures\.

·         Flexible imaging modes – Includes speed\-enhanced modes \(e\.g\., Leap mode\) and optimized reconstruction workflows to support diverse experimental needs\.
