***Olympus Discovery Summit–Looking Forward: A New Era of Research\.*** The 3\-day event will focus on emerging technologies enabling groundbreaking research, next\-generation models for research and best practices for a post\-pandemic workplace\. The topics will range from technology and applications \(FluidFM for CRISPR gene editing, Abbelight, high content screen and AI\) to more academic talks on topics such as compound effects on 3D model cultures and using SMLM for analysis of early SARS\-CoV\-2 replication, as examples\.
