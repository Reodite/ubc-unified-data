**Abbelight &amp; Evident SMLM and TIRF Demonstration**  
This seminar and hands\-on demonstration will introduce single\-molecule localization microscopy techniques, including dSTORM, DNA\-PAINT, PALM, single\-particle tracking, and TIRF microscopy\. Participants will have the opportunity to gain hands\-on experience with the Abbelight SAFe MN360 combined with the Evident IX85, and may also discuss the possibility of imaging their own samples\.

**Online seminar:** Thursday, July 16, 2026, 10:00–11:00 AM PST
