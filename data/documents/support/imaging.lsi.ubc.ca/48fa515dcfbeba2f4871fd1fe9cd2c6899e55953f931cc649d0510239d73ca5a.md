### **Please follow the following steps to access HRMC for training:**

\(1\) Using the following link, sign up for LSI Imaging booking and billing system account, *iLab account*\.

[Register for an iLab account](https://ca.ilabsolutions.com/service_center/show_external/3422)

\(2\) After signing up and receiving account confirmation from iLab, please login and find our facility \(Life Sciences Institute Imaging\), then initiate a training request under “Request Services” tab on the top&gt;&gt;Services&gt;&gt;Training&gt;&gt;Submit

\(3\) We will contact you for training scheduling\. \(Note: Please do not book for training from your side\)

\(4\) A new user can book a time on the microscope after the training session followed by up to 3 supervised bookings\.

### Collaborative Projects:

We are also open to collaborative projects where our staff are here for one or several key steps of your project\(s\): project/experiment planning, sample preparation, image collection, data analysis and manuscript writing\. Please contact us\.
