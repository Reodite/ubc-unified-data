## Welcome to SRMC @ UBC

Super Resolution Microscopy Core/SRMC has grown into a world\-class fluorescence/super\-resolution microscopy imaging facility in Canada, covering the spectra from subcellular structures to cells to tissues, from 2D to 3D and 4D from macro\- to nano\-scale, with research projects from life sciences to biomaterials to food sciences and many others\.

### [Learn More about LSI IMAGING](https://imaging.lsi.ubc.ca/instruments/)
