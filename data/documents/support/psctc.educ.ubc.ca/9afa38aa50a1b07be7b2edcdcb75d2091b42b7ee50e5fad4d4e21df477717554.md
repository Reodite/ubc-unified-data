The PSCTC maintains an up\-to\-date test library of standardized tests, assessment instruments, reference materials and other resources, including a dedicated Social and Emotional Learning collection and computer lab\. A complete inventory of PSCTC Test Library holdings is available at the PSCTC\.

**Test Library Reservation Policy** 

-   All users of the PSCTC Test Library material\(s\) and facility need to be registered with our clinic prior to submitting any reservations
    -   Please email psctc\.reception@ubc\.ca for a copy of the “PSCTC Facilities and Client Rooms &amp; Test Library Registration” form
-   Reserve at least 2 business days \(48hrs\) in advance, as we DO NOT accept same day reservations\.
-   Requests made after 3 pm will be processed on the next business day\.
-   We are open for pick up/drop off from Monday through Thursday from 9 AM – 12 PM &amp; 1 PM – 4 PM\.
    -   Please note we are closed from 12\-1 PM

[Image](https://prtc1-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/10/Test-library-Schedule-Chart-940x940.png)

-   Once your request has been processed, you will receive a separate confirmation email from the PSCTC staff\.
-   **Please note: that there is a late fee of $5 per item per week\.**
-   Fill in this form for access to the PSCTC for test library pickup &amp; room booking
