The UBC Psychological Services and Counselling Training Centre is located on the ground floor of the Neville Scarfe Building in the UBC Vancouver campus\. Pay parking is available in the [West Parkade](https://parking.ubc.ca/), which is accessible on Lower Mall\. The campus is also readily accessible by [public transit](https://www.translink.ca/)\.
