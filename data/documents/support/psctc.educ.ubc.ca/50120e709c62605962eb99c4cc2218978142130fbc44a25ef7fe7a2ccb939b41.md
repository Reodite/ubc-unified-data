### Date: Summer, 2017  
Instructor: Melissa Robichaud

**Social Emotional\-Learning series**  
A series of SEL related workshops was offered in the PRTC in partnership with the Edith Landau Charitable Foundation, The Attias Family Foundation, &amp; The Edward D\. and Anna Mitchell Family Foundation sponsored a week of workshops on the topic of social\-emotional learning\. Speakers included : Dr\. Marc Brackett \(Yale University\), Dr\. Bonnie Leadbeater \(University of Victoria\), Ms\. Amy Walkder \(Commitee for Children, Seattle, WA\), and Diane Gossens \(Saskatoon, SK\)\.

[Summer Workshops](http://ecps.educ.ubc.ca/hdlc/summer-series-workshops-social-and-emotional-learning-schools "Social and Emotional Learning in Schools - Summer Workshops")
