> Plan for your future career with the skills you’ve developed in the Bachelor of Media Studies program\. Explore the different opportunities to kickstart your career in media or further develop your knowledge and skills with specialized graduate programs\.

## What can you do with a BMS Degree?

BMS students graduate with a broad foundation in media studies, opening up future career possibilities that range from marketing and communications, to advertising, project management, and journalism\.

Graduates of the BMS program most commonly find potential careers in the technology industry\. However, our students excel in organizations and environments that emphasize artistic, technical and analytical skills\. Below is a list of potential careers for BMS students\.

### Potential occupations

-   Advertising account executive
-   Art director
-   Broadcast journalist
-   Innovation officer
-   Digital marketing strategist
-   Entrepreneur
-   Journalist
-   Web developer
-   Marketing and communications specialist
-   Media liaison
-   Media planner
-   Multimedia producer
-   Project manager
-   Public relations officer
-   Technology startup lead
-   User experience \(UX\) designer
