Published annually, *Beacon: UBC Journal of Media Studies* is a student\-led, peer\- and faculty\-reviewed online multimedia journal\. It showcases the work of current undergraduate students at the University of British Columbia who explore and study the subject of Media – featuring a range of creative and academic works that offers new perspectives on media and the impact of technological innovation\. Students can submit up to four original pieces that are related to the field of media studies each school year\.

A testament to the diverse, dynamic, and inquisitive media\-related works that UBC undergraduate students produce, Beacon is proud to be a platform that shares students’ works to a greater audience\.
