> The Media Studies Student Association \(MSSA\) aims to foster a community in the Bachelor of Media Studies program\. The MSSA showcases the diversity of our students, and provides opportunities for personal, professional, and academic growth\. By connecting with organizations involved in new media, we can inform about the unique BMS program and support students through their journey\.
