## Booking Notes

-   See [current rates](http://sportfacilities.ubc.ca/dmc/)
-   Rental season is September 1 – April 30
-   Minimum rental is 32 sessions \(not including statutory holidays\)
-   Payments are due monthly beginning August 1
-   Once your ice slot is confirmed by DMC Arena Administration, an ice contract and Electronic Funds Transfer \(EFT\) / credit card authorization form will be issued for your signature
-   A discount will be applied if the payment method is by EFT, cheque, or cash; please call for discount rates – [download EFT authorization form](https://sportfacilities.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/ATHLREC_EFTFormWEB.pdf) \(PDF\)
-   ‘Commercial General Liability Insurance’ is mandatory for all groups – f you do not have your own insurance, this can easily be included with your contract for $200 plus applicable taxes for the entire season
