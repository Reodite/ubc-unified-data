## Facility Overview

Located at the heart of UBC’s Vancouver Point Grey campus, Recreation Centre North \(Rec North\) features an indoor floor area of 101,000 square feet spread across three wood floor gymnasium courts; an elevated, indoor 200m track; a four\-level fitness centre; multipurpose studios; and universal and gender\-specific change areas\. Rec North opened in July 2025 and was constructed to a LEED Gold green building standard and is Rick Hansen Foundation Certified\.

## Facility Features

### Gymnasiums

-   Hardwood flooring
-   Double Court Gymnasiums A &amp; B:
    -   Individual gymnasiums
        -   103 ft \(length\) by 61 ft \(width\) – 6,283 sq ft
    -   Combined dimensions \(when partition drape is raised\)
        -   103 ft \(length\) by 122 ft \(width\) – 12,566 sq ft
-   Single Court Nestor’s Gym:
    -   104 ft \(length\) by 60\.5 ft \(width\) – 6,292 sq ft 
    -   Privacy curtains available
-   Ceiling height is 34\.5 ft to fans and 48 ft to the roof

### Studio

-   Wood flooring
-   50\.5 ft \(width\) by 60\.5 ft \(length\) – 3,055 sq ft
-   Floor\-to\-ceiling mirrors on three walls
-   Stretching bars
-   Capacity: Up 90 people \(exact capacity will depend on activity\)

### Multipurpose Room

-   Rubber matted flooring
-   45\.5 ft \(width\) by 58\.5 ft \(length\) – 2,662 sq ft
-   Floor\-to\-ceiling mirrors on 1 wall
-   Capacity: Up to 75 people \(exact capacity will depend on activity\)

### Rec North Fitness Centre

-   Four floors of fitness centre space
-   Large selection of cardio machines, weight machines and free weights
-   Olympic lifting platforms, power cage combo
-   Equipment such as Cascade Raptor, SCL 885 Stair Climber, Assault AirRunner, Assault AirBike, Precor EFX 885 Elliptical, RBK 685 Recumbent Cycle, SRM 781 Treadmill, UBK 685 Upright Cycle and more

### Indoor Track

-   200m distance
-   3 lanes for running, walking, rolling
-   Rubber padded surface

### Parking

-   UBC Parking is a pay by plate system\. The closest parkade to the Recreation Centre North is North Parkade\. Visit [UBC Parking](https://parking.ubc.ca/) for more information\.
