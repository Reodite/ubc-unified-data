## Booking Notes

-   Bookings must be made at least 14 days in advance
-   A 50% non\-refundable deposit is required at time of booking, with the balance due at least 30 days before the event
-   All bookings require [insurance](https://sportfacilities.ubc.ca/rental/#Insurance) that meet the guidelines outlined by UBC\. Insurance coverage is available for purchase through UBC Athletics &amp; Recreation as part of your rental
-   Bookings are done on a first\-come\-first\-serve basis
-   When making a request, the renter must include sufficient time in their booked block for set up and take down
-   **Gymnasium availability:**
    -   **September 1 – April 30** 
        -   **Weekdays** – Gymnasiums are not available for rent after 4:00PM on Monday – Thursday\.
        -   **Weekends** – Gymnasiums have limited availability on Saturdays and are not available on Sundays
            -   Please note that gymnasiums may be available on holiday weekends and academic breaks \(e\.g\. Reading Break\) when regularly scheduled activities may not be running\.
        -   SRC Gymnasiums are not available for the months of December and April due to exams\.
    -   **May 1 – August 31**
        -   All gymnasiums are available for bookings during regular building hours\.

"\*" indicates required fields
