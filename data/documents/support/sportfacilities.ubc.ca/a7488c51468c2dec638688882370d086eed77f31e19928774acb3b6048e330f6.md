## Facility Overview

The UBC Tennis Centre has a total of 12 indoor courts and 1 outdoor court\. A state of the art 7,175 m2 facility houses 8 indoor courts along with one beautiful outdoor court on the west side of the building offering maximum privacy\. We also have 4 indoor courts inside the UBC Tennis Bubble, which offers another great playing option\.

The updated facility is equipped with full amenities – accessible washrooms / change rooms with showers, day\-use lockers, a water fountain, a meeting room, and multi\-purpose rooms\.

[Facility History](https://sportfacilities.ubc.ca/tennis-centre/history/)

## Facility Rates

Please visit [UBC Tennis Court Bookings](https://recreation.ubc.ca/tennis/court-booking/) for Single Session Court Bookings at the UBC Tennis Centre\.

Please note that UBC Students, UNA members, and CSC members must book in person with a valid ID to receive discounted rates during applicable times\.

## Rental Requests

For day\-to\-day regular court bookings, please book online or by calling the Operations Desk\.

For advanced, group, tournament or multi\-court bookings, please use

[UBC Tennis Centre Rental Request Form](https://sportfacilities.ubc.ca/tennis-centre/form/)

## Facility Features

### UBC Tennis Centre

-   8 indoor courts
-   1 outdoor court
-   Surface – premier court / plexipave standard – Australian and US Open blue – pace is medium / slow

### UBC Tennis Bubble

-   4 indoor courts
-   Surface – plexipave standard – pacific blue – pace is true medium

### Parking

-   There is street parking with meters on East Mall \(west of the Tennis Centre\) and the multi\-level Thunderbird Parkade on Thunderbird Blvd \(across Thunderbird Blvd from the Tennis Centre\)\. Visit [UBC Parking](https://parking.ubc.ca/) for more information\.
