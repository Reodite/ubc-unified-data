## Facility Overview

Home to many UBC Varsity programs and community athletic groups, Thunderbird Park has recently undergone a major overhaul that includes the construction of two new FieldTurf soccer pitches, a brand new baseball stadium, and a new Track and Field Facility, as well as upgrades to the existing grass and artificial fields\.

### Artificial Turf Fields

*All artificial turf fields are fully lit for night play\.*

#### Ken Woods &amp; Warren Fields

-   Home of the UBC Thunderbirds varsity soccer programs
-   FieldTurf surface for natural grass feel and all\-weather use
-   Seating capacity of 150 in adjacent bleachers
-   Scoreboard \(Ken Woods Field only\)
-   4 full size frames and nets for split field play
-   Covered player benches
-   Dimensions Ken Woods: 120 yards long by 75 yards wide
-   Dimensions Warren Fields: 110 yards long by 71 yards wide

#### MacInnes Family Field

-   Astroturf hybrid – artificial turf with sand infill
-   LED field lighting
-   Covered seating area along east edge
-   Play Area Dimensions: 88\.5m \(290′\) x 44\.5m \(146′\)

#### Tourmaline West Stadium

-   The first artificial baseball field in British Columbia
-   Home of the UBC Thunderbirds varsity &amp; junior varsity baseball teams, and the UBC Thunder PBL program
-   AstroTurf surface for natural grass feel and all\-weather use
-   4 AstroTurf Bullpens
-   Portable on\-field mounds and fence that allow for field conversions to softball, slo\-pitch or little league
-   Outfield available for flag football, softball, and ultimate frisbee rentals
-   Dimensions: RF – 98\.1 \(322 feet\); CF – 113\.1m \(371 feet\); LF – 98\.1m \(322 feet\)

#### Wright Field

-   Home of the UBC Thunderbirds varsity field hockey program
-   Artificial turf surface
-   Two sheltered bench areas and scoreboard
-   Dimensions Wright: 100 yards long by 60 yards wide

### Grass Fields

#### Wolfson East, Buck &amp; Lord Fields

-   Home of the UBC Thunderbirds varsity rugby programs; used for recreational ultimate and soccer
-   3 full\-size rugby pitches, with uprights
-   Fully irrigated natural grass fields
-   Change room and washroom facilities in the UBC Rugby Pavilion
-   Dimensions Wolfson East, Lord &amp; Buck: 120m long by 68m wide

#### Rashpal Dhillon Track &amp; Field Oval

-   Home of the UBC Varsity track &amp; field team
-   8 lane, 400m synthetic polyurethane running track
-   300 seat grandstand; long jump pit, steeple chase &amp; shotput area
-   Other equipment available upon request \(hurdles, high jump, etc\.\)
-   Dimensions: 115 yards long by 71 yards wide
