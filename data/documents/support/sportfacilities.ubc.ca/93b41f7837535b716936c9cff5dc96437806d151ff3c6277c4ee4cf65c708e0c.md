## Facility Overview

The UBC Baseball Rose Indoor Training Centre \(RITC\) is home to the UBC Thunderbirds Baseball Team and is a state\-of\-the\-art indoor training facility located beside the Tourmaline West Baseball Stadium\. It is heated allowing a comfortable training environment year round\. Designed for baseball, with 4 full\-size batting cages and pitching machines, the RITC is the premiere baseball facility in British Columbia\. The facility also has the ability to convert and be used for multi\-sport training\.

[Facility History](https://sportfacilities.ubc.ca/baseball-tc/history/)

## Rental Requests

Rentals can be requested by calling 604\-822\-5770 or by emailing [itc\.info@ubc\.ca](mailto:itc.info@ubc.ca) for booking requests\. Visit the UBC Recreation website for more information on [how to book cage rentals](https://recreation.ubc.ca/baseball/cage-rentals/)\.

## Facility Amenities

-   4 full\-size batting cages \(Astroturf\) with dividers
-   1 designated pitching area \(Astroturf\) with 2 pitching mounds and training equipment
-   4 pitching machines
-   3 HitTrax data capture and simulation systems
-   8 L\-screens and 4 pitching targets
-   Big screen 9 panel TV in lobby with live video feed of batting cages
-   UBC Baseball Team Apparel Store
-   Batting area floor space: 160’ long by 32’ wide
-   Pitching area floor space: 75’ long by 24’ wide

### Parking

-   There is street parking available along Wesbrook Mall and East Mall\. Thunderbird Parkade is the closest parkade\. Visit [UBC Parking](https://parking.ubc.ca/) for more information\.
