The UBC Tennis Centre was built over a two year period and completed in June 2011 with a budget of $8,900,000\. The Centre was expanded to include four tournament courts, 4 leisure courts, 1 outdoor court, and multi\-purpose support space\. The tournament courts will have upper\-level seating installed at a later date\.

The Tennis Bubble includes 4 indoor courts\.
