Thunderbird Stadium is looking brand new\! The field has been updated just in time for the upcoming school year where it will be home to UBC varsity football, soccer, and many other recreational programs\.

Start date of project: May 10  
End date: June 25

The new hybrid monofilament, slit film turf is stitched with the Thunderbirds logo at centre, with permanent blue coloured end zones\. The field also has stitched lines for both soccer and football\.

The $4\.9 million dollar UBC Baseball Stadium was completed in April 2018\. The state of the art facility can convert its dimensions to handle all age groups of baseball from little league to varsity\. The stadium includes:

-   New artificial Astroturf playing surface
-   New LED field lights
-   New scoreboard
-   New OF padded fence, dugouts and bullpens
-   Seating grandstand with capacity of 480 which also includes a press box and sound system
