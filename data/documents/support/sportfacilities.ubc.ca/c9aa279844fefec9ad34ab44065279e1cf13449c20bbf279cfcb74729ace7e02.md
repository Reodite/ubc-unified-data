## Facility Overview

Situated in the lower level in the UBC Life Building, the Activities &amp; Recreation Centre \(ARC\) has over 8,000 sq ft of space which complements existing campus fitness facilities\. The ARC opened in February 2018 and is focused on creating a comfortable, welcoming, and inclusive environment for those new to fitness and training activities\.

## Facility Features

-   8,000 sq\. ft of fitness centre space including a large cardio area, squat racks, selectorized equipment, and a circuit training section
-   Large functional training space
-   2 multi\-purpose studio spaces – 1 on Lower Level; 1 on Main Level \(capacities: 45 people\)
-   Universal change room facilities
-   Personal training specific space
-   Scheduled studio drop\-in with speed and heavy punching bags
-   Spin Space – bikes removable to create open studio space
