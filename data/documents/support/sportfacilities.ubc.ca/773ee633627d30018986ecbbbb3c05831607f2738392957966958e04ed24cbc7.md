The new learning facility for the Varsity Football team opened October 2015\. With support from the University, learning will not only happen on the field but off the field as well\. This Centre will aid in bringing academics to the forefront of the football team and help recruit future prospects for the football organization\. Sitting adjacent to Thunderbird Stadium, players now have a space all their own to focus on school as much as they focus on their sport\. Through the generous donations given to UBC Football Academic Centre leads the UBC’s start an evolution campaign, “the most ambitious fundraising and alumni engagement campaign in Canadian university history\.”
