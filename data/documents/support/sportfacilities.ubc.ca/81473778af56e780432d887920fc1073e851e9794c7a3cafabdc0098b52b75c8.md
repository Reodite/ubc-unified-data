The $4\.9 million dollar UBC Baseball Stadium was completed in April 2018\. The state of the art facility can convert its dimensions to handle all age groups of baseball from little league to varsity\. The stadium includes:

-   New artificial Astroturf playing surface
-   New LED field lights
-   New scoreboard
-   New OF padded fence, dugouts and bullpens
-   Seating grandstand with capacity of 480 which also includes a press box and sound system
