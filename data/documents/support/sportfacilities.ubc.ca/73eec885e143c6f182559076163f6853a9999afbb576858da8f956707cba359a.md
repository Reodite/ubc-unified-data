-   Bookings for skating parties requiring skate rentals of more than 50 people must be made at least 14 days in advance
-   A 50% non\-refundable deposit is required at time of booking, with the balance due at least 30 days before the event
-   Bookings are done on a first\-come\-first\-serve basis
-   When making a request, the renter must include sufficient time in their booked block for set up and take down
