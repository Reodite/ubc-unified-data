## Booking Notes

-   Rental season is from:
    -   January 2 – March 29
    -   May 1 – August 30
    -   September 1 – December 30
-   Payments are due at the beginning of every month
-   Credit card authorization are provided at contract confirmation and must be used as primary or back\-up payment
-   Contract confirmation is completed with return to UBC of signed contract and credit card authorization form
