Opened in January 2013, this 5000 square\-foot high\-performance facility includes luxurious team locker rooms, a 100 person lounge overlooking Lord and Buck fields, and office and storage space\. Featuring a top\-of\-the\-line AV system, a 30\-foot reclaimed\-wood bar, and full length window wall capable of being opened to provide unobstructed views of the action on the field, the clubhouse lounge is quickly becoming a highly sought after space on campus\.

In partnership with the BC Rugby Union, the facility is set to host a number of key national and international matches and tournaments at UBC\.

The Gerald McGavin UBC Rugby Centre will benefit rugby, high\-performance sport and social life on campus with the addition of a much\-needed clubhouse lounge for alumni, students and sport groups\.
