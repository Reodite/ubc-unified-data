## Am I eligible?

You are eligible for the services provided by the Faculty of Medicine \(FoM\) Digital Solutions Research Technology team if both of the below criteria are met:

1\. The project for which you are using the service is being led by a Faculty of Medicine member\.

2\. You have a Campus Wide Login \(CWL\)\. This can be a Faculty and Staff \(FAS\) CWL, or a sponsored CWL
