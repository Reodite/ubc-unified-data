## **Project Overview**

Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute \(VCHRI\) offers two award programs that enable practice\-based research led by health care providers that is directly relevant to their clinical practice\.

The [Research Challenge](https://www.vchri.ca/services/starting-your-project/awards-funding/research-challenge) supports Vancouver Coastal Health \(VCH\) point\-of\-care staff to identify a practice area that might be improved and develop a research project to consider how best to improve it\.

The [Team Grant](https://www.vchri.ca/services/starting-your-project/awards-funding/team-grant) supports staff and clinicians from VCH and Providence Health Care to conduct applied health research, community based and/or patient\-oriented research projects aimed at improving practice\.

For both the Research Challenge and Team Grant, clinicians are partnered with experienced researchers who mentor them throughout the entire process\. These VCHRI awards build health research capacity to improve health care and the health system in British Columbia\.

In 2024, VCHRI partnered with the UBC Faculty of Medicine Digital Solutions Data Management team to launch **Survey Monkey Apply** as the new platform for these award competitions\. This involved building a comprehensive grant platform for applicants, collaborators, mentors, peer reviewers, patient family partners, and administrative staff\. The platform supported the entire grant application cycle from the initial letter of intent stage through application submission, administrative review, peer review, and patient family partner review\.
