## **Project Overview**

This single centre study at Vancouver General Hospital aims to enroll 100 patients with an acute traumatic spinal cord injury \(SCI\) and a control group of 20 patients who have suffered a spine trauma but are neurologically intact\.  For participants with an SCI, blood samples are collected in the first 7 days post\-injury, as well as at 6 and 12 month follow\-ups\.  Neurological assessments will also be conducted at the time of study follow\-ups\.  Essentially, the purpose of SCRIBBLE is to evaluate blood\-based biomarkers of SCI and move towards the implementation of these biomarkers in clinical practice and research studies using point\-of\-care technologies\.
