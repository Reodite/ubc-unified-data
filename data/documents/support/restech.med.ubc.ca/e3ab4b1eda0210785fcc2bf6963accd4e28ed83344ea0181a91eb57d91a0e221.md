Ensure optimal security and performance for your desktop environment 

## Service

We help protect your crucial research data by ensuring your desktop environment and specialized software are optimized for security and performance\.

## Possible uses include:

-   Optimizing desktops that use specialized or highly intensive computational applications, storage, or data visualization for health and biomedical research 
-   Optimizing control stations that connect to specialized equipment used for generating research data 
-   Assisting with the installation of research applications \(note: ongoing application support is generally provided by the vendor and can be reviewed on a case\-by\-case basis\) 

For research desktop computing requests, please complete the [Systems intake form](https://rc.med.ubc.ca/redcap/surveys/?s=8P7W4PYAPCTYJJLE) or [contact us](https://med-fom-researchtech.sites.olt.ubc.ca/contact-us/)\.
