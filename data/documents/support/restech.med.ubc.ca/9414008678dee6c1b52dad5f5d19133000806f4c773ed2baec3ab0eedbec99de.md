## **Project Overview**

The Hematology Cell Bank of BC, \(a tissue bank or bio\-repository\) established and REB approved in September of 2005, represents a partnership between clinicians and staff of the Leukemia/Bone Marrow Transplant Program of BC, located at Vancouver General Hospital and research scientists from the Terry Fox Laboratory and collaborating research centers around the world\. Laboratory technician support is provided by the Eaves Stem Cell Assay Laboratory, located at the BC Cancer Research Institute\. The primary goal of Hematology Cell Bank of BC is to maintain the ideals of translational research by providing research scientists access to human bio\-specimens and related clinical data for research related to diseases such as myelodysplastic syndromes, bone marrow failure, leukemia, multiple myeloma, lymphoma \(and other hematologic malignancies/disorders\)\.

More information available at [Hematology Cell Bank of BC \| Division of Hematology](https://hematology.med.ubc.ca/research/hematology-cell-bank-of-bc/)
