## **Project Overview**

The Risk Evaluation and Screening to Tailor prevention and Reduce the incidence of Endometrial cancer \(RESToRE\) project, led by Dr\. Aline Talhouk, is a feasibility and acceptability study to explore a risk directed screening approach to reduce the incidence of endometrial cancer\. The feasibility and acceptability data from this pilot project will inform future clinical trials and decision\-making to significantly reduce the number of unnecessary endometrial biopsies and improve patient health outcomes\.
