# In\-person UBC Zero Waste Market

The in\-person Zero Waste Market at the UBC Vancouver Point Grey campus sells used \(second hand\) furniture, office supplies and more, at affordable prices\. A signed waiver is required for your purchase, see [terms and conditions](https://zero-waste-market.ubc.ca/terms-and-conditions/)\.

## Visit Us\! The Market is open to Everyone

[Image](https://sustain-zerowaste-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/09/Pond-E-Market-Entrance-940x705.jpeg)

**Tuesdays &amp; Wednesdays 11am – 2pm**

The market may be closed unexpectedly due to staff shortages or weather conditions\. Please check this page for updates on the day you plan to visit\.

## Location &amp; Parking

Ponderosa Annex E \(PONE\), Ponderosa Annex E, 2034 Lower Mall

[Image](https://sustain-zerowaste-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/parking-map_with-linev2_flatterened-scaled.jpg)

[Click to enlarge map](https://vpfo-facilities.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/07/furniture-reuse-map.jpg)

Parking is available at the [Fraser River Parkade](https://parking.ubc.ca/map)\. The entrance to Ponderosa Annex E via Lower Mall does not have a sidewalk\. To walk to Ponderosa Annex E use the West Mall sidewalk \(red path in the map above\)\.

## Payment

[Image](https://sustain-zerowaste-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/TEO-Office-2-940x705.jpg)

[Image](https://sustain-zerowaste-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/IMG_3863-scaled.jpg)

Purchases are as\-is and final sale\. Cash and cheque are not accepted\.

**UBC Faculties and departments:** pay by Workday Internal Service Delivery\.

**UBC community and public:** pay by Debit card ONLY\.

## **Collection: Online purchases cannot be picked\-up at the in\-person market but by appointment only\!**

[Image](https://sustain-zerowaste-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/IMG_E1243-8-940x705.jpg)

**Individuals and external organizations:** come prepared to transport your items at the time of purchase\. The Program cannot load purchases into your vehicle\.

**UBC Faculties and departments:** ask us about delivery options\.
