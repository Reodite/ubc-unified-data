## July 2009

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/unc-opens-2009-history.jpg)

The $33\-million, 7,300\-square\-metre University Centre opened\. A hub for student activity and engagement, the UNC includes student learning spaces, collegia lounges, the J\. Peter Meekison Student Centre, a sushi bar, student club space, offices for student service departments and a variety of social and gathering spaces\.
