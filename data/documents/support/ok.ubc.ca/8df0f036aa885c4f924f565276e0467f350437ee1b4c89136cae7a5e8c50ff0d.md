## January 2019

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2019/11/commons-opens-2019-history.jpg)

The $35\-million “Commons” facility opens boasting a spectacular 400\-seat classroom, hundreds of new study areas, a digital technology centre, a graduate student commons and a new café\. Students contributed nearly $10 million to the project, paving the way for future generations to enjoy a world\-class learning experience in a remarkable setting\.
