## October 2015

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/start-an-evolution-2015-history.jpg)

The University of British Columbia’s Okanagan campus completed the ambitious *start an evolution* campaign, which aimed to push cumulative fundraising for UBCO above $100 million and double the number of UBC alumni engaged with UBCO\. Ultimately, the campaign raised $100,096,345 and saw 1,600 alumni volunteer, attend special events, or give back to the university in other ways during the final year of the campaign\.
