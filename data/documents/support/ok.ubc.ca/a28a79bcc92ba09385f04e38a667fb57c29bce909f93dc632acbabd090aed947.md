Get involved with UBC Okanagan this summer—register for a summer camp or explore summer\-time learning opportunities on campus\.

[Two kids at an STEM summer camp](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2022/03/summer-camps-2022-STEM.jpg)

### iSTAND/WWEST STEM Camps

##### Youth, Ages 5–12

Neuroscience, space, coding and so much more\! Explore topics in science, technology, engineering and math \(STEM\)\.

[STEM Camps](https://wwest-cwse.ca/camp-landing-page/)

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2019/05/SOE-summer-camp.jpg)

### Engineering Camps

##### Youth, Grades K\-12

Foster skills in science, technology and engineering\. Explore in\-person and online camps for all ages\.

[Engineering Programs](https://engineering.ok.ubc.ca/programs-admissions/outreach-programs/)

[Two children engaging in UBCO Heat Sport camp](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/01/summer-camp-heat-sport-1000-667.png)

### Heat Sport Camps

##### Youth, Ages 5\-17

Have fun while learning sport\-specific skills and tactics in our Heat Sport Camps: basketball, volleyball, golf and more\!

[Sport Camps](https://goheat.ca/sports/2022/8/9/heat-sport-camps.aspx)

[Indigenous Summer Scholars working on a project\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/02/summer-camps-indigenous-summer-scholars-1000-667.jpg)

### Indigenous Summer Scholars and Leadership Camps

##### Youth, Ages 12\-17

Indigenous youth can experience holistic programming while exploring interests in post\-secondary education and leadership\.

[Indigenous Summer Camps](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/indigenous-students/summer-camps/)
