## September 2015

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/mou-ona-2015-okanagan-campus-celebrates-anniversaries-history.jpg)

A ceremony commemorating the UBC centennial and the 10\[th\] anniversary of the Okanagan campus is held\. As part of the ceremony, UBCO renewed its Memorandum of Understanding with the Okanagan Nation Alliance\.
