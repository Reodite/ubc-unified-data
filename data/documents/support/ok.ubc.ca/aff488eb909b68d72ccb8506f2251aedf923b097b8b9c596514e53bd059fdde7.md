## Why study at UBC’s Okanagan campus

At UBC Okanagan, you’ll earn a degree from one of the world’s top universities in a close\-knit community where your professors know your name and opportunities are within reach\.

Based in Kelowna, UBC Okanagan is just minutes away from an international airport—making it easy to stay connected to home, explore new places and build your future anywhere in the world\.
