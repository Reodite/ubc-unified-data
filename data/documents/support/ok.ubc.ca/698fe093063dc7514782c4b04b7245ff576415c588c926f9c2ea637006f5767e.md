## September 2025

[Incoming students gather on the sports field for group games\.](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2026/02/Jumpstart2024-Team-Time-my-064.jpg)

At Jump Start orientation, incoming students gather on the outdoor turf field for group games\.

UBC Okanagan kicks off a milestone year with record domestic enrolment\.

In the year of its 20th anniversary, UBCO welcomed 3,000 new students\. September saw a busy start, with a dynamic and diverse new cohort joining the campus\. In 2025, many of these students entered their first year, with others beginning graduate studies or transferring from other institutions\.
