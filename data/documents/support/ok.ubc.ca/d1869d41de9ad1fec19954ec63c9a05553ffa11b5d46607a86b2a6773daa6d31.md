## Why Choose UBCO?

[Aerial view of UBC Okanagan campus](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2022/04/ubco-aerials-campus-sunset.jpg)

### Top\-ranked global university

The passion and drive of our students and faculty members consistently place UBC in the top three per cent of universities in the world\.

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/02/faculty-and-staff-2020-1000x600-1.png)

### Close\-knit learning environment

UBC Okanagan combines a globally recognized UBC education with a tight\-knit and entrepreneurial campus community\.

[Co\-op students working in an orchard\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/01/co-op-students-feature-1000x600-1.jpg)

### Real\-world experience

UBCO moves learning beyond the classroom to help students build networks, shape their career aspirations and prepare for graduate studies\.

**[Professional Graduate Programs](https://ok.ubc.ca/academics/degrees-and-programs/professional-graduate-programs/)**

Explore multidisciplinary programs that introduce graduates to skills and connections from various sectors\.

**[Continuing &amp; Professional Education](http://cpe.ok.ubc.ca/)**

Gain knowledge and proficiencies that advance your career with Continuing and Professional Education\.
