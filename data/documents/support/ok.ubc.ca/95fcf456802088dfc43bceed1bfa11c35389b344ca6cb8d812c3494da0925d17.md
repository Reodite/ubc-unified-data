## English Foundation Program

The English Foundation Program \(EFP\) program combines English for academic purposes \(EAP\) classes, labs, tutorials, and other supports with a wide range of first\-year course options\. Now is the time to develop your advanced English language skills and bring your educational goals within reach\.
