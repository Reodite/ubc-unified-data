## March 2015

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/aerial-research-funding-tops-100-million-2015-history-1.jpg)

UBC’s Okanagan campus marked a major milestone when its cumulative total research funding surpassed $100 million\. The figure speaks to UBC Okanagan’s tremendous decade of growth; research funding totaled $6\.9 million in the campus’ initial fiscal year \(2005/06\)\.
