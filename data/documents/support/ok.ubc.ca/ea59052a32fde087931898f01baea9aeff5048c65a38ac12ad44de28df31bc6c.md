## April 2012

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/everest-nepal-research-expedition-2012-history.jpg)

UBC scientists from the School of Health and Exercise Sciences in the Faculty of Health and Social Development led a six\-week international research expedition to Mount Everest to examine high\-altitude health ailments\. The 25\-member team, with researchers from six countries, ascended to the Pyramid Laboratory near the Everest Base Camp and examined oxygen deprivation and reduced blood flow to better understand cardiovascular health\.
