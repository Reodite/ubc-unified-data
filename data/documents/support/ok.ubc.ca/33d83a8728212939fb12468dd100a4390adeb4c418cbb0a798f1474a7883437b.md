UBC Okanagan's close\-knit campus community is full of opportunities to fuel your passions, build connections and open new ways of thinking\.

## Make Yourself at Home

You’re going to spend a lot of time on campus during your studies\. That’s why UBC Okanagan’s campus offers a warm place to lay your head and food to keep you fed\.

[Two students walking down the road between Cascade residence buildings\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/05/student-life-cascades-exterior-1000x600-1.jpg)

### [Living in residence](http://okanagan.housing.ubc.ca/)

Stay on campus in a traditional single room, connected rooms, shared suite or townhouse\. Living in residence lets you shape your experience and make new connections\.

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/05/int-prospective-students-waving-1000x600-1.jpg)

### [Eating on campus](https://food.ok.ubc.ca/)

Made\-to\-order sandwiches, spicy samosas, sushi to\-go, rotating food trucks; vegetarian, vegan and allergy\-aware choices—there are plenty of options to keep you full and fuelled on campus\.

## Transit Around Kelowna

Get off campus and explore the Okanagan Valley\! There is plenty to see and do in downtown Kelowna, up the mountains or on the water\. And it’s just a short trip away\.

### [Transportation](https://transportation.ok.ubc.ca/)

Discover the sustainable options for travelling to, from and around UBC’s Okanagan campus\. Public transit, cycling, car\-sharing and parking are all available and easy to access\.

### [U\-Pass](https://suo.ca/u-pass)

Explore Kelowna and the surrounding area with the Universal Bus Pass \(U\-PASS\) included in student fees\. Buses arrive and depart from the centralized bus loop right on campus\.

[An aerial photo of Okanagan Lake and downtown Kelowna\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/08/aerial-kelowna-2023-1920x600-1.png)

### Explore the Okanagan

Visit local shops, lounge lakeside on the beach, go for a refreshing swim, hike up forested mountains or ski down powdery slopes\. The Okanagan is truly a four\-season playground\.

[Visit tourismkelowna\.com](https://tourismkelowna.com/)

## Stay Healthy and Well

Join a campus that’s passionate about providing a healthy, positive environment for students, faculty and staff\. At UBC Okanagan, we offer a welcoming space that’s supportive of everyone\.

[Two students speaking over a laptop\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/03/admissions-mega-menu.jpg)

### [Student Services](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/)

Searching for resources to support your student experience? Look no further for tips on improving your academic success, finding student jobs and charting your career plan, or getting more involved on campus\.

[A student using an elliptical machine in the Hanger\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/07/student-life-hanger-1000x600-1.jpg)

### [Campus Recreation](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/)

Access on\-campus services or join an intramural sports team\. Campus Recreation is your source for elevating your campus experience and helping you connect with others at the gym, on the field and more\.

### Health clinic and counselling

Maintain and support your health on campus with prescriptions, assessments, referrals, lab tests, or counselling\. Students can access everything they need to promote healthy wellbeing\.

Learn more at [students\.ok\.ubc\.ca/health\-wellness](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/health-wellness)

### Extended Health and Dental Plan

Students are automatically enrolled in the UBC Student Union Okanagan \(UBCSUO\) Extended Health and Dental Plan, which includes many services that are not covered by basic health care\.

Learn more at [suo\.ca/health\-dental](https://suo.ca/health-dental)
