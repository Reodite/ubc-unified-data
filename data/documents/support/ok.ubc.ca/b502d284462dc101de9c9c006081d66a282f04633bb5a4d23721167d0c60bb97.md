## September 2008

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/bc-premier-gordon-campbell-funds-for-new-buildings-2008-history.jpg)

British Columbia Premier Gordon Campbell announces nearly $100 million in provincial government funding that will allow construction of three major new projects—UBC’s Okanagan Health Sciences Centre, the Arts and Sciences 2 Building, and the Engineering/Management Building\.
