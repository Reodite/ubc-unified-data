Explore multidisciplinary programs that will equip you with the skills and connections needed for success in your professional career\.

## Further your education with UBC Okanagan

Continue your studies at one of the world’s top\-ranked universities\. At UBC Okanagan, you can pursue your professional graduate program while collaborating with world\-renowned professors and researchers across multiple disciplines\.

UBCO’s professional programs are designed to bring together students from a variety of educational backgrounds and industry experiences, to open up new ways of thinking as part of your graduate student journey\.

Learn more about program offerings, admission requirements and how you can apply\.

### [a graphic of a gene sequence under a microscope\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/10/Biotechnology-1000x600px.jpg)

### [Master of Biotechnology \(MBtec\)](https://biology.ok.ubc.ca/graduate/biotechnology/)

The Master of Biotechnology is designed to equip students with the skills and knowledge needed to thrive in the biotechnology industry\.

This program delivers a comprehensive education in disciplines that are core to biotechnology, while also emphasizing life\-long skills in critical thinking, hypothesis testing, project management, leadership and communications\.

---

### [Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/10/master-data-science-1000x600-1.jpg)

### [Master of Data Science \(MDS\)](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/programs/okanagan)

Covering all stages of the data science value chain, the Master of Data Science prepares graduates to thrive in one of the world’s most in\-demand fields\.

Over 10 months, you’ll learn how to extract and analyze data in all its forms, turn data into knowledge and clearly communicate your recommendations to decision\-makers\.

---

### [Two women sit in front of a computer screen covered with AI\-generated graphics\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/09/showcase-media-release-masterofdesign-1000x600-1.jpg)

### [Master of Design \(MDes\)](https://engineering.ok.ubc.ca/programs-admissions/grad/master-of-design/)

MDes is a 12\-month program that prepares graduates to lead at the intersection of design, engineering, innovation and entrepreneurship\.

Designed for students from a range of academic backgrounds—including engineering, technology, business, the arts and creative disciplines—the program develops advanced capabilities in human\-centered design, creative problem solving, interdisciplinary collaboration and the application of emerging technologies\.

---

### [Sabre Cherkowski speaking with a student\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/11/featured-program-masters-education-1000x600-1.png)

### [Master of Education \(MEd\)](https://education.ok.ubc.ca/degrees-programs/masters/)

The Master of Education program is designed for professionals looking to advance their careers and enhance their skills to make a meaningful impact across diverse educational settings\.

In our innovative and flexible learning environments, students will collaborate with their peers, build strong professional networks, and, through their coursework, develop expertise in their chosen topic\.

---

### [An view over a group of students working on couches and tables\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2025/03/collaboration_1000x600.jpg)

### [Doctor of Education \(EdD\)](https://education.ok.ubc.ca/degrees-programs/doctor-of-education/)

The Doctor of Education \(EdD\) is designed to inform, empower and engage current and aspiring leaders\.

You will have the opportunity to create meaningful change in your community as you apply newly learned knowledge, contextualize practical insights as well as analyze and address problems of practice in your local contexts\.

---

### [A woman on a tablet device inspecting an engineering project\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/03/featured-program-engineering-grad-1000x600-1.jpg)

### [Master of Engineering \(MEng\)](https://engineering.ok.ubc.ca/programs-admissions/grad/master-of-engineering/)

Focus on innovation at one of Canada’s leading project\-based engineering schools\. Individualized attention and industry connections enable your success in civil, electrical and mechanical engineering\.

In addition to courses in specific technical areas, students take a variety of courses such as technical communications, ethics and leadership\.

---

### [Sumi SIddiqua talking with students\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/12/sumi-siddiqua-students-1000x600-1.jpg)

### [Master of Management \(MM\)](https://management.ok.ubc.ca/academic-programs/pemm/)

The Master of Management is a boundary\-pushing business program designed to give you a competitive edge in any industry\. Immerse yourself in bold thinking while exploring new ways of leading and managing alongside your peers in this blended, part\-time program\.

Ideal for working professionals, the Master of Management addresses the emerging issues of our time while learning with a global network of scholars and practitioners\.

---

### [Two students at a table at Third Space Charity\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2025/03/social-work-1000x600-1.jpg)

### [Master of Social Work \(MSW\)](https://socialwork.ok.ubc.ca/msw-program/master-of-social-work/)

In the Master of Social Work, you acquire a wide knowledge base and clinical skills for effective social work practice with individuals, families and communities\.

The program provides advanced field education so students can graduate job\-ready for professional leadership roles in clinical and direct\-practice organizations\.
