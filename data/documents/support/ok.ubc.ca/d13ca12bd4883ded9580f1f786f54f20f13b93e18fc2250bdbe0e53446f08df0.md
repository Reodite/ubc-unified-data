## **January 2010**

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/engineering2010.jpg)

The Canadian Engineering Accreditation Board \(CEAB\) grants full accreditation to the School of Engineering’s programs in civil, electrical and mechanical engineering\. The designation was something extra to celebrate for the 56 students who received the first\-ever Okanagan\-conferred UBC Bachelor of Applied Science degrees in June 2010\.
