**Jan\. 16, 2024 – 4:30 pm**

Due to current weather conditions, members of the UBCO community are reminded of [UBC Okanagan’s Winter Weather and ‘Snow Day’ Protocol](https://ur.ok.ubc.ca/communications-and-marketing/winter-weather-snow-day-protocol/)\.

For tips on navigating slippery walkways and staying safe in cold conditions, visit [hse\.ok\.ubc\.ca/health/cold\-ice](https://hse.ok.ubc.ca/health/cold-ice/)\.

Transit riders are advised to plan ahead\. For information on public transit conditions, visit [BC Transit](http://www.bctransit.com/) or download its [transit app](https://transitapp.com/)\.

For information on weather conditions in Kelowna, visit [Environment Canada](https://weather.gc.ca/city/pages/bc-48_metric_e.html)\.
