## March 2020

[COVID March 2020](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2021/05/milestones-covid.jpg)

UBCO curtails on\-campus activities for students, faculty and staff and pivots to online learning, research and remote working in mid\-March after the World Health Organization declares COVID\-19 a global pandemic on March 11\.
