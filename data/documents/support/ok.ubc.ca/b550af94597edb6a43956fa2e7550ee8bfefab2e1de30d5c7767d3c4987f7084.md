## June 2025

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/05/int-prospective-students-1000x600-4.jpg)

UBC Okanagan marks 20 years since its 2005 opening\. By 2025, UBCO had conferred over 26,690 total degrees and saw incredible growth\. The student population grew 240% since 2006, and its campus buildings grew by 360% since 2005\. The campus expanded its research, with a 299% increase in research funding since 2014/2015\.

Since 2005, UBCO has built a vibrant and inclusive campus community in partnership and collaboration with the Okanagan Nation Alliance\.
