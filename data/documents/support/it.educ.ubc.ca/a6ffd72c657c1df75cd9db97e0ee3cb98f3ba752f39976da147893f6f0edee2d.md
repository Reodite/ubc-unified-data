Technology training is available for UBC staff and faculty through [Lynda\.com](http://lynda.com/ "Lynda.com") , a service which offers an online collection of self\-guided instructional videos designed to provide staff and faculty with just\-in\-time resources to assist in a variety of areas, including updating software skills, learning new technology tools, and enhancing workplace business practices\.

Membership to [Lynda\.com](http://lynda.com/ "Lynda.com") is available free of charge to all UBC faculty and staff\. Access has been extended to **March 31, 2019**\.

Once enrolled, you will have unlimited access to your chosen tutorials for the duration of the pilot period, including such topics as:

-   Adobe Creative Cloud
-   Business Skills
-   Connect
-   Microsoft Office
-   Mobile Devices in the Classroom
-   Web Design
