### 2\. What are UBC IT's hours of operation?

-   8:00 am – 5:00 pm Monday \- Friday for all incident, request, provisioning, and support services\.
-   7:30 am – 8:00 pm Monday \- Friday for audio visual support services\.

### 3\. What services are covered?

See the Faculty of Education IT Service Catalogue at [it\.educ\.ubc\.ca](https://it.educ.ubc.ca/)\.

### 4\. How do I get a FASmail email account?

Please fill out the UBC IT SOS web form at [it\.ubc\.ca/sos](http://it.ubc.ca/sos) \.

### 5\. How do I get a Campus Wide Login \(CWL\) account?

You can create your [CWL account](http://cwl.ubc.ca/) at [cwl\.ubc\.ca](https://www.cwl.ubc.ca/SignUp/cwlsubscribe/SelfSubscribeIndex.do) \.

### 6\. Where can I go for a recommendation and/or quote for a new computer/printer?

Fill out the SOS web form at [it\.ubc\.ca/sos](http://it.ubc.ca/sos) with your request/requirements and a member of the desktop support team will respond with a recommendation and/or quote\.

See the [UBC IT Service Catalogue](https://it.ubc.ca/services/desktop-print-services/desktop-and-laptop-setup-and-support/desktop-laptop-and-hardware) for additional information about hardware purchases\.

### 7\. How do I get access to a shared drive?

Fill out the UBC IT SOS web form at [it\.ubc\.ca/sos](http://it.ubc.ca/sos)

See the [UBC IT Service Catalogue](https://it.ubc.ca/services/web-servers-storage/teamshare-storage-service) for additional information about the Teamshare service\.

### 8\. How do I book equipment for my class?

The [Chapman Learning Commons](http://learningcommons.ubc.ca/tech-support-2/equipment-lending/?login), located in Room 300 in the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre, offers a variety of computer and AV equipment for loan\.

### 9\. How do I book a computer lab for my class?

Fill out the [Computer Lab Booking web form](https://it-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/book-a-computer-lab/), or contact [educ\.labs@ubc\.ca](mailto:educ.labs@ubc.ca)\.

### 10\. I'm having problems with equipment in my classroom, where do I get help?

Submit a ticket using the UBC IT SOS web form at [it\.ubc\.ca/sos](http://it.ubc.ca/sos) , or contact the UBC IT AV Help Desk at 604\.822\.7956 or [av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:av.helpdesk@ubc.ca)\.

### 11\. Where do I pick up my Xerox printing?

Xerox deliveries will be made to TEO reception \(Scarfe 103\), or can be arranged directly to the receiving department\.

### 13\. My phone isn't working, whom do I contact?

-   Call 611 from an on\-campus phone
-   Call 604\.822\.2101 from an off\-campus phone

### 14\. How do I get website support?

For WordPress support, submit a CMS ticket at [support\.cms\.ubc\.ca/submit\-question\-form](http://support.cms.ubc.ca/submit-question-form/) \.

For non\-WordPress support or new site requests, complete the UBC IT Web Services form at [web\.it\.ubc\.ca/forms/webservices](http://web.it.ubc.ca/forms/webservices) \.

### 15\. How do I connect to 'ubcsecure'?

Step by step instructions are available at the [UBC IT website](http://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/wireless-internet-access/ubcsecure-wireless-network) \.

### 16\. How do I connect using myVPN?

Step by step instructions are available at the [UBC IT website](http://www.it.ubc.ca/service-catalogue/internet-and-telephone/network-management/myvpn/setup-documents) \.

### 17\. If I have an issue or concern related to UBC IT services, whom do I contact?

Contact Baljit Chahal, UBC IT Senior Client Services Manager, at baljit\.chahal@ubc\.ca or 604\.822\.3790\.
