### 2013/02/12

---

Sent **February 12, 2013** by **Dean Blye Frank** to the educ\-faculty, educ\-staff, and educ\-sessional mailing lists

---

Hello everyone:

Over the past several years we have been reviewing our IT environment with the goal of transforming our support of teaching and research\. With the support of UBC IT we undertook a strategic assessment of our IT environment in 2011, and a technology audit late last fall\. Through this process we have developed a better understanding of our overall needs\.

In order to take advantage of a scalable IT environment, access diverse skillsets and develop a more accountable structure, we will be partnering with UBC IT for the delivery of our IT operations\. While we have determined that UBC IT should manage our operations, we still need to define the overall structure, which we will do after consulting across the Faculty\. Our existing LTS and web staff will be reporting to UBC IT effective Feb 12, but they will remain physically located in their current location and will continue to provide day\-to\-day support\.

Norm Daoust has left the Faculty of Education\. We recognize Norm’s contributions and wish him well in his future endeavors\.

Long term, we anticipate that this change will benefit both FoE and our staff, providing them with access to a community of practice, with opportunities for professional and personal development\.

If you have any questions regarding this change, please feel free to contact myself, or Colin Sharwood at [Colin\.sharwood@ubc\.ca](mailto:colin.sharwood@ubc.ca) and Jennifer Burns at [Jennifer\.burns@ubc\.ca](mailto:jennifer.burns@ubc.ca) \(UBC IT\)\.

   
Blye
