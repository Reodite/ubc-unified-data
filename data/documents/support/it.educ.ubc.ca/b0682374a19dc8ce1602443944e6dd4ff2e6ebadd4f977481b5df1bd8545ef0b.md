## Academic Classroom Bookings

For room bookings related to Faculty of Education academic courses on the Vancouver campus, please send a detailed request to [Jennifer Stewart](mailto:jennifer.stewart@ubc.ca "Email jennifer.stewart@ubc.ca") in the Teacher Education Office\.

To view a list of available classrooms, click a building link below, then scroll down and click a classroom link for full details, including classroom images, seating capacity, and AV equipment:

[Neville Scarfe Building](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/buildings/neville-scarfe-building-scrf/)

[Ponderosa Commons](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/buildings/ponderosa-commons-north-pcn/)

Please note that the [School of Kinesiology](http://kin.educ.ubc.ca/) manages its own spaces\.

## Other Classroom Bookings

For all other classroom bookings, please email requests to [Scheduling Services](mailto:schedulingservices@students.ubc.ca "Email schedulingservices@students.ubc.ca")\.

## Meeting Room Bookings

For meeting room bookings, including available rooms and booking processes, please consult [this document](https://it-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/02/Guidelines-for-Room-Bookings-Faculty-of-Education-Jan-8-24.pdf)\.
