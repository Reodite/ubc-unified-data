The Faculty of Education’s central teaching and learning studios may be booked for Education courses and/or workshops\.  
*We are not able to accommodate external booking requests*\.

### To reserve a teaching and learning studio:

-   Bookings must be made **at least 3 business days in advance** of the requested date\(s\)\.  
    Confirmations are sent via email within **2 business days**\.
-   Review the [information for instructors](https://it-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/teaching-and-learning-studio-use-policy/ "Computer Lab Use Policy")\.
-   Check the [Teaching and Learning Studio Schedules](https://it.educ.ubc.ca/teaching-and-learning-studio-schedules/) for availability
-   Fill out the booking form below\.
-   If you are unfamiliar with the Faculty of Education central teaching and learning studios, it is strongly recommend that you visit the teaching and learning studio at least one week before your booking to familiarize yourself with the facilities\.

*Note: As per the Vision of the Teaching and Learning Studios, only one of the two rooms, between Scarfe 1006 &amp; Scarfe 1007 is bookable at any one time to ensure that space is available for drop\-in student use\.* 

Please contact [dnso\.facilities@ubc\.ca](mailto:dnso.facilities@ubc.ca) if you require additional information or wish to change or cancel your studio booking\.

\* *required fields*
