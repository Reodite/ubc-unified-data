### 2014/08/25

Effective August 25, 2014, users will no longer need to sign up for a computer lab account in order to use the Faculty of Education central lab computers\.

The Windows and Mac systems have been migrated to UBC’s Enterprise Active Directory \(EAD\), a centralized authentication and authorization system that synchronizes user accounts, password and related information using [Campus\-Wide Login \(CWL\)](http://it.ubc.ca/node/85 "CWL Account")\. The migration will enable improved IT support and enhanced security\.

Now that the computers have been migrated to EAD, users will login into the lab computers with their [Campus\-Wide Login \(CWL\)](http://it.ubc.ca/node/85 "CWL Account")\.

A temporary guest EAD account will be made available to instructors only to facilitate access for classes booked into the computer labs\.
