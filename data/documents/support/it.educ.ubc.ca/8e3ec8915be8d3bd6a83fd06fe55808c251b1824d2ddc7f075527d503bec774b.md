The Faculty of Education’s Van may be booked for the Faculty’s operational needs\.  
*We are not able to accommodate external booking requests*\.

### To reserve the van:

-   Bookings must be made **at least 1 week in advance** of the requested date\.  
    Confirmations are sent via email within **2 business days**\.
-   Review the van user agreement\.
-   Check the [Faculty of Education van calendar](https://it-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/faculty-of-education-van-booking-calendar/) for availability\.
-   Fill out the booking form below\.

Please contact [dnso\.facilities@ubc\.ca](mailto:dnso.facilities@ubc.ca) if you require additional information or wish to change or cancel your van booking\.

\* *required fields*
