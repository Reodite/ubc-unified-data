Conferencing services for audio and web\-based collaboration is provided by UBC IT Voice Services through TELUS\.

The TELUS conference bridge allows subscribers to:

-   Create large ad\-hoc or operator attended conferences
-   Share documents and other materials online

Access is available through local and international toll\-free numbers\.

### Getting Started

-   Contact your Departmental Voice Administrator to request a TELUS teleconferencing account through Pinnacle\. It can take up to 48 hours for your account to be provisioned\.
-   [Costs](http://it.ubc.ca/node/2271 "Costs")  *\(Note: Due to confidentiality, this information is intended for a UBC audience and can only be viewed on a UBC network\)*
-   [Voice Administrators – Managing Teleconferencing Requests](http://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/teleconferencing/managing-teleconferencing-requests "Managing Teleconferencing Requests")
-   [General Information](http://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/teleconferencing "Teleconferencing")

### Support

-   If you need support during a conference call, press “\*0” to speak to a TELUS teleconference representative\.  
     
-   For inquiries regarding teleconferencing, contact UBC IT Voice Services:
    -   Via phone: 604\.822\.2555
    -   Via [web form](http://web.it.ubc.ca/forms/isf/ "Contact Voice Services")
