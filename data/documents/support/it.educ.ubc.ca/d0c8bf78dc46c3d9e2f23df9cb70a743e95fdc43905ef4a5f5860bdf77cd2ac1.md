*Sent **November 8, 2013** on behalf of **Dr\. Tom Sork** to the educ\-faculty, educ\-staff, and educ\-sessional mailing lists*

---

Subject: **Audio Visual Services Transition Update**

Dear Colleagues,

As a follow\-up to my email of [September 16th](https://it.educ.ubc.ca/audio-visual-services-transition-update/), I can provide further details about the transition of Audio Visual support to UBC IT\.

Effective **Tuesday, November 12th, 2013**, AV Support Services will be transferred to UBC IT\.  The contact information for various services is:

| Service | Supported By | Contact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desktop support, FASmail, servers, storage, applications | UBC IT | 604\.827\.HELP or [SOS Form](http://it.ubc.ca/sos) |
| Websites, database development | UBC IT | [Web Services Form](http://web.it.ubc.ca/forms/webservices) |
| Technology\-enhanced teaching &amp; learning | ETS | 604\.822\.6333 or [ets\.educ@ubc\.ca](mailto:ets.educ@ubc.ca) |
| AV services | UBC IT, AV Services | 822\.7956 or [av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:av.helpdesk@ubc.ca) |

UBC IT Audio Visual Services has established a zone service hub in Scarfe Room 1, consisting of three AV technicians\.  UBC IT staff will honour equipment bookings until **Friday November 29th**, at which time the desk will close\.  In January, the AV hub will relocate to space within Scarfe, sharing with UBC IT Desktop Services\. The equipment in stock is being distributed to units that use it most often\.  Please discuss with your Department or Unit Administrator if you have concerns, or email [colin\.sharwood@ubc\.ca](mailto:colin.sharwood@ubc.ca)\.

Xerox deliveries will be made to TEO reception, or can be arranged directly to the receiving department\.

Once again, we express our thanks to Larry and Edna for the dedicated support they have provided over the years\.

For questions about the AV Services Transition, please contact Colin Sharwood at [colin\.sharwood@ubc\.ca](mailto:colin.sharwood@ubc.ca)\.

   
Tom\.
