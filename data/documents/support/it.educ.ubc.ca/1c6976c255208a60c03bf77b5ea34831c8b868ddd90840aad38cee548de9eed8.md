For over 50 years, UBC’s Faculty of Education has served the international education community through leadership in research, service, and advocacy\. One of the leading faculties of education in the world, we prepare professionals for practice in a wide range of education\-related fields, from preschool through adulthood\.

More than 45% of BC’s elementary teachers, the majority of secondary teachers, and a significant proportion of BC’s special needs and First Nations teachers, counsellors, school administrators, school psychologists, and vocational rehabilitation counsellors, are prepared by the UBC Faculty of Education for their important roles\.
