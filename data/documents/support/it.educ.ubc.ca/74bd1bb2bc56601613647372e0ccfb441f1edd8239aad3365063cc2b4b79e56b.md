### 2012/09/05

---

Faculty of Education **Learning &amp; Technology Services \(LTS\)** is the new name that replaces Computing and Media Services \(CMS\)\.

The new name reflects a long\-term goal to bring closer together both IT and Educational Technology / e\-Learning Services\. The new name marks a first step toward integration that will be followed by additional changes and enhancements to services over the next year\.

Note, there have been no changes to IT staffing or office locations\.
