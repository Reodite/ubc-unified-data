Computers, hard drives, and other end\-of\-life technical devices which store data are decommissioned by UBC IT\. Equipment is re\-purposed or disposed of in an environmentally friendly manner, and appropriate security measures are taken prior to re\-purposing/disposal, including hard drive reformatting\.

For disposal of electronic equipment without data storage, an eWaste Bin has been provided in the Scarfe basement \(by the main staircase – classroom block\)\.

The most common items that can be disposed in the eWaste bin include:

-   Computer Terminals &amp; Computer Parts
-   Desktop Computers
-   Laptops &amp; Tablets
-   Keyboards &amp; Mice
-   Monitors
-   Printers
-   Zip drives &amp; External Hard Drives

This service is available for devices owned by departments or units in the Faculty of Education\.

[UBC Policy \#108, Disposal of Surplus Equipment and Materials](http://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/files/2010/08/policy108.pdf "UBC Policy #108")

### Complete list of accepted eWaste Bin items

> **Have you wiped the data off the device before discarding it?**
> 
> If there is research, student, personnel, or other confidential data on a computer, disk, or device, it must be securely wiped before you can dispose of it\. [Contact UBC IT](https://it.educ.ubc.ca/hardware-decommissioning/#ubc-it) to ensure that your data has been securely wiped before disposing in the eWaste Bin\.
