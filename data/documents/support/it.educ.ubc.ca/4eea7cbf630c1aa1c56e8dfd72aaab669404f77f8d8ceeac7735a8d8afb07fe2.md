### 2013/11/04

---

Sent **November 4, 2013** on behalf of **Colin Sharwood, Director of Finance,** to all Faculty of Education students

---

Dear Students,

Effective **Tuesday November 12th**, Education AV Services will transition to UBC IT\. The following changes may impact you:

-   The print pilot to the library printers on the ground floor will be implemented\. The printers in Room 1 will be de\-commissioned, and not be available for printing from the computer labs\.
-   Please use up the balances in your print accounts, and begin printing in the library\.
-   If necessary, refunds may be arranged through Edna Johnson in Room 1\.
-   Small balances remaining in accounts that are not claimed may be donated to United Way in the new year\.
-   The equipment loan desk managed by Edna will close by November 29th\.
-   The GAA support in Room 1 will be re\-deployed next term\.
-   The laminating, large format poster printing &amp; sales will also discontinue\.

Thank you for your attention\.

Regards,
