UBC classrooms are centrally supported by UBC IT AV Services, including University and departmentally\-managed classrooms in the following buildings:

[Neville Scarfe Building](http://students.ubc.ca/campus/discover/buildings-and-classrooms/SCRF)

[Ponderosa Commons](http://students.ubc.ca/campus/discover/buildings-and-classrooms/PCOM)

The [School of Kinesiology](http://kin.educ.ubc.ca/) manages their own spaces\.

Support includes issues with projectors and other audio visual equipment\.

Most centrally managed classrooms in the buildings listed above are equipped with an overhead projector, mounted data projector, and a combination DVD/VCR\. See the links below for detailed information\.

The following are a list of classrooms, both centrally and departmentally\-controlled in Scarfe and Ponderosa Commons:  

### Classroom Services Managed Classrooms

### Classroom Services Managed Classrooms

Click a building link to view a list of available classrooms, then scroll down and click a classroom link for full details, including classroom images, seating capacity, and AV equipment:

[Neville Scarfe Building](http://students.ubc.ca/campus/discover/buildings-and-classrooms/SCRF)

[Ponderosa Commons](http://students.ubc.ca/campus/discover/buildings-and-classrooms/PCOM)

Contact the [Teacher Education Office](http://teach.educ.ubc.ca/) or [Scheduling Services](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/enrolment-services/student-records-systems-management/scheduling-services) to book rooms in these buildings\.
