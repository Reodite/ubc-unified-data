### 2012/11/01

---

LTS is piloting access to the Education Library printer from its labs\.

All computers in LTS facilities \(Scarfe 155, 1006, 1007\) can now print to the Education Library print queues\.

To use these printers:

-   print to either the Black and White or Colour Print Queue
-   go to the Education Library Print Station, insert your copy card and login with the user **grizzly**
-   release your job to the printer

For more information see the [UBC Library How to Print pages](http://services.library.ubc.ca/computers-technology/how-to-print/)\.
