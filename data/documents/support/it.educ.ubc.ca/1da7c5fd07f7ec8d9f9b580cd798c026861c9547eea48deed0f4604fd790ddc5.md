### 2013/09/16

---

Sent **September 16, 2013** on behalf of **Dr\. Tom Sork** to the educ\-faculty, educ\-staff, and educ\-sessional mailing lists

---

Subject: **Audio Visual Services Transition Update**

Dear Colleagues,

As communicated previously by Dean Blye Frank on February 12th, 2013, and me on May 14th, 2013, we are in the final steps of transitioning  IT operational support for the Faculty of Education to UBC IT\.

We have completed the review of Audio Visual Services and have today begun to transition these services to UBC IT\.  The anticipated completion date will be mid\-November, 2013\.

Edna Johnson and Larry DeFehr will be leaving the Faculty at the end of the transition period\.  I would like to take this opportunity to acknowledge their long and loyal service to the Faculty, with Edna recently celebrating 35 years of service\. Both Edna and Larry will continue to serve your needs during the transition\.

There is no immediate change to the service delivery model which was described on May 14th\.  Please continue to request support through the contact methods outlined below:

| Service | Supported By | Contact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desktop support, FASmail, servers, storage, applications | UBC IT | 604\.827\.HELP or [SOS Form](http://it.ubc.ca/sos) |
| Websites, database development | UBC IT | [Web Services Form](http://web.it.ubc.ca/forms/webservices) |
| Technology\-enhanced teaching &amp; learning | ETS | 604\.822\.6333 or [ets\.educ@ubc\.ca](mailto:ets.educ@ubc.ca) |
| AV services, equipment loans | Larry DeFehr and Edna Johnson | 604\.822\.5372 or [educ\.av@ubc\.ca](mailto:educ.av@ubc.ca) |

During the transition, UBC IT Audio Visual Services will establish a zone service hub in Room 1, consisting of three AV technicians\.  We will communicate further when there are changes to service delivery\.

Once again, I would like to express my appreciation, on behalf of the Faculty, to Larry and Edna\. We will miss their cheerful support\.

For questions about the AV Services Transition, please contact Colin Sharwood at [colin\.sharwood@ubc\.ca](mailto:colin.sharwood@ubc.ca)\.
