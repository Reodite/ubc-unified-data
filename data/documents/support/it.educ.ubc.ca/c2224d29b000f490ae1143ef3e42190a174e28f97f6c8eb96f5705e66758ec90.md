## Website Hosting

UBC website hosting is provided on the central UBC Content Management System \(CMS\)\. This service is based on WordPress, and comes with Campus Wide Login \(CWL\)\-enabled authentication and a UBC Common Look and Feel \(CLF\) theme\.

Drupal hosting support is available for legacy Faculty of Education unit websites\.

Requests for websites should be submitted by unit administrators, heads of units, or faculty members leading projects\.  
[Request a Website](http://web.it.ubc.ca/forms/webservices/ "Request a Website")

[UBC Content Management System \(CMS\)](https://cms.ubc.ca/about/wordpress-initiative/ "UBC Content Management System")

[UBC Brand \(CLF\)](http://brand.ubc.ca/working-with-our-brand/web/ "UBC Brand (CLF)")

## Website Host Names

UBC IT Web Services manages the Faculty of Education domain space, *\*\.educ\.ubc\.ca*:

-   Education unit hostnames are of the format *unit\.educ\.ubc\.ca* or *subunit\.unit\.educ\.ubc\.ca*\.
-   Significant projects or research projects are normally of the format *project\.unit\.educ\.ubc\.ca* or *project\.research\.educ\.ubc\.ca*\.
-   Faculty member \(tenure track\) sites are normally of the format *name\.faculty\.educ\.ubc\.ca* or *name\.unit\.educ\.ubc\.ca*\.

Requests for website hostnames should be submitted by unit administrators, heads of units, or faculty members leading projects\.  
[UBC Domain Guidelines](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/subdomain-registration "UBC Domain Guidelines")

## Website Content Updates

Faculty of Education website content is normally maintained by the site owner\.

For training and support for using WordPress, see the schedule of workshops and drop\-in clinics offered by the [Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology \(CTLT\)](http://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/ "CTLT Workshop Schedule") \.

UBC IT Web Services can provide assistance with content updates for special requests on a fee\-for\-service basis\. Requests should be sent by the website owner\.  
[CMS Support](http://support.cms.ubc.ca/ "CMS Support")

[CTLT Workshop Schedule](http://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/ "CTLT Workshop Schedule")

[UBC IT Web Services](http://it.ubc.ca/services/web-servers-storage/websites "UBC IT Web Services")

## Website Development &amp; Management

UBC IT Web Services designs, develops, and maintains websites and web\-applications on desktop and mobile devices on a fee\-for\-service basis\.

Requests for web services should be submitted by unit administrators, heads of units, or faculty members leading projects\.  
[UBC IT Web Services](http://it.ubc.ca/services/web-servers-storage/websites "UBC IT Web Services")
