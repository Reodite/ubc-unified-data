### 2013/03/25

---

Sent **March 25, 2013** on behalf of **Dr\. Tom Sork, Senior Associate Dean, International and Administration,** to the educ\-faculty, educ\-staff, and educ\-sessional mailing lists

---

Hello All,

On February 14th, Dean Blye Frank announced that IT operational support for the Faculty of Education would transition to UBC IT\. The IT team has spent the last month working to develop a new IT model\. During this time, the team has:

-   Held 11 sessions with 43 staff and faculty to review requirements for the new IT model
-   Continued to provide IT support services
-   Investigated establishing a IT support hub in the Scarfe building
-   Worked with Dr\. Mark Edwards and Dr\. Natasha Boskic to determine the support to be provided for learning services

To complete the information gathering phase, we would like to invite you to attend a session to review the proposed changes, and receive your feedback\.

**Two sessions are being offered:**

-   **Wednesday, March 27th, 1:30pm\-2:30pm , Scarfe 310**
-   **Wednesday, April 10th, 1:30pm\-2:30pm, Scarfe 310**

**Please click on the link below to register for the session that you would like to attend\.**

[events\.educ\.ubc\.ca/it\-model/](http://events.educ.ubc.ca/it-model/)

All the best\. Tom\.
