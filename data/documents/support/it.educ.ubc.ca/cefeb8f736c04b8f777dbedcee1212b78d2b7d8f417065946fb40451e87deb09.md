UBC IT AV Services provides assistance with video capturing lectures and events, and can also provide video recording services for non\-event/lecture requests, such as head interviews\. This is offered on a limited basis and needs to be booked well in advance\.

In addition, video assets can be edited, formatted and/or duplicated for a variety of destinations including websites, DVD and Blu\-ray\. Turnaround times will vary with availability\.
