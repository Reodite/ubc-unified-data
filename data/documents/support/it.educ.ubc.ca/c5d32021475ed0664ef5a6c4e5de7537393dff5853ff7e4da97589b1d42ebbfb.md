*Sent **January 15, 2014** on behalf of **Colin Sharwood, Director of Finance,** to all Faculty of Education students\.*

---

Subject: **Registering for Computer Lab Accounts**

Dear Students,

You can now register for computer lab accounts in the Education Library\. All the public computers now have access\.

Please log on to:  [lab\.cms\.educ\.ubc\.ca:3000/register/](http://lab.cms.educ.ubc.ca:3000/register/ "Register for a Computer Lab Account")

[https://it\-educ\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2014/02/account\-registration\.jpg](https://it-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/02/account-registration.jpg)[Click on "Register for a Lab Account"](https://it-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/02/account-registration.jpg)

[https://it\-educ\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2014/02/register\-for\-account\.jpg](https://it-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/02/register-for-account.jpg)[Click on "Register for a Lab Account"](https://it-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/02/register-for-account.jpg)

Note that it can take up to one business day for computer lab account registrations to be processed\.

Please carefully record your username and password, since there may be a delay if you need to recover it\. The recovery procedure is as follows:

Using the email account that you have registered with UBC \([students\.ubc\.ca](http://students.ubc.ca/ "students.ubc.ca")\), send an email message to [educ\.labs@ubc\.ca](mailto:educ.labs@ubc.ca "Email educ.labs@ubc.ca") requesting to recover your Education computer lab password\. Include the following information:

-   Full name
-   Computer lab account username
-   Student number

After your information has been verified, the password will be emailed back to your UBC\-registered email address by the end of the next business day, unless otherwise notified\.
