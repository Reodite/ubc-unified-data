### 2014/07/09

---

Sent **July 9, 2014** by **Tracy Wyman, Project Director** to the educ\-faculty, educ\-staff, and educ\-sessional mailing lists

---

The Faculty of Education computer labs, Scarfe 1006, 1007, 155 \(Library\), will be closed during the period, **August 18 – 22** for a computer system migration\.

The Windows and Mac systems will be migrated to UBC’s Enterprise Active Directory \(EAD\)\. EAD is a centralized authentication and authorization system that synchronizes user accounts, password and related information using [Campus Wide Login \(CWL\)](http://it.ubc.ca/node/85 "CWL Account")\. The migration will enable improved IT support and enhanced security\.

A key benefit of the migration is that users will no longer need to sign up for a computer lab account in order to use the lab computers\. Once the computers are migrated to EAD, users will be able to login into the lab computers with their [Campus Wide Login \(CWL\)](http://it.ubc.ca/node/85 "CWL Account")\.

> The 150MB of personal storage that is associated with existing computer lab user accounts will no longer be available after the migration\. Please take steps to transfer any files that you require from personal storage to another storage location or device by **August 14**\.
