Sent **May 14, 2013** by **Dr\. Tom Sork** to the educ\-faculty, educ\-staff, and educ\-sessional mailing lists

---

Subject: **IT Transition Update for FoE Faculty and Staff**

Dear Colleagues,

As communicated previously, we are in the process of transitioning IT operational support for the Faculty of Education to UBC IT\. As part of this transition, a new IT model has been developed and reviewed with stakeholders\. As a result, there will be some changes in organization that will affect service offerings; in particular, how to request and receive IT support\.

In addition, a new Educational Technology Support \(ETS\) unit has been formed\. The team, managed by Dr\. Natasha Boskic, is responsible for instructional design, online course development, blended learning support and WebCT/Connect support\. Team members include the following staff: Ian Linkletter, Sharon Hu and Andrea Hankinson\.

**Effective today, May 14, 2013, you can request IT services support through the contact methods outlined below**:

| Service | Supported By | Contact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desktop support, FASmail, servers, storage, applications | UBC IT | 604\.827\.HELP or [SOS Form](http://it.ubc.ca/sos) |
| Websites, database development | UBC IT | [Web Services Form](http://web.it.ubc.ca/forms/webservices) |
| Technology\-enhanced teaching &amp; learning | ETS | 604\.822\.6333 or [ets\.educ@ubc\.ca](mailto:ets.educ@ubc.ca) |
| AV services, equipment loans | Larry DeFehr and Edna Johnson | 604\.822\.5372 or [educ\.av@ubc\.ca](mailto:educ.av@ubc.ca) |

The complete list of service offerings along with contact information can be found in the [service catalog\.](https://it.educ.ubc.ca/)

If you have concerns about any of the services provided by UBC IT, Balli Chahal is our Client Services Manager; he can be reached at [baljit\.chahal@ubc\.ca](mailto:baljit.chahal@ubc.ca) or 604\.822\.3790\.

For questions about the new support model, please contact Colin Sharwood at [colin\.sharwood@ubc\.ca](mailto:colin.sharwood@ubc.ca) or Jennifer Burns at [jennifer\.burns@ubc\.ca](mailto:jennifer.burns@ubc.ca)\.

Attached is information about the issues escalation process now in place\.

   
Tom\.
