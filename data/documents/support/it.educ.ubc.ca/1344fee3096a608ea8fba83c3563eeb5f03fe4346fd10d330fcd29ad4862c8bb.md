Printing from the three central Faculty of Education [computer labs](https://it.educ.ubc.ca/computer-labs/ "Computer Labs") is now available via UBC\`s new [Pay for Print](https://payforprint.ubc.ca:9192/user? "Pay 2 Print") service\.

Pay for Print allows students, faculty, staff to print, copy, and scan documents and pay via a centralized payment system using the [UBCcard](http://ubccard.ubc.ca/ "UBCcard") \. Specially marked Pay for Print printers are available in various locations across campus, including the Education Library in the Neville Scarfe Building\.

An EAD\-enabled [Campus Wide Login \(CWL\)](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl "CWL") account and an activated [UBCcard](http://ubccard.ubc.ca/ "UBCcard") with funds available are required to use the Pay to Print service:

-   Students use their Campus Wide Login \(CWL\) username suffixed with “\.stu”:  **cwl\.stu**
-   Faculty and staff use their CWL username:  **cwl**
