### 2013/03/01

---

On February 12, 2013, Dean Blye Frank announced that IT support for the Faculty of Education would transition to UBC IT\. Currently, all IT operations activity is being managed by UBC IT Client Service Manager, Balli Chahal\. [Click here](https://it.educ.ubc.ca/it-announcement/) for a copy of the email\.

Day\-to\-day operational IT support remains the same, and you can get help by contacting us as always\. We are currently developing a new IT model, but until the model is confirmed and implemented, we’ll continue to operate as usual\.

## IT Support

IT support, including audio\-visual and web services, are provided by the former LTS staff\. For Education online courses, this team will continue to be responsible for adding users and changing course access dates\. Note, course shell creation is now an automatic process in the new LMS, Connect\. The current list of services that are supported by the team can be found online at [lts\.educ\.ubc\.ca](http://lts.educ.ubc.ca/)\. Day\-to\-day IT support can be accessed in the same manner as before via email at [lts\.educ@ubc\.ca](mailto:lts.educ@ubc.ca), or via telephone at 604\.822\.3700\. The people working in this unit are: Adrian Bartel, David Brabbins, Paul Chan, Larry DeFehr, Vicki Domansky, Edna Johnson, Roman Piotrowski, Michael Shepard, and Sue Wood\. Issues or concerns about IT support can be escalated to Balli Chahal, UBC IT Client Service Manager\. Balli can be reached at [baljit\.chahal@ubc\.ca](mailto:baljit.chahal@ubc.ca) or 604\.360\.8450\.

## Teaching and Learning IT Support

Instructional design, online course development, and blended learning support\. People working in this unit include: Natasha Boskic, Sharon Hu, and Ian Linkletter\. Issues or concerns can be escalated to Natasha Boskic at [natasha\.boskic@ubc\.ca](mailto:natasha.boskic@ubc.ca) or 604\.822\.1831\.
