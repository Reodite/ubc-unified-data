### Telephone Moves

With the conversion to Voice\-over\-Internet \(VoIP\) telephones, faculty and staff are now able to move their phones themselves by simply unplugging the phone in the old location, then plugging it into an active Internet port in the new location\.

The connection order is:     wall  &gt;&gt;  telephone  &gt;&gt;  computer

> Please note: In order to maintain 911 records, Department Voice Administrators must submit a Pinnacle move request indicating the new location of the telephone\. UBC IT Voice Services technicians will not attend a VoIP move request; this is for records purposes only\.
