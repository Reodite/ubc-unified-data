### Style Sheet

When formatting a paper to submit to the UBCWPL for publication, please refer to the  
style sheet below:

-   [Style Sheet](https://lingpapers.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/02/ubcwpl-stylesheet_0228.pdf)

UBCWPL style sheet\-adherent templates are provided in Office Open XML \(\.docx\) format \(compatible with Microsoft Word and OpenOffice\.org word processors\) as well as LaTeX \(\.tex\) format\. \(Note that the templates are provided as \.ZIP archives of directories containing both the template file and its read\-only PDF counterpart\.\)

-   [Microsoft Word \(\.zip\)](https://lingpapers.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/03/ubcwpl-msword-template-20190305.zip)
-   [LaTeX \(\.zip\)](https://lingpapers.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/03/ubcwpl-latex-template-20190305.zip)
