Dear valued patients,

We want to inform you of an upcoming adjustment to our service pricing\. Like many organizations, we are experiencing the impact of rising operational costs due to inflation and other real\-world economic factors\. To continue providing the high\-quality service you expect, we need to revise our pricing structure\.

Our price adjustment will take effect on October 6, 2025\. We remain committed to being transparent and fair, and our goal is to ensure that our services remain accessible while maintaining the standards you rely on\.

Thank you for your continued support and understanding\.

***The PTRC Team***

## Our affordability rates will stay the same\.

### Affordability Rate

| Appointment Type | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Student PT | 50 |
| Licensed PT | 50 |
