Sports Injuries &amp; Rehabilitation is specifically catered to helping athletes return to their sporting pastimes and passions\. Assessment of movement patterns, strength, and mobility is combined to create an individualized treatment plan that is designed to help athletes return to their sport/activity pain\-free\.

[Physical therapy](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/services/) for sports injuries can also emphasize injury prevention by focusing on strengthening and mobility exercises that fine\-tune the body to meet the demands of the sport\. From recreational athletes to Olympic contenders, sports injury services can be beneficial for anyone who wishes to participate in any sport, at any level\. 

## Benefits of Sports Injuries &amp; Rehabilitation:

-   Quicken returns to practice and play in your sport 

-   Decrease the risk of injury or re\-injury 

## Services:

-   [Exercise prescription/exercise therapy](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/exercise-therapy/)

-   [Manual therapy](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/manual-therapy/) \(joint mobilization/manipulation, soft tissue work, trigger point\) 

-   Return to sport testing 

-   Movement assessment to screen for injury risk
