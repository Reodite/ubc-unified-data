Cancer treatments vary greatly\. Functional changes can be caused by surgery, radiation and chemotherapy\. Managing side effects through cancer rehabilitation during and after cancer treatment can significantly improve your quality of life, long\-term survival, and help you get back to doing what you love to do\.

## Benefits of Cancer Rehabilitation:

-   Improved range of motion

-   Reduce fatigue

-   Maintain and improve exercise tolerance and muscle strength

## Services:

-   Physical rehabilitation

-   Personalized [exercise programming](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/exercise-therapy/)

-   Pain management
