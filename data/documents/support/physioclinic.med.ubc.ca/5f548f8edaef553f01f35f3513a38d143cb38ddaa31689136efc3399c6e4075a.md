The UBC Physical Therapy &amp; Research Clinic is located on the second floor of the Friedman Building at the UBC Point Grey Campus\.

If you’re a first\-time visitor to the Friedman building, it can be a little tricky to find\.  The following links provide downloadable maps on how to find convenient parking options as well as key landmarks to help find our clinic\.

[Map to Friedman Building](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/08/Map-to-Friedman-Building.pdf)

#### Finding the Clinic inside the Friedman Building

1.  As you enter the building through the main doors walk directly to the elevator\.
2.  Take the elevator to the second floor, exiting immediately to your left\.
3.  Take a few more steps and turn another left to find the UBC Physical Therapy &amp; Research Clinic\.
