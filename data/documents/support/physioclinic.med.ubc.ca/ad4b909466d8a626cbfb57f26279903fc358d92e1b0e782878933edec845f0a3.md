### Here are a few things that people had to say about us:

  
  

[Image](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/04/P5060090-720x960.jpg)

“We are very happy with what you’re doing in the clinic\. I see the changes in my wife – she is always improving for almost a year\. When she started here she was in a power wheelchair and using oxygen\. Now she has no oxygen and walks with a walker\.”

> “She has her own independence back in the home and she can do things by herself\. I believe she will continue to improve\.”

*Oswaldo G* – *Translated from Spanish*

  
  

“I’ve been receiving treatment from UBC PT clinic off and on for nearly 2 years addressing my injury\. They are a joy to work with; they explain the treatment and give encouragement at all times\. The students are very dedicated and professional, the supervisory staff are second to none and have a wealth of knowledge\. I have measurable improvement after each session\. I would recommend them to anyone\. My outlook on recovery has improved since attending the clinic”

*Dave F* 

[Image](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/PT_Clinic_ServiceImages_WorkSafe.jpg)

  
  

[Image](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/04/P5060142-720x960.jpg)

“From a surgery 21 months ago to remove a benign brain tumour that left me with balance issues and a myriad of other brain challenges, to the Brain Wellness Centre and Physical Therapy &amp; Research Clinic at UBC it has been a journey I never thought I would be taking\. After the initial intake call, I was put in touch with Sally Anne\.

> The absolute care and attention that I have received to date have given me back my will, my hope to walk\.

“First with walking sticks and now I walk without the sticks\. The ongoing encouragement from the staff members and students has helped me regain my feeling of “I am safe in my body\!” It’s a good thing to be alive\.”

*Mel H*
