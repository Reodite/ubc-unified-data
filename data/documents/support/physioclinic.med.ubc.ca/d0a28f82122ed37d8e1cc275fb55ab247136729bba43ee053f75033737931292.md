Concussions \(also known as a mild traumatic brain injury\) are an injury to the brain which can occur due to a direct or indirect blow to the head\. Concussions usually self\-resolve within 1\-2 weeks; however, in some cases, individuals can experience persistent symptoms that linger for a longer period and need concussion rehabilitation\. Symptoms can include headaches, dizziness, vision or balance issues, or feeling “out of it” or not like yourself\.

Physiotherapy for concussion rehabilitation can include education regarding the best management of the concussion, vision/balance/neurological assessment and treatment, manual therapy and graded re\-introduction of physical activity\.

## Benefits of Concussion Rehabilitation:

-   Decrease headaches, dizziness

-   Increase mental awareness

-   Regulation of sleep cycles and decrease fatigue

-   Increase balance

-   Stabilization in the mood – feeling more like yourself and more “with it”

## Services:

-   Vision assessment and treatment

-   Balance assessment and treatment

-   Manual therapy

-   [Exercise therapy](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/exercise-therapy/)
