Your physiotherapy treatment at the UBC Physical Therapy &amp; Research Clinic \(PTRC\) will be performed by one of our students under supervision or by a licensed physiotherapist if you prefer\. All of our supervisors and mentors are fully qualified and passionate about equipping the next generation with the skills required for an all\-around treatment\.  

Many of our student therapists are well into their studies by the time they’re gaining hands\-on experience at UBC PTRC and will continue to refine their skills until the end of their program\.

The AMS/GSS Health &amp; Dental Plan was designed by students for students to take care of expenses not covered by basic health care plans, such as [British Columbia’s Medical Services Plan \(MSP\)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/health-drug-coverage/medical-services-plan/bc-residents/msp-brochure.pdf), equivalent provincial health care plans, and private basic plans\. 

All AMS and GSS members \(excluding those taking distance education courses only\) are automatically enrolled, including full\-time and part\-time students, international students, and those auditing a course\. If you are covered, the cost of the Plan is part of your student fees, and no further action is required\.  

***We do not bill on behalf of iMED, ICBC, or WorkSafe BC**\.* 

**Other Insurance Information** 

For a more efficient reimbursement process, register and submit your claims on the [Pacific Blue Cross \(PBC\) website\.](https://service.pac.bluecross.ca/member/login/) Our policy number is **43979** \(health, travel, and dental\), and your ID number is your student number\.  

If you’re covered by another plan, you can combine this plan with the AMS/GSS Health &amp; Dental Plan to maximize your overall coverage and eliminate out\-of\-pocket costs\.

To start the process of joining the class, we usually conduct an initial assessment\. This allows us to see if you are eligible for the class and our options to help you recover\. MSP and other extended health benefits do not cover the FAME classes\. If you have a health spending account, that unfortunately has to be paid out of pocket and submitted by the primary holder of the insurance manually\.
