### Here are a few things that people had to say about us:

  
  

[Image](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/PTRC_ABC-940x588.png)

Being part of the [Fame group](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/fame-for-neurofitness/) has been a wonderful experience\. The team has continued to push me to improve\. I have noticed leg strength, better balance, and improved arm function\.

> Other big milestones are that I am now able to walk with walking poles and less with my walker\. As well, I can get off the floor which is so important for my safety and mobility\.

The patience and positivity of the group have been wonderful for me\. I look forward to the class, it has really improved my quality of life both physically and mentally\.

Thank so much 

*Rainer H\. – [Fame Program](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/fame-for-neurofitness/)*

  
  

Too Great\! The program has been so educational and helpful\. Exercises are really focused and simple to remember, Claire is so supportive, clear, enthusiastic, and provides realistic support\. The program is very organized, and Emma and the front desk staff are terrific\! 

I am very grateful, feeling stronger and more optimistic\.

*Gail H\. – [Gla:d Program](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/glad-canada/)*
