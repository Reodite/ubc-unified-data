Musculoskeletal \(MSK\) injuries can affect the muscles, joints, ligaments, tendons, and bones\. They may occur suddenly \(e\.g\., sprains or strains\) or develop over time due to repetitive stress, posture, or activity demands\. Common presentations include back and neck pain, tendon injuries, joint stiffness, and movement limitations that affect daily function

MSK conditions are a leading cause of pain and disability and can impact mobility, strength, and daily function if not addressed\.

## Why treatment matters

Early, evidence\-based physiotherapy can:

-   Reduce pain and improve function  
    
-   Restore mobility and strength  
    
-   Prevent chronic symptoms and re\-injury  
    
-   Support a timely return to daily activities

## How we can help

Our care is personalized and evidence\-based, and may include:

-   **Exercise therapy** to improve strength, mobility, and function  
    
-   **Manual therapy** to support movement and reduce pain  
    
-   **Education** to guide recovery and prevent recurrence
