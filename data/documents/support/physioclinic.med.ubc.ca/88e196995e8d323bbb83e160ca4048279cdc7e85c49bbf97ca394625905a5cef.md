### What is GLA:D Canada?

It is a 7\-week education and exercise program for individuals with stiff or painful knees and/or hips, or individuals with knee and/or hip osteoarthritis\. Research shows participants in GLA:D® are more physically active, report less pain, reduce the use of pain medications and may delay or prevent the need for surgery\.

To determine if you will benefit from the GLA:D® program, you will undergo an assessment by a healthcare practitioner, which will include a physical assessment as well as some functional tests\. Once you have been told the program is right for you, the GLA:D® program consists of:

-   Two 60\-minute group education sessions
-   Twelve 60\-minute exercise sessions completed with other GLA:D participants
-   Data collection for quality monitoring

Option to register in the GLA:D\[TM\] Canada database to provide your outcomes and complete follow\-up surveys \(pre\-program, 3 months, and 12 months after completing GLA:D\[TM\] Canada program\. This information will monitor how the program is working for people with knee and/or hip osteoarthritis across the country and help us improve the program\.
