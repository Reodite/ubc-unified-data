Neurological rehabilitation involves the assessment and ongoing treatment of individuals who have movement and functional challenges caused by changes or injuries to the nervous system\.

## Benefits of Stroke &amp; Neurological Rehabilitation:

-   Improve balance

-   Optimize functional movement

-   Improve strength and endurance

-   Learn new movement strategies
