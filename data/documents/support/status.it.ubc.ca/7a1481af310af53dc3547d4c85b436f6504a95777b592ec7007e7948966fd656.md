## Resolved

We have resolved the license expiration message issue with Adobe\. We thank you for your patience\.

Posted Sep 01, 2026 \- 09:12 PDT

## Update

Adobe continues to work on resolving the problem\. An update will be provided at 09:00 PT Tuesday morning\. The expiry warning does not impact access to the Adobe software products at this time\.

Posted Aug 31, 2026 \- 16:32 PDT

## Update

Adobe continues work to resolve the issue\. Estimated time of resolution is not yet available\. The expiry warning does not impact access to the Adobe software products at this time\.

Posted Aug 31, 2026 \- 13:56 PDT

## Update

As of 12:00 PM, we are still waiting for an update from Adobe regarding the resolution of the issue\.

Posted Aug 31, 2026 \- 12:08 PDT

## Identified

Whilst Adobe users may receive a license expired notice, adobe software will continue to operate despite the notice\. We continue to work with Adobe to resolve the issue\.

Posted Aug 31, 2026 \- 10:21 PDT

## Update

We are aware of issues with Adobe licensing this morning, affecting access to Acrobat and Creative Cloud applications\. We are currently working with Adobe to resolve the issue\. Another update will be provided at 10:30 PT\.

Posted Aug 31, 2026 \- 09:44 PDT

## Investigating

Users may experience issues with their Adobe subscriptions\.  
  
Some users may experience not being able to login into Adobe\.  
  
UBC IT technicians are currently investigating the issue\.

Posted Aug 31, 2026 \- 09:32 PDT

This incident affected: Other \(Other \- Vancouver, Other \- Okanagan\)\.
