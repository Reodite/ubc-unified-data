## Scheduled

The TouchNet PROD site will be undergoing scheduled maintenance\. During the update window the TouchNet PROD site will be offline for logging in by staff as a TouchNet user and for customers making new payments\. During this time when the payor clicks "pay now" TouchNet will display a screen informing the payor that the system is off line for maintenance and to try again after the system is back online\.  
For any questions please contact [dpp\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:dpp.support@ubc.ca)

Posted Aug 24, 2026 \- 10:12 PDT

This scheduled maintenance affected: Other \(Other \- Vancouver, Other \- Okanagan\)\.
