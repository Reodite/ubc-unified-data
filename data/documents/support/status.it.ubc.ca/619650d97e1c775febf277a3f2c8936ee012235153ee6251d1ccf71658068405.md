## Scheduled

EASD will enable a new Omni Gateway in the Anypoint Platform on September 21, 2026, from 16:00 to 19:00 PT  
  
The release will configure proxy\.api\.ubc\.ca, apply the required TLS certificates, and enable the Archibus proxy API\.  
  
No impact to end users\.  
  
All services should remain available throughout the change\.

Posted Sep 15, 2026 \- 14:17 PDT

This scheduled maintenance affects: Other \(Other \- Vancouver, Other \- Okanagan\)\.
