## Identified

Some student photos are currently missing from course rosters generated through the Final Grade Submission Tool \(FGST\) due to a synchronization issue\.  
  
The root cause has been identified, and teams are working to confirm the timeline for implementing a fix\. Further updates will be provided as they become available\.

This incident affected: Other \(Other \- Vancouver, Other \- Okanagan\)\.
