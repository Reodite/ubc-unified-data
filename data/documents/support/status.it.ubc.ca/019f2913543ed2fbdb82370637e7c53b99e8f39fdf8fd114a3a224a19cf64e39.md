Scheduled Maintenance Report for [UBC](https://status.it.ubc.ca/)

## Completed

The scheduled maintenance has been completed\.

Posted Sep 10, 2026 \- 00:00 PDT

## In progress

Scheduled maintenance is currently in progress\. We will provide updates as necessary\.

Posted Sep 08, 2026 \- 00:00 PDT

## Scheduled

During this maintenance window, users will be unable to use their EPBC accounts to authenticate to the following UBC services:  
  
\* Applicant Service Centre \(ASC\)  
\* UBC's UNI CRM  
\* CWL Account Upgrader \(CWLU\): for CWL account creation  
\* Science One  
\* UBC Student Housing and Community Services myAccount \(including UBC Card\) \- includes Faculty/Staff  
\* UBC Vancouver StarRez  
\* UBC Okanagan StarRez  
  
Impact:  
Users relying on EPBC credentials to sign in to these services will be unable to authenticate for the duration of the maintenance window\. No action is required from users; access will resume automatically once maintenance is complete\.

Posted Aug 13, 2026 \- 12:31 PDT

This scheduled maintenance affected: Other \(Other \- Vancouver, Other \- Okanagan\)\.
