## Resolved

This issue has been resolved\. Students should now be able to swap courses and course sections in Workday as expected\.  
  
Teams will continue to monitor the service for any further issues\.

Posted Sep 16, 2026 \- 14:03 PDT

## Investigating

Students are currently unable to swap courses or course sections in Workday\. The issue is being investigated\.  
  
In the meantime, students who require a course or section change are advised to contact their academic advisor, who are able to make the change on their behalf\.  
  
Further updates will be provided as more information becomes available\.

Posted Sep 16, 2026 \- 12:06 PDT

This incident affected: Workday\.
