## Resolved

Web/ASDM Firewall Login Issue via Cisco ISE issue has been resolved\. Cisco has released a software fix to address this issue, which has now been applied to our firewall\.

Posted Sep 04, 2026 \- 12:10 PDT

## Investigating

We are currently experiencing an issue affecting ASDM authentication via Cisco ISE in order to manage some departmental ASA firewall contexts\.  
We are working closely with the vendor to resolve this\. Currently a fix or workaround has not been identified\. We will provide an update as soon as one is available\.  
Thank you for your patience and cooperation\.

Posted Jun 26, 2026 \- 13:53 PDT

This incident affected: Other \(Other \- Vancouver, Other \- Okanagan\)\.
