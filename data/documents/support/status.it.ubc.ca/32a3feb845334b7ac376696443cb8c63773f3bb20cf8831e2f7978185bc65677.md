## Scheduled

Upcoming Outage – Learner Management \(ODMT\)  
  
  
  
Date &amp; Time:  
  
Deployment Activities: Tuesday, 02 Sep 2026 15:00 – 16:00 PT  
Validation activities: will continue until Wednesday, 03 Sep 2026 16:00  
  
What is being deployed and why?  
  
  
What is being deployed and why? We are deploying updates to the Official Document Management Tool \(ODMT\) to reflect a recent leadership change in the College of Graduate Studies and to resolve a data transmission issue affecting XML transcripts\. These changes ensure Program Completion Letters reflect current signatory and contact information, and that receiving institutions get complete transcript data\.  
  
Key Improvements  
  
  
ODMT  
  
  
  
Update Confirmation Enrollment Letters templates according to new signatures\.  
Restored 3 missing transmission data attributes on XML transcripts\.  
  
  
Who's impacted?  
  
  
Primary Users:  
  
Enrolment Services Records staff processing transcript requests, parchments, and courier shipments\.  
Graduate Studies staff managing document orders and FedEx shipping\.  
Secondary Impact:  
ODMT users \(students and staff\) will experience temporary inability to access ODMT during the 60\-minute maintenance window  
Students with financial holds will now be correctly identified in the system  
Students receiving official transcripts will receive properly formatted documents  
System Impact &amp; Availability  
During Maintenance \(60 minutes\):  
ODMT will display a maintenance page to all users  
Students cannot access ODMT to place new orders or check order status  
Staff cannot process orders or update tracking information  
SCT will remain available  
  
  
After Maintenance:  
  
Updated Program Completion Letter template and corrected XML transcript output will be immediately available\.  
  
  
Detailed Feature Information  
What is the new way of doing things?  
For Enrolment Services Records Staff / Students \(ODMT\):  
Before:  
  
  
Outdated Confirmation Enrollment Letters  
  
  
  
After:  
  
  
Updated Confirmation Enrollment Letter according to new templates\.
