## Investigating

The Staples punchout catalogues are currently unavailable and cannot be used to purchase items through Workday\. Staples is working to resolve the issue\. Faculty and staff are advised to create a purchase requisition using the Non\-Catalogue option to purchase from Staples, or try again later\.

Posted Sep 15, 2026 \- 15:43 PDT

This incident affected: Workday\.
