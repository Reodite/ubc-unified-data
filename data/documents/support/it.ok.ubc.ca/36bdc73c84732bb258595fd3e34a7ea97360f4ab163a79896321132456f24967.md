## Overview

UBC IT Okanagan Voice Services offers several features to meet the needs of departments or groups to enable engagement with the general public, or for internal communications\.

### Features &amp; Benefits

Voice Services consults with the department or group to recommend a solution to meet their specific needs\. The solution may include the following:

-   Phones for assigned spaces \(e\.g\., labs or meeting rooms\) or for general department use
-   Dedicated department phone number
-   Group call notifications and answering
-   Interactive voice menus and messages
-   Voicemail to generic email for department number
-   Call recording
-   Call centre systems &amp; technology
-   Teleconferencing
-   Intercom systems and panic buttons
-   Conference or meeting rooms systems &amp; technology, in conjunction with Audio Visual

**Note:** Fax services are no longer offered by UBC IT Okanagan and can be purchased directly from [SRFax\.](https://www.srfax.com/) Voice services can provide help or advice\.

### Costs

There may be costs for the solution, depending on which system and features are required, which will be discussed during the consultation\.

### Getting Started

Please use this to request a consultation with Voice Services:  [UBCO Voice Support Request](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_1Yx9fA0rDxsJJu6)

### Support

Support is available on campus, during regular UBCO IT business hours\.
