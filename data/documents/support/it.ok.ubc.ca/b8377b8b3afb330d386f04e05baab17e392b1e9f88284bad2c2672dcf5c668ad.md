## Overview

UBC IT Okanagan offers access to a variety of computing facilities and software for instructional and individual student use: 

-   Open computer labs available to all students, faculty and staff  
-   Multi\-use instructional labs available to all faculty for instructional purposes 
-   Faculty restricted labs restricted to faculty members

### Features &amp; Benefits

-   Open computer labs provide student access to required software titles to complete course activities 
-   Student access to printing available in all computer labs

### Requirements &amp; Eligibility

-   Open computer labs can be utilized by all active UBC Okanagan students, faculty and staff
-   To determine your eligibility to book multi\-use instructional labs, contact Enrolment services for general space and Classroom Scheduling for Academic spaces\.
-   Computer lab availability is subject to [academic scheduling](https://bookspace.ok.ubc.ca/academic-space/) and are open during [regular hours of operation](https://finance-operations.ok.ubc.ca/corm/building-hours/ "https://finance-operations.ok.ubc.ca/corm/building-hours/")\.

### Further Information

[UBCO Computer Labs](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/computers-printers/labs/lab-details/)

-   Request for [UBCO Computer Labs – Software Requests](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/computers-printers/lab-software-requests/)

### Getting Started

-   To book labs, contact Classroom Scheduling to plan and coordinate lab booking
-   For other needs, contact [Book Space](https://bookspace.ok.ubc.ca/) 
-   If you are unsure as to which lab would meet your technical requirements, please submit a [**Request Access to IT Service**](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=17a3df611b9129105a0999b4bd4bcbdb) in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice) ticket 
-   For research related needs, please visit [Research Computing \(ubc\.ca\)](https://rc.ubc.ca/) 

### Support

-   **Online** with the [UBC Self–Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice) which is the *fastest and most efficient way* to receive IT support
-   **By phone** at [250\-807\-9000](tel:250-807-9000) \(79000 from on campus\) for urgent, time\-sensitive support needs

To request that software be updated in particular lab, or new software be installed, please submit a [**Request Access to IT Service**](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=17a3df611b9129105a0999b4bd4bcbdb) in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice) for a consultation\.
