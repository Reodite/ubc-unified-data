We support, empower, and inspire each other in a positive workplace built on trust, integrity, and pride in our accomplishments\.

## We are committed to\.\.\.

-   Fostering a positive, respectful, and helpful environment\.
-   Offering professional, accurate, and timely customer service\.
-   Delivering a secure information infrastructure on campus with a “serve and protect” emphasis\.
-   Providing consultation and leadership, and leveraging out team’s expertise to deliver best practices and custom solutions\.
-   Hearing feedback and being the IT advocate for our clients\.
-   Engaging with students, faculties and researchers to evaluate technologies that support endeavours in teaching, learning, and research\.
-   Empowering our clients through education and training\.
-   Recommending alternative solutions, if possible, because “no” is not a complete answer\.
-   Helping clients [a](http://https/)lign with and/or identify collaboration opportunities\.
-   Supporting the individual and organizational efforts to respect the diverse environment in which we operate\.
