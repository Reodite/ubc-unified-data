At UBC’s Okanagan campus, we have adopted new technologies to enhance the traditional classroom experience\.

As a student, that means not only can you register for courses and view exam schedules and grades through the [Student Service Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/), but you can also access course materials posted by your professors through [Canvas](https://about.canvas.ubc.ca/)\. You also have access to a growing number of courses delivered remotely via videoconferencing and webcast\.

Below are some of the resources that contribute to UBC’s positioning as a world\-class teaching institution\.

### Canvas

Canvas is a powerful, user\-friendly online course management system that allows UBC faculty to be even more creative and efficient in how they teach\.

[About Canvas](https://about.canvas.ubc.ca/)

### CTL Okanagan – Learning Technologies

The Centre for Teaching and Learning provides resources to learning technology to help UBCO faculty enhance their teaching practice\.

[Learn more about CTL Learning Technologies](https://ctl.ok.ubc.ca/)

### Faculty Service Centre \(FSC\)

The Faculty Service Centre portal is the Administrator interface for managing the class lists, grades, scheduling, and ad hoc room bookings\.

[Learn more about the FSC](http://it.ubc.ca/services/campus-systems/faculty-service-centre-fsc)

### Research Systems \(RISe\)

Research Systems \(RISe\) is an online tool that aids researchers and administrators in the management of online applications\.

[Learn more about RISE](https://it.ubc.ca/services/campus-systems/research-information-systems)

### Integrated Service Centre \(ISC\)

The Integrated Service Centre \(ISC\) manages [Workday](https://isc.ubc.ca/about/workday "About Workday") at UBC\. We support UBC faculty, staff and student employees who use Workday, and partner with departments and faculties on system improvements\.

[Learn more about the ISC](https://isc.ubc.ca/)

### Student Services

This portal is for UBC applicants and students to access admissions, student records, registration, grades, awards and more\.

[Learn more about the Student Services](https://students.ubc.ca/)
