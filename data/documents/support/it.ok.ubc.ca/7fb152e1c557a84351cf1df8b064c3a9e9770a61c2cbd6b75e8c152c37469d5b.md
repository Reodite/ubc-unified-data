### Technical Support

For student printer locations or general student technology assistance, please contact the Student Support Analyst \(SSA\) at [students\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:students.helpdesk@ubc.ca)

All student printers are monitored daily\. To report a problem with a printer, submit information using the [UBC Self\-Service Portal\.](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)
