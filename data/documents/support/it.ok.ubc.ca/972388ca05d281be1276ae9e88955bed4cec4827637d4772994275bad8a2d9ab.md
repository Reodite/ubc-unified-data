## Overview

UBC IT arranges campus\-wide licensing agreements for common and widely used software titles and provides consultation on the purchase of non\-standard software for research, teaching or administrative purposes\.

For more information on available software, please visit the [UBC IT Software Licensing](https://it.ubc.ca/services/desktop-print-services/software-licensing) page\.  For specific software requirements and needs, UBC IT Okanagan provides consultation and advice on purchasing\. This page provides more information on specific software requirements\.

### Requirements &amp; Eligibility

-   Eligibility for software products may be restricted based on your affiliation with the university \(e\.g\., student, staff, faculty, research, graduate research\); restricted to specific faculties and activities \(e\.g\., department owned software\); or restricted to specific purposes such as teaching and/or research
-   The majority of the UBC software catalog is offered for UBC managed devices only\. However, some software may have availability for personal devices
-   Departments wishing to purchase non\-standard software are responsible for engaging vendors or resellers to obtain licensing options and quotes, receiving an approved [Privacy Impact Assessment](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/privacy-impact-assessment), and engaging with Procurement prior to purchasing

### Costs

-   UBC faculty and staff are eligible to access a wide array of software for teaching and research purchases
-   Costs for software are dependent on the type of license and form of agreement UBC has with the vendor\. We recommend checking the software catalog for more information on specific titles of interest
-   Faculty and staff can purchase software not offered by UBC \(non\-standard software\)\. Contact [UBCO Engagement Services](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/engagement-services/) to discuss your specific needs and to consult on the software purchase process

### Getting Started

-   Software required for new or transferring UBC Okanagan staff can be requested using the [Okanagan – Employee IT Onboarding](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_guide&amp;sys_id=3b25cc431b83d010c7b02069bc4bcb10) ticket
-   See [Software Licensing](https://it.ubc.ca/services/desktop-support-services/software-licensing) for a list of available software
    -   Select software applications can be downloaded from the [Software Centre](https://ubc.service-now.com/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0014982) \(Windows\) or Self\-Service \(Mac\)
    -   Otherwise, in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice), submit an [Order New Software](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=58955b844d04c1006da56e4eb8b4d46d) ticket to request support to download
-   Software requests for computer labs – please refer to the [UBCO Computer Labs](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/computers-printers/labs/) page for more information on requesting software installation or purchases for instructional use

### Support

-   For support downloading an application, submit an [**Order New Software**](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=58955b844d04c1006da56e4eb8b4d46d) ticket in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)
-   Support on how to use instructional software is available from the Centre for Teaching and Learning \(CTL\) or [Learning Technology Hub \(LT Hub\)](https://lthub.ubc.ca/) or [Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/)
-   Otherwise, please refer to the vendors’ website, LinkedIn learning, or other resources
