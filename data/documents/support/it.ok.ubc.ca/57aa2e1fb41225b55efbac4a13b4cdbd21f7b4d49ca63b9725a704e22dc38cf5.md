Time Zone Update Notice for IT Staff

British Columbia officially moved from observing Pacific Standard Time \(PST, UTC−8\) and Pacific Daylight Time \(PDT, UTC−7\) on a seasonal basis, to a single permanent time zone called Pacific Time \(PT, UTC−7\)\. This means clocks no longer change in the fall or spring\.    
   
**To ensure continuity of service, all IT Staff at UBC should review any systems, scheduled tasks, or integrations managed and update them to reflect this change before November 1, 2026\.**     
   
If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the IT Service Centre Help Desk\.
