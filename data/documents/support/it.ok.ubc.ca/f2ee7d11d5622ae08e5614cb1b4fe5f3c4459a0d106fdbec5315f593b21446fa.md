## Overview

UBC Okanagan IT \(UBCOIT\) is available to support your Audio Visual needs for on\-campus events\. We will help identify your AV needs and provide comprehensive, hands\-on training and guidance for your event, big or small\.

### Features &amp; Benefits

-   UBCOIT offers consultation services to define AV equipment needed for event
-   Training and information on AV equipment is available during regular business
-   A limited selection of equipment is available for use by faculty and staff

### Requirements &amp; Eligibility

-   Available to UBC faculty and staff
-   Event must be held on the UBC Okanagan campus; off\-site event support is not available

### Costs

Currently, UBCOIT consultation, support and equipment is available for use at no cost for active UBC faculty and staff

### Further Information

-   Information about on\-campus bookable rooms can be found on the [Learning Spaces Website](https://learningspaces.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   For non\-UBC clients, please contact the [Conferences and Accommodations](https://stayatubco.ca/host/meetings-events/) team
-   For event capture support, contact [UBCO Studios Production Services](https://ubcstudios.ok.ubc.ca/services/professional-production-services/)
-   Equipment is also available for loan at no cost to the UBCO community from [UBCO Studios Equipment Lending](https://ubcstudios.ok.ubc.ca/do-it-yourself/equipment-lending/)
-   **AV Support for Grad Thesis Defence**
    -   UBC Okanagan IT has moved away from in person AV support for thesis defenses\.
    -   Detailed AV instructions for each meeting room are now available on the Facilities Planning Bookable Meeting Rooms site under [*“More Info”* HERE](https://facilitiesplanning.ok.ubc.ca/bookable-meeting-rooms/)\.
    -   UBC IT AV support is available:  
        
        -   *In advance of the defence*, UBC Okanagan IT/AV is happy to meet with supervisors or Admin to provide training on the use of the videoconference equipment\.  You can [Request Desktop or AV Support](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=cc7f5fb9874e0100bbee70406d434df9) to book training/orientation to the equipment
        
        -   *On the day of the defence*, if IT/AV assistance is required, please contact the [Service Centre](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/support/) for time\-sensitive support\.

### Getting Started

-   To begin planning for your event, please submit a **[Request Access to IT Service](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=17a3df611b9129105a0999b4bd4bcbdb)** in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice) for a consultation\. Advanced notice of the event is preferable\. Please contact UBCOIT as early as possible

### Support

-   [Self Support and Instructional Videos](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/audio-visual-creative-services/self-support/)
-   Detailed AV instructions for bookable meeting rooms are available on the [Facilities Planning Bookable Meeting Rooms site](https://capital-planning.ok.ubc.ca/find-a-space/meeting-rooms/) under *“More Info”*\.
-   UBCOIT AV support is available during regular business hours
-   For general support, please submit a request using the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)
