##### IT Service Centre and Deskside Support

If you have an equipment issue or would like to speak with a technician, please request an appointment using the links below\.If you have a quick question or pressing issue, please call 250\-807\-9000 \(79000 from on campus\)\.

[Submit a UBC Self\-Service Portal ticket](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)

### **You’re invited to UBC IT Okanagan’s Holiday Open House**

Join us for our annual holiday\-themed showcase in **ADM 024 \(and the best campus event of the year\!\)**\. We’re transforming the halls into a festive hub of innovation, complete with immersive experiences and next\-gen holiday vibes\.

#### **Explore the Engine Behind UBCO’s Digital Campus** 

Meet the teams that power your connectivity, safeguard your data and ensure classroom tech runs seamlessly for teaching and learning\. Meet the people and see the systems that keep our campus running at the speed of thought\. 

#### **No RSVP Required** 

Just bring your colleagues, your most festive attire \(yes, there’s a photo booth\), and your curiosity\. Connect, celebrate and experience the tech that powers UBCO\.
