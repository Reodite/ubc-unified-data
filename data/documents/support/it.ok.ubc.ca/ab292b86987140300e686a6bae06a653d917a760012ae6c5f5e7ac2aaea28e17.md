## Overview

UBCO has implemented a central pay for print service to meet students’ printing and copy needs\.  This has been established to support UBC’s sustainability objectives\.   

Student printers are provided by UBCO and are accessible for use by all active students\.

[Login to Pay for Print Portal](https://payforprint.ok.ubc.ca/user)

### Features &amp; Benefits

-   Print only what you need when you Swipe to Print; print from any student UBC fleet printer and release your print job using your student card \(also known as the *Student\-FindMe* function\)\.

### Student Printer Locations

-   Student printer locations are listed here:  [Pay for Print for Students](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/computers-printers/student-printing-at-ubco/student-printer-locations/)

### Requirements &amp; Eligibility

To print using the Pay for Print function, you must meet the following criteria:

-   Active student
-   Money is in your Pay for Print account \(*pay\-for\-print uses your meal plan/convenience plan account*\)

### Costs

-   Print pricing can be found here:  [Print pricing at UBCO](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/computers-printers/printing-at-ubco/pricing/)

### Copyright Restrictions

-   Please take note that there are copyright restrictions to photocopying, printing and scanning materials\. For more information, please see [UBC Copyright Guide](http://copyright.ubc.ca/)\.
