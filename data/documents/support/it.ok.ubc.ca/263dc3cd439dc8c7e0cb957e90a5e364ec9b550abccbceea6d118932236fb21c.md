## UBCO CBM Online Payment Form is now Retired

With the launch of UBC’s Digital Payment Program, UBCO CBM is no longer accepting any requests to develop new online payment form\. All requests will be redirected over to the DPP team\. You may find more information here: [https://vpfo\-dpp\-2022\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/](https://vpfo-dpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/)
