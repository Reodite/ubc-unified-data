## Overview

UBC IT Okanagan Desktop Services offers configuration and ongoing support for IT managed desktops, laptops, and peripherals\.  IT managed devices include CRP devices and department devices purchased through Workday catalogs\. 

For research equipment and support, see [Research Computing](https://rc.ubc.ca/)\.

### Features &amp; Benefits

-   Available during business hours \(Standard hours are 8am\-4pm Monday\-Friday\)
-   Service requests available via ticket submission with onsite and/or remote support
-   [Computer Replacement Program \(CRP\)](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/computers-printers/computer-refresh-program/)
-   UBC system configuration and installation
-   Equipment consultations and recommendations
-   Loaner equipment for term instructional positions \(based on availability\)
-   Move and setup of IT managed equipment and devices
-   Limited support for non\-IT managed devices

### Requirements &amp; Eligibility

-   Deskside Service support is available for IT managed equipment and devices\.  IT managed devices are identified by an IT Service Tag
-   Equipment not purchased through Workday will have limited support and may not meet [UBC Cybersecurity](https://it.ubc.ca/services/cybersecurity-services/ubc-information-security-office/cybersecurity) minimum requirements

### Further Information

-   To ensure devices can function within UBC’s environment including all [UBC Cybersecurity](https://it.ubc.ca/services/cybersecurity-services/ubc-information-security-office/cybersecurity) requirements, it is highly advisable to only purchase devices through Workday\.  Devices acquired outside of Workday may not operate effectively within our specific environment and will not be supported by UBCO IT\.
-   [How do I find the service or asset tag of my IT managed computer and devices?](https://ubc.service-now.com/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0019192) 
-   [How to Submit an IT Equipment Consult or Purchase Request using the UBC Self\-Service Portal?](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article_view&amp;sysparm_article=KB0019617)
-   [How to request IT equipment moves for UBCO staff or faculty?](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article_view&amp;sysparm_article=KB0019615)

### Getting Started

-   New hire? Complete the [**IT Onboarding**](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_guide&amp;sys_id=3b25cc431b83d010c7b02069bc4bcb10) request to ensure the equipment meets employee needs and is and is available on their first day\.   
-   Discontinue services or transfer to another department? Complete the **[IT Offboarding](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_guide&amp;sys_id=c13a5065db2ba010fd8e2a9a48961950)** request in addition to Workday forms\. 
-   If you need to order hardware for your department and would like support determining which device to purchase, please submit a request for consultation in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=cc7f5fb9874e0100bbee70406d434df9)\.

### Support

-   For general support, please submit a ticket at [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)\.
