### Dell partners with The University of British Columbia\!

Due to our relationship with Dell, we are pleased to announce that Dell has extended discounting on personal technology to our university\!

Through Dell’s Member Purchase Program, all students and faculty are eligible to access the member discounts\.

Member site: [www\.dell\.ca/mpp/ubc](https://www.dell.com/en-ca/lp/dellmember?link_number=3433254&amp;tfcid=1139509&amp;cid=316360&amp;lid=5993986&amp;dgc=ms&amp;cpn=ArmMppM_ArmMppCP_ArmMppS_ArmMppVAR)

**How to access your discount:**

1.  1.  Visit www\.dell\.ca/mpp/ubc for your coupon\. Use your university email address\.
    2.  Check your email for a coupon and redeem at: www\.dell\.ca/mpp/ubc
    3.  The coupon can be applied at checkout or by contacting our sales team at Canada\_MPP@Dell\.com\. If you don’t have a university email please contact our sales team for a coupon\.

**  
Dell Member Purchase Program Benefits:**

-   Extra discount\(s\) off the lowest advertised price
-   Free enrollment into the Dell Rewards Loyalty Program: 3% back
-   Dedicated account team to help place orders and escalate support requests
-   Huge selection of PCs, electronics and accessories
-   Free shipping on all orders

For questions or concerns on the program please contact [UBCO IT Engagement Services](mailto:ubco.engagement.services@ubc.ca)\.
