### [UBCO Gitea is Moving to OpenID Connect \(OIDC\)](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/2026/08/20/ubco-gitea-is-moving-to-openid-connect-oidc/)

August 20, 2026 No Comments

UBCO Gitea is transitioning from LDAP to OpenID Connect \(OIDC\) authentication\. Existing users need to link their current Gitea account to their CWL\-based sign\-in by September 30, 2026 to retain access to existing repositories, permissions, and settings\.
