Multimedia production support from video production to streaming as well as AV equipment bookings and support\.

### System Design &amp; Specialty Spaces

If you are thinking of renovating or building a meeting room, conference room, lab, auditorium or classroom, we can provide guidance in AV system design, procurement, and audio visual equipment installation\.

[Learn more about System Design &amp; Specialty Spaces](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/audio-visual-creative-services/specialty-spaces/)

### Events and AV Support

Our UBC IT Okanagan is available to support your audio visual needs for on\-campus events\. We will help identify your AV needs and provide comprehensive, hands\-on training and guidance for your event, big or small\.

[Learn more about Events and AV Support](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/audio-visual-creative-services/event-support/)

### Classroom Support

UBC IT Okanagan supports AV technology in centrally funded teaching spaces\. These spaces are maintained by IT to ensure a consistent classroom experience for faculty and students\.

[Learn more about AV Classroom Support](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/audio-visual-creative-services/classroom-support/)

### UBC Studios Okanagan

The UBC Studios is dedicated to the production of video and its relationship to teaching and learning within UBC’s Okanagan campus community and beyond\.

[Learn more about UBC Studios Okanagan](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/audio-visual-creative-services/ubco-studios/)

### Video Conferencing

There are many supported videoconference \(VC\) options available to Faculty and Staff at UBCO, from apps on your phone or laptop through to large on\-campus VC rooms\.

[Learn more about Video Conferencing options at UBCO](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/audio-visual-creative-services/video-conferencing/)

### Digital Signage

Digital signage provides the functional capacity to deliver announcements, news, student activities, videos, campus\-generated content and events, as well as critical campus updates such as weather advisories and emergency information\.

[Learn more about Digital Signage](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/audio-visual-creative-services/digital-signage/)

### Self support and instructional videos

Are you curious to see what we have to offer?  Learn more about the audio visual technology available on campus, AV support videos and suggestions\.

[Access AV self\-support and instructional videos at UBCO](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/audio-visual-creative-services/self-support/)
