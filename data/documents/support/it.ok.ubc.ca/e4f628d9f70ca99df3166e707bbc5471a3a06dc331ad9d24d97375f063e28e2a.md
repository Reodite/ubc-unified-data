## Overview

UBC staff, faculty and students can book space on campus to support operations and student learning\. UBC IT Okanagan develops and maintains a small number of online room booking websites used by groups at the Okanagan campus called the Meeting Room Booking System \(MRBS\)\.

#### Examples:

-   [Centre for Health Behaviour Change Booking System](https://bookings.ok.ubc.ca/chbc)

### Features &amp; Benefits

-   This service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week except during scheduled and unscheduled maintenance\.
-   Users can book, change and cancel space bookings online\. 

### Requirements &amp; Eligibility

-   This services is available to staff, faculty, and researchers\.
-   Users must have an active CWL to authenticate and book a space\.

### Costs

The creation and maintenance of an online booking service is centrally funded and provided at no cost\. 

### Getting Started

To request an online room booking site, please submit a [**Request Access to IT Service**](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=17a3df611b9129105a0999b4bd4bcbdb) ticket in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)\.

### Support

-   For general support, please submit a [**Request Access to IT Service**](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=17a3df611b9129105a0999b4bd4bcbdb) in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)
-   To report an incident or issue with this service, please submit a [Report an Issue](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=28835cdb6dc334006da5c8aceaa441ee) ticket\.
