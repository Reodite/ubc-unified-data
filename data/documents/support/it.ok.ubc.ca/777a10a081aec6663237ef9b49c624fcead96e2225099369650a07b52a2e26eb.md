##### IT Service Centre and Deskside Support

If you have an equipment issue or would like to speak with a technician, please request an appointment using the links below\.If you have a quick question or pressing issue, please call 250\-807\-9000 \(79000 from on campus\)\.  
  
**UBC Okanagan Employees:**   
[Submit a UBC Self\-Service Portal ticket](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)  
[Book a Technician for an in person appointment](https://outlook.office.com/book/bookingsOKITUBCOITServiceDesk@m365.svc.ubc.ca/?ismsaljsauthenabled)  
  
**Students:** [Student Tech Support](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/student-tech-support/)

### ***You’re invited to UBC IT Okanagan’s ‘Tis the Season Open House 2024*** [https://events\.ok\.ubc\.ca/event/ubco\-it\-holiday\-open\-house\-2024/](https://events.ok.ubc.ca/event/ubco-it-holiday-open-house-2024/)[Image](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/24/2024/11/IT-Holiday-Open-House-2024-Exchange-768x461.png)

***Tuesday, December 3  
12:30 pm to 2:30 pm  
ADM 024***  

You’re invited to the annual holiday\-themed open house hosted by UBC IT Okanagan\. 

We’re decking the halls of ADM 024 and filling them with festive food and drinks for campus staff and faculty\. 

Come see behind the scenes and meet the teams who help reset your password, keep the wifi running and make sure the classroom tech supports the teaching and learning needs of our campus\.  

We will have sweet and savory treats, a hot chocolate bar and more\. Challenge your team members to a game of Mario Kart or Duck Hunt, try your hand at flying \(and landing?\) a plane in the flight simulator or test your cybersecurity know\-how\. 

No RSVP needed, just grab your co\-workers and your most festive holiday attired \(did we mention there’s a photo booth?\) and share some holiday spirit with your fellow UBCO employees\. [Add it to your calendar from the UBCO Events page HERE\.](https://events.ok.ubc.ca/event/ubco-it-holiday-open-house-2024/) 

[View UBCO Event Listing](https://events.ok.ubc.ca/event/ubco-it-holiday-open-house-2024/)
