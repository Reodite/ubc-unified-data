## Overview

UBC Okanagan hosts several wireless networks to support students, faculty and staff on campus and at partner institutions\. You can check your e\-mail, surf the web and access the UBC network from almost everywhere on campus\! It’s easy to use and free for faculty, staff and students\.

**Please Note:**  Many Wi\-Fi enabled devices that are designed for a typical home WiFi network **will not function as expected** or be able to connect to UBC Wifi as they do not meet minimum security standards\.  Consult with IT prior to purchasing or bringing onto campus\.

### Features &amp; Benefits

UBC Okanagan has multiple services to support your wireless needs:

### [ubcsecure](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30771)

Encrypted and secure wi\-fi connection

### [eduroam](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30767)

Roaming wi\-fi access with partner higher education institutions worldwide

### [ubcoprivate](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30769)

For direct connection to internal department networks

### [ubcvisitor](https://it.ubc.ca/node/30773)

Public wi\-fi service for UBC guests

### [myVPN](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/myvpn)

Virtual Private Network \(VPN\) to facilitate access to restricted on\-campus resources

### Requirements &amp; Eligibility

-   Devices on UBCO’s wireless network must be able to support the following capabilities for wireless authentication: 
    -   support authentication by acceptance of terms and conditions via pop\-up \(for ubcvisitor\), or
    -   by sending a username and password \(use of 802\.1x; for ubcsecure and eduroam wireless\)
-   Requirements for specific networks are provided via clicking on the boxes above
-   All users of UBC networks are expected to follow guidelines and policies set out in:
    -   [Summary of Appropriate Use Policy](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/wireless-internet-access/appropriate-use) 
    -   [ISS U11, Securing Internet of Things \(IoT\) Devices](https://cio.ubc.ca/information-security-standards/U11) 
    -   [SC14, Acceptable Use and Security of UBC Electronic Information and Systems](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/wireless-internet-access/appropriate-use) 
    -   [SC11, Management of the Wireless Network](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/wireless-network-policy/)

**Please Note:** Not all Wi\-Fi enabled devices are built to support enterprise security standards and as such may not be able to connect to the UBCO Wi\-Fi networks, especially home automation or entertainment devices\. Prior to purchasing devices or bringing devices from home, request a consultation with a UBCO IT network specialist by submitting a [**Request Access to IT Service**](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=17a3df611b9129105a0999b4bd4bcbdb) in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)\.

### Costs

WiFi is available free of charge for all UBC faculty, staff and students as well as campus guests and affiliates\.

### Further Information

-   [UBC Wireless Coverage map](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/wireless-internet-access/wireless-coverage)

### Getting Started

-   To connect your device to Wifi:  [Wireless internet access set\-up documents](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/wireless-internet-access/wireless-internet-access-setup-documents)
-   For Wifi &amp; VPN services that requires authentication \(ubcsecure, eduroam, ubcprivate, myVPN\):
    -   staff and students can register for a CWL using the [CWL Sign Up](https://activate.id.ubc.ca/iamweb/) page
    -   Affiliates or guests can be [sponsored by a faculty or staff member](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/how-sponsor-guest) to receive a CWL which will enable access to WiFi service that require authentication \(i\.e\. ubcsecure\)\. 

### Support

-   For urgent, time\-sensitive issues, phone support is available during regular office hours at 250\-807\-9000 \(79000 from on campus\)
-   For support accessing UBC Okanagan WiFi, please submit a [**Request Access to IT Service**](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=17a3df611b9129105a0999b4bd4bcbdb) in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)\.
-   For support accessing the ShawOpen WiFi, please contact Shaw directly at 1\-888\-407\-0251
-   To request a consultation with a UBCO IT network specialist on a wireless device, please submit a [**Request Access to IT Service**](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=17a3df611b9129105a0999b4bd4bcbdb) in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)\.
