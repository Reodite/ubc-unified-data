Do you have student technology\-related questions? Are you having difficulty accessing and using UBC software and programs for online learning? Do you have questions about what technology is available to students?

The Student Support Analyst is here to help you with various learning technologies and can support you with:

-   Canvas and other UBC learning technologies
-   Access to UBC programs and software
-   Microsoft Office
-   Connecting to Wi\-Fi
-   Printing on campus
-   Troubleshooting software issues
-   General IT Questions
-   For Workday questions and support, [go here](http://students.ok.ubc.ca/workday)

### Important for Newly Admitted Students:

If you have been admitted to UBC and have received your student number, you will be instructed to create a CWL account as part of your application process through the Applicant Service Centre\. Use the [**CWL Account Upgrader**](https://activate.id.ubc.ca/iamweb/cwlUpgrade/create.xhtml) to create your Student CWL by logging in with one of the supported user accounts \(e\.g\., EducationPlannerBC\) or by email authentication using SecureLink\. A Student CWL account is required for full access to campus systems\.

If you have already [created a Student CWL](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/how-create-account#student) account but are experiencing issues with accessing campus systems, please submit a detailed ticket including:

-   Full name \(Legal and preferred name\)
-   Email
-   CWL &amp; Student Number
-   Did you use the CWL Upgrader link in your acceptance email, or did you create your CWL directly?

### Wi\-Fi

Do you need access to Wi\-Fi? The UBCsecure Wireless network allows students to connect to the internet on the UBC campus at high\-speeds 24×7\.

[How to connect to UBCsecure](https://it.ubc.ca/autoconnect)

### Printing

There are several student printers located across campus\. Find out how to add money to your printing account and where to locate the nearest printer\.

[Printing and Copying for Students](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/computers-printers/student-printing-at-ubco/)

### Campus\-Wide Login

The UBC Campus\-Wide Login \(CWL\) is the primary authentication system for students in the Okanagan\. This login provides a single account to access University\-wide applications, email, computer workstations, and Microsoft software\.

[Learn about CWL](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/student-tech-support/student-accounts/)

### Multi\-factor Authentication \(MFA\)

As of November 1, students accessing applications with their Campus\-wide Login \(CWL\) from off\-campus will require multi\-factor authentication \(MFA\) to safeguard personal information and protect against unauthorized access\.

[Find MFA Resources](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/multi-factor-authentication-mfa)

Microsoft 365 Office offers a range of Microsoft applications including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and OneNote\. Currently registered UBC students with student email are qualified for a free Microsoft 365 Office download\.
