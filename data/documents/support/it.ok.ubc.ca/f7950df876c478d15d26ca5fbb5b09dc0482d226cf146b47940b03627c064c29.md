Collaboration software including instant messaging \(IM\), blogs, and video conferencing\.

### UBC Survey Tool

The UBC Survey Tool is provided by Qualtrics\. It is an easy\-to\-use, top\-tier survey tool platform that offers a wide range of features\. It complies with the BC Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act \(FIPPA\) because the survey data is kept secure and is stored and backed up in Canada\.

[Learn more about Qualtrics](https://it.ubc.ca/services/teaching-learning-tools/survey-tool)

### UBC Blogs

UBC Blogs can be used to build a course website, allow group authoring on a blog, provide peer review of authored content, provide class webspace development, and provide a personal portfolio for students, faculty and staff\.

[UBC Blogs](https://blogs.ubc.ca/)

### Microsoft Teams

MS Teams enables users across the University to connect via instant messaging, video conferencing, online meetings, and simultaneously work on a document in real\-time together\.

[UBC Microsoft Teams](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/microsoft-teams)

### Zoom Video Conferencing

UBC has a campus\-wide license for Zoom as a platform for delivering courses online and larger meetings\. Zoom is an easy\-to\-use video conferencing platform that offers a wide range of features\.

[Zoom Video Conferencing](https://it.ubc.ca/services/teaching-learning-tools/zoom-video-conferencing)
