Support of internet services inclusive of services that support wireless connectivity and VPN\.

### Wireless Access

In addition to wired access, UBC’s Okanagan campus offers wireless access to the internet around campus to help you stay connected\.

[Learn about Wireless Access](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/internet-telephone/wireless-access/)

### VPN \(Virtual Private Network\)

Use the UBC myVPN service to encrypt and protect data that is transferred between your computer and the campus network\.

[Learn more about myVPN](http://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/myvpn)
