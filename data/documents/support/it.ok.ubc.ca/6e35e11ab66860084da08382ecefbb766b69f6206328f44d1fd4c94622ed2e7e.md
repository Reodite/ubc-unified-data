### Hardware &amp; Software:

-   Computer labs vary in size from 16 stations up to 41 stations and are arranged by tier\.
    -   **Tier 1 labs** are considered general\-use classrooms, bookable through the [campus academic scheduling process\.](https://bookspace.ok.ubc.ca/academic-space/)
    -   Computer labs are subject to availability and are open during [regular hours of operation](https://finance-operations.ok.ubc.ca/corm/building-hours/ "https://finance-operations.ok.ubc.ca/corm/building-hours/")\.
        -   **Hardware:**
            -   Equipment is supported, and replaced based on life cycling funded through UBCO IT\.
            -   If you would like to request changes to the hardware in a computer lab, please submit a ticket to the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice) for assistance\.
        -   **Software:**
            -   All computer labs come pre\-loaded with basic software such as Microsoft Office and Adobe Acrobat Reader\. Individual labs may also have a variety of other specific software installed to meet instructional needs, through a request process by the programs or courses scheduled to use the specific space\.  A list of available software is provided below\.
            -   If you would like to request that software be updated in particular lab, new software be installed, please see our [Computer Labs – Software Requests](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/computers-printers/labs/lab-software-requests/ "Software Requests") page\.
        -   **New hardware and software requests** for Tier 1 computer labs must be requested as soon as possible as capital planning is cyclic and hardware upgrades are planned on a multi\-year cycle\.
    -   **Tier 2** **labs** are department\-specific, with restricted access for bookings by the assigned departments only\.
        -   **Hardware:**
            -   Equipment life cycling, maintenance and support is the responsibility of the assigned department\.
            -   If you would like to request changes to the hardware in a computer lab, please submit a ticket to the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice) for assistance\.
        -   **Software:**
            -   If you would like to request that software be updated in particular lab, new software be installed, please see our [Computer Labs – Software Requests](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/computers-printers/labs/lab-software-requests/ "Software Requests") page\.

### Booking:

-   **Tier 1 spaces** for teaching requirements are booked through the [campus academic scheduling process](https://bookspace.ok.ubc.ca/academic-space/) facilitated by Enrolment Services\.
-   Single event bookings are possible after the academic scheduling process has completed\.
-   If you are unsure as to which lab would meet your technical requirements, please submit a [**Request Access to IT Service**](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=17a3df611b9129105a0999b4bd4bcbdb) in the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)
