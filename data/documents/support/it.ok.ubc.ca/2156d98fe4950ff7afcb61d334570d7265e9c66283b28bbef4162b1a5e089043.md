We are deeply saddened to learn of the death of Garth Evans August 3, 2026 in Calgary, Alberta after a brief battle with cancer\.

Garth joined UBC in January 2012 and over thirteen years built something genuinely remarkable here\. Before his work in emerging media, he spent years as the primary AV and technology support for the Southern Medical Program \(SMP\), where he became something of a legend\. The SMP community adored him\. Faculty, staff, and students alike regularly went out of their way to say so, and the accolades he received from that group over the years were extraordinary\. He brought the same care and commitment to the distributed medical program that he later brought to cutting\-edge visualization technology, which tells you something about who he was\.

In his later years at UBCO, Garth was one of the driving forces behind the Visualization and Emerging Media Studio \(VEMS\), one of the most advanced immersive visualization environments in Canadian higher education, and a founding member of the Constellation initiative, which brought together emerging media, the library, and creative research in ways that continue to shape how we think about that space\. His role was redefined at that time in recognition of the scope of what he had built, a position that had grown well beyond its original boundaries because Garth kept growing it\.

What made Garth irreplaceable wasn’t the technology\. It was the way he moved through the institution\. He was curious, generous, always connecting people who didn’t yet know they needed to know each other\. Researchers, faculty, students, vendors, colleagues across campus: he made everyone feel supported and seen\. His enthusiasm, genuine curiosity and innate desire to support the academic and research vision of everyone at the institution was extraordinary, contagious and his absence will be felt by many\.

Our entire team and everyone he touched will miss him greatly, and the spaces and relationships he built will carry his fingerprints for years to come\.
