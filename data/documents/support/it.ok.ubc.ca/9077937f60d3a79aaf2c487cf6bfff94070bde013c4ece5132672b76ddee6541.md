Start the New Academic Year Strong: Essential IT Resources at UBC

With a new academic year upon us, UBC offers helpful IT resources you may need for your day\-to\-day work at UBC:  

**Resources available to faculty, staff, students:**

-   [UBC Survey Tool](http://www.it.ubc.ca/survey/): Create surveys with this Canadian\-hosted solution
-   [Canvas](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/canvas-student-guide/): Canvas provides a secure, online classroom for each of your courses at UBC\.
-   [Workday](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/welcome-workday): Workday is UBC’s system for finance and HR processes – access your pay, book vacation, get tax information all in one place\.
-   [Research Services](https://it.ubc.ca/top-services-research): Discover services that support UBC researchers including high performance computing, research data management, and more\.
-   [Working Remotely](https://it.ubc.ca/ubc-it-guide-working-campus "UBC IT Guide to Working off Campus"): Find out what you need to work from off\-campus\.
-   Stay Cybersafe: Keep your information secure\. Follow UBC [guidelines on how to stay safe](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/resources/tips-students)\. If you become aware of a privacy or information security incident, contact [security@ubc\.ca](mailto:security@ubc.ca)\.

**Services for students:**

**M365**: Students are eligible for a Microsoft 365 subscription which includes the [Microsoft Office Suite](https://it.ubc.ca/services/desktop-support-services/software-licensing/microsoft-365-office-students "Microsoft 365 Office for Students")\. [Teams](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/microsoft-teams) is available for virtual meeting spaces, video conferences, group chats, and real\-time collaboration, and you can use [OneDrive](https://it.ubc.ca/services/web-servers-storage/microsoft-onedrive "Microsoft OneDrive") to securely store, share, and synchronize your files\.

-   [Student email](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/ubc-student-email-service "UBC Student Email Service"):  Students are eligible to sign up for a @student\.ubc\.ca email address\.
-   [Workday Student](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/): Access your student records, registration information, transcripts, and grades\.
