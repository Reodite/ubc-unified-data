## Overview

UBC Okanagan IT \(UBCOIT\) supports AV technology for centrally funded teaching spaces, otherwise known as***general use teaching*** spaces\. These spaces are available across campus and are managed and maintained by UBCOIT to ensure a consistent classroom experience for faculty and students\.

### Features &amp; Benefits

Most general use teaching spaces are equipped with hybrid audio and visual equipment to support remote and in\-person teaching and learning needs of the space, which may include:

-   Lectern\-based computers or connections for instructors’ laptop computers
-   Wireless presentation systems
-   Touch panel or button panel control systems
-   Document cameras \(large rooms only\)
-   Various microphone options

### Requirements &amp; Eligibility

-   Equipment in general use teaching spaces is available for use by any UBC staff, faculty or student during building operating hours
-   Faculty, staff, students and external groups may book general use space at UBC’s Okanagan campus\.  More information is available here: [Book Space](https://bookspace.ok.ubc.ca/)

### Costs

Spaces which are considered *general use teaching* spaces are shared resources available to any faculty, department, or student to use\.  The AV equipment, support, and repairs for these spaces are centrally funded\.

Academic spaces, labs, and meeting rooms managed by departments with access is restricted to specific users or groups are considered *department use* spaces\. The department is responsible for AV equipment purchases and repairs costs\.

For information on UBCOIT AV support, equipment maintenance and replacement for specialty or department owned spaces please submit a ticket for the [AV Services Team in the UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=28835cdb6dc334006da5c8aceaa441ee&amp;issue=Audio%20Visual)\.

### Further Information

-   Information about on\-campus bookable rooms can be found on the [UBCO Okanagan Learning Spaces](https://learningspaces.ok.ubc.ca/) website
-   For information on classroom availability, see [Online Timetable and Classroom Availability](https://cbo.ok.ubc.ca/resources/onlinetimetable/)
-   Book Space [Classroom Policies, Forms &amp; Resources](https://cbo.ok.ubc.ca/resources/)
-   Disability Resources [Instructional / Classroom Accommodations](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/disability-resources/instructional-classroom-accommodations/)
-   Information on general use computer labs, can be found here: [UBCO Computer labs](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/computers-printers/labs)

### Getting Started

-   To begin using UBC Okanagan classrooms, please refer to the [Self Support and Instructional Videos](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/audio-visual-creative-services/self-support/) on available classroom technologies

### Support

-   For general support, or to book an orientation session, please submit a request using the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)
-   Urgent, time\-sensitive support is available during regular office hours\. Contact UBCO IT at [250\-807\-9000](tel:2508079000) \(79000 from on campus\)
-   For Canvas, Kaltura, or Collaborate Ultra support, contact the [Centre for Teaching and Learning](https://ctl.ok.ubc.ca/) at 250\-807\-9293 \(79293 from on campus\)
