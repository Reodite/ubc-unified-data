## Overview

Phone services are available to employees on a cost recovery basis\.  There are both software\-based and hardware based telephone options available\. The UBCO Softphone Service enables users to make calls, control desk phones, manage contacts, view call history, and access voicemail via a desktop computer or mobile application, using the internet\.  This service is provided through an application called *MiCollab Client*\.

### Features &amp; Benefits

-   Uses an assigned UBC Okanagan phone number
-   Easy to use interface to make and manage calls
-   Removes the requirement to have a desk phone with an option to use a desk phone if preferred
-   Make calls from anywhere\!  Users can stay connected no matter where they are – at home, on campus, traveling, or wherever they are with an internet connection

### Requirements &amp; Eligibility

The service requires: 

-   An internet connection with good signal strength if using wireless
-   A computer with Windows or MacOS
-   A headset or built\-in computer speaker and microphone, when opting for Softphone only \(no desk phone\)

### Costs

The UBCO Softphone Service is $10 per month, including long\-distance\.  Staff can request a desk phone for an additional $5 per month\.

Pricing is subject to change, and will be communicated in advance\.

### Further Information

-   [Knowledge Article](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article&amp;sysparm_article=KB0019315)
-   [Quick Reference Guide](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article&amp;sysparm_article=KB0019317)

**Note:** Mobile phones are not provided by UBC\. Departments may opt to reimburse staff and faculty for personal mobile phones\. To determine eligibility, speak with your supervisor or department admin\.

### Getting Started

Use the following to request the UBCO Softphone Service:  [UBCO Voice Support Request](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_1Yx9fA0rDxsJJu6)

### Support

-   Support is available during UBCO IT regular business hours\. Troubleshooting off\-campus is not available for home network issues, peripheral devices, or non\-UBC computing devices\.
-   Due to the flexibility of the UBCO Softphone Service, support may be limited; specifically using the service on mobile phones or non\-UBC computing devices\.
