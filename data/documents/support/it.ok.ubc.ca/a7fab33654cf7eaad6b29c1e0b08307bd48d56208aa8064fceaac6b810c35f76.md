## UBC IT Okanagan

### IT Service Centre – Employee Support

**Quick question or urgent issue?**

#### Phone Support

**7:30 am to 4:30 pm** Weekdays \(Closed Stat Holidays\)

-   [250\-807\-9000](tel:250-807-9000) \(on\-campus dial 79000\)
-   [855\-807\-9001](tel:855-807-9001) \(Toll free\)

#### Online Support

Try the 24 hour [**UBCO Knowledge Assistant \(Chatbot\)**](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/knowledge-assistant)

[UBCO IT Chatbot](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/knowledge-assistant)

**Computer or software issue? Need to speak with a technician?** Please submit a ticket\.

[Submit a Ticket](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=17a3df611b9129105a0999b4bd4bcbdb)

For research software or hardware support, please submit a ticket to the Research Support team\.
