We provide web content management system and hosting services to help promote UBC Okanagan faculties, departments, and administrative units\.

### UBCO Content Management System \(OKCMS\)

UBC IT Okanagan hosts a website content management system \(OKCMS\) based on WordPress\. Serves as a common platform that enables sustainable creation and on\-going maintenance of websites with the use of UBC Common Look and Feel \(or CLF\)\.

[Learn about the UBCO CMS](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/web-publishing-hosting/ubco-cms/)

### ePayment \(CBM\)

With UBC’s [Digital Payment Program](https://vpfo-dpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/) to replace ePayment, Online Payment Form requests or ePayment onboarding requests will be redirected to DPP\. 

[Learn more](https://epayment.it.ubc.ca/news/digital-payment-program-dpp)

ePayment allows departments/units to accept credit card payments and have the monies deposited to a specified account/speedchart through UBC’s Financial Management System \(FMS\)\.

[Learn more about ePayment](https://epayment.it.ubc.ca/)

### UBCO Subdomains

Faculties, departments, and UBC\-affiliated organizations based at the Okanagan campus may apply for a fourth\-level Internet subdomain in order to be registered under ok\.ubc\.ca\.

[Learn about UBCO Subdomains](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/web-publishing-hosting/ubco-subdomains/)

### Wikis

Wikis allow easy creation and editing of collaborative resources websites\. UBC IT Okanagan offers private wikis to Okanagan campus faculty and staff\.

[Learn about Wikis](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/services/web-publishing-hosting/wikis/)
