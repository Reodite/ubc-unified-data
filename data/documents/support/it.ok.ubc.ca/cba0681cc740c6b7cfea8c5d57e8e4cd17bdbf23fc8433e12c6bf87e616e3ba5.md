UBC IT Staff Hit the Ground Running at Vancouver Sun Run

Congratulations to our staff members who represented UBC IT at the Vancouver Sun Run\! The Vancouver Sun Run is Canada’s largest 10K road race\. On April 19, 2026, the 42 \[nd\] annual Sun Run featured approximately 57,500 registered participants, representing the second\-highest total in the event’s history\.  

Our staff kept the momentum going and represented [Move UBC](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/move-ubc) with pride\. Most importantly, everyone made it across the finish line\! While the event focused on promoting team wellness, it also included a bit of friendly competition as the two fastest runners each received a $50 voucher\. Congratulations to our top finishers, Benny Rajan and Swati Mittal\. 

A massive thank you to all our runners, walkers, and amazing cheerleaders who supported us along the way\. Way to go Team UBC IT\!
