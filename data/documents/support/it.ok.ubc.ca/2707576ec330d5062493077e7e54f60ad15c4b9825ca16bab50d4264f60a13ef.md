The Mitel Softphone service provides voice services over the Internet including access to voicemail, call history, and contact information\.

**Mobile &amp; Services**

IT Services no longer provides managed services for mobile phones or cellular plans\.  Departments may opt to reimburse staff and faculty for personal cellular plan use, or cover a plan at the department level\.  UBC does have access to corporate plans and employee discount rates through our telecom provider partners\.  More information on deals available to UBC can be found on the finance website: [UBC Finance Portal](https://finance.share.ubc.ca/buy-pay/SitePages/Cellular-devices-and-services.aspx) \(CWL required\)\.
