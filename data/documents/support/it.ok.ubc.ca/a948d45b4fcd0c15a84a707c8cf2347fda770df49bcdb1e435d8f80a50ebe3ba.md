UBCO Gitea is changing the way users sign in\. Beginning August 13, 2026, we are transitioning authentication from LDAP to OpenID Connect \(OIDC\) using UBC’s CWL Service\.

If you already have a UBCO Gitea account that was created using LDAP, you will need to **link your existing account** to the new OIDC sign\-in **by September 30, 2026**\.

Linking your account is important because it ensures you continue using your existing Gitea profile, including your repositories, permissions, and settings\.

## What do I need to do?

The next time you [sign in to Gitea](https://gitea.ok.ubc.ca/), follow these steps:

### 1\. Choose OpenID Connect

Go to the UBCO Gitea login page and select **OpenID Connect** as your login option\.

[Image](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/24/2026/08/Choose-OpenID-Connect.png)

### 2\. Sign in with your CWL

You will be redirected to UBC’s sign\-in service\. Authenticate using your **CWL credentials**\.

[Image](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/24/2026/08/Sign-in-with-your-CWL-1024x768.png)

### 3\. Link your existing account

After signing in, Gitea will ask whether you want to Register a New Account or Link an Existing Account\.

If you have previously used Gitea with LDAP, select **Link an Existing Account**\.

**Do not select “Register a New Account”** if you already have a Gitea account\. Doing so may create a duplicate account\.

[Image](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/24/2026/08/Link-your-existing-account.png)

### 4\. Enter your previous Gitea credentials

Enter the LDAP username and password you previously used to sign in to Gitea, along with the displayed CAPTCHA\.

This allows Gitea to connect your CWL/OIDC identity with your existing account\.

[Image](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/24/2026/08/Enter-your-previous-Gitea-credentials.png)

### 5\. You’re done

Once the accounts are linked, you should be signed in to your usual Gitea profile with your existing repositories, permissions, and settings\.

From then on, use **OpenID Connect** and your CWL credentials when signing in to Gitea\.

## When do I need to do this?

The transition begins August 13, 2026\.

Please link your existing Gitea account **no later than September 30, 2026**\.

Completing the process before the deadline will help ensure uninterrupted access as we move away from LDAP authentication\.

## Why are we making this change?

Moving Gitea authentication to OpenID Connect provides:

-   Improved security and compliance
-   Better integration with UBC authentication and Single Sign\-On
-   A simpler sign\-in experience using your CWL
-   Improved account management

## Need help?

If you accidentally create a new account instead of linking your existing account, or encounter problems during the linking process, please [contact the UBCO IT support team](https://it.ok.ubc.ca/support/) for assistance\.
