Data management plan templates available to help researchers

A new Data Management Plan \(DMP\) pre\-funding template, available through DMP Assistant, has been customized for the UBC research community\. The simplified template helps researchers thoughtfully plan their data management from the start of their project while meeting funder requirements\. 

[Learn more](https://rdm.ubc.ca/data-management-plans)
