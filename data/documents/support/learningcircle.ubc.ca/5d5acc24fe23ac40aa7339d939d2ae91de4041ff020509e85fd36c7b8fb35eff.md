# [Image](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/09/1022-banner.jpg)

Land\-based fitness is a blueprint for using Land\-based exercise and holistic teachings to connect to traditional activities, improve health, and explore the world around us\. While the research for Land\-based Fitness was done within an Indigenous worldview to revitalize cultural learning, this workshop is for everyone who would like to learn more ways to enjoy functional \(and fun\!\) movement\. Wear comfortable clothing as we will be moving our bodies with this webinar\!

### **About the Presenters:**

[Image](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/09/Alana-Headshot-200x300.jpg)  

**Alana Cook**

Alana is a proud Métis woman from the Desjarlais family of St\. Francois\-Xavier with Saulteaux, Scottish, and French ancestry\.  A former adjunct professor and a current PhD student at the University of British Columbia, Alana also moonlights as a professional MMA fighter and the creator of Walking Like Warriors Self\-Defence\. A graduate of the Masters of Educational Foundations program in the Indigenous Land\-based Cohort from USask, Alana looks forward to sharing her research on Land\-based fitness with you\!
