## [Research Circle: Michele A\. Sam “Indigenous Knowledge and Research”](https://learningcircle.ubc.ca/2013/04/11/research-circle-michele-a-sam-indigenous-knowledge-and-research/)

Join Michele A\. Sam \(Ktunaxa\) for an engaging presentation at the UBC Learning Circle\! Michele will be sharing Indigenous approaches to developing research relationships that support Indigenous knowledge relationships\. She will examine the context between institutional research approaches and community\-based knowledge needs\. You will learn about how research can be de\-myth\-ified in useful, meaningful ways \[…\]
