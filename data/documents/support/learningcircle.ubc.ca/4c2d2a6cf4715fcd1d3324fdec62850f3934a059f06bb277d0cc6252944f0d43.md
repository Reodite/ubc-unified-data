### **About the Presenters:**

[Image](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/angel-169x300.jpg)  

**Angel Miness** is a proud Ojibwe woman from Long Plain First Nation in Treaty 1 Territory, Manitoba\. She brings both lived experience and a strong professional background, working directly in a Health Care Setting\. This experience provided valuable insight into the importance of holistic, person\-centered care and continues to shape her approach to supporting the health and wellness of First Nations individuals, families, and communities\.

In her role with FNHA, Angel works closely with First Nations communities, their members, and health partners to build meaningful relationships and increase awareness of available Health Benefits programs and services\. She provides community outreach, education, and support by sharing information, answering questions, addressing concerns, and helping connect community members with the resources they need\. Through community visits, presentations, and ongoing engagement, Angel is committed to supporting access to culturally safe health benefits and services that contribute to overall wellness\.
