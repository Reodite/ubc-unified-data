# [Image](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/a4.jpg)

Join featured speaker Reid Hala for an engaging webinar exploring the vital role of Michif language\(s\) in shaping Métis identity, culture, and wellbeing across their traditional homeland\. Discover how Michif carries generations of knowledge, connection, and resilience, making it central to the strength of Métis communities\. The session will also highlight inspiring, community\-driven efforts to revitalize Michif and ensure it thrives for future generations\.

### **About the Presenters:**

[Image](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/Reid_headshot-277x300.jpg)  

**Reid** is a linguist working for Metis Nation British Columbia in the ministry of Culture, Heritage &amp; Language\. For the past six years he has been earnestly learning the language known as Southern Michif from first\-language speakers, along with the culture and history of Michif\-speaking communities\. His journey started in 2020 when he began learning and researching Michif to earn his MA in Applied Linguistics, and his professional career has been focused on Michif language and culture ever since\. He is passionate about building language into every bit of work he does for the ministry and is pleased to be able to share what he has learned with anyone willing to listen\.
