Join the UBC Aboriginal Youth Learning Circle  for an exciting and interactive session with [YouthCO HIV and Hep C Community Outreach](http://youthco.org/)\! **Jessica St\. Jean**, Program Manager of YouthCO’s Aboriginal Youth Program and **Dakota Prince** will be presenting up\-to\-date, non\-judgmental information about sex and sexuality  that is key to keeping youth healthy\. This workshop will explore stereotyping, social determinants of health, STI testing, HIV prevention and much more\!

---

All youth \(and adults who work with them\) are welcome to attend and participate in this FREE live videoconference and computer webinar session\.

**Date**: Wednesday, September 25, 2013

**Time**: 10 a\.m\. to 12 p\.m\.

**Where**: Participate live via videoconference **OR** computer webinar\. *Want to know the difference between videoconference and computer webinar? Click [**here**](https://learningcircle.ubc.ca/2013/04/whats-the-difference-between-videoconference-and-webinar/) to find out\.*
