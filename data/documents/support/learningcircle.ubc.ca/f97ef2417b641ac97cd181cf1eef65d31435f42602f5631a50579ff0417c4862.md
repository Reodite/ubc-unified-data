Indigenous students \(undergraduate and graduate\) are invited to learn about internal and external funding resources and Indigenous financial resiliency with campus and community partners\. This is a partnership event with UBC Indigenous Graduate Initiatives\.

---

### We would like to reiterate that everyone is welcome to our UBCLC sessions\.

Our events aim to embody a safe space for everyone of all different backgrounds to have their opinions and voices equally heard\.

**Time:** 10 a\.m\. to 11:30 am
