Do you remember how concerned we were about H1N1 last year? We are all glad that the H1N1 influenza pandemic is over, but what did we learn from the experience? Can we apply what we learned in future flu seasons? This is important because every  year the flu still causes illness and contributes to deaths in our communities, and no one knows when another pandemic might occur\.

Would you like to have the opportunity to discuss the response to the H1N1 pandemic in BC First Nations?
