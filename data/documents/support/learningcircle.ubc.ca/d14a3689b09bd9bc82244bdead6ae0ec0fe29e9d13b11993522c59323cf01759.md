[https://med\-fom\-learningcircle\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2011/12/082\.jpg](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/12/082.jpg)[OLYMPUS DIGITAL CAMERA](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/12/082-259x300.jpg)On **Wednesday, January 11th, 2012**, from **1:00 – 4:00pm**, Judy Davies\-North will return to the UBC Learning Circle to lead a workshop entitled: “Helping the Hurting: Suicide Intervention Training”\.

Have a question about suicide or working with the suicidal individual? Please send in questions which will be incorporated into this 3 hour afternoon presentation\.

-   Current topics – How do we:  
    Know if someone is considering suicide?  
    Ask about suicide in such a way as to receive an honest response?  
    Respond to the suicidal person?  
    Create a safety plan?  
    Take care of ourselves when working with the suicidal?

**About the Speaker**

Until recently Judy \(Davies\) North was employed as the Child &amp; Youth Suicide Clinician with a Vancouver Coastal Health crisis team\.  She gave up that position to begin private consultation and training regarding suicide intervention and postvention, and critical incident stress debriefings\.  She can be reached at [judy@endsuicide\.ca](mailto:judy@endsuicide.ca) to obtain suicide and crisis related consultation or to arrange highly informative and interactive trainings\.
