On **November 24\[th\], 2010**, Christine Westland &amp; Janet Christie will discuss **Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder \(FASD\)** with the UBC Learning Circle\.

Chris\. Westland is a RN who has worked in maternal childcare, psychiatry, addictions and most recently the adolescent mental health consultant for FNIH\. She has been presenting workshops on FASD for several years with an emphasis on supporting women with addictions in pregnancy and child raising years\.

Janet Christie is a birth mom, facilitator of moms groups and a trainer\.  She has worked as a FASD key worker and written a manual on how to set up programs to prevent FASD\.
