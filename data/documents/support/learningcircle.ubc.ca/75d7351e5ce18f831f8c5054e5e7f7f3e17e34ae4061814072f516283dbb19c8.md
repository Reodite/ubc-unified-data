# [Image](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/UBCLC-Promo-banners-2.jpg)

This webinar will go over the benefits and credit payments that Indigenous people could be eligible for and how to apply for them\. Additionally, it will also cover how filing your taxes each year helps to ensure that you keep getting these benefits\.

## Resources

-   **For General Inquires, please visit: [https://www\.canada\.ca/en/revenue\-agency/corporate/contact\-information/individual\-income\-tax\-enquiries\-line\.html](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/corporate/contact-information/individual-income-tax-enquiries-line.html)**
    -   **Or Phone 1\-800\-959\-8281\.**
-   If you need help determining if land is on a reserve, visit the Crown\-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs Canada webpage at [canada\.ca/crown\-indigenous\-relations\-northern\-affairs](https://www.canada.ca/en/crown-indigenous-relations-northern-affairs.html?utm_campaign=not-applicable&amp;utm_medium=vanity-url&amp;utm_source=canada-ca_crown-indigenous-relations-northern-affairs)\.
-   To find out if you are entitled to be registered under the Indian Act and how to apply for Indian status, go to the Indigenous Services Canada webpage at [canada\.ca/indian\-status](https://www.sac-isc.gc.ca/eng/1100100032374/1572457769548)\.
-   For more information on tax\-exempt employment\-related income and non\-exempt income, visit [canada\.ca/section87\-tax\-exemption](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/indigenous-peoples/information-indians.html?utm_campaign=not-applicable&amp;utm_medium=vanity-url&amp;utm_source=canada-ca_section87-tax-exemption) or call 1\-800\-959\-8281\.
-   To do your taxes, you need to have a social insurance number, also known as a SIN\. If you don’t have a SIN, call Service Canada at 1\-800\-622\-6232
-   For more information on the GST/HST credit, visit [canada\.ca/gst\-hst\-credit](http://canada.ca/gst-hst-credit)
-   For more information on the DTC, the benefits, credits, and deductions you may be entitled to, and long\-term savings programs provided by the Government of Canada, visit [canada\.ca/disability\-tax\-credit](http://canada.ca/disability-tax-credit)
-   Child Disability Benefit: [canada\.ca/child\-disability\-benefit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/segments/tax-credits-deductions-persons-disabilities/disability-tax-credit.html?utm_campaign=not-applicable&amp;utm_medium=vanity-url&amp;utm_source=canada-ca_disability-tax-credit)
-   Canada Workers benefit: [canada\.ca/canada\-workers\-benefit](http://canada.ca/canada-workers-benefit)
-   Canadian Dental Care Plan: [canada\.ca/dental](http://canada.ca/dental) or  call 1\-833\-537\-4342
-   Benefit Finder: [canada\.ca/benefits\-finder](http://canada.ca/benefits-finder)
-   CRA Calculator: [canada\.ca/child\-family\-benefits\-calculator](http://canada.ca/child-family-benefits-calculator)
-    Free tax clinic: [canada\.ca/get\-tax\-help](http://canada.ca/get-tax-help)
-    Tax Service Provider: [canada\.ca/efile\-individuals](http://canada.ca/efile-individuals)
-    Certified software products available: [canada\.ca/netfile](http://canada.ca/netfile)
-    Download a tax package at [canada\.ca/taxes\-general\-package](http://canada.ca/taxes-general-package) or call the CRA at 1\-855\-330\-3305
-   Uncashed cheques: [canada\.ca/cra\-uncashed\-cheques](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/uncashed-cheque.html?utm_campaign=not-applicable&amp;utm_medium=vanity-url&amp;utm_source=canada-ca_cra-uncashed-cheques)
-   CRA My Account: [canada\.ca/my\-cra\-account](http://canada.ca/my-cra-account)
-   CRA Factsheets: [canada\.ca/outreach\-materials](http://canada.ca/outreach-materials)
-   Learn about taxes: [canada\.ca/learn\-about\-taxes](http://canada.ca/learn-about-taxes)
-   Report a scam to the Canadian Anti\-Fraud Centre at [antifraudcentre\.ca](http://antifraudcentre.ca/) or by calling 1\-888\-495\-8501\.
