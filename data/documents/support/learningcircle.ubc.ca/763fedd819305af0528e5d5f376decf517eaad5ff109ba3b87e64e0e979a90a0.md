[https://med\-fom\-learningcircle\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2011/03/photo840\.png](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/03/photo840.png)[photo840](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/03/photo840.png)On **Wednesday, March 16th, 2011**, representatives from the Mary Pack Arthritis Program will discuss “Living with Arthritis: Common Challenges and What to Do with Them” with the UBC Learning Circle\.

Tips on managing Arthritis pain from an Occupational therapist and Physiotherapist from the Victoria Arthritis Centre \(also known as the Mary Pack Arthritis Program in Victoria\.\) Both Penny Bond, PT  and Charles Young, OT have traveled on multiple outreach clinics to both the Namgis Nation in Alert Bay and more recently to the Carrier Sekani Nation in the Prince George area\.

**About the Mary Pack Arthritis Program**

The Mary Pack Arthritis Program has 4 sites in BC – Vancouver, Cranboook, Penticton, and Victoria\. All sites offer services to any one who has Arthritis\.
