Please mark your calendars for a OneMatch Stem Cell &amp; Marrow Network Presentation\!  \(There is a FN shortage of donors, find out more information and how you can help\!\)

When: October 17th, 2012, 10:00 am\-11:30 am

Where: Videoconference, in partnership with the UBC Learning Circle

This presentation will focus on the Stem Cell &amp; Marrow Network\.  The Canadian Blood Services is a program dedicated to recruiting healthy, committed volunteer donors for patients in need of stem cells\. **Becky Luk, Patient &amp; Transplant Liaison Specialist** and **Dr\. Tanya Petraszko, Medical Director of the CBS OneMatch Cord Blood Program**, will lead a session for the UBC Learning Circle\. They will be speaking about stem cells, the current shortage of First Nations donors, and how to become a donor\. Joining her will be **Genie and Gabe** to share their personal experiences\.

\*\*Missed the Learning Circle? What to read ahead? Click **[here](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2012/10/October-17-OneMatch-PowerPoint-PDF.pdf)** to view the material which will be used at this presentation\.\*\*
