**Date**: Thursday, May 14, 2015

This special session organized by **Suzanne Johnson**, Program Nutritionist at the [First Nations health Authority](http://www.fnha.ca/) features two school programs that are making a difference in children’s nutrition and promoting healthy lives\. Representatives from [Farm to School BC](https://farmtoschoolbc.ca/) and [DASH BC](http://dashbc.ca/) will talk about their programs and help you to get started planning to bring more health promotion opportunities to your next school year\. This session will highlight several of the provincial Healthy School\-related initiatives that provide opportunities for holistic health promotion in schools\.  First Nations schools can access these initiatives which in some cases provide Aboriginal\-specific resources as well as training and funding opportunities\.

---

#### **Session Topics**

1.  Learn about three agencies that provide support in the promotion of improved school health\.
2.  Discover how easy and applicable these programs can be for First Nations schools and hear some First Nations schools success stories\.
3.  Discuss the possibility of coordinating future webinar/videoconference training for communities\.

---

#### **About the Organizations**

**Farm to School BC** was established in 2007 and is a diverse and expanding provincial network that promotes, supports, and links Farm to School activity, policy and programs across the province\. Farm to School programs bring healthy, local food into schools and provide students with hands\-on learning opportunities that foster food literacy, all while strengthening the local food system and enhancing school and community connectedness\.

**The Directorate of Agencies for School Health \(DASH BC\)** is a not\-for\-profit society committed to promoting, supporting and facilitating the creation of healthy schools in BC\. DASH has a vision that ALL students are healthy, engaged, and connected at school\. We use our deep understanding of a Comprehensive School Health \(CSH\) approach and our network of leaders to deliver and facilitate innovative healthy schools initiatives to directly benefit the health and learning of students and school communities\.
