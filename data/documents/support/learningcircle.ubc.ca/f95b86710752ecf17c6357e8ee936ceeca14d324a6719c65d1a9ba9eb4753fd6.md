This workshop will take a user\-friendly approach to technology and explore the FNHA’s progress in developing a telehealth network in First Nations communities across BC\. 

---

**Session Topics**

*Introduction to Telehealth*

-   What is it?
-   How can it be used to improve health &amp; wellness?

*Telehealth Expansion Project Overview*

-   Approach
-   Readiness &amp; Needs Assessment Process
-   Analysis
-   Implementation
-   Data
-   Opportunities

*Questions &amp; Answers*
