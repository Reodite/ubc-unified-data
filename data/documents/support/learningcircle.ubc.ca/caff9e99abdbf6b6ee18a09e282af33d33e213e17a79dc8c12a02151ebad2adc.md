Please join us for an important First Nations Health Council update to BC First Nations on Thursday, September 29th, 2011 from 10am to 12pm\. Chief Federal Negotiator Ian Potter, Grand Chief Doug Kelly, and Arlene Patton \(ADM Population and Public Health\), will discuss the Tripartite framework agreement, evolving relationships between First Nations and the federal government, and the next steps in transition\.
