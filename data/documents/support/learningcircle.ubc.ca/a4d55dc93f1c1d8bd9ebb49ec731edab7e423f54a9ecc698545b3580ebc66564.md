## Cycle of Juvenile Justice: The Crisis of Aboriginal Youth

The **UBC Learning Circle** is partnering with the **SFU First Nations Student Association** to present the second in a series of speaker panels that examine the depths of Aboriginal youth in the criminal justice system, policy measures, and community programming\.

---

**Date**: Wednesday, March 6, 2013

**Time**: 6:30 p\.m\. to 9:30 p\.m\.

**Place**: Via video conference

---

## *Panelists include:*

**Flora Raynes**, Aboriginal Specialist, Youth Course Native Courtworkers  
**Pamela Shields**, Barrister &amp; Solicitor, Aboriginal Services Legal Society  
**Richard Lavelle**, Aboriginal Liaison, Vancouver Police Department  
**Stephen Anderson**, Bail Supervisor &amp; Probation Office, Ministry of Justice  
**Steve Hanuse**, Constable, Musqueam Liaison
