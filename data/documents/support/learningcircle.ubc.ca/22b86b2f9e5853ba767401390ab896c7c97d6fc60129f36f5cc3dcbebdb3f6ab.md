**The BC Services Card**

Please mark your calendars to hear about the new BC Services Card coming soon\!

In early 2013, the B\.C\. government will begin issuing the new BC Services Card, which will replace the CareCard and serve as both a health card and government\-issued identification\.

Please join the Ministry of Health project team on **Thursday, November 15 from 1:00\-2:00 pm** to learn about what’s new, get a sneak peak of the new card, and hear about its benefits followed by an interactive question and answer session\.

The presentation will be held via video conference **OR** Adobe Connect in partnership with the UBC Learning Circle\.

For more information on the BC Services Card, please visit [BCServicesCard\.ca](https://learningcircle.ubc.ca/2012/10/25/save-the-date-november-15-2012/BCServicesCard.ca)\.
