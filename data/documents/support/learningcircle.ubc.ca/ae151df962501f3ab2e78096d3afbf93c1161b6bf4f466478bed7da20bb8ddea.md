## [ENT in the North with Dr\. Michael Clifford Fabian](https://learningcircle.ubc.ca/2011/04/20/ent-in-the-north-with-dr-michael-fabian/)

On Wednesday, April 20th, 2011, Dr\. Michael Clifford Fabian will discuss ENT in the North with the UBC Learning Circle\. This talk will cover medicine \(particularly Otolaryngology/Head and Neck Surgery\) in the North\.  Some of the challenges with medical care for rural, remote and Aboriginal communities in the North with will be outlined\.
