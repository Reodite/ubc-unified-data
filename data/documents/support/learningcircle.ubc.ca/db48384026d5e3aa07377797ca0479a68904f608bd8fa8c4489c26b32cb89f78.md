## [November 4th, 2025 – Indigenous Youth Access of Support for Mental Health and Wellness with Dr\. Melanie Nelson and Adam Williams\-Fakhari](https://learningcircle.ubc.ca/2025/09/08/indigenous-youth-support-for-mental-health-and-wellness/)

Indigenous youth navigate Western and Indigenous worlds, and draw from both to support their mental health and wellness\. This session describes how youth from one Lower Mainland community support their wellness and will be followed by a Q&amp;A session with the researcher and a UBC student who is a youth from the community\.
