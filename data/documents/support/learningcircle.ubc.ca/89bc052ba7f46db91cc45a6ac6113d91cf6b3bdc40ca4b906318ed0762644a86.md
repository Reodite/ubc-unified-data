#### **The UBC Learning Circle is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and productive environment so that you can fully engage in learning with your peers and colleagues\.**

The UBC Learning Circle is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and productive environment so that you can fully engage in learning with your peers and colleagues\. We appreciate the investment of your time to be with us in a culturally safe and free\-speaking space\. Every one of us in this community has the responsibility to create and maintain this environment by exercising consideration in our speech and actions and refraining from demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behaviour and speech\.

#### **The UBC Learning Circle does not tolerate:** 

-   -   -   #### Any form of discrimination related to race, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, or religion 
            
        -   #### Violence, threats, or harassment directed towards any of our participants
            

Violations of these policies may result in a written warning, followed by removal from our webinar or event if the policies are not adhered to\. If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns about our community guidelines, we welcome you to contact us at [learning\.circle@ubc\.ca](mailto:learning.circle@ubc.ca)\.
