Come get to know Ivo and Jessica\. They’ll be facilitating an eCircle every month on different topics\!

On Wednesday, November 23rd, 2011, they’ll discuss:  
• Condoms

• Sexuality

**About the Speakers**

**Ivo Haggerty**, is Cree of Cowessess, born and raised on Coast Salish Territories \(aka the East Side of Vancouver\), and is a queer two\-spirited male\-bodied, non\-violence paciFIST who has a passion for challenging beliefs/values and disarming passive stigma through cooperative dialogues\. Ivo is also a slam poet and die\-hard Star Wars fan\. Likes bannock; dislikes the 2010 Olympics\.  
**Jessica St\. Jean** is from the Squamish Nation of North Vancouver\. She became passionate about social justice, activism and sexuality through volunteering at a transition house and crisis line while attending university\. She is excited to work with the youth of our communities to empower through knowledge sharing, breaking down stigma and opening discussion around colonization and our sexuality\.
