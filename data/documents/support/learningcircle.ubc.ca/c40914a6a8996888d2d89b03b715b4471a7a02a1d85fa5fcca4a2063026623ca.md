With the legalization of cannabis, more and more individuals are inquiring about its’ use for medical purposes\. In this Learning Circle, the presenters will cover the following topics:

-   What is Marijuana?
-   Safety of medicinal cannabis
-   Efficacy of medicinal cannabis on selected medical conditions
-   Types of preparations available for medicinal cannabis

### Meet Our Presenters:

[Image](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/02/Larry-Leung-Headshot-200x300.jpg)**Larry Leung** is a Lecturer, Interprofessional Co\-Lead and Clinical pharmacist at the Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences, University of British Columbia\. As a pharmacist, he works at the Faculty’s Pharmacists Clinic, a university\-affiliated, licensed, pharmacist\-led patient care clinic\. As a Lecturer, he teaches in the Entry\-to\-Practice PharmD program and is a course coordinator for a first year seminars course and co\-coordinator of an Indigenous Health elective\. He is also the interprofessional education co\-lead for the Faculty\. His areas of interest include primary care and Indigenous Health\. Particularly, he has a strong passion for collaborating with Indigenous communities in the development and delivery of culturally\-safe pharmacy services\. Larry will be joined by **Lily Chen** is a fourth year pharmacy student in the Entry\-to\-Practice PharmD Program\. She is currently on an experiential non\-direct patient care practicum focused on academic teaching\.
