### **About the Presenters:**

[Image](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/09/IMG_4641-scaled.jpg)  

**Elijah Buffalo** is a Nehiyaw from the Samson Cree Nation in Maskwacis, Alberta, Treaty 6\. He lives in Lekwungen territory with his family and attends the University of Victoria where he studies and researches the role and function of sports in Indigenous identity, culture, and medicine\.
