## [Introduction to Trauma\-Informed Practice with Diane Smylie](https://learningcircle.ubc.ca/2013/04/30/introduction-to-trauma-informed-practice/)

The UBC Learning Circle is pleased to present an introductory workshop by Diane Smylie \(MSW, RSW\) on “Trauma\-Informed Practice\.” This 2\-hour workshop will introduce key concepts and principles of trauma\-informed practice\. Trauma\-informed services take into account an understanding of trauma in all aspects of service delivery and place priority on the person’s safety, choice and control\. \[…\]
