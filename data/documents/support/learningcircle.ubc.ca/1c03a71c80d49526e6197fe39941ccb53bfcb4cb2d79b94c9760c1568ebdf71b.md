The UBC Learning Circle invites Aboriginal Health and Education Directors, Aboriginal Human Resources, and Aboriginal students to a special live presentation on **two** exciting initiatives\.

The **UBC Aboriginal Health Strategy Working Group** will present on the proposed **UBC Centre of Excellence in Indigenous Health**\.

The **Cedar Project Partnership** will present on their multi\-year community\-based research project that will address the impact of historical trauma – specifically, parental attendance at residential schools – on the health of young Aboriginal people who are at risk for HIV and Hepatitis C\.

---

**Date**: Wednesday, May 29, 2013

**Time**: 1:00pm – 3:00p\.m\. PST

**Place:** Participate by video conference OR online via computer webinar \(Adobe Connect\)\.

*Not sure which method to choose? Click **[here](https://learningcircle.ubc.ca/2013/04/whats-the-difference-between-videoconference-and-webinar/)** to learn about the difference between video conference and computer webinar\.*

**Who Should Attend**: This session is oriented towards Health and Education Directors, Human Resource professionals, Aboriginal leadership and current or future post\-secondary students but any interested individuals are welcome to attend\.

**[Download a poster](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/05/May.29.2013.AHSWG_.Cedar_.Project1.pdf)** to share with your colleagues and friends\!

**Session Objectives**

The UBC Aboriginal Health Strategy Working group would like to engage Indigenous peoples in BC about the proposed Centre of Excellence in Indigenous Health\. We would like your feedback on the working draft of the proposal and how we can best support you in your goals of achieving wellness in your communities\.
