### **About the Presenters:**

[Image](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/9e7e1fe9feba4ae4311019ae4be69db6.png)  

Martin Morberg is a Two\-Spirit Northern Tutchone and Tlingit man born and raised in the Yukon Territory\. He is a member of the Na Cho Nyak Dun First Nation\. Much of Martin’s work and activism is rooted in community and grassroots initiatives and he acknowledges that many Indigenous leaders and community members have guided and supported him in growing into the activist he is today\. He hopes to pay this knowledge and support forward to Indigenous communities and Two\-Spirit people and contribute to the meaningful work and reclamation of Two\-Spirit culture\.
