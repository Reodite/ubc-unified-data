## Aboriginal Offenders: The Generational Healing

The UBC Learning Circle is once again partnering with the SFU First Nations Student Association for our second Aboriginal Criminology Series Event\. Panelists at this session will discuss the historical evolution of the Canadian Criminal Justice System and the move from Western methods of justice to incorporating Aboriginal values and ways of healing\. We encourage all to attend this free event\.

---

**When**: Wednesday, March 27, 2013

**Time**: 6:30 to 9:30 p\.m\.

**Where**: Via video conference **OR** online via Adobe Connect \(for computers\)

---

### **Panelists:** 

***Doug Dowhaniuk***, Correctional Program Officer, Correctional Service of Canada 

***Arthur Paul***, Regional Manager, Native Courtworkers and Counselling Association of BC

***Gerald Adams***, Executive Director, Circle of Eagle Lodges Society

***Laura Baird***, Aboriginal Community Development Officer, Correctional Service of Canada
