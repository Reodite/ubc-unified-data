## [March 13th, 2025 – Between Membership &amp; Belonging: Life Under Section 10 of the Indian Act with Dr\. Damien Lee](https://learningcircle.ubc.ca/2025/02/07/damien-lee-membership-belonging/)

2025 marks the 40th anniversary of section 10 band membership – an aspect of the Indian Act that allows First Nations to determine their own membership lists\.
