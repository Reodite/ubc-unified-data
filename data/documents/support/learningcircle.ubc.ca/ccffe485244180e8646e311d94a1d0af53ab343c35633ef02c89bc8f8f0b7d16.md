**Jessica St\. Jean**, Program Manager of [YouthCO’s Aboriginal Youth Program](http://youthco.nationbuilder.com/programs) and **Dakota Prince,** Outreach Educator, will be presenting a workshop about contraception, or “Sex: how to have the fun part without the babies\!”

Babies are awesome when we’re ready and family planning is important for taking care of ourselves and others\. This session will feature a non\-judgmental talk about our contraception options\. We promise to make this as fun as possible\!

---

All youth \(and adults who work with them\) are welcome to attend and participate in this FREE live videoconference and computer webinar session\.

**Date**: Wednesday, November 13, 2013

**Time**: 10 a\.m\. to 12 p\.m\.

**Where**: Participate live via videoconference **OR** computer webinar\. *Want to know the difference between videoconference and computer webinar? Click [**here**](https://learningcircle.ubc.ca/2013/04/whats-the-difference-between-videoconference-and-webinar/) to find out\.*

**About the Presenters**

Formerly an educator at *Healing Our Spirit*, **Jessica St\. Jean \(**Program Manager of YouthCO’s Aboriginal Youth Program\) is passionate about working with our communities to share knowledge and have some fun while we’re at it\. This passion, Jessica believes, comes simply from being a Squamish woman\. As her mentor and friend says, “we were the original feminists and the original hippies\.”

**Dakota Prince** is the new Yúusnewas Outreach Educator at YouthCO\. She is a Cree and Ojibway youth from Vancouver, BC\. Dakota has been working with the community with other organizations from theatre arts to digital media since she was 15\. Working with fellow youth around important issues has been the most fulfilling “work” she has done\.
