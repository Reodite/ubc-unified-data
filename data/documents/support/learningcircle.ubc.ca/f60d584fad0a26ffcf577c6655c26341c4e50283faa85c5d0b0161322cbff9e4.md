### **About the Presenters:**

[Image](https://med-fom-learningcircle.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/09/joyce-768x1104.png)  

**Joyce Fossella** is from the Lil’wat nation\. Joyce has worked in the Indigenous community for over thirty years in education, health and wellness\.  She is the Executive Director of Warriors Against Violence Society and has worked there for over 25 years\. Joyce has presented at various conferences nationally and internationally regarding a solution to violence in the Indigenous community\.
