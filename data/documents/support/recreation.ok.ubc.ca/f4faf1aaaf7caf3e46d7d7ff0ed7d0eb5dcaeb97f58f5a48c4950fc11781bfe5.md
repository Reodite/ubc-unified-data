UBC Okanagan faculty and staff can participate in almost all recreation programs and activities, including [Group Fitness](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fitness-programs/group/), [Instructional Programs](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fitness-programs/instructional/) and [Register &amp; Play](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/sports/register-play/)\. We also help faculty and staff incorporate more movement into their day with the [**Move U crew**](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/about/move-u/)\.

### [Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2025/08/SStretching-150x150.png)

### Movement breaks

Request the Move U Crew to lead a free guided movement break for your class, meeting, seminar or event\.

[Get started](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/about/movement-breaks/)

### [Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2025/08/Cornhole-1-150x150.png)

### Lawn game rentals

Add some excitement to your event with our complimentary lawn game rentals for on\-campus departments\.

[Learn more](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/facilities/lawn-games/)

### [Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2025/08/18-150x150.png)

### Memberships

All recreation memberships include access to the Hangar Fitness Centre, group fitness classes, and open gym\.

[Memberships and rates](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/membership-rates#rates)

### Move UBC Month

Move UBC Month is a UBC\-wide initiative that takes place each February aiming to increase physical activity and reduce the time students, faculty, staff, and the UBC community spend being inactive\.

[Learn more](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/move-ubc)

### Move UBC Grants

Do you have an idea to get the UBC community moving? Grants of up to $1,500 are now available for UBC students, faculty, staff planning projects or initiatives that support the wellbeing of our community by moving more\!

[Learn more](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/moveubcgrants)

## Staff &amp; Faculty Sports Day

[Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2025/08/54498709732_3b618b39d3_c-300x225.jpg)

Faculty and staff are invited to our annual afternoon of inclusive and fun activities, hosted by UBC Human Resources and Recreation to support a healthy workplace\.

Teams of 4\-6 compete in a series of physical and intellectual challenges and team building activities with awards for teams who are best dressed, most honest, most spirited, and more\.

[Learn more](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/events/faculty-staff-sports-day/)
