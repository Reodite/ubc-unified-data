## FREE Hangar Fitness Centre services

We get that life can be expensive\. Check out our free services that can take your experience at the Hangar Fitness Centre to the next level at no extra cost:

### Complimentary Hangar Fitness Centre Orientations

New to fitness, new to campus, or just looking for some guidance? Sign up for a free fitness centre orientation today\.

-   Meet with an experienced and educated staff member who will show you what to expect of your fitness routine at The Hangar including the functionality of the equipment and basic gym etiquette\.
-   Orientations will be approximately 30 minutes in length\.
-   Sign up for an orientation and someone will be in touch with you within three business days\.

Orientations are intended to show users the equipment, how to generally use it, and to answer basic questions about the facility\. Orientations should not be used for those looking for guidance on specific exercises or fitness routines\. If this is what you’re looking for, refer to our personal training options\.

[Hangar orientation](https://anc.ca.apm.activecommunities.com/ubcokanagan/membership/search/detail/208?onlineSiteId=0)

### Free Virtual Workout Program

This workout focuses on major muscle groups and common movement patterns in day\-to\-day life, making it an exceptional functional training program\.

The program is very user\-friendly and provides modifications to adapt the program to an appropriate level of difficulty\. Follow along with demonstration videos that guide you through each exercise to ensure you are using correct form while completing the workout\.

Become acquainted with fundamental exercises before exploring more personalized exercise programs\. If you are looking for a specific and personalized training program, please refer to our personal training options\.

If you have questions, please contact [recreation\.okanagan@ubc\.ca](mailto:recreation.okanagan@ubc.ca)
