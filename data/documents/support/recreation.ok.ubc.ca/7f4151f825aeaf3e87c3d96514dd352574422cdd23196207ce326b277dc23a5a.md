## Register &amp; Play

Play a semi\-organized, recreational sport on a regular weekly basis\. Games are “self\-reffed” with a focus on fun and participation, though a moderate level of competition is to be expected\. Open to students, staff &amp; faculty\.

Program availability offers throughout the year, so visit our online registration activity portal to find out what programs are currently being offered along with dates, timing, and pricing\.

### Available sports

All Register or Play sports are offered year\-round
