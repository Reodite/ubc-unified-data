UBC Recreation is here to help you stay active and healthy, with fitness options from group fitness classes and fitness centre memberships to personal training and instructional programs\.

### [Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2025/08/Rec-Group-Fitness-class-icons-150x150.png)

### Fitness Classes

Join a wide range of classes in an inclusive environment and get ready to move your body\. A valid Hangar Membership is required to participate in Group Fitness Classes\.

[GROUP FITNESS](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fitness-programs/group/)

### [Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2025/09/Personal-Training-2-150x150.png)

### Personal Training

Do you want to shape up your old fitness routine? Our personal trainers would love to help\. Sign up to be matched with a trainer that fits your personal goals and abilities\.

[Personal Training](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fitness/personal-fitness/)

### [Memberships &amp; Rates](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fitness/membership-options/)

Get access to all that UBC Recreation has to offer under one membership\. Hangar Fitness Centre, group fitness classes, and gymnasium [drop\-in](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/sports/register-play/) included\.

### [Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2025/08/paddle-boarding-150x150.png)

### Instructional Programs

Try something new or revisit a favourite\. Choose from our on\-campus activities or explore one of our off\-campus partner locations — all abilities are welcome and no experience needed\!

[Instructional programs](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fitness-programs/instructional/)

### [Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2025/08/salsa-dancing-150x150.png)

### Fun, Free &amp; Friendly

We’re proud to offer a number of free and low barrier activities suitable for all abilities, backgrounds, and fitness levels\. 

[Free programs](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fun-free-friendly/)

### [Virtual Start\-up Workout Program](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fitness-programs/workout-program/)

Looking for a training program without the costs of personal training? Check out our fundamental workout program and customize the exercises to suit your fitness level\.
