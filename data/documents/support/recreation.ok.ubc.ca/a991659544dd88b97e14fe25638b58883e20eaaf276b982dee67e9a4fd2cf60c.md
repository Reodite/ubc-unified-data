The UBC Okanagan Athletics &amp; Recreation Code of Conduct outlines expectations for all participants, including students, staff, coaches, officials, volunteers, and visitors\. Our goal is to promote a positive, inclusive, safe, and respectful environment in all programs, facilities, events, and online spaces connected to UBCO Athletics &amp; Recreation\.

### 1\. Respect for Others

• Treat all participants, staff, officials, and spectators with dignity and respect\.  
• Refrain from harassment, discrimination, bullying, or threatening behaviour in any form\.  
• Celebrate diversity and support an inclusive environment for people of all backgrounds, identities, abilities, and perspectives\.  
• Abide by the Respectful Environment Statement and other UBC policies\.  
• Contribute to a welcoming, supportive community for all participants, staff, officials, and spectators\.

### 2\. Safe and Responsible Behaviour

• Follow all facility rules and etiquette, program guidelines, and instructions from staff, instructors, and coaches\.  
• Use equipment and spaces responsibly and safely\.  
• Refrain from behaviours that could endanger yourself or others\.  
• Participants must refrain from participating in Athletics &amp; Recreation programs or use facilities while under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, or other substances that may impair judgment or safety\.  
• Respect personal boundaries and obtain consent before physical contact, instruction, or assistance, except where safety requires immediate action\.  
• Report unsafe conditions, injuries, or concerns to staff immediately\.

### 3\. Integrity and Fair Play

• Demonstrate honesty, fairness, and good sportsmanship at all times\.  
• Compete and participate ethically, without cheating or seeking unfair advantage\.  
• Respect officials’ decisions and engage in constructive, calm communication\.

### 4\. Commitment to Well\-Being

• Support a positive environment that encourages physical, mental, and social well\-being\.  
• Follow health and safety protocols, including those related to injury prevention and facility access\.  
• Avoid disruptive or aggressive behaviour that negatively affects others’ ability to enjoy the space\.

### 5\. Facility &amp; Equipment Care

• Keep facilities clean, organized, and free of damage\.  
• Return equipment to designated areas after use\.  
• Use personal items and shared spaces in a way that respects others’ comfort and safety\.

### 6\. Digital Conduct

• Engage respectfully in online platforms connected to UBCO Athletics and Recreation\.  
• Avoid posting harmful, discriminatory, or disrespectful content\.  
• Protect the privacy and well\-being of others in all digital communications\.

### 7\. Accountability

• Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action, including suspension of facility access, removal from programs, or referral under university policies\.  
• Participants are expected to cooperate with staff and follow processes for investigation and resolution\.

### 8\. Commitment to Improvement

• UBCO Athletics and Recreation encourages feedback to improve the safety, inclusiveness, and quality of our programs and facilities\.  
• All members of our community share responsibility for fostering a respectful and thriving environment\.  
• UBCO Athletics &amp; Recreation is committed to accessibility\. Participants are encouraged to communicate accommodation needs to staff so we can support safe and meaningful participation\.
