Wellbeing starts with you, and you're starting in the right place—here at UBC Recreation\. Learn more about what we offer and how you can get involved\.

[Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2023/05/DSC01147-scaled.jpg)

### [Contact Information &amp; Hours of Operation](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/)

Recreation staff are available to answer any questions you may have about getting involved with UBCO Recreation\. Register for Intramurals, buy a membership, attend an event, or apply for a job\.

[Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2023/05/DSC01169-scaled.jpg)

### [Work Experience](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/about/work-experience/)

Looking to get some work experience and build technical skills while at school? Athletics &amp; Recreation regularly have job postings available to students throughout the year\.

[Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2023/05/DSC01227-scaled.jpg)

### [Recreation Newsletter](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/recreation-newsletter/)

The Recreation E\-newsletter is a monthly newsletter sent on the last Sunday of each month to keep you informed on all Recreation updates and announcements, member connect stories, events, programs and information\.
