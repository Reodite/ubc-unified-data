### Workout Buddies

[A group jumping in the air with excited expressions\.](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2021/12/zachary-nelson-98Elr-LIvD8-unsplash-768x512.jpg)

If you are new to the gym, don’t know how to start, or would like a friend to workout with, Workout Buddies is for you\! Workout Buddies will partner you with a Health &amp; Exercise Sciences student that can show you around the Hangar and complete your workout with you\! This is not a personal training session, but your Workout Buddy will be very knowledgeable in the gym and will be able to provide you with physical activity education, accountability, and show you proper form for exercises\! All abilities and fitness levels are welcome and encouraged to register\.

Sign up today and get 4 workout sessions in a 1\-month period from the date of your first session\.

***Available during the Academic Year Only \(September\-March\)***
