UBC Okanagan’s recreation facility is home to the Heat\. This facility opened in 1994 and has been the venue for many provincial, national, and international events\.

## Gymnasium features

### **Gym floor**

The North, South, and Central courts are multi\-sport surfaces with equipment available for basketball, volleyball, indoor soccer, and badminton\. The availability and schedule for these spaces changes every week\.

### **Running corridor**

The indoor walking/running track is an open space located on the second floor of the gymnasium, circling the gym floor below\. It is open year\-round\. Only clean indoor shoes are permitted on the track\. 1 lap \= 164\.6m, 6\.1 laps \= 1km, 9\.78 laps \= 1 mile\.

### **Upstairs loft**

This second floor area adjacent to the track is an open space for cardio workouts or studying space\.

## Location &amp; layout

### Location

The UBC Okanagan Gymnasium is located on Athletics Court, northwest of the Creative &amp; Critical Studies building\. Parking is available in [Lot M](http://maps.ok.ubc.ca/map/)\.

### **Features**

-   1632 sq\. metres of gym floor space \(room for four courts of volleyball or two courts of basketball or four courts of badminton\)
-   Indirect lighting and 40 feet \(12 metre\) clear height to the ceiling
-   864 theatre\-style bleacher seats
-   Wheelchair accessible

### **Dimensions**

-   48\.3m x 34m – 1632 sq\. meters
-   12 metre \(40 feet\) clear height to ceiling

## Gymnasium rentals

### Booking

-   Four hour minimum block generally required\.
-   A $25 fee may be charged for each change or cancellation\.
-   Discounts may be available for bulk bookings\.
-   Rate does not provide exclusive facility use\. Other bookings, users, or noise may occur during non\-exclusive times\. Book space accordingly\.
-   Use of facility equipment may require additional costs\.
-   Additional costs will be required for bookings requiring use of bleachers, additional cleaning, or security\.
-   All groups must be pre\-booked and provide payment/insurance prior to gym use\. Certificate of insurance terms can be viewed on the [UBCO Liability Terms \(PDF\)](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2019/06/UNIVERSITY-OF-BRITISH-COLUMBIA-OKANAGAN-LIABILITY-TERMS-AND-CONDITIONS-Mar-2017.pdf)\.
