## Department and student club events

Add some excitement to your event with our complimentary lawn game rentals for on\-campus department and club events\. The following games are available:

-   Bocce \(is heavy, can only be played on grass\)
-   Cornhole
-   Croquet
-   Disc Golf
-   Giant Connect 4
-   Giant Jenga \(is large and heavy\)
-   Ladder Golf
-   Lawn Darts
-   Skipping Ropes
-   Ring Toss
-   Spikeball
-   Frisbee

Please bring your own cart to collect the rented items\.

[LAWN GAME REQUEST FORM](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_4MYdYsDQUdaPRhs)

## Day use rentals

Sport balls and some lawn games are also available to the UBC community for complimentary day use to encourage informal activities and social opportunities anywhere on campus\. Equipment can be borrowed from the Customer Service desk in the lower lobby of the gymnasium in exchange for collateral \(student/staff/faculty ID\)\.

Collateral will be returned when equipment is returned\.  Sport ball and lawn game options may include:

-   Beach volleyball
-   Cornhole
-   Football
-   Frisbee
-   Ladder golf
-   Rugby ball
-   Spikeball
-   Soccer balls
-   Slacklines
