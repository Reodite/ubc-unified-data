## **employment &amp; Volunteer opportunities**

Applications for student staff opportunities are now closed\.

### Customer Service Representative

*Do you want to improve the lives of others through health and fitness?* 

-   Frontline representation for all Athletics &amp; Recreation programs and services\.
-   Assist customers with membership sales and program registrations\.
-   Provide a clean, presentable, and user friendly facility\.
-   Event and equipment set up and take down for all practices, games, and other events\.

For more information contact: [recreation\.okanagan@ubc\.ca](mailto:recreation.okanagan@ubc.ca)

*Note: To become a Hangar Fitness Centre Monitor, you must first be hired as a Customer Service Representative\.*

### Intramural Referee

*Looking for a job in the evenings, for only 4 to 12 hours a week?*

-   All applicants need to be confident independently reffing sport games\.
-   Set\-up and take down of game day equipment\.
-   Greeting participants for their games and confirming registration\.
-   Reffing intramural sport games
-   Provide general risk management, supervision, and score keeping for intramural leagues\.

For more information contact: [recreation\.okanagan@ubc\.ca](mailto:recreation.okanagan@ubc.ca)

### Certified Group Fitness Instructor

*Do you enjoy helping others become active in a fun, inclusive environment? Join our team of Group Fitness Instructors on campus\!*

-   Must have certification in area of expertise and liability insurance\.
-   Instruct safe, inclusive, and enjoyable classes\.
-   Promote and support other instructors and programs\.

For more information contact: [recreation\.okanagan@ubc\.ca](mailto:recreation.okanagan@ubc.ca)

### Certified Personal Trainer

*Would you like to help people get fit and healthy? Join our team of Personal Trainers on campus to make a difference in someones life\!*

-   Must be certified by a Canadian recognized organization\.
-   Must have personal liability insurance\.
-   Assist patrons to ensure safe and effective training\.
-   Design and implement individual programs for clients\.

For more information contact: [recreation\.okanagan@ubc\.ca](mailto:recreation.okanagan@ubc.ca)

### Recreation Communications &amp; Marketing

*If you’re passionate about social media, marketing, writing and school spirit, this is the job for you\!*

-   Create content for Recreation social media channels
-   Shoot photos and video of Recreation events and programs
-   Share Recreation events and programs with UBCO Events, UBCO Life &amp; UBCO Exchange
-   Create and share the Recreation e\-newsletter

For more information contact Crystal Westgate: [crystal\.westgate@ubc\.ca](mailto:crystal.westgate@ubc.ca)

### Varsity Events

*Looking to get involved in varsity athletics and learn the ins and outs of sporting events on campus?*

-   Assist with set up and tear down of events
-   Assist with sport table management and operations
-   Assist with team statistics and data management
-   Assist with varsity sport promotions
-   Represent the University and department on the event floor
-   Game Day operations \(statistics, scorekeeping, team services, entertainment\)

For more information contact Ellie Punnett: [ellie\.punnett@ubc\.ca](mailto:ellie.punnett@ubc.ca)

### Varsity Communications &amp; Marketing

*If you’re passionate about social media, marketing, writing and school spirit, this is the job for you\!*

-   Provide coverage of Heat varsity teams on Instagram, Twitter, TikTok and Facebook
-   Write game recaps and athlete features for goheat\.ca
-   Create weekly content for Heat social media channels
-   Shoot photos and video of Heat varsity games
-   Plan and execute promotional ideas and varsity game theme nights

For more information contact Jamie Howieson: [jamie\.howieson@ubc\.ca](mailto:jamie.howieson@ubc.ca)

### Varsity Webcast Production Crew

*Ever wanted to know what goes into the production of a sports broadcast? Join our webcast production crew\!* 

-   Assist with set up and tear down of CanadaWest\.tv broadcasts 
-   Shoot and produce live Heat home games in soccer, basketball and volleyball
-   Provide play\-by\-play and colour commentary of Heat home games 

For more information, contact Jamie Howieson: [jamie\.howieson@ubc\.ca](mailto:jamie.howieson@ubc.ca)  

### Student Trainer/Host Trainer/GameDay Host

*Are you an HES student looking to work with varsity teams during game days and practices? Apply to join the Student Trainer team\!*

-   provide first aid and acute injury management to varsity athletes
-   maintain a stocked trainer kit and manage inventory throughout the season
-   help facilitate warm\-ups and cool\-downs as needed for games and practices
-   keep track and take notes on injuries and rehab exercises using log book

\(NOTE: you don’t need to have these skills to apply, just a keen demeanor and positive attitude\)

*This is a volunteer or practicum role; practicum students must be in their fourth year\. Hourly expectations are as follows:*

-   *Team Trainer: 15\-20 hours \+ weekend travel*
-   *Host Trainer &amp; Game Day Host: 6\-8 hours/week*

For more information contact: [athletic\.therapy@ubc\.ca](mailto:athletic.therapy@ubc.ca)

### Strength &amp; Conditioning Intern

*Are you an HES student interested in assisting Varsity Athletes with their strength and conditioning? Apply to be a Strength &amp; Conditioning Intern\!*

-   facilitate team workouts in the HPL
-   assist with correcting faulty movements during exercise
-   assist with spotting during heavy lifts
-   ensure safety is maintained during team and individual workouts
-   regularly inspect equipment to ensure safe operation

\(NOTE: you don’t need to have these skills to apply, just a keen demeanor and positive attitude\)

*This is a volunteer or practicum role; practicum students must be in their fourth year\. Hourly expectations are 6 hours/week*

For more information contact: [athletic\.therapy@ubc\.ca](mailto:athletic.therapy@ubc.ca)
