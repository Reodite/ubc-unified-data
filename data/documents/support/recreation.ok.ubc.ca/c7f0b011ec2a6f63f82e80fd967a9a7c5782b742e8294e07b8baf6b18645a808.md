Quality fitness classes at student\-friendly prices\.

We’ve partnered with a variety of fitness locations throughout the Okanagan to offer special off\-campus fitness classes at discounted student rates, available exclusively for UBCO Students\.

## How it works

-   Visit our [online ActiveNet activity portal](https://anc.ca.apm.activecommunities.com/ubcokanagan/activity/search?onlineSiteId=0&amp;activity_select_param=2&amp;activity_keyword=OCF&amp;viewMode=list) to view all the upcoming discounted student classes offered with our partner facilities\.
-   Register and pay directly in ActiveNet\.
-   New class and session dates are continually being added, so check back often to see what’s new\.

[View upcoming off\-campus partner classes](https://anc.ca.apm.activecommunities.com/ubcokanagan/activity/search?onlineSiteId=0&amp;activity_select_param=2&amp;activity_keyword=OCF&amp;viewMode=list)

## Our partners

We’re proud to collaborate with the following community fitness partners to offer reduced\-cost activities for students:

-   F45
-   SpinCo Cycle
-   Oxygen Yoga
-   Strong Pilates
-   PACE Spin Studio
-   Hot Yoga
-   Our Yoga Space

We’re adding new partners all the time, so check back regularly for our up\-to\-date list\.

[Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2025/01/Screenshot_24-1-2025_1307_strongpilates.ca_.jpeg)

## Off\-campus instructional programs

Looking to learn a new activity or build your skills? We also offer a number of discounted multi\-session instructional programs with off\-campus partners, with activities ranging from golf to aerial silks to curling lessons and more\!

[More about our instructional programs](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fitness-programs/instructional#off-campus)

## Winter sports

We’re pleased to offer student rates each winter with our partners for activities like downhill and cross\-country skiing, snowboarding, snowshoeing, and more\.

[view our partner discounts for winter sports](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/events/skiing_snowboarding/)
