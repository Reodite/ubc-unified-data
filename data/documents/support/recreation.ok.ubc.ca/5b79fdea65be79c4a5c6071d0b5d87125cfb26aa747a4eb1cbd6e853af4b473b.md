Try something new or revisit a favourite\. Choose from our [on\-campus](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fitness-programs/instructional/#on-campus) activities or explore one of our [off\-campus](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fitness-programs/instructional/#off-campus) partner locations — all abilities are welcome and no experience needed\!

Program availability offers throughout the year, so visit our [online ActiveNet activity portal](https://anc.ca.apm.activecommunities.com/ubcokanagan/activity/search?onlineSiteId=0&amp;activity_select_param=2&amp;activity_category_ids=21&amp;viewMode=list) to find out what programs are currently being offered along with dates, timing, and pricing\.
