No registration necessary\. Hangar or Girls Who Lift Membership or drop in required\.

## Open Gym

Whenever the gym is not booked for a particular activity, the gym is considered “open” for drop\-in sports on a first\-come first\-served basis\. Participation in Open Gym requires an active facility membership or drop in pass\. Gym schedules are subject to change without notice, though we do our best to keep the online gym schedule as up to date as possible\.

[Group Fitness Classes](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/fitness-programs/group/)

## Optional Services

### **Locker Service – $60\.00**

Members are able to rent a locker in the change rooms at the gymnasium \(May 1, 2026\-April 30, 2027\)\. Purchase only available in person\. Discount is applied if service purchased in Term 2\.

### **Towel Service – $60\.00**

Members are able to use a towel at the gymnasium \(May 1, 2026\-April 30, 2027\)\.  Members are able to request a towel from the Customer Service Desk and all towels must be returned to the Customer Service Desk after each use\.

[Purchase](https://anc.ca.apm.activecommunities.com/ubcokanagan/membership/search?onlineSiteId=0&amp;keyword=towel)

## Terms &amp; conditions

-   All memberships are non\-transferable\. Refunds are granted within ten days from date of purchase and are subject to administrative fees\. After ten days from date of purchase, no refunds or credits will be granted unless due to medical reasons with a medical note provided\. [Contact us](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/contact/) for more information\.
-   Everyone who uses the facility is required to create an account and check in\. This can be done [online](https://anc.ca.apm.activecommunities.com/ubcokanagan/createaccount?onlineSiteId=0&amp;from_original_cui=true) or by visiting the Customer Service desk\.
-   Memberships can be purchased quickly and easily online using a credit card\. Once you purchase a membership online, bring your UBC card to the Customer Service Desk in the Gymnasium to have your membership validated\.
-   UBC Vancouver students are eligible for Student rates
-   Anyone under the age of 18 \(who is not a UBC Okanagan student\) requires adult supervision and will require a guardian/parent’s signature on their drop\-in waiver\.
-   All users will need to abide by Recreation’s [Facility Etiquette](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/facilities/hangar-fitness-wellness-centre/)
