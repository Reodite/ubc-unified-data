Do you feel tired, low in energy, disconnected, or have symptoms related to inflammation?  
  
Escape with us for a weekend getaway to learn and experience with Dr\. Barnett how nutrition, creativity, and connection can remedy these symptoms\.

**What's Included:**  
Informational discussions on nutrition and the creative arts\!  
Continuous glucose monitors offered to learn in real time what is happening to your blood sugar and body throughout the day, and evidence\-based strategies to lower blood glucose  
Physician\-curated menu designed to reflect a low glycemic index and anti\-inflammatory diet  
Guided yoga, meditation, drumming, group acupuncture, dance to live music, 5 rhythms dance, sound healing, and creative art and design  
Shared experiences and connections through ceremony and talk circles  
Beautiful nature surroundings to enhance the healing experience

**Cost: $1500/person**
