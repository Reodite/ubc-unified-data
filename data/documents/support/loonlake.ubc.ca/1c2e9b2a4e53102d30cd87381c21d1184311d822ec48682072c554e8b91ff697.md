### event details

Date: December 18\-20, 2026

Hosted By: Yoga Ninja and Mały Polak w Kanadzie \(Little Pole in Canada\)

[Image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/569ebff4c647ad650f36d23c/8d6f972c-cd36-499e-8330-40c96284e100/winter+yoga+art+retreat.png)

✨ A Cozy Winter Escape Before the Holidays  
  
Before the holiday rush takes over, escape to the forest for two cozy nights of yoga, creativity, sauna, music, chef\-catered meals and connection with other women\.  
  
No cooking\. No planning\. No errands\. Just time to move, create, laugh, connect and relax while someone else takes care of the details\.  
  
And there’s a holiday bonus\. 🎁  
  
During our creative workshops, you’ll learn to make beautiful, high\-quality handmade pieces to bring home as meaningful gifts for friends and family — so you can enjoy a weekend away while getting some of your holiday gift list done at the same time\.  
  
Come alone or bring friends\. No yoga, art or AcroYoga experience is necessary\.

### about the organizers

This retreat is organized by Yoga Ninja and Mały Polak w Kanadzie \(Little Pole in Canada\)\.  
  
Proceeds from this retreat support Mały Polak w Kanadzie, a registered non\-profit organization dedicated to supporting children, families, and Polish cultural development in Canada\.  
  
🌟 Meet Your Retreat Teachers  
  
🧘🎨 Kasia Mazur  
Yoga • Meditation • Art  
  
Kasia has been practicing yoga since 2008 and teaching since completing her first teacher training in 2011\. She has taught throughout the Lower Mainland and trained in Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin, Restorative, Pregnancy, AcroYoga and Aerial Yoga\.  
  
With a background in Graphic Design and a lifelong love of art, Kasia has explored everything from painting and stained glass to pottery, jewelry, soap making and macramé\.  
  
Inspired by creativity, movement and adventure, she loves bringing yoga and art together to create playful, welcoming experiences where people can relax, connect and try something new\.
