Play like no one’s watching\!

Specializing in creative and active play programming, Stellar Play creates single activity, half\-day &amp; full\-day experiences for kids, youth, and adults\. All activities are designed to get everyone moving, laughing, and having a great time\!

### **Activities offered:**

\- Forest Laser Tag  
\- Bubble Ball  
\- Battle Archery  
\- GeoHunt  
\- Race to Survive

### **Costs:**

Please contact [info@stellarplay\.ca](mailto:info@stellarplay.ca?) for custom quote and program examples\.

### **FOREST LASER TAG**

Experience Laser Tag like never before\. Using the industries \#1 tactical tagger, experience the heart pumping thrill of playing laser tag in the forest around Loon Lake in Maple Ridge\.

Learn more about [Forest Laser Tag](https://stellarplay.ca/activities/forest-laser/)

### **Bubble Ball**

Run, bump and roll, in this high energy and hilarious activity\! All participants playing wear giant inflatable bubbles that cover everything but the knee and below\. Bump into each other, roll around and play fun games like: Bubble Soccer, British Bull Dog, King/Queen of the Ring, and many more\.

Learn more about [Bubble Ball](https://www.stellarplay.ca/activities/bubble-ball/)

### **Battle Archery**

Archery and dodgeball wrapped into one exciting game\! Players are geared up with bows, face masks and arm guards, and run around firing giant, foam tipped arrows at the opposing team just like dodgeball\. Groups will learn how to fire a bow and arrow, then get ready to play\!

Learn more about [Battle Archery](https://www.stellarplay.ca/activities/battle-archery/)

### **Geohunt**

Explore Loon Lake’s beautiful surroundings and find hidden caches\. Tailor made for each group, participants use technology to look up coordinates and find the next location on their hunt\. Race to find all of the hidden caches or search at your own pace, it’s your choice\! 

Learn more about [GeoHunt](https://stellarplay.ca/activities/geo-hunt/)

**RACE TO THE SURVIVE**

Experience your personal survival challenge and see if your team has what it takes\. Choose from a variety of difficulty levels and event styles\.

Learn more about [Race to Survive\.](https://stellarplay.ca/race-to-survive/)
