Nestled in the Lower Mainland, Loon Lake is a truly special place\. This 56\-hectare private lake is reserved for non\-motorized activities, with fishing prohibited, helping to protect its natural beauty\. As a result, the lake remains a pristine haven for local birds and an exceptional living lab for scientific research\.

### **Activities offered:**

We encourage our guests to enjoy the lake in a responsible fashion and are happy to offer the following services:

**Beginner's Swimming Hole  
**Our square\-shaped dock features an interior swimming area that’s ideal for beginner swimmers\. Always close to the dock yet spacious enough to learn and play, it provides a safe and confidence\-building introduction to lake swimming\.

[Image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/569ebff4c647ad650f36d23c/747928a1-4cbe-44a3-a9b1-f0187191a1a1/LoonLake-Waterfront%2BFirepits-48.jpg)

**Canoeing  
**Loon Lake has 18 canoes available for group rental\. Spend some time paddling out across the lake and enjoying a classic Canadian pastime\. A canoe rental rate of $30 per canoe per day or $250 per day for all canoe applies\.

*\*Life jackets are available for use by all guests and are located at the Thunder Inn\. Life jackets are required for all persons canoeing\.*
