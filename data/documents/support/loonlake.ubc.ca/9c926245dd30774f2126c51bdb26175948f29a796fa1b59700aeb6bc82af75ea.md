Elevate your retreat at Loon Lake with a curated yoga class for you and your group\. Through a guided session with Cheyenne, participants will recharge, reduce stress, and foster a sense of clarity and connection while they’re in the research forest\. Creating time for a yoga practice during your stay is investing in your group’s well\-being — creating a more harmonious, energized, and innovative gathering\. Let this wellness addition to your retreat be the reset your people deserve\.

**Main benefits of yoga and meditation:**

-   Stress and anxiety management
    
-   Cognitive function and focus
    
-   Improved sleep
    
-   Boosted immunity
    
-   Pain relief
    
-   Enhances self\-awareness
    

**What the experience looks like:**

-   An all\-levels, safe &amp; inclusive class\. Beginners are welcome\!
    
-   Mats and props included
    
-   Set in the meeting space of your building OR outside on the dock \(season/weather dependent\)
    
-   Time of day and length of class is your choice based on your itinerary \(subject to scheduling availability\)
    

[Contact cheyenne](mailto:hello@cheyennemerlo.com?)

### **About Cheyenne**

Cheyenne has over 15 years of teaching experience\. Her specialty is creating a warm and welcoming environment for folks to come and relieve some stress through moving their body gently\. You can learn more about Cheyenne from her [website](https://www.cheyennemerlo.com/)\.
