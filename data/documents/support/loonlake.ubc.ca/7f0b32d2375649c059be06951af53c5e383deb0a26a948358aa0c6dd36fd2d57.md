## RECONNECT to YOUR HEART

[Screen Shot 2021\-06\-10 at 11\.48\.36 AM\.png](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/569ebff4c647ad650f36d23c/1623350947593-QT0J09JNNDSG8GOPSQR1/Screen+Shot+2021-06-10+at+11.48.36+AM.png)

Our annual summer retreat in Maple Ridge will be held at the beautiful Loon Lake Lodge and Retreat Centre nestled among tall fir trees that frame the majestic Garibaldi Mountain Range\. 

A small gathering of women to ensure we have a safety\-conscious, socially distanced, weekend of self\-care\.

Your all\-inclusive weekend includes 2 nights of private or shared accommodation, 6 healthy meals, and 7 engaging and inspiring workshops to fuel your Mind, Body, and Spirit\.

Unplug, relax, and connect with a collective of women, who are [inspirational](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/essential-someone-winners) and lift YOU up\!

### **Weekend Package Includes:**

-   2 nights private hotel\-style accommodation\-full linens and private bath
    
-   6 healthy and delicious meals
    
-   Daily workshops from our world\-class wellness facilitators on Saturday and Sunday
    
-   \(see the itinerary below for workshops\)
    
-   Morning yoga, flow and yoga Nidra
    
-   Our *signature* Blossom Box workshop for manifesting your goals
    
-   This is YOUR weekend\-take in all the workshops or non of them\-do what feels right in your heart
    
-   Time for rest, connection and relaxation
    
-   Beautiful forest and lakeside location
    

Rates from $595\+ Tax \(Shared\); $795\+Tax \(Private\)\.
