Nurture your body, mind and spirit amidst this sacred nature spot during a yoga retreat at Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre\. This 2\-night all inclusive rejuvenation package is sure to bring more balance to your being and to your life\.

Reset with yoga classes, meditation and healthy vegetarian meals, as well as energize with a swim in the lake or a hike through forest trails\. Open yourself and connect to community\.

Loon Lake is on private property in a protected forest area, located approximately one\-hour east of downtown Vancouver\. It is an accessible retreat to escape, relax and welcome the echoes of nature… It’s the perfect staycation\.

Previous yoga experience necessary\.

**Dates:** May 22 \- May 24, 2026**
