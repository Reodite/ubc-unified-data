Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre is proud to partner with UBC’s Wild &amp; Immersive\. Wild &amp; Immersive delivers an unparalleled outdoor educational experience that provides youth and adults the opportunity to build confidence and connectivity within a natural environment\.

Whether you are looking for a half\-day outdoor team\-building experience or a 3\-day immersive field\-camp, Wild &amp; Immersive will plan a program to meet your objectives and enhance your connection with the outdoors\.

**Wild Youth:** Programs developed for Pre\-School to Grade 12 students with all programs taking place outdoors\. Empower youth and enhance their connection to the environment\. Our youth programs incorporate interactive games, creative exploration, and enhanced learning through hands\-on activities\. Choose from modules such as: Life as a Tree, Habitat Trackers, Wetlands &amp; Amphibians, Survive the Wild, Wayfinder in the Woods and more\!

Rates: $400\* for up to 5 hours of programming \| $600\* for up to 8 hours of programming

\*Rate is based on groups of up to 25 students\. Additional charge for larger group sizes\.

**Wild Adults:** Programs designed for adult groups that offer educational and collaborative outdoor experiences\. Our adult programs are designed to strengthen your connection with the outdoors, your team, and with yourself\. Gain a deeper understanding of the local environment while participating in immersive and stimulating hands\-on activities\. Choose from modules such as: Wilderness Survival, Scavenger Hunt, Learn Your Team, Guided Nature Hike and more\!

Rates: $500 for up to 4 hours or programming\. Based on group sizes up to 25 adults\. Additional charge for larger group size\.

For more information, visit [Wild &amp; Immersive](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/)
