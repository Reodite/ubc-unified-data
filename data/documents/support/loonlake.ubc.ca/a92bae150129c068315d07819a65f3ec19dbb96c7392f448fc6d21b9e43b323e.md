### [**WALTER C\. KOERNER FORESTRY CENTRE**](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/koerner-lodge)

*10,193 sq\. ft\. Sleeps 16 to 40 guests with shared accommodation*

Built in 2006, the Walter C\. Koerner Forestry Centre offers full [retreat services](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/submit-your-event)\. This is the ideal place for meetings, retreats, and conferences\. Enjoy comfortable accommodations for up to 40 people, a conference room with modern AV equipment and WiFi, a private dining area, and fireside lounging all under one roof\.

Meals inclusive rates start from $218 CAD/person per night based on shared occupancy and group size\.

*Ideal for: Private corporate connections and lakeside facilitated group learning*

### [**Johnson Yew and Arbutus Cabins**](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/yew-arbutus-cabins)

*8,471 sq\. ft\. Sleeps 10 to 48 guests with shared accommodation*

Opened in 2009, these 2 adjoining modern log cabins are a welcome addition to Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre\. Both the Yew and Arbutus Cabins are *each* complete with comfortable lounge areas, cosy fireplaces, large multi\-purpose rooms with heated floors, and 8 peaceful sleeping rooms per building\.

Each bedroom has 1 bunk bed, 1 single bed, a writing desk, and WiFi, and both buildings are connected on the ground floor\.

Meals\-inclusive rates start from $139 CAD/person per night \(Adult\) and $109 CAD/person per day \(Youth\) based on shared occupancy\. Linen service extra\.

*Ideal for: Everyday group gatherings that balance comfort, space and price*

### [**THE BENTLEY FAMILY DINING HALL**](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/bentley-family-dining-hall)

For many people coming to Loon Lake, the Dining Hall is the heart of the experience\. Mealtime allows people to gather together and share their excitement and experiences\. The dining hall is a 2\-minute walk from the sleeping accommodations in camp and and comfortably accommodates up to 160 adults in the winter months\. In the summer months an additional 40 can be seated outside on the deck\. The dining hall can be configured for a variety of uses\. 

Our professionally\-equipped kitchen and trained staff offer healthy, nutritious meals throughout the day\. Choose from our menu options or let our superior kitchen staff plan great meals for you\. When more than one group is booked into Loon Lake, guests may be required to share the dining hall with other guests\. If your group requires a private lunch, please request this with our Reservations Coordinator\. All meals are served buffet style and include coffee, tea, hot chocolate a selection of juices and water as well as dessert with both dinner and lunch\.
