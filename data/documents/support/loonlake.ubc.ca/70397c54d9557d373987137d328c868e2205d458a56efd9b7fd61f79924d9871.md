### **WHY CHOOSE SAUNA:**

Unwind while reaping benefits like improved circulation, detoxification, stress reduction, and a boost to your immune system\. Pair your session with a cold plunge in the lake for an invigorating, transformative experience\.

A hot and cold cycle alternates between heating your body in a sauna and cooling it down with a cold plunge or cold exposure\. This practice promotes relaxation, circulation, and recovery\.

Enjoy the sauna with friends, family, or colleagues\. Rotate your group as needed, but we recommend spending at least 1\.5–2 hours to fully benefit from the experience\. The sauna can accommodate a maximum of 10 people at a time\.
