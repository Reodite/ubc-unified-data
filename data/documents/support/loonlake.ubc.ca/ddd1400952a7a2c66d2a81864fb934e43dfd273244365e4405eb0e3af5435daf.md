Loon Lake offers a variety of self\-guided trails designed to help visitors explore, connect, and engage with the natural landscape in different ways\. Each trail provides a unique opportunity to experience Loon Lake at your own pace\.

### **TRAIL OPTIONS:**

Different trail options that vary in length and ability\.

**Discovery Trail  
**Explore and experience Loon Lake’s dynamic temperate rain forest environment and community of living things by following the Ecology Path\. Alternatively, take the Mindfulness Path to become more in tune with yourself and the nature of Loon Lake\.

Distance: 1km Duration: 45 minutes Level: Easy

**Loon Lake Trail  
**The Loon Lake landscape is dripping with moisture\! Follow the Loon Lake Trail all the way around the lake and discover how all of this water affects what lives and grows here\.

Distance: 4\.5km Duration: 2\.5 hours Level: Intermediate

**Dogwood Trail  
**Take a scenic walk where gentle forest surroundings reward you after a short uphill climb from either end of the trail\. Once at the top, you’ll encounter a flat comfortable hike through the trees\. This trail also connects the Discovery Trail to the Wildlife Adventure Loop if you’re looking for a longer hike\.

Distance: 700m Duration: 30 minutes Level: Easy

**Wildlife Adventure Loop  
**This 2\.5 km self\-guided forest loop features a series of wooden playground and fitness structures thoughtfully placed along the trail\. Along the route, you’ll discover ten interactive stations, designed to foster social awareness, teamwork, and physical literacy\. This loop encourages participants to move beyond their comfort zones \- building group cohesion, mindfulness, and trust along the way\.

Distance: 2\.5km Duration: 2 hours Level: Moderate \- suitable for any group size\!

Check out our [Wildlife Adventure Loop Activity Guide](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/s/Wildlife-Adventure-Loop-Guide-1.pdf) to see each station and plan your visit\!
