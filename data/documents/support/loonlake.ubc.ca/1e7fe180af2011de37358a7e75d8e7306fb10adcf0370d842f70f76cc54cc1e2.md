### Experienced Cook &amp; Prep Cook \(Part\-Time\)

Loon Lake Camp Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre, located in the UBC Malcolm Knapp Research Forest, is currently looking for an experienced cook or prep cook to join our family\.

-   Job Title: Cook/Prep cook
    
-   Department: Food Services
    
-   Job Type: Part\-time
    
-   Hours /week: 24\-40; depending on business requirements and your availability
    
-   Starting Wage: $16\-$18 per hour depending on experience
    
-   Benefits: May be available in the future if position turns to full\-time
    
-   Start Date: May 30, 2018
    

Do you want to be a part of a friendly team in a beautiful and inspiring environment? 

Why you should you work with us?  
We are a unique year\-round facility that provides lodging and meals for group functions\. Guests use our location to host their own events or programs\. As a valued member of our team, you would gain the following benefits:

\- Access to our beautiful private location in the magical UBC Malcolm Knapp Research Forest  
\- Work with a caring, unique and inviting team of people  
\- Use of our private lake and canoes on days when the facility is not rented out  
\- Staff meals at no cost during your shift

Basic Duties of the Position  
\- Portioning, preparing, and cooking buffet\-style meals for groups from 15\-110 people  
\- Using modern commercial grade equipment \(ovens, grills, deep\-fryers etc\.\) to cook meals and bake desserts  
\- Ensuring all food items are ready to be served at set meal times  
\- Making coffee, and replenishing juice machine  
\- Cleaning \(mopping kitchen and dining hall floors, washing tables, counters and sinks, washing/putting away dishes  
\- Laundry \(aprons, towels, cleaning cloths, bedding\)   
\- Recording food/supplies that are running low  
\- Recording cooler temperatures\.

Necessary Requirements  
\- Valid Food Safe Certificate  
\- Good knowledge and prior experience in the culinary industry  
\- Must have own transportation; no access to transit at our location  
\- Valid First Aid certificate is an asset\.

Skills and Attributes  
In addition to the duties and requirements of the position, we are interested in someone who:  
\- Has the ability to work as part of a team, and unsupervised  
\- Has great leadership skills  
\- Has excellent people skills; is friendly, kind, and approachable  
\- Is willing to work some week/weekend days and provide relief for other staff holiday/sick days  
\- Has experience cooking for large groups of people\.

If you think this job sounds right for you, send us your information\!

Please forward your resume and cover letter to loon\.lake@ubc\.ca \(or use this [submission form](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/work-with-us/)\) above by May 20, 2018, and include "Part\-time Cook" in your subject line\.

We thank all who apply\! Only those considered will be contacted\.
