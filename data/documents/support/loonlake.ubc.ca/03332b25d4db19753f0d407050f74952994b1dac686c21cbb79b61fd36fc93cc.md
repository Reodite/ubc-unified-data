**Workshops, Experiential Learning &amp; Cultural Services** 

Len Pierre Consulting \(LPC\) has partnered with UBC and Loon Lake to bring education, workshops and Indigenous cultural services to groups at Loon Lake\. LPC can support education and experiential opportunities around Indigenous cultural safety, anti\-racism &amp; allyships, decolonizing education, substance use, men’s health, and much more\. Explore our current course offerings [here](https://www.lenpierreconsulting.com/courses)\. LPC can also make it easy for groups to access Indigenous cultural services and knowledge keepers, including Elder services, cedar\-twinning workshops, medicine walks and more\. 

  
**To inquire about booking a workshop or cultural service at Loon Lake, find our contact form** [**here**](https://www.lenpierreconsulting.com/copy-of-contact)**\.**

### **About Len pierre consulting**

Len Pierre Consulting \(LPC\) is an Indigenous owned company specializing in education around cultural safety, Indigenous knowledge, decolonization and reconciliation\. Founded by Len Pierre of Katzie First Nation, LPC creates safe, connected spaces to support people of all backgrounds in doing transformative work\. We approach our work with humility, integrity, vulnerability, authenticity and empathy to support people on their own journeys\. We practice what we preach, grounding ourselves in our practices and Indigenous ways of knowing and being\.
