Loon Lake Camp Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre, located in the UBC Malcolm Knapp Research Forest, is currently looking for a housekeeper to help us out\.  
  
**Job Title: Housekeeper**  
Department: Cleaning &amp; Maintenance Services   
Job Type: Part Time  
Hours /week: 20\-30; depending on business requirements and your availability  
Starting Wage: $16\-$18 per hour depending on experience  
Benefits: May be available in the future if position turns to full\-time  
Start Date: October 1, 2018  
  
Do you want to be a part of a friendly team in a beautiful and inspiring environment? Come and join us\!  
**  
Why should you work with us?**  
We are a unique year\-round facility that provides lodging and meals for group functions\. Guests use our location to host their own events or programs\. As a [valued member](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/experienced-cook) of our team, you would gain the following benefits:  
  
\- Access to our beautiful private location in the magical UBC Malcolm Knapp Research Forest  
\- Work with a caring, unique and inviting team of people  
\- Use of our private lake and canoes on days when the facility is not rented out  
\- Staff meals at no cost during your shift\.  
  
**Basic Duties of the Position**  
\- Reporting to the Reservations Coordinator to verify rooms/buildings that need cleaning  
\- Changing sheets and making beds  
\- Cleaning and scrubbing bathrooms, and various surfaces throughout the facility  
\- Vacuuming and mopping  
\- Dusting  
\- Gathering dirty laundry, and transporting to washing machines  
\- Folding and putting away clean laundry  
\- Stocking and maintaining the housekeeping closets/rooms   
\- Complying with all Health and Safety requirements including wearing adequate protective equipment when working with hazardous cleaning materials\.  
  
**Necessary Requirements**  
\- Physically capable; ability to be on your feet; bending, lifting, and moving heavy items for an extended period of time  
\- Experience an asset but not necessary  
\- Must have own transportation; no access to transit at our location  
\- Valid First Aid certificate is an asset\.  
  
**Skills and Attributes**  
In addition to the duties and requirements of the position, we are interested in someone who  
\- Has the ability to work as part of a team, and unsupervised   
\- Has excellent people skills; is friendly, kind, and approachable

When submitting your resume \(to: loon\.lake\(at\)ubc\.ca\) please reference Housekeeping Position in the subject line\.
