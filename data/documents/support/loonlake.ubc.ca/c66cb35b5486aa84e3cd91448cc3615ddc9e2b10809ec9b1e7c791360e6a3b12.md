Join us for an immersive healing experience at this lakeside yoga retreat\! This retreat focuses on emotional release, physical ease, inner clarity and reset the nervous system\. Gentle Yoga therapy, guided breathing techniques, relaxation by the lake \- you'll reconnect with your body and restore your energy\.

For every woman who deserves a break, this is your time\. Step away from the busy routine and treat yourself to a weekend of wellness, self\-care, and connection by the beautiful lake and in nature\. No experience needed—just come as you are\! 

Reconnect, recharge, and honor your needs\. Reserve your spot today\! 

\*No previous yoga experience required\.
