## Enable Class Discussions

Interactive discussion topics for students can be set up in your Canvas course\. You can create graded or ungraded discussion boards where students can post, read other responses, and reply\.

> ### Set up discussion boards in Canvas
> 
> #### Follow UBC how\-to instructions
> 
> 1.  [Set up a discussion board in your Canvas course](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/canvas-instructor-guide/#set-up-discussion)
> 
> #### Dive deeper with UBC resources
> 
> -   [Design for student\-to\-student interaction](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/52088/pages/4-dot-3-designing-for-interaction?module_item_id=1879761) with guidance from the UBC Online Teaching Program\.
> -   [Find other discussion tools](https://lthub.ubc.ca/tool-finder/discussions/) supported at UBC for facilitating synchronous and asynchronous conversations\.

> **Tip:**  
> Explore more about Canvas and read answers to FAQs in [UBC’s Canvas instructor guide »](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/canvas-instructor-guide/) and [UBC’s Canvas student guide »](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/canvas-student-guide/)

## Share Information

One of the fastest ways to reach all students in a course is using announcements in Canvas\. Alternatively, you can message students through the Canvas Inbox, which is like a personal email tool within Canvas\.

> ### Send messages through Canvas
> 
> #### Follow UBC how\-to instructions
> 
> 1.  [Communicate with your Canvas course](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/canvas-instructor-guide/#communicate)
> 
> #### Dive deeper with UBC resources
> 
> -   [Consider your teaching presence](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/52088/pages/6-dot-2-establishing-teaching-presence?module_item_id=1881174) in how you communicate with guidance from the UBC Online Teaching Program\.

> **Tip:**  
> You can also email students through the [UBC Faculty Service Centre](https://ssc.adm.ubc.ca/fsc/home) \(FSC\)\. Emailing through the FSC is good for academic communications requiring an official paper trail or if you don’t want to depend on Canvas\.

## Set Up Office Hours

Zoom is a ​video/audio web\-conferencing and collaboration tool that lets you meet with students in real time\. You can use it to host virtual office hours to meet with individuals or groups of students\.

> ### Schedule &amp; run a virtual office with Zoom
> 
> #### Follow UBC how\-to instructions
> 
> 1.  [Schedule office hours with Zoom](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/zoom-instructor-guide/#schedule-session)
> 
> #### Dive deeper with UBC resources
> 
> -   [Engage with students online](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/94578/pages/m2-engaging-with-your-students?module_item_id=4776267) using tips in the UBC “Introduction to Teaching Online” course\.

> **Tip:**  
> Explore more about Zoom and read answers to FAQs in [UBC’s Zoom instructor guide »](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/zoom-instructor-guide/) and [UBC’s Zoom student guide »](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/zoom-student-guide/)
