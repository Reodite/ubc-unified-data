## Deliver Real\-Time Lectures

Zoom is a ​video/audio web\-conferencing and collaboration tool​ that lets you meet with students in real time online\. Through Zoom you can do many classroom activities: present using whiteboards, share screens and resources, interact via chats or Q&amp;A, enable small group discussions, and administer polls\.

> ### Schedule &amp; run lectures with Zoom
> 
> #### Follow UBC how\-to instructions
> 
> 1.  [Request a UBC Zoom account and install Zoom](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/zoom-instructor-guide/#request-account-and-install)
> 2.  [Schedule a lecture with Zoom](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/zoom-instructor-guide/#schedule-session)
> 3.  [Run a lecture with Zoom](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/zoom-instructor-guide/#run-session)
> 
> #### Dive deeper with UBC resources

## Record &amp; Upload Lectures

If you want to record your lectures ahead of time, you can use Camtasia \(a desktop\-capturing software\)\. The quickest way to record is to present your slides on your computer as you talk over them\. Then you can upload the recording for students to stream in your Canvas course\.

> ### Capture &amp; share video with Camtasia
> 
> #### Follow UBC how\-to instructions
> 
> 1.  [Download and install the Camtasia application](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/camtasia-instructor-guide/#download-and-install)
> 2.  [Record a lecture with Camtasia](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/camtasia-instructor-guide/#record)
> 3.  [Share your Camtasia lecture recordings in Canvas](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/camtasia-instructor-guide/#share-recording)
> 
> #### Dive deeper with UBC resources
> 
> -   Watch this UBC video for tips on successfully recording lectures:
>     
>     [Embedded content: OnlineTips\_UBCStudios\_March18\_2020](https://api.ca.kaltura.com/p/161/sp/16100/embedIframeJs/uiconf_id/23450235/partner_id/161?iframeembed=true&amp;playerId=kaltura_player&amp;entry_id=0_mgkdr5zy&amp;flashvars%5BstreamerType%5D=auto&amp;flashvars%5BlocalizationCode%5D=en&amp;flashvars%5BleadWithHTML5%5D=true&amp;flashvars%5BsideBarContainer.plugin%5D=true&amp;flashvars%5BsideBarContainer.position%5D=left&amp;flashvars%5BsideBarContainer.clickToClose%5D=true&amp;flashvars%5Bchapters.plugin%5D=true&amp;flashvars%5Bchapters.layout%5D=vertical&amp;flashvars%5Bchapters.thumbnailRotator%5D=false&amp;flashvars%5BstreamSelector.plugin%5D=true&amp;flashvars%5BEmbedPlayer.SpinnerTarget%5D=videoHolder&amp;flashvars%5BdualScreen.plugin%5D=true&amp;flashvars%5Bhotspots.plugin%5D=1&amp;flashvars%5BKaltura.addCrossoriginToIframe%5D=true&amp;&amp;wid=0_4isaw88t)

> -   [Design effective lecture videos](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/52088/pages/5-dot-3-asynchronous-online-lectures?module_item_id=1880893) with guidance from the UBC Online Teaching Program\.

> **Tip:**  
> If you prefer to record on campus, many classrooms have built\-in recording equipment, and mobile recording kits are available for others\. Explore your [recording and streaming options in UBC classrooms »](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/recording-and-streaming-classrooms)
> 
> **Tip:**  
> Make lecture recordings more accessible by [adding closed captioning »](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/kaltura-instructor-guide/#add-captions) and providing transcripts\.
> 
> **Tip:**  
> Explore more about Camtasia in [UBC’s Camtasia instructor guide »](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/camtasia-instructor-guide/)
