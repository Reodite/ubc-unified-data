## Manage Grades

You have three main options for grading with Canvas: grade in a spreadsheet \(Gradebook\), grade while viewing student work \(SpeedGrader\), or grade offline \(export to a spreadsheet\)\. Regardless of whether you use Canvas or not, grades will ultimately need to be manually uploaded to the UBC Final Grade Submission Tool \(FGST\) in Workday\.

> ### Add grades to Canvas and the FGST
> 
> #### Follow UBC how\-to instructions
> 
> 1.  [Grade in your Canvas course](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/canvas-instructor-guide/#grade)
> 2.  [Upload grades to the FGST](https://confluence.it.ubc.ca/display/IRPTCM/Training+Hub+for+Instructors)
> 
> #### Dive deeper with UBC resources
> 
> -   [Conclude your online course successfully](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/94578/pages/introduction-to-module-3?module_item_id=4776278) with help from the UBC “Introduction to Teaching Online” course\.
