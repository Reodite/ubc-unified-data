## Primary Contacts

### UBC Vancouver

If you teach primarily at UBC Vancouver, please contact your faculty’s [Instructional Support Unit](http://lthub.ubc.ca/support/instructional-units/) first\.

You are also invited to call \(604 827 4775\) or email \([LT\.hub@ubc\.ca](mailto:LT.hub@ubc.ca)\) the **Learning Technology Hub** to reach central instructional support staff\. You can also receive drop\-in assistance Monday\-Friday between 9:00‑4:30 \(excluding statutory holidays\) by accessing the [LT Hub online](https://lthub.ubc.ca/dropin)\.

### UBC Okanagan

If you teach primarily at UBC Okanagan, please call \(250 807 9293\) or email \([ctl\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:ctl.helpdesk@ubc.ca)\) the **Centre for Teaching and Learning** for assistance\.

## Self\-Paced Courses

### Introduction to Teaching Online

View the [UBC “Introduction to Teaching Online” course](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/94578), a self\-paced course designed for UBC instructors who are new to teaching online courses\. Over 8\-10 hours, the course walks participants through recommended, evidence\-based practices to use when preparing for and teaching online\. Videos, readings, activities, and just\-in\-time checklists help participants apply these practices to their specific contexts\.

### Online Teaching Program

Explore the Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology’s longer, self\-paced [Online Teaching Program](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/all-our-programs/online-teaching-program/)\. Designed in response to the university\-wide pivot online in March 2020, the program can help you adapt your course to the online environment and prepare you to teach online\. You can also reference the [standalone Online Teaching Program resources](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/all-our-programs/online-teaching-program/resources/) for guides covering inclusivity, academic integrity, assessments, open educational resources, and other online teaching topics\.

## Training &amp; Professional Development

The Learning Technology Hub offers [training and professional development opportunities](https://lthub.ubc.ca/support/training-and-professional-development/) for teaching with technology\. These opportunities allow you to learn more about pedagogical practices, get familiar with available technologies, and explore effective ways of integrating technology into your teaching\.
