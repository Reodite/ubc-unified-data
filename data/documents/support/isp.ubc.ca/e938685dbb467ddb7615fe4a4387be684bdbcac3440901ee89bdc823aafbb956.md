## **Indigenous Strategic Plan Executive Advisory Committee \(ISPEAC\)**

Part of the Indigenous Strategic Plan \(ISP\) [Guiding Network](https://isp.ubc.ca/foundations/guiding-network/), an innovative, networked, Indigenous human\-rights based model for ISP implementation support, the Indigenous Strategic Plan Executive Advisory Committee \(ISPEAC\):

-   Serves on a cross\-campus basis: both UBC Okanagan and UBC Vancouver
-   Advises Indigenous Strategic Plan Coordinating Committee \(ISPCC\) on ISP priorities
-   Reviews implementation and progress reports
-   Provides recommendations on funding priorities and projects
