### UBC Resources

-   [Abacus Data Repository](http://data.library.ubc.ca/) – UBC’s repository for all types of data\.
-   [UBC Geospatial Technology and Resources](https://gis.ubc.ca/) – UBC’s GIS initiatives\.
-   [UBC Library Map Collection](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/maps-atlases) – An assortment of maps and atlases for public access located on the second floor of Koerner Library\.

### External Resources

-   [BC Data Catalogue](https://catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca/) – Access data from the BC provincial government such as [Terrain Resource Inventory Mapping \(TRIM\)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/data/geographic-data-services/topographic-data/terrain) program data and BC Forest Cover Data\.
-   [GEO\.ca](https://geo.ca/) – Natural Resources Canada’s site for open geospatial data\.
