The following digitized film records can be used to look up details such as date, locality and camera specification for air photos published by the B\.C\. Provincial Government \(roll number starts with **BC**\)\. These records were previously obtained from the B\.C\. Provincial Government webpage and the GIC is not responsible for nor cannot guarantee their accuracy\.

For photos newer than 1962 please use [GeoBC Imagery Finder](http://a100.gov.bc.ca/pub/wimsi/AirphotoSearch) instead\.

In order to find the correct film catalog first look up the year that the photo was taken on [Film Roll Year Correlation](https://gic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/03/Film_Roll_Year_Correlation.pdf)\. For federal photos \(A\-XXXXX\) use the [Federal Film Roll Year Correlation\.](https://gic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/04/Film_Roll_Federal.pdf)
