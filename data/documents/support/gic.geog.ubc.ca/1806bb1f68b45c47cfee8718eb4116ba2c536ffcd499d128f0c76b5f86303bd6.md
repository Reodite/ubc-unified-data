We require a Google Earth ***\.kmz*** or ***\.kml*** map file for most air photo orders in order to perform the search accurately\. A kmz file can be easily created on Google Earth in minutes and we have created a brief tutorial to walk you through the necessary steps\.

To view or download this tutorial as a PDF file [click here](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2018/08/Creating-a-KMZ-file.pdf)\.

### 1\. Download and install Google Earth

Google Earth can be downloaded for free at:  
[https://www\.google\.com/earth/desktop/](https://www.google.com/earth/desktop/)  
  
 

### 2\. Open Google Earth\. Draw &amp; indicate your area of interest

Zoom onto your site in Google Earth \(or enter a city/address onto the search bar on the left, *e\.g\. 1984 West Mall, Vancouver*\)\. You may use either the **Add Placemark**, **Add Polygon**, or **Add Path** tool to indicate your site\.  
  
[Image](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/ge01.jpg)  
  
 

You can move and adjust the pinpoint to place it directly on your site\. Once you are satisfied with the placement of the pinpoint/polygon/path, click **OK** on the dialog box to close it\.  
  
[Image](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/ge02.jpg)  
  
 

#### ***Add Placemark*** tool example

For sites with a specific address:

### 2\. Saving the file

Once the pop\-up dialog box has been closed, there should now be a new layer of the feature under the *Places* sidebar\.  
  
[Image](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/ge07.jpg)

**Right\-click** on the layer and choose **“Save Place As”**\.  
  
[Image](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/ge08.jpg)

Name and save the file to your desired destination\. Make sure to save as type **Kmz**\.  
  
[Image](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/ge09.jpg)  
  
 

### Bonus: Multiple sites in a single kmz file

If your project requires air photos of many locations, it might be easier to save them all as one single *\.kmz* file\.

Draw all of your sites as shown above:  
  
[Image](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/ge10.jpg)  
  
 

Under the *Places* sidebar, **right\-click** on the *“Temporary Places”* folder\. Choose **Add &gt; Folder**:  
  
[Image](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/ge11.jpg)  
  
   
Enter a name for the new folder, and click **OK** on the dialog box  
  
[Image](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/ge12.jpg)  
  
 

Your new folder should appear under the *Places* sidebar\.  
  
[Image](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/ge13.jpg)  
  
 

Select all your feature layers \(hold down **CTRL** on keyboard to select multiple layers at once\) and move/drag them into the new folder\. All the feature layers should appear inside the folder in the files tree:  
  
[Image](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/ge14.jpg)  
  
 

**Right\-click** on the **folder name** and choose **Save Place As**\.  
  
[Image](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/ge15.jpg)

This way all the sites should now be saved within one single *\.kmz* file\.
