The Geographic Information Centre \(GIC\) offers support services for Geography undergraduate students, graduate students, faculty and the general public\. Holdings include maps specializing in BC and Canada, atlases, books, video recordings, course reserves on geographical topics, and the University Air Photo Collection\. The holdings form a teaching, reference and research centre located in the Department of Geography\. The GIC welcomes the University Community and the General Public to use the centre\. Individuals representing a commercial interest wanting to use the historical air photo collection as a commercial reference source should follow instructions listed on the air photo request page\.
