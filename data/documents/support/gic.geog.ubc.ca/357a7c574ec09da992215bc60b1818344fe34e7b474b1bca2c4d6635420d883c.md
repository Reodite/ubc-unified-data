#### [Largest BC aerial photo collection now housed at GIC\!](https://gic.geog.ubc.ca/2014/09/18/largest-bc-aerial-photo-collection-now-housed-at-gic/)

Since the 1930s, the Provincial Air Photo Warehouse have housed approximately 2\.3 million aerial photographs, recording BC’s geographic history for many decades\.  In April 2010, the Provincial Air Photo Warehouse closed down its warehouse access\.
