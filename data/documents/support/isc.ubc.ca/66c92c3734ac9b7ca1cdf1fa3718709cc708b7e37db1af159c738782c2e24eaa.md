Our online [Workday Knowledge Base](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_view4&amp;kb_knowledge_base=af5ffe361b8ad4105edd43b4bd4bcb09) \(CWL required\) hosts over 400 resources on how to use Workday, both as an employee and to perform tasks as part of your job\. These include step\-by\-step instructions on specific processes, FAQs, and more on a range of topics\. Depending on their format and focus, knowledge resources may include images of steps in Workday, tips, and links to other related resources\.

**Knowledge Resources for Workday Student**

For knowledge resources on Workday Student, visit the new [Workday Student category](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_category&amp;kb_category=6973520733e5521049edb832cd5c7b25) in the Workday Knowledge Base or visit the [Training Hub](https://confluence.it.ubc.ca/pages/viewpage.action?spaceKey=IRPTCM&amp;title=Training+Hub) on Confluence \(CWL required\)\.

### How to Use the Knowledge Base 

Our Workday Knowledge Base is accessible to all faculty, staff, and student employees with an active CWL\. 

To find information on a particular topic, you can use search or browse by category \(e\.g\. HR Tasks\) or ask the [ISC chatbot](https://isc.ubc.ca/resources-support/isc-chatbot "ISC Chatbot")\. For more detailed instructions, see [UBC Workday Knowledge Base 101](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article&amp;sysparm_article=KB0017793)\.

**Note:** Our knowledge resources are updated regularly, based on updates to Workday and community feedback\. Always check the current version to ensure you’re following the latest guidance\.

### When to Use 

The knowledge base is useful when you need quick help to complete a specific task or run into an error in Workday, while our [training courses](https://isc.ubc.ca/resources/workday-training) \(CWL required\) provide a more complete overview by role \(e\.g\. Manager\) or topic \(e\.g\. expenses\)\. The knowledge base resources cover Workday processes, but may not address all scenarios or include details specific to a UBC unit or faculty\.

Find a list of popular resources on the [Access the Workday Knowledge Base](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=e82eea1d1b73b010352a8730604bcb03) page in the UBC Self Service Portal\.

### New to UBC?

New employees can see [Onboarding in Workday](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article&amp;sysparm_article=KB0017901) in the [Workday Knowledge Base](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_view4&amp;kb_knowledge_base=af5ffe361b8ad4105edd43b4bd4bcb09) for guidance on completing their onboarding tasks in Workday\. Additional recommended Workday Knowledge Base resources based on your role will also appear on your [Onboarding dashboard](https://wd10.myworkday.com/ubc/d/task/14860$26.htmld) in Workday\. 

### New to your role?

If you recently changed jobs at UBC, our [training courses](https://isc.ubc.ca/resources/workday-training) cover specific roles and topics in more detail\.
