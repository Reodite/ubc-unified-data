Whether you have a suggestion for a [Workday enhancement](https://isc.ubc.ca/continuous-improvement/workday-enhancements), an idea to improve a [Workday resource](https://isc.ubc.ca/resources "Resources"), or want to partner with us on an improvement initiative, we want to hear from you\.

We regularly work with Central HR, UBC Finance, and Payroll Operations on updates to the areas of Workday that they oversee, but we also collaborate with other departments, faculties, and units with unique needs, such as Research Finance or the Department of Athletics\.

The role of the ISC is to generate ideas and explore solutions from a technical perspective on Workday as a system, while the operational owners of Workday \(HR, Finance, Payroll\) decide on what changes or updates will be made based on UBC policy, procedure, and best practices\.

The ISC also works with other higher education institutions to exchange ideas on system improvements, innovative solutions, community support and training, and other aspects of Workday\.

If you are interested in collaborating with the ISC, [contact us](https://isc.ubc.ca/contact "Contact Us")\.
