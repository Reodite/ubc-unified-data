The Integrated Service Centre \(ISC\) supports and improves UBC’s use of [Workday](https://isc.ubc.ca/about/workday "About Workday")\. We provide UBC faculty, staff and student employees with day\-to\-day [support](https://isc.ubc.ca/resources/workday-support "Workday Support") and access to [resources](https://isc.ubc.ca/resources "Resources") for Workday HR \(HCM\), Finance, Payroll, Student and more\. 

The ISC also manages Workday at UBC as a system, including process improvement, regulatory requirements, and Workday\-delivered updates \(e\.g\. user experience, [Workday releases](https://isc.ubc.ca/continuous-improvement/workday-releases)\)\. 

We partner with process owners \(e\.g\. Central HR, UBC Finance, UBC Payroll Operations, Enrolment Services\) and other key stakeholders on changes to Workday to better meet their and UBC’s needs\. We work closely with IT, Enterprise Data Governance, and other groups on UBC\-wide initiatives, such as data and reporting\.

If you are interested in collaborating with us on a Workday initiative, please [contact us](https://isc.ubc.ca/contact "Contact Us")\. The ISC also directly works with Workday \(company\) and collaborates with other post\-secondary institutions on Workday improvements and best practices\. 

Learn more about [our teams](https://isc.ubc.ca/about/teams "Our Teams") and what they do\.
