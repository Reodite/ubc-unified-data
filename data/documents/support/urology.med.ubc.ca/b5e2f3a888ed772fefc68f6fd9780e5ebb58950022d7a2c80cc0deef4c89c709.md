## The Department of Urologic Sciences encompasses a diverse range of clinical urologic specialties, and provides world class care to patients who are treated by our faculty\.

  

The pages below provide a range of information for urologic disorders treated by our specialists\. The content of these pages changes frequently and does not replace the advice of a formal urologic consultation\. The documents presented here are for information purposes only\.

To make an appointment, please contact your family doctor and ask for a referral to our clinic **\(Urology Clinic 604\-875\-5003 clinic@ubcurology\.com\)**\. We are accepting new patients daily\.
