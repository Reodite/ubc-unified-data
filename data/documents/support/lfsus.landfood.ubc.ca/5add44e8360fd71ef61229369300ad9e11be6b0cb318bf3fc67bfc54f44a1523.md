## REGGIE REWARDS

LFS Student Benefits Program 

​

What is Reggie Rewards?

​

Reggie Rewards is LFS\|US’s student benefits program exclusive to students within the Faculty of Land and Food Systems\. LFS\|US’s Reggie Rewards aims to provide benefits to students, as well as promote various sustainable, local businesses in the Lower Mainland\. All LFS students have access to a FREE Reggie Rewards sticker to access exclusive discounts to our partnered brands\. 

[2806debcbe881d9a4d6f27cdf3a46916\.jpg](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/4dc098_06116db3e0b34bf3a09524c4082fc836~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_147,h_110,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,blur_2,enc_avif,quality_auto/4dc098_06116db3e0b34bf3a09524c4082fc836~mv2.jpg)

How to get your sticker?

​

Visit LFS\|US executive’s year\-round office hours at MCML 64 to request for a sticker\. You will need to provide your name, email address, year level, program, and student number, by showing your SSC page, to any LFS\|US executive\. This program is only open to BSAB, BSFE, BSFN, and BSGR students\. Once we have your information, you will receive the sticker along with information on how to use it\.  

[Image](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/4dc098_5c2e4f9bfcd147398a26507ad1354bf3~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_810,h_128,al_c,q_85,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/4dc098_5c2e4f9bfcd147398a26507ad1354bf3~mv2.png)

Please fill out this [form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd50DSiE3fLY5Yon9vpELzlWLBIqutntXlUd3XFVKTx_VanRg/viewform?usp=sf_link) prior to coming to the office hours\. The reason why is that sometimes office hours can be unexpectedly cancelled\. By filling out the form, we can make sure that the pick\-up can go smoothly\. View the LFS\|US office hours schedule [here](https://www.lfsus.landfood.ubc.ca/reach-out)\. 

​

How to use the Reggie Rewards sticker?

​

Place the sticker on the top right corner of your UBCcard, as shown below\. You can now use your UBCcard, with the Reggie Rewards sticker at any of our partnered brands\. 

[Image](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/4dc098_7fe4c8bd179d4f21be2ab0be2cb22e5a~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_600,h_213,al_c,q_85,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/4dc098_7fe4c8bd179d4f21be2ab0be2cb22e5a~mv2.png)

To use your card, simply present your UBCcard – make sure the sticker is intact – to any of our partnered brands\. This should be shown to one of the employees at a partnered location\. Make sure to check the list of partners on our website or social media to verify a partnership’s location and the discount\.  

Do I have to pay to get a Reggie Rewards sticker?  

No\! All currently enrolled LFS students can receive ONE Reggie Rewards sticker\. Make sure you provide your information \(name, year level, and program\) accurately by showing your SSC page\. 

How do I use the online code for a discount?  

For online purchases, make sure to enter the specific code as shown on our partners list for each business at checkout\! 

Additional Important Information  

If you lost your Reggie Rewards sticker, or your UBCcard with the Reggie Rewards sticker on it, please contact [lfsus\.vpexternal@gmail\.com](mailto:lfsus.vpexternal@gmail.com) for a replacement sticker\. Due to the limited availability of the stickers at this time, please keep in mind that you might not be able to receive a replacement sticker\.
