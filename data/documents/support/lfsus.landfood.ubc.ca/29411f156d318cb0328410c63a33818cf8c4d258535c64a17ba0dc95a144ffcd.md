## THE COUNCIL

The Land and Food Systems Undergraduate Society \(LFS\|US\) is committed to building a sense of community within the Faculty of Land and Food Systems \(LFS\)\.

The council is elected by the Land and Food Systems students to organize activities that promote student well\-being, and to make sure LFS is represented in University and Faculty affairs\.
