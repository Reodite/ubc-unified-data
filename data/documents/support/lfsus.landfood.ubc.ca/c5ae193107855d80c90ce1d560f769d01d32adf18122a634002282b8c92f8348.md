## GRADUATION PHOTOS

Graduation photos \(class composite and graduation portraits\) for UBC Land and Food Systems students are taken at Evangelos Photography Ltd\. Evangelos provides all necessary UBC caps, hoods, and gowns\.

​

It is your responsibility to plan in advance to make sure your picture is taken before the deadline\. Please book your appointment AS SOON AS POSSIBLE\. You can book your appointment through Evangelos' website only: [www\.evangelosphotography\.com](http://www.evangelosphotography.com/)\. 

Deadline : TBD

Evangelos Photography Ltd\. address: 3156 W Broadway, Vancouver BC  
 

If you would like to go ahead with the full graduation session there is the $45\.00 refundable cash deposit to be paid at the time of your appointment\.
