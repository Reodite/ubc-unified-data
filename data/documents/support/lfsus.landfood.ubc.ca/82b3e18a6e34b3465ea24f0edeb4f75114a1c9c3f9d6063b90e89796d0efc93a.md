## THE POD

THE POD IS CURRENTLY OPEN\!

​

The Pod was opened back in January 2018 and we're excited that we get to share this space with students\! The Pod is a quiet space perfect for studying and taking naps\. We also have a pool table and board games to borrow\! Use this space to rest, eat, charge your devices, or hang out with friends\!

​

             11:00 am \- 5:00 pm

The Pod is located in MCML 66, and regularly opened Tuesday to Friday from 11:00 AM \- 5:00 PM during the academic year\. It is available to be booked out for times outside of regular hours to LFS affiliated organizations\.  
   
However, we do ask you fill in a request \*at least 2 weeks\* prior of your event\. Note: If anything is damaged or we see the lounge is not cleaned\-up after the event, we will charge a fee to cover the cost\. We want to respect the space that we are given so that everyone can use it\.  
   
If you have any questions or would like to book The Pod, please email the lounge and apparel coordinator at lfsus\.lounge@landfood\.ubc\.ca for further inquiries\. Thank you\!
