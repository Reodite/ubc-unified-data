1\) Ensure your parcel is properly sealed and addressed, with the recipient address information clearly printed on the outside of your parcel\.

2\) [Fill out our parcel/courier form](https://campusmail.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/04/UBCV-Parcel-Form.pdf), and attach it to the outside of your parcel\. All options available are on this form\. Please ensure the form is complete to reduce any delays in processing\.

3\) Leave your parcel in your department’s mail room \(or other designated area where Campus Mail picks up from\)\. We will pick it up between 8AM and noon, and it will be sent out the same day to your specifications on the form\.
