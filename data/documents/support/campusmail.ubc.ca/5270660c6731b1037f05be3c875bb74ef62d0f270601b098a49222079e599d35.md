If you are planning on sending out a bulk shipment \(&gt;100 pieces\) of mail across campus, then you must ensure that it is sorted appropriately\. Bulk orders must be sorted first into UBC mail zone \(1\-4\) for Vancouver departmental mail, and then by department name \(both campuses\)\. If this sorting is not completed, there will be significant delays in sorting and distributing your mail\. If you are having printing done by an off\-campus printer, please ensure they are boxed by mail zone and then by department within that zone\.

If you are sending out a bulk shipment externally, please contact us at 250\-807\-9772 to discuss your needs\. The more advanced notice we have, the better we will be able to ensure we can meet your deadlines and keep your postage costs low\. There may be substantial savings available if your mail meets certain requirements, and we are happy to help you realize them\.

We highly recommend that you consult Canada Post’s ‘Machineable Mail Advisor‘ if you would like us to send out a high volume of mail, postcards, advertisements, etc\. on your behalf\. This website provides specific guidelines of what is requred to have mail billed as ‘standard’ \(cheaper\) versus ‘non\-standard’ \(more expensive\)\. By making sure your mail is compliant with Canada Post’s guidelines, you could save up to 50% on your postage costs\.

CRMS is not responsible for either increased postage costs, or items being returned to sender, if your mail is deemed not compliant by Canada Post\. To save yourself unnecessary delays, increased printing costs, and original postage charges, please consult Canada Post’s website before having your items printed\. Please call us at 250\-807\-9772 if you have any questions\.
