CRMS will not pick up or deliver mail and/or parcels that exceed 30 kilograms \(66 pounds\) during our mail runs for safety reasons\. If you require anything to be picked up that exceeds this, or you have something being delivered to CRMS from Canada Post that may exceed this, please contact us at 250\-807\-9105 to discuss your options and give us advanced notice\. We may be able to assist you\. If items are too heavy for us to handle, the services of UBC Okanagan Facilities’ crew may have to be used, and these will be billed on a cost\-recovery basis to your department\.
