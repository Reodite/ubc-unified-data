We are excited to announce that, effective October 21, 2024, UBC Campus Mail will be relocating from the USB building to the UBC Bookstore\. This move reflects our commitment to providing essential services where they are most needed on Campus\. It also enables prudent operational efficiencies and synergies between Mail and the Bookstore, while offering much\-needed space for our colleagues in Facilities\.

Although we anticipate a brief adjustment period as we integrate Mail and Bookstore operations into the same building, we are confident that the Bookstore’s central location will make mail services more accessible for the UBC community, offering a variety of shipping options at the most competitive rates on campus for both domestic and international mail and parcels\.
