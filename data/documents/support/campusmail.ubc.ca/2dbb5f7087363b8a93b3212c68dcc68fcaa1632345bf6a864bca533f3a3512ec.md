We can also pick up inter\-departmental mail, as well as mail that needs to be delivered to external recipients, during our daily mail runs\. Policies and processes for outgoing mail can be found on our Outgoing Mail Processing section\. If your mail is not ready to be picked up when we drop off your daily mail, we will not make a second run to retrieve any items\. You may drop off mail at CRMS in person if you have missed our pickup for the day\.

Each department has one mail pick\-up point per department\. CRMS will only pick up from this designated location, not from individual offices\. If you need more information on your department’s pick\-up locations, please contact your departmental administrator or contact CRMS\.
