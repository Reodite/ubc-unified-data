If you are a new department on campus, or if your department is changing locations, please contact us at 250\-807\-9105 to discuss your changes\. It is the responsibility of each department to ensure they inform CRMS of any changes occurring\.

Please note that CRMS can only deliver to one location per department, per building\. As we sort departmental mail by department name within each building, and not by individual offices, it is impossible for us to arrange for several drop off points per department\. Departments are responsible for sorting and distributing mail to individuals within their units from their designated centralized mail room\.

Anybody requesting service other than UBC departments must be approved by the President’s Office
