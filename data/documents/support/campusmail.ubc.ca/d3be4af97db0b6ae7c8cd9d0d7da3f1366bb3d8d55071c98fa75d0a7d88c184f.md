In order to facilitate billing to departments on a monthly basis, departments who would like to use the outgoing mail/parcel processing services of CMS must fill out and submit an Application for Metering Services\. Please note that it is the responsibility of departments to ensure that the information we have on file for their department is correct and up\-to\-date\. Please ensure you contact us at 604\-822\-6046 or at campus\.mail@ubc\.ca immediately if there are any changes to your account\.
