##### Addressing overview

Both incoming and outgoing mail and parcels must be addressed properly to ensure items get to the intended recipients in a timely manner\. If mail or parcels coming in to CRMS are incompletely or incorrectly addressed, we must return them to sender directly for re\-addressing\. This will mean that mail or parcels will be substantially delayed in reaching you\. If mail we are picking up to be sent out is addressed improperly, it may be refused by Canada Post or other carriers and may be sent back to you for re\-addressing\. So, following the below guidelines is crucial in making sure your mail or parcels reach their destination\.

No matter where your mail or parcels are going, please ensure only one address is present on the envelope or parcel\. If more than one address is present, we may have to return it to you to verify the correct address\. This is especially critical for inter\-departmental envelopes that are re\-used multiple times\.

Please note that due to the volume of mail and parcels we receive, we are not able to research where items are to go if they are addressed improperly\. We will not consult the faculty and staff directory to identify recipients, as all required information should be on the envelope or parcel\.

##### Mail and parcels addressed to UBC Okanagan recipients

Mail coming in from external senders to the Okanagan campus must have recipient name, department name, building address, the city, the province, and the postal code\.

The following format should be used:

John Smith  
Central Receiving and Mail Services  
3287 University Way  
Kelowna BC     V1V 1V7

Please note that the common address ‘3333 University Way’ should no longer be used\. All buildings on the Okanagan campus now have their own address\. If you wish to look up an address you can do so by visiting the [UBC Directory](https://www.directory.ubc.ca/index.cfm) and searching for the name of your recipient\.

##### Mail addressed to UBC Vancouver recipients

Mail coming from external senders to the Vancouver campus must have the recipient name, the department name, the building address, the city, and the postal code on the label\. The following format should be used:

John Smith  
Campus Mailing Services  
2329 West Mall  
Vancouver, BC    V6T 1Z4

The building name itself \(i\.e\. University Services Building\) is optional as each building has a unique address\.

##### Mail addressed to off\-campus recipients

All mail being addressed to off\-campus recipients must comply with the addressing guidelines of Canada Post\. These can be found [on Canada Post’s website](http://www.canadapost.ca/tools/pg/manual/PGaddress-e.asp)\.

Some examples of acceptable addressing for off\-campus delivery \(see Canada Post’s page for more examples\):

**Within Canada \(no country required\):**  
John Smith  
2329 University Street  
Vancouver, BC    V6S 2S7

**USA/International \(country required\):**  
John Smith  
2499 Main Street  
Washington DC    20019\-1649  
USA
