Postage charges are posted to FMS on a monthly basis to account code 641000\. If departments require backup or supporting documentation of these charges for audit purposes, they can obtain this by printing monthly statements available online, signing them, and filing them for records\. Discrepancies can be looked into by contacting 604\-822\-6046 or by [using our online portal\.](https://cbs.campusmail.ubc.ca/)
