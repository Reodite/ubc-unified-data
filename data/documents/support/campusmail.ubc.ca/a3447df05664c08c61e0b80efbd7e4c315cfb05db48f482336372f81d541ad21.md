#### [SERVING THE UBC COMMUNITY BY EASILY PROVIDING COURIER SERVICES AT THE LOWEST COST](https://campusmail.ubc.ca/2017/04/04/https-campusmail-sites-olt-ubc-ca-2024-10-08-campus-mail-moving-to-the-ubc-bookstore/)

## What we Do

-   Pick up inter\-departmental and outgoing mail from across campus
-   Act as an on\-campus courier for UBC departments, faculty, and staff
-   Provide free letter mail services to the Okanagan campus
-   Keep postage and courier costs low for UBC by utilizing low negotiated courier and postage rates\.

## Who We Serve

### UBC Departments

We provide mail sorting and delivery service to departmental mail rooms across campus\. We also provide no\-markup outgoing mail and parcel services and inter\-departmental mail transfers\. We provide a low\-cost on\-campus courier to move time\-sensitive or valuable items across campus everyday\. We also deliver Xerox print jobs as well as Staples deliveries\.

### UBC Student Residents

Campus Mail receives mail and packages, sorts them into designated zones, and distributes them across UBC\. Please note the following key details:

-   **Package Deliveries**: Campus Mail does not sign for any packages\.
-   **Tracking Updates**: If your Canada Post tracking shows "delivered," it means your package has arrived at the Canada Post distribution center in Richmond\.
-   **Sorting &amp; Distribution:** Once received, Campus Mail sorts the packages, and distribution across campus begins the following business days\. Deliveries to residences occur twice a week\.
-   **Package Pickup:** UBC community members are welcome to pick up their packages at the Bookstore between 10 AM and 4 PM, Monday to Friday\.

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to Campus Mail support at [campus\.mail@ubc\.ca](mailto:campus.mail@ubc.ca)\.
