We pickup outgoing parcels at the same time we pickup outgoing mail from each department daily\. This is done between 8AM and noon daily, Monday to Friday\. So, please ensure that your items are prepared and ready for pickup before your department’s scheduled pickup time\.

If your item is particularly time sensitive, please call us at 604\-822\-9495, and we can pick it up via our on\-campus courier for a small fee\.
