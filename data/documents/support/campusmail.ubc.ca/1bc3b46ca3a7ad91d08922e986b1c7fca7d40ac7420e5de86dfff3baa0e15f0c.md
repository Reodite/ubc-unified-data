Both incoming and outgoing mail and parcels must be addressed properly to ensure mail gets to its intended recipient in a timely manner\. If mail coming in to Campus Mailing Services is incompletely or incorrectly addressed, we must return the mail to Canada Post or to the sender directly for re\-addressing\. This will mean that mail or parcels will be substantially delayed in reaching you or your recipient\. If mail we are picking up to be sent out is addressed improperly, it may be refused by Canada Post or other carriers and may be sent back to you for re\-addressing\. So, following the below guidelines is crucial in making sure your mail or parcels reach their destination\.

Please note that due to the volume of mail and parcels we receive, we are not able to research where items are to go if they are addressed improperly\. We will not consult the faculty and staff directory to identify recipients, as all required information should be on the envelope or parcel\.

### Use One Address

No matter where your mail or parcels are going, please ensure only one address is present on the envelope or parcel\. If more than one address is present, we may have to return it to you to verify the correct address\. This is especially critical for inter\-departmental envelopes that are re\-used multiple times\.

### Mail addressed to UBC Vancouver recipients

Mail coming from external senders to the campus must have the recipient name, the department name, the building address, the city, and the postal code on the label\. The following format should be used:

John Smith  
Bookstore  
6200 University Blvd,  
Vancouver, BC V6T 1Z4

The building name itself \(i\.e\. University Services Building\) is optional as each building has a unique address\.

### Mail addressed to off\-campus recipients

All mail being addressed to off\-campus recipients must comply with the addressing guidelines of Canada Post\. These can be found on Canada Post's website\.

Some examples of acceptable addressing for off\-campus delivery \(see Canada Post's page for more examples\):

Within Canada \(no country required\):  
John Smith  
2329 University Street  
Vancouver, BC V6S 2S7

USA/International \(country required\):  
John Smith  
2499 Main Street  
Washington DC 20019\-1649  
USA
