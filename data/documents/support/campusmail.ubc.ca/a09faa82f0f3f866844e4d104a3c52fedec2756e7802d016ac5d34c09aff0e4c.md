Campus Mailing Services handles pick up, sorting, and distribution of UBC’s inter\-departmental mail free of charge\.

For outgoing mail and parcels, departments benefit from discounted rates, as our high\-volume agreements with multiple couriers provide significant savings for departments\. We can ship with virtually any carrier and service level you require\.

We also pick up mail and parcels for UBC off\-campus locations, though this service is not available daily\. Please connect with our support team for more details\.

**Further questions? Want a quotation?**

If you have any questions about our parcel service, or would like some guidance on what various services may cost, please contact us at **604\-822\-6046** – 604 822 9495 or email us to Campus Mail Support address &lt;[campus\.mail@ubc\.ca](mailto:campus.mail@ubc.ca)&gt;

**Why Use Our Service Instead of Booking Couriers Directly?**

1.  **Save Money**  
    Our agreements with multiple carriers, volume discounts, and team of trained mail professionals help maximize savings for your department\. We compare rates across multiple carriers to ensure you get the best option at the lowest cost\.
2.  **Save Time**  
    No need to fill out courier forms, schedule pickups, or reconcile payments\. Simply attach a quick form to your addressed parcel, leave it in your department’s mailroom, and we’ll handle the rest—including accounting, billing, and carrier coordination\.
3.  **Support Sustainability**  
    By consolidating courier pickups at a single location on campus, we significantly reduce the number of courier vehicles, lowering emissions and minimizing campus congestion\.

**Questions or Need a Quote?**

For inquiries about our parcel services or pricing, contact us at **604\-822\-6046** or **604\-822\-9495**, or email **Campus Mail Support** at [campus\.mail@ubc\.ca](mailto:campus.mail@ubc.ca)\.
