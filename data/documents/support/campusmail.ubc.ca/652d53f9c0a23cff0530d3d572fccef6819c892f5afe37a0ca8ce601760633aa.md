##### Sending or receiving lots of similar mail?

CRMS endeavors to have all mail sorted and delivered one business day after it has been received by Canada Post\. We also endeavor to have all mail processed and sent out across campus and externally within one business day of picking it up from departments\. However, large, unexpected shipments may not allow us to meet this target\.

If you are expecting to send out a large volume of mail \(&gt; 100 pieces\), please give CRMS as much notice as possible by contacting us at **250\-807\-9772**\. We are always willing to work with you to ensure your deadlines get met, and are best able to do so with plenty of notice\. Please notify us if you expecting this volume of mail to arrive from an independent print shop or courier as well\. Please note that departements will be billed for large mail outs of AdMail, flyers, marketing materials, surveys, and other non\-core mail outs\. Payment must be made to CRMS via JV for these services\. If you wish to know about how much a particular mail out will cost, please contact us at 250\-807\-9772 to discuss your options\.

If you are sending out a bulk mail shipment across both campuses, then you must ensure that mail is first **sorted by UBC mail zone \(Vancouver\), then by department name within that zone****\.** Mail destined for UBC Okanagan should be separated from mail going to UBC Vancouver

If bulk mail is not sorted properly prior to reaching CRMS, or if we are not advised in advance of its arrival, there may be unnecessary delays in having your mail sent out across campus\. If mail is not sorted properly prior to arriving at CRMS, we may have to return it to sender for re\-sorting\.
