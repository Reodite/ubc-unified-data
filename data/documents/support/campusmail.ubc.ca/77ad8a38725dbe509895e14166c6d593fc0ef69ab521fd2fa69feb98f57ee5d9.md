### Mailing and Distribution Overview

Campus Mailing Services in Vancouver handles the sorting and distribution of all internal mail and parcels at no charge for the entire UBC Vancouver campus\.  Additionally, we offer outgoing mail and parcel services, mail redirection, on\-campus courier services, and other related mailing solutions\.

Please note that the services of Campus Mail are for University business only\. Personal items that are not for UBC use should not be delivered to the University\.
