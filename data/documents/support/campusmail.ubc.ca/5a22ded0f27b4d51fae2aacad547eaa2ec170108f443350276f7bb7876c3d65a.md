In order to facilitate billing to departments on a monthly basis, departments who would like to use the outgoing mail processing services of CMS must fill out and submit an [Application for Courier and Metering Services](https://ubcca-my.sharepoint.com/personal/ruel_inocentes_ubc_ca/_layouts/15/onedrive.aspx?id=%2Fpersonal%2Fruel%5Finocentes%5Fubc%5Fca%2FDocuments%2FForms%20and%20Labels%2FWD%20Application%20For%20Courier%2DMetering%20R1%2Epdf&amp;parent=%2Fpersonal%2Fruel%5Finocentes%5Fubc%5Fca%2FDocuments%2FForms%20and%20Labels)\. Please note that it is the responsibility of departments to ensure that the information we have on file for their department is correct and up\-to\-date\. Please ensure you contact us at 604\-822\-6046 or at [campus\.mail@ubc\.ca](mailto:campus.mail@ubc.ca?subject=Update%20Mail%20Metering%20Account) immediately if there are any changes to your account\.
