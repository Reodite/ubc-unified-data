For new departments on campus, or departments that are changing locations, please fill out an [Application for Mail Pick up and Delivery](https://campusmail.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/04/UBCV-Application-for-Mail-Pick-Up-and-Delivery.pdf)\.

For departments wishing to send outgoing mail, please fill out an [Application for Courier Metering Services](https://ubcca-my.sharepoint.com/:b:/g/personal/ruel_inocentes_ubc_ca/ERe30HE1BoJKgQQ0NS93EA4Bb42lFbQ4G2ZdMw2za_dWQw)\.

For departments wishing to set up a Business Reply account, please fill out an [Application for Business Reply](https://campusmail.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/05/UBCV-Application-for-Business-Reply.pdf)\.

All forms can be submitted to [campus\.mail@ubc\.ca](mailto:campus.mail@ubc.ca)\.

To send out parcels/courier items, use the [Parcel/Courier form](https://campusmail.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/04/Courier-Parcel-Form.pdf)\.

The following Canada Post forms and labels are available at Campus Mailing Services:

-   US and International small packet declaration stickers
-   International Surface/Air Parcel Label
-   US XpressPost Parcel Label
-   International XpressPost Parcel Label
-   Registered Letter – Canadian and International stickers and Trace Mail Receipt Booklet
-   US Expedited Parcel\.

Additional Canada Post forms and labels may be picked up at an official Canada Post outlet\.

### **Questions?**

For inquiries about our parcel services or pricing, contact us at **604\-822\-6046** or **604\-822\-9495**, or email **Campus Mail Support** at [campus\.mail@ubc\.ca](mailto:campus.mail@ubc.ca)\.
