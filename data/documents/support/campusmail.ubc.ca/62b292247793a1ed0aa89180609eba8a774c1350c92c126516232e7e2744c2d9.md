For new departments on campus, or departments that are changing locations, please fill out an [Application for Mail Pick up and Delivery](https://campusmail.ubc.ca/sites/campusmail.ubc.ca/files/UBCV%20Application%20for%20Mail%20Pick%20Up%20and%20Delivery.pdf)\.

For departments wishing to send outgoing mail, please fill out an [Application for Metering Services](https://campusmail.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/11/Application-for-Metering-Services.pdf)\.

For departments wishing to set up a Business Reply account, please fill out an [Application for Business Reply](https://campusmail.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/11/Application-for-Metering-Services.pdf)\.

All forms can be submitted to [campus\.mail@ubc\.ca](mailto:campus.mail@ubc.ca)\.

To send out parcels/courier items, use the [Parcel/Courier form](https://campusmail.ubc.ca/sites/campusmail.ubc.ca/files/UBCV%20Parcel%20Form.pdf)\.

The following Canada Post forms and labels are available at Campus Mailing Services:

-   US and International small packet declaration stickers
-   International Surface/Air Parcel Label
-   US XpressPost Parcel Label
-   International XpressPost Parcel Label
-   Registered Letter – Canadian and International stickers and Trace Mail Receipt Booklet
-   US Expedited Parcel\.

Additional Canada Post forms and labels may be picked up at an official Canada Post outlet\.

For any inquiries regarding the above, you can contact us at **604\-822\-9495**\.
