Thank you for choosing to re\-home your functional electronics with reuse\-it\!

UBC produces tonnes of electronic waste that go to the recycling on a regular basis; however, many of these items still work and can find a new life in another department on campus\. Do your part to reduce waste on campus and help UBC build a circular economy of reuse\.

[Proceed to the reuse\-it\! site](https://reuseit.ubc.ca/)

*\*\*Make sure that no personal and/or confidential data remains on the electronic product prior to pick up or drop off\.\*\**

Unsure what e\-waste can be recycled? Check out [UBC Building Operations’ E\-Waste page](https://buildingoperations.ubc.ca/accountability/sustainability/zero-waste/e-waste/?login)\.
