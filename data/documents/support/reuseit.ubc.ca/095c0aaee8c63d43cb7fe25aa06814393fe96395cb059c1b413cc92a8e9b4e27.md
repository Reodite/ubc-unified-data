**Support On\-Campus Sustainability with reuse\-it\!**

*Is your storage room crowded with items you don’t need? Looking for equipment or office items on a budget?* 

---

**reuse\-it\! UBC is a virtual online warehouse** that allows current UBC Vancouver employees to find and exchange items between departments\. This can include furniture, office supplies, lab equipment, and more\! 

## **Reusing surplus assets**…

-   Reduces unnecessary waste 
-   Shrink’s UBC’s GHG footprint 
-   Saves departments money
-   Maximizes utilization of assets 
-   Reduces the number of items in storage\.

## **Powered by Rheaply AxM**

The updated version of reuse\-it\! UBC is powered by Rheaply’s Asset Exchange Manager \(AxM\) to provide you a seamless, easy\-to\-use service\.

**With AxM, you can:** 

-   Manage your profile 

-   Create listings, make requests, and browse or search for items  

-   Make and manage offers 

-   Message users

-   Get notifications for new availabilities or listing updates

---

## **Getting Started**

If you are a current UBC Vancouver staff or faculty member at the Point\-Grey campus, you are eligible to use our service\.

**Registering is as easy as logging in with your UBC email and CWL credentials\.** 

[Login to Rheaply AxM](https://axm.rheaply.com/)
