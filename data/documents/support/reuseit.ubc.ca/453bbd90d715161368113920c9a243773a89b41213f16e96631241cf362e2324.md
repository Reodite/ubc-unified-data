## reuse\-it\!

reuse\-it\! is designed to help you and your department save money, while helping to meet UBC’s goals for zero waste and climate action\. 

reuse\-it\! is operated by UBC Financial Operations in collaboration with UBC Campus and Community Planning, Sustainability and Engineering unit\.

## Rheaply

[Rheaply Inc\.](https://rheaply.com/) is a software company, based in Chicago, that creates Enterprise Asset Management \(EAM\) technology to help promote a circular economy\. Rheaply’s Asset Exchange Manager \(AxM\) is their primary product and was released in 2016\.
