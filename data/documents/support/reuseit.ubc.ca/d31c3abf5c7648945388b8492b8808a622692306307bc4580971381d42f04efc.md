reuse\-it\! UBC is an important component of UBC’s Zero Waste Action Plan, as it fosters waste reduction and reuse, and supports the advancement of the circular economy\.

A 2016 UBC SEEDS study demonstrated that a reuse program dedicated to surplus office furniture alone could produce potential cost savings of over $600,000 for the university\. reuse\-it\! aims beyond that to include other streams of surplus assets, such as lab equipment which is a substantial area of reuse for UBC\. 

Read more about waste diversion at UBC and download the Zero Waste Action Plan [here](https://sustain.ubc.ca/campus/recycling-waste/zero-waste-action-plan)\.
