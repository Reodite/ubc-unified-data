ANTH 409A (3) Topics in Applied Anthropology (Kemple, Thomas ; Vivaldi, Ana)
Advanced study of the theory and practice of applied, action, and consultancy anthropology. Topics

Indigenous Knowledge, Systems (IKS), ways of knowing, being, teaching and learning draws on social

                                               Education


EDUC 344 (2) Issues in Indigenous Education (Blain, Karen)
No description available.


EDUC 440 (3) Aboriginal Education in Canada (Multiple Instructors)
Aboriginal Education in Canada is intended to provide teacher candidates with opportunities to
explore how to respectfully and meaningfully integrate Aboriginal/Indigenous history, content, and
world views. You will examine the role of Indigenous knowledge and ways of knowing in
Aboriginal/Indigenous societies and learn how to make use of this knowledge in your planning for the
classroom, school and community. The goal is to assist all educators to make a contribution to
transforming Aboriginal education in order to improve educational outcomes for
Aboriginal/Indigenous learners and enhance learning for all students.


EDUC 442A (1) Critical Issues in Indigenous Education (Archibald, Jo-ann)
Post-practicum students will explore how a school program may need to be modified in order to
integrate more fully First Nations history, content, and world views.
