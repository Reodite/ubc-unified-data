In 1997, the British Columbia Library Association, the First Nations Interest Group, and the University of British Columbia [First Nations House of Learning](http://www.longhouse.ubc.ca/) created [an endowed scholarship](https://ischool.ubc.ca/awards/gene-joseph-first-nations-scholarship/) in honour of Gene Joseph \(MLS, 1982\)\. The Gene Joseph Scholarship is awarded to an Indigenous graduate student at UBC’s [iSchool](https://ischool.ubc.ca/)\. The award is made on the recommendation of the iSchool, the First Nations House of Learning and [Faculty of Graduate Studies](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/)\.
