## [Image](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/files/2016/12/Xwi7xwa_Copper_Cropped_SD-146x300.jpg)Gifts in Kind

Donations of books and archival materials enhance our collections\. Due to limitations in space and staff resources, we are only able to accept unique items that meet our current collection needs\. If you have a book or collection that you would like to donate to X̱wi7x̱wa, ple ase contact the [X̱wi7x̱wa Head Librarian](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/about/contacts/)\.
