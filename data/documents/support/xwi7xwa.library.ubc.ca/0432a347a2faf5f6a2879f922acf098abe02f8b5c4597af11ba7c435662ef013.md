This page is intended to help with questions you may have about, or connections you hope to make with, Indigenous Peoples\.

### X̱wi7x̱wa Library &amp; the First Nations House of Learning \(FNHL\) Longhouse

[**X̱wi7x̱wa Library**](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/) is a branch of UBC Library, and houses academic and community Indigenous scholarship\.

X̱wi7x̱wa librarians can help:

-   Find published resources for academic, personal, or community research through [research appointments](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/services/make-an-appointment/)
-   Navigate UBC library collections, research tutorials can be [found here](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/library_tutorial)
-   Provide [library instruction](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/services/make-an-appointment/)

Contact X̱wi7x̱wa:

-   Research assistance: [xwi7xwa\.library@ubc\.ca](mailto:xwi7xwa.library@ubc.ca), \(604\)822\-8738
-   Book a tour/class visit: [access\.xwi@ubc\.ca](mailto:access.xwi@ubc.ca)

[**The First Nations House of Learning \(FNHL\) Longhouse’s**](https://fnhl.ubc.ca/longhouse) mandate is to **make the University’s vast resources more accessible to Indigenous students** and to improve its ability to meet their needs\. The Longhouse has information on Indigenous Engagement on their [portal here](https://indigenous.ubc.ca/indigenous-engagement/)\.

### **Curriculum Development &amp; Teaching**

[Indigenous Initiatives at the Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology \(CTLT\)](https://indigenousinitiatives.ctlt.ubc.ca/about/) develops and offers programming, resources, and consultations focused on Indigenous engagement in curriculum, pedagogy, classroom climate, and professional development\. **It provides support and professional development for faculty, teaching assistants, and graduate students\.**

-   [Consultations and Services](https://indigenousinitiatives.ctlt.ubc.ca/consultations-services/)
-   [Research and Resources](https://indigenousinitiatives.ctlt.ubc.ca/research-resources/)
-   [Programming](https://indigenousinitiatives.ctlt.ubc.ca/programming/)

### **Contacting Elders and/or Knowledge Holders**

Neither the Longhouse nor X̱wi7x̱wa connects researchers to Elders and/or knowledge holders directly\. UBC faculty, staff and students are encouraged to connect with members of their department and/or faculty about its existing Indigenous engagement efforts, or with [CTLT](https://indigenousinitiatives.ctlt.ubc.ca/about/) for curriculum development and teaching support\.

The [UBC protocol office](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/protocol-at-ubc/?login) has guidelines for:

-   [Acknowledging the Musqueam People at Ceremonies and Events](https://ceremonies.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/08/Guidelines-Musqueam_July-2019.pdf)
-   [Land Acknowledgments at Virtual Ceremonies and Events](https://ceremonies.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/05/Land-Acknowledgment-July-16-2020.pdf)
-   [Indigenous people’s language guidelines](https://assets.brand.ubc.ca/downloads/UBC_Indigenous_Peoples_Language_Guide_2024.pdf)

### **Musqueam**

The [Musqueam Protocol Office](https://www.musqueam.bc.ca/departments/finance-admin/protocol/) facilitates contact with the Musqueam Indian Band and Chief and Council and responds to requests for Musqueam representation in the broader community\.

You can learn more about Musqueam through their listed [Educational Resources](https://www.musqueam.bc.ca/our-story/educational-resources/), or X̱wi7x̱wa’s research guide, [xʷməθkʷəy̓əm \(Musqueam\)LibGuide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/Musqueam)\.
