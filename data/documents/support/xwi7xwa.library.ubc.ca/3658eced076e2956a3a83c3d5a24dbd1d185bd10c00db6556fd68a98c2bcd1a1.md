## Xwi7xwa Library is open to everyone and we look forward to engaging with you

We offer [borrowing services](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/services/borrowing/ "https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/services/borrowing/") and [technology](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/services/technology-at-xwi7xwa/ "https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/services/technology-at-xwi7xwa/") to provide access to our [unique collections](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/collections/ "https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/collections/")\.

We support research through [bookable appointments](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/services/make-an-appointment/ "https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/sv_5prqpdurq6k8xog"), [classroom instruction](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/services/make-an-appointment/ "https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/sv_5prqpdurq6k8xog") and research guides\.

We engage with community through [bookable tours](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/services/make-an-appointment/ "https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/sv_5prqpdurq6k8xog") and welcome your [feedback](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/forms/feedback/ "https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/sv_5prqpdurq6k8xog")\.
