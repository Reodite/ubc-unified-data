Each term, instructors will invite students to their course on PrairieTest – the reservation system used by ORCA to manage sessions and reservations\. Here is a list of steps you will need to do each term, for each course that is using the ORCA for assessments:

1.  Login to Canvas\.
2.  Click on “ORCA Reservations” on the left sidebar\.
3.  Login to PrairieTest using your UBC CWL\.
4.  Enrol into the PrairieTest course\.
5.  Make your first booking\!

For subsequent bookings, you can go directly to https://us\.prairietest\.com to view or edit your bookings\.
