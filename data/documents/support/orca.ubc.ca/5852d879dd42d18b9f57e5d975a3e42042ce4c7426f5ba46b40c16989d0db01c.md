-   [ICCS building](https://maps.ubc.ca/?code=ICCS) hours are 7:30 AM to 7:30 PM Monday to Sunday
-   Please arrive to your session **via the side entrance to floor 0** \(see image below\) early enough so that you can make sure you can enter the building before your scheduled reservation\.
-   The side\-entrance also has a ramp for wheelchair accessibility\.
-   All students enrolled in a CPSC course are automatically granted access to enter the building after hours via their UBC student card\.
