On this page are ORCA policies relevant faculty members and course teaching staff\. There are more policies relevant for students [on this page](https://science-orca-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/students/policies/)\.

## Allowed materials in ORCA exams

Before students start their assessments, all student belongings must be placed at the front of the room \(backpacks, bicycles, skateboards, purses, laptops, cell phones, etc…\)\.

Computers in the ORCA restrict access to web\-based resources using a firewall\.

Exams will now be made visible by default and students will be able to make reservations for in\-term tests on Tuesdays at 10 AM two weeks before your exam window starts\.

Students may not bring in any reference material \(cheat sheets, memory aids, any paper\-based resources\) into the ORCA – reference material can be provided to students digitally through your assessments\.

The ORCA provides scrap paper for all students during their assessments\.

Students must return all scrap paper to the proctors at the end of the session\.

Earplugs are provided for all students, if they request them\.

Wireless headphones and noise cancelling headphones are not permitted, except for students with accommodations\.

To listen to some classical piano music on the ORCA computers, wired headphones are permitted for all students\.

Washroom breaks are permitted during students’ assessments unfortunately, we cannot provide extra time for washroom breaks\.

Instructors \(or course coordinators\) are responsible for adding CfA accommodations to PrairieTest

Students whose accommodations the ORCA can support will need to write their exams in the ORCA \([https://orca\.ubc\.ca/students/accommodations](https://orca.ubc.ca/students/accommodations)\)\. The CfA will cancel all bookings for courses that are using the ORCA *unless* the student has a Private Space or other accommodation we are not able to support\.

If students miss a session in the ORCA, they can visit the ORCA and request for a Proctor to delete their session so they can rebook in another session during the exam window\. ORCA proctors do not have the authority to extend the exam window\.

ORCA computers includes the standard Windows Calculator, as well as a physical calculator for all exams\. Students are not permitted to bring in their own calculators\.

ORCA computers include a basic Text Editor that students can use to take notes during their assessment\. All digital files are wiped from the computers after students log out\.

To prevent students from using resources on Canvas, the included calculator and/or text editor, Respondus Lockdown Browser must be used\.

In case of technical issues, or other extraordinary circumstances, ORCA proctors have the authority to extend exam sessions by up to 10 minutes; a member of the teaching team, or the ORCA Director must authorize a further extension\.

Before running a final exam in the ORCA, courses must run at least one lower\-stakes assessment in the ORCA during the term so students don’t have to navigate an unfamiliar setting at a stressful time\.

ORCA sessions may need to be cancelled due to operational reasons \(low enrolment, double bookings, prioritizing exams over viewing, proctor unavailability, etc…\) at the discretion of the ORCA staff \(though, we expect this will be VERY rare\)\. The ORCA will do our best to accommodate students affected by cancelled sessions\.

## [Final Exams](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/policies/#final-exams)

**Courses using the ORCA for a self\-scheduled final exam should not request a final exam through their department or faculty\.**

### [Scheduling your Final Exam in the ORCA](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/policies/#scheduling-your-final-exam-in-the-orca)

There are \(at least\) three cases you fall into if you’d like to run your exam in the ORCA:

A\) If you have been assigned a final exam date by UBC

In this case, you just need to make sure your exam window \(ideally a minimum of 3 days\) overlaps with your scheduled time\. Then in the session when your exam runs, we will hold back a few spots just to make sure all students have an opportunity to do their exam during the scheduled time\.

B\) If you are a first/second year course and have not been assigned a final exam date by UBC

Because you don’t have a centrally assigned exam slot, you’ll need to be extra careful and make sure your exam window is long enough to make sure that students aren’t forced into hardship scenarios because of when your window is\. We suggest making your window \~4 days and asking students to contact you if that will be a problem, and then use PrairieTest Overrides to handle exceptions \(at your discretion\)\.

C\) If you are a third/fourth year course, don’t have a final exam date scheduled, but want to do one anyway

Upper\-year courses have a bit more latitude with final exam scheduling \(and your courses are smaller, so hopefully that automatically means fewer issues\)\. I suggest starting by picking an exam window that works for your course team and see what happens after it’s been released\.

All final exams will have the same rules \(no physical cheat sheets, no physical calculators, all reference material within the exam, standard rules for headphones, earplugs, etc…\), and students will be allowed to leave the final exam whenever they are ready to leave\.

Telling UBC that you are doing your exam in the ORCA means you are opting for a self\-scheduled exam, where students choose a time between a range of dates\. If you would like your exam to strict\-scheduled, that is, at a specific time where all students write at once, then you should let UBC schedule a date and time\. You can still use the ORCA in this case, and we will book the ORCA rooms for just your course at that time\.

### [ORCA Final Exam sessions](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/policies/#orca-final-exam-sessions)

Final Exams in the ORCA have a fixed length of **130** mins \(2h10m\) for all courses\.

-   This will allow us to have 5 sessions/day and get more students through the ORCA, at our busiest time:
    -   Session 1 – 09:00\-11:30
    -   Session 2 – 11:30\-14:00
    -   Session 3 – 14:00\-16:30
    -   Session 4 – 16:30\-19:00
    -   Session 5 – 19:00\-21:30

If you plan to have students write your exam in the ORCA over a period of a few days \(self\-scheduled\), you \***should not**\* request a final exam date and room from the University \(and Jimmy\)\. Instead, you should submit a request of your ideal final exam window with the ORCA through the survey so we can build a schedule and find a suitable window so students can self\-schedule your exam with greater flexibility\. We are trying to solicit exam dates up front this term so we can release the final exam schedule early\.

It is usually a good idea to have this information in your Syllabus and to make an announcement reminding students that the final exams will be self\-scheduled in the ORCA ‘sometime during the official UBC exam period’\.

**Please note that the** [**UBC Senate Policy on Examinations**](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/academic-assessment/use-formal-examination-periods) **is:**

> > “The holding of any examination, formal or in\-term, **is forbidden during the two weeks preceding the formal examination periods** of the Winter Session, usually held in December and April; and, the one week preceding formal examination periods of the Summer Session, usually held in June and August\.
> > 
> > This restriction does not apply to bi\-weekly tests or to traditional and current practices in laboratories\.”
> 
> Courses must have **at least 5 tests/quizzes/examlets** \(spread out\) in the 14\-week period prior to the formal examination period for the course to be qualified as having “bi\-weekly tests” \(and thus exempt from the restriction forbidding assessments in the last two weeks before the formal examination period\)\.
