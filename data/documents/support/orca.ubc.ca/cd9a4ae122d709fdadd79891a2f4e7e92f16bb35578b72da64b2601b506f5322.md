## [Signing up to use the ORCA](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/faculty-frequently-asked-questions/#signing-up-to-use-the-orca)

If you are interested in using the ORCA for your course, fill out this survey:

[Submit an ORCA Request](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_7PC8CChB5fHcpw2?)

## [Adding Students](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/faculty-frequently-asked-questions/#adding-students)

To add students to your PrairieTest course:

1.  Log in to [PrairieTest](https://us.prairietest.com/pt), select your course and then the Students tab at the top of the navigation\.
2.  Click the “Add Students” button\.
3.  Copy/paste in a list of cwl@ubc\.ca email addresses separated by new lines or commas\.

Students cannot make reservations until they have been added to your course\.

## [Linking Exams](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/faculty-frequently-asked-questions/#linking-exams)

1.  On [PrairieTest](https://us.prairietest.com/pt), choose the exam Settings tab and copy the listed “PrairieLearn access” rule with the format:  
      
    `{ "mode": "Exam", `  
    `"examUuid": "41330c3e-8ffa-4ce2-bd8e-73b3029a717b", `  
    `"credit": 100 }`  
    
2.  On [PrairieLearn](https://us.prairielearn.com/pl), navigate to the corresponding exam and choose the Files tab at the top\. Open `infoAssessment.json` and paste the PrairieLearn access rule from the previous step into the `allowAccess` array \(i\.e\., between the brackets\)\. Click on “Save and sync”\.
3.  Refresh PrairieTest\. You should see the link to the PrairieLearn assessment\.

## [Unable to Make Reservations](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/faculty-frequently-asked-questions/#unable-to-make-reservations)

There are multiple reasons why a student would be unable to make a reservation\.

-   Students have not been added to the PrairieTest Course \(see “Adding Students” above\)\.
-   Student has an existing reservation and the session has already passed\. The student needs to visit a ORCA lab to have a proctor clear their missed reservation\.
-   Testing window has closed \(see “Creating Overrides” below \)\.
-   There are not open times for testing\. Students should check additional days or request an override\.

## [Creating Overrides](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/faculty-frequently-asked-questions/#creating-overrides)

To extend the original testing window for a single student:

1.  Choose the exam that needs an override on the course homepage on PrairieTest\.
2.  Select the “Overrides” tab\.
3.  Click the “Add override” button\.
4.  Enter the student’s netid and the start and end date for the override\.
5.  Click “Add override\.”

## [Creating Exams](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/faculty-frequently-asked-questions/#creating-exams)

To create an exam, follow the procedure outlined below\. Your course’s exams will be automatically imported into PrairieTest using the information you provide in the ORCA Intake Survey\. If you need to modify your exam list during the term, for example to add a new exam, please reach out on the ORCA Slack\.

## [Missed or Rescheduled Exams](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/faculty-frequently-asked-questions/#missed-or-rescheduled-exams)

ORCA staff can help students reschedule their exams if there is still time in the testing window\. See the [instructions](https://orca.ubc.ca/students/policies/#missed-reservations) for details\.
