The Open Resource Centre for Assessments \(ORCA\) aims to solve a key problem in teaching and learning: **helping instructors run digital assessments at scale securely and equitably**\. ORCA is best described as a distributed centre \(multiple rooms scattered across campus\) of network\-filtered computer labs dedicated to running digital assessments in 50\-minute increments throughout the day, staffed by trained invigilators\.

The ORCA provides students with a consistent, reliable, and comfortable space to write their exams\. Through identify verification, network filtering, and trained invigilators, we aim to make the testing experience smooth and reduce stress as much as possible\!

The ORCA is a centralized service provided by the Office of the Provost and Vice\-President, Academic and sits under [LTIC \(Learning Technology Innovation Centre](https://ltic.ubc.ca/)\)\. More details about the partial charge\-back funding model [can be found here](https://orca.ubc.ca/about/costs/)\.

## Getting Started

If you’re interested in learning more about the ORCA, [visit our support page](https://orca.ubc.ca/support/) for next steps, including weekly drop\-in tours, and virtual office hours\.

## [Request to use the ORCA](https://orca.ubc.ca/#request-to-use-the-orca)

For returning courses, instructors and course coordinators can request to use the ORCA here:

[Submit your course to ORCA](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_7PC8CChB5fHcpw2?)

**NEW\!** We are soliciting courses that would benefit from using the ORCA as pilots in the fall or spring semesters\. Get in touch with us by email [orca\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:orca.support@ubc.ca)\!

[More information about the location of the rooms can be found here](https://orca.ubc.ca/students/)\.
