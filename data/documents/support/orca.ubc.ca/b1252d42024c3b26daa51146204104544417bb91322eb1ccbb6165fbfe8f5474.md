This step is needed to ensure that only students registered in your course get access to book assessments in the ORCA\. You will need to do this regardless of the assessment system \(PrairieLearn, Canvas, DrRacket, etc…\) used in your class\. There are 3 options, depending on which platform you’re using \(Canvas, PrairieLearn, or something else, as well as which department or faculty your course belongs to\.

## [Get student CWLs](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/student-management/#get-student-cwls)

To enrol students into PrairieTest \(so they can book assessments in the ORCA\), PrairieTest requires a list of UIDs, which at UBC is indicated by their `CWL@ubc.ca` \(note: this is **not** a real email address\)\. To get the CWLs of students enrolled in your course, there are currently three options:

### [Option 1: Generate the PrairieTest self\-enrol link and add it to Canvas \(Recommended\)](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/student-management/#option-1-use-classlist-tool-no-student-action-needed)

Instructors can create a unique link for their PrairieTest course and add it to the Canvas left sidebar so students click on it and are automatically enrol into your PrairieTest course\. You can find this link in your Course Settings on PrairieTest\.

Here’s a quick video to show you the self\-enrolment link and add it to your Canvas course:

[Embedded content: Instructors: PrairieTest student self\-enrol Link in Canvas Sidebar](https://player.vimeo.com/video/1224284530?dnt=1&amp;app_id=122963)

This works very well and is the recommended way of getting your students onto PrairieTest\!

### [Option 2: Obtain a list of CWLs for registered students \[Canvas\]](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/student-management/#option-2-use-the-new-self-enroll-link)

Unfortunately, there is no easy way for instructors using Canvas to get student CWLs\. Fortunately, there exist tools in the UBC ecosystem to convert between names/student IDs to CWLs\. Instructors are encouraged to reach out to their faculty or departmental instructional support staff to see if there is an option available to extract CWLs for registered students in your course\. For instance, instructors of courses in the computer science department have access to the `classist` tool \([docs here](https://my.cs.ubc.ca/docs/classlist)\)\.

### [Option 3: Use the PrairieLearn Gradebook \[PrairieLearn only\]](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/student-management/#option-3-use-the-prairielearn-gradebook-prairielearn-only)

Instructors using PrairieLearn for their assessments can access the CWLs of enrolled students in the Canvas Gradebook\. The Gradebook can be exported, the UID column corresponds to the CWL@ubc\.ca, which can be directly to add students to the course\.  

## [Adding Your Student Roster To PrairieTest](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/student-management/#adding-your-student-roster-to-prairietest)

Once you have a list of CWLs, you will need to add them to PrairieTest \(unless you chose Option 1\)\. To do this, login to PrairieTest, click on your Course, then click on Students\. You will see a box where you can paste in UIDs in the format: `CWL@ubc.ca`\. The box is pretty forgiving, you can paste the list separated by commas, whitespaces, line breaks, or semicolons and any students already in the course will be ignored\.

[A screenshot of the add students popup in PrairieTest\.](https://science-orca-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/add_students-940x270.png)

Instructors should do this at the start of term, and then close to the Add/Drop deadline to minimize the work needed to check/verify/remove students that are no longer enrolled in your course\. Fortunately, if you do have to upload twice, there is a “Synchronize student list” feature in PrairieLearn that allows you to compare and synchronize your roster \(see screenshot\)\.

## [Synchronizing Student Lists](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/student-management/#synchronizing-student-lists)

Once the Add/Drop date has passed, you must add all students to PrairieTest to ensure they can book sessions\. Additionally, synchronize your class roster, to ensure students who have dropped the course are removed\.

[A screenshot of the synchronize student list popup in PrairieTest\.](https://science-orca-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/synchronize_student_list-940x371.png)

This will ensure our numbers are updated and we can better predict usage\.

## [Students Writing in the CfA](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/student-management/#students-writing-in-the-cfa)

If student accommodations can be met in the ORCA, they will need to write their assessment in the ORCA – [see details of this here](https://orca.ubc.ca/students/accommodations)\.  
However, if their accommodations cannot be met in the ORCA, then they will need to write their assessments in the CfA\. To facilitate this in PrairieLearn \(PL\)/PrairieTest \(PT\):

1.  Add a new PL rule restricted by UID to the CfA students attached to the same general PT UUID, with a password\.
2.  Add an “Unproctored” PT session solely for students writing in the CfA, with time bounds that span the whole self\-schedule period \(adjust the constraint on start time as well; it defaults to 15 minutes but must cover the whole period\)\.
3.  Manually add a reservation for CfA students in the special PT session\. The time limit on the exam remains the same; so they can start anytime but only have the usual time to finish plus time accommodations\.

Caveats and other details:

-   The password should not be shared with the student; the CfA invigilator should enter the password on the student’s behalf\.
-   Once you set this up for one exam it’s easy to copy to subsequent exams\. PT has a button to copy a session that is handy for this\.

## [Adding Time During an Exam](https://orca.ubc.ca/instructors/student-management/#adding-time-during-an-exam)

PrairieTest allows for additional time to be added to exams that have already begun\. Proctors may do this in some standard scenarios without notifying instructors, for example if a student’s computer was slow to turn on or if they lost time due to an unexpected technical issue\.

For Canvas quizzes, NEW Canvas quizzes do NOT allow for extensions for exams that have already begun\. However, Classic Canvas quizzes do allow for extensions for in\-progress exams\.
