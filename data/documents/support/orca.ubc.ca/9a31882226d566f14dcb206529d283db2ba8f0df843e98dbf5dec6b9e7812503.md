## [Drop\-in virtual office hours \(for Faculty and Staff\)](https://orca.ubc.ca/support/#drop-in-virtual-office-hours-for-faculty-and-staff)

If you have preliminary questions about the feasibility of using the ORCA for your assessments and courses, you’re welcome to send an email to orca\.support@ubc\.ca\. ## [Additional Support](https://orca.ubc.ca/support/#additional-support)

For more support, please reach out to us by email, orca\.support@ubc\.ca\.

If you’re a student having trouble booking a session for your course, please be sure to include the following information so we can help you:

-   CWL
-   Course \(and section, if there are more than one\)
-   Instructor
-   A clear description of the issue you’re having, and what steps \(if any\) you’ve taken to address the problem

You are welcome to use AI tools to proofread or edit your work, but out of respect for our staff \(and for faster service\!\), we ask that you refrain from sending vague, non\-specific AI\-generated emails and responses\.
