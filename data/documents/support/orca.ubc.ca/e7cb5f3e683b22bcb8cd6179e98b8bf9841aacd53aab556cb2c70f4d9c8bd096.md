## Funding

Prior to July 1st 2026, initial implementation funding was [provided by the](https://tlef.ubc.ca/funded-proposals/entry/914/?login) [TLEF](https://tlef.ubc.ca/funded-proposals/entry/914) \(PI: Dr\. Cinda Heeren\) and operational funding for the CBTF \(computer\-based testing facility\) was provided by the UBC Computer Science Department\.

Since July 1st 2026, the CBTF became ORCA and was centralized under the Office of the Provost and Vice\-President, Academic, [details here](https://orca.ubc.ca/about/costs/)\.
