### Login to Existing RISe Account 

If you have created a CWL account, and also created a RISe account \(**These are two separate accounts**\)\.  
  
Please login with the below CWL button\.

[https://rise\.ubc\.ca/rise/sd/publiccustomlayouts/cwl/loginShib](https://rise.ubc.ca/rise/sd/publiccustomlayouts/cwl/loginShib)[Image](https://www.rise.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/CWL%20Login_0.jpg)

### **Self\-Register RISe Account \(UBC Staff/Faculty/Students ONLY\)**

**Self\-registration for RISe accounts is recommended for UBC staff, faculty, and students\.**

When you log in with your **CWL linked to a valid UBC Employee ID or UBC Student ID**, the system will automatically associate your credentials and allow for self\-registration\.  
 

**IMPORTANT**   
\*\***If the "Register" Button does not show up**, your CWL used to login might not be linked to a UBC Employee ID or Student ID or **UBC Workday has already automatically registered your account****\.** 

If this is the case, please use the **"Register RISe account \(UBC Students/Faculty/Staff\)"** steps in the other menus

### Register CWL Account \(Separate from RISe account\)

If you do not have a CWL account, follow these steps:  
  
**Creating your CWL account is a preparatory step and separate from the RISe account creation\. Once your CWL account is established, our team will proceed with creating your RISe account\.**

1.  Go to [https://activate\.id\.ubc\.ca/iamweb/](https://activate.id.ubc.ca/iamweb/)
2.  Click “**Create a CWL Account**”
3.  Follow the instructions for setting up a Standard CWL Account\.
    -   UBC Faculty, Staff or Students should sign up as FACULTY, STAFF, or STUDENT\.
    -   **Non UBC employees are advised to select the BASIC Account\.**
4.  Complete your identification information and **choose your own CWL username and password\.**
5.  Once your account has been created, you will be prompted to check your email for a confirmation message, whereupon you are expected to **validate your email address\.**
6.  Once your CWL account has been created, **please choose from the above options of registering for a RISe account**  
     

**PLEASE NOTE:** 

**Our RISe team is limited in our ability to assist with CWL Accounts\.** If you are unable to create a CWL account or require assistance resetting your CWL account, please contact UBC IT \- [Service\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:Service.helpdesk@ubc.ca)\)

**Please note:  Response to requests may take up to 1\-2 business days\.** 

**Should we experience higher than expected volume of requests, response times may take longer\. We sincerely appreciate your patience as our team works to complete your requests\!**
