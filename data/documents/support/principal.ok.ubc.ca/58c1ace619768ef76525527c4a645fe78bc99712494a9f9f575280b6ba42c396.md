Join us in celebrating Dr\. Sadiq's achievements as UBC Okanagan's Provost and Vice\-President, Academic

Named a UBC Distinguished University Scholar in 2021, Dr\. Rehan Sadiq has served UBC Okanagan for over 16 years\.

Dr\. Sadiq is a world\-leading researcher in the areas of asset management of water supply systems, environmental risk analysis and lifecycle assessment of built environment\.

He is the author of more than 600 peer\-reviewed journal and conference articles, book chapters and technical reports\. With nearly 16,000 citations, he is among the top cited environmental engineering researchers globally; consistently ranked in the top 2% of scientists in the category of environmental science\.

Rehan is an exceptional leader, highly knowledgeable and deeply empathetic\. He joined the School of Engineering six months before I did, and in those early days, I relied on his advice often\. As many of you know, he enjoys walking\. Before we had our engineering building, we had our offices in trailers\. He would invite anyone from the SoE to join him for a walk starting from our trailers\. Those walks became my crash course in the many parts of academia no one formally trains us for\. Rehan encouraged me to broaden my horizons and work across disciplines\. Our first collaborative grant, a strategic project, was a masterclass in teamwork\. Four faculty members brainstormed for months, producing draft after draft \(I believe we reached version 29\)\. Even when NSERC’s feedback was that we lacked large\-project experience, Rehan turned it into an opportunity\. He suggested we refine the components, find the right partners, and submit the combined scholarship as a journal paper\. That paper won a best\-paper award and has now been cited more than 800 times, giving us the confidence to pursue and secure many large projects since\. He also played a key role in founding the Green Construction Research &amp; Training Center\. Now, a brief confession for comic relief: from the start, I considered Rehan such a strong role model that I decided to live near him twice\. We became neighbours in Glenmore \(where he graciously served as my culinary guinea pig\)\. When Rehan moved to Quail Ridge, I, purely by coincidence, of course \(😊\), moved six months later and ended up next door again\. He insists it was a chance; I call it strategic mentoring by proximity\. Above all, Rehan has been a trusted colleague, a generous collaborator, and a true friend\. His leadership, integrity, and kindness have left a lasting mark on our community\. He will be deeply missed, and his impact will continue to guide us\.
