*September 5, 2017*

On June 30th, 2017, after several bargaining sessions, a Tentative Agreement on Collective Agreement for Faculty at UBC was reached\.  To read the joint Communique announcing the tentative agreement, please [follow this link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/tentative-agreement-collective/)\. The Information Package accompanying the Communique is available at: [https://ubcfa\.wpengine\.com/wp\-content/uploads/UBCFA\-Membership\-Ltr\-Ratification\-Package\-2017\.pdf](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/UBCFA-Membership-Ltr-Ratification-Package-2017.pdf)\. 

The Collective Agreement was ratified by all parties in August 2017\. More information is available in this [Bargaining 2016 notice to the membership](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/member_notice/collective-agreement-ratified/)\. \(A copy of the 2016 – 2019 Collective Agreement is available [at this link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/Faculty-CA-2016-to-2019_V_6July2018.pdf)\.\) 

Our Bargaining Blog discusses many of the issues involved in this round of Bargaining\. To see these posts, visit our Bargaining Blog [at this link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/category/bargaining-updates-2016/) or select “Bargaining Blog 2016” on the sidebar\.
