###### UBC Faculty Association Fall GM  
Thursday, October 28, 2021 at 2:00 pm

##### Join us for a discussion on preparations for Bargaining presented by Dr\. Elizabeth Hodgson, Chair of the UBCFA Bargaining Committee

The UBCFA Constitution requires that a General Meeting be held every fall term\. The first order of business will be to reappoint Hedden Chong LLP as auditors for the 2022 fiscal year\. Following the business portion of the meeting, Dr\. Elizabeth Hodgson will do a presentation on preparations for bargaining which will begin in the spring of 2022\.

Our meeting will be held via Zoom\. Members wishing to join the meeting can [**RSVP to the Faculty Association**](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#c8aea9abbda4bcb1e6a9bbbba7aba1a9bca1a7a688bdaaabe6aba9f7bbbdaaa2adabbcf59aadb9bdadbbbce89d8a8b8e89e88e8f85e8a5adadbca1a6afe8a4a1a6a3) for the meeting link\. We expect the meeting to end no later than 3:30 p\.m\.
