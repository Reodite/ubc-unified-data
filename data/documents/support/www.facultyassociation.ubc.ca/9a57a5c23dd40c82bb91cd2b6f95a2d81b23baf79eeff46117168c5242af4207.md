UBC is inviting comments on proposed Policy \#118 \(Safety and Security Cameras\)\. UBC’s rationale is available on the [UBC Board of Governors’ site](http://bog3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/09/3.4_2015.09_Policy-118.pdf)\.

Please review Proposed Policy \#118 and if you have any questions or concerns, let us know by sending an email to the Faculty Association \([\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f7919694829b838ed996848498949e96839e9899b7829594d99496)\) by Monday, November 16, 2015 to be considered as we formulate the Association&amp;rsquos response\. You also have the option of forwarding your questions or concerns directly to the [Office of the University Counsel](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#cebba0a7b8abbcbda7bab7e0ada1bba0bdaba28ebbacade0adaf) by November 30, 2015\. If you do submit a response to the University directly, please copy us \([\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)\) so that we’re aware of your comments\.
