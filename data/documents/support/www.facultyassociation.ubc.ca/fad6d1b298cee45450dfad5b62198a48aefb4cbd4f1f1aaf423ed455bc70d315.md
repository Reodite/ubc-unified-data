The UBC Faculty Association Librarians and Archivists Committee \(FA LAC\) is seeking new members\. The Committee’s mandate is to keep informed of the affairs of the Library and bring to the attention of the Association matters that directly concern Librarians and Archivists on the implementation of the Collective Agreement\. The Committee also acts as a forum for ideas regarding internal library matters put forth by Librarians and Archivists, and promotes and encourages means of continuing education and professional development for Librarians and Archivists\.

Please have a look at the [Terms of Reference on our website](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/tor_lac_EG.pdf) and [contact Alan Doyle,](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#a0c1ccc1ce8ec4cfd9ccc5e0d5c2c38ec3c19fd3d5c2cac5c3d49df5e2e3e6e180ecc9c2d2c1d2c9c1ced380c1cec480e1d2c3c8c9d6c9d3d4d380e3cfcdcdc9d4d4c5c5) FA LAC Chair, if you are interested in joining the committee or if you have any questions\.
