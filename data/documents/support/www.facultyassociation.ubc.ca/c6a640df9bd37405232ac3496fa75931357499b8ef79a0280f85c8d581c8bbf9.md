Posted on March 06, 2020 at 11:00 AM

Hopefully, a Membership Services Officer will be visiting the Okanagan campus soon, depending on how the current public health situation rolls out\. Please check back for updates\. In the meantime, if you would like to make an appointment with a Membership Services Officer, please [send an email](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#8aecebe9ffe6fef3a4ebf9f9e5e9e3ebfee3e5e4caffe8e9a4e9ebb5f9ffe8e0efe9feb7d8effbffeff9feaaebe4aaebfafae5e3e4fee7efe4fe) with your request or give our office a call \(2\-3883\)\. Include a short summary of the nature of your issue, your rank and your department\. 

***Please note: If you already have an active case, continue to work with your assigned MSO\. Contact information is [available on our website\.](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/about-us/staff/)***

Thank you\.
