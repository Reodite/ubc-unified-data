Dear Colleagues,

This year we will be holding our Fall General Meeting on **Thursday, Oct 30th from 1 to 3pm**\.

This FGM will be held online via Zoom, and you will be required to RSVP to receive the Zoom link prior to the meeting\. Notifications with RSVP instructions will be sent out next week along with the meeting Agenda\.

We look forward to seeing you at the meeting\.

**Dory Nason,  
President**
