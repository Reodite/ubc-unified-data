From the day you’re short\-listed until the day you retire from UBC, the Faculty Association is here to assist you\.

Whatever your type of appointment, we can give you information and guidance when you are being recruited, when first hired and in your first appointments and reappointments\.

Achieving [tenure and promotion](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/worklife/promotion-tenure-process/) will be easier for you because we’ve standardized these processes and can guide you through them\.

If you are [on leave](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/worklife/leaves/) or take a reduced appointment, we can assist you\.

When you consider [retirement](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/worklife/retirement/), we have options which can support your decisions\.

Throughout your employment at UBC, the Association can help you be [familiar with and protect your career earnings](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/worklife/salaries/), and we can point you to resources for [professional development](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/worklife/career-development/)\.
