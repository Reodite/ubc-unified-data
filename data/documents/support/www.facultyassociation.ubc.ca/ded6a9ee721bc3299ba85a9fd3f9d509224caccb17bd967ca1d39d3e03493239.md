## Fair Employment Week

Every fall, the Canadian Association of University Teachers \(CAUT\) and its member associations including the UBC Faculty Association, organize and host a week\-long celebration of contract faculty\. We also take the opportunity to remind the University of the importance of contract faculty to the academic community\. Last year, the Faculty Association sent a letter to President Benoit\-Antoine Bacon and Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal Lesley Cormack, [posted here](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Nason-signed-LT-Bacon-Cormack_2025_FEW.pdf)\.

This year Fair Employment Week will be held **Oct\.13\-15, 2026** at UBC\. Scheduled events include luncheons for contract faculty on both campuses, as well as a series of online lectures about the rights of contract faculty under the new Collective Agreement\. **Check your [email](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/member_notice/fair-employment-week-2026-1/) for further details and registration**\.

## **Contract Faculty Colloquium** 

The UBC Faculty Association Contract Faculty Committee, in partnership with the Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology, regularly hosts an **Annual Contract Faculty Colloquium** to showcase the scholarly contributions of contract faculty to the academic community\. This is our opportunity to share and learn about the innovative pedagogies and research interests of our contract faculty colleagues\.

The Colloquium is usually held in the spring, both in\-person and virtually over Zoom\. Lunch and light snacks are served for those able to attend in person\. Watch your inbox for the call for papers for the next Colloquium\.

A copy of the [**2026 Colloquium Program**](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/AgendaOnly_ColloquiumApr2026.pdf) is available here\.

## **Sessional Conference Fund** 

**The UBC Faculty Association and UBC regularly provide travel grants for sessional faculty who will be presenting papers at academic conferences\. Please note that applications will only be accepted after a call for applications has been made – generally at the end of January\.** 

The deadline for applications was February 27, 2026 for this calendar year\.

Last year’s grant was up to a maximum value of up to $1500\.00 for travel, accommodation, conference fees and meals \(UBC per diem\)\. Sessionals awarded grants can claim expenses for presenting at a conferences attended in 2026, regardless of whether were on contract at the time of the conference\. Claims for reimbursement can be made once you return to employment, so long as it is prior to February 26\[th\] 2027\.

Although not guaranteed every year, this grant is available to sessional lecturers who present papers at conferences within the calendar year\. Applications are only accepted **after a call has been made** \(usually in January\) and MUST be submitted by the deadline for conferences in that calendar year in order to qualify \(unless otherwise specified\)\. **Applications made after this date are not accepted\.**

For additional information, [visit the Sessional Conference Fund page](https://hr.ubc.ca/career-development/professional-development-funding/faculty-professional-development-funding/sessional-conference-fund)\.
