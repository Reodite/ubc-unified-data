Posted on November 08, 2018 at 11:05 AM

As part of our preparations for contract negotiations on the next Collective Agreement, Elizabeth Hodgson, Chair of the Bargaining Preparation Committee, and Bronwen Sprout, FA President, would like to invite you to join us in a discussion of issues that are important to you\. This will help us identify key areas to pursue when we meet with the university at the bargaining table in the new year\.

**We’d like to extend an invitation to all faculty at UBC Okanagan to join us on:**

###### DATE: Monday, 26 November 2018  
TIME: 3:00 p\.m\. to 4:30 p\.m\.  
LOCATION: Arts 110 

Participation by faculty members in the lead up to negotiations is important to us and makes our Association strong and effective\. We look forward to hearing your views\.
