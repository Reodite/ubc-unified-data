### Chair, Member Services and Grievance Committee \(MSGC\)

The UBCFA would like congratulate **Susan Paterson** \(Koerner Library\) who has been elected by acclamation to the position of Chair, Member Services and Grievance Committee, effective July 1st\.

MSGC committee members volunteer their time to guide the Faculty Association’s operational and policy decisions by overseeing the resolution of complaints &amp; grievances and providing recommendations around arbitration proceedings\. We would like to thank Susan for standing for the role of Chair and her dedication to the important work the MSGC\.

If you have any questions about standing committee roles, the work of the Member Services and Grievance Committee, or this by\-election, please contact the Faculty Association at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#9cfafdffe9f0e8e5b2fdefeff3fff5fde8f5f3f2dce9feffb2fffd)
