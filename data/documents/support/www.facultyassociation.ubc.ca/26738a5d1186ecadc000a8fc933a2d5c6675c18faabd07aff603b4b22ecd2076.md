The UBC Faculty Association makes every effort to keep our membership informed on issues affecting work\-life at our Vancouver and Okanagan campuses and other locations\. Email serves as an important and timely channel to provide information regarding university\-related issues, bargaining updates and policy items along with notices of meetings and events\. Most email notices to faculty are archived here\. Bargaining updates are issued throughout the bargaining process and are accessible in the Bargaining blog section\. For information on the bargaining process resulting in the current Collective Agreement, please see our [bargaining blog](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/collective-agreement/bargaining-2025/)\.
