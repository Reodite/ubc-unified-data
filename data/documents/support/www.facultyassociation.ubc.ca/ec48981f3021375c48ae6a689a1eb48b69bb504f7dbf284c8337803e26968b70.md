# Final Call for Nominations

## 2023 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members

The UBCFA Nominations Committee is seeking candidates wishing to run in the upcoming Executive Committee election\. There are 6 \(six\) 2\-year term vacancies and 1 \(one\) 1\-year term vacancy\. The nomination period will remain open for the month of February\.

If you are interested in standing for election, please [download the nomination form](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/assets/media/ExecElection2023_NominationForm_wNC.pdf) and submit it to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#2f494e4c5a435b56014e5c5c404c464e5b4640416f5a4d4c014c4e105c5a4d454a4c5b1261404246414e5b4640410f69405d420f6e5b5b4e4c474a4b) by **4:30 p\.m\., Today, Wednesday, March 1, 2022**\. If you would like a nomination form sent to you, please [email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#3e585f5d4b524a47105f4d4d515d575f4a5751507e4b5c5d105d5f014d4b5c545b5d4a036c5b4f4b5b4d4a5750591e5f1e70515357505f4a5751501e78514c53)\.

-   **Chair, Equity Committee**

-   **Chair, Member Services &amp; Grievances Committee\***
    
    *Nominee\(s\) to date:*

    *Nominee\(s\) to date:*

    -   Michael Lanthier, History, UBCV

*All Chair positions will be granted a one\-half teaching release\.  
\*The Chair of the Membership Services &amp; Grievances Committee must have prior service on the MSGC\.*

The Election will run from Wednesday, March 15 to Wednesday, March 29, 2022, closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#751314160019010c5b1406061a161c14011c1a1b350017165b16144a0600171f101601482037363334504745300d101650474530191016011c1a1b5047453b1a181c1b14011c1a1b065047454745474750474524001006011c1a1b)\.
