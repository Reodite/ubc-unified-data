###### Get Involved

The UBC Faculty Association welcomes your ideas and assistance as we work on your behalf\. If you’d like to get involved, whether it’s as an expert on the issues, a member of a committee or to give us your opinions on what’s important to you as a UBC faculty member, get in touch with us [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#03656260766f777a2d6270706c606a62776a6c6d437661602d60623c707661696660773e446677234a6d756c6f756667)\.

###### Email Lists

If you’re an active faculty member you should be receiving email from us regarding issues affecting your worklife, upcoming Faculty Association events and bargaining updates\. If you haven’t heard from us lately, [send us an email](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#bddbdcdec8d1c9c493dcceced2ded4dcc9d4d2d3fdc8dfde93dedc82cec8dfd7d8dec980f8d0dcd4d19df1d4cec9ce) and we’ll add you to our list\.
