## Results of the Proposed Changes to the Constitution &amp; Bylaws, 2022

Dear Colleagues,

On June 16th 2022 the Faculty Association proposed a special resolution to change our Constitution and Bylaws\. These changes included the establishment of an Anti\-Racism and Indigenous Initiatives Committee and the expansion of our Status of Women Committee to include a broader focus on equity\. We are happy to announce that the resolution passed with the required three\-quarters majority\.

Thank you to all our members who cast a ballot\.

More information on the results can be seen in the [**Voting Results**](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/Results-2022-ConstitutionBylaws.pdf)\.

**Dory Nason**

**President**
