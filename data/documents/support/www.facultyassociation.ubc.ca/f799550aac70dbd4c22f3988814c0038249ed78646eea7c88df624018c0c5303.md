October 17, 2012

[Intellectual Property Rights at Risk](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/intellectual-property-rights/)

The Faculty Association is very concerned about proposed revisions to Policy 88, Intellectual Products \(formerly called Patents and Licenses\)\. Some of the elements, if implemented, would appear to seriously erode your intellectual property rights, and negatively impact your academic freedom\.

We have attached the Association&amp;rsquos response to the proposed changes, and you can also find it [here](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/Policy88Proposal_UBC.pdf)\. A copy of the proposed policy is here\. I urge you to review our concerns, and send an email to [Hubert Lai, University Counsel](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#bacfd4d3ccdfc8c9d3cec394d9d5cfd4c9dfd6facfd8d994d9db85d9d987dcdbd9cfd6cec394dbc9c9d5d9d3dbced3d5d4facfd8d994d9db), copied to us, to express your concerns\. Comments on the revised policy are due to Mr\. Lai by October 7, so you must act quickly\. We do not think it is in the best interest of faculty members to have the revised policy implemented and I think a strong signal should be sent about this to the University\.

Respectfully,  
Nancy Langton  
President
