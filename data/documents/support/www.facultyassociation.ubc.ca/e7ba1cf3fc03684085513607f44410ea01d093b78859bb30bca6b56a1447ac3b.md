Posted on March 01, 2016 at 12:00 PM

Tags: [board of governors](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/tag/board-of-governors/), [governance](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/tag/governance/), [presidential search](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/tag/presidential-search/)

Members should have received a notice regarding the presentation of a petition to the UBC Faculty Association signed by over 450 faculty seeking to have a membership vote on a no confidence motion\. The motion, sponsored by Jonathan Ichikawa \(Philosophy\), Juliet O’Brien \(FHIS\), and Alan Richardson \(Philosophy\) is as follows:

“Be it resolved that the Faculty Association of the University of British Columbia has no confidence in the University of British Columbia Board of Governors\.”

A Special General Meeting has been called for all faculty to discuss the motion on Tuesday, March 22 from 2 pm to 3:30 pm\.

Meeting Locations:  
UBC Vancouver: Jack Poole Hall South, [UBC Alumni Centre](http://alumnicentre.ubc.ca/welcome-centre/map/), 6163 University Boulevard  
UBC Okanagan: SCI 337, [Science Building](http://maps.ok.ubc.ca/map/)
