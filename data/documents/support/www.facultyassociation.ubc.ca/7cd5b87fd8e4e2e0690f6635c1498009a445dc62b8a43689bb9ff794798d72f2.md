### VANCOUVER MEETING LOCATION CHANGED: Robert H\. Lee Family Boardroom \(RHLAC 2323 – 2nd Floor\), R\.H\. Lee Alumni Centre

### Join us for a Special Presentation by the UBCFA Bargaining Committee on Thursday, October 20, 2016

We hope you’ll be able to attend the UBCFA Fall General Meeting on Thursday, October 20 at 1:00 p\.m\. PLEASE NOTE THAT THE UBC VANCOUVER LOCATION HAS CHANGED\. Following the appointment of our Auditors for 2016, members of the UBCFA Bargaining Committee will be making a presentation relating to both the recent Arbitration Settlement and the onset of a new round of bargaining\. After the presentation, you’ll be invited to ask questions and discuss the issues\.

After the presentation, there will be a discussion where members are invited to ask questions and talk about the issues\.

Date and Time: **Thursday, October 20th starting at 1:00 p\.m\.**

**Locations:**

-   UBC Vancouver **\*NEW LOCATION\*** – Robert H\. Lee Family Boardroom \(RHLAC 2323 – 2nd Floor\), [UBC Alumni Centre](http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?show=y,n,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=n&amp;locat1=797), 6163 University Boulevard
-   UBC Okanagan \(Video Conference\) – Sci 331

**We hope you can join us\!**
