## Change to P&amp;T Sessions, Wednesday, May 15:  
Single Workshop only starting at 1 pm

As most members are interested in the afternoon P&amp;T Workshop \(1 pm – 3 pm\) we are combining the sessions for faculty members in the Educational Leadership stream and the Research stream into a single workshop beginning at 1:00 pm\. Promotion and tenure processes for both streams will be covered in this comprehensive session\. Although registration closed on May 8th, members who were unable to register are invited to attend on a first come, first serve basis, depending on the number of seats available\.

##### Speakers:

-   **Margot Filipenko,** Chair, Senior Appointments Committee
-   **Gillian Henderson,** Director, Human Resources
-   **George Athans**, Director Faculty Affairs, Provost Office
-   **Robin Roff,** Senior Membership Services Officer, UBC Faculty Association
-   **Kristin Cacchioni**, Senior Manager, Faculty and Employee Relations

This workshop will be held in **ART104**\. Light refreshments will be served\.
