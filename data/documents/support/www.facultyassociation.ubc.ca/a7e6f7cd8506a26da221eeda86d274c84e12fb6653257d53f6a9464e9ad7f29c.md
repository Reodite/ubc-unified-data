As an incorporated non\-profit society, we comply with the provisions of the [Societies Act](http://www.bclaws.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/15018_01)\. This Act requires the organization to have a constitution and bylaws that set out the rules that govern our activities\. The Faculty Association’s constitution and bylaws address many governance issues including membership, committees, elections, meetings and finances\.

Read our [Constitution &amp; Bylaws](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/UBCFA-Constitution-Bylaws_Spring-2022-approved.pdf)\.
