The Nominations Committee of the UBC Faculty Association is seeking candidates for the upcoming election of Executive Committee members: there are ten vacancies, including 3 positions open only to Okanagan Faculty members\. This is a reminder that the nomination period ends **Tuesday, March 7, 2017,** closing at 4:30pm\. The Election will run from Monday, March 20 to Thursday, April 6, 2017 closing at the start of the 2017 UBCFA Annual General Meeting\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the executive positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#e58384869089919ccb8496968a868c84918c8a8ba5908786cb8684da9690878f808691d8b0a7a6a3a4c0d7d5b4908096918c8a8b96c0d7d5a09d8086c0d7d5a68a88888c91918080c0d7d5ab8a888c8b84918c8a8b96c0d7d5d7d5d4d2) or 604\.822\.3883\.

**There are ten vacancies \(two\-year terms – with exception of the term for President\):**

-   **President \(*1\-year term*\)**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Alan Richardson \(Philosophy, UBCV\)*
-   **Chair, Librarians &amp; Archivists Committee**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Alan Doyle \(University Archives, UBCV\)*
-   **Chair, Membership Services and Grievance Committee**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Margaret Wright \(Social Work, UBCV\)*
-   **Chair, Status of Women Committee**
-   **Members\-at\-Large \(3 Positions\)**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Stephen Guy\-Bray \(English, UBCV\), Juliet O’Brien \(FHIS, UBCV\), Karen Smith \(Microbiology and Immunology, UBCV\)*

---

**Okanagan Faculty Members only:**

-   **Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: James Johnson \(Economics, UBCO\)*
-   **Vice\-Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Michael Pidwirny \(Arts &amp; Science, Unit 7, UBCO\)*
-   **2nd Vice\-Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Jennifer Gustar \(English, UBCO\)*

If you are interested in standing for election, please complete the nomination form [available on our website](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/UBCFAExecElection2017_NominationForm_R.docx) and submit it to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#4c2a2d2f39203835622d3f3f232f252d382523220c392e2f622f2d733f392e26292f387102232125222d382523226c0a233e216c0d38382d2f242928) by March 7, 2017\. If you would like a nomination form sent to you, please [email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#e3858280968f979acd8290908c808a82978a8c8da3968180cd8082dc90968189868097deb1869296869097c6d1d3ad8c8e8a8d82978a8c8dc6d1d3a58c918e)\.

*Members of the 2017 UBCFA Executive Election Nominations Committee: Mark Mac Lean, President \(Mathematics\), Vinayak Vatsal, Vice President \(Mathematics\), Diana Carter \(Critical Studies, UBC Okanagan\), Elizabeth Hodgson \(English\), Tina Loo \(History\), and Deena Rubuliak \(Executive Director/Ex\-Officio\)\.*
