### Voting Begins July 3rd

#### Vote to Accept the Recommendation of the Data Committee

Pursuant to the Annual General Meeting of June and September 2022, the Data Collection Committee is recommending approval of the report\. You can review this report [here](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Data-Cmte-Report-May-2024.pdf)\. In order for these recommendations to be ratified they must first be accepted by the membership through an electronic vote\.

The vote will run for two weeks from Wednesday, July 3 to Wednesday, July 17, 2024, closing at 4:30p\.m\.

Members will receive a unique email link to vote online from SimplyVoting\. To cast your vote, you will be required to enter the password contained in your email invitation as well as your 7\-digit UBC Employee ID number\. Our electronic voting is conducted through third party software to ensure anonymity\. Please check your junk folder if you do not receive your email invitation\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please contact the Faculty Association at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#e48285879188909dca8597978b878d85908d8b8aa4918687ca8785db9791868e818790d9b1a6a7a2a5c1d6d4a19c8187c1d6d4a1888187908d8b8ac1d6d4d6d4d6d0c1d6d4b5918197908d8b8a)\.
