# Members Lunch at UBCO  
[RSVP Required](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#37515654425b434e1956444458545e56435e585977425554195456084442555d5254430a6564616717747174176275747817717275177b4259545f)

## Wednesday, February 15th, 12:00\-1:30PM

We were delighted to see so many faces at our Fair Employment Week Lunch back in October\. Thank you to those who were able to make it\. The Contract Faculty Committee and Okanagan Faculty Committee jointly invite you to join us for a second lunch at UBCO to discuss sessionals and lecturers\. Please bring your questions about the terms and conditions of employment and your thoughts on how the Faculty Association can continue our work to advance the interests of contract faculty at UBCO\.

If you are unable to join us at lunch but would like to meet with the Chair of the Contract Faculty Committee to discuss concerns or hear more about what the Committee is doing in the next few months, there will also be two scheduled coffee hours at the Faculty Association Office \(Arts 353D\), 10:30AM to 11:30AM and 2:00PM to 3:00PM\. Please email [Sarika Bose, Chair](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#95e6bbf7fae6f0d5e0f7f6bbf6f4aae6e0f7fff0f6e1a8c7c6c3c5b5d6faf3f3f0f0b5ddfae0e7) to schedule a time to meet\.

LOCATION: Room Arts 353D\.  
**Space is Limited** so please [RSVP to the Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#76101715031a020f5817050519151f17021f191836031415581517490503141c1315024b2425202656353035562334353956303334563a0318151e) by **Monday, February 6th** to reserve your seat\.
