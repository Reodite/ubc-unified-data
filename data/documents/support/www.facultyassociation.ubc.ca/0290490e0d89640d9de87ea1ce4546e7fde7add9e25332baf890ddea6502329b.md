**2021 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members**

Your UBCFA Executive Committee members volunteer their time to help guide the Faculty Association’s operational and policy decisions including advocacy, negotiation, education, communication and planning decisions\. This year, a vote is being held to elect three \(3\) Members\-at\-Large\. UBC Okanagan Faculty will also be voting on a Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee \(OFC\)\. The nomination period closed Monday, March 1st at 4:30 p\.m\.

The Election will run from Monday, March 15 to Monday, March 29, 2021, closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. You will be directed to the voting website and required to enter the password contained in your email invitation as well as your 7\-digit UBC Employee ID number\. Our electronic voting is conducted through third party voting software to ensure that your vote is anonymous\. \(Please check your junk email if you do not receive your email invitation\)\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#a5c3c4c6d0c9d1dc8bc4d6d6cac6ccc4d1cccacbe5d0c7c68bc6c49ad6d0c7cfc0c6d198f0e7e6e3e4809795e0ddc0c6809795e0c9c0c6d1cccacb80979597959794809795f4d0c0d6d1cccacb) or 604\.822\.3883\.
