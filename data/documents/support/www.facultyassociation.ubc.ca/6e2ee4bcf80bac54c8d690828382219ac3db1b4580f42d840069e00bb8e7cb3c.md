## Campus Vision 2050

Curious about planning the physical campus to be ready for the future of teaching, learning and research? Join us to learn about UBC Campus \+ Community Planning’s work and share your ideas for the future of the campus through Campus Vision 2050\!

Join the Faculty Association and UBC Campus \+ Community Planning \(C\+CP\) for an interactive session to learn more about C\+CP’s work, with a focus on the comprehensive land\-use planning process they are leading called [Campus Vision 2050](https://campusvision2050.ubc.ca/) that will guide the physical development of the Vancouver campus\. The session will include a presentation, discussion, and optional time for you to share your input on 5 Big Ideas for the future of the campus and choices we will have to make in planning the future campus\. C\+CP’s presentation will include background on the [Trek endowment](https://finance.ubc.ca/budgeting-reporting/endowment/main-endowment-pool-background/land-trek-endowment-%E2%80%93-ubc-vancouver) and how academic space planning is being considered as part of Campus Vision 2050\.

The session will be held on Tuesday Sept\. 27th from 4\-5:30pm in the BC Hydro Theatre in the CIRS Building, 2260 West Mall \(light refreshments will be provided\)\. There is also a Zoom option for those unable to attend in person\.

If you are interested in attending, please [RSVP](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#583e393b2d342c2176392b2b373b31392c313736182d3a3b763b39672b2d3a323d3b2c650a0b0e08781b3935282d2b780e312b313736786a686d68)\.
