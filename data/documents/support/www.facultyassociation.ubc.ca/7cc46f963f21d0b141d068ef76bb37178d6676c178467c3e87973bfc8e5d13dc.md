##### 2025 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members

The Nominations Committee is seeking candidates wishing to run in the upcoming Executive Committee election\. There are 6 \(six\) 2\-year term vacancies and 1 \(one\) 1\-year term vacancy\. The nomination period will remain open for the month of February\.

If you are interested in standing for election, please [download the nomination form](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/ExecElection2025_NominationForm_Final.pdf) and submit it to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#21474042544d55580f4052524e42484055484e4f615443420f42401e5254434b4442551c6f4e4c484f4055484e4f041311674e534c016055554042494445) by **4:30PM, Saturday, March 1st, 2025**\. If you would like a nominations form sent to you, please [email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#2b4d4a485e475f52054a58584448424a5f4244456b5e494805484a14585e49414e485f16794e5a5e4e585f42454c0e191b4a0b65444642454a5f4244450b6d445946)\.

 **Nominations are open for:** 

-   **Treasurer, Executive Committee**  
    \(1\-year term\)
-   **Chair, Equity Committee**
-   **Chair, Membership Services &amp; Grievances Committee**
-   **Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee**
-   **3 \(three\) Members\-at\-Large**

*All chair positions will receive a one\-half teaching release\.*

The Election will run from Wednesday, March 12 to Wednesday, March 26, 2025, closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#7c1a1d1f09100805521d0f0f131f151d081513123c091e1f521f1d430f091e16191f0841293e3f3a3d594e4c3904191f5c3910191f081513125c32131115121d081513120f5c4e4c4e495c2d09190f08151312)\.
