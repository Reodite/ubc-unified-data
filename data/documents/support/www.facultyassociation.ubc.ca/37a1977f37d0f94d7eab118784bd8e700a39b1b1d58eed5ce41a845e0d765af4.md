Dear Colleagues,

Your UBCFA Executive Committee members volunteer their time to help guide the Faculty Association’s operational and policy decisions including advocacy, negotiation, education, communication and planning decisions\. This year, a vote is being held to elect our Association President for a 1\-year term and for three Members\-at\-Large for 2\-year terms\. Nominations closed Tuesday, March 7 at 4:30 pm\. More information on the candidates and the election will follow next week\.

###### Nominees standing for Election

**President \(1\-year term\):**

-   **Nancy Langton** \(Sauder School of Business\)
-   **Alan Richardson** \(Philosophy\)

**Member\-at\-Large \(3 positions, 2\-year term\):**

-   **Jennifer Berdahl** \(Sauder School of Business\)
-   **Stephen Guy\-Bray** \(English\)
-   **Parisa Mehrkhodavandi** \(Chemistry\)
-   **Juliet O’Brien** \(FHIS\)
-   **Karen Smith** \(Microbiology\)

Congratulations to the following nominees who have been elected by acclamation:

-   Chair, Librarians &amp; Archivists Committee: **Alan Doyle** \(University Archives\)
-   Chair, Membership Services &amp; Grievance Committee: **Margaret Wright** \(Social Work\)
-   Chair, Status of Women Committee: **Dory Nason** \(First Nations &amp; Indigenous Studies, English\)
-   Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee: **James Johnson** \(Economics\)
-   1st Vice\-Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee: **Peter Wylie** \(Irving K\. Barber School of Arts &amp;amp; Sciences Unit 8\)
-   2nd Vice\-Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee: **Jennifer Gustar** \(Critical Studies\)

The election will take place on\-line starting the week of March 20th and ending at the start of the Annual General Meeting, 1:00 p\.m\., April 6, 2017\.

We thank all candidates who have agreed to stand for positions on our Executive\.

*The UBCFA Nominations Committee*
