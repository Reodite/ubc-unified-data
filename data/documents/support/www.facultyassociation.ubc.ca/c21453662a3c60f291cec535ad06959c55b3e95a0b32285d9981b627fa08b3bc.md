### Please join us on Thursday, April 11, 2019 at  
2:00 pm for the UBC Faculty Association  
Annual General Meeting

### Update on Collective Bargaining  
Presented by Dr\. Elizabeth Hodgson,  
Chair, Bargaining Preparation Committee

The UBCFA Annual General Meeting will be held to review the UBC Faculty Association’s 2018 audited financial statements, 2019 budget, and to receive the Annual Report\. Due to changes in the BC Societies Act, unless quorum is met, the Faculty Association must hold a subsequent meeting the following week to conduct the business of the Association\. Quorum under our Constitution and Bylaws is currently 50 members\. Should quorum not be met, then a second meeting will be held on Thursday, April 18 at which time business can proceed\.

**April 11 Meeting Locations:**

-   **UBC Vancouver – Robert H\. Lee Family Boardroom, UBC Alumni Centre, 6163 University Boulevard**
-   **UBC Okanagan \(Video Conference\) – EME 4218**

We hope you will be able to attend\.
