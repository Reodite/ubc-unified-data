## Fair Employment Week  
October 13\-15

Every fall, the **Canadian Association of University Teachers** \(CAUT\) and its members including **the UBC Faculty Association**, hold a week\-long campaign to raise awareness of the precarious working conditions and rights of contract faculty\. It is also an opportunity to celebrate the important work of Sessionals and Lecturers\. This year’s program includes a series of online workshops and in\-person luncheons\. Be sure to register in advance to secure your spot\!

---

**Collective Agreement 101 – Know your rights workshops**

Online Zoom sessions for contract faculty who want to learn more about their rights and responsibilities under the Collective Agreement\. This is a member only event specifically oriented towards the concerns and issues facing contract faculty\. *Please register in advance before Oct\. 13\.*
