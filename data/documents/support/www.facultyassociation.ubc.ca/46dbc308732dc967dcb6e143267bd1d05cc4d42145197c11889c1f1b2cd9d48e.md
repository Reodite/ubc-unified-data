Our correspondence to Hubert Lai, Q\.C\., University Counsel is available [at this link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/LT-HLai-FA-Response-UBCPolicy3_6Feb19.pdf)\. Thank you to those members who provided their comments as we formulated our response\.
