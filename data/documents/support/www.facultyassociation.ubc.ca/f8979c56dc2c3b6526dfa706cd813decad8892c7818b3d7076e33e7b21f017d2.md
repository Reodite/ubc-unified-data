# UBCFA Annual General Meeting  
Thursday, June 16, 2022 at 1:00 pm

## Dear Colleagues,

Further to our message on May 12, our Annual General Meeting will be held on June 16 from 1\-3pm\. Along with a presentation from the Executive Committee on our annual report, with a review of our 2022 budget and 2021 audited financial statements, the Faculty Association will open a discussion on a special resolution to amend our bylaws\. We will also be presenting 2 motions from the membership and the Executive Committee’s response to those motions\. Please find the full agenda with more background and information on these items linked here: [**Annual General Meeting Agenda\.**](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/AGM-Agenda-June-2022-final.pdf)

As with most events at the University for the past many months, our meeting will be held \(June 16th at 1\-3PM\) via Zoom\. Members wishing to join the meeting can [RSVP to the Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#dfb9bebcaab3aba6f1beacacb0bcb6beabb6b0b19faabdbcf1bcbee0acaabdb5babcabe28d8c898fff9e9892) for the meeting link which will be sent early next week\. Thanks to those who have already contacted us\. We expect to conclude the meeting by 3:00 p\.m\.

We do hope you will join us\.

**Alan Richardson,**

**President**
