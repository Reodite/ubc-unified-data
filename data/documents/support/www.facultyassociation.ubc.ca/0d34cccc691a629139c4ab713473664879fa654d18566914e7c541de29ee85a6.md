Every year, campuses across Canada dedicate a week in October to raising awareness of the issues and working conditions faced by Contract Faculty\. This event is organized nationally by the Canadian Association of University Teachers [\(CAUT\)](http://makeitfair.caut.ca/) and locally by your union, the [UBC Faculty Association](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/)\.

This year, the Faculty Association’s Contract Faculty Committee and Okanagan Faculty Committee Chairs invite you to join your colleagues for a special afternoon reception on campus\. This event is being held to recognize and celebrate the contributions of Contract Faculty to the university and to acknowledge the significance of their work in helping UBC become one of the top educational institutions in Canada\. **Please join us:**

**Date and Time: Tuesday, Oct\. 15 from 1 to 3 pm**  
**Location: EME 0020 Lower Foyer**

**We look forward to seeing you\!**
