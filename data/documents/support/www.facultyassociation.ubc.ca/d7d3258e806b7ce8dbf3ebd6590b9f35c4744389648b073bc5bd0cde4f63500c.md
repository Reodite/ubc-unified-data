# **P&amp;T Workshop for Faculty Members**

## **Monday, May 15, 2023, and Tuesday, May 16, 2023  
9:00AM\-11:00AM \(Registration Required\)**

If you are coming up for tenure and/or promotion, consider attending one of these workshops\. Our speakers will guide you through all aspects of the tenure and promotion process at UBC\. You’ll leave with a better sense of how the process unfolds and your role in it\. Please join us to ask questions, discuss concerns and share experiences\.

Please note, this session is for faculty members going up for tenure and/or promotion\. A session for Heads and Administrators will be held on Wednesday, May 17\.

**Speakers:**

-   **Moura Quayle**, Vice\-Provost and Associate Vice\-President Academic Affair \(May 15th\)
-   **Gage Averill**, Provost and Vice\-Provost Academic Affairs UBC Vancouver \(May 16th\)
-   **Dory Nason**, President, UBC Faculty Association
-   **Alex Fisher**, Chair, Senior Appointments Committee, UBC Vancouver
-   **Robin Jane Roff**, Associate Executive Director, UBC Faculty Association
-   **Mark Trowell**, Executive Director, Faculty Relations &amp; ALDP, UBC Vancouver
-   **Kristin Cacchinoni**, Senior Manager, Human Resources, UBC Okanagan

**Please register for your preferred session:**

1.  [Register for 9:00 a\.m\. to 11:00 a\.m\., May 15](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#82e4e3e1f7eef6fbace3f1f1ede1ebe3f6ebedecc2f7e0e1ace1e3bdf1f7e0e8e7e1f6bfd0e7e5ebf1f6e7f0a2d2d6a2d1e7f1f1ebedeca2cfe3fba2b3b7f6ea)
2.  [Register for 9:00 a\.m\. to 11:00 a\.m\., May 16](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#bbdddad8ced7cfc295dac8c8d4d8d2dacfd2d4d5fbced9d895d8da84c8ced9d1ded8cf86e9dedcd2c8cfdec99bebef9be8dec8c8d2d4d59bf6dac29b8a8dcfd3)
