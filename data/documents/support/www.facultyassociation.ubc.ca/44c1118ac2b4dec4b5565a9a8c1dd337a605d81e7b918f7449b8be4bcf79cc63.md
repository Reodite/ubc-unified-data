###### Mark your Calendar\!

#### Second Annual Contract Faculty Publication Display  
Thursday, February 16

The UBC Faculty Association Contract Faculty Committee, in partnership with UBC Library, presents the Second Annual Contract Faculty Publication Display\.

##### Date: Thursday, February 16, 2017  
New time: 12 noon to 4:30 p\.m\.  
Location: Lillooet Room, Irving K\. Barber Learning Commons

Showcased will be a selection of books, videos and articles published by your contract faculty colleagues\. **We hope to see you there\.**

---

###### Coming up on Thursday, March 23, 2017 –

The UBC Faculty Association Contract Faculty Committee, in partnership with UBC Library, presents their **Third Annual Contract Faculty Colloquium**\. This is an opportunity to share with and learn from your contract faculty colleagues across campus in a genial, relaxed atmosphere\. **Mark your calendars for this event:**

##### Date: Thursday, March 23, 2017  
Time: 10:30 a\.m\. – 5:00 p\.m\.  
\*NEW Location\*: Chilcotin Room, I\.K\. Barber Learning Commons

**Registration details and a list of topics and speakers are coming soon\.**
