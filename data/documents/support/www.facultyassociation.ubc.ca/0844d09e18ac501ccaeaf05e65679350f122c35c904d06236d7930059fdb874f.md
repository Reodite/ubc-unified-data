The Faculty Association is looking for new and continuing volunteers who would like to participate in our many committees\. We invite Faculty members from different disciplines, backgrounds, and positions to take part in the rewarding work of the Association\. If you are currently serving on one of our committees, we kindly ask that you confirm whether you wish to continue\. We encourage you to lend a hand on one of the following:

-   Contract Faculty Committee
-   Librarians &amp; Archivists Committee
-   Anti\-Racism &amp; Indigenous Initiatives Committee
-   Equity Committee\*
    -   BIPOC Working Group
    -   Status of Women Working Group
    -   2SLGBTQIA\+ Working Group
    -   Disability Working Group
-   Membership Services &amp; Grievances Committee\*\*

*Terms of Reference for all our standing committees are available via our [Standing Committee](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/about-us/standing-committee/) website page\.*

If you are interested in volunteering or continuing to volunteer for one of these Committees, or if you would like to learn more about this opportunity, email us at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#62475052040301170e161b4c0311110d010b03160b0d0c221700014c01035d111700080701165f340d0e170c160707100b0c0542150b160a42160a07422423)\. In your email we kindly request that you specify which Committee you would like to participate in\.

\*Please note that if you would like to be a member of our Equity Committee you can also choose to indicate a working group you are interested in participating in as an addition to the overarching Equity Committee\.

\*\*The Membership Services &amp; Grievances Committee deals with highly confidential information and requires the utmost discretion\. Upon being ratified to the committee there will be a mandatory orientation for new members\.
