2021 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members

The Nominations Committee of the UBC Faculty Association is seeking candidates for the upcoming election of Executive Committee members\. There are five \(5\) vacancies, each for a term of two \(2\) years\. There are also two \(2\) non\-Executive positions on the Okanagan Faculty Committee which are up for election\. In order to give the membership time to consider these opportunities, the nomination period will remain open until **Monday, March 1, 2021, closing at 4:30 p\.m\.**

The Election will run from Monday, March 15 to Monday, March 29, 2021, closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#1d7b7c7e68716964337c6e6e727e747c697472735d687f7e337e7c226e687f77787e6920485f5e5b5c382f2d5865787e382f2d5871787e69747273382f2d53727074737c697472736e382f2d2f2d2f2c382f2d4c68786e69747273) or 604\.822\.3883\.
