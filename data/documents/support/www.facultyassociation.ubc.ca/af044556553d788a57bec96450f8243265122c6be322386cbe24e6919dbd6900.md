###### Pub Social

The Contract Faculty Committee warmly invites you to join your colleagues at the next pub social\.

***Date:* Thursday, April 6**  
***Time:* 3:30 pm – 5:30 pm**  
***Location:*** **Gallery 2\.0 \(Top floor of The Nest\)**

The first drink is on us\!

---

###### Join us for the next Contract Faculty Marking Party\!

***Date:* Monday, April 10**  
***Time:* 10:30 a\.m\. to 3:30 p\.m\.**  
***Location:* Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre, Chilcotin Room 256**  
[\(Register here\)](http://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/contract-faculty-marking-party-2/)

Come and join your fellow contract faculty members in a quiet room where we can all concentrate on our grading as we prepare to return term papers and final assignments to our students\. You can come for the whole session, or drop in when convenient\. Although this will be a quiet room, we will be doing our work in a collegial atmosphere\.

Coffee and snacks will be provided\.

To register, please visit [the CTLT registration page\.](http://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/contract-faculty-marking-party-2/)
