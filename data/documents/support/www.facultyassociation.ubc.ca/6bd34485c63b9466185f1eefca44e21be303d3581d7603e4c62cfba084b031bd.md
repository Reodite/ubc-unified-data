Posted on September 08, 2025 at 10:31 AM

On behalf of the Okanagan Faculty Committee \(OFC\), I am writing to wish you all a warm welcome back to the UBC Okanagan Campus\. This term we will be hosting monthly drop\-ins for our Okanagan members\. We invite you to join us in creating opportunities for connection, collaboration, and support among faculty at UBC Okanagan\. Be sure to come by for friendly discussions and refreshments\. Our monthly drop ins will be held on:

**September 18th, October 16th, November 20th**  
**2:00\-4:00PM**  
**EME Foyer**

We also welcome faculty to participate in our Okanagan Faculty Committee\. The OFC works diligently to develop local activities and bring attention to concerns raised by our Okanagan Faculty members\. Membership on this committee is open to all members working on the Okanagan Campus\.

*Terms of Reference for our Okanagan Faculty Committee is available via our website, please visit our [Standing Committee](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/about-us/standing-committee/) page\.*

If you are interested in serving on this Committee, which is recognized as Service under the Collective Agreement, or if you would like to learn more about this opportunity, email us at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#ddf8efedbbbcbea8b1a9a4f3bcaeaeb2beb4bca9b4b2b39da8bfbef3bebce2aea8bfb7b8bea9e08bb2b1a8b3a9b8b8afb4b3bafdaab4a9b5fda9b5b8fd9b9cfd929b9e)\.

Looking forward to seeing you on the 18th,

Peter  
Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee
