The Okanagan Faculty committee of the UBC Faculty Association is happy to invite you to our upcoming Community Building event\. We’ll be joined by Deputy Vice\-Chancellor, Lesley Cormack\. This informal gathering will be taking place on **March 20th,** in **EME 1123** from **2:00\-3:00PM**\. Come have a drink and some snacks with us and meet your fellow colleagues from across the campus\.
