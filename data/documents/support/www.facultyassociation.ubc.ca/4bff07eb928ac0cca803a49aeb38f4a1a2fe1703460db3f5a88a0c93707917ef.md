A letter from Mark Mac Lean, President  
On behalf of the UBC Faculty Association Executive Committee

“[This link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/LT-SBelkin_FA-3Feb2016.pdf) will take you to our open letter to the new Chair of the Board of Governors, Mr\. Stuart Belkin\. The letter has also been copied to Premier Clark and Minister Wilkinson\.

We are asking for an external review of the Board and its operations\. This request is prompted by our experiences with Board processes over the past six months, and by the revelations of documents and emails leaked last week \. \. \.”

The complete letter of February 4th to the membership is available [at this link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/enotice/enotice_MemberNotice_BelkinLtr_4Feb2016_ol.htm)
