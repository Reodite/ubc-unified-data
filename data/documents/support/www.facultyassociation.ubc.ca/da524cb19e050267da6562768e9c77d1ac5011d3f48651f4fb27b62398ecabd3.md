CUFA BC: Faculty mourn the deaths of 215 children from former Kamloops Indian Residential School \(xʷməθkʷəy̓əm \(Musqueam\), Sḵwx̱wú7mesh \(Squamish\) &amp; səlilwətaɬ \(Tsleil\-Waututh\) Territories – Vancouver, BC, June 1, 2021\) – The Confederation of University Faculty Associations of British Columbia \(CUFA BC\) stands in solidarity with the Tk’emlúps te Secwépemc First Nation and with all Indigenous faculty, students, and community members following the discovery of 215 children who were buried in unmarked graves at the Kamloops Indian Residential School\. CUFA BC stands with First Nations in seeking the truth about missing and murdered children at the hands of the state in what is a horrific act of genocide whose legacy continues today\.
