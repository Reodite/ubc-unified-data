###### Have your questions about the new vacation entitlements answered

The Faculty Association will be holding an online forum for Librarians and Archivists to learn more and ask questions about the recent settlement with UBC regarding vacation for Librarians and Archivists\. The forum will take place online on **Monday, February 8 at 1 p\.m\.**

**To register and receive your link to the Forum, please [contact the Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#a1c7c0c2d4cdd5d88fc0d2d2cec2c8c0d5c8cecfe1d4c3c28fc2c09ed2d4c3cbc4c2d59ce7ced3d4cc81f3c4c6c8d2d5d3c0d5c8cecf81edc8cfca)\.** If you are unable to attend the forum, but have questions [please email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#1d7b7c7e68716964337c6e6e727e747c697472735d687f7e337e7c226e687f77787e69204c68786e697472733d7c7f7268693d4b7c7e7c697472733d5873697469717870787369)\.
