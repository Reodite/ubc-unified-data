UBCFA President Mark Mac Lean spoke at today’s meeting with the UBC Board of Governors\. He discussed a number of concerns with Board operations previously outlined in a written submission sent to the Board earlier this week\. The UBCFA submission to the Board of Governors may be found [here](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/UBCFA-Submission-to-BoG_12April2016.pdf)\.
