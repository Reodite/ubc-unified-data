**Please take the opportunity to express your views on UBC’s Proposed Amendments to Policy 93 – Closed Meetings of the Board of Governors**

The University is inviting comments on [Proposed Amendments to Policy 93](http://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/files/2016/09/Proposed-Amended-Policy-93.pdf) regarding Closed Meetings of the Board of Governors\.

The rationale that was presented to the Board of Governors for the amendments to Policy 93 is available at this link: [bog3\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2016/09/5\.2\_2016\.09\_Policy\-93\.pdf](http://bog3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/09/5.2_2016.09_Policy-93.pdf)\.

Please review the Proposed Amendments to Policy 93 and if you have any questions or concerns with the proposed amendments, let us know by responding to this email or sending an email to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f99f989a8c958d80d7988a8a969a90988d909697b98c9b9ad79a98c6aa8c9b939c9a8dc4a99695909a80dccbc9c0cadccbc9ba989595dccbc99f968bdccbc9ba9694949c978d8a) by Friday, October 21, 2016 to be considered as we formulate the Association’s response\. You also have the option of forwarding your questions or concerns directly to the [Office of the University Counsel](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#d4a1babda2b1a6a7bda0adfab7bba1baa7b1b894a1b6b7fab7b5eb87a1b6beb1b7a0e984bbb8bdb7adf1e6e4ede7f1e6e497b5b8b8f1e6e4b2bba6f1e6e497bbb9b9b1baa0a7) \(Deadline: October 31, 2016\)\. If you do submit a response to the University directly, please copy us \([\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)\) so that we’re aware of your comments\.

Thank you\.
