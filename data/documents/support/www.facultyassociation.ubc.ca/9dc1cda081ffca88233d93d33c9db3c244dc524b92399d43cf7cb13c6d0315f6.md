# Call for Nominations

## 2023 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members

The UBCFA Nominations Committee is seeking candidates wishing to run in the upcoming Executive Committee election\. There are 7 \(seven\) 2\-year term vacancies\. The nomination period will remain open for the month of February\.

If you are interested in standing for election, please [download the nomination form](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/ExecElection2023_NominationForm_wNC.pdf) and submit it to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#c1a7a0a2b4adb5b8efa0b2b2aea2a8a0b5a8aeaf81b4a3a2efa2a0feb2b4a3aba4a2b5fc8faeaca8afa0b5a8aeafe187aeb3ace180b5b5a0a2a9a4a5) by **4:30 p\.m\., Wednesday, March 1, 2022**\. If you would like a nomination form sent to you, please [email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#1d7b7c7e68716964337c6e6e727e747c697472735d687f7e337e7c226e687f77787e69204f786c68786e6974737a3d7c3d53727074737c697472733d5b726f70)\.

**Nominations are open for:**

-   **Chair, Equity Committee**
-   **Chair, Member Services &amp; Grievances Committee**
-   **Chair, Anti\-Racist and Indigenous Initiatives Committee**  
    \(1\-year term\)
-   **Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee**
-   **3 \(three\) Members\-at\-Large**

*All Chair positions will be granted a one\-half teaching release\.*

The Election will run from Wednesday, March 15 to Wednesday, March 29, 2022, closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#553334362039212c7b3426263a363c34213c3a3b152037367b36346a2620373f303621680017161314706765102d303670676510393036213c3a3b7067651b3a383c3b34213c3a3b267067656765676770676504203026213c3a3b)\.

*Members of the 2023 UBCFA Election Nominations Committee: Dory Nason \(President\), Alan Richardson \(Past President\), Daniel Heath Justice \(Critical Indigenous Studies, UBCV\), Janette Bulkan \(Forestry, UBCV\), Ramine Ald \(Faculty of Creative &amp; Critical Studies, UBCO\) and Deena Rubuliak \(ex\-officio\), and Deena Rubuliak \(ex officio\)\.*
