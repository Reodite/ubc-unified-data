The University is inviting comments on Proposed Amendments to Policy 87 – Research\. The Rationale is available at [http://bog3\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2015/11/2\.3\_2015\.12\_Policy\-87\.pdf](http://bog3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/11/2.3_2015.12_Policy-87.pdf)\.

Please review the Proposed Amendments to Policy 87 and if you have any questions or concerns, let us know by responding to this email or sending an email to the Faculty Association at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) by Wednesday, January 20, 2016 to be considered as we formulate the Association’s response\. You also have the option of forwarding your questions or concerns directly to the Office of the University Counsel \([\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#6c1902051a091e1f051815420f0319021f09002c190e0f420f0d)\) by January 29, 2016\. If you do submit a response to the University directly, please copy us \([\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#5d2833342b382f2e342924733e3228332e38311d283f3e733e3c)\) so that we are aware of your comments\.
