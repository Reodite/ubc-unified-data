Bargaining for the period between July 1, 2014 and June 30, 2016 has been completed\. Documents, including the Arbitration Award, are available to view through the links below\. 

We are now preparing for another round of bargaining\. As with previous rounds, we will keep you advised through member notices and bargaining updates posted to this site\. To see all the Bargaining Updates for the 2014 round of bargaining, select “Bargaining Blog 2014” on the sidebar, or if you’re using your mobile device, [follow this link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/category/bargaining-2014/)\.
