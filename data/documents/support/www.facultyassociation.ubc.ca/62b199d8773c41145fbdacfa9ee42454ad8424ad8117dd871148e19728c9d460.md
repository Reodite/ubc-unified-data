###### 2020 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members

The Nominations Committee of the UBC Faculty Association is seeking candidates for the upcoming election of Executive Committee members\. There are twelve vacancies, each for a term of 2 years \(unless otherwise specified\)\. In order to give the membership time to consider these opportunities, the nomination period will remain open until **Sunday, March 1, 2020, closing at 4:30 p\.m\.**

The Election will run from Monday, March 16 to Monday, March 30, 2020, closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#7b1d1a180e170f02551a08081418121a0f1214153b0e191855181a44080e19111e180f462e39383d3a5e494b3e031e185e494b3e171e180f1214155e494b35141612151a0f121415085e494b494b494b5e494b2a0e1e080f121415) or 604\.822\.3883\.
