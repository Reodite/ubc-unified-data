Posted on November 09, 2018 at 11:38 AM

The Faculty Association is currently preparing for contract negotiations on the next Collective Agreement and expects to begin bargaining early in 2019\. As part of the preparations, we’d like to invite Librarians and Archivists to join us in a discussion of issues that are important to you\. This will help us identify key areas to pursue when we meet with the university at the bargaining table\.

**Please join us on:**

DATE: Wednesday, 14 November 2018  
TIME: 11:00 a\.m\. to 12 noon  
LOCATION: Chilcotin Room \(256\), IKBLC

A light lunch will be served\. Please RSVP to [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#86e0e7e5f3eaf2ffa8e7f5f5e9e5efe7f2efe9e8c6f3e4e5a8e5e7b9f5f3e4ece3e5f2bbcaefe4f4e7f4ffa6c4e7f4e1e7efe8efe8e1a6c5e9e8f5f3eaf2a6b7b7a6aba6b7b4) if you will be attending\.

Participation by faculty members in the lead up to negotiations is important to us and makes our Association strong and effective\. We look forward to hearing your views\.
