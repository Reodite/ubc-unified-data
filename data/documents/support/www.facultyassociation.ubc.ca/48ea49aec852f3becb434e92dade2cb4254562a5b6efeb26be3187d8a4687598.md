Posted on December 04, 2023 at 12:59 PM

The UBC community is invited to comment on the [proposed amendments to the COI Policy \(SC3\) and its associated Procedures](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Proposed-Amended-COI-Policy-SC3.pdf) \(together referred to as the “**Policy**”\)\. The Policy has been in place since 1992 and last underwent a comprehensive review and revision in 2005\.

A Policy Development Committee \(the “**Committee**”\) has been convened to develop proposed amendments to the Policy and has prepared a draft proposal\.

For further information about the proposed amendments, please see: [https://bog3\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2023/11/4\_2023\_11\_Proposed\-Policy\-Amendment\-Conflict\-of\-Interest\-Policy\-SC3\.pdf](https://bog3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/4_2023_11_Proposed-Policy-Amendment-Conflict-of-Interest-Policy-SC3.pdf)

The proposed amendments are now published for community consultation and all members of UBC’s community are encouraged to provide their comments\. You can provide your comments to [the Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#096f686a7c657d7027687a7a666a60687d606667497c6b6a276a68) before **Monday, January 8th, 2024** to be considered as we formulate the Association’s response\.

You also have the option of forwarding your questions and concerns regarding the Policy Proposal directly to the University Counsel at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#483d26213e2d3a3b213c31662b273d263b2d24083d2a2b662b29)\. If you submit to the University directly, we would appreciate you copying us \([\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)\)\. The University submission deadline is **Sunday, January 21, 2024**\.

Thank you\.
