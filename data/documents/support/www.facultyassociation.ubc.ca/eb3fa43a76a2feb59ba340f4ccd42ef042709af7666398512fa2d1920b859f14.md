Implementation of [Campus Vision 2050](https://planning.ubc.ca/vision) has been underway since Board approved the long\-term vision for the campus at the end of 2023, and provincial adoption of UBC’s updated Land Use Plan in 2024\. A range of university and community priorities emerged through the extensive planning and engagement process and ongoing dialogue with the university community, including housing choice and affordability, services and amenities for a growing population, and student experience and wellbeing, to name just a few\. These priorities are reflected in recent campus projects and are informing the development of more detailed plans and strategies to guide future growth\.

UBC’s Campus \+ Community Planning team has just launched a webpage that provides an update on [Campus Vision 2050 implementation](https://planning.ubc.ca/CV2050-implementation), including highlights of recent and upcoming projects and current planning work in the following areas:  

-   Supporting academic projects
-   Housing, essential services and community amenities
-   Ecology and open space
-   Transportation and mobility
-   Musqueam presence
-   Climate action

**Interested members of the UBC community are invited to learn more on the webpage and then provide comments through a webform, which is open from October 16\[th\] – November 21\[st\]:** **[https://planning\.ubc\.ca/CV2050\-implementation](https://planning.ubc.ca/CV2050-implementation)****\.**
