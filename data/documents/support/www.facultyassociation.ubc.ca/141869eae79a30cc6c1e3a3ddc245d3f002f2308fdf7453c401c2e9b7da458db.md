Posted on April 02, 2024 at 8:46 AM

This year a vote was held to elect the President, and three \(3\) Members\-at\-Large\. See our [previous communication](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/member_notice/acclaimed-executive-notification/) for a list of those positions that have already been acclaimed\. 

Congratulations to the following faculty members who have been elected to fill the roles of President and three \(3\) Members\-at\-Large\.

Elected:

-   **President**
    -   Dory Nason \(Inst\. for Critical Indigenous Studies &amp; Social Justice Inst\., UBCV\)
-   **Member\-at\-Large** \(3 positions\)
    -   Sarika Bose \(English, UBCV\)
    -   Adam Hofri\-Winogradow \(Law, UBCV\)
    -   Stephen O’Leary \(Applied Science, UBCO\)

We would like to thank all the candidates who participated in this election\. Their willingness to serve is much appreciated\. We’d also like to thank our outgoing Executive members for their service\. Thank you to the membership for your participation\.
