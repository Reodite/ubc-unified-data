## Call for Nominations

## 2022 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members

The UBCFA Nominations Committee is seeking candidates wishing to run in the upcoming Executive Committee election\. There are 9 \(nine\) 2\-year term vacancies and 1 \(one\) 1\-year term vacancy\. The nomination period will remain open for the month of February\. Work for the UBCFA Executive counts as service to the University; positions with teaching release are indicated on the nomination form\.

If you are interested in standing for election, please [download the nomination form](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/UBCFAExecElection2022_NominationForm.docx) and submit it to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#70161113051c04095e1103031f13191104191f1e300512135e13114f0305121a1513044d3e1f1d191e1104191f1e50361f021d503104041113181514) by **4:30 p\.m\., Tuesday, March 1, 2022**\. If you would like a nomination form sent to you, please [email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#88eee9ebfde4fcf1a6e9fbfbe7ebe1e9fce1e7e6c8fdeaeba6ebe9b7fbfdeae2edebfcb5daedf9fdedfbfcadbab8c6e7e5e1e6e9fce1e7e6adbab8cee7fae5)\.
