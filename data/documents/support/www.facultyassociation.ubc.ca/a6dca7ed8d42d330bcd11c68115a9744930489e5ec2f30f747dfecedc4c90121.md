# Call for Nominations

## 2022 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members

The UBCFA Nominations Committee is seeking candidates wishing to run in the upcoming Executive Committee election\. There are 9 \(nine\) 2\-year term vacancies and 1 \(one\) 1\-year term vacancy\. The nomination period will remain open for the month of February\.

If you are interested in standing for election, please [download the nomination form](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/UBCFAExecElection2022_NominationForm.docx) and submit it to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#e2848381978e969bcc8391918d818b83968b8d8ca2978081cc8183dd91978088878196dfac8d8f8b8c83968b8d8cc2a48d908fc2a3969683818a8786) by **4:30 p\.m\., Tuesday, March 1, 2022**\. If you would like a nomination form sent to you, please [email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#bfd9dedccad3cbc691deccccd0dcd6decbd6d0d1ffcadddc91dcde80cccaddd5dadccb82eddacecadacccb9a8d8ff1d0d2d6d1decbd6d0d19a8d8ff9d0cdd2)\.

**Nominations are open for:**

-   **President**
-   **Vice President**
-   **Secretary**
-   **Treasurer**
-   **Chair, Contract Faculty Committee**
-   **Chair, Librarians &amp; Archivists Committee**
-   **Chair, Member Services &amp; Grievance Committee  
    *\(1\-year term\)***
-   **3 \(three\) Members\-at\-Large**

The Election will run from Monday, March 14 to Monday, March 28, 2022, closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#36505755435a424f1857454559555f57425f595876435455185557094543545c5355420b6374757077130406734e5355130406735a5355425f595813040678595b5f5857425f5958451304060406040413040667435345425f5958) or 604\.822\.3883\.
