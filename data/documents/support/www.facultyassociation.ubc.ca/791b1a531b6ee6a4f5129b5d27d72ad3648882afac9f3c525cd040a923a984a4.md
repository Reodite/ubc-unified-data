Posted on June 25, 2026 at 11:10 AM

Faculty Relations is working with the Integrated Service Centre \(ISC\),  Human Resources and Dean’s Offices to arrange for all increases for 2025 and 2026 to be processed\.  The 2025 3% General Wage Increase \(GWI\)  and the Labour Market Salary Adjustment \(LMSA\) of 1% for EL Faculty and 1\.5% for Lecturers, will be processed at the same time as the 2026 increases \(GWI, LMSA, Career Progress, etc\)\. FR anticipates these will be processed on the August 15\[th\] paycheque\.

The 1% lump sum paid to regular members of the bargaining unit has been manually calculated and includes the 3% GWI and \(as applicable\) the LMSA for 2025, and will be paid on the June 30\[th\] paycheque\. The lump sum payment will appear under the payment plan  “Faculty – Annual Lump Sum”\. This payment is made as per [Article 6 of Part 2 of the Agreement](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Final-Collective-Agreement-2022-2025-January-31-2024.pdf#page=33) \(Salaries and Economic Benefits\)\.

We also anticipate processing the 2025 &amp; 2026 GWI and LMSA for Sessional Lecturers on the August 15\[th\] paycheque\. The lump sum for Sessional Lecturers will be paid as per usual on the September 30\[th\] paycheque\.

If you have questions regarding your 2026 Merit and PSA awards please contact your department administrator\.

Collective Agreement
