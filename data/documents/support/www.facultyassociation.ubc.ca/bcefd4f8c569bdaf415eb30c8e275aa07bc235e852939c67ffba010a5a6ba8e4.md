Tags: [pay equity](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/tag/pay-equity/)

On February 3, 2011, the Faculty Association Status of Women Committee presented a panel discussion on male/female faculty pay equity and other gender\-related issues at UBC\. [Presentations](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/swc2011_apeufw_presentations.pdf) included:

Equity: Key Issues for Women Faculty at UBC \([view video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C-J770_jHlU)\)  
Analysis of the Gender Pay Gap in Professional Salaries at UBC \([view video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vKMOh-COBzQ)\)  
Fixing Pay Inequity at UBC: Recommendations from the Structural Measure and Resolution Tactics \(SMART\) Working Group \([view video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rxynKrxRkPk)\)  
The Science of Unconscious Bias \([view video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gd1S-udvNHw)\)

For copies of the handouts, select [this link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/swc2011_apeufw_Whandouts.pdf)\.
