##### 10:30 a\.m\. to 4:00 p\.m\., Monday, December 5, 2016  
Irving K Barber Learning Centre, Yukon Room 181  
[\(Register here\)](http://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/contract-faculty-marking-party/)

Come and join your fellow contract faculty members in a quiet room where we can all concentrate on our grading as we prepare to return term papers and final assignments to our students\. You can come for the whole day, or drop in when convenient\. Although this will be a quiet room, we will be doing our work in a collegial atmosphere\. This session will also be a follow\-up from our marking session, and there will be some materials available for consultation, if needed\.

Coffee and snacks will be provided\.

To register, please visit [the CTLT registration page\.](http://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/contract-faculty-marking-party/)
