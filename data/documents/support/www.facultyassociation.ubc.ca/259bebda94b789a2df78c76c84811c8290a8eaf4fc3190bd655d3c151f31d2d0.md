Dear Members,

We are pleased to announce that the Faculty Association and the University have reached a tentative Collective Agreement for the period of July 1, 2022 – June 30, 2025\. The University and the Faculty Association will now begin our respective ratification processes\.

We would like to thank our respective negotiating teams for all their hard work in achieving the tentative settlement\.

| --- | --- |
| Dory Nason President, UBC Faculty Association | Deborah Buszard Interim President and Vice\-Chancellor of the University of British Columbia |
