Join your colleagues including Nancy Langton, your UBCFA President, for a beverage and a selection of appetizers at our Fall Faculty Reception on Friday, September 22, 2017, from 3:00 pm to 5:00 pm\.

**Location: The Ballroom, UNC 200 \(2nd Floor University Centre\)**

Please [RSVP to the UBC Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#8fe9eeecfae3fbf6a1eefcfce0ece6eefbe6e0e1cffaedeca1eceeb0dcfaede5eaecfbb2dddcd9dfafdacdccc0afc9eee3e3afc9eeecfae3fbf6afddeaeceafffbe6e0e1) by **end\-of\-day, Monday, Sept\. 18**\. We hope to see you there\!
