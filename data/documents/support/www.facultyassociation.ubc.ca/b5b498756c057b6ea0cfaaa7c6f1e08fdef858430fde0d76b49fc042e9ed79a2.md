Posted on June 09, 2022 at 4:12 PM

Our [2021 Annual Report](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/AnnualReport_2021_wCover.pdf "2021 Annual Report") is available for view with our Annual General Meeting coming up on Thursday, June 16th\.

Please find the full agenda with more background and information linked here: [Annual General Meeting Agenda](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/AGM-Agenda-June-2022-final.pdf "Annual General Meeting Agenda")\.

As with most events at the University for the past many months, our meeting will be held \(June 16th at 1\-3PM\) via Zoom\. Members wishing to join the meeting can [RSVP to the Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#83e5e2e0f6eff7faade2f0f0ece0eae2f7eaecedc3f6e1e0ade0e2bcf0f6e1e9e6e0f7bed1d0d5d3a3c2c4ce) for the meeting link which will be sent early next week\. Thanks to those who have already contacted us\. We expect to conclude the meeting by 3:00 p\.m\.

We do hope you will join us\.
