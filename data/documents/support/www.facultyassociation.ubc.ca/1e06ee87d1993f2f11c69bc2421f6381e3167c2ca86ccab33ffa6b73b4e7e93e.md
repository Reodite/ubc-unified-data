###### Seeking New Committee Members

##### UBCFA Status of Women Committee

This Committee is responsible for women faculty mentoring/networking, policy development, and advocacy on gender equity issues including pay equity, discrimination \(particularly in recruitment, retention, and promotion\), working climate, and work\-life balance\.

The Chair especially invites members who will bring an intersectional approach to equity issues, particularly identifying how race, Indigeneity, sexuality and gender diversity impact equity issues\.

If you are interested in applying for a volunteer role on this Committee, or if you would like to learn more about this opportunity, email us at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#ad8dcbccced8c1d9d483ccdedec2cec4ccd9c4c2c3edd8cfce83cecc92ded8cfc7c8ced990fbc2c1d8c3d9c8c8dfc4c3ca8dcbc2df8dd9c5c88dfed9ccd9d8de8dc2cb8dfac2c0c8c38deec2c0c0c4d9d9c8c8) or call 604\-822\-3883\.
