Posted on February 09, 2018 at 12:33 PM

The Faculty Association has become aware that faculty members have been advised that the Faculty Association has formally agreed to differential teaching loads arising out of a review of annual reports\. We are therefore advising that the Association has **not** agreed to this, nor to post\-tenure reviews\. We encourage you to [contact us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#c2a4a3a1b7aeb6bbeca3b1b1ada1aba3b6abadac82b7a0a1eca1a3fdb1b7a0a8a7a1b6ff81adaca1a7b0acb1e2b0a7e296a7a3a1aaabaca5e283b1b1aba5acafa7acb6) if you have any concerns regarding your teaching assignment\.
