## Faculty Association AGM  
2:00 pm, Thursday, April 18

In accordance with Section 8 in our Bylaws, the AGM on Thursday, April 11 was adjourned as a quorum was not met\. The meeting will reconvene on Thursday, April 18 at 2:00 pm to proceed with the review of the UBC Faculty Association’s 2018 audited financial statements, 2019 budget, and to receive the Annual Report\.

April 18 Meeting Locations:

-   UBC Vancouver – SPPH 143, 2206 East Mall
-   UBC Okanagan \(Video Conference\) – EME 2124
