## Campus Vision 2050 – Faculty Consultation \#2

Over the past year, UBC Campus \+ Community Planning \(C\+CP\) has been engaging members of the campus community and neighbourhoods on how the Vancouver campus should change to support the needs of the University, its community and its host nation, the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm\. Thanks to community input, alongside technical analysis, C\+CP now has a draft 30\-Year Vision, entitled Campus 2050, which is now being presented to members for input and refinement\.

On January 24th, the Faculty Association and C\+CP are hosting a second consultation meeting for faculty\. **Join us on Zoom on Tuesday January 24th from 4\-5PM to learn about the draft 30\-Year Vision and provide your feedback\.** Your input will shape the final Vision which will be presented to the UBC Board of Governors in March 2023\. Register for the event at [this link](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5IsdOGrqjIvGtLK60tPv4lx2uX3ItUSA_8A), and send any questions to [Madeleine Zammar \(Manager, Engagement at C\+CP\)](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f09d9194959c95999e95de8a919d9d9182b0859293de9391cf8385929a959384cdb3919d808583d0a69983999f9ed0c2c0c5c0d0b69193859c8489d0b39f9e83859c849184999f9e)\.

If you are unable to join the event but would like to learn more about the draft 30\-Year Vision and share your feedback via a survey or by joining other events, visit the [Campus Vision 2050](https://campusvision2050.ubc.ca/get-involved) website\.
