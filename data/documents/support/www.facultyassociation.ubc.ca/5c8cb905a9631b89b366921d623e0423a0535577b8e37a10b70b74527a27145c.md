Please take the opportunity to express your views on UBC’s **Proposed Amendments to Policy 3 \(Discrimination and Harassment\)**

The University is inviting comments on [Proposed Amendments to Policy 3\.](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/10-Dec18-Policy-3_Call-For-Comments.pdf) Policy 3 addresses the process by which concerns relating to matters of discrimination on the grounds protected by the BC Human Rights Code \(“Code”\) are addressed by the University\. The new Policy \#3 amendments are intended to bring the policy into alignment with current practices\. In the Call for Comments, the University is describing the proposed changes as follows:

-   changing the title of the Policy from “Discrimination and Harassment” to “Discrimination” and removing most references to “harassment” in the policy and procedures;
-   updating names of offices and positions;
-   focusing the role of the Administrative Head of Unit;
-   clarifying the jurisdiction of the Director of Investigations to investigate complaints;
-   updating the privacy and confidentiality language to create consistency with other policies; and
-   deleting the one\-year limitation period to bring a complaint

The rationale that was presented to the Board of Governors for the amendments to Policy 3 is available [at this link](https://bog3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/11/3_2018.11_Policy-3-Discrimination-and-Harassment.pdf)\.

Please review the [Proposed Amendments to Policy 3](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/10-Dec18-Policy-3_Call-For-Comments.pdf) and if you have any questions or concerns with the proposed amendments, let us know by responding to this email or [sending an email to the Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#96f0f7f5e3fae2efb8f7e5e5f9f5fff7e2fff9f8d6e3f4f5b8f5f7a9e5e3f4fcf3f5e2abd5f9fbfbf3f8e2e5b6e4f3acb6c6f9fafff5efb6a5) by **Friday, January 18, 2019** to be considered as we formulate the Association’s response\. You also have the option of forwarding your questions or concerns directly to the [Office of the University Counsel](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#03766d6a756671706a777a2d606c766d70666f437661602d60623c707661696660773e406c6e6e666d77702371663923536c6f6a607a2330) \(Deadline: February 1, 2019\)\.

If you do submit a response to the University directly, please copy us \([\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)\) so that we’re aware of your comments\.

Thank you\.
