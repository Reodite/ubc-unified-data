Posted on December 07, 2016 at 12:33 PM

###### 1:30 pm – 3:00 pm, Thursday, January 5, 2017  
The UBC Faculty Association Boardroom  
[112 Auditorium Annex B, 1924 West Mall](http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?show=y,n,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=n&amp;locat1=863-2&amp;locat2=#showMapCampus)

The UBC Faculty Association would like to invite all Lecturers to the Faculty Association Office starting at 1:30 p\.m\. on January 5, 2017 to share some issues and suggestions for the Bargaining Committee as they prepare for their formal meetings in January\.

If you would like to attend, [please RSVP](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#b7d1d6d4c2dbc3ce99d6c4c4d8d4ded6c3ded8d9f7c2d5d499d4d688c4c2d5ddd2d4c38adbd2d4c3c2c5d2c5c497d5d6c5d0d6ded9ded9d097d4d8d9c4c2dbc3d6c3ded8d997868d84879a848d8787)\. This will help us in planning\.

Participation by faculty members in the lead up to negotiations is important to us and makes our Association strong and effective\. We look forward to hearing your views\.
