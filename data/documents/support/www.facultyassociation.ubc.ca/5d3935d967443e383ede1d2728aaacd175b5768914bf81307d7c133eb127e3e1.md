October 21, 2015

[Join the Dialogue: Proposed Policy \#118 \(Safety &amp; Security Cameras\)](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/join-the-dialogue-proposed-policy-118-safety-security-cameras/)

UBC is inviting comments on proposed Policy \#118 \(Safety and Security Cameras\)\. UBC’s rationale is available on the [UBC Board of Governors’ site](http://bog3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/09/3.4_2015.09_Policy-118.pdf)\.

Please review Proposed Policy \#118 and if you have any questions or concerns, let us know by sending an email to the Faculty Association \([\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#c1a7a0a2b4adb5b8efa0b2b2aea2a8a0b5a8aeaf81b4a3a2efa2a0)\) by Monday, November 16, 2015 to be considered as we formulate the Association&amp;rsquos response\. You also have the option of forwarding your questions or concerns directly to the [Office of the University Counsel](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#fb8e95928d9e8988928f82d598948e95889e97bb8e9998d5989a) by November 30, 2015\. If you do submit a response to the University directly, please copy us \([\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)\) so that we’re aware of your comments\.

[Read more](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/join-the-dialogue-proposed-policy-118-safety-security-cameras/)
