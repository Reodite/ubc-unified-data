Dr\. Jennifer Berdahl, one of the two UBC Vancouver faculty representatives elected to the Presidential Search Committee, has resigned\. Her full resignation communication is at [http://jberdahl\.blogspot\.ca](http://jberdahl.blogspot.ca/2016/03/my-resignation-from-ubcs-presidential.html) and outlines her key reasons\.

Members may view the full text of the notice to members regarding the resignation at [this link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/enotice/enotice_MemberNotice_PSCResignation_March2016_ol.htm)
