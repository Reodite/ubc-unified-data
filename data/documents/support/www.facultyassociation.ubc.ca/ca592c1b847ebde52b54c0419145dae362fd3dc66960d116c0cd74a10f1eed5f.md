The **UBC Faculty Association Okanagan Faculty Committee \(OFC\)** is seeking new members\. The OFC provides a forum for faculty on the Okanagan Campus to raise and consider matters specific to the Campus, provide a forum for the expression of views of faculty, and bring to the attention of the Association issues of concern \(for example, academic freedom, collegial governance, new policies and procedures, and the like\)\. The Committee will work to develop local activities to build community and connection among Okanagan faculty members\. We hope you will consider joining us\.

If you are interested in membership on this Committee, or if you would like to learn more about this opportunity, [email us at \[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#3c190e0c5a5d5f49504845125d4f4f535f555d485553527c495e5f125f5d034f495e56595f48016a535049524859594e55525b1c5a534e1c4854591c737a7f)\.

Following ratification by the Executive Committee, new members to the OFC will receive an orientation\. The Committee generally meets once per month during the Fall and Winter terms\.
