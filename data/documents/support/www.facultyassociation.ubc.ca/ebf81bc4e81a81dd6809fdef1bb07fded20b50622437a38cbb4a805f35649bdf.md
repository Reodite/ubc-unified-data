**Monday, May 11, 9:00am\-11:00am &amp;  
Tuesday, May 12, 9:00am\-11:00am  
\(Registration Required\)**

If you are a faculty member coming up for tenure and/or promotion, consider attending one of these workshops\. Our speakers will guide you through all aspects of the tenure and promotion process at UBC\. You’ll leave with a better sense of how the process unfolds and your role in it\. Please join us to ask questions, discuss concerns and share experiences\.

Please note, the sessions on Monday, May 11th and Tuesday, May 12th are for faculty members going up for tenure and/or promotion\. There will be a session on Thursday, May 14th from 9:00am\-11:00am for Heads and Directors\.

**Speakers:**

-   **Dr\. Marie Tarrant**, Provost and VP Academic pro tem, UBC Okanagan
-   **Dr\. Dory Nason**, President, UBC Faculty Association
-   **Mark Trowell**, Executive Director, Faculty Relations &amp; ALDP, UBC Vancouver
-   **Dr\. Robin Jane Roff**, Associate Executive Director, UBC Faculty Association
-   **Kristin Cacchioni**, Director, Human Resources, UBC Okanagan
-   **Dr\. Siân Echard**, Professor, SAC Chair, UBC Vancouver

**Please register for your preferred session:**
