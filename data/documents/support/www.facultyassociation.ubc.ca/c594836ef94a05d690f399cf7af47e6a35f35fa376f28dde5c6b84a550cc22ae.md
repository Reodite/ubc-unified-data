Reporting to the Executive Committee, the Faculty Association has six standing committees that assist with the overall mandate of the Association\.

Committees are chaired by an elected representative, who is also a member of the Faculty Association’s Executive Committee, and comprised of other volunteers drawn from the general membership of the Association\. Below are the current Committee Chairs and a brief description of each committee’s mandate\. For more information on the work of Faculty Association committees, please contact the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#41272022342d35386f2032322e22282035282e2f013423226f2220)\. 

###### Contract Faculty Committee

Chair, Contract Faculty Committee

## Stephanie Tolman

Language &amp; World Literatures

604\.822\.2344

[\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#e7949382978f86898e82c993888b8a8689a7928584c98486)

The mandate of this Committee includes facilitating, networking, advocacy and assistance with policy development on behalf of contract faculty; formulating contract faculty priorities for collective bargaining; and, ensuring that the rights of contract faculty under the Collective Agreement are respected\. [View the Terms of Reference](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Terms-of-Ref-CFC-Update-2023.pdf) for this Committee\. 

###### Librarians and Archivists Committee

Chair, Librarians and Archivists Committee

## James Bachmann

Law Library

604\.822\.2396

The Librarians and Archivists Committee acts as a forum for ideas regarding internal library matters, and promotes and encourage means of continuing education and professional development for Librarians and Archivists\. [View the Terms of Reference](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Terms-of-Ref-LAC-Update-2023.pdf) for this Committee\. 

###### Member Services &amp; Grievance Committee

Chair, Member Services and Grievance Committee

## Susan Paterson

Koerner Library

604\.822\.5832

The Member Services and Grievance Committee is responsible for overseeing the processing and resolution of complaints and grievances, and recommending for or against arbitration proceedings\. [View the Terms of Reference](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Terms-of-Ref-MSGC-Update-2023.pdf) for this Committee\.

###### Equity Committee

Chair, Equity Committee

## Shirley Chau

Social Work

[\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#d2a1babba0beb7abfcb1bab3a792a7b0b1fcb1b3)

This Committee is responsible for identifying structural sources of ableism, bias, inequity and exclusion within the University and exploring measures to reduce and eliminate these systemic barriers to inclusion\. Members also assist the FA in networking, advocacy and promotion of social justice, equity, diversity, accessibility and inclusion within the FA, the University and the UBC Community more broadly\. [View Terms of Reference](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Terms-of-Ref-EC-2023.pdf "EC Terms of Reference") for this Committee\.

###### Okanagan Faculty Committee

Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee

## Peter Arthur

Education

[\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#4a3a2f3e2f38642b383e223f380a3f282964292b)

###### Anti\-Racism and Indigenous Initiatives Committee

Chair, Anti\-Racism and Indigenous Initiatives Committee

## Katrina Plamondon

Health &amp; Social Development

[\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#0c476d787e65626d227c606d6163626863624c796e6f226f6d)

Anti\-Racism and Indigenous Initiatives Committee: This Committee is responsible for identifying sources of structural racism, anti\-Blackness and colonialism at UBC and exploring measures to reduce and eliminate these and other forms of bias and discrimination against racialized members\. Members engage in advocacy, networking and other activities to promote and advance social justice, reconciliation and decolonization within the FA, UBC and the University Committee more broadly\. [View Terms of Reference](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/ToR-ARIIC-2023.pdf "Terms of Reference for the ARIIC") for this committee\.
