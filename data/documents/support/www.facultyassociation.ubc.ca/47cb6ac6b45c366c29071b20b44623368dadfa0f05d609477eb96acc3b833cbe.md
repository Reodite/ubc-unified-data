**Please take the opportunity to express your views on UBC’s Proposed Amendments to Policy 93 – Closed Meetings of the Board of Governors**

The University is inviting comments on [Proposed Amendments to Policy 93](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/Policy93_Call-For-Comments.pdf) regarding Closed Meetings of the Board of Governors\. The rationale that was presented to the Board of Governors for the amendments to Policy 93 is available [at this link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/Policy93-4_2018.09_Open-Closed-and-In-Camera-Meetings-of-the-BoG.pdf)\.

Please review the [Proposed Amendments to Policy 93](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/Policy93_Call-For-Comments.pdf) and if you have any questions or concerns with the proposed amendments, let us know by responding to this email or [sending an email to the Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#33555250465f474a1d5240405c505a52475a5c5d734651501d50520c404651595650470e635c5f5a504a130a00) by **Wednesday, October 31, 2018** to be considered as we formulate the Association’s response\. You also have the option of forwarding your questions or concerns directly to the [Office of the University Counsel](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#01746f68776473726875782f626e746f72646d417463622f62603e7274636b6462753c516e6d686278213832) \(Deadline: November 5, 2018\)\.

If you do submit a response to the University directly, please copy us \([\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)\) so that we’re aware of your comments\.

Thank you\.
