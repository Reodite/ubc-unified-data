October 30, 2019

[Alternatives to Student Evaluations of Teaching](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/bargaining/alternatives-evaluations-teaching/)

On the matter of student evaluations of teaching \(SEoT\), our position is clear: we propose that these measures not be used in the summative evaluation of teaching for appointment, reappointment, promotion, and tenure\. The invalidity of these instruments has been known for a long time; the evidence that they are also biased against protected classes of people increases seemingly weekly\.

[Read more](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/bargaining/alternatives-evaluations-teaching/)

October 25, 2019

[The Need for Rational Job Titles in the Educational Leadership Stream](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/bargaining/rational-educational-leadership/)

We are all familiar with the titles associated with the research professoriate: Assistant, Associate, and full Professor\. UBC’s “Educational Leadership” stream has titles that are quite a bit less tidy\. In 2010 we entitled the final rank in the then\-new Educational Leadership stream “Professor of Teaching,” but the first and second ranks in that stream don’t have matching titles\. We’ve been trying to fix that\.

[Read more](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/bargaining/rational-educational-leadership/)

October 23, 2019

[Library Workload and the Confirmation Process](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/bargaining/library-workload-confirmation/)

The Association continues to seek to modify [Part 5, Conditions of Appointment for Librarians](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/Faculty-CA-2016-to-2019_V_6July2018.pdf#page=87) to ensure that workload is collegially assigned in a fair and equitable manner, and to create Heads language for the Library that mirrors Heads language for faculty \(i\.e\., term appointments, stipend, administrative leave\)\. In addition, in this round we have proposed a change in the confirmation review process and introduced definitions upon which candidates for confirmed appointments will be judged\.

[Read more](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/bargaining/library-workload-confirmation/)

June 18, 2019

[Bargaining Update: Lecturers](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/bargaining/lecturers-2/)

Lecturers are the fastest\-growing cohort of faculty at UBC, but their terms of work have not kept pace with their increasing presence on our campuses\. Lecturers are designed to be a teaching stream with teaching and service as their assigned responsibilities; they are full\-time salaried faculty members with one\- to three\-year appointments and a presumptive right of reappointment as long as the work is available\. But many other elements of their work\-lives at UBC need attention\.
