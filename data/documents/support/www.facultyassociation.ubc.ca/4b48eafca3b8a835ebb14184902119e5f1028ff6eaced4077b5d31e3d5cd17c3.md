##### 2025 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members

The Nominations Committee is seeking candidates wishing to run in the upcoming Executive Committee election\. There are 6 \(six\) 2\-year term vacancies and 1 \(one\) 1\-year term vacancy\. The nomination period will remain open for the month of February\.

If you are interested in standing for election, please [download the nomination form](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/ExecElection2025_NominationForm_Final.pdf) and submit it to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#20464143554c54590e4153534f43494154494f4e605542430e43411f5355424a4543541d6e4f4d494e4154494f4e051210664f524d006154544143484544) by **4:30PM, Saturday, March 1st, 2025**\. If you would like a nominations form sent to you, please [email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#294f484a5c455d5007485a5a464a40485d404647695c4b4a074a48165a5c4b434c4a5d147b4c585c4c5a5d40474e0c1b1948096746444047485d404647096f465b44)\.

 **Nominations are open for:** 

-   **Treasurer, Executive Committee** \(1\-year term\)  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date:* Lea Caragata \(Social Work, UBCV\)
-   **Chair, Equity Committee  
    ***Nominee\(s\) to date:* Shirley Chau \(Social Work, UBCO\)
-   **Chair, Membership Services &amp; Grievances Committee  
    ***Nominee\(s\) to date:* Timothy Taylor \(School of Creative Writing, UBCV\)
-   **Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee  
    ***Nominee\(s\) to date:* Peter Arthur \(Education, UBCO\)
-   **3 \(three\) Members\-at\-Large  
    ***Nominee\(s\) to date:*
    
    -   Jacquelyn Cragg \(Pharmaceutical Sciences, UBCV\)
    -   Mohamed Gebril \(Oral Health Sciences, UBCV\)
    -   Karen Pinder \(Cellular and Physiological Sciences, UBCV\)
    -   Susan Paterson \(Library, UBCV\)
    -   Ben Britton \(Materials Engineering, UBCV\)

*All chair positions will receive a one\-half teaching release\.*

The Election will run from Wednesday, March 12 to Wednesday, March 26, 2025, closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#751314160019010c5b1406061a161c14011c1a1b350017165b16144a0600171f101601482037363334504745300d10165530191016011c1a1b553b1a181c1b14011c1a1b0655474547405524001006011c1a1b)\.

*Members of the 2025 Nominations Committee: Dory Nason \(President\), Alan Richardson \(Past President\), Ray Taheri \(Applied Science, UBCO\), David Gaertner \(CIS/English, UBCV\), Elisa Baniassad \(Science, UBCV\), Deena Rubuliak \(ex officio\)\.*
