The hiring process at UBC is bound by the [Registrar/Librarian Appointment Policy \(AP11\)](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/registrarlibrarians-appointment-policy), which stipulates that except in extraordinary situations all UBC positions are filled only after an open competition and review by a formally constituted hiring body\.

## Librarian Initial Appointment

There are four different ways a librarian can be appointed that differ based on rank and whether they are ongoing in nature\. These are: \(a\) probationary appointments \(confirmation\-track\); \(b\) appointments without review \(limited term\); \(c\) internal appointments \(candidate must already hold a UBC position as a professional librarian\); and \(d\) confirmed appointments\.

Appointments are made by the President after a recommendation to the University Librarian is made by a Selection Committee of peers and the unit’s Head\.

More information about the process is available on the [Library Confirmation](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/worklife/librarian-confirmation-process/) page\.

## Salary and Benefits

There is a [minimum salary scale](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Final-Collective-Agreement-2022-2025-January-31-2024.pdf#page=39) for Librarians and Archivists that sets the floor for salaries, but not the ceiling – it is possible to be paid higher than the minimum salary\. More information about the benefits associated with your rank can be found on our [Benefits page\.](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/worklife/benefits/)

## Librarian Reappointment

Your initial appointment at UBC will be for three years\. Typically, by the end of your second year of continuous service a recommendation will be made to either grant or deny you a confirmed appointment, analogous to achieving tenure\. The [Collective Agreement](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Final-Collective-Agreement-2022-2025-January-31-2024.pdf#page=103) provides specifics and a general overview is available on the [Library Confirmation](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/worklife/librarian-confirmation-process/) page\.
