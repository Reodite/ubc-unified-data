## [Watch Last Week’s Webcast Online](http://mediasitemob1.mediagroup.ubc.ca/Mediasite/Play/b1c4b9973c2d40d5a6346b89b4c71b461d)

This forum took place on Thursday, November 24th\. The link to the recorded webcast is also on **[our homepage\.](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/)**

### Voting

Voting is currently taking place now and runs until Monday, December 5th\. For links to the candidates’ statements and information on voting, visit: **[https://facultystaff\.students\.ubc\.ca/vancouver\-faculty\-representatives\-board\-governors\.](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/vancouver-faculty-representatives-board-governors)**

We encourage all faculty to vote in this important election for our faculty representatives on the Board of Governors\.

### UBCFA Online Forum – Registration

Members are invited to continue discussions on the UBCFA forum site [\(https://ubcfa\.wpengine\.com/ubcfaforum\)\.](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/ubcfaforum/) Access to forums is available to faculty members only and you MUST register to participate\. Please email the Faculty Association at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#2a4c4b495f465e53044b59594549434b5e4345446a5f484904494b0515595f48404f495e175f48494c4b0a4c45585f470a584f4d43595e584b5e434544) to receive your username and password\. **Registration hours: 8:30 am – 4:30 pm, Monday – Thursday; 8:30 am – 4:00 pm on Fridays**\. Note: Faculty members who have registered for previous online forums are still registered but if you have forgotten your password, please [email the Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#b3d5d2d0c6dfc7ca9dd2c0c0dcd0dad2c7dadcddf3c6d1d09dd0d29c8cc0c6d1d9d6d0c78ed5dcc1d4dcc793d5dcc1c6de93c3d2c0c0c4dcc1d7)\.
