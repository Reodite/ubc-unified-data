**2019 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members and UBCFA Okanagan Faculty Committee Member**

The following faculty members have been elected to fill the roles of Chair of the Member Services and Grievance Committee, Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee, three Members\-at\-Large and the non\-executive position of 2nd Vice Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee\. Their 2\-year terms will begin July 1, 2019\.

**ELECTED:**

-   Chair, Member Services &amp; Grievance Committee:  
    **Kenny Kwok** \(Cellular &amp; Physiological Sciences, UBCV\)
-   Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee \(Okanagan\):  
    **Gino DiLabio** \(Chemistry, Management, UBCO\)
-   Members\-at\-Large \(3 Positions\):  
    **Jim Johnson** \(Economics, UBCO\)  
    **Siobhán McPhee** \(Geography, UBCV\)  
    **Miriam Spering** \(Ophthalmology &amp; Visual Sciences, UBCV\)

Non\-executive position \(UBC Okanagan\):

-   2nd Vice\-Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee:  
    **Shirley Chau** \(Social Work, UBCO\)

The complete results for this Election are [available at this link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/ExecutiveElection2019-Results.pdf)\.

We would like to thank all the candidates who participated in this election\. Their willingness to serve is much appreciated\.
