**2026 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members**

The UBCFA Nominations Committee is seeking candidates wishing to run in the upcoming Executive Committee election\. There are 10 \(ten\) 2\-year term vacancies\. The nomination period will remain open for the month of February\.

If you are interested in standing for election, please [download the nomination form](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/UBCFAExecElection2026_NominationForm.pdf) and submit it to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#95f3f4f6e0f9e1ecbbf4e6e6faf6fcf4e1fcfafbd5e0f7f6bbf6f4aae6e0f7fff0f6e1a8dbfaf8fcfbf4e1fcfafbb0a7a5d3fae7f8b5d4e1e1f4f6fdf0f1) by **4:30 p\.m\., Sunday, March 1, 2026**\. If you would like a nomination form sent to you, please [email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#d1b7b0b2a4bda5a8ffb0a2a2beb2b8b0a5b8bebf91a4b3b2ffb2b0eea2a4b3bbb4b2a5ec83b4a0a4b4a2a5f4e3e19fbebcb8bfb0a5b8bebff197bea3bc)\.

**Nominations are open for:**

        -   Timothy Taylor \(Creative Writing, UBCV\)
-   **Vice President**

-   **Chair, Librarians &amp; Archivists Committee**
-   **Chair, Anti\-Racism &amp; Indigenous Initiatives**
    -   *Nominee\(s\) to date:*
        -   Judith Paltin \(English, UBCV\)
-   **Chair, Contract Faculty Committee**
    -   *Nominee\(s\) to date:*

-   **Member\-at\-Large \(3 Positions\)**

*\*Candidates for this position must have prior service on the Executive Committee\.*

The Election will run from Monday, March 16 to Monday, March 30, 2026, closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#4d2b2c2e38213934632c3e3e222e242c392422230d382f2e632e2c723e382f27282e3970180f0e0b0c687f7d0835282e6d0821282e392422236d03222024232c392422233e6d3c38283e39242223)\.
