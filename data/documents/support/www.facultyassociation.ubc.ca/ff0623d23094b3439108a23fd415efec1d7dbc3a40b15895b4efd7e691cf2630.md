### Chair, Member Services and Grievance Committee \(MSGC\)

The UBCFA Nominations Committee is seeking candidates for the position of Chair, Member Services and Grievance Committee\* to fill a one\-year vacancy, effective July 1st\. The nomination period will remain open to June 2nd\.

*\*Please note: Candidates for this position must have prior service on the Member Services and Grievance Committee*

If you are interested in standing for election, please [download the nomination form](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/MSGCByElection2026_NominationForm.pdf) and submit it to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#3c5a5d5f49504845125d4f4f535f555d485553527c495e5f125f5d034f495e56595f480172535155525d48555352190e0c7a534e511c7d48485d5f545958) by **4:30 p\.m\., Tuesday, June 2, 2026**\. If you would like a nomination form sent to you directly, please [email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#6a0c0b091f061e13440b19190509030b1e0305042a1f080944090b55191f08000f091e57380f1b1f0f191e4f585a24050703040b1e0305044f585a2c051807)

***Candidates to date:***

-   Susan Paterson \(Koerner Library\)

The By\-Election will run from Monday, June 8 to Monday, June 22, 2026, closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will receive a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the position or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#690f080a1c051d1047081a1a060a00081d000607291c0b0a470a08561a1c0b030c0a1d543c2b2a2f284c5b59243a2e2a4c5b592b10442c050c0a1d0006074c5b592706040007081d0006074c5b595b595b5f4c5b59381c0c1a1d000607)

*Members of the 2026 UBCFA Election Nominations Committee: Dory Nason \(President\), Alan Richardson \(past President\), William Bowman \(Computer Science\), Helen Yanacopolous \(Economics, UBCO\), Elisa Baniassad \(Academic Director LTIC\), and Deena Rubuliak \(ex\-officio\)\.*
