## Welcome Back\!

A new term is beginning and once again we’d like to invite you to UBCFA’s annual Fall Reception for our Okanagan Faculty members\. Join your colleagues along with FA President Bronwen Sprout and Vice President Alan Richardson for a beverage and a selection of appetizers from **3 pm to 5 pm, THURSDAY, SEPT\. 20\.**

Location: **The Ballroom, UNC 200 \(2nd Floor University Centre\)**

We hope to see you there\!
