###### 2018 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members

The Nominations Committee of the UBC Faculty Association is seeking candidates for the upcoming election of Executive Committee members: there are nine vacancies\. Nominations must be received by the UBC Faculty Association no later than **11:59 p\.m\., Thursday, March 1, 2018\.** The Election will run from Monday, March 19 to Thursday, April 5, 2018 closing at the start of the 2018 UBCFA Annual General Meeting\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the executive positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#b5d3d4d6c0d9c1cc9bd4c6c6dad6dcd4c1dcdadbf5c0d7d69bd6d48ac6c0d7dfd0d6c188e0f7f6f3f4908785e4c0d0c6c1dcdadbc6908785f0cdd0d6908785f6dad8d8dcc1c1d0d0908785fbdad8dcdbd4c1dcdadbc69087858785848d) or 604\.822\.3883\.

**There are nine vacancies \(two\-year terms\):**

-   **President**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Bronwen Sprout \(UBCV, Library\)*
-   **Vice President**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Alan Richardson \(UBCV, Philosophy\)*
-   **Treasurer**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Doris Doudet \(UBCV, Neurology\)*
-   **Secretary**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Karen Smith \(UBCV, Microbiology &amp; Immunology\)*
-   **Chair, Contract Faculty Committee**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Sarika Bose \(UBCV, English\)*
-   **Chair, Librarians &amp; Archivists Committee**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Alan Doyle \(UBCV, Library\)*
-   **Members\-at\-Large \(3 Positions\)**  
    *Nominee\(s\) to date: Shirley Chau \(UBCO, Social Work\), Meghan Corella \(UBCV, Education\), Amin Ghaziani \(UBCV, Sociology\), Vesna Sossi \(UBCV, Physics and Astronomy\), and Xiaowen Xu \(UBCV, Asian Studies\)*

If you are interested in standing for election, please complete the nomination form [available on our website](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/UBCFAExecElection2018_NominationForm.docx) and submit it to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#8ceaedeff9e0f8f5a2edffffe3efe5edf8e5e3e2ccf9eeefa2efedb3fff9eee6e9eff8b1c2e3e1e5e2edf8e5e3e2accae3fee1accdf8f8edefe4e9e8) by 11:59 p\.m\. on March 1, 2018\. If you would like a nomination form sent to you, please [email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#2c4a4d4f59405855024d5f5f434f454d584543426c594e4f024f4d135f594e46494f58117e495d59495f58091e1c62434145424d58454342091e1c6a435e41)\.

*Members of the 2018 UBCFA Executive Election Nominations Committee: Mark Mac Lean, Past\-President \(Mathematics\), Stephen O’Leary \(Engineering, UBC Okanagan\), Anthony Paré \(Head, LLED\), Sunera Thobani \(Asian Studies, GRSJ\) and Deena Rubuliak \(Executive Director/Ex\-Officio\)\.*
