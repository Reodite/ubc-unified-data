### The Sessional Conference Fund Travel Grants

**The UBC Faculty Association and UBC are pleased to announce travel grants for sessional faculty who will be presenting papers at academic conferences in 2017\.**

Depending on demand, each grant will have a maximum value of up to $1500 and may be used for travel, accommodation, and conference fees\. You may claim expenses for presenting at a conference you attend in 2017 while you are on contract at UBC, or for a conference while you are on leave so long as you return to UBC later in 2017\. \(For example, you may claim expenses for a conference you attend during the summer so long as you are teaching at UBC in the fall\.\)

If you anticipate that you will be delivering a conference paper this year and would like to apply for one of these grants, please fill in the Application Form available [at this link](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/faculty-relations/compensation/professional-development-reimbursement-fund/sessional-conference-fund/)\) and forward it to the UBC Faculty Association at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#8debeceef8e1f9f4a3ecfefee2eee4ecf9e4e2e3cdf8efeea3eeecb2def8efe7e8eef9b0ccfdfde1e4eeecf9e4e2e3adebe2ffaddee8fefee4e2e3ece1add9ffecfbe8e1adcaffece3f9adbfbdbcba) **by Friday, March 24, 2017 \(Revised submission date\)\.** Successful applicants will be notified\.

For more information about the Sessional Conference Fund and Travel Grant, please follow [this link\.](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/faculty-relations/compensation/professional-development-reimbursement-fund/sessional-conference-fund/)
