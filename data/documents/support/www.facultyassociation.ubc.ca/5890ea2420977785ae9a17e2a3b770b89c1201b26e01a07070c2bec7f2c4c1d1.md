Posted on March 22, 2017 at 4:06 PM

The UBCFA Executive Election is currently taking place\. Members should have received an earlier notice from the Association, followed by an invitation to the voting site\.

During the Election period, members are invited to connect with candidates and colleagues on the UBCFA Executive Election 2017 online forum which is now open\. The online forum is located at [https://ubcfa\.wpengine\.com/ubcfaforum](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/ubcfaforum)\. **Access to forums is available to faculty members only and you MUST register to participate\.** Please email the Faculty Association at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f99f989a8c958d80d7988a8a969a90988d909697b98c9b9ad79a98c68a8c9b939c9a8dc48c9b9a9f98d99f968b8c94d98b9c9e908a8d8b988d909697) to register and receive your username and password\. If you have previously registered for any of our online forums and still have your user name and password, you can log in at any time\. **Registration hours: 8:30 am – 4:00 pm, Monday – Friday**\. Note: Faculty members who have forgotten their login credentials may [email the Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#aec8cfcddbc2dad780cfddddc1cdc7cfdac7c1c0eedbcccd80cdcf91dddbccc4cbcdda93c8c1dcc9c1da8ec8c1dcdbc38ec2c1c9c7c08ecddccbcacbc0dac7cfc2dd) to have them forwarded\.
