**Updated Application Deadline: April 30, 2019  
Applications received after this date will not be accepted\.  
**

**The UBC Faculty Association and UBC are pleased to announce travel grants for sessional faculty who will be presenting papers at academic conferences in 2019\.**

Depending on demand, this year’s grant will have a maximum value of up to $1800\.00 and may be used for travel, accommodation, conference fees and meals \(UBC per diem\)\. You may claim expenses for presenting at a conference you attend in 2019 while you are on contract at UBC, or for a conference while you are on leave so long as you return to UBC later in 2019\. \(For example, you may claim expenses for a conference you present at during the summer when you return to teaching at UBC again during the 2019 winter term\.\)

If you anticipate that you will be delivering a conference paper this year and would like to apply for one of these grants, please complete the Application Form available on [the UBCFA website](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/SessionalConferenceFund_ApplicationForm-2019.docx) and on [the Faculty Relations website](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/faculty-relations/compensation/professional-development-reimbursement-fund/sessional-conference-fund/) and forward it to the UBC Faculty Association at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#244245475148505d0a4557574b474d45504d4b4a645146470a47451b7751464e41475019655454484d4745504d4b4a04424b5604774157574d4b4a454804705645524148046356454a50041614151d) **by April 30, 2019\.** **Applications made after this date will not be accepted\.** Successful applicants will be notified\.

For more information about the Sessional Conference Fund and Travel Grant, please visit [the Sessional Conference Fund web page](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/faculty-relations/compensation/professional-development-reimbursement-fund/sessional-conference-fund/)\.
