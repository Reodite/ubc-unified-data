### Proposal for new Policy V\-130: Requirements for the Production, Distribution, and Content of Course Syllabi

The Senate Academic Policy Committee has drafted a new policy on the requirements for course syllabi\. It has become apparent to the FA that not all faculty have received a copy of the draft policy\.

Please [visit this link](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/UBC_Proposal-for-new-Policy-V-130.pdf) to see the draft for your information\. We encourage you to send your feedback as outlined in the draft policy\.
