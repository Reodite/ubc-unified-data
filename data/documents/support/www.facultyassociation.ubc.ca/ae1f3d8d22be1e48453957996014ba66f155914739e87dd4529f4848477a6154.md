##### 12:30 pm, Tuesday, November 8, 2016 [\(Registration required\)](http://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/are-we-getting-an-a-on-our-own-grading-strategies/)  
Irving K Barber Learning Centre, Seminar \(Room 2\.22 A/B\)

---

Title: Are We Getting an A\+ on Our Own Grading Strategies?

Come share some grading strategies with your fellow contract faculty members\. Bring your questions, rubrics and strategies for grading, and we will work together as a community to come up with effective and efficient ideas to help our students learn from our feedback\.

Facilitators: **Sarika Bose and Judy Chan**

To register, please visit [the CTLT registration page\.](http://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/are-we-getting-an-a-on-our-own-grading-strategies/)

---

For more information as well as this year’s schedule of Community of Practice sessions, please visit the Contract Faculty Community of Practice \(CFCoP\) page on the CTLT website\.
