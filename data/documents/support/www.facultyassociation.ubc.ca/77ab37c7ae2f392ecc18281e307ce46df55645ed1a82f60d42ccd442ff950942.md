### A three\-part lecture series for UBC Faculty Association Members

Join us for this year’s lecture series designed to help you navigate the complexities of financial planning as well as prepare for retirement\.

**Time: 12:40 p\.m\. to 1:25 p\.m\.  
Location: Lecture Hall 1, Woodward IRC  
[2194 Health Sciences Mall](http://maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?show=y,n,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=&amp;locat1=473)**

---

### Tuesday, February 13 –  
Cross\-Border Tax Considerations for Americans in Canada

*Presenter: Kaman Kwok CPA, CGA, CPA*

### Tuesday, February 27 –  
Real Estate and Your Financial Plan

*Presenter: Tom Davidoff, Associate Professor, Sauder School of Business*

### Tuesday, March 6 –  
Investment Market Update – UBC Faculty Pension Plan

*Presenters: Mike Leslie, Executive Director – Investments, Faculty Pension Plan; Neil Watson, Vice President, Senior Partner, and Portfolio Manager, Leith Wheeler Investment Counsel Ltd\.*

##### For more details and information on this year’s lecture series, [check out the full announcement on our Programs and Events page\.](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/services-and-assistance/programs-and-events/#workshops)
