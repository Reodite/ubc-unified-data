### Thursday, October 30th – [**RSVP Required**](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#157374766079616c3b7466667a767c74617c7a7b556077763b76742a6660777f707661284746434535535258)

**Date and Time: Thursday, October 30th starting at 1:00pm\.  
Via Zoom \(Link will be sent out Oct\. 30th\)\.**

The UBCFA Constitution requires that a General Meeting be held every fall term\. The first order of business will be to reappoint Achieve CPAs, LLP as auditors for the 2025 fiscal year\. Following the business portion of the meeting, the chair of our bargaining committee, Dr\. Elizabeth Hodgson will do a presentation on bargaining which is currently ongoing\. Please [**follow this link for the Fall General Meeting Agenda**](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/FGM-Agenda-October-2025.pdf)\. Our meeting will be held via zoom to facilitate participation from members on both our campuses\. The minutes of the 2025 AGM are available [at this link\.](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/AGM-Agenda-Minutes-May-2025.pdf)

This meeting is restricted to bargaining unit members\. To ensure the integrity of the meeting and the privacy of participants, please [**RSVP to the Faculty Association**](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#452324263029313c6b2436362a262c24312c2a2b053027266b26247a3630272f202631781716131565030208) so that we know to expect you\. You will receive a link to the meeting the morning of October 30th\. We ask that you do not share this link with others\.

The chat will be enabled for communication between the hosts and participants\. If you have any concerns with your ability to participate in the discussions, please let us know and we will work with you to arrange a suitable accommodation\. You may also email your question or comment to the Faculty Association for review after the meeting\. Please note that emails will not form part of the official meeting minutes\.

We look forward to seeing you at the meeting\.

**Dory Nason,**

**President**
