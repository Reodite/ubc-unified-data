##### Tenure clock extension for new tenure\-track Faculty members hired between April 7, 2020 and December 31, 2020 &amp; confirmation\-track Librarians and Archivists hired between April 7, 2020 and June 30, 2021

The University and the Faculty Association have agreed to provide a one\-year tenure clock extension to tenure\-track faculty members hired between April 7, 2020 and December 31, 2020 and a one\-year extension to Librarians and Archivists confirmation\-track period who were hired between April 7, 2020 and June 30, 2021\. This agreement aligns with the previous April 7, 2020 tenure\-clock and confirmation extension agreements owing to the COVID pandemic\.

For faculty members, the extension will be applied to both reappointment and tenure review schedules\. If a faculty member prefers not to have their reappointment timeline adjusted, they may elect to have the 1\-year extension added to the end of their tenure review cycle\. Those choosing this option must notify their Head in writing by **February 25, 2022**\.

Similarly, if a faculty member, librarian or archivist prefers to not avail themselves of the 1\-year tenure clock/confirmation track extension, they must notify their Head in writing by **February 25, 2022\.**
