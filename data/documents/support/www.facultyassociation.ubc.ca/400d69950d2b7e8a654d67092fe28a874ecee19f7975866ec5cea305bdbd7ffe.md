October 15, 2018

[Background to the proposed changes to the UBCFA Constitution and Bylaws](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/background-proposed-constitution/)

The Executive Committee of the UBC Faculty Association will be presenting an updated Constitution and Bylaws at the Fall General Meeting on October 30, 2018\. The changes to the Constitution and Bylaws were driven by a legislative change to the Society Act, now called the *Societies Act*, SBC 2015, c\. 18 \(the “Act”\) that came into force on November 28, 2016\. The Faculty Association must transition to the new *Act* no later than November 28, 2018\. The Faculty Association has therefore updated our Constitution and  Bylaws to ensure we are in compliance with the new requirements under the Act\.

[Read more](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/background-proposed-constitution/)

June 11, 2018

[Faculty Association Statement on Steven Galloway Arbitration](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/association-statement-arbitration/)

As the exclusive bargaining agent for Faculty, Librarians &amp; Archivists, and Program Directors, the UBC Faculty Association has a duty to fairly represent members in matters arising out of their employment with the University\. Our fundamental role in this regard is to ensure that all members receive fair and due process and the protection of their rights in any proceedings conducted by the University\.

[Read more](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/association-statement-arbitration/)
