Dear Colleagues,

It’s time\! As we prepare for the upcoming round of collective bargaining, we need to hear from you about your issues, priorities, and concerns\. Below is a list of Zoom Consultation Sessions geared to specific constituencies that we’ll be holding over the fall on Mondays between noon and 2 p\.m\. Please RSVP to the session that best fits you and your schedule and we’ll send you the Zoom link\. Your experiences are crucial to how and what we bargain, so please join in\.

**Sessions:**

-   **Equity\-seeking Members** \(BIPOC, gender diverse, neuro diverse, disabled, women\): [Monday, Oct\. 4, 12 pm – 1 pm; **RSVP**](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#2b4d4a485e475f52054a58584448424a5f4244456b5e494805484a14585e49414e485f166e5a5e425f520b684445585e475f4a5f4244450b64485f050b1f070b1a19061a)
