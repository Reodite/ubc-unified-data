June 10, 2015

[History Behind Annual “Lump\-sum Payment,” Part II](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/bargaining/history-annual-payment/)

In our [previous blog](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/category/professional-development-funds/History%20Behind%20Annual%20%E2%80%9CLump-sum%20Payment,%E2%80%9D%20Part%20I) we discussed how contingent productivity payments instituted in the 2004/06 bargaining round disappeared in the 2010/12 bargaining round\. There were two such payments, one predicated on fundraising, which was converted into the annual lump\-sum payment, and one predicated on Tri\-Council research grants\. The question remains, what happened to the contingent “research grant” productivity payments? These were payments, equal to 1% of “regular salary” that would be made in a lump sum at the end of the contract year, contingent on Tri\-Council grant funding exceeding the preceding year’s by a certain amount\.

[Professional Development Funds](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/bargaining/professional-development-funds/)

In every bargaining round the benefit issue on which we get the most comments from members in person and in our survey is the Professional Development Fund\. No wonder\. Our PD is the lowest in British Columbia, and among the lowest in Canada\.
