# Call for Nominations

## 2023 Election of UBCFA Executive Committee Members

The UBCFA Nominations Committee is seeking candidates wishing to run in the upcoming Executive Committee election\. There are **6 \(six\) 2\-year term vacancies and 1 \(one\) 1\-year term vacancy**\. The nomination period will remain open for the month of February\.

If you are interested in standing for election, please [download the nomination form](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/ExecElection2023_NominationForm_wNC.pdf) and submit it to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f7919694829b838ed996848498949e96839e9899b7829594d99496c88482959d929483cab9989a9e9996839e9899d7b198859ad7b6838396949f9293) by **4:30 p\.m\., Wednesday, March 1, 2022**\. If you would like a nomination form sent to you, please [email us](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#c9afa8aabca5bdb0e7a8babaa6aaa0a8bda0a6a789bcabaae7aaa8f6babcaba3acaabdf49bacb8bcacbabda0a7aee9a8e987a6a4a0a7a8bda0a6a7e98fa6bba4)\.

**Nominations are open for:**

-   **Chair, Equity Committee**
-   **Chair, Member Services &amp; Grievances Committee**
-   **Chair, Anti\-Racist and Indigenous Initiatives Committee**  
    \(1\-year term Vacancy\)
-   **Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee**
-   **3 \(three\) Members\-at\-Large**

*All Chair positions will be granted a one\-half teaching release\.*

The Election will run from Wednesday, March 15 to Wednesday, March 29, 2022, closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please contact the Nominations Committee at [\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#3a5c5b594f564e43145b49495559535b4e5355547a4f585914595b05494f58505f594e076f78797c7b1f080a7f425f591f080a7f565f594e5355541f080a74555753545b4e535554491f080a080a08081f080a6b4f5f494e535554)\.
