Dear Colleagues,

Your UBCFA Executive Committee members volunteer their time to help guide the Faculty Association’s operational and policy decisions including advocacy, negotiation, education, communication and planning decisions\. This year, a vote is being held to elect the Chair of the Member Services and Grievance Committee as well as three Members\-at\-Large for 2\-year terms\. Members on the Okanagan Campus will also be voting for the Chair of the Okanagan Faculty Committee and one of two non\-executive positions, 2nd Vice\-Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee, also for 2\-year terms\. The nomination period closed Friday, March 1st at 4:30 p\.m\. More information on the candidates and the election will follow soon\.

**We would like to congratulate the following nominees who have been elected by acclamation:**

-   **Chair, Status of Women Committee: Dory Nason** \(Institute for Critical Indigenous Studies, GRSJ – The Social Justice Institute, UBCV\)
-   **1st Vice\-Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee: Allison McManus** \(School of Health &amp; Exercise Science, UBCO\)

**Nominees standing for election:**

-   **Chair, Member Services &amp; Grievance Committee**  
    *Nominees:*  
    **Arjun Bhardwaj** \(Management, UBCO\)  
    **Kenny Kwok** \(Cellular &amp; Physiological Sciences, UBCV\)
    
-   **Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee** *\(to be voted on by members at our Okanagan Campus only\)*  
    *Nominees:*  
    **Gino DiLabio** \(Chemistry, Management, UBCO\)  
    **Peter Wylie** \(Economics, Philosophy, Political Science, UBCO\)
    
-   **Members\-at\-Large \(3 Positions\)**  
    *Nominees:*  
    **Robert Campbell** \(Education, UBCO\)  
    **James Johnson** \(Economics, UBCO\)  
    **Annamma Joy** \(Management, UBCO\)  
    **Ryuko Kubota** \(Language and Literacy Education, UBCV\)  
    **Siobhán McPhee** \(Geography, UBCV\)  
    **Miriam Spering** \(Ophthalmology &amp; Visual Sciences, UBCV\)

Non\-executive position to be voted on by members at our Okanagan Campus only:

-   **2nd Vice\-Chair, Okanagan Faculty Committee**  
    *Nominees:*  
    **Shirley Chau** \(Social Work, UBCO\)  
    **Craig Nichol** \(Earth, Environmental and Geographic Sciences, UBCO\)

---

The Election will run from Monday, March 18 to Thursday, April 4, 2019 closing at 4:30 p\.m\. Members will be receiving a unique email link to vote online\. If you have any questions about the positions or the election, please [email the Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#7a1c1b190f160e03541b09091519131b0e1315143a0f181954191b45090f18101f190e472f38393c3b5f484a3f021f195f484a3f161f190e1315145f484a484a4b435f484a2b0f1f090e131514) or call 604\.822\.3883\.
