Tags: [Academic Freedom](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/tag/academic-freedom/), [freedom of expression](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/tag/freedom-of-expression/), [join the dialogue](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/tag/join-the-dialogue/)

Dear Colleagues,

Yesterday you were asked by President Ono to provide comments on the draft [Freedom of Expression statement](https://academic.ubc.ca/freedom-of-expression-statement)\. We ask you to provide the Association with your views as well\. This will help guide a Faculty Association response, an important part of the feedback process on this statement\.

Please send us your input by replying to this email or sending an email to the [Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#5b3d3a382e372f22753a28283438323a2f3234351b2e393875383a64082e39313e382f661d293e3e3f34367e696b343d7e696b1e232b293e28283234357e696b183436363e352f28), by Wednesday, November 15\. If you [share your views](https://academic.ubc.ca/freedom-of-expression-statement#shareyourviews) directly to the University, we would appreciate a copy\.

Your voice matters\!

Many thanks\.

Best,  
Nancy

Nancy Langton  
President, UBCFA
