Please take the opportunity to express your views on UBC’s **Proposed Policy 83 \(Travel and Other Business\-Related Expenses\)**

The University is inviting comments on [Proposed Policy 83](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/20Dec18-Policy-83_Call-For-Comments.pdf), stating that the new policy will consolidate a number of narrower policies into one, providing an integrated, principle\-based framework to govern use of UBC funds in connection with business\-related expenses\. They also say that the new Policy will allow for the repeal of a number of related policies governing expenses in order to create user\-friendly, clear and concise guidelines regarding expenditure and claim reimbursements at UBC\.

The rationale that was presented to the Board of Governors for the amendments to Proposed Policy 83 is available [at this link &gt;](https://bog3.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/11/2_2018.11_Policy-83-Travel-and-Other-Business-Related-Expenses.pdf)\.

Please review [Proposed Policy 83](https://ubcfa.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/20Dec18-Policy-83_Call-For-Comments.pdf) and if you have any questions or concerns with the Policy, let us know by responding to this email or [sending an email to the Faculty Association](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#0d6b6c6e78617974236c7e7e626e646c796462634d786f6e236e6c327e786f67686e79304e6260606863797e2d7f68372d5d6261646e742d353e) by **Friday, January 18, 2019** to be considered as we formulate the Association’s response\. You also have the option of forwarding your questions or concerns directly to the [Office of the University Counsel](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f3869d9a859681809a878add909c869d80969fb3869190dd9092cc80869199969087ceb09c9e9e969d8780d38196c9d3a39c9f9a908ad3cbc0) \(Deadline: February 1, 2019\)\.

If you do submit a response to the University directly, please copy us \([\[email protected\]](https://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)\) so that we’re aware of your comments\.

Thank you\.
