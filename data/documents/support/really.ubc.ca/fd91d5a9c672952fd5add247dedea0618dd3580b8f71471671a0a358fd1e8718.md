## How to Apply

The Equity and Inclusion Office is recruiting a new team of students to become Active Witness Workshop Facilitators and help build an inclusive living and learning environment at UBC\.

The **Active Witness program** is an expanded student leadership program, designed to give students the knowledge, skills, and confidence required to act in response to discriminatory comments or stigmatizing situations\. The program not only educates students about diversity, but gives them strategies to be more than a bystander\.

For more information visit [http://equity\.ubc\.ca/education/aw\-facilitator/](http://equity.ubc.ca/education/aw-facilitator/) or contact Rachael Sullivan at rachael\.sullivan@ubc\.ca \(Vancouver\) or Jenica Frisque [jenica\.frisque@ubc\.ca](https://www.mail.ok.ubc.ca/owa/jenica.frisque@ubc.ca/jenica.frisque@ubc.ca) \(Okanagan\) \.
