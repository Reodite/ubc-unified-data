**Rural eMentoring BC is passionate about supporting rural students’ educational and career journeys, so we brought together some of the most relevant resources for rural students, communities and educators\.**  

-   Learn about events that ReMBC and its partners host throughout the year geared towards rural students\.  
-   ReMBC’s Content Corner is your go\-to place for information about college and university, health professions programs, career options, and, of course, mentoring – all with a special focus on rural\. You can also read current and past editions of our newsletter, see the academic research we been a part of, and view our yearly reports\. 
-   Discover other organizations and opportunities that support rural students\. 
-   As part of our effort to support students in the areas of Equity, Diversity, Inclusivity and Indigeneity, we have compiled a list of resources and supports for equity\-deserving students, as well as learning opportunities for those who wish to contribute to a more equitable society\.
