UBC faculty and staff have access to all SVPRO services, including support for individuals who have experienced or been impacted by sexual or gender\-based violence, harassment, or harm\. See information on accessing services, university policies, responding to disclosures, and resources specific to UBC faculty and staff\.

To request education services from SVPRO, please email [**svpro\.vancouver@ubc\.ca**](mailto:svpro.vancouver@ubc.ca)

### Deans and administrators

If you’re a dean or in an administrative role, there are specific safety measures and disciplinary processes to consider, particularly if you are a discipline decision\-maker\.

[Learn more](https://scs-svpro-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/faculty-and-staff/deans-and-administrators/)

### Faculty and teaching Staff

As educators of students, you may receive Disclosures from students\. Learn to respond to a Disclosure and review tips for establishing classroom safety and expectations\.

[See information](https://scs-svpro-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/faculty-and-staff/faculty-and-teaching-staff/)

### UBC staff

If you are a UBC staff seeking support, or you’ve received a Disclosure from someone, there are resources and education options available to help guide you in your understanding of the situation\.

[Find](https://svpro.ubc.ca/faculty-and-staff/ubc-staff/) [out](https://scs-svpro-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/faculty-and-staff/ubc-staff/) [more](https://svpro.ubc.ca/faculty-and-staff/ubc-staff/)
