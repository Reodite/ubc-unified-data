Review frequently asked questions regarding the process for clinical faculty in the Department of Radiology\.

Clinical faculty members are practicing health professionals governed by the [Faculty of Medicine Policy on Clinical Faculty Appointments](https://med-fom-faculty.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/FoM-Policy-on-Clinical-Faculty-Appointments-w-Criteria-FINAL-Feb-2026.pdf)\. For health professionals governed by the [Health Professions Act \(HPA\)](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/00_96183_01), you must be licensed or certified and in good standing with your respective College, Board, provincial or territorial governing body\. For health professionals not governed by the HPA, you must be employed by and in good standing with both a BC health authority \(or similar public agency\) and, if applicable, your professional organization\. For detailed eligibility criteria, refer to Section II A of the Policy\.

The University of British Columbia \(“UBC”\) recognizes that Clinical Faculty members make significant contributions in the education of undergraduate, graduate and postgraduate learners, residents and fellows and are often engaged in research and academic administration at UBC\. Health care education requires the active participation of learners in clinical settings\. The health care professionals who supervise and educate FOM learners in these clinical settings normally require a Clinical Faculty appointment\. Clinical Faculty members are health professionals engaged primarily in the practice of their professions outside UBC, whose appointments have been recommended by Departments/ Schools and the FOM for the purpose of participating in academic activities and have been approved by the UBC Board of Governors\. While teaching is the principal contribution of Clinical Faculty, academic administration and research are encouraged and recognized\.

Clinical Faculty Appointments, Reappointments and Promotions are governed by the University of British Columbia, Faculty of Medicine Policy on Clinical Faculty Appointments\. Depending on the terms of your employment contract, you may be eligible for compensation\.

***Documents needed for promotions:***

-   [UBC abbreviated CV](https://med-fom-radiology-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/Faculty-of-Medicine-Abbreviated-CV.doc) \(please highlight your academic activities completed since your last rank\)
-   Letter of recommendation from your site department head
-   Teaching evaluations – e\.g\., learner evaluations, peer assessments, or a teaching dossier\. \(Please contact Radiology HR if you don’t have copies of your evaluations\) If teaching evaluations are unavailable, include a brief explanation\. [Guide to Academic Activities for Clinical Faculty in Faculty of Medicine](https://med-fom-radiology-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/Guide-to-Academic-Activities-for-Clinical-Faculty-in-the-Faculty-of-Medicine.pdf)
-   Candidate’s Letter of Request**:** a self\-assessment outlining how you have met the criteria by providing a highlight of academic contributions, teaching interest, and service activities;

For promotion to **Clinical Professor**, also provide:

-   Evidence of two most recent significant, impactful contributions, with descriptions of how they meet the criteria \(e\.g\., publications, influence on policy/practice, curricular contributions\)\.
-   List of six potential referees with names and contact information, disclosure of the relationship and rationale for each suggestion :\-
    -   At least two referees must be external to UBC;
    -   Referees should speak to different areas of your academic contributions and how they meet the criteria\.

Please forward your completed documents by September 30th, 2026, for the fall committee meeting to payal\.jha@ubc\.ca

For more information, please refer to [https://www\.med\.ubc\.ca/clinical\-faculty/reappointment\-promotion/](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/reappointment-promotion/)

Clinical Faculty Appointment, Reappointment and Promotion Committee meetings take place twice a year\.

-   April/May – New appointments and reappointments
-   October/November – New appointments and promotions

The deadline to submit your documents for the April/May meeting is end of February\.

The deadline to submit your documents for the October/November meeting is end of September\.

Your promotion will be effective as of July 1 the following year\.

You will get notified by the Clinical Faculty HR Advisor that your application has been reviewed by the committee\.  New appointments will be processed in workday and, you will be added to the Teaching &amp; Tracking Payment system \(TTPS\)\.

Promotions will be forwarded to the Dean for final review\.  Once approved and processed you will get a letter from the Dean’s office after July 1 confirming your promotion\.

Please note: the estimated time frame to receive an academic license is 4\-6 months or longer, depending if a work permit is required\.
