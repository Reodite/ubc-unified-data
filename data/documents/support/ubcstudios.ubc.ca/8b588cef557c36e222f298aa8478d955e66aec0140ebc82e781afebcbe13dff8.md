### Here are the types of immersive media content that we produce\. From our more established services such as Matterport tours to newer technologies such as custom VR tours and photogorammetry, we'll make sure that you are satisfied with the end product\.

IMMERSIVE MEDIA TYPES

---

[Image](https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/04/Matterport.jpg)

[Visual Identity](https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/04/Matterport.jpg)

   
Matterport  
The Matterport Camera and software platform uses Bi\-Optic cameras to easily create 3d tours of spaces that can also be easily put onto streetview\. The quickest and easiest way to 3D capture a space\.  
 

[Image](https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/04/VR_Tours.jpg)

[Image](https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/04/VR_Tours.jpg)

   
Virtual Reality Tours  
VR Tours can be done a variety of ways\. From combining 360 photos into a Google Streetview tour, a fully developed program through Unity, or scanning using state\-of\-the art Lidar technology, we have a solution for you\.  
 

[Visual Identity](https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/04/Photogrammetry.jpg)

[Image](https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/04/Photogrammetry.jpg)

   
Photogrammetry  
Photogrammetry is the process of using hundreds of photos and combining them to create real\-life 3d objects\. A great innovative way to capture objects and specimens that are hard for students to see\.
