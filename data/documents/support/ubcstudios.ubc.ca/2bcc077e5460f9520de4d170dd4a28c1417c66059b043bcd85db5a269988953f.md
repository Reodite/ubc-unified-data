The Challenge  

### UBC Studios was tasked with designing and producing dynamic animated headshots and crowd pumpers for over 100 UBC student athletes\. These media assets are to be presented at home games on the Jumbotron screen to introduce the athletes to the crowd and energize Thunderbird fans\.

WHAT WE DID

-   Concept Development
-   Creative Design
-   Video Production
-   Motion Graphics
-   Post\-Production
