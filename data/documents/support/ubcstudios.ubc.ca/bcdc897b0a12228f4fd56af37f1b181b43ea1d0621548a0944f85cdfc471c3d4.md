UBC Studios brings home Platinum from the AVA Digital Awards for their animation work for “Debunking Desire” – a joint project between the UBC Sexual Health Laboratory and Canadian Institutes of Health Research\. Watch to find out how to keep healthy sexual life style via good mental health and other tips that can help\!

UBC Studios Animation Debunking Desire brings home Platinum from the AVA Digital Awards

Story Posted: January 18th, 2020

   
 

[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://player.vimeo.com/video/378417016)

### “We are extremely proud to be able to receive such an achievement, it really demonstrates that our creations are able to go toe to toe with industry\-leading agencies\.”

A joint project between the Canadian Institute of Health and the UBC Sexual Health Lab, this animation is one that promotes and creates awareness regarding keeping a healthy sexual life style and how to maintain it\. The animation offers tips and suggestions in an effective and intuitive way, making the lessons spread throughout this animation easy to remember and bring into practice\. This video was created in house by UBC Studios: Our producers in charge of managing the project and our visual design team and post\-production team creating the animation and creative assets\.
