Celebrating 60 years of excellence  

### UBC’s Faculty of Dentistry marked its 60th anniversary by showcasing its advanced training facilities, diverse specializations, and donor contributions through a series of media projects highlighting its impactful care and advancements\.

WHAT WE DID

-   Scripted Videos
-   Virtual Tours
-   Photography

Project Overview  

### The faculty’s achievements were celebrated through a showcase of its cutting\-edge training facilities and wide\-ranging work fromgeriatric to pediatric specializations\. Over multiple days of filming, we captured the vibrant learning environments and impactful care provided by the program\. Also, a video unveiling a new donor recognition wall was produced, emphasizing the donors’ transformative influence on dental education and practice\.

The Outcome  

### UBC Studios worked closely with the Faculty to create several professional video, photo, and virtual\-tour assets that highlighted the Faculty’s state\-of\-the\-art facilities, diverse specializations, and impactful donor contributions\. These assets played a key role in the Faculty’s 60th\-anniversary celebrations, used across platforms to promote events, in social media campaigns, outreach efforts, and more\.

### By showcasing their achievements and donor support, the assets aimed to strengthen engagement with alumni, inspire current and prospective students, and deepen relationships with donors, reinforcing the Faculty’s legacy and vision for the future\.
