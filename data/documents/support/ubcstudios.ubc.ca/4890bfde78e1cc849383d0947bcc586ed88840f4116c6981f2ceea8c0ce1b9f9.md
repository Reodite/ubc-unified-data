### Below are some examples of our photography work\. Our team ensures each image aligns with UBC’s brand guidelines and visual identity, while also capturing the tone, energy, and context of every project\. Whether it is showcasing research, highlighting campus life, or supporting communications campaigns, we focus on delivering high quality visuals that feel distinctly UBC\. Since our photographer is UBC staff, all final photos are created for the university and can be reused across your projects without additional licensing or contractor restrictions\.
