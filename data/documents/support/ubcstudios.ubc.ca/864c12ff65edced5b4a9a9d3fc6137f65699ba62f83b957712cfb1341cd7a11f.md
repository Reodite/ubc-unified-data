Project Overview  

### FORWARD, the campaign for UBC, launched in September, 2022 with the goal of raising $3 billion to benefit research, teaching and learning across both Vancouver and Okanagan campuses in three key areas – advancing healthy lives, creating solutions for the planet, and shaping thriving societies\.

WHAT WE DID

-   Aerials
-   Video Production
-   Post\-Production

Why Forward?  

### Following the success of Development and Alumni Engagement’s Blue and Gold Campaign, FORWARD needed to engage donors with a fresh perspective and visual approach\. UBC Studios, working alongside the creative agency TAXI, brought their creative vision to life with several videos\.

The Mission  

### The first goal was promoting the in\-person ‘Forward Thinkers’ event with a series of nine videos featuring each Forward Thinker\. Additionally, two keystone campaign videos were created to build on the excitement by eloquently explaining the case for giving, showcasing why, and how UBC and UBC Okanagan are global leaders, worthy of support while introducing the three themes\.

[Image](https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/03/ForwardCampaignUBCo.png)

The Outcome  

### Through these videos, we helped Development and Alumni Engagement to move people with a sense of urgency to motivate them into giving, and joining us on our journey forward\.  
    
This collaboration between TAXI, Development and Alumni Engagement, and UBC Studios continues with more videos underway as UBC Studios provides the bridge between agency\-driven creatives and high production value videos\.
