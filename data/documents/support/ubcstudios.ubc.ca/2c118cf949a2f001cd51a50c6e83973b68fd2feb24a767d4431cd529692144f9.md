THE CHALLENGE  

### How do we preserve the fragile and rare assets in the University’s collections, while increasing access for learners and researchers, during an era of increasing remote learning? UBC Studios is leveraging 3D scanning and imaging technology to provide more effective and expanded access to UBC’s bounty of unique and sought\-after object collections\.

Why 3D?  

### UBC’s rare and unique collections of specimens and artifacts are crucial to learning and research, but providing access while maintaining their integrity is challenging\. UBC Studios has addressed this challenge by offering 3D scanning and virtual model production, which is especially useful given the growing need for remote access to learning and research resources\.

The Mission  

### Our goal is to facilitate access to learning and research resources for students and researchers, while also preserving the integrity of the objects\. We addressed the ongoing challenge of providing access by creating a robust workflow for 3D scanning of objects and virtual model production\. We also met the growing need for remote access to these resources\.

[Image](https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/03/ED1743_BTS_4.png)

The Outcome  

### The resulting online assets provide ease of access for any number of students and researchers from any location, at UBC and beyond\. The resulting 3D interactive, highly detailed and accurate perspectives often provide more detail and visual information than the in\-person experience, as a result of high\-resolution scanning and reproduction\.  
   
UBC Studios is proud to support learning and research at UBC and beyond by making our resources more accessible to more people, while protecting the fragile and unique objects for future students and researchers\. 3D scanning and imaging by our team is providing a solution that will support the University as it hosts learning and research collections into future generations\.
