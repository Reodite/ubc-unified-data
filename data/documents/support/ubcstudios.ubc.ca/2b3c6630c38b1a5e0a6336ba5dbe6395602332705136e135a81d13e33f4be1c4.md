An exciting update to a new and improved DIY Recording and Lightboard Studios

An updated and refresh of DIY Studios

Written: June 25th, 2025   Written By: UBC Studios Operations

### We’re excited to announce our **brand\-new custom DIY kiosk**, now live in both the **DIY Video Recording** and **DIY Lightboard** studios\. Swing by, plug in, and take full control of your session start to finish\! This also means under certain scenarios, we’re able to start offering complimentary lightboard sessions as well \(please take a look at the below guidelines\)  
 

**DIY Video Recording Studio**:  
– Custom DIY kiosk for quick check\-in and session control  
– Simplified workflow: start session, hit record, and you’re off  
– All your usual gear and setup – added backgrounds\!

**DIY Lightboard Studio Guidelines**:  
– For self\-operating capability: use of just the lightboard and stationary boom mic  
– No overlays or advanced graphics included in the complimentary version  
– Use of our updated intuitive kiosk interface guiding you through setup and recording\.  

   
 

### For bookings or questions, please email [ubc\.studios@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.studios@ubc.ca)\.

   
 

[Learn More or Book a DIY Session](https://ubcstudios.ubc.ca/do-it-yourself)

# Introducing the DIY Video Recording Application

Built by UBC Studios to streamline educational content creation — record, monitor, and export effortlessly\. Fully compatible with OBS for seamless recording integration\.

### What’s New?

-   Designed for ease and speed
-   Export to USB or cloud in one click
-   Clear feedback and notifications
-   Built with privacy and simplicity in mind

### Key Features

-   Email confirmation upon upload
-   Session start/end tracking
-   Dark/light theme toggle
-   Modern responsive UI
