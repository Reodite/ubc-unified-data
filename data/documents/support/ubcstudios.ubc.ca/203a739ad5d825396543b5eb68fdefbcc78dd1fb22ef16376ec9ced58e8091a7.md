UBC Studios projects recognized on international stage at the 2022 Marcom Awards  
Story Posted: December 1st 2022   

[Facebook icon](https://www.facebook.com/sharer.php?u=https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?p=4021&amp;preview=true "Facebook icon")

 

[Image](https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/12/AwardsMarcom.jpg)  
 

Two UBC Studios projects were recognized at the prestigious 2022 Marcom Awards this year\. The Microboards Campaign, a project in partnership with the Canadian Institute for Inclusion and Citizenship, took Platinum\. This project included a visually engaging package of animations and designed printable assets created by our visual design and animation team\. Our second project, a promotional video titled “Working Together to Advance Brain Health” took gold\.

The awards showcase the hard\-work and dedication our staff put in to create engaging content that resonates with the audience both within and outside of the educational sphere\.

   
The Microboards™ Project  

   
We worked with Dr\. Tim Stainton and team from the Canadian Institute for Inclusion and Citizenship to highlight the impacts small structured groups \(Microboards™\) have on social capital and quality of life for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities\. *[View the full project description From the CIHC Website](https://cic.arts.ubc.ca/research-streams/policy-practice-and-ethics/policy-practice-and-ethics-projects/microboards-social-capitol-and-quality-of-life/)*  
   
UBC Studios produced and designed the animation, print materials, and infographic for this project\.

[Check out the Microboards awards submission on the MarCom site\.](https://enter.marcomawards.com/entry/microboards-social-capital-and-quality-of-life/)  
 

Thank you so much to you and your team for your incredible work, creative skills, collaboration, thoughtful and professional communication, and your reliable attention to detail\.

—on behalf of the Canadian Institute for Inclusion and Citizenship team

Working Together to Advance Brain Health  

   
The promotional video titled “Working Together to Advance Brain Health” is a promotional piece that highlights the many teams and researchers from the Djavad Mowafaghian Centre for Brain Health\. Showcasing their cutting\-edge research, work and equipment, the video also demonstrates the continued innovation and research excellence that continues to occur, all under the Centre’s mission to advance brain health through research, education and treatment\.  
   
[Check out the Working Together to Advance Brain Health awards submission on the MarCom site\.](https://enter.marcomawards.com/entry/working-together-to-advance-brain-health/)
