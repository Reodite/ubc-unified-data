What we do

---

### We offer specific DIY media support for **teaching and learning purposes**, as well as **full\-professional media production services**\.  

Media Consultation

Starting from initial conception to production, our producers have a wealth of experience from industry and from working extensively with faculty and staff at the university\. A media consultation is your first step towards a successful project—our producers are available for initial consultation at **no cost** and will help you assess the best approach\.

**Benefits of a media consultation include:**

-   Creative brief development
-   Concept development
-   Project and budget planning
-   Grant application support

[Contact us](https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/contact/) to book a consultation\.
