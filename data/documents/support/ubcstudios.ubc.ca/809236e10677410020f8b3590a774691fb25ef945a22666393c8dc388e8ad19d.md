General Questions

---

Professional Services

### Q: Who can use your professional services?

A: Our mandate is to provide services to organizations and departments within UBC or has a direct affiliation to UBC, such as Vancouver Coastal Health or BC Children’s Hospital\. We also work with other public institutions and government projects that have an established connection with UBC faculty, staff, or researchers\.

### Q: Do you offer photography services?

A: Yes, we provide brand\-aligned photography services for official UBC purposes\. You can see examples and learn more at [ubcstudios\.ubc\.ca/photography](http://ubcstudios.ubc.ca/photography)\. UBC also has a dedicated Brand Photographer, and you can find their guidelines and contact information at [brand\.ubc\.ca/guidelines/photography](http://brand.ubc.ca/guidelines/photography)\.

### Q: Can I book your main production studio for a UBC department or external company shoot?

A: Our main production studio is reserved for UBC Studios productions only\.

### Q: Do you provide ISDN Livehit \(Audio or Video\) services?

A: We do not offer ISDN Livehit services\.

Audio: Contact UBC’s campus radio station CiTR for assistance\.

Video: Contact UBC Media Relations\.

We can provide basic audio recording for pre\-recorded interviews or tape syncs via our DIY Audio Desk\.

### Q: Do you offer research poster printing on campus?

A: UBC Studios no longer provides research poster printing\. There are a few options available, such as Copysmart, Staples and more

[Academic Poster Print Shop](https://academicposterprintshop.ca/) is a service created by our teams past printshop tech, they offer UBC pickup every Monday and Thursday as well as delivery to the lower mainland\.

### Q: Can you livestream or record an event/conference for archival purposes?

A: Livestreaming, webcasting, and conference recording are handled by UBC AV Services\. Contact the AV Helpdesk at av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca or 604\-822\-7956 to connect with their webcast team\.

### Q: Can I book your main studio for photography or headshots?

A: The main studio is reserved for UBC Studios projects\. For photography needs outside of our services, consider reaching out to the UBC Photography Society\.

### Q: How can I find job or internship opportunities at UBC Studios?

A:

Full\-time roles: Check the UBC Careers page for positions listed under UBCIT Creative Media\.

Student co\-op: We regularly post co\-op positions for studio operations, production assistants, and related roles through faculty co\-op programs\.

Extras for shoots: Follow us on social media or email us to be added to our extras list\.

DIY Studios

### Q: How do I book one of your DIY Studios?

A: Please complete our Contact Us form with your preferred booking dates and times, along with a few alternate options\. Include a one\-line summary of your project\. As some of our spaces are also used for professional services or require an operator, all bookings are reviewed and confirmed manually by our staff\.

### Q: I am a UBC student\. Can I book a DIY Studio?

A: DIY Studios can only be booked for UBC\-related purposes, such as a specific course or departmental project\. Please have your UBC faculty or staff supervisor email ubc\.studios@ubc\.ca to confirm the booking is for official university use\.

### Q: Can I book a space for a personal project?

A: As a UBC department, we can only accept bookings directly related to the university and its goals\. While we encourage creative projects, personal work does not meet this requirement\. We recommend exploring the Vancouver Public Library’s Inspiration Lab or the Chapman Learning Commons for alternative options\.

### Q: Do you have a green screen available to rent?

A: Our DIY Recording Studio has a 10x10 ft green screen available for bookings\. The green screen in our K\. Karasin Studio is reserved for UBC Studios projects only\.
