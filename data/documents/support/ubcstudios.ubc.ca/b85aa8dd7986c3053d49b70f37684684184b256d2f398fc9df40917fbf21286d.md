Meeting a pressing need  

### Research shows that nurses often encounter bullying in the clinical workplace\. How can UBC faculty prepare nursing students for this real\-world challenge? UBC Studios collaborated with faculty from UBC Nursing and UBC Theatre &amp; Film in developing interactive videos which help students to explore how to respond to bullying before they enter the workplace\.  

WHAT WE DID

-   Collaborative script development
-   Scenario video production
-   H5P interactivity

---

[Embedded content: The CRAB Project \- Witness](https://h5p.open.ubc.ca/wp-admin/admin-ajax.php?action=h5p_embed&amp;id=612)

Project Overview  

### UBC Studios collaborated with UBC Nursing and Theatre &amp; Film faculty to produce compelling and immersive video scenarios that bring students into the high\-pressure experience of bullying in a safe learning context\. We collaborated with veteran nurses in developing realistic and effective scripts and videos\. We invited and incorporated their emotional calibration during filming and editing with professional actors, to translate their scripts into believable and accurate experiences for students\.  

### Interactivity was developed through collaboration with UBC IT in the implementation of newly supported H5P technology\.  

### As students choose between different responses in evocative scenes, they prepare to better care for patients\. This leads to real\-world impact in supporting the health of nurses and their patients, here in BC and around the world\.  

[Behind the scenes of UBC Nursing interactive scenario video production](https://ubc-studios-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/IMG_0006-scaled.jpg)

The Outcome  

### The interactive scenario videos give UBC Nursing students an opportunity to encounter challenging workplace situations before entering clinical practice, helping them build greater awareness and confidence in responding to bullying\.  

### The project received a Special Jury Award in 2021 from the MEDEA Media &amp; Learning Association\. The collaborative development process was also documented in a scholarly article published in *Quality Advancement in Nursing Education*, with a UBC Studios Producer as a co\-author\.
