### **UBC Studios worked with the Stewart Blusson Quantum Matter Institute to create an annual report for their work and accomplishments in 2020\.**

This report highlighted the important work of researchers, and showcased multiple projects and innovations that the institute accomplished\. The report was awarded Gold at the Hermes Awards in the Print\-Publication category\. View the full PDF on SBQMI’s website [here\.](https://qmi.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/AR_2020_WEB.pdf)
