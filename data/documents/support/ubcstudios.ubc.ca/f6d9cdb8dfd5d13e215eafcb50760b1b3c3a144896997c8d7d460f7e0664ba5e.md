Project Overview  

### Microboards™ are small structured groups comprised of key members who share an interest in helping the lives of individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities\. The Canadian Institute for Inclusion and Citizenship in partnership with Vela Canada and Community Living BC conducted a two year qualitative research study on what effects Microboards™ have on individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities\.

### CIIC approached UBC Studios with a need to communicate their findings to stakeholders and the greater public in a more accessible and engaging way\.

WHAT WE DID

-   Visual Design
-   Animation
-   Concept Development

Project Outcome  

### UBC Studios was able to translate the key findings of the study into an approachable visual metaphor along with weaving a common look and feel throughout a communications toolkit including two printed reports, a two\-page infographic and a 3\.5 minute explainer animation\.

Award Winning Communication  

### The Microboards™ project, was not only a great success but also received widespread recognition for its excellence in strategic communication and research, being awarded the prestigious platinum Marcom award under the Strategic Communications in a Research Study category\.
