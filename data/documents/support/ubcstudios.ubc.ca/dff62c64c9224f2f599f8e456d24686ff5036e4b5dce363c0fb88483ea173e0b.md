The animation titled “Mission to Launch” was awarded Gold at the prestigious Hermes Awards 2023\. This project was produced in\-house by our team, and emphasizes our commitment in pushing the boundaries of media and digital communication, which reinforces UBC’s commitment to innovation\.

The Hermes Creative Awards recognizes excellence in the field of media and communications\. It is an international competition that honours the creative achievements of professionals in various disciplines, including animation\.
