**[ubccard\-front](https://ubccard.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/08/ubccard-front.png)**

**PHOTO:** Digital colour head and shoulder shot on white background

**UNIVERSITY STATUS:**  
**Students:** Classified as Undergraduate, Graduate or Unclassified  
**Faculty &amp; Staff:** Classified as Faculty or Staff\. “Faculty” will be indicated on UBCcards of cardholders who hold both faculty and staff appointments  
**Emeritus &amp; Retired Staff:** Classified as either “Emeritus” or “Retired”

**NAME:** Name of cardholder

**STUDENT OR UBC EMPLOYEE NUMBER:** Student or employee identification number of the cardholder

[ubccard\-back](https://ubccard.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/08/ubccard-back.png)

**MAGNETIC STRIPE:** The magnetic stripe contains the cardholder’s name and identification number

**EXPIRATION DATE:** UBCcards are typically valid for up to five years and must be renewed upon expiration

**STUDENT OR UBC EMPLOYEE NUMBER:** Student or employee identification number of the cardholder

**LIBRARY BARCODE:** Library barcode and barcode numbers are automatically generated during card production

**iCLASS NUMBER**: The iClass number is the 5 digit number following the asterix \(\*\)\. Each iClass UBCcard has an embedded radio\-frequency chip that functions as an access token that permits access into electronically\-controlled areas\.
