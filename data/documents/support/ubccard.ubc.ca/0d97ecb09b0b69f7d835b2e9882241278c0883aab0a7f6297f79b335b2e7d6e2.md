Ubcard applications are accepted on\-line\. Once ready, cards can be picked up from the Access Desk located inside the UBC Bookstore at 6200 University Blvd\. For our hours of operation visit www\.parking\.ubc\.ca 

This is to promote safe physical distancing at this unprecedented time\. Consequently we appreciate your patience as we respond to phone and email contacts in the order in which they are received\.

**UBCcard Requests**

If you need a new or replacement card you **MUST** apply online\.  
[https://ubccard\.ubc\.ca/obtaining\-a\-ubccard/students](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/obtaining-a-ubccard/students)\.[https://ubccard\.ubc\.ca/obtaining\-a\-ubccard/faculty\-staff/](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/obtaining-a-ubccard/faculty-staff/)

if your card is needed immediately:  
1\. Apply online  
2\. We will contact you when your card is ready for curbside pick up  
3\. When picking up your card, don’t forget to bring Govt issued photo ID with you for verification\.

We are not accepting any walk\-in card requests at this time
