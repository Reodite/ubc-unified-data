### Apply online

Students, faculty and staff may apply online for their UBCcards 24 hours a day, seven days a week\. Click on ‘Students: apply online’ button  \(top right hand of this page\), or Faculty &amp; Staff, as well as Emeritus &amp; Retired Staff under the ‘Obtaining a UBCcard’ heading on the left to apply for your UBCcard\. Cards must be picked up at the Card office during regular business hours\.

### UBC Card office

Students, faculty and staff may apply for UBCcards at the UBC Parking Services Desk, located inside the [UBC Bookstore](http://www.bookstore.ubc.ca/) at 6200 University Boulevard\.

### Regular Hours of Operation

Monday\-Friday: 10:00am – 5:00pm

Third Thursday of every month: 11:00\-5:00pm

Visit [UBC Bookstore](http://bookstore.ubc.ca/customer-service/location-and-hours) for extended hours of operation in August, September and January\.
