Once you have received your UBCcard, you will be able to use your student ID number \(located on your card\) to receive a U\-Pass\.

Eligible UBC students can load their U\-Pass onto a Compass Card\. The U\-Pass provides unlimited bus, SeaBus, SkyTrain and Canada Line transit services within Metro Vancouver\. Students should first obtain a Compass Card \(they’re available on campus at the UBC Bookstore\) and then link it to their U\-Pass online at [upassbc\.translink\.ca](http://upassbc.translink.ca/)\.

[For more information and to learn how to load your U\-pass onto your Compass Card](https://ubccard.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/01/UPassBCinfographic-rackcard-1.pdf) 

**Please note U\-Pass is not managed by the UBC carding office and any inquiries should be directed to Translink and / or AMS**\.
