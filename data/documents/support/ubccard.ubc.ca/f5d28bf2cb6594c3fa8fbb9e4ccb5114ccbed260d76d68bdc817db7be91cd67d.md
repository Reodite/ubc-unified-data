Unless you are A\) Living in residence, or B\) You’re registered for in\-person classes, you will only receive a digital version of your UBCcard\. It works for the library and as a picture ID and is accessible on your phone\.

Returning students who already have a physical UBCcard can access a digital version of their card by logging into their account\. This provides a convenient option for students who have misplaced their physical card and need to use their UBCcard while waiting to apply for or receive a replacement\.

**Differences between physical and digital cards:** Please note that digital cards cannot be used to access meal plans, e\-commerce purchases or be used to access buildings or  facilities\.
