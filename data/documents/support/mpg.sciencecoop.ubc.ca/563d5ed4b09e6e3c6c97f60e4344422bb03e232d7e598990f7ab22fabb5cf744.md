**UBC Science Co\-op Wiki**

Travel tips on accommodation, insurance and finances etc are available on UBC Science Co\-op Wiki\. Previous Co\-op students shared their knowledge on working abroad on this Wiki page in hopes that future students can be better prepared for their Co\-op experience in Germany\. You are welcome to contribute and share your expertise with other students as well\. Please follow the format and create additional pages of your own by country and city\.
