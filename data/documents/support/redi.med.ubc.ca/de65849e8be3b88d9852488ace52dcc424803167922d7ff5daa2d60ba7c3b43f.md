### [Resource Hub](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/resources/)

#### REDI Moments That Matter are reflective guides that focus on why certain moments, such as recruitment, onboarding, and conflict, deserve particular attention because of their impact on working and learning environments\. They help make concepts more concrete by offering benchmarks, guiding questions, and examples that support reflection\. These guides are intended to help readers assess their own processes, surface assumptions, and make the case for approaching key moments with greater intentionality and equity awareness\.
