Whether you’re leading a discussion or participating, this tip sheet offers practical guidance for fostering respectful and inclusive discussions, especially when conversations touch on complex or sensitive topics\. Adapted from radical inclusivity practices, these agreements help participants navigate power dynamics, listen mindfully, and engage with openness and empathy\.

## Why Do We Have This Document?

This document supports facilitators and participants in engaging in respectful, inclusive conversations on topics that may bring up strong emotions or differing perspectives\.

The practices below are adapted from radical inclusivity resources developed by the East Bay Meditation Center:  
[Radical Inclusivity Practices](https://eastbaymeditation.org/about/radical-inclusivity/radical-inclusivity-practices/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Facilitators are encouraged to review and revisit these agreements with participants before and throughout discussions\. They may also be adapted based on context and audience needs\.

---

## Community Agreements for Inclusive Dialogue

These agreements are intended to support respectful participation, shared learning, and psychological safety in group conversations\.

---

### 1\. Try on Someone Else’s Shoes

Be willing to consider perspectives that differ from your own, even when they feel unfamiliar or uncomfortable\.

---

### 2\. Practice Self\-Focus

Speak from your own experience and perspective\. Avoid generalizing your experience to entire groups or assuming others share it\.

---

### 3\. Notice Power Dynamics

Be aware of differences in power within the group, including how your own position may shape participation\. Use your influence to support inclusion of others\.

---

### 4\. Practice “Both/And” Thinking

Use “and” instead of “but” when possible to acknowledge multiple truths or perspectives\.

-   This approach supports the idea that more than one experience can be valid at the same time\.

---

### 5\. Avoid Blame and Shame

Engage with curiosity rather than judgment\. Offer feedback in ways that recognize shared responsibility for learning, growth, and change\.

---

### 6\. Move Up / Move Back

Pay attention to participation patterns\.

-   If you tend to speak often, consider stepping back to create space for others\.
-   If you tend to speak less, consider stepping forward to contribute\.

---

### 7\. Practice Mindful Listening

Listen without planning your response\. Stay open to being surprised and learning something new\.

---

### 8\. Remember the Purpose of This Space

This is a learning environment, not a therapy space\. While vulnerability is welcome, participants are encouraged to be mindful of emotional boundaries and the purpose of the discussion\.

---

### 9\. Respect Confidentiality

Take learnings beyond the session, but do not share identifying details about other participants\. If you wish to follow up with someone, ask for their consent first\.

---

### 10\. Exercise the Right to Pass

You are welcome to say “I pass” at any time if you prefer not to speak or if participation feels outside your comfort level\.
