### [Newsletter](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/newsletter/)

[Image](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/Share-research-articles-on-REDI-in-medicine-2400-x-1000-px-940x392.png)

We warmly invite you to **subscribe to our new public REDI Newsletter listserv** to receive upcoming issues, which were previously shared only within the Faculty of Medicine\.

**To subscribe:**  
Email **LISTSERV@LISTS\.UBC\.CA**

In the **body of the email**, type:  
  
**subscribe REDI\-NEWSLETTER@LISTS\.UBC\.CA**

-   Do **not** include any other text or your email signature\.
-   Do **not** hyperlink **REDI\-NEWSLETTER@LISTS\.UBC\.CA** — it must be plain text\.

You’ll then receive a follow\-up email asking you to confirm your subscription\.

If you experience any issues, please contact **[redi\.events@ubc\.ca](mailto:redi.events@ubc.ca)**—we’re happy to help\. You can unsubscribe at any time\.
