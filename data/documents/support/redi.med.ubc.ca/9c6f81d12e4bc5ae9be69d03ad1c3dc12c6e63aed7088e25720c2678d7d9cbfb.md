# Introduction to the REDI Office: Resources for Faculty &amp; Staff

Are you new–or onboarding new–faculty or staff? REDI is here to support you with…

-   EDI skills workshop facilitation
-   Conflict coaching and dialogue
-   Event planning \(grand rounds, retreats and more\)
-   Educational resource development
-   Affinity and community spaces support
-   Strategic planning and EDI committee guidance

## Connect with REDI’s Support &amp; Professional Development Opportunities

Our events invite reflection, connection and action toward more inclusive, equitable healthcare sciences and professions education\.

-   Register for an upcoming educational event: [REDI Events](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/events/all-events/)\.
-   Use turn\-key, REDI\-made slide decks and lesson plans to facilitate EDI discussions in a team meeting or retreat
    -   Introductory Session: [EDI in Our Work](https://ubcca-my.sharepoint.com/:p:/g/personal/joseph_tita_ubc_ca/Ee2-6T5N2OxKsut2pdTvMJsBy5ctSMmtC3wTy1zMg7MYyQ?e=CthsEm)
    -   Intermediate Session: [Anti\-Racism in Our Work](https://ubcca-my.sharepoint.com/:p:/g/personal/joseph_tita_ubc_ca/EVOdvylVQGpCqssD9T0ga8sB0WlON78P73NM9JIxNiDj6Q?e=1jjjcX)
-   Browse and view **over 50 hours of recorded events** in our [REDI Recordings Archive](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/recordings/)
-   Support EDI in your day\-to\-day work, learning environment and workplace culture with our quick\-and\-easy Best Practices documents, or [work with REDI to co\-develop a resource for your needs](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/how-can-we-help/tailored-edi-learning-resources/)\.
    -   Tips for Inclusive [Interviewing](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/v2-in-person-interviews-12.pdf)
    -   Planning [Inclusive Meetings](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/01/Copy-of-Tips-for-Inclusive-and-Accessible-Meetings-13.pdf)
    -   The [O’DEAR Approach to Difficult Conversations](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/redi-best-practices-the-odear-framework-for-difficult-conversations/)
    -   …and many more topics: [REDI Best Practices](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/redi-best-practices/)
-   Join the [EDI Joint Interest Group \(EDI\-JIG\)](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/edi-joint-interest-group/), a community of practice for equity leads, EDI champions or any staff or faculty responsible for advancing or leading EDI initiatives in the Faculty of Medicine\. We meet monthly online\.
    -   [Join the EDI\-JIG email list](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/edi-joint-interest-group/#How-to-subscribe)

## Collaborate with REDI
