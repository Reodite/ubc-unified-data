In this session, participants will discuss why engaging with equity, diversity and inclusion \(EDI\) is important in creating just and healthy communities where everyone feels a sense of belonging\. They will also identify how oppression operates\. This session will provide an opportunity to discuss how to engage in the learning journey to facilitate a strong commitment to EDI\. This is part of the [**REDI Lunch &amp; Learn Series**](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/engagement/initiatives/redi-lunch-learn-series/)\.

**Topic**: Demystifying Equity, Diversity and Inclusion: Starting the Journey of Learning and Unlearning

**Date**: Thursday, March 17, 2022

**Time**: 12:00 – 1:00 pm PDT

### Facilitator: Dr\. Neila Miled, Anti\-Racism Advisor, Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity &amp; Inclusion

[Image](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/05/Neila-Miled-Feature-e1621274984391-150x150.png)

**Dr\. Neila Miled** is the Anti\-Racism Advisor in the Faculty of Medicine Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity &amp; Inclusion\. Neila brings academic expertise in anti\-racism education, first\-hand experience with systemically marginalized and under\-represented groups, and a social justice and equity lens\. Neila is responsible for providing advice, developing and delivering a collaborative, anti\-racist education and skill\-building program, and for implementing and sustaining strategic best practices in equity, diversity, and inclusion\. In collaboration with the REDI team, she will work towards facilitating and implementing organizational change strategies to create systemic change and foster a safer, more respectful, equitable and inclusive learning and working environment\.

[Learn more about Neila in her Community Voices profile](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/the-community-voices-series-dr-neila-miled/)

[anti\-racism\.advisor@ubc\.ca](mailto:anti-racism.advisor@ubc.ca)

### What Will I Learn?

-   Discuss the importance of engaging with EDI
-   Learn about the major concepts related to equity, diversity and inclusion
-   Identify oppression and how it operates
-   Discuss strategies to learn and to unlearn

### What is the REDI Lunch &amp; Learn Series?

The Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity &amp; Inclusion is proud to present a new [**REDI Lunch &amp; Learn**](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/engagement/initiatives/redi-lunch-learn-series/) series featuring our team of advisors: [Robyn Campol](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/about/contact-us/our-team/#Learning_Environment_Advisor), [Dr\. Neila Miled](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/about/contact-us/our-team/#Anti-Racism%20Advisor) and [Derek Thompson](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/about/contact-us/our-team/#Indigenous%20Initiatives%20Advisor)\. This fresh and informative series will be spontaneous and conversational in its approach to talk about issues that are timely and relevant\. These sessions will also highlight the importance of showing up to these necessary conversations with presence and persona – so bring your very best of curiosity and energy\!

Each session will also provide an opportunity for the participants to engage in a Q &amp; A or to simply to reflect on the merits of the conversations\. This exciting series will be offered weekly starting in March 2022, with each advisor presenting their own one hour session\.
