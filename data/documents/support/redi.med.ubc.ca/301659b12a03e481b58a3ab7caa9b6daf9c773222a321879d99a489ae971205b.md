June 21 is [National Indigenous Peoples Day](https://www.rcaanc-cirnac.gc.ca/eng/1100100013718/1708446948967) in Canada, a time to recognize and celebrate the history, heritage, resilience, and diversity of First Nations, Inuit, and Métis communities across Canada\. In honour of this day, we are thrilled to share a curated list of our favourite Indigenous book and media\. We encourage you to explore our curated collection and participate in local festivities throughout British Columbia\.
