### Conflict Engagement

    [REDI Best Practices: Receiving Feedback Well](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/Receiving-Feedback-Well-2.pdf#new_tab)
    
    Feedback is essential for growth, yet receiving it gracefully—especially when it involves EDI—can be challenging\. This tip sheet provides actionable strategies to overcome common barriers—such as defensiveness, emotional reactions, and binary thinking\. It supports you in transforming difficult conversations into meaningful opportunities for personal reflection and professional growth\.
    
-   [https://redi\.med\.ubc\.ca/redi\-best\-practices\-the\-odear\-framework\-for\-difficult\-conversations/](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/redi-best-practices-the-odear-framework-for-difficult-conversations/)[Image](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/The-ODEAR-Framework-for-Difficult-Conversations-768x320.jpg)
    
    [REDI Best Practices: The O’DEAR Framework for Difficult Conversations](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/redi-best-practices-the-odear-framework-for-difficult-conversations/)
    
    This tip sheet provides a practical, step\-by\-step approach for navigating difficult conversations with respect and clarity\. By following the O’DEAR model—Open, Describe, Explain, Ask, and Request—you can express concerns, listen actively, and co\-construct solutions to address sensitive issues\.
    
-   [https://med\-fom\-cpp\-2022\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2024/07/Giving\-Feedback\-to\-Someone\-About\-Their\-EDI\-Mistake\-1\.pdf\#new\_tab](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/07/Giving-Feedback-to-Someone-About-Their-EDI-Mistake-1.pdf#new_tab)[Effective Feedback Strategies for Addressing EDI Mistakes](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/files/2024/07/REDI-Best-Practices-Effective-Feedback-Strategies-for-Addressing-EDI-Mistakes-768x320.jpg)
    
    [REDI Best Practices: Effective Feedback Strategies for Addressing EDI Mistakes](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/07/Giving-Feedback-to-Someone-About-Their-EDI-Mistake-1.pdf#new_tab)
    
    This REDI Best Practices resource offers a thoughtful guide on navigating those tricky moments when someone makes an EDI\-related mistake\. With a focus on preparation and empathy, it helps you determine if feedback is the right approach and provides strategies for having constructive, respectful conversations\. Dive in to learn how to turn these challenging situations into opportunities for growth and understanding\.
    
-   [https://redi\.med\.ubc\.ca/redi\-best\-practices\-the\-5\-ds\-of\-bystander\-intervention/](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/redi-best-practices-the-5-ds-of-bystander-intervention/)[The 5 D’s of Bystander Intervention](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/The-5-Ds-of-Bystander-Intervention-2-768x384.jpg)
    
    [REDI Best Practices: The 5 D’s of Bystander Intervention](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/redi-best-practices-the-5-ds-of-bystander-intervention/)
    
    Imagine you witness harassment unfolding before your eyes\. What steps can you take? This tip sheet breaks down the crucial elements of intervention, starting with a thoughtful consideration of timing\. Is it safe to act immediately, or is it wiser to wait for a safer opportunity? Discover the five key actions you can take from direct intervention to delegation, each “D” offers a strategic approach tailored to various scenarios\.
