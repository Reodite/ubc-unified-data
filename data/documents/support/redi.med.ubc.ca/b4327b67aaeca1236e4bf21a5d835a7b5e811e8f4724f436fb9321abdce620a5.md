### [How Can We Help?](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/how-can-we-help/)

Every Faculty of Medicine program, department, and team has unique EDI goals, audiences, and learning needs\. REDI collaborates with Faculty of Medicine units to design tailored resources and learning supports that translate EDI principles into practical, context\-specific action\. 

Our approach is research\-informed, audience\-centered, and grounded in instructional design and adult learning best practices\. Rather than simply producing documents, we partner with you to design educational assets and knowledge mobilization tools that help your team learn, teach, and apply EDI in practice\. 

[Submit a Request](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_8IiPMuLX8yqx3xA)

---

## **How We Collaborate** 

We partner with Faculty of Medicine units for EDI instructional and resource design:

### **Learning Design &amp; Curriculum Development** 

We design professional development, structured educational experiences, curriculum components, and interactive materials that support active learning, and self\-directed growth\. 

-   **Self\-directed online modules** and interactive e\-learning resources\. 

-   **Flipped\-classroom materials**, including pre\-workshop preparation and post\-session reinforcement\. 

-   **Workshop and retreat design**, including learning objectives, interactive case scenarios, facilitated activities, and facilitator guides\. 

-   **Accreditation\-aligned learning supports** to help programs meet specific educational standards\. 

### **Knowledge Mobilization &amp; Practical Tools** 

We translate complex EDI evidence, policies, and research into highly accessible, practical tools that support decision\-making and inclusive practices in day\-to\-day clinical, academic, and administrative work\. 

-   **Just\-in\-time reference guides**, decision trees, and quick\-access tip sheets\. 

-   **Practical implementation guides** and evidence\-based frameworks for inclusive practices \(e\.g\., inclusive curriculum design, equitable admissions\)\. 

-   **Evidence briefs** and syntheses of current EDI literature tailored to specific medical education contexts\.

## Sample Vignettes

The following anonymized vignettes show how we collaborate with units to design structured learning environments and mobilize practical EDI tools\.

### Vignettes for **Learning Design &amp; Curriculum Development** 

A Faculty of Medicine team wanted to use a flipped classroom approach for a Grand Rounds session on conflict engagement\. REDI co\-designed a self\-paced online module introducing key concepts and practical strategies, allowing participants to spend the in\-person workshop actively practicing skills and discussing real\-world scenarios\.   

Partnering with a department to address workplace culture, REDI designed a comprehensive learning package that integrated preparatory readings, presentation materials, interactive case scenarios, facilitated activities, and a post\-session reference guide\. 

Supporting a postgraduate medical education department’s annual retreat, REDI worked with the planning team to co\-design learning objectives, interactive activities, and supporting materials that the department’s leadership could facilitate with confidence\. 

REDI customized core admissions learning modules to address the unique logistical, clinical, and pedagogical contexts of postgraduate residency programs and diverse health professions programs\.   
*[Explore the Postgraduate &amp; Health Professions Admissions Modules](https://courses.cpe.ubc.ca/browse/ubcv/medicine/redi)*

### Vignettes for **Knowledge Mobilization &amp; Practical Tools** 

To support educators in writing more inclusive clinical scenarios, REDI synthesized complex pedagogical literature into a highly practical guide featuring clear strategies, checklists, and side\-by\-side examples of revised medical narratives\.   
*[View the Inclusive Medical Case Writing Guide](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/redi-best-practices-writing-inclusive-case-studies/)*

REDI collaborated with admissions committees to design evidence\-informed decision\-making tools and guidance documents that scaffolded bias\-mitigation strategies directly into their review processes\.

REDI adapted established undergraduate admissions equity resources to fit the specific institutional constraints, objectives, and evaluation workflows of awards adjudication committees\.
