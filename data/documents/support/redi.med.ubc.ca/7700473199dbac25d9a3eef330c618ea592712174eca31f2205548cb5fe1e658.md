The Graduate and Postdoctoral Education office are developing an exciting new program called [Healthy Environments in Academic Research Teams \(HEART\)](https://grad-postdoc.med.ubc.ca/healthy-environments-in-academic-research-teams-heart-program/)\. HEART aims to equip you and your team with the knowledge and tools you need to create a genuinely healthy and inclusive research environment for everyone\.

They want to hear your thoughts on what makes a healthy research environment in the Faculty of Medicine\. [Read more &gt;](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/healthy-environments-in-academic-research-teams-heart%ef%bf%bc/ "Read more &gt;")
