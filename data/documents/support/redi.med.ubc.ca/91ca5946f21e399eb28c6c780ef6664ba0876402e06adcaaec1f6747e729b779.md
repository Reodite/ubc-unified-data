### [Resource Hub](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/resources/)

#### “REDI Word of the Month” is where we explore a specific REDI concept or term in more detail\.  

---

-   #### [Discrimination](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/discrimination/)
    
    [https://redi\.med\.ubc\.ca/discrimination/](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/discrimination/)[Discrimination](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/files/2024/12/Discrimination.jpg)
    
    Discrimination is conduct that imposes burdens, obligations, or disadvantages on, or limits access to, opportunities, benefits, and advantages for specific individuals or groups, as defined by the BC Human Rights Code\. UBC’s Discrimination Policy \(SC7\) flows from and is interpreted in a manner consistent with the BC Human Rights Code\.

-   #### [Inclusion](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/inclusion/)
    
    [https://redi\.med\.ubc\.ca/inclusion/](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/inclusion/)[Inclusion](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/files/2024/10/Inclusion-3.jpg)
    
    Inclusion refers to “the act or practice of including and accommodating people who have historically been excluded \(because of their race, gender, sexuality, or ability\)\.”¹ In a workplace context, inclusion is about creating an environment where everyone feels welcomed, valued, respected, and provided with opportunities to flourish, thrive, and succeed\.

    [https://redi\.med\.ubc\.ca/trauma\-informed\-pedagogy/](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/trauma-informed-pedagogy/)[Trauma\-Informed Pedagogy](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/Trauma-Informed-Pedagogy.jpg)

-   #### [Intergroup dialogue](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/intergroup-dialogue/)
    
    [https://redi\.med\.ubc\.ca/intergroup\-dialogue/](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/intergroup-dialogue/)[Intergroup dialogue](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Intergroup-dialogue-2.jpg)
    
    Intergroup dialogue is a face\-to\-face, structured, and facilitated group experience that creates opportunities for participants to listen deeply, engage in self\-reflection, and gain new perspectives on polarizing societal issues\.
    
-   #### [Universal Design](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/universal-design/)
    
    [https://redi\.med\.ubc\.ca/universal\-design/](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/universal-design/)[Universal Design](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/Universal-Design.jpg)
    
    Universal Design \(UD\) calls for the removal of barriers to support everyone, not only people with disabilities\. It can be applied to a variety of contexts, including learning and the workplace\.
    
-   #### [Affinity Spaces](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/affinity-spaces/)
    
    [https://redi\.med\.ubc\.ca/affinity\-spaces/](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/affinity-spaces/)[Affinity Spaces](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/Affinity-Groups-4.jpg)
    
    An affinity group is a community of peers that offers support, guidance, resources, and mentorship to its members, who identify as like\-minded with the group\. Affinity groups are particularly valuable as they provide a sense of belonging, community, and collective empowerment to members of historically, systemically, and perpetually marginalized groups\.
