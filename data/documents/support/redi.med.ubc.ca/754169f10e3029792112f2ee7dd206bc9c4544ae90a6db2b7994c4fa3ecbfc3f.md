#### How Can We Help?

REDI provides a full range of services, including EDI Development\.

[Learn More](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/engagement/how-can-we-help/)

### Equity

We will strive to:

-   Achieve parity in policy, process and outcomes for historically and/or currently underrepresented people or groups
-   Ensure equitable access to opportunities, treatment and outcomes for all learners, faculty and staff
-   Create opportunities and remove barriers in recognition of different histories and starting points of individuals and groups
-   Ensure that our programs prepare learners, faculty and staff to meet the needs of diverse communities locally and globally

---

### Diversity

We will strive to:

-   Increase diversity of learners, staff and faculty at all levels \(diversity exists\)
-   Value the presence of a wide range of human qualities and attributes \(diversity is valued\)
-   Harness the strengths of diversity to drive innovation and excellence \(diversity is leveraged\)

---

### Inclusion and Belonging

We will strive to:

-   Actively, intentionally and continuously foster an environment that involves underrepresented groups and individuals in processes, activities and decision\-making to address inequities in power and privilege
-   Build a respectful and diverse community and atmosphere that ensures welcoming spaces and opportunities to contribute and flourish for all
-   Establish a culture where learners, faculty and staff are recognized, valued, comfortable expressing their thoughts and have the ability to make contributions
-   Contribute to the positive impacts on the wellbeing, experiences and success of our learners, faculty and staff
