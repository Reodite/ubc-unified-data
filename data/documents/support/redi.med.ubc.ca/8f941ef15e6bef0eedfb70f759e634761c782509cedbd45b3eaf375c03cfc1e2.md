### [Resource Hub](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/resources/)

#### REDI Facilitation Guides are engaging slide decks designed to support leaders in facilitating team learning sessions that embed EDI principles into their unit’s core practices\. Explore two types of facilitation guides:

-   **Grab ‘n’ Gos**: Bite\-sized slide decks that can be delivered in 15 minutes or less, making them ideal for kicking off team meetings\. These sessions provide ongoing EDI reminders and support continuous professional development in digestible chunks\.
-   **Deep Dives**: Hour\-long learning sessions that explore EDI topics in greater depth, helping teams build knowledge, foster meaningful discussion, and integrate EDI principles into their unit’s core practices\.
