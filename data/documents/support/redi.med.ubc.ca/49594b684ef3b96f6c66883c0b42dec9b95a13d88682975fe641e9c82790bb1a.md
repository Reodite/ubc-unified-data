# The 5 D’s of Bystander Intervention

## What Should You Do If You See Someone Being Harassed?

If you witness harassment, consider the following:

1.  **Consider the timing**  
    Is it safe to take action right now, or is it better to wait?
2.  **Think about the need**  
    How can you best support the person who is being harmed?
3.  **Take action**  
    Once you’ve assessed the situation, choose a response and follow through\.

---

## The 5 D’s of Bystander Intervention

You can choose one or more of the following approaches depending on the situation\.

## DELEGATE

**Say:** “I need your help with a situation\.”

Use others to help intervene or escalate appropriately\.

### Actions

-   Ask a nearby colleague for support\.
-   Delegate tasks to other witnesses\.
-   Find someone in authority to step in\.

### Examples

-   Ask a resident or preceptor to help intervene or co\-witness the situation\.
-   Get the attention of a supervisor and explain what is happening\.

---

## DISTRACT

**Say:** “I really like those shoes—can you help me find the Facilities Department?”

Interrupt the situation to shift attention and reduce harm\.

### Actions

-   Interrupt before the situation escalates\.
-   Redirect attention away from the harmful interaction\.
-   Support the person being harmed in leaving the situation\.

### Examples

-   Physically position yourself between people if appropriate\.
-   Change the subject to something neutral\.
-   Help one person exit the interaction safely\.

---

## DELAY

**Say:** “Yikes, that was bad\. Are you okay?”

Check in after the situation has passed if immediate intervention wasn’t possible\.

### Actions

-   Wait until it is safe to act\.
-   Follow up with the person who was harmed\.

### Examples

-   Acknowledge that harm occurred\.
-   Offer support and presence\.
-   Ask what they would like to do next\.

---

## DOCUMENT

**Say:** “I saw what happened, and I took notes\. What would you like to do?”

Record details of the incident to support follow\-up or reporting\.

### Actions

-   Capture accurate details of what occurred\.
-   Note who was present and what was said or done\.
-   Record time, place, and context\.

### Examples

-   Write down notes immediately after the incident\.
-   Record a voice memo describing what you observed\.

---

## DIRECTLY INTERVENE

**Say:** “Stop\. That is not okay\.”

Step in to address the behaviour in the moment\.

### Actions

-   Address the harmful behaviour directly\.
-   Name what is happening\.
-   Support the person being harmed\.

### Examples

-   Clearly identify the behaviour as inappropriate\.
-   State what will not be tolerated\.
-   Focus on ensuring safety and support for the affected person\.
