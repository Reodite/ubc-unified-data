Monitoring and measuring are critical to successful cultural transformation and a key priority

---

We will develop metrics, collect and analyze data, both quantitative and qualitative, to regularly measure our progress\. Being transparent with the community with respect to progress, successes and failures, is essential to maintaining engagement, sustaining momentum and facilitating key decision\-making\.

This is not a static plan\. It will change over time influenced by our experiences, ongoing dialogue and by the development of new insights\.

We will develop a system that coordinates and integrates data from across the Faculty of Medicine that can be housed in a single accessible space\. We will report progress on this page\.
