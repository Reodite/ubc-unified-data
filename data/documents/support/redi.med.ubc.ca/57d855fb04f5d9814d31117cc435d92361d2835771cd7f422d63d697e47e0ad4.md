## [Intersectionality](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/intersectionality/)

Intersectionality is the recognition that social identities or categorizations \(such as race, class, disability, sexual orientation, and gender identity\) create overlapping and interdependent systems of discrimination or disadvantage\. [Learn more](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/intersectionality/)
