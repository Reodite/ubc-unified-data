## Learn From REDI’s Professional Development Opportunities

Whether it’s through a thought\-provoking speaker series, an interactive workshop or a cultural conversation, each event invites reflection, connection, and action toward creating more inclusive, equitable healthcare and education\.

-   Register for an upcoming event: [REDI Events](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/events/all-events/)
    -   **Featured Event:** [Frybread Power: The Significance of Indigenous Empowerment, Pride &amp; Resilience for Health](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/frybread-power-the-significance-of-indigenous-empowerment-pride-resilience/) \(click to register\)
        -   Sep 21, 9:00 am—4:00 pm Join an Indigenous Speakers Series session featuring Dr\. Evan *Tlesla* Adams and Joe Gallagher\. The session will take place as a full\-day, in\-person event on the UBC Vancouver campus and will also be live\-streamed online\.
-   Browse and view **over 50 hours of recorded events** about EDI in health sciences and professions education, diverse voices in healthcare, Indigenization, and skills for effective change in our [REDI Events Recordings Archive](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/recordings/)
-   Support EDI in your day\-to\-day work, learning environment and workplace culture with our quick\-and\-easy Best Practices documents, with ideas for…
    -   [Communication](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/EDI-ODEAR-Conversation-Framework-2.pdf)
    -   [Inclusive Meetings](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/01/Copy-of-Tips-for-Inclusive-and-Accessible-Meetings-13.pdf)
    -   [Being an Upstander](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/The-5-Ds-of-Bystander-Intervention-6.pdf)
    -   …and many more topics: [REDI Best Practices](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/redi-best-practices/)
