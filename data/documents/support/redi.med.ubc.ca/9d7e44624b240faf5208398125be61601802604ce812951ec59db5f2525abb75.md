*“Equity, diversity, and inclusion are the conditions for attracting and retaining the best and brightest students, staff, and faculty from around the world, and understanding how we best create the environments in which we work, learn, and live\. Inclusion is a commitment for us all, and I look forward to following our progress and learning closely as we work together to achieve the goals in this plan”\.*

*Santa J\. Ono, President and Vice\-Chancellor*

---

The Faculty of Medicine supports the goals of the [Inclusion Action Plan](https://equity.ubc.ca/about/inclusion-action-plan/), encourages the use of the Activating Inclusion tool kit and the adoption of Inclusive Excellence in all work and learning units across the Faculty\.

### Activating Inclusion Toolkit

*UBC Equity and Inclusion Office*

The Activating Inclusion Toolkit is designed to support units across UBC in incorporating equity, diversity, and inclusion into their strategic planning, priority initiatives, and systems and structures more broadly\. The toolkit is an evolving library of tools, guides, and resources that support inclusion planning and implementation\.

[Activating Inclusion Toolkit](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/activating-inclusion-toolkit/)

For more information on the ISAT, visit their webpage or [contact REDI](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about/contact-us/) on how to engage with this tool in your unit\.
