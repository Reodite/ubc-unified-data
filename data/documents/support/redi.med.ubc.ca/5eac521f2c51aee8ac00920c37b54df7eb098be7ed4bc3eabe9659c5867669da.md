Our Plan for Change

---

Culture is the collective mindset and attitude of members of the Faculty of Medicine and it affects how successful we will be in realizing our vision and maintaining excellence\. The Faculty vision states our purpose and goals and Faculty values define our behaviours\. Together they inform our culture\.

The transformation of our culture is a priority commitment\. The transformation is based on creating and sustaining respectful work and learning environments that reflect Faculty values, embody principles of equity, diversity, and inclusion are anti\-racist and support the exercise of human rights for all members of our community\.

Read about the Transforming Culture Initiative launch [here](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/transforming-culture-initiative/)
