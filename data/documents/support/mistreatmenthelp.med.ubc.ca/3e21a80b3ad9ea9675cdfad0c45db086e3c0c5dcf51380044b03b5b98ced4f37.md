### Students &amp; Learners

#### MD Students

Students in the MD Undergraduate Program can access additional support including confidential and personal advising through the following:

-   [MD Student Wellbeing](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/health-wellbeing/)
-   [BC Physician Health Program](https://www.physicianhealth.com/)

#### Postgraduate Medical Residents

Postgraduate medical residents can access additional free and confidential counselling as well as other supports through the following:

-   [Faculty of Medicine Resident Wellness Office](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/)
-   [BC Physician Health Program](https://www.physicianhealth.com/)
-   [VCH Employee and Family Assistance Program](http://www.efap.ca/) which offers 24/7 confidential and personalized support\.

#### Graduate Students &amp; Postdoctoral Trainees

Students and trainees across graduate and postdoctoral programs can access additional resources offered through the following:

-   [Faculty of Medicine Graduate Student Wellbeing](https://grad-postdoc.med.ubc.ca/wellbeing/)
-   [UBC Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/health-wellbeing-safety)

#### All Students in Vancouver and Distributed Sites

-   [Student Support &amp; Resources](https://students.ubc.ca/support): Find one\-on\-one help or explore resources to support you\.
-   [Counselling Services](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services) is the first point of contact for all of your mental health concerns—whether you would like to see a doctor, a counsellor, or get more information about mental health care at UBC and beyond\.
    -   [Here2Talk](https://here2talk.ca/home) offers 24/7 confidential, single\-session counselling services by app, phone or online chat\.
-   [Student Services](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services): Find supports and resources available through Student Services\.

#### All Students in the Okanagan

-   [Student Support &amp; Resources](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/support/): Find one\-on\-one help or explore resources to support you\.
-   [Health &amp; Wellness](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/health-wellness/): Access resources for physical and mental health and wellbeing\.
    -   [Here2Talk](https://here2talk.ca/home) offers 24/7 confidential, single\-session counselling services by app, phone or online chat\.

#### Additional Supports

-   For additional program\-specific support services and resources, please contact your program director/lead for further assistance\.

### Faculty &amp; Staff

UBC offers many health and wellbeing tools and resources to help support you:

-   [UBC Employee and Family Assistance Program](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/benefit-plan-details/employee-and-family-assistance-program)
-   [UBC extended health benefits](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/benefit-plan-details/extended-health-benefits)
-   [UBC mental health supports](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/mental-health)
-   [Immediate, 24/7 mental health support](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/benefit-plan-details/employee-and-family-assistance-program): Call GreenShield Health at 1\-866\-424\-0770 to chat with a counsellor\.
-   [Helping a colleague in distress](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/mental-health/helping-faculty-and-staff-distress)

Visit the [UBC HR Health &amp; Wellbeing](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing) website for a full list of resources and supports\.
