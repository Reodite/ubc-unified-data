> Our UBC Learning Exchange \(LE\) team is made up of a talented and experienced group of people committed to building greater ties between the University of British Columbia and Vancouver communities\. For us, learning is a two\-way street\.

> This means we work to bring about new connections and partnerships that lead to new experiences for community members, students, faculty, and staff—learnings we never could have discovered on our own\.
