## WORK LEARN: APPLY BY MARCH 23\*

**Community\-Based Digital Literacy Assistant:** Work with the UBC Learning Exchange and our community partner, the Downtown Eastside Literacy Roundtable, to tackle the ‘digital divide’\. This role supports community members to learn digital technology through drop\-in tech cafes in a variety of community settings\. $17\.69\-$18\.76/hour\. [Learn more\.](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/index.php?s=jobs&amp;ss=jobs&amp;mode=form&amp;id=7d7ac49cdaa45ac1e012773732de4cba)

**Student Engagement and Orientations Assistant:** Play a key role with the UBC Learning Exchange to support student learning in the Downtown Eastside \(DTES\)\. Help provide orientations and support the facilitation of community\-based experiential learning for fellow UBC students engaging with the DTES community\. $17\.69\-$18\.76/hour\. [Learn more\.](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/index.php?s=jobs&amp;ss=jobs&amp;mode=form&amp;id=d3304fec4b777e07b3573f361c4d321a)

**Animating Connections Assistant:** Work and learn in a dynamic community\-facing program in Vancouver’s Downtown Eastside \(DTES\)\. Empower and support local residents to engage in learning\-oriented activities that develop their interests, capacity and leadership\. The student will draw upon both their own academic strengths and personal interests to design and deliver programming in the form of workshops and special events\. $17\.69\-$18\.76/hour\. [Learn more](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/students/index.php?s=jobs&amp;ss=jobs&amp;mode=form&amp;id=d9636c7dd201343112a6640638dc6443)\.
