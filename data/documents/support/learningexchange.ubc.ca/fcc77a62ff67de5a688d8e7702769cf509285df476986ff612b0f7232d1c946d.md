### The UBC Learning Exchange and Faculty of Music hosted a special night with a student string quartet\.

#### Read about it and see the photos\.  

It’s not every day that the UBC Learning Exchange hosts an intimate evening of music with a string quartet or gets featured in a story in CBC Arts\!  

But that’s exactly what happened because of a new collaboration with UBC’s School of Music through the leadership of Prof\. David Metzer\.   

On April 28, more than 30 people listened to a student string quartet from the school in a special event at the UBC Learning Exchange\.  

The event was featured in CBC Arts and in a story produced by the UBC School of Music\.  

-   CBC Arts: [https://www\.cbc\.ca/arts/ryan\-davis\-learning\-exchange\-dtes\-9\.7178741](https://www.cbc.ca/arts/ryan-davis-learning-exchange-dtes-9.7178741) 

-   UBC School of Music: [https://music\.ubc\.ca/news/a\-night\-of\-music\-at\-the\-learning\-exchange\-ubc\-school\-of\-music\-brings\-students\-and\-the\-downtown\-eastside\-together/](https://music.ubc.ca/news/a-night-of-music-at-the-learning-exchange-ubc-school-of-music-brings-students-and-the-downtown-eastside-together/) 

The night featured a video about, and played music by, Ben Shirley, a Grammy\-nominated composer who returned to music through Los Angeles Street Symphony after experiencing homelessness and hardship\.  

The evening was led by Prof\. Ryan Davis, who helped organize it with Prof\. Metzer, as well as Kathleen Leahy, Director of the Learning Exchange, and Rachael Barton\-Bridges, Animating Connections Coordinator at the Learning Exchange\.
