> The Learning Exchange is a connection point in the Downtown Eastside for both UBC students and faculty\. Here are some of the ways we work with the university community:

[Student learning at the UBC Learning Exchange](https://learningexchangeaug2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/09/student_learning-770x450.jpg)

## Students

Students get involved alongside local residents to learn, support and lead\. Powerful real\-life learning experiences – through volunteering or customized coursework – extend and enrich classroom instruction\. Students gain new perspectives about the Downtown Eastside through their connection with the Learning Exchange and our community partners\.

[How we work with UBC students](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/students/ubc-students/)

## Faculty

We collaborate with UBC faculty to create hands\-on learning experiences that complement coursework\. Our supports can range from embedding experiential learning into existing classes to running entire courses at the Learning Exchange for an immersive, place\-based approach\. We also introduce faculty to our partner community organizations interested in student placements\.

[How we work with UBC faculty](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/students/ubc-faculty/)

## Student Orientation Materials

The UBC Learning Exchange has developed a suite of orientation materials to help students learn about Vancouver’s Downtown Eastside\. A resource for community organizations and UBC faculty, staff and students, the information – which ranges from self\-guided neighbourhood walks to videos where community members talk about the Downtown Eastside – can be customized for individual student experiences\.

[Details about our Student Orientation Materials](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/students/student-orientation-materials/)
