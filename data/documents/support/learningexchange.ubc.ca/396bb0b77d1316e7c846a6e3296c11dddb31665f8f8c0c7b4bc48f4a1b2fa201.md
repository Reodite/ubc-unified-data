### **As a dynamic hub, it’s hard to keep track of all the ways it connects UBC and the Downtown Eastside\. This fun, short video helps explain it\.**

[Embedded content: What is the UBC Learning Exchange?](https://www.youtube.com/embed/j06w0UywUqg?feature=oembed)

**Have you ever wondered what exactly the UBC Learning Exchange does?**

As a multi\-faceted hub, it can be hard to keep track of all the different ways it helps UBC connect with the Downtown Eastside on pressing priorities\.

In the video above you’ll discover the core areas that work together to make the Learning Exchange unique: free community programming, opportunities for students to learn through real\-world experience, and support for community\-based research, as well as free spaces where external groups can meet and collaborate\.

The video also includes personal perspectives from community members, students, and researchers that bring the ideas to life\.

Thanks to everyone who contributed to the video, including Marvin Delorme, Douglas Forrest, Kathleen Leahy, Sean Miles, Dr\. &amp; Elder Roberta Price, Prof\. Lindsey Richardson, Esther Roorda, Louise Schwarz\.
