#### Accessibility

The Learning Exchange has a wheelchair accessible gathering area and washroom on the first floor\. The remainder of the building is only accessible via stairs\. There is no elevator\.

##### Building Entrance

The main entrance is at 612 Main St\.

##### Transit

We are close to a number of bus stops, as well as a 10\-minute walk from the Main Street Skytrain Station\. Plan your trip [here](https://www.translink.ca/trip-planner)\.

##### Bikes

There is a public bike rack on the sidewalk in front of the building\.

##### Parking

Metered street parking is often available near the UBC Learning Exchange building\. There are also a number of paid parkades nearby, such as the [EasyPark\-Lot 7](https://easypark.ca/find-parking/locations-and-lot-information/lot-details/180-keefer-st) at 180 Keefer St\.

##### Washrooms

There are washrooms on every floor\. The first\-floor washroom is wheelchair accessible\.

##### Drinking Water

Drinking water is available from kitchen sinks on the first and third floor, and from a water cooler on second floor\.

##### Telephones

There is a courtesy phone available on the first floor during [Drop\-In hours](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/community-based-programs/drop-in/)\.
