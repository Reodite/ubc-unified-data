> At the Learning Exchange, the success of our work depends on our relationships with community organizations\. Together we share resources to create new connections, new understandings and new experiences that we never could have discovered and delivered on our own\.

[People interacting at the UBC Learning Exchange](https://learningexchangeaug2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/08/le_people-770x450.jpg)

[Access resources for community organizations](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/organizations/access-resources/)

## Community programs

-   The Learning Exchange offers free programs for community residents including workshops and drop\-in activities, both on\-site and elsewhere in the community\.

## Connections to UBC

-   We connect community organizations with UBC students and faculty\. Students gain real\-life learning and faculty develop or support various initiatives in the Downtown Eastside, including community\-based research\.

## Other Resources

-   We have a selection of orientation materials to help students learn about the Downtown Eastside\.
-   Meeting space at the Learning Exchange is available for community organizations\.
