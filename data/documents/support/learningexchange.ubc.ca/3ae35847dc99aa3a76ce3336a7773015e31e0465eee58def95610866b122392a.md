**Where:** UBC Learning Exchange at 612 Main Street

**When:** The Computer Drop\-in Program runs regularly Wednesday and Thursday from 1 p\.m\. to 4:30 p\.m\. Programming also happens on some Tuesday and Friday afternoons\. See [events calendar](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/community-based-programs/event-calendar/) for details\.

**What**: Everyone is welcome to drop in to the Learning Exchange to use our computers or attend any of the activities we offer\. You won’t be on your own – activities are led by members of the local community, UBC students or Learning Exchange staff\. Dates, times and details are in our [event calendar](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/community-based-programs/event-calendar/)\. See below for descriptions of some regular activities\.

[People sitting in chairs in a circle in the UBC Learning Exchange Drop\-In space\. One man has a guitar and is pointing to another person\.](https://learningexchangeaug2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/09/drop-in-marvin.png)

  

## Free Computer Access

Need computer time or a Wi\-Fi connection? No problem – the Learning Exchange provides free public access to computers, Wi\-Fi and high\-speed internet\. Each computer has Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint and photo editing software\. If you have specialty software to install, just ask us and we’ll try to assist\.

Need some help? During Drop\-in hours staff can offer tips on how to use computers\.

## Drop\-in Arts and Cultural Activities

There’s something for everyone at our free arts and cultural activities programs\. Sessions include art workshops and musical events as well as cultural sharing activities such as drum making and salve making\. Many activities happen in collaboration with Elders, Knowledge Keepers and community partners, and are designed to explore new social, cultural, or learning connections\. Activities change often so check the [event calendar](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/community-based-programs/event-calendar/) for details\.

## Referrals

Come to our Drop\-In afternoons or visit our our front desk for referrals and information to other services, facilities, and workshops\. We’re happy to support your next steps\.
