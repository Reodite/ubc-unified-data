#### About the event:

While it may be getting cold outside, preparing for dangerous summer heat in Vancouver’s Downtown Eastside has become a year\-round priority\.  

Please join us **Friday, Nov\. 21** for an afternoon of learning, conversations, and strategizing with community members, community organizations, activists, researchers, students and policymakers about practical heat mitigation priorities in the Downtown Eastside\.  

Hosted by the [CLEAR initiative,](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/community-based-research/climate-equity-action-resilience-clear/) this event builds off feedback from the [Community Climate Justice Forum](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/2025/04/11/five-inspiring-takeaways-from-the-community-climate-justice-forum/) held in May and will feature a lightning round of presentations, moderated discussions, and arts\-based activities focused on identifying immediate actions\.

Learn about the [CLEAR project](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/community-based-research/climate-equity-action-resilience-clear/) and connect with others working for climate justice in Vancouver’s Downtown Eastside\.

#### About CLEAR

The Climate Equity, Action, and Resilience project is a collaboration between a number of Downtown Eastside community organizations, SFU CERi, the UBC Learning Exchange, and UBC Sustainability Hub\. CLEAR aims to increase climate resilience and advance environmental justice by amplifying voices and facilitating more equitable community participation to shape municipal climate action\.
