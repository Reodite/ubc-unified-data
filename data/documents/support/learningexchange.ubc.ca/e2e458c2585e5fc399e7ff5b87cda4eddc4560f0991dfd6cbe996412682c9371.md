> The Learning Exchange supports community\-based research activities\. By bringing together members of the Downtown Eastside community and the University, we can add to the scope and impact of research\.

[Community\-based research at UBC](https://learningexchangeaug2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/09/researchers-770x450.jpg)

## Making connections

We can link community organizations and faculty to facilitate community\-based research and knowledge exchange\.

## Sharing research results

Shared community and university access to research – both academic research findings and community\-generated materials – is a key part of our [Making Research Accessible in the DTES initiative](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/research/making-research-accessible-initiative/)\.

## Space at the Learning Exchange

The Learning Exchange offers a free space that lends itself to research activities while being welcoming to community members\. For more information, please contact [Eliza Ong](mailto:eliza.ong@ubc.ca)\.

Find out more about our [support for researchers](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/research/support-for-researchers/)\.
