> You can learn English with us\. Join our free English conversation sessions and activities\. They are fun and easy – and you can meet new friends\. And, if you want to volunteer as a group facilitator we offer coaching to help build your skills\.

[Learn English at the UBC Learning Exchange](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/files/2018/09/learn_english-770x450.jpg)

## English Conversation sessions

Free conversation sessions are for Canadian citizens, immigrants and refugees who are 19 years of age and older in the Metro Vancouver area who don’t have the means to pay for other learning opportunities\.

#### ARE YOU ELIGIBLE?

Yes, if you are…

-   At least 19 years old
-   Able to attend classes during the workday
-   Not attending another English language program
-   Living in the Lower Mainland
-   A Canadian citizen, permanent resident, refugee, or have a long\-term work permit or study visa
-   Unable to pay for other language learning opportunities

### Learn at a slower pace

There are four levels of conversation sessions, from beginner to intermediate\. We will help find the right session for you\. Small groups of 6\-10 people give everyone the time and opportunity to practice\. Sessions are informal with trained facilitators – members of the local community or UBC students – there to guide you\.

### Choose a session that works for you

We offer in\-person and online sessions once per week\. See our [Event Calendar](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/community-based-programs/event-calendar/) for details\. Each session is about one hour\. Sessions are free but you need to register\. Email [ecp\.info@ubc\.ca](mailto:ecp.info@ubc.ca) or leave a voicemail at 604\-822\-6169 for more information\.

## English Conversation activities

### Learn English with our free, fun activities for seniors

If you like to sing, dance, act or play, we have special activities for you\. You can stay active and improve your English\.

Activities are free but you need to register\. Email [ecp\.info@ubc\.ca](mailto:ecp.info@ubc.ca) or leave a voicemail at 604\-822\-6169 for more information\.

## English Conversation – facilitator opportunities

### Build confidence leading conversation sessions

Do you want to be a facilitator? We can help you develop the skills you need to support language learners at all levels\. No formal language teaching experience is required, just conversational English proficiency\.

If you want to gain leadership abilities in a volunteer setting, contact us to discuss the opportunity further\. Leave a voicemail for us at 604\-822\-6169 or email [spring\.gillard@ubc\.ca](mailto:spring.gillard@ubc.ca)\.
