*Voices UP\!*, a play developed and performed by community members, students, and staff at the UBC Learning Exchange, brought to life stories of the Learning Lab, a dynamic arts\-driven program for Downtown Eastside residents, as part of a program evaluation\.

Wanting to share their stories of making a play together, and inspire others to use theatre in evaluations and other community projects, the cast and creators of *Voices UP\!* decided to create a comic capturing their process\. Just like *Voices UP\!*, this comic was written and drawn collaboratively\. The Voices UP\! Collective hopes it will share the play’s journey with a wide audience\.

This comic was created by: Carolyn Camman, Christopher Cook, Jay, Ken Lee, Andrew Leyland, Suzie O’Shea, Sidney Steel, Sky Thomas, and Teresa Shu\-Tak Wong\.
