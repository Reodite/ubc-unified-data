### Community programs:

-   Drop\-In sessions on Wednesday and Thursday afternoons are closed Dec\. 24 &amp; 25 and Dec\. 31 &amp; Jan\. 1, resuming Wednesday, Jan\. 7\.
-   Tech Cafes are closed from Dec\. 22 to Jan\. 5, resuming, Monday, Jan\. 5\.
-   English Conversation Program sessions are closed from the week of Dec\. 15 to the week of Jan\. 12\.

### Deliveries:

-   No deliveries between Dec\. 22 to Jan\. 6\.

-   Staff are onsite until Thursday, Dec\. 18, and return onsite Tuesday, Jan\. 6\.
