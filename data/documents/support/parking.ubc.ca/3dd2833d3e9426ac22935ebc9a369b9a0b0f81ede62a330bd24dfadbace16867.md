### What should I do if I am trying to buy or redeem a Honk multipack/pass but the system seems to be down? Do I still need to pay for parking?

A parking payment or permit is required whenever parking at UBC\. Feel free to contact our office over the phone should you be experiencing an issue purchasing or redeeming a Honk pass\. You can also make a payment using one of the alternative mobile payment providers or at the Paystations\.

### Can I check online if I have a citation or traffic notice?

Yes, you can check at any time\.

Visit our "Pay a Ticket" page at : [https://ubcparking\.t2hosted\.ca/Account/Portal](https://ubcparking.t2hosted.ca/Account/Portal)\.

Enter your plate info and click "Search"\.  \(make sure you have the correct state or province\)

*\*Valid permit or payment is required at all times for parking\.*

**You may still be notified at a later date by email or mail if a traffic notice is issued using camera enforcement and parking records\. Please ensure payment is in place prior to, or immediately upon, arrival to avoid receiving a Traffic Notice\.**

### How does one receive notification of a Traffic Notice?

There are several ways a Traffic Notice may be issued and communicated:

-   **Notice on Your Vehicle**  
    A physical citation may be placed on your windshield at the time of the violation\.
-   **Email Notification**  
    An initial notice may be sent to an email address on file\.
-   **Mail Notice**  
    A notice may be mailed to the registered owner of the vehicle\.

*\*Parking enforcement may use a combination of the notification methods listed above\.* 

**If you do not see a ticket on your windshield, you may still be notified afterward by email or mail\. Please ensure payment is in place prior to, or immediately upon, arrival to avoid receiving a Traffic Notice\.**

### Can I get a parking ticket from camera enforcement?

Yes\. Automated systems known as “camera enforcement” may issue citations for unpaid parking \(or partial\) using parking records\. These notices are not placed on the windshield; they are emailed or mailed based on system reports and payment records\.

**If you do not see a ticket on your windshield, you may still be notified afterward by email or mail\. Please ensure payment is in place prior to, or immediately upon, arrival to avoid receiving a Traffic Notice\.**

### Can commuting students purchase more than one parking permit?

No\. Commuting students may purchase only one parking permit for the full academic year\.

### Can I cancel my commuting permit and get a refund?

Yes\. You may cancel your permit at any time and receive a refund for the unused portion\.
