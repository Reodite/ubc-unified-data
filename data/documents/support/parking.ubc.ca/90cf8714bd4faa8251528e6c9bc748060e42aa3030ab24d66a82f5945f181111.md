Towed vehicles are held at 2451 East Mall, underneath Thunderbird Residence and can be picked up from 7am to 11pm\. To confirm whether your vehicle has been towed, phone 604\-822\-6786, and select options 1 and then 3\.

To dispute a ticket, [log into or create an account](https://ubcparking.t2hosted.ca/cmn/auth_ext.aspx) and follow the instructions by clicking on Traffic Notice\.

You can pay UBC traffic notices, commonly known as a parking tickets, below\.
