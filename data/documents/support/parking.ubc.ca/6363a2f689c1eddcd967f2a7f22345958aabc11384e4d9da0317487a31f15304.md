### How often are offers sent to resident students?

Permit offers are typically sent weekly, depending on availability\.

### How can I improve my chances of receiving a permit?

Select multiple preferred locations when joining the waitlist\. Consider adding Fairview Underground and/or Thunderbird Parkade to the bottom of you waitlist rankings as these two locations are recommended, and often available until your preferred location becomes available at a later date\. \(If available\)

### What type of permit can resident students purchase?

Resident students are eligible for a monthly renewable permit only\.

### When can I renew my resident permit?

Permits become eligible for renewal on the 21st of each month\. A renewal balance will be added to your parking account, and a reminder email will be sent\.

### What happens if I miss my permit offer?

Expired offers cannot be reissued\. Once expired, the permit space returns to general inventory\. You will again need to add yourself to the waitlist\.

### Are resident student permits refundable?

No\. Resident student permits are non\-refundable\.
