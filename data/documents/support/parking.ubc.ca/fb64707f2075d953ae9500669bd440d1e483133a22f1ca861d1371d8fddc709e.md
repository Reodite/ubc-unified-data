### Do I get a ticket if my electric vehicle finishes charging before I am able to return to it and within the posted time limit?

We acknowledge that the vehicles may take less time to charge than initially anticipated\. However, it is the responsibility of the vehicle owner to manage, plan, and prepare for this possibility and to move their vehicle accordingly\.

### How do I Request Event Parking?

UBC Parking Services works closely with several campus affiliates and non\-affiliates to coordinate traffic and parking logistics for events that occur on campus\. Our Traffic Control and Parking staff ensure that adequate parking is available for guests and that impacts to the campus community are minimized\. To apply for event parking, please contact us at parking\.events@ubc\.ca\. 

Hosting an event at a parking lot? If you're planning to host an event on a parking lot or require the space for an activity other, please a complete an [Outdoor Booking Request Form](http://planning.ubc.ca/vancouver/news-events/outdoor-event-booking/outdoor-booking-request-form)

### How can I use a bike cages and bike lockers?

UBC offers 9 free bike cages and 200 secure bike parking spaces in bike lockers located across campus\. Additionally, UBC’s Vancouver campus has over 9,000 high\-quality outdoor bike parking spaces\.

You'll need to login to Parking's payment portal to make a selection\. 

[Sign in](https://ubcparking.t2hosted.ca/cmn/auth_ext.aspx)

All information about the bike cage and bike locker program, including rates, locations and how to gain access, can be found on the Campus and Community Planning site\.

[Bike Cage Information](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/cycling/bicycle-parking)

### Do I still need to pay for parking if the Paystation is malfunctioning or my preferred mobile payment option is not working?

Yes, a parking payment or permit is required whenever parking at UBC\. We recommend attempting to use an alternative method of payment such as Paystation or one of the available mobile payment providers should your preferred method of payment be temporarily unavailable\.

You can also check our website alert section on the landing page for any outage information, or phone our office for additional guidance or to have a payment made over the phone\. Phone wait times might be longer during outages\.

Have a question? Use the FAQ below, shoot us an email at [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) or give us a call at 604\.822\.6786\.

For event\-related enquiries, please email us at [parking\.events@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.events@ubc.ca?subject=Parking%20and%20Events)\. If you have an event and need parking, please fill out a [request form](https://parking.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/EventsForm.pdf) and email it\.
