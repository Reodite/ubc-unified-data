### Rest easy and use our bike cages and bike lockers

UBC offers 9 free bike cages and 200 secure bike parking spaces in bike lockers located across campus\. Additionally, UBC’s Vancouver campus has over 9,000 high\-quality outdoor bike parking spaces\. ### Staff Access Services

### HONK Mobile App

Faculty/Staff Day Permit or Multi\-pack can be purchased through Honk\.  
To be eligible, faculty and staff members must have both a UBC Parking account and a HONK account registered with the same email address\.  
  
To locate the permit, search for the zone **UBCFSPermit** in your HONK account\.
