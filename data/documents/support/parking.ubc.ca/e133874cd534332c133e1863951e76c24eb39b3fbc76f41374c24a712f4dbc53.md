### How do I get my Emeritus Parking Permit?

Once you received the letter granting you Emeritus status from the Senate, you can e\-mail it to [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) along with your vehicle insurance displaying your name as a driver so we can issue you an Emeritus Parking Permit\.

### Why I can no longer purchase a parking permit for all the 5 parkades?

To better serve the campus community by properly distributing the occupancy among the parking facilities, we have limited the Community Permits to a one parkade\.

### I am a resident student but I don\`t see the location that I would like to park in as an option at the Permit Selection page in my UBC Parking Account\.

UBC resident student parking options are limited and subject to availability\. The options currently visible in your UBC Parking account reflect what is available for purchase at this time\.

For additional parking options through UBC Housing, please visit: [http://vancouver\.housing\.ubc\.ca/applications/fees\-payments/parking/](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/parking/)

Please note that we strongly recommend using all four of your waitlist selections\. Parking areas near or within residence zones are often at capacity and have very limited turnover\. You may wish to include locations such as Fairview Underground or Thunderbird Parkade as your lowest\-ranked waitlist choices\. These can serve as temporary parking options while you wait for availability in your higher\-preference locations\.

### What are the UBC Traffic and Parking Rules?

The UBC Traffic and parking Rules can be found [here](https://parking.ubc.ca/tickets-and-towed-vehicles)

### I am an Acadia resident\. How do I purchase a permit specific to this residence?

For Acadia permit inquiries, please contact [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca?subject=Acadia%20parking%20permits)\.

### Do I have to buy a new permit if I am driving a different vehicle that is not associated with my current permit?

A: No, you may add up to three vehicles to a permit on your Parking Account\. It is recommend to remove a temporary vehicle from your Parking Account as soon as you are no longer driving the vehicle, to avoid possible tickets by future users\.

### What happens if I don’t pay for parking at UBC?

Vehicles parked at UBC are subject to the Traffic and Parking Rules of the University of British Columbia\. If not complied with, vehicles may receive a ticket and be impounded\.

### Is there free parking at UBC?

There is no free parking at UBC Campus\.

### Is there motorcycle parking on campus?

Yes, all parkades have stalls designated for motorcycles\. Get in touch with the Parking office if you wish to purchase a Motorcycle Parking Permit\.

### I have a full\-time permit from another university/campus, like UBCO, SFU or UVic, do I still have to pay for parking?

UBC has reciprocal parking agreements with UBC Okanagan, Simon Fraser University and the University of Victoria\. If you have Faculty, Staff or Emeritus yearly permits from one of these institutions, you can park at UBC for no more than two consecutive days using the permit from your home university\.

Parking with this type of permit is only available at North, Rose Garden, Fraser, Thunderbird, West and Health Sciences Parkades\.

Please forward a copy of your valid full\-time permit or permit purchase receipt to [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) with your license plate number and the dates you will be parking on campus\.
