### How many vehicles can I register under my permit?

Only one vehicle may be registered under a resident permit\.

### How do I update my vehicle license plate?

Please email the Parking Office with your new vehicle license plate information\. \(Our office will not make changes over the phone as request must be formalized in writing\)\.

### Can I change my permit to another parking location?

Yes, but only if you receive a new offer for another location from your waitlist\. Contact the Parking Office once the new offer is received and we can swap your location

### How do I find or arrange accessible parking on campus?

A: Designated accessible parking is available across UBC and reserved for individuals with disabilities that limit mobility\. Vehicles must display a valid SPARC permit and have valid payment or permit to park\. Parking lots with accessible parking stalls can be viewed on the UBC Parking [map](https://parking.ubc.ca/map) by selecting the ‘accessible parking’ filter\.

Faculty or staff looking for more information on Accessible Parking may access [https://hr\.ubc\.ca/health\-and\-wellbeing/workplace\-accessibility/accessible\-parking\-faculty\-and\-staff](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/workplace-accessibility/accessible-parking-faculty-and-staff)

Students needing additional accessible accommodation may reach the Centre for Accessibility at [https://students\.ubc\.ca/about\-student\-services/centre\-for\-accessibility/](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/)

Have a question? Use the FAQ below, shoot us an email at [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) or give us a call at 604\.822\.6786\.

For event\-related enquiries, please email us at [parking\.events@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.events@ubc.ca?subject=Parking%20and%20Events)\. If you have an event and need parking, please fill out a [request form](https://parking.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/EventsForm.pdf) and email it\.
