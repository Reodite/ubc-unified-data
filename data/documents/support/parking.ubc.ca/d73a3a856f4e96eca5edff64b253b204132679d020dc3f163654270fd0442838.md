Students are offered parking permits from waitlists based on availability\. Be sure to add yourself to the waitlists as soon as they becomes available\. Once a permit is offered, students can choose from the available options based on the permit duration and their chosen waitlist location\. Each permit is valid for one designated parkade only and cannot be used at other parking locations on campus\.
