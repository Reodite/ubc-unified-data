#### **Get Involved on Campus**

Home to over 60,000 students and a campus larger than many cities, UBC has a vibrant campus life including an arts and culture district, athletics, and many clubs\. With so many choices, students can learn new skills or sports; discover talents they never knew they had and make friendships that will create memories for a lifetime\.

-   [UBC campus life](https://www.ubc.ca/campus-life/)
-   [Leadership and volunteering](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc-life/getting-involved/leadership-volunteering/)
-   [Arts Co\-op](https://artscoop.ubc.ca/)
-   [Arts Internship](https://students.arts.ubc.ca/involvement/arts-internship-program/)
-   [UBC Work Learn Program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/campus-experiences/work-learn-program)

Student clubs at UBC have been growing strong since the first Glee club was started in 1915\. There are now 370 clubs available to students, so there is a club for nearly everyone’s interests\. Getting involved in clubs or recreation gives you a sense of community and offers a place for new friendships and connections you can take with you long after you’re done with final exams\. Check out exciting events like Storm the Wall, discover intramural sports, a lunchtime workout at the Bird Coop or find a friend to go for a training run for the Great Trek 10K\.

-   [Clubs and societies](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc-life/getting-involved/clubs/)
-   [UBC Recreation](http://www.recreation.ubc.ca/)
