**Distance Library Card** 

Even during your first two years of studies in the Dual Degree, you can access UBC’s Library collections\. The UBC Library has more than six million volumes, and access to a wide range of journals across all research disciplines\. See the [Distance Education eligibility](http://ubccard.ubc.ca/obtaining-a-ubccard/students) information, and [apply directly online](https://services.ubccard.ubc.ca/) at the start of your first year at Sciences Po\.

Upon arrival at UBC for Year 3, you are eligible for a physical UBC Card that will serve as your student card, library card, and many other purposes on campus\.
