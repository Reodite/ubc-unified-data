The UBC Sciences Po Dual Degree program recognizes that your university education is a substantial investment\. Part of this unique degree program is about experiencing two different educational environments\. Not only will you experience two different cultures and learning approaches, but you will find that each institution varies in how tuition is assessed, and how scholarships and financial aid are awarded to students\.

To ensure you have the smoothest transition towards the start of your degree, and also during your mid\-degree transition, it’s important to know how much each year of your degree will cost, and the financial implications this may have for you and your family\.  Both Sciences Po and UBC offer many resources to help with financial planning, and offer support through scholarships, bursaries, and other assistance programs\.
