Both Sciences Po and UBC offer many resources to help with financial planning and budgeting\. To ensure a smooth transition at the start of your degree, and between the two institutions, use the below resources to plan ahead\!

-   [**University of British Columbia**](https://sciencespo.ubc.ca/tuition-financial-aid/financial-planning-resources/#strongUniversityofBritishColumbiastrong-1)

Upon arrival at UBC in July of Year 3, each student will be assigned a dedicated [Enrolment Services Professional \(ESP\)](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-professionals-esp) to help with things like paying tuition and building a financial plan\. ESPs are your first point of contact for any kind of financial help, and are with you from your arrival at UBC until the day you graduate\.
