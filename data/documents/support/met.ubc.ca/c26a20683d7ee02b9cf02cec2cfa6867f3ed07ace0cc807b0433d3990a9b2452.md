## MET Academic Advisor

All questions regarding academic work \(for example, about course transfer credits, registration planning, or your ideas for an [ETEC 580](https://met.ubc.ca/courses/etec-580/) project\) should be addressed to the MET Academic Advisor at [met\.advising@ubc\.ca](mailto:met.advising@ubc.ca)\.

The current MET Academic Advisor is [Dr\. Rachel Horst](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/people/rachel-horst/)
