## Links – Programs

-   **[The Sir Isaac Newton Prize Exam](https://uwaterloo.ca/sir-isaac-newton-exam/):** A physics exam targeted at high school students\.
-   **[GEERing UP\! UBC Engineering &amp; Science for Kids](http://www.geeringup.apsc.ubc.ca/index.php):** Host various events and outreach activities for those living in the GVRD\. More information is available at their website\.
-   **[GIRLsmarts4tech](http://www.cs.ubc.ca/girlsmarts4tech/index.html):** A fun program from the Department of Computer Science at UBC for girls in grades 6\-7 to come learn about problem solving and computers\.
-   **[Science World’s “Science and Innovators in Schools” program](https://outreach.phas.ubc.ca/resources/external-links/_wp_link_placeholder)**: *Scientists and Innovators in Schools \(SIS\)*, is a volunteer\-based program with the goal of inspiring students through exciting, interactive presentations by STEAM professionals\. The *SIS* program is offered free of charge to Grades K\-12 everywhere in BC, through online and in\-person sessions\.

## Links – Educational Websites

-   **[Scale of the Universe](http://htwins.net/scale2/)** An interactive model which lets one see the scale of various objects in the Universe compared to each other, and tells you more information about them\.
-   **[Stellarium](http://www.stellarium.org/)** Free, downloadable virtual planetarium which lets one see the night sky as it would be seen using a telescope\.
-   **[MIT Game Lab](http://gamelab.mit.edu/games/)** Educational games in a myriad of topics produced by MIT’s game lab\. Includes: **[A Slower Speed of Light](http://gamelab.mit.edu/games/a-slower-speed-of-light/)\.** A game that simulates a world where the speed of light is gradually lowered, and shows what relativistic effects we would see in reality\. Very computer intensive\.
-   **[Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/)** Contains a huge selection of videos on many different topics ranging from biology to economics\. Great for self study\.
-   **[KDE Education Project](http://edu.kde.org/)** A website with a large variety of freely downloadable educational software\. Includes: **[KGeography](http://userbase.kde.org/KGeography)\.** A game that lets you browse the maps of the world, including each nation’s flag and capital, and then quizzes you\.
-   **[Kerbal Space Program](https://kerbalspaceprogram.com/)** A game that lets you simulate and manage your own space program\. The demo version is free to play\.
-   **[GeoGebra](http://www.youtube.com/user/GeoGebraChannel)** A YouTube channel that includes geometry, spreadsheet and algebra examples from elementary to university level\.
-   **[Medical Alert Advice](https://www.medicalalertadvice.com/resources/accelerometer-applications/)** An article with information about accelerometers and their many applications
-   **[GeekWrapped Blog](https://www.geekwrapped.com/blog)** A blog with science articles and guides for things like telescopes, books, and podcasts\.

## Elementary School \(pre\-K to Gr 6\)

-   **[Tux Paint](http://www.tuxpaint.org/)** Downloadable drawing program for young children\. Simple and full of fun effects\.
-   [**Science Girls Squad**](https://science-girls-squad.com/) A downloadable series of adventure books featuring girls exploring cutting\-edge science and technology, like artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and robotics\.
-   **[GCompris](http://gcompris.net/-en-)** Downloadable software containing a wide variety of educational activities and games\.
-   **[Loom](http://store.steampowered.com/app/32340/)** An interactive game with a good story, Loom is a good program to help train musical note recognition\.
-   **[Lawrence Hall of Science](http://www.lawrencehallofscience.org/kidsite/)** A website that has a variety of apps and games to help children explore and discover the physical world\.
-   **[Algebra for Kids](https://phas-outreach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/02/algebra-1-Jess-Brewer.pdf)** A set of beginner’s algebra notes created by Jess Brewer\.
-   [**Dragonbox**](http://www.dragonboxapp.com/) An app that teaches young children algebra \(without them realizing it\)\. The 5\+ version is recommended by a faculty member, although she hasn’t tested the 12\+ version with her children yet\.

## Middle and High School \(Gr 7 to Gr 12\)

-   **[Science Buddies](http://www.sciencebuddies.org/)** A website which contains many ideas and guides for various science projects\.
-   **[**Test Tube Games**](http://testtubegames.com/index.html)** A collection of flash games dealing with topics such as particle physics and special relativity\.
-   **[The Particle Adventure](http://www.particleadventure.org/)** An interactive tour of particle physics\. Introduces topics such as quarks, neutrinos, antimatter and accelerators\.
-   **[CERN Physics](http://home.web.cern.ch/about/physics)** A series of articles from CERN explaining various topics in modern physics\.

## University Level and Above

-   **[Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics](http://www.perimeterinstitute.ca/video-library)** Provides many videos and public lectures on advanced physics\.
-   **[PhET](https://phet.colorado.edu/)**: Collection of applets that demonstrate physics and math\.
-   **[MIT OpenCourseWare](http://ocw.mit.edu/index.htm)** Huge database of free online college courses, many complete with full lectures and problem sets\.
-   **[Udacity](https://www.udacity.com/)** Interactive college classes\. Join classes, watch videos, participate in activities and learn about a variety of different subjects\!
