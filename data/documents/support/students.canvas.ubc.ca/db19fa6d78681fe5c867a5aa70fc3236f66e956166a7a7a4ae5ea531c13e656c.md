Need help with Canvas? Help is available\!

## Technical support

If you encounter technical issues while using Canvas, please contact the UBC Information Technology Service Centre for assistance, using their [online form](http://web.it.ubc.ca/forms/isf/)\. Students who are enrolled in Distance Learning courses should refer to their course syllabus to determine if any specialized Canvas support is available to them\.

## Canvas resources

The [Canvas Student Guide](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/canvas-student-guide/?login) provides easy\-to\-follow, detailed instructions on how to do almost anything in Canvas\.

The [Canvas Video Guide](https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-3891#jive_content_id_Students) shows you how to submit assignments, check your grades, and more\.

Check out our [FAQ page](https://students.canvas.ubc.ca/faq/) for answers to questions about Canvas that are most frequently asked by UBC students\.

For more information about the technologies used at UBC, check out the [Keep Learning](https://keeplearning.ubc.ca/) website which compiles resources to help you learn effectively online\.

## Canvas &amp; Privacy

Protecting students’ privacy in learning technologies like Canvas is a top priority at UBC\. To learn more about privacy policies and how information is managed with Canvas, please see the [Canvas &amp; Privacy FAQ](http://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/canvas/privacy/)\. If you have any additional questions related to Canvas and privacy, please contact the [Learning Technology Hub](https://elearning5.sites.olt.ubc.ca/support/lt-hub/)\.
