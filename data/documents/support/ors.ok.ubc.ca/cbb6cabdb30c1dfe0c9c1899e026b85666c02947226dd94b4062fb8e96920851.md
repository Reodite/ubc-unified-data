### Program Description

The Pritchard Graduate Student Grant Program supports training and research activities for graduate students studying in disciplines related to medicine and health at UBCO, with a preference for activities that are clinically focused and support collaboration with the Interior Health Authority\. Applications must include a UBCO faculty member and a clinical collaborator\.

[Full Program Terms](https://research.ok.ubc.ca/funding-support/internal-funding/#pritchard)
