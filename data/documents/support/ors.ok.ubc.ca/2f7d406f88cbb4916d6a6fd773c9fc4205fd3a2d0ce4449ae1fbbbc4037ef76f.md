### Program Description

This funding opportunity aims to unite interdisciplinary research teams to deepen the biological and mechanistic understanding of cancer etiology, genesis, and risk factors\. It focuses on host, lifestyle, environmental, social, and other influences, with the goal of identifying novel targets and strategies for cancer prevention, risk reduction, and early detection\.

CIHR will be hosting webinars to support participants with the requirements of this funding opportunity\. Information on webinar dates, times, and registration is available at: [https://www\.cihr\-irsc\.gc\.ca/e/45096\.html](https://www.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/45096.html)
