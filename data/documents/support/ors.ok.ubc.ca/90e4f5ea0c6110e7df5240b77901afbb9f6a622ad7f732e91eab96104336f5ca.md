Applicants at UBC’s Okanagan campus should return completed applications to the Office of Research Services for review\. Please ensure that you have read the guidelines accompanying each application form\.

### **Study Leave Research Grant**

The Study Leave Research Grant \(SLRG\) provides Revenue Canada\-approved tax savings for applicants for study leave\. Please ensure you review the guidelines accompanying the application form\.

-   [Study Leave Research Grant Application](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/174/2025/09/Study-Leave-Research-Grant-Application_Fillable.pdf)
-   [Study Leave Research Grant Guidelines](https://ors.ubc.ca/funding-opportunities/internal-funding-opportunities)

### **Salary Research Grant**

The Salary Research Grant allows scholars at UBC to receive a research grant in lieu of salary during periods other than study leave\.

-   [Salary Research Grant Application](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/174/2025/09/Salary-Research-Grant-Application_Fillable.pdf)
-   [Salary Research Grant Guidelines](https://ors.ubc.ca/funding-opportunities/internal-funding-opportunities)

### **Revenue Canada Interpretive Bulletins**

Researchers should consult [Revenue Canada S5\-F1\-C1](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax/income-tax-folios-index/series-5-international-residency/folio-1-residency/income-tax-folio-s5-f1-c1-determining-individual-s-residence-status.html): ‘Determination of an Individual’s Residence Status’ Income Tax Folio S1\-F2\-C3: Scholarships, Research Grants and Other Education Assistance or [Revenue Canada S1\-F2\-C3](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax/income-tax-folios-index/series-1-individuals/folio-2-students/income-tax-folio-s1-f2-c3-scholarships-research-grants-other-education-assistance.html): ‘Scholarships, Research Grants and Other Education Assistance to determine regarding tax implications and other Canada Revenue Agency Requirements for Study Leave and Salary Research Grants\.
