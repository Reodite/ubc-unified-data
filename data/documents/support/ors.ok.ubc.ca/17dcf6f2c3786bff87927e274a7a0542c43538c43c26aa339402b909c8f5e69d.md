The Office of Research Services \(ORS\) works collaboratively with UBC Okanagan researchers to help develop, fund and manage their research programs at every stage of the research life cycle\.

**PLEASE NOTE AN IMPORTANT CHANGE TO RPIF SUBMISSIONS:**

The ORS internal deadline time frame has changed from two to five business days prior to the sponsor deadline\.

## research support

Our experienced and dedicated team is available to help researchers navigate the various steps involved in applying for, securing and managing research funds at UBC Okanagan\.

### **How Can We Help?**

We provide assistance with grant development and submission, research account setup, and ensuring due process for ethics and other aspects of institutional and funding agency compliance\.

[Image](https://ors-new.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/174/2022/04/Research_Digest.jpg)

The **UBC Research Digest** is a monthly newsletter summarizing funding opportunities, deadlines, news and events curated from the research support service units on both campuses\.
