All applications to the Discovery Grant competition \(including the Subatomic Physics program\) and the Research Tools &amp; Instruments \(RTI\) competition must be electronically submitted via NSERC’s Research Portal\. Applications should be prepared according to NSERC’s [Discovery Grant](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/discovery-grants-individual-program) and [RTI](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/research-tools-and-instruments-grants-program) program guidelines \.

*As of May 1* *\[st\]**, researchers applying to this competition must comply with new and updated Government of Canada research security requirements\.* To understand how Research Security may impact your grant submission, including any required forms, see the [UBCO ORS Research Security](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/proposal-development-submission/research-security/) website\.

##### Submitting to NSERC

To submit a full application, Discovery Grant \(DG\) applicants must submit an NOI via the research portal by August 1 \(or the next business day if August 1 falls on a weekend or holiday\)\. Applicants submit NOIs directly to NSERC \(they are not forwarded by the Office of Research Services\)\.

In order to obtain institutional endorsement and prior to submitting in the portal, applicants must send an RPIF that is filled out and signed by the PI, Head and Dean \(as appropriate\), along with a final budget and required application pages \(see below\) to ResearchOffice\.ubco@ubc\.ca

### Deadlines: Discovery Grant and Research Tools &amp; Infrastructure Grants

**Discovery Grant full application**

To submit a full application, Discovery Grant \(DG\) applicants must submit an NOI via the research portal by August 1 \(or the next business day if August 1 falls on a weekend or holiday\)\. Applicants submit NOIs directly to NSERC \(they are not forwarded by the Office of Research Services\)\.

**Full application deadlines:**

-   UBCO internal deadline: **5 business days ahead of the Sponsor Deadline**
-   Applicant’s e\-submission deadline: Your final application should be submitted via the Research Portal on **October 29\.**
-   Sponsor deadline: **November 2** 

**Research Tools and Instruments \(RTI\)**

-   UBC internal deadline: **5 business days ahead of the Sponsor Deadline**
-   Applicant’s e\-Submission Deadline: Your final application should be submitted via the Research Portal on **October 23\.**
-   Sponsor deadline: **October 26** 

*While ORS makes every effort to submit all applications on time, applications submitted after the UBC internal deadline will not receive a guarantee of administrative review from ORS, which includes confirmation for application completeness and compliance\. As ORS must forward each application to NSERC after the applicant’s final submission, we cannot guarantee that applications submitted after noon on the sponsor deadline day will be forwarded\.* 

### Application Preparation

Your application must be prepared and submitted using the [Research Portal](https://portal-portail.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/s/login.aspx) and NSERC’s version of the [Canadian Common CV](https://ccv-cvc.ca/)\. [Instructions](http://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/ResearchPortal-PortailDeRecherche/Instructions-Instructions/index_eng.asp) can be found on the NSERC web site \(under “Professors”\)\.

Please select “The University of British Columbia” as the Administering Institution \(do not select “Technical University of British Columbia”\)\.

### UBC Institutional Approval Requirement

Use the [Research Project Information Form](https://research.ubc.ca/support-resources/forms-tools-resources/research-project-information-form) \(RPIF\) to obtain the required internal signatures:

-   UBC Applicant \(Principal Investigator\)
-   Department Head or Director of School or Institute
-   Dean or designate

**Signatures required for co\-applicants**

A co\-applicant signature page, signed by the relevant Department /Unit Head/Dean is required when the co\-applicant is from a different UBC Department/School than the applicant\. Please use the UBC Internal Co\-Applicant signature page, which can be found on the [UBCO ORS forms page](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/forms/)\. Note that a physical signature is not required from the co\-applicants on NSERC’s RTI forms\.

External authorized institution signature is required for co\-applicants outside of UBC\. Please use the ORS [external co\-applicant signature page](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/174/2024/02/External-Co-Applicant-Form_UBCO.pdf)\.

If you are a co\-applicant on a grant application initiated by a PI at another institution, you must still submit a copy of the application with required UBC signatures for ORS review and approval\.

### Submit Required Pages to UBCO ORS

Applicants must submit a draft application and RPIF to the Office of Research Services \([ResearchOffice\.UBCO@ubc\.ca](mailto:ResearchOffice.UBCO@ubc.ca)\) for institutional approval\.

As the ORS processes a large number of applications during NSERC competition periods, the internal deadline is intended to ensure that all applications receive administrative review prior to institutional support / signature \(by ORS\)\. Please ensure you have collected ALL required signatures prior to submitting to ORS \(the full UBCO signature policy can be found [here](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/policies/signature-policy/)\)\. You may submit the entire application if you wish, but the following are required:

-   A signed [UBC Research Project Information Form \(RPIF\)](https://research.ubc.ca/support-resources/forms-tools-resources/research-project-information-form)
-   Complete co\-applicant [forms](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/forms/) if you have any internal or external co\-applicants
-   Sensitive Research and Technology [Attestations](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/proposal-development-submission/research-security/) for all named researchers, if required
-   Identification page \(page 1 of the application\)
-   Supplements/Joint Initiatives \(if applicable\)
-   Summary of Proposal Page
-   Activity Details page \(this page displays the certification requirements\)
-   Proposed Expenditures and budget justification pages
-   Quotations if applicable \(per RTI guidelines, provide two \(2\) recent quotations for any items or any systems costing more than $25,000 before taxes\. If the required number of quotations cannot be submitted, you must provide a justification in the Budget Justification section\.\)

### Submit Online

Upon receiving notice by the ORS to do so, submit your full application online using the Research Portal and NSERC CCV by the portal e\-submission deadline\. To do this, the applicant must complete and link all the forms as described in the NSERC instructions\. First “Verify” to ensure the application is complete\. Then click “Submit” to submit your application to ORS for online approval\.

### ORS approves and forwards your application

After you submit, ORS will approve your application on the Research Portal by NSERC’s deadline\. ORS has read\-only access to your application and cannot make changes\. If the application is not approved, ORS will return it to you for changes\.  Note that at this stage, ORS is unable to examine the application carefully for any problems\.

Once ORS has approved your application, the status on the NSERC Research Portal will change from “Received by Administrator” to “Received by Agency\.” ORS is no longer required to physically sign applications submitted through the NSERC Research Portal\.

Material or revisions received under separate cover \(before or after the deadline date\) will not be accepted by NSERC\.

For your information, the definition of each application status on the NSERC on\-line system is below:

-   In Progress: Application created but not yet completed\.
-   Returned: Application returned to the applicant by the administrator \(research administrator, scholarship liaison officer, liaison officer\) for required changes\.
-   Received by Administrator: Application completed and submitted by the applicant to the administrator at the university, college, CÉGEP or not\-for\-profit organization\.
-   Received by Agency: Application received by NSERC\.
-   Deleted: Application removed from applicant’s account\.

For more information, see NSERC’s [Research Portal – Frequently Asked Questions](http://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/ResearchPortal-PortailDeRecherche/FAQ-FAQ/ResearchPortal-PortailDeRecherche_eng.asp)
