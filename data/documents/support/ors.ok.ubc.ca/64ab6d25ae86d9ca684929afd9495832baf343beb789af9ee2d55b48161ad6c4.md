### Program Description

CIHR Strategic grants provide support for a specific topic or health challenge for researchers to explore/address\. Strategic grants are sponsored by the [13 Institutes](https://cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/9466.html) of CIHR and, to an extent, by CIHR’s [initiatives](https://cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/50077.html)\.

Several Strategic grant programs are launched through [ResearchNet](https://www.researchnet-recherchenet.ca/rnr16/LoginServlet?language=E), with award amounts ranging from thousands to millions of dollars\. Some Strategic grants are well planned in advance as huge, multi\-year initiatives, while others are launched immediately in reaction to a national or worldwide crisis\. Other Strategic grants may recur on annual basis\. The [Catalyst](https://www.researchnet-recherchenet.ca/rnr16/srch.do?all=1&amp;search=true&amp;org=CIHR&amp;sort=program&amp;masterList=true&amp;view=currentOpps&amp;language=E), [Operating](https://www.researchnet-recherchenet.ca/rnr16/srch.do?all=1&amp;search=true&amp;org=CIHR&amp;sort=program&amp;masterList=true&amp;view=currentOpps&amp;language=E), [Team](https://www.researchnet-recherchenet.ca/rnr16/srch.do?all=1&amp;search=true&amp;org=CIHR&amp;sort=program&amp;masterList=true&amp;view=currentOpps&amp;language=E), and [Planning and Dissemination](https://www.researchnet-recherchenet.ca/rnr16/srch.do?all=1&amp;search=true&amp;org=CIHR&amp;sort=program&amp;masterList=true&amp;view=currentOpps&amp;language=E) Grants are examples of common strategic grant programs\.

**CIHR TEAM &amp; LARGE NETWORK GRANTS**

Team &amp; Large Network Grants support collaborative research teams on a major research project\. Given the scale and complexity of the research and group, Team &amp; Large grants typically have lengthy application timelines\.

**CIHR CATALYST GRANTS**

The Catalyst Grant provides seed money to support or kickstart a new research initiative which represents the first step towards pursuing more comprehensive funding opportunities\.

**CIHR OPERATING GRANTS**

The Operating Grant contributes to the creation, dissemination and use of health\-related knowledge, and to help develop research capacity, by supporting original, high\-quality projects\.

**CIHR PLANNING &amp; DISSEMINATION GRANTS**

The Planning &amp; Dissemination Grant supports pre\-project planning and/or knowledge dissemination activities\. The competition typically run twice per year \(Spring and Fall\)\.
