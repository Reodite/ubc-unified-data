### Program Description

Funding for the Project Grant is made available through the annual SSHRC Institutional Award/General Revenue Fund\. This award is specifically designed to promote research in the Humanities and Social Sciences \(HSS\) and to offset research costs up to $7,000\. The main goals of the Explore/Exchange Research Grant program are to: support SSH researchers with modest funding requirements at any stage of their career, to allow researchers to develop new research projects or conduct pilot work, to enable researchers to hire students to participate in research projects, to support the organization of small\-scale knowledge mobilization activities such as workshops and seminars, and to allow researchers to attend or present their research at scholarly conferences and other similar venues\.
