### Program Description

Alliance International Collaboration grants support joint projects between Canadian and international university researchers\. Projects must demonstrate strong impact and benefits for Canada, as well as valuable training opportunities for Canadian students and postdoctoral fellows\.

The Canadian and international teams must jointly prepare an application that clearly describes both teams and their respective roles in the project\. The international collaborator must submit the application to their national funding agency for peer review and funding consideration\. If successful, the Canadian team may apply to NSERC for funding to support Canadian research activities no later than 120 days after the international collaborator receives the official international award letter\.

The Canadian and international projects must start at roughly the same time; the duration of the international grant must be at least equivalent to the Canadian portion of the project\. NSERC expects a balanced level of participation from both the Canadian and international teams\. Each team must play a substantive and integral role in the project, and neither team may limit its involvement to a marginal or peripheral contribution\.

### How to Apply

Submission guidelines for Alliance Grants\.

Canadian researchers who were preparing a collaboration with their international counterparts but were unable to submit an application to NSERC by the May 20, 2025 deadline may still apply, provided the corresponding international award was granted between that date and the program’s reopening and the international project remains viable\. For these applications, the 120\-day submission rule does not apply\. If you are in this situation, contact NSERC to determine whether you may submit an application for the November 25, 2026 deadline\.
