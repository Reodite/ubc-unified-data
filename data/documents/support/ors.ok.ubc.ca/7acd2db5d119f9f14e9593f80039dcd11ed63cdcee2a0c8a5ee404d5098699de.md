The Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council \(SSHRC\) is the federal research funding agency that promotes and supports research and training in the humanities and social sciences\.

##### Submitting to SSHRC

All SSHRC grant applications \(except the Insight Development Grant\) must be electronically submitted using the [SSHRC’s online system](http://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca/)\. Please follow SSHRC’s application procedures described in the [program outlines\.](https://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca/funding-financement/index-eng.aspx)

In order to obtain institutional endorsement, applicants must 1\) submit their completed application in the SSHRC web portal, download a PDF copy of that grant, and 2\) send an RPIF that is filled out and signed by the PI, Head and Dean \(or designates\) to: [ResearchOffice\.ubco@ubc\.ca](mailto:ResearchOffice.ubco@ubc.ca)

Office of Research Services forward all SSHRC applications on applicants’ behalf\. ORS cannot guarantee that applications received after the ORS RPIF deadline will be forwarded\.

### Application Preparation

The application must be prepared using the [SSHRC online system](https://webapps.nserc.ca/SSHRC/faces/logon.jsp?lang=en_CA) for most SSHRC grants, or, for Insight Development Grants, the [SSHRC Research Portal](https://portal-portail.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/s/login.aspx)\.

### UBC Institutional Approval Requirement

Use the [Research Project Information Form](https://research.ubc.ca/support-resources/forms-tools-resources/research-project-information-form) \(RPIF\) to obtain the required internal signatures:

-   UBC Applicant \(Principal Investigator\)
-   Department Head or Director of School or Institute
-   Dean or designate

Note that **any internal UBC cash contributions** included in your application budgeting should be detailed on the RPIF final page, including “worktag” or source account number\.

**Internal UBC Co\-Applicants:** electronic acceptance of Applicant’s invitation to participate in the application meets SSHRC signature requirements\. However, the UBC\-O researchers need Head/Faculty to review and sign a co\-applicant signature page when the co\-applicant is from a different UBC Department/School\. The [**UBC Okanagan Co\-Applicant signature**](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/forms/) page can be found on the Forms page\.

**External Co\-Applicants on UBC submissions**: electronic acceptance of Applicant’s invitation to participate in the application meets SSHRC signature requirements\.

**Co\-Applicants** on a SSHRC application being initiated by a Principal Investigator at another institution need to submit a PDF of the final copy the application, and RPIF, for review to ORS even though the submission is going forward from another institution\.

### Submit RPIF to Office of Research Services

In addition ensuring the **RPIF has been sent to** [ResearchOffice\.UBCO@ubc\.ca](mailto:ResearchOffice.UBCO@ubc.ca%20)  by the UBCO Internal Deadline \(see above\), Applicants must submit their full application in the SSHRC portal by noon on the internal deadline date, and download a PDF of the submitted application, and forward it to the email address above\.

To do this, you must complete and attach all the forms as described in the SSHRC Grants web application instructions\. If applicable, ensure that all co\-applicants and collaborators have submitted their “Accept Invitation Form” and have included all relevant attachments\. “Validate” all the sections of your application\*

***If you have already submitted it in the web portal and need to make a change, you will need to have it returned to you by ORS and then re\-submit\.***

The internal deadline is intended to ensure that all applications receive administrative review prior to institutional support / signature \(by ORS\)\. Please ensure you have collected required [internal signatures](http://ors.ok.ubc.ca/signature-policy/) on your Research project information form prior to submitting to ORS\.

### Submit application in the SSHRC Portal

Following notification of Institutional Approval from the Office of Research Services via email:

Submit your **full application** online no later than **noon \(pacific time\)** on the SSHRC sponsor deadline date\. To do this, you must complete and attach all the forms as described in the SSHRC web application instructions\. If applicable, ensure that all co\-applicants and collaborators have submitted their “Accept Invitation Form” and have included all relevant attachments\. “Verify” all the sections of your application in the web portal\.\*

**Please note that, for Partnership stream grants, your Partner Organization\(s\) will also need to complete their invitation\(s\) in a separate web portal, including uploading a Letter of Engagement and detailing their contributions \(cash and/or in\-kind\)\.**

*If you have already submitted it in the web portal and need to make a change, you will need to have it returned to you by ORS and then re\-submit\.  
*

**Change to process as of March 1, 2023:**

If all SSHRC and UBC requirements are met, ORS will forward your application by the SSHRC submission deadline\. **Note: ORS cannot make change to applications in SSHRC portals\.**

If the application requires changes to align with UBC or SSHRC policies, ORS will return it to you for changes\.

### ORS Reviews and Forwards to SSHRC

ORS has read\-only access to your application and cannot make changes\. If the application is not approved, ORS will return it to you for changes\.

Following successful completion of compliance review of the RPIF and application, ORS will forward the application on your behalf to SSHRC\.

You will know when your application has been submitted by ORS to SSHRC when the application on the SSHRC on\-line system status says to “Received by Agency”\. Material or revisions received under separate cover \(before or after SSHRC’s deadline\) will not be accepted by SSHRC\.

For your information, the definition of each application status on the SSHRC on\-line system is:

-   In Progress: application is being prepared
-   Verified/Validated: application or applicant page is complete and validated
-   **Submitted: application is submitted to ORS by the applicant\. ORS can view the application, including partner and co\-applicant information and CVs, return it to the applicant or forward it to SSHRC\.**
-   Returned: application requires changes and is returned to the applicant by ORS\. The applicant receives a system\-generated e\-mail\. ORS can no longer view the application\.
-   Forwarded: application is approved and forwarded to SSHRC by ORS
-   Received: application has been received by SSHRC and is ready for processing
