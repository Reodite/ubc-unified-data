### Program Description

Health Research BC invites BC\-based researchers and eligible health system staff to apply for the Engage Awards\. This funding opportunity is intended to support collaboration between researchers and research users, with the goal of advancing research and the application of research evidence to address challenges aligned with Health Research BC’s priority themes\.

**Health Research BC Priority Themes**

Proposed activities must align with at least one of the following themes:

-   **Population Aging**: Causes, prevention, treatment, and care of age\-related conditions; and social determinants and supports that promote healthy aging and quality of life\.
-   **Climate Change &amp; Health**: Impacts of climate change on health and the health system, and the health system’s impact on the environment\.
-   **Health Human Resources**: Challenges affecting BC’s health workforce and system capacity\.
-   **Public Health Emergencies**: Prevention, preparedness, response, recovery, and interventions that strengthen community resilience\.

Applicants must clearly demonstrate alignment with at least one theme\. Applications will be assessed for relevance\.
