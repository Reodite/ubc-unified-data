### Program Description

-   The Department of National Defence \(DND\) and the Canadian Armed Forces have launched four new challenges to support innovative solutions to key defence questions\. The four challenges are as follows:
    
    -   [Stay connected: Seamless transitions between mobile broadband networks](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/dba09701027bfc7de855dd49b53a807b/08fc64b8d24cb47f34867c0bd3d0b312/219baacd1858775e26d437936a717448)
    -   [Unmasking biothreats: Identifying biological agents in the field](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/dba09701027bfc7de855dd49b53a807b/87cce9d25153d2b829567c0bd3d0b8b0/219baacd1858775e26d437936a717448)
    -   [Don’t skip a beat: Medical diagnostics and sensing for combat operations](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/dba09701027bfc7de855dd49b53a807b/b108698c3b308066ca767c0bd3d0bf21/219baacd1858775e26d437936a717448)
    -   [Extended communication coverage: Exploiting connections of Earth and space systems](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/click/go/dba09701027bfc7de855dd49b53a807b/1188641c4426394bfbb67c0bd3d0c6e9/219baacd1858775e26d437936a717448)
    
    Funding Ranges:
    
    |  |  |  |  |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | **TRL/SRL Level** | **Funding Available** | **Project Length** | **Milestones** |
    | 1 to 3 | Up to $250,000 | 6 months | 2 |
    | 4 to 5 | Up to $1,500,000 | 12 months | 2 to 4 |
    | 6 to 9 | Up to $5,000,000 | 24 months | 2 to 8 |
