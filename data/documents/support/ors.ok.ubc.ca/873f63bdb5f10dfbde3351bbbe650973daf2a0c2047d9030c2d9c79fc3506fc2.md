### Program Description

[Destination Horizon Grants](https://sshrc-crsh.canada.ca/en/funding/opportunities/destination-horizon-grants/2026/competition.aspx) support researchers affiliated with eligible Canadian postsecondary institutions to build capacity, foster existing partnerships, and further develop networks and/or consortia with European Union \(EU\) and other [associated countries](https://research-and-innovation.ec.europa.eu/strategy/strategy-research-and-innovation/europe-world/international-cooperation/association-horizon-europe_en?prefLang=fr)’ researchers, with the ultimate goal of applying to [Horizon Europe](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/horizon-europe)—Pillar II calls for proposals\.

**Value:** $15K for 1 year from SSHRC; **$15K matched funds provided by the VPRI office** 

**Institutional Requirements:** Only **3 submissions per institution** are allowed per competition

-   **An institutional letter is required** to accompany the SSHRC submission, confirming the $15K matching funds for each submitted application\.

[Full Program Terms](https://sshrc-crsh.canada.ca/en/funding/opportunities/destination-horizon-grants.aspx)

### How to Apply

Please consult [Internal Selection Guidelines](https://sparc.ubc.ca/announcements/ubc-internal-selection-deadline-sshrc-destination-horizon-september-4) for application requirements and submission procedures\. Please send proposals to [kellyl\.cubbon@ubc\.ca](mailto:kellyl.cubbon@ubc.ca) **by 12pm on the internal deadline of September 4\.**

**\*UPDATE\*** as of July 2026: To align with how SSHRC selects proposals for funding in this competition, UBC’s internal selection process will consist of a randomized lottery facilitated by SPARC staff\.
