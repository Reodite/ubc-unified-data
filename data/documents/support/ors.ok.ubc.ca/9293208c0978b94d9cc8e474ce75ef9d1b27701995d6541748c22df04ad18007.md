### Program Description

Through its annual budget, the Office of the Vice\-Principal, Research &amp; Innovation has allocated a limited amount of funding that can provide financial assistance to the UBCO faculty research community, outside of the current internal funding opportunities\. Contingent on the level of funding, the VPRI may offer the following types of support: matching funds, letters of support, cost\-sharing funds \(events/conferences/workshops/seminars\) and other funding requests

[Full Program Terms](https://research.ok.ubc.ca/funding-support/internal-funding/#vpri_support)
