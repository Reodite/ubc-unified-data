### Program Description

## **This program has been paused by Health Research BC\. There will be no 2026 competition\.**

The Health Professional\-Investigator \(HP\-I\) program supports health professionals \(e\.g\. MD, RN, PT\) to develop and advance research with the goal of bringing research evidence into practice within the health system\. The HP\-I awards support health professionals who are actively involved in patient care to build their health research programs, train the next generation of scientists, and make significant contributions to their field\.

[Full Program Terms](https://healthresearchbc.ca/fundingprogram/health-professional-investigator-hp-i-program/)
