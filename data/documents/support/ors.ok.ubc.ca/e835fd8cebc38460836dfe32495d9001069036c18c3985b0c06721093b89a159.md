Health research facilitation at UBCO is primarily offered for Canadian Institute of Health Research \([CIHR](https://cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/193.html)\) and Michael Smith Health Research BC \([MSHR\-BC](https://www.msfhr.org/funding)\) programs\. Support for non\-CIHR sponsored programs, other health, or non\-profit health funding competitions is available as capacity permits\. Please contact us if you would like support for an opportunity not listed below\.

[RESEARCH DEVELOPMENT OFFICER](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/about-us/our-team/)

#### **Faciltiation Support**

The UBCO Research Development Team is the first point of contact for all CIHR PG support for faculty members based at UBC Okanagan\. Services offered:

-   Response to reviews \(if applicable\)
-   Peer Review Committee Selection support
-   Criterial/editorial review, and
-   Technical review

For more information, including how to register for any of these services, please contact [denise\.maines@ubc\.ca](mailto:denise.maines@ubc.ca "mailto:denise.maines@ubc.ca")

### CIHR Strategic Grant Opportunities

#### **SUPPORT FOR RESEARCHERS**

-   One\-on\-one consultations are available for researchers looking to apply for Strategic Grants\. Common topics of discussion include aligning the proposal with program objectives, understanding the evaluation criteria, and the relevance of knowledge translation strategies\. To set up an appointment in person or on zoom, please email the relevant [Research Development Team member](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/about-us/our-team/)\.
-   ORS provides editorial, criteria and technical feedback on well\-developed applications \(e\.g\., Research Proposal\)\. Strategic grant reviews are provided on a case\-by\-case basis\. Please contact the relevant [Research Development Team member](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/about-us/our-team/) **at least 4 weeks in advance of the competition deadline** so we can plan accordingly\.
