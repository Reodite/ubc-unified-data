Welcome to the QReserve research equipment Database – a web\-based searchable inventory of over 2000 research facilities, equipment and services available at UBC Okanagan\. This platform aims to increase the visibility of research programs and facilities by supporting the research community in exploring what is available\. The benefits connected with this database include increased collaborations across research teams on campus and beyond\.
