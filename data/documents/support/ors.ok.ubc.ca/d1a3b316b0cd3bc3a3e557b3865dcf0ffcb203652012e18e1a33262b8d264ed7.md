### Program Description

This funding opportunity aims to leverage the complementary interests and research expertise found across the participating G7 and Nordic countries to support multilateral or bilateral collaborative research projects in quantum science and technologies\.

Proposals may address any challenge in quantum science, but their thrust must aim to advance research in any of the following areas of quantum technologies, or a combination thereof:

-   quantum algorithms/encryption, including post\-quantum cryptography
-   quantum communications
-   quantum computing
-   quantum materials
-   quantum sensing

Proposals may also couple any of these quantum technology thrusts to other research topics in the natural sciences and engineering\.

**Projects must include at least one international collaborator who is eligible for funding from Germany \(DFG\), Japan \(JST\), the United Kingdom \(UKRI EPSRC\) or Nordic countries \(NordForsk\)\.** Multilateral collaborations \(i\.e\., involving Canada and two or more other countries\) are encouraged, but bilateral collaborations \(i\.e\., Canada and one other country\) are also allowed\.

Project duration is up to 3 years\.

### How to Apply

Each Canadian research team can submit only one application to this call\. May participate as an applicant on only one application, but can be a co\-applicant or collaborator on multiple applications\.

The Canadian principal applicant will submit a single collaborative proposal on behalf of the Canadian and international research teams\.

Each funding agency will provide funding to its national researchers, subject to its national rules and budgetary availability\. Canadian applicants are only eligible to receive funding from NSERC, and international researchers are only eligible for funding from their respective national funder\.

See [Eligible Expenses](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/canada-g7-nordic-call-proposals-on-quantum-technologies#toc-tdm-300:~:text=in%20this%20call.-,Eligible%20expenses,-Information%20for%20each) for funding information for each participating country\.

If a private sector partner organization is included in the proposal, the relevant national funders may have specific requirements:

-   For NSERC, the [Risk Assessment Form](https://science.gc.ca/site/science/en/safeguarding-your-research/guidelines-and-tools-implement-research-security/national-security-guidelines-research-partnerships/national-security-guidelines-research-partnerships-risk-assessment-form)  is required\.
-   For DFG, researchers must follow the [Guidelines on the Participation of Commercial Enterprises in Research Projects](https://www.dfg.de/resource/blob/353944/4-02-en.pdf)  and a cooperation agreement must be in place before DFG’s funding decision is made\.
-   For UKRI EPSRC, expectations are set out in standard grant terms and conditions: [UKRI terms and conditions of FEC grants](https://www.ukri.org/publications/terms-and-conditions-for-research-grants/ukri-terms-and-conditions-of-fec-grants/) \.
