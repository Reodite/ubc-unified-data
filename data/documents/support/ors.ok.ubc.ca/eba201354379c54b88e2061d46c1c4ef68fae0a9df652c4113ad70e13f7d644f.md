### Program Description

## **This program has been paused by Health Research BC\. There will be no 2026 competition\.**

Health Research BC’s Scholar Program is designed to attract and foster the development of outstanding health researchers from across BC\. Scholar awards help enable early career researchers to build leading\-edge health research programs, train the next generation of scientists, and make significant contributions to their field\. For the full application, ORS must forward your application to the agency before the agency deadline\. For the NOI, applicants submit directly to Health Research BC
