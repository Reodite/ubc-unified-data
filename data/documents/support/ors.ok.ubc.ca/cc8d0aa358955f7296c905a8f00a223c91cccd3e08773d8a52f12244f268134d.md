### Program Description

**The UBC Okanagan Campus as a Living Lab program is now closed\.**  

UBC Okanagan’s Campus as a Living Lab \(CLL\) program offers its faculty and staff a unique opportunity to incubate, test, and demonstrate innovative research ideas in a real\-world setting, embedded on campus lands \(its buildings, infrastructure, streets, and spaces\) or within its communities\. The CLL competition will provide funding of up to $50,000\.  Each application must include at least one UBC Okanagan Faculty member and one UBC Okanagan Operational Staff member\.  Proposals are invited from across all academic disciplines and across operational areas and must demonstrate meaningful impact in any of the identified priority areas listed in the program terms\.

[Full Program Terms](https://research.ok.ubc.ca/funding-support/internal-funding/)
