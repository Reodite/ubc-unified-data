Our team assists researchers in selecting grant opportunities and developing applications\. Research facilitation is generally organized by national tri\-agency funders for Health, Natural Science and Engineering, and Social Sciences and Humanities research topics, but we also work collaboratively to support multi\- and inter\-disciplinary applications, so please [get in touch](http://ors.ok.ubc.ca/about-us/our-team/) if you aren’t sure where to start\!
