Research Development at UBCO for the Natural Sciences and Engineering is principally offered for Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council \([NSERC](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/index_eng.asp)\) programs, but as capacity permits, support is available for any competition within the natural sciences and engineering domain\. Please contact the Research Development team if you would like support for an opportunity that is not listed below\.

[RESEARCH DEVELOPMENT TEAM](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/about-us/our-team/#res-dev)

**RESEARCH SECURITY:** Researchers applying to most Tri\-Agency competitions must comply with new and updated Government of Canada research security requirements\. More information on research security requirements can be found [here](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/proposal-development-submission/research-security/)\.

[**Alliance Grants**](http://ors.ok.ubc.ca/funding/nserc-alliance/)

#### **Risk Assessment Form**

-   NSERC [Alliance Grant](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/alliance-advantage#a1) applications with a private sector partner must include a risk assessment questionnaire completed by the researcher and validated by UBC \(mandatory prior to submission\)\. Learn more about UBC’s process for [Risk Assessment Forms](https://researchsecurity.ubc.ca/federal-policies-research-funding/completing-risk-assessment-form)\.

#### **Support**

-   The Research Development team is available to support applications from an initial conversation about what is required for a successful Alliance application through to a full editorial review, as capacity permits\. This includes rolling deadlines such as Alliance Advantage, Alliance Society, Alliance\-Mitacs, and Alliance International, and as capacity permits, may include special calls and Alliance Missions\.
-   For all Alliance streams, we strongly recommended you connect with the Research Development team in advance of writing an application to confirm accurate cost\-sharing calculations, partnership structure, and page limits\.
-   If you would like a criteria or editorial review, please provide at least two weeks’ notice\. Note that reviews are subject to availability, and during peak season \(September to October\), turnaround times may be longer\.
-   For Alliance\-Accelerate applications, please reach out to a [Mitacs representative](https://www.mitacs.ca/about/mitacs-advisors/) before beginning your application, in addition to UBCO’s Research Development team\.

#### **RESOURCES**

-   Alliance Program Summary \(to request this resource, contact the relevant [Research Development](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/about-us/our-team/#res-dev) team member *\)*
-   Alliance Annotated Template \(to request this resource, contact the relevant [Research Development](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/about-us/our-team/#res-dev) team member*\)*
-   [Sample Grant Library](https://sparc.ubc.ca/resources-sample-grants) \(CWL required\)
-   [SPARC Resources](https://sparc.ubc.ca/cwl/resources-samples/nserc-resources) \(CWL required\)

    -   Partner Organization Form Guide

[**Discovery Grants**](http://ors.ok.ubc.ca/funding/nserc-discovery-grant/)

#### **Support**

-   **DG Summer School**  
    This suite of materials walks you through each stage of the DG application:
    
    -   *Part 1: What Makes a DG Successful, ‘Thinking like a Reviewer” and NOI prep*
    -   *Part 2: Proposal – a step\-by\-step guide*
    -   *Part 3: Most Significant Contributions, HQP\+EDI, &amp;  Everything Else*

**Materials are available at any time\. Please contact the relevant [Research Development](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/about-us/our-team/#res-dev) team member for access\.** 

-   **NOI reviews** are available in late July\. NOIs are due August 1, \(or the next business day if August 1 falls on a weekend or holiday\), and no RPIF is required\.
-   **Reviews are typically offered in the fall**\. Further information will be shared with researchers who submit an NOI\.

[**Research Tools and Instruments**](http://ors.ok.ubc.ca/funding/nserc-research-tools-and-instruments/)

#### **Support**

-   Reviews are available for RTI applications during September and early October on a first come, first served basis, as capacity permits\. Support for resubmissions is available in the spring or summer\.
-   If you are interested in a review or receiving information on deadlines and resources ahead of the competition, please contact relevant [Research Development](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/about-us/our-team/#res-dev) team member\.

#### **RESOURCES**

-   We suggest you use [RTI Budget Template](https://ors.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/174/2024/05/RTI-Budget-Template.xlsx) in your budget justification

-   The [SPARC](https://sparc.ubc.ca/cwl/resources-samples/nserc-resources) RTI Playbook is a comprehensive guide to the RTI program \(CWL required\)

[**Collaborative Research and Training Experience \(CREATE\) Program**](http://ors.ok.ubc.ca/funding/nserc-create/)

#### **Support**

-   Institutions may submit an unlimited number of Letters of Intent \(LOIs\)\. As such, there will **only** be an internal selection process at UBC for the special calls \(e\.g\., 2026 Quantum call\)\. Details on this process can be found on the [SPARC website](https://sparc.ubc.ca/nserc-programs/nserc-create-program)\.
-   The UBCO Research Development team is available to support applications at any stage\. CREATE applications require institutional letters of support\. Please reach out to the Research Development Team no later than 1 April to coordinate review of the LOI, as well as letter of support finalization and VPRI signatures\.
