### Program Description

The Mobilize Award, a new funding opportunity under Health Research BC’s Impact Pathway Program\.

The award will provide up to $20,000 to support collaborative knowledge mobilization activities that bring researchers and research users together to drive real solutions for health and system challenges\.

The Mobilize Award supports a range of collaborative knowledge mobilization activities, including:

-   **Developing knowledge together**: research co\-production and community\-led research for action\.
-   **Connecting and sharing knowledge**: knowledge synthesis, knowledge brokering, and end\-of\-grant knowledge mobilization\.
-   **Advancing Indigenous\-led solutions**: Indigenous\-led solutions focused on health and wellness research\.
-   **Putting knowledge into use**: commercialization, implementation, spreading and sustaining knowledge use, and scaling impact\.

Applications aligned with one of [Health Research BC’s priority themes](https://www.healthresearchbc.ca/our-work/mobilizing-action/priority-themes/) are encouraged but are not required\.

### How to Apply

[Application Guidelines](https://www.healthresearchbc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026_Mobilize-Award_Guidelines.pdf)

Who can apply:

Eligible health system staff and BC\-based postdoctoral, early career and mid\-career researchers may apply\.

Each application must include a primary applicant, co\-lead, and trainee, with the primary applicant and co\-lead bringing complementary researcher and research\-user perspectives\.
