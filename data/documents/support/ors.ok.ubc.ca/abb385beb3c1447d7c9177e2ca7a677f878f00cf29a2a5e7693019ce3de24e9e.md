Research Facilitation at UBCO for the Social Sciences, Humanities and Creative Practice is principally offered for Social Science and Humanities Research Council \([SSHRC](https://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca/)\) programs\.  Formally scheduled support is offered annually for each cycle of Insight and Insight Development Grants\.  Support for other SSRHC programs and non\-SSHRC sponsored applications are available through one\-on\-one consultations\.
