We are the central administrative office for research support at UBC Okanagan\. We work closely with researchers to facilitate grant applications, oversee research accounts, and ensure research compliance at UBC Okanagan\.

[ORS team](http://ors-new.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/174/2021/08/ORS_About-Us5_1170x500.jpg)

### Mission

To support and facilitate research excellence by providing high quality administrative services to faculty, staff, and external partners\.

### Vision

To advance the quality, intensity and impact of research conducted at UBC Okanagan\.

### Values

We value and respect all members of our research community, each of whom individually and collaboratively make a contribution to create, strengthen, and enrich our research environment\.

---

**Contact ORS if you need assistance with the following:**

-   Finding funding or developing research grant applications
-   Obtaining an institutional signature for a grant application
-   Obtaining a compliance certificate for research involving human subjects, animals or biohazardous materials
-   Setting up a research account and spending limits
-   Transferring research funds to collaborators at another institution
-   Registering a clinical trial
