### Program Description

The Office of the Vice\-President, Research and Innovation \(VPRI\) in partnership with the Support Programs to Advance Research Capacity \(SPARC\) offers pilot Bridge Funding Program to support researchers that wish to improve and re\-submit unsuccessful applications to the preceding Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada \(SSHRC\) competition cycle\. Note that this funding total is a match from VPRI to Department or Faculty funding, up to a total of $2500 from VPRI\.

[Full Program Terms](https://sparc.ubc.ca/sshrc-programs/ssh-internal-funding-opportunities)
