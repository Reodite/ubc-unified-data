### Program Description

The CIHR [Project Grant program](http://www.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/49051.html) is designed to capture ideas with the greatest potential to advance health\-related fundamental or applied knowledge, health research, health care, health systems, and/or health outcomes\. It supports projects with a specific purpose and a defined endpoint\. The best ideas may stem from new, incremental, innovative, and/or high\-risk lines of inquiry or knowledge translation approaches\. ORS must forward your application to the agency before the sponsor deadline\. For the Registration deadline \(NOI/LOI noted above\), applicants submit directly to CIHR\.
