### Program Description

The Michael Smith Health Research BC ‘Advancing the Use of Evidence in Seniors’ Home and Community Care program’ will fund teams of researchers and research users within BC’s health system and academia to implement and evaluate the outcomes of evidence\-based interventions\. The program will provide evidence to help decision\-makers across BC to support programs that help seniors to live longer, healthier lives more independently in their communities\. Funding will be provided through two stages: 1\) letter of intent for a development award and 2\) full application for a team award\.
