[ORS professional service and support team](http://ors-new.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/174/2021/08/ORS_Our-Services_1170x500.jpg)

We can assist with obtaining institutional signatures for a grant application, setting up research accounts, transferring research funds to collaborators at other institutions, declaring a conflict of interest, obtaining a compliance certificate for research involving human subjects, animals or biohazardous materials, registering a clinical trial, and more\.

### **We offer the following services:**

### [FUNDING OPPORTUNITIES](http://ors.ok.ubc.ca/funding-opportunities/)

-   Disseminating information on internal and external funding opportunities and deadlines
-   Investigating and sharing criteria for new funding programs and competitions
-   Providing administrative updates on major funding sources, research opportunities and related events

### [PROPOSAL DEVELOPMENT](http://ors.ok.ubc.ca/proposal-development-submission/research-facilitation/)

-   Providing advice and guidance in the preparation of research grant proposals
-   Hosting workshops to assist with successful preparation of grant proposals
-   Creating and sharing resources \(sample proposals, templates and guides\)

### [RESEARCH ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT](http://ors.ok.ubc.ca/research-account-management/project-grant-account-set-up/)

-   Issuing Project Grants \(PG\) for accessing and managing research funding
-   Transferring research funds to partner institutions
-   Facilitating grant extensions

### [RESEARCH ETHICS](http://ors.ok.ubc.ca/ethics-compliance/research-ethics/)

-   Providing information on the research ethics review and approval process
-   Providing online resources and customized training and support
-   Creating and sharing resources \(sample proposals, application tools and templates\)

### [POLICY COMPLIANCE](http://ors.ok.ubc.ca/policies/)

-   Ensuring grant proposals comply with internal and external policies and procedures, including applicable legal, regulatory and ethical requirements
-   Obtaining an institutional signature for grant applications
