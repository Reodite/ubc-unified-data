### Program Description

The Hampton New Faculty Grant is designed to strengthen the early development of research programs for tenure‑track faculty hired within the past 24 months\. Eligible applicants include Assistant, Associate, and Full Professors, as well as Professors of Teaching, for projects lasting up to two years\. Total funding per project is capped at $15,000\.

This program specifically supports new Assistant Professors as they launch their academic careers and Associate and Full Professors newly joining UBC as they establish their initial research agendas\. Funded projects should help investigators build a research record and position themselves for future external funding\. Proposals must clearly articulate how the Hampton\-funded work will serve as a springboard for subsequent applications to provincial, national, and international agencies—particularly to bodies such as the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council\.
