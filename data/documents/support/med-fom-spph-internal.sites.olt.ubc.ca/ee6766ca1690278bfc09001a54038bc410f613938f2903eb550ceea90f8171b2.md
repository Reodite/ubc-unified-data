**IHI Speakers Series presents: *What constitutes an informed patient/provider visit?***

Guest speakers: Dr\. Barbara Mintzes, Dr\. Aaron M Tejani and Alan Cassels of the Therapeutics Initiative \(TI\)

Monday, January 26th, 5:30 pm to 7:30pm

MSAC VC Room, 2750 Heather Street

Think about what information you want to receive from your health provider \(family physician, dietitian, specialist, pharmacist, etc\.\) going into each consultation\. Now, think about the information that is actually provided to you? Are there any inconsistencies? What is the fundamental purpose of treatment and therapy? Is this being met?

Hosted by the Institute of Healthcare Improvement UBC Chapter with guest speakers from the Therapeutics Initiative of UBC, the event will focus on the information asymmetry in the patient/provider visit and the potential bias in prescribing decisions and how this can be reduced\.

Details on the UBC Chapter’s [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/events/683334885116741/?ref_newsfeed_story_type=regular) page\. Register [online](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/ihi-ubc-speaker-series-registration-15050054117) by Jan\. 21 to reserve your seat\.
