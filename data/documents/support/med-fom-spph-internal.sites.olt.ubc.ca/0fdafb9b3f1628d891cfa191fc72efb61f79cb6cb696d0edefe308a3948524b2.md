**How can the implementation of electronic health records \(EHRs\) lead to quality improvement in healthcare?**

Join [IHI UBC](http://www.ihiubc.com/) for their final event of the Winter Semester in the discussion of the future direction of Electronic Health Records and the impact this will have on healthcare\. The event will have feature presentations from a panel that will consist of Dr\. Martin Dawes, Mrs\. Delia Cooper, Dr\. James McCormack and a representative from the Cerner health record implementation team\. This panel represents an interdisciplinary group of stakeholders in healthcare that are working on a variety of e\-health initiatives\.

The event will also feature presentations from Carly Strang \(HI Open School Director\) and Jessica Perlo \(IHI Open School Community Manager\)\.

Please Register via [eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/ihi-ubc-panel-event-registration-15644970528)\. Doors open at 5:00 pm and presentations will begin at 5:30pm\.
