MHA blog posts will be circulated every second Friday to MHA students and alumni with a focus on events and workshops, internships and career opportunities that may be of interest\. You can manage your subscription through [Feedburner](http://feedburner.google.com/fb/a/myfeeds)\.

Program and cohort specific announcements, to\-do’s and reminders will be sent by email so please continue checking your inboxes for messages from MHA staff\.

#### **Upcoming events and workshops:**

##### **SPPH Grand Rounds**

Did you know you don’t need to get to our Point Grey campus to take part in [Grand Rounds](http://spph.ubc.ca/seminar-series/grand-rounds/), the School’s weekly seminar series? Login and listen to the presentation via Adobe Connect and ask questions through the chat window\. Upcoming speakers include MHA instructors: [Steve Morgan](http://spph.ubc.ca/person/steve-morgan/) \(Jan\. 16, *The economics and politics of Pharmacare reform in Canada*\) and [Chris McLeod](http://spph.ubc.ca/person/christopher-mcleod/) \(Jan\. 30, *topic tbd*\)\.

##### **Getting the Interview\- How to Make Your Application Stand Out**

Learn how to maximize each step of the application process and get the interviews you want\. Offered through the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies, this [workshop](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/about-us/events/12407-getting-interview-how-make-your-application-stand-out) is part of their Graduate Pathways to Success series\.

Date: Friday, November 21, 2014, 1:00pm to 3:00pm

Location: BC Cancer Research Centre, 675 W\. 10th Ave

Registration for this workshop opens on Nov\. 10, 2014\.

##### **MHA Info Session for Interested Applicants**

Help us spread the word about our upcoming [info sessions](http://spph.ubc.ca/programs-and-students/graduate-degree-programs/mha/contact/) for prospective MHA students\.

November 26, 2014: In person session at Robson Square, 6:30 pm to 7:30 pm\.

November 27, 2014: Webinar via Adobe Connect, 3:30 pm to 4:30 pm\.
