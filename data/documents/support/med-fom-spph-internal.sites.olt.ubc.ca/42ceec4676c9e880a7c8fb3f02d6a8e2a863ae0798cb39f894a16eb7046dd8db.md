Join your fellow SPPH students for the launch of the new SPPH student association and help shape its future academic and social activities\. The **Association of Population and Public Health Students** \(TAPPHS\) hopes to be an organized student voice representing the interests of the School’s graduate students\.

**Wednesday, April 8, 11:30 am to 1:00 pm** in the SPPH Foyer, 2206 East Mall\.

Coffee and baked goods sponsored by the Graduate Student Society\. Please [email](mailto:tapphs.ubc@gmail.com) the group or connect with [Daniel Kwan](mailto:danielchkwan@outlook.com), our MHA student rep, for more information\.
