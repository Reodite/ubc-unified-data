On behalf of the EHL, MHA students and alumni are invited to take part in their [Annual Speed Mentorin](http://emerginghealthleaders.ca/event/influencing-for-change/)g event\. Check out this rare opportunity to meet some of the most highly respected and accomplished senior leaders in our BC healthcare system\.

On Thursday, June 11th, our annual speed mentoring event will take place where attendees get to ask any question that could further their professional development\. Speakers are Jan K\. Grude, President and CEO, Bob Smith, President, Perform Management Consulting, Cathie Heritage, Executive Director, Surrey Memorial Hospital\. 

**When**: Thursday, June 11, 2015

**Time**: 6pm\-8pm

**Location**: Room 2254 2F Diamond Healthcare Centre at Vancouver General Hospital\. 2775 Laurel Street, Vancouver, BC

**Cost**: $10 for EHL Members; $15 for Non Members\. Buy tickets [online](http://www.eventbrite.ca/e/annual-ehl-speedmentoring-tickets-17161317964?ref=ebtnebtckt)\.
