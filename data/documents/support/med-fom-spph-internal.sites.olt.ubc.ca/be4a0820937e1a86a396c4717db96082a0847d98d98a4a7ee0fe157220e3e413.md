### **University of British Columbia**

[UBC\_Campus](https://med-fom-spph-internal.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/06/UBC_Campus.jpg)

Of UBC’s 1,970 full\-time faculty, 98% have PhDs, the highest rate of any Canadian university\. An outstanding range of programs at undergraduate, postgraduate and professional levels are available through 12 Faculties and 11 Schools, leading to globally recognized degrees and diplomas\.

Learn more about [UBC](https://ubc.ca/) and [our place among the world’s best](http://www.ubc.ca/about/global.html)\.

### Vancouver, BC

[VancouverBC](https://med-fom-spph-internal.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/06/VancouverBC.jpg)

Learning at UBC means enjoying a superb campus in one of the planet’s truly spectacular regions – beautiful British Columbia\. Vancouver is BC’s cosmopolitan urban centre, providing a temperate climate, safe streets, a vibrant arts and cultural scene, and a welcoming, culturally diverse population\. And if you are from outside Canada, you probably already have a positive impression of our country – a peaceful, progressive society that welcomes all ethnicities, nationalities, and creeds into its cultural fabric\.
