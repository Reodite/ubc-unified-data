Responding to students’ request, the MHA has arranged access to the Canadian Foundation for Health Care Improvement’s webinar series on [Shifting Culture: Shifting Care: From “Usual Care” to Chronic Care](http://www.cfhi-fcass.ca/Elearning/OnCall/shifting-culture-shifting-care), with sessions from April 1 to June 10, 2015\.

To access to the *On* *Demand* materials: Visit the [CFHI website](http://www.cfhi-fcass.ca/Elearning/OnCall/shifting-culture-shifting-care/shifting-culture-shifting-care) and enter the MHA program access password: **20150401SCSC** \(if you copy and paste, please be sure to not select any blank spaces\)\. Access to **Session Materials** is located on the lower right hand corner of the page\.

**Mark your calendars for the upcoming sessions in the series:**

-   **May 8, 2015** – [The Chronic Care Model: Designing Care with ‘Chronic’ as the New Norm](http://www.cfhi-fcass.ca/Elearning/OnCall/shifting-culture-shifting-care/designing-care-with-chronic-as-the-new-norm)
-   **June 3, 2015** – [Supporting Carers of Patients Living with Chronic Disease](http://www.cfhi-fcass.ca/Elearning/OnCall/shifting-culture-shifting-care/supporting-carers-of-patients-living-with-chronic-disease)
-   **June 10, 2015** – [Bridging the Gap between Patients and Providers through Telehealth in Rural and Remote Regions](http://www.cfhi-fcass.ca/Elearning/OnCall/shifting-culture-shifting-care/bridging-the-gap-between-patients-and-providers-through-telehealth-in-rural-and-remote-regions)
-   Access to the April 1, 2015 recording on [Shifting Culture, Shifting Care: From ‘Usual Care’ to Chronic Care](http://www.cfhi-fcass.ca/Elearning/OnCall/shifting-culture-shifting-care/shifting-culture-shifting-care)\.
