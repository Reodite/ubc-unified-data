Congratulations to UBC MPH student Christopher Okpaleke who has been awarded the 2013 Family Planning Research Scholarship by the Contraception Access Research Team/Groupe de recherche sur l’accessibilité à la contraception \(CART/GRAC\) team\.

Christopher is working with Dr\. Wendy Norman and the CART/GRAC team on a project that surveys Canadian abortion providers and clinic directors to gather information on abortion practices and characterize abortion providers’ experiences with stigma and harassment\. CART/GRAC is a new, collaborative [national research network](http://www.cart-grac.ca/canadian-contraception-access-survey/) that will build on successful strategies in family planning practice and research across Canada\. The long\-term goal is improved health for women and their families through improved access to high quality family planning knowledge and services to reduce unintended pregnancies\.

Congratulations Christopher\!
