In case you were wondering about your first weekend for the MHA, here are some details to look forward to your start in the program\. 

***What happens first?***

Students must attend our MHA Orientation on September 13 from 2:00 to 3:00 pm\. This one\-hour session will be held prior to the start of classes\. At this session, we’ll discuss UBC and program\-specific information with MHA program staff to ensure your successful start in the program\.

***What is the class schedule?\****

**SPHA 510 – Canadian Health Policy**

Friday, 3:00 pm to 9:00 pm

Saturday, 9:00 am to 1:00 pm

**SPHA 551 – Design and Measurement**

Saturday, 2:00 pm to 5:00 pm

Sunday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm

\*Coffee and meal breaks are not listed in the above times\. An overview of the MHA weekend course schedule will be presented during orientation\.

***Where do you go?***

All activities will be held at [UBC Robson Square](http://robsonsquare.ubc.ca/find-us/?login), in room C130\.

If you have any questions, please contact the [MHA Program office](http://spph.ubc.ca/programs-and-students/graduate-degree-programs/mha/contact/?login)\.
