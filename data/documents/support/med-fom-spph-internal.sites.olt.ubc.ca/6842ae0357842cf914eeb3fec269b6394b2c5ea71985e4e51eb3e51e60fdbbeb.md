SPPH Professor, **Dr\. Michael Brauer**, has been appointed to the CIHR Institute of Population and Public Health Advisory Board\.

The Institute of Population and Public Health’s \(IPPH\) Advisory Board includes a diverse group of national and international representatives of the public, private and non\-profit sectors, including the research community and health practitioners\. The Institute Advisory Board \(IAB\) provides support and guidance to CIHR’s [Scientific Director](http://www.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/12201.html) and CIHR’s [Governing Council](http://www.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/6953.html) with respect to the full range of Institute activities\.

For more information about the IABs, please consult CIHR’s [Institute Advisory Boards](http://www.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/18156.html) page\.
