With over 350 graduate students enrolled in its graduate programs, the School of Population and Public Health is committed to education and training for researchers, scientists, managers, and practitioners in public health\.

The 2016/7 SPPH Admissions period is now underway, and we want to give all prospective students an opportunity to learn about our academic programs, the admissions process and our School\.

Date: **Wednesday, November 25, 2015**  
Time: **5:00 – 6:00 pm**  
Location: **B151, SPPH Building, 2206 East Mall, UBC**

View session online\! If you are unable to attend in person, please join us via [Adobe Connect](http://webinarfomubc.adobeconnect.com/spph_programs/)

Please **RSVP** to [spph\.msc\-oeh@ubc\.ca](mailto:spph.msc-oeh@ubc.ca) by Friday, November 20, 2015
