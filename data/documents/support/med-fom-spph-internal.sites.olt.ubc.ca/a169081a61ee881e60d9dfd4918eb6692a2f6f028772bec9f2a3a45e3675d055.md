Kim Schonert\-Reichl

Dr\. Kimberly Schonert\-Reichl became the Human Early Learning Partnership \(HELP\)’s Interim Director on July 1, 2014\.

Kim acted as HELP’s Lead Researcher on the Middle Years Development Instrument \(MDI\), and is an expert in the field of social\-emotional learning\. She is a long standing HELP Affiliated Scholar and was a close colleague of Clyde Hertzman’s\. She has an excellent understanding of HELP’s operations and Population Health program of research\.

Pippa Rowcliffe, Interim Deputy Director, and Dr\. Paul Kershaw, Associate Director will work closely with Kim\. Together they will bring stability and new energy to the continuing transition at HELP\.

HELP would like to take this opportunity to thank Jeannie Shoveller, HELP’s outgoing Interim Director, for her wisdom, leadership and commitment during the past year\.
