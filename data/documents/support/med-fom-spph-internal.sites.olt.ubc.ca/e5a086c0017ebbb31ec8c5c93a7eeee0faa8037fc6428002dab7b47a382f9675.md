Come and join us to learn about SPPH graduate programs and the admissions process\! This information session is for all prospective students that would like to know more about any, or all, of the SPPH graduate programs, and about how our admissions process works\.

There will be:

-   Information on all SPPH graduate programs; MPH, MHA, MSc/Phd, MSc OEH
-   Faculty from the programs and program advisors
-   Time for questions from prospective students

Date: Wednesday, November, 2013

Time: 5:00 – 6:00 pm  
Location: Room B151, SPPH Building  
2206 East Mall, UBC

Please RSVP to [spph\.msc\-oeh@ubc\.ca](mailto:spph.msc-oeh@ubc.ca) by Friday, November 22
