The MHA program will be sponsoring two current students \(one from each year\) to attend the 2015 [Quality Forum](http://qualityforum.ca/) on Feb\. 18 to 20 at the Hyatt Regency in Vancouver\. Access over 150 speakers at 56 sessions and workshops on sharing effective strategies and leading practices towards improvement in quality of care\.

Interested students should [email](mailto:mha.program@ubc.ca) the MHA Program with a brief answer \(max\. of 150 words\) to “*How attending the Quality Forum helps your professional goals”*\.  Please submit your responses by **Friday, Jan\. 16, 2015**\. The successful attendees will be notified by Wednesday, Jan\. 21\.
