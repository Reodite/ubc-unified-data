Dr\. Chris Richardson

In a guest blog post for the [Centre of Excellence in Cancer Prevention](https://cancerprevent.ca/), Dr\. Chris Richardson describes how online health messages can be more effective\. His recent work shows that tailored, gender\-specific messages increase online engagement with breast cancer prevention information\.

[Dr\. Richardson](http://spph.ubc.ca/person/chris-richardson/) is an Associate Professor at SPPH, and his research focuses on biopsychosocial development in adolescence and their relationships with patterns of tobacco, alcohol, and other drug use\.
