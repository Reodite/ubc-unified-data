A reminder to all MHA 2nd year students that SPHA 590 grades must be submitted to the MHA Program office by August 30, 2013 in order to meet the Faculty of Graduate Studies deadline for graduation\. You must also complete your [application for graduation](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/graduation/applying-graduate) by August 30, 2013\.
