**Mo Korchinski**, project coordinator for the Women in2 Healing peer health mentor program, part of the Collaborating Centre for Prison Health and Education \(CCPHE\), received the R\. Paul Kerston Community Educator Award\.

This annual award, given by the the Division of Health Care Communication, part of UBC’s College of Health Disciplines, recognizes patient/community educators who have expanded student learning beyond traditional professional boundaries in health and human service programs at UBC\.
