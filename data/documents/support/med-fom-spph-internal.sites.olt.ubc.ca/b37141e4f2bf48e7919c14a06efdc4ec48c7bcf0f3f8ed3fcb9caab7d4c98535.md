For all second year MHA students:  
In preparation for the start of classes in September, please review the following information regarding:

**Course Registration:**  
A reminder that it is your responsibility to ensure your course registration is kept up to date\. Please login to the [Student Service Centre](https://www.students.ubc.ca/ssc) and register for the following courses in Term 1, Winter Session 2013\-14:

SPHA 511, sec\. 401 Government and Policy  
SPHA 502, sec\. 201 Managerial Accounting  
SPHA 542, sec\. 301 Operations and Logistics  
SPHA 532, sec\. 401 Healthcare Economics

Please note that your course registration is linked to your access to [UBC Connect](http://elearning.ubc.ca/connect/) and you will not have access to the courses via Connect if you are not registered in the class\.

**Textbook Pick up:**  
For students living in Greater Vancouver, your textbooks for SPHA 511 and 502 are now ready for pick up at UBC Robson Square at the concierge desk at the plaza level\. Their hours are as follows: Monday to Friday: 7:00 am to 10:00 pm, Saturdays and Sundays: 7:30 am to 5:30 pm\. Please bring ID with you to pick up your books\.

**MHA 2nd year start\-up meeting:**  
Please plan to attend a 30 min\. meeting for 2nd year MHAs from 2:30 to 3:00 pm on **Friday, September 20**\. This brief meeting will be held prior to the start of classes at 3:00 pm\. We will focus on some important information and resources to help you manage your program as you start your 2nd year\. Please also email me if you have any specific topics you’d like to discuss\.  
Please note that this session is mandatory\. Refreshments will be served\.

We look forward to seeing you on September 20\.
