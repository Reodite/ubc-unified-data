[hertzman](https://med-fom-spph.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/08/hertzman.jpg)Dr\. Clyde Hertzman was honoured with the Champion of Human Services Award by the Board of Directors of the Ontario Municipal Social Services Association \(OMSSA\)\.

Dr\. Hertzman was selected as the recipient of this Award because OMSSA believes Clyde was someone who had a proven and respected track record of achievement in human services\. He is recognized as a thought leader and his work has played a significant role in progressing human services in Ontario and across Canada\.

Joanne Schroeder, Deputy Director of the Human Early Learning Partnership \(HELP\) accepted Clyde’s award on his behalf at OMSSA annual conference in Ottawa on June 18th, 2013\.
