PhD trainee **Devon Greyson** is the winner of the American Library Association’s 2013 Jesse H\. Shera Award for the Support of Dissertation Research\.

Greyson’s dissertation proposal, “Health\-related Information Practices and the Experiences of Childbearing and Parenting Youth,” was selected for its exemplary research design and methods, as well as its contribution to ongoing research in information use in health and social issues\.

Congratulations Devon\!
