School starts at UBC on Tuesday, September 8th, and as a result there will be increased traffic coming to the university\. Students, staff and faculty accessing the university by both car and transit will face heavy congestion and delays\. Please ensure that you leave early in order to make your morning classes/appointments\.

Please also remember that the [SPPH Orientation session](http://spph.ubc.ca/students/orientation/) starts at **9 am on September 8th**\. This will take place in Lecture Theatre 101, [Michael Smith Laboratories](http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?show=y,n,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=n&amp;locat1=083), 2185 East Mall\.
