Each September, faculty, staff and students in the OEH Division, along with the Bridge Program Fellows, take advantage of the last of the summer weather to get to one each other on a hike through one of beautiful British Columbia’s wilderness spots\.

This year Dr\. Hugh Davies, Rocky Lis, Amy Hall, Kaylee Byers, Leela Steiner, Mat MacLeod \(and his partner Brittany\), Poushali Maji, and Omid Nobakht hiked to St Mark’s Summit on the North Shore\. Dr\. Davies summed the event up as “great weather; great day\.”
