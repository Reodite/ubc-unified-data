UBC Risk Management Services has partnered with the Faculty of Medicine, Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences, School of Nursing, Student Health Services, and Workplace Health Services to coordinate the free clinics\. This year, inter\-professional teams of medical, nursing and pharmacy students will administer the vaccinations under direct supervision of registered nurses and University faculty\. UBC faculty, students and staff are eligible to receive free flu shots while they last\.

The School will be hosting one of these free flu clinics for UBC students, faculty and staff\.

Date: **November 19, 201**3  
Time: **8:30 am – 11:30 am**  
Location: **SPPH Lobby**

Registration is [now open](http://www.hse2.ubc.ca/gosignmeupCBM/dev_students.asp?action=coursedetail&amp;id=5100&amp;main=Influenza+Immunization+Program&amp;sub1=Flu+clinics+sign+up&amp;misc=264&amp;courseinternalaccesscode=), and you are invited to sign up to receive the flu shot in the comfort of our building\.

**Not available on the 19th?**  
Clinics are being held in a variety of locations during November\. For more information about these clinics, or to review other dates and venues, please [click here](http://riskmanagement.ubc.ca/health-safety/influenza-immunization-program)\.
