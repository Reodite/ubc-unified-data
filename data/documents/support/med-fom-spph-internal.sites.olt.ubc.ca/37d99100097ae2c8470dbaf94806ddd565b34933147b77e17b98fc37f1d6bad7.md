[Lola](https://med-fom-spph.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/02/Lola-200x196.jpg)Exam stress can get to everyone, even our students here at SPPH\. So we are piloting a pet therapy program for SPPH students only\. On Wednesday, April 9th, from 9:30 am until 11:30 am, sign up for a 15 minute time slot, then come to the SPPH Reading Room \(Rm\. 198, just off the lobby\) and snuggle up with SPPH’s resident therapy dog, Lola\.

Please select from the available times below and fill in the required information\. Please note: You may be paired with up to two other students during your time slot\. You will receive a confirmation email in a few days of signing up\.
