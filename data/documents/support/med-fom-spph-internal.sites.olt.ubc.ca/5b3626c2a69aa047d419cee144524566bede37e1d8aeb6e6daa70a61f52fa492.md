The MHA aims to provide learning material to students one month in advance of each session\. For September courses, we will make arrangements to have the material couriered to students who live outside of the Greater Vancouver Regional District\. Please note that this only applies to the first two courses, learning material will be provided onsite at Robson Square after the launch of classes in September\. The cost of textbooks is included in tuition\.

If you currently live outside of the GVRD, please send me your address and phone number so I can make arrangements for courier\.
