PhD student **Elaine Fuertes** won the Best Abstract Award at 2013 [Joint Conference](http://www.ehbasel13.org/) of the International Society for Environmental Epidemiology/International Society of Exposure Science/International Society of Indoor Air Quality and Climate, which took place August 19\-23, 2013 in Basel, Switzerland\.

The abstract was entitled: “A Global Analysis of Associations Between Climate, Vegetation And Intermittent And Persistent Rhinitis Prevalence\.”

Congratulations, Elaine
