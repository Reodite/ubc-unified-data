##### **New Health Professional Student Journal invites submissions**

Supported by the College of Health Disciplines at the University of British Columbia, the [Health Professional Student Journal](http://www.hpsj.chd.ubc.ca/index.php/journal/index) \(ISSN 2368\-8645\) is an online student\-run journal created to foster improved teamwork, collaboration, and communication between health and human service disciplines\. [Submissions](http://www.hpsj.chd.ubc.ca/index.php/journal/about/submissions#onlineSubmissions) can include articles written for UBC courses, including [SPHA 590 projects](https://med-fom-spph.sites.olt.ubc.ca/programs-and-students/graduate-degree-programs/mha/current-students/managing-your-program-3/spha-590/ "Research Project (SPHA 590)"), and are an excellent opportunity to enhance your learning of the publication process while sharing your knowledge with your peers\.
