Welcome again to the MHA\! For any new MHA students who have not submitted their profiles, we need information from you to ensure that program staff and faculty can contact you and learn more about you before classes begin\. Please send updated info, bios and pics\.  This should include a brief paragraph of yourself and your current job title/occupation, along with a headshot of yourself \(jpg\. format\)\.
