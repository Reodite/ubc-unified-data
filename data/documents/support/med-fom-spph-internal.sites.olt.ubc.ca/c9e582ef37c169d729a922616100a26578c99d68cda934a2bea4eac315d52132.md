Congratulations to Dr\. Margo Pearce, who was awarded the CIHR Canadian HIV Trials Network \(CTN\) James Kreppner Fellowship Award\. This award allows Dr\. Pearce to focus on her postdoctoral research, “Recognizing and responding to barriers to HCV treatment among young Indigenous people who use drugs in two Canadian cities,” under the supervision of CTN Investigators Dr\. Marina Klein \(McGill University\) and Dr\. Robert Hogg \(Simon Fraser University\)\.

Dr\. Pearce recently completed her PhD in the UBC School of Population and Public Health, under the supervision of Dr\. Patricia Spittal\.
