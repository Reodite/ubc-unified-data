The Master of Health Science \(MHSc\) degree is a professional degree designed to give the graduate strong research methodology skills that can be applied to their academic and clinical interests\. The program is restricted to applicants who have an MD or equivalent \(e\.g\., dentistry or veterinary medicine\) and the academic requirements of the [Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/) apply to all students\.
