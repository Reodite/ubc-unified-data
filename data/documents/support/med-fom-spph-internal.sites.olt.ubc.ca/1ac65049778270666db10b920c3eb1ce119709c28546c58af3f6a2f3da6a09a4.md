Congratulations to Dr\. Jane Buxton who received the Distinguished Achievement Award for Education \(2014\), and Dr\. Jean Shoveller received the Distinguished Achievement Award for Overall Excellence – Senior Faculty \(2014\), from UBC’s Faculty of Medicine\.

Associate faculty member, Dr\. Gina Ogilvie, was awarded the Distinguished Achievement Award for Clinical/Applied Research\.
