The Canadian Institutes of Health Research \(CIHR\) has announced the results of its inaugural 2014 Foundation Grants competition and the 2015 Operating Grants competition\. These highly competitive rounds of funding has resulted in the following grants being awarded to SPPH faculty and researchers:

### CIHR Foundation Grants \(201409\)

***Integrated global control and prevention of HPV related diseases and cancer*** \($2,714,493 over 7 years\)  
Principal Investigator: **Gina Ogilvie**

### **Partnerships for Health System Improvement \(PHSI\) \(201410\)**

**Principal Investigator\(s\):**  
***Applying syndemic/core group theory to improve integrated prevention and service delivery for HIV and related epidemics*** \($400,000 over 3 years\)  
Principal Investigators: Dionne Gesink; **Mark Gilbert**; **Michelle Murti**; **Gina Ogilvie**; **Naveed Janjua**  
Co\-Investigators: Vanessa Allen; Chris Archibald; Ann Burchell; **Titilola Falasinnu**; James Johnston; Jennifer Jones; **Perry Kendall**; Mel Krajden; Colin, Quek\-Tze Lee; Travis Salway Hottes; **Jean Shoveller**; Marc Steben; Darlene Taylor; **Jason Wong**; Thomas Wong

***Optimizing the detection and management of cervical cancer in the evolving era of molecular based cervical cancer screening*** \($395,000 over 3 years\)  
Principal Investigators: Andrew Coldman; Mel Krajden; Dirk Van Niekerk; **Gina Ogilvie**  
Co\-Investigators: Kathy Ceballos; James Dickinson; Natalie Fitzgerald; Eduardo Franco; Catherine \(Meg\) Mclachlin; Joan Murphy; Mona Saraiya; Gavin Stuart
