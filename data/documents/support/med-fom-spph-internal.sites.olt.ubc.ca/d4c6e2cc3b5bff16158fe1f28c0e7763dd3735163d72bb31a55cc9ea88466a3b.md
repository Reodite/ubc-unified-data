**Quality Forum 2015**

The Quality Forum is an annual three\-day conference with over 56 sessions and 150 presenters “Creating Connections” to improve quality of care\. Conference dates are Feb 18\-20, 2015\. Visit their [website](http://qualityforum.ca/) to learn more and to register\.

**MHA LinkedIn Network** 

Are you on LinkedIn or thinking about joining this professional networking site? If you’re not already a member, we invite you to join our our [MHA group](https://www.linkedin.com/groups?home=&amp;gid=4440593&amp;trk=anet_ug_hm) of students and alumni of the program\. We’re still building this online network of health care leaders so please spread the word as we establish this group further and develop future alumni activities\.
