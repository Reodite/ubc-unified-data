The Institute for Healthcare Improvement \(IHI\) Open School: University of British Columbia \(UBC\) Chapter is an interdisciplinary community of students and professionals striving for a more efficient, effective and safe healthcare system that provides the best possible care for all patients\.

The group is currently hiring for for the 2015\-16 executive committee\. If you are interested in applying, visit their [website](http://www.ihiubc.com/apply-for-exec-2015-16.html) for more information and instructions\.
