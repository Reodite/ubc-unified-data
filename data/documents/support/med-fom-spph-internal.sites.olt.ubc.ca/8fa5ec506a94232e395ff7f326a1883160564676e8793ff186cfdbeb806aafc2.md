Students can now [apply](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/application-admission/apply-online) to the MHA program starting in September 2014\. The application deadline is February 1 for International applicants and March 31 for domestic applicants\. Learn more about our [program](http://spph.ubc.ca/programs-and-students/graduate-degree-programs/mha/program-detail/) and [admission requirements](http://spph.ubc.ca/programs-and-students/graduate-degree-programs/mha/prospective-students/admission-requirements/)\.
