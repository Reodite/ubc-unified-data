[Northview Ventures](http://www.northviewventures.ca/) is excited to announce the second annual Business of Science Scholarship\. The award is designed to encourage students to expand their thinking towards the impact of scientific research and potential for commercial application\. The goal of this program is to expose students to a Life Sciences community focused on real\-world practicality of research and to encourage them to pursue careers that involve Business and HealthCare innovation\. Up to two $2,500 scholarships will be awarded to current graduate\-level students in BC or Ontario with experience in Science or Engineering and Business\.

Access the application [online](http://www.surveygizmo.com/s3/2054983/NVV-Scholarship-2015-MHA)\.

Northview is a Vancouver\-based private equity and consulting firm focused in the Life Sciences\. It is co\-founded by [Anne Stevens](https://med-fom-spph-internal.sites.olt.ubc.ca/2015/04/02/applications-open-for-northview-ventures-business-of-science-scholarship/ca.linkedin.com/pub/anne-stevens/11/24b/221/en), MHA Graduate, Class of 2011\.  This scholarship is part of a broader community outreach program Northview is creating to mentor, encourage, and interact with young scientists and budding entrepreneurs in the Life Sciences space\.
