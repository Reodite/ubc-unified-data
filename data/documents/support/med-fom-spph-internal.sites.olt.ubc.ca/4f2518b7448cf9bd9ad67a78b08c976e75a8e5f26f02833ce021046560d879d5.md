The UBC Division of Health Care Communication in the College of Health Disciplines is seeking a Work Learn student to assist with coordinating our “Talk to Your Doc” High School program and other projects related to informed and shared decision making in health care\. For more information about the Division please go to our website: [http://www\.chd\.ubc\.ca/dhcc/](http://www.chd.ubc.ca/dhcc/)

**Qualifications:** project coordination experience; qualitative and quantitative data analysis skills; well\-developed organizational skills; experience liaising with a range of community groups; experience in project management; experience interviewing others\.

**Hours:** 10 hours / week \(flexible to work around student schedules\)

**Wage:** $19\.84/hour

**To Apply:** Please send your resume/CV along with a cover letter and class schedule to Cathy Kline at [cckline@mail\.ubc\.ca](mailto:cckline@mail.ubc.ca) by **September 4** **\[th\]**\.
