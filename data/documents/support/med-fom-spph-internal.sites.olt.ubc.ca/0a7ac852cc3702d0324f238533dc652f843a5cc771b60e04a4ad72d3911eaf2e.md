Delegation from Japanese Automobile Manufacturers Association meet with Dr\. Hugh Davies and Dr\. Michael Brauer

Drs Hugh Davies and Michael Brauer hosted a delegation from the Japanese Automobile Manufacturers Association on October 15, 2014\.

The delegation was interested in learning about the novel research being conducted in the Occupational and Environmental Health Division, in collaboration with Population Data BC, on the health effects of traffic\-related air pollution and noise\.
