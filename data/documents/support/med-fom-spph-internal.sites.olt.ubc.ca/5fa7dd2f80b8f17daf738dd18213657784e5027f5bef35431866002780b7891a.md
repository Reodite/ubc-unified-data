MHA students are invited to submit your thoughts on Canadian health economics or health policy research for the annual Student Essay competition sponsored by the Justice Emmett M\. Hall Foundation\. Details are in the attached [student essay – invite EN](https://med-fom-spph.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/01/student-essay-invite-EN.pdf)

Deadline is **11:59 p\.m\. \(Eastern Time\) on Tuesday, March 31, 2015**\. Winners are recognized at the annual Canadian Health Services and Policy Research \(CAHSPR\) [conference](http://cahspr.ca/en/conferences/current/2015.) to be held in Montreal, Quebec from May 25 to 28, 2015\.
