Texts for the first two courses are available for pickup at the UBC Point Grey [Bookstore](http://bookstore.ubc.ca/customer-service/location-and-hours)\. It is suggested that students make arrangements to pick up their student ID at the same time at the [carding office](https://www.ubccard.ubc.ca/), also at the bookstore\. Students may apply for the student ID [online](https://www.ubccard.ubc.ca/UBCCardApplication/) and then pick up at the carding office\.

Please note that pickup at the Point Grey bookstore only applies to these first two texts; after classes start in September we will provide learning material in the **UBC Robson Square** classroom\.

Special Instructions for first two texts: Please go to the **Online Order desk** at the back of the Bookstore to sign for the texts with your ID\.

1.  **SPHA 510:** Policy Paradox
2.  **SPHA 551 :** Essential Epidemiology: An Introduction for Students and Health Professionals

**Remote students**: the first two texts have been couriered today\.
