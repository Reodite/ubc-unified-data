#### **Decision Analysis in Health Care**

**Friday May 15, 2015 at 3:30 pm EST \(12:30 pm PST\)**

Hosted by the Canadian Association for Population Therapeutics \(CAPT\)\. One of CAPT’s missions is to advance the health of Canadians through influencing policy and practice, and the research area of decision science is a timely and relevant one, so please join us in this webinar\. Our speaker is **Dr\. Nick Bansback**, Assistant Professor at SPPH and Associate Director, MHA Program\. Check out the attached [CAPTwebinarflyer\_ubc](https://med-fom-spph-internal.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/05/CAPTwebinarflyer_ubc.pdf) for more info\.
