SPPH Professor, **Dr\. Aslam Anis**, is working as a visiting researcher at the Office of Health Economics \([OHE](https://www.ohe.org/news/ohe-welcomes-dr-aslam-anis)\) in London, England, for the month of February\. While in the United Kingdom, Dr\. Anis will be working with Adrian Towse, Director of the OHE, and other members\. On February 23, he will be presenting some of his ongoing research at a seminar, titled, “[The impact of price\-cap regulations on entry and exit by generic pharmaceutical firms in Canada](https://www.ohe.org/events/impact-price-cap-regulations-entry-and-exit-generic-pharmaceutical-firms-canada)\.”

The OHE works to provide authoritative resources, research, and analyses in health economics, health policy and health statistics through both independent research and consulting work\.
