\**Musqueam sʔi:ɬqəy ̓ qeqən, Brent Sparrow Jr\., Musqueam First Nation*  
This post gives the story of the origin of the Musqueam First Nation name: there was once a lake called xʷməm̓qʷe:m \(Camosun Bog\), where the sʔi:ɬqəy̓ \(double\-headed serpent\) came from\. Everything the serpent passed over died and from its droppings a new plant grew, the məθkʷəy̓\. The people of long ago thus named that area xʷməθkʷəy̓əm \(Musqueam – place of the məθkʷəy̓\)\. The post represents Musqueam ancestors and the Nation’s connection with them and the land, through their teachings\. [Click here to read more\.](http://news.ubc.ca/2016/04/06/musqueam-post-dedicated-at-ubc-vancouver-campus-today/)
