MHA students are invited to take part in the Health Interprofessional Mixer on Monday, Sep\. 9 from 5:00 pm to 7:00 pm\. The event will be held at the Life Sciences Centre at UBC’s Vancouver campus and is a valuable educational opportunity to interact with other health disciplines and learn about the importance of collaboration across professions\. More info [online](http://www.chd.ubc.ca/event/health-interprofessional-mixer-hip-mixer) and interested students can [contact](http://spph.ubc.ca/programs-and-students/graduate-degree-programs/mha/contact/) the MHA Program office\.
