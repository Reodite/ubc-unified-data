## University Module Items results

The University encourages instructors to agree to publish the results of the University Module Items \(UMIs\) on [a secure University website](http://webapps.teacheval.ubc.ca/seot/welcome.action)\. *\(Note: Only published UMI results before Winter 2018 are available currently, as a new website is being developed\.\)*

Results are presented for some courses\. Reasons that courses may not appear include:

-   Course enrolment is less than 5
-   It is the instructor’s first year of teaching at UBC
-   It is the first time that a course has been offered at UBC
-   The course has multiple instructors
-   The instructor has chosen not to have results posted

Given the reasons above, no inferences should be drawn if results are not available\.

## Individual and aggregate results

As noted in the menu at left, information is also available about [individual results reports](https://ap-seoi-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/individual-results-reports/) \(including [individual results prior to 2018 Winter 1](https://ap-seoi-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/resources-for-instructors/accessing-your-evaluation-reports/)\), [aggregate reports](https://ap-seoi-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/aggregate-data-reports/) for units, programs, or faculties, and [conversion of previous metrics into new metrics](https://ap-seoi-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/metrics-conversion/)\.
