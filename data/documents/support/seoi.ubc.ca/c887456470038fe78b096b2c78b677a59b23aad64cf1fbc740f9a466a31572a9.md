#### UBC Okanagan Policy on Student Evaluation of Teaching

In 2005, in the establishment of UBC Okanagan, UBCO inherited UBC Vancouver’s Senate policy on Student Evaluation of Teaching\. With the rapid changes occurring in 2007 on the Okanagan campus it was determined not to revise the policy adopted by Vancouver at that time\. However, UBC Okanagan did include the option for faculty to opt in to sharing their quantitative student evaluations results with students\.

#### UBC Vancouver Policy on Student Evaluation of Teaching

Below is a copy of the [UBC Vancouver Senate Policy on Student Evaluation of Teaching, on the UBCV Senate website](https://senate.ubc.ca/vancouver/policies/student-evaluation-teaching)\.

Approved by the UBC Vancouver Senate on May 16, 2007, upon recommendation of the Teaching and Learning Committee, this policy is meant to replace all earlier Senate Policies on Student Evaluation\.
