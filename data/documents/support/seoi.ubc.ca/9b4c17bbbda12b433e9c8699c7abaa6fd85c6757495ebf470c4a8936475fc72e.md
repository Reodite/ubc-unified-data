We thank everyone who attended the open forum on the upcoming changes to the Student Experience of Instruction, on March 10, 2021\.

We will be providing summary notes from the event soon\. You can also see the slides from the forum: [March 2021 Open Forum slide deck with information about the changes](https://ap-seoi-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/03/SEOI-Open-Forum-March-10-2021.pdf) \(PDF\)\.
