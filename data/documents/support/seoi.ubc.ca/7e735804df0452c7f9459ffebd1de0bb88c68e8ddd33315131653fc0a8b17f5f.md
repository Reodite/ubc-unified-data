A [report on progress on the sixteen recommendations endorsed by UBCO and UBCV Senates in May of 2020](https://ap-seoi-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/05/SEOI-Senate-report-2021-VF-updated.pdf) was presented to Senate committees on both campuses in April 2021, and to both Senates in May 2021\. The report is also posted on the “Resources” page of this site\. It contains information about the testing process and timeline for new university module questions, plans for periodic testing of UBC results for bias, and more\.
