The UBC Physics Society lounge is room 307 in the Hennings building\. The building is located between the Irving K\. Barber Learning Center and Hebb Theater, just across from the AMS Nest\. The lounge is open to students on weekdays 8 am – 7 pm \(may change during exam season\)\. The lounge is a great place to hangout between classes, have study groups, or get work done in the silent study area\. There is a fridge and microwave available for Physsoc members to use\.

Contact the club through email or Facebook\. You can also join our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/xXKCS46) and contact council members directly or [join our mailing list](https://physsoc.phas.ubc.ca/contact/join-our-mailing-list/)\.
