UBC Physics Society memberships are $10 per school year and help us provide services, including but not limited to:

-   Producing/revising exam packages
-   The [private tutor forum](https://physsoc.phas.ubc.ca/academic/private-tutors/)
-   Setting up academic programs \(Beef &amp; Pizza, LaTeX review sessions, Math Methods workshops\)
-   Hosting social programs \(Wine &amp; Cheese, game nights, club collaborations\)
-   An awesome mixed study/social space on [Discord](https://discord.gg/xXKCS46)\!
-   Maintaining the lounge in Hennings 307

As a member and a student in a program offered by the Physics &amp; Astronomy Department, you can get 24/7 card access to the Hennings building\. Members also receive discounts on our academic programs, and our exam packs\!

To purchase a membership for the 2025/2026 academic year, please see [this page](https://physsoc.phas.ubc.ca/get-membership/)\.

If you would like to be added to our mailing list please fill out [this form](https://physsoc.phas.ubc.ca/contact/join-our-mailing-list/)\.
