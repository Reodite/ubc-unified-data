## Scholarly Communications

Scholarly communication is the ​process by which researchers create, publish and share their findings with the academic community and the broader public\. It includes traditional forms of publishing, but also the new and evolving systems of scholarship made possible in the digital age\.

Our expert team is here to support UBC faculty and students through workshops, guides and one\-to\-one consultations on a range of scholarly communication issues outlined below\.

[Open Access](https://scholcomm.ubc.ca/open-access/)

[Copyright](http://www.copyright.ubc.ca/)

[Research Metrics](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/citationmetricsworkshop)

[Data Management](http://researchdata.library.ubc.ca/)

[Open Education](https://scholcomm.ubc.ca/open-scholarship/open-education/)

[cIRcle Digital Repository](http://circle.ubc.ca/?login)

[Author Rights](https://scholcomm.ubc.ca/author-rights-publisher-agreements/)
