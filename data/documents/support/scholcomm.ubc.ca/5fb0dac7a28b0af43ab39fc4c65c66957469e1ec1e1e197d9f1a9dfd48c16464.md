UBC Library’s commitment to open access extends to securing licensing agreement and memberships with a variety of publishers and organizations\. This provides benefits to researchers at UBC, often through discounts on article publishing costs \(APCs\) for open access publishing\.  
  
Please note that licensing agreements and memberships are subject to change\. For more information about collection management, planning, and budgeting, refer to the [**Library website**](https://collections.library.ubc.ca/budget-and-planning/)\.  
  
For additional information about open access funding, visit [**Funding Opportunities**](https://scholcomm-7june2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/funding-opportunities/)  
  
**Discounts apply to current UBC faculty, students, and staff unless otherwise stated\.  
All discounts are in USD unless otherwise stated\.  
  
The A to Z list of discounts is organized by publisher name\. Look up the publishers’ name to find the journals available for discount**

### American Chemical Society \(ACS\)

$250

[Discount Available for all ACS Hybrid Journal Titles](https://pubs.acs.org/)

**Application Instructions:** The corresponding author must use an email address with a UBC suffix \(ubc\.ca\) and select UBC from the drop\-down box\. Confirm that the institutional affiliation stated in the body of the manuscript is the corresponding author’s affiliation\.

### Association for Computing Machinery \(ACM\)

100%

[Discount Available for all ACM Journals and Conference Proceedings](https://www.acm.org/publications/journals)

**Application Instructions:** The corresponding author must use an email address with a UBC suffix \(ubc\.ca\) and provide a valid ORCID for the ACM systems to properly identify them as an affiliated author\. There is no limit or maximum number of eligible articles\.

### BioChemical Society

100%

[Discount Available for all BCS Journal Titles](https://www.webofscience.com/wos/woscc/summary/cd02b5ef-f436-491e-8afd-f75daee49cdc-dd1db552/relevance/1)

**Application Instructions:** The corresponding author must use a valid institutional email ending with “@ubc\.ca” and select the University of British Columbia as their institution of affiliation\. ### BioMed Central \(BMC\)

15%

[Discount available for all BMC Journal Titles](https://www.biomedcentral.com/journals)

**Application Instructions:** Submitting from a UBC IP address or using your UBC email address will grant the membership discount automatically\. If submitting from a non\-UBC IP address, select UBC in the dropdown list as the affiliated institution and BMC will verify\. UBC affiliated open access articles are automatically deposited to [cIRcle](http://circle.ubc.ca/), UBC’s institutional repository\.

### Bioscientifica

15%

[Discount for UBC subscribed Journal Titles](https://www.webofscience.com/wos/woscc/summary/b4c5fa08-a10b-4da6-9e4d-96f35de4557c-dd1df784/relevance/1)

Corresponding author must use an email address with a UBC suffix \(ubc\.ca\) and select UBC from the drop\-down box\. Confirm that the institutional affiliation stated in the body of the manuscript is the corresponding author’s affiliation\.

### BMJ Case Reports

100%

[Fellowhisp discount for UBC subscribed Journal Titles](https://casereports.bmj.com/)

**Application Instructions**: [Login with your CWL](https://resources.library.ubc.ca/ubcauth/2335) to access the fellowship code for the discount\.

Open Access fees are separate from fellowship fees\. To learn more about open access costs, review the [BMJ Authors page](https://casereports.bmj.com/pages/authors#open_access)\.
