## [UBC Library research project explores Indigenous perspectives in open education resource development](https://scholcomm.ubc.ca/2023/01/16/ubc-library-research-project-explores-indigenous-perspectives-in-open-education-resource-development/)

By [Erin Fields](https://scholcomm.ubc.ca/author/efields/ "Posts by Erin Fields") on January 16, 2023

UBC librarians are embarking on a new collaborative research project that aims to address a fundamental problem in how open educational practices approach Indigenous Knowledges, and instead replicate colonial concepts of ownership and knowledge transfer\.
