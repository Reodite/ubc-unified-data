### Librarian Statement of Commitment to Open Scholarship

I publicly support the UBC Librarian Statement of Commitment to Open Scholarship and will strive to meet the aspirational goals outlined therein for the Library community and seek to engage in principles of openness in my discussions, decisions, and practices in UBC Library\.
