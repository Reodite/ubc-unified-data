On October 19, 2023, practice how to *drop, cover and hold on* at 10:19 a\.m\. during the annual, province\-wide [Great BC ShakeOut](https://www.shakeoutbc.ca/)\.

As part of ShakeOut, UBC will be testing its emergency notification system, [UBC Alert](https://ready.ubc.ca/get-informed/ubc-alert/)\. Students, faculty and staff will receive a test notification by text and voice message on October 19, 2023\.

**Learn more &amp; find resources on how to run a drill:** [ready\.ubc\.ca/shakeout](https://ready.ubc.ca/are-you-prepared-for-an-earthquake/)
