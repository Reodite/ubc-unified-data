Applications are open for two grants for initiatives supporting rural health, offered by the Rural Coordination Centre of BC \(RCCbc\)\. Applications for both grant programs are open until **Wednesday, April 30**\.

**[Rural Global Health Partnership Initiative](https://rccbc.ca/initiatives/rghpi/):** Are you a physician or medical trainee partnering on a project with an underserved community in British Columbia, Canada, or internationally? Apply to the Rural Global Health Partnership Initiative for a grant of up to $10,000 for projects \(including planetary health projects\) that enhance capacity for generalism in rural B\.C\. and foster reciprocal learning in diverse health systems and community contexts\.

**[Rural Physician Research Grant Program](https://rccbc.ca/initiatives/rprgp/):** The Rural Physician Research Grant Program empowers rural physician researchers — especially new aspiring researchers — to pursue innovative rural research projects that advance rural health in British Columbia\. Through this program, rural physician researchers can apply for research grants of up to $10,000 to support rural health research\.
