Over the past two years, the Faculty of Medicine Digital Solutions team has partnered with the Life Sciences Institute \(LSI\) to develop and enhance technology services that support the institute\.

Building on this collaboration, LSI is transitioning to **Spaces**, a new room booking system that provides a more consistent booking experience and makes it easier to find and book available spaces\.

As the first group to use Spaces, LSI users will have an opportunity to experience the new system, share feedback, and help shape the experience\. This initial rollout will provide valuable insights to inform and support any future expansion of Spaces across the Faculty of Medicine at a later date\.

---

## How to log into Spaces

####   **Please note: a login button will linked here once Spaces is live\.**

With your **CWL**, select “UBC Campus\-Wide Login\.”

Upon your first visit to Spaces, a user demo will play and you can set up your User Profile\.

## FAQ

### Q: How can I view my bookings?

**A:** Select “My Bookings” in the top\-left corner to view your upcoming and past bookings\.

### Q: How can I find a room?

**A**: Select “Resources” in the top\-left corner to browse or search for a specific resource\. 

### Q: Can I make a reoccurring booking?

**A**: Yes\. When creating a booking enable the “Reoccuring” toggle before proceeding to “Add Details\.”

### Q: How is sharing a booking different than swapping ownership?

**A**: Sharing a booking allows another user to access and manage the booking, but it remains also in your “My Bookings” list\. When using the “swap ownership” feature, the booking is transferred to another user, and you will no longer have access to it or see it under “My Bookings\.” 

### Q: How far in advance can I book LSI rooms?

**A**: The booking calendar in Spaces updates dynamically\. You can always book from your current date through to the last day of the following calendar year\.

### Q: I just logged in for the first time\. Why don’t I see any rooms?

**A**: All LSI rooms bookable through Spaces will populate for those who have preexisting bookings\. If this is your first time booking at LSI, please reach out to fom\.dm@ubc\.ca to be added to the user group\. Once you are added, the rooms will be visible upon your next login to Spaces\.
