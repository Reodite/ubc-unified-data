[UBC Alert](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/emergency/emergency-response/#alert) will be sending out a test notification to UBC Vancouver campus students, faculty, and staff via a phone call and text message on **Friday, January 17, 2025**\.

UBC Alert is the university’s mass notification system used to send alerts in urgent situations that pose an immediate safety or security risk to the community\. To ensure you are receiving notifications, make sure your mobile number is [up to date on Workday](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/personal-information/adding-changing-your-contact-information/),and consider downloading the UBC Safe app \([UBC Safe app Vancouver](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/ubc-safe-vancouver-app/), [UBC Safe app Okanagan](https://operations.ok.ubc.ca/security-and-emergencies/ubc-safeapp-and-ubc-alert/)\) to receive notifications for important updates for safety and security risks\.
