​A message from Dermot Kelleher, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, UBC\.

---

Please be advised that the Faculty of Medicine [Full Faculty Meeting](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/office-of-the-dean/governance/full-faculty-meetings/) will be held online via Zoom on **Tuesday, November 21** from 4:30–5:30 pm\.

## Pre\-registration required

Advanced registration for Full Faculty is now open\. All participants are required to register online by **4:15 pm on Tuesday, November 21**\. 

[Register to attend](https://rc.med.ubc.ca/redcap/surveys/?s=KA8YAJJTTL3CHMTK)

## Live electronic voting

-   In order to vote during the meeting, voting members are **required** to [register online](https://rc.med.ubc.ca/redcap/surveys/?s=KA8YAJJTTL3CHMTK) in advance;
-   Upon registration, voting members will receive a meeting agenda containing links to the voting platform\. Please follow in\-meeting instructions from the Chair to cast your vote\.

## Agenda

1.  Welcome, call to order, review of agenda
2.  Approval of the [Minutes of the May 16, 2023 Full Faculty meeting \(PDF\)](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/10/2.0_20230516_FoM_Full-Faculty_Minutes_DRAFT_C.pdf) \(*motion*\)
3.  Business arising from the Minutes
4.  Approval of the [actions taken by Faculty Executive \(PDF\)](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/4.0_202311.21_ActionsTakenByFEXSinceMay2023_V.0A.pdf) since the last meeting of the Faculty of Medicine \(*motion*\)
5.  [Report from the Dean \(PDF\)](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/5.0_20231121_FFReportOfTheDean_V.0A.pdf) to the Faculty of Medicine
6.  Master of Global Health \(*motion*\)
7.  Canada’s Immuno\-Engineering and Biomanufacturing Hub \(CIEBH\)
8.  Adjournment \(*motion*\)
