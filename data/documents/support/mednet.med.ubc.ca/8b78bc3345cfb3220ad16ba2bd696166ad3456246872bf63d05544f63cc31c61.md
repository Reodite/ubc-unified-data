A message from Amy Salmon, Director, Clinical Faculty Affairs, UBC Faculty of Medicine\.

---

Thank you to everyone who attended an information session this fall to learn more about the recent updates to the Policy on Clinical Faculty Appointments\.

In consultation with clinical faculty, administrators and leadership, these updates were made in direct response to feedback from clinical faculty to clarify policies and processes for appointments, expand opportunities for promotion, and align rank criteria to more fully recognize the distinctive and significant contributions of clinical faculty\.

If you were not able to attend one of the information sessions, a summary and frequently asked questions are now available on the Faculty of Medicine website\.

[Read the summary](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/appointment-policies/summary-of-updates/ "Summary of Updates to the Policy on Clinical Faculty Appointments")

Please reach out to the Clinical Faculty Affairs team at [ocfa\.med@ubc\.ca](mailto:ocfa.med@ubc.ca) with any questions\.
