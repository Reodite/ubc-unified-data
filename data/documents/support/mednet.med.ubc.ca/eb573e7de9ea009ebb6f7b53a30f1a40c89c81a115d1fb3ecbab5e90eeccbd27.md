Join BC Children’s Hospital Research Institute, Women’s Health Research Institute and BC Mental Health &amp; Substance Use Services Research Institute on **February 9, 7–8:30 pm** to engage live with inspiring health leaders in an in\-person and virtual event\.

Participation is open to high school students, university students, healthcare professionals and members of the general public\.

To enter the registration lottery draw, complete the registration form by **Sunday, January 22**\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://www.bcchr.ca/women-in-science "Women in Science | BC Children's Hospital Research Institute")
