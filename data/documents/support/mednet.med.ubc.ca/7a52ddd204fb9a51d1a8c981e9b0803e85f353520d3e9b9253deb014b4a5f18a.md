Researchers at UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan are invited to submit proposals for Health After 2020, a UBC Health program offering up to $10,000 per team to support collaborations and capacity building with partners from outside UBC\.

The aims of Health After 2020 are to:

-   Promote interdisciplinary and cross\-institutional collaborations that produce and disseminate new knowledge related to health and health equity;
-   Foster knowledge exchange and dialogue and build capacity in innovative knowledge translation methods; and,
-   Create opportunities for engagement between researchers and the public\.

The deadline to submit a proposal is November 15\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://health.ubc.ca/news-events/stories/ha2020kt)
