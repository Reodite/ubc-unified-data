[Dr\. Ralph Keeler](https://vancouversunandprovince.remembering.ca/obituary/ralph-keeler-1087621289 "Ralph Keeler Obituary | Vancouver Sun and Province") passed away on April 28, 2023\.

A Professor Emeritus, Dr\. Keeler taught in the former Department of Physiology \(now the Department of Cellular &amp; Physiological Sciences\)\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Friday, May 19, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/06/05/ralph-keeler/ "Ralph Keeler | Flag Lowering - UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
