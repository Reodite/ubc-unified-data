In recognition of the UN International Day of Persons with Disabilities \(December 3\), take part in a series of webinars running **November 27 to December 1** and enhance your disability inclusion literacy\. 

The series is offered by the [Centre for Workplace Accessibility](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/workplace-accessibility/centre-workplace-accessibility), a central hub for resources, tools, and programs that help remove barriers for UBC faculty and staff with disabilities or ongoing medical conditions\.

Topics include inclusion in hiring, teaching and learning, and deaf culture in the workplace\.

[See all workshops &amp; register](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/news/october-30-2023/enhance-your-disability-inclusion-literacy-online-events "Enhance your disability inclusion literacy with these online events, Nov 27 - Dec 1 | UBC Human Resources")

> ## In the Faculty of Medicine: Disability in the Workplace interactive workshop
> 
> Deepen your understanding of disability awareness and inclusion in the workplace by joining *Disability in the Workplace*, an online workshop developed specifically for Faculty of Medicine faculty and staff led by postdoctoral fellow Dr\. Jon Breen\.
> 
> Sessions include interactive discussions with participants, interviews with researchers and people with disabilities, and an opportunity to identify and analyze the many disability\-related realities of the workplace\.
> 
> Join the next workshop on **Tuesday, December 5**, or see the full list of workshop dates\.
> 
> [Learn more &amp; register](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/disability-in-the-workplace-interactive-workshop/ "Disability in the Workplace interactive workshop")
