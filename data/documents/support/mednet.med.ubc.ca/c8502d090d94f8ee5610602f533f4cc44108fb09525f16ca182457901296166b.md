An invitation from the Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion\.

---

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/We-Own-These-Stories-768x388.png)

Please join the Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion \(REDI\) for the next [Indigenous Initiatives Speaker Series](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/indigenous-initiatives-speakers-series/ "Indigenous Speaker Series - Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion")\.

The event will feature Louise Bernice Halfe – Sky Dancer, Parliamentary Poet Laureate, who will talk about the Cree expression *Ni tipeyihtenan ōhi acimōwina*, meaning, “We own these stories\. The marrow\.” She will speak about the foundations of Indigenous writing and how her poetry and stories are informed by connections to communities and ancient teachings\.

**Topic:** *Ni tipeyihtenan ōhi acimōwina* \| We own these stories\. The Marrow\. A Conversation with Sky Dancer about Writing and Righting Our Truth and Place in Canada

**When:** Friday, March 25, 2022 from 12:00\-1:30 pm

**What will I learn?** You will learn about the need for creative and meaningful connections with Indigenous peoples\.

[Join the Event](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/indigenous-initiatives-speakers-series/ni-tipeyihtenan-ohi-acimowina-we-own-these-stories-the-marrow/ "Ni tipeyihtenan ōhi acimōwina | We Own These Stories. The Marrow. | Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion")

*Please note pre\-registration is not required\.  
The event will be recorded and made available on [redi\.med\.ubc\.ca](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/ "Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion")\.*
