[Dr\. Andrew \(Andy\) Chalmers](https://www.dignitymemorial.com/en-ca/obituaries/parksville-bc/andrew-chalmers-12890002 "Andrew Chalmers Obituary") passed away on May 14, 2026\.

A respected rheumatologist and dedicated educator, Dr\. Chalmers was a Professor Emeritus in the Department of Medicine, where he previously served as Head of the Division of Rheumatology\.

Dr\. Chalmers also served the Faculty as Associate Dean, Undergraduate Curriculum, playing a key role in a review and redesign of the undergraduate medical program curriculum\. He was recognized for excellence in teaching with awards including the Killam Teaching Prize and the Canadian Rheumatology Association’s Teacher\-Education Award\.

As a rheumatologist, Dr\. Chalmers focused on improving quality of life for patients living with chronic conditions including rheumatoid arthritis and lupus, holding regular clinics in remote communities across B\.C\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Chalmers from the Department of Medicine\.](https://medicine.med.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-andrew-chalmers-division-of-rheumatology/ "In Memoriam: Dr. Andrew Chalmers | Department of Medicine")

The B\.C\., Musqueam and UBC [flags will be lowered on Thursday, July 2, 2026](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/flag-lowering/ "Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies &amp; Events") in remembrance\.
