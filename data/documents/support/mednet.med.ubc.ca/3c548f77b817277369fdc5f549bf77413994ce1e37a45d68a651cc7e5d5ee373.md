Faculty members at UBC Vancouver, submit a proposal to work with the Emerging Media Lab team to explore how to incorporate emerging and immersive technologies into teaching, learning or research projects, including \.

UBC’s Emerging Media Lab is an experimental lab space where faculty, staff, and students from all disciplines collaborate to evolve learning by creating tools and techniques using emerging media technologies, such as virtual or augmented reality\. Explore [current and past project examples](https://eml.ubc.ca/projects/) on the Emerging Media Lab website\.

Submit a project proposal for the winter 2026 intake by **Wednesday, July 15**\.
