The UBC [Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/) \(CTLT\) supports faculty, TAs, grad students and post\-docs with pedagogical resources, consultation, professional development and events\. Explore these upcoming events to support your teaching\.

## Upcoming dates

### [Instructional Skills Workshop for Faculty](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/all-our-programs/instructional-skills-workshop-isw/) \(hybrid\)

This workshop series is designed for faculty or postdoctoral fellows who are new to teaching, and those who wish to refresh and enhance their skills\.

Upcoming 2023 dates:

-   May 9, 11, 16 &amp; 18
-   June 6, 7 &amp; 8

### [Program for Open Scholarship and Education](https://open.ubc.ca/pose/) \(online\)

Running May–July 2023, this 3\-month flexible and blended program helps participants develop an understanding of open scholarship in higher education and the intersections of each area: Open access, education, data and research\.

Register by **May 1, 2023**\.

### [Course Design Intensive](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/all-our-programs/course-design-intensive/) \(online\)

This 3\-day intensive program helps participants work, individually and collaboratively, to design or re\-design a course they will be offering in September or later\.

Register by **June 5, 2023**\.

### [Teaching Development Program for New Faculty](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/faculty-programs/new-to-ubc/teaching-development-program-for-new-faculty/) \(multi\-access\)

This cohort program, running August 2023 to April 2024, supports faculty members across UBC campuses in their professional growth as teachers\.

Applications open **April 25, 2023**\.

Find out more about the program at an [online information session](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/faculty-programs/new-to-ubc/teaching-development-program-for-new-faculty/#2023_Information_Sessions "2023 Information Sessions - Teaching Development Program for New Faculty") on April 25, May 11, or June 12\.
