The UBC Emeritus College is accepting nominations for two awards that honour the research endeavours and community leadership of UBC emeriti\.

These awards shine a light on retired UBC Vancouver faculty who continue to pursue scholarly endeavours with outstanding results and those who display exceptional leadership in community service:

-   [Award for Excellence in Innovative and Creative Endeavours](https://emerituscollege.ubc.ca/awards-and-support/ubc-emeritus-college-award-excellence-innovative-and-creative-endeavours): Recognizes those who, since attaining emeritus status, have demonstrated outstanding achievements in research or artistic creation\.
-   [President’s Award for Distinguished Service](https://emerituscollege.ubc.ca/awards-and-support/presidents-award-distinguished-service-ubc-emeriti): Honours those who show exceptional leadership in volunteer community services\.

Submit a nomination by **Wednesday, February 25, 2026**\.
