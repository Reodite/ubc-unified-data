A message from Mieke Koehoorn, Dean *pro tem*, Faculty of Medicine\.

---

[2025 Faculty of Medicine Awards, Celebrating an extraordinary group of people](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/FoMAwards-2025_MedNet_Banner-940x313.jpg)

I am delighted to announce the recipients of the 2025 Faculty of Medicine Awards\.

These awards recognize and celebrate the extraordinary contributions of faculty and staff who embody our values of respect, integrity, compassion, collaboration and equity — helping us to transform health for everyone\.

The 2025 award categories include:

-   Bill and Marilyn Webber Lifetime Achievement Award
-   Awards for Excellence in Mentoring Early Career Faculty
-   Applegarth Staff Service Awards
-   Dean’s Staff Awards
-   Clinical Faculty Awards
-   CME\-CPD Awards
-   UBC Killam Teaching Prizes *\(previously announced\)*
-   UBC Killam Research Prizes *\(previously announced\)*
-   Distinguished Achievement Awards *\(previously announced\)*

[2025 Award Recipients](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/faculty-of-medicine-awards-2025/)

Please join me in congratulating all of our 2025 award recipients\. I look forward to formally celebrating their accomplishments at our Annual Awards Reception for recipients this fall\.
