The MedIT Service Desk is a single point of contact for all service requests and inquiries\.

---

### Email or call the MedIT Service Desk

[medit\.servicedesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:medit.servicedesk@ubc.ca)

1\.877\.266\.0666

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Option 1 – IT Support** | Monday\-Friday 8:00am – 4:30pm |
| **Option 2 – Videoconferencing Support** | Monday\-Friday 7:00am – 5:00pm |

### How the MedIT Service Desk can help

-   Acquiring and installing software
-   Getting new equipment \(e\.g\. ordering a new computer, headset or webcam\)
-   Configuring your devices and software 
-   Information about device and data security
-   Inquiring about training \(e\.g\. Videoconference training\)
-   IT support for Faculty of Medicine services and equipment \(e\.g\. logging on to your computer or email, CWL account issues, Virtual Private Network \(VPN\), or MedNet\)
-   Moving offices or equipment
-   Questions and concerns about outages
-   File server support and information about storage options 

Not sure we are the right people to ask? Ask us anyway\!
