## Foundational Guidance

**Core institutional guidance, policies, and expectations for the responsible use of AI\.**

**Broad, university\-wide guidance that applies to all faculty and staff using AI\.**

-   [UBC Generative AI website](https://genai.ubc.ca/): university\-wide principles, training, and examples\.
-   [Privacy Matters @ UBC](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/): privacy requirements for AI use, including:
    -   [Risk\-Rating Guidance Reference](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2025-10/PIA_Reviewed_AI_2025-09.pdf)
    -   [UBC Information Security Standards \(ISS\)](https://cio.ubc.ca/information-security-standards/U1): rules for classifying data and systems, access controls and lifecycle needs for AI\-related systems\.

**When you need to check if an AI tool or workflow is allowed\.**

-   [UBC Privacy Impact Assessment \(PIA\) Guidelines for AI Solutions](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/PIA_Guidelines_GenAI.pdf): use of publicly available AI tools from a privacy and information security standpoint\.
-   UBC [PIA Process Overview](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/pia-stra/pia-process-overview): UBC Self\-Service Portal to submit and track PIA inquiries and requests\.
-   [UBC Policy SC14](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/information-systems-policy/): administrative heads’ responsibilities for information security\.
-   UBC FoM [Information Technology Governance Cybersecurity  Policy and Procedures](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/01/Information-Technology-Governance-Cybersecurity-Policy-and-Procedures.pdf): clarity on the application of UBC Policy SC14 \(Acceptable Use and Security of UBC Electronic Information and UBC Information Security Standards\)\.
-   [BC Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act \(FIPPA\)](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96165_00): provincial laws on how personal information is collected, stored, and used\.

## In the Workplace

*Practical guidance for faculty and staff using AI tools to streamline everyday work\.* 

**When you are looking for AI learning resources, training opportunities, or support\.** 

-   [Building AI Literacy](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?page_id=35543&amp;preview=true): access curated learning resources, training, and practical guidance\. 
-   Contact the Office of the Deputy CIO: [dcio\.medicine@ubc\.ca](mailto:dcio.medicine@ubc.ca) for guidance, support with AI adoption or other use cases\. 

#### **M365 Copilot Chat** 

UBC has integrated **this GenAI tool of choice with the UBC M365** suite of tools\.  

-   **To access**: Open Copilot Chat with your UBC login at [m365\.cloud\.microsoft](https://m365.cloud.microsoft/chat/) 
-   **Data &amp; Security Limits:** Approved for low to medium\-risk data only per [UBC’s Information Security Standard \(ISS U1\)](https://cio.ubc.ca/information-security-standards/U1) 
-   **Training and Support:** Register for [UBC IT training sessions for M365](https://it.ubc.ca/microsoft-365/general-training-microsoft-365), including Copilot\. 
-   **Learn more:** Read the full rollout and updates on the [UBC IT Copilot Chat News Page](https://it.ubc.ca/news/june-06-2025/m365ubc-now-copilot-chat)\. 

#### **Zoom AI Companion \(Meeting Summaries\)**  

Available for automatic meeting documentation \(*New as of April 2, 2026*\)\. 

-   **Learn More:** Review the [Zoom AI Companion Terms of Service at UBC](https://it.ubc.ca/services/teaching-learning-tools/zoom-video-conferencing/zoom-ai-companion-terms-service)\. 

#### **Third\-Party Tools** 

If you choose to use tools outside of standard UBC licensing, you must check their compliance status first\. [GenAI tools and privacy risks assessment can be found on the UBC AI website](https://genai.ubc.ca/privacy-and-risk/)\. Each tool includes its risk profile and use conditions\.  

#### **Critical Tool Restrictions:** 

-   **ChatGPT &amp; Claude:** May **only** be used with low\-risk data\. Never input personal or confidential information\. 
-   **DeepSeek:** The use of DeepSeek is **restricted at UBC\.** Review the official mandate via the [UBC Privacy Matters](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/i-want/safe-deepseek-ubc)\.  

**When you need to know how to store, retain, or dispose of AI‑generated content\.**  

-   [FoM Records Management Guidelines](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/facilities/records-management/) 

**When you want to make sure AI\-drafted content and communications** **align with UBC’s editorial standards\.**

-   [Using AI Tools: Basic Principles for UBC Communicators](https://assets.brand.ubc.ca/downloads/Gen_AI_Guidelines_2025_07_30.pdf)
