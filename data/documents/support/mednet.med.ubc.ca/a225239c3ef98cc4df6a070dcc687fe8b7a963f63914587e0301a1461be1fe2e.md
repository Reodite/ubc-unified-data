Registrations are open for the Canadian Cancer Immunotherapy Consortium Annual Meeting  and UHN Collaborative Centre for Immunology to Immunotherapy Symposium, both taking place this October in Toronto, Ontario\.

The Canadian Cancer Immunotherapy Consortium \(CCIC\) Annual Meeting will take place on **October 21–22, 2026**\. This year’s program will bring together leading researchers, clinicians, trainees and industry partners to discuss advances in cancer immunotherapy and translational immunology research\.

Trainees and early\-career investigators are encouraged to submit abstracts by September 21, with selected abstracts to be considered for oral presentations\.

The CCIC meeting will be held back\-to\-back with the UHN Collaborative Centre for Immunology to Immunotherapy \(Ci2i\) Symposium on **October 23, 2026** at the same venue, providing an additional opportunity for scientific exchange in immunology and immunotherapy across immune\-mediated diseases, including autoimmunity, transplantation and infection\. The program will also include opportunities for networking and interdisciplinary collaboration, with a reception following the scientific sessions\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://event.fourwaves.com/ccic-ci2i-2026/ "CCIC Annual Meeting &amp; Ci2i Annual Symposium 2026")
