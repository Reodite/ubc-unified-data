Applications are now open for Michael Smith Health Research BC’s Research Trainee 2025 competition\.

The program provides post\-doctoral fellows or health professional researchers with salary support for dedicated research activities, allowing early\-career researchers to focus on conducting high\-quality, innovative research\. Since 2001, more than 1,400 Research Trainee awards have helped develop, attract and retain the province’s best and brightest health researchers and support the advancement of world\-class health research in B\.C\. 34 [UBC trainees and postdoctoral fellows were awarded funding](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/announcements/ubc-research-trainees-receive-health-research-bc-awards-2024/ "UBC research trainees receive Health Research BC awards - MedNet") through the 2024 competition\.

Submit a letter of intent by **4:30 pm on Thursday, December 12, 2024**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://healthresearchbc.ca/news_article/2025-research-trainee-competition-call-for-applications/ "2025 Research Trainee competition: call for applications | Health Research BC")
