Join the Faculty of Medicine’s [Building the Future – Research Trainee Day and Lecture Series](https://grad-postdoc.med.ubc.ca/professional-development/events/building-the-future/) on March 19\.

This event will bring members of the Faculty of Medicine together to share and learn the diverse and innovative research work of graduate students and postdoctoral fellows\.

-   **Date:** Thursday, March 19, 2026 from 8:30 am–4:30 pm
-   **Location:** In\-person at the [Life Sciences Centre, UBC Vancouver](https://maps.ubc.ca/?code=LSC)

Register to attend by **Monday, March 9**\.

[See details &amp; register](https://grad-postdoc.med.ubc.ca/professional-development/events/building-the-future/ "Building the Future Research Trainee Day | Graduate &amp; Postdoctoral Education")
