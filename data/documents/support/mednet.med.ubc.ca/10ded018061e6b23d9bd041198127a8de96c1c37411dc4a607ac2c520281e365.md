## General Grant &amp; Award Eligibility

An applicant is eligible to apply to a granting agency if he/she meets the funding opportunity’s eligibility criteria\. However, [UBC’s Policy LR2](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/files/2022/05/Research-Policy_LR2.pdf) states that the following UBC persons are eligible to be PG account holders:

-   UBC Persons who have a tenure stream faculty appointment as:
    1.  Professor,
    2.  Associate Professor,
    3.  Assistant Professor,
    4.  Instructor,
    5.  Senior Instructor,
    6.  Professor of Teaching, or
    7.  comparable Emeritus Status
-   UBC Persons who have one of the following faculty term appointments without review and also have the prior written approval of both the appropriate Department Head and Dean:
    1.  Professor,
    2.  Associate Professor,
    3.  Assistant Professor,
    4.  Instructor,
    5.  Senior Instructor,
    6.  Professor of Teaching,
    7.  Adjunct Professor,
    8.  Clinical,
    9.  Honorary, or
    10.  Research Associate
-   Post\-Doctoral Fellows solely where the Funding Terms for a research project specifically require it and they have the prior written approval of both the appropriate Department Head and Dean\.

A [Research Project Information Form](https://research.ubc.ca/support-resources/forms-tools-resources/research-project-information-form) signed by the appropriate individuals will be accepted as “written approval”\.

Accounts will not be granted to students or visitors\. UBC Persons with positions not addressed above will require the written approval of their Department Head, Dean, **and the Provost**\.

## Salary Award Eligibility

An external salary award is defined as a competitive, peer\-reviewed award that is offered by research institutes, provincial, national, or international agencies and is paid as salary to the awardee/faculty member\. Applications for such awards are normally supported by the Faculty, are an important part of the planning process of the Faculty, and must be approved by the Department Head/School Director and the Dean\. External salary awards, such as new investigator awards, from organizations including Michael Smith Foundation for Health Research \(MSFHR\), Canadian Institutes of Health Research \(CIHR\), as well as Canada Research Chairs, carry with them explicit understanding that the individual will devote 60\-80% of their time to research which may impact their contribution to educational, administrative and clinical roles\.

-   Assistant Professors, Associate Professors and Professors in tenure track or grant tenure track appointments are eligible to apply for external salary awards\.
-   Assistant Professors, Associate Professors and Professors in partner or clinical appointments may be eligible to apply for external salary awards\. Please contact [Lily Nizami](mailto:lily.nizami@ubc.ca) to verify your eligibility\.

### For individuals who do not currently hold an eligible Faculty of Medicine appointment:

Department Heads/School Directors intending to submit applications for external salary awards \(such as MSFHR, CIHR, etc\.\) on behalf of individuals who do NOT currently hold an eligible Faculty of Medicine \(FoM\) appointment must first identify the need for this position and will still be required to follow the FoM recruitment procedures for such appointments\. If advertising or a request for a waiver of advertising is not possible prior to submitting an application for the external salary award, the Department Head/School Director will need to demonstrate the following:

1.  Departmental colleague support for the appointment \(e\.g\. departmental standing committee vote\); and
2.  Confirmed funding support for the salary outside of that provided by the award, benefit and salary increase costs, housing assistance, start up and, if stipulated by the award, provisions of funding beyond the end of the term of the award, using the Recruitment Planner\.

The recruitment and advertisement process will be the same as for all other full\-time stream faculty appointments \(for current FoM procedures please visit the [Hiring Faculty](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/hiring-faculty/) page\)\.

For questions regarding recruitment and advertising, please contact your FoM Faculty Appointments and Recruitment Management team representative:

-   [Managing &amp; Developing Faculty](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/hr/managing-faculty/)

### For individuals who currently hold an eligible Faculty of Medicine faculty appointment:

The Faculty of Medicine Research Office requires several documents for all salary award applications\. You will also require a number of signatures in order to complete your application package\. Please review the [Signature Process](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/) and [Required Documents](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/required-documents/) sections of our website\.
