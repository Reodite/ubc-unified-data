UBC faculty, staff and students are invited to develop skills to communicate their research effectively for policy impact with the UBC Centre for the Study of Democratic Institutions \(CSDI\) Policy Communications Clinic\.

The clinic includes a range of interactive hybrid [workshops](https://democracy.ubc.ca/cpcc/workshops/) and personalized [consulting hours](https://democracy.ubc.ca/cpcc/cpcc-consulting-hours/) to prepare you to contribute to public policy conversations\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://democracy.ubc.ca/cpcc/ "Policy Communications Clinic | Centre for the Study of Democratic Institutions")
