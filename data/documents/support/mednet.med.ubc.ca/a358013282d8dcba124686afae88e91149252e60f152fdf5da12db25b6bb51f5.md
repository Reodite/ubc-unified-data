Information regarding supply management and procurement of equipment, furniture and supplies\.

---

## Furniture:

-   **Dean’s Office:** Contact the [Facilities Manager](mailto:eileen.koshi@ubc.ca) for all furniture requests\.
-   **Departments/Schools/Centres/Institutes/Programs:** Contact your unit administrator for more information\.

### Additional Resources:

-   Go to the [Sustainable Purchasing Guide Furniture Section](http://www.sustain.ubc.ca/sites/sustain.ubc.ca/files/uploads/pdfs/Furniture.pdf) and [ReUse\-it\! UBC](https://reuseit.ubc.ca/) to find and exchange low\-value items between units on Point Grey campus\.

## Landlines:

-   **Dean’s Office:** Contact the [Dean’s Office Office Manager](mailto:satoshi.iura@ubc.ca) for all landline requests\.
-   **Departments/Schools/Centres/Institutes/Programs:** Contact your unit administrator for more information\.

### Additional Resources:

-   [UBC Voice Services](https://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/telephones) assist with the provision of landline telephone services for Faculty of Medicine units based at Point Grey campus\. 
-   At hospital sites, phone &amp; voice services are provided by the health authority\.  

## Supplies:

-   Office supplies are managed within each unit\. 
-   **Dean’s Office:** Contact the [Dean’s Office Purchasing Coordinator](mailto:facilities.coordinator@ubc.ca)  to place an order, or for more information\.
-   **Departments/Schools/Centres/Institutes/Programs:** Contact your unit administrator for more information\.

### Stationery and Business Cards:

-   **To order:** Faculty of Medicine stationery and business cards are ordered through Dean’s Office, Office Coordinator\.  
    -   To initiate a business card order, fill out this [form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/Business-Card-Template-Order-Form.docx) and submit it to the [Facilities Coordinator](mailto:facilities.coordinator@ubc.ca) Proof will be provided upon completion\. 
-   Please read the [stationery guidelines](https://brand.ubc.ca/guidelines/?login) before ordering\.
-   Please ensure the “Faculty of Medicine” is included in the address line on all orders\.
-   UBC and XGS have established a strategic partnership that guarantees best price on printing, brand compliance and easy delivery to any UBC office\.  XGS is the only approved supplier of UBC stationery and business cards; **no other printer has been supplied with the authorized templates**\.
