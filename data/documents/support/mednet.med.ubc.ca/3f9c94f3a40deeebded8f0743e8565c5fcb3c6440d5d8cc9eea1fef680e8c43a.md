Register to hear an in\-person conversation with Dr\. Srinivas Murthy \(Department of Pediatrics\) on **Tuesday, April 29 from 12–1 pm**\.

Hosted as part of the Global Health Spotlight Series by the Institute for Global Health at BC Children’s Hospital and BC Women’s Hospital \+ Health Centre, Dr\. Murthy will speak with Dr\. Gina Ogilvie \(School of Population and Public Health\) on his journey to becoming a global health leader — highlighting his defining moments, lessons learned and current projects\.

-   **Date:** Tuesday, April 29, 2025 from 12–1 pm
-   **Location:** Room 2108 BCCHRI, BC Children’s Hospital, Vancouver

[Learn more &amp; register](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/global-health-spotlight-series-in-conversation-tickets-1254746513599 "Global Health Spotlight Series - In Conversation")
