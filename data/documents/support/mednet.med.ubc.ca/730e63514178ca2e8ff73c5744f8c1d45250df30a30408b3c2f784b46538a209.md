Join the Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion \(REDI\) for *[The Nature of All Things Indigenous: Discussing the Changing Climate of Truth, Reconciliation and Nurturing Connections](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/the-nature-of-all-things-indigenous-discussing-the-changing-climate-of-truth-reconciliation-and-nurturing-connections/)* with guests Dr\. David Suzuki and Dr\. Shane Pointe\.

-   **Date**: Tuesday, September 23, 2025, 9 am–4 pm
-   **Location**: In\-person at the [Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre](https://maps.ubc.ca/?code=ALUM), UBC Vancouver  
    *This session will also be streamed online*

[Register now](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0OjB6gf2SQlPS86 "Register for The Nature of All Things Indigenous - UBC Qualtrics")

Derek K Thompson – *Čaabať Bookwilla \| Suhiltun*, Hereditary Chief and Director of Indigenous Engagement, invites you to bear witness to this important conversation with Dr\. David Suzuki, award\-winning scientist, broadcaster, and renowned environmental activist and supporter of Indigenous knowledge systems, and Dr\. Shane Pointe, Knowledge Keeper of the Musqueam First Nation and recipient of the inaugural Honorary Doctorate of Original Laws from the Native Education College\.

Together, they will share their perspectives on climate change, truth, reconciliation and our shared responsibility to care for Mother Earth — learning from Indigenous traditions of respectful coexistence\. You are invited to join in our efforts to heal, to come together, and to anchor our commitments to do and be better — for the present and for the future\.

Explore the [program and speaker bios](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/the-nature-of-all-things-indigenous-discussing-the-changing-climate-of-truth-reconciliation-and-nurturing-connections/) on the REDI website\.
