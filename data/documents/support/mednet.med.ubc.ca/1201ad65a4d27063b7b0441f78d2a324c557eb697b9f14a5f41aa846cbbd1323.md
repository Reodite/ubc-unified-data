## International Scientific Advisory Committee 

ISAC is comprised of global experts in MS, immunology, cell therapies and bioengineering\. They provide scientific guidance on strategic priorities, identify opportunities for collaboration, and help increase visibility of the MS Research Network\.

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/HaroldAtkins_ISAC.jpg)

## Steering Committee

The Steering Committee is comprised of leadership from FoM Office of Research, DMCBH, ATM, VCH and SBME\. They inform the overall strategy of the Network and provide oversight of the MS Research Network’s overall progress\.
