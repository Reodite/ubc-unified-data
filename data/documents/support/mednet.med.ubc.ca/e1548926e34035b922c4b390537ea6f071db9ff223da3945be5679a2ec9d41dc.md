Since the opening of the War Memorial Gym in 1951, UBC has held a ceremony commemorating **November 11**\. This year, 2023 will mark seventy\-three years that UBC has hosted a Remembrance Day ceremony\.

This special ceremony is an opportunity for the UBC community to honour and remember all those who served in times of war, military conflict and peace\.
