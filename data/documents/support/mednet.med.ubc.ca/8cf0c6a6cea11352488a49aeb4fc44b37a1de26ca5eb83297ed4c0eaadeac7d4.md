## Overview

The one45 Security Audit occurs annually and ensures only active Faculty of Medicine Administrators and \*Faculty have accounts in the one45 system\.  The security audit allows group owner administrators to manage user access to their program groups by assigning them specific administrative permissions\. 

\*Faculty with Administrative Access

***Learner accounts are omitted from the audit process***
