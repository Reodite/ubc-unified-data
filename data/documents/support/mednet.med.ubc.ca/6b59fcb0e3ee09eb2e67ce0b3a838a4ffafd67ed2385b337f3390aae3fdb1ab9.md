[Dr\. Neil J\. Yorkston](https://psychiatry.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-neil-j-yorkston-professor-emeritus/ "In Memoriam: Dr. Neil J. Yorkston, Professor Emeritus - UBC Department of Psychiatry") passed away on February 20, 2023\.

An accomplished scholar and clinician, Dr\. Yorkston was a professor emeritus and former head of the Department of Psychiatry\.

Dr\. Yorkston was a renowned researcher across diverse fields of expertise, including psychopharmacological studies in schizophrenia and psychotherapies for patients with personality disorders\. Following his retirement, he remained connected with the Department of Psychiatry, including regular attendance at Annual Research Days\.

Read [more about Dr\. Yorkston’s life](https://vancouversunandprovince.remembering.ca/obituary/neil-yorkston-1087238889 "Neil Yorkston Obituary | Vancouver Sun and Province")\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Saturday, April 1, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/04/13/neil-james-yorkston/ "Neil James Yorkston Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
