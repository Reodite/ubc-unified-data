UBC Library and UBC IT have launched a major project to upgrade the library management and discovery systems\. The project will provide a more stable and future\-ready foundation for how library resources are managed and delivered across both UBC campuses\.

As part of this upgrade, UBC Library will move to a new search and discovery tool, called Primo, in summer 2027\.

See the timeline and explore [frequently asked questions](https://about.library.ubc.ca/news/library-management-platform/faq/) on the UBC Library website\.
