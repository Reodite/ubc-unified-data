[Dr\. Susan Small](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-susan-small/) passed away on January 11, 2022\.

A practicing audiologist, Dr\. Susan Small was the director of the Pediatric Audiology Lab in the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences\. She was also an associate professor and Hamber Professor in Clinical Audiology in the School since 2014\.
