UBC Vancouver faculty members, get help furthering your research while supporting international students in their workplace learning goals through the Work Learn International Undergraduate Research Award Program\.

The program provides UBC Vancouver faculty members an award subsidy to hire international undergraduate students to work on their research projects full time during the summer session \(May 1 to August 31, 2026\)\.

Submit a research proposal through UBC CareersOnline by **Wednesday, January 28, 2026**\. Please contact [worklearn\.iura@ubc\.ca](mailto:worklearn.iura@ubc.ca) with any questions\.

[Learn more](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-affairs/ubc-career-centre/work-learn/work-learn-international-undergraduate-research-awards "Work Learn International Undergraduate Research Awards | UBC Career Centre")
