Find information about mobile phones for business use in the Faculty of Medicine\. 

---

Please refer to the [Voice and Data Usage Policy](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/01/Voice-Data-Usage-Policy-2025.pdf) which allows eligible faculty and staff in the Dean’s Office to use a device of their choice, and consolidates all users to the Faculty of Medicine’s Phone and Data Plan which fully covers reasonable business usage expenses\. Personal expenses incurred by faculty or staff who are on the Dean’s Office plan still remain the responsibility of the employee to pay\. 

The implementation of this policy was initiated on the increasing demand from faculty and staff for different devices, and is designed to provide the flexibility and convenience of choice while maintaining the portable electronic device security needs of the Faculty and University\.  

The [Employee Voice &amp; Data Acknowledgement Form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/01/Voice-and-Data-Usage-Acknowledgement-2025.pdf) outlines the responsibilities that come with owning a Faculty of Medicine mobile device\. The form clarifies the expectations with using the mobile device and ensures employees are adhering to the care and security guidelines\.
