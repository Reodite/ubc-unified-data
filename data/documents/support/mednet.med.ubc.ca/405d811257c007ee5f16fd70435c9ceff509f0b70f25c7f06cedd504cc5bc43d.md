The Faculty of Medicine’s Staff Mentoring Program aims to connect staff, enhancing lifelong professional development through sharing of experiences and knowledge\. Apply to join the next cohort by October 9\.

Whether you are interested in serving as a mentor or being matched with a mentor, the program offers opportunities to build meaningful connections, gain new perspectives, develop skills and support personal and professional growth across the Faculty\. Mentors can make a meaningful impact in the lives of colleagues while also strengthening their own leadership and coaching abilities\.

The upcoming cohort will run from **January to December 2027**, and includes a kick\-off session and regular mentor\-mentee meetings throughout the year\. No prior mentoring experience is required for those applying as a mentor\.

Apply as a mentor or mentee by **Friday, October 9, 2026**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/staff-hr-resources/mentoring/ "Staff Mentoring Program - MedNet")

If you have any questions about the program, contact the Organizational Development team at [FoM\.OrgDev@ubc\.ca](mailto:FoM.OrgDev@ubc.ca)\.
