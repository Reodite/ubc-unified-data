[Celebrate Learning Week](https://celebratelearning.ubc.ca/) is a cross\-campus, week\-long forum for the UBC teaching and learning community to collaborate, network and engage in knowledge exchange presented by the Provosts and Vice\-President Academic from UBCO and UBCV\.

This year’s showcase will run from **May 4–8**, and invites the UBC community to pause, reflect, and celebrate learning in all its forms\. Centered on people and shared experiences, it honours learning that happens across seasons and spaces—inside classrooms, out in the field, and throughout everyday life\.

Faculty and staff are encouraged to submit session proposals that share how your unit celebrates learning, either through a formal session or by presenting a creative activity that celebrates learning in action\.

Sessions can include open lectures, workshops, panel discussions, asynchronous courses and more\. Explore the list of topic and format suggestion and submit your ideas by **March 31**\.

[Explore suggestions &amp; submit a proposal](https://celebratelearning.ubc.ca/program/submission-form/ "Call for Contributions | UBC Celebrate Learning Week")
