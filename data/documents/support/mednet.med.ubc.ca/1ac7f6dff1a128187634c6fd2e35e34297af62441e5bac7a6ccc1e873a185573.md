If you’re looking for a healthy and sustainable way to get around campus while saving time on your commute, consider e\-biking options\. 

## **UBC Vancouver** 

In partnership with Zygg, UBC staff, faculty and students at UBC Vancouver are invited to try an e\-bike for free for an entire week\.  

[Learn more](https://planning.ubc.ca/e-bike)

## **UBC Okanagan** 

An e\-bike and e\-scooter program is now available at UBC Okanagan as part of City of Kelowna’s shared micromobility program\.  

[Learn more](https://sustain.ok.ubc.ca/operations/sustainable-transportation/bike-share/)
