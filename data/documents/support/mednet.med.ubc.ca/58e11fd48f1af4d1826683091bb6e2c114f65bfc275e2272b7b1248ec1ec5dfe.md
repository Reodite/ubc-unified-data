UBC Vancouver faculty and staff are invited to submit a proposal to hire temporary student employees through the UBC Work Learn program\.

The Work Learn program funds approximately 3,500 part\-time hourly appointments each year, subsidizing meaningful work experiences for current UBC Vancouver students\.

Submit a proposal for winter 2026 employment opportunities \(September 1, 2026–April 30, 2027\) by **Monday, June 15**\.

[Learn more &amp; submit a proposal](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-affairs/ubc-career-centre/work-learn "Work Learn program | UBCV Career Centre")

Learn more about the [UBC Okanagan Work Study program](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/hire-a-student/work-study/) and key dates\.
