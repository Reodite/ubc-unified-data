October is Cybersecurity Awareness Month\. Learn how UBC is adapting to new cyber threats, investing in cutting\-edge technologies, and maintaining its commitment to data integrity at the 6th Annual Privacy Matters @ UBC Symposium\.

Register to join the symposium in\-person or online on **October 18, 2023**\. Session topics include:

-   Risks and opportunities associated with generative AI tools, and how to embrace these technologies responsibly\.
-   Improve your file\-sharing practices and protect your data\.
-   Explore the possibilities of cybersecurity as a career\.
-   Learn to recognize scams, strengthen your defenses, and contribute to a safer digital environment\.
-   Candid “Ask Me Anything” session with our cybersecurity experts, addressing everything from privacy to compliance\.

Lunch will be provided for in\-person attendees, with trivia and prize draws throughout the day in\-person and online\.

[See details &amp; register](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/symposium "Symposium 2023 | Privacy Matters @ UBC")
