[Dr\. Jack Taunton](https://familypractice.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-jack-taunton/ "In Memoriam: Dr. Jack Taunton | UBC Department of Family Practice") passed away on October 23, 2024\.

A pioneer in sports medicine, Dr\. Taunton was a Professor Emeritus in the Department of Family Practice, associate member of the Djavad Mowafaghian Centre for Brain Health \(DMCBH\) and a Faculty of Medicine alumnus\.

An accomplished physician, researcher and athlete, Dr\. Taunton co\-founded the UBC Sport and Exercise Medicine Centre \(formerly the Allan McGavin Sports Medicine Centre\), where he served as director for over 25 years\. In 2019, he co\-founded the B\.C\. Brain Wellness Program, which offers free, evidence\-informed exercise and lifestyle classes for individuals living with chronic brain conditions across the province\. Dr\. Taunton also served as a national sports team physician, including as Chief Medical Officer for the Vancouver 2010 Olympics and Paralympics\.

In recognition of his many contributions, Dr\. Taunton was awarded numerous accolades over the course of his life, including the Queen’s Diamond Jubilee Medal, the Lifetime Achievement Award from the Canadian Academy of Sport &amp; Exercise Medicine and induction into the [B\.C\. Sports Hall of Fame](https://www.med.ubc.ca/awards-honours/jack-taunton-named-to-b-c-sports-hall-of-fame/ "Jack Taunton named to B.C. Sports Hall of Fame - UBC Faculty of Medicine")\. Earlier this year, he was [appointed to the Order of Canada](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/ubc-medicine-faculty-members-appointed-to-the-order-of-canada-for-2024/ "UBC Medicine faculty members appointed to the Order of Canada for 2024 - UBC Faculty of Medicine"), one of the country’s highest honours\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Tuesday, November 5, 2024](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2024/11/06/jack-taunton/ "Jack Taunton Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
