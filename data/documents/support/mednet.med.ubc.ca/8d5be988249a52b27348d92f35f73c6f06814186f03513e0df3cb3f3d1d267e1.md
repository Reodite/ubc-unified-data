## Discovery to Commercialization

The Discovery to Commercialization \(D2C\) event series, which was organized in partnership between UBC Faculty of Medicine \(FoM\) and Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute \(VCHRI\), hosted conversations that connected people, ideas, and opportunities that advanced life sciences research and innovation\. Over the span of two years, the series successfully linked the academic and industry communities and enabled opportunities for faculty and industry partners to foster new relationships within the life sciences community\.

The event series is now concluded, but recordings from previous events are available below\.

## **D2C Speaker Series**

The D2C Speaker Series presented virtual sessions that shared key insights about the commercialization process and entrepreneurial ventures from experienced faculty and industry partners\. The sessions aimed to educate and inspire attendees about the local companies in our community\. 

The team behind establishing and executing the D2C speaker series was awarded the [2021 Faculty of Medicine Dean’s Staff Award for Creativity and Innovation](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/2021-faculty-of-medicine-award-recipients/)\. Read more about this instrumental team’s story in developing the series through [VCHRI’s announcement here](https://www.vchri.ca/announcement/2021/11/29/vchri-recognized-2021-deans-staff-award-creativity-and-innovation)\.

Full recordings of all our past D2C sessions are available below\. Recordings can also be found through [VCHRI’s YouTube channel here](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLaW0zpA0Jh_UT53nAjB2ic_zCx8072T-d)\.
