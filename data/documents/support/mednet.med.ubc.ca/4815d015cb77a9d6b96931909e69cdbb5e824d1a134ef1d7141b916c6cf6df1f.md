Learn more about the FoM Curriculum Team\.  

---

The role of the Faculty of Medicine \(FoM\) Curriculum Team is to support proponents through the curriculum change pathway within the FoM and through the submission process to UBC\-V Senate and external governing bodies\.  
  
In alignment with the FoM governance renewal in 2018, the FoM Curriculum Pathways initiative focuses on providing proponents with the tools and guidance to modifying existing curricula and creation of new courses, certificates, and programs\.
