[Dr\. David Hardwick](https://vancouversunandprovince.remembering.ca/obituary/david-hardwick-1082416955) passed away in May 2021\. An internationally recognized pediatric pathologist, Dr\. Hardwick was involved with the UBC Faculty of Medicine for more than 60 years as a student, professor, special advisor and mentor to many\.
