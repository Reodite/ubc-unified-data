The nomination deadline for the annual UBC Faculty Research Awards, including the Killam Accelerator Research Fellowship, is **Wednesday, November 15, 2023**\. These awards acknowledge research excellence across all disciplines at UBC\.

[See full awards list &amp; apply](https://prizes.research.ubc.ca/ubc-faculty-research-award-opportunities "UBC Faculty Research Award Opportunities")

For further information, consult the [awards FAQs](https://prizes.research.ubc.ca/internal-awards/internal-awards-summary-and-faqs "Internal Awards Summary and FAQs | UBC Research Prizes") or contact [research\.prizes@ubc\.ca](mailto:research.prizes@ubc.ca)\.
