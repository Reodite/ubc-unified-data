# **Updates &amp; Reminders** 

## **NEW\!  Federal ‘Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by Non\-Canadians Act’**

As announced recently \(attached\), there is a new federal ‘Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by Non\-Canadians Act’ that came into effect on January 1, 2023 and has a period of two years\. The effective date of this Act means that any non\-Canadians who have already been recruited and are currently working for UBC would not be eligible for home ownership until 2025 or until they meet one of the exemptions from the Act\. This may impact the non\-Canadian faculty members who are eligible and been approved for down payment assistance program and/or PIRL\.  

## **NEW\!  Private Health Coverage Requirements for Temporary Foreign Workers LMIA**

As announced recently, there are new amendments to Immigration and Refugee Protection Act for Temporary Foreign Workers \(TFWs\) with Labour Market Impact Assessment \(LMIA\) which include the requirement of a Private Health Coverage for emergency medical care effective the date of their arrival in Canada and during the period for which the TFW is not covered by their provincial health insurance system\. Please ensure your new hires are informed at the time their appointments are initiated\.

Important details around this new requirement is attached to the email newsletter version sent Feb 8, 2023, with email subject line *Private Health Coverage Requirements for Temporary Foreign Workers LMIA* originally emailed out on Jan 18, 2023\.

The new IRCC regulations do not apply for TMP with LMIA exemptions or returning Canadian citizens who need to satisfy MSP waiting periods\. However, it’s important that they obtain private health coverage during any MSP waiting periods\. Updated [FOM Offer Letter Templates](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/hiring-faculty/making-the-offer/) and [Checklist](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/01/Checklist-Full-Time-Faculty-New-Appointment-Jan-2023.doc) for Full\-Time Faculty have been posted on MedNet, please use current templates and checklist to prepare offer letters for new full\-time faculty hires\.

## **Selecting the Correct Earnings Type**

It is important that the correct earnings/allowance type is selected in Workday\. This is particularly true in the case of Postdoctoral Fellow appointments as they could potentially be in receipt of several different types of earnings/allowances\. Having the correct earnings type will ensure the correct benefit and tax deductions are taken\.  An incorrect earnings type could have a negative impact not only on the Record of Employment \(ROE\) should the employee require one for Employment Insurance \(EI\) Benefits through Service Canada, but also on UBC Supplemental Employment Benefits \(SEB\) for Mat/Parental/Adoption Leave\. 

## **Retroactive Earnings/Compensation Changes**

Retroactive changes to compensation plans may only occur within the tax year\. Changes to a prior year may not be made\. If a department chooses to process an appointment for a postdoc prior to the fellowship earnings being transferred to UBC and they temporarily pay from a UBC source the earnings should still be coded as ‘*Faculty &amp; Postdoc Fellowship’*\.  Once the fellowship earnings are received by UBC it is just a matter of updating the cost center retroactive to the appointment start date\.  The earnings type will not need to be updated\. This will help to ensure that appointments with start dates near the end of the year will be coded correctly on the individual’s tax slip\. Please refer to the [Faculty Relations Updates](https://hr.ubc.ca/managers-admins/managing-and-leading/faculty-relations/faculty-relations-updates/retroactive-earnings-comp-plan-changes) page for full details\.    

## **Extraordinary Expense Fund \(EEF\) &amp; Postdoctoral Fellow Appointments**

All Postdoctoral Fellows appointments \(Employees, UBC paid Award Recipients &amp; externally funded Award Recipients\) are covered under the EEF, which provides [Maternity, Parental &amp; Adoption Leave](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/benefit-plan-details/vacation-leaves/maternity-parental-adoptive-leave/maternity-4) and [Sick Leave](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/benefit-plan-details/vacation-leaves/paid-sick-leave) benefits\. All grant accounts are subject to contributions to the EEF\.  Details of the EEF, the contribution rates, eligibility requirements and what is covered is found on the [Human Resources website](https://hr.ubc.ca/managers-admins/managing-and-leading/employee-staffing-and-operations/extraordinary)\.  

## **Externally Funded Postdocs**

A reminder that postdoctoral fellows in receipt of external funds paid directly to them need to have an appointment in Workday with their external funds recorded in Canadian dollars as an *External Funding* Allowance\. Providing an appointment will ensure that these individuals are covered under UBC’s liability insurance and are covered under the Extraordinary Expense Fund \(EEF\)\.
