The [Run &amp; Ride for Rural Medicine](https://ruralrun.wordpress.com/) is an annual run, walk &amp; ride fundraiser organized by medical students from UBC’s Faculty of Medicine in Vancouver, Victoria, Kelowna, and Prince George\.

Now in its 20\[th\] year, the event is raising funds for Hope Air to support equitable access to medical care in Canada\.  

**When:** 

9 am on **April 27, 2024** in Victoria, Kelowna and Prince George   
9 am on **April 28, 2024** in Vancouver 

[Learn more and register](https://ca-p2p.engagingnetworks.app/28470/runforruralmedicine)
