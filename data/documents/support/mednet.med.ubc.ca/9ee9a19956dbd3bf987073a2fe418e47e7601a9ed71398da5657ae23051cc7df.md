UBC Vancouver research excellence clusters are invited to apply to the 2025/26 Grants for Catalyzing Research Clusters \(GCRC\) Competition\. 

The competition is open to new and returning clusters of research excellence in all disciplines on the Vancouver campus\. Cluster leads must submit a notice of intent by **November 1, 2024**\. Full applications are due January 8, 2025\.

Groups interested in applying are encouraged to register for one of two information sessions, held on **October 18 &amp; 22**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://research.ubc.ca/vpri-competitions-initiatives/research-excellence-clusters/grants-catalyzing-research-clusters "Grants for Catalyzing Research Clusters | UBC Research + Innovation")
