[June 7, Together\|Ensemble at UBC, Advancing the SDGs in British Columbia](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/05/Sustain_TogetherEnsemble-2023-940x529.png)

Join the UBC Sustainability Hub in partnership with the Vancouver Economic Commission \(VEC\) and the British Columbia Council for International Cooperation \(BCCIC\) for Together\|Ensemble at UBC\.

UBC faculty, students, staff, community members and organizations across B\.C\. are invited to take part in a day of activities focusing on decolonization, the dignity and rights of humans and more than human world, education, climate justice, and just transition\.

A special weaving workshop led by a local Musqueam artist will be optional to participants at an extra fee\.

This full\-day event is part of Canada’s national Together\|Ensemble conference devoted to tracking progress on the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals \(SDGs\) through academic\-led initiatives\.

-   **Date**: Thursday, June 7, 8 am–5 pm
-   **Location**: BC Hydro Theatre  
    Centre for Interactive Research on Sustainability  
    2260 West Mall, UBC Vancouver campus

[See full agenda &amp; register](https://sustain.ubc.ca/events/togetherensemble-ubc-%E2%80%93-advancing-sdgs-british-columbia "Together|Ensemble at UBC – Advancing the SDGs in British Columbia")
