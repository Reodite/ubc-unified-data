The [UBC Micro\-certificate in Health Data Analytics: Opportunities and Applications](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/programs-credentials/health-data-analytics-opportunities-applications-microcertificate) is a part\-time technical program developed by the Department of Medicine and Data Science Institute, in consultation with health sector leaders from government, academia and industry\. It provides learners with career development and upskilling opportunities to fill the data literacy gap in our evolving data\-driven health sector\.

Health Data Analytics is a StrongerBC Future Skills Grant eligible program, which is open to most B\.C\. residents and covers up to $3,500 for eligible programs\.

Join an information session on **July 29** to learn more about the program, meet the instructors and explore how data\-driven insights are transforming healthcare decision\-making and outcomes\.

-   **Date:** Wednesday, July 29 from 12—1 pm
-   **Location:** Online via Zoom
