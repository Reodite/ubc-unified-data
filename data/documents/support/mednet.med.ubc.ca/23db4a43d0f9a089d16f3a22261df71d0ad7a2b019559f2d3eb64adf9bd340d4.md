A message from Dermot Kelleher, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health\.

---

[2023 Faculty of Medicine Awards, Celebrating an extraordinary community of people](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/03/FoMAwards-2023_Banner_RevB_1200x400-940x313.png)

I am delighted to announce the recipients of the 2023 Faculty of Medicine Awards\.

These awards recognize and celebrate the extraordinary contributions of faculty and staff who embody our values of respect, integrity, compassion, collaboration and equity — helping us to transform health for everyone\.

The 2023 award categories include:

-   Bill and Marilyn Webber Lifetime Achievement Award
-   Awards for Excellence in Mentoring Early Career Faculty
-   Applegarth Staff Service Awards
-   Dean’s Staff Awards
-   Clinical Faculty Awards
-   CME\-CPD Awards
-   UBC Killam Teaching Prizes
-   UBC Killam Research Prizes
-   Distinguished Achievement Awards \(previously announced\)

[2023 Award Recipients](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/faculty-of-medicine-awards-2023/)

Please join me in congratulating all of our 2023 award recipients\. I look forward to formally celebrating the recipients and their accomplishments at our Annual Awards Reception\.
