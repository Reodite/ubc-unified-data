## Guidelines

Visit the UBC Brand website for a [social media toolkit](http://brand.ubc.ca/brand-toolkit/social-media/):

-   [Best Practices Guide](http://assets.brand.ubc.ca/downloads/ubc_social_media_best_practices.pdf) \(outlines best practices that will help you succeed at managing and creating exceptional content for your UBC social media channels\)  
-   [Moderation Guidelines and Response Framework](http://assets.brand.ubc.ca/downloads/ubc_social_moderation_and_response.pdf) \(Privacy, Negative Posts\) 
-   [Protocol: Discrimination Reporting via Social Media](https://med-fom-faculty.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/12/Discrimination-Reporting-via-Social-Media_Oct20201.docx)
-   [Personal or faculty use guidelines](https://www.dropbox.com/s/q6nep8bisftbb5k/Social_Media_PersonalFacultyUse_Guidelines_2016.doc?dl=0)

## Key considerations

Before getting started with social media, define your strategy\. This includes defining **objectives**, identifying your target **audience**, ensuring availability of ongoing **time and resources** to manage social media, planning for **content**, and choosing the right social media channel\.

Watch this [training video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?time_continue=2&amp;v=vW84QthNfGU) from UBC before getting started\.
