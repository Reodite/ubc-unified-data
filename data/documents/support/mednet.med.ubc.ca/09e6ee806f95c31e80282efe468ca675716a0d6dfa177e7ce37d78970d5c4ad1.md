The Centre for the Study of Democratic Institutions \(CSDI\) and Knowledge Exchange Unit, VP Research &amp; Innovation have announced the full workshop schedule for the new CSDI Policy Communications Clinic \(CPCC\)\.

The CSDI Policy Communications Clinic offers workshops and individualized consulting hours to help UBC faculty, staff, and students communicate their research effectively to stakeholders for policy change\.

[See all workshops &amp; register](https://democracy.ubc.ca/cpcc/workshops/ "Policy Communications Workshop Series | UBC Centre for the Study of Democratic Institutions")

Members of the UBC community can also [sign up for individualized consulting time](https://democracy.ubc.ca/cpcc/cpcc-consulting-hours/ "CPCC Consulting | UBC Centre for the Study of Democratic Institutions") with senior members of CSDI including staff and faculty consultants\.
