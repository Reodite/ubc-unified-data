It is with great sadness that we share the news of the recent passing of a valued member of the MD Undergraduate Program community, Dr\. Małgorzata \(Mal\) Kamińska, Assistant Dean, Student Affairs, Northern Medical Program \(NMP\), and Assistant Professor in the Division of Medical Sciences, University of Northern British Columbia \(UNBC\)\. We are saddened by the unexpected loss and grieve alongside her family and community\.

Known as Mal to her friends and the University community, Dr\. Kamińska joined the UBC’s NMP in 2016 and quickly became known for her enthusiasm for medical education and her passion for supporting students\.

That passion led her to organize UNBC’s first Mini\-Med School in 2017, giving members of the public a chance to learn about the medical profession, from how bones heal to the science behind diabetes\. During the COVID\-19 pandemic, she found a new way to engage young learners, helping launch A Science Escape at UNBC, where high school students received take\-home experiment materials and connected virtually with team members to solve science puzzles\.

That same drive to support students shaped her career path\. In 2023, she was appointed as the NMP’s Assistant Dean, Student Affairs\. It’s a role she sought out specifically so she could provide individual guidance to students throughout their medical school journey\. As Assistant Dean of Student Affairs, she always prioritized the diverse needs of students\.

Dr\. Kamińska earned her medical degree at the University of Toronto in 2005 and completed a family medicine residency through the University of Calgary’s Rural Alberta South Program in Lethbridge\. Throughout her tenure at UBC/ UNBC, the University of Saskatchewan, and the University of Calgary, she taught nearly a thousand future doctors and resident doctors\. She consistently innovated her teaching styles, leading to exciting new practices\. Her teaching included commitments within Portfolio, FLEX, Clinical Skills, as well as the Department of Family Medicine\. She will be remembered fondly by the NMP community, whom she cared for greatly\.

She is survived by her husband, Richard, her mother, Elzbieta, and brother, Piotr, and her service dog in training, Leo\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Kamińska from the MD Undergraduate Program’s Northern Medical Program](https://www.unbc.ca/northern-medical-program/news/memory-dr-malgorzata-kaminska)\.

The B\.C\., Musqueam and UBC [flags will be lowered on Thursday, September 24, 2026](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/flag-lowering/ "Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
