An invitation from the Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion\.

---

[Virtual symposium by the Office of REDI, Race Ideology: Historical Perspectives, Current Realities and Re\-imagining the Future, May 25, 2022 8:30 to 1 pm](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/REDI-symposium-registration-copy.png)

Registration is now open for the Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion \(REDI\) inaugural virtual symposium: “[**Race Ideology: Historical Perspectives, Current Realities and Re\-imagining the Future**](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/race-ideology-historical-perspectives-current-realities-and-re-imagining-the-future/ "Race Ideology: Historical Perspectives, Current Realities and Re-imagining the Future | Respectful Environments, Diversity &amp; Inclusion")\.”

[Register Now](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5wkcuCspjgvEteAqNNaPROZ42NeZNsPwuuq "Event registration | Zoom")

*Please note the event will be recorded\.*

---

**When:** Wednesday, May 25, 2022

**Time:** 8:30 am–1 pm

**Why should you come?**

-   Be inspired to have courage in talking about racism within health care\. 
-   Honour the power of the experiential knowledge of Indigenous, Black and People of Color\.
-   Engage with different anti\-racist pedagogies and strategies\. 
-   Participate in a wider dialogue across the Faculty of Medicine and beyond\.

Find further [information on the symposium](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/race-ideology-historical-perspectives-current-realities-and-re-imagining-the-future/ "Race Ideology: Historical Perspectives, Current Realities and Re-imagining the Future | Respectful Environments, Diversity &amp; Inclusion")\.
