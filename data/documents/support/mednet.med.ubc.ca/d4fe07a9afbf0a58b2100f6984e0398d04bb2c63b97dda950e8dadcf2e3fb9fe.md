Applications for the Indigenous Health Administration and Leadership Program \(IHALP\) are now open\.

Offered by the Centre for Excellence in Indigenous Health through the UBC Certificate Program, this one\-year part\-time program is rooted in the core values of resiliency, culture and Indigenous perspectives\. The program is designed to build and enhance your knowledge of health administration so you can confidently design, lead and deliver holistic health and wellness programming in Indigenous organizations and communities\.

Apply for the 2023 program by **April 5**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://health.aboriginal.ubc.ca/programming/ihalp/ "Indigenous Health Administration and Leadership Program (IHALP) | Centre for Excellence in Indigenous Health")

Join the [IHALP certificate program information session](https://learningcircle.ubc.ca/2023/01/09/ihalp-2023/ "February 16, 2023 — Indigenous Health Administration and Leadership Program (IHALP) 2023 Information Session | UBC Learning Circle") on **February 16**\. Ask questions, hear about alumni experiences, and chat directly with course instructors to see if this program is right for you\.
