Develop your leadership skills through the Faculty of Medicine Faculty Emerging Leaders Program \(FELP\) — apply to join the 2026 cohort by November 3\.

FELP provides faculty with the opportunity to develop key leadership skills and prepare for future leadership roles within the academic environment\. The program will feature six modules held over six months, from January to June, 2026\. Four modules will be delivered virtually, with the first and final modules delivered in person in Vancouver\.

Submit your application by **Monday, November 3, 2025**\. If you have any questions, contact the Organizational Development team at [FoM\.OrgDev@ubc\.ca](mailto:FoM.OrgDev@ubc.ca)\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/faculty-hr-resources/career-professional-development/leadership-development/ "Leadership Development: Faculty Emerging Leaders Program - MedNet")
