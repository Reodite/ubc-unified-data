Find information and resources for preparing recommendation letters for appointments, reappointments, and promotions\. 

---

Recommendation letters from the Head/Director to the Dean summarize the outcome of the review process, highlighting any relevant considerations, and provide a clear recommendation regarding appointment, reappointment \(if applicable\), or promotion\. 

## Guidelines

-   Confirm effective date of appointment, reappointment \(if applicable\), or promotion \(e\.g\., July 1, 2026\)\.
-   Clearly identify the date of the CARP meeting at which a vote was taken\. 
-   Include number of votes for, against, and abstentions \(with an explanation of the negative votes and abstentions\)\. Unexplained abstentions are usually to be treated as tantamount to a negative vote\. 
-   Clearly indicate Head/Director’s recommendation and the basis for the recommendation related to the criteria\. 
-   Provide information on special conditions of the candidate’s appointment, including, but not limited to, reduced teaching or administrative responsibilities, unusually extensive teaching or administrative responsibilities, medical, maternity or parental leaves or responsibilities in more than one unit\. 
-   Provide information on Department/School norms at the rank being considered with regard to teaching expectations, and other expectations of the appointment\.
-   Provide a summary of the evidence supplied by the candidate from their dossier\.
-   Provide a summary of the referee letters, where applicable\.  
-   Address all relevant concerns raised by referees, if applicable\. 
-   If serious concerns, confirm that the candidate was given the opportunity to respond prior to the vote and include the candidate’s written response to concerns raised by the CARP prior to the vote\. 

**Please Note:** The letter should not exceed two pages\. [See Head/Director Letter of Recommendation to the Dean template](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/?page_id=33292#templates)\.
