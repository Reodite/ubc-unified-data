Thrive month is just around the corner, and there are a few [signature events](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/news/october-10-2023/discover-thrive-2023-signature-events) to highlight as we prepare for our month\-long mental health campaign, including UBC’s largest dance class on November 3 and the first\-ever Thrive research roundtable on November 21\.

But Thrive isn’t just about big events\. Throughout the month, you can also join events developed by the UBC community for the UBC community\. Check out the full Thrive calendar at [thrive\.ubc\.ca/events](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/wellbeing-campaigns-and-initiatives/thrive/calendar/2023-11)\.
