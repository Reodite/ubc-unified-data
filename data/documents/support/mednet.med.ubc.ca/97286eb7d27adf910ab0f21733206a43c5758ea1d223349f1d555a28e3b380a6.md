## UBC Health and Safety Policy

The University strives to create and maintain a safe and healthy environment for all members of the UBC community\. For further information, review the UBC [Health and Safety Policy \(Policy SC1\)](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/health-and-safety-policy/)\.

## Working Alone Program and Tool

Faculty specific programs include the [Working Alone Program](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/12/FoM-Working-Alone-Program-2024-1.pdf), which is mandatory for workers in situations where assistance is not readily available in case of emergency or if the worker is injured or in ill health\.

To help implement a working alone check\-in plan, the Faculty of Medicine Health and Safety Team have developed a [Working Alone tool](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3BQ3xy0hZuKQlMO) for employees planning to work alone\. The [tool](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3BQ3xy0hZuKQlMO) does the following:

-   Acts as a Working Alone Agreement between worker and supervisor
-   Establishes a worker’s eligibility for working alone based on the tasks being done
-   Provides time intervals required for checking in based on a risk assessment

The Faculty of Medicine Health &amp; Safety Team works in conjunction with the Department of Safety and Risk Services \(SRS\)\. SRS provides guidance and technical expertise to ensure the health and safety of people and the environment at UBC\. For further information on university wide programs and resources, please visit the [Safety and Risk Services website](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/) or contact the [Faculty of Medicine Health and Safety team](mailto:fom.safety@ubc.ca)\.

Occupational First Aid is available for all faculty, staff and student\-staff 24 hours a day on the Vancouver Campus\. You can contact the mobile first aid team by calling 604\-822\-4444 or 2\-4444 \(UBC landline\)\. For further information, visit the Safety and Risk Services [website](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/safety-programs/first%20aid/workplace-first-aid-for-employees/)\.

Occupational First Aid is available by contacting Campus Security\. For further information, visit the [Health, Safety and Environment website](https://hse.ok.ubc.ca/safety/firstaid/)\. 

Occupational First aid is available by contacting UNBC Security Services\. For further information, visit the [UNBC Security Services website](https://www.unbc.ca/security)\.

Occupational First Aid is available by contacting UVic Campus Security\. For further information, visit the [UVic Campus Security website](https://www.uvic.ca/security/home/medical/index.php)\.

Refer to your site\-specific first aid response process or check our our [Building Safety Contact Sheets](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/health-safety/building-safety-contact-sheets/)\. If you have any questions, contact your [Local Safety Team](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/health-safety/committees-teams/) or [FoM Health &amp; Safety](mailto:fom.safety@ubc.ca)\.

## Emergency Preparedness and Response Resources

The Faculty of Medicine strives to provide a safe and healthy working environment for all faculty, staff and students\. For information on emergency preparedness and response procedures in your area, click on the site link below\.

-   [UBC Vancouver Campus Emergency Procedures](https://ready.ubc.ca/)
-   [UBC Okanagan Campus Emergency Procedures](https://emergency.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   [University of Northern British Columbia Emergency Procedures](https://www.unbc.ca/emergency-operations)
-   [University of Victoria Emergency Procedures](https://www.uvic.ca/services/emergency/be-prepared/index.php)

For off\-campus/hospital sites, see your site\-specific response procedures\. All hospitals in British Columbia use a [centralized code emergency system](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/11/Hospital-Site-Codes.pdf)\.
