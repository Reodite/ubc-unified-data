A message from Dermot Kelleher, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health\.

---

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/11/20220914_ValterCiocca.png)

I am pleased to announce the appointment of **Dr\. Valter Ciocca** and reappointment of **Dr\. Paola Colozzo** as interim Co\-Directors, School of Audiology and Speech Sciences, effective to December 31, 2022\. Dr\. Ciocca’s appointment follows the interim leadership of Dr\. Lorienne Jenstad as interim Co\-Director of the School\.

Dr\. Ciocca is a professor at the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences, and previously served as director of the School from 2007 to 2017\. His research expertise includes the perceptual and acoustic analyses of speech produced by speakers with and without communication disorders, the perception and production of lexical tones, and auditory scene analysis\. Dr\. Ciocca is also the Chair of the Board of the Council for Accreditation of Canadian University Programs in Audiology and Speech\-Language Pathology\.

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/07/PaolaColozzo_600x600.png)

Dr\. Colozzo is an associate professor in the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences\. Her areas of expertise and specialization are language development, childhood language disorders, and interactions between language and cognition\. Dr\. Colozzo has also been the MSc Program Director at the School for the last five years\.

Please join me in thanking Drs\. Ciocca and Colozzo for their interim leadership, and Dr\. Jenstad for the leadership and support she has provided\.
