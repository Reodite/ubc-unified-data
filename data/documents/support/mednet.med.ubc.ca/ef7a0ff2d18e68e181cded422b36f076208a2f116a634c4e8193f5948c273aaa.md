This May, the Faculty of Medicine will be upgrading its learning management system, Entrada, to the cloud version\.

Entrada is used by faculty, staff and learners in the MD Undergraduate and Postgraduate Medical Education programs, as well as the Occupational Science &amp; Occupational Therapy and Audiology &amp; Speech Sciences programs\. If you currently use Entrada to teach, or support program scheduling and assessments, learn more about the upcoming upgrades\.

## Elentra overview

The transition to the cloud\-based version — known as Elentra — is mostly a behind‑the‑scenes upgrade, and minimal changes are expected to how you use the platform\.

With Elentra Cloud, you will also be able to access the platform via a mobile app, allowing you to complete Entrustable Professional Activities \(EPAs\) on the go, check schedules from your phone and stay connected in clinic or on the move\.

### Key dates

The cloud\-based version, Elentra, will go live on Monday, May 11\.

While the migration takes places, the system will not be available for use between Friday, May 8 at 4 pm and Monday, May 11 at 9 am\.

-   **Migration**:
    -   **Friday, May 8, 2026 at 4 pm**: Entrada shuts down
    -   **May 8–11**: Blackout period, no access to Entrada
-   **Go live**:
    -   **Monday, May 11, 2026 at 9 am**: Elentra Cloud goes live

### How to access Elentra

To access Elentra you’ll visit a new URL, then sign in as usual using your CWL\.

The new URL will be communicated prior to launch day\. Existing links to Entrada will also redirect to the new site\.

## More information

You should see minimal changes to how you use the platform for teaching and how learners use the system\. Your content, schedules, assessments and tools will migrate to the cloud version ready for you to use on May 11\.

To ensure the change is as smooth as possible, an impact assessment has been completed, and programs across the Faculty have helped with testing the cloud system and confirming that your regular work will stay on track\.

Learn more, explore frequently asked questions and see the latest timelines on the project page on MedNet\.

[Learn more about the Elentra Cloud migration](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/teaching/education-administrative-systems/entrada-migration-to-elentra-cloud/)
