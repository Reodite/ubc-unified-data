The [Run &amp; Ride for Rural Medicine](https://ruralrun.wordpress.com/ "UBC Run &amp; Ride for Rural Medicine") is an annual run, walk &amp; ride fundraiser organized by a team of medical students from UBC’s Faculty of Medicine in Vancouver, Victoria, Kelowna, and Prince George\.

Now in its 19th year, the event is raising funds for Hope Air to support equitable access to medical care in Canada\.

**When**: 9 am on March 11, 2023

**Where**: Jericho Beach, Vancouver B\.C\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://support.hopeair.ca/runforural2023 "Run for Rural Medicine 2023")
