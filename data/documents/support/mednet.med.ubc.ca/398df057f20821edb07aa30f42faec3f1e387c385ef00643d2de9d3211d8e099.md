Build meaningful relationships and support professional growth through the Faculty of Medicine Clinical Faculty Mentoring Program\. Apply as a mentor or mentee by July 17\.

The program connects clinical faculty across the Faculty of Medicine through flexible, one\-on\-one mentoring relationships that encourage the sharing of experiences, knowledge and perspectives\. The 2026–27 cohort will run from October 2026 to April 2027, with optional professional development training sessions available to all participants\.

Apply as a mentor or mentee by **Friday, July 17, 2026**\. If you have any questions, contact the Organizational Development team at [FoM\.OrgDev@ubc\.ca](mailto:FoM.OrgDev@ubc.ca)\.
