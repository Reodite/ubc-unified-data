## Peer Review Teaching Tools

Two Peer Reviews of Teaching are required for all promotion and tenure cases\. 

On May 16, 2007, UBC Senate approved the Policy on Student Evaluation of teaching which requires Department and Schools to conduct peer evaluations of teaching\.  The following peer review tools have been adapted from Departments and Schools across the Faculty of Medicine:

-   [Grand Rounds Evaluation Form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/Grand-Rounds-Evaluation-Form.pdf)
-   [Lecture Lab\-Observation Form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/Promotion-Tenure-Lecture-Lab-Observation-Form.pdf)
-   [Tutor Observation Form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/Promotion-Tenure-Tutor-Observation-Form.pdf)
-   [Draft – Observation Form for Peer Review of Small Group Teaching](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/Promotion-Tenure-Small-Group-Peer-Review-Draft-Form.pdf)
-   [ASSESSMENT of TEACHER by LEARNER – Large group session \(Lecture AHD Lab\)](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/ASSESSMENT-of-TEACHER-by-LEARNER-Large-group-session-Lecture-AHD-Lab.pdf) \(Ver May 2017\)
-   [ASSESSMENT of TEACHER by LEARNER – Clinical](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/ASSESSMENT-of-TEACHER-by-LEARNER-Clinical.pdf) \(Ver May 2017\)
