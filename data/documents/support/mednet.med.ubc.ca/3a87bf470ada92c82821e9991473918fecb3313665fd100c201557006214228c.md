Help embed wellbeing in your team by becoming a Workplace Wellbeing Ambassador — submit an application by **August 15**\.

The Workplace Wellbeing Ambassador Program is designed to promote and enhance a culture of health and wellbeing within UBC\. Open to faculty and staff at UBC Vancouver and Okanagan, ambassadors play a vital role in championing health and wellbeing for their departments and among their peers, with support and resources from the Workplace Wellbeing team and opportunities for professional development\.

-   Length of term: Ambassadors are asked to commit to this role for a minimum of one year, with the option to recommit annually
-   Time commitment: 1–2 hours per month

Apply for the 2025\-2026 year by **12 pm on Friday, August 15**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/workplace-wellbeing-programs/workplace-wellbeing-ambassador-program "Workplace Wellbeing Ambassador program | UBC Human Resources")
