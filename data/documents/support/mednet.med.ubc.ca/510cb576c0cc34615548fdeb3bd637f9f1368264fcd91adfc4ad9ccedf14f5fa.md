##   
Required Form Details 

In order to process a curriculum change request, some or all of the following forms are required\. If you have any questions on any of the forms, or the required forms for any type of curriculum change, reach out to the [FoM Curriculum Team](mailto:fom.curriculum@ubc.ca)\. Additional information on required forms can also be found in the UBC\-V Senate [Guide to Curriculum Submissions](https://scs-senate-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/Guide-to-Curriculum-Submissions-for-UBC-Vancouver.pdf)\.

##   
New Program Development &amp; CIP 51 Health Review

The [UBC Office of the Provost and Vice\-President, Academic](https://academic.ubc.ca/teaching-learning/new-program-proposals/new-program-proposal-resources) houses helpful resources, and required forms for new program development, including new degree programs, diplomas, certificates, and non\-credit credentials\.  

All new programs, including new degree programs, new certificates, and new non\-credit program certificates must complete the CIP 51 Health Review by the Ministry of Post\-Secondary Education and Future Skills \(PSFS\)\. Proponents must complete the [CIP 51 Health Review Consultation Template](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/05/Health-Review-Policy-Template-.docx), and submit the completed document to [fom\.curriculum@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.curriculum@ubc.ca)\.  
  
Further details on the CIP 51 Health Review can be found via the [Ministry of PSFS CIP 51 Review Policy\.](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/post-secondary-education/institution-resources-administration/degree-authorization/health_program_review_policy.pdf)

##   
FoM\-Specific Forms  
  

[**FoM Curriculum Change Initial Request Form**](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/Curriculum-Change-Initial-Request-Form.docx) 

This form is designed to guide you through the inception stage and help to develop your new program or new certificate concepts onto paper\. This document will evolve into the proposal summary during submission\. If you have already developed a proposal summary, there is no requirement to fill out this form\. Instead, please address all questions from this form into the proposal summary before submission to the [FoM Curriculum Team](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/teaching/curriculum-pathways/about-us/)\. 

  
**FoM Finance Curriculum Change Consultation Form** 

This form is a required internal form for the development of the financial model for your new program, certificate, and/or new course\. This form varies depending on the proposal type\. All proposal types require review and endorsement from FoM Finance\.
