Do you know how to ensure your UBC office or workstation is safe?

Find out by completing a short survey and be entered into a draw to win one of two Emergency Preparedness Kits\.

---

**All Faculty of Medicine faculty and staff who work onsite in office/administrative spaces** are asked to answer 10 short questions about their UBC office or workstation by **Friday, January 31, 2025**\.

This annual survey is part of the [Faculty of Medicine Health &amp; Safety](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/health-safety/) team’s Worksite Safety Inspection Initiative\. In 2024, input from almost 600 completed surveys from 60 different buildings allowed the team to make our worksites safer for everyone\. This year, the team aims to reach even more\.

You do not need to complete the survey for laboratory locations or home offices\. If you work at multiple locations, you only need to complete the survey once\.

[Complete the survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_296AUbgiQHMGO5E "UBC Faculty of Medicine Annual Inspection Initiative 2025: Offices &amp; Workstations")

## Did you know?

All UBC workers must complete and document a check\-in procedure with their supervisors before working alone or in isolation\.

The Faculty of Medicine Health &amp; Safety Team has created an easy\-to\-use tool to help create and complete such a document\. Find the **Working Alone Program** document on the Health &amp; Safety [Policies &amp; Programs](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/health-safety/policies-programs/) page, along with resources on First Aid, emergency preparedness and more\.

The survey consists of 10 quick questions and only takes a few minutes to complete\.

Questions include checking that aisles and doorways by your workspace are free of obstructions, that power outlets are not overloaded, and confirming that you are aware of the evacuation muster station for your office\.

All workers \(including faculty, staff and paid students\) who have a worksite on UBC Vancouver campus or any UBC site, institute or centre are asked to complete the survey\.

You do not need to complete the survey for your home office and you only need to complete the survey once\. If you work at multiple locations on UBC premises, or work at a shared or communal workstation, choose one office/workstation to complete\.

Inspecting worksites regularly is a [WorkSafeBC requirement](https://www.worksafebc.com/en/law-policy/occupational-health-safety/searchable-ohs-regulation/ohs-regulation/part-03-rights-and-responsibilities#SectionNumber:3.3 "Occupational Health and Safety Programs | WorksafeBC")\. You are encouraged to complete the survey every year\.

Inspecting worksites regularly is a [WorkSafeBC requirement](https://www.worksafebc.com/en/law-policy/occupational-health-safety/searchable-ohs-regulation/ohs-regulation/part-03-rights-and-responsibilities#SectionNumber:3.3 "Occupational Health and Safety Programs | WorksafeBC")\. The Local Safety Team in your area will follow up with respective supervisors to ensure all locations have been inspected\.

The survey link will be open until **Friday, January 31, 2025**\.

For some issues, resolution is simple and instructions are provided within the survey\. For other issues, the Local Safety Team in your area will connect with you to ensure the hazard is resolved\.

All questions can be directed to the Health &amp; Safety team at [fom\.safety@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.safety@ubc.ca)\.
