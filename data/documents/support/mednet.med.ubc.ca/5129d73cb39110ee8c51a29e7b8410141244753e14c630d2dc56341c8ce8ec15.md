## Faculty of Medicine Awards

  - **Header cell 1 (colspan 2):**
  
    Applegarth Staff Service Awards

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    Description
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The Faculty of Medicine Applegarth Staff Service Awards are annual awards recognizing outstanding contributions by UBC staff members to the Faculty of Medicine\. The awards consist of a plaque and $1000 each to three Faculty of Medicine employees – one to a secretarial/clerical support staff recipient, one to a management/professional recipient and one to a non\-union technician or research assistant recipient\.

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    Description
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The Faculty of Medicine Dean’s Staff Awards honour exceptional staff contributions in specific areas that are important to the Faculty of Medicine\. All nominees should have demonstrated a strong commitment to living the Faculty of Medicine values of respect, integrity, compassion, collaboration, and equity\. The awards consist of a plaque and $1000 for each individual recipient/team\. Three categories are available:  
      
    \-Leadership \(for individuals\)  
    \-Respectful Environment, Equity, Diversity, Inclusion \(for individuals or teams\)  
    \-Creativity/Innovation \(for individuals or teams\)
