Join UBC’s [Queer Faculty and Staff Collective \(QFSC\)](https://equity.ubc.ca/queer-faculty-and-staff-collective/) at the 2024 Vancouver Pride Parade on **Sunday, August 4**\.

QFSC has successfully received a spot in the 2024 parade, and invites all UBC 2SLGBTQIA\+ members to get involved\. Join the organizing committee or sign up to walk in the parade\. Every participant will receive a \#UBCPRIDE t\-shirt\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_9LHNc2OkeWstbFQ "QFSC 2024 Vancouver Pride Parade registration form | UBC Qualtrics")
