Dr\. Tali Alborz Conine passed away on November 28, 2025\.

Dr\. Conine was a Professor Emerita and former Director of the School of Rehabilitation Sciences, the precursor to the current Department of Physical Therapy and Department of Occupational Science &amp; Occupational Therapy\.

Globally recognized as a leader in rehabilitative sciences, Dr\. Conine was a Fellow of the American Society of Allied Health Professionals, and received awards including the Distinguished Service Medal from the Government of Japan\.
