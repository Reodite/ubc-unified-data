How to apply to be an Advisor in FLEX:

---

Groups of eight FLEX students are assigned to an Advisor by the course\.  Advisors guide students as they learn to develop learning goals and objectives, select appropriate FLEX activities, and develop skills important for self\-assessment and life\-long learning\.  Advisors work with students individually and in a small group setting to foster self\-regulated and self\-directed learning\. Advisors also provide formative feedback to their students as well as in the domains of Scholarship, Communication, Collaboration and Professionalism\.

If you are interested in becoming a FLEX Advisor in the Vancouver Fraser Medical Program \(VFMP\), please review the following **position descriptions** and apply via the **application portal link** below:  

[MEDD 419 \(Yr 1 FLEX\) Advisor Positions for 2026\-27](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/MEDD-419-Yr-1-FLEX-Advisor-Positions-2026-2027.pdf)
