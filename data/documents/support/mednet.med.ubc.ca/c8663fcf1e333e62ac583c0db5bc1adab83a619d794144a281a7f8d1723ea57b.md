Situated within BC’s thriving biomanufacturing and life sciences ecosystem, the **BC MS Cell Therapies Translational Research Network** \(MS Research Network\) collaborates with research partners across the province, country and globe to leverage the latest advances in cell and immuno\-engineering to develop innovative therapies for patients with MS\.
