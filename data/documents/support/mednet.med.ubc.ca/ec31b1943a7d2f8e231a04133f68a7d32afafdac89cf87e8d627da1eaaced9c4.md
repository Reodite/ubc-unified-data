As a manager, these are the steps you will need to follow to advertise job opportunities\.

---

## UBC Careers Website

### Postings Date

-   Posting dates will be automatically adjusted if the job opening approval is received after the original posting date\. 

### Postings

-   Postings appear on the UBC Careers website at midnight on the opening date and are removed at 11:59pm the day before the posting end date\. For example, if the posting end date is June 1st, the posting will be visible on the Career site until 11:59pm on May 30th\. The posting will not be visible as of June 1st\.
-   Positions must be posted for a minimum of 5 business days to a maximum of 30 calendar days\.
-   Postings that did not identify a successful candidate may be extended\. Please contact your recruiter\. 

### CUPE 2950

-   Positions of 3 months of longer must be posted\. \(3 months \= January 1\-March 31st not January 1\-April 1st which is 3 months plus a day\)\.
-   Initial postings are generally recommended to be for the minimum 5 day period as there are often recall/placement candidates and internal candidates to consider first\. Therefore posting for extended periods or on external websites is generally not recommended for initial postings\.  
-   If the initial posting did not identify a successful candidate, you may elect to advertise externally\. The posting must be re\-posted/extended on the UBC website\. Direct applicants from the external advertising to apply via the UBC website\. the same prioritization of applicants for the re\-post is required, as there will likely be additional internal candidates to consider from the second posting\. 

### M&amp;P

-   Positions of 12 months or longer must be posted \(12 months \= January 1st\-December 31st not January 1st\-January 1st which is a year plus a day\)\.
-   When a position has a highly specialized or unique skills set, you may decide to advertise an M&amp;P position externally\. This is permitted provided the posting appears first on the UBC careers website\. Direct external candidates to apply via the UBC Careers website\.

### TRA’s

-   Positions of 12 months or longer must be posted \(12 months \= January 1st\-December 31st not January 1st\-January 1st which is a year plus a day\)\.
-   When a position has a highly specialized or unique skills set, you may decide to advertise a position externally\. This is permitted provided the posting appears first on the UBC careers website\. Direct external candidates to apply via the UBC Careers website\.
