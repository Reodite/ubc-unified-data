The [UBC University Killam Professors](https://prizes.research.ubc.ca/awards/university-killam-professor) designation recognizes exceptional members of faculty who are extraordinary teachers and researchers, who are leaders in their academic fields and who have received national and international recognition\.

The Faculty of Medicine is launching an internal selection process to nominate up to two candidates to be considered for the University Killam Professors competition\.  Submit nomination packages for internal review by **July 6, 2026**\.

[Learn more &amp; nominate](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/research/internal-funding-opportunities/university-killam-professors/ "University Killam Professors - MedNet")
