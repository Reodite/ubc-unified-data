Join UBC Connects at Robson Square for “Rewiring the Mind: How Science, Lifestyle and Wellness Shape Brain Health” on **Monday, November 3**\.

Hosted in partnership with the Djavad Mowafaghian Centre for Brain Health, hear from Dr\. Lara Boyd, Dr\. Teresa Liu\-Ambrose and Dr\. Silke Appel\-Cresswell as they share insights on how learning reshapes the brain, the role of exercise in aging and how diet and lifestyle are essential to lifelong brain wellness\. An audience Q&amp;A session and reception will follow\.

-   **When:** Monday, November 3 from 6–9 pm
-   **Where:** UBC Robson Square

[Learn more &amp; register](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/events/rewiring-the-mind/ "Rewiring the Mind: How Science, Lifestyle and Wellness Shape Brain Health | UBC Community Engagement")
