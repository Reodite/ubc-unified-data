Register to join the Meeting for Advanced Synthetic Biology and Systems Bioengineering \(MASSIV\) 1\.0 in Vancouver, **January 19–22, 2026**\. 

Research faculty, staff and trainees are welcome to the four\-day conference hosted by the School of Biomedical Engineering, which will bring together leading researchers, innovators and trainees from around the world to discuss the future of synthetic biology and biomedical engineering\.

-   **Date**: January 19–22, 2026
-   **Location**: [AMS Student Nest](https://maps.ubc.ca/?code=NEST), UBC Vancouver

[Learn more &amp; register](https://www.massivconference.com/)
