There are a few sacred spaces available for quiet reflection, prayer and relaxation on campus\.

Several spaces, for student use only, around the Vancouver UBC Campus can be found [here\.](https://students.ubc.ca/campus-life/religion-spirituality/ubc-multifaith-prayer-rooms)

Vancouver Coastal Health also has several spaces at their various locations, [here\.](https://www.vch.ca/en/service/sacred-spaces-and-gathering-places#wysiwyg--70161)

One of the newest, albeit it smaller space, for a couple of persons at a time is the LSC Sacred Space\.
