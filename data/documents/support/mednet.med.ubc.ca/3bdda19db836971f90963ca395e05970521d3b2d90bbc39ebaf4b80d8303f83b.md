As we step into 2026, the 75\[th\] anniversary signifier will be officially discontinued\. 

If you or your unit added the 75\[th\] anniversary signifier to your banners, memorabilia, letterheads or email signature, please update these to reflect the standard Faculty of Medicine, department or school logo\. 

[Learn more and download UBC logos](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/communications/logos/) from the Logos page on MedNet\.

For further information or questions, please contact the Office of Creative &amp; Communications at [digitalcomms\.med@ubc\.ca](mailto:digitalcomms.med@ubc.ca)\.

## How to update your email signature

To add the UBC Faculty of Medicine logo to your email signature in the Outlook app:

1.  Download the Faculty of Medicine logo email signature file\.
2.  In your Outlook app or browser, open a **New Email**\.
3.  Select **Signature**, and click **Edit Signatures…** to open the Signatures editor\.
4.  Select an existing signature and click **Edit**, or create a new one by clicking **\+**
5.  In the edit signature window, click **Pictures** and browse for the file: UBCMedicine\_EmailSignature\. Select the file and click **Insert**\.
6.  Ensure the image is below your signature text\. Do not alter the size of the image\.
7.  Click **OK** to save your new signature\.

[Download the email signature file](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fo/qggk06c2gcoyq4jcymt5y/AFsevJfZiMvELWJIxtT9I9Y?rlkey=8g1h2t6dmj4tjmm277etx7hj2&amp;st=jcigyb2s&amp;dl=0)
