## **About the Project**

Since 2018, the Faculty of Medicine has been occupying office space at the City Square Shopping Centre that is leased by Vancouver Coastal Health \(VCH\)\. Later this year, the lease for this space is coming to an end\. As a result, the four Faculty of Medicine units currently working out of the City Square location will be transitioning to new work locations \. The units directly affected by this change include Postgraduate Medical Education \(PGME\), Continuing Professional Development \(CPD\), Faculty Development, and Finance \(Academic and Education &amp; Administration\)\.

---

## **What’s Changing**

The lease on the City Square office is coming to an end in late October, 2026\. As a result, units currently located in the City Square office will transition to new work locations in late September\-early October, 2026\. Planning for the relocation will continue over the summer to prepare and support teams through the transition\.

Postgraduate Medical Education \(PGME\), Continuing Professional Development \(CPD\), and Faculty Development teams will be relocating from the City Square office space to 520 West 6th Avenue \(5th Floor\)\. The 520 West 6th space is an existing VCH office that Faculty of Medicine units will be sharing with other VCH units\. The office has a mix of individual workstations and bookable meeting spaces \(with videoconferencing capabilities\), as well as small rooms for confidential meetings or calls\. It is located across the street from the Olympic Village SkyTrain station, and means units will still be in close proximity to VGH\. Teams will also continue to have access to bookable space at other Faculty of Medicine sites, such as DHCC, and IRC and DMCBH on the UBC\-Vancouver campus\.

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/map-pic-1-940x733.jpg)

Finance teams will be relocating from City Square to existing Faculty of Medicine shared spaces in IRC 3rd Floor and DMCBH at the UBC Vancouver Campus, with the flexibility to access shared space at DHCC and 520 West 6th Avenue \(5th Floor\) as they need to\.

The goal when identifying these new work locations was to find spaces that continued to support connection and collaboration while also providing the appropriate mix of spaces units need to do their best work\. Integrating units within available and approved work locations was prioritized as it aligns with the Faculty’s goal of using physical spaces flexibly and sustainably, in ways that reflect evolving [ways of working](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/workplace-evolution/)\.

---

## **Supporting the Change**

The planning process for the transition of work locations is well underway and is being jointly led by the Office of the Vice Dean, Education, Faculty of Medicine Finance, and the Faculty of Medicine Space Planning &amp; Facilities Management team\.  
A key priority throughout the project is to ensure the transition is as smooth as possible for all units\. The project team is engaging with unit Directors and staff throughout the planning process\. Affected units will be supported through the transition with regular communications, meetings, guidelines, training, orientation, new technology and tools \(as required\), as well as other resources as needed\.

## **Project Schedule**

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/Screenshot-2026-06-12-112418.png)

The project is taking place over three phases\.

**Phase 1:** Planning and Engagement \(January – August\): This phase is focused on identifying space requirements, storage needs, meeting and videoconferencing needs, and other operational considerations\. Additional requirements gathering will happen now that a location has been confirmed\.

**Phase 2:** Moves and Decant \(August – beginning of October\): This upcoming phase focuses on planning the logistics and detailed schedule for the transition of work locations\.

**Phase 3:** Evaluation and Wrap\-up \(October – November\): Supporting the Faculty’s commitment to continuous improvement, the evaluation phase will assess the planning process and the transition of work locations\. The goal is to understand what went well, what challenges were encountered, and what could be improved or approached differently in future projects\. This phase will include seeking feedback from staff across units\.

**About the Project**

The workspace the Faculty of Medicine has been occupying at City Square Shopping Centre is leased by Vancouver Coastal Health\. That lease is ending at the end of October 2026, which means that units will need to transition work locations\.

The project is being jointly led by Shilpa Samji, Administrative Director, Office of the Vice Dean, Education, Stephen Arentsen, Senior Planner, and Eileen Koshi, Facilities Manager and will be overseen by the UBC\-PHC\-VCH Executive Academic Space Committee\.

**Planning and Implementation**

Postgraduate Medical Education \(PGME\), Continuing Professional Development \(CPD\), and Faculty Development teams will be relocating from City Square office space to 520 West 6th Avenue \(5th Floor\)\.

Finance teams will be relocating from City Square to existing Faculty of Medicine shared spaces in IRC 3rd Floor at the UBC Vancouver Campus, with the flexibility to access shared space at DMCBH, DHCC and 520 West 6th Avenue \(5th Floor\) as they need to\.

The decision regarding 520 West 6th was made in collaboration with VCH\. It was based on what existing spaces were available in close proximity to VGH, and that could accommodate the size and mix of spaces that Faculty of Medicine units need to accomplish their work\.

The transition of work locations is taking place in late September\-early October\.

The goal is to sustainably reuse and reallocate items that are not being moved to the new office locations, to the greatest extent possible\.

**Communications and Engagement**

Hearing from staff throughout the planning process is important to help ensure a successful transition\. Staff will continue to have opportunities to share input, including team meetings, sharing feedback with Directors, and through the anonymous feedback form at the bottom of this page\.

Staff will be kept informed through regular communications from Directors, meetings, and updates to this page\.

If you have questions that haven’t been addressed, please share them with your Director first\. They will be meeting regularly with the project team and can communicate staff feedback and updates\.

**Staff Wellbeing**

The project team recognizes that changes in work location can bring uncertainty\. Throughout the planning process, the project team and Directors will work together to keep staff regularly informed about key decisions and timelines\. Staff wellbeing is a top priority, and we are committed to ensuring a smooth transition\.

If you are feeling anxious or uncertain about the transition of work location, consider reaching out to your Director\. They can help answer questions and provide support during this change\.

**Shared Workspace and Hybrid Work**

Yes, all the work locations that teams are transitioning to operate under a shared workspace model\.

Staff with documented workplace accommodations and/or ergonomics needs supported by a UBC ergonomics assessment will be supported with appropriate accommodations at the new work locations\. If you have ergonomic needs or other require other workplace accommodations that have not yet been documented, please speak with your Director\. You can also find more information through these UBC Health and Wellbeing resources:

[https://hr\.ubc\.ca/health\-and\-wellbeing/ergonomics/ergonomic\-assessments](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/ergonomics/ergonomic-assessments)

[https://hr\.ubc\.ca/health\-and\-wellbeing/workplace\-accessibility/workplace\-accommodations](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/workplace-accessibility/workplace-accommodations)

Ergonomic footrests can stay under desks and do not need to be removed at the end of each workday\. Smaller ergonomic items, such as keyboards, mice or wrist rests, can be stored in the desk pedestals until the next time you book the desk as workspaces are used by multiple people throughout the week\. Please note that pedestals cannot be locked\.

Virtual and hybrid work arrangements will be part of the discussion regarding how teams operate in their new work locations\. This will be determined on a unit\-by\-unit basis and will be communicated to staff through their Director\.

Teams will be transitioning to work locations that are shared with other units\. This approach aligns with the Faculty’s goal of flexibly and sustainably using work locations to their full capacity and in a way that reflects [new ways of working\.](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/workplace-evolution/)

UBC/VCH workplace etiquette guidelines have been developed around noise, kitchen use, access to outside spaces etc\. You can read through it [here](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/Workplace-Etiquette-5th-Floor-520-2.pdf)
