The UBC Faculty of Medicine Space Planning and Facilities Management unit in conjunction with Digital Solutions \(formerly MedIT\) are responsible for supply management and the procurement of computer equipment\.   

---

## Hardware and Software Procurement

UBC Faculty of Medicine offers standardized computer equipment, including desktops, laptops, printers, servers, and audio\-visual equipment \(e\.g\. headset and webcam\)\. Through standardization, we can minimize cost and turnaround times for support\. 

Should you wish to procure any IT related equipment or devices, or software for these devices, please peruse the following links\.

**To order desktop equipment hardware** [Click Here](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/it/equipment/)

**To view software options covered by UBC IT:** [Click Here](https://it.ubc.ca/services/desktop-support-services/software-licensing/software-downloads)

## Servers and Data Centre Hosting

While most equipment is standardized, Digital Solutions can build and configure the appropriate server to suit your needs\. They also provide virtual servers to improve the stability, manageability, and affordability of computing and storage\.

Digital Solutions provides data centre hosting services to manage and monitor your network, server and storage gear\. Our facilities provide physical security, cooling, back\-up power and high\-speed connectivity to UBC networks\. Storing your equipment in our data centres also frees up your internal networks and allows your organization to focus on its core business, not the technological infrastructure that supports it\.

**For more information:** email or phone the [Digital Solutions Service Desk](mailto:medit.servicedesk@ubc.ca) \| 1\.877\.266\.0666\.

## Videoconference Equipment

Digital Solutions works to standardize Video Conference equipment across Faculty of Medicine teaching and learning spaces\.  Should you wish to view an existing space, or book a room for your own conference please visit the Room Finder Page [linked here\.](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/room-booking/)

**For more information:** email or phone the  [Digital Solutions Service Desk](mailto:medit.servicedesk@ubc.ca) \| 1\.877\.266\.0666\.
