Michael Smith Health Research BC’s Scholar and Research Trainee awards provide funding to support BC’s next generation of health researchers in advancing science and improving the health of British Columbians\. Applications are now open for the below competitions, with letters of intent due by **December 5, 2022**\.

The [Scholar awards](https://healthresearchbc.ca/fundingprogram/scholar-program/ "Scholar Program – Health Research BC") help BC researchers build leading\-edge health research programs, train the next generation of scientists, and make significant contributions to their field\. The award provides salary support to early\-career researchers, allowing them to dedicate 75 percent of their time to health research activities\.

The [Research Trainee awards](https://healthresearchbc.ca/fundingprogram/research-trainee-program/ "Research Trainee Program – Health Research BC") support health researchers in training to build health research capacity and contribute to the success of BC’s health research landscape\. This award provides post\-doctoral fellows or health professional researchers with salary support for dedicated research activities\.

[Learn more and apply](https://healthresearchbc.ca/news_article/2023-scholar-and-research-trainee-competitions-applications-now-open/ "2023 Scholar and Research Trainee competitions: Applications now open – Health Research BC")
