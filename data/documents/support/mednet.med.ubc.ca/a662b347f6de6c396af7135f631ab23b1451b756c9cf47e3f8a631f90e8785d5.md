Join the the eighth annual Celebrate the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\) event, in\-person at UBC Vancouver on Thursday, October 9\.

Hosted by UBC’s Institute for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning and the Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology, Celebrate SoTL is an opportunity for UBC SoTL practitioners to share their stories from practice, engage in dialogue around teaching and learning topics and network with colleagues across disciplines\.

Whether you’re new to the world of SoTL or a seasoned practitioner, register to engage in insightful conversations about teaching and learning in the classroom\.

-   **Date:** Thursday, October 9, 2025 from 10 am–3 pm
-   **Location:** In\-person at the [Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](https://maps.ubc.ca/?code=IBLC), UBC Vancouver

[Learn more &amp; register](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/celebrate-sotl-2025/)
