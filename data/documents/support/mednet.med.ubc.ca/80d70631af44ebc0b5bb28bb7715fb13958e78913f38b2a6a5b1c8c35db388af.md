Registrations are open for the Conference on Longitudinal Integrated Clerkships \(CLIC 2026\), taking place **September 16–20, 2026** in Calgary, Alberta\.

Hosted by the University of Calgary’s Cumming School of Medicine, the conference will include workshops, plenary sessions, presentations, networking opportunities and opportunities to connect with colleagues working in longitudinal and distributed medical education\.

This year’s theme, *Convergence: From Place to Practice — Learning through Connection*, will bring together medical educators, physicians, learners, researchers and leaders from across Canada and around the world to explore the impact of longitudinal integrated clerkships and place\-based medical education\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://www.clicmeded.org/ "Conference on Longitudinal Integrated Clerkships 2026")
