The UBC community is invited to comment on the Statements on Responsible Investing in preparation for the submission to the Board of Governors in the spring of 2023\.

UBC’s Statements on Responsible Investment are currently drafted in collaboration between UBC Treasury, Office of the University Counsel, and UBC Investment Management to refine the responsible investing approach to reflect current environmental, social and governance findings into our policy\.

The Statements will replace UBC’s current Responsible Investing Policy, which was last updated in 2014\. Since its initial adoption, UBC has undertaken additional work to refine the responsible investing approach and define responsible investing commitments and targets\.

Share your feedback via email to [finance\.sustainability@ubc\.ca](mailto:finance.sustainability@ubc.ca) by **February 17, 2023**\.
