[Dr\. Jim Frankish](https://spph.ubc.ca/memoriam-jim-frankish/) passed away on July 4, 2021\. He was a Professor Emeritus at the School of Population and Public Health, where he was also a member of for 28 years\. Dr\. Frankish was a UBC alumni and a clinical psychologist\.

Throughout his career, Jim focused on community\-based public health and taught both undergraduate and graduate courses\.
