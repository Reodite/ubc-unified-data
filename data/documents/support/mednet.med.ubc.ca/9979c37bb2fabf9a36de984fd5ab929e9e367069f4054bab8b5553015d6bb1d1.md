If you’re looking to build capacity in your team to advance departmental priorities or are in need of additional support, submit a proposal to hire temporary student employees through UBC Vancouver’s Work Learn program\.

The Work Learn program funds approximately 3,500 part\-time hourly appointments each year, subsidizing meaningful work experiences for current UBC Vancouver students\. Proposals for the winter 2023 employment opportunities \(September 1, 2023–April 30, 2024\) will be accepted until **June 19**\.

[See all details &amp; submit a proposal](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-affairs/ubc-career-centre/work-learn "Work Learn program | UBCV Career Centre")

Learn more about the [UBC Okanagan Work Study program](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/hire-a-student/work-study/) and key dates\.
