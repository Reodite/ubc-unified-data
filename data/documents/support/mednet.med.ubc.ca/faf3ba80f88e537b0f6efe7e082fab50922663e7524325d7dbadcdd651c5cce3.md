Join the Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity &amp; Inclusion \(REDI\) from **12–3:30 pm on Wednesday, September 20, 2023** for “We Welcome the Children Back Home: The Burden of Sorrow &amp; Survival of the Indian Residential School Experience in Canada”\.

Part of the REDI Indigenous Speaker Series, this virtual event brings together a panel of survivors of the Indian Residential School experience in Canada\. The session will welcome and honour these brave and resilient survivors, as they lead a discussion about the urgency and motivation to right and write a new history in Canada that is based on a proper redress for Indigenous peoples and communities\.

[See panelists &amp; register](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/we-welcome-the-children-back-home-the-burden-of-sorrow-and-survival-of-the-indian-residential-school-experience-in-canada/ "We Welcome the Children Back Home - Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity &amp; Inclusion")
