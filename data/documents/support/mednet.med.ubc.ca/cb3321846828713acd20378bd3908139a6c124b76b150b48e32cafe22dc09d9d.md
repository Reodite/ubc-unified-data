If you are responsible for managing a research facility at UBC Vancouver, the VP Research &amp; Innovation Office invites you to apply for additional funding through the Shared Research Platforms Competition\.

The Shared Research Platforms Competition offers research facilities an opportunity to receive support and resources for their ongoing operations, in support of UBC’s Strategic Plan goal to strengthen shared infrastructure and resources to support research excellence\.

Submit an expression of interest by **5 pm on May 1**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://research.ubc.ca/vpri-competitions-initiatives/shared-research-platforms-competition "Shared Research Platforms Competition | UBC Research + Innovation")
