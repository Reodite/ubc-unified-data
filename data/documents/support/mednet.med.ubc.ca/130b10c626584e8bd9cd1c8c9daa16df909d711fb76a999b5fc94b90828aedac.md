Adjunct Professors provide teaching without patient care services across the MD Undergraduate Program\. This teaching is captured in the Teaching Tracking and Payment System \(TTPS\) and payment is processed in UBC’s Workday System\.

## TTPS System Timelines for Adjunct Professor Teaching

Capturing teaching provided by Adjunct Professors introduces new timelines and delivery periods for each step in the track to pay process in the TTPS\. 

[Adjunct Professor System Timelines for 2026](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/TTPS-Adjunct-Professor-System-Timelines-2026.pdf)

## Tracking to Payment Workflow – TTPS &amp; Workday 

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/02/TTP-Workflow-APs-2023-1.jpg)

**Activity Trackers, please note:** Since payment is made through UBC Payroll and not linked through the TTPS, all Adjunct Professors are marked as eligible for payment but with no payment amount associated in TTPS\. VDE HR applies the correct payment amount when they prepare the EIB report for UBC payroll\.

Adjunct Professors do not have access to view their teaching in the TTPS, instead they can view their teaching statements in Workday under Personal &gt; Documents\.
