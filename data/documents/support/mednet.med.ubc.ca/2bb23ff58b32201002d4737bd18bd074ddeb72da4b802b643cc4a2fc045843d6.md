Community\-engaged researchers, register for an upcoming “Partnering in Research” professional development workshop\. The workshop series, a collaboration between [UBC Knowledge Exchange](https://kx.ubc.ca/) and [UBC Community Engagement](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/), is open to UBC researchers, staff and students engaged in community\-partnered research\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/our-work/partnering-in-research/ "Partnering in Research - UBC Community Engagement")

---

## Upcoming workshops

### September 20 \| [How to Keep the Lights On: Strategic and Financial Planning for Sustainability in Community Engaged Research](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/events/2024-september-pir-workshop/) \(online\)

Learn to craft a compelling business case for your community\-engaged research program\. Gain insights into the business case framework, access practical tools, and identify strategies for financial sustainability\.

### October 8 \| [Crafting a Compelling Narrative: How to Tell Your Research Story](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/events/2024-october-pir-workshop/) \(online\)

Learn the art and science of storytelling to tell your research story with authenticity and impact\. Gain tools to captivate funders’ attention and showcase the significance of your work\.

### November 7 \| [Demystifying UBC’s Financial System: Supporting Fair and Timely Payments for Community Partners](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/events/2024-november-pir-workshop/) \(online\)

Through interactive exercises, real\-life scenarios, and practical tips on how to navigate the financial system and manage common challenges, participants will learn valuable skills and insights for processing timely, equitable compensation that can help to strengthen and sustain their relationships\.
