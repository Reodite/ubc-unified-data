The *Students as Partners* fund provides grants to encourage student\-faculty partnerships in undergraduate course design at UBC Vancouver\.

Faculty and undergraduate student partners may receive up to $7,100 to design or revise existing undergraduate courses, as well as dedicated support from an assigned Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology staff member, and access to a learning community\.

Submit an application by **3pm on June 16**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://sap.ubc.ca/ "Students as Partners fund")
