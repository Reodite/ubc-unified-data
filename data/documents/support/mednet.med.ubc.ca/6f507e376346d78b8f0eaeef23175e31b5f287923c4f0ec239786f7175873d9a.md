Effective November 2, 2020 UBC Financial Management System \(FMS\) was replaced by the new Integrated Renewal Program \(IRP\) Workday system\. As of October 28, 2022, legacy PeopleSoft tools including FMS have been decommissioned\. Tableau reports replicating PeopleSoft screens are now accessible\. For more information, see [PeopleSoft Decommissioning](https://cio.ubc.ca/data-governance/data-reporting/reports-dashboards/peoplesoft-decommissioning)
