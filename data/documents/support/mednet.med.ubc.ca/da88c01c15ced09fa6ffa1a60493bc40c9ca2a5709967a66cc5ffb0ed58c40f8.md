The Principal’s Staff Awards celebrate the contributions of individuals instrumental in helping UBC’s Okanagan campus achieve its strategic imperatives, and to contribute to the commitment to create an outstanding work environment\.

All UBC Okanagan faculty, staff and students are invited to nominate staff for the below awards by **4 pm on Friday, June 5, 2026**\.

[Learn more &amp; nominate](https://hr.ok.ubc.ca/learning-and-engagement/awards-recognition/principals-staff-awards/ "Principal's Staff Awards | UBC Okanagan Human Resources")

---

## Award categories

Nominate individuals and teams across the five award categories:

-   Collaborative Excellence \(team\)
-   Creativity and Innovation \(individual or team\)
-   Emerging Leadership \(individual\)
-   Leadership \(individual\)
-   Nurture a Strong and Vibrant UBC Community \(individual or team\)
