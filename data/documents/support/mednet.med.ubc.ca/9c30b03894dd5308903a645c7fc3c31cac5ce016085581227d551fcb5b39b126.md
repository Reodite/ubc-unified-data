This holiday season, help save energy as part of UBC Sustainability’s Seasonal Shutdown, which lowers the temperature in buildings during the winter break to reduce energy use and emissions on campus\.

[Before you leave for the holidays\.\.\. Switch off electronics, turn off the lights, unplug small appliances, close windows and blinds](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/12/20221213_Sustainability_SeasonalShutdown_1000x563-940x529.png)

Before you leave for the holidays:

-   Switch off electronics
-   Unplug small appliances
-   Turn off the lights
-   Close windows and blinds
-   Report any air or water leaks
-   Shut fume hood sashes
-   Shut down non\-essential lab equipment

[Learn more &amp; download the Seasonal Shutdown checklist](https://sustain.ubc.ca/get-involved/campaigns/seasonal-shutdown "Seasonal Shutdown | UBC Sustainability")
