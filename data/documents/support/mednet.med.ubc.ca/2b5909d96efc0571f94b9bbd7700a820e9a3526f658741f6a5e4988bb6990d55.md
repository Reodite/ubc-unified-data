Nominations are now open for the 2025 Marion Spencer Fay Award\.

The [Marion Spencer Fay Award](https://drexel.edu/medicine/Community-Engagement/institute-for-womens-health-and-leadership/programs/marion-spencer-fay-award/) recognizes a woman physician or scientist who has made an exceptionally significant contribution to health care as a practitioner, medical educator, administrator or research scientist\.

We invite you to consider colleagues who could be eligible for these distinctions\. Contact [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca) to declare your intent to nominate, then submit the application directly via the award website by **Friday, March 28, 2025**\.

If your nomination package requires institutional support, please include a draft letter for consideration and email the completed nomination package to [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca) by **March 7, 2025** for internal review\.

If you have questions, please contact [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca)\.

[Learn more &amp; nominate](https://drexel.edu/medicine/Community-Engagement/institute-for-womens-health-and-leadership/programs/marion-spencer-fay-award/ "Marion Spencer Fay Award | Drexel University College of Medicine")
