A message from Wyeth Wasserman, Vice Dean, Research and Roanne Preston, interim Chief Medical Officer, BC Women’s Hospital and Health Centre\.

---

We are pleased to announce that Dr\. Gillian Hanley has been appointed Director, Women’s Health Research Institute \(WHRI\), UBC Faculty of Medicine and Executive Director, WHRI effective to April 30, 2031\.

Dr\. Hanley is an associate professor in the Department of Obstetrics &amp; Gynaecology, a researcher at WHRI and the Canada Research Chair in Population‑Based Gynecologic and Perinatal Outcomes\.

[Dr\. Gillian Hanley](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/GillianHanley-5K4A0592_400x400.jpg)

An internationally recognized leader in ovarian cancer prevention, Dr\. Hanley’s research focuses on improving women’s health through population‑based data, with particular emphasis on gynecologic cancer survivorship and perinatal mental health\. Her work demonstrating the effectiveness of ovarian cancer prevention strategies has informed clinical practice guidelines in Canada and beyond\. She has also served as Co\-Associate Head of Research in the Department of Obstetrics &amp; Gynaecology, as a member of the Ovarian Cancer Canada’s Prevention Task Force, and a scientific advisory board member for the Ovarian Cancer Research Alliance\.

As Director, WHRI, Dr\. Hanley will oversee the Institute’s operations and research, helping to foster a collaborative, inclusive and excellence‑driven research culture to support faculty, trainees and staff in advancing high‑impact women’s health research\. She will also oversee the development and implementation of a new strategic plan for WHRI, in alignment with the strategic goals of the Faculty and health authorities\. As Director, Dr\. Hanley will continue to strengthen collaborations with academic, health system, government and community partners across Canada and around the globe\.

Dr\. Hanley’s appointment follows the interim leadership of Dr\. Gina Ogilvie\. We would like to thank Dr\. Gina Ogilvie for her leadership and steady guidance during this transition period\.

Please join us in congratulating Dr\. Hanley on her appointment\.
