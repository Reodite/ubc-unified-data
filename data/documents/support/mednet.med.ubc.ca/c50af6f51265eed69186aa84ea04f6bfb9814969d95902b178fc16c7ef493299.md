Find information and resources to support the reviewing and processing of clinical faculty promotions\. 

---

Promotion review for clinical faculty is **optional**\. Recommendations and decisions regarding promotion are based on a review of the clinical faculty’s contributions to the FoM and UBC relative to [the criteria](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies) for the applicable rank—not on time spent in the current rank\. Clinical Faculty are expected to maintain an up\-to\-date record of their academic activities using the [UBC FoM Clinical Faculty CV template](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#templates)\. Heads/Directors \(or delegate\) are responsible for providing Clinical Faculty with regular feedback, including guidance on eligibility, criteria, and expectations for promotion\. 

## **Promotion Timeline** 

Recommendations for promotions are submitted to the President twice per year for review and approval\. Heads/Directors are encouraged to use effective dates of January 1 and July 1\. Other dates may result in delays\. 

If you require guidance, please reach out to the [Faculty HR team](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/contacts/)\.  

Department/Schools are responsible for communicating internal deadlines to their clinical faculty members\.  

The Faculty of Medicine’s and UBC’s deadlines for review and approval will typically be:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clinical Faculty Promotion Timeline** | **Batch 1** | **Batch 2** |
| Deadline for Submission to Faculty HR \(if a Faculty Committee review is required\) | March 1 | September 1 |
| Deadline for Submission to Faculty HR \(if no Faculty Committee review is required\) | May 1 | October 1 |
| Effective Date of Promotion | July 1 | January 1 |

## **Roles in Preparing for and Communicating About Promotion** 

### Clinical Faculty

-   Initiate meeting with the Head/Director \(or delegate\) a year in advance 
-   Confirm promotion review timing 
-   Get feedback on their contributions and promotion package 

### **Head/Director \(or Delegate\)** 

-   Provide clinical faculty with feedback, including: 
    -   Timing of review 
    -   Eligibility
    -   Criteria 
    -   Expectations for promotion
    -   Feedback on draft promotion dossier \(if available\)
-   Upon CARP review, notify the clinical faculty in writing of their recommendation to the Dean \(the candidate shall not be informed of the specific results of the CARP Committee vote\) 
-   If recommendation is negative, provide detailed and specific reasons and feedback for improvement, if applicable

## **Promotion Requirements** 

Requirements for promotion are provided to clinical faculty on the [Reappointment and Promotions](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/reappointment-promotion/#promotion-requirements) page\. 

## **Promotion Review Process** 

1\. **Ensure promotion submission is complete\.** Once the candidate submits their promotion application package, review to ensure all required documents are provided, depending on the promotion rank that the candidate is applying for\. 

In addition to the candidate’s application package, up to two supplemental documents/letters may be included: 

-   A **UBC Division Head’s Letter of Support** \(applicable only to those Departments with Divisions\)
-   A **Letter Outlining Contextual Opportunities**\. This letter may be solicited as needed to provide context about the candidate’s site and to outline opportunities or possible challenges for contribution at that location\. It should address the period since the candidate’s **last** promotion or appointment to their present rank \(i\.e\., letter can be from a RAD, VFMP hospital sites/site director, or a leader who can speak to the opportunities for contribution at the site\)\.   

2\. **If applicable, solicit reference letters\.** For promotion to the rank of **Clinical Professor,** the Department/School will need to solicit **two reference letters** that will form part of the package for review\. For more information on soliciting referees, please refer to the [Soliciting Referees page](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/soliciting-referees/)\. 

3\. **Assess evidence of teaching effectiveness\.** If the candidate cannot retrieve student evaluations of teaching, assist them in finding other ways to obtain evidence\. 

4\. **Review against promotion criteria\.** All application for promotion must be reviewed by the CARP Committee to assess whether the promotion criteria are met in accordance with the [Clinical Faculty Policy](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\.  

5\. **CARP review\.** Recommendations and decisions regarding promotion are based on a review of the Clinical Faculty member’s contributions to the FoM and UBC relative to [the criteria](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies) for the applicable rank\. CARP Committees should place primary emphasis on assessing contributions made by the Clinical Faculty member at their present rank, meaning since the time of their **last** promotion or appointment to their present rank\. 

If the CARP review raises concerns relative to the criteria, please refer to [Handling Concerns on the Establishing a CARP Committee page](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/establishing-a-carp-committee/#handling-concerns)\. 

6\. **If applicable, FCARP review\.** Promotion reviews to the rank of **Clinical Professor** require consultation with FCARP\. 

7\. **If promotion is recommended, prepare recommendation letter\.** The Head/Director will need to prepare a [Letter of Recommendation to the Dean](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#templates) with a rationale explaining the evidence to support/not support a promotion to the requested rank, based on the criteria set out in the [Clinical Faculty Policy](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\. For more information on preparing recommendation letters, please refer to the Preparing Recommendation Letters page\. 

The Dean will review the letter and make an independent recommendation to the President\. 

8\. **Submit promotion\.** Submit your list of promotions via FoM HR OneDrive using the Enterprise Interface Builder \(EIB\) template provided by the Faculty HR team\. When submitting the promotion for approval, please ensure the following documents are included: 

**Via Files for FoM HR OneDrive**  

-   Candidate’s promotion package 
-   Head/Director’s Letter of Recommendation, including the vote of the CARP Committee
-   UBC Division Head’s Letter of Support \(if applicable\)
-   Letter Outlining Contextual Opportunities \(if required\)  
-   Two letters of reference \(for clinical professor only\)  
-   Supplemental evidence of teaching effectiveness \(where not included in candidate’s promotion package\)  

## Committee Review

When there is a discrepancy between the CARP Committee and the Head/Director on the recommendation for promotion, the file moves to the [FCARP Committee](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/contacts/#committee) to review, with the final decision on the promotion to be made by the Dean\. 

In situations where the Head/Director and the CARP Committee does not recommend promotion, the applicant may request a review of the recommendation by the [FCARP Committee](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/contacts/#committee), with the final decision on the promotion to be made by the Dean\.
