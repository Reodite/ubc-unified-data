## Checking into a personal workspace

On days when you have an individual workspace booked, the first time you log in to the Eptura Engage desktop site a large check\-in box will appear and prompt you to check\-in\.

On the Eptura Engage mobile app, a blue check\-in button will appear on the bottom of the app screen prompting you to check in\. Eptura Engage mobile app, also, offers an “Auto check in” feature under your profile option\.

If you don’t check in by 9:30 a\.m\. for a morning \(half\-day\) or whole\-day booking/1:30 p\.m\. for an afternoon booking, the space will be released and made available for others to book\. The system will send you an automated check\-in reminder at approximately 9:20 a\.m\. or 1:20 p\.m\. to remind you to check in\.

## Editing/cancelling a personal workspace booking

If you have finished using your workspace, and the day is not over, please click the “release” button on the Eptura Engage home page to make the workspace available for others\.

Please remember to update or release your workspace in Eptura Engage if your schedule changes\. Doing so will allow others to better plan out their workweek when making their bookings\.

## **Booking a personal workspace on behalf of another person**

No, only people who have admin rights can book on behalf of other people\.

## Eptura Engage Issues and Outages

Please report the issue to FoM Facilities at  [facilities\.coordinator@ubc\.ca](mailto:facilities.coordinator@ubc.ca)

If the Eptura Engage system is down, for outages lasting 1 day to 1 week, workspace allocation will shift to a first\-come, first served basis\. For outages lasting more than 1 week, a temporary booking system will be implemented\.

If you leave your workspace and it is claimed by someone else, you will need to find another available workspace when you return\. The workspaces operate on a first\-come, first\-served basis during an outage\.

During an outage, access to ergonomic equipment and single\-occupancy offices will remain available and be prioritized based on need\. Please reach out to [simona\.dziaugyte@ubc\.ca](mailto:simona.dziaugyte@ubc.ca) if you require access to specific equipment\.

If you have questions or need help during an outage, please reach out to [fomfacilities\.ops@ubc\.ca](mailto:fomfacilities.ops@ubc.ca)\.

## Booking meeting spaces and other rooms

Booking meeting spaces will continue through RoomFinder and Advance Booking Manager, which allow teams to make recurring bookings up to six months in advance\. The transition to shared space does not impact this process\. For more information, visit the Room Booking website on MedNet: [https://mednet\.med\.ubc\.ca/resources/room\-booking/](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/room-booking/)
