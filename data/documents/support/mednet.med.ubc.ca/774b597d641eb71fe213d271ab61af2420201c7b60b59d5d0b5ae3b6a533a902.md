Do you know how to ensure your UBC office or workstation is safe? Find out by completing a short survey, and be entered into a draw to win one of two personal Emergency Readiness Kits\!

The survey is part of the Faculty of Medicine Health &amp; Safety team’s Worksite Safety Inspection Initiative\. **All Faculty of Medicine faculty and staff** who work onsite in office/administrative spaces are asked to answer 10 short questions about their UBC office or workstation by **January 31**\.

You do not need to complete the survey for laboratory locations or home offices\. If you work at multiple locations you only need to complete the survey once\.

[Complete the survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2hm8VYoyHmsqMey "UBC Faculty of Medicine Inspection Initiative 2023 - Qualtrics survey")

---

## Workplace safety tips

1.  Beware of heavy items stored unsecured on top of high shelves\. Anything that presents a falling hazard should be stored securely, or, better yet, stored close to the ground\.
2.  Know who to call\. Make sure you have easy access to local First Aid and Security numbers should you need them in an emergency\.

## Survey frequently asked questions

The survey consists of 10 quick questions and shouldn’t take longer than a few minutes to complete\. Questions include checking that aisles and doorways by your workspace are free of obstructions, that power outlets are not overloaded, and confirming that you are aware of the evacuation muster station for your office\.

Every worker \(faculty, staff and paid student\) who has a worksite on UBC Vancouver campus or any UBC site, institute or centre is asked to complete the survey\.

You do not need to complete the survey for your home office and you only need to complete the survey once\. If you work at multiple locations on UBC premises, or work at a shared or communal workstation, choose one office/workstation to complete\.

Inspecting worksites regularly is a [WorkSafeBC requirement](https://www.worksafebc.com/en/law-policy/occupational-health-safety/searchable-ohs-regulation/ohs-regulation/part-03-rights-and-responsibilities#SectionNumber:3.3 "Occupational Health and Safety Programs | WorksafeBC")\. The Local Safety Team \(LST\) in your area will be following up with respective supervisors to ensure all locations have been inspected\.

The survey link will be open until **January 31, 2023**\.

For some issues, resolution is simple and details will be provided\. For other issues, the Local Safety Team \(LST\) in your area will connect with you to ensure the hazard is resolved\.

All questions can be directed to the Health &amp; Safety team at [fom\.safety@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.safety@ubc.ca)\.
