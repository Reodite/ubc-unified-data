A message from Dr\. Hannah Carolan, Associate Dean, Admissions, MD Undergraduate Program, &amp; Nadir Sliti, Interview Officer, MD Undergraduate Admissions, UBC Faculty of Medicine\.

---

The MD Undergraduate Program Admissions Office invites you to [participate in the 2026 admission interviews](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/FINAL-Joint-Prospective-Information-Sheet-2025-26.pdf) for the MD Undergraduate Program\. Your participation in this important process is integral to selecting the next class of UBC medical students\.

All admissions interviews and training sessions will be held in a virtual format\. Sign up to become an interviewer by **Friday, October 31**\.

## 2026 Multiple Mini\-Interviews \(MMI\)

Invited d MD undergraduate program applicants will meet 10 different interviewers in a video\-chat environment\.

Key dates:

-   The MMI dates are **January 31, February 1 and 7, 2026**\.
-   Interviewers must attend a virtual training session on **January 28**\. Attending the training session is mandatory, even if you have been an interviewer before\.

Learn more in the [MMI Interviewer Information Package](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/FINAL-Comprehensive-MMI-Interviewer-Information-2025-26.pdf)\.

## 2026 Northern &amp; Rural Panel Interviews \(NRPI\)

The NRPI provides invited applicants the opportunity to express their interest in rural medicine, highlight their rural experiences and connections, and speak to their suitability for the northern and rural seats in the MD Undergraduate Program\.

Clinicians, faculty, community members and medical students living in northern and rural regions of British Columbia can apply to be an interviewer\.

Key dates:

-   The NRPI date is **February 8, 2026**\.
-   Interviewers must attend a virtual training session on **February 4**\. Attendance at this training session is mandatory for all interviewers\.

Learn more in the [NRPI Interviewer Information Package](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/FINAL-Comprehensive-NRPI-Interviewer-Information-2025-26.pdf)\.

## How to sign up

-   When completing the interviewer sign\-up form, please indicate your interest in participating in the MMI, the NRPI or both\.
-   Please note that you cannot be an interviewer if you, a family member, personal friend or close acquaintance is applying for medical school this year or in the near future\.
-   Sign up as an interviewer by **Friday, October 31, 2025** at 11:59 pm PST\. The form takes approximately 10 minutes to complete\.
-   For help, please review the [Interviewer Sign\-Up Form Help Guide](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/Help-Guide-Interviewer-Application-2025-26.pdf)\.

[Sign up now](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/FINAL-Joint-Prospective-Information-Sheet-2025-26.pdf)

We know that your time is valuable, and we thank you for considering this important role in our admissions selection process\. Without your support, we would not be able to evaluate so many qualified applicants\.

If you have any additional questions regarding either the MMI or the NRPI, please contact the MD Admissions Office at [interviews\.md@exchange\.ubc\.ca](mailto:interviews.md@exchange.ubc.ca)\.
