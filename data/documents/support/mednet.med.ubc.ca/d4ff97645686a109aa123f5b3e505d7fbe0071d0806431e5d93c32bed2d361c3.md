UBC is refreshing its [Wellbeing Strategic Framework](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/node/5116) to reflect our community’s evolving needs and priorities\.

Faculty, staff and students are invited to help shape future wellbeing priorities across our campuses, by visiting pop\-up booths on both campuses, participating in workshops and sharing your thoughts through an online feedback form\.

Your input will help shape the refreshed Framework and strengthen alignment with UBC’s Strategic Plan and other intersecting priorities, including Indigeneity, equity and sustainability\.

## **Upcoming open house sessions**

Share your input and enjoy conversation, engagement activities and opportunities to share feedback on wellbeing at UBC at the below open house events\.

-   **UBC Vancouver:** Thursday, June 4 from 12–2 pm 
-   **Virtual:** Wednesday, June 10 from 12–1 pm
-   **UBC Okanagan:** Tuesday, June 16 from 12–2 pm
