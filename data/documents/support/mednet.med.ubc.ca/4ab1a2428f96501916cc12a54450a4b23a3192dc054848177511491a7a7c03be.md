## What is one45?

one45 is a software specifically\-built for medical schools to manage, schedule, track &amp; assess curriculum &amp; people more effectively\.

## Educational Administrative Systems Support Analyst – one45

This role will support users of administrative systems across both the MD Undergraduate &amp; Post Graduate Medical Education Programs\.  To make the one45 support process more effective, the Educational Administrative Systems Support Analyst will support both the MDUG and PGME programs with respect to:

-   Triaging support requests
-   Troubleshooting problems 
-   one45 Training 
-   Aiding in the implementation of business process improvement recommendations related to one45 
-   Initiating the annual security audit in one45

The one45 support team will still be involved in solving the technical problems at the request of the System Support Analyst\.

## one45 Support Process

All faculty and staff requests relating to one45 should be submitted to the Faculty’s Educational Administrative Systems Support Analyst; [fomeas\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:fomeas.support@ubc.ca)\.  You can expect your request to be:

-   Reviewed within one business day 
-   Resolution within two business days or an expected completion date

When submitting your support requests, please include the following information:

-   Name
-   Contact Information \(Email, phone, etc\)
-   one45 Group
-   Request Type
-   Issue

*Support request types have been included below for your reference\.*  

If you should have any questions regarding the one45 support process, please do not hesitate to contact [fomeas\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:fomeas.support@ubc.ca)\.

## Support Request Types 

-   Setting up lotteries 
-   New block dates 
-   User imports \(Adding or Deleting Users\) 
-   Rotation Schedule \(Schedule imports / Schedule rollovers\) 
-   Setting up rotation linkages 
-   Academic Sessions 
-   Curriculum Schedules 
-   Permissions 
-   Evaluations 
-   Creating/editing forms \(up to 2 weeks\)
-   Combining multiple accounts 
-   Deactivating accounts 
-   TRAINING
-   Other

*Tasks that will be escalated to one45 \(24 hrs\. \+\):* 

-   Creating new groups 
-   Creating new block templates 
-   Academic Schedule Calendar access 
-   Rolling back block sendouts that have forms sent out
-   Rolling over a curriculum schedule 
-   Importing mappings and objectives 
-   Creating/editing encounter logs
