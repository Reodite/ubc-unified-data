[Dr\. Jennifer Oates](https://psychiatry.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-jennifer-oates-clinical-instructor/ "In Memoriam: Dr. Jennifer Oates, Clinical Instructor - Department of Psychiatry") passed away August 8, 2023\.

A current Clinical Instructor, Dr\. Oates joined the Department of Psychiatry in 2015\. She taught and practiced as a geriatric psychiatrist on Vancouver Island\.

Read more [about Dr\. Oates’ life](https://www.mccallgardens.com/obituaries/dr-jennifer-sibilla-maria-oates "Dr. Jennifer Oates Obituary | McCall Gardens Funeral and Cremation Service")\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Thursday, August 31, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/09/13/jennifer-oates/ "Jennifer Oates Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
