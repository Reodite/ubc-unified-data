Move UBC is an annual university\-wide initiative to encourage faculty, staff and the wider university community to add movement into their daily lives\.

This February, Move UBC marks 10 years of inspiring our campus community to move more\. From boosting wellbeing to building a more connected campus, Move UBC encourages our campus community to move in ways that feel empowering, comfortable, and motivating\.

[Learn more](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/move-ubc "Move UBC")

## Get involved

See ways to get involved this Move UBC month:

-   **[Move UBC events calendar](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/move-ubc/move-ubc-events-calendar):** Explore ways to get moving at UBC Vancouver and Okanagan campuses, with kick\-off events, free fitness classes, Active Wear Wednesdays and more\.
    -   **February 24 \| [Move UBC Keynote: The Power of Movement for Resilience with Olympian Clara Hughes](https://recreation.ubc.ca/2026/01/21/move-ubc-keynote-the-power-of-movement-for-resilience-with-olympian-clara-hughes/):** Featuring Olympian and mental health advocate Clara Hughes, join on campus at UBC Vancouver and via a livestreamed UBC Okanagan watch party\. Clara will share her experiences as a high\-performance athlete and explore how movement can support resilience, mental health and overall wellbeing both on campus and beyond\. This event will include opening remarks from UBC President and Vice\-Chancellor, Dr\. Benoit\-Antoine Bacon\.
-   **Physical activity resources:** Small changes can have a big impact\! Explore [tips to help you move](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/move-ubc/make-your-move) regularly throughout your day\.
-   **Host a movement break:** Visit [UBC Rec on Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCMqboWQbrq6_Zl5mWin8l0A) for a virtual 5\-minute stretch break — and include during your meetings or classes to help participants re\-energize\.
-   **Invite the Move U crew:** [Request a live movement break](https://recreation.ubc.ca/get-moving/move-u-crew/) at UBC Vancouver or UBC Okanagan to bring a fun pause to your class or meeting\.
