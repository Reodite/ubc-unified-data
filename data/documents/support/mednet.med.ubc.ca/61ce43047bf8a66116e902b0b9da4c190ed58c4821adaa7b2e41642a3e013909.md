Community\-engaged researchers, register for an upcoming “Partnering in Research” professional development workshop\. The workshop series, a collaboration between [UBC Knowledge Exchange](https://kx.ubc.ca/) and [UBC Community Engagement](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/), is open to UBC researchers, staff and students engaged in community\-partnered research\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/our-work/partnering-in-research/ "Partnering in Research - UBC Community Engagement")

---

## Upcoming workshops

### May 21 \| [Defining and Describing Community\-Engaged Scholarship for Promotion and Tenure](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/events/defining-and-describing-community-engaged-scholarship-for-promotion-and-tenure/) \(online\)

Tenure and promotion can be a daunting road for scholars who practice engaged research methods\. Learn how to effectively showcase your community\-engaged scholarship, focusing on outputs, outcomes, and processes, to gain recognition and appreciation across your portfolio\.

### June 3 \| [I’m a Community Engaged Researcher\. Should I Start a Non\-Profit?](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/events/should-i-start-a-non-profit/) \(in\-person, UBCV\)

Running a non\-profit demands time and resources but it can also offer an avenue to grow the impact of your engaged research and implementation\. Explore the pros and cons of non\-profit organizations as a model for research and research implementation and assess whether launching one is appropriate for your community\-engaged research initiatives\.
