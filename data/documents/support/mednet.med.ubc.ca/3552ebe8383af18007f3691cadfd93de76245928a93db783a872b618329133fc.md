TransLink and UBC Vancouver have partnered to expand the Mobi e\-bike share program to campus\. Five electrified bike share stations will be installed on campus this fall, with more anticipated by next summer\.

While the existing HOPR bikes on campus are useful for travelling shorter distances around campus, Mobi’s e\-bikes will provide a bike share option for trips off campus and are great for longer rides, better connecting us with the rest of the region\.

UBC faculty, staff and students can also access discounts on Mobi annual memberships\.
