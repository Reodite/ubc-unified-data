If you are a new faculty member at UBC, new to teaching, or have moved into a new position \(including faculty, postdoctoral fellows and graduate students/TAs\), explore these professional development opportunities from the UBC [Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/) \(CTLT\)\.

---

## Resource

### [Faculty Primer](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/resources/new-faculty-orientation-guide/faculty-primer/)

Explore a list of curated resources, workshops and people that can help with your teaching responsibilities\.

## Upcoming dates

### [Teaching Development Program for New Faculty](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/tdp) \(multi\-access\)

This cohort program supports faculty members across UBC campuses in their professional growth as teachers\. The intended audience is faculty who are either new to teaching, new to UBC, or both, with an emphasis on those who are new to teaching\.

-   Program runs from August 18, 2023–April 24, 2024
-   Apply by **August 18**\.

### [New Faculty Teaching Orientation: Making Connections](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/nfto) \(multi\-access\)

This one\-day orientation at UBCV serves as a space for new faculty to learn about the academic culture, policies, and practices of the institution, as well as the resources available to support their teaching, research, and service\.

-   August 17, 10 am–12:30 pm

### [CTLT Summer Institute](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/summerinstitute) \(in\-person, online &amp; multi\-access\)

The CTLT Summer Institute focuses on the fundamentals of teaching and learning for new instructors and teaching assistants\.

-   The Summer Institute runs from August 21–25, 2023\.
