Nominations are now open for the Canadian Medical Association \(CMA\) Awards for 2025\.

The CMA Awards recognize the dedication, successes and talents of Canadians who are making significant contributions to our health and health care\.

We invite you to consider colleagues who could be eligible for these distinctions\. Contact [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca) to declare your intent to nominate before submitting nominations directly per the [nomination instructions on the CMA website](https://www.cma.ca/cma-awards-and-nominations/how-nominate-someone-cma-award)\. The submission deadline is **November 30, 2024**\.

If you have questions, please contact [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca)\.

[Learn more &amp; nominate](https://www.cma.ca/cma-awards-and-nominations#cma-awards "CMA Awards")
