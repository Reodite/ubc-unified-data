Faculty members at UBC Vancouver are invited to submit a proposal to work with UBC’s [Emerging Media Lab](https://eml.ubc.ca/) to explore how to incorporate technologies such as virtual reality, augmented reality, artificial intelligence and machine learning into your teaching, learning or research projects\.

The Emerging Media Lab is an experimental lab space where faculty, staff, and students from all disciplines collaborate to evolve learning by creating tools and techniques using emerging media technologies\.

Submit a proposal for the summer 2025 cohort by **March 15, 2025**\.

[Learn more &amp; submit a proposal](https://eml.ubc.ca/call-for-proposals/ "Call for Proposals - Emerging Media Lab")
