Register for an upcoming event from Indigenous Initiatives at the UBC Centre for Teaching, Learning, and Technology \(CTLT\)\.

[Indigenous Initiatives at the CTLT](https://indigenousinitiatives.ctlt.ubc.ca/) develops and offers programming, resources and consultations focused on Indigenous engagement in curriculum, pedagogy, classroom climate and professional development\.

## Indigenous Initiatives Evaluation Series

What does it mean to design evaluation in ways that center community accountability, build trust and challenge colonial assumptions? Over three half\-day in\-person workshops in **February 2026**, this new series invites UBC faculty, staff and students to reimagine how we assess success and impact; moving towards more relational, community\-centered approaches to evaluation\.

Please note that there are limited spaces in this workshop series\. Apply by **January 19, 2026**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/indigenous-initiatives-evaluation-series/ "Indigenous Initiatives Evaluation Series | UBC CTLT Events")

## More upcoming events

### November 27 \| [Flourish: Building a Trauma Informed Classroom Community](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/flourish-building-a-trauma-informed-classroom-community/) \(in\-person\)

Join an interactive conversation focused session on supporting, developing and maintaining a safer classroom climate that upholds a dynamic and rigorous learning space while navigating complex classroom conflicts, discussions and situations\.

### December 10 \| [From Reverence to Erasure to Queer Indigenous Joy: Two\-Spirit and Indigiqueer Realities Over Time](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/from-reverence-to-erasure-to-queer-indigenous-joy-two-spirit-and-indigiqueer-realities-over-time/) \(in\-person\)

This session explores the contemporary expressions of Two\-Spirit and Indigiqueer \(2SIQ\) identities, focusing on resilience, connection, and joy\. While histories of erasure have shaped 2SIQ experiences, stories and kinship practices have endured and flourished\. Together, we’ll reflect on how these narratives continue to grow in both community and virtual spaces, highlighting the strength, creativity and surthrivance of queer Indigenous peoples today\.
