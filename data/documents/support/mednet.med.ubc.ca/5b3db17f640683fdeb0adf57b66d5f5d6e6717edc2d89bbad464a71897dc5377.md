As a new UBC community member, we encourage you to get acquainted with the University\.

---

## Online Resources

-   For general information about your employment and services both on and off campus, please refer to the [Before You Arrive](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/faculty-relations/immigration/before-you-arrive/) page\.
-   For information about relocating to UBC and Vancouver, review the [Welcome Guide](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/worklife-relocation/vancouver-profile/welcome-guide-vancouver/) on the UBC Work\-Life Relocation Services Centre website\.
-   **Virtual Orientation:** Explore [UBC HR’s Orientation website](https://www.hr.ubc.ca/orientation/)\.

## Campus Sessions

-   **Vancouver Campus:** The university\-wide orientation provides a great way to connect with others who are new to campus\. The orientations are coordinated by the Department of Human Resources\. Visit the [University\-Wide Orientations](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/orientation/orientations-vancouver/) page to view upcoming staff orientation dates\.
-   **Okanagan Campus:** Contact UBC Human Resources at the Okanagan campus \(250\.807\.8833\) for information on new Faculty and Staff Orientation activities\. Learn more about [Okanagan Orientations](http://www.ubc.ca/okanagan/hr/learning.html)\.  
-   **Office/Department/Unit Orientations:** Each Department or Unit will have its own orientation plan tailored for its own operational needs\. You can go over a department/unit\-based checklist with your [Department/School administrator or HR advisor in the Faculty of Medicine](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/Dept-School-Centre-Admin-Contact-List.pdf) to ensure you have everything you need \(e\.g\., for keys, facility tours, business card orders, site orientation, etc\.\)\.
