Password overload is real — but it’s fixable\.

Join Navigating the Future of Your Digital Identity, an online workshop with the UBC Cybersecurity team on **February 24** to learn password best practices and how password managers can make your digital life easier and more secure\.

You’ll walk away with practical tips you can use right away to reduce password fatigue, avoid resets and better protect your accounts\.

-   **When:** Tuesday, February 24, 2026 from 11 am–12 pm
-   **Where:** Online via Zoom
