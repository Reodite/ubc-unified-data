In partnership with the B\.C\. Ministry of Health, UBC Health is offering seed funding grants to researchers from UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan to address provincial health priorities in collaboration with policymakers\.

Submit a Notice of Intent by **Thursday, October 8, 2026**\. Shortlisted applicants will be invited to submit full applications in late November\.

[More information &amp; how to apply](https://health.ubc.ca/interdisciplinary-health-research/ministry-health-research-seed-grant-program "Ministry of Health Research Seed Grant Program | UBC Health")

## Information session

Prospective applicants can [register for an online information session](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/Zb2o1WIoQFuXPR8uYuiINg) on Thursday, September 24 at 10:30 am\.
