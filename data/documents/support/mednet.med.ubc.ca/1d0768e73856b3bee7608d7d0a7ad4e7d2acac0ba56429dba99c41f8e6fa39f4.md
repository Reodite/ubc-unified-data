[Dr\. Steven Kent](https://pediatrics.med.ubc.ca/about/in-memoriam/ "In Memoriam - Department of Pediatrics") passed away on May 22, 2025\.

A dedicated medical educator and a pioneer in pediatric pulmonology and cystic fibrosis, Dr\. Kent was a current Clinical Assistant Professor in the Department of Pediatrics and a practicing pediatrician with Island Health\.

Dr\. Kent was involved in the launch of B\.C\.’s newborn cystic fibrosis \(CF\) screening program, and was instrumental in establishing Vancouver Island’s first CF clinic at Victoria General Hospital\.

Read [more about Dr\. Kent’s life](https://www.mccallgardens.com/obituaries/dr-steven-norman-kent/ "Dr. Steven Norman Kent Obituary | McCall Gardens Funeral Service") and accomplishments\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Thursday, July 10, 2025](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2025/07/17/steven-kent/ "Steven Kent Flag Lowering | UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
