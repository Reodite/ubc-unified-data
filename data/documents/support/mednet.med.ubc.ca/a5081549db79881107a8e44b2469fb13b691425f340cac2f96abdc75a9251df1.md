The Teaching Tracking and Payment System \(TTPS\) is a province\-wide system, which the Faculty of Medicine uses to document the teaching contributions of UBC tenure stream faculty, clinical faculty and adjunct professors that teach in the MD Undergraduate Program \(MDUP\) and/or Postgraduate Medical Program \(PGME\)\. The TTPS streamlines administrative processes for managing teaching activities and provides a central place for recording teaching activities to facilitate reporting for the Faculty of Medicine to support the operational, budgetary and strategic priorities for the MDUP and PGME\. 

Currently, only MDUP teaching activities will be recorded in the TTPS for UBC tenure stream faculty\. PGME teaching activities will be onboarded in the future\. 

[Login to TTPS](http://ttps.med.ubc.ca/)

The TTPS provides a detailed quarterly record of teaching activities for UBC tenure stream faculty which will: 

-   Enable UBC appointed tenure stream faculty to review teaching activities and identify any inaccuracies; and 

-   Provide a record of teaching which can be used to support annual reports\. 

## Review your Record of Teaching

For each fiscal quarter \(Apr\-Jun, Jul\-Sept, Oct\-Dec &amp; Jan\-Mar\) you will receive an email notification that your draft record of teaching for the previous quarter is ready to review\. You can review the draft statement and report any discrepancies **within five business days**\. 

For an overview of the tenure stream faculty tracking cycle, please review our [Quarterly Processing Timelines](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/TTPS-Timelines-for-TSF-2026-2027.pdf)\. 

## How to Access your Record of Teaching

-   Ensure you have a [UBC Campus\-Wide Login \(CWL\) account](https://www.myaccount.ubc.ca/myAccount/)\. 

-   Click on login button above and sign in using your CWL credentials\.  

-   View your record of teaching on the home screen or click “draft” to view a PDF version\. 

-   Review the [Quick Guide](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/TTPS-Quick-Guide-for-Tenure-Stream-Faculty.pdf) for step\-by\-step instructions\.  

## Reporting Discrepancies on your Record of Teaching

-   If you have a question about your record of teaching or believe the information presented is inaccurate, you can click on the teaching activity and select [submit an inquiry](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/TTPS-Quick-Guide-for-Tenure-Stream-Faculty.pdf)\.   

-   If a teaching activity delivered for the MDUP is missing from your record of teaching, please contact your department immediately to notify them to correct it\. 

## Need Help?

If you need system support or have further questions, please reach out to your department\.
