Find forms and checklists for processing tenure steam appointments\.

---

​​The following forms and checklists are specific to the Faculty of Medicine and are used to initially appoint and  reappoint full\-time tenure track and grant tenure track faculty members\. 

## Checklists

-   [Checklist – Full\-Time Faculty – New Appointment](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/01/Checklist-Full-Time-Faculty-New-Appointment-July-2024-.pdf) \(Rev July 2024\): To process a new appointment for the ranks of tenure stream faculty\.
    -   **NOTE:** For new appointments at the rank of at the rank of Associate Professor of Teaching with Tenure, Professor of Teaching, Associate Professor or Professor, in addition to following the checklist above, please submit a promotion &amp; tenure package by following the [P&amp;T checklist](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/hr/managing-faculty/promotion-tenure/) and submit them via OneDrive\.
-   [Full\-Time Faculty – Reappointm](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/managing-faculty/reappointments/) [ent](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/managing-faculty/reappointments/): To process a reappointment through the Dean’s Office &amp; Faculty Relations\.
