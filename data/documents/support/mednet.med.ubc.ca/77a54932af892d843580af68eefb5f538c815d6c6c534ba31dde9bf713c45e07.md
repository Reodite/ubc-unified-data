Join UBC Botanical Garden on **Thursday, July 17** for BranchingOUT, an evening event celebrating the 2SLGBTQIA\+ community in the lead\-up to Vancouver Pride Festival\.

Participants will learn from Garden staff about the collection, and explore the Garden from new heights on the Greenheart TreeWalk\. Light snacks and drinks will be provided\.

All donations from the event will support [CampOUT\!](https://campout.ubc.ca/), a transformative summer camp for queer, trans and Two\-Spirit youth across B\.C\. and the Yukon\.

-   **Date:** Thursday, July 17, 2025 from 6 to 9 pm
-   **Location:** UBC Botanical Garden, 6804 SW Marine Dr\., Vancouver B\.C\.

[Learn more &amp; purchase tickets](https://botanicalgarden.ubc.ca/branching-out-ubc-botanical-gardens-signature-pride-celebration/ "BranchingOUT – UBC Botanical Garden’s Signature Pride Celebration")
