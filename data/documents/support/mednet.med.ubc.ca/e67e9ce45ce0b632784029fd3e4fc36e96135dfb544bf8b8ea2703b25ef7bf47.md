Nominations are now open for the 2027 [Ho\-Am Prize](https://www.hoamfoundation.org/eng/foundation/intro.asp)\.

The Ho\-Am Prize is presented each year to people of Korean heritage who have contributed to academics, the arts and social development, or who have furthered the welfare of humanity through distinguished accomplishments in their respective professional fields\. The award category for medicine is for those who have made outstanding achievements in the areas of medicine and pharmacy\.

We invite you to consider colleagues who could be eligible for this distinction\. Contact [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca) to declare your intent to nominate and obtain a nomination form\.

Internal submission deadline for the full nomination package is **Friday, October 2, 2025**\. The Ho\-Am Prize submission deadline is October 31, 2025\.

If you have questions, please contact [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca)\.
