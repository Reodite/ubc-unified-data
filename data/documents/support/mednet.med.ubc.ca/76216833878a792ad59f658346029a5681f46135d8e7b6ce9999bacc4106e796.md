Learn more about the Faculty’s Finance Team\.

The structure consists of two portfolios:

-   The **Academic Finance** team provides specialized finance services to Faculty of Medicine Departments, Schools, Centres, and Institutes\.
-   The **Education and Administrative Finance** team provides specialized finance services for Education, the Office of the Dean, and other central faculty services, as well as supporting research and new program development\.

Additionally, there are small teams devoted to financial planning and analysis for the Faculty\.
