[Dr\. Annette Stark](https://vancouversunandprovince.remembering.ca/obituary/annette-stark-1083335454 "Annette Stark Obituary | Vancouver Sun and Province") passed away March 9, 2020\.

A Clinical Associate Professor Emerita, Dr\. Stark taught in the former Department of Health Care and Epidemiology \(now the School of Population and Public Health\)\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Friday, April 14, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/04/21/annette-stark/ "Annette Stark | Flag Lowering - UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
