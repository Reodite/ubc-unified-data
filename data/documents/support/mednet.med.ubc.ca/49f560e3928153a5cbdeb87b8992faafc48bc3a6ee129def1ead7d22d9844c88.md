With the recent developments regarding [Canada’s response to U\.S\. tariffs on Canadian goods](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/programs/international-trade-finance-policy/canadas-response-us-tariffs.html), UBC Finance recognizes that these changes may impact your procurement\. The procurement team is closely monitoring the situation and doing their best to minimize disruptions\.

UBC Finance encourage you to consider sourcing from Canadian and Indigenous suppliers, and have provided resources and information to support you with doing so through the UBC Finance portal\. If you have questions about your current purchase with a U\.S\. supplier, please contact the designated Buyer or Procurement Officer who is facilitating your order\.

[Learn more \(CWL required\)](https://finance.share.ubc.ca/buy-pay/SitePages/U.S.-tariff-updates-for-imports-into-Canada.aspx)
