## Mapping AI

To help our community connect, collaborate, and share insights, we are creating a central registry for AI projects, tools, and research in our Faculty\. 

## **Where We Are Right Now**

We are mapping the landscape by creating a detailed catalog of initiatives across the Faculty\. As we build out this database, we are focusing on:

-   Identifying active AI research projects and clinical pilots\.
-   Documenting AI tools that educators and administrator are currently tested or using\.
-   Setting up secure access protocols\. This will help our community share project data safely and appropriately\.

## **Help us Map the Landscape**

Are you or your team working on an AI\-driven project, tool, or research initiative? Even if it is in the early stages, we want to know about it\.
