# **Dates &amp; Deadlines**

## **2023 Annual Honorary Reappointment Process**

A reminder that the deadline to return your School/Department’s completed Honorary Reappointment spreadsheet is **Wednesday, April 26\[th\], 2023**\. Faculty Relations has confirmed that a separate Termination BP is not required for anyone for which there is **“N”** in Column A \(Terminate position as of June 30, 2023\)\.

Please upload the completed spreadsheet to the *2023 HON Reappointment* folder we have shared with you and send an email to [Fern Moran](mailto:fern.moran@ubc.ca) to let us know that it has been completed\. Reappointment letters/communications should be produced and provided to those individuals being reappointed\. A copy does not need to be provided to our office\. A reminder that Board Notices are not issued\.

The original announcement was sent out March 22, 2023, and was also attached to the email version of this newsletter, sent out April 3, 2023\.

## **Appointment Processing in Preparation for the July 1\[st\] Salary Increase**

Normally appointment transactions are entered in Workday no more than 2 pay periods in advance of the effective date\. However, at this time of the year there are a few transactions that should be entered further in advance:

-   **Resignations, Retirement &amp; Terminations:**  Please ensure that retirements and resignations for faculty members effective June 30, 2023 or earlier have been entered in Workday **before March 31, 2023**\.  Any June 30 retirements or resignations that you become aware of after that date should be entered in Workday right away\. Also, ensure that any appointments that are not to be renewed are also terminated as early as possible\.
-   **Reappointments**:  All July 1 reappointments must be initiated and approved by Department &amp; Dean’s Office administrators **by early May** to provide enough time for FR/HR to review and approve before the Increase Spreadsheets are produced\. Reappointments with an effective date of July 2\[nd\] or later should wait until after the July 1 faculty salary increases have been processed\.
-   **Retirement Options &amp; Reduced Workloads:** Please hold off on processing Retirement Options and Reduced Workloads with an effective date of July 1 or later until after the July 1 salary increases have been loaded to Workday\.
-   **Note:** In due course we will be providing a list of BPs later on in the process that can and cannot be processed while we are in the midst of the increase process\.

As a reminder, the Department/ Schools should submit their merit and OAP by **April 14, 2023** and PSA requests by **April 21, 2023**\. The FoM Guide and the presentation on March 10, 2023 from the Faculty HR Managers regarding Best Practices in Merit/OAP/PSA has been posted here: [https://mednet\.med\.ubc\.ca/hr/managing\-faculty/workshops/\.](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/managing-faculty/workshops/)

## **Check Emeritus Faculty &amp; Librarian on Workday**

To prepare for the launch of the new job code/profile for Post Retirement Appointments, please review emeriti list in your Department/School by running the report **Find Emeriti as List – Distributed** and check the following:

-   **Does the report capture all your living emeriti**\. Check against any internal departmental list or against the emeriti list found in either the [UBCV Calendar](https://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?page=appendix2) or the [UBCO Calendar](https://www.calendar.ubc.ca/okanagan/index.cfm?go=appendix2)\.  If an emeritus who is living is missing, please email [Fern Moran](mailto:fern.moran@ubc.ca)\.
-   **Are all emeriti who have passed away noted as Deceased\.** For those emeriti recorded in Workday and who have passed away, their Empl Status on the report should be “Deceased”\.  If it is not, please process a termination on their record selecting “Involuntary&gt;Death” as the reason\. You will need to attach an obituary or deceased memo to enter the date of death in order to process the termination\. You may also want to review the age of the individuals as there are many emeriti at age 95 and above\.
-   **Check Employee Type\.** All emeriti without a Post Retirement Appointment \(i\.e\. their position notes emeritus status only\) must have an Employee Type of “Unpaid \(Ongoing\)”\.  Emeriti with an active appointment will have an Employee Type of either “Unpaid \(Term\)” or “Term \(Fixed Term\)”\.  If the Employee Type is not correct, please update Workday\.
