Join UBC’s [Advanced Research Computing \(ARC\)](https://arc.ubc.ca/) for a public dialogue event, “Let’s Talk About Your Data: A Discussion of Public Health Data” on **Wednesday, November 29**\.

Hosted by UBC Connects at Robson Square, join the discussion of sharing public health data and technological advances that assure security with health professionals, researchers, and patient representatives presenting their community health research endeavours\.

-   **When:** Wednesday, November 29, 2023 from 6–9 pm
-   **Where:** UBC Robson Square

[Learn more &amp; register](https://events.ubc.ca/event/lets-talk-about-your-data/ " Let’s Talk About Your Data: A Discussion of Public Health Data | UBC Events")
