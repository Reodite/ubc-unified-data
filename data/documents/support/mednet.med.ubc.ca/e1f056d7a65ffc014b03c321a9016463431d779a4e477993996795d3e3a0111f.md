An invitation from the Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity &amp; Inclusion

---

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/redi-virtual-symposium-300x300.png)

The Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion \(REDI\) invites you to its first virtual symposium: **“Race Ideology: Historical Perspectives, Current Realities and Re\-imagining the Future\.”**

**When:** Wednesday, May 25, 2022

**Time:** 8:30 am – 1:00 pm

**What will I learn?**

-   How racist ideology led to the categorization of people into “races”\. 
-   How centuries of medical knowledge, health care, research and health professions education have continued to perpetuate systemic racism\.
-   Who benefits from racism and discrimination and how racist systems and structures are sustained\.
-   How to engage with anti\-racism strategies and pedagogies aimed to dismantle racist structures and re\-engage in a more equitable future\.

**Speakers:**

-   **Shane Pointe –** ***Te’ta\-in***  
    Knowledge Keeper, Musqueam Nation 
-   **Dr\. Amy Tan**   
    Clinical Associate Professor &amp; Anti\-Racism Support Program Faculty Mentor, Department of Family Practice, UBC Faculty of Medicine 
-   **Dr\. Danièle Behn Smith**   
    Deputy Provincial Health Officer, Indigenous Health, Ministry of Health 
-   **Dr\. Handel Wright**  
    Professor, Department of Education Studies, UBC Faculty of Education,  
    &amp; Senior Advisor to the UBC President on Anti\-Racism and Inclusive Excellence 
-   **Dr\. Saroo Sharda**  
    Assistant Clinical Professor, Department of Anesthesia, McMaster University, &amp; Medical Advisor &amp; Diversity, Equity, Inclusion Lead, College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario

Information on registrations and symposium content will be made available in the upcoming weeks\.
