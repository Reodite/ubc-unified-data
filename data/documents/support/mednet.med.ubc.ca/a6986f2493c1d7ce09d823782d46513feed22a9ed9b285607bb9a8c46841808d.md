[Dr\. Peter Grantham](https://www.dignitymemorial.com/en-ca/obituaries/north-vancouver-bc/peter-grantham-12799217 "Peter Grantham Obituary") passed away on March 20, 2026\.

A passionate advocate for medical education, Dr\. Grantham was a Professor Emeritus in the Department of Family Practice, where he previously served as the first Department Head\. He was also a Faculty of Medicine alumnus\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Grantham from the Department of Family Practice\.](https://familypractice.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-peter-r-grantham-1932-2026/)

The B\.C\., Musqueam and UBC [flags will be lowered on Thursday, April 9, 2026](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2026/04/peter-grantham/ "Peter Grantham Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies &amp; Events") in remembrance\.
