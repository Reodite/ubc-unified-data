Procedures and timelines for the New Frontiers in Research Fund \(NFRF\) – International competition\. Please be aware that there are **3 stages** to this application: Notice of Intent, Letter of Intent, and Application\.

---

## Notice of Intent \(NOI\)

| ​**NOI Important Dates** |  |
| --- | --- |
| **March 2, 2026 \| 12:00pm** | **Applicant submits application on Convergence** |
| **March 3, 2026 \| 4:30pm** | Sponsor deadline |

### Procedure

1.  Signatures are not required for the NOI stage\.
2.  Your NOI application must be submitted via Convergence by **12:00pm on March 2, 2026**\. ORS will need to provide online approval by 4:30pm on March 3, 2026\.

---

## Letter of Intent \(LOI\)

| **LOI Important Dates** |  |
| --- | --- |
| Check with your Department/School | Application package sent to Department Head/School Director office for review &amp; approval\.See below for application package details\. |
| **June 1, 2026** | Application package sent to Dean’s Office or affiliated hospital research office via email for review &amp; approval \(**FoM Internal Deadline**\)\. |
| June 3, 2026 | Application package approved by Faculty and sent to ORS \(**UBC internal deadline**\) |
| **June 8, 2026 \| 12:00pm** | Applicant submits application on NFRF research portal \(**Applicant’s e\-submission deadline**\) |
| June 9, 2026 \| 4:30pm | ORS approves application on research portal \(**NFRF LOI deadline**\) |

### Procedure

1.  Your application package must be submitted to the Dean’s Office or affiliated hospital research office by **Monday, June 1, 2026** for Faculty approval\. Please email it to the Faculty [signature contact for your site](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/signature-process/#signaturecontacts)\. Note that Lily Nizami \([fom\.grantapprovals@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.grantapprovals@ubc.ca)\) is the contact for Faculty approval for those located at the UBC Point Grey campus\. See below for a complete list of the required items\.
2.  After Faculty approval, your application package will be sent to ORS\.
3.  Your application must be submitted via the NFRF research portal no later than **12:00pm on Monday, June 8, 2026**\.
4.  ORS will electronically approve your application via the research portal for submission to NFRF\.

Please remember that you can continue working on your research proposal up until you submit on the research portal on June 8, even after you have submitted your application package for Faculty approval\.

### Required Documents

Required Documents to be submitted to the FoM for the New Frontiers in Research Fund grant competition:

1.  Completed [Research Project Information Form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/required-documents/) including the following signatures:
    1.  the Principal Investigator
    2.  Department Head or School Director
    3.  Research Centre Director \(if using research space within a Senate\-Approved Research Centre, identified with an asterisk on the [Academic &amp; Research Units](http://med.ubc.ca/about/departments-schools-centres/) page\)
2.  Completed and signed confirmation of eligibility form for all external co\-PIs and co\-applicants\. If co\-PIs and co\-applicants are within UBC, they will need to determine with their Department/Faculty whether they need to review and sign the application\.
3.  Title page \(page 1\), including list of participants
4.  Participants’ eligibility profile pages
5.  Project description page

## Application \(by invitation only\)

| **​NOI Important Dates** |  |
| --- | --- |
| Check with your Department/School | Application package sent to Department Head/School Director office for review &amp; approval\. **See below for application package details\.** |
| **October 26, 2026** | Application package sent to Dean’s Office or affiliated hospital research office via email for review &amp; approval \(**FoM Internal Deadline**\)\. |
| October 28, 2026 | Application package sent to ORS for approval \(**UBC Internal Deadline**\) |
| **November 2, 2026 \| 12:00pm** | Applicant submits application on Convergence |
| November 3, 2026 \| 4:30pm | ORS approves application on Convergence \(**NFRF Application Deadline**\) |

### Procedure

1.  Your application package must be submitted to the Dean’s Office or affiliated hospital research office by **Monday, October 26, 2026** for Faculty approval\. Please email it to the Faculty [signature contact for your site](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/signature-process/#signaturecontacts)\. Note that Lily Nizami \([fom\.grantapprovals@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.grantapprovals@ubc.ca)\) is the contact for Faculty approval for those located at the UBC Point Grey campus\. See below for a complete list of the required items\.
2.  After Faculty approval, your application package will be sent to ORS\.
3.  Your application must be submitted via the NFRF research portal no later than **12:00pm on Monday, November 2, 2026**\.
4.  ORS will electronically approve your application via the research portal for submission to NFRF\.

Please remember that you can continue working on your research proposal up until you submit on the research portal on November 2, even after you have submitted your application package for Faculty approval\.

### Required Documents

Required Documents to be submitted to the FoM for the New Frontiers in Research Fund grant competition:

1.  Completed [Research Project Information Form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/required-documents/) including the following signatures:
    1.  the Principal Investigator
    2.  Department Head or School Director
    3.  Research Centre Director \(if using research space within a Senate\-Approved Research Centre, identified with an asterisk on the [Academic &amp; Research Units](http://med.ubc.ca/about/departments-schools-centres/) page\)
2.  Completed and signed confirmation of eligibility form for all external co\-PIs and co\-applicants\. If co\-PIs and co\-applicants are within UBC, they will need to determine with their Department/Faculty whether they need to review and sign the application\.
3.  Title page \(page 1\), including list of participants
4.  Participants’ eligibility profile pages
5.  Project description page
6.  Budget and budget justification pages \(note: budget should include 25% ICR as per NFRF guidelines\)

Required Documents to be submitted to the FoM for the New Frontiers in Research Fund grant competition:

1.  Please submit your online application via Convergence by 12pm one working day before the sponsor deadline\.
