In partnership with the B\.C\. Ministry of Health, UBC Health is offering up to $50,000 in seed funding to researchers from UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan to address priority research questions identified by the ministry\.

Submitt a Notice of Intent by **Wednesday, October 8, 2025**\. Shortlisted applicants will be invited to submit full applications in November\.

[More information &amp; how to apply](https://health.ubc.ca/interdisciplinary-health-research/ministry-health-research-seed-grant-program "Ministry of Health Research Seed Grant Program | UBC Health")

## Information session

Prospective applicants, [register for an online information session](https://health.ubc.ca/events) and Q&amp;A on Thursday, September 25\.
