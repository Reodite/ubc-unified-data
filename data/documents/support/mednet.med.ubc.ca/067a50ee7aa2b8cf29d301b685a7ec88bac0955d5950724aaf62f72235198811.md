A physician at Vancouver General Hospital and medical director for the BC Women’s Hospital Sexual Assault Service, Dr\. Tracy Pickett was a clinical professor in the Department of Emergency Medicine\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Pickett](https://emergency.med.ubc.ca/2023/09/29/remembering-dr-tracy-pickett/) from the Department of Emergency Medicine\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags was lowered on Friday, November 03, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/11/09/tracy-pickett/ "Tracy Pickett Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
