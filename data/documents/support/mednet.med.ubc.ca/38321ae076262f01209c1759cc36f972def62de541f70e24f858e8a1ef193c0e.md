[Dr\. Richard Wall](https://apt.med.ubc.ca/news/dr-richard-wall-passed-away-on-august-19th-2023/ "Dr. Richard Wall passed away on August 19th, 2023 | Department of Anesthesiology, Pharmacology &amp; Therapeutics") passed away on August 19, 2023\.

A Professor Emeritus, Dr\. Wall served in the Department of Anesthesiology, Pharmacology and Therapeutics\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Friday, September 22, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/09/28/richard-wall/ "Richard Wall Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
