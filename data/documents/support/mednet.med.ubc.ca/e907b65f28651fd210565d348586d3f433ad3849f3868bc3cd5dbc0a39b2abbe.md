Faculty, staff and students in Vancouver and the Okanagan are invited to apply for UBC’s newly forming advisory committee on Asian Canadian racial equity and inclusion\.

This advisory committee will advise on a forthcoming project that builds on UBC’s sustained efforts to address anti\-Asian racism, including the 2021 National Forum on Anti\-Asian Racism\. The committee will meet virtually once a month\.

Apply online by **Friday, April 10**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://equity.ubc.ca/news-and-stories/call-for-applications-advisory-committee-on-asian-canadian-racial-equity-inclusion-april10/ "Call for applications: Advisory committee on Asian Canadian racial equity &amp; inclusion")
