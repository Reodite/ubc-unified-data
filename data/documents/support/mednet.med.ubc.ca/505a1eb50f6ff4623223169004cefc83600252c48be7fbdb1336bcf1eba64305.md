*Updated March 11, 2026:*  The nomination deadline for all prizes has been extended to **Tuesday, March 31, 2026**\.

The University of British Columbia is currently seeking nominations for the following three research prizes\.

The prizes, each with a value of **$50,000**, are awarded to experts who demonstrate research excellence and innovation in the areas of **cancer**, **brain disorders** and **heart disorders**\.

*New for 2026:* All nominations must include a 250\-word lay summary as part of the nomination package\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://www.med.ubc.ca/research/prizes/ "National Prizes - UBC Faculty of Medicine")

---

## About the prizes

### Dr\. Chew Wei Memorial Prize in Cancer Research

The Dr\. Chew Wei MBBS \[HK\] FRCOG \[ENG\] Memorial Prize in Cancer Research is awarded annually to a Canadian physician or scientist who makes outstanding contributions to the treatment, amelioration or development of therapies for cancer\.

Information and online nomination form: [med\.ubc\.ca/chewwei](http://med.ubc.ca/chewwei "Dr. Chew Wei Memorial Prize in Cancer Research - UBC Faculty of Medicine")

### Margolese National Brain Disorders Prize \(external\)

The Margolese National Brain Disorders Prize is awarded annually to a Canadian physician or scientist who makes outstanding contributions to the treatment, amelioration or cure of brain disorders\.

*Based on the prize guidelines, nominations for the 2026 competition must be for individuals **external** to UBC\.*

Information and online nomination form: [med\.ubc\.ca/margolese\-brain](http://med.ubc.ca/margolese-brain "Margolese National Brain Disorders Prize - UBC Faculty of Medicine")

### Margolese National Heart Disorders Prize \(external\)

The Margolese National Heart Disorders Prize is awarded annually to a Canadian physician or scientist who makes outstanding contributions to the treatment, amelioration or cure of heart disorders\.

*Based on the prize guidelines, nominations for the 2026 competition must be for individuals *****external***** to UBC\.*

Information and online nomination form: [med\.ubc\.ca/margolese\-heart](http://med.ubc.ca/margolese-heart "Margolese National Heart Disorders Prize - UBC Faculty of Medicine")
