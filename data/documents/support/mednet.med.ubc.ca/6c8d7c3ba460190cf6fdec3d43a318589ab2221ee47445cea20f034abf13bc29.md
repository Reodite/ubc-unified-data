Find information and resources to support the reviewing and processing of clinical faculty reappointments\.

---

Recommendations and decisions regarding reappointment are based on a review of the Clinical Faculty’s contributions to the FoM and UBC\. Clinical Faculty are expected to maintain an up\-to\-date record of their academic activities using the [UBC Faculty of Medicine Clinical Faculty CV](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#templates)\. The Head/Director \(or delegate\) is responsible for providing clinical faculty with regular feedback, including eligibility, criteria, and expectations for reappointment\. 

A review for clinical faculty reappointment is **mandatory** and must be completed well in advance of the appointment end date \(normally June 30\)\. It is recommended that reappointment reviews be completed **at least two months** before the end of the term, with **notice of non\-renewal provided no later than one month prior\.**

## **Reappointment Requirements** 

Requirements for reappointment are provided to clinical faculty on the [Reappointment and Promotions](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/reappointment-promotion/) page\.  

## **Reappointment Review Process** 

1\. **Pull reports** to identify appointment end dates and review teaching activity:

-   Workday report *Employees with Upcoming Term End Date – Distributed* identifies upcoming end dates\.
-   TTPS/PowerBI report *TTPS Teaching Contributions by Service Provider \(All Active Clinical Faculty\)* provides teaching contributions in the undergrad and postgrad programs by hours for teaching without patient care \(TwoPC\) and by sessions for teaching with patient care \(TwPC\) and is used in conjunction with Department/School records, where applicable, to verify teaching contributions\. [Refer to TTPS Power BI Report Visual Guide for information on how to export into excel\.](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#information-guides)

2\. **Confirm eligibility** for clinical faculty members governed by the Health Professions Act \(HPA\) by reviewing evidence of good standing with the respective provincial/territorial governing body \(e\.g\., CPSBC license\)\. For clinical faculty members whose health profession is not governed by the HPA, confirm employment and good standing with both their BC health authority \(or similar public agency\) employer and, if applicable, professional organizations applicable to the profession\.

If the license has any restrictions, [please contact the Faculty HR team](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/contacts/) to discuss\.

3\. **Confirm teaching activities** as recorded in TTPS/PowerBI report and Department/School teaching records over a two\-year period \(typically expected to be 50 hours as per the [Clinical Faculty Policy](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\)\. 

4\. **Confirm Clinical Faculty intent** by contacting those whose appointments are ending to request confirmation of their intent to be reappointed, and provide a clear deadline for their response\. If the clinical faculty member is at a distributed site, please connect and coordinate with the administrator at the site\. Three variations \(choose based on context/unit discretion\) are included in *Section 1: Confirmation of Clinical Faculty Member’s Intent for Reappointment* within the [Reappointment Confirmation and Notification templates](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#templates) for this purpose\.

-   If there is insufficient evidence of academic contributions, request an updated CV outlining recent academic activities \(*Section \#1, Option C template*\) 
-   If no response is received, at least two follow\-ups are strongly recommended \(*Section \# 2 template*\) 

5\. **Review against reappointment criteria\.** The Head/Director \(or delegate\) reviews whether the reappointment criteria are met in accordance with the [Clinical Faculty Policy](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\. Reappointment at any rank may be recommended by the Head/ Director to the Dean directly without review by the CARP Committee as long as they meet the [reappointment criteria outlined in Appendix A](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\.

6\. **If applicable, CARP Review\.** If considering non\-reappointment, the Head/Director consults with the unit’s CARP Committee in a review of the case\. See Committee Review below for escalation and discrepancy processes and Establishing a CARP Committee for further guidance\. The [Faculty HR team](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/contacts/) can consult on modifications to templates to use to meet documentation requirements in these cases\.   

7\. **Notify Clinical Faculty** by sending an official notification informing the clinical faculty member of the outcome with the [Reappointment Confirmation Notification templates](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#templates): 

-   If reappointed, confirm new term end date and terms of appointment \(*Section \#3, Options A and B*\) 
-   If CARP recommends non\-renewal, notify the clinical faculty member that they will not be reappointed \(*Section \# 3, Option C\)*  

8\. **Submit batch reappointment spreadsheet \(or process the BP in WD if the end date is not June 30\)** by entering all reappointments, short\-term/provisional extensions and terminations into the spreadsheet provided by Faculty HR\. Return completed spreadsheet to Faculty HR **via OneDrive** by the deadline\. This spreadsheet constitutes the Head/Director’s recommendation\.

Consult with the [Faculty HR team](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/contacts/) for any reappointments that do not appear on the spreadsheet\.

This process applies to appointments with an end date of **June 30**\. Reappointments with a different end date must be **processed manually in Workday**\. 

The Dean will review the Head/Director’s recommendation and make a final decision\. 

### **Committee Review**

If non\-reappointment is being considered, the CARP Committee must be consulted in the review and recommendation process\. In such cases, the Head/ Director informs the Clinical Faculty of the concerns in writing and provides an opportunity for the member to respond to those concerns for consideration by the CARP Committee before proceeding to a vote and recommendation\.  

A one\-year appointment extension may be granted by the Head/ Director to provide the individual with an opportunity to meet [the criteria for reappointment](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\.  

In cases where reappointment criteria are not met, where licensing restrictions affect teaching activities, or where there are concerns related to professional standing or regulatory matters, the file must be referred to the CARP Committee for formal review\.  

If gaps or concerns arise regarding teaching quality, quantity, or documentation, the Head/Director should provide feedback and assist the Clinical Faculty member in developing an improvement plan, which may include identifying teaching opportunities, mentoring, training, or adjustments to their teaching approach\.  

When there is a discrepancy between the CARP Committee and the Head/ Director on the recommendation for reappointment, the reappointment file moves to the [FCARP Committee](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/contacts/#committee) to review with the final decision by the Dean\. 

If reappointment is not recommended by the CARP Committee and the Head/ Director, a rationale must be provided in writing to the Clinical Faculty member\. The individual may request a review of the recommendation against reappointment by the [FCARP Committee](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/contacts/#committee)\. If the reappointment is under review at the FCARP Committee, the individual’s appointment will be maintained until there is a final decision by the Dean\.
