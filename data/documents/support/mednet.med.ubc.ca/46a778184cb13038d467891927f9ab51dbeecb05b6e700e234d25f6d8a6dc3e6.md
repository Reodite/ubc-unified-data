As a new staff member, we realize the importance of helping you to feel welcomed, connected, equipped and settled as you begin your role within the Faculty of Medicine and with the University\. In support of this, the Orientation area of our site has been developed with you in mind\.

---

Please take a moment to visit the following, keeping in mind that the site is continually growing with new information\. Don’t be afraid to ask questions\. We’re here to help\. 

## Getting Started 

-   [Your first days at UBC](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/your-first-days-ubc)
-   Review the [“Mandatory Training for all UBC Workers”](https://srs.ubc.ca/training-and-general-education-courses/mandatory-training-for-all-ubc-workers/)  with your manager and make arrangements to complete all necessary courses\.
-   Review the [UBC\-IT Information Security &amp; Usage Policies](https://it.ubc.ca/services/cybersecurity-services/ubc-information-security-office/security-policies)\.
-   Activate your [Campus\-Wide Login \(CWL\) ID](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl) to access UBC’s online applications including [Workday](https://wd10.myworkday.com/ubc/login.htmld) and [FASMail](https://mail.ubc.ca/)\. An email was sent to you with a link for the activation\. You must know your UBC employee number and CWL PIN code, both of which should have been sent to you\. If you don’t know or have not received them, please contact your unit HR administrator\.
-   After your CWL ID is activated, please contact your IT support group to have your [UBC FASMail](http://it.ubc.ca/services/email-voice-internet/ubc-faculty-staff-email-fasmail) \(e\-mail\) account created\. Email signatures must [follow university guidelines](https://brand.ubc.ca/guidelines/downloads/)\.  

## Around the office

### Useful Information 

MedNet is your “one\-stop shop” for faculty information, forms, procedures, contacts, and how\-to’s\. Some of the more common services &amp; resources are listed below\. Many more can be found using the broad heading or the search engine at the top of this page\. Note that “Team Sites” are password\-protected\. Please contact your supervisor if you require access\.

-   Please be aware of the [Faculty of Medicine’s policies &amp; guidelines](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/office-of-the-dean/governance/policies-guidelines/)
-   Please also be aware of [UBC’s policies &amp; guidelines](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/board-of-governors-policies-procedures-rules-and-guidelines/policies/)\. 
-   Please discuss with your supervisor about access to unit\-specific resources and other university systems\.
-   Remember to [enroll in payroll, benefits, and pension packages](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/enrolment-enrolment-changes) for new employees\. Follow the instructions stated on the page\.
-   Your office administrator can help with building access &amp; security\-related requests\. Building information can be [found here](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/facilities/building-information/)\.
-   Please [see here](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/communications/) for official FoM document templates, logos, &amp; other communications materials\.
-   Please discuss with your supervisor about additional purchases such as business cards, cell phones, and extra equipment\.
-   Please contact the office coordinator, administrator, or department assistant about ordering office supplies and mail services\.
-   Please contact the office coordinator or administrator about facilties &amp; maintenance requests\.
-   Complete Room Booking &amp; Videoconferencing information and instructions can be [found here](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/room-booking/)\.

## Dean’s Office Staff Forms: Onboarding and Departing 

Supervisors must complete the onboarding form for all units under the Dean’s Office\.   
A departing form is required for any staff member leaving the Dean’s Office, but remaining at UBC\.   
Include special requests in the notes sections e\.g\. add to specific listservs, shared drives, etc\. \(or remove from\)\.
