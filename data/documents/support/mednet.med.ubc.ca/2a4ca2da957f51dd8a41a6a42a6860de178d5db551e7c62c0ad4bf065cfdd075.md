The [UBC University Killam Professors](https://prizes.research.ubc.ca/awards/university-killam-professor) \(UKP\) designation recognizes exceptional members of faculty who are extraordinary teachers and researchers, who are leaders in their academic fields and who have received national and international recognition\.

The Faculty of Medicine is launching an internal selection process to nominate up to two candidates to be considered for the UKP competition\.  Submit nomination packages for internal review by **June 27, 2024**\.

[Learn more &amp; nominate](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/research/internal-funding-opportunities/university-killam-professors/ "University Killam Professors - MedNet")
