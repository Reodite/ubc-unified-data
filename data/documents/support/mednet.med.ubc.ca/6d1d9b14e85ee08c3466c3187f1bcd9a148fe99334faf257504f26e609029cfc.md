Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day is back this year and will be held in\-person at UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan on **May 5**\.

Whether you want to participate as an office, department, group of colleagues or an individual, there are a variety of challenges and activities to choose from\. Virtual activities will also be available from May 2 to 5\.

Learn more &amp; register:

-   [UBC Vancouver Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/vancouver/)
-   [UBC Okanagan Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day](https://hr.ok.ubc.ca/health-wellbeing-2/sportsday/)
