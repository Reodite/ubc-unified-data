As we approach the holidays, explore tips for reducing energy use, keeping workspaces secure and staying safe on campus\.

## Safety &amp; security tips from UBC Campus Security

Before the holidays, please take a moment to help keep our campus secure and yourself safe during the holiday closure\. UBC Security share tips for keeping your office space secure, and for staying safe if you’re working late or alone\.

[Learn more](https://security.ubc.ca/2025/12/05/holiday-safety-security-tips-ubc-campus-security/ "Holiday safety &amp; security tips from UBC Campus Security")

## Help save energy as part of UBC’s Seasonal Shutdown

This holiday season, help save energy by completing UBC’s Seasonal Shutdown Checklist\. By taking simple actions like switching off electronics, unplugging appliances, and shutting fume hood sashes, you can help reduce UBC’s energy footprint\.
