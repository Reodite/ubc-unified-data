Need to get to somewhere on UBC Vancouver campus but have a temporary or permanent condition that impacts your mobility?

The UBC Accessibility Shuttle is a free, shared ride service available for all students, staff, faculty, residents, and visitors on UBC Vancouver campus who have temporary or permanent conditions that impact mobility\.

The Accessibility Shuttle is run by the [UBC Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility) in collaboration with Campus and Community Planning, and is operated by UBC student staff\. The shuttle travels along a designated route, making stops within the central pedestrian areas of campus that can otherwise only be accessed by foot\.

[Learn more &amp; book](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility/ubc-accessibility-shuttle "UBC Accessibility Shuttle")
