A message from Wyeth Wasserman, Vice Dean, Research and Craig Mitton, Acting Director, School of Population &amp; Public Health\.

---

We are pleased to announce that Dr\. Daniel Steel has been appointed interim Director, W\. Maurice Young Centre for Applied Ethics \(CAE\), effective to December 31, 2027\.

Dr\. Steel is an associate professor in the School of Population &amp; Public Health and an associate member in the Department of Philosophy at the UBC Faculty of Arts\. A leading scholar in the field, his research focuses on the philosophy of science, ethics and public policy, exploring how scientific evidence informs decision making on issues such as public health, climate change and risk assessment\. Dr\. Steel has also led major research initiatives supported by Canada’s Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council\.

[Dr\. Daniel Steel](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/DanielSteel_400x400.jpg)

As interim Director, CAE, Dr\. Steel will provide strategic, administrative and scholarly leadership, overseeing CAE’s research priorities, operations and partnerships to advance interdisciplinary, high quality research aligned with the Faculty’s strategic goals\. He will support investigators and trainees by fostering an inclusive and respectful research culture and promoting research excellence, integrity and knowledge translation\.

Dr\. Steel’s appointment follows the leadership of Dr\. Susan Cox, who served as interim Director since July 2024\. Her dedication and vision have left a lasting impact on the Centre’s community and helped strengthen its contributions to applied ethics\. We would like to thank Dr\. Cox for her leadership, dedication and commitment over the past two years\.

Please join us in congratulating Dr\. Steel on his appointment\.
