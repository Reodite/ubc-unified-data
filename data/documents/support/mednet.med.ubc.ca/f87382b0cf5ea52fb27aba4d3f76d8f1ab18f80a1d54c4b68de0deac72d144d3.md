[Dr\. Bruce Mohr](https://emergency.med.ubc.ca/2023/07/17/remembering-dr-bruce-mohr/ "Remembering Dr. Bruce Mohr | Department of Emergency Medicine") passed away on May 28, 2023\.

An emergency physician, Dr\. Mohr was a current Clinical Assistant Professor in the Department of Emergency Medicine\.

Read more [about Dr\. Mohr’s life\.](https://www.tributearchive.com/obituaries/28091571/dr-bruce-mohr "Dr. Bruce Mohr Obituary | Tribute Archive")\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Friday, September 22, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/09/28/bruce-mohr/ "Bruce Mohr Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
