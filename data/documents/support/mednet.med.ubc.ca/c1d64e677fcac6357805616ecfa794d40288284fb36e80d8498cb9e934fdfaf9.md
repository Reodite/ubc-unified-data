[Dr\. Patricia Baird passed away](https://www.legacy.com/ca/obituaries/theglobeandmail/name/patricia-baird-obituary?id=62195248 "Patricia Baird Obituary") on August 7, 2026\.

A distinguished scientist and international leader in medical genetics, Dr\. Baird was a Professor Emerita and former Head of the Department of Medical Genetics — the first woman to serve as Head of a clinical department at UBC\.

A groundbreaking researcher in the epidemiology of birth defects, Dr\. Baird published some of the first population\-based estimates for the prevalence of congenital anomalies, intellectual disability and genetic diseases\. She later lead the federal Royal Commission on New Reproductive Technologies, guiding the federal regulation of advanced reproductive technologies in Canada\. Among her many accolades, she was honoured with the Order of British Columbia, the Order of Canada and the Queen’s Jubilee Medal for service to the community\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Baird from the Department of Medical Genetics](https://medgen.med.ubc.ca/remembering-dr-patricia-baird-a-remarkable-leader-scientist-and-former-head-of-the-ubc-department-of-medical-genetics/)\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Sunday, August 23, 2026](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2026/09/patricia-ann-baird/ "Patricia Ann Baird Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
