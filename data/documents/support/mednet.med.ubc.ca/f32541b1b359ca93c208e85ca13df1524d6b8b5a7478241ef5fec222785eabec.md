# **Dates &amp; Deadlines**

## **2023 Faculty Salary Increases**

The process to administer the July 1, 2023 faculty salary increases for Bargaining Unit faculty members has begun\.  Please return your finalized Department/School summary of Merit and OAP award decisions to the Faculty HR Team via OneDrive by **Thursday, May 18th, 2023\.**  

## **2023 Faculty of Medicine Awards**

Nominations are now open for the 2023 Faculty of Medicine Awards \(see [attached announcement](https://usend.ubc.ca/sendy/l/HsadpV76Ruh3ciGgeqi8bQ/JT1LgHYtf1Le1GScxcwMpg/4BfCeWBt2Iqz5evZMW3J763Q)\)\. Please review the new award categories and nominations guidelines carefully\. Please submit your nominations by **4:00 p\.m\. on Friday, June 9, 2023** to [**fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca**](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca)**\.**

## **2024 Canada Gairdner Momentum Awards**

This year application deadline is **Saturday, July 15th, 2023\.** For a full description of the award and nomination guidelines, please visit: [https://www\.gairdner\.org/award/canada\-gairdner\-momentum\-award](https://www.gairdner.org/award/canada-gairdner-momentum-award) Webinar sessions will be held by the Foundation to answer questions and help with the nomination process\. Those interested can register for a session at the following link: [https://form\.jotform\.com/231164298364056](https://form.jotform.com/231164298364056)

## **Expiring Faculty PD Funds**

On April 21, 2023, Central HR sent out a customized e\-statement to faculty members who have [PD Funds](https://hr.ubc.ca/career-development/professional-development-funding/faculty-professional-development-funding) expiring on June 30\[th\], 2023, please see attached for a copy of the communication\. The communication was not sent to those who do not have funds expiring this year\. If faculty members have questions about their expiring PD funds amount, please contact HR at [faculty\.pd@ubc\.ca](mailto:faculty.pd@ubc.ca)\.

# **Updates &amp; Reminders**

## **Updated Faculty Job Advertisement Template**

In this updated version, we added tips in different sections of the template to create an objective advertisement that encourages applications from qualified candidates from different backgrounds\. Please download the latest template from [MedNet](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/hiring-faculty/advertising/)\.

## **2023 Annual Honorary Reappointment Process**

Thank you for getting your submissions in ahead of the deadline\. The data has been sent along to Faculty Relations for upload to Workday\. If you have follow\-up questions, please reach out to [fern\.moran@ubc\.ca](mailto:fern.moran@ubc.ca)\.

## **Increase of CRA Prescribed Interest Rate**

The [CRA Prescribed Interest Rate](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/prescribed-interest-rates/2023-q2.html) has been increased from **4%** to **5%**, effective **April 1\[st\], 2023** \(see attached announcement\)\. This rate will apply to all [UBC Prescribed Interest Rate Loan](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/housing-immigration-and-relocation-services/housing-programs/faculty-home-ownership-0)s activated on/after April 1\[st\], 2023\.

## **HR Roundtable**

A reminder that there will be a HR Roundtable on Conflict Engagement on Thursday, May 18th from 2:00 to 3:30pm\. This is an opportunity to meet and speak with Robyn Campol, Learning Environment Advisor in the Faculty of Medicine Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity &amp; Inclusion \(REDI\)\.  Please RSVP via this [link](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_cv78xnA0jnOFdP0) by **Monday, May 8th, 2023**\. 

## **Clinical Faculty Mentoring Program**

The Clinical Faculty Mentoring program is now accepting submissions for the next cohort\. Following a record number of mentorship pairings last fall, the highly popular Clinical Faculty Mentoring program is now accepting submissions for Junior clinical faculty and new educators to be paired with senior Clinical Faculty mentors from UBC’s Faculty of Medicine\.  Those Clinical Faculty who are interested can find out more information and register for the October 2023 cohort at the following link: [https://ubccpd\.ca/clinical\-faculty\-mentoring](https://ubccpd.ca/clinical-faculty-mentoring)

For more information, please review: [Advertising Lecturer Appointments](https://hr.ubc.ca/managers-admins/managing-and-leading/faculty-relations/faculty-relations-updates/advertising-lecturer-appointments)

## **Salary Increases for Research Associates**

Research Associates have been granted General Wage Increases as approved by the Board of Governors, along with an increase in their minimum salary\. Detailed information on the exact increase amounts and the process for their application in Workday, as well as the new minimum salaries were sent out on May 5, 2023 with the email version of this newsletter\.
