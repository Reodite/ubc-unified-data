The Bluma Tischler Postdoctoral Fellowship Award is for MD or PhD graduates in Medicine, Biochemistry, Genetics, or associated disciplines carrying out research on the biochemical or genetic aspects of intellectual developmental disorders or other neurological disorders\.

Submit your application online by **Friday, March 31, 2023**\. For any submission\-related queries, contact [med\.research@ubc\.ca](mailto:med.research@ubc.ca)\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://grad-postdoc.med.ubc.ca/bluma-tischler-postdoctoral-fellowship/ "Bluma Tischler Postdoctoral Fellowship | Faculty of Medicine Gradudate &amp; Postdoctoral Education")
