Planning and running an event in Faculty of Medicine spaces involves a few key steps\. Use this guide to make sure your event goes smoothly\. 

---

### Before Your Event

-   **Book your resources** \(academic learning spaces, FoM Zoom\) using [RoomFinder](https://rooms.med.ubc.ca/find/rooms) and include service requests as needed \(e\.g\., recording, technical support\.\) 

-   Confirm that you’ve received a **booking confirmation** via email from the Resource Coordination Team\. 

-   **Disseminate site access information** for your participants and presenters at all locations\. 

-   If using videoconferencing, **share Zoom details** \(Meeting ID digits, passcode\) with participants in advance\. 

-   **Check technology and equipment needs** early\. If you or your presenters are unfamiliar with the room technology, in\-room user training is available — visit the [In\-Room User Training webpage](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/it/collaboration-services/user-training/) to learn more or submit a request\. 

-   **Identify a backup person** who can assist or take over coordination if you’re unavailable\. 

-   **Confirm all event requirements**\. Certain sites require additional steps for catering, custodial, security, or other support services if your booking is after\-hours\. Contact the Resource Coordination Team for more information\. 

### During Your Event

-   **Arrive early** to set up and test audio/visual equipment\. 

-   **Check your connections:** 
    -   Ensure all videoconference sites or hybrid participants are connected\. 
    -   Verify that everyone can see and hear clearly\. 

-   **Test presentation materials** to make sure they display correctly at all locations\. 

-   **Contact the MedIT Service Desk** \(1\-877\-266\-0666, option 2\) immediately if you experience technical difficulties\. 

-   **Start on time** and make sure the session stays within your booked time slot\. 

### After Your Event

-   **End the session on schedule** so the next group may start their session on time\. 

-   If videoconferencing, **end the Zoom meeting** so the call disconnects and recording \(if applicable\) is ended at an appropriate time\. 

-   **Leave the room clean and tidy** and remove any personal items or garbage\. 

-   **Report any issues** \(technical, custodial, or facility\-related\) to the Resource Coordination Team or MedIT Service Desk\. 

-   **Provide feedback** if you encounter recurring issues or have suggestions for improving event support\. 

### Need Help?

For assistance with bookings, contact the **Resource Coordination Team** at [fom\.resourcecoordination@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.resourcecoordination@ubc.ca)\.  

For technical issues during your event, call the **MedIT Service Desk** at **1\-877\-266\-0666 \(option 2\.\)**
