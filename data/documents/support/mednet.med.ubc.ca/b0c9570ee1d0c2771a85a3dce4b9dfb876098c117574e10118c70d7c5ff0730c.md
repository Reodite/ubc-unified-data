Early\-career faculty in their first three years at UBC are invited to register for the *Maximizing Impact* mentorship and coaching program\.

*Maximizing Impact* is a six\-month cohort\-based program now entering its fourth year\. It is open to all non\-tenured early\-career researchers/faculty members at the level of Assistant Professor who are within their first three years at UBC\. This unique and intentionally designed program provides mentorship and coaching\. It engages early\-career researchers/faculty in maximizing the impact of their academic endeavours\.

The program will start late November 2023 and continue through the end of April 2024\. Register by **October 31, 2023**\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://academic.ubc.ca/academic-community/news-announcements/news/deadline-extended-maximizing-impact-2023-24)
