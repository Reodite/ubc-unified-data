[Dr\. Bryan Bass](https://www.hwwallacecbc.com/obituary/dr-bryan-bass/) passed away on January 20, 2025\.

A current Clinical Instructor in the Department of Family Practice, Dr\. Bass was a practising family physician in the Cowichan Valley, Vancouver Island\.

The B\.C\., Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Thursday, September 25, 2025](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2025/09/26/bryan-bass/ "Bryan Bass Flag Lowering | UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
