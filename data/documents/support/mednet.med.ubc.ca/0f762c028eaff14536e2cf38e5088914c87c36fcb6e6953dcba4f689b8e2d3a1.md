​A message from Sharmila Anandasabapathy, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, UBC\.

---

Please be advised that the [Faculty of Medicine Full Faculty Meeting](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/office-of-the-dean/governance/full-faculty-meetings/) will be held online via Zoom on **Tuesday, May 12** from 4:30–5:20 pm\.

## Registration required

Advanced registration for Full Faculty is now open\. All participants are required to register online by **12 noon on Tuesday, May 12**\.

[Register to attend](https://rc.med.ubc.ca/redcap/surveys/?s=PEKNKH78KMP4CKEK "Full Faculty Meeting Voting Registration")

## Meeting details &amp; voting:

-   Upon registration, all participants will receive a confirmation email containing the meeting agenda as well as the Zoom link required to join the meeting\.
-   Voting members will receive a meeting agenda containing links to the voting platform\. Please retain this email and follow in\-meeting instructions from the Chair to cast your vote\.

### Agenda:

1.  Welcome, call to order, review of agenda
2.  Approval of the [November 18, 2025 meeting minutes](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/2.0_20251118_FoM_Full_Faculty_Minutes.pdf) *\(motion\)*
3.  Approval of the [actions taken by Faculty Executive](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/3.0_2026.05.12_ActionsTakenByFEXSinceNov2025_V.0A.pdf) since the last meeting of the Faculty of Medicine *\(motion\)*
4.  [Report from the Dean](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/4.0_ReportOfTheDean-May-12-2026.pdf) to the Faculty of Medicine
5.  New Graduate Certificate in Healthy Aging &amp; New Course AGE\_V 500 Understanding Aging from Societies to Cells  *\(motion\)*
6.  Site Visits Update
7.  Candidates for Degrees *\(motion\)*
8.  Adjournment
