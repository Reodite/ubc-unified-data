Promotion and tenure reviews are some of the most, if not the most, important decisions we make as an academic community in the Faculty of Medicine\.  Here you will find guides, checklists, templates and summary maps of the promotion and tenure process\.

---

## Guides

-   [The SAC Guide to Reappointment, Tenure, and Promotion at UBC](https://hr.ubc.ca/career-development/tenure-and-faculty-appointments) \(Updated September 2025\):  The UBC Guide to Reappointment, Promotion, and Tenure Procedures details the Department Head/School Director’s responsibilities during the reappointment, promotion and tenure review process\. 
-    [FoM Guideline for the Streamlined Appointments Process for Senior Faculty Appointments](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/FoM-Streamlined-Appointment-Process-for-Faculty.pdf): Information on the criteria and guidelines for the Streamlined Appointment Process\.
-   [FoM Teaching Evidence Guide](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/FOM-Guide-Teaching-Evidence-for-DHs.pdf) for Department Heads and School Directors \(rev Nov 2012\)
-   [DH Guidelines – Presentations to Faculty ARPT Committee](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/DH-Guidelines-Presentations-to-Faculty-ARPT-Committee.pdf) \(rev Apr 2024\)
-   [Guidelines – Professional Contributions](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/05/Guidelines-Professional-Contributions-2025.pdf) \(Updated May 2025\)
-   [Guidelines – Scholarship of Teaching](https://med-fom-faculty.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/10/Guidelines-Scholarship-of-Teaching-2024.pdf) \(Updated August 2024\)

## Summary Maps

-   [Overall Summary Map for the Promotion &amp; Tenure Review Process](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/Process-Maps.pdf): This process maps show the full promotion &amp; tenure review process from beginning to end\.
-   [Faculty ARPT Process Map](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/Faculty-ARPT-Process-Map.pdf): This process map shows the flow of promotion and tenure review documents once they arrive in the FoM Dean’s Office\.

## Templates  

-   [Record of Meeting with Department Head/School Director](https://med-fom-faculty.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/2024-Memorandum-of-Annual-Meeting-with-Head-of-Unit.docx)\(2024 update\): Suggested format to record the annual meeting between the candidate and the Department Head or School Director\.
