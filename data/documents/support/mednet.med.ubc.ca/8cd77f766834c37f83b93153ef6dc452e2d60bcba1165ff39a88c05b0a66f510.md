The Faculty of Medicine’s Emerging Leaders Program provides faculty the opportunity to develop key leadership skills and prepare for future leadership roles within the academic environment\.

---

*Applications for the sixth cohort of the Faculty Emerging Leaders Program, starting January 2026, are now closed\. Please email* [*FoM\.OrgDev@ubc\.ca*](mailto:FoM.OrgDev@ubc.ca) *if you would like to be notified when the next application period opens*

## ****Who should participate?****

Faculty members who are interested in academic leadership positions or are early in their leadership journey\.

## ****What is the commitment?****

-   **Participate** in six modules held over six months \(January to June 2026\) as follows:
    -   Two **in\-person** events in Vancouver: **Kick\-off &amp; Module 1** \(January 2026\) and **Wrap\-up &amp; Module 6** \(June 2026\)
    -   Four **virtual** modules: held on the last Tuesday of each month from February to May 2026, 4–7 pm
-   **Complete** assigned pre\-work, requiring 1 to 2 hours per month
-   **Provide** feedback through module and program evaluations to support future program improvements

## ****Why participate?****

-   Strengthen your leadership capabilities and gain insight into your personal leadership approach and perspective 
-   Learn about the support and resources available to you through the Faculty and University 
-   Connect with and build a network of diverse emerging leaders within the Faculty 

## What is the cost to participate?

Program costs will be covered by the Faculty of Medicine, including travel from distributed sites for the first and final in\-person modules\. 

## Questions?

For questions, please email the Faculty of Medicine’s Organizational Development team at [FoM\.OrgDev@ubc\.ca](mailto:FoM.OrgDev@ubc.ca)\.

**Date:** Tuesday, January 27, 2026

**Time:** 10\-4 pm

**Location:** In person

Kick off the program by connecting with your cohort in person\. Spend the morning setting the stage for the six\-month journey, exploring program goals and expectations, and building a foundation for shared learning and collaboration\. In the afternoon, discover a learning model for effectively and accurately understanding the motives behind behaviours\. Using the Strength Deployment Inventory \(SDI\) 2\.0, deepen self\-awareness, build skills to navigate conflict, and enhance personal effectiveness when relating to others\. This session strengthens your ability to reflect, assess, and identify the areas of your leadership with the greatest potential for development and positive impact\. 

**Date:** Tuesday, February 24, 2026

**Time:** 4\-7 pm

**Location:** Virtual

Explore the elements of effective decision making and practical solutions for overcoming common decision\-making challenges\. Learn key strategic concepts and proven techniques for finding the best options in the face of complexity, uncertainty and conflicting objectives\. Increase your confidence as a skilled leader in making optimal decisions through the use of these concepts and tools\. 

**Date:** Tuesday, March 31, 2026

**Time:** 4\-7 pm

**Location:** Virtual

Learn about, reflect on, and gain an understanding of leadership strategies, actions, and tools to better support mental health, resilience, and psychological safety in the workplace\. Identify foundational elements for creating workplace environments that are supportive, resilient and adaptable\. Recognize individual skills and behaviours that support wellbeing, as well as actions that can enhance it at the academic unit level\. 

**Date:** Tuesday, April 28, 2026

**Time:** 4\-7 pm

**Location:** Virtual

Gain a better understanding of what it means to be a leader at this moment in time\. Become aware of barriers to belonging and thriving for underrepresented groups\. Build interpersonal skills for supporting individuals and identify leverage points for shifting systems to support individuals from underrepresented groups\. 

**Date:** Tuesday, May 26, 2026

**Time:** 4\-7 pm

**Location:** Virtual

Recognize different perspectives of conflict and apply communication skills for effectively engaging in difficult conversations and positively navigating through conflict\. 

**Date:** Tuesday, June 23, 2026

**Time:** 11\-3 pm

**Location:** In Person

Celebrate the completion of the program by connecting in person with your cohort and with Faculty of Medicine leaders\. Gain valuable insights from their leadership experiences, hearing firsthand the challenges they have faced and the lessons they have learned on their journey as leaders\. This culminating session offers space to reflect on your growth, strengthen connections, and celebrate the achievements of the past six months\.
