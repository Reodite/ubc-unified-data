Are you looking for gently used furniture for your home or office?

The UBC Furniture Reuse program accepts donations of gently\-used, surplus furniture from faculty and departments, and resells to the UBC community and broader public to help divert waste from disposal\.

By reimagining furniture waste as a reusable and valuable resource, the Furniture Reuse Program has kept over 1900 furniture items \(60 garbage trucks\) from disposal, avoided 95 \(tonne CO2e\) of carbon emissions, and saved over $950,000 in cost\-avoidance\.

**To donate:** Contact [furniture\.reuse@ubc\.ca](mailto:furniture.reuse@ubc.ca) and a program representative will be in touch to arrange inventory and furniture viewings and/or pick up option\.

**To purchase:** Visit the [Zero Waste Market](https://facilities.ubc.ca/projects/ubc-furniture-reuse-program/#ubc-zero-waste-market) on campus at UBC Vancouver, open **every Wednesday from 12–2 pm** at Ponderosa Annex E, Room 127\. *The store’s schedule and opening hours are subject to change, please check the website prior to visiting\.*

[Learn more](https://facilities.ubc.ca/projects/ubc-furniture-reuse-program/ "UBC Furniture Reuse Program | UBC Facilities, Vancouver")
