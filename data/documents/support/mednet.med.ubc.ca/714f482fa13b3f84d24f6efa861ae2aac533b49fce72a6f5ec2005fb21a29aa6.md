Join UBC Connects at Robson Square for “Reimagining the Mind: Innovations in Mental Health Research and Care” on **Wednesday, January 28**\.

Hosted in partnership with the Djavad Mowafaghian Centre for Brain Health, hear from Dr\. Fidel Vila\-Rodriguez, Dr\. Julie Robillard and Dr\. Tamara Vanderwal as they explore the technologies reshaping mental health care\. From brain stimulation therapies like TMS, to ethical applications of social robotics, to movie\-based neuroimaging tools, discover how innovation is expanding the ways we support mental wellness across the lifespan\.

-   **When:** Wednesday, January 28 from 6–9 pm
-   **Where:** UBC Robson Square

[Learn more &amp; register](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/events/reimagining-the-mind/ "Reimagining the Mind: Innovations in Mental Health Research and Care | UBC Community Engagement")
