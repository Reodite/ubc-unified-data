Nominations are now open for the BC College of Family Physicians \(BCCFP\) Awards for 2026\. These awards celebrate the dedication, expertise, and impact of family physicians delivering comprehensive care to communities throughout the province\.

Submit a nomination by **10 am on Thursday, February 26, 2026**\.

## Award categories

-   BC Family Physician of the Year
-   First Five Years of Family Practice \(rural and urban recipients\)
-   My Family Doctor Award \(patient\-nominated\)
-   5 Resident Awards of $1500 each

[Learn more &amp; nominate](https://www.bccfp.bc.ca/news/bccfp-2026-awards "2026 BCCFP Awards Nominations Now Open -BC College of Family Physicians")

*Nominees must be BCCFP members and not current board members\.*
