Nominations are now open for the Clinical Trials BC Awards 2024\.

These awards honour individuals and organizations who have made significant contributions to strengthening the B\.C\. clinical trials ecosystem\. Award recipients exemplify excellence in leadership, advocacy for clinical trials, and outstanding service and support to advance clinical trials research and improve the health of people across British Columbia\.

Submit nominations by **December 10, 2023**\.

[Learn more &amp; nominate](https://healthresearchbc.ca/news_article/2024-clinical-trials-bc-awards-now-open-for-nominations/ "2024 Clinical Trials BC Awards now open for nominations - Health Research BC")
