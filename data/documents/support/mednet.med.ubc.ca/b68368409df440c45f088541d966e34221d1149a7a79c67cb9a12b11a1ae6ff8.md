Are you a faculty member interested in integrating climate change and wellbeing content and approaches into your existing undergraduate or graduate course?

Presented by the UBC Sustainability Hub and UBC Wellbeing, the Climate and Wellbeing Education Grants offer up to $6,000 in funding to help you develop new course materials, host workshops, and create new teaching resources that focus on the connections between climate change and human health and wellbeing\.

Submit an application by **Friday, June 5, 2026**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://sustain.ubc.ca/education/climate-change-and-wellbeing-education-grants "Climate and Wellbeing Education Grants | UBC Sustainability")
