UBC faculty are invited to put in proposals to lead a Global Seminar of their making in summer or Reading Week 2024\. Propose a new seminar by **June 30, 2023**\.

Global Seminars, led by UBC faculty and supported by [Go Global](https://global.ubc.ca/go-global), give UBC students the opportunity to dive deeply into a topic with a UBC professor in context\-rich locations around the world\.

There are new funding opportunities available this year, including a faculty travel grant for program and partnership development; subsidies for new programs in the first year to lower the costs for students; and grants promoting inclusion of students with disabilities in Global Seminars\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://global.ubc.ca/news-events/stories/apr-5-2023-ubc-faculty-take-your-students-around-world-next-year-apply-now "UBC Faculty: Take your students around the world next year. Apply now! | UBC Global")
