Prioritize wellbeing in your team by becoming a UBC Vancouver Workplace Wellbeing Ambassador\.

UBC’s Workplace Wellbeing team is looking for volunteers who will play a vital role in championing health and wellbeing for their departments and among their peers\.

This year’s Workplace Wellbeing Ambassador \(WWA\) cohort will begin on September 1\. If you have enthusiasm and passion for health and wellbeing, apply by **August 14** to join the WWA program and play a vital role in fostering wellbeing in your team/unit/department\.

-   Length of term: Ambassadors are asked to commit to this role for a minimum of one year, with the option to recommit to the role annually
-   Time commitment: 2\-4 hours per month \(this may vary depending on training and programs\) 

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/news/july-07-2023/prioritize-wellbeing-your-team-become-ubc-vancouver-workplace "Prioritize wellbeing in your team: become a UBC Vancouver Workplace Wellbeing Ambassador | UBC Human Resources ")

*UBC Okanagan WWA applications open in 2024\.*
