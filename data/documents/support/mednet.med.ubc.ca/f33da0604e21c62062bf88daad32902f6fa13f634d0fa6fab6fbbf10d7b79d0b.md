[Dr\. Michael C\. Klein](https://everloved.com/life-of/michael-c-klein/obituary/ "Michael C. Klein Obituary") passed away on June 10, 2026\.

A leading figure in the de\-medicalization of childbirth in Canada and around the world, Dr\. Klein was a Professor Emeritus in the Departments of Family Practice and Pediatrics, and previously served as the founding Head of the former Division of Maternity and Newborn Care\. He also served as Head of the Department of Family Practice at the Children’s and Women’s Health Centre of British Columbia\.

Dr\. Klein was best known for his pioneering research demonstrating complications associated with routine episiotomy, which is credited with leading to significant reductions in episiotomy and other surgical interventions in Canada and beyond\. He is also noted for his leadership in transdisciplinary maternity care education and research, including the promotion of midwives and doulas\. For his many contributions to family medicine and maternity care, he was appointed to the Order of Canada in 2016\.
