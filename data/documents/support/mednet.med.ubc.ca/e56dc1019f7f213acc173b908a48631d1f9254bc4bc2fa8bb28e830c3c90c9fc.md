The UBC Indigenous Strategic Initiatives \(ISI\) Fund is open for applications from all faculty, staff and students at UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan\.

The ISI Fund supports implementation of [UBC’s Indigenous Strategic Plan](https://isp.ubc.ca/)\. Over the past two years, funding has been awarded to sixty projects advancing Indigenous initiatives at the Vancouver and Okanagan campuses across multiple Faculties, departments and non\-academic units\.

A total of $2 million dollars in ISI funding is available for Cycle 3: 2023\-2024, across three streams:

1.  Innovative Projects: $30K–$100K for one\- or two\-year projects led by faculty &amp; staff
2.  Transformative Projects: $50K–$250K for projects up to three\-years led by faculty &amp; staff
3.  Student\-led Projects: Up to $50K for projects led by graduate &amp; undergraduate students

Submit a letter of intent by **January 12, 2024**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://isp.ubc.ca/isi-fund/ "ISI Fund - UBC Indigenous Strategic Plan")
