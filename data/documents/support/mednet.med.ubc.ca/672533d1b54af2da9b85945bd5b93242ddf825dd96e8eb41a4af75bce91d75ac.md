The MD Undergraduate Program Admissions Office invites you to [apply to participate in the 2023 admission interviews](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/files/2021/10/Joint-Prospective-Information-Sheet-2021-22.pdf "Joint Prospective Information Sheet | MD Undergraduate Admissions") for the MD Undergraduate Program\. Your participation in these events is welcomed to help select the next class of medical students at UBC\.

All interviews and training sessions will be held in a virtual format\.

[Learn more and apply](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/announcements/community-update/md-undergraduate-admissions-interviewers-needed/)
