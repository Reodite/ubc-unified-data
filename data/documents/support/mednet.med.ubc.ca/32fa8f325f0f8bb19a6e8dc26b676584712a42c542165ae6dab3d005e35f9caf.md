Apply for up to $1,500 for community\-university collaboration and relationship building through the Partnership Recognition and Exploration \(PRE\) Fund\.

UBC Community Engagement is now accepting applications for 2023 PRE funding, which aims to fill small resource gaps and empower community partners to build reciprocal relationships with UBC students, faculty, and staff\.

The fund especially welcomes collaborative initiatives that advance:

-   Equity, diversity, and inclusion
-   Indigenous engagement and reconciliation
-   Health innovation and community wellbeing
-   Sustainability and climate action
-   Teaching, learning, and research excellence

PRE funding is available from **May to December 31, 2023**, OR until available funds are exhausted\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/news/2023-pre-fund-application/ "Apply for the 2023 Partnership Recognition and Exploration Fund – UBC Community Engagement")
