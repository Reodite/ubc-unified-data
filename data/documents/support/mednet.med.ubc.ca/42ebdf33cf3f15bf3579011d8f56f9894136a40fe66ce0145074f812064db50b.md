[Aerial photo of UBC with overlaid text: Roadmap for Change: Implementing anti\-racism commitments at UBC, January 25, 2 to 3:15 pm](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/01/events.ubc_.ca-1280-x-720px-100-940x529.jpg)

On **January 25 at 2 pm**, join UBC’s leadership in a virtual forum to learn how UBC is moving forward with implementing anti\-racism commitments\.

Learn about next steps for implementing the recommendations from the Anti\-Racism and Inclusive Excellence \(ARIE\) Task Force Final Report, and how you can build a more equitable and anti\-racist UBC\. All UBC community members are welcome, including students, faculty and staff across the Vancouver and Okanagan campuses\.

[RSVP for the virtual forum](https://ubc.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_Ozs6SCFETIKIWY6QFf6E3w "Roadmap for Change: Implementing anti-racism commitments at UBC - Zoom Webinar Registration")

See more details [about the event and the list of speakers](https://equity.ubc.ca/events/roadmap-for-change-implementing-anti-racism-commitments-at-ubc/ "Roadmap for Change: Implementing anti-racism commitments at UBC - UBC Equity &amp; Inclusion Office")\.
