Dermot Kelleher, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health  
invites you to join a virtual event on**  
Tuesday, September 27, 2022**  
10:00–11:30 am PT\.  
*Given the discussion topic, this event may go beyond 11:30 am*

Please join us in coming together in a concerted effort to reflect on the purpose of National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, also known as Orange Shirt Day\.

[Download calendar event](https://www.med.ubc.ca/files/2022/09/Orange-Shirt-Day-2022.ics "Event calendar invitation (download)")

Learn more [about the event](https://www.med.ubc.ca/2022-commemorative-event-for-orange-shirt-day-national-day-for-truth-and-reconciliation/ "UBC Faculty of Medicine 2022 Commemorative Event for National Day for Truth and Reconciliation | Orange Shirt Day") and the Faculty of Medicine’s 2021 Apology and Response to the TRC Calls to Action\.
