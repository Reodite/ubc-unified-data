## Criteria

The proposed implementation, or re\-launch of a new course, program, or certificate should have the following attributes for consideration:

1.  Use a cross\-disciplinary approach to create modules that leverage expertise between departments/schools/institutes 
2.  Deliver sustainable benefits to learners either directly \(e\.g\. enhancement and long\-term viability of content; experiential learning opportunities, career trajectory\) and/or indirectly \(e\.g\. training of faculty and staff in new leaching/learning tools\) 
3.  Achieve higher enrolment and net revenues over a multi\-year horizon
4.  Ensure it can be delivered within existing UBC facilities and infrastructure\.
5.  Be deliverable within the existing infrastructure of UBC facilities 
6.   Align with the goals and objectives of the UBC Faculty of Medicine 2021\-2026 Strategic Plan, [*Building the Future*](https://stratplan.med.ubc.ca/), in particular, the Education Pillar and UBC’s strategic plan 2018\-2028 in [*Shaping UBC’s Next Century*](https://strategicplan.ubc.ca/)\.

## Pathways

The timeline for a curriculum change depends on its Category, which are defined in the UBC\-V Senate [Guide to Curriculum Changes\.](https://scs-senate-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/Guide-to-Curriculum-Submissions-for-UBC-Vancouver.pdf) All proposed changes will travel through the four key stages: Inception, Endorsement, Faculty Approval and External Approval\. ## ****Submission Deadlines for Annual Sessions and Calendar Releases****

All proposed changes to curricula must first be submitted to the [FoM Curriculum Team,](mailto:fom.curriculum@ubc.ca) and approved by the FoM before moving forward to UBC\-V Senate Office for final approval\.  The [FoM Curriculum Team](mailto:fom.curriculum@ubc.ca) is the single point of contact between proponents, the FoM, UBC\-V Senate, and external governing bodies to support and facilitate all curriculum changes\.

Below is the estimated timeline for Category 1 and Category 2 curriculum change proposals to be tentatively accepted for the 2027W or 2028S session, and reflected in the 2027/2028 UBC Calendar release\. This is provided as a ‘best estimate’ only, and can be subject to change based upon decisions made by FoM committees or external governing bodies/committees during any step in the review process\.

These submission deadlines do not pertain to new program, degree, diploma, certificate, or micro\-certificate proposals\. For timelines associated with new program, degree, diploma, certificate, or micro\-credential proposals, please contact the  [FoM Curriculum Team](mailto:fom.curriculum@ubc.ca) at [fom\.curriculum@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.curriculum@ubc.ca)\.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 24\-Jul\-26 | 07\-Oct\-26 / 04\-Nov\-26 | 10\-Nov\-26 | TBA | TBA | TBA | 2027/2028 Academic Calendar |

To identify which council/committee is appropriate for your proposal to travel through, please contact the [FoM Curriculum Team](mailto:fom.curriculum@ubc.ca) at [fom\.curriculum@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.curriculum@ubc.ca)\.
