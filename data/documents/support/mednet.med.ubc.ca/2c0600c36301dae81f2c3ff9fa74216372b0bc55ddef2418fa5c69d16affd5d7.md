UBC is aware of phishing scams currently targeting UBC students and researchers with fraudulent job postings or research aid\. These scams often appear legitimate, impersonating UBC faculty or staff\.

See tips for identifying a phishing scam from UBC Privacy Matters, and report any suspicious emails to UBC Information Security team at [security@ubc\.ca](mailto:security@ubc.ca)\.

[Learn more](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/job-scam "Urgent Alert: Active Job Scams Targeting UBC Students and Researchers | Privacy Matters @ UBC")
