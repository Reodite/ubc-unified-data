## Procedure

For the CIHR Fall 2026 Project Grant competition, the Faculty of Medicine \(FoM\) has established the following procedures, in alignment with the Office of Research Services timelines:

1.  Your application package must be submitted to the Dean’s Office or affiliated hospital research office by **August 28, 2026** for Faculty approval\. Please send it via email to the Faculty [signature contact for your site](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/signature-process/)\. Note that Lily Nizami \([fom\.grantapprovals@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.grantapprovals@ubc.ca)\) is the contact for Faculty approval for those located at the UBC Point Grey campus\. See below for a complete list of the required items\.  
    
2.  Your NSGRP Risk Assessment Form, applicable when applying with a private sector partner, must be submitted directly to [research\.security@ubc\.ca](mailto:research.security@ubc.ca) **as soon as possible**\.  
    
3.  After Faculty approval, your application package will be forwarded to ORS\.  
    
4.  Your application must be submitted via ResearchNet no later than **September 2**, 2026**** \(for application **involving private sector partners**\) **OR** ****September 8, 2026**** \(for application **without private\-sector partner**\) \.  
    
5.  ORS will electronically approve your application via ResearchNet for submission to CIHR\.

Please remember that you can continue working on your research proposal up until you submit on ResearchNet, even after you have submitted your application package for Faculty approval\.

**To avoid system issues which may cause delay in the submission process on the deadline day, you are highly encouraged to submit early\.** While ORS makes best efforts to submit all applications on time and to facilitate support for our research community, application packages submitted for Faculty approval after August 28 may not receive a guarantee of administrative review from ORS which includes confirmation of application completeness\.

**Research Security Requirements for CIHR Project Grant**  
Please note that starting May 1, 2024, new federal research security requirements apply to applications for research funding from the Tri\-Agencies and Canada Foundation for Innovation when the project aims to advance certain sensitive technology research areas\. The CIHR Project Grant program is affected by these changes\. Please review the policies and whether they are applicable to your application via [**UBC Research Security website**](https://researchsecurity.ubc.ca/) and by completing the [**Federal research security funding policy self\-check**](https://researchsecurity.ubc.ca/federal-policies-research-funding/self-check)\. 

## Required Documents

Required Documents to be submitted to the FoM for the CIHR Fall 2026 Project Grant competition:

1.  Completed [Research Project Information Form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/required-documents/) including the following signatures:
    1.  the Principal Investigator
    2.  Department Head or School Director
    3.  Research Centre Director \(if using research space within a Senate\-Approved Research Centre, identified with an asterisk on the [Academic &amp; Research Units](http://med.ubc.ca/about/departments-schools-centres/) page\)
    4.  Life Sciences Institute Director \(if using research space within the Life Sciences Building, but excludes researchers using space within the Centre for Blood Research\)
2.  Complete application, including the following sections:
    -   All pages of the CIHR Application Detail sections \(download from ResearchNet under “Preview Application Materials”\)
    -   Complete Summary page
    -   Other Applicants pages, *if applicable*
    -   Budget modules, including written justifications and quotes, where applicable
    -   Partner budget details, *if applicable*
    -   Signed letter\(s\) of support from Partners \(cash or in\-kind\), *if applicable*

Please note, the letters from collaborators are *not required* for UBC internal submission\.

Please also review the ORS website for the CIHR\-specific [Agency Application Requirements &amp; Procedures](https://ors.ubc.ca/proposal-development-submission/agency-application-requirements-procedures#5)\.

If you have any questions regarding this process, please contact Lily Nizami at FoM \([lily\.nizami@ubc\.ca](mailto:lily.nizami@ubc.ca)\)\.
