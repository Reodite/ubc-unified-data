[Dr\. Judith Dowd](https://vancouversunandprovince.remembering.ca/obituary/judith-anne-dowd-1093355054 "Judith Dowd Obituary | Vancouver Sun and Province") passed away on November 15, 2025\.

A Clinical Assistant Professor Emerita, Dr\. Dowd taught in the Department of Anesthesiology \(now the Department of Anesthesiology, Pharmacology &amp; Therapeutics\) alongside her work at St\. Paul’s Hospital\.

The B\.C\., Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Thursday, January 22, 2026](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2026/02/judith-anne-dowd/ "Judith Dowd Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies &amp; Events") in remembrance\.
