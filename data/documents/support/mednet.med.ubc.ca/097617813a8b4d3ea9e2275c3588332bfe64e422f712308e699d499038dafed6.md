A message from Dermot Kelleher, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, UBC\.

---

Nominations are now open for the 2023 UBC Faculty of Medicine Distinguished Achievement Awards\.

The Distinguished Achievement Awards provide an important opportunity to acknowledge faculty members who exemplify exceptional contributions to the Faculty of Medicine in the areas of education, research and/or service, and who are dedicated to advancing both the Faculty of Medicine’s values and vision of transforming health for everyone\.

Please review the [eight available categories](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/03/2023-DAA-Criteria-Process.pdf), with up to two awards available in each category\. Depending on the category, recipients will be selected based on performance within the past assessment year or on both past assessment year and overall career achievement\.

Nominations must be made by department heads or school directors by **Friday, April 14, 2023**\. Recipients will be announced in the summer, and the awards will be presented at the Faculty of Medicine Awards Reception in the fall\.

---

*This message was sent to Department Heads, School Directors, Senior Administrators and full\-time faculty\.*
