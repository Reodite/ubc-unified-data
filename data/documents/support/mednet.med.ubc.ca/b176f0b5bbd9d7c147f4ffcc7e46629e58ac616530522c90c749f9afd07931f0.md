## Purpose and Authority

The purpose of this executive subcommittee is to:

-   Supports the governance of the MD Undergraduate Program \(MDUP\) by providing oversight of resource planning, financial sustainability, and coordinated program operations\.
-   Accountable to the Undergraduate Medical Education \(UGMEC\) for the quality, alignment, and coordination of its recommendations on program and policy matters, and to the Distributed Program Planning Committee \(DPPC\) and the Dean for financial and resource\-related advice\.
-   Authorized to review, assess, and endorse proposals and budget recommendations, and serves as the designated body through which initiatives with material fiscal, human resource, or technological implications must be considered prior to advancement through formal governance processes\.
-   Operates in an advisory and coordinating capacity and does not exercise decision\-making authority on behalf of UGMEC\. Final approval of:
    -   academic and program matters rest with UGMEC
    -   financial and resource decisions rest with DPPC and the Dean

## Composition

This subcommittee is made up of ex\-officio members\.  
The Faculty of Medicine’s commitment to equitable and diverse membership on its committees and advisory councils guides its nomination and selection process\.

#### Ex\-officio

-   Regional Associate Deans \(IMP, NMP, SMP, VFMP\) biennial co\-chair rotation \(4\)
-   Associate Dean, Undergraduate Medical Education, co\-chair
-   Senior Director, Education Program and Services, Office of the Vice Dean, Education
-   Administrative Director, Office of the Vice Dean, Education 
-   Regional Administrative Directors \(IMP, NMP, SMP, VFMP\) \(4\)
-   Manager, Office of Undergraduate Medical Education

Guests may be invited to join specific meetings or portions of specific meetings at the co\-chairs’ discretion\.

## Appointment Process

Ex officio members are members by virtue of their administrative appointment\.

## Term

Ex officio members are members as long as they hold their administrative appointment\.

## Chair

Co\-chaired by one Regional Associate Dean \(biennial rotation\) and the Associate Dean, UGME\. This co\-leadership model reflects the distributed site structure of the MDUP\.

## Meeting Schedule and Administration

Normally meets monthly or more frequently at the call of the co\-chairs\.

All members are expected to attend all meetings in person or via videoconference or phone\.

A staff member from the MDUP will capture meeting minutes\. Minutes will be circulated to all members\.

Records will be maintained in accordance with UBC and [Faculty of Medicine records retention procedures](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/facilities/records-management/)\.

## Quorum and Decision\-Making Process

Meetings will generally be cancelled if fewer than half of the members can attend or if no agenda items are submitted\.

The subcommittee provides advice that reflects input from all members\. While members typically reach consensus on that advice, differing perspectives may be noted if consensus cannot be achieved\.

## Lines of Accountability and Communication

Members are accountable for ensuring timely, accurate and bi\-directional communication between the UGMEC and their respective teams, supporting consistent implementation of decisions, alignment with approved policies, and operational clarity across the MDUP\.

The UMEE supports integrated governance by facilitating the efficient flow of information, issues, and risks between program\-wide and regional leadership structures, minimizing duplication and enabling coordinated and aligned action\.

**Financial Stewardship Accountabilities:**

The UMEE is accountable for effective financial oversight and resource planning for the MDUP, including:

-   Developing and recommending the program’s annual budget and multi\-year financial forecasts for submission to the DPPC and subsequent approval by the Dean;
-   Monitoring expenditures, risks, and variances against the approved budget, and ensuring transparent in financial reporting  to the DPPC;
-   Reviewing and endorsing the feasibility and sustainability of proposals with material fiscal, human, resource or technological implications that exceed the program’s approved budget or scope prior to advancement through governance processes;
-   Providing advice to the DPPC and the Dean on resource requirements, priorities, and sustainability considerations;
-   Liaising with relevant committees, subcommittees and advisory councils to ensure alignment in resource planning across the program\.

## Responsibilities

**Policy and Program Recommendations for UGME Committee \(UGMEC\)**

This subcommittee:

-   Ensures consistent bidirectional communication across the MDUP, including program\-wide and regional leadership structures;
-   Supports UGMEC governance processes by receiving direction from UGMEC and providing coordinated input, analysis, and recommendations on program\-wide issues and initiatives;
-   Provides advice and recommendations to UGMEC on policies, new programs, and substantive program changes; the UMEE does not exercise decision\-making authority on behalf of UGMEC;
-   Supports continuous quality improvement and accreditation activities, including identifying risks and areas requiring attention;
-   Reviews program risks, regional performance, and progress against annual plans, and escalates issues requiring UGMEC consideration;
-   Supports comparability of curriculum delivery and assessment across regional sites;
-   Reviews these Terms of Reference biennially, or earlier in response to significant structural, governance, or funding model changes\.

**MDUP Resource Utilization and Management**

This subcommittee:

-   Reviews and assesses proposals with regional, operational, or resource implications to determine feasibility and sustainability, and provides recommendations to:
    -    UGMEC \(programmatic implications\), and
-   Serves as the designated body for review and endorsement of proposals with material fiscal, human resource, or technological implications that exceed the program’s approved budget or scope;
-   Develops and recommends the MDUP annual budget and multi\-year financial forecasts, in collaboration with FoM Finance, for submission to the DPPC; Final financial approval authority rests with the DPPC and the Dean;
-   Monitors and reviews financial performance, including quarterly reports and identifies pressures, risks, and mitigation strategies;
-   Advises on resource allocation and financial priorities, including recommending additional resources where required through DPPC;
-   Supports alignment between program priorities and resource allocation, ensuring financial sustainability;
-   Liaises with relevant committees, subcommittees, and advisory bodies to ensure coordination and alignment of resource planning and governance processes\.

## Approval
