Faculty and clinical faculty members within the first years of their appointment in the Faculty of Medicine can apply for up to $10,000 in research funding\.

The New Faculty Research Award is for faculty members in the first two years of their first appointment as Assistant Professor or higher, or Adjunct Professor in the Faculty of Medicine\. Clinical Faculty members within four years of their first appointment as Clinical Assistant Professor or higher are also eligible to apply\.

The funds may be used for research expenses including equipment, supplies, support salaries or dissemination of results\. Submit an application by **4 pm on Tuesday, June 16, 2026**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/research/internal-funding-opportunities/new-faculty-research-award/ "New Faculty Research Award - MedNet")
