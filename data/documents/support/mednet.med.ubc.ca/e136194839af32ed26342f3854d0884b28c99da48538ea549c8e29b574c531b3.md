Join UBC’s Privacy Matters team for a series of virtual events focusing on privacy and information security during Data Privacy Week 2026 \(**January 27–29**\)\.

Through these sessions, faculty and staff are invited to explore what privacy means in your work, why it matters and how UBC is addressing emerging challenges\. Sessions include:

-   **Jan\. 27 \| What’s New in Privacy at UBC?:** Hear from B\.C\.’s Information and Privacy Commissioner and gain an inside look at UBC’s ongoing efforts to strengthen privacy practices\.
-   **Jan\. 28 \| **Demystifying Freedom of Information \(FOI\) Requests**:** Discover how B\.C\.’s FIPPA shapes your day\-to\-day work\. Learn what counts as a record, what you’re responsible for and how to handle requests while protecting privacy\.
-   **Jan\. 29 \| **AI Privacy &amp; Information Security: Same Game, Faster &amp; Smarter Players**:** Explore how AI is reshaping the privacy and information security threat landscape and learn ways to protect yourself\.

[See all event details &amp; register](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/data-privacy-week "Data Privacy Week | Privacy Matters @ UBC")
