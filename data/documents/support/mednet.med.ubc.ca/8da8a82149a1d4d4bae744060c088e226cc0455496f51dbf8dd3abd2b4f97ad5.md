[Dr\. Sylvie Argouarch](https://psychiatry.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-sylvie-argouarch-clinical-instructor/ "IN MEMORIAM: Dr. Sylvie Argouarch, Clinical Instructor - UBC Department of Psychiatry") passed away on August 2, 2025\.

Dr\. Argouarch was a current clinical instructor in the Department of Psychiatry and practising geriatric psychiatrist with the Vancouver Island Health Authority at Royal Jubilee Hospital\. As a dedicated clinical instructor with the Island Medical Program, she contributed to training and mentoring medical students, as well as residents and health professional learners on the Island\.

Read [more about Dr\. Argouarch’s life](https://www.mccallgardens.com/obituaries/sylvie-juliette-argouarch/ "Sylvie Argouarch Obituary | McCall Gardens Funeral Service") and legacy\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Friday, September 5, 2025](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2025/09/19/sylvie-argouarch/ "Sylvie Argourarch Flag Lowering | UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
