Registration is now open for Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day on **Friday, May 1**\.

Faculty and staff at UBC Vancouver and Okanagan are invited to take part in a range of activities designed for all experience, fitness and ability levels\. Gather a team of 4–6 colleague \(or you can compete as an individual\) and get ready for a mix of physical challenges, brain teasers and a good helping of fun\.

-   For UBC Vancouver, register for one of two time slots by **Monday, April 20**\.
-   For UBC Okanagan, register by **Monday, April 27**\.

Applications are also open for volunteers to support the events\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/news/march-12-2026/registration-now-open-2026-faculty-staff-sports-day " Registration is now open for 2026 Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day | UBC Human Resources")
