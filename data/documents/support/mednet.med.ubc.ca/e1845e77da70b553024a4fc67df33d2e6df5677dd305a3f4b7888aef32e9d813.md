The UBC community is invited to comment on proposed amendments to the Administrative VPs Policy \(AP14\)\.

The proposed amendments include removing fixed term lengths for Administrative Vice Presidents and adding annual performance reviews and institutional reviews of each Administrative VP’s portfolio on a five\-year basis\.

All members of the UBC community are encouraged to provide their comments by **January 31, 2024\.**
