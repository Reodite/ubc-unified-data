[Dr\. David Vincent Godin](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/08/DavidVincentGodin_300x375-edited.jpg)

[Dr\. David Vincent Godin](https://apt.med.ubc.ca/news/dr-david-vincent-godin-passed-away-on-july-9-2023/ "Dr. David Vincent Godin passed away on July 9, 2023 | Anesthesiology, Pharmacology &amp; Therapeutics") passed away on July 9, 2023\.

A Professor Emeritus, Dr\. Godin had a long career as a teacher and researcher in the Department of Anesthesiology, Pharmacology &amp; Therapeutics\.

Read more [about Dr\. Godin’s life](https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/vancouver-bc/david-godin-11367833 "David Godin Obituary - Dignity Memorial")\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Thursday, August 3, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/08/29/david-godin/ "David Godin Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
