Members of the UBC Vancouver research community are invited to participate in a strategic consultation on services offered through [Innovation UBC](https://innovation.ubc.ca/), aimed to enhance the support for sponsored research, technology transfer \(patenting and licensing\) and entrepreneurship\.

Your feedback will help inform plans focused on improving support and services for translating research into positive economic, environmental, health and social benefits\.

Register for a virtual consultation session on either **Thursday, December 14, 2023** or **Friday, January 12, 2024**\. Written submissions are also accepted\.

[Learn more](https://uilo.ubc.ca/announcement/innovation-ubc-strategic-consultation "Innovation UBC strategic consultation")
