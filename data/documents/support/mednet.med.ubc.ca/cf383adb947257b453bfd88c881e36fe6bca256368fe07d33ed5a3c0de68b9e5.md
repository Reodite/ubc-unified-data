[Dr\. Edith McGeer](https://psychiatry.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-edith-g-mcgeer-professor-emerita/ "In Memoriam: Dr. Edith G. McGeer, Professor Emerita - Department of Psychiatry") passed away August 28, 2023\.

A Professor Emerita, Dr\. McGeer first joined the UBC Department of Psychiatry in 1954, and was a founding member and Head of the Division of Neuroscience\.

A leading researcher in neurodegenerative diseases, her research was recognized through many honours including a UBC Faculty of Medicine Lifetime Achievement Award, a Queen Elizabeth II Diamond Jubilee Medal, and appointments as a Fellow to the Royal Society of Canada, and Officer to both the Order of British Columbia and the Order of Canada\.

Read more [about Dr\. McGreer’s life](https://vancouversunandprovince.remembering.ca/obituary/edith-mcgeer-1088797145 "Edith McGeer Obituary | Vancouver Sun and Province")\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Friday, September 22, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/09/28/edith-g-mcgeer/ "Edith G. McGeer Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
