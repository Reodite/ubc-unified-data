[Dr\. Henry F\. Mizgala](https://kearneyfs.com/obituaries/henry-frank-mizgala-md-frcpc-facc-faha "Henry Mizgala, MD, FRCPC Obituary | Kearney Funeral Services") passed away on December 11, 2022\.

A professor emeritus, Dr\. Mizgala joined the Faculty of Medicine in 1980 as Head of the Division of Cardiology\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Thursday, January 12, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/01/10/henry-f-mizgala/ "Henry F. Mizgala | Flag Lowering, UBC Ceremonies") in remembrance\.
