A message from Wyeth Wasserman, Vice Dean, Research and Craig Mitton, Acting Director, School of Population &amp; Public Health\.

---

We are pleased to announce the appointment of **Dr\. Anne Gadermann** and **Dr\. Eva Oberle** as interim Co\-Directors of the Human Early Learning Partnership \(HELP\), effective to September 30, 2027\. Drs\. Gadermann and Oberle will take on this role while Dr\. Mariana Brussoni is on a one\-year administrative leave\.

[Dr\. Anne Gadermann](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/AnneGadermann_400x400.jpg)

Dr\. Gadermann is an associate professor in the School of Population &amp; Public Health and a Canada Research Chair in Bio\-Eco\-Social Determinants of Child and Youth Mental Health\. She is also a research scientist at the Centre for Advancing Health Outcomes\. Dr\. Gadermann’s research focuses on the social determinants of mental health and wellbeing of children and youth, examining how family, school and neighbourhood factors shape mental health outcomes to inform prevention and intervention strategies\. Her accomplishments include major national funding, a Michael Smith Foundation for Health Research Scholar Award and recognition through the UBC Killam Accelerator Research Fellowship\.

[Dr\. Eva Oberle](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/EvaOberle_400x400.jpg)

Dr\. Oberle is an associate professor in the School of Population &amp; Public Health\. Her research focuses on the impacts of social connectedness, relationships, school climate and social\-emotional learning on the development and wellbeing of children and adolescents\. Dr\. Oberle is widely recognized for translating research into practice through partnerships with schools, communities and government\. Her research has informed approaches to social\-emotional learning, school\-based mental health promotion and youth wellbeing at provincial, national and international levels\.

As interim Co\-Directors, Drs\. Gadermann and Oberle will provide administrative and academic leadership for HELP, continuing to advance its research programs and strategic priorities\. They will work closely with senior leadership to guide operations, advance planning and decision\-making, and support faculty and staff\. They will advance HELP’s commitment to fostering an inclusive, respectful and high\-performing research environment\.

Please join us in thanking Drs\. Gadermann and Oberle for their interim leadership\.
