**Update Oct\. 17:** Cheque mailing has resumed but delays are expected, as the national strike has moved to rotating strikes\. [Learn more \(CWL login\)](https://finance.share.ubc.ca/buy-pay/SitePages/Updates-regarding-university-cheque-mailing-on-campus.aspx "Updates regarding university cheque mailing on campus | UBC Finance Portal") 

UBC Finance advise faculty and staff to expect delays in mail delivery, including cheque delivery for payroll and suppliers, due to the [Canada Post strike](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/our-company/news-and-media/corporate-news/negotiations/2025-09-25-cupw-has-launched-a-national-strike-that-will-cause-postal-delays) launched on September 25, 2025\.

UBC employees who receive payment by cheque can find information on updating their payment information to electronic funds transfer or picking up cheques in person is available on the UBC Finance website\.

Information on supplier payments and contact information is also available on the UBC Finance website\.

[Learn more](https://finance.ubc.ca/news/disruptions-to-university-cheque-mailing-due-to-canada-post-job-action/ " Disruptions to university cheque mailing due to Canada Post job action | UBC Finance")
