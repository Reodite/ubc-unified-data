Are you a faculty member, postdoc or PhD student interested in researching in Germany?

The Office of the Vice\-Provost International invites you to an information session and networking event on **February 28, 2023**\.

Join in\-person or online to learn about the German research landscape, opportunities, and funding programs in support of research stays in Germany and collaborations with German partners\.
