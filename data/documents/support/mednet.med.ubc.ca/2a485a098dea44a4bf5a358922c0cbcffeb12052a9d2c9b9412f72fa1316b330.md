[Rick Kornelsen](https://www.mbfunerals.com/obituaries/kornelson/richard "Richard Rick Kornelsen Obituary") passed away on August 3, 2025\.

Mr\. Kornelsen was a current staff member, working as a Research Technician in the Djavad Mowafaghian Centre for Brain Health\. A [member of the 25 Year Club](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/21-faculty-of-medicine-staff-inducted-into-ubcs-25-year-club/ "21 Faculty of Medicine staff inducted into UBC’s 25 Year Club - UBC Faculty of Medicine"), he first joined UBC in 1997 and worked with various labs in the former Brain Research Centre and Pacific Parkinson’s Research Centre\.

Read a [tribute to Mr\. Kornelsen](https://www.centreforbrainhealth.ca/news/remembering-rick-kornelsen/ "Remembering Rick Kornelsen - Djavad Mowafaghian Centre for Brain Health") from the Djavad Mowafaghian Centre for Brain Health\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Thursday, August 14, 2025](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2025/08/28/rick-kornelsen/ "Rick Kornelsen Flag Lowering | UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
