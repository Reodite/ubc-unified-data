The UBC community is invited to comment on proposed amendments to the Disaster Management Policy \(SC10\)\.

The proposed amendments will modernize the policy and ensure it aligns with industry best practices and UBC’s current environment, including establishing a comprehensive Emergency Management and Continuity Planning Program\. 

All members of the UBC community are encouraged to provide their comments by **Monday, July 20, 2026**\.

[Learn more](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policy-consultation-proposed-amendments-disaster-management-policy-sc10 "Policy Consultation – Proposed Amendments to the Disaster Management Policy (SC10)")
