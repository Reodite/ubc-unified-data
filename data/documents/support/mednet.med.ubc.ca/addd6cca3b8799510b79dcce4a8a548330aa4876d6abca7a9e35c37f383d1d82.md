Faculty and staff who work with suppliers can access Financial Operations training workshops offered by the UBC Finance team on Workplace Learning \(WPL\)\.

Join an upcoming session of one of two new workshops, or explore the range of sessions available\.

-   [**Setting up a Supplier: Foundational Overview**](https://wpl.ubc.ca/browse/financial-operations/courses/setting-up-a-supplier-foundational-overview) addresses all the requirements for correctly setting suppliers up in Workday, including the reasons behind the requirements\.
-   If you ever need to create Payments without Invoices, such as reimbursements for guest speakers, visiting professors or payments of awards to sub\-recipients, register for [**Supplier Invoice Request Module: “No\-invoice” Solutions**](https://wpl.ubc.ca/browse/financial-operations/courses/supplier-invoice-request-module-no-invoice-solutions)\. This session covers all requirements necessary for creating and making payments without invoices\.
