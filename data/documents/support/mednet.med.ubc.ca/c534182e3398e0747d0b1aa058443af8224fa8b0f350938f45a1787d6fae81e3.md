[Dr\. Bernard Bressler](https://vancouversunandprovince.remembering.ca/obituary/bernard-bressler-1093406695 "Dr. Bernard Bressler Obituary | Vancouver Sun and Province") passed away on December 29, 2025\.

A distinguished researcher and leader, Dr\. Bressler was a Professor Emeritus in the former Department of Anatomy \(now the Department of Cellular &amp; Physiological Sciences\), where he previously served as Head\. He served as the Faculty’s Associate Dean, Research and Graduate Studies, and as UBC’s Vice President, Research\.

Dr\. Bressler also held leadership roles at Vancouver Coastal Health, including serving as as Vice\-President, Research, and oversaw the creation of the Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute \(VCHRI\), where he later served as Executive Director\.

In recognition of his many contributions, Dr\. Bressler was honoured with the Life Sciences BC Lifetime Achievement Award, and was inducted as a Fellow of the Canadian Academy of Health Sciences\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Bressler from VCHRI](https://www.vchri.ca/announcement/2026/01/11/celebrating-life-and-distinguished-career-dr-bernard-h-bernie-bressler)\.

The B\.C\., Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Wednesday, February 25, 2025](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2026/03/bernard-bernie-bressler/ "Bernard Bressler Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies &amp; Events") in remembrance\.
