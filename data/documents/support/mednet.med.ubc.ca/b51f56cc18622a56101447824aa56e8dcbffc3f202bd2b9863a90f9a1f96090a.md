[Dr\. Keith G\. Dawson](https://www.legacy.com/ca/obituaries/theglobeandmail/name/keith-dawson-obituary?id=59261718 "Keith Dawson Obituary - The Globe and Mail") passed away on August 14, 2025\.

A respected endocrinologist who devoted his professional life to the care of people living with diabetes, Dr\. Dawson was a Professor Emeritus in in the Department of Medicine\. He gave many years of service to the Canadian Diabetes Association and was devoted to supporting physicians working with remote First Nations communities\.

In recognition of his contributions, Dr\. Dawson received the Gerald S\. Wong Award and the Prime Minister’s Distinguished Service Award\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Friday, September 5, 2025](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2025/09/19/keith-g-dawson/ "Keith G. Dawson Flag Lowering | UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
