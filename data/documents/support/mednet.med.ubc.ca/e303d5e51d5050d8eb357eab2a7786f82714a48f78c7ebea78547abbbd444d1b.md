Find information and resources to celebrate extraordinary faculty and staff members\.

---

Whether you lead a single team member or oversee a large team of professionals, this guide will equip you with the tools and knowledge needed to cultivate a culture of meaningful recognition that empowers employees and fuels organizational success\.

Aligned with the first of the four [Organization strategies](https://stratplan.med.ubc.ca/pillars-and-strategies/organization-goals-and-strategies/), this guide supports the Faculty’s efforts to “*promote engagement and well\-being by fostering respectful and supportive learning and work environments where individuals are acknowledged for their contributions*”\.

This guide explores the fundamental principles of employee recognition, delves into the various types and methods of recognition, and outlines practical steps for creating a culture of appreciation within your team\. The guide provides insights on key topics such as:

-   [Manager\-to\-Employee Recognition](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/FoM-Recognition-Guide.pdf#page=12)
