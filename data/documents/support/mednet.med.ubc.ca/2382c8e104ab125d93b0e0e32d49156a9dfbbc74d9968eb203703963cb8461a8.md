[UBC Faculty of Medicine 1950–2025: 75 years of excellence across British Columbia](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/1_UBC-Crest-Baseline_Standard_RGB_75thAnniversary.png)

2025 marks the 75\-year anniversary of the Faculty of Medicine\.

To commemorate this extraordinary milestone, an anniversary signifier \(emblem\) has been created to honour 75 years of excellence in medical education, research and healthcare innovation at UBC\.

The signifier is available in a variety of formats and can be applied to a range of assets, including banners, memorabilia, letterheads — and even your email signature\.

Before using the signifier, be sure to review the [usage guidelines](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/UBC-Faculty-of-Medicine-Signifier-Guidelines_V1.0.pdf)\.

[Learn more &amp; download the signifier](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/communications/templates/anniversary-signifier/ "Communications: Anniversary signifier - MedNet")

Further information on the Faculty’s anniversary celebrations will be announced in the coming months\.
