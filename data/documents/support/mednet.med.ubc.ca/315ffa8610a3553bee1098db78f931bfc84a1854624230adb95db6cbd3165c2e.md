UBC Vancouver research excellence clusters are invited to apply to the 2026/27 Grants for Catalyzing Research Clusters \(GCRC\) Competition\. 

The competition is open to new and returning clusters of research excellence in all disciplines on the Vancouver campus and affiliated hospital sites, with a limit of three cycles of GCRC funding\. Cluster leads must submit a notice of intent by **November 10, 2025**\. Full applications are due January 8, 2026\.

Groups interested in applying are encouraged to [register for one of two information sessions](https://research.ubc.ca/vpri-competitions-initiatives/research-excellence-clusters/grants-catalyzing-research-clusters/gcrc "GCRC Info Sessions registration | UBC Research + Innovation"), taking place on **October 17 and 21**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://research.ubc.ca/vpri-competitions-initiatives/research-excellence-clusters/grants-catalyzing-research-clusters "Grants for Catalyzing Research Clusters | UBC Research + Innovation")
