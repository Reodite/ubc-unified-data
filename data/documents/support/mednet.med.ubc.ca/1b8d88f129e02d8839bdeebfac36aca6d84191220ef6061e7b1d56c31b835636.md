A UBC tradition, the annual Welcome Back BBQ offers faculty and staff a complementary BBQ lunch, live entertainment and an opportunity to build connections with colleagues and community partners\.

## UBC Okanagan

-   **Date:** Tuesday, August 26, 2025
    -   11:30 am–1 pm: Welcome Back BBQ and Info Fair
-   **Location:** [Central Courtyard](https://maps.ok.ubc.ca/?code=OPOI10013), UBC Okanagan

*More information and the RSVP will be distributed in early August via email to faculty and staff\.*

[Learn more](https://hr.ok.ubc.ca/2025/06/27/celebrate-the-start-of-a-new-academic-year-at-the-2025-faculty-and-staff-welcome-back-bbq-info-fair-in-ubcos-20th-year/ "Celebrate the Start of a New Academic Year at the 2025 Faculty and Staff Welcome Back BBQ Info Fair — in UBCO’s 20th Year! - UBC Okanagan Human Resources")

## UBC Vancouver

-   **Date:** Wednesday, September 10, 2025
    -   Time Slot 1: 11 am–12:30 pm
    -   Time Slot 2: 12:30 pm–2 pm
-   **Location:** [Flag Pole Plaza](https://www.maps.ubc.ca/?code=VPOI10007 "Flag Pole Plaza - UBC Maps"), UBC Vancouver

Register by **Wednesday, August 20** through your department contact\.
