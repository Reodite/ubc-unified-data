**Abstract Submissions Now Closed\.**

We are now accepting abstracts for **research projects related to precision health** for the Symposium poster session\. Clinical care delivery projects not primarily designed as research are not accepted\. Poster presenters will be required to discuss their research project during the designated poster presentation period \(breaks, lunchtime\) at the Symposium\.

-   Abstracts will be accepted for in\-person poster presentations only
-   Abstracts will be reviewed and up to 48 will be accepted for poster presentation\. 
-   Posters are judged by post\-doctoral fellows and faculty members based on their content and presentation\.
-   Poster awards will be announced at the end of the Symposium\.

**Please review the Guidelines below before submitting an abstract\.** 

View:

Expand All

Collapse All

Each abstract should contain:

-   Title
-   Authors
-   Author affiliations
-   Abstract \(max\. 300 words\)
-   Funding source
-   Word count

*Additional abstract information*

-   Only \.doc or \.docx files will be accepted
-   12 pt Times New Roman preferred
-   No references
-   Multiple paragraph abstracts \(with or without headings\) will be merged into one paragraph, and the headings removed \(if applicable\)
-   Abstracts containing “results will be discussed/determined” will be rejected
