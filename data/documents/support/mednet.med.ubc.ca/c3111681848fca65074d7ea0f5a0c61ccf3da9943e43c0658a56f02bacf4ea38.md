Faculty and staff at UBC Vancouver are invited to submit proposals for Small Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(TLEF\) Innovation projects by **Thursday, November 13 at 3 pm**\.

Small TLEF Innovation projects are one\- to three\-year projects that are intended to support experimentation with pedagogical approaches that have strong potential to enhance learning through grassroots projects\. Projects are eligible to receive up to $50,000 over the life of the project\. Small TLEF Innovation projects should advance UBC’s Strategic Plan and be aligned with one or more of the TLEF priorities for 2026/27\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://tlef.ubc.ca/application/ "Application - Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund")

## **Consultation sessions**

For more information and support, register for the [TLEF general information session](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/tlef-online-general-information-consultation-session/) on **September 25**\.

The Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology \(CTLT\) will also host a series of [drop\-in TLEF consultation sessions](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/event-category/tlef/) to provide feedback and assistance to potential TLEF applicants\.
