[Dr\. Lukas Klopper](http://www.ominecamedicalclinic.com/ "In Loving Memory of Dr. Lukas Klopper - Omineca Medical Clinic") passed away May 1, 2023\.

Dr\. Klopper was a current Clinical Instructor with the Department of Family Practice\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Friday, September 22, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/09/28/lukas-klopper/ "Lukas Klopper Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
