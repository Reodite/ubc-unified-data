[Dr\. Mary Louisa Miller](https://vancouversunandprovince.remembering.ca/obituary/mary-miller-1089389692) passed away on January 28, 2024\.

A clinical assistant professor emerita, Dr\. Miller served in the Department of Anesthesiology, Pharmacology and Therapeutics\.

Dr\. Miller was one of the first women in Canada to obtain a fellowship in anesthesia, followed by a nearly 40\-year career at Vancouver General Hospital\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Thursday, March 28, 2024](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2024/04/05/mary-louisa-miller/) in remembrance\.
