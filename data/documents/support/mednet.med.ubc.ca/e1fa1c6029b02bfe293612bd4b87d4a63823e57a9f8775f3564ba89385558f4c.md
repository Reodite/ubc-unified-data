Join UBC’s Advanced Research Computing \(ARC\) team for an information session on **September 26**\.

This one\-hour virtual session is designed for those new to UBC or interested in learning more about its services\. The session will introduce ARC’s core offerings, including high\-performance computing, secure data storage, cybersecurity, and privacy consultation\.

Participants will receive a practical overview of the tools, infrastructure, and expert guidance available through ARC\. The session is ideal for anyone looking to explore ARC for the first time or gain a clearer understanding of its role in supporting research\.

-   **Date:** Friday, September 26, 2025 from 10–11 am
-   **Location:** Online

[Learn more &amp; register](https://arc.ubc.ca/events/ubc-advanced-research-computing-information-session "UBC Advanced Research Computing Information Session | UBC Advanced Research Computing")
