The 2025 Campus as a Living Lab Fund Competition is now open\. UBC Vancouver faculty and staff, apply for seed funding to co\-develop interdisciplinary living lab projects that respond to global sustainability challenges grounded in research, real\-world application, and institutional learning\.

Projects can be environmental, ecological and technical, or bold, creative and interdisciplinary, exploring the social, cultural and systemic aspects of sustainability\. A total of up to $500,000 in seed funding is available through two project streams\.

Submit a notice of intent by **Tuesday, September 30**, with full applications to be submitted by November 28\.

Interested applicants can also [register for the September 16 information session](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6rOhfqJAqk8n0bk "Information Session - 2025 UBC Vancouver Campus as a Living Lab Fund Competition")\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://livinglabs.ubc.ca/programs-and-initiatives/cll-fund-competition "2025 UBC Vancouver CLL Fund Competition | UBC Campus as a Living Lab")
