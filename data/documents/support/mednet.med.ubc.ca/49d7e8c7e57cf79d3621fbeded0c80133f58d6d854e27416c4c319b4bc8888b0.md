Hear from UBC’s newest University Killam Professors, including Dr\. Marco Marra \(Medical Genetics\), in a virtual event on **Wednesday, November 12**\.

In this time of global uncertainty and unrest, the role of research is more crucial than ever\. Hosted by alumni UBC and the Office of the Vice\-President, Research &amp; Innovation, the event will feature UBC’s three newest University Killam Professors — the highest honor UBC can confer on faculty members — as they explore the local and worldwide impact of their innovative research in biological, socio\-political and legal systems\.

Attend this illuminating session to discover how their research is both being shaped by, and could help respond to, the shifting global landscape\.

-   **Date**: Wednesday, November 12, 2025 from 5:30–6:45 pm
-   **Location**: Online

[Learn more &amp; register](https://alumni.ubc.ca/event/challenges-changes-and-research-insights-from-ubcs-university-killam-professors/ "Challenges, changes, and research: Insights from UBC’s University Killam Professors - alumni UBC")
