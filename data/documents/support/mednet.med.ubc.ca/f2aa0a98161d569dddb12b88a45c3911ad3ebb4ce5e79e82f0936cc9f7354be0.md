The UBC Medicine Spring Gala is an annual charity event organized by and featuring UBC medical students\. Now in its 31st year, the gala brings together students from all four years of the UBC medical program to showcase their artistic talents outside the medical realm, through performance and visual artworks\.

Faculty and staff, alumni, family and members of the community are all welcome to attend\. Tickets start at $20, with all profits donated to Covenant House Vancouver\.

-   **Date:** Saturday, April 12, 2025 from 7 pm
-   **Location:** [Chan Centre for Performing Arts](https://maps.ubc.ca/?code=CHAN), 6265 Crescent Road, UBC Vancouver

[Learn more &amp; purchase tickets](https://www.ubcmedspringgala.com/ "UBC Medicine Spring Gala")
