[Celebrate Learning Week 2026](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/CLW-2026_960x540.jpg)

Join the 17th annual **Celebrate Learning Week** online and in\-person from **May 4–8, 2026**\. UBC faculty, staff and learners are invited to join this cross\-campus, week\-long teaching and learning forum, collaboratively hosted by UBCV and UBCO\.

This year’s showcase invites the UBC community to pause, reflect, and celebrate learning in all its forms\. Centered on people and shared experiences, it honours learning that happens across seasons and spaces—inside classrooms, out in the field, and throughout everyday life\.

Explore the full schedule, or see featured event highlights below\.

[See events schedule &amp; register](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/2026-celebrate-learning-week/ "Celebrate Learning Week 2026")

---

## Featured events

### **May 4 \| Keynote featuring Dr\. Mia Zamora — [The Future of Learning Must Invite Us to Imagine: Freedom Dreaming for Liberatory Education](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/keynote-the-future-of-learning-must-invite-us-to-imagine-freedom-dreaming-for-liberatory-education/)**

Join this virtual keynote session exploring the role of imagination in education and its impact on how we shape the future\.

### **May 5 \| Workshop —** **[Building Capacity for the IBPOC Teaching and Learning Community: Celebrating Little Transformations as a Bridge Towards Large\-Scale Change](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/building-capacity-for-the-ibpoc-teaching-and-learning-community-celebrating-little-transformations-as-a-bridge-towards-large-scale-change/)**

The session is dedicated to creating educational, networking, resource sharing and wellness opportunities for self\-identifying Indigenous, Black and People of Colour \(IBPOC\)\.

### **May 5 \| **GenAI panel —** [Making the Tech Happen for AI in Teaching](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/panel-making-the-tech-happen-for-ai-in-teaching/)**

What does it actually take to bring an AI\-powered teaching tool to life? In this panel discussion, faculty teams share the behind\-the\-scenes stories of developing AI tools for their courses – the ideas, the challenges, and the moments it all came together\.

### **May 6 \| Faculty &amp; student panel — [Journeys in Teaching Innovation: Faculty and Instructor Stories of Idea to Impact](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/journeys-in-teaching-innovation-faculty-and-instructor-stories-of-idea-to-impact-panel/)**

Focusing on real experiences at UBC, this session takes you behind the scenes of the innovation journey, offering practical insights, inspiration and a clearer picture of how to begin \(or continue\) your own journey from idea to impact\.
