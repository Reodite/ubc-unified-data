Nominations are now open for the Sherrold Haddad Excellence in Medicine Award Program\.

The award offers funding to support research in one of the identified priority areas for a faculty member who performs clinical duties at Surrey Memorial Hospital, Czorny Alzheimer Centre or Jim Pattison Outpatient Care and Surgery Centre\.

Each award totals $120,000, distributed over two years, and may be used to cover clinical buy\-out time as well as research expenses\. This initiative is made possible by a donation from the Haddad family to Surrey Hospitals Foundation, which honours Sherrold Haddad’s legacy and his dedication to supporting physicians whose innovative research is transforming patient care\.

Completed application packages are due by **12 pm on Friday, June 12, 2026**\. Please contact [ocfa\.med@ubc\.ca](mailto:ocfa.med@ubc.ca) with any questions\.
