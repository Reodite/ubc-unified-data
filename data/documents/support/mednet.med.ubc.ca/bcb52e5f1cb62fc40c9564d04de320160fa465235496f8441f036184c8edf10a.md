This February, explore ways to fit physical activity into your day during Move UBC month\.

Small changes can add up to big impacts — moving more can help improve mental health and resilience, boost your creativity and productivity, improve your sleep, and even help our planet\!

Explore a range of in\-person and virtual events to find great ways to get moving in ways that are empowering, comfortable, and motivating for you\. Learn more at [move\.ubc\.ca](http://www.move.ubc.ca/ "Move UBC | UBC Wellbeing")\.
