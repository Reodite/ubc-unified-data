A message from Sharmila Anandasabapathy, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, UBC\.

---

[https://www\.med\.ubc\.ca/news/faculty\-of\-medicine\-awards\-2026/](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/faculty-of-medicine-awards-2026/)[2026 Faculty of Medicine Awards, Celebrating an extraordinary group of people](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/FoMAwards-2026_MedNet-Banner_960x320-940x313.jpg)

I am delighted to announce the recipients of the 2026 Faculty of Medicine Awards\.

These awards celebrate faculty and staff who have made exceptional contributions to the Faculty — helping to strengthen our community and advance our values and our vision of transforming health for everyone\.

The 2026 award categories include:

-   Bill and Marilyn Webber Lifetime Achievement Award
-   Awards for Excellence in Mentoring Early Career Faculty
-   Applegarth Staff Service Awards
-   Dean’s Staff Awards
-   Clinical Faculty Awards
-   CME\-CPD Awards
-   UBC Killam Teaching Prizes *\(previously announced\)*
-   UBC Killam Research Prizes *\(previously announced\)*
-   Distinguished Achievement Awards *\(previously announced\)*

[2026 Award Recipients](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/faculty-of-medicine-awards-2026/)

Please join me in congratulating all of our 2026 award recipients, who will be formally recognized at the Faculty of Medicine Awards Reception this fall\.
