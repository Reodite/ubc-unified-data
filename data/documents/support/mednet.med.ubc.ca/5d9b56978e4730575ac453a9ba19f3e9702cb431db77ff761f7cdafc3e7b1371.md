​How you get to campus shapes daily life at UBC Okanagan\.

Share your experiences in the 2025 Transportation Survey, and you’ll help guide decisions that can make commuting easier, more affordable and more sustainable for all UBCO faculty and staff\.

Complete the survey by **October 10, 2025**\.

[Learn more and take the survey](https://campusplanning.ok.ubc.ca/planning-and-development/plans-and-policies/transportation-planning/transportation-reports-studies/ "Transportation Reports and Studies | UBC Okanagan Campus Planning")
