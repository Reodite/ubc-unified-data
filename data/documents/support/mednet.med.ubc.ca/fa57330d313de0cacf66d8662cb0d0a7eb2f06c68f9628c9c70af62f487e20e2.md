Do you know how to ensure your UBC office or workstation is safe? Find out by completing the annual Worksite Safety Inspection survey\.

---

**The 2026 survey has now closed – thanks to everyone who responded\!**

Each January, **all Faculty of Medicine faculty, staff and paid students who work in office/administrative spaces**, including those at hospital sites, are asked to complete a short survey about their UBC office or workstation\.

You do not need to complete the survey for laboratory locations or home offices\. If you work at multiple locations, you only need to complete the survey once\.

## About the survey

This annual survey is part of the Faculty of Medicine Health &amp; Safety team’s Worksite Safety Inspection Initiative\.

This year, input from 700 completed surveys from over 70 different buildings helped make our worksites safer for everyone\.

The survey consists of 11 quick questions and only takes a few minutes to complete\.

Questions include checking that aisles and doorways by your workspace are free of obstructions, that power outlets are not overloaded, and confirming that you are aware of your local emergency procedures\.

All workers \(including faculty, staff and paid students\) who have a worksite on UBC Vancouver campus or any UBC site, institute or centre are asked to complete the survey\. This includes UBC staff at Health Authority sites\.

Please note that wet laboratories have separate safety procedures and the survey does not need to be completed for these sites\.

You do not need to complete the survey for your home office and you only need to complete the survey once\. If you work at multiple locations on UBC premises, or work at a shared or communal workstation, choose one office/workstation to complete\.

Inspecting worksites regularly is a [WorkSafeBC requirement](https://www.worksafebc.com/en/law-policy/occupational-health-safety/searchable-ohs-regulation/ohs-regulation/part-03-rights-and-responsibilities#SectionNumber:3.3 "Occupational Health and Safety Programs | WorksafeBC"), so please complete the survey each year\.

The Local Safety Team in your area will follow up with respective supervisors to ensure all locations have been inspected\.

The survey link will be open until **Friday, January 30, 2026**\.

For some issues, resolution is simple and instructions are provided within the survey\. For other issues, the Local Safety Team in your area will connect with you to ensure the hazard is resolved\.

All questions can be directed to the Health &amp; Safety team at [fom\.safety@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.safety@ubc.ca)\.
