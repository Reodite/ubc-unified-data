Dr\. Sherrie Kelly, Senior Editor, *The Lancet,* Canada presented a Workshop into the behind\-the\-scenes world of editorial decision\-making at a high\-impact medical journal\-*The Lancet*, and shared what editors are looking for in paper submission, and what the common pitfalls can be for prospective authors\.

Dr\. Kelly joined *The Lancet* in 2024 as the first Canadian\-based editor at The Lancet Group\. For the past 20 years, she collaborated with over 30 low\- and middle\-income countries to better invest in their HIV, TB, malaria, maternal\-child health, and COVID responses\. Her research interests also extend to climate health and health systems strengthening\. Before joining *The Lancet*, she served as a Deputy Editor with JIAS, and scientific researcher at UNAIDS in Switzerland and at health institutions in Australia, Denmark, and Canada\. She was honoured to have been a keynote speaker at IAS2019 and a speaker at AIDS2020\. Dr\. Kelly was awarded PhD in disease modelling in 2017 from Monash University \(Australia\) and holds an MSc in biochemistry and medical genetics and a BSc Honours in medical genetics\. She is based in Ottawa\.

The workshop was held at UBC\-Vancouver in May 2024\. A video recording of the session and slides \(in PDF\) are at the links below\.
