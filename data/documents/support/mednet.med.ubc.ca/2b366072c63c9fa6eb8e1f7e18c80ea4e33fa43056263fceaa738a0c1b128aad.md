Nominations are now open for the 2026 John McNeill Excellence in Health Research Mentorship Award\.

The John McNeill Excellence in Health Research Mentorship Award recognizes outstanding mentorship by faculty members at any stage in their academic career in any of UBC’s health\-related disciplines and who have served a minimum of two continuous years at UBC’s Vancouver Campus\.

We invite you to consider colleagues who could be eligible for this distinction\. Send completed packages to [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca) for review and internal adjudication where applicable by end of day **Friday, September 25, 2026**\.

If you have questions, please contact [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca)\.
