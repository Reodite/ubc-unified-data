A message from Sharmila Anandasabapathy, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, UBC\.

---

I am pleased to announce the reappointment of Dr\. Zu\-hua Gao as Head, Department of Pathology &amp; Laboratory Medicine for a five\-year term effective to June 30, 2031\.

Dr\. Gao is a professor in the Department of Pathology &amp; Laboratory Medicine\. He also serves as Provincial Program Medical Director for Pathology and Laboratory Medicine at BC Cancer\.

An internationally recognized academic pathologist, Dr\. Gao’s clinical expertise is in gastrointestinal and liver pathology\. His research spans cancer biology, biomedical engineering, digital pathology and artificial intelligence, with more than 190 peer\-reviewed publications in leading medical and scientific journals\. Dr\. Gao is also a dedicated educator whose textbooks have supported the education of medical students, residents and practicing physicians worldwide\.

[Dr\. Zu\-hua Gao](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/Zu-huaGao_400x400.jpg)

Dr\. Gao is a Fellow of the Canadian Academy of Health Sciences and the Royal Society of Medicine \(UK\), and was recently elected to the Board of Directors of the Canadian Academy of Health Sciences\.

In his first term as Head, Dr\. Gao led significant organizational and academic transformation within the Department\. Under his leadership, the Department developed and implemented its first five\-year strategic plan, strengthened academic career development and succession planning for clinical and research faculty, and enhanced engagement with the Department’s 320\+ distributed clinical faculty members throughout the province\. Dr\. Gao also led the Department’s revitalized undergraduate, graduate, residency and fellowship programs and expanded its research capacity\.

Building on this strong foundation, in his second term Dr\. Gao will focus on advancing research excellence through strategic recruitment and support for emerging investigators, expanding clinician\-scientist development and strengthening education and mentorship in the Department\. He will continue the Department’s work accelerating innovation in precision medicine, digital pathology and artificial intelligence, while fostering an equitable and inclusive culture, and deepening partnerships across UBC, B\.C\.’s health system and the broader scientific community\.

Please join me in congratulating Dr\. Gao on his reappointment\.
