If you have an innovative idea for embedding sustainable health and wellbeing strategic initiatives within teaching, learning and research environments, apply for funding up to $5,000 through the UBC Wellbeing Strategic Initiative Fund\.

This new pilot funding stream is open to UBC faculty, staff and students\. Applications will be accepted until **January 22, 2024**, or until available funds have been allocated\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/ubc-wellbeing-strategic-initiative-fund "UBC Wellbeing Strategic Initiative Fund")
