Join the [UBC Research Security](https://researchsecurity.ubc.ca/) team for a session on research security, the new Policy on Sensitive Technology Research and Affiliations of Concern \(STRAC\) released by the Government of Canada and how it relates to your research work\.

These sessions are specific to researchers in the Faculty of Medicine and affiliated centres &amp; institutes\. The content will be the same across the sessions, so you need only attend once\.

-   **May 9, 1–2 pm**
-   **May 23, 11 am–12 noon**

[Learn more &amp; register](https://researchsecurity.ubc.ca/events/navigating-research-security-policies-federal-funding-faculty-medicine-1 "Navigating Research Security Policies for Federal Funding - Faculty of Medicine | UBC Research Security")
