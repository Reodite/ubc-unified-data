UBC Vancouver research excellence clusters are invited to apply to the 2024/25 Grants for Catalyzing Research Clusters \(GCRC\) Competition\. 

The competition is open to new and returning clusters of research excellence in all disciplines on the Vancouver campus\. Cluster leads must submit a notice of intent by **November 1, 2023**\.

This year, a number of [revisions to the GCRC program](https://research.ubc.ca/vpri-competitions-initiatives/research-excellence-clusters/gcrc/revisions "GCRC Revisions | UBC Research + Innovation") have been made, following an external review and in consultation with the UBC research community\.

Groups interested in applying are encouraged to [register for an information session](https://research.ubc.ca/content/gcrc-202122-info-sessions "GCRC 2024/25 Info Sessions | UBC Research + Innovation"), held on **October 16 &amp; 17**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://research.ubc.ca/vpri-competitions-initiatives/research-excellence-clusters/grants-catalyzing-research-clusters "Grants for Catalyzing Research Clusters | UBC Research + Innovation")

*GCRC competitions are open to clusters led by researchers at UBC Vancouver\. Clusters led by researchers at UBC Okanagan are supported through the [Eminence program](https://research.ok.ubc.ca/funding-support/internal-funding/ "Internal Funding | UBCO Research and Innovation")\.*
