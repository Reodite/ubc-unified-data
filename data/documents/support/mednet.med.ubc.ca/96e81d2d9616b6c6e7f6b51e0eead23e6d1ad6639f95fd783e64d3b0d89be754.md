Do you have an idea to advance wellbeing at UBC? Faculty at Vancouver and Okanagan campuses are invited to apply to the Wellbeing Fellows Program by March 16\.

The new program offers up to $20,000 per year for two years to bring a wellbeing project to life, with ongoing support and connection through cohort meetings\.

Priority themes for the 2026 program include:

-   Nurturing a strong &amp; vibrant UBC community
-   Systems &amp; culture level improvements
-   Integrating wellbeing into teaching &amp; learning
-   Digital wellbeing &amp; accessible technologies

Submit applications by **5pm on Monday, March 16, 2026**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/wellbeing-fellows-program "Wellbeing Fellows Program | UBC Wellbeing")
