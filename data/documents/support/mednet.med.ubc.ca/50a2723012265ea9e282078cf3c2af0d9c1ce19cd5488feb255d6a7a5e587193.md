The Edwin S\.H\. Leong Healthy Aging Program is presenting two talks in January by Dr\. David Almeida, Professor of Human Development and Family Studies and a faculty member of the Center for Healthy Aging at Penn State University\.

## Healthy Aging Public Lecture Series: “Finally, Some Good News about Stress\! The Benefits of \(Some\) Daily Challenge”

On **January 23 at 6 pm**, join the Healthy Aging Public Lecture Series for “Finally, Some Good News about Stress\! The Benefits of \(Some\) Daily Challenge”\. Join the public lecture online or in\-person to learn how some daily challenge can benefit our health\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://healthyaging.med.ubc.ca/events/event/healthy-aging-public-lecture-series)

---

## Research talk: “Health as a Daily Experience: Lessons from 42,243 Days of US Adults”

On **January 24 at 12 pm**, Dr\. Almeida will give a research talk on “Health as a Daily Experience: Lessons from 42,243 Days of US Adults”\. Attend in person at the Rudy North Lecture Theatre in the Centre for Brain Health or over Zoom\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://healthyaging.med.ubc.ca/events/event/research-talk-by-dr-david-almeida)
