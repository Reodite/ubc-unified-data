UBC Continuing Professional Development \(UBC CPD\) has launched a refreshed strategic plan\.

Developed in collaboration with stakeholders, the UBC CPD Strategic Plan 2023–2027 advances the Faculty of Medicine’s four pillars of education, research, partnership and organization through program development, systems\-focused collaborations, applied research and living the values of a learning organization\.

Explore the new plan to read about UBC CPD and the division’s plans for the next five years\.
