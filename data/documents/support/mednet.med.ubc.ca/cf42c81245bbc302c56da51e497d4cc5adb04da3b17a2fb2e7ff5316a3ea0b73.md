The 2026\-27 Community\-University Engagement Support \(CUES\) Fund is now open\. Get up to $25,000 to support community\-university partnerships that benefit communities across the province and advance collaborative research, teaching and learning\.

The fund is open to B\.C\.\-based community partners and UBC faculty, staff and students\. Interested applicants are invited to submit a Notice of Intent by **Monday, September 28, 2026**\.

Paid directly to community partners, CUES funding removes financial barriers and prioritizes reciprocal, inclusive engagement so all communities — especially those that have been and continue to be underserved, marginalized or excluded — can benefit\. Since 2018, the fund has awarded $4,691,930 to 172 different community groups — including 55 Indigenous communities and organizations — and university partners from 18 different faculties across UBC Vancouver and Okanagan campuses\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/news/2026-community-university-engagement-support-fund/ "Apply for the 2026-27 Community-University Engagement Support Fund Program")
