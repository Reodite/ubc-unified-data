These appointments are part\-time, for a specified, limited term no longer than one year at a time\.

There is no implication that the appointee will be considered for any further appointment of this or any other kind on the expiration of the specified term\. Reappointments can be made subject to the terms and conditions of Policy AP4 – [Faculty Term Appointments](https://universitycounsel-2015.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/08/Faculty-Term-Appointments-Policy_AP4.pdf) \(pdf, formerly Policy \#42\)\.

These appointees are members of the [Faculty Association](http://www.facultyassociation.ubc.ca/)\.
