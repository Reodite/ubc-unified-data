Learn more about the Faculty of Medicine Joint Occupational Health and Safety Committee \(JOHSC\) and Local Safety Teams \(LST\)\.

---

The Faculty of Medicine Health &amp; Safety Program is governed by multiple Joint Occupational Health and Safety Committees \(JOHSCs\):

-   [UBC Children’s &amp; Women’s Hospital JOHSC](mailto:fom.safety@ubc.ca)
-   [UBC Point Grey Campus JOHSC](mailto:fom.safety@ubc.ca)
-   [UBC St\. Paul’s Hospital JOHSC](mailto:fom.safety@ubc.ca)
-   [UBC Surrey Memorial Hospital JOHSC](mailto:fom.safety@ubc.ca)
-   [UBC Vancouver General Hospital JOHSC](mailto:fom.safety@ubc.ca)

The purpose of the joint committees are to:

-   Recommend policies and procedures to the Dean that are designed to **protect and improve health, safety, and personal security** of all Faculty of Medicine faculty, staff and students, and ensure compliance with applicable WorkSafeBC regulations\.
-   **Review safety concerns** regarding corrective measures to address unsafe working conditions and confirm that corrective measures are effectively implemented\.
-   **Collaborate with Faculty of Medicine Local Safety Teams** \(see contact info below\) to address local health and safety issues that have not been resolved at the local level\.
-   Provide a forum for faculty, staff and student representatives to **collaborate on identifying and resolving health and safety issues** in the workplace\.

For further information on the Faculty of Medicine JOHSCs, including contact lists of members and recent minutes, click [here](https://safetycommittees.ubc.ca/johsc/joint-occupational-health-safety-committees/provost-vp-academic-a-to-l/zmedicine/)\.

## Faculty of Medicine Local Safety Teams

Local Safety Teams support the Faculty of Medicine health and safety program by providing site specific safety information\. The Faculty of Medicine has a variety of work groups and workplaces within its portfolio, with a diverse group of functions and unique hazard potential\. These teams meet regularly and collaborate with the Faculty of Medicine JOHSC\.
