## Procedure

For the NSERC 2025 Research Tools &amp; Instruments competition, please adhere to the following deadlines and procedures:

1.  Your application package must be submitted to the Dean’s Office or affiliated hospital research office by **Thursday, October 16, 2025** for Faculty approval\. Please email it to the Faculty [signature contact for your site](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/signature-process/#signaturecontacts)\. Note that Evelina Tolstykh \([fom\.grantapprovals@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.grantapprovals@ubc.ca)\) is the contact for Faculty approval for those located at the UBC Point Grey campus\. See below for a complete list of the required items\. 
2.  After Faculty approval, your documents will be forwarded to ORS by October 20, 2025\.
3.  Your electronic copy must be submitted via NSERC’s on\-line system no later than **12:00pm on Friday, October 24th, 2025**\.
4.  ORS will electronically approve your application via NSERC’s on\-line system for submission to NSERC\.

**Please remember that you can continue working on your research proposal up until you submit via the Research Portal on October 24th, even after you have submitted your application package for Faculty approval\.**

## Required Documents

*Required documents to be submitted to the FoM for the NSERC Research Tools &amp; Instruments grant:*

1.  Completed [Research Project Information Form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/required-documents/) including the following signatures:
    1.  the Principal Investigator
    2.  Department Head or School Director
    3.  Research Centre Director \(if using research space within a Senate\-Approved Research Centre, identified with an asterisk on the [Academic &amp; Research Units](http://med.ubc.ca/about/departments-schools-centres/) page\)
    4.  Life Sciences Institute Director \(if using research space within the Life Sciences Building, but excludes researchers using space within the Centre for Blood Research\)
2.  If you have any internal or external co\-applicants, please also submit an [ORS signature page](http://ors.ubc.ca/sites/ors.ubc.ca/files/documents/Internal+ExternalCo-PIsignature%20page_20180115_template_0_0.pdf)\. If co\-applicants are within UBC, they will need to determine with their Department/Faculty whether they need to review and sign the application\. External authorized institution signature is required for co\-applicants outside of UBC\.
3.  Identification page \(page 1 of the application\)
4.  Co\-applicants page
5.  Summary of Proposal page
6.  Proposed expenditures and budget justification pages
7.  Quotations, if applicable \(per RTI guidelines, provide two \(2\) recent quotations for any items or any systems costing more than $25,000 before taxes\. If the required number of quotations cannot be submitted, you must provide a justification in the Budget Justification section\.\)
8.  If there are other organizations providing financial contributions, written confirmation of these must be submitted to ORS\. An email from the organization providing the funds will suffice\.

Please also review the ORS website for the NSERC\-specific [Agency Application Requirements &amp; Procedures](https://ors.ubc.ca/nserc-research-tools-instruments-research-portal)\.

If you have any questions regarding this process, please contact Evelina Tolstykh at the FoM \([evelina\.tolstykh@ubc\.ca](mailto:evelina.tolstykh@ubc.ca)\)\.
