#### Requirements, deadlines and procedures for the MSHRBC Health Professional\-Investigator Award\.

The MSHRBC Health Professional\-Investigator Program supports health professionals who are actively involved in patient care to build their health research programs, train the next generation of scientists, and make significant contributions to their field\.

---

**MSHRBC has launched the new 2025 Health Professional\-Investigator \(HPI\) Competition\.** 

Competition guidelines and instructions are available on the MSHRBC website at [https://healthresearchbc\.ca/fundingprogram/health\-professional\-investigator\-hp\-i\-program/](https://healthresearchbc.ca/fundingprogram/health-professional-investigator-hp-i-program/)\.

## Procedure

1.  For the MSHRBC full application, the Dean’s ApplyNet system account email is ****fomdean\.mshrbc@ubc\.ca****\. Please check with your UBC Department Head/School Director for their ApplyNet system account email address\.
2.  Check with your Department/School for its internal deadline for the review of your MSHRBC HPI application\. See below for a list of the required items\.
3.  Your application package must be sent to [evelina\.tolstykh@ubc\.ca](mailto:evelina.tolstykh@ubc.ca) via email by **January 8, 2025\.**
4.  The UBC Dean and UBC Department Head/School Director will strive to submit their forms on ApplyNet by January 21st, 2025\. **Do not submit the Department Head form on ApplyNet until you hear from Evelina Tolstykh\.**
5.  Submit your application on ApplyNet by **4:30pm on January 23, 2025**\.

Please remember that you can continue working on your research proposal up until you submit on ApplyNet on January 23rd, even after you have submitted your application package for review\.

Please note that unlike last program cycles, **reference letters are no longer required** \(letters of collaboration are optional\)

---

## Required Documents

A complete application package consists of the following items:

1.  [Research Project Information Form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/required-documents/), signed by the UBC Department Head or School Director\. UBC Centre Director signature is also required if the applicant will be using research space within a Centre\.
2.  [Request for Dean’s Approval Form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/required-documents/), signed by the UBC Department Head or School Director, and Centre Director if applicable\.
3.  First few pages from MSHRBC Full Application, up to and including the Research Proposal **section** \(*not* the full research proposal\)\.
4.  Department Head form, **in MS Word format**\. Please note the following:
    1.  **Use the template** \(see “Dean and Department Head Forms” section\)
    2.  **Signature on Department Head form not required for the FoM internal deadline** \(but will be requested once Dean’s Office review is completed\)
5.  Sponsor letter, if applicable \(**see Sponsor Letter” section for details**\)\.

Please email your application package to [evelina\.tolstykh@ubc\.ca](mailto:evelina.tolstykh@ubc.ca)\.

## Sponsor Letter

Partner and Clinical faculty applicants have an appointment at UBC, but may be employees of \(i\.e\. paid by\) another institution\. In such cases, the applicant may be required to obtain a letter from their employer, addressed to their UBC Department Head/School Director, to be included in the application package for Departmental and Faculty approval\. This sponsor letter should address the following:

1.  **Required** – Confirmation of 50% of the applicant’s time protected for research activities
2.  **If applicable** – Available resources to the applicant \(salary support, administrative support, infrastructure and equipment, human resources, etc\.\)

If the UBC Department Head/School Director has the authority to comment on items 1\-2 above, then the sponsor letter will not be required\.
