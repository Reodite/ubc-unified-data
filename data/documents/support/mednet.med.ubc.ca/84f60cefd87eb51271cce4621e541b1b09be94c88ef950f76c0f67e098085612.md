The Centre for Health Education Scholarship is holding an in\-person open house on **November 8** from 5:30\-7:30 pm and a virtual open house on **November 16** from 5:30\-7:30 pm for those interested in learning more about the [Master of Health Professions Education](https://ches.med.ubc.ca/programs/mhpe-canada/) \(MHPE\-Canada\) and [Clinical Educator Fellowship Program](https://ches.med.ubc.ca/programs/clinical-educator-fellowship-program/) \(CEFP\)\.

Attendees will meet Dr\. Rose Hatala, Director of MHPE\-Canada and CEFP; Chief CEFP Fellows Lucas Streith and Brian Kim, and MHPE\-Canada students\.

Learn more and register for an open house on the [Centre for Health Education Scholarship](https://ches.med.ubc.ca/programs/call-for-applications-2023-2024/) website\.
