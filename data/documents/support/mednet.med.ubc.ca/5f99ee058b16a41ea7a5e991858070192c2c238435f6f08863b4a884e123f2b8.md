A message from Mieke Koehoorn, Vice Dean, Academic Affairs, Faculty of Medicine\. 

---

I am pleased to announce the reappointment of Dr\. Amy Salmon as Director, Clinical Faculty Affairs for a second three\-year term, effective to March 31, 2029\.

Dr\. Salmon is a clinical associate professor in the School of Population and Public Health and Director of the Centre for Advancing Health Outcomes\. Dr\. Salmon leads research initiatives focused on the development and evaluation of health systems and services that support people with complex health and social care needs, including mental health and substance use services, as well as older adults through her role as the inaugural Conconi Family Foundation Distinguished Scholar in Seniors Care\.

[Dr\. Amy Salmon](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/AmySalmon_400x400.jpg)

In her first term as Director, Clinical Faculty Affairs, Dr\. Salmon led the implementation of the Faculty’s clinical faculty engagement plan\. This included the launch of the revised Policy on Clinical Faculty Appointments, helping improve consistency, enhance recognition, and expand engagement and career progression opportunities for clinical faculty across the Faculty of Medicine\. Dr\. Salmon also played a key role in strengthening partnerships to enable more research funding opportunities for clinical faculty\.

In her second term, Dr\. Salmon will continue to contribute to the implementation and refinement of Faculty\-wide policy and processes to strengthen engagement and recognize the essential contributions of clinical faculty to the Faculty’s teaching, service, scholarship and leadership activities\.

Please join me in congratulating Dr\. Salmon on her reappointment\.
