Dr\. Terrence \(Terry\) Waters passed away on September 7, 2025\.

Dr\. Waters was a current Clinical Professor in the Department of Anesthesiology, Pharmacology &amp; Therapeutics, which he first joined in 1984\. He was also an anesthesiologist in Acute Care at Vancouver General Hospital\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Waters](https://apt.med.ubc.ca/news/dr-terrence-terry-waters-passed-away-on-september-7-2025/ "Dr. Terrence (Terry) Waters passed away on September 7, 2025 - UBC Anesthesiology, Pharmacology and Therapeutics") from the Department of Anesthesiology, Pharmacology &amp; Therapeutics\.

The B\.C\., Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Thursday, September 25, 2025](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2025/09/26/terrence-terry-waters/ "Terrence (Terry) Waters Flag Lowering | UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
