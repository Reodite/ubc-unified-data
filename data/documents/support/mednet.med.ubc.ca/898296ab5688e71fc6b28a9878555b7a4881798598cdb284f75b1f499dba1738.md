Digital Solutions \(formerly MedIT\) advances education and research through digital innovation to transform health for everyone\. We deliver digital solutions in collaboration with our partners in the Faculty of Medicine, UBC, and the health system\. 

---

Digital Solutions’ mandate is to provide Faculty of Medicine\-wide services to enable the Faculty’s Strategic Plan and support its vision of [transforming health for everyone](https://vision.med.ubc.ca/)\. 

## Five digital capabilities in support of the Faculty

Digital Solutions supports the Faculty’s four strategic pillars – education, research, organization and partnership – through five key digital capabilities:  

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/Digital-Capabilities-2026.png)

-   Core platforms for Translational Medicine
-   Data Management, Training &amp; Workshops
-   Health Data Support
-   Data Analytics &amp; Visualization

-   Artificial intelligence \(AI\)
-   Extended reality \(XR\)
-   Learning Analytics
-   Innovation in curriculum delivery and assessment

-   Curriculum &amp; Assessment tools
-   Faculty administrative tools
-   Specialized research support
-   Business intelligence

-   IT Governance
-   Cybersecurity
-   Alignment with Institutional delivery partners

-   Partnerships and shared governance of Academic and Health platforms

---

## Services offered

We provide direct support for Education and Research technology, Collaboration platforms, Teaching and Learning platforms, Video and media, and guidance for large investments\.   
  
For a full list of our services and how to contact us, please visit our [Main page](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/it/)\. 

For equipment and troubleshooting, please contact the [MedIT Service Desk](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/it/getting-help/)\.
