The AI Strategy enables a shared direction and learning\-based approach for the responsible use, integration and stewardship of AI in the Faculty\.

## AI Vision

***Advancing health through human\-centered and responsible AI​***\.

The Faculty of Medicine, in collaboration with institutional, health system, and Indigenous partners, will support its faculty, staff and learners in the use of AI to intentionally enrich education, advance discovery, and improve health outcomes for the communities we serve\.

## What the Strategy Means for You

The introduction of this strategy won’t bring sudden, major shifts to daily routines\. There is no need to be an AI expert or adopt every new tool\. Instead, the strategy offers a vision for how AI can strengthen our work across the Faculty and provides a roadmap for building the skills needed to navigate and lead in a changing digital world\. 

Ultimately the strategy is designed to:

-   Provide guidance for using AI tools with confidence\. 
-   Support informed decision\-making and understanding of safe use\.
-   Enable easy access to training and resources for learning at an individual pace\. 
-   Advance AI initiatives with the right supports in place\.

The strategy also provides academic programs a way to align with Faculty\-wide principles\. 

For our learners, the impact of this strategy will cascade through to their specific academic programs\.

## A Note on Professional Discretion

The Faculty believes that AI use will depend on the situation\. Choosing not to use AI is also a valid option, and individuals can decide to avoid it if they wish\. ​However, in settings where AI is embedded in systems or processes, AI use may be unavoidable\.

## Guiding Principles

The Faculty’s AI strategy is based on **5 guiding principles**\. These provide practical tips for making wise AI decisions\.

### 1\. **Human\-Centered and Purposeful​** 

AI should enhance, not replace, human judgment, relationships, accountability, and wellbeing, and support the goals, values, and agency of faculty, staff, learners, patients, and communities, grounded in diverse and culturally\-informed perspectives\.

### 2\. **Ethical, Equitable, and Responsible**

AI use must reflect Faculty values and uphold privacy, fairness, transparency, accountability, and Indigenous data sovereignty, maintaining trust with individuals, communities, and health partners\.

### 3\. **Critical Engagement and Competence**

AI should strengthen critical thinking and professional judgment and be used with appropriate AI literacy, reflection, and informed skepticism\.

### 4\. **Intentional Integration in Education**

AI should be integrated into education with clear purpose and transparency, aligned with learning objectives, professional standards, and ethical frameworks\.

### 5\. **Responsible Innovation and Continuous Evaluation**

AI should responsibly advance research, education, and operations while maintaining rigor, safety, environmental responsibility, and ongoing evaluation as technologies and risks evolve\.

## Strategy in Action

We’re advancing a set of priority initiatives that bring the AI Strategy to life\. These actions demonstrate where work has already begun and where we are headed next\. 

### Faculty Values

Establish a Faculty wide AI stewardship and coordination framework to guide the responsible and evolving use of AI, enable cross unit collaboration, respond to emerging opportunities, risks, and regulatory changes, and maintain alignment with institutional and external health partners\. 

**Actions**:

-   Establish Faculty\-wide stewardship and coordination framework\.
-   Integrate AI strategy into next Faculty strategic plan\.

Establish Faculty wide safeguards and practices to ensure AI use is ethical, equitable, consent\-based, accountable, and maintains trust\.  

**Actions**:

-   Create a centralized [registry of AI initiatives](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/?page_id=34717)\.
-   Conduct a Faculty AI maturity assessment and ongoing AI evaluation\.

### People &amp; Organization

Strengthen partnerships with health systems, research organizations, government bodies and Indigenous partners and communities to advance responsible, culturally informed AI research, education, and real\-world application\. 

**Actions**:

-   Formalize collaboration with health authorities, research partners, and provincial/national bodies\. 
-   Establish ongoing engagement with Indigenous experts and communities\. 

Integrate responsible, ethical, equitable, and culturally safe AI learning into curricula to prepare learners to critically and effectively engage with AI while preserving foundational skills and professional judgment\. 

**Action**:

-   Establish an Education\-focused AI working group\.

Build Faculty\-wide capacity by developing AI literacy, knowledge, skills and guidance required for responsible and purposeful AI use\.

**Actions:**

-   Create a centralized Faculty [AI resource hub](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/?page_id=34944)\.
-   Offer tiered AI education offerings \(foundational AI literacy, role specific\-guidance\)\. 
-   Establish continuous engagement and feedback loops with faculty, staff, and learners\. 

### Technology &amp; Integration

Provide secure, scalable AI infrastructure and experimentation pathways to enable responsible innovation and cross disciplinary collaboration\. 

**Actions:**

-   Establish a Faculty AI sandbox\.
-   Invest in shared, sustainable AI infrastructure\.
-   Pilot AI enabled learning tools \(simulation labs, virtual patients, adaptive feedback, etc\.\)\.

### Data Readiness &amp; Stewardship

Strengthen Faculty\-wide data readiness, governance and data literacy to ensure that data quality and integrity uphold data sovereignty, and enable safe, ethical and effective AI use across education, research, operations\.

**Actions:**

-   Conduct a Faculty data readiness assessment\.
-   Define priority data domains and AI use cases\.
-   Develop a data\-sharing framework\.
-   Develop governance approaches respecting Indigenous data sovereignty\.

## Stewardship

To support responsible use without slowing innovation, the Faculty is currently developing an **AI Stewardship Framework**\.

The framework will streamline oversight and provide a coordinated path for teams to advance AI initiatives with the right supports in place, while preserving the autonomy, speed, and creativity our community values\.
