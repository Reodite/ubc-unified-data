The Office of Research is responsible for advancing the Faculty’s research priorities, providing support for researchers, and facilitating student research opportunities\.

---

## Grants &amp; Funding

-   Facilitate the [approvals process](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/) for research funding applications
-   Administer [internal research awards](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/research/award-funding-opportunities/) and funding opportunities
-   Communicate research funding opportunities though [The Research Roundup](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/research/award-funding-opportunities/research-roundup/)
-   Coordinate Dean’s Letters of Support required for research grant applications

## Strategic Programs &amp; Proposal Development

-   Oversee [Canada Research Chairs](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/announcements/funding-awards/funding-opportunities/canada-research-chairs-2/) including internal adjudications and submissions
-   Offer proposal development for research funding applications through [SPARC](https://sparc.ubc.ca/)
-   Assist in soliciting and coordinating nominations for [honours, awards, and prizes](https://prizes.research.ubc.ca/directory-award-opportunities)
-   Liaise with the [Institutional Programs Office](https://ipo.ubc.ca/) to manage federal and provincial funding programs

## Reviews &amp; Searches

-   Support the academic reviews and evaluations of research [centres and institutes](https://www.med.ubc.ca/research/centres-institutes/)
-   Prepare research funding data and analysis for planning and reporting
-   Lead searches for research centre and institute directors, strategic research chairs, and professorships

## Research Support

-   Provide a [Research 101 Handbook](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/FoM-Research-101-document_Aug2026.pdf) and information sessions for new researchers
-   Facilitate and coordinate international research partnerships
-   Engage in advocacy and strategic initiatives to support the health research community at the local, provincial, national, and international level

## Student Research

-   Administer and oversee [graduate &amp; postdoctoral education](http://grad-postdoc.med.ubc.ca/) and research
-   Coordinate curriculum changes and supervisory approvals with the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies
-   Facilitate [undergraduate student research](http://www.med.ubc.ca/current-learners/research/) opportunities \(MD and non\-MD\), including the Summer Student Research Program and the Multidisciplinary Research Program in Medicine \(MRPM\)

Visit [Research](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/) for more information\.
