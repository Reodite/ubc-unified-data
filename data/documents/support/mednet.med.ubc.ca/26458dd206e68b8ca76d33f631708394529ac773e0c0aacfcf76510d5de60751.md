[BC Children's Hospital's Centre for International Child Health and BC Women's Hospital \+ Health Centre present Global Health Conference 2023: Global Equity &amp; Cancer Control, 26 January 2023 in\-person &amp; virtual](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/11/ghc-2023-poster-final-940x529.png)

The Centre for International and Child Health \(CICH\) at BC Children’s Hospital and the BC Women’s Hospital \+ Health Centre are thrilled to announce the **5th Annual Global Health Conference** \(GHC23\)\.

GHC23 is an annual event bringing together a community of students, trainees, clinicians, researchers, and faculty from across health professions and disciplines to share knowledge\. The conference will host a wide range of speakers from around the world, giving us the opportunity to re\-examine cancer control from a global equity lens with a particular focus on the global cancer burden on children and women\.

-   **When**: Thursday, January 26, 2023 \| 8 am–1 pm PST
-   **Where**: Hybrid event:
    -   In\-person at BC Children’s Hospital’s Chan Centre for Family Health Education

[Learn more &amp; register](https://www.bcchr.ca/global-health-conference "Global Health Conference | BC Children's Hospital Research Institute")
