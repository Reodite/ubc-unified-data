​A message from Sharmila Anandasabapathy, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, UBC\.

---

Please be advised that the [Faculty of Medicine Full Faculty Meeting](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/office-of-the-dean/governance/full-faculty-meetings/) will be held online via Zoom on **Tuesday, November 18** from 4:30–5:35 pm\.

## Registration required

Advanced registration for Full Faculty is now open\. All participants are required to register online by **12 noon on **Tuesday, November 18****\.

[Register to attend](https://rc.med.ubc.ca/redcap/surveys/?s=A7R773RYXC7NM8RJ "Full Faculty Meeting Voting Registration")

## Meeting details &amp; voting:

-   Upon registration, all participants will receive a confirmation email containing the meeting agenda as well as the Zoom link required to join the meeting\.
-   Voting members will receive a meeting agenda containing links to the voting platform\. Please retain this email and follow in\-meeting instructions from the Chair to cast your vote\.

### Agenda:

1.  Welcome, call to order, review of agenda
2.  Welcome and introduction to the new Dean, Faculty of Medicine
3.  Approval of the [Minutes of the May 6, 2025 Full Faculty meeting](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/11/3.0_20250506_FoM_Full_Faculty_Minutes.pdf) *\(motion\)*
4.  Business arising from the minutes
5.  Approval of the [actions taken by Faculty Executive](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/11/5.0_2025.11.18_ActionsTakenByFEXSinceMay2025_V.0A.pdf) since the last meeting of the Faculty of Medicine *\(motion\)*
6.  [Report from the Dean](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/11/6.0_2025.11.18_ReportOfTheDean-FINAL.pdf) to the Faculty of Medicine
7.  UBC’s Strategic Directions
8.  Campus Vision 2050
9.  Candidates for Degrees *\(motions\)*
10.  Adjournment
