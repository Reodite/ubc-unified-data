The Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\) Seed Program helps you explore and understand the impact of pedagogical choices by providing graduate research assistants, collegial collaborations, and complementary project funding\.

This year, the program has been expanded to include all faculty and staff at UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan\.

All UBC faculty and staff are invited to apply by **May 22**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-seed-program/ "SoTL Seed Program | ISOTL")
