[Dr\. Marko Yurkovich](https://obits.legacy.com/legacy/marko-yurkovich "Marko Yurkovich Obituary - Legacy.com") passed away on October 4, 2025\.

A current Clinical Instructor  in the Department of Family Practice, Dr\. Yurkovich was also an alumnus of the Family Practice Postgraduate Residency Program\. He founded the Genetica Medical &amp; Wellness Centre, where he practiced and taught medical students and residents\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Yurkovich](https://familypractice.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-marko-yurkovich-1986-2025/ "In Memoriam: Dr. Marko Yurkovich - UBC Department of Family Practice") from the Department of Family Practice\.

The B\.C\., Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Tuesday, November 25, 2025](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2025/11/marko-yurkovich/ "Marko Yurkovich Flag Lowering | UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
