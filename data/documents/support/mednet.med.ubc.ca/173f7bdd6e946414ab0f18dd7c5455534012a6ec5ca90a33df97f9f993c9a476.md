Applications are open for Innovation UBC’s Lab2Market Validate Foundations Pacific program\. The fall 2026 program is open to graduate researchers \(including postdoc, masters and PhD\) interested in commercializing their health research\.

[Innovation UBC](https://innovation.ubc.ca/) works with UBC’s researchers to support and accelerate the translation of their research into real\-world impacts\. Their programs are designed for UBC entrepreneurs looking to further their venture opportunity through in\-depth training, mentorship and resources\.

Over three online sessions, participants will hear directly from seasoned entrepreneurs and PI\-entrepreneurs, learn from industry experts, and leave with the knowledge needed to take the first steps toward commercializing their research\. This program will be delivered by the Lab2Market Pacific Hub \(Innovation UBC\) at no cost to participants\.

The  program consists of 2\-hour live virtual sessions across 3 days  on  September 8, 9 &amp; 10, 2026\. Apply by **Friday, September 4, 2026**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://innovation.ubc.ca/lab2market-validate-foundations "Lab2Market Validate Foundations | Innovation UBC")
