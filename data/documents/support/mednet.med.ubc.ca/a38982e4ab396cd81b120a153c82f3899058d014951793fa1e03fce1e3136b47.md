Applications are open for Michael Smith Health Research BC’s **Engage Award**, the first of two awards under their [newly\-launched Impact Pathways funding program](https://www.healthresearchbc.ca/news-events/new-funding-program-strengthens-health-research-collaboration/)\.

The Engage Award provides up to $5,000 in short\-term funding to support activities that bring together researchers and research users to build and strengthen relationships, and to explore opportunities for future Knowledge Mobilization activities\. The award supports activities that:

-   Form new connections or relationships between researchers and research users;
-   Strengthen existing connections or relationships between researchers and research users; and
-   Support researchers and research users to explore the potential for collaborative production of research and mobilizing research evidence into practice, policy and the public sphere\.

Apply by **4:30 pm on Monday, May 25, 2026**\.

Potential applicants can also register to attend an applicant information session on **Wednesday, May 6**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://www.healthresearchbc.ca/funding/impact-pathways-engage-award/ "Impact Pathways: Engage Award | Health Research BC")

The Mobilize Award, a second funding opportunity under the Impact Pathways Program, will open later in 2026 and support knowledge mobilization activities\. The award will provide up to $20,000 in funding\. More information will be available this fall\.
