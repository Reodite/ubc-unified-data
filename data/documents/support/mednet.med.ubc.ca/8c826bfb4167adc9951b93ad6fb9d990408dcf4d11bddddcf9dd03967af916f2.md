Faculty and staff at UBC Vancouver are invited to submit letters of intent for Large Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(TLEF\) Transformation projects by July 16\.

Large TLEF Transformation projects are multi\-year projects that are intended to advance UBC’s Strategic Directions relating to enhancing experiential education, excelling in the development and application of emerging technologies, and creating flexibility for learners\. Projects are eligible to receive up to $200,000 in funding and can span up to three years\. Typically, these projects impact multiple courses or programs, have the potential to involve collaboration across departments or Faculties\.

Please note: Ideas for all proposals that involve Faculty of Medicine Programs must first be reviewed by the [Faculty of Medicine TLEF Review Advisory Council](https://tlef.ubc.ca/medicine/) prior to starting the TLEF online application\.

Submit the the Faculty’s pre\-screen form by **12 pm on Friday, June 19**\.  Letters of intent must be submitted by **3 pm on Thursday, July 16, 2026**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://tlef.ubc.ca/application/ "Application - Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund")

A call for Small TLEF Innovation proposals \(up to $50,000\) will be announced in September 2026\.
