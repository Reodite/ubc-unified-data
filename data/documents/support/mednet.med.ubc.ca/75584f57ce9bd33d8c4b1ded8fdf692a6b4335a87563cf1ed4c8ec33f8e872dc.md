Access training and orientation required for all Faculty of Medicine faculty, staff, and paid students\.

---

As a new employee, a number of courses are required to meet WorkSafeBC and UBC requirements\. At UBC, a worker includes faculty, staff, student employees and practicum/clinical placement students\.

-   For workers based in Vancouver, complete the [**UBC General Required Courses \(Vancouver\)**](https://wpl.ubc.ca/browse/srs/programs/wpl-srs-ucglrd)
-   For workers based in the Okanagan, complete the [**UBC General Required Courses \(Okanagan\)**](https://wpl.ubc.ca/browse/ubcohse/programs/wpl-hse-ucglrd)
-   For students beginning practicum/clinical placements, complete the [Clinical Placements: An Introduction to Workplace Health &amp; Safety](https://learn.ubccpd.ca/elearning/ppe/home.html) module

Depending on the work being done, additional job specific training courses may be required to complete\.

For more information, view the [required training for all UBC workers](https://srs.ubc.ca/training/required-training-for-all-ubc-workers/)\.
