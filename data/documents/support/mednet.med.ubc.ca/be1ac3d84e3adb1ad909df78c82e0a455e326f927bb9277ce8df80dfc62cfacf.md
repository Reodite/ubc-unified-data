[Women’s Health Seminar Series](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/WHRC_SeminarSeries2024_1040x585-940x529.png)

Join the [Women’s Health Research Cluster](https://womenshealthresearch.ubc.ca/) for an upcoming session in their annual Women’s Health Seminar Series, with this year’s first session taking place on **Monday, September 23**\.

The seminars feature international experts presenting on a wide range of research covering the biological, psychological, behavioural, economic and social impacts on women’s health outcomes\.

Each virtual session consists of a 45\-minute talk followed by an interactive 15\-minute question and answer period\. The series is open to anyone interested in attending\.

[See all sessions &amp; register](https://womenshealthresearch.ubc.ca/womens-health-seminar-series/womens-health-seminar-series-20242025 "Women's Health Seminar Series 2024/2025 | Women's Health Research Cluster")
