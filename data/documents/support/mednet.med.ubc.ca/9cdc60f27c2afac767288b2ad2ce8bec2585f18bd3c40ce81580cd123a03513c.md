Register for an upcoming event from Indigenous Initiatives at the UBC Centre for Teaching, Learning, and Technology \(CTLT\)\.

[Indigenous Initiatives at the CTLT](https://indigenousinitiatives.ctlt.ubc.ca/) develops and offers programming, resources and consultations focused on Indigenous engagement in curriculum, pedagogy, classroom climate and professional development\.

## Upcoming events

### August 20 \| [Building Capacity—Networking and Resource Sharing for IBPOC Teaching and Learning Community](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/building-capacity-networking-and-resource-sharing-for-ibpoc-teaching-and-learning-community-2/) \(in\-person\)

Join the CTLT’s Anti\-Racist Teaching and Learning team for this networking, wellness and educational space offered to the IBPOC identifying teaching and learning community during the CTLT Summer Institute\. This is an intentional space to connect IBPOC faculty, students, and staff across the University, to cultivate kinship, community care and support\. Acknowledging that connection is foundational to anti\-racist teaching and learning, this session is for new and returning IBPOC educators at UBC to connect and share teaching, learning and wellness resources and ideas in a mutually supportive space\.

### September 22 \| [Writing Positionality Statements from the Lens of Anti\-Oppression and Anti\-Racism](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/writing-positionality-statements-from-the-lens-of-anti-oppression-and-anti-racism/) \(in\-person\)

In this workshop, you will be introduced to key concepts, reflective practices and definitions that assist in exploring identity and positionality; important tools for visioning, creating and choosing stylistic choices for positionality statement writing\. With a focus on exploring personal and research value systems that inform positionality statements, this session will weave together theoretical and methodological approaches to assist you in engaging in deep critical reflection on their personal approaches to research and writing\.

### September 23 \| [Classroom Climate Series: Guidelines for Incorporating and Honouring Musqueam Protocol at UBC Events](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/classroom-climate-series-guidelines-for-incorporating-and-honouring-musqueam-protocol-at-ubc-events/) \(in\-person\)

Learn from Liz King Osadczuk, Senior Director, UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events about guidelines and protocols, including:

-   What Musqueam protocol is for events at UBC is and why it is important
-   When it is appropriate to do a land acknowledgement vs\. when it is appropriate to request a Musqueam representative for a UBC event and the process to make that request
-   How to ensure Musqueam representatives are respectfully welcomed at events

You will also get a chance to learn about the work that UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events does in partnership with Musqueam\.

### October 7 \| [Fostering and Measuring Psychological Safety in the Classroom](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/fostering-and-measuring-psychological-safety-in-the-classroom/) \(online\)

This session is designed to assist in competency and capacity building around cultures of belongingness, affect in the classroom and engaging diverse communities through principles of inclusivity and psychological safety in the classroom\. This session will be grounded in the foundational principles of anti\-racism, to facilitate learning and discussion about the framework of psychological safety\.

### October 22 \| [Supporting Classroom Climate with Place\-Based Resources](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/supporting-classroom-climate-with-place-based-resources/) \(in\-person\)

Please join CTLT Indigenous Initiatives team for an opportunity to learn about UBC\-specific teaching and learning, place\-based resources that support students and classroom climate\. Participants will have the opportunity to learn more about Musqueam\-focused resources\. This session will showcase resources from community partners, share considerations for how the teaching and learning community might bring these resources into their pedagogical design and have opportunities for discussion and Q&amp;A\.

### October 27–29 \| [Applying Anti\-Racist Pedagogies in the Classroom](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/applying-anti-racist-pedagogies-in-the-classroom/) \(in\-person\)

The Applying Anti\-Racist Pedagogy in the Classroom program is a cohort\-based program where educators will explore anti\-racist approaches and practices to teaching and learning\. In this installation, we will ground our conversations within the body of work of race\-based traumatic stress, anti\-racism pedagogies, specifically culturally sustaining pedagogy, and trauma informed teaching and learning, with a focus on building teaching and learning practices that grapple with the often messy yet realistic intersections and nuance of cultural strength and trauma in the classroom\.
