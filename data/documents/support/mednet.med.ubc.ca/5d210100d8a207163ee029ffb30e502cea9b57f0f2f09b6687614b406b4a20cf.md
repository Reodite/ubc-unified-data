The Centre for Workplace Accessibility is offering cost\-sharing funding to help UBC units incorporate accessibility enhancements into faculty and staff events\.

Through a 50/50 cost\-sharing model, eligible organizers can receive up to $1,000 per event to support accessibility measures that help create more inclusive and welcoming experiences\.

Funding can be used for enhancements such as captioning, interpretation services, audio\-visual and assistive supports, sensory kits, and more\. By considering accessibility early in the planning process, event organizers can help reduce barriers and foster a greater sense of belonging across the UBC community\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/workplace-accessibility/workplace-accommodations/workplace-accommodation-fund " Workplace Accommodation Fund - UBC Human Resources")
