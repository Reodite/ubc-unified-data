Join the Office of Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion \(REDI\) for *[Frybread Power: The Significance of Indigenous Empowerment, Pride &amp; Resilience for Health](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/frybread-power-the-significance-of-indigenous-empowerment-pride-resilience/)* with guests Dr\. Evan *Tlesla* Adams and Joe Gallagher\.

-   **Date**: Monday, September 21, 2026, 9 am–4 pm
-   **Location**: In\-person at the [Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre](https://maps.ubc.ca/?code=ALUM), UBC Vancouver
    -   Virtual option available

*A recording will be available to view after the event*\.

[Register now](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_40fi1Yu38nnUFYq "Register for Frybread Power: The Significance of Indigenous Empowerment, Pride &amp; Resilience for Health - UBC Qualtrics")

Derek K Thompson – *Čaabať Bookwilla \| Suhiltun*, Hereditary Chief and Director of Indigenous Engagement invites you to join this full\-day event, featuring a conversation with Dr\. Evan *Tlesla* Adams and Joe Gallagher, members of the *Tla’amin* Nation\. Dr\. Adams is the Deputy Chief Medical Officer, Public Health at the First Nations Health Authority and host of the docuseries *The Conversation*\. Mr\. Gallagher is the Vice President, Indigenous Health and Cultural Safety at the Provincial Health Services Authority\.

Through reflections on leadership, storytelling, medicine and systems change, this session will explore Indigenous empowerment, pride and resilience for health, and pathways toward thriving Indigenous futures\.

Explore the [program and speaker bios](https://redi.med.ubc.ca/frybread-power-the-significance-of-indigenous-empowerment-pride-resilience/#Program) on the REDI website\.
