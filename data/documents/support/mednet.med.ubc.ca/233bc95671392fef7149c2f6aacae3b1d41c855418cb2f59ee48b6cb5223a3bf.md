The UBC Vancouver\-Okanagan Collaborative Research Mobility Awards \(CRMA\) are now open\.  

A total funding envelope of $100,000 is available for this one\-year funding cycle to support research collaborations across UBC researchers in Vancouver and the Okanagan\.  

Faculty members from all disciplines eligible to hold Tri\-Agency research grants are invited to apply by **May 6, 2024** \(first deadline\) and **November 4,** **2024** \(second deadline\)\.  

[Learn more and apply](https://research.ubc.ca/vpri-competitions-initiatives/collaborative-research-mobility-awards/ubc-vancouver-okanagan)
