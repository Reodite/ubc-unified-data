A message from Sharmila Anandasabapathy, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health and Ravi Sidhu, Vice Dean, Education\.

---

We are pleased to announce the reappointment of Dr\. Cary Cuncic as Regional Associate Dean, Vancouver Fraser, for a second five\-year term effective to July 31, 2031\.

Dr\. Cuncic is a clinical associate professor in the Department of Medicine’s Division of General Internal Medicine, and a practising general internist at Vancouver General Hospital\. She has previously served in a number of education leadership roles in the Faculty and is an alumna of the Clinical Educator Fellowship Program at UBC’s Centre for Health Education Scholarship\.

[Dr\. Cary Cuncic](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/CaryCuncic_400x400.jpg)

During her first term as Regional Associate Dean, Vancouver Fraser, Dr\. Cuncic led the implementation of the Vancouver Fraser Medical Program \(VFMP\) Learning Communities, an evidence\-based approach to strengthening the learning environment, while fostering belonging across the program’s expanded class size\. She also played a pivotal leadership role in the MD Undergraduate Program’s successful accreditation, supported the development of a pilot framework for operationalizing student accommodation at the VFMP, and championed initiatives advancing equity, diversity, inclusion and Indigenous engagement\.

In her next term, Dr\. Cuncic will continue to provide academic and strategic leadership for the VFMP in close partnership with local health authorities\. Her priorities include supporting the implementation of the VFMP strategic plan, advancing continuous quality improvement for the Learning Communities, and deepening relationships with local Indigenous communities to explore land\-based learning, in alignment with the Faculty of Medicine’s strategic goals\.

We are grateful for Dr\. Cuncic’s dedication to excellence in education, innovation and community engagement, and we look forward to her ongoing contributions to the Faculty\. Please join us in congratulating Dr\. Cuncic on her reappointment\.
