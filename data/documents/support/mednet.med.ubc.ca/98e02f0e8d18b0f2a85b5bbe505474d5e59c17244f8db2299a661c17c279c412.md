The Department of Orthopaedics has launched a new strategic plan: Mobility for All, 2023–2028\.

[Mobility for All, UBC Department of Orthopaedics Strategic Plan: 2023–2028](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/03/Orthopaedics_StratPlan-2023-2028_800x450-360x203.png)

With the vision of “pain free mobility for all” at the core, this plan outlines their new strategic framework supported by our ‘stronger together’ values and ‘local to global’ commitments in three distinct areas: Exemplary educational experience, insightful discovery and engaged practitioners\.

Developed in collaboration with a wide variety of stakeholders, the UBC Orthopaedics Strategic Plan 2023–2028 advances the Faculty of Medicine’s four pillars of education, research, partnership and organization through fostering supportive and safe learning environments, pursuing impactful research, and developing orthopaedic care partnerships both locally and globally\.

[Learn more &amp; read the plan](https://orthopaedics.med.ubc.ca/about/strategic-plan-2023-2028/ "Strategic Plan 2023-2028 | UBC Department of Orthopaedics")
