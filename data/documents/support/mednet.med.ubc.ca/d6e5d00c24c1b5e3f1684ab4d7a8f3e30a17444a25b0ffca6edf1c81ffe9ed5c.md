UBC Human Resources have shared updates to the UBC benefit plans, effective January 1, 2024\. These enhancements are designed to continue to support your health and wellbeing\.

Updates include:

-   Increased coverage for massage and physiotherapy
-   Separate coverage for eye exams
-   Introduction of the Personal Spending Account \(PSA\) for CUPE 2950 and Management &amp; Professional employee groups
-   Health Spending Account increases for Faculty Association, Management &amp; Professional and CUPE 2950 employee groups

[Learn more](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/news/november-11-2023/looking-forward-your-new-benefits-2024 "Looking forward to your new benefits in 2024 | UBC Human Resources")
