A UBC tradition, the annual Welcome Back BBQ offers faculty and staff a complementary BBQ lunch, live entertainment and an opportunity to build connections with colleagues and community partners\.

## UBC Okanagan

-   **Date:** Tuesday, September 1, 2026
    -   11:30 am–1 pm
-   **Location**: [Central Courtyard](https://maps.ok.ubc.ca/?code=OPOI10013), UBC Okanagan

*More information will be distributed via email to faculty and staff* *in August\.*

[Learn more](https://news.ok.ubc.ca/2026/08/18/welcome-back-fall-town-hall-bbq-and-info-fair/ "Welcome Back: Fall Town Hall, BBQ and Info Fair - UBC Okanagan News")

## UBC Vancouver

-   **Date:** Wednesday, September 9, 2026
    -   Time Slot 1: 11 am–12:30 pm
    -   Time Slot 2: 12:30 pm–2 pm
-   **Location:** [Flag Pole Plaza](https://www.maps.ubc.ca/?code=VPOI10007 "Flag Pole Plaza - UBC Maps"), UBC Vancouver

Register by **Wednesday, August 19** through your department contact\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/staffandfacultybbq/ "UBC Vancouver Staff and Faculty BBQ | UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events")
