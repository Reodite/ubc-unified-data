Have online meetings/sessions without booking a physical room\.

---

Given Faculty of Medicine academic learning spaces are in short supply, it is prioritized for teaching and learning that requires in\-person participation for pedagogical needs, accreditation requirements, or governance decision\-making\. Virtual meeting options are encouraged for activities whenever possible and when in\-person participation is not needed\.

## Available Tools 

The Faculty of Medicine currently has three different means to hold purely virtual meetings:

-   FOM Zoom
-   UBC Zoom
-   UBC Microsoft Teams

## How to Book FOM Zoom

1.  Navigate to rooms\.med\.ubc\.ca and login using your CWL and CWL Password\.
2.  Select “Request a Reservation” tab from the top of the page\.[Image](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/room-booking/how-to-meet/how-to-book-a-virtual-meeting/)  
    [Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/09/The_University_of_British_Columbia.png)  
    
3.  While filling out the form ensure you have selected “**Yes**” to the question “Is this a videoconference session” and “**Yes**” to the question “Is this session purely online with no physical rooms?[Image](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/room-booking/how-to-meet/how-to-book-a-virtual-meeting/)  
    [Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/09/How-To-Book-Step-3.jpeg)
4.  Under video conference bridge select FoM Zoom\.  
    [Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/09/Request_Rooms.png)
5.  After submitting your request you will receive a confirmation e\-mail indicating your request has been received\. Please review the information in the e\-mail to verify its accuracy\.
6.  Within 1\-2 business days you will receive an e\-mail with the information regarding your session’s connection information using FoM Zoom\. See the areas highlighted in yellow for your connection information including password and host key\.   
    [Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/09/How-To-Book-Step-5.jpeg)
