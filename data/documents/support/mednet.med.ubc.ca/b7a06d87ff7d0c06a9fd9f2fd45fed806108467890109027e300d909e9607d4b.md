The [Centre for the Study of Democratic Institutions](https://democracy.ubc.ca/) \(CSDI\) and the [Knowledge Exchange Unit, VPRI](https://kx.ubc.ca/) are delighted to announce the launch of the CSDI Policy Communications Clinic\.

The [CSDI Policy Communications Clinic](https://democracy.ubc.ca/cpcc/) is a free service for students, staff, and faculty at UBC\. The clinic offers workshops and individualized consulting hours to help students, faculty, and staff communicate their research effectively to stakeholders for policy change\.

CSDI will manage and conduct an integrated series of six workshops across UBC on policy communications\. Each workshop will have two versions – one for a ‘sciences’ audience and one for a ‘social sciences and humanities’ audience\.

The first workshops will take place on **February 1 and 2** \(both offered hybrid\)\.

[Learn more and register](https://democracy.ubc.ca/cpcc/policy-communications-workshops-series/)
