UBC Library has announced upcoming changes to its collections for UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan\. 

Effective **December 31, 2025**, UBC Library will end its ‘all titles’ subscription agreement with the publisher Sage, changing how library users access some Sage titles moving forward\. 

The library has also announced changes to its publishing agreement with the Public Library of Science \(PLOS\)\. 

Find more [information and FAQs](https://collections.library.ubc.ca/budget-and-planning/ "Budget and Planning | UBC Library Collections") on the UBC Library website\.
