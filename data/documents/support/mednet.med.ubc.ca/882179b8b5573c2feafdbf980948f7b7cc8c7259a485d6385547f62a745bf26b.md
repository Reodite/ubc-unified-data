Find information and resources to support the reviewing and processing of new clinical faculty appointments\.   

---

Requests for clinical faculty appointments are submitted to the relevant Department Head or School Director \(or delegate\) through the [Application for Clinical Faculty Appointment found on the Resources page](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#forms)\. Appointments align with the applicant’s area of training and practice\.  

An initial appointment can be at any rank, provided the criteria and expectations of that rank \([see Appendix A of the Clinical Faculty Policy](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\) are met at the time of application and, where applicable, recognize the highest rank held by the applicant at a previous academic institution\. If you are unsure which rank is most appropriate for a new clinical faculty member, please [connect with the Faculty HR team](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/contacts/)\. 

Initial granting of a clinical faculty appointment may be done at any time during the year\. Where the teaching need is immediate, the [streamlined appointment process](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/new-clinical-faculty-appointments/#streamlined-appointment-process) may be utilized\. 

## **Appointment Requirements** 

Requirements for a new clinical faculty appointment are provided on the [Becoming Clinical Faculty](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/becoming-clinical-faculty/) page\. 

## **Appointment Review and Submission Process** 

1\. **Ensure appointment submission is complete\.** Once the candidate submits their request**,** review the application to ensure all required documents are provided, depending on the rank that the candidate is applying for\. If the candidate is located at a distributed site \(i\.e\., [Island Medical Program](https://imp.med.ubc.ca/about/contacts/), [Southern Medical Program](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/about/contact-people/), [Northern Medical Program](https://www.unbc.ca/northern-medical-program/contact)\), please make sure to connect with the appropriate administrator to coordinate gathering the new appointment documents\. 

In addition to the candidate’s application, up to two supplemental documents/letters may be included: 

-   A **UBC** **Division Head’s Letter of Support** \(applicable only to those Departments with Divisions\); 
-   A **Letter Outlining Contextual Opportunities**\. This letter can be solicited as needed to provide additional context at the applicant’s work location \(i\.e\., hospital or distributed site\), and outline opportunities or possible challenges for contribution at that location\. For example, a letter can be provided from the Regional Associate Dean, Island Medical Program, who can speak to the opportunities for contribution at that distributed site\. 

2\. **If applicable, solicit reference letters\.** For applications to the rank of **Clinical Professor,** the Department/School will need to solicit **two letters of reference** that will form part of the package for review, unless the applicant has held an equivalent rank at another institution\. For more information on soliciting referees, please refer to the [Soliciting Referees page](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/?page_id=33383)\. 

3\. **Confirm eligibility\.** For clinical faculty members governed by the Health Professions Act \(HPA\) review evidence of good standing with the respective provincial/territorial governing body \(e\.g\., CPSBC license\)\. For clinical faculty members whose health profession is not governed by the HPA, confirm employment and good standing with both their BC health authority \(or similar public agency\) employer and, if applicable, professional organizations applicable to the profession\. 

If the license has any restrictions, please [contact the Faculty HR team](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/contacts/) to discuss\. 

4\. **Review against appointment criteria\.** The Head/Director \(or delegate\) reviews the documents provided by the applicant \(or via the distributed site\) to assess whether the appointment criteria are met in accordance with the [Clinical Faculty Policy](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\. 

Appointment at the rank of **Clinical Instructor** may be recommended by the Head/Director to the Dean directly without review by the CARP Committee as long as they meet the [appointment criteria outlined in Appendix A](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\. 

5\. **If applicable, CARP review\.** An appointment at the rank of **Clinical Assistant Professor or higher**, will require review and a vote by the CARP Committee\. [See Committee Review below](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/new-clinical-faculty-appointments/#committee-review) for escalation and discrepancy processes, and [Establishing a CARP Committee](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/?page_id=33371) for further guidance\. 

6\. **If applicable, prepare recommendation letter\.** For appointments at the rank of **Clinical Assistant Professor or higher**, the [Head/Director will need to prepare a Letter of Recommendation to the Dean](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#templates) with a rationale explaining the evidence to support/not support an appointment at that specific rank, based on the criteria set out in the [Clinical Faculty Policy](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\. For more information on preparing recommendation letters, please refer to the [Preparing Recommendation Letters page](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/?page_id=33386)\. 

The Dean will review the letter and make an independent recommendation to the President\. 

7\. **Prepare appointment letter\.** Once the applicable committees have convened and a recommendation has been made, prepare and send the [clinical faculty appointment letter](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#templates)\. 

8\. **If applicable, request completion of teaching tracking and payment information\.** In coordination with the distributed site \(as applicable\), send the following documents to the clinical faculty member for completion and return: 

-   [Clinical Faculty Teaching Tracking Form](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#forms), for those teaching in the MD and PG programs
-   [Electronic Fund Transfer \(EFT\) Request](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#forms), for those eligible for payment  

9\. **Submit appointment\.** When submitting the appointment for approval, please ensure the following documents are included:

**Via Workday** 

-   [Application for Clinical Faculty Appointment Form](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#forms) 
-   Signed [Clinical Faculty appointment letter](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#templates) 
-   Evidence of eligibility, such as proof of good standing with the respective provincial/territorial governing body \(e\.g\., CPSBC license\)\. For clinical faculty members whose health profession is not governed by the HPA, confirm employment and good standing with both their BC health authority \(or similar public agency\) employer and, if applicable, professional organizations applicable to the profession\. 
-   Applicant’s Curriculum Vitae **OR** [Record of Academic Activities Template](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#templates) 
-   [Clinical Faculty Teaching Tracking Form](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#forms) \(for those teaching in MD and PG programs\) 
-   [Electronic Fund Transfer \(EFT\) Request](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/?page_id=33292#forms) \(if eligible for payment\) 

**Via Files from FoM HR OneDrive**

-   UBC Division Head’s Letter of Support \(if applicable\) 
-   Letter Outlining Contextual Opportunities \(if required\) 
-   [Head/Director’s Letter of Recommendation](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#templates) \(for clinical assistant professor or higher\), including the vote of the CARP Committee 
-   Two letters of reference \(for clinical professor only\) 

**Please Note:** Consult with [Teaching Tracking and Payment System \(TTPS\) support](mailto:ttp.support@ubc.ca) for questions about TTPS entry or payment eligibility\. 

## **Committee Review**

When there is a discrepancy between the CARP Committee and the Head/Director on the recommendation for appointment, the file moves to the [FCARP Committee](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/contacts/#committee) to review, with the final decision on the appointment and rank to be made by the Dean\. 

In situations where the Head/Director \(and, where relevant, the CARP Committee\) either does not recommend the initial appointment or recommends an appointment that is at a rank lower than the one held at a previous academic institution, the applicant may request a review of the recommendation by the [FCARP Committee](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/contacts/#committee), with the final decision on the appointment and rank to be made by the Dean\. 

## **Streamlined Appointment Process** 

A streamlined process may be utilized in circumstances where the teaching need is immediate\. Streamlined applications are reviewed at the rank of Clinical Instructor with the Head/Director’s recommendation directly to the Dean\. Initial appointments are to be one year in length only\. Promotion to a higher rank can happen at any time and follow the Department/School’s normal process\. 

When submitting a streamlined appointment for approval, ensure the following documents are included and that you are coordinating with the relevant distributed site, which can assist with gathering the documents for review and approval\.

**Via Workday:**  

-   [Application for Clinical Faculty Appointment Form](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#forms) 
-   Signed [Clinical Faculty appointment letter](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#templates) 
-   Evidence of eligibility, such as proof of good standing with the respective provincial/territorial governing body \(e\.g\., CPSBC license\)\. For clinical faculty members whose health profession is not governed by the HPA, confirm employment and good standing with both their BC health authority \(or similar public agency\) employer and, if applicable, professional organizations applicable to the profession\. 
-   A CV or Record of Academic Activities Template is **not** required for the streamlined appointment process 
-   [Clinical Faculty Teaching Tracking Form](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#forms) \(for those teaching in MD and PG programs\) 
-   [Electronic Fund Transfer \(EFT\) Request](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/?page_id=33292#forms) \(if eligible for payment\) 

**Please Note:** Consult with [Teaching Tracking and Payment System \(TTPS\) support](mailto:ttp.support@ubc.ca) for questions about TTPS entry or payment eligibility\.
