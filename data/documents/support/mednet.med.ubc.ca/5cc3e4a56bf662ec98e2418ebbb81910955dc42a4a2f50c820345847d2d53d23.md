Dr\. Pieter Swart passed away on May 25, 2023\.

A Clinical Associate Professor, Dr\. Swart taught in the Department of Anesthesiology, Pharmacology &amp; Therapeutics\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Swart](https://apt.med.ubc.ca/news/we-have-sadly-lost-one-of-our-dear-colleagues-very-unexpectedly/ "We have sadly lost one of our dear colleagues very unexpectedly | UBC Anesthesiology, Pharmacology &amp; Therapeutics") from the Department of Anesthesiology, Pharmacology &amp; Therapeutics\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Tuesday, June 6, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/06/21/pieter-swart/ "Pieter Swart Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
