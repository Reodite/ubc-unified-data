A message from Wyeth Wasserman, Vice Dean, Research and Gavin Stuart, Vice President, Research, Vancouver Coastal Health\.

---

We are pleased to announce the appointment of Dr\. Stirling Bryan as interim Associate Dean, Research, Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute \(VCHRI\), UBC Faculty of Medicine and the Executive Director, VCHRI, effective February 1, 2026\.

Dr\. Bryan is a professor in the School of Population &amp; Public Health, and a renowned health economist whose work has shaped health system policy in British Columbia and internationally\. He is widely recognized for his contributions to economic evaluation, health technology assessment and evidence\-informed policy\. His leadership experience spans academic, health system and provincial research organizations, with a strong track record in advancing patient\-oriented research and integrating evidence into health system practice\.

[Dr\. Stirling Bryan](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/StirlingBryan-2026_0049_400x400.jpg)

His current research portfolio includes two genomics\-focused projects: one examining the use of pharmacogenomics in the clinical context of major depression and another assessing the cost\-effectiveness of epitope compatibility to guide organ allocation in kidney transplantation\. Across his work, Dr\. Bryan demonstrates a consistent commitment to patient\-oriented research\.

To assume this joint role, Dr\. Bryan will step down from his position as Chief Scientific Officer at Michael Smith Health Research BC\. He will continue his academic and research appointments in the School of Population and Public Health and senior scientist with the Centre for Clinical Epidemiology and Evaluation\.

A formal search process will be initiated this year to identify a permanent incumbent for this role\. In the interim, we look forward to benefiting from Dr\. Bryan’s strategic perspective and collaborative leadership as he supports the UBC Faculty of Medicine and VCHRI research communities during this transition\.

Please join us in welcoming Dr\. Bryan to this role\.
