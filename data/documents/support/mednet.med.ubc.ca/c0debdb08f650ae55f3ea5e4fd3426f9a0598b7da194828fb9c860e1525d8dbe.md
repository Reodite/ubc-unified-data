Faculty and staff at UBC Vancouver are invited to submit proposals for grants that support the use of open educational resources \(OER\) in UBC credit courses through the [Open Educational Resource Fund](https://oerfund.open.ubc.ca/)\.

OER are teaching and learning materials that are openly licensed to permit free access as well as adaptation and sharing\. Since 2019, UBC has provided over $825,000 in OER grant funding for 79 projects across nine Faculties via the two OER Fund grant pathways\.

## OER Affordability Grants

Grants of up to $25,000 for UBC Vancouver faculty who wish to incorporate open educational resources as the required materials into their credit courses\. Apply by **3pm on January 18, 2024**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://oerfund.open.ubc.ca/oer-affordability-grants/ "OER Affordability Grants - UBCV OER Fund")

## OER Rapid Innovation Grants

Grants of up to $2,000, available to UBC Vancouver faculty, staff and students for innovative activities that increase open educational resource development, awareness and capacity building\. **Applications are accepted on a rolling basis** until the year’s funds are exhausted\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://oerfund.open.ubc.ca/oer-rapid-innovation-grants/ "OER Rapid Innovation Grants - UBCV OER Fund")

## Consultation sessions

For more information and support, register for the [OER Fund general information session](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/oer-fund-info-session-october-18-2023/) on **October 18, 2023**\.

The Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology \(CTLT\) will also hold [weekly drop\-in consultation sessions](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/event-category/open-education/) to provide feedback and assistance to potential OER Fund applicants **through to January 17, 2024**\.
