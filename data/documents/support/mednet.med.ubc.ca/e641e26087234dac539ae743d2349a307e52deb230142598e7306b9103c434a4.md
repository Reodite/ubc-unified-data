[Partnering in Research Conference, UBC Knowledge Exchange Unit, UBC Community Engagement](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/04/KX_Event_PartneringInResearch_1350x600-940x418.jpg)

On **June 15, 2023**, join the [UBC Knowledge Exchange Unit](https://kx.ubc.ca/) and [UBC Community Engagement](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/) for UBC’s inaugural Partnering in Research Conference\.

The event is open to UBC faculty, staff, graduate students and external partners who collaborate with UBC\.

Explore the ethical, cultural and political challenges of partnered research and develop strategies for driving real change\. The conference will bring together 200 faculty, staff, students and community partners to UBC Robson Square to discuss how we can collaborate meaningfully for greater impact\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://kx.ubc.ca/partnering-in-research "UBC’s 2023 Partnering in Research Conference | UBC Knowledge Exchange")
