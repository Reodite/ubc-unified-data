UBC Okanagan faculty are invited to submit a proposal for the Aspire\-2040 Learning Transformations Fund \(ALT\-2040\) by **Monday, December 15, 2025 at 3 pm**\.

The ALT\-2040 fund supports course and program innovation, student experience enhancement, and educational resource development in order to advance transformative teaching and learning priorities at UBC Okanagan\.

Funding is available under three streams:

-   Open Educational Resources \(OER\)
-   Learning experience enhancement
-   Constellation Protostar fellowship for introducing new media technology elements

[Learn more &amp; submit a proposal](https://alt-2040.ok.ubc.ca/ "ALT-2040 Fund | UBC Okanagan")
