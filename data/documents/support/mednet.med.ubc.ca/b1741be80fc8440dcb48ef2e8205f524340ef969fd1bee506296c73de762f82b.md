The VinFuture Prize is set up to honour breakthrough scientific research and technological innovations that create meaningful change in the everyday lives of millions of people\.  

The VinFuture Prize comprises:  

-   A grand prize of US$ 3 million, awarded annually for breakthrough research and technological innovations\. 
-   Three additional special prizes of US $500,000, awarded annually, each with a focus on promoting diversity, equity, and new fields of study\. 

Nominations are open until **April 17, 2024**\. 

[Learn more &amp; nominate](https://prizes.research.ubc.ca/awards/vinfuture-prize)
