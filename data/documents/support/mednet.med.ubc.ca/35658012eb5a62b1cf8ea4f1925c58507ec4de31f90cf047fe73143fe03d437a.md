Join [Clinical Trials BC](https://healthresearchbc.ca/clinical-trials-bc/about-us/) on **November 28** for their 2023 symposium, “Approaches for Accessible Clinical Trials”\. Admission is free, with in\-person seats reserved for B\.C\.\-based attendees\.

The symposium brings together B\.C\.’s clinical trials community to share knowledge and discuss how to build a more robust clinical trials ecosystem that improves health and healthcare\.

This year’s theme focuses on new approaches, technology, pathways and regulatory initiatives to make clinical trials more accessible for people in the clinical trials ecosystem\.

-   **Date**: November 28, 2023 from 8 am–5 pm
-   **Location**:
    -   In\-person: Jack Poole Hall – Robert H\. Lee UBC Alumni Centre, UBC Vancouver
    -   Virtual via Zoom

[See agenda &amp; register](https://healthresearchbc.ca/clinical-trials-bc/info-and-resources/clinical-trials-bc-symposium/ "Clinical Trials BC Symposium - Health Research BC")
