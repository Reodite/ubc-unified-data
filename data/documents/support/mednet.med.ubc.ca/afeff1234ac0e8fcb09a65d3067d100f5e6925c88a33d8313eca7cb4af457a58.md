Faculty360 is a faculty activity management and reporting system that has been developed by and for the FoM\. 

It is currently approved for use by the following units: Anesthesiology, Pharmacology, and Therapeutics\. If your unit is interested in learning more, please contact us at [faculty360\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:faculty360.support@ubc.ca)\. 

## For Faculty Members 

Faculty360 allows faculty members to record and track their scholarly and teaching activities to **produce CVs and Annual Activity Reports**\. You can: 

-   Import data from existing sources such as Scopus, RISe, and Workday  

-   Copy/paste text from other sources for ‘quick import’ of new activities  

-   Search existing scholarly activities  

-   Customize CV output, including numbering and citation style  

-   View a dashboard of publications, grant information, and research keywords   

## For Department and Faculty Administrators 

In the future, Faculty360 will **enable unit\-level and Faculty\-wide reporting**, providing a view of research and teaching activities to support strategic decision\-making and showcase the Faculty’s academic and clinical impact\. *Reporting features are currently under development\.*
