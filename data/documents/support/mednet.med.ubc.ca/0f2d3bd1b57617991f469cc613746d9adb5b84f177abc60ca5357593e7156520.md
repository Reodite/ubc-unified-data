Integrate climate change content into your UBC course with a guest lecture delivered by qualified UBC graduate students, through the Climate Teaching Connector program\.

Coordinated by the UBC Sustainability Hub, the program offers instructors an accessible way to integrate climate change\-related content into UBC courses\. The program connects faculty with graduate students from diverse disciplines, who deliver 50\- or 80\-minute guest presentations grounded in their graduate program, research, lived experiences and unique climate perspectives\. 

Graduate student speakers are selected, interviewed, and compensated directly by the program\. Since its launch, the Climate Teaching Connector has facilitated more than 100 guest presentations across 50 academic departments, engaging over 4,500 students\.

[Learn more &amp; find a presenter](https://sustain.ubc.ca/programs/climate-teaching-connector "Climate Teaching Connector | UBC Sustainability")
