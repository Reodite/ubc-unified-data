Starting **Friday, November 1, 2024**, faculty and staff will have a new [Employee and Family Assistance Program](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/benefit-plan-details/employee-and-family-assistance-program) \(EFAP\) provider through GreenShield Health\.

The new EFAP will provide eligible faculty, staff and their dependents with free access to comprehensive, personalized mental and physical health support, including unlimited counselling and work\-life consultations\.

Eligible faculty and staff will receive an email on November 1 from UBC’s HR Benefits team with details to sign up to GreenShield’s digital platform\.

For more information and a demonstration of the new platform, attend the [orientation session on **November 7**](https://wpl.ubc.ca/browse/wellbeing/wellness/courses/understanding-your-ubc-benefits-new-efap-provider)\.

[Learn more](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/news/october-1-2024/introducing-our-new-efap-greenshield-health "Introducing our new EFAP: GreenShield Health | UBC Human Resources")
