*Submissions have now closed\. Explore a gallery of just a few of the submitted [warm greetings for the Dean](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/welcome-messages/ "Welcome to UBC, Dr. Sharmila Anandasabapathy, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice-President, Health, UBC - UBC Faculty of Medicine")\.*

To welcome the [new Dean, Faculty of Medicine, and Vice\-President, Health, Dr\. Sharmila Anandasabapathy](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/internationally-renowned-clinician-scientist-to-lead-faculty-of-medicine-and-ubc-health/), as she begins her role on November 1, share a warm greeting and recommend one must\-see place to visit in the beautiful province of British Columbia\.

Whether you’re in Northern B\.C\., on Vancouver Island, in the Interior, Fraser or the Lower Mainland, share your favourite spot in nature, your go\-to restaurant, hidden gem, community space or place to learn about local Indigenous art and culture\. We look forward to sharing your thoughts and suggestions\.

Complete this short online form to submit your welcome message\.

[Submit your welcome message](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0Op56CDqcYK3gsS)

We will share a selection of messages in an online gallery on the Faculty’s website and in *The Link* newsletter in November when Dr\. Anandasabapathy begins her term\.
