In partnership with the Ministry of Health, the Faculty of Medicine and UBC Health are offering researchers up to $50,000 to carry out a research project that responds to pertinent health\-related questions identified by the ministry\.

Apply by submitting a Notice of Intent by **December 9, 2022 at 12 pm**\.

Shortlisted project leads will be invited to consult with the Ministry of Health in January 2023 about their research question and proposed project\. Final applications must be submitted by February 10, 2023\.

[More information &amp; how to apply](https://health.ubc.ca/interdisciplinary-health-research/ministry-health-research-seed-grant-program "Ministry of Health Research Seed Grant Program | UBC Health")
