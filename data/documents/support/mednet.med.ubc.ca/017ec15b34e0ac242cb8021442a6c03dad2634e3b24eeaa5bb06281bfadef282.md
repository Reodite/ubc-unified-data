The UBC community is invited to comment on proposed amendments to the Land Use Policy \(UP12\)\.

The proposed amendments are meant to update outdated information and streamline processes, including dissolving the Land Use and Operational Sustainability Committee and reassigning its responsibilities to Campus &amp; Community Planning\. 

All members of the UBC community are encouraged to provide their comments by **Thursday, April 30, 2026**\.

[Learn more](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/board-of-governors-policies-procedures-rules-and-guidelines/open-policy-consultations/proposed-amendments-up12/ "Policy Consultation – Proposed Amendments to the Land Use Policy (UP12)")
