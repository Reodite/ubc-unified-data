The Faculty of Medicine Office of Research and the Graduate and Postdoctoral Education Office invite FoM students, faculty, trainees, and staff to join an online workshop intended to help researchers navigate the research landscape at UBC\.

This informal webinar will cover the following topics:

-   This informal webinar will cover the following topics:
-   Funding opportunities, types and sources of funding;
-   Guidance through the administrative processes for the submission of funding applications;
-   University\-wide resources available for graduate and post\-doctoral researchers\.

The session will be most useful for new faculty, early\-career researchers, research support staff, and graduate and post\-doctoral researchers, however it is open to all\.

**Time &amp; Date:** 10:00 – 11:00am \(PT\) Thursday, August 20th  
**Location:** Online via Zoom, link to be sent via email  
**Contact:** Lily Nizami \([lily\.nizami@ubc\.ca](mailto:lily.nizami@ubc.ca)\)

  
Please register ***[here](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_5hVJvhF9pRoFVH0)*** \(registration closes 4pm PT on August 17\)\.
