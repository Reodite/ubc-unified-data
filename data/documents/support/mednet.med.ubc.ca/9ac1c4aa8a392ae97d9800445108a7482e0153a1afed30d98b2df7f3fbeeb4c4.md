The Faculty of Medicine Summer Student Research Program is now accepting applications for 2026\. Submit student\-supervisor applications by February 13\.

The program provides funding for undergraduate students to explore a summer health research project supervised by a Faculty of Medicine researcher\. The competition is open to UBC MD \(years 1 and 3\) and non\-MD undergraduate students, partnered with a current faculty appointment in the UBC Faculty of Medicine \(instructor or higher, including clinical and affiliate Faculty of Medicine appointments\)\.

Funding of up to $3200 is provided in the form of a student stipend; supervisors are responsible for all other expenses\. The deadline to submit supervisor\-student applications is **Friday, February 13, 2026** **at 3 pm**\.

If you are a supervisor with an eligible project but do not yet have a student partner, [submit your project](https://www.med.ubc.ca/current-learners/summer-student-research-program/forms/) to be shared with students via the program webpage by **Tuesday, January 20**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](http://med.ubc.ca/current-learners/summer-student-research-program/ "Summer Student Research Program - UBC Faculty of Medicine")

If you have any questions, please contact [fom\.ssrp@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.ssrp@ubc.ca) for support\.
