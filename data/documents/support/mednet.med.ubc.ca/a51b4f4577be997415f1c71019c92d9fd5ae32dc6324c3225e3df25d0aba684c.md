As influenza \(flu\) season approaches, protect yourself by taking the flu vaccine\. UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan are offering free, appointment\-based flu immunization clinics this fall\.

At UBC Vancouver, flu clinics will run from **October 30 to November 7**\. You can book an advance appointment through the provincial government’s [Get Vaccinated system](https://www.getvaccinated.gov.bc.ca/)\.

At UBC Okanagan, flu clinics will be available from **November 3 to 9**\. [Book an appointment](https://hse.ok.ubc.ca/flu/)

You can also get a flu shot at a public clinic off campus or through a community pharmacy\.

Find out more: [UBC Vancouver](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/safety-programs/communicable-disease-prevention-framework/flu/) \| [UBC Okanagan](https://hse.ok.ubc.ca/flu/)
