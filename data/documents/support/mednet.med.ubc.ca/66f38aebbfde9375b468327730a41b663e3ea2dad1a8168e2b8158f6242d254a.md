UBC staff are invited to apply for funding to attend International Staff Training Week 2026 in Berlin, Germany from **June 22–26, 2026**\.

Offered by the Erasmus\+ Programme in partnership with UBC Global Engagement, four UBC administrative/non\-academic staff will receive funding to attend the training week\. This opportunity is open to full\-time and part\-time staff at UBC Vancouver or UBC Okanagan\.

International Staff Training Week, hosted by the Freie Universität Berlin, is one of Europe’s largest and most diverse Staff Weeks\. The 2026 program will bring together over 250 higher education professionals from more than 50 countries, and will cover a broad range of topics from community\-building and student mobility to equity, global outreach and digital transformation\.

Apply by **Sunday, January 18, 2026**\. Please direct all questions to Global Partnerships at [global\.partnerships@ubc\.ca](mailto:global.partnerships@ubc.ca)\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://global.ubc.ca/partner-ubc/erasmus "Erasmus+ opportunities - UBC Office of Global Engagement")
