[Dr\. William Alastair McLeod](https://derm.med.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-alastair-mcleod-clinical-professor-emeritus/ "IN MEMORIAM: Dr. Alastair McLeod, Clinical Professor Emeritus | Department of Dermatology and Skin Science") passed away on October 7, 2023\.

A Clinical Professor Emeritus, Dr\. McLeod served in the UBC Division of Dermatology \(now the Department of Dermatology and Skin Science\)\. He also served as Head of the Division of Dermatology at St\. Paul’s Hospital, as well as in many leadership and executive positions in Canadian dermatology\.

Amongst his accomplishments as a clinician, researcher and educator, Dr\. McLeod is noted for his extensive work in HIV and AIDS care, beginning in the early stages of the epidemic\. His work was recognized with the AccolAIDS Lifetime Achievement Award in 2003\.

Learn more [about Dr\. McLeod’s life](https://vancouversunandprovince.remembering.ca/obituary/william-mcleod-1089016350 "William McLeod Obituary | Vancouver Sun and Province")\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Thursday, December 14, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/12/21/william-alastair-mcleod/ "William Alastair McLeod Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
