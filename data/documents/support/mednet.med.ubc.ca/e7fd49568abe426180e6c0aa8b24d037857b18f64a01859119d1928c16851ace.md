Procedures and timelines for the CIHR 2026 Health System Impact Embedded Early Career Research Award

Please note the FoM deadline will be set based on the institutional timelines\. 

---

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **​Important Dates** \(application important dates\) | Details |
| Check with your Department/School | Application package sent to Department Head/School Director office for review &amp; approval\. See below for application package details\. |
| **January 28, 2026 \| 12pm** | **FoM Internal Deadline:** Application package sent to Dean’s Office \(fom\.grantapprovals@ubc\.ca\) for review &amp; approval\. |
| **February 4, 2026 \| 5pm** | **CIHR Application Deadline:** Applicant submits application on ResearchNet\. |

## Procedure

For the CIHR 2026 HSI Embedded Early Career Research Award competition, the Faculty of Medicine \(FoM\) has established the following procedures, in alignment with the Office of Research Services timelines:

1.  Your application package must be submitted to the Dean’s Office or affiliated hospital research office by **January 28, 2026** for Faculty approval\. Please send it via email to Evelina Tolstykh \([fom\.grantapprovals@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.grantapprovals@ubc.ca)\)\. See below for a complete list of the required items\.
2.  After Faculty approval, your application package will be forwarded to ORS for institutional review and signature on the Routing Slip \(CIHR signature page\)\. 
3.  Once ORS signs, they will return the Routing Slip to the applicant for submission on ResearchNet\.
4.  Your application must be submitted via ResearchNet no later than ****February 4, 2026****\.

Please remember that **you can continue working on your research proposal up until you submit on ResearchNet**, even after you have submitted your application package for Faculty approval\.

While FoM Office of Research makes best efforts to review all applications and to facilitate support for our research community, application packages submitted for Faculty approval after January 28 may not receive a guarantee of administrative review and approval by the deadline\.

## Required Documents

Required Documents to be submitted to the FoM for the CIHR 2026 HSI Embedded Early Career Research Award competition:

1.  Completed **[Research Project Information Form](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/required-documents/)** including the following signatures:
    -   the Principal Investigator
    -   Department Head or School Director
    -   Centre Director \(if using research space within a Senate Approved Research Centre, signature details can be found [here](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/research/application-procedures/signature-process/)\)
2.  Completed [**FoM Request for Dean’s Approval Form**](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/Request-for-Deans-Approval-form_April2025_updated_20250417.pdf) including the following signatures:
    -   Department or School HR representative
    -   Department Head or School Director
    -   Centre Director \(if using research space within a Senate Approved Research Centre\) 
3.  Application package, including the following sections:
    -   Routing Slip \(signature pages\) – signed by the Supervisor\(s\)
    -   Project summary
    -   Budget page
    -   Academic Mentor letter \(signed by the Academic Mentor, *FoM will obtain co\-signature from the Dean*\)
    -   Primary Mentor letter \(signed by the Mentor\)

If you have any questions regarding this process, please contact Evelina Tolstykh at FoM \([evelina\.tolstykh@ubc\.ca](mailto:evelina.tolstykh@ubc.ca)\)\.
