#### Requirements, deadlines and procedures for the MSHRBC Research Trainee Award\.

---

The MSHRBC Research Trainee Program is designed to attract, recognize, and support outstanding early career researchers who have recently completed a PhD, or are a health professional eligible for a fellowship \(or equivalent\) program by providing funding that helps protect time for health research activities\.

---

**MSHRBC has launched the new 2026 Research Trainee Award competition\. ApplyNet is now accepting applications\.**

Competition guidelines and instructions are available on the MSHRBC website at [https://healthresearchbc\.ca/fundingprogram/research\-trainee\-program/](https://healthresearchbc.ca/fundingprogram/research-trainee-program/)\.

## Procedure

For the 2025 MSHRBC Research Trainee Award opportunity, the Faculty of Medicine \(FoM\) has agreed with the Office of Research Services \(ORS\) on the following procedures/timelines:

1.  Your application package must be submitted via email to the Faculty [signature contact for your site](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/submit-applications/signature-process/#signaturecontacts) by **Friday, January 23, 2026**\. Note that Evelina Tolstykh \([fom\.grantapprovals@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.grantapprovals@ubc.ca)\) is the contact for Faculty approval for those located at the UBC Point Grey campus\. See below for a complete list of the required items\.
2.  After Faculty approval, your documents will be sent to ORS\.
3.  Your application must be submitted via ApplyNet no later than **4:30pm on Thursday, January 29, 2026\.**
4.  ORS will electronically approve your application via ApplyNet for submission to MSHRBC\.

Please remember that you can continue working on your research proposal up until you submit on ApplyNet on January 29, even after you have submitted your application package for Faculty approval\.

## Required Documents

1.  Completed [Research Project Information Form](http://www.research.ubc.ca/vpri/research-project-information-form), including signatures of your supervisor, their Department Head/School Director, **and Centre Director** \(if you are using research space at a Centre\)\.
2.  MSHRBC Full application: Pages 1\-2 including organization affiliation up to requested award duration
3.  MSHRBC full application: Project title and lay summary
4.  MSHRBC full application: Supervisor and co\-supervisor page

If you have any questions regarding this process, please contact Evelina Tolstykh at the FoM \([evelina\.tolstykh@ubc\.ca](mailto:evelina.tolstykh@ubc.ca)\), or [ors@ors\.ubc\.ca](mailto:ors@ors.ubc.ca) at ORS\.
