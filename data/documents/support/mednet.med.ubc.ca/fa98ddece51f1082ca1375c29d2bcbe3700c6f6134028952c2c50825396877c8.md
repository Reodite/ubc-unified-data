## Purpose and Authority

The purpose of this advisory council is to:

-   Review credentials of candidates for teaching and portfolio coaching roles in the MD Undergraduate Program \(MDUP\) who do not have any formal affiliation with the UBC Faculty of Medicine and who are not eligible for appointment in any of its departments or schools\.
-   Advise the Associate Dean, UGME regarding suitability of teaching candidates for appointment as adjunct faculty for the MDUP to ensure that all UGME teachers are accountable to the program and that students are assured a high quality of instruction, in accordance with CACMS Elements 4\.4 and 8\.5\. 

## **Composition**

This advisory council is made up of ex officio and appointed members\.

The UBC Faculty of Medicine’s commitment to equitable and diverse membership on its committees and advisory councils guides its nomination and selection process\.

### **Ex officio**

-   Director of Curriculum \(chair\) \(1\)
-   Director of Curriculum \(chair\) \(1\)

### **Appointed**

-   Three members of the Years 1&amp;2 Curriculum Subcommittee, from a mix of disciplines and regions \(3\)

Guests may be invited to contribute to specific deliberations at the chair’s discretion\.

## **Appointment Process**

Ex officio members are members by virtue of their administrative appointment\.

Appointed members are appointed by the Director of Curriculum \(Chair\)\.

## Term

Ex officio members are members as long as they hold their administrative appointment\.

Appointed members are members for a three\-year term and are eligible for renewal\.

## Chair

Chaired by the Director of Curriculum, UGME\.

## Meeting Schedule and Administration

1.  Normally conducts business by email\. Meetings may be called by the chair as needed\.
2.  All members are expected to provide input into each application\.
3.  The HR Manager, Dean’s Office will keep all correspondence with external parties, including applications for appointments, as well as all electronic correspondence among the advisory council members about candidates and the advice to be provided\.
4.  Records will be maintained in accordance with UBC and [Faculty of Medicine records retention procedures](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/HR/myHRstaff/Orientation/Documents/x%20Records%20Mgmt%20Guidelines%20-%20Jan%209-2013.pdf)\.

## Quorum and Decision\-Making Process

This advisory council provides advice based on the input of all members\. Members typically reach consensus on that advice but may convey multiple perspectives if consensus does not emerge\.

Although the advisory council does not make formal decisions, the chair may call a vote to further clarify the advice the council is providing\. Notwithstanding the outcome of a vote, differing perspectives are also recognized as part of the advice for consideration\.

This advisory council provides advice on candidate suitability for adjunct appointment to the Associate Dean, UGME\. Based on that advice, and on the recommendation by the Regional Associate Dean, the Associate Dean, UGME makes formal adjunct appointment recommendations to the Dean, who then submits them to the Board of Governors for final approval\.

## Lines of Accountability and Communication

This advisory council:

-   Advises the Associate Dean, UGME\.

-   Liaises with faculty leadership at each of the four distributed sites to gather information to inform advice, as needed\.

## Responsibilities

This advisory council:

1.  Receives and reviews applications for appointment as adjunct faculty in the MDUP\.
2.  Ensures that all required documentation is available for review\.
3.  Communicates, as necessary, with course directors, regional associate deans, UBC Faculty of Medicine Faculty Affairs, and UBC Faculty Relations \(as required\) regarding any issue that may arise concerning an application\.
4.  Maintains a current record of adjunct faculty members in the MDUP\.
5.  Advises the Associate Dean, UGME on the suitability of candidates for adjunct appointments in the MDUP\.
6.  Reviews its terms of reference on an annual basis and recommends updates to the Associate Dean, UGME, as needed\.

## Approval

This version of these terms of reference has been approved by the Associate Dean, UGME on Feb 2026\.
