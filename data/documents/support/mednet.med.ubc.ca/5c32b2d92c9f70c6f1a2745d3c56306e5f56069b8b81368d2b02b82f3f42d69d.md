As Generative Artificial Intelligence \(Generative AI or GenAI\) continues to emerge as a significant tool in education, explore resources and events developed to help UBC educators navigate this new landscape and explore the opportunities generative AI presents\. 

---

## [AI in Teaching and Learning website](https://ai.ctlt.ubc.ca/)

Find ongoing support and up\-to\-date tools and resources to support the use of Generative AI in teaching and learning, from the Centre for Teaching, Learning, and Technology \(CTLT\) at UBCV and the Centre for Teaching and Learning \(CTL\) at UBCO\.

## CTLT events &amp; workshops

### Weekly GenAI support clinics

One\-hour drop\-in clinics on teaching and learning topics to do with the use of tools such as ChatGPT, Stability AI, Google Bard and others, every Wednesday\.

-   [September 13](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/ctlt-generative-ai-support-clinic-online-drop-in-september-13-2023/ "CTLT Generative AI Support Clinic (Online Drop-in) – September 13, 2023")
-   [September 20](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/ctlt-generative-ai-support-clinic-online-drop-in-september-13-2023-2/ "CTLT Generative AI Support Clinic (Online Drop-in) – September 20, 2023")
-   [September 27](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/ctlt-generative-ai-support-clinic-online-drop-in-september-27-2023/ "CTLT Generative AI Support Clinic (Online Drop-in) – September 27, 2023")

### Integrating GenAI in Education at UBC 30\+30 workshops

30\-minute interactive presentations followed by 30\-minutes of discussion designed to assist faculty and staff in navigating this new landscape\.

-   **Approaches to Prompting**
    -   [September 18](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/approaches-to-prompting-september-18-2023/ "Approaches to Prompting September 18, 2023 - CTLT Events")
    -   [October 23](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/apporaches-to-prompting-october-23/ "Approaches to Prompting October 23, 2023 - CTLT Events")
-   **GenAI Issues and Challenges**
    -   [October 5](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/genai-issues-and-challenges/ "GenAI Issues and Challenges October 5, 2023 - CTLT Events")
