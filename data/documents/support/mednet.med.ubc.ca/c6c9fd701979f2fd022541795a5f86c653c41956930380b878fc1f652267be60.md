[StEAR Roadmap for Change update: Reporting on equity and anti\-racism progress](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/12/StEAR_UpdateEvent2024-01_1170x400-940x322.png)

Launched in January 2023, UBC’s Strategic Equity and Anti\-Racism \(StEAR\) Framework set out the university’s integrated approach to advancing equity and anti\-racism commitments and priorities drawn from across a number of institutional plans\.

Since that time, a number of efforts have moved the implementation of this work forward\.

To learn more about the progress of institutional\-level actions, register to join the upcoming StEAR Roadmap for Change update: Reporting on equity and anti\-racism progress townhall:

-   [**UBC Okanagan: January 29, 2024**](https://equity.ok.ubc.ca/events/event/stear-roadmap-for-change-update-reporting-on-equity-and-anti-racism-progress/ "StEAR Roadmap for Change update: Reporting on equity and anti-racism progress - UBC Okanagan")
