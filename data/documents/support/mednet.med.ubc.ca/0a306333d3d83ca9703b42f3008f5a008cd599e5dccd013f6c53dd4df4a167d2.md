The UBC Vancouver Travelling Health Fair is an annual free mobile health clinic offering one\-on\-one health assessments\.

The 2026 fair is focused on summer health, with guidance and support on summer health topics, including heat exposure, wildfire smoke, sun damage, insect bites and allergies, to help you stay safe, active and informed\.

Book a free 30\-minute appointment at one of a range of locations across UBC Vancouver campus between **May 26–28, 2026**\. Faculty, staff, post\-doctoral fellows and student staff are welcome\.

[Learn more &amp; book an appointment](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/physical-wellbeing/travelling-health-fair/ " Travelling Health Fair | UBC Human Resources")
