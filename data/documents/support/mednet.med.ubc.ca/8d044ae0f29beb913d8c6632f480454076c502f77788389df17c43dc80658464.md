Applications are now open for INSPIRE: The Developing Leaders Program, UBC’s flagship leadership development experience for newer staff managers\. 

The five\-month program is open to Management &amp; Professional \(M&amp;P\) staff at the Vancouver and Okanagan campuses with 1–4 years of total management experience and who manage one or more direct reports \. Through interactive workshops, practical activities and peer learning, participants strengthen their leadership skills while building connections with leaders from across the university\.

The 2026/27 program runs from September 2026 to January 2027, across a mix of virtual and in\-person sessions\. Interested applicants must receive approval from their direct manager/supervisor to participate in the program\.

Apply by **12 noon on Monday, July 20, 2026\.** If you have any questions, please contact the program team at [leadership@hr\.ubc\.ca](mailto:leadership@hr.ubc.ca)\.
