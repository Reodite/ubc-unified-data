Join UBC Connects at Robson Square for “Repairing the Mind: The Future of Brain Injury Research and Recovery” on **Monday, April 20**\.

Hosted in partnership with the Djavad Mowafaghian Centre for Brain Health, hear from Dr\. Cheryl Wellington, Dr\. Wolfram Tetzlaff and Dr\. Stefanie Blain\-Moraes as they explore the future of brain injury research and recovery\. From blood\-based biomarkers to personalized repair strategies and technologies that assess consciousness, this event highlights how research is opening new paths to recovery\.

-   **When:** Monday, April 20 from 6–9 pm
-   **Where:** UBC Robson Square

[Learn more &amp; register](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/events/repairing-the-mind/ "Repairing the Mind: The Future of Brain Injury Research and Recovery | UBC Community Engagement")
