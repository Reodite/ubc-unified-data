November 20 is Trans Day of Remembrance, an opportunity to honour the memory of those lost to anti\-trans violence\.

On November 20, we honour those lost to violence, those who live through violence and transphobia, and recognize the need to disrupt the violence and oppression that trans people continue to face\.

Learn more about this day, ways to observe it, and access resources related to trans wellbeing and inclusion\.

[Learn more](https://events.ubc.ca/tdor/ "Transgender Day of Remembrance at UBC")
