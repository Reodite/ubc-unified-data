The Students as Partners \(SaP\) Fund provides grants to encourage student\-faculty partnerships in undergraduate course redesign at UBC Vancouver\.

Faculty and student partners may receive up to $7,100 per project, as well as dedicated support from an assigned Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology staff member, and access to a learning community\. During 2022–2024, up to 40 projects will be funded, with the intention to distribute funds across a diversity of disciplines and academic units\. 

Submit an application by **3pm on February 24\.**

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://sap.ubc.ca/ "UBC Vancouver Students as Partners Fund")
