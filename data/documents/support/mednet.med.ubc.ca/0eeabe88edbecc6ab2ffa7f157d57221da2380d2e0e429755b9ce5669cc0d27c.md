UBC faculty members and PhD student teams are invited to submit a proposal for the PhD CoLab Pilot Initiative, offered by UBC Graduate &amp; Postdoctoral Studies\.

The PhD CoLab pilot provides guidance and awards up to $100,000 to support inter/transdisciplinary teams of UBC PhD students, faculty members, and others as appropriate to work together on the co\-production of new knowledge and/or applications to address complex questions or problems\. Funds can be used to support student stipends, research expenses and/or dissemination/mobilization activities\.

Teams are invited to submit a Letter of Intent by **5 pm on November 2, 2023**\. Full proposals are due by January 22, 2024\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://collabphd.ubc.ca/ "Announcing the PhD CoLab pilot program")
