*Transforming Health Insights* is a new podcast series exploring how biomedical discoveries move into real\-world impact\. The podcast is brought to you by the Faculty of Medicine Office of Research and produced by EdTech: Digital Solutions for Education\.

The series features researchers, trainees, clinicians and private and public sector partners from across B\.C\.’s thriving life sciences ecosystem\. Through their stories, listeners gain insights into the translational medicine journey and how we can accelerate research and innovation to shape better healthcare for people across B\.C\., Canada and the world\.
