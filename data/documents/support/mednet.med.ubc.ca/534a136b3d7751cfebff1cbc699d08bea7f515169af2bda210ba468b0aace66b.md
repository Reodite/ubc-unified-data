Find information about clinical faculty, including appointment policy and criteria\. 

---

Clinical faculty are health professionals engaged mainly in the practice of their professions outside UBC, who supervise and educate Faculty of Medicine learners in clinical and research environments\. Their primary contribution is teaching, while service—such as educational or academic leadership—and scholarly activity are also encouraged and recognized\. 

[Eligibility for clinical faculty appointments](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/becoming-clinical-faculty/#eligibility) is outlined in the [Faculty of Medicine Policy on Clinical Faculty Appointments \(Clinical Faculty Policy\) found on the Resources page](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\.

Before teaching and assessing UBC learners, health professionals must hold a UBC faculty appointment as per the [Board Appointments Policy \(AP3\) found on the Office of the University Counsel website](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/board-appointments-policy/)\.  

Clinical faculty appointments are considered part\-time term faculty appointments under the [Faculty Term Appointments Policy \(AP4\) as shown on the Office of University Counsel website](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/faculty-term-appointments-policy/) and are governed under the [Clinical Faculty Policy](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies)\. Clinical faculty are normally considered independent contractors due to their expertise in their respective health professions\. As such they are appointed \(not employed\) by the University and are not members of the Faculty Association\.

## **Criteria and Expectations of Clinical Faculty Ranks**

Specific criteria and expectations for each rank for appointment, reappointment and promotion are listed in [Appendix A of the Clinical Faculty Policy\.](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies) 

**Please note:** Departments and schools should not introduce additional criteria not outlined in the [Clinical Faculty Policy and appendix\.](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#policies) 

Find examples of clinical faculty activities in the [Guide to Academic Activities for Clinical Faculty in the Faculty of Medicine](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/resources/#information-guides) found on the Resources pages\.

## **Review Committees for Clinical Faculty**

Clinical Appointment, Reappointment, and Promotion \(CARP\) committees at the Department/School level and the Faculty Clinical Appointment, Reappointment, and Promotion \(FCARP\) committee support the review of clinical faculty appointments, reappointments, and promotions\. [Refer to the Establishing a CARP Committee page](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/cf-hr/establishing-a-carp-committee/) and the [FCARP description](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/contacts/#committee) for more information\.
