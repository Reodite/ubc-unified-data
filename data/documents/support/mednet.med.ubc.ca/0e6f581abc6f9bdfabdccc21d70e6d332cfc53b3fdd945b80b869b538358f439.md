A message from Sharmila Anandasabapathy, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, UBC\.

---

I am pleased to announce that Dr\. Trevor Dummer has been appointed as Director *pro tem*, School of Population and Public Health, effective to June 30, 2027\.

Dr\. Dummer is a professor in the School of Population and Public Health \(SPPH\)\. Most recently, he has served as the School’s Associate Director, Education\.

Dr\. Dummer’s research explores how the built and natural environment influences cancer risk, with the goal of generating stronger cancer prevention policies and practice\. He holds the Canadian Cancer Society Endowed Chair in Cancer Primary Prevention, and serves as the National Scientific Co\-Director of the Canadian Partnership for Tomorrow’s Health \(CanPath\), Canada’s national longitudinal cancer and chronic disease cohort\.

[Dr\. Trevor Dummer](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/TrevorDummer_720x405-edited.jpg)

As Director *pro tem*, Dr\. Dummer will work alongside the School’s faculty, staff and learners to support the continued advancement of the School’s academic mission, including the delivery of outstanding educational programming for students and trainees and the advancement of research to improve population and public health locally and globally\.

Please join me in congratulating Dr\. Dummer on his appointment\.
