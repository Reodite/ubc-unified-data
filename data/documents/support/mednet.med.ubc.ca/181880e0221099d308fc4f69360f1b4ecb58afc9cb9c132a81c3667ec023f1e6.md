If you have an activity, research project, or other learning opportunity that you think will be of interest to medical students, find out how to submit a proposal\.

---

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/09/activity-proposal-poster.jpeg)

To advertise a particular research project or other learning opportunity to undergraduate medical students, potential Activity Supervisors complete an online [**FLEX Activity Proposal form**](https://apex.med.ubc.ca/ords/r/flex/public/proposal-intake?session=9397690546021)\. This form collects the details of the proposed activity \(description, completion time, contact information, etc\.\) for review by the FLEX course\.  Approved activities are added to the FLEX Repository from which students can select projects or find mentors working in their area of interest\.

The link to the form for submitting FLEX proposals can be found below:  
  
**[Link to FLEX Activity Proposal Form](https://apex.med.ubc.ca/ords/r/flex/public/proposal-intake?session=9397690546021)**  
\(Operates only on Chrome and Firefox web browsers\)
