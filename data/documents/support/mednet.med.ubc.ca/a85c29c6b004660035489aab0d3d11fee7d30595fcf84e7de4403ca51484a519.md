Dr\. Lynn Straatman passed away on November 28, 2025\.

Dr\. Straatman was a current Clinical Assistant Professor in the Department of Medicine, Division of Cardiology, as well as the Division of Palliative Care and the Department of Pediatrics\. She also served as the medical director of the Cardiac Function Clinic at Vancouver Coastal Health \(VCH\)\.

A champion of equity, diversity and inclusion \(EDI\), Dr\. Straatman served as chair of the VCH EDI Steering Committee as well as leading numerous initiatives at both VCH and UBC\. She also led initiatives to address medication affordability, and the award\-winning ON TRAC program supporting youth with chronic conditions transitioning from pediatric to adult care\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Straatman from the Department of Medicine](https://medicine.med.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-lynn-straatman-division-of-cardiology/), and [from the Division of Cardiology](https://www.ubccardio.com/in-memoriam-dr-lynn-straatman/)\.

The B\.C\., Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Tuesday, December 16, 2025](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2025/12/lynn-straatman/ "Lynn Straatman Flag Lowering | UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
