The Developing Leaders Program — which has evolved from the Managing at UBC Program — is now taking applications for its Winter 2024 program\. 

Offered by UBC Human Resources, this five\-month program is aimed at equipping early\-career managers at UBC with the skills and knowledge required to lead with confidence and effectiveness\. It is designed for people\-leaders who are currently in a management role and have been leading staff for three years or less \(in or outside of UBC\)\.

Submit your application by **12pm on Friday, November 24**\. If you have any questions, please contact the program team at [leadership@hr\.ubc\.ca](mailto:leadership@hr.ubc.ca)\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://hr.ubc.ca/career-development/leadership-programs/developing-leaders-program "Developing Leaders Program | UBC Human Resources")
