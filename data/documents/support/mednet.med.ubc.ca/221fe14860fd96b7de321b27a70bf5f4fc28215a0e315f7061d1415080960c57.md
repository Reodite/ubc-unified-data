Applications are open for the 2026 UBC AI and Health Network Fellows Program\.

Now in its second year, the UBC AI and Health Network program supports UBC principal investigators to recruit, mentor and support postdoctoral fellows conducting research at the intersection of artificial intelligence and health\.

Three streams are available for one\-year awards of $100,000:

1.  A general stream focused on AI and health
2.  A cancer\-specific stream supported by the BC Cancer Foundation
3.  A province\-wide stream supporting education, training and upskilling activities\.

Explore [previously funded projects](https://aihealth.med.ubc.ca/2026-fellows-program/previous-year/ "2025 Fellows Program - UBC AI &amp; Health Network") on the UBC AI &amp; Health website\. Please contact [ai\.healthnetwork@ubc\.ca](mailto:ai.healthnetwork@ubc.ca) with any questions\.

Submit applications by **12 noon on Tuesday, July 7, 2026**\. 

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://aihealth.med.ubc.ca/2026-fellows-program/ "2026 Fellows Program - UBC AI and Health Network")
