Post\-Retirement Appointments are for retired emeritus faculty members who provide a specific benefit to the unit or faculty after they have retired\. 

---

Emeritus faculty may be appointed into active faculty positions post\-retirement\. For more information, visit the Faculty Relations website regarding [Post\-Retirement Appointments](https://hr.ubc.ca/managers-admins/retirements-and-resignations/faculty-retirement)\.
