Learn how to use RoomFinder through an online training session\.

---

Digital Solutions offers training on the use of our online booking portal RoomFinder\. Training will help users orient themselves with the tool, understand the features and booking process, and provide best practices when making requests\. 

These training sessions are led by a member of the Resource Coordination Team over Zoom, at a mutually agreed\-upon time\. Sessions are 30 minutes in length\.

Please allow a minimum of one week in advance to schedule your session\.

## How to Book Training

Complete the brief survey form below\. We will ask you for your contact information, features you’d like to focus on, as well as your availability in order to schedule the training\.

[RoomFinder Training Request Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_5AWZSV70TiRBNbM)  

Please contact the Resource Coordination Team at [fom\.resourcecoordination@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.resourcecoordination@ubc.ca) with any questions you may have\.
