The Faculty of Medicine at the University of British Columbia \(UBC\) is committed to advancing global collaboration in research\. Through these partnerships, the Faculty of Medicine aims to align efforts in order to enhance research initiatives, promote knowledge exchange, and tackle global challenges through interdisciplinary cooperation\. These collaborations include co\-hosted symposia to share research findings, exchanges for post\-doctoral fellows and graduate students and research collaborations\.
