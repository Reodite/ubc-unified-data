The UBC community is invited to comment on proposed amendments to the Financial Investigations Policy \(SC15\)\.

The proposed amendments include expanding the scope of the policy to include anyone who can affect money, assets, or resources that belong to UBC \(such as volunteers and contractors\), and alignment with other Board policies\.

All members of the UBC community are encouraged to provide their comments by **Wednesday, February 19, 2025**\.
