## Applications

*Applications for the 2026 Program are now closed*\.

## About the Program

The Program connects clinical faculty across the Faculty of Medicine to foster collegial relationships and support ongoing professional and career development through the sharing of experiences, knowledge, and perspectives\.

Participants have the opportunity to build meaningful and supportive professional relationships that encourage collaboration, reflection, and growth\.

## Program Commitment

The program is  a **7\-month commitment**\. The next cohort will run from **October 2026 to April 2027**\. Applications will be accepted from June to mid\-July 2026\.

During the program, each mentor\-mentee pair will be asked to:

-   Attend a **1\.5\-hour virtual orientation** session
-   **Meet at least once per month** in a format that works best for them \(e\.g\., in\-person, Zoom, telephone, etc\.\)
-   Respond to **virtual check ins** by program team
-   Participate in optional **professional development training**
-   **Provide feedback** through program evaluations to support future program improvements

## How the Program Works

Each mentoring pair will be able to negotiate the frequency, length, format, and focus of their meetings\. Mentees will create learning goals to guide mentorship conversations and support their development throughout the program\.

Based on the needs and goals of mentees, mentors may provide guidance and support in areas such as teaching, scholarship, leadership, wellness, career navigation, academic advancement, and clinical practice\.

### Matching Process

Mentee applicants will have the opportunity to review mentor profiles and rank their top three preferred mentors\.

If you have someone in mind as a mentor, encourage them to take part and to be matched in the program\.

## Benefits

### Mentees

-   Receive guidance and support from an experienced colleague
-   Gain insights to support your professional growth, career development, and academic advancement
-   Build meaningful relationships and expand your professional network

### Mentors

-   Support the growth and success of a colleague
-   Learn from the experiences and perspectives of your mentee while strengthening your own leadership and mentoring skill
-   Build meaningful relationships and expand your professional network

## Eligibility

-   **Mentees:** Hold a clinical faculty appointment within the Faculty of Medicine\.
-   **Mentors:** Faculty members who are interested in supporting the professional growth and development of clinical faculty colleagues\.

## Program Costs

There is no cost to participate\.

## Credits

The Division of Continuing Professional Development, University of British Columbia Faculty of Medicine \(UBC CPD\) is fully accredited by the Continuing Medical Education Accreditation Committee \(CACME\) to provide CPD credits for physicians\.

-   **Mentors**: This activity is an Accredited Group Learning Activity \(**Section 1**\) as defined by the Maintenance of Certification Program of the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada, and approved by UBC CPD\. You may claim a maximum of **1\.5** hours \(credits are automatically calculated\)\. This activity meets the certification criteria of the College of Family Physicians of Canada and has been certified by UBC CPD for up to **1\.5 Mainpro\+® Certified Activity** credits\. Each physician should claim only those credits accrued through participation in the activity\.

-   **Mentees:** This activity is an Accredited Simulation Program \(**Section 3**\) as defined by the Maintenance of Certification Program of the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada, and approved by UBC CPD\. You may claim a maximum of **9\.5 hours** \(credits are automatically calculated\)\. This activity meets the certification criteria of the College of Family Physicians of Canada and has been certified by UBC CPD for up to **9\.5 Mainpro\+®** **Certified Activity** credits\.  Each physician should claim only those credits accrued through participation in the activity\.
