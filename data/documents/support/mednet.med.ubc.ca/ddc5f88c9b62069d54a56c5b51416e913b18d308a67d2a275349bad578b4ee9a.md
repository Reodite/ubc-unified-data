On **Thursday, October 17, 2024 at 10:17 am**, practice how to *drop, cover and hold on* during the annual province\-wide [Great B\.C\. ShakeOut](https://www.shakeoutbc.ca/)\.

As part of ShakeOut, UBC will be testing its emergency notification system, [UBC Alert](https://srs.ubc.ca/health-safety/emergency/emergency-response/#alert)\. Students, faculty and staff will receive a test notification by text and voice message during this drill\.

[Learn more &amp; find resources](https://srs.ubc.ca/shakeout "Are you prepared for an earthquake? | UBC Safety &amp; Risk Services")
