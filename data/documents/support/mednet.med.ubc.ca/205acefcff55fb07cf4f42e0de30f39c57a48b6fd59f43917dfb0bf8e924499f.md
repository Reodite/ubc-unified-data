[entrepreneurship@UBC 2023 Immersion Week](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/09/Entrepreneurship_ImmersionWeek-2023_1120x630-940x529.png)

Join entrepreneurship@UBC’s fourth annual Immersion Week\. Running in\-person and online from **September 26–28, 2023**, this event brings together the UBC and wider regional ecosystem to celebrate entrepreneurship and innovation through fireside chats with keynote speakers, panels, workshops, networking socials and more\.

The 2023 theme is **Climate Change and the Public Health Crisis**, engaging thought\-leaders, entrepreneurs and experts to learn how we can take action, together\. Climate change is the single biggest health threat facing humanity, and the climate crisis threatens to widen the divide of existing health inequalities across communities and global regions\. As we think about entrepreneurship for public good, what are the opportunities in B\.C\., a region whose climate and human health ecosystems are thriving, to lead at this intersection?

For a limited time, faculty and staff members can [register for a complimentary ticket](https://entrepreneurship.ubc.ca/special-guest-registration-immersion-week-2023) using promo code EIWGuest2023\.
