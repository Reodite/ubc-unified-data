The Faculty’s [Finance Team](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/finance/team/) is committed to providing professional financial advice, guidance, and service to the Faculty\.

---

## [Budgets](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/finance/budgets/)

Resources for financial planning, including GPOF, budget allocations, transfers &amp; opening non\-research worktags\.

## [Tools](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/finance/tools/)

How to administer clinical faculty payments, endowments, financial reports, internal controls, PGME funding, research overhead &amp; fiscal year\-end\.

## [Training](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/finance/training/)

Find online training courses to support financial administrators\.

## [Accounts Payable](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/finance/accounts-payable/)

Find information on Purchase Requisitions, Invoice Requests, Expense Reports and Policies\.

## [Accounts Receivable](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/finance/accounts-receivable/)

Find forms &amp; guidelines for cash receipts, APP and salary bill backs\.

## [Accounting Journal](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/finance/accounting-journal/)

Find information on accounting adjustments, including accounting journals, formerly known as journal vouchers, or JV’s\.  

## [Team](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/finance/team/)

Learn about the Faculty’s  Finance Team\.

## [Workday: Security Roles](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/finance/workday-security-role-requests/)  

Find HR &amp; Finance Security Role process changes\.

## [Workday: Staff &amp; Student Honoraria](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/finance/workday-security-role-requests/) 

Approval Process: All staff and student honoraria \(one time payment\) require Dean’s approval in Workday\.
