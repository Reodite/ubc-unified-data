The Work Learn &amp; Work Study programs subsidize meaningful work experiences for current UBC students\.

## UBC Okanagan Work Study

The Work Study program has $400,000 available in subsidies to enable faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan to create outstanding student employment opportunities\.

If you have a work opportunity to help students develop their professional skills and contribute to a work environment, submit a proposal to the Work Study program by **Tuesday, January 20, 2026**\.

[Submit a UBCO Work Study proposal](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/about-the-avp-students-portfolio/hire-a-student/work-study/)

## UBC Vancouver Work Learn

UBC Vancouver faculty and staff are invited to submit a proposal to hire temporary student employees through the UBC Work Learn program\.

The Work Learn program funds approximately 3,500 part\-time hourly appointments each year, subsidizing meaningful work experiences for current UBC Vancouver students\. Submit a proposal for the Summer 2026 session by **Monday, January 26, 2026**\.

[Submit a UBCV Work Learn proposal](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-affairs/ubc-career-centre/work-learn)

UBCV faculty can also submit a proposal for a summer research project through the [Work Learn International Undergraduate Research Awards Program](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/announcements/funding-awards/work-learn-international-undergraduate-research-awards-2026/) by **January 28**\.
