## **Privacy Matters @ UBC Symposium**

Cybersecurity is not just about technology, it’s about the people who use it and their habits\. Join the conversation at the Privacy Matters @ UBC Symposium on **Wednesday, October 28**\.

Bringing together guest experts from Gartner, UBC professors and IT teams, this year’s Symposium explores AI governance, institutional resilience, and more\. Gain fresh insights, networking opportunities, and practical takeaways to help you protect what matters most\. Complimentary lunch and giveaways included\.
