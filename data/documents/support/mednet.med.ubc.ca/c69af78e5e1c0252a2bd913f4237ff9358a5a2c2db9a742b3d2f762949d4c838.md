Registration is open for the Fall 2024 semester of Introduction to Regulatory Affairs, one of two ten\-week courses that make up the Regulatory Affairs in the Life Sciences Micro\-certificate\.

The UBC Micro\-certificate in Regulatory Affairs in the Life Sciences is a part\-time program delivered and developed by the [Academy of Translational Medicine](https://atm.med.ubc.ca/) and the Faculty of Medicine in consultation with world leaders in regulatory affairs\. It offers a foundational understanding of Canadian and global regulatory systems, processes and stakeholders, to equip learners with skills critical for the life sciences, biotechnology, biomanufacturing, medical device and pharmaceutical industries\.

Register to join Introduction to Regulatory Affairs for the next start date, **September 9, 2024**\. A limited number of 50% tuition bursaries are available to eligible learners\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://atm.med.ubc.ca/education/ubc-micro-certificate-in-regulatory-affairs-in-the-life-sciences/ "Micro-certificate in Regulatory Affairs in the Life Sciences | Academy of Translational Medicine")
