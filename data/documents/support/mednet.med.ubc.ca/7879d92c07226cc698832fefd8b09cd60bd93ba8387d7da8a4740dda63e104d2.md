Join Innovation UBC’s sixth annual Immersion Week, in\-person at UBC Robson Square **September 23–24**\.

​Immersion Week brings together the UBC community and broader innovation ecosystem to explore a range of globally relevant themes that entrepreneurs and researchers are poised to solve \(or are solving\)\.

This year’s theme is AI &amp; Security, with topics including how AI is transforming teaching and learning, regulation and strategic policy, AI &amp; Health and Climate, Indigenous perspectives, IP sovereignty and economic resilience, deep dive roundtables on investing and funding AI solutions, and more\.

[See agenda &amp; register](https://innovation.ubc.ca/impact-updates/events/immersion-week-2025 "Immersion Week 2025 | Innovation UBC")
