Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day is back this year and will be held in\-person at UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan on **Friday, May 3**\.

Whether you want to participate as an office, department, group of colleagues or an individual, there are a variety of challenges and activities to choose from\. Virtual activities will also be available\.

Learn more &amp; register:

-   [UBC Vancouver Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day](https://recreation.ubc.ca/sportsday/register/) \(Register by **April 24**\) 
-   [UBC Okanagan Faculty &amp; Staff Sports Day](https://hr.ok.ubc.ca/health-wellbeing/physical-and-nutritional-wellbeing/physical-wellbeing/faculty-staff-sports-day) \(Register by **April 29**\)
