UBC is aiming to increase compliance with required training for staff and student\-staff, as mandated by legislation and UBC policies\.

On **Monday, March 6**, staff and student\-staff who are not up to date on their [UBC Required Training](https://srs.ubc.ca/training/required-training-for-all-ubc-workers/ "Required training for all UBC workers | UBC Safety &amp; Risk Services") will receive a notification in Workday as a reminder to complete the training\(s\)\.

This new Workday notification will be sent out every March and October, annually\. Please keep an eye out for this new notification and check that you have completed the most up\-to\-date version of a necessary required training\.

[Learn more](https://srs.ubc.ca/required_training "Have you completed all UBC Required Training? | UBC Safety &amp; Risk Services")
