[Dr\. Andrew Moore](https://www.legacy.com/ca/obituaries/nsnews/name/andrew-moore-obituary?id=52283269) passed away on June 14, 2023\.

A Clinical Associate Professor Emeritus, Dr\. Moore taught in the Department of Urological Sciences\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Thursday, July 13, 2023](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2023/08/01/andrew-moore/ "Andrew Moore Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
