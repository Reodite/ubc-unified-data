Nominations are now open for the [2025 John McNeill Excellence in Health Research Mentorship Award](https://health.ubc.ca/awards-scholarships/john-mcneill-excellence-health-research-mentorship-award)\.

The John McNeill Excellence in Health Research Mentorship Award recognizes outstanding mentorship by faculty members at any stage in their academic career in any of UBC’s health\-related disciplines and who have served a minimum of two continuous years at UBC’s Vancouver Campus\.

We invite you to consider colleagues who could be eligible for this distinction\. Send completed packages to [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca) for review and internal adjudication where applicable by end of day **Friday, September 26, 2025**\.

If you have questions, please contact [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca)\.
