[Registration now open, Orthopaedic Update and BCOA Annual Meeting, May 4 to 5, 2023, Morris J Wosk Centre for Dialogue, Vancouver BC \| Online](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/03/Orthopaedics_Event_OrthoUpdate-BCOA-2023_500x500-300x300.png)

Registrations are now open for the UBC Department of Orthopaedics and BC Orthopaedic Association \(BCOA\) annual Orthopaedic Update **May 4–5, 2023**\.

Join in\-person at the Morris J Wosk Centre for Dialogue in Vancouver, or remotely via Zoom\-Panopto livestream\.

This year’s theme, ‘Bringing It All Together’, features sessions on SCI care, patient appropriateness and optimization for surgery, orthopaedic oncology, radiculopathy and many more\.

The annual Orthopaedics Research Day will take place **May 3** at the Gordon and Leslie Diamond Health Care Centre\. This one\-day event features UBC Orthopaedics residents, fellows, and graduate students research\.

[See the agenda &amp; register](https://ubccpd.ca/learn/learning-activities/course?eventtemplate=396-orthopaedic-update-bcoa-annual-meeting "Orthopaedic Update &amp; BCOA Annual Meeting | UBC CPD")
