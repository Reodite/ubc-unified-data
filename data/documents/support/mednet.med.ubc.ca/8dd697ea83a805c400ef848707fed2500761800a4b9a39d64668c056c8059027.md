A message from Mike Allard, Vice\-Dean, Health Engagement and Shanda Jordan Gaetz, Managing Director\.

---

[Dr\. Amy Salmon](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/04/AmySalmon_600x750.png)

We are pleased to announce the appointment of Dr\. Amy Salmon as Director, Clinical Faculty Affairs, for a three\-year term effective to March 31, 2026\.

Dr\. Salmon is a clinical associate professor in the School of Population and Public Health and the Associate Director of the Centre for Health Evaluation &amp; Outcome Sciences \(CHÉOS\)\. She also serves as the FLEX Site Co\-Director for the Vancouver\-Fraser Medical Program\.

As Director, Clinical Faculty Affairs, Dr\. Salmon will provide leadership to promote clinical faculty development and initiatives in teaching and scholarly activity, educational leadership and research\. With more than 10,000 clinical faculty across B\.C\., this position encourages participation of clinical faculty in the activities of the Faculty, its departments and programs throughout the province\.

Dr\. Salmon’s appointment follows the leadership of Ms\. Alexis Davis\. We would like to sincerely thank Ms\. Davis for her outstanding contributions and leadership in the role\.

Please join us in congratulating Dr\. Salmon on her appointment\.
