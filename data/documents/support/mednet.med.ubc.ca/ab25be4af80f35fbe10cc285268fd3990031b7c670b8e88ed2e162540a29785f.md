## **Competition Overview**

The Sherrold Haddad Excellence in Medicine Award Program is pleased to announce an Open Call competition to support up to one clinician who holds a faculty position in the Faculty of Medicine at UBC and performs clinical duties at one or more of the following institutions: Surrey Memorial Hospital, Czorny Alzheimer Centre or Jim Pattison Outpatient Care and Surgery Centre\. This award provides essential salary buy\-out funding and research support enabling recipients to dedicate more time to research aligned with Surrey Hospitals Foundation’s pillars of support\.

This initiative is made possible by a donation from the Haddad family to Surrey Hospitals Foundation which honours Sherrold Haddad’s legacy and his dedication to supporting physicians whose innovative research is transforming patient care\.

**Application Deadline:** June 12, 2026, 12:00 PM PST

**Submission Email:**[ocfa\.med@ubc\.ca](mailto:ocfa.med@ubc.ca)

## **Overview &amp; Eligibility**

-   **Research Areas:** Candidates must be actively engaged in research in one or more areas prioritized by the Surrey Hospital Foundation: maternal care, women, children and youth, mental wellbeing, chronic diseases and therapy, elderly care, specialized care, or surgery and outpatient care\.
-   **Eligible Candidates:** Must perform clinical duties \(**full\-time or fee\-for\-service**\) at Surrey Memorial Hospital, Jim Pattison Outpatient Care and Surgery Centre, or Czorny Alzheimer Centre, and must hold a clinical faculty appointment in the Faculty of Medicine at UBC\.
-   **Award:** Each award totals **$120,000**, distributed over two years, and may be used to cover clinical buy\-out time as well as research expenses\.

## **Submission Requirements**

**Application Submission Deadline:** June 12, 2026, 12:00 PM PST

A complete application package includes:

1.  [**Completed application form**](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/01/SHEM-Award-Application-Form.docx)
2.  **Candidate’s CV**
3.  [**Support and Attestation Form**](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/01/SHEM-Support-and-Attestation-Form.pdf)

**Please submit the full application to Faculty of Medicine Office of Clinical Faculty Affairs:**  [ocfa\.med@ubc\.ca](mailto:ocfa.med@ubc.ca)

## **Deliverables**

The expectation is that the awardee will be productive academically as determined by the usual metrics of scholarship\. They will be expected to publish at least one peer\-reviewed manuscript per year on average over the 2\-year term\. They will be expected to apply for at least 1 grant per year, and at least one national major competition \(e\.g\., CIHR\) at least once during the 2\-year term\. They will also be expected to disseminate their work in major local or national venues, or peer\-reviewed conference abstracts/presentations twice per year\. Additionally, an annual progress report will be required to track and assess the awardee’s achievements and adherence to these expectations\.

For any questions regarding the application process, please contact [ocfa\.med@ubc\.ca](mailto:ocfa.med@ubc.ca)\.
