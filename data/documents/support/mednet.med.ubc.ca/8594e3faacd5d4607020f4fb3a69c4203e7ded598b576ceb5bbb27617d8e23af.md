The Centre for Teaching and Learning \(CTLT\) [Spring Institute](https://institute.ctlt.ubc.ca/spring-institute/) is a forum for UBC educators to collaborate, network and engage in knowledge exchange\. The 2024 CTLT Spring Institute will be hosted online and in\-person June 3–7, 2024\.

Faculty and staff are invited to submit workshop and discussion proposals that demonstrate purposeful innovation in teaching practices, educational research, student services, and learning technology to support student success and experiences\.

Submit your proposal by **12 noon on Monday, February 26, 2024**\.

[Learn more &amp; submit a proposal](https://institute.ctlt.ubc.ca/spring-institute/spring-institute-call-for-contributions/ "Spring Institute: Call for Contributions | CTLT Institutes")
