Nominations are open for Canadian Medical Hall of Fame laureates\.

The Canadian Medical Hall of Fame \(CMHF\) honours individuals whose leadership, integrity and contributions to medicine and the health sciences have made a lasting impact on human health\. Laureates reflect the breadth of the health sciences, from clinicians and researchers to policy leaders and innovators\.

Eligible nominees are Canadian citizens whose work demonstrates excellence in:

-   Leadership in advancing health for Canadians and global communities
-   Leadership in health promotion, disease prevention, and patient care
-   Leadership in research with nationally or internationally recognized impact

Nominations that help broaden representation, including individuals who identify as women or non\-binary, and those who are Black, Indigenous or People of Colour are encouraged\.

Submit a nomination by **Monday, June 8, 2026**\.
