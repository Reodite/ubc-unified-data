​A message from Dermot Kelleher, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, UBC\.

---

Please be advised that the [Faculty of Medicine Full Faculty Meeting](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/office-of-the-dean/governance/full-faculty-meetings/) will be held online via Zoom on **Tuesday, November 15** from 4:30–6 pm\.

## Pre\-registration required

Advanced registration for Full Faculty is now open\. All participants are required to register online by **4:15 pm on Tuesday, November 15**\.

[Register to attend](https://rc.med.ubc.ca/redcap/surveys/?s=4W4KHPAF4P3WJNAE "Full Faculty Meeting Voting Registration")

### Live electronic voting:

-   In order to vote during the meeting, voting members are REQUIRED to [register online](https://rc.med.ubc.ca/redcap/surveys/?s=4W4KHPAF4P3WJNAE) in advance;
-   Upon registration, voting members will receive a meeting agenda containing links to the voting platform\. Please follow in\-meeting instructions from the Chair to cast your vote\.

## Agenda:

1.  Welcome, call to order, review of agenda
2.  Approval of the [Minutes of May 16, 2022](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/AboutUs/AdminAndGoverningBodies/FullFacultyMeetings/Documents/2022%20Nov/2.0_20220516_Full_Faculty_Meeting_Minutes_Draft_V0A.pdf) Full Faculty meeting \(motion\)
3.  Business Arising from the Minutes
4.  Approval of the [actions taken by Faculty Executive](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/AboutUs/AdminAndGoverningBodies/FullFacultyMeetings/Documents/2022%20Nov/4.0_20221115_ActionsTakenByFEXSinceMay2022_V.0A.pdf) since the last meeting of the Faculty of Medicine \(motion\)
5.  [Report from the Dean](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/AboutUs/AdminAndGoverningBodies/FullFacultyMeetings/Documents/2022%20Nov/5.0_20221115_FFReportOfTheDean_V.0A.pdf) to Faculty of Medicine
6.  Faculty of Medicine Educational Programs Expansion updates
7.  MD Undergraduate Program Expansion \(motion\)
8.  REDI \(Respectful Environments, Equity, Diversity &amp; Inclusion\) Office update
9.  Development &amp; Alumni Engagement update
10.  Adjournment \(motion\)
