[Dr\. Theresa van der Goes](https://familypractice.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-theresa-van-der-goes/ "In Memoriam: Dr. Theresa van der Goes - UBC Department of Family Practice") passed away on May 19, 2025\.

A leader in medical education and assessment, Dr\. van der Goes was a current Clinical Assistant Professor in the Department of Family Practice, where she also served in a number of education leadership roles including Residency Assessment Director for the Family Medicine Postgraduate Program\.

For her outstanding accomplishments and innovation in learner assessment and evaluation, she was invited to join a number of College of Family Physicians of Canada initiatives, presented internationally with the Association for Medical Education in Europe and the International Conference on Residency Education, and was awarded a Canadian Association for Medical Education Certificate of Merit\.

Read [more about Dr\. van der Goes life](https://www.dignitymemorial.com/en-ca/obituaries/nanaimo-bc/theresa-van-der-goes-12383691 "Theresa van der Goes Obituary - Dignity Memorial") and accomplishments\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Wednesday, June 18, 2025](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2025/06/19/theresa-van-der-goes/ "Theresa van der Goes Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
