It’s not too late\! Apply for up to $1,500 for community\-university collaboration and relationship building through the Partnership Recognition and Exploration \(PRE\) Fund\.

PRE funding fills small resource gaps and empowers community partners to build reciprocal relationships with UBC students, faculty, and staff\.

Applications are open until December 31, 2022, OR until available funds are exhausted\. Community Engagement has funded over 24 community\-university initiatives this fall and are halfway through the available funds for this year\.

Find [more information and how to apply](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/our-work/partnership-recognition-and-exploration-fund/ "Partnership Recognition and Exploration Fund - UBC Community Engagement") on the Community Engagement website\.
