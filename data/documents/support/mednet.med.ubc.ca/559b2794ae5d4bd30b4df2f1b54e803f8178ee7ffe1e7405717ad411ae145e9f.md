A message from Mike Allard, Vice\-Dean, Health Engagement and Shanda Jordan Gaetz, Managing Director\.

---

[Dr\. Ravinder Dennis Bhui](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/04/RavinderDennisBhui_250x250.png)

We are pleased to announce that Dr\. Ravinder Dennis Bhui has been appointed to the new role of Associate Director, Clinical Faculty Affairs, for a three\-year term effective to March 31, 2026\.

Dr\. Bhui is a clinical instructor in the Department of Ophthalmology &amp; Visual Sciences\. An ophthalmologist specializing in retinal surgery, he also serves as Chief of Surgery at Burnaby Hospital\.

In this newly created role, Dr\. Bhui will support the Director, Clinical Faculty Affairs, promoting clinical faculty engagement and participation and acting as a liaison for clinical faculty members across the province\.

Please join us in congratulating Dr\. Bhui on his appointment\.
