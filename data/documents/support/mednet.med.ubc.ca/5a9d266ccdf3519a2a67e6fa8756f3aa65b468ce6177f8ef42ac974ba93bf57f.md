Applications are open for Innovation UBC’s Venture Founder program\.

[Innovation UBC](https://innovation.ubc.ca/) works with UBC’s researchers to support and accelerate the translation of their research into real\-world impacts\. Their programs are designed for UBC entrepreneurs looking to further their venture opportunity through in\-depth training, mentorship and resources\.

Venture Founder is a 16\-week program open to UBC researchers bridging the gap from lab to market and ready to pressure\-test their startup ideas\.

Through experiential workshops, mentorship and practical training, the program provides support to validate your opportunity, strengthen your skills and cover the essentials to launch a viable startup company\.

The fall 2026 cohort runs from September 2026 to February 2027\. Apply by **Sunday, May 31, 2026**\.

Interested applicants can also register for info sessions running May 8, 15 &amp; 27\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://innovation.ubc.ca/entrepreneurship-ventures/entrepreneurial-pathways/venture-founder "Apply For Venture Founder Fall 2026 — Innovation UBC's 16-Week Entrepreneurship Program ")
