The UBC community is invited to comment on proposed amendments to the CV Policy \(HR1\)\.

The proposed amendments are meant to generally update the Policy to reflect current practices at UBC and to allow for changes to be made in the future\. 

All members of the UBC community are encouraged to provide their comments by **Sunday, October 5, 2025**\.
