Move UBC is an annual university\-wide initiative in February to encourage faculty, staff and the wider university community to reduce time spent being inactive\. During Move UBC this month, find out about: 

-   The benefits of moving more and breaking up sedentary behaviour
-   Tips for fitting physical activity into your day
-   Events and initiatives

[Learn more](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/campaigns-initiatives/move-ubc "Move UBC")

## 5 ways to move this February

Finding ways to break up sedentary or stationary behaviour throughout the day can help you move more and feel better\. Try these five easy ways to get moving during Move UBC:

1.  Visit [UBC Rec on YouTube](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/movement-breaks/) for a 6\-minute guided stretch break\. You can also request the [UBCV Move U Crew](https://recreation.ubc.ca/get-moving/move-u-crew/) or the [UBCO Move U Crew](https://app.cyberimpact.com/click-tracking?ct=FEGN2jy812LZMtwfDNxLkGzuIDOIdNdrbHOlB5nY-FIb9p4YpuZxj07Qb_Bj2aQjAS-pIxWORTeNiG1WWZUX22tOPu6t3SNFihXI7KdboIhGogGYL9PJ0GETJJ3Pxv72) to lead a free, 5\-10 minute in\-person or online movement break during your meeting\.
2.  UBCO: Join the [Faculty and Staff Movement Break Challenge](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/staff-faculty-movement-break-challenge/) brought to you by Move UBC and UBC Okanagan HR\.
3.  Try a walking or rolling meeting with a friend or colleague\. On the Vancouver campus register for a [Wellbeing Walk](https://recreation.ubc.ca/get-moving/walking/ "Walking Programs | UBC Vancouver Recreation")\! On the Okanagan campus, check out [campus trails](https://campushealth.ok.ubc.ca/voice-action-groups/environment-built-and-natural/ "UBC Okanagan campus trails")\.
4.  Skip the car and take the bus or bike to class or work\.
5.  Grab a friend and join a free fitness class or event on the [Move UBC Calendar](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/wellbeing-campaigns-and-initiatives/move-ubc/calendar/2024-02/)
