Nominations are now open for the King Faisal Prize in Medicine for 2025\.

The [King Faisal Prize](https://www.kingfaisalprize.org/aboutPrize) in Medicine recognizes the outstanding work of individuals who have carried out and published original scientific research on the prize’s topic that resulted in major benefits to humanity\. For the 2025 prize, the topic is “**Cellular Therapy**”\.

We invite you to consider colleagues who could be eligible for this distinction\. Send completed nomination packages to [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca) for internal review by **March 15, 2024**\.

[Download the nomination package](https://kingfaisalprize.org/files/nomination/medicine2025.pdf)

The King Faisal Prize submission deadline is March 31, 2024\. Please contact [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca) with any questions\.
