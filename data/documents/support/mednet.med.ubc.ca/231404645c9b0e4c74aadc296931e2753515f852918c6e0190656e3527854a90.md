Don’t forget to use available professional development \(PD\) funding, and submit claims in Workday by **March 3, 2023**\.

PD funds are intended to support learning activities that will enhance your workplace knowledge or skills and support your career growth at the university\.

Staff and research associates can submit claims through Workday on a first\-come, first\-served basis, until the annual funding pool is exhausted or by March 3, whichever comes first\.

[Learn more about PD Fund claims](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/news/january-01-2023/staff-submit-your-professional-development-fund-claims-march-3 "Staff: Submit your Professional Development Fund claims by March 3 | UBC Human Resources")
