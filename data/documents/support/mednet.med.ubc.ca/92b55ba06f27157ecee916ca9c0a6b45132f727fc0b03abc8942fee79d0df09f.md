Nominations are now open for the annual UBC Faculty Research Awards\. These awards acknowledge research excellence across all disciplines and at different career stages at UBC\.

Three new awards are launching this year, including the Killam Emerging Faculty Research Fellowship, the Engaged Research Award and the Innovation Award\. Explore the full list of awards and guidelines on the UBC Office of Research website\. 

Application process for 2026:

-   Submit an Expression of Intent by **Tuesday, September 15** \(not requested for the Killam Accelerator Research Fellowship\)\.
-   Final submissions for all awards must be made online by **Monday, November 16**\.

[See full awards list &amp; apply](https://prizes.research.ubc.ca/faculty-research-award-opportunities "UBC Faculty Research Award Opportunities")

For further information, consult the [awards FAQs](https://prizes.research.ubc.ca/internal-awards/internal-awards-frequently-asked-questions) or contact [research\.prizes@ubc\.ca](mailto:research.prizes@ubc.ca)\.
