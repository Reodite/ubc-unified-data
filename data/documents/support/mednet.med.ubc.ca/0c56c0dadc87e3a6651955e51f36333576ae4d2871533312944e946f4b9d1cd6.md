**Update Dec\. 17:** Cheque mailing has resumed now as normal\. [Learn more](https://finance.ubc.ca/announcements/university-cheque-mailing-resuming-normal-starting-december-17 "University cheque mailing resuming as normal starting December 17 | UBC Finance")

UBC Finance advise faculty and staff to expect delays in mail delivery, including cheque delivery for payroll and suppliers, due to the ongoing [Canada Post strike](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/our-company/news-and-media/corporate-news/negotiations/2024-11-15-cupw-announces-national-strike-that-will-cause-service-delays-for-canadians)\.

Employees who receive payment by cheque are being contacted directly with information on how to collect their payment\.

A temporary process for suppliers is also in place, and faculty and staff who are fielding supplier questions about payment are encouraged to direct them to information on the UBC Finance website\.

[Learn more](https://finance.ubc.ca/announcements/university-cheque-mailing-disrupted-due-canada-post-strike "University cheque mailing disrupted due to Canada Post strike | UBC Finance")
