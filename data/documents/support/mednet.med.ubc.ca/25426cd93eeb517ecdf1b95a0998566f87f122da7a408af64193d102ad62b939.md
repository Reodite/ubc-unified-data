UBC Library has added journals by Oxford University Press and Elsevier to the list of Open Access publishers and organizations offering discounted or free publishing costs to UBC authors\.

As part of its commitment to open access, UBC Library has institutional memberships with a variety of Open Access publishers and organizations, providing benefits including discounts on article submission costs to UBC faculty, staff and students\.

[Learn more](https://about.library.ubc.ca/2024/04/18/ubc-researchers-eligible-for-new-open-access-publisher-discounts-from-elsevier-and-oxford-university-press/ "UBC researchers eligible for new open access publisher discounts from Elsevier and Oxford University Press – UBC Library")

Find information about [all open access publisher discounts](https://scholcomm.ubc.ca/open-access-publisher-discounts/ "Open Access Publisher Discounts | Scholarly Communications @ UBC") available to authors at UBC\.
