Applications are open for the Catalyst Grant program offered by RNA Innovation @ UBC\.

This new opportunity, funded by the UBC Office of the Vice\-President, Research and Innovation, is designed to stimulate new interdisciplinary collaborations, accelerate innovative RNA\-focused research and catalyze the development of projects that position investigators for future external funding, partnerships and translational impact\.

Applications must be submitted by a team of at least two eligible UBC faculty members, and must demonstrate a new collaboration between those investigators\. Additional collaborators from other institutions, health authorities, industry or community organizations are also welcome\.

Up to four grants will be awarded in the 2026 round, at a maximum of $50,000 each\. Apply by **Tuesday, September 22, 2026**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://ubcrna.msl.ubc.ca/grants/ "Catalyst Grants - RNA Innovation at UBC")
