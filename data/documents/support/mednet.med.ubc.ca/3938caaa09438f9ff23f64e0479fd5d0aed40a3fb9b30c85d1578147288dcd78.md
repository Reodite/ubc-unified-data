> 
> ## Governing Bodies
> 
> By legislation, the **[UBC Board of Governors](https://bog.ubc.ca/)** is responsible for the management, administration and control of the property, revenue, business and affairs of the University\.
> 
> The academic governance of UBC and its Faculties is vested in two senates: **[Okanagan Senate](https://senate.ubc.ca/okanagan/)** and **[Vancouver Senate](https://senate.ubc.ca/vancouver/)**\. The **[Council of Senates](https://senate.ubc.ca/council/)** is responsible for matters pertaining to the broader UBC system\.
