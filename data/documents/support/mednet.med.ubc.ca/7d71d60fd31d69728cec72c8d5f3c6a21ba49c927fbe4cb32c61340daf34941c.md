Explore the below list of resources related to safety and research security for university travellers\.

Please visit the [Global Affairs Canada](https://travel.gc.ca/travelling/advisories "Travel advice and advisories - Travel.gc.ca") webpage for regularly updated information on travel advisories, visa requirements and border security\.

-   **[UBC’s Travel and Field Safety Program](https://travelfieldsafety.ubc.ca/)** \| Guidance, tools and resources to support the safety of UBC community members who are travelling and/or performing field work abroad\.
-   **[UBC Research Security: Travel Security Guidance](https://researchsecurity.ubc.ca/protecting-your-research/travel-security)** \| Practical tips and considerations for secure travel with research materials and devices\.
-   **[Privacy Matters @ UBC: Travelling with Electronics](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/i-want/travel-with-electronics)** \| Information on how to safeguard your personal and institutional data when travelling with digital devices\.
-   **[U15 and Universities Canada Travel Security Guide for University Researchers and Staff](https://univcan.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/travel-security-guide-for-university-researchers-and-staff-dec-2019.pdf)** \| A foundational guide to securing your travel, published by U15 and Universities Canada\.
-   **[Student Safety Abroad](https://safetyabroad.ubc.ca/)** \| Students travelling outside of Canada for university activity need to complete UBC’s travel authorization requirements\.
-   [**Emergency Assistance:** **International SOS**](https://travelfieldsafety.ubc.ca/emergency-assistance/) \| Guidance for when to contact International SOS \(ISOS\), a firm under contract to UBC to provide health and security assistance to UBC students, faculty and staff\.

For more information, explore the UBC Research Security website for a range of [research security resources](https://researchsecurity.ubc.ca/resources "Workshops and Resources | UBC Research Security") and [upcoming workshops](https://researchsecurity.ubc.ca/events "Workshops Calendar | UBC Research Security")\.
