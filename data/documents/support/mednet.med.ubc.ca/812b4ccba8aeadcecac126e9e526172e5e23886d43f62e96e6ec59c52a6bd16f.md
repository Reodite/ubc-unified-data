Managing the leaving process, including retirement, resignation and termination\.

---

## Resignation

Individuals planning or intending to resign from their current position should ensure that notice well in advance of the intended resignation date is provided to the University\. Faculty members who are members of the Faculty Association should be mindful of the [required notice period](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/faculty-collective-agreement#7)\.

Resignations should be made in writing to the responsible Head, Director, or Dean indicating the effective date of resignation\. Please refer to the [Departure Form](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/faculty-relations/files/departure_form.doc) to help ensure a smooth transition from UBC for your faculty member\.

It should be noted that any unused [vacation](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/benefit-plan-details/vacation-leaves/vacation) must be taken within the period of appointment; unused vacation time is not paid out\.

## Retirement

If a faculty member wishes to retire, s/he must give written notice of retirement to their Head of Academic Unit well in advance of their intended retirement date\.

Please note that faculty members are expected to retire on **June 30th or December 31st**\. Please be mindful of the [notice period](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/faculty-collective-agreement#7)  required and of [http://www\.hr\.ubc\.ca/faculty\-staff\-resources/leaving\-ubc/](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/faculty-staff-resources/leaving-ubc/)[what needs to be considered when a faculty member leaves UBC](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/life-events-and-your-benefits/leaving-ubc)\.

There is also information about [post\-retirement](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/faculty-relations/retirement/post-retirement-ubc/) such as ongoing privileges, emeritus status and post\-retirement appointments that can be helpful in discussing retirement options with a faculty member:   

-   [Planning for Retirement](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/retirement-planning)
-   [Working Past Your Normal Retirement Date](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/working-past-age-65)
-   [Post\-Retirement](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/faculty-resources/faculty-titles-ranks-and-descriptions/emeritus-status-and-post)  – Maintaining Your Connection to UBC  

## Termination  

Anyone considering the termination of a faculty member should contact the Faculty HR Manager for their portfolio in the Faculty of Medicine before taking action\.
