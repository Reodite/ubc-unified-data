On **Thursday, October 16, 2025 at 10:16 am**, practice how to *drop, cover and hold on* during the annual province\-wide Great BC ShakeOut\.

As part of ShakeOut, UBC will be testing its emergency notification system, [UBC Alert](https://srs.ubc.ca/emergency/get-notified/)\. Faculty, staff and students will receive a test notification by text and voice message during this drill\.

[Learn more &amp; find resources](https://srs.ubc.ca/shakeout "Are you prepared for an earthquake? | UBC Safety &amp; Risk Services")

For more information on earthquake preparation and safety, join ShakeOut BC for a public [Ask\-the\-Experts earthquake webinar](https://www.shakeoutbc.ca/event/ask-the-experts-earthquake-webinar-for-everyone/ "Ask-the-Experts: Earthquake Webinar for Everyone - Shakeout BC") on **Wednesday, October 15 at 7 pm**\.
