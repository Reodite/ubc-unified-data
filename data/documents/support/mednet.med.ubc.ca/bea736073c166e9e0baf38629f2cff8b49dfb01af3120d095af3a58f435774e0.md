Nominations are now open for the **Association of Faculties of Medicine of Canada \(AFMC\) Awards** for 2026\. We invite you to consider colleagues who could be eligible for these award categories:

-   Administration Award
-   Award for Outstanding Contribution to Faculty Development in Canada
-   Charles Boelen International Social Accountability Award
-   Clinical Teacher Award
-   Clinician\-Scientist Award
-   Indigenous Health Advocacy Awards
-   John Ruedy Award for Innovation in Medical Education
-   John Ruedy Award for Excellence in Graduate Health Professions Education Scholarship
-   Learner Changemaker Awards
-   May Cohen Equity, Diversity and Gender Award
-   Mentorship Award
-   President’s Award for Exemplary National Leadership in Academic Medicine
-   Scientist Award
-   Wellness Award
-   Young Educators Award
-   Leadership Award for the Promotion of Health in Francophone Communities – Lifetime Achievement
-   The Edwin Kroeger Global Emerging Scientist Award

Nomination instructions are available on the AFMC website\. Send completed packages to [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca) for review and internal adjudication where applicable by **Monday, September 15, 2025**\.

If you have questions, please contact [fom\.recognition@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.recognition@ubc.ca)\.

[Learn more &amp; nominate](https://www.afmc.ca/awards/ "Awards - AFMC")
