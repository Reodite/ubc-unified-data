Learn about the Faculty of Medicine’s pathways for submitting proposals for curriculum, academic calendar changes and new programs/certificates\.

Learn how to submit proposals for curriculum changes, including new programs or certificates, and updates to the UBC Academic Calendar for the Faculty of Medicine \(FoM\)\. All curriculum changes must first be approved by the FoM before they can move forward to the UBC Vancouver \(UBC\-V\) Senate for final approval and implementation\.  

The FoM Curriculum Team, housed in the Office of the Vice Dean, Education \(VDE\), acts as the main point of contact between proposal leads, the FoM, the UBC\-V Senate, and external governing bodies, and provides support through the various review steps needed for approval and implementation of new curriculum changes\. All inquiries related to curriculum changes, and/or the creation of new courses, certificates or programs within the FoM can be directed to the [FoM Curriculum Team](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/teaching/curriculum-pathways/about-us/) via [fom\.curriculum@ubc\.ca](mailto:fom.curriculum@ubc.ca)
