A message from Sharmila Anandasabapathy, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, UBC\.

---

The rapid evolution of artificial intelligence is transforming teaching and learning, research and innovation, and operations and administration across the Faculty of Medicine\. To help our community lead and navigate this evolution, I am pleased to launch the Faculty’s **Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) Strategy**\.

The strategy provides a coordinated approach for AI use that reflects the unique priorities of our Faculty, informed by input from faculty, staff and learners, as well as a review of institutional, healthcare, Indigenous and government AI frameworks\.

Whether you are exploring AI for teaching, clinical education, research or administrative work, I invite you to visit the new AI section on MedNet, where you can explore the strategy and priority actions, alongside a wide range of curated resources to support responsible, safe and effective AI use\.

[Explore the AI Strategy and resources](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/ai/)

Thank you to the many faculty, staff, and learners whose expertise and perspectives helped shape the development of this strategy\. I look forward to embarking on this next chapter with all of you as we advance health through human\-centered and responsible AI use\.
