UBC Health invites researchers at UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan to submit proposals to the Health After 2020 program\. Apply by **November 15, 2023** for up to $10,000 funding per team to support research collaborations and capacity building in knowledge translation\.

In partnership with the [UBC Knowledge Exchange Unit](https://kx.ubc.ca/), Health After 2020 is designed to support researchers to engage in interdisciplinary, cross\-institutional collaborations that aim to create change in health and health\-producing systems and build a community of UBC Health Scholars\. The program aims to create opportunities for meaningful engagement between researchers and the public by supporting collaborations that explore innovative approaches to knowledge translation rooted in community engagement\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://health.ubc.ca/interdisciplinary-health-research/health-after-2020 "Health After 2020 | UBC Health")
