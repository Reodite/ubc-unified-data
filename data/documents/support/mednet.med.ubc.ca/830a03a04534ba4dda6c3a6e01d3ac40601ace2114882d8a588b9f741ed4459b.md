Apply to join UBC’s virtual delegation at COP31 to observe the international climate negotiation process, do primary research and build global climate connections and partnerships\.

The annual UN Climate Change Conference of the Parties \(COP\) brings together countries, civil society, companies and people on the frontline of climate change to accelerate action on the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change\. The 31st United Nations Climate Change Conference of the Parties \(COP31\) summit is taking place in Antalya, Türkiye from **November 9–20, 2026**\.

Full\-time UBC faculty, staff and students can apply for the chance to join UBC’s virtual delegation to attend COP31, observe global negotiations, take part in exclusive global networking spaces and hybrid side events\. A limited number of in\-person badges will be offered to top applicants\.

Applications close at **11:59 pm on Wednesday, July 8, 2026**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://sustain.ubc.ca/cop31 "UBC at COP31 | UBC Sustainability")
