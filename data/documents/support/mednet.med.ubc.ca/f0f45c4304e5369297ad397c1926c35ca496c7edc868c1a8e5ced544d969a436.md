[Dr\. Jean\-Michel \(JM\) Lavoie](https://www.dignitymemorial.com/en-ca/obituaries/victoria-bc/jean-michel-lavoie-12919399 "Jean-Michel Lavoie Obituary") passed away on June 5, 2026\.

Dr\. Lavoie was a current Clinical Assistant Professor in the Department of Medicine’s Division of Medical Oncology\. He practiced as a clinician and researcher in Victoria\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Lavoie from the Department of Medicine](https://medicine.med.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-jean-michel-lavoie-division-of-medical-oncology/)\.

The BC, Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Sunday, August 23, 2026](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/flag-lowering/ "Flag Lowering | UBC Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
