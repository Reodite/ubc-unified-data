Dr\. Jordan Cohen passed away on September 14, 2025\.

A current Clinical Associate Professor in the Department of Psychiatry, Dr\. Cohen was a psychiatrist with a focus on youth mental health and substance use treatment with Vancouver Coastal Health\.

Dr\. Cohen served in a number of leadership roles within the Department of Psychiatry, including as Associate Head for Postgraduate Education and Continuing Professional Development, and as a core member of a number of the department’s committees\.

Read a [tribute to Dr\. Cohen](https://psychiatry.ubc.ca/in-memoriam-dr-jordan-cohen-clinical-associate-professor/) from the Department of Psychiatry\.

The B\.C\., Musqueam and UBC [flags were lowered on Friday, October 17, 2025](https://ceremonies.ubc.ca/2025/10/22/jordan-cohen/ "Jordan CohenFlag Lowering | UBC Protocol, Ceremonies and Events") in remembrance\.
