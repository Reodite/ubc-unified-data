### Related to Undergraduate Medical Education

The [Adjunct Appointment Advisory council](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/committees/ugme-adjunct-appointment-advisory-council-terms-of-reference/) advises the Associate Dean, UGME regarding suitability of teaching candidates for appointment as adjunct faculty for the MDUP to ensure that all UGME teachers are accountable to the program and that students are assured a high quality of instruction, in accordance with CACMS Elements 4\.4 and 8\.5\. 

The Advisory Council reviews credentials of candidates for teaching and portfolio coaching roles in the MD Undergraduate Program \(MDUP\) who do not have any formal affiliation with the UBC Faculty of Medicine and who are not eligible for appointment in any of its departments or schools\.

Student Affairs collaborates with the UGME Committee but is purposefully distinct from the UGME structure\. The [Student Affairs Advisory Council](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/committees/ugme-student-affairs-advisory-council/) advises the Associate Dean, MD Student Affairs, who reports directly to the Executive Associate Dean, Education\.

Learner Access collaborates with the UGME Committee but is purposefully distinct from the UGME structure\. The [Learner Access Advisory Council](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/committees/learner-access-advisory-council/) advises the Associate Dean, UGME, who reports directly to the Executive Associate Dean, Education\.

The [UGME Teacher Assessment Support Advisory Council](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/committees/ugme-teacher-assessment-support-advisory-council-terms-of-reference/) oversees UGME Teacher Assessment policies and procedures and is advisory to the Director Teacher Assessment, who reports directly to the Associate Dean Faculty Development for the Faculty of Medicine\.
