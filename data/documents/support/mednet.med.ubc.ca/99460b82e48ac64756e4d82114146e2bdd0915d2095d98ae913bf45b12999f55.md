Get a hands\-on crash course on how to use UBC’s high\-performance computing platform to support research that uses large datasets and computation\. This bootcamp aims to introduce researchers to high\-performance computing using UBC’s Advanced Research Computing \(ARC\) Sockeye platform, as well as educate current users on how to use the newly implemented Slurm job scheduler\.

This year’s bootcamp will be held in\-person only\.

-   **Date:** November 7–9, 2023
-   **Location:** Michael Smith Labs Room 102

Register by **Friday, November 3**\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://arc.ubc.ca/2023bootcamp)
