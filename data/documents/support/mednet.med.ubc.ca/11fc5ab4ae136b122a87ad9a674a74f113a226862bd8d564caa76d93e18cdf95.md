External referees are one of the key components in the assessment of a new appointment, promotion and/or tenure cases\. Here is information and templates to assist you in the process\.

## Letters of Reference Chart and Referee Selection Summary

-   [Letters of Reference Chart](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/FoM-Letters-of-Reference-Chart.pdf) \(rev Jan 2022\): Letter of reference requirements\.
-   [Referee Selection and Summary](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/Referee-Selection-and-Summary.pdf): Suggested format for the list of referees as well as guidance on choosing referees\.​
