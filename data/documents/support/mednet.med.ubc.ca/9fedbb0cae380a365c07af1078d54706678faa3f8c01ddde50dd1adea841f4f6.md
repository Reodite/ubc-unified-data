Have an idea to get the UBC community moving, but need some funds to make it happen?

Grants of up to $1,500 are now available for UBC faculty and staff planning projects or initiatives that support the wellbeing of our community by moving more\!

Move UBC Grants have two funding streams available:

-   Small grants: Projects requesting quick\-start funds under $200 — available year\-round
-   Large grants: Projects requesting funds between $200–$1,500 — apply by **December 15, 2022**

[Learn more and apply](https://wellbeing.ubc.ca/moveubcgrants "Move UBC Grants | UBC Wellbeing")
