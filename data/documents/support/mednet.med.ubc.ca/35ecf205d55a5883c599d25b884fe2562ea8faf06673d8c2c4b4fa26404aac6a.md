**Application closed\.**

The Seminar Series Fund provides secondary support for interdisciplinary seminars of educational value with up to $1,500 per seminar series\.

## Eligibility

The Seminar Series Fund is available to support seminar series within the UBC Faculty of Medicine that meets the following criteria:

1.  The seminar series must be interdisciplinary and open to all interested Faculty of Medicine faculty members, staff, and students\.
2.  The seminar series must be of clear educational value to graduate and/or undergraduate students\. In addition to attendance at formal seminars, active interaction of students with visiting speakers is encouraged\.
3.  To qualify as a series, the seminars must consist of multiple sessions held on a regular schedule \(e\.g\., weekly, biweekly, or monthly\)\. One\-time or ad hoc events are not eligible\.
4.  In\-person, online, and hybrid seminar series are all welcome to apply\. 
5.  The majority of funding \(50% or more\) must be obtained from other sources\.
6.  Only one application per [UBC Faculty of Medicine unit](https://www.med.ubc.ca/about/departments-schools-centres/) will be accepted\. 

## Application

To complete your application, please use the [online application form](https://rc.med.ubc.ca/redcap/surveys/?s=7M3LFM9TXPCKT44Y)\.

Applications must be signed by a Department Head/School Director or Centre/Institute Director at the UBC Faculty of Medicine \([Seminar Series Fund Signature Page](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/SSF_SignaturePage_2026.pdf)\)\.  Please upload and submit the completed page as part of the online application form\. 

## Contact

Please email Lilian Cheng \([lilian\.cheng@ubc\.ca](mailto:lilian.cheng@ubc.ca)\), Senior Program Assistant, Office of Research  if you have any questions about this opportunity\.
