As part of UBC’s commitment to advancing equity and inclusion, faculty, staff and students are invited to submit a proposal to the Strategic Equity &amp; Anti\-Racism \(StEAR\) Enhancement Fund for projects that advance UBC’s equity and anti\-racism priorities\.

This year, a total of $300,000 is available across UBC Vancouver and UBC Okanagan to support campus community\-led projects\. Applicants can receive $500\-$10,000 per project\. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis until August 2024 and will be evaluated cyclically, every six to eight weeks\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://equity.ubc.ca/stearfund)
