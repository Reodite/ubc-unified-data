A message from Sharmila Anandasabapathy, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, UBC\.

---

I am delighted to announce the recipients of the **2026 Faculty of Medicine Distinguished Achievement Awards**\.

These awards recognize faculty members who have made exceptional contributions in education, research or service—honouring those whose work is improving lives, advancing knowledge and making a meaningful difference to communities here and around the world\.

The 2026 award categories include:

-   Excellence in Education
-   Excellence in Basic Science Research
-   Excellence in Clinical or Applied Research
-   Service to the University and Community
-   Advancement of Equity, Diversity and Inclusiveness
-   Overall Excellence — Early Career
-   Overall Excellence — Mid\-Career
-   Overall Excellence — Senior Faculty

Please join me in congratulating our 2026 award recipients, who will be formally recognized at the Faculty of Medicine Awards Reception this fall\.
