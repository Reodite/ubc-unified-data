UBC Finance offers a number of training sessions to equip staff in the administration of their finance functions\. A list of courses from basic accounting to accessing UBC’s online systems is offered on a regular basis free of charge\.

---

## Finance Training 

Finance Training can be found on demand in Workday through the report, [My UBC Workday Training\.](https://wd10.myworkday.com/ubc/d/task/1422$2679.htmld)

This report returns a list of enrollment links for Workday Training courses that are relevant to you\. This list is based on your employment criteria \(e\.g, salaried/hourly/faculty/staff\) and current Workday security roles \(e\.g, Manager, Timekeeper, HR Analyst, etc\.\)

Additional useful resources may be found below\. 

## Finance Resources &amp; Community of Practice

Finance Community of Practice is hosted by FoM, Procurement Partner, Anna Grewal and others\.  If you are interested in attending the informative and well\-attended **Workday AP Q&amp;A** sessions visit, [**Finance Community of Practice: Events**](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/resources/finance/training/finance-community-of-practice/)\.  This page includes other upcoming events, as well as, access to past sessions’ presentations and recordings\.
