UBC faculty members are invited to an in\-person open house event at the Centre for Interactive Research on Sustainability \(CIRS\) on **Thursday, October 19**\.

Meet colleagues from across disciplines working on sustainability\-related topics, and learn how units in the CIRS can support your teaching and research\. Snacks and refreshments will be provided\.

-   **Date**: 1–3 pm, Thursday, October 19, 2023
-   **Location**: In\-person at the BC Hydro Theatre, CIRS  
    2260 West Mall, UBC Vancouver

[Learn more &amp; register](https://sustain.ubc.ca/events/faculty-open-house-centre-interactive-research-sustainability "Faculty Open House - Centre for Interactive Research on Sustainability")
