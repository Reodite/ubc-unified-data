Information and resources about transportation to and from UBC Faculty of Medicine sites\.

---

## Vancouver

-   Visit UBC’s [Transportation Planning](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation) website for information about transit, cycling and carpooling to and from the Point Grey campus\.
-   Visit [UBC Parking](https://parking.ubc.ca/) for information about faculty and staff parking passes on the Point Grey campus\.
-   Visit [Translink](http://www.translink.ca/) for regional transit, cycling and commuting options in Metro Vancouver\.
-   **Interhospital Shuttle**: There is a free van shuttle service for faculty and staff between UBC Hospital, VGH, St\. Paul’s, BCCA and Children’s &amp; Women’s Hospital\. View the [shuttle schedule](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/InterHospital-Shuttle-Schedule.pdf)

## Kelowna

-   Please see [Transportation Choices](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/transit/u-pass-compass-card) for campus commuting options including transit, parking, and cycling\.
-   [Kelowna Regional Transit System](https://www.bctransit.com/kelowna/home)
