Participate in the Fall 2023 Go by Bike Week from **October 16 – 29**\.

Go by Bike Week aims to get more people to cycle more often\. During the event, HUB Cycling encourages people to get out and ride their bikes anywhere and everywhere, and shares resources like maps to equip them with the knowledge they need to cycle for transportation\.

Register solo or as a team with your co\-workers, roommates, family or friends for a chance to win prizes\.

[Learn more &amp; register](https://planning.ubc.ca/go-bike-week)
