# **Welcome to Elentra Cloud** 

Entrada has now moved to Elentra Cloud as of 9am May 11, 2026\.

[**Elentra** **Cloud**](https://ubc.elentra.cloud/)

---

## **What’s Chang**ed

The move to the cloud\-based platform, now called Elentra, is largely a behind\-the\-scenes upgrade\. Day\-to\-day use of the system will remain mostly the same\.

-   The platform you know as Entrada will now be called **Elentra**
-   You will be redirected to log in at a new URL at [https://ubc\.elentra\.cloud/](https://ubc.elentra.cloud/) using your Campus Wide\-login \(CWL\)
-   The new system is hosted in a secure Canadian cloud environment, ensuring compliance with Canadian data privacy standards

#### View Program Specific Changes:

[**UGME Change Highlights**](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/teaching/education-administrative-systems/entrada-migration-to-elentra-cloud/ugme-change-highlights/)

## Known Issues

The following are known issues for the Elentra Cloud Migration that we are actively working on resolving\. 

If you are a PGME resident seeing the UGME dashboard in Elentra, use the Org Switcher to change your organization to “PGME Student\.” This will display the correct program\. Instructions are available [here\.](https://education.med.ubc.ca/faq/pgme-faq/my-entrada-session-changed-i-no-longer-have-my-resident-dashboard-instead-it-shows-what-i-saw-as-an-undergrad-medical-learner/)

When accessing the site via a mobile browser, the sidebar shows by default\. Hide it by clicking the icon below the main banner

## **FAQs** 

Below are answers to common questions:

If you are a UGME faculty or administrator receiving an error while trying to access an undergraduate course page, please submit a support request with your role and the access needed\.

[**Submit Support Request**](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=bd44c124873d56509435a7dd3fbb35c9)

Users can continue to report issues with Elentra or request training the same way they do now by submitting a ticket: [Entrada Support Form](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=bd44c124873d56509435a7dd3fbb35c9)\. Our Support Services team will work with you to resolve your issue or escalate it to Elentra if required\. 

There are no new features that anyone is required to use immediately\. We anticipate minimal disruption, but this will be confirmed through the impact assessment\. 

Elentra accounts are not issued automatically\. If you have an account and are experiencing issues logging in visit this page for [troubleshooting steps](https://education.med.ubc.ca/faq/i-cannot-log-in-to-entrada-with-my-cwl-login-account/)\.

If you do not currently have an account in Elentra and require access, please submit a request to the [Elentra Support Team](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=bd44c124873d56509435a7dd3fbb35c9)

**Access UBC Elentra support documents or reach out to our support team:** The Help icon in the primary navigation will no longer be present in Elentra\. Instead, access help via the Helpful Link, the UBC Elentra Support link can be found on the left side bar of Elentra
