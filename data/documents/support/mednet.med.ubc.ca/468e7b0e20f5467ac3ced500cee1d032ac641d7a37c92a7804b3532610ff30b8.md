The Work Learn &amp; Work Study programs subsidize meaningful work experiences for current UBC students\.

## UBC Vancouver Work Learn

UBC Vancouver faculty and staff are invited to submit a proposal to hire temporary student employees through the UBC Work Learn program\.

The Work Learn program funds approximately 3,500 part\-time hourly appointments each year, subsidizing meaningful work experiences for current UBC Vancouver students\. Submit a proposal for the Summer 2024 session \(May 1–August 31, 2024\) by **January 22, 2024**\.

[Learn more &amp; submit a proposal](https://facultystaff.students.ubc.ca/student-affairs/ubc-career-centre/work-learn "Work Learn | UBC Vancouver Student Services")

UBCV faculty can also submit a proposal for a summer research project through the [Work Learn International Undergraduate Research Awards Program](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/announcements/funding-awards/work-learn-international-undergraduate-research-awards-2024/) by **January 30**\.

## UBC Okanagan Work Study

UBC Okanagan faculty and staff, learn more about the [UBC Okanagan Work Study program](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/hire-a-student/work-study/) and submit a proposal for the Summer 2024 session by **January 16**\.
