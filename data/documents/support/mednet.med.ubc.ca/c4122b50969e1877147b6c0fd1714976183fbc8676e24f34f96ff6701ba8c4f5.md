Learners

Open Close

**Use the Elentra Mobile App for the best EPA completion experience** when you’re on the go or in clinical settings\. Download the app and sign in with your university credentials\.  

[Download Elentra Mobile iOS App](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/elentra-mobile-app/id6462688254)

[Download Elentra Mobile Android App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elentra.elentra&amp;pcampaignid=web_share)

**Please note:** The mobile app does not include a Form Preview option, so forms cannot be previewed before submission\. If you need to preview a form before submitting, please use a desktop, laptop, or mobile browser\.

---

Follow these **steps to access the Elentra Mobile app:**

1\. Download Elentra Mobile app using the links provided above

2\. Enter the UBC Elentra URL \(to be provided at system go\-live\)\. Click **‘Proceed to Sign In’**, then click **‘Single Sign On’**

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/image-14.png)

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/image-15.png)

3\. Enter your UBC CWL credentials, then authenticate via the [Duo Mobile](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/multi-factor-authentication-mfa) step

4\. Success\! If you encounter issues, please [submit a support ticket](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_cat_item_v2&amp;sys_id=bd44c124873d56509435a7dd3fbb35c9) to the UBC Elentra Support team\.

5\.  Review ‘[Getting Started with the Elentra Mobile App](https://help.elentra.com/introduction-to-elentra-mobile)‘ from Elentra Help\. If you are using the Elentra Mobile App to complete assessments, [review the ‘Forms’ help guide](https://help.elentra.com/elentra-mobile-app-complete-forms)\.

If you are using the Elentra Mobile App to complete assessments, see [review the ‘Forms’ help guide](https://help.elentra.com/elentra-mobile-app-complete-forms)

The assessor’s program/department is no longer shown next to their name\. Rely on the person’s **name and email** to ensure you select the correct assessor \(doublecheck before submitting\)\.

**Please note:** You’re responsible for deciding when an external assessor is required\. Only create an external assessor if the person **does not** already have a university/system account\.

New visual summary dashboards support **Faculty Development**, **Program Evaluation**, and **Program Oversight** use cases\. They centralize key metrics and improve visibility into trends over time\. Access is limited to Program Directors and Coordinators\.

-   **Faculty Development:** Faculty assessment metrics including total EPAs observed, average entrustment score, and average words per comment\.

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/Picture8.png)

-   **Program Evaluation:** EPA acquisition metrics for a single program across one or more academic years, including: EPAs acquired per learner, rating and monthly distributions, and average words per comment

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/Picture9.png)

-   **Program Oversight:** Cross program comparison of total EPAs acquired, EPAs per learner, rating distribution, and mean words per comment\.

[Image](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/Picture10.png)
