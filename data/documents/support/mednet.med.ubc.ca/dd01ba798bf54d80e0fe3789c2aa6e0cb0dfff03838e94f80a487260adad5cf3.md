UBC is seeking nominations of faculty members or teaching teams to put forward for the West Coast Teaching Excellence Awards 2026\. The awards celebrate excellence in teaching in colleges, institutes and universities in British Columbia and the Yukon\.

UBC Vancouver &amp; UBC Okanagan will each select two nominees to put forward for this regional award\. Nominations are open until **3 pm on Friday, November 28, 2025**\.

Learn more &amp; apply:

[UBC Vancouver](https://academic.ubc.ca/awards-funding/award-opportunities/teaching-awards/west-coast-teaching-excellence-awards "West Coast Teaching Excellence Awards | UBC Vancouver Provost and VP Academic")

[UBC Okanagan](https://provost.ok.ubc.ca/awards-funding/teaching-awards/west-coast-teaching-excellence-award/ "West Coast Teaching Excellence Awards | UBC Okanagan Provost and VP Academic")
