Applications are open for the inaugural 2025 UBC AI and Health Network Fellows Program — apply by September 8\.

The newly\-launched program, offered by the UBC AI and Health Network, empowers UBC principal investigators to recruit mentor and support postdoctoral fellows, with the aim of cultivating the next generation of leaders driving innovation at the intersection of artificial intelligence and health\.

Funded fellows are expected to engage in projects that align with one or more of the UBC AI and Health Network’s core focus areas:

-   Pillar 1: Clinical Practice and Patient Experience 
-   Pillar 2: Biomedical Innovation and Translational Sciences 
-   Theme 1: Data Sharing and Privacy 
-   Theme 2: End\-user Acceptance of AI in Healthcare 
-   Theme 3: Province\-wide Education, Training and Upskilling 

Submit applications by **12 pm \(noon\) on Monday September 8, 2025**\.

[Learn more &amp; apply](https://aihealth.med.ubc.ca/funding-opportunities/ "Funding Opportunities - UBC AI and Health Network")
