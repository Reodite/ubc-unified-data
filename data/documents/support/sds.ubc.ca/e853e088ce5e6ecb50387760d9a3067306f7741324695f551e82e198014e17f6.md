February 5, 2021

### **Situation:**

The Human Early Learning Partnership \(HELP\) research unit was looking to embark on the development of a 5\-year pricing strategy for their Middle Years Development Instrument \(MDI\) survey tool\. HELP engaged SDS to support them in the pricing strategy exercise for their MDI tool\.

### **Our Actions:**

In tandem with an identified working group from HELP, we were able to:

-   Define vision &amp; principles
-   Identify the market opportunity
-   Verify the market scope
-   Identify and benchmark competitors
-   Provide recommendations around MDI’s suite of services
-   Identify key features within the market deployment strategy
-   Determine the optimal pricing model
-   Create the 5\-year pricing &amp; revenue model

### **Results**:

-   HELP gained clarity on the factors that affect strategic pricing as it relates to the suite of services offered for the MDI tool\.
-   A detailed pricing forecast was created to illustrate the financial impact of recommended pricing and market specific solutions\.
