Why Media Makers

## Great media starts with a plan, the right tools, and people who know how to help\.  
UBC Studios can help\.

01

Plan

Scope your idea, scout your space and prep scripts, storyboards and shot lists with ready\-made templates\.

02

Capture

Shoot, record or animate using gear you can borrow on campus and step\-by\-step setup guides\.

03

Edit &amp; share

Cut it together, polish your audio, and publish to the right platform for your audience\.
