We would like to extend our heartfelt congratulations to the 2026 Summer SoTL Seed Program cohort on embarking on a reflective journey to research and evaluate diverse pedagogical practices\.

**Full SoTL Seed Projects**

-   Bavisha Kalyan – Putting the Action in the Classroom: Working with students to co\-create participatory pedagogies
-   Elizabeth Trudel – Redesigning a first\-year engineering laboratory course to promote inquiry, reflection, and evidence\-based reasoning when faced with open ended problem\.
-   Farzaneh Farhang Mehr – Designing for Sustained Cognitive Engagement and Participation in Large\-Enrollment Engineering Materials Classrooms
-   Jennifer Brown – Teaching Science Communication Design Through Learning Theory in PHAR 480 \(Pharmaceutical Topics and Communication Seminar IV\)
-   Jon Beasley\-Murray – Contract Grading: Motivation and Quality in an Era of Anxiety and AI
-   Katie Fitzpatrick – Between Peer and Expert: The Student Journal Editor and the Culture of Undergraduate Writing
-   Kemi Ola – Exploring Students’ Design Beliefs and Thinking Skills in a Project\-Based Course
-   Scott Covey – Understanding Career Readiness in Students of Molecular Biosciences

**Limited Support Seed Projects**

-   Casey Keulen: Belonging, Confidence, and Embodied Experience: A Phenomenological Study of Women in Manufacturing Engineering in Pursuit of Improved Program Design
-   Jason Min: Engagement and Action for Indigenous Cultural Safety at Burnaby Hospital Pharmacy
-   Moberley Luger: Linguistic Justice and Belonging in the UBC Classroom
-   Lexis Ly: Making meaning of complexity: Using reflection to assess systems thinking, relevant course components, and transferable learning in One Welfare education
-   Yilin Pan: Teaching responsive AI use in research
