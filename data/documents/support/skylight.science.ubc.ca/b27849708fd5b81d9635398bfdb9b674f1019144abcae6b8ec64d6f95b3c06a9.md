A wide range of educational resources is available for instructors and students, including reviews of key principles in teaching and learning, research\-based recommendations on instructional techniques, and science communication resources\. 

### [Carl Wieman Science Education Initiative](https://cwsei.ubc.ca/)

Resources for undergraduate science education through evidence\-based, interactive teaching methods\.

[Carl Wieman Science Education Initiative](https://cwsei.ubc.ca/)

### [Inclusive Teaching](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/resources/inclusive-teaching)

A curated collection of resources to help instructors make their courses more inclusive\.

[Inclusive Teaching](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/resources/inclusive-teaching)

### [Learning Technology Resources](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt)

Resources for incorporating learning technologies into hybrid and online learning environments to support and sustain innovative teaching and learning practices\.

[Learning Technology Resources](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt)

### [Mid\-Course Feedback Surveys](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/resources/mid-course-surveys)

Two Qualtrics templates that instructors can use to get feedback from students, with some best practices for feedback surveys\.

[Mid\-Course Feedback Surveys](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/resources/mid-course-surveys)

### [Pre\-Lab Survey](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/resources/pre-lab-survey)

A pre\-lab survey template that instructors can use to connect with students before the term and find out about their learning and wellbeing needs\.

[Pre\-Lab Survey](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/resources/pre-lab-survey)

### [Scholarly Publications](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/publications)

We contribute to the science education literature by disseminating our work at conferences and through peer\-reviewed journals\.

[Scholarly Publications](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/publications)

### [Science Communication Resources](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/resources/science-communications)

We have collaborated with other units to develop collections of science communication resources\.

[Science Communication Resources](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/resources/science-communications)
