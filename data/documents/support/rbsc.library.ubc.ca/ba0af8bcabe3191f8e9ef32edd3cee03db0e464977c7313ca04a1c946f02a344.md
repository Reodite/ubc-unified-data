Rare Books and Special Collections is well\-known for our extensive collections on labour in British Columbia\. We were excited therefore to see a new digital collection from the University of Saskatchewan on labour in that province\.   The collection contains over 2,494 images or textual documents scanned from over 32 fonds and collections at 8 different archival institutions\.  Users can search, or browse by theme or occupation\. Visit the site by [clicking here](http://scaa.sk.ca/gallery/labour/index.html)\.

RBSC’s labour union records include the [International Union of Mine, Mill and Smelter Workers](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/I/International_Union_of_Mine_Mill_and_Smelterworkers_Canada.pdf), the [Industrial Workers of the World Vancouver Branch](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/I/Industrial_Workers_of_the_World_Vancouver_Branch.pdf), the [Service, Office and Retail Workers Union of Canada](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/S/Service_Office_and_Retail_Workers_Union_of_Canada.pdf) and many others\.  To find others, [search the RBSC site](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/search.html) for the keywords “labour union\.”
