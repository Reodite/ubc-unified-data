Modernize Tailors is a Vancouver institution, having been operating in Chinatown since 1913\. The business and the two brothers who run it, Bill and Jack Wong, was recently featured on Global News:

Bill and Jack’s brother was the well\-known Vancouver businessman and philanthropist Milton Wong, who [passed away recently](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/news/loss-chinese-canadian-philanthropist-milton-wong)\. Watch the Global News clip for the interesting story of their business, the building it is in, and why their brother Milton entered the professional world why they \(both graduates of Engineering from UBC\) kept up the family business\.
