In a new series of blog posts, [Rare Books and Special Collections](http://library.ubc.ca/spcoll) will be featuring a historic document, photograph or map related to one of the B\.C\. towns represented in the room names of the [Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](http://www.ikebarberlearningcentre.ubc.ca/)\.

[http://blogs\.ubc\.ca/rbscnew/files/2010/11/rbsc\_arc\_1546\_1\_24\_0001\.jpg](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2010/11/rbsc_arc_1546_1_24_0001.jpg)[Receipt from A\.C\. Christensen &amp; Son, Bella Coola](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2010/11/rbsc_arc_1546_1_24_0001-300x291.jpg)  

Receipt from A\.C\. Christensen &amp; Son, Bella Coola

To kick things off, we’ll start with Bella Coola which is a small town on the Central Coast of B\.C\.  The Bella Coola area is famous for the [MacKenzie Rock](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Alex_MacKenzie_from_Canada_by_land.jpg), where in 1793 Alexander MacKenzie wrote his name on a rock to commemorate completing the first recorded journey across North America\.  In the early to mid 20\[th\] century, the Bella Coola area was home to the Tallheo Cannery, which is where our featured document comes from\.  The Tallheo Cannery was built in 1912 by the [Canadian Fishing Company](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/inventories/rescolc.html#Canadian%20Fishing%20Comp)\.  The [archives of the Tallheo Cannery](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/T/Tallheo_Cannery.pdf) include administrative records such as correspondence, financial documents, and fishermen’s statements\.  The document shown is a receipt for purchases made by the Cannery from a Bella Coola store, A\.C\. Christensen &amp; Son, who were dealers in “dry goods, boots and shoes, hardware and groceries\.”

In the Barber Centre, the Bella Coola room is number 193, a meeting room on the first floor of the building\.
