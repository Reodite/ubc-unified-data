## [Holiday closure](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2010/12/22/holiday-closure-2/)

[C\.P\.R\. Christmas labels](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/ccimages/ChristmasLabels.jpg)The Chung Collection exhibition space will be closed for the same duration as UBC Library: from December 24 at 3 pm until 9 am on January 4th\.  However, if you are on campus before we close down, please feel free to come and view not only the Chung Collection exhibition, but also a special holiday exhibition in the reading room of [Rare Books and Special Collections](http://www.library.ubc.ca/welcome.html)\. “‘[Tis the Season](http://blogs.ubc.ca/librarynews/2010/12/10/tis-the-season-at-ubc-library/)” features historic children’s holiday books, a collection of West Coast holiday books, and holiday items from fine presses such as Heavenly Monkey, Charles Van Sandwyck and Robert Sabuda\.  The Christmas labels from the Chung Collection,above, are also featured in a case dedicated to the jolly old elf himself\.  The Chung Collection and Rare Books and Special Collections are located on the first floor of the [Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](http://www.ikebarberlearningcentre.ubc.ca/)\.
