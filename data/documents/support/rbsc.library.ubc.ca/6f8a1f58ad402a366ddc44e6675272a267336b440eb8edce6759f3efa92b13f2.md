The end of the year for an archivist can feel like… well, like Christmas\! At the end of the year a lot of people get around to adding to their archives\. As a little preview, next year you can expect to see additions to:

-   The [Douglas Coupland fonds](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/C/Coupland_Douglas.pdf)
-   The [Ann Blades fonds](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/B/Blades_Ann.pdf)
-   The [Chung Collection](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/)
-   The [John Keenlyside Legal Research Collection](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/KL/Keenlyside_John.pdf)

Happy holidays and stay tuned in the New Year for new and growing collections\!
