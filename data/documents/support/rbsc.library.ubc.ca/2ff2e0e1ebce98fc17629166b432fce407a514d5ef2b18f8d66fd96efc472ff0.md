[Click here](http://blogs.ubc.ca/libnews/files/2011/04/4-1-MONTE-Artifacts_Antiques-2.pdf) to read an article in the Spring 2011 Montecristo Magazine in which Dr\. Chung talks about the importance of having a hobby:

*“Everyone should develop an extra interest outside of their profession\. This helps not only to reduce stress but \[there is also\] the joy of finding something you have been looking for, for years\.”*

He also speaks in the article to the experience of restoring model ships, of which there are two on display: the [Empress of Asia](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/exhibition/empress-asia) here at UBC, and the Empress of Japan at the [Vancouver Maritime Museum](http://www.vancouvermaritimemuseum.com/)\.

[Montecristo](http://montecristomagazine.com/) is a Vancouver\-based lifestyle and culture magazine\.
