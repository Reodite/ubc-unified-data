A new initiative from John Price \(University of Victoria\) and Henry Yu \(University of British Columbia\) is bringing together scholars of Asian Canadian studies online\. [Asian Canadian Studies](http://www.asiancanadianstudies.ca/) “is a global network of academic and community researchers that promotes university\-community partnerships to advance Asian\-Canadian studies as a distinct field of study, research and cultural production for social justice\.”  Anyone can register and this allows you to post events, publications, and organizations to the network, and also hold discussions in the forums\. This will be a great way for students, faculty and community researchers alike to connect over their research topics and also keep up to date in the fields within Asian Canadian Studies\.
