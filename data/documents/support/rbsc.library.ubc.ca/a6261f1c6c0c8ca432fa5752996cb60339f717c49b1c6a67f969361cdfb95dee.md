**Rare Books and Special Collections will provide one free hour of scanning to patrons \(per research project\) before the below fees apply\.**

For reasonable requests, there is no additional fees for reproduction, display, or distribution of materials \(i\.e\., commercial use\), however, we do ask patrons to submit a form requesting permission for any use beyond personal research\. To submit a request for the reproduction of materials and / or permission, please use our [online reproduction / permission form](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/visiting/reproduction-requests/)\.

Once you have submitted your order and received an invoice, you can [pay for your reproduction through our secure online store](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/library-account/paynow/rbsc/)\.
