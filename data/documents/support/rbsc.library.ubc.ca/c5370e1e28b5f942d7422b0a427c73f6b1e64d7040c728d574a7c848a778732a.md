## [Featured photograph: snowy mountain hike](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/11/23/featured-photograph-snowy-mountain-hike/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on November 23, 2012

It’s that time of year in Vancouver when we’re all longing for the rain and mist to lift so we can see the beautiful, snowy North Shore mountains\. While we wait, you can gaze upon this photograph instead:

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/mountain_hike.jpg)

In this turn\-of\-the\-century [photo](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/5531), a group of unidentified hikers enjoys a snowy hike on a mountain\. How do we know it’s a mountain? There are a number of photographs in the collection \(found in Box 55\) of the same group showing them ascending the mountain on their hike\. There unfortunately is not any accompanying information with the photographs, so we do not know the exact location\- but they could very well be on the North Shore given the scenery\.

To see all the photos of these folks, a keyword search for “[men and women on a mountain](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7Cmen%20and%20women%20on%20a%20mountain%7CAND/as_media_type/8/media_type/8)” will find them all\. To find more photos of mountains, search for the keyword “[mountain](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7Cmountain%7CAND/as_media_type/8/media_type/8)” with “photographic material” as the type\.

## [Bowen Zhang wins Jack Webster Award](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/11/13/bowen-zhang-wins-jack-webster-award/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on November 13, 2012

Congratulations to Bowen Zhang who recently won the [Jack Webster award](http://www.jackwebster.com/awards/winners.php) for Best Reporting\- Chinese Language for his piece “Heroes of Chinatown,” which aired on OMNI news\! “Heroes of Chinatown” is about the soccer teams of the 1930’s and 40’s in Vancouver’s Chinatown\. Bowen came to the Chung Collection and did some of the filming in the Chung Collection exhibition, and featured a number of Chung Collection photographs in the documentary piece\. We have a [copy](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=5954744%20) of the DVD of the piece in both English and Mandarin for use by researchers at Rare Books and Special Collections\.

To see photographs of the soccer teams in the Chung Collection, you can search for photos and the keywords “[football](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7Cfootball%7CAND/as_media_type/8/media_type/8)” or “[soccer](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7Csoccer%7CAND/as_media_type/8/media_type/8)\.”
