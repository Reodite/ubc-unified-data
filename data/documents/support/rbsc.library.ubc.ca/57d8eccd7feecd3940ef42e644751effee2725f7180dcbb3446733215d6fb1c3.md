The opening hours will be reduced in RBSC / University Archives effective July 30 – August 31\. During this time period, RBSC \(including the Chung Room\) / UA will be open to the public from 10am – 4pm Monday – Friday\. Please direct any RBSC\-related questions to [rare\.books@ubc\.ca](mailto:rare.books@ubc.ca) \. Please contact [Chris Hives](mailto:chris.hives@ubc.ca) with any concerns about University Archives accessibility\.
