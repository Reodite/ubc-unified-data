Leonard Guy McCann \(1927\-2015\)

This past spring, Rare Books and Special Collections at UBC Library lost a dear friend, and Vancouver’s museum, historical, and archival communities lost a leader\. Leonard Guy McCann, who was curator of the Vancouver Maritime Museum for 45 years, and a contributor to our own collections, passed away in late March at the age of 88\. We would like to share [Stephen Hume’s lovely farewell in the *Vancouver Sun*](http://www.vancouversun.com/opinion/columnists/Stephen+Hume+Farewell+museum+builder/11145186/story.html) in celebration of Len’s life and legacy\.
