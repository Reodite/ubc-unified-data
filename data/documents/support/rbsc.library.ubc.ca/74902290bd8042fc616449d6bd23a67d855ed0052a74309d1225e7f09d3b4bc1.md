## [Come and visit\- we’re open\!](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/09/15/come-and-visit-were-open/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on September 15, 2012

The Chung Collection and [Rare Books and Special Collections](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/) are back to our school\-term Saturday opening schedule, meaning we’re open on Saturdays from 12\-5\. This is in effect from today \(September 15\) to December 15 and we will be open on Saturdays in the seceond semester also\. Please feel free to come by and visit the [Chung Collection Exhibition](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/exhibition)\!

UBC Campus is a great destination on a Saturday\- you could start your day at the [UBC Farm Market](http://ubcfarm.ubc.ca/markets-and-events/markets), and visit the [Museum of Anthropology](http://moa.ubc.ca/), the [Beaty Biodiversity Museum,](http://www.beatymuseum.ubc.ca/) or the [Belkin Art Gallery](http://belkin.ubc.ca/) before coming to visit us at the Chung Collection\! Hope to see you this afternoon, or maybe next weekend\.
