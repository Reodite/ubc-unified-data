## [Featured photograph\- a royal visit](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/02/06/featured-photograph-a-royal-visit/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on February 6, 2012

After a brief hiatus, we are recommitted to featuring monthly photograph with the aim of showcasing the diverse photographic holdings of the Chung Collection\.

With Queen Elizabeth’s Diamond Jubilee starting today, Canadians have royals on the mind, as we anticipate a [variety of celebrations this year](http://www.pch.gc.ca/eng/1296669421850)\. The Chung Collection has a [photograph album](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/4070) of the royal visit of King George VI and Queen Elizabeth \(the Queen Mother, mother of our current Queen Elizabeth II\) in May 1939, when they travelled to Canada on the C\.P\.R\. steamer Empress of Australia\.

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/kinggeorge.jpg)

In this particular photograph from the album, the King and Queen are being greeted by First Nations people in traditional dress\. The album contains 26 photographs, many of them taken aboard the ship \(check out their [stateroom](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/9690)\!\)

There do not seem to be any photographs of Queen Elizabeth II in the Chung Collection, although during in 1939 she obviously would have been Princess Elizabeth\. We do however have plenty of photographs of Princess Elizabeth the [steamship](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/simple/results/term/%22Princess%20Elizabeth%22/media_type/8)\!

## [Special exhibition at Chinese Canadian Military Museum](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/01/20/special-exhibition-at-chinese-canadian-military-museum/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on January 20, 2012

For a limited time, you will be able to experience a special exhibition at the Chinese\-Canadian Military Museum entitled “[Chinese\-Canadian Veterans: Loyalty to Country](http://www.ccmms.ca/features/chinese-canadian-veterans-loyalty-to-country/)\.” The exhibition will explore the history behind those Chinese\-Canadians who fought for the Canadian military, despite being subjected to discriminatory laws\. The exhibition will run from January 24 to January 27, so be sure to make it down to the museum during that time\.

## [The loss of Chinese\-Canadian philanthropist Milton Wong](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/01/03/the-loss-of-chinese-canadian-philanthropist-milton-wong/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on January 3, 2012

Vancouver has lost a vibrant member of its business, philanthropic and Chinese\-Canadian communities\. Milton Wong passed away on New Years Eve after a battle with pancreatic cancer\. Like Dr\. Chung, Wong was the son of an immigrant Chinese tailor, who ran the family business out of a historic building in Vancouver’s Chinatown at Carrell and Pender\. Their shop, Modernize Tailors, opened in 1913\. Wong later purchased the building to restore it and convert the upper level to housing for seniors\.

Wong was an alumnus of UBC and was active with both UBC and with Simon Fraser University\. UBC has written up a tribute [here](http://www.publicaffairs.ubc.ca/2012/01/02/milton-wong-1939-2011/), and Todd Wong has written some nice personal reminiscences and a round up of news articles [here](http://www.gunghaggis.com/blog/_archives/2012/1/3/4971389.html)\.

## [Featured photograph: Christmas on board the Empress of Australia](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2011/12/22/featured-photograph-christmas-on-board-the-empress-of-australia/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on December 22, 2011

Where are you spending the holidays this year? Would you spend them on a cruise ship? This is exactly what passengers of the C\.P\.R\. steamship Empress of Australia did in 1929:

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/empress_christmas.jpg)

The [photograph](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/4503) above shows Christmas party decorations on board the Empress of Australia, and the wait staff all dressed very smartly, ready to serve Christmas dinner\. We also have in the collection the [menu](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/3327) for Christmas dinner on that cruise\. They served:

\-Melon Supreme  
– Caviar on toast  
– Eggs Strasbourgeoise  
\-Mixed olives  
\-Celery  
\-Salted nuts  
\-Turtle au Medere  
– Cream of chicken Marie Louise  
– Fried silver smelts, sauce Figaro  
\-Supreme of Turbot Yvonne  
\-Breast of Chicken aux truffles  
\-Coeur d’artichauds  
\-Baron of Scotch beef a l’anglaise  
\-Potatoes persillee chateau  
\-Cauliflower sauce creme  
\-Punch Romaine  
\-Roast turkey, cranberry sauce  
\-Wafer chips  
\-Heart of lettuce  
\-Thousand Island dressing  
\-Xmas pudding  
\-Yule log  
\-Bombe Dame Blanche

Sounds… elaborate\! May you have a wonderful holiday season and an easier to prepare menu than this one\.

To search for photographs and ephemera related to the CPR cruise ships, try searching for keywords such as “[tour](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/simple/results/term/empress%20tour)” or “[cruise](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/simple/results/term/empress%20cruise)” in conjunction with “Empress,” or the name of a specific ship\. Happy searching and happy holidays\!

A reminder that the Chung Collection and [Rare Books and Special Collections](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/2011/12/22/holiday-closure/) is closed over the holidays\- from Dec\. 24 to January 2 inclusive\. We re\-open at 9 am on January 3\.
