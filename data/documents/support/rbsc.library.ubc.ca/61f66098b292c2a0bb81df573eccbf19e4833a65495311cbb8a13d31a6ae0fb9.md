Chung and Madeline H\. Chung Collection and Phil Lind Klondike Gold Rush Collection**\. The new exhibition space in the Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre on UBC’s Vancouver campus brings together two library collections of rare and culturally significant materials from Canada’s history\.

Read more about the Chung \| Lind Gallery:

Thank you so much for your patience\! We look forward to welcoming you to the new space and also introducing you to the Phil Lind Klondike Gold Rush Collection for the first time\.

The [Chung \| Lind Gallery](https://gallery.library.ubc.ca/), on level 2 of the [Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](https://maps.ubc.ca/?show=y,y,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=y&amp;locat1=516&amp;locat2=431), is open Tuesday\-Saturday, 10 am\-5 pm\. The gallery is free and open to the public, and people of all ages are encouraged to attend\. Small group tours and class visits are available by appointment\. For more information, please contact \(604\) 822\-3053 or rare\.books@ubc\.ca\.
