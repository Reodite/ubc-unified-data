If you’re new to Rare Books and Special Collections, you may be surprised to find a spicy introduction through our *Love\! In the library?* exhibition\. The exhibition is located in the back of the Chung Collection exhibition room, within Rare Books and Special Collections, until February 19th \(last day Feb\. 18th\)\.

The focus of most of our collections is British Columbia history\. One case in the exhibition will tell you a love story related to an early B\.C\. printer of city directories\.

[http://blogs\.ubc\.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib\-008\.jpg](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-008.jpg)[Image](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-008-300x200.jpg)

Another focus of our collections is fine press printing\. There are a couple of wonderful examples in this exhibition, including [Barbarian Press](http://www.barbarianpress.com/catalog/index.html)‘s wonderful new *The Play of Pericles* and *Aphrodite’s Cup* illustrated with woodblocks by George Kuthan; we also have Kuthan’s woodblocks, as listed in the finding aid for his [archives](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=3184897)\.

[http://blogs\.ubc\.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib\-004\.jpg](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-004.jpg)[Image](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-004-300x200.jpg)

[http://blogs\.ubc\.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib\-007\.jpg](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-007.jpg)[Image](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-007-300x200.jpg)

Another connection in the exhibition to our archival collections is a love \(actually love\-stricken\) letter from Toni Onley to Yukiko Onley, as it appears in *Love Toni*, printed by Yukiko Onley and Robert Reid\. We have [Toni Onley](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=3157176)‘s archival collection as well as [Robert Reid](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=3157124)‘s\.

[http://blogs\.ubc\.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib\-005\.jpg](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-005.jpg)[Image](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-005-300x200.jpg)

We also collect the archives of several major publishers in British Columbia, including [Arsenal Pulp Press](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=3157124)\. In addition to finding their books in the library catalogue, you can explore their publishing archives through the [finding aid](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=3156702)\.  Arsenal Pulp Press is well known for publishing gender studies, gay and lesbian literature, and as shown in the exhibition, erotica:

[http://blogs\.ubc\.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib\-006\.jpg](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-006.jpg)[Image](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-006-300x200.jpg)

Finally, lovers of books love to express their love through a custom\-designed bookplate\. Rare Books and Special Collections has been building a  [digital collection of bookplates](http://digitalcollections.library.ubc.ca/cdm4/index_bookplate.php?CISOROOT=/bookplate) for a number of years, which now includes over 800 bookplates\.

[http://blogs\.ubc\.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib\-003\.jpg](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-003.jpg)[Image](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/files/2012/02/loveinthelib-003-300x200.jpg)

We hope you enjoy the exhibition and use this opportunity to learn more about our collections\! The exhibition is open during our regular opening hours, Monday to Friday 9 \-5 pm and Saturday 12\-5 pm\.
