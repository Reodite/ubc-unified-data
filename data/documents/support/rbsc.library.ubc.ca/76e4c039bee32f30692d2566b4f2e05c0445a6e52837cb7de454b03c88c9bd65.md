[Appointments](https://libcal.library.ubc.ca/reserve/rbscuareadingroom/space) for Rare Books and Special Collection \(RBSC\) and [University Archives](https://archives.library.ubc.ca/) \(UA\) Reading Room visits are **strongly encouraged**\. Walk\-ins may be possible for easily retrievable materials, but researchers who make appointments will have materials waiting for them when they arrive, avoiding wait times for retrieval\.

[Appointment bookings](https://libcal.library.ubc.ca/reserve/rbscuareadingroom/space) for the Reading Room should be made at least 24 hours in advance, **during weekday business hours**\. In order to allow sufficient time to retrieve materials, particularly from the Library’s automated storage and retrieval system \(ASRS\), we encourage library patrons to provide as much advanced notice as possible for their requests\.

If you have an urgent research request or are unable to schedule an appointment using our [online booking system](https://libcal.library.ubc.ca/reserve/rbscuareadingroom/space), please email [rare\.books@ubc\.ca](mailto:rare.books@ubc.ca) or call 604 822\-2521\.

Reading Room hours for Winter term I \(September – December, 2026\) will be Monday\-Friday, 9 am\-3 pm\.
