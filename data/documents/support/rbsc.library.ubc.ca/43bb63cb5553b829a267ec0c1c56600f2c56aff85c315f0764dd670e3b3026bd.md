The Chung Collection is one of UBC Library’s featured success stories on the [Start An Evolution campaign website](http://startanevolution.ubc.ca/)\. The site describes the impact of Dr\. Chung’s donation of the Chung Collection on teaching and research, and how it helped Dr\. Henry Yu apply for funding to create a [Chinese Canadian history web portal](http://chinesecanadian.ubc.ca/)\. [Click here](http://startanevolution.ubc.ca/successstories/chung-collection/) to read about it\.
