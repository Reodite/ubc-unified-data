## [Workshop announcement: 2006 Early\-Modern Komonjo and Kuzushiji workshop](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2006/02/12/workshop-announcement-2006-early-modern-komonjo-and-kuzushiji-workshop/)

By [kalsbeek](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/kalsbeek/ "Posts by kalsbeek") on February 12, 2006

The Department of Asian Studies at the University of British Columbia is now accepting applications for the 2006 Early\-Modern Komonjo and Kuzushiji Workshop\. The Workshop will take place July 16\-August 12 on the UBC campus in Vancouver, Canada\.
