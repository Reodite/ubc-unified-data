## [“Passage of Dreams” nominated for Golden Sheaf Award](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2013/04/09/passage-of-dreams-nominated-for-golden-sheaf-award/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on April 9, 2013

*Passage of Dreams* is a documentary filmed in 2012 and premiered at a special event earlier this year at UBC about Dr\. Chung and his collection\. We’re really excited to announce that it has been [nominated](http://about.library.ubc.ca/2013/04/09/chung-collection-documentary-nominated-for-award/) for a [Golden Sheaf Award](http://www.goldensheafawards.com/default.aspx?page=207) which is part of the Yorkton Film Festival in Saskatchewan\. Much of the documentary was filmed at the Chung Collection in the Rare Books and Special Collections branch, which was a fun and interesting experience for all of our staff\. Our congratulations to director Karin Lee\!

If you don’t happen to be in Saskatchewan and would like to see the film, DVD’s can be [borrowed](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=6416748) at several library branches\.

## [Featured Photograph: Cherry Blossoms](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2013/04/05/featured-photograph-cherry-blossoms/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on April 5, 2013

If you’re lucky enough to live in Vancouver or a place with a similar climate, you’re starting to see the cherry blossoms bloom\. Vancouverites look forward to this time of year as our city is home to many cherry trees\. According to the [Vancouver Cherry Blossom Festival](http://www.vcbf.ca/the-cherry-tree/history-of-our-cherry-trees), the trend for planting cherry trees started in Vancouver in the early 1930’s when the mayors of Kobe and Yokohama donated 500 cherry trees to be planted in Vancouver, to honour Japanese Canadians who fought in WWI\.

To honour this time of year, our featured photograph for the month of April shows cherry blossoms in Tokyo:

[Image](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/ubcCLF/Uyeno_Tea_House_Tokio_Japan.jpg)

This [photograph](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/12315), like many from the Chung Collection depicting international scenes, is part of a [scrapbook](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/4106) kept by world travelers on the Canadian Pacific Railway lines\. These particular travelers took both the Empress of India steamship throughout Asia, and a train tour in North America\. You can see in the bottom right corner that the photograph was distributed commercially and sold to travelers for their photograph album\- this was common practice before owning a camera for your personal use became common\. This album was assembled in 1910\.

To check out good cherry blossom viewing and related events in Vancouver, be sure to check the Vancouver Cherry Blossom Festival [website](http://vcbf.ca/)\!

## [Featured photograph: Locomotive](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2013/03/18/featured-photograph-locomotive/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on March 18, 2013

Have you come to visit us at the Chung Collection lately? If you have, you will have noticed one of our new additions\- a model locomotive, built by a former CPR Engineer as a retirement hobby project and given to Dr\. Chung several years ago:

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/train_side.jpg)

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/train_front.jpg) We think it’s pretty neat\! We have had great feedback from visitors already\. It’s a very nice companion to the [model Empress of Asia](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/exhibition/empress-asia) steamship, which has long been the “centrepiece” of the exhibition\.

This new addition to the collection got me to thinking about CPR locomotives, and how photographs from the Chung Collection can really be used to show the evolution of train locomotives over time\. Take our new model in contrast, for example, to [this photograph](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/4652) from ca\. 1890:

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/CPR_locomotive.jpg)

What a difference\! The model is of a K1a\-class locomotive, which were in use from around 1930\-1950’s\. If you know what kind of locomotive is depicted in this photograph we’d love to hear from you\- leave a comment, or email us at chung\.collection@ubc\.ca

To search for more images of locomotives, search for [“locomotive” under Photographic Material](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7Clocomotive%7CAND/as_media_type/8/media_type/8), or more broadly for “[train](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7Ctrain%7CAND/as_media_type/8/media_type/8)\.”
