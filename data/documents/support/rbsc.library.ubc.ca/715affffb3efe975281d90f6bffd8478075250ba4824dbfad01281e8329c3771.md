Please note that Rare Books and Special Collections, the Chung Collection and University Archives will be closed on Good Friday and Easter Monday; however, we are open as usual on Saturday the 30th, from 12\-5\. Please check the Library’s [Hours and Locations](http://hours.library.ubc.ca/) portal to see hours for other branches over Easter weekend\.

Amongst our English literature collections at Rare Books and Special Collections is the [Norman Colbeck Collection](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/collenglish.html#Colbeck) which includes more than 450 authors and over 13,000 volumes\. In the collection are two copies of *Christmas\-Eve and Easter Day* by Robert Browning:

Christmas\-Eve and Easter\-Day by Robert Browning

This work was the first Browning published after his marriage to Elizabeth Barrett\. It was published by Chapman &amp; Hall in 1850\.

Easter\-Day by Robert Browning

The two sections, *Christmas\-Eve* and *Easter\-Day* are often referred to as separate, but were published together in this one volume\. To see what other works by Robert Browning, or Elizabeth Barrett Browning, we have in RBSC, you can search the [library catalogue](http://search.library.ubc.ca/#catalogue)\. If you are interested in the Colbeck Collection, the catalogue *A Bookman’s Catalogue* is available both [in print](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=547240) and [online](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=3919528)\.
