Last semester students from the School of Library, Archival and Information Studies class ARST 515 updated a number of archival finding aids to be compliant with the current Rules for Archival Description\. They did a great job and hopefully found it to be a useful real life experience\.  The updated finding aids are:

[Archaeological Society of British Columbia fonds](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/A/Archaeological_Society_of_British_Columbia.pdf), by Joanna Hammerschmidt, Judy Hu, and Asiyih Modarai

[Labour History Association fonds](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/B/BC_Teachers_Federation.pdf), by Sara Church, Carolyn Downs, and Myron Groover

[Patricia Blondal fonds](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/B/Blondal_Patricia.pdf), by Adam Gold and Sarah Rathjen

[Frank Charnley fonds](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/C/Charnley_Frank.pdf), by Shalene Williams, Jen Busch, and Marisa Tutt

[E\.M\. Delafield fonds](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/D/Delafield_EM.pdf), by Ellie Kim, Kelly E\. Lau, and Patrick Ansah

[John Emerson fonds](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/EFG/emerson_john.pdf), by Andrew Berger, Peter Houston, Jayson Kennedy, Tony Power

[George Gee fonds](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/EFG/Gee_George.pdf), by Yolanda Bustos, Tristan Campbell, and Jessie London

[Irene Howard fonds](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/H/howard_irene.pdf), by Sergey Kovynev, Lisa Snider, and Lindsay Szymanski

[International Order of the Good Templars fonds](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/AZ/PDF/I/international_order_of_good_templars_linnea.pdf), by Massimiliano Grandi, Myles Legacy, Jamie Sanford, Amanda Tomé

Thanks to all of the students for their hard work, and to adjunct professor Jennifer Douglas for coordinating\.
