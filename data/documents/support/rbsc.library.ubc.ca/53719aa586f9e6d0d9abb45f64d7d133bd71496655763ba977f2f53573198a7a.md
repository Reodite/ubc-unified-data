## [“The Vintage Appeal\-” Dr\. Chung featured in Trek Magazine](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/12/06/the-vintage-appeal-dr-chung-featured-in-trek-magazine/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on December 6, 2012

We frequently have vistors that count themselves amongst UBC Alumni so we’re thrilled that Dr\. Chung and his collection has been profiled in Trek Magazine, published by the UBC Alumni Association\. You can read the article [online](http://trekmagazine.alumni.ubc.ca/2012/fallwinter-2012/features/the-vintage-appeal/)\. It was written by Larissa Bjus, who also wrote a forthcoming book about Dr\. Chung and the Chung Collection, which we will have available in the library soon\!
