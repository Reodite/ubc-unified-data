[https://library\-rbsc\-2017\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2015/11/HP\-Facebook\.png](https://library-rbsc-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/11/HP-Facebook.png)[HP\-Facebook](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/files/2015/11/HP-Facebook.png)In honour of UBC Library’s [Harry Potter related exhibition and events](http://collections.library.ubc.ca/featured-collections/harry-potter/?login) this fall, Irena Trebic, Reference Librarian David Lam Management Research Library, has created a wonderful [LibGuide for the Harry Potter series](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/harrypotter)\. The guide explores the connections between themes in the books and the collection at the David Lam Library \(books, articles, and theses\)\. The guide also has a link to a [video recording](http://mediasitemob1.mediagroup.ubc.ca/Mediasite/Play/92a3a1b3eed54a8db2c1ba9c78ec0e3a1d) of our recent colloquium [Harry Potter, Brands of Magic](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/c.php?g=397708&amp;p=2703696)\. Enjoy\!
