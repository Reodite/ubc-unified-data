The pamphlet collection, or “SPAM” includes monographs of less than 100 pages, short runs of serials, pamphlets, broadsides, leaflets, posters, prints, newspapers, and off\-prints\. The Rare Books and Special Collections Pamphlet Collection is accessible from the [Catalogue](http://www.library.ubc.ca/home/research.html)\.

There are also online finding aids for the four collections listed below:

-   [Paris 1968 Poster Collection](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=2503139)
-   [Berkeley 1969\-1973 Poster Collection](https://open.library.ubc.ca/collections/berkpost)
-   [World War Posters and Broadsides](https://open.library.ubc.ca/collections/wwposters)
