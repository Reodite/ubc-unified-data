To send a message to Rare Books and Special Collections, ask a question, or provide feedback, contact us at [rare\.books@ubc\.ca](mailto:rare.books@ubc.ca) or our general line at 604\-822\-2521\. You can also find a [list of our team members](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/people/index/SearchDivision.div_id:30) in the UBC Library directory\.

We’re located on level 1 of the [Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](https://maps.ubc.ca/?show=y,y,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=y&amp;locat1=516&amp;locat2=431) at 1961 East Mall\. If you come in through the East Mall side of the building and keep to your right down the short set of stairs, you will see our reading room entrance\.
