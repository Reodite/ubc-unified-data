Dr\. Harry Colin Slim is a UBC graduate and Professor of Music at the University of California Irvine who donated his collection of 123 Stravinsky items to UBC Library in 1999\. As a student at the then Department of Music at UBC, Slim conducted the Canadian premier of Les Noces in 1952\. The collection includes a thoroughly annotated catalogue prepared by Dr\. Slim, in the form a book that provides a detailed context for each item\. This catalogue has been distributed to music libraries throughout the world\.

**Publication:** Slim, H\. Colin\. Annotated catalogue of the H\. Colin Slim Stravinsky collection donated by him to the University of British Columbia Library\. Vancouver : University of British Columbia Library, 2002\.

The collection reaches into many aspects of Western popular culture through the centuries\. The songs are predominantly in English, but French, Gaelic, German and other ethnic groups are also represented\. The collection contains a wealth of material useful in the analysis of the incidence and significance of popular song in society\. “Popular” is used here to indicate that the songs are “of the people”; they are not of an elite nor are they simply a commercial product\. The collection thus omits specifically fine art song and the ephemeral products of the commercial music industry\.

Songs in popular culture include: ballads, folk lyrics, lullabies, nursery rhymes, work songs, sea chanties, hymns, carols, spirituals, gospel, dance songs, national and ethnic songs, soldier songs, bawdy songs, topical songs, political and protest songs, blues, songs for games, parodies, nonsense songs, limericks, community and camp songs; college songs, instrument instruction; songsters, broadsides; biography and criticism; etc\.

**Publication:** Thomas, Philip J\. Indexes for The Philip J\. Thomas popular song collection\. University of British Columbia\. Library\. Special Collections and University Archives Division, 2000\.
