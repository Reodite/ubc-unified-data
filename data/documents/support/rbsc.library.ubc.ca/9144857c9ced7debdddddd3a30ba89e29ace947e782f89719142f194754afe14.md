We’ve been [celebrating the arrival of a new book to our collection](http://about.library.ubc.ca/2016/08/23/ubc-library-acquires-a-copy-of-the-kelmscott-chaucer/?utm_source=Library+Update+List&amp;utm_campaign=62f304aff7-LU_Aug25_2016&amp;utm_medium=email&amp;utm_term=0_e262695394-62f304aff7-335992641?login): *The* *Works of Geoffrey Chaucer,* published by [William Morris](http://www.vam.ac.uk/content/articles/b/biography-of-william-morris/)’s Kelmscott Press in 1896\. The book, more colloquially known as the “[Kelmscott Chaucer](http://www.bl.uk/collection-items/the-kelmscott-chaucer),” is one of only 438 copies printed and one of only 48 copies with a stunning binding created by the [Doves Bindery](http://www.artsandcraftsmuseum.org.uk/Arts_and_Crafts_Movement/Emery_Walker_Library/The_Private_Press_Movement/Doves_Press.aspx) and designed by Morris\. This Kelmscott Press masterwork was described by the poet William Butler Yeats as the “most beautiful of all printed books\.”
