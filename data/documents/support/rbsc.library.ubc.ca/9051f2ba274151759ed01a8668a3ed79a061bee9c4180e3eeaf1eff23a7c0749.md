## [Featured photograph: Locomotive](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2013/03/18/featured-photograph-locomotive/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on March 18, 2013

Have you come to visit us at the Chung Collection lately? If you have, you will have noticed one of our new additions\- a model locomotive, built by a former CPR Engineer as a retirement hobby project and given to Dr\. Chung several years ago:

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/train_side.jpg)

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/train_front.jpg) We think it’s pretty neat\! We have had great feedback from visitors already\. It’s a very nice companion to the [model Empress of Asia](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/exhibition/empress-asia) steamship, which has long been the “centrepiece” of the exhibition\.

This new addition to the collection got me to thinking about CPR locomotives, and how photographs from the Chung Collection can really be used to show the evolution of train locomotives over time\. Take our new model in contrast, for example, to [this photograph](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/4652) from ca\. 1890:

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/CPR_locomotive.jpg)

What a difference\! The model is of a K1a\-class locomotive, which were in use from around 1930\-1950’s\. If you know what kind of locomotive is depicted in this photograph we’d love to hear from you\- leave a comment, or email us at chung\.collection@ubc\.ca

To search for more images of locomotives, search for [“locomotive” under Photographic Material](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7Clocomotive%7CAND/as_media_type/8/media_type/8), or more broadly for “[train](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7Ctrain%7CAND/as_media_type/8/media_type/8)\.”
