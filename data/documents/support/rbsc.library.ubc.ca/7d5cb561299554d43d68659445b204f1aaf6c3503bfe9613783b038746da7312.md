## [Remembering our Chinatowns: book launch and reading](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2011/11/18/remembering-our-chinatowns-book-launch-and-reading/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on November 18, 2011

The Chinese Canadian Historical Society of B\.C\. and the Initiative for Student Teaching and Research in Chinese Canadian Studies \(INSTRCC\) at UBC are co\-presenting a book launch and readings for a trio of books by three local authors next week\. Larry Wong \(*Dim Sum Stories*\), Rebecca Lau \(*Mami*\) and Chad Reimer \(*Chilliwack’s Chinatowns, a history*\) will read from their respective books next week at the Museum of Vancouver\. The event starts at 7 pm \(doors at 6 pm\) and light refreshments will follow\. The event is free for members of the CCHS or the Museum of Vancouver, regular museum admission for others\. For details, check the [Museum of Vancouver events calendar](http://www.museumofvancouver.ca/programs/calendar)\.
