[Image](https://library-rbsc-2017.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/05/congress_background_main-360x203.jpg)UBC is excited to host this year’s [Congress of the Humanities and Social Sciences](https://www.congress2019.ca/) from June 1\-7, 2019\. During Congress 2019, Rare Books and Special Collections will have special weekend open hours on Saturday, June 1, and Sunday, June 2, from 10 a\.m\.\-2 p\.m\. Materials will not circulate, but visitors will be able to enjoy the permanent exhibition of the Wallace B\. Chung and Madeline H\. Chung Collection, as well as the temporary special exhibition “A Queer Century, 1869\-1969\.” We look forward to welcoming you during Congress 2019\!
