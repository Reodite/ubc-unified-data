## [Sun Yat Sen exhibition at Chinese Cultural Centre](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2011/08/09/sun-yat-sen-exhibition-at-chinese-cultural-centre/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on August 9, 2011

Between August 2 and 28, the [Chinese Cultural Centre of Greater Vancouver](http://www.cccvan.com/museum_en.html) is hosting a traveling exhibition from the Memorial Museum of Generalissimo Sun Yat Sen’s Mansion, entitled *Sun Yat Sen and American and Canadian Chinese*\. Dr\. Sun Yat Sen’s visits to British Columbia had a great impact on the Chinese\-Canadian community, which is why Vancouver’s classical Chinese gardens are [named after him](http://www.vancouverchinesegarden.com/about/dr_sun_yat_sen.htm)\. The exhibition is open Tuesday through Sunday, 11 am – 5 pm\.

[Image](http://library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/sunyatsen.jpg) From the Chung Collection, [this image](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/9351) of Dr\. Sun Yat Sen and his wife bears an inscription to the Vernon branch of the Chinese Nationalist League\. For related items, try searching for the keywords “[Chinese Nationalist\.](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/simple/results/term/Chinese%20Nationalist)“

## [Saltwater City for Youth](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2011/07/26/saltwater-city-for-youth/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on July 26, 2011

Last weekend I was very pleased to host a group of summer\-campers who are enrolled in the [Chinese Canadian Historical Society of B\.C\.’s](http://www.cchsbc.ca/) summer youth program, [Saltwater City for Youth](http://www.cchsbc.ca/2011/07/07/saltwater-city-for-youth-vancouver%E2%80%99s-chinese-roots-%E2%80%93-begins-this-saturday/)\. This day camp includes visits and walking tours to a variety of locations around the city, including the Museum of Vancouver, the Chinese Canadian Military Museum, and of course, the Chung Collection\! The participants are learning about Chinese\-Canadian history, citizenship and leadership\. The campers were very enthusiastic and knowledgable, and after the tour they completed a scavenger hunt for historic facts in the [Chung exhibition](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/exhibition)\.

Thank you to the Chinese Canadian Historical Society for the visit, and congratulations on a terrific summer program\!

## [Featured photo of the month: Fruit Ranch at Kelowna](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2011/07/19/featured-photo-of-the-month-fruit-ranch-at-kelowna/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on July 19, 2011

The Chung Collection is a rich source of historical photographs on a mind\-boggling array of subjects\. To showcase this amazing variety, we plan to feature a photograph each month, related to the season or current/historic events\.

In homage to the wonderful British Columbia fruit available this time of year, the feature photograph is of a [fruit ranch in Kelowna](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/9844) taken in July of 1921:

[Image](http://library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/fruit_ranch.jpg)

This photograph is part of an album of photos of a [“journey across Canada,”](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/4071) taken by an unknown photographer\. This is an extensive album of more than 300 photographs from across the country, featuring landmarks, scenery, and shots of the Canadian Pacific Railway\.

If you’re interested in the history of fruit growing in British Columbia, an interesting resource are brochures issued by the Canadian Pacific Railway Company, describing the opportunities in British Columbia for fruit farming\. These can be found in the collection by searching for books with the keyword [“fruit”](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7Cfruit%7CAND/media_type/11)\.

Speaking of fruit, here at UBC we’re all enjoying the [Blueberry Fest](http://www.calendar.events.ubc.ca/cal/event/showEventMore.rdo;jsessionid=A1BFF70089E0D33417226C82FC44382B)\!

## [Celebrate our cultural mosaic at the Your Kontinent festival](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2011/07/13/celebrate-our-cultural-mosaic-at-the-your-kontinent-festival/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on July 13, 2011

From July 21 to 24, Richmond is hosting the [Your Kontinent Film and Media Arts Festival](http://ykfestival.ca/), which is showcasing a variety of films and multi\-media artworks, with an emphasis on Asian film through its collaboration with the [DocuAsia Forum](http://ykfestival.ca/docuasia-forum)\. The name “Your Kontinent” is a variation of the concept of “Urkontinent,” meaning one large continent which would theoretically create a mosaic of cultures on one continent\- not unlike Richmond itself\! Artists and viewers are encouraged to think about “one’s individual perception of their continent or culture\.”

Check out their website for the [festival schedule](http://ykfestival.ca/festival-schedule) or [ticket information](http://ykfestival.ca/tickets)\.

## [From C to C: Chinese Canadian Stories of Migration](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2011/07/08/from-c-to-c-chinese-canadian-stories-of-migration/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on July 8, 2011

Airing Saturday July 16th at 5 pm on CBC \(Channel 3\), “From C to C” is a documentary film which contrasts the struggles of early Chinese migrants to Canada with the experience of Chinese Canadian youth today\. The documentary was produced by Simon Fraser University and [S\.U\.C\.C\.E\.S\.S\.](http://www.successbc.ca/eng/)

More information is available on the [C to C website](http://www.sfu.ca/fromctoc/), including an historical timeline, and sections on current issues and personal stories\.

This documentary won a [Leo award](http://www.sfu.ca/sfunews/stories/tlc-documentary-wins-leo.html), so it’s a must see\! Tune in on July 16th\.
