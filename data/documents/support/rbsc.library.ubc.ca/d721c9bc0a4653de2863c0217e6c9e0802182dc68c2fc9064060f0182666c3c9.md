The planned summer opening hours are:

-    **June 17\-28, 2025: Closed**
-    **July and August, the Gallery will be open Wednesdays to Saturdays from 10 am\-5 pm**

As opening hours are subject to change, please check the [hours portal](https://hours.library.ubc.ca/#view-gallery "https://hours.library.ubc.ca/#view-gallery") for the most up\-to\-date information\.

During our reduced hours, we will have limited availability for guided tours and class visits\.

We invite you to enjoy our [audio highlights tour](https://gallery.library.ubc.ca/explore/chung-lind-gallery-highlights-audio-tour/ "https://gallery.library.ubc.ca/explore/chung-lind-gallery-highlights-audio-tour/"), our [audio guide](https://gallery.library.ubc.ca/explore/audio-guide/ "https://gallery.library.ubc.ca/explore/audio-guide/"), or our [360\-degree virtual tour](https://my.matterport.com/show/?m=UBUqCu2DEYx "https://my.matterport.com/show/?m=UBUqCu2DEYx")\. You can also browse digitized materials from the [Chung](https://open.library.ubc.ca/collections/chung "https://open.library.ubc.ca/collections/chung") and [Lind](https://open.library.ubc.ca/collections/plind "https://open.library.ubc.ca/collections/plind") Collections, and enjoy stories from the [Chung \| Lind Gallery Blog](https://gallery.library.ubc.ca/news-and-stories/ "https://gallery.library.ubc.ca/news-and-stories/")\.

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us through the [RBSC contact form](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/contact-form/ "https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/contact-form/") or by sending an email to [rare\.books@ubc\.ca](mailto:rare.books@ubc.ca "mailto:rare.books@ubc.ca")\. Thank you again for your understanding and interest in the Chung \| Lind Gallery\!
