Running a hand laundry was a popular occupation for early Chinese migrants to North America due to the low start\-up costs\. It was so common that the sterotype of the “Chinese laundryman” became prevalent in North American popular culture\.[Image](http://library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/laundry.jpg)

A program tonight on CBC Radio One’s [Ideas](http://www.cbc.ca/ideas/), promises to go beyond the sterotypes by exploring what it was like to grow up in a Chinese laundry\. Tune in at 9 pm \(9:30 in Newfoundland\)\.

In the Chung Collection, try searching for keywords such as “[laundry](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/simple/results/term/laundry)” or “[laundryman](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/simple/results/term/laundryman)” to see items related to laundries\.
