If you’ve been quietly sobbing into your copy of the *Compact Oxford English Dictionary* since missing last [Friday’s talk by Professor Stefan Dollinger,](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/news/the-oxford-english-dictionary-the-grimm-brothers-and-miley-cyrus/ "Friday's talk by Professor Stefan Dollinger") dry your eyes\! Iva Cheung, a local professional editor and a current student in the UBC course *English 320: History of the English Language,* wrote a terrific [blog post](http://www.ivacheung.com/2013/11/stefan-dollinger-on-the-changing-expectations-of-the-oxford-english-dictionary/ "blog post") about the event, which provides a summary of the talk and some of her thoughts\. Thanks so much for coming, Iva, and for blogging\!
