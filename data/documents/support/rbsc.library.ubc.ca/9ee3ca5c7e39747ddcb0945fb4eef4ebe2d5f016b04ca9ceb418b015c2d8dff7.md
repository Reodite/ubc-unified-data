Robert and Lisa\. Uno Langmann Family Collection of B\.C\. Photographs\. UL\_1597\_0225

Happy holidays from the RBSC family to yours\!

Just a reminder that Rare Books and Special Collections be closed Monday, December 26; Tuesday, December 27; and Monday, January 2\.

RBSC’s offices will reopen on Tuesday, January 3, but our [reading room will remain closed through January 31, 2023 for upgrades](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2022/07/29/reading-room-closed/)\.

During the reading room closure in January, RBSC and UA will be able to provide some reproduction services and remote research support\. Please contact [Rare Book and Special Collections](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/contact-form/) or [University Archives](https://archives.library.ubc.ca/contact-us) for more information\. You can also [contact specific members of the RBSC team](https://directory.library.ubc.ca/people/index/SearchDivision.div_id:30)\.

Thank you so much for your patience and support over the past year during these necessary upgrades\!
