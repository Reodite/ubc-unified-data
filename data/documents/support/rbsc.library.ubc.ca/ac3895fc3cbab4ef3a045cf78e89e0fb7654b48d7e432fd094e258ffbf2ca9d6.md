## [History of Chinese medical services in Vancouver and Montreal](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/02/24/history-of-chinese-medical-services-in-vancouver-and-montreal/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on February 24, 2012

One of the wonderful things about giving tours of the Chung Collection exhibition space is what I learn from the visitors, many of whom have backgrounds in history and knowledge to share\. We recently welcomed students from Corpus Christi College, and their instructor, Dr\. Jacqueline Gresko told me about the Chinese nursing sister in this [photograph](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/8899):

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/hospital.jpg)

Dr\. Gresko explained that the nursing sister was Teresa Fung, who was brought from China to Vancouver to serve in one of the clinics run by the Missionary Sisters of the Immaculate Conception, a Montreal\-based mission which had previously focused on missions and medical services in Canton, but eventually spread to include Chinese immigrants in Canada\. There is an excellent history of their medical services for the Chinese populations in Montreal and Vancouver written by Hugette Torcotte, which is [freely available online](http://www.cchahistory.ca/journal/CCHA2004/Turcotte.pdf) through the Canadian Catholic Historical Association [*Historical Studies* website](http://cchahistory.ca/historical-studies/)\.

## [Linsanity: the 1940’s version](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/02/16/linsanity-the-1940s-version/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on February 16, 2012

[Jeremy Lin](http://tinyurl.com/6lz99zb) is creating serious buzz in the sports world not only for being the first American of Chinese descent to play in the N\.B\.A\., but for the inspiring Cinderella\-esque story that has seen him go from sleeping on his brother’s couch to leading the New York Knicks to 7 straight victories\.

While Chinese\-Canadian athletic history is better known for soccer than basketball \(read a description of Vancouver’s Chinese\-Canadian soccer team on the [Chinese Canadian Stories website](http://ccs.library.ubc.ca/en/stories/viewItem/2/4/25/#%21CHRP_Projects)\) the Chung Collection does have one [photograph](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/9405) of a Chinese\-Canadian basketball team which won a championship in 1946:

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/basketball.jpg)

We would love to learn more about this team, such as what league they played in, and whether this was their only victory\! If you know something about this history and would like to share your knowledge with other Chung Collection researchers, please email us at chung\.collection@ubc\.ca, or leave a comment in the [digital image](http://digitalcollections.library.ubc.ca/u?/coll0803-7,410)\.

To search for items in the Chung Collection related to Chinese\-Canadian athletics, try searching for the subject heading [“Athletes” with the keyword “Chinese\.”](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7CChinese%7CAND/as_subject/Athletes/subject/1142)

## [Featured photograph\- a royal visit](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/02/06/featured-photograph-a-royal-visit/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on February 6, 2012

After a brief hiatus, we are recommitted to featuring monthly photograph with the aim of showcasing the diverse photographic holdings of the Chung Collection\.

With Queen Elizabeth’s Diamond Jubilee starting today, Canadians have royals on the mind, as we anticipate a [variety of celebrations this year](http://www.pch.gc.ca/eng/1296669421850)\. The Chung Collection has a [photograph album](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/4070) of the royal visit of King George VI and Queen Elizabeth \(the Queen Mother, mother of our current Queen Elizabeth II\) in May 1939, when they travelled to Canada on the C\.P\.R\. steamer Empress of Australia\.

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/kinggeorge.jpg)

In this particular photograph from the album, the King and Queen are being greeted by First Nations people in traditional dress\. The album contains 26 photographs, many of them taken aboard the ship \(check out their [stateroom](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/9690)\!\)

There do not seem to be any photographs of Queen Elizabeth II in the Chung Collection, although during in 1939 she obviously would have been Princess Elizabeth\. We do however have plenty of photographs of Princess Elizabeth the [steamship](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/simple/results/term/%22Princess%20Elizabeth%22/media_type/8)\!

## [Celebrate the Lunar New Year this weekend](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/01/27/celebrate-the-lunar-new-year-this-weekend/)

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/goldendragon.jpg)Gung hay fat choy\! The beginning of the Chinese New Year was this past Monday and there are lots of ways you can celebrate the Year of the Dragon this weekend:

The annual [Chinese New Year parade](http://www.cbavancouver.ca/parade.html) begins on Sunday at noon at the Milennium Gates in Vancouver’s Chinatown\. The Chinese Benevolent Association also has a [parade map](http://www.cbavancouver.ca/map.html) online\.

The Chinese Benevolent Association is also presenting a two\-day [Chinatown Spring Festival](http://www.cbavancouver.ca/parade.html) on Saturday and Sunday, at the Dr\. Sun Yat Sen plaza on Pender Street\.

Or, check out the great round\-up of events provided by the [Vancouver Sun](http://www.vancouversun.com/Life/Must+parade+ushers+Year+Dragon/6054383/story.html)\.

## [Special exhibition at Chinese Canadian Military Museum](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/01/20/special-exhibition-at-chinese-canadian-military-museum/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on January 20, 2012

For a limited time, you will be able to experience a special exhibition at the Chinese\-Canadian Military Museum entitled “[Chinese\-Canadian Veterans: Loyalty to Country](http://www.ccmms.ca/features/chinese-canadian-veterans-loyalty-to-country/)\.” The exhibition will explore the history behind those Chinese\-Canadians who fought for the Canadian military, despite being subjected to discriminatory laws\. The exhibition will run from January 24 to January 27, so be sure to make it down to the museum during that time\.

For information on hours and location, see the Chinese\-Canadian Military Museum [website](http://www.ccmms.ca/)\.
