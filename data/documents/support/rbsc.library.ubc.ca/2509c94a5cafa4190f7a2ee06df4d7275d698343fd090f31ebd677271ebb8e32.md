## [Workshop on Taishan and Zhongshan Immigrants in North America](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2010/05/17/workshop-on-taishan-and-zhongshan-immigrants-in-north-america/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on May 17, 2010

The UBC Asian Library is presenting a workshop on Taishan and Zhongshan immigrants in North America, for genealogists, students and scholars\.  After guest speeches and opportunities to collaborate, participants will have the opportunity to tour the Asian Library and the Chung Collection\.

See the [Asian Library](http://www.library.ubc.ca/asian/celebrating50/May18workshop.html) for more information\.

## [Welcome to the new Chung Collection website\!](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2010/05/05/welcome-to-the-new-chung-collection-website/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on May 5, 2010

Welcome to the re\-designed Chung Collection website\! We hope that you will all find this site helpful and easy to use\.  A few highlights:

-   Use the [search](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/simple) or [advanced search](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced) functionality to search for items in the collection\. If you find something you would like to consult in person, it can be requested at [Rare Books and Special Collections](http://library.ubc.ca/spcoll)\.  If there is a digital version available, you will see a thumbnail version in the catalogue record\- click on it to see the full version\.

-   For background information on the subjects of the Chung Colleciton \(early\. B\.C\. history, immigration and settlement and the Canadian Pacific Railway Company\), click and [Themes](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection-themes) and explore the various subjects\.

-   The Chung collection has a permanent [exhibition](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/exhibition), open to the public free of charge\.  The link has more information and some sample photos\.

We’d love to hear your questions and comments\- please send feedback to chung\.collection@ubc\.ca
