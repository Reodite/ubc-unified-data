## [Mark your calendar: Brokering Belonging panel](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/09/27/mark-your-calendar-brokering-belonging-panel/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on September 27, 2012

On October 19th at the Coast Coal Harbour Hotel is a [free public event](http://www.ache-chea.ca/eng/pdf_files/CHEA-Brokering-Oct%2019-2012.pdf) entitled “Brokering Belonging or Contesting White Supremacy: Controversies in Chinese and Asian Canadian Educational Histories and their Implications\.” A four person panel will lead the discussion, comprised of John Price \(University of Victoria\), Lisa Mar \(University of Maryland\), Timothy Stanley \(University of Ottawa\) and Henry Yu \(University of British Columbia\)\. The event will take place from 7 – 9 pm and is co\-sponsored by the [Chinese Canadian Historical Society of B\.C\.](http://www.cchsbc.ca/), the [Canadian History of Education Association](http://www.ache-chea.ca/eng/), and the [Chinese Canadian Stories Project](http://chinesecanadian.ubc.ca/)\.
