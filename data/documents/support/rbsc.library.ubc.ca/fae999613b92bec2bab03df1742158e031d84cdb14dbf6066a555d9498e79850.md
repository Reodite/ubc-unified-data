## [Featured photograph: Locomotive](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2013/03/18/featured-photograph-locomotive/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on March 18, 2013

Have you come to visit us at the Chung Collection lately? If you have, you will have noticed one of our new additions\- a model locomotive, built by a former CPR Engineer as a retirement hobby project and given to Dr\. Chung several years ago:

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/train_side.jpg)

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/train_front.jpg) We think it’s pretty neat\! We have had great feedback from visitors already\. It’s a very nice companion to the [model Empress of Asia](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/exhibition/empress-asia) steamship, which has long been the “centrepiece” of the exhibition\.

This new addition to the collection got me to thinking about CPR locomotives, and how photographs from the Chung Collection can really be used to show the evolution of train locomotives over time\. Take our new model in contrast, for example, to [this photograph](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/4652) from ca\. 1890:

[Image](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/CPR_locomotive.jpg)

What a difference\! The model is of a K1a\-class locomotive, which were in use from around 1930\-1950’s\. If you know what kind of locomotive is depicted in this photograph we’d love to hear from you\- leave a comment, or email us at chung\.collection@ubc\.ca

To search for more images of locomotives, search for [“locomotive” under Photographic Material](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7Clocomotive%7CAND/as_media_type/8/media_type/8), or more broadly for “[train](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/results/as_term/any%7Ctrain%7CAND/as_media_type/8/media_type/8)\.”

## [Gung hay fat choy\! The year of the snake](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2013/01/28/gung-hay-fat-choy-the-year-of-the-snake/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on January 28, 2013

Gung hay fat choy\! Chinese New Year is on February 10, which will mark the beginning of the Year of the Snake\. To celebrate, we thought we’d use our monthly featured photograph blog post to find some snakes in the Chung Collection\.

The Chung Collection actually holds several photographs of snakes\- why would that be? It’s because travellers aboard CPR cruises around the world sometimes took photographs or bought postcards of snake charmers in exotic locales along their journey, such as [this photograph](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/9583) from Trinidad:

[Photograph of snake handlers](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/snake1.jpg)

And [this photograph](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/10143) from Mumbai:

[Photograph of snake charmer](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/snake2.jpg)

Although people born in the year of the snake are thought to be careful, independent and charismatic, we know that snakes the reptilian creatures are not everyone’s favorite\. For those who suffer from ophidiophobia \(fear of snakes\), the Chung Collection has [a photograph](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/9892) for you too:

[Photograph of hedge in the shape of a snake](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/snake3.jpg)

This snake is a hedge of flowers, taken in Vancouver in 1921\. Phew\! That’s better\!

To search for snakes, or any other animal in the Chinese zodiac, use your keyword in an [advanced search](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/advanced/) and specify “photographic material” as the media type\. You can also see our Chinese zodiac [round\-up from 2011](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/news/gung-hay-fat-choy)\. Happy year of the snake and happy searching\!

If you are in the Vancouver area, be sure to check out the Chinese Benevolent Association’s [annual parade](http://www.cbavancouver.ca/parade.html) and the [Year of the Snake Temple Fair](http://vancouverchinesegarden.com/calendar/chinese-new-year-2013/) at the Dr\. Sun Yat\-Sen Chinese Classical Garden on February 17\.

## [New\- Railway related government publications](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2013/01/14/new-railway-related-government-publications/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on January 14, 2013

We’re pleased to let you know that we have added 159 government publications such as sessional papers, reports, addresses and printed correspondence related to the development of railways in Canada and the United States\. This is a great source of information for anyone interested in the political aspects of railway building, and the importance of the development of trans\-continental railways to Canada and the U\.S\.

You can search for all of these publications by searching for the phrase “[Railway Government Publication](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/search/collection/simple/results/term/%22Railway%20Government%20Publication%22)\.”

## [VHF Lecture\- Chinatown as a National Historic Site](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2013/01/02/vhf-lecture-chinatown-as-a-national-historic-site/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on January 2, 2013

We’re huge fans of the Vancouver Heritage Foundation’s lectures, and Chung Collection users may be interested in an upcoming evening lecture, to be held on February 19 at 7:30 pm at the University Women’s Club at Hycroft\. The lecture on “Chinatown as a National Historic Site” will be given by historian John Atkin and city planner Jeanette Hlavach, on the process and significance of Vancouver’s Chinatown becoming a National Historic Site\.

## [Featured photo: 97 lb Christmas cake](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/2012/12/20/featured-photo-97-lb-christmas-cake/)

By [sromkey](https://rbsc.library.ubc.ca/author/sromkey/ "Posts by sromkey") on December 20, 2012

In [last December’s](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/news/featured-photograph-christmas-board-empress-australia) featured photo, we showed an elaborate Christmas dinner being served on the Empress of Australia\. This December, we found another culinary feat on the high seas:

[Chef showing large Christmas cake](http://www.library.ubc.ca/spcoll/images/cc/Christmas_cake.jpg)

According to the [photograph’s](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/12480) caption, the cake weighed 97 pounds\! A challenge to bake anywhere, let alone on a ship\. This photograph appears in a [scrapbook](http://chung.library.ubc.ca/collection/details/4107) from an Empress of Australia world cruise from 1927\-1928\.

A reminder that Rare Books and Special Collections and the Chung Collection will be closed over the holiday season\- from December 25 to January 1 \(more information on our holiday hours [here](http://blogs.ubc.ca/rbscnew/2012/12/20/holiday-hours/)\)\. We look forward to seeing lots of visitors in 2013\. There will be lots of exciting Chung Collection news in 2013, so please stay in touch with us\!
