Due to a software upgrade to our Automated Storage and Retrieval System **many** of our archival collections and **some** of our book collections will be unavailable to request on March 25 and 26\. We anticipate the system being fully functional again on March 27th\.

How do you know if an item is affected or not? If the catalogue record \(like [this one](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=3156948)\) gives the location as “RARE BOOKS &amp; SPECIAL COLLECTIONS ASRS storage” it will **not** be available\. If the catalogue record \(like [this one](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=3156686)\) gives the location as “RARE BOOKS &amp; SPECIAL COLLECTIONS” it **will** be available\.

Thank you for your patience during this time\.
