Open UBC is held in conjunction with International Open Access Week, which encourages the academic community to come together to share and learn about open scholarship initiatives locally and worldwide\. Open UBC showcases a week of diverse events highlighting areas of open scholarship in which UBC’s researchers, faculty, students and staff participate\.
