[Pressbooks](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/) is an online platform for creating and publishing open textbooks\. Students can choose to read the textbook online, download a PDF, read the textbook on their e\-reader, or they could even request a print copy of the textbook, paying only the printing fees\.

## *Getting started* How do I get started?

Drafting a project plan and timeline for your open textbook project is an important step in creating a resource using Pressbooks\. Whether you are working in a project team or hiring students, a plan will provide you with guidance during the entirety of your project\. There are a number of resources to help you develop out your project plan:

-   [Open Textbook Publishing Guide – Project Plan](https://open.ubc.ca/open-publishing-guide/project-plan/)
-   [Project Plan \[Word\]](https://wiki.ubc.ca/File:Project_Plan_Template.docx "File:Project Plan Template.docx") template
-   [TLEF Fund Budget \[Excel\]](https://tlef2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/05/2024-TLEF-LP-Budget.xlsx) and the [OER Fund Budget \[Excel\]](https://open-2019.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/08/OER-Fund-Implmenation-Grant-Budget-Template.xls)

### Timeline

Taking the time to draft out a timeline will give you some guidance on how much work each part of the project will take up\. This will also help you in the budgeting process if you are going to be hiring other people to help you with this project\. We’ve compiled some [time estimations \[PDF\]](https://open-2019.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/OER-Fund-Library-Support_Budget.pdf) based off of past Pressbooks projects \(use the UBC Library [Style Sheet \[Word\]](https://wiki.ubc.ca/images/1/13/Style_Sheet_Template.docx) and [Attribution Tracking \[Word\]](https://wiki.ubc.ca/images/9/92/Attribution_Tracking_Template.docx) templates to save time\)\. UBC Library has created a [Pressbooks Template](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/ubclibrarytemplate/) that provides a general structure to help you get a head start on creating your text\. To import the template, in your Pressbook menu go to Tools &gt; Import\.

Here is an example of what a Project Timeline looks like:

-   [BCcampus Project Timeline \[Word\]](https://opentextbc.ca/selfpublishguide/wp-content/uploads/sites/194/2017/07/BCcampus-Open-Ed-Self-Publishing-Project-Timeline-template.docx)

### Functionality

Pressbooks has several features and options that you can use to enhance your textbook\. *NOTE: H5P and Hypthes\.is are not FIPPA compliant, and should be used to encourage student engagement but not as required assignments\.*

#### Glossary

The Glossary feature allows you to mark and define words within a chapter\. Readers can click on the word and the definition comes up in a tool\-tip\. If you want to create a Glossary page in your back matter, Pressbooks will use the definitions you have already created to auto\-generate this page for you\.

An example of a Pressbooks Glossary can be found in [Spectacles in the Roman World](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/spectaclesintheromanworldsourcebook/back-matter/appendix-i-glossary/)\.

#### H5P

With [H5P](https://h5p.org/content-types-and-applications) you can embed interactive elements into the textbook such as quizzes, clickable timelines, and flash cards\. Through H5P’s iframe embedder, you can embed Google Slides presentations into your textbook, as well as record audio\.

An example of H5P can be found in [Let’s Read French Books](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/letsreadfrenchbooks/chapter/activites-pour-decouvrir-le-texte/)\.

#### Hypothes\.is

[Hypothes\.is](https://web.hypothes.is/) is a browser plugin that can be used for students to highlight, annotate, comment on the text and engage in discussions with other students\. You can create an [annotating group](https://web.hypothes.is/help/annotating-with-groups/) for your class that students can sign up to join, and consult [Student Privacy and Consent Guidelines](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:Student_Privacy_and_Consent_Guidelines "Documentation:Student Privacy and Consent Guidelines") for how to go about doing this\.

An example of Hypothes\.is in Pressbooks can be found in [Publishing with Pressbooks: A Visual Guide](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/pressbooksvisualguide/chapter/hypothes-is/#chapter-350-section-1)\.

#### Media Attributions

Pressbooks has an option to include at the end of every chapter an automatically generated list of media attributions \(using the [TASL](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/best_practices_for_attribution) format\)\. The information for attribution will be drawn from the licensing information attached to each photo\.

An example of Pressbooks Media Attribution can be found in [Laboratory Manual for Introduction to Physical Geography, First British Columbia Edition](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/geoglabs2020/chapter/lab-03-atmospheric-structure-and-pressure-systems/)\.
