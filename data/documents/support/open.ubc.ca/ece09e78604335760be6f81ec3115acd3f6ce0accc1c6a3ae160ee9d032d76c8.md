## [Open Dialogues: Daniel Heath Justice on Decolonizing Open](https://open.ubc.ca/open-dialogues-daniel-heath-justice/ "Open Dialogues: Daniel Heath Justice on Decolonizing Open")

By [Will Engle and Valeria De La Vega](https://open.ubc.ca/open-dialogues-daniel-heath-justice/ "Posts by Will Engle and Valeria De La Vega") on August 19, 2020

-   [Profiles](https://open.ubc.ca/category/updates/profiles/)
-   [UBCV](https://open.ubc.ca/category/campus/ubcv-campus/)
-   [Updates](https://open.ubc.ca/category/updates/)

Dr\. Daniel Heath Justice, a professor in the Department of First Nations and Indigenous Studies and Canada Research Chair, states that if "people are interested in decolonizing open education, they have a lot of work to do\. It's not just a matter of inclusion; it's questioning the very foundations of what knowledge is\. \[Not only\] in the Academy, but also in society\. And thinking very much about how power informs, influences, and limits the possibility of a much more humane and just approach to learning to knowledge and understanding\." [Read More\.\.\.](https://open.ubc.ca/open-dialogues-daniel-heath-justice/ "Read More...")
