As part of this project the Faculty of Geography created OER, including a new open textbook on Natural Hazards and created tutorials for learning open source software \(QGIS\) for GIScience\. They developed 360 video content for accessible field trips and created a new WordPress plugin for field trip management \([http://FieldPress\.ca](http://fieldpress.ca/)\. Students were encouraged to create public scholarship and the project created a website [http://environment\.geog\.ubc\.ca](http://environment.geog.ubc.ca/) to host student\-produced, curated content on environment and sustainability issues\.
