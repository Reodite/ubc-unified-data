The Faculty of Medicine has developed several teaching programs that incorporate a blend of online and face\-to\-face materials that are available to be shared and adapted to various departments in medicine to use\- following the Train the Trainer model\. They also support this adaptation of our resources and will then categorize and share them\. A number of the resources are Creative Commons Licensed [View site](https://facdev.med.ubc.ca/)
