A series of self\-directed courses on sustainable forest management \(SFM\) for the Asian\-Pacific region\. The courses are intended to enable senior forestry students, young faculty and mid\-career professionals to improve their knowledge of SFM flexibly\. All or parts of each course are welcomed to be repurposed in current and other courses and programs to support flipped, blended, and online learning\. Specific courses include: [Sustainable Forest Management in a Changing World](http://blogs.ubc.ca/apfnet01/); [Forest Governance, Public Relations, and Community Development](http://blogs.ubc.ca/apfnet02/); [International Dialogue on Forestry Issues](http://blogs.ubc.ca/apfnet03/); [Restoration of Degraded Forest Ecosystems and Forest Plantation Development](http://blogs.ubc.ca/apfnet05/course-overview/course-syllabus/); [Sustainable Use of Forest Ecosystem Services](http://blogs.ubc.ca/apfnet04/); and [Forest Resource Management and Protection](http://blogs.ubc.ca/apfnet06/) [View site](http://apfecm.forestry.ubc.ca/sfm-online-courses/)
