iPeer is an open source web application that allows instructors to develop and deliver rubric\-based peer evaluations, to review and release student comments and scoring\. Instructors can analyze evaluation results and provide students with grades and feedback for their group performance\. [View site](http://ipeer.ctlt.ubc.ca/)
