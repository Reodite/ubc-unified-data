StatSpace brings together open, vetted and adaptable resources including animations, web visualizations, activities and questions, for teaching and learning statistics, with an emphasis on introductory statistics\. StatSpace also provides suggestions for use, including teaching pitfalls and how students use and misuse resources\. StatSpace was developed as part of the UBC Flexible Learning Introductory Statistics Project initially funded by The University of British Columbia’s Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund, The Faculty of Medicine, The Faculty of Science, and The Faculty of Arts\. Additional support has been provided by the Statistical Society of Canada, UBC’s Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology, UBC Faculty of Science’s Skylight and the UBC Department of Statistics\.
