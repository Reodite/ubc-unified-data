[Someone holding Wikipedia with their hands](https://open-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/07/Wikipedia_mini_globe_handheld-150x150.jpg) To celebrate [World Soil Day](http://www.fao.org/world-soil-day/about-world-soil-day/en/) on December 5th, UBC will be hosting a [Wikipedia Edit\-a\-thon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Meetup/World_Soil_Day_2017_Edit-a-thon) focused on adding more content to Wikipedia about soils and soil science\.

Editing Wikipedia is a way for faculty, students and staff to engage in open collaboration, and contribute to public knowledge\. Wikipedia Edit\-a\-thons improve the encyclopedia and can be a great way to work with the platform and consider how it might be used in your courses or teachinag and learning practice\.

The event will take place on **Tuesday, December 5, 2017 from 1 to 4pm in the Orchard Commons Study Lounge**\.  More information is available on [the event project page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Meetup/World_Soil_Day_2017_Edit-a-thon)\. The edit\-a\-thon is open and no registration is needed\. Please bring your own laptop\. No prior experience necessary\!

### Event Details

-   *Location*: Orchard Commons Study Lounge, 6363 Agronomy Rd\., UBC campus, Vancouver, BC, Canada, or Online
-   *Time*: 1 to 4pm PST
-   *Event URL*: [https://en\.wikipedia\.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Meetup/World\_Soil\_Day\_2017\_Edit\-a\-thon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Meetup/World_Soil_Day_2017_Edit-a-thon)
