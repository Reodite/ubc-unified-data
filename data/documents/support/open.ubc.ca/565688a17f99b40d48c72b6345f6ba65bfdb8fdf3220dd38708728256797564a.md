## Repository Services

Get help depositing your scholarly works to cIRcle, UBC’s open access research repository\. Our services include : DOI and persistent links; permissions management; standardized metadata for discovery and access; usage statistics and more\. Find out more at [circle\.ubc\.ca](http://circle.ubc.ca/)

## Open Learning Technologies

UBC’s Learning Technology ecosystem contains many different learning technologies that can be used to support open education projects including publishing and collaboration tools \([UBC Wiki](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Main_Page), [UBC Blogs](https://blogs.ubc.ca/), [UBC CMS](https://cms.ubc.ca/)\), interactive content \([UBC H5P Open Hub](https://h5p.open.ubc.ca/)\), open problem sets \([WebWorK](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/webwork-instructor-guide/)\), computational tools \([Open JupyterHub](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/jupyterhub-instructor-guide/)\), and more\.
