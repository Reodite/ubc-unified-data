## [Open UBC Snapshot 2020: Significant Commitments to Open Resources](https://open.ubc.ca/2020-open-ubc-snapshot/ "Open UBC Snapshot 2020: Significant Commitments to Open Resources")

By [Will Engle](https://open.ubc.ca/author/wmengle/ "Posts by Will Engle") on March 9, 2020

-   [Open UBC Snapshot](https://open.ubc.ca/category/updates/reports/open-snapshots/)
-   [Reports](https://open.ubc.ca/category/updates/reports/)
-   [UBCV](https://open.ubc.ca/category/campus/ubcv-campus/)
-   [Updates](https://open.ubc.ca/category/updates/)

In the 2019/20 academic year \(September 2019 to April 2020\), an estimated 18,440 students enrolled in courses using open resources in place of paid textbooks or readings\. This replacement of traditional textbooks with open resources has potentially saved UBC students an estimated $1\.8 to $2\.5 million this academic year\. [Read More\.\.\.](https://open.ubc.ca/2020-open-ubc-snapshot/ "Read More...")
