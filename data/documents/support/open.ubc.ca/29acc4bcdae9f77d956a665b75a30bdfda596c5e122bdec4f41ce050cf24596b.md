The Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(TLEF\) was created to enrich student learning by supporting innovative and effective educational enhancements\. It is financed entirely by a portion of the student tuition paid to UBC Vancouver\. Each year, the Office of the Provost invites all UBC\-V Faculties and Colleges to apply for TLEF funding for initiatives that will improve the student learning experience at the University\. A priority focus for these grants is the creation and integration of open educational resources to make education more affordable and accessible to students\.
