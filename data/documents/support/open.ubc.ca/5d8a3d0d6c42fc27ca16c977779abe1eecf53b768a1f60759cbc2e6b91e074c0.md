UBC Library’s Open Collections include digital photos, books, newspapers, maps, videos, theses and more\. These publicly\-accessible collections are constantly growing and reflect the research interests of the UBC community and beyond\. Open Collections includes a research API allowing users to run powerful queries, perform advanced analysis, and build custom views, apps, and widgets with full access to the Open Collections’ metadata and transcripts\.
