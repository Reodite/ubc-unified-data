LAST100, “Introduction to Latin American Studies” provides an overview of the culture and society of Latin America from ancient to contemporary times, and from Argentina to Mexico\. Particular attention is paid to the ways in which the region is constructed and represented, and to the cultural politics of race, gender, and class\. The course website provides provides Creative Commons licensed videos and other materials from the instructor and students\. [View site](http://last100.arts.ubc.ca/)
