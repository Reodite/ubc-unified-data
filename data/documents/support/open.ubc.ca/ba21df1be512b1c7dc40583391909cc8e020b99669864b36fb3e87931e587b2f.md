The UBC Okanagan Open Education Working Group develops awareness and builds capacity for the adoption, adaption, and creation of open educational resources at the University of British Columbia Okanagan by sharing information and coordinating efforts among key stakeholders who lead and support open education initiatives on campus and across the university\. The UBC Okanagan Open Education Working Group commits to working in a coordinated and synergistic manner

The current goals of the working are:

-   Develop a data collection strategy and create an inventory of open educational practices at UBC Okanagan\.
-   Plan and deliver open education events for advocacy and promotion and collaborate with UBC Vancouver to enhance offerings and build community\.
-   Support student advocacy around OER\. 

To join the working group, contact Donna Langille \([donna\.langille@ubc\.ca](mailto:donna.langille@ubc.ca)\) Community Engagement &amp; Open Education Librarian\.
