Arts One Open is an open, online extension or complement to [UBC’s Arts One program](https://artsone-open.arts.ubc.ca/) that enables anyone to join this voyage of discovery and critical analysis\. It provides Creative Commons licensed recordings and other material from lectures given by some of UBC’s most experienced and distinguished teachers\. These instructors hope to provoke you to think in new ways about authors from Plato to Shakespeare, Defoe to Coetzee, and about issues such as knowledge, monstrosity, science, and politics\. [View site](https://artsone-open.arts.ubc.ca/)
