A 2011 survey by the Wikimedia Foundation found that fewer than 10% of Wikipedia contributors identified as women\. In 2014, Art \+ Feminism was launched by librarians, artists, curators, and educators to correct the gender imbalance in Wikipedia contributors and content\. Since then, the project has grown worldwide\.

[Icon on the Art \+ Feminism Edit\-a\-thon](https://open-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/02/ArtFeminism.png)

This year, the **UBC Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery** – in collaboration with the Art History Students’ Association \(AHSA\); the Art History, Visual Art &amp; Theory Department \(AHVA\); Centre A; Hatch Gallery; the Music, Art &amp; Architecture Library; and the Visual Art Students’ Association \(VASA\) – will host an Art \+ Feminism Wikipedia Edit\-a\-thon event\.

This in\-person event will be held at the Belkin from **11 am\-2:30 pm** on **Tuesday March 12, 2024**\. 

We invite participants of all genders and expressions to join us and help balance representational imbalances at this year’s event\.

More details on how to participate can be found here:
