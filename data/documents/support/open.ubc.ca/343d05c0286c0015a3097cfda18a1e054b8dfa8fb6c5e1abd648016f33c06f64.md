## [Open UBC Snapshot: Significant Use and Support for Open Resources](https://open.ubc.ca/2019-open-ubc-snapshot/)

By [Rie Namba](https://open.ubc.ca/author/rienamba/ "Posts by Rie Namba") on January 3, 2019 in [Uncategorized](https://open.ubc.ca/category/uncategorized/)  

---

In 2018, UBC published a new [Strategic Plan](https://strategicplan.ubc.ca/) that articulated the intention to expand the creation and dissemination of open educational resources as well as recognized the contributions that UBC faculty, student and staff have made in this area\. These contributions have had a significant impact: in academic year 2018, an estimated 15,388 students were impacted by courses using open resources in place of paid textbooks or readings\. This replacement of traditional textbooks with open resources has potentially saved UBC students an estimated $1\.5 to $2\.3 million dollars this academic year\. The wide diversity of UBC open education initiatives and efforts, from strong AMS advocacy and innovative instructor efforts to increased strategic support and funding, is helping to ensure that the adoption and use of open resources continues to be a significant practice at UBC\.
