Interactive business analytics content, right from the classroom — Harish Krishnan, professor of operations and logistics at UBC’s Sauder School of Business, saw an opportunity to use open resources to develop in\-demand business analytics skills using ‘real\-life’ business problems\.

Inspired by open code repositories used in disciplines like data science and computer science, his newly\-launched hub [Analytics@Sauder](https://analytics-at-sauder.github.io/) hosts open and interactive business analytics materials\.

Students were employed to help build the site and content with a grant from the Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund\. And while the site is live, Harish is quick to note that this is not a final product — he aims to build a community by encouraging participation from students, instructors and alumni in related disciplines\.
