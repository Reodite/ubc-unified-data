This course covers the legal and regulatory aspects of telecommunications, broadcasting and the Internet\. In addition to dealing with the legal and regulatory aspects of statutorily determined quasi\-monopoly business environments regulated by the CRTC and subject to administrative law oversight, the course encompasses the digital age of emergent technologies that provide a significant degree of freedom and control to individual users\. The instructors are providing open access to course content, including lecture notes and slides, as well as open discussion on the course site\.
