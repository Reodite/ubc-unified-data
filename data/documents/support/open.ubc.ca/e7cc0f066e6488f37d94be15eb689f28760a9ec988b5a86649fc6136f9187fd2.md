The [Allard Research Commons \(ARC\)](https://commons.allard.ubc.ca/) is the open access institutional repository of scholarship for the Peter A\. Allard School of Law at UBC\. [DTES Research Access Portal](https://open.ubc.ca/dtes-research-access-portal/)
