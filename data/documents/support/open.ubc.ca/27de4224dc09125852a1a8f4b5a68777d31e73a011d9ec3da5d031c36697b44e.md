This is an open\-access introductory logic textbook, prepared by Jonathan Ichikawa, based on P\.D\. Magnus’s forallx\. This book is an introduction to sentential logic and first\-order predicate logic with identity, logical systems that significantly influenced twentieth\-century analytic philosophy\. It contains content, practice exercises, symbolic notations, and solutions to selected exercises\. [View Textbook](https://philpapers.org/rec/MAGFXU)
