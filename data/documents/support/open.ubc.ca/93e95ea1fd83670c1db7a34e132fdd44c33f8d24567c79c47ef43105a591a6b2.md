### Making Research Accessible initiative

May 21, 2019

The Making Research Accessible Initiative seeks to improve access to academic research and community\-generated materials with a focus on Vancouver’s Downtown Eastside \(DTES\)\. The need to access high\-quality research and information was brought to the attention of the UBC Learning Exchange by people living and working in the DTES\. These groups voiced their concern with research projects that extracted information and findings that were locked behind publisher paywalls\. Members of community organizations also identified the need to preserve community\-generated materials\.

  
  
[https://learningexchange\.ubc\.ca/community\-based\-research/making\-research\-accessible\-initiative/?login](https://learningexchange.ubc.ca/community-based-research/making-research-accessible-initiative/?login)

### StatSpace

March 1, 2021

StatSpace brings together open, vetted and adaptable resources including animations, web visualizations, activities and questions, for teaching and learning statistics, with an emphasis on introductory statistics\. StatSpace also provides suggestions for use, including teaching pitfalls and how students use and misuse resources\. StatSpace was developed as part of the UBC Flexible Learning Introductory Statistics Project initially funded by The University of British Columbia’s Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund, The Faculty of Medicine, The Faculty of Science, and The Faculty of Arts\. Additional support has been provided by the Statistical Society of Canada, UBC’s Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology, UBC Faculty of Science’s Skylight and the UBC Department of Statistics\. [https://statspace\.elearning\.ubc\.ca/](https://statspace.elearning.ubc.ca/)

### UBC Projects in the Open Science Framework

May 21, 2019

This is a list of UBC faculty and staff using the Open Science Framework \(OSF\), a free and open source project management repository that supports researchers across their entire project lifecycle\. OSF helps researchers work on projects privately with a limited number of collaborators and make parts of their projects public, or make all the project publicly accessible for broader dissemination with citable, discoverable DOIs\.
