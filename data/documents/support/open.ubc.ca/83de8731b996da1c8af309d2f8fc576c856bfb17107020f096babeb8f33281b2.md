The Learning Commons is an evolving collection of UBC\-selected learning resources that guide students through a process of discovery\. Through an emphasis on student\-driven initiatives and shared\-decision making, the Learning Commons enriches the academic support and enhance the experience of all UBC students\. It’s original content is published under the Creative Commons\-Attribution 4\.0 license to allow for sharing and re\-publishing\. [View site](http://www.learningcommons.ubc.ca/)
