Reading books is a great way to learn a language\. It helps to discover the culture, to learn new words, to be exposed to different language structures in the context\. But, one of the biggest challenges in the language and literature classes is to encourage the students to read\. This project aims to make reading literary books easier for the students by creating an open textbook reader using Public Domain 19th century French literature\. Along with the text, there are activities for students to engage with and discover the text\. [https://pressbooks\.bccampus\.ca/letsreadfrenchbooks/](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/letsreadfrenchbooks/)
