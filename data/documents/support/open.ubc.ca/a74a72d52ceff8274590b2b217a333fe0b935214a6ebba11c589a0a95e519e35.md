### Support Available

-   in\-house support for UBC Wiki and WordPress are available at the **WordPress and Wiki clinic** from **1\-3pm** every **Thursday**, located at the [LT Hub](http://lthub.ubc.ca/) \(Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre, room 2\.27 Fraser River Room\) \.
-   If you are not available during that time, basic support is also available at the LT Hub from **9am – 4:30 pm** as well\.
-   Alternatively, you can also email to [lt\.hub@ubc\.ca](mailto:lt.hub@ubc.ca) if you are looking for support\.
