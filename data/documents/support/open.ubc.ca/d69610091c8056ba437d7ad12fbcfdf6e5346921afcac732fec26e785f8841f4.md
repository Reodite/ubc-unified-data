The UBC Okanagan Open Education Working Group is conducting a short survey about open educational practices at UBC Okanagan\. The results from this survey will help inform future programming, policy development, and other open education initiatives across campus\.  
The survey is open to all those who teach at UBC Okanagan\. To get started, visit:  
 **[ubc\.ca1\.qualtrics\.com/jfe/form/SV\_7ZE1KXetmTO4WNw](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_7ZE1KXetmTO4WNw)**
