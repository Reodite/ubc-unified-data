## [Allard Research Commons](https://open.ubc.ca/allard-research-commons/)

By [Will Engle](https://open.ubc.ca/author/wmengle/ "Posts by Will Engle") on January 22, 2025 in [Collections](https://open.ubc.ca/category/access-examples/collections/), [UBC Collections](https://open.ubc.ca/category/access-examples/ubc-collections/)

The [Allard Research Commons \(ARC\)](https://commons.allard.ubc.ca/) is the open access institutional repository of scholarship for the Peter A\. Allard School of Law at UBC\. ARC preserves the broad range of scholarship done by scholars at or affiliated with Allard Law and makes that scholarship available to the public\.
