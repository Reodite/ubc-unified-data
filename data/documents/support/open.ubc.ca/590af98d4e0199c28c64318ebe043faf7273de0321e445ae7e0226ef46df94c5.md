Phyto’pedia is an online encyclopaedia of common phytoplankton from the coast of British Columbia, Canada\. Inside, the reader will find an extensive database of high\-resolution images indicating the characteristic features of a variety of genera and species paired with carefully written descriptions\. [View Site](https://www.eoas.ubc.ca/research/phytoplankton/)
