UBC’s English 470D \(Canadian Studies\), focuses on the intersection of Canadian Literature and Web 2\.0\. In 2017, the course featured a Wikipedia Edit\-a\-thon in which students were asked to address the exigence of equitable representation in Wikipedia by contributing new or expanding existing articles about Canadian literature\.
