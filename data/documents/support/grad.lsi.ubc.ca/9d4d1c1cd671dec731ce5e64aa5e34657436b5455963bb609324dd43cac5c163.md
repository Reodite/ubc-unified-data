## Wondering how to buy gifts for your labmates, friends, family

## and pay tuition in January?

## Answer: Tuition Deferral\!\!\!

UBC offers a tuition deferral option to students receiving a stipend \(aka Research Assistantship\) or Teaching Assistantship\. Tuition is removed from your paycheques over the course of the semester\.

Download [Tuition Payroll Deuctions/Fee Deferral Application and Instructions](https://gradlsi1.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/11/fees_tuitiondeferralUBC.pdf)\.
