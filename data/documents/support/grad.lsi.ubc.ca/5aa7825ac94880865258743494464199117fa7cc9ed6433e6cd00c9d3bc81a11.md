## [Centre for Blood Research \(CBR\)](https://grad.lsi.ubc.ca/2010/09/08/centre-for-blood-research-cbr/ "Centre for Blood Research (CBR)")

By [admin](https://grad.lsi.ubc.ca/author/admin/ "admin") on September 8, 2010 (Wednesday, September 8th, 2010, 11:00 am)

Impairments in blood and the circulation of the blood are features common to all diseases\. CBR trainees have opportunities to apply a broad range of leading edge basic science, biotechnological, engineering and preclinical and clinical investigative approaches to address the major challenges in blood, blood\-related diseases and processes\.
