After Fify and I started in December 2021/January 2022, we found that a lot of our Division Members were looking for more opportunities to connect in non\-clinical settings\. A few Saturdays ago, we hosted one of our first initiatives to address this: the 1st Annual Site Lead Appreciation Event\.

The team from [Art from the Heart](https://www.artfromtheheartyvr.com/about) hosted us and led us step by step to create these beautiful sunset/mountainscapes in acrylic on canvas\. The feedback was very positive and we look forward to hosting similar events in the future\.

A big **THANK YOU** to everyone in a Site Lead and/or a Preceptor role for our Palliative Care residents and off\-service residents\. Your hard work and dedication, does NOT go unnoticed\. We couldn’t support/develop our future palliative colleagues and palliative skills without what you do\.
