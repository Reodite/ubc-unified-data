##### **We are pleased to resume our Faculty Spotlight series, highlighting the outstanding work and contributions of our faculty members\. We’re excited to restart the series with Dr\. Sabrina Gustafson\-Vickers, whose work and dedication continue to make a meaningful impact in our community\. Read below to learn more about her:**

***· What inspired you to choose palliative care/family medicine?***

I observed a palliative care physician in Vancouver on her rounds when I was a medical student on elective\.  How she sat with the patient, human to human\. I knew from that moment that this was the branch of medicine I wanted to practice\.

***· Who has been an influential mentor for you, and why?***

Dr\. Charlie Chen was my YAC program director and first mentor\.  His use of nonviolent communication to aid how we relate with patients and with each other was eye opening to me\.  And his passion and aptitude for medical education\!

***· What part of your training has surprised you the most?***

Patients teach me something new every day; I still have not come away from an encounter feeling unsurprised\.

***· If you weren’t in medicine, what career might you have chosen?***

Neuroscientist

***· What’s something outside of medicine that brings you joy?***

Being in nature with my kids

***· A podcast, show, or movie you’d recommend right now:***

All There Is with Anderson Cooper

***· Where’s your favourite place to relax or recharge?***

At my kitchen table with a warm coffee and my journal

***· Favourite comfort food:***

Is coffee a food?

***· Favourite way to spend a day off:***

A long run followed by channeling my inner chef to make a gourmet meal for people I love

***· A small thing that always makes you smile:***

A handwritten note left on my desk from a colleague, either with handover, or an update on cookies in the staff room, or just to say hi
