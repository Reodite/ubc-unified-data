Today’s second half of our double feature spotlights Dr\. Sheila Webster from the Interior\!  Read on to learn about her favourite travel destination \(not what you may think\!\) and her favourite Canadian read\.

**Site\(s\):  
**Interior Health \(Penticton and Kelowna\)

**What’s the best part about your job?  
**The privilege of accompanying our peers  \(\=patients\) along their journey\.

**Favourite song:**

Anything Blue Rodeo

**Favourite past\-time activity:  
**Bird watching

**Favourite restaurant in your community:**

Terrafina

**Favourite travel destination:**

British Columbia, in particular the south Okanagan

**A book or article you would recommend:**

A New Season by Terry Fallis \(lighthearted for a good Canadian laugh\)

**What are you creating in the world right now?**

I would hope to be creating a widespread, integrated palliative care program to assist family doctors in Interior Health but it is a tough slog…\.

**Best piece of advice for new physicians:**

Be curious
