### [**BC Centre for Palliative Care**](https://bc-cpc.ca/)

This has palliative resources for patients, families and health care professionals in BC\.

### [Canadian Virtual Hospice](http://www.virtualhospice.ca/)

This is a Canadian website which contains a lot of useful information for both patients and families as well as health care professionals

### [Cicely Saunders Institute](http://www.csi.kcl.ac.uk/)

This is a British site with similar goals to Canadian Virtual Hospice, but is based around a physical site of care delivery\.
