## [The CSPCP Humanitarian Award: Congratulations Ed Dubland\!](https://palliativecare.med.ubc.ca/the-cspcp-humanitarian-award-congratulations-ed-dubland/)

By [chloe yeh](https://palliativecare.med.ubc.ca/author/chloe-yeh/ "Posts by chloe yeh") on March 20, 2020

Congratulations to Ed Dubland for being awarded with the CSPCP Humanitarian Award\! The Humanitarian Award is given annually to a CSPCP member who significantly improves the lives of underserved populations nationally or internationally through direct service provision and/or development of humanitarian programs\.

## [2019 MCC Outstanding Achievement Award in the Evaluation of Clinical Competence](https://palliativecare.med.ubc.ca/2019-mcc-outstanding-achievement-award-in-the-evaluation-of-clinical-competence/)

By [chloe yeh](https://palliativecare.med.ubc.ca/author/chloe-yeh/ "Posts by chloe yeh") on August 30, 2019

Rose Hatala was recently awarded with the ‘2019 MCC Outstanding Achievement Award in the Evaluation of Clinical Competence’\. This award recognizes Rose’s excellence in Canadian health care and health\-care research\. The recipient is someone who ensures that health\-care professionals meet the high standards that Canadians enjoy and expect\.  Congratulations, Rose\!
