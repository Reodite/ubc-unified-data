**Associate Head of Research: Dr\. Pippa Hawley**

Let’s get to meet our new Associate Heads beyond their impressive CV’s\. Here’s a quick Q&amp;A we did with our new leadership team:

1.  [Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/09/Screen-Shot-2022-09-08-at-9.27.09-AM-225x300.png)
    
    Pippa and Sid the Cat
    
    **Where is the farthest country you’ve travelled to from Vancouver and a notable memory from the trip?** Australia—When I was in Perth, during a day trip, we decided to take a “short cut” back to the holiday house we were staying in\. Needless to say, we got lost, and we’re just ready to give up and hike back the way we came, which would have taken hours, but came into a trail which brought us out right to the house\. It was quite an adventure\!

    Bean Around the World, but ​not fussy, Skimmed Decaf Latte \(“why bother?”\)
3.  **What is the boardgame/card game of choice you would choose to play?**  
    Scrabble, by a mile
