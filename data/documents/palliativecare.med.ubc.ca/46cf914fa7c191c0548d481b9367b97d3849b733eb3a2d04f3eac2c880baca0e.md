Earlier this week we bid ado to our newly minted 2023 Palliative Care graduates\. It has been such a privilege to support their learning and growth over the past year\. Congratulations to

-   Dr\. Michael Baker
-   Dr\. Linda Hu
-   Dr\. Bre Irving
-   Dr\. Lucy Ni

We also took the opportunity to celebrate and thank our outgoing Program Director Dr\. Anoo Tamber\. She had led 4 classes of residents and taken us through 3 internal/external reviews\! We couldn’t be more appreciative of her amazing work, kindness and warmth she shared with every interaction be that with residents, faculty or staff\.  Thank you Anoo, for everything you’ve done and given to the UBC Palliative Care Residency Programs\. We will miss you\!
