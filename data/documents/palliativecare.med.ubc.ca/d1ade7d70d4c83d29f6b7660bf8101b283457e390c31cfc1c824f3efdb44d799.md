-   [Pan Canadian Palliative Care Research Collaborative \(PCPCRC\):](https://pcpcrc.ca/) PCPRC aims to develop a Pan\-Canadian network of practice\-focused research groups producing high\-quality palliative care research\. Membership to PCPCRC will provide opportunities for connecting with other researchers, getting feedback on research projects at their bi\-annual hybrid meetings, and peer\-review of grant proposals\. PCPCRC also hosts funding competitions and support with Knowledge Translation activities\.
