#### **This week’s Faculty Spotlight features Dr\. Lindsey Eggen, a palliative care physician whose journey into the specialty was shaped by finding the right team and the right fit in Nanaimo\. Read on to learn more about Dr\. Eggen’s path into palliative care, what brings her joy outside of medicine, and the values that guide her work\.**

[Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Lindsay-E-238x300.jpg)

-   **What inspired you to choose palliative care/family medicine?**  
    The team here in Nanaimo\.  The doctors and all the allied health staff\.  I was pretty keen on surgery/ob during training \(usually surprises people who know me now\)\.  Then I did my palliative rotation with Robin Love and the Nanaimo team\.  I found my crew and have since shifted away from family med into palliative care\.   It’s my fit\.
-   **If you weren’t in medicine, what career might you have chosen?**  
    Right now I am surrounded by dinosaur talk, dinosaur lego and the Jurassic World kids show so I think I’d make a pretty good paleontologist\.   I did choose another career and was a dietitian before becoming a doctor\.   So, more realistically, something in the health and fitness space\.
    
-   **What’s something outside of medicine that brings you joy?**  
    Being at the beach with my kids\.  Watching them walk in the tide pools deeper than their boots\.  Hearing, seeing, touching, smelling things\.   Crunchy sand in all our snacks\.  We’ve run out of dry socks and pants\.  My car trunk is full of random crab parts and broken sea shells that we had to keep – How could I even question that?\!
    
-   **Where’s your favourite place to relax or recharge?  
    **The opposites: skiing on mountains or swimming in a lake/ocean\.  Both are the best\.
    
-   **One thing you’ve learned recently \(inside or outside medicine\):  
    **With kids in kindergarten and grade one I learn random daily things\.  Most recently: where does all water come from? \(a complex ecosystem/weather issue\) Why do turtles have shells? \(mobile home and shield\) Why can’t we bring dinosaurs back to life like in Jurassic World \(??\)  Why does mom like coffee so much \(??\)
    
-   **Favourite comfort food:**  
    Is coffee a comfort food? I think so\.  Specifically Americanos\.
    
-   **Favourite way to spend a day off:**   
    Back to the beach with my kids eating snacks\.  Otherwise running, biking, and swimming are my recharge states\.
    
-   **Best advice you’ve ever received \(medical or personal\):  
    **My dad Mike Diack, a retired RCMP, taught me growing up that the right thing to do is usually the harder thing\.  He embodies and role models to me a very high ethical standard of doing what’s right in the world and what’s best to help care for other people\.  Even when it’s the harder choice and often easier to say and do nothing\.
