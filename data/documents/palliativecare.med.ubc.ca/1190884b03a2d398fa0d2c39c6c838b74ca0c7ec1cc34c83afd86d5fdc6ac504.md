After a brief break, we’re back with our Faculty Spotlights and today we are featuring Dr\. Leanne Chew in our double header\. Read below to learn about her favourite two Asian restaurants \(child friendly and adult options\) as well as her favourite past time\.

**Site\(s\):**

Abbotsford

**What’s the best part about your job?**

The amazing &amp; compassionate team that I get to work with everyday\! Also helping people hear the truth that they need to so they can prepare and prioritize the time they have left\.

**Favourite song:**

Fifteen Words by Hey Ocean\!

**Favourite past\-time activity:**  
Spending time with my kids and getting cuddles

**Favourite restaurant in your community:**

Akedo Showten Ramen \+ Gyoza \(for practical purposes to ensure my toddler will actually eat\), and Yugafu japanese bistro \(for special occasions without the kids\)

**A book or article you would recommend:**

A Deadly Education by Naomi Novik

**What are you creating in the world right now?**

Sourdough

**Best piece of advice for new physicians:**

There are many people who are going to want your time as you get started, so pace yourself with committing to projects and work that you are genuinely invested in\. Also know that working “full time” doesn’t need to be 5 days per week – especially if you have a young family\.[Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/leanne2-360x247.jpg) [Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/leanne3-331x300.jpg) [Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/leanne1-360x270.jpg)
