1.  **What’s the best part about your residency experience:**  
    The people\! I think everyone in the program has been incredibly supportive, receptive to feedback, and help create a friendly, collegial culture\.
2.  **What are you most looking forward to in your career ahead:**   
    Probably the flexibility of making my own schedule and practicing in a diversity of settings \(I get bored if I do too much of the same thing\!\)
3.  **What’s the best part about your job?**  
    Being able to really taking the time to learn about a patient’s values, beliefs, and goals, and work with patients to develop a plan that is in line with their wishes\. I appreciate the privilege of guiding patients through their most difficult times, and I find it incredibly rewarding\.
4.  **Favourite song:**  
    Hey Soul Sister, by Train 
5.  **Favourite past\-time activity:**  
    Fishing \(as evident in the picture\)
6.  **Favourite restaurant in your community:**  
    Shiro Japanese Restaurant
7.  **A book or article you would recommend:**  
    I love high fantasy, so would recommend The Way of Kings, by Brian Sanderson
8.  **What are you creating in the world right now?**  
    I’m trying to foster a culture of wellness in residency one small step at a time\!
9.  **Best piece of advice for new physicians**:  
    Just enjoy the journey – don’t just look forward to the end of residency, make sure to have fun along the way\!
