**Congratulations to Dr\. Adam Fowler on His Appointment as Division Head of UBC Division of Palliative Care\.**

We are pleased to extend our heartfelt congratulations to **Dr\. Adam Fowler** on his appointment as the new **Division Head of the UBC Division of Palliative Care**\.

Dr\. Fowler brings a wealth of experience, compassion, and leadership to this role\. His dedication to advancing palliative care, supporting patients and families, and mentoring the next generation of healthcare professionals has had a lasting impact on the field and the community\.

We look forward to the vision and excellence Dr\. Fowler will bring to the Division as he leads in shaping the future of palliative care at UBC and beyond\.

Please join us in celebrating this well\-deserved recognition and exciting new chapter in Dr\. Fowler’s career\.

**Congratulations, Dr\. Fowler\!**
