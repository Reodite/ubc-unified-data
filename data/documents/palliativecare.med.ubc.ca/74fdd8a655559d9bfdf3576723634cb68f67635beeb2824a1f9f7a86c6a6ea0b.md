Yesterday, we held the 2023 Orientation Day at our offices at St John Hospice on the UBC Campus\. It was an opportunity to welcome our newest Palliative Residents in both the Enhanced Skills and Subspeciality programs\. Welcome to:

-   Liz Berry
-   Wenna Deng
-   Kevin Li
-   Kirstin Moritz
