We are delighted to share that *Stephanie Quon*, a second\-year medical student in Vancouver, has been awarded the **2025 J\.H\. MacDermot Writing Prize** \($750\) for her reflective essay published in the *BC Medical Journal \(BCMJ\)*\.

Stephanie participated in the Legacy Project, an experience that inspired the writing recognized by this award\. Her achievement highlights the meaningful impact of reflective storytelling within palliative care education and practice\.

The Legacy Project continues to grow, with its approach increasingly recognized as an important component of care for people living with life\-threatening illnesses\. At the same time, it offers students a powerful opportunity to deepen empathy, reflection, and connection through narrative medicine\.

You can read [here](https://bcmj.org/news/2025-jh-macdermot-writing-prize-winners) for more details\.

We warmly congratulate Stephanie on this well\-deserved recognition and celebrate the ongoing work of the Legacy Project and all who contribute to its success\.
