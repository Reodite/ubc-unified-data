**Congratulations to Our Graduating Residents\!**

The UBC Division of Palliative Care is proud to celebrate the graduation of our residents\. Throughout their training, they have demonstrated exceptional dedication to compassionate patient care, clinical excellence, scholarship, and teamwork\.

We congratulate our graduates on this important milestone and thank them for their many contributions to our program\. We wish them every success as they begin the next chapter of their careers and continue to make a meaningful impact in the lives of patients and families\.
