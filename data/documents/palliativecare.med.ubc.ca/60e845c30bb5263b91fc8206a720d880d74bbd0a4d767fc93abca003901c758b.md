We’re excited to introduce you to Dr\. Kelvin Lou, our first Royal College Resident feature, who will actually be graduating this week\! Keep reading to learn more about this inspiring gentleman\.

[Dr\. Kelvin Lou smiling at restaurant with the sun setting and boats on the water in the background\.](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/08/KL-360x263.png)

Dr\. Kelvin Lou smiling at restaurant with the sun setting and boats on the water in the background\.

**Favourite part about your residency experience so far:**

-   I have really enjoyed the diverse learning opportunities throughout my residency training\. I particularly enjoyed meeting and talking with like\-minded people who are passionate about their work

**Favourite song:**

-   AC/DC – It’s A Long Way To The Top \(If You Wanna Rock ‘N’ Roll\)\. Excellent wake up song for early mornings\.

**Favourite past\-time activity:**

-   Reading and cooking

**Favourite restaurant in your community:**

-   BeiJiang Restaurant \(北疆飯店\)
    
    [Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/08/Kelvin-Lou-360x270.jpg)
    
    Dr\. Kelvin Lou and his dog Colbi adventuring on a paddleboard\.
    

**A book or article you would recommend:**

-   The Wild Edge of Sorrow by Francis Weller\. Great overview of grief work\.

**Who is someone who inspires you?**

-   My sister, who is also a palliative care doctor, and an ongoing source of inspiration\.

**What are you creating in the world right now?**

-   A pork shoulder in the smoker at the moment\.

**What are you most looking forward to in your career ahead?**

-   Continuing to learn and grow within a passionate and supportive community \.
