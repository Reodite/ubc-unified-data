This week, we are excited about spotlighting Dr\. Michael Baker\. I really enjoyed learning a bit more about him\. Read on to learn more about this inspiring colleague\!

**Sites:**  
Fraser North, Providence Health\.

**Best Part:**  
The opportunity to teach &amp; be taught by trainees, peers, patients &amp; their loved ones\.

**Favourite Song:  
**Right now, Half A World Away by R\.E\.M\.

**Favourite Past\-Time:  
**Hiking Outdoors, Reading Indoors\.

[Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/MB2-360x239.jpg)

Dr\. Michael Baker hiking in Kauai, Hawaii\.

**Favourite Restaurants in my Neighbourhood:**  
Oidé Coffee in the morning, Grapes &amp; Soda in the evening\.

**Recommended Book:  
**Babel by R\.F\. Kuang\.

**What Am I Creating:  
**A perpetually half\-finished work of fiction\.

**Piece of Advice**:  
Value your own recharging time – the first weeks to months of practice are demanding and being able to step away to regroup kept me grounded\.
