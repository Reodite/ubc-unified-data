1.  **What’s the best part about your residency experience:**   
    Learning from all the lovely palliative care physicians and allied health in Vancouver, Victoria and Nanaimo\.  
    [Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/Emily-MacLean-Photo-2--226x300.jpeg)
2.  **What are you most looking forward to in your career ahead:  
    **To continue to learn and grow, and embrace the exciting opportunities that lay ahead\.   
      
    
3.  **What’s the best part about your job?**  
    The opportunity to connect with patients and their families and learn their stories\.   
      
    
4.  **Favourite song:** I love the whole Hamilton soundtrack\!  
      
    
5.  **Favourite past\-time activity:** Hiking in the summer and cross\-country skiing in the winter\.  
    [Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/Emily-MacLean-Photo-3-299x300.jpeg)  
    
6.  **Favourite restaurant in your community:** Nori Sushi in Nanaimo  
      
    
7.  **A book or article you would recommend:  
    **Next on my reading list is Cutting for Stone by Abraham Verghese\.  
      
    
8.  **What are you creating in the world right now?  
    **I’m just starting to plan my wedding for next year and it is definitely taking a lot of time and creativity\.  
      
    
9.  **Best piece of advice for new physicians:  
    **Try to find balance and remember what you enjoy doing outside of medicine\.
