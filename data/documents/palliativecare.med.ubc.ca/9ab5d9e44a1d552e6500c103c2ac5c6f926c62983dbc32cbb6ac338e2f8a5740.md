Today’s first of a double feature\. We are excited to spotlight Kathleen Yue, a dedicated and inspiring member of our faculty\. Her innovative teaching methods and commitment to fostering a dynamic learning environment make her an invaluable part of our institution\. Read on to learn about her favourite past times and take a peek at her adorable photos of her grandson and dog\.

[Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/04/kathleen3-225x300.jpg)

**1\. Site\(s\):**  
BC Centre for Palliative Care [https://www\.bc\-cpc\.ca/](https://www.bc-cpc.ca/)\. My role is the Director of Strategic Initiatives\. I’m a nurse, and have worked in home and community care,  acute medicine, and oncology\.

**2\. What’s the best part about your job?  
**Collaborating with people in all different sectors and disciplines across BC, Canada and internationally\. I also love encouraging the professional growth of others\.

**3\. Favourite song:**  
The whole soundtrack of the musical Come From Away

**4\. Favourite past\-time activity:**  
Crafting – follow me on Instagram @craftinghappytimes, theatre, going for coffee or bubble tea drives with my family and spending time with my new grandson

**5\. Favourite restaurant in your community:** In Victoria – Jam cafe for breakfast\. I could eat breakfast for every meal\!

**6\. Favourite travel destination:**  
New York or Florence

**7\. A book or article you would recommend:**  
360 Leadership by John C\. Maxwell

**8\. What are you creating in the world right now?**  
I’m doing a series of pictures called art is all around us – to seek out beauty in our daily lives\.

**9\. Best piece of advice for new physicians:**  
You don’t have to ever work alone, lean on your team\.
