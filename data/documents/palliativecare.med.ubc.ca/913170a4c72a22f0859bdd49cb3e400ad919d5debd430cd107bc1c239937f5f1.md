**Site:**

Surrey Memorial Hospital, BC Cancer Surrey, RCH, and New Westminster community – in that order\.

**What’s the best part about your job?**

Having the privilege to work with a wonderful team to care for patients and their families at a time when they need it most\. [Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/08/Screenshot_20230801-165450-303x300.png)

**Favourite song:** 

How much a dollar cost By Kendrick Lamar\. It’s a good reminder and a powerful message\.

**Favourite past\-time activity:** 

Putting on a good pair of shoes and exploring the world around me with a nice long run\.

**Favourite restaurant in your community:** 

Harambe on commercial\! Best Ethiopian food in the city\.

**A book or article you would recommend:**

Can’t Hurt Me by David Goggins\.

**What are you creating in the world right now?** 

Warmth and an eased burden in everyday interactions \(trying anyhow\!\)\.

[Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/08/Screenshot_20230801-163829-139x300.png)

Dr\. Mathieos Belayneh on a hike\.

**Best piece of advice for new physicians:** 

Build your career slowly and try different roles\! That way you can find where you thrive best and balance your professional life with your personal life\.
