This week, we are spotlighting Dr\. Jody Anderson From Victoria, British Columbia\! Dr\. Jody Anderson is a dedicated and passionate member of our faculty, whose work stimulates both her intellect and her heart\. Known for her enthusiasm and drive, she turns to her favorite confidence\-boosting song, “Use the Force” by Jamiroquai, to energize herself before exams\. When she’s not immersed in her professional duties, Dr\. Anderson loves indulging in food\-related activities—whether it’s cooking, baking, or savoring a meal, she finds joy in every bite\. Her favorite local restaurant is Marilena, a cherished spot in the community\.

1.  **What’s the best part about your job?  
    **I find that it fully stimulates both my head &amp; my heart\!
2.  **Favourite song:  
    **Well, my favorite “theme song” to get my confidence up is *Use the Force* by Jamiroquai\.  I use it to pump me up before exams and it’s worked well so far\!
3.  **Favourite past\-time activity:  
    **Anything food related – cooking, baking &amp; eating\!
4.  **Favourite restaurant in your community:  
    **Marilena
5.  **A book or article you would recommend:  
    **A wonderfully wise book – Standing at the Edge: Finding Freedom Where Fear and Courage Meet by Joan Halifax\.
6.  **What are you creating in the world right now?  
    **Working hard to support a growing, mutually supportive, expert palliative physician team on the Island\!  I’m just one piece to that puzzle\. Delighted to be a part of such a great team\.
7.  **Best piece of advice for new physicians:  
    **Continue to learn from your colleagues across all disciplines and incorporate their knowledge &amp; wisdom into your practice\.
8.  **Site:**  
    Victoria, BC\.
