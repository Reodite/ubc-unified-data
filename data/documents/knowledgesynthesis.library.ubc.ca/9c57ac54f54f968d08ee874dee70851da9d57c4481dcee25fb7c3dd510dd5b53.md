## Tools

UBC Library subscribes to [Covidence](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/covidence), software which helps you deduplicate your search results, manage your team’s screening process, and conduct data extraction and quality assessment\.

Citation management software such as Zotero may also be helpful\. Learn more on the UBC Library Research Commons [citation management guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/citationmanagement)\.

See the [Knowledge Synthesis Research Guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/KnowledgeSynthesis) for information about other tools\.
