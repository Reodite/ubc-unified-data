## Venue

[harbour](https://tek.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/02/harbour.jpg)

The conference is being held at the [Pinnacle Hotel Harbourfront](http://www.pinnacleharbourfronthotel.com/) in the heart of downtown Vancouver\. This downtown waterfront hotel is located in the beautiful Coal Harbour area and has breathtaking views of the city\. It is centrally located in close proximity to the major venues and attractions in Vancouver – just steps away from the famous Stanley Park Seawall, Vancouver Convention Centre, Canada Place Cruise Ship terminal and the world\-class shopping and dining of Robson Street\.

For directions and location information please visit the [hotel website](http://www.pinnacleharbourfronthotel.com/location.html)\.

## About Vancouver

[vancouver](https://tek.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/02/vancouver-1.jpg)  
Vancouver is often ranked as one of the world’s most livable cities\. It is two cities rolled into one with interests for one and all\. For an outdoor lover, this western Canadian city is a nature paradise, with miles of scenic hiking and bike trails that sweep along the Strait of Georgia, the tree\-lined waterway that connects Vancouver with the Pacific Ocean\. For urbanites, Vancouver is a sophisticated destination, with thriving immigrant enclaves, an ever\-expanding restaurant scene, quirky neighborhoods, distinctive shops and lively bars\. Part of the fun is weaving your way through Vancouver’s two sides\. Is also one of the most diverse cities in Canada with 52% of the population speaking a first language other than English\.

## Vancouver Highlights

[Stanley Park](http://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/stanley-park.aspx) – Third largest urban core park in North America

[Granville Island](http://granvilleisland.com/) – Explore the farmers’ market, artist studios, and food court on this unique, and arts\-focused island\.

[The Seawall](http://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/seawall.aspx) – 10 km along the length of Stanley Park and English Bay

[Vancouver Art Gallery](http://www.vanartgallery.bc.ca/) – Home to an impressive collection of British Columbian and international artists

[Vancouver Aquarium](http://www.vanaqua.org/) – See the iconic marine mammals of the Pacific Coast

[Historic Gastown](http://www.gastown.org/) – Vancouver’s oldest neighborhood

[Chinatown](http://www.vancouver-chinatown.com/) – East of Gastown lies Canada’s largest Chinatown

Visit [Tourism BC](http://www.hellobc.com/) and [Tourism Vancouver](http://www.tourismvancouver.com/) to find out more about what the city has to offer\!
