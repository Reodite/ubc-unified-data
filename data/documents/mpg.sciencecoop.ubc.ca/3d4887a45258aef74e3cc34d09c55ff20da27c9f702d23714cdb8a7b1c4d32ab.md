## Placement Information

**Year you started your placement**  
2012

**Placement period**  
May – August, September – December

**Supervisor’s name**  
Luca Ghiringhelli

## Project Details

**Overview**  
Implementing the nudged elastic band method in the current computational platform\.  
Test different functionals for DFT on gold clusters\.

**Project objectives**  
Implementing and testing the nudged elastic band method for finding minimum energy path\. Seeing that the path found is in deed a minimum energy path\.  
Analyze the result of energetic calculations on gold clusters using different functionals\.

**Project status**  
Completed

## My Co\-op Experience at MPI

**Why did you apply for a placement with Max Planck Institute?**  
The institute itself is well reputed world wide\. It is a great experience to be able to work here and experience the life of a researcher\. In addition, been able to go to Europe \(with covered flight\) is in itself a great experience\.

**What recommendations do you have for students who wish to participate in MPI\-UBC exchange program?**  
Learn, and enjoy your life\.

**Tell us about your travels and cultural experiences in Germany and the rest of the Europe**  
Cathedrals, fjords, castles, and medieval towns, it is truly breathtaking to be able to see these walking out of the books and coming to life in front of your own eyes\.
