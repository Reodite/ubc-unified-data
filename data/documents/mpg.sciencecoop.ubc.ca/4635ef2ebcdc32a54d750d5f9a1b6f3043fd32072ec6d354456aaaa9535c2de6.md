## Placement Information

**Year you started your placement**

**Placement period**  
May – August, September – December

**Supervisor’s name**  
Hiroshima Fukuyama

## Project Details

**Overview**  
To try and create nanoribbon networks on graphite and graphene surfaces\.

**Project objectives**  
To figure out the parameters required and processes required for nanoribbon creation

**Research outcomes**  
Successfully patterned graphene  
Selectively formed defects across both graphene and graphite surfaces  
Selectively removed regions of graphene and graphite

However undesired formation of defects also occurs across nanoribbon regions\.

**Project status**  
In progress

**Expected completion date**  
04/02/2019

## My Co\-op Experience at MPI/ UTokyo

**Why did you apply for this placement?**  
I wanted to try research in the fields of materials\. The job posting seemed like a perfect opportunity\.

**What recommendations do you have for students who wish to participate in this exchange program?**

I would recommend that if you like repetitive measurements, then this job is perfect for you\. This job is a true indication of how tedious research could get and I would not recommend spending 8 months on this project\. 4 month is sufficient to get a handle on the experimental procedures and data analysis\.

**Tell us about your travels and cultural experiences**  
It was an amazing experience\. I took a week of break from work and traveled around Hiroshima, Osaka, Nara and Kyoto\. I got to see so many temples, eat so much amazing food and just really experience what Japan was like\.
