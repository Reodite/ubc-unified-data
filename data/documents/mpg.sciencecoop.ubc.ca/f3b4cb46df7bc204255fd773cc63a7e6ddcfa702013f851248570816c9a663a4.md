## Project Details

**Overview**

Mechanical pressure is a demonstrably powerful way to controllably modify atomic bonds and thereby tune the properties of correlated and quantum materials\. However, most experimental and theoretical considerations of strain in relation to material properties, take only either the microscopic or macroscopic response into account\.The objective of this project is to develop new in situ mechanical strain capabilities for high\-resolution \(scanning\) transmission electron microscopy to provide the first insights into the nano\- and micro\-scale lattice  
response of quantum materials under scientifically relevant strains\.

**Project Objectives**

We utilize a microelectromechanical system \(MEMS\)\-based device to apply in situ uniaxial tension and heating to Vanadium dioxide \(VO₂\), enabling dynamic nanoscale investigation of the structural phase transition via scanning transmission electron microscopy \(STEM\)\.

**Research Outcomes**

These experiments demonstrate the ability to dynamically control strain and temperature simultaneously in the S/TEM, allowing us to observe the nanoscale structural response of VO2\. This work serves as proof\-of\-concept for high\-resolution structural and spectroscopic characterization of quantum materials under in situ strain conditions, setting the stage for extension across a wide range of electronic and magnetic materials of interest

## My Co\-op Experience at MPI

**Why did you apply for this placement?**

The Max Planck Institute is a very renown institution for Physics, and all fundamental sciences\. I thought it would be a great opportunity to work at the forefront of condensed matter physics research with very talented groups and gain collaborations around the world\. I had done my previous co\-op term in Germany and I knew that I wanted to go back to continue my studies and work there in the future\.

**What recommendations do you have for students who wish to participate in this exchange program?**

The MPI offers so many opportunities to get involved with different research groups, present at conferences/seminars/workshops and be involved in publications, it is definitely worth it to take advantage of all these things\. In addition, at the MPI you are surrounded with a giant PhD student community, they regularly host events and it is great to get involved to make friends for in and outside of the workplace \(this is also great to learn a lot about many different research projects to help you determine future interests\)\. It is also a great opportunity to travel around Europe on weekends and vacation\!

**Tell us about your travels and cultural experiences\.**

It is very easy to travel in Europe by train and cheap flights which allows you to visit many different countries \(I think I visited around 10\)\. I particularly enjoyed going hiking and skiing in the Alps with friends\. I also really enjoyed learning German, the MPI will pay for a course for you and I think this is really nice especially to have basic understanding \(but you also don’t need to – you can get by easily with English\)\.
