**Lab Description**

In the Living with Aphasia Lab, we investigate how adults with aphasia and their family members live with their language disorder, with the ultimate goal of finding ways to improve their quality of life\.

**Who We Are**

The Living with Aphasia Lab is directed by Dr\. Tami Howe, Assistant Professor within the UBC School of Audiology and Speech Sciences\.
