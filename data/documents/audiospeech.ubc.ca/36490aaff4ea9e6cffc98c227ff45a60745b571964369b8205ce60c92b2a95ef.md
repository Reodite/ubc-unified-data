It is with great sadness we learn of André Lafargue’s passing\. André was part of the class of 1976 at the School and went on to to pioneer an audiology and speech language pathology department in central Newfoundland\. Please view the obituary notice [here](https://yorkfh.com/tribute/details/6772/Andr-Lafargue/obituary.html?fbclid=IwAR0c3J2WZbdq6KrF3-6XZZv0j5cjzRGMqHly5AOEzFaED_Fp55rbiy2YNyI#tribute-start)\.

On behalf of everyone at the School, we would like to send out condolences to André’s family and friends\. He will be remembered fondly\.
