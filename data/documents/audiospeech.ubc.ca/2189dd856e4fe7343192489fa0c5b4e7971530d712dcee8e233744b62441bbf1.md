As a prerequisite, applicants to the MSc programs in both Audiology and Speech\-Language Pathology must observe and/or meet with professionals in **both** Audiology **and** Speech\-Language Pathology\.

Applicants must arrange to observe and/or meet with at least one Audiologist **AND** one Speech\-Language Pathologist\. Virtual or informational interviews over the phone are acceptable\. Most applicants identify local SLPs or Audiologists through an internet search or by using Speech and Hearing BC’s “Find a Professional” tool: [https://speechandhearingbc\.ca/public/find\-a\-professional/](https://speechandhearingbc.ca/public/find-a-professional/)\. 

At the end of your letter of intent \(statement\), include a list of the names of the Audiologist AND Speech\-Language Pathologists whom you have observed in clinical practice and/or met with to discuss the profession\. Please note that only a list of the clinicians’ names is required\. Observation hours are not required\.
