Congratulations to Danielle Lafleur \(School of Audiology and Speech Sciences, PhD student\) for receiving the 2024 Student Poster Outstanding Research Award \(1st Prize\) from the Canadian Academy of Audiology 2024 Conference\.

Danielle presented her project titled: What do primary care provider know, believe, and do about the hearing health of older adults? \(Authors: Danielle Lafleur, Lorienne M\. Jenstad, Brenda Poon, and Craig Stevenson\)\.

The Canadian Academy of Audiology 2024 Conference took place in Kelowna, British Columbia, from October 6\-9th\.

Congratulations, Danielle\!
