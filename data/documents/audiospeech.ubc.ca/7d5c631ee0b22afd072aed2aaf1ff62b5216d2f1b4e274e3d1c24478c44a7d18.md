The upcoming World Congress of Audiology \(WCA2016\) is a highly collaborative congress that will feature the latest innovations and research in hearing health care\. It is the biennial meeting of the International Society of Audiology and is jointly hosted by the Canadian Academy of Audiology and Speech\-Language and Audiology Canada\.

We look forward to welcoming hearing health professionals and scientists from around the world to Vancouver for this much\-anticipated congress\. The WCA2016 program includes plenary roundtable sessions, pre­conference workshops, special themed sessions and opportunities for socializing and networking\.

Registration is open online at [http://www\.wca2016\.ca/registration](http://www.wca2016.ca/registration)\. Take advantage of early bird rates by registering before May 31, 2016\!

Dates: September 18­\-21, 2016

Location: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Venue: Sheraton Vancouver Wall Centre

Website: [http://www\.wca2016\.ca/](http://www.wca2016.ca/)

For more information about this event, please contact [WCA2016info@mci­group\.com](mailto:WCA2016info@mcigroup.com)\.
