A Criminal Record Check \(CRC\) which covers Children and Vulnerable Adults must be completed by the BC Criminal Records Review Program under the school’s account\.

To submit your CRC electronically click here: [https://justice\.gov\.bc\.ca/criminalrecordcheck](https://justice.gov.bc.ca/criminalrecordcheck)

Access Code: M6USELS86K

Follow the instructions to request a new Criminal Record Check\.

If you experience any difficulties submitting online, please email the Clinical Program Assistant at [clinical\.assistant@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:clinical.assistant@audiospeech.ubc.ca)\.

Be aware there is a small fee to complete the CRC\. The fee is the responsibility of the student\.

Regardless of if the form is submitted electronically or by paper, submissions are required by **August 31 at 3:00pm** of your entry year into the program\.
