### Clinical Education Strategies: Putting Ideas into Practice

The following is a collection of resources for clinical educators regarding the education process\. These resources are meant to assist in the implementation of educational strategies when working with SASS students\.

The School welcomes feedback from clinical educators as to which of these resources are helpful\. Information regarding additional effective approaches is also welcomed\.

Please send feedback to: [clinical\.assistant@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:clinical.assistant@audiospeech.ubc.ca)
