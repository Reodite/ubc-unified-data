Applicants must have all **prerequisite courses completed by the end of the academic winter session prior to the requested program entry date\.** For example, an applicant who is applying for entrance to the 2027 Winter session \(i\.e\., beginning September 2027\) must have all prerequisite coursework successfully completed by the end of 2026 Winter Term 2 \(i\.e\., April 30th, 2027\)\. Please note that we do not count prerequisite coursework over 10 years old, as we expect students to be up\-to\-date and current with the prerequisite requirements for our MSc programs\.

### Audiology

MSc Audiology minimum prerequisites \(all 6 required**\***\):

1.  Introduction to Physics \(energy and waves\)
2.  Developmental Psychology
3.  Sensation/Perception
4.  Research Methods
5.  Introduction to Linguistics
6.  Neuroanatomy for Audiology and Speech\-Language Pathology\*

### Speech\-Language Pathology

As part of our Speech\-Language Pathology curriculum renewal, the prerequisites for admission to the MSc Speech\-Language Pathology program have been updated\. New minimum prerequisites \(all 6 required**\***\):

1.  Phonology, Morphology, Syntax **or** Semantics
2.  Language Acquisition
3.  Phonetics – *must include phonetic transcription*
4.  Developmental Psychology
5.  Neuroanatomy, Human Anatomy, Human Physiology, **or** Human Anatomy and Physiology\*
6.  Statistics

**The completion of one 3\-credit course \(or equivalent\) is sufficient for meeting each prerequisite requirement, unless otherwise noted\. Courses must have been completed within the past 10 years to count towards a prerequisite requirement\.**

**\*Note: Starting 2026W Term 2, AUDI 402 course will be delivered in a hybrid asynchronous format, with all course content online except for the final exam\. Students will be required to write the final exam in person at the UBC Point Grey campus\.**

### Course Prerequisite Guide

Refer to the [Course Prerequisite Guide](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/Course-Prerequisite-for-Web_Jun2026_working-file-4.pdf) for information about prerequisite requirements, course equivalents, and more\.

For courses not listed in the Course Prerequisite Guide, applicants are required to submit a full syllabus to [inquiry@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:inquiry@audiospeech.ubc.ca) for evaluation by the Admissions Committee\. Please provide details of the specific prerequisite that you would like the syllabus evaluated for: e\.g\., Prerequisite: Sensation/Perception, Course: PSYC367, Institution: UBC\.  

Applicants can submit a course syllabus for evaluation at any time throughout the year\. However, the deadline to submit your course syllabus for evaluation for the **current** admissions period is **December 1**\. Questions or requests for evaluation can be sent to [inquiry@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:inquiry@audiospeech.ubc.ca)\.
