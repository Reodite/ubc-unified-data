**On November 28, join UBC’s School of Audiology and Speech Sciences for a free older adult hearing health event**

[http://www\.audiospeech\.ubc\.ca/wordpress/wp\-content/uploads/2014/11/UBC\-Hearing\-Health\-Event\-Poster\.jpg](http://www.audiospeech.ubc.ca/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/UBC-Hearing-Health-Event-Poster.jpg)[UBC Hearing Health Event Poster](http://www.audiospeech.ubc.ca/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/UBC-Hearing-Health-Event-Poster-1024x768.jpg)

Are you over fifty and looking to learn more about hearing loss or tinnitus? Or perhaps you’d like to find out what kind of assistive listening device or hearing aid may be best for you?

Join UBC’s School of Audiology and Speech Sciences on Friday, November 28 for Hearing, Health and You – a free hearing information forum for adults\.

During the event, hosted by UBC audiology graduate students, you’ll learn about hearing loss, assistive listening devices, communication strategies, tinnitus and hearing aid selection and styles\. There will also be an opportunity to have your hearing screened for free\.

See below, or [click this information brochure](http://www.audiospeech.ubc.ca/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/UBC-Hearing-Health-Event-Poster.pdf) , for full event details\.

Who: The event is open to all adults \(age 50\+\) who do not wear hearing aids\.  
When: Friday, November 28, 2014  
Time: 1:00\-3:00pm  
Location: 2177 Wesbrook Mall, Vancouver \(UBC Campus\)\. Friedman Building 3rd Floor\.  
Cost: Free\. Coffee and snacks will be served\.  
RSVP: Limited seats are available, so please RSVP to [UBCAudiologyHearingHealth2014@gmail\.com](mailto:UBCAudiologyHearingHealth2014@gmail.com) or call 604\-827\-5920
