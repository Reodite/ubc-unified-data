February 1 is the deadline to apply for September 2025 entry to the MSc Audiology or Speech\-Language Pathology programs\. However, please note that the referee letter deadline is February 15, 2025\. Ensure that your application and all other documents are submitted by the deadlines\!

If you have any questions and would like to schedule an admissions advising appointment, please email office\.assistant@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca\.
