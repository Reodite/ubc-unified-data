[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/10/vsaranphotodotcom-WEB-438-639x960.jpg)The [Alumni Builder Awards](https://www.alumni.ubc.ca/alumni-builder-awards/) were established as part of *alumni UBC 100*, the alumni association’s centennial, to recognize alumni who, in their own way, have made important contributions to the success of UBC and its alumni community\.

SASS alumna and clinical faculty member Shelagh Davies, MSc’75, was presented with a 2019\-20 Alumni Builder Award for her outstanding service, dedication, and contributions to SLP education, the university, and the alumni community\.
