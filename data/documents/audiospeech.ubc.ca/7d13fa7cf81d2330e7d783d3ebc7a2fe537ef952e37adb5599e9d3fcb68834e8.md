*It is with much sadness and shock that I recently received news of Patrick Byrne’s illness and passing away\. Patrick was an M\.Sc\. student in the School from 2003\-2005\. He will be remembered for his love of language, people, and bringing his wealth of experience, creativity, compassion, and wisdom to the School’s learning environment, and I’m sure, to those he served in his many years as a Speech\-language Pathologist\.*

*On behalf of the School, I wish to extend our sincere condolences to Patrick’s family, friends, and colleagues\.*

Jeff Small, Ph\.D\., Director &amp; Associate Professor
