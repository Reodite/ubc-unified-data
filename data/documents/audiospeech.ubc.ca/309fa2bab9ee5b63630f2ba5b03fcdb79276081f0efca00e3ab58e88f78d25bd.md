Starting January 2027, AUDI 402 Neuroanatomy for Audiology and Speech\-Language Pathology will be offered in a hybrid online asynchronous format with an **in\-person final exam requirement at the UBC Point Grey campus**\.  
  
All students must be able to attend in person to complete the final exam\. We recognize that this may present challenges for students who are not local or who are unable to travel to Vancouver\. We encourage students to review our [Course Prerequisite Guide](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/prospective-students/msc-program/admissions-requirements/prerequisite-coursework-requirements/) for approved neuroanatomy equivalents offered at other Canadian universities\.  
  
If a course is not listed and you would like it considered for equivalency, please submit a full syllabus to [inquiry@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:inquiry@audiospeech.ubc.ca) for review\.  
  
We appreciate your understanding as we implement this change\.
