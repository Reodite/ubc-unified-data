[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/03/JHVG-stepping-down-July-1988-cropped-360x300.jpg)This photo was kindly shared with the School by SASS Emerita Noelle Lamb\.

It was taken back in July 1988 at a faculty party in honour of Dr\. John Gilbert stepping down as Director of the School\. Having founded the program in 1969, he had served the School for 19 years by this date\!

Here you can see John accepting a painting given to him by the faculty in great appreciation for all his years of dedication to this program from its beginning\.
