Last Saturday twenty of our Audiology and Speech students, along with six faculty members attended the Special Olympics Healthy Athlete event and ran the Healthy Hearing screening program\. They screened for hearing loss, middle ear disease, removed ear wax and cleaned hearing aids\. We are very proud of our students’ and faculty’s involvement at this event\!
