Communication is crucial to life\. Audiologists and speech\-language pathologists are committed to helping improve the lives of people living with hearing or communication disorders\.

### Child Language Development

If you would like information on child language development, [visit our Child Language and Cognition Lab page\.](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research/the-child-language-and-cognition-lab/)

### Clinical Services

SASS does not currently provide clinical services to individuals with communication disorders\. If you or someone in your family requires assessment please contact [Speech and Hearing BC](https://speechandhearingbc.ca/)\.

### Professional Organizations

Various [professional organizations](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/partners/professional-organizations/) are valuable resources if you want to learn more about the assessment and treatment of speech, language and hearing disorders\.

### Culturally Relevant Books for Speech Language Pathologists

The [Book List](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/07/Book-list.pdf) offers a selection of culturally\-relevant therapy materials for children of First Nations, Métis and Inuit descent\.
