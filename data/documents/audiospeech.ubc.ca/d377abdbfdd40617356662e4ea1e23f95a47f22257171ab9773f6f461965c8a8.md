## A SASSy pet for many: Romeo the Raccoon

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/Romeo-posing-240x300.jpg)

#### Romeo the raccoon was spotted outside SASS speech\-language pathology student Marie Shuman’s kitchen window\. Awoken by mean crows who chased him out of the tree that he was sleeping in, Romeo raced for safety on a nearby rooftop\.

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/Romeo-digging-353x300.jpg)

#### Some of his favourite activities include washing his paws …

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/Romeo-face-in-eave-279x300.jpg)

#### … eating slimy sticks out of the rain gutter, napping on the sloped roof, and creeping silently around the garden at night\.
