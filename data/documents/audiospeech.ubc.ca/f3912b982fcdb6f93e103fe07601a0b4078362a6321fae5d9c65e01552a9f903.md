Thank you for your interest in booking a hearing assessment\. We look forward to serving you\!

-   The SASS Audiology Clinic will be in operation on Saturdays only, from January 17 to March 28, 2026 \(please note: the clinic will be closed February 14 and February 21, 2026\)\.
-   The clinic is open on Saturdays from 9:00 am to 4:30 pm, during the operating period\.
-   Each hearing assessment appointment is 1\.5 hours long \(90 minutes\) and is conducted by UBC graduate Audiology students, supervised by a Clinical Audiologist \(registered with the College of Health and Care Professionals of BC\)\.
-   Upon completion of the hearing assessment, you will be provided with your test results and recommendations\. If your results indicate you are in need of a medical referral or further services, additional information will be provided to you\. The cost for the assessment is $65\.00 or at an Affordability Rate of $45\.00 \(seniors over 60 years; UBC students\)\.

### Request an Audiology Clinic Appointment

Your privacy is important to us\. Responses will be kept confidential, and we will only collect information that is necessary for the purposes of the hearing assessment\.

Thank you\. We look forward to meeting you\.

Darlene Hicks &amp; Stephanie Kore, SASS Audiology Clinic Coordinators
