We invite you to join us for our upcoming colloquium with **Tony van der Mout** and **Christiane Menard** from **Speech\-Language and Audiology Canada**\.  
  

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/02/Colloq-Date-26-February-2025-940x403.png)

  
The presentation is designed as a broad introduction to the CETP Exams\-AUD/SLP and will features an overview of the exams, how to enrol, exam preparation tips, what to expect on the day of the exam, how the exam is scored\.  The presentation will also touch on the newly restructured SAC Certification program and its links to the regulatory entry\-to\-practice exams\.  The presentation will also include regular opportunities to ask questions or clarification on the information presentation\.

  
**BIO:**  
****Tony van der Mout****  is the CETP Exams\-AUD/SLP Officer as well as the SAC Certification Program Officer\. As the CETP Exams\-AUD/SLP Officer Tony is primarily responsible for the enrolment and the administration in exam centres and through virtual proctoring\.   He directly engages with exam candidates throughout their exam journey\. Tony is also responsible for the implementation of the newly restructured SAC Certification program, that is based on passing the CETP Exams\.  

  
**Christiane Menard** is the CETP Exams Manager and of the SAC Certification Program Manager\.  She has a wide breadth of experience in the health care sector and has been responsible for the CETP Exams from its inception in April 2020\.  Currently, her main responsibility lies is the development of policies and procedures as well as working with the Audiology and Speech\-Language Pathology CETP Exam Validation Committee for the development of each exam version\. 

  
**We look forward to seeing you on February 26th at 12:10pm\! If you plan to join us virtually, please click [here to RSVP for the Zoom session](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3yprONSrDqxLmmO)\.**
