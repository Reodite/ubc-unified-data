I have worked as a clinical Speech\-Language Pathologist for more than 25 years, in both public and private settings, with children with communication problems, and children and adults who stutter\. I worked in the fluency program at The Hospital for Sick Children in Toronto, Ontario for seven years, which is where I developed my interest in stuttering and fluency disorders\. I worked in full\-time private practice for many years and returned to the public sector in a management position with Vancouver Coastal Health where I had the privilege to work with and lead an exception team of SLPs\. I am a member of the School’s team of Academic Coordinators of Clinical Education and work with clinicians in the community to arrange practica for our students\.
