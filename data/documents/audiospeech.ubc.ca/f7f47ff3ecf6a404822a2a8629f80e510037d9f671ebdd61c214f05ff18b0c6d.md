UBC School of Audiology &amp; Speech Sciences \(SASS\) continues our tradition of offering awards in Excellence in Clinical Education, and we are seeking nominations for the Elizabeth MacLeod Award for Excellence in Clinical Education \(Speech\-Language Pathology\), and the Noelle Lamb Award for Excellence in Clinical Education \(Audiology\)\.

These awards allow the School to recognize a speech\-language pathologist and an audiologist who demonstrate excellence in and commitment to the clinical education of students\.  The recipients will be one audiologist and one speech\-language pathologist with a current clinical faculty appointment in the Faculty of Medicine, who have:

1.  demonstrated excellence in clinical education in their field,
2.  created an environment that fosters learning, respect, enthusiasm, and open communication in the clinic or classroom, and
3.  shown a willingness to supervise students from SASS on a regular basis\.

SASS students, recent graduates, clinical faculty, clinical educators, and academic faculty are entitled to nominate\.  Please complete the appropriate nomination form \(Note that there is a separate form for each award\)\.

For more information and to submit a nomination, please see [https://audiospeech\.ubc\.ca/faculty\-staff/clinical\-faculty](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/clinical-faculty)

Deadline for nominations is October 31, 2016\.

If you have any further questions on this process, feel free to contact:
