**What:** A 10\-week program for people with Primary Progressive Aphasia \(PPA\) and their partners\. The program will focus on education around PPA and communication tools and strategies, delivered through a conversation approach\. The program will be delivered by Speech\-Language Pathology \(SLP\) students, under the supervision of certified SLPs\. People with PPA and their partners will have the opportunity to share their experiences with each other and students\.

**When**: Mondays from January 22 – April 8, 2024, 2:00PM – 3:30PM

Please note: \*No program on February 19 or March 18

**Where**: The program will be delivered online, via Zoom\. Zoom links will be shared upon confirmed registration\.

How to register: Please complete the referral form below\. Referrals can be made by healthcare professionals, family members/friends or people with PPA\.

[**Referral Form**](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/primary-progressive-aphasia-mentors-referral-form/)

Once you have submitted the form, you will receive a confirmation email and an invitation to an orientation session\.
