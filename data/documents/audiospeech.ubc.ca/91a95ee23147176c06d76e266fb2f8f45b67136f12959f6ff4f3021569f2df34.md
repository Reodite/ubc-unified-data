## Karma

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/07/Karma-red-bandana-223x300.jpg)

#### Karma, a 14 year\-old cockapoo, spent most of her life living in southern Ontario\. She recently moved across the country to help her mom, SASS Speech\-Language Pathology student Tess Partland \(and Arika and Lindy\!\)

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/07/Karma-at-the-beach-252x300.jpg)

#### Karma loves her stuffed whale and always rocks a red and black plaid bandana\.

#### [Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/07/Karma-with-moms-360x226.jpg)

#### She has officially taken on the west coast and plans to spend the rest of her days laying at Kits beach and exploring the seawall\.
