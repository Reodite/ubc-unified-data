The School of Audiology and Speech Sciences would like to congratulate two of our School’s 2019 grads, Jessica Niemela \(Audiology\) and Brianne Senior \(SLP\), who won the SAC National Clinical Certification Exam Award\! Brianne and Jessica were awarded the prize for attaining the highest scores on the 2019 clinical certification exams\.

For more information about the award and to hear about other deserving winners, visit the [SAC website](https://www.sac-oac.ca/2020-award-winners#Top)\.
