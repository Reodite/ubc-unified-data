We are thrilled to announce that Marcia Choi, Associate Professor of Teaching and Academic &amp; Coordinator Lead for Clinical Education at SASS, has been invited to speak at the Faculty of Medicine’s 75th anniversary event, **Beyond 75: Imagining the Future of Medicine at UBC**\. This prestigious invitation is a significant honor for both Marcia and our school, highlighting the importance of health professions in shaping the future of medicine\.

To learn more about the event or to register for this online celebration, please visit the Faculty of Medicine’s website\.

[Beyond 75: Vancouver – UBC Faculty of Medicine](https://www.med.ubc.ca/beyond75/vancouver/)
