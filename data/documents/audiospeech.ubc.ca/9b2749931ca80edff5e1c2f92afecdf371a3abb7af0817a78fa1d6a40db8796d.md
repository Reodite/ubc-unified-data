The application portal for the September 2026 intake will open on **November 1, 2025**\. If you are planning to apply and are currently registering for courses, please note:

All prerequisite courses must be listed in our approved [Course Prerequisite Guide](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/New-Course-Prerequisite-for-Web_Nov2024-1.pdf)\.

If you are taking a course not currently listed in the Guide, you must submit a complete course syllabus for evaluation\. Submit syllabi to [inquiry@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:inquiry@audiospeech.ubc.ca) by December 1, 2025\.

Late submissions may not be considered, so we encourage applicants to plan ahead\.
