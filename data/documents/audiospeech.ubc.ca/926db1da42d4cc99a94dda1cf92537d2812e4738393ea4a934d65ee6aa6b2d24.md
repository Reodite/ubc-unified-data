UBC Department of Physical Therapy are offering a Neuroanatomy Refresher course on October 27 and 28, 2018\. The interdisciplinary, continuing education course is suitable for all clinicians interested in neuroscience and rehabilitation\.

The two day course will be taught by Dr\. Claudia Krebs \(Department of Cellular and Physiological Sciences\), Dr\. Lara Boyd \(Department of Physical Therapy\) and Tara Klassen \(PhD candidate in Rehabilitation Sciences\)\.

It will be hosted at UBC Point Grey Campus and will have a satellite location at UNBC in Prince George\.

For more information about what will be covered, see the [course flyer](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/06/Neuroanatomy-Refresher-Marketing_Flyer-2018-New.pdf) or visit their [website](https://ubccpd.ca/courses-neuroanatomy-refresher-2018)\.
