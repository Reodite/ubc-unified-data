#### **1\. Apply for Graduation**

Refer to [https://www\.grad\.ubc\.ca/current\-students/graduation/applying\-graduate](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/graduation/applying-graduate) to apply on\-line for graduation\. Application period: usually mid June to mid September each year\.

#### **2\. Thesis Preparation and Submission**

Refer to Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies for thesis preparation, submission, and guidelines at: [http://www\.grad\.ubc\.ca/current\-students/final\-dissertation\-thesis\-submission](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/final-dissertation-thesis-submission)\. Further details will be provided by the School\. See also Appendix “A” in the Student Handbook for SASS MSc Thesis Research Process\.

#### **3\. Clinical File**

You are responsible for ensuring that your clinical file is complete\. You must ensure that your clinical hours sheets are filled out, signed by your supervisors, and in your clinical file\. You are expected to check your clinical file to determine that all clinical hours forms are there\. You are also responsible for completing the required actions for formal documentation of your clinical program\.

**Vancouver Students:** You can ask the Clinical Program Assistant, Room 443 or a Clinical Coordinator for access to your clinical file\.

**Victoria Students:** You can ask the Program Administrator, Bailey Fennellow, for access to your clinical file\.

#### **4\. School Check\-out**

The Office Assistant will distribute a “Settlement of Student Accounts” survey to you in your final year\. This survey is a reminder to take care of administrative/housekeeping items before you leave the School\. Please meet with the Office Assistant or the Clinical Program Assistant \(Vancouver students\) or the Program Administrator \(Victoria students\) to verify that:

-   -   All books and test materials have been returned to Room 441 \(Vancouver students\) or Rooms 127 and 115 \(Victoria students\) and other UBC libraries\.
    -   All photocopy and printing accounts have been settled\.
    -   The lock on your locker has been removed and locker has been wiped clean\.
    -   You have provided a forwarding postal and email address \(other than your alumni account\)\.

#### **5\. Degree Completion and College of Health and Care Professionals of B\.C\. Registration**

#### The process for submitting program completion letters to the CHCPBC is as follows:

For those students who have:

-   -   successfully completed all coursework in the program, including all externships \(which includes completed Clinical Files as outlined in \#3 above\),
    -   passed the comprehensive exam \(in the Audiology stream only\),
    -   grades posted for all courses, and
    -   completed the “School Check\-out” items listed in \#4 above,

A final Graduating Requirement Completion form will be signed by the Graduate Advisor once all program requirements are satisfied\. If there are exceptions due to serious personal or health circumstances, arrangements will be made on an individual basis\.

The signed form will be emailed to G\+PS, who will review the student’s account to ensure all fees are paid and grades posted, and then close out the student’s account\. The student will then download his/her completion letter on Workday \(see [instructions](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/doc/page/downloading_your_program_completion_letter_0.pdf)\)\. ***This step usually takes approximately 3\-5 business days\.*** 

**For those students who complete a thesis, essay, or project,** their supervisor must sign off on the successful completion of the research and inform the Graduate Student Programs Manager, who will then post the “P” to their transcript\. G\+PS will go over the student’s account to ensure all fees are paid and grades posted\. They will then close out the student’s account, and the student can download his/her completion letter on Workday\. 

Once the student downloads their completion letter from Workday, he/she must send it as an email attachment to the Office Assistant, who will then email it to the College of Health and Care Professionals of BC \(CHCPBC\) on the student’s behalf\. The College will **not** accept the letter if received directly from the student\. ***This step usually takes 1\-3 business days\. You are also responsible for*** [***ordering the required official UBC transcript***](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/records/ordering-transcript)  ***for your CHCPBC application\. The School does not issue official transcripts\.*** 

Students who have paid more than the minimum installments for the degree will have their tuition fees prorated to the end of the month in which G\+PS confirms that all degree requirements have been completed\. This includes submission of either their major paper or final project to the School, or their thesis to cIRcle \(see [final, post\-defense submission instructions](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/final-dissertation-thesis-submission/final-submission-instructions)\)\. 

**Please note** that the School will not provide students with any other “letters of completion”\. It is the responsibility of the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies to authorize SASS MSc degree completion status\.
