Congratulations to Dr\. Navid Shahnaz and the other recipients of the Canadian Hearing Services \(CHS\) Global Partnerships for Research &amp; Innovation grants\! The funding will support research to improve the quality of life for Deaf and hard of hearing individuals\.

Dr\. Shahnaz’s research, *Assessment of the long\-term auditory and vestibular function in hospitalized and non\-hospitalized COVID\-19 patients*, will investigate COVID\-19’s long\-term effects on hearing and balance systems\.

To learn more, please visit the CHS website: [CHS Global Partnerships Research Innovation Awards Four Research Grants](https://www.chsglobalpartnerships.com/chs-global-partnerships-research-innovation-awards-four-research-grants-institutions-leading?utm_source=Social-Media&amp;utm_medium=Paid&amp;utm_campaign=Global-Partnerships)\.
