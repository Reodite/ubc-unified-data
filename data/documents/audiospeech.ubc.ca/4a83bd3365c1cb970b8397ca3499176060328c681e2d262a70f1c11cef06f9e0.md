We accept applications for our MSc program only from individuals who have Canadian citizenship or permanent residency status\. We are unable to accept applications from individuals who require a student visa to study in Canada\.

The *minimum* criteria for the MSc Program are as follows:

-   A four\-year undergraduate degree from an accredited university
-   A minimum overall average in the B\+ range \(76% at UBC\) in third\- and fourth\-year courses **OR** academic standing with at least 12 credits of third\- or fourth\-year courses in the A grade range \(80% or higher at UBC\) in the field of study\.
-   [Prerequisite coursework](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/prospective-students/msc-program/admissions-requirements/prerequisite-coursework-requirements/) in specific content areas, completed within the past 10 years\.
-   [Observations of and/or meetings with](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/prospective-students/msc-program/admissions-requirements/observation-prerequisites/) Speech\-Language Pathologists **AND** Audiologists
-   A total of three \(3\) references who meet our [guidelines listed on \#4 of our Application Checklist](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/prospective-students/msc-program/admissions-requirements/application-checklist/)
-   Demonstrated [English language proficiency](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/prospective-students/msc-program/admissions-requirements/english-competency/) \(If English was not the primary language of instruction of your undergraduate degree, and you do not have at least four years of continuous post\-secondary education in an English\-speaking university\)

**Applicants must have all prerequisite courses completed by the end of the regular academic session prior to the desired program entry date\.** For example, to apply for the 2027\-2028 Winter Session intake \(i\.e\., September 2027\), your prerequisite coursework must be successfully completed by the end of Term 2 of the 2026\-2027 Winter Session \(i\.e\., April 2027\)\.
