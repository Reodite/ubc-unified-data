Registration for this year’s Sea to Sky Aphasia Camp is now open\! Follow [this link](http://www.seatoskyaphasiacamp.com/registration/) to access the application forms\. 2019 marks the camp’s 10 year anniversary so the team are planning something special\!

The Sea to Sky Aphasia Camp is based on a community\-university partnership that brings together people with aphasia, their family members, and students from a wide variety of health professions\. The weekend\-long camp offers an opportunity for all participants to connect with and learn from one another while enjoying organized recreational activities in the beauty of Mission, BC\.

The 2019 anniversary weekend will take place from Sept 20 – 22, 2019 at Zajac Ranch, Mission, BC\.

If you are a member of the School’s clinical faculty and are interested in being a Clinical Lead for the weekend, please contact Eavan Sinden: [esinden@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:esinden@audiospeech.ubc.ca)

To hear more about the camp, read [this article from UBC Health](https://health.ubc.ca/news-events/stories/may-28-2019-changing-lives-and-supporting-interprofessional-collaborative-0)\.
