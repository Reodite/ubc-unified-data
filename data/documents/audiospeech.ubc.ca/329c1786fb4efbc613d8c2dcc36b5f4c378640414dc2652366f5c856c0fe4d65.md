Before entering your program and your future clinical practices, it is mandatory that you have an immunization review at UBC Student Health Service \(SHS\) for Vancouver students and UVic’s Student Wellness Centre \(SWC\) for Victoria students\. This is required in order for you to take part in clinical practice\. SHS and SWC can provide all of your vaccinations and lab tests for you during your appointments with us\.

Your responsibility prior to your appointment is to complete the following:

1\. Locate your childhood and adult immunization records and bring them with you to your appointment\. This is mandatory\.

If you do not have your records at home, then contact the Public Health Unit of your childhood home, your family physician, or a travel clinic you have visited, who will have records for you\.

If you have had immunizations in British Columbia, also try [Healthgateway\.gov\.bc\.ca](http://healthgateway.gov.bc.ca/)\.

Note: If your records are in a language other than English, please have them officially translated prior to your appointment with Student Health Service\.

Paper copies are preferred to virtual ones\.

2\. a\) **If you are in Vancouver**, please call and book an appointment right away with Student Health Services for **November or December**\. You can reach Student Health Services at 604\-822\-3811 ext 2 Option 2\. You will require **two** appointments, two to three days apart\. Appointments are typically 20 minutes for the first and 40 minutes for the second\. Please review the current SASS Fall Timetable for when you are available between classes\. These appointments should not conflict with your classes\. Please review [Immunization Welcome Letter\_2026\-2027](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/Immunization-Welcome-Letter_2026-2027.pdf), [Immunization Review Instructions\_2026\-2027](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/Immunization-Review-Instructions_2026-2027.pdf), and [Immunization Review FAQ\_2026\-2027](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/Immunization-Review-FAQ_2026-2027.pdf) for further details\.

2\. b\) **If you are in Victoria**, please call and book an appointment right away with the Student Wellness Centre at 250\-721\-8563\. Book your appointment as soon as possible\. Your first two appointments must be done **before September 30** \(see [Immunization Review Guidelines PT\_SLP 2026](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/Immunization-Review-Guidelines-SLP-2026_.pdf) and [Immunization Review FAQ PT\_SLP 2026](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/Immunization-Review-FAQ-PT_SLP-2026.pdf) for further details about booking\)\. You will also need to book one appointment for November or December to get your TB test done\. The TB test must be done 6 months prior to your first day of placement\.

The following is a list of vaccines that will be reviewed and are necessary to have:

**Tetanus/diphtheria and Pertussis:**  
• Required – Childhood series of 5 doses \(or 4 if the 4th dose is administered after your 4th birthday\) or an adult series of 3 doses \(at 0\-1\-7 months apart\)  
• Required – A Tetanus booster within the last 10 years\. \(Note: A Tetanus vaccine with Pertussis is highly recommended\.\)

**Polio:**  
• Required – Childhood series of 5 doses \(or 4 if the 4th dose is after your 4th birthday\) or adult series of 3 doses \(at 0\-1\-7 months\)  
• Recommended – Adult booster dose

**Measles, Mumps and Rubella:**  
• Required\- Two documented doses of MMR\.  
• Please note that SEROLOGY IS NOT ACCEPTED by Health Authorities

**Chicken pox:**  
• Required\-Serology proving immunity \(anti\-VZV IgG\) OR two doses of VZV vaccine if negative serology\.

**Hepatitis B:**  
• Required\-Childhood or adult series of three doses AND serology which includes the following:  
1\. Hepatitis B surface antigen \(HBsAG\)  
2\. Hepatitis B core antibody \(anti\-HBc\)  
3\. Hepatitis B surface antibody \(anti\-HBs\)

**Tuberculosis screening:**  
• Required\-TB skin test is required within 6 months of the start of your clinical placement, regardless of history of BCG\. The TB skin test must not be done within 4 weeks of MMR or VZV vaccine  
• If you have had a previous positive skin test, a chest x\-ray will be required within one year of the start of your clinical placement\. We can arrange for this, but if you choose to have this done prior to your appointment at Student Health, please bring a report of this x\-ray\.  
• If you have a positive skin test we will refer you to BC\-CDC TB Control\. You will be required to forward us a copy of their recommendations\.

When you finish the program at SASS, we will return this documentation to you for your own records\.
