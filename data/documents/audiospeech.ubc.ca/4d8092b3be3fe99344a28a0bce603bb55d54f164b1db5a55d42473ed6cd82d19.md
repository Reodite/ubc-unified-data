UBC School of Audiology and Speech Sciences is pleased to announce that we will be taking part in the West Coast Virtual Fairs\.

This virtual fair is a collaboration between Simon Fraser University \(SFU\), the University of British Columbia \(UBC\), and the University of Victoria \(UVic\)\. Students and alumni of each institution will be able to connect with different employers, recruiters and graduate schools\!

**Date**: Tuesday, November 17 &amp; Wednesday, November 18, 2020

**Time**: 9:00AM – 6:00PM \(PDT\)

Reserve [now](https://www.westcoastvirtualfairs.com/students) for a 1:1 advising session with our Graduate Student Coordinator – Winnie Wong\!
