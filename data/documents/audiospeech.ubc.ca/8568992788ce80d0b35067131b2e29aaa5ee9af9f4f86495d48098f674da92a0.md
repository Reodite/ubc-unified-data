## Let Us Introduce Angel and Oatmeal

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/07/Angel-picture-225x300.jpg)[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/07/Oatmeal-picture-225x300.jpg)

#### Angel and Oatmeal are the furry babies of SASS PhD student Sylvia Chan\.

#### [Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/07/Angel-by-tree-360x270.jpg)

#### Angel was adopted 14 years ago in London, Ontario, where Sylvia attended the University of Western Ontario\. Angel is gentle, shy, and hides away when she sees new faces at home\.

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/07/Oatmeal-sassy-360x270.jpg)

#### Oatmeal was adopted in Calgary when she was only 8 weeks old\. She is very sassy \(\!\), vocal, and full of curiosity\. She loves to be the centre of attention\.

#### Do they get along? Well, not really… Angel thinks Oatmeal is too rambunctious\. Oatmeal thinks Angel is too uptight and obsessed with seniority\.
