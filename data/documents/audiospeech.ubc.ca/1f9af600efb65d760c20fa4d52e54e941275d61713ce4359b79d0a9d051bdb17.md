We will be hosting a Colloquium talk on March 17th entitled “Looking across languages at children’s speech development: Vive les différences\.” The talk will be given by B\.M\. Bernhardt \(SASS\) and J\.P\. Stemberger \(UBC Department of Linguistics\)\. It will be held in Room 355 of the Friedman Building from 12\-1pm\. We encourage everyone who is interested to attend, as it is certain to be an interesting lecture\.
