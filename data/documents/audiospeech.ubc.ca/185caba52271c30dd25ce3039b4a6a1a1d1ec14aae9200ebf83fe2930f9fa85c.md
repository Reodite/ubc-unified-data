The School’s strongest asset is its people – a committed group of faculty members, clinical educators, and administrative staff committed to a shared educational and research mission\.

With dedicated faculty members who span a wide variety of diverse specializations, SASS is internationally known for both its experienced professors and excellent junior faculty\. The School’s large group of qualified clinical faculty and educators provide students with excellent professional training in clinical settings throughout the province and across Canada\. In addition, the School’s knowledgeable and helpful support staff bring efficient administration to the School’s many programs\.
