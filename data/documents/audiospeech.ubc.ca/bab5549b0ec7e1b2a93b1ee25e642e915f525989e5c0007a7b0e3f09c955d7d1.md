The Pediatric Audiology Lab at the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences is conducting a study which seeks to improve the ability to accurately assess hearing loss in newborns and infants\. In addition, they hope to learn more about how infants differ from adults when it comes to the development of our hearing\.

If your child is 18 months old or younger, he or she may be eligible to participate in this study\! Infants with normal hearing are invited to participate in this study\.

For more information and details on who to contact, [click here\.](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/09/PAL-Infant-Poster.pdf)
