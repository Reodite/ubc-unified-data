Congratulations to SASS PhD student **Katharine Davies**, who was selected to receive a travel grant from The Canadian Institutes of Health Research – Institute of Aging \(CIHR\- IA\) and the Canadian Conference on Dementia \(CCD\) to attend the Canadian Conference on Dementia\.

Kate will be presenting her poster based on some of her PhD research entitled: “*’I used to be a storyteller*’: People with primary progressive aphasia share their perspectives on communication needs and concerns for themselves and their family members\.”

The 11th Canadian Conference on Dementia, held in Toronto on November 2\-4, 2023, brings together a group of national and international scholars to share clinically relevant, cutting\-edge developments in dementia research\. This year’s theme is ‘Better and Earlier Detection of Dementia in a Changing Landscape\.’

Great job, Kate\!
