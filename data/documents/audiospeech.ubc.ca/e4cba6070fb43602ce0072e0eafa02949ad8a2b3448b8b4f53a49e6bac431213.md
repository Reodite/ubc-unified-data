UBC School of Audiology and Speech Sciences’ Dr\. Lorienne Jenstad, along with UBC School of Population and Public Health’s Dr\. Brenda Poon, share some timely communication tips from their research at the Wavefront Centre for Communication Accessibility: [https://beyond\.ubc\.ca/communicate\-better\-while\-wearing\-a\-face\-mask/](https://beyond.ubc.ca/communicate-better-while-wearing-a-face-mask/)
