UBC School of Audiology and Speech Sciences’ **Dr\. Tami Howe** and **Dr\. Lorienne Jenstad**, along with former Speech\-Language Pathology Master’s thesis student **Kelsea Ross**, sat down with UBC Language Sciences to discuss aphasia, the impacts on participating in recreational activities, and how to make similar environments more accessible\.
