Brent McNeill, a long serving Audiologist in our BC community, has passed away\. Please view the obituary notice [**here**\.](https://www.legacy.com/ca/obituaries/timescolonist/name/brent-mcneill-obituary?n=brent-mcneill&amp;pid=201632705)

Brent was awarded the Noelle Lamb Excellence in Clinical Education Award in 2010\. He will be remembered for his kindness, mentorship, and dedication to the audiology community\.
