These resources are designed for clinicians working with graduate students in speech\-language pathology and audiology\.

#### [Externship Handbooks &amp; Forms](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/clinical-faculty/clinical-education-resources/externship-handbooks-forms/)

Includes externship handbooks and forms used for placements, as well as SASS policies relating to externships

#### [Clinical Education Strategies](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/clinical-faculty/clinical-education-resources/clinical-education-strategies/)

Includes summaries of research related to clinical education topics and suggestions for implementing new approaches in your clinical teaching
