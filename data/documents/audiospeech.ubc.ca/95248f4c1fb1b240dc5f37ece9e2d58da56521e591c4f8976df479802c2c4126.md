Congratulations to Selena Vermey, year 2 Audiology student, who has won the SAC 2015 Isabel Richard Student Paper Award \(Below Doctoral Level\)\.  This award recognizes an SAC student associate at the below\-doctoral level for the best paper written on a specific topic related to the professions\. Selena’s paper is entitled “Improving Effective Communication With Families by Learning to Embrace Silence”\.
