We are very pleased to welcome Ms\. Grace Shyng, Aud\(C\), RAUD, RHIP to our School effective June 1\. In this part\-time role, Grace joins Cheryl McGee on a new ACCE project team which will be increasing new in\-house clinical training opportunities for our Audiology and Speech\-Language Pathology students\. The School’s Faculty and staff are delighted to have Grace’s expertise, creativity and leadership on this important project\.
