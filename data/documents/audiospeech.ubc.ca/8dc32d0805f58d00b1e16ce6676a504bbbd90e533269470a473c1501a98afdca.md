**Immunizations**  
Clinical externship sites require proof of current immunizations prior to your placements\. Student Health Services at UBC and the Student Wellness Centre at UVic will review your immunizations, update any required immunizations, and provide the certified documentation\. To review more about your immunizations, click [here](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/clinical-package-for-new-students/immunizations-requirements-for-clinical-placements/)\.

**Criminal Record Check \(CRC\)**  
A Criminal Record Check must be completed before you can attend clinical externship sites\. Click [here](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/clinical-package-for-new-students/criminal-record-check-crc-instructions/) for Instructions on how to complete the CRC\. Completed CRC applications with approved ID are due **August 31 at 3:00PM**, prior to the start of your program\.

**New Student Survey**  
To help us plan your clinical externships, you are required to complete a quick survey to answer a few questions\. The answers will be kept within the school, and used solely for school and clinical purposes\. Please complete the survey [here](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_abBGDyUeUNrhFJj)\. Be sure to include any name changes as we will use the name provided to create your Student Externship ID\.  
*As we require information from the survey for the Orientation, please ensure that survey has been responded to by* **August 31 at 3:00PM\.**

**Student Externship ID**  
The school provides photo identification for all MSc students for their clinical placements\. In order to create your ID, the school requires you to upload a photo\. Photos are requested within the [*New Student Survey*](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_abBGDyUeUNrhFJj)\. IDs will be prepared for you during the first two weeks of class\. In order to have the IDs printed in time, please ensure that your photo and preferred name has been provided by **August 31 at 3:00PM\.** 

\*\*If you are attending from the **Victoria campus**, we will require your photo **by June 30** in order to facilitate a site access badge\. Please either submit your photo using the [*New Student Survey*](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_abBGDyUeUNrhFJj) or email a head and shoulders photo with blank background to [clinical\.assistant@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:clinical.assistant@audiospeech.ubc.ca)\.
