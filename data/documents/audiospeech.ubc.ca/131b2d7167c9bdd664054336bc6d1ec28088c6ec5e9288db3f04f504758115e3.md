## **Welcome to the home page of our  
****UBC School of Audiology and Speech Sciences student\-led, in\-house clinics\!**

### **SASS Audiology Clinic:** 

Our student\-led Audiology clinic will be running on Saturdays from January – March 2026\. Please visit the [SASS Audiology Clinic](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/sass-clinics/sass-audiology-clinic/) page for more details\. 

### **SASS Speech\-Language Pathology \(SLP\) Clinic:**

The student\-led SASS SLP Clinic, in partnership with the UBC Physical Therapy and Research Clinic, was a pilot project that ran from September 27th to December 9th, 2021\. The SLP clinic provided speech\-language assessment and treatment services to adults with neurogenic communication disorders\. **The pilot project is now completed and is no longer accepting new patients at this time**\. **However, if you wish to stay informed of any future clinics, please provide your contact information via the** [referral form](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/sass-clinic/sass-clinic-referral-form/)\.

For more information on the SLP Clinic, please visit [*SASS SLP Clinic*](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/sass-slp-clinic/) or email Cheryl McGee, SASS SLP Clinic Coordinator, [cheryl\.mcgee@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:cheryl.mcgee@audiospeech.ubc.ca)\.
