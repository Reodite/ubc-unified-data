We are please to announce that Dr\. Paola Colozzo and Dr\. Lorienne Jenstad have been extended as Interim co\-Directors from January 1, 2023 until June 30, 2023\.  Dr\. Jenstad’s appointment follows the leadership of [Dr\. Valter Ciocca as Interim co\-Director](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/school-of-audiology-speech-sciences-leadership/) of the School from September to December 2022\.

To read the official announcement from Dermot Kelleher, Dean, Faculty of Medicine and Vice\-President, Health, please click the link below:
