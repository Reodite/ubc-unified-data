### Available Methods of Payment

-   Visa / Mastercard
-   Cheque
-   Bank transfer
-   Wire transfer \(must be in CDN or USD\)

### Payment and Cancellation Policy

For China Links Core Program / Vancouver only registrants:

-   A $500 non\-refundable deposit is required at the time of registration
-   Full payment is required by March 1, 2012
-   In case of cancellation between March 1 and March 15, 2012, registrants will be refunded 50% of the program fee \(funds can be used as credit towards other China Links programs, less the $500 deposit\)
-   No refunds will be available after March 15, 2012 \(funds can be used as credit towards other China Links programs, less the $500 deposit\)

For China Links Shanghai only registrants:

-   A $500 non\-refundable deposit is required at the time of registration
-   Full payment is required by April 1, 2012
-   In case of cancellation between April 1 and April 15, 2012, registrants will be refunded 50% of the program fee \(funds can be used as credit towards other China Links programs, less the $500 deposit\)
-   No refunds will be available after April 15, 2012 \(funds can be used as credit towards other China Links programs, less the $500 deposit\)

If you cannot attend, substitutions are welcome for all programs\. 

### Terms and Conditions

In the event of a last minute scheduling problem, we reserve the right to substitute a fully qualified speaker who is not listed in the course description\.

Although very rare, a seminar might be cancelled due to insufficient enrollment or extremely poor weather\. If a seminar is cancelled for any reason, the China Links’ liability is limited to a refund of the program fee or a credit note, whichever you prefer\.
