China Links covers important topical areas directly relevant for lawyers who have a professional interest in China, whether those who provide advisory services related to China’s business environment or for those who engage with Chinese clients on an ongoing basis\.  For any law firm looking to build on their existing knowledge base surrounding China, having key lawyers take part in China Links programs provide a crucial step towards this goal\.

Legal and regulatory areas covered in China Links programs:

-   Intellectual property
-   Contracts and property
-   Business permit process
-   Due diligence
-   Ethics and integrity

China Links is a registered continuing professional development course \(CPD\) in the province of British Columbia and a registered continuing legal education \(CLE\) course in Washington state\.
