#### ***Shanghai integrated Instructional workshop***

Second module of the [China Links Core Program](https://chinalinks.sites.olt.ubc.ca/cl-core-program/ "China Links Core Program")

**  
Dates: May 7\-11, 2012  
Location: Shanghai, PRC  
Fees: While it is strongly encouraged for participants to combine the Vancouver module with the Shanghai module as part of the China Links Core Program, participants were able to take this module on a stand\-alone basis for $5,895 CDN \(approximately 36,450 RMB\)\.  
**

**This five\-day workshop was held in collaboration with our local partners – the [Shanghai Academy of Social Sciences \(SASS\)](http://english.sass.org.cn/) and the [China Executive Leadership Academy Pudong \(CELAP\)](http://218.78.215.163:8080/)\.  Participants received advanced instruction from both North American and Chinese instructors on important issues facing foreign businesses and government departments as they conducted their work in China today\.  Complementing the intensive four days of seminars, participants also took part in a full day of site visits to strategic locations around the Shanghai area, including a North American\-owned wholly\-owned foreign enterprise and a Chinese government agency\. Site visits allowed participants to gain further perspective on how various issues covered during instruction were confronted on the ground by practitioners and business leaders\.**

**Curriculum:**

-   Dispute resolution, arbitration, and mediation
-   Contracts and property
-   Cross\-cultural communication and human resources management
-   Negotiation strategies and practices
-   Environmental management and PRC policy
-   Private\-public sector engagement
-   Due diligence
-   Site visits to various important commercial and governmental locations around Shanghai

**Includes:**

-   Course materials and readings
-   6 nights accommodation is provided in a private room at the luxurious [Jin Jiang Hotel, Shanghai](http://jj.jinjianghotels.com/portal/jinjiang_en/jjindex.aspx)
-   Includes all breakfasts and lunches, as well as two dinners
-   Transportation to and from venues in Shanghai

**\*\*Fees did not include airfare to and from Shanghai; reduced fees were available for Shanghai residents who did not require accommodation\*\***

**\*\*The China Links Shanghai session was a registered event with Air Canada – reduced prices were available for China Links participants for return airfare tickets to Shanghai \(conditions apply\)\*\***
