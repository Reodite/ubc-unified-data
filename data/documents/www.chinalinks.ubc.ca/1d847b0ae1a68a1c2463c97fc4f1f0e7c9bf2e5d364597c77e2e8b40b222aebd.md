China Links delivers important instruction for a range of professional in the non\-profit sector, including policy analysts, program managers, researchers, economists, and lawyers\.  Participants leave China Links programs with a 360 degree understanding of the Chinese operating environment, and the various ways in which information should be sought and gathered in China\.  Partipants also develop sound cross\-cultural communication skills, important in any form of engagement with China\.

#### Tuition Waiver for Non\-Profits

Participants from non\-profits automatically receive a tuition waiver of 20 percent off regular China Links course fees\.
