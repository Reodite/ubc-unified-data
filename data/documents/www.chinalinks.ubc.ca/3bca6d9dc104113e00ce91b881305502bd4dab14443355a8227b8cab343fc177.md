Location: University of British Columbia campus  
Fees: $4,500

This three\-day workshop is held on the University of British Columbia campus\.  Participants receive a comprehensive overview of the current Chinese operating environment and the various risks and opportunities that are prevalent\.  Participants strengthen their decision\-making capabilities through experiential exercises that allow them to understand problem\-solving approaches by working through situations that are commonly encountered in China\.

Curriculum:

-   Contracts &amp; property
-   Intellectual property
-   Integrity and social responsibility
-   Operating environment overview
