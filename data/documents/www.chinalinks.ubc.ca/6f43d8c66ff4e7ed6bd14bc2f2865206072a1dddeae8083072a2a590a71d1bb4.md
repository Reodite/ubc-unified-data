Dates: April 12\-14, 2012 in Vancouver \(UBC Point Grey Campus\), May 7\-11, 2012 in Shanghai \(Shanghai Academy of Social Sciences and China Executive Leadership Academy Pudong\)  
Fees: $7,495 CDN plus 12 percent HST

**The [first module in Vancouver](https://chinalinks.sites.olt.ubc.ca/chinalinks-in-vancouver/ "CHINA LINKS IN VANCOUVER") is held on the UBC Point Grey campus\.  [For the second module in Shanghai](https://chinalinks.sites.olt.ubc.ca/chinalinks-in-shanghai/ "CHINA LINKS IN SHANGHAI"), instruction is held in conjunction with the Shanghai Academy of Social Sciences \(SASS\) and the China Executive Leadership Academy, Pudong \(CELAP\)\.  The Vancouver segment entails a welcome dinner and keynote talk and two full days of intensive instruction by our highly experienced team, as well as role\-playing and simulation exercises\.  The Shanghai segment is a week long and continues the emphasis on expert instruction and experiential learning through role\-playing exercises and simulations\.  The day of site visits takes participants to important commercial and governmental locations around Shanghai\.**

**Includes:**

-   6 full days of instruction
-   All course materials and readings
-   Most meals
-   Transportation to and from site visits
-   6 nights accommodation in Shanghai

\*\* Accommodation can be provided on the University of British Columbia campus during the Vancouver module for a nominal sum\*\*

\*\*Fees do not include air transport to and from Vancouver or to and from Shanghai\*\*

\*\*The China Links Shanghai session is a registered event with Air Canada – reduced prices are available for China Links participants for return airfare tickets to Shanghai \(conditions apply\)\*\*
