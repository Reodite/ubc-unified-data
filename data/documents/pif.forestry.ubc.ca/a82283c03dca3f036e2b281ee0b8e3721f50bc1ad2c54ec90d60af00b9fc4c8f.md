Hello, thanks for stopping in at my bio\!

I go by the names kwiwks, Eliza, and Elizabeth Peters

I am from Lil’wat Nation here in BC and I am so pleased to be connected to the UBC Vancouver Department of Forestry

Serving my community through this work is such an honor and adventure; from farms to centers to books and courses, the creativity and vision is real: empower the community to thrive in health, environmental stewardship and inclusive access to traditional knowledge to return to being a thriving People

With immense gratitude I get to work on all this with my best friends: Tonya Smith, Koskas Dan

They’ve been walking with me long enough to watch my family grow, I have 3 beautiful children and find many life purposes with them here in this world, I am so honored for this responsibility

For the children, with the children

For each other, with each other

At the speed of trust

Forward we must

That’s me for now, thank you for taking the time to hear me
