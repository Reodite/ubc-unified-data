## Welcome to our classroom

The Alex Fraser Research Forest is part of the [Faculty of Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship](https://forestry.ubc.ca/) at the University of British Columbia\.

Situated on close to 10,000 hectares of crown forest in the Cariboo region of south\-central British Columbia, it is one of two UBC Research Forests managed to create opportunities for research, education and demonstration of sustainable forest management\.

The Alex Fraser Research Forest has 3 sites:

-   Main office located on 55 acres just south of the Williams Lake Airport
-   Gavin Lake Research Forest
-   Knife Creek Research Forest
