These maps show the approximate location of graduates as of 2016 based on the survey and internet research\.

For Canada and the United States, the following logic was used: if survey respondents indicated their province or state, that was used\. If they didn’t indicate their location but they did indicate location of their employer, that location was used\. Otherwise, we conducted further research\.

The depth of colour indicates the relative number of graduates in that area\. Dark red indicates the greatest number of graduates, pale yellow the fewest, and grey, zero\.
