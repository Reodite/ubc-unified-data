Prof\. Orlando Rojas presented a webinar about recent advances in the utilization of nanomaterials derived from biomas, and fundamental nanotechnologies for the future of our bioeconomy\.

The webinar’s format carried an international flavour; the slides are in English and its delivery in Spanish\. Prof\. Orlando presented his webinar on 5 May 2020\.

You are invited to view [this webinar](https://youtu.be/1Z5WxILLXPA)\.
