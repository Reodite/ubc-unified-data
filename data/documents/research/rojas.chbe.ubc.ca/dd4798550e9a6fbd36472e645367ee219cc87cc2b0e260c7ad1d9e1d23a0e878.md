**Dr\. Sarah Gergel** was elected as a [Fellow of the American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://www.aaas.org/news/aaas-honors-accomplished-scientists-2018-elected-fellows) – Geology &amp; Geography section\.

The AAAS bestows this lifetime honor in recognition of extraordinary achievements in advancing science\. This year’s Fellows were selected for diverse accomplishments, including pioneering research, leadership within their field, teaching and mentoring, fostering collaborations and advancing public understanding of science\. Dr\. Gergel will be presented the special rosette pin at the 2019 Annual AAAS Meeting in February 2019\.

The full list of honours is available on the [AAAS website](https://www.aaas.org/news/aaas-honors-accomplished-scientists-2018-elected-fellows)\.

*Source: Forestry Faculty e\-news, December 6, 2018\.*
