**Drs\. Shawn Mansfield** \(cross field category\) and **Jörg Bohlmann** \(plant and animal science\) were both named [2018 Highly Cited Researchers on the Web of Science](https://hcr.clarivate.com/?utm_campaign=EM_Highly_Cited_Researchers_Product_SAR_Global_2018_Email_1&amp;utm_medium=email&amp;utm_source=Eloqua)\. Scholars are selected for their exceptional research performance, demonstrated by production of multiple highly cited papers that rank in the top 1% by citations for field and year in Web of Science\.

*Source: Forestry Faculty e\-news, December 6, 2018\.*
