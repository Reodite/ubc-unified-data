### **BioAlliance Stakeholder Update – May 24th    [https://ubc\.ca1\.qualtrics\.com/jfe/form/SV\_06CAJbtDghFdhNr](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_06CAJbtDghFdhNr)[Image](https://chbe-rojas.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/03/registernow.png)**

**When:**

-   Friday May 24\[th\] 8:30 am – 1:00pm\.

**Where**:

-   Discussions/Presentations at 8\.30 am – Kaiser Building – Room 2020 \(2332 Main Mall\) – [click here for directions](http://maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?show=y,n,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=&amp;locat1=313)
-   Lunch starts at 12:00 pm – Pulp and Paper Centre \(2385 East Mall\) – [click here for directions](http://maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?show=y,n,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=&amp;locat1=747)

**Objective:** 

-   Showcase the BC Pulp &amp; Paper Bio\-Products Alliance as a true collaboration between Industry, Government, Research and Academia \(world class innovators FPI and UBC\) accelerating bio\-product development tailored for BC
-   Further engage Provincial and Federal gov’t in the building of the BC’s &amp; Canada’s bio\-based economy
-   Update stakeholders on the status and next steps of our four development themes
-   Acknowledge the support from our key funders

**Who should attend:**

-   Senior Industry executives
-   Senior Gov’t &amp; Ministerial staff
-   Alliance Members
-   UBC and FPI Management
-   Research leads for each development theme
-   Provincial and Federal funders
-   Sector participants/partners

**Agenda**

-   8:30 – Coffee &amp; meet other attendees
-   9:00 – 9:10 – Gail Murphy, UBC Vice President of Research and Innovation, Welcome
-   9:10 – James Olson, Dean of Applied Science, BioProducts Institute update and direction
-   9:20 – Neil Huff, Vice President, Business Development, FPI update and direction
-   9:30 – Meggin Messenger, Executive Director, BC Ministry of Forests, Lands, Natural Resource Operations and Rural Development, BC sector update and direction
-   9:40 – Bob Lindstrom, BioAlliance Program lead,  update and direction – 4 Theme leads \(FPI and UBC\) 5 min each, objective and approach
-   10:20 – Carlo Del Monte, BioAlliance Steering Committee Chair,  Wrap up and direction
-   10:30 – coffee break
-   11:00 – Tour of UBC Bio\-Product Facilities
-   12:00 – Networking Lunch

Please see links below to each of the event locations

-   **Main meeting –** 8:30am to 12:00pm – Kaiser Building – Room 2020 – second floor \(2332 Main Mall\) – [click here for directions](http://maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?show=y,n,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=&amp;locat1=313)
-   **Lunch and Tour –** 12:00pm to 1:00pm – Pulp and Paper Centre \(2385 East Mall\) – [click here for directions](http://maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?show=y,n,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=&amp;locat1=747)
