Bio

Open Close

Inspired by an early encounter with a pair of barn swallows nesting in his home, my research seeks to understand how wildlife can persist and thrive within rapidly changing cities, aiming to identify both ecological thresholds and policy barriers that limit wildlife integration in urban environments\. My work examines how urban landscapes reshape food web dynamics that support threatened bat and bird species, with particular attention to how hyper localized microclimates and municipal conservation policies influence prey availability and habitat quality\. I hold undergraduate degrees in Animal Cognition from Arizona State University and Ecosystem Science and Sustainability from Colorado State University\. I am the recipient of the 2025 Peter Wall Research Award and the 2024 Werner and Hildegard Hesse Research Award in Ornithology\.

My favourite pursuits outside of academic life tend to revolve around food\. I am especially drawn to good seafood, French bakeries, ice cream, barbecue, and fried chicken, and I enjoy seeking out places where food becomes a social and cultural experience\.
