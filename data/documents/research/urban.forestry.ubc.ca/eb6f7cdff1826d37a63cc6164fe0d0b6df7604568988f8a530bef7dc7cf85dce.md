Bio

Open Close

Arantxa is a PhD student and a biologist by training, holding a B\.Sc\. from the National Autonomous University of Mexico \(UNAM\) and an M\.Sc\. in Natural Resources and Rural Development from El Colegio de la Frontera Sur \(ECOSUR\) in Mexico\. Her research examines how environmental stewardship groups in tourist cities create networks to protect access to blue and green spaces\. She is particularly interested in the effects of touristification and local advocacy, exploring the critical connections between people, place, and the collective care of the environment\.

Away from the field, her two biggest passions are cheering on her favorite football team, Pumas, and collaborating with her nephew on complex Lego engineering projects
