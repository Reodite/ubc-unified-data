Bio

Open Close

Evan recently graduated from the Bachelor of Urban Forestry in December of 2025\. Over that last summer of his undergraduate he was awarded an NSERC Undergraduate Student Research Award \(USRA\), with Dr\. Melissa R\. McHale, to advance the use of satellite derived land surface temperature data for urban heat research\. He has been working in the Urban Ecology and Sustainability lab since September of 2024\. Prior to his research experience, Evan spent eight month’s on co\-op with the City of Surrey’s the Natural Areas field crew\.

In the summer Evan can be found mountain biking, and spends every weekend possible adventuring\. In the winter months, he fills his time skiing at Whistler and has been getting into ski touring over the last few years\.
