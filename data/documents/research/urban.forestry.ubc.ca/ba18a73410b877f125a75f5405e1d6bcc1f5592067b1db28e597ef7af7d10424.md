Bio

Open Close

Finn completed their undergraduate in the University of British Columbia’s Urban Forestry program\. During this time Finn was involved in the UBC Tree Inventory project and worked with SEEDs to collect and analyze tree data\. Their interest was focused on assessment of biodiversity and ecosystem services which led to their current research on heat and runoff mitigation of urban trees\. Additionally, Finn has led talks on UBC urban forests for UBC biodiversity days, botanical garden, and for the climate emergency week\.

Outside of work I enjoy physical activities such as skateboarding and biking\. I also like expressing myself creatively though photography, drawing, and cooking\.
