Bio

Open Close

I am a cultural anthropologist trained at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, with experience working alongside Indigenous and rural communities in Wallmapu, southern Chile, as they navigate environmental transformations driven by climate change and shifts in land use\. My work is grounded in participatory action research, aiming to bridge social and natural sciences with Indigenous and local knowledge, finding novel approaches to land defense and stewardship\. Through pursuing an MSc in Forestry, I hope to acquire tools to better support these community\-led processes\.

Apart from academics, I love to grow vegetables in my garden and weave in my spare time\.
