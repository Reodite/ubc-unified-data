Bio

Open Close

Ryan Senechal \(Victoria, BC\) has been practicing in arboriculture and urban forestry work environments across sectors since 2004\. He has contributed to the development and maintenance of arborist/urban forestry regulations, apprenticeship curriculum, safe work procedures, and industry credentials across Canada and internationally\. He is a graduate of the Humber College Urban Arboriculture program and has a Master’s of Urban Forestry Leadership from UBC\. His work in the non\-profit sector is community\-focused blending urban forest advocacy, community involvement, and nursery operations\.

Being a dad, riding BMX and mountain bikes, playing guitars, and propagating trees\.
