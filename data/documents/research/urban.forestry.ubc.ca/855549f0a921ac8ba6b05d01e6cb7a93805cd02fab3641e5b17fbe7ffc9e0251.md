Bio

Open Close

My research focuses on using publicly\-accessible data to answer ecological questions about urban environments\. I began my PhD degree in Geospatial and Environmental Analysis at Virginia Tech, and followed my advisor \(Dr\. Day\) to UBC\. Prior, I completed a Master of Science degree in Biology from the University of Alabama, where I studied urban forest biometrics under the supervision of Dr\. Christina Staudhammer\.

I love teaching, and have taught several classes \(and won a teaching award at Virginia Tech\!\)\. Additionally, I hold positions in graduate student associations whenever possible as a means to build community\.

In my free time, I enjoy spending time with my partner and our two dogs\. I’ve also been learning to mountain bike, yikes\!
