Bio

Open Close

Chloe has a background in wildlife biology and holds a BA in Human Ecology from College of the Atlantic\. Her hands\-on experience comes from her work as a wildlife field technician at Acadia National Park in Bar Harbor, ME\. Through this work, Chloe has studied mammals of all sizes, birds, fish, and amphibians\. Her work has specialized in threatened and endangered bat populations since 2023, and she has presented her work at several conferences\. Currently, she is investigating how agricultural pest control practices and landscape structure impact bats in the Lower Fraser Valley\.

Outside of work Chloe enjoys hiking, whitewater canoeing, ocean swimming, dancing, and painting\. She also enjoys creating educational social media content about wildlife, ecology, and sustainable living\.
