I completed my Bachelor’s degree in Geography and Resource Management at The Chinese University of Hong Kong\. I am interested in the interaction between plant and soil as well as the ecosystem services that it could provide\. My thesis research explores the hydrological properties of manufactured soils under urban forests/plant communities\.  
  
Outside of work, I like running, hiking and gardening\.
