Bio

Open Close

I grew up on a small island in the San Juan Archipielago of the Pacific Northwest\. Much of my youth was spent exploring and playing the coastal forests of my island home\. I went on to study environment science at the University of Washington\. After University I went to to become a certified arborist\. Connections in the arborist world led to my participation in tree canopy research and trainings with Latin American biologists in Colombia, South America\. I am currently part of the Transfor\-M program\. Last year I studied in Sweden, this year is in Vancouver\.

My hobbies include oil painting, hiking, surfing, and woodcarving\.
