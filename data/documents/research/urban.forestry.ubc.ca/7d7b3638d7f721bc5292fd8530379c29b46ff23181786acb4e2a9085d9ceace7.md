Bio

Open Close

Avery is a PhD student specializing in bird population dynamics, with a focus on the effects of urban development and human activities in Vancouver, British Columbia\. He earned both his BSc and MSc in Environmental Science from the University of Washington\. For his master’s thesis, Avery investigated the impacts of recreational activities on tundra\-breeding birds in Denali National Park, Alaska\. Avery has previously worked as an avian field technician in Yosemite National Park and Mount Baker\-Snoqualmie National Forest, contributing to various avian research projects\.

Outside of research, Avery owns a small Bird Friendly certified coffee roasting business named Big Year Coffee\. He loves to birdwatch, climb, and spend time in nature with his friends and family\.
