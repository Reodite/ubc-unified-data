Bio

Open Close

Caroline is an assistant researcher in the RISE lab at the Faculty of Forestry\. She is an experienced architect and urban planner, holding an M\.S\. in Urban and Regional Planning from the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil\. Her research focuses on balancing the lifestyles of traditional fishery communities with the preservation of National Parks\.

Caroline has collaborated with municipalities and state governments to develop Master Plans, Social Housing policies, Regional Plans, and censuses of precarious settlements\. She has also designed social housing neighborhoods\. Over 17 years, she has held various teaching positions at renowned universities in Brazil, teaching subjects such as architectural drawing, urban renovation, sustainable planning, and landscape design\. She has supervised numerous students in their final coursework, guiding them in architecture, landscape, and urban planning and design projects\.

Currently, Caroline is enrolled in the Urban Studies program at UBC to gain Western and Canadian perspectives\.

Outside of work Caroline enjoys spending time with her family, exploring the landscapes of Vancouver and its surroundings, and practicing hiking, yoga, drawing and cooking\.
