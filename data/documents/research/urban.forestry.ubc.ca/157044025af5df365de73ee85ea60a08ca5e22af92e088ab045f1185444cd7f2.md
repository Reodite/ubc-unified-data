Bio

Open Close

Roberta is a Master of Applied Science \(M\.A\.Sc\.\) student in Forestry, co\-supervised by Dr\. Tahia Devisscher and Dr\. Matthew Mitchell\. She holds a Bachelor degree in Urban Forestry from UBC, with a minor in Landscape Recreation and Planning\. Her academic background and research focuses on fostering coexistence between urban environments, nature, and wildlife, aiming to create sustainable spaces for both people and ecosystems\.  
  
Outside of work Roberta likes to paint and crochet\.
