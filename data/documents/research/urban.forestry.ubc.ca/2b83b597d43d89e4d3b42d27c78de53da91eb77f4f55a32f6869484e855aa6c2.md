Bio

Open Close

Asim Khanal is an urban planner and researcher interested in understanding how diverse user groups experience urban blue\-green spaces\. He intends to push the theoretical interpretation and practical implementation of ‘access’ by integrating the dimensions of vision and touch\. In his doctoral dissertation, he also uses mixed methods including walking interviews and third\-party big data to estimate spatiotemporal use dynamics of urban blue\-green spaces or public spaces along shorelines\.  
  
Asim completed his Bachelor of Civil Engineering from Punjab Engineering College, Chandigarh, India and an M\.Sc\. in Sustainable Critical Infrastructure from the Masdar Institute in Abu Dhabi, which was opened in collaboration with the Massachusetts Institute of Technology \(MIT\)\. He has worked as an Urban Planner, a freelance consultant, and a volunteer for federal, provincial, and local governments in multiple countries, including Nepal, India, and the United Arab Emirates\.  
  
In his leisure time, he likes camping, swimming in freshwater lakes, playing guitar and releasing his experimental music compositions on Spotify\.
