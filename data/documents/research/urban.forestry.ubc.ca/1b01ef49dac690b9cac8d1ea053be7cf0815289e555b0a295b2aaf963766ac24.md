Bio

Open Close

Tijmen graduated with a BSc in Forest and Nature Conservation and an MSc in Climate Sciences at the University of Wageningen \(WUR\)\. After working on climate adaptation strategies for Dutch wildlife preserves, he is now continuing his academic career at the faculty of forestry and UBC\. His PhD analyses the interplay between urban heat, urban water use and landscape characteristics, specifically for semi\-arid cities\.

Outside of academia he enjoys drawing, playing the guitar and karaoke\. He has 3 cats back in the Netherlands\.
