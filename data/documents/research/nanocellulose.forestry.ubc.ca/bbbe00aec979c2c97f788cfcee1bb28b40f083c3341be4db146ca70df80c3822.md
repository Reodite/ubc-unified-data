Congratulations to [Feng Jiang](https://profiles.forestry.ubc.ca/person/feng-jiang/) appointed as a [Canada Research Chair](https://research.ubc.ca/federal-government-announces-investment-canada-research-chairs-program) in Sustainable Functional Biomaterials\.

Feng’s research in advanced nanotechnology has been applied to isolate high\-performance nanocellulose from forest biomass, and to regulate their assembly into more complex structures for expanded applications\.
