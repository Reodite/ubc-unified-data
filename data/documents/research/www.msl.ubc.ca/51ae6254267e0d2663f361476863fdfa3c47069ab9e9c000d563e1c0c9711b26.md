UBC’s [Proteomics Core Facility](https://pcf.msl.ubc.ca/) was launched more than a decade ago as a part of the Michael Smith Laboratories and conducts more than 150 analytical projects a year using mass spectrometers\. [Dr\. Leonard Foster](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-leonard-foster/) is the Director of the Proteomics Core Facility which conducts protein analysis on a broad range of subjects varying from bees to spinal cord samples\. In a [story written by UBC’s Faculty of Science](https://medium.com/ubcscience/protein-potential-1d9783c6b565), Dr\. Foster talks about the importance of proteomics\.
