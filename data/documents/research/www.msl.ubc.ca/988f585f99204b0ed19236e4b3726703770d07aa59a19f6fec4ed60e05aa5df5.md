**Phil Hieter** \(Univ of British Columbia, Canada\) will become Vice\-President of the Genetics Society of America in 2011, succeeding to President in 2012\.  **Judith Berman** \(Univ of Minnesota, Minneapolis\), **Bonnie Bartel** \(Rice Univ, Houston\), and **Jeannie T\. Lee** \(Harvard Med Sch and Mass Gen Hosp\) will serve as Directors from 2011\-2013\.  “It has been encouraging to see the high level of membership participation in this election\.  I look forward to working with the new Board members and the general membership on GSA activities,” said GSA Executive Director Sherry Marts\.
