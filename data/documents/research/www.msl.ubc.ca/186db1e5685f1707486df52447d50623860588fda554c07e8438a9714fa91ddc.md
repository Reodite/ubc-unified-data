Winner of the Gene Screen BC 2011 People’s Choice Award for the film **“The Greatest Drug in the World”** \. This film defines and presents a realistic view of personalized medicine, with a specific focus on pharmacogenomics\. It begins with an infomercial that exaggerates the current state of medicine and then transitions to the reality of personalized medicine today and in the near future\. The video can be viewed at:
