Last week, UBC and the [Advanced Molecular Biology Laboratory](https://www.bioteach.ubc.ca/) hosted its annual Girls\-Only [Maker Camp](https://www.bioteach.ubc.ca/portfolio/maker-camp/), a week\-long camp focused on STEAM \(science, technology engineering, art, and math\) education and designed for girls entering grades seven and eight\.  Hosted by [Dave Ng](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-dave-ng/), the schedule included basic programming workshops, craftivism and Ugly Doll creation \(by Kim Werker, read about her post [here](https://www.kimwerker.com/2019/07/05/mighty-ugly-rocks-at-girls-only-maker-camp/)\), breadboarding and circuit building, metal workshops, and many other various science activities\. Read about this year’s camp went on the UBC Maker Camp 2019 blog\.
