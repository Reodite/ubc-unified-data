Joerg Bohlmann was named by the American Society of Plant Biologists \(ASPB\) as one of the authors from around the world publishing the most influential science in the field of plant science\. The ASPB analyzed citations to their journals \(*Plant Physiology* and *The Plant Cell*\) for papers published between 2004 and 2008 to identify these authors\.

[A complete list of these authors can be found here\.](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/aspbtopauthors_JB_0.pdf)
