Creative, supportive, resourced, and empowered\. Home to more than 300 researchers, the Michael Smith Laboratories combines biology and technology to discover innovative solutions to society’s most important problems\.

We work in diverse clusters of interdisciplinary researchers from the faculties of Science, Medicine, Applied Science, Forestry, and Land and Food Systems to address society’s most pressing health and environmental needs\.

Local, national, and international awards including a Nobel Prize and nine Royal Society of Canada Fellows\.

Interdisciplinary researchers from the faculties of Science, Medicine, Applied Science, Forestry, and Land and Food Systems\.

Including top\-tiered journals such as Science, The Plant Journal, Cell, Genetics, Nature Biotech, Blood, PNAS, and more\.

The Michael Smith Laboratories uses donations to fund the next generation of Canadian life sciences leaders\. Contributions support graduate students and postdoctoral fellows in their research and helps fund the educational outreach programs through the Michael Smith Laboratories’ Advanced Molecular Biology Laboratory\.

These gifts inspire and enable the brightest minds to excel, support our impact on Canadian health and welfare, and allows the Michael Smith Laboratories to connect to BC communities through science\.
