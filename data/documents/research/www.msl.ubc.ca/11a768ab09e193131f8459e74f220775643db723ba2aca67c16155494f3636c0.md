AbCellera, a spin\-out from UBC and a biotech company focusing on the discovery of therapeutic antibodies, is ranked \#63 out of 100 for the fastest\-growing companies in Canada, according to the [Globe and Mail’s list of Canada’s Top Growing Companies](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/rob-magazine/article-canadas-top-growing-companies/)\. The Report on Business ranks Canadian companies based on three\-year revenue growth\.
