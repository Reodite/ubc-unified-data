[Biofilm MEDIA](https://www.biofilmmedia.com/), with support from [Nikon Canada Inc\.](https://en.nikon.ca/index.page), recently met with the Michael Smith Laboratories’ [Leslie Lab](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-sabrina-leslie/) to feature their work in single molecule imaging\.

“We’re also pushing the limit with optics to look at untethered single macromolecules\. And with partners like Nikon, we are excited to be rapidly advancing the field of genomic medicines to reshape healthcare, and benefit everyone,” – Dr\. Sabrina Leslie \(Michael Smith Laboratories, [Department of Physics &amp; Astronomy](https://phas.ubc.ca/)\)\.
