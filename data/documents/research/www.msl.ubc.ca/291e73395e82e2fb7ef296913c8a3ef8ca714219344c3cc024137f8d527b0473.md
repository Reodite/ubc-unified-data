On November 16, 2018, the [Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute](https://www.vchri.ca/stories/articles/2018/11/16/recently-uncovered-cell-could-stop-cancer-its-tracks) highlighted [Dr\. Wilfred Jefferies](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-wilfred-jefferies/)‘ research on a cancer marker cell in tumours which could potentially play a major role in preventing the spread and growth of cancer\. The details of this work was published in [*Scientific Reports*, a *Nature Research* journal in February 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-20608-6)\. Read the full news story on this research on [Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute’s website](https://www.vchri.ca/stories/articles/2018/11/16/recently-uncovered-cell-could-stop-cancer-its-tracks)\.
