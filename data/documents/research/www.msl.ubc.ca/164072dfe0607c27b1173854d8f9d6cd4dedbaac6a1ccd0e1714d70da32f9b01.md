Michael Smith Laboratories’ researcher, [Leonard Foster](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-leonard-foster/), is trying to help bees fight off deadly mites\. “The dream is a gene pool so strong we won’t have to worry about disease,” he says\. Foster’s research is highlighted in an article in *Science Magazine* called, “Breeders toughen up bees to resist deadly mites”\.
