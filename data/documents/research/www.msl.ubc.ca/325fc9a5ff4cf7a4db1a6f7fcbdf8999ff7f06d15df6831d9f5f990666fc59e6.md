An article published on September 18, 2019 by Steve Burgess in The Tyee called “To Teach Kids that Know More of Pokemon than Phytoplankton, David Ng Made a Game of It” features [Dr\. Dave Ng](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-dave-ng/) from the [Advanced Molecular Biology Laboratory](https://www.bioteach.ubc.ca/)\. The articles describe the [Phylo Project](https://phylogame.org/), a natural\-world Pokemon\-type card game\.
