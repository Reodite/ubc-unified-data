The [BC Proteomics Network \(BCPN\)](http://www.bcpn.ca/) was formed in 2007  to unify BC professionals who share a common interest in proteomics research\. This network inspires collaboration, training, and resource sharing between BC professionals who work in proteomics\.

Michael Smith Laboratories faculty members, Drs\. [Thibault Mayor](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-thibault-mayor/) and [Leonard Foster](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-leonard-foster/), are a part of this growing network and have [shared some thoughts on how the BCPN has benefited them in the Fall 2018 issue of Spark](https://www.msfhr.org/spark/7?story=2), a quarterly publication from the [Michael Smith Foundation for Health Research \(MSFHR\)](https://www.msfhr.org/)\. Read about how the BCPN has given them an advantage in their work and how it has collectively saved over $400,000 a year in operating costs\.
