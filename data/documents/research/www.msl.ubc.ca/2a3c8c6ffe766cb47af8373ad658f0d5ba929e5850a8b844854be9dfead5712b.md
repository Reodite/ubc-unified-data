On the evening of Tuesday October 16\[th\] 2018, AbCellera opened its doors to the local biotech, research, and business community to celebrate the official opening of its new space\. AbCellera moved from its incubation space at UBC to a 21,000 square foot building with new offices and custom\-built laboratories\. [Carl Hansen](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-carl-hansen/), CEO of AbCellera, thanked UBC and colleagues in the Vancouver biotech community for playing a crucial role in helping the company get started\.

To read the full article on AbCellera’s website, please [click here\.](https://www.abcellera.com/news/2018-10-18-abcellera-celebrates-opening-new-facility)
