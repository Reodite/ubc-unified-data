[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/MSmith_bracelet.jpg)The annual Michael Smith Distinguished Lecture is organized in partnership with the [Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology](https://biochem.ubc.ca/) and the Michael Smith Laboratories\. This event honours Nobel Laureate [Dr\. Michael Smith](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/about-us/#dr-michael-smith), whose academic career was deeply rooted at UBC and where his legacy continues to inspire young generations of scientists\. 

Smith’s groundbreaking work in chemical synthesis of DNA that led to the development of site\-directed mutagenesis — a technique that allows us to make specific mutations in specific genes and test the consequences of these genetic changes in living cells — has revolutionized the study of biology\. Not only was he known for what he accomplished in the laboratory, but also for his generosity and compassion\. When Smith won the Nobel Prize in 1993, he donated all of his Nobel prize money to support education and research in Canada, and he convinced the provincial and federal governments to provide matching funds creating a total endowment of more than $2 million\.  His good deeds inspired others to donate further\.

Organized by the Michael Smith Laboratories and the [Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology](https://biochem.ubc.ca/), the first Michael Smith Distinguished Lecture was held in 2005\. Since then, many prestigious researchers have been invited to UBC to share their research with eager students, faculty, and staff\. Many of the speakers have gone on to win Nobel Prizes of their own\. Below is a list of invited speakers for the Michael Smith Distinguished Lecture\.

**Dr\. Sarah Teichmann** is the chair in Stem Cell Medicine at the University of Cambridge, within the Department of Medicine and the Cambridge Stem Cell Institute\.

**Talk title:** Mapping the Human Body One Cell at a Time

**Dr\. Sue Biggins** is the Director and a Full Professor in the Division of Basic Sciences at the Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center, as well as an Affiliate Full Professor in the Department of Biochemistry at the University of Washington\.

**Talk title:** How do cells inherit the right chromosomes?

**Dr\. Karen Miga** is an Assistant Professor of Biomolecular Engineering at the University of California Santa Cruz \(UCSC\), the Associate Director of UCSC Genomics Institute, Co\-Founder of the Telomere\-to\-Telomere \(T2T\) consortium and Project Director of the Human Pangenome Reference Consortium \(HPRC\) Production Centre at UCSC\.

**Talk Title:** Expanding studies of global genomic diversity with complete, telomere\-to\-telomere \(T2T\) assemblies

**Professor Dame Carol Robinson** is the Director of the Kavli Institute for Nanoscience Discovery and professor at the Department of Chemistry at the University of Oxford\.

**Talk Title:** A new phase for structural biology

**Dr\. Sangeeta Bhatia** is a John J\. and Dorothy Wilson Professor of Engineering and the Director of the Laboratory for Multiscale Regenerative Technologies at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology \(MIT\)\.

**Talk Title:** Tiny Technologies and Medicine

**Dr\. Michael Rosen** is a Professor in the Department of Biophysics at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Centre and the Howard Hughes Medical Institute\.

**Talk Title:** Regulation of Cellular Biochemistry by Liquid\-Liquid Phase Separation

**Dr\. Feng Zhang** is a Professor in the Departments of Brain and Cognitive Sciences and Biological Engineering at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology \(MIT\)\. 

**Talk Title:** Advances in Genome Editing Technologies and Beyond

As this year was the 25th anniversary of Dr\. Michael Smith winning his Nobel Prize, a [symposium](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/ms-25th-anniversary-celebration-of-michael-smiths-nobel-prize-symposium-in-review/) was held to commemorate\.

**Dr\. Brian Kobilka** is a Professor in the Department of Molecular and Cellular Physiology at Stanford University School of Medicine\.

**Talk Title:** Structural insights into the dynamic process of G protein coupled receptor activation

**Dr\. David Julius** is a Chair in the Department of Physiology and a Professor at the University of California, San Francisco School of Medicine\.

**Talk Title:** Natural Products as Probes of the Pain Pathway: From Physiology to Atomic Structure

**Dr\. Robert T Sauer** is a Salvador E\. Lauria Professor of Biology at MIT\.

**Talk Title:** Machines of Protein Destruction

**Dr\. Tom Cech** is a Distinguished Professor at the University of Colorado Boulder and Director of the BioFrontiers Institute\.

**Talk Title:** From the RNA World to the RNP World: Ribozymes, Telomerase, and IncRNAs

**Dr\. Tak Mak** is a Professor in the Department of Medical Biophysics at the University of Toronto\.

**Talk Title:** Future Anti\-Cancer Targets: Put the Cart Before the Horses?

**Dr\. Marco Marra** is the Director of Canada’s Michael Smith Genome Sciences Centre \(GSC\) at the BC Cancer Agency and a Professor and Head of the Department of Medical Genetics at the University of British Columbia\.

**Dr\. Peter Wright** is a Professor in the Department of Integrative Structural and Computational Biology at the Scripps Research Institute\.

**Dr\. Martin Chalfie** is a University Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at Columbia University in New York\.

**Dr\. David Botstein** is a Professor in the Department of Molecular Biology at Princeton University\.

**Sir John Sulston** is the Founding Director of the Wellcome Sanger Institute\.

**Dr\. Peter Agre** is a Bloomberg Distinguished Professor at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health and Johns Hopkins School of Medicine, and Director of the Johns Hopkins Malaria Research Institute\.

**Dr\. Tom Steitz** is the Sterling Professor of Molecular Biophysics and Biochemistry, Professor of Chemistry, and Investigator of the Howard Hughes Medical Institute\.
