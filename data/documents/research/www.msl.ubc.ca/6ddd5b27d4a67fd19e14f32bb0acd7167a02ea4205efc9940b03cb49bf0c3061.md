How does the microbiome affect older and elderly individuals? [In the November 2018 issue of ZOOMER Magazine](https://www.pressreader.com/canada/zoomer-magazine/20181126/281797105027301), [Dr\. Brett Finlay](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-brett-finlay/) discusses why the microbiome is critical to healthy aging\. In Finlay’s new book, *The Whole\-Body Microbiome*, he connects the microbiome to a number of health issues including gut inflammation, asthma, obesity, depression, and cardiovascular disease\.
