Dr\. B\. Brett Finlay received the Queen Elizabeth II Diamond Jubilee Medal on April 11, 2012\.  The Medal is awarded to Canadian citizens or permanent residents who have made significant contributions to Canada or for an outstanding achievement abroad that has brought great credit to Canada\.
