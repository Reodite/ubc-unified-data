Six MSL faculty members are involved in projects recently funded through the Canadian Institutes of Health Research \(CIHR\) Project Grant: Spring 2026 competition\. This includes a project led by Dr\. Thibault Mayor studying cell quality\-control systems and disease\-causing protein mutations, and one led by Dr\. Xin Tang using AI models to find therapeutic targets for synovial sarcoma\.

This funding competition awarded approximately $412 million across Canada in research grants, and an additional $1\.9 million in priority announcement grants\. At UBC, 51 research projects earned a total of $45\.8 million in Project and Priority Announcement Grants\.

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Spring-2026-CIHR-thumbnail.png)

Left to right, top row: Drs\. Thibault Mayor, Jörg Gsponer, Marco Marra\. Left to right, bottom row: Drs\. Xin Tang, Martin Hirst, Leonard Foster

### **Project Grants:**

*[Molecular Principles Governing Degron Recognition in Misfolded Cytosolic Proteins with Pathological Missense Mutations](https://webapps.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/decisions/p/project_details.html?applId=563394&amp;lang=en)*  
Principal Investigator\(s\): **Dr\. Thibault Mayor** \(MSL, Biochemistry &amp; Molecular Biology\)  
Co\-Investigator\(s\): **Dr\. Jörg Gsponer** \(MSL, Biochemistry &amp; Molecular Biology\)  
$1,143,676 \(5 years\)

*[Deciphering Immune Evasion in Synovial Sarcoma through Interpretable AI and Validation Experiments](https://webapps.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/decisions/p/project_details.html?applId=566470&amp;lang=en)*  
Principal Investigator\(s\): **Dr\. Xin Tang** \(MSL, Computer Science\), Dr\. Michael Underhill \(Cellular &amp; Physiological Sciences; School of Biomedical Engineering\)  
Co\-Investigator\(s\): **Dr\. Martin Hirst** \(MSL, Microbiology &amp; Immunology\), Dr\. Kelly McNagny \(School of Biomedical Engineering, Medical Genetics\), Dr\. Torsten Nielsen \(Pathology &amp; Laboratory Medicine\)  
$948,600 \(5 years\)

*[A single\-cell spatial analysis of the impact of air pollution exposure on molecular pathways in lung cancer](https://webapps.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/decisions/p/project_details.html?applId=565187&amp;lang=en)*  
Principal Investigator\(s\): Dr\. Emilia Lim \(Biochemistry &amp; Molecular Biology\)  
Co\-Investigator\(s\): **Dr\. Marco Marra** \(MSL, Medical Genetics\), Dr\. Anna McGuire \(Surgery\), Dr\. Julia Naso \(Pathology &amp; Laboratory Medicine\)  
$910,352 \(4 years\)

*[Alamar discovery of plasma biomarkers that distinguish dementia subtypes, inform on disease staging and predict rate of cognitive decline in Alzheimer’s Disease and Related Dementias](https://webapps.cihr-irsc.gc.ca/decisions/p/project_details.html?applId=564794&amp;lang=en)*  
Principal Investigator\(s\): Dr\. Cheryl Wellington \(Pathology &amp; Laboratory Medicine\)  
Co\-Investigator\(s\): Calon, Frédéric; Chavula, Thandiwe W; Chertkow, Howard M; Cooper, Jennifer; Dixon, Roger A; Domeier, Phillip P; **Foster, Leonard J** \(MSL, Biochemistry &amp; Molecular Biology\); Futhey, Naomi; Hirsch Reinshagen, Veronica; Horwitz, Marc S; Hsiung, Ging\-Yuek R; Mackenzie, Ian R; Smith, Rachael J; Stukas, Sophie; **Zhong, Huan** \(MSL Postdoctoral Fellow\)  
$1,699,389 \(4 years\)
