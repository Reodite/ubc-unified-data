[Dr\. Marco Marra](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-marco-marra/) \(Michael Smith Laboratories, Department of Medical Genetics\), through his role as the Terry Fox Leader in Cancer Genome Science, played a key role in announcing the ‘Finish It’ initiative\.

The initiative, a new joint brand platform between the Terry Fox Foundation and Terry Fox Research Institute, was launched with the premiere of a short film to represent how members of the Marathon of Hope Cancer Centres Network are joining forces across the country to finish Terry Fox’s marathon against cancer\.
