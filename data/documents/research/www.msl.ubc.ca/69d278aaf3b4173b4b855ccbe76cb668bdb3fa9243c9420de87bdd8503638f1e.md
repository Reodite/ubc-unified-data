In the January edition of the [Blue and Goldcast](http://www.blueandgoldcast.com/) produce by [Cited Media Productions](https://www.citedmedia.ca/), UBC President Santa Ono and Dr\. Jennifer Gardy meet two professors who are mixing science and art with their [ASIC 200](http://www.blueandgoldcast.com/?ep05#) class\. [Dave Ng](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-dave-ng/), a biologist and Director of the Advanced Molecular Biology Laboratory at the Michael Smith Laboratories, and Allen Sens, a Professor of Teaching in UBC’s political science department, explore a unique interdisciplinary teaching approach, one of the first of its kind in Canada\.
