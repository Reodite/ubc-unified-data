Each year the American Association for the Advancement of Science elects members whose “efforts on behalf of science or its applications are scientifically or socially distinguished”\.  This year Dr\. Joerg Bohlmann is being honoured for distinguished contributions to the field of plant biology, particularly for genomics and biochemistry of plant defence against insects\.
