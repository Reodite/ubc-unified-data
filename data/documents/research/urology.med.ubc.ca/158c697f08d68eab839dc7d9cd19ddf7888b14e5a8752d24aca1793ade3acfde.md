As the flagship research institute of UBC’s Department of Urologic Sciences, the M\. H\. Mohseni Institute of Urologic Sciences brings together multidisciplinary teams to advance research, innovation, and patient\-centred care across the spectrum of urologic health

[Explore the M\. H\. Mohseni Institute of Urologic Sciences](https://mius.ca/)

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/06/mhm-940x529.jpg)

## The Centres

The M\. H\. Mohseni Institute of Urologic Sciences hosts six centres, each at the forefront of pioneering research and advanced treatments in urology\. These centres tackle complex challenges through innovative research, clinical trials, and the development of next\-generation therapeutic solutions, significantly impacting patient outcomes and advancing urological science\.

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/Vancouver-Prostate-Centre-Urologic-Oncology.webp)

### Vancouver Prostate Centre \+ Centre for Urologic Oncology

The Vancouver Prostate Centre is Canada’s largest prostate cancer research program, globally recognized for its leading talent, advanced facilities, and translational research\. Since 1998, the centre has pioneered targeted therapies, patents, and biotech growth\.

[Learn More](https://mius.ca/the-centres/the-reproductive-sexual-medicine-centre/)

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/Centre-for-Functional-Urology-and-Reconstructive-Surgery.webp)

### Centre for Functional Urology \+ Reconstructive Surgery

The Centre for Functional Urology and Reconstructive Surgery excels in female pelvic medicine, reconstruction, gender affirmation surgery, and urodynamics\. Partnering with urogynecology, plastic surgery, orthopeadics, and rehab medicine, it advances diagnostics and treatments for urinary disorders\.

[Learn More](https://mius.ca/the-centres/centre-for-functional-urology-and-reconstructive-surgery/)

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/Centre-Kidney-Transplantation-Immunology-e1788542905596.webp)

### Centre for Kidney Transplantation \+ Immunology

The Centre for Kidney Transplantation and Immunology advances kidney transplantation through research, clinical innovation, and equity\. Focusing on immune function, AI, and novel therapies like ex\-vivo graft modification, it improves outcomes for patients across British Columbia\.

[Learn More](https://mius.ca/the-centres/centre-for-kidney-transplantation-and-immunology/)

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/Reproductive-Sexual-Medicine-Centre.webp)

### The Reproductive \+ Sexual Medicine Centre

The Reproductive and Sexual Medicine Centre advances male reproductive and sexual health through clinical care, education, research, and technological innovation\. It focuses on infertility, regenerative medicine, AI diagnostics, and new therapies for sexual dysfunction, aiming to transform patient care with cutting\-edge research\.

[Learn More](https://mius.ca/the-centres/the-reproductive-sexual-medicine-centre/)

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/Centre-Kidney-Stones-Minimally-Invasive-Surgery.webp)

### Centre for Kidney Stones \+ Minimally Invasive Surgery

The Centre for Kidney Stones and Minimally Invasive Surgery pioneers research and treatments for kidney stones and BPH\. Uniting scientists and clinicians, it develops advanced stents, stone\-breaking technology, and minimally invasive methods, transforming urology worldwide\.

[Learn More](https://mius.ca/the-centres/centre-for-kidney-stones-minimally-invasive-surgery/)

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/Centre-Adolescent-Transitional-Urology-e1788539599820.webp)

### The Centre for Adolescent \+ Transitional Urology

The Centre for Adolescent and Transitional Urology sets the standard for care as patients transition from pediatric to adult healthcare\. It offers a specialized clinic with expert providers and focuses on clinical research to advance the understanding and management of patients during this key transition\.

[Learn More](https://mius.ca/the-centres/centre-for-adolescent-and-transitional-urology/)

## The Core Facilities

The M\. H\. Mohseni Institute of Urologic Sciences is committed to pushing the boundaries of urological science through innovative research, compassionate patient care, and collaboration\. Its state\-of\-the\-art research platforms are built around five complementary cores, each contributing to a holistic approach to urological research and treatment\. Together, the five cores drive innovation, improve patient outcomes, and set new benchmarks in urological science\.
