## [M\. H\. Mohseni Institute of Urologic Sciences ▶](https://urology.med.ubc.ca/research/mh-mohseni-institute-of-urologic-sciences/)

The M\. H\. Mohseni Institute of Urologic Sciences serves as the flagship research institute of UBC’s Department of Urologic Sciences\. Bringing together leading scientists, clinicians, and trainees across six specialized research centres, the Institute advances discovery, innovation, and knowledge translation to improve the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of urologic disease in British Columbia and beyond\.
