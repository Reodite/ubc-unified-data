## Call For Editors 2026/27

UBC’s Journal of Political Studies \(JPS\) is seeking passionate editors for our 2026/27 Editorial Board\.

As an editor, you’ll develop skills in critical reading and analysis, providing constructive feedback, and collaborative decision\-making through a highly team\-oriented process\. Editors will receive training in copy\-editing and research methods, then engage in several lively rounds of debate and discussion to select articles for the journal’s publication in April 2027\. Most of the work occurs in Term 2, and Editors can expect a commitment ranging from 3\-5 hours per week during the more intense editorial rounds\.

Joining the board is a unique opportunity to gain hands\-on experience in editing, writing, and publishing with one of Canada’s leading undergraduate journals\.

## What We Look For:

Successful applicants should demonstrate an avid interest in political studies, regardless of their chosen field of study, and an excellent command of language\. All editors will receive training, so formal editing experience is not a requirement\. Due to the rigorous nature of the papers and the prerequisite knowledge required, we are seeking students in their third years and above—please send us an email if you feel that you would be a valuable member of the team and do not meet this requirement\.

If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the Editors\-in\-Chief, Lillian and Ege, at editor\.ubcpssa@gmail\.com\. We would love to have you join our team\!
