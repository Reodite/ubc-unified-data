### UBC Undergraduate Journal of Psychology \(UBCUJP\)

The UBCUJP is an annual, student\-run, and peer\-reviewed journal\. Our goal is to provide a platform for undergraduate students to showcase their research, allowing them an invaluable opportunity to have a published academic paper early in their careers\.
