## Submit now to UJP

Call for Submissions for Volume 7 of the Undergraduate Journal of Psychology is now **OPEN**\. Visit our [OJS page](https://ojs.library.ubc.ca/index.php/ubcujp/about/submissions) for the submission process\!

### Who can submit?

First author must be a UBC undergraduate student when the research was done\.

### What kind of research can I submit?

Unpublished, original research and analytical theses

Research done at UBC, independently, for a class, or has a direct connection with UBC

Related to psychological research, completed since 2019

Relates to one of the following sections:

-   Clinical
-   Health
-   Social/Cultural/Personality
-   COGS/Neuroscience
-   Developmental Psychology

### Do you have a step\-by\-step guide?

Yes, check out our submission guidelines [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1H44A3dVF82K1oEjJ_NwggA7CPb6H5bBD60KwL31H-0c/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.gduycgifg7ls) for a detailed breakdown of the process\.
