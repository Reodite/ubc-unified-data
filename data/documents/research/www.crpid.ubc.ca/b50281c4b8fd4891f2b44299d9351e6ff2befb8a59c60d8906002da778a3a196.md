A few events to look out for in the next few weeks:

  

Screening of “I Go Gaga,

  

> “I Go Gaga, My Dear” in Richmond, BC on Sunday, September 22nd\. This very personal documentary allows the viewer to follow the lives of an elderly Japanese couple through the lens of their documentarian daughter\. “My mother has dementia, my father is hard of hearing, and I live away from home\.” “A documentary of an elderly couple, filmed by their daughter, over 1200 days\.”

  

Tickets: [**https://www\.eventbrite\.ca/e/i\-go\-gaga\-my\-dear\-tickets\-69956817725?aff\=ebapi**](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/i-go-gaga-my-dear-tickets-69956817725?aff=ebapi)

## "Mistaken Orders by Orange Cafe" in Vancouver, BC on Saturday,October 5th\. This event features people living with dementia as servers in a cafe\.
