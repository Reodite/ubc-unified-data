# National Dementia Strategy for Canada

[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://www.crpid.ubc.ca/post/national-dementia-strategy-for-canada)

Paulina Malcolm

Jun 26, 2019

1 min read

EXCITING NEWS\! Canada joined the host of other countries when they finally announced a National Dementia Framework last week\. The strategy has three main objectives: to prevent dementia; to advance therapies and find a cure; and to improve the quality of life of people living with dementia and caregivers\. Here is a link to the full document: [**https://www\.canada\.ca/en/public\-health/services/publications/diseases\-conditions/dementia\-strategy\.html\#s6\.2**](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/publications/diseases-conditions/dementia-strategy.html#s6.2)

  

Jim Mann, a well\-known BC dementia advocate and active researcher with the CRPD, was a member of the Ministerial Advisory Board on Dementia and is pictured below at the national unveiling of the document in Ottawa\!
