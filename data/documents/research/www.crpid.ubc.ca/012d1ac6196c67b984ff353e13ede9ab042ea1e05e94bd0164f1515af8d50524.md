# Opportunity to work with the Imagination Network – Raising the Curtain

[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://www.crpid.ubc.ca/post/tips-on-optimizing-your-landing-page)

Paulina Malcolm

Mar 15, 2021

1 min read

Updated: Jun 28, 2021

**Are you interested in building an inclusive community with people with dementia? Would you like to learn about collaborative creative engagement?** Raising the Curtain is a community\-based participatory research partnership between health care, the arts and education\. People living with dementia play an important role in the project\. We use creative engagement workshops to learn about dementia, create art, and engage in dementia advocacy\. Currently, we are recruiting **collaborators with dementia** and **facilitators** who are interested in learning more about this work\. To learn more about these roles please visit [https://imaginationnetwork\.org/spring\-cohort/](https://imaginationnetwork.org/spring-cohort/) **This free creative engagement course** will give you an opportunity to:  

-   experience safe community with an opportunity for self\-reflection;
    
-   connect with people with dementia and allies working alongside them;
    
-   engage in conversations and art that challenge dominant understandings of dementia\.
    

**Session Dates:** Wednesday, April 21st Wednesday, April 28th Wednesday, May 5th Wednesday, May 12th Wednesday, May 19th Wednesday, May 26th Wednesday, June 2nd Wednesday, June 9th

  

**Session Times:** Each session is 2 hours long **1pm – 3pm** All sessions will be **via Zoom** **We can provide support with helping you get connected** If you are interested in joining us or learning more about this opportunity, please contact **Vicky by March 31st at** [**contact@imaginationnetwork\.org**](mailto:contact@imaginationnetwork.org)
