# Portable Poetry Session\-a\-Day

[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://www.crpid.ubc.ca/post/portable-poetry-session-a-day)

Paulina Malcolm

Mar 24, 2020

1 min read

**For a daily dose of creativity, head over to this “portable poetry session\-a\-day” page:** [**http://www\.alzpoetrymn\.org/**](http://www.alzpoetrymn.org/)

The Alzheimer’s Poetry Project Minnesota, an offshoot of the Alzheimer’s Poetry Project **\(**[**http://www\.alzpoetry\.com/**](http://www.alzpoetry.com/)**\),** is providing easy access to a poem, song, and visual art piece during these days of quarantine\.

Enjoy a creative break, wherever you may be\.
