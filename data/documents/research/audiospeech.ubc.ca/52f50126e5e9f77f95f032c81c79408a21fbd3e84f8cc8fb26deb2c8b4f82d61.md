Hearing aids and amplification, auditory aging

### Lab Description

The main area of research in this lab is hearing aid amplification:

1.  the effects of amplification signal processing on speech acoustics and intelligibility;
2.  determining candidacy for types of signal processing, particularly for older adults\.
