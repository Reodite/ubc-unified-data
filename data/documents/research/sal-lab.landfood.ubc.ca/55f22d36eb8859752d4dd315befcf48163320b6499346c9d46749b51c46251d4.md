**Education**

-   BSc, Applied Biology, Majoring in Plant and Soil Science, Honors, University of British Columbia \(2019\)

**Research Interests**

-   Sustainable soil management
-   Nutrient cycling and management 
-   Climate change mitigation and adaptation for agriculture
-   Land use change
-   Integrated pest management

I am interested in making conventional agriculture more environmentally friendly as well as adaptive to the changing climate through improved nutrient management and sustainable soil management techniques\. I believe there is an intersection between organic and conventional production that can sustainably manage the needs of future generations while protecting and improving the agroecosystems of today\.
