 I am currently an undergraduate MITACS research intern in the Sustainable Agriculture Lab, visiting for 3 months from the University of Exeter, United Kingdom\. Throughout the summer of 2023, I will be working on the ‘[Quantification and Mitigation of GHG Emissions from High\-Value Agricultural Production Systems in British Columbia’](https://www.landfood.ubc.ca/research/lfs-research-centres-groups/soil-water-and-sustainability-group/aggp-lfv/) project, under the supervision of Sean Smukler and Jordan Kersey\. My activities thus far have included KCl extractions, soil and crop sampling at UBC farm, aggregate stability, N analysis in R studio, as well as the co\-development of carbon fractionation protocol for the lab, with future analysis to follow\.
