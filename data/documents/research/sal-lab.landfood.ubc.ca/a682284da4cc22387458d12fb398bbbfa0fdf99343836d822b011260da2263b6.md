Email:lyndsey\.dowell@gmail\.com[Image](https://lfs-sal.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/06/31731413501_9cb96347a4_o.jpg)

[My Linkedin Profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lyndsey-dowell-b1a58382/)

**Education**

-   BA Environmental Sciences, University of Virginia \(2014\)

**Research Interests**

-   Remote sensing and geospatial analysis
-   Ecosystem services
-   Land cover and land use change
-   Agroecology
-   Sustainable soil management

I am interested in how data\-supported insights can open communication channels that improve working relationships between farmers and the public sector\. Utilizing remotely sensed data, I look at regional changes that will affect environmental and economic benefits provided by the landscape \(i\.e\. soil, plants, animals\), primarily in an agricultural context\.
