**SAL Lab Mitacs Intern  
Undergraduate Student, BASc Environmental Engineering\.**

Education  
UNESP Universidade Estadual Paulista “Júlio de Mesquita Filho”, Presidente Prudente campus, Brazil\.

**Research Interests:** My major interest in environmental field relies on technologies and methods that may decrease or change the bad impacts of our current way of living\.

**SAL Research:** I am a Globalink Research Intern from Brazil, I applied for Mitacs Globalink program to work in Canada during 2014 summer, for 12 weeks\. I have worked with Jason Lion on the [Lower Fraser Valley Nutrient Database project](http://https//sal-lab.landfood.ubc.ca/projects/nutrient-database-2/) from June until August\. We have been researching different pretreatments for plant tissue and manure samples to analyze them by Diffuse Reflectance Infrared Spectroscopy \(DRIFTS\) in order to reach a more accurate calibration\.
