# ***Visiting Professor –*** ***PhD in Crop Genetics and Breeding***

[Image](https://lfs-sal.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/06/songliang-210x300.png)

Email: [wsoloedu07@126\.com](mailto:wsoloedu07@126.com)

[ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Songliang_Wang/stats/report/weekly/2018-06-24)  [Linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/)

**Education**

-   PhD –Crop Genetics and Breeding, Fujian Agriculture and Forestry University
-   MSc – Farming system, Fujian Agricultural University \(1997\)
-   BSc – Agronomy, Fujian Agricultural College \(1990\)

**Research Interests**

-   Agroecology
-   Agroecosystem service
-   Ecological agriculture and Community Supported Agriculture integration

I am an Agroecology professor from the Fujian Agriculture and Forestry University \(FAFU\)\.  I have been teaching Agroecology in FAFU since 1990\.  My research interests include Agroecology, Agroecosystem Service Evaluation &amp; Realization \(policy implication\), and Climate Smart Agriculture and so on\.

I am currently a visiting professor of SAL lab at UBC（April 2018 –April 2019\)\.

**Current Research**

***Study 1:***

Comparative studies on agroecosystem service evaluation between China and Canada\. The research aims at an institutional arrangement for compensation rather than a impulsion subsidies by government\.

For more detail: Click [here](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Songliang_Wang/stats/report/weekly/2018-06-24)

***Study 2:*** 

Evaluation and development of Internationalizing faculties in China’s university\. The research aims at building the evaluation index for internationalizing faculties for China’s universities and colleges\.

For more detail: Click [here](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Songliang_Wang/stats/report/weekly/2018-06-24)
