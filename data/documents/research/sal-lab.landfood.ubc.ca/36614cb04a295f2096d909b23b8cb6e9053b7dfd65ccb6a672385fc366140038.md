**Projects at the SAL Lab  
**

At the SAL Lab, I am assisting the team working on the [Okanagan Bioregion Nutrient Management Project](https://lfs-sal.sites.olt.ubc.ca/projects/okanagan-bioregion-nutrient-management/)\. My tasks involve mapping nutrient flows and hotspots in the Okanagan Bioregion to identify opportunities for improved nutrient management\.
