***Graduate Student –PhD in Sustainable Development   [Image](https://lfs-sal.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/05/Julio-Reis.jpg)***

***Visiting Researcher***

Email: [julioecn@gmail\.com](mailto:julioecn@gmail.com) 

**Education**

-   MSc – Economics, Center for Development and Regional Planning \(Cedeplar\)/UFMG \(2008\)
-   BSc – Economics, Federal University of Minas Gerais \(2005\)

**Research Interests**

-   Sustainable development
-   Agricultural economics
-   Sustainable agricultural systems \(Integrated crop\-livestock – forest systems\)
-   Macroeconomics

My main research interest is evaluating the economic potential of alternative agriculture systems \(e\.g\. integrated crop\-livestock\-forest and agroforestry\) to be used as a sustainable development strategy in the Amazon and in the Brazilian Cerrado\.

**Current Research**

***Study 1:*** ***Establishment of Technological and Economic Performance units in the state of Mato Grosso: Economic Viability Approach***

The objective of this research is to propose an economic evaluation approach for integrated crop\-livestock\-forestry systems \(iCLF\) in Mato Grosso\(iLPF\)\.

***Study 2:*** ***Methodologies and New Approaches to Economic Evaluation of Integration Systems for Agricultural Livestock \(iLPF\)***

The objective of this research is to validate an economic evaluation approach of integrated crop\-livestock\-forestry systems \(iCLF\) in different regions of Brazil\.
