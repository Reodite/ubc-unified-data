I am an undergraduate Work Learn in the Sustainable Agriculture Lab, primarily assisting with the RCRV project\. My responsibilities involve a combination of field work and lab analysis\. When in the field, my tasks include gathering soil samples, collecting greenhouse gas measurements using gasmat readings, installing resin probes, and administering seed or compost treatments\. In the lab, I process soil samples, recharge resin probes, and analyze gasmat data\.
