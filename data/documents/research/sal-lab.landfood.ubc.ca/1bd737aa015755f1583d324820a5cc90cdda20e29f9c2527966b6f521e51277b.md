**Projects at the SAL Lab**

I am working on [*Agricultural Greenhouse Gases*](https://www.landfood.ubc.ca/research/lfs-research-centres-groups/soil-water-and-sustainability-group/aggp-lfv/) and [*Too Much Water or Too Little*?](https://sal-lab.landfood.ubc.ca/projects/too-much-too-little-climate-resilient-vegetable-farming/) projects as a research assistant\. I work in the field and lab, monitoring soil greenhouse gas fluxes and assisting with planting at the UBC farm\. In the lab, I process and extract soil samples for plant available nitrogen analysis\.
