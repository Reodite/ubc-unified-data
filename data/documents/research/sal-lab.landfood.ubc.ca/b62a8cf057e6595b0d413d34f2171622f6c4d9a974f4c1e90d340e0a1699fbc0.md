**Education**

-   BSc – Environmental Science and Terrestrial Resource Management, University of Washington \(2015\)
-   AS – Biology, Edmonds Community College \(2012\)

**Research Interests**

-   Regenerative agriculture and sustainable soil management
-   Mitigation of agricultural impacts on climate change
-   Use of audiomedia in dissemination of scientific research
-   Historic First Nations agricultural practices and indigenous ways of knowing

**Research**

My Master’s project focused on on climate adaptation strategies for organic vegetable growers in B\.C\., investigating impacts of [overwinter soil management strategies](https://sal-lab.landfood.ubc.ca/projects/too-much-too-little-climate-resilient-vegetable-farming/); fallow, tarped, and cover cropped\. Currently, I coordinate the Vancouver Island winter vegetable variety trial and am the project lead developing the B\.C\. cover crop database\.
