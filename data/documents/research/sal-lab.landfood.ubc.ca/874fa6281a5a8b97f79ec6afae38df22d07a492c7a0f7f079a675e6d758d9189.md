**Current Research**

I am a Mitacs Globalink Research student from China, and I’m working in the SAL lab from July to October 2019\. I have worked on the agricultural greenhouse gases project \(AGGP\) to explore climate change studies\. We have been recording the concentration of CO2, N2O and CH4 using the Gasmet portable FTIR and developing R codes to calculate soil greenhouse gas fluxes\.
