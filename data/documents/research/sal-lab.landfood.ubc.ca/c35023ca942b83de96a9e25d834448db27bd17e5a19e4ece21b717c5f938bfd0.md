**Research Interests**

-   Nutrient management and cycling
-   Soil health/fertility
-   Sustainable agricultural systems
-   Soil\-plant nutrient relationships
-   Soil chemical processes

I am interested in carbon, plant\-nutrient, and water dynamics within agricultural landscapes and how these may be mediated by management practices\. Specifically, looking at climate\-adaptation strategies to increase the sustainability of regional agricultural systems\.
