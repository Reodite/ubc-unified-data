**Education**

-   BSc – Agriculture and Environmental Science, Specializing in Plant Production, Minor in Field Studies, McGill University \(2020\)

**Research Interests**

-   Regenerative agriculture
-   Agroecology
-   Biodiversity conservation
-   Climate change resilience

**Current Project**  
As an NSERC USRA, I assisted with research on the Organic Vegetable Nutrient Management Project\. While in the lab, I collected and processed plant and soil samples, set up research plots, managed data, and monitored greenhouse gas emissions with a Gasmet portable FTIR spectrophotometer\. For more information, visit the project page [HERE](https://sal-lab.landfood.ubc.ca/projects/organic-vegetable-nutrient-management/)\.
