Research Interests

Growing up on a small horse farm nestled on the Niagara Escarpment instilled in me a great appreciation for the environment from a young age\. Since then, my passion for the environment has continued to grow and has been nurtured throughout my education\. After exploring various branches of sustainability, I have developed a keen focus on regenerative, sustainable food systems, soil health, and their connections to climate change mitigation\. Specifically, I am interested in understanding how to better support and implement regenerative agricultural practices in hopes of fostering sustainable, socioecological, climate\-resilient communities\.

Research

My research focuses on reducing the climate impacts of organic vegetable production by exploring agricultural management practices that minimize greenhouse gas emissions, while maintaining crop yield and soil health\. Specifically, my research takes place on the UBC Farm and looks at different methods of tillage and tarping practices\. Using my findings, I hope to provide best management practice recommendations that minimize climate impacts to better inform and support small\-scale, organic farmers in the Lower Mainland who rely on similar practices\.
