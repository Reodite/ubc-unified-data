**Current Research**

***Study 1: Soil Carbon Management in*** ***Okanagan Orchards***

I am currently working on carbon recapture strategies for perennial orchard systems in the Okanagan B\.C\., investigating the impacts of soil surface management strategies: organic mulches and cover crops\. Investigating the resilience of carbon in agricultural soils to climate warming\. More information about this project can be found here\.
