**Current Research**

As a WorkLearn student with the [Organic Vegetable Nutrient Management Project](https://sal-lab.landfood.ubc.ca/projects/organic-vegetable-nutrient-management/) \(OVNM\),  I take part in field preparation and monitoring at the UBC Farm\. I collect crop, root and soil samples, and conduct nutrient analysis in the lab\. Key findings examining the impact of compost applications on Brassica root strategies from my work with the OVNM project can be [found here](https://lfs-sal.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/05/APBI499_Friesen.pdf)\.
