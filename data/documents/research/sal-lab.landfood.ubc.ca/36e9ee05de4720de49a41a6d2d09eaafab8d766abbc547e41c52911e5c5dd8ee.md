As a WorkLearn student with the [Organic Vegetable Nutrient Management Project](https://sal-lab.landfood.ubc.ca/projects/organic-vegetable-nutrient-management/), I process crop and soil samples for analysis as well as maintain research plots at the UBC farm\. I have also done work with Dr\.
