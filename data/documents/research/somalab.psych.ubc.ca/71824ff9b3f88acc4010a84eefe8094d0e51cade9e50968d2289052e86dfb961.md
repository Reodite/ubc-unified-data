Marie completed her BA in Psychology in 2018\. She was in the [Psychology Honours Program](https://psych.ubc.ca/undergraduate/honours-program/?login) and presented her research at the [UBC Psychology Undergraduate Research Conference](http://www.uroubc.ca/conferences/)\. For her Honours project, she worked with Whitney Krieger to develop novel methods to measure the motivation to exercise in rats\.

Marie then completed a JD at UBC Law School\.
