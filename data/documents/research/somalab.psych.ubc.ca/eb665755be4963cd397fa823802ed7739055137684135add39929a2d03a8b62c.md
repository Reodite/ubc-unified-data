#### Hormones play many important roles in brain and immune function\. The Soma Lab explores how steroid hormones regulate the nervous system and immune system, using a multidisciplinary approach and some of the most advanced analytical tools available\. We welcome collaborations, especially for steroid analyses by our state\-of\-the\-art mass spectrometry \(LC\-MS/MS\) system\.

#### Our work is supported by CIHR, NSERC and CFI\.
