Bobby was a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Soma Lab \(2011\-2013\) and studied how food availability impacts social behaviour, neuropeptides, and neurosteroids\. He received a prestigious Postdoctoral Fellowship from NSERC\. He also collaborated with [Prof\. Emma Guns](http://www.prostatecentre.com/about-us/people/dr-emma-s-tomlinson-guns) \(Vancouver Prostate Centre\) to study how food quality \(macronutrient composition\) affects androgen synthesis within prostate tumors in mice, particularly the beneficial effects of a low\-carbohydrate high\-protein diet\.

Bobby is currently an [Associate Professor at Rollins College](https://fokidislab.weebly.com/) in Florida, USA\.
