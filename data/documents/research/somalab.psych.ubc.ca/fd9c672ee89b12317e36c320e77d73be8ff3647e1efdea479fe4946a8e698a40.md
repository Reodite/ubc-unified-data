[Jenn](https://twitter.com/JenniferKFerris) worked in the Soma Lab as an undergraduate and examined two neuropeptides that regulate reproduction \(GnRH and GnIH\)\. She received a Faculty of Arts [AURA award](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/research/grant-funding-opportunities/internal-arts-grants/arts-undergraduate-research-awards-aura/) to complete this project\. From her work in the Soma Lab as an undergraduate, Jenn is a co\-author on [2 papers](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=Vlu_duIAAAAJ&amp;hl=en) and the [first\-author on a paper](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=Vlu_duIAAAAJ&amp;hl=en) in *Journal of Neuroendocrinology*\.

Jenn completed a MSc in Neuroscience and is currently a PhD student in Rehabilitation Science at UBC\.
