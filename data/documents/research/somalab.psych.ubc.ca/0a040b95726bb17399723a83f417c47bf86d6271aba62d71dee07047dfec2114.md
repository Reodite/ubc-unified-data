Amy was a PhD student in the Soma Lab \(2004\-2009\) and focused on the interactions between glucocorticoids and DHEA\. She received Graduate Fellowships from NSERC and MSFHR\. She also collaborated with [Prof\. Scott MacDougall\-Shackleton](http://psychology.uwo.ca/people/faculty/profiles/macdougall-shackleton.html) \(Univeristy of Western Ontario\) to study how corticosterone and DHEA regulate adult neurogenesis\. She was then an NSERC Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Guelph\.

Amy is currently an [Associate Professor](https://www.uoguelph.ca/ib/newman) at the University of Guelph in Ontario\.
