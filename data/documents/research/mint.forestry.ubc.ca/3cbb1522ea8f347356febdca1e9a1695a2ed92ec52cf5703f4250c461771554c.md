MINT offers Forest Therapy Experiences for UBC students and staff\. An Indoor Forest Therapy Experience is offered on the **second Wednesday of** each month, with two sessions at **1:30 pm and 2:00 pm**\. An Outdoor Forest Therapy Experience is offered on the **third Wednesday** of each month **at 1:30 pm\.**

Advance registration is required at least two days prior to the session \(by 12:00 pm on Monday\)\.  
Registration for each session will close once capacity is reached\. You are welcome to register in advance for a future session\.  
A confirmation email will be sent 1–2 days before the session to confirm your spot\.

Indoor Forestry Therapy experience will take 20\-30 minutes, outdoor Forestry Therapy experience will take about 60 minutes in group\.
