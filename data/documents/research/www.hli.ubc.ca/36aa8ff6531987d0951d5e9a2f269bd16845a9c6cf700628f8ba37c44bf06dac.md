Established in 2018, the HLI Trainee Association’s mission is to enhance the academic experience of all trainees by providing an environment to foster enhanced collaboration, education, professional growth, and career success\.

Since 2018, the Trainee Association has organized a number of events, including the annual Trainee Research Day in June 2019, as well as several pub nights and outdoor events like hiking, a beach potluck, soccer games, and more\.

[Image](https://www.hli.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Trainee-Research-Day-2019-1600x898.jpg)

[Image](https://www.hli.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/TRD-Pub-2019-1600x898.jpg)

[Image](https://www.hli.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Soccer-2-2019.jpg)

These events not only welcome new trainees to the Centre and allow trainees from different research groups to socialize, but more importantly, create a sense of community to support trainees as they pursue their own research projects\.
