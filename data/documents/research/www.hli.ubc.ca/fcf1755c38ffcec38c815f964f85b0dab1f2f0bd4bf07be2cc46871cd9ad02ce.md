**Understanding the mechanisms that keep the human body alive and functioning\.**

## The human body in health and disease

By studying the mechanical, physical, and biochemical functions of the human body, and well as its organs, and the cells of which it is composed, our researchers develop a better understanding of how all of these systems function\. This work can lead to better strategies for health and can inform decisions made during disease\.
