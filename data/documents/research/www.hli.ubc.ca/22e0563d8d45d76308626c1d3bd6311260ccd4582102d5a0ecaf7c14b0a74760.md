## St\. Paul’s Foundation

The Centre for Heart Lung Innovation within St\. Paul’s Hospital has been supported over the years by the St\. Paul’s Hospital Foundation which raises funds for research, patient care, equipment and investigator/trainee salary awards\. Support us by donating to the St\. Paul’s Foundation online, or call the foundation office directly at 604\-682\-8206\.

[Donate](https://donate.helpstpauls.com/HLI)

## University of British Columbia

The Centre for Heart Lung Innovation is also a Centre of Excellence for Heart, Lung, Vessel and Critical Care research within the University of British Columbia, and all of our researchers are Professors within the University training the next generation of scientists, clinicians and healthcare workers\. Support our work and education today through the University of British Columbia website\. Select “other” and type in “Centre for Heart Lung Innovation” to donate to us directly\. If you prefer to speak directly with a representative, please call us at 604\-822\-8900 or toll\-free in North America at 1\-877\-717\-GIVE\.
