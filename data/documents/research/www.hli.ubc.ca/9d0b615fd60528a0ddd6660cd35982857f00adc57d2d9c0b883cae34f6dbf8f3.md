## High\-resolution imaging techniques

Our fleet of confocal microscopes and micro\-computed tomography scanner enable visualization of samples at the highest resolutions\. Guided by their imaging results, our investigators make cutting\-edge advances in their research to uncover the mechanisms of disease\.
