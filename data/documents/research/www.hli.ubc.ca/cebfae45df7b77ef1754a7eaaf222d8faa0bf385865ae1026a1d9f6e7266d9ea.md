## Microanatomy of cells, tissues, and organs

Equipped with cutting edge technologies, and staffed by a CSMLS certified histotechnologist, the Histology Facility provides both routine and specialized histological support to the Lower Mainland research community, including specific services listed below, and assay development and optimization\.

### Processing and Embedding

Parrafin embedding, OCT embedding for cryosectioning, methylmethacrylate for electron microscopy\.

### Sectioning

Paraffin, frozen, methylmethacrylate, decalcification \(acid\)

### Staining

Histological staining \(see below\), immunohistochemistry \(peroxidase and/or alkaline phosphatase\), immunofluorescence, TUNEL staining, in situ hybridization \(ISH\)\.

### Histological Stains Available

General morphology: Hematoxylin and Eosin, Toluidine Blue, Giemsa, TTC staining\.

Carbohydrates: Periodic Acid\-Schiff \(PAS\), PAS/diastase \(glycogen\), Alcian blue, Alcian blue with PAS\.

Connective Tissue: Trichromes \(Masson, Gomori\), Movat’s Pentachrome, Gomori’s Aldehyde Fuchsin, Verhoeff/Van Gieson, Picrosirius Red, Gomori’s silver impregnation

Lipids: Oil Red O

Microorganisms: Gram, Brown and Brenn, Zeihl\-Neelsen, Grocott

Nucleic Acids: Feulgen

Intracellular Granules: Masson Fontana \(melanin\), Von Kossa \(calcium\), amyloid, Perl’s Prussian Blue \(hemosiderin\)\.

Hematology: Hansel’s \(Eosinophils\), Wright’s
