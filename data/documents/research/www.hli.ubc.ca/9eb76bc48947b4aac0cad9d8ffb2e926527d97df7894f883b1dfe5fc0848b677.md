## Vision and Mission Statement

**Vision:** Fostering the next generation of world\-leading cardiopulmonary scientists\.

**Mission:** We aim to enhance the academic experience of HLI trainees by promoting a training environment enriched with collaboration, education, professional growth and career success\.

[http://donate\.helpstpauls\.com/hli](http://donate.helpstpauls.com/hli)[Image](https://www.hli.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Trainee-Research-Day-2019-1600x898.jpg)

## Join us\!

Are you an undergraduate student, a graduate student, or a postdoctoral fellow at HLI? Join the HLI Trainee Association\! We organize HLI Research Days, social events, and educational programming targeted toward the needs of our trainees\. If you want to get more involved in our academic community, shape the trainee experience at HLI, and meet other trainees, email us at [trainees@hli\.ubc\.ca](mailto:trainees@hli.ubc.ca) or stay tuned for announcements from Reception on our meeting times\.
