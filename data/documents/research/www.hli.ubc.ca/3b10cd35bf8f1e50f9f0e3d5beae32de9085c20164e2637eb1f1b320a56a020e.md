Congratulations to HLI Principal Investigators on receiving awards from the UBC Faculty of Medicine\!

[Image](https://www.hli.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Keith_Walley-e1598909424121.jpg)

### **Dr\. Keith Walley \(Division of Critical Care\) –** ***2020 Bill and Marilyn Webber Lifetime Achievement Award***

The Bill and Marilyn Webber Lifetime Achievement Award recognizes extraordinary members of the Faculty of Medicine who have had sustained distinguished careers at UBC in the areas of research, teaching and/or service\.

[Image](https://www.hli.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/leipsic.jpg)

### **Dr\. Jonathon Leipsic \(Division of Cardiology\) –** ***2020 Distinguished Achievement Award for Overall Excellence – Senior Faculty*** 

In recognition of outstanding contributions in the areas of research, education, and service\.

[Image](https://www.hli.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/1DE_1613-scaled-e1599247004482-1176x1600.jpg)

### **Dr\. Andrew Krahn \(Division of Cardiology\) –** ***2019 UBC Killiam Research Prize***

Awarded to full\-time faculty members in recognition of outstanding research and scholarly contributions\.

Congratulations all\! Click [here](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/2020-faculty-of-medicine-award-recipients/) for the full list of recipients\.
