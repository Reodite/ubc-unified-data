## HLI EDI Committee

The HLI EDI Committee is a volunteer\-led committee that aims to provide education and increase awareness around EDI\-related issues\. The committee provides resources, direction and guidance on EDI policies, and organizes events and workshops mainly focusing on issues faced by equity\-deserving groups\.

**Vision:** We create initiatives to establish lasting meaningful change to make the academic workplace environment more equitable, diverse, and inclusive for faculty, staff, and students at HLI\.

**Mission:** The HLI EDI Committee defines and implements educational programs to empower minority members within HLI and enhance recruitment and retention of diverse researchers\.

**Values and Guiding Principles:**

-   Equity
-   Diversity
-   Inclusion
-   Humility
-   Courage
-   Commitment

### Basak Sahin \(she/they\)

Co\-Chair, EDI Committee

MPCL Technician Basak Sahin is passionate about enhancing the working environment for everyone at the HLI\.

As Co\-Chair of the EDI Committee, she has played a key role in 1\) increasing knowledge of the barriers faced by underrepresented groups in research, 2\) identifying ways to address these barriers, and, 3\) ensuring equitable access to HLI facilities and inclusion of researchers of various backgrounds and identities\.
