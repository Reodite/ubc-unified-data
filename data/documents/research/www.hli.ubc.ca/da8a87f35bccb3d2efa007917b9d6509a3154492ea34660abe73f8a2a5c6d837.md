HLI investigators Drs\. Liam Brunham and Simon Pimstone led a team to assess trends in the incidence, risk factors, and sex\-related differences in premature atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease from 2000 to 2016\. Using a provincial  
cardiac registry to identify young patients \(men &lt;50 years, women &lt;55 years\), the team found that despite the overall decrease in cardiovascular events and death in the general population, these trends did not extend to young patients\.

[Image](https://www.hli.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/SAVE-BC_SP-LB.jpg)

To address this important health challenge, Drs\. Brunham and Pimstone are enrolling patients into [SAVE BC](https://savebc.ca/) \(Study to Avoid CardioVascular Events in BC\), a study to identify, treat, and prevent premature heart disease\. This study was published in the [Journal of the American Heart Association](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/JAHA.119.012178), and was featured in this Daily Scan [article](http://thedailyscan.providencehealthcare.org/2019/07/study-on-young-people-and-heart-disease/)\. First author Diana Vikulova is a HLI graduate student\.
