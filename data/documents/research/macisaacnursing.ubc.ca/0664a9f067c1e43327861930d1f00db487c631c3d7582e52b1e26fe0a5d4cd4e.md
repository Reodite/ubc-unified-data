Multiple UBC School of Nursing faculty members have been recognized with research chairs and professorships, underscoring their exceptional contributions to the field of nursing and beyond\. These research chairs provide vital support to faculty as they advance innovative solutions to pressing healthcare challenges, improve patient care, and shape policy\.
