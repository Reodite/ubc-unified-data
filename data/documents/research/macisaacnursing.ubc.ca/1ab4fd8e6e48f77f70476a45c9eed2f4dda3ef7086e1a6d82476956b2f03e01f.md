The Office of Nursing Research &amp; Teaching Scholarship \(ONRTS\) aims to provide timely support to aid UBC Nursing researchers to identify and apply for research funding\. This includes assistance with obtaining institutional approval for funding applications\.

In general, UBC requires a preview of the application in advance of their providing institutional approvals in either hardcopy or electronically\. Elements of the application that are required in advance include:

-   Title Page;
-   Agency Signature Page \(*if applicable*\);
-   Abstract or Summary of Proposed Project;
-   Agency Ethics Certification Requirements;
-   Budget and accompanying financial information; and
-   Letters of support for any matching funds \(*if applicable*\)\.
-   A signed [Research Project Information Form](https://research.ubc.ca/support-resources/forms-tools-resources/research-project-information-form) \(RPIF\)

For electronic submissions, this creates a double internal deadline:

-   **Nursing internal deadline**: 1 business day before UBC deadline \- for dept head sig on RPIF
-   **UBC internal deadline**: 5 business days before sponsor’s deadline
-   **Applicant’s web submission deadline**: 2 business days before funder’s deadline
-   **Funder’s deadline**: UBC to click final submission button on funder’s online system

  
Contact Merrilee Hughes in the Office of Nursing Research &amp; Teaching Scholarship \(ONRTS\) to confirm your intent to submit, internal deadlines, and/or request application support\.

### 2026 SSHRC Postdoctoral Fellowship

UBC postdoctoral applicants apply directly to the funder, so a UBC Research Project information Form \(RPIF\) is not required at this stage\. However, the SSHRC postdoc application does require an Institutional nomination and commitment form to be completed by the Department Head\. The Director of the School of Nursing requests that related materials and formal request be provided 3\-weeks in advance of the funder's deadline\. Please contact the Office of Nursing Research &amp; Teaching Scholarship \(ONRTS\) for more information\.
