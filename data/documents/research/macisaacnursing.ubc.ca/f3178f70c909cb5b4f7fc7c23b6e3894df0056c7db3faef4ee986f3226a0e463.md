Our research units, centres and research clusters support multi\-year collaborative teams and programmatic funding in specific areas\.

The rich diversity of foci and approaches encompassed by nursing research defies simple categorization\. In addition to exploring the research concentrations listed below, please view researcher profiles in [**Our People**](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/about/our-people "Our People") or search by keyword to identify experts in specific areas\. 

Click the button below to browse UBC Nursing's Focus on Faculty 2025/26 brochure to learn more about individual faculty members' areas of expertise\.

## Research

[ACCESS Logo](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2025-03/Access%20Aging%2C%20Cancer%20Care%20%26%20Support.jpg.webp?h=8ebca6e1&amp;itok=62xmBevq)

### [Aging, Cancer Care \+ Support \(ACCESS\) Research Lab](https://accesslab.nursing.ubc.ca/)

ACCESS convenes older adults with lived experience, clinicians, and researchers from across the country focused on cancer and aging\.

[Capacity Logo](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2025-08/capacity-logo.png.webp?h=0bbfb5e8&amp;itok=vaZl9QVK)

### [Capacity: The Centre for Research in Community Engagement &amp; Gender Equity](http://capacitycentre.ubc.ca/)

Capacity: The Centre for Research in Community Engagement &amp; Gender Equity is an interdisciplinary, multi\-sectoral, applied research centre focusing on advancing the knowledge necessary to progress gender equity as a global health and human rights concern\.

[Public Health nurses\- alumni of 1920's\- stand in front of their vehicles](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2024-10/Card%20-%20Consortium.jpg.webp?h=4c1fc98e&amp;itok=FzDYOnX5)

### [The Consortium for Nursing History Inquiry](https://blogs.ubc.ca/nursinghistory)

The Consortium serves as a resource for students, faculty, and the wider community to explore and scrutinize nursing and health care’s past\.

[CRiHHI Logo](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2024-10/Card%20-%20CRiHHI.jpg.webp?h=4c1fc98e&amp;itok=W-699bjn)

### [Critical Research in Health and Healthcare Inequities \(CRiHHI\)](https://crihhi.nursing.ubc.ca/)

CRiHHI promotes greater equity in health and health care through the uptake of critical knowledge in practice, policy, education, and research\.

[Gero Logo over grey\-haired person sitting on a bench](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2024-10/Card%20-%20GERO.jpg.webp?h=4c1fc98e&amp;itok=dhCQkSMA)

### [Gerontological Education Research &amp; Outreach \(GERO\)](https://gero.nursing.ubc.ca/)

GERO is committed to advancing the nursing care of older adults through education, research, and community engagement\.

[HEET logo](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2024-10/Card%20-%20HEET.jpg.webp?h=4c1fc98e&amp;itok=KOciFHPR)

### [Healthcare Ethics &amp; Equity Team \(HEET\)](https://heet.nursing.ubc.ca/)

The Healthcare Ethics &amp; Equity Team is dedicated to community\-engaged research, knowledge translation, and education to address ethical issues and promote health equity\.

[HERO logo](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2025-11/hero-hero-940x470.png.webp?h=3eed97fe&amp;itok=bK8bFIQP)

### [HERO Lab \- Health Equity &amp; Resilience Observatory](https://herolab.nursing.ubc.ca/)

HERO brings together frontline clinical expertise, cutting\-edge AI, and global health leadership to transform evidence into action and limit the spread of misinformation, especially in times of crisis\.

[HERON Lab Logo](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2026-04/heron-lab-unit-card.jpg.webp?h=4c1fc98e&amp;itok=EzztTGYo)

### [HERON Lab](https://heronlab.nursing.ubc.ca/)

HERON Lab focuses on developing evidence\-based frameworks to address disparities in neurotrauma prevention, treatment, and recovery among equity\-deserving groups of children and adolescents – a comprehensive approach examining social determinants of health, access to care, and patient outcomes across underserved communities\.

[HOPE logo](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2024-10/Card%20-%20Hope%20Lab.jpg.webp?h=4c1fc98e&amp;itok=gRg8z7Af)

### [HOPE Lab](https://hope.nursing.ubc.ca/)

The Healthy Outcomes by Improving Patient &amp; Provider Experiences \(HOPE\) lab works to shed light on the importance of the healthcare providers' experience and its connection to quality of care\.

[IDEA Lab Logo](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2024-10/Card%20-%20Idealab.jpg.webp?h=4c1fc98e&amp;itok=3axtZgbS)

### [Innovation in Dementia and Aging \(IDEALab\)](https://idea.nursing.ubc.ca/)

This lab aims to innovate in dementia care for elderly patients, often through the use of new technology\. This research is primarily patient\-oriented and developed by working closely with many patient partners to improve outcomes of patients\.

[Learn Lab Logo](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2024-10/Card%20-%20Learn%20Lab.jpg.webp?h=4c1fc98e&amp;itok=DfyhqS48)

### [Leadership, Excellence, and Research in Nursing Education \(LEARN Lab\)](https://learnlab.nursing.ubc.ca/)

The LEaRN Lab team is committed to exploring, understanding, and cultivating excellence in teaching, learning, and nursing practice through educational research and knowledge mobilization\.

Wellstream researchers are dedicated to generating innovative solutions to strengthening mental health and reducing substance use harms for children, youth, and their communities\.

##### Reducing Male Suicide

This Research Excellence Cluster aims to address men's mental health inequities, optimize men's mental health help\-seeking experiences, and equip community\-based and professional mental\-health care services to expertly evaluate and treat men's mental illnesses and suicidality\.

Read more on [Reducing Male Suicide\.](https://reducingmalesuicide.ubc.ca/)

*Cluster Lead:* [*Dr\. John Oliffe*](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/john-oliffe)

##### Transformative Health &amp; Justice

This Research Excellence Cluster is a partnership\-based, transdisciplinary, intersectoral and cross\-social group aiming to support equity\-oriented, decolonizing and culturally safe research, program, and policy development to address health and justice inequities\.​

Read more on [Transformative Health and Justice\.](https://transformhealthjustice.ubc.ca/)

*Cluster Lead:* [*Dr\. Helen Brown*](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/helen-brown)

##### Re\-Imagining Supports for Children with Medical Complexity and their Families

Families of children with medical complexity experience physical, social, and economic exclusion\. Medical complexity is characterized by the presence of complex, chronic conditions requiring specialized care, substantial health needs, functional dependence and/or limitations, and frequent healthcare usage\. This cluster will advance knowledge, clinical programs and policy to support these families through a community\-led and comprehensive research program and knowledge exchange activities\. 

Read more at: [https://thrivingkids\.ubc\.ca/](https://thrivingkids.ubc.ca/) 

*Cluster Lead:* [Dr\. Jennifer Baumbusch](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/jennifer-baumbusch)

##### Justice &amp; Equity in Perinatal Services

This pan\-Canadian coalition of researchers, perinatal health workers, service users, and community organizations is actively engaged in advancing equity and justice in reproductive health\.

Read more on [Justice &amp; Equity in Perinatal Services\.](https://perinataljustice.ubc.ca/)

*Cluster Members:* [*Dr\. Emmanuela Ojukwu*](https://perinataljustice.ubc.ca/people/emmanuela-ojukwu)*,* [*Dr\. Helen Brown*](https://perinataljustice.ubc.ca/people/helen-brown)

##### Designing for People \(DFP\)

UBC's Designing for People research network is accelerating creativity at the nexus of social and technical problem\-solving, by promoting interdisciplinary teamwork and methods, finding new paths to societal impact through partners and community, and creating sharable resources\.

Read more on [Designing for People\.](https://dfp.ubc.ca/)

*Cluster Members:* [*Dr\. Fatawu Abdulai*](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/abdul-fatawu-abdulai)*,* [*Dr\. Kristen Haase*](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/kristen-haase)*,* [*Dr\. Lillian Hung*](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/lillian-hung)*,* [*Dr\. Manon Ranger*](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/manon-ranger)

##### Nursing Simulation Research and Teaching

This website combines simulation\-based health education pedagogy, maternal\-infant health \(specific to lactation\) and teaching/learning design\. Project Lead: [**Dr\. Suzanne Campbell**](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/suzanne-campbell "Suzanne Campbell")

[Nursing Simulation](https://simulation.nursing.ubc.ca/)

##### Care for Complex Co\-occuring Disorders \(CCD\)

Care for Complex Co\-occuring Disorders \(CCD\), is a prospective cohort study that examines individuals with co\-occurring psychiatric, substance use disorders and physical health conditions to track trajectories of service use and health outcomes in this population\. Principal Investigator, [**Dr\. Angela Russolillo**](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/angela-russolillo "Angela Russolillo")\.

[CCD Website](https://www.careforccd.ca/home)
