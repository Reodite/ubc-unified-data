ICaRN is a multidisciplinary research network formed at the University of British Columbia that promotes an end\-to\-end approach to improve the productivity of block and panel cave mines\. The research network includes a group of representatives from industry and academia covering the fields of geology, cave mining, geomechanics, and mineral processing\.

[About ICaRN](https://icarn.ubc.ca/)
