**THINKING YOU MIGHT WANT TO JOIN OUR ZOO?**

Awesome\! Thanks for considering us\! Check out the links below for the type of position you want\.

---

[For Prospective Graduate Students](https://peerlab.psych.ubc.ca/for-prospective-graduate-students/)

This means people interested in applying to the UBC Psychology MA/PhD graduate program, to be in this lab\.

[For Prospective Undergraduate and Post\-Bac Research Assistants](https://peerlab.psych.ubc.ca/for-prospective-students/for-prospective-undergraduates-and-post-bacs/)

This means undergraduates at UBC or elsewhere, or recent graduates taking time off between undergrad and grad school, who wish to be a research assistant in this lab\. This includes volunteer research assistants, PSYC240 students, paid positions such as the Quinn or Work\-Learn, honours students, and students who wish to complete a directed studies or any other type of independent research project \(includes capstone or other courses where a lab and a supervisor is required to complete a research project\)\.
