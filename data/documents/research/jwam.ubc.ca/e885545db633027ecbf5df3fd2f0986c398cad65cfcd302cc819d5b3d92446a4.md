> This project highlights how cultural and intercultural communication manifest in plurilingual students’ academic writing\.

Through mixed research methods, this research focuses on: a\) cross\-examining the variations in writing between monolingual students and plurilingual students; b\) examining how intercultural rhetoric is represented in their writing\. Theoretical and pedagogical implications will also be addressed\.
