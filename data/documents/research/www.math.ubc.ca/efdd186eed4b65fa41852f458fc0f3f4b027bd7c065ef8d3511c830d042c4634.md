##  Research Sub\-Navigation

UBC’s Department of Mathematics is one of Canada’s best, with a strong international reputation and links around the world\. The Department’s research activities take place in a number of loosely affiliated research groups, which collaborate not only in research, but also in graduate and postdoctoral supervision and sponsorship\. Most groups run their own weekly seminar series\. If you are thinking about coming to UBC for graduate school in mathematics or to carry out postdoctoral research, or you are just curious about what’s happening in our department, we encourage you to look through our research groups’ pages to see what we do\. We also have a growing collection of interviews with department members to help you get to know us\.
