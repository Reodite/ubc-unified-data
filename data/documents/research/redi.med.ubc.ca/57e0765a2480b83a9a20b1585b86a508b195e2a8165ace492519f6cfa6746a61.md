*Research Voices from the Field* showcases cutting\-edge research that breaks barriers and promotes inclusion in medicine\. Each edition spotlights a research publication and includes insights directly from the authors—revealing their motivations, the significance of their findings, and why the research matters for healthcare professionals everywhere\.

In this edition, ****Faisal Khosa****, Professor of Radiology and Chair of the Inclusion, Diversity, and Equity Advisory Committee for Doctors of BC, reflects on his edited book “*[Equity, Diversity and Inclusion: Sisyphean Undertaking to an Achievable Reality](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/equity-diversity-and-inclusion/khosa/978-0-443-26728-4)*” and shares his hope for a kinder, more inclusive future that we can build together, one gentle step at a time\.

[Equity, Diversity and Inclusion: Sisyphean Undertaking to an Achievable Reality](https://med-fom-cpp-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/61vDOWAyoML._SL1231_-780x960.jpg)

> This second book grew out of a simple conviction: everyone deserves to feel seen, valued, and able to belong\. My first book focused on the health professions, but its reception made something clear to me: the search for equity and belonging is not confined to medicine\. It is shared across professions, institutions, and communities\. So this time I widened the lens to eleven disciplines, from medicine and pharmacy to law, education, engineering, and beyond, because the barriers may take different forms, but the human aspiration is remarkably similar\.
> 
> The title captures both the difficulty and the possibility of this work\. Advancing equity can sometimes feel Sisyphean, like pushing a boulder uphill only to watch hard\-won progress slip backward\. But the central message of this book is that the summit is reachable, especially when we stop pushing alone\. Each chapter therefore moves beyond describing inequity to offer lived experiences, evidence, practical wisdom, and actions that individuals and institutions can begin taking today\.
> 
> That is where I believe the multiplier effect begins\. One person who feels included can find the confidence to contribute\. One mentor who opens a door can alter the trajectory of a career\. One leader who changes a policy can create opportunities for hundreds of people they may never meet\. And when those individuals go on to mentor, lead, teach, and advocate for others, a single act of inclusion can ripple across generations\. Equity becomes transformative when those who are empowered empower others\. 
> 
> For medicine, that multiplier effect reaches even further\. When our workforce better reflects the people we serve, colleagues feel they belong, learners see possibilities for themselves, and patients are more likely to feel understood and respected\. The benefits of inclusion therefore do not stop with the individual clinician\. They travel through teams, institutions, and ultimately to every patient and community touched by their work\.
> 
> Seeing medicine alongside ten other disciplines also reminds us that none of us is doing this work alone\. We can learn from one another, borrow what works, avoid repeating one another’s mistakes, and turn isolated efforts into collective progress\.
> 
> I hope and pray that readers close this book not simply believing that a kinder and more inclusive future is possible, but recognizing that they have a role in creating it\. The first step may belong to one person, but its impact need never end with them\.

#### [Equity, Diversity and Inclusion: Sisyphean Undertaking to an Achievable Reality](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/equity-diversity-and-inclusion/khosa/978-0-443-26728-4)

#### **Editor:** Faisal Khosa

#### **Abstract**

*Equity, Diversity and Inclusion: Sisyphean Undertaking to an Achievable Reality* explores critical themes across diverse academic disciplines, including business, education, engineering, graduate medical education, health sciences, law, medicine, pharmacy, psychology, public health, and veterinary\. Each chapter traces the evolution of its respective discipline, examining how landmark social movements, civil rights milestones, and legislative reforms have shaped the discourse on equity\. Alongside these histories, authors introduce key theoretical frameworks that illuminate the concept of intersectionality\.  
This book also confronts contemporary challenges head\-on\. From systemic discrimination and racial profiling to gender inequality and barriers to social and economic mobility, the chapters candidly address the obstacles faced by marginalized groups\. More importantly, this volume moves from identifying problems to driving solutions: it offers practical strategies and evidence\-based best practices for cultivating equity, diversity, and inclusion \(EDI\) in workplaces, educational institutions, and communities\. Ultimately, it invites readers to embrace the journey toward a more just and inclusive society\. It reminds us that while the challenges may be great, the transformation begins with everyone taking that first step\.
