The Research\-based Theatre Lab promotes theatre as research and collaborative initiatives between researchers, artists, and community members\.

Explore a recent special issue in [Qualitative Inquiry](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/10778004221097641) on ethics and Research\-based Theatre \(RbT\), edited by Research\-based Theatre Lab scholars Susan Cox, Marilys Guillemin, Jennica Nichols, and Monica Prendergast\.

Learn more about our initiatives and get involved\.

### Dark Secrets

Dark Secrets is part of a research project being led by the UBC School of Social Work that focuses on student to student abuse in Canada’s Residential School System\.

[See more](https://vpri-rbtlab-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research-projects/)

[Image](https://vpri-rbtlab-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/12/sm-onlineworkshop_0-2.jpg)

### Using RbT with D/deaf and hearing teachers

Simangele Mabena will share a work\-in\-progress based on the exploration of D/deaf and hearing teachers’ roles and pedagogy in teaching South African Sign Language using RbT, while navigating her role as a researcher\.

[See more](https://vpri-rbtlab-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research-projects/)

[Image](https://vpri-rbtlab-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/12/rock_the_boat_-_poster-2.jpg)

### Rock the Boat

Don’t Rock the Boat began as a live theatre workshop focusing on wellbeing issues that arise within the context of graduate student supervisory relationships\.

[See more](https://vpri-rbtlab-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/research-projects/)
