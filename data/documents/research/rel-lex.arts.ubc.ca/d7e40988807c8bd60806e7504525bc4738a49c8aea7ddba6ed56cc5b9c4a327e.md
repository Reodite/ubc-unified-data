New Frameworks for Community\-Informed Dictionary Work

Our literature review seeks to create a publicly available Relational Lexicography toolkit of resources to which readers can refer to inform and guide their dictionary projects\. We are building an annotated reference list of scholarly and other relevant literature on dictionaries and dictionary making\.

These annotated references provide a snapshot of current work in the field\. This toolkit is an open access resource and supports others developing dictionary projects\. To our knowledge, no such publicly\-available literature review exists in support of language workers initiating dictionary projects\.

Click [here](https://www.icerpress.ca/reports/ic56) to access or download our literature review, which was published with the [Institute for Community Engaged Research](https://icer.ok.ubc.ca/#:~:text=The%20Institute%20for%20Community%20Engaged,in%20Kelowna%2C%20BC%2C%20Canada) \(ICER\) Press at UBC Okanagan\.
