## Diseases studied by I3  labs:

-   Infectious diseases: West Nile Virus, Hepatitis C, pandemic flu, HIV, tuberculosis\.
-   Inflammatory diseases: colitis, inflammatory bowel disease, arthritis, atherosclerosis\.
-   Autoimmune diseases: diabetes, myocarditis\.
-   Immune cell cancers: T and B cell lymphomas, multiple myeloma\.
-   Metastatic cancers: Melanomas, breast and ovarian cancer\.

## Major research themes:

1.  How pathogens cause infections, manipulate host cells, and evade the immune system –[Drs\. Jean,](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/dr-francois-jean/) [Thompson](http://barn.lsi.ubc.ca/current-membership/dr-charles-thompson/), [Harder](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/dr-kenneth-harder/), [Horwitz\.](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/dr-marc-horwitz/)
2.  How immune cells respond to pathogens and tumor cells; How immune responses to pathogens and tumors can be enhanced – Drs\. [Johnson](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/dr-pauline-johnson/), [Gold](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/dr-mike-gold/), [Abraham](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/ninan-abraham/), [Harder,](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/dr-kenneth-harder/) [Matsuuchi\.](http://celldevelopment.ubc.ca/research/researchers/dr-linda-matsuuchi/)
3.  How immune cells travel to sites of infection; How cells move and how these processes influence immune cell trafficking and Tumor cell metastasis – Drs\. [Gold,](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/dr-mike-gold/) [Roskelley](http://celldevelopment.ubc.ca/research/researchers/dr-cal-roskelley/)\.
4.  How immune cells cause and resolve inflammation; How immune dysfunction leads to autoimmune and inflammatory diseases – Drs\. [Horwitz,](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/dr-marc-horwitz/) [Harder](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/dr-kenneth-harder/), [Gold](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/dr-mike-gold/),  [Johnson\.](https://iii.lsi.ubc.ca/primary-members/dr-pauline-johnson/)
