Crow Mobile Dictionary App

## Language Name

Crow\.

## Alternate Language Names

Apsáalooke aliláau\.

## Region

Montana, United States\.

## Who

The Language Conservancy, Crow Language Consortium \(CLC\)\.

## Others Involved

There is no information included in this resource about others involved\. There may be more information available once the app is downloaded\.

## Publishing Information

This app was released November 2020 on both the Apple Store and Google Play\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not appear to cite any authors or contributors\. There may be more information available once the app is downloaded\. The Crow Language Consortium \(CLC\) is recognized on the The Language Conservancy but not on the app’s download page\.

Crow to English, English to Crow\. The dictionary is searchable in both directions\.

This is bilingual, bidirectional, mobile app dictionary\.
