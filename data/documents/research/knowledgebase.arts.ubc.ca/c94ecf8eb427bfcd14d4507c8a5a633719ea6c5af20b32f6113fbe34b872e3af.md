Tŝilhqot’in Digital Verb Book

## Language Name

Chilcotin\.

## Alternate Language Names

Nenqayni Chʼih, Tŝilhqotʼin, Tsilhqotʼin, Tsilhqútʼin\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Aaron Plahn \(Tŝilhqot’in National Government\)\.

## Others Involved

Version 1\.0 includes ‘Third Person Singular Paradigms’ from Bella Alphonse\.

More information about others involved in the compilation and development of this app may be accessible once the app is downloaded\.

## Publishing Information

This app was published on Google Play in February of 2019, and was last updated in March of 2019\.

## How People are Cited

Without downloading the app we are unable to determine how, or if, people are cited within the dictionary\. The description of the app on Google Play cites contributors\.

## How Information is Cited

Without downloading the app we are unable to determine how, or if, information is cited in this dictionary\. The description of the app on Google Play cites contributors\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The app is described on Google Play as including ‘Third Person Singular Paradigms’ from Bella Alphonse, as well as information from the William Myers’ Dictionary Project \(3 year ongoing TNG project to record audio and make William’s dictionary searchable\), and Maria Myers \(paradigms with Dr\. E\.D\. Cook, audio from Royal BC Museum with Maria’s permission\)\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a mobile app published on Google Play\.

## Access

This app is free and open access, but is only available through Google Play\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Based on the images included on Google Play, this dictionary appears to be Chilcotin to English, but is searchable in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, digital, verb dictionary with paradigms\.
