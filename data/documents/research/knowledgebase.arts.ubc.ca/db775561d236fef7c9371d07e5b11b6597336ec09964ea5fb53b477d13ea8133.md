Salish – Qlipse \(Pend d’Oreille\) Online Dictionary

## Language Name

Salish\.

## Alternate Language Names

Kalispel\-Pend d’oreille, Kalispel\-Spokane\-Flathead, spoqín, Spokan, Montana Salish\.

## Region

Montana and Washington, United States\.

## Who

Native Teaching Aids \(Development\)\.

## Others Involved

Felicite McDonald, Eneas Vanderburg, Mary “Dolly” Linsebigler, Francis Stanger, Pat Pierre, Sophie Haines, Noel Pichette, Louie Adams, Eva Boyd, Frances Vanderburg, Harriet McDougal, Annie Vanderburg, Stephen Smallsalmon, Johnny Arlee, Shirley Trahan, Lucy Vanderburg, Tony Incashola, Andrew Woodcock, Chauncey Beaverhead, Stan Bluff, Francis Cullooyah \(Elders\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published by the Salish and Qlispe People\.

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch dates or update dates\.

## How People are Cited

Elders are cited by name on the *Snlciʔtn* tab\.

This dictionary does not cite any other contributors\.

## How Information is Cited

Information is cited on the *Snlciʔtn* tab under the *Bibliography* heading\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from Elders and previously published works, including: the 2001 *Language Vocabulary: Word\-Guide Book 1* and the 1998 *Common Names of the Flathead Language* by the Salish\-Pend d’Oreille Culture Committee, the 2011\-2016 *A Beginning Course in Salish &amp; Pend d’Oreille Dialect: Salish Language Curriculum* by Salish\-Pend d’Oreille People, and the 2010 *Seliš nyoʔnuntn: Medicine for the Salish Language\. English to Salish Translation Dictionary* by Tachini Pete\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a multimedia website, and includes images and audio\. It is also available as a mobile app\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Salish to English\. The search function works in both directions online, but only English to Salish on the mobile app\.

## Dialects Included

The website states: “We hope to include the language dialects of the Bitterroot Salish, Upper Kalispel, Lower Kalispel and Spokane tribes\. We want this to be an inclusive language tool for all to use and share”\.

However, dialects are not identified\.

## Type of Dictionary

The online resource is a bilingual, multimedia verb dictionary\. Other parts of speech are stored in the Media Files\.

The mobile app is a bilingual, mono\-directional, multimedia dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries on the online dictionary are organized alphabetically by Salish or English, depending on which language is selected while in the browse function\. Entries in the *Media Library* do not appear to have a specific organisation\.

Entries on the mobile app are organized alphabetically by English by category \(e\.g\. Action, Age, Animals, Audio and Video, Baby, Bathroom, etc\.\) and within the category\.

## Other Notes

The online dictionary seems to only contain verb entries\. Any other parts of speech are housed in the *Media Library*, which is not organized beyond language and form of item \(i\.e\. images and audio\)\. The multimedia entries are separated based on the form of item\.

The app is more clearly organized, housing all parts of speech together, sorted topically, containing both image and audio within the same file\. The app also contains stories and directs users to helpful information regarding physical games made available by the developer\.
