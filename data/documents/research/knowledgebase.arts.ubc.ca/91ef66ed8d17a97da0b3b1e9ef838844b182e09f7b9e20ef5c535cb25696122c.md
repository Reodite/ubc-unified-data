Ayajuthem Mobile App

## Language Name

ʔayajuθəm\.

## Alternate Language Names

Comox, Sliammon, Klahoose, Homalco, Ayajuthem\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

The four ʔayʔaǰuθəm speaking communities: ɬəʔamɛn, k̓ómoks, χʷɛmaɬkʷu, and ƛohos\. Mother Tongues Dictionaries\.

## Others Involved

Aidan Pine \(Developer\), Henry Davis\.

## Publishing Information

This app was published on Google Play in March 2018 by Aidan Pine \(of Mother Tongues Dictionaries\), and was last updated January 2019\. It is a work\-in\-progress and is part of a larger in progress dictionary project\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a mobile dictionary published on Google Play using Mother Tongues Dictionaries\.

## Access

This dictionary is free and open access, but can only be downloaded from Google Play\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

ʔayajuθəm to English\. The dictionary is searchable in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary includes language from all four ʔayajuθəm speaking communities: ɬəʔamɛn, k̓ómoks, χʷɛmaɬkʷu, and ƛohos\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, digital dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by ʔayajuθəm\. Based on the images available on Google Play, entries include a translation, example sentence, and audio\.
