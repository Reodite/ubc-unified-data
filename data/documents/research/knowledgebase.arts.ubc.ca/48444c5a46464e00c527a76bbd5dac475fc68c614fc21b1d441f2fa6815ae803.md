Quapaw \(Okáxpa\) Language Dictionary

## Language Name

Quapaw

## Alternate Language Names

Arkansas, Okáxpa

## Region

Arkansas and Oklahoma, United States\.

## Who

The Quapaw Nation Language Department\.

## Others Involved

As of mid\-2025, this dictionary is not yet published to know who is involved in creating this dictionary\. However, those listed on the Quapaw Nation Language Department’s website are as follows:

Anna Beaver, Alice Crawfish Gilmore, Fannie Goodeagle Richards, Louis Quapaw, Mary Lane Redeagle, Mary Wilson, Maude Supernaw, Odestine McWatters \(Speakers\); Robert Rankin \(Language Documentation\); Billy Supernaw Proctor \(Language Teacher\); Unnamed Language Recorders; Tehya Deardorff \(Web Development\)\.

## Publishing Information

As of mid\-2025, this dictionary is not yet published\. However, the dictionary page of the Quapaw Nation Language Department website was launched in 2024 and was most recently updated in 2025\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers are listed on the *Dictionary* page of the Quapaw Nation Language Department website under the heading *Audio Recordings*\. Other contributors to the project are listed on the *About* page below *Acknowledgements* and *Development Credits*\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers and previously documented and/or published materials are cited on the *Dictionary* page of the Quapaw Nation Language Department website below the headings *Written Works* and *Audio Recordings*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from audio recordings of speakers throughout the twentieth century and previously documented language works by linguists throughout the nineteenth, twentieth, and twenty\-first centuries\. These include works by George Izard, Lewis F\. Hadley, James Owen Dorsey, and Robert L\. Rankin\.
