Ulirnaisigutiit: an Inuktitut\-English Dictionary of Northern Quebec, Labrador, and Eastern Arctic Dialects \(with an English\-Inuktitut index\)

## Language Name

Inuktitut\.

## Alternate Language Names

Inuttitut, Inuktut\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Nunatsiavut \(Newfoundland and Labrador\), Nunavik \(Quebec\), Nunavut, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Lucien Schneider \(Allatausimajut\)\.

## Others Involved

Dermot Ronan F\. Collis \(Uiguitituurtunit qallunaatitutuulirtitausimajut\) \(Translator\); Department of Indian and Northern Affairs Canada, Les Presses de l’Université Laval \(Publisher\); Christine Nutaraaluk, Salome Mitiarjuk \(Uqausinginnik\); Aatami Ainalinmut, Nua Qupiqrualummut, Jiimi Quakkaimut, Siaja Nuvalinngamut, Litia Aanaqattamut, Aani Angutiggimut, Qumannguaq Tunirmut, Maata Qaukkaimut, Saami Metcalfemullu \(Aarqigiartausimajut\); Leah Qumaluk \(Povungnituk\) \(Cover Credit\)\.

Note: the roles listed in the *front matter* which is available in the Google Books preview are only provided in Inuktitut\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1985 by Les Presses de l’Université Laval, Québec\.

The author \(Lucien Schneider\) is cited on the cover\. Others involved are cited on the first two pages of the dictionary\.
