Bodega Miwok Dictionary

## Language Name

Coast Miwok\.

## Alternate Language Names

Bodega Miwok, Marin Miwok\.

## Region

California, United States\.

## Who

Catherine A\. Callaghan\.

## Others Involved

Unnamed contributor \(Speaker\); Dorothy Jones, Judith Ross \(Typing\); Mary R\. Haas \(Support\); the Survey of California Indian Languages of the Department of Linguistics of the University of California, the American Association of University Women \(Funding\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1970 by University of California Press as part of the University of California Publications in Linguistics, Vol\. 60\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited on the title page and in the *Introduction*\.

## How Information is Cited

The speaker is cited in the *Introduction*\. However, they are not identified by name or community\. Previously published works and fieldwork notes are also cited in the *Introduction*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary mainly comes from the speaker who worked with the author during her fieldwork in 1960 and 1964\. Forms from neighbouring Indigenous languages are from previously published works and field notes by other researchers, as listed in the *Introduction*\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

## Access

This dictionary is accessible through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Coast Miwok to English; English to Coast Miwok\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary includes information from Bodega Miwok, which is considered a dialect of Coast Miwok\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organised alphabetically by Coast Miwok \(according to the alphabet listed on p\. 7\) in the first section, *Bodega Miwok–English*\. Entries include the Coast Miwok headword \(or affix\), the part of speech, and the English definition \(or a description of the word where a one\-to\-one definition is not possible\)\. Many entries also include example words or phrases in both Coast Miwok and English and related words\. Verb entries contain information to do with conjugation and tense\. Entries are organised alphabetically by English in the *English–Bodega Miwok* section, which is to be used as an index of the first section\. Therefore, entries only include the English headword and the Coast Miwok translation\(s\)\.

There is a brief *Grammatical Synopsis* prior to the dictionary sections which explains the types of morphemes used in Coast Miwok, and information on nouns, verbs, and sentence structure\. Just prior to this, there is a description of the sound system\.
