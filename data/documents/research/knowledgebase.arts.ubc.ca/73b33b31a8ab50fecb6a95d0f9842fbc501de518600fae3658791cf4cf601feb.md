Everyday Tlingit

## Language Name

Tlingit\.

## Alternate Language Names

Lingít\.

## Region

British Columbia and Yukon, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Sealaska Heritage Institute \(Publisher\)\.

## Others Involved

X̱ʼunei Lance Twitchell, Daaljíni Mary Folletti, Koolyéik Roby Littlefield, Daal At\.ja Casey Moats, Goldbelt Heritage Foundation, Skíl Jáadei Linda Schrack, Dag Júus Robert Yates, Nang Jáadaas Gudangáay Ḵ’íinganggang Susie Edwardson, Kugíin\-g Dúu Lauryn Framke, Shu Guyna Donna May Roberts, Shiggoap Alfie Price \(SHI Staff and Contributors\); Kelsey Mata Foote \(Illustrator\); Mckenzie Hyde \(Designer\)\.

## Access

This dictionary is currently inaccessible outside of the language communities\. According to the SHI press release detailing this dictionary’s publication, SHI mailed this dictionary to schools and community members in Anchorage, Juneau, Sitka, and Wrangell\. As of mid 2024, this dictionary is currently unavailable for purchase\. However, SHI hopes to further their distribution and release future editions with more entries\.

## How are Entries Organised

We are unable to access this dictionary to determine how entries are organized\. However, according to SHI’s press release announcing its publication, Tlingit words are “superimposed over everyday scenes, including the bedroom, bathroom, laundry room, clothes, kitchen, food and drinks, living room, mudroom, shoreline, underwater, forest, river, plants, insects, camping and weather\. They also teach body parts, colors, shapes and numbers\. Each volume includes a glossary with English translations\.”
