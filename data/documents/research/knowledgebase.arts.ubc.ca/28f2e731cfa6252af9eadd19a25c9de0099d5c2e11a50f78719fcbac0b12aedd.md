Tanêks\-Tąyosą Kadakathi: Biloxi\-English Dictionary

## Language Name

Biloxi\.

## Alternate Language Names

We are not aware of any alternate language names\.

## Region

Mississippi, Louisiana, and Texas, United States\.

## Who

David Kaufman\.

Published 2011 by University of Kansas Libraries\.

A third edition of this dictionary, titled *Biloxi Dictionary*, was published in 2020 by Exploration Press\.

## How People are Cited

## How Information is Cited

The two main previously documented and published sources used in creating this dictionary are listed in the *Acknowledgements* and *Introduction*\. Speakers who contributed to these previously published sources are listed under the *Biloxi Language Consultants*\. Other previously published materials used are listed in the *References*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary is a revised version of the Biloxi portion of James Dorsey and John Swanton’s 1912 *A Dictionary of the Biloxi and Ofo Languages*\. It also includes the unpublished language documentation done by Albert Gatschet in 1886\. Other previously published materials used are listed in the *References*, including a grammar, several articles, and comparisons of several Indigenous languages\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as both a physical and digital book\.

## Access

The physical dictionary is accessible through select libraries\. The digital resource \(a PDF\) is open access through KU ScholarWorks\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Biloxi to English; English to Biloxi\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Biloxi in the *Biloxi\-English Dictionary* and contain the Biloxi headword, the part of speech, the English translation, and the entry’s source which includes the initials of the linguist/language documenter and the page number in the original source\. Entries with more than one English definition have the definitions listed as numbered subentries\. Some entries include cross references, cognate forms from related languages \(particularly Ofo and Tutelo\), example phrases or words from the original sources, the literal English translation, cultural notes, or gender notes \(i\.e\., designated male or female speech\)\. Certain entries are broken down into their compounds in brackets followed by the English gloss\.

Entries in the *English\-Biloxi Index* are listed alphabetically by English\. These entries include the English headword, the part of speech, and the Biloxi translation\(s\)\. No other information is included in these entries\.

Prior to the dictionary section, there is brief grammatical introduction that includes information about the Biloxi alphabet and sound system, stress, and the basics of Biloxi grammar\.

Following the dictionary there are eight appendices which include a list of affixes, numbers, days of the week, human body parts, flora and fauna, medicinal plants, mythological names, and kinship\. Most of these appendices are word lists, listed alphabetically by Biloxi, and entries include the Biloxi headword and the English translation\. Entries in the affix list also contain the part of speech and example words/terms\. Entries in the numbers list are listed by the number followed by the Biloxi equivalent\. Entries in the days of the week list also include the English gloss of the Biloxi term\. The human body appendix is a labelled diagram of a human body\. Flora and Fauna entries are organized topically, and then alphabetically by Biloxi below that\. Entries in the medicinal plants section also include information about each plant’s use\.
