## Other Notes

Most of the words in this dictionary appear in the *Tahltan Children’s Illustrated Dictionary* \(Carter and Tahltan Tribal Council 1994\)\.

There is another resource associated with Thorman titled *English and Tahltan Vocabulary*, it contains 11 pages of translations \(English to Tahltan\) and 4 pages of notes\.
