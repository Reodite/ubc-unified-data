Mutsun\-English English\-Mutsun Dictionary: mutsun\-inkiS inkiS\-mutsun riica pappel

## Language Name

Southern Costanoan\.

## Alternate Language Names

Southern Ohlone, Mutsun\.

## Region

California, United States\.

## Who

Natasha Warner, Lynnika Butler, and Quirina Geary\.

## Others Involved

This dictionary does not include information on contributors other than the three compilers, although it does cite Mrs\. Ascension Solarsano as the last fluent speaker and the source of much of the dictionary’s information\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2016, as a special publication of Language Documentation and Conservation, University of Hawai’i Press, Honolulu, Hawai’i\.

## How People are Cited

The three compilers are cited on the front cover\. The last fluent speaker—Mrs\. Ascension Solarsano—is cited in the *Introduction*\. No other contributors are cited\.

## How Information is Cited

The source of information is briefly explained in the *Introduction* to the dictionary\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from work with the last fluent speaker of Mutsun before 1930, and other native speakers included in the large written corpus of Mutsun\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a digital book\. It may also be available as physical book\.

## Access

This book is primarily available as an open access digital book through the Language Documentation and Conservation \(University of Hawai’i\)\. It may also be available as either a physical or digital book through a few libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Mutsun to English; English to Mutsun\. Both directions have similar information included in the entries\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in both halves of the dictionary are organized alphabetically by either English or Mutsun\.

Information on what is included in each entry can be found in *3\.1 What is in an English\-Mutsun Entry*, and *3\.2 What is in a Mutsun\-English Entry*\. All entries include a headword, translation, and part of speech\.
