Mvskoke Language Resources

## Language Name

Creek\-Seminole\.

## Alternate Language Names

Creek, Mvskoke\.

## Region

Oklahoma, Alabama, Georgia, and Florida, United States\.

## Who

Muscogee Creek Nation\.

## Others Involved

Staff involved in the Language Program include: Judy N\. Harjo\-Montiel \(Program manager\); Koko Lowe \(Clerical assistant\); Elizabeth “Eli” Rowland\-Chang \(Project manager\); Jordan Squire \(Project/Media coordinator\); Rebecca \(Byrd\) Barnett, Gracine Hicks, Carolyn McNac, Steven Powell, Phillip Harjo \(Language instructors\); Michael Cook \(Graphic designer\)\.

## Publishing Information

This is a website of languages resources from the Muscogee Nation that doesn’t display any information on launch dates\. It has regularly updated as of 2024\.

This resource is not a dictionary, but links to the *Muscogee \(Creek\) Dictionary* hosted on Webonary, as well as other language resources\.

## How People are Cited

Language program staff are cited in the *About Us* tab under the *Staff &amp; Program* subheading\. The names of each member of staff is listed along with a picture and short bio\.

## How Information is Cited

The sources of information for the language materials included on this website are not cited\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The sources of information for the language materials included on this website are not cited, but can be assumed to be compiled for use in the language program\.

## Tools and Framework used

This website is not a dictionary, but is a collection of Mvskoke language resources\. This is a multimedia website, as many of the resources include images and audio\.

## Access

This website is open access\. Many of the language materials can be downloaded\.

There is a section of the website that is members only\. It is not clear what information is included is this section of the website\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Mvskoke to English, English to Mvskoke\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for these language resources\.

## Type of Dictionary

This website is not a dictionary, but is a collection of bilingual Mvskoke language resources\. The site offers language classes, beginner, intermediate, and advanced learning materials, grammar tools, and vocabulary building tools\.

## How are Entries Organised

Each resource on this website is organised differently\. Links to the *Muscogee \(Creek\) Dictionary* on Webonary, grammar materials, and language activities can be found under the *Resources* tab\. More basic materials and pronunciation information can be found under the *Learn* tab\.
