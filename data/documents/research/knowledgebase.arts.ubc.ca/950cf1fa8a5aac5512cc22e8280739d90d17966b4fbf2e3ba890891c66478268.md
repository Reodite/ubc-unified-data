Stoney Language Dictionary

## Language Name

Stoney\.

## Alternate Language Names

Nakoda, Nakota, Isga, Lithga, Alberta Assiniboine\.

## Region

Alberta, Canada\.

## Who

Alexis Nakota Sioux Nation\.

## Others Involved

Unnamed Elders of Alexis Nakota Sioux Nation; Alexis Education\.

## Publishing Information

This app was developed by AIVIVA Inc\. According to the iTunes listing, it was launched in 2019 and, as of mid\-2025, most recently updated in 2023\. The Google Play listing states that it was released in 2019 and was most recently updated in mid\-2025\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not cite any authors or contributors by name\. Elders of Alexis Nakota Sioux Nation are thanked on the app’s opening screen and acknowledged in the listing on both iTunes and Google Play stores\. We are unable to access the full dictionary, so people may be cited by name elsewhere\.

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary does not cite the source of its information by name\. Elders of Alexis Nakota Sioux Nation are thanked on the app’s opening screen and acknowledged in the listing on both iTunes and Google Play stores\. We are unable to access the full dictionary, so language materials and speakers may be cited by name elsewhere\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary seems to come from Elders of the Alexis Nakota Sioux Nation\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a mobile app\.

## Access

The dictionary is available for free from both the iTunes and Google Play stores\. However, the full dictionary, including audio files, is only accessible to language community members and requires log in credentials to access the entire database\. It seems that a significant amount of the database is available without credentials\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Stoney; Stoney to English\. The search function operates in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

Information in this dictionary comes from Alexis Nakota Sioux Nation and includes the Alexis dialect of Stoney\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional mobile app dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by either Stoney or English, depending on the direction of the dictionary being used when looking at entries in the *Search Words* function\. Entries are organized topically in the *Browse by Category* section, and categories include first/second/third person, adjectives, animals, clothing, and many more\. Within categories, entries are listed alphabetically by either Stoney or English, depending on the direction of the dictionary in use\. Within both the *Search* and *Categories* organizational structures, users have the option to select the language direction \(i\.e\., Stoney to English or English to Stoney\)\.

Entries include the Stoney headword, an audio recording of the Stoney word \(only accessible with community\-approved log in credentials\), the English translation, and the categories\. Some entries include a picture\. Some verb entries include example sentences\.
