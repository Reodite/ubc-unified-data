Mandan Dictionary

## Language Name

Mandan\.

## Alternate Language Names

Nų́ų́ʔetaa íroo\.

## Region

North Dakota, United States\.

## Who

The Mandan, Hidatsa and Arikara Nation, and The Language Conservancy

## Others Involved

MHA Nation Tribal Education Department, Nueta Hidatsa Sahnish College, NHSC Native American Studies Department\.

Bernadine Young Bird, Valerian Three Irons, Albino Junior Garcia, Leon Little Owl, John P\. Boyle, Ryan Kasak, and Jonnia Torres\.

This website does not identify which members of the the Mandan, Hidatsa and Arikara Language Project \(Mandan\) worked to create the dictionary\.

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch dates\. The website shows as copyright 2015\. The posting date for each entry is displayed in the itself entry\.
