Kwakw’ala Dictionary: Kwak’wala to English and English to Kwak’wala

## Language Name

Kwak’wala\.

## Alternate Language Names

Kwakiutl\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Patricia A\. Shaw \(Editor\)\.

## Others Involved

This dictionary uses information from David Grubb’s 1977 publication *A Practical Writing System and Short Dictionary of Kwakw’ala \(Kwakiutl\)*\. However, we are unable to access the original resource to determine others involved\.

No other contributors are cited\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2006, First Nations Endangered Languages Program, UBC, Vancouver\.

This dictionary is published in two parts: Kwak’wala to English and English to Kwak’wala\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not cite any specific contributors\. Other contributors may be cited in the 1977 *A Practical Writing System and Short Dictionary of Kwakw’ala \(Kwakiutl\)*\.

## How Information is Cited

The previously published resource used in this dictionary is cited on the first page of the dictionary, along with the resource title and author\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary is based on the 1977 *A Practical Writing System and Short Dictionary of Kwakw’ala \(Kwakiutl\)* written by David Grubb\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

## Access

This dictionary is only accessible through the X̱wi7x̱wa Library branch of the UBC Library\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Kwak’wala to English, English to Kwak’wala\. The two sections are published as separate volumes\.

## Dialects Included

Dialects are not specified for most entries\. In cases where an entry is only relevant to a specific dialect, the dialect is cited in the entry\.

## Type of Dictionary

This dictionary is a bilingual, bidirectional, wordlist\.

## How are Entries Organised

The entries are organized alphabetically by Kwak’wala in the Kwak’wala to English dictionary, and by English in the English to Kwak’wala dictionary\.
