Takelma Dictionary

## Language Name

Takelma\.

## Alternate Language Names

Taakelmàʔn\.

## Region

## Who

## Tools and Framework used

This is a multimedia website which includes audio\.

## Access

Open Access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

The dictionary includes both English and Takelma\. The main dictionary search page has English headwords with Takelma translations\. When and entries is clicked an entry page opens up and is organized with Takelma as the main headword and English as the translation language\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, online, searchable dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries on the search page of the dictionary are organized by English headword followed by a Takelma translation\. When clicked the entry page opens\. The entry page first includes the Takelma word, followed by the English translation, a phonetic pronunciation, and an audio file\. At the bottom of the entry page there is a part of speech category for the word in both English and Takelma\.
