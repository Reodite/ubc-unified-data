Dictionnaire innu\-français and Dictionnaire français\-innu

## Language Name

Cree\-Montagnais\-Naskapi\.

## Alternate Language Names

Cree, Naskapi, Montagnais, ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐍᐏᐣ, Nēhiyawēwin, Innu\-aimun\.

## Region

Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, Newfoundland and Labrador, and Northwest Territories, Canada\.

The Innu\-aimun or Montagnais language is spoken in Labrador and Quebec\.

## Who

Marie\-Odile Junker, and Marguerite MacKenzie\.

## Others Involved

Jérémie Ambroise\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2016 as part of the Algonquian Dictionaries Project\. The *Dictionnaire innu\-français* and *Dictionnaire français\-innu* are published separately\. The *Dictionnaire innu\-français* is published as one volume, while the the *Dictionnaire français\-innu* is published in two\.

The Innu\-English Dictionary and English\-Innu Dictionary appear to be the English editions of these dictionaries\.
