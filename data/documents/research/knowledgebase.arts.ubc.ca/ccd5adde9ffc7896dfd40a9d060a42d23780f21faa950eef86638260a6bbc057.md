Chitimacha\-English Dictionary

## Language Name

Chitimacha\.

## Alternate Language Names

Sitimaxa\.

## Region

Louisiana, United States\.

## Who

Morris Swadesh\.

## Others Involved

John Reed Swanton, A\. S\. Gatschet\.

We are unable to access information about others who may have been involved in creation of this dictionary\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary is unpublished\. The information from this dictionary was collected between 1908 and 1931\. It may be held at the National Anthropological Archives\. As of the late 1990s the community has regained access to this resource and has developed a language course with Rosetta Stone \(Sitimaxa Rosetta Stone\) which has been distributed to tribal members\.
