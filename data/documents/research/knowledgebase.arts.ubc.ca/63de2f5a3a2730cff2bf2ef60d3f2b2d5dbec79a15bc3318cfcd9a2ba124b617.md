Southern Pomo Language – Intro

## Language Name

Southern Pomo\.

## Alternate Language Names

We are not aware of any alternative language names\.

## Region

California, United States\.

## Who

Miromaa Aboriginal Language and Technology Centre \([https://www\.miromaa\.org\.au/](https://www.miromaa.org.au/)\)\.

“Miromaa was founded in 2002 as the Arwarbukarl Cultural Resource Association Incorporated \(ACRA\)\. The original focus was to revive the Awabakal language and culture in Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and the Lower Hunter Valley\. Since 2008 the organisation has operated under the trading name Miromaa Aboriginal Language &amp; Technology Centre \(MALTC\)” \(Miromaa *About* page\)\. They are an organization which supports Indigenous language technologies\.

## Others Involved

California Indian Museum &amp; Cultural Center \([https://cimcc\.org/](https://cimcc.org/)\)\.

“The purpose of the California Indian Museum and Cultural Center is to culturally enrich and benefit the people of California and the general public\. The goals of the Museum and Cultural Center are to educate the public about California Indian history and cultures, to showcase California Indian cultures, to enhance and facilitate these cultures and traditions through educational and cultural activities, to preserve and protect California Indian cultural and intellectual properties, and to develop relationships with other indigenous groups\.” \(CIMCC *About* page\)
