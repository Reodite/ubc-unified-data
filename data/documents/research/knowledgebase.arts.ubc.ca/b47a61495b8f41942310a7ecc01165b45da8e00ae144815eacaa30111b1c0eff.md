Uummarmiut uqalungiha mumikhitchirutingit \= Basic Uummarmiut Eskimo Dictionary

## Language Name

Uummarmiutun\.

## Alternate Language Names

Uummaġmiutun, Canadian Iñupiaq, Iñupiaq, Inuvialuktun\. Uummarmiutun is considered a variant or dialect of Iñupiaq, Inuvialuktun or North Alaskan Iñupiaq\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Canada\.

## Who

Ronald Lowe\.

## Others Involved

Committee for Original Peoples Entitlement; Inuvialuktun Language Commission; The Department of Indian and Northern Affairs; Inuit Cultural Institute; Territorial Indigenous Languages Development Fund; Unnamed Speakers\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1984 by Committee for Original Peoples Entitlement, Inuvik, Canada\.

## How People are Cited

The author \(Ronald Lowe\) and Committee for Original Peoples Entitlement are cited on the cover of the dictionary\. Speakers are cited as “Unnamed Speakers”\.

## How Information is Cited

The information in this dictionary is cited under the *Author’s Note* and *Foreword*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers and previous works\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

## Access

The physical copy of this dictionary is accessible through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Iñupiaq to English\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary is of the Ummarmiutun dialect, spoken in the Mackenzie Delta community of Aklavik\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

The entries in this dictionary are organized topically by morpheme and alphabetically by English\.
