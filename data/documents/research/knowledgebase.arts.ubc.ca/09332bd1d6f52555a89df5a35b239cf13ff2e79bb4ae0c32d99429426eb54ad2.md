Potawatomi Dictionary \(Kansas Heritage Group\)

## Language Name

Potawatomi\.

## Alternate Language Names

Pottawatomie, Bodéwadmimwen, Bodéwadmi Zheshmowen, Neshnabémwen\.

## Region

Ontario, Canada; Michigan, Wisconsin, Oklahoma, Indiana, Kansas, United States\.

## Who

Kansas Heritage Group\.

## Others Involved

Jim McKinney, Martha Lewis, Lozenzo Mattawaoshe, Maynard Potts, Isaac ‘Ike’ Bennick, Ruby Shuckahosee, Billy Daniels, Mary Daniels \(Contributors\)\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch dates\. The last updated date \(July 2, 1997\) is displayed at the top of the page under the title\.

## How People are Cited

Sources for this dictionary should be cited on the main page under the *Sources* section\. Currently this window does not load correctly\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers for each entry are cited directly in the entry\. No other sources are cited\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary uses a simple text based website that can be scrolled\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Potawatomi, Potawatomi to English\. The directionality can be switched by selecting the language name under the title on the main page\.

## Dialects Included

Kansas Potawatomi\.

## Type of Dictionary

This dictionary is a bilingual wordlist\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by roman characters, with English to Potawatomi as the default\. The organization can be switched to Potawatomi to English by selecting the language name under the title on the main page\. The dictionary is sorted and is able to be navigated by the first letter of each entry\.
