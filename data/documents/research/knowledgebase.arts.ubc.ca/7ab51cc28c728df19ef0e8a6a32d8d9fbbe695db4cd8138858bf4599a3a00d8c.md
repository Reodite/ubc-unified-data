c̓išaaʔatḥ FirstVoices

## Language Name

Nuu\-chah\-nulth\.

## Alternate Language Names

Nootka, t’aat’aaqsapa, tahkaht, Westcoast\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

c̓išaaʔatḥ \(Tseshaht\) Nation\.

## Others Involved

This dictionary was built using FirstVoices, from the First People’s Cultural Council\. Others involved such as speakers and language recorders are cited in individual entries\.

## Publishing Information

As of early 2023, the most recent update to the dictionary was in May 2023\. There is no information on when this dictionary was launched\. This dictionary is published on the FirstVoices platform\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers are cited by name under the metadata tab for each entry, the information section of each audio file, and the *Acknowledgement* heading\. The person who recorded each audio file is also cited by name\.

## How Information is Cited

The sources of each entry \(which includes text information and language events\) is cited under the metadata tab\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers and written language materials, such as [www\.kwistuup\.net](https://kwistuup.net/) \(cited for each entry in the metadata tab\)\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a multimedia website and includes images and audio\. It is built using the FirstVoices platform\.  

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Nuu\-chah\-nulth to English\. The dictionary is fully searchable by English or Nuu\-chah\-nulth\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of the Barclay dialect\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, online word list\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Nuu\-chah\-nulth by default, and can be filtered by first letter\. When using the search feature, entries are organized by relevance\.

Entries can also be sorted by Categories or part of speech\.

Each entry includes the Nuu\-chah\-nulth entry, and the English translation\. Most entries also include an audio recording of the Nuu\-chah\-nulth entry\.
