## Other Notes

There are several other Lakota\-language resources available for purchase on the publisher’s website from the same author/fieldwork\. These include:

1\. *Animal Nation and Plant Nation – A Field Guide for Lakóta Children and for all those adults who still care about Creation* \(2007\), which offers scientific descriptions; spiritual and mythological background information; names of the animals and plants in Lakota and English, German, and Spanish; ecological and environmental information and context; and a small dictionary of the names of the animals and plants, which is bilingual \(Lakota and English\) and bidirectional\. It is available for purchase for €30 and comes with a CD\-ROM\.

2\. *Lakota for Classroom Sessions* \(2010\), which provides vocabulary and phrases typically used in the classroom\. It is organized semantically and intended to be used by teachers, volunteers, social workers, and healthcare workers to communicate with bilingual Lakota youth\. It is available for purchase for €15 and comes with a CD\-ROM\.

3\. *Lakóta Wóiyaksape – Lakota Proverbs* \(2012\), which contains proverbs and sayings from a range of historical Lakota figures\. These have been written in Lakota and translated into English, German, and Spanish\. It is available for purchase for €22\.50 and comes with a CD\-ROM\.

4\. *Lakota Lexicon* \(2008\), which is organized in two parts: *Part I: Nature: Flora, Fauna, Biotypes in the Lakota Language, English, German, and Spanish* and *Part II: Regional Studies: History, Figures, Present: Facts and Figures – Sioux Culture from A \-Z*\. Part I functions as a lexicon \(featuring language about the natural world\), and Part II is an encyclopedia of Lakota culture and history\. It is available for purchase for €40 and comes with a CD\-ROM\.

5\. *Treasure Trove of the Western Sioux: Myths, Fairy Tales, and Legends of the Lakota\. Creation Myths, Animal Tales, Legendary Places, and Deeds* \(2009\), which is a collection of Lakota myths and fairy tales\. It is available for purchase for €35 and comes with a CD\-ROM\.
