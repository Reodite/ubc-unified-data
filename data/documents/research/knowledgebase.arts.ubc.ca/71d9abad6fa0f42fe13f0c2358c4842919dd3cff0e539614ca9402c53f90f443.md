Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓ Words: An English\-to\-Hul̓q̓umn̓um̓ and Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓\-to\-English Dictionary

## Language Name

Halkomelem\.

## Alternate Language Names

Halq̓eméylem, Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓, hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada; Washington, United States\.

## Who

Donna B\. Gerdts \(Compiler\); Chemainus, Nanaimo, and Nanoose First Nations, School District No\. 68 \(Publishers\)\.

## Others Involved

Wildred Aleck, Anne Bob, Jim Bob, Leonard Edwards, May Frenchie, Hazel Good, Margaret James, Emily Manson, Dora Sampson, Steve Samson, Sr\., Bill Seward, Chester Thomas, Eva Thomas, Theresa Thorne, Anderson Tommy, Ellen White, George Wyse \(Elders\); Nanoose First Nation, Nanaimo First Nations Treaty Negotiations Office \(Support\); Barbara Barltrop, Jenny Bob, Reinouw Abbott \(Nanoose First Nation Project Assistance\); Brian Compton \(Ethnobiologist\); First People’s Cultural Foundation, Provice of British Columbia, Ministry of Education, Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada, Simon Fraser University \(Funding\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1997 by Chemainus First Nation, Nanaimo First Nation, Nanoose First Nation, and First Nations Education Serives, School District 68 \(Nanaimo\-Ladysmith\)\.

## How people are cited

People are cited in the dictionary’s front matter \(pg\. iii\-v\)\.

## How information is cited

Information is cited in the dictionary’s front matter \(pg\. iii\-v\) and the *References* page\.

## Where is information coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from speakers and previously published resources as listed on the *References* page\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book and digital book\.

## Access

The physical book is available through libraries\. The digital book is open access through Simon Fraser University\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓; Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓ to English\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓, the Island dialect of Halkomelem\. This dictionary contains language information of the Chemainus, Nanaimo, and Nanoose dialects of Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\. The first section of the dictionary is topical\.

## How are entries organised

This dictionary contains three sections: *Words by Semantic Category*, *Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓\-to\-English*, and *English\-to\-Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓*\.

Entries are organized topically in the *Words by Semantic Category*\. Categories include *Animals*, *Birds*, *the Body*, *Buildings*, and *Clothing and Personal Effects* \(which is further subdivided\)\. Entries include the English head word, the Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓ translation, and an English gloss\. Entries pertaining to biological information also include the Latin name and other biological information about the species of plant or animal\.

Entries are organized alphabetically by Hul’q’umi’num’ in the *Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓\-to\-English* section, and include the Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓ head word, the English translation, and any related information regarding word usage or borrowings\.

Entries are organized alphabetically by English in the *English\-to\-Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓* section, and include the English head word, the Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓ translation, and any related information regarding part of speech or a more detailed English gloss and synonyms\.
