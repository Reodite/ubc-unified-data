Natchez Dictionary

## Language Name

Natchez\.

## Alternate Language Names

We are not aware of any alternative language names\.

## Region

Louisiana, Mississippi, and Oklahoma, United States\.

## Who

Natchez Nation\.

## Others Involved

There is no information included in this resource about others involved\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary is published as a downloadable word document on the Nation’s website, and includes the date it was last updated\. As of early 2023, it was last updated January 26th 2020\. This dictionary does not display any information on launch dates\.

## How People are cited

The second page of the dictionary document states that information has been “taken from all available speakers and resources”\. No specific speakers or resources are cited\. Many entries include letter and number abbreviations which may document the speakers and sources the entries come from\. There is no guide to these abbreviations included\.
