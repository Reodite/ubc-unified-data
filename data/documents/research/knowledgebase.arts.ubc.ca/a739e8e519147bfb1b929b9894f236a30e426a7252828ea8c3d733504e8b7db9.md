Dene K’é̜é̜ T’ahsíi Ts’uuzi Gha Edi̜htł’éh Nadaatł’éh – South Slavey Topical Dictionary, Second Edition

## Language Name

Slavey\.

## Alternate Language Names

Sahtúot’ı̨nę Yatı̨́, Slavi, Slave, Dené, Mackenzian\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Canada

## Who

Teaching and Learning Centre; Dehcho Divisional Board of Education\.

## Others Involved

Leo Norwegian; Henry Deneyoua; Alfred Thomas; Johnny Tonka; Drank Denethlon; Vic Monus; Philip Howard\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1993\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited in the *Acknowledgements*\.

## How Information is Cited

We are unable to access this dictionary to determine how materials are cited\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this resource comes from speakers and the previous edition of this dictionary\.

## Tools and Framework used

According to the Dehcho Divisional Education Council website, this dictionary is available as a physical book\. As of early 2024, there will soon be an affiliated interactive PDF, which will include sound files for each word\.

## Access

This dictionary is available for purchase for $25 through Dehcho Divisional Education Council\. As of early 2024, according to the Dehcho Divisional Education Council website, the interactive PDF and online publishing are not yet available\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Slavey\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary includes information from the South Slavey dialect\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, topical dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized by topic\. We are unable to access this dictionary to determine what specific topics are included\. Entries are organized alphabetically by English within each topic\.
