Tunica\-English Dictionary App

## Language Name

Tunica\.

## Alternate Language Names

Luchi Yoroni, Tonica, Yuron\.

## Region

Louisiana, United States\.

## Who

This app was developed by the Tunica\-Biloxi Working Group \(KYLY – Kuhpani Yoyani Luhchi Yoroni\) and the Tunica\-Biloxi Tribe of Louisiana Language and Culture Revitalization Program \(LCRP\)\.

## Others Involved

Without downloading the app we are unable to access information about others who may have been involved in creation of this dictionary\.

## Publishing Information

This app was published on Google Play in September 2019, and was last updated December 2021\. It was published by the Tunica\-Biloxi Tribe of Louisiana Language and Culture Revitalization Program\.

## Access

This app is open access, and can be downloaded for free from Google Play\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Tunica\. It is searchable in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mobile dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Based on the images includes on Google Play, entries are organized alphabetically by English\. They appear to include grammatical information\.
