Cree Dictionary and Everyday Phrases &amp; Words

## Language Name

Cree\-Montagnais\-Naskapi\.

## Alternate Language Names

Cree, Naskapi, Montagnais, ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐍᐏᐣ, Nēhiyawēwin\.

## Region

Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, Newfoundland and Labrador, and Northwest Territories, Canada\.

## Who

Ochékiwi Sípí Cree Nation\.

## Others Involved

We are unable to access information about others involved in the creation of this dictionary\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary was originally published in 2005 by the Manitoba First Nations Education Resource Centre as two dictionaries which can be purchased together as a set: The *Ochékiwi Sípí Cree Dictionary*, and the *Cree Phrases and Everyday Words\.* An online version seems to have been created around the same time, available at the url https://fisherrivercree\.manitobafirstnations\.ca/\.

## Where is Information Coming from

According to the description by the publisher, this dictionary was written by the last remaining fluent Cree Elders of Ochekiwi Sipi Cree Nation\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a set of two print books\. The set was digitized at an unknown date and is available as a multimedia website, search filters and audio files\.

## Access

The print dictionary is available through libraries and for purchase from the publisher for $19\.95 CAD\. The digital dictionary is open access online\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Cree\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of Swampy Cree \(Maskékomowina\)\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, monodirectional wordlist and phrasebook\. The digital dictionary is a bilingual, monodirectional multimedia website\.

## How are Entries Organised

We are unable to access the print set to determine how entries are organised\. According to the description from the publisher, each entry in the Dictionary contains the English headword followed by the Cree word in Roman orthography, phonetics, and Cree syllabics\.
