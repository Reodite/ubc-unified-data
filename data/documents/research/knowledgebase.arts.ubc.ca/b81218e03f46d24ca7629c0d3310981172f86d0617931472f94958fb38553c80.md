A practical writing system and short dictionary of Kwakw’ala \(Kwakiutl\)

## Language Name

Kwak’wala\.

## Alternate Language Names

Kwakiutl\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

David McClintock Grubb\.

## Others Involved

Agnes Alfred, Dora Cook, Agnes Cranmner, Barbara Hunt, Emma Hunt, Helen Hunt, Henry Hunt, Stanley Hunt, Thomas Hunt, James C\. King, Daisy Moon, Daisy Neel, Annie Nelson, Billie Robertson, Gertrude Robinson, Catherine Scow, Flora Sewid, James A\. Sewid, Peter Smith, Ed Walkus, Billy Sunday Willie, Dan Willie, Robert Willie, and Alexandra Willie\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1977, National Museum of Canada, Ottawa\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited in the *Acknowledgements* section\.

## How Information is Cited

We are unable to access this dictionary to determine how, or where source materials are cited\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes directly from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework Used

This dictionary is available as a physical and digital book\.

## Access

The physical edition of this dictionary is accessible through libraries\. The digital book is available to purchase through the University of Ottawa Press for $11\.99 CAD, as of 2023\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Kwak’wala to English, English to Kwak’wala\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of the Kwagulh dialect\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional grammar, which includes a word list\.

## How are Entries Organised

The entries are organised alphabetically by Kwak’wala or English, depending on the dictionary section\. Additional appendices are included\.
