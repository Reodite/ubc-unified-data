Halq’eméylem FirstVoices

## Language Name

Halkomelem\.

## Alternate Language Names

Halq̓eméylem, Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓, hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada; Washington, United States\.

## Who

Stó:lō First Nation\.

## Others Involved

This dictionary was built using FirstVoices, from the First People’s Cultural Council\. Others involved such as speakers and language recorders, are cited in individual entries\.

## Publishing Information

As of late 2023, the most recent update to the dictionary was in July 2023\. There is no information on when this dictionary was launched\. This dictionary is published on the FirstVoices platform\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers are cited by name under the metadata tab for each entry and in the information section of each audio file\. The names of the individuals who recorded each entry are also included in the information section of each audio file\.

## How Information is Cited

The source of each entry is cited under the metadata tab\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers, who are cited in the metadata tab\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a multimedia website, and includes images and audio\. It is built using the FirstVoices platform\. The dictionary is also available as a mobile app developed by Mother Tongues Dictionaries and FirstVoices\. The app is available on both Google Play and the Apple App store\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Halkomelem to English\. The search function works in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of the Upriver dialect of Halkomelem, specifically the the dialect of the Stó:lō people\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, multimedia, online dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Halkomelem by default, and can be filtered by first letter\. When using the search feature, entries are organized by relevance\.

Entries can also be sorted by categories or part of speech\.

Each entry includes the Halkomelem entry, and the English translation\. Most entries also include an audio recording of the Halkomelem entry\.
