Gitsenimx̱ FirstVoices

## Language Name

Gitxsan\.

## Alternate Language Names

Gitksan, Gitxsanimaax, Gitikshan, Gityskyan, Giklsan, Gitsenimx\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Art Mathews \(Language Coordinator\); Wanda Good \(Gitxsen FirstVoices Language Administrator\)

## Others Involved

This dictionary was built using FirstVoices, from the First People’s Cultural Council\. Others involved, such as speakers and language recorders, are cited in individual entries\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch dates\. As of 2023, it is still actively updated\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers are cited by name in the information section of each audio file\. The person who recorded each audio file is also cited by name\.

## How Information is Cited

The sources of each entry is cited under the metadata tab\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers \(cited in the metadata tab of each entry\)\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a multimedia website, as it includes both images and audio\. It is published on the FirstVoices platform\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Gitxsan to English\. The search function works in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary is of the Gyeets dialect\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, online wordlist\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Gitxsan by default, and can be filtered by first letter\. When using the search feature, entries are organized by relevance\.

Entries can also be sorted by categories or part of speech\.

Each entry includes the Gitxsan entry, and the English translation\. Most entries also include an audio recording of the Gitxsan entry\.
