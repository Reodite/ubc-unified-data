Na\-Cho Nyak Dun Northern Tutchone Dictionary

## Language Name

Northern Tutchone\.

## Alternate Language Names

Dän kʼí\.

## Region

Yukon, Canada\.

## Who

John Ritter \(Author\)\.

## Others Involved

The *Introduction* briefly mentions that there are others involved, but does not cite any other contributors by name\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch or update dates\. Both the PDF and the website do not include any information on publication year\.

## How People are Cited

The *Introduction* is written by John Ritter, and he is cited at the end\. No other contributors are cited\.

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary does not cite the source of any of its information\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary does not indicate where information is coming from or what sources were used\. This dictionary follows the same structure as the *Mayo Indian Language: Noun Dictionary* \(also by Ritter\), and is likely a version of the same dictionary\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a website with an embedded PDF\. The PDF can be downloaded as a digital book\.

The website, which is called *docplayer\.net* is a platform used to uploaded documents online\. It includes ads, and no background on the language community or dictionary\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Northern Tutchone\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of the Mayo dialect of Northern Tutchone\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional wordlist\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organsied topically\. Each entry includes the English headword followed by the Northern Tutchone translation\. No other information is included\.
