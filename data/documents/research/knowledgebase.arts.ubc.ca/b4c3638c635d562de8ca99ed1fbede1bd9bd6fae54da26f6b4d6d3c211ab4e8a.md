Mi’kmaw Language

## Language Name

Mi’kmaq\.

## Alternate Language Names

Mi’kmawi’simk, Micmac, Migmaw, Mikmaw, Míkmaq, Míkmaw, Mìgmao, Lnuismk, L’nui’suti\.

## Region

Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, and Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada; Maine and Massachusetts, United States\.

## Who

Aboriginal Language Initiative, AFNTS \(Atlantic First Nation Tech Services\)\.
