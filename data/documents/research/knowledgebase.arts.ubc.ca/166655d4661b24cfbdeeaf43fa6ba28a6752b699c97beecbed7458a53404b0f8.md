Gwich’in Alpha

## Language Name

Gwich’in\.

## Alternate Language Names

Gwitch’in, Kutchin, Kootchin, Loucheux, Loucheaux, Takudh, Tukudh\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Yukon, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Margaret Erasmus, Rosaline Cayen \(Project coordinators\); Yamózha Kúé Society \(Developer\)\.

## Others Involved

Rosa Mantla, Andy Norwegian of Tlicho Community Services Agency, Jessie Campbell of Dehcho Divisional Education Council, William Firth of Sahtu Divisional Education Council, Eileen Beaver of Gwich’in Teaching and Learning Centre, Mary Rose Sundberg of Paul William Kaeser High School; Gladys Norwegian of Goyatiko Language Society \(Contributing Dene language specialists\)\.

## Publishing Information

This app was first launched on October 24th 2014 and was last updated on January 7th 2016\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited in the *Credits* section of the app\.

## How Information is Cited

The source of information for this app is cited under *Credits*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information for this app comes from speakers and Elders\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is in the form of an app\. It can be downloaded from the App Store and Google Play, and it is available on all Apple and Android devices\.

## Access

This app is free to download on Google Play and the App Store\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Gwich’in\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional topical app\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized by topical module under the *Language* button; there are 23 modules\. Some of the topical modules include *Numbers, Time, Hunting* and *Trapping*\. The entries can also be searched in English with the *Search* button\. The entries are presented in English until they are pressed on, then they are spoken in Gwich’in and the translation appears on the gray line on the screen\. In the list of entries under the *Learn* tab in each module are three buttons: one red with a microphone on the left \(to record your own voice\), a green play button in the middle \(does not seem to work\), and a blue button with the replay symbol on the right \(repeats the entry that was just heard\)\.

Each topical module also includes *Games* and *Quizzes* \(besides the *Learn* feature\)\. The user can choose from 3 difficulty levels \(*Easy, Medium* and *Hard*\) for the *Games* and 3 different categories for the *Quizzes* \(*Listening, Speaking* and *Reading*\)\.
