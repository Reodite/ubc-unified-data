Online Gitksan Dictionary

## Language Name

Gitxsan\.

## Alternate Language Names

Gitksan, Gitxsanimaax, Gitikshan, Gityskyan, Giklsan\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Gitksan Research Lab\.

## Others Involved

Aidan Pine \(Mother Tongues Developer\); University of British Columbia \(Resources\); Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council \(Funding\); Ken Mowatt \(Artist\)\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary published with Mother Tongues that doesn’t display any information on launch dates or recent updates\.

## How People are Cited

Project contributors are cited on the *About* page of the website\. Speakers are identified by their initials within individual entries\.

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary does not cite the source of any of its information\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary does not indicate where information is coming from or what sources were used\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a multimedia website, as it includes both images and audio\. It is also available as a mobile app, which is available on the Google Play store\. Both formats are published through Mother Tongues\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Gitxsan to English\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, online wordlist\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Gitxsan by default, and can be filtered by first letter\.

Each entry includes the Gitxsan head word, and the English translation\. Most entries also include an audio recording of the Gitxsan headword\.

The website has a “Random” function where ten random words and their definitions are pulled up\. Users can also bookmark entries\.
