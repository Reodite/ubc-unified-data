The Official Dakotah Scrabble Dictionary

## Language Name

Dakota\.

## Alternate Language Names

Dakhota, Dakhótiyapi, Dakȟótiyapi, Dakotah\.

## Region

North Dakota, South Dakota, Nebraska, Minnesota, and Montana, United States; Manitoba and Saskatchewan, Canada\.

## Who

Tammy DeCoteau \(Editor, Text Editing, and Layout\)\.

## Others Involved

Association on American Indian Affairs Native Language Program \(Creator\); Hasbro, Inc\. \(Game Trademark\)\.

## Access

This dictionary is accessible at one WorldCat affiliated library\. It is also available for purchase from the Sisseton\-Wahpeton Oyate Dakotah Language Institute’a website for $20 USD or for free when purchasing the *Dakotah Scrabble* game\. ## Other Notes

The Sisseton\-Wahpeton Oyate Dakotah Language Institute focuses on creating language learning materials for their community\. These materials include dictionaries/word lists, children’s books, flash cards, and posters\. These can be found on their website in the *Mazopiya \(Shop\)* sections\.

This dictionary was created to accompany the *Dakotah Scrabble* game which used to be available for purchase on the Sisseton\-Wahpeton Oyate Dakotah Language Institute’s website\. As of mid\-2025, the game is no longer available on the website and may not be accessible\. However, the dictionary seems to remain available\.
