A Brief Word List of Eastern Pomo

## Language Name

Eastern Pomo\.

## Alternate Language Names

Clear Lake Pomo\.

## Region

California, United States\.

## Who

Sally McLendon\.

## Others Involved

Leonard Bateman, Maude Bateman Boggs, Frances Posh Dennison, William Graves, Ralph Holder \(language consultants\); Elsa Lattey \(manuscript typing\); Patricia Dodge, Henry Mauldin \(research assistance\)\.

## Publishing Information

This is an unpublished manuscript from 1975\. It is hosted digitally on the California Indian Museum and Cultural Center website\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers and other contributors are cited by name on page 3 of the manuscript\.

## How Information is Cited

Some brief background information and the names of speakers who contributed to this manuscript are included on page 3 of the manuscript\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Entries in this dictionary come directly from Sally McLendon’s fieldwork with speakers over multiple summers \(prior to 1975\)\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a digital book \(PDF\)\. It is a scan of a physical resource\.

## Access

This wordlist is open access online\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Eastern Pomo to English, English to Eastern Pomo\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this wordlist\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional wordlist\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in the Eastern Pomo\-English section are organised alphabetically by Eastern Pomo headword, followed by its English translation\. Entries in the English\-Eastern Pomo section are organised alphabetically by English word or phrase, with its Eastern Pomo equivalent\.
