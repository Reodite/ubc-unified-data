A Vocabulary of the Powhatan: with Two Word\-lists of Pamunkey and Nansemond from other sources

## Language Name

Powhatan\.

## Alternate Language Names

Virginia Alqonquian\.

## Region

Maryland, North Carolina, and Virginia, Untied States\.

## Who

John Smith \(Author\), Frederic W\. Gleach \(Compiler and Editor\)\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Entries in this dictionary come primarily from a dictionary compiled by John Smith in the 1620s, with supplementary materials from an 1858 publication by Edwin A\. Dalrymple and a 1907 publication by James Mooney\. Speakers who would have worked with these authors are not cited\.
