Lingít Dictionary: Northern Dialect

## Language Name

Tlingit\.

## Alternate Language Names

Lingít\.

## Region

British Columbia, Yukon, Canada; Alaska, United States

## Who

Lance A\. Twitchell\.

## Others Involved

University of Alaska Southeast; Goldbert Heritage Foundation; Troubled Raven Productions \(Publisher\)\. However, we are unable to access the dictionary to determine if there is anyone else involved\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2005 by Troubled Raven Productions, Fairbanks, Alaska\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited in the *Introduction* and beside entries when available\.

## How Information is Cited

The information in this dictionary is cited in the *Introduction* and beside entries when available\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from fluent speakers and past works\. However, we are unable to access this dictionary to determine what specific sources were used\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

## Access

The physical copy of this dictionary is accessible through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Tlingit to English, English to Tlingit\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary is of the Northern dialect\.
