Ho\-Chunk Dictionary Online

## Language Name

Ho\-Chunk\.

## Alternate Language Names

Winnebago, Hoocąk, Hocąk\.

## Region

Wisconsin, Nebraska, Iowa, South Dakota, Illinois, and Minnesota, United States\.

## Who

Ho\-Chunk Nation of Wisconsin, and The Language Conservancy\.

## Others Involved

The Ho\-Chunk Nation and the Tribal Council, Adrienne Thunder and Henning Garvin\.

**Speakers:** Allen Cloud, Andy Thundercloud, Ashley Rave, Brandi Redbird, Chloris Lowe III, Chloris Lowe Sr\., Connie Lonetree, Elliott, Francis Decorah, Georgia Lonetree, Gordon Thunder, Janice Rice, Jim Greendeer, Joyce Warner, Levi Winneshiek, Lilian Thundercloud, Parmenton Decorah, Shane Yellowthunder, Wilbert Cleveland, and Wilfrid Cleveland\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is an online multimedia dictionary which includes audio\. It was built by the Language Conservancy\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Ho\-Chunky to English, English to Ho\-Chunk\. The dictionary is searchable in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary likely includes information from the Hocąk \(Wisconsin\) dialect of Ho\-Chunk\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is bilingual, bidirectional, online dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by either Ho\-Chunk or English depending on the search direction\. Each entry includes the headword, translation, audio, and the part of speech\.
