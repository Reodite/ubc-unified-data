Tolowa Dee\-ni’ Nation Dictionary

## Language Name

Tolowa\.

## Alternate Language Names

Checto\-Tolowa, Siletz Deen\-ni, Tolowa\-Chetco\.

## Region

Oregon, United States\.

## Who

Wee\-ya’ \(Language\) Program\.

## Others Involved

Loren Me’\-lash\-ne Bommelyn, Nicol Nik\-la Aubrey, Pyuwa Bommelyn, Mendy Hinshaw, Andromeda Na’lh\-nii\-tr’e’\-sdvm Lopez \(Wee\-ya’ Committee\)\.

There is no other information included in this resource about others involved\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Tolowa to English\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Tolowa\. Entries include the Tolowa headword, the part of speech, the English translation, and any variations\. The headwords are hyperlinked, allowing users to click on them\. However, this does not provide users with more information than what can be seen in the overall list\. Some entries offer a hyperlink to semantic domains\. However, this does not provide more information nor does it link to other words from the semantic domain\.

The dictionary’s search function can be done in English or Tolowa, based on language and part of speech\.
