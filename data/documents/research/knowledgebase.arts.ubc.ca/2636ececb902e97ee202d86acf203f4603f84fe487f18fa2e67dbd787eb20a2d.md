Upper Kuskokwim Athapaskan Dictionary

## Language Name

Upper Kuskokwim\.

## Alternate Language Names

Kolchan, Goltsan\.

## Region

Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Raymond Collins, Sally Jo Collins \(Compilers\)\.

## Others Involved

The main language informants for this dictionary are Willie Petruska, Junior Gregory, Bobby Esai, Philip Esai, and Nick Dennis from Nikolai\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1966 by the Summer Institute of Linguistics, Fairbanks, Alaska\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers are cited in the *Introduction*\. The compilers are cited on the front page\.

## How Information is Cited

Information is cited in the *Introduction*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information comes from the speakers Willie Petruska, Junior Gregory, Bobby Esai, Philip Esai, and Nick Dennis from Nikolai\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical and digital book\.

## Access

The physical book is accesible through libraries, while the digital book is open access as PDF through Alaska Native Language Archive\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Upper Kuskokwim\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of Upper Kuskokwi Athapaskan spoken by the people of Nikolai and Telida Alaska\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional, topical noun dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized topically, with English headwords followed by Upper Kuskokwim translations\. Within each section entries are organized alphabetically by English\.

A Upper Kuskokwim to page number *Index* is included at the end of the dictionary\.

## Other Notes

This dictionary was primarily intended and designed for native speakers of Upper Kuskokwim\.

Many of the nouns in this dictionary are included in the 1979 *Dinak’i \(Our Words\): Upper Kuskokwim Athabaskan Junior Dictionary*\.
