Spokane Dictionary

## Language Name

Salish\.

## Alternate Language Names

Séliš, Salish\-Spokane\-Kalispel, Kalispel\-Pend d’oreille, Kalispel\-Spokane\-Flathead, spoqín, Montana Salish\.

## Region

Montana and Washington, United States\.

## Who

Barry Carlson \(Compiler\); Pauline Flett \(Speaker and Compiler\)\.

## Others Involved

Margaret Sherwood, Salina Pascal, Albert Sam, Antoine Andrews, Nancy Flett, Lucy Peuse \(Speakers\); Alice Abrahamson, John B\. Flett, Agnes Wynn, Mary Moses, Sadie Boyd, Catherine Andrews, Gibson Moses, Smokey Abrahamson, Antoine Peuse \(Contributors\); Bob May, Nancy Turner \(Ethnobotanical Data\); Chief Alex Sherwood \(Cultural Liason\); Anthony Mattina, Robert Hsu, Timothy Montler, Deirdre Black, Larry Eby, the University of Montana \(Technical Assistance and Support\); the University of Victoria, the National Science Foundation, the Canada Council, the Jacobs Funds of the Whatcom Museum, the Spokane Tribe of Indians, the Alex Sherwood\-Mary Owhi Moses Language Trust \(Funding\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1989 by UMOPL \(University of Montana Occasional Papers in Linguistics\), No\. 6, Missoula\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited on the title page, in the *Acknowledgements*, and *Introduction*\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers are cited by name on the title page, in the *Acknowledgements*, and *Introduction*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from speakers, based on fieldwork from the compilers’\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

## Access

This dictionary is accessible through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Salish to English; English to Salish\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary includes information from the Spokane and Kalispel dialects\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional root dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Salish in the *Spokane\-English* section according to the alphabet on p\. iv\. The headwords in this dictionary are roots and lexical suffixes\. Entries include headword, its English translation\(s\), and derivational forms \(written as grammatical analysis\) in Salish followed by the English translation \(often translated as a sentence in English\)\. Variations in spelling of the headword are included in parentheses directly after the headword\.

Entries in the *English\-Salish* section are organized alphabetically by English\. Entries include the English headword, the Salish root equivalent \(if applicable\), the Salish translation \(often requiring a root and several affixes\), derivational forms or similar words or phrases in English followed by the Salish translation\.
