Hlg̱aagilda X̱aayda Kil FirstVoices

## Language Name

Haida\.

## Alternate Language Names

X̱aat Kíl, X̱aadas Kíl, X̱aayda Kil, Xaad kil\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada; Alaska, Untied States\.

## Who

The Hlg̱aagilda X̱aayda Kil Language Team, and the Skidegate Haida Language House\.

## Others Involved

FirstVoices; First Peoples’ Cultural Council \(FPCC\)\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary is available on www\.firstvoices\.com\. As of early 2023, the most recent update to the dictionary was in May 2022\. There is no information on when this dictionary was launched\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers are cited by name under the *metadata* tab for each entry and in the information section of each audio file\. The person who recorded each audio file is also cited by name\.

## How Information is Cited

The sources of each entry \(which includes text information and language events\) is cited under the metadata tab\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary come from speakers and written language materials \(cited for each entry in the *metadata* tab\)\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is an online multimedia website which includes audio and images\. It is searchable by both English and Haida\. The dictionary is also available as a mobile app developed by Mother Tongues Dictionaries and FirstVoices\. The app is available on both Google Play and the Apple App store\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Haida to English\. It can be searched by both Haida and English\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of Alaskan Haida\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Haida by default, and can be filtered by first letter\. When using the search feature, entries are organized by relevance\.

Entries can also be sorted by *Categories* or *part of speech*\.
