Deg Hit’an–Holikachuk Placenames

## Language Name

Deg Xinag and Holikachuk\.

## Alternate Language Names

Deg Xinag: Deg Hit’an, Kaiyukhotana, Ingalik\.

Holikachuk: Innoko, Innoko\-khotana, Tiëgon\-khotana\.

## Region

Alaska, United States\.

## Who

James Kari\.

## Others Involved

There is no information included in this resource about others involved\.

## Publishing Information

This resource appears to be a compilation of multiple placename lists and maps, and includes repeats of lists at different stages of revision\. Alaska Native Language Archive dates it as 1978, although some sections include more recent resources\. It was revised in 2001\.

The *Deg Hit’an–Holikachuk Place Names* from 2001, begins on page 12 of the PDF and goes to page 22\. Older versions of the same word list are included in other parts of the PDF\.

There is no distinction between Deg Xinag and Holikachuk in the resource, so it is unclear if it includes both languages\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not any contributors other than the author\. James Kari is named at the beginning of the pages with versions of the *Deg Hit’an–Holikachuk Place Names\.*

## Included Languages and Directionality

Deg Xinag or Holikachuk \(the language is not clear\) to English\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary does not specify which dialects are included, or clarify if it includes both Deg Xinag and Holikachuk\. The top of the *Deg Hit’an–Holikachuk Place Names* notes that it excludes Kuskokwim \(a dialect of Deg Xinag\)\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional, placename dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

The entries have either Deg Xinag or Holikachuk \(the language is not clear\) headwords, followed by the English translation\. They do not appear to be organized in any way, other than that they are grouped by area\.
