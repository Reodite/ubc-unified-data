Croatoan Word List

## Language Name

Carolina Algonquian\.

## Alternate Language Names

Pamlico, Croatoan, Lumbee\.

## Region

North Carolina, United States\.

## Who

Scott Dawson\.

## Others Involved

Blair A\. Rudes \(Extended Definitions\); Coastal Carolina Indian Center \(Site Host\)\.

## Publishing Information

This word list was published as an article in the Blair A\. Rudes Indigenous Language Collection in December of 2011\.

## How People are Cited

Dawson and Rudes are cited in the article title and introduction\.

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary does not cite the source of any of its information\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary does not indicate where information is coming from or what sources were used\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a web\-hosted word list\.

## Access

This dictionary is available open access online\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Carolina Algonquian to English\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional word list\.

## How are Entries Organised

This short word list is organised alphabetically by Carolina Algonquian headword, followed by its English gloss\. No other information is included in entries\. In the brief introductory section, four words are given with extended etymology and a breakdown of their morphemes\.
