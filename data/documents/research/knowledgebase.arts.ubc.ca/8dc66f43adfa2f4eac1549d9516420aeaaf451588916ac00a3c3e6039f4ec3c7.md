## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary is an enhanced and expanded version of Kaufman’s earlier dictionary titled *Tanêks\-Tąyosą Kadakathi: Biloxi\-English* Dictionary, published in 2011\. The 2011 dictionary is a revised version of the Biloxi portion of James Dorsey and John Swanton’s 1912 *A Dictionary of the Biloxi and Ofo Languages*\. It also includes the unpublished language documentation done by Albert Gatschet in 1886\. According to this third edition’s description on Amazon, this dictionary also incorporates language notes from Mary Haas and Morris Swadesh\.
