Hän FirstVoices

## Language Name

Hän\.

## Alternate Language Names

Han, Moosehide, Dawson, Gens de Fou, Han Gwich\-in, Han\-Kootchin, Hanukutchin, Haen\.

## Region

Yukon, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Tr’ondëk Hwëch’in First Nation \(Author\)\.

## Others Involved

Chris Harvey \(Keyboard creator\); Georgette McLeod \(Hän language administrator and contact for inquiries\); Mabel Henry \(Speaker\); Mabel Henry, Mary Henry, Clair Dragoman \(Recorders\); Percy Henry, Edward Roberts, Mary Henry, Mabel Henry, Doris Roberts \(References\)\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch dates\. As of 2023, this dictionary was last updated early 2022\.

## How People are Cited

Within the *Learn our Language* tab, there are sections titled *Our Keyboards* and *Contact Information*, where Chris Harvey and Georgette McLeod are cited\. Under the same *Learn our Language* tab, there are other tabs called *Words* and *Phrases*; within these tabs is where all the recorded words are, and where all speakers, recorders and references involved are cited \(under the entry *metadata*\)\.

## How Information is Cited

Some information is cited in the entry *metadata* \(found in both the *Words* and *Phrases* sections\)\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a multimedia website which includes audio and images\. It is hosted on the FirstVoices website\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Hän to English\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary is specific to the Tr’ondëk Hwëch’in First Nation\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in this dictionary are organized alphabetically, but can also be searched or filtered by letter in Hän\. It is also searchable in both directions\.

Words and phrases are found in separately sections of the online dictionary under *Learn our Language*\. Each entry contains a recording of the word in English followed by the Hän translation\. Clicking on the Hän word opens a new page where the same information is displayed, as well as the speaker and recorder who are cited under the *i* icon\.

## Other Notes

Both the *Photo Gallery and* *Songs* and *Stories* sections do not contain any content\.

Under the *Learn our Language* tab, there are keyboard installation guides for all types of software\.

Within entries, the *metadata* includes which Clan the speaker or reference individual comes from\.
