A Dictionary of the Eastern Miwok from the Nineteenth and Early Twentieth Century Sources

## Language Name

Bay Miwok, Plains Miwok, Northern Sierra Miwok, Central Sierra Miwok, Southern Sierra Miwok\.

## Alternate Language Names

Bay Miwok – Saclan, Saklan; Plains Miwok – Valley Miwok; Northern Sierra Miwok – Saclan; Central Sierra Miwok – we are unaware of any alternate language names; Southern Sierra Miwok – Meewoc, Mewoc, Me\-Wuk, Miwokan, Mokélumne, Moquelumnan, San Raphael, Talatui, Talutui, Yosemite\.

## Region

California, United States\.

## Who

Adrienne Ford\.

## Others Involved

Dr\. Otto Sadovszky \(support\); Dr\. Gerald Harley \(UNIX system assistance\); Ed Hall \(formatting and editorial assistance\); Angela Farrell, Diana McSwain \(unspecified support\)\.

## Publishing Information

This work was published in 1982 through the University of California at Fullerton\. It was photocopied by Ann Arbor through University Microfilms in 1984\.

## How Information is Cited

Each entry in this dictionary includes entry metadata which indicates it source\. These works are described extensively under *Sources* and listed formally under *References\.*

## Where is Information Coming from

Entries in this dictionary come from previous works made by speakers and linguists\.

## Tools and Framework used

The dictionary is available as a physical and digital book, and a tape recording is available through the Department of Anthropology at California State University, Fullerton\. This dictionary was prepared using the UNIX Fullerton operating system\.

## Access

Both the physical and digital versions of this dictionary are available through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Saclan\-English, Plains Miwok\-English, Northern Sierra Miwok\-English, Central Sierra Miwok\-English, Southern Sierra Miwok\-English; English\-Saclan, English\-Plains Miwok, English\-Northern Sierra Miwok, English\-Central Sierra Miwok, English\-Southern Sierra Miwok

## Dialects Included

When a dialect variant is included, it is specified\. Dialect forms cited are Groveland, North Stanislaus, Yosemite, and Calavaras River\.

## Type of Dictionary

This resource is made up of multiple bilingual, bidirectional dictionaries of Miwok langauges\.

## How are Entries Organised

This resource, made up of multiple bilingual wordlists of Miwok languages, includes the many Miwok\-English wordlists in one section, followed by all English\-Miwok sections in the next section\. Each entry includes the headword, translation in either English or the Miwok language, and source information\. Entries in the Miwok\-English dictionaries are organized alphabetically by the Miwok languages, while entries in the English\-Miwok dictionaries are organized alphabetically by English \(except for flora and fauna entries in the English\-Miwok dictionaries, which are organized alphabetically by their binomial names\)\. Entries consist of the headword, a translation in either English or Miwok, and a description of flora and fauna in relevant entries for some languages\.

The Northern Sierra Miwok and Central Sierra Miwok dictionary sections are also followed by *Texts* sections, which included a segmented narrative with English translations\.
