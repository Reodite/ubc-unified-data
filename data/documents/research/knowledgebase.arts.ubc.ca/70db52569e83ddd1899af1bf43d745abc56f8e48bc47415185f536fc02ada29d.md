A Practical Heiltsuk\-English Dictionary with a Grammatical Introduction

## Language Name

Heiltsuk\.

## Alternate Language Names

Híɫzaqv\(ḷa\), Haíɫzaqv\(ḷa\)\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

John C\. Rath \(author\)\.

## Others Involved

Speakers: Eli Wallace; Evelyn Windsor; Lillian Gladstone; Clarence Martin; George Housty; Angus Campbell; William Freeman; Willie Gladstone; Willie Brown; Beatrice Brown; Hannah Hall; Henry Hall, Robert Hall; John and Mable Humchitt; Edwin Hunt; Maggie Windsor; Marshall Windsor\.

Norma Brown \(typist\); Jennifer Carpenter \(administrator and community editor\); Dr\. Neville J\. Lincoln \(peer editor\); Ian A\. Timmers \(peer editor\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1981, National Museums of Canada, Ottawa\.

Published in two volumes\. The dictionary is published in part two\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers and other contributors are listed in the *Preface* of the dictionary\.

## How Information is Cited

Information is cited in the *Preface* of the dictionary\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary is based on speakers’ knowledge and contribution\.

The vocabulary of Franz Boas’ publication, Bella Bella Texts \(1923\), was used by the dictionary’s compiler\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a digital book \(PDF\) created by scanning typewritten text and as a physical book\.

## Access

The physical and digital book are both accessible through libraries\.

The digital book is available to purchase through University of Ottawa Press for $7\.99 CAD, as of 2023\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Heiltsuk to English\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This a bilingual, mono\-directional word list introduced by a thorough grammar including a phonetic guide, word structure, sentence structure, and grammatical paradigms\.

## How are Entries Organised

The entries are organized alphabetically by Heiltsuk\. The alphabetical order of Heiltsuk used in this dictionary is explained on page 145, in the *Introduction*\. Most entries include only the Heiltsuk word, and an English translation\. Some entries include additional information\.
