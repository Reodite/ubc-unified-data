English\-Secwepemc Dictionary

## Language Name

Secwepemctsín\.

## Alternate Language Names

Shuswap, Secwepemc\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Elders’ Language Committee; Language Advisory Committee\.

## Others Involved

Participants in the Elders’ Language Curriculum Committee \(Editing and Language Data\); Mona Jules, Dr\. Strang Burton \(Compilers\); the Shuswap Training and Employment Program, the First Nations Education Council from School District No\. 73 \(Funding\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2001 by Secwepemc Cultural Education Society, Kamloops\.

This is a third version\. The previous two versions do not have publication dates\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited in the *Acknowledgements* and *Forward*\. However, speakers are not cited individually\.

## How Information is Cited

Contributors and previous publications are cited in the *Acknowledgements* and *Forward* of the dictionary\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This is a third version of this dictionary based on the previous two versions\. The first version was compiled by linguist Aert Kuipers\. The second version was largely compiled by Dr\. Dwight Gardiner\. New information in this version comes from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book and a digital book\.

## Access

The physical dictionary is accessible through some libraries\. The digital dictionary is open access through Arca\. The physical dictionary available through UBC Library is English to Secwepemctsín, and the digital dictionary is Secwepemctsín to English\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Secwepemctsín \(physical dictionary\); Secwepemctsín to English \(digital dictionary\)\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional word list\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by English in the *English\-Secwepemc* section \(the physical book\)\. Entries include the English headword/phrase, the Secwepemctsín translation, and the part of speech\. Entries are organized alphabetically by Secwepemctsín in the *Secwepemc\-English* section \(the digital book\)\. Entries include the Secwepemctsín headword, the English translation, and the part of speech\. No other information is included in either section’s entries\.

The *Acknowledgements* of this dictionary refer to this version of the dictionary having two parts: *Secwepemc\-English* and *English\-Secwepemc*\. The physical book available through UBC Library only contains the *English\-Secwepemc* section of the dictionary\. The digital book available through Arca \(open access download\) only contains the *Secwepemc\-English* section of the dictionary\.
