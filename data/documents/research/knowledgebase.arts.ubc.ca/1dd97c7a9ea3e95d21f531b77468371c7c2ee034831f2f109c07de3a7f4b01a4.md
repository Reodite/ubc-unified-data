Nyan Whut’en Hubughunek \(Cheslatta Carrier Dictionary\)

## Language Name

Dakelh\.

## Alternate Language Names

Carrier\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Bill Poser \(Compiler\)\.

## Others Involved

We are unable to access information about others who may have been involved in creating this dictionary\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2009 by Cheslatta Carrier Nation, Burns Lake, BC\.

This dictionary is listed in various sources online with several titles of slight variations\. While each of its titles include *Nyan Whut’en Hubughunek*, the rest of the title varies\. It is listed as *Cheslatta Carrier Nation Dictionary* on WorldCat, *Cheslatta Carrier Dictionary* on Bill Poser’s website, and *Cheslatta Carrier Lexicon* on the Yinka Dene Language Institute’s website\.

## How are Entries Organised

We are unable to access this dictionary to determine how entries are organized\.

According to the description on Bill Poser’s website, this resource includes a grammar sketch, discusses the relationship of Dakleh dialects, and contains information on the history of the Cheslatta people\. This dictionary has several lists, including a topical index, a root list, stem lists, and an affix list, and indexes of place names, scientific names, and loanwords\.
