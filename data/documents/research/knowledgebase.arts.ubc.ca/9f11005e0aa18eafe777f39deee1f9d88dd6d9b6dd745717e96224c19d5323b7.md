Waapanuutaahch cheimis pei iiyiyuu : iinuu ayimuwin : atimaapiisim \= Eastern James Bay Cree Dictionary: Northern Dialect, Cree\-English

## Language Name

Cree\-Montagnais\-Naskapi\.

## Alternate Language Names

Cree, Naskapi, Montagnais, ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐍᐏᐣ, Nēhiyawēwin\.

## Region

Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, Newfoundland and Labrador, and Northwest Territories, Canada\.

## Who

Luci Salt, Alice Duff, Marie\-Odile Junker, Marguerite MacKenzie, Delaise Torkornoo, Cree School Board, Cree Programs
