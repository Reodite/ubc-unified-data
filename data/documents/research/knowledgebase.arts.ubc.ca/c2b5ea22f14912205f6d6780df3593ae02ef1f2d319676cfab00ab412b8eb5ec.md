Samala\-English Dictionary: a Guide to the Samala Language of the Ineseño Chumash People

## Language Name

Samala\.

## Alternate Language Names

Ineseño, Central Chumash, Alaxulapu\.

## Region

California, United States\.

## Who

Santa Ynez Band of Chumash Mission Indians of the Santa Ynez Reservation California\.

## Others Involved

John P\. Harrington \(linguist\); Maria Ysidora del Refugio Solares \(cultural consultant\); Vincent Armenta, Richard Gomez, Kenneth Kahn, Gary Pace, David Dominguez \(Business Committee\); Sarah Moses, Kathleen Marshall, Rachel Pace\-McGrath, Jacy Romero\-Ontiveros, Robert “Ted” Ortega, Virginia Ortega, Dominica Valencia \(Education Committee\); Dolores Cross, Inez Palato, Morry Talaugon \(former members\); Richard Applegate \(linguist\); Dr\. Frederick Loveys, Tricia Piceno \(unnamed contributions\); Dr\. John Johnson, Katherine Klar \(biographical information\); Susan Jorgensen \(cover photo\); Frances Snyder \(support\)\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary was published by the Santa Ynez Band of Chumash Indians in 2007\.

## How People are Cited

Contributors are cited under *Acknowledgements*, and the primary consultant, Maria Solares, is cited in the *Introduction*\.

## How Information is Cited

John P\. Harrington and Maria Solares are cited in the *Introduction*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from previous work by John P\. Harrington in collaboration with Maria Solares, a matriarch who spoke Samala, Barbaren᷈o, Yokuts language\(s\), and Spanish\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\. The physical book includes a CD with an audio pronunciation guide\.

## Access

This dictionary is available through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Samala to English; English to Samala\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

The *Samala\-English Dictionary* section is organised alphabetically by Samala headword, shown in boldface\. Each entry contains the English definition and related words\. Many entries, mostly verbs, contain example sentences broken down into their component parts\. The *English to Samala Index* is organised alphabetically by English headword with Samala equivalents listed\.
