Sm’algya̱x Living Legacy Talking Dictionary

## Language Name

Coast Tsimshian\.

## Alternate Language Names

sm’álgyax, sm’algya̱x\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Ts’msyen Sm’algyax Authority and the Sm’algyax Committee of School District 52\.

## Others Involved

The many others who were involved in the creation of this online resource are cited in the *Contributors* section of the main page\. The contributors are sorted into sections: *Lexical Database and Software Development*, *Recording and Data Review/Confirmation, Sound Clips from Archival Recordings*, I*mages Provided*, *Recording and Editing Sound Clips*, &amp; *Image Acquisition*, *Financial Support*, and *Software and Support*\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch dates\. The most recent update was in 2018\.

The Sm’algya̱x FirstVoices website holds the same content as this dictionary and is regularly updated, while this website is not\.

## How people are cited

Speakers and contributors are listed under the *Acknowledgements* section of the website\.

## How information is cited

Information is cited under the *Acknowledgements* section of the website and at the bottom of specific entries\.

## Where is information coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from John A\. Dunn’s *A Practical Dictionary of the Coast Tsimshian Language* \(1978\), Franz Boas’ publications on the Tsimshian language, and *Draft Dictionary* \(2000\) printed by School District 52 in Prince Rupert\. Speakers then confirmed and also added to these sources to create this dictionary\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a multimedia website, as it includes both images and audio\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Sm’álgyax to English, English to Sm’álgyax\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary includes language informed by speakers from Txałgiiw, Lax Kw’alaams, Gitxaała, and Metlakatla\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, online dictionary\.

## How are entries organised

Entries are sorted alphabetically by Sm’álgyax or English, depending on the directionality of the search\. Entries can also be filtered by first letter\.
