Comanche Dictionary

## Language Name

Comanche\.

## Alternate Language Names

Nʉmʉ Tekwapʉ̲, Tekwapʉ

## Region

Oklahoma, Texas, New Mexico, Kansas, and Colorado, United States\.

## Who

Comanche Language and Cultural Preservation Committee\.

## Others Involved

Comanche Nation Language Department, Comanche Language Cultural Preservation Committee \(support\)\.

## Publishing Information

This online dictionary was published in 2020 by the Comanche Language and Cultural Preservation Committee using SIL’s Webonary\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not cite any individual authors and contributors\. The *credits &amp; acknowledgements* page for this dictionary is still “under construction” as of 2024\.

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary does not cite the source of any of its information\. The dictionary’s last update date can be found on the homepage\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary does not indicate where information is coming from or what sources were used\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a website using SIL’s Webonary\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access online\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Comanche to English\. The English to Comanche section is still under construction as of 2024\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

As of 2024 this is a bilingual, mono\-directional online word list\.

## How are Entries Organised

This dictionary is searchable by English and Comanche\. Special characters used in Comanche are included above the search box to assist users with typing in Comanche\.

The full word list is alphabetized by Comanche, and is available under *Browse Comanche*\. Users can then filter the dictionary based on the first letter of the entry\.

Each entry consists of the Comanche headword in boldface type followed by its English definition\. Some entries use more than one headword for each definition\.

A *Browse English* section is under development as of mid\-2024\.
