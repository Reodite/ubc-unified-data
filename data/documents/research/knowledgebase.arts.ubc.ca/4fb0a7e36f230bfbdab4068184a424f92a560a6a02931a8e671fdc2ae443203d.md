Phonology, Dictionary and Listing of Roots and Lexical Derivates of the Haisla language of Kitlope and Kitimaat

## Language Name

Haisla\.

## Alternate Language Names

X̄a’islak̓ala, X̌àh̓isl̩ak̓ala\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Neville J\. Lincoln; John C\. Rath\.

## Others Involved

George Paul Wilson, Gordon Robertson \(Language Consultants\); Heather Ballantyne \(Research Assistant\); H́isλàm̩s Ğʷailàs Society \(Project Direction\); Vancouver Office of the Department of the Secretary of State, Multiculturalism Programme of the Department of the Secretary of State, Simon Fraser University, Dean of the Faculty of the Arts \(Funding\); Rick Sharpe, Dr\. T\. Perry \(Typesetting Assistance\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1986 as part of the Mercury Series by the National Museum of Man, Ottawa\.

This dictionary is published in two volumes\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited in the *Preface* found in *Volume One*\.

## How Information is Cited

References are cited in the *Preface* and *References Cited* sections, found in *Volume One* and *Volume Two* respectively\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from speakers, including fieldwork done by the authors and previously collected and archived data, and previously published materials as listed in the *References Cited* of *Volume Two*\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book and a digital book\.

## Access

The physical edition of this dictionary is accesible through libraries\. The digital book is available to purchase through the University of Ottawa press\. Each volume must be purchased separately\. As of 2023 each volume is $7\.99 CA\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Haisla to English\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary primarily includes information from the Kitlope dialect, but also includes some information from the Kitimaat dialect\.

## Type of Dictionary

This dictionary is a bilingual, mono\-directional dictionary, introduced by a brief grammar and concluded by a root list\.

## How are Entries Organised

The entries in this dictionary are organized alphabetically by Haisla according to the aphabet included on pg\. 52\. Entries include the Haisla head word, an English gloss, and the Haisla root on which the head word is based\. Some entries include examples of the use of the head word listed below the main entry\.

The grammar that introduces the dictionary contains information about the Haisla phonemic system, morphophonemics, and syntactic concepts\.

The root list included after the dictionary \(in *Volume Two*\) is organized alphabetically by Haisla\. Root entries include the Haisla root, an English gloss, a reference number connected to the previously published *North Wakashan Comparative Root List* \(1980\) by the same authors, and a list of entries related to the root\.
