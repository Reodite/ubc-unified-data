Inuvialuktun One

## Language Name

Uummarmiutun\.

## Alternate Language Names

Uummaġmiutun, Canadian Iñupiaq, Iñupiaq, Inuvialuktun\. Uummarmiutun is considered a variant or dialect of Iñupiaq, Inuvialuktun or North Alaskan Iñupiaq\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Canada\.

## Who

Yamósha Kúé Society\.

## Others Involved

Government of Northwest Territories Department of Education; Culture and Employment; Inuvialut Cultural Resource Centre\.

## Publishing Information

Published on June 6th, 2013 \(App Store\), and January 15th, 2015 \(Google Play\)\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited in the *About* section of this app on the App Store and Google Play\.

English to Inuvialuktun, Inuvialuktun to English\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary is of the Uummarmiutun dialect, spoken in the Northwest Territories, Canada\.

This is a bilingual dictionary, organized topically, with search capabilities\. When the app works this dictionary also includes games, quizzes and language practice\.
