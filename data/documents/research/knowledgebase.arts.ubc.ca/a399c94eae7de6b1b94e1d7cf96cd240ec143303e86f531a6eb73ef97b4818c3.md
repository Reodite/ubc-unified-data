Tlingit Verb Dictionary

## Language Name

Tlingit\.

## Alternate Language Names

Lingít\.

## Region

British Columbia, Yukon, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Gillian L\. Story and Constance M\. Naish \(Compilers\)\.

## Others Involved

Constance V\. Youngkin \(Illustrator\); Summer Institute of Linguistics \(Compiler\); University of Alaska, Alaska Native Language Center \(Publishers\); Rev\. George R\. Betts, Robert Zuboff, Andrew P\. Johnson \(Consultants\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published on July 23rd, 1973 by the University of Alaska, Alaska Native Language Center, College, Alaska\.

## How people are cited

The compilers, illustrator, and publishers are cited on the first three pages of the dictionary\. Speakers, consultants and other contributors are cited in the *Preface* of this dictionary\.

## How information is cited

The information in this dictionary is cited in the *Preface*\.

## Where is information coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical and digital book\.

## Access

The digital copy of this dictionary is open access through the Alaska Native Language Archive and SIL websites, while the physical copy is accessible through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Tlingit, Tlingit to English\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of the Central dialect, as spoken mainly in Juneau, Sitka, Hoonah and Angoon\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\. It is in the form of an alphabetically organized dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

The dictionary is split up into two parts: *Part 1* \(English\-Tlingit\) and *Part 2* \(Tlingit\-English\)\. Entries in *Part 1* are organized alphabetically by English, while those in *Part 2* are organized alphabetically by Tlingit\.

## Other Notes

A review about the dictionary was published October 1977 on the International Journal of American Linguistics\. The review is an introduction to the dictionary and its structure, and discusses the purpose of the publication, the nature of Tlingit and many other components that make up *Tlingit Verb Dictionary*\.
