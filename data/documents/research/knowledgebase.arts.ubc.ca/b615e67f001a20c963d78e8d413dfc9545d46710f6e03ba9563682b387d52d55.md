## Included Languages and Directionality

Kwakw’ala to English, English to Kwak’wala\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a spelling workbook and picture dictionary for children which includes bidirectional wordlists at the end of the book\.

## How are Entries Organised

The interactive section of this dictionary is organized alphabetically by Kwak’wala\. The wordlists at the back of the book are organized alphabetically by Kwak’wala in the Kwak’wala to English section, and by English in the English to Kwak’wala section\.
