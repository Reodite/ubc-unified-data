Castel’s English\-Cree Dictionary and Memoirs of the Elders Based on the Woods Cree of Pukatawagan, Manitoba

## Language Name

Cree\-Montagnais\-Naskapi\.

## Alternate Language Names

Cree, Naskapi, Montagnais, ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐍᐏᐣ, Nēhiyawēwin\.

## Region

Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, Newfoundland and Labrador, and Northwest Territories, Canada\.

## Who

Robert J\. Castel, David Westfall\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary was published in 2001 by the Brandon University Northern Teacher Education Program\. It is printed in two volumes: the *Dictionary and Memoirs*, and the *Appendices*\.

## How People are Cited

Elders and speakers are cited under *Acknowledgements*\.

## How Information is Cited

Photographs and illustrations are cited alphabetically in the *List of Illustrations*\. Speakers and Elders are cited under *Acknowledgements*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Entries in this dictionary come from speakers and Elders\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\. There is a CD\-ROM that accompanies this dictionary, but it is unclear whether it is still accessible\.

## Access

This dictionary is available through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Cree to English; English to Cree \(sentence examples, glossary\)\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of Pukatawagan Woods Cree\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, monodirectional dictionary with texts\.

## How are Entries Organised

The *English\-Cree Dictionary* is an alphabetized list of example sentences and phrases\. Each entry begins with an English headword in boldface type \(eg: able, be\) followed by a list of related sentences, first in English, then in Cree \(written in the Latin/Roman alphabet\)\. The headword itself is not translated into Cree\.

*Memoirs of the Elders* are transcribed interviews with community Elders, interviewed by students\. Most of the interviews are written line by line in latinized Cree, with the English translation in smaller text below\. A few of them are written entirely in English\. A short section of *Student Stories* is included as well, written in Cree\. These stories were created or translated \(from English fables\) by Cree students in the Pukatawagan BUNTEP class\.

The *Appendices* comprise the entirety of *Volume II*\. The volume contains a series of topical word lists including *Kinship Terms, Weather, Trees and Shrubs, Mammals, Numbers*, and more\. Next is the *Cree\-English Glossary*, which is a on\-to\-one word list from Cree to English\. Entries are organised alphabetically by Cree headword\. Each entry contains the grammatical classification, the English gloss, a citation of a previous source the form is attested in \(if applicable\) and comparative forms\. Last is the *English\-Cree Index*, which is organised alphabetically by English headword\. Each entry contains only the corresponding Cree word, to be looked up either in this volume or in the example sentence section of *Volume I*\.
