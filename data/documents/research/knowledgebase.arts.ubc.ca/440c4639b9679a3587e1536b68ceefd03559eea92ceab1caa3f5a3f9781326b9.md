Koasati Dictionary App

## Language Name

Koasati\.

## Alternate Language Names

Coushatta, Kowassá:ti\.

## Region

Texas and Louisiana, United States\.

## Who

Koasati Language Committee \(compiler\); Joshua Poirier and Emerald Skye Byrd \(developers\)\.

## Others Involved

Jack Martin \(database contributor\); Jonathan Bennett \(logo artist\)\.

Coushatta Heritage Department, Dr\. Linda Langley, and the Southern Coushatta Tribe of Louisiana \(unspecified contributions\)\.

## Publishing Information

This app was launched on the Apple store in 2017, and was last updated in 2023\.

## How People are Cited

Contributors are cited on the *About* page under *More\.*

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary does not cite the source of its information\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary does not indicate where information is coming from or what sources were used\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a mobile app for Apple devices\. It does not appear to be available for another other operating systems\.

## Access

The dictionary is free to download from the Apple App store\. It is only available to users of Apple devices\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Koasati, Koasati to English\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

This entries in this dictionary are organized alphabetically by headword\. Users can access full entries by clicking on the headwords\. Examples sentences are only included in the *English\-Koasati* section\.

In the *Koasati\-English* section, entries include the Koasati headword, the English translation, a transcription in the International Phonetic Alphabet, and the entry’s part of speech\.

In the *English\-Koasati* section, entries include the English headword, the Koasati translation, a transcription of the Koasati entry in the International Phonetic Alphabet, the entry’s part of speech, and example sentences\.
