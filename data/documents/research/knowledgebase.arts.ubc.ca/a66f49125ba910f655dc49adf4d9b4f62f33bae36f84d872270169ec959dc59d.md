Dictionary of Upriver Halkomelem: Volume I and II

## Language Name

Halkomelem\.

## Alternate Language Names

Halq̓eméylem, Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓, hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓\.

## Region

British Clumbia Canada; Washington, United States\.

## Who

Brent D\. Galloway\.

## Others Involved

Over eighty speakers are cited by name, community, and dialect, including Elders from the Coqualeet7a Elders Group, Stalo Heritage Project Elders Group, Halkomelem Workshop Elders Group \(Nooksack Tribe\), those who contributed at the Elders meetings at Sardis, and Elders on Oliver Well’s tapes\. This includes speakers of the Chillikwack, Chehalis, Tait, Sumas/Kilgard, and Downriver dialects\.

Ralph Maud, Marie Weeden \(transcription and annotation of Oliver Wells’ tapes\); Mary Wilde, Darren Okemaysim, Sonja van Eijk \(research assistants\); Dr\. Bob Hsu \(Lexware programming assistance\), Dr\. Tim Montler, Pam Cahn \(computational data entry\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2009, University of California Press, Berkeley\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited by name and community in the *About the Elders* section of the dictionary, and by initials within each entry\.

## How Information is Cited

Information is cited in the *About the Elders* and *References* sections of the dictionary\. References to specific sources, which includes specific Elders, are cited within entries\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary is from speakers, gathered by the author between 1970 and this dictionary’s publishing \(2009\), and numerous previously published resources listed in the *References* section of the dictionary\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as both a physical and digital book\.

## Access

Both the physical and digital books are available through libraries\. The physical book can be purchased from the publisher for $94\.95 CA\. The digitized version is open access through the University of California\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Halkomelem to English; English to Halkomelem \(Index\)\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of the Upriver dialect of Halkomelem, specifically the subdialects of Chilliwack, Chehalis, Tait, and Sumas/Kilgard\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\. It is introduced by a thorough grammar and introduction to the language and the speakers\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Halkomelem in the *Halkomelem to English* section of the dictionary, and by English in the *English to Halkomelem Index* that follows\.

The *Halkomelem to English* section is formatted as a root dictionary\. Each entry includes a headword in the Stó:lō orthography, followed by the morphological category, IPA transcriptions, a form with segmented affixes, sometimes followed by phonemic and/or phonetic transcriptions\. Entries also include source information and example sentences\.
