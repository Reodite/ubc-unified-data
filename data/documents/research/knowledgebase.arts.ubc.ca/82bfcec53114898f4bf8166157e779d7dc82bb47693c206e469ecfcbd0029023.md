Kenai Tanaina Noun Dictionary

## Language Name

Dena’ina\.

## Alternate Language Names

Tanaina\.

## Region

Alaska, United States\.

## Who

James M\. Kari \(Author\)\.

## Others Involved

**Organisations**

Alaska Native Language Center \(Publisher\); National Endowment for the Humanities \(Support\)\.

**Individuals**

Peter Kalifornsky, Fedosia Sacaloff, Bertha Monfor, Ephem Baktuit \(Speakers\); George Miller Jr\. \(President of the Kenai Natives Association\)\.

## Publishing Information

This preliminary version was published April 1974 by the Alaska Native Language Center, University of Alaska, Fairbanks, Alaska\.

## How People are Cited

The author is cited on the cover of the dictionary\. Others involved, including speakers are cited by name in the *Foreward*\.

## How Information is Cited

The source of information for this dictionary is cited in the *Foreward*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from named speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical and digital book\.

## Access

The digital copy \(PDF scan\) is open access through the Alaska Native Language Archive\. The physical copy can be accessed through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Dena’ina\.

## Dialects Included

The dialect included in this dictionary is the Outer Inlet dialect from Kenai\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

The entries in this dictionary are organized topically by English\. Each entry has an English headword, which sometimes includes sub\-entries, and a Dena’ina translation\. No other information is included in the entries\.

## Other Notes

The author points out that this dictionary is only a preliminary version and is therefore incomplete and likely contains mistakes\.

The Alaska Native Language Archive provides 5 different PDFs in the post of the dictionary which include annotated, edited, and errata versions of the dictionary\.
