Tahltan Children’s Illustrated Dictionary

## Language Name

Tahltan\.

## Alternate Language Names

Tałtan ẕāke, dah dẕāhge, didene keh\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

## Other Notes

There is a news article in the Terrace Standard from 2022 announcing that members of the community are using this dictionary in combination with rapid word collection workshops to create a new updated dictionary app for the language\.

Entries from this dictionary are included on the Tāłtān FirstVoices\. It is unclear if the dictionary app referred to in the Terrace Standard article is the FirstVoices website, or if the community is working on a separate app\.
