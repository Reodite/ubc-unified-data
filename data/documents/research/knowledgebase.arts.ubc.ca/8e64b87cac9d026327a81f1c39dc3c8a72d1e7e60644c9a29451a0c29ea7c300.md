Talk Sauk: Dictionary

## Language Name

Sauk\-Fox\-Kickapoo\.

## Alternate Language Names

Fox, Mesquakie, Meskwaki, Mesquakie\-Sauk, Mesquakie\-Sauk\-Kickapoo, Sauk\-Fox, Sac and Fox, Meshkwahkihaki, Thâkiwâtowêweni, Kikapú\.

## Region

Oklahoma, Iowa, and Kansas, United States; Coahuila, Mexico\.

## Who

Sac &amp; Fox Nation\.

## Others Involved

Henrietta Massey, Maxine Cobb, Christine Williamson \(Elders\); Lee Bass, Jr\. \(Unspecified Contributions\)\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary is composed of several pages on the Sac and Fox Nation’s *Sauk Language* website\. It does not display any information about launch dates or update dates\.

## How People are Cited

Elders are cited on the home page under the header *Kîmâchipena Let’s Come Together\.*

## How Information is Cited

Elders are cited on the home page\. No other sources of information are cited\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary does not cite the source of any of its information\.\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a multimedia website that uses hyperlinks, audio files, and images\.

## Access

This dictionary is available open access online\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English and Sauk\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of Sauk\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, online, multimedia dictionary that uses audio files, images, and hyperlinks\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in this dictionary are organised thematically under the *Words\-Phrases* tab\. Each topic contains two word lists: one with English, and one only in Sauk\. Each entry in these word lists contains the headword, either in English and Sauk or only Sauk, and an image\. Clicking on the image plays a recording of the Sauk word\.

This website also includes Sauk stories and educational videos, as well as Sauk learning games\.
