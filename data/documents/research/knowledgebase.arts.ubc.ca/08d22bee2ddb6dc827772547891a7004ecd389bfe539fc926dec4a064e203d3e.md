Kanesatake Mohawk Dictionary \= Kanesata’ké: ha tekawenna’ thé: tha

## Language Name

Mohawk\.

## Alternate Language Names

Kanien’kéha, Kanyen’kéha\.

## Region

Ontario and Quebec, Canada; New York, United States\.

## Who

Harvey Satewas Gabriel \(Speaker and Author\)\.

## Others Involved

Kahnawake \(Supporter\); Susan Gabriel \(Editor\); Cheryl Kanerahtenhá:wi Gabriel \(Editor, Illustrator\)\.

## Publishing Information

The first edition of this dictionary was published in 2014\. The second edition was published in 2017 by Harvey Satewas Gabriel\.
