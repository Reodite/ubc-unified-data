## Language Name

Palewyami\.

## Alternate Language Names

## Region

California, United States\.

## Who

Geoffry Gamble\.

## Others Involved

According to Berman 2002 John P\. Harrington is the recorder of the original version of this Palewyami wordlist from 1916 with speaker Estevan \(Steve\) Soto\.

Published in 1991 as part of A Festschrift for William F Shipley, edited by Sandra Chung and Jorge Hankamer, Santa Cruz, California, Syntax Research Center\.
