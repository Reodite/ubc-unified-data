Hidatsa Dictionary \(Pre\-Release Version\)

## Language Name

Hidatsa\.

## Alternate Language Names

hiraaciré’\.

## Region

North Dakota, South Dakota, and Montana, United States\.

## Who

The Language Conservancy, and the Mandan, Hidatsa and Arikara Nation\.

## Others Involved

There is no information included in this resource about others involved\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary is published as a ‘pre\-release version’\. It is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch dates\. The bottom of the page webpage shows that the website is copyright 2014\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not cite any authors or contributors other than the Language Conservancy, and the Mandan, Hidatsa and Arikara Language Project\.

## How Information is Cited

The *About the Dictionary* page notes that the Mandan, Hidatsa and Arikara Language Project spend every summer recording Hidatsa speakers\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Although this dictionary does not explicitly indicate where information is coming from or what sources were used, based on the *About the Dictionary* page*,* information in this dictionary likely comes directly from speakers who have worked with the Mandan, Hidatsa and Arikara Language Project\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a multimedia website which includes audio\.

## Access

This online dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Hidatsa to English, English to Hidatsa\. The dictionary is searchable in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is bilingual, bidirectional, online dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by either Hidatsa or English depending on the search direction\. Each entry includes the headword, translation, and the part of speech\.
