Hidatsa Dictionary App

## Language Name

Hidatsa\.

## Alternate Language Names

hiraaciré’\.

## Region

North Dakota, South Dakota, and Montana, United States\.

## Who

The Language Conservancy, and the Mandan, Hidatsa and Arikara Nation\.

## Others Involved

There is no information included in this resource about others involved\. There may be more information available one the app is downloaded\.

## Publishing Information

This app was released August 2017, and is only available on Google Play\. It was last updated July 2020\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a mobile app published on Google Play by the MHA Language Project\.

## Access

This dictionary is free and open access\. The app can only be accessed through Android mobile devices, due to it being published on Google Play\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Hidatsa to English, English to Hidatsa\. The dictionary is searchable in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is bilingual, bidirectional, mobile app dictionary\.
