Our World—Our Ways: T’aat’aaqsapa Cultural Dictionary

## Language Name

Nuu\-chah\-nulth and Ditidaht\.

## Alternate Language Names

Nuu\-chah\-nulth: Nootka, t’aat’aaqsapa, tahkaht, Westcoast\. Ditidaht: Nitinaht, diiʔdiitidq\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Dr\. Jay Powell \(Compiler/Editor\); Andrew Callicum \(Series Editor\); Caroline Little \(Proofreader\); Nuuchahnulth Tribal Council

## Others Involved

Andrew Callicum; Martin Charles; Cecilia Tate Charles; Rose Cootes; August Dick; Juliana George; Jessie Hamilton; Hilda Hanson; Mary Hayes; Jerry Jack; Harold Little; Ambrose Maquinna; Rose McCarthy; Morris McLean; Bob Martin; Ruth Martin; Lily Michael; James Nicolaye; Micheal Oscar; Alice Paul; Lawrence Paul; Helen Robinson; Adam Shewish; Joe Smith; Willie Sport; John Thomas; Archie Thompson; Josephine Thompson; Sarah Tutube; Charlie Watts\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1991, Nuuchahnulth Tribal Council\.

A previous version of this dictionary was published in 1989\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers and contributors are cited on the title page of the dictionary, *The Contributors* section on page 5, and the *Special Thanks* section on page 6\.

## How Information is Cited

Contributing speakers are cited on the title page\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes directly from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a digital book \(PDF\) created by scanning typerwritten text and as a physical book\.

## Access

The physical book is available through libraries\.

The digital book is open access through the Huu\-ay\-aht First Nations website\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Ditidaht to English to ten Nuu\-chah\-nulth dialects for *Part 1\.*

Ditidaht to English to twelve Nuu\-chah\-nulth dialects for *Part 2*\.

English to five Nuu\-chah\-nulth dialects for *Part 3*\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a multilinguial, multi\-directional word list introduced by a brief grammar\.

## How are Entries Organised

The entries are organized topically with Part 1 characterized as “Things,” Part 2 as “Qualities and Characteristics,” and Part 3 as “Actions and States of Being\.”

There is English appendix at the end of the dictionary organized alphabetically by English\.
