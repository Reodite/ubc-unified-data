Dena’ina Topical Dictionary

## Language Name

Dena’ina\.

## Alternate Language Names

Tanaina\.

## Region

Alaska, United States\.

## Who

James M\. Kari \(Compiler\)\.

## Others Involved

We are unable to access information about others who may have been involved in creation of this dictionary\.

## Publishing Information

The first edition was published 2007; the revised second edition, was published 2013\. Both editions were published by the Alaska Native Language Center, Fairbanks\.

The second edition includes and additional chapter, *Chapter 33: Basic Terminology for Grammar and the Verb\.*

## Other Notes

The Alaska Native Language Archive has two archived entries relating to this dictionary which include *Chapter 33: Basic Terminology for Grammar and the Verb*, which was not included in the 2007 edition; and Keren D\. Rice’s 2009 review of Dena’ina Topical Dictionary\.

Rice’s review goes quite in depth explaining some specific contents of the dictionary and includes sample entries as well as quotes from Alan Boraas, Peter Kalifornsky, and Jonathon S\. Ross\.
