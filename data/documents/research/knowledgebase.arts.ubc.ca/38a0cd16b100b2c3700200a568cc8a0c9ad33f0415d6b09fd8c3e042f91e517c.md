Oneida Language: Wisconsin

## Language Name

Oneida\.

## Alternate Language Names

Onʌyota’aːka, Onʌyota’aːká, Onʌyotaʔeːka\.

## Region

Ontario, Canada; New York and Wisconsin, United States\.

## Who

Lance Etcitty \(Developer\)\.

## Others Involved

Bob Brown, Maria Hinton, Hudson Doxtator, Helen Skenandore \(Speakers\)\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary app was published by Lance Etcitty\. According to the listing on Google Play, this app was published in 2012, and was most recently updated on August 27, 2024

## How People are Cited

Contributors are cited within individual entries\.

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary doesn’t cite any information other than information coming from speakers who are cited as indicated above\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary seems to come directly from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is an app which is available on Google Play and the Apple app store for smartphones and tablets\.

## Access

This dictionary can be accessed for free through Google Play and the Apple app store\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Oneida\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional, topical dictionary which translates phrases instead of lone words\.

## How are Entries Organised

This dictionary organizes phrases alphabetically and gives users the option to search by phrase type, or to access all phrases it contains\. When searching by phrase type, the user can search for phrases in the following categories: *Clans*, *Family Greetings*, *Greetings*, and *Who are You*\.

Each entry is organized in the middle of the screen, starting with the phrase in English, then the category the phrase belongs to, or *Group Phrase*\. This is followed by the phrase in Oneida and the name of the speaker\. At the bottom is a button labeled *Listen* which allows users to hear the pronunciation of the phrase\.
