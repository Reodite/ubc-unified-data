## Instructions/Manuals/Guides

[**1\. How To’s**](https://www.miromaa.org.au/help-resources/how-to-s)

**Resource Type:** Website

Miromaa Aboriginal Language &amp; Technology Centre\. “How To’s\.” *Help &amp; Resources*\. [https://www\.miromaa\.org\.au/help\-resources/how\-to\-s](https://www.miromaa.org.au/help-resources/how-to-s/miromaa-software-help)\. Accessed May 18, 2023\.

---

The *How To’s* section of the Miromaa website provides guides and instructions for how to use the software\. This resource offers walk\-throughs for the software, covering various topics, such as installing Miromaa, backing up work, making customizations, importing multimedia, creating wordlists, and more\. Each entry for the various topics include screenshots and step\-by\-step instructions for the task\. Some entries include video demonstrations\.

This page is a one\-stop resource for users to learn more about Miromaa and access information regarding issues encountered while working on a project\.

This web page can be found [here](https://www.miromaa.org.au/help-resources/how-to-s)\.

Note: The developers encourage license holders to contact the Miromaa team with further questions or issues\.

[**2\. Video How To’s**](https://www.miromaa.org.au/help-resources/video-how-to-s)

**Resource Type:** Website

Miromaa Aboriginal Language &amp; Technology Centre\. “Video How To’s\.” *Help &amp; Resources*\. [https://www\.miromaa\.org\.au/help\-resources/video\-how\-to\-s](https://www.miromaa.org.au/help-resources/video-how-to-s)\. Accessed May 18, 2023\.

---

The *Video How To’s* section of the Miromaa website houses the videos found within the *[How To’s](https://knowledgebase.arts.ubc.ca/miromaa-reviews-guides/#miromaa-how-tos)* section featured above\. Broken into sections with several videos nested under the main video, this resource offers viewers direct access to Miromaa’s video tutorials\.

This resource is helpful for users looking for video tutorials only\.

The videos can be accessed [here](https://www.miromaa.org.au/help-resources/video-how-to-s)\.

[**3\. *Your Language Journey* Workbook**](https://www.miromaa.org.au/help-resources/your-language-journey-workbook)

**Resource Type:** Website

Miromaa Aboriginal Language &amp; Technology Centre\. Miromaa Project\. *‘Your Language Journey’ Workbook*\. [https://www\.miromaa\.org\.au/help\-resources/your\-language\-journey\-workbook](https://www.miromaa.org.au/help-resources/your-language-journey-workbook) Accessed December 22, 2022\.

---

*Your Language Journey* is the companion workbook to training programs offered by Miromaa, and is freely available as a series of pages on their website\. The workbook is divided into six sections, and is designed to support with project planning, equipment information, and practical how\-to guides for the software\. The six sections are:

1.  Introduction and Overview
2.  Language Journey
3.  Language Documentation with Miromaa – our way
4.  Multimedia, Recording and Language Equipment – The Miromaa way
5.  Miromaa Workshop Exercises
6.  Miromaa Planning Exercises

Users new to Miromaa, or those looking to expand the software’s capabilities or their own tech skills, will find a detailed step\-by\-step guide to all functions of the Miromaa software\.

This workbook can be found [here](https://www.miromaa.org.au/help-resources/your-language-journey-workbook)\.

[**4\. Workshops**](https://www.miromaa.org.au/training-workshops/workshops)

**Resource Type:** Website

Miromaa Aboriginal Language &amp; Technology Centre\. “Miromaa Training Workshops\.” *Training\.* [https://www\.miromaa\.org\.au/training\-workshops/workshops](https://www.miromaa.org.au/training-workshops/workshops)\. Accessed June 8, 2022\.

---

The *Training Workshops* page on the Miromaa website has a list of workshops offered by Miromaa\. The workshops are offered by the Miromaa Aboriginal Language &amp; Technology Centre and cover language project planning, mastering record tools, digital archiving, and creating resources via Miromaa\.

Users looking to connect to the wider Miromaa community while mastering the Miromaa software may be interested in reaching out to the Miromaa team to access the resources detailed on this page\.

The workshops can be found [here](https://www.miromaa.org.au/training-workshops/workshops)\.

Note: There are no details of when and where these training workshops are offered, and it is likely that a license must be obtained to access this information\. Users are encouraged to contact Miromaa if they are interested in any of the workshops\.

**How Indigenous Conceptions Shape the Work:**  
**The Case of the Miromaa Aboriginal Language and Technology Centre**

This resource is an audio recording of a presentation given at the 3rd International Conference on Language Documentation and Conservation \(ICLDC\)\.  
  
*Description:*

“This paper presents a case study of the Miromaa Aboriginal Language and Technology Centre in Australia\. MALTC is special in two ways: \(1\) it has been entirely independent and indigenous\-run from the time of its conception; and \(2\) its work has involved the reclamation of a language that was not spoken for more than a century\. The paper explores how MALTC sees language and culture as inseparable and tightly linked to identity, and how this vision has been at the heart of the centre’s work, shaping its goals, methods, and outputs\. First, we discuss MALTC’s Wildlife Dictionary, which relates to the centre’s lexicographic work\. Here the primary focus has been on uncovering the knowledge systems and cultural practices that are seen as the definition of words\. Wildlife was chosen as the primary topic for a thematic dictionary because it relates to the creation of the world, to the very essence of a land, and to the identity of individuals as they pass through different phases of their lives\. Second, we explore the centre’s focus on developing technologies to support their language and cultural work, as embodied in the Miromaa software and in Puliima, an indigenous language and technology forum, organized by MALTC\. Unlike traditional language materials, new and powerful information technology allows the sort of multimedia and multiplatform approaches capable of creating resources that do justice to complex aboriginal conceptions of language, as well as making the work more engaging, practical and effective\. Third, we will discuss the MALTC’s work with heritage artifacts, specifically the traditional Possum Skin Cloak, which is made from a series of panels containing symbols with great significance to aboriginal communities and to the lives of the individuals who wear them\. The process of translation and exploration of semantic connotation provides access to previously unknown symbolic meanings, hence to the uncovering and creation of knowledge and a deepening of culture\. Thus the language is not seen as the end point of language documentation and reclamation, but as a path to something broader, including cultural understanding, the enhancement of community knowledge, and identity\. This paper illustrates how indigenous conceptions and ideologies can lead to choices of activities and methods of LDC that are distinct from those determined by academic linguists\. Language centres, in being locally grounded, tied to community, and focused on strengthening languages, are in a unique position to present alternative models of LDC work\.”

This recording can be accessed [here](https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/09af0b35-b80c-4755-85b2-f6d49738c00d)\.
