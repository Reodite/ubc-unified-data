Oskēh\-Wāēpeqtah Omāēqnomenēweqnaesen Wēhcekanan/ A Beginner’s Dictionary of Menominee

## Language Name

Menominee\.

## Alternate Language Names

Menomini, Oma͞eqnomenew\.

## Region

Wisconsin, United States\.

## Who

Monica Ann Macaulay, Marianne Milligan\.

## Others Involved

Steve Askenette \(Wīs\-Ānahkwat\), Marie Floring \(Mānīh\), Lillian Nelson \(Wāqsecewan\), Lavina Shawano \(Sāwanūhkiw\), Sarah Skubitz \(Wāēpecewan\), and Tillie Zhuckkahosee \(Kewāēhoniahkiw\) \(Elders\); Ron Corn Jr\., Nathan Culwell\-Kanarek, Ryan Hanke, Rebecca Shields, Sara Ziemendorf, Amy Latessa, Christine Lemley, Heidi Reiter, Jeff Sauer \(Compilation Assistance\); National Science Foundation, University of Wisconsin\-Madison Graduate School \(Funding\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2009 by the Menominee Indian Tribe of Wisconsin, and developed under the supervision of the Menominee Language and Culture Code Commission\.
