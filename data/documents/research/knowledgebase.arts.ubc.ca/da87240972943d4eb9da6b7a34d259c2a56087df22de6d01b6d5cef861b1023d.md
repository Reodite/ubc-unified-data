New Lakota Dictionary – Pro

## Language Name

Lakota\.

## Alternate Language Names

Lakȟótiyapi, Lakhota, Lakhóta, Teton, Teton Sioux\.

## Region

Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, and Ontario, Canada; Montana, Wyoming, Colorado, North Dakota, South Dakota, Nebraska, Kansas, Oklahoma, Texas, Minnesota, and Iowa, United States\.

## Who

Jan Ullrich \(Compiler and Editor\)\.

## Others Involved

Over 300 speakers, Elders, and consultants, as well as other collaborators, are cited in this dictionary\.  

## Publishing Information

The first edition launched in 2014\. An updated second edition was launched in 2018 by the Lakota Language Consortium\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited on the *About the New Lakota Dictionary* and *Acknowledgements* pages within the *Program Help* section of the software \(accessed via the green question mark at the top right of the main screen\)\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers are cited in the *Acknowledgements* section\. Previous works and editions of this work are cited in the *About the New Lakota Dictionary* section\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from speakers who have worked with language initiatives since 1992, which includes the Lakota Language Consortium’s publication of the physical dictionary *New Lakota Dictionary: Lakȟótiyapi\-English/English\-Lakȟótiyapi* \(first published in 2008\)\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as downloadable software\.

It is also available as a mobile app under the title *New Lakota Dictionary App* and a physical book under the title *New Lakota Dictionary: Lakȟótiyapi\-English/English\-Lakȟótiyapi*\.

## Access

This desktop software can be purchased for $29\.95 USD from the online LLC Bookstore\. A thirty\-day free trial is available upon downloading the software\.

The mobile app is free and open access on the Apple App Store and Google Play\. The physical book can also be purchased from the online LLC Bookstore for $49\.95 USD\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Lakota; Lakota to English\. The search function operates in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional, digital desktop software\.

## How are Entries Organised

In order to access entries, users must use the search bar\. When a user searches a word, all entries pertaining to that word \(numbered in order of relevance\) are listed on the right side of the screen below the headword \(in whichever language it was searched\)\. The entries contain the translation, the definition in English, the part of speech, and variations in spelling and pronunciation\. All Lakota words are hyperlinked\. When double\-clicked, users are taken to the full entry of that word, which also contains an audio recording, a breakdown of the word, example sentences, and more related forms\.

When users search a word, they can select on the left side of the screen to see a list of results from the full dictionary corpus\. This lists all English or Lakota entries \(depending on which language the search function was performed in\), all translation entries, all definitions, and all examples that have the searched word in the entry\. Each of the listed items is hyperlinked and when clicked, takes users to that specific entry\.
