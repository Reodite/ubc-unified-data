Gitxsanimx Speller: Text for Adult Gitxsanmixw Class

## Language Name

Gitxsan\.

## Alternate Language Names

Gitksan, Gitxsanimaax, Gitikshan, Gityskyan, Giklsan\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Jane Smith\.

## Others Involved

Joe Smith, John Smith, Emma Smith, Mary Smith, Fanny Smith, and Chief Billy Blackwater \(Teachers and Speakers\); Vera Wale, and Lucille Stoney \(Editing\); House of Hanamuuxw \(Funding\)\.

## Publishing Information

There is no publishing information within this dictionary\. However, according to the Northwest East \(Smithers and Area\) Aboriginal Health Improvement Committee’s \(2017\) *Gitxsan Phrase Book for Health Care Providers* \([https://www\.indigenoushealthnh\.ca/sites/default/files/resources/documents/gitxan\-phrase\-booklet\.pdf](https://www.indigenoushealthnh.ca/sites/default/files/resources/documents/gitxan-phrase-booklet.pdf)\), this dictionary was published in 2009\.
