Sq’éwlets Glossary

## Language Name

Halkomelem\.

## Alternate Language Names

Halq̓eméylem, Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓, hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Sq’éwlets First Nation\.

## Others Involved

Contributors to the larger Sq’éwlets website are credited on the *Project Credits &amp; Acknowledgements* page\. It is unclear which specific members of the team compiled the glossary\.

## Publishing Information

Launched 2016, Stó:lō Research and Resource Management Centre, Stó:lō Nation\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited by name in the *Project Credits &amp; Acknowledgements* section of the website\.

## How Information is Cited

This glossary does not cite the source of its information\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This glossary is a compilation of words used on the Sq’éwlets website\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is an online, multimedia glossary, hosted on the Sq’éwlets website\.

## Access

This glossary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Halkomelem to English\.

## Dialects Included

This is a glossary of the Upriver dialect of Halkomelem, specifically the dialect of the Stó:lō people\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional glossary, which only contains Halkomelem words users may encounter on the Sq’éwlets website\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Halkomelem, and contain an audio recording, an English translation, roots and notes, pronunciation, and IPA spelling\.

Information about the Stó:lō writing system and Halkomelem sound system is listed below the glossary\.
