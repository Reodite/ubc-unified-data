## How are Entries Organised

Entries in the *Monachi\-English* section are organised alphabetically by Mono headword\. Each entry contains the part of speech, the practical English definition, and comparative forms if known\. The *English\-Monachi* section is intended as a finder list, organised alphabetically by English headword\. Each entry contains the Mono equivalent and part of speech\.
