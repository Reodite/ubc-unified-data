Ute Mobile Dictionary App

## Language Name

Colorado River Numic\.

## Alternate Language Names

Ute, Southern Paiute, Ute\-Southern Paiute, Ute\-Chemehuevi\.

## Region

Nevada, California, Utah, Arizona, and Colorado, United States\.

## Who

Ute Mountain Ute Tribe, and The Language Conservatory\.

## Others Involved

There is no information included in this resource about others involved\. There may be more information available once the app is downloaded\.

## Publishing Information

This app was released November 2021, and is only available on the Apple Store\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not appear to cite any authors or contributors\. There may be more information available once the app is downloaded\. The Ute Mountain Ute Tribe is recognized on the The Language Conservancy but not on the app’s download page\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a mobile app published on the Apple Store by the The Language Conservancy\.

## Access

This dictionary is free and open access\. The app can only be accessed through iOS \(Apple\) mobile devices due to it being published on the Apple Store\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Ute to English, English to Ute\. The dictionary is searchable in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is bilingual, bidirectional, mobile app dictionary\.
