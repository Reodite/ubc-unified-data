Tłıchǫ Yatıì Enįtł’è Cehkoa gha/Dogrib Primary Dictionary

## Language Name

Dogrib\.

## Alternate Language Names

Tłı̨chǫ Yatıì, Tlicho, Tłı̨chǫ\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Canada\.

## Who

Tłı̨chǫ Community Services Agency \(Publisher\)\.

## Others Involved

Mary Siemens, Cecilia Zoe\-Martin, Lucy Lafferty, Rose Mantla, Ernestine Steinwand, Tammy Steinwand, Christine Liske \(Development Team\); Edward Weyallon, Gabe Lafferty \(Culture/Language Resources\); Inkit Ltd\. \(Design/Illustrations\); Secretary of State Canada \(Funding\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2007, Tłı̨chǫ Community Services Agency\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited on the copyright page\.

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary does not cite the source of its information\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary does not indicate where information is coming from or what sources were used\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as an ebook, and contains hand drawn illustrations\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access through the Tlicho Government website\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Dogrib to English\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Dogrib\. There are several hand drawn illustrations on each page, each labelled with the corresponding Dogrib word\.

Entries include Dogrib and the English translation\.
