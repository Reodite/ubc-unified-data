Vuntut Gwich’in FirstVoices

## Language Name

Gwich’in\.

## Alternate Language Names

Gwitch’in, Kutchin, Kootchin, Loucheux, Loucheaux, Takudh, Tukudh\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Yukon, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

This dictionary was created by the Vuntut Gwich’in people using FirstVoices\.

## Others Involved

Mary Jane Moses, Joel Peter, Jane Montgomery, John Joe Kyikavichik, Brandon Kyikavichik \(Speakers\); Jay Greenway, Frances Bruce, Brandon Kyikavicvhik, Henri Vaillancourt, Bernice Garcia, Jane Montgomery, Linda Driedger \(Recorders\); Martin Holmes, Theresa Levesque, Christine Walkem, Kyle Minnabarriet \(Unnamed contributions\)\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch dates\. As of 2023 this dictionary was last updated early 2022\.

## How People are Cited

Under the *Learn our Language* tab, there are other tabs called *Words* and *Phrases*; within these tabs is where all the recorded words are, and where all others involved are cited \(under the entry metadata\)\.

## How Information is Cited

Some information is cited in the entry metadata, under the *i* icon\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary appears to come directly from speakers and the Gwich’in Cultural Society\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a multimedia website which includes audio\. It is hosted on the FirstVoices website\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Gwich’in to English\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary is specific to the Vuntut Gwich’in First Nation\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional online dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in this dictionary are organized alphabetically, but can also be searched or filtered by letter in Gwich’in\.

Words and phrases are found separately, each within a tab titled the same, under *Learn our Language*\. Each entry contains a recording of the entry in English followed by Gwich’in\. Clicking on the Gwich’in word opens a new page where the same information is displayed, as well as the speaker and recorder who are cited under the *i* icon\.
