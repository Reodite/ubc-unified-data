Siglit Inuvialuit Uqausiita Kipuktirutait: Basic Siglit Inuvialuit Eskimo Dictionary

## Language Name

Siglitun\.

## Alternate Language Names

Inuvialuktun\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Canada\.

## Who

Ronald Lowe\.

## Others Involved

Louis\-Jacques Dorais; Lawrence Osgood; Unnamed Siglit Informants\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1984 by the Committee for Original Peoples Entitlement, Inuvik, Northwest Territories, Canada\.

## How people are cited

People are cited in the *Introduction*\.

## How information is cited

The information in this dictionary is cited in the *Sources* section\.

## Where is information coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers and previous works\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical and digital book\.

## Access

The physical and digital copies of this dictionary are accessible through libraires\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Siglitun to English, English to Siglitun\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of the Siglitun dialect from Tuktoyaktuk, Paulatuk and Sachs Harbour on Banks Island\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are entries organised

The Siglitun to English entries are organized topically by morpheme\. The English to Siglitun section is organized alphabetically by English\.
