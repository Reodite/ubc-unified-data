Mi’gmaq\-Mi’kmaq Online Talking Dictionary

## Language Name

Mi’kmaq\.

## Alternate Language Names

Mi’kmawi’simk, Micmac, Migmaw, Mikmaw, Míkmaq, Míkmaw, Mìgmao, Lnuismk, L’nui’suti\.

## Region

Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, and Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada; Massachusetts and Maine, United States\.

## Who

Mi’kmaq Online\.

## Others Involved

Unnamed Mi’kmaq speakers and Elders\.

## Publishing Information

This is a web\-hosted dictionary that displays no information regarding launch dates or update dates\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not cite any authors or contributors\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers are cited by their initials in the recording links\. Previous dictionaries of Mi’kmaq are cited in entries\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Entries in this dictionary come from previous dictionaries and speakers\. In particular, entries are taken from the *Pacifique Dictionary Manuscript* \(de Valigny\)\. Audio recordings and pronunciation information come from speakers and Elders\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a multimedia website that uses images of previous dictionaries, audio recordings from speakers and Elders, and a search function\.

## Access

This dictionary is available open access online\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Mi’kmaq to English, English to Mi’kmaq\.

## Dialects Included

This is primarily a dictionary of the Listuguj dialect, with some contributions from speakers of the Unama’ki dialect\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is an online multimedia bilingual talking dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries can be viewed alphabetically by Mi’kmaq headword, searched by Mi’kmaq or English, or by categories based on English keywords and grammatical features\. Each entry displays the headword in boldface text, its English definition in plain text, and an audio button\. Clicking on an entry opens its own sub\-page\. Each sub\-page lists the headword, a minimum of three audio files, the English translation and grammatical information, sentence examples, alternate grammatical forms, and the source of the entry in previous dictionaries\.
