Dictionary of Shm’algyack

## Language Name

Coast Tsimshian\.

## Alternate Language Names

sm’álgyax, sm’algya̱x\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Donna May Roberts\.

## Others Involved

Ira Booth, Lilliam Buchert, Frances Duncan, Bernard Guthrie, Solomon Guthrie, Russell Hayward Harold Hudson, and Charles Ryan \(Elders\); Mel Booth, Ruth Booth, John Dalton, Doris Reece, and Conrad Ryan Sr\. \(Contributors\); Administration for Native Americans, Sealaska Corporation \(Funding\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2009 by the Sealaska Heritage Foundation, Juneau, Alaska\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers and contributors are listed in the dictionary’s *Acknowledgements*\.

## How Information is Cited

The source of information is cited in the dictionary’s *Acknowledgements*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book and a digital book\.

## Access

The physical dictionary is available at libraries\. The digital copy of the dictionary is open access, available through the Alaska Native Language Archive and Sealaksa Heritage\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Sm’álgyax to English, English to Sm’álgyax\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary includes language informed by speakers from Metlakatla\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional wordlist introduced by a brief grammar\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Sm’álgyax in the *Sm’álgyax\-English* section \(the alphabetically order is determined on page 6\), and by English in the *English\-Sm’álgyax* section\.

Entries in the *Sm’álgyax\-English* section include the headword, part of speech, translation, and an example sentence\.

Entries in the *English\-Sm’álgyax* section include multiple *Sm’álgyax* equivalent translations which can be referenced in the *Sm’álgyax\-English* section\. No other information is included in the *English\-Sm’álgyax* section\.
