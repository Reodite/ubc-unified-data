Bilingual Dictionary: English\-Chukchansi Chukchansi\-English

## Language Name

Valley Yokuts\.

## Alternate Language Names

Far Northern Valley Yokuts/Delta Yokuts, Northern Valley Yokuts, Southern Valley Yokuts\.

## Region

California, United States\.

## Who

Holly Wyatt, Jane Wyatt \(Language Consultants\); Niken Adisasmito\-Smith \(Compiler\)\.

## Others Involved

Jamie Boley \(Cover Image\); Narayana Kat Adisasmito\-Smith \(Lead Developer\); Brian Agbayani, Charles Ettner, Chris Golston, Fresno State Students in the Field Methods in Linguistics Course \(Assistance with 2016 Edition\)\.

## Publishing Information

The most recent published version of this dictionary is 2023\. It is not clear what edition of this dictionary it is\. Earlier editions include a fifth edition published in 2016 and a third edition in 2011\. We are unable to determine when the first edition was published\. The publisher for all editions is California State University, Fresno, California\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited on the title pages and in the *Brief Note* sections of the 2023 and 2016 editions\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers are cited on the title page and in the *Brief Note* section\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary primarily comes from speakers\. This dictionary project began in 2009, and the primary language consultant throughout its duration has been Holly Wyatt\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a digital book\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access through the Fresno State website\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Northern Valley Yokuts; Northern Valley Yokuts to English\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary includes information from the Chukchansi/Chuk’chans dialect of Northern Valley Yokuts\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is an online, bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organised alphabetically by English in the *English–Chukchansi* section and by Northern Valley Yokuts in the *Chukchansi–English* section\. Entries include the headword \(the *Chukchansi–English* entries have two forms as headwords\), the part of speech, the translation, and two example sentences written in both languages\. In both sections, most entries include two forms of the word in Northern Valley Yokuts: noun and adjective entries include the subject and object forms, and verb entries include the recent past and remote/narrative past forms\. The example sentences each illustrate one of the two included forms\. Many entries also have additional forms listed below\. Noun and adjective entries have forms related to ownership, use, and location\. Verb entries have forms related to tense, commands, suggestions, and hypotheticals\.
