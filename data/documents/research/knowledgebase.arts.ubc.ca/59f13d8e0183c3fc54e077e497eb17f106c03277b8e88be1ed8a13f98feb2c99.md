Working Dictionary of Tübatulabal

## Language Name

Tübatulabal\.

## Alternate Language Names

paka:nil\.

## Region

California, United States\.

## Who

Charles F\. Voegelin\.

## Others Involved

Elsie F\. Dosch \(orthographic conversion\); Glen D\. Turner \(editorial assistance\)\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary was published in 1958 in Volume 24, Number 3 \(the Franz Boas Centennial Volume\) of the International Journal of American Linguistics\.

## How People are Cited

Contributors are cited in the short *Acknowledgements* section\. Speakers and consultants that contributed to this working dictionary are not cited\.

## How Information is Cited

The speakers or other sources used to compile this working vocabulary are not cited\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This working vocabulary was used by Charles F\. Voegelin for reference during fieldwork\. He does not specify where the information included in the dictionary comes from\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as both a physical and digital book\.

## Access

This dictionary is available through libraries as a physical copy, or through JSTOR with institutional login as a PDF\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Tübatulabal to English\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, monodirectional word list\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in the *Vocabulary* are listed alphabetically with Tübatulabal headwords followed by the English gloss in italics\. When more than one Tübatulabal term is relevant, or the Tübatulabal form has a related or variant term, the words are separated by a tilde \(\~\)\. No other information is included in entries\.
