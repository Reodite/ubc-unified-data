A Classified English\-Sechelt Word\-List

## Language Name

Sechelt\.

## Alternate Language Names

she shashishalhem, shíshálh\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Jan Timmers\.

## Others Involved

Cecilia August, Reg Paul \(Language Consultants\); Netherlands Organization for the Advancement of Pure Research \(Funding\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1977, by the Peter de Ridder Press, Lisse \(Netherlands\)\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited by name in the *Preface*\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers are cited in the *Preface*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

## Access

This dictionary is accessible through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Sechelt\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional, topical wordlist\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized topically, as outlined on p\. 3\. Topics include Numerals, Nature, Animals, Fish, Birds, Plants, Body, Spirit/Games, Human Relations, Man\-Made Things, Qualities, Pronouns/Adverbs/Greetings, Inflected \(Grammatical\) Forms, and Actions \(Verbs\)\. Within the Actions \(Verbs\) section, entries are listed alphabetically by English\.

Entries consist of the English headword followed by the Sechelt translation\. No other information is included in entries\.
