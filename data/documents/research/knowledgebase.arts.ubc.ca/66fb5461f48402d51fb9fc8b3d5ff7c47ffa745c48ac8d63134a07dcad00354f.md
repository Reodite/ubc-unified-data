Tuscarora

## Alternate Language Names

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from previously published materials, as listed in the *Introductory Remarks*\. These primarily include grammars, ethnological articles, and various field notes\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as both a physical and digital book\.

## Access

Both versions of this dictionary are accessible through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Tuscarora to English\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Tuscarora\. The Tuscarora head words in these entries are roots, some of which are bound morphemes \(i\.e\., the smallest form of language with meaning that must be joined to another morpheme to create a word\)\. Entries include the Tuscarora root, the English translation, and the root classification \(e\.g\., verb root\)\. Many entries also include the stative, habitual, and punctual aspects alongside the root classification, along with further linguistic or cultural meaning\. Listed below this information are examples from the previously published resources accessed\. These are listed in Tuscarora first, followed by the English translation\. A phonetic pronunciation of the Tuscarora is listed below in square brackets\.

After the dictionary there is an English index which is alphabetized and gives the entry number the English word has been included in\.

In the dictionary’s *Introductory Remarks* section, prior to the dictionary, there are lists of verb paradigms\.
