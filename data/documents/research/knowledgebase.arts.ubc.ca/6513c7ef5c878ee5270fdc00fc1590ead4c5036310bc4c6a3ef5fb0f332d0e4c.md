ᒪᐢᑿᒋᐢ ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐍᐏᐣ – Maskwacis Cree FirstVoices

## Language Name

Cree\-Montagnais\-Naskapi\.

## Alternate Language Names

Cree, Naskapi, Montagnais, ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐍᐏᐣ, Nēhiyawēwin\.

## Region

Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, Newfoundland and Labrador, and Northwest Territories, Canada\.

## Who

First Peoples’ Cultural Council, First People’s Cultural Foundation\.

## Others Involved

This dictionary was built using FirstVoices, from the First Peoples’ Cultural Council\. Others involved such as speakers and language recorders are cited in individual entries\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that does not display any information on launch dates or update dates\. It was published by FirstVoices\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers are cited by name in entries under the *Acknowledgment* header and in audio file metadata\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers are cited in entries\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Entries and audio files in this dictionary come from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a multimedia website on the FirstVoices Archive\. It uses audio files and a filtered search function\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access online\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Cree to English\. It can be searched by Cree or English\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of Plains Cree\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional online dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries can be searched in Cree or English, or browsed alphabetically by Cree syllabics under *Words*, by *Category*, or by *Phrases*\. Each entry is listed by the Cree headword or head phrase in syllabics, followed by the English translation\. When clicked on, a pop\-up appears on the right side of the screen\. Each entry is headed by the Cree word in syllabics, followed by the part of speech and English translation, a clickable audio file with the speaker’s name, and four headers: *Pronunciations*, *Categories*, *Related Content*, and *Acknowledgement*\. *Pronunciations* shows a syllabic breakdown of the word using English/Latin alphabet conventions; *Categories* lists the semantic domains of the word, each hyperlinked to the *Categories* section of the dictionary; *Related Content* shows example phrases; and *Acknowledgement* cites the speaker who contributed the entry\. Not all entries show content in the *Categories* section\.
