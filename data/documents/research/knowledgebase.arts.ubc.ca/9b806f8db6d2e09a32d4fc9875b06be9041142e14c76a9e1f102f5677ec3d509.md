Innu\-English Dictionary and English\-Innu Dictionary

## Language Name

Cree\-Montagnais\-Naskapi\.

## Alternate Language Names

Cree, Naskapi, Montagnais, ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐍᐏᐣ, Nēhiyawēwin, Innu\-aimun\.

## Region

Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, Newfoundland and Labrador, and Northwest Territories, Canada\.

The Innu\-aimun or Montagnais language is spoken in Labrador and Quebec\.

## Who

Marguerite MacKenzie, and José Mailhot\.

## Others Involved

Will Oxford, and Mamu Tshishkutamashutau\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2016 as part of the Algonquian Dictionaries Project\. The Innu\-English Dictionary and English\-Innu Dictionary are published separately\.

The *Dictionnaire innu\-français* and *Dictionnaire français\-innu* by Marie\-Odile Junker and Marguerite MacKenzie seem to be the French editions of these dictionaries\.
