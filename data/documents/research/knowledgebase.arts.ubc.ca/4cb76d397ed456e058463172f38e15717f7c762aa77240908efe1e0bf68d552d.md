Kickapoo Dictionary: Spanish\-Kickapoo

## Language Name

Sauk\-Fox\-Kickapoo\.

## Alternate Language Names

Fox, Mesquakie, Meskwaki, Mesquakie\-Sauk, Mesquakie\-Sauk\-Kickapoo, Sauk\-Fox, Sac and Fox, Meshkwahkihaki, Thâkiwâtowêweni, Kikapú\.

## Region

Oklahoma, Iowa, and Kansas, United States; Coahuila, Mexico\.

## Who

Dr\. Mike King\.

## Others Involved

Unnamed language consultants; Edward S\. Wallace \(Copier\); UC Berkeley Archives \(Holding\)\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary was compiled in 1957 and copied in 2001\. It was never formally published\.
