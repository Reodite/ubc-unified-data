Nuxalk FirstVoices

## Language Name

Nuxalk\.

## Alternate Language Names

Bella Coola, Nuxalkmc\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Nuxalk First Nation\.

## Others Involved

This dictionary was built using FirstVoices, from the First People’s Cultural Council\. Others involved such as speakers and language recorders are cited in individual entries\.

## Publishing Information

As of late 2023, the most recent update to the dictionary was in August 2023\. There is no information on when this dictionary was launched\. This dictionary is published on the FirstVoices platform\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers are cited by name under the metadata tab for each entry where appropriate \(some speakers/sources are simply listed as ‘Nuxalk Elder’\) and in the information section of each audio file\.

## How Information is Cited

The sources of each entry is cited under the metadata tab\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers \(cited for each entry in the metadata tab\)\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a multimedia website, and includes images and audio\. It is built using the FirstVoices platform\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Nuxalk to English\. The search function works in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, online, multimedia dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Nuxalk by default, and can be filtered by first letter\. When using the search feature, entries are organized by relevance\.

Entries can also be sorted by categories or part of speech\.

Each entry includes the Nuxalk entry, and the English translation\. Most entries also include an audio recording of the Nuxalk entry\.
