A Short Practical Dictionary of the Gitksan Language

## Language Name

Gitxsan\.

## Alternate Language Names

Gitksan, Gitxsanimaax, Gitikshan, Gityskyan, Giklsan\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Lonnie Hindle; Bruce Rigsby\.

## Others Involved

Chris Harris, Jonathan Johnson, Sarah Hindle, Wallace Morgan, and Johnson Williams \(Speakers\); Sarah and Bernard Hindle \(Financial Contribution\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1973\. Reprinted from Northwest Anthropological Research Notes, Vol\. 7, No\. 1\., Moscow, Idaho\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers and contributors are cited in the *Co\-author Acknowledgement* section at the beginning of the dictionary\.

## How Information is Cited

Information is cited in the *Co\-author Acknowledgement* section at the beginning of the dictionary\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book and digital book\.

## Access

The physical dictionary is available through libraries\. The digital copy of the dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Gitxsan\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary included the dialects spoken in Hazelton and Kispiox\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, monodirectional wordlist, introduced by a brief grammar\. It is followed by several appendixes\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by English\. Entries include the English headword, Gitxsan translation, and the part of speech\.

Information in the appendixes is sorted topically according to numbers, pronominals, and kinship terms\.
