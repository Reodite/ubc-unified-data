Southern Tutchone FirstVoices

## Language Name

Southern Tutchone\.

## Alternate Language Names

Dän kʼè\.

## Region

Yukon, Canada\.

## Who

This dictionary was created by the Kwanlin Dün First Nation using FirstVoices\.

## Others Involved

Most entries cite Gerald Dickson as the speaker, Sophia Smith as the recorder, and Chris Harvey as the keyboard creator\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch dates\. As of 2023 this dictionary was last updated early 2022\.

## How People are Cited

Chris Harvey is cited under *Our Keyboards* in the *Learn our Language* tab\. Under the *Learn our Language* tab, there is another tab called *Words* and *Phrases*; within these tabs is where all the recorded words are, and where Gerald Dickson and Sophia Smith are cited \(under the entry metadata\)\.

## How Information is Cited

Some information is cited in the entry metadata, under the *i* icon\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary appears to come directly from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a multimedia website which includes audio\. It is hosted on the FirstVoices website\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Southern Tutchone to English\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary is specific to the Kwanlin Dün First Nation\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in this dictionary are organized alphabetically, but can also be searched or filtered by letter in Southern Tutchone\.

Words and phrases are found separately, each within a tab titled the same, under *Learn our Language*\. Each entry contains a recording of the entry in English followed by Southern Tutchone\. Clicking on the Southern Tutchone word opens a new page where the same information is displayed, as well as the speaker and recorder who are cited under the *i* icon\.

## Other Notes

This dictionary is primarily phrases; the *Words* tab only includes 5 entries\. In the home page, the *Photo Gallery* and *Kids Portal* \(except for *Games*\) do not include any content\. Furthermore, within the *Learn our Language* tab, the *Songs* and *Stories* tabs do not contain any content\.

Under the *Learn our Language* tab, there are keyboard installation guides for all types of software\.
