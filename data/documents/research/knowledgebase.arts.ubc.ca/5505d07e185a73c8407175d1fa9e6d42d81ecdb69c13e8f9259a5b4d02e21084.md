Cherokee Nation Word List Online

## Language Name

Cherokee\.

## Alternate Language Names

Tsalagi \(ᏣᎳᎩ\), ᏣᎳᎩ ᎦᏬᏂᎯᏍᏗ, Tsalagi Gawonihisdi, Giduwa\.

## Region

North Carolina, Alabama, Oklahoma, and California, United States\.

## Who

The Cherokee Nation\.

## Others Involved

This dictionary doesn’t provide any information on others who may have been involved\.

## Publishing Information

This online dictionary does not display any launch or update dates\. It is hosted by the Cherokee Nation website on the *Language Department* page\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not cite any authors or contributors\.

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary does not cite the source of its information\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary does not indicate where information is coming from or what sources were used\.

The Cherokee Language Consortium is hyperlinked in the description of the dictionary, which may imply that some of the information included in this dictionary is from the online word list\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is an online dictionary with a search function\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access online through the Cherokee Nation’s website on the *Language Department* page\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Cherokee\. The search function works in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\. Based on the reference to the *Cherokee Language Consortium*, this dictionary may include information from both the Western \(i\.e\., Overhill, Otali, or ᎣᏔᎵ\) and Eastern \(i\.e\., Middle, Kituwah, or ᎩᏚᏩ\) dialects\. Dialects are not marked within entries\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional, online word list\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are not listed on the website\. Rather, users can access entries through the search function, which can be searched in either English or the romanized representation of the Cherokee\. When a word is searched, the entry includes the English head word, the romanized representation \(called the phonetic form\) of the Cherokee, the syllabary version, and an audio file of the Cherokee\. If there is more than one entry associated with the searched word, entries are listed alphabetically by English\. No other information is included in entries\.
