Kaskaskia Illinois\-to\-French Dictionary

## Language Name

Miami\-Illinois\.

## Alternate Language Names

Myaamia\.

## Region

Illinois, Indiana, Ohio, Kansas, Michigan, and Oklahoma, United States\.

## Who

Carl Masthay \(Editor\)\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary was published in 2002\. The original manuscript was compiled sometime in the mid to late 1600s\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary is an edited reprint of a manuscript that was created around 1630\-1640\. It is estimated to have been transcribed by either Gabriel Marest or René Tartarin during a French missionary expedition\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

## Access

This dictionary is available through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Miami\-Illinois to French; French to Miami\-Illinois\.

## Dialects Included

Based on information in a review by Willem F\. H Adelaar, this is a dictionary of Illinois\.

## Type of Dictionary

Based on information in a review by Willem F\. H Adelaar, this is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.
