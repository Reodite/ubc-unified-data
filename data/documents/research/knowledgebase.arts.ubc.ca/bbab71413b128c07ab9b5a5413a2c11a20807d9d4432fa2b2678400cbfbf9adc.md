Yana Dictionary

## Language Name

Yana\.

## Alternate Language Names

Yanan\.

## Region

California, United States\.

## Who

Edward Sapir, Morris Swadesh \(Compilers\)\.

## Others Involved

Mary Haas \(Editor\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published in 1960, by University of California Press, Berkeley\.

According to the excerpt of a review by Stanley Newman available on the University of Chicago Press Journal website, the raw notes for this dictionary were collected by Edward Sapir, and then organized and compiled into a dictionary by Morris Swadesh, which was then edited by Mary Haas\.
