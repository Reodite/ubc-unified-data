Gwich’in to English Dictionary

## Language Name

Gwich’in\.

## Alternate Language Names

Gwitch’in, Kutchin, Kootchin, Loucheux, Loucheaux, Takudh, Tukudh\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Yukon, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Clarence Alexander, Virginia “Ginny” Alexander \(Compilers\)\.

## Others Involved

Richard Mueller, Shandaa, Neerihinjik, Clarence Alexander \(Consultants\); Ed Alexander \(Sold copies of the dictionary\)\.

We are unable to access information about others who may have been involved in creation of this dictionary\.

## Publishing Information

Self\-published in 2005 by Clarence Alexander and Virginia Alexander, with revisions made in 2008 and 2011\. Each revised edition is longer than the edition which came before it\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from various other Gwich’in resources, including *Dinjii Zhuu Ginjik Nagwan Tr’iltsaii: Gwich’in Junior Dictionary* \(1979\) compiled by Katherine Peter of the Alaska Native Language Center, notes from Lillian Garnet’s and Marily Savage’s classes, and from Richard Mueller, Shandaa, Neerihinjik and Clarence Alexander\.

## Other Notes

Out of the 4 editions the Alaska Native Language Archive has catalogued, only the second 2005 post and the 2011 edition have a file linked in the post\. However, neither are the actual dictionary\. The 2005 post has a scanned page on *Dictionary sales* which describes Ed\. Alexander \(Clarence and Virginia Alexander’s son\) selling the self\-published dictionary Carlson Center in Fairbanks, Alaska\. As of 2023, the file link in the 2011 post on ANLA is a mis\-linked music cataloging file and is not the dictionary\.

In the *Dictionary sales* file linked on ANLA Ed Alexander is quoted saying that for the last five years or more his parents would “work on \[the dictionary\] every morning between 6 and 8am, except for weekends”\.
