Stó:lō Shxwelí

## Language Name

Halkomelem\.

## Alternate Language Names

Halq̓eméylem, Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓, hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada; Washington, United States\.

## Who

Stó:lō Shxwelí Halq’eméylem Language Program\.

## Others Involved

Others involved, such as speakers, are cited in individual entries\.

Each member \(current and past\) of the Stó:lō Shxwelí Halq’eméylem Language Program is highlighted on the *Meet Our Team* page of the website\.

First Peoples’ Cultural Council \(funding support\)\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch or update dates\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers are cited for each audio file\. The citation information appears when hovering over the ‘play’ icon in the dictionary entry\.

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary does not cite the source of all of its information\. However, cited information offers links to the previously published resources it was gathered from within the specific entry\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary does not indicate where all of the information is coming from\. However, cited information in this dictionary is often attributed to Galloway’s *Dictionary of Upriver Halkomelem* \(2009\) and Stó:lō First Nation’s *Halq’eméylem FirstVoices* site\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a multimedia website that includes audio\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Halkomelem to English\. The search function works in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of the Upriver dialect of Halkomelem, specifically the dialect of the Stó:lō people\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, online, multimedia dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Upon entering the dictionary portion of the website, several words are suggested at random\. Users then have the option to search for specific words in Halkomelem or English using the search bar\. Once a user has entered at least three letters into the search bar, a drop down menu of suggested words will appear below the search bar\.

There is no option to browse all words in the dictionary\.

## Other Notes

This dictionary is part of a multimedia website that includes other language materials, including videos, digital storybooks, lesson plans, songs, audio recordings, and other digital resources\. The website was created by the Stó:lō Shxwelí Halq’eméylem Language Program, which functions to support Halkomelem language teachers by providing curriculum, resources, and methodologies for teaching languages\. This dictionary, therefore, seems to be intended for speakers who already have intermediate knowledge or beyond of Halkomelem\.
