A Navajo/English Bilingual Dictionary: Áłchíní Bi Naaltsoostsoh

## Language Name

Navajo\.

## Alternate Language Names

Navaho, Diné bizaad, Naabeehó bizaad\.

## Country, State/Province

Arizona, Colorado, New Mexico, Utah, United States\.

## Who

Alyse Neundorf\.

## Others Involved

University of New Mexico Press and Ramah Navajo School Board\. We are unable to access information about others who may have been involved in creation of this dictionary\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2005, University of New Mexico, Albuquerque\. The eBook was published in 2015\. Originally published in 1983 as part of a Ramah Navajo School Board, Inc\.

## How People are Cited

We are unable to access information about how people are cited in this dictionary\.

## How Information is Cited

Reference to the 1983 edition is included on the University of New Mexico Press listing for the dictionary\. We are unable to access information about how information is cited in this dictionary\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary is was originally published in 1983 as part of a Ramah Navajo School Board, Inc\. initiative to develop bilingual education materials in the Navajo language\. We are unable to access this dictionary to determine other details on where the information is coming from, such as the names of speakers or consultants\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as physical book or a digital book \(eBook\)\.

## Access

This dictionary is available through libraries as a physical book, and can be purchased through the University of New Mexico Press as either a physical book \($85 USD\) or a digital book \($85 USD\)\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Navajo to English \(main body\), English to Navajo \(index\)\.

## Dialects Included

We are unable to access this dictionary to determine which dialects are included\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional, dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in the main body of the dictionary are organized alphabetically by the thirty\-six letter Navajo alphabet\. Within each letter of the alphabet nouns are listed first and are followed by nouns\.
