Tagish FirstVoices

## Language Name

Tagish\.

## Alternate Language Names

Gunana, Nahane, Nahani, Si\-him\-E\-na, Tagisch, Tahgish, Tahkeesh, Tahk\-heesh\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, and Yukon, Canada\.

## Who

This dictionary was created by the Carcross\-Tagish First Nation using FirstVoices, from the First Peoples’ Cultural Council\.

## Others Involved

Lucy Wren, Norman James, Clara Schinkel \(Consultance and guidance\); Pat Moore \(Linguistic consultant\); John Elliot, Elmer Derrick, Tracey Herbert, Tamara Knott, Deanna Daniels \(Development team\); Peter Brand, Alex Wadsworth, Ivy Charleson \(Friends and support\); Karyn Armour, Cheryl Mclean, Jeannette Poyton, Violet Clethroe, Tina Jules \(Yukon Government officials\); Brian Shanahan \(Carcross Community School representative\); Sophia Smith \(Unnamed contributions\); Lucy Wren, Clara Schinkel \(Speakers\); Angela Sydney, Dora Wedge, Daisy Smith, Clara Schinkel, Ida Calmagne \(Tagish revitalization efforts\)\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch dates\.

## How People are Cited

Most people are cited under the *Our People* tab which is found under the *About* main tab\. Some contributors have personal introductions but most are cited under the *Acknowledgements* and in individual entries\.

## How Information is Cited

The source of the entry is cited in the entry *Acknowledgements*, which is within in each entry\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary appears to come directly from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a multimedia website which includes audio\. It is hosted on the FirstVoices platform\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Tagish to English\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary is specific to the Carcross\-Tagish First Nation\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional online dictionary\. It is searchable in both directions\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in this dictionary are organized alphabetically, but can also be searched or filtered by letter in Tagish\.

Words and phrases are found separately, each within a tab titled the same, under *Dictionary*\. Each entry contains a recording of the word in English followed by Tagish\. Clicking on the Tagish word opens a new page where the same information is displayed, as well as the category the word belongs to \(i\.e\. *Numbers*\), and speaker and source who are cited under the speaker icon and *Acknowledgements*\.

There are 44 words and 477 phrases included in this dictionary\. There is no content in the *Songs, Stories* or *Games* tabs, all found within the *Learn* tab\.

It is specified that the Beta Version of FirstVoices is used, but it is unclear what the difference is between the regular version of the website\.
