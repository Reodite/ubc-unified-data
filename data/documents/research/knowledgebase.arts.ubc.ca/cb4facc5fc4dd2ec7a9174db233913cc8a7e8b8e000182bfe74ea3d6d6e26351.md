English Aleut Dictionary Online

## Language Name

Aleut\.

## Alternate Language Names

Unangam Tunuu\.

## Region

Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Authors of this dictionary are unknown, and are not identified on the Glosbe website\. Glosbe is an online dictionary site which draws its data from other websites, and allows anyone to contribute to it anonymously\. The entries on this website have not been verified\.

## Others Involved

Glosbe, Wiktionary Project; Jerzy Kazojc project; FreeDict project; Open Subtitles; The DGT Multilingual Translation Memory of the Acquis Communautaire; OmegaWiki; Tatoeba; Volunteer contributors\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary appears to have been created on Glosbe in February 2019, and has been added to since then\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not cite any authors or contributors\.

## How Information is Cited

The website where entry information is pulled from is shown beside translation\. Most of the Aleut words in this resource come from the Aleut Wiktionary page:

\([https://en\.wiktionary\.org/wiki/Category:Aleut\_language](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Aleut_language)\)\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from websites, anonymous volunteer contributors, and other dictionaries on the website\. It is important to note that there is no verification process for entries on this website\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a website with a built\-in search function, but no multimedia elements\. The Glosbe website was used to create this dictionary\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Aleut, Aleut to English\. The search language can be reversed on the home page\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Each entry must be searched for, and there is no way to browse existing entries\.
