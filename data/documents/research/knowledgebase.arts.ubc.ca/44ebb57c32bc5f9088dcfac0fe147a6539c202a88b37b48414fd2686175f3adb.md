Cowlitz Coast Salish Dictionary App

## Language Name

Cowlitz\.

## Alternate Language Names

ƛʼpúlmixq\.

## Region

Washington, United States\.

## Who

The Language Conservancy, and the Cowlitz Indian Tribe\.

## Others Involved

There is no information included in this resource about others involved\. There may be more information available once the app is downloaded\.

## Publishing Information

This app was released February 2022, and is only available on Google Play\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not appear to cite any authors or contributors\. There may be more information available once the app is downloaded\. The Cowlitz Indian Tribe is recognized on the The Language Conservancy but not on the app’s download page\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a mobile app published on Google Play by the The Language Conservancy\.

## Access

This dictionary is free and open access\. The app can only be accessed through Android mobile devices due to it being published on Google Play\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Cowlitz to English, English to Cowlitz\. The dictionary is searchable in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is bilingual, bidirectional, mobile app dictionary\.
