A Short Bella Coola Dictionary

## Language Name

Nuxalk\.

## Alternate Language Names

Bella Coola, Nuxalkmc\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Hank F\. Nater\.

## Others Involved

Margaret Siwallace, Felicity Walkus, Andy Schooner Sr\., Hank King \(Speakers\); Bella Coola Band \(Field Support\)\.

## Publishing Information

There is no information available regarding a specific publication date or publisher\. It is likely to have been published sometime in the 1970s\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited in the *Preface*\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers are cited in the *Preface*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Entries in this dictionary come from work with speakers during the author’s field work\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

## Access

This dictionary is available through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Nuxalk to English, English to Nuxalk\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Nuxalk in *Part A: Bella Coola–English* according to the alphabet included on pg\. 1\. Entries in *Part B: English–Bella Coola* are organized alphabetically by English\. Entries include the headword in Nuxalk or English and the translation in the other language, depending on the section\.

## Other Notes

This dictionary is not comprehensive of the Nuxalk language\. It was created to accompany *Learning the Bella Coola Language* by Hank Nater \(seemingly published at the same time as this dictionary\)\. Therefore, this dictionary only covers the vocabulary taught in the perviously mentioned resource\.
