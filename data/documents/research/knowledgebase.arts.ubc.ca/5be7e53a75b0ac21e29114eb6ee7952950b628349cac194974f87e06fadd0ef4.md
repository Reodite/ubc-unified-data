The Milluk Language: Ghaala

## Language Name

Miluk

## Alternate Language Names

Lower Coquille, Milluk\.

## Region

Oregon, United States\.

## Who

Patty Whereat \(Compiler\)\.

## Others Involved

Lolly \(Lorry\) Hotchkiss Metcalf, Daisy Wasson Codding, Annie Miner Peterson \(Speakers\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2001\. It appears to be self\-published\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited in the *A Note About the Milluk Coos Language*\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers who worked with previous language documenters are cited in the *A Note About the Milluk Coos Language*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from previously documented language from speakers throughout the beginning of the twentieth century\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a digital book \(PDF\)\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access through the Way Back Machine, a website which records past renditions of websites\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Miluk

## Dialects Included

This dictionary does not specify a dialect\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional, topical word list\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized topically\. Topics include body parts, animals, plants, and many others\. Within topics, entries are organized alphabetically by English and include the English headword and the Miluk translation\. Some entries include related English terms, an English gloss, or cultural notes pertaining to the entry\.

The dictionary is preceded by a Miluk sound system\.
