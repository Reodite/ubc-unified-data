New Lakota Dictionary: Lakȟótiyapi\-English/English\-Lakȟótiyapi

## Language Name

Lakota\.

## Alternate Language Names

Lakȟótiyapi, Lakhota, Teton, Teton Sioux\.

## Region

Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, and Ontario, Canada; Montana, Wyoming, Colorado, North Dakota, South Dakota, Nebraska, Kansas, Oklahoma, Texas, Minnesota, and Iowa, United States\.

## Who

Jan Ullrich \(Author\)\.

## Others Involved

Over 300 speakers, Elders, and consultants, as well as other collaborators, are cited in this dictionary\.  

## Publishing Information

First edition published 2008\. A second edition was printed in 2011 and a third edition in 2016\. All editions have been published by Lakota Language Consortium\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited on the publication page and in the *Acknowledgements*\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers are cited in the *Acknowledgements* section\. Previous publications are cited in the *Bibliography*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from speakers who have worked with language collection initiatives from 1992 to the dictionary’s first publication in 2008\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

It is also available as a desktop software under the title *New Lakota Dictionary – Pro* and a mobile app under the title *New Lakota Dictionary App*\.

## Access

This physical dictionary can be purchased for US$49\.95 from the online LLC Bookstore\. It is also accessible through libraries\.

The desktop software can be also purchased from the online LLC Bookstore for US$29\.95\. The mobile app is free and open access on iTunes and the Google Play store\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Lakota to English; English to Lakota\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organised alphabetically by Lakota in the first section *Lakȟótiyapi–English \(Lakota–English\)*\. Entries include the Lakota headword, the part of speech, the English translation \(or description where a one\-to\-one translation is not possible\), variations in spelling, and cross references\. Many entries also include an example phrase\. Affix entries contain example words that use the head affix\. Entries are organised alphabetically by English in the *English–Lakȟótiyapi \(English–Lakota\)* section\. Entries include the English headword, the Lakota translation, and the part of speech\. Entries with more than one Lakota translation are listed numerically and include an English definition/description for each word\. Some entries also include variational spelling\.

Between the two dictionary sections there is a detailed grammar that includes information about Lakota orthography and alphabet, phonology, verbs, nouns, pronouns, determiners, postpositions, conjunctions, “T\-words” \(i\.e\., words in Lakota that start with ‘tu\-‘ or ‘to\-‘\), enclitics, adverbs, interjections, sentence structure, changes in fast speech, and changes as the language modernizes\.

Prior to the dictionary sections, there is an introduction to the Lakota language that includes a history of lexicography with Lakota\.
