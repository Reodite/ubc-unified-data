Snchitsu’umshtsn–The Coeur d’Alene Language: A Modern Course

## Language Name

Coeur d’Alene\.

## Alternate Language Names

Snchitsu’umshtsn\.

## Region

Idaho, United States\.

## Who

Lawrence G\. Nicodemus \(Speaker and Creator\)\.

## Others Involved

Bernard J\. LaSarte \(Preface\); Noel Campbell \(Tribal Liason\); Lawrence Aripa, Gary LaSarte \(Illustrations\); Edward A\. Tennant \(Design and Production\); Joseph N\. Bitar \(Orthography and Transcription\); Dr\. Dave Warren \(Support\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1975, the Coeur d’Alene Tribe\.

The digitized version is hosted on the Plateau Peoples’ Web Portal, a website published by Washington State University, National Endowment of the Humanities, NorthWest Coast Academic Computing Consortium, and American Council of Learned Societies, using the Mukurtu content management system\. The host website does not display any information on when it was launched or updated\. There is no information on when this resource was digitally archived or uploaded to the website\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited in the *Preface* and *Introduction* of the textbook\.

## How Information is Cited

Information is cited in the *Preface* and *Introduction* of the textbook\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this resource comes from speakers, specifically the course creator, Lawrence Nicodemus\.

## Tools and Framework used

This resource is a language course textbook, available as a physical book and an ebook\. The ebook has accompanying audio files, and is hosted on the Plateau Peoples’ Web Portal, a multimedia digital repository which hosts a range of digitally archived items\. The website was created using the open\-source content management system, Mukurtu\.

## Access

The physical book is available through libraries\. The ebook is open access through the Plateau Peoples’ Web Portal\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Coeur d’Alene to English, English to Coeur d’Alene\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this resource\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual language course textbook\. Word lists included at the end of each lesson are mono\-directional and thematic in content\. The *Glossary* section is a bidirectional word list\.

## How are Entries Organised

The language lessons, which account for most of the textbook, are organised thematically\. The lessons begin very simple, supplying learners with concrete, everyday objects and phrases to help with speaking Coeur d’Alene\. The lessons gradually increase in difficulty, introducing new sentence patterns and forms\. The word lists included at the end of each lesson, while not organised in any particular way, are topical in content, containing terms introduced in the corresponding lesson\.

Entries in the *Glossary* section of the course book \(pages 125–140\) are organised alphabetically by Coeur d’Alene in the *Coeur d’Alene—English* section, and alphabetically by English in the *English—Coeur d’Alene section*\. The glossary entries include the Coeur d’Alene or English headword \(depending on the direction of the section\) and the translation\.
