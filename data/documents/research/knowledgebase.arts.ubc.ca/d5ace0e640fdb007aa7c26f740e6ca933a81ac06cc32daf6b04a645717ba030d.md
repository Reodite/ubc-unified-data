L’nui’suti

## Language Name

Mi’kmaq\.

## Alternate Language Names

Mi’kmawi’simk, Micmac, Migmaw, Mikmaw, Míkmaq, Míkmaw, Mìgmao, Lnuismk, L’nui’suti\.

## Region

Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, and Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada; Massachusetts and Maine, United States\.

## Who

Mi’kmaw Kina’matnewey\.

## Others Involved

Mi’kmaq Kina’matnewey Elders Advisory Committee \(Elders\); Alwyn Jeddore \(Narration\); Faye Googoo, J\.R\. Isadore \(Programming and Layout\); Gerald Gloade, J\.R\. Isadore, Fox Thomas \(Illustrations\); James Foulds \(Audio Recording\); Yolanda Denny, Blaire Gould, Jane Meader \(Contributors\); Atlantic First Nation Tech Services, Confederacy of Mainland Mi’kmaq, Mi’kmaq Kina’matnewey, Mi’kmaq Language Advisory Committee, Mi’kmaqwey Debert Cultural Centre, Ogoki Learning Systems \(Support\)\.

## Publishing Information

This dictionary was first released on both the Apple Store and Google Play in 2015\. It appears to have been most recently updated on Google Play in 2023, and on the Apple Store in 2019\.

## How People are Cited

Contributors are cited by name in the *Credits*\.

## How Information is Cited

People are cited in the *Credits*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Entries in this dictionary and their final translations come from speakers and are reviewed by the Mi’kmaq Kina’matnewey Elder’s Advisory Committee\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a mobile phone application \(app\)\.

## Access

This dictionary is free and open access on both the Apple Store and Google Play\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Mi’kmaq\.

## Dialects Included

This is primarily a dictionary of the Unama’ki \(Cape Breton\) dialect\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional word list with audio and images\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in this dictionary can be viewed on the *Search* tab or the *Category* tab\. Under *Search*, entries can be browsed alphabetically by English headword or searched in both Mi’kmaq and English\. Under *Category*, entries are organised by semantic domain, including *Animals*, *Body*, *Colours*, and more\. Entries are organised alphabetically within categories\. Each entry contains the English headword, followed by the Mi’kmaq word or words, a button to play the audio, and in some entries, an image to illustrate the meaning\.
