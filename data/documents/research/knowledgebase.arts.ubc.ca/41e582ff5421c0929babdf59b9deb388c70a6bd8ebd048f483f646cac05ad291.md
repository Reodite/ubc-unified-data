Basic Kangiruarmiut Eskimo Dictionary \= Kangiryuarmiut uqauhingita numiktittitdjutingit

## Language Name

Inuktitut\.

## Alternate Language Names

Inuttitut, Inuktut\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Nunatsiavut \(Newfoundland and Labrador\), Nunavik \(Quebec\), Nunavut, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Ronald Lowe \(Author\)\.

## Others Involved

Committee for Original Peoples’ Entitlement \(Publisher\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 1983, Committee for Original Peoples’ Entitlement, Inuvik, Northwest Territories\.

## How people are cited

People are cited in the *Foreword* and *Introduction\.*

## How information is cited

We are unable to access this dictionary to determine how, or where source materials are cited\.

## Where is information coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from consultants of Holman on Victoria Island, Northwest Territories\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

## Access

The physical copy of this dictionary is accessible through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Inuinnaqtun to English, and included a English to Inuinnaqtun section\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of the Inuinnaqtun and Kangiryuarmiut dialects\. The information in the dictionary comes from speakers from Holman on Victoria Island, Northwest Territories\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual dictionary\.

## How are entries organised

Entries are organised topically\. Within each section entries are organized alphabetically by morpheme\. There is an English section which is organized alphabetically\.
