## Other Notes

This resource is no longer listed on Yinka Dene Language Institute’s website and appears to be inaccessible\.

According to the reference on WorldCat, the hardware requirements for using this resource are quite specific\. Users would require a Pioneer 2200 laserdisc player, a Pioneer UC\-V104BC barcode reader, a colour monitor or TV set, a barcode reader to videodisc player cable, and a RF or A/V cable to connect the videodisc player and monitor to use this resource\.
