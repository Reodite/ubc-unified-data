Assiniboine Dictionary

## Language Name

Assiniboine\.

## Alternate Language Names

Assiniboin, Hohe, Nakota, Nakoda, Nakon, Nakona, Stoney\.

## Region

Saskatchewan, Canada; Montana, United States\.

## Who

Jerome Fourstar\.

## Others Involved

We are unable to access information about others who may have been involved in the creation of this dictionary\.

## Publishing Information

Details on publication are not clear for this dictionary\. Linda A Cumberland’s PhD, *A Grammar of Assiniboine: A Siouan Language of the Northern Plains* \(2005\), cites this dictionary as being completed in 1978 in Wolf Point, Montana but left unpublished\. Vincent Collette and Wilma Kennedy’s *A Concise Dictionary of Nakoda \(Assiniboine\)* \(2003\) and the WorldCat description identify this dictionary as having been self\-published in 1978\. We are unable to determine by whom it was published or if this dictionary was ever formally published\.
