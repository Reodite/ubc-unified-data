Gwich’in Wordlist 1962

## Language Name

Gwich’in\.

## Alternate Language Names

Gwitch’in, Kutchin, Kootchin, Loucheux, Loucheaux, Takudh, Tukudh\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Yukon, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Dorothy John, Ezias James \(Authors\)\.

## Others Involved

Elliott Canonge \(Unnamed contributions at the Summer Institute of Linguistics\); Michael Krauss \(Unnamed contributions at the University of Alaska\)\.

Dorothy John and Eliza James \(Speakers\)\.

## Publishing Information

This appears to be an unpublished wordlist from February 24th 1962\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited on the top of the first two pages of the wordlist\.

## How Information is Cited

The information in this wordlist is cited on the top of the first two pages\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this wordlist comes from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This wordlist is available as a digital book\. This is a typescript and handwritten wordlist\.

## Access

This wordlist is open access through the Alaska Native Language Archives from the University of Alaska Fairbanks\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Gwich’in\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary is of the Kuchin dialect from Arctic Village\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional wordlist\.

## How are Entries Organised

The entries in this wordlist do not seem to have a particular method of organization\. The English headwords are typed while the Gwich’in translations are handwritten straight across\.

## Other Notes

This is an unpublished wordlist containing only 46 entries\. It is addressed to Michael Krauss at the University of Alaska, although it is unclear what the purpose of the wordlist was\.

The entries are written as semi\-phonetic transcriptions\.
