Haida Verb Dictionary

## Language Name

Haida\.

## Alternate Language Names

X̱aat Kíl, X̱aadas Kíl, X̱aayda Kil, Xaad kil\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Erma Lawrence \(Compiler\)\.

## Others Involved

Jack O’steen, Trisha McConell \(Technical Consultation\); Alaska Native Language Center, University of Alaska, for the Ketchikan Haida Society\. \(Financial and Technical Support\); The Writers Workshop Project; National Endowment for the Humanities; and the Ketchikan Alaska Gateway Borough School District Indian Education Program\.

## Publishing Information

The publication date for this dictionary is unclear\. The *Haida Dictionary* \(1977\) also compiled by Erma Lawrence, indicates that the verb dictionary was published in 1973, while the Alaska Native Language Archive has a draft of the verb dictionary dated 1975\.

The version looked at here is the draft from Alaska Native Language Archive\.

## How People are Cited

Contributors are cited by name in the handwritten text on the first page\.

## How Information is Cited

The source of information is not clearly cited in this dictionary\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Although the source of information is not clearly cited in this dictionary, the words included in this dictionary likely come directly from the compiler, Erma Lawrence\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a PDF scan of a typed document with handwritten notes, and is available a digital book\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access through Alaska Native Language Archive\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Haida to English\.

## Dialects Included

This is a dictionary of Alaskan Haida\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono directional, verb dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Haida, followed by three forms of each verb, and an example sentence in both English and Haida\. English translation are provided for the headword and the example sentence, but are not included for the additional forms for the verb\.

Information from this dictionary, along with the *Haida Noun Dictionary* \(1972/1975\) was used to compile the *Haida Dictionary* \(1977\)\.

Two pages of handwritten notes have been added to the beginning of the scan of the draft on ANLA\.
