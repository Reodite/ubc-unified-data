Dictionnaire esquimau\-français du parler de l’Ungava et contrées limitrophes

## Language Name

Inuktitut\.

## Alternate Language Names

Inuttitut, Inuktut\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Nunatsiavut \(Newfoundland and Labrador\), Nunavik \(Quebec\), Nunavut, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Lucien Schneider\.

## Others Involved

Les Presses de l’Université Laval \(Publisher\)\.

## Publishing Information

The first edition was published in 1966 by Les Presses de l’Université Laval, Québec\. A revised version of the French edition was published by the same publisher in 1970\. Editions of the English version, *Ulirnaisigutiit : an Inuktitut\-English dictionary of Northern Quebec, Labrador, and Eastern Arctic dialects \(with an English\-Inuktitut index\)* were published in in the following years in 1985 and 2014\.
