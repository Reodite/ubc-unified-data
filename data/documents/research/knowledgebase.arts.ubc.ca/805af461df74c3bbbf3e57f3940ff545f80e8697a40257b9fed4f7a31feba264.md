Eskimo English English Eskimo Inuktitut Dictionary

## Language Name

Inuktitut\.

## Alternate Language Names

Inuttitut, Inuktut\.

## Region

Northwest Territories, Nunatsiavut \(Newfoundland and Labrador\), Nunavik \(Quebec\), Nunavut, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Arthur Thibert\.

## Others Involved

Research Center for Amerindian Anthropology \(Publisher for the 1958 edition\); Hippocrene Books \(Publisher for the 2004 edition\)\.

## Publishing Information

The first edition was published in 1954 by the Research Center for Amerindian Anthropology, University of Ottawa, Ottawa\. Multiple revised editions were published after 1954 by other publishers, with the most recent revised edition from either 2004 or 2005\.

## Other Notes

This dictionary was originally published as *Dictionary, English\-Eskimo, Eskimo\-English\.* According to WorldCat, the dictionary has multiple editions\.

One reviewer of the dictionary notes that this is actually Greenland dialect, and another states that it is a reprint of a 40\+ year old missionary work\.
