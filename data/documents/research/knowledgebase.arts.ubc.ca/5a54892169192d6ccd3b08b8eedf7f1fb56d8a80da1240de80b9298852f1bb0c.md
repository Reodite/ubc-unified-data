Haisla FirstVoices

## Language Name

Haisla\.

## Alternate Language Names

X̄a’islak̓ala, X̌àh̓isl̩ak̓ala\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Haisla Nation\.

## Others Involved

This dictionary was built using FirstVoices, from the First People’s Cultural Council\.

## Publishing Information

As of early 2023, the most recent update to the dictionary was in March 2022\. There is no information on when this dictionary was launched\. This dictionary is published on the FirstVoices platform\.

## How People are Cited

Most entries do not cite speakers in the entry metadata or in the audio information\. Some entries cite speakers initials in the entry metadata\. This dictionary does not cite or acknowledge any other contributors\.

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary does not cite the source of any of its information\. The FirstVoices platform allows communities to add citation data in the metadata tab of entries\. Most entries do not have any metadata\. A few entries cite speakers with their initials\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary does not indicate where information is coming from or what sources were used\. Typically entries on the FirstVoices platform come directly from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a multimedia website and includes images and audio\. It is built using the FirstVoices platform\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Haisla to English\. The dictionary is fully searchable by English or Haisla\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, online wordlist\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Haisla by default, and can be filtered by first letter\. When using the search feature, entries are organized by relevance\.

Entries can also be sorted by Categories or part of speech\.

Each entry includes the Haisla entry, and the English translation\. Most entries also include an audio recording of the Haisla entry\.
