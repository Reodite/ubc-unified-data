English\-Tlingit Dictionary: Nouns

## Language Name

Tlingit\.

## Alternate Language Names

Lingít\.

## Region

British Columbia, Yukon, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Constance M\. Naish, Gillian Story \(Compilers of the 1963 edition\), Henry Davis \(Editor of the 1976 edition\)\.

## Others Involved

The 1963 edition, published by the Summer Institute of Linguistics, does not cite any others involved, besides the two compilers\.

The 1976 edition, published by Sheldon Jackson College, thanks Marie Thiemeyer, Andy Hope III, Claribel Davis, Robert H\. Davis \(Tlingit language class, Editors\); Jeff Leer \(Linguistic Consultant\); Vesta L\. Dominicks \(Formatting Contributor\); Mr\. and Mrs\. Richard Dauenhauer \(Contributors\); Mr\. and Mrs\. William Davis \(Consultants\); and Sheldon Jackson \(Support\)\.

## Publishing Information

The first edition was published in 1963 by the Summer Institute of Linguistics, Fairbanks, Alaska\. The second edition, which is a revised version of the first edition was published in 1976 by Sheldon Jackson College\. The second edition was re\-printed in 1996\.

## How People are Cited

The first edition cites the two compilers and the publisher on the cover, but does not cite or thank any others involved\. In the second edition the editor, the consultants, researchers and contributors are all cited in the *Acknowledgements*, while the publisher is cited on the first page of the dictionary\.

## How Information is Cited

The first edition does not does not cite the source of any of its information\. The second edition cites contributors in the *Acknowledgements*, but still does not cite the source of most of the dictionary’s information\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Both editions of this dictionary do not cite the source of any of their information\. The second edition thanks language learners who have worked to add more words to the dictionary\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical \(1976 edition\) and digital book \(both editions\)\.

## Access

The digital copy of this dictionary is open access through the Alaska Native Language Archive website for both editions, and the SIL website for the 1963 edition\. The physical copy for the 1976 edition is accessible through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Tlingit\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional \(English to Tlingit\) noun dictionary that is organized topically\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized topically and split up into three sections: *1\. Non\-Human,* *2\. Human, 3\. Miscellaneous\.*

## Other Notes

The 1976 edition of *English\-Tlingit Dictionary: Nouns*, is an edited version of the 1963 edition\. It contains changes to the Tlingit writing system, additional entries, and some changes to previous entries\.
