Haida Noun Dictionary

## Language Name

Haida\.

## Alternate Language Names

X̱aat Kíl, X̱aadas Kíl, X̱aayda Kil, Xaad kil\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Erma Lawrence \(Compiler\)\.

## Others Involved

Robert Cogo, Nora Cogo, Christine Edenso \(Consultants\); Beatrice Starkweather \(Illustrator\); Jack Osteen, Trisha McConnell \(Technical consultants &amp; designers\)\.

## Publishing Information

The first, preliminary edition was published in 1972\. The second edition was published 1975 by the Society for the Preservation of Haida Language and Literature, in Ketchikan, Alaska\.

## How People are Cited

The compiler and other contributors are cited on the opening pages of the dictionary\.

## How Information is Cited

We are unable to access this dictionary to determine how, or where source materials are cited\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

## Access

This dictionary is accessible through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Haida to English and English to Haida\.

## Dialects Included

The dialect included in this dictionary is Alaskan Haida\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional, noun dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Alphabetically by Haida and English\.
