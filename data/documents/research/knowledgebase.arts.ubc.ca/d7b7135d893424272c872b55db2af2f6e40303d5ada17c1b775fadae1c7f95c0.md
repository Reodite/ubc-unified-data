Potawatomi Dictionary \(Citizen Potawatomi Nation\)

## Language Name

Potawatomi\.

## Alternate Language Names

Pottawatomie, Bodéwadmimwen, Bodéwadmi Zheshmowen, Neshnabémwen\.

## Region

Ontario, Canada; Michigan, Wisconsin, Oklahoma, Indiana, and Kansas, United States\.

## Who

Citizen Potawatomi Nation\.

## Others Involved

Many speakers and Elders are cited by name in individual entries\. No single list of all contributors is given\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that does not display any information about launch dates or update dates\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers and Elders are cited in individual entries under *Source*\.

## How Information is Cited

Speakers and previous documentation are cited in entries under *Source*\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Information in this dictionary comes from speakers and Elders, as well as previous documentation by linguists and anthropologists\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a multimedia website that uses images and audio files\.

## Access

This dictionary is available open access online\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Potawatomi to English; English to Potawatomi\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional, multimedia online dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in this dictionary can be searched by Potawatomi or English gloss, browsed alphabetically in either language, or browsed thematically under *Tags*\. Each entry is its own sub\-page on the dictionary, and contains the Potawatomi headword, a button to play a recording of its pronunciation, and the pronunciation spelled out with English conventions\. Below this is a numbered list of all possible English glosses, any inflected forms, and the source of the entry\. Also included are example sentences with their own audio files, and a list of related words that are hyperlinked to their own entries\. Each entry’s tags are listed beside the glosses as hyperlinks\.
