The First French\-Huron Dictionary by Father Jean de Brebeuf and his Jesuit Brethren

## Language Name

Huron\-Wyandot\.

## Alternate Language Names

Wendat, Wyandot\.

## Region

Quebec and Ontario, Canada; Oklahoma, United States\.

## Who

John Steckley\.

## Others Involved

Father Jean de Brébeuf, Father Pierre Potier \(Reference Text Authors\)\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2010 by the Edwin Mellen Press in Lewiston, New York\. The original dictionary referenced in this resource was compiled sometime c\. 1640\-1650\.

## Where is Information Coming from
