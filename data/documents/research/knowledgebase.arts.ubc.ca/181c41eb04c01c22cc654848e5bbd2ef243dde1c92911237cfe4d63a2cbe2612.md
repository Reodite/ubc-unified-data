## UBC Wiki Database

The Dictionaries *Knowledgebase* uses UBC Wiki as its backend database, with the each Wiki post embedded as a filterable post on this website\. The data held on the UBC Wiki is the same as the information shown on this website, aside from the Technologies *Knowledgebase*, which is only available through this website\.

The UBC Wiki backend serves as a secure and reliable back\-up for our Dictionaries database to ensure the longevity of this project’s work beyond the potential lifespan of this website\. UBC Wiki is where our *Knowledgebase* started and where our team continue to input data\. The public\-facing *Knowledgebase* was designed to be more user friendly with filters added to support ease of navigation\.
