she shashishalhem FirstVoices

## Language Name

Sechelt\.

## Alternate Language Names

she shashishalhem, shíshálh\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Shishalh \(Sechelt\) Nation\.

## Others Involved

This dictionary was built using FirstVoices, from the First People’s Cultural Council\. Others involved such as speakers and language recorders, are cited in individual entries\.

## Publishing Information

As of late 2023, the most recent update to the dictionary was in August 2023\. There is no information on when this dictionary was launched\. This dictionary is published on the FirstVoices platform\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers are sometimes cited by name under the metadata tab in individual entries\. Entries with audio recordings have the speaker and the individual who recorded the audio cited by name in the information section of the audio file\.

## How Information is Cited

When marked, the sources of an entry is cited under the metadata tab\.

## Where is Information Coming from

Cited information comes from speakers\. However, there are many entries which do not indicate where information is coming from or what sources were used\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is a multimedia website, and includes images and audio\. It is built using the FirstVoices platform\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Sechelt to English\. The search function works in both directions\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, multimedia, online dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries are organized alphabetically by Sechelt by default, and can be filtered by first letter\. When using the search feature, entries are organized by relevance\.

Entries can also be sorted by categories or part of speech\.

Each entry includes the Sechelt entry, and the English translation\. Many entries also include an audio recording of the Sechelt entry\.
