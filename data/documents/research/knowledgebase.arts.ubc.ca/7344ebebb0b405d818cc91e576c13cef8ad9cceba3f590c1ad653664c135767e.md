Waapanuutaahch cheimis pei iiyiyuu : iinuu ayimuwin : shaawanuutaahch \= Eastern James Bay Cree Dictionary: Cree\-English, Southern Dialect

## Language Name

Cree\-Montagnais\-Naskapi\.

## Alternate Language Names

Cree, Naskapi, Montagnais, ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐍᐏᐣ, Nēhiyawēwin\.

## Region

Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, Newfoundland and Labrador, and Northwest Territories, Canada\.

## Who

Ruth Salt, Anna Blacksmith, Patricia Diamond, Pearl Weistche, Marie\-Odile Junker, Marguerite MacKenzie, Cree School Board, Cree Programs\.
