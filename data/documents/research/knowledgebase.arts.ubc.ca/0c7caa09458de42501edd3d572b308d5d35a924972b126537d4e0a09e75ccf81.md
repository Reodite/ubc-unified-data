Interior Tlingit Noun Dictionary: The Dialects Spoken by Tlingit Elders of Carcross and Teslin, Yukon, and Atlin, British Columbia

## Language Name

Tlingit\.

## Alternate Language Names

Lingít\.

## Region

British Columbia, Yukon, Canada; Alaska, United States\.

## Who

Jeff Leer; Doug Hitch; John Ritter\.

## Others Involved

Speakers: Elizabeth Nyman \(Atlin\); Bessie Cooley \(Teslin\); Lucy Wren \(Carcross\); Mabel Johnson \(Teslin\); Frank Sydney \(Teslin\); Tom Smith \(Teslin\); Frank and Rosie Johnston \(Teslin\); Sam Johnston \(Teslin\); Emma Sam \(Teslin\); Margaret Bob \(Teslin\); Liz Smith \(Teslin\); Gladys Johnston \(Teslin\); Virginia Smarch \(Teslin\); Watson Smarch \(Teslin\); Lucy Wren \(Carcross\); Angela Sidnery \(Carcross\)\.

Sponsors: Aboriginal Language Services \(Government of Yukon\) through the Canada\-Yukon and Funding Agreement on the Development and Enhancement of Yukon Aboriginal Languages; Yukon Native Language Centre\.

## Publishing Information

Published 2001 by the Yukon Native Language Centre, Whitehorse, Yukon\.

## How People are Cited

People are cited in the *Introduction* of this dictionary\.

## How Information is Cited

Information is cited in the *Introduction* of the dictionary\.

## Where is Information Coming from

The information in this dictionary comes from speakers from Atlin, Teslin, and Carcross, confirming and expanding the previous work done on the coastal dialects by Constance Naish and Gillian Story\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a physical book\.

## Access

The physical copy of this dictionary is accessible through libraries\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

English to Tlingit, Tlingit to English\.

## Dialects Included

This dictionary includes the following dialects: Atlin, Teslin and Carcross\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, bidirectional dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

In the *Main Section*, entries are organized semantically, and then alphabetically by English within the semantic categories\. The *Tlingit Section* is organized alphabetically by Tlingit, as determined by the alphabet included on the first page of the section\. The *English Section* is organized alphabetically by English\.

The dictionary is introduced by a brief grammar including a description of Interior Tlingit consonants and vowels\. The dictionary sections of this resource include a semantically organized English to Tlingit section, a Tlingit to English section, and an alphabetically organized English to Tlingit section\. The final section of the resource includes information on classifactory verbs\.
