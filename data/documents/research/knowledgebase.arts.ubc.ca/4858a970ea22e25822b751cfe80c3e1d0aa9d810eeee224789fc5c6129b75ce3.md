Choctaw Dictionary

## Language Name

Choctaw\.

## Alternate Language Names

Chahta\.

## Region

Oklahoma, Mississippi, Louisiana, and Tennessee, United States\.

## Who

Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma\.

## Others Involved

There is no information included in this resource about others involved\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that doesn’t display any information on launch or update dates, but was likely made public online in late 2022\. Prior to 2022 an earlier version of this dictionary was available as an open access PDF\.

## How People are Cited

This dictionary does not cite any authors or contributors\. The Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma is shown in the website’s banner and is listed as the copyright holder in the website’s footer\.

## How Information is Cited

This dictionary does not cite the source of any of its information\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary does not indicate where information is coming from or what sources were used\.

## Tools and Framework used

This dictionary is available as a multimedia website, with audio and a built\-in search function\.

## Access

This dictionary is open access online\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Choctaw to English\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, mono\-directional online dictionary\.

## How are Entries Organised

This dictionary is organised alphabetically by Choctaw, and can be filtered based on the first letter of the entry\. Entries include the Choctaw headword, the English definition, the part of speech, and an audio recording of the Choctaw word\.
