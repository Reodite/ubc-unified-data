## Region

## Who

## Other Notes

This picture dictionary appears to only be available with the Beginner’s Packet available on the Comanche Language and Cultural Preservation Committee’s website\. The packet contains the revised Comanche Dictionary with English to Comanche translation; DVD \# 332 that teaches the Comanche Sound System; A Picture Dictionary; CD \#81 for the Picture Dictionary; a set of three Flash Cards; and CD \#82 for all the Flash Card words\.
