## Language Name

Dakelh\.

## Alternate Language Names

Carrier\.

## Region

British Columbia, Canada\.

## Who

Irene Antoine, Catherine Bird, Ileen Heer, Mildred Martin, and Florence Sam \(Co\-authors\)\.

## Others Involved
