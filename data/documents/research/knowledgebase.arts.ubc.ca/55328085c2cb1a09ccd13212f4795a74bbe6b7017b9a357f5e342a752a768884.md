The Arapaho Language Project

## Language Name

Arapaho\.

## Alternate Language Names

Arapahoe, Hinónoʼeitíít\.

## Region

Wyoming and Oklahoma, United States\.

## Who

University of Colorado Boulder\.

## Others Involved

Dr\. Andrew Cowell \(linguist\); Alonzo Moss, Sr\., William J\. C’Hair, Alfred Redman \(permissions\); Florence Bell, Wayne C’Hair, William C’Hair, Alonzo Moss, Sr\., Marian Scott, Andy Brown, Ed Underwood \(audio recordings\); unnamed Elders \(language consultants\); Joshua Raclaw, Finn Thye, Irina Vagner, and Allison Sanders, Jena Hwang \(student assistants\); the University of Colorado’s ASSETT program and Department of Linguistics \(funding\)\.

## Publishing Information

This is a digital dictionary that does not display any information about launch dates or update dates\.

## How People are Cited

Speakers and contributors are cited under *Acknowledgements*\.

## How Information is Cited

Audio files in this dictionary comes from speakers who are cited in the *Acknowledgements*\. No additional sources of information are cited\.

## Where is Information Coming from

This dictionary was created with the help of speakers from the Northern Arapaho Language and Cultural Commission and Elders who participated in the 2013 Cheyenne\-Arapaho Language Conference\. Any previous works or additional sources of information are not cited\.

## Tools and Framework used

This is a multimedia website that uses images and audio files\.

## Access

This dictionary is available open access online\.

## Included Languages and Directionality

Arapaho to English\.

## Dialects Included

No dialect is specified for this dictionary\.

## Type of Dictionary

This is a bilingual, multimedia word list\.

## How are Entries Organised

Entries in this word list are listed topically under *Speaking Arapaho*\. Each topic, including Child and Daycare, Phone Calls, Good Wishes, and more, is intended to be useful for everyday life\. Entries in these lists can be words or phrases\. Each entry contains the Arapaho word or phrase and a button to play the audio recording, followed by the English translation\. No other information is included in entries\.
