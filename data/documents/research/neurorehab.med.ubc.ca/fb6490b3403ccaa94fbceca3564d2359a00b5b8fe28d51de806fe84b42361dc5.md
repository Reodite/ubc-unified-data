The equipment for GRASP is relatively inexpensive and many items can be found at home or at a Dollar Store, but a few pieces can be more difficult to find\.

Our hospital \(GF Strong Rehab Centre\) assembles some of the pieces and sells this at cost\-recovery to patients\.  They do not sell kits to patients who are not undergoing stroke rehabilitation at GF Strong Rehab Centre or external clinicians\.  See more information at [Equipment List](https://med-fom-neurorehab.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?p=579 "Equipment List") &amp; [Equipment Vendors\.](https://med-fom-neurorehab.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?p=583 "Equipment Vendors")

There is also a **GRASP Equipment Kit** available at the **Gift Shop of Vancouver General Hospital \(Vancouver BC\)**: [GRASP kit](https://www.shopvgh.ca/collections/grasp-kit)\.
