************The Arm Capacity and Movement Test \(ArmCAM\)************

---

**ArmCAM** is a measurement of upper extremity function developed to be remotely administrated by videoconference for telerehabilitation purposes\.  It has excellent test retest reliability and good validity with the Fugl\-Meyer Assessment for Upper Extremity and Action Research Arm Test\.
