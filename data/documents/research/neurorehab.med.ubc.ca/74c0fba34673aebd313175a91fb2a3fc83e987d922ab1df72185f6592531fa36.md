Dr\. Janice Eng is only taking **postdoctoral fellows** who have or are completing a PhD at this time\.  If you are interested in postdoctoral positions, please contact [Dr\. Eng](mailto:janice.eng@ubc.ca) directly\.

Dr\. Eng is **not** accepting applications for Masters or PhD \(doctoral\) students\.

Postdoctoral funding may be available through the following sources:

-   [Canadian Institute of Health Research \(CIHR\)](https://cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/795.html)
-   [Michael Smith Foundation for Health Research](https://healthresearchbc.ca/fundingprogram/research-trainee-program/)
-   [Banting Post\-doctoral Fellowship](https://banting.fellowships-bourses.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html)
-   [Heart and Stroke Foundation of Canada](https://www.heartandstroke.ca/what-we-do/research/for-researchers)
-   [Killam Post\-doctoral fellowship \(UBC\)](https://www.postdocs.ubc.ca/award/killam-postdoctoral-research-fellowship)
-   [Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada \(NSERC\)](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/students-etudiants/pd-np/pdf-bp_eng.asp)
