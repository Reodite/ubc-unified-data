### How to calculate target heart rate

This calculates the target heart rate based on a person’s resting heart rates and their target training zone using the heart rate reserve or Karvonen method\.

Age \(years\)

Resting heart rate \(bpm\)

Should be following a minimum of 5 minutes of quiet sitting \(i\.e\., no movement or talking\), either manually for 60 seconds or using a heart rate monitor\.

Is the patient taking beta\-blocker medication?

Yes No Don’t know

If “Don’t know”, the standard equation will be used\. Beta\-blockers lower patients’ heart rates\. A list of commonly prescribed beta\-blockers can be found [here](http://www.heartandstroke.ca/heart/treatments/medications/beta-blockers)\.

HRR \(heart rate reserve\) \= HRmax − HRrest

Predicted HRmax:

-   Without beta\-blocker medication \= 206\.9 – \(0\.67 × age\) \([Gellish et al\., Med Sci Sports Exerc 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17468581/)\)
-   With beta\-blocker medication \= 164 – \(0\.7 × age\) \([Brawner et al\., Am Heart J 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15523326/)\)

Target HR \= HRrest \+ \(X% of HRR\)
