### Hospital GRASP\- Patient Exercise Manual Form

GRASP is free, but requires you to complete a short form\. It is important that we know whether GRASP is being utilized, and by who\. Thus, we ask that you complete a few short questions to help improve our programs\. Upon completion of this form, the Hospital GRASP patient books and instruction manuals can be freely accessed \(with versions in English, French, Chinese, Punjabi, Portuguese, Hindi or Vietnamese\)\.

Thanks,

Janice Eng, PhD, PT/OT

To get started with GRASP\-Hospital Version, please read through the [GRASP Terms and Conditions](https://neurorehab.med.ubc.ca/files/2013/06/GRASP-Terms-and-Conditions.pdf)\.
