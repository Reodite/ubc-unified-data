#### **Walk ‘n Watch is a pragmatic, multi\-site implementation trial demonstrating that a structured, progressive walking protocol delivered by physical therapists significantly improves walking endurance, balance, and quality of life in patients with subacute stroke, with no serious adverse events\.**
