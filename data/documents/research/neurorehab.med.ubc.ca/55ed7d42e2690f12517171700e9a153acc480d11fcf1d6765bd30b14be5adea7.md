### Home GRASP \- Participant Exercise Manual Form

GRASP is free, but requires you to complete a short form\. It is important that we know whether GRASP is being utilized, and by who\. Thus, we ask that you complete a few short questions to help improve our programs\. Upon accepting the GRASP Terms and Conditions and completion of this form, the Home GRASP patient books and instruction manuals can be freely accessed\.

Thanks,

Janice Eng, PhD, PT/OT
