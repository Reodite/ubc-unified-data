UBC’s Radiology department is broadly engaged in research ranging from imaging technology validation to the latest in radiopharmaceuticals\. Established in 1972, our department has built a reputation as one of Canada’s strongest academic radiology departments, and continues to build on this reputation with interdisciplinary innovative clinical and fundamental science\.

## Message from the Vice Chair, Research

[Coree Laule](https://med-fom-radiology-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/coree-laule-new.jpg)

Welcome to Research in the UBC Department of Radiology\!

At the University of British Columbia’s Department of Radiology, our research community is driven by curiosity, collaboration, and a shared mission to improve health through innovation in medical imaging\. From cutting\-edge imaging physics and AI\-driven analytics to patient\-centred clinical trials as well as health equity and EDI research, our work spans the full spectrum of translational research\.

We are proud to be a diverse and inclusive community of scientists, clinicians, trainees, and staff, united by a commitment to excellence and impact\. Our research is enriched by interdisciplinary partnerships and a strong culture of mentorship and education\. Together, we aim to advance knowledge, improve diagnostic and therapeutic tools, and help shape a more equitable and effective healthcare system—locally and globally\.

Explore our site to learn more about the people, projects, and opportunities that define research in the UBC Department of Radiology\.

— **Cornelia Laule, PhD, Professor**
