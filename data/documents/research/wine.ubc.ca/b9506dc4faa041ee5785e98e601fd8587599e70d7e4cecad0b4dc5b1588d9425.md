## Grape and Wine Biochemistry Research Labs

## of the UBC Wine Research Centre

[Susan Murch](https://fm-wine-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/11/susan_murch_350x350.jpg)

### The PlantSMART Lab

Dr\. Susan Murch

PlantSMART investigates the chemicals produced by plants and how plant chemicals affect human health\. Research themes include: chemistry of cannabis and other medicinal plants; plant chemistry for food security; chemistry of natural non\-protein amino acids; chemical regulation of plant signalling behaviour; chemistry of plant responses to light\.

[Visit website](https://chemistry.ok.ubc.ca/research/)

[Vivien Measday](https://fm-wine-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/11/vivian_measday_350x350.jpg)

### The Measday Lab

Dr\. Vivien Measday

Studying yeast populations associated with the vineyards of British Columbia’s Okanagan Valley\. We partner with the BC Wine Grape Council and associated wineries to perform spontaneous fermentations and isolation of the wine yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae\. Using high\-throughput genotypic analyses, we generate a genetic fingerprint of each strain and compare it to a fingerprint database of S\. cerevisiae commercial wine yeast strains that we have generated to identify indigenous strains to the Okanagan Valley with enological potential\.

[Visit website](https://www.landfood.ubc.ca/vivien-measday/?login)

[Wesley Zandberg](https://fm-wine-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/12/wesley-zandberg-350x350-1.jpg)

### The Zandberg Lab

Dr\. Wesley Zandberg

The Zandberg lab is challenging currently accepted models of how grape exposure to forest fire smoke leads to tainted wines either initally or through the ageing process\. By exploring volatile phenol interaction with aromatic compositions of wines, this lab is finding innovative solutions to a threat to local and global wine production with extreme weather events and climate change pressures\.

[Visit website](https://chemistry.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/wesley-zandberg/)

[Image](https://fm-wine-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/Screenshot-2026-01-06-110753.png)

### The Measday Lab

Jay Martiniuk M\.Sc\.

As a PhD student in Dr\. Vivien Measday’s lab, Jay is working on indigenous yeast strains and their significance to commercial wine production\. Jay is also a lecturer at the UBC Vancouver campus instructing hundreds of students each year in Wine Science 1 and 2, Microbiology of Fermented Foods and Beverages and facilitates the Grapes to Glass Seminar Series\.

[Visit website](https://www.landfood.ubc.ca/jay-martiniuk/?login)

[Joerg Bohlmann](https://fm-wine-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/11/joerg_bohlmann_350x350.jpg)

### The Michael Smith Laboratory

Dr\. Jörg Bohlmann

The MSL is focused on terpenoid and phenolic pathways of plant metabolomics\. More recently, the research has been integrating various “omics” strategies to discover genes and enzymes involved in the biosynthesis of high\-valued plant bioproducts\.

[Visit website](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-jorg-bohlmann/)
