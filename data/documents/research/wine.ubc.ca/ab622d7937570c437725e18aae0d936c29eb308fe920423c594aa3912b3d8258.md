### The Kuus Lab

Dr\. Merje Kuus

Geographical imaginary in wine – exploring the resonance between wine and place: how such resonance is created, maintained, and communicated\. Focusing on Okanagan Valley, British Columbia, investigating the wines and wine experiences that treat place as an integral part of the product and thus foster loyalty to the place and not only the product\. Understanding how political actors—whether diplomats or wine entrepreneurs—perceive places and their own connections to places\.

[Visit website](https://blogs.ubc.ca/kuus/)
