In partnership with the UBC Wine Research Centre, [Vancouver International Wine Festival](https://vanwinefest.ca/) \(VIWF\) hosted a panel of esteemed academics and industry leaders\. The panel guided a tasting of BC and international wines to generate phrases and descriptors that evoke the wine’s typicity and regional identity\. Wine is a cultural product representing a place of origin\. What can BC learn from the world’s iconic regions? Does BC have or need a unified wine identity? What does the future hold for BC’s wine image globally?

Read more about the event and the panelists [here](https://wine.ubc.ca/2023/03/22/vancouver-international-wine-festival/)\.
