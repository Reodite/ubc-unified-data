## Viticulture and Climate Resilience Research Labs

## of the UBC Wine Research Centre

[Image](https://fm-wine-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/Screenshot-2026-01-05-143239-1.png)

### The Hart Lab

Dr\. Miranda Hart

Microbial ecology of soil microbes; the role and the applications of microbes in sustainable agriculture, viticulture, and ecological restoration\.

[Visit website](https://mirandahart.ca/)

[Image](https://fm-wine-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/Simone-1.jpg)

### The Castellarin Lab

Dr\. Simone Castellarin

Viticulture and berry physiology; genotype selection and the development of new agronomical strategies to enhance desirable fruit quality and mitigate the effects of climate change on premium fruit production\.

[Visit website](https://castellarin-lab.landfood.ubc.ca/)

[Thorsten Knipfer](https://fm-wine-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/11/thorsten_knipfer_350x350.jpg)

### The Knipfer Lab

Dr\. Thorsten Knipfer

Water stress in perennial agrifood crops such as grapes and hazelnuts; developing advanced strategies for the selection of crop genotypes with improved drought resistance; creating management practices that allow for sustainable crop production through water savings\.

[Visit website](https://www.landfood.ubc.ca/thorsten-knipfer/?login)

[Elizabeth Wolkovich](https://fm-wine-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/11/elizabeth_wolkovich_350x350.jpg)

### The State of Wine

Dr\. Elizabeth Wolkovich

Climate change impacts on wine grapes; adaptive viticultural practices; phenological shifts and adaptation through planting diversity\.

[Visit website](https://stateofwine.org/)

[Nathan Pelletier](https://fm-wine-2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/nathan-pelletier-350x350-1.jpg)

### Food Systems Prism Lab

Dr\. Nathan Pelletier

Sustainable food systems; life\-cycle analysis; ecological economics; sustainable agriculture system measurement and management\.

[Visit website](https://prismlab.weebly.com/)
