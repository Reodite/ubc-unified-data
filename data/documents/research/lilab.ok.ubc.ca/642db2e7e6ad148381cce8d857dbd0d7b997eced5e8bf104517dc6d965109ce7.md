## Our team

Our lab offers a unique opportunity to work within a collaborative, close\-knit team focused on cutting\-edge interdisciplinary research from single\-molecule to multi\-cellular studies\. Our current projects span a variety of dynamic research areas, including:

-   Force\-responsive DNA nanostructures
-   Quantitative and functional super\-resolution imaging
-   Intercellular mechano\-bio\-engineering
-   Extracellular vesicle bioengineering

We are always seeking exceptional students from diverse academic backgrounds — chemistry, physics, cell biology, and bioengineering — who are passionate about advancing scientific discovery\. We welcome students from around the globe to contribute their unique perspectives and expertise\. To learn more about our research and available opportunities, please contact Dr\. Isaac Li at [isaac\.li@ubc\.ca](mailto:isaac.li@ubc.ca)\.

### Graduate position &amp; resources

If you are passionate about creative, hands\-on, and multidisciplinary research, this opportunity is for you\. We welcome students from all backgrounds, particularly those with a strong track record in the physical sciences or engineering\. In my lab, you will have the chance to develop diverse skills in areas such as single\-molecule techniques \(fluorescence and force\), super\-resolution microscopy, DNA nanotechnology, instrumentation, and bioengineering of cell behaviour\. Interested students should contact [Isaac Li](mailto:isaac.li@ubc.ca), and apply through the [College of Graduate Studies at UBC Okanagan](https://gradstudies.ok.ubc.ca/)\.

### Undergraduate positions &amp; resources

We offer several undergraduate research positions each year\. Students usually begin in the summer, funded through programs such as the Science URA, NSERC USRA, or Work\-Study, and often continue with a 2\-term Honours Thesis or Directed Studies\. The application deadline for summer research programs is typically in January\. For more information or to discuss potential opportunities, please contact [Isaac Li](mailto:isaac.li@ubc.ca)

Honours Thesis &amp; Directed Studies:  
Students wishing to do Honours Thesis / Directed Studies \(CHEM448/449 &amp; BIOC448/449\) should contact me directly\.

Summer research:  
Students at UBC Okanagan can apply for [URA and NSERC\-USRA](https://science.ok.ubc.ca/awards/undergraduate-3/)\.

Work\-Study program:  
We have N Work\-Study positions open for summer and fall terms\. Please check the UBC student [job board](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/career-experience/job-board/)\.

International students:  
Please check out [Mitac Globalink Research Internship](https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/globalink-research-internship-students/)\.
