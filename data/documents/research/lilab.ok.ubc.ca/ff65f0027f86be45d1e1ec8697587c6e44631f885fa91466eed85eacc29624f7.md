### Please Register by March 9th to Reserve a Spot in the Conference\.

[Register Here](https://lilab.ok.ubc.ca/conference/biophysics2016/register.html)\. Only a limited amount of seats are available, on a first\-come\-first\-serve basis\.

The Western Canada Biophysics Conference \(WCBC\) is a forum for discussion on a variety of topics in the biophysical sciences\.  
Invited speakers, including professors and students from UBC\-O, will be given opportunities to share their latest research in chemistry, engineering, and physics which bridges life, physical, and computational sciences into the field of biophysics\.  
Keynote guest speaker, Prof\. Nancy Forde from SFU's Department of Physics, will be presenting her recent lab's work on the mechanics of collagen\. For a complete list &amp; abstracts of all the speakers, please visit the [Speakers](https://lilab.ok.ubc.ca/conference/biophysics2016/speakers.html) page\. For the event's itinerary, visit the [Program](https://lilab.ok.ubc.ca/conference/biophysics2016/speakers.html) page\.  
  
The event welcomes all students &amp; faculty to attend this lively educational session\. Free snacks, drinks, and lunch will be served to those who [register](https://lilab.ok.ubc.ca/conference/biophysics2016/register.html)\.
