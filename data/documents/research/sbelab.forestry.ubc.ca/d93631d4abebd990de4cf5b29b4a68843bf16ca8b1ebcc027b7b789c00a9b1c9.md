#### [**Wood 395 Sustainable Timber Built Technologies**](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/course-descriptions/subject/woodv)

-   Introduce sustainability in timber materials and manufacturing\.
-   Describe possible solutions to achieve timber materials manufacturing and installation sustainability in the industry\.
-   Conduct 3D design for timber buildings with BIM, and analyze energy and carbon performance\.

#### [WOOD 491 Environmental Facilities Design](https://sustain.ubc.ca/wood-491-environmental-facilities-design)

-   Introduce environmental concepts in timber manufacturing\.
-   Illustrate noise, duct, VOC control regulations and systems in timber manufacturing\.
-   Understand design and selection of waste recycling systems, and environmental performance assessment of timber manufacturing facilities\.

#### [**Micro\-certificate:** Circular Bioeconomy Business Development](https://forestry.ubc.ca/future-students/certificates/circular-bioeconomy-development/courses-and-timeline/)

-   Bioeconomy and Circular Bioeconomy Policies\.
-   Biotechnologies Enabling Circular Bioeconom\.
-   Circular Bioeconomy Business Case Studies\.
-   Circularity Assessment Strategies\.

#### [**Micro\-certificate:** Zero Carbon Building Solutions](https://forestry.ubc.ca/future-students/certificates/zero-carbon-building-solutions/courses-and-timeline/)

-   Building Embodied Carbon Accounting\.
-   Building Operational Carbon Accounting\.
-   Negative Carbon Emission Strategies\.
-   Zero Carbon Building Practices\.
