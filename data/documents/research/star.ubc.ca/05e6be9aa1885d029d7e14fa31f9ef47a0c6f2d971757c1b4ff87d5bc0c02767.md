UBC STAR has helped catalyze $4\.5M in Industrial and Technological Benefits \(ITB\) investments in the BC Interior\.

UBC STAR has helped small to mid\-sized firms leverage [Canada’s Industrial and Technological Benefits \(ITB\) Policy](https://www.ic.gc.ca/eic/site/086.nsf/eng/home), which contractually obligates companies awarded defence procurement contracts to undertake business activity in Canada equal to the value of the contracts they have won\.

## Liquid Armour

A pioneering transatlantic partnership

[**BAE Systems**](http://www.baesystems.com/en-ca/home) and **[Helios Global Technologies](http://www.heliosglobaltech.com/)** signed a Memorandum of Understanding \(MoU\) in 2017 to develop applications for liquid armour technology for the defence, sport and automotive sectors\. The new protective materials are now being tested at the **[UBC STAR Impact Research Facility \(SIRF\)](https://star.ubc.ca/sirf)** in Kelowna, BC\.

## Armourgel
