Renée Woodward holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from the University of British Columbia\. Her research focuses on mental health, sexual health, migrant youth, and sexual and gender minority youth\. Renée has collaborated with BC Mental Health &amp; Substance Use Services Research Institute investigators on research relating to forensics and risk assessment for mental health patients, obsessive\-compulsive disorder, psychosis, and sexual health, and has volunteered with BLUSH VCH to help deliver comprehensive sexual health education to youth in BC\. These experiences have led her to join SARAVYC to contribute to research on the intersection of many of these topics, particularly health disparities among communities affected by discrimination and racism\.
