## Annie Smith

### Executive Director, McCreary Centre Society

Annie has been  the Executive Director of McCreary Centre Society \(McCreary\) since 2006\. McCreary  is a non\-government not\-for\-profit committed to improving the health of BC youth through research, evaluation and community based projects\. Best known for the BC Adolescent Health Survey, a province wide survey of youth in grades 7\-12, the Society also conducts regular surveys of youth not captured in a mainstream school based survey\. McCreary partners with SARAVYC on a number of ongoing projects\.
