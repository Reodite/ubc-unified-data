## Sheila Marshall, PhD

### Professor, University of British Columbia

Dr\. Sheila Marshall is a professor in the School of Social Work and associate faculty in the Division of Adolescent Health and Medicine at the University of British Columbia\. Her research focuses on adolescent development in the relational contexts of family, peers, and non\-familial adults\.
