Caroline has a PhD in Social Psychology from Simon Fraser University, where she investigated how group\-based identity impacts mental health and empowers individuals and groups to mobilize for climate change\. Currently she is interested in the mental health impacts of climate change on adolescents, access to health care and health services among vulnerable youth, and protective factors that foster resilience in the mental health of migrant and LGBTQ\+ youth\. She is a second\-generation Chinese/Scottish settler Canadian, and brings these cultural perspectives to her work\.
