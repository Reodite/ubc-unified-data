# Since 2006, rigorous and award\-winning research on youth health, stigma, and resilience

We study how stigma, discrimination, violence, and trauma affect young people’s health and identify the factors that help youth thrive despite these challenges\. By collaborating with youth and their communities, we develop culturally relevant interventions that improve health outcomes, advance equity, and influence policies and practices worldwide\.

## Participate in our workshops

Both workshops are free, co\-developed with community advisors, and open across Canada\. Research participation is optional and comes with an honorarium\.

For youth · ages 14–25

### For Youth Like Me

A sexual health workshop for 2SLGBTQ\+ youth, facilitated in a judgment\-free, affirming environment\. Online or in person across Canada\.

-   →Two 1\-hour sessions, one week apart
-   →Online \(Zoom\) or in person
-   →Flexible — lunchtime or after school

Optional surveys available — participants receive a **$50 honorarium**\.

[Learn more and sign up](https://www.saravyc.ubc.ca/research/current-projects/)

For parents &amp; caregivers

### Help us support youth sexual health education

For parents, caregivers, and guardians of migrant, newcomer, racialized, and refugee youth\. Multilingual materials and interpreters available\.

-   →One 2\-hour session, online or in person
-   →Materials in multiple languages
-   →Catering at in\-person sessions

Optional 15\-minute feedback survey — honorarium or catering provided\.

[Learn more and sign up](https://www.saravyc.ubc.ca/research/current-projects/)

About SARAVYC

## Youth facing stigma have distinct health needs

We study resilience, stigma, discrimination, violence, and trauma and translate our findings into resources for communities, educators, and health providers\.

#### Research

Community\-based research on stigma and resilience among 2SLGBTQ\+ youth, Indigenous youth, and newcomer communities\.

[View our research →](https://www.saravyc.ubc.ca/research/)

#### Publications

Journals, reports, infographics, and education materials from 20\+ years of research on vulnerable youth health\.

#### Resources

Free, evidence\-based tools for educators, service providers, families, and youth in English and French\.
