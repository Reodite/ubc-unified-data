SARAVYC is offering a suite of free, evidence\-based programs supporting 2SLGBTQ\+ youth, their families, and their communities across Canada\. All programs are co\-developed with community advisors, available in English and French, and offered online or in person\. This project is funded by [Health Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada.html)\.

## Supporting Youth

## For Youth Like Me

**Sexual health workshop for 2SLGBTQ\+ youth**

Designed for 2SLGBTQ\+ youth ages 14–25, this workshop explores sexual health with trained facilitators in a judgment\-free, affirming environment\. Sessions are available online \(Zoom\) or in person\.

-   Two 1\-hour sessions, one week apart
-   Flexible scheduling — lunchtime or after school
-   One pre\-survey, and two post\-surveys
-   Available in English and French
-   Currently offered in Alberta, Ontario, Newfoundland, and Quebec, with expansion to Yukon

Participants who complete the optional surveys receive a **$50 honorarium** as a thank\-you for their time\.  

[Download Flyer](https://apsc-saravyc.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/Youth_Recruitment_Poster.pdf)

## Taking Pride

**Healthy relationships program for 2SLGBTQ\+ youth**

Taking Pride is a healthy relationships and dating violence prevention program created by and for 2SLGBTQ\+ youth\. It focuses on building skills for healthy romantic and friendship relationships\. Sessions are available online or in person, in English and French\.

-   Focused on healthy relationships and dating violence prevention
-   Available online or in person
-   Available in English and French
-   Open to 2SLGBTQ\+ youth across Canada

Youth can enroll in For Youth Like Me, Taking Pride, or both programs together\.

[Learn More](https://www.saravyc.ubc.ca/taking-pride-2slgbtqia-youth-relationship-workshop-training-resources/)

## Supporting Parents and Caregivers

## Help Us Support Youth Sexual Health Education

**Workshop for newcomer parents, caregivers, and guardians**

This workshop is designed for parents, caregivers, and guardians of migrant, racialized, and refugee youth\. Learn practical strategies for supporting your child’s sexual health, in a culturally relevant and welcoming setting\.

-   One 2\-hour session, online or in person
-   Materials available in multiple languages including Hindi, Mandarin, and Tagalog
-   Interpreters and translators available on request
-   Online \= $20 honorarium, In\-person \= catered meal

An optional 15\-minute feedback survey is included\. Participants receive an **honorarium or catering** depending on session format\.

[Download Flyer](https://apsc-saravyc.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/Parent_Recruitment_Poster.pdf)

## Resources for Parents

**Bilingual factsheets and infographics for families**

SARAVYC is developing bilingual factsheets, infographics, and presentations tailored for parents in rural areas and newcomer communities — providing accurate, evidence\-based information about trans and non\-binary youth health and wellbeing\.

-   Available in English, French, and additional languages
-   Tailored for rural and newcomer communities
-   Designed to counter misinformation with accurate, research\-based information

Check back for updates as resources become available\.
