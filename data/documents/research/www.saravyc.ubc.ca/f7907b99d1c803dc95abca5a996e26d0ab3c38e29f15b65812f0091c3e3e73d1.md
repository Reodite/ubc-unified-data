Clover is a Taiwanese immigrant and MSc student in the School of Population and Public Health at the University of British Columbia\. Their research weaves together narrative and arts\-based approaches to inquiry with social nutrition, an interest first animated during their BSc in Food, Nutrition, and Health\. Clover is dedicated to working with equity\-deserving communities, particularly racialized diasporas and 2SLGBTQIA\+ populations, to better understand lived experience and advance compassionate, affirming health care\.
