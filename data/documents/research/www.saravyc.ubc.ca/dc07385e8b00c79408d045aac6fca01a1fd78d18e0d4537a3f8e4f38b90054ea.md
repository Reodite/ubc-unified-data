Gursahil Purba is a Research Assistant at the Stigma and Resilience Among Vulnerable Youth Centre \(SARAVYC\) and an undergraduate student in the Faculty of Science at the University of British Columbia, where he is pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Neuroscience\. His research interests include adolescent mental health, climate and health, health equity, and knowledge synthesis\. He is also interested in cognitive neuroscience, with a focus on visual perception and cognition\. He hopes to contribute to research that advances equitable health care and improves health outcomes for children and adolescents\.
