South Asian LGB\+ youth in British Columbia face higher risks of suicidal ideation and suicide attempts than their heterosexual peers\. These risks are closely linked to experiences of victimization, including discrimination, harassment, bullying, and violence\.

At the same time, supportive relationships can make a meaningful difference\. Strong family connections, feeling safe and connected at school, having caring adults in the community, and having close friends can all help reduce the likelihood of suicidal thoughts and attempts\.

The infographic highlights the importance of culturally sensitive, inclusive, and welcoming environments for South Asian LGB\+ youth\. It also points to the need for greater parental acceptance, stronger school supports, and community relationships that help young people feel seen, valued, and cared for\.
