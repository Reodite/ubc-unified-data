Research

## What we study and why it matters

SARAVYC conducts rigorous, community\-engaged research on the health of young people who face stigma, discrimination, and violence\. Our work identifies what puts youth at risk, and what helps them thrive\.

Featured research

### For Our Youth: Health Canada Sexual Health Workshops

Two free, evidence\-based workshops currently enrolling across Canada\. One is designed for 2SLGBTQ\+ youth ages 14–25 \(Taking Pride\), and one for newcomer parents, caregivers, and guardians\. Both were co\-developed with community advisors\. Participants receive an honorarium\.

## Current projects

### For Our Youth: Health Canada Sexual Health Workshops

Free workshops for 2SLGBTQ\+ youth and newcomer parents, co\-developed with community advisors\. Currently enrolling across Canada\. Participants receive an honorarium\.

Sexual health 2SLGBTQ\+ youth Migrant youth

### Bridging Divides: Immigrant Health and Well\-Being

Population\-based analysis of 20\-year trends in sexual and mental health among migrant, racialized, and refugee youth in Western Canada\. Funded by the Canada First Research Excellence Fund\.

Migrant youth Mental health

### TranscendentHealth

Adapting a text\-based teen pregnancy prevention program for transgender boys\. A national randomized controlled trial is planned to test the updated program\.

Gender diversity Sexual health

### Peer Navigator: HIV Prevention for Street\-Involved Youth

Adapting and scaling a peer\-based HIV prevention and treatment intervention for street\-involved and homeless youth across Canadian and Kenyan sites\. Funded by CIHR\.

Homelessness Sexual health

### Dating Violence Intervention for 2SLGBTQ\+ Youth

Co\-developing healthy relationship education modules with and for 2SLGBTQ\+ youth\. Piloting across BC and scaling nationally\.

2SLGBTQ\+ youth Family and community

### Tracking Health Disparities for Ethnoculturally Diverse LGB Youth

Analyzing BC Adolescent Health Survey data to identify health disparities and protective factors for LGB youth from Indigenous, East Asian, and South Asian backgrounds\. Funded by CIHR Foundation Grant\.

2SLGBTQ\+ youth Migrant youth School climate

## Completed projects

### Canadian Trans Youth Health Survey \(2014, 2019, 2024\)

Canada’s first and largest national health survey of transgender and non\-binary youth\. Findings have shaped clinical guidelines, human rights cases, and legislation across Canada and internationally\.

### GSA Effects Using SLEPHI

Award\-winning research establishing that Gender Sexuality Alliances make schools safer for all students, and that the longer a GSA exists, the greater the impact\.
