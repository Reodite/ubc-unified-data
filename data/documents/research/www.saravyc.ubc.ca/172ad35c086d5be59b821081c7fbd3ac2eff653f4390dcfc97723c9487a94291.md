This infographic summarizes findings from a secondary analysis of the 2013–2023 British Columbia Adolescent Health Survey \(BC AHS\), a province\-wide survey of students in Grades 7–12\. It highlights how injury and concussion patterns changed over time, how these trends differed by sex and migrant status, and what mechanisms most commonly contributed to injuries\. It also points to key gaps in health care access and the need for injury prevention strategies that reflect the experiences of BC’s increasingly diverse youth population\.
