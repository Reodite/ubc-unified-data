## Research Facilities

[A microscope image of purple proteins](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_300x150/public/2025-11/Facilties%20-%20ASTRID.jpg.webp?itok=Tq1aiGtm)

### [Advanced Structural Biology for Re\-emerging Infectious Diseases](https://astrid-lsi.sites.olt.ubc.ca/)

The Advanced Structural Biology for Re\-emerging Infectious Diseases \(ASTRID\) facility supports research into the structural analysis of proteins and protein\-protein interactions through a group of shared facilities and equipment\.

[Learn more](https://astrid-lsi.sites.olt.ubc.ca/)

[Liquid handling instrument](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_300x150/public/2026-01/Screenshot%202026-01-06%20at%209.18.36%E2%80%AFAM.png.webp?itok=Dx6RHw8U)

### [Biofactorial High\-Throughput Biology Facility](https://lsi.ubc.ca/biofactorial/#:~:text=The%20High%20Throughput%20Biology%20Facility,at%20new%20economies%20of%20scale.)

The Biofactorial High\-Throughput Biology Facility is equipped with high\-throughput automation infrastructure, including for liquid handling and dispensing, plate reading, cell plating, colony picking, and library preparation for sequencing, and additionally provides access to metagenomic and chemical compound libraries for screening\.

[Learn more](https://lsi.ubc.ca/biofactorial/#:~:text=The%20High%20Throughput%20Biology%20Facility,at%20new%20economies%20of%20scale.)

[Microscopy equipment](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_300x150/public/2025-11/Facilities%20-%20LSI%20Imaging.jpg.webp?itok=y-GM5A-y)

### [Super Resolution Microscopy Core](https://imaging.lsi.ubc.ca/)

The Super Resolution Microscopy Core \(SRMC\) facility provides access to state\-of\-the\-art equipment for super resolution and confocal microscopy, high content imaging, live cell imaging, and analysis software\. Users benefit from expert training and support on all instrumentation\.

[Learn more](https://imaging.lsi.ubc.ca/)

[three large freezers in a laboratory](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_300x150/public/2025-11/Facilities%20-%20FINDER.jpg.webp?itok=_cSVvcqt)

### [Facility for Infectious Disease and Epidemic Research](https://finder.ubc.ca/)

The Facility for Infectious Disease and Epidemic Research \(FINDER\) is a 6,700\-square foot containment level 3 facility offering self\-contained suites for microbiology, proteomics, imaging, flow cytometry, and freezer storage\. FINDER supports research in bacterial and viral infectious diseases and the development of vaccines and therapeutics for risk group 3 pathogens\.

[Learn more](https://finder.ubc.ca/)

[A colourful crystallographic protein structure](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_300x150/public/2025-11/Facilities%20-%20HRMEM.png.webp?itok=W8tKkRMH)

### [High Resolution Macromolecular Cryo\-Electron Microscopy](https://cryoem.med.ubc.ca/)

The High Resolution Macromolecular Cryo\-Electron Microscopy \(HRMEM\) facility provides researchers with resources for high\-resolution cryo\-electron microscopy, including Talos, Glacios, and Krios microscopes, and equipment for sample preparation\.

[Learn more](https://cryoem.med.ubc.ca/)

[A hand holding a white mouse and a black mouse\.](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_300x150/public/2025-11/Facilities%20-%20CDM_1.jpg.webp?itok=hvrMuTWc)

### [Centre for Diseaes Modelling](https://animalcare.ubc.ca/)

The Centre for Diseaes Modelling \(CDM\) is a small rodent animal facility\. The CDM is part of the UBC Animal Care and Use Program, a university\-wide program that provides training and resources to all UBC persons who work with animals in their research\.

[Learn more](https://animalcare.ubc.ca/)

[Freezers](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_300x150/public/2026-07/Screenshot%202026-07-23%20at%208.39.37%E2%80%AFAM.png.webp?itok=PmPp2MlQ)

### [UBC BioBank](https://biobank.mbim.ubc.ca/)

The UBC BioBank provides researchers with resources and expertise for streamlined sample handling and management\. Key services include secure, monitored sample storage, sampling support, shipping logistics, collections archiving, on\-the bench lab services, and inventory management training for students and staff\.

[Learn more](https://biobank.mbim.ubc.ca/)

[Mouse cages](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_300x150/public/2026-07/Screenshot%202026-07-23%20at%208.41.45%E2%80%AFAM.png.webp?itok=8Y3C5MeA)

### [UBC GnotoCore](https://lsi.ubc.ca/ubc-gnotocore/)

The UBC GnotoCore is a state\-of\-the\-art facility advancing gnotobiotic research in Canada\. The GnotoCore offers specialized resources to support academic and industry\-based researchers from UBC, affiliated sites, and external companies or institutes who want to study host\-microbiota interactions\.

[Learn more](https://lsi.ubc.ca/ubc-gnotocore/)
