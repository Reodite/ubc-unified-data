Research to improve care and relieve suffering for those nearing end of life\.

Palliative and End of Life research focuses on finding innovative ways to improve the quality of life for those living with life\-limiting medical conditions and declining health as well as exploring strategies to relieve suffering at end of life\.

Topics also include palliative care and the development of medical assistance in dying \(MAiD\) in the Canadian context\.
