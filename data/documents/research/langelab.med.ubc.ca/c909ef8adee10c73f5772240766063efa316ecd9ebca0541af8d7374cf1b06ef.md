## Inspiring the Next Generation

We’re thrilled that **ScienceVega**, a fantastic initiative bringing real research to high school students, featured our work in their latest issue\! Our team discusses how we use proteomics to understand patient tumors, what a day in the lab looks like, and our hopes for the future of pediatric cancer research\.

Please share this with any budding scientists you know\!

[Read the Issue \(See Page 42\)](https://www.sciencevega.com/flipbook/ScienceVega-July-Issue-3.html)
