Postdoctoral Fellow \(PATH\)

#### About Me

> Coming from a cancer histopathology background, my work aims to integrate spatial biology and proteomics to identify clinically meaningful signatures in neuroblastoma \(the most common extracranial childhood tumor\) that may help refine prognostication, better understand disease progression, and inform precision oncology strategies\. When I’m not in the lab, I enjoy playing piano and my guitar and biking around Vancouver with my husband\.

[Sharvari Somayaji](https://med-fom-lange-lab.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/Shar-scaled-e1757811239115.jpg)

Sharvari Somayaji

PhD Student \(Joint with Kaigala Lab, BMEG\)

**Email:** somayaji@student\.ubc\.ca

#### About Me

> I work on integrating microfluidic tools with mass spectrometric techniques to spatially probe microscale regions of cell monolayers and tissues, and analyze their proteomic profiles\. The aim is to gain a comprehensive understanding of heterogeneous cell populations within the tumour microenvironment, which can ultimately inform better treatment strategies for these pathologies\. Apart from work, I enjoy singing, storytelling, practicing yoga, hiking, travelling, and playing table tennis and board games\.

[Verena Goebeler](https://med-fom-lange-lab.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/Goebeler-Verena-Nov-2019-Photoshoot-4-scaled-e1757809948531.jpg)

Verena Goebeler, PhD

Senior Research Technician \(BRAvE\)

**Email:** verena\.goebeler@bcchr\.ca

#### About Me

> I specialize in laser capture microdissection \(LCM\) and proteomics, techniques I use to uncover the molecular signatures of cancer\. My work is integral to the field of precision oncology, as I use advanced methods to isolate specific cell populations from tissue samples\. This enables detailed proteomic analysis, providing critical insights into the unique protein profiles of tumours\. The data I generate directly supports the development of personalized treatment strategies\. I enjoy collaborating with other scientists to advance our collective research, and in my free time, I find balance and clarity on the open road—a passionate cyclist, rain or shine\.

Jehan Nazri

#### About Me

> As the mass spec technician of the lab, my role involves day\-to\-day operation and maintenance of our mass spectrometer instruments, in addition to supporting various other activities of the lab and its members\. I am also focused on developing new targeted proteomics methods, using parallel reaction monitoring \(PRM\), to quantitatively assess relevant players of key signalling pathways in pediatric cancers\. When I am not in the lab, I enjoy composing floral arrangements and catching a play at the theatre\.

[Maria Jose Garcia](https://med-fom-lange-lab.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/Maria-Jose-Garcia-Picture-scaled-e1757811372594.jpg)

Maria Jose Garcia

PhD Student \(Joint with Reid Lab, EXMD\)

**Email:** mariajose\.garcia@bcchr\.ca

#### About Me

> My research focuses on investigating how early\-life infections can impact B\-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia \(B\-ALL\) progression and how we can utilize these immune responses to induce anti\-leukemic activity\. I aim to utilize both in vivo and in vitro models to generate key insights for B\-ALL prevention and explore a potential new clinical application of immune system modulation in treating children with B\-ALL\. Outside the lab, I enjoy crochet, baking and reading fiction\.
