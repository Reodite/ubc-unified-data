## Dr\. Meagan Curtis

###### PhD, 2023, Faculty of Forestry, University of British Columbia

Meagan completed her PhD on agricultural food sufficiency in Alberni\-Clayoquot, Canada in 2023 found here:

[https://open\.library\.ubc\.ca/soa/cIRcle/collections/ubctheses/24/items/1\.0434226](https://open.library.ubc.ca/soa/cIRcle/collections/ubctheses/24/items/1.0434226)

**Keywords:**

food security; food justice; food sufficiency; food economics; food ethics; metaphysics of food; right to food; supply chain economics; regulatory ethics; food governance
