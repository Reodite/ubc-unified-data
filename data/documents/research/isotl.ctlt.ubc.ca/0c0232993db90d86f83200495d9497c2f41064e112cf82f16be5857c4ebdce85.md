Many people work to support the Institute for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning:

-   the [Leadership team](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/people/isotl-leadership/) implements and co\-ordinates the activities and services of the Institute;
-   the [SoTL Specialists](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/people/sotl-specialists/) work closely with faculty to support SoTL inquiries;
-   the [ISoTL Members](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/people/isotl-members/) work to strengthen the UBC community of SoTL practitioners; and
-   the [Advisory Committee](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/people/advisory-committee/) provides advice and strategic recommendations on the activities and programming of the Institute\.

We express gratitude to the [former ISoTL Directors](https://isotl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about/people/isotl-leadership/past-directors/) and [former Specialists](https://isotl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about/people/sotl-specialists/alumni-sotl-specialists/), who contributed greatly to the work we do today\.
