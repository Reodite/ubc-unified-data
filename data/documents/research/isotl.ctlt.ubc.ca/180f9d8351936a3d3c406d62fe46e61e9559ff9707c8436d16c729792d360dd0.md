#### **Celebrate SoTL 2019**

The second annual Celebrate SoTL Day, co\-hosted by the Institute for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(ISoTL\), the Centre for Teaching, Learning, &amp; Technology \(CTLT\), and the SoTL Community of Practice, took place Thursday, October 24, 2019\. Over 60 faculty, staff and students attended the full\-day event which provided the opportunity for novice and experienced SoTL practitioners to share their stories, and engage in dialogues around teaching and learning\.

In case you missed it, you can read a recap of the event [here](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/2019/11/06/celebrate-sotl-2019/)\.

**Schedule of Events**

-   Keynote: *Threshold Concepts in SoTL:* Dr\. Andrea Webb – Instructor, Faculty of Education, Department of Curriculum and Pedagogy
-   Panel Discussion: *What is Learning and What is Worth Measuring?*
-    e\-Poster session: *[Celebrate SoTL ePoster Program 2019](https://isotl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/11/Celebrate-SoTL-ePoster-Program-2019.pdf)*
-   Workshop: *Inclusive SoTL Research*
