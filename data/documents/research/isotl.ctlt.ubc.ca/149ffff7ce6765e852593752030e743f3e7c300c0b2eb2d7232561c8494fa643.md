Principal Investigator: Chris Addison, Associate Professor of Teaching, Chemistry

## Project description

This project will have three components: \(1\) The applicants will develop a set of classroom materials that offer “training” module in the use of ChatGPT \(coupled with an existing unit on academic integrity\)\. \(2\) Incorporation of ChatGPT into a major writing assignment, where students will compare their original writing to a ChatGPT\-generated version\. \(3\) Survey and focus groups of students who complete the classroom module to assess their level of comfort using ChatGPT pre/post, how the use of ChatGPT influenced their writing practice, and if they can identify discipline\-specific errors made by the program\.

## Research questions

-   To what extent are students comfortable using ChatGPT and AI as a tool in communication?
-   Does training with the tool enhance that comfort?
-   In what ways can ChatGPT be used by students to reflect on and improve their own writing practices?
-   Can students recognize the limitations of AI tools in chemical communication, and can they correct errors?
