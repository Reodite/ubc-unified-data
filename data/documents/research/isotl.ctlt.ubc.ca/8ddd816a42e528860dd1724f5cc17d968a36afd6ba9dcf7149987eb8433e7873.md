Project Lead: Moberley Luger \(Associate Professor of Teaching, English Language and Literatures\)  
Co\-Leads: Sandra Zappa\-Hollman \(Associate Professor, Language &amp; Literacy Education\), and Marie\-Eve Bouchard \(Assistant Professor, French, Hispanic &amp; Italian Studies\)

## Project Summary

The project is focused on promoting linguistic justice at UBC; it acknowledges that linguistic discrimination \(e\.g\. accent discrimination\) is insidious in our classrooms, affecting both instructors and students who speak English in non\-normative ways; it responds to the StEAR Framework’s recommendation to “develop resources on raciolinguistic diversity\.” Understanding what emerged in our focus groups is a first step toward this work\.

## Research Questions

1.  What are some of the challenges that multilingual students face in UBC classrooms?
2.  How do non\-normative accents and dialects impact belonging at UBC?
3.  What can we learn from multilingual UBC students about how to promote linguistic justice in our classrooms?
