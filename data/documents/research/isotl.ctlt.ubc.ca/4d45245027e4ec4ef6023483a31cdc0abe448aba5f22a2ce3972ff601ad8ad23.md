Project Lead: Siobhán Wittig McPhee \(Associate Professor of Teaching, Geography\)

## Project Summary

This project evaluates the impact of interactive Digital/AI Literacy modules \(on the Tapestry Plus platform\) on undergraduate students\. I am investigating whether scenario\-based, critical instruction on topics like cyber safety significantly increases students’ critical digital literacy self\-efficacy and prompts measurable behavioural changes\. It is critical because students often lack the ethical framework to navigate the data\-intensive, AI\-driven world, which poses risks to their academic integrity, privacy, and future professional lives\. The findings will establish an evidence base for scaling critical AI/digital literacy\.

## Research Questions

1.  How does engaging with the interactive modules affect students’ critical digital literacy self\-efficacy \(data privacy, cyber safety, information evaluation\) over the term?
2.  To what extent does the Tapestry Plus interactive, self\-paced format promote deeper student engagement and understanding of complex concepts like surveillance capitalism?
3.  What are students’ reported transferable learning outcomes and intended behavioural changes regarding their personal and academic use of digital/AI tools?
