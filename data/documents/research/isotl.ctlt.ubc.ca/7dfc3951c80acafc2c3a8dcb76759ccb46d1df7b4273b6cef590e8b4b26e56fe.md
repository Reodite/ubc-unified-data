In 2020, an interdisciplinary Advisory Committee was established with the goal of providing advice and strategic recommendations on the activities and programming of the Institute\. This includes identifying strategic lines of scholarly activity that the Institute may want to support/promote, advising on plans of action to align ISoTL programming with UBC’s strategic plans, promoting ISoTL in their home departments/units, and generally offering feedback and guidance on ISoTL service offerings\.

The ISoTL Advisory Committee is chaired by the Director, Institute for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Elisa Baniassad, and current membership is:

-   Elisa Baniassad \(Professor of Teaching, Science; Academic Director, CTLT, *pro tem*\)
-   Silvia Bartolic \(Associate Professor of Teaching, Arts\)
-   Simon Bates \(Professor of Teaching, Science; Vice\-Provost and Associate Vice\-President, Teaching and Learning\)
-   Adriana Briseño\-Garzón \(Senior Manager, Research and Evaluation, CTLT\)
-   Sara Harris \(Professor of Teaching; Senior Associate Dean, Science\)
-   Christina Hendricks \(Professor of Teaching, Arts; Vice Provost and AVP *pro tem* \(until June 2024\)\)
-   Siobhán McPhee \(Associate Professor of Teaching; Associate Dean, Equity, Innovation &amp; Strategy, Arts\)
-   Jaclyn Stewart \(Professor of Teaching; Associate Dean Academic, Science\)
-   Andrea Webb \(Associate Professor of Teaching, Education\)
