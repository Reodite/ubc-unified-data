Project Lead: Scott Covey \(Associate Professor of Teaching, Biochemistry and Molecular Biology\)  
Co\-Leads: Nina Radisavljevic \(Postdoctoral Teaching Fellow, Biochemistry and Molecular Biology\), and Hannah Shariati \(Graduate Student, Biochemistry and Molecular Biology\)

## Project Summary

Career readiness is a key concern for many students, and new technologies and scientific techniques are rapidly changing the molecular biosciences employment landscape\. As educators, competitiveness in the job market is an important goal for our students; however, a deeper understanding of the specific skills, knowledge, and qualifications that employers are seeking is required in order to better align teaching practices with this desired outcome\. This project will survey employers of molecular biosciences graduates in both industry and academia across Canada\. The information obtained will be used to inform course content within the Biochemistry and Molecular Biology department at UBC\.

## Research Question

Which technical skills, disciplinary knowledge, transferrable skills, and qualifications are most important for employers of molecular biosciences graduates in biotechnology industries and academia?
