## Project Summary

As Ungrading has evolved in our courses over the last 5 years, we have introduced individual student designed learning goals to guide students in monitoring their progress throughout the term\. Each week, students are asked to state their learning objectives before coming to class and reflect on these goals at the end of each class\. These reflections are used as part of the Ungrading portfolio\. By emancipating students from our own constructs of learning goals, we honour the prior knowledge they bring in as well as other ways of understanding the learning process\. A survey and analysis of student engagement metrics will be conducted to assess the impact of designing learning goals on students\.

## Research Questions

1.  Does designing learning goals impact students’ ownership of their learning, and in what ways?
2.  Does designing learning goals increase or decrease student engagement with course material?
3.  Do learning goals change in depth/quality with student experience in writing these goals?
