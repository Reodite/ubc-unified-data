Project Lead: Johana Thomas Zapata \(Postdoctoral Fellow, Mathematics\)  
Co\-Leads: Fok\-Shuen Leung \(Professor of Teaching, Mathematics\); Alexis Okabe \(Indigenous Education Program Advisor, NITEP\)

## Project Summary

This project is centred on a capstone group assignment in a section of MATH 335 \(Introduction to Mathematics\) designed exclusively for the NITEP Indigenous Teacher Education Program\. It investigates students’ responses to the open\-ended task of designing a mathematics lesson using the K\-7 BC Curriculum and presenting the lesson to their classmates\. Students are encouraged, but not required, to incorporate cooperative learning structures and culturally responsive practices, including Indigenous ways of knowing, into their lessons and presentations\. By examining their work and their reflections on it, this project will generate evidence of effective practices that support culturally responsive mathematics teaching\.

## Research Questions

1.  How do the design and discussions of the assignment reflect culturally responsive approaches to mathematics teaching, particularly in relation to Indigenous ways of knowing?
2.  What forms of pedagogical Indigenous knowledge emerge in students’ lesson designs and classroom interactions during the assignment?
3.  In what ways does engaging with Indigenous perspectives through the assignment influence teacher candidates’ confidence and identities as future mathematics educators?
