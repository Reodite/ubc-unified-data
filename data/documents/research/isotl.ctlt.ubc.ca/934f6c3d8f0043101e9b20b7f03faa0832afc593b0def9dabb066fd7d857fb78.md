Project Lead: Jon Beasley\-Murray \(Associate Professor, French, Hispanic, and Italian Studies\)

## Project Summary

This project will examine the effect of alternative grading \(specifically, the contract grading system that I have developed\) on student attitudes, experience, and motivation in a broad Arts course that most of them are taking as an elective\. I am particularly interested in the impact of contract grading in an environment increasingly shaped 1\) by student anxiety and uncertainty, perhaps above all in Arts courses, whose expectations often are felt to be opaque, and 2\) by the ready availability of genAI tools \(ChatGPT, Claude, etc\.\), which at first sight offer them easy ways to bypass those expectations\.

## Research Questions

1.  To what extent might contract grading relieve anxiety and stress, allowing students to do their best work unburdened from the constant pressure of the grade?
2.  To what extent might contract grading lead to other forms of motivation, whether intrinsic or extrinsic, encouraging students to do more and perhaps even better work without the promise of the grade?
3.  To what extent might contract grading diminish the temptation to see genAI as a quick fix, empowering students to recognize and draw on their own abilities and voice?
