Project Lead: Yilin Pan \(Assistant Professor in Economics of Educational Equity, Educational Studies\)

## Project Summary

This project investigates an inquiry\-based, experiential, and comparative approach to teaching responsible AI use in research\. Rather than prescribing AI use or prohibition, students first complete research tasks manually, then use AI tools to support or replicate the same tasks, and finally compare, verify, and reflect on the differences\. The project examines whether this guided empirical comparison helps students develop critical AI literacy and evidence\-based criteria for appropriate AI use\. Findings may inform AI\-related pedagogy in research methodology courses and beyond\.

## Research Questions

1.  How does structured comparison between human\-generated and AI\-generated research products shape students’ understanding of responsible AI use in educational research?
2.  What kinds of AI errors, omissions, or distortions do students identify when using tools for literature review and research design?
3.  Does this activity improve students’ confidence and ability to verify AI\-generated research outputs?
