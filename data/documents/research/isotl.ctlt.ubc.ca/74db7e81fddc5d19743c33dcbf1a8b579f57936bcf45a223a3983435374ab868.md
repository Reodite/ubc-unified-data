Our 8th annual Celebrate SoTL Day took place in\-person on Thursday, October 9th, 2025\. This year we offered **two lightning talk sessions**, as in the past these have proven to be an engaging way for our UBC SoTL community to connect and share their important work\. These 12 **lightning talks** provided a way to celebrate and reflect on the amazing SoTL happening across our institution\. The talk slides are included below, but please connect with any of the presenters directly to learn more about their work:

Our final session this year was a **keynote** from Simon Bates, Vice Provost and Associate Vice\-President, Teaching and Learning\. He shared insights and data in his talk: “Institutional Culture around Teaching Advancement: Where SoTL plays a critical role”\. He also shared the exciting update that the [SoTL Linkages Grants](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-linkage-grants/) will be renewed, so look forward to updates on that coming soon\!

Celebrate SoTL provided an opportunity for roughly 40 UBC SoTL practitioners to share their stories from practice, engage in dialogue around teaching and learning topics, and network with colleagues across disciplines\. We hope you enjoyed the day and hope to see you next year\!
