#### **Celebrate SoTL 2018**

The first UBC “Celebrate SoTL” event, co\-hosted by the Institute for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(ISoTL\), the Centre for Teaching, Learning, &amp; Technology \(CTLT\), and the SoTL Community of Practice, took place on November 6, 2018\. Over 40 faculty, staff and students attended workshops on taking the very first steps in SoTL and disseminating SoTL work, we also had 15 e\-poster presentations on SoTL research\. This full\-day event was an opportunity for novice and experienced practitioners across UBC to share their stories, connect with peers, and move SoTL work forward\.

**Schedule of Events**

-   Workshop: *Getting started with SoTL*
-   Keynote: *Why UBC needs \(more\) SoTL:* Dr\. Simon Bates – Associate Provost, Teaching and Learning
-   e\-Poster session: *[Celebrate SoTL e\-Poster Program](https://isotl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/11/Celebrate-SoTL-e-Poster-Program-2018.pdf)*
-   Workshop: *Getting your SoTL work published*
