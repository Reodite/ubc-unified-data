The UBC Institute for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(ISoTL\) offers a grant opportunity for faculty members to conduct research in Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\)\.

The SoTL Seed program provides graduate research assistant support, collegial collaborations, and complementary funding for SoTL projects\.

Selected projects incorporate research on teaching and learning in higher education with a potential for significant contributions to the UBC community and beyond\. Projects are strategically selected to reflect the breadth of work at UBC in terms of disciplines, scholarly approaches, and seniority\.

The application deadline has been extended to November 15, 2017, and results will be announced on December 8\.

To apply, click [here](https://survey.ubc.ca/s/SoTLSeed/application2017w1/)\.

For a list of projects that are funded through the program, please see the [2014](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/get-involved/sotl-seed-program/2014-projects/), [2015](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/get-involved/sotl-seed-program/2015-projects/), [2016](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/get-involved/sotl-seed-program/2016-projects/), [Winter](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/sotl-seed-program/sotl-awarded-projects/winter-2017-awarded-projects/) [2017](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/sotl-seed-program/sotl-awarded-projects/winter-2017-awarded-projects/) and [Summer 2017](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/sotl-seed-program/sotl-awarded-projects/summer-2017-awarded-projects/) awarded projects\.
