The Institute for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(ISoTL\) supports research, inquiry and reflection on teaching and learning at UBC\.

Explore this section of our website to learn more about: what it means to [participate in](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/about/sotl/) the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\), the [history and goals](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/about/isotl/) of ISoTL at UBC, the [team](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/about/people/) that makes up ISoTL and the [different ways we can work together](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/)\.
