**PhD student, Educational Studies**

Diego holds a B\.A\. in Sociology from the Pontifical Catholic University of Peru and an M\.A\. in Policy Studies in Education from the University College London\. Before joining UBC, he worked as a consultant and research specialist in education policy, conducting qualitative research and evaluations to inform policymaking\. His current research interests include the politics of higher education, digital technologies, knowledge production, and education policy and governance\.
