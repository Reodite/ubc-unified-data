## Project Summary

This project investigates the motivations and goals for instructor\-formed groups in undergraduate classrooms\. Group work facilitates active learning strategies, improves student academic performance, and supports the development of soft skills required for their future careers\. This project will discern how instructors form teams, the pedagogical context of team activities, and whether student experiences align with the target learning goals\.

## Research Questions

1.  What criteria do instructors consider when making teams?
2.  What do students look for in an ideal team?
3.  How does the students’ team experience align with the instructor’s learning goal?
