## Project Summary

The redesign of APSC 183 addresses a gap in the explicit teaching of laboratory skills prior to second\-year disciplinary courses\. The course uses a scaffolded pedagogical structure that introduces progressively complex concepts in data analysis and scientific writing, reinforced through laboratory experiments where students immediately apply these skills\. Students progress from structured reporting tasks toward greater autonomy, culminating in an independent project involving background research, experimental design, data collection and analysis, and communication of findings\. This project investigates how foregrounding laboratory skills influences students’ ability to engage in inquiry, reflection, and evidence\-based reasoning when solving open\-ended problems\.

## Research Questions

1.  How does scaffolded pedagogical structure manages to emphasize / develop inquiry, reflection, and evidence\-based reasoning in a laboratory environment?
2.  How isolating and foregrounding laboratory skill development in first year, better prepares students for open\-ended problem solving in upper\-year courses?
