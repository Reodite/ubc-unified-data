## Project Summary

This SoTL project explores how to strengthen experiential learning in pharmacy primary care practice settings by examining the intrinsic motivations and pedagogical practices that make precepting meaningful\. Using an appreciative inquiry framework, the study aims to move beyond barrier\-focused research to identify values, rewarding experiences, and effective teaching approaches that inspire engagement\. Having completed the Discovery phase, the Dream, Design, and Destiny phases remain, with the aim to co\-develop strategies to enhance placement capacity and enrich student learning\.

## Research Questions

1.  What are primary care pharmacists’ visions for an ideal primary care learning experience for pharmacy students and the potential contributions students could make in this setting?
2.  What structures, resources, and practice\-integrated activities do primary care pharmacists identify as necessary to design and support a feasible and effective primary care practicum model?
3.  What actions, partnerships, and ongoing supports do primary care pharmacists view as essential to implementing and sustaining primary care preceptorship opportunities over time?
