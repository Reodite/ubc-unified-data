Project Lead: Jennifer Brown \(Assistant Professor of Teaching, Pharmaceutical Sciences\)

## Project Summary

I developed a learning\-theory\-informed communication framework that focuses on cognitive load, multimedia, audience attention, and accessibility\. This framework states that a presentation should encourage the audience’s motivation, access, reception, processing, and assimilation \(MARPA\) of information to be able to effectively support the audience in understanding the key messages\. This project aims to examine how the explicit instruction of science communication practices through this learning\-theory framework impacts students’ slide design, oral delivery, and confidence\. This work is important because my integrated approach to teaching science communication is novel\. I anticipate this could contribute to more effective and inclusive presentation practices\.

## Research Questions

1.  How does explicit instruction grounded in learning theory influence the slide design choices, oral delivery, and quality of students’ scientific presentations in PHAR 480?
2.  How do presentations following the MARPA approach impact audience understanding and perceived cognitive load?
3.  How do students report their self\-efficacy and confidence after engaging with this instruction?
