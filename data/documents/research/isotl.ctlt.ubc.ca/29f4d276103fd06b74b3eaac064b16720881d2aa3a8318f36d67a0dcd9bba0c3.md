Project Lead: Luana Carlvalho \(Assistant Professor of Teaching, Oral Health Sciences \(Dentistry\)\)

## Project Summary

This study will assess the impact of instructional videos on first\-year dental students’ learning in the simulation lab\. Since 2020, Restorative Dentistry modules have incorporated procedure\-based videos to support the development of essential psychomotor skills\. The research will evaluate their effectiveness by comparing student performance before and after video use, alongside surveys and interviews for qualitative insights\. Guided by the Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning, the study explores how combining visuals and description enhances understanding and reduces cognitive load\. Findings are expected to show improved theoretical knowledge

## Research Questions

1.  To what extent do instructional videos improve first\-year dental students’ understanding of restorative procedures?
2.  How do these videos impact students’ practical skills in restorative dentistry during simulation lab sessions?
3.  What are students’ perceptions of the effectiveness of these instructional videos in aiding their learning?
