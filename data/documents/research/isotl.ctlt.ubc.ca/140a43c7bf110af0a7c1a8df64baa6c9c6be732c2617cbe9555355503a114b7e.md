**Principal Investigator**: Saranaz Barforoush, Assistant Professor of Teaching, School of Journalism, Writing, and Media

## Project description

Since I started teaching core classes in journalism education, I have always asked my students the first day ” why they want to do journalism ” each year the amount of students interested in doing journalism for the traditional values of objective news reporting is declining\. Most students , at least in a informal class surveys, are stating they are interested in journalism because of its idealistic nature and to use the skills for advocacy causes , which may seem contradictory to those invested in traditional journalism values and guidelines\.

## Research questions

-   What is motivating journalism student to chose journalism school?
-   What are the main values journalism students identify in journalism?
-   What do most students hope to gain from a degree in journalism?
