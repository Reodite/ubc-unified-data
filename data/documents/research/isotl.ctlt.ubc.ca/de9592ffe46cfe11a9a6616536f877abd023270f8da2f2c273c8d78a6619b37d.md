co\-Leads: Bruce Rusk \(Associate Professor, Asian Studies\) and Clayton Ashton \(Lecturer, Asian Studies\)

## Project Summary

This project aims to enable students to acknowledge, identify, and define the nature of free ChatGPT as a tool in their learning process in CHIN 465 \(Fantasy in Chinese Literature\), an advanced content\-based Chinese language and literature course\. By encouraging students to take a comparative approach to reading, analyzing, and evaluating the answers they get from ChatGPT, students are expected to strengthen their own critical skills of reading, analyzing, and interpreting a literary text\. Ultimately, students will take the learning agency into their own hand\.

## Research Questions

1.  What difference does ChatGPT make to students’ close reading of a literary work?
2.  What kind of learning activities can enable students to know that ChatGPT is not an authority that replaces them, but a tool that is pending on their review, in their learning process?
3.  What benefits can students get from the traps set up by ChatGPT hallucinations?
