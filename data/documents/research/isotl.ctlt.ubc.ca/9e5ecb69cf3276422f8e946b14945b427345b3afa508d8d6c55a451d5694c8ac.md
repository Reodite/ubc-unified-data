-   **[SoTL Seed Fund](https://isotl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-seed-program/):** Funding for starting a new SoTL Project\. Up to $5K of in\-kind support from a SoTL specialist who can help you begin your SoTL journey\.

-   [**SoTL Linkage Grants**](https://isotl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-linkage-grants/): Funding for interdisciplinary collaborative SoTL projects\. Up to $25K of direct funding to facilitate multi\-disciplinary groups of SoTL researchers to collaborate towards joint scholarly works\.

-   **[SoTL Dissemination Fund](https://isotl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-dissemination-fund/):** Funding is available for travel to conferences or publication fees\.
