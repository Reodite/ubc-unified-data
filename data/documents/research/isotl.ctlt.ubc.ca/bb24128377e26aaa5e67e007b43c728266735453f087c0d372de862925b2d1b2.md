and Larry Leung \(Associate Professor of teaching, Pharmaceutical Sciences\)

## Project Summary

This project examines the perspectives of Indigenous patients and healthcare providers at Burnaby Hospital as part of their commitment to develop tangible and actionable responses to improving cultural safety\. Using mixed methods, the project will identify opportunities for the pharmacy department to develop new programming specific to their hospital, including clinical processes when traditional medicines are used\. By identifying knowledge gaps, service barriers, and educational needs, the study will inform the design of practical interventions for staff and pharmacy students\. This work will then be incorporated into pre\-experiential education curriculum and in the existing cultural safety curriculum in pharmacy\.

## Research Questions

1.  How do Indigenous patients experience care at Burnaby Hospital, particularly regarding access to cultural supports and traditional medicines, and what learning needs do these experiences reveal for pharmacy education and practice?
2.  What are the current practices of healthcare providers, particularly pharmacy staff, in supporting culturally safe care and traditional medicines, and what changes are needed to enhance this practice?
