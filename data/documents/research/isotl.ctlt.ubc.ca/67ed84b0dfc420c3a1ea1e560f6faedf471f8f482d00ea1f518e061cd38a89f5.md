Project Lead: Bavisha Kalyan \(Assistant Professor, Civil Engineering\)

## Project Summary

This pilot project will explore how participatory education and community\-based research can be integrated into engineering pedagogy to promote deeper student engagement, ethical awareness, and real\-world problem solving\. Using a mixed\-methods, classroom\-based research design, students in a one term Community\-Engaged Engineering \(CIVIL 489\) course will serve as participants in a study that applies Participatory Action Research \(PAR\) principles\. In phase two of the pilot, materials will be tailored to test in a core undergraduate one\-semester course, Impacts of Civil Engineering \(CIVIL 302\)\. The research will investigate how students’ perception of the engineering profession, engagement with course materials, and learning of participatory design methods shift through involvement in participatory and justice\-oriented curriculum\.

## Research Questions

1.  What pedagogical practices foster a participatory and liberatory learning environment in engineering classrooms?
2.  How do students engage with issues of power, equity, and collaboration when participatory action research \(PAR\) principles are embedded in course design?
3.  In what ways does participation in a community\-engaged engineering course transform students’ understanding of their role as future engineers?
