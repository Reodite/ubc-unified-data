## Project Summary

We are investigating how social constructivist \(SC\) pedagogies support the development of integrity\-based behaviours and/or reduce the risk of academic misconduct\. We wish to better understand the faculty and the student perspective of a SC classroom as well as their experience beliefs around academic integrity \(AcI\) and academic misconduct within this setting\. We believe that a SC approach to curriculum design prioritizes relationship building and a strong social contract which creates a classroom culture in which students are less likely to commit misconduct\. A survey of both faculty and undergraduate students will be conducted to enhance our understanding of the relationship between SC and AcI\.

## Research Questions

1.  How do STEM educators implement SC approaches in their teaching, and what are students’ experiences with these methods?
2.  What are the faculty experiences with academic misconduct in SC classrooms?
3.  What are students’ experiences and beliefs about academic misconduct within classrooms that utilize SC approaches?
