## Project Summary

While knowledge forms the base of Bloom’s taxonomy of educational objectives, this same work comments that ease of instruction and evaluation at this level may produce an emphasis on memorization that is out of touch with its usefulness\. This project will investigate the perceived role of memorization in the Biochemistry and Molecular Biology department at UBC\. We seek to better understand the value of memorization by both students and instructors in the department\. The aim of this work is to initiate discussions on how best to align teaching practices with program\-level learning outcomes\.

## Research Questions

1.  How important is memorization as a learning goal for teaching faculty in the BMB department?
2.  How important is memorization for the perceived success of students in their biochemistry course or degree?
3.  How does the value placed on memorization change based on educational level or choice of major?
