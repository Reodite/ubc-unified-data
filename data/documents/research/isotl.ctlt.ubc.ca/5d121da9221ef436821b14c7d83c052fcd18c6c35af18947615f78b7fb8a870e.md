Project Lead: Kasim Husain \(Lecturer, Coordinated Arts Program\)

Co\-Leads: Evan Mauro \(Lecturer, Coordinated Arts Program\); Nickolas Toney \(Admissions Advisor \(Indigenous Applicants\), UBC Enrolment Services\); Tashiya Mathuin \(BA Student, Environment and Sustainability\)

## Project Description

We are two lecturers in CAP partnering with two Indigenous alumni of our program to evaluate Indigenous students’ perspectives on how to meaningfully Indigenize university writing course curricula\. We will hold focus groups to determine students’ perspectives on: \(1\) whether Indigenization takes place through course content, assignment design, or other means \(if at all\); and \(2\) how Indigenous students experience Indigenization \(or its absence\)\. We plan to publicize our work as a SOTL research paper discussing our partnership process and focus group outcomes\.

## Research Questions

1.  What are Indigenous students’ actual experiences of extant attempts at Indigenized curriculum?
2.  How can settler faculty and Indigenous students partner to develop Indigenized writing curriculum resources that are responsive to Indigenous student experiences, at the same time as responding to wider institutional mandates to Indigenize higher education?
