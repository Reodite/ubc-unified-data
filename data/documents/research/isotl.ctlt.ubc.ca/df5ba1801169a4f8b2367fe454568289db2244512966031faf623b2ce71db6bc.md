## Project Summary

The goal of this project is to build an inventory of oral proficiency assessment practices that highlights strengths, challenges, and recommendations based on the experiences of instructors in UBC Faculty of Science’s First Year Seminar in Science \(Scie113\)\. The ubiquitous adoption of generative AI tools, and the challenges it presents to academic integrity when assigning students at\-home written homework, has brought back into consideration the crucial role of oral assessments\. The project will include gathering feedback from Scie113 instructors in pilot sections on their practices, and surveying them to learn about their experience and challenges with oral assessments\.

## Research Questions

1.  What characteristics \(e\.g\., teacher\-led, peer\-led or self\-regulated; technology driven\) of the oral assessment methods have proven effective?
2.  What are the most reliable criteria for assessing oral proficiency in university students learning about science communication in several forms?
3.  How consistently are these criteria applied by the selected instructors?
