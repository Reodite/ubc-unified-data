Project Lead: Farzaneh Farhang Mehr \(Assistant Professor of Teaching, Materials Engineering\)

## Project Summary

This project investigates how a deliberately varied sequence of structured multimodal in\-class learning activities influences sustained cognitive engagement in large engineering materials classrooms\. The project will explore guided collaborative activities, interactive concept exploration, and other activity modes embedded within lectures to support active participation and conceptual reasoning while maintaining focus\. The study will examine how these activities influence participation persistence, students’ cognitive engagement, perceptions of different engagement modes, and movement beyond memorization toward conceptual understanding\. This work is important because active\-learning approaches in large engineering classrooms can produce uneven participation, declining engagement over time, or cognitive overload for some students\.

## Research Questions

1.  How do structured multimodal in\-class learning activities influence student participation during lecture sessions and across an academic term?
2.  How do these activities influence students’ sustained cognitive engagement and conceptual understanding in large\-enrolment engineering materials classrooms?
3.  How do students perceive different activity modes as supporting, sustaining, or distracting from cognitive engagement and learning during large engineering materials lectures?
