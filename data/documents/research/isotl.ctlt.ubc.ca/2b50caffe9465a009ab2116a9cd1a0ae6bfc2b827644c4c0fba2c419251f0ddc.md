Project Lead: Fernanda Tomaselli \(Assistant Professor of Teaching, Forest Resources Management\)

## Project Summary

Youth often experience difficult emotions when facing the scale of climate change\. Eco\-anxiety and other emotions can motivate action, but have also been linked to hopelessness and pessimism\. With this project, I aim to investigate the impact of the course Visualizing Climate Change \(CONS 210\) on students’ climate\-related emotions over the duration of the term\. I will examine how shifts in these emotions relate to specific pedagogies in the course such as an action\-based workshop, films, small group discussions and other active learning activities\. This work is important because few studies have examined educational interventions for eco\-anxiety and other climate emotions\.

## Research Questions

1.  How do students’ positive \(e\.g\., hope, empowerment\) and negative \(e\.g\., anxiety, fear\) climate\-related emotions change from the beginning to the end of the term in CONS 210?
2.  Which pedagogical strategies and classroom activities in CONS 210 are associated with observed shifts in students’ climate\-related emotions?
3.  Are shifts in climate\-related emotions linked to changes in students’ self\-reported willingness to take action on climate change?
