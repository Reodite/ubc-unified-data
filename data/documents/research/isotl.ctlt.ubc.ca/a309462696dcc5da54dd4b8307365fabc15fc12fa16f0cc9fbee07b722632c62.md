## Project Summary

This study takes place in Biology 307: Natural History Collections and Biodiversity Science, a new undergraduate biology course that weaves anti\-colonial science throughout the course\. The goal is to investigate the impact of this course design \(deliberately weaving anti\-colonial science throughout the course\) on student learning and attitudes about science and natural history collections\. This practice of weaving the colonial context of science throughout a course is fairly novel for a biology course\. The data will include student written work in the form of weekly reflections written in course journals, and pre\-, mid\-, and post\-course survey responses\. The study methodology applied will be grounded theory\.

## Research Question

Does the inclusion of anti\-colonial science enhance student knowledge and value of natural history collections and biodiversity science?
