The Institute for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning develops events and workshops to meet the needs of the UBC SoTL community\. We offer workshops on a repeating cycle \(typically we repeat the workshop every 6 months\), with [upcoming sessions](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/upcoming-workshops/) added regularly\. Resources from past workshops can be found on the [past events page](https://isotl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/events-2/past-events/)\. Our annual [Celebrate SoTL Event](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/events-2/celebrate-sotl/) \(typically hosted in October\) invites SoTL practitioners, faculty, staff and students the opportunity to share learnings and excitement related to SoTL work at UBC\.
