**PhD student, Cognitive Psychology**

Gray is interested in connections between cognitive science and media studies\. In research conducted during a B\.S\. in Cognitive Science at UC Santa Cruz and during an M\.A\. in Psychology at UBC, he studied the impact of virtual reality on time perception\. As a Ph\.D\. student, he now uses eye tracking to study how readers are affected by stylistic choices in literary writing\.
