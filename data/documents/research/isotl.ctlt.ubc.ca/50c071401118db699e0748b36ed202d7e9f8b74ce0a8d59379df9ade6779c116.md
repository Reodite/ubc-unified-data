Project Lead: Negar Harandi \(Assistant Professor of Teaching, Biomedical Engineering\)  
Co\-Leads: Jenna Usprech \(Associate Professor of Teaching, Biomedical Engineering\); Jonathan Verrett \(Associate Professor of Teaching, Chemical &amp; Biological Engineering\); Lily Chen \(Manager, Applied Science Co\-op\)

## Project Description

Work\-integrated learning \(WIL\) experiences integrate quality experiences in a workplace or practice setting with a student’s academic studies\. Co\-operative education \(Co\-op\) or internships are examples of common WIL experiences for UBC engineering students\. The Faculty of Applied Science \(APSC\) Co\-op office currently runs student and employer surveys and student self\-reflection exercises\. We are looking to ensure co\-op program surveys and reflection exercises better capture data on student skill growth, sense of inclusion and overall learning experience during their engineering co\-op placements\. Improving the surveys and activities will allow the co\-op office to further improve student outcomes from their WIL experiences\.

## Research Questions

1.  How can the Co\-op Program survey and reflection activities be revised to better capture students’ reflections on their development of technical and non\-technical skills, sense of belonging, and overall learning experiences during their engineering co\-op placements?
2.  Do student see reflection during a co\-op experience as a valuable exercise? If not, how could it be made more valuable?
3.  What framework or frameworks would be best suited to evaluate reflection quality in the context of engineering co\-op experience?
