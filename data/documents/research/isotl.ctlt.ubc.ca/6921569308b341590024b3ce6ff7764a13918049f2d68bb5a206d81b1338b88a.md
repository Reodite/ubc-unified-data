| **Our 2026 Celebrate SoTL day will be held Tuesday, October 20th, 2026\.** [**Register today\!**](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/celebrate-sotl-2026/) |

This annual event provides SoTL experts and novices \(faculty, staff and students\!\) across UBC a chance to share, learn and create new collaborations to advance SoTL work\. Typically Celebrate SoTL involves a keynote speaker, an interactive workshop and/or a poster session\.
