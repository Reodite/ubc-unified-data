Project Lead: Anna Dawczyk \(Lecturer, Sociology\)

## Project Summary

Despite the rapid increase in research on students’ AI use, instructor perspective remains strikingly underexamined—leaving a critical gap in our understanding of how AI is shaping teaching practices\. This project investigates the pedagogical impact of AI in university teaching by examining how instructors incorporate AI into teaching \(e\.g\., lectures, assessment design, active\-learning, evaluation\), the perceptions of effectiveness, and pedagogical implications\. Using surveys and interviews, the proposed project will identify emerging practices as this work is essential for developing evidence\-based, ethical guidelines that support effective and equitable instructor use of AI in higher education\.

## Research Questions

1.  How are university instructors integrating AI tools into teaching practice \(e\.g\., lectures, assessment design, active\-learning strategies, evaluation\)?
2.  What perceptions do instructors have about the pedagogical effectiveness and limitations of AI in supporting student learning and enhancing teaching practice?
3.  How does the decision to avoid AI in teaching impact pedagogy?
