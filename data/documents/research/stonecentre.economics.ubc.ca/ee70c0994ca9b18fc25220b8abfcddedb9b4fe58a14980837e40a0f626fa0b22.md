We are excited to introduce the newest cohort of fellows joining the Stone Centre on Wealth and Income Inequality\. This inspiring group of scholars brings fresh ideas, innovative approaches, and a deep dedication to addressing the urgent challenges of wealth concentration and economic inequality\. Their work will play a vital role in driving forward our mission to generate knowledge, spark dialogue, and shape solutions to some of today’s most pressing issues\.

Visit the ‘**[Fellows](https://stonecentre.sites.olt.ubc.ca/fellows/)‘** tab to learn more about each of our fellows\.

**Postdoctoral Fellow**

**[Linda Wu](https://sites.google.com/view/lindawu)** is a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Stone Centre on Wealth and Income Inequality\. Her main research fields are public finance and labor economics, with a focus on wealth transfers and capital taxation\.

**PhD Fellows**

**[Daniel Araújo](https://sites.google.com/view/danielmaraujo/research)** is a PhD candidate in Economics at UBC\. His research focuses on income inequality and intergenerational mobility, particularly emphasizing the dynamics of modern slavery and the long\-term impacts of economic policies\. 

**[Pierre\-Loup Beauregard](https://economics.ubc.ca/profile/pierre-loup-beauregard/)** is a PhD candidate in Economics at UBC\. His research centers on the complex relationship between spatial and wealth inequality\.

**[Sara Benetti](https://economics.ubc.ca/profile/sara-benetti/)** is a PhD candidate in Economics at UBC\. Her research examines how socio\-economic shocks shape inequality in culturally diverse communities\.

**[Angel Espinoza](https://economics.ubc.ca/profile/angel-espinoza-escobar/)** is a is a PhD candidate in Economics at UBC\. His research interests lie at the intersection of labor economics and international trade, with a particular focus on how these fields relate to income and wealth disparities\. 

**[Daniel Guzmán](https://daniel-guzmangiron.com/)** is a PhD candidate in Economics at UBC\. His research lies at the intersection of labour, macroeconomics, and public finance\.

**[Prerna Kundu](https://economics.ubc.ca/profile/prerna-kundu/)** is a PhD candidate in Economics at UBC\. Her research examines the legal and social determinants of wealth inequality, with a focus on gender and land ownership in India\.

**[Sebastian Melo\-Martin](https://economics.ubc.ca/profile/sebastian-melo-martin/)** is a PhD candidate in Economics at UBC\.

**[Vibhu Pratyush](https://economics.ubc.ca/profile/vibhu-pratyush/)** is a PhD candidate in Economics at UBC\.

**[Xiomara Pulido](https://economics.ubc.ca/profile/xiomara-pulido/)** is a PhD candidate in Economics at UBC\. Her research explores how environmental policies and climate shocks shape income and wealth inequality\.

**[Diana Ricciulli](https://economics.ubc.ca/profile/diana-ricciulli-marin/)** is a PhD candidate in Economics at UBC\. Her research examines how wealth inequality shapes tax systems and the effects of taxation on firms, workers, and housing markets\.

**[Ratzanyel Rincón](https://economics.ubc.ca/profile/ratzanyel-rincon/)** is a PhD Candidate in Economics at UBC\. His research focuses on studying how labour regulations and public policy affect inequality in the labour market\. 

**[Carla Srebot Roeder](https://pe.linkedin.com/in/carla-srebot)** is a PhD Candidate in Economics at UBC\. Her research examines how labor market institutions influence inequality and worker mobility\.

**MA Thesis Fellows**

**Danny Lu** is an MA Thesis student in Economics at UBC\. His research interests span economic history, political economy, and behavioural economics\. 

**Brian Wall** is an MA Thesis student in Economics at UBC and an academic emergency physician with over 15 years of clinical and teaching experience across three Canadian provinces\. 

**MA Fellows**

Shihan Chen, Dui Davidsson, Victoria Fernandes, Roa’a Hamed, Harlan Hofmann\-Miller, Anya Petek, and Katherine Townson\-Carolan are Master’s of Arts in Economics students at the Vancouver School of Economics\.
