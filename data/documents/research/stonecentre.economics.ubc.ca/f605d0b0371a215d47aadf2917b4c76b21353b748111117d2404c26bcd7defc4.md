Join us for a seminar with Professor Nicole Fortin, who will be presenting her paper, “Gender Differences in Career Progression Among PhDs in Economics\.” The event is hosted by the VSE Women in Economics group and supported by the Stone Centre on Wealth and Income Inequality\.

Professor Fortin will share insights on how gender influences career trajectories in economics, highlighting key trends and insights from her work\. The event is open to all—students and faculty with an interest in gender and inequality are especially encouraged to attend\.

**Abstract**

This article reviews a selection of recent papers that leverage new analytics of career progression, emphasizing potential sources of gender disparities among PhDs in Economics\. It offers a unique and comprehensive perspective by examining gender representation, from student choices to end\-of\-career rewards\. The review provides a thorough, in\-depth analysis of the topic, highlighting the progress made in valuing women’s contributions to economic research, including two Nobel Prize winners in the past five years\. However, it also points out some recent transformations rooted in the empirical and “big data” revolution, which necessitates large teams of co\-authors and favours PhD applicants with pre\-doc experience, potentially disadvantaging women\.
