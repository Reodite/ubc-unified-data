Applications for the Stone Centre’s 2026/27 Postdoctoral Fellowship program are now open\.

We welcome applications from scholars who have received their PhD \(or equivalent, such as a DPhil\) within the last three years by the start date of the fellowship\. Candidates who expect to have successfully defended their dissertation by the start date will also be considered\.

We are particularly interested in researchers across all areas of economics—especially labor, public, development economics, political economy, or macroeconomics—whose work examines the causes or consequences of economic inequality, as well as potential policy solutions\.

This is a full\-time Postdoctoral Research Fellow position for one year, with the possibility of renewal for a second year, contingent on funding availability and review by the Stone Centre committee\. The position offers an annual salary of $90,000 plus benefits, along with up to $10,000 in research support during the first year of the fellowship\.

Applications are now closed\.
