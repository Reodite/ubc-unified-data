The Stone Centre on Wealth and Income Inequality at the Vancouver School of Economics brings together a diverse community of scholars, students, and staff dedicated to advancing research on the causes, consequences, and potential remedies for economic inequality\. Led by internationally recognized economists and supported by staff, our team fosters innovative research, supports the next generation of inequality scholars, and contributes evidence\-based insights to public policy discussions in Canada and beyond\. Through collaboration, mentorship, and engagement, we are building a vibrant hub for the study of wealth and income inequality\.
