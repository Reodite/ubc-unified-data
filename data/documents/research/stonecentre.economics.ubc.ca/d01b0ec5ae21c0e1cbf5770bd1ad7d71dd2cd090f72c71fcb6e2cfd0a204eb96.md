On March 18, join us at the Stone Inequality Seminar Series and be a part of an insightful seminar from Camille Landais, Chairman of the CAE and Professor of Economics at the London School of Economics and Political Science\. Landais will be presenting his paper, titled “Gender Without Kids\.”

**Event Details** 

**Date:** March 18, 2025 

**Time:** 3:30 – 5:00pm 

**Location:** Iona 533, 6000 Iona Dr, Vancouver, BC 

—

**Camille Landais** is Chairman of the CAE and Professor of economics at the London School of Economics and Political Science, and Director of the STICERD\. In addition to his academic position, he maintains affiliations with the Institut des politiques publiques, Institute for Fiscal Studies, IZA Institute of Labor Economics, and the European Economic Association, on whose council he sits\. Camille Landais holds a PhD from the Paris School of Economics\. His research focuses on public finance and labour economics and his articles have appeared in various leading journals such as the American Economic Review, or the Quarterly Journal of Economics\. He was awarded the 2016 Prize for French Best Young Economist, and received the Philip Leverhulme Prize in Economics\.
