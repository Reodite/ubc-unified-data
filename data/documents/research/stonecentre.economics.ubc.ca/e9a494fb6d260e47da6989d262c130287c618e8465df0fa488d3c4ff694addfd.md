Registration is now open for the **First Annual Canadian Labour Economics Forum \(CLEF\) Conference**, taking place on **April 25\-26, 2025**, at the Novotel North York hotel in Toronto, Ontario\.

Hosted by the Department of Economics at York University, in partnership with the Stone Centre on Wealth and Income Inequality at UBC, this conference will serve as a key gathering for researchers, practitioners, and policymakers in the field of labour economics\. 

We are happy to announce that our keynote speaker will be **Ioana Marinescu**, an Associate Professor at the University of Pennsylvania School of Social Policy &amp; Practice and the principal economist at the US Department of Justice Antitrust Division\.

[Click here to learn more](https://stonecentre.economics.ubc.ca/clef2025/)
