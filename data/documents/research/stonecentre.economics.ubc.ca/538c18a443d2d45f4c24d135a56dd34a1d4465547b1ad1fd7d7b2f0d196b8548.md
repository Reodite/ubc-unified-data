The Stone Centre on Wealth and Income Inequality at UBC is proud to partner with the Canadian Labour Economics Forum and York University for the **First Annual Canadian Labour Economics Forum \(CLEF\) Conference**\.

Taking place at the Novotel North York hotel in Toronto, Ontario, on **April 25\-26, 2025**, this event promises to bring together leading researchers, scholars, and practitioners in the field of labour economics\.

The conference will feature a keynote address from **Ioana Marinescu \(University of Pennsylvania\)**, whose influential work continues to shape our understanding of labour markets and policy\.

We invite submissions of completed papers or extended abstracts in any area of labour economics\. The deadline for submissions is **January 15, 2025**\.

[For more information and to submit your work, click here](https://stonecentre.economics.ubc.ca/clef2025/)
