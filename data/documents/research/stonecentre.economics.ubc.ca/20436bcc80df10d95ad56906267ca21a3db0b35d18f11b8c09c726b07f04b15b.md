The Stone Centre on Wealth and Income Inequality is now accepting applications for its 2025/26 PhD Fellowship program\.

PhD Fellows will receive $20,000 in funding per year for up to 2 years\. They will also receive an additional $3,000 annual research stipend to cover costs related to data collection and analysis, and travel to conferences\.

Applicants for this program must be UBC PhD students in Economics entering their third year or higher\. The last day to apply for the fellowship is March 25, 2025\.
