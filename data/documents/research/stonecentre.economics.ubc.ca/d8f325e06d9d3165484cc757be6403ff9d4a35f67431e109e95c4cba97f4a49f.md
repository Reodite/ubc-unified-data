Stone Centre Affiliated Faculty are scholars from across UBC whose research and activities contribute to the Centre’s mission of advancing understanding of the causes, consequences, and potential remedies of wealth and income inequality\. Affiliation recognizes faculty members who actively engage with the Centre through research collaboration, intellectual exchange, student mentorship, policy engagement, and/or knowledge mobilization\. Together, our affiliated faculty bring diverse disciplinary perspectives and expertise that strengthen the Centre’s research excellence, expand its reach, and foster a vibrant interdisciplinary community dedicated to understanding inequality in Canada and around the world\.
