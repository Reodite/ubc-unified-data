To complete all the paperwork on time and in the easiest way possible for you, please send the following information to [elena\.hidalgolerma@ubc\.ca](mailto:elena.hidalgolerm@ubc.ca): 

-   [Visitor reimbursement form](https://stonecentre.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/09/Visitor_Reimbursement_Form.pdf) filled out and signed\. It is crucial that your name and mailing address are complete\. We will use this information to send a cheque with your reimbursement\. 
-   All the tickets, receipts, and invoices for the purchases made during the travel, such as flight tickets, taxi or Uber\. These can be scanned, photos, or screenshots\. 

 Due to UBC Expenses policy, your name and dates must be shown on the airfare and hotel receipts\.
