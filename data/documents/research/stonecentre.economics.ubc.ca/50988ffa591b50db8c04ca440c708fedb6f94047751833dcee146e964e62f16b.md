> The **James M\. and Cathleen D\. Stone Centre on Wealth and Income Inequality at UBC** is a research centre at the Vancouver School of Economics funded by a generous gift of the James M\. and Cathleen D\. Stone Foundation\. The Stone Centre’s purpose is to support research on the causes and consequences of wealth and income inequality, and possible remedies\.
