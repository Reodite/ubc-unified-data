The Stone Centre is dedicated to advancing the study of income and wealth inequality in Canada and beyond\. Our faculty affiliates are experts in their fields, driving forward impactful research on the causes and consequences of inequality\. We support a vibrant research community by offering grants, publishing influential studies, and engaging in diverse projects that address key questions about inequality\. Explore the sub\-tabs to learn more about our current research projects, access our latest publications, and find information on applying for Stone Research Grants\.
