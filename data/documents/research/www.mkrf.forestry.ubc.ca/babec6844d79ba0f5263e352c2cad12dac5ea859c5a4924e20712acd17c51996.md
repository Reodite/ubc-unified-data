Date: Based on availability

Duration: Sessions are 2 hour in length

Ages: 5\-18

Cost: $75/hour \($150 per session\)

Please fill out the form below to book Nature Tutoring and Mentorship sessions\. Please note session bookings are based on instructor availability\.

**Nature Tutoring and Mentorship Policies**

**Payment:** Payment is due in full prior to the start of the first session\. Failure to pay will result in cancellation of the sessions\. If you wish to use DL funding, please let us know\.

**Cancellation Policy:** A lot of preparation and effort goes into planning our sessions\. Tutoring and Mentorship sessions can be cancelled with a minimum of 14 days notice and a refund minus an administration fee will be provided\. Cancellations with a minimum of 7 days notice will be provided a 50% refund\. Cancellations with less than 7 days notice will not receive a refund\.

**Acknowledgement of Risk Form:** A Participant Information and Acknowledgement of Risk form must be filled out by each participant and signed by the child’s parent or legal guardian\. Any registrants who do not submit this form will not be able to attend the session\.

**Illness Policy:** There is zero tolerance for any child attending a session while sick or exhibiting any cold or flu like symptoms\. If you child is ill, do not send them to the session\.

**What to Bring:** Please be prepared\! Sessions are outdoors and run in all weather conditions \(unless the weather is a safety concern ie: winds, lightning\)\. Please make sure participants have appropriate clothing and footwear for all weather conditions \(ie: waterproof clothing, running shoes, hiking shoes and/or boots, gloves, hats, and a change of clothes for after the activities\)\. Inappropriate clothing and footwear may result in the session being cancelled\.

**Scheduling:** Tutoring and Mentorship sessions are based on instructor availability\. Registration through this form does not guarantee a booking\. We will contact you with available dates and times\.

**Questions?**  
If you have any questions, please contact Wild &amp; Immersive at 604\-463\-8148 Option 4 or [wild\.immersive@ubc\.ca](mailto:wild.immersive@ubc.ca)\.
