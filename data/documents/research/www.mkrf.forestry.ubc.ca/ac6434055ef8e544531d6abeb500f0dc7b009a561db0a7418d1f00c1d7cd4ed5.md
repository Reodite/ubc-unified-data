The Malcolm Knapp Research Forest is currently closed for all non\-essential activities\.

Field research is allowed on a case\-by\-case basis\. UBC guidelines for research can be found here: [https://research\.ubc\.ca/covid\-19\-curtailing\-research\-activities\-ubc\-campuses](https://research.ubc.ca/covid-19-curtailing-research-activities-ubc-campuses)\. Please read the entire document, and pay special attention to the “FIELDWORK” paragraph\.

UBC researchers that want to apply for an exemption, can use the form found at: [https://research\.ubc\.ca/sites/research\.ubc\.ca/files/vpri/Exemption\_Form\.pdf](https://research.ubc.ca/sites/research.ubc.ca/files/vpri/Exemption_Form.pdf)\. Once the exemption is obtained, please forward a copy to Ionut Aron, Research Coordinator, at [ionut\.aron@ubc\.ca](mailto:ionut.aron@ubc.ca)\.

Non\-UBC researchers that want to conduct field work at Malcolm Knapp Research Forest need to submit a COVID19 safety plan with answers and explanations to the FIELDWORK guidelines from the link above to Ionut Aron, Research Coordinator, at [ionut\.aron@ubc\.ca](mailto:ionut.aron@ubc.ca)\.

Thank you for your understanding\.
