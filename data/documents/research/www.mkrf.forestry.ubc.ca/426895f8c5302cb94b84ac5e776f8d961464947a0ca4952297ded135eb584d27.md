Reimagining UBC Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship’s Malcolm Knapp Research Forest: A hub for nature\-based research and education\. New and expanded facilities are needed in place of aging infrastructure and in response to growing demand for its exceptional educational, research and public outreach opportunities\. The renewal goal is to create a hub for engagement and knowledge mobilization, inspiring visitors and informing decision\-making about the future of forest and ecosystem management\.
