The Malcolm Knapp Research Forest is a Volunteer Observer for the Meteorological Service of Canada\. We record daily weather observations and report them into the National Climate Data and Information Archive\. If you would like to view the weather data:

1\. Go to: [http://climate\.weather\.gc\.ca/historical\_data/search\_historic\_data\_e\.html](http://climate.weather.gc.ca/historical_data/search_historic_data_e.html)

2\. Click on “Customized Search”

3\. Search by Station Name \(type in Haney for name and select the dates you want\)

4\. Select **Haney UBC RF Admin** and the period you are looking for

5\. Click the “Go” button\.
