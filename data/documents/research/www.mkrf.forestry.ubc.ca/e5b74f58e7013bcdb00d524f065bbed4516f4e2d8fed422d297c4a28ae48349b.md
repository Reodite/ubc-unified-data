**Dates:** All SD42 Pro\-D days  
**Time:** 8:30am – 3:00pm  
**Location:** Malcolm Knapp Research Forest \| 14500 Silver Valley Rd Maple Ridge

Please fill out this form to register your child for Wild &amp; Immersive’s Pro\-D Day Camps\. Open to ages 5\-12\.

**Questions?**  
If you have any questions, please contact Wild &amp; Immersive at 604\-463\-8148 Option 4 or [wild\.immersive@ubc\.ca](mailto:wild.immersive@ubc.ca)\.

Please note that Wild &amp; Immersive collects personal information during the registration process in order to plan and run our camps and programs\. It is our legal authority to request the information for registration purposes\. This information will only be used in the running of our programs, and will not be used for any other purpose\. If you have any questions, please contact wild\.immersive@ubc\.ca\.
