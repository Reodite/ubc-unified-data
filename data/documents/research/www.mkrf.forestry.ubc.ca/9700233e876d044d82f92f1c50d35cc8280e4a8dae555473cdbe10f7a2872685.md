**Age:** 13\-17 \(birth years 2009\-2013\)    \|    **Time:** 9:30am\-2pm \|    **Date:** Tuesdays OR Thursdays September 2026 – May 2027

**Cost:** $350 \+gst \| **Location:** Malcolm Knapp Research Forest 14500 Silver Valley Rd\. Maple Ridge

Fill out the form below to apply for Wild &amp; Immersive’s Forest Leaders peer mentorship program\. Participants in this program will join one of our Nature Explorers age 5\-7 groups on Tuesdays or Thursdays in a peer leader role\. Participants will be selected based on their fit with the program\. Submitting a registration does not guarantee a spot, and applicants may be required to complete a short interview to determine their fit\.
