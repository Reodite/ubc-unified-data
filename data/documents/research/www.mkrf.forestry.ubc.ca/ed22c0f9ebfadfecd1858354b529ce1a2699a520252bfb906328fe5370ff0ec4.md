Lost items found within the research forest may be turned into the front office Lost &amp; Found\. Any items turned into Lost &amp; Found will be kept for **2 weeks** from the date they are handed in\. Any items not claimed within 2 weeks will be donated\.

Lost items brought down from Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre will also be kept for a maximum of 2 weeks\. These items will be kept at the front office of the research forest\. Unclaimed items will be donated after 2 weeks\. If you are a Loon Lake guest looking for an item, please contact Loon Lake directly\. They will arrange to have the item brought down for pickup at the front gate\.

Please contact 604\-463\-8148 to see if your lost item has been turned in\.
