Age: 3\-5 \(birth years 2021\-2023\)   \|    Time: 8:30am\-11:30am \|    Date: Mondays September 14, 2026 – May 31, 2027  
Location: Malcolm Knapp Research Forest 14500 Silver Valley Rd\. Maple Ridge

***Please note the program does not run on the following dates:*** September 21, October 12, December 14, December 21, December 28, January 4, February 15, March 15, March 22, March 29, &amp; May 24\.

Fill out the form below to register your child in Wild &amp; Immersive’s Little Forest School Mornings program\. Please note that submitting registration does not guarantee your space in the program\. You will be emailed confirmation of your space and a link for payment\. Your space is only secured once payment has been made\. The program is split into 2 terms: Fall \(Sept\- Dec\) and Winter/Spring \(Jan\- May\)\. Children must be at least 3 years old by the start of the term in which they are registered\. Children must be fully potty trained to attend\.

**Questions?**  
If you have any questions, please contact Wild &amp; Immersive at 604\-463\-8148 Option 4 or [wild\.immersive@ubc\.ca](mailto:wild.immersive@ubc.ca)\.

Please note that Wild &amp; Immersive collects personal information during the registration process in order to plan and run our camps and programs\. It is our legal authority to request the information for registration purposes\. This information will only be used in the running of our programs, and will not be used for any other purpose\. If you have any questions, please contact wild\.immersive@ubc\.ca\.
