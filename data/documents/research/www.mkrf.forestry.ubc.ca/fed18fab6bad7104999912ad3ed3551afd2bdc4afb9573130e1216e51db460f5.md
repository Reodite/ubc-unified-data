The Mike Lake Trail is a scenic 2km loop that goes around Mike Lake located in Golden Ears Provincial Park\. A portion of that trail \(about 400m\) comes through the Malcolm Knapp Research Forest, and has been identified as a priority area for upgrades\.

We are pleased to share that these improvements are now complete\.

With an investment of $30,000, the project focused on enhancing both safety and overall trail quality\. Key upgrades included rehabilitation of the boardwalk through the replacement of deteriorated sections, installation of extruded metal anti\-slip panels, resurfacing of the trail, and removal of hazardous trees\.

These improvements significantly enhance the user experience, making the trail safer and more enjoyable for all visitors\.

Take a look at the photos below to see the newly upgraded trail, along with our dedicated volunteer Becky, who played a key role in completing much of this work\.
