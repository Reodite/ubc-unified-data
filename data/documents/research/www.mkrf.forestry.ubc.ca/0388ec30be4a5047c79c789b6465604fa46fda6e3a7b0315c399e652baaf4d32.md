On September 11, 2025, Faculty of Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship welcomed British Columbia’s Minister of Forests, Ravi Parmar, to the [Malcolm Knapp Research](https://www.mkrf.forestry.ubc.ca/) [F](https://www.mkrf.forestry.ubc.ca/) [orest](https://www.mkrf.forestry.ubc.ca/) \(MKRF\) to witness the critical work being done to advance sustainable forest management and educate the next generation of foresters\.

[BC Minister of Forests Ravi Parmar talking with Hélène Marcoux, manager of the Malcolm Knapp Research Forest](https://forestry-2022.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/MKRF-visit-3-1.png)

Minister Parmar talking with MKRF Manager, Hélène Marcoux

The tour, led by [Dr\. Dominik Roeser](https://forestry.ubc.ca/faculty-profile/dominik-roeser/), Associate Dean of Research Forests and Community Outreach, and joined by [Dr\. Robert Kozak](https://forestry.ubc.ca/faculty-profile/robert-kozak/), Professor and Dean of the Faculty of Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship and [Hélène Marcoux](https://www.mkrf.forestry.ubc.ca/about/our-team/), Malcolm Knapp Research Forest Manager, provided an important opportunity to showcase MKRF’s role in bridging scientific research, education and practical forest management\.
