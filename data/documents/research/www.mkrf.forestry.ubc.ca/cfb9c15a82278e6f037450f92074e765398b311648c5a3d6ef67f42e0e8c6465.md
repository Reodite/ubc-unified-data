The Malcolm Knapp Research Forest welcomes groups to use the Arboretum area for organized events\.

To request a booking, please contact us at least two weeks in advance for availability and approval\. A nominal fee applies to help support maintenance and upkeep\. For inquiries and reservations, please email [Liz Smith](mailto:liz.smith@ubc.ca) with your event details\.
