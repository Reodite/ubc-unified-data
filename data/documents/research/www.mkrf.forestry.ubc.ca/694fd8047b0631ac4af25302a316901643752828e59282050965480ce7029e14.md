#### **February 2027**

**Trail CLOSURES** from February 5 – June 30, 2024, due to ongoing research logging operations\.

Below is the map showing the trail that is OPEN during the duration of the research project operations \(blue trail\)\. The trail is highlighted in yellow\. Please note that the trail does not loop, and is an out and back\. You will be unable to access other trails during this time\. We thank you for your understanding and cooperation\.

There may also be some road interruptions during this time\. Please obey signs and flaggers\. Please wait for permission from flaggers or machine operators to pass\.
