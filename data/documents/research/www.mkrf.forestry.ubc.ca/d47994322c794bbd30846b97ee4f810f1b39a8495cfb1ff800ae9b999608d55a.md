Experience the new self\-guided Malcolm Knapp Research Forest History Sign Tour\! Signs explaining what you see around you today in the context of the forest’s rich history, have been installed throughout the forest, allowing forest users to visit places they might not otherwise visit, all while learning along the way\. Download the interactive map that will lead you through the forest and along the tour\.

The map available on this page has been referenced with GPS coordinates in order to help you find your way around the forest and navigate between the tour signs\.  Please download “Avenza Maps” – a free app available from your favourite app store\. Upload the Forest History Sign map into Avenza and you will see exactly where you are on the map, and it will help guide you through this adventure\.
