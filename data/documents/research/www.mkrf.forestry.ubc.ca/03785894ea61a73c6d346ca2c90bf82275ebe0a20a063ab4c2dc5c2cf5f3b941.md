Gallant Enterprises Ltd\. is a full service custom cut sawmill and timber manufacturer located within the Malcolm Knapp Research Forest\. The mission of Gallant Enterprises Ltd\. is to provide the highest quality custom wood products to their clients through the use of sustainably grown timber that has been harvested from the research forest – including Western Red Cedar, Coastal Douglas\-fir, Yellow Cedar and Western Hemlock\. They specialize in manufacturing beams and large timber, along with custom pieces for appearance grade applications\.

Gallant is instrumental in providing education and research opportunities to various groups from UBC, other post\-secondary institutions, and corporate teams\. Educational tours showcase industrial and business operations and its integration with the research forest\. Gallant also supplies wood for education or research needs – and welcomes opportunities to collaborate with researchers\.

More information about Gallant Enterprises Ltd\. can be found here: [https://www\.gallantmill\.ca/](https://www.gallantmill.ca/)
