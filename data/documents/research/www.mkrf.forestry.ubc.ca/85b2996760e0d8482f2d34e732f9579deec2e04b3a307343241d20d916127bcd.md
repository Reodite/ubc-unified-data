Age: 5\-12 \(birth years 2014\-2021\)    \|    Time: 9:30am\-2pm \|    Date: Wednesdays September 16, 2026 – May 26, 2027

***The program does not run on the following dates:*** September 30, November 11, December 16, December 23, December 30, January 6, March 17, &amp; March 24\.

Location: Malcolm Knapp Research Forest 14500 Silver Valley Rd\. Maple Ridge

Fill out the form below to register your child for Wild &amp; Immersive’s Forest Club multi\-age program\. This form is for individuals\. Please go to [this form](https://mkrf-forestry.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?page_id=2951) if you are registering siblings\. Please note that submitting registration does not guarantee your space in the program\. Since this program is designed for siblings, priority is given to sibling registrations first\. Individual registrations will be accepted based on available space\. You will be emailed confirmation of your space, and a link for payment\. Your space is only secured once payment has been made\.
