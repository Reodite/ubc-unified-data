Episode 35 – [Dr\. Elizabeth “Betsy” Lublin](https://clasprofiles.wayne.edu/profile/aj8580) \(Wayne State\)

[Image](https://meijiat150.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/06/lublin.jpeg)In this episode, Dr\. Betsy Lublin \(Wayne State University\) outlines moral reform campaigns carried out by women in the WCTU during the Meiji Period as they sought to redefine civic morality and good citizenship\. We discuss campaigns against prostitution, drinking, and smoking, the increasing prevalence of tobacco in Japan during the Meiji and Taishō Periods, and societal views of smoking\. [Click here to listen\.](https://meijiat150.podbean.com/e/episode-35-elizabeth-betsy-lublin-wayne-state/)

Episode 34 – [Dr\. Daniel Botsman](https://history.yale.edu/people/daniel-botsman) \(Yale\)

[Image](https://meijiat150.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/06/botsman.jpg)In this episode, Dr\. Daniel Botsman \(Yale\) stresses the ruptures in Japanese society caused by the Meiji Restoration, especially as seen in the Meiji penal system, new conceptions of freedom during the Freedom and Popular Rights Movement, and the emancipation of Outcaste communities\. We discuss the emancipatory proposals of Ōe Taku, peasant protests, notable connections between Yale and Meiji\-period Japan, and the politics of commemorating the anniversary of the Meiji Restoration\. [Click here to listen\.](https://meijiat150.podbean.com/e/episode-34-dr-daniel-botsman-yale-university/)
