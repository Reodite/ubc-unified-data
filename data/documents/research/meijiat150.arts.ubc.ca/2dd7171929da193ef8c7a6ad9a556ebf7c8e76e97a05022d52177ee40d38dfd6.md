14 March 2019 5:30 pm

-   **Venue:** [IK Barber Learning Centre, Dodson Room](https://meijiat150.arts.ubc.ca/events/venues/ik-barber-learning-centre-dodson-room/)
-   **Categories:** [Events](https://meijiat150.arts.ubc.ca/events/category/events/)
