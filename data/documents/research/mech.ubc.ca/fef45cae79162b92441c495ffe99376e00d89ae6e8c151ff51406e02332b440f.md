The Department of Mechanical Engineering is home to a successful and recognized research department, with numerous connections to international industry\. There are over 200 graduate and 30 undergraduates working in our research labs, funded by over $11 million annually in government and industry grants\. We have [five Canadian Research Chairs, as well as six industry\-funded research chairs and professorships\.](https://mech.ubc.ca/our-department/teaching-research-awards/)

Our research spans the fields of applied solid mechanics, biomechanics &amp; medical devices, computational engineering, energy &amp; environment, engineering education, fluid mechanics, manufacturing automation &amp; robotics, mechanics &amp; instrumentation, and naval architecture &amp; marine engineering\.

## **APPLIED SOLID MECHANICS**

The Applied Solid Mechanics area pursues fundamental research on mechanics of solids, materials and structures using modern analytical, computational, and experimental tools\.

[Source link](https://mech.ubc.ca/research-main/applied-solid-mechanics/)

## **BIOMECHANICS &amp; MEDICAL DEVICES**

Faculty in the Biomechanics and Medical Devices research area develop engineering techniques and technologies to investigate biological systems and address healthcare needs\.

 [https://mech\.ubc\.ca/research\-main/biomechanics\-biomedical\-engineering/](https://mech.ubc.ca/research-main/biomechanics-biomedical-engineering/)

## **COMPUTATIONAL ENGINEERING**

The faculty in the Computational Engineering area develop and use mathematical models and numerical simulation tools for problems in fluid and solid mechanics\.

## **ENERGY &amp; ENVIRONMENT**

The Energy &amp; Environment area conducts research to support Canada and the world in meeting energy needs without causing severe impacts on the environment and human health\.
