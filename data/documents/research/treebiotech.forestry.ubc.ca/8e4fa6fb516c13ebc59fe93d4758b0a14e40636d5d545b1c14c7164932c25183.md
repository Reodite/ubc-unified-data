#### PhD Candidate

Email: yucenpu@student\.ubc\.ca

### Project

Augmenting lignin acylation via exogenous acyl\-CoA ligases

### Research Interests

My research focuses on engineering plant cell walls to improve the processing and valorization of lignocellulosic biomass for biofuels and biorefineries\. In particular, I am interested in expanding lignin chemistry beyond canonical monolignols by increasing the incorporation of ester\-linked phenolic monomers that can introduce more readily cleavable bonds and enhance biomass digestibility\.
