### Education

Ph\.D – Wood Chemistry, Seoul National University, Korea, 2018  
MA – Wood Chemistry, Seoul National University, Korea, 2013  
BS – Program in Environmental Materials Science, Seoul National University, Korea, 2011

### Research Interests

-   Biomass fractionation and biorefinery based on bio\- and thermo\-chemical approach, especially feedstock preparation and biomass pretreatment\.
-   Lignin decomposition, recovery, depolymerization, and characterization to enhance feasibility of biomass conversion and application
