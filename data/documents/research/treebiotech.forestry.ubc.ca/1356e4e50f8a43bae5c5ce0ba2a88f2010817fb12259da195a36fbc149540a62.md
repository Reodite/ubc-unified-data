### Research Interests

*Cellulose*, *plant cell wall*, *carbohydrate biosynthesis*, *physiology*, *Arabidopsis*, *Poplar*

Cellulose is the most abundant biopolymer on earth, acting as the main structural carbohydrate in plant cell walls\. The specific deposition of this component affects cell shape, size, structure, and function\. While we know much of the important basics around cellulose synthesis in plant cell walls, there are still many unanswered questions\. My research interests lie in integrating genetics, molecular biology, plant physiology, and analytical chemistry to elucidate the nuanced contributions of *Arabidopsis thaliana* cellulose synthase 4, 7 and 8 to the production of cellulose in the secondary cell wall\. Utilizing complementary approaches that span from nanoscale cellulose architecture to macroscopic plant traits, my research aims to connect molecular mechanisms of cellulose synthesis to their effects on whole\-plant structure and function
