### Created by Dr\. Dana Di Pardo Leon\-Henri \(University of Marie and Louis Pasteur\)

#### Intended Audience

The activity is designed for undergraduate students in English language courses for non\-majors \(LANSAD – Langues pour spécialistes d’autres disciplines\) in multilingual university contexts\. It is particularly suitable for learners at B1–B2 level \(CEFR\), though it can easily be adapted for beginner, intermediate, or advanced learners\. With appropriate scaffolding, the activity can also be adapted for secondary education contexts \(high school\) and, in simplified forms, for younger learners exploring storytelling and creative writing\.

#### Learning Objective

The learning objectives are to develop narrative writing skills, encourage creative language production through storytelling, and foster critical engagement with popular culture narratives\. Through the fan fiction activity, students practice using dialogue, description, and narrative structure while reflecting on how stories can be reinterpreted from different perspectives\.

#### About the Creator

Dana Di Pardo Léon\-Henri teaches English for non\-majors at the University of Marie and Louis Pasteur \(France\) and is a researcher at ELLIADD \(UR 4661\)\. Her teaching and research focus on creative language learning, mediation, and the use of digital culture and storytelling in multilingual classrooms\.
