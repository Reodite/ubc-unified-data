[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/hypothetically-194x300.png)Hello\! This comic is about what it might feel like to navigate the medical system as a child with complex behaviours or needs\. For both the child and the caregivers, it can be anything from frightening to tedious to frustrating to traumatic\. I wanted to try and capture that experience through a series of comparisons\. If you’ve experienced these things, I hope you feel seen and heard\. If you haven’t, I hope you gain an understanding of how they can feel\! Thank you for reading\!  
Eve
