### Senior Program Assistant

### \(She/Her\)

Charli Brown is a recent graduate of UBC’s MA in Children’s Literature program\. She researches how gender, sexuality, and dis ability intersect with youth pop culture, especially fairy tales and monster media\. In her free time, Charli loves to read webcomics, watch romcoms, and thrift vintage clothes\. As Senior Program Assistant, Charli assists the Pop Culture Cluster with administration and communications\.

You can contact Charli via email at [pop\.culture\.comms@ubc\.ca](mailto:pop.culture.comms@ubc.ca)
