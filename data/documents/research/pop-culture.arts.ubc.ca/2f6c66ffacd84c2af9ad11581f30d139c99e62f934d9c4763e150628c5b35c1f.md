#### Event Details

-   **Start:** 3 February 2026 12:30 pm
-   **End:** 3 February 2026 1:30 pm

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/comic-book-club-graphics-1280-x-720-px.png)

Our interactive tour of the [X̱wi7x̱wa Library](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/?login) will be on [Tuesday, 3 February 2026](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/?login) [from 12:30pm to 1:30pm](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/?login) at 1985 West Mall\.  
[Optional registration through this link](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_1ACOPW9ZNjogP1I) to get future updates from the club\. Please note that the library visit is limited to UBC students, faculty, and staff\. 

From January to March 2026, [**Understanding Through Comics: A Comics and Comics Journal Club**](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/comics-studies/understanding-through-comics/) will continue to host a series of meetings and social events, including a start\-of\-term welcome luncheon, a series of discussions, one library visit, one speaker, and an end\-of\-term comics making socials\. We invite students, staff, and faculty from across the university to join us in reading comics as entertainment, research, and social change\. We especially welcome folks new to comics\.
