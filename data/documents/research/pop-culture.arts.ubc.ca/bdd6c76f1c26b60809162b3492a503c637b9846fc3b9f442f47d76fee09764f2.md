We are hosting an online event with Czech illustrator and comics arts [Štěpánka Jislová](https://www.jislova.com/) on Monday, Oct 20th at 11:30AM\!

Štěpánka Jislová’s graphic memoir “[Heartcore](https://www.jislova.com/portfolio/heartcore/)” is an intimate autobiography about love, relationships and the toxic cycles we often find ourselves in\. This type of “confessional” comics is a relatively new phenomenon in the Czech scene and we’re looking forward to hearing about her experiences creating this personal work\.
