Comics Talks is the Pop Culture Cluster’s public lecture series on comics and comics studies\. The series brings together scholars, cartoonists, educators, and community partners to explore comics as a form of cultural expression, critical inquiry, and social engagement\. Through talks, panels, and conversations, Comics Talks highlights comics as both a creative medium and a site of research, public dialogue, and knowledge mobilization\.
