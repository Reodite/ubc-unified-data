### Graduate Fellow

My name is James Gibson and I am a computer programming, game design, and 3D modeling teacher working through the public school system in Victoria, BC\. As a person, I am super interested in technology, games, and how they can be combined to be an art, science, or an educational tool\. I’m really interested in how technology is influencing our society for both good and ill and how philosophy and psychology are exploring these challenges\. I am currently a student in UBC’s Master’s of Educational Technology program taking online classes while I continue to work full time in my school, run clubs \(D&amp;D and Miniwargaming\), write, and do a bunch of other stuff all at the same time\.
