### Created by Alifa Bandali \(Assistant Professor Teaching, GRSJ\)

#### Intended Audience

The target audience is for university students, however, the assignment and lecture materials may be adapted for students in high school as well\.

#### Project Purpose

The purpose of this assignment is to re\-write and re\-imagine a film disclaimer for an appropriate film of your choosing\. Students are asked to not only rewrite a disclaimer \(or how they are reconceptualizing one\) for their chosen film, but also the intentions of their disclaimer\. For example, students should consider using: challenging dominant storytelling, norms and representations; an intersectional frame that allows for the recognition of how the film upholds stereotypes and tropes or reinforces erasure; understandings of the colonial imaginary; the notion of representation for representation’s sake\.

#### About the Creator

Alifa Bandali is an Assistant Professor of Teaching at the Institute for Gender, Race, Sexuality and Social Justice \(GRSJ\)\. Her research and teaching interests include: intersectional approaches and thinking; gender, race and migration; feminist activism in institutional and creative spaces; Muslim women’s representations; emotional pedagogies; along with the media and popular culture\. In particular, her teaching praxis takes seriously the role of emotions, the media and everyday life\.
