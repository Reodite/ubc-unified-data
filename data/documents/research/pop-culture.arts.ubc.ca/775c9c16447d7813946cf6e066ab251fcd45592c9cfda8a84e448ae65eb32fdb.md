On May 24th, 2025, Vancouver’s newest comics event, [**Rain City Comic Con**](https://raincitycomiccon.com/), was [featured on Gobal News](https://globalnews.ca/video/11195812/vancouvers-newest-comic-book-convention-celebrates-creators-and-artists/)\. Reporter Safeeya Pirani interviewed co\-organizer **John Anders of Soaring Penguin Press** about supporting the local comics and cartooning community and [**Alina Pete \(Cree\)**](https://alina-pete.com/), collaborator on the Comics Studies Cluster’s [“Remember Comics” Project](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/comics-studies/csc-projects/the-remember-comics-project-with-the-homalco-first-nation/) and artist in [*Xwémalhkwu Hero Stories* \(2024\)](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/celebrating-indigenous-storytelling-through-comics-the-xwemalhkwu-hero-stories-project/), about creating comics for Indigenous Youth\. 

*Rain City Comic Con is a collaboration between the UBC Comics Studies Cluster and John Anderson of Soaring Penguin Press\.*
