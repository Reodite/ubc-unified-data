### PhD Candidate

Fabiola del Rincón Fernández is a PhD candidate in Hispanic Studies at UBC\. Her interdisciplinary research examines transnational and multimedial poetic works from Latin American and Caribbean authors and activists\.

She writes poetry and fiction\.
