#### Event Details

-   **Start:** 29 February 2024 1:00 pm
-   **End:** 29 February 2024 2:00 pm
-   **Categories:** [Events](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/events/category/events/)

---

**Join us for a Reading and Talk with Cartoonist Walter Scott, where he will read from his beloved comic series, *Wendy*, and will discuss his creative process and career as a cartoonist and visual artist\.**

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/02/Visiting-Writer-Walter-Scott-Reading-360x203.jpg)

**Walter** is an interdisciplinary artist working across comics, drawing, video, performance and sculpture\. His comics series, Wendy, chronicles the continuing misadventures of a young artist in a satirical version of the contemporary art world\. Wendy has been featured in Canadian Art, Art in America, and published online on the New Yorker\. Recent exhibitions include *Reframed Into Oblivion*, Naughton Gallery \(Belfast\), and *The Pathos Of Mandy,* at the ISCP, New York\. His newest graphic novel, *Wendy, Master of Art*, is available from Drawn and Quarterly\.

*The event is open to everyone, including the UBC community and the general public\. Registration is required\.*
