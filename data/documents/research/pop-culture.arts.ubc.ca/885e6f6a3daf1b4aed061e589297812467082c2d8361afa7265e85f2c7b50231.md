#### Event Details

-   **Start:** 16 October 2024 3:00 pm
-   **End:** 16 October 2024 4:00 pm
-   **Categories:** [Events](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/events/category/events/)

---

Join us for a conversation with Caldecott Medal\-winning authors Jillian Tamaki and Mariko Tamaki on their forthcoming comic, Roaming \(September 2023\)\. Moderated by alum Emily Chou, MFA 2023\.

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/55569218-200x300.jpg)Mariko Tamaki is a Canadian writer living in California\. She is the co\-creator of the graphic novels Skim and This One Summer with Jillian Tamaki, and Laura Dean Keeps Breaking Up with Me with Rosemary Valero\-O’Connell\. She writes superhero comics for DC Comics, Darkhorse and Marvel\. Mariko was the recipient of the Eisner for Best Writer in 2020\. Collectively, her works have received Printz Honors, Eisner, Ignatz, Ringo and Prism awards\. She is the curator of the Abrams LGBTQ imprint, Surely Books\.

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/headshotamcopy-229x300.jpg)

Jillian Tamaki is a cartoonist, illustrator, and educator raised in Calgary, Alberta\. She is the author of the Eisner Award\-winning graphic novels SuperMutant Magic Academy and Boundless, and the author\-illustrator of two picture books, including most recently Our Little Kitchen\. With her cousin Mariko Tamaki, she is the co\-creator of the young adult graphic novels SKIM and This One Summer, which won a Governor General’s Award and Caldecott Honor\. She lives in Toronto, Ontario\.

**Where:** Dodson Room, IKBLC
