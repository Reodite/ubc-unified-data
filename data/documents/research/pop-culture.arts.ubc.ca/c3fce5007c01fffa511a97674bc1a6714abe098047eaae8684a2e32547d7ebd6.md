## Welcome

The **Accessible Comics &amp; Described Anime as Inclusive Pedagogy** is a 60\-minutes\-long participatory workshop held on 6 May 2026 for the [9th Annual Graduate Students in Teaching Conference 2026](https://gsit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/)\. The workshop is aimed at attendees across disciplinary and linguistic fields who wish to include accessible popular media in their classrooms\. The workshop draws from the [UBC StEAR grant funded programming series Understanding Through Comics](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/comics-studies/understanding-through-comics/) held at the University of British Columbia in 2025 –2026 that introduced accessible popular media to disabled and nondisabled students, faculty, and staff\.

The workshop will begin with a think\-pair\-share opening exercise inquiring about attendees’ past experiences with accessible media\. Next, will be a brief lecture on the state of accessible popular media creation in Canada and beyond\. In the second half of the workshop, attendees will engage with selected accessible popular media in small groups\. Specifically, they will read and listen to one audio comic and/or read one described textually comic sample before listening to audio described anime produced by AI voices and human narrators\. The workshop will close with each small group reflecting on what they learned\. At the end of the workshop, attendees will leave with a stronger understanding of how the arts and popular culture are not purely visual mediums but rather multisensory ones with the inclusion of accessible popular media products opening our classrooms to more learners\.

The workshop was lead by V Rahbar \(UBC iSchool PhD program\) with collaboration from Care Barker \(UBC iSchool MLIS program\)\.

### Part Two: Group Exercise \| Instructions &amp; Questions

##### *Group Exercise Information*

The workshop facilitators will divide you into two groups\. Each facilitator will screenshare the workshop website as well as share with you the URL\. At the end of your group exercise, you will re\-join the main room to engage in a reflection\. Please do take notes as you listen and read accessible popular media\.

**In groups, you will…** 

-   Listen to two audio described anime samples in human narrator English and AI voice in language of your choice \(English, French, Italian, German, Spanish, or Russian\)​\.
-   Discuss experience with the group​: What did you gain from listening to audio described anime? What was it like to listen to an AI voice compared to human narration?
-   Listen to one audio comic sample and/or read one text described comic sample in English
-   Discuss experience with the group​: What did you gain from listening or reading accessible comics? ​Were there specific characteristics you noticed through audio or textual description that you would have missed through visual examination alone?​
-   Prepare for reflection: How would you include accessible popular media in your teaching?​

### Part Two: Group Exercise \| Anime Materials

##### *Accessing Accessible Anime* 

Your workshop facilitator will stream samples from accessible anime to your group\. While everyone will be listening to the human narration samples in the same language \(English\), you may select an AI voice described anime in the in the language of your choice with each sample having options in languages other than English\.

Please select only one human narrated anime and only one AI voice described anime to listen to\. You may enjoy the other samples following the workshop\.

Note: The workshop’s lead facilitator, Rahbar, only has proficiency in English, Japanese, and \(un poquito\) Spanish\. Therefore, only samples in English and Spanish has been screened for content\.
