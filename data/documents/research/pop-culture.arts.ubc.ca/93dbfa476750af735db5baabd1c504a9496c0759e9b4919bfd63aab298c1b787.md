### Junior Fellow

Hello\! I like growing food, collecting metal, and compiling lists\. Currently, I am a third year undergraduate student in Global Resource Systems at UBC, specializing in sustainable agriculture and Scandinavia\. Over my many years of surfing the indie web, I have grown quite fond of virtual spaces and the fitting heterogeneity of their constituents\. I am particularly a fan of experimental and/or accessible game engines, dithered images, iframes, random generators, and of course — personal websites\. At the intersection of food justice and critical play \(specifically, poetic computation\), I am interested in: living spaces, political creativity, and low\-tech technologies\. My current work consists of; turning compost, working under Germaine Koh on Slow Fashion Season 2026 \(exciting\!\), writing for the campus radio magazine, designing static sites, and helping out at our community bike shop\. Visit me \! –&gt; [kairnzhou\.com](https://kairnzhou.com/) 🙂
