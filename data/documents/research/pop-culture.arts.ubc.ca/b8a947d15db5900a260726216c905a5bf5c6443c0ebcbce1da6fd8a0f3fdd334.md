[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/comic-book-club-graphics-1280-x-720-px-2.png)Our seventh discussion will look at Gender and 2SLGBTQIA\+ Equity through the lens of *Ducks: Two Years in the Oil Sands* by Kate Beaton\. It will be held on **Tuesday, 17 March 2026** from 12:30pm to 1:30pm in Koerner Library, room 548\.

**[Register here\!](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_1RK2ZHr0wpidHCu) Please note this event is only open to UBC students, staff and faculty\.**

We will be covering the first two chapters of the book in three different formats: as a print comic, a described comic, and as an audio comic\. Please email comics\.studies@ubc\.ca for a link to the ePub file\!​\. For the audio, please visit our public facing website at [https://pop\-culture\.arts\.ubc\.ca/comics\-studies/understanding\-through\-comics/\#duck](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/comics-studies/understanding-through-comics/#duck "https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/comics-studies/understanding-through-comics/#duck")\. We ask that you take notes while you read, consider how it feels to listen to the comic compared to reading it in text as well as print form\.

From January to March 2026, [**Understanding Through Comics: A Comics and Comics Journal Club**](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/comics-studies/understanding-through-comics/) will continue to host a series of meetings and social events, including a start\-of\-term welcome luncheon, a series of discussions, one library visit, one speaker, and an end\-of\-term comics making socials\. We invite students, staff, and faculty from across the university to join us in reading comics as entertainment, research, and social change\. We especially welcome folks new to comics\.
