The following guide offers resources on teaching New Media to students\. We take New Media to mean social media and digital media\. We hope th is guide offers an opportunity to consider how platforms like Instagram, Tiktok, Facebook, and others reflect cultural practices and ideas\. The guide includes lesson plans developed by the team, links to archives, academic journals on New Media, and other useful material\.  

### Archives

#### **The Library of Congress’s Twitter Archive**  

In 2010, the Library of Congress acquired the entire archive of public tweets made on Twitter\. Up until 2017, the Library continued to archive all public tweets until 2018 when they decided to collect more selectively\. The project provides a window to the difficulties of collecting the vast amounts of material posted online as well as the ethical dimensions of such a practice\.   

[https://blogs\.loc\.gov/loc/2017/12/update\-on\-the\-twitter\-archive\-at\-the\-library\-of\-congress\-2/](https://blogs.loc.gov/loc/2017/12/update-on-the-twitter-archive-at-the-library-of-congress-2/) 

[https://www\.npr\.org/sections/thetwo\-way/2017/12/26/573609499/library\-of\-congress\-will\-no\-longer\-archive\-every\-tweet](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2017/12/26/573609499/library-of-congress-will-no-longer-archive-every-tweet) 

[https://www\.forbes\.com/sites/kalevleetaru/2017/12/28/the\-library\-of\-congress\-in\-the\-digital\-age\-from\-archiving\-twitter\-to\-innovation\-lab/](https://www.forbes.com/sites/kalevleetaru/2017/12/28/the-library-of-congress-in-the-digital-age-from-archiving-twitter-to-innovation-lab/) 

#### **The Library and Archives of Canada’s Web and Social Media Preservation Program** 

The Library of Canada includes a branch that specifically targets webpages connected to significant moments in Canadian history\. For instance, they’ve archived websites related to the Covid\-19 pandemic that might otherwise disappear as the websites are updated\.  

[https://library\-archives\.canada\.ca/eng/services/government\-canada/web\-social\-media\-preservation\-program/Pages/web\-archive\.aspx](https://library-archives.canada.ca/eng/services/government-canada/web-social-media-preservation-program/Pages/web-archive.aspx) 

#### **The Smithsonian’s Web and Social Media Archiving** 

The Smithsonian Institution Archives collects its own webpages over the years since the website’s release in 1995\. The archives “demonstrate the evolution of web design and the rise of social media in the early 21st century\.” The linked webpage offers a look at how the Smithsonian saves and stores their material\.  

[https://siarchives\.si\.edu/what\-we\-do/digital\-curation/web\-and\-social\-media\-archiving](https://siarchives.si.edu/what-we-do/digital-curation/web-and-social-media-archiving) 

#### **The Digital Art Museum** 

The Digital Art Museum, founded in 2003, includes the work of digital pioneers in the 1960s and 70s as well as more contemporary work\. Their website offers access to hundreds of photos of digital works of art\.  

[https://dam\.org/museum/artists\_ui/artists/](https://dam.org/museum/artists_ui/artists/) 

### Academic Journals

#### **Leonardo** 

“*Leonardo* is the leading international peer\-reviewed journal on the use of contemporary science and technology in the arts and music and the application and influence of the arts and humanities on science and technology\.\.\.\.*Leonardo* is interested in work that crosses the artificial boundaries separating contemporary arts and sciences\. Featuring illustrated articles written by artists about their own work as well as articles by historians, theoreticians, philosophers and other researchers, the journal is particularly concerned with issues related to the interaction of the arts, sciences and technology\.” 

[https://direct\.mit\.edu/leon](https://direct.mit.edu/leon) 

#### **Media and Communication** 

*“Media and Communication* is an international, peer\-reviewed open access journal dedicated to a wide variety of basic and applied research in communication and its related fields\.” 

[https://www\.cogitatiopress\.com/mediaandcommunication](https://www.cogitatiopress.com/mediaandcommunication) 

#### **The International Journal of Performance Arts and Digital Media** 

*“The International Journal of Performance Arts and Digital Media* draws its contributions from academics and practitioner\-researchers at the interface of new media technologies and performance\. It acts as a forum for critical scholarship, innovative practice, and creative pedagogy, addressing themes that may be domain\-specific \(e\.g\. theatre, dance, music, live art, computer science\) or situated at the convergence of two or more disciplines\.” 

[https://www\.tandfonline\.com/journals/rpdm20](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/rpdm20) 

### Popular Writing &amp; Other Media

#### **Wired** 

Founded in 1993, *Wired* is a digital and physical magazine that covers a range of topics related to New Media\. 

[https://www\.wired\.com/](https://www.wired.com/) 

#### **The Verge**  

The Verge covers topics related to technology in the sciences and the arts\.  

[https://www\.theverge\.com/](https://www.theverge.com/)
