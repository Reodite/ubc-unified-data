### Junior Fellow

I’m Audrey, a third\-year English student with a minor in Creative Writing\. As a designer and writer passionate about the intersection of language and visual storytelling, I create digital experiences that communicate meaning beyond the screen\. Design is not just a visual craft but a narrative practice—where words, images, and interactions come together to tell a story\. It’s about guiding users through meaningful experiences, translating complex ideas into intuitive interfaces, and creating moments that feel both functional and emotionally resonant\. My work spans crafting user\-centered interfaces to developing marketing campaigns and content strategies that engage and inspire\. I thrive in collaborative environments where ideas can be tested, iterated, and elevated through thoughtful design\. Whether I’m designing a web or app experience, shaping a brand voice, or experimenting with interactive media, my goal is always to create experiences that are intuitive, engaging, and meaningful for the people who use them\.
