### Join the UBC Comics Studies Cluster next Saturday, May 24th from 10 AM\-5 PM to celebrate local comics creators and art at [Rain City Comic Con](https://raincitycomicon.com/)\!

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/05/Rain-City-Comicon-logo-with-rain-animation-base.gif)

#### [Rain City Comic Con](https://raincitycomicon.com/) is Vancouver’s newest comics event dedicated to showcasing the work of comic artists in the Greater Vancouver Area\. Started by a coalition of independent publishers and comics affiliates, the mission of Rain City Comic Con is to bring a second creator\-led comics event to the Greater Vancouver Area, so that comic creators have yet another opportunity to present their work to the general public\.

Date: Saturday, May 24\[th\], 2025  
Time: 10 AM to 5 PM  
Location: [Yaletown Roundhouse](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ws6jeUjhy8Es1A7P7)

Cost: FREE\!\!

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/05/RainCityComiconPoster_Emma1-742x960.png)

*Rain City Comic Con is a collaboration between the UBC Comics Studies Cluster, comics event organizer Weird Space, and John Anderson of Soaring Penguin Press\.*

*While our initial event is scheduled for May at the Roundhouse – a time and venue typically associated with VanCAF – Rain City Comic Con has no intention of competing with or replacing VanCAF\. Moreover, we look forward to supporting VanCAF upon their return\!*
