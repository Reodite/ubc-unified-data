The following guide offers resources on teaching comics to students\. We hope the guide offers an opportunity to consider how comics reflect cultural practices and ideas\. The guide includes lesson plans developed by the team, links to archives, academic journals on comics, and other useful material\.

### Teaching Guides

#### **Graphic Medicine \- Syllabus Repository**  

A public repository of syllabi on health\-focused graphic narratives hosted by the non\-profit Graphic Medicine International Collective\.

[https://www\.graphicmedicine\.org/resources/teaching/](https://www.graphicmedicine.org/resources/teaching/)

#### **UCLA Research Guide for Comics**  

“A research guide designed to support, inform, and streamline research on the history, themes, and technique of comics\. Users will find helpful clarifications to the many formats of comics, specific citation support, and relevant databases\.” 

[https://guides\.library\.ucla\.edu/c\.php?g\=1037230&amp;p\=8081558](https://guides.library.ucla.edu/c.php?g=1037230&amp;p=8081558)

#### **Teaching Comics Guide from The Centre for Cartoon Studies** 

"This page both archives teaching content from National Association of Comics Art Educators, along with dozens of additional teaching comics resources for all ages developed by The Center for Cartoon Studies community\." 

[https://www\.cartoonstudies\.org/teachingcomics/](https://www.cartoonstudies.org/teachingcomics/) 

#### **Teaching with Comics \- University of Lethbridge** 

"A very comprehensive document that covers the history of comics as a literary medium\. This project explores how comics are an imaginitive and diverse literary style that have a definite place in the ELA classroom\. Includes a "dissection of a comic book" and a list of recommended comics for the classroom\." 

[https://www\.uleth\.ca/sites/default/files/Teaching%20with%20Comics%20\-%20ASmith%20\-%20Unit%20Plan\.pdf](https://www.uleth.ca/sites/default/files/Teaching%20with%20Comics%20-%20ASmith%20-%20Unit%20Plan.pdf) 

#### **How Comics Work \- University of Winnipeg** 

"To facilitate students’ reading and appreciation of This Place, One Book University of Winnipeg has commissioned a 20–page comic, illustrated by Ojibwe comics creator Alice RL\. How Comics Work walks students through the basic terms and concepts of comics studies in order to encourage discussion of how This Place works as a visual\-verbal text\." 

[https://www\.uwinnipeg\.ca/1b19/how\-comics\-work/free\-downloadable\-comic\.html](https://www.uwinnipeg.ca/1b19/how-comics-work/free-downloadable-comic.html)\.  

#### **The Center for Cartoon Studies \- Teaching Comics**

Includes teaching resources, lesson plans, and individual exercises\.

[https://www\.cartoonstudies\.org/teachingcomics/](https://www.cartoonstudies.org/teachingcomics/)

#### **The Comic Book Legal Defense Fund \(CBLDF\)** 

A non\-profit organization dedicated to the protection of the First Amendment rights of the comics art form and its community of retailers, creators, publishers, librarians, and readers\. Includes library and educator tools\.

[https://cbldf\.org/](https://cbldf.org/)

#### **Spin, Weave, and Cut** 

Nick Sousanis, an Eisner\-winning comics author and an associate professor at San Francisco State University, posts guides for teaching comics, classroom exercises, and syllabi on his website, Spin, Weave, and Cut\. His syllabi often resemble the design of modern comics/graphic novels\.  

[https://spinweaveandcut\.com/education\-home/](https://spinweaveandcut.com/education-home/) 

### Archives &amp; Databases

NOTE: Because many comics are protected by copyright laws, they can be difficult to access online via the public domain\. Some of the options below are paid services\.

#### **The Digital Comics Archive**  

The DCA is one of the largest repositories of comics in the public domain\. After signing up for a free account, users can access thousands of pre\-1959 comics associated with the “Golden Age of Comics\.” The archive includes the works of dozens of publishers who went defunct during the comics crash of the 1950s\.  

[https://digitalcomicmuseum\.com/](https://digitalcomicmuseum.com/) 

#### **The Grand Comics Database \(GCD\)**  

The Grand Comics Database \(GCD\) is a nonprofit, internet\-based organization of international volunteers dedicated to building an open database covering all printed comics throughout the world\.

[https://www\.comics\.org/](https://www.comics.org/)

#### **Alexander Street Archives on Underground and Independent Comics and Archie Comics** 

*Alexander Street*, ProQuest's platform for visual media, hosts two comics archives related to Underground and Independant Comics and another on Archie comics\. These services can usually be accessed through a university library\.  

-   Underground and Independent: [https://search\.alexanderstreet\.com/comx](https://search.alexanderstreet.com/comx) 
-   Archie comics: [https://search\.alexanderstreet\.com/com3](https://search.alexanderstreet.com/com3)  

#### **Marvel Unlimited and DC Universe Infinite** 

These monthly, paid services provide access to thousands of new and back\-catalogued comics related to the two major superhero publishers\. Their catalogues extend over almost a century\. While largely composed of superhero comics, both archives include works in other genres published by Marvel and DC \(or their subsidiaries\)\. Note: DC’s application currently only works on mobile devices\. 

-   Marvel: [https://www\.marvel\.com/mu\-25ironheart?gad\_source\=1&amp;gad\_campaignid\=16738551966&amp;gbraid\=0AAAAADctkeeGZEkTq37bq0klU1EXehuRJ&amp;gclid\=CjwKCAjwvO7CBhAqEiwA9q2YJRyQffFxzhxxDZbo26SOX07uyqmK9bst3W0ygxfcYDU2IgbunsQNNhoCuOAQAvD\_BwE](https://www.marvel.com/mu-25ironheart?gad_source=1&amp;gad_campaignid=16738551966&amp;gbraid=0AAAAADctkeeGZEkTq37bq0klU1EXehuRJ&amp;gclid=CjwKCAjwvO7CBhAqEiwA9q2YJRyQffFxzhxxDZbo26SOX07uyqmK9bst3W0ygxfcYDU2IgbunsQNNhoCuOAQAvD_BwE) 
-   DC: [https://www\.dcuniverseinfinite\.com/coming\-soon\-apps/](https://www.dcuniverseinfinite.com/coming-soon-apps/)\.  

#### **The Graphic Novel Collection at Columbia University** 

This collection at Columbia University includes significant writings by American underground artists working in the 1970s, 80s and 90s that were precursors to the graphic novel\. Unfortunately, many of the links to finding guides are broken\. While the collection could represent a significant step in preserving archival material related to comics \(a medium not often archived\), the website’s current state is severely lacking\.  

[https://guides\.library\.columbia\.edu/c\.php?g\=363467&amp;p\=2455683](https://guides.library.columbia.edu/c.php?g=363467&amp;p=2455683) 

### Academic Journals

#### **INKS: The Journal of the Comics Studies Society** 

“Inks: The Journal of the Comics Studies Society features scholarly research on sequential art, graphic narrative, and cartooning\. The journal brings together scholarly essays, archival materials, and conversations with leading comics professionals\. Inks highlights scholarship from a wide range of academic disciplines and engages all periods of comic history, with both a US and an international comics focus\.” 

[https://comicsstudies\.org/inks/](https://comicsstudies.org/inks/) 

#### **ImageTexT** 

"The objective of *ImageTexT* is to advance the academic study of an emerging and diverse canon of imagetexts\. Chief among these are comic books, comic strips, and animations, but also represented are illustrated fiction, children’s picture books, digital\-concrete poetic forms, visual rhetoric, etc\. Any work or works, tradition, school of thought, or critical method that foregrounds the intersections, interrelations, and disjunctions between text and image is an appropriate subject for inquiry and debate in these pages\.” 

[https://imagetextjournal\.com/](https://imagetextjournal.com/) 

#### **The International Journal of Comic Art** 

“International and multidisciplinary in scope, *IJOCA* aims to publish scholarly and readable research on any aspect of comic art, defined as animation, comic books, newspaper and magazine strips, caricature, gag and political cartoons, humorous art, and humor or cartoon magazines\.” 

[http://www\.ijoca\.net/](http://www.ijoca.net/) 

### Popular Writing &amp; Other Media

#### **The Comics Journal**

The TCJ doubles as an academic journal and a source of news\. They frequently review new graphic novels and feature interviews with creators\.  

[https://www\.tcj\.com/](https://www.tcj.com/)
