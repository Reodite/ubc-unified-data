### Professor

Anita Sinner is Professor of Art Education in the Department of Curriculum and Pedagogy, The University of British Columbia\. Her areas of interest include artwork scholarship, ABER, international art education, relational geographies, historical perspectives, life writing, collaborative online learning and community education\. She continues to work extensively with stories as pedagogic pivots, with particular emphasis on artful inquiry in relation to curriculum studies and social and cultural issues in education\. Anita is lead editor of the Artwork Scholarship book series \(Intellect\)\.
