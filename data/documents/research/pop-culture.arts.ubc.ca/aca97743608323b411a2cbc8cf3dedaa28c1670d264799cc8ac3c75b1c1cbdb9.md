### Pop Culture Cluster Director Elizabeth “Biz” Nijdam receives Public Engagement Award from the UBC\-V Public Humanities Hub\.

These awards are given to individuals who have exhibited outstanding public humanities engagement in the past two years and whose work has contributed to the expansion of the range of voices in public discourse\.

See [here](https://publichumanities.ubc.ca/public-engagement-award/public-engagement-award-winners-2024/) for a full list of the recipients of the 2024 Public Engagement Award\.
