### Created by E\. Kalu Amah \(PhD Student, University of Tulsa\)

#### Intended Audience

• Middle and High School English Language Learner \(ELL\) classrooms  
• Multilingual composition courses  
• Teacher education and English methods courses  
• Culturally responsive pedagogy workshops

#### Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:  
1\. Articulate personal meaning orally using a popular culture artifact as a prompt\.  
2\. Engage in iterative rehearsal to refine spoken English without fear of error\.  
3\. Demonstrate increased confidence in oral expression through studio\-based feedback\.  
4\. Assert interpretive ownership of texts without reliance on “correct” readings or “right answers\.”  
For teacher education contexts, pre\-service teachers will additionally be able to:  
5\. Identify how studio\-based pedagogy shifts authority from correction to coaching\.  
6\. Reflect on the implications of voice\-centered instruction for equitable language teaching\.
