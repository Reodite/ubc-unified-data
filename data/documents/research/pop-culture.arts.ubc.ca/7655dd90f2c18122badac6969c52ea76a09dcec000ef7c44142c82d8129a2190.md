### Created by Dr\. Brennan Thomas \(Saint Francis University, Professor of English\)

CORE 407: Analyzing Walt Disney’s Blueprint Animated Films

#### Target Audience

The syllabus and project descriptions are for an advanced general education \(or core curriculum\) seminar for upper\-level undergraduates from all disciplines\. Multiple sections of CORE 407 \(also referred to as CORE III or the Senior CORE\) are offered every semester, with each section addressing an overarching topic\. This CORE 407 section focuses on Walt Disney’s blueprint films: Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs, Pinocchio, Fantasia, Dumbo, and Bambi\.

#### Course Overview

CORE 407 serves as the culmination of students’ research experiences at Saint Francis University, with students critically reassessing familiar elements of daily life such as popular media using established theoretical frameworks\. In this specific seminar, students will analyze the first five animated films of Walt Disney Studios: Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs, Pinocchio, Fantasia, Dumbo, and Bambi\. These five films from the Disney studio’s “Golden Age” \(1937\-1942\) are considered by many film scholars to be not only the studio’s most important contributions to animation but also the blueprints for all subsequent Disney\-animated features\. Drawing from the works of Disney historians and media studies scholars alike, this seminar provides an in\-depth examination of these first five films and their impact on the studio’s animated filmography since 1942\. In this course, students will learn how to peel back the layers of these five films to uncover their hidden messages, to identify the various factors that shaped each film’s creation and map their impact on that film’s initial reception and subsequent placement in the Disney canon\.

#### Course Goals

1\. Identify the narrative elements \(e\.g\., genre, plot, structure\) that distinguish each of the first five Disney films from one another\.  
2\. Compare these early films’ narrative elements with those of later Disney films\.  
3\. Examine each early film’s impact on moviegoers upon its initial release \(during the Great Depression and/or early days of WWII\), as well as its impact on audiences in 2025\.  
4\. Analyze scenes and other aspects from these five films in terms of their commentary on cultural and social themes \(e\.g\., marriage, parenthood, ecology, class mobility\), as well as highlight additional subtle or hidden messages and their impact on viewers\.  
5\. Demonstrate an understanding of these film components in various course assignments\.  
6\. Support ideas with evidence from relevant credible texts \(e\.g\., peer\-reviewed scholarly journal articles\)\.

#### About the Creator

Brennan Thomas is a Professor of English at Saint Francis University in Loretto, Pennsylvania, where she directs the university’s writing center and teaches courses in writing, creative writing, composition theory and pedagogy, and film studies\. She offers introductory and advanced seminars on Disney animated films and specializes in the socio\-political, economic, cultural, and aesthetic themes of Disney’s “Golden Age” films released between 1937 and 1942\.
