### Community Fellow

Wren Handman is a queer novelist, screenwriter, and videogame writer\. She is a co\-founder of Ludic Lemur Entertainment, a queer women\-led indie studio making games that are radically playful, where she is the Creative Director\. Wren has published eight novels for both adult and teen audiences, including *A Midnight So Deadly* \(Parliament House Press\) and *Havoc &amp; Happiness* \(Wandering Roots Press\)\. Wren was the lead writer for the award\-winning sitcom *The Switch*, now the first TV show to ever be released into the Creative Commons\.
