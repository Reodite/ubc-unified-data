### Curated and designed for BC Campus – Digital Literacy

*License: [CC BY 4\.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)*

**Part 1: Intro to Pressbooks by Harper Friedman**

A webinar series that discusses how to use Pressbooks to create and share open educational resources \(OER\)\. The facilitator, Harper Friedman, introduces Pressbooks and shows how to create and share OER with students and fellow educators\. Pressbooks is an online self\-publishing tool available to all post\-secondary faculty and staff in B\.C\. and the Yukon\.

When giving students an assignment or activity that requires the use Pressbooks, you can provide them with this recording to support their process\. This resource can also be used by educators, staff, or students learning about digital literacy, specifically how to create and share OER with Pressbooks\.

**Part 2: OER Production by Kaitlyn Zheng**

This webinar is a part of the OER Production Series and introduce how to find, use, and share OER\. Are your students new to open education and not sure where to start? Are you just starting your own OER project and wondering where to find other openly licensed materials and what to do with them? This webinar answers all these questions by introducing open education and OER, discussing copyright and Creative Commons licenses, and going over how to find, use, and properly attribute OER\.

This resource supports creation and curation as it helps students develop confidence and ability in presenting, creating, reusing, or sharing digital information\. It also helps with ethical and legal considerations as well as information literacy because it enhances understanding of the differences between copyright and open\-source information and helps the learner know how to use online information based on its sources\. In addition, it supports communication and collaboration and digital scholarship because it helps learners use Pressbooks to find, communicate, and make valuable contributions in digital learning spaces\.

When giving students an assignment or activity that requires finding, using, or sharing OER, provide them with this recording to support their learning\.
