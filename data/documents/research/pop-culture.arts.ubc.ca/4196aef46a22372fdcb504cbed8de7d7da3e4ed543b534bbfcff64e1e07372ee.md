### **Please join the [UBC Comics Studies Cluster](https://comics.arts.ubc.ca/) &amp; [CMS Narratives Group](https://narratives.migration.ubc.ca/) on Thursday, March 21 &amp; Friday, March 22** **for the Games for Decolonization Workshop\!** 

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/Games-for-Decolonization-Workshop-HORIZONTAL-PANEL-1280-x-720-px-940x529.png)

*The Games and Decolonization Workshop is an interdisciplinary forum dedicated to examining the intersections between board games and discourses of settler colonialism, decolonization, Indigenous sovereignty, and social justice\. This two\-day event will bring together Indigenous and settler scholars to engage in critical conversations regarding the role of gaming cultures and tarot in the work of social justice and decolonization\.
