## Welcome

[PhD Collab: Arts\-Based Interventions in Graduate Research](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/phd-collab/) is working on amazing projects involving Arts\-Based Research \(ABR\) and Arts\-Based Educational Research \(ABER\)\. Check out some of our projects, on\-going and completed, below\.

### Upcoming Projects

V Rahbar’s work focuses on accessible popular media\. Recent works include [Accessible Comics and Described Anime as Inclusive Pedagogy](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/arts-based-research/aber-research-projects/comics-anime-described/), a 60\-minutes\-long participatory workshop held at the [9th Annual Graduate Students in Teaching Conference 2026](https://gsit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/) in May 2026\.

### Completed Projects

V Rahbar’s work focuses on multisensory art\. Recent works include [The Accessible Arts and Popular Culture Workshop](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/arts-based-research/aber-research-projects/accessible-arts-and-pop-culture-workshop/), a project that explored the relationship between touch and the arts through student\-focused programming on non\-visual engagements with Ainu traditional crafts and Japanese popular culture\. The workshop was generously funded by [the Pop Pedagogies Award Program](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/pop-pedagogies/award/)\.

### Get Involved

Faculty, graduate students, artists, and community partners interested in arts\-based research are invited to [join our mailing list](http://eepurl.com/jk00ww) to learn more about upcoming events, collaborations, and opportunities\. If you’re interested in partnering on a project or showcasing your research, email [pop\.culture@ubc\.ca](mailto:pop.culture@ubc.ca)\.
