### Curated and designed by Thompson Rivers University for BC Campus – Digital Literacy

*License: [CC BY 4\.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)*

This is a course focused on scholarly podcasting\. The four\-week self\-paced class covers the following topics:

-   Week 1: Critical listening skills
-   Week 2: Recording and editing
-   Week 3: Distribution and marketing
-   Week 4: Scoping the project

This self\-directed course first supports creation and curation as it provides guidance on how to critically and creatively assemble and develop podcasts that are specific to different audiences\. It also helps students learn to use WordPress efficiently to record, edit, and share their podcasts \(technology support\)\. In addition, it helps communication and collaboration; through the assignments of this course, learners can develop their skills in effectively crafting messages via WordPress\.

#### Learning Outcomes

-   Understand whether podcasting is the right medium for their project\.
-   Understand the different ways podcasting can be used for mobilizing scholarly knowledge\.
-   Have exposure to basic experience of recording and editing audio\.
-   Have exposure to basic experience of using WordPress for podcast distribution\.
-   Have a scope for development of their own scholarly podcast\.
