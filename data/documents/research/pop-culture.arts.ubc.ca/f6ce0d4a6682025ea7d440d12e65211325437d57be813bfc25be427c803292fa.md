### Created by Sara Ann Knutson \(Assistant Professor Teaching, UBC Dept\. History\) &amp; Abbie Collett \(UBC Student\)

#### Intended Audience

Teachers of undergraduate students\.

#### Learning Objectives

After this discussion class, students will be able to:

-   Identify the diverse, complex ways that the Viking\-Age past is engaged in the present day through a variety of popular media forms
-   Explain what is meant by the historicizing process and how engaging this skill is relevant to the analysis of representations of the past in contemporary pop culture
-   Build on their existing historical analysis skills of primary sources from the Viking\-Age in order to similarly identify the more contemporary historical context, authorship, intended audience, and potential promoted messages that a pop culture source can reflect
-   Evaluate contemporary representations of the Viking Age and situate this content in historical context using primary sources from the Viking Age
-   Explain what issues are at stake in how we understand and interpret Viking\-Age history in the present day, including the relevance of these issues to contemporary social debates and social justice initiatives\.
