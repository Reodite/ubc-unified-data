**Speaker Bio**

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/xaytun-ennasr-300x300.jpeg)

[**Xaytun Ennasr**](https://xennasr.com/) is an interdisciplinary artist, designer, and community organizer\. Her work is rooted in revolutionary cultural production and uses science fiction, magic, technology, and folklore as tools for worldbuilding, especially around trans and Palestinian liberation\. It includes videogames, drawings, ceramics, textile, and poetry\. Xaytun’s work is featured in publications including: This Death is Not One by Jasbir Puar and Nasser Abourahme, and I Will Always Be Looking For You – A Queer Anthology on Arab Art by Haven for Artists\. Her work was exhibited internationally at institutions including Sursock Museum – Beirut, Alt Space Loop – Seoul, Darat Al Funun – Amman, Participant Inc\. and Salma – New York, and The Mosaic Rooms – London\. She is the recipient of the Artist Changemaker Program grant by Global Fund for Women in recognition of her work’s commitment to gender justice\.
