#### Dr\. Elizabeth “Biz” Nijdam was invited to serve as program host for Diana Schutz, who received an honorary Doctor of Letters from the University of British Columbia at the Spring 2026 Congregation ceremony on May 20, 2026\.

A Canadian\-born writer, editor, teacher, and mentor, Schutz is one of the most influential figures in North American comics publishing\. Over a career spanning more than four decades, she served as editor\-in\-chief of Comico during the company’s peak years before joining Dark Horse Comics, where she worked for 25 years in senior editorial roles\. Her editorial work helped shape some of the most celebrated works in modern comics, including Frank Miller’s *Sin City* and *300*, Matt Wagner’s *Grendel*, Stan Sakai’s *Usagi Yojimbo*, and Paul Chadwick’s *Concrete*\.

Schutz’s contributions have been recognized through major comics industry honours, including Eisner Awards for Best Anthology, a Harvey Award for Best Anthology, an Inkpot Award, the Friends of Lulu Award for Women of Distinction, and induction into the Canadian Comic Book Creator Hall of Fame\. In addition to her editorial work, she has written comics, taught comics history and criticism, and played a significant role in expanding the cultural and literary legitimacy of graphic storytelling\.

The newly minted Dr\. Diana Schutz shares words of wisdom with the 2026 graduating class, including the quote from Spider\-Man “With great power comes great responsibility”\.

#### UBC’s honorary degree materials described Schutz as “one of the most influential editors and advocates in the world of comics and graphic storytelling,” noting her “meticulous eye” and deep respect for artistic voice\.

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/IMG_3003-768x576.jpeg)

President and Vice\-Chancellor Bacon congratulates Dr\. Diana Schutz\.

The invitation to Dr\. Nijdam reflected her research and leadership in comics studies at UBC, including her work with the UBC Comics Studies Cluster and related public humanities initiatives\. As program host, Dr\. Nijdam helped welcome Schutz to campus, accompanied her during honorary degree events, and supported opportunities for Schutz to engage with students, faculty, and colleagues whose work intersects with comics, graphic narrative, and visual storytelling\.

Schutz’s official visit includes an honorary degree luncheon, the degree conferral ceremony, and a President’s reception for honorary degree recipients\.
