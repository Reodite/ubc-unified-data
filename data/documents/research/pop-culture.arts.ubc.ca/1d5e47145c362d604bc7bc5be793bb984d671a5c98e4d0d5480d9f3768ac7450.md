### Comics Studies Resources

The University of British Columbia offers various comic studies resources for students interested in studying and creating comics\. Below is a continuously updated list of materials available to students\.

### Comics Studies Courses

The University of British Columbia offers various comics studies coursework for students interested in studying and creating comics\. The listing below provides some examples of past courses which may focus entirely or partially on comics\. We encourage students to visit the UBC Student Service Centre for current course availability\.
