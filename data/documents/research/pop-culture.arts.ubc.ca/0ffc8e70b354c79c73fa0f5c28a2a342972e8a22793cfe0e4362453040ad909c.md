#### **Event Information**

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/12/13JAN26-lunchevent-1-768x960.png)

From January to March 2026, [**Understanding Through Comics: A Comics and Comics Journal Club**](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/comics-studies/understanding-through-comics/) will continue to host a series of meetings and social events, including a start\-of\-term welcome luncheon, a series of discussions, one speaker, and two end\-of\-term comics making socials\. We invite students, staff, and faculty from across the university to join us in reading comics as entertainment, research, and social change\. We especially welcome folks new to comics\.

Our bi\-annual welcome luncheon will be held on Thursday, 15 January 2026 from 12:30pm to 1:30pm in the iSchool Terrace Lab\. We’ll be providing food plus bringing art supplies and graphic novels\. We invite you to bring examples of your own work and your favourite artists\.
