### Curated and designed by Jamie Drozda \(Thompson Rivers University\) for BC Campus – Digital Literacy

*License: [CC BY 4\.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)*

Twine is an open\-source tool for telling interactive, nonlinear stories\. It does not require any coding skills\. Twine publishes directly to HTML, so you can post your Twine stories almost anywhere online\. This resource is a step\-by\-step guide to creating your first Twine game\.

This website supports creation and curation\. It encourages creative expression using digital media\. It also provides technology support as it teaches strategies to use new technologies\. It supports communication and collaboration as well as digital scholarship in a way that learners participate online comfortably using technology to communicate and share complex ideas\.

Students who need to create an online site could use this resource as a support\.

#### Learning Outcomes

-   Students will be able to apply digital media \(Twine\) to create and present content creatively\.
