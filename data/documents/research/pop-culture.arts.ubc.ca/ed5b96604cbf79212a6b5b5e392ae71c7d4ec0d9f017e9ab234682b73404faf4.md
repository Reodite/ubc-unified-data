#### Event Details

-   **Start:** 10 February 2026 12:30 pm
-   **End:** 10 February 2026 1:30 pm
-   **Categories:** [Comics Studies](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/events/category/comics-studies/), [Events](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/events/category/events/)

---

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/comic-book-club-graphics-1280-x-720-px-1.png)

Our sixth discussion will be on the theme of Disability Equity &amp; Blindness in comics through the lens of [*Dancing After TEN: a graphic memoir*](https://www.fantagraphics.com/products/dancing-after-ten?srsltid=AfmBOoqIBRBcU-Ktv0ExCnepAhgH7EYsXLIaKpLACiiJqH3Uh2ZidDxl) by Vivian Chong &amp; Georgia Weber\. It will be held on Tuesday, 10 February 2026 from 12:30pm to 1:30pm at the UBC Library\. [Register here](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_5731goaCSAbZw5o) so we know how many people to expect\! **Please note this event is only open to UBC students, staff and faculty\.**

From January to March 2026, [**Understanding Through Comics: A Comics and Comics Journal Club**](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/comics-studies/understanding-through-comics/) will continue to host a series of meetings and social events, including a start\-of\-term welcome luncheon, a series of discussions, one library visit, one speaker, and an end\-of\-term comics making socials\. We invite students, staff, and faculty from across the university to join us in reading comics as entertainment, research, and social change\. We especially welcome folks new to comics\.
