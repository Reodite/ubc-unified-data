### Created by Matt Clarke \(Theatre Artist, UBC MA Children’s Literature Candidate\)

This script excerpt is intended to stimulate exchange between senior elementary school students and high school students about social issues like housing and affordability\. It also offers young people the opportunity to read scripted text out loud and “play” in an educational setting, as well as a chance to discuss the broader impact of youth taking agency within performance arts\.

#### About the Creator

Matt Clarke grew up in Vancouver and studied Acting in New York at the New Actors Workshop\. He is a performer, director, writer, producer, researcher and teacher of Theatre for Young People with special focuses on Improvisation and Adaptation\. He received his BA from UBC in English Literature and Nordic Studies, and is currently in the MA in Children’s Literature \(MACL\) program at UBC studying youth agency in adapted Theatre for Young People\.
