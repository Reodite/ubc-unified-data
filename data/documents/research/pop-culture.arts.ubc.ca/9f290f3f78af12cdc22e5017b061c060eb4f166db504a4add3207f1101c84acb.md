### Researcher

Charlotte Schallié is a Professor of Germanic Studies and Chair of the Department of Germanic and Slavic Studies at the University of Victoria\. Her teaching and research interests include memory studies, visual culture studies &amp; graphic narratives, teaching and learning about the Holocaust, genocide and human rights education, community\-engaged participatory research, and arts\-based action research\. Together with Andrea Webb, she is the project co\-director of a 7\-year SSHRC\-funded Partnership Grant entitled “Visual Storytelling and Graphic Art in Genocide and Human Rights Education”
