### Program Assistant

### \(She/Her\)

Sally White is a second\-year student in UBC’s MA of Children’s Literature program\. She previously studied and worked in the children’s publishing industry where she fell in love with Middle Grade novels\. Her research explores scary stories for kids and a spatial analysis of the worlds they exist in\. As the PA for the Pop CC, she assists with communication on a number of projects, including helping organize the Understanding Through Comics book club\. In her free time, she loves gardening, watching scary movies, and petting any dogs in her vicinity\.
