### Created by Dr\. Ben Screech \(Lecturer, University of Gloucestershire\)

#### Intended Audience

This podcast is for any student interested in the intersection between YA literature and popular culture/pop music\.

#### Learning Objective

To explore, in audio format \(podcast\), the representations of pop music in contemporary YA fiction\.

#### Materials

-   *What, Like It’s Hard?* \(Podcast Ep\): [This One Tape Had All These Memories: Pop Music, Mixtapes and Young\-Adult Fiction](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/Link-to-podcast.pdf)

#### About the Creator

Dr Ben Screech is a Lecturer in Education with an interest in young adult literature\. He currently teaches at the University of Gloucestershire in the UK\. He has previously been a Visiting Lecturer at the University of the West of England and Visiting Fellow at the International Youth Library\.
