#### **Event Information**

From October 2025 to March 2026, **Understanding Through Comics** will host two start\-of\-term welcome luncheons, seven discussions, one colloquium, and two end\-of\-term socials\. We invite students, staff, and faculty from across the university to join us in reading comics as entertainment, research, and social change\. We especially welcome folks new to comics\.

Our first get\-together is a start\-of\-term welcome luncheon held in\-person and virtually at the UBC iSchool on Friday, October 3, 2025 from 12:00 PM to 1:30 PM\. Pizza, salad, and sweets will be served\. We encourage you to bring your favourite comics to foster conversation and community\.
