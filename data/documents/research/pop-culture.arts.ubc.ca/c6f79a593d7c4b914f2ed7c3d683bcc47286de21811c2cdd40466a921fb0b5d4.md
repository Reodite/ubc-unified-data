The Critical Play Lab is an intellectual space for critical play, creative game design, and transformative research and pedagogy in analog, digital, and embodied game studies\. We are a community of scholars, students, and members of the gaming industry and local game design community collaborating to support and facilitate new research, knowledge mobilization activities, and game design initiatives\.

## Critical Play Fellowships

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/03/182A2748.png)

The Critical Play Fellows comprise an interdisciplinary community of graduate students, faculty members, industry professionals, and independent designers for monthly programming that explores the intersections of digital, analog, and embodied gaming cultures, pedagogy, and critical theory\. The primary goal of the fellowship is to create and convene a local games\-based community\-of\-practice rooted in critical play\.   

Sessions will include monthly fellows’ meetings, guest speaker s and facilitators from the field s of game studies, game design, and/or community\-engaged gaming practice, as well as hands\-on workshop s\. Through these gatherings, participants will engage deeply with questions around the politics of play, design ethics, decolonial mechanics, radical imagination, narrative interventions, and community\-centered gaming\. The gathering s encourage both critical reflection and practical experimentation, offering a unique space to build connections between academic research, game design, and social justice work\. Join us in reimagining what games can do—and who they’re for\.  

[Learn More](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/critical-play-lab/critical-play-fellowship/)

## Critical Play Speakers Series

These events will take place on the UBC Vancouver campus and in the local Vancouver area and will be co\-hosted by various programs and units on campus and in the community\. The events will feature local, national or international speakers presenting on analog or digital game studies, innovative approaches to game design, or other aspects of critical play\.

*Check our [Event Listings](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/events/) for upcoming events\.*

## Games and Social Justice Lecture Series

In 2024\-2026, the Pop Culture Cluster is hosting a series of speaker events that examined the intersections between digital and analog gaming cultures and the work of social justice\. We commissioned local artist [Sarah Heng Hartse](https://sarahdrawsacrowd.com/) to live draw the events and provide a graphic recording of our time together\.
