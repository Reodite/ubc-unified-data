## [Critical Play Lab in The Conversation: “Not just a fun hobby: Board games can help build connections and reduce stress”](https://theconversation.com/not-just-a-fun-hobby-board-games-can-help-build-connections-and-reduce-stress-279299#new_tab)

Researchers at the University of Plymouth recently confirmed what board game fans and role\-playing game \(RPG\) enthusiasts have known for decades: that tabletop games “enhance well\-being, foster inclusion, and support learning, with strong evidence that games improve engagement\.” The researchers were particularly interested in how board games benefit people who display autistic traits, but tabletop gaming has social benefits that support personal well\-being for everyone\.
