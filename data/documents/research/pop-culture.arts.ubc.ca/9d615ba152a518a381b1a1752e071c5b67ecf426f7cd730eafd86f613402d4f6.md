Please join us in congratulating Spring 2025 Comics\-to\-Research Graduate Award winners Laen Hershler \(Language and Literacy Education\) and Cynthia Liu \(Creative Writing\)\! 

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/07/Comics-to-Research-Award-Insta.png)

Laen’s project *Memory in Motion: Dialoguing with Holocaust Survivor Voices through Research\-based Theatre and Performative Inquiry* explores Holocaust memory through the embodied practice of theatre and audience reflection\. Cynthia is working on a graphic novel entitled *Splendid China, USA* that blends historical research, feminist critique, and speculative fiction to explore immigration and reproductive justice\. You can learn more about these amazing projects [here](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/comics-studies/comics-to-research-graduate-awards/)\! 

The Comics\-to\-Research Graduate Awards allow UBC graduate students to commission a cartoonist or comic artist to create an 8–12\-page comic based on their graduate research\. Capitalizing on visual storytelling as a form of arts\-based knowledge mobilization, this opportunity allows awardees to share their work with a broader audience\.  

Interested in applying for the Comics\-to\-Research Graduate Awards? Learn how [here](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/comics-studies/comics-to-research-graduate-awards/)\!
