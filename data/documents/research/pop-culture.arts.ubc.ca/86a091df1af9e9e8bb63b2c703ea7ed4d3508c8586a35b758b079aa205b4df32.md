### Created by Darren Paltrowitz

#### Intended Audience

The module is designed for Media &amp; Cultural Studies mini course\. It explicitly integrates branding theory \(including brand equity, synergy, authorship, cultural myth, and legacy branding\) and applies these concepts through a sustained pop culture case study\.

#### Learning Objectives

This KISS module takes a slightly different analytical angle compared to the Van Halen module\. It emphasizes visual identity, merchandising, and brand structure as primary drivers of long\-term cultural relevance\.

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

1.  Move from theory to applied case work\.
2.  Understand concepts like brand equity, cultural branding, authorship and legacy branding\.
3.  Be able to analyze interview\-based media\.
