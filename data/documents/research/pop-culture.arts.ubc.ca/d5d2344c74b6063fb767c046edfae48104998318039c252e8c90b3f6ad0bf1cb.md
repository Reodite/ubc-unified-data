### PhD Student

Manisha Tripathy is a PhD student in Curriculum Studies \(Art Education\) at the University of British Columbia, Canada\. Her research focuses on the aesthetics of algorithms and questions of authority and authorship in conditions of digital saturation\. Working with image\-generating artificial intelligence in contemporary art education, she explores how creativity and identity are shaped through human\-algorithmic relationships and how visual culture influences the ways we see and understand ourselves and others\.
