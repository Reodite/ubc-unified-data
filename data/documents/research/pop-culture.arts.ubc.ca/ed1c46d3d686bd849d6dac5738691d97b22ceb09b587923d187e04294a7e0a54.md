## [Dorothee Leesing](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/dorothee-leesing/)

Dorothee Leesing is an Education Technology manager and media studies scholar whose work bridges digital learning, game studies, and the digital humanities\.

## [Kayla McIntyre](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/kayla-mcintyre/)

Kayla McIntyre \(they/she\) is a PhD student at UBC in the department of Theatre and Film\. Kayla’s work explores queerness and \(dis\)ability in game\-based performances\.

[David Durand\-Delacre](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/david-durand-delacre/ "David Durand-Delacre")[David Durand\-Delacre](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/david-durand-delacre-285x300.png)

## [David Durand\-Delacre](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/david-durand-delacre/)

I am a human geographer and critical migration researcher, currently Visiting Assistant Professor in the UBC Geography Department\.

[Rocco Ruan](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/rocco-ruan/ "Rocco Ruan")[Rocco Ruan](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/rocco-ruan-225x300.jpg)

## [Rocco Ruan](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/rocco-ruan/)

I’m a PhD student at UBC Computer Science, supervised by Karon MacLean\. I develop physical, multi\-player games played through small, remotely\-connected robot “swarms”, with the goal of promoting healthy online social interactions\.

[Dakota Brown](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/dakota-brown/ "Dakota Brown")[Dakota Brown](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/Brown_Dakota_2025-300x300.jpg)

## [Dakota Brown](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/dakota-brown/)

Dakota Brown \(she/her\) is pursuing an MA in Children’s Literature at the University of British Columbia\. She holds a BS\.Ed\. in Secondary Education and a BA in English from the University of Idaho\.

[Adri M](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/sebastian-saldarriaga-2/ "Adri M")[Adri M](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/adri-m-208x300.jpg)

## [Adri M](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/sebastian-saldarriaga-2/)

I am a trans refugee from the land of the Huetar people \(so\-called Costa Rica\)\. I am currently pursuing a Master’s degree at the Institute for Gender, Race, Sexuality, and Social Justice at the University of British Columbia\.

[Audrey Sum](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/audrey-sum/ "Audrey Sum")[Audrey Sum](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/Audrey-Sum-Headshot-289x300.jpeg)

## [Audrey Sum](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/audrey-sum/)

I’m Audrey, a third\-year English student with a minor in Creative Writing\. As a designer and writer passionate about the intersection of language and visual storytelling, I create digital experiences that communicate meaning beyond the screen\.

[Sebastian Saldarriaga](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/sebastian-saldarriaga/ "Sebastian Saldarriaga")[Sebastian Saldarriaga](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/Sebastian-Saldarriaga-300x292.png)

## [Sebastian Saldarriaga](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/sebastian-saldarriaga/)

I am a Biomedical Engineer and Master of Arts student in Science Education at UBC\. My research explores how culturally grounded digital storytelling can support STEAM education by integrating game\-based learning, serious games, and Indigenous pedagogies such as the First Peoples Principles of Learning\.

[Morgan Cselinacz](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/morgan-cselinacz/ "Morgan Cselinacz")[Morgan Cselinacz](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/Morgan-Cselinacz-223x300.jpg)

## [Morgan Cselinacz](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/morgan-cselinacz/)

With a Bachelor of Science in Psychology, Morgan Cselinacz also completed a Certificate in Computer Game Development during her undergraduate career, sparking her interest in the intersection between psychology and video game design\.

[Cynthia Liu](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/cynthia-liu/ "Cynthia Liu")[Cynthia Liu](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/files/2025/06/cynthia-liu-sq.png)

## [Cynthia Liu](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/cynthia-liu/)

Cynthia Liu is a diasporic North American Chinese writer \(she/hers\)\. Her MFA receives support from a British Columbia Arts Council fellowship and she is a 2025\-2026 Critical Play Lab Fellow in UBC’s Pop Culture Cluster\.
