#### Event Details

-   **Start:** 4 December 2024 7:00 pm
-   **End:** 4 December 2024 8:00 pm
-   **Categories:** [Events](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/events/category/events/)

---

Please join us on for the celebration and launch of [Issue 52 of the Bank Street *Occasional Paper Series*: **“****The Adventures of Trans Educators: A Comic Book Issue”**](https://www.bankstreet.edu/research-publications-policy/occasional-paper-series/ops-52/)

**Where: via Zoom**

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/download-289x300.png)The volume aims “**to celebrate and learn from the presence of trans educators in young people’s lives and to work toward a field of education that is far more inviting to trans people of all ages**” and was co\-edited by [**Harper B\. Keenan**](https://educ.ubc.ca/dr-harper-keenan/ "https://educ.ubc.ca/dr-harper-keenan/ Cmd+Click or tap to follow the link") \(Assistant Professor, Department of Curriculum and Pedagogy, UBC\), graduate student **[Lee Iskander](https://edcp.educ.ubc.ca/lee-iskander/)** \(graduate student, Department of Curriculum and Pedagogy, UBC\), and **Rachel Marie\-Crane Williams** \(Dean of Liberal Arts, University of North Carolina School of the Arts\)\.

**You can register [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeD8IJrGma6ekxMY77WLZv_sV63DVY-D7wIkiBABTDAl2BSig/viewform?pli=1&amp;pli=1)\.**
