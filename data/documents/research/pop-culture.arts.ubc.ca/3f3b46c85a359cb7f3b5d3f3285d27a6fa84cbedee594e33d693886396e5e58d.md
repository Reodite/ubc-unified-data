### Lecturer

Committed to art museum education for more than twenty\-five years, Marie\-France was Responsable des visites at the Musée d’art contemporain de Montréal from 1995\-2008, and was gallery educator for 11 years at the Vancouver Art Gallery\. She is currently a fulltime lecturer in art education and museum education at the University of British Columbia – Vancouver\. Marie\-France is president of the SIG on Education and Mediation in Museum \(SIGEMM\) within CSSE, and co\-editor of the journal *Canadian Art Teacher*\. After an M\.A\. in Art History from l’Université de Montréal, she completed a Ph\.D\. in Art Education at UBC\. Her research interests include: the notion of the art museum educator as the Deleuzian ‘mediator’, interrogating the concepts of interpretation and knowledge in the art museum, the critical historiography of art education in Canada, and the unsettling of art education through an attunement to place\. In her spare time, she is a devoted apprentice of ceramics and cello\.
