#### Event Details

-   **Start:** 27 January 2026 12:30 pm
-   **End:** 27 January 2026 1:30 pm
-   **Categories:** [Comics Studies](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/events/category/comics-studies/), [Events](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/events/category/events/)

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/D5-header-with-QR-1280-x-720-px.png)

From January to March 2026, [**Understanding Through Comics: A Comics and Comics Journal Club**](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/comics-studies/understanding-through-comics/) will continue to host a series of meetings and social events, including a start\-of\-term welcome luncheon, a series of discussions, one library visit, one speaker, and an end\-of\-term comics making socials\. We invite students, staff, and faculty from across the university to join us in reading comics as entertainment, research, and social change\. We especially welcome folks new to comics\.

Our fifth discussion will be held on Tuesday, 27 January 2026 from 12:30pm to 1:30pm at the UBC Library\. Don’t forget scissors and clear tape\.

[Click here to register\.](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_808UXEhFF6sdFQ2) **Please note this event is only open to UBC students, staff and faculty\.**
