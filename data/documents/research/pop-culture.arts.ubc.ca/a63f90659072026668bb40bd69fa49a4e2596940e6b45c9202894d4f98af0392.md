### Graduate Fellow

Dakota Brown \(she/her\) is pursuing an MA in Children’s Literature at the University of British Columbia\. She holds a BS\.Ed\. in Secondary Education and a BA in English from the University of Idaho\. Dakota previously taught high school in her hometown before moving to begin a graduate program\. Her research centers on how young adults shape moral value systems and social\-emotional development through interactions with media\. Dakota is especially interested in the impact of narratives on ethical reasoning\. She is currently writing her thesis, which is a case study of performative heroics found in the animated series My Hero Academia, focused on creating pedagogical, pop culture content for secondary ELA classrooms\.
