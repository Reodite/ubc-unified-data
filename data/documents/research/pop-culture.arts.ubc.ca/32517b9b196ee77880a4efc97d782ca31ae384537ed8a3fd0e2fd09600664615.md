#### **Description**

Join the Pop Culture Cluster and the [Centre for Teaching, Learning, and Technology \(CTLT\)](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/) for a two\-part workshop that will focus on how to develop and implement pop pedagogical methods, specifically critical play, into traditional or community\-based sites of learning\. Co\-hosted with the CTLT, this workshop will be led by facilitators Joseph Topornycky and PhD Candidate, Hans Ghezzi\. Part 1 will focus on the pedagogy of play, specifically on play as advancing pedagogical goals\. In Part 2, participants will develop an instructor guide for their play resources, so they can be used by others to teach a key idea or topic in their discipline\.

This workshop is open to all UBC faculty, staff, and students\.

#### **Details**

Both workshops will take place in the **Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre** \(note the different rooms\) with a livestream option\.  

-   Part 1: March 17, 2026 from 2\-5 PM in **Dodson Room \(302\)**
-   Part 2: March 24, 2026 from 2\-5 PM in **Peña Room \(301\)**

*Please note that these workshops will NOT be recorded, and some of the in person activities will not be available for webinar attendees\.*
