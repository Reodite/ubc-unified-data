### Created by Lindsay Knisely \(Continuing Lecturer, UC Santa Cruz Writing Program and Oakes College\)

#### Intended Audience

This is an exercise developed for college students in an introductory composition course\.

#### Learning Objectives

By the end of this exercise, students will be able to:

1.  Critically think about how they might connect their own experiences with ideas in texts\.
2.  Present their ideas from their assigned section to their peers\.
3.  Understand and be able to use analytical questions\.
