> “What matters is creating a framework for critical thinking and examining how media reflects as well as shapes social realities…Pop culture is far more than entertainment – it’s a catalyst for connection and change\.”

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/The-Source-feat.-image-e1757115470245.jpg)

The UBC Pop Culture Cluster was featured in the cover story for the September 5, 2025 edition of *The Source*\. The article discusses the many ways PopCC uses popular media to create accessible and engaging pedagogical materials for a range of audiences\. If you’re interested in learning more about how the Pop Culture Cluster got its start and how its paving the way for a more inclusive educational future, you can [read the article here](https://thelasource.com/cover-story/2025/08/19/breaking-barriers-using-pop-culture-in-pedagogy-and-community-engagement/)\!
