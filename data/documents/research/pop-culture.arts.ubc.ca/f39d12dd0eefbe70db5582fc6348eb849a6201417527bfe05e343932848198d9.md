# SAVE THE DATE

**Day 1: October 22, 12\-5 PM**

-   Lightning talks from climate researchers

**Day 2: October 23, 9 AM – 5 PM**

-   Game jam inspired by climate talks

**Location: Research Commons Presentation Room \(5th Floor\), UBC Library**

This two day event will feature a half\-day symposium with UBC climate researchers, who will present short lightning talks on their specific research\. Attendees will return the following day for a facilitated game jam session to rapidly prototype games inspired by an aspect of the climate research featured in the lightning talks\.
