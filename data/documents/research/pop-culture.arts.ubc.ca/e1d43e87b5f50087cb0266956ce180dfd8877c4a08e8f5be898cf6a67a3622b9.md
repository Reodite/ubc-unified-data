[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/10/Trauma-Informed-Health-Care-Report-Cover-232x300.png)This project brings together pediatric health researchers, the Canadian Paediatric Society, and comics artists to co\-create knowledge mobilization tools that centre youth voices in trauma\-informed health care\. Guided by a national Youth Advisory Council \(YAC\), the collaboration draws on community\-based participatory methods to ensure that young people’s lived experiences shape how trauma\-informed care is understood and practiced\.

Through this initiative, artists will work directly with youth advisors and the research team to produce **two case study comics \(8–12 pages each\)** that present youth perspectives in accessible and engaging formats\. These comics will serve as public\-facing resources for clinicians, policymakers, and communities, highlighting the value of comics as a medium for sharing complex, lived knowledge\.

This collaboration exemplifies the Pop Culture Cluster’s commitment to arts\-based approaches that connect scholarship, community voices, and creative practice\.
