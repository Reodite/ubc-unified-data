Meet **Kristina Luu**, **James Lloyd**, and **Ivana Filipovich**: the artists working together to brin g youth perspectives on trauma\-informed care to life\. Working in collaboration with the **Canadian Paediatric** **Society** and the **BC Children’s Hospital’s** **Youth Advisory Council** and research team, including Lead Youth Researcher and UBC Presidential Scholar **Damilola Mabadeje** who spearheaded this comics\-to\-research project, these comics creators will transform lived experiences into powerful visual narratives\. Through their distinct artistic styles and storytelling approaches, they will help ensure that youth voices are not only heard but felt – by clinicians, policymakers, and communities across Canada\. The project kicked off earlier this month and is expected to wrap up in the fall\. Let’s meet our artists\!

### **Kristina Luu** 

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/kristina-luu-199x300.png)

**Kristina Luu \(she/they\)** is a Vietnamese Canadian comic artist and illustrator from Vancouver, BC\.  
Her art focuses on creating impactful and diverse stories full of meaning and wonder\. From drawing classes to educational comics, she specializes in using art and comics as a form of meaningful community engagement and accessible learning\. 

Kristina is best known for illustrating the “BESTIES” middle\-grade graphic novels series written by Kayla Miller and Jeffrey Canino, published through HarperCollins\. She is also the head organizer of an independent Canadian comic collective called “Fruit Salad Comics”\. She enjoys self\-publishing zines and collecting doujinshi in her spare time\. 

---

### **James Lloyd** 

**[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/james-lloyd-246x300.png)James Lloyd** is a Vancouver cartoonist who has worked both professionally and independently in comics for over two decades\. He is best known for his work on the **Bongo Entertainment** line of comics, particularly as the lead artist on the **Futurama Comics** title \(based on the popular animated series\)\. In the professional realm, he has contributed art and stories to publishers like **First Second** books, **Boom\! Comics**, and **Oni Press**\. He has also worked in Vancouver’s animation industry since the mid\-90’s, returning after a hiatus on productions for **Dreamworks Animation** and **The Family Channel**, among others\.  

In recent years his creative focus turned to neighbourhood matters when he began to document housing issues and tenants’ rights battles in comic form\. He has drawn several stories concerning Vancouver neighbours facing wrongful evictions and contributed art to the campaigns formed to defend the tenants involved \(several of which have been successful, resulting in overturned evictions or compensatory actions\)\.  

In 2016, James stepped into the realm of comics education, playing a key role in the forming of **Langara College’s Graphic Novel and Comix program**, where he instructed both World Building and Comics History courses\. He has held presentations, workshops &amp; mentorship programs throughout his twenty years plus of drawing professionally and has finally learned how not to smudge his signature at conventions\.

### **Ivana Filipovich** 

[Image](https://phh-comicstudies-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/Ivana-Filipovich--295x300.png)

A Serbian\-Canadian cartoonist, illustrator, and writer, **Ivana Filipovich** worked in educational media at Simon Fraser University for over two decades before returning to cartooning in 2018\. Since then, her comics have been published in Canada and internationally, including in Sweden, Belgium, Czechia, Slovenia, and Serbia, and exhibited at venues such as L’Institut français galleries across the Balkans, the Angoulême International Comics Festival in France, and the Belgrade International Comics Festival\. 

Her first Canadian graphic novel was published by Conundrum Press in 2023 and was included on the CBC list of *20 Canadian Comics to Check Out in Fall 2023* as one of only three titles for an adult audience\. In the same year, she received the Doug Wright Award \(DWA\) for Best Self\-Published/Small Press Comic Book for her collection of short stories, *Where Have You Been*, becoming the first DWA recipient from Western Canada\. 

Her body of work includes hundreds of pages of comics published by respected publishers and leading literary and cultural magazines in Europe, while her educational illustrations have been featured in Canadian and Serbian museums\. As a designer, illustrator, art director, and executive producer, she has received numerous international and national awards with her SFU team, including the invitation\-only United Nations World Summit Award for the world’s best culture website, a Leo Award for Best Documentary in British Columbia, an Advertising &amp; Design Club of Canada \(ADCC\) award and an *Applied Arts Magazine* design awards\. 

Ivana’s comics\-related activism includes serving as a board member of StripoTetke \(Comics Aunties\), an organization that promotes diversity in the comic arts in Serbia and the Balkans\. As a storyteller, she is interested in impactful, psychologically grounded narratives featuring strong, diverse female characters\.
