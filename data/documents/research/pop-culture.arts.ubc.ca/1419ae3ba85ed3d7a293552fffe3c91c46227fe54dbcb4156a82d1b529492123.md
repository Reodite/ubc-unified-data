## [Call for Student Graphic Artists for Text Encoding Initiative Conference\!](https://pop-culture.arts.ubc.ca/comics-studies/comics-to-research/#new_tab)

The UBC Comics Studies Cluster and the Text Encoding Initiative Conference Organizing Committee invite applications from up to three student graphic artists/illustrators to create a graphic recording or a 1\-2\-page comic summarizing the highlights for three events happening at the international Text Encoding Initiative conference in Vancouver August 10\-14, 2026\. Artists should expect to engage with themes and topics around digital humanities, critical encoding methods, and computational markup practices\.
