### Community Fellow

Torien Cafferata \(they/he\) is an AuDHD interdisciplinary artist originally from Treaty 4 and Treaty 6 territory where they earned their BFA in Drama and trained as a performer, playwright, director, dramaturge, and educator\. As co\-Artistic Director of [It’s Not A Box Theatre](https://itsnotabox.ca/) they have toured immersive performance and installation work to the Prague Quadrennial, SummerWorks, and across Fringe Festivals\. Torien’s research spans a host of forms: devised creation methodology, social practice, site\-specific, mixed\-reality, game design, critical ludology, cultural theory, and clown\. He is an avid trifler with digital platforms and aesthetics, often using them in explorations of mad studies, interactivity and interpassivity, non\-places and hauntology\. For his MFA at Simon Fraser University, Torien experimented with lo\-fi human\-avataring to examine mad labour and play\. Most recently he has been teaching youth in playground and tabletop game design at Brainiacs Adventures, and working with Z\-space to facilitate interdisciplinary games and ludology research clusters in so\-called Vancouver\.
