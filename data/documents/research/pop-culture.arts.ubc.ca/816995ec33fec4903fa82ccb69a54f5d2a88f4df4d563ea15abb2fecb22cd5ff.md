### Created by Dakota Brown \(UBC MA Children’s Literature\)

#### Intended Audience

These mini lessons are written keeping in mind an audience of 6\-8th grade secondary students in an English Language Arts \(ELA\) classroom setting\.

#### Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

1.  Identify and define literary devices\.
2.  Be able to distinguish between different types of irony \(dramatic, verbal, and situational\)\.
3.  Understand the differences between parables and fables\.
4.  Produce thoughtful written reflections connecting themes to personal experiences\.
5.  Be able to debate narrative decisions and support with evidence from the source material\.

#### About the Creator

Dakota Brown \(she/her\) is pursuing a MA in Children’s Literature at the University of British Columbia\. She holds a BS\.Ed\. in Secondary Education and a BA in English from the University of Idaho\. Dakota previously taught high school in her hometown before moving to begin a graduate program\. She has recently defended her thesis, which is a case study of performative heroics found in the animated series *My Hero Academia*, and is focused on creating pedagogical, pop culture content for secondary ELA classrooms\.
