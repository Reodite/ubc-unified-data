### PhD Student

Imroze Singh Goindval is a PhD student in the School of Population and Public Health at the University of British Columbia\. His research examines caregiving for mandhbuddhi \(intellectual and developmental disabilities \[IDDs\]\) within Panjabi communities across Panjab \(India\) and the Panjabi diaspora in British Columbia\. Drawing on medical anthropology, feminist political economy, and critical disability studies, his work conceptualizes caregiving as a form of social reproduction and life\-making under conditions of migration, state neglect, and structural inequality\. Using transnational ethnography, his research traces how families assemble care across borders and navigate biomedical and disability support systems\. Imroze is also the founder of Kosh Health, where he works on inclusive, community\-informed approaches to public health communication, knowledge/language translation, and accessible systems design\.
