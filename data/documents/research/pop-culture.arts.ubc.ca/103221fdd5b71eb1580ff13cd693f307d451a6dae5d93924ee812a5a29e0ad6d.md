### Curated and designed for BC Campus – Digital Literacy

*License: [CC BY 4\.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)*

This lesson plan and activity asks students to analyze cultural differences and their impact on how we communicate in digital spaces\. Using worksheets and digital collaboration tools, students will compare cultures, reflect on their own, and discuss how cultural norms appear in digital spaces\. They will create group presentations highlighting their findings using tools like Padlet, Google Slides, or Canva\.

#### Learning Outcomes

-   Students will analyze and reflect on cultural differences and norms by comparing their own culture with others\.
-   Students will use digital tools to collaboratively create a presentation showcasing their findings\.
