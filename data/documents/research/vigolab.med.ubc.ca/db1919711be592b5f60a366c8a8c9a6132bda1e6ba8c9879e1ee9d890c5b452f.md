Dr\. Vigo is Chair of the Mental Health Services Workgroup of the WHO World Mental Health Surveys Initiative \(WMHSI\), led by Ron Kessler\. The WMHSI is the largest global collaboration of psychiatric epidemiology, with more than 160,000 respondents across 30 countries\. Dr\. Vigo is also the Canada PI of the WHO\-International College Survey Initiative, a partner in the Student E\-Mental Health Project\.
