Curious as to how *Minder* was designed and developed? This paper outlines the co\-development process our lab used to conceptualize and create *Minder\.* We detail the phases of development from initial to final app design to introduce all the main components of *Minder*, and highlight the importance of UBC student input in all aspects of design\.

*Vereschagin, M\., Wang, A\. Y\., Leung, C\., … &amp; Vigo, D\. \(2023\)\. Co\-developing tools to support student mental health and substance use: Minder app development from conceptualization to realization\. Journal of Behavioral and Cognitive Therapy\. 33, 1\. 35\-49\.*

[*https://doi\.org/10\.1016/j\.jbct\.2023\.02\.002*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbct.2023.02.002)
