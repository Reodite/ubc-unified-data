See a timeline of major milestones conducted by the Office of Chief Scientific Advisor for Psychiatry, Toxic Drugs &amp; Concurrent Disorders\.

---

- **Row 1**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### **September 4, 2026**

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    #### Beginning construction on mental\-health, substance\-use treatment facility in Surrey
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Construction has begun on the mental\-health and substance\-use facility in Surrey, which will expand access to care for people with complex needs\.
    
    The renovated facility will add 60 new beds, including involuntary admission capacity, to the health\-care system and will also include treatment and rehabilitation services to help people in the Fraser Health region who have the most severe mental\-health and substance\-use issues\.
    
    Read the full announcement by [BC Gov News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HLTH0034-001028)

- **Row 3**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### **July 10, 2026**

- **Row 4**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    #### Adding involuntary care facilities in Surrey, Prince George
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Updates on involuntary care facilities in Surrey and Prince George are announced\.
    
    The two facilities will add 132 mental\-health treatment beds for people requiring involuntary care\. In Prince George, a 72\-bed treatment centre will replace the former youth corrections facility\. In Surrey, a site will be renovated to provide 60 new mental\-health treatment beds\.
    
    Read the full announcement by [BC Gov News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HLTH0029-000808)

- **Row 5**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### **December 5, 2025**

- **Row 6**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    #### Guidance on applying the Mental Health Act for children and youth
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Dr\. Daniel Vigo issued a guidance document for doctors and psychiatrists across all health authorities that provides clarity about how the Mental Health Act can be applied to admit and treat people younger than 19\.
    
    This guidance builds off the guidance for treatment of adults under the Mental Health Act released in March 2025\.
    
    Read the full announcement by [BC Gov News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/33116) and the [guidance document](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/managing-your-health/mental-health-substance-use/general_guidance_for_physicians_on_treatment_of_children_and_youth_with_substance_use_disorders.pdf) on gov\.bc\.ca\.

- **Row 7**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### **September 26, 2025**

- **Row 8**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    #### **Expansion of voluntary &amp; involuntary beds for Prince George**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The opening of involuntary care facilities at Prince George and Surrey is announced\. Premier David Eby announces new involuntary care beds in Surrey and Prince George, in addition to more on the way at pre\-existing facilities elsewhere\.
    
    Read the media release by [PrinceGeorge\.ca](https://www.princegeorge.ca/city-hall/news-notices/involuntary-care-facility-announcement-prince-george-ubcm)

- **Row 9**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### **June 3, 2025**

- **Row 10**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    #### **Opening of Alouette Homes**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The opening of Alouette Homes in Maple Ridge is announced\.
    
    Alouette Homes is a designated mental\-health facility with 18 new involuntary care beds designed to support people who meet the criteria under the Mental Health Act\. These are people who have severe and persistent mental\-health disorders, often combined with other challenges, such as addictions and brain injuries, which may impact their behaviour and ability to interact safely with others\.
    
    Read the full announcement by [BC Gov News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HLTH0053-000534)\.

- **Row 11**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### **April 24, 2025**

- **Row 12**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    #### **New involuntary care beds open at Surrey Pretrial Services Centre**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    10 new involuntary care beds are now open at Surrey Pretrial Services Centre, providing people in custody who are in crisis and have overlapping mental\-health and addiction challenges, as well as brain injuries due to toxic\-drug overdoses, with specialized involuntary care\.
    
    Read the media release by [BC Gov News\.](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HLTH0035-000373)

- **Row 13**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### **March 12, 2025**

- **Row 14**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    #### **Mental Health Act Memo for Adults released**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Dr\. Daniel Vigo issued a guidance document to the clinical community, including doctors and psychiatrists across all health authorities, to provide clarification on how the Mental Health Act can be used to provide involuntary care for adults when they are unable to seek it themselves\.
    
    Read the full announcement by [BC Gov News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HLTH0015-000202) and the [guidance document](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/managing-your-health/mental-health-substance-use/general_guidance_for_physicians_on_the_use_of_the_mental_health_act.pdf) on gov\.bc\.ca\.

- **Row 15**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### **September 15, 2024**

- **Row 16**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    #### **BC to expand care for people with concurrent brain injury, mental health and substance use challenges**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Dr\. Daniel Vigo joined Premier David Eby and others in announcing his recommendations to improve outcomes for people with severe mental illness, substance use disorder, and acquired brain injury, including expanding secure facilities for individuals under the Mental Health Act and to enhance mental health treatment within BC corrections for those with mental health and addiction challenges\.
    
    Read the announcement by [BC Gov News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HLTH0015-000202)\.

- **Row 17**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    ##### **June 5, 2024**

- **Row 18**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    #### **Dr\. Daniel Vigo’s Appointment as British Columbia’s Chief Scientific Advisor for Psychiatry, Toxic Drugs &amp; Concurrent Disorders**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Announced by BC Premiere David Eby, Dr\. Vigo will focus on improving care for individuals with mental illness and substance use disorders, with \(or at risk of\) acquired brain injury due to exposure to toxic drugs\.
    
    In this role, Dr\. Vigo will collaborate with several health authorities, Indigenous partners and individuals with lived experiences\. He will access all relevant data sources, conduct centralized data analysis and interpretation, and provide ongoing evidence\-based recommendations to enhance the health system’s response\.
    
    Read the announcement by [BC Gov News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024MMHA0028-000873) or watch the [livestream recording](https://www.youtube.com/live/pAj-15exF8U?si=Ydz5diqZcq3GGQPf)\.
