MAGIC’s has 4 lab spaces, several offices and various media and graphics equipment\. Associates of MAGIC and their students may use the facilities by contacting us\.

Our facilities include:  
meeting room  
usability lab \(about 100 sqft\)  
student desk space and computers in either the large lab or small lab  
some visiting faculty office space  
high\-end video edit suite  
hemisphere dome display area \(eLumens\)  
The following equipment can be loaned to MAGIC affliates and students\.  
Multisync LT84 Video projector  
Sony Digital Video Camera Recorder DCR\-VX2000  
Sony Digital HD Video Recorder HDR\-FX1  
Sony HDD videocamera DCR\-SR100  
Canon Powershot Digital Camera S5IS
