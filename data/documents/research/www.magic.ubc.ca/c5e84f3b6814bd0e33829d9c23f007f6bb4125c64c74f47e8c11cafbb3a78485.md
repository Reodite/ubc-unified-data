**EwE6 – Mockup of Decision Support tool:**  
  
The LOFP has completed the first two years of the project which is to create a new version of EwE6 that has been successfully released to the scientific community\. It is currently halfway through the second phase of the project which is to build on the gaming and visualization software\. This outcome will also result in a single player gaming version of EwE as well as a multiplayer gaming version with extensive visualization and decision\-making system capabilities\.  
  
The project requires four years of development and will be directed by Dr\. Villy Christensen of the Sea Around Us Project, and will be based at the Fisheries Centre, University of British Columbia\. It is funded by the [Lenfest Ocean Program](http://www.lenfestoceanfutures.org/) and partnered with MAGIC\.
