MAGIC’s has lab spaces, several offices and various media and graphics equipment\. Associates of MAGIC and their students may use the facilities by contacting us\.

Our facilities include:

-   meeting room
-   usability lab \(about 100 sqft\)
-   student desk space and computers in either the large lab or small lab
-   some visiting faculty office space
-   high\-end video edit suite
