### Welcome to the Public Screen &amp; Personal Interaction Project

This project is designed to explore the domain of public interaction with large screens using personal devices such as cell phones or hand held devices\.

[Image](https://www.magic.ubc.ca/wiki/uploads/Projects/display.png)  
Large Public Displays

The proliferation of public displays with minimal interaction and personal devices such as cell phone or PDAs with small screens and limited AV offers a unique and growing environment for public\-private dual screen interaction\.

We address this space to explore the design of interactive applications that use the combined strengths of the two to produce rich unified interactive experiences\.
