### Overview

MAGIC is an interdisciplinary research lab with research projects in Ubiquitous Computing \(ubicomp\), HCI, systems, and multimedia\. Current areas of interest include an exploration of large screen public displays, work on mobile multimedia services, vision techniques including perosn, object and gesture recognition, multi\-modal interfaces for large screen displays, and middleware abstractions for ubiquitous computing systems\.
