-   [Open House](https://www.magic.ubc.ca/wiki/pmwiki.php/MobileCampus/OpenHouse) – Is an integration of mobile services, social networks, e\-commerce, location\-awareness, QR\-codes, and validation technologies to stage campus\-enlivening, place\-embedded experiences\. Users may participate in various modes including remotely, anonymously, and in\-person\.
-   [Campus Home](https://www.magic.ubc.ca/wiki/pmwiki.php/MobileCampus/CampusHome) – prototype a hybrid user interface combining the best of a native user experience with the flexibility of HTML5\. The approach will extend the existing directory concept with a native ‘front page’ acting as initial user interface and offering a more integrated user experience\.
-   [MAGIC Digital U](https://www.magic.ubc.ca/wiki/pmwiki.php/MobileCampus/MAGICDigitalU) – Digital\-U Competition designed to engage faculty, staff, students, and residents to share in the imagination and creation of digital services that significantly enhance the UBC experience\. The inaugural theme will be exciting mobile services to extend the Kurogo platform\.
