Overview

Rhetoric is defined as the field of effective communication\. Video games are an interactive medium that engage participants\. Rather than looking at in\-game interactions, our focus is outside\-of\-the\-game rhetorical appeal in which we concentrate on games with a purpose \(e\.g\. mainstream games, games for change, games for good all have a particular purpose\) trying to understand how games are designed to produce change \(not necessarily change in the positive sense, but change as in difference\)\. This knowledge could then provide the foundation for the design of a prototype game that illustrates rhetorical appeal through gameplay\.  
Relevant Areas

Human Communication Technologies, Interactive Media, Game Studies, Rhetoric, Human Behaviour  
Questions of interest

Regarding the history of gameplay, what key moments \(board, video, mobile\) over time shaped the current state of gameplay?  
What are the rhetorical strategies involved to help achieve a particular purpose beyond the game?  
Is there a particular theoretical \(e\.g\. art movement\) or rhetorical framework that could be used to adequately critique the rhetorical mechanisms of a game?
