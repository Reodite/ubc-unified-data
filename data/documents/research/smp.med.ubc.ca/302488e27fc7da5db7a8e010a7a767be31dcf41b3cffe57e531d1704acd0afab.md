The Southern Medical Program strives to deliver research excellence, knowledge translation and exchange that responds to the health care needs of British Columbians, in particular those living within the BC Interior\.

[Image](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/93/2018/12/Faculty-Research-768x307.jpg)

### Centre for Chronic Disease Prevention and Management

Based at UBC Okanagan, the Centre for Chronic Disease Prevention and Management harnesses the strengths of researchers from both Okanagan and Vancouver campuses and Interior Health to support new discoveries and knowledge translation\.

[Learn more](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/research/chronic-disease-prevention-program/)

[Image](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/93/2018/12/Student-Research-Third-Level-768x307.jpg)

### Student Research

Students have numerous opportunities to engage in research opportunities in the BC Interior including Summer Student Research Projects and Studentships with the British Columbia Cancer Agency\.

[Learn more](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/research/student-research/)

[Image](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/93/2018/12/Faculty-Research-Landing-768x307.jpg)

### Faculty Research

The Southern Medical Program provides assistance with research projects and can help recruit students to provide research support\.

[Learn more](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/research/faculty-research/)

[Image](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/93/2023/10/2023-ISHC-768x307.jpg)

### UBC Okanagan Interdisciplinary Student Health Conference \(ISHC\)

The ISHC brings together students from across UBC Okanagan to showcase their health science research and public health experiences at an engaging presentation event\.

[Learn more](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/research/ishc/)
