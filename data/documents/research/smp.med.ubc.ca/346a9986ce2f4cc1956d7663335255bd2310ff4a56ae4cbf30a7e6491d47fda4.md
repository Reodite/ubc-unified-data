The UBC Okanagan Interdisciplinary Student Health Conference \(ISHC\) brings together students from across UBC Okanagan to showcase their health science research and public health experiences through engaging presentations\.

This student\-led conference is centred on showcasing undergraduate and graduate student researchers\. Students are invited to submit abstracts for poster or oral presentations\. Exemplary presentations will be recognized at the end of the conference\.

Thank you to all our student presenters, adjudicators, volunteers, and the planning committee for making the 2026 Interdisciplinary Student Health Conference such a tremendous success\. Your dedication, expertise, and enthusiasm brought this year’s event to life\. Details for next year’s conference will be announced October 2026\.

***Resolution of retinal folds in nanophthalmos over 15 years*

**Predictors of Health &amp; Health Technologies: Sage Wiebe  
***The power of peer support: a longitudinal study of online peer support, health, and wellbeing*

**Biomedicine and Pharmaceuticals: Nicole Bushfield  
**Cytokine secretion and circulating immune cell counts in response to a 48\-hour fast with and without aerobic exercise

**Community and Social Health: Raza Noor  
***Pain in context: sociodemographic influences in fibromyalgia*

**QI Research and Health Systems Change: Reegan Forsyth  
***Building confidence to act: an inclusive and affordable, peer\-led approach to basic life support training*

**Foundational Science &amp; Health Technologies: Christine Ashenhurst  
**Enhancing hypertension awareness and health promotion through community outreach events

**QI Research and Health Systems Change: Lakoda Thomas  
***Indigenous cultural safety in psychedelic\-assisted psychotherapy*

**Biomedicine and Pharmaceuticals: Janaeya Baher  
***Rethinking chronic lung disease through three\-dimensional alveolar models*
