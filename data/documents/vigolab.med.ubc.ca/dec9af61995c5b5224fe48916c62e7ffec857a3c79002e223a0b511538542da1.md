Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias not only cause suffering to the individual but have high societal and economic costs\. This column estimates that, globally, these diseases contributed to a loss of 33\.1 million years of full health \(measured in disability\-adjusted life years\) in 2019\. Over the next 30 years, the loss could more than triple, with the burden increasingly falling on low\- and middle\-income countries\. To ensure the future health and functioning of an ageing global community, the global community must invest effectively and efficiently in R&amp;D and scale effective supportive interventions for Alzheimer’s and related dementias\.
