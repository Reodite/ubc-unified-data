**September 15, 2024**

Dr\. Daniel Vigo joined Premier David Eby and others in announcing plans to expand secure facilities for individuals under the Mental Health Act and to enhance mental health treatment within BC corrections for those with mental health and addiction challenges\.

Read the announcement by [BC Gov News,](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024PREM0043-001532) the related article by [Times Colonist](https://www.timescolonist.com/local-news/only-a-few-hundred-people-likely-to-need-involuntary-care-under-new-plan-psychiatrist-9556385), and an interview of Dr\. Vigo by [The Tyee](https://thetyee.ca/News/2024/10/12/Man-Behind-NDP-Involuntary-Care-Proposal/)\.
