# Overview &amp; Important Information

The online UBC Zero Waste Market resells used \(second hand\) furniture and accessories to support the transition to a circular economy and reduce waste\. Items cannot be shipped and must be picked\-up from the UBC Vancouver Point Grey campus during business hours\.

[Image](https://sustain-zerowaste-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/IMG_4152-940x705.jpg)

**UBC Faculties and departments**

Pay by ISD instead of credit card\. At payment enter the UBC promo code [found here](https://sustain-zerowaste-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/ubc-promo-code/)\.

Contact us to pick up the items\.

We **strongly recommend** researching or viewing the item prior to purchase by contacting [furniture\.reuse@ubc\.ca](mailto:furniture.reuse@ubc.ca), as no refunds are provided\.

Please read the item description carefully to understand the collection information\. Items sold at the online and in\-person market are different\. Items purchased online are **not** available for collection at the in\-person market and must be collected by appointment\.

A signed waiver must be completed prior to collection, see [terms and conditions](https://sustain-zerowaste-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/example/)\.

*Note that **taxes are included** in the prices shown for the **online** market items\.*
