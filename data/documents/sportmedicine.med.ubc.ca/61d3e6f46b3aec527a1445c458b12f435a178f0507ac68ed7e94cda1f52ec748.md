At UBC Sports Medicine we value mental health, recognizing the role it plays in your well being\. If you or someone you know is looking for mental health supports we have a few recommended resources to help get you started\.

Low Cost Resource from Willow Tree: [https://willowtreecounselling\.ca/wp\-content/uploads/resources/reduced\-cost\-counselling\.pdf](https://willowtreecounselling.ca/wp-content/uploads/resources/reduced-cost-counselling.pdf) 

PSCTC Counselling Resources: [https://prtc1\-educ\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2023/10/Counselling\-Resources\.\-August\-2023\.pdf](https://prtc1-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/10/Counselling-Resources.-August-2023.pdf) 

SportMedBC has a directory of trusted Mental Performance Consultants: [https://sportmedbc\.com/practitioners/category/mental\-performance\-consultant/](https://sportmedbc.com/practitioners/category/mental-performance-consultant/)

For adult clients \(min\. 19 yrs old\) who may be seeking sport mental performance support, we have limited appointments with our on site Psychologist\. Please contact our front desk to inquire\. Note: availability is limited for this practitioner\.
