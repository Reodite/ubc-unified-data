**Director UBC Gunn IMS Program, Division of Sport Medicine**

Lyndal works with professional dancers, professional musicians, and elite athletes, yet she equally enjoys treating people from all walks of life who want to improve musculoskeletal function, physical capacity, and quality of life\. She has extensive training in manual therapy and exercise prescription, with a dedicated focus in Gunn IMS\.

She is director of the [UBC Gunn IMS](https://www.gunnims.com/) program and is also a [Senior Instructor](https://www.gunnims.com/gunn-ims-instructors.html) in the program\.

She serves as chair of the Acupuncture and Dry Needling Division of the Canadian Physiotherapy Association\.

**Education**

University of Sydney, BAppSc\. \(Phty\)  
University of Western Australia, MManTher\.  
University of British Columbia, PhD

**Research Interests**

The neurophysiological characteristics of persistent pain and its management with Gunn IMS\.
