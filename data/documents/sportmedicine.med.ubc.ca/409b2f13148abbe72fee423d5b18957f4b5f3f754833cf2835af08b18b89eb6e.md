### At the **[UBC](http://ubc.ca/) SEM**, we help maximize your involvement with sport and exercise to live a happy and healthy life\. We are committed to excellence in education, research and service\. Our goal is to embed sport and exercise medicine as a core treatment to help improve patient outcomes and deliver sustainable health change\.

## *Zero Tolerance Policy*

We do not accept:

-   Abusive, aggressive, or threatening behavior
-   Discriminatory or harassing language or actions
-   Verbal or physical intimidation

Any form of inappropriate conduct may result in the refusal of service and, if necessary, notification to the appropriate authorities\. We are here to help\. Please be kind and respectful to everyone\.
