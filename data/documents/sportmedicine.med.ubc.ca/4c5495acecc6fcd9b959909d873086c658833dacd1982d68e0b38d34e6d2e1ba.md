## **Parking**

Parking options include the Thunderbird Parkade \(6085 Thunderbird Boulevard\) which is approximately 80m from the centre\.

There are also some metered parking spaces on the other side of Wesbrook Mall\.

All parkades and many surface lots have designated accessible spaces\.

-   **[Surface lots](http://parking.ubc.ca/surface-lots)**
-   **[Parkades](http://parking.ubc.ca/parkades)**

For information about parking rates, visit the **[UBC Parking](https://parking.ubc.ca/)** website\.

[Check here](https://thunderbirdarena.ubc.ca/events/) for a list of events that may affect parking availability in the Thunderbird Parkade\.

***UBC SEM does not assume responsibility for any parking related issues\. Please arrive with sufficient time to park comfortably before your appointment\. For parking support, please visit [https://parking\.ubc\.ca/\.](https://parking.ubc.ca/)***
