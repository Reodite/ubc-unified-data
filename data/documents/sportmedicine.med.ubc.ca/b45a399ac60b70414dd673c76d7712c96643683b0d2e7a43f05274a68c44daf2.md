Through the [**Enhanced Skills Residency Program \(ESP**](https://postgrad.familypractice.ubc.ca/enhanced-skills-program/training-programs/)\), UBC provides a number of residency positions for family physicians in BC for the purpose of developing specific advanced skills\. These positions represent a commitment on the part of the Department of Family Practice, and the Faculty of Medicine, to respond to community needs in an effective way\. 

The Sport and Exercise Medicine Residency is a year\-long program that is administered by UBC ESP\. Please refer to the Enhanced Skills [site](http://postgrad.familymed.ubc.ca/enhanced-skills-program/) for program contacts and application details\. Visit the CASEM website [here](https://casem-acmse.org/education/fellowships/) for a list of Enhanced Skills Sports Medicine Residency programs in Canada\.

**From the Enhanced Skills Program website:**

UBC SEM residents in this program will be competent in the following areas:

-   Assessment and treatment of MSK and sport\-related medical problems
-   Sideline assessment and return\-to \-play decisions
-   Role and responsibilities of team physician
-   SEM procedures including injections, joint reductions and bracing
-   Education of patients, learners and the community
-   Team collaboration
-   Participation in research and clinical studies
-   Practice of family medicine

The program combines several longitudinal clinics with core rotations throughout the year\.

**Note:**

The UBC Enhanced Skills residency programs, including Sport and Exercise Medicine, are only open to physicians who hold their Certification in the Canadian College of Family Physicians \(CCFP\), or who are eligible to attain this certification within their training year\. We are not a clinical fellowship program, and are unable to train international graduates who do not hold that certification\.

If you are eligible according to what is stated above, you can apply to the program via the online form on our admissions page – [https://postgrad\.familymed\.ubc\.ca/enhanced\-skills\-program/admissions/](https://postgrad.familymed.ubc.ca/enhanced-skills-program/admissions/)
