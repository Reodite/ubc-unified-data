Tuesday June 17, 2025,

UBC Sport &amp; Exercise Medicine was well represented at the CONCACAF GOLD CUP Canada vs Honduras Men’s soccer match, hosted at BC Place in Vancouver\. Members of our multidisciplinary sports medicine team were on\-site to assist with venue medical coordination, on field emergency response, as well as doping control: providing world\-class support for the venue’s elite athletes on game day\.

Our physicians specialize in managing sports injuries, acute and chronic muscle injury, tendon and joint pains, as well as non surgical interventions\.

Located in Vancouver, right on the UBC Campus, we commonly see conditions such as:

· ACL/MCL/meniscal tears and sprains

· Hamstring pulls and quad strains

· Rotator cuff pain

· Ankle sprains

· Adductor injuries

· Back pain

· Hip pain

· Knee pain \(including osteoarthritis\)

At the UBC sports medicine clinic, we provide comprehensive diagnostic evaluation, personalized rehabilitation plans, IMS Physiotherapy services, and ultrasound\-guided injections \(including Platelet Rich Plasma \(PRP\)\) for patients of all ages and ability levels\. We guide our patients through return\-to\-sport and collaborate with orthopedic surgeons and sport\-specific physiotherapists as needed\. Our goal is to return you to the activities you love, stronger and safer\.

Do you want to receive the elite level care of a professional athlete? Get a [referral](https://sportmedicine.med.ubc.ca/referrals/) to the UBC Sport &amp; Exercise Medicine Clinic today\.
