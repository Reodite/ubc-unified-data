**Our team recently wrapped up our annual conference in beautiful Whistler, BC\.**  
This event offers an excellent opportunity to collaborate across disciplines with a shared focus on the management and treatment of musculoskeletal \(MSK\) conditions\.

Our session brought together experts from the [Allan McGavin Sports Medicine Centre](https://www.allanmcgavinphysio.com/) and the UBC Sport &amp; Exercise Medicine team, fostering rich dialogue and innovation in MSK care\.
