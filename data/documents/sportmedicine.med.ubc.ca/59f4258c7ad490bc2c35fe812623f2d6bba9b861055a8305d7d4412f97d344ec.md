Every month, care providers at the Chan Gunn Pavilion have discussions about journal articles\. These discussions are *internal* rounds that happen within the department\. On this page you can see what we’re reading and talking about in these educational rounds\.

Journal Club happens on the first Wednesday of the month\. It is currently available to members of our centre only\. Group discussions happen over Zoom\.

Questions? Email [sem\.clinic@ubc\.ca](mailto:sem.clinic@ubc.ca)\.
