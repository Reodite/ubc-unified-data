If our clinic has requested for you to get an MRI:

1.  Please ensure you wait a full six weeks from the date we put a referral in to receive an appointment date\. **Please do not contact our office prior to the six week deadline about MRI timelines\.**
2.  If you have not received an appointment following the 6 week timeline please contact central intake regarding the status of your MRI at :1\-866\-588\-6954
3.  Note: If you wish to communicate information regarding your availability for MRI, to be put on a cancelation list etc you must: a\) contact central intake at the number above to see which hospital has been assigned your MRI\. 2\) Contact the radiology department of that hospital and communicate with them directly\. ***Our clinic does not support with any MRI related scheduling inquiries\.***
