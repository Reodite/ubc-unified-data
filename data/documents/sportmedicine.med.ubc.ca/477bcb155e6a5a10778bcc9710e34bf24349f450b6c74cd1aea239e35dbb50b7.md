***BA, MSc, CSEP\-CEP, SENR***

Alan is a performance nutritionist and clinical exercise physiologist with over 15 years of experience helping athletes, executives, and active individuals optimize health, body composition, and performance\. Alan works across high\-performance sport, clinical practice, and corporate wellness, combining evidence\-based performance strategies with practical, real\-world application\.  
Alan has supported professional and elite athletes across a wide range of sports in Europe and North America, including the NFL, MLB, soccer, rugby, golf, endurance sport, and combat sports\. His experience includes working within high\-performance environments such as EXOS and delivering applied sports nutrition support to athletes ranging from youth development to professional level\.  
Alan served as Head of Science &amp; Education, EMEA for Glanbia Performance Nutrition, where he led global education initiatives and contributed to product innovation and scientific communication for brands including Optimum Nutrition\. He has also delivered corporate wellness and performance programs for organizations including Google, Microsoft, and Fitbit\. In addition to his applied work, Alan has lectured in exercise and nutrition science at University College Dublin and regularly delivers education workshops and presentations on sports nutrition, performance, longevity, metabolic health, and body composition assessment\.

Alan holds a Bachelor’s degree in Exercise Science from Waterford Institute of Technology and a Master of Science in Exercise Nutrition Science from University of Chester\. He is a Certified Exercise Physiologist \([CSEP\-CEP](https://store.csep.ca/pages/member-directory?srsltid=AfmBOooaXnqpvytvm594eyhQmgTnDnZsZuhqavG9Wb-vZZCwX2kE3VSi)\) through the Canadian Society for Exercise Physiology and is also a [SENr](https://www.bda.uk.com/senr-sport-and-exercise-nutrition-register/senr-listing/alan-kenny-alan-kenny-nutrition-vancouver-canada-1012167.html)\-accredited sports nutrition practitioner under the British Dietetic Association\.
