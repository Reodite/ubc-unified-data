As a recognized [FIFA Centre of Excellence](https://www.fifa.com/about-fifa/medical/centers-of-excellence), we welcome collaboration with sports organizations and teams to support their athletes\. On our campus we work closely with UBC Athletics to support varsity athletes\. Some current notable partnerships include: The Canadian Sport Institute \(which supports Olympic and Paralympic athletes\), The BC Lions football team, The Whitecaps soccer team and the Vancouver Canucks hockey team\. 

We offer medical care to all UBC varsity athletes\. Our exclusive partnerships allow us to provide elite care to the athletes on our campus\.

Think we would be a good partnership for you? We are always happy to collaborate and discuss potential new partnerships\. If you would like to discuss further please email us at [sem\.clinic@ubc\.ca](mailto:sem.clinic@ubc.ca)\. 

**Note:** We follow the provincial MSP guidelines for healthcare services\. International collaboration opportunities may be limited by the government policies\.

## Bauerfeind Medical

We’re proud partners of Bauerfeind Medical, offering their trusted products alongside other high\-quality options\. While we don’t refer exclusively to Bauerfeind, their world\-class supports remain a valued part of the solutions we provide\. *Read more about out partnership [here](https://sportmedicine.med.ubc.ca/partnerships/our-partnership-with-bauerfeind/)\.*
