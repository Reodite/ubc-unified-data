**A Legacy in Motion** – Celebrate Dr\. Jack Taunton’s lifelong commitment to movement and medicine by supporting a new entrance award for future medical leaders\.

Dr\. Jack Taunton’s life was defined by resilience, service, and an unshakable belief in the power of exercise to transform lives\. From co\-founding the Vancouver Sun Run and serving as Chief Medical Officer for the Olympics, Jack built a legacy rooted in mentorship, innovation, and community\.

Now you can help carry that legacy forward\. Your gift will empower the next generation of physicians and leaders in sports and exercise medicine, those who will shape the future of health and performance in Canada and beyond\.

**Donate today to support the Jack and Cheryl Taunton – MD Entrance Award**

This is more than a donation\. It’s a chance to honour a remarkable life by investing in those who share Jack’s passion for movement, medicine, and making a difference\.

**Give today\. Inspire tomorrow\.**  
Let’s continue what Jack and Cheryl started together\.

For more information please review the award page [here](https://give.ubc.ca/projects/jack-cheryl-taunton-md-entrance-award/)\.
