The Story Collider is delighted to partner with the [University of British Columbia’s Ocean Leaders](https://oceanleaders.ubc.ca/) for this very special edition of our show\! After two intensive days of workshopping with us, five people will take the stage to share true, personal stories of how the oceans and its denizens have shaped their lives\.

This is Story Collider’s first show in Vancouver – you won’t want to miss it\!

Hosted by Erin Barker and Liz Neeley\. Doors open at 7:30 pm, and show starts at 8:00 pm\. Please note: This venue is 19\+\.

Purchase tickets [here\.](https://www.storycollider.org/shows/2017/11/3/vancouver-oceans)
