**Engaging with Aboriginal Populations**

2\-4:30pm, Thursday April 20th

AERL room 216, Hakai Node

This workshop, sponsored by Ocean Networks Canada, will discuss how to work with aboriginal populations on ocean and coastal issues\. The workshop will include discussions of past experiences and present applied strategies and approaches\.

To RSVP, please email [ocean\.leaders@ubc\.ca](mailto:ocean.leaders@ubc.ca)
