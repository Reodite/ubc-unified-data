#### The fish you don’t know you eat  
A panel discussion featuring UBC’s Global Reporting Program and the Ocean Leaders Fellows who participated in the project

Friday, 10 January 2020  
11:00 AM PST  
\[Aquatic Ecosystems Research Laboratory \(AERL\) Theatre\)\]  
2202 Main Mall, Vancouver, BC V6T 1Z4

25% of fish caught in the ocean don’t land directly on our plates\. They’re churned into something called fishmeal\. That fishmeal is mainly used to feed farmed fish, which account for more than half of the seafood we consume globally\. But what are the true costs of this process? NBC News with UBC’s Global Reporting Program and three Ocean Leaders Fellows travelled to China, Peru and West Africa – key locations in the global fishmeal industry – to find out\.

**[Image](https://geog-oceanleaders.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/01/Fish-you-dont-know-you-eat-digital-slide-360x203.jpg)**
