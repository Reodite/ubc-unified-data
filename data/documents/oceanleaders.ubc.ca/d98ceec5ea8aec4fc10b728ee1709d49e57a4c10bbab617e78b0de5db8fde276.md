## **Welcome to Ocean Leaders\!**

Training Our Future Ocean Leaders \(Ocean Leaders\) is an exciting program at UBC dedicated to training the next generation of marine researchers\. Learn more about our program by visiting our [About](https://geog-oceanleaders.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about/) page, and learn about our [dynamic team,](https://geog-oceanleaders.sites.olt.ubc.ca/people/) and our [diverse group of partners](https://geog-oceanleaders.sites.olt.ubc.ca/people/collaborators/)\.
