## People

We gratefully acknowledge the financial support for this project provided by UBC Vancouver students via the Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund\. We are also grateful for the financial support received from the Kwantlen Polytechnic University 0\.6% Professional Development Award\. We would like to recognize the support we’ve received from the UBC Vancouver Centre for Teaching, Learning, and Technology—in particular from Jeff Miller, Jason Myers, Will Engle, and Trish Varao\-Souza\.
