#### **The PSCTC is back in the newly renovated space in the Neville Scarfe Building\!  
****Monday, March 14\[th\] from 4:00 – 6:00pm PST**

---

**The Psychological Services &amp; Counselling Training Centre \(PSCTC\)** at the University of British Columbia invites you to join us as we celebrate our newly renovated space\. Now that we are back on campus, we are finally able to welcome students, staff, faculty, and clinicians to our updated space\. Enjoy a look inside our clinician and supervision rooms, test library, and social\-emotional learning resource centre and hear from our GAAs about the new features the centre has implemented since its reopening\.

This is a drop\-in event – no registrations are needed\.
