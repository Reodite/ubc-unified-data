#### **[Workshop Flyer](https://prtc1-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/11/Career-Development-and-Mental-Health-Flyer.pdf)**

#### Date: Monday, November 29th, 2021  
Time: 5:00 to 6:00 pm PST  
Platform: UBC Zoom \(the link will be emailed to registered participants\)

The Psychological Services &amp; Counselling Training Centre \(PSCTC\) at the University of British Columbia is excited to host a panel featuring Dr\. Dave E\. Redekopp, Dr\. Michael Huston and Dr\. Lílian Weber on the topic of career development and mental health\. Career development practitioners know intuitively and through client stories that career development practice has an impact on mental health\. The field as a whole, however, has gathered almost no evidence in this regard and has done little to conceptualize the connections between connections between career development and mental health\. The panel will discuss recent efforts to explain these connections and the possible implications for practice\.

This workshop is pending approval from CCPA to qualify for Continuing Education Credits\.

\*All participants will receive a certificate of participation\.
