Find resources related to CIRTL\.

#### General Resources Related to CIRTL:

-   [Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology](http://ctlt.ubc.ca/)
-   [Centre for Teaching and Learning at UBC\-O](https://ctl.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   [UBC Graduate Pathways to Success](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/graduate-pathways-success)
    
-   [UBC Library Research Commons:](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/library_research_commons) support for Graduate Students’ research
    
-   [Tomorrow’s Professor](https://tomprof.stanford.edu/): online professional development from Stanford University
    
-   [Longitudinal Study of Future STEM faculty](https://ceils.ucla.edu/2016/04/24/new-report-building-a-better-future-stem-faculty-how-teaching-development-programs-can-improve-undergraduate-education/)
    

The following are some opportunities for Graduate Students &amp; Postdoctoral Fellows interested in specific areas of professional development that are related to teaching and learning\.

#### Teaching &amp; Learning Deep Dives:

-   [Certificate Program in Advanced Teaching &amp; Learning \(CATL\)](http://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/all-our-programs/graduate-program-in-advanced-teaching-and-learning/) \(postdocs welcomed to apply, but priority is given to graduate students\)
-   [Graduate Student Formative Peer Review of Teaching](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/all-our-programs/graduate-formative-peer-review/peer-review-of-teaching-for-graduate-students/) \(postdocs welcomed to use either the Graduate or [Faculty](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/2019/11/28/peer-review-of-teaching/) Peer Review Programs\)
-   [A\.I\. in Teaching and Learning](https://ai.ctlt.ubc.ca/) \(resources and links to events\)
-   [T\.A\. Handbook](https://wiki.ubc.ca/images/3/3d/Ta-handbook.pdf)

#### Indigenizing Curriculum &amp; Reconciliation:

-   [Indigenous Learning Pathways](https://indigenousinitiatives.ctlt.ubc.ca/programming/ilp/) \(Series of Self\-Paced Canvas Courses\)
-   [Weaving Relations Self\-Paced Canvas Course](https://apsc.ubc.ca/weaving-relations)
-   [Indigenous Design &amp; Engagement in UBC Applied Science and Land &amp; Food Systems \(IDEAL\) Program](https://apsc.ubc.ca/ideal-certificate)
-   [Indigenous Initiatives Design Series](https://indigenousinitiatives.ctlt.ubc.ca/programming/design-lab-series/) \(may need to be an instructor of a course\)
-   [Indigenous Initiatives Virtual Coffee Hours](https://indigenousinitiatives.ctlt.ubc.ca/programming/virtual-coffee-drop-ins/) \(drop in to talk about topics related to teaching and Indigenizing spaces\)

#### Justice, Equity, Diversity, &amp; Inclusion:

-   [Equity, Diversity &amp; Inclusion in Teaching and Learning Self\-Paced Canvas Course](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/31444)
-   [Inclusive Teaching Series](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/event-category/inclusive-teaching/)

#### Open Education Resources \(OER\):

-   [OER Toolkits](https://open.ubc.ca/toolkits/)
-   [Program for Open Scholarship and Education \(POSE\)](https://pose.open.ubc.ca/open-education/oer/?login) \(3 month cohort\-based program exploring Open Access in Academia through open resources, open research, and open education\)

#### Scholarship of Teaching &amp; Learning Resources \(SoTL\):

-   [iSoTL Workshops](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/events-2/) \(a cycle of workshops to support those learning about SoTL\)
-   [iSoTL How\-to Guides](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/resources/how-to/)
-   [SoTL Dissemination Fund Information](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-dissemination-fund/)
-   [iSoTL Scholarly Outputs](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/scholarly-output/) \(see what others at UBC have produced in multiple outputs &amp; submit your own to share\)
-   [UBC’s iSoTL Press](https://ojs.library.ubc.ca/index.php/ISOTL/index)
-   [UBC’s Office of Ethics Behavioural Research](https://researchethics.ubc.ca/about-behavioural-research) \(find out what sort of ethics approval you may need, templates, and individual support\)

#### Conferences &amp; Institutes:

-   [CTLT Institute Series](https://institute.ctlt.ubc.ca/) \(typically held three times a year\)
-   [Graduate Students in Teaching Conference](https://celebratelearning.ubc.ca/) \(open to postdocs as well; website changes every year, but you should be able to find a link to the new registration link on the website linked\)
-   [TA Institute](https://institute.ctlt.ubc.ca/ta-institute/past-ta-institute-events/) \(held in January\)
-   [UBC Celebrate Learning Week](https://celebratelearning.ubc.ca/)

#### General Skill Development:

-   [Facilitation Skills Training](https://wpl.ubc.ca/browse/ifn/programs/wpl-ifn-fst) \(Series of Self\-Paced Canvas Courses\)
-   [Cascades of Change](https://apsc.ubc.ca/cascades-of-change-inclusive-leadership-and-respectful-engagement)
