### **What is TAR?**

TAR \(Teaching\-as\-Research\) is a program that gives graduate students and postdoctoral fellows the opportunity to use their research skills and knowledge of their discipline to help better understand undergraduate education within their discipline\.  Frequently, TAR projects overlap with SoTL \(Scholarship of Teaching and Learning\) projects\.

The TAR program is meant to be a learning opportunity for participants to stretch themselves and gain new knowledge and skills\.  While it is always great when a project runs smoothly and delivers great results, this is **not** an expectation of this program\.  We want to give participants the best possible chance to succeed but also to redefine success from having a perfect project\.  TAR projects are **not necessarily** meant to be at the level of a thesis chapter and frequently are not\.

### What is the time commitment?

We estimate that your project should take an average of 2\-3 hours per week over the course of a semester\.  This can be rearranged, happening over a year, over two semesters, etc\.  It is up to you to manage your time\.

### What are the different ways I can participate in TAR?

Everyone who wants to participate in TAR must fill out the application form that is available on the website under the Graduate Student Award tab \(even if you are not applying for the award\)\.  Anyone can apply, including postdocs and graduate students who are not eligible for the award\. Without the award, you will still be considered a TAR Intern and still have access to all the resources that any Awardee may have\.  The only difference is that Awardees are graduate students that will be entering at least the second year of their program and who successfully compete for one of the awards\.  The award is typically $3000\.

### What happens after the application and the pre\-TAR workshops?

After the workshops and the application deadline, all applications will be reviewed by a panel\.  Each application will be assigned one main reviewer who will have no conflicts of interest with the applicant\.  Any conflicts of interest will be noted in the adjudication process for the award\.  All applications will receive feedback on their package often with some advised revisions\.  For some potential awardees, you will be given a conditional award should you make the revisions suggested and satisfy the panel’s concerns\.  The applications are viewed for their specificity, potential contribution to improving education, feasibility, and the likelihood of receiving ethics approval\.

Following the adjudication period, applicants will be notified of the review of their application\.  They will be given time to make those changes and/or meet with those reviewing their package or the CIRTL Program Developer for further discussion\.

Throughout the next year, the CIRTL Program Developer will organize workshops, approximately once every month\.  These workshops will address common areas of interest or problem areas for participants to help them have a successful project\.  Additionally, you are welcome to have one\-on\-one consults with the CIRTL Program Developer and reach out for particular resources you may be struggling to access\.

The CIRTL Program Developer will also reach out to you throughout the year with opportunities related to the work you are doing \(i\.e\. potential interesting talks, opportunities to present\)\.

### What am I responsible for?

Beyond working on your project, you are also responsible for completing the ethics application and providing the CIRTL Program Developer with evidence that your project has been approved and completing both a midterm and final report\.

### Will you notify my advisor?

No, not unless you would like us to\.  We do encourage as much as possible being open with your supervisor, but we realize this is not possible for everyone\.  We hope that for the Awardees, that winning an award will help validate the time you will spend on this project\.

### What do you I get for participating in this program?

We hope that there are a number of benefits for our participants, both formal and informal\.  We hope to give you support in developing or rethinking skills that can help improve your teaching and potentially also your academic research\.  We believe the program will offer you opportunities to improve your knowledge around accessing previous research, different methods of research, and statistics\. The program offers you a chance to start to build a learning community around education\.  You will be able to meet and interact with your peers in our workshops, meet experts who will lead some of the workshops, and gain mentorship from those who you will be working with during your project\.

We also strongly encourage that you share your findings and/or experience\.  This could be in a poster, giving a talk, or publishing a paper\. We try to connect you with opportunities to share your findings\.

As part of CIRTL programming, completing this program will give you the qualifications for CIRTL Practitioner status\.  If you publish or present your results, you are eligible for CIRTL Scholar status\.
