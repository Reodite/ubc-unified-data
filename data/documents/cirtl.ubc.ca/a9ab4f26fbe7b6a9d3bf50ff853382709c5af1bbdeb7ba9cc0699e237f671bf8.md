A CIRTL Scholar, has added knowledge to the teaching and learning community\. At UBC, you can become a CIRTL Scholar through either of these options:

1.  Presentation of your Teaching as Research \(TAR\), Scholarship of Teaching &amp; Learning, Disciplined\-based Educational Research, or [Certificate Program in Advanced Teaching and Learning](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/all-our-programs/graduate-program-in-advanced-teaching-and-learning/) \(CATL\) project at all\-university, regional, national or international conferences
2.  Peer\-reviewed publication of your project

Once you have presented/published your  findings, you can fill out the [form to get your CIRTL Scholar status](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3k3G25XVGUbV3hk)\.
