### Open Databases

Xwi7xwa regrets that the [Indigenous Community Borrower Card](http://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/library-cards/unaffiliated/#AboriginalCommunity-1) does NOT provide access to most of the Library’s licensed database resources\. The Library maintains a [list](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/az/databases?am=65) of all databases that are openly accessible\. This list is multidisciplinary\.

[Green open lock icon, used to indicate Open Access databases in the UBC Library's indexes and databases list\.](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/files/2017/03/open-access.png)

#### Museum of Anthropology \(MOA\)

[Museum of Anthropology Logo](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/03/MOA-logoWithTag_GreenTea-300x155.jpg)For a complete [guide](http://moa.ubc.ca/collections/) to the following resources, please visit the MOA website:

-   [MOACAT:](http://collection-online.moa.ubc.ca/home) MOA’s online object catalogue, where the majority of its 40,000 collection records are available for browsing and searching\.

-   [Reciprocal Research Network](https://www.rrncommunity.org/): an online research environment that provides access to First Nations items from the Northwest Coast and British Columbia\. It allows you to search through items from MOA as well as many other institutions\.
