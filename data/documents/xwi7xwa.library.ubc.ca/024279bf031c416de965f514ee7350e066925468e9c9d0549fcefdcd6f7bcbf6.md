We are re\-opening participant applications for the Rebuilding Relations Gathering until **September 25, 2026**\. We have space to invite up to ten additional participants\. This round of applications is open to Indigenous students and community members from all areas of practice, study and knowledge levels\. Artists, writers and storytellers are especially encouraged to apply\.

Rebuilding Relations is a novel, art and storytelling\-based gathering that invites Indigenous participants \(students, staff, faculty, and community members\) to learn about the concepts of Indigenous data sovereignty and governance and rebuild relationships with Indigenous data housed at X̱wi7x̱wa Library\.

The Gathering is ideal for participants who are curious about Indigenous data sovereignty and willing to contribute their time and energy to create a supportive learning environment\.

The format is conversational and participant\-driven\. The gathering will take place within a talking circle, led by Jessie Hemphill, a Kwakwaka’wakw and Métis Facilitator\. The focus will be on discussion, creation and shared experience within the circle rather than on formal presentations\.

The Gathering will also include the co\-creation of artwork and stories, developed through participants’ contributions over the course of the day\.

Rebuilding Relations Gathering: Saturday, 17 October 2026

■ **When:** 9:30 AM – 5:00 PM \(lunch and tea/coffee will be provided by [Potluck Catering](https://www.potluckcatering.org/menu)\)\.

■ **Where:** First Nations House of Learning, Longhouse on UBC Vancouver Campus\.

■ **What:** Jessie Hemphill of [Sanala Planning](https://www.sanalaplanning.com/jessie-hemphill) will facilitate discussions and collaborative art creation supported by [Manny Strain](https://www.manuelaxelstrain.com/)\.

■ A modest monetary gift of appreciation will be offered for participants time\.

■ [More info and participant application here](https://ubc.yul1.qualtrics.com/jfe3/preview/previewId/e6fcb6c4-c136-4ea5-9e86-ba33f787a2bd/SV_ekRC5C0ZWtcj9oq?Q_CHL=preview&amp;Q_SurveyVersionID=current)\!

The Gathering is guided by Dr\. Warren Cardinal\-McTeague \(Assistant Professor, Department of Forest and Conservation Sciences\), Kayla Lar\-Son \(Head of X̱wi7x̱wa Library\) and Daphne Boyer \(Visual artist and plant scientist, [https://www\.daphneboyer\.com/](https://www.daphneboyer.com/)\)\.
