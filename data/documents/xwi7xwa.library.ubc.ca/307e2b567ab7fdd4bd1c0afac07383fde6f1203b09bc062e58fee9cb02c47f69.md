[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/04/Distance-Research-Libguide-.png)Our [Distance Research Guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/c.php?g=715538) is intended for UBC students, faculty, staff, and researchers who have been impacted by the recent changes in services due to global health concerns\. It has updates, information on getting your devices connected to UBC services, and tips and tricks for researching Indigenous topics remotely\.

[View our other research guides here](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/xwi7xwaresearchguide)\.

Our staff are working from home and are available through email at *xwi7xwa\.library@ubc\.ca to:*

-   answer research questions
-   find online materials for papers &amp; research topics
-   find alternative materials to items only available in print
-   cancel late fees from March 16 until the situation changes
