[Poster with large balloons of the number 30\. Text reads "Join us for an open house celebrating 30 years of Xwi7xwa Library\. May 25th, 12\-3pm\. All are welcome\. Cake will be provided\.](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/05/1-1-300x169.png)

On Thursday, May 25th, 2023, we are celebrating 30 years of Xwi7xw*a* Library\. Come join us for an open house complete with cake and activities\. All are welcome from 12PM – 3PM at the branch\!

**Address:** 1985 West Mall, Vancouver\.

**When:** Drop by anytime between 12pm – 3pm on Thursday May 25th\.

Make sure to check out the [‘Celebrating 30 Years’](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/celebrating_30_years/) online exhibit to learn more about the [history of the branch](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/x%CC%B1wi7x%CC%B1wa/) and the First Nations House of Learning\.

**Curious about Indigenous Librarianship?**

Take a look through our [Indigenous Librarianship LibGuide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/Indiglibrarianship) for resources on Indigenous Libraries, Indigenous Knowledge Organization, and more\.
