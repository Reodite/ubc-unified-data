## xʷməθkʷəy̓əm \(Musqueam\) &amp; UBC

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/11/digitalsignage-libguides_xwi7xwa_Oct2021_Slide1_DigitalSignage-300x169.jpg)

The University of British Columbia is located on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓ speaking xʷməθkʷəy̓əm \(Musqueam\) people\. This research guide provides resources and research strategies for deepening knowledge of this relationship and of Musqueam\.

## [Using Xwi7xwa Library’s Research Guides](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/2021/01/04/using-xwi7xwa-librarys-research-guides/)

By [Karleen Delaurier\-Lyle](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/author/karleen-delaurier-lyle/ "Posts by Karleen Delaurier-Lyle") on January 4, 2021

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/LibGuides-Presentation-CTLT-1-300x225.jpg)

[Register here](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/using-xwi7xwa-librarys-research-guides-january-19-2021/)\! Join us January 19, 2021 from 2:30 pm – 4:00 pm

[Xwi7xwa Library’s Research Guides](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/xwi7xwaresearchguide "https://guides.library.ubc.ca/xwi7xwaresearchguide")  are a collection of resources gathered to support common questions we receive from patrons\. Patterned patron curiosity informs what topics Xwi7xwa’s guides cover but they must also speak to an individual’s relationship to the topic, therefore the scope of perspectives the guides connect to are broad\. One of the main goals of our guides are to place Indigenous knowledges, perspectives, and pedagogy at the forefront of beginning to answer researcher curiosity\. This session will cover what research guides are, why specific guides have been created, and how we think responsibly about the information we gather and give\.
