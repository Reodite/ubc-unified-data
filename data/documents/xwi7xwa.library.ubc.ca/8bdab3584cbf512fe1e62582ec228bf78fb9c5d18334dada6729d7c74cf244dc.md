X̱wi7x̱wa Library is celebrating Pride Month by sharing resources from our collection and online\!

1.  Check out our [Two\-Spirit &amp; Indigiqueer Research Guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/twospiritandindigenousqueerstudies)\!
2.  Check out the Smithsonian Learning Lab’s *Conversations: Queer Inuit Art*\.
    -   Alice Qannak Glenn, host of the [Coffee &amp; Quaq](https://www.coffeeandquaq.com/) podcast, is joined by [Jenny Irene Miller](https://www.jennyirenemiller.com/) and [Ossie Michelin](http://osmich.ca/about/) for a conversation on queer art, providing Inuit perspectives\.
    -   \[youtube\]https://youtu\.be/\_SlEIkTCJ8M\[/youtube\]
3.  Daniel Heath Justice UBC Professor &amp; Canada Research Chair in Indigenous Literature and Expressive Culture at UBC\. Colorado\-born Canadian citizen of the Cherokee Nation/ ᏣᎳᎩᎯ ᎠᏰᎵ\.
    -   Titles from Heath Justice at UBC: [https://webcat\.library\.ubc\.ca/vwebv/search?searchType\=7&amp;searchId\=26410&amp;maxResultsPerPage\=10&amp;recCount\=10&amp;recPointer\=0&amp;resultPointer\=0&amp;headingId\=7817820](https://webcat.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/search?searchType=7&amp;searchId=26410&amp;maxResultsPerPage=10&amp;recCount=10&amp;recPointer=0&amp;resultPointer=0&amp;headingId=7817820) \\ 
4.  Brandi Bird is Cree, Saulteaux and Métis and grew up on Territory 1 territory in Winnipeg, Manitoba\.
    -   X̱wi7x̱wa does not yet have a copy of Bird’s publication *I Am Still Too Much* however it can be purchased [here](https://rahilasghostpress.com/portfolio/i-am-still-too-much-by-brandi-bird/)\. UBC Library has an e\-copy of [Best Canadian Poetry 2020](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=11461126) with Bird’s work\. 
    -   Room Podcast on Indigenous Brilliance with Brandi Bird &amp; Billy\-Ray Belcourt: [https://roommagazine\.com/the\-indigenous\-brilliance\-podcast\-episode\-2\-april\-16\-2021\-poetry\-with\-brandi\-bird\-and\-billy\-ray\-belcourt/](https://roommagazine.com/the-indigenous-brilliance-podcast-episode-2-april-16-2021-poetry-with-brandi-bird-and-billy-ray-belcourt/)  
5.  T’áncháy Redvers \(findable in libraries as: Tunchai Redvers\) is a 2S indigiqueer, social justice warrior, writer, speaker, creator, performer, &amp; actor\.
