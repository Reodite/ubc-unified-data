The list of courses with Indigenous content is now available\!

According to the 2019 University of British Columbia Course Calendar and departmental course descriptions, there are 114 courses, from 33 different departments, that have a significant amount of Indigenous content being offered for the Summer 2019 session\.

To download the course list click [here](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/06/UBC-Courses-with-Indigenous-Content-Winter-2019-20_KD.pdf)\.

Xwi7xwa does not endorse the courses listed\. Courses are added based on descriptions only\. Anyone wishing to provide feedback on course content should refer to these confidential resources:

1.  [Ombuds Person for Students](https://ombudsoffice.ubc.ca/) \(if you’re not satisfied with the quality of instruction in a course, the Ombuds Office will help you contact the head of the department the course is offered in\)
2.  [Equity &amp; Inclusion Offices’s Conflict Engagement](https://equity.ubc.ca/how-we-can-help/conflict-engagement/)
3.  [Aboriginal Portal’s Student Life resource page](http://aboriginal.ubc.ca/students/student-life/)
