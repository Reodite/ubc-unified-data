Visit us for research help, to see our  collections, or to find a place to study\. At X̱wi7x̱wa Library everyone is welcome\!

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/1200x600_library3-300x150.jpg)

##### Image credit: Paul Joseph

X̱wi7x̱wa Library \([pronunciation](https://namedrop.io/xwi7xwalibrary)\) is a centre for academic and community Indigenous scholarship\. Our collections and services reflect Indigenous approaches to teaching, learning, and research\. To learn more about the branch see our “About” section [here](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/about/), as well as this article from [UBC Today](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/stories/inside-xwi7xwa-library-canadas-only-indigenous-branch-academic-library)\.
