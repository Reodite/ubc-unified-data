[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/12/December-Storytelling-Spotlight-p2.jpg)

This has been an incredible year for Indigenous story\-tellers, including the film adaptation of Monkey Beach, which swept the [American Indian Film Festival](https://www.aifisf.com/2020-winners?fbclid=IwAR236Kw3yCgr2GhksySwQrnEk2aIQLUHXLSJXibYXgqq21Dle06Tv9rV0Fs) earlier this autumn and was an official selection at both the Vancouver and Toronto International Film Festivals\. Though Monkey Beach is not yet available at home, we’d like to highlight a number of story\-tellers whose work is available to stream\. Even as we stay at home this December, we can still share and be inspired by these stories\.

*Film and Television*

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/12/Trickster.jpg)

[Trickster](https://gem.cbc.ca/season/trickster/season-1/dcdc337f-fe75-4001-8c4d-93ededf90d6c?cmp=DM_SEM_TRICKSTER)

Another captivating story adapted from the works of Eden Robinson\. This supernatural drama series is available to stream through CBC Gem\.

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/12/lesser-blessed.jpg)

[Lesser Blessed](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=9410571)

Based on the novel by Richard Van Camp, this beautiful coming\-of\-age story is available to stream through the UBC Library\.  Before you get started, please refer to our guide to [streaming media through UBC](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/streamingmedia)\.

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/12/Amanda-Strong.jpg)

[Short Films by Amanda Strong](http://webcat2.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/search?searchArg=%22amanda%20strong%22%20ONLINE&amp;searchCode=GKEY%5e*&amp;setLimit=22&amp;searchType=0&amp;recCount=100&amp;setLimit=15&amp;filter=Y)

Amanda Strong is an Indigenous filmmaker based out of the unceded Coast Salish territory in Vancouver, British Columbia\. A collection of Amanda Strong’s mesmerizing short films are available to stream through our library\. See behind the scenes of her stop\-motion animation process [here](https://youtu.be/hoWM2AXL98U)\.

*Live Performances and Recordings*

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/12/Tanya-Tagaq.jpg)

[Tanya Tagaq](http://tanyatagaq.com/visuals/)

Watch performances and talks by Inuit author and throat singer Tanya Tagaq\. Her remarkable first novel, [Split Tooth](http://resolve.library.ubc.ca/cgi-bin/catsearch?bid=9316020), is also available through X̱wi7x̱wa\.

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/12/Highway.jpg)

[Tomson Highway](https://youtu.be/FlYte8tGEXw)

This recording features legendary storyteller, playwright, and musician Tomson Highway perform a selection of his songs on piano, along with commentary and conversation\.
