As the summer weather greets us, there’s no better time to read some comics\! X̱wi7x̱wa Library has a graphic novel section with plenty of Indigenous comics to enjoy\. We have standalone stories, anthologies collecting short stories, and ongoing series in a variety of genres\. From cautionary tales to non\-fiction memoirs, check out these new stories in the collection\!

***The Woman in the Woods*, edited by Kel McDonald, Kate Ashwin, and Alina Pete**

Call Number: [YUB M33 W66 2022](https://webcat.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/search?searchArg=%20YUB%20M33%20W66%202022&amp;searchCode=CALL%2B%252B&amp;searchType=1)

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/woman-in-the-woods-200x300.jpg)

This collection of cautionary tales delights the reader with beautiful black, white, and greyscale art and gripping stores\. Containing 8 thrilling, chilling, and inspiring tales, this anthology will acquaint you with Loup Garou, trickster rabbits, and spirits with names that can’t be spoken\! A great read for young adults and up\.

***The Rez Doctor*, written by Gitz Crazyboy and illustrated by Veronika Barinova, Azby Whitecalf, and Toben Racicot**

Call Number: None yet – ask a staff member if you’re interested in checking out this resource\!

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/the-rez-doctor-195x300.jpg)

This is an inspiring graphic novel for young adults about a boy’s dream to become a doctor when he grows up\. Ryan Fox, the boy in question, faces isolation when he goes to university and turns to partying to deal with his stress\. With slipping grades and mounting bills, can he regain his footing and keep walking the path toward his dreams?

***A Girl Called Echo Omnibus*, written by Katherena Vermette and illustrated by Scott B\. Henderson and Donovan Yaciuk**

Call Number: [YUC V47 G57 2023](https://webcat.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/search?searchArg=%20YUC%20V47%20G57%202023&amp;searchCode=CALL%2B%252B&amp;searchType=1)

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/a-girl-called-echo-omnibus-by-katherena-vermette-illustrated-by-scott-b-henderson-195x300.avif)

Echo Desjardins is a teenager adjusting to a new school and a new home\. When she is transported back in time to the prairies of 1814, she experiences pivotal events in Métis history up close and personal\. As she learns about her people’s struggles and resistance, she reconnects with her heritage\. The omnibus collects the four volumes of *Pemmican Wars*, *Red River Resistance*, *Northwest Resistance*, and *Road Allowance Era* in one book\.

***A Howl: An Indigenous Anthology of Wolves, Werewolves &amp; Rougarou,* edited by Elizabeth LaPensée**

Call Number: [YR L37 H69 2021](https://webcat.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/search?searchArg=%20YR%20L37%20H69%202021&amp;searchCode=CALL%2B%252B&amp;searchType=1)

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/a-howl-197x300.jpg)

With over 20 tales collected in one volume, *A Howl* is a mature graphic novel anthology with werewolf depictions ranging from wholesome creatures to unbridled terrors\. The art styles are beautiful and varied and each story offers something new\. At the end of the book are over 10 gallery images using different mediums, from beading to formline to digital art\. Funded on Kickstarter, this anthology can no longer be purchased, so getting it from X̱wi7x̱wa Library is an easy way to read it\!

***Redbone: The True Story of a Native American Rock Band*, written by Christian Staebler and Sonia Paoloni and illustrated by Thibault Balahy**

Call Number: [CU S73 R43 2020](https://webcat.library.ubc.ca/vwebv/search?searchArg=%20CU%20S73%20R43%202020&amp;searchCode=CALL%2B%252B&amp;searchType=1)

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/redbone-214x300.jpg)

Rock ‘n’ roll pioneers Redbone take the spotlight in this well\-researched graphic novel based on the personal and professional experiences of Pat Vegas, founding member of the band\. The art is energetic and the inventive page layouts immerse the reader into the history of the band and the Native American civil rights movement\. A great non\-fiction read\!
