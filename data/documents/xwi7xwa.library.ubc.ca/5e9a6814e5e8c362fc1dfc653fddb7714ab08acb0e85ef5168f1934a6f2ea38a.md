### Out ‘n  About at UBC Farm – August 25

Today librarian Karleen was out ‘n about at the UBC Farm\!

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/Selfie-300x300.jpg) [Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/Carrot-225x300.jpg)

“The UBC Farm is the Centre for Sustainable Food System’s main teaching and learning space\. A 24\-hectare integrated production farm, the UBC Farm is located on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the [Musqueam people](https://www.musqueam.bc.ca/)\. Situated within a 90\-year\-old coastal hemlock forest, the UBC Farm comprises cultivated annual crop fields, perennial hedgerows and orchards, pasture, teaching gardens, and forest stands\.” – [UBC Farm](https://ubcfarm.ubc.ca/about/)

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/Garden-300x225.jpg) [Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/Greenhouse-300x225.jpg)

Due to COVID many events and volunteer opportunities have been postponed but you can still [register to be a volunteer](https://ubcfarm.ubc.ca/volunteer-program/?login)\!

Karleen’s tips to get gardening:

-   [Join a local garden in Vancouver](https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/community-gardens.aspx) 
-   [Check out the seed lending branches of Vancouver Public Library](https://www.vpl.ca/collection/seed-library) 
-   [If you don’t want to garden try out a nature walk](https://naturevancouver.ca/category/nature-walk/)
-   Stay hydrated and bring snacks
-   Be [weather ready](https://www.theweathernetwork.com/ca/weather/british-columbia/vancouver) and wear a hat
