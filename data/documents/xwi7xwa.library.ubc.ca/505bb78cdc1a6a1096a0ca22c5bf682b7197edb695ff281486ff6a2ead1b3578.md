We are pleased to release our newest research guide: [Decolonization &amp; Racism Research Guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/antiracism/decolonization_anti-racism)\.

This guide was created to assist folks interested in decolonial &amp; anti\-racist research, projects, class assignments, and course work\. With the 2020 launch of [UBC’s Indigenous Strategic Plan \(ISP\)](https://aboriginal-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/UBC.ISP_C2V13.1_Spreads_Sept1.pdf) we realize many people will be interested in how they can incorporate the ISP at every level\.

Xwi7xwa Library would like to thank the [CTLT Indigenous Initiatives team](https://indigenousinitiatives.ctlt.ubc.ca/?login), Alex Ash &amp; Vicki Lynne George \(Office of Indigenous Strategic Initiatives\), &amp; [Sajni Lacey](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/sajni-lacey/) \(UBCO Learning and Curriculum Support Librarian\) for their time, energy, advice, care, and contributions in this guide\.
