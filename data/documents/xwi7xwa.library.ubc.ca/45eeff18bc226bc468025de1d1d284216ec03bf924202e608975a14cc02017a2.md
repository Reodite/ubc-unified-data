### Out ‘n About with Kent Monkman – August 21

This Friday our librarian Karleen was out ‘n about with Kent Monkman’s Shame and Prejudice: A Story of Resilience\!

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/Selfie_1-300x225.jpg) [Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/Selfie_2-225x300.jpg)

“This exhibition features nearly 80 pieces, including Monkman’s own paintings, installations and sculptures, in dialogue with historical artifacts loaned from museums and private collections across Canada\. MOA is the final stop for this critically acclaimed travelling exhibition, which has been on a multi\-year, cross\-country tour to nine cities\.” –[MOA](https://moa.ubc.ca/exhibition/shame-and-prejudice/)

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/MacDonald-300x225.jpg) [Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/08/MissChief-300x225.jpg)

Karleen’s advice for this activity:

-   Plan ahead, they’re only letting a small number of people into MOA at a time so [book your ticket](https://tickets.ubc.ca/Online/default.asp?doWork::WScontent::loadArticle=Load&amp;BOparam::WScontent::loadArticle::article_id=258DD177-FEF1-44D4-96F7-006BBA82FDD8)
-   If you can’t make it to MOA check out the [exhibit online](https://moa.ubc.ca/exhibition/shame-and-prejudice/)
-   Make sure you snack beforehand because you can’t bring food in
-   Stay safe, check out the [safety procedures](https://moa.ubc.ca/moa-re-openingnew-procedures-for-a-safe-visit/) ahead of time
