### Driving and Parking Directions

[https://goo\.gl/maps/oy4s1rn9Pxq](https://goo.gl/maps/oy4s1rn9Pxq)[Image](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/files/2017/02/Google-Image.jpg)

X̱wi7x̱wa Library is at the corner of West Mall and Agricultural Road at **1985 West Mall**\. The nearest parkade is the [Fraser River Parkade](http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?show=y,n,n,n,n,y&amp;bldg2Search=n&amp;locat1=052) at 6440 Memorial Rd\. If you have any questions please call [UBC Parking](https://parking.ubc.ca/) at 604\-822\-6786 or email [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca)\. Parking is by plate number and the lots are patrolled regularly\.

**Note:** Place Vanier parking is for permit holders only\. **DO NOT** park here, as UBC will tow cars\.

### Accessibility

[Image](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/Screenshot-2024-04-24-at-10.21.11-AM-300x240.png)

Wheelchair accessible pick\-up and drop\-off can be found on Lower Mall next to the Longhouse\. X̱wi7x̱wa can be accessed using the pathways alongside the Longhouse\. All parkades and many surface lots have designated spaces for people with mobility issues\. Please note that to use disabled parking you require a [SPARC parking permit](https://www.sparc.bc.ca/parking-permits/)\. For current accessible and walking routes see [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/1985+West+Mall,+Vancouver,+BC+V6T+1Z2,+Canada/UBC+Bus+Loop,+University+Endowment+Lands,+BC/@49.2666789,-123.2549812,617m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m14!4m13!1m5!1m1!1s0x548672b45adbc9a5:0x5d05da4c2046f23b!2m2!1d-123.2573817!2d49.2655666!1m5!1m1!1s0x548672b787aa42fd:0x38e3cff4a7d52c2d!2m2!1d-123.2477071!2d49.2678531!3e2?entry=ttu&amp;g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDUwMi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)\.

### Public transit

Local transportation routes, fair, etc\. are available at [TransLink](https://www.translink.ca/)\.

The UBC bus loops are about a 10 minute walk from X̱wi7x̱wa Library\. Either use the stairs to X̱wi7x̱wa or use the ramp to the left of the library and circle back along the sidewalk between the Longhouse and the library\. From the bus loop you will find our roof \(pictured left\)\. Use the wheelchair ramp and enter the branch using the walkway that connect the Longhouse and Library \(pictured right\)\.
