## xʷməθkʷəy̓əm  
FNHL Elder in Residence and Adjunct Professor of First Nations and Endangered Languages Program

### [Larry Grant](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/04/Larry-Grant-Elder.jpg)Bio

Adjunct Professor, First Nations and Endangered Languages Program  
Mr\. Grant is an Elder from the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm \(Musqueam\) First Nation in Vancouver, BC\. He is an Adjunct Professor in the University of British Columbia First Nations Languages Program, the Language and Culture Consultant for Musqueam First Nation\.

More about Mr\. Grant and his work at UBC – [https://fnel\.arts\.ubc\.ca/](https://fnel.arts.ubc.ca/)
