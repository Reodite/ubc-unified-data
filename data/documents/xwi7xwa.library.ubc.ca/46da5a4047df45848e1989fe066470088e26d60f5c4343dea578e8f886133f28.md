Our services will be closed on September 30th to honour \#OrangeShirtDay\. On this day we will be supporting our families, friends, communities, and one another\.

Our team will be participating in the Intergenerational March to Commemorate Orange Shirt Day from 11:45 AM – 2:00 PM; we encourage you to join us\. See: [https://bit\.ly/3kyn9dr](https://bit.ly/3kyn9dr)

We will also be visiting the Generations Lost: Healing the Legacy of Residential School exhibit in Irving K\. Barber Library: [https://bit\.ly/2ZwXedZ](https://bit.ly/2ZwXedZ)
