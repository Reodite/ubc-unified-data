UBC Library presents, in partnership with [UBC Forestry](https://forestry.ubc.ca/) and the [Simon K\. Y\. Lee Global Lounge and Resource Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/simon-k-y-lee-global-lounge-resource-centre), a conversation with Dr\. Robin Wall Kimmerer on Friday, January 29 \(1:00 \-2:30 p\.m\. PST\)\. The acclaimed author of [*Braiding Sweetgrass: Indigenous Wisdom, Scientific Knowledge and the Teachings of Plants*](https://milkweed.org/book/braiding-sweetgrass) will be joined by moderators, Dr\. Daniel Heath Justice and Corrina Sparrow to discuss the author’s influence on multidisciplinary understandings of her work and how readers can integrate this into our connections with land and each other through our respective disciplinary lenses\.

**Date and time:** Friday, January 29 \(1:00 \-2:30 p\.m\. PST\)

**Location:** Online

**Forest \(Reads\) Initiative Course:**

Forest\(Reads\) is a joint initiative between the Faculty of Forestry’s Equity, Diversity and Inclusion Council and the Associate Dean, Academic of the Faculty\. The Initiative has a canvas course that anyone with a UBC affiliation can register in, which can be accessed here \([https://canvas\.ubc\.ca/courses/69762](https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/69762)\)\.

The course allows participants access to discussion boards as well as access links for our zoom conversations\. Participants in the course will be entered in a book draw in early February for a few copies of Braiding Sweetgrass\!

For UBC e\-book copies &amp; background bios see [here](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/c.php?g=717718&amp;p=5135171)\.
