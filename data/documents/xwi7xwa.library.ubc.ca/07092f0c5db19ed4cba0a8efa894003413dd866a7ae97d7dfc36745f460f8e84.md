The [Indigenous Music &amp; Dance Research Guide](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/indigenousmusicanddance) provides strategies and information for researchers searching for Indigenous artists, dance and music performances\. It provides resources like books to begin research from, search terms, music and dance videos, current news, ways to find Indigenous artists on websites like Youtube, and a list of local BC artists to add to your music playlists\.

The [Browse by Genre page](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/indigenousmusicanddance/browsebygenre) has a short list of Indigenous artists sorted into different genres\. If you know of an artist that should be on our list, email us and we’ll add it\!

Happy listening\!
