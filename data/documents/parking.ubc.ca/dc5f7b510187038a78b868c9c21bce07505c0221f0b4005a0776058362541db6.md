### How do I get my 25 Years Club Retired Parking Permit?

You may present the letter acknowledging your 25 years retirement status sent by the President of the 25 Years Club to the Parking Office or e\-mail us at [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca)\. Be sure to include your valid vehicle insurance documents displaying you as a registered driver so a permit can be issued\.

### I have an Emeritus/25 Years Club Parking Permit\. How do I access and manage my UBC Parking account?

You may reach Parking Services at [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) to update your vehicle or personal information on your parking account\. Remember to send the current vehicle insurance information displaying your name as one of the drivers when updating vehicle information\.

### Where does my Emeritus/25 Years Club Permit enable me to park?

Your permit will provide you with the same parking privileges as a Faculty/Staff Permit\.  Any multi\-level parkades and surface lots that displays signs indicating Faculty/Staff Permits are valid for your permit\.

### Are there cameras in the parkades?

 Yes, for your protection and safety, cameras are in use at parkades across campus\. For more details visit [https://universitycounsel\.ubc\.ca/safety\-and\-security\-cameras](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/safety-and-security-cameras)\.

### Why the EV charging stations across campus have signs limiting how much time a vehicle can use them per day?

This is done so we can enable more vehicles to use them by having a higher turnover\. Vehicles take an average of 2\.5hrs to charge, so allowing them to stay much longer than that would prevent other vehicles from charging as well\.

### Can you use a 4 hour maximum EV charging station for 2 hours, leave and come back to the same or a different EV charging station for 2 more hours for a total of 4 within the same day?

Yes\. The time limit posted on the EV stations is per day and per location so you can use EV stations at more than one location on the same day as long as you valid payment or permit\.
