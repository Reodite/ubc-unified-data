Events
UBC Parking Services works closely with several campus affiliates and non-affiliates to coordinate traffic
and parking logistics for events that occur on campus. Our Traffic Control and Parking staff ensure that
adequate parking is available for guests and that impacts to the campus community are minimized.




Hosting an event at a parking lot
Should you be planning to host an event on the actual parking lot or require the space for an activity
other than parking vehicles, please a complete an Outdoor Booking Request Form available at
http://planning.ubc.ca/vancouver/news-events/outdoor-event-booking/outdoor-booking-request-form


Event Parking Request Form
Submit this form to UBC Parking when:

    •   A specific area needs to be reserved to accommodate parking for an event.
    •   Ten or more vehicles are expected on campus.
    •   Oversized vehicles such as trailer, bus or high cube delivery truck need to be parked in
        areas of campus not designed to accommodate them.
    •   Your event may require additional access for guests with disabilities.
