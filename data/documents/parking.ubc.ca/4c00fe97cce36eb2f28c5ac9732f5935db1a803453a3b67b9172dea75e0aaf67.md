UBC offers 9 free bike cages and 200 secure bike parking spaces in bike lockers located across campus\. Additionally, UBC’s Vancouver campus has over 9,000 high\-quality outdoor bike parking spaces\.

You'll need to login to Parking's payment portal to make a selection\. 

[Sign in](https://ubcparking.t2hosted.ca/cmn/auth_ext.aspx)

All information about the bike cage and bike locker program, including rates, locations and how to gain access, can be found on the Campus and Community Planning site\.

[Bike Cage Information](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/cycling/bicycle-parking)
