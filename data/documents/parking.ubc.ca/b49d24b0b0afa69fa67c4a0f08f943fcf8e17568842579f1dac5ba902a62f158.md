### Do I need to display my parking receipt or permit on the dashboard of my vehicle?

No, it is not necessary to display a receipt or permit on the dashboard\. The license plate associated with the payment is reflected in our computer system\.

### Is my permit valid on the date it expires?

Daily, monthly, yearly and term permits are valid until 11:59pm of the day they expire\.

### How do I know when my permit expires?

You can view permit expiry dates in your Parking Account under “view my permits”\.

### Is there tax on parking permits and rates?

Hourly rates available at Honk and Pay Stations include all applicable taxes in the displayed price, while parking permits have taxes added at checkout\.

Effective August 1, 2025, the Metro Vancouver Parking Tax will increase from 24% to 29%\. As a result, the total tax on parking will rise from 30\.2% to 35\.45%\. This total includes the 5% GST applied to both the parking fee and to the Metro Vancouver Parking Tax itself\.

Visit Translink for further info: [https://www\.translink\.ca/about\-us/about\-translink/taxes\-and\-charges](https://www.translink.ca/about-us/about-translink/taxes-and-charges) 

### Is there short\-term parking on campus?

There are designated 15 and 30 minutes parking locations throughout campus\. Parking at these locations can be paid using the pay machines, [Honk,](http://www.honkmobile.com/#) [PayByPhone](https://m.paybyphone.com/#/onboardingCountrySelector), and [ZipBy](https://zipby.app/)\. Find short term parking using the [Parking Map](http://p2.dp7prod.webi.it.ubc.ca/map)\.

### How do I pay for parking?

Daily and long\-term parking permits can be purchased online through your Parking Account\.

Hourly parking can be purchased through [Honk,](http://www.honkmobile.com/#) [PayByPhone](https://www.paybyphone.com/), [ZipBy](https://zipby.app/) or at pay machines and meters with cash and credit cards\.
