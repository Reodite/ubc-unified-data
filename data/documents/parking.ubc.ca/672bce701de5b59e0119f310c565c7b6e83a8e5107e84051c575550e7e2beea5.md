### What are the Vancouver Campus Parking Permits: Terms &amp; Conditions

The Vancouver Campus Parkign Permits: Terms &amp; Conditions can be found [here](https://parking.ubc.ca/tickets-and-towed-vehicles)

### I am a resident student but I don\`t see the location that I would like to park in as an option at the Permit Selection page in my UBC Parking Account\.

UBC resident student parking options are limited and subject to availability\. The options currently visible in your UBC Parking account reflect what is available for purchase at this time\.

For additional parking options through UBC Housing, please visit: [http://vancouver\.housing\.ubc\.ca/applications/fees\-payments/parking/](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/fees-payments/parking/)

Please note that we strongly recommend using all four of your waitlist selections\. Parking areas near or within residence zones are often at capacity and have very limited turnover\. You may wish to include locations such as Fairview Underground or Thunderbird Parkade as your lowest\-ranked waitlist choices\. These can serve as temporary parking options while you wait for availability in your higher\-preference locations\.

### Why I can no longer purchase a parking permit for all the 5 parkades?

To better serve the campus community by properly distributing the occupancy among the parking facilities, we have limited the Community Permits to a one parkade\.

### Where should I park if my vehicle requires over height parking?

Please email the purpose of your trip, dates and the location you are attending at UBC to [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) so we can assist you on finding a parking location that can accommodate your vehicle\.

### How do I get my Emeritus Parking Permit?

Once you received the letter granting you Emeritus status from the Senate, you can e\-mail it to [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) along with your vehicle insurance displaying your name as a driver so we can issue you an Emeritus Parking Permit\.

### How long is the Emeritus Parking Permit valid for?

The Emeritus Parking Permit runs from April 1st to end of March\. To renew your permit e\-mail [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) along with your current vehicle insurance document displaying your name as a driver\.

Have a question? Use the FAQ below, shoot us an email at [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) or give us a call at 604\.822\.6786\.

For event\-related enquiries, please email us at [parking\.events@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.events@ubc.ca?subject=Parking%20and%20Events)\. If you have an event and need parking, please fill out a [request form](https://parking.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/EventsForm.pdf) and email it\.
