To support UBC’s commitment to a hybrid workplace, UBC Parking is now offering eligible faculty and staff 10\-day flexible use passes\. This product is called **“10 day Multipack\."**

Eligible staff and faculty can purchase this package and use each day pass as needed\. **If you are working remotely three days or more a week, this is the most cost\-effective option for you\.**

We’ve partnered with our parking app provider, Honk Mobile, to make this flexible parking solution available\. Please follow these simple steps:

1.  Eligible staff and faculty must [login](https://parking.honkmobile.com/login) or [create](https://parking.honkmobile.com/signup) a HONK account using the same e\-mail in both their UBC Parking account and Honk account\.
2.  Once you create an account \(or are logged in\)  [go to this page to purchase the 10\-day Multipack](https://parking.honkmobile.com/monthly/zones/UBCFSPERMIT)
3.  To redeem one day of parking, login to your Honk account and click on the redeem button from the "Sessions" tab once are parked on campus\.
4.  Once all 10 passes have been redeemed, users may purchase a new 10\-day Multipack\.

*Multipacks are non\-refundable and expire 6 months after purchase\. Additionally there is a Honk service fee applied at purchase\.*

[Create an account Using the email associated with your parking account \(once logged in click on link below\)](https://parking.honkmobile.com/signup)
