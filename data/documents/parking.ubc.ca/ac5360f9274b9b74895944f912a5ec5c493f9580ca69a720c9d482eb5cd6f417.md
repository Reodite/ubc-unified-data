### Where do I pick up a towed vehicle?

Impounded vehicles can be picked up from 7am to 11pm at 2451 East Mall, underneath Thunderbird Residence\. To confirm whether your vehicle has been towed, phone 604\-822\-6786, and select options 1 and then 3\.

### My vehicle was damaged or broken into\. What should I do?

We recommend that you file a report with the RCMP on campus at 604 224 1322 and contact your insurance provider\. You can also inform UBC Campus Security by calling 604\-822\-2222\.

### I paid for parking at a pay station but did not receive a receipt\.

In an effort to maintain a sustainable campus, UBC strives to minimize the use of paper\. As a result, parking receipts are not automatically printed unless “Print Receipt” is selected at the machine\. Should you need a copy of your parking receipt, please email parking\.support@ubc\.ca\.

### What happens if I don't pay my ticket?

You have 14 days to pay the reduced amount of your ticket\. If the debt owed is not paid by the deadline, it may be referred to a collection agency and the vehicle may be towed next time it’s parked on campus\.

### What happens if I don’t pay for parking at UBC?

Vehicles parked at UBC are subject to the Traffic and Parking Rules of the University of British Columbia\. If not complied with, vehicles may receive a ticket and be impounded\.

### I disputed my ticket\. How long should it take to receive a reply?

Response times vary depending on the volume of disputes received by the Parking Office\. When a decision is made, you will receive a response to the mailing address or email address provided\. Should the Traffic Notice be upheld, you will have 14 days from the date of the decision to pay the base amount of the Traffic Notice\.

Have a question? Use the FAQ below, shoot us an email at [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) or give us a call at 604\.822\.6786\.

For event\-related enquiries, please email us at [parking\.events@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.events@ubc.ca?subject=Parking%20and%20Events)\. If you have an event and need parking, please fill out a [request form](https://parking.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/EventsForm.pdf) and email it\.
