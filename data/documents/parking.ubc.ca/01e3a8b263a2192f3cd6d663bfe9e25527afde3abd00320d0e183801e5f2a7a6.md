### Can I change my permit to another parking location?

Yes, but only if you receive a new offer for another location from your waitlist\. Contact the Parking Office once the new offer is received and we can swap your location\.

### Can I register more than one vehicle on my permit?

Yes\. Up to three vehicles may be registered, but only one vehicle may be parked on campus at any time\.

### What if I am only taking term one only\. Can I cancel my commuting permit and get a refund for the remaining year?

Yes\. You may cancel your permit at any time and receive a refund for the unused portion\.

### Can commuting students purchase monthly parking permit?

No\. Commuting undergraduate students may purchase only a parking permit for the full academic year\.

### How are resident student permits assigned?

Resident student permits are issued through a waitlist system based on availability\.

### Does joining the waitlist guarantee a permit?

No\. Joining the waitlist does not guarantee a permit offer or one specifically in your preferred top ranked areas\. Some locations are more difficult to get into as they are centralized in a very populated area\.
