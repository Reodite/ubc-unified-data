### What should I do if I am trying to buy or redeem a Honk multipack/pass but the system seems to be down? Do I still need to pay for parking?

A parking payment or permit is required whenever parking at UBC\. Feel free to contact our office over the phone should you be experiencing an issue purchasing or redeeming a Honk pass\. You can also make a payment using one of the alternative mobile payment providers or at the Paystations\.

### Do I still need to pay for parking if the Paystation is malfunctioning or my preferred mobile payment option is not working?

Yes, a parking payment or permit is required whenever parking at UBC\. We recommend attempting to use an alternative method of payment such as Paystation or one of the available mobile payment providers should your preferred method of payment be temporarily unavailable\.

You can also check our website alert section on the landing page for any outage information, or phone our office for additional guidance or to have a payment made over the phone\. Phone wait times might be longer during outages\.

### How can I use a bike cages and bike lockers?

UBC offers 9 free bike cages and 200 secure bike parking spaces in bike lockers located across campus\. Additionally, UBC’s Vancouver campus has over 9,000 high\-quality outdoor bike parking spaces\.

You'll need to login to Parking's payment portal to make a selection\. 

[Sign in](https://ubcparking.t2hosted.ca/cmn/auth_ext.aspx)

All information about the bike cage and bike locker program, including rates, locations and how to gain access, can be found on the Campus and Community Planning site\.

[Bike Cage Information](https://planning.ubc.ca/transportation/cycling/bicycle-parking)

### Do I get a ticket if my electric vehicle finishes charging before I am able to return to it and within the posted time limit?

We acknowledge that the vehicles may take less time to charge than initially anticipated\. However, it is the responsibility of the vehicle owner to manage, plan, and prepare for this possibility and to move their vehicle accordingly\.

### Can you use a 4 hour maximum EV charging station for 2 hours, leave and come back to the same or a different EV charging station for 2 more hours for a total of 4 within the same day?

Yes\. The time limit posted on the EV stations is per day and per location so you can use EV stations at more than one location on the same day as long as you valid payment or permit\.

### Why the EV charging stations across campus have signs limiting how much time a vehicle can use them per day?

This is done so we can enable more vehicles to use them by having a higher turnover\. Vehicles take an average of 2\.5hrs to charge, so allowing them to stay much longer than that would prevent other vehicles from charging as well\.

### Where does my Emeritus/25 Years Club Permit enable me to park?

Your permit will provide you with the same parking privileges as a Faculty/Staff Permit\.  Any multi\-level parkades and surface lots that displays signs indicating Faculty/Staff Permits are valid for your permit\.

### I have an Emeritus/25 Years Club Parking Permit\. How do I access and manage my UBC Parking account?

You may reach Parking Services at [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) to update your vehicle or personal information on your parking account\. Remember to send the current vehicle insurance information displaying your name as one of the drivers when updating vehicle information\.

### How do I get my 25 Years Club Retired Parking Permit?

You may present the letter acknowledging your 25 years retirement status sent by the President of the 25 Years Club to the Parking Office or e\-mail us at [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca)\. Be sure to include your valid vehicle insurance documents displaying you as a registered driver so a permit can be issued\.

### How long is the Emeritus Parking Permit valid for?

The Emeritus Parking Permit runs from April 1st to end of March\. To renew your permit e\-mail [parking\.support@ubc\.ca](mailto:parking.support@ubc.ca) along with your current vehicle insurance document displaying your name as a driver\.
