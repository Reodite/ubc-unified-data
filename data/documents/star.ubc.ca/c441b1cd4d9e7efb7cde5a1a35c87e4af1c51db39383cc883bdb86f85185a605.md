-   ## Virtual Medical Triaging
    
    **Designing with an eye to the future\.** Capstone student, Carla Mather, mixed reality \(XR\) designer and developer, works with the Canadian Special Forces Operations Command to design a virtual medical triaging program using real\-time biometric data\.
    
    [Embedded content: Carla Mather: Hololens](https://www.youtube.com/embed/-o5y5Q_hoHU?feature=oembed)
    
-   ## Cloud Chamber
    
    **Five UBC Okanagan students dreamed of putting their own cloud chamber into space\.** After building a tight\-knit team studying Physics, Engineering, and Computer Science, and working in UBC Okanagan’s world\-class labs, the students created a project of such a high caliber it drew the attention of the Canadian Space Agency\.
