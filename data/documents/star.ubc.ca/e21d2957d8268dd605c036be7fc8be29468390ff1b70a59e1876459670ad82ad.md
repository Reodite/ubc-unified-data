Our multidisciplinary research platform consists of researchers, partners and world\-class technical resources working together to solve present and future challenges in a range of sectors and disciplines\.

### STEP 1:

### We Create Opportunities

We collaborate closely with researchers to understand their vision and develop strategies that maximize the impact of UBC innovation\. We cultivate partnerships that align with our R&amp;D and academic interests and facilitate strategic connections, from one\-to\-one introductions to hosting annual group symposiums\.

### STEP 2:

### We Mobilize Resources

We assemble and support multi\-disciplinary research teams that work directly with solution seekers in the public, private, and non\-profit sectors to create, innovate and problem solve\. Our university industry liaison office creates dynamic, flexible partnership agreements that meet the needs of all stakeholders\.

### STEP 3:

### We Solve Problems

We leverage our expertise and global partner networks to solve real\-world problems\. From developing new material development, rapid prototyping, hazard\-sensing, improved decision\-making processes, to validation of complex computational analysis and simulation, we bring ideas and solutions to life\.

### STEP 4:

### We Catalyze Information

We are deeply committed to achieving successful outcomes for our partners and collaborators\. We provide valuable decision\-support, commercialization advice and implementation recommendations that ensure our research outcomes have the greatest opportunity for impact and benefit to all Canadians\.

## Our Focus

We pilot new models of technical and social innovation on multiple scales, from personal protection and human performance to community level resilience and long term regional planning\.

Our consultative and interdisciplinary approach to R&amp;D has led to cross\-sectoral advancements in a variety of sectors, including defense, health and sport, these include human performance in extreme environments, advanced materials and manufacturing, and disaster foresight and recovery\.

### Intelligent Materials

Advances in composites, nano\-materials, additive manufacturing and the internet of things are opening up new pathways to the next generation of protective materials capable of communicating in their operating environments\.

### Before and After Disaster

From individual to regional crisis, we work to create a better understanding of the causes of crisis and the conditions of resilience that enable a more expedient return to normal, turning complex challenges into solvable problems\.

### Decision Support

We take a holistic approach to supporting rapid hazard\-response, from building standards for hazard reporting or creating new organizational approaches and tactics to respond to changing threats from wildfire, pandemic or criminal activity\.

## Our Approach

[Image](https://star.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/52/2018/05/STAR-hub-lab-tech-780x780-300x300.jpg)

**We apply an adaptive, holistic, and integrated approach to problem\-solving that matches academic expertise to partner and societal problem\.** From multi\-year, consortium\-driven R&amp;D projects to on\-demand technical support, UBC STAR supports partner organizations ranging from startups and small\-to\-medium size enterprises to large multi\-national firms\.

Our research teams and partners have direct access to specialized lab facilities, advanced technology and equipment, including rapid prototyping, 3D scanning and modelling, and blast and ballistics impact testing\.
