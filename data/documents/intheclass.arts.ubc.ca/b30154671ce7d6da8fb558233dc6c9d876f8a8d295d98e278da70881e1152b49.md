The *Renewed What I Learned in Class Today Facilitation Guide and Toolkit* supports faculty, staff, and students interested in facilitating discussions using resources from the *What I Learned in Class Today* \(WILICT\) research project\. The guide supports learners in cultivating a robust understanding of the context of the project and its materials while building up the confidence to host discussions with peers\. While the guide’s purpose is to support peer\-to\-peer  facilitated discussions, there are plenty of opportunities for self\-directed learning and reflection\.
