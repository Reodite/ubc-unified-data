**When is the admissions deadline for the undergraduate programme?**

The deadline for undergraduate admissions for the 2013 Summer Session and 2013\-2014 Winter Session has passed\. Deadlines for the Summer Session are typically mid\-January while the deadline for the Winter Session is typically end of January\. For more information, [click here](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc/vancouver/dates.ezc)\.

For graduate studies, the deadline for admission varies by department\. For more information, [click here](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/graduate-degree-programs/master-of-arts-sociology)\.
