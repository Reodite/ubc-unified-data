Our team consists of educators from a wide range of departments across the Faculty of Arts, alongside technology specialists from Arts ISIT\. Together, we’re tackling the challenges and opportunities of writing in the age of Generative AI\. With our combined expertise in writing instruction, academic integrity, and learning technology, we support students and faculty in using these new tools responsibly, while ensuring that ethical writing practices remain central to learning\.
