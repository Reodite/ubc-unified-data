***25 things we are proud of***

Now that we are midway through Building the Future: 2021\-2026, we are delighted to share just a few of our many important impacts to date\.

Ranked among the world’s top medical schools with the fifth\-largest MD enrollment in North America, the UBC Faculty of Medicine is a leader in championing innovation and excellence in education, medical science and research translation to improve health and wellbeing for society\. Our people at all levels are leading the way in influencing research and policy, developing talented researchers, educators and trainees, and engaging patients and the public\.

1

### **Canada’s Immuno\-Engineering and**  
**Biomanufacturing Hub**

Bringing together provincial, national and international partners to [accelerate the development and manufacturing](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/canadas-immuno-engineering-and-biomanufacturing-hub-research-teams-awarded-140m-in-federal-funding/) of lifesaving medicines for Canadians\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/1b_FOM_Icon-1.png)

2

### ********New admissions pathway for Black medical students********

Helping to [address the underrepresentation](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/faculty-of-medicine-launches-new-admissions-pathway-for-black-medical-students/) of Black physicians in B\.C\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/2a_FOM_Icon.png)

3

### ********World’s largest known gift for MS research********

[Expanding multiple sclerosis research and care](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/33-8m-gift-to-transform-ms-research-and-save-more-lives-sooner/) to advance treatments and improve patient lives — thanks to an unprecedented $33\.8M donation\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/3a_FOM_Icon.png)

4

**Advancing Truth and Reconciliation**

Delivering on the commitments outlined in [our Response](https://www.med.ubc.ca/about/indigenous-health/) to the Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Canada’s Calls to Action through new programs and initiatives\.

5

********Planetary Healthcare Lab********

Shrinking healthcare’s carbon footprint in B\.C\. and beyond with the [ﬁrst\-ever initiative of its kind](https://pathwaysmagazine.med.ubc.ca/from-planetary-health-to-planetary-healthcare/) — anywhere in the world\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/5a_FOM_Icon-1.png)

6

**Groundbreaking vaccine technology**

[Unlocking the potential of self\-replicating mRNA](https://biomedicalinnovation.pathways.med.ubc.ca/how-a-million-doses-can-save-the-world/) for safer, faster and more accessible vaccines and treatments\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/6a_FOM_Icon.png)

7

********Micro\-credential training********

Equipping learners with critical skills for the future of medicine, with certiﬁcates on everything from [health data analytics](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/programs-credentials/health-data-analytics-opportunities-applications-microcertificate) to [regulatory affairs](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/programs-credentials/regulatory-affairs-life-sciences-microcertificate) in the life sciences\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/7b_FOM_Icon.png)

8

**Next\-generation dementia research**

[Revolutionizing Alzheimer’s treatments](https://biomedicalinnovation.pathways.med.ubc.ca/how-lab-grown-mini-brains-could-reveal-the-inner-workings-of-dementia/) through stem\-cell technology and advanced neuroimaging\.

9

**The Data Science and Health \(DASH\) Cluster**

Working across the university to [harmonize health research data](https://datascienceandhealth.ubc.ca/) in B\.C\. to improve health outcomes\.

10

********Family Practice Innovation Support Unit********

Working in partnership with local communities and clinical teams to [evaluate and strengthen primary care](https://isu.ubc.ca/) in B\.C\.

11

********Road to Recovery********

Supporting [seamless addiction care](https://www.bccsu.ca/road-to-recovery/) through innovative, evidence\-based models\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/17b_FOM_Icon-300x300.png)

12

**Drug discovery at the atomic level**

Using [cryo\-electron microscopy](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/drug-discovery-at-an-atomic-scale/) to accelerate new treatments and therapies for cancer and other diseases\.

13

********Healthy Environments in Academic Research Teams \(HEART\) Program********

Creating more [inclusive and collaborative research environments](https://grad-postdoc.med.ubc.ca/healthy-environments-in-academic-research-teams-heart-program/) for faculty, staff and learners\.

14

**********Embedded Counsellor Program for research graduate students**********

Expanding health and wellbeing [supports](https://grad-postdoc.med.ubc.ca/current-students/student-wellbeing/counselling/)  
for learners\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/13a_FOM_Icon-300x300.png)

15

********Rural Doctors’ UBC Chair in Rural Health********

Working in partnership to [improve health equity](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/ubc-chair-will-advance-rural-health-education-and-research-in-b-c/) in rural, remote and Indigenous communities throughout B\.C\.

16

******************Faculty Emerging Leaders Program******************

Providing academic and clinical faculty with the skills and opportunities to become [academic health leaders of tomorrow](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/hr/faculty-hr-resources/career-professional-development/leadership-development/)\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/15a_FOM_Icon-300x300.png)

17

**********Promising new treatments for Type\-1 diabetes**********

Creating new hope for patients with an [innovative stem cell\-based treatment](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/stem-cell-based-treatment-controls-blood-sugar-in-people-with-type-1-diabetes/) and new [JDRF Centre of Excellence at UBC](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/ubc-launches-canadas-first-jdrf-centre-of-excellence-in-type-1-diabetes-research/)\.

18

************Supercharging drug development through AI************

Bringing new treatments to patients quickly with the world’s fastest [drug screening technology](https://biomedicalinnovation.pathways.med.ubc.ca/supercharging-drug-development-through-ai/)\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/18a_FOM_Icon.png)

19

****************Revolutionizing treatments for childhood cancers****************

Leveraging [precision medicine](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/could-chicken-eggs-help-revolutionize-cancer-treatment-for-children/) breakthroughs to improve treatment and save lives\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Family_FOM_Icon.png)

20

**Leaders in single\-cell genomics**

Mapping the evolution of aggressive [breast](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/single-cell-genome-sequencing-provides-new-insights-into-deadly-cancers/) and [ovarian](https://biomedicalinnovation.pathways.med.ubc.ca/stopping-an-invisible-killer-in-its-tracks/) cancers to save lives\.

21

************************Pushing the boundaries of spinal cord research at UBCO and UBCV************************

Developing [implantable technologies](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/pushing-the-boundaries-of-spinal-cord-research/) to help people with spinal cord injury live longer, healthier lives\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/Spine2_FOM_Icon.png)

22

************Clinical Educator Fellowship Program — Indigenous Stream************

Embracing an Indigenous approach and building new opportunities to develop skills in [clinical education](https://ches.med.ubc.ca/programs/clinical-educator-fellowship-program-indigenous-stream/)\.

23

********Strategic Investment Fund********

Since 2021, nearly $4M in [32 faculty\- and staff\-led projects](https://stratplan.med.ubc.ca/strategy-to-action/investment-fund/), from seed2STEM and the Disability in the Workplace workshops to the Drone Transport Initiative\.

24

**********************Groundbreaking cardiac medicine**********************

Designing innovative [new blood thinners](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/innovative-treatment-targets-blood-clots-without-increased-bleeding-risk/) to better treat stroke, heart attack and other cardiac events\.

[Image](https://stratplan-in-action.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/AnatomicalHeart_FOM_Icon.png)

25

********Graduating more doctors and health professionals for B\.C\.********

Expanding and distributing [MD](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/ubc-enhances-training-for-medical-students-in-the-fraser/), [residency](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/ubc-welcomes-186-new-family-medicine-resident-doctors/), [physical therapy](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/expanded-ubc-master-of-physical-therapy-program-coming-to-surrey/), [occupational therapy](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/ubc-expands-occupational-therapy-program-in-northern-bc/) and [midwifery](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/graduating-more-midwives-for-b-c/) programs to help meet the growing healthcare needs of British Columbians\.
