**Title:** Communications Manager   
**Program:** Master of Physical Therapy Program  
**Location:** Vancouver

***What does it mean to you to help train and graduate more health professionals for patients and communities across B\.C\.?***

In my role, I am privileged to dedicate myself to promoting and expanding healthcare education for aspiring physical therapists\. I get to connect with passionate students and physiotherapists across the province, including in rural and remote areas, and promote physical therapy as a career path\. I find it incredibly fulfilling to see the impact of our efforts as we graduate diverse and skilled professionals who will contribute to better patient care and community wellness, and address the dynamic healthcare needs in British Columbia\.
