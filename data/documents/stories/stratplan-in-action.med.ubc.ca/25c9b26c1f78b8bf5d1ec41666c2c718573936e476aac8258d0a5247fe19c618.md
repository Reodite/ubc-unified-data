**Title:** Speech\-Language Pathology Coordinator  
**Program:** Speech\-Language Pathology \(SLP\)  
**Location:** Victoria

***What does it mean to you to help train and graduate more health professionals for patients and communities across B\.C\.?***

The distribution of UBC’s SLP program in Victoria will provide students with valuable opportunities for training and building relationships on a local level\. These connections will help grow and sustain our workforce and improve access to SLP care across the Island\.
