Our Story

Our programs are designed to plant the seed of environmental curiosity by educating people about British Columbia’s diverse ecology\. We believe hands\-on activities and outdoor recreation creates understanding and lifelong connection to the natural world\.

[Wild &amp; Immersive team member holding up wild raspberries](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/wild-raspberries.jpg)

### Our mission is to inspire people to care about the environment by providing them with uplifting nature\-based experiences\.

[Two adults looking through devices at trees during a wild and immersive retreat](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/adults-looking-at-trees.jpg) [Group of young kids lining up at forest school](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/kids-learning-at-wi.jpg)

## Learning organically

Our children’s programs offer your kids a place to play and learn about nature away from the classroom\. Sign them up for our year\-round [Forest School](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/maple-ridge/forest-school/) or check out our [Pro\-D day camps](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/maple-ridge/pro-d-day-camps/) and watch them grow into confident, outdoorsy, and environmentally conscious humans\.

For adults, we have a variety of [special events](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/events/adults/?event_location=mapleridge) that expand the mind and reconnect you to nature\. Companies can also sign up for [retreats](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/maple-ridge/retreats/) where their teams can connect and bond more naturally\.

[Map of where the Malcom Knapp Research Forest is located in relationship to Maple Ridge, Vancouver, and Abbotsford](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Maple-Ridge-Map-1.png)

## A diverse educational space

Our forest is 5,157 hectares of protected “working forest” situated in the heart of British Columbia’s Fraser Valley\. Bursting with life, it features over 100 species of flora and fauna, including protected old growth\.

## Did you know?

**Nature has been linked to physical, mental, emotional, and cognitive benefits** that positively influence an individual’s development and well\-being\.  
*\(Chawla, 1998; Kellert, 2002; Louv, 2005; Hartig et al\., 2014\)\.*

There is a **link between childhood outdoor recreation and pro\-environmental attitudes in later life**\.  
*\(Chawla and Derr, 2012; Ewert et al\. 2005; Wells and Lekies, 2006\)\.*

Environmental education is **one the most significant tools in reversing current trends in biodiversity loss**\.  
*\(Feinsinger, 1998; Jacobson and McDuff 1998; Wilson, 1996\)\.*

## Our Wild Timeline

### 2017

Inspired Learning was born as a low\-cost programming option for groups staying at Loon Lake\. Our goals were to increase guests’ experiences and get youth, school, and adult groups to learn more about the local environment\.

We then grew to include low\-cost field trip options at the research forest, as well as bringing in school groups to learn about nature\-based topics\.

### 2018\-2019

We changed our name to Wild &amp; Immersive and our team grew\! Our programming expanded to include Pro\-D days and spring break camps, family special events and after school programs\.

### 2020\-2022

When the pandemic hit, our field trips and programs shut down for a couple months\. So, we got creative and Forest School started to grow\. Starting as a bi\-monthly program, it has since grown into a weekly program with 4 age group sessions to choose from\.

### 2022\-on\-going

The future is now – we offer a full spread of programs including [Forest School](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/maple-ridge/forest-school/), day camps, overnight camps, [field trips](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/maple-ridge/field-trips/), [retreats](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/maple-ridge/retreats/), and [special events](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/special-events/?event_location=mapleridge)\.
