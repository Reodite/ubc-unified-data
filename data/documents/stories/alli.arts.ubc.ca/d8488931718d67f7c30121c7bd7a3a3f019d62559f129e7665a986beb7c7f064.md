### ALLI’s lasting impact

Everyone involved in ALLI took valuable experiences and new knowledge from it\. The collaborative nature of the programming meant that facilitators and participants alike gained much\. The projects produced during ALLI attest to the impact of ALLI on the students, as does the feedback we received from them\.

Furthermore, ALLI reached beyond its immediate beneficiaries\. The public was invited to the [film festival](https://alli-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/film/), the [orality panel](https://alli-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/stories/), and the [revitalization symposium](https://alli-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/symposium/)\. The [theatre production](https://alli-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/theatre/) was particularly influential: 20 first nations people from outside ALLI performed the piece, which was interactive with the audience\. The result lives on in video format\.
