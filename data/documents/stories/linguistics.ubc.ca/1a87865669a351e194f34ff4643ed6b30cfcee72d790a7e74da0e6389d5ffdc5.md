## About

“If the right spells are spoken, the lost words might return\.”  
— Robert Macfarlane

**Research Interests:** 

As part of my dissertation, I’m developing an interactive online language learning tool for ʔayʔajuθəm \(also known as Comox\-Sliammon\), a Coast Salish language traditionally spoken by four communities along the Northern Strait of Georgia in British Columbia\.

In addition, I’ve been working on:

-   Degree constructions
-   Determiners and demonstratives
-   Deixis and gesture
-   Modality and evidentiality
-   Wh\-constructions

“If the right spells are spoken, the lost words might return\.”  
— Robert Macfarlane

**Research Interests:** 

As part of my dissertation, I’m developing an interactive online language learning tool for ʔayʔajuθəm \(also known as Comox\-Sliammon\), a Coast Salish language traditionally spoken by four communities along the Northern Strait of Georgia in British Columbia\.

In addition, I’ve been working on:

-   Degree constructions
-   Determiners and demonstratives
-   Deixis and gesture
-   Modality and evidentiality
-   Wh\-constructions
