Two faculty members, [Eric Bateson](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/profile/eric-vatikiotis-bateson/) and [Michael Rochemont](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/profile/michael-rochemont/), passed away recently after many years in the department\.

Their profile pages are preserved here\.  

Eric Bateson

Eric Bateson examined the structure of communication across domains from an unusually integrated perspective\.

[Source link](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/profile/eric-vatikiotis-bateson/)

Michael Rochemont

Michael Rochemont pioneered research into the relation between syntax and information structure\.

[Source link](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/profile/michael-rochemont/)
