## About

Hi there\! My name is Starr Sandoval, and I’m a third year PhD student in the [Department of Linguistics](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/) at the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/)\. My primary research interests lie in semantics and fieldwork\. My supervisor is [Marcin Morzycki,](https://morzycki.github.io/) and my committee includes [Ryan Bochnak](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/profile/ryan-bochnak/) and [Hotze Rullman](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/profile/hotze-rullmann/)\.

Prior to UBC, I worked as a lab manager for the Language Development Lab at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, where I helped develop and run eye\-tracking and EEG experiments\. Before then, I received my BA in Linguistics from New York University, where I worked as a research assistant for [Sudha Arunachalam](https://steinhardt.nyu.edu/people/sudha-arunachalam) at the [LEARN Lab](https://wp.nyu.edu/learnlab/) and [Ailís Cournane](https://wp.nyu.edu/cournane/) at the [Child Language Lab](https://wp.nyu.edu/cournane/child-language-lab/)\.

If you have questions, comments, or similar interests, I’d love to hear from you\!  
starr\.sandoval\[at\]ubc\.ca

---

## Research

Theoretically, I’m interested in developing a semantics for subsective adjectives that accounts for their influence the interpretation of nominals\. I additionally research intensification, *as\-*phrases, hypothetical comparatives, nonlocal readings of adjectives, the grammar of zero, and question word used to ask for manners and reasons across languages\.  You can read more about my research and the projects I’m involved in [here](https://blogs.ubc.ca/starrsandoval/research/)\.

My fieldwork involves the documentation and analysis of [Ktunaxa](https://www.ktunaxa.org/), a language isolate spoken in interior British Columbia and parts of Idaho, Washington, and Montana\. I am a member of the Ktunaxa lab, where we work to develop preservation and teaching resources\. To learn more about Ktunaxa, you can check out [First Voices](https://www.firstvoices.com/explore/FV/sections/Data/Ktunaxa/Ktunaxa/Ktunaxa) or [@ktunaxapride](https://www.instagram.com/ktunaxapride/?hl=en) on Instagram\.
