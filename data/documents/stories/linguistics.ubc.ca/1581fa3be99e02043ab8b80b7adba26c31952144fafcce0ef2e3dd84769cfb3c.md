## About

Ph\.D candidate doing fieldwork on Kumeyaay/Kumiai \(Baja California\) and nɬeʔkepmxcín \(British Columbia\)\. My dissertation concerns temporal interpretation in nɬeʔkepmxcín \(in main clauses, in subordinate clauses, and in co\-ordinated clauses\)\. My main research interests include language documentation, linguistic fieldwork, and semantics \(in particular modality, tense, aspect, and mood\)\. For more about me and my research, visit: [https://www\.ellahannon\.com\.](https://www.ellahannon.com/)
