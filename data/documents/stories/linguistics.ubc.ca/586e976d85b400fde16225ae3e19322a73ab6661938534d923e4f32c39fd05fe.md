## About

I am a linguist and fieldworker\. My research interests lie mainly in linguistic semantics, with a strong focus on cross\-linguistic variation, semantic fieldwork, and language documentation\. Topics that I have worked on include gradability and comparatives, tense and aspect, modality, complementation, and questions\.
