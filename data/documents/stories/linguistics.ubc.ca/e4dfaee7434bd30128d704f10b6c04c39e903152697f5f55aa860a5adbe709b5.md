## About

Aaní is a Ph\.D\. student from Sheetʼká \(Sitka, Alaska\) studying her ancestral language, Lingít\. She is Kiks\.ádi \(Raven\-Frog clan\) from the Steel House, and centers the needs of her home community in all her language work\.

She is interested in linguistic variation in the Lingít language, with a particular focus on speakers from her home community and variation in their speech across gender and clan\. Currently, she is studying phonetic correlates of stress in the Lingít verb phrase, with broader interests in prosody and intonation\.  She is also involved in Lingít transcription and translation efforts, currently with Sealaska Heritage Institute\.
