## About

My research focuses on the syntax and semantics of  Ktunaxa, a language isolate spoken in interior BC and parts of Montana and Idaho, and exclamative constructions in Québec French\. For both Ktunaxa and Québec French, I am interested in the relation between the syntax of clause typing and speech acts, and I hope that my research contributes to the modelling of the interfaces between syntax and semantics, and syntax and pragmatics\. Otherwise, I am passionate about contemporary dance, yoga and everything movement related\.

---

## Research

I participate in three research labs:

-   [Eh\-lab](http://syntaxofspeechacts.linguistics.ubc.ca/welcome/), under the supervision of Martina Wiltschko
-   Ktunaxa Lab, under the supervision of Martina Wiltshcko
-   [TAP\-lab](https://blogs.ubc.ca/taplab/), under the supervision of Lisa Matthewson and Hotze Rullmann
