## About

I am an Associate Professor and Canada Research Chair in Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning in Linguistics &amp; the School of Information \(joint\), and Computer Science \(Associate Member\) at The University of British Columbia\. My areas of research interest are Natural Language Processing, Deep Learning of Natural Language, Arabic Natural Language Processing, and Social Media Mining\. In my Lab, our goal is to create intelligent ‘social’ machines that can interact naturally with humans\.
