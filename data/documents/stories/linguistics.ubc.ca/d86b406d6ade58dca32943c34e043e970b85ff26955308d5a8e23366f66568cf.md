## About

I am a 2nd year PhD student in linguistics at the University of British Columbia\. My research interests are primarily in how semantic constraints, such as animacy, agentivity, and information and discourse structure preferences, facilitate comprehension and motivate production of less common and/or syntactically complex structures\.
