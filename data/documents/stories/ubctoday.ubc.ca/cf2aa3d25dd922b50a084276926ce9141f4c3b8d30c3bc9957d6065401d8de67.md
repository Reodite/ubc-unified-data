Summer is almost here, signalling the warmer months that encourage us to spend more time outdoors\. 

A few weeks ago, we asked you – our faculty and staff community – to share a snapshot of the beauty of the birds and wildlife around you\. A huge thank you to everyone who took the time to send us a photo and share their comments and captions\. We are excited to share the beautiful images which we hope will further inspire you to get into nature this summer\.

And now, without further ado, take a moment to enjoy the pictures of the colourful and majestic birds and creatures captured by your colleagues\.
