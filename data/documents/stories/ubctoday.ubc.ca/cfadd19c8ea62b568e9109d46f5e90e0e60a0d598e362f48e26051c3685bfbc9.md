Our Partner for Purpose series highlights how UBC faculty and staff partnerships create meaningful change, both within the university and beyond\.

In 2025, we asked our faculty and staff to tell us how they were partnering with others to make an impact\. Thank you to everyone who shared ideas with us\. While we could not profile everyone, you can learn more about our colleagues and their initiatives in a [gallery below recognizing their efforts\.](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/stories/april-1-2026/celebrating-faculty-and-staff-who-are-partnering-for-purpose#gallery)

**Climate Action Planning Micro\-Certificate**  
*Sustainability Hub*  
*UBC Vancouver*

After two successful offerings reaching over 40 participants from a diversity of sectors, the team will collaborate once again to deliver a third iteration in the Fall of 2026\.
