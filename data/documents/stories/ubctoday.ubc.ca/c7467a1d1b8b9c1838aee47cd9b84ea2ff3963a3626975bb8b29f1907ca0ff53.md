Traditionally, this time of the year has been marked by our delightful holiday\-inspired gallery featuring submissions from you, our fellow faculty and staff community\. While other commitments have resulted in us taking a break from the usual process this season, our team has curated a special collection of our holiday favourites submitted by the community in previous years to spread cheer\. We invite you to explore and enjoy these photos with us\.

As we embark on this journey of reminiscing, don't forget to capture your own holiday favourites this season\. Next year, we'll be running a call for holiday photos again, and your memories could be a part of our collective celebration\. Wishing you joy and warmth this holiday season\!
