In spring of 2024, we asked our faculty and staff to tell us who among them were leading the way in generating positive change at the university\. In this five\-part series, we've explored a few of their stories\.

Thank you to everyone who shared their ideas with us\. Unfortunately, we were unable to profile everyone, but you can learn more about our colleagues and their stories in [a gallery](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/stories/october-21-2024/celebrating-faculty-and-staff-who-are-generating-positive-change-university#gallery) recognizing their efforts\.
