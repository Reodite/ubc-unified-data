Spring is here \(even if the wind, rain, and hail signal otherwise\)\. Nonetheless, this time of year is cause for celebration and gives all of us a chance to appreciate the warmer breeze and new seasonal blooms\.

A few weeks ago, we asked you – our faculty and staff community – to share a photo \(or two\) that captures seasonal buds, blooms, and greenery\. A huge thank you to everyone who took the time to send us a photo and share their comments and captions\.

**If you're an Okanagan faculty or staff member who missed your chance to submit a photo the first time around, we invite you to** [**share your photos with us by May 4**](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/april-04-2022/another-chance-share-snapshots-spring-blossoms)[**\.**](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/news/april-04-2022/another-chance-share-snapshots-spring-blossoms) 

And now, without further ado, take a moment to enjoy the unique visual reflections submitted by your fellow colleagues\.
