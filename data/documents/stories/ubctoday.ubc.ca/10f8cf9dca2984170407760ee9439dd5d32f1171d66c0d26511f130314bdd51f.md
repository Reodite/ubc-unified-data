Summer is in full swing, which is cause for celebration and an appreciation for the sunnier days, warmer weather and new seasonal growth\. 

A few weeks ago, we asked you – our faculty and staff community – to share a snapshot of what has captured your attention in the great outdoors, and what's growing in your home or garden\. A huge thank you to everyone who took the time to send us a photo and share their comments and captions\. 

And now, without further ado, take a moment to enjoy the seasonal plants, produce, herbs, blooms, and greenery submitted by your fellow colleagues\.
