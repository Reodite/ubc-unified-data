In spring of 2023, we asked our faculty and staff community to tell us who among them were taking steps or making efforts to advance sustainability at UBC\. In this five\-part series, we've explored a few of their stories\.   
  
Thank you to everyone who shared their ideas with us\. Unfortunately, we were unable to profile everyone, but you can learn more about our colleagues and their stories in [a gallery](https://ubctoday.ubc.ca/stories/october-30-2023/celebrating-faculty-and-staff-advancing-sustainability-university-gallery#gallery) recognizing their efforts\.
