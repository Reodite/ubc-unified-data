## Our new student placements have just begun\! Learn more about our student physiotherapists below:

[Image](https://med-fom-physioclinic.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/PTRC_Janzen_Picture-817x960.jpeg)

## Anna Janzen

### Student Physiotherapist

[Book Now](https://ubcphysioandresearchclinic.janeapp.com/locations/ubc-physical-therapy-research-clinic/book#/staff_member/238)

Anna is a Master of Physical Therapy student at the University of British Columbia, currently completing her final clinical placement\. Prior to physiotherapy school, she earned a Bachelor of Kinesiology \(Exercise Science\) from the University of the Fraser Valley and a Diploma in Outdoor Recreation Management from Capilano University\.  
  
Throughout her training, Anna has gained experience working with a diverse range of clients in both hospital and private practice settings, including older adults, individuals with spinal cord injuries, patients recovering from hip and knee replacements, and people with musculoskeletal conditions\.  
  
Outside of the clinic, Anna enjoys spending time in the mountains and on the water\. She has worked as a mountain guide on Mt\. Fuji in Japan, a kayak guide in BC’s glacial lakes, and at ski resorts\. In her free time, you’ll often find her bouldering or rock climbing in Squamish, strength training, or baking\.  
  
Anna believes that effective rehabilitation begins with understanding what matters most to each person\. She enjoys working collaboratively with clients to build confidence in their movement, improve their function, and help them “return to the activities they love\.”

## Janice Chan

### Student Physiotherapist

[Book Now](https://ubcphysioandresearchclinic.janeapp.com/locations/ubc-physical-therapy-research-clinic/book#/staff_member/241)

Janice is a second\-year student in the Master of Physical Therapy program at UBC\. Prior to entering the program, she completed her Bachelor of Kinesiology\.  
  
During her training, Janice has worked with a variety of client populations, with specific interests in musculoskeletal injuries and neurological rehabilitation\. Her most recent placements involved treating post\-operative orthopaedic patients, sports injuries, and stroke patients\.  
  
In her spare time, Janice enjoys running, hiking, and any racquet sports\. Having experienced past injuries, she understands the importance of active rehabilitation with a patient\-centered approach\. She aims to empower clients with the knowledge and confidence to return to the activities that matter most to them\. Outside the clinic, Janice loves to explore new food and spend time in nature with friends &amp; family\.  
  
Janice is fluent in English and Cantonese and is happy to provide care to Cantonese\-speaking patients\.
