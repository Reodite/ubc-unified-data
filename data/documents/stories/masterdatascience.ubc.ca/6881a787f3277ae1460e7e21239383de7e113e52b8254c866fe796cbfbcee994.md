*“This program stands out due to its unique blend of data science and NLP courses, serving as a quick refresher and bridges the knowledge gap for students like me who completed their bachelor's degrees years ago and are transitioning from non\-STEM fields\.”*

#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor’s degree in English Language and Literature from Zhejiang University  
**Program:** MDS Computational Linguistics, Class of 2023

I love learning and teaching languages and I’ve also always been passionate about leveraging data to solve business problems at work\. This has led me to realize that computational linguistics is the perfect crossroads of these interests\.

#### **About Meiyu Zheng**

Meiyu has a Bachelor’s degree in English Language and Literature from Zhejiang University\. Prior to entering the MDS Computational Linguistics program, Meiyu was a curriculum designer at Duolingo where she realized the she wanted to pursue a master’s degree in data science with a focus on Natural Language Processing\. When not in the MDS Computational Linguistics program, Meiyu enjoys biking and learning new languages\.
