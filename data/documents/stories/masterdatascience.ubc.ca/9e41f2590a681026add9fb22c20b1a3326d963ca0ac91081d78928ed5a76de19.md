#### ****Previous Degree****: Bachelor of Arts – Language and Intercultural Relations, French &amp; Language and Cognition from Toronto Metropolitan University  
****Program****: MDS Computational Linguistics, Class of 2023

I’ve always been a very analytical person, and though I came from a qualitative background I wanted to fulfill the part of me that was curious about the quantitative side of things\. Combining my interest in linguistics with my interest in quantitative methodologies, MDS Computational Linguistics seemed like a perfect fit\. 

#### ****About Kai Maurin\-Jones****

Kai completed his Bachelor of Arts in Language and Intercultural Relations, French &amp; Language and Cognition from Toronto Metropolitan University\. During his second year, he worked as a Research Assistant working on a project investigating multilingual codeswitching practices in the Philippines, which introduced him to the world of data science\. The top benefits of the program for Kai included the diversity of the students coming into the program, the breadth of content covered in such a short time, and the non\-academic resources available to the students\. In his free time, Kai enjoys keeping active and music, where in another life he was an audio engineer and has recorded music professionally\.
