#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor of Science in the Behavioural Neuroscience stream from the University of British Columbia  
**Program:** MDS Vancouver, Class of 2026

In my third year of undergrad, I joined a datathon just to give it a try, and I ended up really enjoying the technical challenges\. The datathon helped me realize I could combine my domain knowledge with technical know\-how to work in a career that both interested and challenged me\. I continued to stay involved in data science clubs and activities for the rest of my undergrad, growing my interest and understanding of job possibilities in data science\.

#### **About Victoria Farkas**

Before MDS, Victoria had just completed her Bachelor’s from UBC in Behavioural Neuroscience and was working as a data manager for Population Data BC\. Victoria wanted to formalize and expand on her technical knowledge so she applied to the MDS program to get herself career\-ready as quickly and effectively as possible, so the 10\-month accelerated format was perfect for her\. Away from MDS, Victoria is a dancer and also enjoys baking or building little arts and crafts projects\.
