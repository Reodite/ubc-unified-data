#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor of Science in Behavioural Neuroscience from the University of British Columbia        
**Program:** MDS Vancouver, Class of 2023     
 

During my undergrad, I signed up for a programming course on a whim and ended up really enjoying it\. I decided to take more electives over the years in data structures and algorithms, statistical modeling, database design, and applied machine learning and found a particular interest in data science\.

#### **About Lauren Zung**

While completing her Bachelor’s of Science Behavioural Neuroscience from the University of British Columbia, Lauren worked as a junior data scientist for the UBC Digital Emergency Medicine lab where she was developing machine learning models to aid in the detection of lung pathologies\. It was this experience that made Lauren realize she wanted to learn more in this area and found MDS’ comprehensive curriculum gave her the breadth and depth of knowledge she desired\. Outside of MDS, Lauren is a big foodie and loves trying new restaurants around Vancouver\. Lauren is also a film buff and gaming enthusiast\.
