## *Building the Future: 2021\-2026* is a strategic plan for the Faculty of Medicine at the University of British Columbia\.

It builds on the foundation of the *Building the Future: 2016\-2021* and acts as a roadmap to help guide our decisions and actions\.

The refreshed strategic plan was developed in alignment with [UBC’s strategic plan: *Shaping UBC’s Next Century*](https://strategicplan.ubc.ca/) and [UBC’s Indigenous Strategic Plan,](https://indigenous.ubc.ca/indigenous-engagement/indigenous-strategic-plan/?login) and complements the [UBC Health strategic plan](https://health.ubc.ca/strategicplan), and it’s supported by strategic plans at the department, school, centre and institute level\.
