She completed her PhD degree in Interdisciplinary Studies \(emphasis in Anthropology and Media\) at the University of British Columbia\. She joined the School of Nursing’s Stigma and Resilience Among Vunlerable Youth centre in September 2014 as a Project Coordinator\. Now as Managing Director, she works to grow the centres’ capacity for effective applied research and success of its trainees so that SARAVYC’s research continues to directly improve policy and practice that serves youth\. | [Image](https://nursing-alumni.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/04/Hotel-fixture-hot-foil-half-e1555531814344-153x300.png) |
