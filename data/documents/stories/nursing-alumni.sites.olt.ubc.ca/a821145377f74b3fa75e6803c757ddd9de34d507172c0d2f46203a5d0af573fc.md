We have a number of ways of staying connected to the School of Nursing\. We look forward to welcoming you in whatever way you can keep in touch\.

Check our [News and Events](https://nursing.ubc.ca/news-events) listing page on our website\.

Follow us on Twitter [@ubcnursing](https://twitter.com/ubcnursing)\.

Connect on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ubc-school-of-nursing)\.

See your friends and colleagues on [Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/photos/ubcschoolofnursing/)\.

Join the [UBC Nursing Alumni Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/groups/ubcnursingalumni/) \(managed by individual alumni members\)\.

Like the [UBC Nursing Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/ubcnursing/) \(managed by Nursing Communications\)\.

Learn about [Nursing’s Rising Stars](https://apsc.ubc.ca/students/student-stars) \(via the Faculty of Applied Science\) or on our [Where We Are](https://nursing.ubc.ca/alumni/where-we-are) page\.

Subscribe to the UBC Nursing [Alumni E\-Newsletter](http://apsc.ubc.ca/alumni/stay-connected/nursing-newsletter)\.

Change of address: [Online form](https://www.alumni.ubc.ca/services/update-your-address/) or [email](mailto:tara.newell@ubc.ca?subject=Change%20of%20Address) our Alumni Relations Coordinator
