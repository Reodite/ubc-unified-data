The School of Nursing Centenary Medal of Distinction has been created to mark the celebration of the School of Nursing’s 100th Anniversary\. The medal serves to recognize individuals who have brought high honour to the school and/or to the profession of nursing, and/or who have made long\-standing/significant contributions to advance its vision, mission, and mandate\.

We heartily congratulate the winners of this once\-in\-a\-lifetime award\.
