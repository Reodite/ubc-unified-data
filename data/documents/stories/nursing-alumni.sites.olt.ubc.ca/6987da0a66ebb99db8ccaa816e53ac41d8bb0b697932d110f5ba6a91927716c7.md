Obituary originally appeared in Vancouver Sun October 18, 2025

[A white and pink rose lies on a white table](https://nursing-alumni.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/rose-301406_1280-Monika-from-Pixabay-360x234.jpg)

Judith Barrie Killam died peacefully on October 5, 2025 with her children by her side\. Judy was born on June 1, 1942, and spent most of her life in Vancouver\. She attended the nursing school at UBC, where she met and married Bill Killam on July 24, 1965\. They moved shortly after to Ontario, where Judy worked as a public health nurse\. \(She later earned a graduate certificate in gerontology from SFU, further demonstrating her commitment to others\.\) Bill and Judy returned to Vancouver a few years later with son, Michael, in tow\. Although Judy had a number of different jobs in her lifetime, her family was at her core, and Katherine arrived shortly after their return to Vancouver\.

Judy was more than just a devoted mother and wife\. She fully participated in her children’s lives, which meant hours of drop\-offs and pick\-ups at the local ski mountains \(Mike\) and driving – and parking and reversing\! – horse trailers \(Katherine\)\. She was a guide leader \(Snowy Owl\) for several years, and volunteered extensively at school events, including those of her grandchildren\.

She was a giving person, and not only did her children benefit from her caring and kindness, but this extended widely to family and friends\.

Judy was predeceased by her husband Bill \(2013\) and grandson Grady \(2014\)\. She is survived by her sister, Moyra \(Phil\), children Mike \(Keara\) and Katherine \(Steve\), grandchildren Kristen, Aksel, Maddy and Dayton, and great\-grandchildren Jackson, Riley and Carter\.
