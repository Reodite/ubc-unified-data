We are delighted to share with you the stories of our Amazing Alumni\. And we just know that your story is amazing too\. If you graduated from the UBC School of Nursing, or if you have a story to share about one of our alumni, [we’re here to help you tell it](mailto:Alumni.Stories@nursing.ubc.ca)\!

# We want to hear your stories\!

We have lots of examples on these Amazing Alumni pages and we love to feature alumni in our [Touchpoints and other newsletters](https://nursing.ubc.ca/Newsletters)\. New alumni graduate every year, and each one of you has a story to tell\.

-   Where did you grow up?
-   Who inspires you?
-   What drew you to nursing?
-   Why UBC?
-   What was your student experience?
-   What was your most influential lesson?
-   What have you been up to since you graduated?
-   What is your family unit like?
-   What do you do for fun?
-   Will you share a photo or two?

We would love to hear from you\.

Send your story, or a colleague’s story, an *in memoriam*, or a news article to **[Alumni Stories](mailto:Alumni.Stories@nursing.ubc.ca?subject=Alumni%20Story)**
