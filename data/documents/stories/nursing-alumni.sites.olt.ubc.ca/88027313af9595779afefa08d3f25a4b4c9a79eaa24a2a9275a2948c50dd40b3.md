    My research interests include developing interventions that address the following cancer survivorship issues: Return to work \(RTW\), fear of cancer recurrence, managing cancer\-distress, uncertainty, and coping\. I am the co\-director of Cancer and Work \(www\.cancerandwork\.ca\), a Canadian bilingual website created with a multidisciplinary team and expert writers to address the gap in return to work support for cancer survivors\. I am currently co\-leading a clinical trial for the management of fear of cancer recurrence among breast and gynecological cancer survivors and developing cancer and work tool to assess readiness for return to work\.
