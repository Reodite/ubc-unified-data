[Image](https://nursing-alumni.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/10/Joan-Lansdell-227x300.jpg)Joan graduated from VGH in 1957 and from UBC with a BSN in 1958\. On completion of nursing at UBC, she commenced work at Vernon Jubilee Hospital\. Her subsequent postings include the Central Vancouver Island Health Unit, Dawson Creek, Squamish, the Boundary Health Unit, South Okanagan Health Unit and Penticton\. Highlights of her career include developing a new prenatal program for BC Public Health nurses, making a movie of birth and breast feeding, and giving each nurse a special area to develop as she saw the need\.
