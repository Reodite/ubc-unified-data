Meet Dr\. Marcia Docherty, Regional Director of Administration for UBC Distributed Programs — Vancouver Island\.

---

### My pronouns:

She/Her

### Title:

Regional Director, Administration

### Department/unit:

UBC Distributed Programs – Vancouver Island \(DPVI\)

[Dr\. Marcia Docherty](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/MarciaDocherty-2026_500x500.jpg)

### Campus:

I am based at the University of Victoria and oversee a distributed team that also work from the Vancouver Island Technology Park, two Clinical Academic Campuses \(the Royal Jubilee Hospital &amp; Victoria General Hospital\) and four Affiliated Regional Centres \(Cowichan District Hospital, Nanaimo Regional General Hospital, Comox Valley Hospital &amp; Campbell River Hospital\)\.

### How long have you worked at the UBC Faculty of Medicine?

I started in February 2026, so I am very much a newbie\!

### **Tell us what you do at the Faculty in one or two sentences:**

I provide strategic and operational leadership to ensure the effective administration of the DPVI\. I work closely with the Regional Associate Dean and lead a wonderful team of professional administrators on Vancouver Island who are dedicated to operational excellence and support learners and faculty in undergraduate and postgraduate medicine, physiotherapy and speech language pathology programs\.

### **What’s your favourite thing about your work?**

With a graduate degree in distributed learning, I love working in a distributed model where I get to collaborate with a variety of people and teams remotely and in person\. I am enjoying building relationships with my UBC, UVic and Island Health colleagues by working from and visiting the different sites\.

### What’s one thing we might be surprised to learn about you?

I am a UBC alumni — I completed my undergraduate degree in the Faculty of Arts before going on to become a Nuclear Medicine Technologist\.

### What do you do regularly to invest in your mental and/or physical health?

I enjoy being out in nature and recently learned the practice of forest bathing with the team\. I love being in the water and won’t say no to an AquaFit class or swimming/ kayaking in a lake\. I try to do something active every day, which is easy to achieve in Victoria\.

### Where is your favourite vacation spot, and why?

This is hard to choose\! If I had to pick one spot at this moment, I would pick Mexico City because of the many fabulous museums and galleries, the cosmopolitan vibe, the ease of travelling to it from B\.C\., the ease of travelling to neighbouring cities in Mexico and, of course, the glorious hot weather\.
