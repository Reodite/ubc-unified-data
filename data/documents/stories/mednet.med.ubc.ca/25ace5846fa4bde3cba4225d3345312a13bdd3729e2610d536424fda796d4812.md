Meet Shelly Au, Senior Manager, Research Programs and Services\.

---

[Shelly Au](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/11/Shelly-Au.jpeg)

### Title:

Senior Manager, Research Programs and Services

### Department/unit:

Faculty of Medicine, Office of Research

### Campus:

UBC Vancouver

### How long have you worked at the UBC Faculty of Medicine?

Since August 2018

### Tell us what you do at the Faculty in one or two sentences:

I work together with other pillars within the Faculty \(Education, Organization, Partnerships\) to operationalize strategic priorities, manage all research\-related resources for faculty members, and oversee/promote research activities\.

### What’s your favourite thing about your work?

Every day I have the opportunity to work with our amazing faculty members and staff who inspires me\. There’s never a dull moment and every challenge is different, which keeps me engaged\.

### Best piece of advice:

Take every opportunity that comes your way and have fun with it\.

### One thing we might be surprised to learn about you?

While completing my PhD, I was also enrolled in the Freelance Makeup Artistry program at Blanche MacDonald and became a freelance makeup artist \(side gig\) to help rediscover my passion and creativity\.

### What is the song you sang out loud as a teenager?

Anything Backstreet Boys\! All day, every day\.

### Most healthy habit:

Right now, I would say constantly staying connected with family and friends around the world, thanks to technology\.

### What is your favourite vacation spot and why?

Definitely Maui – holds a special place in my heart\.
