## Placement Information

**Year you started your placement**  
2012

**Placement period**  
May – August, September – December

**Supervisor’s name**  
Dirk Manske

## Project Details

**Overview**  
Researching the band gap of the unconventional superconductor Strontium Ruthenate

**Project objectives**  
Calculate the susceptibility of strontium ruthenate from first principles, determine a model for the effective interaction, and attempt to confirm or deny the pairing mechanism of the unconventional superconductor as p\-wave\.

**Project status**  
In progress

**Expected completion date**  
12/14/2012

[Progress Report](https://mpi-sciencecoop.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2012/10/Trisha_R_Sr2RuO6_Report.pdf)

## My Co\-op Experience at MPI

**Why did you apply for a placement with Max Planck Institute?**  
I wanted the experience of living abroad, particularly in Germany, and the MPI’s research is well\-known and respected\.

**What recommendations do you have for students who wish to participate in MPI\-UBC exchange program?**  
It’s a wonderful opportunity both academically and socially\.

**Tell us about your travels and cultural experiences in Germany and the rest of the Europe**  
It’s very easy and cheap to travel around Europe\. I’ve been to Lake Konstanz, Germany; Athens, Greece; Paris, France; and Cadarache, France \(where the ITER project is housed\)\.
