## Principal Investigator

-   [Dr\. Jiaying Zhao](https://zhaolab.psych.ubc.ca/assets/img/people/JZ.jpg)
    
    #### Dr\. Jiaying Zhao she/her, [CV](https://zhaolab.psych.ubc.ca/pdfs/Jiaying_Zhao_CV.pdf)
    
    Dr\. Jiaying Zhao \(aka JZ\) is a Professor in the Department of Psychology and the Institute for Resources, Environment and Sustainability at the University of British Columbia\. She is a leading behavioral scientist tackling critical financial and environmental challenges with behavioral solutions to promote sustainability\. JZ directs the Behavioral Sustainability Lab at UBC to design innovative, high\-impact behavioral solutions to address pollution, waste, and poverty\. She's also a UBC Sauder Distinguished Scholar, faculty affiliate with the Center for Effective Global Action at UC Berkeley, and an invited researcher at J\-PAL at MIT\. JZ completed her PhD in cognitive psychology at Princeton University, and recently received a King Charles III Coronation Medal, Killam Research Prize, and Canada Research Chair in Behavioral Sustainability\. JZ has advised dozens of governments, NGOs, and businesses, including the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, the OECD International Network on Financial Education, the US Social and Behavioral Sciences Team, and the Privy Council Office of Canada\.

## PhD Students

    #### Jade Radke
    
    Jade Radke is a PhD student in the Department of Psychology at UBC\. Her research focuses on the use of positive reinforcement to promote climate action and improve well\-being\. Jade holds an MA in Resources, Environment, and Sustainability from UBC, a BA in Honours Psychology, and a certificate in Behavioral Interventions \(BICA\) from MacEwan University\. Jade has been awarded a SSHRC Doctoral Fellowship along with UBC’s Four\-Year Doctoral and Indigenous Graduate Fellowships\. Apart from research, Jade has spent most of her life as a competitive dancer and has been teaching various styles of dance to all ages and levels for over ten years\.
