## About

In her role as the Program Advisor for the School of Social Work, Jaynus works with BSW, MSW, and PhD students and advises on academic and other student\-facing matters\. She is often the first point of contact for School of Social Work students and supports students from the initial admission process through to graduation\. She holds a BFA in Visual Arts \(UBC ’04\) and an MA in Art Education \(Concordia ’07\) and enjoys painting and beachcombing in her free time\.
