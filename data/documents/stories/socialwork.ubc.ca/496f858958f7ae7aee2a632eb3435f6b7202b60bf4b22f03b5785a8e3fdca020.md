## About

Barbara Lee is an Assistant Professor in the School of Social Work, Director of the [Centre for the Study of Services to Children and Families](http://www.csscf.ok.ubc.ca/), [Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization Scholar at the University of British Columbia](https://kx.ubc.ca/ubc-kxm-scholars), an academic affiliate of the [SIM Social Work Research Lab at Carleton University](https://carleton.ca/socialwork/simlab/), and an Affiliated Investigator at [Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute](https://www.vchri.ca/our-research)\.

**Ph\.D\.** University of Toronto  
Factor\-Inwentash Faculty of Social Work &amp; Ethnic and Pluralism Studies Collaborative Graduate Program

**M\.S\.W**\. University of Toronto  
Factor\-Inwentash Faculty of Social Work &amp; Ethnic and Pluralism Studies Collaborative Graduate Program

**B\.S\.W\.** University of Victoria  
School of Social Work – Child Welfare Specialization

**B\.A\.** Queen’s University  
Faculty of Arts – Psychology

Barbara Lee is the 2025 recipient of the UBC Dean of Arts Mentorship Award, recognizing her commitment to cultivating the next generation of social work scholars and practitioners\. She was also awarded the 2016 Council of Social Work Education \(CSWE\) Transforming Child Welfare Dissertation Award for her research entitled “Examining child welfare outcomes for Asian\-Canadian children and families: A mixed methods study”\.

Barbara’s research is informed by her direct practice experience as a youth worker for new immigrant youth, child and youth care counselor in residential group home settings, and as a frontline child protection worker providing intervention for children and youth who have experienced or at risk of child maltreatment\. She uses community\-engaged, program evaluation, administrative data, survey designs, and mixed methods approaches to examine social service processes and outcomes, and to promote evidence\-informed practices with children, youth, and families\.

As a Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization \(KxM\) scholar, Barbara builds university\-community partnerships to co\-create and mobilize knowledge that promotes social change\. In this pursuit, Barbara serves as the founding editor\-in\-chief for the open\-access e\-journal [Research and Evaluation in Child, Youth, and Family Services](https://ojs.library.ubc.ca/index.php/RECYFS) to encourage dialogue and advancing impactful research to promote positive change in child, youth, and family services\. Barbara is also the creator, producer and host of the [Knowledge Exchange: A Partnership\-in\-Action Podcast](https://blogs.ubc.ca/swkx/) that highlights inspiring community\-engaged partnerships in social work\.

About *keyboard\_arrow\_down*

Barbara Lee is an Assistant Professor in the School of Social Work, Director of the [Centre for the Study of Services to Children and Families](http://www.csscf.ok.ubc.ca/), [Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization Scholar at the University of British Columbia](https://kx.ubc.ca/ubc-kxm-scholars), an academic affiliate of the [SIM Social Work Research Lab at Carleton University](https://carleton.ca/socialwork/simlab/), and an Affiliated Investigator at [Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute](https://www.vchri.ca/our-research)\.

**B\.S\.W\.** University of Victoria  
School of Social Work – Child Welfare Specialization

**B\.A\.** Queen’s University  
Faculty of Arts – Psychology

Barbara Lee is the 2025 recipient of the UBC Dean of Arts Mentorship Award, recognizing her commitment to cultivating the next generation of social work scholars and practitioners\. She was also awarded the 2016 Council of Social Work Education \(CSWE\) Transforming Child Welfare Dissertation Award for her research entitled “Examining child welfare outcomes for Asian\-Canadian children and families: A mixed methods study”\.

Barbara’s research is informed by her direct practice experience as a youth worker for new immigrant youth, child and youth care counselor in residential group home settings, and as a frontline child protection worker providing intervention for children and youth who have experienced or at risk of child maltreatment\. She uses community\-engaged, program evaluation, administrative data, survey designs, and mixed methods approaches to examine social service processes and outcomes, and to promote evidence\-informed practices with children, youth, and families\.

As a Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization \(KxM\) scholar, Barbara builds university\-community partnerships to co\-create and mobilize knowledge that promotes social change\. In this pursuit, Barbara serves as the founding editor\-in\-chief for the open\-access e\-journal [Research and Evaluation in Child, Youth, and Family Services](https://ojs.library.ubc.ca/index.php/RECYFS) to encourage dialogue and advancing impactful research to promote positive change in child, youth, and family services\. Barbara is also the creator, producer and host of the [Knowledge Exchange: A Partnership\-in\-Action Podcast](https://blogs.ubc.ca/swkx/) that highlights inspiring community\-engaged partnerships in social work\.
