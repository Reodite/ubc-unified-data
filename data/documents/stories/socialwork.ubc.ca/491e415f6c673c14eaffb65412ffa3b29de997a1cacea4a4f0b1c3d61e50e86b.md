## About

Laura Earle is a doctoral student at The University of British Columbia School of Social Work\. Her doctoral research centres the experiences of people who have accessed counselling for complex trauma and their insights into the responses of mental health professionals\. Laura’s research area reflects questions that surfaced during client discussions over her 20 years of practice experience\. She carries with her valuing of client voices and embodied experiences from clinical practice into her research approach\. Alongside her academic pursuits Laura continues her private clinical practice\.

Laura’s clinical social work practice focuses on various forms of stress responses, including acute, complex, relational and developmental trauma, and the interplay of systemic influences with these experiences\. Laura has extensive training in a range of therapeutic modalities with an emphasis on experiential and somatic therapies, including sandplay and other forms of play therapy, trauma sensitive yoga, advanced EMDR, internal family systems therapy and sensorimotor psychotherapy\.

Throughout her clinical pursuits, Laura has enjoyed supervising Bachelor and Masters level social work and clinical psychology students, and postgraduate clinical registry applicants for the Alberta College of Social Workers\. Laura has shared her expertise on trauma\-focused clinical practices to professionals, community members and organizations\. She has also provided consultation on trauma sensitive yoga and clinical ethics for a University of Lethbridge research study on the relationship of cancer prevention and trauma\.

Laura earned her Masters of Social Work from Dalhousie University and her Bachelor of Social Work from The University of British Columbia\. She is a Registered Clinical Social Worker with the Alberta College of Social Workers and Registered Social Worker with the British Columbia College of Social Workers\. Prior to entering private practice, Laura held a number of positions in community, school and non\-profit settings where she provided a range of services from prevention and early intervention to interpersonal and sexual violence survivor counselling\.
