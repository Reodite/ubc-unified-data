## About

Fareed Mojab is a 3\[rd\] year PhD student\. He is fascinated with research topics at the intersection of psychology, philosophy and social work \(e\.g\., normal versus abnormal\)\. He currently works as a psychotherapist\.

---

## Research

**Research Interests**

-   Mental health, ADHD, developmental psychology, attachment and bioecological theories
-   Demographic focus: immigrant and Muslim communities
