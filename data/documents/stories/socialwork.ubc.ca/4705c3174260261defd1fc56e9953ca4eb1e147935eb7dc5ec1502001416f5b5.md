## About

Michelle is the Educational Services Coordinator for Continuing Education and Certificate Programs\. She is overseeing the development of a new Post\-Baccalaureate Certificate, and supports the administration and program development of the school’s growing continuing education and certificate offerings\. Michelle holds an MLIS and a PhD from UBC’s School of Information, where she is also a Sessional Instructor\.
