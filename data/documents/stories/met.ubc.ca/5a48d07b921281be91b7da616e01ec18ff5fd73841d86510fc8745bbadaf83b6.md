### Faculty Liaison, UBC Centre for Teaching, Learning, and Technology

### MET Instructor

Email: [sunah\.cho@ubc\.ca](mailto:sunah.cho@ubc.ca)

MET Courses: [ETEC 500](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/courses/etec-500/), [ETEC 512](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/courses/etec-512/)

Web / Social: [https://sunahcho\.wixsite\.com/home](https://sunahcho.wixsite.com/home)

“I have built a service\-focused career at UBC as a Faculty Liaison in the [Centre for Teaching, Learning, and Technology](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/) \(CTLT\), where I strengthen partnerships with the [Sauder School of Business](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/) and support innovation in pedagogy and learning design\.

With expertise in educational consulting, learning design, and project management, I collaborate with faculty to integrate technology into courses, curricula, and programs, enhancing teaching and learning across face\-to\-face, blended, and online environments\.

My research interests include student engagement, personalized learning, universal design for learning \(UDL\), social network analysis, and designing online assessments\. I am particularly interested in enriching and advancing the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\)\. My ultimate goal is to advance UBC’s pedagogical innovation through flexible curricula and adaptable technologies\.”
