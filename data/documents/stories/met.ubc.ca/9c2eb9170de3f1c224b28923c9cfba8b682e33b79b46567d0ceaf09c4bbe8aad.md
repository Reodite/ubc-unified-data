MET faculty, including course authors and instructors, work on the leading edge of curriculum design and technology integration, digital culture, youth culture, digital literacy, gaming, and interface design\. Our management and support team offers comprehensive administrative support to current and prospective students\. Below you’ll find a list of faculty and staff associated with the MET program\.
