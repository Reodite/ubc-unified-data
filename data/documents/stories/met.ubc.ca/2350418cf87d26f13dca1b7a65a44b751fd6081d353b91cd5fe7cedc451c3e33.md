### Professor, Department of Language &amp; Literacy Education

### MET Faculty

Email: [teresa\.dobson@ubc\.ca](mailto:teresa.dobson@ubc.ca)

Web / Social: [http://blogs\.ubc\.ca/tmdobson/](http://blogs.ubc.ca/tmdobson/)

“My research for the past two decades has focused primarily on the production of knowledge and its reception in digital environs\. I employ collaborative, digital humanities approaches in developing experimental reading interfaces and in studying reader experience of such interfaces\. I am a founding member of the [Implementing New Knowledge Environments](http://inke.ca/projects/tools-a) \(INKE\) project\. With INKE’s Interface Design team, I have contributed to the development of a series of prototypes for scholarly use\.

I am also a literacy and literary education researcher interested in learner engagement in multimodal environments, GPS\-enabled interfaces for place\-based writing and reading, and teaching literature for social justice\. I have been active in the MET program since its inception in 2001, first as a member of the inter\-departmental committee that formulated the initial MET curriculum, and since then as a member of, variously, the MET Advisory, Management, and Executive Committees\.

I co\-authored and taught \(with Jeff Miller\) one of the first two courses offered through the MET Program, [*Text Technologies: The Changing Spaces of Reading and Writing* \(ETEC 540\)](https://drive.google.com/open?id=16hzoy9bT9PNMiUYJIraO59CTunfwYIb4NARJSOBDxJM), which won the BC Technology Users Group Innovation Award in 2004 for early implementation of social media in online education\.”

Dr\. Dobson  also served as MET Director from 2018\-2021 and again in 2022\-2023\.
