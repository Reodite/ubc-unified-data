### Associate Professor, Language &amp; Literacy Education

### Associate Professor, [First Nations and Endangered Languages Program](https://fnel.arts.ubc.ca/) in the [Institute for Critical Indigenous Studies](https://cis.arts.ubc.ca/) \(Faculty of Arts\)

Email: [candace\.galla@ubc\.ca](mailto:candace.galla@ubc.ca)

MET Courses: [ETEC 521](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/courses/etec-521/)

Web: [http://www\.candacekgalla\.com/](http://www.candacekgalla.com/)

“Aloha kāua\! He Kanaka Hawaiʻi au\. ʻO Hawaiʻi kuʻu one hānau\. Raised in a sugar plantation town, Pāhala in Kaʻū, I was exposed to a diversity of languages and cultures from a young age – some of which had influence on Hawaiian Creole English \(also referred to as Hawaiian Pidgin\)\. Although ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi \(Hawaiian\) was not spoken in my home, I engaged in Hawaiian cultural practices – specifically hula\. Through hula I began to learn ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi, which is the foundation of hula and without it cannot exist\. I continued to learn Hawaiian language and culture formally at [Kamehameha Schools](https://www.ksbe.edu/) Kapālama\. Upon graduation, I attended the [University of Arizona](https://www.arizona.edu/) on the original homelands of the Tohono O’odham and Pascua Yaqui nations where I received a B\.A\. in Linguistics, an M\.A\. in Native American Linguistics and a Ph\.D\. in Language, Reading and Culture\. While I resided in Tucson, I was the Program Coordinator of the [American Indian Language Development Institute](https://aildi.arizona.edu/) \(AILDI\) – the program that reignited my passion back into language renewal and inspired my research on Indigenous language revitalization and technology\. I returned to Hawaiʻi and taught in [Ka Haka ʻUla O Keʻelikōlani College of Hawaiian Language](http://www.olelo.hawaii.edu/khuok/) at the University of Hawaiʻi Hilo where I taught linguistics and Indigenous language education courses\. As an Associate Professor in the department of [Language and Literacy Education](https://lled.educ.ubc.ca/) \(Faculty of Education\) and the [First Nations and Endangered Languages Program](https://fnel.arts.ubc.ca/) in the [Institute for Critical Indigenous Studies](https://cis.arts.ubc.ca/) \(Faculty of Arts\) at the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/), I am grateful for the opportunity to learn and teach on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓ speaking [Musqueam](https://www.musqueam.bc.ca/) people\.”
