**Michelle Stack,** PhD, is an associate professor, former senior policy advisor, and public commentator on education**\.** Her research interests include equity and education, media education, representations of mental health and illness, the role of media in the policymaking process, and community and youth engagement\.

She is the author of a book on higher education rankings, titled *Global University Ranking and the Mediatization of Higher Education* and is currently editing a second book on rankings, journal impact factors, and inequity in higher education\.

Michelle has led numerous courses and workshops focused on building the capacity of scholars and scholar\-practitioners to engage media to expand research informed public conversations and policy debates\.
