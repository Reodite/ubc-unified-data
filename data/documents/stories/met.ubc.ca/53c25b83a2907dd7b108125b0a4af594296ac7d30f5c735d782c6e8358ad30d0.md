### MET Instructor

Email: [tatiana\.bourlova@ubc\.ca](mailto:tatiana.bourlova@ubc.ca)

MET Courses: [ETEC 510](https://met-sb.sites.olt.ubc.ca/etec-510/ "ETEC 520: Design of Technology Supported Learning Environments (core)"), [ETEC 524](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/courses/etec-524/)

Tatiana Bourlova, Ph\.D\., is an online instructor and experienced researcher specializing in instructional design methods and the societal dimensions of educational technologies\. With two decades of experience in the MET program \(2005\-2025\), including teaching online ETEC 520, ETEC 524, and ETEC 510, her expertise spans areas such as strategies for the institutional implementation of educational technologies, digital modelling, and innovations in instructional design and curriculum development\.

Tatiana excels in developing innovative learning solutions, demonstrated by her consulting role on the award\-winning *The Law Project*, which promoted critical engagement with complex legal and social issues in BC through online movie creation\. Her leadership as Director at WWDPI involved strategic curriculum development and professional growth initiatives\. She contributed to the Health Information Management Group’s implementation of electronic record systems within Vancouver Coastal Health\.

As an Educational Technologist and UDL Specialist at the Centre for Teaching Learning and Research \(Vancouver Community College\), Tatiana designed online learning experiences, graphic course layouts, and digital resources, including the programs on Anti\-racist Facilitation and Digital Literacy for Educators, contributing to the college\-wide strategy for educational technologies implementation\.
