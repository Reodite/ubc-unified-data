## Cultural safety

Drew St\. Laurent, Director of Operations, and Dr\. Patricia Spittal, Associate Director, Research, from the [Centre for Excellence in Indigenous Health](https://health.indigenous.ubc.ca/), discuss what is cultural safety, why it’s important and some of the ways that UBC is training doctors and health professionals to provide quality, culturally\-safe care\.
