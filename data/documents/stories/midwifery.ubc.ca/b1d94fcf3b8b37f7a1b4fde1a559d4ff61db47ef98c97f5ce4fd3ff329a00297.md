The UBC Midwifery Program is proud to spotlight **Echo Sulin**, a member of the Ulkatcho First Nation and second\-year student, who has been awarded the [Chief Joe Mathias Scholarship](https://chiefjoemathias.ca/) \($5,000\) and the [Vancouver Foundation First Nations Scholarship](https://health.indigenous.ubc.ca/students/awards/) \($10,000\)\. 

We spoke with Echo about what these awards mean to her, her journey into midwifery, and her hopes for the future\. 

*Photos courtesy of Echo Sulin\.*

[Young smiling Indigenous woman sitting on a log on a beach\.](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/photo-1-720x960.jpg)

## “*I hold a sense of pride in receiving academic awards for Indigenous students\. I accept with intention on behalf of the hard work I have put into my studies, but also with mindfulness and gratitude for my parents and ancestors who have paved the path before me\.*“

**Congratulations\! How did you feel when you learned you had received both scholarships?** 

I feel incredibly honoured to be selected for these awards\. Receiving both awards was definitely a surprise, and I feel recognized for the dedication and effort I have put into this education thus far\. 

**What does receiving these awards mean to you personally and professionally?** 

I hold a sense of pride in receiving academic awards for Indigenous students\. I accept with intention on behalf of the hard work I have put into my studies, but also with mindfulness and gratitude for my parents and ancestors who have paved the path before me\. These awards also relieve financial stress, and this allows me to put more time and effort into my studies and wellbeing\. I moved to Vancouver specifically for this program, and the elevated cost of living within this city has not gone unnoticed during the past year\.

**What are you most excited about in your upcoming year of learning and clinical experiences?** 

I am so excited to work in my first few clinical placements of this degree and to be able to practice so many of the learnings I have received\. I am also looking forward to learning one\-on\-one with my preceptors\. 

**Do you have any words of advice for other students applying for scholarships and awards?** 

My words of advice are to simply apply to any and all awards you qualify for, even if you don’t think you will receive it — you may surprise yourself\! 

We congratulate Echo on this well\-deserved recognition and look forward to her continued contributions to midwifery and Indigenous health\.
