### Support Services

MSL IT Support is here to provide technical resources, services, and support to help everyone in the MSL work smarter, faster, and safer\. From helping professors collaborate on research, to helping people get the software they need to work, to helping protect your crucial work related data, we are part of the information technology backbone that supports the university\.

### Location

Our office is located in room 227 in the second floor office block\.

### Hours Of Operation

Operating hours are between 8:30am to 4:30pm, Monday to Friday\.

### Responsible Use Of Information Technology

Accessing all UBC networks and information systems require agreement to UBC Policies SC11 and SC14 prior to access\. Please ensure that you have reviewed these policies to understand your rights, limitations, and liabilities\.
