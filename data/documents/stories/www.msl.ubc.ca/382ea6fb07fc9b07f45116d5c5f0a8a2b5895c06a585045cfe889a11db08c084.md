The Centre for High\-Throughput Biology \(CHiBi\) merges with the Michael Smith Laboratories in October 2015\. This merge introduced seven researchers to the Michael Smith Laboratories’ faculty including Drs\. [Leonard Foster](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-leonard-foster/), [Jörg Gsponer](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-joerg-gsponer/), [Carl Hansen](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-carl-hansen/), [Martin Hirst](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-martin-hirst/), [Thibault Mayor](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-thibault-mayor/), [Paul Pavlidis](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-paul-pavlidis/), and [Stephen Withers](https://www.chem.ubc.ca/stephen-withers)\.
