The Biotechnology Laboratory appoints Dr\. [Doug Kilburn](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-doug-kilburn/) as the new Director\. He played an integral role in developing the associate member program and built key relationships with the genome sciences centre\. Well\-recognized and highly\-reputable, Dr\. Kilburn succeeded Dr\. Michael Smith as the Director of the Biotechnology Laboratory\.
