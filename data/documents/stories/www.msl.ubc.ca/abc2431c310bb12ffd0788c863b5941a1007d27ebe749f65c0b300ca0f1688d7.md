[Professor Wilfred Jefferies](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-wilfred-jefferies/)’ innovative strategies and outstanding inventions that enable cancer immunotherapies and vaccines have been recognized with his induction as a Fellow of the [National Academy of Inventors](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/professor-wilfred-jefferies-becomes-the-first-canadian-immunologist-to-be-elected-as-a-fellow-of-the-national-academy-of-inventors/) \(NAI\)\.  Election as a Fellow of the NAI is the highest professional distinction accorded solely to eminent academic inventors\. Jefferies is the first Canadian immunologist to be inducted into the NAI\.
