[Dr\. Xin Tang](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-xin-tang/) joins the MSL as an Assistant Professor in the field of Computational Cell Biology, holding a joint appointment with the Department of Computer Science\. His [research program](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/new-faculty-member-dr-xin-tang-brings-expertise-in-decoding-the-complexity-of-cells-using-ai/) focuses on the integration of artificial intelligence \(AI\) with single\-cell biology, using AI to better understand and interpret cellular processes\. This work can have various biomedical applications, such as the prediction of individual patient response to treatment, allowing individuals with disabilities to control computers or communicate through thought, and promoting healthy aging\.
