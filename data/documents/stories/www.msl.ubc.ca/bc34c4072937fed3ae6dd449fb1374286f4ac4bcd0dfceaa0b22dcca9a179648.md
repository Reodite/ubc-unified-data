Dr\. [Charles Haynes](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-charles-haynes/), a chemical engineer, was recruited to the Biotechnology Laboratory in 1993\. The central objective of Dr\. Haynes’ research program is to improve the understanding of the interfacial behavior of biomolecules and cellular systems and to use this fundamental knowledge to invent new technologies and instruments\.
