AbCellera Biologics Inc\., a [spin\-out company from the Michael Smith Laboratories](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/msl-research-group-spins-out-an-industry-leading-biotechnology-company/), is leading the industry in next\-generation antibody therapies using new and innovative technology developed at UBC\. Led by Dr\. [Carl Hansen](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-carl-hansen/), AbCellera is becoming one of the fastest\-growing biotech companies in BC\.
