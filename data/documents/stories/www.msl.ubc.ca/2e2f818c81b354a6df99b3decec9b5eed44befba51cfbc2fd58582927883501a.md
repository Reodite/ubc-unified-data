Dr\. [Sabrina Leslie](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-sabrina-leslie/) is awarded a [2021 Killam Accelerator Research Fellowship](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/sabrina-leslie-awarded-2021-killam-accelerator-research-fellowship/)\. The Killam Accelerator Research Fellowships recognize and support early career researchers with innovative, impactful research projects, propelling them forward to the next stage\. The award supports the Leslie lab in expanding the applications and impact of their Convex Lens\-Induced Confinement \(CLiC\) platform, which allows researchers to observe the behaviours of individual molecules\. Leveraging the data collected through this platform, the lab hopes to inform the development of more effective and personalized medicines to improve health outcomes in the future\.
