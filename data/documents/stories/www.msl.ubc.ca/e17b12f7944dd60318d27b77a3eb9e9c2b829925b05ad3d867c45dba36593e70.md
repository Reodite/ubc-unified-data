In 1987, UBC made a bold investment by establishing Canada’s first interdisciplinary biotechnology unit\. Dr\. [Michael Smith](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/about-us/#dr-michael-smith) was named the founding Director of the Biotechnology Laboratory where he recruited some of the brightest minds\. This unit quickly became a resource for the entire university, encouraging biotechnology advancements across the campus and in the wider scientific community\.
