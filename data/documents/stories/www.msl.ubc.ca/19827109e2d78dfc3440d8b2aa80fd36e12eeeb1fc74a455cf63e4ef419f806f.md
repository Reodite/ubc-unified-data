Dr\. [Stephen Withers](https://www.chem.ubc.ca/stephen-withers) and collaborators have [identified a new, more powerful group of enzymes](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/gut-bacteria-could-provide-key-to-making-universal-blood-type/) that can turn any type of blood into the universally usable type O, expanding the pool of potential blood donors and making blood matching safer and easier\. The [results of this study](https://www.acs.org/content/acs/en/pressroom/newsreleases/2018/august/gut-bacteria-provide-key-to-making-universal-blood-video.html) were presented at the National Meeting &amp; Exposition of the Americal Chemical Society \(ACS\) in Boston, MA\.
