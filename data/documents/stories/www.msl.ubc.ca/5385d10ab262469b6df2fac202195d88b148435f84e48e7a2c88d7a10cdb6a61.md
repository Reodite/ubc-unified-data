Since the emergence of the COVID\-19 pandemic, the work of MSL faculty has been instrumental in understan ding and combat ting the virus\. Much of the progress made through variant sequencing, vaccine development and life–saving treatments have roots in research carried out within the within the MSL\. Highlights of the significant contributions made to the global effort to curb the pandemic include: 

**March 2020**  [AbCellera and Eli Lilly to Co\-develop Antibody Therapies for the Treatment of COVID\-19](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/abcellera-and-lilly-to-co-develop-antibody-therapies-for-the-treatment-of-covid-19/) 

**April 2020**  
[Drs Wilfred Jefferies, Leonard Foster and team secure MSFHR funding to develop potential COVID\-19 vaccines via the Novel COVID\-19 Vaccine Initiative](https://covid19.research.ubc.ca/research/novel-covid-19-vaccine-initiative) 

**May 2020** [Dr\. Martin Hirst, Thibault Mayor and other UBC researchers collaborate to address the COVID\-19 testing challenge](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/how-b-c-scientists-are-addressing-the-covid-19-testing-challenge/) 

**June 2020**  [Dr\. Terry Snutch Leads Canadian Researchers in Sequencing Covid\-19 Virus Genome](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/dr-terry-snutch-leads-canadian-researchers-in-sequencing-covid-19-virus-genome/) 

**October 2020**  
[Vaccine expert Dr\. Anna Blakney joins Team Halo, an initiative started by the United Nations and Vaccine Confidence Project, which connects scientists and clinicians working on COVID\-19 to the general public over TikTok](https://blakneylab.msl.ubc.ca/scicomm/) 

**December 2020** [Adjunct Professor Carl Hansesn’s company AbCellera makes waves with successful IPO](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/adjunct-professor-carl-hansen-featured-in-media-for-successful-abcellera-ipo/) 

[Dr\. Martin Hirst’s lab pilots new COVID\-19 testing and cleaning protocols](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/hirst-lab-and-partners-pilot-new-covid-19-testing-and-cleaning-protocols-for-use-in-educational-spaces/) 

**April 2021** [Dr\. Leonard Foster speaks on how cloud computing support accelerates COVID\-19 vaccine improvements](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/cloud-computing-support-accelerates-covid-19-vaccine-improvements/) 

[Dr\. Wilfred Jefferies collaborates with Eyam Vaccines and Immunotherapeutics on COVID\-19 vaccine pre\-clinical trials](https://www.vancouverisawesome.com/coronavirus-covid-19-local-news/covid-19-canadian-company-testing-vaccine-candidates-that-target-variant-strains-3628843)
