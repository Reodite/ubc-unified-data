Bleeding is a major cause of preventable death in surgery and on the battlefield\. CoMotion Drug Delivery Systems, a spin\-off from Dr\. [Christian Kastrup](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-christian-kastrup/)‘s lab, was created to help manage this bleeding and prevent future deaths\. The company is making great strides and has already licensed the patent application for their research\. In addition, CoMotion Drug Delivery Systems is performing collaborative research with major players in trauma care\.
