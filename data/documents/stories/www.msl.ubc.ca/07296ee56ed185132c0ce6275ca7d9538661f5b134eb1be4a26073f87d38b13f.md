Dr\. [Terry Snutch](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-terry-snutch/) was inducted into the National Academy of Inventors at a ceremony at the John F\. Kennedy Presidential Library and Museum on April 6, 2017\. A rare honour, he is one of only three researchers in Canada inducted into the most recent cohort of Fellows\. Dr\. Snutch’s statement of induction notes the accolade is “in recognition of exceptional accomplishments in innovation and invention to the benefit of society\.”
