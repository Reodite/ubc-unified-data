In 2000, Dr\. [Jörg Bohlmann](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-jorg-bohlmann/) joins the Biotechnology Laboratory\. Dr\. Bohlmann’s research involves the molecular biochemistry and biology of plant secondary metabolites, genomics of conifers, grapevines, and various medicinal plants, and plant defense against insects\.
