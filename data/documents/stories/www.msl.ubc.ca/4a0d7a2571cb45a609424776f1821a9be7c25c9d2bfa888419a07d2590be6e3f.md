The first in situ analysis of red blood cell concentrate was conducted by Dr\. [Robin Turner](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-robin-turner/), in collaboration with Dr\. Michael Blades and Dr\. Dana Devine\. They used Raman spectroscopy to assess fitness of the blood cells for transfusion\. This [non\-invasive method](https://doi.org/10.1039/C5AN02461G) was outlined in a paper published in *Analyst\.*
