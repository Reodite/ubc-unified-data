Dr\. [Brett Finlay](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-brett-finlay/) and collaborators have developed the first animal model for childhood malnutrition\. This model replicates the imbalance of gut bacteria associated with the difficult\-to\-treat disease since malnutrition includes a variety of environmental factors\. Their [findings](http://www.nature.com/ncomms/2015/150804/ncomms8806/abs/ncomms8806.html) were published in *Nature Communications\.*
