In 2001, a Royal Society of Canada expert panel, chaired by Drs\. [Brian Ellis](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-brian-ellis/) and Conrad Brunk, published *[Elements of Precaution: Recommendations for the Regulation of Food Biotechnology in Canada](https://rsc-src.ca/en/elements-precaution-recommendations-for-regulation-food-biotechnology-in-canada)\.* This report was prepared by the Royal Society of Canada at the request of Health Canada Canadian Food Inspection Agency and Environment Canada\.
