Blood platelets have one main job: Stop bleeding by forming clots\. Sometimes, however, these tiny cell fragments fail when they are needed most – when a person is experiencing massive bleeding, usually due to trauma\. Dr\. [Christian Kastrup](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-christian-kastrup/) developed a potential strategy for endowing platelets with extra powers so they can rise to the occasion and continue coagulation\. If it’s proven to work in clinical situations, such “superplatelets” might become a standard part of emergency department supplies, along with bandages, oxygen, and saline\. The [research](https://doi.org/10.1111/jth.14006) was described in the *Journal of Thrombosis and Haemostasis*\.
