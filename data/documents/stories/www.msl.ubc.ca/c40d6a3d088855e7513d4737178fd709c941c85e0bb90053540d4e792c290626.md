Canadian and Swedish scientists today released genome sequences of two of the most economically important forest trees in the world\. Dr\. [Jörg Bohlmann](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-jorg-bohlmann/) collaborated on this project to decode the spruce genome, Canada’s most important forest tree\. This [research](https://rdcu.be/bCxPP) was published in *Nature\.*
