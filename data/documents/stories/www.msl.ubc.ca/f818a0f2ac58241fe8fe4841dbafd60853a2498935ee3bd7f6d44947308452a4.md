Dr\. [Brett Finlay](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-brett-finlay/) co\-authors, [*Let Them Eat Dirt*](https://science.ubc.ca/news/why-your-kids-should-play-dirt), a book describing why dirt can be a child’s best friend\. Dr\. Finlay uses the latest research to highlight the critical role bacteria play in growing bodies\. The book offers tips on how parents can ensure their kids get exposed to plenty of healthy bugs\.
