Dr\. [Brett Finlay](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-brett-finlay/) co\-authors [*The Microbiome Master Key*](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/how-to-hack-your-microbiome-for-lifelong-health/), teaming up once more with his daughter to explore the unexpected ways that microbes influence the body\. The book dives into the latest microbiome research, dispels common myths, and provides science\-backed tips for leveraging the microbiome to promote healthy living and aging\.
