Researchers from the Biotechnology Laboratory have developed the first high\-performance fiber\-optic probes suitable for long\-term use in pulsed ultraviolet resonance Raman spectroscopy applications in the deep\- UV \(DUV, 205\-250 nm\)\. The [results](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.37.000170) were published in a paper in *Applied Optics*\. Faculty members, Drs\. [Robin Turner](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-robin-turner/) and [Charles Haynes](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-charles-haynes/), were involved in this project\.
