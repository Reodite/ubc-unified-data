With the Biotechnology Laboratory officially open, Dr\. Michael Smith made it his first priority to recruit some of the finest up\-and\-coming scientists and engineers to UBC\. The first professional appointments to the Biotechnology Laboratory from 1988\-1989 included Drs\. [Brett Finlay](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-brett-finlay/), [Wilfred Jefferies](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-wilfred-jefferies/), [Jim Kronstad](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-jim-kronstad/), [James Piret](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-james-piret/), [Terry Snutch](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-terry-snutch/), [Robin Turner](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-robin-turner/), John Carlson, and Louise Glass\. Academic appointments span across five faculties including Science, Medicine, Applied Science, Forestry, and Land and Food Systems, emphasizing a cross\-disciplinary approach to the full range of exciting biotechnology issues\.
