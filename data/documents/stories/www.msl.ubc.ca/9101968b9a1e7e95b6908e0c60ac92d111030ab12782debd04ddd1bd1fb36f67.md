SonoSep Technologies Inc\., a spin\-out company from Dr\. [James Piret](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-james-piret/)‘s lab and collaborators, developed a new cell separation device\. Particulate material suspended in a fluid could be separated and recycled using the device’s ultrasonic resonance wave\. This [research](https://www.nature.com/articles/nbt0394-281.pdf) was outlined in *Nature Biotechnology*\.
