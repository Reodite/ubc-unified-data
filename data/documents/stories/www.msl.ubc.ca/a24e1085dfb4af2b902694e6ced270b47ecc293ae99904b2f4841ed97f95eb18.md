Dr\. [Robin Turner](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-robin-turner/) and collaborators developed Process Analytical Technologies \(PAT\) based on Raman spectroscopy\. Raman spectroscopy has many practical attributes that make it a promising PAT, such as its ability to be used nondestructively on live cells and is receptive to automated operation\.
