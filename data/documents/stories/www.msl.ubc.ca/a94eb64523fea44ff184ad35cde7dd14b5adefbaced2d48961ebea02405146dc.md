Canada Research Chairs improve our depth of knowledge and quality of life, strengthen Canada’s international competitiveness, and help train the next generation of highly\-skilled people through student supervision, teaching and the coordination of other researchers’ work\. On November 13, 2018, it was announced that Dr\. [Peter Zandstra](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-peter-zandstra/) was awarded the CIHR Tier 1, Canada Research Chair in Stem Cell Bioengineering\.
