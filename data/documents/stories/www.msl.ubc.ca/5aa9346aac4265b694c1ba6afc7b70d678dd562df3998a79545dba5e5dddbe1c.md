The Biotechnology Laboratory welcomes Dr\. [Xin Li](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-xin-li/), an expert in plant immunity\. Dr\. Li’s lab uses a combination of molecular genetics, biochemical, and genomics to understand the natural resistance mechanisms of plants against microbial pathogens including fungi, bacteria, and viruses\.
