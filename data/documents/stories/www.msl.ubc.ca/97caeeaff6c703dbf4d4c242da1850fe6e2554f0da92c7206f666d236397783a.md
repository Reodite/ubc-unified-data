In 1993, Dr\. [Michael Smith](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/about-us/#dr-michael-smith) received the [Nobel Prize in Chemistry](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1993/summary/) for his research on site\-directed mutagenesis, a technique where a specific DNA sequence could be altered in a targeted manner\. Smith shared the Prize with Dr\. Kary Mullis for his invention of the PCR method\. Smith’s research opened the door to entirely new fields such as genetic engineering and synthetic biology\.
