[Two MSL faculty members are awarded 2025 Killam Prizes](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/killam-prizes-in-health-sciences-and-engineering-awarded-to-msl-faculty-members/) by the Killam Trusts\. [Dr\. Marco Marra](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-marco-marra/) receives the Killam Prize in Health Sciences, and [Dr\. Peter Zandstra](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-peter-zandstra/) receives the Killam Prize in Engineering\. Among Canada’s most distinguished research awards, the Killam Prizes recognize “active Canadian scholars who have distinguished themselves through sustained research excellence, making a significant impact in their respective fields\.”

Dr\. Marra is recognized for how he has “driven the expansion of genomics in Canada”, leading research on the study of cancer cell mutations to open the door to personalized cancer treatment\.

Dr\. Zandstra is celebrated for being a “globally recognized leader in cell therapy bioprocess engineering”, focusing on stem cell bioengineering and regenerative medicine for the development of therapies to treat immune disorders and cancers\.
