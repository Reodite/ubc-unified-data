The Biotechnology Laboratory welcomes Dr\. [David Ng](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-dave-ng/) as the Director of the [Advanced Molecular Biology Laboratory](http://www.bioteach.ubc.ca/) \(outreach and science education component\)\. Dr\. Ng is a geneticist, science educator, science literacy academic, and faculty member\. His current research interests involve concepts of science and creativity, as well as game\-based learning of science concepts\.
