Dr\. [Christian Kastrup](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-christian-kastrup/) and his lab have created a supercharged platelet that might be capable of healing major wounds, busting clots or blocking inflammation\. Unlike most cells, platelets lack a nucleus and do not have DNA\. The Kastrup lab discovered a method to inject the platelets with DNA and other ingredients needed to make RNA, creating the first platelets ever to have synthetic nuclei \(RNA\)\. This [research](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201506500) was published in the journal *Angewandte Chemie International Edition*
