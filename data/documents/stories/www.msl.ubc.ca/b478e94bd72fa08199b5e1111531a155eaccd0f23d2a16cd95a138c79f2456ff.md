[Dr\. Brett Finlay](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/celebrating-the-career-and-retirement-of-dr-brett-finlay/) was one of the first faculty members recruited by Dr\. Michael Smith to the MSL \(then the Biotechnology Laboratory\), joining the lab in 1989\. Dr\. Finlay became an internationally recognized leader in the field of host\-microbe interactions, and was celebrated with a number of significant awards and accolades for his research and mentorship throughout his career\.

Dr\. Charles Haynes joined the MSL just a few years later, in 1993\. Dr\. Haynes is a widely\-recognized expert in downstream processing of biologic therapeutics, studying the behaviour of biomolecular and cellular systems for the development and manufacturing of diagnostics and therapeutics\. Dr\. Haynes was also celebrated with numerous prestigious awards and accolades during his time at UBC\.
