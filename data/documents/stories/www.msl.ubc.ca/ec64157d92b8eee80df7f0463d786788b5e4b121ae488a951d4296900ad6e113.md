In 1997, Dr\. [Phil Hieter](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-phil-hieter/) joins the Biotechnology Laboratory\. The Hieter lab develops and applies genetic and biochemical methodologies in the model organism, *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* \(bakers yeast\), to better understand the molecular components required for chromosome transmission\. The overarching goal of the Hieter lab is to relate the work with yeast to human cancer\.
