Dr\. [Charles Haynes](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-charles-haynes/) and collaborators have developed and patented a method for improved DNA sequencing\. The technology removes a former bottleneck in high‐throughput DNA sequencing and has enabled major projects such as the [Human Genome Sequencing program](https://science.sciencemag.org/content/291/5507/1304) to be completed faster and cheaper than originally planned\.
