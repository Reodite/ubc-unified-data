Today’s business students live and learn in a world undergoing significant environmental, technological, political and societal changes\. Post\-secondary institutions are responsible for embracing modern global developments to prepare students for the evolving role of finance in society today\. By incorporating ESG considerations, fintech solutions and climate finance into experiential learning models, UBC Sauder is preparing students to be high\-functioning financial experts in today’s modern world\.
