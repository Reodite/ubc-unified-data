On this week’s faculty spotlight, we are featuring Camara Van Breemen\. Read here to learn more about her favourite past time \(which happens to be a lot of people’s favourite lately\) and take a peak at her cute dog\!

**1\. Site\(s\):**  
Canuck Place Children’s Hospice, British Columbia Children’s Hospice, and Home visiting – lower mainland \(and sometimes to other health authorities to see Kids on our program\)\.

**2\. What’s the best part about your job?**   
Community Care – keeping kids at home for supported end of life care\.  It’s a privilege to get to know the children and their parents and work as a team to improve quality of life for them no matter what part of the journey they are on\.

**3\. Favourite song:**  
So many but I currently love listening to newest Gillian Welsch album Woodland

**4\. Favourite past\-time activity:**  
Yes – I’m going to say it – PICKLEBALL

**5\. Favourite restaurant in your community:** D  
Depends on my mood – Just had a great meal at Vij’s

**6\. Favourite travel destination:**  
Philippines and India

**7\. A book or article you would recommend:**  
I just listened to an inspirational  episode on Peacemakers podcast called **Affectionate Awe and Radical Kinship: Transforming lives at Homeboy industries\.**

**8\. What are you creating in the world right now?**  
My new garden of flowers and shrubs……\.\.looking forward to spring and summer\.

**9\. Best piece of advice for new physicians:**  
Get to know something new \(not illness related\) about your patient \(or their caregiver\) every time do a consult\.
