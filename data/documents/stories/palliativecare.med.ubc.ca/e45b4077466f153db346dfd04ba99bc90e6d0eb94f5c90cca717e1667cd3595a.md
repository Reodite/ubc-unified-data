Teaching in India

**Site:**  
Canuck Place Children’s Hospice

**What’s the best part about your job?**  
Working with supportive, innovative and dedicated colleagues who are constantly thinking outside the box to provide patient and family centered care\.

**Favourite song:**

Don’t Stop Believin’ by Journey

**Favourite past\-time activity:**

Traveling, biking, diving\.

[Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/06/biking-on-vancouver-island-225x300.jpg)

Biking in Vancouver

**Favourite restaurant in your community:  
**Sashimiya

**A book or article you would recommend:**

Deep: Freediving, Renegade Science, and What the Ocean Tells Us about Ourselves by James Nestor\.

**What are you creating in the world right now?**

Along with some colleagues at Canuck Place, we are working with Two Worlds Cancer Collaboration and Sunflower Children’s Network to raise awareness, educate and work with local physicians to create pediatric palliative care programs in South East Asian\.

[Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/06/Diving-in-Okinawa-360x270.jpg)

Diving in Okinawa

**Best piece of advice for new physicians:**

You will often run into patients and colleagues with whom you disagree\. This can be

Stressful and can be made easier by taking time to reflect and trying to understand the issue

from the other person’s perspective or point of view\.
