Dr\. Lauren Daley is a dedicated and insightful member of our faculty, known for her commitment to excellence in both clinical care and education\. She brings a wealth of experience and a compassionate approach to her work\. Her enthusiasm for teaching and mentorship significantly enriches the learning environment for both students and colleagues\.

1.  **Site**:  
    St\. Paul’s Hospital, St\. John Hospice and Mt\. St Joseph Hospital\.
2.  **What’s the best part about your job?**  
    Getting to experience the full breadth of the human experience \(big ups and big downs\) each and every day\.
3.  **Favourite song:**  
    Fast Car by Tracy Chapman\.
4.  **Favourite past\-time activity:**   
    Beach walks with my 95 lb lap dog, Marvin\.
5.  **Favourite restaurant in your community:**   
    Impossible to choose just one\! Kissa Tanto for a fancier night out\. Disco Cheetah for takeout\. Batard bakery for coffee and croissants\.
6.  **A book or article you would recommend:**  
    The Year of Magical Thinking by Joan Didion\.
7.  **What are you creating in the world right now?**  
    These days my time is mostly spent trying to keep a tiny human alive so not a lot of time for creativity\!
8.  **Best piece of advice for new physicians:  
    **Setting boundaries is the key to real self\-care\.
