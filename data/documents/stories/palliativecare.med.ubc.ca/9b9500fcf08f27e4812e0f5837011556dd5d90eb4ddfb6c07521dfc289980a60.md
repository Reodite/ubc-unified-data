This week, we are proud to shine our Faculty Spotlight on Dr\. Manisha Sharma, a dedicated educator, researcher, and mentor whose work continues to inspire both students and colleagues alike\.

 **1\. Site:**  
Lionsgate Hospital on the North Shore\.

**2\. What’s the best part about your job?**  
Undoubtedly the culture: the docs, allied health, nurses, social work, have welcomed me with open arms, also…SO MANY treats\!\!

**3\. Favorite song:**  
This is an impossible question\.  Recently found myself in an Etta James sort of mood \(At last, Sunday kind of love\)\.  But my heart is with 50s/60s Bollywood,  Mohammad Rafi, Kishore Kumar classics\.

**4\. Favorite past\- time activity:**  
Forest walks in lighthouse park or along the spirit trail in Ambleside\.

**5\. Favorite restaurant in your community:**  
Tamarind hill in North Vancouver\.

**6\. Favourite travel destination:**  
Jaipur, any beach in Goa, tea estates in the hills of Kerela

**7\. A book or article you would recommend:**  
Anything by Allan Watts\.  Also, recently fell in love with Robin Wall Kimmerer’s book, Braiding Sweetgrass\.  You see the natural world with different eyes after reading her books\.

**8\. What are you creating in the world right now?**  
A new life in our new home\!  Still finding our feet in this place 4000 KM from where I was born and raised\.

**9\. Best piece of advice for new physicians?**  
Go back and read your old med school admissions essay\.  Make sure you protect some semblance of the wide\-eyed, naive, enthusiastic idealist you once were\.  We are so, SO fortunate to be physicians, remain mindful and grateful for that opportunity in some way every day\.
