Dr\. Armon Molavi is a distinguished member of our faculty, celebrated for his dedication to advancing medical practice and education\. Dr\. Molavi combines deep clinical expertise with a passion for teaching and mentorship\.

1.  **Site:**  
    Nanaimo
2.  **What’s the best part about your job?**  
    The privilege of having such varied work in palliative care, addiction medicine, and longitudinal family practice, and the unique perspectives each discipline lends to the others\.
3.  **Favourite past\-time activity:**To name only a few — board game nights, tennis, soccer\.
4.  **Favourite restaurant in your community:**  
    Nori Sushi
5.  **A book or article you would recommend:**  
    Nonviolent Communication by Dr\. Marshall Rosenberg
