The pictures in the Kaska Language Course Scrapbook are from the 2002 UBC Kaska Language course\. Classes were held at the school in Ross River, at a camp in MacMillan Pass at the NWT border, and at Frances Lake in southeastern Yukon\. Twenty\-five students participated in the 2002 UBC Kaska language course\. In addition to Josephine Acklack and Pat Moore, the UBC instructors for the course, twelve Kaska elders provided extensive teachings about Kaska language and culture\.
