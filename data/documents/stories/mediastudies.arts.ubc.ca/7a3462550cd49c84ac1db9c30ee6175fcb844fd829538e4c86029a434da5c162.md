## About

**Dr\. Jillian Lerner** specializes in nineteenth\-century visual culture, with an interest in print, photography, and technologies of seeing\. Along with articles in *Grey Room*, *History of Photography*, *Oxford Art Journal*, and *The Art Bulletin*, she is the author of two books, *Graphic Culture: Illustration and Artistic Enterprise in Paris, 1830\-1848* \(2018\) and *Experimental Self\-Portraits in Early French Photography* \(2021\)\.

Jillian Lerner currently teaches **ARTH 227: What is Modern Art?** and **ARTH 480: Visual and Digital Media Theory** in the BMS program\.
