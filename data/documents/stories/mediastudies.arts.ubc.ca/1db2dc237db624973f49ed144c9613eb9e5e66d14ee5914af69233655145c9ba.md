## About

Vicente Regis is the new BMS Program Coordinator\. Father of two teenagers, he brings a background in educational programming, community engagement, and interdisciplinary collaboration\. Vicente previously worked as a Program Specialist at the UBC Institute for the Oceans and Fisheries, where he coordinated and developed curriculum for international and professional education initiatives, including the Vancouver Summer Program, micro\-credential courses, and internship\-based learning opportunities\.

Vicente holds a Master of Arts in Media and Technology Studies in Education from UBC and is currently a PhD candidate in Curriculum Studies\. His work is grounded in creating inclusive, engaging learning environments that connect students, faculty, and communities\. Earlier in his career, Vicente worked with the University Neighbourhoods Association, developing community programs focused on safety, belonging, and intercultural connection\. He also has a background as a music educator and events coordinator, experiences that continue to shape his approach to program design, collaboration, and creative engagement\.
