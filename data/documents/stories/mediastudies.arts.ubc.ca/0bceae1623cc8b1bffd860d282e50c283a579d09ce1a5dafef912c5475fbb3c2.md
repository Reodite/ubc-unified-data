## About

Having been a storyteller for nearly 20 years, Jennifer Moss has worked in theatre, print journalism, radio, new media, and interactive media production – writing stories that primarily focuses on arts, local issues, and character\-driven stories\. She is interested in cross\-genre and cross\-departmental collaboration, and in continually exploring how emerging technologies open new doorways for writers\.

Jennifer Moss currently teaches **CRWR 302: Writing for Podcast** and **CRWR 312: Interactive Storytelling** in the BMS program\.
