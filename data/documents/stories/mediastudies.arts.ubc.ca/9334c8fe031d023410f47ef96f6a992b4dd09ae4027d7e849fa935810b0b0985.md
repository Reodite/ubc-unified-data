## About

With research interests that primarily focuses on masculinities in American film &amp; television, business histories of the home entertainment industry, and attention\-capture through interface design &amp; interaction peripherals, Brent Strang’s most recent publication is “Armchair Harmonics: Radio Remote Controls and the Historical Persistence of Push\-Buttons” published in *The Routledge Companion to Media Technology and Obsolescence* \(2018\)\. 

Brent Strang currently teaches **FIST 100: Introduction to Film Studies** in the BMS and the Media Studies stream of the Coordinated Arts Program\.
