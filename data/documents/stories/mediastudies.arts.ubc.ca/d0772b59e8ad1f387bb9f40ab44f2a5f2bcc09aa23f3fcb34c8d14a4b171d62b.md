## About

Timothy is the Media Studies program’s Digital Media Specialist\. He facilitates the production of cutting\-edge, high quality digital media projects by Media Studies students\. A lifelong creator, he has cultivated technical skills in a broad range of professional digital technologies and techniques\. His artistic practice spans photographic and interactive media, physical computing, sculptural light, and music production\.

Previously, he worked in UBC’s Department of Art History, Visual Art &amp; Theory, where he created the AHVA New Media Lab\. He holds a Master’s Degree in Digital Media, and a Bachelor of Arts in Cognitive Systems\.
