## About

T’ai Smith’s research, teaching, and advising focuses on the areas of modern and contemporary European and American art and design, visual studies, critical theory, and media theory\. She focuses on the politics of mediation, gender, labor, and economy\.

T’ai Smith currently teaches **ARTH 480: Visual and Digital Media Theory** in the BMS program\.
