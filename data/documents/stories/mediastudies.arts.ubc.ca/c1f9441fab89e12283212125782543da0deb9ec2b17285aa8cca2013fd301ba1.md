## About

Alexandra Peña is the Community Engagement Coordinator for the Bachelor of Media Studies \(BMS\)\. Her background spans community engagement, policy, research and program delivery across post\-secondary education, government, and non\-profit organizations\.

Prior to joining BMS, Alexandra worked with the First Nations Education Steering Committee and the Indigenous Adult and Higher Learning Association, where she contributed to provincial and federal initiatives, funding opportunities, and projects centred on the experiences and priorities of First Nations learners and institutes across British Columbia\. She has also worked with the UBC Arts Co\-op office supporting student programming, and with the Government of British Columbia supporting policy and First Nations engagement\.

Alexandra is excited to return to UBC, where she earned her Bachelor of Arts in International Relations and Political Science\. In her role with BMS, she focuses on strengthening connections between the program and community, and creating meaningful opportunities for students, faculty, and community partners to work together\.

Outside of work, Alexandra can usually be found dancing salsa or trying a new sweet treat\!
