## About

A scholar and poet with interests in contemporary North American poetics, improvisation and social aesthetics, intersections of music, text and media, intermediality and the poetics of listening, Kevin’s work investigates the intersections of music, text and media\.

Kevin McNeilly currently teaches **ENGL 332: Approaches to Media History** in the BMS program\.
