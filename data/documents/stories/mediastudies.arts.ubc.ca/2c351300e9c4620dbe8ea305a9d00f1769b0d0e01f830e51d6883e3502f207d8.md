## About

Jodie Leung is a BMS alumna and holds a Master of Management from UBC Sauder School of Business\. Her areas of interest include media theory, emerging technology development, and the intersection of business strategy and the media industries\. As an interdisciplinary scholar, her teaching portfolio spans media production, applications of artificial intelligence in team\-based environments, Human\-Computer Interaction design, and agile project management\.

Jodie currently teaches ***MDIA 290: Collaborative Media Methods***\.
