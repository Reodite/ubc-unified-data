## About

Christine D’Onofrio has been teaching in the BMS program since its inception in 2014\. As an artist researcher and educator, she works in analogue and digital lens based media, interactive media, printmaking, sculpture, book works, and installation\.

Christine currently teaches **MDIA 150: Media Communities** in the BMS program, and sits as the Chair for the BMS program\.
