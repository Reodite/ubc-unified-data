## About

Ernest researches the receptions of what can broadly be called ‘alternative cinema’ – cult film, genre cinema, independent films, David Cronenberg, and European horror films\. He has also written on *The Lord of the Rings,* Belgian Cinema, the reality TV series *Big Brother*, the literature of Thomas Pynchon, the art of Joseph Beuys, and on theories of mimesis\.
