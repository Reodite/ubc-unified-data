## About

Co\-Founder of the Bachelor of Media Studies Program, Richard Cavell is an English professor whose research interests in media theory and Canadian studies finds common ground in his publications on foundational media theorist Marshall McLuhan\.

Richard Cavell currently teaches **ENGL 232: Approaches to Media Studies** in the BMS program\.
