## Dr\. Kiran K\. Soma, Director, Soma Lab

Kiran K\. Soma is a Professor at the University of British Columbia in Vancouver, Canada and a member of the [Psychology Department](https://psych.ubc.ca/) and [Djavad Mowafaghian Centre for Brain Health](https://www.centreforbrainhealth.ca/soma-kiran)\. Dr\. Soma joined the faculty at UBC in 2004\. He received his BA from Stanford University, his PhD from the University of Washington, and his postdoctoral training at UCLA\. He has published [over 150 papers](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=K32vd7sAAAAJ) on neural circuits, hormones, behaviour, and immune function, and these papers have received [over 10,000 citations](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=K32vd7sAAAAJ&amp;hl=en&amp;oi=ao)\.
