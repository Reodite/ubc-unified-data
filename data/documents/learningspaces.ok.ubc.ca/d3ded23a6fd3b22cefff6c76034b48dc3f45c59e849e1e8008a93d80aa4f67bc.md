## Have questions for us or just want to let us know about something?

Get in touch and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible\.

-   For Canvas, Kaltura, or Collaborate Ultra support, contact the [Centre for Teaching and Learning](https://ctl.ok.ubc.ca/) at 250\-807\-9293 \(79293 from on campus\)
-   For information on booking a classroom, please visit the [UBC Okanagan Bookspace](https://bookspace.ok.ubc.ca/) page
-   If you need general Audio Visual support, or to book an orientation session, please submit a request using the [UBC Self\-Service Portal](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice)
-   Urgent, time\-sensitive support is available during regular office hours\. Contact UBCO IT at 250\-807\-9000 \(79000 from on campus\)
