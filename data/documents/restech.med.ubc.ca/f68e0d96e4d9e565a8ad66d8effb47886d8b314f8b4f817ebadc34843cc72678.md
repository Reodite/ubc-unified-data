## **Purpose**  

The BC Centre for Disease Control \(BCCDC\) developed a COVID\-19 consent registry of patients who have recovered or are recovering from COVID\-19, and have consented to be contacted about research initiatives related to COVID\-19\. The purpose of the consent registry is to support research aimed at improving care for those previously infected with COVID\-19 and to discover strategies for managing the epidemic in the future\.   

[https://med\-fom\-researchtech\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2021/11/project\-1\-image\.jpg](https://med-fom-researchtech.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/11/project-1-image.jpg)[Covid\-19 consent to contact registry database](https://med-fom-researchtech.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/11/project-1-image-940x707.jpg)

## **Our Involvement**  

In collaboration with the BCCDC, we developed and will maintain this consent registry\. REDCap is being used for participant self\-registration, and the registry is currently being stored and managed on Oracle APEX\.
