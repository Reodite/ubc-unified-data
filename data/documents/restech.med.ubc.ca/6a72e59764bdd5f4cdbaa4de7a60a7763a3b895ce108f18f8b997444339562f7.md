## **Project Overview**

The BC Centre for Disease Control \(BCCDC\) developed a COVID\-19 consent registry of patients who have recovered or are recovering from COVID\-19, and have consented to be contacted about research initiatives related to COVID\-19\. The purpose of the consent registry is to support research aimed at improving care for those previously infected with COVID\-19 and to discover strategies for managing the epidemic in the future\.
