## **Project Overview**

The Canadian Pulmonary Hypertension Registry \(CPHR\) and Canadian Pediatric Pulmonary Hypertension Registry \(CPPHR\) currently involves a total of 15 active sites across Canada\.  The CPHR/CPPHR aims to improve the care of Canadians with PH and the knowledge of this rare disease through standardized recording of PH patient characteristics, treatments, diagnosis modalities, and outcomes\.

The registry will serve as a quality improvement tool, allowing investigators and clinicians to verify that patients are receiving standard of care in Canadian pulmonary hypertension centers\.  It will be a resource to answer focused research questions and quality improvement questions\.
