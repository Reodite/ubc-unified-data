## **Project Overview**

Active Steps is a virtual physical activity intervention for children with type 1 diabetes living in the BC Interior\. Over 12 weeks, children and their parents take part one\-on\-one Zoom sessions with a physical activity counsellor\. Each 45\-minute session is guided by a workbook filled with age\-appropriate learning materials and interactive activities\. Children also set individual physical activity goals, which they track using a provided Fitbit Versa 4\.

## **What Makes This Project Special**

A customized Fitbit API integration, developed by our team, automatically pulls participant activity data into REDCap daily, requiring no action from users\.
