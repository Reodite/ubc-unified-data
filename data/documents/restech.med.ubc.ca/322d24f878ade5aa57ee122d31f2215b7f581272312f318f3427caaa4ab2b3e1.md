## Spinal Cord Injury Blood Biomarker Longitudinal Evaluation \(SCRIBBLE\)

This single\-centre study at Vancouver General Hospital aims to enroll 100 patients with an acute traumatic spinal cord injury \(SCI\) and a control group of 20 patients who have suffered a spine trauma but are neurologically intact\.

[Read more  ›](https://restech.med.ubc.ca/spinal-cord-injury-blood-biomarker-longitudinal-evaluation/)
