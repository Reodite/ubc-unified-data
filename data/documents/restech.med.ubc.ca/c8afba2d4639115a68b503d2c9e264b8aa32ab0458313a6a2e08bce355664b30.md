Assess your IT needs

## Service

We are always available to engage and help assess your IT needs so that you can make an informed decision when including IT costs in your grant applications\.  

## These grants include, but are not limited to:  

-   Canadian Institutes of Health Research \(CIHR\) 
-   Michael Smith Foundation for Health Research \(MSFHR\) 
-   Canada Foundation for Innovation \(CFI\), in collaboration with UBC ARC
-   National Institutes of Health \(NIH\)
-   Genome BC / Genome Canada
-   Various charities &amp; hospital foundations  

For IT grant consultation requests, please complete the [Systems intake form](https://rc.med.ubc.ca/redcap/surveys/?s=8P7W4PYAPCTYJJLE) or [contact us](https://med-fom-researchtech.sites.olt.ubc.ca/contact-us/)\.
