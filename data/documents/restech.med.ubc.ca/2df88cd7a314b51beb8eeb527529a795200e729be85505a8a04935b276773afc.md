## **Project Overview**

The Global Hip Dysplasia Registry \(GHDR\) project is led by the Hippy Lab at BC Children’s Hospital, with Dr\. Kishore Mulpuri and Dr\. Emily Schaeffer as lead investigators\. The Hippy team leads and coordinates several prospective global research registries in pediatric orthopaedics\. The size and scope of these registries involves upwards of 30 centres across 8 countries and 5 continents, with thousands of patients enrolled worldwide\. GHDR is an global pediatric research registry that follows children with developmental dysplasia of the hip \(DDH\), a malformation of the hip socket causing hip dislocation\. The goals of this registry are to develop a better understanding of DDH, including risk factors, screening, or surveillance of the hip, and different treatment approaches such as bracing and surgery\.

## **What Makes This Project Special:**

Supported by our team since its inception, GHDR is built on REDCap and now evolving with AMBRA integration for imaging and custom Power BI dashboards for advanced reporting and visualization\.
