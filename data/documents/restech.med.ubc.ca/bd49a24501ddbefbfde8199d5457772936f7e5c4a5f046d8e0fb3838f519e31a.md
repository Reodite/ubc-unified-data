**SurveyMonkey Apply is a workflow management platform used to collect, review, and manage submissions for grants, applications, and other programs\. It **centralizes data****, **simplifies submission tracking, and automates workflows\.**

## What can I use it for?

-   Manage the progress of submissions and generate detailed reports for single programs
-   Manage multi\-tiered review processes for complex programs or grants
-   Streamline workflows with automated review and approval processes
