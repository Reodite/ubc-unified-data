## Service

The systems administration service provides server administration expertise for new research projects, services, and application hosting\. Our systems administration team is trained to ensure your research systems are compliant with current UBC cybersecurity standards\.

## Possible uses include:

-   Hosting a website to promote your research project 
-   Hosting a PACS \(Picture Archiving and Communication System\) to store electronic images 
-   Hosting a database to store research data collected from microscopes
