## Electronic Data Collection with REDCap

[Image](https://med-fom-researchtech.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/redcap-1.png)

**Research Electronic Data Capture \(REDCap\) is a secure and easy\-to\-use web application for building and managing online surveys and databases\.**

It is specifically designed to collect research data, though it can be used for any type of data collection\. No background knowledge or technical experience is needed to use REDCap, giving researchers the ability to directly control and manage their own projects\.

## What can I use it for?

-   Research registries 
-   Randomized clinical trials 
-   Quality Improvement \(QI\)/ Quality Assurance \(QA\) projects

## Getting Started

### Logging into FoM REDCap for the first time

#### With CWL credentials

1.  Go to [FoM REDCap](https://rc.med.ubc.ca/redcap/)
2.  Log in with your CWL credentials
    -   NOTE: You must have a [Standard Account or Guest Account](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/about-cwl#term-33-horizontal)
    -   If you do not, please contact your project administrator or a UBC Staff/ Faculty member on your team to sponsor your account\. Please refer to this page for information on [how to sponsor a guest](https://it.ubc.ca/services/accounts-passwords/campus-wide-login-cwl/how-sponsor-guest)\.

#### With PHSA/VCH/PHC/FH credentials

Provincial Health Service Authority \(PHSA\), Vancouver Coastal Health \(VCH\), Providence Health Care \(PHC\), and Fraser Health \(FH\) users can choose to login using their staff login\.

1.  Go to [FoM REDCap](https://rc.med.ubc.ca/redcap/)
2.  Log in with your PHSA/VCH/PHC/FH credentials
