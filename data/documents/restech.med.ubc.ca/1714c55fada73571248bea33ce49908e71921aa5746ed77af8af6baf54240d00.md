Research Projects

## Projects

The Data Management Team supports a wide range of research and operational initiatives within the Faculty of Medicine and Heath Authority partners\. Our projects are centered around data collection and insights through secure systems like REDCap, OpenSpecimen, SurveyMonkey Apply, and Power BI\. We collaborate closely with clinical and research teams to streamline data collection, build visual dashboards, and develop custom workflows that support evidence\-based decisions and high\-impact research\.

From longitudinal registries and quality improvement initiatives to training modules and web\-based reporting tools, each project reflects our commitment to responsible data stewardship, user\-centric design, and innovative solutions in health research\.
