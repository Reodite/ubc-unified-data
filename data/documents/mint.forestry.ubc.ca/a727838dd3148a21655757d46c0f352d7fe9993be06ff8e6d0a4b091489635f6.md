#### **4\. COM\-3200PROⅡ  Air Ion Counter**

[Image](https://mint.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/03/WechatIMG1028-300x231.png)

COM\-3200PROⅡ  Air Ion Counter is a handheld meter designed to measure ion density – the number of ion per cubic centimetre \(ion/cc\) in air\. The 2 million range is sufficient for almost all situations\.

It measures this number separately for positive and negative ions \(\+ and – ions are usually present simultaneously\), which is

-   Ideal for outdoor natural environment measurement, since a continuation 8\-time test is possible with the built\-in battery
-   The world range power supply in which overseas use is possible is used
-   The PC software of the data collection display with an expansion range corresponding to the network　 which can be used with an overseas personal computer is attached\.

#### **5\. Intelligent Health Assessment Pad**

[Image](https://mint.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/Health-Assessment-Pad-300x204.jpg)

An advanced health monitoring device designed to comprehensively evaluate various physiological aspects\. It offers:

-   Blood Pressure and Heart Rate Monitoring
-   Electrocardiogram \(ECG\) Monitoring &amp; Playback
-   Activity Tracking: Records step count, calorie expenditure, and distance traveled, aiding in monitoring daily physical activities\.
-   Sleep Monitoring: Tracks sleep patterns, offering insights into sleep quality and duration\.

This innovative pad is instrumental in comparing human physiological indicators before and after participating in forest therapy activities\. It enables comprehensive health assessment and facilitates the observation of changes in various health parameters, contributing to a better understanding of the effects of forest therapy on overall well\-being\.

#### **6\. MindWave Mobile 2: Brainwave Starter Kit**

[Image](https://mint.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/11/MindWave-StarterKit1.jpg)

MindWave Mobile 2: Brainwave Starter Kit is a device that monitors brainwave activity\. It helps us understand brain’s activity and offers some useful functions:

-   Brainwave Detection: It measures and records your brainwaves, presenting them in charts to help you understand your mental state\.
-   Attention and Relaxation Monitoring: By analyzing brainwaves, it shows your levels of attention and relaxation at different times, aiding in managing your mental state\.
-   Electroencephalogram \(EEG\) Monitoring: It records and plays back your EEG, allowing you to observe detailed brain activity\.
-   Versatile Applications: It can be used with various applications and devices, such as focus training, relaxation techniques, and interactive gaming\.

This device assists in deeper insights into the brain activity, making it practical for exploring focus, relaxation, and overall brain states\.

#### 7\. Salivary amylase tester 

 [https://mint\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2024/03/%E5%94%BE%E6%B6%B2%E4%BB%AA\.jpg](https://mint.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/%E5%94%BE%E6%B6%B2%E4%BB%AA.jpg)[Image](https://mint.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/%E5%94%BE%E6%B6%B2%E4%BB%AA-227x300.jpg)

Made in Japan, which uses the activity of salivary amylase in saliva as a basis for judgment of mood and stress\.

A salivary amylase tester can measure the activity of salivary amylase, with units in KIU/L\.

-   According to data from the Taiwan Forestry Research Institute:

“For normal adults, salivary amylase activity below 30 kIU/L indicates no stress; 31\-45 kIU/L indicates mild stress; 46\-60 kIU/L indicates moderate stress; and above 61 kIU/L indicates severe stress\.” Quoted from Taiwan Forestry Research Institute, “Forestry Research Newsletter” Issue 23, Volume 5, Page 19\.

-   Testing range: 10 to 200 kIU/L \(with a margin of error of plus or minus 20%\)\.
-   Reproducibility: 10% using temperature: 20°C to 30°C\.
