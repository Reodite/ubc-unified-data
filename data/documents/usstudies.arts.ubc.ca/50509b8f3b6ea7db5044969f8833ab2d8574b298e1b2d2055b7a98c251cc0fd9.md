The U\.S\. Studies Program offers an exciting interdisciplinary program especially through the collaboration of the departments of [Political Science](http://www.politics.ubc.ca/), [Economics](http://www.econ.ubc.ca/) and [History](http://www.history.ubc.ca/)\.The program provides undergraduates with the opportunity to combine course work on the U\.S\. across departments and gain an in\-depth understanding of U\.S politics, economics and history\. Students are able to take courses in areas as diverse as the U\.S\. presidency, African American history and American foreign policy\. The program will provide Canadian government, corporations and other institutions with graduates with an excellent understanding of the U\.S\. and Canada\-U\.S\. relations\.The program will allow graduates to better respond to employment opportunities in Canada and the U\.S\.
