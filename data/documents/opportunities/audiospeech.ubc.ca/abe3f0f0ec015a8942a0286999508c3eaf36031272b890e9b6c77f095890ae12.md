### SASS Clinical Faculty Awards

The School of Audiology and Speech Sciences is grateful for the support and contribution of all our Clinical Faculty\.

In 2009, the School established the Clinical Faculty Awards to recognize exceptional Clinical Faculty who demonstrate excellence in and commitment to the clinical education of our students\.

The Awards include the [Elizabeth MacLeod Award for Excellence in Clinical Education](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/clinical-faculty/awards/elizabeth-macleod-award-slp/) \(Speech\-Language Pathology\) and the [Noelle Lamb Award for Excellence in Clinical Education](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/clinical-faculty/awards/noelle-lamb-award-audiology/) \(Audiology\)\.

### Current Clinical Faculty Award Recipients

The **Elizabeth MacLeod Award for Excellence in Clinical Education \(Speech\-Language Pathology\)** for 2015 has been awarded jointly to **Alisa Ferdinandi** and **Caroline Marcoux**\. Alisa is a Clinical Instructor in our School and works as an SLP at Columbia Speech &amp; Language Services\. Caroline is a Clinical Instructor in our School and works as an SLP at G\.F\. Strong Rehabilitation Centre\. Both Alisa and Caroline are graduates of the UBC School of Audiology &amp; Speech Sciences\.

The **Noelle Lamb Award for Excellence in Clinical Education \(Audiology\)** for 2015 has been awarded to **Werner Roth**\. Werner is a Clinical Instructor in our School and works as an Audiologist at Miracle Ear Rockyview in Calgary\. From Brandon, Manitoba, Werner received his Audiology degree from Colorado State University\.

The faculty and administration of the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences are pleased to have the opportunity to recognize Ms\. Ferdinandi, Ms\. Marcoux, and Mr\. Roth for their many contributions to the School’s educational mission, and extend sincere congratulations to them all\.
