**Hey Everyone\! Do we have a show for you\!**

Join us on Sunday, November 30th for our 2025 Faraday Show\! This Free event is for all young scientists and their families who are keen to discover science\.

This year’s theme is: ***Expanding our Senses &amp; Movements with Tech\!*** We will explore questions such as: *What is it like to see beyond the dark like reindeer, or snakes? How can we sense invisible forces?* and *How can tech improve our balance?* 

All these questions and more will be answered through fun demonstrations and hands\-on activities\!

This show is proudly hosted by UBC students and faculty from the Department of Physics &amp; Astronomy\.

**Where**: [HEBB Theatre](https://www.maps.ubc.ca/?code=VPOI10439) 2045 East Mall, UBC Vancouver campus\. The [North Parkade](https://parking.ubc.ca/map) is the closest parking lot to the HEBB building\.

**When**: **SUNDAY, November 30th, 2025**

-   -   Science Fair: 1:00PM – 1:55PM
    -   Stage Show: 2:00PM – 3:00PM

**Cost: FREE\!** We ask that you please bring non\-perishable food items to support Greater Vancouver Food Bank member, [*The Kettle Society*](https://www.thekettle.ca/)\. **RSVP [HERE](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/faraday-show-2025-tickets-1854436713129?aff=oddtdtcreator)** so we know that you’re coming\! We recommend arriving 15\-20 minutes early for good seats\.

**What: The Faraday Show** is UBC’s annual science lecture, designed for children and all those who are ‘young at heart’\. It is presented by UBC Physics &amp; Astronomy students, faculty and staff\. Spread the word\! Download the poster and share this with your friends\! All are welcome 🙂

**\*Planning Tip\*** Plan a day on campus\! Mention “Faraday Show” a t the [Beaty Biodiversity Museum](https://beatymuseum.ubc.ca/) on Sunday November 30th, between 10am\-5pm and get 50% off admission\.
