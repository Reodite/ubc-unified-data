## 2016 Faraday Show: Good Vibrations\! The Physics of Sound, Music, and Concerts

**WHEN:** SUNDAY, December 11, 2016, 2:00\-3:30 PM

**WHERE:** [HEBB THEATRE, 2045 EAST MALL](http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?showMapCampus=y&amp;bldg1ID=656) \(UBC CAMPUS\)\. The North Parkade is the closest parking lot to the Hebb Theatre\. ***Hebb Theatre has limited capacity \(375 people\), so come early\!***

**COST : FREE\!** \(But bring non\-perishable food items to support the Greater Vancouver Food Bank\!\)  
No RSVP required, though we recommend arriving 15\-20 minutes earlier for good seats\.

[Image](https://phas-outreach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/11/Music_Faraday_Iconv3-300x300.png)The **Faraday Show** is UBC’s annual science lecture, designed for children, presented by UBC Physics &amp; Astronomy\. **This year this show will be full of special effects, music performances, and fun physics\!** We can hear sound and music at concerts – can we actually see sound and vibrations? What do they look like? How do different instruments work, and how can we make music as loud as possible? And, can you create lights of different colors on stage? We will explore these questions, and more, through fun demos and hands\-on activities\. **This show is for children of ALL AGES, and adults who are young at heart\!**

**MAKE IT A FULL DAY ON UBC CAMPUS\!** Mention “Faraday Show” at the Beaty Biodiversity Museum to get 50% off admission 10am – 5pm on December 11\.

For more information please contact the outreach program at: outreach@phas\.ubc\.ca / 604\-822\-3675\.
