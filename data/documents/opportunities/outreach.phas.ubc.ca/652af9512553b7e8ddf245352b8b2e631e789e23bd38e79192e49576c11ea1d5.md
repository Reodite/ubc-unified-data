### What is the Youth Group Program?

Welcome to our Youth Group Outreach page\! We mentor and train UBC physics &amp; astronomy student volunteers who then bring physics lessons to your group meetings\. Visits include a physics or science lesson with a build and/or take\-away component\. Visits are coordinated and confirmed via our registration form, below and through communications with our outreach coordinator\. Volunteers participate in training during the Fall term and are available for visits starting in Winter term \(January – April, 2027\)\. 

See below for more information about lessons and how to register your group for this program\.

**\*Please Note**: We do our best to fulfill as many visit requests as we can, which depend upon volunteer availability, the time/date in the academic calendar and the proximity of your group \(\*Note that most of our volunteers use public transit from the UBC area\)\. We may not be able to accommodate all requests\. If your group meets outside of the Lower Mainland area, we invite you to bring your group to UBC campus, where we have an outreach classroom space available\.

**\*\*Fall 2026**\-: UBC no longer has a Covid\-19 vaccine status mandate for students/staff/faculty\. If you have any questions or concerns please contact us at outreach@phas\.ubc\.ca\. 

### Lessons

We offer both free and for purchase lessons to groups\. For purchase lessons include electronics\.

| Lesson | Price per individual |
| --- | --- |
| LED flashlights \+ Diffusion glasses \(simple circuit\) | $5/individual |
| Homopolar Motors \(simple motor\) | $2/individual |
| Spaghetti Towers \(engineering challenge\) | FREE |
| Tumble Toy \(momentum\) | FREE |
| \*General physics and/or astronomy topic \[can be negotiated\] | FREE |
