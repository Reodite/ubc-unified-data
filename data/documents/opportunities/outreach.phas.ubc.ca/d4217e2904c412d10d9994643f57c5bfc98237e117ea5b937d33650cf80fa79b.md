### **Welcome to our Camp Descriptions page**\. 

We have created two years of teaching content with alternating years \(\*note that some key activities are the same – rockets, the treewalk tour, and swim sessions, for example, but the teaching concepts will vary by year\): this means campers can take the same camp two years in a row, with different lessons, concepts and activities\. As well, we bring in volunteer guest speakers from our undergraduate, graduate and Phd students who provide *unique lessons* each year for student learning\.

Browse our camps, below, to get a sense of what we offer\. Need more information? Read *[**About Our Camps**](https://outreach.phas.ubc.ca/about-our-camps/)* to see what we’re talking about\! Have any questions? Please check our [***FAQ page***](https://phas-outreach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/summer-camps/faq/ "FAQ") for more information, or contact us at camps@phas\.ubc\.ca / 604\-822\-0596\.

#### Fun with Physics Camp

**Age**: Going into Grades 2\-4 in the fall \(completing Grades 1\-3 by July 2026\)  
**Dates**: We are running four camps this year:

-   Fun with Physics 1 \(July 6\-10\) \*this camp is now full\*
-   Fun with Physics 2 \(July 13\-17\) \*this camp is now full\*
-   Fun with Physics 3 \(July 20\-24\) \*this camp is now full\*
-   Fun with Physics 4 \(July 27\-31\) \*this camp is now full\*

**Time**: 9am to 4pm  
**Price**: **$300 \| [Register](https://forms.phas.ubc.ca/physics-camps-registration/?uuid=01kpva1ps0fc98pchqw44zsv1n)**

This camp introduces students to topics such as light, electricity, force and motion through demonstrations and hands\-on activities\. Emphasis will be on physics in our everyday life\. Learn about speed, gravity, mass, momentum, flotation, solar cells and circuits\! Daily outdoor games, a pool session and a tree walk tour is also included\. The week ends with a physics show complete with ice\-cream made from liquid nitrogen\. [Back to Top](https://outreach.phas.ubc.ca/summer-camps/camp-descriptions/#top)

#### Advanced Physics Camp

**Age**: Going into Grades 5\-7 in the fall \(completing Grades 4\-6 by July 2026\)  
**Dates**: We are running two camps this year:

-   Advanced 1 \(July 20\-24\) \*this camp is now full\*
-   Advanced 2 \(July 27\-31\) \*this camp is now full\*

**Time:** 9am to 4pm  
**Price**: **$325 \|** **[Register](https://forms.phas.ubc.ca/physics-camps-registration/?uuid=01kpva1ps0fc98pchqw44zsv1n)**

This camp covers similar topics as Fun with Physics but with more advanced activities such as building a dimmable tricolour LED flashlight and working with complex electrical circuits\. Students will learn about energy, acceleration, gravity and the concepts of pressure and motion through activities\. The week includes a pool session, a tree walk tour, daily outdoor games, a visit to UBC’s Engineering Physics robots lab \(\!\), and a fabulous physics show\. [Back to Top](https://outreach.phas.ubc.ca/summer-camps/camp-descriptions/#top)

#### Astronomy Camp

**Age**: Going into Grades 4\-6 in the fall \(completing Grades 3\-5 by July 2026\)  
**Dates**: We are running two camps this year:

-   Astronomy 1 \(July 6\-10\) \*this camp is now full\*
-   Astronomy 2 \(July 13\-17\) \*this camp is now full\*

**Time**: 9am to 4pm  
**Price**: **$325 \| [Register](https://forms.phas.ubc.ca/physics-camps-registration/?uuid=01kpva1ps0fc98pchqw44zsv1n)**

This camp is ideal for students interested in learning more about our universe\. Students will learn about the scale of our solar system, star elements, observe the sun using a solar telescope, and learn the requirements of good rockets and landers through hands\-on activities\. Expert astronomers from UBC’s Department of Physics &amp; Astronomy will talk about exciting new discoveries in astronomy\. The week includes a pool session and the physics show and ice cream party\. ### Camp Cancellation Policy

If you cancel your child’s reservation before two weeks prior to the start of the camp, we will refund your payment minus a $25 administrative fee\. We will not give any refund for cancellations after this time\.
