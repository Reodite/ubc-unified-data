### 2024 Faraday Show: Sunday, December 8th, 2024 in HEBB Theatre

\[Click on the poster below to enlarge\]

[https://phas\-outreach\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2024/10/11x17\_poster\_Oct112024\.png](https://phas-outreach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/10/11x17_poster_Oct112024.png)[Image](https://phas-outreach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/10/11x17_poster_Oct112024-194x300.png)

We are thrilled to announce the date for our **2024 Annual Faraday** **Show: on** **Sunday December 8th**, in\-person, at the UBC\-Vancouver campus\.

This year’s theme is: ***Physics in your House\!*** How does the best **fire alarm** work? Where does **static electricity** come from? Why do your windows **mist up** in the winter?

All these questions and more will be answered through fun demonstrations and hands\-on activities\!

We’re working hard on this show, recruiting students and coming up with the most incredible and gripping physics presentations and demonstrations we can pull out for this exciting, science\-inviting, invigorating physics children’s performance\!

**WHERE**: [HEBB Theatre](https://www.maps.ubc.ca/?code=VPOI10439) 2045 East Mall, UBC Vancouver campus\. The [North Parkade](https://parking.ubc.ca/map) is the closest parking lot to the HEBB building\.

**WHEN**: **SUNDAY, December 8th, 2024**

-   -   Pre\-Show \(table top demonstrations\): 1:15PM – 1:55PM
    -   Stage Show \(stage presentations\): 2:00PM – 3:00PM

**HOW MUCH: FREE\!** We ask that you please bring non\-perishable food items to support Greater Vancouver Food Bank member, *The Kettle Society*\. **RSVP [HERE](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/2024-faraday-science-show-for-kids-tickets-1048971751747?aff=oddtdtcreator)** so we know that you’re coming\! We recommend arriving 15\-20 minutes earlier for good seats\.

**WHAT: The Faraday Show** is UBC’s annual science lecture, designed for children and all those who are ‘young at heart’\. It is presented by UBC Physics &amp; Astronomy students, faculty and staff\. Spread the word\! Download the poster and share this with your friends\! All are welcome\!

**\*Planning Tip\!** Plan a day on campus\! Mention “Faraday Show” and get 50% off admission at the Beaty Biodiversity Museum on Sunday December 8th, between 10am\-5pm\!

**Questions?** Contact us at outreach@phas\.ubc\.ca, or tel: 604\.822\.0596\.
