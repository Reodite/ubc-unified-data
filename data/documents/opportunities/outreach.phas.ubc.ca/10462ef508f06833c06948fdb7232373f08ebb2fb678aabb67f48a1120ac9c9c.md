The 2023 Faraday show was all about the wonders of space\! How do rockets get into orbit? How big are our planets? How explosive is compressed air? Can you make a crater? What do black holes eat?

[Image](https://phas-outreach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/10/FaradayPoster_Final_2023-621x960.png)

We are thrilled to announce the date for our **2023 annual Faraday Show: Sunday December 3rd**, in\-person, at the UBC Vancouver campus HEBB Theatre\. 

Our students have started planning their demonstrations, and we’re in a flurry of building, visualizing, creating and making physics happen again in the most exciting ways possible, to give you the best show you’ve ever seen\!

**WHERE**: [HEBB Theatre](https://www.maps.ubc.ca/?code=VPOI10439) 2045 East Mall, UBC Vancouver campus\. The [North Parkade](https://parking.ubc.ca/map) is the closest parking lot to the HEBB building\.

**WHEN**: **SUNDAY, December 3rd, 2023**

-   Pre\-Show \(table top demonstrations\): 1:15PM – 1:55PM
-   Stage Show \(stage presentations\): 2:00PM – 3:00PM

**COST: FREE\!** We ask that you please bring non\-perishable food items to support Greater Vancouver Food Bank member, *The Kettle Society*\. **No RSVP required**, although we recommend arriving 15\-20 minutes earlier for good seats\.

**The Faraday Show** is UBC’s annual science lecture, designed for children and presented by UBC Physics &amp; Astronomy students, faculty and staff\. This year we are exploring the wonders of space\! How do **rockets** get into orbit? How big are our **planets**? How explosive is **compressed air**? Can you make a **crater**? What do **black holes** eat? All these questions and more will be answered through fun demos and hands\-on activities\.

All are welcome\!

**Questions?** Contact us at outreach@phas\.ubc\.ca, or tel: 604\.822\.0596\.
