**REGISTRATION NOW CLOSED**

###  THANK YOU FOR PARTICIPATING IN OUR CAMPS\!

July 20, 2026: All our remaining camps are full for this season\. Thank you to everyone who has registered in our camps – we hope that you are having the best camp experience ever\!

Registration for 2027 Summer will open next Spring\.

### Instructions

-   Please review [camp descriptions](https://phas-outreach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/summer-camps/camp-descriptions/ "Camp Descriptions") and [FAQ](https://phas-outreach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/summer-camps/faq/ "FAQ") before registering your child\.
-   We have made changes to our registration system and camp descriptions\. Please make sure to review camp descriptions and the registration form carefully before submitting your registration\. If you have any questions, please contact us at 604\-822\-0596 or [camps@phas\.ubc\.ca](mailto:camps@phas.ubc.ca)\. Thank you in advance for your patience\!
-   If the camp you are interested in registering in is full, please register your child for the waiting list – we do occasionally receive cancellations and will contact parents as soon as possible when a spot is available\. To make sure you receive information about camp opening early next year, consider [signing up for our newsletter](http://eepurl.com/g3ZQ5)\.
