The University of British Columbia is the proud host of ***Science Rendezvous***, an annual festival held across Canada showcasing the Art in Science and emphasizes science, technology, engineering, art, and math \(STEAM\) research and innovation\.

Save the date\! The UBC 2026 festival date will be posted next year\.

-   See here for the 2025 Science Rendezvous festival\! [Science Rendezvous \| UBC Science](https://science.ubc.ca/events/rendezvous)
-   See here for the 2024 Science Rendezvous festival\! [Science Rendezvous 2024 \| UBC Science](https://science.ubc.ca/events/science-rendezvous-2024)
-   See here for the 2023 Science Rendezvous festival\! [Science Rendezvous 2023 \| UBC Science – Faculty of Science at the University of British Columbia](https://science.ubc.ca/science-rendezvous-2023)

This event, which offers a variety of activities across UBC campus, is free for everyone\. All are welcome to join to win cool prizes, tour UBC, and of course, learn about all the amazing feats of science and engineering happening right here at home\!

For a complete list activities happening on UBC campus, [visit the UBC Science Rendezvous webpage for more information](https://www.sciencerendezvous.ca/event_sites/ubc/)\.
