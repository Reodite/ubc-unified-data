Skylight's Supper Series on science education provides a place for faculty members to participate in interactive seminars on teaching and learning issues presented by their colleagues\. Meet your peers, enjoy a light meal and refreshments, share your thoughts, and hear about the exciting progress our science community is making in learning and teaching\.

## Upcoming Events

Thank you for your interest in our Supper Series\! During the pandemic, we transitioned from in\-person events to virtual events and changed the name of this series to the [Online Teaching Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/online-teaching-series "Online Teaching Series")\. In 2023, we are returning to in\-person events and changing the name of the series again to the [Skylight Teaching Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/skylight-teaching-series "Skylight Teaching Series")\.

We hope to see you soon at one of our upcoming events\!
