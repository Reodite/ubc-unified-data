Skylight supports community\-building and professional development by organizing science education events\.

### [Skylight Teaching Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/skylight-teaching-series)

A series of interactive seminars where faculty members can present their work and discuss topics related to science education\. Join us to engage with your colleagues, share your ideas, enjoy light refreshments, and celebrate the advances our community is making in teaching and learning\.

[Learn more about the Skylight Teaching Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/skylight-teaching-series)

### [Lab Accessibility Speaker Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/lab-accessibility-speaker-series)

This speaker series features a variety of workshops, seminars, and panel discussions designed to strengthen community engagement, raise awareness of accessibility barriers in labs, and highlight the lived experiences of people with disabilities in STEM\.

[Learn more about the Lab Accessibility Speaker Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/lab-accessibility-speaker-series)

### [Science Education Open House](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/open-house)

An annual spring showcase of teaching and learning projects across the Faculty of Science with talks, a poster session, and workshops\.

[Learn more about the Open House](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/open-house)

### [STEM Education Research Exchange \(SERE\) Sessions](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/sere)

Regular cross\-disciplinary meetings with a focus on discipline\-based education research \(DBER\), Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\) research, and pedagogical practices in STEM at UBC's two main campuses\.

[Learn more about SERE](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/sere)

### [Special Events](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/special-events)

Skylight hosts a variety of special events and workshops designed to foster community and professional development in science education\.

[Learn more about our special events](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/special-events)
