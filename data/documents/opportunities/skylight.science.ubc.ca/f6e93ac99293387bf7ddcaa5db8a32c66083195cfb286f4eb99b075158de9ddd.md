Hosted by Skylight on Monday, April 9, 2018 in the Earth Sciences Building \(ESB\), 2207 Main Mall, UBC\.

## Morning Session *\(9:30am \- 10:30am, ESB 1013\)*

-   Introduction and highlights from teaching and learning in the Faculty of Science, Sara Harris \(Associate Dean, Academic\) and Gülnur Birol \(Director, Skylight\)
-   Acknowledgement of graduate students participating in CIRTL this past year
-   CWSEI Summary and Outcomes, Warren Code \(Associate Director, Skylight\)
-   Scaffolding Authentic Practice in Environmental Sciences Prompted by Community\-focused Videos, Tara Ivanochko \(EOAS and Director, Environmental Science\)

## Student Panel *\(10:45am \- 11:45am, ESB 1013\)*

-   Featuring upper\-year students in Science sharing experiences from their programs
