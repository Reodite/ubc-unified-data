## **Speaker Series**

Launched in March 2022, this invited speaker series featured talks from leaders in inclusive STEM education on topics related to their expertise and relevant to the UBC Science teaching and learning community\.

[Learn more about the Speaker Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/speaker-series/teaching-learning-science-through-lens-indigeneity-equity-diversity-and-inclusion "Speaker Series: Teaching &amp; Learning in Science through the Lens of Indigeneity, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion")

## **Online Teaching Series**

We began offering this series of virtual events in place of our in\-person Supper Series in 2020 in response to the pandemic\.

[Learn more about the Online Teaching Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/online-teaching-series "Online Teaching Series")

## **Supper Series**

Skylight's Supper Series on science education provided a place for faculty members to participate in interactive seminars on teaching and learning issues presented by their colleagues\. Faculty could meet their peers, enjoy a light meal and refreshments, share their thoughts, and hear about the exciting progress our science community has made in learning and teaching\.

[Learn more about the Supper Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/supperseries "Skylight Supper Series")
