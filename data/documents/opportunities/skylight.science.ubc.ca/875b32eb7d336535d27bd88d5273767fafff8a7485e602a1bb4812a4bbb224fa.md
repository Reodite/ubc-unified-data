Over the past few years, we've developed a new Computer Science course in which UBC CS alumni teach workshop\-style classes on the latest technologies used in industry\. The alumni speakers teach skills that are directly relevant to the field and are founded in best practices that have been shaped by real\-world experiences\. Students taking the course practice these skills on a full\-term group project, which are assessed by both faculty and other industry guests\. The alumni speakers also provide career advice and personal stories of their experiences, which give students insight into the paths that are open to them post\-graduation\. 

We will discuss our process in forming this course, including the value of providing students with this experience, and our lessons learned in incorporating alumni speakers into a course\.
