## **UBC\-Vancouver and Bulkley Valley Research Centre**

Dr\. Kira Hoffman is a fire ecologist studying wildfire behavior and Indigenous\-led fire stewardship\. Her Postdoctoral Fellowship at the University of British Columbia focuses on historic fire activity and wildfire resilience in northwest British Columbia\. Her previous research explored cultural burning’s ecological legacies and informs contemporary forest management, blending Indigenous knowledge with western science\.
