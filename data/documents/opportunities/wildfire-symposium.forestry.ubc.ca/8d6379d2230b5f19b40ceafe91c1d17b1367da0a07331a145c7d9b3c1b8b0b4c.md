Costs are incurred by the conference organizers for all cancellations and changes \(non\-refundable credit card processing fees and administrative fees\)\. **Cancellations Saturday May 18, onward:** No refunds for cancellations

We place high importance on the values of integrity, responsibility, equity, diversity and inclusion\. Our goal is to enable all participants to learn from others, grow our understanding, and find new ways to address, understand and implement solutions to complex wildfire problems\. We aim to maintain a positive, empowering, and inclusive environment that enables all participants to feel safe and valued when contributing to the Coexisting with Wildfire: Solutions Symposium\.  We request that all symposium attendees agree to adhering to these principles\.

If you are subjected to unacceptable behavior, notice that someone else is being subjected to unacceptable behavior, hear of any such incidents, or have any other concerns, please notify a symposium representative immediately and report the incident\. Means to report any occurrences of inappropriate behavior include talking to the symposium organizers, a session moderator, or anyone staffing the registration tables \(watch for green name tags\) or report your concerns by email to [wildfire\.symposium@ubc\.ca](mailto:wildfire.symposium@ubc.ca) and we will respond as soon as possible\.
