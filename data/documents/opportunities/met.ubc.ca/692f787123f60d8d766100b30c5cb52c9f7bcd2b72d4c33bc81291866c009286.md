Tuition is charged on a per\-course basis \(one MET course \= 3 credits\)\. Course materials and annual student fees are not included in the tuition fee\. Students in both the MET degree program and the Graduate certificate program pay the same tuition for ETEC courses\.

Tuition fees are in Canadian dollars, are reviewed annually by the UBC Board of Governors, and are subject to change\.

Current tuition fees for ETEC courses for domestic and international students in the MET program and graduate certificate, based on year of admission, are listed in the [UBC Calendar tuition fee section for Specialized Master’s Degrees](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?tree=14,266,773,1450#18018)\.

[https://met\.ubc\.ca/tuition\-estimator/](https://met.ubc.ca/tuition-estimator/)[Image](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/03/calculator-150x150.png)

### Calculate Fees for the Courses You Plan to Complete

For an estimate of the estimated tuition to be paid as you complete courses in the MET or Graduate Certificate program, use this [tuition estimator](https://met.ubc.ca/tuition-estimator/)\.

*Tuition payments can be made online, by mail, or through your Canadian financial institution\.*

### Other Student Fees

Students in the Master of Educational Technology program are also assessed a small Capital Improvement Fund fee and the Graduate Student Society fee, annually\. Graduate certificate students do not pay these fees\. See the UBC Calendar for [more information on student fees](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?tree=14,267,784,0)\.
