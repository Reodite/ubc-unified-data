Join our international online community of learners and discover how the informed use of digital media and technology can inspire, engage, and support learners and educators within and beyond classrooms, around the world\.

## Program  
Overview

Our fully online graduate program offers two internationally\-recognized credentials\. Graduates of the program follow a broad range of career paths in education and digital learning technologies leadership\.

[Learn More](https://met.ubc.ca/programs/)

## Core &amp; Elective  
Courses

MET core and elective courses are designed to ensure that program graduates will have a sophisticated understanding of learning supported by digital technologies in a range of different organizational contexts\.

[Learn More](https://met.ubc.ca/courses/)

## Admission  
Requirements

Learn about admission requirements to our program\. Applicants are not required to have worked in education, although professional experience in an educational area may strengthen your application\.

[Learn More](https://met.ubc.ca/prospective-students/admission-requirements/)

## Tuition  
&amp; Fees

Tuition is charged on a per\-course basis, in Canadian dollars\. Course materials and annual student fees are not included in the tuition fee\. Tuition fees can be paid online, by mail, or through Canadian financial institutions\.

[Learn More](https://met.ubc.ca/prospective-students/tuition-fees/)
