We are excited to host a workshop series this term with Robyn Stalkie, the Arts Career Strategist\.

This second part of the series will focus on exploring how we might apply our personal/UBC experiences beyond our undergraduate degree\. We will take a research approach in job researching, reflect on what skills are needed for a role, and go over some job search strategies that can set you up for success\. Come with any questions you may have about how to use your IDST degree for post\-graduation plans\.

---

### Meet Robyn Stalkie

Image As the Arts Career Strategist, Robyn’s focus is on supporting Arts students to make connections between their time at UBC and the many career possibilities that lay ahead\. She provides custom reflective and exploratory experiences through in\-class workshops, 1\-1 advising, and career programming through The Arts Compass\.
