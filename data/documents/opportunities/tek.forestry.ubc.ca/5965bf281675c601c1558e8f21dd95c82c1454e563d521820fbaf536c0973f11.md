We are pleased to offer an unforgettable evening at one of Canada’s foremost cultural attractions, the [Museum of Anthropology](http://moa.ubc.ca/) at the University of British Columbia, located on the traditional ancestral unceded territory of the Musqueam First Nation\.

[Museum of Anthropology](http://moa.ubc.ca/)  
at the University of British Columbia  
6393 N\.W\. Marine Drive  
Vancouver, B\.C\. V6T 1Z2

[https://tek\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2017/02/MoA\_receptionGreatHall\.png](https://tek.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/02/MoA_receptionGreatHall.png)[Image](https://tek.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/02/MoA_receptionGreatHall-300x200.png) [Image](https://tek.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/02/MOA_reception_catering-300x197.png)

-   Doors will open at 5 pm, dinner will be served **outdoors** at 6:30 pm\. Guests will have access to the Museum galleries for the duration of the event\.
-   Dinner will be seated, buffet\-style and menu will be posted here closer to the date\.
-   Dress code: informal\.  
    Note this is an outdoor affair; tables &amp; chairs will be set on a grassy area, and will be rain\-proofed with a tent structure\. Please plan for cooler summer evening \(i\.e\. wear layers\), as the venue is waterfront and in a forest; have comfortable outdoor\-friendly footwear \(high heels not recommended\)\.
-   Accompanying persons are welcome to join, please ensure you [reserve their ticket during registration](https://tek.sites.olt.ubc.ca/register/)\. Additional ticket purchase will be available on site, but prices may be higher\.
-   Transportation:
    -   if you attend the [in\-conference tour](https://tek.sites.olt.ubc.ca/conference-program/program/site-visit/), transportation from Pinnacle Hotel will be provided at noon\. Return buses to Pinnacle Hotel will leave MoA around 8:30 pm – 9 pm\.
    -   guests wishing to join the dinner directly at 5 pm are welcome to use public transit or own transportation to campus\. See [Translink website](http://www.translink.ca/) or [Yellow cab website](http://www.yellowcabonline.com/contact-us.php) to plan your trip to campus\. Return buses to Pinnacle Hotel will leave MoA around 8:30 pm – 9 pm\.
