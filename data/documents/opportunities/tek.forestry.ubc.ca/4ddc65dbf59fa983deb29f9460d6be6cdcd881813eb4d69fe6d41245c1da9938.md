## Student Accommodations

Student accommodation is based on shared occupancy \(2 to 4 individuals\)\.

Don’t have a roommate? Post in our [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/382909585385068/) to find a roommate to share a room with during the conference\. One student will have to give their credit card details when reserving a room\.

[Book Student Room](https://aws.passkey.com/event/48961823/owner/2075/home)

Please note the number of rooms is limited; **book early to secure your room at the special conference rate \(available up to May 10, 2017\)**\.
