Pursuing graduate studies is a significant commitment, both personally and financially\. We are dedicated to making your educational journey as accessible as possible\. All admitted graduate students in our program are funded through a combination of sources such as graduate research assistantship \(GRA\), scholarships, sponsorship, and/or other awards\. Many also pursue teaching assistantship \(TA\) opportunities to supplement their income and gain valuable experience\.

## **Funding**

**Graduate Research Assistantship \(GRA\):** Supervisors provide ***minimum stipends*** to graduate students without external funding, contingent upon satisfactory academic performance\. Beyond the nominal program duration, the supervisors determine stipends\. The standard funding period and amount in our department as of June 1, 2024 are:

-   **MASc/MSc:** 2 years at $26,000 minimum per year\.
-   **PhD:** Effective September 2026 onward and as outlined on the G\+PS website, all full\-time students will be offered a ***minimum funding package*** equal to $40,000 for each of the first four years of their PhD\. The funding package may consist of any combination of internal or external awards, teaching\-related work, graduate research assistantships, and graduate academic assistantships\. More information can be found [here](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/minimum-funding-policy-phd-students)\.

## Awards

UBC, the provincial government, and the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada \(NSERC\) offer a variety of scholarships\. We strongly encourage students to apply for all scholarships for which they are eligible, as these often serve as top\-ups to their GRA funding\. For a complete list of awards available, please visit the [G\+PS Awards Portal](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/scholarships-awards-funding/award-opportunities)\.

## UBC Scholarships

Outstanding incoming students are automatically considered for the following UBC awards\.  
***Please note that in some cases, continuing students may also be eligible\.*** 

UBC has a number of central fellowships that range in values between $175 and $40,000\. Please consult the UBC awards database, as well as their webpages on the [Affiliated Fellowships Doctoral Program](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/affiliated-fellowships-doctoral-program) and [Affiliated Fellowships Master’s Program](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/affiliated-fellowships-masters-program)\.

[Awards](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/bpoc-graduate-excellence-award) have been made available by the University of British Columbia for outstanding graduate students who identify as Black or as a Person of Color, with preference for domestic students and for incoming research Master’s students\. The minimum value of each award is $1,500\.

The [fellowship](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/four-year-doctoral-fellowship-4yf) provides $18,200 per year plus full tuition for up to 4 years, with supervisors offering an additional top\-up of GRA to ensure the minimum funding package is met\. PhD students are considered within the first 12 months of their program, and those transferring from MASc to PhD are considered within the first\-year post\-transfer\.

The [GSI](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/graduate-support-initiative-gsi-awards) funds provide tuition fee awards to eligible graduate students in both the PhD and Master’s programs\. A student is eligible if they do not hold a fellowship or industrial sponsorship that either includes tuition fee coverage or exceeds $15,000 per year \(in which case the supervisor must provide top\-ups to meet the departmental minimum, including the tuition fee award\)\. 

-   For **PhD students**, the tuition award is provided for the first two years and extended to years 3 and 4 if the student advances to candidacy within 25 months of starting the program\. 
-   For **Master’s students**, the award is provided for the first year and renewed for the second year if the student completes 12 credits of coursework with a minimum average of 76%\. 

The award amount is the same for all eligible students but may vary annually based on available GSI funding and the number of qualifying students\. Typically, the award is approximately **$2,000**\. *For current students, this internally can be listed as an APSC award\.*

[International Tuition Award](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/international-tuition-award) provides international graduate students up to $3,200/ year with their tuition fees if they are registered full\-time in research\-based master’s and doctoral programs and if they meet the other eligibility requirements stated on the award page\.

This [award](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/presidents-academic-excellence-initiative-phd-award) recognizes the contributions of PhD students to the university’s research activities\. It is open to all new and continuing PhD students\. Award values range from $500 to $1,500, depending on the student’s status and year of study\.

## **NSERC Scholarships**

The Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada \(NSERC\) offers several prestigious scholarships to support graduate students in research\-intensive programs:

**IMPORTANT:**  
As part of a larger [harmonization of Tri\-Agency graduate and postdoctoral funding programs](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/NEWSDETAIL-DETAILNOUVELLES_ENG.ASP?ID=1518), the previous CGS\-M and CGS\-D programs will become part of the Canada Graduate Research Scholarship – Master’s \(CGRS M\) and – Doctoral \(CGRS D\) respectively, effective for the fall 2025 competition\.  
**Note: Up to 15% of all doctoral awards may now be granted to international applicants who were enrolled in a Canadian institution at the time of application\.**

**Value**: $27,000 for 12 months\. 

**[Eligibility](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/canada-graduate-scholarships-masters-cgs-m-program)**: Be a Canadian citizen, a permanent resident of Canada or a Protected Person\. The award may only be held at eligible Canadian post\-secondary institutions\. Full\-time master’s students in their first year of study with high academic standing and demonstrated research potential\. 

**Value**: $40,000 per year for three years\. 

**[Eligibility](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/canada-graduate-scholarships-doctoral-cgs-d-program)**: Be a Canadian citizen, a permanent resident of Canada, a Protected Person, or foreign citizen\. Foreign citizens must be enrolled in a doctoral program of study at an eligible institution in Canada at the time of application\. Up to 15% of the pool will be allocated to international applicants\. Doctoral students demonstrating high academic standing and research aptitude\.

## Provincial Scholarship

The province of British Columbia provides one\-time funding for merit\-based graduate student scholarships\. Recipients of this award are nominated by their home department\. 

**Value:** $17,500 for a minimum duration of one year\. 

**[Eligibility](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/british-columbia-graduate-scholarship/):** Be a Canadian citizen or permanent resident and registered as a full\-time master’s or doctoral student throughout the funding period\. 

## Graduate Academic Assistantship \(GAA\)

A number of students in our program work within the Department and the wider university as a Graduate Academic Assistant and opportunities may be found via [Workday Jobs](https://ubc.wd10.myworkdayjobs.com/ubcstaffjobs)\.

## Teaching Assistantship \(TA\)

The majority of graduate students in our department hold a teaching assistantship throughout their studies to supplement their GRA stipends and/or scholarships\. Graduate students can visit our [TA portal](https://mtrl.ubc.ca/graduate/teaching-assistantships/) for information on eligibility and application guidelines\.
