We invite companies/organizations from diverse industries with a demonstrated commitment to advancing equity and supporting Black professionals to apply and participate in showcasing meaningful career opportunities\. 

Participating employers will represent a broad range of sectors, offering various opportunities while highlighting workplace cultures that champion diversity, belonging, and professional growth\. 

## Date and Location

-   Monday, March 9th, 2026 
-   3:00 pm – 6:00 pm 
-   Walter Gage, Isabel MacInnes Ballroom 
-   5959 Student Union Boulevard, Vancouver, BC 

---

## Expectations for Participating Employers

To ensure the fair reflects our goals and values, we prioritize the following criteria when selecting participating employers:

-   Organizations led by or actively engaging with Black communities, or those with a demonstrated history of meaningful collaboration with them\. 
-   Intentional representation at the fair, ideally through Black staff members who can engage with students and offer insights grounded in their lived and professional experiences\. While this representation is important, we also welcome allies who are committed to supporting and advancing Black talent\. 
-   A proven commitment to equity, diversity, and inclusion, shown through respectful, reciprocal relationships with Black employees, clients, and communities\. 
-   Opportunities tailored for students and emerging professionals, including internships, co\-op placements, summer positions, volunteer opportunities, and entry\-level roles\. 
-   A demonstrated understanding of the intersection of Blackness and anti\-Black racism, reflected in intentional actions that create safe, respectful, and empowering spaces for Black professionals and job seekers\.  
-   Willingness to adhere to our community guidelines to ensure a supportive and positive experience\. 

---

## Steps to Participate—for Employers

1.  **Complete the Employer Survey Form:** We value your input in helping us create a career fair that truly meets the needs of Black students\.  

Your responses will help us ensure our goals align with what students have highlighted as most important\. They will also give us a clearer understanding of your organization’s mission, current opportunities, community partnerships, and commitment to fostering a welcoming and inclusive environment for Black professionals\. 

 Please complete [**this form**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_808QNqRQMwYebS6) by **February 9th, 2026**\.  

Selected employers will be contacted on or after **February 6th, 2026**\. Go to the **Survey Form**\. Note: If you have any accessibility issues with the form, please contact us at the email address listed below\. 

2.  **Selection Process:** Selected employers will be invited to participate and sent details on setup, schedule, and preparation to ensure a rewarding experience at the event\. 
3.  **Register with UBC CareersOnline:** Once selected, you are required to create an account with UBC CareersOnline\. Once you have an account, you can find and register for the fair\. Only employers with an official invitation to participate will have their registration approved\. 
4.  **Participate in the Fair:** Use this time to share information about your organization and to showcase opportunities to Black students\.
