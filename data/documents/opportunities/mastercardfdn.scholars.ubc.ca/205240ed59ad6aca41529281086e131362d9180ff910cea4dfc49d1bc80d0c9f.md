## [$25M grant brings African students to UBC](https://mastercardfdn.scholars.ubc.ca/2013/04/22/25m-grant-brings-african-students-to-ubc/)

By [maya zwang](https://mastercardfdn.scholars.ubc.ca/author/maya-zwang/ "Posts by maya zwang") on April 22, 2013

More than 110 students from Africa will receive comprehensive scholarships to live and learn at the University of British Columbia thanks to a $25 million grant from The MasterCard Foundation announced today\. UBC is among the first Canadian universities to join The MasterCard Foundation Scholars Program and one of only 15 institutions in the world \[…\]
