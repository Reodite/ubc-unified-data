> “Using my education to help others is crucial, whether through direct service, advocacy, or contributing to initiatives promoting education and sustainability\.” – Daisy Mboya

**Can you tell us a little about your background and where you’re from?**

My sister raised me in Eldoret, Kenya, after my father’s retirement on medical grounds from 2006 until I completed high school in 2012\. In 2013, I got to join the best university in Kenya, the University of Nairobi\. I was lucky enough to have my eldest sister support my first year of university\. During my second year on campus, financial obligations constrained my sister to a position where she could no longer help me in school\. At this point, I had to start working over the weekend to get some extra cash to pay fees and keep going\. I never enjoyed my time on campus because I was either working or worried about how I would get the next day’s meal\. Thinking about how determined I was to get the best out of my situation, having been told that ‘education is the key to success, I can only look back and thank all those who supported and believed in my strengths\.

**What motivated you to apply for the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program? Was there someone or something that inspired you?**

I got motivated to apply for the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at UBC to get an opportunity to join the UBC community, learn from the best in the field of GIS, and advance my skills\. I applied for this scholarship to be part of a community of people who share the values of nurturing young minds, improving young women’s livelihoods through education, and empowering generations through mentorship\. I applied for this scholarship because, through the Mastercard Foundation, I would be better placed to make a greater impact in my society\.

**How has the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program shaped your educational journey so far?**

We are nearing the end of the first semester, and the depth and knowledge acquired differ from any form of learning I have done so far \(both self\-paced and directed\)\. The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program has also exposed me to some of the best professionals in the industry who have taught me the power of networking, time management, and giving back\. Additionally, the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program has provided me with a safe space to maximize my potential through learning, gaining skills, and applying them to create a positive impact\.

**How did you first hear about the opportunity? Was the application process challenging?**

I found out about the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at UBC on LinkedIn\. The application process was straightforward, with no major challenges encountered\.

**In what ways has the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program impacted your academic life? What have been some of your biggest accomplishments?**

The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program has also exposed me to some of the best professionals in the industry who have taught me the power of networking, time management, and giving back\. My biggest academic accomplishment since receiving the scholarship has been mastering synthesizing GIS data using the most recent approaches\. Beyond academics, being part of the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program has taught me that excellence is an interplay of balance\. Balance of interpersonal skills, social skills and academic skills\.

**Have you faced any new challenges during your scholarship journey? How do you manage the expectations of being a Mastercard Foundation Scholar?**

My biggest challenge during this journey is ‘perception through the eye of an African\.’ To overcome this challenge, I have sought to understand the country’s culture to adapt and thrive in this diverse, multicultural environment\. As a Mastercard Foundation Scholar, I’m also expected to embody the program’s core values\. Meeting this expectation has been about sharing my experiences, learning from others, and building meaningful connections\.

**How has the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program influenced your career goals? What’s your biggest goal after graduation?**

Receiving this scholarship has significantly shaped my career goals by providing me with the financial support and resources to pursue my academic interests without the burden of economic stress\. It has allowed me to explore various fields related to my study, helping me narrow down my focus\. Additionally, my connections through Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program\-related events have opened my eyes to potential career paths I had yet to consider\. I aim to achieve a strong foundation in my field of study and develop the skills necessary to make a meaningful impact\. I aim to engage in research projects, internships, and networking opportunities to enhance my practical knowledge and prepare me for a successful career\. Ultimately, I aspire to contribute positively to my community and industry\. My major goal is to transition into a new role and practice most, if not all, of what I have been taught\. Use the knowledge and skills acquired to solve real\-world problems\.

**How do you plan to give back to your community once your studies are completed?**

After completing my studies, I plan to give back to my community by volunteering with local organizations, offering mentorship to students in my field, and applying my skills to address community challenges\. Using my education to help others is crucial, whether through direct service, advocacy, or contributing to initiatives promoting education and sustainability\.  
Moreover, I want to establish programs that support young teenage mothers in accessing resources and opportunities that can help them succeed\.

**What advice would you give to students applying for scholarships?**

My advice to other students applying for this scholarship is to be authentic and thorough in your applications\. Take the time to reflect on your experiences, achievements, and aspirations, and articulate them clearly in your essays\. Don’t hesitate to showcase your unique perspective and what differentiates you from other candidates\. Furthermore, seek feedback from mentors or peers to refine your application before submission\.

**Looking back, what has been the most rewarding part of this journey? Can you share a memorable moment?**

Looking back, the most rewarding part of this journey has been the personal growth and development I’ve experienced\. The scholarship has provided financial support and opened doors to new opportunities, allowing me to build meaningful connections, enhance my skills, and gain confidence in my abilities\. This journey has truly shaped my academic and career aspirations\. A memorable moment from my scholarship experience was attending a Mastercard Foundation Scholars’ Networking Event\. During this event, I had the opportunity to meet the Mastercard Foundation team, who shared the Foundation’s story, vision, and mission\.

**If you could sum up your journey as a Mastercard Foundation Scholar in one sentence, what would it be?**

My Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program journey has been a transformative experience that has empowered me to pursue my passions, connect with inspiring individuals, and prepare for a meaningful future\.

*Despite early challenges, Daisy’s determination and the scholarship’s support have shaped her journey, providing her with opportunities to grow academically, develop vital skills, and connect with inspiring professionals\. Grateful for the transformative experience, she aspires to use her education to make a meaningful impact in her community and empower others through mentorship and advocacy\.*
