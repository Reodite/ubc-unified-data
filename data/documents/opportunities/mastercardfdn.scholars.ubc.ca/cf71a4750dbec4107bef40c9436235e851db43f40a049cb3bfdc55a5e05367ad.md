**Faculty: Land and Food Systems**

**Program: Master of Food and Resource Economics**

**Year: 2024**

**Country: Nigeria**

My name is Abdulwasiu Alabi Ghazali, a Nigerian Agricultural and Resource Economics graduate from the Federal University of Technology, Akure \(FUTA\)\. My achievement during high school and undergraduate studies demonstrates my motivation for academic excellence\. I completed high school as the most outstanding student receiving a LearnAfrica award as the best student in the 2013/2014 NECO examination in Niger State, Nigeria\. Similarly, in my bachelor’s degree, I graduated at the top of my class, emerging as the best\-graduating student in my department and the entire faculty of agriculture\. Besides, I am a strong advocate of the SDGs and have active experience as a global volunteer for food and the environment with the Youth in Landscapes Initiative\(YIL\) in Bonn, Germany\.

My passion for food, international trade, and policy is a deep\-rooted commitment ignited during my professional internship with an agri\-food sustainability company\. In this transformative experience, I was privileged to assist more than 300 cocoa smallholders in implementing the safety, ethical, social, and environmental dimensions of sustainable agri\-food production\.

The MasterCard Foundation Scholars Program through the MFRE program and the leadership experience will offer me the perfect study track for economic knowledge, a blend of theories, policies, and the network to further impact the agri\-food sector in my country\. To sum up, I am excited to work with and learn from the agri\-food experts in MFRE and the pool of professionals at the MasterCard Foundation on real\-world issues in the agri\-food sector and designing modern solutions\.
