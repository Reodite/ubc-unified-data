**Faculty: Forestry**

**Program: Master of Geomatics for Environmental Management**

**Year: 2024**

**Country: Kenya**

-   [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/daisy-m-85b39a60/)

I am a passionate geospatial analyst from Nairobi, Kenya, driven by a commitment to leveraging GIS technology for positive social impact\. My academic journey began at The University of Nairobi, where I earned my undergraduate degree in Geospatial Engineering\. During my studies, I conducted a research project identifying suitable sites for wind farms in Laikipia County, which deepened my interest in sustainable energy solutions\.  
  
Following graduation, I worked as a GIS officer at The Rural Electrification and Renewable Energy Corporation in Kenya\. In this role, I surveyed and mapped potential areas for connecting communities to the national electricity grid, which reinforced my dedication to using geospatial information to support renewable energy initiatives\.  
  
At UBC, I am eager to explore the intersection of geospatial technologies and sustainability, particularly in the areas of spatial data analysis, forest carbon sequestration, and sustainable land management\. I am excited to contribute my skills to research projects in the Forestry Department and collaborate with professors who share my passion for sustainability\.  
  
I am also looking forward to experiencing Vancouver’s vibrant cultural scene and stunning natural beauty, which resonate with my desire to give back to the community\. I believe that UBC’s collaborative environment will allow me to make a meaningful impact in the field of sustainable development while furthering my knowledge and expertise\.
