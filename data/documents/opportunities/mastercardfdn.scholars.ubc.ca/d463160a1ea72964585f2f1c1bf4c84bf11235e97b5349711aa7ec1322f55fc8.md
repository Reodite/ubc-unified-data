I was very excited when I heard, because I understood the value of working for a multinational company\.  This would be an experience that would surely stand out on my resume\. — Sharon Shamuyarira is a MasterCard Foundation Scholar from Zimbabwe pursuing an undergraduate degree in Accounting at the University of British Columbia\. She had the opportunity to intern for AngloAmerican, one of the world’s largest Mining company located in Zimbabwe\.
