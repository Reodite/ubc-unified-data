Gain teaching experience and science communication skills while lending your knowledge to the bright minds of tomorrow\. Wild &amp; Immersive’s UBC Grad Student Teacher program brings talented UBC graduate students together with Wild &amp; Immersive’s Forest School program for ages 5\-13\. 

Wild &amp; Immersive operates an outdoor weekly forest school program for youth ages 5\-13 on a variety of nature\-based and environmental topics\. Run out of the UBC Malcolm Knapp Research Forest in Maple Ridge, Wild &amp; Immersive is looking for knowledgeable, energetic and passionate graduate students to be guest speakers for our forest school program\. Graduate students will create a module on a topic of interest and will spend the day leading the module to the forest school class\. Modules must include a hands\-on activity\.

*\*Please note that registration does not guarantee acceptance into the program\. The registrant and Wild &amp; Immersive must agree on the topic, date\(s\) and activities\.*
