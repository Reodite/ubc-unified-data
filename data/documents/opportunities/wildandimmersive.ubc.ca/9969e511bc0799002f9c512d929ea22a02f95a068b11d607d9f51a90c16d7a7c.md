We’re looking for positive, caring, and energetic volunteers to be a part of our outdoor\-education team\.

#### Our ideal volunteers have:

-   [Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/icon-forest-school-1.svg)
    
    A love of the outdoors &amp; a love for working with children
    
-   [Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/list-icon-3.svg)
    
    Passion for the environment
    
-   [Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/icon-forest-school-3.svg)
    
    An interest in getting experience in an alternative classroom
    

## Expand your experience

We have volunteer opportunities available year round in our Forest School program, our day and overnight camps, and other special events and specialty camps\.

#### What you’ll gain from volunteering with Wild &amp; Immersive:

-   Experience assisting groups of children in an outdoor setting
    
-   How to facilitate and develop activities
    
-   Knowledge of local environments and ecosystems
    
-   Safety awareness and risk assessment in an outdoor setting
    
-   Behind the scenes look at forest school curriculum development
    
-   Resiliency in the elements from working in all weather conditions
    

## Volunteer with us

Our programs range in length, activities, and duration\. Whether you’re looking for work experience, curious to see if the outdoor\-education field is right for you, or just want to spend your time outdoors with others, this volunteer position is perfect for you\.
