Findlay Family Bursary

At Wild &amp; Immersive, we believe every child should have the opportunity to participate in our programs, regardless of their financial situation\. Thanks to the generous support and funding provided by the Colleen Findlay Foundation, the Wild &amp; Immersive Findlay Family Bursary allows us to offset some, if not all, of the costs for a child to attend our programs\.

[Two kids jumping in a puddle](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/kids-jumping-in-puddle.jpg)

[Group of preschool age kids learning at Wild &amp; Immersive](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/preschool.jpg)

## What we can cover

We offer funding support for most of our programs, including:

-   [Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/list-icon-7.svg)
    
    Weekly Forest School
    
-   [Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/list-icon-6.svg)
    
    Pro\-D Day Camps
    
-   [Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/list-icon-9.svg)
    
    Spring Break Camps
    
-   [Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/list-icon-2.svg)
    
    Summer Camps
    

Funding is typically 25\-100% coverage but if you’re looking for a specific sum, please let us know in your application\.

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Colleen-Findlay-Foundation-1-1024x1024.jpg)

The bursary program is made possible by the generosity of the Colleen Findlay Foundation\.

## Here’s how to apply

Considerations for bursary recipients include circumstances such as income and family size\. Please provide as much detail as possible during the application process\. Proof of hardship or income will be requested\.

Applications are taken on an ongoing basis\. The value of each bursary will vary according to the needs of the qualifying family\.

[Apply Now](https://mkrf-forestry.sites.olt.ubc.ca/wild-immersive-findlay-family-bursary-application/)

**Note:** An application does not guarantee the receipt of a bursary, or the amount that you have applied for\. Receipt of a bursary one year does not guarantee funds for the following year\. Families will need to reapply for the program at each registration period\.
