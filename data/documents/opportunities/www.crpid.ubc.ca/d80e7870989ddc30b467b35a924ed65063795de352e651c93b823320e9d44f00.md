# UPCOMING EVENTS

### Dementia in the Outdoors  
Fireweed Club Nature Walk​  
   
Join us for a one\-hour, slow\-paced nature walk through the Pacific Spirit Park Forest\. The walk will be followed by lunch, with food, coffee, and tea provided if weather permits\. This inclusive, community\-focused walk is open to individuals, families, and friends who want to spend time outdoors, connect with others, and enjoy nature at a relaxed, unhurried pace\.  
Spring Walk Dates: April 24th, May 22nd, and June 19th  
​
