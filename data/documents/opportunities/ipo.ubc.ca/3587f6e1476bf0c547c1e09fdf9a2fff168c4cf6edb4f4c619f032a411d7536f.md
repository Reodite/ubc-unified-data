June 8, 2020

The CFI has launched an updated call for proposals for the Exceptional Opportunities Fund – COVID\-19 \(EOF\-COVID\-19\) competition with a submission deadline of July 6, 2020\. [Click here for full details from CFI\.](https://www.innovation.ca/awards/exceptional-opportunities-fund-covid-19)

UBC researchers should note our internal process for applying to the competition as noted on our EOF competition page\.

[Details on UBC's Process for EOF COVID\-19 Competition](https://ipo.ubc.ca/exceptional-opportunities-fund-covid19)
