December 14, 2020

The Canada Foundation for Innovation is expected to make a call for proposals for the 2022 Innovation Fund this coming year\. 

The Institutional Programs Office \(IPO\) is pleased to offer virtual information sessions for UBC faculty and staff seeking more information on the upcoming CFI IF 2022 opportunity\. All potential CFI IF applicants are encouraged to attend\. There will be some time at the end of the presentation for Q&amp;A\. Please register below to receive zoom session details\.

The IPO will be offering Zoom information sessions on the following dates:

Tuesday, January 12, 2\-3 pm  
Wednesday, January 13, 11\-12 pm  
Tuesday, January 19, 2:30 – 3:30 pm

If you are looking for one\-on\-one support, please contact [ipo\.admin@ubc\.ca](mailto:ipo.admin@ubc.ca) to book a 30\-minute timeslot with the UBC CFI advisor and IPO team\.

Please register for your preferred virtual information session using the form below\. 

Event or Deadline Date

Mon, 01/11/2021 \- 16:00
