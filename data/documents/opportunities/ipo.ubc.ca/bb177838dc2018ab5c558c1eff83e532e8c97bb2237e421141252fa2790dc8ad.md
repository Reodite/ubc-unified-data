Applicants to all Canada Foundation for Innovation funding opportunities must request for a Renovation &amp; Infrastructure Cost Estimate \(RICE\) prior to submitting their application to the IPO internal peer review\. RICE requests are not required for off\-campus \(i\.e\. hospital site\) proposals\.

UBC Facilities Planning will work with you to request all necessary approvals to ensure the successful installation or construction of your CFI\-funded facility or equipment\. 

#### Steps to receive a Renovation &amp; Infrastructure Cost Estimate \(RICE\) for CFI Applications

Follow this process to receive your Renovation and Infrastructure Cost Estimate \(RICE\) in time for your internal peer review\. The deadline to submit your RICE Request Form \(step 2\) is based on the complexity of the work required, and is categorized into renovation tiers \(see step 1\)\.

[View RICE deadlines \(for all tiers\)](https://ipo.ubc.ca/node/1619)

### 1\. Researcher determines the appropriate Tier that best describes their proposal using the self\-assessment\.

This step will help determine the appropriate deadline to submit a RICE Request \(step 2\)\. If you are unsure which renovation tier applies best to your proposal, please contact [renovations\.cfi@ubc\.ca](mailto:renovations.cfi@ubc.ca)

[Begin Self\-Assessment](https://ipo.ubc.ca/cwl/rice-self-assessment)

### 2\. Researcher submits a completed RICE Request form on/before the relevant deadline\.

### \(Between 52 and 0 weeks prior to IPO internal peer review deadline\)

-   The researcher may need to compile vendor specifications \(i\.e\. data sheets\) to submit with the RICE request\.
-   A departmental administrator may need to request UBC technical services' review on the researcher's behalf for the proposal\. Their conclusions may need to be attached to the RICE request\.

[Start RICE Request \(CWL Required\)](https://ipo.ubc.ca/cwl/renovation-and-infrastructure-cost-estimate-request)

Once a RICE request has been submitted, you may now submit your application for the IPO internal peer review\.

### 3\. UBC Facilities Planning prepares the scope document\.

Once the researcher has submitted the RICE Request, Facilities Planning will prepare a scope document in partnership with UBC Project Services\. A Facilities Planner will schedule a meeting with the applicant to better understand their needs and follow up with a site visit of the approved room\(s\)\. The scope document will require the researcher's approval before moving to the next step\.

-   Tier 4 and 5 requests may require approvals and consultation from Campus \+ Community Planning or UBC's Site Selection Committee\.

### 4\. UBC Project Services prepares the Renovation and Infrastructure Cost Estimate \(RICE\) and issues it to the researcher prior to their relevant CFI application deadline\.

Using the scope document from Facilities Planning, Project Services prepares the RICE and issues it to the researcher\.

### 5\. Researcher submits the RICE to IPO for review by finance analysts and uploads the RICE with the final CFI application on/before IPO and CFI deadlines\.

Please review any relevant internal submission deadlines and procedures\. Contact the IPO at [ipo\.admin@ubc\.ca](mailto:ipo.admin@ubc.ca) if you have any questions or require assistance with this step\.

Note that RICE requests are not required for off\-campus proposals\.

[Begin Self\-Assessment \(CWL Required\)](https://ipo.ubc.ca/cwl/rice-self-assessment)
