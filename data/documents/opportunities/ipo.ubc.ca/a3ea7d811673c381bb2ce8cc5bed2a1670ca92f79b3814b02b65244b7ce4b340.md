In alignment with Canada's Biomanufacturing and Life Sciences Strategy, the objectives of [Canada Biomedical Research Fund](https://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca/funding-financement/cbrf-frbc/index-eng.aspx) \(CBRF\) and the [Biosciences Research Infrastructure Fund](https://www.innovation.ca/apply-manage-awards/funding-opportunities/biosciences-research-infrastructure-fund) \(BRIF\) are to help ensure that Canada is prepared for future pandemics by increasing domestic capacity through investments and partnerships across the academic, public, private and non\-profit sectors to produce life\-saving vaccines and therapeutics\.

In March 2023, five hubs were awarded through the BRIF Stage 1 competition, including [Canada's Immuno\-Engineering and Biomanufacturing Hub](https://immunoengineeringhub.ca/) \(CIEBH\) located at UBC\. The second BRIF competition was also launched as a partnership with the Canada Biomedical Research Fund \(CBRF\)\. This competition will award up to $570 million for cutting\-edge research, talent development and research infrastructure projects associated with the five biomedical research hubs\. The CFI will invest up to $277 million in research infrastructure funding through the CBRF BRIF Stage 2 competition and will fund up to 60 percent of a project’s eligible infrastructure costs\. In addition, the CFI will provide up to $83 million for associated operating costs through the Infrastructure Operating Fund\.
