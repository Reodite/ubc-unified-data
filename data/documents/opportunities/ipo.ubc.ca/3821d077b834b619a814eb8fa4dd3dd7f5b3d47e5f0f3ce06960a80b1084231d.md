November 28, 2019

An investment of more than $61 million was annoucned for state\-of\-the\-art research labs and equipment through the Canada Foundation for Innovation’s \(CFI\) [John R\. Evans Leaders Fund](https://www.innovation.ca/awards/john-r-evans-leaders-fund) \(JELF\)\. This investment will support 261 projects at 40 universities across Canada\.

[Read the announcement](https://www.innovation.ca/about/communiques-presse/government-canada-invests-cutting-edge-tools-universities)

A total of 31 projects at UBC were awarded combined funding of $6\.0m
