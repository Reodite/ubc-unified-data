The Autoimmune Biomedical Collaborative \(ABC\) Symposium aims to bring together people from various autoimmune disease areas, disciplines, career stages, and lived experiences with the goal of forging connections across research, clinical practice, and patient needs to guide new interventions for autoimmune diseases\.
