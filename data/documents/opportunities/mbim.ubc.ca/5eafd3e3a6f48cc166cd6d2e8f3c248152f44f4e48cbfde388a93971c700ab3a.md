**Antibodies against measles virus**

Dr\. Saphire’s research explains, at the molecular level, how and why viruses are pathogenic and can trigger chronic disease, and provides the roadmap for medical defense\. She solved the first\-ever structure of the entire human antibody, and provided the world's first images of the surface proteins of the Ebola, Sudan, Marburg, Bundibugyo, and Lassa virus glycoproteins, which explained how they remodel these structures as they drivethemselves into cells, how their proteins suppress immune function, and where human antibodies can defeat these viruses\. Dr\. Saphire was the galvanizing force behind the Viral Immunotherapeutic Consortium which united 55 former competitors across five continents to accelerate the discovery pipeline against these viruses and puttherapeutics into place where there were none before\. Her recent work has provided the world's first look at the human antibody response to Measles virus, uncovering potent natural human antibodies that could be used to present or treat measles infection, as well as the world’s first look at the endogenous human retroviruses, silent components of our cells that reactivate in cancer and autoimmune disease\. She currently leads an ARPA\-H\-funded national consortium to develop defenses against childhood viruses that prime adults later in life for cancer and chronic disease\.
