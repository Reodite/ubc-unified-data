## 2026

What a thrilling competition we had this year\! Our 2026 high school teams revealed a bevy of advanced physics skills and some amazing physics aptitudes for fast\-paced questions and applying practical concepts and formulas in the labs\. The prebuilds were ingenious and inspired – so cool\! We heartily encourage all participants to continue in physics and science and we enthusiastically congratulate our participating physics teachers and coaches on a very good competition year\!

## 2025

Amazing skills shown by all\! 2025 was definitely the year of thrilling physics and super\-creative teams\! We were pleased to host 81 teams, all of which are now honorary members of our physics family\! This event’s impressive showing was only surpassed by the amazing spirit of competition and inclusion that you brought here\. Thank you teams\! Hope to see you all next year\!

## 2024

What an incredible event\! This year we hosted 930 high school students from across BC who were supported by almost 100 teachers, parents, and friends, and assisted by 80 student/staff and faculty volunteers\. It was rowdy, tremendous and incredible\! Thank you participants\! You inspired all of us\!

## 2023

Cheers to BC students, teachers and coaches for a wildly successful 45th annual Physics Olympics\! We hosted 718 students, 65 teams, 60 schools and welcomed a nice representation of the province here at UBC campus\. What fantastic talent\! Kudos to all participants for your great energy, smarts and enthusiasm\!
