December 8, 2017

## About

**Time**: 9\-10am

**DESCRIPTION**

This webinar will explore the gendered experiences of substance use, mental health and trauma among men and boys and provide examples of engagement strategies that are informed by an understanding of masculine norms related to health and wellness, help seeking, work and caregiving\.

**WHO SHOULD ATTEND**

This webinar is for people planning or providing substance use services, as well as allied health and social services and systems\.
