## About

**from Noon to 2:00 PM *Eastern Standard Time***

This webinar is a one\-day event on the fourth anniversary of cannabis legalization in Canada where we will bring together youth experts and advocates, community organizations, and researchers to showcase innovations in youth cannabis harm reduction projects\. We will share and discuss new developments in harm reduction, youth\-led approaches, and how to be inclusive to the diverse needs of youth who use cannabis\.

***In Person:***
