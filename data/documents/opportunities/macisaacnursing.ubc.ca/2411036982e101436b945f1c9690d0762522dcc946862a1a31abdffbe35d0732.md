## About

This webinar is part two of the four\-part Flipping Stigma webinar series\. The team members of the [Flipping Stigma on its Ear Toolkit project](https://www.flippingstigma.com/), including action group members who live with dementia and researchers, will be invited to each webinar to help us explore the toolkit and share their stories and experiences\.
