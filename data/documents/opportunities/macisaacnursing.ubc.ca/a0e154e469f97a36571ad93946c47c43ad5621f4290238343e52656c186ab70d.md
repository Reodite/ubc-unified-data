## About

Advancing Health Talks WiP Seminar Series presents work in progress Dr\. Angela Russolillo and Michelle Carter

This presentation will describe the results of two Delphi studies that developed integrated models of care for two mental health populations \(perinatal and concurrent disorders\)\. We will explore the development of each consensus\-based framework that prioritizes the perspectives of people with lived and living experience alongside health professionals to address fragmentation in the mental health care system\.

**ANGELA RUSSOLILLO**  
Scientist, Advancing Health  
Assistant Professor, UBC School of Nursing  
Investigator, Michael Smith Health Research

**MICHELLE CARTER**  
Clinical Nurse Specialist, St\. Paul's Hospital  
Doctoral Trainee, UBC School of Nursing
