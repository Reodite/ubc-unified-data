## Health In \| Equity Conference \(University of Toronto\)

Times are in Eastern Time

CONFERENCE THEMES

-   Healthcare Inequities and Health care System Barriers Among All Children, Youth and Communities
-   Impact and considerations of various social determinants of health and inequity on the well\-being of children, youth and communities
-   Solutions to address system level inequities among vulnerable and marginalized populations from birth to adolescence using community and patient engagement\.
