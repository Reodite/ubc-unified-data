# UBC Reads Sustainability with Kate Marvel

A public lecture using nine emotions to explore the science and stories behind climate change\.

Live on stage for her first reading and public talk in Canada, Kate Marvel offers to take us on a soaring journey – one filled with mythology, physics, witchcraft, bad movies, volcanoes, Roman emperors, sequoia groves, and the many small miracles of nature we usually take for granted\.

Wed\. Apr 15, 6\-8:30pm  
CIRS Building,2260 West Mall

Register today\!

FEATURING  
• Light reception  
• Opening remarks by Angelique Pilon and Dr\. Tara Ivanochko, UBC Sustainability Hub  
• Audience Q&amp;A, moderated by Professor Simon Donner, Climate Solutions Research Collective

Presented by the UBC Sustainability Hub &amp; Climate Solutions Research Collective\.
