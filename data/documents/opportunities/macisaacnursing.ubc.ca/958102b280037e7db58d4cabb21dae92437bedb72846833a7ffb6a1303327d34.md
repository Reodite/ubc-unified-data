## About

**Time**: 12\-1pm

**NEW DATE**: Rescheduled to Thursday, May 23, 2019

The EQUIP Team is an interdisciplinary team that uses a tripartite leadership model \(practice, research and Indigenous leadership\) to study and advance equity in health care\. This presentation will overview how the team is using the model and an integrated Knowledge Translation approach to support the uptake of these three key dimensions of equity oriented care in direct care and throughout the health care system\.
