## About

**Time**: 4\-6pm

**An arts\-based knowledge translation event for UBC students, staff, and faculty\.**

The Trans Youth Hormone Therapy Decision Making Study explored how trans youth and their parents made decisions about initiating hormone therapy and how health care providers navigated ethical challenges arising in practice with trans youth and their families\.

Through the Trans Youth in Translation project, trans youth from across BC have participated in creative arts workshops with trans artist mentors, drawing on their unique perspectives and amazing talents to bring research findings to life through visual art, scene writing, story, poetry, song, and board game design\.

For more information, please e\-mail: [beth\.clark@alumni\.ubc\.ca](mailto:beth.clark@alumni.ubc.ca)
