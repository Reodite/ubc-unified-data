## About

**Geoff Wong** \(University of Oxford\) is a highly experienced and internationally recognised expert in realist synthesis and evaluation and works closely with researchers in the field of evidence syntheses of complex health and social interventions\.

Hosted by **Maura MacPhee** \(UBC School of Nursing\) &amp; **Christopher McLeod** \(UBC School of Population and Public Health\)

**Peter Wall International Visiting Research Scholar** [Dr\. Geoff Wong](https://www.medsci.ox.ac.uk/study/graduateschool/supervisors/geoffrey-wong) will facilitate these sessions\.

**March 3\-5, 2020**  
*Interested individuals can attend any portion of these 3\-day presentations\.*

**Time:** 10:00am \- 4:00pm

Realist methods are relatively new in Canada\. If you are a student or researcher who would like to know more about realist methods, please join us for informal presentations of student/researcher realist reviews and evaluations \- followed by participant discussions and Q&amp;A\.

Contact [maura\.macphee@ubc\.ca](mailto:maura.macphee@ubc.ca) for the schedule and related readings\.
