The [Critical Research in Health and Healthcare Inequities](http://www.crihhi.nursing.ubc.ca/) \(CRiHHI\) is hosting its first seminar in 2021 as a virtual event \- A roundtable discussion on the theme of Collective Nursing Scholarship Towards Social Justice\. This roundtable discussion will be moderated by Dr\. Colleen Varcoe:

-   To learn more about the diverse research units, centres, and programs of scholarship and their intersection with social justice\.
-   To enable newly\-hired faculty members and graduate students in the School of Nursing – who during COVID\-19 have had less opportunity to learn from one another – to hear more about faculty research and critical scholarship\.
