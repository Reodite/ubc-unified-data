## About

4:00 PM \(7:00 PM EDT\)\.

This series on Climate, Health, and Healthcare: A Speaker Series for Health Professional Learners is presented by CASCADES Canada with the first session on March 9, 2022 \(Climate, Health, and Healthcare: Introduction\)\.

> **Raluca Radu**, on behalf of CANE and CANE\-BC presents the fourth session of the series, Environmental advocacy for healthcare learners\.

The series is organized in partnership by UofT Green Meds, CASCADES, the Canadian Association of Nurses for the Environment \(CANE\), the Canadian Association of Physicians for the Environment \(CAPE\), and the Canadian Federation of Medical Students \(CFMS\) Health and Environment Adaptive Response Task force \(HEART\)\.

Each one\-hour session begins with a speaker or panel sharing experiences pertaining to climate, health and healthcare, followed by a live Q&amp;A session\.
