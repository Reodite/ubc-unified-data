## About

**Time:** 12\-1pm

**Speaker:** [Dr\. Annette Browne](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/annette-browne), Professor, UBC School of Nursing, &amp; Recipient of the 2015 John McNeill Excellence in Health Research Mentorship Award

Dr\. Annette Browne’s research focuses on strategies for fostering health equity, with a particular focus on implications for Indigenous populations\. She is currently leading research on: the impact of organizational\-level interventions to improve health equity in primary health care clinics; promoting health for Indigenous women experiencing violence; and improving health care for Indigenous and non\-Indigenous people in emergency departments\. In this talk, she discusses the importance of mentorship for students, early career faculty members, and community partners\.
