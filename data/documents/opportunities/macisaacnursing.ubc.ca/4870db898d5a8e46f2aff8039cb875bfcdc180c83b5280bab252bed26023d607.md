## About

[Portrait of Lillian Hung](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Hung%2C%20Lillian%202023%20no%20glare.jpg)The theme of the 2024 INCREASE BC conference is **“Revolutionizing rehabilitation through collaboration\.”** INCREASE BC is a collaborative initiative between the UBC Division of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation, Rehab Research Program, Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute \(VCHRI\), and the UBC Rehabilitation Sciences Executive Committee\. 

We are privileged to present Dr\. Lillian Hung, Assistant Professor at the School of Nursing, University of British Columbia, as our Noon Keynote Speaker of INCREASE BC 2024 with ‘**Empowering Care: Bridging Technology and Compassion in Transdisciplinary Collaboration**\.’
