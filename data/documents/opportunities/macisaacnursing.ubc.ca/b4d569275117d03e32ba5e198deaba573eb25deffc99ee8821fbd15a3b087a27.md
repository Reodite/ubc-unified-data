## About

In collaboration with community partners, UBC School of Nursing and BC Cancer, the Cancer and Aging Research Engagement \(CARE\) team have conducted focus groups and interviews with nearly 90 British Columbians from across the province about the care experiences of older adults affected by cancer\. This event will include a short presentation and a chance to talk and exchange views about the priorities identified through this work\.

Jewish Community Centre, 950 West 41st Avenue, Vancouver
