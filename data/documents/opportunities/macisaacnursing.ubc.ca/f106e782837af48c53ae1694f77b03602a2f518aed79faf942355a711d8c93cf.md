## About

### Providence Health Care Event

#### Are you a healthcare provider who wants to learn more about diabetes? 

#### This is your conference\!

**Target audience**  
RN, RPN, NP, CNS, RD, Pharmacist, Kinesiologist, Social Work, Speech Language Pathologist, Physiotherapist, Occupational Therapist \(in and out\-patient\)

**Learning Objectives**  
By the end of this session, the participants should be able to:  
• Describe how diabetes can impact sleep health  
• Name 3 approaches to managing diabetes through nutrition  
• Describe 3 approaches that can lead to remission of diabetes  
• List 3 types of insulin pumps and CGM devices and how they work in glucose control  
• Outline 3 strategies for a person with diabetes to implement to self\-manage their diabetes

**Cost**  
$40 plus Eventbrite fee \(non\-refundable\) Includes light breakfast, refreshments and lunch\.
