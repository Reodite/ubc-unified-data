## About

The goal of this event is to create a space for shared learning and dialogue on the pervasive systemic discrimination within the justice system for Indigenous peoples across Canada\. Held in the Sty\-Wet\-Tan Great Hall, with dinner provided, this event will feature a panel of experts who will discuss the challenges and opportunities for systemic change within the justice system\.
