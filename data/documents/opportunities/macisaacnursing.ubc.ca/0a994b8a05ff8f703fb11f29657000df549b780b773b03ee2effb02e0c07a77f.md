## About

An opportunity is provided to weigh in with your opinions with the architects and Property Trust\.

Due to precautions in place for COVID\-19, the public consultation for this project will take place online\. Please visit the project website \([https://planning\.ubc\.ca/gateway\-building](https://planning.ubc.ca/gateway-building)\) to submit comments via the project feedback form or register to a attend a virtual public meeting on February 4, 2021 \(two opportunities provided to meet either at 11:00 am or 4:00 pm\)\.

The public comment period for this project closes February 12, 2021\.
