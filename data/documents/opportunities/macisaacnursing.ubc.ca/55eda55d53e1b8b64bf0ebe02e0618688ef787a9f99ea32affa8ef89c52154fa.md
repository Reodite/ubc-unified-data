## About

The School of Nursing is pleased to invite all alumni, donors and community members to the 6th annual Nursing Alumni and Partnership Awards Gala on Thursday, May 4th, 2017\.

This annual event is our opportunity to thank all of you – our alumni and supporters who have made a deep and lasting commitment to nursing education, teaching, and research\. We will also be presenting a number of awards – the Award of Distinction, the Alumni Recognition Award, the Young Alumni Award, and the School of Nursing Partnership awards – to celebrate the special contributions and achievements of our colleagues\.
