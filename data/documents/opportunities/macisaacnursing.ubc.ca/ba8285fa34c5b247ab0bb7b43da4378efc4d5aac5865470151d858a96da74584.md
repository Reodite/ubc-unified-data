# Substance use is relevant in every setting: Resources for enhancing teaching and practice 

•    Presenter: [Dr\. Colleen Varcoe](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/colleen-varcoe)  
•    Date: Wednesday, June 3 @ 12\-1pm  
•    Venue: Room 5228, 5th floor, UBC Gateway Health Building or ONLINE via ZOOM

**WATCH THE RECORDING:** [**https://youtu\.be/gjZQcRhWxV4**](https://youtu.be/gjZQcRhWxV4) 

[**VIEW THE SLIDES**](https://nursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/substance-use-is-relevant-in-every-setting.pdf)

Nurses in every setting from maternal child to palliative care provide care to people who use substances in ways that may harm their health\. Further, in every setting, a high percentage of patients/clients will have experienced some form of violence\. This session will share approaches and resources for practice and teaching to promote substance use health, using culturally safe and trauma and violence informed approaches\. Resources shared will include a substance use safety planning tool, an animation on substance use and a mini\-video\. Participants will be invited to play a game for use in teaching or supporting teams to use strength\-based approaches\.

### Dr\. Colleen Varcoe

**Dr\. Colleen Varcoe, RN, PhD** is a professor emeritus in the University of British Columbia School of Nursing\. Building on her practice as a critical care and Emergency nurse, she has taught nursing for over 40 years\. Her work aims to decrease inequity and violence including interpersonal and structural forms of violence such as racism and poverty\. Her completed research includes studies of risks and health effects of violence and health promotion for women who experience violence, especially Indigenous women\. She has studied how to promote equity\-oriented healthcare \(cultural safety, substance use health, and trauma\- and violence\-informed care \- TVIC\) at the organizational level and worked with various Indigenous communities, organizations and issues, including in health care and criminal justice contexts\.
