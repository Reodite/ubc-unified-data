[2016 Event Program](https://nursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/Symposium%202016%20Program.pdf) 

### **Abstract deadline: April 4th**, **2016** \(***extended***\)

The Graduate Student Research Symposium provides an opportunity for nursing students to share innovative projects, scholarship, and research in a dynamic, interactive, and supportive setting\. Students in nursing graduate and undergraduate programs are invited to submit abstracts for oral or poster presentation\. A variety of submissions are welcome including:

-    Theoretical, philosophical, or policy papers
-    Nurse practitioner capstone projects
-    Baccalaureate of Science in Nursing \(BSN\) synthesis or honours projects
-    Thesis or dissertation proposals
-    Master in Science in Nursing \(MSN\) major essays
-    Completed research \- innovative projects

[Submit your abstract](https://nursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/2016%20Graduate%20Student%20Research%20Symposium%20Call%20for%20Abstracts_Extended%20Deadline.pdf)
