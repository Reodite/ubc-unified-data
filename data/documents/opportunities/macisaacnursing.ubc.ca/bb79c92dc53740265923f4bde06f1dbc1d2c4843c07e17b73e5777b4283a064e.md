## About

**Lunch Seminar :** 12\-1pm @ Rm\. B104, UBC School of Population Health  
**Afternoon Tea** : 4\-5pm @ Rm\. T206, UBC School of Nursing

**RSVP to:** [js\.parrilla@ubc\.ca](mailto:js.parrilla@ubc.ca)

The [Transformative Health &amp; Justice Research Cluster](https://transformhealthjustice.ubc.ca/) \(THJRC\) @UBC invites you to join us in hosting Dr\. Emily Wang\. The THJRC is co\-led by Dr\. Ruth Elwood Martin \([CCPHE](http://ccphe.ubc.ca/)\) &amp; Dr\. Helen Brown \([CRiHHI](http://www.crihhi.nursing.ubc.ca/)\)\.

*Director of the Health Justice Lab \(Yale School of Medicine\), and co\-founder of the Transitions Clinic Network, **Dr\. Wang** has served on the National Academy of Sciences/Institute of Medicine's Health and Incarceration Workshop, and the Steering Committee on Improving Collection of Indicators of Criminal Justice System Involvement in Population Health Data Program\.*

**Co\-Hosted by:**

-   **UBC Transformative Health &amp; Justice**: [https://transformhealthjustice\.ubc\.ca](https://transformhealthjustice.ubc.ca/)
