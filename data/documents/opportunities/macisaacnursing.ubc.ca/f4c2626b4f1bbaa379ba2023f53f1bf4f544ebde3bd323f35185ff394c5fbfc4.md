## About

The Graduate Student in Nursing Association \(GSNA\) at UBC is hosting a School of Nursing Research Symposium event on May 8th from 10:30am to 4:00pm\. This free event is an opportunity to showcase nursing students' hard work, and enjoy networking, a free lunch, and refreshments\! The theme for this year’s symposium is: *The Power of Collaboration: Advancing Together*\. The deadline for abstract submission is **Friday, April 11th**\.   
  
The research symposium is held the same day as the [UBC Nursing Gala](https://nursing.ubc.ca/community/events/2025/2025-nursing-gala) for those planning on attending that event\. Prizes will be handed out for the top presentations\! The tentative schedule of the research symposium is below:  
  
10:30am \- Symposium Start \(coffee &amp; network\)  
11:00am \- Land Acknowledgement &amp; Keynote Speaker  
11:45am \- Poster Session  
12:15pm \- Lunch  
1:00pm \- Oral Presentations\*  
2:30pm \- Networking Break  
2:45pm \- World Café \(networking event\)  
3:45pm \- Awards &amp; Closing Remarks  
*\*Please note there are a limited number of spots available for oral presentations\.* 

Registration is open to nursing students at the undergraduate and graduate levels enrolled in any nursing program in BC\. Registration is free for both presenters and attendees\. 

-   To register, click here: [https://ubc\.ca1\.qualtrics\.com/jfe/form/SV\_8qVUxKSRtPVcsD4](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_8qVUxKSRtPVcsD4)
