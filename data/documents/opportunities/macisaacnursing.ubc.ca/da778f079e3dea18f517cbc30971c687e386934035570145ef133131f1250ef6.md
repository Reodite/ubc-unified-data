[**Join WAITLIST**](http://nursing-axis.sites.olt.ubc.ca/home/events-and-conferences/realist-methods-workshop-waitlist/)

This 3\-day realist methodology workshop is for researchers and students\. Peter Wall International Visiting Research Scholar Dr\. Geoff Wong will discuss the fundamentals and conduct for realist review and evaluation; and knowledge translation, reporting and publishing through a realist lens\. Dr\. Wong will use examples from his own realist research projects to illustrate policy development and creative knowledge translation for diverse audiences\. Attendees will practice quality appraisal of realist reports and publications using RAMESES standards, and they will learn how to navigate the RAMESES website\.

***Geoff Wong** \(University of Oxford\) is a highly experienced and internationally recognised expert in realist synthesis and evaluation and works closely with researchers in the field of evidence syntheses of complex health and social interventions\.*
