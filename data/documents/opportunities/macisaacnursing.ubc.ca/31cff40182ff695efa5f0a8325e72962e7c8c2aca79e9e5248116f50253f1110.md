## About

You've spent months on your research, but have only 10 minutes to present it to the world\. Yikes\! In this Research Toolbox session, Dr\. Elizabeth Saewyc will demonstrate practical ways to make it memorable\.

**BIO**:  
Elizabeth Saewyc, PhD, RN, FSASHM, FCAHS, is Professor and Associate Director of Research &amp; Teaching Scholarship, and heads the Stigma and Resilience Among Vulnerable Youth Centre\.
