## About

Four presenters from Brazil's Federal University of Ceara collaborate with UBC's Nursing and Pharmaceutical Science faculty to present a two\-day event to demonstrate the value of international collaboration and generate interest in resuming a pre\-pandemic initiative\. Plan to attend one or more sessions in person or via Zoom as follows\.

Zoom Links for each day can be obtained from Suzanne\.Campbell@ubc\.ca
