## About

## The 21st Century Landscape of Professional Nursing in Canada

Please join UBC School of Nursing PhD student Claire Pitcher as she presents an overview of her NURS 690 Directed Studies course taught by Dr\. Lydia Wytenbroek\!

The presentation will explore the landscape of 21st century professional nursing in Canada while grappling with the question, "Is that landscape bountiful or bleak?
