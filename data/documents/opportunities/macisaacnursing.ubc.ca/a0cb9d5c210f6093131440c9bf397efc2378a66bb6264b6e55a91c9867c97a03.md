## About

UBC Thriving Kids Research Cluster webinar

# Exploring the Healthcare Experiences of Racialized Newcomer Families of Children with Medical Complexity

-   WHEN: June 25, 2025, 12PM\-1PM PST
-   WHERE: Zoom
-   REGISTER FOR WEBINAR HERE: [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/\_hBmiSeTRry8t082j2s\-OQ](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/_hBmiSeTRry8t082j2s-OQ) 

[Dr\. Vanessa Fong](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/vanessa-fong) will be presenting her research findings on the experience of accessing and navigating health and social services by newcomer racialized parents of children with medical complexity in BC\. Findings will be shared to inform policy and practice recommendations that advance health equity and support culturally safe care\.

This webinar is part of a series presented through the [UBC Thriving Kids research cluster](https://thrivingkids.ubc.ca/) led by [Dr\. Jennifer Baumbusch](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/jennifer-baumbusch), which is a network of researchers, clinicians, advocates and community members working together to re\-imagine supports for children with medical complexity and their families\.
