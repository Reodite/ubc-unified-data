## PRECEPTOR AWARDS

**2023 Erickson Kline Moerike Nurse Practitioner Preceptor Award** 

*This award recognizes excellent NP preceptors in the Master of Nursing – Nurse Practitioner program at the UBC School of Nursing\.*

-   **Ms\. Danielle Mlinaritsch**
-   **Dr\. Zia Fahim**

**2023 Award for Excellence as a BSN Clinical Preceptor** 

*This award is given to a preceptor who has demonstrated a commitment to teaching, excellence in practice, and fostering positive and respectful learning/practice environments\.* 

-   **Ms\. Mitzi Manansala**
-   **Ms\. Jane Tosoff**

---

## ALUMNI AWARDS

**2023 UBC School of Nursing Recent Alumni Award** 

*This award is given to a UBC Nursing Alumni who, within a relatively short period after graduation \(maximum 10 years\), has demonstrated exceptional engagement in professional, civic or community activities\.* 

-   **Dr\. Charlene Ronquillo**

**2023 UBC School of Nursing Award of Distinction** 

*This award is given to a UBC Nursing Alumni who has made a significant impact by contributing to the advancement of nursing and bringing honour to the nursing profession at a Canadian and/or international level\.*

-   **Dr\. Kelli Stajduhar**

**2023 UBC School of Nursing Alumni Recognition Award** 

*This award is given to a UBC Nursing Alumni who has made a sustained and meaningful contribution to nurses and/or nursing at a local, community and/or provincial level\.*  

-   **Dr\. Chantel Canessa**
