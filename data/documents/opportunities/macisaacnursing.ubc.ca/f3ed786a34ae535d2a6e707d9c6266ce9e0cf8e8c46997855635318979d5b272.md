## About

The UBC Transformative Health &amp; Justice Research Cluster and ART &amp; Justice are co\-hosting **a film screening and fundraiser** at The Cinematique in Vancouver on April 7th @ 530 pm\.   
  
Come enjoy a free screening of SING SING – a documentary about a theatre program offered in Sing Sing Correctional Facility in New York State, and learn about ART &amp; Justice – a local initiative supporting art and storytelling for federally incarcerated people in BC\. Art created by incarcerated artists will be available for sale, with all funds going to purchase high quality art supplies for people in prison\.   
  
This event is open to the public\.No registration required\.
