## About

The overall objective of this workshop is to learn about and discuss academic integrity within the UBC community\. Participants will work through a variety of academic integrity topics in a group learning setting, such as the values related to academic integrity, the importance and benefits of academic integrity, and promoting academic integrity in the classroom\. This workshop will provide resources and information to instructors so they can best support their graduate students in learning with integrity at UBC\.
