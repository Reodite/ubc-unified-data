## About

**Time:** 9am \- 1pm

[Image removed\.](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/core/misc/icons/e32700/error.svg)

The UBC Graduate Students in Nursing Association \(GSNA\) is thrilled to announce that the annual Research Symposium will be held virtually on November 2nd, 2022, from 9am\-1pm\. This year's symposium will showcase research and projects of UBC’s nursing graduate students\. In light of the many challenges faced by nursing in recent years both in Canada and internationally, the theme this year’s symposium will be the “future of nursing”\.

Submission will be open to all graduate students in the UBC School of Nursing; however, all members of the school are welcome to attend, including faculty, staff, and undergraduate students\.

We are also excited to announce that the keynote speakers will be Amie Archibald\-Varley RN, BScN, MN\-CP Women's Health and Sara Fung, RN, BScN, MN of [The Gritty Nurse](https://www.grittynurse.com/) podcast\.
