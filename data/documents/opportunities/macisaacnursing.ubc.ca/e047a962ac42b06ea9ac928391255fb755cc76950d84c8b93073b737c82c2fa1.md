## About

Registration is now open for the Science of Physiologic Birth Cluster’s upcoming webinar, “The Health Impacts of Unconventional Oil and Gas Development \(Fracking\) on Neonatal Outcomes for Mothers and Infants” with Élyse Caron\-Beaudoin and Dr\. Margaret McGregor on June 10 at 2pm \(PT\)\.

The webinar will explore the health impacts of unconventional oil and gas development \(fracking\) on mothers and infants, highlighting epidemiological evidence and findings from a biomonitoring study of pregnant women living near fracking sites in Northeast British Columbia, Canada\.
