## About

### Men’s health equity in the context of depression and the commercial determinants of health

**Presenter**: [Dr\. John Oliffe](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/john-oliffe)

**Abstract**: This UBC Nursing Rounds presentation from Dr\. John Oliffe offers an overview of nationally funded projects in men’s intimate partner relationships and peer support suicide prevention programs with a focus on health equity\. Connecting these issues to male depression, the implications for the commercial determinants of men’s health are discussed with a view to scaling and sustaining tailored interventions\.
