## About

**Time**: 12\-1pm

**Learning Objectives:**

-   Raise awareness of caregiver\-reported outcomes research\.
-   Introduce the approaches of qualitative research and patient\-oriented research\.
-   Illustrate our qualitative, patient\-oriented, multidisciplinary approaches in cancer research\.
-   Present initial insights that highlight emotional challenges and preferences for the assessment and discussion of caregiver\-reported outcomes\.
