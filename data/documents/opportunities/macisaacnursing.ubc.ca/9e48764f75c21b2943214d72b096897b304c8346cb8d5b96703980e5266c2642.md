**About the Study**

The long\-term care home sector is navigating two urgent priorities in the context of climate change: emergency management during extreme weather events, and building a climate\-resilient health care system through environmental sustainability initiatives\.

**Background**

In the Fall of 2025, our team conducted a survey with long\-term care home operators in British Columbia, Canada to better understand how they are addressing these challenges\.

**Webinar**

In this one\-hour webinar, we will share key findings from the survey and discuss practical recommendations for strengthening climate readiness across the sector, with a focus on affiliated/contracted sites\.

**Study Team and Funding**

Dr\. Jennifer Baumbusch from the UBC School of Nursing and Lara Croll from the BC Care Providers Association are the project co\-leads\. This study was funded by Michael Smith Health Research BC\.
