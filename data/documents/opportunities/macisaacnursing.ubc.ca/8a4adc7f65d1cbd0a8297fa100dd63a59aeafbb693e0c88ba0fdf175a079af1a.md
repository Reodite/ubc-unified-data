## About

###### UBC NURSING ROUNDS

•    Presenter: Dr\. Helen Brown, Sarah Sutanto, &amp; Zoë Esseiva   
•    Date: Thursday, Nov\. 5, 2026 @ 12\-1pm  
•    Venue: Room 5228, 5th floor, UBC Gateway Health Building or online via Zoom

In this dialogue, we would like to invite participants to consider the question: why might nursing need anarchism? 

We will introduce key principles and practices of anarchism, such as mutual aid, direct action, care as political, and militant joy\. 

We will invite attendees to consider extending nursing’s social justice mandate to a praxis that resists, dismantles, and builds alternative futures for a more just society\.

### Zoë Esseiva \(they/them/she/her\) and Sarah Sutanto \(she/her\) 

Zoë Esseiva \(they/them/she/her\) and Sarah Sutanto \(she/her\) are academically feral graduate students and anarchist nurses in the School of Nursing at UBC\. The two have been in conversation with Dr\. Helen Brown through a directed studies course on anarchist nursing philosophy\.  

Zoë is a PhD student studying workplace violence with Dr\. Farinaz Havaei\. Zoë's clinical background spans emergency nursing and primary care\. 

Sarah is an MSN student studying nurse\-patient ratios with Dr\. Farinaz Havaei\. Sarah’s clinical background in medical\-surgical nursing and long\-term care nursing education\. 

### Dr\. Helen Brown

Dr\. Brown’s research uses community\-based and participatory methods to support equity and wellbeing with and for incarcerated and Indigenous Peoples across Western Canada\. Her work spans healthcare and carceral settings, aiming to support individual and community priorities around health, wellness, cultural continuity, and language revitalization\. Her research network includes Indigenous Elders and communities, Peers with lived experience of incarceration, non\-profits, and Correctional Service of Canada\.
