## About

*PHSA’s minimum Nurse\-to\-Patient Ratio \(mNPR\) team and Dr\. Farinaz Havaei are pleased to present:*

## Virtual Learning Round  
Insights into Nurse\-to\-Patient Ratio Research

Host: Shardae Brown, Director mNPR, Provincial Health Services Authority  
Speakers: [Dr\. Farinaz \(Naz\) Havaei](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/farinaz-naz-havaei), Associate Professor, UBC Nursing &amp; Claire Song, PhD student, UBC Nursing

Attendees will gain insight into:

-   The research questions guiding this work
-   Methodologies used to explore nurse\-to\-patient ratios
-   Engagement strategies with frontline staff and partners
-   Key findings and emerging themes
-   Next steps in this research
