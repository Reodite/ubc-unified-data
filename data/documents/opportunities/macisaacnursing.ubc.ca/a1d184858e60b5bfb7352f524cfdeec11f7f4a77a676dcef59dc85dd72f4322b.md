## About

# NURSING ROUNDS \- **People, Processes and Products in the Context of the Men’s Depression and Suicide Network**

**Time:** 8:00\-8:50 am

This presentation showcases the six intervention products and the networks evaluation outcomes\. An overview and retrospective of the centralized processes for knowledge dissemination is provided along with details about the catalyzing efforts of people working within the network\. Chronicled also are lessons that can aid research design and project management in an array of contexts\.
