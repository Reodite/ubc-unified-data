## Realist Review In Action 

### With Dr\. Maura MacPhee

Please join a series of sessions spanning from 9 am to 4 pm on Friday, July 3, 2026, at The University of Victoria \(UVic\)\.

**"Realist Review in Action"\-\-14:15 pm to 15:15 pm**, with professor emeritus [**Dr\. Maura MacPhee**](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/maura-macphee "Maura MacPhee")\. 

This session brings realist reviews to life through two applied case studies, demonstrating both their practical value and methodological depth\. 

Dr\. Maura MacPhee presents a review conducted in partnership with the Medical Director of Long\-Term Care at Fraser Health to walk through the key methodological steps of a realist review\. This case will emphasize the critical role of identifying underlying mechanisms, as well as the importance of sustained stakeholder engagement\. 

The second presentation \(Dr\. Nancy Clark \- UVic\) explores a realist review on integrated mental health care for refugees, illustrating how context\-sensitive findings can inform more responsive and equitable service delivery for complex populations\.
