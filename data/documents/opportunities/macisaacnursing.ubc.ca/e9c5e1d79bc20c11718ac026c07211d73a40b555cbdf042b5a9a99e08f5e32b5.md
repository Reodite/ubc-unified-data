## About

DATE**: April 30\[th\] @ 2\-3:30pm**\. 

VENUE: **Kaiser Building, Room 2020/2030** \(second floor\)

The Scholarship of Teaching and Research \(STaR\) committee is pleased to announce the UBC School of Nursing’s inaugural Faculty Research Showcase, an event created to encourage connection, creativity, and meaningful discussions within our community\. 

Attendees will have the opportunity to explore research posters from Nursing faculty members, providing an engaging space for researchers to present their work, share insights, and exchange innovative ideas\.
