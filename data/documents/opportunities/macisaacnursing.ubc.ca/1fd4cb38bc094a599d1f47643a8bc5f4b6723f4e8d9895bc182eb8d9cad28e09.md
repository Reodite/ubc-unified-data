## About

INNOVATIVE METHODS &amp; EMERGING CHALLENGES IN RESEARCH WITH SEXUAL &amp; GENDER DIVERSE YOUTH  
  
**Guest Speaker:** Dr\. Ryan Watson, Associate Professor, University of Connecticut  
  
**Where:** UBC School of Nursing \(Room T182\), 3rd floor of the UBC Hospital, 2211 Wesbrook Mall  
**When:** Wednesday, November 26  
**Time:** 3:00–4:30 PM \(PT\)  
**Hosted by:** UBC [Stigma and Resilience Among Vulnerable Youth Centre](https://www.saravyc.ubc.ca/) \(SARAVYC\)  
  
**About the talk**

Dr\. Ryan Watson will share his ongoing research using a large national dataset of sexual and gender diverse youth\. His talk will highlight:  
  
• New insights from harmonizing two major Sexual and Gender Diverse Youth datasets  
• Use of novel analytic methods \(e\.g\., Exhaustive CHAID\)  
• How online data is being affected by mischievous and fraudulent responses  
• Which groups of Sexual and Gender Diverse Youth experience disproportionate burdens in health behaviors based on intersections of gender, sexuality, race, and ethnicity  
  
**Who should attend?**

Researchers, graduate students, clinicians, and anyone working in or interested in Sexual and Gender Diverse youth health, resilience, data methods, and ethics in youth research\.
