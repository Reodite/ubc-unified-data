## About

**Alzheimer Society of BC** *WEBINAR*

-   Thursday, June 22 from noon to 1 p\.m\.

In recent years, B\.C\. has experienced a rapid increase in climate\-related weather emergencies including heat domes, atmospheric rivers and wildfires\. People living with dementia are uniquely at risk during climate emergencies\. In this webinar, [Dr\. Jennifer Baumbusch](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/jennifer-baumbusch), professor and Canadian Institutes of Health Research Chair in Sex and Gender Science at the UBC School of Nursing, shares strategies to help people living with dementia and their care partners navigate these challenging situations\.

For info: [https://alzheimer\.ca/bc/en/help\-support/programs\-services/webinars](https://alzheimer.ca/bc/en/help-support/programs-services/webinars)
