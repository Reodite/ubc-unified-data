## About

### Community Health Nurses of Canada \(CHNC\) 

presents their 2026 conference: 

## Growing Together In Action \- Strengthening the Roots of Community Health Nursing\!

Join hosts [Sue Foster](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/sue-foster "Sue Foster"), [Jill Mahy](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/jill-mahy "Jill Mahy") and [Ranjit Dhari](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/ranjit-dhari "Ranjit Dhari") for a three\-day conference on campus in Vancouver\. 

The National Community Health Nursing Conference attracts community health nurses from every province and territory across Canada\. It brings together between 200\-300 nurses, educators and administrators from public health, home health and other areas of Community Health Nursing\.
