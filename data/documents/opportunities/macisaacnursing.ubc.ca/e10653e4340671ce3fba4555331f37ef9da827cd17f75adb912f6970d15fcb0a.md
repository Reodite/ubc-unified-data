## About

**Time**: 12\-1pm

**Description**: Come for a 'must\-know' overview from the [International Institute of Qualitative Methodology](https://www.ualberta.ca/international-institute-for-qualitative-methodology) \(IIQM\), the world's largest qualitative academic educational institute, and learn about the world of qualitative methods and how these can add value to your work\.  Suitable for all disciplines and career stages, especially non\-qualitative researchers\.
