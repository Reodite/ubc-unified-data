## About

The Providence Health Care Centre for Research Training \(CREST\) for Nursing and Allied Health is hosting its first annual [CREST Conference](https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/media/9671) on November 6\[th\], at St\. Paul’s Hospital \(with virtual attendance options\)\. [Registration](https://surveys.vch.ca/4cc3cb38-21c7-428d-8884-e1d19adc26f4) is encouraged to help us plan for the event and share communication, and there is no cost to attend\. Attendees may drop\-in to the sessions of their choice\.

Nursing professionals are uniquely positioned to contribute to evidence\-generation, being vital members of the interdisciplinary team and seeing the many dimensions of health and service delivery beyond medical treatment\. This conference focuses on the important role of the nursing and allied health clinician\-scientist, with presentations and keynotes delivered by emerging and seasoned clinician\-scientists\.

For more information about this event, contact Riley Louie at [researchtraining@phc\.ca](mailto:researchtraining@phc.ca)\.

At the St\. Paul's Hospital Conference Centre, with UBC\-V Nursing participants [Dr\. Lillian Hung](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/lillian-hung "Lillian Hung") \(one of two keynote addresses\) and [Dr\. Sandra Lauck](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/sandra-lauck "Sandra Lauck") \(panelist\)\.
