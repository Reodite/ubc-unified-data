## About

In an era where healthcare systems are stretched to their limits, nurses are at the forefront, facing daunting challenges every day\. Emergency nurses are under immense strain as severe staffing shortages and dangerous wait times create a high\-stress work environment\. Coupled with poor nurse\-to\-patient ratios, these conditions are leading to a significant decline in mental health among nurses, with many experiencing PTSD\. This increasing pressure is driving numerous skilled nurses away from bedside care, exacerbating the crisis\.

The ENABC 2024 Conference: "Crisis in Emergency" will delve into these pressing issues, providing a platform for discussion, support, and innovative solutions to sustain our nursing workforce\. This conference brings together leading experts, practitioners, and scholars to address these unique challenges\. This year's theme underscores the stigma surrounding mental health while highlighting the critical skills needed for effective emergency resuscitation\.

Join us **November 1st\-3rd, 2024** to gain valuable insights, enhance your skills, and contribute to developing sustainable practices for the future\. Your participation is crucial in shaping a healthier and more sustainable nursing environment\.

### Event Features

-   **In\-Depth Workshops:** Participate in hands\-on sessions focused on advanced emergency resuscitation techniques and mental health strategies for nurses\.
-   **Expert Panels:** Hear from top professionals and leaders in nursing, mental health, and emergency care who will share their insights and experiences\.
-   **Interactive Discussions:** Engage in meaningful dialogues about the challenges and solutions related to staffing shortages, dangerous wait times, and nurse\-to\-patient ratios\.
-   **Networking Opportunities:** Connect with fellow nursing professionals, share experiences, and build a supportive community\.
-   **Innovative Solutions:** Discover the latest research, tools, and practices designed to improve mental health and support nurses in their roles\.
-   **Resource Exhibition:** Explore a wide range of resources from leading healthcare organizations aimed at supporting nursing staff in emergency settings\.

### Target Audience

-   **Nurses and Nursing Students:** Those working in or aspiring to enter emergency care, seeking to enhance their skills and knowledge\.
-   **Mental Health Professionals:** Practitioners interested in the intersection of mental health and emergency nursing\.
-   **Emergency Medical Practitioners:** Healthcare providers focused on improving patient outcomes and nurse well\-being in high\-pressure environments\.
-   **Healthcare Administrators and Leaders:** Decision\-makers looking to understand the challenges faced by nursing staff and implement effective solutions\.
-   **Researchers and Educators:** Academics and trainers dedicated to advancing the field of nursing and emergency care through education and research\.
-   **Healthcare Policy Makers:** Individuals involved in shaping policies that affect the nursing profession and emergency healthcare systems\.
