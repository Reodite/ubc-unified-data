## About

Dr\. Jamie Forrest, postdoctoral research fellow, will give an oral presentation at UBC Postdoc Research Day 2025 on **"Mapping AI Diagnostic Innovation Networks in Sub\-Saharan Africa: A Synthetic Network Analysis of Trials, Institutions, and Clinical Research Funding\."** His postdoc supervisor is Dr\. Meaghan Thumath\.

Postdoc Research Day celebrates the diverse research of UBC’s postdoctoral fellows and research associates\. The day will feature oral and poster presentations, keynote talks, and networking opportunities with peers and faculty across disciplines\. The theme this year is “Fostering Diversity and Innovation in Uncertain Times”\.

Research Day runs from 9 am to 6 pm on December 1st, 2025, at the Life Sciences Centre LSC 1002\.
