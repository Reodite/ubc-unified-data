## About

The University of Western Ontario's Arthur Labatt Family School of Nursing is virtually hosting an event in recognition of Black History Month featuring the UBC School of Nursing's [Dr\. Emmanuela Ojukwu](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/emmanuela-ojukwu)\. The event is organized by the school's Decolonization, Anti\-Racism, Anti\-Oppression \(DARAO\) Committee and co\-hosted with the Canadian Black Nurses' Alliance \(CBNA\) \- Western Chapter to recognize Black History Month\.
