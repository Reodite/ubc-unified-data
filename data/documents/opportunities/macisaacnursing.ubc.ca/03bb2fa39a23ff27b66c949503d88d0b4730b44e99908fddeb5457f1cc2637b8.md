# Putting Patients First 2026

Are you interested in exploring collaborative ways to improve BC’s health system? Don’t miss the **Putting Patients First 2026** conference \(PPF26\)\!

On **March 3, 2026**, learn how bringing people of diverse experiences together to solve shared health problems – including people with lived and living experience – can improve health and health research in BC\. This event will continue the discussion on learning communities and learning health systems in the province\.

Everyone is welcome to attend, from patients to healthcare providers to policymakers\. Attend in\-person in Vancouver or online\.

Hosted by the [BC SUPPORT Unit](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bc-support-unit/), part of [Michael Smith Health Research BC](https://www.linkedin.com/company/michael-smith-health-research-bc/), this event is being developed and organized with Patients Included\[TM\] principles\.

Spots are limited\. If you are interested, you are invited to register as soon as possible to secure your spot\.
