## About

This webinar brings together scholars, researchers, and academic leaders\. Through expert discussions, we will examine leadership development, organizational influences on leadership effectiveness, and the critical dynamics between leadership and followership\. Additionally, we will highlight key areas for future research and innovation in leadership, drawing from diverse disciplinary perspectives\.

Register online for a 1\.5 hour webinar featuring Dr\. Julian Barling, Dr\. Elizabeth Saewyc, and Dr\. Leigh Chapman\.
