# Shaping the future of nursing in BC: The role of the Chief Nursing Advisor

The Ministry of Health recently appointed a Chief Nursing Advisor in the Province of B\.C\. The role, through engagement and collaboration, will recommend how to optimize the role of nursing in British Columbia\. Join this discussion to understand the work of the Nursing Policy Secretariat and provide input and advice to shape the future of nursing in B\.C\.

**Bio**: [Dr\. David Byres](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/david-byres), DNP, MSN, BA, RN, CHE  was recently appointed to the role of Chief Nursing Advisor in B\.C\. He is a registered nurse who has held senior executive positions in health care in BC for more than 10 years\. He is also an Adjunct Professor with the UBC School of Nursing\.
