## Future BC Children's Hospital Centre for Health Complexity: Information Sharing and Q&amp;A

Please join online for a presentation from Tessa Diaczun and Tamara Crozier on the new BC Children's Hospital centre for health complexity\. The centre is slated to open in early 2028 and will be the first centre of its kind in Canada\. Presentation will be followed by a Q &amp; A session\.

For more information on the centre and timeline for completion: [https://www\.bcchildrens\.ca/about\-us/current\-projects/centre\-health\-complexity](https://www.bcchildrens.ca/about-us/current-projects/centre-health-complexity)
