## About

Pride Month at SARAVYC

Experiences of South Asian Sexual Minority Youth in Canada  
June 19, 2025 @ 12:30\-1:00 PM  

As part of Pride Month at [SARAVYC](https://saravyc.ubc.ca/), join us for a webinar featuring [Dr\. Monica Rana](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/monica-rana), who will share findings from recent research on South Asian sexual minority youth in Canada\. The presentation will highlight results from the 2023 BC Adolescent Health Survey and a qualitative study exploring the experiences of stigma and the role of protective factors—such as connectedness and community—in supporting youth mental health and resilience\.
