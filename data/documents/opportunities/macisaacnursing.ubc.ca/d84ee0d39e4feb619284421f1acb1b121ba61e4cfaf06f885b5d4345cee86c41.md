## About

# Journeys in Care: Stories from Nurses of Indian Heritage 

This exhibit was curated by [Dr\. Lydia Wytenbroek](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/lydia-wytenbroek "Lydia Wytenbroek"), with the support of [Ranjit Dhari](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/ranjit-dhari "Ranjit Dhari"), and with several UBC students and educators as contributors\. 

The exhibit explores these themes: Coming to Canada, Becoming a Nurse, Nursing Education, Firsts and Challenges, &amp; Inspiring the Next Generation, and includes two filmed interviews and 3 audio kiosks in which 5 nurses share their stories in their own words\.

In advance of the formal opening of the exhibit, Surrey Museum hosted a very successful "Preview" Launch Event on March 14th\. The exhibit runs until July 19, 2026\. Admission is free\.
