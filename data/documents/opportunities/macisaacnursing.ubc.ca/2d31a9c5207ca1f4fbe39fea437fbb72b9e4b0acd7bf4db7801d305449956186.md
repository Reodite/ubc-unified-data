## About

Sepsis is accountable for one in every five global deaths, impacting 75,000 Canadians annually\. With proper care, sepsis can be managed, saving countless lives\. 

Curious about enhancing your preparedness to tackle sepsis? Healthcare trainees \(medical &amp; nursing students\), health professionals, researchers, patient partners and others, are invited to join us for a **FREE** event\. Hear a survivor story and learn about global and local efforts to improve sepsis care at Action on Sepsis’ **World Sepsis Day, September 13, 2023\.** 

in\-person \(FREE food\) or virtually\.
