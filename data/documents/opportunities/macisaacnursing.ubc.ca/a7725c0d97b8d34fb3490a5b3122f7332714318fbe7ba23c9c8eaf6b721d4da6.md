## About

[LIVING WELL WITH CANCER IN LATER LIFE](https://accesslab.nursing.ubc.ca/2026/02/02/living-well-with-cancer-in-later-life-march-31-ubc-o-kelowna/)  
TUESDAY, MARCH 31, 2026  
UNC 200 – UNIVERSITY BALLROOM – University of British Columbia Okanagan, 3272 University Way, Kelowna V1V 1V7  
2:00 – 4:00PM PST  
Light refreshments served

Bringing UBC\-O’s Embrace Aging month to a close, this feature event brings together clinical and research experts to explore how older adults can maintain wellbeing, function, and quality of life throughout the cancer journey\. The event will feature a keynote presentation by Dr\. Lauren Capozzi, followed by an expert panel with Dr\. Flavia DeAngelis, Bonnie Leung, and Dr\. Capozzi, facilitated by Dr\. Kristen Haase\. Together, they will discuss practical, evidence\-based strategies to support resilience, recovery, and healthy aging before, during, and after cancer treatment\.

What You’ll Learn:

-   Strategies to support physical function, energy, and independence in later life with cancer
-   How approaches like rehabilitation, supportive care, and lifestyle interventions can improve wellbeing
-   Ways to strengthen resilience, recovery, and emotional health during and after treatment
-   How care teams can better support older adults and their caregivers
