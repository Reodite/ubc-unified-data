## About

Please join NNPBC online for an informative learning series on climate change\. Learn more about nursing’s important role in helping communities adapt to climate change and building a more sustainable future\.

Kaitlin Bloemberg \(formerly Reich\) is a recent MSN graduate and the winner of the 2022 Excellence in SPAR project award\. As a result of her SPAR, she will be presenting some of the work she did last semester\. She presents alongside Helen Boyd at the NNPBC Climate Change Series on Climate Justice and Extreme Heat Events\.
