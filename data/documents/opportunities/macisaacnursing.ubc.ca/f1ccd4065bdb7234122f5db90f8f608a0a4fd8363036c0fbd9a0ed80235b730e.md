## About

**Thursday, May 13, 2021 \| 6:30 PM \| VIA Zoom**

This annual event is an opportunity to thank you, our alumni and supporters who have made a deep and lasting commitment to nursing education, teaching, and research\. You won’t need to commute or find parking, but we encourage you to see this event as an opportunity to sparkle\!

The 2021 UBC School of Nursing awardees are:

**Caryn Dooner** \| Recent Alumni Award  
**Lynne Palmer** \| Alumni Recognition Award  
**Marilynn “Marnie” Wood** \| Alumni Award of Distinction  
**Jacqueline Lum** \| Erickson Kline Moerike Nurse Practitioner Preceptor Award  
**Dr\. Diane Wozny** \| Erickson Kline Moerike Nurse Practitioner Preceptor Award  
**Rajwinder Kaur** \| BSN Preceptor Award  
**Kathy Paradis** \| BSN Preceptor Award
