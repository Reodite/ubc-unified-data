## About

**Mixed\-Methods Evaluation of Dan's Legacy: A Trauma\-Informed Counselling and Wrap\-Around Support Program for Youth**  
  
Join online for the next Lunch with [SARAVYC](https://saravyc.ubc.ca/) session with [Dr\. Monica Rana](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/monica-rana) and [Dr\. Elizabeth Saewyc](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/elizabeth-saewyc)\.

In 30 minutes, the presenters will share results from a mixed\-methods evaluation of the Dan’s Legacy program, which provides trauma\-informed counselling and wraparound support for youth across the Lower Mainland\.  
  
You’ll hear:  
•    How the evaluation was conducted  
•    Key findings about youth outcomes  
•    Practical implications for service providers and researchers
