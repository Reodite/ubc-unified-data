## About

The School of Nursing regrets that the Nursing Awards Gala will not be held in 2020\. 

This annual event is our opportunity to thank all of you – our alumni and supporters who have made a deep and lasting commitment to nursing education, teaching, and research\. In spite of current restrictions, we are committed to presenting a number of awards – the Award of Distinction, the Alumni Recognition Award, the Young Alumni Award, and the School of Nursing Preceptor awards – to celebrate the special contributions and achievements of our colleagues\. Watch this space for further details\.

Thank you to all of our recipients for their contributions to the nursing community and for their efforts to improve nursing and health care for patients and practitioners here in BC and around the world\.

For further information regarding the School of Nursing Alumni and Partnership Awards Gala, please contact our APSC Alumni Engagement Office at [alumni@apsc\.ubc\.ca](mailto:alumni@apsc.ubc.ca)\.\.
