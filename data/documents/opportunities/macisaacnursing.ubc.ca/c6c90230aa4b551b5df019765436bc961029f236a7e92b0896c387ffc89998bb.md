## Call for Speakers and Posters

**VIRTUAL CONFERENCE DATES:** OCTOBER 6\-7, 2021 8:30 AM – 12:30 PM  
**SPEAKER APPLICATION DEADLINE**: JULY 1, 2021  
**POSTER APPLICATION DEADLINE**: AUGUST 1, 2021

We are pleased to invite interested speakers to submit proposals for presentations and posters at our 18th annual conference\! We seek dynamic speakers to present on their knowledge of patient safety and effectively engage a diverse set of attendees in a virtual space\.

We accept applicants from across the care spectrum and strongly encourage patients and family members to submit proposals about their experiences\. This year’s conference is a regional collaboration between Washington, Oregon, and British Columbia, and we welcome speakers with knowledge of the diversity of our Pacific Northwest Region\.

DETAILS in the attached poster, or at the Northwest Patient Safety Conference [website](https://www.qualityhealth.org/wpsc/wpsc-events/patient-safety-conference/)\.
