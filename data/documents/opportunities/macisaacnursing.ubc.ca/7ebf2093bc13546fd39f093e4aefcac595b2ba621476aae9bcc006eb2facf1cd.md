## About

Hello Nursing Graduate Students,

The DFP CREATE Design Showcase will provide an opportunity for graduate students to share their research with an interdisciplinary network of researchers and industry partners\. We encourage graduate students from Nursing to submit their research to the showcase\. To find more information about the showcase, you can visit our website\.

[**DFP Website**](https://dfp.ubc.ca/)

### DEADLINES

-   For students who may be interested in submitting their research, we ask that you **submit a notice of intent by April 6th** so that we can organize the event to accommodate your poster or demo\.
-   You may **submit your poster or demo by May 4th\.**

We hope to see students from Nursing share their design research with the DFP community at UBC and beyond\!
