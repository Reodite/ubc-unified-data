## About

THRIVING KIDS UBC RESEARCH CLUSTER: WEBINAR SERIES

Presenters: BC Complex Kids Society

This webinar highlights findings from the BC Complex Kids Society’s recent white paper, *Beyond Survival: Recommendations for Transforming Support Systems for Children &amp; Youth with Medical Complexity and Their Families in British Columbia*\.

-   Includes a Q&amp;A and interactive discussion\. 
-   Please find the downloadable White Paper here: [https://www\.bccomplexkids\.ca/advocacy](https://www.bccomplexkids.ca/advocacy) 

This inaugural webinar is hosted by the **UBC Thriving Kids Research Cluster**, a network of researchers, clinicians, advocates and community members led by [Dr\. Jennifer Baumbusch](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/jennifer-baumbusch), to re\-imagine supports for children with medical complexity and their families\.
