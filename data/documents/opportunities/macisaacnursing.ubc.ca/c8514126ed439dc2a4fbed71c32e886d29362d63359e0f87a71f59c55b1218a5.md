## About

Each virtual hour\-long session takes place on the last Wednesday of the month\. The sessions are broken into a 35\-minute presentation, a 10\-minute response from the respondent, and a 15\-minute Q&amp;A with attendees\.

**Wednesday March 29\[th\] from noon\-1:00pm**

Researcher: Dr\. Kanna Hayashi

Respondent: Dean Wilson

Topic: Vancouver Injection Drug User Study \(VIDUS\)

[Register](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/551839666297) 

**Wednesday April 26\[th\] from noon\-1:00pm**

Researcher: Dr\. Danya Fast

Respondent:  Kali\-olt Sedgemore

Topic: Youth Harm Reduction Calls for Action 

[Register](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/553636340197)

**Wednesday May 31\[st\] from noon\-1:00pm**

Researcher: Dr\. Lindsey Richardson

Respondent: Tara Taylor

Topic: Social/Economic Disadvantage and Well\-being among Drug Users

[Register](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/553677573527) 

We invite anyone with an interest in the substance use field to join us for this speaker series\. This includes people with lived and living experience, their friends and family, frontline responders, program and service providers, clinicians, policymakers, researchers and their staff, the media, and anyone in the general public\. If you are receiving this invitation, we felt that you or someone in your organization might be interested in attending\. Please share this invitation with any friends or colleagues who might be interested in attending\!

Looking forward to seeing you there\!

To receive updates on this series, [sign up for our newsletter](https://www.bccsu.ca/research-dialogue-sign-up/)\.
