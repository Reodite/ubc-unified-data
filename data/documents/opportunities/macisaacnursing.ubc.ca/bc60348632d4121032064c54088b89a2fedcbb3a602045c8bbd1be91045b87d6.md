## About

 **Transforming Health for Everyone: Strengthening Medical Curriculum in an era of Truth, Reconciliation and Inclusion**

Join REDI virtually on **Wednesday, November 26 from 12–2 pm** for an *It Starts With Us* panel discussion on transforming the Faculty of Medicine’s curriculum to better reflect Indigenization, decolonization, and inclusion\. Hear from **Derek K Thompson, Dr\. Nadine Caron, Lauren Casey, Dr\. Rose Hatala, and Shannon Field**, who are reimagining teaching and learning to better prepare future health professionals to serve B\.C\.’s diverse communities\.
