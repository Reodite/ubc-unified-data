## About

Dr\. Emily Carasco and her student Emily Vincent from the University of Calgary will be giving a talk titled, **"Incorporating Inclusive Technologies and Best Practices for Supportive Engineering Education"**

While their presentation focuses on post\-secondary engineering education, it is open to everyone at APSC and UBC interested in exploring software tools that are useful for students with a range of neurodiversities\. We would also appreciate if you shared this invite broadly with colleagues and students interested in the subject area in and outside APSC\.
