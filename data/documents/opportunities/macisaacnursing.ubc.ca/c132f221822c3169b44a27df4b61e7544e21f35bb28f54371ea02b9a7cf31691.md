## About

This workshop will discuss the application process for Tri\-Council Doctoral Competitions \(CIHR &amp; SSHRC\), and discuss the School of Nursing Internal Review process\. The internal review is a review process that allows students to receive feedback on their application prior to final submission to the CIHR or Faculty of Graduate &amp; Postdoctoral Studies \(G\+PS\) \(for SSHRC and UBC Affiliated Awards\)\.

We encourage all eligible doctoral students to attend\. 

If you have questions about the workshop, please contact [Merrilee Hughes](mailto:onrts@nursing.ubc.ca?subject=CIHR%20Doctoral%20Workshop%3A%20Event%20Question) in the Office of Nursing Research &amp; Teaching Scholarship \(ONRTS\)\.  If you have questions about Graduate Scholarships for incoming UBC Students, please contact Rana Hakami \- Manager, Student Services\.
