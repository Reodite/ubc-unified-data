Substance use among Canadian youth is an urgent and complex challenge with far\-reaching implications for educational equity, mental wellness, and community participation\. Schools are important social and structural determinants of health and are uniquely positioned to reduce substance\-related harms through upstream prevention that promotes engagement, belonging, and resilience\. Yet school staff report a lack of evidence\-informed guidance and system\-level support to inform their efforts\. 

The Anchoring Change initiative brings together national partners to address this gap through a new pan\-Canadian Standard that translates current research into actionable, voluntary guidance for K–12 systems\. Led by Wellstream: The Canadian Centre for Innovation in Child and Youth Mental Health and Substance Use at the MacIsaac School of Nursing at UBC, the Canadian Association of School System Administrators, the Canadian Centre on Substance Use and Addiction, Physical and Health Education Canada, and the Students Commission of Canada, the initiative aims to reduce burden, strengthen consistency in practice, and support more effective school\-based substance use prevention, education, and intervention\.

This session will introduce the Standard and the broader Anchoring Change initiative\. Attendees will learn how the Standard was developed, including its underpinning evidence base and structure, and explore the next phase of work to support implementation across school communities\.

**BIOS:**

### **Dr\. Emily Jenkins** 

Dr\. Emily Jenkins is a Registered Nurse and Professor in the MacIsaac School of Nursing at the University of British Columbia\. She is also the Scientific Director of Wellstream and holds a Canada Research Chair in Population Health Approaches to Mental Health and Substance Use\. Her research is responsive to emergent, priority health and social issues and includes interventions to promote mental health and well\-being and minimize the harms of substance use for children, youth, and communities\. Dr\. Jenkins co\-leads the Anchoring Change initiative, which recently released Canada’s new standard for substance use prevention, education, and intervention in K\-12 schools, representing a significant step in translating research into coordinated action across Canadian school systems\.

### **Dr\. Tonje Molyneux** 

Dr\. Tonje Molyneux is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Wellstream and a CASEL Weissberg Scholar\. She brings over twenty years of experience in education spanning classroom teaching, the design of social and emotional learning programs, and mixed\-methods research\. Her research program bridges school belonging, social and emotional learning, implementation science, mental health promotion, and risk prevention\. Dr\. Molyneux co\-leads the Anchoring Change initiative and has contributed to the development of the suite of resources supporting implementation of the Standard across K–12 school communities\. She is committed to translating evidence into practical, care\-based approaches that strengthen the everyday work of educators and school\-based mental health professionals\.
