## Leading Through Crisis: Lessons on Reducing Health Inequities and Navigating Disinformation During Health Emergencies\.

with Featured Speaker, [Dr\. Meaghan Thumath](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/our-people/meaghan-anne-thumath "Meaghan Anne Thumath")

Dr\. Meaghan Thumath is a Clinician Scientist and Assistant Professor at the UBC School of Nursing, specializing in health equity during public health emergencies\. Her work bridges clinical practice, research, and policy to strengthen emergency response and preparedness, with a particular focus on human rights, gender, and marginalized populations\.
