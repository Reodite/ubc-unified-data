## About

## **THROUGH THEIR LENS**: THE IMPACT OF CLIMATE CHANGE ON OLDER ADULTS’ MENTAL HEALTH

JOIN US ON OCTOBER 1 FOR INTERNATIONAL DAY OF OLDER PERSONS

-   IN PERSON PANEL DISCUSSION: OCTOBER 1, 2026, **@ 5:00\-6:30 PM PST**
-   WEBINAR: OCTOBER 1, 2026 **@ 12PM\-1PM PST**
-   REGISTER HERE: [HTTPS://UBC\.ZOOM\.US/MEETING/REGISTER/HMQRTRLQQCUUGHEJ38OFMA](https://ubc.zoom.us/MEETING/REGISTER/HMQRTRLQQCUUGHEJ38OFMA) 

Join Dr\. Jennifer Baumbusch and team for the launch of this travelling exhibition, featuring photographs by older adults in British Columbia exploring how climate change impacts their mental health\. The purpose of this exhibition is to amplify public dialogue about climate change and aging\.

For more information, please email us at [gero@nursing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:gero@nursing.ubc.ca)
