## About

In this session, Dr\. Bernie Garrett will report on a 2015 case series exploring the home use of VR therapy with chronic pain patients\. A live demonstration will also be available\.

**Bio**: Dr\. Bernie Garrett has a background as a Clinical Nurse Specialist in the UK, and a PHD in information science\. He studies health applications of virtual and augmented reality, and deceptive practices in healthcare\.
