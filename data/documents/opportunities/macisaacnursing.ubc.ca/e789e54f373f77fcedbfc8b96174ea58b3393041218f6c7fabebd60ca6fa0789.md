## About

**Time:** 12\-1pm

Britney Dennison and Andrew Munroe share proven approaches on how to plan, execute, and disseminate research films from the [Men’s Health Research](http://menshealthresearch.ubc.ca/) unit\.

Using the short films Evidence of a Struggle, Contact\! Unload, and Still Here as case studies, each of which served a very different purpose, we’ll look at some of the ways that films can be a big help in meeting the overall goals of our health research\. We’ll also touch on the practical side, including budgeting, realistic timelines, and storyboarding\.
