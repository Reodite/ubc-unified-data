## About

**Time**: 12\-1pm

**[Watch Recording](https://youtu.be/DISHUOg7lzk)**

Join UBC Behaviour Ethics Research Board \(BREB\) senior staff, Jean Ruiz and Maria Valente, for this practical workshop on:

-   Reducing anxiety around getting BREB approvals
-   Clarify guidelines on differences between QA/QI versus research
-   The benefits of going through the ethics process
-   The types of supports – templates, samples, checklists, BREB staff
-   Mapping the steps and typical timelines
-   Common issues in educational health profession related research
-   Power over relationships \(students\-teachers\)
-   Student recruitment – ethical considerations
-   Consent
-   PLUS a couple of case examples
