## About

5:00 PM PST

As part of **Indigenous Peoples Month**, dancers from coast to coast to coast gather online, Dancing to Honour\. Event sponsored by the UBC cluster *Transform Health Justice* for the **Indian Residential School Survivors Society** to uplift and support the families and survivors affected by the issue of Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and Girls\. Transform Health Justice, led by Helen Brown \(Nursing\) and Amanda Slaunwhite \(BCCDC\) is collaborating to sponsor the event\.
