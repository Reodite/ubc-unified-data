## About

UBC Nursing Faculty Research Showcase

WHO**: Nursing FACULTY ONLY**  
DATE**: Wednesday, April 29, 2026 @ 2\-3:30pm** *\(followed by a faculty social\)*  
VENUE: **UBC Gateway Health Building in Room 5120** \(5\[th\] floor\)

The Scholarship of Teaching and Research \(STaR\) committee is pleased to host the UBC School of Nursing’s second annual Faculty Research Showcase, an event designed to foster connection, innovation, and dialogue across our community\. Attendees will have the opportunity to explore research posters from Nursing faculty members, providing an engaging space for researchers to present their work, share insights, and exchange innovative ideas\. 

Thank\-you to all the faculty who submitted posters\!
