## About

***Partners on the Path: Working with Children with Medical Complexity and their families as they navigate the healthcare system\.***

**Dr\. Jennifer Baumbusch** presents with family partners:

-   Brenda Lenahan, Family Partner 
-   Hoda Mohamoud, Family Partner 
-   Diana Salcedo, Family Partner

Hosted by the Department of Pediatrics, UBC Faculty of Medicine  
Moderated by Dr\. Esther Lee

LEARNING OBJECTIVES

-   Exploring the lived experience of children with medical complexity and their families as they navigate the healthcare system
-   Describe individual and systemic factors that facilitate or hinder meaningful partnerships with this population on their healthcare journey
-   Identify recommendations for practice and policy that support family\-centered care for children with medical complexity and their families

No registration required\. In person only\.
