## About

Empowering Nurses in Research: A Student\-Led Research Symposium

The GSNA is hosting a SoN Research Symposium event on May 16th from 12:30pm to 4:30pm\! This free event is an opportunity to showcase your hard work, network with faculty about SPAR/thesis ideas, and enjoy lunch on us\! Submitting a presentation to this peer\-reviewed event will also be a great addition to your CV\. The research symposium is held the same day as the UBC Nursing Gala for those planning on attending that event\. Prizes will be handed out for the top presentations\!

The tentative schedule of the research symposium is below:

1:00pm \- Symposium Start  
1:30pm \- Keynote Speaker  
2:00pm \- Oral\* &amp; Poster Presentations  
3:00pm \- Meet &amp; Greet with Faculty  
4:00pm \- Award Presentation &amp; Closing Remarks

*\*Please note there are a limited number of spots available for oral presentations\.* 

Registration is open to all nursing students at the undergraduate and graduate levels\. Registration is free for both presenters and attendees\. To register, click here: [https://forms\.gle/ek9FDcFEZEk56FbE8](https://forms.gle/ek9FDcFEZEk56FbE8) 

If you have any question, feel free to reach out to Kayoung Lee \(PhD Student\) at [Klee2@student\.ubc\.ca](mailto:Klee2@student.ubc.ca) or Aliya Khan \(MSN Student\) at [akhan65@student\.ubc\.ca](mailto:akhan65@student.ubc.ca)\. We hope to see you all there\!
