## Join us for a celebration of Nursing Week 2025\!

Gather with friends and colleagues to review the accomplishments and activities of the past year and to award the achievements of three nursing alumni, two preceptors from the BSN program, and two preceptors from the Nurse Practitioner Program\. 

Jack Poole Hall \| Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre 

5:30 pm Doors Open \| Register \| Refreshments  
6:30 pm Presentation of awards  
7:30 pm \- 8:30 pm: Refreshments

**2025 UBC SCHOOL OF NURSING ALUMNI AWARDS**

Recent Alumni Award  
**Arveen Sandhu**

Alumni Recognition Award  
**Maja Kolar**

Award of Distinction \(to be presented in absentia\)  
**Chinnama Baines** 

**2025 UBC SCHOOL OF NURSING PRECEPTOR AWARDS**

Erickson Kline Moerike Nurse Practitioner Preceptor Award  
**Jennifer Lehoux** \(to be presented in absentia\)  
**Navdeep Dhillon**

Award for Excellence as a BSN Preceptor  
**Nathan Uy**  
**Karyne Diwag** \(to be presented in absentia\)
