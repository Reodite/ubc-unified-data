## About

The session will explore opportunities and strategies to bridge research across academic and clinical practice to capitalize on resources, and maximize scientific productivity and knowledge translation\.

**BIOS:**

[*Dr\. Sandra Lauck*](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/sandra-lauck) is a clinician scientist and clinical nurse specialist at the Heart Centre at St\. Paul's Hospital\. Her program of research focuses on the clinical evaluation of patient\-reported outcomes and health care delivery models to support innovation in cardiac care\.

[*Dr\. Jennifer Baumbusch*](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/jennifer-baumbusch) directs a research programme on health care delivery and nursing practice with older adults\. She also has a focus on knowledge translation and exchange, with a particular interest in collaborative approaches involving partnerships with clinicians, decision\-makers and policy\-makers\.
