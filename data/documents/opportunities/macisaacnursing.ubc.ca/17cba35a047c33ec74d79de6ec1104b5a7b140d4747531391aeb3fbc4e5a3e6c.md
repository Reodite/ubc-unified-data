## About

**Time:** 5\-6:30pm PST

in the new Green College series  
*Moving On: New Research on Migration, Borders and Health*

In this first event of the Moving On series, Green College Leading Scholars Dr\. Anna Blakney \(Biomedical Engineering\), Dr\. Matias Margulis \(Public Policy and Global Affairs\) and [Dr\. Lydia Wytenbroek](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/lydia-wytenbroek) \(Nursing\) will engage in a roundtable discussion on the question: How does the global flow of ideas, knowledge and technology shape our research projects in relation to our disciplinary background? They will discuss this question from the perspectives of a nurse and historian, political scientist and bioengineer, and also reflect on how the flow of people, ideas and technologies shapes our world\.
