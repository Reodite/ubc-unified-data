## About

The 7th annual UBC Farm Fall Fair is happening on Saturday, September 27 from 10 a\.m\. to 2 p\.m\. at UBC Farm\! Celebrate the changing seasons and learn about sustainable food\! 🥳🍂

The Fall Fair features many activities from the best\-pie contest, a fall\-themed photo booth, to more than 40 different local vendors selling produce, fruit, mushrooms, eggs, baked goods, beverages, sweet treats, alcohol, artisan goods, and more\! 

Think you’ve got the best pie around? 🥧 Bring your homemade, from\-scratch pie by 11:15 a\.m\. to enter\! Or be on the judging end — taste a sample of the pies by donation and vote on your favourite\. Winners announced at 12:45 p\.m\. with special UBC Farm prizes\!
