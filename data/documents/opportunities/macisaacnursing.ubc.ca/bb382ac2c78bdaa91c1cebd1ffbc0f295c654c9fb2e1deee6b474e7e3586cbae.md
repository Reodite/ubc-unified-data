## About

##### UBC Thriving Kids Research Cluster webinar

Dr\. Ben Mortenson and Dr\. Vanessa Fong will be presenting their research findings around the **experiences of families of children with medical complexities during extreme weather events related to climate change**\. The presentation will also include a firsthand account from one of our family partners, Sandra Kahle, of evacuating from wildfires with a child who has complex medical needs\. The webinar will include recommendations for preparing for future evacuations and will be followed by a Q&amp;A

This webinar is part of a series presented through the [UBC Thriving Kids Research Cluster](https://thrivingkids.ubc.ca/), a network of researchers, clinicians, advocates and community members working together to re\-imagine supports for children with medical complexity and their families\.  

**WHEN:** Friday June 13th, 2025, 12PM\-1PM PST

**WHERE:** Zoom
