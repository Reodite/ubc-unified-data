## About

**The 5th Annual Agnes Dillon Randolph International Nursing History Conference**

The [student\-led book club](https://nursing.ubc.ca/community/news/2021/undergraduate-anti-racism-book-club) has had their paper, *Reading history to transform nursing care,* accepted to the Agnes Dillon Randolph International Nursing History Conference\. The two\-day online conference will be hosted by the Bjoring Centre, University of Virginia School of Nursing in Charlottesville, Virginia\.

Contributors to the paper include: Allison Bray, Elliot Cordingley, Rachel O’Reilly, Emily Peacock, Selyen Singh, Ailsa Sirois, Julie Sou, Hannah Sutherland, Eleanor Wearing, and Lydia Wytenbroek

As part of their 20\-minute presentation, the students, some of whom may not be present due to preceptorship schedules, will provide a brief video, sharing what the book club meant for them\. Please visit this page for the link to that video after the conference\.
