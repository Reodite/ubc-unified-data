## About

We’re excited to invite you to **AI &amp; the Future of Medicine: Bridging AI Innovation and Health Equity**, a 1\.5\-day event hosted by the UBC DASH Research Cluster and MIT Critical Data\. 

This dynamic weekend gathering will explore the intersection of artificial intelligence, health equity, and ethics in healthcare\.  

Highlights include: 

-   **Keynote by Dr\. Leo Celi** \(MIT Laboratory for Computational Physiology\) – *“AI and the Future of Medicine: Teaching Machines to Doubt”* 
    
-   **Interactive and engaging panel discussions** on AI in health education, research and underserved populations 
    
-   **Hands\-on collaborative workshops** tackling real\-world health AI challenges 
    
-   **Networking opportunities** with clinicians, researchers, healthcare professionals, students, and patients 
    

**Event Details** 

-   **Dates:** Saturday, November 15 and Sunday, November 16, 2025  
    
-   **Location:** Life Sciences Centre, UBC Point Grey Campus, Vancouver  
    
-   **Event Page:** For more details, please visit the [website](https://datascienceandhealth.ubc.ca/events/ai-future-medicine-bridging-ai-innovation-and-health-equity) 
    

**Registration** 

-   **Day 1 \(Nov 15, Half Day\): Health AI Summit** – Free \(in\-person &amp; online\) 
    
-   **Day 2 \(Nov 16, Full Day\): Collaborative Workshops**  
    
-   Academics: $25 \($15 early bird until Oct 15\) 
    
-   Industry &amp; for\-profit organizations: $250 \($150 early bird until Oct 15\)
