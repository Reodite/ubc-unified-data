*The Mr\. and Mrs\. P\.A\. Woodward's Foundation has generously supported the annual Marion Woodward Lecture since 1969\.* 

**Thursday, October 16, 2025**   
VIFF Theatre \(1181 Seymour St\)  
In\-Person and online

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is reshaping how nurses practice, learn, and generate new knowledge\. This talk explores the future of nursing with AI by examining practical applications in clinical settings, including patient monitoring\. It also considers the profound implications AI holds for nursing education—how we teach and what we teach in an increasingly digital environment\. Key challenges such as digital literacy gaps, algorithmic bias, and the risk of dehumanized care will be discussed\. Finally, the session will highlight new directions in nursing research and leadership that are emerging in response to AI, emphasizing the need for critical, interdisciplinary approaches that center equity, ethics, and the unique contributions of nursing to health systems\.

### MARION WOODWARD LECTURE: 7\-8:30PM PST

[**Dr\. Charlene Chu**](https://bloomberg.nursing.utoronto.ca/profile/charlene-chu/) is an Associate Professor at the Lawrence Bloomberg Faculty of Nursing at the University of Toronto\. Dr\. Chu is a Registered Nurse with training in the Intelligent Assistive Technology and Systems Lab where she acquired the skills in co\-design and technology development, validation, and evaluation\.  
Her primary area of research is focused on co\-designing technology\-enabled interventions that support the quality of life of older adults and their caregivers in post\-acute care settings \(e\.g\. long\-term care, rehabilitation, community\)\. A major research focus includes the use of artificial intelligence to support healthy aging and aging\-in\-place\.  
Dr\. Chu leads an interdisciplinary team including engineers, computer scientists, physiotherapists, and nurses\. She holds two patents about novel computer vision applications for the functional assessments of older adults\. She is also a member of the Nursing and Artificial Intelligence Leadership Consortium\.
