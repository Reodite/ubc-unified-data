## About

What’s the fuss about participatory research and why do we do it? What do we gain from sharing power with key partners, and why is it so hard?

Doctoral student Bonnie Leung will facilitate this speaker series featuring Dr\. Kristen Haase and other speakers from the community, including Occupational Science &amp; Occupational Therapy and Geography\.
