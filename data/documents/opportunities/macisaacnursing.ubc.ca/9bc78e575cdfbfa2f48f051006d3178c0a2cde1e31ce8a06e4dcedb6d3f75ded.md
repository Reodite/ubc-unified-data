# Submit a proposal for UBC’s official side event at COP31

The 31st United Nations Climate Change Conference of the Parties \(COP31\) summit is taking place in Antalya, Türkiye, from November 9\-20, 2026\.    
  
UBC staff, faculty, and students can submit a proposal for UBC’s official side event at COP31\. The top applicant will be invited to submit their side event proposal to the UNFCCC portal in July 2026 and will receive one in\-person badge to attend the Conference\.   
  
**Submit by May 1\.**
