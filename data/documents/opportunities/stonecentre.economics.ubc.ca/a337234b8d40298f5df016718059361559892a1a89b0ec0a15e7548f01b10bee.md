The Stone Inequality Seminar Series at UBC convenes leading international scholars to present cutting\-edge research on the causes and consequences of income and wealth inequality\. Spanning topics such as labour markets, macroeconomic dynamics, public policy, and social institutions, the series provides a forum for rigorous academic exchange and interdisciplinary perspectives\. By showcasing innovative empirical and theoretical work, the seminar series advances understanding of inequality and informs policy discussions in Canada and beyond\.
