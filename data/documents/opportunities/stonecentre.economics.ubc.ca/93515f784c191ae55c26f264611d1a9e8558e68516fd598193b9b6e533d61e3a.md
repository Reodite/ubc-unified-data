We encourage everyone to consider sharing a taxi or Uber between the Hotel and UBC, and vice versa\. Carpooling not only helps reduce our carbon footprint but also provides a wonderful opportunity to connect with fellow attendees\. 

**YVR \(Vancouver International Airport\) to Best Western Sands Hotel**

*1755 Davie St, Vancouver, BC V6G 1W5*

**Taxi or Ride\-Sharing**

-   **Time:** Approximately 30 – 40 minutes, depending on traffic\.
-   **Cost:** Around CAD $35 – $50 / USD $25\.90 – $37\.00

**Best Western Sands Hotel to UBC Iona Building**

*6000 Iona Drive, Vancouver BC, V6T 1L4*

**Taxi or Ride\-Sharing**

-   **Time:** Approximately 15 – 20 minutes, depending on traffic\.
-   **Cost:** Around CAD $15 – $25 / USD $11\.10 – $18\.50

**Best Western Sands Hotel to Downtown Vancouver**

1.  **Walking**

-   **Time:** Approximately 10 \-15 minutes, depending on your pace and exact destination in downtown\.

2.  **Bike\.** Vancouver is bike\-friendly, and renting a bike can be a fun and efficient way to get to Downtown\.

-   **Time:** About 10 minutes, depending on speed and route\.
-   **Cost:** Varies based on rental fees\.

3.  **Taxi or Ride\-Sharing**

-   **Duration:** About 5 \-10 minutes, depending on traffic\.
-   **Cost:** Approximately CAD $10 – $15 / USD $7\.40 – $11\.10
