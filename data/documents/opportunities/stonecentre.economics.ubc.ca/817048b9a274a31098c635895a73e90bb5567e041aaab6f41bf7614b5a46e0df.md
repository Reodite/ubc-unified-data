The Stone Centre supports affiliated faculty\-led research that deepens our understanding of wealth and income inequality in Canada and beyond\. Through Stone Research Grants, past research projects collectively focus on understanding the causes, dynamics, and consequences of wealth and income inequality, using a wide range of empirical, historical, and policy\-oriented approaches\. Overall, this body of work provides a comprehensive, multidisciplinary understanding of inequality as a product of interacting social, economic, and historical forces, while informing potential policy responses\.

### **Eligibility**

Only Stone Centre affiliated faculty are eligible to apply for Stone Research Grants\. We particularly welcome applications from affiliated faculty who are in the early stages of their careers, including post\-doctoral researchers, and untenured faculty\. The affiliated faculty must be the lead researcher\.

Applications can be submitted by an individual researcher or a team of researchers\. Research teams may include non\-affiliated faculty and/or PhD students as co\-applicants, provided they are collaborating on the broader research initiative led by the affiliated faculty member\(s\)\.

Non\-academic researchers can be part of the team, but that as in the case of SSHRC grants, not all research expenses of non\-academic collaborators \(e\.g\., buying equipment\) can be covered by a Stone Grant\. Applicants should check with the Stone Centre staff if they have questions about eligible expenses for non\-academic researchers\.

Funding is not provided for multiple projects by the same individual within the same competition round\. Applying for two different projects in close succession is acceptable, and a brief overlap between funded projects may be permitted \(e\.g\., grant 2 begins October 2026 while grant 1 ends November 2026\)\. That said, applicants should not expect to receive support for a new project while extending a previous project over a prolonged period \(e\.g\., close to two years\)\. In addition, each proposed project must be substantively distinct, as Stone Research Grants are not intended to provide ongoing or repeat funding for the same line of work\.

### **Project Funding**

Stone Research Grants provide awards of **up to** **$25,000** for a one\-year research project\. Grant funds may be used for the following purposes:

-   Research Personnel
    

-   Research Activities and Data
    
-   Participant and Community Engagement
    
-   Travel and Dissemination
    

Requests for larger amounts may be considered on a case\-by\-case basis\.

### **How to Apply**

Please complete the online grant application via Qualtrics by August 30, 2026\. See below link\.

**Applications for the 2026/27 round of Stone Research Grants [are received here](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_085koyKtJNrnbIW)\.**

### **Proposal Evaluation**

Stone Research Grants are competitive in nature\. A committee of Stone Centre affiliated faculty will select projects that best align with the Centre’s mission to understand the causes and consequences of wealth and income inequality, and possible remedies\.

### **Timelines**

Applications open: July 2, 2026

Application deadline: 11\.59 PM August 30, 2026

Notification of results: Late September, 2026

### **Terms and Conditions**

-   All awards are subject to the availability of funds\.
    
-   Grant funds can only be used for the activities listed in the application\. Any changes to proposed spending \(including project timeline\) will require approval from the Stone Centre\.
    
-   Unspent funds will be retained by the Stone Centre\.
    
-   Any publications or external communication arising from work funded by a Stone Research Grant should acknowledge the Stone Centre\.
    
-   A summary report will be due three months after the grant period closes\.
