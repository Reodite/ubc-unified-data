Our Postdoctoral Fellowship program supports exceptional emerging researchers in pursuing impactful inequality research, developing professional skills, and contributing to the Centre’s mission\. Fellows benefit from mentorship, collaboration opportunities, and hands\-on experience working with diverse partners to address complex research challenges\. We will post details about future fellowship application opportunities as they become available\.

### **For Questions**

Questions may be directed to the Stone Centre Research Manager, Aara Johnson, at [stone\.inequality@ubc\.ca](mailto:stone.inequality@ubc.ca)\.
