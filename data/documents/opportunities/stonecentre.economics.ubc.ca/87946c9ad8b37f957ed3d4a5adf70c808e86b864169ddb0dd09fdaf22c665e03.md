As a proud member of a global network of leading research institutions funded by the Stone Foundation, the Stone Centre is uniquely positioned to contribute to an international dialogue on wealth inequality\. This event brings together an interdisciplinary community of scholars and students to explore economic inequality and its far\-reaching societal impact\. Attendees will have the opportunity to connect with thought leaders who are driving change in understanding and addressing one of the most urgent challenges facing societies worldwide\.
