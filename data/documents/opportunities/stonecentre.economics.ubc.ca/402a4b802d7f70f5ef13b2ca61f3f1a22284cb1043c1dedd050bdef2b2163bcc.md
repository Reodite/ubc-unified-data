The Stone Centre is dedicated to advancing the study of inequality through rigorous research and comprehensive training programs\. We offer fellowship opportunities for both master’s and doctoral students, designed to attract and support emerging scholars passionate about understanding and addressing various dimensions of inequality\. Our fellows engage in specialized coursework, hands\-on research projects, and collaborative initiatives that prepare them for influential roles in academia, government, and international organizations\. Explore the sub\-tabs to learn more about our MA and PhD fellowship programs and how you can become a part of our vibrant research community\.
