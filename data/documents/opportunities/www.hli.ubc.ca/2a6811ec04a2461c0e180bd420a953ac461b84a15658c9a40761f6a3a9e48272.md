## Current Opportunities

-   The [Wang Lab](https://www.hli.ubc.ca/researchers/ying-wang/) is hiring a **Research Assistant in Data Analysis** to support cardiovascular ‘Omics’ research\. This role focuses on analyzing RNA\-sequencing and other high\-dimensional datasets using established pipelines, with opportunities to develop new analysis tools\. Candidates should have prior experience working independently with RNA\-seq workflows and be comfortable generating publication\-quality data visuals\. Self\-motivated candidates will have opportunities to work in close collaboration with scientists and industry partners to explore clinically relevant questions in cardiovascular disease\.
    -   Read more and apply [here](https://ubc.wd10.myworkdayjobs.com/ubcstaffjobs/job/UBC-Off-Campus-Hospital-Sites/Research-Assistant-Data-Analysis_JR20366-1)\.
