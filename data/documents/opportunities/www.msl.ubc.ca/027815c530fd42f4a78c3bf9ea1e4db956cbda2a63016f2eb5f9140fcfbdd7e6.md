## January 10, 2020 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/lsi-seminar-jan10-2020.jpg)

*The Life Sciences Institute \(LSI\) presents a seminar:*

Dr\. Mike Gordon  
Associate Professor  
Department of Zoology, Life Sciences Institute  
University of British Columbia  
**Seminar Title:** Combinatorial cellular and molecular codes underlie salt taste in *Drosophila*

Dr\. Ben Matthews  
Assistant Professor  
Department of Zoology  
University of British Columbia  
**Seminar Title:** Thirsting for blood and hunting for water: genetics and neurobiology of mosquito behaviour

**Date:** Friday, January 10, 2020  
**Time:** 3:00 PM – 4:00 PM   
**Location:** 2350 Health Sciences Mall, LSC 3

Social mixer 4 PM onwards in LSI 5th floor pod\.
