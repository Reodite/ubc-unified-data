## December 13, 2023 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/MSL-Trainee-Talks-Dec-13th-2023.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Phil Hieter

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:  
**[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5UrceqoqjwrGtTxNhYchGs4n2B\_hs38K\_Hl](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5UrceqoqjwrGtTxNhYchGs4n2B_hs38K_Hl) 

### Seminar Schedule:

**Trainee Talk 1:** Yuka Takemon – Marra Lab  
*Mapping in silico genetic networks of KMT2D to reveal function and cancer cell vulnerabilities*

**Trainee Talk 2**: Sarah Ebert – Miller lab  
*Understanding the brain meningeal cells at the single cell level*

**Trainee Talk 3**: Eric Chu – Pavlidis lab  
*Preservation and* *detection of* *transcriptional regulatory signals in gene co\-expression*

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Brett Finlay  
*The role of the microbiome in Parkinson’s Disease*
