## June 29, 2021 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/MSL-Virtual-Poster-Session-June-2021-1000px.jpg)

Kick off the summer season with our first ever **Virtual Poster Session** on **June 29th, from 3:00\-4:30pm**\! This is a fantastic opportunity for researchers \(grad students, postdocs, research associates, technicians, or other\) to share research progress and reconnect with fellow researchers outside their immediate group\. 

How do virtual poster sessions work?

-   Each presenter will prepare a digital poster presentation\.
-   Presentation sessions will run for 8 minutes \(3\-5 minutes/presentation and 3 mins for Q&amp;A\)\.
-   The audience will circulate to the different posters via breakout rooms in Zoom\. Sessions will rotate every 10 minutes, so you will have a chance to visit 6 posters\.
-   We will regroup afterwards and hand out a few prizes\! 

**Zoom registration link:**  
[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5wudeChqj8jGtfDfbCispZKU9Ge7qHksleC](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5wudeChqj8jGtfDfbCispZKU9Ge7qHksleC)

***Please make sure you are running on Zoom V\. 5\.3 or later**, otherwise the breakout room functionality will not be available to you\. If need be, please update your zoom program to the latest version\.*

Join us in getting together \(virtually\) and celebrating the exciting work being done during the pandemic\!
