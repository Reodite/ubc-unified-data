Event Series: [ECOSCOPE: The R tidyverse workshop](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/series/ecoscope-the-r-tidyverse-workshop/)

## January 21, 2020 @ 1:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

*ECOSCOPE presents a workshop:*

In this workshop, we delve into data manipulation using [dplyr](https://dplyr.tidyverse.org/) / [tidyr](https://tidyr.tidyverse.org/) and graphics with [ggplot](https://ggplot2.tidyverse.org/) in R\. We teach different ways to manipulate data in tabular and text forms as well as the critical concepts underlying the grammar of graphics and how they are implemented in R\. We will use [RStudio](https://www.rstudio.com/), a powerful but user\-friendly R environment, and teach you how to use it effectively\.

For more information, please visit [ecoscope\.ubc\.ca/events](http://ecoscope.ubc.ca/events/)\.
