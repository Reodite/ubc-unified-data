## October 23, 2019 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Seminar-Series-October-23-2019.jpg)

**Kickstart Project Presentions**

1\. Rachel Jun and Jorge Pena Diaz \(Tokuriki and Finlay labs\)  
Title: Selective disruption of bacterial communication using Quorum Quenching enzymes to modulate microbiota composition

2\. Jennifer Bui and Roza Bidshari \(Gsponer and Hansen labs\)  
Title: GPCR\-targeting therapeutic Abs with specific agonistic or inverse agonistic effects

3\. Wilf Jefferies and Jamie Piret \(Jefferies and Piret Labs\)  
Title: Manipulating primary Type 2 innate lymphocytes \(ILC2\) in vitro

4\. John Tyson and Shams Bhuiyam \(Snutch and Pavlidis labs\)  
Title: Identification and Functional Analysis of Novel Brain Calcium Channel Isoforms with Long\-Read Sequencing

5\. Jose Celedon and Leonard Foster \(Bohlmann and Foster labs\)  
Title: Conifer secretory trichomes – characterizing the transcriptome, proteome, and metabolome of a new type of specialized cell factories for conifer defense

**Host:** [Dr\. Peter Zandstra](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-peter-zandstra/)
