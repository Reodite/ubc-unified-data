## September 13, 2019 @ 3:30 pm \- 5:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/welcome-back-mixer-header.png)

End of summer got you down? MSL has an event for you – so save the date\!

We’re starting the new academic year with a **Welcome Back Mixer** in the atrium complete with games and prizes as well as catering and bartending by Scholar’s\.

**The short and sweet of it:**

Where: MSL Atrium \(second floor\)

When: September 13, 3:30\-5:30 pm

What: Free food and drinks \(one per person\), games, music, prizes and more\!

**More details:**

The Welcome Back Mixer is a casual networking event aimed to provide a chance to catch up with your colleagues after the summer, meet the new students of MSL, and enjoy some free food and drinks\. One drink ticket per person will be provided \(find Linda H\. or Nina R\. at the event for your drink ticket\) followed by a cash bar – **please ensure you drink responsibly and secure a safe ride home afterwards\!** There will also be a mysterious contest for the chance to win an additional drink ticket\! We will be having an open mic from 4 pm onwards; please email Nina \([nina\.radis@msl\.ubc\.ca](mailto:nina.radis@msl.ubc.ca)\) if you’d like to perform\.

This event is open to MSL personnel only\.
