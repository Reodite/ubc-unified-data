## December 18, 2024 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/MSL-Trainee-Talks-December-18-2024.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Leonard Foster

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5Yodu6tpjIvGNdikuwZbegP\-aIc6Hwv1d3Z](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Yodu6tpjIvGNdikuwZbegP-aIc6Hwv1d3Z)

### Seminar Schedule:

**Trainee Talk 1**: Eric Boateng – Leslie lab  
*Single\-particle and Single\-Cell Microscopy of LNP\-mRNA Interactions and Dynamics: to Characterize, Understand, and Improve Nanomedicines*

**Trainee Talk 2**: Cathy Yan – Marra lab  
*Single\-cell ’omics for precision medicine*

**Trainee Talk 3**: Clara Xia – Jefferies lab  
*Deconvolution of the cancer immune surveillance apparatus using single\-cell RNA sequencing*

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Martin Hirst  
*EpiATLAS – a reference for human epigenomic research*
