## February 12 @ 10:30 am \- 11:30 am

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Feb-12-EL-Faculty-Search-Series-Dr.-Callie-Chappell.png)

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.
