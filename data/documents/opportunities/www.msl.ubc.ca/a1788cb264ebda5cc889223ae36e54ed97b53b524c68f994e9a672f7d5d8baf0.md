## March 26, 2020 @ 2:00 pm \- 3:30 pm

## POSTPONED

To all of **MSL**‘s established and aspiring **data wranglers**, **programmers**, **code whisperers** and **computer scientists…****WE WANT YOU** for our very first and super awesome **MSL Bioinformatics User Group \= MSL BUG\!**

We would like to:

-   Share our research and technical expertise
-   Inspire new approaches at problem\-solving
-   Discuss and brainstorm computational issues we face
-   Learn how to use and adapt new tools
-   Spark productive collaborations

**Our first meeting will be on** **Thursday, March 26\[th\] in the MSL 337** **from 2:00 to 3:30 p\.m\.**

&gt;&gt;&gt;There will be **coffee** and **homemade cake** \!&lt;&lt;&lt;

**Everyone is welcome\! Students, staff and faculty from all of MSL’s disciplines** \(e\.g\. dealing with sequencing/metabolomics/electrophysiology/population dynamics/behavioural/… data,script/software development, distributed computing, machine learning approaches, modelling, statistics etc\.\)

**If you would like to attend, please sign up\!**

[Sign Up Here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScs_i8k16mTWvmRPf7cZlq3NUUJtkQgLvU-IHCOcQHs4oAPEA/viewform?usp=sf_link)

**First meeting agenda**: Round table with short individual introductions where we identify common interests and set up an outline for subsequent meetings – and did we mention the homemade cake and coffee already?

We look forward to seeing you there\!

**Your MSL BUG organizers:** Quentin Geissmann \(Haney Lab\), Sarah Grasedieck \(Hirst Lab\), Guillaume Poirier\-Morency \(Pavlidis Lab\)

**Faculty oversight:** Paul Pavlidis and Martin Hirst
