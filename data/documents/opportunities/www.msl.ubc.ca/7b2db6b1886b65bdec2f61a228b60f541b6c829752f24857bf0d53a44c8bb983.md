## November 26, 2024 @ 12:30 pm \- 1:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Zymeworks-poster_with-MSL.png)

This seminar will be co\-presented by the Department of Microbiology and Immunology and the Michael Smith Laboratories

**Host:** Dr\. Michael Murphy

A guest from Zymeworks Inc\. will be joining us to say a few words and to present the new 2024/25 *Zymeworks – Michael Smith Laboratories – Department of Microbiology and Immunology Fellowships in Advanced Protein Therapeutics* recipients with their awards\.

### **Speakers:**

**Thomas Worthington:** Horwitz Lab, Microbiology and Immunology

**Talk title:** Targeting latent γ\-herpesvirus infection in an animal model of multiple sclerosis

**Lauren Durland:** Zandstra Lab, Michael Smith Laboratories

**Talk title:** Optimizing pluripotent stem cell\-derived T cell function and expansion
