## January 22, 2019 @ 1:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/2019.01.22_tidyverse_workshop.jpg)

*ECOSCOPE presents a workshop:*

Do you have unmanageable tables of data? Are you craving beautiful figures for an upcoming proposal? Do you want to learn R but don’t know where to start?

ECOSCOPE’s winter term of R workshops begins with a 2\-hour [Introduction to R](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/53932550711) on Jan 17 followed by a 6\-hour data manipulation and visualization workshop is [the R tidyverse](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/53932542687) on Jan 22 &amp; 24\.

[View Workshop Poster](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/2019.01.22_tidyverse_workshop.pdf)

For more information, please visit [ecoscope\.ubc\.ca/events](http://ecoscope.ubc.ca/events/)\.
