## March 25, 2021 @ 11:30 am \- 1:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Leadership-Administration_Twitter.png)

#### Description:

Dr\. Carry and Professor Williams will draw from their rich experience and expertise to share insights on the issue of underrepresentation of Black people in academic leadership administration\.
