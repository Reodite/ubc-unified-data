## June 16, 2021 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/MSL-Trainee-Talks-June-16th-2021.jpg)

### **Event details:**

**Date:** Wednesday June 16th, 3:00\-4:30pm

**Host:** Dr\. Christian Kastrup

**Zoom registration link:  
**[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5EodOmppzMpHNZ8H5vzZ017BC7ZSrpXH38d](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5EodOmppzMpHNZ8H5vzZ017BC7ZSrpXH38d)

### Seminar Schedule:

Trainee Talk 1: **Matthias Kretschmer** **–** Kronstad Lab  
*Mixed carbon sources trigger hallmarks of biotrophic growth in a pathogenic fungus* 

Trainee Talk 2: **Danielle Jeong** – Miller Lab   
*Characterization of the neural stem cell response to demyelinating injury in the adult mammalian brain* 

Trainee Talk 3: **Jordan Sicherman** – Pavlidis Lab  
*Large\-scale mining of differential expression data reveals insight into gene function* 

Faculty Talk: **Dr\.** **Martin Hirst  
***Revisiting Lamarck*
