## March 16, 2022 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/MSL-Trainee-Talks-Mar-16th-2022.jpg)

### **Event details:**

**Date:** Wednesday March 16th, 3:00\-4:30pm

**Host:** Dr\. Charles Haynes

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:  
**[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5IsduGgqzwpEtQZi4pki0xfBbnL6b6F55mU](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5IsduGgqzwpEtQZi4pki0xfBbnL6b6F55mU)

### Seminar Schedule:

Trainee Talk 1:  **Nuthan** **Bathula** – Blakney Lab  
*Self\-amplifying* *RNA* *technology* *for* *antibody\-based* *i* *mmunotherapy*

Trainee Talk 2: **Benedikt** **Golisch** – Brumer Lab  
*Why your oatmeal matters\! – Insights into mixed\-linkage beta\-glucan utilization by* Prevotella copri

Trainee Talk 3: **Dr\.** **A** **icha** **Houfani** – Foster Lab  
*Discovery and* *validation* *of* *factors* *i* *mpacting* *mRNA LNP* *activity* *and* *tolerability*

Faculty Talk: **Dr\.** **Sabrina Leslie  
***Single\-molecule biophysics of DNA, RNA, protein interactions and applications to advancing genetic medicines*
