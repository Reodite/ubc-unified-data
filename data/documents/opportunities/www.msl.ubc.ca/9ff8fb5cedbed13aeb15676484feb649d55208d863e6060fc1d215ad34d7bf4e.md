## May 17, 2023 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/MSL-Trainee-Talks-May-17th-2023.png)

### **Event details:**

**Date:** Wednesday May 17th, 3:00\-4:00pm

**Host:** Dr\. Leonard Foster

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:  
**[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5Ylc\-uvrz0vGtPEvImh8LZ7GnCH09CXl\-GB](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Ylc-uvrz0vGtPEvImh8LZ7GnCH09CXl-GB)

### Seminar Schedule:

**Trainee Talk 1:** Aastha Dalal – Mayor lab  
*Identifying the key players involved in cytosolic protein quality control*

**Trainee Talk 2:** Ashleigh Willis – Miller lab  
*Neural stem cell\-mediated oligodendrogenesis for adult brain remyelination*

**Trainee Talk 3**: Dylan Perera – Bohlmann lab  
*Bioengineering of Montbretin A Production in Nicotiana benthamiana – Towards Scalable Production of a New Type 2 Diabetes Treatment Option*

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Steve Withers  
*New and improved glyco\-enzymes for glycoprotein analysis via metagenomics and mutagenesis*
