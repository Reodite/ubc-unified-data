## February 26 @ 10:30 am \- 11:30 am

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Feb-26-EL-Faculty-Search-Series-Dr.-Avril-Metcalfe-Roach.png)

**Speaker:** Dr\. Avril Metcalfe\-Roach – *Research Associate, University of British Columbia*

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. The speaker will be delivering their talk in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/j/69583862146?pwd\=a7QLWlEcLut2DsG6p6fNthec908F5r\.1](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/69583862146?pwd=a7QLWlEcLut2DsG6p6fNthec908F5r.1)

**Talk title:** *Teaching during a technological boom: virtual research experiences and future\-proofing the curriculum*
