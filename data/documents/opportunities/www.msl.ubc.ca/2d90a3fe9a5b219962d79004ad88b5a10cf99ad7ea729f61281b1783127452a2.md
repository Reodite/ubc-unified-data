## May 8, 2024 @ 12:00 pm \- 1:00 pm

​Are you thinking about translating your research into a product or service for societal impact? The Michael Smith Laboratories has invited [entrepreneurship@UBC](https://entrepreneurship.ubc.ca/) to come inform our department members about its upcoming Venture Founder intake \(free lunch\)\. 

[Venture Founder](https://entrepreneurship.ubc.ca/engage-us/venture-founder) is a UBC funded 16\-week program that provides aspiring deep tech founders with a business skillset and surrounds them with highly skilled mentors to support their start\-up\-building process\. Lynn Kulli, the Lab2Launch Venture program lead, will be joined by two alumni of the program from **Michael Smith Laboratories** and **Center of Brain Health**, who will share their firsthand experiences with the program\.

During the info session, you can expect to:

-   ​Gain a comprehensive overview of the Venture Founder program, including its structure, process, and benefits\.
-   ​Hear inspiring stories and lessons learned from past participants who have gone on to launch ventures from their research projects\.
-   ​Have the opportunity to ask questions and engage in discussions with Lynn, the alumni, and fellow attendees\.

​Please join us if you’re looking to take the next step with your research invention or simply curious about the entrepreneurial landscape\.

Please RSVP by May 6\[th\] for catering purposes at: [https://lu\.ma/920aozjo](https://lu.ma/920aozjo)

You can also RSVP or send inquiries to: [info@entrepreneurship\.ubc\.ca](mailto:info@entrepreneurship.ubc.ca)
