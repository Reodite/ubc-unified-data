## September 22, 2021 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:30 pm

[event poster](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/MSL-Trainee-Talks-Sept-22nd-2021.jpg)

### **Event details:**

**Date:** Wednesday September 22nd, 3:00\-4:30pm

**Host:** Dr\. Anna Blakney

**Zoom registration link:  
**[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5YvceyqrjgoG90dGXOOXVBmd746SeGkoibE](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5YvceyqrjgoG90dGXOOXVBmd746SeGkoibE)

### Seminar Schedule:

Trainee Talk 1:  **Albert Kamanzi** – Leslie Lab  
*Simultaneous, single\-particle measurements of size and loading give new insights into the structure of drug\-delivery nanoparticles*

Trainee Talk 2: **Shreyas Rangan** – Turner Lab  
*Raman spectroscopy as a process analytical technology for regulatory T\-cell manufacturing*

Trainee Talk 3: **Lyann Sim** – Withers Lab  
*Development of an E\. coli platform strain for* *sialylated* *mucin\-type O\-glycosylation of therapeutic proteins*

Faculty Talk: **Dr\.** **Jörg Bohlmann  
***BioEngineering MbA – A New Treatment Option for Type\-2 Diabetes and Obesity*
