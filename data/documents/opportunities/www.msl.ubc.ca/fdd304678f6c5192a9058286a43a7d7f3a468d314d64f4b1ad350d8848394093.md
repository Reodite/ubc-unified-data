## September 17, 2025 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/MSL-Trainee-Talks-September-19-2025.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Jim Kronstad

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/PysAGBG\-TFS2NmhyhuyBQQ](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/PysAGBG-TFS2NmhyhuyBQQ)

**Seminar Schedule:**

**Trainee Talk 1:** Suiyang Liao – Blakney lab  
*Lipid barcoding for RNA medicine screening*

**Trainee Talk 2:** Isabel Tarrant – Finlay lab  
*Early\-life gut microbiome and asthma: Potential for live biotherapeutic products*

**Trainee Talk 3:** Tina Tan – Li lab  
*Hydrophobins in fungal biology*

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Peter Zandstra  
*Engineering Collective Behaviour*
