## September 23, 2025 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Brett-Finlay-book-talk-and-signing.png)

Dr\. Brett Finlay’s new book, *The Microbiome Master Key: Harnessing Your Microbes to Unlock Whole\-Body Health and Lifelong Vitality* has just published in Canada\! We invite you to join us for a talk by Dr\. Finlay about the subject of the book: microbes, health, and healthy aging\.

Copies of the book will be available for purchase outside the lecture theatre from 2:30 – 4:15pm, and Dr\. Finlay will be available to sign copies following his talk\.

**Speaker:** Dr\. Brett Finlay – *UBC Michael Smith Laboratories, Microbiology &amp; Immunology, Biochemistry &amp; Molecular Biology*

**Talk title:** The Microbiome Master Key: Exploiting the microbiome for healthy living and aging

**Abstract:** We have come to realize the microbiome \(all the microbes living in and on us\) play a fundamental role in human health and disease, affecting most of our normal physiological processes\.  In the past few years, we have begun to understand how we can exploit this understanding to promote beneficial microbiomes, which in turn enhances health and aging\.  In this talk we will explore some of these concepts, and what can be done, based on science, to enhance health and healthy aging\.
