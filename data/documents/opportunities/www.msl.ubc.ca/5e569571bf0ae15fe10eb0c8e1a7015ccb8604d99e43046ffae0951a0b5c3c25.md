## February 23 @ 10:00 am \- 11:00 am

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/EDI-Basics_digital-for-web_2026.png)

The MSL EDI committee is happy share we will be once again running our *Equity, Diversity and Inclusion \(EDI\) Basics* workshop\.

During this workshop, we will facilitate two activities: 1\) a team\-oriented exercise to build a mobile and 2\) an individual reflection activity of identity mapping\. Through these activities, we will explore and start a conversation around relevant EDI concepts and their direct application\.

The workshop will be offered **Monday, February 23 from 10\-11am in MSL 102\.** The workshop will be facilitated by MSL EDI Committee members David Ng and Matthew Langley\.

Everyone is welcome to attend, and snacks will be provided\. Please register for the workshop here: [bit\.ly/4tnoZiQ](https://bit.ly/4tnoZiQ)

We look forward to seeing you there\!
