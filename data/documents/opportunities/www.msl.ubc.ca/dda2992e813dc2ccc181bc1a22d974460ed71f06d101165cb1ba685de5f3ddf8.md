## March 18, 2020 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Seminar-Series-Mar-18-2020.jpg)

**Trainee Talks:** Piret lab, Snutch lab, Tokuriki lab

Piret lab, Katherine MacDonald  
Title: Manufacturing thymus\-derived regulatory T cells for cellular therapy

Snutch Lab, Samrat Thouta  
Title: Alteration of Neuronal Excitability and Synaptic Plasticity in the Amygdala circuitry of a Mouse Model of Temporal Lobe Epilepsy

Title: Evaluation of fluctuation in enzyme activity by single enzyme kinetics measurement

Title: How the immune system lost its sense of tumour

**Hosted by:** Dr\. Jamie Piret
