## MSL EDI Workshop 4: Microaggressions

The MSL EDI committee is happy to present the next workshop in our EDI Workshop series: Microaggressions\. During this workshop, we will learn what microaggressions are, why they are harmful, and how to disarm them\. In addition, we will go through a variety of scenarios to become better at identifying and responding to microaggressions\. The workshop will be offered in MSL 101 on the following dates:

-   **Monday January 29,** 3\-4:30pm
-   **Friday February 2**, 11am\-12:30pm

A Zoom link to attend virtually will be provided closer to the date\.

To register, please go to: [bit\.ly/EDIMicroaggressions](http://bit.ly/EDIMicroaggressions)

See you then\!

MSL EDI Workshop Committee
