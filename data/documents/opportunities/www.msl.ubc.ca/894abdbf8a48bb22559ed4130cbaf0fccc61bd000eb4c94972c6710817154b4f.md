## June 29, 2022 @ 2:00 pm \- 5:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/BBQ-Research-Recognition-event-slide.png)

We hope you can join us in celebrating our community’s recent achievements and milestones\! On the afternoon of **Wednesday June 29th, from 2\-5pm**, this event will begin with a poster session \(outdoors, weather permitting\!\), followed by a BBQ, announcements, speeches and cake\!

We look forward to gathering together to share the important research we do, acknowledge the conclusion of Peter Zandstra’s term as Director, celebrate the passing of the torch to our incoming leadership team of Martin Hirst and Freda Miller and other important faculty milestones\.

*This event is open to all MSL community members\.*

**Schedule of events:**

-   2\-4pm – Poster Session \(mingle with MSL researchers and learn about their work\)
-   3\-4pm – BBQ \(burgers, chips and drinks served – vegetarian options available\)
-   4\-5pm – acknowledgement speeches and cake serving
