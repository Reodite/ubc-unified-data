## December 17, 2025 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Zymeworks-Fellowship-Seminar-Dec-17_V2.png)

*This seminar is co\-presented by the MSL and Department of Microbiology and Immunology*

**Host:** Dr\. Martin Hirst

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/rhulFw4CSYSbvzmTuAUgZQ](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/rhulFw4CSYSbvzmTuAUgZQ) 

At this seminar, we will also present the new 2025/26 *Zymeworks – Michael Smith Laboratories – Department of Microbiology and Immunology Fellowships in Advanced Protein Therapeutics* recipients with their awards\.

### **Speakers \(2024 fellowship recipients\):**

**Dane Sands:** PhD Student, Mayor Lab – *Michael Smith Laboratories and Department of Biochemistry &amp; Molecular Biology, UBC*  
**Talk title:** Supercharging Secretion: Transforming *K\. phaffii* into an Antimicrobial Peptide Factory

**Xianya Qu:** PhD Candidate, Kronstad Lab – *Department of Microbiology &amp; Immunology and Michael Smith Laboratories, UBC*  
**Talk title:** Endoplasmic Reticulum Function Contributes to Caspofungin Tolerance in *Cryptococcus neoformans*
