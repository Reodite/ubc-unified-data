## January 21 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/MSL-Trainee-Talks-January-21-2026.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Peter Zandstra

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/cshckBCURvmXgebZfbrlVw](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/cshckBCURvmXgebZfbrlVw)

**Seminar Schedule:**

**Trainee Talk 1:** Olivier Hinse – Foster lab  
**Cis\-transpeptidation as a potential inductor of autoimmunity in rheumatoid arthritis**

**Trainee Talk 2:**  Rachel Schwartz – Pavlidis lab  
**Large scale re\-analysis of single\-cell brain studies**

**Trainee Talk 3:** Zhiqin Long – Todesco lab  
**Positive Selection Across Scales: Genetic Architecture of Adaptation and Connectivity in Wild* *Sunflowers**

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Xin Li  
*Breaking the “one nucleus, one whole genome” rule: lessons from fungi*
