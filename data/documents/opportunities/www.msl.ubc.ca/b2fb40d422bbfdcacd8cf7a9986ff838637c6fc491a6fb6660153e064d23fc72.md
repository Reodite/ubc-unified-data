## September 20, 2023 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/MSL-Trainee-Talks-Sept-20th-2023.jpg)

### **Event details:**

**Date:** Wednesday Sept\. 20th, 3:00\-4:00pm

**Host:** Dr\. Anna Blakney

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:  
**[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5IlcuutqzwqGtx\_OFyg2CEIMGsy7EmoUlxd](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5IlcuutqzwqGtx_OFyg2CEIMGsy7EmoUlxd)

### Seminar Schedule:

**Trainee Talk 1:** Kate Donald – Finlay Lab  
*IgA in maternal milk protects against allergic disease through modulation of the gut microbiota*

**Trainee Talk 2:**  Daria Oleinichenko – Snutch Lab  
*Z944, a T\-type calcium channel inhibitor, as a treatment for opioid withdrawal\-induced pain*

**Trainee Talk 3**: Laura Stankiewicz – Zandstra lab  
*Sex\-biased thymus architecture guides T cell development through spatially defined niches*

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Joerg Bohlmann  
*Back to the Beginnings – Plant Natural Products*
