## January 11, 2023 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/MSL-Trainee-Talks-Jan-11th-2023.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Harry Brumer

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:  
**[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5Yvd\-qhqDssH9GqHUfOqZZckTM5YttTS7eW](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Yvd-qhqDssH9GqHUfOqZZckTM5YttTS7eW)

### Seminar Schedule:

Trainee Talk 1: **Sumanjit Datta\-** **Gsponer Lab  
***Condensin mediated chromosome compaction – a way to make loopy DNA*

Trainee Talk 2: **Kiera Lee – Hirst Lab**  
*Distinct enhancer states define subgroups of primary synovial sarcoma tumors*

Trainee Talk 3:  **Rhea Bains – Withers Lab**  
*From Discovery to Application: Engineering New Enzymes to Synthesize Sulfated Glycans Involved in Immune Recognition*

Faculty Talk: **Dr\. Anna Blakney  
***Next\-generation RNA vaccines and therapies*
