## October 15, 2025 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/MSL-Trainee-Talks-October-15-2025.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Phil Hieter

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/Ma4zScT5TX66vcLLgd4iLg](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/Ma4zScT5TX66vcLLgd4iLg)

**Seminar Schedule:**

**Trainee Talk 1:** Fangwu Wang – Hirst lab  
*Epigenetic and 3D chromatin regulation of immune cell fate decision*

**Trainee Talk 2:**  Sarah Ebert – Miller lab  
*Defining the composition and injury response of the murine leptomeninges: cortical brain interface*

**Trainee Talk 3:** Nikko Jeffreys – Zandstra lab  
*Extracellular matrix mechanics regulates T\-cell development from human stem cells*

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Nobuhiko Tokuriki  
*Evolutionary dynamics of enzymes and metabolic pathways*
