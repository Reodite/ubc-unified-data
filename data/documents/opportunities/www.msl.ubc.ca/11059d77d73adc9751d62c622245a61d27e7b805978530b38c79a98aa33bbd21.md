## February 19 @ 10:30 am \- 11:30 am

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Feb-19-EL-Faculty-Search-Series-Dr.-Sreeparna-Vappala.png)

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. The speaker will be delivering their talk in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.
