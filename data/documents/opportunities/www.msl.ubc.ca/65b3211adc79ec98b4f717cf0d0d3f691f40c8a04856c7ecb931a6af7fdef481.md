## May 21, 2025 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Pathway-to-Independence-Talks-May-21-V2.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Freda Miller

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/w1W6TziSRlOv4aI4Im4pzw](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/w1W6TziSRlOv4aI4Im4pzw) 

### **Speakers:**

**Dr\. Lars Kruse** – Bohlmann lab  
*Elucidating short\-chain alkane biosynthesis for the development of biofuels*

**Dr\. Marissa Lithopoulos** – Miller lab  
*It’s all in the vessels – Exploring the role of the neonatal brain vasculature on neural stem cell function*

**Dr\. Ross Jones** – Zandstra lab  
*Uncovering genetic circuit design rules to program human stem cells and development*

**Dr\. Santanu Sasidharan** – Tokuriki lab  
*De novo *d* *esigned* *t* *herapeutics* *t* *argeting SARS\-CoV\-2 and related* *b* *eta\-coronaviruses**
