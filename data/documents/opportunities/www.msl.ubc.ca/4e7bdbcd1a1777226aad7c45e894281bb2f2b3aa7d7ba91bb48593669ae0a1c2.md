## December 9, 2019 @ 1:00 pm \- 2:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/karen-guillemin.png)

*The Department of Microbiology and Immunology presents a seminar:*

Dr\. Karen Guillemin  
Philip H\. Knight Chair and Professor of Biology  
Institute of Molecular Biology  
University of Oregon

**Date:** Monday, December 9, 2019  
**Time:** 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM  
**Location:** LSC 3

**Title:** Teaming with Microbes: lessons from the zebrafish intestine

**Abstract:**Microbial colonization of the digestive tract is a crucial event in vertebrate development, required for maturation of host immunity and establishment of normal digestive physiology\. We have developed gnotobiotic zebrafish as a model system to study how intestinal microbial communities are established, how they influence host development, and how perturbations of the microbiota can result in loss of intestinal homeostasis and inflammation\. Using defined microbial communities that we can visualize with light sheet microscopy, we are exploring bacterial colonization dynamics of the intestine\. We are also using bacterial genetic approaches to identify bacterial factors that influence host programs of intestinal epithelial renewal, pancreatic beta cell expansion, and innate immune system maturation\. Finally, we make use of zebrafish genetics and transgenesis to explore the host pathways that perceive and respond to bacterial factors that influence intestinal homeostasis\.

**Hosted by:** [Dr\. Brett Finlay](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-brett-finlay/) and Kylynda Bauer \(Ph\.D\. Candidate – Finlay lab\)
