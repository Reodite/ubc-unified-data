## August 13, 2019 @ 12:00 pm \- 7:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/W-Lamson-Seminar-Announcement.jpg)

*The Centre for Blood Research in partnership with UBC School of Biomedical Engineering presents a special event:*

## **CBR Research Day – August 13, 2019**

Join us at our upcoming CBR Research Day on Tuesday, August 13th\! This is always a spectacular event, starting off with lunch and terrific presentations by our 40\+ summer students\. This will be followed by a keynote address by **[Dave Ireland](https://www.davehireland.com/)**, a highly sought\-after speaker and educator with over 17 years of experience studying, conducting research, teaching and working in conservation biology and ecology\. And finally, there will be loads of posters, with refreshments and a BBQ dinner for all to enjoy\!

**Date:** August 13, 2019  
**Time:** 12:00 PM – 7:00PM  
**Location:** West Atrium/LSC 3, 2350 Health Science Mall

For more information, please visit [cbr\.ubc\.ca/events/research\-day](https://cbr.ubc.ca/events/research-day/)\.
