## March 2, 2020 @ 5:30 pm \- 6:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/burgess-lane-poster-final-scaled.jpg)

*The Faculty of Forestry, Department of Wood Science, and the Bioproducts Institute presents the 21\[st\] Burgess\-Lane Memorial lectureship:*

Dr\. Derek G\. Gray  
Professor Emeritus, Department of Chemistry, McGill University  
Winner of the Marcus Wallenberg Prize \(2013\)

**Date:** Monday, March 2, 2020  
**Time:** 5:30 PM   
**Location:** FSC 1005

**Title:** Forest\-based Nanocellulosic Materials

Dr\. Gray will discuss the innovative potential of wood\-based nanomaterials to supplement traditional wood products\.

The lecture will be followed by refreshments and a poster session from Forestry and Bioproducts Institute researchers\.
