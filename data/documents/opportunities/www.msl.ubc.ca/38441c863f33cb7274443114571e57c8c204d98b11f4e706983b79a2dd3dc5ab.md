## May 19, 2021 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/MSL-Trainee-Talks-May-19th-2021.jpg)

### **Event details:**

**Date:** Wednesday May 19th, 3:00\-4:30pm

**Host:** Dr\. Nobuhiko Tokuriki

**Zoom registration link:**  
[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5wtfuCvrz8rGd3k7SHbSm\-7zpjxejrdFrPt](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5wtfuCvrz8rGd3k7SHbSm-7zpjxejrdFrPt)

### Seminar Schedule:

Trainee Talk 1: **Anthony** **Oppedisano –** Hieter Lab  
*Analysing* *Structure\-Function and Dominant Synthetic\-Lethal MUS81* *Missense* *Mutations* 

Trainee Talk 2: **Axel** **Hauduc** – Hirst Lab   
*Assessing the impact of personalized reference genomes* *on* *ChIP**\-seq alignment*

Trainee Talk 3: **Yale Michaels** – Zandstra Lab  
*An engineered* *signalling* *environment supports efficient T cell differentiation from pluripotent stem cells* 

Faculty Talk: **Dr\.** **Cara Haney  
***Getting to the root of host\-microbe interactions*
