## September 26, 2019 @ 2:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

*ECOSCOPE presents a workshop:*

In this workshop, we introduce you to R and RStudio at the beginner level\. This condensed 2\-hour workshop is meant to get you started in R and acts as a pre\-requisite for ALL our other workshops

For more information, please visit [ecoscope\.ubc\.ca/events](http://ecoscope.ubc.ca/events/)\.
