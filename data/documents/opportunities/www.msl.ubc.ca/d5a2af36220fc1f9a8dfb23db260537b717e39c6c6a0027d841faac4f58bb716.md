## January 21, 2019 @ 12:00 pm \- 1:00 pm

*iGEM of UBC presents a Competition Recruitment Info Session:*

Interested in synthetic biology or biological engineering?

Join the UBC iGEM team and compete at Giant Jamboree in Boston\.

We will be holding two information sessions about iGEM on Friday, January 18th at 4:00pm in LSC 1510 as well as on Monday, January 21st at 12:00pm in LSC3\. Refreshments will be provided\!

**Date:** Friday, January 18, 2019  
**Time:** 4:00 PM  
**Location:** LSC 1510

[View Recruitment Flyer](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/ubc-igem-recruitment.pdf)

[View Information Session Flyer](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/igem-flyer-2019.pdf)

For more information, please visit [http://www\.ubcigem\.com/join\-us/\.](http://www.ubcigem.com/join-us/)
