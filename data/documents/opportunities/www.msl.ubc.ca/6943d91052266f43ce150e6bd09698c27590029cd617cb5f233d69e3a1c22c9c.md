## May 25, 2020

*The Life Sciences Institute presents the 11\[th\] Annual Retreat of ImmunoBC:*

The keynote speaker will be:

Sir Marc Feldmann, FRS  
Emeritus Professor  
University of Oxford

**Date:** Monday, May 25, 2020  
**Time:** TBA  
**Location:** Diamond Auditorium located on Simon Fraser University’s main campus in Burnaby

Registration procedures and the full conference program will be available online soon\.
