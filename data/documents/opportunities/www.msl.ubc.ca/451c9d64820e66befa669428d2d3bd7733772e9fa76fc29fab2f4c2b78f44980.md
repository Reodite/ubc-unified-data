## April 15 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Pathway-to-Independence-Talks-April-15.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Dave Ng

We invite you to join us for a seminar featuring talks from the recipients of the [2025 *MSL Pathway to Independence Awards*](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/three-msl-members-receive-2025-pathway-to-independence-award/)\. **This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/SY4t5Ia9SBOnUKhvg8HMlw](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/SY4t5Ia9SBOnUKhvg8HMlw)

### **Speakers:**

**Dr\. Jeff Doherty** – Foster lab  
*Eau de hairworm: a multiscale approach to pheromone\-driven behaviour in parasites*

**Dr\. Tiam Heydari** – Zandstra lab  
*Quantifying Collective Immune Response Activation in Defined Micro Islands*
