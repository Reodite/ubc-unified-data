## November 21, 2019 @ 8:30 am \- 5:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/invite.symposium.4x6.v4.png)

**Date:** Thursday, November 21, 2019  
**Time:** Presentations are 8:30 AM – 5:30 PM\. Reception at 6:00 PM\.  
**Location:** Sheraton Wall Centre, Grand Ball Room  
**Hosted by:** Canada’s Michael Smith Genome Sciences Centre at BC Cancer

In celebration of its 20th anniversary, Canada’s Michael Smith Genome Sciences Centre at BC Cancer is hosting a scientific symposium\.

Our partners, collaborators and alumni will share their genome science breakthroughs that are improving cancer care, advancing research and technology, and adding insight to our fundamental understanding of molecular biology\.

For more information, please visit [bcgsc\.ca/symposium](https://bcgsc.ca/symposium)
