## April 28 \- April 29

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Updated-SCS-header.png)

The Michael Smith Laboratories, Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique \(CNRS\), and Ambassade de France au Canada are excited to present the **Single Cell Symposium\!** This symposium will run **April 28\-29, 2026** at the University of British Columbia, with the goal of bringing together leading single\-cell researchers from Canada and France\.

The symposium is centred around three key themes:

1.  The role of single\-cell analysis in **cancer biology**
2.  Emerging insights into the complexities of **neuroscience**
3.  The transformative development of **single\-cell technologies**

We are excited to welcome **Dr\. Geneviève Almouzni** from the Institut Curie, and **Dr\. Marco Marra** from UBC as our keynote speakers for this event\.
