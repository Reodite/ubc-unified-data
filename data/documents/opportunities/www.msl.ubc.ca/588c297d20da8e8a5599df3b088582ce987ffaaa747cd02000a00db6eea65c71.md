## October 23, 2024 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/UPDATED-MSL-Trainee-Talks-October-23-2024.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Paul Pavlidis

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5cpf\-mpqjMjGdTau8tV8g4f7l3d1yci\_Tp3](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5cpf-mpqjMjGdTau8tV8g4f7l3d1yci_Tp3)

### Seminar Schedule:

**Trainee Talk 1**: Tiam Heydari – Zandstra lab  
*Engineering the collective behavior in stem cell\-driven colonies*

**Trainee Talk 2**: Sruthi Purushothaman – Miller lab  
*Dissecting multicellular spatial heterogeneity during mammalian fingertip \(digit\-tip\) regeneration using single cell spatial transcriptomics*

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Phil Hieter  
*Connecting novel disease gene discoveries to functional characterization research in model organisms*
