## November 16, 2022 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/MSL-Trainee-Talks-Nov-16th-2022.png)

### **Event details:**

**Date:** Wednesday November 16th, 3:00\-4:30pm

**Host:** Dr\. Thibault Mayor

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:  
**[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5Irc\-urrjIuHNFOL43zVD\_HNjdU1Cg3lXzX](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Irc-urrjIuHNFOL43zVD_HNjdU1Cg3lXzX)

### Seminar Schedule:

Trainee Talk 1: **Melanie Bailey – Hieter Lab  
***Modelling a potential drug strategy for the MUS81 endonuclease using cross\-species genetics*

Trainee Talk 2: **Alina Kunitskaya – Piret Lab  
***Impact of concentrated cell medium conditions during T\-cell bioprocessing*

Research Associate Talk: **Dr\. Charlotte Miton – Tokuriki Lab  
***Exploring the mutability landscape of the N\-terminal domain of SARS\-CoV\-2*
