## February 11, 2020 @ 1:30 pm \- 3:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Early-Career-Invited-Lecture-Award-Winners-Symposia-2020.jpg)

*The Michael Smith Laboratories presents a symposia:*

**Date:** Tuesday, February 11\[th\], 2020  
**Time:** 1:30PM – 3:00PM  
**Location:** MSL, Room 102 \(lecture hall\)

Featuring:

**Dr\. Gabi Fragiadakis**  
Assistant Professor from the University of California, San Francisco  
Seminar title: **Human studies and high\-dimensional data: profiling immune state and the microbiome**

**Dr\. Giuliana Rossi**  
Postdoctoral Fellow from École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne  
Seminar title: **Embryonic organoids recapitulate early heart organogenesis**

The symposia is hosted by Dave Ng, Dr\. Fragiadakis is hosted by the Kronstad lab, and Dr\. Rossi is hosted by the Zandstra lab\.

The [Early Career Invited Lecture initiative](https://science.ubc.ca/research/lecture-award) gives units within UBC Science a unique opportunity to introduce promising researchers from around the world to the inclusive, top\-tier community of scholars already working within the Faculty\. Departments and academic units identify top women senior graduate students and post\-docs in their fields\. These rising stars are invited to UBC Vancouver to deliver a unit\-level lecture, network, and experience British Columbia\. Funding is provided by the **Dean of Science**, the **Office of the Provost and Vice\-President Academic**, and the **Strategic Initiatives Fund**\. For more information, please visit [science\.ubc\.ca/research/lecture\-award](https://science.ubc.ca/research/lecture-award)\.
