## December 15, 2021 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/MSL-Trainee-Talks-Dec-15th-2021.jpg)

### **Event details:**

**Date:** Wednesday December 15th, 3:00\-4:30pm

**Host:** Dr\. Freda Miller

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:  
**[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5Ircu6hqjkiGtwGrt7Sholy7pI5nhmhwoop](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Ircu6hqjkiGtwGrt7Sholy7pI5nhmhwoop)

### Seminar Schedule:

Trainee Talk 1:  **Dr\. Omnia Gamal El\-Dien** – Bohlmann Lab  
*Genomic Selection in Western Redcedar*

Trainee Talk 2: **Dr\. Antonio Serapio Palacios** – Finlay Lab  
*Pathogenic and Commensal Bacteria Utilize Antibacterial Weapons to Mediate Niche Occupancy in the Gut*

Trainee Talk 3: **Ryan Wong** – Mayor Lab  
*Fantastic Yeasts and the Pursuit of Production*

Faculty Talk: **Dr\.** **Harry Brumer  
***Humans as bioreactors: Molecular insight into dietary fiber utilization by our gut microbiota*
