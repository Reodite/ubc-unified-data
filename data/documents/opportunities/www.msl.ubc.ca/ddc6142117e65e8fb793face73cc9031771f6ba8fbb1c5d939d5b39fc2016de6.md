## November 17, 2019 \- November 20, 2019

The Canadian Epigenetics, Environment and Health Research Consortium \(CEEHRC\) is pleased to partner with the International Human Epigenome Consortium \(IHEC\) to host the IHEC Annual Meeting and the 6th Canadian Conference on Epigenetics from November 17 to 20, 2019\. The meeting will take place at the Rimrock Resort nestled in the heart of the Canadian Rockies and Banff National Park in Banff, Alberta, Canada, approximately 1\.5 hours from Calgary\. This joint conference will be the largest gathering of Epigenetics researchers in Canada to date and builds on three very successful Canadian meetings \(2016\-2018\) and seven IHEC Annual Meetings \(2012\-2018\)\. 

[For More Information](https://web.cvent.com/event/ae3c33df-fb04-4edc-9d29-6d5718c181fa/summary)
