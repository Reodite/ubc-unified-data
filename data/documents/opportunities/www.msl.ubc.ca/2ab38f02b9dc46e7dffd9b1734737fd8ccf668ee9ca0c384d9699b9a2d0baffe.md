## November 18, 2020 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Seminar-Series-Nov-18th.jpg)

***The Michael Smith Laboratories Seminar Series***

**Wednesday,** **November 18th from 3 – 4:30pm, Zoom**                              

Join Zoom Meeting

[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/j/67301140804?pwd\=YXRJNHdMcnI5Z1dRVElpbmlFUHB5QT09](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/67301140804?pwd=YXRJNHdMcnI5Z1dRVElpbmlFUHB5QT09)

Hosted by Dr\. Steve Withers

Trainee talks 

Jefferies Lab, Maria Baeva

Title: “Are eyes more than just windows to the soul? Exploring ocular vascularization and amyloid deposition in a mouse model of Alzheimer’s disease”

Haney Lab, David Thoms

Title: “Distinguishing Friend from Foe: How Innate Immunity Shapes the Plant Microbiome”

Dr\. Phil Hieter

Title: “Synthetic lethality in model organisms and cancer \(APOYG\)”
