## November 22, 2023 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/msl-trainee-talk-november-22-2023.png)

## Event Details

**Date:** Wednesday Nov\. 22nd, 3:00 – 4:00pm

**Host:** Dr\. Marco Marra

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link: [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5Yofu2vpzwvGNIGmP8lub9AihVR1q\-vJN7s](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Yofu2vpzwvGNIGmP8lub9AihVR1q-vJN7s)**

### Seminar Schedule:

**Trainee Talk 1:** Axel Hauduc – Hirst Lab

**Trainee Talk 2**: Clara Wenjing Xia – Jefferies lab

**Trainee Talk 3**: Karen Thulasi Devendrakumar – Li lab

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Thibault Mayor
