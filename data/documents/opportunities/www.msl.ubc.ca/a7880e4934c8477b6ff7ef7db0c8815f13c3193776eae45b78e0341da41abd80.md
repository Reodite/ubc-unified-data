## July 15, 2019 @ 10:00 am \- 11:00 am

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/armando-villalta-seminar.jpg)

*The Biomedical Research Centre Seminar Series presents a seminar:*

Prof\. Armando Villalta  
Assistant Professor  
University of California, Irvine  
Physiology and Biophysics Institute of Immunology 

[View Seminar Poster](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Prof.-Villalta-Talk-July-15-2019.pdf)

**Date:** Monday, July 15 2019  
**Time:** 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM

**Title:** Immunological regulation of muscle injury and regeneration  
during muscular dystrophy

Light refreshments provided\.
