## June 12, 2023 @ 1:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/12-06-2023-MSL-Single-Cell-Symposium-FINAL.png)

The MSL welcomes Drs\. Sam Aparicio and Karen Cheung, world experts and pioneers in the field of experimental and computational approaches to quantitative single cell analysis\.

This mini\-symposium features two faculty talks from 1\-3pm and a panel discussion from 3\-4pm\.  A social hour will follow the speaking series, please join us in the MSL atrium from 4\-5pm for refreshments\.

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\.

**Invited Speakers:  
**

-   **Dr\. Sam Aparicio  
    ***Professor, UBC Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine*
    
    **Talk title:** *Decoding cancer dynamics at single cell resolution*
    
-   **Dr\. Karen Cheung  
    ****Professor, UBC Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering*
    
    *
    
    **Talk title:** *Microfluidics, imaging, and inkjet for single cell technology  
    *
    

**Panel Discussion:  
**Drs\. Sam Aparicio, Karen Cheung, Marco Marra &amp; Adi SteifModerated by Dr\. Dave Ng

**Host:** Dr\. Martin Hirst
