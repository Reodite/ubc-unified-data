## October 9, 2019 @ 1:00 pm \- 2:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/2019-Mike-Page-Award-Ceremony.jpg)

*The Centre for Blood Research presents an award ceremony:*

The Michael John Page Postdoctoral Fellow Award recognizes a Postdoctoral Fellow who reflects Dr\. Page’s academic excellence and his passion for life\.

Dr\. Peter Rahfeld, 2019 Postdoctoral Fellow Award Recipient  
Withers Lab  
Department of Chemistry &amp; CBR

**Date:** Wednesday, October 9, 2019  
**Time:** 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM  
**Location:** LSC 3

**Title:** Discovery Of New Enzymes For Universal Donor Blood Production

*Refreshments to follow at 4:00 PM – 5:30 PM at the CBR 4th floor pod\.*

**Sponsored by:** The Centre for Blood Research, Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, and the Michael Page Endowment\.
