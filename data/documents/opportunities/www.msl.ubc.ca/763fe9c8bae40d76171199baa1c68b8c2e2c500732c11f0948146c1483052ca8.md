## January 22, 2025 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/MSL-Trainee-Talks-January-22-2025.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Joerg Bohlmann

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/ET\-voachSNK89psQCH0vGw](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/ET-voachSNK89psQCH0vGw) 

**Seminar Schedule:**

**Trainee Talk 1**: Ali Chamkalani – Piret lab  
*Seeking the Culture Critical Process Parameters that Impact T\-cell Growth and Phenotypes*

**Trainee Talk 2**: Heather Baker – Mayor lab  
*The co\-chaperone DNAJA2 buffers proteasomal degradation of cytosolic proteins with missense mutations*

**Trainee Talk 3**: Alireza Omidi – Gsponer lab  
*AlphaFold\-Multimer accurately captures interactions and dynamics of intrinsically disordered protein regions*

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Paul Pavlidis  
*Easing the path to genomics data re\-use and meta\-analysis*
