## January 15 @ 10:30 am \- 11:30 am

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Jan-15-EL-Faculty-Search-Series-Dr.-Elisabeth-Adkins-Marnik.png)

**Speaker:** Dr\. Elisabeth Adkins Marnik – *Director of Science Education &amp; Outreach, MDI Biological Laboratory*

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. The speaker will be delivering their talk in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/j/64475656236?pwd\=QqK8irv3dtI2QMTwke7WP37c2PiQjD\.1](https://ubc.zoom.us/j/64475656236?pwd=QqK8irv3dtI2QMTwke7WP37c2PiQjD.1)

**Talk title:** *Building Immunity: How Adaptive Learning Shapes Both Host Defense and Student Success*
