## October 18, 2023 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/MSL-Trainee-Talks-Oct-18th-2023-edit.png)

### **Event details:**

**Date:** Wednesday Oct\. 18th, 3:00\-4:00pm

**Host:** Dr\. James Piret

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:  
**[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5wkd\-yurjwrHtRMqT\-FZDQhLK4z0cVZ5pD1](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5wkd-yurjwrHtRMqT-FZDQhLK4z0cVZ5pD1)

### Seminar Schedule:

**Trainee Talk**: Elizabeth Stephens – Hieter lab  
*Mutational mapping using dominant synthetic lethal screening to uncover cohesin structure\-function relationships*

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Jörg Gsponer  
*A new phase for white\-collar workers of cellular systems*
