## February 19, 2025 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/MSL-Trainee-Talks-February-19-2025.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Wilfred Jefferies

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/6uYK\_qx5S0qIb43yghvm9w\#/registration](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/6uYK_qx5S0qIb43yghvm9w#/registration)

**Seminar Schedule:**

**Trainee Talk 1**: Michael Easson – Bohlmann lab  
*Advancing Bioengineered Production of* *Montbretin* *A\-Explorations in Enzyme Engineering*

**Trainee Talk 2**: Mahebali \(Makpal\) Tabusi – Finlay lab  
*Early\-Life Enteric Bacterial Infection Alters Hippocampal Cell Fate and Leads to Cognitive Deficits*

**Trainee Talk 3**: Anthony Oppedisano – Hieter lab  
*Using Dominant Genetics to Identify Inhibitors of the MUS81 Endonuclease*

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Leonard Foster  
*Prospects for single\-cell ‘omics beyond RNAz*
