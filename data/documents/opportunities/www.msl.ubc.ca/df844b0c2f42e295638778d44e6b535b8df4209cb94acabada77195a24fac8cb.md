## August 22, 2023 @ 10:00 am \- 10:30 am

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/MSL-ChatGPT-seminar.jpg)

Please come join the RNA &amp; Formulation Core summer volunteer, Gurubaran Nathan, in MSL 101 for a casual introduction on how to incorporate ChatGPT into your research\. Guru will provide a brief summary of ChatGPT, followed by the essential tools to make the most of the AI platform\. He will provide tips, examples, and other AI platforms that can help accelerate calculations, spreadsheet generation, prompt engineering, generating ideas/text, and literature review\. Don’t forget to bring your laptop\!

**Talk title:**  
Introduction to ChatGPT for Researchers

**Speaker:**

Gurubaran Nathan – *RNA &amp; Formulation Core Volunteer*

**Location:**  
MSL 101
