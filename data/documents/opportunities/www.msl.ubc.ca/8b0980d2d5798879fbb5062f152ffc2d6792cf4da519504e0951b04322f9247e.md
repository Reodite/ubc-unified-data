## June 17 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:00 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/MSL-Trainee-Talks-June-17-2026.png)

**Host:** Dr\. Brett Finlay

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:** [https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/FAFvfL\-yQsWRY8qXhQP\_jw](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/FAFvfL-yQsWRY8qXhQP_jw)

**Seminar Schedule:**

**Trainee Talk 1:** William Stewart – Brumer lab  
*Systems based characterization of the xyloglucan utilization system from the human gut bacterium Segatella copri*

**Trainee Talk 2:** Sam Oguntimehin – Bohlmann lab  
*Metabolic engineering of MbA in heterologous plant systems*

**Trainee Talk 3:** Harry Ooi – Piret lab  
*Deep\-well Plate Scale Down System for Mammalian Cell Bioprocess Development*

**Faculty Talk:** Dr\. Wilfred Jefferies  
*On the Needle and the Damage Done?*
