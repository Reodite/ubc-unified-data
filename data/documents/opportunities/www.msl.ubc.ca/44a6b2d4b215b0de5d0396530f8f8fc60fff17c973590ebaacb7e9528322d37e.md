Wendy Lamson, B\.Sc\. LL\.M  
Partner and Patent Agent  
Perley\-Robertson, Hill &amp; McDougall LLP

[View Seminar Poster](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/W-Lamson-Seminar-Announcement.jpg)

**Date:** Wednesday, August 21, 2019  
**Time:** 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM  
**Location:** Life Sciences Centre 3 \(LSC3\)

**Title:** Tips and tricks for optimizing patent protection of nanomedicine innovation

**Abstract:** Protecting intellectual property need not be complicated\. Wendy will walk you through highlights of the entire process of building a patent portfolio, covering key  
discussion points such as:

-   Doing your own literature searches prior to applying for a patent
-   Fundamentals of patent drafting
-   Timing and claim strategy
-   Country selection
-   Best practices for arguing before the Patent Office to secure allowance of broadest claims
-   Strategies for extending patent life \(e\.g\., file continuations and/or divisional patent applications, risk of double patenting\)
-   Best practices for invention mining
-   Budgeting and keeping costs in check
-   Determining when to maintain trade secrets rather than pursue patent protection

**Hosted by:** Dr\. Chris Tam \(tam7@mail\.ubc\.ca\)
