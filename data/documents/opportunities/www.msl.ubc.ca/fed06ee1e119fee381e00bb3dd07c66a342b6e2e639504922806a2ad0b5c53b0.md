## December 25, 2019 \- January 1, 2020

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/UBC_Xmas_2019_Social_VAN_1200x1200.png)

The MSL Admin Office will be closed from December 25, 2019 to January 1, 2020\. The Admin Office will reopen on January 2, 2020\. Happy Holidays\!
