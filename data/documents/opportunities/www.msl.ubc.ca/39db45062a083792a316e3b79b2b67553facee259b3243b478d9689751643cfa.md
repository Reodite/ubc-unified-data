## October 16, 2019 @ 6:00 pm \- 7:30 pm

$10

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Brain-and-Gut-Poster-v3.png)

*The BrainTalks Speakers Series presents an event called “The Brain and the Gut”:*

[Dr\. Brett Finlay](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/people/dr-brett-finlay/)  
Role of the Microbiome on the Brain

[Dr\. Leslie Wicholas](http://www.mdabc.net/meet-doctors)   
Diet and Depression: An Introduction to Nutritional Psychiatry

[Thara Vayali](https://www.tharavayali.ca/)   
The Brain in your Gut

**Date:** Wednesday, October 16, 2019  
**Time:** 6:00 PM  
**Location:** Paetzhold Auditorium, Vancouver General Hospital \(VGH\)

Join us at the next BrainTalks Speaker Series event: The Brain and the Gut\! There will be multiple speakers presenting on the emerging link between the gut microbiome and brain disease\. This will be followed by a panel discussion, with treats to be enjoyed after the talks\. Get [tickets here](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/the-brain-and-the-gut-tickets-71824648457)\!

**Hosted by:** The BrainTalks Team
