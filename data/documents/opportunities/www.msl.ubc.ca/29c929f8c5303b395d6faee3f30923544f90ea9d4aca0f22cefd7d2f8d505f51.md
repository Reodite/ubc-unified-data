## November 17, 2021 @ 3:00 pm \- 4:30 pm

[Image](https://www.msl.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/MSL-Trainee-Talks-Nov-17th-2021.jpg)

### **Event details:**

**Date:** Wednesday November 17th, 3:00\-4:30pm

**Host:** Dr\. Paul Pavlidis

**This seminar will be presented in a hybrid format\. All speakers will be delivering talks in person at the MSL Lecture Theatre \(room 102\)\.** Audience members are welcome to attend either in person or via the zoom link\. Those connecting via zoom will be able to ask questions during the Q&amp;A portion using the chat function\.

**Zoom registration link:  
**[https://ubc\.zoom\.us/meeting/register/u5Ivf\-ysqDIjGteaN13pGa3f5LetWjNG4Ndk](https://ubc.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Ivf-ysqDIjGteaN13pGa3f5LetWjNG4Ndk)

### Seminar Schedule:

Trainee Talk 1:  **Adele Khavari** – Kastrup Lab  
*Role of coagulation factor XII in decompression sickness* 

Trainee Talk 2: **Christopher Lee** – Kronstad Lab  
*Investigating the role of 3\-hydroxyanthranillic acid on iron uptake and virulence in the fungal pathogen ‘Cryptococcus neoformans’*

Trainee Talk 3: **Anne\-Sophie Sack** – Snutch Lab  
*The Role of Cav3\.2 T\-type Calcium Channels in Dentate Gyrus Excitability*

Faculty Talk: **Dr\.** **Wilf Jefferies  
***Current Research Vignettes*
