The LinguisticsNEW workshop brings together the linguistics departments at UBC and Simon Fraser University to discuss our recent work and generally build community\. This year it will consist of two rounds of poster sessions\. If you would like to present your work, [submit an abstract here](https://auth.oxfordabstracts.com/?redirect=/stages/78568/submitter)\.
