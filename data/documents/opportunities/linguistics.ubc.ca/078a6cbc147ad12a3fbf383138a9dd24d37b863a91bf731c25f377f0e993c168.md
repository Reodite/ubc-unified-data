Land &amp; Food Systems, Life Sciences Institute, and the Department of Linguistics presents “Careers after your TAship”, a career panel event on April 12, 2\-4 pm PT in the GSS Loft \(Room 4202 in The Nest\)\. Four panelists will be invited to discuss their career experiences, emphasizing the skills they’ve acquired in teaching roles that are transferrable to \(alt\-\)ac careers\. **Register here:** [https://ubc\.ca1\.qualtrics\.com/jfe/form/SV\_2senVpHDBHTksaG](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2senVpHDBHTksaG)

**Panelists**

Hannah Green: Language Program Coordinator at First People’s Cultural Council

Michael Fry: Applied Scientist, Amazon Alexa Game Control

David Oliver: Associate Professor of Teaching, UBC Department of Microbiology and Immunology

Alisha Buttar: Registered Dietitian &amp; M\.Sc\. Candidate, UBC Faculty of Land &amp; Food Systems
