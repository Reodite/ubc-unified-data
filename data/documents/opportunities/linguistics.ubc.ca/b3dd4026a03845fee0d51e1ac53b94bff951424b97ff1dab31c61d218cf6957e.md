The Northwest Linguistics Conference will be held at UBC\. The NWLC is an annual conference, hosted on an alternating basis by Simon Fraser University, University of British Columbia, University of Victoria, and University of Washington\. It is an opportunity for students to present their research and make connections across universities\. Here is the [program](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/27/2026/03/NWLC-Program-5.3.pdf)\.
