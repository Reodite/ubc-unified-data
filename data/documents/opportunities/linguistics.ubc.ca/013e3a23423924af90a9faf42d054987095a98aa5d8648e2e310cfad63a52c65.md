Have you collected customer/client data but don’t know how gain meaningful insights from it? Do you have a great idea for a data project but don’t have the time, resources or tools to get it started? Why not get our students to help?

The UBC Master of Data Science \(MDS\) in Computational Linguistics program is now seeking capstone partners from a variety of organizations to submit project proposals that can unlock the meaning behind their data\. You can apply for the opportunity to work with up to four students, who specialize in modern data science tools and techniques, such as statistical analysis and visualizations\. You will receive a customized solution \(e\.g\. new app, analytic reports, scripts or software\) for your problem\.

By participating as a capstone partner, you will not only have access to data scientists in training but will also build a long\-term relationship with the UBC MDS program\.

If you have questions about MDS Capstone and how to submit a proposal, [sign\-up for our information session](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/employers/capstone-project?utm_campaign=mds+r2026+capstone+info+session&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_source=ling+website&amp;utm_content=shared&amp;utm_term=#virtual_information_session) on **September 12, 2025 at 3pm PT**
