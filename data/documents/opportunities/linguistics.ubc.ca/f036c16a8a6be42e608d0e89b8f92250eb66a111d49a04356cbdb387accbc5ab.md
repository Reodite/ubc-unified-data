Join us for the second Language Sciences Graduate Student and Postdoctoral Fellow Research Day, which this year will be held virtually\! Please [RSVP](https://app.cyberimpact.com/click-tracking?ct=gcbzJJoL6PHElPwV0xQLyS_vgZjPZrLyRdMLL9mv8GPyylCNDzblRMyVgPtDiKmQUfNohM4InuxzwmVU1UDd3UV_VvzqYe8Se-roVN0KmUk~)\. The [**schedule**](https://app.cyberimpact.com/click-tracking?ct=k5CPLYrhzBrZZLBxOQ--eTspr93WrA6qO0AyujzFa13Ux3BxglBSQf0Pd0KoAYW3ppw_YzkR0X632qsr8EV670Ob7szQXffj1PihPUUj_UU~) is available\.

Featuring presenters from Psychology, Electrical and Computer Engineering, Music, Linguistics, Sauder School of Business, English Language and Literatures, and more, as well as from UBCO, SFU and Dalhousie University, this conference will showcase the work of graduate students and postdoctoral fellows in the diverse fields of language sciences\.

The 21 talks will be held across two rooms, moderated by faculty members, while our virtual poster session will utilize the gather\.town platform\. And, chat with other researchers in our virtual coffee room, open all day\.

Everyone is welcome to attend this free conference, so please spread the word\!

If you have any questions, please email language\.sciences@ubc\.ca\. If you require an accessibility\-related measure \(e\.g\.: sign language interpretation, captioning, or any other accessibility\-related measure\) please contact Ella Fund\-Reznicekat ella\.fr@ubc\.caor 604\-822\-7435\.
