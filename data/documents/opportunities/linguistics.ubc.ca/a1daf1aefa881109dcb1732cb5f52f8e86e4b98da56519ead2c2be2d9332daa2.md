You are invited to join UBC Master of Data Science in Computational Linguistics \(MDS\-CL\) students and alumni for a virtual information session to learn more about the student experience in the UBC MDS\-CL program in Vancouver, British Columbia\.

At this live panel, **MDS Computational Linguistics students and alumni** will:

-   Share their student experiences and learning in the MDS Computational Linguistics program
-   Talk about the intensive 10\-month program
-   Discuss how they’re applying their MDS Computational Linguistics degree in their fields
-   Answer your questions live\.

**Participants include**:

-   **Moderator:** Ryan Taylor, *Career Planning and Placement Coordinator, MDS\-CL*
-   **Panelists:** 
    -   Celine Liu, *Current Student, MDS\-CL Class of 2021*
    -   Jonathan Chan, *Alumnus, MDS\-CL, Class of 2020*
    -   Komal Mistry, *Alumna, MDS\-CL, Class of 2020*
