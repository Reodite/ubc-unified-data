You are invited to join UBC Master of Data Science in Computational Linguistics alumni for a virtual information session to learn more about the student experience in the UBC MDS Computational Linguistics program in Vancouver, British Columbia\.

At this live panel, **MDS Computational Linguistics alumni** will:

-   Share their student experiences and learning in the MDS Computational Linguistics program
-   Talk about the intensive 10\-month program
-   Discuss how they’re applying their MDS Computational Linguistics degree in their fields
-   Answer your questions live\.

**Participants include**:

-   **Moderator:** Sonya Thomlinson, *Career Advisor, MDS Computational Linguistics*
-   **Panelists:** 
    -   Jata McCabe, *Alumna, Class of 2023*
    -   Juan Roesel, *Alumnus, Class of 2021*
    -   Millie Lou, *Alumna, Class of 2020*
