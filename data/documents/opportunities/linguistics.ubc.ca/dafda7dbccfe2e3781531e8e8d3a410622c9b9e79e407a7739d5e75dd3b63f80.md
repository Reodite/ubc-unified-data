The University of British Columbia is pleased to host a joint conference bringing together [SULA \(Semantics of Under\-represented Languages in the Americas\)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/sula/) and [TripleA \(Semantics of Languages of Africa, Asia, Australia and Oceania\)](https://semantics-triplea.org/)\. Here is [the program](https://blogs.ubc.ca/sulatriplea/program/)\. This will be the first time that these conferences will be combined into one joint event\. The conference also marks the 25th Anniversary of SULA, which took place for the first time in 2001\. The goal of the conference is to bring together researchers working on languages or dialects which are under\-represented in the literature on formal semantics\. There will be a speaker panel on Indigenous perspectives on linguistics research and language revitalization\.

The invited speakers are:

-   **[Ana Laura Arrieta Zamudio](https://sites.google.com/view/analauraarrietazamudio/home)** \(University of British Columbia\)
-   [**Heather Burge**](https://uas.alaska.edu/dir/hdburge.html) \(University of Alaska Southeast\)
-   [**Reginald Duah**](https://www.ug.edu.gh/linguistics.ug/people/duah) \(University of Ghana\)
-   **Herman Fillmore** \(Washoe Tribe of Nevada and California\)
-   [**Terrance Gatchalian**](https://terrancegatchalian.github.io/) \(University of Toronto\)
-   [**Zahra Mirrazi**](https://www.zahra-mirrazi.com/) \(University of Göttingen\)
-   [**Roberta Pires de Oliveira**](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Roberta-Pires-De-Oliveira-2) \(Federal University of Santa Catarina\)
-   [**Justin Royer**](https://justinroyer.lingspace.org/) \(Université de Montréal\)
-   [**Eve Suharwardy**](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/persons/eve.suharwardy-postgrad) \(University of Manchester\)
