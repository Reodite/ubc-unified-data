Murray Schellenberg will present a research seminar on new data management requirements for funding\. The event will take place in the TFS seminar room\.

**Linguistics and the New Tri\-Agency Data Management Policy** 

Abstract: Earlier this year, the Tri\-Agencies \(SSHRC, NSERC, CIHR\) released their new Data Management Policy about data\-related requirements for scholars applying for funding\. This talk is intended to be a discussion of the new requirements and how the requirements can be met by \(and perhaps even be made useful to\) linguists\.
