Chris Hammerly will facilitate a workshop on *Indigenous Language Representation in Linguistics Classrooms* from 3:30 – 4:30 pm \(TFS seminar room\)\. This workshop is offered as part of the department’s TA Training program, and aimed at both TAs and Instructors in the department\.

TAs are then invited to take part in an in\-camera follow\-up TA discussion from 4:30 – 5:30 pm with Kaili Vesik, Ana Laura Arrieta\-Zamudio, and Bruce Oliver in the grad lounge\.
