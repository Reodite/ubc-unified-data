Kaili Vesik and Rachel Soo will facilitate a workshop on Pedagogical Strategies\. This workshop is offered as part of the department’s TA Training program, and aimed at TAs in the department\. We’ve incorporated the feedback TAs provided at last year’s workshop and designed new content and activities tailored to specified TA needs\. This year’s workshop will feature concrete examples of online tools \(for in\-person and online learning\) and real world case studies from both computational and theoretical linguistics courses\. As such, even if TAs have attended this workshop in the past, they are highly encouraged to attend this year’s iteration\!
