**How do Spanish\-learning infants discover morphological suffixes?**

Suffixes like English *\-s*, *\-ed* and *\-ing*, and Spanish *\-a* and *\-o* are the basic building blocks of syntax\. In this talk, I will first identify the mechanism by which English\-learning infants find suffixes\. To do so, I will use modeling to evaluate various mechanisms for affix discovery against the developmental trajectory uncovered in experiments with English\-learning infants\. Then, in two experiments I will test the predictions of the successful model on infants learning Spanish\. I will end by discussing the essential properties of a successful affix discovery mechanism, and its implications for acquisition of other languages\.
