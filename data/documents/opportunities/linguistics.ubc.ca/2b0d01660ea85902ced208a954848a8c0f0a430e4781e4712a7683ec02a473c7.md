The UBC Speech &amp; Linguistics Student Association \(SALSA\) is organizing a Labs of Ling event, in which [various labs](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/research/labs-groups/?fbclid=IwZXh0bgNhZW0CMTAAAR1r1pciATwaRC8EHB5rvVxXskBRShXl2bnuzocZmcdxJ_ETEwugz3smy2I_aem_d3ScjgecSBd1nC83K8q_uQ) in the department will provide information for undergraduates interested in getting involved in linguistics research\. Of course, undergrads can also do this anytime through one\-on\-one interaction with faculty\.
