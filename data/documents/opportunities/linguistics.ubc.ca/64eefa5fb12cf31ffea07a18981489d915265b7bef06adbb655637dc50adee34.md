Dr\. Darko Ordic \(UBC Psychology\) will give an in\-person research seminar 3:30\-4:30pm, January 26th, 2024\. The title and abstract for the talk can be found below\.

**Title**: How much language is in thought?

**Abstract**: In this talk, I present recent work from my lab arguing that language acts as instructions for directing – but not creating – thoughts\. I argue that: \(1\) Language does not automatically activate “predictive cascades” \(i\.e\., Bayesian priors\) or that, if it does, those do not affect our perception; \(2\) That language can indicate the relevant sortals for perceptual enumeration \(both breaking and combining objects freely\); and \(3\) That language provides instructions for superlative comparison in many perceptual operations\. My ultimate argument is that language is a tool for improving the performance – not competency – of perception and cognition\.
