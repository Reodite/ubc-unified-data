Through our outreach programs, our donors support students and teachers in the community and at UBC\. Their contributions are matched with funding from the UBC Mathematics Department and the Faculty of Science, resulting in an increased impact of all of our activities\. We thank them for their important investment in mathematics education\.

If you would like to support the UBC Math Outreach Programs, please fill out this [form](https://www.donate.startanevolution.ca/ea-action/action?ea.client.id=1730&amp;ea.campaign.id=16774&amp;direct%20my%20Gift%20to%20Module=N193) or contact our department head:
