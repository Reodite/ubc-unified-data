Your Arts degree opens the doors to different industries and opportunities\. However, do you know how to effectively pitch your degree to employers? Join the Arts Undergraduate Society and UBC Center for Student Involvement and Careers event, “How to Pitch Your Arts Degree to Employers,” to learn how to talk about your degree in a way that makes sense to them and enhance your ability to market your brand\. You will learn about tips for resumes, cover letters, interview skills, and more\! Following the panel, you will have an opportunity to network with the employers in attendance\. This event welcomes all Arts students and all year levels\!

Registration is required, so RSVP below to get a chance to win a $25 Amazon Gift Card\!
