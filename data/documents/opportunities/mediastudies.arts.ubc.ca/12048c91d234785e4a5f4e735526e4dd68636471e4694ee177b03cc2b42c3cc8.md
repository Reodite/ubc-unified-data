A 5\-part series, Matriarch Illuminations will feature Jessica Wood, known as Si Sityaawks \(Woman who creates change\) from the Gitxsan and Tsimshian First Nations with roots among the Tahltan and Nisga’a Nations, and the current Policy Practitioner Fellow at the UBC School of Public Policy and Global Affairs\. She will host fellow Matriarchs from across Turtle Island and discuss the theme of “Storytelling as Guidance\.”

Students, staff, faculty and community members are invited to pour themselves a cup of tea and sit around the virtual table to listen\.

**Format**: A 45 min discussion, 15 min Q&amp;A from 5:00 pm – 6:00 pm on Zoom

**Audience**: All are welcome\. Questions will be prioritized from Indigenous, Black and People of Colour attendees\.

Visit the [website](https://sppga.ubc.ca/events/event/matriarch-illuminations-storytelling-as-guidance/) for more details\.
