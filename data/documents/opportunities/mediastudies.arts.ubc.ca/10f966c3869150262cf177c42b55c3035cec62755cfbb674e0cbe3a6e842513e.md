UTown UBC from UBC Campus \+ Community Planning is hosting a design competition to gather community\-curated content for the permanently installed overhead projection system on campus at Lee Square\. The Lee Square Projector is a permanently installed, overhead projection system that illuminates the ground of the square with colourful light designs throughout the year\. With the goal of activating the campus space at night, we are excited to use the projector to feature art and animation by the community\.

In this design competition, we invite you to create an art or animation that showcases what lights you up\. The winning designs will be selected by a judging panel and projected onto UBC’s Money and Raymond M\.C\. Lee Square \(by the UBC Bookstore\) and will receive a cash prize of up to $300\. There are various prize categories to enter, and this competition is open to children, youth, students, faculty, staff and alumni at UBC campuses, UNA and Acadia Park\.

Submissions due by November 18, 2020\.

For more information on design guidelines and submissions, please check out [https://utown\.ubc\.ca/lights](https://utown.ubc.ca/lights)
