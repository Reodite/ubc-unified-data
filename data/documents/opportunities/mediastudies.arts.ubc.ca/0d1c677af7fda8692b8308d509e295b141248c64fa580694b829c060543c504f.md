Hey First\-Year BMS students\!

Share your ideas, artworks, and thoughts about Media with us so it can get the recognition it deserves\! We are accepting all works: Essays, Short Films, Visual Artworks, Music, and more\. Not limited to works created in the BMS program – submit your personal/passion projects as well\!

We will compile all your works together which will not only be given to you as a keepsake to document your First\-Year in the BMS, but also be shared to a greater audience via our communication platforms\.

In addition, every student who submits will be entered in a draw to win a $25 Starbucks gift card\!

For more information, check out our [submission guidelines](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1bHpM8pwQQEWX4vIE1gzCAsKUlJJNB9DA8erkuBxlAQU/present?slide=id.p) or contact [@bms\_peermentor](https://www.instagram.com/bms_peermentor/) on Instagram or by email at [bmspeermentor@gmail\.com](mailto:bmspeermentor@gmail.com)\. We are accepting submissions from now until March 25th, 2021 @11:59pm PST\. We can’t wait to see what you’ve got\!
