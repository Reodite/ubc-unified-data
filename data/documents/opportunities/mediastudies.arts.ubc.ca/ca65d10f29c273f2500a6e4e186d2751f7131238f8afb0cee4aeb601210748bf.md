Calling all BMS First\-Year students\! Are you looking for help with your CPSC and CRWR classes? BMS Peer Mentors Teann and Jueun have organized a virtual mentorship session all about CPSC exam tips and advice for CRWR projects\. They will also highlight some CPSC and CRWR courses that you might consider taking in the future\. Teann and Jueun will also provide extra \(fun\) resources for further exploration on Media Studies\.

Attending your peer mentorship sessions provides a great opportunity to connect with your peers and BMS students in different years of the program\. Hui and Claire are looking forward to seeing you all\. Don’t miss out\!

Location: On Zoom\. \(Zoom details have been sent to your email, please email arts\.mediastudies@ubc\.ca to retrieve the link to join the session if you didn’t receive it\.\)
