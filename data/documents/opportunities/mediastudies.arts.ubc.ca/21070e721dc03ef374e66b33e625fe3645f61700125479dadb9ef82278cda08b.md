At the UBC Sexual Violence Prevention and Response Office, we consider it our responsibility to raise our voices in support of women, trans, nonbinary people, and in particular IBPOC, to make room for their stories, share the pain, and celebrate defiance and joy\.

Check out the following events happening throughout this term:

🔹 GlobalEYES Talks Club Native – Mar\. 24th, 5\-6 pm

-   Join Lauren Casey and Tashia Kootenayoo in a discussion on the documentary film Club Native\.

🔹 Disclosure – Mar\. 31, 3\-6 pm

-   Join us for a group screening of the documentary film Disclosure, followed by a conversation with the Director, Sam Feder\!

🔹 SVPRO’s Movie Night: Music Video Remix – Apr\. 15, 5\-6 pm

-   Take a closer look at how relationships and gender are portrayed in the music video industry\. Join the conversation\!

Read more about each event and register through this [link\.](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2beXAEuyQTJ5ioC)
