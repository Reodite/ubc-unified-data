Participate in the AUS Photography and Digital Art contest as a part of the Arts Undergraduate Society’s initiative to revamp the Buchanan Meekison Arts Students’ Space \(MASS\)\.

As part of the decoration plan, we want to showcase your art/photos in a designated space in the Buchanan MASS space by hosting a contest\.

**Theme**

Environmental sustainability

**Sub\-themes**

Climate change, resource management, natural environment and biodiversity

**Objective**

Create a digital graphic or photograph that depicts at least one of the 3 themes on environmental sustainability: climate change, resource management, and natural biodiversity\.

**Prizes**   
Grand prize: $100 Amazon Gift Card  
Top 5 winners: $25 Amazon Gift Card each

Please check out the submissions guideline [here](https://www.facebook.com/events/907875463337118/)\.

[Submission form](https://forms.gle/HQhg3aQ9btXCZUex6)
