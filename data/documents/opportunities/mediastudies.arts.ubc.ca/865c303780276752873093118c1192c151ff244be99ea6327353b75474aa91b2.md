UBC Public Humanities presents a Public Scholarship Workshop series: Curating Exhibitions as Public Scholarship\. This moderated conversation will discuss how to create an exhibition\. Panelists will demonstrate the process of developing a scholarly exhibit as a new mode of research at varying skill levels, how to work with a variety of tangible and intangible objects, how to work with various institutions, and more\. ⁣  
⁣  
This event is free and open to the public, but registration is required\. The conversation will be hosted via Zoom\. A link to the event will be sent to the email given in your RSVP\.

Please register for the event [here](https://publichumanities.ubc.ca/events/event/curating-exhibitions-as-public-scholarship/?login)\.
