> UBC Bachelor of Media Studies \(BMS\) is an innovative flagship program that blends practice, theory, and research across eight disciplines, aiming to cultivate agents of transformation that will thrive in rapidly evolving media industries\.

Applying to the BMS

Review the two admissions pathways and the requirements, application process, and deadlines for each\.

[Source link](https://mediastudies.arts.ubc.ca/prospective-students/admissions/)

Your Supplemental Application

The supplemental application is where you'll upload your portfolio and written responses, which are the primary criterion for admissibility to the BMS\.

[Source link](https://mediastudies.arts.ubc.ca/prospective-students/supplemental-application/)

Transferring into the BMS

If you've already started postsecondary studies and hope to join the Year 2 cohort, learn about the Foundational Preparatory Academic requirement\.

[Source link](https://mediastudies.arts.ubc.ca/prospective-students/year-2-entry/)
