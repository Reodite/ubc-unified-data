Drop\-in to Arts Alumni Talks to meet with a UBC Arts alumna, Connie Wu \(BMS’20\) for casual – and candid – conversations about their UBC experience, student life and career journeys following graduation\.

Having just recently graduated from UBC in 2020 with a degree in Media Studies, Connie Wu \(BMS’20\) was able to quickly transfer her degree and skills to a professional role in digital media and events\. Meet Connie at the next Alumni Talks to learn some valuable tips that helped launch her career after graduation\!

Connie is a communicator, passionate about creating and delivering content through multimedia storytelling\. Born and raised in Asia, Connie is excited to be involved in the promotion of Asian cultures and languages, here in Canada, while she is apart from her home\.

This is a free event and lunch will be provided\. Attendance is limited and will be granted on a first\-come, first\-served basis\.
