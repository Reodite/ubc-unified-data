As Arts students, the skills you’re developing as undergrads—such as critical thinking, writing and research—are perfectly suited to the field of communications\. From digital marketing to community engagement, public relations to publishing, there is a breadth of communications roles available to you when you launch your career\.

Join us for a virtual networking opportunity held by the UBC Faculty of Arts on Thursday, January 21st, at 5:30\-6:30PM PST\.
