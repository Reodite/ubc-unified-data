**A virtual workshop series aimed at those interested in translating family lore into the digital age**

## About this Event

Interested in how you can translate family lore into the digital age? Join PCHC’s virtual storytelling workshop series to gain experience in storytelling, filmmaking, and sharing virtual family history that can be passed on for generations to come\!

Our three workshops will guide you through the basics of storytelling, interviewing, filming, and editing with phone and computer applications\. Participants will also have the opportunity to share their own film and view each other’s stories\. No prior experience required\.

Registration fee: $15 – for all 3 workshops \(April 15, April 29, May 13 – all at 5:30pm – 7:30pm\.\)

Spaces are limited\. Please register by April 13th through this [link](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/pchc-virtual-stories-webinar-series-tickets-148659367169)\.

This project is produced in collaboration with UBC ACAM and UBC INSTRCC\.
