### Why Donate?

Magnify your impact and change lives, again and again\.

A recurring payroll pledge makes an incredible impact on our community\. Select an amount that works for you and it will be automatically donated from your paycheck\. Even a small amount made regularly has a huge impact on our local community\.

During the annual fall campaign, you will receive personalized emails from pledge@unitedway\.ubc\.ca with your unique link to make a payroll pledge\. If you don’t have the link or need another one please email [unitedway\.ok@ubc\.ca](mailto:unitedway.ok@ubc.ca)\.

You will be sent a summary of your donations for tax purposes annually\.
