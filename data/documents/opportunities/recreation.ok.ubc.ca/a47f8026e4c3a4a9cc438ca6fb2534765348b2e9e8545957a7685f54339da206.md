## Glow with the Flow

### [Image](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/105/2019/10/Glow-image-1024x683.jpg)

Glow sticks\. Face paint\. Live DJ\.

One of UBCO’s must\-attend events, our annual glow\-in\-the\-dark evening yoga session is open to all and held in the campus gym\.

There will be a pre\-party with glow face\-paint, a DJ, and a photo booth\.

**November 30, 2025**

Pre\-party starts at 5:30\-6:45pm

Doors open at 6:45pm, make sure you bring your yoga mat\!

Yoga starts at 7:00\-8:00pm

[Register Here\!](http://ca.apm.activecommunities.com/ubcokanagan/Activity_Search/46977)
