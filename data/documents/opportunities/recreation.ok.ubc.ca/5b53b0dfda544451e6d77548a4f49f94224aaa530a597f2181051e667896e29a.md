## **ROCK CLIMBING DROP INS**

ALL SUMMER LONG

Student, staff &amp; faculty drop\-in Tuesdays and Fridays until the end of August\. Climb anytime during the day for $12\.00 drop\-in which is a $3\.00 savings\. Shoe and harness rental for $2\.25\.

No registration is required, just show your student card or UBC ID\!

**Dates:** Tuesdays &amp; Fridays

**Time:** Anytime during open hours

**Cost:** $12

**Location:** [Beyond the Crux](http://www.beyondthecrux.info/)

[LEARN MORE](http://www.beyondthecrux.info/)

## bike or hike at big white

ALL SUMMER LONG

All UBCO students will receive Youth Pricing on mountain biking and scenic chairlift tickets this summer at Big White Ski Resort\. Simply present a valid student card at the Ticket Office in the Village Centre to receive the discount\.

**Dates:** July 7 – September 5

**Location:** [Big White Ski Resort](https://www.bigwhite.com/geo/kel) 

[LEARN MORE](https://www.bigwhite.com/summer)
