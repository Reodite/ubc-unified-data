## **A redesigned high and low ropes course is coming in 2027\.**

We’re excited to share that Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre is undertaking a full redesign and rebuild of its high and low ropes course, with a new course anticipated to open in Spring 2027\.

The new ropes course is part of our ongoing commitment to providing safe, sustainable, and high\-quality experiences for our guests\. The redesigned course will create new opportunities for challenge, collaboration, learning, and outdoor recreation, while supporting group programming and youth experiences for years to come\.

Learn more about the new ropes course [here](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/news/a-new-ropes-course-is-coming-to-loon-lake)\.

## Partner With Us

**Help Bring Outdoor Adventure to Life at Loon Lake**

With the new ropes course on the horizon, we are looking for a qualified programming partner to operate the ropes course and deliver recreational programming at Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre\.

This opportunity is intended for an experienced organization with the expertise to safely operate a ropes course and deliver high\-quality outdoor recreation and team\-building experiences for Loon Lake guests\.

The successful partner will have the opportunity to work with Loon Lake to deliver programming across the site, including the new high and low ropes course and a range of other outdoor recreation activities\.

We welcome proposals from qualified organizations interested in becoming a long\-term programming partner at Loon Lake\.

View the [Request for Proposal](https://mkrf-forestry.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/UBC-Loon-Lake-Recreation-Request-For-Proposal-Aug-2026.pdf)
