Is your teen looking for an unforgettable summer adventure?

We’ve got two exciting summer camps just for teens ages 13–16:

🦉 Ecology Camp \| July 13–17  
Explore the forest, learn about local ecosystems, conservation and environmental stewardship, practice mindfulness, and learn about cooking with wild ingredients\.

🔥 Survival Skills Camp \| August 4–7  
Build confidence outdoors while learning practical wilderness survival skills, trip planning and preparedness, basic first aid, navigation, and more\.

These camps are perfect for teens who love nature, adventure, and spending time outside\! Spots are limited so register early to save your place\.

**Camp Information:**  
**Date:** Ecology Camp July 13\-17 \| Survival Camp August 4\-7  
**Time:** 8:30am\-3pm daily  
**Ages:** 13\-16  
**Cost:** Ecology Camp $395 \+gst \| Survival Camp $316 \+gst
