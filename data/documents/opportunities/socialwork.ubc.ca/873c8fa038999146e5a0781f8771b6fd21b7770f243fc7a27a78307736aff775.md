We are happy to announce that **Professor Stephanie Wahab**, from Portland University School of Social Work, has been selected as our **90th Anniversary fellow**\. She has been invited to give a Keynote Lecture on “Teaching Social Justice in Dangerous Times: Practices of Hope” on **March 9th, 2020 @ 5:30 – 7:30 pm** at the Place of Many Trees \([map](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/events/event/90th-anniversary-fellow-keynote-lecture-with-prof-stephanie-wahab/)\)\.

Registration for the Keynote Lecture has been closed\.

Fell free to download the [poster](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2019/11/Poster-for-March-9th-Keynote-Lecture.pdf)\.
