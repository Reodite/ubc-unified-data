Have you ever thought about doing a PhD? Join this informal, virtual information session to learn about the doctoral program in Social Work at UBC\. We’ll talk about funding, admission process, coursework and opportunities\! And even more generally, learn about the doors that a PhD might open for you and the career possibilities that can result\! Informal, friendly and useful, even if it isn’t part of an immediate plan\!

**Tuesday, October 17, 2023**  
**11:30 am\-1:00 pm Vancouver time**

Please register below and an email will be sent to you with Zoom information\.
