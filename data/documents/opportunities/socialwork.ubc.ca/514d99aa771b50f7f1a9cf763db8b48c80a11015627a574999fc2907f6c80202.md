The School\-wide orientation is scheduled for Tuesday, September 13, from 9:30am to 1:45pm\.

Location: Ponderosa Ballroom \(across the street from the Jack Bell Building\)

-   Meet faculty, staff and other students
-   Hear from the winners of the 2022 Inspiring Social Worker of the Year Award at the Paul Cheng Memorial Lecture
-   Enjoy a bagged lunch and meet with others in breakout rooms\*
-   Ask any questions you may have about what to expect in the term

\*Breakfast will not be provided so please bring your own food and beverages\. An email will be sent in mid\-August asking for any dietary restrictions for the bagged lunches \(sandwich \+ fruit\)\. If you wish you may bring additional food or beverages for lunch\.
