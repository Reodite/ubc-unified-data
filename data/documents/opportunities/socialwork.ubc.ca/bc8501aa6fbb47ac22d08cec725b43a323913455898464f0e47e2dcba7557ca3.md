The School of Social Work will be having a School Orientation for all students on Tuesday, September 4, 2018\. **All students are expected to attend**\.

Checking in begins at 9:30 am and the orientation will begin at 10:00 am\. We are excited to welcome the 2018 Inspiring Social Worker Award recipient, who will share her insights and experiences at the 2018 Paul Cheng Memorial Lecture\. After 1:30 pm, the third\-year BSW students and MSW students will split into separate program\-specific orientations, lasting until about 4:00 pm\. Fourth\-year BSW students will finish their orientation at 1:30 pm\.

Lunch will be provided\. To help us reduce waste at the event, please bring your own water bottle and/or beverage\.

The orientation will take place in the Jack Poole Hall \(2nd floor\), at the Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre on the UBC Vancouver campus\. [Click here for information on accessibility](https://alumnicentre.ubc.ca/visit-us/)\.

The Orientation Program will be available soon with more information\. Please check back here for updates\.

See you in September\!
