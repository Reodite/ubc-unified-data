The School of Social Work will be having a School Orientation for all students on Tuesday, September 7, 2021\. **It is recommended that all students attend, although it is not mandatory\.**

The orientation will begin at 10:00 am\.

---

Zoom details will be emailed directly to students\.

---

Following the School orientation and break, BSW students will re\-assemble online for the BSW orientation from 1\-3:30pm\.

MSW will have their afternoon session online from 1:30\-3pm\.

PhD students will meet in Room 200 in the Jack Bell Building for their session, 1:30\-3:30pm\.

Zoom details for individual program orientations will be sent by the Program Chairs\.
