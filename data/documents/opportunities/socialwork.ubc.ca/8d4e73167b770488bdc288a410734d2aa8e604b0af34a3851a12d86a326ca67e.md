Join us for an afternoon to learn about

-   Registration Process with the BC College of Social Workers
-   The BC Association of Social Workers and how it can be a resource for you as a professional
-   Tips for writing the RSW Licensure Exam \(for both BSW and MSW students\)
-   Practice Questions from the exam\!

**Where:** Online – click [here](https://ca.bbcollab.com/guest/9a3dddc1f2794748b0a26892a264e36f) to join

**When:** Friday, April 17, 2020  1\-4pm

\(We recommend joining before the start time so we can assist with any technical issues\.\)

Feel free to download the [poster\.](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2020/03/Exam-prep-Workshop-1.pdf)

If you have questions, please contact Antoine Coulombe, Christiana Bratiotis or Kelly Allison\.
