Join us on November 18 for *An Evening with a Social Worker,* featuring Allison Winick, a recent MSW graduate from UBC\.

ImageAllison will be sharing insights from her international practicum in Aotearoa \(New Zealand\) within the field of Perinatal Health\. She spent the summer 2024 semester in Taranaki, where she learned from the Perinatal Mental Health and Antenatal/Pregnancy Choice Counselling team within Te Whatu Ora \(New Zealand Health Authority\)\.
