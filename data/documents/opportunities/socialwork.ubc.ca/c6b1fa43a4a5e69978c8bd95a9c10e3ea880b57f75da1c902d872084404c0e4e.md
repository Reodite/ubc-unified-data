## *Coming together for learning and creating change*

Join us for our Orange Shirt Day Initiative\.

A Zoom link will be emailed just prior to the event\.

---

ImageThe ongoing destructive impact of the student\-to\-student abuse that occurred within the Indian Residential School System is a devastating legacy of Canada’s attempt at the cultural genocide of Indigenous peoples\. Little is known about what has been described as a living monster that continues to negatively impact Indigenous peoples and communities\. Using Indigenous research methodologies in conjunction with a research\-based theatre approach, an interdisciplinary team of Indigenous and non\-Indigenous scholars, students and community collaborators worked with oral testimonies from residential school survivors to identify stories and themes of peer\-to\-peer violence\. The team then co\-created a play, ***Dark Secrets***, presented through this podcast, which offers insight into the phenomenon\.

A 30\-minute audio podcast presentation will be followed by a panel presentation and interactive discussion\.

We invite you to wear an orange shirt to honour survivors and those who did not make it home\.
