World AIDS Day is an international day dedicated to raising awareness about HIV/AIDS and demonstrating international solidarity\. To commemorate this important day, we are organizing an event where we will share our research findings, including a photovoice exhibition of research projects conducted on immigrants and refugees living with HIV\.

The event will take place on November 29, 2024, from 10:00 am to 1:00 pm at the [School of Population and Public Health, Room B151](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/classrooms/spph-b151)\.

This event is organized in collaboration with the UBC School of Social Work, UFV School of Social Work, and the UBC Centre for Migration Studies\.

Please RSVP your attendance [here](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0MxHPNkCd7vWvz0)\.
