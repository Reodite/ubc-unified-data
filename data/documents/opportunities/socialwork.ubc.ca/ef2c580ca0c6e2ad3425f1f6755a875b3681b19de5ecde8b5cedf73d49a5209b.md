Dear SOWK community,

You are warmly invited to participate in the BSW Strategic Renewal Town Hall\. This session is part of the Bachelor of Social Work’s strategic renewal process and offers an opportunity to come together as a learning community to reflect on the program, share perspectives, and help identify priorities for the future of the BSW\.

**Why participate?**  
Your experiences, ideas, and priorities will directly shape the next phase of the BSW strategic renewal\. Input gathered during the town hall will help to:

-   Inform concrete strategic recommendations for the program
-   Guide ongoing discussions and decision\-making within the School of Social Work
-   Influence future curricular and strategic changes impacting BSW students

**Discussion Themes**  
Participants will be invited to engage with four interconnected themes:

-   Practice Readiness &amp; Curriculum Balance
-   Program Structure, Workload, and Student Experience
-   Equity, Representation, and Belonging in the BSW
-   Program Identity &amp; Future Directions

We hope you will join us for this important conversation and contribute to shaping the future of the BSW program\.
