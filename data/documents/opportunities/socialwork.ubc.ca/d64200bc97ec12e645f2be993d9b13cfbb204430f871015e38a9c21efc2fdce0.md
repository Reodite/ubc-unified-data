### Help end violence against women and children

On May 11, 2023, join Indigenous and non\-Indigenous Canadians from across Canada to support reconciliation and help end violence against all women and children\.

Supporters are encouraged to wear and share the moose hide pins on the day \(and every day\!\) and organize events with their organizations, schools or communities\.

Moose hide pins are available in the Jack Bell Building on the first floor, near the Statement of Accountability and Commitment plaque\.
