The final monthly virtual workshop of the UBC School of Social Work and the Association of Neighbourhood Houses BC partnership will be held Thursday, May 27, 2021 from 1:30\-3 pm\. We invite you to attend our final session “Safeguarding Asian children and families in the midst of Anti\-Asian racism” hosted by Barbara Lee\.

A Zoom link has been sent to students and faculty\. Please email [sowk\.program@ubc\.ca](mailto:sowk.program@ubc.ca) if you would like the link to be emailed to you\.
