Join us in the Intergenerational March in honour of Orange Shirt Day on **Monday, September 30, 2024**\.

**Event Details:**

-   **Date:** Monday September 30th
-   **Meeting Time:** 11:30 AM
-   **Location:** Courtyard area beside School of Social Work, PondA, building
-   **Information:** [2024 Intergenerational March to Commemorate Orange Shirt Day](https://apsc.ubc.ca/event/2024/2024-intergenerational-march-to-commemorate-orange-shirt-day)

Image**Orange Shirt Day** is a significant occasion that allows us to come together to remember and acknowledge the experiences of Indigenous survivors of residential schools, their families, and communities\. By participating in this march, we can demonstrate our commitment to reconciliation, healing, and education\.

For those joining the Intergenerational March, we will gather beside the School of Social Work, PondA Building at 11:30 AM\. At 11:40am, we will walk together to the Indian Residential School History and Dialogue Centre amphitheatre\. Come dressed in an orange shirt or any orange attire to show your solidarity\. Please extend this Invitation your friends, family, and colleagues to join us for this meaningful event\.

We look forward to seeing you at the Intergenerational March on September 30th as we unite in the spirit of reconciliation and hope\.
