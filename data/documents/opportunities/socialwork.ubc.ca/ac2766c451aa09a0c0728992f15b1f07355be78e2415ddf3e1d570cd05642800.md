Join us for a special lecture with **Margaret F\. \(Meg\) Gibson**, **Julia Bullard** and **Hannah Kia**, who will explore the shifting concepts of social justice and its impact on academia and social movements\. This panel will delve into the complexities of justice and equity, reflecting on how different generations of scholars and activists have shaped, challenged and redefined these terms\. The discussion will focus on how we navigate historical contexts, institutional influences and social change efforts while resisting regression\.

**Date**: Tuesday, January 21  
**Time**: 5:00–6:30 PM  
**Location**: Coach House, Green College, UBC &amp; livestreamed

For more information, visit the Green College [website](https://greencollege.ubc.ca/civicrm/event/info%3Fid%3D1777%26reset%3D1)\.
