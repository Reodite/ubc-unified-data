The UBC School of Social Work recognized May 5, 2020 as one of several days throughout the year to remember missing and murdered Indigenous women and girls so they will not be forgotten and to strengthen the call for concrete actions to end violence against Indigenous women and girls\.

Download the poster [here\.](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2020/05/May5RedDress.pdf)

For more information visit the [National Inquiry website](http://www.mmiwg-ffada.ca/)\.
