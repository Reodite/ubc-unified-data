## In recognising that youth in \(or from\) care, whether foster care, a group home, or a shelter, face extra obstacles in becoming involved in science, the Fostering Science Mentorship Program was born out of a desire to support these students\.  It is designed and run by a group of scientists who are passionate about science and youth, in partnership with the **Federation of BC Youth in Care Networks** and the **Science Fair Foundation BC**\.

# Our Goals

-   To increase participation of youth in or from care at science fairs
-   To work with youth in or from care to inspire an interest in science and engineering
-   To provide youth in or from care with the opportunity to develop positive relationships with science mentors in the community
-   To bring interactive, hands\-on science/ engineering to youth in or from care
