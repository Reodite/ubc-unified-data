#### To Attend the Conference

Videorecordings of our sessions are now available to stream, embedded within our program\! Please note that not all presentations were recorded, per the preferences of speakers\. If you were a presenter and wish to have anything altered or removed, please contact the organizers\. We anticipate that this site should be available through 2022\.

#### Public Program Information

For daily programs with abstracts, please select the type of session in the menu to the left\. Additionally, all sessions in the program overview below are hyperlinked to their respective session details\. 

Times in the conference program are given according to the time zones of the three host institutions:

-   University of British Columbia, Vancouver, Canada: **Pacific Daylight Time \(PDT\)**
-   Queen’s University Belfast, UK: **British Summer Time \(BST\)**
-   University of Allahabad, India: **India Standard Time \(IST\)**

We recognize that one of the best parts of face\-to\-face conferences is the serendipitous conversation with friends, colleagues and absolute strangers who inspire us, and with this in mind, we have scheduled two “[Social Hours](https://unpredictable-conf-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/special-events/#social-hours)” during the conference at times that span as many parts of the globe as possible\.
