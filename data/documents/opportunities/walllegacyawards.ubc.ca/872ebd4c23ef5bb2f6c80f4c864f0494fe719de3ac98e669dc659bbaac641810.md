**The 2026 application cycle has closed\. The below is listed for your information only\. The next application cycle will open in January 2027\.**  

-   To apply for a Wall Research Award, **starting January 27, 2026**, applicants must log into [UBC Research \+ Innovation Apply](https://apply.research.ubc.ca/) using their campus wide login \(CWL\), and follow the instructions provided\.  
    
-   A Notice of Intent \(NOI\) must be submitted by **February 24, 2026** to progress to the full application\. An NOI is required to provide time for eligibility review and enable the recruitment of reviewers\. It is not adjudicated\.  
    
-   Once the NOI is submitted, the full application will become available, and is **due 5:00 pm PT on May 5, 2026**\.
-   An information session is being held on February 10 at 10 am\-11:30 am\.
