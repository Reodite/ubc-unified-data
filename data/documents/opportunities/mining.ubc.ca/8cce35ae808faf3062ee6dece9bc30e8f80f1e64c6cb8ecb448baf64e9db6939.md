## About

**APSC First Year Program Fair — March 6, 2026 \(Kaiser Atrium\)**  
Come by and chat with students and faculty in engineering programs you are interested in\! Explore different disciplines, ask questions, and start discovering which engineering path feels right for you\.

**While you're here, don’t miss the Mining Engineering Career Fair happening just across the way in the Earth Sciences Building Atrium\!**  
Location: [Earth Sciences Building \(Google Maps\)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/d44RYEuGCKhPXvkb9)

Drop in to:

-   Meet industry professionals from leading mining companies
-   Chat with current Mining Engineering students \(2nd, 3rd, and 4th years\) about courses, co‑ops, and what the program is really like
-   Explore real career paths in mining — from sustainability to technology, operations, design, and more
-   Enjoy free pizza 🍕
-   Enter our raffle for amazing prizes\! \(Previous years have included high‑value swag — even a bar of silver\!\)

If you're curious about the Mining program, want to make industry connections early, or just want some pizza while learning something new, come by and say hello\!
