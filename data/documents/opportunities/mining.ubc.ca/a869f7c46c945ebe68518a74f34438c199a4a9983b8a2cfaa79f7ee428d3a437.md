## About

Image

[2027 UBC Mining Alumni Dinner \- Save the date flyer](https://mining.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/original_image/public/2026-05/eng-mining-alumni-dinner-2026-save-the-date-website.jpg.webp?itok=EHNab5j8)

The UBC Mining Engineering Alumni Dinner is a long‑standing tradition, and in January 2027 we are proud to mark a special milestone: **our 30th Annual Dinner\.**  
 

This annual event brings together alumni, students, faculty, and industry partners for an evening of connection, reflection, and celebration\. It is an opportunity to reconnect with colleagues, share experiences, and celebrate the continued impact and strength of the UBC Mining community — past, present, and future\.

  
Save the Date: Saturday, January 23, 2027  
Location: Downtown Vancouver \(full details to follow\)

  
Full event details and ticket sales will be released closer to the date\. We encourage you to register your interest to be notified when tickets become available\.
