Are you working on an [OER Fund](https://oerfund.open.ubc.ca/) project or developing and incorporating open educational resources \(OER\) into your teaching and learning? Do you have specific questions on how to format your OER to be accessible for all learners? Drop\-in to an online OER Accessibility clinic to chat with specialists about how to design or adapt OER so they are accessible and open to all\. Participants should come prepared with a list of questions specific to their projects\.

The OER Accessibility Clinics will be held every other Fridray from 11am and 12pm \(click link for more info and to register\):

-   May 26
-   [June 9](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/oer-accessibility-clinic-drop-in-online-june-9-2023/)
-   [June 23](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/oer-accessibility-clinic-drop-in-online-june-23-2023/)
-   [July 7](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/oer-accessibility-clinic-drop-in-online-july-7-2023/)
-   [July 21](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/oer-accessibility-clinic-drop-in-online-july-21-2023/)
-   [August 4](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/oer-accessibility-clinic-drop-in-online-august-4-2023/)
-   [August 18](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/oer-accessibility-clinic-drop-in-online-august-18-2023/)
