    **Project Summary**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    We propose to host an OER awareness and capacity\-building event focused on novel, open\-source systems for equation\-based model building and real\-time, automated instructional feedback in environmental sciences and engineering education\. The goal of these systems is to make scientific computing, and understanding of the complex dynamics of environmental systems more accessible to learners of diverse disciplinary backgrounds\.  
      
    The event will involve a demonstration and then interactive tutorial and brainstorming session\. The invited speaker will walk\-through the synthesis of three technologies: 1\) the open\-source PrairieLearn online learning and assessment system \(https://us\.prairielearn\.com/\) which can host online computational environments; 2\) reactive computational notebooks \(https://plutojl\.org/\) which provide real\-time feedback for student coding exercises; and 3\) equation\-based geoscientific model development \(https://earthsci\.dev/dev/\), which allows dynamical models \(even complex, 3D ones\) to be specified as a system of equations and then automatically compiled to a runnable simulation\. With resources from the OER fund, we will convene a community of practice around the creation of OER lessons to expand the use of these novel technologies at UBC
