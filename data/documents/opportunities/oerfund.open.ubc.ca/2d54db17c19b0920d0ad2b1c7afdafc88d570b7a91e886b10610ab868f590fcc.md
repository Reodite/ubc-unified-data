    The Teaching &amp; Learning Champions Reception is an event hosted by the AMS in collaboration with CTLT and UBC Library to recognize the individuals advancing Open Educational Resources \(OER\) and Universal Design for Learning \(UDL\) at UBC\. These efforts are not only about accessibility but also about making education more affordable and inclusive for all students\. OERs and UDL are advanced through the collective efforts of staff, administration and faculty who create, support, and implement the resources for the affordability and accessibility of students’ futures at UBC\. UBC has been a leader in Canada’s OER movement, prioritizing accessibility and affordability\. The AMS’s textbook broke campaign in 2017 helped advance this effort, resulting in OER Excellence and Impact Awards and funding opportunities for faculty to adopt open materials\. These initiatives have made course resources more affordable and ensured students have the materials they need to succeed\.  
      
    Research shows that students in OER\-supported courses perform on par or higher than those using traditional textbooks, proving OER’s ability to reduce financial burdens while supporting academic achievement\. We are continuing the addition of the UDL Champions Award in this year’s event\. When integrated with OER, UDL enables faculty to design courses that accommodate varied learning styles, linguistic backgrounds, and accessibility requirements, ensuring that open materials are affordable and inclusive to all students\. At UBC, initiatives such as the UDL Fellows Program and the Universal Design for Learning Hub have strengthened this connection, reinforcing the role of OER in advancing equitable learning opportunities\. More importantly, it will reinforce the importance of OER and UDL in shaping the future of education at UBC, ensuring that students and faculty alike benefit from a more accessible, inclusive, and equitable learning environment\. By investing in the Teaching &amp; Learning Champions Reception, the OER Rapid Innovation Fund will directly contribute to the recognition and expansion of open education and inclusive learning practices at UBC, furthering the university’s mission to foster student success and academic excellence\.
