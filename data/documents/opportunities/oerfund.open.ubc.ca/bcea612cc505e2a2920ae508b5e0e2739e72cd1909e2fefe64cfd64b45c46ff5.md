  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The aim of this project is to evaluate existing OER content in the Faculty of Forestry\. Through several workshops, we will identify opportunities for the development of new OER content, raise awareness of the value of OER among faculty members, and establish a shared tool with metrics that can be used for reporting OER usage in the faculty\.  
      
    I will host two meetings during 2025 Winter Term 2 that target faculty who are currently teaching with OER or are interested in teaching with OER\. These meetings will be scheduled at different times and days of the week to accommodate various teaching schedules\. During these meetings, I will prompt faculty to discuss any OER they are currently using to help us create an inventory\. Then, I will shift to discuss strategies for metrics that can be used to evaluate the OER and what kind of tools, features, and metrics would help to support faculty in this regard\. I will take notes of these discussions with the aid of a GAA and then use this information to create an interactive tool for reporting on OER content used in the faculty\.  
      
    I envision that the tool will serve both to help faculty report about OER usage and also at least partially implement the BCCampus Faculty Guide for Evaluating Open Education Resources in a more interactive way\.
