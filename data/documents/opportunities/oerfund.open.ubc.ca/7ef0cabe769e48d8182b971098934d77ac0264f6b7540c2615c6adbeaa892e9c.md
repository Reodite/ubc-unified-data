    **Project Summary**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    An antiracist framework for French as a second or additional language is a professional learning book created to educate current and future French teachers on antiracist pedagogy and how to implement it into French language programs in Canada\. Our framework synthesizes important contextual knowledge of teaching French, theories of second language learning, and practical examples of how to engage in critically\-oriented antiracist teaching practices\.  
      
    The framework will be available in English and French and published via UBC Pressbooks in an online format with option of a downloadable PDF or eBook in order to make the resource accessible\. Our book, which is under development, is co\-authored by nine French educators from British Columbia, Ontario, Manitoba, and Quebec\. Integrating an antiracist approach to teaching French in Canada as one way to encourage equitable language learning that supports students’ many identities and languages and promotes multilingualism in Canada\. The framework responds to UBC’s and the Faculty of Education’s strategic priorities on advancing equity and antiracism through teaching\.
