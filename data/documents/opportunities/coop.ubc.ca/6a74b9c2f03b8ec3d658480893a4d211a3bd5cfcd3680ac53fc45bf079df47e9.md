UBC's Co\-op Programs grew to a record total of 8,185 work terms in 2024/25 — an increase of 6\.6% over last year\.

2024/25 Work Terms by Program

-   Applied Science: 3,182
-   Arts: 614
-   Forestry: 118
-   Sauder: 535
-   Science: 3,487
-   UBCO Interdisciplinary\*: 249

\[subscript: \* Includes Arts, Fine Arts, Health &amp; Exercise Sciences, Management, Media Studies, Science, and Sustainability\]

#### **Expanding Global Co\-op Opportunities**

International co\-op opportunities continue to flourish, taking students to exciting corners of the world for personal and professional growth\.

In 2024/25, we reached a new high of 542 international work terms, a considerable 35% increase from the previous year\.

Students worked in 36 different countries, including Belgium, Brazil, Cambodia, Hungary, Egypt, India, Kenya, Kazakhstan, Philippines, Rwanda, and Saudi Arabia\.
