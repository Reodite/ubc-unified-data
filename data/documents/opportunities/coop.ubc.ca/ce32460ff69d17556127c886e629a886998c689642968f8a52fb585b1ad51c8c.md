#### Get answers to your Frequently Asked Questions here\.

##### Start by selecting whether you're an employer or student\.

-   [Employer](https://coop.ubc.ca/about-us/faqs?field_faq_type_value=Employer%20Section)
-   [Student](https://coop.ubc.ca/about-us/faqs?field_faq_type_value=Student%20Section)

-   ### How do I apply to join co\-op?
    
    Intake for UBC's co\-op programs ranges from first to third year, and deadlines and processes vary by discipline\. Find the [co\-op program](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/139) relevant to your discipline to learn when and how to apply\.
    
-   ### Can I do a co\-op term abroad?
    
    Yes, co\-op work experience can be anywhere in the world\. 
    
    UBC Co\-op offers exciting international work opportunities to students\. Completing a co\-op work term abroad can be a great opportunity to develop life skills, build self\-confidence, and learn about new cultures\. Both domestic and international students are eligible to apply for roles abroad\.
    
-   ### What are the costs of being in co\-op?
    
    Once you’re admitted into co\-op, you will pay a co\-op administration and workshop fee that supports specialized training and pre\-employment conferences\. This training will help prepare you for applying to jobs and increase your success in finding work, both in your co\-op program and after graduation\.  
       
    For every work term you secure\*, you will be registered in a co\-op course to maintain your full\-time registration status with the university\. You will pay a course fee for each work term, which contributes to the services offered by your co\-op office, such as job sourcing, application preparation, and professional development support\.   
       
    For the most up\-to\-date fee information, see the [UBC Calendar](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/?tree=14,296,0,0)\.
    
    \[\* The fee structure is the same in all co\-op programs, except in Arts Co\-op, where students are also registered in a search term course and the course fee is split between the search and work term\.\]
    
-   ### Does co\-op count for credit? Am I graded on a work term?
    
    Students on a co\-op work term are registered in a co\-op course and they maintain full\-time student status\. However, co\-op courses are non\-academic and the credits do not count towards degree requirements\. A co\-op work term does not replace, or add academic course credits for that term\.
    
    Co\-op courses are pass/fail only\. You must complete all work\-term deliverables on time in order to pass the course\. This includes completing a site visit with your Co\-op Coordinator, submitting work term assignments, and having your supervisor complete an employer evaluation\.
    
-   ### What is a co\-op designation?
    
    When you complete the required work terms for your co\-op program, you will have a co\-op designation noted on your degree parchment\. This designation signals to employers the professionalism and relevant work experience that you’ve gained by participating in a formal program for work\-integrated learning\.
    
-   ### What kinds of services and support can I get from my program’s co\-op office?
    
    In addition to exclusive access to your program’s job portal and extensive pre\-employment training, the staff at your co\-op office provide personalized guidance, including application reviews, one\-on\-one interview preparation, and ongoing support during your work terms\.
    
-   ### As an international student, how do I obtain a work permit?
    
    As of April 1, 2026, a Co\-op work permit is no longer required to participate in a Co\-op, internship, or practicum that is part of your program of study\. 
    
    If work is required to complete your academic program, such as a Co\-op, a practicum, or an internship, you will now be able to use your [on\-campus](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/campus-work/) or [off\-campus](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/off-campus-work/) work eligibility\. 
    
-   ### Will I be paid and if so, how much can I expect to make?
    
    Yes, co\-op work terms are paid\. Compensation varies greatly between different disciplines and industries\. Please consult your [program’s website](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/139) for details regarding average salaries\.
    
-   ### Am I eligible for co\-op?
    
    Students are generally required to have full\-time registration status and meet a minimum cumulative average, however, specific eligibility requirements vary by program\. 
    
    International students are eligible to participate in co\-op at UBC\. Graduate level students in certain departments may participate in co\-op program as well\. You can find links to each program [here](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/139) to learn about detailed eligibility requirements\.
    
    Intake for UBC's co\-op programs ranges from first to third year, and generally takes place in late September\. Find the [co\-op program](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/139) relevant to your discipline to learn when and how to apply\.
    
-   ### Can I participate in Go Global and co\-op?
    
    Yes\. Keep in mind that applying for positions and securing a job while abroad will require greater diligence\. We recommend connecting with your program’s co\-op office to discuss how to best support your plans\.
    
-   ### How does co\-op affect my graduation date?
    
    In co\-op, students alternate between work and study terms, so they typically complete their degree in 4\.5 to 5 years\. While it may take longer to graduate, students get a head start on their career journeys, with industry connections, job search skills, and valuable, paid work experience\.
    
-   ### Are co\-op students guaranteed jobs?
    
    You’re not guaranteed a co\-op position, as securing a job is a competitive process\. 
    
    We provide students with pre\-employment training, personalized coaching and exclusive access to an online co\-op job board, but your success depends greatly on the effort you put into the job search and application process\. 
    
-   ### How do I find a co\-op position?
    
    You will be able to apply for exclusive co\-op positions through your program’s job portal, which includes challenging, career\-related positions with local, national and international employers\.   
           
    In addition to this, we encourage you to expand your efforts by developing employment leads and exploring opportunities through your personal connections and at networking events\.   
       
    If you secure a position outside the co\-op job portal, it will need meet co\-op requirements and be approved by your Co\-op Coordinator\.
    
-   ### What kinds of employers are available?
    
    Hundreds of employers have hired UBC Co\-op students to work in a range of jobs in the public, private and non\-profit sectors, from small to large organizations, and from a wide variety of industries\.
    
    Please consult your [program’s website](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/139) for information about employers applicable to your discipline\.
    
-   ### How many work terms do I need to complete? Is there a standard schedule?
    
    The number of work terms as well as the work/study vary between different disciplines and faculties\. Please consult the [co\-op program](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/139) relevant to your discipline for more detailed information\.
    
-   ### What is co\-op?
    
    Co\-op \(more formally known as Co\-operative Education\) allows students to gain professional workplace experience, by alternating between study terms and paid, full\-time work terms\.   
          
    Co\-op students graduate with experience in areas relevant to their studies and interests\. Throughout their time in co\-op, students are provided with career resources, personalized guidance and access to a diverse career network\. As a UBC Co\-op graduate, you will be well prepared, academically and professionally, for your future career\.
    
-   ### What can I get out of being in co\-op?
    
    As a co\-op student, you’d be able to:
    
    -   Gain insight into career opportunities in your chosen field through a variety of work experiences
    -   Build a professional network of valuable employment contacts
    -   Finance your degree with relevant, paid work
    -   Integrate classroom learning with industry work experience
    -   Access to workshops for career skills training, one\-on\-one coaching and employer events specifically for co\-op students\.
    
    Read more [here](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/78)\.
