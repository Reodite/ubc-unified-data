UBC Co\-op students from the Arts, Humanities, and Social Sciences are educated as society’s future leaders, who possess highly sought\-after transferable skills necessary in today’s global economy\. These students offer strengths in critical thinking, research, writing, project management, technical skills, and creative problem solving\.

Students in the undergraduate program come from all disciplines in the Social Sciences, Humanities, and Creative and Performing Arts, including: Anthropology, Economics, English, Geography, History, International Relations, Media Studies, Political Science, Psychology, and Sociology\.

Our program also features graduate level students with professional expertise from the UBC iSchool, Master of Public Policy &amp; Global Affairs, as well as select Master and PhD programs

##### Students: Apply to Co\-op

To learn more about how and when to apply to co\-op in the Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences, visit the co\-op program pages for [UBC Vancouver](https://artscoop.ubc.ca/prospective-students/) or [UBC Okanagan](https://coop.ok.ubc.ca/current-ubc-students/)\.
