-   ### What disciplines can I hire from?
    
    Students are available from across almost every discipline:
    
    -   Applied Science
    -   Arts, Humanities &amp; Social Sciences
    -   Business &amp; Management
    -   Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship
    -   Health &amp; Exercise Sciences
    -   Land &amp; Food Systems
    -   Science
    
    Learn more about each in our [Program Spotlight](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/88)\.  
     
    
-   ### Is my position eligible for co\-op?
    
    To qualify as a co\-op work term, the position you’re hiring for should be:
    
    -   At least **420 hours** per 4\-month work term \(full\-time work\)
    -   **Paid** at an appropriate salary for the industry and level of responsibility, as well as meeting regional or provincial/federal employment laws
    -   **Supervised** by a professional who offers mentorship and feedback throughout the work term 
    -   **Challenging**, providing the student with work experience relevant to their academic pursuits and/or career development
    
    If you have any questions, please [contact us](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/139) for clarification\.  
     
    
-   ### Is there a deadline for hiring a co\-op student?
    
    We begin posting jobs 4 months in advance of the work term start dates of January, May and September\. The posting period continues throughout the 4 months leading up to the work term, and it’s up to you to set your application deadlines\. 
    
    Job offers are made to students on a first\-come, first\-served basis, so we recommend beginning the recruitment process early during the co\-op posting period\. 
    
-   ### Is there a cost to access co\-op student hiring?
    
    Co\-op students need to be paid a regular wage or salary, but there is no fee to post jobs with our programs, shortlist candidates for interview, or to hire\.
    
-   ### How are co\-op students paid?
    
    Employers set the wages, which should be competitive for the work the student is doing, reflective of industry standards, and in line with regional, provincial, or federal employment standards\.
    
    Some employers offer additional benefits, such as relocation or tuition incentives in order to attract applicants\.
    
    We can advise you on typical salary ranges for specific jobs and levels of experience\. [Contact us](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/139) to learn more\.
    
-   ### What are my responsibilities as a co\-op student’s supervisor?
    
    Supervisors often take on the role of mentor for their co\-op students, supporting them throughout the work term by offering advice and providing feedback\. 
    
    There are some additional responsibilities for co\-op:
    
    **Beginning of the work term**  
    The student will set learning objectives, which we encourage the supervisor to establish together in order to set realistic and challenging goals\.
    
    **Midway through the term**  
    A Co\-op Coordinator will schedule a site visit with the student and supervisor in order to discuss the student’s learning, progress, and performance thus far\.
    
    **At the end of the term**  
    The supervisor will complete an evaluation form that assesses the student's performance and progress throughout the term\. There may be other requirements for evaluating co\-op work term assignments or reports, which vary by co\-op program\.  
    
-   ### What kinds of skills do UBC Co\-op students have?
    
    Students come from a range of disciplines and are skilled in:
    
    -   Business operations
    -   Data analytics &amp; visualization
    -   Health &amp; wellness
    -   Lab, fieldwork &amp; safety
    -   Marketing &amp; outreach
    -   Research &amp; development
    -   Research, analysis &amp; policy
    -   Sustainability &amp; resource management
    -   Web &amp; software development
    
    For more details on each set of skills and to post a job, [click here](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/117)\.
    
    Students also have skills that would be valuable to employers in any sector, including:
    
    -   Team work
    -   Written &amp; verbal communications
    -   Professionalism
    -   Digital literacy
    
    Developed through academic studies, previous work or volunteer experiences, and co\-op pre\-employment training, these skills are common to the pool of UBC Co\-op students across our programs\.  
     
    
-   ### When should I post my co\-op job?
    
    Students are available starting in January, May, and September each year\. We begin postings jobs up to 4 months before the start date\. Start the recruitment process early to make sure you get access to the biggest pool of available candidates\.
    
    | For Start Date In | Post Jobs During |
    | --- | --- |
    | January | September\-December |
    | May | January\-April |
    | September | May\-August |
    
-   ### Why should I hire UBC Co\-op students?
    
    We've boiled it down to these top 5 reasons:
    
    **1\) Access Pre\-Screened Candidates**  
    We pre\-select students so you can feel confident you’re getting the best candidate for the job\. We also prepare our students for workplace success with pre\-employment training\.
    
    **2\) Get Work Done**  
    Co\-op students offer a low\-risk, cost\-effective way to complete special projects and support peak work periods\. 
    
    **3\) Recruit with Ease**  
    We work with you on your hiring timelines, not ours\. Our process is flexible, allowing you to set your own deadlines for applications and offers\. 
    
    **4\) Feed Your Talent Pipeline**  
    Working with co\-op students on a short\-term basis is a low\-risk way to find star employees to hire after graduation\. You can assess students’ skill sets and test for cultural fit throughout the co\-op term; it’s like an extended interview\.
    
    **5\) Tap Into Our Services &amp; Support**  
    We support your recruitment process by promoting your jobs, coordinating interview schedules, and providing on\-campus interview rooms if needed – all at no cost\. Once students are on their work terms, we also support their success in the workplace through guidance and midterm site visits\.
    
-   ### Do I have to help international students with their Canadian work permits?
    
    As of April 1, 2026, a co\-op work permit is no longer required for students to participate in a co\-op, internship, or practicum that is part of the student's program of study\. See [Immigration, Refugees, and Citizenship Canada announcement](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/notices/simplifying-co-op-work-permit-requirement-post-secondary-international-students.html) for details\.
