#### Skills for Any Employer

Students also have skills that would be valuable to employers in any sector, including

-   Team work
-   Written &amp; verbal communications
-   Critical thinking
-   Problem solving
-   Digital literacy
-   Professionalism

Developed through academic studies, previous work or volunteer experiences, and co\-op pre\-employment training, these skills are common to the pool of UBC Co\-op students across our programs\.
