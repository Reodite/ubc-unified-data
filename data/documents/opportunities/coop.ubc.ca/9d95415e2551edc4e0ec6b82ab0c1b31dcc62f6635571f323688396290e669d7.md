The Science Co\-op Program provides undergraduate students in the Faculty of Science with an opportunity to integrate their academic education with experiential learning through relevant, monitored, full\-time and paid work opportunities with hiring employers\.

The Co\-op option is available to all students in the Faculty of Science\. In addition, Science Co\-op facilitates co\-op programs for students in Engineering Physics, the Faculty of Land and Food Systems and Master of Statistics\.

Students undergo a rigorous screening process in order to be admitted to Science Co\-op\. Our co\-op coordinators work closely with the students and facilitate career preparation workshops\. Students who successfully complete the program receive a Co\-op designation on their degree parchment upon graduation\.

With a diversified set of programs ranging from Atmospheric Sciences to Zoology, a highly motivated and qualified group of students and year\-round student availability for 4, 8, 12 or 16 month periods, Science Co\-op meets employment needs of its partner employers all across the globe\. 

##### Students: Apply to Co\-op

To learn more about how and when to apply to co\-op in Science, visit the co\-op program pages for [UBC Vancouver](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective) or [UBC Okanagan](https://coop.ok.ubc.ca/current-ubc-students/)\.
