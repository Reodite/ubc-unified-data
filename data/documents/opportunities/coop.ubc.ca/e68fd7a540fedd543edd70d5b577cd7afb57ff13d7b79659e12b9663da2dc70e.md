For over 40 years, UBC’s co\-op programs have provided tens of thousands of students with transformational, educationally enriched, experiential learning opportunities with diverse community partners across Canada and around the world\. 

Together, we represent the largest co\-op program in Western Canada, with students from a wide range of disciplines in the Vancouver and Okanagan campuses:  

-   [Applied Science](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/90)
-   [Arts, Humanities &amp; Social Sciences](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/92)
-   [Business &amp; Management](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/94)
-   [Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/93)
-   [Health &amp; Exercise Sciences](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/89)
-   [Land &amp; Food Systems](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/91)
-   [Science](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/114)
