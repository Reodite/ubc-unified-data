##### Because you’re busy, we’ll keep this simple\.

**The best is the only thing you’ll get\.**     
We pre\-select students so you can feel confident you’re getting the best candidate for the job\.

**The future of your organization is our business\.**     
Consistently ranked among the top public universities in the world, UBC immerses students in cutting\-edge research\.

**Get the edge you’re looking for\.**     
Our students are motivated to help take your organization to the next level\. Their enthusiasm and work ethic set them apart from other candidates\.

**The best choice is easy to make\.**     
The opportunity you’re providing our students is invaluable\. That’s why we’ll provide you with support for recruiting, interviewing and hiring\.

**Your chance to shape the future\.**     
Not only will you be hiring the best for your organization, you’ll also be shaping the leaders of tomorrow in your field\.
