[International Students](https://coop.ubc.ca/students/international#InternationalStudents)

#### Eligibility

International students are welcome to apply to UBC Co\-op Programs\. Most co\-op job postings are open to all UBC Co\-op students, but some roles may have hiring restrictions for Canadian citizens only\.

#### Co\-op Fees

All students admitted to our programs pay the same fees, regardless of whether they have domestic or international student status\. For the latest fee information, please refer to the [UBC Calendar](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/?tree=14,296,0,0#18094)\.

#### Work Permit

As of April 1, 2026, a Co\-op work permit is no longer required to participate in a Co\-op, internship, or practicum that is part of your program of study\. 

If work is required to complete your academic program, such as a Co\-op, a practicum, or an internship, you will now be able to use your [on\-campus](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/campus-work/) or [off\-campus](https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/working-canada/off-campus-work/) work eligibility\. 

[Work Abroad](https://coop.ubc.ca/students/international#WorkAbroad)

#### Gain International Work Experience

UBC Co\-op offers exciting international work opportunities to students\. Completing a co\-op work term abroad can be a great opportunity to develop life skills, build self\-confidence, and learn about new cultures\. Both domestic and international students are eligible to apply for roles abroad\.

#### Costs

While some employers offer relocation assistance, students are expected to cover their own relocation, visas and housing costs\. Housing is not provided by employers or the co\-op offices\. However, we are happy to provide support with applying for the necessary visas for working abroad\.

For more information on international opportunities, please visit the relevant [co\-op program website](https://coop.ubc.ca/node/139)\.
