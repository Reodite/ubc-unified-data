Ready to hire? Complete the form below to post a job with UBC Co\-op\.

If you need help getting started, contact us at [hire\.coop@ubc\.ca](mailto:hire.coop@ubc.ca) or call 604 822 4450\.

By posting a job with us, you are agreeing to our [UBC Co\-op Employer Expectations and Responsibilities](https://coop.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/UBC-Co-op-Employer-Expectations-Responsibilities.pdf) \(PDF\)\. 

#### Contact Details

#### Co\-op Job Description

Job Location

If fully/partially onsite, enter work address\. If fully remote, type "remote"\.

Your Job Number

If applicable

Position Duration

A typical co\-op term is 4 months long and must be a minimum total of 420 hours\. Choose all options available for your position, but note that different co\-op programs may have varying guidelines on length\.

Work Term Start Date

When do you require the position to start?

Salary

Students must be paid an hourly rate of at least minimum wage during their work terms\. As of November 1, 2023, employers are required to include the expected pay or the expected pay range for a specific job opportunity that they advertise publicly in accordance with B\.C\.’s [Pay Transparency Act](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/bills/billscurrent/4th42nd:gov13-1)\.

If this position is funded through a grant or wage subsidy, please indicate the name of the funding source and any hiring restrictions \(e\.g\. Canadian citizenship\)\.
