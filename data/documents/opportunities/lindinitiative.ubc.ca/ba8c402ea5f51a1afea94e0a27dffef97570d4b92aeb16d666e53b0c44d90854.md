On CBC Radio’s Ideas with Paul Kennedy, Robert Reich shared how Donald Trump’s view of the world does not reflect those of the majority of North Americans\.

In a lecture at the University of British Columbia on March 1, 2017, followed by an interview with Paul Kennedy, the former U\.S\. Secretary of Labor and Professor of Public Policy at University of California at Berkeley details how understanding the circumstances that led to the election of Donald Trump can help shape a new democratic political sensibility\.

### [**Listen**](http://www.cbc.ca/radio/ideas/the-rise-of-the-anti-establishment-where-do-we-go-from-here-1.4077287)

*\*\*This episode originally aired on CBC Radio on April 20, 2017\.*

**[Robert Reich](http://robertreich.org/)** is a Professor of Public Policy at the University of California at Berkeley, the former Secretary of Labour under Bill Clinton and the author of fourteen books on economics and public policy\. He delivered his **2017 Lind Initiative in U\.S\. Studies lecture** at UBC earlier this year as part of the series on “[The Trump Impact: Change, Challenges, Responses](https://lindinitiative.ubc.ca/series/the-trump-impact/)\.”
