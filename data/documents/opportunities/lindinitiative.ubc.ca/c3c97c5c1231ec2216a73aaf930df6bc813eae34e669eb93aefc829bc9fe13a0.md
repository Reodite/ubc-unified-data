Shad

Shad \(Shadrach Kabango\) is Rwandan\-Canadian rapper with 7 full\-length albums since his debut in 2005\. He won the Juno Award for Rap Recording Of The Year in 2011 and 5 of his albums have been shortlisted for the prestigious Polaris Music Prize for Canadian Album Of The Year\. As a presenter, Shad has hosted the CBC radio show Q and is the host of Netflix’s Peabody Award\-winning documentary series Hip\-Hop Evolution\.
