Susan Rice

Susan Rice is the former US Ambassador to the United Nations \(2009\-2013\) and former National Security Advisor to the Obama administration \(2013\-2017\)\. She was also one of the many women on the presumptive Democratic presidential nominee Joe Biden’s Vice\-President list in 2020\.

She was influential in garnering support through the UN for sanctions for Iran and North Korea, and oversaw the coordination of military and intelligence efforts in a period marked by battle with ISIS, civil war in Syria, Russian aggression, and the ongoing emergence of China\.
