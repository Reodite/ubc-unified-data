How is it that Canada has Trudeau while the United States has Trump? As a tumultuous U\.S\. presidential campaign ends, this [OpenCanada](https://www.opencanada.org/) series explores the conditions that have set Canada up to emerge, in 2016, as a bright light on a dark political stage\. The series is published as part of UBC’s fall 2016 Lind Initiative in U\.S\. Studies series\. Explore the pieces below:

[Canadian Exceptionalism](https://www.opencanada.org/features/canadian-exceptionalism/) by Stephen Marche\.

[No Matter Why Canadians are Different, We’re Just Happy We Are](https://www.opencanada.org/features/not-matter-why-canadians-are-different-were-just-happy-we-are/) by Susan Delacourt\.

[Mark Canada’s ‘Moment,’ But with More Honest Self\-Examination](https://www.opencanada.org/features/mark-canadas-moment-more-honest-self-examination/) by Luke Savage\.

[If Anything, We Have Sound Immigration Policy to Thank](https://www.opencanada.org/features/if-anything-we-have-sound-immigration-policy-thank/) by John Ibbitson\.

[Canada is no Exception\-Just Look at Its Record on Indigenous Rights](https://www.opencanada.org/features/canada-no-exceptionjust-look-its-record-indigenous-rights/) by Andray Domise\.
