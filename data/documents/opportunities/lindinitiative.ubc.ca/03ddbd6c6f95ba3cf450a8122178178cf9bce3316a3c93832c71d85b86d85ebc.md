Early on in his coverage of COVID, journalist Ed Yong realized it was more than just a science story — it was an omni\-crisis\. His journalism focuses on exposing the cracks in society exacerbated by the pandemic\. He delivered this lecture as part of the Phil Lind Initiative’s *Future of the Media* series at the University of British Columbia in April 2022\.

Listen to his lecture as part of CBC’s Ideas with Nahlah Ayed\.
