The Centre for Business Law and the Liu Institute for Global Issues welcomed author, professor and former United States Labor Secretary Robert Reich to the Peter A\. Allard School of Law for a public talk on March 1st, 2017 as part of the Lind Initiative in U\.S\. Studies series on **The Trump Impact: Change, challenges, responses\.**

Watch the conversation with Cristie Ford, Associate Professor and Director, Centre for Business Law, and Associate Professor Wei Cui as well as the question &amp; answer period [**here**](http://mediasitemob1.mediagroup.ubc.ca/Mediasite/Play/9e27e7ac0c13486a82eea5834b30fa4a1d)\.
