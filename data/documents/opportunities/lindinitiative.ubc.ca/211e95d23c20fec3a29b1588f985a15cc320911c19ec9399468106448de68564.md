Peter Klein

Peter Klein is a three\-time Emmy Award\-winning journalist\. He’s a full professor at UBC’s School of Journalism, Writing and Media, and a faculty affiliate at the School of Public Policy and Global Affairs\. He’s also the founder and director of the Global Reporting Centre, a Faculty of Arts\-based journalism non\-profit\. He was previously executive editor of NBC News, overseeing investigative reporting at the network, was a longtime producer at CBS News 60 Minutes, and was one of the founding producers of The New York Times’ video unit\.
