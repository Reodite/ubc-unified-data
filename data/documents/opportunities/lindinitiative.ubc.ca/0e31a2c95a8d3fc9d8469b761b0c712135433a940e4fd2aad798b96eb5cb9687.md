“But the pandemic hasn’t just been a science story\. It is an omnicrisis that has warped and upended every aspect of our lives\.”

Ed Yong, award\-winning science writer for *The Atlantic* and a Phil Lind Initiative speaker for our 2022 series on *The Future of Media*, discusses how the COVID\-19 pandemic has been an unprecedented science story that is intertwined in all aspects of life in his article for *The Atlantic*\. Yong further remarks on science journalism’s insufficiencies in writing about COVID\-19 and the need to adjust accordingly\.

Read the full article [here](https://www.theatlantic.com/science/archive/2021/10/how-pandemic-changed-science-writing/620271/)\.
