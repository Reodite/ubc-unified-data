In a world confronted with escalating environmental crises, are academics asking the right questions and advocating the best solutions? Lind Seminar Instructor and UBC Postdoctoral Fellow [Justin Alger](https://lindinitiative.ubc.ca/course/) asks this question in his new book, *A Research Agenda for Global Environmental Politics*, co\-authored by UBC Political Science Professor Peter Dauvergne on the future of research in global environmental politics\.
