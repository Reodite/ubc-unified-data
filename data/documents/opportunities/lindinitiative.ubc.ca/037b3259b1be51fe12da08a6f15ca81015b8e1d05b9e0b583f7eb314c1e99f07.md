Pulitzer Prize\-winning journalist and author **Carlos Lozada** brings a singular blend of curiosity, empathy and precision to his analysis of political nonfiction writing in America\. An opinion columnist for *The New York Times*, and author of *The Washington Book: How to Read Politics and Politicians,* Lozada gains nuanced insight into political leaders through what they reveal in their own writing\.

Moderated by [**Max Cameron**](https://lindinitiative.ubc.ca/speakers/max-cameron/), Professor at UBC’s School of Public Policy and Global Affairs and Political Science Department\.
