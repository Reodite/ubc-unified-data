“Racism is complicated\. White people feel personally responsible for racism when they should understand the problem as systemic\. It is interfering as much with their lives as with the lives of people of colour\.” In an interview by The Guardian, Claudia Rankine, author of *Citizen: An American Lyric* and a 2020 Phil Lind Initiative speaker, shares her thoughts on what racism means, why it is inescapable, and how she writes about it\.
