Teju Cole

Teju Cole is a writer, art historian, and photographer\. He is the Distinguished Writer in Residence at Bard College and photography critic of the New York Times Magazine\. He is the author of two books, a novella, Every Day is for the Thief, shortlisted for the PEN/Open Book Award, and a novel, Open City, which also featured on numerous book of the year lists\.
