Francis Fukuyama , author of *The End of History and the Last Man* and 2018 Lind Initiative speaker, speaks to the Marxist ideology, the threats faced by liberal democracy, and the potential implications of a US\-China war in an interview by *NewStatesman*\. Regarding the resurgence of socialism, Fukuyama comments, “If you mean redistributive programmes that try to redress this big imbalance in both incomes and wealth that has emerged then, yes, I think not only can it come back, it ought to come back\.”
