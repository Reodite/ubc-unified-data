*“If we lie to ourselves about history, then we end up with these big lies in the present\.”*

Timothy Snyder, Professor of History, Yale University; Award\-winning author, and 2021 Phil Lind Initiative speaker for the series *The Anti\-Democratic Turn* was featured on NPR’s podcast *Throughline,* where he talks about the recent Capitol insurrection\. Snyder also emphasizes how the Covid\-19 pandemic can affect the way Americans perceive information, particularly on social media, which can often lead to echo chambers and a switch from a ‘trust’ society to a ‘belief’ society\.

Listen to the full episode [here](https://www.npr.org/2021/01/20/958828047/the-anatomy-of-autocracy-timothy-snyder)\.
