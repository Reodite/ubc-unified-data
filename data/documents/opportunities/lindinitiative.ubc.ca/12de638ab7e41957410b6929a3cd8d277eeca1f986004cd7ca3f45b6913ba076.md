In an op\-ed for the *Vancouver Sun*, Professor Max Cameron \(SPPGA Former Acting Director; Political Science\), comments on the feasibility of a lottery\-based admission process among qualified applicants at the university level\.  This idea was introduced by Professor Michael Sandel, Harvard Professor, author of *The Tyranny of Merit*, and a Phil Lind Initiative Speaker for our 2021 series *The Anti\-Democratic Turn*\. Professor Sandel’s latest book highlights the problem with an increasingly meritocratic society, particularly in how university admissions perpetuate this issue\.

Read the full op\-ed [here](https://vancouversun.com/opinion/max-cameron-should-university-admissions-be-decided-on-the-basis-of-a-lottery-among-qualified-applicants)\.
