Enjoy this recording from the Phil Lind Initiative series on The Future of Media with [Douglas Rushkoff](https://lindinitiative.ubc.ca/speakers/douglas-rushkoff/), a world\-renowned philosopher, futurist, and author of Team Human\.

The conversation was held on February 3, 2022 and was moderated by Taylor Owen, Beaverbrook Chair in Media, Ethics and Communications, the founding director of The Center for Media, Technology and Democracy, and Associate Professor, Max Bell School of Public Policy at McGill University\.

This event was hosted by the School of Public Policy and Global Affairs at the University of British Columbia\.
