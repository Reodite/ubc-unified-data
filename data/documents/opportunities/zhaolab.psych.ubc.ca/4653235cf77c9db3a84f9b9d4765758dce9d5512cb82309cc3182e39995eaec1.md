-   #### Real\-time energy display launched\!
    
    March 2016
    
    We are launching a [real\-time energy display project](http://www.ubc-realtime-energy.com/) in 15 academic and residential buildings on UBC campus\. You can select any building on the first page and see what the display looks like\. The goal of this project is to see how real\-time energy consumption information influences actual energy consumption behaviors of the inhabitants in the building\.
    
    [http://www\.ubc\-realtime\-energy\.com/](http://www.ubc-realtime-energy.com/)[Real\-time Energy Display](https://zhaolab.psych.ubc.ca/assets/img/events/energy_display.jpg)
    
-   #### Droppler: A water conservation device
    
    January 2016
    
    Based on our research, Nascent Objects have developed a modular device called Droppler that listens to water sounds in the environment and offers visual feedback of water use in real time, in order to reduce water consumption\. Here is more information about the device: [UBC Psychology](https://psych.ubc.ca/prof-jiaying-zhaos-research-inspires-a-new-product-to-change-water-use-habits/), [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2016/01/79738-droppler-water-monitor-empowers-consumers-with-live-water-use-feedback-to-immediately-reduce-water-consumption/), [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/53546-droppler-water-monitoring-gadget.html), and [Co\.Exist](https://www.fastcompany.com/3055377/this-gadget-can-transform-into-anything-you-need)\.
    
    [Droppler Water Conservation Device](https://zhaolab.psych.ubc.ca/assets/img/events/Droppler.png)
    
-   #### Sorting game launched\!
    
    January 2016
    
    We have launched an [online sorting game](https://yuluo.psych.ubc.ca/studies/Sorting_MD/) to train people on how to sort their waste correctly based on UBC sorting policies\. The goal of this project is to see whether this game improves actual recycling and composting rates on campus\.
    
    [https://yuluo\.psych\.ubc\.ca/studies/Sorting\_MD/](https://yuluo.psych.ubc.ca/studies/Sorting_MD/)[Sorting Game](https://zhaolab.psych.ubc.ca/assets/img/events/Raccoon.jpg)
