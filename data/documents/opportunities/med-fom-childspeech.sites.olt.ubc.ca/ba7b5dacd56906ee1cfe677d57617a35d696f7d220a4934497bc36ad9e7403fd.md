## **Looking for Participants\!**

## **Get Involved in Our Project In\-Person** **or** **Online\!**

We are looking for **children with typical speech development** to help us learn how well they say long words\.

Children in **Kindergarten to Grade 6** can participate\.

Children can **choose** to participate **online** \(on Zoom\) **OR** **in\-person** \(at the university or at school\)\.

We will **look at lots of pictures** and do **5 talking activities**\. We will **look at the parts of the mouth** that are used for talking\.

The activities will take about **45 minutes**\.

We will send your family **a small gift card** to thank you for your participation\.

The research will **help SLPs** \(Speech\-Language Pathologists\) **give better tests and therapy**\.

**To have your child participate in the study or for any questions, contact us at:**

**speechdevelopmentlab@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca**

We look forward to hearing from you\. Thank you for your interest in our project\!

[**Click to Inquire**](mailto:speechdevelopmentlab@audiospeech.ubc.ca)
