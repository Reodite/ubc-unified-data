Hello instructors\. Please find below a round\-up of the latest developments regarding the [Instructure/Canvas incident](https://status.it.ubc.ca/) – this update has information regarding contacting students, accessing course materials, and alternative course delivery platforms\.

***Note***: as information is changing quickly, please view the following two links for the most up\-to\-date information\.

**Resources:** Visit [https://ltic\.ubc\.ca/alternative\-course\-hosting/](https://ltic.ubc.ca/alternative-course-hosting/) to see what is now available, what is in progress, and what is currently unavailable\.

**Discussion forum:** We are using a Piazza course to share up\-to\-date information\. You can access it here to view information and ask questions: [https://piazza\.com/ubc\.ca/summer2026/ubc\_s1\_all](https://piazza.com/ubc.ca/summer2026/ubc_s1_all)\.

***Communicating with students***

**Pull class roster from Workday**

You can download your class roster from Workday\. Steps \(including diagrams\) are available in [this Piazza thread](https://piazza.com/class/moxconx6oqc4f6/post/14)\.

**Contacting students**

See these [instructions on how to use Workday’s Student Communication Tool \(PDF\)](https://ltic-2026.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/How-to-Email-Students-Registered-in-Your-Courses.pdf) to contact your students for your course through Workday\.

**Add your course to “Find My Course”**

We have published a Course Directory page at [https://findmycourse\.ubc\.ca/](https://findmycourse.ubc.ca/)\. If you log in with your faculty CWL, you can add your course information to the page\.

***Canvas course materials***

**Canvas exports into SharePoint**

We have exported most portions of course content from Canvas for ST1 and are in the process of uploading it to SharePoint\. We were able to extract:

-   pages \(wiki page content\)

-   assignments

-   quizzes \(classic quizzes, not new quizzes\)

-   discussions

-   module structure

-   Rubrics

-   Uploaded files and attachments \(see note\)

Note: Most exports include uploaded files and attachments; however, a small number of course exports are not exporting files at this time\. We are looking into the issue and hope for a resolution shortly\.

**How you will receive your course materials**

Once your materials are ready on OneDrive, LT Hub will send you an email with access details and instructions on next steps\.

If you would like to request course materials from a past semester \(such as 2025 ST1\), please view this thread on Piazza: [https://piazza\.com/class/moxconx6oqc4f6/post/10\_f3](https://piazza.com/class/moxconx6oqc4f6/post/10_f3)

**I have my course materials\. Now what?**

When the files are ready, you can:

-   Open the HTML files in a web browser to view directly \(attachments will not display\)

-   Import the files into UBC Blogs using the import tool

-   Download the attachments onto your computer

Starting tomorrow, you will be able to:

-   Ask LTHub to activate your course in Moodle \(an LMS platform\)

-   A few hours later you will be able begin building out your course in Moodle

We are working on next steps for those who will need support in uploading materials into Moodle\. Follow this thread in Piazza to see the latest updates: [https://piazza\.com/class/moxconx6oqc4f6/post/16](https://piazza.com/class/moxconx6oqc4f6/post/16)

***Alternative course hosting options***

**Moodle** 

We are in the process of creating a UBC Moodle site, an alternate LMS for your course delivery\. Moodle supports assignment activity, secure file uploads, blind marking, and simple direct grading and rubrics\.

On Moodle, we have enabled:

-   CWL integration

-   Your course content from Canvas into Moodle

-   Automatic syncing of your course registration list into Moodle

**Upcoming Moodle workshop \(Monday, 11:00 am\)**

CTLT is hosting an online workshop on Teaching a Course with Moodle Learning Management System, on Monday, May 11, 11:00 am – 12:30 pm\. The workshop will be recorded\. Register at [https://events\.ctlt\.ubc\.ca/events/teaching\-a\-course\-with\-moodle\-learning\-management\-system/](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/teaching-a-course-with-moodle-learning-management-system/)\.

*About the workshop:* 

This 90\-minute virtual workshop provides an orientation to teaching and learning with Moodle for faculty wishing to explore using an alternative to the primary learning management system\. Participants will explore key Moodle features that support communication, course content organization, modules and files, and assessment tools\. The workshop is designed to help faculty quickly develop the foundational skills needed to use Moodle effectively to support teaching and student learning\.

**UBC Blogs** 

UBC Blogs is an open platform hosted at UBC Vancouver available to all faculty at UBC\. You can set up your course on Blogs at: [https://blogs\.ubc\.ca/](https://blogs.ubc.ca/)\.

One benefit of using Blogs is that you can set it to “subscriber only”, to limit access to students registered in the class\. Access is enabled through CWL\. Note that a student will need to create a UBC Blogs account before they can be added to a Blog\.

-   View the [recording of the Friday May 8 UBC Blogs workshop](https://learning.media.ubc.ca/media/teaching-with-ubcs-blogs.mp4/0_h0j1b58k)\.

-   View our [Teaching With UBC Blogs FAQ](https://ubcca-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/richard_tape_ubc_ca/IQBl2y7BWPU7RYRRidnygCC-AUhsPNXRN4GmcraiHXkrrMk?e=j70Loo)

**UBC Wiki**

UBC Wiki is another open platform to post your course materials\. Note that materials will be available publicly on this platform\. There are a number of instructors who have used UBC Wiki for courses in the past\. For more information on using UBC Wiki for courses, see: [https://wiki\.ubc\.ca/Course](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Course)\.

**MS Teams** 

Microsoft Teams is a collaboration tool that you can use for course delivery\. The tool allows for file\-sharing, real\-time document editing, and can be used to meet and work with UBC instructors, students and staff\.  Microsoft Teams complies with UBC’s privacy requirements and is FIPPA\-compliant\.

Notes:

-   Students require an @student\.ubc\.ca email address to access UBC MS Teams; they can do this at [https://www\.myaccount\.ubc\.ca/myAccount/](https://www.myaccount.ubc.ca/myAccount/)

-   A course’s student list is not automatically populated, but you can invite students to join your Team:

-   If you select “Manage Team” and switch to the “Settings” tab, you can go to the “Team code” section and click the \[Generate\] button to generate a MS Teams join code\. You can then share the code with your students via Workday’s Student Communication Tool \(SCT\)\. You can use the “Pending requests” section to approve requests to join your Team\.

***Get help***

**When to contact the LT Hub**

Contact the LT Hub directly for help in the following areas:

-   If you are teaching a summer course that began **before** May 11, 2026

-   If you are teaching a 2025W1 or W2 course that is still running

Reach the LT Hub through [https://lthub\.ubc\.ca/](https://lthub.ubc.ca/) or email [LT\.hub@ubc\.ca](mailto:lt.hub@ubc.ca)\.

We also recommend that faculty, staff, and students continue to [be vigilant against phishing](https://privacymatters.ubc.ca/i-want/phishing-emails) and follow best practices for protecting their accounts and data, including using strong passwords and enabling multi\-factor authentication where available\.
