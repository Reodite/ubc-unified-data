### Overview

Sports enthusiasts will find plenty to cheer about at UBC\. There are more than a dozen high\-performing Thunderbird varsity teams, including basketball, volleyball, hockey, soccer and football\. Join the UBC Thunderbirds fan club — check game times or buy tickets [online](https://gothunderbirds.ca/calendar.aspx)\.
