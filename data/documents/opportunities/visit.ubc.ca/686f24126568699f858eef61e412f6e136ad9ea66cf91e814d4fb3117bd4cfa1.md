## Museums and Art Galleries

[AHVA Art Gallery](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/ahva_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

This contemporary art gallery within the Audain Art Centre hosts free exhibits, lectures and performances by UBC Art History, Visual Art and Theory \(AHVA\) students, as well as faculty and visiting artists\. Everyone is welcome at the UBC AHVA Gallery, but hours and exhibitions vary throughout the year, so check the website before your visit\.

[Visit Website](https://ahva.ubc.ca/ahva-gallery-home/?utm_source=wc_website&amp;utm_campaign=location_detail)
