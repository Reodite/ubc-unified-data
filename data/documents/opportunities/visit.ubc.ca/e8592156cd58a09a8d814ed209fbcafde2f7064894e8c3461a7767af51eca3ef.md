### Overview

West Coast Suites is a premium UBC hotel offering all the comforts of home at an affordable nightly rate\. Open year\-round, the West Coast Suites offer both spacious one\-bedroom suites, economically sized studio rooms, conveniently located on the scenic UBC Vancouver campus, with easy access to downtown via car or public transit\. Accessible rooms are also available\.
