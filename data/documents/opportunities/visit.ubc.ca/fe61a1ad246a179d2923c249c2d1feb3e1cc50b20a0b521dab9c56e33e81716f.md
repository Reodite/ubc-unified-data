### Overview

The University Golf Club is one of Canada’s best public facilities, popular with casual golfers and regulars alike\. The golf course is only minutes away from UBC and downtown Vancouver\. You can book your tee time at the scenic 18\-hole par\-72 course up to six months in advance\. Clubs and carts are also available for rent\.

[Visit Website](https://universitygolf.com/)
