## Athletics and Recreation

[UBC Aquatic Centre](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/aquatic_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

Whether you’re looking to get in your daily laps, join an aquafit class or just enjoy a day splashing around with your kids, the UBC Aquatic Centre has got you covered\. There are three distinct pool areas offering a lazy river and hot tub, a recreation pool with two diving boards, and a 50m lap pool for serious training; check the [public swim schedule online](https://recreation.ubc.ca/aquatics/aquatics-drop-in/)\.
