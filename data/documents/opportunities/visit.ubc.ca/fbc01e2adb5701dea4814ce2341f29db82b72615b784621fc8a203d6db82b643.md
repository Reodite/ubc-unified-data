### Overview

Get cozy in UBC’s Gage Suites — studio, one\-bedroom and two\-bedroom units offering all the comforts of home at a great rate\. The Gage Suites is open from May to August\. Our Gage Suites offering full kitchen facilities, free wi\-fi and easy transit accessibility, they’re ideal accommodation for solo and family travellers\. Accessible rooms are also available\.
