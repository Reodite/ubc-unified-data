### Overview

Since 1995, the UBC School of Music’s opera program has helped gifted young singers launch their careers on the world stage\. The UBC Opera company performs three full\-scale productions and six tea concerts each season at several Vancouver campus venues, including the Old Auditorium and the [Chan Centre for the Performing Arts](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/theatre-and-music/chan-centre-for-the-performing-arts/)\.
