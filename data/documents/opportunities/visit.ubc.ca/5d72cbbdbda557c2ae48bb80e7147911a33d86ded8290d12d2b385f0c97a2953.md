### Overview

Beloved by locals and tourists alike, the Nitobe Memorial Garden is one of the most authentic Japanese gardens outside of Japan\. Carefully cultivated to provide year\-round beauty, the garden is especially photogenic in spring and fall when colorful cherry blossoms and maple leaves brighten the tranquil pathways\.
