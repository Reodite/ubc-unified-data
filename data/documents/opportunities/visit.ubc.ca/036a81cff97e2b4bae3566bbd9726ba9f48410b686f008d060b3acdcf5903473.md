## Theatre and Music

[Chan Centre for the Performing Arts at UBC](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/chan_3-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

With its striking design and superb acoustics, the intimate Chan Centre for the Performing Arts delights performers and audiences alike\. Many UBC music concerts and events happen here throughout the year, as well as performances by internationally acclaimed artists\. Concert tickets are available [online](https://chancentre.com/tickets/) or through the on\-site box office\.
