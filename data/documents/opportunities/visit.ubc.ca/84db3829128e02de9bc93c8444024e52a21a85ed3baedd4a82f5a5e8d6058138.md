## The UBC Welcome Centre is waiting for you

Think of it as UBC’s friendly front door: the UBC Welcome Centre in the Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre is the perfect place to start your exploration of the Vancouver campus\. Bring your questions and the knowledgeable staff will help you get the most from your UBC visit\. Book a [campus tour](https://visit.ubc.ca/campus-tours), discover events on campus or grab a map for directions to the nearest restaurant\. And feel free to phone for help while planning your visit\.
