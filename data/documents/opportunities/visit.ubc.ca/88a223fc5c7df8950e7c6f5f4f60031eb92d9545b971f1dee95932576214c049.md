## Food Courts

[Open Kitchen](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/OpenKitchen_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

The Orchard Commons building at UBC is home to the popular Open Kitchen\. Here, visitors will find a delicious array of dishes that represents the best of Vancouver’s food scene\. After a visit to the nearby [Beaty Biodiversity Museum](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/museums-and-art-galleries/beaty-biodiversity-museum/), stop by for butter chicken, vegan stir fry or poutine\. Hero Coffee &amp; Market, which offers delicious coffee and treats, is also found here\. Open Kitchen is open throughout the week\.

#### Options at Orchard Commons

-   [Open Kitchen](https://food.ubc.ca/place/open-kitchen/) \(cafeteria\)
-   [Hero Coffee &amp; Market](https://food.ubc.ca/place/hero/) \(café\)
