## Theatre and Music

[UBC Vancouver theatre and film students](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/theatre_2-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

Theatre and film fans are spoiled for choice at UBC Vancouver\. There’s a full season of UBC theatre shows starring talented student actors, designers and directors\. As well, there’s an annual film festival featuring the work of up\-and\-coming filmmakers\. Catch a show on campus at the Frederic Wood Theatre or at the [Chan Centre for the Performing Arts](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/theatre-and-music/chan-centre-for-the-performing-arts/)\. Visit the [UBC Theatre and Film site](https://theatrefilm.ubc.ca/?utm_source=wc_website&amp;utm_campaign=location_detail) to learn more and buy your tickets\.
