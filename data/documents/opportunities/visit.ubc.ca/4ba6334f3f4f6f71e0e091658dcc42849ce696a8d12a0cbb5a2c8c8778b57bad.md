## Theatre and Music

[UBC School of Music](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/music_3-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

Take your pick of outstanding performances from the UBC School of Music concert calendar\. From classical to contemporary, there’s something to suit every taste — and budget\. Concert tickets range from no\- to low\-cost for performances featuring talented students, faculty and visiting artists\. Whether you’re looking for an afternoon of chamber music or a night of [UBC Opera](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/theatre-and-music/ubc-opera/), you’ll find a great seat at on\-campus venues such as the Roy Barnett Recital Hall, the Old Auditorium and the renowned [Chan Centre for the Performing Arts](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/theatre-and-music/chan-centre-for-the-performing-arts/)\.
