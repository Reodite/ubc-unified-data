## Food Courts

[AMS Student Nest](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/nest_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

The food court at the AMS Student Nest is one of the most popular places to grab lunch at UBC\. You’ll find everything from spicy tacos to vegan stews to bubble tea\. It’s best to visit on a weekday, as some options are closed on weekends\.

#### Options at the AMS Student Nest

-   Blue Chip Café \(café\)
-   Grand Noodle Emporium \(pan\-Asian\)
-   Honour Roll \(sushi\)
-   [Kyros Kitchen](https://kyroskitchen.ca/) \(Mediterranean\) 
-   The Delly \(sandwiches\)
-   The Soup Market \(soup\)
-   Seoul Hotdog \(Korean\)
