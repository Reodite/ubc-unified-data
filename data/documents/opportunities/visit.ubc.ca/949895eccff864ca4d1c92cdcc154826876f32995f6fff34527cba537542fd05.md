## Restaurants, Lounges and Pubs

[Gallery Patio and Lounge](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/ubcfood_2-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

Located on the top floor \(level 4\) of the AMS Student Nest, the Gallery Patio and Lounge serves favourites like burgers and fish and chips\. As well as food, the Gallery has an extensive beer, wine and cocktail menu\. On sunny days, venture out to the rooftop patio for a birds\-eye view of UBC\. The Gallery is closed on weekends, so visit during the week\.

[Visit Website](http://ams.ubc.ca/the-nest/eat)
