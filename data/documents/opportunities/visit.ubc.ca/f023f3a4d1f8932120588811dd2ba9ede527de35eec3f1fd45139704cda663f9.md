## Gardens and Nature

[Wreck Beach](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/wreckbeach_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

It’s a bit of a workout getting down to Vancouver’s legendary clothing\-optional beach — look for the steep staircases of Trail 6 at the west end of University Boulevard to find your way to beautiful Wreck Beach \(part of [Pacific Spirit Regional Park](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/gardens-and-nature/pacific-spirit-park)\)\. Come for the beautiful nature views, but please give other beach\-goers their privacy\. There are often vendors selling snacks and drinks on the beach, but it’s a good idea to come prepared with your own supplies\.
