Start the day with a nourishing breakfast\. Enjoy a farm\-fresh salad for lunch\. Watch the sunset over a dinner of Pacific salmon\. Toast the evening with a pint of local craft beer\. Whatever you’re craving, you’ll find it on UBC’s Vancouver campus with dozens of restaurants, coffee shops and fast\-food outlets to fuel you\.

Browse through your eating options below, or use the [visitors’ map](https://visit.ubc.ca/plan-your-trip/ubc-attractions-map/) to find a food spot near you\.

[Search for restaurants on the map](https://visit.ubc.ca/plan-your-trip/ubc-attractions-map/)
