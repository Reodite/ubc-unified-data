### Overview

Although Great Dane is a little off the beaten track at UBC, their selection of coffee, tea and baked\-in\-house pastries are worth the walk\. Take a detour on your way to the [Museum of Anthropology](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/museums-and-art-galleries/museum-of-anthropology/) or [Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/museums-and-art-galleries/irving-k-barber-learning-centre/) for a treat\. If you’re lucky, you might see Lady, the café’s resident Great Dane\.
