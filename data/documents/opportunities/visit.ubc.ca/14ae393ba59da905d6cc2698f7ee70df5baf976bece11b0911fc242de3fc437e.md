### Overview

Blue Chip Café is famous at UBC for their addictive cookies \(try the Marbelous cookie\!\)\. The café also offers coffee, tea, cold drinks, and selection of vegan and gluten\-free treats\. You can find this popular spot inside the AMS Student Nest\.

[Visit Website](http://ams.ubc.ca/the-nest/eat/)
