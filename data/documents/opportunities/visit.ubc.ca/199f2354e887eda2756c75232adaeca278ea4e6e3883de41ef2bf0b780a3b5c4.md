### Overview

Located at the south end of the UBC Vancouver campus in Wesbrook Village, Sports Illustrated Clubhouse offers a vibrant space to enjoy delicious food and refreshing drinks in an elevated setting\. It’s the perfect spot to celebrate sports with friends\. The restaurant is a good option for those visiting nearby [Thunderbird Park](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/athletics-and-recreation/thunderbirds-varsity-games/)\. Sports Illustrated Clubhouse is open throughout the week for lunch and dinner, and it also offers brunch\.
