## Food Courts

[Families eat on a restaurant patio at Wesbrook Village near UBC\.](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/wesbrook_2-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

Wesbrook Village offers many food and drink options to those visiting the south side of UBC\. It’s a great place to stop after visiting the [UBC Botanic](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/gardens-and-nature/ubc-botanical-garden/) [a](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/gardens-and-nature/ubc-botanical-garden/) [l Garden](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/gardens-and-nature/ubc-botanical-garden/) or [Pacific Spirit Park](http://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/gardens-and-nature/pacific-spirit-park/)\. You’ll find pizza, fresh baked goods, and sushi at Wesbrook Village\. On warm days, enjoy your meal in one of the surrounding parks\.

#### Options at Wesbrook Village

-   [Blenz Coffee](https://blenz.com/locations/?store_id=56) \(coffee\)
-   [Chef Hung Taiwanese Beef Noodle](https://www.chefhungnoodle.com/home.php) \(Taiwanese\)
-   [Doughgirls Comfort Kitchen &amp; Bakeshop](https://www.doughgirlsbakeshop.com/) \(bakery\)
-   [Freshii](http://www.freshii.com/) \(salads and wraps\)
-   [Neptune Chinese Kitchen](http://www.neptunechinesekitchen.com/) \(Chinese\)
-   [Rogers Chocolates](https://www.rogerschocolates.com/) \(chocolates and ice cream\)
-   [Togo Sushi](http://www.togosushi.ca/) \(sushi\)
-   [Big Star Sandwich](https://bigstarsandwich.com/)
-   [Nicli Pizzeria \+ Bar](https://www.niclipizzeria.com/) \(Italian\)
-   [Sports Illustrated Clubhouse](https://sportsillustratedeats.com/)
-   Share Tea \(bubble tea\)
-   Poke by Ryuu
-   Lucky’s Exotic Bodega \(snacks\)
