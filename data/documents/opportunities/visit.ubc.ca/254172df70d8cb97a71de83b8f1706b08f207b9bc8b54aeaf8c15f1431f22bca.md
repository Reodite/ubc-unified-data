UBC’s [Nitobe Memorial Garden](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/gardens-and-nature/nitobe-memorial-garden/) features in a new documentary exploring the complex history of Japanese gardens in western Canada\. *Borrowed From Nature*, by Kino Sum Productions, reveals hidden testaments to an enduring Japanese influence\.
