## Food Courts

[University Boulevard Central](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/blvdcentral_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

Just steps away from the [UBC Welcome Centre](http://visit.ubc.ca/contact-us/)[,](http://visit.ubc.ca/contact-us/) University Boulevard has food to satisfy every craving\. You’ll find everything from pitas and sushi to coffee and ice cream\. It’s a great place to eat on weekends, as spots are open all week\. On sunny days, you can eat your meal on the outside patios\.
