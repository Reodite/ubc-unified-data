## Athletics and Recreation

[UBC Tennis Centre](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/tennis_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

The only public indoor tennis facility in Vancouver, the UBC Tennis Centre has eight indoor courts, one outdoor court, and an additional four courts available nearby\. Membership is not required to book courts up to 24 hours in advance\. The centre also offers one\-off private coaching sessions and racket rentals\.
