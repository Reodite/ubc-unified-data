## Restaurants, Lounges and Pubs

[Koerner's Pub](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/koerners_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

Koerner’s Pub is a popular UBC spot to meet up with friends for burgers, tacos, nachos and craft beer\. On sunny days, enjoy your meal on their garden patio\. Due to its location across from the [Museum of Anthropology](http://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/museums-and-art-galleries/museum-of-anthropology/) and [Nitobe Garden](http://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/gardens-and-nature/nitobe-memorial-garden/), Koerner’s Pub is an ideal lunch spot for visitors — but note that it’s only open on weekdays\.
