[Pacific Museum of Earth at UBC](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/pme_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

A glittering gemstone\-and\-mineral gallery and a go\-ahead\-and\-touch\-it dinosaur bone are just two of the popular exhibits that make the UBC Pacific Museum of Earth unique among Vancouver museums\. There’s something for all ages\! Sign up for one of the year\-round learning programs, or drop by to enjoy a free self\-guided tour of the earth’s 4\.5\-billion\-year history\.
