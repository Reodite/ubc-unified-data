## Food Courts

[Food Court](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/traveltips_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

If you’re looking for a bite to eat near the [Irving K\. Barber Learning Centre](http://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/museums-and-art-galleries/irving-k-barber-learning-centre/) or [Morris and Helen Belkin Gallery](http://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/museums-and-art-galleries/morris-and-helen-belkin-art-gallery/), there is a cluster of dining options along UBC’s Main Mall that will satisfy your hunger\. 

#### Options at the food court along Main Mall

-   [Presto](https://food.ubc.ca/places/presto/) \(pasta\) 
-   [Tim Hortons](https://food.ubc.ca/place/tim-hortons/) \(coffee\)
-   [Triple O’s](https://food.ubc.ca/place/triple-o/) \(burgers\)
