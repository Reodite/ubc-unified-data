## Find great food and accommodation at UBC

Whether you’re coming to UBC for a day, a week or longer, you’ll find food and a place to stay that’s right for you\.

Accommodation ranges from hostels to high\-end hotels\. Dining options run from breakfast cafés to restaurants serving fine West Coast cuisine\. Craft beer and cocktail lovers have a wide choice of lounges and watering holes\. And coffee\-shop culture is bustling on the Vancouver campus\. Come hungry and stay late\!
