The famous West Coast coffee\-shop culture is alive and well at UBC\. You’re never too far from a good cup of mocha or mint tea\. As well as Starbucks and Tim Hortons, UBC has a wide range of popular indie cafés such as Great Dane, Loafe Café, The Boulevard Coffee Roasting Co\. and Bean Around the World\.

Browse through the café options below, or use the [visitors’ map](https://visit.ubc.ca/plan-your-trip/ubc-attractions-map/) to find a spot near you\.
