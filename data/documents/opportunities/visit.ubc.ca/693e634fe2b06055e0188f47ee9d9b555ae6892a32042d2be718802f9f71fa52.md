## Museums and Art Galleries

[Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery at UBC Vancouver](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/belkin_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

Art that starts you thinking: that’s the promise of the Belkin, a nationally acclaimed contemporary art gallery with bold exhibits that challenge the eye and mind\. With free admission and more than 4,500 artworks, the Belkin Art Gallery is a highlight of the UBC visual arts scene and home to one of BC’s largest public art collections\.
