[A visitor enjoys a pizza from Mercante at UBC Vancouver\.](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/mercante_2-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

Mercante is a popular spot at UBC for made\-to\-order pizzas delivered hot from the stone\-hearth oven\. You can also find a variety of fresh pasta and salad options here\. The restaurant is located near many of UBC’s attractions, including the [AHVA Gallery](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/museums-and-art-galleries/ahva-gallery/), the [Pacific Museum of Earth](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/museums-and-art-galleries/pacific-museum-of-earth/) and [Wreck Beach](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/gardens-and-nature/wreck-beach/)\.
