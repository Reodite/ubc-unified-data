## Accommodation

[Pacific Spirit Hostel](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/hostel_1-1940x1216_2x.jpg)

### Overview

From May to August, UBC’s Pacific Spirit Hostel at Place Vanier offers budget travellers private single and double rooms with high\-speed wifi, shared washrooms and on\-site laundry\. The shared lounge is a great place to make new friends from all over the world\.
