Summer is one of the best times to experience UBC\. Whether you’re visiting Vancouver, exploring campus for the first time or rediscovering a favourite destination, here’s how to make the most of a summer day at UBC\.

---

## **START YOUR MORNING AT THE UBC FARMERS’ MARKET** 

If you’re visiting on a Saturday, begin your day at the [UBC Farm Farmers’ Market](https://ubcfarm.ubc.ca/markets/market-information/)\. Browse local produce, artisan goods, and fresh\-baked treats while enjoying the vibrant community atmosphere before exploring campus\.  

Looking for fresh produce during the week? Visit the [UBC Farm’s Tuesday evening or Wednesday campus markets](https://ubcfarm.ubc.ca/markets/market-information/) for seasonal vegetables, fruit, herbs and flowers grown locally at UBC\. 

[Image](https://visit.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/farmers-market.jpg)

---

## **ADVENTURE IN THE TREETOPS** 

Wander through UBC Botanical Garden’s diverse plant collections, then head into the forest canopy on the [Greenheart TreeWalk](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/gardens-and-nature/greenheart-treewalk/) for a unique perspective of Vancouver’s coastal rainforest\. 

Image

---

## **REFUEL ALONG THE WAY** 

From quick bites to sit\-down meals, UBC offers plenty of [dining options](https://visit.ubc.ca/eat-drink-and-stay/) to fuel your next adventure\.  

Don’t miss out on our favourite [Blue Chip](https://visit.ubc.ca/eat-drink-and-stay/cafes/blue-chip-cafe/) cookies and [Mercante](https://visit.ubc.ca/eat-drink-and-stay/restaurants/restaurants-lounges-and-pubs/mercante/)’s traditional Italian pizzas, baked to perfection in a fiery hot stone\-hearth oven\. 

On hot summer days, nothing beats cooling down with a scoop of [Vancouver’s iconic ice cream](https://www.rainorshineicecream.com/contact)—right on campus\. 

Image

---

## **EXPLORE UBC’S MUSEUMS** 

From Canada’s largest blue whale skeleton at the [Beaty Biodiversity Museum](https://beatymuseum.ubc.ca/) to Indigenous art and cultural collections at the [Museum of Anthropology](https://moa.ubc.ca/), UBC is home to museums that inspire curiosity and discovery\.

## **DISCOVER A HIDDEN GEM AT NITOBE MEMORIAL GARDEN** 

Just a short walk away, you will find one of UBC’s most peaceful destinations\. [Nitobe Memorial Garden](https://botanicalgarden.ubc.ca/nitobe-memorial-garden/) invites visitors to slow down and immerse themselves in nature\. 

Image

---

## **END THE DAY BY THE OCEAN** 

Before heading back into the city, make your way to [Wreck Beach](https://visit.ubc.ca/see-and-do/gardens-and-nature/wreck-beach/), Tower Beach, or one of UBC’s scenic viewpoints overlooking the Salish Sea\.
