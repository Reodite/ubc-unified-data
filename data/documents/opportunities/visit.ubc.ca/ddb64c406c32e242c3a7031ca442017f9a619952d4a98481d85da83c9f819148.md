### Overview

TRIUMF House is a boutique Vancouver guesthouse offering a variety of comfortable rooms and suites to short\- and long\-stay guests\. TRIUMF House offers complimentary in\-room wi\-fi and other amenities such as a fully equipped kitchen, a 24\-hour fitness room, secure underground parking and bike rentals\.  

[Visit Website](https://triumfhouse.ca/)
