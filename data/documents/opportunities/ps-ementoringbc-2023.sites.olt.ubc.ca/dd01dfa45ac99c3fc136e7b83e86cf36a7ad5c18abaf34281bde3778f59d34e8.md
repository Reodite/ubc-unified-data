**This constellation map highlights how we have connected rural high school students to colleges and universities across the province\. It shows that ReMBC is bringing college and university student mentors within reach of all BC rural high school students, no matter the distance\!**

#### Map Legend

-   ➖ Each line represents a connection between a college/university and a rural high school
    -   Each colour represents a different college/university\.
-   ⭐ Mentor College/University
-   ⚫ Mentee High School

#### Using the Map

-   Press \+ or − to zoom in and out, or scroll with your mouse wheel
-   Click and drag to move around the map
-   Press the play button to watch schools and connections appear over time
-   Click any glowing dot to learn more about that location
-   Click any line to learn more about the connection
-   Use the full\-screen button \(top left\) for the best viewing experience \(**strongly recommended\)**

New to the map? Check out our [Map Guide](https://ps-ementoringbc-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/ReMBC-Interactive-Map-Guide.pdf) for a full walkthrough of every button and feature\.

#### *Notes:*

-   N/A indicates data is either missing or hidden to protect anonymity\.
-   High schools not connected to a line: These mentors attended high school outside BC\. All mentors have a BC connection, though some now live out of province and continue to volunteer\.
-   Mentor schools not connected to a line: UBC MD distributed sites are not included in this version of the constellations, but they do appear on the mentor schools layer\. We hope to add them in a future update\.
