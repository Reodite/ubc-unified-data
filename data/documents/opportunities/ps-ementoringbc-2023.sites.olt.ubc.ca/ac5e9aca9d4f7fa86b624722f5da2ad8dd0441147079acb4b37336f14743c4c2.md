**Online mentoring for BC’s rural students**

We respectfully acknowledge that Rural eMentoring BC at UBC Vancouver is located on the traditional, ancestral and unceded territory of the [xʷməθkʷəy̓əm](https://www.musqueam.bc.ca/) \(Musqueam\), the [Tsleil\-Waututh](https://twnation.ca/) and the [Skwxwú7mesh](https://www.squamish.net/) \(Squamish\) peoples, and we thank them for allowing us to reside, learn, work and play on their traditional territories\.

## **At a glance**

**Rural eMentoring BC provides mentorship opportunities for rural students\. All of our offerings are provided free of charge\.**

[Image](https://ps-ementoringbc-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/High-school-940x505.jpg)

[High school program](https://ps-ementoringbc-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/high-school-students/ "Rural students grades 7-12 plan for their future with the guidance of a college or university student mentor. ")

[Image](https://ps-ementoringbc-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/Pre-health-940x534.jpg)

[Pre\-health undergraduate program](https://ps-ementoringbc-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/undergraduate-rural-pre-health-program/ "Rural-minded undergraduates interested in healthcare careers learn from senior students in the health professions programs of their choosing.")
