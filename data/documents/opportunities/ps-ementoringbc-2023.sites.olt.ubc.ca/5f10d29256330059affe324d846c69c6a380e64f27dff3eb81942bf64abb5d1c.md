## ReMBC hosts a variety of events throughout the year\. All are free to attend, and you don’t have to be a part of the program to join\!

# Upcoming events

### **Going Rural 2026\! **UBC Health and Rural eMentoring BC****\. **Exploring Careers in Rural Healthcare\.  
January 27, 2026**

Ready to shape the future of rural healthcare? *Going Rural 2026* brings students together with rural healthcare trainees, admissions teams, and professionals for an interactive, info\-packed evening\. Hosted in collaboration with UBC Health:

-   Discover real stories from rural students navigating pathways into medicine, nursing, pharmacy, dentistry, and more\. 
-   Learn how to strengthen your applications, explore interprofessional training in rural communities, and find out what it’s *actually* like to study or practice in a rural setting\. 
-   Connect directly with admissions reps and programs ready to support your journey\.

**When:** January 27, 2026 \| 5–8 PM Pacific Time \(doors open at 4:30\)

**Where:** ReMBC’s virtual Gather\.town website

**Who:** High school, college &amp; university students curious about rural healthcare
