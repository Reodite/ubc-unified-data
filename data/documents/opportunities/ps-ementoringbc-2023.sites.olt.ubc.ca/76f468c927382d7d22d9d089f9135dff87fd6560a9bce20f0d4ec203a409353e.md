eMentoring participants,

We are writing to let you know that we will be making some changes to the eMentoring platform in preparation for the next school year\. These changes are based on user feedback and will hopefully make your experience with the program easier and more enjoyable\.

Expect to see some changes starting late this week and early next week\. The changes are fairly straightforward, but at the bottom of each page you will find a “Need help” section that will have some tutorials explaining the changes\. We recommend at least viewing the “Online Platform Tutorial” document or video\.
