### **Invite a mentor to speak to your group**, Make a LIVE connection to real post\-secondary students\.  
**This is a great opportunity for high school careers classes, leadership classes, student clubs, or any other class or student group\!**

[Image](https://ps-ementoringbc-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/07/Virtual-Visits.jpg)

​

Choose from our [diverse pool of mentors](https://ps-ementoringbc-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/learn-about-our-ementors/)\. Our mentors are eager to share their experiences, give advice, and answer questions\. They can speak about the following topics, and much more:

-   How to be successful in high school
-   “Adulting” \(i\.e\. living independently\)
-   Moving away from home
-   Paying for school
-   Budgeting
-   How they chose their post\-secondary school and program
-   Comparing post\-secondary courses to high school courses
-   Life as a post\-secondary student
-   School\-work\-life balance
-   Preparing for a career
-   Choosing a career
-   Anything else\!

**You choose the format of the visit**: presentation, Q&amp;A, panel\-style, or anything else you can think of\.

You choose the virtual platform \(Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, etc\.\)

**To arrange a virtual mentor visit:**

[Contact us](https://ps-ementoringbc-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/contact-us/) with your preferred date and time\. We will share the opportunity with our mentor pool and provide you with the bios of the available mentors\. Choose the mentors you wish to invite and we will arrange the visit\. Please allow at least two weeks to arrange the visit\.
