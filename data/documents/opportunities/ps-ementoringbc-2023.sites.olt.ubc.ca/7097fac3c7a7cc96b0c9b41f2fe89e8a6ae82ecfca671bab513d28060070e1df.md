**ReMBC’s 10th anniversary is both a celebration and a moment for reflection\. This map highlights the impact of the three programs we’ve launched over the past decade and the communities they’ve reached along the way\.**

#### Map Legend

-   🟣 Rural Pre\-Health Program
-   🔵 Pathways to Healthcare Presentation
-   🟢 High School eMentoring Program and Virtual Visits

-   Press \+ or − to zoom in and out, or scroll with your mouse wheel
-   Click and drag to move around the map
-   Press the play button to watch schools appear over time
-   Click any glowing dot to learn more about that location
-   Use the full\-screen button \(top left\) for the best viewing experience \(**strongly recommended\)**
