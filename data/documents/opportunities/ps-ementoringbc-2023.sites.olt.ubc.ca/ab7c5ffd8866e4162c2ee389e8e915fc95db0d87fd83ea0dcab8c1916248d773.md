## Please fill out the Sign\-up form:

### **Have questions?  
  
Want more information?**

[**Contact Us**](mailto:e.mentoring@ubc.ca)

## **After you submit the form:**

**We will contact your parent/guardian/teacher:** to get their permission

​**Rural eMentoring BC team will set you up with an eMentoring account:** You will receive email instructions on how to login to our online platform, and set up your account\.

​**You will create a profile** as part of your account\. Fill out your profile completely, and with lots of detail–we use this to match you to your mentor\. 

​

**That’s it\!**

​

Once your profile is created, the ReMBC team will match you to a mentor based on stuff in your profile\. 

​When you’re matched, you will be able to see the activities, and send messages to your mentor\.
