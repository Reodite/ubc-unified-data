Master of Journalism September 2026 Admissions Information Session

### Join us on Wednesday, November 5th, at 5:30 PM PST for an information session on the admissions process for the UBC Master of Journalism’s September 2026 intake\.

The School of Journalism, Writing, and Media \(JWAM\) invites prospective applicants to an online information session to learn more about its renowned Master of Journalism program\. This is your opportunity to find out more about the program, ask questions, and meet faculty and students\. The session will cover most aspects of the degree, including but not limited to:

-   Admissions requirements and recommendations for crafting strong applications
-   An overview of the program structure over five terms
-   A glimpse into our mandatory and elective journalism courses covering digital media, AI, innovation, and community\-based, global, cultural, environmental and investigative reporting
-   Internship opportunities
-   Career paths and job options after graduation
-   Scholarships and entrance awards
-   Life as a graduate student at the prestigious University of British Columbia
