Mary Lynn Youn and Candis CallisonJoin professors [Candis Callison and Mary Lynn Young on Thursday September 3](http://sej.org/calendar/reckoning-journalism-s-limits-and-possibilities) for a discussion on how journalists and educators can act to change the culture of journalism and journalism education to embrace a more situated and systemic journalism\.

The discussion with the authors of [*Reckoning: Journalism’s Limits and Possi*bilities](https://oxford.universitypressscholarship.com/view/10.1093/oso/9780190067076.001.0001/oso-9780190067076) comes at a time when journalism faces a reckoning as it struggles to remain relevant to a global citizenry of increasing diversity and social awakening\.

Donovan Quintero, a reporter with the Navajo Times, is hosting the event, which is co\-sponsored by the Society of Environmental Journalists and The Earth Institute at Columbia University\.

Register for the [discussion on Zoom here](https://columbiauniversity.zoom.us/meeting/register/tJcuc--hpjMtEtbkHXx7WnhqaYbYSFKD24iH) on Thursday September 3 at 11 a\.m\. EST \(8 a\.m\. PST\)\.
