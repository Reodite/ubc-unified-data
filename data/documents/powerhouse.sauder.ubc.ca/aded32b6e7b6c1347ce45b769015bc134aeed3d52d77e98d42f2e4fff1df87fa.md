Extraordinary global issues are challenging our world, creating a need for bold thinking and fresh answers\. We need innovators from diverse backgrounds to collaborate and problem\-solve across disciplines to advance new ideas and technologies\. UBC Sauder is at the forefront of revolutionizing business education\. By increasing access to experiential learning and mentorship, UBC Sauder is bridging the gap from research lab to start\-up\.
