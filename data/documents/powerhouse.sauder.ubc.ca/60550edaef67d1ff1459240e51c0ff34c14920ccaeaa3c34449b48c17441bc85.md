[UBC Sauder](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/thinkoutside) is proud to announce the creation of the [Spitz Centre for Indigenous Business Education](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/indigenous), made possible by a transformative $5M gift from long\-time supporters and alumni, Warren and Maureen Spitz\. With additional funding from the school, the Centre will launch with combined support totaling $12M\. This landmark donation to the school marks a major step forward in fostering Indigenous student success, community economic development and reconciliation through business education\.
