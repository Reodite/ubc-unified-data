Increase capacity for climate\-focused business degrees, programs and research

The effects of climate change now impact environmental, societal and economic health around the globe\. The pace and scale of the climate emergency calls for rapid, meaningful action\. The world of business is in a prime position to make a tangible difference, but faces resource constraints and talent gaps that hinder progress\. UBC Sauder’s strength lies at the intersection of finance and innovation, with a commitment to accelerate the business transition towards a sustainable future through education, research and cross\-sector collaboration\.
