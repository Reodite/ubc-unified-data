We are located in room 1807 in the Kenny Building\!

The Kenny building is at 2136 West Mall at the University of British Columbia Main Campus \(Point Grey, Vancouver\)\. Dr\. Amori Mikami’s office is in room 1904 close to the lab\. These rooms are all on the first floor of the Kenny Building, on the southwest side of the building\.

Please see the map below; the red arrow points to our building\. The closest parking \(if you are driving or looking for a bike cage\) is the West Parkade\.

Some participants in our research are families from the community, while others are UBC students\. If you are coming from the community for an in\-person study visit, a staff member will meet you in front of the Kenny building\. There is parking for study participants coming from the community, and when you book an appointment a staff member will explain how you access it\.
