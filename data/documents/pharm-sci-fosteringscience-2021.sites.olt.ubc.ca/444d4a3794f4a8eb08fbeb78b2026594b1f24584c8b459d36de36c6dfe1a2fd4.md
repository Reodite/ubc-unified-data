## We believe that all youth should have equal opportunities to discover the wonder of science\.

But the reality is youths in or from care face many barriers to involvement in science, including: lack of opportunities, lack of mentors, lack of long\-term mentorship, lack of awareness of opportunities to participate in science fairs, and financial barriers to participate in science endeavours\.

[Image](https://pharm-sci-fosteringscience-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/05/experiment-explore-try-attempt-concept-73271449.jpeg)

[Image](https://pharm-sci-fosteringscience-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/05/istockphoto-1022573162-612x612-1.jpeg)

## Your generosity helps give youths a chance to foster an interest in science and engineering\.

Fostering Science is dedicated to motivate youth in or from care to study science and engineering and to pursue careers in these fields and to bring interactive, hands\-on science/engineering experiences to youth in or from care\. Your sponsorship will go towards covering the costs of the mentorship and participating in science fairs\.

[Image](https://pharm-sci-fosteringscience-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/05/What-does-yourdonation-mean-1-940x470.png)
