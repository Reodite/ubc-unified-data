The Ludmilla and Henry Zeldowicz Award is an endowment in the name of the late Drs\. Zeldowicz with the designation that be for “a resident or Fellow at the University of British Columbia in the neurosciences including psychiatry who has made significant contribution to a research project”\.

This award has been alternating between the Division of Neurology and the Department of Psychiatry\.
