Biography &amp; Research Interest:

Completed Undergraduate training at the University of Cape Town and Neurology residency training at UBC\. Fellowship training in Neuromuscular Disease/ALS was completed at VGH prior to joining the St\. Paul’s Hospital Neurology group in 1993\. Currently in General Neurology practice with an interest in Neuromuscular Disease and a consultant at St\. Paul’s Hospital\. Neurology Division Head at SPH/PHC since 2011\.

Member of American Epilepsy Society \(AES\) and Canadian Neurological Society Federation \(CNSF\) and Ex\-president of the Canadian Society of Clinical Neurophysiology \(CCNS\)\. He is the ex\- Canadian representative for the International Federation of Clinical Neurophysiology \(IFCN\)\.
