Biography &amp; Research Interest:

Dr Atkins is a neurologist with fellowship training in neuro\-ophthalmology\. He works in Victoria where his outpatient practice is focused on neuro\-ophthalmology\. He is an elected fellow of the North American Neuro\-Ophthalmology Society, with a research interest in disorders of the optic nerve\.
