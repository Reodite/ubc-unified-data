Dr\. Amir Parsoei

  
University of British Columbia – Division of Neurology – Clinical Instructor

Clinic: Lions Gate Hospital

### Biography:

I completed my undergraduate degree in human biology and neuroscience at UofT\. I then completed my medical school at the University of Saskatchewan in Saskatoon\. Finally I completed my Adult Neurology training in Vancouver, BC\. I have been working as a staff in North Vancouver and Lions Gate Hospital\.
