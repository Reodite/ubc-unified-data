Biography &amp; Research Interest:

Dr\. Hayden has been the clinical neuropsychologist in the Clinic for Alzheimer’s Disease and Related Disorder since 1993\.  She also has a private clinical neuropsychology practice focused upon assessment of traumatic brain injury\.  Dr\. Hayden’s research interests involve neuropsychological and anxiety issues in various populations including Mild Cognitive Impairment, Neurodegenerative Diseases, Traumatic Brain Injury, as well as Oncology Patients\.
