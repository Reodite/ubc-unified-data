Biography &amp; Research Interest:

Dr\. Joshua Lee is a Clinical Assistant Professor of Neurology at the University of British Columbia\. He is neurologist and researcher specializing in multiple sclerosis and related disorders\. He completed a PhD in Medical Genetics at the University of British Columbia, medical school at the University of Toronto, neurology residency at the University of British Columbia, and fellowship in Multiple Sclerosis and Neuroimmunology at Cedars\-Sinai Medical Center\. In addition to an active general neurology practice, Dr\. Lee’s clinical work includes multiple sclerosis and related neuroinflammatory disorders\. Dr\. Lee’s research program focuses on clinical application of advanced MRI biomarkers in MS and related disorders\. He is a member of the UBC MS MRI Research Group\. His translational research aims to better understand chronic active \(smouldering\) inflammation through neuroimaging to improve diagnosis, prognostication, and clinical decision\-making to optimize clinical outcomes for people living with these conditions\.
