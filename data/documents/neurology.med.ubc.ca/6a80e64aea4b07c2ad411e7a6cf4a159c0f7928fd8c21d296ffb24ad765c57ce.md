Dr\. Chetan Vekhande, MD, DM, FRCPC  
Specialty: Stroke and Movement Disorders  
Clinic: SRAU at Victoria General Hospital, PMDC at Royal Jubilee Hospital  
  

Biography and Research Interest:

I was born and raised in India, where I completed my early education, medical school, and neurology residency training\. My passion for neurology led me to Canada, where I pursued fellowship training in Stroke and Movement Disorders at the University of Alberta\.

Following my fellowship, I worked as an Assistant Professor at the University of Manitoba from 2023 to 2025\. In July 2025, I moved to Victoria, British Columbia, where I continue my clinical work in neurology\.

My professional interests include stroke neurology, movement disorders, and medical education\. I am passionate about providing compassionate, evidence\-based care to my patients while contributing to the training and development of future healthcare professionals\.

Outside of medicine, I enjoy hiking, playing the Casio keyboard, and traveling\. I find that exploring nature, music, and experiencing different cultures helps me maintain balance and brings fresh perspectives to both my personal and professional life\.

I am grateful for the opportunities I have had to train and work across different healthcare systems, and I remain committed to lifelong learning, patient care, and advancing the field of neurology\.
