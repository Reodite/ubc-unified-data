**Biography &amp; Research Interest:** 

Dr\. Spacey is a Clinical Associate Professor in the Division of Neurology at UBC\.  She completed a residency in Neurology at UBC and a fellowship in Neurogenetics at Queens Square, London England\.  She is Director of the UBC Headache Clinic and UBC Neurogenetics Clinic\.  She is past president of the Canadian Headache Society and Director of the UBC Clinician Investigator Program\.   She lectures nationally and interntionally\.  Her research interest and publications are in the field of calcium channels and the pathogenesis and molecular genetics of migraine\.
