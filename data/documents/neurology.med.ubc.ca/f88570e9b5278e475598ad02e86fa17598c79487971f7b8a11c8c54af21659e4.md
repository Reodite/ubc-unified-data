This award has been made available through an endowment established in honour of Dr\. Stephen D\. Clarke for a third or fourth year Neurology resident who demonstrates strong clinical and communication skills as well as commitment to and strength in teaching\. The award will be used for a Neurology resident to attend a conference related to the recipient’s interest in Neuroscience or medical education\.

  
This award was created in honour of Dr\. Clarke, a masterful clinician and a dedicated and consummate teacher for the Division of Neurology at St\. Paul Hospital between 1985 to 2014\. The award is made on the recommendation of the Neurology Awards Committee\.
