Clinical Assistant Professor  
  
Clinic: 1108\-750 W Broadway  
Vancouver, BC V5Z 1J1

Dr\. Chen completed medical school at the University of Manitoba, followed by Adult Neurology Residency Training at the University of British Columbia where he remains on faculty as Clinical Assistant Professor\. His academic interests include medical education where he serves as Associate Program Director for the Adult Neurology Residency Program, as well as inpatient acute neurology with several publications including case reports and clinical teaching cases\.
