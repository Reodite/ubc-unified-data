Biography &amp; Research Interest:

Dr\. Sina Marzoughi completed his medical degree at the University of Calgary and Adult Neurology residency at the University of British Columbia\. He currently practices general neurology with a subspecialty focus in headache medicine\. His clinical and academic interests include the diagnosis and management of primary headache disorders, such as migraine and cluster headache\.

Dr\. Marzoughi is actively involved in medical education and research, with a focus on advancing the understanding and care of headache conditions\.
