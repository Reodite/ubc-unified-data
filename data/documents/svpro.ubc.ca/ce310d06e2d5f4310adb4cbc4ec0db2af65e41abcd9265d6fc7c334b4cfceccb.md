#### There are many ways for students to be involved with SVPRO and create culture change\.

If you are a faculty or staff member, see [ways to take action](https://scs-svpro-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/faculty-and-staff/)\.

### Get involved with SVPRO

-   If you are seeking consultation for your department, team, or group, please email [**svpro\.vancouver@ubc\.ca**](mailto:svpro.vancouver@ubc.ca)
-   Become part of the SVPRO community by following us on [**Instagram**](https://www.instagram.com/ubc.svpro/) where you can learn about our services, access educational resources, and find upcoming events\.

### Additional actions

-   Create a policy or code of conduct for your student group or society with support from SVPRO\. Email [**svpro\.vancouver@ubc\.ca**](mailto:svpro.vancouver@ubc.ca)
-   Take the time to learn about accountability for actions that have caused harm\. Resources are available at SVPRO\.
-   Share SVPRO campaigns in your space online\. Email [**svpro\.vancouver@ubc\.ca**](mailto:svpro.vancouver@ubc.ca) to request digital or physical resources\.
-   Take SVPRO’s Informational module on Canvas, “[**Intro to Sexual Violence Prevention &amp; Response**](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/KJJXG6)“, and contact SVPRO for follow\-up steps\.
