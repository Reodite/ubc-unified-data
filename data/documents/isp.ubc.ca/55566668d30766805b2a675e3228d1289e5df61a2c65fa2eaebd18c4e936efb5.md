For Cycle 4: 2024\-2025, Indigenous Strategic Initiatives \(ISI\) funding is allocated across two streams\. Project teams applying to the Faculty and Staff led Projects Stream are required to have completed the [ISP Self\-Assessment Tool](https://oisi-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/implementation/implementation-tools-resources/#isp-self-assessment-tool) at the Letter of Intent \(LOI\) stage\. Project teams applying to the Student led Projects Stream are exempt\.

## Faculty and Staff Led Projects

### Funding:

#### $30k – $100k

### Project Length:

#### 12 – 24 months

### Available To:

#### Faculty, staff and postdoctoral fellows

The faculty and staff led projects stream provides funding for existing projects or seed funding for new initiatives that advance the faculty or unit’s Indigenous strategy, achievement and enhancement goals and advance any of the 43 Actions of the Indigenous Strategic Plan \(ISP\)\.

These projects will have an impact at the department, unit or faculty level with the potential to scale up in the future\. Cost\-shared initiatives are welcomed\. Eligible applicants are staff, faculty members and post\-docs at either UBC campus\.

[Learn More](https://oisi-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/take-action/isi-fund/funding-streams/stream-1-innovative-projects/)

## **Student Led Projects**

## Funding:

#### $25k

## Project Length:

#### 12 – 18 months

## Available To:

#### Students \(undergraduate or graduate\)

The student led projects stream provides funding for student led initiatives that advance any of the 43 Actions of the Indigenous Strategic Plan \(ISP\) and have impact at any level, including unit, faculty, department, institution or other\.

All undergraduate or graduate students are eligible to apply at either UBC campus\.

[Learn More](https://oisi-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/stream-3-student-led-projects/)
