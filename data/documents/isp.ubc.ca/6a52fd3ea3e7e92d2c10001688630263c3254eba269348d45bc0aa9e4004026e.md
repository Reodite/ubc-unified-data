First Indigenous Strategic Initiative \(ISI\) Fund Showcase On November 12, 2025, UBC hosted its first Indigenous Strategic Initiative \(ISI\) Fund Showcase\.   ISI Fund Project Teams, including Staff, Faculty, Students, and Community Partners, along with Senior Leaders and Indigenous Strategic Plan Guiding Network Members, gathered together on the Point Grey Campus for day full of connection, dialogue, and inspiration\.   \[…\]
