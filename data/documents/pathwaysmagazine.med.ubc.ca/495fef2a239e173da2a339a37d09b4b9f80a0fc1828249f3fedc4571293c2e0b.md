## A day in the life at the Conconi Family BioDevice Foundry

Meet Sofia Graham Arshavsky, Michael Smith Health Research BC and Conconi Family Foundation BioDevice Innovation Fellow in the UBC School of Biomedical Engineering\.

At the [Conconi Family BioDevice Foundry](https://bme.ubc.ca/biodevice-foundry/), the next generation of biomedical engineers and scientists are creating highly innovative biodevices to solve research and clinical problems, under the guidance and mentorship of faculty and staff\. From point\-of\-care diagnostic tools to organ\-on\-a\-chip therapeutic testing, these devices are transforming how we detect, monitor and treat disease\.

Follow along as Sofia takes us through a day at the Foundry\.
