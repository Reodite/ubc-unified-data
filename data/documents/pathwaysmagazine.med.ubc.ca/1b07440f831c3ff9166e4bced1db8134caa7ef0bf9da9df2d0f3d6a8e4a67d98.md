## As one of the world’s top medical schools, the University of British Columbia Faculty of Medicine is revolutionizing health at home and across the globe\.

## Here are just a few of the many ways the Faculty is creating a healthier future through innovation in education, training and research\.  
  
**[Printable PDF version](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/20_Things_Pathways.pdf)**

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/ByNumbers_WC_rule_450x8.png)

## 1

#### A national leader in medical education

UBC was among the first in North America to distribute and deliver its fully accredited medical education and training programs at regional campuses and communities across the province\. With significant investment from the Government of B\.C\., UBC recently welcomed the largest MD class in its history, with **328** first\-year medical students

## 2

#### Province\-wide impact through partnership

Together with our academic, health and community partners, UBC trains more than **5,000** learners each year across [**145\+** training sites](https://www.med.ubc.ca/about/campuses/) in B\.C\. — embedding students in communities where care is needed most and inspiring many to practice where they train

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/3_FOM_Icon.png)

## 3

#### Canada’s largest family doctor training program

Every year, the Faculty of Medicine trains **205** first\-year UBC family medicine resident doctors in urban, rural, remote and Indigenous communities across B\.C\.

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/4_FOM_Icon.png)

## 4

#### Dedicated to Truth and Reconciliation with Indigenous Peoples

We are advancing the commitments outlined in the [UBC Faculty of Medicine’s Response](https://www.med.ubc.ca/about/indigenous-health/) to the Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Canada’s Calls to Action

## 5

#### Global leader in medicine and life sciences

UBC is consistently ranked among the world’s top medical schools according to QS World University Rankings and the Times Higher Education

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/1_FOM_Icon.png)

## 6

#### Investing in student health and wellbeing

UBC and the Faculty provide a wide range of integrated resources and supports — including health and counselling services — to enhance student wellbeing at every stage of their journey

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/5_FOM_Icon.png)

## 7

#### UBC grads stay and practice where they train

A growing number of UBC MD grads who completed residency training at UBC are staying in B\.C\. to practice — including **95%** of family medicine residents and **98%** of emergency medicine residents

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/7_FOM_Icon.png)

## 8

#### Strengthening B\.C\.’s healthcare workforce

UBC trains [physical therapists](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/ubc-expands-two-health-profession-programs-to-vancouver-island/), [occupational therapists](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/ubc-welcomes-health-profession-students-to-new-training-facility-in-surrey/) and [midwives](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/graduating-more-midwives-for-b-c/) in regions across the province — with **95%** or more of graduates now practicing in B\.C\.

## 9

#### Graduating more Indigenous doctors

The Indigenous MD Admissions Pathway is inspiring more Indigenous youth to pursue medicine with [**162** Indigenous medical graduates](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/walking-the-path-to-medicine-together/) since 2006

## 10

#### One of North America’s largest residency programs

Each year, UBC welcomes [**425** first\-year resident doctors](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/celebrating-ubc-resident-doctors-2026/) — the second largest intake in the country — across **76** specialty and subspecialty programs including surgery, emergency medicine, neurology and more

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/6_FOM_Icon.png)

## 11

#### Nearly 13,000 UBC clinical faculty across B\.C\.

Every day, [UBC clinical faculty](https://pathwaysmagazine.med.ubc.ca/feature-stories/a-day-in-the-life-of-ubc-clinical-faculty/) teach and mentor the next generation of doctors, health professionals and researchers at more than **145** training sites province\-wide

## 12

#### Accelerating lifesaving research for patients

Each day, **2,472** graduate students and postdoctoral fellows are training alongside faculty and [contributing to new discoveries and translating research](https://pathwaysmagazine.med.ubc.ca/snapshots/training-locally-building-research-capacity-for-bc-and-the-world/) into innovative health solutions in areas like cancer, brain health, public health and more

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/12_FOM_Icon.png)

## 13

#### Training the next generation of biomedical innovators

UBC is training highly skilled talent to power B\.C\.’s life sciences sector — the fastest growing in Canada — through initiatives like [Canada’s Immuno\-Engineering and Biomanufacturing Hub](https://immunoengineeringhub.ca/) and the [School of Biomedical Engineering](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/ubc-opens-gordon-b-shrum-building/), a unique partnership between the UBC Faculties of Medicine and Applied Science

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/13_FOM_Icon.png)

## 14

#### Enhancing rural and remote care

Innovative programs such as the [Rural Immersion Family Medicine Program](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/ubc-faculty-of-medicine-launches-new-rural-family-medicine-residency-program/) give UBC medical students and resident doctors hands\-on training in rural and northern communities such as Daajing Giids, Duncan, Trail and more

## 15

#### Growing a diverse community of healthcare practitioners

Programs like the [Black Student MD Admissions Pathway](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/faculty-of-medicine-launches-new-admissions-pathway-for-black-medical-students/) and [Indigenous Internal Medicine Residency Pathway](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/snapshots/bringing-an-indigenous-perspective-to-internal-medicine/) are making medical education and training more accessible to students from underrepresented groups

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/15_FOM_Icon.png)

## 16

#### Future\-ready micro\-credential training

Equipping the healthcare workforce with critical skills on everything from AI, [health data analytics](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/programs-credentials/health-data-analytics-opportunities-applications-microcertificate) and [innovation leadership](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/programs-credentials/innovation-leadership-medical-bio-innovations-microcertificate) to [regulatory affairs](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/programs-credentials/regulatory-affairs-life-sciences-microcertificate) in the life sciences\.

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/16_FOM_Icon.png)

## 17

#### Clinical placements that expand team\-based care

From Prince Rupert to [Surrey](https://education.pathways.med.ubc.ca/snapshots/expanding-patient-care-through-student-led-clinics/) and beyond, UBC health professions students train side by side with health partners in student\-led clinics, expanding access to team\-based care for patients and communities

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/17_FOM_Icon.png)

## 18

#### Delivering lifelong learning to more than 40,000 health professionals each year

From best practices in cancer care to innovations in rural practice, UBC [Continuing Professional Development](https://ubccpd.ca/) delivers evidence\-based learning activities for practicing health professionals across B\.C\., Canada and the world including in India, Australia and Nigeria

## 19

#### Innovating health education through simulation

Together with health partners, UBC Faculty of Medicine students are preparing for real world practice through 1,800\+ simulation training hours per year across the province, using high\-fidelity mannequins, virtual reality and standardized patient programs

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/19_FOM_Icon.png)

## 20

#### UBC grads are shaping the next generation of health practitioners

A growing number of UBC alumni now serve as UBC faculty and clinical faculty, [mentoring future doctors and health professionals](https://pathwaysmagazine.med.ubc.ca/feature-stories/a-day-in-the-life-of-ubc-clinical-faculty/) while delivering exceptional care to patients and communities

[Image](https://med-fom-pathways-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/20_FOM_Icon.png)
