## A day in the life of a postdoctoral fellow

Meet Loay Al\-Salehi, postdoctoral fellow in the [Orthopaedic and Injury Biomechanics Group](https://injury.mech.ubc.ca/)\. A dedicated mentor to undergraduate and graduate students, he’s passionate about training the next generation of scientists and biomedical engineers\.

Follow along as Loay takes us through a day at the School of Biomedical Engineering\.
