**Title:** Professor and Research Lead  
**Program:** Northern Medical Program  
**Location:** Prince George

***What does it mean to you to help train and graduate more doctors and health professionals for patients and communities across B\.C\.?***

In my community, training the next generation of health professionals is part of a critical and ongoing effort to achieve health equity for communities in northern B\.C\. It’s urgent work, because these health disparities are significant — and due in large part to inequitable access to health services and professionals\.
