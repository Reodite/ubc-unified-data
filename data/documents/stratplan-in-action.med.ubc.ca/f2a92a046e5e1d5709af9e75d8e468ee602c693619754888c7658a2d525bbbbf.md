**Title:** Co\-Director  
**Program:** Master of Science in Genetic Counselling  
**Location:** Vancouver

***What does it mean to you to help train and graduate more health professionals for patients and communities across B\.C\.?***

The reality is that British Columbia needs more genetic counsellors and services\. We are excited to be launching our first distributed cohort of students in Victoria, and see this as an important step toward increasing equitable access to genetics and genomics care in the communities that we serve\.
