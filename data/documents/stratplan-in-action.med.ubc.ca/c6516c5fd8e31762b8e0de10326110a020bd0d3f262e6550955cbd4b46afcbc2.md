**Title:** Senior Director, Education Programs and Services  
**Program:** Office of the Vice Dean, Education, UBC Faculty of Medicine  
**Location:** Vancouver

***What does it mean to you to help train and graduate more doctors and health professionals for patients and communities across B\.C\.?***

For me, it’s about making a meaningful difference, by fostering a collaborative, inclusive and driven environment where individuals are encouraged, empowered and supported to achieve excellence\. I have been a part of the Faculty of Medicine for over 25 years and there is a strong sense of ownership among team members**\.** Everyone strives to be the best they can be in supporting our learners\. It’s truly inspiring to see such cohesion and such a strong commitment to cultivating a positive and inclusive culture that positions programs and service delivery teams well for continued success — whether curriculum renewal or the implementation and expansion of our medical and health professions programs\. I am proud to be a part of these incredible teams that are helping to train and graduate more doctors and health professionals in the province of B**\.**C\. Together, we are truly transforming health for everyone\.
