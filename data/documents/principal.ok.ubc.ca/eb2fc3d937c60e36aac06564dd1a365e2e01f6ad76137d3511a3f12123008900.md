Dear Colleagues,

I am writing to express my heartfelt thanks to each of you who volunteered your time and energy to make the Class of 2026 graduation ceremonies on June 4 and 5 such a memorable success\. Convocation is one of the most meaningful moments in the life of our university, and it happens only because so many of you step forward, year after year, to welcome our graduates and their families to the UBC Okanagan campus\.

I want to extend particular gratitude to Suann Brown, Erin Podmorow and Liz King Osadczuk, whose leadership and careful planning set the tone for the entire celebration\. Their dedication, alongside the tireless work of the University Relations team, ensured that every detail came together seamlessly and that our graduates were celebrated with the dignity and joy this milestone deserves\.

A celebration of this scale is truly a campus\-wide effort, and I would also like to recognize the many teams whose contributions were felt throughout the two days\. Our grounds and facilities teams ensured the campus looked absolutely beautiful, providing a stunning backdrop that our graduates and their families will remember for years to come\. Our colleagues in IT made it possible for loved ones near and far to share the moment through their seamless support for the live stream\. Our Alumni Relations team offered our graduates a warm and fitting welcome into the community they now join\. Each of these contributions, together with so many others, made the celebration what it was\.

To all of our volunteers and to the broader campus community who lent a hand – thank you\. Your warmth, professionalism, and generosity of spirit are what make our ceremonies so special, and they reflect the very best of who we are as a campus\. The pride our graduates and their families felt over those two days is a direct result of your collective effort\.

It is a privilege to work alongside such a committed and caring community\. Thank you for all that you do, and for once again helping us send our newest alumni into the world with such a fitting send\-off\.

Sincerely,

Lesley Cormack  
Principal and Deputy Vice\-Chancellor  
University of British Columbia, Okanagan campus
