The Principal chairs and participates in a number of committees and advisory groups, both internal and external, as part of UBC Okanagan’s shared leadership model and to gather advice on key administrative decisions for the campus\.

### Okanagan Leadership Council

The Okanagan Leadership Council \(OLC\) is the senior advisory body to the Principal and Deputy Vice\-Chancellor on strategic direction, resource prioritization and policy decisions for UBC’s Okanagan campus\. It is a key table as part of UBC Okanagan’s shared leadership structure\.

### Indigenous Advisory Committee

The Indigenous Advisory Committee considers the current state of Aboriginal activities on UBC’s Okanagan campus, identifies priority areas of focus for the future, and helps implement strategies to advance these initiatives\.

Current priorities for the Committee include development of a professional development program for Indigenous awareness and cultural sensitivity, establishment of an Aboriginal mentorship program for staff and faculty through an Indigenous Caucus, and Indigenous curriculum development\.

### President’s Advisory Committee on Student Discipline

Pursuant to the University Act, the President of the university has the power to suspend a student and to deal summarily with any matter of student discipline\. Student discipline at UBC’s Okanagan campus is governed by the [Student Discipline section](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/okanagan/index.cfm?tree=3,54,0,0) of the UBC Okanagan Calendar\.

The President’s Advisory Committees on Student Discipline advise the President in his decisions with respect to academic discipline by impartially considering allegations of misconduct against students in light of relevant evidence presented\.

#### Structure

Cases that involve Okanagan campus students will be heard by The President’s Advisory Committee on Student Discipline \(Okanagan\); committee membership includes faculty and student appointees from the Okanagan campus\. The Chair provides recommendations to the President based on deliberations of the committee following a hearing\.

#### Okanagan Campus Membership

Membership of the President’s Advisory Committee on Student Discipline \(Okanagan\) can be found [here](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/discipline/academic-misconduct/)\.

Please consult the website of the Office of the University Counsel for further information on the [academic misconduct process](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/discipline/academic-misconduct/), including rules and forms related to President’s Advisory Committee on Student Discipline\.

### UBC Okanagan Regional Leadership Table

The UBCO Regional Leadership Table was established in 2023 as a successor to the UBCO External Advisory Council and, previously, the UBC President’s Community Advisory Council\. It serves as a gathering place for senior Okanagan leaders where topics of wide\-ranging economic, social and cultural significance to the Okanagan region can be explored and addressed\.

The UBCO RLT provides critical opportunities for connection between the university and community leaders and creates space for dialogue and knowledge exchange\. Members help ensure that the constituencies and regions they represent are aware of UBC’s academic, research and community engagement endeavours and, likewise, raise opportunities for the university to connect with their constituencies\.

The UBCO RLT Terms of Reference can be found [here](https://principal.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/101/2023/07/UBCO-Regional-Leadership-Table-Terms-of-Reference.pdf)\.
