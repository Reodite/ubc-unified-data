Dear colleagues,

The ongoing regional wildfires have had a significant impact on students, faculty, staff and the broader community — through evacuations, displacement and smoke\-related health concerns\. For many, the effects will be felt long after the immediate emergency has passed, and recovery will take time\.

I have been deeply moved by how this community has responded\. Thank you to everyone who has reached out to ask how they can help and to colleagues across Housing and Community Services, Facilities, Operations and Emergency Management who have worked long hours to keep our campus running, supporting those who needed somewhere to go and our firefighters\. It is in trying times like these that we see the very best of UBC Okanagan, and I am heartened by how you continue to show up for one another and for our neighbours\.

Many of you have asked how you might support the wider recovery effort\. One option is [United Way BC’s Wildfire Recovery Fund](https://uwbc.ca/campaign/wildfires/)\. Working through local partnerships, the fund provides emergency assistance, recovery supports and resources that help communities rebuild and build resilience for increasingly frequent wildfire seasons\. United Way BC has deep, long\-standing relationships with local agencies, community organizations, First Nations and emergency response partners across British Columbia\. Those relationships allow them to identify emerging needs quickly and direct funding where it can have the greatest impact\. Donations made through this campaign stay in the region, supporting Okanagan residents and communities affected by this summer’s fires\.

If you are in a position to contribute, you can make a one\-time donation through [the fund](https://uwbc.ca/campaign/wildfires/), and you’ll receive a charitable tax receipt\. If giving is not possible right now, particularly if you or your family are among those directly affected, please know there is no expectation\. Caring for yourself and for each other matters just as much\.

As a reminder, for the most current information, please follow the [BC Wildfire Service](https://wildfiresituation.nrs.gov.bc.ca/dashboard) and [EmergencyInfoBC](https://www.emergencyinfobc.gov.bc.ca/)\. Evacuees can register for Emergency Support Services through the BC Services Card app or at [ess\.gov\.bc\.ca](https://ess.gov.bc.ca/)\. Additional resources for affected UBC Okanagan students, faculty and staff are available in my [previous post](https://principal.ok.ubc.ca/2026/08/04/regionalwildfiresandresources/)\.

Sincerely,

**Dr\. Lesley Cormack**  
Principal and Deputy Vice\-Chancellor  
UBC Okanagan
