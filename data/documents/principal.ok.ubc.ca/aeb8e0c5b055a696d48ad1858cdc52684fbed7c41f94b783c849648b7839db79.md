Dear colleagues,

Many of us are watching wildfires in the region with real concern\. Some members of our community have been placed on evacuation alert or order, others are worried about family, friends and neighbours in the Okanagan\.

At the same time, I want to reassure our community that campus is open and operating as usual\. Employees are expected to report to work as normally scheduled\. There is currently no threat to UBCO, and we do not anticipate any disruption to classes, research or services\.

If you are affected, support is available\.

[Emergency funding](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/courses-money-enrolment/finances/financial-support/emergency-funding/) is available for students facing urgent financial pressure due to wildfire or other exceptional circumstances\. Counselling and mental health support is available through [Student Wellness](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/health-wellness/counselling-mental-health/)\. If the situation is affecting your coursework, please connect with your instructor and see the guidance on [support for unanticipated circumstances](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/support/support-for-unanticipated-circumstances/)\.

If you are faculty or staff, please stay in touch with your supervisor or department head if you are evacuated, displaced or facing disruption to your work\. For supervisors, should you have questions on how to support impacted employees, your [HR Advisor](https://hr.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact-us/advisors/) can assist\. In the meantime, please take it upon yourself to check in on the safety and wellbeing of the people on your team and do not hesitate to connect with HR or leadership for support\. Confidential counselling is available at any time through the [Employee and Family Assistance Program](https://hr.ubc.ca/benefits/benefit-plan-details/employee-and-family-assistance-program)\.

I also want to acknowledge the work happening quietly on our own campus\. UBC Okanagan is collaborating with local emergency services to provide housing for evacuees and wildfire personnel as requested\. I am enormously appreciative of the residence, facilities and emergency management teams who made that possible on short notice\. It is a small contribution next to what firefighters and first responders are doing, or to the unimaginable stress faced by those evacuated, but I’m grateful that we can offer a safe harbour in trying times\.

For the most current information, please follow the [BC Wildfire Service](https://wildfiresituation.nrs.gov.bc.ca/dashboard) and [EmergencyInfoBC](https://www.emergencyinfobc.gov.bc.ca/)\. Evacuees can register for Emergency Support Services through the BC Services Card app or at [ess\.gov\.bc\.ca](https://ess.gov.bc.ca/)\.

Sincerely,

**Dr\. Lesley Cormack**  
Principal and Deputy Vice\-Chancellor  
UBC Okanagan
