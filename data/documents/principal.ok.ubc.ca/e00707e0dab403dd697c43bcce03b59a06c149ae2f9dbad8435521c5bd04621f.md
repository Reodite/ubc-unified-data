Listen to Debroah Buszard as she discusses her experiences as a woman in STEM on this podcast from WWEST – from being one of three female students when she first began her studies agriculture to being the first female dean of agriculture to receiving an honourary degree from UBC\.

The podcast also features two other incredible women from the UBC Okanagan community sharing their experiences in STEM: Dr\. Bowen Hui, instructor in Computer Science; and Dr\. Mina Hoorfar, professor of Electrical &amp; Mechanical Engineering\.
