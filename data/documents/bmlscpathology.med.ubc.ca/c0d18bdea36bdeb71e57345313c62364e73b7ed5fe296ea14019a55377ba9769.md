About the program

# The *BMLSc* at UBC

An undergraduate science degree built around the laboratory\. Four ways in — point at one to open it\.

What the degree is

An undergraduate science degree that integrates theoretical knowledge with practical lab experience\.

UBC’s Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Science teaches students the disciplines behind the work of hospital and research laboratories — and gives them the tools to meaningfully engage in real\-world scientific research and biotechnology\.
