BMLSc – Small Intestine

At the conclusion of their studies, graduates will have fulfilled most of the prerequisites for application to medical, dental or graduate school\.

The following degree requirements are taken as a [Standard Timetable](https://med-fom-bmlscpathology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/05/BMLSc-3rd-Year-Timetable-2026W.pdf)\.

For full course descriptions, visit  [UBC’s Academic Calendar](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/course-descriptions/subject/pathv)\.

### PATH\_V 438 Directed Studies \(6cr\)

The objective of PATH\_V 438 is to provide students with experience in research\. Research projects are carried out in the laboratory of a Faculty member of the Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine who acts as a supervisor\. The student is expected to participate in the design of the project as well as perform experimental manipulations and data analysis\.

To learn more about PATH\_V 438 Directed studies visit [https://pathology\.ubc\.ca/pathology\-438/](https://pathology.ubc.ca/pathology-438/)
