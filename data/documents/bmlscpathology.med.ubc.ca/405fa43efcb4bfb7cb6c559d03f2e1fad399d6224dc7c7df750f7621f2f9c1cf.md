Your degree, many possibilities

Whether you’re exploring options or aiming at a specific goal — health professional, researcher, industry leader — the BMLSc provides the foundation to pursue any number of paths\.

Our graduates have become doctors, research scientists, laboratory directors, and entrepreneurs\. The hands\-on training and scientific foundation you gain here prepares you for success in whatever direction you choose\.

Graduates who move directly into careers are predominantly employed as research assistants or technicians in academic, clinical, or industry laboratories\. Some are hired by biotechnology or pharmaceutical companies as technicians, sales representatives, or technical support\. A few examples:

-   Associate Histotechnologist, Wax\-it Histology
-   Research Coordinator, Hematology Research Program, UBC / VGH
-   Clinical Research Assistant, BC Centre for Excellence in HIV/AIDS
-   Senior Program Coordinator, Social Diversity for Children Foundation
-   Research Assistant / Technician 2, Centre for Molecular Medicine &amp; Therapeutics
-   Client Services Associate, ALS Laboratories
