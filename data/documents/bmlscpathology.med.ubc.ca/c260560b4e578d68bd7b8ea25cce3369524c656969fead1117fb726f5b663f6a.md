# Tuition &amp; *financial support*

Like many undergraduate programs at UBC, BMLSc tuition is calculated on a **per\-credit basis** — so what you pay each year follows your credit load\. Because the BMLSc runs on a fixed Standard Timetable, that load is already set for you\.

The cost model

## How your tuition is worked out

Domestic and international rates differ, and they’re reviewed each year\. Student Services publishes the current figures\.
