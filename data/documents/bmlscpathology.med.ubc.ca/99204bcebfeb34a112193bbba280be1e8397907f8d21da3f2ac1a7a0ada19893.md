Prospective Students

# How to *apply*

Applicants must meet UBC’s general admission requirements and have completed — or be in the process of completing — the [prerequisite courses](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/home-page/prospective-students/entrance-requirements/) by April of the year they’re applying for September entry\.

## The application year

Four dates to know

Applications open

Early October

The UBC application opens for the following September’s entry\.

Deadline

January 15

Applications and supporting documents are due\.

Prerequisites done

April

All prerequisite courses must be complete\. You may apply while still finishing them\.

Decisions

Early–mid May

Applicants are contacted; status is visible any time in the ASC\.

Successful applicants begin the BMLSc at **Year 3 in September**\.

The process

## Four steps, start to submit

Work through the first three before the deadline, then submit through the route that matches your situation\.

1

Confirm you meet the entrance requirements

Make sure you’ve met all of the [entrance requirements](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/home-page/prospective-students/entrance-requirements/) and have completed — or are in the process of completing — the prerequisite courses\.

2

Gather your supporting documents

Collect all supporting documentation, including official transcripts, submitted either by mail or as e\-transcripts from participating BC institutions\. [Document submission details](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/applied/submit-documents/#canadian_university_or_college_transfer_student)\.

Current UBC students don’t need to provide UBC transcripts\.

3

Prepare to pay the application fee

Make arrangements to pay the non\-refundable UBC application fee when you submit\.

4

Submit through your route

How you apply depends on your current relationship with UBC — choose one of the three below\.
