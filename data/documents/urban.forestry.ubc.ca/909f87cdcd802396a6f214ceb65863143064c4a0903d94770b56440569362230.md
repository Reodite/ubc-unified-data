Reed is an MSc student in the Urban Natures Lab studying urban forestry and climate resilience\. They completed their BSc in Biology at McGill University\. Their research focuses on assessing climate risk in urban forests and examining how tree planting and tree care impact the climate resilience of vulnerable communities\.

In their free time, Reed loves running and cycling around Vancouver, spending time in nature, and cooking\.
