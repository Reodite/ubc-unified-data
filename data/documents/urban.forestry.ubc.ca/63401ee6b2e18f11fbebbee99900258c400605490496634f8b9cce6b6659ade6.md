Bio

Open Close

Josefina is a PhD student working with Professor Lorien Nesbitt, and is part of the Urban Nature Lab\. Her convictions and interests are in connecting research, education and action, with a focus on participatory and transdisciplinary approaches\. Her research centers on the biocultural strategies and relationship with nature held by Indigenous peoples and local communities in urban ecosystems\. Her roots are in Chile, where she has spent the past eight years conducting research\-action projects\. Josefina is also a member of ECOS co\-laboratory, an interdisciplinary research team dedicated to study complex social ecological systems in a diverse and ancestral territory\.

Her roots are in Chile, attached to the southern Andes\. Beyond studies and work, Josefina enjoys visiting the forest, food markets and stadiums\. She is passionate about soccer, novel books, birds, and edible plants\. Even better if it is with her friends\.
