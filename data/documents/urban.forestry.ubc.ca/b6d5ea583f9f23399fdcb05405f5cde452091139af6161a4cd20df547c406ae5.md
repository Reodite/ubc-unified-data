Bio

Open Close

Lucas is a MSc student working at on topics related to urban greening, community engagement, and recognitional justice\. His research in the Urban Natures Lab explores how a growing understanding of underrepresented stewardship practices of urban green spaces can lead to more equitable neighborhood outcomes\. Before joining UBC, Lucas spent five years in Philadelphia’s nonprofit sector supporting community\-led grantmaking for grassroots community organizing\.  
  
When not conducting research, Lucas can be found cooking vegetarian, organizing men around issues of masculinity, playing ultimate frisbee, and tackling long\-distance hiking trails\.
