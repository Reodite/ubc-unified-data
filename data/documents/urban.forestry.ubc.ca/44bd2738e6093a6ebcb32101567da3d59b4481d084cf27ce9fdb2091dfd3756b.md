Bio

Open Close

Aubrey is passionate about all things related to plants, people, and their interactions with each other\. She completed her MSc in the Urban Ecology Lab in September 2024 and has since continued working on several projects under the supervision of Dr\. McHale and Dr\. Devisscher\. Her current research focuses on how private and public land managers can leverage urban forestry practices to mitigate extreme summertime temperatures\.

In her spare time, Aubrey enjoys exploring the city and the mountains\.
