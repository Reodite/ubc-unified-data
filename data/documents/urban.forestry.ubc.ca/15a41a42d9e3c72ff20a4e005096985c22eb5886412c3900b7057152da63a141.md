Bio

Open Close

Nuala earned her BSc from McGill University, where she studied Environmental Biology\. She found her passion for urban ecology through her Honors research, where she studied urban carnivores\. Under Dr\. McHale and in collaboration with the City of Kelowna, she is now studying the intersections between urban forestry, ecology, and policy\.

Outside of research, Nuala has far too many hobbies\. She is a member of UBC A Capella, a very casual figure skater, and enjoys various other activities including reading, drawing, hiking, bird watching, and playing Dungeons &amp; Dragons\.
