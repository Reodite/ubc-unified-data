Bio

Open Close

Reagan is an MSc student under the supervision of Dr\. Melissa McHale\. She received her BSc in conservation biology from North Carolina State University\. Her master’s research aims to promote equitable urban forest expansion by integrating geospatial, socioeconomic, and infrastructural analyses in the assessment of tree planting feasibility in Kelowna, BC\. Reagan has a passion for and background in environmental justice advocacy and community organizing\.

In her free time, Reagan loves reading, photography, hiking, and traveling near and far\.
