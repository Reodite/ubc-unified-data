The Government of Canada’s Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade [website](http://www.international.gc.ca/americas-ameriques/priorities_progress-priorites_progres.aspx?lang=eng) highlights the contribution of the ADRN: “Canada supported the development of the Andean Democracy Research Network which produces timely, impartial and high\-quality assessments of democracy in the Andean region\. The network also provides a forum to analyze and debate ways to strengthen the implementation of the Inter\-American Democratic Charter\.” Versión en español [aquí](http://www.international.gc.ca/americas-ameriques/priorities_progress-priorites_progres-Spa-Por.aspx)\.
