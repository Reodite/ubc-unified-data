The Andean Democracy Research Network is a project headed under the auspices of The [Centre for the Study of Democratic Institutions](https://democracy-arts.sites.olt.ubc.ca/) \(CSDI\) in the [Department of Political Science](http://www.politics.ubc.ca/) at [The University of British Columbia](http://www.ubc.ca/)\.

The ADRN is a network of [research collaborators](https://democracy-network.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about-us-2/research-collaborators/) from within Latin America doing research on electoral and constitutional democracy, as well as participatory and representative democracy in the region\. The ADRN has the support of an [Academic Advisory Council](https://democracy-network.sites.olt.ubc.ca/academic-advisory-council/) and key [Senior Partners](https://democracy-network.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about-us-2/research/partners-academic-advisory-council/)\.

Initial funding for the pilot project was provided by the Martha Piper Fund and the [Glyn Berry Program for Peace and Security](http://www.international.gc.ca/glynberry/index.aspx) in the [Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade](http://www.international.gc.ca/international/index.aspx) of the Government of Canada\. Currently, funding is provided from the [Ford Foundation](http://www.fordfoundation.org/)\.

The ADRN works under a common [methodology](https://democracy-network.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about-us-2/research/) and and agreed upon [statement of partnership](https://democracy-network.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about-us-2/statement-of-partnership/)\.
