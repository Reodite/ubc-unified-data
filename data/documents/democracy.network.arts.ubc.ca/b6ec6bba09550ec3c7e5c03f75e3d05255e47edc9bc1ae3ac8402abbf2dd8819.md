Our initiative builds on Canada’s substantial investment in democracy in the Americas, from the creation of the Unit for the Promotion of Democracy in the [Organization of American States](http://www.oas.org/en/default.asp) \(OAS\), to the Quebec City Summit and the [Inter\-American Democratic Charte](http://www.oas.org/charter/docs/resolution1_en_p4.htm) [r](http://www.oas.org/charter/docs/resolution1_en_p4.htm)\. Canada was instrumental in the development of the Democratic Charter, and the idea of the [Friends of the Charter](http://www.cartercenter.org/peace/americas/friends.html) as well as the international research network to monitor and report on democracy which emerged from meetings in Canada\.
