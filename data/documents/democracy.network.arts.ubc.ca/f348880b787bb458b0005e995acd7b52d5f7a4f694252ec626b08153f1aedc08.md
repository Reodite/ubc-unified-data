CSDI’s Democracy Network hosted at workshop in Washington D\.C\. at American University, June 22 &amp; 23rd, 2010, entitled, “Participation and Representation in Latin America”\.

Research collaborators presented their research proposals and received feedback from members of the Network’s Academic Advisory Council\. They will undergo their research over six months, then gather for a second workshop in December in Buenos Aires, where they will discuss their findings\.
