> What does co\-op look like for an IDST student? We spoke with three current IDST students about their experiences, what they learned along the way, and their advice for other IDST students considering co\-op\.

---

Image **Meet Charlene** 

**Thematic study option**: Creative Expression in Social Justice

**Co\-op position**: Student Advisor, Training &amp; Development Assistant at UBC Arts Co\-op Program

**What made you decide to join the Arts Co\-op program?** 

I joined because I was unsure about many things including what I wanted my career to look like\. Co\-op gave me the opportunity to test out a different work environment and learn beyond the classroom\. I was interested in learning how I’d apply the skills and knowledge I’ve acquired in my interdisciplinary degree into real\-life work situations\. The training and support that the program provides for new co\-op students also helped me gain more confidence in the job search\.

**How has being an IDST student helped you in your co\-op role?** 

The interdisciplinary research process has highly valuable skills that translate well in the workplace, such as the ability to ask thoughtful and informed questions, ability to take initiative, and so much more\. In other courses, I worked on multimedia projects and assignments that require audio and video editing\. The technical skills I developed and strengthened in those courses equipped me with the ability to provide valuable technical support during virtual and in\-person events of 200\+ attendees as a Student Advisor\.

**Did your co\-op experience change the way you think about your career goals or future plans?** 

Definitely\! Before I completed a co\-op position, I was very uncertain and anxious\. By reflecting on my experiences during and after my work terms, I realized that I highly value projects that support the wellbeing and growth of individuals, specifically young adults from diverse backgrounds\. Aside from gaining a better understanding of the type of work I want to explore, I also learned about what practical things work best for me in a workplace \(i\.e\. working hybrid, having flexibility, supportive team environment, etc\.\)\.

**What advice would you give to an IDST student considering co\-op?**

Let your curiosity guide you\! Look around and talk to people who have completed co\-op positions and ask if they can have a quick chat with you\. Coffee chats can go a long way both in the present and in the longer term\. It’s normal to feel nervous and uncertain, but remember that you’re an IDST student\! Your unique perspectives and your proactiveness will make you well\-equipped to enter the co\-op program and/or workplace\.

“A valuable thing about being an IDST student is that I must be proactive in shaping my degree\. Such proactiveness is a significant quality that is required in any workplace\.”

Charlene

Image **Meet Aryana** 

**Thematic study option:** Social Justice Communication and Digital Humanities\.

**Co\-op positions**: Summer Program Assistant at North Shore Women’s Centre, and Marketing Assistant at IM4 Media Lab

**What made you decide to join the Arts Co\-op program?** 

It was important for me to have both educational skills and real work experience\. I wanted the opportunity to explore different career paths and fields while learning about and becoming involved in community organizations\. I believe my co\-op experience is going to help me once I graduate, both in finding jobs and being able to demonstrate the practical applications of my degree program\.

**How has being an IDST student helped you in your co\-op roles?**

Because of my varied interests and educational breadth, I can understand and take on responsibilities that require multidisciplinary thinking\. I have been able to use the information I learned in communication classes coupled with my knowledge of social justice to design curriculums, give presentations to over 200 people, and acquire funding, all of which are tasks that require interdisciplinary knowledge\.

**Did you co\-op experience change the way you think about your career goals or future plans?** 

Absolutely\. Before entering co\-op, I wasn’t sure what was out there for me as someone with so many different interests, but through co\-op I realized there are roles and organizations that value unique perspectives and backgrounds\. Employers want you to learn and grow, so be ready to ask questions and chase what you’re interested in\.

**What advice would you give to an IDST student considering co\-op?** 

Keep an open mind about what roles you might be interested in and try everything\! Even if at first glance, a job title doesn’t sound like it’s for you, read the job description or the company mission and you may be surprised at what you find\.

“Co\-op is an unmatched opportunity to gain professional experience, build meaningful connections, and discover what you're truly capable of before graduation\.”

Daniel

Image **Meet Daniel** 

**General study option:** Economics, Commerce, and Information Studies

**Co\-op positions:** Financial Business Analyst at BC Infrastructure Benefits, Business Insights Analyst at RBC, and Data Analyst at Electronic Arts

**What made you decide to join the Arts Co\-op program?** 

As an international student, gaining professional work experience before graduation was incredibly important to me\. The program gave me access to opportunities that are often reserved for co\-op students, including positions in government and large organizations\. It allowed me to work full\-time while completing my degree and gain meaningful industry experience before entering the job market\. The program also provides valuable resources such as resume reviews, cover letter feedback, mock interviews, and career guidance, which helped me continuously improve throughout my co\-op journey\.

**How has being an IDST student helped you in your co\-op roles?** 

Being an IDST student has given me a unique perspective because I’ve been exposed to both quantitative and qualitative ways of thinking\. My coursework helped me develop strong analytical skills through Economics and Statistics while also strengthening my communication, teamwork, and critical thinking skills through my Arts background\. Throughout my co\-op terms, managers have often told me that one of my strengths is being able to balance technical problem\-solving with effective communication, which is a valuable skill in any workplace\.

**Did your co\-op experience change the way you think about your career goals or future plans?** 

Absolutely\. Before my first co\-op, I was worried that I wasn’t technical enough to pursue analytics or work in the technology industry because I came from an Arts background\. However, I was able to challenge that assumption, build strong technical foundations, and learn from professionals in the field\.  Another valuable part of co\-op was being able to explore different roles and industries\. Through my co\-op experiences, I gained a better understanding of what type of work I enjoy and where I see myself growing in the future\. It gave me the confidence to pursue a career in data analytics and data science because I was able to discover that path through real\-world experience rather than just assumptions\.

**What advice would you give to an IDST student considering co\-op?** 

My biggest advice is to embrace the interdisciplinary nature of IDST and use it as a strength\. Don’t be afraid to explore opportunities outside of what you initially think is your “ideal” career because co\-op is designed to help you learn through experience\. One of the biggest advantages of co\-op is that students have the opportunity to try different roles, industries, and workplace cultures before graduating\. You have the privilege of experimenting, making mistakes, and discovering what you enjoy and what you don’t enjoy in a professional setting\. Every experience teaches you something valuable\. Stay curious, keep learning, and show employers that you are willing to grow, adaptable, and coachable\.
