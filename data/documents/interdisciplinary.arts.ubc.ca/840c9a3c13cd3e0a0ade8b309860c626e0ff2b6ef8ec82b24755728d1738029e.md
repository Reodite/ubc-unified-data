Are you interested in exploring multiple academic fields and tailoring your education to your unique interests? The Interdisciplinary Studies \(IDST\) program offers the flexibility to design a personalized academic path that bridges disciplines\.

**What to Expect:** 

-   Overview of the Program: Learn about how you can combine subjects across humanities, social sciences, and creative and performing arts to create a unique degree
-   Admission &amp; Application Process: Get detailed information on how to apply, and requirements

Why Attend?

-   Explore how the program can be customize to fit your passions and goals
-   Get answers to your questions directly from the program, and Arts Academic Advising

Whether you’re unsure or certain that Interdisciplinary Studies program is for you, this session will give you all the information you need to make an informed decision\.
