This orientation will be held in\-person on **Tuesday, September 8, 2026** **in Buchanan Tower \(BuTo\) Room 225 from 2:00 to 3:30 pm**\. The IDST Program Chair and Student Program Coordinator will be available to answer any questions or concerns about the program\. Arts Academic Advising will join us for reminders to keep in mind while completing the rest of your degree\. The IDST Student Association will also be around to welcome you into the program\.

We **highly encourage** all new IDST students to attend this orientation\. There are no required courses as an IDST student, so this a great opportunity to meet fellow students and like\-minded individuals with similar programs/course selection\. This might be your chance to meet another student in the same courses as you this year\!
