### Announcement

IDST\-SA is recruiting an Outreach Coordinator for the 2026\-27 academic year\. If you are interested, we encourage you to join\!

[Source link](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeF0LW7Poqa5Wbsrye3DGeuQj4q-IHxza9b_YwLp-tkXrzTeg/viewform?pli=1)

**The Interdisciplinary Studies Student Association \(IDST\-SA\) is here, and they’re excited to continue to build a community among IDST students and beyond\!** 

The team is looking forward to planning events and creating opportunities for connection, collaboration, and support\. Keep an eye out for upcoming opportunities to get involved, meet fellow students, and help shape the IDST community\.

**[https://interdisciplinary\.arts\.ubc\.ca/wp\-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/09/Jiyajeet\-Boparai\-scaled\.jpg](https://interdisciplinary.arts.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/09/Jiyajeet-Boparai-scaled.jpg)ImageMeet Jiyajeet \(she/her\)** 

**Co\-President and Marketing Coordinator**, General study option: Psychology, Political Science, and Creative Writing

**What’s one thing you’re excited about this year in the IDST\-SA?** 

I am thrilled to have the opportunity to do more for IDST students and plan more events than in previous years\. I am also really excited to establish IDST\-SA as a lasting club that future IDST students can continue to be a part of\.

**What campus resource, club, or activity would you recommend to every student?**

I would encourage everyone to get involved in as many clubs as possible and take advantage of the valuable opportunities to develop leadership and teamwork skills, while also making new friends\. Building connections can be a daunting challenge for many students, and being part of a club is a great way to step outside your comfort zone and find a supportive community\.

**What’s your current favourite book, podcast, show, or playlist?** 

I am really into rereading some of my favourite book series, such as Shatter Me, Throne of Glass, ACOTAR, The Selection, The Fourth Wing, and more\.

[https://interdisciplinary\.arts\.ubc\.ca/wp\-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/09/Amy\-Perrin\-scaled\-e1788475713794\.jpg](https://interdisciplinary.arts.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/09/Amy-Perrin-scaled-e1788475713794.jpg)Image **Meet Amy \(she/her\)**

**Co\-President and Events Coordinator**, Thematic study option: Ethics, Responsibility, and Social Change

**What’s one thing you’re excited about this year in the IDST\-SA?** 

I am excited to connect with other IDST students and to work with other clubs and student associations to put on some great events\.

**What campus resource, club, or activity would you recommend to every student?**

I recommend making use of the free language tutors from the FHIS department, and the aquatic centre\.

**What’s your current favourite book, podcast, show, or playlist?** 

My current favourite book is Anxious People or anything by Fredrik Backman\.

**[https://interdisciplinary\.arts\.ubc\.ca/wp\-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/09/Diya\-Mahajan\.jpg](https://interdisciplinary.arts.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/09/Diya-Mahajan.jpg)ImageMeet Diya \(she/her\)**

**Treasurer,** General study option: Economics, Psychology, Commerce, and Philosophy

**What’s one thing you’re excited about this year in the IDST\-SA?** 

The thing that excites me the most about this year is how I have a chance to bring IDST\-SA to my fellow UBC students\! IDST is a wonderful program, and I know that students who can’t find just the right program for themselves, will find one in IDST\.

**What campus resource, club, or activity would you recommend to every student?**

I would always recommend being in touch with your prospective/current program’s club as the information provided by the respective club is too valuable to lose\.

**What’s your current favourite book, podcast, show, or playlist?** 

I have always been into fantasy genre books, and I am very excited to read Barbie: Dreamscape by Alex Aster\.

**[https://interdisciplinary\.arts\.ubc\.ca/wp\-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/09/Larissa\-Ng\-Quimson\-scaled\-e1788994351474\.jpg](https://interdisciplinary.arts.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/09/Larissa-Ng-Quimson-scaled-e1788994351474.jpg)ImageMeet Larissa \(she/her\)**

**Secretary,** General study option: English, Romance Studies, Asian Canadian and Asian Migration Studies, Asian Studies, History, and Sociology

**What’s one thing you’re excited about this year in the IDST\-SA?** 

I am excited to strengthen the IDST community by meeting new and current IDST peers at all the lovely events we have in store\!

**What campus resource, club, or activity would you recommend to every student?**

I recommend attending Clubs Day and checking out the Makers Market because they are casual yet fun ways to socialize and get comfortable being a part of a student body as vast as UBC’s\.

**What’s your current favourite book, podcast, show, or playlist?** 

My current favourite book is Wild Reverence by Rebecca Ross, and one of my favourite shows is the Kdrama “My Name”\.
