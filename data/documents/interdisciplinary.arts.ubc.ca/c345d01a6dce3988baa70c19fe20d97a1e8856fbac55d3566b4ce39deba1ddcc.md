Please submit changes to your approved Interdisciplinary Studies \(IDST\) program to the IDST Program Chair\. Once the Program Chair has reviewed your requested changes, you will receive an email to either confirm your request, or to book a meeting to discuss your proposed changes\. Please read the information below before revising your program:

-   Restricted course registration requests should be communicated with the department offering that course
-   Check your Academic Progress Report to confirm your course selection in your approved IDST program
-   Please include all course revisions in one form instead of sending multiple forms

Please remember that credits **cannot** be earned towards your BA degree from upper\-level courses taken outside of your approved courses in the primary focus area\. The IDST program also **does not** allow the inclusion of courses taken with Credit/D/Fail grading\.

Please note that program revisions sent during the add/drop deadline could face a possible delay in response due to the high volume of requests received during that period\.
