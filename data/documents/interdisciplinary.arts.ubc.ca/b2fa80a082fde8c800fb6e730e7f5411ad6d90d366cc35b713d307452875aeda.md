### Announcement

IDST\-SA is recruiting for the 2026\-27 academic year\. If you are interested in meeting new people, planning events, and helping grow the IDST community, we encourage you to join\!

[Source link](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeF0LW7Poqa5Wbsrye3DGeuQj4q-IHxza9b_YwLp-tkXrzTeg/viewform?usp=publish-edito)

### It might be a little late in the term for introductions – but as they say, better late than never\! The Interdisciplinary Studies Student Association \(IDST\-SA\) is here, and they’re excited to start building a community among IDST students\.

As the term continues, the team is looking forward to planning events and creating opportunities for connection, collaboration, and support\. Keep an eye out for upcoming opportunities to get involved, meet fellow students, and help shape the IDST community\.

#### [https://interdisciplinary\.arts\.ubc\.ca/wp\-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/03/Shannon\-Lee\-scaled\.jpeg](https://interdisciplinary.arts.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/03/Shannon-Lee-scaled.jpeg)ImageMeet Shannon \(she/her\)

**President,** General study option: Asian Studies, English, and Creative Writing

**What made you choose IDST, and what do you enjoy most about it?** 

Choosing IDST felt natural to me because it has allowed me to pursue my studies in the subjects I am most passionate about, which happen to be across multiple disciplines\.

**What’s your go\-to spot on campus to unwind between classes?** 

I love being out in nature so my go\-to places for hanging out on campus have some really good views, such as Nitobe Garden and the Rose Garden\. When I’m indoors, the Asian Centre and Allard Hall are my favourite places to study and relax, or for doing some browsing among the bookstacks\.

**A fun fact or hobby you have outside of your studies:** 

I love going for long walks, writing \(especially poetry and short stories\), reading, browsing bookstores and shops, and spending time with my bird \(and any other pet for that matter\)\! I also enjoy a good documentary or television show, cooking/baking, and going out for meals with my family and friends\.

---

“IDST also allows me the flexibility to personally curate my course selections so I can truly focus on taking courses that are the most relevant and meaningful towards my interests, while also focusing on the skillsets I want to build\.”

Shannon

President

#### [https://interdisciplinary\.arts\.ubc\.ca/wp\-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/03/Amy\-Perrin\.jpg](https://interdisciplinary.arts.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/03/Amy-Perrin.jpg)ImageMeet Amy \(she/her\)

**Events Coordinator,** Thematic study option: Ethics, Responsibility, and Social Change

**What made you choose IDST, and what do you enjoy most about it?** 

I love that IDST gives me the flexibility and independence to follow my interests and take the classes that engage and inspire me\.

**What’s your go\-to spot on campus to unwind between classes?** 

I tend to end up anywhere with lots of windows, so I can get some light even on rainy days, so the Nest and the upper floors of Brock Commons South are some of my favourite places to pass time on campus\.

**A fun fact or hobby you have outside of your studies:** 

I love collaging, ice skating, reading, walking on the beach, and going out for an iced coffee with my friends\.

---

“I chose IDST because I knew what I wanted to study, but it didn't fit into just one department\.”

Amy

Events Coordinator

#### [https://interdisciplinary\.arts\.ubc\.ca/wp\-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/03/Jiyajeet\-Boparai\.jpg](https://interdisciplinary.arts.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/68/2026/03/Jiyajeet-Boparai.jpg)ImageMeet Jiyajeet \(she/her\)

**Communication &amp; Social Media Coordinator,** General study option: Psychology, Political Science, and Creative Writing

**What made you choose IDST, and what do you enjoy most about it?** 

I chose IDST because it fit perfectly when I was indecisive of whether to take Psychology or Political Science as my major\!

**What’s your go\-to spot on campus to unwind between classes?** 

In between my time with study rooms at Ponderosa Commons and IKB, I like to go to Triple O’s if I need people around me or Starbucks near Forestry\. Bean Around the World is a safe space for creativity with the busy nature of the cafe \(especially when you are stuck in fiction writing and need inspiration\)\.

**A fun fact or hobby you have outside of your studies:** 

I have volunteered with many different organizations in the past, and now volunteering at two\. I am a member of UBC groups such as NRHH, shooting club, Pre\-law, and more\. If after all that I get some free time, I love binge\-watching some comfort shows, dancing, listening to music, reading and I have a job\.

---

“IDST is a perfect blend of both Psychology and Political Science and gives me a chance to balance the best of both along with skill\-building in creative writing as a reader and writer\.”

Jiyajeet

Communication &amp; Social Media Coordinator
