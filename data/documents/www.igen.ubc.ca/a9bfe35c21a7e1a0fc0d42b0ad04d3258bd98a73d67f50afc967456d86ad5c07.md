As of April 2026, Academic Advising for Integrated Engineering students is led by Engineering Academic Services for questions about course planning and registration\.

-   [**Course Planning – Integrated Engineering**](https://academicservices.engineering.ubc.ca/integrated-engineering) \(main reference\)
-   [Booking appointments and drop\-in advising with EAS for Academic Advising](https://academicservices.engineering.ubc.ca/contact-us/)

Please contact the [Integrated Engineering](https://www.igen.ubc.ca/about/contact-us/) advisors directly for support with: 

-   Career Planning 
-   Industry contacts 
-   Reference letters 
-   Feedback for courses in\-progress 
-   IGEN 230 &amp; 330 &amp; 430 [Forms](https://www.igen.ubc.ca/students/forms/):
    -   Expense reimbursements 
    -   Requesting purchases via MTRL Shipping /Receiving 
    -   Submitting work jobs to MTRL Machine Shop
