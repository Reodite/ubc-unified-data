Progressive representatives in American Congress like Representative Alexandria Ocasio\-Cortez have been pushing for a Green New Deal, which would strive to eliminate the use of fossil fuels by 2030\. Lind Initiative speaker Winona LaDuke argues that this may be the best course of action in today’s falling stock market\.
