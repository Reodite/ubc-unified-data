“There’s no such thing as a ‘not racist’ or ‘race neutral’ policy\.” In an interview with CBC News, Ibram X\. Kendi, an American author of *How to Be an* *Antiracist* and a 2020 Phil Lind Initiative speaker, unravels the facets to racism and argues that people fall into either one of the two categories – racist or antiracist – and that there is no in\-between\.
