The business of liberal democratic politics and society is messy and confusing – but that does not mean it cannot be improved\. This should not be the year that the liberal global order dies, but rather one where democracy sits confidently at the center of our public discourse\.

Learn more in this Globe and Mail op\-ed by Professor Taylor Owen \(UBC School of Public Policy and Global Affairs and UBC Journalism\) and Visiting Fellow Robert Muggah who are are leading the Phil Lind Initiative this term at UBC\.
