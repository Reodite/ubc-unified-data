*“I thought Biden could have put him on the spot right then and there and told America what Trump refused to just do\.”*

In an interview with CBC, Ibram X\. Kendi, award\-winning author of *How To Be An Antiracist* and a Phil Lind Initiative speaker for our 2020 series *Thinking While Black,* reflects on how racism plays a significant role in the 2020 U\.S\. Election, voter suppression, and the recent Trump\-Biden presidential debate\. Kendi hopes that illuminating these issues will make people question what causes racial disparities in the structures of society\.
