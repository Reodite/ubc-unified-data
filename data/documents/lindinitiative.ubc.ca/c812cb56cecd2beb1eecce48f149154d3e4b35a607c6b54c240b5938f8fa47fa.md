“I think you see more diversity on camera\. But as all of us know, the real power, right, is the person in the meeting that talks about what the direction of the network is going to be\. So that’s not always the anchor\.”

Soledad O’Brien, award\-winning journalist and a Phil Lind Initiative speaker for our 2022 series on *The Future of Media*, joins *Yahoo Finance Live*‘s “A Time For Change” to examine the underrepresentation of racial groups and lack of diversity in the media industry\. O’Brien also discuss these topics in connection to her HBO show “Black in America\.”
