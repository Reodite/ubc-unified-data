Wielding sharp political insight and a characteristically irreverent sense of humour, **Hasan Piker** is redefining what political commentary looks like in the digital age\. A journalist and wildly\-popular content creator on the streaming platform Twitch, Piker has become an authoritative voice for millennials and Gen Z\.

Moderated by [Nojang Khatami](https://lindinitiative.ubc.ca/speakers/nojang-khatami/), Assistant Professor of Political Science at Fordham University in New York City\.
