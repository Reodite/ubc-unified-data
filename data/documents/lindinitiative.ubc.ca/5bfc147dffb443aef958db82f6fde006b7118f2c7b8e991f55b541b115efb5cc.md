**Interviews and Live Taping**

Watch the 2016 Lind Initiative in U\.S\. Studies Fall series video recordings on the Liu Institute for Global Issues at UBC YouTube channel\. Also find interviews with speakers Peter Beinart and Jane Mayer on the U\.S\. Election\. Watch by clicking on the links below:

**Sept\. 15th, 2016:  
**[Editors David Frum and Peter Beinart, moderated by Andrew Coyne with introductory remarks by Liu Director Moura Quayle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zb38dYLnegk)\.  
Watch [an interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GdRGKVQF4tg&amp;feature=youtu.be) by UBC’s Liu Institute for Global Issues with Peter Beinart, well\-known columnist, author and Associate Professor of Journalism and Political Science at City University of New York\. Beinart speaks about the role of media in the 2016 US presidential race, internal party politics\. and a potential Clinton Administration’s foreign policy\.  
Watch [an interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=10A0vXO6PnA&amp;feature=em-upload_owner) by UBC’s  Liu Institute for Global Issues with Andrew Coyne, political columnist and regular CBC panelist about the wider implications an American political unraveling\.

**Sept\. 23rd, 2016:**  
[Author &amp; New Yorker Staff Writer Jane Mayer, moderated by UBC Journalism Professor Peter Klein](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hSg3ffgjv8g&amp;spfreload=10)\.  
Watch [an interview](https://youtu.be/meq09V7XVdg) by the Liu Institute for Global Issues with Jane Mayer, staff writer at the New Yorker and author of “Dark Money\.” Mayer provided UBC audiences with an acute analysis of how super PACs and big donor money has shaped the 2016 U\.S\. election, or in the case of the GOP, how this money failed to counter the rise of Trump\.

**Oct\. 14th, 2016:**  
[Author and columnist E\.J\. Dionne, moderated by Erik Snowberg, UBC Professor of Economics and Political Science](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z385YAfoREU&amp;feature=em-upload_owner)\.
