“In just a few days, tech has shown it can make meaningful and tough choices about who can use its platforms and how they can be used and make the world a better place in the bargain — a silver lining amid all this gloom in Ukraine\.”

Kara Swisher, Silicon Valley’s ‘most powerful’ tech journalist and a Phil Lind Initiative speaker for our 2022 series on The Future of Media, writes an op\-ed for *The New York Times* about the choices Big Tech has been making to alter the social media experience during the ongoing Russia\-Ukraine conflict\. She discusses some specific methods Big Tech uses such as “de\-amplification” and de\-platforming\.
