Enjoy this full recording from the Phil Lind Initiative series on *The Anti\-Democratic Turn* with [Anne Applebaum](https://lindinitiative.ubc.ca/speakers/anne-applebaum/), a staff writer for The Atlantic and a Pulitzer Prize\-winning historian, as part of our 2021 Phil Lind Initiative series on The Anti\-Democratic Turn\.

The event was moderated by Charmaine de Silva, News Director, NEWS 1130 &amp; CityNews Vancouver\. The discussion touched upon how the problem of the growing discontent of democracy has many sources, not only economic\.

This event was hosted by the School of Public Policy and Global Affairs at the University of British Columbia on February 25, 2021\.
