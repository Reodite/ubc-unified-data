“It’s not enough that every American has access to free healthcare … it must be high quality\.”

In an interview with TIME, Ibram X\. Kendi, award\-winning author of *How To Be An Antiracist* and a Phil Lind Initiative speaker for our 2020 series *Thinking While Black,* outlines the relationship between healthcare and racism during the COVID\-19 pandemic, as well as his findings from his spearheaded project, the COVID Racial Tracker, a joint project between the COVID Tracking Project and the Boston University Center for Antiracist Research\.

Watch the full interview [here](https://time.com/5892968/ibram-x-kendi-time-100-talks/)\.
