Apolitical’s Climate 100 List includes two speakers from the 2019 Phil Lind Initiative series: Dr\. Robert Bullard, who is known as one of the 13 environmental leaders of the century, and among the list’s top 20, is Bill McKibben, 350\.org co\-founder\.

> At a time when young people everywhere are marching to demand action on climate, it’s more important than ever to celebrate and support those with the most power to act: the people driving tangible policy change\. Drawing on hundreds of nominations form experts and leading organisations, this list celebrates politicians, civil servants, academics, and activists\.
