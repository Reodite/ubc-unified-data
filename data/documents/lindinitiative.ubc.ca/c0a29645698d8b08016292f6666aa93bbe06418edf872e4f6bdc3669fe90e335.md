2019 Phil Lind Initiative speaker Bill McKibben reflects on his beginnings in the environmentalist movement, and where the movement and climate change are headed\.

> We’ll continue to work very hard\. But we’re not going to get out of this anywhere near unscathed\. And the sad truth is that Mother Nature is a very powerful educator\. Last fall, some combination of reports from the U\.N\., the ongoing idiocy of Trump, and those California wildfires combined to change something\. And we’re at a moment — some kind of new inflection point\. Let’s see what we can make of it\.
