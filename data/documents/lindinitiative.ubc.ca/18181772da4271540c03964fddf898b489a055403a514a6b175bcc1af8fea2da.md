Jarrett Martineau

Jarrett Martineau is a creative multi\-hyphenate and a leading voice in Indigenous music, media, and cultural production\. He holds an M\.A\. and Ph\.D\. in Indigenous Governance from UVic and was a Fulbright Visiting Scholar at Columbia University\. His scholarly research and writing examine Indigenous art as emergent forms of political communication, and he curates Indigenous\-led events at UBC’s Chan Centre, VIFF and Indian Summer Festival\. Creator and host of CBC’s award\-winning *Reclaimed*, he has also co\-founded numerous projects including Revolutions Per Minute, SoundON, the Imaginarium series and *RISE* for VICELAND\. Jarrett is nêhiyaw \(Plains Cree\) and Denesųłiné from Frog Lake Cree Nation in Alberta, currently based in Vancouver\.
