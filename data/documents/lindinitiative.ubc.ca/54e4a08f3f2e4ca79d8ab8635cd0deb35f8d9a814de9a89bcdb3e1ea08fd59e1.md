Interdisciplinary artist and activist **Dread Scott** has spent his career using art to disrupt norms, question corrupt systems of power, and ignite necessary political conversations\. His work, he writes, “looks towards an era without exploitation or oppression,” challenging the notion that current political and economic structures of America as inevitable\.

Moderated by [Elliott Ramsey](https://lindinitiative.ubc.ca/speakers/elliot-ramsey/), curator of The Polygon Gallery\.
