*“Understanding people as they understand themselves gives us a chance to pursue a shared social truth\.”*

In an op\-ed for *The Washington Post*, Danielle Allen*,* James Bryant Conant University Professor at Harvard University, and a Phil Lind Initiative speaker for our 2021 series *The Anti\-Democratic Turn,* emphasizes the commitment to seeking truth in response to the Capitol insurrection in four aspects: forensic; personal; emotional; and social truth\. Allen believes that the conceptualization of these truths can help reinvigorate people’s hunger for personal empowerment with an embrace of deep pluralism within society\.

Read the full piece [here](https://www.washingtonpost.com/opinions/2021/01/21/four-kinds-truth-america-needs-pursue-reconciliation/)\.
