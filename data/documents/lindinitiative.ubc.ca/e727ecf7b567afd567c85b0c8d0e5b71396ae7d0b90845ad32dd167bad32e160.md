Pulitzer\-winning journalist Linda Greenhouse calls the Supreme Court both a “symptom and a cause” of political polarization in the United States\. In *Who Owns the US Constitution?,* Greenhouse discusses the state of the court and the historical rulings that have shaped its current character\. This talk is moderated by Anna Maria Tremonti\.
