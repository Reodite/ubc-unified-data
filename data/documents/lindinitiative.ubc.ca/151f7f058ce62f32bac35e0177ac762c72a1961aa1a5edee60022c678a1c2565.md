America First, America Alone? Global Politics in an Age of Uncertainty

**The Phil Lind Initiative 2026 \| January \- March 2026**

The resurgence of America First\-driven unilateralism is reshaping the global order\. As the U\.S\. moves further from its post\-war role as a global leader and consensus\-builder, the 2026 Lind Initiative investigates the evolving drivers of U\.S\. foreign policy under President Trump, the weakening of multilateral institutions, and the rise of rival powers like China and Russia\. What happens when the world’s most powerful country stops playing by the rules it helped to write – and who steps in when it does?

From hard\-hitting journalism and critical foreign policy analysis to political commentary and provocative art, this year’s speakers interrogate the consequences of American disengagement\. Together, these conversations explore the fragility of alliances, the realignment of global influence, and the future of democratic norms in an era marked by rising authoritarianism, contested truths, and geopolitical uncertainty\. Whether through headlines, histories, or cultural critique, each speaker offers a lens into what a “post\-American” world might look like and who stands to shape it\.

The Lind Initiative is presented by [UBC’s School of Public Policy and Global Affairs](https://sppga.ubc.ca/), in partnership with the [Chan Centre for the Performing Arts,](https://chancentre.com/)
