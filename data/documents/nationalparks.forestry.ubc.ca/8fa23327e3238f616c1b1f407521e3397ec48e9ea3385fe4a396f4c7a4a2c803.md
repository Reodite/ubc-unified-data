The Asia\-Pacific Forest Network \(APFNet\) has been a source of great support for our research unit\. APFNet works, in general, to improve sensitive ecosystems throughout Asia and the Pacific by promoting sustainable land management\. The organization has been instrumental in UBC\-NPRC project development over the last decade\. APFNet currently supports the two main national projects in progress\.

**International Partners**
