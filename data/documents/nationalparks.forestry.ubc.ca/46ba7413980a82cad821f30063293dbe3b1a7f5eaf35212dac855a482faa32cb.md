## **National Parks Research Center**

***Research\-Based Support for National Park Management*** 

Welcome to the National Parks Research Center at the University of British Columbia \(UBC\-NPRC\)

We study national park systems all over the world in order to contribute to a growing body of knowledge on protected areas\. Through our various projects, our team investigates how to best manage a coherent system of protected areas to secure biodiversity and ecosystems services well into the future\.
