**Research Interests:**

Dr\. Yang’s research interests include i\) structure and functions of urban ecosystems in the context of global environmental change, ii\) impacts of urbanization on biodiversity at multiple scales, iii\) urban ecosystem services and human wellbeing, iv\) applications of remote sensing\.
