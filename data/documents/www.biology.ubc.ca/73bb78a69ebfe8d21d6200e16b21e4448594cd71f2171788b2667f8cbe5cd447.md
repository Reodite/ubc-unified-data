                    BIOL 548O – Advanced Topics in Biology:
                       Dealing with Data Graduate Module
                          General Course Syllabus (as of April 2019)

About the Course:
Course Description: BIOL 548O is a short course designed to help graduate students
develop the skills necessary to organize a computational project, keep records of
changes (version control), efficiently optimize a dataset for analyses and future
distribution, and improve the reproducibility of analyses

Course Format: Lecture. The course consists of 12 lecture hours (8 class periods).
Credit: 1
Pre-requisites: Enrollment in Zoology graduate program (or, with permission, other
graduate programs)


Course Learning Objectives:
By the end of this course, students should learn:
   • Principles of version control.
   • How to use git and GitHub for version control for computational projects.
   • Organizational principles for computational research projects.
   • Advanced R programming skills.
   • Managing R-based projects in RStudio.
   • Principles of tidy data.
   • How to use the ‘tidyverse’ suite of R packages to clean up and reformat untidy
       data.
   • Principles of relational databases and R tools for working with these.
   • How to script analyses to improve reproducibility.
   • Principles of good metadata.
   • Best practices for distributing data.


Grading Scheme / Evaluation:
The course grade is calculated as follows:
   •   Course participation and homework: 100%
