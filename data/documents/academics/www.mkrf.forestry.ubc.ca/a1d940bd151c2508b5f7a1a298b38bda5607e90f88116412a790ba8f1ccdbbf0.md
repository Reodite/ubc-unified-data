Please note the following Findlay Family Bursary Policies:

Applicants must explain their financial need, and proof of hardship or income will be requested\. Applicants attests that the information they have provided is true and accurate\.

Children must be in the age rage for the program applied for\. Bursaries cannot be transferred to another participant or program\.

The value of the bursary will vary according to the needs of the qualifying family\. An application does not guarantee the receipt of a bursary, or the amount applied for\. An application also does not guarantee an available space in the desired program\. Receipt of a bursary one year does not guarantee funding for the following year\. Families will need to reapply for the program at each registration period\.

Applications are taken on an ongoing basis\.
