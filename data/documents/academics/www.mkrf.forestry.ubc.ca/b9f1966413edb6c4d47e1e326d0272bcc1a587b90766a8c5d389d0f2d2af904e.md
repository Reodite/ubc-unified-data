**Dates:** Weekly June 29 until August 21, 2026 \(no camp July 1 and August 3\)  
**Time:** 8:30am – 3:00pm  
**Location:** Malcolm Knapp Research Forest \| 14500 Silver Valley Rd Maple Ridge

Fill out this form to request registration for your child in Wild &amp; Immersive’s Summer Camps for age 9\-12\. Your child must be born in the years 2014\-2017 to register\. Registration is for the full week\. Participants may only register for a maximum of 2 summer camp weeks\. Please note that submitting registration does not guarantee your space in the camp\. You will be emailed confirmation of your space, and a link for payment\. Your space is only secured once payment has been made\.

Questions? If you have any questions, please contact Wild &amp; Immersive at 604\-463\-8148 Option 4 or wild\.immersive@ubc\.ca\.

Please note that Wild &amp; Immersive collects personal information during the registration process in order to plan and run our camps and programs\. It is our legal authority to request the information for registration purposes\. This information will only be used in the running of our programs, and will not be used for any other purpose\.
