## Overview

The UBC Cornea Division, part of the UBC Faculty of Medicine’s Department of Ophthalmology &amp; Visual Sciences, offers comprehensive medical and surgical treatment for a wide range of corneal and external eye diseases\. Our expertise includes cataract surgery, treatment of congenital and degenerative corneal conditions, and advanced corneal transplants, such as DALK, DMEK, DSAEK, and full\-thickness PKP\. We also specialize in managing dry eyes, ocular surface diseases, and trauma of the anterior eye segment\.

Our academic mission is to train the next generation of ophthalmologists, including medical students, resident physicians, and fellows specializing in cornea and external diseases\. We are also committed to global ophthalmology, focusing on treating blindness in underserved populations\. Our research efforts, both clinical and basic science, aim to advance corneal health and improve patient outcomes\.
