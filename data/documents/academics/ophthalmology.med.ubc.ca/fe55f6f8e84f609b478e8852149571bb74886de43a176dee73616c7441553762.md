**Biography**

Dr\. Wise completed a honours bachelor of science degree at Queen’s University in Kington, Ontario, before returning to Vancouver to attend medical school at the University of British Columbia \(UBC\)\. After finishing her medical degree, Dr\. Wise went on to complete her ophthalmology residency at UBC\.

Dr\. Wise is a clinical instructor with the University of British Columbia’s Ophthalmology Department\.

She sits  on the UBC Ophthalmology Residency Training Committee and serves on the Providence Health Medical Advisory Committee for Excellence with a focus on Quality Improvement within the Department of Ophthalmology\.
