## Overview

The Division of Comprehensive Ophthalmology consists of a group of university affiliated ophthalmologists whose primary interest is in the delivery of medical and surgical care to a standard consistent with the departmental mission statement\. Their patient base is focused on the Greater Vancouver Region but frequently is delivered to patients from around the province\. The division members are based in hospital centers in the lower mainland area\. Their focus is mainly, but not exclusively, on cataract surgery and they continually work with their peers and with the operating room nurses to create two of the nation’s leading sites in the delivery of cataract and anterior segment surgery\.

The members provide the bulk of resident’s and medical student’s medical and surgical training in the Eye Care Centre Outpatient Clinics, Eye Care Centre operating rooms, Providence operating rooms, as well as at the Ft\. St\. John Satellite Clinic\.
