Are you a Clinical Educator taking a student in 2025 and onwards? Are you a clinician interested in student evaluation? Join this initial information session on the new Canadian Assessment of Clinical Competence\.

**Wednesday, February 19\[th\]\. 12:00pm\-1:00pm via Zoom\.**

This new assessment tool was developed through years of collaboration by the Canadian Academic Coordinators of Clinical Education \(CACCE\)\.

Select SASS Faculty have worked alongside all twelve audiology and S\-LP university programs across the country, creating a national assessment framework that clarifies clinical competencies required for entry\-to\-practice in Canada\.
