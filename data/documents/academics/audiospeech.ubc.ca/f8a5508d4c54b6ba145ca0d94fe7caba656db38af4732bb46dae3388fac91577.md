Dr\. Stacey Skoretz is presenting a one day workshop entitled “New Directions and Innovations in Swallowing” at Vancouver General Hospital on Saturday 27 October, 2018\.

This interprofessional one\-day workshop will focus on new directions and innovations, which revisit foundations in swallowing and related areas\. Attendees will participate in hands\-on, active learning modules that use cross\-systems perspectives on swallowing while interacting with novel technologies and approaches\. These modules will be grounded by short, didactic large group sessions, followed by small group work\.

See the [Workshop Brochure](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/09/VGH-2018-Workshop-Brochure-Skoretz.pdf) for further information and registration details\.
