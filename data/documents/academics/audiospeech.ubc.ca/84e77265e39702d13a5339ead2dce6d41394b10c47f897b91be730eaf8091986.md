The UBC School of Audiology and Speech Sciences is pleased to announce the inaugural John H\.V\. Gilbert Lecture, an event dedicated to highlighting the impactful research contributions of scientists in the field of Communication Sciences and Disorders\.  
  
This special occasion will take place on Friday, May 8th from 1:30\-5:30 pm and will feature a keynote address by [**Dr\. Shari Baum**](https://www.mcgill.ca/scsd/shari-r-baum-phd), titled “Speech prosody in French\-English bilinguals: Behavioural and EEG evidence\.” The program will also include poster presentations showcasing current research in the field\.  
  
A reception with refreshments will follow, offering an opportunity for discussion and networking\.  
  
We warmly invite you to join us for this engaging and scholarly event\.  
  
Please RSVP to [**https://bit\.ly/4sLLqxf**](https://bit.ly/4sLLqxf) before April 17th, 2026\.
