BC Family Hearing Resource Centre have dedicated a page of their Fall 2018 newsletter to their partnership with SASS\.

[Read it now](http://www.bcfamilyhearing.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/2018-Fall-reduced-size.pdf) \(see page 8\) to hear about their annual ‘parent panel’, whereby Audiology students from our program here at UBC are given the opportunity to hear directly from parents with children who are currently enrolled in services at the centre\.

*“This was an exceptional opportunity for audiology students to hear what is going on in the minds of the family receiving the information that we provide them\- this was very helpful for future practice\.”* **SASS Audiology student\.**
