We will be holding admissions advising sessions with our Graduate Students Programs Manager, Winnie, in the new year\!

15\-minute phone advising sessions will be held on **Tuesday, January 3 and Wednesday, January 4 between 8:45 am – 12:00 pm\.** 

To book an appointment, please contact [office\.assistant@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:office.assistant@audiospeech.ubc.ca) with your preferred date and time slot, and your contact information\. *Be advised that during the school closure from December 23 – January 2, the email will only be checked once a week\.* 

Additional appointment slots will be offered in the coming months\. Please check the [News and Events](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/about/news-events/) page for further announcements\.

*Please note that many questions are answered in our [MSc Program FAQs](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/prospective-students/msc-program/faqs/)\. Please check there before booking your appointment\.*
