In the month of November, the Alzheimer Society of British Columbia is hosting a series of webinars exploring the relationship between dementia and communication\.

On **Wednesday, November 29, 2 p\.m\. PT**, the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences’ Kate Davies and UBC’s Division of Neurology’s Dr\. Robin Hsiung will be presenting:

**Primary progressive aphasia and supporting communication**

This webinar provides an overview of primary progressive aphasia, how speech, language, and communication can be affected by primary progressive aphasia, and how speech\-language pathologists can support those living with primary progressive aphasia and their family members\.
