The 2013 Clinical Faculty Awards were presented at UBC SASS on October 29\. We are pleased to announce the recipients\.

The Elizabeth MacLeod Award for Excellence in Clinical Education \(Speech\-Language Pathology\) was presented to Kelly Kovach\. Kelly is a Clinical Instructor in our School and works as an SLP for Vancouver Coastal Health\.

The Noelle Lamb Award for Excellence in Clinical Education \(Audiology\) was presented to Elizabeth Brooks\. Elizabeth is a Clinical Assistant Professor in our School and works as an Audiologist at BC Children’s Hospital in Vancouver\.

Congratulations, and thank you for all your support, to Kelly and Elizabeth\.
