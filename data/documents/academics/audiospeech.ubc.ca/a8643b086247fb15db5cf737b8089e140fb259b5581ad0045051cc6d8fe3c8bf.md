Would you like to know more about the School’s SimMan – a life\-size, plastic mannequin programmed to help speech\-language pathology students develop their swallowing\-assessment and treatment skills?

Check out [The Ubyssey](https://bit.ly/2IF3DaG) \(UBC’s student newspaper\) to hear from Dr\. Stacey Skoretz about how SimMan provides students with an opportunity to practice their clinical skills before they go out and practice on live patients\.
