## Say ‘Hi’ to Bagel

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/06/Bagel-lounging-1-360x200.jpg)

#### Bagel is a 1 year old Seal Mink Snow Bengal cat\. She is the most mischievous, curious and persistent cat you’ll ever meet\. She’s named after her human \(1st year audiology student\) Triny Fong’s favourite bread\.

#### [Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/06/Bagel-window-225x300.jpg)

#### She loves people and craves attention\. The only time she is not clinging to someone in the house is when she is staring out the window pondering the mysteries of life or playing a game of hide and seek\.

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/06/Bagel-on-leash-1-224x300.jpg)

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/06/Bagel-in-the-snow-225x300.jpg)

#### Even though she acts like a dog inside the house, she has yet to venture beyond the driveway and the backyard\. Perhaps one day she will build up the courage to explore the world beyond the yard\.

#### Her favourite pastimes include playing fetch, climbing on things she’s not supposed to climb on, and napping, but all on her own terms of course\.

#### [Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/06/Bagel-fetching-225x300.jpg)
