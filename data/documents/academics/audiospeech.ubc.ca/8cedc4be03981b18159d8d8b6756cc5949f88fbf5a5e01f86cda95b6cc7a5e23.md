As you know André\-Pierre Benguerel passed away recently\. Some of his friends would like to dedicate a bench to him in Stanley Park, where he would often walk and take pictures\.

An ‘in memory fund’ has been set up to receive donations towards the cost of the bench\. Donations are considered charitable donations and therefore provide a tax credit\. Information about donations is kept private by the Parks Board administrators\.

The bench commemorating André\-Pierre will be on Lost Lagoon, near the bird sanctuary\.

We hope you will support this initiative in memory of André\-Pierre and contribute to this fund as detailed below\. If you have any questions, please contact François Dumas at [fmdumas1@gmail\.com](mailto:fmdumas1@gmail.com)\.

**Andre\-Pierre Benguerel In Memory Fund**

Donations can be made to the **Andre\-Pierre Benguerel** In memory Fund established with the Vancouver Park Board\.

Donations will be used to dedicate a park bench for **Andre\-Pierre Benguerel** in Stanley Park for 10 years\.

Donations can be made on line at [https://donate\.vancouver\.ca/sslpage\.aspx?pid\=352](https://donate.vancouver.ca/sslpage.aspx?pid=352)

\(Please choose the **Andre\-Pierre Benguerel** designation\)
