Did you know that May is Speech and Hearing Month in Canada?

Each year, [Speech\-Language &amp; Audiology Canada](https://www.sac-oac.ca/) \(SAC\) dedicates the month of May to raising public awareness about communication health\. They work to highlight the importance of early detection and intervention in the treatment of communication disorders, and the role that their members and associates play in helping people to “Speak well\. Hear well\. Live well\.”

Log on to their [website](https://speechandhearing.ca/) to view the latest events, activities and news from Speech and Hearing Month and to find out how you can get involved in helping to spread the important message\!
