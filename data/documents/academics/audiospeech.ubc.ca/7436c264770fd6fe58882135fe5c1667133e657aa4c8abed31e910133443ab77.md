## **Meet our first ever SASSy Pet, Hugo\.**

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/Hugo-2-240x300.jpg)

#### Hugo was abandoned as a kitten and was recently adopted by SASS student Laura Dand\.

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/Hugo-4-sleeping-225x300.jpg)

#### Hugo is a sweet ball of energy and fluff\. He enjoys bathtime, sleeping in strange positions, and scavenging for people food \(spicy Doritos have him sprinting\)\.

#### He loves sitting on your shoulders, sleeping on your head, and waking you up every morning with kisses\.

#### Hugo is excited to meet his SASS friends when the time comes\. He wishes everyone love, cuddles, and good health during this time\.
