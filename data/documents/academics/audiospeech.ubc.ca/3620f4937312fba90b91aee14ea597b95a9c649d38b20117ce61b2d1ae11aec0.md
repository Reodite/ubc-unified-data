Seeking to expand your knowledge on the aerodigestive tract? In search of the latest updates in cutting\-edge research?

Search no further and welcome to our first ever AeroDigest symposium\! During this FREE two\-day hybrid event \(in\-person and virtual\), we will host multidisciplinary sessions by world\-renowned scientists on aerodigestive tract anatomy, biomechanics, physiology, and more\! Speaker information is available here: [https://swallowinginnovationslab\.com/symposium/](https://swallowinginnovationslab.com/symposium/)\.

**Registration will close on October 3\[rd\], 2023\. Register now as space is limited and filling up fast\! Please use the following link for registration:** [**https://ubc\.ca1\.qualtrics\.com/jfe/form/SV\_8qBoRbuti1zwVq6**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_8qBoRbuti1zwVq6)

**When:** Saturday October 14\[th\] \(0845\-1800h PST\) and Sunday October 15\[th\] 2023 \(0800\-1800h PST\)\.

Options are available for partial attendance during registration\.

**Where:** This is a hybrid event\. Lunch will be provided for those attending in person\.
