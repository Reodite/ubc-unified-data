Please join UBC School of Audiology and Speech Sciences’s [Dr\. Paola Colozzo](http://audiospeech.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/academic-faculty/paola-colozzo/) and ECPS at University of British Columbia’s [Dr\. Pat Mirenda](http://ecps.educ.ubc.ca/person/pat-mirenda/?login) on April 30th from 3\-4PM \(pacific time\) for their talk, ‘What Can I Do with My Child All Day?’

This free one\-hour webinar, hosted by [ACT\-Autism Community Training](http://www.actcommunity.ca/), will provide learning strategies for parents of young children and the service providers who support them\.

No registration required\! Please see the details in the event listing [here](http://www.actcommunity.ca/education/act-events?gclid=EAIaIQobChMIvd3Em7aB6QIVAz6tBh1LXAk5EAAYASAAEgJGfvD_BwE)\.
