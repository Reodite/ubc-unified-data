### **Finding the Clinic Inside the Friedman Building**

1.  As you enter the building through the main doors, walk straight towards the elevator\.
2.  Take the elevator to the 3\[rd\] floor, and you will see signs for the SASS Speech and Language Clinic in front of you\. Rooms 341 and 342 will also be the first rooms you see as you come off the elevator\.
3.  After your Speech and Language session, you will take the elevator to the 2\[nd\] floor to pay for your session at the Physical Therapy &amp; Research Clinic\.
