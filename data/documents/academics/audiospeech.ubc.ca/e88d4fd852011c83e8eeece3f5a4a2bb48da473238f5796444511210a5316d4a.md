## Supporting Early Social &amp; Communication Development through Parent Coaching

#### April 23, 2021  
9:00 a\.m\.to 2:30 p\.m\. Pacific Time

#### Presented by Pat Mirenda, PhD, BCBA\-D; Paola Colozzo, PhD, RSLP; Veronica Smith, PhD; Wendy Ungar, PhD and other members of the PACE Coaching team

The findings of this recently concluded 3\-year research project, carried out in partnership with child and family development agencies across BC, has important implications for supporting parents and communities with effective early intervention for toddlers with special needs\.

For more information and to register, click **[here](https://www.actcommunity.ca/education/act-events/parent-coaching-conference-2021)**\.
