We are pleased to announce that we will be taking part in the West Coast Virtual Fairs: Career, Volunteer &amp; Graduate School event on May 17th and 18th\!

Students and alumni from Simon Fraser University \(SFU\), the University of British Columbia \(UBC\), and the University of Victoria \(UVic\) from all faculties and disciplines are welcome to participate\.

SASS Graduate Student Programs Manager, Winnie Wong, will be available for 1\-on\-1 virtual advising sessions\.

The Fair takes places Wednesday, May 17 &amp; Thursday, May 18 from 10:00am – 3:00pm PT\.

#### You can register starting May 10 for your individual video chat session by clicking this **[link](https://www.westcoastvirtualfairs.com/students)**\.
