We are deeply saddened to share the passing of Jill Chernecki\. Jill was an MSc student in the School from 2015\-2017 and was a cherished member of the speech\-language pathology community\. She had gift for building meaningful connections with those around her\. Jill will be remembered for her remarkable kindness, compassion, and warmth\.  
  
On behalf of everyone at the School, we would like to extend our condolences to Jill’s family and friends\. She will be remembered fondly\.
