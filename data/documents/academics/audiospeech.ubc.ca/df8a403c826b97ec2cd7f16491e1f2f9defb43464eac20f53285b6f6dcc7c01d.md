The only program in British Columbia to educate Audiologists and Speech\-Language Pathologists, the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences \(SASS\) offers a strong theoretical foundation\. The academic component is enhanced by externships: real\-life experience in the field, where students learn and work alongside practitioners\.

As a graduate department, the School offers two degrees:

-   The **Master of Science** is the basis for certification as an Audiologist or Speech\-Language Pathologist\. Students may choose a Master of Science degree in [Audiology](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/prospective-students/msc-program/audiology/) or [Speech\-Language Pathology](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/prospective-students/msc-program/speech-language-pathology/)\.

-   The **[Doctor of Philosophy](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/prospective-students/phd-program/)** degree provides students with the credentials to specialize academically\.
