Katharine \(Kate\) Davies, Ph\.D\., is a registered speech\-language pathologist and Assistant Professor of Teaching at the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences\. Before completing her Ph\.D\. at the University of British Columbia, Kate completed a B\.A\. in International Relations, M\.A\. in Linguistics, and a M\.Sc\. in Speech\-Language Pathology\.

**Research Interests**  
Dementia\-related communication disorders; Primary progressive aphasia and other language\-led dementias; Knowledge translation; the World Health Organization International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health; Rights\-based approaches to communication disability\.
