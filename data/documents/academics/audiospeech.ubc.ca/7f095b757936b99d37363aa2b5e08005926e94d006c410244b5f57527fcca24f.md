As a final celebration evening for their course, the students of AUDI 540 will be presenting another inspiring Arts Night\. The focus is on the North, with traditional tales, music and tragic history in a set of woven vignettes\.

Date: Friday, November 6, 6\-8pm \(Doors open at 5:40pm\)  
Location: Friedman Building Rm\. 153  
\(Entrance is by voluntary donation of a food bank item or cash for refugee funds\)

Come learn, enjoy and support fellow classmates and students, and bring a friend or family member\. There will be some First Nations inspired cuisine to sample after the performance and artwork and therapy materials on display created by second year students\.
