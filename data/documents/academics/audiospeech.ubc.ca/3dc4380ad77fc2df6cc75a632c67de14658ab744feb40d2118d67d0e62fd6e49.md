In 2019 UBC’s School of Audiology and Speech Sciences celebrated its 50\[th\] anniversary as a leader in educating audiologists and speech\-language pathologists, as well as research in communication and related disorders\.

What have the past 50 years looked like at the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences? \(*Click to enlarge*\)

[https://med\-fom\-audiology\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2019/02/SASS\-50th\-anniversary\-timeline\.pdf](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/02/SASS-50th-anniversary-timeline.pdf)[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/02/SASS-50th-anniversary-timeline-v2.jpg)

On September 13, 2019, the School’s community gathered to celebrate its 50\[th\] anniversary with a day of events, which included an open house at the Friedman Building, discipline\-related lectures by eminent scholars, a panel discussion and poster session, followed by a gala dinner with entertainment\. School founder John Gilbert was in attendance and shared some of his memories of the School\.

The full schedule and photos from the day are available [here\.](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/about/sass-50th-anniversary/50th-anniversary-celebration/)

We are also hosting a special [50\[th\] anniversary colloquium series](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/about/colloquium-series/) throughout the year\.
