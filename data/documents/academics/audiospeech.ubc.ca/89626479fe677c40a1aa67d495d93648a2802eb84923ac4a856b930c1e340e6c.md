The School’s PhD program provides research and educational opportunities for students to develop scholarly excellence in the study of human communication\.

Click on the names below for an insight into the research topics, interests and background of current SASS doctoral students\.

### **May Elbar**

**Supervisor:**

Dr\. Navid Shahnaz

**Bio:**

May is a PhD student at the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences, UBC\. She received both her Bachelor of Medicine and General Surgery and Master Degree in Audiology at Menoufyia University, Egypt\.  May worked as an audiologist in Egypt for 7 years at Menoufiya University Hospital\. She worked as an assistant lecturer of Audiology at the faculty of Medicine Menoufyia University, Egypt\.

**Research Topic:**

The long\-term effect of COVID\-19 on Auditory and Vestibular system\.

### **Yu Cen Gao**

**Supervisor:**

Dr\. Paola Colozzo

**Bio:**

Yu Cen is a PhD student at the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences and a practicing Speech Language Pathologist\. She received both her M\.Sc\. in Speech\-Language Pathology and B\.A\. in Speech Sciences and French at UBC\. Her primary clinical interests include working with various paediatric speech, language and social communication disorders and delays in preschool and school\-age populations\. She has valuable experience working in public schools and community settings, using a multi\-disciplinary team approach\. She values family\-centred practice and provides a play\-based, child\-led therapy approach while supporting families from diverse cultural and linguistic backgrounds\.

**Research Interests:**

Raising awareness of Developmental Language Disorders \(DLD\) in the educational setting, early identification of DLD for children entering school, and the process of identifying DLD in children who are bilingual or multilingual\.

### **Ka Keung \(Leon\) Lee**

[https://med\-fom\-audiology\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/faculty\-staff/doctoral\-students/lee\-ka\-keung\-leon/](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/doctoral-students/lee-ka-keung-leon/)[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/07/Lee-Ka-Keung-Leon.jpg)**Supervisor:**

Dr\. Alexis Black

**Bio:**

Hi, my name is Ka Keung Lee, you can call me Leon:\) I am a PhD student at the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences\. Before I joined UBC, I received my BA in Bilingual Studies from the Hong Kong Polytechnic University, and my course\-based and thesis\-based Master's in Linguistics from the Chinese University of Hong Kong and Simon Fraser University\. In my previous research, I have conducted robot‑assisted prosodic training with children with ASD, carried out EEG experiments with typically developing adults, and performed MRI scanning with elderly monolingual and bilingual speakers\.

**Research Topic:**

I am interested in understanding why infants/ young children acquire languages differently from adults and older individuals\. Specifically, I would like to focus on the cognitive, neural, and articulatory mechanisms that underlie these developmental differences, particularly in multilingual populations\. By uncovering these mechanisms, I aim to contribute to the development of non\-invasive diagnostic tools and treatments for language disorders tailored to different age groups\.

**Research Interests:**

Neurolinguistics, Psycholinguistics, Language Acquisition, Cognitive Neuroscience, and Language Disorders

### **Anna Yekta**

**Supervisor:**

Dr\. Navid Shahnaz

**Bio:**

Anna is a clinical audiologist in Guildford, BC, and a PhD candidate under the supervision of Dr\. Navid Shahnaz\. Her research focuses on the diagnostic accuracy of broadband noise acoustic stapedial reflex testing compared to auditory brainstem response for detecting auditory neuropathy spectrum disorder, as well as the sensitivity of newborn hearing screening protocols in identifying slight to mild hearing loss\.

**Research Interests:**

Pediatric audiology, newborn hearing screening, auditory neuropathy spectrum disorder, early intervention
