Come and see us at [UBC’s Graduate and Professional Schools Fair](https://students.ubc.ca/career/career-events/graduate-professional-schools-fair)\!

**Date:** Friday, September 28, 2018  
**Time:** 11:00am – 4:00pm  
**Location:** UBC Life Building Concourse \(6138 Student Union Blvd\)

Representatives from the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences will be attending the fair and answering any questions you may have about the School, the program, and the application process\.

The event will also feature over 65 other schools within Canada and abroad to give students an opportunity to learn about academic opportunities beyond their undergraduate degrees\. If you are considering more schooling after your UBC degree, this is your chance to explore diverse graduate and professional programs and scholarship opportunities\.
