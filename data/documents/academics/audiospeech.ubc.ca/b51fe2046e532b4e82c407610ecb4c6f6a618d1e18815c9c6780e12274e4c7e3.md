The UBC MSc Speech\-Language Pathology \(SLP\) program is fully distributed, with two student cohorts, one in Vancouver and the other in Victoria\.

The Vancouver cohort of 36 students attend lectures and labs at the UBC Point Grey campus\. An additional 8 students attend lectures and labs at the Vancouver Island Technology Park \(VITP\) site in Victoria\. Students from both sites are UBC students, but the MSc SLP Victoria cohort is also affiliated with UVic\.

The distributed program is delivered using video\-conferencing technology that allows instructors and students to interact in real\-time at multiple locations\. At both the Point Grey and VITP sites, all MSc SLP students complete the same curriculum at the same time\.

In the online application to the MSc SLP program, applicants will be asked to indicate a preferred study site: Vancouver Point Grey or Victoria VITP\. **Site preference will not impact consideration of your application\. Applicants will be evaluated in the same way, regardless of preferred site\.** 

For successful SLP applicants, offers of admission to the distributed program will include assignment to either the Vancouver or the Victoria cohort\. **Although stated site preference will be considered as much as possible, please note that not all applicants may be assigned to the cohort at their preferred site\.**
