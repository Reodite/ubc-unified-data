## **Juliet**

### **January 2010 – May 2020**

### **May she rest in peace\.**

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/06/Juliet-snuggly-360x296.jpg)

#### Juliet brought love, joy, and comfort to many students, staff, and faculty at SASS\. She liked to receive visitors in the office when Lorienne Jenstad \(Assoc Prof of Audiology\), escorted her to work for the day, usually carrying Miss Jules in a backpack so her delicate paws didn’t need to touch the ground\.

#### [Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/06/Juliet-lil-neck-vest-360x203.jpg)

#### Princess Julietta was rather delighted to have surgery last year and receive strict instructions from the vet that she was to be carried everywhere while she healed\.

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/06/Juliet-shade-snooze-360x203.jpg)

#### She adored worshipping the sun, lounging in one of her many snuggly pink blankets, and soaking up attention\. She particularly loved all her aunties at the school and all the students who mysteriously turned up more often for office hours when it was announced a dog would be there\.

#### ***Juliet, you will be missed\.***

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/06/Juliet-sweater-309x300.jpg)
