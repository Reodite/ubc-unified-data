## Meet Bradley

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/Bradley-fave_cropped-268x300.jpg)

#### Bradley is originally from Vanderhoof and was adopted in 2010 from the Vancouver BCSPCA\.  He is a wonderful gentle friend and Marcia, Instructor and Academic Coordinator Lead for Clinical Education at SASS, and her family love him dearly\.

#### [Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/Bradley-hiking-225x300.jpg)

#### His favorite places are the UBC Endowment Lands, anywhere people are eating, and riding in the car\.

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/Bradley-cone-225x300.jpg)

#### His most unusual ‘trick’ is sticking his head into the Cone of Shame with no hesitation\. It’s not playing Frisbee, but it is pretty fun to see how proud he gets\.

#### When the Friedman building re\-opens and you see his picture on Marcia’s whiteboard, he is working at the SASS that day\. Please feel free to say hi\.
