If English was not the primary language of instruction of your undergraduate degree, and you do not have at least four years of continuous post\-secondary education in an English\-speaking university, there are English competency requirements that must be fulfilled upon applying to the MSc programs\.

Applicants must provide proof of proficiency in English by meeting the following requirement:

-   Provide a copy of your official test scores from either the TOEFL or IELTs tests, ensuring that you meet the **minimum stated individual component scores** **AND the overall score** for admission to our program as follows:
