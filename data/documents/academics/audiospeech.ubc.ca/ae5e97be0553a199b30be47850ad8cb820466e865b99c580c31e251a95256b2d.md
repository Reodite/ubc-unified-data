Good news\! We have extended our Ph\.D\. application deadline for September 2024 entry to May 1, 2024\. Please submit your complete application and references by **May 1, at 11:59 pm Pacific Time**\.  
  
If you have any questions about the application process, visit the [Application Process](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/prospective-students/phd-program/application-process/) on our website, If you need any help with your application, please contact Winnie Wong, Graduate Student Programs Manager at [program\.advisor@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:program.advisor@audiospeech.ubc.ca)\.
