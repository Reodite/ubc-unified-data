## Cutie, Deuce, High Flyer, and Star: Neighbours’ Chooks

## The “Easter Basket” Variety

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/baby-chicks-cropped-235x300.jpg)

#### Born April 1, 2019, and living next door to SASS Professor Emerita Barbara May Bernhardt since April 6, 2019, High Flyer, Star, Cutie, and Deuce started out as soft and fluffy little chicks\.

#### [Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/all-grown-cropped-257x300.jpg)

#### After having flown the coop, these teens love hanging out in the backyard …

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/star-and-cutie-with-herbs-cropped-279x300.jpg)

[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/05/chickens-with-popcorn-cropped-284x300.jpg)

#### … and snacking on grass, herbs, and popcorn\. Yummy\!

#### In return for hen\-sitting, Barbara May and her partner Joe get to share in the Easter basket variety\. As aunt and uncle, all the benefits and none of the work … get your neighbours to buy some chooks\.
