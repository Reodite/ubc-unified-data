[Image](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/05/Kate-Davies.jpg)Congratulations to Kate Davies, who has met all the requirements to advance to PhD candidacy at the School\.

Kate, who is supervised by Dr\. Tami Howe, completed a BA in International Relations, MA in Linguistics, and a MSc in Speech\-Language Pathology\.

Her research interests include dementia\-related communication disorders and primary progressive aphasia, and she has received a 3 year SSHRC doctoral award for her research\.

Congratulations, Kate\!
