### Our Programs

#### [Master of Science \(MSc\)](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/prospective-students/msc-program/)

*Basis for certification as an audiologist or speech\-language pathologist\.*

-   -   [MSc Admission Deadlines](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/prospective-students/msc-program/application-process-2/)
    -   [MSc Application Checklist](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/prospective-students/msc-program/admissions-requirements/application-checklist/)

> ***The application deadline for the next intake of the MSc Audiology and Speech\-Language Pathology program is February 1, 2027\. The 2027/28 MSc program starts in September 2027\. Note: Applicants are notified of admissions decisions in April via email\.***

> **Important Notice for Prospective Applicants – Fall 2027 Intake**  
> The application portal for the September 2027 intake will open on **November 1, 2026**\. If you are planning to apply and are currently registering for courses, please note:
> 
> All prerequisite courses must be listed in our approved [Course Prerequisite Guide](https://med-fom-audiology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/Course-Prerequisite-for-Web_Jun2026_working-file3.pdf)\.
> 
> If you are taking a course not currently listed in the Guide, you must submit the course syllabus for evaluation\. Submit syllabi to [inquiry@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:inquiry@audiospeech.ubc.ca) by **December 1, 2026**\.
> 
> Late submissions may not be considered, so we encourage applicants to plan ahead\.

> ***Applicants must have all prerequisite courses completed by the end of the academic winter session prior to the requested program entry date\.*** For example, for entrance to the 2027\-2028 Winter Session intake \(i\.e\., beginning September 2027\), applicants must have all prerequisite coursework successfully completed by the end of 2026\-2027 Winter Term 2 \(i\.e\., April 30, 2027\)\.
