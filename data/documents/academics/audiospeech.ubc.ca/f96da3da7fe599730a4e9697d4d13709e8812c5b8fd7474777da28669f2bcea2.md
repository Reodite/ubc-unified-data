The Canadian Hard of Hearing Association \(CHHA\) has released a new report on the impact of COVID\-19 on individuals who are Deaf and hard of hearing\.

Among those involved in the survey are SASS Associate Professor, Dr\. Lorienne Jenstad, and SASS audiology student, Kaitlyn Angelozzi\.

You can read the full report [**here**](https://www.chha.ca/covid-19-report/)\.
