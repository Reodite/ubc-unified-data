We are pleased to announce that Dr\. Paola Colozzo has been appointed interim Director at SASS, effective July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2025\. Congratulations, Dr\. Colozzo\! We are excited to have you at the helm during this progressive time at the School\.

Many thanks go to Dr\. Lorienne Jenstad and Dr\. Valter Ciocca for their terms serving as interim Co\-Directors, with Dr\. Colozzo, over the past year\. You revealed your dedication to the School during challenging times and we are very appreciative for all your hard work\.

SASS is indebted to Dr\. Jeff Small \(currently on a one\-year administrative leave\) for his stalwart leadership from 2017–2022\. Dr\. Small led the School with patience, compassion, and extraordinary commitment\. Thank you\.
