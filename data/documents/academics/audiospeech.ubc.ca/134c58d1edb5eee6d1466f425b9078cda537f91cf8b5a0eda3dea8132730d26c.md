The 2012 Clinical Faculty Awards were presented at the BCASLPA meeting in Richmond on October 13\.  We are pleased to announce the recipients\.

The Elizabeth MacLeod Award for Excellence in Clinical Education \(Speech\-Language Pathology\) was presented to Dorothy Mathews\-Dana\.  Dorothy is a Clinical Instructor in our School and works as an SLP for the Cowichan Valley School District\.

The Noelle Lamb Award for Excellence in Clinical Education \(Audiology\) was presented to Henry Lam\.  Henry is a Clinical Instructor in our School and works as an Audiologist at the Western Institute for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing in Vancouver\.

Congratulations, and thank you for all your support, to Dorothy and Henry\.
