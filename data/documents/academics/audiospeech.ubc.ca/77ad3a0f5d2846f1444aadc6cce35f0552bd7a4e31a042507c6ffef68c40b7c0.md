Darlene’s areas of interest include clinical education, pediatric audiology and early identification of hearing loss\. She worked in public health audiology in BC for 30 years,  providing diagnostic and rehabilitative audiology services to the pediatric population\.

As part of the UBC team of Academic Clinical Coordinators of Education, she arranges Audiology externship experiences across Canada for UBC students\.
