Sharon was active on School faculty from 2001 to 2017\.

I have worked as a Clinical Audiologist for many years, primarily at Vancouver General Hospital and WorkSafeBC\.  My interests are diagnostic audiology and occupational hearing loss, including prevention of noise\-induced hearing loss, and Clinical Education in Audiology\.  I am proud to work as a Clinical Director for the B\.C\. Special Olympics Healthy Athlete program\.

It is my pleasure to work with our School’s team of Clinical Coordinators, interfacing with the many excellent Audiologists in the community and arranging clinical experiences for our students\.
