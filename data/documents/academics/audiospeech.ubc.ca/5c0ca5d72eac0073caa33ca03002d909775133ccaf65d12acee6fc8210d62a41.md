Are you a Speech\-Language Pathology graduate in the class of 2022 or later?

The project, *Developing the professional identity of Speech\-Language Pathology students through Written Reflective Practice*, is looking for participants to take part in a study on how SLP students learn their professional identity during our program, and specifically how reflective writing does or can support this aspect of professionalism\. The study involves a 15\-minute survey and an optional online 60\-minute interview conducted by a research assistant\.

This study is being conducted by Marcia Choi \(Primary Investigator\), an Assistant Professor of Teaching at the School of Audiology &amp; Speech Sciences \(SASS\), Faculty of Medicine, University of British Columbia \(UBC\), along with co\-investigator, Ariane Faria dos Santos, a PhD student from the UBC Faculty of Education\.

For more information, please contact Marcia Choi at [marciac@audiospeech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:marciac@audiospeech.ubc.ca) or 604\-822\-4533, or Ariane Faria dos Santos at [arianefs@mail\.ubc\.ca](mailto:arianefs@mail.ubc.ca)\.

To participate in the study please click the following link to complete the online survey: [https://ubc\.ca1\.qualtrics\.com/jfe/form/SV\_ezFF7nKBr60asfk](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_ezFF7nKBr60asfk?fbclid=IwAR004R4HPZ8QUv8z9Rg5pG1eTqjN_T9qZePgtt02ijPKc7Mz0Xey5BLsixw)
