New resource available\!

We are excited to announce that we have a new website dedicated to Clinical Educators up and running\! As the School of Audiology and Speech Sciences continues to grow, we look to expand our Clinical Educators Development Program\. It is through the guidance and unwavering commitment to the next generation that our students are able to grow as young professionals\.

Developed as a ‘one\-stop shop’ for externship\-related information and materials, it will be populated in the months ahead with items that assist our clinical educator community\. Visit our website for important dates, contact details, CoP membership information, and so much more\!

Website Link: [http://clinicaled\.audiospeech\.ubc\.ca/](http://clinicaled.audiospeech.ubc.ca/)
