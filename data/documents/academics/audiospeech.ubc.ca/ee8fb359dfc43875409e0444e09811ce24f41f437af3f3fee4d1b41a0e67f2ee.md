The School of Audiology and Speech Sciences is looking for amazing Audiologists and Speech\-Language Pathologists to support our teaching this fall \(2024\) in Victoria and Vancouver\.

The SLP program has expanded its original location in Vancouver\-Point Grey to a second site on Vancouver Island\. Students and instructors will be physically present at each site where state\-of\-the\-art teaching spaces will allow real\-time interaction using video\-conferencing\.

We are seeking a number of Clinical Faculty members to fill Clinical Skills Assistant roles, on a part\-time basis, that support the learning of the Victoria and Vancouver cohorts of students when the primary instructor is located at the alternate site\.

For more information and to apply before the **June 28th deadline**, please visit our [Careers](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/about/careers/) page \([https://audiospeech\.ubc\.ca/about/careers/](https://audiospeech.ubc.ca/about/careers/)\)\.
