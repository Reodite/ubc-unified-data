### **COVID\-19 SAFETY PROTOCOLS**

In order to keep everyone safe, here is what to expect at the SASS Speech and Language Clinic:

### **PREPARING FOR YOUR APPOINTMENT:**

-   Masks are mandatory in all indoor spaces\. Please wear a mask at all times\.
-   If you do not have a mask or forget to wear a mask, a medical mask will be provided for you\.
-   Complete the COVID\-19 Pre\-screening Questionnaire online via Jane \(our online booking system\) **before** coming to your appointment\.
-   One friend or family member is allowed to attend the appointment with you\.

### **WHEN YOU ARRIVE:** 

-   Please arrive on time\.
-   Public, wheelchair\-accessible washrooms are available on the **1\[st\] floor** of the Friedman Building\. As you enter the building through the main doors, turn right and go approximately half\-way down the hallway\.  The washroom is located on the left\-hand side of the hallway\.
-   The SASS Speech and Language Clinic is located on the **3\[rd\] floor**\. As you enter the building through the main doors, walk straight towards the elevator\. Take the elevator to the 3\[rd\] floor, and you will see signs for the SASS Speech and Language Clinic in front of you\.
-   Your appointment will be in Room 341 or Room 342\. You will see these rooms as you come off the elevator on the 3\[rd\]
-   You will be asked to sanitize your hands before entering the clinic room\.
-   Student clinicians and the Speech\-Language Pathologists will be wearing masks at all times, and other Personal Protective Equipment \(PPE\) when needed \(e\.g\., gloves\)\.

### **WHEN YOU LEAVE:** 

-   After your Speech and Language appointment, you will go to the **2\[nd\] floor** to the Physical Therapy &amp; Research Clinic \(PTRC\)\.
-   You will pay the Administrative Assistant for your Speech and Language session\.
-   Payment will be accepted via tap \(e\.g\., debit or credit card\)\.
-   Insurance \(if applicable\) will be direct billed\.
-   Receipts for your session will be emailed\.
-   Please clean your hands before leaving the PTRC and the Friedman Building\.

### **WHAT WE ARE DOING TO HELP PROTECT YOU:**

-   There is no late cancellation policy\. If you are sick, call [**\(604\) 827\-6959**](tel:%20604-827-6959) to cancel the appointment\.
-   All ‘high\-touch’ surfaces \(e\.g\., chairs, tables, door handles, equipment, etc\.\) will be cleaned and disinfected after each client session and throughout the day\.
-   There will be a 15\-minute period between appointments to ensure there is enough time for cleaning and disinfecting\.
-   All clinic staff are required to complete a COVID\-19 self\-assessment screening tool before starting their workdays\.

### **WHAT WE EXPECT OF YOU:** 

-   **Do not attend if you have tested positive for COVID\-19 or have been in contact with someone who has tested positive for COVID\-19\.**
-   **Do not attend if you have a cough, fever, or have been sick or in contact with someone with these symptoms within the past 2 weeks\.**
-   Do not attend if you have been out of the country or out\-of\-province within the past 2 weeks\.
-   Get in touch if you have any questions or concerns\!
-   Virtual sessions are also an option if you prefer\.
