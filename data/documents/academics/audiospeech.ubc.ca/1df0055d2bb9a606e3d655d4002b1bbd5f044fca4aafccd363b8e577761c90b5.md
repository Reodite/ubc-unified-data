The School of Audiology and Speech Sciences hosts guest speakers in our colloquium series throughout the academic year\. These events are open to the public and are of interest to faculty, clinical faculty, current students, alumni and other professionals and researchers in the community\. The colloquia are held in Room 355 in the School and virtually on Zoom\.
