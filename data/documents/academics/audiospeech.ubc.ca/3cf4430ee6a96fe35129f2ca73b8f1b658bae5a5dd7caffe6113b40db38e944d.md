Our annual poster symposium in AUDI 575 \(Language Development and Disorders in the School Years\) will be held next week, on Wednesday, Nov\. 27, between 11:30 am and 1 pm, and on Friday, Nov\., 29, between 1pm and 2:30 pm in room 354\.

The students in the class have put a lot of thought and effort into their projects, and they are sure to be very interesting\. This is an exciting educational experience for all involved and you are invited to come and see the projects that the students have been working on throughout the term, and to share your knowledge and insights with the presenters\.

This symposium is open to all SASS students, staff, faculty and lab research assistants and volunteers\.
