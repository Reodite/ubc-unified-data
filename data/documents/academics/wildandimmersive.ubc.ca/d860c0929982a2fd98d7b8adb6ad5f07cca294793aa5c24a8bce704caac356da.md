Field Trips &amp; Special Programs

[Kids sitting and listening during wild &amp; immersive forest school events in williams lake](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/kids-learning-at-wild-immersive.jpg)

[Kid holding up large stick in the forest](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/carrying-big-log.jpg)

Come out to the Alex Fraser Research Forest with your class or youth group\. With hands\-on and experiential activities, our instructors encourage and facilitate your students through movement, exploration and creativity\. Choose from our unique nature\-based modules including:

-   Animal habitats
    
-   Wetlands
    
-   Birds
    
-   Survival skills
    
-   Insects
    
-   Geology
    
-   Plant and species identification
    
-   and more\!
    

Programs run from 2 to 5 hours\. Please contact us regarding pricing for extended programs over 5 hours\.

Cost: $350/class\*

\*Classes include up to 25 students\. Additional charge per student for larger class sizes\.

Can’t make it to the forest, we can come to you\! Contact us about hosting a Wild &amp; Immersive program at your school\.
