#### Forest Explorers Summer Camp

Explore the outdoors this summer with our fun and exciting summer camps\. We believe in building a child’s confidence, communication, and curiosity through play\. Featuring a unique blend of hands on learning exploration, nature inspired art, inventive play, and storytelling, our programs offer something to get excited about\.

#### Little Forest Explorers Summer Camp

Come join us for fun summer days filled with nature, play, and imagination\! Our Little Forest Explorers summer camp will spark your child’s curiosity and passion for nature\. We’ll go on little adventures, make art with things we find outside, play fun games, and listen to magical stories\. We’ll use our eyes, ears, and hands to explore the world around us and have lots of giggles along the way\. Let’s grow, play, and discover together\!
