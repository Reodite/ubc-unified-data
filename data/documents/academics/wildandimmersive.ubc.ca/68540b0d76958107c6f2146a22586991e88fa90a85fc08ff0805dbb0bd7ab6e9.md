Every kid should be able to experience and enjoy the great outdoors\.

Through our Forest School programs, children will learn and have fun with their peers while exploring nature\.

### 5 unique programs &amp; age groups

### Weekly nature\-inspired activities

### Connected to the BC curriculum

Designed for the home learning community, but open to any child looking to experience the outdoors\. Sessions feature weekly themes and engaging activities designed to encourage students’ interests\.

-   Birds
    
-   Wetlands &amp; toads
    
-   Insects
    
-   Tree and plant ID
    
-   Animal habitats
    
-   Geology

### Ages 13\+ \(birth years 2009\-2013\)

#### Forest Leaders

Our Forest Leader peer mentorship program is for those forest school students who have aged out of the program, but wish to stay involved as mentors and develop their leadership skills\. Participants will take on a peer leader role within one of our Nature Explorers \(ages 5\-7\) groups, helping to guide and inspire younger students\. There are very limited spaces in this program, and selection is based on a candidate’s fit\.

#### Learning outcomes for this age group:

-   Learning how to model positive behaviour and problem\-solving\.
-   Developing confidence in guiding and supporting younger peers\.
-   Learning to work collaboratively with both peers and adult mentors\.
-   Learning how to inspire younger students to care for the environment\.
-   Building resilience, adaptability, and self\-confidence\.

DATES: Weekly on Tuesdays OR Thursdays from September 15/17, 2026 until May 25/27, 2027

TIME: 9:30am\- 2:00pm

COST: $350 \+GST

Interested participants must apply to be a part of the program, and will be accepted only if deemed a fit for the program\. Applicants may be required to complete a short interview to determine fit\. Previous forest school experience, or other outdoor experience required\. Participants must be able to attend a minimum of 2 sessions per month\.

[Apply Now](https://mkrf-forestry.sites.olt.ubc.ca/forest-leaders-application-2)
