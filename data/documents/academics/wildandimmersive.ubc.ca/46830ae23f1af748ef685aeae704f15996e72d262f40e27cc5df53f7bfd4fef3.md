Wild &amp; Immersive has the ability to run our programs through a combination of program fees, grants &amp; donations, and subsidization from our research forests \(Malcolm Knapp Research Forest for Maple Ridge, and Alex Fraser Research Forest for Williams Lake\)\. In order to continue offering our programs at an affordable cost to families, we are always looking for sponsors and partners to support our work\.

#### Some examples of ways your donations support us are:

-   New washrooms and handwashing stations
    
-   Loose parts playground to encourage creative play
    
-   Participant costs for low\-socioeconomic communities
    
-   Participant gear
    
-   Program planning costs
    
-   Professional development opportunities for staff
    
-   Employee gear
    

Any donation amount is welcome and greatly appreciated\.

[Donate Now](https://give.ubc.ca/wild-immersive)
