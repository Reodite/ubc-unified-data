Here at Wild &amp; Immersive, we want to promote every opportunity possible to connect youth to nature\. We believe there should be no boundary to connecting people to the beautiful outdoors\. We have listed some of our top picks for outdoor learning and nature based Spring Break programs in the Greater Vancouver Area\!

Check out more information below, organized by geographical location for great environmentally focused spring break camps\!

## **Vancouver Area:**

### **Grouse Mountain**

Outdoor exploration atop [Vancouver’s Grouse Mountain](https://www.grousemountain.com/camps/about)\! Exciting spring break camps for kids to be immersed into nature through wilderness adventure and exploration\!

### **Vancouver Aquarium – Aquacamps**

Dive down below the bubbles and explore the amazing creatures of the ocean at the [Vancouver Aquarium](https://www.vanaqua.org/education/camps-and-clubs/aquacamps)\! Great hands\-on activities that engage youth in a supportive learning environment to explore wildlife, ecosystems, and sustainability\!

### **Soaring Eagle Nature School**

Explore the changing forests of various Vancouver parks with [Soaring Eagle Nature School](https://soaringeaglenatureschool.org/programguide/#seasonal-programs)\! Their spring camps work to deepen camper’s connection to the natural world\.

### **Sasamat Outdoor Centre**

The [Sasamat Outdoor Centre](https://www.sasamat.org/spring-break-day-camp) puts on spring break day camps for children ages 6\-12\. They are a great way for kids to spend a week outdoors reconnecting with nature in a variety of adventure based activities\.

## **Sea\-to\-sky Area:**

### **Whistler Outdoor Learning**

Join [Whistler Outdoor Learning](https://www.whistleroutdoorlearning.com/wolf-play-spring-break-camp) for nature based learning, games, and exploration in the forests around Cheakamus\!

### **Saplings Outdoors Program**

Get creative out in the forest with [Sapling’s Camps](https://www.saplingsoutdoorprogram.ca/camps-1)\. Every day is filled with fun and excitement through forest exploration and outdoor learning programs\.

## **Fraser Valley Area:**

[Two girls sitting in the forest writing in their workbooks at Wild &amp; Immersive Spring Break Camp](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/benefits-outdoor-education.png)

### **Wild &amp; Immersive**

Celebrate spring with Wild &amp; Immersive’s [spring break camps](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/)\. Choose from three camp offerings and be prepared for a blossoming experience\. Days will be filled with skill\-based training, hands\-on activities, games, and an adventurous journey through our coastal temperate forest\. Your child will leave these camps with more confidence, self\-awareness, creative thinking, and a greater appreciation of the natural world\.

### **Saplings Outdoors Program**

Get creative out in the forest with [Sapling’s Camps](https://www.saplingsoutdoorprogram.ca/camps-1)\. Every day is filled with fun and excitement through forest exploration and outdoor learning programs

### **Greater Vancouver Zoo – Jr Zoologist**

[Camps](https://gvzoo.com/) for kids aged 7\-12 to explore the wonderful world of animals at the zoo\.

### **Ecodairy** **Spring Break Camps**

Week\-long themed camps run by [EcoDairy](http://ecodairy.ca/2020/02/07/ecodairy-camps/) in Abbotsford that connect kids more with their food and the land\! Hands\-on activities that involve kids in caring for the animals and working the land to better understand agriculture\.  

*By Andrea McDonald*
