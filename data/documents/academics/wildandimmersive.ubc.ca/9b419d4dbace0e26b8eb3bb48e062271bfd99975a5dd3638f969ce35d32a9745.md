### Ages 5\-8

#### Summer Camp

 **All weeks are FULL\. Email to get on the waitlist\.**

Our week\-long summer day camps immerse children in the wonder of the forest through play\-based, hands\-on exploration\. Each day invites campers to discover nature up close while also building confidence, sparking environmental curiosity, and strengthening teamwork along the way\.  

Through a balance of engaging educational activities, outdoor adventures, and meaningful social connection, children will learn, grow, and make lasting memories all while having a whole lot of fun\.

#### Activities for each week include:

-   Hiking
-   Survival skills
-   Tree and plant ID
-   Animal Habitats
-   Scavenger Hunts
-   and more\!

DATES: Weekly camps from June 29 until August 21, 2026 \(no camp on July 1 and August 3\)\.

\*All weeks are FULL\. Email for the waitlist\.

TIME: 8:30am\-3pm daily

COST: $320 \+gst \($256 \+gst for 4 day weeks\)

*\*Please note that each participant is only able to register for a maximum of 2 camp weeks\.*

[Waitlist](mailto:wild.immersive@ubc.ca)

### Ages 9\-12

#### Wilderness Adventure Camp

We’re taking summer camp to the next level with an exciting adventure deep in the forest for ages 9\-12\! Campers will explore advanced hiking trails, enjoy the beauty of Marion Lake with classic summer water activities like swimming an canoeing, and experience the thrill of Battle Archery, led by [Stellar Play](https://stellarplay.ca/)\. The week wraps up with a BBQ lunch by the lake – the perfect ending to an unforgettable adventure\!

#### Adventure activities each week include:

-   Advanced hiking and wilderness preparedness
-   Canoeing and swimming activities
-   Survival Skills
-   Battle Archery with Stellar Play\!
-   End of the week cookout
-   and more\!

DATES: Weekly camps from June 29 until August 21, 2026 \(no camp on July 1 and August 3\)\.

\*June 29\- July 3, July 6\-10, July 20\-24, July 27\-31, August 4\-7, August 10\-14 &amp; August 17\-21 FULL\.

TIME: 8:30am\- 3pm daily

COST: $395 \+gst \($316 \+gst for 4 day weeks\)

*\*Please note that each participant is only able to register for a maximum of 2 camp weeks\.*

[Register for Ages 9\-12](https://www.mkrf.forestry.ubc.ca/wild-immersive-wilderness-adventure-camp-registration-ages-9-12/)

### Ages 13\-16

We’re excited to offer two unique day camp experiences designed for teens aged 13–16\.

#### Teen Ecology Summer Camp

Our Teen Ecology Summer camp is a transformative summer experience designed for teens who are passionate about the environment, hands\-on learning, and deepening their connection with the natural world\.

At this immersive camp, participants will explore ecology, stewardship, and indigenous ways of knowing, while gaining practical skills that foster self\-reliance, mindfulness, and respect for the land\.

#### This program includes:

-   Ecology, conservation, and environmental stewardship
-   Indigenous knowledge and traditional ways of understanding the land
-   Mindfulness and nature connection techniques
-   Hands\-on skills like whittling, beading, and using traditional tools
-   Foraging for edible and medicinal plants
-   Cooking with wild ingredients and learning their uses in healing and nutrition

DATES: July 13\-17, 2026

TIME: 8:30am\-3pm daily

COST: $395 \+gst

#### Teen Survival Skills Camp

Designed for teens ready to challenge themselves and build real\-world outdoor skills, this hands\-on summer camp goes beyond the basics\. Over the week, teens will develop confidence, leadership, and resilience while learning practical wilderness skills in a supportive, team\-based environment\.

This program empowers teens to step outside their comfort zone, think critically, and work together all while having fun and making unforgettable memories with their peers\.

#### This program includes:

-   Hiking/Preparedness/ Trip Planning
-   Emergency camp setup and safety assessments
-   First aid
-   Hands\-on skills \(tool use &amp; knot tying\)
-   Navigation and orienteering
-   Foraging &amp; plant ID

DATES: August 4\-7, 2026

TIME: 8:30am\-3pm daily

COST: $316 \+gst

[Register for Teen Camp](https://www.mkrf.forestry.ubc.ca/teen-summer-camps-2025/)
