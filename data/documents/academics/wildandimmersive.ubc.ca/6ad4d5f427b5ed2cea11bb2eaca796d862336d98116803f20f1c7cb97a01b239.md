                                     JOB OPPORTUNITY
Job Title: Wild & Immersive Program Facilitator

Schedule: TBD; Wednesdays & Thursdays, occasional Fridays. Additional hours may be
available based on programming.

Salary: $20 - $25/hr, based on experience

Job Location: 795 Campbell Road, Williams Lake, BC

Job Description:

Wild & Immersive is an outdoor educational program that aims to create environmentally
curious and healthy individuals through inspiring nature-based experiences. You can learn
more about our programs at www.wildimmersive.ubc.ca.

We are currently seeking responsible Program Facilitators to support our outdoor Forest
School, Little Forest School, and school-break day camp programs.

Duties include:

•   Facilitating outdoor educational day camps for youth ages 3-12
•   Assisting in organization of programs and camps
•   Ensuring program materials are organized, cleaned, and in safe working condition
•   Develop and maintain trail networks and outdoor play spaces
•   Additional duties as required

Wild & Immersive Program Facilitators are passionate about working with children, and
sharing their knowledge and enthusiasm for the outdoors. Facilitators are patient, reliable,
and able to meet the physical demands of the job, including working outside.

Facilitators must be able to provide a current Criminal Record check, and will be trained in
OFA Level 1.

To Apply:

E-mail resume with a short cover letter to jodi.taylor@ubc.ca.
