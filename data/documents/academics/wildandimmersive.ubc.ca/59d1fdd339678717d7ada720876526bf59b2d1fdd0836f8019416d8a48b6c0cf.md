Williams Lake Forest School

Every kid should be able to experience and enjoy the great outdoors\.

Through our Forest School program, children will learn and have fun with their peers while exploring nature\.

#### What your child will be doing at Forest School:

-   [Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/list-icon-3.svg)
    
    Hands\-on learning, done mostly outdoors
    
-   [Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/list-icon-2.svg)
    
    Exploring a variety of nature\-based and environmental topics
    
-   [Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/list-icon-1-1.svg)
    
    Socializing and interacting with outdoor\-loving peers
    

### Weekly nature\-inspired activities

### Connected to the BC curriculum

Designed for the home learning community, but open to any child looking to experience the outdoors\. Sessions feature daily themes and engaging activities designed to encourage students’ interests\.

-   Tree and plant ID
    
-   Animal habitats
    
-   Archaeology
    
-   Storytelling
    
-   Foraging
    
-   Crafting
    

[Rotating text that reads Programs Rooted in Nature](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/logo-rotating-circle.svg)

[Kid holding up stick in the forest](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/building-in-forest.jpg) [a young girl holding a gigantic pinecone in her arms in a wild and immersive classroom](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/outdoor-learning-pinecones-winter.jpg)

While engaging in outdoor activities, our facilitators will also help your child work on important life skills\.

#### What they’ll learn:

-   Listening
    
-   Teamwork
    
-   Sharing
    
-   Problem\-solving
    

Sessions are all fully supervised so you have peace of mind when dropping your child off for the day\.

## Let’s begin

Choose your child’s age:

-   Ages 3\-5
-   Ages 5\-12

### Ages 3\-5

#### Little Forest School

Nurture your child’s relationship with nature and pique their interest in the environment with our Little Forest School sessions\. The combination of child\-led, interactive educational elements with fun and games will foster socialization, communication, problem\-solving, and observational skills\.

\* All children must be fully potty trained to attend Forest School\. Child must be 3 years of age by the start of the term for which they are registered\.

**Forest School will not be operating for the 2026/2027 school year\.**

### Ages 5\-12

#### Forest School

Our Forest School program encourages your child’s appreciation of nature\. These lessons lay the foundation for kids to develop into secure, at\-ease, and environmentally\-conscious adults\.

**Forest School will not be operating for the 2026/2027 school year\.**
