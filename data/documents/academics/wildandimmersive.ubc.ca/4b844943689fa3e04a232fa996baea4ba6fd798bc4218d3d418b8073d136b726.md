Spring Break Camps

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/IMG_3465.jpg)

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/IMG_0719-scaled.jpg)

Give your child a spring break filled with fresh air, hands\-on learning, and outdoor adventure\. Our 5\-day Spring Break Camp is designed to keep kids engaged, curious, and active while exploring nature at the Alex Fraser Research Forest\. Each day is designed to encourage individual confidence, inspire environmental curiosity, and promote teamwork through interaction with peers\.

-   Orienteering and navigation
    
-   Seed propagation
    
-   Fire building and fire safety
    
-   Wild cooking
    
-   Whittling
    
-   and more\!
    

With each day filled with a balance of exploration, creativity, and practical outdoor skills, this camp encourages confidence, teamwork, and a deeper connection to nature\.

**DATES:** March 16\-20 &amp; March 23\-27, 2026  
**TIME:** 8:30am – 3:30pm daily  
**AGE:** 5\-12 years \(child must be in this age range at the time of camp\)  
**COST:** $300 \+GST / week
