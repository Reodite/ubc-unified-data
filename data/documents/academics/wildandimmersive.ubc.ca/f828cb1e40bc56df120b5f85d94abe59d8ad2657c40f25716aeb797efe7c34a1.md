Spring Break Camps

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/WI-Summer-camp-2024-39.jpg)

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/RobertaMcLeanPhotography_VancouverFamilyPhotographer-152.jpg)

Our spring break camps will take participants through forest and nature exploration with play\-based, hands\-on activities\. Each session is designed to encourage individual confidence, inspire environmental curiosity, and promote teamwork through interaction with peers\.

-   Winter survival
    
-   Habitat tracking
    
-   Bird watching
    
-   Fire building
    
-   Knot tying
    
-   and more\!
    

Each day will be filled with educational aspects and activities along with social interaction and a whole lot of fun along the way\.

DATES: March 16\-20 &amp; March 23\-27, 2026\* \(Both weeks are FULL\. Click below to get on the waitlist\)  
TIME: 8:30am – 3pm daily  
AGE: 5\-10 \(child must be in this age range at the time of camp\)  
COST: $315 \+GST

*\*Participants may only register for 1 week of spring break camp\.*

[Waitlist](mailto:wild.immersive@ubc.ca)

### Kanaka Spring Break Camps

For students of Kanaka Creek Elementary School, we offer three Spring Break day camps during their April break\. Full of hands\-on, play\-based activities, these outdoor camps will encourage participants to gain confidence in nature while fostering environmental appreciation and curiosity\. Each day will be a mix of place\-based play, environmental education, and exploration\.

[Image](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/maple-ridge/spring-break-camps/)

DATES: March 30, April 13 &amp; 20, 2026  
TIME: 8:30am\- 3pm daily  
AGE: 5\-12  
COST: $68 \+GST per day

[Register Now](https://www.mkrf.forestry.ubc.ca/kanaka-spring-break/)
