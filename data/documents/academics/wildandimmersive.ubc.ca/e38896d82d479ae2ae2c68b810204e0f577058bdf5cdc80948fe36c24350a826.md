## **Wild at Home –** Day 1

Today is Day 1 of our Wild at Home online programming, and it’s all about Printable resources about nature, and activities the whole family can enjoy at home\.

### Colouring Pages \(Ages 3\-12\)

[Ollies Woodlot Adventures \- Image of the books](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/ollies-woodlot-adventures-workbook.jpeg)

**[Ollie’s Woodlot Colouring Book](https://woodlot.bc.ca/colouring-book/%29) by the Federation of British Columbia Woodlot Association**

Ollie the Owl’s Adventure takes you to a woodlot licence where you will learn about trees and how a forest is managed sustainably\. The colouring book is full of games, fun information about trees and colouring pages\!

[Book covers of Fun in the Forest and Logging with Splinter](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/fun-with-splinter-workbook.png)

**[Splinter the Pileated Woodpecker Colouring Books](https://www.canadianwomenintimber.com/education) by Canadian Women in Timber**

The Splinter Activity Books include word facts, puzzles, games and worksheets, drawing and colouring projects\. Younger children will enjoy “Fun in the Forest”, while older kids will love the challenges in “Logging with Splinter”\. 

### Colouring Pages \(Ages 12\+\)

[Sea Wolves Colouring Page, part of Wild at Home's day 1 activity printable resources\.](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sea-wolves-colouring-pages.jpeg)

[**Claire Watson Illustrations**](http://clairewatson.com/westcoast-colouring-pages)  
Claire, who lives on Vancouver Island, draws beautiful illustrations of all the wonderful ecosystems we have here in British Columbia\. Get to know more about Claire and her work on her website\!

### Phylo\-mon Card Game

[Printable resources Phylo trading cards with a breakdown of what each part of the card means and some examples](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/phylo-trading-cards-nature-crafts.jpeg)

**[Phylo Card Game](https://phylogame.org/) created by UBC Beaty Biodiversity Museum**

Did you know that the average kid knows more about Pokemon than they do about the animals that live around them? This game is here to change that\! The Phylo Card Game mimics the Pokemon Card game, in a fun, educational way\! This 2\-player game will teach you about local animals, their ecosystems and the dynamic food chain system that surrounds them\!

### Paper Animal Models

[Paper Bird model instructions](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/paper-bird-model-instructions.jpeg)

**[Paper Animal Models](https://www.ducks.ca/resources/educators/paper-animal-models/) by Ducks Unlimited Canada**

Learning about wetlands, wildlife and nature is fun\! The best way is to get outside and explore but on the days that you can’t, our animal models make a fun indoors activity\. Create your own fun art project with these 3\-D paper models\. Nature notes appear in English and several other languages and each piece includes a different wetland creature\.

Discover more home activities and projects on [Ducks Unlimited Canada’s website](https://www.ducks.ca/)\!

### Paper Crystal Gems

[Image of paper gems, one of the printable resources highlighted in Wild at Home\.](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/paper-gems-crafts.jpeg)

**[Paper Crystal Gems](http://www.minieco.co.uk/paper-gems-new-templates/) by MiniEco**

These paper gems can be made using the large or small templates, and make great Christmas tree decorations\! Discover more projects on [MiniEco’s website](http://www.minieco.co.uk/)\!

### Make Your Own Insect

[Step by step guide for how to Create your own insect](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/create-your-own-insect-craft.jpeg)

**[Make your own Insect by Orkin](https://www.orkin.com/scienceeducation/kids-and-teachers-games)**

Does your kid have an imagination? Get them to create their own cool bug using the guidelines on the page\! We can’t wait to see what they come up with\! Be sure to discover more educational activities on [Orkin’s website](https://www.orkin.com/)\!

### Printable Resources for All Ages

These are just a few ideas to get you started\! The internet is full of nature\-inspired printable projects, all you need are some paper, supplies and a working printer\! Have fun and send pictures of your activities\!

 \-Wild &amp; Immersive
