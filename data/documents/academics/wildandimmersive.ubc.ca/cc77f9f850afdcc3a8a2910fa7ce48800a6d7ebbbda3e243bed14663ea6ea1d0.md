Wild &amp; Immersive is proud to announce that they have won the [2023 City of Maple Ridge Heritage Award](https://www.mapleridge.ca/240/Heritage-Awards) – Stewardship of a Community Cultural Asset\.

This award recognizes a group who conserves a physical area, practices, expressions, knowledge and/or skills to advance the appreciation and understanding of a community cultural asset\.

## Special Thanks for this Award

Wild &amp; Immersive would like to thank the City of Maple Ridge’s [Community Heritage Commission](https://www.mapleridge.ca/128/Community-Heritage-Commission) for this recognition, as well as all the families and community members who support Wild &amp; Immersive’s programs\. Without the members of this community, we wouldn’t be able to share our passion for the environment and [outdoor learning](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/maple-ridge/our-story/)\.
