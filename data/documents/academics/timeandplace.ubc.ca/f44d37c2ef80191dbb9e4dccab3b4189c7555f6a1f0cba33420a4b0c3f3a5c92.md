## Collaborators &amp; Acknowledgement

The timeline was developed by a UBC student, [Hanae Tsukada](http://ctlt.ubc.ca/about/people/teaching-learning-professional-development/#hanae), in collaboration with Aboriginal community members at UBC\.

[Amy Perreault](http://ctlt.ubc.ca/about/people/aboriginal-initiatives/), Coordinator of Aboriginal Initiatives at the Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology, was the backbone of the project\. She supported the entire process of the project, including developing the project plans, connecting the project with UBC Aboriginal community members, and providing feedback at different stages of the project\.

[Kim Lawson](http://fnsp.arts.ubc.ca/first-nations-studies/events/kim-lawson.html), Reference Librarian at Xwi7Xwa Library, guided the literature search\.

[Thane Bonar](http://aboriginal.ubc.ca/author/thaneb/), Research and Communications Officer at the First Nations House of Learning, and [Graeme Joseph](http://aboriginal.ubc.ca/strategic-plan/special-initiatives/), Coordinator of Strategic Aboriginal Initiatives at the Office of Vice\-President, Students, provided stories behind the statistical data on Aboriginal students at UBC with a wealth of knowledge and critical insight\.

Michael Wynne, MLIS student, collected the images found in the project, and provided technical support in the construction and maintenance of the timeline\.

This project is indebted to their expertise and generous support\. In addition, the project was informed by and built upon the previous work done by many others who are listed in the references\.
