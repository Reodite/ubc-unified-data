### Electives

While the UBC Faculty of Medicine offers a wide range of postgraduate visiting elective opportunities, our Program is not able to help set up elective rotations for any trainees or residents outside of our own program due to limitations in manpower and clinical resources\.

It is the responsibility of applicants to seek out and arrange their own postgraduate resident electives\.

For more information on eligibility, application procedures, and required documentation, please visit the [Postgraduate Visiting Electives webpage](http://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/prospective-trainees/postgraduate-visiting-electives/) on UBC’s Postgraduate Medical Education Office website\.

### Transfers

Our Program is unable to accept transfers from any program outside of BC\.
