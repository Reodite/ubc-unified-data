If you are in crisis or distress and require an immediate response, please contact the [Crisis Centre](http://crisiscentre.bc.ca/contact-us/) at 1\-800\-SUICIDE \(1\-800\-784\-2433\)\.

The [UBC Resident Wellness Office](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/rcaps/?_gl=1*t8s8b4*_ga*MTkwMjQ3NTkyNy4xNzM4Nzk3NTgy*_ga_661615T9R4*MTczODk3MjU1MC40OC4xLjE3Mzg5NzI3NjkuNjAuMC4w) offers free and confidential counseling within the hours of Monday to Thursday, 8am – 4pm\.

If you have been mistreated or have witnessed mistreatment, please [click here](https://mistreatmenthelp.med.ubc.ca/?login) for supports\.
