Today, over 300 UBC Family Medicine residents are engaged in training across British Columbia\. Our Program, representing the largest Family Medicine residency program in Canada, delivers training at 18 sites, ranging from large urban centres to rural regions of the province\. Each site has its own site director, as well as a number of faculty leads, who are responsible for overseeing the teaching program and meeting program\-wide goals and outcomes\.
