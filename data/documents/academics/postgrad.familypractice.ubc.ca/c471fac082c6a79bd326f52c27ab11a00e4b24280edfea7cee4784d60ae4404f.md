## Site Director Message

Established in 2020, Kelowna Regional builds on 30 years of family physician training excellence in the Okanagan\. Our two Kelowna sites form one connected program, offering a shared vision while showcasing more of what the Okanagan offers\. Kelowna combines a tertiary care centre and a city large enough for diverse clinical experiences, with the collegiality and accessibility of a smaller community—all in one of BC’s most beautiful settings\.

**A Strong Foundation in First Year**  
Both Kelowna cohorts come together for shared academic teaching and a block\-based clinical curriculum\. The regional site includes an additional family medicine block in place of the ICU rotation\. This structure offers broad exposure across core specialties, helping residents build and consolidate clinical skills\. It can be challenging, but it also builds confidence and competence across diverse clinical settings\.

**Second Year: Flexibility and Individualization**  
Second year centres on flexibility and personal choice\. Residents rotate through electives, rural family medicine, and Kelowna family practice, with added time for areas of special interest through selectives\. Residents have considerable autonomy in choosing clinics and experiences aligned with their learning goals and future practice interests\. Residency should help you build the practice you want—so you wake up excited to go to work\.

**Looking Beyond the Clinic**  
We are passionate about preparing residents for the evolving realities of family medicine, including integrating evidence\-based lifestyle medicine into both teaching and clinical practice—an increasingly important part of comprehensive family medicine\.

**A Supportive Place to Learn and Grow**  
Above all, we strive to create a safe, supportive, collegial, and fun learning environment\. Residency is demanding, and we want residents challenged while feeling supported every step of the way\. Our goal: help you work hard, learn, discover your strengths, and become the family physician you want to be—with a team behind you\.

We’d love to hear from you—reach out with questions or connect with our residents\!

## Lead Resident Message

Welcome to the Kelowna Regional Family Medicine Program\! Curriculum and rotation schedules matter, but what makes this program exceptional is its people, opportunity, and flexibility\.

From day one, we’ve been struck by how invested our preceptors are in teaching\. They genuinely want to see us succeed and are generous with their time, whether we’re learning a procedure, refining clinical reasoning, or exploring a niche interest\. As family medicine residents are among the few resident learners in the hospital, we rarely compete for cases\. We’re often first in line for reductions, orthopaedic procedures, emergency interventions, airway management, intubations, and other hands\-on experiences, and may take on senior responsibilities when an internal medicine or paediatric senior isn’t present\.

Throughout R1, we rotate through nearly every area of medicine, building the breadth and confidence needed for increasing independence\. Whatever your goals — comprehensive family medicine, emergency medicine, women’s health, hospital medicine, sports medicine, palliative care, or something else — you’ll find mentors eager to help you build the practice you envision\.

In R2, Regional residents spend four months in a selected rural community and four months developing their future practice in Kelowna\. During the Kelowna portion, residents work in family medicine clinics while tailoring parts of their schedules toward interests like emergency medicine, procedures, palliative care, dermatology, addictions, or women’s health\. Few programs offer this much freedom before graduation\.

The community is just as special\. Some of our closest friendships have come from this program, with people who share a love of medicine, the outdoors, good coffee, and making the most of days off — skiing, pool hopping, golfing, or team multisport\.

Kelowna gives you the best of both worlds: exceptional clinical training and an incredible place to live, learn, and thrive\.

David Sahai \(MD\) &amp; Samantha Seminoff \(MD\), Kelowna Rural &amp; Regional Site Leads
