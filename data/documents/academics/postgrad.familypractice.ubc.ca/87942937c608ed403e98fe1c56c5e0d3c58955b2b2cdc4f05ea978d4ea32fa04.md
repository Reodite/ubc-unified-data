## Site Director Message

Welcome to the Indigenous Family Medicine Site\. We are a distributed site with four separate CaRMS matches – Mainland Vancouver, Victoria, Rural Vancouver Island, and Northern\. We are focused on training family physicians who have a strong interest in working with and for Indigenous peoples in ways that transform health care practices and systems\.

Our program welcomes both Indigenous and non\-Indigenous residents with a focus in anti\-racist approaches to care\. Our residents gain unique advocacy skills to help address the effects of historical, geographic and political factors that impact the health status of Indigenous people in Canada\. Graduates from our program enter practice as competent well\-rounded family physicians who provide care in multiple settings, with the ultimate goal of improving health for Indigenous patients and communities\.

Healthy relationships are critical to supporting healthy individuals and communities and in recognition of this our program focuses on building strong relationships between our residents, Indigenous communities, Elders, and site faculty\. We are deeply respectful of Indigenous ways of knowing and as such incorporate traditional teachings and ceremony into our various academic activities\.

Our residents have opportunities to work in some of the most spectacular Indigenous territories in BC and elsewhere\. If you are someone who is interested in approaching Family Medicine from a unique perspective, are eager to work for Indigenous communities, and like the appeal of a close\-knit program with a focus on relationship, we look forward to welcoming you to our site\!

Dr\. Chelsea Monell  
Site Director

## Lead Resident Message

Welcome to the Indigenous Family Medicine Site\!  We are focused on training family medicine physicians who have a strong interest in working with and for Indigenous peoples in ways that transform health care practices and systems\.  

Our program is rooted in Indigenous ways of knowing and trauma\-informed care, guided by Elders and a two\-eyed seeing approach to medicine\. We seek residents who share ideals of Indigenous Excellence, Cultural Humility, and Anti\-Racism\. If you share this vision—Indigenous or not—we invite you to apply\. 

We are the first Family Medicine Residency site in Canada focused on Indigenous health\. Our program administrators and director are deeply committed to the well\-being of our residents and supporting our learning goals\. This is a small cohort, which allows us to create a close\-knit and supportive community\.  

We have four streams for applications: Vancouver, Victoria, Rural Vancouver Island, and Northern BC\. Our urban centres \(Victoria, Vancouver\) offer inner city medicine at larger medical centres locally, and the opportunity to work with diverse patient populations, while our rural sites primarily offer learning opportunities in small, community\-based centres\. All sites offer flexibility for residents to shape their training through a combination of urban and rural experiences that align with their interests and goals\. 

A highlight of our program is the Indigenous site\-specific academic curriculum which aims to be delivered through an anti\-racist and anti\-colonial lens This includes bi\-monthly virtual academic days incorporating resident wellness time, and 3\-4 in\-person academic weeks together at each of our sites across the province\. These weeks blend hands\-on procedural and didactic sessions with community, Elder teachings, and relationship building\. 

If this resonates with you as a future family medicine physician, we encourage you to consider applying and joining our program\.  

Lead Residents,

Drs\. Krysta Willett, Priscilla Lawson\-Pires and Will Chukra
