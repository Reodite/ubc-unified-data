### Program Goals

-   To graduate residents who have developed the competencies they need to begin independent family practice in diverse community settings, and
-   To deliver an effective academic, clinical, and scholarly educational program\.

### Outcomes

-   Graduates are competent and well\-equipped to begin independent family practice in diverse community settings\.
-   Graduates are inspired, resilient and balanced\.
-   Graduates demonstrate evidence\-based and reflective practice\.
-   The educational program provides a working and learning environment that ensures residents, faculty, and staff feel respected and supported\.

### Logic Model

For a detailed overview of our program goals and how we reach them, please see our [logic model](https://med-fom-fmprpostgrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/06/FP_LogicModel_PostgradResidencyUBC_20220624-1.pdf)\.
