### Is the IMG\-BC Clinical Assessment required to apply?

IMGs seeking to be matched to a residency position in British Columbia for CaRMS 2018 and beyond are required to participate in UBC’s Clinical Assessment Program \(CAP\)\. The CAP provides a transparent and equitable process designed to support UBC residency programs not only assess candidates’ past clinical experience, but evaluate their potential for success in residency training and suitability for working in communities across B\.C\. For more information about UBC’s Clinical Assessment Program, visit the [IMG Office website](http://imgbc.med.ubc.ca/resource/clinical-assessment/)\.

### Is the NAC &amp; MCCQE 1 required?

Yes\. All applicants **must** have passed the NAC and MCCQE 1\. See [CaRMS eligibility criteria](https://www.carms.ca/match/r-1-main-residency-match/eligibility-criteria/)\.

### Can I pass the English Language Proficiency requirement after the CaRMS application process closes?

All language test results must be submitted by the *start* of the rank order period\.

For those applicants who are required to submit evidence of IELTS testing but are having difficulty in securing English language testing appointments due to COVID related restrictions please contact UBC PGME at [postgrad@postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca](mailto:postgrad@postgrad.med.ubc.ca) for more information\.

### Can a PGY3 count as a Return of Service?

No\. IMGs are el igible for Enhanced Skills programs prior to completion of the Return of Service contract, but a PGY3 will defer or pause a Return of Service contract and does not fulfill the Return of Service obligation\. Please review the [Enhanced Skills application process](https://postgrad.familypractice.ubc.ca/enhanced-skills-program/admissions/) for more details\. 

### I am a Canadian citizen studying abroad\. Should I go through the IMG\-BC Clinical Assessment?

Yes\. Canadian citizens studying abroad are considered IMGs and must follow the criteria outlined for IMGs\. For more information about UBC’s Clinical Assessment Program, visit the [IMG Office website](http://imgbc.med.ubc.ca/resource/clinical-assessment/)\.

### Where can I find the Return of Service contract?

A copy of the return of service contract can be found on the UBC [IMG website](http://imgbc.med.ubc.ca/path-to-residency/ubc-post-graduate-year-1/)\.
