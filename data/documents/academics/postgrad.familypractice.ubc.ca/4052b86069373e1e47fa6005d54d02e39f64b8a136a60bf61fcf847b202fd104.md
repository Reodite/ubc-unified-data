## Site Director Message

Welcome to the Abbotsford\-Mission Site\!

You have a lot of big decisions coming up: Which specialty are you going to pursue? Which program? Which city? Rural or urban? 

What do you really want out of a residency program? 

Well – those are big questions\! If you’ve come this far, we hope it’s because you have chosen Family Medicine, one of the most important and impactful specialties\. Our job is to help you become competent in all aspects of Family Medicine\. Whether it’s community practice, ER, hospitalist, OB, rural, prison health, addictions, or palliative, our graduates have worked in all of these\. We hope you will consider our site, which is known for its great teaching, innovation, and supportive community

We are located in the neighboring cities of Abbotsford and Mission, BC\. Family physicians are the main healthcare providers in this region\. You will be placed in a community clinic which will be your home base for two years\. You will also rotate in acute care at Abbotsford Regional Hospital\. Training at ARH includes a Hospitalist rotation covering 160 beds \(FP Hospitalists\), a busy Maternity ward with over 2,800 births per year \(FP Obstetrics\), a tertiary Palliative Care unit, and one of the province’s busiest Emergency Departments run by CCFP\-EM colleagues\. Residents also receive specialty teaching from preceptors in Cardiology, Internal Medicine, Anesthesia, Pediatrics, Surgery, Orthopedics, Psychiatry, and Addictions\. Additional community educational opportunities include Youth Clinic and Prison Medicine\.  

Most importantly, we have a group of dedicated family doctors and specialists who are committed to your education, support your journey into independent practice, and want you to become their colleagues\. Because we have a small number of learners and do not compete with other resident disciplines, you get one\-on\-one teaching, lots of opportunities to perform procedures, and see a large number of patients\. Residents have a lot of responsibility\! And we hope you find fun and friendships along the way\.  

Good luck on your CARMS journey\! 

Dr\. Holden Chow and Dr\. Monika Wojtera

## Lead Resident Message

Welcome to Abbotsford, the best Family Practice Residency site at UBC\! Abbotsford is the “city in the country”, with all the amenities of a city on a rural backdrop\. Residents do inpatient rotations at the Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre, a 300\+ bed tertiary care hospital servicing the Fraser Valley\. Most of our outpatient Family Practice clinics are in Abbotsford, but you could also be placed in Mission for Family Medicine, which is just a 15\-minute drive away\. Every Thursday, you will attend academic half day in the morning and then return to Family Medicine clinic in the afternoon for that longitudinal care component\. 

With just 9 residents, our program is very tight\-knit\. Staff will know you by name, and given our small cohort, residents quickly become great friends\. We are almost always the only learners on any service, putting us first in line for interesting cases and procedures\. That being said, our program is not service based at all, so staff are eager to teach and have you there, but they are not dependent on us for volume\. 

Our ER is a busy level 3\-trauma centre providing lots of hands\-on experience and our maternity ward has high numbers of deliveries for you to manage\. We have high\-yield rotations in hospitalist medicine, nephrology, cardiology, addictions, and palliative care that prepare you for inpatient or outpatient care\. Our call is 1\-in\-4, but only until 11 pm on every rotation except for obstetrics which is a 24\-hour call\. Our graduates practice in every area of family medicine and many stick around Abbotsford and are our preceptors\. Here you will have the flexibility to meet your learning goals, whatever they look like\! 

We’d love to hear from you with any questions about the program\! Good luck and we look forward to meeting you on the CaRMS tour\! 

Dr\. Angela Katelieva and Dr\. Japneet Gill
