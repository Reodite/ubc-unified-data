# **UBC Interview Process**

UBC Family Practice Postgraduate Program will hold live, virtual Multiple Mini Interviews during the UBC interview period\. 

Shortlisted applicants will complete one centralized UBC Family Practice Postgraduate Program interview, regardless of the number of UBC training sites to which they apply\. Individual sites do not conduct separate interviews\. 

The interview is mandatory for shortlisted applicants and will be conducted synchronously through the Kira Talent platform\. Applicants will receive scheduling, login, and technical information with their interview invitations\. 

**Support on Your Interview Day**

A virtual UBC Welcome Room will be available each interview morning via Zoom\. Applicants can join before their interview to ask questions and receive support from the UBC team\. 

The Welcome Room is separate from the interview and is not part of the applicant’s assessment\. 

**Our MMI Approach** 

The Multiple Mini Interview provides applicants with several opportunities to share their experiences, thoughts, and approach to situations relevant to family medicine\.

For the 2027 interview cycle, applicants and interviewers will be instructed to reserve two or more minutes for at least one follow\-up question\. This change was made in response to feedback and is intended to support a more conversational interview experience, with an opportunity to explore an applicant’s response in greater depth\. 

Applicants complete only one UBC Family Practice Postgraduate Program interview and do not need to prepare separately for each training site\.
