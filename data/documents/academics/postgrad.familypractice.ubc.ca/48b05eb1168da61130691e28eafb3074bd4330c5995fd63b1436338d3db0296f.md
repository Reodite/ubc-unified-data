# Prince Rupert

**Number of Residents:** 1CMG  
**Location:** Small Town/ Rural &amp; Remote, BC  
**Community:** 13,400  
**Hospital:** Prince Rupert Regional Hospital

**Curriculum Type:** Longitudinal FP  
**R2 Elective Time:** Resident driven

## The Town

Prince Rupert is a coastal community with a rich history as a multicultural trading hub and is currently home to over 12,000 residents\. Located at the western end of Kaien Island it sits at the end of highway 16 and at the mouth of the Skeena river\.  

It is the ancestral territory of the Ts’mysen Nation and serves as the hub for many surrounding coastal communities including Metlakatla, Lax Kw’alaams, and Kitkatla\. 

Prince Rupert has had many identities over its long history including being the third largest port in Canada, the former halibut capital of the world, the rainiest city in Canada, and as it is currently known: the City of Rainbows\.

## The Clinical Experience

Residents who come to Prince Rupert will gain clinical experience in a setting offering a wide scope of rural opportunities\. At our site we aim to provide an experience that captures the breadth and diversity of skills expected of rural family physicians, and to foster independence and confidence needed for independent practice\. 

As a medical community Prince Rupert is home to 14 family physicians and 2 FP\-anesthetists\. We also have a variety of specialists in the community including 2 general surgeons, an internist, urologist, and an OBGYN\. 

Prince Rupert Regional Hospital is a 30 bed facility that offers learners experiences in inpatient medicine, ambulatory care and procedural skills, ER, OR, maternity and obstetric care, and anesthesia\. At our site we have CT, ultrasound, x\-ray, and laboratory services\. We are also proud to have a wide variety of allied health supports including physiotherapy, occupational therapy, pharmacists, social work, Indigenous patient liaisons, and mental health and substance use nurses\. Adjacent there is also a long term care facility—the Acropolis Manor— supported by our local family physicians as well\.  

Residents will work in two clinics in the community as part of their outpatient clinic experience and will work with a team of supportive physicians familiar with the community and its needs\.  

For those with a special interest in providing care to our outlying communities, there may also be opportunities for outreach\.

As part of the Great Bear Rainforest the community is surrounded by picturesque mountains, ocean, and natural trails for hiking\. For those looking for aquatic activities Prince Rupert provides opportunities for fishing, kayaking, canoeing, and whale and bear watching tours

Prince Rupert is also home to a variety of restaurants with focuses on mediterranean food, sushi, and more\. 

Whatever your particular interest is Prince Rupert has something for everyone to enjoy\.
