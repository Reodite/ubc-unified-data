**Favourite part of your residency experience so far:** 

I have a wonderful cohort of five residents\. We meet every other Wednesday on our academic day and we also have lots of fun socials together\.

**Favourite song:**

The Street Where Wind Lives by pianist Yukiko Fanmura and erhu player Masao Sakashita\.

**Favourite past\-time activity:**

Reading\.

**Favourite restaurant:  
**Saku in Vancouver\. I really enjoy the tonkatsu\!

[Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/06/image_50403329-225x300.jpg)

Dr\. Lucy Ni and her dog, Maple\.

**A book or article you would recommend:** 

We Are All Perfectly Fine by Dr\. Jillian Horton\.

**Who is someone that inspires you?**

My mother inspires me continuously to better myself\. She is intelligent, dedicated, selfless and is always the most witty person in the room\.

**What are you creating in the world right now?**

I hope to create an inclusive health care environment that celebrates diversities\.

**What are you most looking forward to in your career ahead?**

I am looking forward to providing more palliative care education for patients and health care professionals\!
