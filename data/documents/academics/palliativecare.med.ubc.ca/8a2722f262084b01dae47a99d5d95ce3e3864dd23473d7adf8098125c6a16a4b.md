## **Transition to Practice EPAs**

### **1\. Leading a Palliative Medicine Service**

**Key Features:**

-   This EPA focuses on the role of the physician most responsible for patient care\.
-   This includes managing the clinical, operational, and administrative aspects of patient care, including judicious use of resources, and planning for discharge or transitions in care\.
-   It also includes leveraging relationships with colleagues in the interprofessional team to coordinate, expedite and optimize care, as well as supporting and enhancing wellbeing within the team\.
-   It may include other responsibilities of the attending physician, such as supervising and completing assessments for junior learners\. \(e\.g\., “junior attending” role\)\.
-   The observation of this EPA is not based on a single patient encounter, but rather on the resident performance over a period of at least one week\.

**Assessment Plan:**

-   Direct and indirect observation by supervisor
-   Required Numbers: 2 observations of achievement
    -   At least 1 inpatient setting

**CanMEDS Milestones:**

1.  ME 1\.1 Demonstrate responsibility and accountability for decisions regarding patient care, acting in the role of most responsible physician
2.  L 4\.1 Set priorities and manage time to fulfil diverse responsibilities
3.  ME 2\.4 Manage urgent and complex clinical situations
4.  S 3\.4 Integrate best evidence and clinical expertise into decision\-making
5.  COL 1\.2 Work effectively with the interprofessional team
6.  L 4\.1 Integrate supervisory and teaching responsibilities into the overall management of the clinical service
7.  P 4\.3 Monitor and support well\-being within the team

### **2\. Sharing Information with Patients and Families**

**Key Features:**

-   This EPA focuses on the application of communication skills, to include demonstrating empathy and compassion, attending to expressions of emotion, and using plain language\.
-   It includes identifying and managing barriers to giving and receiving information e\.g\., need for translator\.
-   An important aspect of this EPA is creating safety and room for open discussion in conveying relevant information regarding the patient’s case, including conversations about disease trajectory, prognosis, and death\.

**Assessment Plan:**

-   Direct observation by supervisor or other health care professional
-   Required Numbers: 2 observations of achievement
    -   At least 1 observation by supervisor

**CanMEDS Milestones:**

1.  COM 1\.2 Optimize the environment for patient and family comfort, dignity, privacy, engagement, and safety
2.  COM 1\.1 Demonstrate empathy, respect, and compassion
3.  COM 3\.1 Provide accurate information
4.  COM 3\.1 Use plain language and avoid medical jargon
5.  COM 1\.5 Recognize when strong emotions \(such as anger, fear, anxiety, or sadness\) are affecting an interaction and respond appropriately
6.  COM 3\.1 Verify and validate the patient’s and/or family’s understanding
7.  COM 4\.3 Answer questions from the patient and/or family
8.  COM 4\.1 Communicate in a manner that is respectful, non\-judgmental, and culturally aware
9.  COM 5\.1 Document the encounter to convey the discussion and its outcome

### **3\. Managing the On\-Call Duties of Palliative Medicine**

**Key Features**

-   This EPA includes responding to calls from patients and families as well as physicians, surgeons, and other health care professionals and may be observed during daytime as well as overnight/weekend activities\.
-   It includes being available when unexpected/unscheduled events arise, gathering information, recognizing the urgency of a request, triaging, and providing appropriate advice about initial management and/or patient disposition as well as recognizing issues that can be managed remotely \(by telephone\) versus those that require in\-person assessment\.
-   It also requires documentation of clinical encounters and safe and effective handover\.
-   An important aspect of this EPA is recognizing one’s own limits and seeking assistance\.

**Assessment Plan:**

-   Direct observation and/or review of cases and documentation
-   Required Numbers: Collect 2 observations of achievement
    -   At least 2 different assessors

**CanMEDS Milestones:**

1.  P 1\.1 Respond punctually to requests or calls from patients or other health care professionals
2.  ME 2\.2 Gather information to establish the clinical question
3.  ME 2\.1 Determine the urgency of the situation
4.  ME 2\.4 Provide recommendations for management
5.  ME 2\.4 Determine if an in\-person assessment is required
6.  L 2\.1 Apply knowledge of local protocols and resources
7.  P 1\.1 Work within one’s own limits, asking for assistance as needed
