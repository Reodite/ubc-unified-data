This week on Resident Spotlight is Dr\. Henry He is a distinguished member of our faculty, known for his commitment to advancing medical knowledge and patient care\. With a strong focus on both research and clinical practice, Dr\. He brings a wealth of experience and insight to his role\. His dedication to his work is matched by his enthusiasm for teaching and mentoring the next generation of medical professionals\.

**What’s the best part about your job?  
**Bearing witness to the moments of love and strength in each of our patients’ journeys\.

**Favourite song:  
**Hard to pick a single one, but Won’t Live Here by Daniel Caesar\.

**Favourite past\-time activity:  
**Laying down in the park or the beach and watching the clouds pass by\.

**Favourite restaurant in your community:  
**Saku\!\!\! Mouth\-watering katsu \(pork, chicken, and vegetarian options too\) with unlimited rice, salad, and miso soup\! 

**A book or article you would recommend:  
**The Myth of Normal by Gabor Maté and The Life\-Changing Magic of Tidying Up by Marie Kondo; both have drastically changed how I view the impact of trauma on health, and my personal relationship with the material world\.

**Best piece of advice for new physicians:  
**“Pain is what happens to a body, but suffering is what happens to a person” – Dr\. Nicola Macpherson \(The Jedi Approach to Pain, Episode I: Pain Assessment\)\.
