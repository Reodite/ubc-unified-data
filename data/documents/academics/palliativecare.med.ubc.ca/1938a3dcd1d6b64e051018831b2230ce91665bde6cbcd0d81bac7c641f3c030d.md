**Associate Head of Education: Dr\. Mei Wen**

Let’s get to meet our new Associate Heads beyond their impressive CV’s\. Here’s a quick Q&amp;A we did with the newest members  of our leadership team:

1.  [Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/09/Screen-Shot-2022-09-08-at-9.37.47-AM-209x300.png)
    
    Mei Surfing
    
    ****Where is the farthest country you’ve travelled to from Vancouver? What is a memorable moment from this trip?  
    ****The farthest I have travelled is Ghana\. The most memorable part of this trip was hitch\-hiking across the country after being stranded in a remote village in northern Ghana\!
    
2.  **What is your favourite coffee shop and drink of choice?  
    **Birds &amp; the Beets\. And admittedly, I come here more for their freshly baked ginger cookies as I’m not a big coffee drinker\!  
    
3.  **What is the boardgame/card game of choice you would choose to play?**  
    I like anything that involves acting/charades and since I do love Ellen, I would have to say Heads Up\.
