This week, we are featuring Dr\. Sonia Brar\! She is in her second year of pediatric palliative care residency\. Read on to learn more about her hobbies and favourite restaurant in Surrey, BC\!

**What’s the best part about your residency experience?  
**The children and families at Canuck Place\.

[Image](https://med-fom-fpit.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/20231226_135720_Original-300x300.jpeg)

**What are you most looking forward to in your career ahead?  
**Continuing to work within Pediatric Palliative Care\!

**What’s the best part about your job?  
**Knowing that the work we are doing is playing a part in making the worst part of a child’s and family’s life a little less painful\.

**Favourite past\-time activity:  
**Cooking/watching TV

**Favourite restaurant in your community:  
**Apna Chaat House in Surrey, BC

**A book or article you would recommend:  
**The Palace of Illusions\.
