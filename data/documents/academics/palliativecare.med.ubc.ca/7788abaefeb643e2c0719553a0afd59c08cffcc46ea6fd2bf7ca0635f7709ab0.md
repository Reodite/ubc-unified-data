I would like to extend our heartfelt thank you to Dr\. Matthieu Hubert for his dedication and leadership during his time in the educational leads role\. Dr\. Hubert’s commitment to advancing our educational rounds has been invaluable, and we greatly appreciate the time and effort he has put into this important work\.

I am pleased to announce that Dr\. Esaba Kashem will be stepping into the role of Educational Rounds Lead starting on March 27, 2025 \. Dr\. Kashem brings a wealth of knowledge and experience to the position, and we are excited to have her lead our educational initiatives and continue fostering an enriching learning environment\.

Let’s give a warm welcome to Dr\. Kashem in her new role\.
