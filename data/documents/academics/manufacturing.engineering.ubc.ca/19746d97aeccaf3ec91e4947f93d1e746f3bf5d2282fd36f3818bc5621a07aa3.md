## Your Journey Begins Here: Life as a UBC Manufacturing Student

Being a UBC Manufacturing Engineering student means more than just attending classes—it’s about embracing a community, pursuing passions, and creating memories that last a lifetime\.

Whether you’re collaborating on groundbreaking projects, leading a student organization, or enjoying the vibrant campus life, every day is an opportunity to grow, connect, and thrive\.

## Unleash Your Potential: Academics and Beyond

Experience a dynamic learning environment where state\-of\-the\-art labs, cutting\-edge technology, and inspiring faculty fuel your education\. Benefit from peer support through study groups, academic workshops, and guidance from upper\-year mentors\. Gain hands\-on experience through co\-op placements, research opportunities, and industry projects that turn classroom theory into real\-world application\.

[Explore Careers in Manufacturing](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/careers/careers-in-manufacturing "Careers in Manufacturing")

## Make Connections: A Community Like No Other

Join a close\-knit community where you’ll connect with peers from around the globe, sharing knowledge and experiences\. Enjoy a vibrant social scene with activities ranging from movie nights to engineering\-themed socials and retreats\. Take advantage of networking opportunities by meeting industry leaders, alumni, and professors at events, seminars, and through student societies\.

[Find Out About Your Student Clubs](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/student-experience/clubs "Clubs")

## Lead and Innovate: Shape Your UBC Experience

Step into student leadership by leading clubs, organizing events, and representing your peers in student government\. If you’re passionate about innovation, tap into UBC’s entrepreneurial resources to start your own venture\. Expand your horizons with global perspectives by gaining international experience through exchange programs, global seminars, and collaborative projects\.

[Explore Design Teams](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/student-experience/design-teams "Design Teams")
