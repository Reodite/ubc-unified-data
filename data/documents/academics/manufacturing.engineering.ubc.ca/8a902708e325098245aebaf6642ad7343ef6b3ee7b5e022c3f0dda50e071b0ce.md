## Engage with Tomorrow’s Manufacturing Leaders Today\!

The Manufacturing Engineering program prepares tomorrow's engineers for sought\-after careers in the exceptionally demanding and evolving domain of advanced design and manufacturing by providing them with a combination of technical and managerial skills\.

Our graduates:

-   Design, operate, and optimize complex manufacturing systems\.
-   Lead innovations in product development\.
-   Communicate effectively\.
-   Drive efficiencies in production processes, ensuring they are equipped to meet the challenges of a dynamic industry\.

Whether you’re looking to collaborate on innovative projects, share industry insights, or scout top talent, there are many ways to get involved\.

## Industry Partnerships

Collaborate with UBC to work on cutting\-edge research and innovation projects that benefit your business and the academic community\.

## Guest Lectures and Workshops

Share your expertise with students through guest lectures or hands\-on workshops\. Your real\-world experience can provide invaluable learning for our students\.

## Mentorship Programs

Guide the next generation of manufacturing engineers by participating in our mentorship programs\. Help students navigate their career paths, offering advice and insights based on your industry experience\.

## Capstone Projects

Sponsor a capstone project where students tackle real\-world challenges relevant to your industry\. This is a great way to gain fresh perspectives while contributing to the educational experience\.

## Career Fairs and Recruitment

Meet and recruit UBC Manufacturing Engineering students at career fairs and recruitment events\. Discover the talent that can drive your business forward\.
