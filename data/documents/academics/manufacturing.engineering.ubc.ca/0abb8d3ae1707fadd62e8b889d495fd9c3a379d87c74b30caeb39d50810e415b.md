At the UBC Manufacturing Engineering program, we foster innovation through research and projects\.

Our students engage in cutting\-edge research and project work to tackle real\-world challenges, equipping them with crucial skills required for their future careers\. Through hands\-on experience and collaboration with industry leaders, our students drive advancements in manufacturing technologies and contribute to sustainable, efficient, and impactful engineering solutions\.

[Two students demonstrating their project](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_lg/public/Research%2BProjects_Landing_Capstone%20Projects.jpg.webp?h=e9e13113&amp;itok=YA-9wnkB)

### Capstone Projects: Real\-World Solutions, Real\-World Experience

Capstone projects are the pinnacle of our students’ academic journey, allowing them to apply their knowledge gained throughout their degree to solve open\-ended, real\-world industry problems\. These projects provide valuable industry experience and the opportunity to develop innovative solutions\. 

[Learn More About Our Capstone Projects](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/research-projects/capstone-projects "Capstone Projects") 

[Students working on 3D Image scanning](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_lg/public/UBC_20250212_PJ_5103_1.jpg.webp?h=f9c03373&amp;itok=vKQOK8yK)

### Year\-round Projects: Innovation in Action

Our students engage in year\-round projects that ensure continuous learning and hands\-on practical experience\. These projects focus on developing essential skills in engineering products, teamwork, and practical problem\-solving\. Through project\-based learning, students refine their abilities and stay at the forefront of making things happen\. 

[See Ongoing Year\-Round Projects](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/research-projects/year-long-projects "Year-long Projects")
