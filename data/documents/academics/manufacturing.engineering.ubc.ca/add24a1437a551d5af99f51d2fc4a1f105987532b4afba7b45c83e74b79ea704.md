Our program offers a multidisciplinary education in engineering and blends foundational knowledge with hands\-on, project\-based learning in advanced manufacturing technologies\.

Whether you're interested in integrating robotics in factories, developing digital twins, advancing biomanufacturing of life\-saving vaccines, or managing the production of next\-generation EVs, our program equips you with both a recognized degree and the skills needed to succeed in a rapidly evolving industry\. Our program is accredited by the Canadian Engineering Accreditation Board \(CEAB\)\.

[Two students presenting their project\.](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_lg/public/image%202.jpg.webp?h=1c9b88c9&amp;itok=GCum6PZm)

### Innovate\. Design\. Lead\.

Manufacturing engineers are the architects of making things happen, overseeing everything from initial design to final product\. They play a pivotal role in creating efficient, sustainable, and safe manufacturing environments\.

[Explore the World of Manufacturing Engineering](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/our-program/why-manufacturing-engineering "Why Manufacturing Engineering?")

### From Concept to Creation

Our curriculum is designed to develop multidisciplinary skills in engineering\. You'll deep dive into courses covering mechanical, materials, computer, and electrical engineering, along with specialized topics in manufacturing technology and production management\.

[Find Out What You’ll Study at MANU](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/our-program/what-will-you-study-in-manu "What will you study in MANU?")

### Leaders in Innovation

Our faculty are not just educators but pioneers in Manufacturing Engineering\. With extensive industry experience and cutting\-edge research, they bring real\-world insights that shape the future of manufacturing\. Learn from the best and discover how their expertise can enrich your educational journey\.
