We support students by providing a comprehensive array of resources designed to enhance their academic journey at UBC\.

Explore our resources to discover the support that will help you navigate your path to success at UBC\.

### Accessibility

The Centre for Accessibility facilitates disability\-related accommodations and programming initiatives designed to remove barriers for students with disabilities and ongoing medical conditions\. Ensure you have the support you need to succeed\. 

[Learn About the Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility)

[Group of cheerful UBC students posing for a photo\.](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_lg/public/Resources_Image-2_EDI.jpg.webp?h=265e640d&amp;itok=L3YHnLeG)

### Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion \(EDI\)

The MANU Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion \(EDI\) Committee is dedicated to fostering an equitable experience in manufacturing, where individuals are respected and have a sense of belonging and inclusion\. The goal is to integrate EDI within all aspects of the Manufacturing Engineering Program and to address the changing needs of our community, especially for those who are most marginalized\. If you wish to submit feedback or voice your EDI concerns, please fill out the anonymous [APSC EDI Feedback Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_eXxG4K8Qw0B3CGa)\.

[Learn about EDI\.I at APSC](https://apsc.ubc.ca/EDI.I)

### Health and Wellness

Your health should always come first\. It is important to understand what challenges are normal and to know what to do when you do not feel well\. Access a range of resources and support services to maintain your physical and mental well\-being\. 

[Learn About Health and Wellness](https://students.ubc.ca/health)

### International Students

Our diverse community welcomes international students from various educational backgrounds\. If you have inquiries about life as an international student, our International Student Advising team is here to help\. Our advisors are Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants \(RCICs\) or Regulated International Student Immigration Advisors \(RISIAs\) and can support you throughout your UBC journey\. 

[Find out about International Student Advising](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/international-student-advising)

[An APSC student discussing her challenges with a dedicated faculty counsellor](https://manufacturing.engineering.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_lg/public/Resources_Image-5_Counsellor.jpg.webp?h=b3660f0d&amp;itok=WsNXTIdi)

### Counselling services

Counselling Services is your first stop for mental health support at UBC\. Whether you are looking to connect with a counsellor, speak with a doctor, or explore mental health resources on campus and beyond, our team is here to help\. We offer virtual, phone, and in\-person appointments for both initial and follow\-up visits\.

[Counselling Services at UBC](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services/)
