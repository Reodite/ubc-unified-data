## More Than Just Lectures: Dive into UBC’s Dynamic Student Life

There’s more to university than lectures, labs, and late\-night study sessions\. At UBC Manufacturing Engineering, your student experience extends far beyond the classroom\.

You’ll have countless opportunities to meet people from all over the world, try new things, and leave your mark on the university community\. Discover over 30 Engineering Design Teams at UBC, where students collaborate on design projects and gain hands\-on experience with campus and external partners\. Imagine collaborating with friends to build a solar car, heading off on a field trip to Silicon Valley, or shaping your learning environment by participating in student societies and clubs\.
