In partnership with the Faculty of Science departments\-internal LT and IT teams and in close collaboration with the LT\-HUB, CTLT and UBC\-IT, the Skylight LT team offers a range of LT related services and support including: LT eco\-system support, LT consulting, LT innovation and LT\-related research and evaluation\.

-   We coordinate technical support efforts and issues resolution for learning technologies across the Faculty of Science and with partners at UBC central\.
-   We provide personalized and customized guidance to help the faculty and departments select and implement the best learning technology to meet learning and teaching needs\.
-   We conduct feasibility and fit analysis to ensure that technology use aligns with the learning and teaching experience needs\.
-   We support pilots to test and refine new technologies, pedagogies, and concepts in a controlled environment before full\-scale implementation\.
-   We devise and execute long\-term strategic plans for integrating innovative technologies \(Learning Technology, LTs for Data Science, Artificial Intelligence\) into curriculum and courses across all Science departments\.
-   We conduct research studies to analyze how the use of technology impacts different aspects of the learning experience\.
