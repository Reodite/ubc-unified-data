Canvas is UBC’s primary learning management system for delivering and creating customized online course content and assessments\. In Canvas, instructors can easily share course materials \(e\.g\. syllabus, lecture notes, readings\) via various formats \(text or multimedia\), communicate with students \(via messages or announcements\), promote student collaboration, discussion of topics, create quizzes and assignments, calculate grades, administer exams, and much more\.  

Canvas can also be used with other learning technologies such as Kaltura, Zoom, WeBWorK, iClicker, Piazza, and Gradescope\.

### Key Features

-   **Share course content:** share course materials \(e\.g\. syllabus, lecture notes, readings\) via various formats \(e\.g\. text, files, videos, audios, links\)
-   **Communicate with students:** communicate with students using Announcements and Inbox \(the Canvas messaging tool\) 
-   **Create assignments and quizzes:** create assignments of various formats \(e\.g\. online, with external tools, essays, file uploads\) and quizzes \(e\.g\. practice quizzes, graded quizzes, surveys\) 
-   **Facilitate discussions:** create discussion forums for students to ask questions and course topic discussions 
-   **Facilitate group work**: create groups to facilitate group assignments and work 
-   **Manage grades and provide feedback:** assign grades for assessments, manage options for grading, and provide individual feedback to students using the SpeedGrader and Gradebook

### FAQs

##### 1\. **How can I merge different course sections of the same course into the same Canvas site?**

For course section merging, please contact us at [LT\.support@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:LT.support@science.ubc.ca) and include the course section information you would like merged\. 

##### 2\. **How come my students are unable to see my Canvas course site?**  

There are multiple possible reasons why your students are unable to see your Canvas course site\. The following are the most common\. 

###### **Your course is not published** 

If your course is unpublished, your students will not be able to see it\. To publish your course:  

1.  Go to your course in Canvas and click on **Home** from the **Course Navigation** on the left\-hand side\.  
2.  Under “**Course Status**” on the right\-hand side, click on “**Publish**”\.  

[Under "Course Status" on the right\-hand side of the screen, clicck on "Publish" to publsih your course\.](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Canvas_FAQ_2.png)

3.  Once you have published your course, you will see a green confirmation message that your course was published\.  

[Once you have published your course, you will see a green confirmation message that your course was published\.](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Canvas_FAQ_2.2.png)

Learn more about [publishing a course](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Instructor-Guide/How-do-I-publish-a-course/ta-p/1185)\.  

###### **The start date of your course has not arrived yet\.** 

**By default, the course start date is set to the first day of class\.**  

You can check whether your course start date has arrived by going to the **Settings** in the Course Navigation of your course\. To change the course start date to an earlier date, please follow the instructions below:  

1.  Click on **Settings** from the Course Navigation on the left\-hand side \(if not already\)\.  
2.  Click on the “**Course Details**” tab\. 
3.  Change the course participation dates to “Course” and manually add an earlier start date\.  
    1.  **Note**: If you manually change the course start and end dates, we recommend that you also set a course end date\. Otherwise, students will have indefinite access to the site even after the term ends\. 

Learn more about changing the [start and end dates for a course](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Instructor-Guide/How-do-I-change-the-start-and-end-dates-for-a-course/ta-p/452354)\. 

###### **Your course material is locked**  

It’s possible that you have locked modules, files, assignments, discussions, or quizzes\.  

**For Modules:**  

1.  Click on **Modules** from the Course Navigation in the left\-hand side\.  
2.  Click on the 3 dots \(right side\) of a module and click on “**Edit**”\. 

[Click on the 3 dots on the right side of a module and click on "Edit"\.](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Canvas_FAQ_2.3.png)

3.  If selected, deselect the module\.  

[In the settings of a module, if the option "Lock until" is selected, deselect the module\.](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Canvas_FAQ_2.4.png)

4.  Click on “**Update Module**” to save changes\.  

[If you made changes, click on "Update Module" to save any changes that you made\.](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Canvas_FAQ_2.5_0.png)

**For assignments:**  

1.  Click on **Assignments** from the Course Navigation on the left\-hand side\.  
2.  Click on an assignment and then on “**Edit**”\. 
3.  Scroll down to the section called “**Assign**”\.  
4.  Check if there is a date in “Available from” and “Until”\. If there is one, remove it or change it so students can access the assignment\.  

[Check if there is a date in “Available from” and “Until”\. If there is one, remove it or change it so students can access the assignment\.](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Canvas_FAQ_2.6.png)

5.  If you make any changes, click on “**Save**” afterwards to save those changes\.  

For discussions and quizzes, please follow the same steps as above\.

##### 9\. **Why are students able to see their quiz responses?** 

When you set up your quiz, you probably chose a [quiz option](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Instructor-Guide/What-options-can-I-set-in-a-quiz/ta-p/683) that allows your students to see their answers after the quiz\.  

To disable this option:  

1.  Go to your quiz and click on **Settings** from the Course Navigation\. 
2.  In the **Options**, deselect the option “**Let Students See Their Quiz Responses**” and deselect any options below it \(e\.g\. “Only Once After Each Attempt”, “Let Students See The Correct Answers”\)\. 

##### 10\. Some students report not being able to see the html files in my course\. How can I fix this? 

This issue in Canvas seems to be related to html files and third party cookies in particular\.

This table shows the browsers in which students are generally able or unable to view and download HTML files in Canvas\. 

|  |  |  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | **Firefox** | **Firefox \(Private browser\)** | **Chrome** | **Chrome \(Incognito\)** | **Safari** | **Safari \(Private browsing\)** |
| **View HTML Files** | Yes | Yes | Yes | ***No*** | ***No*** | ***No*** |
| **Download HTML Files** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Here is what students might see in Chrome Incognito and in Safari \(Normal and Private Browsing\):  

[Access denied message when accessing html files in Chrome Incognito and Safari](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Access%20denied%20message%20in%20Chrome%20incognito%20and%20Safari.png)

Please follow the suggestions below to fix this:  

-   Recommend your students to use Firefox \(Normal and Private\) and Chrome \(Normal\) to view and download the html files instead of using other browsers
-   If your students still want to use Safari \(Normal and Private\), they can go to Safari Settings and do the following :  
    -   1\. Settings &gt; deselect "Prevent cross\-site tracking" in Privacy, 
    -   2\. Ensure "Enabled Javascript" is enabled in Security 
    -   3\. Ensure "Block all cookies" is deselected in Advanced 
-   For Safari Private, your students will probably need to close Safari, clear History, and login into Canvas again for the new settings to work 
-   If your students still want to use Chrome Incognito, they can either:  
    -   Enable third party cookies \(Settings &gt; click on Third\-party cookies &gt; click on "Allow third\-party cookies"\) OR  
    -   Keep their settings as is and add "canvas\.ubc\.ca" to the "Sites allowed to use third\-party cookies by clicking on "Add" in Settings &gt; Third\-party cookies\.
