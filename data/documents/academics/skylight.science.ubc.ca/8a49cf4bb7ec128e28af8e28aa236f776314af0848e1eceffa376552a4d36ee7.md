iClicker Cloud is an online student response system that allows students to engage with class material using their mobile device or laptop\. Instructors can collect and grade student responses to questions\. Instructors can receive instant feedback from students and evaluate student learning and understanding of concepts\.  

### Key Features

-   **Instant feedback:** distribute and receive automatic grade results after a quiz or poll 
-   **Track student attendance**: observe student attendance and trends in a course 
-   **Offers variety of polling question types:** use multiple choice, multiple answers, numeric, and short answers 
-   **Maintain active engagement of students**: use polls or quizzes to assess student understanding of concepts and help students stay engaged 
-   **Access on different devices**: accessible with a mobile phone, laptop, and other device \(**Note**: students no longer require a physical iClicker\) 
-   **Online gradebook**: provide real\-time reports of student grades 

### Getting Started

To use iClicker Cloud in your class, you will first need to [install iClicker Cloud on your computer and create an account](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-instructor-guide/#install-and-create-account) and then [create an iClicker course](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-instructor-guide/#create-course)\.  

**Note**: To download iClicker Cloud, you will need either a 64\-bit processor running Windows 10 with \.NET 4\.5 or newer or macOS High Sierra \(10\.13\) or newer\.

### FAQs

#### 1\. **How can I provide my TA with access to the iClicker Cloud course?** 

To provide your TA with access to your iClicker Cloud course, you need to manually add them as an instructor\. 

1.  In the iClicker Cloud instructor portal, go to **People &gt; Instructors** and select **Add Instructor\.** 

2.  You will need to use the TA’s ubc\.ca email address that they are using in Canvas\. Once you are done filling in their email and name, under **Role**, select **Teaching Assistant**\.  

3.  Once you have added the TA\(s\), they can run class sessions through the iClicker Cloud application directly and manage the course results through the [iClicker Cloud Instructor login](https://instructor.iclicker.com/#/onboard/login) web interface\.  

**Note**: TAs are currently unable to access iClicker Cloud with the "iClicker Sync" link in Course Navigation in Canvas\. 

#### 2\. **How can I merge multiple student iClicker Cloud accounts?**  

You can follow the instructions from the LT guide to learn [how to merge multiple student iClicker Cloud accounts](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/iclicker-cloud-instructor-guide/#merge-multiple-accounts)\.
