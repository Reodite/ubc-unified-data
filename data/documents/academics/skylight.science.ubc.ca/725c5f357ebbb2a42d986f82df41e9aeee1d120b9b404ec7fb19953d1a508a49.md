For more than 20 years, we have worked with the UBC Science community and external partners to expand the body of theoretical and practical knowledge about teaching science and help students expand the depth and breadth of their learning\. Our goal is to create an environment that supports reflective teaching and learning practices and facilitate discourse and system\-wide transformational improvements in science education\.

## Projects and Initiatives

### [Enhancing Lab Accessibility in Science](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/enhancing-lab-accessibility-in-science)

Launched in 2025, this project aims to enhance accessibility in undergraduate laboratory courses by partnering with students, instructors, and support staff to identify barriers and develop practical resources, training, and community support for more inclusive hands\-on learning experiences\.

[Learn more about enhancing lab accessibility](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/enhancing-lab-accessibility-in-science)

### [UBC Science AI Curriculum Initiative](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/ubc-science-ai-curriculum-initiative)

The UBC Science AI Curriculum Initiative is a program providing curricular and instructional design support to UBC Science instructors developing or redesigning courses with an emphasis on artificial intelligence \(AI\) concepts or tools\. The program aims to accelerate innovation in AI education across the Faculty, strengthen curriculum alignment within and across programs, and build instructor capacity for the ethical, inclusive, and responsible integration of AI into teaching and learning\.

[Learn more about the UBC Science AI Curriculum Initiative](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/ubc-science-ai-curriculum-initiative)

### [Learning Analytics and Teaching and Learning Data Access](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/learning-analytics-and-teaching-and-learning-data-access)

Learning analytics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and reporting data about learners and learning environments in order to understand and optimize learning\. Skylight is working to analyze the use of teaching and learning data in UBC Science with the goal of providing faculty with resources and supporting departments and programs with data\-driven decisions toward student success and research/evaluation projects related to teaching and learning\.

[Learn more about learning analytics](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/learning-analytics-and-teaching-and-learning-data-access)

### [Inclusive Teaching in the Sciences](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/inclusive-teaching)

Inclusive teaching is an approach to develop equitable, inclusive, and engaging learning opportunities for all students across disciplines\. It draws on universal design of learning as well as anti\-colonial and critical curricula and pedagogies to facilitate active, student\-centered, and authentic learning experiences that value multiple ways of knowing\.

[Learn more about inclusive teaching](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/inclusive-teaching)

### [ISP Engagement in Skylight](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/skylight-ISP-engagement)

In 2021, the Skylight team started a process of reflection, planning, and learning to work toward the goals outlined in UBC’s Indigenous Strategic Plan \(ISP\)\. We have documented our process to provide a roadmap and resources for other groups interested in implementing the ISP within their academic unit at UBC\.

[Learn more about Skylight's engagement with the ISP](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/skylight-ISP-engagement)

### [STEM Education Research Exchange](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/sere)

The STEM Education Research Exchange \(SERE\) committee organizes regular cross\-disciplinary meetings with a focus on discipline\-based education research \(DBER\), Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\) research, and pedagogical practices in STEM at UBC’s two main campuses\.

[Learn more about SERE](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/sere)
