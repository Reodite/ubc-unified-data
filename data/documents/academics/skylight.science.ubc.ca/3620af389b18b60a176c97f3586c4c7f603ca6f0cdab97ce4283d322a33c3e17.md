## Skylight Development Grants

-   For Science teaching and learning resource development projects, including those with an EDI focus
-   Up to $5,000 per project
-   Biannual cycle 
-   [https://skylight\.science\.ubc\.ca/grants/skylight](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/skylight)
-   UBC Science instructors and staff must be listed as principal applicants or co\-applicants; students may be listed as co\-applicants

## Science Student Equity Diversity and Inclusion \(EDI\) Funds

-   For speakers and events that benefit students and are related to EDI, teaching, and learning
-   Up to $1,000 per speaker or event
-   Rolling basis until all funds are depleted
-   [https://skylight\.science\.ubc\.ca/grants](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants)
-   For more info: [skylight@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:skylight@science.ubc.ca)
-   UBC Science students, instructors, and staff

## Strategic Equity &amp; Anti\-Racism Enhancement Fund

-   This fund is designed to support community\-led initiatives that advance broad campus equity and anti\-racism goals and priorities aligned with UBC’s StEAR Framework and Roadmap for Change\.
-   Up to $10,000 per project
-   Biannual cycle
-   [https://equity\.ubc\.ca/resources/funding\-opportunities/strategic\-equity\-anti\-racism\-enhancement\-fund\-faculty\-staff\-stream/](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/funding-opportunities/strategic-equity-anti-racism-enhancement-fund-faculty-staff-stream/)
-   UBC \(both campuses\) faculty, staff, and students

## Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(TLEF\)

-   For teaching and learning enhancement projects, including those with an EDI focus
-   Up to $200,000 for Large TLEF projects and up to $50,000 for Small TLEF projects
-   Annual cycle
-   [https://tlef\.ubc\.ca/](https://tlef.ubc.ca/)
-   UBC Vancouver instructors, staff, and students are eligible to apply as principal applicants for TLEF funding\. If the Principal Applicant is a student, the application must be supported by a Co\-applicant who is a faculty member\.

## Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\) Seed Fund

-   For projects that research and evaluate impactful pedagogies, including those with an EDI focus
-   Up to 70 hours for a SoTL Specialist, up to $200 for research expenses, and up to $500 for presentations in meetings/conferences
-   Biennial cycle
-   [https://isotl\.ctlt\.ubc\.ca/services/sotl\-seed\-program/](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-seed-program/)
-   UBC Vancouver; priority is given to faculty members who teach at the undergraduate level and who have not had an active seed project in the last 12 months

## TA Training Fund

-   Provides financial and consultative support to Faculties for the development, delivery, and assessment of TA training, which could include EDI training
-   Biennial cycle
-   [https://ctlt\.ubc\.ca/programs/graduate\-student\-ta\-programs/ta\-training\-program/ta\-training\-support/](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/graduate-student-ta-programs/ta-training-program/ta-training-support/)
-   UBC Vancouver

## Community\-University Engagement Support \(CUES\) Fund

-   Provides financial support for community\-university partnerships that benefit communities across the province and advance collaborative research, teaching, and learning\. 
-   Up to $25,000
-   Annual cycle
-   [https://communityengagement\.ubc\.ca/scholarly\-resources/cues/](https://communityengagement.ubc.ca/scholarly-resources/cues/)
-   Community groups working with UBC on a shared social, environmental, health\-related, or cultural project
