Skylight offers early internal feedback on potential submissions with the objective of assisting prospective applicants with the proposal development process\. 

Skylight staff can help applicants connect with similar existing and/or proposed Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(TLEF\) projects \(including cross\-faculty applications\), connect with support units such as the Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology \(CTLT\), and provide pedagogical, technical, evaluation, project planning and/or budget consultation\. If you are submitting a Large TLEF, Skylight staff are also available to work with you on your Letter of Intent \(LOI\) which is due mid\-July\. 

Please visit the [TLEF website](https://tlef.ubc.ca/application/) for up\-to\-date information on the application process\.

## Course Releases for 2027/28 Small TLEF Projects

For this funding round, Small TLEF applicants are eligible to request a course release up to a maximum of $10,000\. The cost of a course release is split 50/50 between the Office of the Provost &amp; VP Academic and the Faculty\. The Faculty's portion of the cost is funded by the applicant's home department\. Applicants must secure support and confirmation of matching funds from their home department before including a course release request in their application\.

Please visit the [Budget Considerations](https://tlef.ubc.ca/application/developing-your-tlef-budget/) page on the TLEF website for more information\.

## Consultation Sessions

For information and support in developing your proposal, please register for the [TLEF General Information and Consultation Session](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/tlef-general-information-and-consultation-session/) on September 23, 2026\. CTLT will also host a series of [online drop\-in TLEF proposal consultation sessions](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/tlef-oer-fund-online-proposal-consultation-sessions-2026/) between September 17 and November 16 for potential TLEF applicants\. Register for the TLEF orientation and consultation sessions by visiting the [CTLT events website](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/event-category/tlef/)\.

You can also send in a [consultation request](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_42SmwyMDBioYVGS) to CTLT if you have any questions or want feedback on your TLEF idea or application\.

## Contact Information

The Faculty Liaison serves as the primary point of contact for TLEF applicants in the Faculty of Science\. This position is temporarily vacant, but we anticipate filling it in the coming months\. 

In the meantime, please direct any questions to Jeff Miller, Senior Associate Director, Projects and Faculty Partnerships \([jeff\.miller@ubc\.ca](mailto:jeff.miller@ubc.ca)\), or Gülnur Birol, Director of Skylight \([birol@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:birol@science.ubc.ca)\)\.   
  
You may also want to connect with your departmental [Science Education Specialist \(SES\)](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/contact) for guidance and support in developing your proposal\.

## Available Skylight Support

### Proposal Development Support

The Skylight early internal feedback submission process invites faculty members, students and/or staff to receive feedback and support on the development of TLEF grant proposals\. The areas we can provide advice and/or support include: 

-   Project idea development, including pedagogy and learning technology\.
-   Helping with decisions on grant size \(large or small TLEF\)\.
-   Project plans:
    -   needs analysis;
    -   scoping;
    -   timelines;
    -   budgets;
    -   resourcing \(e\.g\. media services support, staffing decisions\);
    -   evaluation;
    -   conversation facilitation\.
-   Connecting project team with resources and support services across UBC and Dean’s office\.
-   Providing feedback on proposal drafts including but not limited to budgets, timelines, pedagogy, learning technology and evaluation\.

If you would like to receive early feedback, please contact Jeff Miller, Senior Associate Director of Projects and Faculty Partnerships \([jeff\.miller@ubc\.ca](mailto:jeff.miller@ubc.ca)\), or Gülnur Birol, Director of Skylight \([birol@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:birol@science.ubc.ca)\), as soon as possible\.

### Project Partnership

Skylight may be available to partner with project teams\. We have expertise in discipline\-based education research methodologies and the scholarship of teaching and learning, qualitative and quantitative methods, evaluation for quality assurance, course design, pedagogy, learning technology\-enabled pedagogy, instructional design, science communication, interdisciplinary science, learning analytics, and strategic planning\. We also have experience facilitating and supporting the coordination of large projects and teams\.

Please contact Gülnur Birol, Director of Skylight \([birol@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:birol@science.ubc.ca)\), to discuss possible partnerships\.
