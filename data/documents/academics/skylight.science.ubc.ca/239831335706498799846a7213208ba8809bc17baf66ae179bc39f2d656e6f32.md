Piazza is an online discussion tool that facilitates discussion of topics in a question\-and\-answer format with a dedicated area for students and instructors to respond to questions\. Students can post and answer questions, help their peers outside of the classroom, and encourage discussion\. Instructors can answer student questions, which provide correct and “expert” answers to students and are marked with a yellow symbol, differentiating their posts from student posts\. Piazza is also fully integrated with Canvas and offers other interactive features, such as anonymous posting and polls\.  

### Key Features

-   **Question\-and\-answer format:** ask questions and discuss topics in a question\-and –answer format 
-   **Student collaboration**: multiple individuals can contribute and answer questions by editing the same post to formulate the best answer to a question 
-   **Open learning environment**: anonymously post questions and answers 
-   **Check student understanding**: create polls to assess student understanding of class concepts 
-   **Announcements:** create announcements to inform students of important class information 
-   **Canvas integration**: easily accessible as Piazza is fully integrated with Canvas  

### Getting Started

To start using Piazza, you must first [enable Piazza in your Canvas course](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/piazza-instructor-guide/#enable-in-canvas) \(either by adding Piazza to the Course Navigation or adding a link to Piazza from a module\)\. Next,  [create and set up a Piazza class](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/piazza-instructor-guide/#create-and-set-up) for your students to enroll in\.  

#### Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act \(FIPPA\)

If you decide to use Piazza in your course, you must use Canvas integration to ensure its use complies with the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act \(FIPPA\)\. The Canvas integration link directs students to the proper Piazza course and masks the student’s identity before it is sent to the site\. To comply with BC privacy legislation, students will need to create a Piazza account and agree to the terms of use the first time they use the tool\. While Piazza adheres to strict U\.S\. privacy regulations \(FERPA\), UBC cannot guarantee the security of student’s private details on servers outside of Canada\. Students should be reminded to exercise caution whenever using personal information, and that they may use a pseudonym to protect their privacy if they have concerns\. Creating a Piazza course outside of Canvas and providing students with the external URL **is not FIPPA compliant and is not recommended\.**

### FAQs

##### 1\. How can I restrict access to my Piazza course only to students who are registered in my Canvas course?

To restrict access to only students registered in your Canvas course, you can set up an access code\. Once the access code is shared with your students \(e\.g\. through your course syllabus, Canvas announcement\), they will be asked to enter an access code when joining your Piazza course\. Entering the access code will become the only way to join your Piazza course\. 

Visit the Piazza guide on [how to set up an access code in your Piazza course](https://support.piazza.com/support/solutions/articles/48000616720-instructors-set-an-access-code)\.  

**Note**: Being a registered student does not allow them to bypass the access code\. Registered students will still need to enter the access code in order to join the Piazza course\.  

**Note**: The access code will block non\-registered students from joining your Piazza course only to an extent\. For example, the access code could be accidentally shared by registered students to non\-registered students\. If this happens, the only option is to remove the non\-registered students manually\.  

To remove students manually: 

1.  Go to your Piazza course\.
2.  Click on "Manage Class" at the top\.
3.  Select all of the students you want to remove from the roster\.
4.  Click on "Unenroll selected students"\.
