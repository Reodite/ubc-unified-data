WeBWorK is an online assessment platform focused on numeric problems and questions\. It is integrated with Canvas, allowing for easy access and grade syncing\. Instructors can create custom problems or select from a library of problems with various assessment types \(homework sets, tests, proctored tests\) to assess student understanding\. By completing problems at their own pace and receiving instant feedback, students are empowered to practice and strengthen their understanding of new concepts\. 

### Key Features

-   **Different assessment types:** create homework sets and quizzes \(tests, proctored tests\) to assess student understanding 
-   **Easy access and flexible format**: easily access WeBWorK from Canvas and work on homework sets at one’s own pace 
-   **Customize problems**: create problems or choose from thousands of problems in the Open Problem Library 
-   **Automatic grading:** provide students with instant feedback after students have submitted their answers 
-   **Randomly generated problems:** parameters are randomly generated within the problems 

### Getting Started

To use WeBWorK in your course, you will first have to [create a WeBWorK course using Canvas](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/webwork-instructor-guide/#create-course)\.  

### Instructions

-   Create and edit homework sets using [publicly available problems](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/webwork-instructor-guide/#create-public-problem-assignment) or create [custom problems](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/webwork-instructor-guide/#create-custom-problem-assignment) 
-   [Create tests \(tests and proctored tests\) and exams](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/webwork-instructor-guide/#create-assessment) 
    -   Tests are quiz versions of a homework set with features like passwords and time limits\. 
-   [Copy homework sets between WeBWorK courses](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/webwork-instructor-guide/#copy-problems) 
-   [Set\-up grade syncing between WeBWorK and Canvas](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/webwork-instructor-guide/#set-up-grade-syncing) 
-   [Review student progress on assessments and submit assessments for students](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/webwork-instructor-guide/#review-student-progress) 
-   [Export grades from WeBWorK to a CSV](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/webwork-instructor-guide/#export-grades) 
    -   Edit your grades in a spreadsheet instead of syncing them\.

### FAQs

#### **1\. How do I copy over WeBWorK sets from my previous Canvas courses?**

You can follow the instructions in our guide to [copy WeBWorK assignments from a previously\-offered course](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:WeBWorK/How_to_reuse_individual_assignments_from_term-to-term)\. 

If the WeBWorK assignments you would like to copy over include customized problems, you can follow the instructions in our guide on [how to reuse assignments with customized problems](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:WeBWorK/How_to_reuse_assignments_with_customized_problems)\.  

#### **2\. Why are the students’ grades not being updated in Canvas even though I have set up Canvas grade sync?**

Grade sync between WeBWorK and Canvas occurs after a student’s submission\. The automatic grade sync between WeBWorK and Canvas occurs roughly once every day and will capture the state of the WeBWorK grade at that time\. It's important to note that there can be a delay of a day or two for the grade sync during busy times\. 

You can also manually sync the grades through the **Hmwk Set Editor**\. You can refer to the following page for more information on [Canvas grade syncing](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:WeBWorK/How_to_set_up_Canvas_grade_sync#Important_information_about_Canvas_grade_sync)\. 

#### **3\. How do I export my grades from WeBWorK onto Canvas without using the automatic grade sync?**

You can follow the instructions from our guide on [how to download your WeBWorK assignment grades and upload them to Canvas](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:WeBWorK/How_to_download_grades_and_upload_them_to_Canvas)\.  

#### **4\. Can I use WeBWorK on a tablet or mobile device?**

Yes, you can\. However, we do not recommend this\. Launching WeBWorK from the Canvas mobile app may cause some issues in loading the WeBWorK portal\. Using Safari on your tablet/mobile devices may also cause some loading errors\.

#### **5\. Is it possible to check a student’s WeBWorK submission?**

Yes, you can track a student’s progress in WeBWork\. You can click on **Student Progress** on the left navigation menu and click into the desired information you’d like to check upon\.

#### **6\. My students are having difficulties submitting answers onto their WeBWork\. Can I submit on their behalf?**

Yes, you can\. You can refer to our guide to learn [how to submit on behalf of students](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:WeBWorK/How_to_submit_drafts_on_behalf_of_students_for_grading)\.  

#### **7\. How do I provide WeBWorK accommodation for my students?**

You can refer to our guide to help [extend an individual WeBWorK assignment](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:WeBWorK/How_to_extend_individual_WeBWorK_homework_sets) for your student\.  

If you are using WeBWorK for your exams, you can refer to the guides below on how to provide students with accommodation: 

-   [How to extend exam time for an individual student](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:WeBWorK/How_to_extend_exam_time_for_an_individual_student) 
    

-   [How to give an extra attempt for an individual student](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:WeBWorK/How_to_give_an_extra_attempt_for_an_individual_student) 
    

#### **8\. Why are the pictures in my WeBWorK set not loading?**

This may have happened because you have modified the graph without adding an additional line of code that allows WeBWorK to refresh the image\. You can refer to the instructions in our guide to learn [how to add the code correctly to images](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:WeBWorK/How_to_refresh_the_cached_image_PG_problem)\.  

#### **9\. How do I change the email address that receives emails when students click on the "Email WeBWorK TA" button?**

You can refer to the instructions in our guide on [how to set up the email infrastructure tool](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:WeBWorK/How_to_set_up_Email_Instructor_feature)\.
