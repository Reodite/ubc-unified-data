Kaltura is a media platform that allows instructors and students to record, upload, and share video content directly in Canvas courses\. Kaltura allows users to embed media into any item with a Rich Content Editor, edit media, create interactive video quizzes and trim clips from existing media on Canvas\.  

**Important:**In April 2023, UBC moved Kaltura videos to the Kaltura cloud as part of the [Enterprise Video Platform](https://lthub.ubc.ca/initiatives/evp-project/kaltura-cloud-migration/) project\. 

### Key Features

-   **Upload media**: upload and store audio and video files to My Media in Canvas 
-   **Record media:** record screen, audio, and webcam using either the [New Webcam Recorder or the Kaltura Capture application](https://knowledge.kaltura.com/help/kaltura-capture-admin-config-express-capture-vs-kaltura-capture) 
-   **Share and embed in Canvas:** share media through the Media Gallery in Canvas and embed media in places with a Rich Content Editor 
-   **Create video quizzes:** create interactive video quizzes \(multiple choice, true/false, reflection point\) to increase student engagement 
-   **Captions and transcripts**: add closed captions and transcripts to videos to increase accessibility of media to students 

### Getting Started

Kaltura is already integrated with Canvas\. Once your video has been recorded, you can upload your media to My Media in the Course Navigation\. Follow the instructions below to share your video in your Canvas course\.

### FAQs

##### **1\. Why do Kaltura Interactive quizzes not appear in the Media Gallery?**

Kaltura interactive video quizzes cannot be added to the Media Gallery\. Instead, you can assign interactive quizzes as assignments to allow them to be synced with the Gradebook:  

1.  Select **External Tool** for submission type and select **Kaltura \- Video Quiz**\. 
2.  Select a video from **My Media**\. 
3.  Click on **Select** again in the **Configure External Tool** window\. 
4.  Save and publish the assignment\. 

##### **2\. How do I allow students to download videos?**

To enable media downloads: 

1.  Go into **Media Gallery** and select the video you’d like students to download\. 
2.  Click on the three dots, then select the **Edit** icon\. This will bring you to a new page, where you can edit your video\. 
3.  Scroll down and select **Downloads**\. 
4.  Select the format you’d like to make available, then click on **Save**\. 

Students should now be able to find the download option under **Details**\. 

##### **3\. How do I migrate media from a previous term \(even content that I do not own\)?**

You may want to use media in a course where the original ownership rests with someone else\. First, you need to ensure that the copyright holder\(s\) of the media have agreed to provide UBC with a non\-exclusive irrevocable license to use the media as part of UBC course materials\. Next, you can contact the LT Hub at  [LT\.hub@ubc\.ca](mailto:LT.hub@ubc.ca) to help transfer administrative ownership of the media from their Kaltura account to yours\. In taking on ownership, you will accept full responsibility of the content and agree to adhere to any copyright permissions previously assigned to the media\.  

##### **4\. How to become the owner of someone else’s Kaltura media?**

You need to ensure you have written approval from the original copyright holder\(s\) of the media\. Please forward the approval to Science LT at [LT\.support@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:LT.support@science.ubc.ca) and request to transfer the administrative ownership of the media to your account\. In taking on ownership, you will accept full responsibility for the content and agree to adhere to any copyright permissions previously assigned to the media\.

Please refer to the guide for [downloading, editing, and uploading a video from Zoom to Kaltura](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/Download_Edit_Upload_Video_from_Zoom_to_Kalutra.pdf)\. 

For further FAQs, visit the LT Hub’s [Kaltura Instructor Guide’s FAQs](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/kaltura-instructor-guide/#faq) at the bottom of the page\. 

##### 6\. **How can I publish a video outside of Canvas?**  

If you need to share videos **outside of Canvas**, you can upload them to the Kaltura web portal \(learning\.video\.ubc\.ca\.\) and obtain a shareable link or embed code to post on another platform\. 

###### **Upload a video to the Kaltura web portal** 

1.  Log into learning\.video\.ubc\.ca using CWL\. 
2.  Click on **"\+Create”** blue buttonin the upper right corner\. 
3.  Click on **Upload**\.   
4.  Drag and drop your files or click on **Select Files\.** 
5.  After your media has been processed, click on **Edit\.** 
6.  Add description and tags under the **Details** tab\. 
7.  Select publishing status as unlisted and set copyright settings as per your preference under the **Publishing** tab\. 
8.  Click on **Save\.** 

###### **Embed/Link a video from the Kaltura web portal to another platform** 

1.  Click on your profile in the top right corner\. 
2.  Click on **My Media**\. 
3.  Click on and go into the specific video you would like to embed/link\. 
4.  Scroll below the video and click on the **Share** tab\. 
5.  To get a shareable link to the video, go to the **Link to Media Page** tab\. You can copy the shareable link that starts with learning\.ubc\.ca\. 
6.  To get the embed code of the video, go to **Embed** tab\. 

##### 7\. What are my options for recording a video using Kaltura?

There are two options to record a video using Kaltura: \(1\) **New Webcam Recorder** and \(2\) **Kaltura Capture**\. The table below describes the differences between the two\.  

- **Row 1**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    **New Webcam Recorder**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    **Kaltura Capture**

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    -   No application download required before recording 
    
    -   Records you but does not record your computer screen 
    
    -   Cannot pause while recording 
    
    -   Automatically uploads to your personal repository \(**My Media**\) in Canvas\.
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    -   Must download Kaltura Capture before recording 
    
    -   Records you and your computer screen 
    
    -   Can pause while recording 
    
    -   Automatically uploads to your personal repository \(**My Media**\) in Canvas

Learn more about the features and the differences between the [New Webcam Recorder and Kaltura Capture](https://knowledge.kaltura.com/help/kaltura-capture-admin-config-express-capture-vs-kaltura-capture)\.  

##### 8\. How can I add captions and transcripts to my videos on Canvas?

To add captions to your videos, please visit the guide from the Kaltura Knowledge Center for [uploading and managing captions\.](https://knowledge.kaltura.com/help/upload-and-manage-captions)  

To add transcripts to your videos, please visit the guide from the Kaltura Knowledge Center for [enabling and displaying transcripts](https://knowledge.kaltura.com/help/transcript)\.  

##### 9\. How do I create chapters in my videos on Canvas?

To create chapters in your videos, please visit the guide from the Kaltura Knowledge Center for [creating and managing chapters](https://knowledge.kaltura.com/help/manage-chapters-and-slides)\.
