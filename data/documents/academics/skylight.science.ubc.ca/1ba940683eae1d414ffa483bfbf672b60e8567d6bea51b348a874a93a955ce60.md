We have worked with the UBC Science community and our campus partners since 2001, expanding the body of theoretical and practical knowledge about teaching science and math while helping tens of thousands of UBC undergraduates expand the depth and breadth of their learning and classroom experience\.

## 2025

-   Skylight surpasses $1M in funding distributed through [Skylight Development Grants](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/programs-grants/skylight)\.

## 2024

-   Skylight adds an SES position in the Department of Microbiology of Immunology\. SESs are now embedded in 8 out of 9 departments in the Faculty of Science\.
-   Launched the Skylight Indigenous Strategic Plan \(ISP\) Working Group\.

## 2023

-   Launched the [Skylight Teaching Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/skylight-teaching-series) \(in place of the Online Teaching Series\)

## 2022

-   Launched the [Skylight Speaker Series: Teaching and Learning in Science Through the Lens of Indigeneity, Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/speaker-series/teaching-learning-science-through-lens-indigeneity-equity-diversity-and-inclusion "Speaker Series: Teaching &amp; Learning in Science through the Lens of Indigeneity, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion")

## 2021

-   Launched the [STEM Education Research Exchange \(SERE\)](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/sere "STEM Education Research Exchange (SERE)")
-   Skylight hires a research analyst to advance work in [learning analytics](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives/learning-analytics-and-teaching-and-learning-data-access "Learning Analytics and Teaching and Learning Data Access")

## 2020

-   Launched the [Online Teaching Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/online-teaching-series "Online Teaching Series") \(in place of the long\-running Science Education Supper Series\)
-   Developed the [Science Online Teaching 2020](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/7JHBRR) resource as part of Skylight's support of the emergency transition to online teaching
-   Developed mid\-course survey templates, canvas templates, and other online teaching resources
-   Provided substantial support for the [Faculty of Science strategic planning process](https://plan.science.ubc.ca/about), including a survey of all faculty, staff, and students

## 2019

-   Published the [SEI Handbook](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/seihandbook/)

## 2018

-   [Student Diversity Initiative](https://equity.ubc.ca/about/strategic-planning/student-diversity-initiative/) launched
-   Data Science Curriculum project initiated with support from Skylight
-   [Teaching Start\-up Initiative](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/programs-grants/paired-teaching "Paired teaching") began
-   Skylight publishes a [prospectus](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/SkylightProspectus.pdf) and the first in its series of [annual reports](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/reports "Reports and Briefs")

## 2017

-   Skylight creates seven Science Education Specialist \(SES\) positions to support departmental strategic priorities
-   [Academic Equipment Fund](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/academic) is launched in the Faculty of Science and administered by Skylight

## 2016

-   Skylight [Faculty Advisory Council](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/advisory-council "Skylight Faculty Advisory Council") established

## 2015

-   Skylight joins NSF\-funded [TRESTLE project](https://trestlenetwork.ku.edu/)

## 2014

-   [Paired Teaching Initiative](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/programs-grants/paired-teaching "Paired teaching") in PHAS and EOAS launched
-   Skylight joins the [Bay View Alliance](https://bayviewalliance.org/)
-   CWSEI's activities merged with Skylight, end\-of\-year event transitions to the [Science Education Open House](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/open-house "Science Education Open House")

## 2013

-   Skylight formally supports CWSEI with the addition of a dedicated staff member
-   Learning technology ecosystem support model implemented in partnership with CTLT
-   [UBC Flexible Learning Initiative](https://flexible.learning.ubc.ca/) launched
-   BSc Degree Outcomes discussions begin

## 2011

-   Skylight and the Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology \(CTLT\) partner to hire a learning technology strategist for the Faculty of Science

## 2010

-   First permanent staff position created in Skylight

## 2009

-   CWSEI [end\-of\-year event](http://www.cwsei.ubc.ca/EOYevent.html) launched

## 2007

-   [Carl Wieman Science Education Initiative](https://cwsei.ubc.ca/) \(CWSEI\) established at UBC

## 2005

-   Embedded experts hired in BIOL, CHEM, and MATH

## 2004

-   First embedded expert hired in PHAS

## 2001

-   Skylight established
-   [Science Education Supper Series](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/events/supperseries "Skylight Supper Series") established
-   [Skylight Development Grants](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/programs-grants/skylight "Skylight Development Grants") established
