This guide provides an overview of the Canvas tools available to help you set up your course for online teaching\. The information below provides you with the core set\-up to get your course running in an online environment\.

### 1\. Canvas for Online Teaching

#### 1\.1 Providing course materials online

All courses at UBC can be delivered through [Canvas\.](https://faculty.canvas.ubc.ca/) If you have never logged in to your Canvas course site, go to [canvas\.ubc\.ca](https://canvas.ubc.ca/) and log in with your CWL\. There are lots of [online resources](https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-10460-canvas-instructor-guide-table-of-contents) and [video tutorials](https://community.canvaslms.com/community/answers/guides/video-guide#jive_content_id_Instructors) that are available to help you get started\. Canvas course templates are also available; contact [LT\.support@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:LT.support@science.ubc.ca) for information\.

-   Use [Modules](https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-13129-415241424), [Files](https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-12963-4152314203), and [Pages](https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-13018-4152724253) to organize your course materials\.
-   Use [Kaltura](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/kaltura/) or [Zoom](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/guides/zoom "Zoom") to post media files \(e\.g\. video lectures\)\.
-   Use the [Library Online Course Reserves \(LOCR\)](https://services.library.ubc.ca/borrowing-services/using-course-reserves/) to have all your readings in one place\.

#### 1\.2 Delivering online lectures/tutorials/office hours \(real\-time\)

-   Use [Zoom](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/guides/zoom "Zoom") to meet with your students remotely\. You can use the virtual whiteboard, create breakout rooms, conduct online polls, and share PowerPoint slides/files\.
-   To learn more about Zoom's features, refer to the [Zoom Features](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/guides/zoom/zoom-features "Zoom Features") page\.

#### 1\.3 Recording lectures

-   Use [Zoom](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/guides/zoom "Zoom") to record your live lectures and publish them on Canvas\.
-   Use [Kaltura](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/kaltura/) to record and/or upload pre\-recorded lectures to Canvas so students can watch them in their own time\.

#### 1\.4 Communicating with students

-   Use the [Canvas Inbox](https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-12721-4152719681) to contact your students\.
-   Use [Canvas Announcements](https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-10405-415250731) to keep students informed of course changes\.
-   Use the [Canvas Discussion Board](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Instructor-Guide/How-do-I-create-a-discussion-as-an-instructor/ta-p/1029) and [Piazza](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/guides/piazza "Piazza") for offline course discussions\.

#### 1\.5 Implementing online assessments

-   Use [Canvas Quizzes](https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-12943-4152724267) to deliver assessments, quizzes, and exams\.
-   Use [Canvas Assignments](https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-9873-415267003) to collect student submissions online\. 

#### 1\.6 Grading

-   Use the [Canvas Speedgrader](https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-12774-415255021) to distribute grades and feedback to students on their assignments\.

### 3\. Considerations for Large Enrolment Courses

If you are teaching large enrolment courses or if your Canvas course is merged with multiple sections, there are some limitations that you should be aware of:

-   **Zoom participant limit\.** There is a limit on the number of participants per session\. Zoom can host up to 300 participants in a regular session and 1,000 participants in webinar mode\. Meetings in webinar mode have reduced functionalities, which are outlined in the [Zoom Features](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/guides/zoom/zoom-features "Zoom Features") page\.
-   **Canvas Quiz load issues\.** Having all of your students start a quiz at the exact same time may result in students experiencing problems loading the quiz\. To avoid this issue, separate students into smaller groups \(e\.g\., by first/last name, course section, tutorial, or lab\) and stagger the start times by five minutes\. This will prevent load issues when starting and submitting the quiz\.
-   **Canvas Gradebook/Speedgrader load issues\.** Assignments with a large number of submissions can result in delayed loading times\. Filter the gradebook and view the students by section, or create multiple assignments assigned to each section instead of one assignment for all students\.
