A range of resources providing guidance on the use of GenAI\.

-   [AI, Assessment, and the Student and Educator Experience: Reflections from a Celebrate Learning Week Workshop](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/ai-guidance/generative-ai-workshop-reflections-resources "AI, Assessment, and the Student and Educator Experience: Reflections from a Celebrate Learning Week Workshop"): A reflective essay and resources from a 2024 Celebrate Learning Week workshop about the potential and limitations of using Generative AI in the context of higher education\. 
    
-   [UBC guidance on GenAI](https://genai.ubc.ca/guidance/): Guide on Generative AI, its purpose at UBC, and strategies for mitigating associated risks\. 
    

-   [Resources for faculty, students, staff and researchers](https://genai.ubc.ca/resources/#faculty): A comprehensive array of tools and methods for Generative AI use by faculty, students, staff, and researchers\. 
    
-   [Privacy and risk management](https://genai.ubc.ca/privacy-and-risk/): An overview of the Privacy Impact Assessment status for Generative AI use at UBC, including tools under evaluation and additional articles on risk management\.
