[Network graph illustrating the collaboration between authors from different departments in the Faculty of Science on scholarly publications issued between 2000 and 2023](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/inline-images/Flourish_Network_Map.jpg)

We have compiled a list of scholarly publications with a focus on teaching and learning in post\-secondary education published by UBC Science faculty and staff between 2000 and 2023\. You can browse the list of publications by clicking the link below and visiting our Zotero library:

-   [UBC Science Teaching and Learning Zotero Library](https://www.zotero.org/groups/5261824/science_tl_publications/library)

We have also created visualizations using Flourish and Tableau to illustrate the collaborations between authors from different departments in the Faculty of Science and allow you to explore publications data filtered by author, journal, tags, etc\. You can access these visualizations using the following links:

-   [Flourish Network Map](https://public.flourish.studio/visualisation/17146477/)
-   [Tableau Data Visualizations](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/science.ltrs/viz/UBCScienceTeachingLearningPublications/DeptPub)

Finally, we have created a Qualtrics form to allow you to submit publications to be added to the UBC Science Teaching and Learning Zotero library as well as provide us with feedback and suggestions on the Flourish network map and Tableau visualizations of the publications data\. You can access the form using the following link:

-   [UBC Science Teaching and Learning Publications Submission Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_dnx5D3pEgYOMh02)

If you are interested in learning more about this project, please check out [our poster](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2024-05/Skylight%20Open%20House%20-%20Science%20T%26L%20Publications%20Poster.pdf) from the 2024 Science Education Open House, or contact us at [LT\.support@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:LT.support@science.ubc.ca)\.

Thank you to our UBC Science Learning Technology Rovers for their efforts to create the library and visualizations\!
