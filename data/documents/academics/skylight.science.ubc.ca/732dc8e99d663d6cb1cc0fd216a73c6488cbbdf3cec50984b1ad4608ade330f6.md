A list of resources for online exams is included below\. The information in these resources offers recommendations and best practices for incorporating online alternatives that students can undertake remotely\.

### Alternate Exam Resources

This guide offers ideas and processes for how to approach online exams, discussing both Scheduled Online Exams and Take\-home exams\.

This guide also covers topics such as:

-   Academic Integrity
-   Question Design
-   Exam Invigilation
-   Considerations for Accessibility and Accommodation
-   How to Incorporate Hand\-Written Responses
-   Student In\-Exam Support

[Guide for Alternate Exam Resources](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/resources-and-best-practices/alternate-exam-resources "Alternate Exam Resources")

### Learning Technologies for Online Exams and Invigilation

This guide offers information concerning various learning technologies and their features and constraints, both from the perspective of an instructor and a student\.

The guide covers the following learning technologies:

-   Canvas
-   Gradescope

[Guide for Learning Technologies for Online Exams and Invigilation](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/online-exams "Learning Technologies for Online Exams and Invigilation")

### Using Zoom for Exam Invigilation

This guide offers information about using Zoom for exam invigilation\.

The guide covers:

-   Communication with Students
-   Recording Invigilation Sessions
-   Roles and Permissions
-   Setting up Zoom for Exam Invigilation

[Using Zoom for Exam Invigilation](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/guides/zoom/zoom-exam-invigilation "Use Zoom for Exam Invigilation")

### Promoting Academic Integrity

This handout is from the Skylight Online Workshop – Academic Integrity and Inclusive Practices in Online Courses

The handout covers:

-   Creating a culture of academic integrity
-   Engaging with design processes for online assessment
-   Tailoring quizzes and exams for the online environment

[Handout: What can Instructors do to Promote Academic Integrity?](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11/handout-instructors-academic-integrity.pdf)

### Best Practices for Online Exams

This guide offers recommendations for how to best follow\-through with online exams\.

It covers topics such as:

-   Exam set up
-   Student Recommendations
-   Managing large class sizes
-   Technical Difficulties
-   Accessibility and Accommodations

[Guide for Best Practices for Online Exams](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/best-practices-online-exams "Best Practices for Online Exams")
