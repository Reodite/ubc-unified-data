Below is a summary of UBC funding competitions that can be used to support teaching and learning projects\. If you want to talk about your ideas, learn more about the different funds, identify funding opportunities for your project ideas, and/or want feedback on your application, please contact us at [skylight@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:skylight@science.ubc.ca)\.

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Large Teaching &amp; Learning Enhancement Fund](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/programs-grants/tlef-grant-applications "TLEF Grant Applications")
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The Large TLEF is intended to advance UBC’s strategic goals related to transformative learning\. Large TLEF projects typically aspire to impact multiple courses or programs across UBC\-V\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    Principal Applicant: UBC\-V faculty and staff
    
    Co\-applicants: UBC\-V faculty, students, staff, and community members
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Up to $200,000 for 1–3 years
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    Letter of Intent: Mid\-July
    
    Full Proposal: Mid\-October

- **Row 3**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Small Teaching &amp; Learning Enhancement Fund](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/programs-grants/tlef-grant-applications "TLEF Grant Applications")
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The Small TLEF is intended for grassroots projects that support experimentation with pedagogical approaches with strong potential to enhance the learner experience\. Projects are typically smaller in scope and scale than Large TLEF projects\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    Principal Applicant: UBC\-V faculty, students and staff
    
    Co\-applicants: UBC\-V faculty, students, staff, and community members\.
    
    Note: if the Principal Applicant is a student, the application must be supported by a co\-applicant who is a faculty member\.
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Up to $50,000 for 1–3 years
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    Mid\-November

- **Row 4**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Strategic Equity &amp; Anti\-Racism \(StEAR\) Enhancement Fund](https://equity.ubc.ca/resources/funding-opportunities/strategic-equity-anti-racism-enhancement-fund/)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The StEAR Enhancement Fund is designed to support community\-led initiatives that advance broad campus equity and anti\-racism goals and priorities aligned with UBC’s StEAR Framework and Roadmap for Change\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    UBC\-V and UBC\-O faculty, staff, and student groups affiliated with the Alma Matter Society, the Graduate Student Society, Students' Union Okanagan of UBC, a Faculty or School, or another officially\-recognized UBC organization\.
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Applicants can apply for funding between $500 to $10,000 per project\.
    
    This year, a total of $200,000 is available\.
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    The application window for Call 1 is September 15 to October 15, 2025\. 
    
    The application window for Call 2 is January 12 to February 8, 2026\.

- **Row 5**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Indigenous Strategic Initiatives \(ISI\) Fund](https://isp.ubc.ca/isi-fund/)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The ISI Fund provides funding for transformative and innovative projects that support collective, collaborative, and sustained implementation of UBC’s Indigenous Strategic Plan \(ISP\) and UBCO’s Commitments to Truth and Reconciliation\. There are 3 funding streams under which project proposals may be submitted:
    
    Stream 1: Innovative Projects \(new, sustainable initiatives to advance strategy, goals\) led by faculty or staff\)    
      
    Stream 2: Transformative Projects \(supporting systemic or high\-level change led by faculty or staff\)    
      
    Stream 3: Student\-led Projects
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    UBC students, staff and faculty members from any campus, unit, or level, are welcome to apply\. 
    
    Application requirements vary by funding stream\.
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Stream 1: Up to $80,00 for up to 2 years
    
    Stream 2: Up to $100,00 for up to 2\.5 years
    
    Stream 3: Up to $25,000 for up to 18 months
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    Applications will be accepted between October 13, 2026 – January 14, 2027

- **Row 6**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Open Education Resources \(OER\) Affordability Grants](https://oerfund.open.ubc.ca/oer-affordability-grants/)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Funding and staff support to UBC\-V faculty who wish to incorporate open educational resources as required materials into their UBC\-V undergraduate credit courses\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    Principal Applicant: UBC\-V faculty
    
    Co\-applicants: UBC\-V faculty, students, staff, and community members
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Up to $25,000 for 2 years
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    Mid\-January

- **Row 7**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Open Education Resources \(OER\) Rapid Innovation Grant](https://oerfund.open.ubc.ca/oer-rapid-innovation-grants/)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Funding for a wide range of activities and events that seek to engage the UBC community in increasing awareness and capacity for supporting and sustaining the adoption, adaption, and creation of OER at UBC\-V\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    UBC\-V faculty, students and staff
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Up to $2,000 for 1 year
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    Rolling until funds depleted \($24,000 allocated each year\)

- **Row 8**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Scholarship of Teaching &amp; Learning \(SoTL\) Seed](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-seed-program/)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The SoTL Seed Program provides graduate research assistants, collegial collaborations, and complementary funding for projects, for faculty interested in exploring and understanding the impact of their pedagogical choices\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    All members of the UBC\-V and UBC\-O community\. Priority given to faculty who teach at the undergraduate level\.
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Up to 70 hours of GRA support plus some discretionary funds to cover research and dissemination costs
    
    Duration for 1–2 years
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    Twice a year: Late Spring and Late Fall

- **Row 9**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Scholarship of Teaching &amp; Learning \(SoTL\) Dissemination Fund](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-dissemination-fund/)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The fund supports disseminations and publications that describe research on teaching and learning in higher education with direct implications for UBC\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    Any full\-time faculty member at UBC\-V\. Funds can be received by individual faculty every 24 months\.
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Up to $1,000 for conference costs
    
    Up to $2,000 for journal publication costs
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    Rolling until funds depleted

- **Row 10**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\) Linkage Grants](https://isotl.ctlt.ubc.ca/services/sotl-linkage-grants/)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    This funding program supports the formation of interdisciplinary teams of SoTL researchers inquiring into fundamental questions in higher education that benefit from investigation in multiple pedagogical and curricular contexts\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    The lead applicant must be a UBC\-V tenure\-track faculty member \(research or educational leadership\), and at least two other tenure\-track faculty \(from UBC\-V, UBC\-O, or another institution\) from different disciplines and/or units must be included as co\-applicants on the proposal\.
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Up to $25,000 for up to 2 years
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    Late\-June

- **Row 11**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Sustainability Education Fellows Program](https://sustain.ubc.ca/programs/sustainability-education-fellows-program)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Sustainability Education Fellowships are awarded to UBC Vancouver faculty members who are leading the design of new sustainability courses and programs\. The associated funding looks to advance and diversify sustainability and wellbeing education opportunities for students across campus in all disciplines\. As part of the program, fellows meet monthly to share and explore teaching and learning practices and ideas, share their progress on their curriculum projects, and collaborate to advance sustainability education on campus\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    UBC Vancouver faculty members who are leading the design of new sustainability courses and programs\. Typically, teams are made up of two faculty members from different departments, faculties or disciplines, but more than two is also acceptable\.
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Up to $20,000 over 2 years to support curriculum development projects
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    Mid\-February

- **Row 12**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Climate and Wellbeing Education Grants](https://sustain.ubc.ca/teaching-applied-learning/climate-education-grants)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The Climate and Wellbeing Education Grant program aims to improve the depth and delivery of climate change content in existing courses, bolstering topics such as climate adaptation and resilience, climate justice, climate science, climate economics, climate law, planning and policy, complex systems thinking, and the nexus between climate and human health and wellbeing\. All areas of inquiry related to climate change and teaching are eligible\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    UBC faculty looking to update, enhance or revise an existing undergraduate or graduate course, with climate change and climate wellbeing content and pedagogy\.
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Up to $6,000 for 1 year
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    The next grant cycle will be during the 2026/2027 academic year\.

- **Row 13**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [Universal Design for Learning Fellows Program](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/programs-grants/universal-design-learning-udl-fellows-program "Universal Design for Learning (UDL) Fellows Program")
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The UDL Fellows Program will support faculty and staff to develop expertise in UDL through a cohort\-based professional development program\. It will be comprised of a series of facilitated workshops, online modules, and a project\-focused set of activities to help participants use UDL principles to redesign their courses to be more inclusive and accessible\.
  
  - **Cell 3:**
  
    Each Faculty will be asked to nominate a project team with a description of the course being proposed for a UDL redesign\. Nominations for the Program will be made by the Associate Deans Academic\.
    
    Each project team should include a UBC\-V instructor and a support staff member\.
  
  - **Cell 4:**
  
    Up to $10,000 in funding per department to support faculty who participate in the program
  
  - **Cell 5:**
  
    Interested teams from the Faculty of Science should submit a letter of interest on or before December 15th, 2025\. 
    
    More information about the Faculty of Science nomination process can be found [here](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/universal-design-learning-udl-fellows-program)\.
