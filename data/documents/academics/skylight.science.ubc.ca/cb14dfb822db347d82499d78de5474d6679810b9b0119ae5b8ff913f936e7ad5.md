Skylight directly funds small teaching and learning projects through annual competitions and offers consultation on UBC funding opportunities\.

## Programs

### [Paired Teaching: Teaching Start\-Up](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/programs-projects/paired-teaching)

Paired teaching is an efficient and effective model for introducing faculty to evidence\-based pedagogy through collaborative teaching\. Teaching Start\-Up is UBC Science's strategy for implementing the paired teaching model\. As part of the start\-up package, the Dean’s Office splits the cost of additional paired teaching credits with departments\.

[Learn more about paired teaching](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/programs-projects/paired-teaching)

## Skylight Grants

### [Skylight Development Grants](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/skylight)

Skylight Development Grants provide up to $5,000 in matching funds for projects that by their scale or focus are unlikely to receive TLEF funding, but would significantly benefit undergraduate or graduate teaching and learning\.

[Learn more](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/skylight)

### [Science Academic Equipment Fund](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/academic)

UBC Science provides funding of up to $100,000 a year for academic equipment that supports teaching and learning\. Funding is allocated through an annual competition announced in February\.

[Learn more](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/academic)

## UBC\-Wide Grants

### [Funding Opportunities Across UBC](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/funding-opportunities-across-ubc)

UBC offers many opportunities for funding that can be used to support teaching and learning projects\. Visit this page for a chart complete with a list of a curated selection of competitions\.

[Learn more](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/funding-opportunities-across-ubc)

### [Early TLEF Feedback](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/tlef)

Skylight offers early internal feedback on potential UBC Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund submissions within the Faculty\. We help applicants connect with similar projects, and provide pedagogical, technical, evaluation and project consultations\.

[Learn more](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/tlef)

## Other Grants

### [Universal Design for Learning \(UDL\) Fellows Program](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/universal-design-learning-udl-fellows-program)

Supporting faculty and staff to develop expertise in UDL through a cohort\-based professional development program to help participants use UDL principles to redesign their courses to be more inclusive and accessible\.

[Learn more](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/grants/universal-design-learning-udl-fellows-program)
