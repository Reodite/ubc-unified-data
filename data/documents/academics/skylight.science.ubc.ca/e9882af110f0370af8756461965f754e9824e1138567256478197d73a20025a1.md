Skylight identifies, plans, leads, and evaluates a wide range of science teaching and learning projects and initiatives with partners across and beyond UBC\. In partnership with CTLT and departments, Skylight also leads the strategic management and enhancement of the learning technology ecosystem in the Faculty of Science\.

### Who is Skylight?

Connect with Skylight's team of science education specialists, learning technology experts, strategic specialists, and educational leaders\. Skylight collaborates with leading institutions across North America and works with faculty advisors from diverse departments\.

-   [Contact our team](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/contact)
-   [Meet the Faculty Advisory Council](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/advisory-council)
-   [Discover our external partners](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/partners)

### Vision, Model, and Services

Skylight's distributed model empowers us to support teaching and learning activities across the Faculty of Science and beyond\. Skylight offers a wide range of services and expertise in educational research, pedagogy, curriculum development, and learning technologies\.

-   [Vision and purpose](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/vision)
-   [History of Skylight](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/history)
-   [Our distributed, embedded expert model](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/structure)
-   [Expertise and services](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/services)

### Publications and Reports

Each year, Skylight compiles a list of the publications, reports, and briefs produced by our team members\. These works showcase our contributions to science education research and innovation at UBC\.

-   [Our publications](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/publications)
-   [Reports and briefs](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/about/reports)
