Skylight is a research, community\-building, consultation, and support unit advancing the science behind education\. Skylight has facilitated institution\-wide improvements in teaching and learning and has helped position UBC as a leader among North American research universities in transforming science education\.

### [Projects and Initiatives](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives)

We offer support in science educational research, curriculum development, teaching and learning enhancement, and learning technologies\.

[Projects &amp; Initiatives](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/projects-initiatives)

### [Learning Technology](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt)

Learning technologies can help bring your classroom to life, enriching the teaching and learning experience at UBC\. We can help save you time and effort in navigating UBC Science's many learning technologies\.

[Learning Technology](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt)

### [Programs and Grants](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/programs-grants)

Skylight directly funds small teaching and learning projects through annual competitions and offers consultation on UBC funding opportunities\.

[Programs &amp; Grants](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/programs-grants)

Learn about our vision, history, team members, distributed model and faculty advisory council\.
