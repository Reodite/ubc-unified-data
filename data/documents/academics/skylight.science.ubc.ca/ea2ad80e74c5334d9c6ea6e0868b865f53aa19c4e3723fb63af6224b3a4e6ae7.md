Instructors may want to record and/or stream their in\-person lectures to accommodate students who are unable to attend classes in person\. This guide summarizes the recording options that are available to instructors\.

### Recording and streaming options

UBC IT Audio Visual \(AV\) Services provides three options for recording and streaming lectures\. These options are summarized below, and more information, including quick start guides and available support, can be found on the [Learning Spaces website\.](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/recording-and-streaming-classrooms)

#### In\-Room Recording Systems

General Teaching Spaces \(GTS\) have been upgraded to include in\-room recording systems for lecture capture and live streaming\. The upgrades include an auto\-tracking camera, microphone, and recording software\.

Find out if your classroom is equipped with recording/streaming capability by referring to the [Recording and Streaming in Classrooms](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/recording-and-streaming-classrooms) page\. **You must** [**fill out this recording request form**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_d0CchruSKrBVigS) **to have your recordings preset and pre\-scheduled by UBC IT AV Services ahead of time**\. Requests have to be submitted at least 48 hours before the initial recording and we highly recommend that you submit your request early\. Your recordings will then automatically start and stop based on your class time\. If live streaming is required, please make sure to indicate it in the request form\.

Refer to the [Using the In\-Room Recording Systems video tutorial](https://ubc.ca.panopto.com/Panopto/Pages/Viewer.aspx?id=a293713f-204c-4c9a-8af9-ad79017fe9b9) for an overview of the set up\.

Refer to the [Built\-in Classroom Recording and Streaming Quick Start Guide](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/sites/learningspaces.ubc.ca/files/Built-in%20Classroom%20Recording.pdf) or the [Temporary Add\-on Classroom Recording and Streaming Quick Start Guide](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/sites/learningspaces.ubc.ca/files/Add-on%20Classroom%20Recording.pdf), depending on which set\-up your classroom is equipped with\.

Refer to the [GTS Recording Feature Matrix](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/sites/learningspaces.ubc.ca/files/GTS-Recording-Feature-Matrix.pdf) or use the [Find a Space](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/find-space) search to see the recording capabilities of each classroom\.

#### Drop\-in Recording Kits

If you are teaching in a classroom that is not equipped with recording/streaming capacity, drop\-in recording kits are available for instructors to use\. **You must** [**fill out this recording request form**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/preview/SV_d0CchruSKrBVigS?Q_CHL=preview&amp;Q_SurveyVersionID=current) **to have your recordings preset and pre\-scheduled by UBC IT AV Services ahead of time**\. Requests have to be submitted at least 48 hours before the initial recording and we highly recommend that you submit your request early\. Your recordings will then automatically start and stop based on your class time\. If live streaming is required, please make sure to indicate it in the request form\.

Refer to the [Using the Drop\-in Recorder Kit video tutorial](https://ubc.ca.panopto.com/Panopto/Pages/Viewer.aspx?id=bfeee354-6476-4768-98e4-ad7a01597d84) for an overview of the set up\.

#### Mobile Zoom Carts

If you require unique features offered by Zoom when teaching in the classroom, you can request a mobile Zoom cart equipped with Zoom for recording\. You will be able to connect to your Zoom meeting by entering your meeting ID and passcode\. If recording is a priority, it is recommended that you use the In\-Classroom Recording Systems and/or the Portable Recording Kits option\.

Refer to the [Using the Mobile Zoom Cart video tutorial](https://ubc.ca.panopto.com/Panopto/Pages/Viewer.aspx?id=7f0cce49-4049-42de-ab09-ad7a017e5a72) and the [Mobile Zoom Cart Quick Start Guide](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/sites/learningspaces.ubc.ca/files/Mobile%20Cart.pdf) for an overview of the set up\.

To request a Mobile Zoom Cart, please contact [av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:av.helpdesk@ubc.ca)\. UBC IT AV Services will assess whether this is the best option for you\.

If you have any questions about the three recording options listed above or if you would like to schedule a time with a technician to go over the option you choose, please contact UBC IT AV Services at [av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:av.helpdesk@ubc.ca)\.

For a list of frequently asked questions, please refer to the [Recording and Streaming FAQ](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/recording-and-streaming-faq)\.

### DIY Recording using Zoom

If the above three options are unavailable to you, you can use Zoom to record your in\-person lecture using your own webcam, audio, and slides\. If you need to borrow professional recording equipment, such as a webcam or microphone, DIY Recording Loaner Kits are available by request\. Please submit a request to [av\.helpdesk@ubc\.ca](mailto:av.helpdesk@ubc.ca) to reserve a kit\.

Refer to the [Record Your In\-person Lecture with Zoom](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/zoom-instructor-guide/#record-an-in-person-lecture-wi-4) guide for an overview of the set up\.

### Editing class recordings

After your recordings are processed, you may wish to edit them\. If you need to cut out certain segments of the video or separate the video into smaller segments, you will need to first download the recorded file\.

If you are uploading your videos to Kaltura and you wish to make some basic edits, you can use the Kaltura built\-in video editor\. Refer to the [Editing videos in Kaltura](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/guide/kaltura#edit-a-video-using-the-video-editor) instructions in the [Kaltura tool guide](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/lt/guides/kaltura "Kaltura")\.

For videos not uploaded to Kaltura or require more advanced editing options, refer to the [Editing Videos in Camtasia](https://skylight.science.ubc.ca/sites/skylight.science.ubc.ca/files/Editing%20Videos%20in%20Camtasia.pdf) guide\.
