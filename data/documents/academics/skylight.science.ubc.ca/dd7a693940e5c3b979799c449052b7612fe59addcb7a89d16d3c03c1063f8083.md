iPeer is a peer review application that enables students to evaluate and provide anonymous feedback on their group member’s work and contributions using set criteria\. It is commonly used to facilitate the assessment of students’ involvement and/or participation in group activities or projects\. 

### Key Features

-   **Create specific criteria:** create criteria to guide students’ evaluation of one another 
-   **Feedback options:** share ratings and comments either with the evaluated students or keep them private between the instructor and evaluator 
-   **Various evaluation formats:** three types of formats available: simple, mixed, and rubrics\- 
-   **Download and review evaluation results:** download evaluation results for review, analysis, or grading 
-   **Canvas integration:** import Canvas groups to iPeer and sync iPeer grades to Canvas 

### Getting Started

You will need to have an iPeer account before you can set up an iPeer course\. Please email Science LT at [LT\.support@science\.ubc\.ca](mailto:LT.support@science.ubc.ca) to request an account\.  

Once you have an iPeer account, you can proceed with [setting up your iPeer course](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/ipeer-instructor-guide/#set-up-course)\. 

### Instructions

-   [Create student groups by importing groups from Canvas or by manually creating them in iPeer](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/ipeer-instructor-guide/#create-student-groups) 
    -   **Note**: To ensure students are added to the proper group in iPeer, we recommend creating groups in Canvas and then importing them into iPeer prior to creating iPeer evaluations\. 
-   [Make your own criteria for iPeer evaluations](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/ipeer-instructor-guide/#make-evaluation-criteria) 
-   [Create a iPeer Evaluation Event](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/ipeer-instructor-guide/#create-evaluation) 
-   [Release iPeer evaluation outcomes \(e\.g\. comments, grades\)](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/ipeer-instructor-guide/#review-and-release-outcomes) 
-   [Sync iPeer grades to Canvas](https://lthub.ubc.ca/guides/ipeer-instructor-guide/#send-grades-to-canvas)
