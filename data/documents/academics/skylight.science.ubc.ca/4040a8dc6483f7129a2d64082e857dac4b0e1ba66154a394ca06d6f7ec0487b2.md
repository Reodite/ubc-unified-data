In 2014, the Earth, Ocean and Atmospheric Sciences \(EOAS\) and Physics and Astronomy \(PHAS\) departments received a generous donation from the Harris family to pilot paired teaching as part of the Carl Wieman Science Education Initiative \(CWSEI\)\. Six Science Teaching and Learning Fellows \(STLFs\) were hired to support faculty pairs\.

Between 2014 and 2018, 27 instructors taught 18 courses to over 2,500 students in the pilot program, and research was disseminated through scholarly publications \([Stang et al\., 2017](https://open.library.ubc.ca/cIRcle/collections/facultyresearchandpublications/52383/items/1.0363340) and [Holland et al\., 2018](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/87567555.2018.1463505)\)\. The success of the program led to its full implementation in UBC Science, supported by the Dean and department heads\. In 2018, paired teaching became part of the package offered to new faculty in all UBC Science departments\.
