*Feb\. 20, 2025, 1 to 5 pm*

*Room: PCN 1009*

*Introduction and facilitation of discussion:* *Dr\. Steven Taubeneck and Dr\. Barbara Weber*

*Organized by the Interdisciplinary Studies Graduate Program*–

*A poet, a professor and the enigmatic guide “Stalker,” enter the* post\-apocalyptic “*Zone”, a forbidden area rumored to contain a room that grants a person’s deepest wishes\.* As they journey through the surreal, desolate landscape, the film explores existential themes of faith, knowledge, and the human soul, challenging the characters’ motivations and beliefs\. With its slow, meditative pacing, dreamlike cinematography, and deep allegorical layers, *Stalker* offers a time and space to contemplate the nature of imagination and power of dreams
