### Continuous Registration

**

You must maintain active registration in your program \(regardless of course work\) by registering in Thesis section \(Master’s: INDS 549; PhD: INDS 649\)\. The Thesis section is only a “placeholder”, rather than an actual course\. Please make a habit of doing this twice a year \(Fall/Winter: September; Summer: May\)\.  
For correct section numbers, please go to: [https://isgp\.ubc\.ca/current\-students/courses\-offered/](https://isgp.ubc.ca/current-students/courses-offered/)  

**

### ISGP Email List Subscription

**

You must subscribe to the ISGP email list, as we communicate important announcements and deadlines to you\. You may subscribe or manage your subscription [here](https://lists.ubc.ca/scripts/wa.exe?SUBED1=ISGP-STUDENTS&amp;A=1)\.  
Please also ensure that your supervisors are subscribed to the ISGP Supervisors email list by managing their subscriptions [here](https://lists.ubc.ca/scripts/wa.exe?SUBED1=ISGP-SUPERVISORS&amp;A=1)\.

**

### Non\-UBC Supervisors/Committee Members

**

At UBC, you may have up to 50% of your supervisors/committee members outside of UBC, upon approval\.  
Please submit recommendation package to the ISGP for approval: [https://www\.grad\.ubc\.ca/forms/recommendation\-non\-gps\-member\-join\-supervisory\-committee](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/forms/recommendation-non-gps-member-join-supervisory-committee)  
Detailed descriptions of supervisors and committee members roles and responsibilities can be found on the Grad Studies web site: [https://www\.grad\.ubc\.ca/current\-students/supervision\-advising](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/supervision-advising)

**

### Leave of Absence

**

If you feel that your academic progress is being hindered by extenuating circumstances, please contact the ISGP office immediately to discuss the possibility of a [leave of absence](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/forms/request-leave-absence)\. Simply send us a brief email letting us know that you are experiencing difficulties\.  An email of this nature can later be used to support your request for a leave of absence\. 

**Please note that G\+PS** ***discourages retroactive requests of any nature**\.* The sooner you contact the office, the easier it is for us to help you find a solution\.

**

### Directed Studies Courses

**

You may take up to 6 credits of Directed Studies at the ISGP\. Please submit a course proposal to the ISGP office for approval: [https://isgp\.ubc\.ca/current\-students/courses/](https://isgp.ubc.ca/current-students/courses/)\.  
Once approved, you will be registered into a course section\. At the end of the course, please ask your instructor to email us a % grade to be entered\.

**

### Records \(SSC\)

**

You can manage all your records at UBC through the Student Service Centre \(SSC\): [https://ssc\.adm\.ubc\.ca/sscportal/](https://ssc.adm.ubc.ca/sscportal/)  
You can find out the break\-down of your fees, register for courses, change your contact information, accept awards, order UBC transcripts, download Proof of Enrollment letter, and more\!

**

### Appointments with ISGP Program Chair

**

Please email the ISGP Program Chair directly for appointments\.

**

### Signature Protocol

**

Almost all UBC forms require the signature of one of your UBC co\-supervisors and then the ISGP Program Chair’s for approval\. Please submit all forms to the ISGP office for processing\.
