## ISGP Funding Support \(for Current Students\)

The ISGP can provide modest financial support to students in the program\.

**Master’s**: A one\-time funding support \(per degree\) of up to $2,000 is available to students in their 3rd year \(or higher\)\.  
**Doctoral**: A one\-time funding support \(per degree\) of up to $2,500 is available to students in their 5th year \(or higher\)\.

Final funding amount is subject to review and approval\. To submit a funding request, please send an email to [isgp\.office@ubc\.ca](mailto:isgp.office@ubc.ca) to outline your academic progress, and reasons for the funding support\.  
Deadline: Submit your funding request before the end of semesters \(e\.g\. December, April, August\)

## **UBC Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies \(G\+PS\): Graduate Student Travel Fund**

Graduate students may also apply to the Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies’ Graduate Student Travel Fund\.

Payment and application procedure information for this grant is available on [G\+PS Awards and Financial Aid](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/graduate-student-travel-fund) web page\.
