### Student Seminars

**

The 2nd **Graduate Student Seminar** took place online on 28 November 2023\.

Leena Alkhammash, one of the recent PhD candidates from ISGP, presented on "Comparing the student journey in physical and virtual anatomy labs" and Collincia Rouse, an ISGP PhD student, gave a talk on "Value: a scene study of the show Atlanta exploring the Black female body as an intra\-racial medium\." Prof\. Weber was the Seminar Chair\.

We will soon announce our 3rd Student Seminar\!
