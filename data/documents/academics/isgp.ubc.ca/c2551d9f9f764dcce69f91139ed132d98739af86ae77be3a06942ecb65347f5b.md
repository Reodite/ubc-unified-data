#### Location

The conference will take place in **Thea’s Lounge**, GSS Building, UBC \(6371 Crescent Rd\., Vancouver, BC\)\.

#### Registration

You can register to attend Nexus 2020 [here](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2bjavguEigCxYW1)\.

#### Conference Schedule

This year Nexus is double the fun as it has been expanded to a two\-day event\!

##### March 12th

Today is a series of workshops that teach essential skills for public scholarship\.  These workshops are delivered by graduate students for graduate students\.

[Image](https://isgp2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/03/March-12thFINAL.png)

##### March 13th

Today is our attempt to transform the classic conference into something that better celebrates Scholarship \[In\] Action\. It will be a day of creative presentations and collaborative activities to build capacity and networks to support public scholarship in graduate research\.

[Image](https://isgp2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/03/March-13-Schedule-768x1024.jpg)

We look forward to celebrating interdisciplinary scholars from across campus together\. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the conference co\-chairs \(Maya Lefkowich &amp; Jennica Nichols\) at nexus\.isgp@gmail\.com\.
