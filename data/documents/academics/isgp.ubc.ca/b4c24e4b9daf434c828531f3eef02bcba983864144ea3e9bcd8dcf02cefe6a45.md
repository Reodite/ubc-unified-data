Connect with the ISGP community by creating your own UBC Grad Student Profile\! UBC Grad Profiles provide and excellent opportunity for you to build your network and to introduce yourself to the rest of the program while also highlighting:

-   -   Your current research and its community and social impacts
    -   Your motivations for pursuing your current degree
    -   What you like about your current program\.

**All we need from you is to fill out the form [here](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2h1BD5sIUlYDoCF)\.** Once your profile gets published onto the UBC Grad [page](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/meet-our-students), we’ll link to it from our Current Student Research [page](https://isgp.ubc.ca/current-students/current-research/)\.
