Kev Kokoska’s clinical, creative, and academic work focuses on finding health in hard places\. He works with incarcerated men through his private practice, Otherside Health, creates mixed media mental health content, and connects these processes through his interdisciplinary PhD studies at UBC\. His last name means “broody hen”\.

Mirrored missing pieces in men’s mental health: repurposing population\-specific interventions to serve as preventative public care\.
