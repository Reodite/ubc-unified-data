The Interdisciplinary Studies Graduate Program \(ISGP\) is supported by its own Faculty Advisory Committee \(FAC\), with representatives from different disciplines across UBC\. The main function of the FAC is to facilitate in the governance of the program by evaluating program policies and procedures, in addition to evaluating applications to program admission and awards, and to ensure the continued high quality and integrity of the graduate programs in the ISGP\. The FAC advises the ISGP on setting priorities, procedures, and direction for interdisciplinary education\.
