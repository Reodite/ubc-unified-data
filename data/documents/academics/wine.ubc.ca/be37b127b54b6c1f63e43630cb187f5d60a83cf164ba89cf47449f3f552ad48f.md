Grapes to Glass is a four\-session Wine Education and Tasting Series\. You will use your senses as you learn about the important elements of wine production, from grape\-growing to enjoying the finished product, with a special focus on B\.C\. wines\. Over the course of these sessions, you will taste over a dozen unique wines and tour the UBC Wine Library – home to more than 4,000 unique bottles of wine\.

Check the Grapes to Glass Seminar Series webpage for updates and upcoming offerings\!
