[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/11/Black_A-Picture-256x300.jpg)Anna started life in Germany, with stops in Seattle and Houston on her way to Vancouver\. She completed her undergraduate degree and medical school at UBC\. Despite being introduced to Urology early on, she discovered her passion for the specialty during her third\-year longitudinal community clerkship, inspired by the Chilliwack Urology team\. She was impressed by the tight\-knit, talented team of residents during her UBC electives, and is thrilled to have the opportunity to train in Vancouver\. Outside of medicine, she enjoys spending time on, in, and under the water as well as hiking, climbing, and ski touring, and looks forward to further adventures in British Columbia’s outdoors\.
