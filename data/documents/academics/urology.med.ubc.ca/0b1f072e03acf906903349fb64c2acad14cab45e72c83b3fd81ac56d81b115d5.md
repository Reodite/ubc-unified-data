## AI\-driven disease detection levels\-up prostate cancer surgery

### A new technology brings real\-time pathology to the operating room

[Dr\. Miles Mannas](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/10/dr_miles_2023.jpg-224x300.webp)

Dr\. Miles Mannas

A groundbreaking study led by Dr\. Miles Mannas is exploring how stimulated Raman histology \(SRH\), an AI\-powered rapid tissue assessment tool, can transform prostate cancer surgery\. The technology allows surgeons to analyze tissue biopsies in real time during robotic radical prostatectomy, reducing surgical times and enhancing the precision of cancer removal\.

Unlike traditional pathology methods that can take over an hour, SRH delivers accurate margin assessments within minutes, offering surgeons faster and more informed decision\-making during operations\. The approach could significantly reduce recurrence risk and improve patient recovery times, marking a major step forward in AI\-assisted surgery\.
