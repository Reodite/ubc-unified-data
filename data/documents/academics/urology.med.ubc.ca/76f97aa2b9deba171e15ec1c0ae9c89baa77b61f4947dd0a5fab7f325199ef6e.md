Dr\. Morgan Roberts works to understand how bladder cancer and the immune system interact, paving the way for improved immunotherapy approaches\.

We’re pleased to share a recently published Q&amp;A with **[Dr\. Morgan Roberts](https://www.prostatecentre.com/node/560)** from the Department of Microbiology and Immunology\.

Dr\. Roberts earned her PhD in the Department of Microbiology and Immunology at UBC and is now an Assistant Professor in the Department of Urologic Sciences and a Senior Research Scientist at the Vancouver Prostate Centre\.

In this interview, she reflects on her experience in the Department of Microbiology and Immunology, her research on bladder cancer immunology, and her career path from graduate training to her current role at UBC\.
