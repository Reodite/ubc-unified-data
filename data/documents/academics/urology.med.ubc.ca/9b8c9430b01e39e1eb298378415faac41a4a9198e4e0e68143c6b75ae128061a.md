## **Dr\. Alexander Wyatt appointed UBC President’s Excellence Chair in Precision Oncology**

[Dr\. Alexander Wyatt, UBC President’s Excellence Chair in Precision Oncology](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/10/Alexander-Wyatt-photo-1920x1080-1-360x203.png)

*Dr\. Alexander Wyatt, UBC President’s Excellence Chair in Precision Oncology*

The Department of Urologic Sciences congratulates Dr\. Alexander Wyatt on his appointment as the UBC President’s Excellence Chair in Precision Oncology\!

An associate professor in the Department and a senior research scientist at the Vancouver Prostate Center and BC Cancer, Dr\. Wyatt is internationally recognized for his pioneering work in cancer genomics\. His research on circulating tumour DNA \(“liquid biopsies”\) to better understand and support the treatment of aggressive prostate and bladder cancers has led to genomic biomarker tests now used in clinical practice to improve cancer care\.
