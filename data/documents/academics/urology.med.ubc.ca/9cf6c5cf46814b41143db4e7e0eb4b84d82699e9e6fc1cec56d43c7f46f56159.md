## Celebrating the accomplishments of Anna Black, Ryan Yan, and Ghizlane Moussaoui

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/11/website-r5-360x203.png)

The Department of Urologic Sciences extends its warmest congratulations to our R5 residents on successfully passing their Royal College examinations\. 🎉

We are pleased to recognize Anna Black, Ryan Yan, and Ghizlane Moussaoui for achieving this significant milestone in their medical training\. Their dedication, perseverance, and commitment to advancing excellence in patient care and urologic practice have been evident throughout their residency\.

This accomplishment reflects not only their hard work but also their professionalism, integrity, and the strong clinical and academic foundation they have built over the past five years\. We are confident they will continue to make meaningful contributions as they move forward in their careers\.

Please join us in congratulating them on this important milestone and wishing them continued success in the years ahead\. 💙✨
