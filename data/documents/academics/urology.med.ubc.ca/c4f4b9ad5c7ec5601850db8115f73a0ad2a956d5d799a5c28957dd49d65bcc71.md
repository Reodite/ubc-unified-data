Congratulations to Dr\. Amina Zoubeidi on receiving a Faculty of Medicine Award for Excellence in Mentoring Early Career Faculty\.

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/09/zoubeidi-263x300.jpg)

The award recognizes faculty members who have formally been identified as mentors and who exemplify a deep commitment to fostering the professional and personal development of faculty members in the early stages of their academic careers\.
