Congratulations to **Reid Vassallo**, recipient of this year’s **Professor Madanlal T\. Wasan Inspiration Award**\.

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/Reid_W1A2684-1-360x240.jpg)  
Reid Vassallo, recipient of the Professor Madanlal T\. Wasan Inspiration Award\.

This award recognizes the Department’s top PhD graduate from the past year\. Reid completed the PhD component of his MD/PhD training in biomedical engineering, where his work focused on developing tools to improve the accuracy and accessibility of prostate cancer diagnosis using ultrasound\.

His research included an industrial collaboration with Exact Imaging to implement new capabilities using high\-resolution clinical ultrasound, as well as a collaboration with Caltech to develop a novel PSMA\-targeted ultrasound contrast agent\.

Congratulations, Reid, on this well\-deserved recognition\!
