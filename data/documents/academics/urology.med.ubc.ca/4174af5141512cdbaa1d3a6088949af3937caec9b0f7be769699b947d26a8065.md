As our graduating residents begin their next chapters, the Department of Urologic Sciences would like to recognize and congratulate our 2026 graduating chief residents: [Dr\. Anna Black](https://urology.med.ubc.ca/anna-black/), [Dr\. Ryan Yan](https://urology.med.ubc.ca/ryan-yan/), and [Dr\. Ghizlane Moussaoui](https://urology.med.ubc.ca/ghizlane-moussaoui/)\.

[2026 graduating chief residents \(Dr\. Ryan Yan, Dr\. Ghizlane Moussaoui, Dr\. Anna Black\) with Dr\. De Los Reyes and Dr\. Peter Black\.](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/W1A2384-940x627.jpg)

2026 graduating chief residents with Dr\. De Los Reyes, Residency Program Director and Dr\. Peter Black, Department Head\. \(From left: Dr\. Thomas De Los Reyes, Dr\. Ryan Yan, Dr\. Ghizlane Moussaoui, Dr\. Anna Black, and Dr\. Peter Black\.\)

Thank you for your hard work, dedication, and professionalism throughout your residency training\. You have grown into skilled surgeons and clinicians, and we are proud to celebrate this important milestone with you\.

We wish Dr\. Yan all the best as he transitions to practice in Prince George, and Dr\. Black and Dr\. Moussaoui all the best as they begin their urologic oncology fellowship at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Centre in New York\. We look forward to welcoming you back to the BC urologic community in two years\.

Congratulations to our graduating chief residents\!
