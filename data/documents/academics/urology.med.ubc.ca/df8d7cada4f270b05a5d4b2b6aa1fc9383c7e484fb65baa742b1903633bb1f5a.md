> We’re pleased to announce that Julie Wong \(PGY\-5\) was awarded the CUA Resident Award of Excellence in Education for 2025\. This national award is given to one Canadian urology resident annually who has significantly contributed to and demonstrated excellence in the field of education, both in their own training program and beyond\. Dr Wong’s contributions throughout her 5 years of residency include founding the UBC Urology Residents for Gender Equity \(URGE\) in 2021\. She has also participated in resident advocacy at the provincial and national level as an executive member of the Board of Directors of Resident Doctors of BC, and as Chair of the CUA Resident and Fellows Committee\. Congratulations Dr Wong\!
