We are delighted to invite you to the *19th Annual Lorne D\. Sullivan Lectureship and Research Day*, hosted by the [UBC Department of Urologic Sciences](https://urology.med.ubc.ca/) and the [Vancouver Prostate Centre](https://www.prostatecentre.com/)\.

The day will feature:

-   Keynote lecture by [Dr\. Ganesh S\. Palapattu](https://medschool.umich.edu/profile/1669/ganesh-palapattu)
-   Research presentations from students, residents, fellows and faculty
-   Presentations from research scientists at the Vancouver Prostate Centre
-   A multidisciplinary lineup of topics spanning urology, oncology, pathology, radiology and more\.

**Event Details:**

*Date*: Tuesday, June 10, 2025

*Time*: 8am – 3pm \(PT\)

*Location*: Paetzold Health Education Centre, Jim Pattison Pavilion : 899 W 12th Ave, Vancouver

*Keynote Speaker*: Dr\. Ganesh S\. Palapattu, Chief of Urology, Michigan Medicine, George F\. and Sandy G\. Valassis

*Agenda*: **[2025 Research Day Program with Abstracts\_20250606](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/05/2025-Research-Day-Program-with-Abstracts_20250606-1.pdf)**
