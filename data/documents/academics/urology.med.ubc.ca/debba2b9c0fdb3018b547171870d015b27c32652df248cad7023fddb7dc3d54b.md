#### Congratulations to Dr\. Connor Forbes on receiving the 2025 Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute Investigator Award\.

The Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute \(VCHRI\) Investigator Awards provide salary support to clinician\-scientists, enabling them to reduce clinical duties and focus on advancing health research, supporting innovative research aimed at improving patient care\.
