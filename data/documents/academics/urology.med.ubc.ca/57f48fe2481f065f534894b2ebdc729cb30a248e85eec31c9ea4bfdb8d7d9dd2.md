[UBC Department of Urologic Sciences](https://urology.med.ubc.ca/) and the [Vancouver Prostate Centre](https://www.prostatecentre.com/) are delighted to be hosting the 20th Annual Lorne D\. Sullivan Lectureship and Research Day\. This milestone event will be a day of collaboration, discovery, and celebration of academic excellence\.

Highlight of the Day

-   -   A keynote lecture by [Professor Caroline Moore](https://www.uclh.nhs.uk/our-services/find-consultant/professor-caroline-moore)

-   -   Research presentations from students, residents, fellows, and faculty

-   -   Presentations by research scientists at the Vancouver Prostate Centre

-   -   A multidisciplinary program covering a broad range of topics of great interest to Urologists as well as colleagues in Medical Oncology, Radiation Oncology, Engineering, Cancer Research, Radiology, and Pathology
