The Department of Urologic Sciences is pleased to share a new publication in *Cancer Discovery* titled,

“Germline CDK12 variants in aggressive prostate cancer\.”

[Dr\. Sofie Tolmeijer and Dr\. Alexander Wyatt](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/Dr.-Wyatt-Dr.-Tolmeijer--360x270.jpg)

Dr\. Sofie Tolmeijer and Dr\. Alexander Wyatt

The study identifies rare inherited CDK12 variants in patients with aggressive metastatic prostate cancer\. These findings challenge the previous assumption that CDK12 alterations in prostate cancer are exclusively somatic, meaning acquired in the tumour rather than inherited\.

The research, with Dr\. Alexander Wyatt as senior author and Dr\. Sofie Tolmeijer as lead author, suggests that inherited CDK12 mutations may represent a rare but important new form of hereditary prostate cancer\. The findings have potential implications for hereditary cancer testing, family risk assessment, and earlier screening strategies for individuals at risk\.

This work reflects an international collaboration involving researchers from the University of British Columbia, including the Department of Urologic Sciences, Vancouver Prostate Centre, and M\.H\. Mohseni Institute of Urologic Sciences \(MIUS\), as well as BC Cancer, Vancouver Coastal Health Research Institute, the University of Washington, and partner institutions in Australia, the Netherlands, Spain, and Belgium\.

Congratulations to Dr\. Wyatt, Dr\. Tolmeijer, and all researchers and collaborators involved in this important publication\!
