Khatereh Aminoltejari was born in Iran and moved to Vancouver when she was 12\. She completed a Bachelors of Integrated Sciences Honours, Masters in Neuroscience and Medical school at UBC\. She became interested in urology thanks to her mentor in the Island Medical Program, Dr\. Suzanne Montemuro\. She explored the country’s urology programs through many electives and UBC became her chosen training program as soon as she saw and experienced the excellent work by the urologists and the trainees\. Her interests outside of work include running, travelling and reading \(non\-urology\) books\.
