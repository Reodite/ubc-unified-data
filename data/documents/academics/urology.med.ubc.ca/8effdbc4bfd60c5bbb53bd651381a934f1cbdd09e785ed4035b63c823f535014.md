Colin was born and raised in Victoria, BC\. He went on to complete his undergraduate degree in Mathematics at Purdue University, Fort Wayne followed by medical school at UBC in the Island Medical Program\. He has been married for 11 years to Tara\-Lynn and they have a four year old daughter, Elsie Joy\. Outside the hospital Colin enjoys spending time with his family and cycling\.
