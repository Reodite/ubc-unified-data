[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/WONG-Alex-300x300.jpg)Alex Wong was born and raised in Hong Kong\. After completing medical school at the University of Hong Kong, he did several years of residency in urology in Hong Kong\. He loves everything urology has to offer – a perfect blend of medicine and surgery, a wide spectrum of operative techniques, excellent patient outcomes, friendly colleagues, and never\-ending innovations\. Since realizing that Canada is the right place for him and his family to thrive, he has always dreamt of becoming a resident at UBC Urology\. With leading experts in every field, an enormous caseload, and a great program infrastructure, he is thrilled to start a new page in his career to serve the population of British Columbia\.

Work aside, Alex has recently become a father and he cherishes every moment with his family, including changing diapers\. He also enjoys playing volleyball \(was once the captain of his high school volleyball team\), cooking \(has two cookbooks as thick as Campbell’s\) and coffee roasting\.
