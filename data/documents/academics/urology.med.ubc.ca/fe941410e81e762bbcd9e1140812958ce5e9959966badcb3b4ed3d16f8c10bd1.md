**Congratulations to Dr\. Kishor M\. Wasan, who has been appointed Chair of the Scientific Board of the Prostate Cancer Foundation Canada, starting on November 1, 2025\!**

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/11/Dr-Marwan-announcement-360x270.jpg)

Dr\. Wasan is Co\-Founder &amp; Co\-Director of the Neglected Global Diseases Initiative \(NGDI\) at the University of British Columbia and a Distinguished University Scholar &amp; Adjunct Professor in the Department of Urologic Sciences\. He is also a Distinguished Alumnus of the UT MD Anderson Cancer Center and has made significant contributions to pharmaceutical sciences and global health research throughout his career\.

Learn more: [Prostate Cancer Foundation Canada – Scientific Board](https://prostatecanada.ca/about-us/our-team#dr-kishor-m-wasan)
