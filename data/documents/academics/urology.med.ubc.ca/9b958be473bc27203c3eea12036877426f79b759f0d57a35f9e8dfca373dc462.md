The Department of Urologic Sciences was pleased to host the 20th Annual Lorne D\. Sullivan Lectureship &amp; Research Day on June 12, 2026\.

This year’s event brought together trainees, faculty, staff, and invited guests to celebrate research excellence and academic achievement across the department\. The program featured 24 podium presentations, 49 poster presentations, and two State of the Art Lectures by [Dr\. Connor Forbes](https://urology.med.ubc.ca/connor-forbes/) and [Dr\. Faraz Hach](https://www.prostatecentre.com/about-us/people/dr-faraz-hach)\.

## 2026 Sullivan Research Lecturer

We were also honoured to welcome [Professor Caroline Moore](https://www.uclh.nhs.uk/our-services/find-consultant/professor-caroline-moore) as part of this year’s program\.

## Research Day presentations and prizes

Thank you to all presenters, judges, organizers, and attendees for making this year’s event a success\! To learn more about the day’s agenda, presenters, and featured research projects, explore the [Program and Abstracts \(PDF\)](https://urology.med.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/2026-LDS-Research-Day-Program-with-Abstracts_Final.pdf)\.

Congratulations to this year’s Research Day prize recipients, **Megha Srinivas** and **Dr\. Rohit Malyala**\.

### Prize for Best Basic Science Paper Presentation

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/2026-Research-Day-Best-Basic-Science-Paper_Megha-Srinivas-940x940.jpg)

**Megha Srinivas**

**Paper Title:** “Engineering methods to study spatial tumor\-immune cell interactions temporally through biopatterning”

Megha Srinivas, a PhD student in UBC’s School of Biomedical Engineering, has developed and tested a novel biopatterning platform in collaboration with UBC’s Department of Urologic Sciences and the Kaigala Lab at the Vancouver Prostate Centre, enabling researchers to precisely study how the spatial organization of immune and tumour cells influences anti\-cancer immune responses\. This work provides valuable insights into how immune cells interact with cancer and may help inform the development of future immunotherapies\.

### Prize for Best Clinical Paper Presentation

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/2026-Research-Day-Best-Clinical-Paper_Rohit-Malyala-940x940.jpg)

**Dr\. Rohit Malyala**

**Paper Title:** “Automating standardization of prostate cancer biopsy and histopathology reports with privacy\-preserving local large language models”

Dr\. Rohit Malyala, a Urology Resident in UBC’s Department of Urologic Sciences, has developed and validated an AI system that accurately converts prostate biopsy and pathology reports into structured research data while maintaining patient confidentiality\. This innovation has the potential to accelerate prostate cancer research and improve the efficiency of clinical data collection\.
