The Department of Urologic Sciences is pleased to congratulate [Dr\. Chris Hoag](https://med-fom-urology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/03/Hoag-Chris-2018-2.pdf), the inaugural recipient of the Jamie Wright Clinical Faculty Award\.

[Dr\. Chris Hoag](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/Chris-Hoag_W1A2687-360x240.jpg)

Dr\. Afshar \(left\), Dr\. Hoag \(centre\), and Dr\. Black \(right\)\.

This new award recognizes outstanding clinical faculty contributions within the Department of Urologic Sciences\. Dr\. Hoag was recognized for his leadership and service, including his contributions to the BC Urological Society and the Consultant Specialists Society of BC\.

Congratulations, Dr\. Hoag, on this well\-deserved recognition, and thank you for your continued contributions to the department and the broader urologic community\!
