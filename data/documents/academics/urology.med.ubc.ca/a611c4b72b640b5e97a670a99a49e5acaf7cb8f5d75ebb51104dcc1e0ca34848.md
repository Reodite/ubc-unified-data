[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/08/Anthony-Emmott-1-214x300.jpg)Anthony Emmott grew up in Port Moody, BC and completed his undergraduate studies in Pharmacology as well as a medical school at UBC\. He stumbled upon Urology early in medical school and became involved in research project with Dr\. Ben Chew to and Dr\. Dirk Lange in the Stone Center\. He enjoyed the exposure to urology through Dr\. Chew’s mentorship and was able to meet many of the excellent residence at UBC who he aspires to be like\. He feels very fortunate to be able to train with incredible staff and residents in the UBC program\! Outside of the hospital, Anthony enjoys a healthy dose of competition whether that is playing ice hockey, boardgames or on the courts where he has recently picked up tennis\. He also enjoys hiking and skiing in Vancouver‘s backyard and has a bucket list of hikes for future trips\.
