I was born and raised in London, Ontario, where I completed my undergraduate in electrical engineering and medical school at Western University\. I was introduced to urology early during medical school and was initially captured by the technical aspect of the specialty\. I am grateful to wonderful mentors at Western who further developed and encouraged my interest\. I had an excellent experience during my elective at VGH and was impressed with the residents’ proficiency and teamwork\. I was thrilled to have matched here and am excited to begin my training and contribute to the team\. When outside the hospital, I enjoy rowing, cycling, and hiking and am keen to explore the unparalleled British Columbia outdoors\.
