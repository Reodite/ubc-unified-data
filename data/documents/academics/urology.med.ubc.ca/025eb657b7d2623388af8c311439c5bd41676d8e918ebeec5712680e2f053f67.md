#### Congratulations to Dr\. Kishor M\. Wasan who was honoured with the Canadian Society for Pharmaceutical Sciences Lifetime Achievement Award\.

The CSPS Lifetime Achievement Award is established to honor pharmaceutical scientists who have demonstrated exceptional dedication and excellence throughout their careers, leaving a lasting impact on the field of pharmaceutical sciences\.

*[Unconventional thinking: how soil science can join the fight against neglected tropical diseases \- Speaking of Medicine and Health](https://speakingofmedicine.plos.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2023/07/Kishor-Wasan.png)*
