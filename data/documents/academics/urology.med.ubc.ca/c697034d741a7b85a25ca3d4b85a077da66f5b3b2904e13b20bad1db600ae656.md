**Gabriela Maria Leon**  was born in Prince George and grew up in Kelowna\. She completed her undergraduate studies at the University of Toronto in Global Health\. She then completed her master’s degree at the University of Oxford in the Nuffield Department of Women’s &amp; Reproductive Health, returning to the University of Toronto for medical school\. Outside of work, her hobbies include finding the best food/coffee shops in the city, running and trialing different workout studios, and travelling\.
