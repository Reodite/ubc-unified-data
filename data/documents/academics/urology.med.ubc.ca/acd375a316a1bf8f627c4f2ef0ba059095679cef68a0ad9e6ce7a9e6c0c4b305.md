Matthew Ho was born and raised in Vancouver\. He attended UBC for undergrad and medical school, and was fortunate enough to match to Urology in his hometown\. Going forward, Matt is looking forward to applying the excellent training he is receiving at UBC to a fellowship and later a practice based around Urologic Oncology\. When not working, studying or sleeping, Matt loves to play basketball, bike, work out and spend time with his family\.
