### **[Vancouver General Hospital](https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital)**

VGH is our largest hospital\.

The clinical focus here includes: Uro\-oncology, Transplant, Endourology and stones, Sexual and reproductive medicine

### **[St\. Paul’s Hospital](https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/locations/st-pauls-hospital)**

SPH is situated in the heart of downtown\.

Located only a few blocks from the water, it is home to a robust Transplant Program

General Urology, Endourology\.

### **[BC Children’s Hospital](https://urology.med.ubc.ca/hospital/bc-childrens-hospital/)**

BCCH is the primary tertiary care pediatric center in BC\.

Here, you will find four urologists covering the entire spectrum of tertiary pediatric urology\.

Residents rotate here during all years of their training\.

### **[Image](http://www.canadarunningseries.com/svhm/charity/images/vghubc_pic1.jpg)[University of British Columbia Hospital](https://www.vch.ca/en/location/ubc-hospital)**

UBC is situated in the picturesque UBC campus\.

Here, several urologists rotate for surgical days, handling various procedures such as reconstruction, infertility, oncology and endourology\.

The Bladder Care Centre is also housed here, conducting urodynamic studies\.

### **Gordon &amp; Leslie Diamond Health Care Center**

DHCC is home to our clinics and resident academic half\-days throughout the year\.

It is located directly across the street from VGH\.

### **Burnaby General Hospital:**

BGH is one of the largest hospital outside the City of Vancouver\. It is operated by the Fraser Health Authority \(FHA\) and is a university\-affiliated hospital with UBC, serving as a training facility for the Family Practice Residency Program and the department of Orthopedics at UBC\.

### [**Lions Gate Hospital**](https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital):

LGH is part of the Coastal Health Services Delivery Area \(HSDA\) of Vancouver Coastal Health\. It provides a full range of general and many specialized acute care services including a Lever 2 Neonatal Intensive Care Unit, a specialist neurosurgical center, a full service Community Chemotherapy Center, a recognized Level 3 Trauma Center and regional Advanced Stroke Center\. 

### **Ridge\-Meadows Hospital:**

RMH is a community hospital provides primary and secondary acute care and specialty services including 24/7 emergency, ambulatory care, day surgery, general and internal medicine, rehabilitation, maternity care, general surgery, orthopedics, urology , critical care, pharmacy, medical imaging, and medical laboratory\. 

### **Royal Columbian Hospital:**

RCH located in the city of New Westminster, is the oldest hospital in British Columbia\. It is the only hospital in BC with trauma, cardiac, neurosciences, high\-risk maternity and neonatal intensive care on one site\. It is also a teaching hospital and UBC Clinical Academic Campus\. 

### **Surrey Memorial Hospital:**

Operated by Fraser Health, it is a publicly funded hospital in the city of Surrey, BC\. it is the second largest hospital in BC and has the busiest emergency department\. SMH specializes in Cancer and Renal Care, kidney dialysis and sleep disorders\. In addition there is an Adolescent and Adult Psychiatry inpatient unit\.
