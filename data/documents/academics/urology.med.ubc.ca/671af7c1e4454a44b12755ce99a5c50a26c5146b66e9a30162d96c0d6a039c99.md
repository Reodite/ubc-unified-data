[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/05/Cyrus-215x300.jpg)I was born and raised in Vancouver and did my undergraduate studies at UBC prior to moving to Ottawa for medical school\. I gained a keen interest in Urology through shadowing and by getting involved in Pediatric Urology and Urologic Oncology research\. I was drawn towards the wide variety of surgery Urologists perform and ability to use these different approaches to individualize patient care\. I am beyond excited continue the next phase of my journey here in Vancouver with the opportunity to train alongside world experts and to give back to my community\. Outside of work, I enjoy playing tennis, cooking, and am an amateur runner\.
