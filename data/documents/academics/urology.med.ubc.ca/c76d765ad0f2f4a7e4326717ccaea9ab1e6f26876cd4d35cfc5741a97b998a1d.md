The Department of Urologic Sciences warmly congratulates [Dr\. Andrew MacNeily](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/09/MacNeily-Andrew-2019.pdf) on receiving the **Canadian Urological Association Lifetime Achievement Award**\.

This prestigious honour recognizes Dr\. MacNeily’s extraordinary contributions to urologic education, including the creation of QUEST and T\-Res, his national leadership with the Royal College in implementing competency\-by\-design training, and his many years of exemplary clinical care as a leading pediatric urologist in Canada\.

Dr\. MacNeily’s work has impacted an entire generation of urologic trainees across the country\.

We are proud to recognize and celebrate this well\-deserved achievement\.
