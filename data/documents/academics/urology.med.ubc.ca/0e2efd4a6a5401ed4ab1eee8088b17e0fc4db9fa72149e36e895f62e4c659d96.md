# **Meet the New Fellows and Residents of the Department of Urologic Sciences**

## We’re excited to welcome our newest cohort of talented clinicians and researchers to the Department of Urologic Sciences\!

## **Fellows:**  
Dr\. Sarah Brink, Dr\. Salah Khudhair, Dr\. Emma Clarebrough, Dr\. Genevieve Laberge and Dr\. David Mueller

## **Residents:**  
Dr\. Sergey Kens, Dr\. Ali Khatib, Dr\. Gabriela Maria Leon, and Dr\. Vikas Arora

### Fellows:

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/10/Photo_SarahBrink-267x300.jpeg)

**Sarah Brink** is from Kansas City, Missouri and completed a Bachelor of Science in Neurobiology at the University of Kansas\. At the Medical College of Wisconsin, she discovered her love for using the operating microscope and decided to become a urologist, which led her to residency at Hackensack University Medical Center in New Jersey and finally to Andrology fellowship at UBC\! Outside of work, she enjoys baking, new restaurants, travelling, fantasy/scifi books and TV, and exploring Vancouver\!

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/07/Salah-Khudhair_Photo-215x300.jpg) **Salah Khudhair**

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/07/Emma-Clarebrough-244x300.jpg)  
**Emma Clarebrough** is a urologist from Melbourne, Australia\. She completed a Bachelor of Science with Honours at the University of Melbourne, a medical degree at Deakin University, a Master’s degree at the University of Sydney, and her urological training in Victoria\. She completed a robotic  fellowship at St\. Vincent’s Hospital Melbourne prior to moving to Vancouver with her young family\.

She has a special interest in uro\-oncology and is completing a one\-year clinical fellowship to hone her skills in the medical and surgical management of complex urological cancers\.

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/07/Laberge-218x300.jpg)

**Geneviève Laberge** is a urologist who completed her medical degree in Quebec City, followed by a urology residency at Université de Sherbrooke\. She has had a longstanding interest in uro\-oncology, with a special focus on the surgical management of urologic cancers\. She is currently pursuing fellowship training in preparation for a future position as a uro\-oncologist at Hôtel\-Dieu de Lévis, in the province of Quebec\.

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/07/2025-Mueller-Portrait-214x300.jpg)

**David Muller\-Sartori** is an MD\-PhD from Switzerland\. He graduated from his urology residency at the University Hospital in Basel before joining the Wyatt Lab at the Vancouver Prostate Centre as a Postdoctoral Fellow in 2022\. His research focus is on urothelial cancer biomarkers\. His hobbies are running and hiking\.

### Residents:

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/10/Kens_S-scaled-e1742521182821-273x300.jpg)

**Sergey Kens** was born in Russia and came to Canada as an international student\. He began his academic journey studying Environmental Technology at Niagara College and worked as an analyst before moving to Saskatchewan to complete a Bachelor’s degree in Anatomy and Cell Biology\. He stayed in Saskatchewan to pursue his MD, where he discovered urology\. During medical school, Sergey loved shadowing community urologists and was drawn to the specialty’s blend of unique patient connection and technological innovation\. The idea of improving patients’ quality of life through innovative technology inspired Sergey to complete a research project with Dr\. Kunal Jana on the use of Irreversible Electroporation for treating kidney cancer\. Sergey’s passion for improving urologic care also led him to work with Dr\. Garson Chan on assessing the safety of transitioning prosthetic urologic surgeries to same\-day discharge protocols\. Sergey is deeply grateful and excited to continue his training at UBC and to join its world\-class urology team\. Outside the hospital, he enjoys snowboarding, challenging hikes, hitting the gym, and spending time with his partner and dog\.

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/10/Khatib_A-scaled-e1742521246831-243x300.jpg)

**Ali Khatib** was born and raised in Ottawa, Ontario, where he completed his undergraduate degree in Biomedical Sciences and a Master’s in Biomechanics before moving to Vancouver for medical school at the University of British Columbia\. During his clerkship in Chilliwack, Ali was introduced to Urology by Dr\. Goodman and quickly became drawn to the field’s balance of surgical precision, innovation, and long\-term patient care\. Outside the hospital, he enjoys hiking BC’s Mountain trails, training in martial arts, and spending time with family and friends\.

[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/10/Leon_G_-e1742521413921-218x300.jpg)

**Gabriela Maria Leon**  was born in Prince George and grew up in Kelowna\. She completed her undergraduate studies at the University of Toronto in Global Health\. She then completed her master’s degree at the University of Oxford in the Nuffield Department of Women’s &amp; Reproductive Health, returning to the University of Toronto for medical school\. Outside of work, her hobbies include finding the best food/coffee shops in the city, running and trialing different workout studios, and travelling\.

**[Image](https://med-fom-urlgsci.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/10/Vikas_N_D-Picture--e1742521482727-283x300.jpg)**

**Vikas Arora** was born and raised in India, where he completed medical school and a residency in General Surgery\. His love for urology began as a clinical interest and evolved into a deep professional calling\. He later moved to Canada, carrying a long\-held dream of becoming a Urologist\. His interest in urology is driven by its integration of surgical precision and medical management, broad procedural variety, and opportunities for innovation\. His role as an Associate Physician inspired him to pursue residency training at UBC Urology, renowned for its world\-class mentorship, surgical volume, and clinical excellence\.

Beyond his clinical interests, he is passionate about backcountry exploration, and cooking and is an amateur singer—enthusiastically untrained but fully committed to off\-duty performances
