The MFRE program offers highly competitive tuition fees in relation to comparative professional programs at similarly ranked universities\.   

### **Tuition Fees for the Full One\-Year MFRE Program**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **UBC Credentials** | Master of Food and Resource Economics \(MFRE\) |
| **Program Location** | Vancouver, BC |
| **Program Length** | September – August \(Plus entry August Summer Program\) |
| **Tuition\*** | $25,031\.10 CAD for domestic students \(Canadian citizens and Permanent Residents\) $58,265\.80 CAD for International Students\. |

*\*Tuition fees cover the entire 12\-month period required to complete the program \(September through August\)\. Tuition is divided into three equal installments due in September, January and May\.*

\*\*Review the UBC Grad Studies [Graduate School website](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/graduate-degree-programs/master-of-food-resource-economics#ga-Tuition-and-Finances) for any tuition updates\. *Note:* *All fees for the year are subject to adjustment and UBC reserves the right to change any fees without notice at any time, including tuition and student fees\. Tuition fees are reviewed annually by the UBC Board of Governors\. In recent years, tuition increases have been 2% for continuing domestic students and between 2% and 5% for continuing international students\.*

### **Additional Fees and Expenses**

In addition to tuition, all students must pay the fees indicated below\. You can read more about these fees on the [UBC Graduate Students website](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/tuition-fees-cost-living/graduate-student-fees)\.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **MFRE Summer Program** | $700 CAD for domestic and international students\. |
| **UBC Student Fees\*\*** | $1,116\.60 CAD / year includes mandatory transit U\-Pass fee\. |
| **AMS/GSS Extended Health Plan\*\*\*** | [Visit website](https://www.ams.ubc.ca/support-services/health-dental/) |
| **Living Expenses** | Calculate an estimate of living expenses [Graduate School Cost of Living Calculator](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/tuition-fees-cost-living/cost-living)\. |

***\*\*UBC student fees:** includes mandatory transit U\-Pass fee\. Student fees are charged in three equal installments along with tuition fees\.*  
*\[\*\*\*\]**AMS/GSS Extended Health Plan:** You must pay your AMS/GSS Extended Health Plan fee in full in January\. There are [opt\-out provisions](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?tree=14,298,0,0#18109) for students with equivalent personal insurance\. All international students new to Canada must also pay an [annual $180 i\-Med fee](https://students.ubc.ca/health-wellness/health-insurance/health-insurance-details-international/imed-temporary-health-insurance-international) \(payable in full in January\)\.*
