At the Master of Food and Resource Economics \(MFRE\) program, the student journey extends beyond academics\. Our events and activities provide opportunities to connect with industry professionals, alumni, and peers while creating a strong sense of community\. Explore the wide range of events that make MFRE a unique and enriching experience, from professional networking to cohort bonding and celebration of achievements\.

## **Social Events**

At MFRE, social events provide students with opportunities to unwind, bond, and build lifelong friendships\. From festive celebrations to team\-building activities, these experiences foster a vibrant community\. Signature events combine skill\-building, cohort bonding, and opportunities to showcase accomplishments to industry professionals\.

**Highlights:**

-   **Outdoor Adventures**
    
    MFRE students explore Vancouver’s beauty and attractions through group hikes, visits to iconic spots like Grouse Mountain and the Vancouver Aquarium, and fun activities like bowling nights\. These adventures foster camaraderie, team spirit, and a stronger connection to the UBC community and the city\.
    
-   **Summer Bootcamp**
    
    The Summer Bootcamp refreshes students’ skills in data analytics, economics, and statistics while offering opportunities to explore the UBC campus and Vancouver\. These activities enhance learning, foster camaraderie, and build a strong cohort community\.
    
-   **Scavenger Hunt &amp; Icebreaker Activities**
    
    During the Summer Bootcamp, students join scavenger hunts and icebreaker activities that promote teamwork and collaboration\. These events explore UBC’s campus and Vancouver landmarks, helping students bond, discover their surroundings, and build lasting friendships while fostering a strong sense of community\.
    
-   **Graduating Project Presentation \(August\)**
    
    In August, students present their group projects to faculty, industry partners, and the UBC community, showcasing their application of MFRE skills to real\-world challenges\. This event celebrates their achievements, provides industry exposure, and offers valuable feedback, marking a key milestone in their MFRE journey\.

## **Alumni &amp; Industry Networking Opportunities**

At MFRE, alumni actively support students by sharing insights, tips, and career experiences, inspiring them to succeed academically and explore diverse opportunities in food, resource economics, and sustainability\.

**Highlights:**

-   **Alumni Networking Workshops**
    
    A workshop where alumni and students exchange ideas, discuss strategies for academic success and explore careers trajectories in Food, Climate and Environment\.
    
-   **Industry Leaders Networking**
    
    Every week, students connect with industry leaders from Sustainability, Climate, Food and Environment sectors gaining insights into current trends and real\-world applications of their learning\. These sessions bridge classroom knowledge with practical industry experience, empowering the students to see their future impact\.

## **Graduation Event**

The MFRE Graduation event is a momentous occasion that marks the end of an incredible journey and the beginning of new opportunities\. This event brings together students, faculty, family, and friends to honor the hard work, dedication, and accomplishments of our graduates\. Alongside the formal ceremony, the event includes the prestigious Leadership Awards, recognizing individuals who have made outstanding contributions to the program and their communities\.

**Highlights:**

-   **Leadership Awards**
    
    At MFRE, We celebrate and honor exceptional achievements in leadership for our students\. These awards recognize students who have demonstrated outstanding dedication to making a difference, both within the MFRE program and beyond\.
    
-   **Graduation Ceremony**
    
    A heartfelt celebration where graduates are joined by faculty, family, and peers to commemorate their success\. This special occasion includes the presentation of certificates, moments of reflection on the journey, and the joy of sharing accomplishments with loved ones\. It’s a time to celebrate you and your achievements, providing an opportunity for the MFRE community to meet and connect with your families, who have supported you throughout this journey\.

## **MFRE Cohort**

At The MFRE program brings together a vibrant and diverse cohort of students from across the globe, including countries such as Mexico, China, Chile, Ecuador, Australia, South Korea, Nigeria, Germany, Japan, and the USA\. In addition, the MFRE students come from a wide range of academic disciplines, including economics, business, agriculture, engineering, forestry, and environmental science, as well as varied professional experiences and universities\. This diversity fosters a rich learning environment where students exchange unique perspectives, collaborate across cultures, and build a global network\.
