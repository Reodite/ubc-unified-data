## Career Ready Skills

We know that today’s leaders require more than just academics to thrive in the workplace\. MFRE offers students several opportunities to gain professional experience, from the Graduating Project to professional development workshops, highly curated to build skills for successful careers\.

## MFRE Graduating Project

[Image](https://lfs-mfre-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/WE-GP1_resized-940x627.jpg)

**The Key Component of our professional degree**

The Graduating Project is a 6\-credit work experience course that takes place in the last 4 months of the degree; the course is designed to provide students with professional work and learning experience\. The Graduating Project is a highlight for many MFRE Students because it’s when they get to apply their academic knowledge towards a research project, consultancy or an internship with a real\-world Industry Partner\.

**Highly Customized and Targeted**

MFRE sources projects from a wide range of Industry Partners and customizes them based on each year’s  
cohort\.

-   Work on a project with government, non\-profit, private sector or academic institution
-   Gain direct mentorship through one\-on\-one support via UBC faculty supervisors, Industry partners and MFRE team support
-   Apply real\-world economic solutions to issues in the climate, food and environment sector

[Image](https://lfs-mfre-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/05/WE-GP2-1.jpg)

### Are you an Industry Professional looking for talent?

**Become a Project Partner and work with our students to obtain high quality deliverables for your organization\.**

\.

-   Win\-win outcomes: Receive high quality deliverables, and provide an opportunity for students to obtain valuable real\-world professional experience\.
-   Targeted expertise: Access to students and UBC faculty with practical skills specific to the climate, food and environment sectors\.
-   Innovative thinking: A fresh perspective to help you address an existing problem or research a new opportunity\.
-   Mentorship: Opportunity to mentor or train young talent in your field of work\.
-   Valuable network: Potential year\-to\-year project collaboration, or referrals for potential employees through the growing number of highly trained alumni\.

**How to Become a Project Partner**

\.

If you work in the climate, food, agri\-food or environment sector, email [janelle\.tan@ubc\.ca](mailto:janelle.tan@ubc.ca) to inquire about becoming a Project Partner\. We develop project partnerships between November to February each year, for projects to take place between May to August\. However, we are happy to connect with you year\-round\!

We work with all types of organizations in the climate, food, agri\-food and environment sector\. This includes start\-up’s, local, national and multi\-national companies, government agencies, non\-profit organizations, and academic institutions\.
