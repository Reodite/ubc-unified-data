The MFRE program is a one\-year course based graduate degree that is designed to provide students with the knowledge and skills to tackle the most pressing challenges and opportunities in the climate food, and the environmental sector\.

  

The MFRE degree offers a strong combination of economic, policy and business rigour combined with a 4‐month work experience project, and a full year of data analytic and career\-ready workshops\. The program offers four concentration options including Strategic Business Management, Data Analytics, Economic Modelling, and Policy Analysis\. MFRE graduates have diverse job prospects and an ability to contribute actionable insights to the climate, food, and environmental sector\.

Studying at UBC not only means gaining a top\-tier graduate degree but also provides you access to an inspiring academic environment, renowned for its commitment to academic excellence, sustainability and innovation and for being located in one of the world’s most picturesque settings, surrounded by Vancouver’s breathtaking landscapes and vibrant cultural scene\.
