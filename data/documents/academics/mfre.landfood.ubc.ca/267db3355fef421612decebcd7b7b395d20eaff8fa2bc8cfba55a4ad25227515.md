At MFRE, we utilize the strong expertise of UBC MFRE faculty members, industry professionals, managers, and staff to ensure our students have the most relevant academic and real\-world skills plus strong support as they develop their knowledge and competencies and launch their careers in the climate, food and environmental sector\.

The MFRE Team includes the Academic Director, Managers and Educational Researchers who are all experienced professionals in economics and business and proud alumni of the MFRE program\. They work to foster an enriching learning environment, supporting the academic success and professional growth of the MFRE students\.

[Image](https://lfs-mfre-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/12/mfre-team-1-940x627.jpg)

  
  

[Image](https://lfs-mfre-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/12/54124533459_5e29b86c39_o-scaled.jpg)

**MFRE Faculty Members** bring their expertise and research knowledge in the areas of ESG metrics, policy analysis, agribusiness, international development, data analysis, environmental economics, commodity analytics, big data analytics, and trade to provide a core knowledge base for MFRE academic courses

**MFRE industry Professionals** bring the most current tools, topics, analysis, and networks from the climate, food, and environmental sector including the areas of forestry, ESG, quantitative research methodology, and commodity trading into the classroom\.
