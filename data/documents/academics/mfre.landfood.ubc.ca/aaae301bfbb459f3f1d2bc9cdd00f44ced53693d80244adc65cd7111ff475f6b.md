The MFRE program has expanded our efforts in knowledge transfer and training in the environment and climate change area with a major focus on professionals and leaders in private firms, NGOs, and public agencies\. Our efforts to date include the successful launch of the Micro\-Certificate in Environmental Metrics for Sustainability Reporting, new and exciting academic courses, professional workshops, and virtual micro\-certificate topic courses being added over the past year – and more plans for the year as well\!

  
  
  

## **Micro\-Certificate in Environmental Metrics for Sustainability Reporting**

  
  

**The MFRE Micro\-Certificate in Environmental Metrics for Sustainability Reporting** is a flexible 15\-week online professional development program that provides insight into the fundamentals of sustainability reporting, with a unique emphasis on environmental metric data management and decision\-making\. The micro\-certificate content is delivered through online lectures, case studies, software applications, real\-world examples, and expert guest speaker sessions\.

**Micro\-Certificate Pathway**

**Courses 1 and 2: ESG Fundamentals \(Required\)**

-   1\. ESG Fundamentals and Environmental Decisions \(3 weeks\)
-   2\. Data Gathering and Management for GHG Emissions and Food Waste \(3 weeks\)

**Course 3: Specialize with Two Electives \(Choose two of four\)**

-   3a\. ESG in Agribusiness \(3 weeks\)
-   3b\. Sustainable Packaging Decision Making for Food Service and Processing \(3 weeks\)
-   3c\. Impact of Business on Biodiversity \(3 weeks\)
-   3d\. Sustainable Waste Management: Food Waste and Diversion \(3 weeks\)

**Course 4: Strategy and Reporting \(Required\)**

-   4\. Strategy Creation and Sustainability Reporting \(3 weeks\)

## What Will You Learn?

-   **Sustainability Reporting &amp; ESG Fundamentals:** Gain a strong foundation in ESG principles, reporting frameworks, and carbon footprint measurement \(Scope 1, 2, and 3\)\.
-   **Specialized Sustainability Topics Across Business and Food Systems:** Explore sector and topic specific areas in depth including ESG in agribusiness, sustainable packaging, biodiversity impacts, and food loss and waste depending on your selected specialization\.
-   **ESG Principles in Agribusiness:** Obtain knowledge of ESG principles and climate risk in the agribusiness context\.
-   **Applied Risk, Impact, and Strategy Development:** Learn how organizations assess environmental impacts and risks and translate them into practical strategies and actions relevant to your chosen specialization\.
-   **Practical Data Tools &amp; Decision\-Making:** Learn to manage environmental metrics using Excel and Power BI, with real\-world applications in GHG and waste reporting\.

By the end of the course, participants will be equipped with the skills to support their company’s sustainability efforts and create impactful environmental reports\.
