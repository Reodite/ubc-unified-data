## MFRE Program

The UBC Master of Food and Resource Economics \(MFRE\) program is a one year course\-based program that is structured to prepare students for impactful careers in the climate, food, and environmental sectors\. Spanning three terms, the program combines academic knowledge with practical experience and career skills to equip graduates with the skills and confidence needed to address complex challenges and offer innovative economic solutions in the climate, food, and environmental sector\.
