With over 400 graduates, the MFRE alumni network is a testament to the program’s strength and impact\. Our alumni excel in diverse roles across industries, leveraging the program’s blend of economic, financial, polocy, and data analytical training to address real\-world challenges\.

MFRE graduates have secured positions such as Economic Analyst, Data Analysis Managers, Environmental Economists, Sustainability Managers, Supply Chain Manager, Investment Banking Analysts, Trade Commissioners, Bankers, and Business Intelligence Analysts\. These roles span sectors like agri\-food, finance, consulting, government, and global trade, reflecting the program’s versatility and relevance\.

Our alumni are known for their ability to combine data\-driven analysis with strategic thinking, making them highly sought after by employers\. Their achievements highlight the value of an MFRE education in equipping professionals to lead in the evolving and growing climate, food and environmental sectors\.
