The **Master of Food and Resource Economics \(MFRE**\) program is a one‐year course‐based graduate program\. Our program focuses on economic solutions for climate, food and the environment, offering a strong combination of academic concentrations, paired with a 4‐month work experience project and a full year of professional and data analytic workshops\.
