Embarking on a journey with the Master of Food and Resource Economics \(MFRE\) means more than just attending a program\. It means becoming an integral part of a community that’s diverse\.  In the MFRE Program, you will get the unparalleled experience of being part of the greater UBC community\. In and outside the classrooms, you will be supported by dedicated staff and brilliant faculty\. Student organizations, campus activities, and Various MFRE Events will enrich your educational Experience\. In this thriving ecosystem, your unique background, culture, and experiences are the seeds of our collective learning garden\.

## Diversity and Inclusion

In the Master of Food and Resource Economics \(MFRE\) program, we understand that the global challenges surrounding food, resources, and climate are as varied as the individuals they impact\. That’s why we don’t just accept diversity — we celebrate it as a fundamental element of our educational approach\.
