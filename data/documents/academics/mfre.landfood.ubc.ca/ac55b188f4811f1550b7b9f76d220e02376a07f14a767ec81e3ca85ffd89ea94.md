#   
The MFRE Experience 2027

The MFRE student experience is energetic, collaborative, and rewarding — a year where the cohort works hard, learns from one another, and builds new skills and ideas together\. Students connect with faculty members, industry partners, and alumni, gaining real\-world insight, support, friendships, and networks that last well beyond the program\.

At MFRE we have

-   **Fun Events and Field Trips:** Build friendships and explore the real\-world climate, food, and environmental sector beyond the classroom\.  
      
    
-   **Classroom Learning**: Economics, Policy and Business analytics in action\.  
      
    
-   **Cohort and Networking**: Small connected cohort with industry connections\.  
      
    
-   **Real\-World Applications**: Apply your learning to a industry partner project\.

#   
Unique Academic Courses and Career\-Ready Skills

The MFRE program offers courses in **economics, business, and policy** that emphasize real\-world application to the climate, food, and environmental sectors\.  
  
Students move from core courses in finance, markets, and policy to specialized concentrations in **Economic Modeling, Policy Analysis, Strategic Business Management,** and **Data Analytics** in the Food and Resource sector applying tools like **Python, R, SQL, Excel,** and **Power BI** to real\-world challenges and opportunities in the climate, food, and environmental sectors\.

A year\-long professionalism and data analytics workshops complements the academic courses, helping students gain technical, analytical, and leadership skills important for career success\.

Our MFRE Alumni have diverse and exciting careers path across leading companies, organizations, and agencies in Canada and around the world\.
