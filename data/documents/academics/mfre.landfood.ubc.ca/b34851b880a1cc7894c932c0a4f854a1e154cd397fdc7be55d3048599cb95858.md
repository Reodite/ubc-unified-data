Mastering data analytics in the MFRE program empowers students to tackle the complex, data\-driven challenges shaping the future of climate, food, and environmental systems\. The program goes beyond technical proficiency, emphasizing critical thinking, strategic insight, and the ability to translate data into actionable solutions\.

The MFRE Program Data Analytics Curriculum includes a year\-around data analytics workshop series comprised of the **Summer Data Software Fundamentals Bootcamp** and **Term 1, 2, and 3 Data Tools and Applications Series**\. By applying cutting\-edge tools like R, Python, Power BI, Tableau, and SQL to real\-world economic and business problems, MFRE students develop the analytical and storytelling skills necessary to drive impactful decision\-making and gain a competitive edge in the job market\. MFRE Graduates emerge as highly skilled professionals, ready to lead with data\-driven innovation in a range of industries including the climate, food, and environment sector\.

## Programming Fundamentals for Economists

Gain Python and R, essential tools for data analytics, through real\-world projects focused on Climate, Food, and Environment, and quickly build strong programming skills required for success in the MFRE coursework\.

[instructor helping a student on a laptop](https://lfs-mfre-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/12/data5.jpg)

## Data Wrangling and Manipulation

Acquire expertise in data manipulation and analysis using Python libraries like NumPy and Pandas, along with R’s tidyverse, building a strong foundation for success in the MFRE program\.

[instructor helping a student on a laptop](https://lfs-mfre-2024.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/12/data2.jpg)

## Applications and Real World Case Studies

Gain practical skills in data analytics through real\-world case studies, applying concepts to solve challenges in Climate, Food, and Environment sectors\.

### **Key Skills**: **Python coding**, **Data manipulation**, **Data organization**, **Programming fundamentals**

Learn how to work with data stored in databases using SQL queries\. Explore how advanced analytics can help you find patterns, make predictions, and support informed decision\-making\.

## **Data Analytics Applications for MFRE**

Data Analytics in the MFRE Program focuses on applying real\-world datasets\. Students work on projects that integrate economic modeling, statistical techniques, and advanced analytics to address issues in the Climate, Food and Environment\. By tackling industry\-relevant problems, students develop practical skills that align with the demands of today’s data\-driven economy\.
