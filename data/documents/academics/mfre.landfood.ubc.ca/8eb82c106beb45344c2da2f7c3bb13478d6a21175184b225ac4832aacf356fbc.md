In Term 1, MFRE students take all courses together giving them the opportunity to gain core knowledge connected to the climate, food, and environmental sector and to be ready to move forward to Term 2 concentrations\.

FRE 525

## **Environmental Economics &amp; Policy Analysis**

  
Analyze environmental market failures and the role of policy in addressing them\. Apply economic tools to climate, resource, and urban environmental challenges\.

**Topics:** Climate change policy, natural resource economics, energy, property rights, and market\-based versus regulatory solutions\.

FRE 525

[Source link](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Course:FRE525)

FRE 502

## **Food Market Analysis in the Food and Resource Sector**

Explore the intersection of environmental, global food policy, and trade economics\. Using debates, lectures, and presentations\.

**Topics:** Policy impact on welfare, trade flows, resource use, and climate, international taxation of carbon emissions, along with the application of R\.

FRE 502

[Source link](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Course:FRE502)

FRE 528

## **Applied Econometrics in the Food and Resource Sector**

Learn data and regression foundations while developing quantitative skills for real\-world applications in the food and resource sector\.

**Topics:** Tableau for data visualizations, complex data communications such as CO2 emissions and impact, along with the application of R\.

FRE 528

[Source link](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Course:FRE528)

FRE 516

## **Financial and Sustainability Reporting**

Learn financial, investment and ESG principles and analytics most relevant to the food and resource sector\.

  
  
**Topics:** Fundamental finance, ESG metrics, green financial sector, capital budgeting, and Advanced Excel and Power BI as finance tools\.

FRE 516

[Source link](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Course:FRE516)

FRE 520

## **MFRE Professional Seminar**

Gain insights to the climate, food and environmental sector through seminars from industry leaders and researchers\.

**  
Topics:** Climate Change and Climate Impact, Environmental Management, Business and Agribusiness, Food Processing &amp; Finance\.

FRE 520

[Source link](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Course:FRE520)
