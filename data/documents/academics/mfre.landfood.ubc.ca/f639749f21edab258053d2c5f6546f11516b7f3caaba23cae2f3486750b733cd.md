## MFRE Graduating Project

The MFRE Graduating Project is a highlight for MFRE students time and time again\. It is a 6\-credit capstone course that takes place during the final 4 months of the degree\. Students gain real\-world experience by conducting a project for Industry Partners in private, public or non\- profit sectors\.

## Data Analytics Workshops

The Climate, Food and Environment sector are in need of strong data professionals that can transform data into actionable insights\. The MFRE Program offers a series of data analytics workshops using a variety of software tools so students are equipped with the skills to interpret, and leverage data effectively\.
