### Opportunities to Study/Work Abroad

**Study at some of the top universities in the U\.S\.**

-   American University \(Washington\)
-   University of California \(choose from 8 different campuses, including Berkeley and UCLA\)
-   University of Virginia
-   University of Washington
-   University of Notre Dame

### Washington Centre Internships

-   Earn credit and valuable work experience through an internship with the Washington Centre
-   Learn advocacy skills with Amnesty International, the U\.S\. Commission on Civil Rights or Oxfam America
-   Gain business and IT experience with the U\.S\. Department of Treasury, U\.S\. Chamber of Commerce or the Inter\-American Development Bank
-   Immerse yourself in the legislative and diplomatic process with positions in a Congressional or Senate office \(past program placements have included positions in the offices of former House Speaker Nancy Pelosi and Hillary Clinton\)
