There are no special admission requirements for the US Studies Major or Minor\. However, students should be aware of the prerequisites for certain courses that are part of U\.S\. Studies \(especially for the [Department of Economics](http://www.econ.ubc.ca/)\)\.

Students can declare the USST Major on Workday\.

If you have yet to declare a specialization or wish to change your current specialization to the USST Major, please follow the steps outlined in this Workday tutorial: [**Declaring or changing your primary major, honours, or specialization**\.](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/degree-planning/declaring-or-changing-your-primary-major-honours-or-specialization/)  

If you wish to add the USST Major to your existing specialization \(e\.g\., a student who wishes to complete the USST Major alongside their existing Sociology Major\), please follow the steps outlined in this Workday tutorial: [**Adding a program of study \(second major or minor\)**\.](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/degree-planning/adding-a-program-of-study-second-major-or-minor/)
