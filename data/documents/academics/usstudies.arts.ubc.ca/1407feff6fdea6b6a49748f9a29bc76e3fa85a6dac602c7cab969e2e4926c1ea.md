A degree in US Studies prepares graduates for a variety of careers\. A short list of potential career\-paths available includes:

-   **Graduate school**  U\.S\. studies allows students to continue their studies in a wide array of disciplines \(political science, history, economics, business, law, journalism\)\.
-   **U\.S\. Government**  use your U\.S\.\-specific knowledge to find employment in any of the numerous departments and agencies that comprise the U\.S\. civil service \(for US citizens\)\.
-   **Canadian Government**  join Foreign Affairs and work on issues involving the U\.S\. \(just about all of them\), find a placement in the Embassy in Washington, or work on US related issues in other agencies\.
-   **Politics** find exciting opportunities on Capitol Hill working in a Congressional or Senatorial office, or in a state capitol, or a consulting or lobbying firm\.
-   **Business**  work for Canadian companies seeking to break into or expand their positions in the U\.S\. marketplace\.
-   **Journalism**  cover U\.S\. politics
