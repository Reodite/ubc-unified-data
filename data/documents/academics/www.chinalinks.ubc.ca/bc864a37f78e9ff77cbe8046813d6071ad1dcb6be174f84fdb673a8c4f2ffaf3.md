China Links and BMO Financial Group are pleased to announce the BMO Bursary for Non\-Profit Enterprises in the amount of $2,000\.  The BMO Bursary for Non\-Profit Enterprises will be awarded under the following conditions:

-   To be issued to a registrant from a registered non\-profit organization, charitable organization, or academic institution
-   The bursary must be used towards the China Links Core Program \(both module I and II combined – Vancouver and Shanghai\)
-   Applications close March 10, 2012
-   Bursaries are awarded based on demonstrated institutional and professional focus on China or the Asia\-Pacific region \(in the application form below, please upload CV and write a paragraph explaining how China Links and practical training on China will advance your organization’s mandate\)
-   Applicants are welcome to upload supporting documents such as reports or journal articles\.
