### **CHINA LINKS: PREPARING NORTH AMERICAN ORGANIZATIONS FOR SUCCESS WITH CHINA**

China Links offers essential training outcomes for North American private and public sector organizations that engage with China in various capacities\.  The program focuses on practical knowledge and informed decision\-making capabilities through the delivery of innovative educational approaches\. China Links draws upon extensive research on performance norms and institutions, and coordinated compliance conducted through the Institute of Asian Research’s Asia Pacific Dispute Resolution Program\. The primary goal of China Links is to give participants the tools and approaches necessary to work successfully in and with China\.

[DOWNLOAD THE CHINA LINKS BROCHURE](https://chinalinks.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2012/02/ChinaLinksBrochure_01.12.pdf)

Who should attend China Links seminars:

• Managers across a range of functional areas  
• Executives and executive directors  
• Project managers  
• Lawyers  
• Policy analysts and advisors  
• Professionals who engage with China in all types of capacities
