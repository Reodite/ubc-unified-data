### Profile

## Bio

Dr\. Hirani is a mental health researcher whose work focuses on promoting mental health and well\-being among people who experience marginalization and those at higher risk of poor mental health outcomes\. With research experience in both Canada and Pakistan, she is committed to working with diverse communities and understanding the contextual factors that shape mental health\. Her work is grounded in applied,community\-engaged approaches aimed at generating evidence to improve mental health outcomes for equity\-deserving populations\.

### Research

## Area of Research

Dr\. Hirani’s program of research is equity\-oriented and focuses on improving mental health and well\-being among individuals and families, particularly women and youth experiencing socioeconomic challenges\. She examines social and contextual factors that influence mental well\-being and contributes to the development and evaluation of community\-based interventions that strengthen the capacity of individuals, families, and communities to support mental health\. Her work emphasizes collaboration with multidisciplinary teams and community partners across various settings to inform inclusive and equitable approaches to mental health promotion, including the development, implementation, and evaluation of community\-based interventions aimed at improving the mental health outcomes\.

### Teaching

## Teaching

Dr\. Hirani believes in engaging students in intellectual inquiry by taking them from known to unknown\. Her extensive teaching experience includes mental health, quantitative research methods, and statistics to undergraduate and graduate students\. She has completed several certificate courses related to health professional education and teaching and learning pedagogies\.
