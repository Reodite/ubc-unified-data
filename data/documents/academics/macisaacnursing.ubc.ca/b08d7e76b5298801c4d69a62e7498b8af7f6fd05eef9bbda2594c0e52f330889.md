We have a lot of information on our website, but if you are a little lost, try one of these buttons\!

**Prospective students:** Welcome\! Navigating the whole application process can seem a bit daunting at first\! We get a lot of questions every day, and giving each question our full attention takes time, so we encourage you first to explore our pages, gather your resources, find the answers to your questions \(and the answers to questions you didn't even know to ask\), and when you're ready, start your application process\. You've got this\! We congratulate you on choosing to begin or advance your career in nursing and we are here to guide you\.

**Other questions about the School of Nursing:**  [send us an email](mailto:admin.support@nursing.ubc.ca?subject=I%20have%20a%20question%20about...) We respond between 8:30 am and 4:30 pm Monday to Friday\.
