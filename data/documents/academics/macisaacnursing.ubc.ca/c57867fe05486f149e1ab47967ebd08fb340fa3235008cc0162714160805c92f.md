### Profile

## Bio

Menaka Ponnambalam is a Nurse Practitioner in Cardiac Surgery at Vancouver General Hospital\.  She has over 18 years of experience delivering comprehensive, longitudinal care across the cardiac surgery continuum\. She previously practiced in Cardiac Surgery at the University of Ottawa Heart Institute \(2019\-2025\) and St\. Michael’s Hospital in Toronto \(2008\-2019\)\.  She served as Adjunct Faculty at the University of Toronto Bloomberg Faculty of Nursing \(2011\-2019\) where she lectured in the *Managing Urgent and Emergent Clinical Problems in Adults* course\. 

Menaka regularly presents nationally and internationally and has contributed to multiple peer\-reviewed publications\. A strong advocate for team\-based care and interdisciplinary collaboration, she is dedicated to advancing high\-quality, patient\-centered cardiac surgical care\.
