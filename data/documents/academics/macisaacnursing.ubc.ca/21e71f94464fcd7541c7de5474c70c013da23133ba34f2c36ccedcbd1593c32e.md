## About

Created by colleagues, alumni, and friends in memory of Sheena Davidson, Assistant Professor at the UBC School of Nursing, and in recogntion of her special interest in maternal\-child health, the Sheena Davidson Nursing Research Endowment provides support for research in Nursing, specifically for the study of issues related to childbearing women and their families\.

### Donate Instructions

2.  Click the **DONATE** button to open the **giveUBC** page\. You will be taken to the UBC main site\.
