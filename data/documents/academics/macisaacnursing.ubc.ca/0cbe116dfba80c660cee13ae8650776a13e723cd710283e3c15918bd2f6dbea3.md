[Find Parking](https://parking.ubc.ca/map)

The School of Nursing is located in the **Gateway Health Building** at the corner of University Boulevard and Wesbrook Mall \(5955 University Blvd\) down the street from the Robert H Lee Alumni Centre\. 

The parkades closest to the Gateway Health Building are 

-   **Health Sciences Parkade** 2250 Health Sciences Mall \(behind UBC Hospital via Agronomy Road&gt;Health Sciences Mall\)
-   **North Parkade** 6115 Student Union Boulevard \(north, past main bus loop, near residences\)

The surface lots \(in blue on the map\) under the Strangway building, in front of the Bookstore, under The Nest, or in front of the Hospital are not run by UBC Parking and **DO NOT** accommodate parking passes\. *If you have been provided with a parking pass, please do not use these surface lots unless specifically advised to do so*\.

If you do not have a parking pass, payment is by machine in the parkades or surface lots, or by using the HONK mobile app\.

If you have been provided with a parking pass, please follow the instructions in the email that accompanied it\.
