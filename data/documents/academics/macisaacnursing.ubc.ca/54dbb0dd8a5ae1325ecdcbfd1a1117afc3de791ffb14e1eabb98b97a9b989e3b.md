### Profile

## Bio

Ms\. Thiessen is a lecturer in the Masters of Nursing\- Nurse Practitioner program\. Her work in the program involves lecturing, clinical assessment, and committee work on student research\. She has a strong background in acute and chronic conditions, specifically in cardiology, and the older adult population\.

### Teaching

## Teaching

## Teaching Interests

Ms\. Thiessen’s teaching is shaped by her practice in both acute and chronic disease management, with a specialty in cardiology and older adult populations\. She currently practices in an outpatient cardiac clinic\.  She provides various learning modalities to cultivate a rich learning experience including problem based learning, interactive technology and kinesthetic learning\.
