### Profile

## Bio

Jillisa Byard is an accomplished nursing professional with over 13 years of experience spanning cardiology, critical care, and clinical informatics\. In her current role as Clinical Nurse Specialist \(CNS\) for the Provincial Adult Congenital Heart Program at Providence Health Care\. Her initiatives emphasize quality improvement, knowledge translation, and the integration of informatics into everyday clinical practice to optimize patient outcomes for individuals and families with congenital heart disease\. 

She currently serves as Co\-Director of Membership for the Canadian Nursing Informatics Association \(CNIA\) and is the Founder of the Community of Clinical Informatics Practice \(CCIP\)\.

Jillisa’s unique blend of cardiac expertise &amp; informatics leadership reflects a commitment to advancing nursing practice, fostering digital health competencies, and shaping the future of technology\-enabled care within complex healthcare systems\.
