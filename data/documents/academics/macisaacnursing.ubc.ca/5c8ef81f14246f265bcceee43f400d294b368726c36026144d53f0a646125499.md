## About

The Lyle Creelman Endowment Fund In Public Health was established in 2008 through a legacy gift from Dr\. Creelman to UBC and is to fund research by the School of Nursing\. The late Dr\. Lyle Morrison Creelman was a provincial, national and international nursing leader\. Born in 1908 in Nova Scotia, she received an undergraduate degree in nursing at UBC and a master's degree at Columbia University\. Dr\. Creelman worked in a variety of nursing roles in postwar Europe, including chief nurse in the British zone of occupied Germany\. She was later appointed Chief Nursing Officer of the World Health Organization, a position she held for 14 years\. Her visionary efforts helped ensure that countries would achieve self\-sufficiency in health care, often through the introduction of primary nursing care\.
