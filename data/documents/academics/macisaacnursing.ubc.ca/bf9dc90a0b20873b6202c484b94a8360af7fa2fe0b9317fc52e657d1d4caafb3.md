### Credentials

## Profile

**Educational Background**

-   BSN \(Nursing\), University of British Columbia
-   MSN \(Nursing\), University of British Columbia

**Service**

As a Senior Practice Leader\-Perinatal, my clinical responsibilities include coordinating the advanced care planning for the most complex childbearing families\. My role includes supporting nurses to advance their practice through educational offerings on Evidence\-Based Practice\. I liase with Physicians, Midwives, Nurses, Clinical Educators, Program Coordinators and Program Directors on aspects of advanced clinical care in the Acute Perinatal Programs at BC Women’s \(Diagnostic/Ambulatory, High Risk Antepartum/Postpartum, and Birthing\)\. I support system wide practice changes to achieve implementation of best practices\.

### Research

## Area of Research

**Completed Projects**

I am currently developing a 4 hour course for nurses at BC Women’s on Evidence\-Based Practice, and have been involved in our "Best Practices" project at BC Women’s\. I also currently teach a 4 hour workshop on normal labour and birth with a multidisciplinary team for 3rd year medical students, nursing and midwifery students\.

### Teaching

## Teaching

**Current Teaching**

I am an advanced practice nurse responsible for planning, implementing and coordinating clinical care of childbearing women, families and/or neonates with complex needs\. My scope of practice includes consultation, education, research, and administration\.
