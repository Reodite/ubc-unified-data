## Bio

As the recently appointed UBC\-Providence Health Care Professorship in Indigenous Cultural Safety and Nursing and \(acting\) Assistant Professor, Stephanie Van Haute’s work fosters crucial dialogues for truth and reconciliation, promoting health equity and community wellness\. As a Red River Métis scholar, she is committed to Indigenous health equity and reconciliatory practice within nursing and the wider healthcare systems' change rooted in social determinants of health and intersectionality\.
