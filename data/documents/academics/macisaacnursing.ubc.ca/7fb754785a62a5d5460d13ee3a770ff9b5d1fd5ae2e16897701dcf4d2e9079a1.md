Our alumni are part of a community of exceptional nurses, leaders, and educators\. Generations of alumni have enriched the reputation of our School through their many achievements in nursing, and the generosity of their time and resources\.

Whether it’s been five or fifty years since graduation, we would love to hear from you\.

**Share your ideas\!**

-   Do you have suggestions for events, or upcoming reunions and celebrations?
-   Is your graduating class organizing an event?
-   Do you have photos or stories \(new or old\) that you would like to share?
-   Have you or any alumni you know recently won an award related to Nursing?

Please stay in touch\. To share your ideas, find out how you can get involved with the School, or update your contact information, please email [Stephen Li](mailto:Stephen.m.Li@ubc.ca), Alumni Engagement Officer or telephone at 604\-827\-1513\.
