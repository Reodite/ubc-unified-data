### Profile

## Bio

Ms Mahy teaches primarily in the undergraduate nursing program, focusing on clinical practice, classroom theory and lab simulation learning\.  She has clinical and nursing  leadership expertise in perinatal and mental health and substance use with a passion for women and family health equality, diversity, accessibility\.  Ms\. Mahy supports optimal student learning through the exchange and application  of nursing research, enhanced knowledge and best practice\.
