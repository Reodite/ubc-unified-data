### Teaching

## Teaching

While practicing as a Clinical Nurse Specialist in cardiac care for ten years, I engaged in ongoing educational presentations about the VCH/ PHC Regional Heart Failure Strategy and heart failure management at the regional level; UBC Cardiology Grands Rounds; the Regional Cardiac Program; BC Patient Safety Quality Council Quality Forum; UBC Heart Failure Symposium; PHC Heart Center Nursing Education Day 2023\-2022 and at the national level; to the Canadian Cardiovascular Society annual congress and the Canadian Heart Failure Society annual conference\.

Frequent education session and outreach talks to primary and community care nurses and allied health about heart failure management and ongoing updates to the Canadian Cardiovascular Society Heart Failure Guidelines\.
