## About

The Kathleen S\. Wheeler Endowment for Student Support was established in memory of Kathleen S\. Wheeler, who valued education and viewed nursing as a profession that helped humanity\. Income from the fund supports initiatives for School of Nursing undergraduate students, including student projects, financial support, travel assistance, and teaching support\.
