## About

Gifts from friends and colleagues have established the Elizabeth Kenny McCann Professorship/Scholar in Nursing in memory of Beth McCann, a well\-loved faculty member for 35 years who served the UBC school of Nursing in a variety of roles, including Acting Director\. The scholarly activities of the McCann Professor/Scholar enhance research and leadership in nursing\.
