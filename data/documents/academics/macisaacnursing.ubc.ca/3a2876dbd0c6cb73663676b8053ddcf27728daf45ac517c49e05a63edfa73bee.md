### Thesis Option vs\. Non\-Thesis Option

Both options provide students with an opportunity to conduct original scholarly work\.

-   ### Thesis
    
-   ### Non\-Thesis
    

##### Thesis

The thesis course \(NURS 599\) focuses on the advancement of nursing knowledge and practice within a variety of areas by emphasizing skill development in research\. A range of courses in the foundations of advanced nursing knowledge and practice, evidence and research appraisal, and research design and methodology are available to support thesis work and consolidate students’ learning\. The NURS 599 Thesis \(9 credits\) builds on previous course work\.

[NURS 599 Thesis Guidelines](https://nursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2024-08/thesis%20guidelines%202024.pdf)

[NURS 599 Thesis Approval Form](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/forms/masters-thesis-approval)

[Looking for inspiration for NURS 599? Go through examples of past MSN theses\!](https://open.library.ubc.ca/search?q=*&amp;sort=6&amp;circle=y&amp;campus=UBCV&amp;affiliation=Nursing,%20School%20of&amp;program=Nursing&amp;degree=Master%20of%20Science%20in%20Nursing%20-%20MSN)

##### Non\-Thesis

MSN students entering the program prior to September 2024 who select the non\-thesis route will complete NURS 595: 3 credit Scholarly Practice Advancement Research project\. The SPAR is an original piece of scholarship that builds on knowledge and skills students have acquired from coursework to address topics and questions relevant to the discipline of nursing, including clinical, educational, leadership, health policy, or health administration\. See link: [NURS 595 SPAR Guidelines](https://nursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/n595-spar-guidelines-december-2025.pdf)

MSN students starting their program after September 2024 who select the non\-thesis route will complete the NURS 595: 3 credit Capstone\. The Capstone is scholarly work undertaken by cohorts of UBC MSN and MN\-NP students who work with one supervisor\. The Capstone papers can be completed by individual students or in groups of up to 3\. See link: [NURS 595/596 Capstone Guidelines](https://nursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2025-10/nurs-595-596-capstone-guidelines_sept-25-2025.pdf)

View the capstone syllabus [here](https://nursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2026-08/nurs-595-capstone-syllabus_2026.pdf)\.

### Some Important Considerations until Program Completion

###### Maintaining Registration

When you are not enrolled in courses you must maintain your registration by registering in the thesis \(NURS 599\) or a SPAR project \(NURS 595\) \(this includes the summer session\)\. Students must declare their choice of either thesis or spar by the end of the first year of study\. Students must remain enrolled throughout the entirety of their degree\. 

### UBC Policies for Thesis/SPAR/Capstone Writing

Students are expected to conform to the [UBC Scholarly Integrity Policy \(SC6](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/scholarly-integrity-policy/)\) for their thesis and SPAR work\. 

For thesis work, students are expected to conform with [UBC Human Research Policy \(LR9\)](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/policies/human-research-policy/)
