**It's time to get together\!**  
Whether it has been 5, 25, or 50 years since you graduated, a class reunion is a great way to reconnect with classmates and reacquaint yourself with the UBC School of Nursing\! With your shared experience at UBC, class reunions provide an excellent opportunity for you to renew old friendships, share your stories, and make new connections\. A reunion can be what you want, whether that is a campus tour, a boat cruise or a themed cocktail night among other activities\!

  
If you are thinking of planning a reunion, check out this UBC [Reunion Planning Handbook](https://apsc.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/2018_applied_science_reunion_planning_handbook.pdf) to get a head start on how to plan your reunion\. Our alumni relations team organize countless events throughout the year and are available to assist you every step of the way\. They can provide you with class lists, ideas for programming, advice, and ongoing support\. The Reunion Planning Guide from alumni UBC is also a valuable resource to help with suggested activities, venues, tools, and more\.

  
Reunions say as much about the future as they do about the past and the UBC School of Nursing has a proud past and exciting future that would not have been made possible without you\. We would love to profile your successful reunion and publish a story and photo about your get\-together, so please contact us if you have already had your reunion and you would like to share your story with us\!

**When should I hold a reunion?**  
A great time to hold your celebration year reunion is around UBC's Annual Alumni Weekend in May\. Alumni Weekend is an ideal time to combine your reunion ideas with a full service alumni event already occurring on campus\. Even if you're not in a reunion year, Alumni Weekend offers events open to all alumni\! The start of the school year in September is also an excellent time for a reunion as the campus will be buzzing with activity that is bound to rekindle fond memories\. If you would like to know more about upcoming UBC Alumni events, visit the [Events Calendar](http://www.alumni.ubc.ca/events/upcoming-events/)\.

**Contact Us**  
Please contact [Alumni Engagement](mailto:alumni@apsc.ubc.ca) via email or via phone at 604\-827\-1513 to help you plan your reunion event\.
