### Research

## Area of Research

### **Research Interests** 

My research interests are related to Nurse Practitioner \(NP\) and Advanced Practice Nurse \(APN\) education, regulation and practice, as well as role implementation, development and evaluation locally and globally\. I also have an interest in the lived experiences of APNs, and leadership development\.
