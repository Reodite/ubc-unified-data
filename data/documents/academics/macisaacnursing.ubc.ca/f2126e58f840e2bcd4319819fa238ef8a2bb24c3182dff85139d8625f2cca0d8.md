### Profile

## Bio

Dr\. Laura Sandström is part of Associate professor Jennifer Auxier’s team, a multidisciplinary research group whose work focuses on integration of parent engagement into digital health services for families and children experiencing the perinatal journey\. She is from Finland with a background in midwifery, and brings this cultural perspective and knowledge to her work\. She did her PhD and Master’s in Health Sciences, developing an intervention for multiparas with fear of childbirth and testing the feasibility of the intervention\.
