#### Use of Digital Technology Policy

This policy sets up obligations for students regarding the use of digital information\-sharing technology \(including devices such as mobile phones, tablets, laptops or other devices\) ensuring privacy and confidentiality in their role as health professional

[2021\-08\-09 Use of Digital Technology Policy\_3\.pdf](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/policies/2021-08-09%20Use%20of%20Digital%20Technology%20Policy_3.pdf) \(216\.9 KB\)

#### Use of Student Work for Teaching and Learning Purposes

This policy refers to cases when faculty may want to use 'well done' work of individual students, as a learning example for other students\.  The author is asked to sign a Consent Form for the SoN to use Personal Work as a Learning Resource for others\.

[Use of Student Work for Teaching \- Learning Purposes\_0\_0\.pdf](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/policies/Use%20of%20Student%20Work%20for%20Teaching%20-%20Learning%20Purposes_0_0.pdf) \(172\.89 KB\)
