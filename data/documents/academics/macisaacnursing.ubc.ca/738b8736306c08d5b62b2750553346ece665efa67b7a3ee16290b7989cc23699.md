## Profile

**Educational Background**

-   Registered Nursing Diploma, Royal Alexandra Hospital
-   BScN, University of Alberta
-   MSN, University of British Columbia

**Affiliations**

-   Canadian Heart Rhythm Society: Board of Directors Member \(Allied Health Representative\)
-   Canadian Council of Cardiovascular Nurses
-   Heart Rhythm Society

**Honours &amp; Awards**

-   2012: CRNBC Award of Excellence in Nursing Education
-   2011: CCCN Cardiovascular Nursing Clinical Excellence Award

**Service**

-   Provide clinical leadership in the development and implementation of nursing practice standards, guidelines, education strategies, quality improvement, research initiatives and system changes within the Transcatheter Heart Valve and Heart Rhythm Program\.
-   Participation in unit specific, program and hospital wide development of protocols, guidelines and nursing standards\.
-   Extensive experience presenting educational sessions and cardiac processes of care at hospital, regional and national conferences\.
