### Profile

## Bio

Dr\. Krist is a Lecturer in the Master of Nursing \- Nurse Practitioner \(NP\) program\. She provides teaching expertise in primary care maternity, paediatrics, women's health, IUD insertions, mentorship and leadership\. Dr\. Krist has a part\-time maternity practice working in a collaborative, team\-based care clinic in Surrey\. She is also the Fraser Regional Lead for NP Initiatives in BC's Primary Care Networks \(PCN\)\. Dr\. Krist completed her DNP in 2019 with a study focus on quality improvement in preeclampsia risk factor screening and early disease education in pregnant women\.
