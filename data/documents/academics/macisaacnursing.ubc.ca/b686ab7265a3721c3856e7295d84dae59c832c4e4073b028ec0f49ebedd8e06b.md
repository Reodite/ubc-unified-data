## About

Established by Helen Mussallem, this endowment provides significant resources for students in the School of Nursing to participate in meetings and conferences of professional associations and societies nationally and internationally\. Helen was born in Prince Rupert BC and studied at the School of Nursing at Vancouver General Hospital from 1934 to 1937\. She was the first Canadian nurse to earn a doctoral degree from Columbia University, and was the Executive Director of the Canadian Nurses Association from 1963 to 1981\. The fund enriches the experience of students in nursing, encouraging them to develop their personal and professional skills beyond the classroom\.
