### Profile

## Bio

An innovative nursing professor dedicated to fostering student engagement and interest through experiential learning\. Skilled in designing targeted, audience\-centered lectures that employ effective teaching strategies to enhance knowledge acquisition\. A creative and adaptable educator who values interdisciplinary collaboration and allows learner needs to shape the direction of instruction for a more impactful educational experience\.

With over five years of experience as a nursing instructor, I have contributed to developing and enhancing nursing programs by designing innovative curricula and delivering highly engaging didactic and clinical instruction\. Passionate about advancing nursing education, I am committed to preparing knowledgeable, skilled, and confident registered nurses to meet the evolving demands of healthcare\.

### Teaching

## Teaching

I teach across all five terms of the BSN program, specializing in Adult Medical\-Surgical Nursing, Pediatrics, and Primary Care\. My teaching spans multiple modalities: theory, lab, simulation, and clinical practice\.

Drawing from a diverse nursing background in pediatrics, medical\-surgical, emergency, primary care, and rural and remote settings, I strive to create a collaborative and supportive learning environment\. My goal is to help students achieve their learning objectives while encouraging them to surpass their own expectations\.
