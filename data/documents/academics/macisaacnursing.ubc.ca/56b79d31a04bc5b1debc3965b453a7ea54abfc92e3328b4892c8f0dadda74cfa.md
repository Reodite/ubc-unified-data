### Profile

## Bio

MoonKi Choi is an Associate Professor at the College of Nursing, Kangwon National University, specializing in gerontological nursing\. She joins the University of British Columbia as a Visiting Faculty Member and looks forward to engaging in academic exchange and collaboration with colleagues at the School of Nursing\.

Her research interests include the intrinsic capacity and health of older adults, particularly in relation to maintaining independence and quality of life among those living with chronic conditions\. Her recent research has focused on health\-promoting interventions for older adults living alone, including the prevention of physical frailty and sarcopenia\. She is also interested in patient safety and fall prevention and has led research on the use of artificial intelligence–assisted video monitoring to support fall prevention in hospital settings\. 

In teaching, she works with undergraduate nursing students in fundamentals of nursing, health assessment, and gerontological nursing, and teaches advanced gerontological nursing at the graduate level\. She is particularly interested in teaching approaches that help students connect nursing knowledge with clinical practice and apply what they learn to real\-world nursing situations\.

Through her research and teaching, she hopes to contribute to nursing knowledge and practice, with a particular interest in promoting health, independence, and quality of life among older adults\.
