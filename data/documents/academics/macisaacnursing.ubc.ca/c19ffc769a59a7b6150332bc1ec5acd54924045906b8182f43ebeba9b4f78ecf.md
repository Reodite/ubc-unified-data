### Profile

## Bio

Dr Abdulai’s program of research examines how trauma\-informed approaches to digital health innovation can enhance equitable access to sexual and reproductive health services\. He explores both *what* trauma\-informed care principles should be integrated and *how* they can be meaningfully embedded in the design, evaluation, and utilisation of sexual and reproductive health\-related technologies\. His work draws on three intersecting areas including Trauma\-Informed Care, User Centered Design, and Health Equity Science, to address gaps in how digital technologies are developed, implemented, and experienced by marginalized population groups\. His current clinical focus includes but not limited to STIs, Contraception, Options for Pregnancy choices, and Maternal Health\.

### Research

## Area of Research

**Research areas**

Dr\. Abdulai's research focuses on the application of trauma\-informed care and equity science approaches to the design, evaluation, and utilisation of sexual and reproductive health\-related digital technologies\. He primarily conducts digital health, informatics, and human\-computer interaction research with a primary focus on contraception and abortion, pregnancy choice options, STIs, and Women’s health\.
