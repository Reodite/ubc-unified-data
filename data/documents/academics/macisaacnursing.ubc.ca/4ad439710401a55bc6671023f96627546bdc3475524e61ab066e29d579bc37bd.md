## About

The Verna Huffman Splane Global Health Endowment was established in 2006 by Dr\. Verna Huffman Splane, a longtime friend and supporter of the UBC School of Nursing who holds the position of Honorary Professor\. Dr\. Splane's public health nursing expertise led her to roles as Canada's first Principal Nursing Officer, Vice\-President of the International Council of Nurses, and Nursing Advisor to the World Health Organization\. Income from the endowment supports global health education, international projects, and interdisciplinary global health initiatives within the School of Nursing\.
