Specialized Domains of Nursing Practice \- Organizational Considerations for Fostering Health \(3\)

[MSN](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/course-programs/msn)

[MN\-NP](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/course-programs/mn-np)

[MHLP](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/course-programs/mhlp)

[PhD](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/course-programs/phd)

The section of NURS 586 is specially designed for the students' enrolled in the [MHLP](https://apscpp.ubc.ca/programs/mhlp/) in Seniors Care program\.  It focuses on the organizational considerations for fostering health and managing health threats for seniors\. 

PHD, MSN and MN Students may be eligible to register with the instructor's permission\. Please send a statement of intent \(1 page / 250 words\) to demonstrate knowledge in the substantive area to instructor, and copy [student\.services@nursing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:student.services@nursing.ubc.ca) to obtain permission\.
