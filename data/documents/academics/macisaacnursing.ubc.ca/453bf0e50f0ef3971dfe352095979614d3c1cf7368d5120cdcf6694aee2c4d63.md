**Initial Applications for the MacIsaac Nursing BSN program for the September 2026W intake have now CLOSED\. Applications for the 2027W intake will open in early October 2026\.**

Thank you for your interest in the MacIsaac Nursing BSN Program at UBC Vancouver\.

We offer an innovative advanced standing Bachelor of Science in Nursing \(BSN\) program designed to prepare leaders in nursing practice\. Students enter at the third year level and commit to an intensive full\-time program of theoretical and clinical coursework delivered over five consecutive academic terms\. The BSN program is an IN\-PERSON program based at the Point Grey Campus\. Classes and labs are delivered in\-person, and attendance at in\-person experiences is required\.

Please explore the information available throughout this website to learn more about the program and the application process\.
