## About

School of Nursing Student Support Fund \- Contributions to this fund are used to support student bursaries, field placements, and equipment\. This fund will also cover emergency costs for students as necessary\. Alumni support helps to remove financial barriers and enables Nursing students to engage full\-time in their demanding clinical and campus\-based training\. A $500 gift to this fund would send a student to a conference, providing exposure to the newest ideas in his or her field of nursing; a contribution of $100 would allow a student to purchase additional textbooks and learning resources\.
