The School of Nursing has two practicum opportunities for MSN Students:

-   [NURS 530](https://nursing.ubc.ca/course/nurs-530) \- Advanced Practice in Nursing
-   [NURS 579A](https://nursing.ubc.ca/course/nurs-577a) \- Advanced Practice in Nursing and the Role of the Administrator

It is normally recommended that students have completed their first year in the MSN Program, and their four core courses \(NURS 511, 504, 502, 552\) prior to engaging in practicum opportunities\. Also, please note that students are required to request or sign up for their practicum opportunity three to six months in advance of their practicum start date\.
