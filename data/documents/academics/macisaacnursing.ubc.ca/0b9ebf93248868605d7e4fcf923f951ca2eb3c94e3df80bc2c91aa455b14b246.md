### Profile

## Bio

Dr\. Harding is an Assistant Professor of Teaching in the nurse practitioner \(NP\) program\. In 2024, she completed a PhD in Nursing to learn from Indigenous peoples about how cultural safety is enacted in health care with the goal of supporting NPs in providing culturally safe care in specific health care situations\. She continues to work in direct clinical practice as an NP in primary care\.
