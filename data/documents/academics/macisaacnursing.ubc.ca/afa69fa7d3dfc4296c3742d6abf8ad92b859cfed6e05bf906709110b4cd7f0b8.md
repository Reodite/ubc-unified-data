## WHY NURSING?                 

Nurses make a real difference to the health and well\-being of individuals, families, communities and populations—at home in Canada and globally\. We influence the lives of others in so many ways\. We know how the social determinants of health \(e\.g\., safety, security\) influence quality of life\. We are internationally\-renowned for our cutting edge contributions to health research, policy, practice and leadership\. Are you ready to join us in making  a real difference? 

## WHAT MAKES US UNIQUE?

### Research 

We are a top\-ranked , internationally renowned research university

### International Engagement

We have formal partnerships with universities around the world

### Interprofessional Practice 

We share the campus with our other healthcare colleagues\-\-Medicine, Pharmacy, Nutrition Science, Occupational Therapy, Physiotherapy, Dentistry, and many more\! 

### Indigenous Health 

The School of Nursing Indigenous Cultural Safety Committee guides the co\-creation of infrastructure, processes, resources, and capacity for Indigenous cultural safety and anti\-racist care within the School of Nursing\.
