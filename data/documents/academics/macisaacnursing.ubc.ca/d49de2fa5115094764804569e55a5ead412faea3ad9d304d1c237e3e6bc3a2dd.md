**UBC Nursing**  
UBC Gateway Building  
Room 5280 – 5955 University Blvd\.  
Vancouver, BC Canada   
V6T 1Z1   
604\.822\.9588

The Gateway Building co\-locates the School of Nursing \(Faculty of Applied Science\), School of Kinesiology \(Faculty of Education\), Integrated Student Health Services \(Office of the Vice\-President, Students\) and components of UBC Health together in a building that will facilitate inter\-program interaction and synergies and contribute to students’ health and wellbeing and to the continued advancement and growth of the two Schools nationally and globally\. [Learn more about the Gateway Health Building](https://facilities.ubc.ca/projects/projects-on-campus/current-projects/gateway-building-nursing-kinesiology-ubc-health-integrated-student-health-services/)
