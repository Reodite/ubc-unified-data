**Service**

Multiple births’ specialist  
Developed and operates Multiple Births Support Program with services for multiple birth families during pregnancy and parenthood, health professionals, and multiple birth organizations at the local, regional, and national levels\.  
Services for multiple birth families include: networking assistance, information, education, consultation, referrals, breast feeding support, counseling, and advocacy\. Help is provided via telephone, electronic mail, and face\-to\-face in community settings\.  
Services for health professionals and multiple birth organizations include consultation and education\.  
Participates in the planning and delivery of health and parenting services for the multiple birth community\.  
Annual publication, with online access to Twins, Triplets, and More\! Resource Guide for Multiple Pregnancy and Parenthood\.  
Publishes in the professional and parenting literature\. Manuscript reviews for professional journals\.  
Member of Multiple Births Canada Advisory Board\.
