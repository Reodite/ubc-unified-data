### Profile

## Bio

Jeffrey Wong \(he/him\) is a Research Assistant at the [Innovation in Dementia and Aging Lab](https://idea.nursing.ubc.ca/) at the University of British Columbia\. He supports projects focused on technology co\-design, social prescribing, and community\-based social supports to improve health and social care for older adults and people living with dementia\.
