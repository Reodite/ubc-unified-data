## Bio

Ms\. Vajihollahi's primary teaching work is within the undergraduate program in the clinical and lab settings at UBC and BCIT\. Her goal is to facilitate a safe, inclusives, and supportive learning environment for students in the context of palliative and primary care services\. Her career goal is to enhance the equity orientated Primary Care practicum experience by expanding connections with organizations and partners within community\.
