## 2019 Centenary Medal of Distinction Awardees

The 2019 Recognition medals were presented at the [Nursing Gala](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/community/events/2019/centenary-gala "Centenary Gala") on May 2, 2019 in the form of **The School of Nursing's Centenary Medals of Distinction**\. The medal was created to recognize individuals who have brought high honour to the School of Nursing or to the profession of nursing, and/or who have made long standing/significant contributions to advance the school's vision, mission and mandate\. Read about our century's worth of awardees here:

[Medal of Distinction Awardees](https://nursing-alumni.sites.olt.ubc.ca/100awardees/)
