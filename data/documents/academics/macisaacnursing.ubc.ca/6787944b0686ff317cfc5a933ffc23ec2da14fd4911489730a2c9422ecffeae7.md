### Research

## Area of Research

**Staff Roles**

-   Assisting in identifying potential research collaborators and partners for inter\- and multidisciplinary grant applications
-   Providing critical reviews of draft proposals, with particular attention to ensuring that the research proposal and the funding program objectives are matched, and that applications meet UBC research policies
-   Coordinating peer\-supported analysis and strategizing for unfunded grant proposals
-   Providing faculty members with School and institutional signature services
-   Access to sample grant proposals &amp; information on funding opportunities
-   Support the development of Letters of Intent &amp; grant writing assistance
-   Copy\-editing and formatting of grant applications and research publications
-   Budgets and budget planning consultations
-   Expedite grant proposals and ethics applications
-   Assist with RISe introduction
-   Research poster layout and liaising with printers
-   Coordinate research seminars and workshops
-   Reporting on unit research metrics
