## What's Up with Our Alumni?

**TEACHING THE SCIENCE AND HUMANITY OF ADDICTION**  
**July 2022**

Tim Gauthier, graduated with a BSN with the Class of 2009\. He is now director, BCCSU Addiction Nurse Practitioner Fellowship Program at St\. Paul's Hospital\. Read more about his work in this article in [PROMISE MAGAZINE 2022](https://helpstpauls.com/promise/2022/addiction-medicine-fellowships/) \(St\. Paul's Hospital Foundation\)\.

> We are more than five years into the public health emergency\. Our fellows are out there on the front lines\. And we’re giving them what they need to respond to this emergency with the urgency it deserves\.    \~Tim Gauthier

## We want to hear your stories\!

We have lots of examples on our [Amazing Alumni pages](https://nursing-alumni.sites.olt.ubc.ca/), and we love to feature alumni in our [Touchpoints and other newsletters](https://nursing.ubc.ca/community/newsletters/touchpoints)\. New alumni graduate every year, and each one of you has a story to tell\.

-   Where did you grow up?
-   Who inspires you?
-   What drew you to nursing?
-   What was your student experience?
-   What was your most influential lesson?
-   What have you been up to since you graduated?
-   What is your family unit like?
-   What do you do for fun?

We would love to hear from you\.

Send your story, or a colleague's story, an *in memoriam*, or a news article to [**Alumni Stories**](mailto:Alumni.Stories@nursing.ubc.ca?subject=Alumni%20Story)**\.**
