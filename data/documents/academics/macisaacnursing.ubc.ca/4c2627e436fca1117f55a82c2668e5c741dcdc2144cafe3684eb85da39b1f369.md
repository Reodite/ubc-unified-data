### Profile

## Bio

Zachary Daly \(he/him\) is a registered nurse, whose clinical work was in tertiary adolescent and child mental health, in both inpatient and outpatient settings\. In addition to teaching, he is currently a doctoral candidate at the University of British Columbia School of Nursing\. His research is on the mental health impacts of climate change on youth, with a focus on understanding how these impacts show up in clinical spaces, and on what forms of support young people find helpful\. He is also the British Columbia co\-representative for the Canadian Association of Nurses for the Environment \(CANE\), a national nursing non\-profit organization which is a part of the Canadian Nurses Association’s \(CNA\) Network of Nursing Specialties\.
