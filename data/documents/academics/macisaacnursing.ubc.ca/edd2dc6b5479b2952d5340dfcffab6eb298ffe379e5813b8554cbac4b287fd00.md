#### Academic Concession Policy for the BSN Program

This policy informs BSN students about the UBC School of Nursing \(Vancouver\) processes and expectations for academic concession\. This policy applies to all academic work, as well as to missed time in practice\-based courses\. Practice\-based courses include Foundational Skills and Competencies for Nursing Practice \(FSCP, lab\) and clinical courses\.

[Academic Concession Policy for the BSN Program\_July 2024\.pdf](https://macisaacnursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/policies/Academic%20Concession%20Policy%20for%20the%20BSN%20Program_July%202024.pdf) \(118\.61 KB\)
