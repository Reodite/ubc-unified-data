The UBC School of Nursing has some e\-publications to help keep you engaged with current activities and events\. Subscribe to one or more newsletters to hear updates on research advances, student scholars, community involvement, alumni achievement, and much more\.

### Nursing Matters

Published monthly, this newsletter is for those interested in nursing research and scholarship\. It features news about UBC Nursing faculty and graduate students, research achievements, events, and recent publications\.
