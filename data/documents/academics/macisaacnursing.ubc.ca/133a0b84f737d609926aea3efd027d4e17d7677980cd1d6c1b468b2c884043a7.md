### Profile

## Bio

Rachael Borley Aplerh\-Doku is an administrative and client\-service professional with over five years of experience in healthcare administration, procurement, customer service, and front\-desk operations\. She has experience supporting healthcare, education, and community\-focused organizations through appointment coordination, patient communication, records management, vendor coordination, and administrative support in fast\-paced environments\.

Rachael has worked in medical reception and administrative roles, contributing to efficient clinical and office operations while providing professional and patient\-centered service\. She also has procurement and operational support experience from her work with Total Family Health Organization, where she supported vendor coordination, purchasing processes, and administrative operations\. She is passionate about teamwork, service excellence, and continuous professional growth\.
