## About

Scholarships for graduate students allocated by the School of Nursing\. 

### Donate Instructions

1.  Once you have found the fund you wish to donate to, click the **COPY FUND NAME** button below the title of the fund to copy it to your clipboard\.
2.  Click the **DONATE** button to open the **giveUBC** page\. You will be taken to the UBC main site\.
3.  Click the arrow next to **CHOOSE CAUSE\*** and choose **OTHER** from the dropdown menu\.
4.  Place your cursor on the new line that appears and paste \(CTRL\-V or ⌘\-V\) the name of the fund onto the line\.
5.  Now you can choose your donation type, select the amount, and then click **NEXT**\.
6.  You will provide your details and payment info in the next two windows, and confirm your donation\.

[Donate](https://donate.give.ubc.ca/page/68046/donate/1)
