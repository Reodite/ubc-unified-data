## About

This UBC Nursing Rounds presentation is hosted by members of the [LEaRN Lab](https://learnlab.nursing.ubc.ca/), a team at the UBC Vancouver School of Nursing committed to exploring, understanding, and cultivating excellence in teaching, learning, and nursing practice through educational research and knowledge mobilization\. LEaRN Lab members [Nassim Adhami](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/nassim-adhami), [Elisabeth Bailey](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/elisabeth-bailey), [Carla Ferreira](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/carla-ferreira), and [Ash Scott](https://nursing.ubc.ca/our-people/ashley-scott) will provide an overview of what we mean when we talk about Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\), explore how SoTL relates to educational research, and share examples of current projects that fall along the spectrum from SoTL to research\.
