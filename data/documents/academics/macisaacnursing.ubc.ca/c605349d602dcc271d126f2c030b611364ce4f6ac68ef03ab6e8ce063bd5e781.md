As nursing students you are responsible for being prepared for and actively engaging in all learning activities, seeking and utilizing feedback throughout the learning process, integrating the skills and competencies required for entry level practice in nursing, and understanding the values and behaviours upheld in the [Standards for Nursing Practice in British Columbia](https://www.bccnm.ca/RN/PracticeStandards/Pages/Default.aspx)\. We believe that a healthy learning climate requires recognition of and support for the value of diversity as well as resources to meet diverse learning needs and styles\.   
  
Preparing for the demands of academic life can present many challenges\. The links and resources provided here are simply a few of the available resources through UBC and beyond that can help support you to achieve success with your academic endeavours\.

### Need to Request Academic Concession?

BSN Students who miss **In\-term Work** or who miss a **Final Exam** in **ANY** course and are requesting academic concession must submit an online concession request form within 48 hours of their absence or missed assignment\(s\)\.   
  
If you are requesting academic concession for multiple courses \(theory and/or practice\) you must make a separate submission for each course\. There is no limit to the number of times you can submit this form\. Academic concessions are generally granted when students are facing an unexpected situation or circumstance that prevents them from completing graded work\. Students may request an academic concession for unanticipated changes in personal responsibilities that create a conflict, medical circumstances, or compassionate grounds\.

[**Academic Concession \(In\-Term Work\)**](http://nursing-acrequest-2019.sites.olt.ubc.ca/)        [**Academic Concession \(Final Exam\)**](http://nursing-acrequest-2019.sites.olt.ubc.ca/final-exam/)

### Research Resources

Anyone considering a nursing or allied health career or researching health topics will find extensive information and links to websites here\.

[UBC Library](http://www.library.ubc.ca/) is much more than just books\. In addition to over six million volumes, the Library offers instructional sessions, research help, tutorials, online real\-time chat support, and a range of quick reference guides to help you find the resources you need\. [AskAway Chat Support](http://help.library.ubc.ca/) offers real\-time chat support with a librarian so you can get help with research, citations, finding materials, and more\.​ Connect to the UBC Library from home \- UBC students, faculty and staff can access licensed Library resources including EJournals, Electronic Indexes &amp; Databases, and other resources from off\-campus computers\.

### Submission of Undergraduate Student Assignments

Students are responsible to make every effort to complete assignments on time\. Assignment submissions are expected on the date determined by faculty\. If extensions are necessary, they may be requested up to 48 hours prior to the assignment due date, and should be submitted in writing to the faculty member involved\. In exceptional situations, extension requests within the 48 hour period may be considered\.

### Canvas

Canvas is UBC's primary learning platform for delivering online course content\. In Canvas, instructors can share materials \(text or multimedia\), enable student collaboration and discussion, manage assignments and quizzes, and assign grades\.

[UBC's Canvas website for students](https://students.canvas.ubc.ca/) includes an [FAQ](https://students.canvas.ubc.ca/faq/) and links to [training and support resources](https://students.canvas.ubc.ca/help/), as well as a [Canvas blog](https://students.canvas.ubc.ca/category/canvas-blog/), written by students on UBC's Learning Technology Environment Renewal project team\.
