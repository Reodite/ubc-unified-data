#### Where can I find information about graduate student housing?

There are several on\-campus housing options designated specifically for graduate students\.  Visit [UBC Housing and Conferences](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/graduate-students/) for more information\.

The housing application process is entirely separate from the admissions process \- please **do not wait** until you are admitted before applying for housing\.

#### Where do I go if I need emergency financial aid?

If you are experiencing a financial emergency, Enrolment Service Advisors can help you explore your options and manage your finances\.

Should students in the School of Nursing have emergency financial needs beyond what UBC is able to provide assistance with, they should contact their program advisor for more information\.

#### Where is the Lost and Found?

General items left in the lab or classrooms will be moved to the Nursing Lounge \(T252\)\.

Electronics and other valuable items left in the lab will be kept at T168 by the CSL team until claimed\. If left in classrooms, administration reception at room T201 will hold until claimed\.

Unclaimed items will be donated or disposed of at the end of the year\.

#### Where should I have my referees send their references for me\.

Please have your referees send their references via the online link that they had received when you submitted your application\. Eacg referee has the option of using the template that is sent to them from UBC, or uploading a reference of their own\.
