## About

Established by Professor Emerita Helen Shore, a faculty member in the School of Nursing from 1964 to 1990, the Helen Shore Nursing Endowment supports nursing research at UBC\. The fund enhances research in nursing care related to adults, especially senior adults, living with chronic health conditions\. Preference is given to Master of Nursing students, then Ph\.D\. nursing program students as well as tenure track nursing faculty during the pre\-tenure period\.
