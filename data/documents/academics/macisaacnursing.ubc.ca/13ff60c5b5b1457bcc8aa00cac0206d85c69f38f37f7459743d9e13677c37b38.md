MN\-NP is UBC's full\-time, two\-year Master of Nursing\-Nurse Practitioner program that will fully prepare you to practice nursing collaboratively and autonomously in primary health care\. Graduates will be prepared to assess, diagnose, and treat common and predictable conditions across the lifespan, and will be eligible to apply for registration as Nurse Practitioner \(Family\) in British Columbia\.

# Frequently Asked Questions 

### How does the clinical placement process work?

-   UBC works closely with other provincial Nurse Practitioner Programs, Health Authorities and private clinicians to coordinate provincial placements to meet students’ needs and program\-specific requirements\. Responsibility for placement equity, keep current information, and networking is shared amongst the four educational institutions\.
-   Placement needs are determined several months in advance and is based on current and projected student needs\. 

### How are placements assigned?

-   Placements are determined by the MN\-NP Faculty Team\.
-   Course Leaders are key contacts for placement related issues\. 

### What should I expect for my clinical placement experience?

-   Your placement experiences could involve physician and/or Nurse Practitioners in settings such as Family Practice, Specialty clinic, Acute Care, community health, and outreach\.
-   Your preparation to access sites and schedules must include access to your transportation and anticipating shift and weekend work\.
-   After receiving placement details, you are responsible for contacting your preceptor/site to coordinate scheduling arrangements, including any site and course Leader updates with any changes in your schedule or absences\.
-   Preceptors role is that of a mentor and students are encouraged to be proactive in communicating your learning needs and goals\.
