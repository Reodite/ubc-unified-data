### Profile

## Bio

Pamela Ratner, Professor Emeritus, joined the School of Nursing in 1996\. She was promoted to professor in 2004 and subsequently became the Senior Associate Dean, Administration &amp; Innovation, for the Faculty of Education\. In 2015, she became the university’s Vice\-Provost and Associate Vice\-President, Faculty Planning\. Professor Ratner retired in 2023\. She currently serves as a UBC Board of Governors’ appointed Trustee of the UBC Faculty Pension Plan\.

### Research

## Area of Research

**Research Interests**

Professor Ratner’s research, based in computational socio\-behavioural epidemiology and psychometrics, primarily focuses on heart health and behaviour associated with cardiovascular disease\. She is particularly interested in latent variable modelling as a means of testing health behaviour theory and evaluating health outcomes measurement\.
