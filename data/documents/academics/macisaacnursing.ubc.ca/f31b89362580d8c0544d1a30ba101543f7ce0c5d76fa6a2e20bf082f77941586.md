### Research

## Area of Research

### **Research Focus**

The primary focus of my research during my academic career was the role of nursing in the provision of community\-based health care for children with complex health needs and their families\. I conducted both quantitative and qualitative studies:

-   The development, implementation, and evaluation of an education/support program for parents of children with complex health care needs;
-   Nursing’s role in home care of children with complex health care needs and their families,
-   The contributions of advanced practice nurses to the community\-based health care of children with chronic health conditions and their families\.
