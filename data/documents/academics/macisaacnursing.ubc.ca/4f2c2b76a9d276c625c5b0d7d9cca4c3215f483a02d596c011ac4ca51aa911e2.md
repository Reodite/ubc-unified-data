### Research

## Area of Research

Dr\. Tovillo’s research focuses on equitable access to HIV and sexually transmitted and blood\-borne infection \(STBBI\) treatment and prevention, particularly among Canada’s 2SLGBTQ\+ community\. His doctoral work used narrative inquiry to explore the experiences of Canadian young men who have sex with men \(YMSM\) in relation to HIV pre\-exposure prophylaxis \(PrEP\) access\. He has also contributed to research examining the experiences of Canadian immigrants and refugees living with HIV, as well as the impact of the COVID\-19 pandemic on HIV/AIDS service providers\. Currently, Dr\. Tovillo is a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Northern British Columbia \(formerly at the University of Victoria\) and supports projects focused on innovative HIV/STBBI treatment and prevention strategies: doxycycline as an STBBI prevention method and long\-acting injectable HIV treatments for sexual minorities in Canada\.

Dr\. Tovillo continues to collaborate with provincial and national HIV/STBBI researchers, leaders, and stakeholders to advance public health in Canada\. He remains actively engaged in research, education, and community service initiatives aimed at promoting the sexual health and well\-being of the 2SLGBTQ\+ community and addressing the HIV/STBBI epidemics across Canada\.

### Teaching

## Teaching

**Teaching**

Dr\. Jose Benito Tovillo earned his PhD in Nursing from the University of Alberta in 2024 and has more than eight years of post\-secondary teaching experience across institutions in Alberta and British Columbia\. He also has over a decade of experience as a registered nurse in emergency, medicine, psychiatry, and geriatrics – all of which inform his approach to teaching and nursing education\.

Dr\. Tovillo’s passion for nursing education began during his undergraduate studies, leading him to pursue a Master of Nursing \(Teaching Stream\) at the University of Alberta in September 2017\. Prior to transitioning into the PhD in Nursing program after his first year of MN studies, he completed the core teaching courses and earned a Graduate Embedded Certificate in Teaching and Learning in Nursing Education\.

Dr\. Tovillo has taught a wide range of nursing and healthcare courses since, including classroom lectures, seminars, laboratories, simulations, and clinical instruction\. These experiences have enhanced his ability to provide high\-quality nursing education and effectively prepare future registered and advanced practice nurses in Canada\.

**Teaching Philosophy**

Dr\. Jose Benito Tovillo’s teaching philosophy is rooted in the belief that education should be student\-centred, fostering a learning environment that is supportive, empowering, and conducive to the exploration of each student’s potential\. He values multiple ways of knowing and incorporates Indigenous perspectives, anti\-racist pedagogy, and cultural safety principles into his teaching\.

Dr\. Tovillo also believes in tailoring teaching methodologies to accommodate the unique needs of each student, ensuring their success in the learning process\. Overall, his goal is to prepare nursing students with strong clinical skills and the confidence, compassion, and critical consciousness required to provide equitable care and to advocate for patients, families, and communities in British Columbia and Canada\.
