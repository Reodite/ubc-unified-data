## About

The Consortium contributes to a critical understanding of nursing and health care history\. It operates as a resource in connection with faculty and student projects in nursing and health care history and will seek to connect faculty, students and members of the wider community through scholarly events, lectures and activities\. Budget is managed by Dr\. Geertje Boschma\.
