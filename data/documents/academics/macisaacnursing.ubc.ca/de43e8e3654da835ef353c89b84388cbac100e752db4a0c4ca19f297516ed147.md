Through the contributions of donors, the UBC School of Nursing is pleased to offer the inaugural Graduate Student Conference Travel Award to support full\-time and part\-time trainees registered in a master's or doctoral nursing program to present their research or other scholarly products \(oral or poster\) at peer\-reviewed national or international \(i\.e\., out\-of\-province\) conferences or symposia\. 

Doctoral student recipients of this award are generously supported through the [Ratner Johnson Endowment for Doctoral Education in Nursing](https://nursing.ubc.ca/giving/ratner-johnson-endowment-for-doctoral-education-in-nursing)\.

-   The value of the Travel Award is $500, which is limited to once per degree program\.
-   The total amount available for this funding opportunity annually is $5,000, enough to fund 10 awards/calendar year \(Jan\-Dec\)\. 
-   Applications are accepted on a first come, first served basis\. Once the annual award quota has been met, the program is suspended until the fund resets in January of the next year\. 

*\* At this time, post\-doctoral fellows and interdisciplinary studies graduate program students are not eligible to apply\.*

[APPLICATION FORM](https://nursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/grad_student_conference_travel_award_application_form.docx)

[INSTRUCTIONS](https://nursing.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/guidelines_-_ubc_nursing_graduate_student_conference_travel_award.pdf) \(or see below\)  
 

### **STUDENT ELIGIBILITY**

The UBC School of Nursing Graduate Student Conference Travel Award assists graduate students in sharing the results of their research and other scholarly products with scholarly and/or professional audiences, to contribute to students' career development, and to provide them with advancement opportunities\. Students must be registered in a research based UBC\-Vancouver graduate nursing program including MSN, NP and PhD\. This excludes a small number of professional programs, such as MHLP and certificate programs\. 

### **PRESENTATION** **ELIGIBILITY**

-   Students must be selected through a competitive peer\-reviewed open, and publicly advertised process, to present a paper or poster in person at an academic conference, such as annual disciplinary meetings or more focused workshops or symposia\.
-   The conference or competition must take place while the student is enrolled in a UBC\-Vancouver Nursing graduate degree program\. Students on official on\-leave status at the time of the presentation are not eligible\. Enrollment ceases at the end of the month when all degree requirements are completed, not at the time of convocation\.
-   Multiple students cannot receive the full award for the same paper / poster\. In cases where there are multiple presenters of a co\-authored paper or poster, a single student \(normally, the student who made the greatest contribution to the paper or poster\) may apply for and receive the award\.
-   Students must not have previously received the award during the same degree program\.

### **ELIGIBLE EXPENSES**

Registration fees and expenses associated with travel to give a conference presentation are eligible including flights \(economy fare\), hotels, parking, taxis/transit, and meal per diems \([UBC rates](https://finance.ubc.ca/travel/pay/meals-diem-mileage-rates)\)\. 

**NOTE:** 

-   If students have questions about eligibility, they may be addressed to Merrilee Hughes, Scholarship Development &amp; Grant Facilitator \([ONRTS@nursing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:ONRTS@nursing.ubc.ca)\)\.

Students are encouraged to also apply to the UBC Graduate Student Travel and Research Dissemination Fund \([https://www\.grad\.ubc\.ca/awards/graduate\-student\-travel\-research\-dissemination\-fund](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/graduate-student-travel-research-dissemination-fund)\)

### **AWARD PAYMENT** 

-   PAYMENT TO STUDENT: Upon approval of the application form and proof of conference presentation, applicant can submit invoices for reimbursement up to a maximum of $500 to the UBC Nursing finance team \(include award REF\# with all submissions\)\.  If funded, the student will receive additional instructions including a UBC Nursing student expenses form\.  
-   PAYMENT TO UBC FACULTY SUPERVISOR: In some cases, the applicant may wish for their UBC faculty supervisor to be paid the award funds \(i\.e\. if their faculty supervisor covering the remaining travel expenses to the conference\)\.   Upon receipt of the application form and proof of conference presentation, funds will be transferred directly from the Fund into the faculty supervisor's research account in Workday\.

### **DEADLINE**

Applications are accepted at any time throughout the year and considered on a first\-come basis\. Student applications will be accepted at time of conference abstract acceptance\. 

-   Submit applications to [ONRTS@nursing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:ONRTS@nursing.ubc.ca)\.

There is a 1\-week turn around for confirmation of funding availability, but funds will not be awarded until receipts are submitted for reimbursement\.
