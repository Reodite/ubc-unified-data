### Profile

## Bio

Dr\. Ollivier practices clinically in the areas of complex gynecology and women’s cardiovascular health in addition to her role as Chair of the BC Women's Hospital NP Community of Practice\. She is also a Menopause Society Certified Practitioner \(MSCP\)\. She was a key member of the team responsible for developing the new Menopause &amp; Midlife Health Program at the BC Women’s Hospital &amp; Health Centre, which launched in early 2025\. Dr\. Ollivier is passionate about bridging research with clinical practice in women’s health\.

### Research

## Area of Research

**Research Interests**

Dr\. Ollivier’s research focuses predominantly on postpartum sexual health and global maternal health, though she has been involved with a number of other women’s health projects\. From a methodological perspective, her expertise is in the use of feminist post\-structuralism informed by discourse analysis\. She is also involved in creative, patient\-centered knowledge translation strategies, having developed the first\-ever patient educational tool discussing sexual health after birth in Canada\. She has also appeared on numerous podcasts and televised interviews to share her research\.

### Teaching

## Teaching

Dr\. Ollivier has experience teaching primarily in clinical simulation and skills laboratory settings with a medical\-surgical focus\. Over the course of her nursing career, specific to academic, research, and community\-based roles, she has mentored undergraduate and graduate learners, including medical students and residents\. Dr\. Ollivier regularly guest lectures in various programs across Canada, including but not limited to MN\-NP, MD, PhD, and BSN programs\.
