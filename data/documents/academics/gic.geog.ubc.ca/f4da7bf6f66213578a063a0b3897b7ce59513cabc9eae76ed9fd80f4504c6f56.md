Reserve readings for all geography courses are now permanently located behind the student access computer desks by the entrance\.

Readings are first come first serve basis and holding for reserves will not be available\.  Students are welcome to use the readings inside the Geographic Information Centre during our operating hours or can be taken out for two hour loans\.  Please return reserve books on time because they are high in demand and each book that is returned past due time is a dollar per hour\!
