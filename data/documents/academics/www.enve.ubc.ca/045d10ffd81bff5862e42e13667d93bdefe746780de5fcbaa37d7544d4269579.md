## How to apply

Applications to the joint UNBC/UBC Environmental Engineering \(ENVE\) Program can **only be made through the [UNBC online application](https://www.unbc.ca/calendar/undergraduate/undergraduate-admissions)**\. You may **not** apply to the program through an application made at UBC\.

Please explore the specific admission requirements for the Program through the [UNBC website](http://www.unbc.ca/calendar/undergraduate/undergraduate_programs/environmental_engineering.html#admissions)\. If you have further questions, you may contact the [UNBC Admissions Office\.](mailto:unbc4u@unbc.ca)

Applicants who are currently students in first year Engineering at UBC must consult with [UNBC’s admissions office](https://www.unbc.ca/calendar/undergraduate/undergraduate-admissions) and refer to [this link about transfers](https://www.enve.ubc.ca/prospective-students/transfers/) for more details\.

Please note that we currently do not offer graduate studies in this program\.
