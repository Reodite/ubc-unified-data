*By Emily Bickel*

This year, ENVE hosted a UBC Environmental Engineering Industry Night\. The event took place at the Wolf and Hound in Kitsilano and had an excellent turnout between students and industry\. Both students and industry enjoyed playing networking bingo, an icebreaker game which involved bingo cards with questions designed to help attendees connect with each other\. This event is a great one to come to if you are interested in learning about what you can do with your environmental engineering degree, hearing intriguing stories from industry guests, and making industry connections\.
