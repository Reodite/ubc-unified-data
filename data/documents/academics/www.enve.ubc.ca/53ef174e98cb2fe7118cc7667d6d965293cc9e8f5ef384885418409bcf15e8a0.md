Sydney Roy is honoured as top female athlete at UNBC\.

Sydney Roy, an undergraduate student in the joint UBC\-UNBC Environmental Engineering \(ENVE\) program, has been named University of Northern BC’s top female athlete of 2015\. Roy set a new record for goals in a season when she scored her sixth goal in a season’s final match against Thompson Rivers University\.

More information on this story can be found [here](http://www.princegeorgecitizen.com/sports/unbc-honours-athletes-1.1828043)\.
