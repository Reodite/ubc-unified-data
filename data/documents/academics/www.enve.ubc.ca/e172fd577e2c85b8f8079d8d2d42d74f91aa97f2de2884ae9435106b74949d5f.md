## About the Program

This distinct Environmental Engineering Program is offered jointly by the University of Northern British Columbia in Prince George, and the University of British Columbia in Vancouver\. Students in the Program benefit from the comprehensive learning environments of both institutions and graduate with a fully\-accredited Bachelor of Applied Science in Environmental Engineering\.

This website is for the **joint UNBC/UBC Environmental Engineering \(ENVE\) program**\. For information on the UBC\-based 4\-year program, please visit [Environmental Engineering \(ENVL\)](https://environmental.engineering.ubc.ca/)\. For information on the UNBC\-based 4\-year program, please visit [here](https://www.unbc.ca/programs/environmental-engineering?gad_source=1&amp;gad_campaignid=21459105804&amp;gbraid=0AAAAADk3ZomsbyswakLBU-aYjMKiw4vbH&amp;gclid=EAIaIQobChMIvOODsrrokAMVoCytBh00KzM9EAAYASAAEgJ3L_D_BwE)\.
