The [Supermileage](http://www.supermileage.ca/) student design team was featured on Global News on March 24, 2018\. The segments includes demonstrations of the student team’s two vehicles – their prototype and urban class – and interviews with two team members\.

The Mechanical Engineering department is proud to support Supermileage, and several of the UBC’s other [student design teams](http://teams.engineering.ubc.ca/the-teams/), with funding, expertise, and space/facilities support\.

View the full segment on Supermilage [here](https://globalnews.ca/video/4104242/ubc-supermileage-team-builds-fuel-efficient-vehicles)\.
