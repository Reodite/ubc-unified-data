Susana Zoghbi, a Ph\.D\. student in the CARIS lab has recently been selected as one of the nine out of twenty\-eight students to attend the 2009 Young HRI Pioneers Workshop immediately preceeding the IEEE Human Robot Interaction Conference in San Diego California, on March 10, 2009\.
