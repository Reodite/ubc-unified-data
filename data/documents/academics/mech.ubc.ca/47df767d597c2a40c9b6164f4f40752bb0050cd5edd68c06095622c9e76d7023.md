General contacts for UBC Mechanical Engineering Student Services, Technical Services, and Administrative Services are provided below\. For more detailed contact information, please visit our [Contact Us](https://mech.ubc.ca/contact-us/) page or [Faculty and Staff Directory](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/our-department/faculty-staff-directory/)\.

### Student Services

Mechanical Engineering Student Services is open and we welcome students to drop by for any quick questions or advice\. Make an an appointment with one of our advisors via the [APSC PD Portal](https://pdportal.apsc.ubc.ca/students/student-login.htm) for longer academic advising and course planning appointments, which can be conducted in\-person, via Zoom, or phone\. Course registration requests are processed via email at [students@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:students@mech.ubc.ca)\.

### Machine Shops &amp; Makerspace

Our facilities include a Student Machine Shop, a Makerspace open 24/7, and Support Machine Shop for research and teaching services\. Find hours and access information on our [Technical Services](https://technicalservices.mech.ubc.ca/machine-shop/) website and email [mshop@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:mshop@mech.ubc.ca) with any questions\.

### Electronics Shop

Find Electronics Shop and Student Lab hours and staff contact information, as well as equipment, component, and services information on our [Technical Services](https://technicalservices.mech.ubc.ca/electronics-shop/) website, and email questions to  [eshop@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:eshop@mech.ubc.ca)\.

#### **Book Study Space\!**

Looking for study space? Find available classrooms through the [UBC Online Timetable](https://sws-van.as.it.ubc.ca/)\. You can also book classrooms through UBC’s central [Learning Spaces](https://learningspaces.ubc.ca/find-space) website\.

#### Other Spaces

Book meeting room CEME 2202 for research group meetings or doctoral defenses by emailing [reception@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:reception@mech.ubc.ca)\.

MECH Lab schedules are available on our [Technical Services](https://technicalservices.mech.ubc.ca/facilities/lab-calendars/) website\.

#### Engineering Academic Services

While our Student Services team can help you with MECH\-specific details, [Engineering Academic Services](https://academicservices.engineering.ubc.ca/academic-advising/contact-us/) \(EAS\) provides advising for wider Engineering topics such as procedures for 2nd year placement, requests for standing deferred exams and late withdrawals, academic standing and probation, transfer credit, and first\-year admissions\.

##### APSC EDI\.I

You can also submit [Equity, Diversity, &amp; Inclusion feedback](https://academicservices.engineering.ubc.ca/equity-diversity-and-inclusion-student-feedback) to EAS\.

#### Counselling and Wellbeing Support

Students who need mental health support can book a counselling appointment with [UBC Counselling Services](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services/) \(604\.822\.3811\) or access wider [UBC Health and Wellbeing](https://students.ubc.ca/health) services\.
