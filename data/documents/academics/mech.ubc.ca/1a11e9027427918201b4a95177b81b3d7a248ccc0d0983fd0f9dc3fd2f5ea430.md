##### Selected Awards

-   Killam Teaching Prize, The University of British Columbia, 2022\.

### Research Interests

-   Systemic functional linguistics in engineering design education\.
-   Academic buoyancy and student wellness\.
-   Student motivation and engineering career choice\.
-   Rotorcraft aerodynamics
-   Rotorcraft aeroacoustics
-   Blade\-vortex interaction noise

### Teaching Activities

-   APSC 172 – Engineering Analysis I
-   APSC 173 – Engineering Analysis II
-   MECH 220S – Engineering Software
-   MECH 222T – Thermodynamics
-   VANT 150 – Sustainability and Engineering Design

### Teaching Interests

-   Integration of language and engineering education\.
-   First\-year engineering education\.
-   CAD education and best practices in solid modeling\.
-   Active learning\.
