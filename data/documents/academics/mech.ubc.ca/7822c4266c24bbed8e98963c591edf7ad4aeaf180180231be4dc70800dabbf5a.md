Congratulations to MECH Professor Gary Schajer on his latest publication: [Hole\-Drilling Method For Measuring Residual Stresses](https://books.google.ca/books?id=c8BIDwAAQBAJ&amp;printsec=frontcover&amp;source=gbs_vpt_reviews#v=onepage&amp;q&amp;f=false)\. This book is intended as a practical guide for students and practicing engineers and researchers, and details the history and practice of the adaptable method of drilling holes to measure residual stresses\.

Electronic copies of the book can be accessed by UBC students, staff, and faculty at the following [link](http://www.morganclaypool.com/doi/pdf/10.2200/S00818ED1V01Y201712SEM001), when connected to the UBC secure wireless network, or when signed in on the UBC Library system\.
