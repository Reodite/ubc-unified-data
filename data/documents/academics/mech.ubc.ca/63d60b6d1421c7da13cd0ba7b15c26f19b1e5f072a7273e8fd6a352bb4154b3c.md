[Eena](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/09/Eena1.jpg)The Department of Mechanical Engineering at UBC Vancouver is a world leader in mechanical engineering and research\. Offering strong undergraduate and graduate education, ranging from an award\-winning, specialized second year program to unique courses spanning diverse research areas, the Department is made up of students and professors who push their limits\.

Our faculty members hold multiple research chairs, investigate a variety of areas:

-   [Applied Solid Mechanics](https://mech.ubc.ca/research-main/applied-solid-mechanics/)
-   [Biomechanics and Biomedical Engineering](https://mech.ubc.ca/research-main/biomechanics-biomedical-engineering/)
-   [Computational Engineering](https://mech.ubc.ca/research-main/computational-engineering/)
-   [Energy &amp; Environment](https://mech.ubc.ca/research-main/energy-environment/)
-   [Fluid Mechanics](https://mech.ubc.ca/research-main/fluid-mechanics/)
-   [Manufacturing Automation and Robotics](https://mech.ubc.ca/research-main/manufacturing-automation-robotics/) 
-   [Mechatronics &amp; Instrumentation](https://mech.ubc.ca/research-main/mechatronics-instrumentation/)
-   [Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering](https://mech.ubc.ca/research-main/naval-architecture-marine-engineering/)

Their work spans many laboratories, and they perform cutting\-edge research that keeps them at the forefront of their fields\.

## Our Mission &amp; Values

UBC Mechanical Engineering strives to address society’s most pressing challenges through our scholarship, teaching, and innovation\.

Members of the Department of Mechanical Engineering value:

-   Student, staff, and faculty growth and well\-being
-   Fairness, courtesy, and good faith
-   Passion, authenticity, and integrity

We hold our values and mission as an integral frame of reference to inform our decisions and actions\.
