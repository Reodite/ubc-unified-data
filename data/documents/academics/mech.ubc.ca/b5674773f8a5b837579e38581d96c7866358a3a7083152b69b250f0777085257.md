# Featured Research Labs

UBC Mechanical Engineering is home to a successful and recognized research department, where our experts pursue [innovative solutions to real world problems](https://mech.ubc.ca/research/impact/) and collaborate with international industry\. Our laboratories are where professors and their teams of [graduate](https://mech.ubc.ca/graduate/) and [undergraduate](https://mech.ubc.ca/undergraduate/research-opportunities/) students, postdoctoral fellows and research staff investigate interdisciplinary issues in the areas of applied solid mechanics, biomechanics &amp; medical devices, computational engineering, energy &amp; environment, engineering education, fluid mechanics, manufacturing automation &amp; robotics, mechanics &amp; instrumentation, and naval architecture &amp; marine engineering\.

The featured labs below are a small sample of the work our researchers do – learn about more about the innovations in technology and real\-world impact by our faculty members by exploring our [research areas](https://mech.ubc.ca/research/)\.
