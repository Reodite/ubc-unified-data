### Research Interests

My research interests are in the area of CAD/CAM/CAI \(Computer\-Aided Design, Manufacturing, and Inspection\) with emphasis on:

-   Highly flexible and direct CAD modeling
-   Three\-axis and five\-axis CNC machining
-   Computational tools for 3D printing and 3D laser scanning

### Current Research Work

-   Multi\-axis CNC machining simulation and process verification
-   Generic postprocessor development for multi\-axis machine tools
-   Optimal printing paths and adaptive infill structures for 3D printing
