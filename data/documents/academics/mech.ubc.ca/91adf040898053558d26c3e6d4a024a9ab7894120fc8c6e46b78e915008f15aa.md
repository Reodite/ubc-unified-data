#### Professor Emeritus

P\.Eng; B\.Sc\. \(Sharif Univ\. of Tech\., Tehran\); M\.Sc\., Ph\.D\., \(Manc\.\); Life Fellow, ASME; Life Fellow, I\. Manf; Fellow, EIC; Fellow, CSME; Fellow, Engineers Canada; Senior Member, IEEE, Joined UBC July 1982\.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **email:** | [sassani@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:sassani@mech.ubc.ca) |
| **office:** | CEME 2208 |

### Research Interests

-   Mechanical systems automation
-   Analysis, modeling, simulation, and control of production, service and electromechanical systems
-   Manufacturing processes
-   Industrial Engineering

### Current Research Work

-   Fault detection, localization, and diagnosis in sensor/actuator systems
-   Planning and scheduling of operations
-   Monitoring of processes
