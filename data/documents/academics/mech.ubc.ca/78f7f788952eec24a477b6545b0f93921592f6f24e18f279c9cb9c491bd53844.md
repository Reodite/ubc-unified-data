## [Walter Mérida named Wall Scholar](https://mech.ubc.ca/2017/04/28/walter-merida-named-wall-scholar/)

April 28, 2017

Walter Mérida, PEng, a UBC mechanical engineering professor, has received a Wall Scholar Research Award from the Peter Wall Institute for Advanced Studies\. Recognizing UBC faculty members with exceptional research records and a demonstrated interest in interdisiplinary research, the award provides recipients, who may belong to any academic discipline, a one\-year residency at the Wall Institute and $20,000 for research\-related expenses\. \[…\]
