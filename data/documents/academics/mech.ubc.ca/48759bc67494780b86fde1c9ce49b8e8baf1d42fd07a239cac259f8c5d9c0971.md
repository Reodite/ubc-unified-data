Our Electronics Lab is a 20 bench/40 seat facility currently used for Mechanical Engineering courses for hands\-on teaching of electronics and instrumentation skills and for project work\. This electronics assembly and test facility open for Mechanical Engineering and Manufacturing undergraduate student use, and is also available to MECH graduate students, faculty and staff\.

The lab is located in KAIS 1210, adjacent to the [Electronics Shop](http://technicalservices.mech.ubc.ca/electronics-shop/)\. Each of the benches is equipped with two seats, two soldering irons, two waveforms generators, two oscilloscopes, two multimeters, and one DC power supply\. Students are given limited 24\-hour access to build and test electronic systems for their projects, and can access this space for their personal projects as well\.

Learn more about our electronics lab, electronics shop, and services and support provided by our staff technicians on our [Technical Services website](https://technicalservices.mech.ubc.ca/electronics-shop/)\.
