Congratulations to the Class of 2026\!  We were delighted to celebrate the achievements of the class prior to convocation in Kaiser 1180, our main lab room \(transformed for the occasion\)\.

Major awards included:

The Letson Prizes, given to the Head of the Graduating Class for each Option:

-   Andy Peng received the Letson Prize in Mechanical Engineering
-   Christian Pikor received the Letson Prize in Aerospace
-   Allyssa Eng received the Letson Prize in Biomechanics and Medical Devices
-   Victor Miguel Silveira de Moraes received the Letson Prize in Energy and Environment
-   Ethan Gray received the Letson Prize in Mechatronics
-   Grayson Hamilton received the Letson Prize in Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering\.

The Canadian Society for Mechanical Engineering \(CSME\) Gold Medal is given for overall outstanding achievement\. This year’s winner was Christian Pikor\.

The Dale Cherchas Memorial Prize is endowed in memory of Dr\. Dale Cherchas, a leading researcher in the field of Mechanical Engineering, and recognizes the top students in the area of Controls &amp; Dynamics\. This year’s recipients were Ethan Gray and Connor Salpeter\.

The Jaya\-Jayant Prize in Mechanical Engineering was endowed by Dr\. V\.J\.Modi, a Professor in Mechanical Engineering, on the occasion of his being awarded the Killam Prize\. It recognizes a graduate student in Mechanical Engineering for their academic excellence\. This year’s recipient was Sabiha Bhuiyan\.

The David Nikkel Memorial Prize in Engineering Design is given to one graduating student who has excelled in design throughout their 4*th* year\. This year’s recipient was Nachiket Adhvaryu\.

Congratulations to all graduates for outstanding achievement\.
