The Department of Mechanical Engineering would like to congratulate the Department Scholar Award winners, Leadership Award winners, and the NX Certified students of this year\. Each group of winners had their own celebration to recognize the achievements of exceptional students within the Mech department\. Congratulations once again to all recipients\!

**2013 Department Scholars** *\(**This honourary designation may be given to a student at any point in their graduate program, and they hold the designation through the remainder of their degree\)*:  
*\(Back row, from left to right\):* Eric Buckley, Maziyar Jalaal, Devin Todd, Philip Wang  
*\(Front row, from left to right\):* Marjan Zare Bezgabadi, Dr Sheldon Green, Dr Carl Ollivier\-Gooch, Edward Angus, Kai Kin Gary Yan  
*Not pictured:* Yuen Alexander, Lawrence Buchan, Lee JeongHyun, Shalaleh Rismani, Ale Etrati Khosroshahi Seyed Ali\.
