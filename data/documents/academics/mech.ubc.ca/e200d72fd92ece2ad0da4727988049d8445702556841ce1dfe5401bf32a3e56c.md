The Mechanical Engineering Department offers Master of Applied Science \(MASc\), Master of Engineering \(MEng\), Master of Engineering Leadership \(MEL\), and Doctor of Philosophy \(PhD\) programs at the graduate level\. Whether you are prospective student, or a current student, you contact to our Graduate Advisors for different types of inquiries\.
