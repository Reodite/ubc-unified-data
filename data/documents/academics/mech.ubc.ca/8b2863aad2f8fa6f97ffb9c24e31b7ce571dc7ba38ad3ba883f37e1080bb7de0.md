Welcome to the online tour of UBC Mechanical Engineering\!

Exploring these pages will give you a taste of life as a UBC mechanical engineering student, from social spaces to student support services, and our department’s groundbreaking research\. You’ll also learn about spaces and services within the Faculty of Applied Science that will be used by all engineering students\.

Join us for a look into life as a MECH student and explore what our department has to offer:  
   
[Student Workspaces](https://mech.ubc.ca/news-events/tours/virtual-tour/student-workspaces/) [Support Services](https://mech.ubc.ca/news-events/tours/virtual-tour/support-services/) [Social &amp; Community Spaces](https://mech.ubc.ca/news-events/tours/virtual-tour/social-community-spaces/) [Featured Labs](https://mech.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/featured-labs/)

We occasionally hold in\-person tours\. Please check our [tours page](https://mech.ubc.ca/our-department/tours/) for the latest information\.
