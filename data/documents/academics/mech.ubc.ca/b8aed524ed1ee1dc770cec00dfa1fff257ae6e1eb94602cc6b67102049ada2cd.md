## [MECH undergraduate duo wins 2nd place in Simon Cox Design Competition](https://mech.ubc.ca/2022/05/17/mech-undergraduate-duo-wins-2nd-place-in-simon-cox-design-competition/)

May 17, 2022

Undergraduate students Phoebe Cheung and Andrew Mah have won the Nancy Lear Achievement Award for their design of a Triple Cueing Module to help people living with Parkinson’s\.
