UBC Mechanical Engineering Virtual Tour

[More Student Workspaces](https://mech.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/student-workspaces/) [Virtual Tour Home](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/)

Located across from the student Machine Shop in the Fred Kaiser Building, this facility is a multipurpose “wet” lab space for undergraduate MECH students\. This room contains equipment used for undergraduate engineering labs, including a national gas engine, vehicle chassis dynamometer, and engine dynamometer\. In addition, the open lab benches provide students with ample space to dismantle and rewire projects, or draw up a new solution for their course projects\.

The large layout of this lab space also makes it a versatile space to accommodate the needs of our undergraduate engineering courses\. During the winter term, the lab opened is up to [MECH 2](https://mech.ubc.ca/undergraduate/mech-2/) students for designing and prototyping their projects for the second year design course\. It is also rearranged into a competition space for the last phase of their design project, where student teams test their prototypes and compete for the best score\.
