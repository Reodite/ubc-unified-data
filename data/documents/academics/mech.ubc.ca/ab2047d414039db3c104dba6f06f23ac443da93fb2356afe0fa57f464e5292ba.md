UBC Mechanical Engineering Virtual Tour

[More Support Services](https://mech.ubc.ca/news-events/tours/virtual-tour/support-services/) [Virtual Tour Home](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/)

The Engineering Co\-op Office is located on the first floor of the Pulp and Paper Centre at UBC\. Mechanical Engineering students have the option of completing co\-op work terms that are relevant, technical, and paid as part of their degree\. At the Engineering Co\-op Office, students will receive dedicated support from Co\-op Coordinators and staff to ensure that they find success in the work force\.

Their office includes multiple interview and conference rooms that employers reserve to conduct on\-campus interviews\.

Once accepted into co\-op, students looking for support in interview preparation, or resume and cover letter review, are always welcome to drop in to the reception, or book an appointment with a Co\-op Coordinator\.

[Image](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/01/ErinRose-Pulp-and-Paper-1.jpg)

For access to information about Engineering Co\-op, recruitment dates, a list of Co\-op Coordinators and other resources, please visit [UBC Engineering Co\-op](https://coop.engineering.ubc.ca/)\.
