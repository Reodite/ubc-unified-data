UBC Mechanical Engineering is part of a vibrant, bustling faculty, located at UBC’s beautiful Point Grey Campus\. Our tight\-knit community, engaged students can be found in all aspects of campus life, from design teams to varsity teams, from student societies to research labs\. We value giving back\. Our students often inspire others, and the department is committed to hosting outreach activities inspiring youth to pursue engineering\.
