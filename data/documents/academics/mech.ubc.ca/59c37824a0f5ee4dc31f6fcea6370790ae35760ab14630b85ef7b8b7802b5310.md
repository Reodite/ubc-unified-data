Go farther with graduate studies at UBC Mechanical Engineering\.

UBC Mechanical Engineering offers MASc, PhD, MEng and MEL programs for people wishing to deepen their expertise with a graduate degree\. With both research and professional programs, and eight research groups investigating a range of areas, graduate studies at MECH let you further your goals by enhancing your knowledge and skills\.
