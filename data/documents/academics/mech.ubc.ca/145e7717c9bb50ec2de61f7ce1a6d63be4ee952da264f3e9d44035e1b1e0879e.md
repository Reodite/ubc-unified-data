Mechanical Engineering researchers have been awarded over $1 million in new funding from the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada \(NSERC\), which is funding five projects through their [Discovery Grants](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/professors-professeurs/grants-subs/dgigp-psigp_eng.asp), including one [Discovery Launch Supplement](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/professors-professeurs/grants-subs/DLS-STVLD_eng.asp), as well as critical research equipment through the [Research Tools and Instruments](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Professors-Professeurs/RTII-OIRI/RTI-OIR_eng.asp) grant\.

## NSERC Discovery Grants

The NSERC Discovery Grant program fosters Canadian research innovation by funding long\-term research projects\. The additional Discovery Launch Supplement is awarded to early career researchers embarking on their initial Discovery Grant\.

**Stochastic approaches to design of structures and materials in Additive Manufacture \(StochAM\)  
**Principal Investigator: [Dr\. Adam Clare](https://mech.ubc.ca/adam-clare/)

**Enhancing the Concepts of Machine Intelligence in the Context of Mechanical Dynamic Systems  
**Principal Investigator: [Dr\. Clarence de Silva](https://mech.ubc.ca/clarence-de-silva/)

**Sustainable and Resilient Smart Large\-Scale Infrastructure through Game\-Theoretic Predictive Control  
**Principal Investigator: [Dr\. Dominic Liao\-McPherson](https://mech.ubc.ca/dominic-liao-mcpherson/)  \|  Supported by the Discovery Launch Supplement

**Advanced Control of Floating Offshore Wind Farms  
**Principal Investigator: [Dr\. Ryozo Nagamune](https://mech.ubc.ca/ryozo-nagamune/)

**Mechanics of materials across time, length, and composition scales  
**Principal Investigator: [Dr\. Mauricio Ponga de la Torre](https://mech.ubc.ca/mauricio-ponga/)

## NSERC Research Tools and Instruments

Supporting innovation by contributing to cutting\-edge facilities, the Research tools and Instruments grant enables researchers to purchase key equipment for their work\.

**[Dr\. Dana Grecov](https://mech.ubc.ca/dana-grecov/), Industrial and Biological Multiphysics Laboratory
