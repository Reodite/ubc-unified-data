The *Ada* team and industry supporters and mentors

On Monday, March 5, the Department of Mechanical Engineering and [Sailbot](https://ubcsailbot.org/) student design team held a private event for team members \(current and alumni\), industry supporters and mentors, and faculty members to celebrate the return of *Ada*, the autonomous sailboat\.

After missing for months at sea, Ada was [found by a research vessel](https://mech.ubc.ca/2017/12/05/after-more-than-a-year-lost-at-sea-ada-has-been-found/) off the coast of Florida in December 2017\. Thanks to the hard work of the Sailbot team and the Mechanical Engineering department, *Ada* returned to UBC in mid February, 2018\.

At the event, the Sailbot team shared their initial investigation into Ada’s components and the factors that led to her disappearance, as well as the design improvements they plan on making as a result\. They announced their ambitious goal for their next [autonomous sailboat](https://www.ubcsailbot.org/) \(named *Raye* after [Raye Montague](http://abcnews.go.com/GMA/video/meet-woman-broke-barriers-us-navy-45602319)\): to sail in the Victoria\-Maui yacht race\. The team is currently looking for [support](https://www.ubcsailbot.org/) in building their next boat, and hope to one day build one that will sail around the world\.

*Ada*‘s story has captured the attention of many audiences, and was featured on many news sources: [CTV](https://www.ctvnews.ca/sci-tech/self-driving-sailbot-returns-home-to-vancouver-after-being-lost-at-sea-1.3828846), CBC \([here](http://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/ada-the-sailbot-in-vancouver-1.4562491) and [here](http://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/newfoundland-labrador/sailbot-returns-home-after-lost-at-sea-1.4562482)\), and in the [Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/news/british-columbia/after-more-than-a-year-lost-at-sea-self-piloting-sailbot-returns-home/article38216842/), [National Post](http://nationalpost.com/pmn/news-pmn/canada-news-pmn/self-driving-sailbot-returns-home-to-vancouver-after-being-lost-at-sea)and [Castanet](https://www.castanet.net/edition/news-story-220295-3-.htm#220295) \(also appeared in:  Times Colonist, Metro News and  Chek\)\. Other stories appeared on [Global](https://globalnews.ca/news/4064758/ubc-sailbot-ada-home-vancouver/), [Breakfast TV](https://ubc.us2.list-manage.com/track/click?u=41d2f5a5ac495d104c6619f5f&amp;id=0b76e89ea4&amp;e=3736cd994f), Black Press \([here](https://www.coastmountainnews.com/trending-now/photos-b-c-research-students-look-to-upgrade-first-sailbot-at-sea/)\), and the [Ubyssey](https://www.ubyssey.ca/science/ada-homecoming/)\.
