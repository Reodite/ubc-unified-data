##### Selected Awards

-   Engineers Canada Medal for Distinction in Engineering Education, 2023\.
-   Margaret Fulton Award, The University of British Columbia, 2017\.

### Research Interests

-   Teaching innovation
-   Team dynamics
-   Student well\-being
-   Aerodynamics of sport

### Teaching Activities

-   APSC 100 &amp; 101 – Introduction to Engineering I and II
-   APSC 366 – The Art of the Possible: Engineering for Non\-Engineers
-   MECH 328 – Mechanical Engineering Design Project

### Educational Leadership

-   Chair of First Year Engineering
-   Board of Directors and Past President, Canadian Engineering Education Association
-   Chair of the Institute for Engineering Teaching
-   Engineering Graduate Attributes Development Project
