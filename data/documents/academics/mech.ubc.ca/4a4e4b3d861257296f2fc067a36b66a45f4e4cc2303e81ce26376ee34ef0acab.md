August 26, 2015

UBC MECH students have contributed to the design of an expandable vehicle that won the PACE Reconfigurable Shared Use Mobility System \(RSMS\) global competition\. The futuristic vehicle, which is designed for use in ride\-sharing services like Zipcar, took top prizes for market/client needs, interior design, product engineering, and manufacturability\.
