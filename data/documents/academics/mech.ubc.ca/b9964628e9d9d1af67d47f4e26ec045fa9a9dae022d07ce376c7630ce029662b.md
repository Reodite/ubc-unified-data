## [Naval Architecture &amp; Marine Engineering program wins Peter Larkin Award](https://mech.ubc.ca/2016/04/06/naval-architecture-marine-engineering-program-wins-peter-larkin-award/)

April 6, 2016

UBC’s Master of Engineering in Naval Architecture &amp; Marine Engineering program has received the 2015/16 Peter Larkin Award\. The award is granted by the VP Students Office to a graduate program or department that has contributed to student development, in honour of Peter Larkin’s commitment and passion for teaching and graduate education\.
