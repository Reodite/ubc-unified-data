## Self\-Guided Virtual Tour

We are unable to offer in\-person tours at this time, but please check out our [self\-guided virtual tour](https://mech.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/) to learn about our Mechanical Engineering student workspaces, support services, community spaces, and featured labs\!

[Take the MECH Virtual Tour\!](https://mech.ubc.ca/news-events/tours/virtual-tour/)

### Campus Tours

If you’re a prospective student or high school group visiting Vancouver campus to see everything the University of British Columbia has to offer, you can sign up for an in\-person campus tour, featuring a range of student amenities\.

[Find a UBC Campus Tour](https://account.you.ubc.ca/s/events?campus-tours&amp;_gl=1) [Arrange a UBC Campus Tour](https://you.ubc.ca/tours-events/campus-tours)

### Undergraduate Learning Spaces Tour

Are you a First\-year Engineering or Science student curious about the UBC Mechanical Engineering program? Join us for a quick 30\-minute introduction to several of our key undergraduate learning and technical spaces, including our undergraduate lab, MakerSpace and Machine Shop\!

**Upcoming tours:** *Unfortunately, we are not offering Learning Spaces tours at this time\.*
