The following are some of the social and community spaces that students can use for studying, collaborating with peers, and relaxing\.

[https://mech\.ubc\.ca/virtual\-tour/socialcommunity\-spaces/club\-mech/](https://mech.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/socialcommunity-spaces/club-mech/)[UBC Engineering club room](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/05/IMG_8490-Crop.jpg)

### [Club MECH](https://mech.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/socialcommunity-spaces/club-mech/)

Club Mech is our undergraduate student group that organizes social and professional development events, and represents student issues\. It’s also a room where undergraduates can relax, study and socialize\.

[https://mech\.ubc\.ca/virtual\-tour/socialcommunity\-spaces/wayne\-and\-william\-white\-engineering\-design\-centre\-edc/](https://mech.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/socialcommunity-spaces/wayne-and-william-white-engineering-design-centre-edc/)[UBC Engineering Design Centre](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/05/IMG_1132-2.jpg)

### [Engineering Design Centre](https://mech.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/socialcommunity-spaces/wayne-and-william-white-engineering-design-centre-edc/)

A hub for all engineering students, the EDC is home to design team spaces, workshop facilities, meeting rooms, classrooms and faculty offices\.

[https://mech\.ubc\.ca/virtual\-tour/socialcommunity\-spaces/engineering\-student\-centre\-esc/](https://mech.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/socialcommunity-spaces/engineering-student-centre-esc/)[Engineering Student Centre](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/10/ESC_front.jpg)

### [Engineering Student Centre](https://mech.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/socialcommunity-spaces/engineering-student-centre-esc/)

Home to the Engineering Undergraduate Society, the ESC is a student\-run building with study spaces, a games room, eatery, couches, and more\.
