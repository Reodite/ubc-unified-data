[2024 recipients of the UBC Mechanical Engineering Student Leadership Awards](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/Front-Page-Slider-Celebrating-MECH-Student-Leaders.png)

Each year, the Mechanical Engineering Student Leadership Awards celebrate students in our undergraduate and graduate programs who go above and beyond to enhance the student experience for their peers outside of the classroom\. These awards recognize the important contributions leaders in our extracurricular groups make in fostering a mechanical engineering community that expands our students’ abilities, connections, and university experiences\.

### Celebrating our 2023\-2024 Student Leaders

The Department is proud to present our Student Leadership Awards for the 2023 academic year to the following students who have shown exceptional dedication to serving their student design teams, clubs and the broader engineering community:

-   Luke Gallant, President of the Mechanical Engineering Undergraduate Club
-   Alyona Glazyrina, Captain of Mars Colony
-   Edwin Mooney, Captain of Formula Electric
-   Kyle Swoboda, Captain of Baja
-   Jessica Wolf, Past President of the Mechanical Engineering Graduate Association
-   Yichen Zhou, Captain of Thunderbots

Our student leaders were presented with their awards by Associate Head of Teaching, Dr\. Antony Hodgson at our Leadership Awards Lunch on April 10\[th\] 2024\. Congratulations to you all, and thank you for your tireless contributions to the student community at UBC Mechanical Engineering\!
