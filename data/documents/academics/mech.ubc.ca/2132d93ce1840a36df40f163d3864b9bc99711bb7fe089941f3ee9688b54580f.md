Atefeh Einafshar, a Mechanical Engineering PhD candidate, won the Best Poster Award at UBC Chemical &amp; Biological Engineering’s *Research Day 2014* for her work on *Multi\-Satellite Failure Evasion through Colored Petri Net Reconfiguration Modeling*\. She was presented her award by Prof\. Michael Hoffmann \(Caltech\), and Dr\. Roger Gaudreault \(Corporate Director Scientific Development of Cascades\)\.

Congratulations Atefeh\!
