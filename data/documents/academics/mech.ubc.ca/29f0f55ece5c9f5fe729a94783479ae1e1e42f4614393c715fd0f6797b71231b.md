## [UBC Heavy Lift team takes third in SAE Aero Design West Regular Class](https://mech.ubc.ca/2009/06/05/ubc-heavy-lift-team-takes-third-in-sae-aero-design-west-regular-class/)

June 5, 2009

The UBC Heavy Lift team placed third overall \(Regular Class\) in the recent SAE Aero Design West international competition, held March 6\-8th in Los Angeles, California\. The team also took second in the oral presentation\. The team’s 8lb plane successfully completed the empty weight bonus flight and scored a flight lifting 20\.3lbs before crashing spectacularly \[…\]
