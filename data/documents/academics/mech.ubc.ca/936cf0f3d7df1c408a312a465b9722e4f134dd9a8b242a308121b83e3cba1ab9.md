### **Mech 220 fee payment**

This fee is used to cover the cost of your student accident insurance, your 220 course notes package, as well as a fraction of the cost of your Maglev materials and your printing costs in the PACE lab\.

If you experience any issues or have questions about the payment process, or you cannot use a credit card or INTERAC to pay the fee, please contact Finance Manager Rinna Gotay at [rinna\.gotay@ubc\.ca](mailto:rinna.gotay@ubc.ca) or \(604\) 822\-4163\.

*Please retain a copy of the transaction for your records\. This fee is non\-refundable, as specialized supplies are purchased before the beginning of term\. If you have questions about this, please contact [students@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:students@mech.ubc.ca)\. This charge will appear on your credit card or bank statement as “UBC Vancouver BC\.”*
