UBC Mechanical Engineering Virtual Tour

[More Support Services](https://mech.ubc.ca/news-events/tours/virtual-tour/support-services/) [Virtual Tour Home](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/)

Located in the Civil and Mechanical Engineering Building \(CEME\) room 2054 within the [MECH Administrative Office](https://mech.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/support-services/mech-administrative-office/), our Student Services Team acts as Mechanical Engineering students’ main point of contact throughout their studies\.

From undergraduate and graduate advising, to awards and admissions, to event coordination, our office is here to ensure students are supported throughout their degree\. We are available from 8:00am – 4:00pm, Monday to Friday, feel free to get in touch with us at [students@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:students@mech.ubc.ca) with any questions or concerns you may have\. We welcome students to drop\-by our office for any quick questions or advice, however, we will continue to be conducting any longer academic advising and course planning appointments virtually via Zoom or phone\. You can make an advising appointment with one of our advisors via the [APSC PD Portal](https://pdportal.apsc.ubc.ca/students/student-login.htm)\.

A full list of our staff and services can also be found [here](https://mech.ubc.ca/our-department/faculty-staff-directory/)\.

#### Who do I talk to about advising?

For MECH undergraduate or graduate advising appointments, please book a time online through [Mechanical Engineering Advising Bookings](https://pdportal.apsc.ubc.ca/students/student-login.htm)\.

For advising for first year engineering students, please visit [Engineering Academic Services](https://academicservices.engineering.ubc.ca/academic-advising/contact-us/)\.

Information for prospective undergraduate students can be found [here](http://students.engineering.ubc.ca/prospective/)\. Prospective graduate students can find information on our [website](https://mech.ubc.ca/graduate/prospective/)\.
