The Department of Mechanical Engineering invites you to a special networking event on **Wednesday, November 8, 2017**\. The evening will feature a private tour of [Seaspan Vancouver Shipyards](https://www.seaspan.com/vancouver-shipyards) \(hosted by a fellow alumnus\!\), followed by drinks, appetizers, and optional brewery tours at the nearby [Hearthstone Brewery](http://www.hearthstonebrewery.ca/)\.

Note that there is limited space on the shipyard tour, and participants will be required to bring steel\-toed boots and government\-issued photo ID\. If you are not able to attend the tour, we encourage you to still sign up for the social, where you’ll have the opportunity to mingle with students, faculty, and fellow alumni, while enjoying some of BC’s finest craft beer\!

Please RSVP for one or both of the evening’s festivities below\.

**Update: The Seaspan tour is now sold out\. You may still register for the tour below to be added to the wait list\. We will contact you nearer to the date to let you know if there have been cancellations\. And you can of course still sign up for the social\!**
