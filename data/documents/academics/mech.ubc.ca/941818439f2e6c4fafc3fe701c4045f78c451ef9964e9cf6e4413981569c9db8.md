To support UBC’s social distancing protocols, many Mechanical Engineering employees are working remotely\. While our Administrative and Student Services Office spaces are closed, both offices are fully available online\. Our Machine Shop and Electronics Shop are also providing online services\.  Please email us with your query\!

General contacts are provided below, and for more detailed contact information, please visit our general [Contact Us](https://mech.ubc.ca/contact-us/) page or [Faculty and Staff Directory](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/our-department/faculty-staff-directory/)\.

### Machine Shop

The Machine Shop is currently closed, but is accepting jobs to queue for when we reopen\.  To access these services, please use the [online submission form](http://technicalservices.mech.ubc.ca/machine-shop/support-machining-services/job-submission-form/)\.

Please email **Markus Fengler** at [mfengler@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:mfengler@mech.ubc.ca) with any Machine Shop enquiries\.

### Facilities and Safety

Department buildings are currently closed to students, faculty, and staff, except for those with specific approvals\.  For information about building access, please email [access@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:access@mech.ubc.ca)\. For all other questions about facilities or safety, please email [facilities@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:facilities@mech.ubc.ca), and Jen Pelletier or Monica Clarkson would be happy to assist you\.
