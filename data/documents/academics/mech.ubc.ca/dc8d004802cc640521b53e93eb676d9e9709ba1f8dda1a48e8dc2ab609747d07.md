Andrea Palmer, one of our fourth\-year Mechanical Engineering students, won the Dillon Consulting Ltd\. Undergraduate Women in Engineering Scholarship for the 2014/15 school year\. Congratulations, Andrea\!

“Palmer has been team captain as well as the technical lead of the autonomous soccer\-playing robot team, the UBC Thunderbots\. This past year, she served as President of the Engineering Undergraduate Society and has been involved with Engineers without Borders as a Fair Trade Liaison\. In the greater UBC community she has acted as a MUG leader and hosted an Imagine Day Meet the Dean session, all designed to encourage new students at UBC\. Palmer is also an ambassador for women in engineering\. She is a student co\-ordinator for the Women in Engineering program, helping to mentor and support female engineers throughout their time at UBC\.”\[1\]
