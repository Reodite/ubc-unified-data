## **UBC Mechanical Engineering Alumni Networking Night**

The Department of Mechanical Engineering invites you to a special networking event on Wednesday, November 8\[th\] at Hearthstone Brewery in North Vancouver\. Come mingle with Mech alumni, faculty, and students while enjoying appetizers and local craft beer\! Hearthstone will also be providing brewery tours for those interested\!

**  
Wednesday, November 8, 2017**  
6:30\-9:30 pm

[Hearthstone Brewery](http://www.hearthstonebrewery.ca/)  
1015 Marine Drive  
North Vancouver
