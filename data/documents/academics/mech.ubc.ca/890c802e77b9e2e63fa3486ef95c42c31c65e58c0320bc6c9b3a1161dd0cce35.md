# Navigating Your Program

Whether you’ve just been admitted to UBC or you’ve been here for a while, the information in the pages below will help you navigate your program\.

Browse the tabs below for helpful information on getting started in your program, exploring opportunities to get involved in student groups or experiences abroad, and how to stay on top of your program requirements and funding opportunities\.

## [Welcome Guide for New Graduate Students](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/graduate/current/welcome/)

Welcome to UBC Mechanical Engineering\! You’ve just been admitted to one of our graduate programs–congratulations\! Check out our Welcome Guide to find out what comes next, from registering for courses to getting health insurance and more\.

This guide goes over everything you should do to prepare yourself for your arrival at UBC\. If you have any questions please contact the [Student Services Office](https://mech.ubc.ca/contact-us/)\.
