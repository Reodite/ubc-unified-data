Dr\. Srikantha Phani, a Mechanical Engineering Tier II Canadian Research Chair \(CRC\), is featured in this [UBC Story](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc_stories/)\. Dr\. Phani, an exceptional emerging mind, holds a five\-year, renewable once, $100,000 per annum grant for his work on the Dynamics of Lattice Material and Devices\.

UBC Mechanical Engineering has four professors who hold CRC Research Chairs\.
