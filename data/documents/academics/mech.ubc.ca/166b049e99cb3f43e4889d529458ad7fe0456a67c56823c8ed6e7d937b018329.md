Please join the Department of Mechanical Engineering in celebrating the life of our late friend and colleague, Alan Steeves\.  Alan was not only an exceptional IT person, he was also a wonderful artist, a conservationist, and a master chef\. Alan was a humble man, a kind man, and a man of principles\.

Alan will live on in our thoughts and through the enormous impact he has had in Mechanical Engineering and the broader community\.  One of the ways we will remember Alan is through photographs\.  Please submit any digital or digitized photographs you wish to share to [undergrad@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:undergrad@mech.ubc.ca) by December 1, 2014\.

Please note that, due to space limitations, attendance is limited to respondents\.
