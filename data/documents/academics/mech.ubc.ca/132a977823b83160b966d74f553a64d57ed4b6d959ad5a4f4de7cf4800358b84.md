## **Google PhD Fellowship**

The Google PhD Fellowship Program was created to recognize outstanding PhD graduate students doing exceptional work in computer science and related fields\. The fellowship provides full tuition and fees \(enrollment fees, health insurance, books\) plus a stipend to be used for living expenses, travel and personal equipment for up to three years\. Recipients are also matched with a Google Research Mentor\.

-   **Eligibility**: Full\-time UBC graduate students pursuing a PhD, who will have completed graduate coursework by the Fall of the award year \(Fall 2025\), when the Fellowship begins\. Complete eligibility requirements, including eligible research areas, are indicated in the webpage linked below\.
-   **Application deadline:** Monday, April 7, 2025 at 4:00 pm PT\.

For more information, including detailed application procedures, please see: [https://www\.grad\.ubc\.ca/awards/google\-phd\-fellowship](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/google-phd-fellowship)\.
