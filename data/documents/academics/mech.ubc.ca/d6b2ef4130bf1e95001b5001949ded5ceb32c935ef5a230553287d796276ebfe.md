## Celebrating the Class of 2021\!

#### Virtual Reception, Awards &amp; Guestbook

Congratulations to the class of 2021\! We’re excited for you to celebrate your university achievements, and to embark on your next chapter and the bright future ahead\.

If you couldn’t make the Virtual Graduation Reception and Awards Ceremony on June 1st you can watch the recording here, featuring words from Dean of Applied Science Dr\. James Olson and Mechanical Engineering Department Head Dr\. Steve Feng, as well as a toast to the graduating class by Club Mech President Émilie Boras\.

[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/5e005SYeQ0c?rel=0)

Read below to learn about this year’s Mechanical Engineering award recipients\. We also have a [guestbook](https://mech.ubc.ca/2021-june-graduation/#guestbook) below, and we welcome everyone to leave a message of congratulations for our graduates\.

---

### Award Winners

#### Major Awards

##### Letson Prizes

The Letson Prizes are given to the head of the graduating class for each program Option\. Our 2021 winners are:

-   **General Mechanical Engineering:** Janelle Lawson
-   **Biomedical:** Yizhe Jiang
-   **Mechatronics:** Connor Schellenberg
-   **Thermofluids**: Gary Guan

##### **Canadian Society for Mechanical Engineering Gold Medal**

The CSME Gold Medal is awarded to a student for overall outstanding academic achievement in Mechanical Engineering\.

**2021 recipient:** Sebasatiaan Mainardis

##### Dean’s Achievement Award in Engineering

This award is given to one graduating student in each Department who shows great promise\.

**2021 recipient:** Janelle Lawson

##### **Dale CHERCHAS Memorial Prize**

This award is endowed in memory of Dr\. Dale Cherchas, a leading researcher in Mechanical Engineering, and recognizes the top student in the area of Controls &amp; Dynamics\.

**2021 recipient:** Gary Guan

##### **Philipp Krebs Award for Perseverance**

This award celebrates undergraduate students who have demonstrated great perseverance and determination throughout their degree

**2021 recipient:** Liz Vasilkovs

#### Design Awards

##### **Capstone Design Award**

This award is given to the best performing team in our 4\[th\] year capstone design course\. This year, the instructors of MECH 45X have chosen two teams to receive this award – both the “COVID PPE for those with limited ability” &amp; the “Surgical Tool Tracking” teams exemplified stellar performance in innovation and design practice\.

This award recognizes the top undergraduate student who has demonstrated exceptional leadership skills and has made outstanding contributions to the Department and Faculty, specifically in the realms of student affairs, design competition teams, professional association student chapters, and other related activities\.
