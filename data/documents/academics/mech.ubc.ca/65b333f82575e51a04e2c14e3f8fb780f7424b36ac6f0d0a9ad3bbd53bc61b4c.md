We have a number of committees to serve Mechanical Engineering faculty, staff, and students\. View the pages below to learn more about what is happening here in the department\.

[Equity, Diversity, Inclusion &amp; Indigeneity](https://mech.ubc.ca/our-department/committees/edi-i/)

The EDI\.I Committee has a mandate to advise the department on ways to create a welcoming, inclusive community, and on identifying and incorporating best practices for equity and diversity within our policies, procedures, and practices\.

[Industry Advisory Committee](https://mech.ubc.ca/our-department/committees/industry-advisory-committee/)

The Mechanical Engineering Industry Advisory Committee \(MEAC\) advises the Department Head on matters relating to the Department’s strategic initiatives and future direction\.
