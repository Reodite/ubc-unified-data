UBC Mechanical Engineering Virtual Tour

[More Support Services](https://mech.ubc.ca/news-events/tours/virtual-tour/support-services/) [Virtual Tour Home](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/)

The Engineering Academic Services Office supports prospective students, current students, and new students seeking academic advice that is not specific to Mechanical Engineering\. Their team is located centrally in the Fred Kaiser building \(KAIS\) room 1100 and can help with:

-   Academic advising
-   Appeals
-   Complementary Studies
-   Registration
-   Transfer Credits
-   English/communication requirements
-   2nd year placement

Students are welcome to book an appointment to get access to the right resources\. For contact information and to book an appointment, please visit [UBC Engineering Academic Services](https://academicservices.engineering.ubc.ca/academic-advising/contact-us/)\.
