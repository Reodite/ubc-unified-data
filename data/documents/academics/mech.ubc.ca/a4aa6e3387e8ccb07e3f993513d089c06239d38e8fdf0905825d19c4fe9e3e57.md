### Research Interests

-   Imaging and sensing
-   Robotics
-   Photonics
-   Ultrasound
-   Machine learning
-   Marine and biomedical applications

### Current Research Work

Dr\. Desjardins’ interdisciplinary research is focused on the development of imaging and sensing modalities and autonomous robotics, with marine and biomedical applications\. This program involves close collaboration with industry partners\.
