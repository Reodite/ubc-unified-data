### Research Interests

-   Computational mechanics
-   Finite element analysis
-   Topology optimization
-   Scientific machine learning
-   Data\-driven computing and model reduction
-   Marine structures and ship production

### Current Research Work:

-   Structural analysis with deep learning and physics\-augmented neural networks
-   Machine learning\-assisted topology optimization of large\-scale structures
-   Structural vibrations and underwater noise
-   Failure limit states of lightweight structures
-   Low\-heat input welding techniques and welding simulations
-   Analysis, design and production of marine structures
-   Effects of marine environment on ships

### Awards &amp; Recognitions:
