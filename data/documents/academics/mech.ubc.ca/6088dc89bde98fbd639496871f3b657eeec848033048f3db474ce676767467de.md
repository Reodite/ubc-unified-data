### [NAME Design Studio](https://mech.ubc.ca/virtual-tour/student-workspaces/name-design-studio/)

The Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering \(NAME\) Design Studio provides our NAME MEng and MEL graduate students with a dedicated space for lectures, computer access, lockers and study lounge\.

Our reconfigurable undergraduate lab is a unique space for hands\-on design work, technical labs, student design competitions, and a variety of activities are held\.
