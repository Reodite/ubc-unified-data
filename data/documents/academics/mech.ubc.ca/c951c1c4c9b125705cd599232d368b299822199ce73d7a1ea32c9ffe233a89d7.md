## [MECH TAs win Killam Graduate Teaching Assistant Awards](https://mech.ubc.ca/2020/04/20/mech-tas-win-killam-gta-awards/)

April 20, 2020

Two Mechanical Engineering Teaching Assistants, Mahsa Khalili and Masoud Malakoutian, have been recognized with a Killam Graduate Teaching Assistant Award for the 2019/2020 year\. This award recognizes their outstanding work and contributions to undergraduate learning at UBC\.  Malakoutian is a Mechanical Engineering PhD student, while Khalili is pursuing her doctorate in Biomedical Engineering\.
