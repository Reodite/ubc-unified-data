## [Event: Hamnavaz Ensemble Concert March 4, 2012](https://mech.ubc.ca/2012/03/03/event-hamnavaz-ensemble-concert-march-4-2012/)

The Hamnavaz Ensemble presents it next major concert on campus this Sunday at 5pm in UBC’s Fredericwood Theater\.
