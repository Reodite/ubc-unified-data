## UBC Mechanical Engineering Alumni Networking Night

[Image](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/02/Alumni-Event-Ad-2018-v2-1.png)

The Department of Mechanical Engineering invites you to a special networking event on **Wednesday, March 21, 2018**\. The evening will feature a private tour of the [UBC Campus Energy Centre](http://energy.ubc.ca/projects/district-energy/campus-energy-centre), followed by drinks and appetizers at the nearby [Biercraft Wesbrook](http://biercraft.com/wesbrook-at-ubc/#)\.

Note that there is limited space on the Campus Energy Centre tour\. If you are not able to attend the tour, we encourage you to still sign up for the social, where you’ll have the opportunity to mingle with students, faculty, and fellow alumni, while enjoying some of BC’s finest craft beer\!

Please RSVP for one or both of the evening’s festivities below\.

**Wednesday, March 21, 2018**

TOUR  
6:00 – 7:00 pm  
UBC Campus Energy Centre  
6130 Agronomy Rd, Vancouver  
\(Parking available in the [Health Sciences Parkade](https://parking.ubc.ca/health-sciences-parkade) or the [Thunderbird Parkade](https://parking.ubc.ca/thunderbird-parkade)\)

SOCIAL  
6:30 – 9:30 pm  
[Biercraft Wesbrook](http://biercraft.com/wesbrook-at-ubc/)  
3340 Shrum Lane  
Vancouver  
\(Some street parking available, or in the parking lot off [Berton Ave\.](http://www.discoverwesbrook.com/shops_services#directory-map)\)

**Please RSVP by filling in your information below by March 14\.**

Questions? Contact communications@mech\.ubc\.ca
