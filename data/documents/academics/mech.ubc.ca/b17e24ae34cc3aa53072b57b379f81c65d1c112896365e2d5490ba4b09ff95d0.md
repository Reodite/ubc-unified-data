#### **Professor Emeritus**

Higher Doctorate Sc\.D\. \(Cambridge\); Ph\.D\. \(MIT\); Ph\.D\. \(Cambridge\); D\.Eng\. \(Hon, Waterloo\); D\.Sc\. \(Hon, OUSL\); B\.Sc\. Eng\. \(Hon 1, Ceylon\); M\.A\.Sc\. \(Toronto\), Fellow of: IEEE, ASME, ASI, CAE, &amp; Royal Society of Canada; Distinguished Visiting Fellow of Royal Academy of Engineering; Senior Canada Research Chair in Mechatronics and Industrial Automation

### Research Interests

-   Multi\-robot Systems and Applications including Homecare
-   Intelligent Systems and Automation
-   Mechatronics; Integrated and Automated Design
-   Control Sensors, Actuators and Instrumentation
-   Automated Machine Monitoring, Diagnosis, and Repair
-   Telemedicine and Health Monitoring
-   Environmental Monitoring

### Current Research

-   Cooperative mobile robots for human rescue
-   Haptic teleoperation and autonomous mobile robots in homecare applications
-   Telemedicine and health monitoring
-   Intelligent sensor fusion for machine health monitoring and supervisory control
-   Instrumentation of processes for monitoring and control \(sensors, actuators, etc\.\)
-   Formal techniques for mechatronic system design and evaluation \(Mechatronic Design Quotient or MDQ; Automated design evolution, etc\.\)
-   Automated spatiotemporal monitoring of the quality of water sources
-   Learning and AI in machine monitoring and diagnosis
-   Diagnosis and automated repair of pipelines
