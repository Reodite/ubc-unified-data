### Research Interests

The [**PRIME** \(**P**erceptive **R**easoning and **I**ntelligent **M**achin**E**ry\) Robotics Lab](https://chaoliu.tech/group/) at UBC conducts cutting\-edge robotics research at the intersection of robotics, perception, and artificial intelligence\. Our vision is to develop innovative robotic systems that can interact with and adapt to their environment in a seamless and intelligent manner\. We aim to push the boundaries of what is possible in robotics, enabling robots to operate effectively in complex and dynamic environments\.

### Current Research Work

-   Robotic manipulation and grasping
-   Swarm and modular robotics
-   Motion planning and control
-   Multimodal perception and reasoning
