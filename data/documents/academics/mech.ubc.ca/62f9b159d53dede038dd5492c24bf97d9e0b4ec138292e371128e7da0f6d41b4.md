[Ming Pao Canada](http://ubc.us2.list-manage.com/track/click?u=41d2f5a5ac495d104c6619f5f&amp;id=883462be00&amp;e=53cca64553)[Ma headshot](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/Headshot-Ma-Hongshen-Kai-Jacobsen.png) reported on research done by Mechanical Engineering professor Dr\. Hongshen Ma that may soon impact cancer detection and treatment\. Dr\. Ma developed a new method for isolating cancer cells that have escaped from a tumor\. A special device squeezes cells from a blood sample through funnels, which separate the cancer cells and blood cells based on differences in their size and softness\.

[Dr\. Hongshen Ma](https://mech.ubc.ca/hongshen-ma/) specializes in microfluidics, the flow of liquids through channels smaller than a human hair\.
