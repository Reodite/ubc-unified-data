## Studying at UBC and Mechanical Engineering

[https://mech\.ubc\.ca/research/](https://mech.ubc.ca/research/)[grad student works on digital model of a skeleton](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/12/DSC_5394.jpg)

### [Strong Research](https://mech.ubc.ca/research/)

The department has one of the most active research programs in Canada, working to advance solutions in a range of fields, from health and sustainability to robotics and aerospace\. Our researchers collaborate with local and international partners in industry, government and community organizations\.

[Our Research](https://mech.ubc.ca/research/)

### [World Class Education](https://mech.ubc.ca/our-department/teaching-research-awards/)

UBC consistently [ranks within the top 40 universities world\-wide](https://www.ubc.ca/about/facts.html), and our professors are recognized nationally and internationally as leaders in their fields\. We are home to multiple national and industry Research Chairs, and part of a variety of Research Institutes and Centres\.

[Our Expertise](https://mech.ubc.ca/our-department/teaching-research-awards/)

UBC offers a number of scholarships and top\-ups to external awards, and our department is committed to providing Graduate Research Assistantships to accepted MASc and PhD students\. Students can apply for Teaching Assistantships as well\.

[Awards &amp; Funding](https://mech.ubc.ca/graduate/funding)

## Living in Vancouver

[UBC Vancouver Campus](https://mech2.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/12/22874524144_7924a0c606_o-e1470641669321.jpg)

### [Amazing Location](https://mech.ubc.ca/graduate/vancouver-and-ubc)

The gateway to the Pacific Rim, Vancouver, British Columbia is consistently ranked as the most liveable city in North America \(*The Economist*\)\. The city has a mild climate, with average temperatures between 0°C and 22°C–hardly the frozen expanse of snow Canada is stereotypically known for\. It’s easy to get here, too – we’re minutes away from YVR, a major international airport\.

### Great Recreation

Vancouver’s mild climate leads to amazing recreation possibilities\. Vancouver offers many bike routes, great water\-based activities like kayaking and sailing, and skiing at the local mountains or Whistler \(which is just a bus ride away\)\. Campus itself is nestled at the edge of the city between the beach and Pacific Spirit Park, a forest boasting 54 km of walking and cycling trails\.

### Multicultural Community

UBC and Vancouver offer a great mix of people from all over the world\. This leads to a very accepting, inclusive community\. A wide range of cultural and spiritual associa­tions are available if you would like to learn more about other cultures, or if you wish to stay connected to your own\. Another bonus is the amazing food from all over the globe\.

### Career Opportunities

Our degrees are recognized world\-wide, giving you great mobility in the world economy\. Are you an International student hoping for Canadian work experience? No problem\! Inter­national students are eligible for work permits during their studies and up to one year after graduation – and so are their spouses \(including common\-law partners\)\.
