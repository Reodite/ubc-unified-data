## [“My Robocup Experience”: UBC Thunderbots on their trip to Hefei, China](https://mech.ubc.ca/2016/01/05/my-robocup-experience-ubc-thunderbots-on-their-trip-to-hefei-china/)

January 5, 2016

In July 2015, the UBC Thunderbots–a Mechanical Engineering student design team–traveled to Hefei, China to compete in the Robocup International Competition\. The team is comprised of members from various disciplines and year levels, who work together to design and build autonomous soccer\-playing robots\.
