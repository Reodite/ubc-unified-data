## [UBC Engineering Design Teams Thrive in Competitions](https://mech.ubc.ca/2014/06/23/ubc-engineering-design-teams-thrive-in-competitions/)

June 23, 2014

The Mechanical Engineering Department supports several UBC Engineering Design Teams, and encourages students to participate to apply their academic knowledge to real\-world situations and expand their horizons\.
