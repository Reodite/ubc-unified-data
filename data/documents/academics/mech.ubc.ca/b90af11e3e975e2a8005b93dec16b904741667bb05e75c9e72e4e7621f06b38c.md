The Department of Mechanical Engineering invites you to a special networking event on **Wednesday, November 20, 2019**\. Join us from **3:30 – 5 PM** for a **private tour of BC Place**, featuring the retractable roof – one of the largest cable\-supported fabric roofs in the world – as well as the broadcast booth, media suite, BC Lions and Whitecaps locker rooms, and the BC Sports Hall of Fame\. After the tour, there will be a **networking social** with drinks and appetizers from **5 – 8 PM** across the street at the Red Racer Taphouse\.

If you are not able to attend the tour, we encourage you to still sign up for the social, where you’ll have the opportunity to mingle with fellow alumni and faculty\. All attendees receive a drink ticket\.

Please **RSVP for one or both** of the parts of the event below, as spaces are limited\!

-   **DATE:** Wednesday, November 20, 2019
-   **BC PLACE TOUR:** 3:30 – 5:00 pm \| [BC Sports Hall of Fame](https://bcsportshall.com/), Gate A of BC Place Stadium, on Beatty St between Robson \+ West Georgia \[[map](https://www.google.ca/maps/place/BC+Sports+Hall+Of+Fame+and+Museum/@49.277457,-123.112385,15z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0xd915a17c9dda795d!8m2!3d49.277457!4d-123.112385)\] \| *Tour Full*
-   **NETWORKING SOCIAL:** 5:00 – 8 pm \| [Red Racer Taphouse](https://www.redracertaphouse.com/), 871 Beatty St \[[map](https://www.google.ca/maps/place/Red+Racer+Taphouse/@49.2769485,-123.1175621,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x5486717d9f54b983:0xc57e315d64885d04!8m2!3d49.276945!4d-123.115368)\] \| only 50 spots available

Since there are limited spots in the BC Place Tour, attendees will need to pay a subsidized fee of $10 to encourage attendance\.

*If you have any questions, please email [communications@mech\.ubc\.ca](mailto:communications@mech.ubc.ca)\.*
