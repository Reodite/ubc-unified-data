## [Professor Hongshen Ma receives $100,000 Grand Challenges Explorations Grant for Innovative Global Health Research](https://mech.ubc.ca/2009/10/21/professor-hongshen-ma-receives-100000-grand-challenges-explorations-grant-for-innovative-global-health-research/)

October 21, 2009

Professor Hongshen Ma, from the Department of Mechanical Engineering, has received a $100,000 \(USD\) grant from the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation\. This grant is part of the Grand Challenges Explorations program to support research in Global Health\.
