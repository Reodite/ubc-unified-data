### Research Interests

-   Biomaterial manufacturing and engineering
-   Nano/microfabrication and 3D bioprinting
-   Tissue engineering and organ regeneration
-   Therapeutic strategies for cardiovascular disease
-   Biomimetic hydrogel scaffold

### Current Research Work

-   Manufacturing and characterization of functional hydrogels as bioink for 3D bioprinting of cardiac tissue\.
-   Spatiotemporal control of micro\-vessel regeneration and modeling of tissue vascularization\.
-   Tailoring cues from scaffold microenvironment to modulate function and maturation of therapeutic cells\.
