## Physio Refresh assists registrants to prepare for and succeed at the Canadian Physical Therapy Examination \(CPTE\) and transition into clinical physical therapy practice in Canada\.

[Image](https://med-fom-iepep.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/SidebarLogo.jpg)

The program is open to internationally\-trained and Canadian\-trained Physiotherapists who wish to refresh their skills or competencies, who have been out of practice and are required to sit the Canadian licensing exam for entry or re\-entry into practice, or who are new to physiotherapy practice in Canada\. Physio Refresh combines self\-directed learning with instructor\-led online review, and encompasses all components of the CPTE\. Practice examinations and virtual workshops with instructors assist participants to review core physical therapy knowledge, clinical skills, and clinical reasoning\.

[Each workshop session is led by an experienced instructor using multiple\-choice questions \(MCQs\) case studies and other valuable resources to review key elements in the respective area of practice\. The sessions cover Musculoskeletal, Neurological, and Cardiovascular\-respiratory content\.](https://med-fom-iepep.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/01/HomePageGraphics_LeadingEdgeExpertise.png)
