## We are excited to announce the launch of our **newly updated Physio Refresh Program**, now aligned with the updated [**CPTE exam**](https://alliancept.org/canadian-physiotherapy-examination/)\! 

## To better support internationally educated and Canadian\-educated physiotherapists, we are offering **a new program option**:

### Physio Refresh Program – Competency Refresh \(CoRe\)

The Physio Refresh Program – CoRe consists of self\-directed learning across five modules: **Musculoskeletal, Neurology, Cardiorespiratory, Other Systems, and Professional Practice &amp; Transition to Practice**\.

The Physio Refresh Essentials Program runs over 8 weeks in total\. 

[Learn More](https://physiorefresh.med.ubc.ca/competency-refresh-core/)
