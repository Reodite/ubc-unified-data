## General

The only requirement for registration is the payment of the registration fee\. However, we strongly recommend completing the credentialing process with the Canadian Alliance of Physiotherapy Regulators \(CAPR\) before registering\. 

Physio Refresh events are conducted ‘live’ on Zoom\.  You will be provided with Zoom links for the workshops – a strong internet connection, webcam, and microphone are required\.  Physio Refresh tutors and facilitators are highly adept at engaging in group discussions on Zoom, and the sessions are a great opportunity to ask questions, discuss study strategies, etc\. 

There are a number of options available: you can ask your peers in the program on the discussion forum, reach out to the Physio Refresh program team, or ask a tutor during the live events\. 

Physio Refresh does not offer financial assistance for paying program fees; however, past registrants have found assistance through services such as WorkBC and Skills Connect Canada\. 

Back in Motion’s [Career Paths for Skilled Immigrants](https://med-fom-iepep.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/07/BIM-CPSI-Flyer-2.pdf) offers support and financial support to eligible candidates visit [Back in Motion](http://www.backinmotion.com/employment-services/career-paths-skilled-immigrants) to learn more\. 

Health Match BC \(HMBC\), in partnership with the BC Ministry of Health and the [Canadian Alliance of Physiotherapy Regulators](https://alliancept.org/), will support eligible Internationally Educated Physiotherapists who want to work in BC by offering financial support to help pay for costs associated with credential assessment, English language proficiency testing, exam preparation, and bridging education\. Please visit the [HMBC](https://www.nursingjobsbc.ca/iept-bursary/) website to learn more\. 

There may be additional options in your province\.  

Physio Refresh registrants do not need to live in Canada and do not require a Canadian student or work Visa\.  

You can register at any time after registrations open\. As most of the program is self\-directed, register when you feel it will work best for your personal study plan\. However, make sure you register before the first event, as attendance at all program events is mandatory to be eligible for bursary reimbursement\. 

The majority of Physio Refresh is **self\-directed**, meaning registrants work through the online materials at their own pace, without a program instructor or facilitator\. There are a few scheduled events delivered via Zoom\. Participants will receive the confirmed schedule in advance so they can plan to attend accordingly\. All live sessions are scheduled in Pacific Standard Time \(PST\)\.

Each Live Tutorial Session is approximately 3\.5 hours long\. The Capstone Tutorial session is approximately 2\.5 hours long\. The Transition to Practice workshop is 3 hours long\. The Communications workshop is 1\.5 hours long\. The Baseline and Final Exams are each approximately 4\.5 hours long \(i\.e\., a half day\)\.  

Unfortunately, due to privacy policies, we are unable to record our sessions\.  

Full participation in all program components is required in order to receive a letter of completion\. This letter is necessary for those seeking reimbursement through the bursary\. 

Your access to the **online content** remains open until the national exam date – if you need to review material the night before the exam, you can still access all of the online content\. 

Contact the Physio Refresh team at [physio\.refresh@ubc\.ca](mailto:physio.refresh@ubc.ca)
