The Physio Refresh Program – CoRe consists of self\-directed learning across five modules: **Musculoskeletal, Neurology, Cardiorespiratory, Other Systems, and Professional Practice &amp; Transition to Practice**\.  

### **WHAT’S INCLUDED:** 

**Access to an Online Curriculum:** 

-   Over 50 clinical skills videos featuring key examinable components of the National Exam;  
-   23 Virtual patient Cases \(interactive multimedia presentations of complex cases\);  
-   Practice and Final exams, which include 40 MCQs and 4 Oral Stations  
-   A discussion board which provides a connection with peers in the program\.

**Virtual synchronous events:** 

-   Baseline and Final Exams 
    -   Station\-based oral exams: designed to align with the CPTE oral exam format, plus 
    -   40 MCQ exam \(Baseline\) / 100 MCQ exam \(Final\) 
-   Six live tutorial workshops   
    -   Covers Musculoskeletal, Neurological, Cardiovascular\-respiratory, Other Systems, Communication and Professional Practice areas  
    -   Developed to review key content and practice exam questions\.  
-   Two Transition to Practice workshops  
-   One Capstone Tutorial workshop 

**In\-person events *\(optional\)*:**  

-   One clinical lab workshop weekend \(held at UBC – Point Grey Campus\) 
    -   Covers Musculoskeletal, Neurological, Cardiovascular\-respiratory and Professional Practice areas
