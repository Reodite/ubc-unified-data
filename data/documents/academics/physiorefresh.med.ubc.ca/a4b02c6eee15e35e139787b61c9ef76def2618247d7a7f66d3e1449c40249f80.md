### The Physio Refresh program has been adapted to align with the delivery of the new Clinical Physiotherapy Examination \(CPTE\)\.  

Online Curriculum \+ Virtual Sessions: **$4,000\.00** 

**Optional**: Clinical Lab Weekend Workshops \(in\-person\): **$500\.00** 

-   The Clinical Lab Weekend is dependent on meeting a minimum number of participants\. Registrants will receive a full refund of the additional fee if the event is cancelled\. 

-   Fees must be paid in full at the time of registration\.  

-   Fees may be paid online by credit card \(Visa or MasterCard\)\. 

**Cancellation**  

-   There are NO refunds for participant cancellations\.
