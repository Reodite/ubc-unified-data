### Instructions:

Articulator: Vocal folds

Point of Articulation:

Manner of Articulation: \(Glottal\) Stop – A complete closure is made between the articulator and the point of articulation\. Since the glottal passage is blocked off, air pressure builds up before the glottis\. When the closure is stopped, an abrupt release occurs\.
