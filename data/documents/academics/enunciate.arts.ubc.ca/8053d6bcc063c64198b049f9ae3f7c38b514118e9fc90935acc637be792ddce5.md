    Labiodental Nasal \(Left\-hook M\)

[Go Back to Chart](https://enunciate.arts.ubc.ca/linguistics/consonants-pulmonic/ "&lt;i class=\"icon-arrow-left\"&gt;&lt;/i&gt; Go Back to Chart ")

### Instructions:

Articulator: lower lip

Point of Articulation: upper front teeth

Manner: \(Nasal\) Stop – Velum is lowered and the air goes through the nasal passage\.

Voice: voiced – Vocal folds are close and vibrating\.

Occurrence: Catalan, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Finnish, Greek, Hebrew, Italian, Kukuya \(Teke\), Macedonian, Norwegian, Polish, Romanian, Serbo\-Croatian, Slovene, Spanish, Swedish, West Frisian\.  
\(Source: [https://en\.wikipedia\.org/wiki/Labiodental\_nasal\#Occurrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labiodental_nasal#Occurrence)\)
