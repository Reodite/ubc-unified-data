    # /ɲ/
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Palatal Nasal \(Left\-tail N\)

### Instructions:

Articulator: middle or back part of the tongue

Point of Articulation: hard palate

Manner: \(Nasal\) Stop – Velum is lowered and the air goes through the nasal passage\.

Voice: voiced – Vocal folds are close and vibrating\.

Occurrence: \!Kung, Albanian, Aranda, Basque, Burmese, Catalan, Chinese \(Wu\), Czech, Dinka, Dutch, French, Galician, Greek, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Irish, Japanese, Korean, Latvian, Macedonian, Malagasy, Malay, Malayalam, North Frisian \(Mooring\), Norwegian \(Northern, Southern\), Occitan \(Northern Southern, Gascon\), Polish, Portuguese \(many dialects, Brazilian, European\), Quechua, Romanian \(Transylvanian dialects\), Scottish Gaelic, Serbo\-Croatian, Slovak, Spanish, Ukranian, Vietnamese, West Frisian, Yanyuwa, Yi, Zulu\.
