### Instructions:

Articulator: blade of the tongue

Point of Articulation: alveolar/postalveolar/palatal

Manner of Articulation: Approximant: An articulator approaches a point of articulation, but the constriction degree is not so narrow as fricatives or stops\. While the vocal folds are vibrating\.

Voice: Voiced: Vocal folds are close and vibrating\.
