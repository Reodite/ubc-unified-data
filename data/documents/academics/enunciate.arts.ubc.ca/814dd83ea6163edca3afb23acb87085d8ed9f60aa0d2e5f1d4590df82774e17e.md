    # /ɳ/
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Retroflex nasal \(Right\-tail N\)

[Go Back to Chart](https://enunciate.arts.ubc.ca/linguistics/consonants-pulmonic/ "&lt;i class=\"icon-arrow-left\"&gt;&lt;/i&gt; Go Back to Chart ")

### Instructions:

Articulator: back of the tip of the tongue

Point of Articulation: alveolar/postalveolar/palatal area

Manner: \(Nasal\) Stop – velum is lowered and the air goes through the nasal passage\.

Voice: voiced – Vocal folds are close and vibrating\.

Occurrence:  
Enindhilyagwa, Hindi, Kannada, Khanty \(Eastern and some northern dialects\), Malayalam, Marathi, Marshallese, Norwegian, Oriya, Pashto, Punjabi, Swedish, Tamil, Telugu, Vietnamese\.  
\(Source: [https://en\.wikipedia\.org/wiki/Retroflex\_nasal\#Occurrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retroflex_nasal#Occurrence)\)
