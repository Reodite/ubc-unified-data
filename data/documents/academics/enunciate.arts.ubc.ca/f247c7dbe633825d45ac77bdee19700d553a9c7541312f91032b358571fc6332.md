### Welcome to Japanese Pronunciation Page\!

Welcome to the eNunciate\! Japanese pronunciation page\! This website lets you independently practice the most challenging speech sounds in Japanese\. It will help you understand how native Japanese speakers produce those sounds by providing you with images and video of actual tongue positions and movements\. You should start with the videos in the “46 Sounds of Japanese” and the “Introduction to Phonetics” sections, and then move on to the sections for the various challenging sounds\.
