Ultrasound images help us to visualize what is going on inside our mouth when we speak\. In the **Introduction to Phonetics 1** video, you will learn how ultrasound machines work, how to interpret ultrasound images, and what part of the ultrasound images to focus on while watching those videos\. In the **Introduction to Phonetics 2**, you will learn how we actually produce sounds, as well as important terminology\.
