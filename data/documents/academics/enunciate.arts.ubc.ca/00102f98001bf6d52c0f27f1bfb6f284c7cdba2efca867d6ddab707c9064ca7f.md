    Close\-mid front rounded vowel \(Slashed O\)

### Instructions:

Height: mid \(close mid\)  
Backness: front  
Roundedness: rounded

Note: Used as Cardinal Vowel No\. 10\.

Occurrence:  
Afrikaans \(Standard\), Bavarian \(Amstetten dialect\), Chinese \(Wu\), Danish \(Standard\), Dutch \(Northeastern\), English \(Broad South African, General South African, Tyneside\), Faroese, Franco\-Provençal, French, German \(Southern Bernese, Standard\), Hungarian, Limburgish \(Most dialects, Rural Weerts\), Lombard \(Western\), Ngwe \(Mmockngie dialect\), Norwegian \(Standard Eastern\), Portuguese \(Micaelense, some European speakers\), Rotuman, Swedish
