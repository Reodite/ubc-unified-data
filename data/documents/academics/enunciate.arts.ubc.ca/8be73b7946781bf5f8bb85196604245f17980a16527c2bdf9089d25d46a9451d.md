    # /ɰ/
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Velar Approximant \(Long\-leg Turned M\)

[Go Back to Chart](https://enunciate.arts.ubc.ca/linguistics/consonants-pulmonic/ "&lt;i class=\"icon-arrow-left\"&gt;&lt;/i&gt; Go Back to Chart ")

### Instructions:

Articulator: back of the tongue \(tongue dorsum\)

Point of Articulation: velum

Manner of Articulation: Approximant: An articulator approaches a point of articulation, but the constriction degree is not so narrow as for fricatives or stops\.

Voice: voiced – Vocal folds are close and vibrating\.

Occurrence:  
Cherokee, Danish \(Old speakers\), Dutch \(Randstad, Southern Netherlandic\), French \(Belgian\), Greek \(Cypriot\), Guarani, Icelandic, Irish, Korean, Ripuarian \(Colognian\), Spanish, Swedish \(Central Standard\), Tagalog, Tiwi, Turkish\. \(Source: [https://en\.wikipedia\.org/wiki/Velar\_approximant\#Occurrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velar_approximant#Occurrence)\)
