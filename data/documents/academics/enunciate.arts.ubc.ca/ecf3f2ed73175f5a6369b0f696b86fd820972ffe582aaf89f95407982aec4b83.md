    # /ɯ/
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Close back unrounded vowel \(Turned M\)

[Go Back to Chart](https://enunciate.arts.ubc.ca/linguistics/vowels/ "&lt;i class=\"icon-arrow-left\"&gt;&lt;/i&gt; Go Back to Chart ")

### Instructions:

Height: high \(close\)  
Backness: back  
Rounded: unrounded

Note: Used as Cardinal Vowel No\. 16\.

Occurrence:  
Acehnese, Alekano, Azeri, Bashkir, Chinese \(some Min Nan dialects, some Wu dialects, Xiang\), Crimean Tatar, Garifuna, Irish \(Ulster\), Korean, Kyrgyz, Ongota, Sakha, Scottish Gaelic, Sundanese, Thai, Turkish, Turkmen, Vietnamese\.
