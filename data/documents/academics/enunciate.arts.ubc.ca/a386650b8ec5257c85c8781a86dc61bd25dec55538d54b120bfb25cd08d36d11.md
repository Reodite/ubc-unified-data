    Labiodental Approximant \(Script V\)

[Go Back to Chart](https://enunciate.arts.ubc.ca/linguistics/consonants-pulmonic/ "&lt;i class=\"icon-arrow-left\"&gt;&lt;/i&gt; Go Back to Chart ")

### Instructions:

Point of articulation: front upper teeth

Articulator: lower lip

Manner of Articulation: Approximant: An articulator approaches a point of articulation, but the constriction degree is not so narrow as for fricatives or stops\.

Voice: voiced – Vocal folds are close and vibrating\.

Occurrence:  
Armenian \(Eastern\), Assyrian Neo\-Aramaic, Danish \(Standard\), Dutch \(Netherlandic\), English, Finnish, German \(Standard, Swiss\), Guarani, Hawaiian, Hindi, Marathi, Norwegian \(Standard Eastern\), Nsenga, Portuguese \(some speakers\), Miyako, Punjabi, Serbo\-Croatian, Slovene, Swedish, Tamil, Turkish, West Frisian\.
