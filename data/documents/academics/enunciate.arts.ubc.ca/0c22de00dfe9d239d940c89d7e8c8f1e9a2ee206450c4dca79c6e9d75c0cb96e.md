### Instructions:

Articulator: front of the tongue

Point of Articulation: palate

Manner of Articulation: Approximant: An articulator approaches a point of articulation, but the constriction degree is not so narrow as fricatives or stops\.

Voice: Voiced – Vocal folds are close and vibrating\.
