Currently, the *eNunciate* project supports self\-directed  **linguistic \(phonetic\)** modules and pronunciation in the following languages:

-   **Japanese**
-   **Cantonese**
-   **Mandarin** \(under development\)
-   **German** \(under development\)
-   **French** \(under development\)
-   **Spanish** \(under development\)

Please make a selection from the side menu to access a course\.

*Note to instructors:* We welcome anyone interested in creating eNunciate course materials to contact us\! See [this page](https://vispro.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources-for-instructors/) for more details\.
