    Voiced Retroflex Fricative \(Right\-tail Z\)

[Go Back to Chart](https://enunciate.arts.ubc.ca/linguistics/consonants-pulmonic/ "&lt;i class=\"icon-arrow-left\"&gt;&lt;/i&gt; Go Back to Chart ")

### Instructions:

Articulator: back of the tip of the tongue

Point of Articulation: alveolar/postalveolar/palatal area

Manner of Articulation: Fricative: The articulator makes a narrow constriction against the point of articulation, so when the air flow goes through the oral passage, audible frication noise is created\.

Voice: voiced – Vocal folds are close and vibrating\.

Occurrence:  
Abkhaz, Adyghe, Chinese \(Mandarin\), Faroese, Italian \(Marked accents of Emilia\-Romagna\), Lower Sorbian, Marrithiyel \(Marri Tjevin dialect\), Pashto \(Southern dialect\), Polish \(Standard, Southeastern Cuyavian dialects, Suwalki dialect\), Russian, Serbo\-Croatian, Sicilian, Slovak, Tilquiapan Zapotec, Torwali, Ubykh, Upper Sorbian \(Some villages north of Hoyerswerda\), Vietnamese \(Southern dialects\), Yi\.
