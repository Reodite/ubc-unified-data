    # /ɴ/

    Uvular Nasal \(Small Capital N\)

### Instructions:

Articulator: the back of the tongue

Point of Articulation: Uvula

Manner: \(Nasal\) Stop – Velum is lowered and the air goes through the nasal passage\. Voice: voiced – Vocal folds are close and vibrating\.

Occurrence: Armenian, Netherlandic Dutch, Georgian, Inuit, Japanese, Kalaallisut, Klallam, Quechua, Spanish
