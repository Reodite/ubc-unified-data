    # /ɤ/
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Close\-mid back unrounded vowel \(Ram’s horns\)

[Go Back to Chart](https://enunciate.arts.ubc.ca/linguistics/vowels/ "&lt;i class=\"icon-arrow-left\"&gt;&lt;/i&gt; Go Back to Chart ")

### Instructions:

Height: mid \(close mid\)  
Backness: back  
Rounded: unrounded

Note: Used as Cardinal Vowel No\. 15\.

Occurrence:  
Alekano, Chinese \(Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien\), English \(Received Pronunciation\), Irish, Kaingang, Korean \(Gyeongsang dialect\), Önge, Scottish Gaelic, Sundanese, Thai\.
