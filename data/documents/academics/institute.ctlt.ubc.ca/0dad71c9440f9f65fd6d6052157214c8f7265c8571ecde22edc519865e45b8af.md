### [Concretizing the abstract: “Poetic Challenges” as practical application in the blended classroom](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/concretizing-the-abstract-poetic-challenges-as-practical-application-in-the-blended-classroom/)

Wednesday, May 22 \| 9 – 10:30 a\.m\. \| IKBLC Seminar Room \(Room 2\.22\)

Pillar: Transformative Learning

How do we create a seamless relationship between what students are doing on their own \(i\.e\. online\) and the active learning component in the classroom? What makes a meaningful learning experience? How do we boost higher\-order thinking while practicing authentic application of skills? Join us to tackle what we came to call the Poetic Challenges in our process to make the in\-class portion of a blended learning course incorporate concepts covered in the weekly online materials and make practical applications that are useful, relevant and concrete for learners\.  
[Register &gt;](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/concretizing-the-abstract-poetic-challenges-as-practical-application-in-the-blended-classroom/)

### [Dissemination of undergraduate research: A best practices roundtable symposium hosted by writing across the curriculum](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/dissemination-of-undergraduate-research-a-best-practices-roundtable-symposium-hosted-by-writing-across-the-curriculum/)

### [What is on the horizon at UBC? Creating a UBC specific Horizon Report](https://events.ctlt.ubc.ca/events/what-is-on-the-horizon-at-ubc-creating-a-ubc-specific-horizon-report/)
