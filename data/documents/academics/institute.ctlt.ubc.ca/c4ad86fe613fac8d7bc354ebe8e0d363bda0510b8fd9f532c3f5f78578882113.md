### Learning objectives

By the end of the module you will be able to:

-   Reflect on the teaching strategies you currently have to ensure effective teaching occur
-   Describe your own teaching priorities and the rationale behind them

### Read and reflect

Creating a lesson requires more than choosing content and delivering the lesson effective\. As a TA or instructor, your role is to help your students learn\. What does that mean and how do you go about doing so? Write notes in another document/on paper about your current teaching strategies\.
