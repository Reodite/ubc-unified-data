The CTLT Spring Institute offers you an opportunity to share practice and research around teaching, learning and technology\. At each Institute, speakers and facilitators were invited from a broad range of backgrounds and expertise in order to expand understandings of teaching and learning\.

Here you will find a collection of the many resources that presenters have offered from previous CTLT Institutes\. Please feel free to revisit past Institutes and reference the work that you see shared\.
