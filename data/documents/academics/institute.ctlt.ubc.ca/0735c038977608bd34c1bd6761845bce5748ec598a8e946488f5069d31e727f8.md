### CTLT Institutes

Hosted each year by the [Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology](http://ctlt.ubc.ca/) \(CTLT\), the [Summer](https://institute2-ctlt.sites.olt.ubc.ca/summer-institute/) and [Winter](https://institute.ctlt.ubc.ca/winter-institute-2/) Institutes feature interactive workshops where participants have an opportunity to network, gain practical ideas and share best practices\.

### 2026 CTLT Winter Institute, December 7 and 8th, 2026

#### **Beyond One\-Size\-Fits\-All: Intersectional Approaches to Teaching**

We invite UBC educators, researchers, and scholars to propose interactive workshops that explore what it means to put intersectional thinking into action—and how intersectional perspectives can shape what we teach, how we teach, how we assess, and how we create learning environments\.
