### Learning objectives

By the end of the module you will be able to:

-   Articulate what your current priorities are towards teaching development
-   Reflect on your goals as an instructor

### Read and reflect

Whether it is the first time you are teaching in your discipline or the tenth time you are teaching the same material, there is always something in your teaching that can be improved upon\. Learning about teaching is a never\-ending process\. What does teaching mean to you and what makes it stand apart?
