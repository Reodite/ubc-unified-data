### Learning objectives

By the end of the module you will be able to:

-   Discuss the role of reflection in your life

### Read and reflect

Reflection can bring about new insights but often times it is not part of the teaching practice\. In these session, we discuss the value reflection can bring to your role as a TA/instructor as well as what it can mean for your learners\.
