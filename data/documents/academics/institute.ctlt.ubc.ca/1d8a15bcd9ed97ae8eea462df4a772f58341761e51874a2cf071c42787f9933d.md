**To celebrate learning this year, CTLT Institutes presents Journeys in Teaching Innovation: Faculty and Instructor Stories of Idea to Impact,** which takes you behind the scenes of what it *really* looks like to move from spark to meaningful impact on student learning\.

Hear real experiences from UBC faculty and instructors as they share practical insights, lessons learned, and a few concrete examples to help you get started \(or keep going\)\.

Whether you’re just beginning to explore an idea or already experimenting in your course, this session offers inspiration and a clearer path forward\.
