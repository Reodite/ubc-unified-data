*Connect with faculty members in neuroscience across a range of disciplines\.*

UBC’s neuroscience program at the Djavad Mowafaghian Centre for Brain Health is a hub for brain research and training with more than 80 faculty members, each leaders in the field of neuroscience\. Our faculty supervisors bring a diversity of perspectives to your neuroscience education\.

If you have questions about our faculty, or for assistance in connecting with a potential supervisor, please email the Graduate Program Coordinator at [ubc\.neuroscience@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.neuroscience@ubc.ca)\.

If you are a faculty member and you require updates to your faculty profile, please email [ubc\.neuroscience@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.neuroscience@ubc.ca)
