#### [Aboriginal Languages &amp; Literacy Institute](https://alli.arts.ubc.ca/aboriginal-languages-literacy-institute/)

[Read More](https://alli.arts.ubc.ca/aboriginal-languages-literacy-institute/ "Read More")

**The Aboriginal Languages and Literacy Institute aims to increase awareness of endangered languages and to provide concrete expertise and skills to address community needs\.**

In an exciting summer program in 2006, ALLI organized a productive month of courses and activity\. Researchers, educators, community members and other interested parties all participated\.  They were given powerful tools to document, understand, and spread aboriginal languages with the ultimate goal of ensuring that these languages endure as part of a linguistic and cultural heritage for generations to come\. This website showcases the outputs of the 2006 event\.
