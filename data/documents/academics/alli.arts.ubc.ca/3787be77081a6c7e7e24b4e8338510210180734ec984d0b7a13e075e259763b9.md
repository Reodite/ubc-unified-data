To register in ALLI, it is not neccesary to be enrolled as a student at UBC, nor do you need to hold a highschool diploma\. Below, you will find information or links to the application process, ALLI fees, UBC accomodation, and band sponsorship\.

[Image](https://alli-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/06/swiggle.gif)

#### How to apply to UBC:

Please visit [UBC Academic Calendar](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/) to find information about applications\.

There are many different UBC Admission options\. Explore [UBC Access](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/non-degree-studies/non-degree-studies-access-studies) to find out more\.

#### Registration Information:

For information regarding registration, please check [this page](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration)\.

[Image](https://alli-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/06/swiggle.gif)

#### Fee Structure and Costs:

-   UBC Application Fee : $ 60\.00
-   Registration Deposit\* : $ 100\.00
-   Tuition per 3 credit course : $409\.20
-   Student Fees :  $ 120\.44

\* deposit applied towards tuition

#### UBC accommodation costs:

[UBC Conferences and Accommodation](https://suitesatubc.com/): $40/night

E\-mail: [reservations@housing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:reservations@housing.ubc.ca)

Phone: 888\-331\-4194

[Image](https://alli-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/06/swiggle.gif)

#### Band Sponsorship information:

Information regarding Band Sponsorship can be found [here](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/funding-studies/financial-support-options/financial-support-indigenous#funding-resources-for-indigenous-students)\.

ALLI courses are accredited towards:

-   UBC B\.A\.
-   UBC B\.Ed\.
-   UBC NITEP
-   Transfer credits to other post\-secondary institutions
