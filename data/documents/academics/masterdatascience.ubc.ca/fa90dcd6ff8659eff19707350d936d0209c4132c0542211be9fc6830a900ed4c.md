Course ID COLX 525

Campus Computational Linguistics

Semester Winter

Course Block 5

Course Description What happens when the word is not small enough, and our systems need to pick them apart further? Learn how linguistics has defined “meaning bearing units”, and how we adapt that knowledge to gain insight about how to interpret the output of AI in several modalities: text and speech\.

Instructor\(s\) with link [Garrett Nicolai](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/index%2Ephp/why-ubc/people/faculty/garrett-nicolai)
