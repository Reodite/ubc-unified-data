Course ID COLX 563

Campus Computational Linguistics

Semester Winter

Course Block 6

Course Description Topics in LLMs\. This course connects the fundamentals learned in COLX 561 and DSCI 531 to some of the concerns of building and using LLMs today\. Topics include scaling laws, GPUs, embedding models, and architectural choices\.
