Course ID DSCI 531

Campus UBC Vancouver

Semester Fall

Course Block 2

Course Description How to \(and how not to\) visualize data\. Graphical grammars via ggplot in R and Altair in Python\. Creating effective data visualizations for exploratory data analysis and to communicate insights to others\.
