Course ID COLX 523

Campus Computational Linguistics

Semester Winter

Course Block 5

Course Description We have the data, so what can we do with it? From annotation to deployment, this course introduces students to data stewardship and ownership\. Students create and deploy a corpus and dashboard that is informative and accessible, all while taking ownership through an AGILE framework\.
