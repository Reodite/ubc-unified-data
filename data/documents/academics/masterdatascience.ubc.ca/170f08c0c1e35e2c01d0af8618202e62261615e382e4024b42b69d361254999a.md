Course ID COLX 561

Campus Computational Linguistics

Semester Winter

Course Block 4

Course Description An introduction to Large language models \(LLMs\)\. How do we get AI models that can understand and produce human language? This course introduces the idea of creating semantic vector spaces through language generation, as well as the architectures used in LLMs\.  

Instructor\(s\) with link [Isabel Papadimitriou](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/index%2ephp/why-ubc/people/faculty/isabel-papadimitriou)
