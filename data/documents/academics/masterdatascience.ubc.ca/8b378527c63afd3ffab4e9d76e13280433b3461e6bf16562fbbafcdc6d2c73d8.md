Course ID COLX 581

Campus Computational Linguistics

Semester Winter

Course Block 6

Course Description We’re in the Big Data revolution, but not all languages have equal amounts of data\. Learn how to adapt large language models to smaller data, including efficient methods of selecting from limited examples, bootstrapping and ensembling, LoRA and PEFT, and in context and few\-shot learning\.  

Instructor\(s\) with link [Garrett Nicolai](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/why-ubc/people/faculty/garrett-nicolai)
