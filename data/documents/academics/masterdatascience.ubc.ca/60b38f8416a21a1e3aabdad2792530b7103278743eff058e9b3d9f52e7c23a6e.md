#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor of Arts Honours, Linguistics and a Bachelor of Science 3\-year in Computer Science from the University of Saskatchewan  
**Program:** MDS Computational Linguistics, Class of 2026

I was inspired by a unit in my Grade 11 English class where we read the play Pygmalion and watched the film adaptation My Fair Lady\. I was looking for extra classes in my first year of Computer Science and chose the Introduction to Linguistics course out of curiosity\. I ended up loving it from the start and was so enthusiastic to learn about it\. I loved searching for the patterns and learning about the inner workings of how we use language\.

#### **About Kara Leier**

Kara Leier studied at the University of Saskatchewan and earned a Bachelor of Arts Honours in Linguistics and a Bachelor of Science 3\-year in Computer Science\. After completing her degrees, Kara wasn’t interested in pursuing a career in Speech Pathology but became interested in furthering her education in computational linguistics after taking a course during her undergrad as it allowed her to combine two things she loved: computer science and linguistics\. When not in MDS Computational Linguistics, you can find Kara crocheting, taking walks with her partner, and learning how to dance\!  [Read Kara’s story](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/why-data-science/student-success-stories/kara-leier)\.
