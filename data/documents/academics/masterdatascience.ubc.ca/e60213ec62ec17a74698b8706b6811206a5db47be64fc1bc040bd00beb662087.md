Course ID COLX 531

Campus Computational Linguistics

Semester Winter

Course Block 5

Course Description How are large language models actually built and deployed? This course covers the major stages of modern LLM engineering, including how to scale up in pre\-training, how methods like RLHF and RLVR are used in post\-training, and how to design LLM inference engines for real\-world deployment\.
