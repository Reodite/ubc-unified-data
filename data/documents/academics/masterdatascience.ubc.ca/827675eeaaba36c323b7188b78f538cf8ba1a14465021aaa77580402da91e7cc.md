### MDS Admissions Q&amp;A

Join us for a virtual information session to learn everything you need to know and get your questions answered about the MDS Vancouver, Okanagan and Computational Linguistics programs at the University of British Columbia\.

The event will cover:

-   Introduction to UBC’s Master of Data Science Vancouver and Okanagan programs as well as the Master of Data Science in Computational Linguistics and what makes our programs unique
-   Overview of admission requirements and application process
-   Chance to engage in a live Q&amp;A with faculty from Vancouver, Okanagan and Computational Linguistic programs\.

Register here:

-   **MDS Admissions Q&amp;A:** Tuesday, October 20, 2026 \- 12:30pm\-1:30pm PT

Missed the Alumni Panels? Click on the "Past Events" tab to view videos from our recent series of virtual information sessions to learn more about the UBC Master of Data Science programs\.
