Are you a creative problem solver with a love for data? UBC’s Master of Data Science \(MDS\) was designed for you\. Developed to give students a fast track to a great career, the program is helping to meet a growing need by producing skilled professionals who can turn data into knowledge\.

Utilizing descriptive and prescriptive techniques, students extract and analyze data from both unstructured and structured forms, uncover insights, and learn to effectively communicate their results with stakeholders\.

87% of our alumni are working full\-time as a data scientist 6\-months after graduation\. They are highly trained, qualified data scientists who can help businesses and organizations, across all domains, improve strategy, products, services, health, safety, and so much more by translating their findings into solutions that enable and inform action\.

#### Who

Students with ANY undergraduate degree, from the Sciences, to the Arts, and Business, who are inquisitive and have a passion for learning

#### What

10\-month, full\-time, in\-person, accelerated data science master's program

#### Where

The University of British Columbia, Vancouver or Okanagan campuses

#### When

Begins each September\.

#### How

An intensive schedule of 24 one\-credit courses clustered into four\-week segments to provide focus and cover topics in\-depth

#### Why

To translate data into knowledge, communicate its stories, and help decision\-makers take action

## Learn more about each program

[Vancouver](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/programs/vancouver "MDS Vancouver Campus")

This program provides graduates with a unique set of specialized, practical computational and statistical skills to successfully launch a career in the field of data science\. The program allows students to learn amongst peers with a wide variety of skills and backgrounds\.

[Okanagan](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/programs/okanagan "MDS Okanagan Campus")

UBC Okanagan's program trains students with in\-demand, industrial data science skills for careers in a variety of fields\. With a cohort limited to 60 students, it provides a more intimate and personalized learning environment\.

[Computational Linguistics](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/programs/computational-linguistics "Computational Linguistics")

UBC’s newest Master of Data Science program equips students with a foundation in data science then branches off to focus on techniques for processing language\.
