#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor of Music in Jazz Saxophone from Capilano University  
**Program:** MDS Okanagan, Class of 2022

Before the MDS Okanagan program, I was working contract\-to\-contract as a cruise ship musician on international trips\. Then, when the pandemic canceled my ongoing contracts, I got more and more curious about programming and data science fundamentals in an attempt to re\-skill into a more sustainable and fulfilling career\. The MDS Okanagan program is uniquely designed for students with technical abilities and interests, but not necessarily requiring a technical undergraduate degree – students exactly like me\. 

#### **About Gavin Grochowski**

Originally from Vancouver, Gavin obtained his Bachelor of Music in Jazz Saxophone from Capilano University\.  When his working situation changed as a result of the COVID\-19 pandemic, Gavin pivoted to re\-skill into a more sustainable and fulfilling career\. With a love for coding and problem solving, Gavin began looking for data science programs\. UBCO’s reputation for quality of education, along with MDS Okanagan program is uniquely designed for students with technical abilities and interests, made it the right fit for Gavin\. Having now completed the program, Gavin is excited to apply the technical skills learned over the course of the program to pursue employment as a Data Scientist or a Data Analyst in order to support key stakeholders in making key business decisions\.
