[Master of Data Science Vancouver Zac Warham](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/large/public/media-images/zac-warham-square-headshot.jpg.webp?itok=j0xf-BwY)

#### **Course Coordinator, MDS Vancouver**

*"I love how data science skills are used to show the breadth of knowledge in all fields, particularly for marine biology in my case\. The more visuals the better\!"*

Zac move to Canada from Australia in August of 2021 and joined the MDS team in January 2022 as a TA whilst studying his Masters of Science in Oceans and Fisheries at UBC\. Prior to his time at UBC, Zac worked as an IT Support Assistant and an Aquarium Aquarist whilst he studied a combination of Marine Biology &amp; Web Development\. Zac looks forward to being a part of the learning journey for the next generation of data scientists\.
