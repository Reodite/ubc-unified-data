Course ID DATA 599

Campus UBC Okanagan

Semester Spring

Course Description A mentored group project based on real data and questions from a partner within or outside the university\. Students will formulate questions and design and execute a suitable analysis plan\. The group will work collaboratively to produce a reproducible analysis pipeline, project report, presentation and possibly other products, such as a dashboard\.
