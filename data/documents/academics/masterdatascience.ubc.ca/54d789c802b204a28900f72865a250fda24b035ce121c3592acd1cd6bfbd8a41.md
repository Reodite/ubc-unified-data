Course ID DSCI 575

Campus UBC Vancouver

Semester Winter

Course Block 6

Course Description Explore advanced machine learning methods for natural language processing \(NLP\) applications, including Markov chains, hidden Markov models, recurrent neural networks, self\-attention and transformer architectures, and the fundamentals of large language models\.
