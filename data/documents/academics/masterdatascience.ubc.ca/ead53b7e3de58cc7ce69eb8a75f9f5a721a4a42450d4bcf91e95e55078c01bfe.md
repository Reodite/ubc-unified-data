#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor of Science, Honours in Neuroscience from the University of Calgary  
**Program:** MDS Okanagan, Class of 2022

During my undergrad, I found that the coursework I preferred was more analytical in nature\. After taking a few computer science courses, I realized I wanted to learn how to apply computational methods to real\-world problems\. The structure of the program, designed for people from almost any educational background, along with the opportunity to learn in a small cohort, really tipped the scales in MDS\-O’s favour\. Now that I have come through the program, I have far more technical data science experience and the skills to continue learning on my own\.

#### **About Sara Hall**

Sara obtained Bachelor of Science in in Neuroscience from the University of Calgary, before moving directly into the MDS Okanagan program\. It was during her time completing her undergraduate degree where Sara fostered a desire to learn how to apply computational methods to real\-world problems\.  Situated in beautiful city of Kelowna, the MDS\-O program, with its small cohort size and real\-world experience through its capstone projects, caught Sara’s attention\. Sara will look to apply the expertise taught in the MDS program on the Okanagan campus to pursue a career related to climate risk management\.
