Course ID COLX 595

Campus Computational Linguistics

Semester Spring

Course Description A mentored group project based on real data and questions from a partner within or outside the university\. Students will formulate questions and design and execute a suitable analysis plan\. The group will work collaboratively to produce a project report, presentation, and possibly other products, such as a web application\.  
  

Instructor\(s\) with link [MDS Computational Linguistics Staff](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/why-ubc/people#computational-linguistics)
