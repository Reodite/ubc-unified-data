*"The UBC MDS program offered a great balance of the skills needed to succeed in machine learning and data science\.  In addition, the 10\-month duration of the program was very attractive as it was really the fastest way for me to pivot with additional education\."*  
 

#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor of Technology in Accounting from British Columbia Institute of Technology  
**Prior Experience:** Senior Accountant / Consultant, KPMG  
Program: MDS Vancouver, Class of 2022

At KPMG, I worked on a few projects where we utilized data analytics and data visualization which introduced me to the world of data science\. I saw the value that data science could provide to companies, and after some time found my true passion which is machine learning\. mainly worked with large banking, credit union, wealth management, manufacturing, and real estate clients\.

#### **About Nico Van den Hoff**

Vancouver native Nico obtained his Bachelor of Technology in Accounting from British Columbia Institute of Technology and was working in finance at KPMG LLP prior to joining the MDS Vancouver program mainly working with large banking, credit union, wealth management, manufacturing, and real estate clients\. It was while working at KPMG that Nico realized his passion for machine learning\. In Nico’s spare time, he enjoys long distance running \(marathons\), playing golf, and collecting whisky and wine\.
