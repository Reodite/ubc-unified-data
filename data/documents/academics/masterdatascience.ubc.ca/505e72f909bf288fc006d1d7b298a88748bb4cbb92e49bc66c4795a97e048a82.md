*"I felt that this program really combined my love for linguistics and my previous experience in computer science\. I like using my technical skills and my ideal career involves me working with language data so I felt like the MDS Computational Linguistics program was a good match\."*

#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor of Science in Cognitive Systems from the University of British Columbia  
**Program:** MDS Computational Linguistics, Class of 2022

I think data science is being used to solve a lot of really cool problems\. Natural language data that can be used for really impactful applications such as improving health care or flighting climate change\. It feels like a really important field right now\. I knew I wanted to pursue a career in data science but a lot of the positions required or strongly preferred a Master’s level education\. I liked that the program was only 10\-months long and that I would be able to focus specially on state\-of\-the art NLP techniques\.

#### **About Saffrin Granby**

Saffrin completed her Bachelor of Science in Cognitive Systems at UBC where she specialized in Computer Science\. During her undergraduate degree, Saffrin worked at the CANARY lab at UBC where she worked on using linguistic features to detect the presence of Alzheimer’s disease\. When not studying, Saffrin enjoys long bike rides and hanging out with friends by going on walks or trying restaurants together\.
