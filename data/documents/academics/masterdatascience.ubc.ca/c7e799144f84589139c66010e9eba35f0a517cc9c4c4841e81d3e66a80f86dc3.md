Course ID COLX 565

Campus Computational Linguistics

Semester Winter

Course Block 5

Course Description A research seminar\-style class, reading recent papers in the field and discussing their implications\. Main focuses are interpretability \(methods for understanding what’s happening in the black box of LLMs\) and the effects of LLMs on human psychology and society\.  

Instructor\(s\) with link [Isabel Papadimitriou](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/why-ubc/people/faculty/isabel-papadimitriou)
