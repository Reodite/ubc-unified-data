[Saisree Gownipalli Ramanaiah Master of Data Science Okanagan](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/large/public/media-images/sairsee-headshot-square-master-of-data-science-okanagan.png.webp?itok=iRm8HoMN)

*"Since data science was my goal, I was looking for a top university that either focused on data science or had a master of computer science program with a thesis option with a hope to research in data science\.*"

#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Communication Engineering from St\.Joseph’s College of Engineering, India  
**Program:** MDS Okanagan, Class of 2022

Before the MDS Okanagan program, I was working as a Senior Systems Engineer with Infosys and saw firsthand how data driven decisions are impactful\. This encouraged me to explore machine learning to optimize businesses\. Getting a master’s degree in something that excited me felt like a good start\. The University of British Columbia reputation as a top\-ranked university with great research support led me to choose the Master of Data Science program and the university\. There, I worked on a data science project as a researcher in addition to being a full\-time student\. Having now completed the program, I am looking forward to applying the knowledge I gained from the MDS program to pursue data scientist and machine learning engineer roles with an interest in data modeling that drive business decisions\.

#### **About Saisree Gownipalli Ramanaiah**

Originally from India, Saisree obtained her Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Communication Engineering from St\. Joseph’s College of Engineering\. Following time as a Senior Systems Engineer with Infosys, Saisree decided to return to school in hopes of pursuing a career in data science\. The world\-class infrastructure and access to learning resources, along with UBC’s reputation as a top 40 university, convinced Saisree that UBCO was the right place for her\. Having now completed the program, Saisree is excited to apply the expertise learned over the course of the program and is planning on pursuing data scientist and machine learning engineer roles in the future\.
