#### **Program Manager, MDS Computational Linguistics**

*“The MDS Computational Linguistics program is where rigorous data science meets the complexity of human language\. What excites me most is watching students arrive with raw potential and leave with the confidence—and skillset—to shape how the world uses AI and language\.”*

Tom Coules is the Program Manager for the Master of Data Science in Computational Linguistics \. He works closely with students, faculty, and partners to support every stage of the program—from recruitment to graduation—making sure the experience is both academically strong and personally supportive\. Tom is passionate about building thoughtful systems, fostering community, and helping students translate complex ideas into real\-world impact\.
