#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor of Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering from Anna University  
**Program:** MDS Vancouver, Class of 2022

My bachelor’s capstone project was based on machine learning in which my team and I built a machine learning model to predict whether an earthquake can cause a tsunami given the earthquake characteristics\. During my professional experience, I worked on building conversational AI agents for major multinational retail and financial corporations\. All these contributions made me realize my interest in this field, and I wanted to pursue a professional master’s degree to gain in\-depth academic knowledge\.

#### **About Gautham Pughazhendhi**

After graduating with a Bachelor of Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering from Anna University, Gautham worked as a software engineer as part of a machine learning team at Sirius Computer Solutions, a CDW company\. Because of the intense nature of the MDS program, Gautham credits the program for teaching him to manage my priorities efficiently and be productive\. When not in class, avid swimmer, Gautham frequented the UBC Aquatic Center\.
