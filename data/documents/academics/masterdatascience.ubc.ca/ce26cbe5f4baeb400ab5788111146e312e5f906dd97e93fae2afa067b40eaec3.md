## [Freya Kan](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/why-ubc/people/alumni/freya-kan)

*"\[MDS Computational Linguistics\] teaches underlying concepts in depth, from neural networks to LLMs and AI agents\. At a time when familiarity with AI is becoming a requirement across many industries, this knowledge is extremely valuable\."*

#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor’s in Language Interpretation and Translation from Hong Kong Baptist University  
**Program:** MDS Computational Linguistics, Class of 2026

As much as I love languages, I also have a keen interest in a wide range of subjects, from engineering to philosophy and astronomy to economics\. When ChatGPT took the world by storm in 2022, I naturally became interested in language technology\. That was when I first learned about LLMs and computational linguistics\.

#### **About Freya Kan**

Freya Kan holds a Bachelor’s in Language Interpretation and Translation from Hong Kong Baptist University\. During her undergrad, Freya was taking mathematics and programming courses that sparked her interest in language technology\. Freya applied to the MDS Computational Linguistics program because she liked how the program combined data science, computational linguistics with industry experience\. Outside of MDS Computational Linguistics, Freya enjoys hiking and sightseeing with friends from the program\. ## [Saumi Rahnamay](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/why-ubc/people/alumni/saumi-rahnamay)

#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor’s of Philosophy, Minor in Social Data Analytics from Simon Fraser University

On the Social Data Analytics side of my degree, I had a course on computational text analysis, which served as a primer for many of the concepts I would explore in more detail during the MDS Computational Linguistics program\. Stuff like sentiment analysis, syntax parsing, word embeddings, topic modelling, etc\.

Prior to the MDS Computational Linguistics program, Saumi was finishing his bachelor’s degree at SFU in Philosophy and was exposed to data science and NLP from his minor in Social Data Analytics\. After switching away from Philosophy, Saumi started working on a data analyst portfolio to try to land a position as a data analyst but decided he needed more schooling would help him land a position in the field; which is when he decided to apply to the MDS Computational Linguistics program\. Outside of MDS Computational Linguistics, Saumi likes to play guitar \(electric, classical\) with his band\. ## [Kara Leier](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/why-ubc/people/alumni/kara-leier)

*"The professors do a fantastic job of keeping up to date with the current times and topics\. Many of our classes were adapted to the current AI environment and discourse"*

#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor of Arts Honours, Linguistics and a Bachelor of Science 3\-year in Computer Science from the University of Saskatchewan  
**Program:** MDS Computational Linguistics, Class of 2026

I was inspired by a unit in my Grade 11 English class where we read the play Pygmalion and watched the film adaptation My Fair Lady\. I was looking for extra classes in my first year of Computer Science and chose the Introduction to Linguistics course out of curiosity\. I ended up loving it from the start and was so enthusiastic to learn about it\. I loved searching for the patterns and learning about the inner workings of how we use language\.

#### **About Kara Leier**

Kara Leier studied at the University of Saskatchewan and earned a Bachelor of Arts Honours in Linguistics and a Bachelor of Science 3\-year in Computer Science\. ## [Lavanya Gupta](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/why-ubc/people/alumni/lavanya-gupta)

*“UBC MDS stood out for how deliberately it is structured\. The curriculum strikes a balance between statistical foundations and practical application, and the capstone project, where you work on a real problem with an actual industry partner, was a big draw\.”*

#### **Previous Degree:** Bachelor’s of Engineering in Computer Engineering from Thapar Institute of Engineering &amp; Technology  
**Program:** MDS Vancouver, Class of 2026

During my time at Ericsson, I worked on the technical side of project management and naturally found myself drawn toward the data\. I kept looking for patterns and trying to make sense of numbers that were often treated as background noise\. Over time, I realized I was less interested in just observing the data and more interested in analyzing it and translating it into insights that could support decisions and be communicated clearly to stakeholders\.

#### **About Lavanya Gupta**

Lavanya completed her Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering from Thapar Institute of Engineering &amp; Technology in India, and then joined Ericsson Global India Pvt\. Ltd\., where she worked in Project Management Operations\. It was this combination of academic and industry exposure that ultimately led Lavanya toward data science\. Before applying to the MDS program, Lavanya reached out to a few alumni before applying, and they spoke about how the program genuinely prepares you for industry, not just academically but in terms of how you think and approach problems\. When not in MDS, you will find Lavanya reading murder mystery novels and seeking out new experiences unrelated to data science\.
