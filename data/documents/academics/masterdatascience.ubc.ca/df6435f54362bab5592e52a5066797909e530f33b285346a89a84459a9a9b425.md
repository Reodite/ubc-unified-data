We hope you will join us and be part of our vibrant community of world\-renowned faculty and students\.

When you click on the Apply Now button below you will leave this website and go to the UBC Graduate Studies Online Application\. Before you proceed, we highly recommend you review the [Admission Requirements](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/admissions/) and detailed Application Process information\.

Please note that you will be required to complete and submit a separate application form for each program\.

Applications for **September 2027** are **now open** for **MDS Okanagan only**\. Application deadline is **January 31, 2027**\. **Reference Deadline:** 7 days after application deadline

Applications for **September 2027** for MDS Vancouver and Computational Linguistics will **open October 1, 2026**\. [Subscribe for news and updates](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/contact-us/sign-updates)\.

Allow yourself 45 to 60 minutes to complete the online application\.
