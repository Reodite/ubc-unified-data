> The Arts Vancouver Summer Program offered by the University of British Columbia’s Faculty of Arts \(Arts VSP\) is a four\-week summer program for international undergraduate students that offers courses in the humanities, social sciences, and creative and performing arts\.

Since its inception in 2013, Arts VSP has been providing 1000\+ international students with an enriching educational, social, and cultural experience\. Join the vibrant and diverse community of students living and studying on the UBC’s Vancouver campus and experience first\-hand what it is like to learn at a top\-ranked research university\.
