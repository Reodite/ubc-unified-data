The Perinatal Wellbeing Research Team, part of the Midwifery Program within UBC’s Department of Family Practice, focuses on advancing research in maternal mental health and pregnancy outcomes\. Led by [Dr\. Hamideh Bayrampour](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/hamideh-bayrampour/), Associate Professor at UBC, the team is dedicated to improving care for expectant parents through evidence\-based studies\. Current projects include research on screening for pregnancy\-specific anxiety, with more details available on their [website](https://pwrt.med.ubc.ca/) \.
