UBC Midwifery is establishing a new five\-year plan\. Pursuing alignment on critical program focuses to drive our mission forward, re\-vitalizing the program\.

Strategic planning work started earlier this year with stakeholder feedback and audits culminating with in person strategic planning work days in late May\.

[Image](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/IMG_1346-1-1.jpg)

[Image](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/IMG_1347-1-1.jpg)

What we worked on\.

-   Defining the changing trends which may impact the Midwifery Program in the next four years\.
-   Identifying the most significant internal strengths and weaknesses, and the external opportunities and threats to be addressed in the upcoming strategic plan\.
-   Identifying the strategic priorities for the Midwifery Program\.

And, of course, so much more\. We are working to better serve the diverse needs of the communities we serve\.

Thank you to all of the faculty, staff, preceptors and stakeholders that contributed feedback\.
