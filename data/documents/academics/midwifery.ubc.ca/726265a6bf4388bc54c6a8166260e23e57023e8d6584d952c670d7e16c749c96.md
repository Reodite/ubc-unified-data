Role

Open Close

The Co\-Lead, Clinical Placements supports the coordination and allocation of clinical placements for undergraduate and IEMBP students\. This role focuses on student placement processes, preceptor engagement and collaboration with course instructors, tutors and program staff\.

Biography

Open Close

Dana is a healthcare operations professional with extensive experience supporting clinical teams, optimizing workflows, and coordinating complex systems across multiple care environments\. She has worked closely with physicians, nurse practitioners, and administrative teams to support onboarding, training, and efficient, high\-quality patient care\. In her role with the UBC Midwifery Program, Dana brings a strategic, solutions\-focused approach and values strong relationships and clear communication to support learners and clinical partners\.
