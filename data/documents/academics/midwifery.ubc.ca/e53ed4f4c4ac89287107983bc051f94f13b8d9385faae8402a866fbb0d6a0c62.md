Dear UBC Midwifery Program alumni,

The UBC Midwifery Program turns 20 this year and we are celebrating on [November 19th](https://trk.cp20.com/click/ceuj-2l5w9p-jkjj6k-5nf78ba0/) in Vancouver\! As part of the 20th Anniversary Celebration, we are hosting a **birth art show**, *Birth and its Meaning*, to showcase some of the amazing birth\-related artwork that has been created by almost 20 cohorts of students over the years\. Now we need your help\!

We are welcoming alumni to loan us their artwork – new works or from last year, or from 5, 10, or 15 years ago – to display at the Celebration\. The show will be up for the day on November 19th and will be viewed by students, alumni, and special guests at the event only\. A short artist’s statement to accompany your artwork \(1\-2 paragraphs\) will be requested\.

**How to submit your artwork:**

Step 1: Take a photo of your artwork\.

Step 2: Complete the [short submission form](https://trk.cp20.com/click/ceuj-2l5w9p-jkjj6l-5nf78ba1/) and attached your photo\(s\)\.

Step 3: You will be notified by mid\-October with more details regarding dropping off and return of your artwork\.

**Submission deadline: October 14, 2022**

We look forward to seeing you and your artwork at our 20th Anniversary Celebration on November 19th – registration opens soon\!
