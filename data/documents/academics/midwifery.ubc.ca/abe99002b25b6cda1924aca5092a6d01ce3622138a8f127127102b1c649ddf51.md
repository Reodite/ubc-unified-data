**Recap: MMRC Speaker Series with Allison Campbell – Parenting Behind Bars**

The **McMaster Midwifery Research Centre \(MMRC\) Speaker Series** recently featured **Allison Campbell, RM, MA**, in a compelling discussion on **“Parenting Behind Bars: Maternal\-Child \(Connection and\) Separation in BC’s Correctional Facilities Designated for Women\.”**

Drawing on her expertise in midwifery and social justice, Allison examined the **history and current use of Mother\-Child Programs in BC prisons**, exploring their impact through the lens of **reproductive justice and prison abolition**\. Her talk shed light on the challenges incarcerated parents face and the systemic barriers that shape their experiences\.
