**Update – February 26\[th\], 2015**

Midwifery Interview Invitations:

UBC Midwifery will be contacting all applicants for the 2015 Fall class regarding interviews during the week of March 2\[nd\]\.   Candidates chosen to participate in the interviews will be invited to attend on either Friday, March 27\[th\] or Saturday, March 28\[th\]\.  The date of each applicants interview will be chosen by the program\.
