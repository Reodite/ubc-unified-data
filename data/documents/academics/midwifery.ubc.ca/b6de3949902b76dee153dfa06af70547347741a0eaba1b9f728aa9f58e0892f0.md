To help identify areas of educational development and improvement, you’re invited to participate in a needs assessment to learn more about your interest in CPD and the types of programming you would like to see\.

Midwives are encouraged to complete this [short, anonymous survey](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_dmrzcVQFLp1dihU) by June 30, 2024\.

For more CPD Midwifery content and resources, please visit their [dedicated webpage](https://ubccpd.ca/learn/tailored-initiatives/midwifery)\.
