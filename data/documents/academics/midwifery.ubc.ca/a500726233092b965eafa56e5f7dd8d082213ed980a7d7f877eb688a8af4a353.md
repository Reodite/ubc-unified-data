### Step 1: UBC Application

Submit a UBC application through [Undergraduate Programs and Admissions](http://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/how-to-apply/) and pay the application fee\. Midwifery must be selected as the first choice program\. Please contact [Undergraduate Programs and Admissions](http://you.ubc.ca/contact-us/) if you have any application questions\. Former or current UBC students must also apply to UBC for readmission or transfer by logging into the [Applicant Services Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) with their CWL\.

### Step 2: Midwifery Program Application

Submit a [supplemental Midwifery Program application](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/UBC-Midwifery-Supplemental-Application-2026-Updated.pdf) and pay the application fee\. You will receive access to the supplemental application through the [Applicant Services Centre](https://myapplication.ubc.ca/) once you have completed **step one**\.

### Step 3: Transcripts

Have all official transcripts sent to UBC Undergraduate Admissions by January 15\[th\], including institutions where you are currently registered in courses\. Unofficial transcripts can be uploaded to the Applicant Services Centre through the document upload option\. Official transcripts will be required for all successful applicants as a condition of their offer of admission\. Prerequisite courses must be completed and final transcripts received by Undergraduate Admissions no later than June 30\[th\]\. 

### Step 4: Interviews

The top 50 candidates will be invited to complete an asynchronous MMI interview to be held in April\. For more information on the interview process, please see the comprehensive [Midwifery admissions process](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/09/UBC-MIDWIFERY-ADMISSIONS-PROCESS-2024-1.pdf) overview\.

The online application for admission to the University of British Columbia opens in the fall of each year\. **As a Midwifery Program candidate, your UBC application must be submitted by December 5\[th\]\.**

Once the UBC application has been submitted and the application fee has been paid, the Midwifery Supplemental Application will be available in your Applicant Services Centre account \(step 2\)\. **The Midwifery Supplemental Application must be submitted, and the supplemental fee must be paid by January 15\[th\]\.**

Undergraduate Programs and Admissions require all **transcripts to be submitted by January 15\[th\]\.**

1.  Meet UBC’s general admission requirements
2.  Have confirmation of completion or proof of registration of the Midwifery Program prerequisites \(BIOL 155 &amp; WRDS 150\)
3.  Submit the UBC application and pay the application fee
4.  a\) Submit the Midwifery Supplemental Application and pay the application fee  
    b\) Have all transcripts either uploaded to your Applicant Services Centre account or sent directly to Undergraduate Admissions
