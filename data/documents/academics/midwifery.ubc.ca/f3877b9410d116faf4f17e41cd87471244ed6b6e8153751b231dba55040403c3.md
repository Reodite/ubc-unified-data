UBC Midwifery proudly shares that Dr\. Cecilia Jevitt, Program Director and Professor, has been named a 2024 Top Scholar by ScholarGPS\.

  
This distinction recognizes Dr\. Jevitt as one of the top 5 percent of lifetime scholars in the field of midwifery worldwide\. ScholarGPS honours researchers whose sustained contributions have advanced their disciplines and helped shape the future of academic scholarship\.

  
Dr\. Jevitt’s leadership and research continue to elevate the impact of midwifery in British Columbia, across Canada, and internationally\. Her inclusion on this global list reflects her longstanding commitment to improving reproductive health, advancing midwifery education, and supporting strong, evidence\-based care for birthing people and families\.  
We congratulate Dr\. Jevitt on this well\-deserved recognition\.
