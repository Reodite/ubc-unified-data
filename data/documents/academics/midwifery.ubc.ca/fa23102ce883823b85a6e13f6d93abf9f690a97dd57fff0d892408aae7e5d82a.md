The UBC Midwifery Program will hold information sessions around the province during the month of October\. The Student Services Manager will first review the program and then present the admissions requirements and application procedures\. A Question and Answer period will follow, with a current student midwife and practicing midwife joining him to answer your questions\. Registration is not required, so please feel free to join us at any of the three following locations:

###### Thursday, October 17\[th\], 2013

###### Location: UBCO Kelowna, BC

###### Time: 3:00 – 5:00 pm  

###### Room  LIB 305 

###### Thursday, October 24\[th\], 2013

###### Location: UNBC Prince George, BC

###### Time: 3:00 – 5:00 pm

###### Room 7\-238 – Weldwood Lecture Theatre

###### Thursday, October 31\[st\], 2013

###### Location: VIU Nanaimo, BC

###### Time: 3:30 – 5:00 pm

###### Building 180 / Room 134
