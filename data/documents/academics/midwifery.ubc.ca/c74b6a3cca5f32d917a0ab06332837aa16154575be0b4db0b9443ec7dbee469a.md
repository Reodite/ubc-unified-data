Biography

Open Close

Alice Luan is a Research Assistant with the Perinatal Wellbeing Research Team within the Midwifery Program in the Department of Family Practice at the University of British Columbia \(UBC\)\. She is a fourth\-year Psychology Honours student at UBC and is interested in the impact of positive and negative daily experiences on aging and well\-being\. Alice hopes to further her studies at the graduate level and pursue a career in health research\. Outside of the lab, she enjoys painting, baking, and going for walks with her dog\.
