Lee may be best known to students for her teaching in the UBC Health Integrated Curriculum\. She is a frequent guest lecturer in midwifery courses and CPD\. Lee is a co\-investigator in the BC Rural Site Visits Project \(RCCbc\) and has been a principal investigator in several other rural research projects\. Lee is the Vice Chair, Inquiry Committee, with the BC College of Nurses and Midwives and a member of the Rural Surgical and Obstetric Networks Advisory Committee, RCCbc\. She received a Distinguished Alumni Award from McMaster University’s School of Rehabilitation Sciences in 2019\. Lee’s leadership moves midwifery forward\!
