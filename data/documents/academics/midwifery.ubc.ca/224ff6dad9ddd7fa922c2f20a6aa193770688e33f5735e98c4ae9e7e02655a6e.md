Named in honour of [Elaine Carty](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/elaine-carty/), a pioneer in midwifery education, Canadian Medical Hall of fame inductee, and the founding Director of the UBC Midwifery Program\. This initiative brings esteemed clinicians and scholars from around the world to UBC\. Visiting scholars engage with students, deliver seminars to faculty, and present public lectures, enriching our academic environment and fostering global collaboration\.

Meet the exceptional scholars we’ve welcomed to UBC through the Elaine Carty Visiting Scholars initiative\. Learn more about their expertise and how they’ve engaged with our students, faculty, and community\.
