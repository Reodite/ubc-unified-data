Role

Open Close

Arash is the Clinical Lab Coordinator in the UBC Midwifery Department\. He specializes in organization and inventory management to ensure our clinical labs for students run smoothly and efficiently\. He is proud to be part of a team that supports the next generation of midwives, helping to create an environment where students can focus on hands\-on learning and clinical excellence\.

Biography

Open Close

Ashnoor is a recent graduate from the University of British Columbia, where he studied Microbiology and Immunology\. He was born in India and moved to Canada with his family when he was three, growing up in North Delta for most of his life\. 

He is passionate about bridging healthcare and education, and is excited to bring that perspective to the Midwifery program and team\. Ashnoor’s hobbies include playing tennis, snowboarding, and watching hockey\.
