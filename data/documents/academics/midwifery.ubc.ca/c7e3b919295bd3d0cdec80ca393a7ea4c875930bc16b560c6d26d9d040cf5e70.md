Bio

Open Close

Maryam Sarfjoo Kasmaei holds a Master of Health Sciences from UNBC and degrees in Civil and Environmental Engineering\. At the Birth Place Lab, she coordinates research, communications, stakeholder engagement, and knowledge mobilization, with a focus on equity, cultural safety, and inclusion\. Her graduate research examined the impacts of COVID\-19 on care aides in long\-term care\. She has published in both health services and engineering, reflecting her interdisciplinary approach\. Outside academia, Maryam is a certified squash coach and referee, passionate about sport as a tool for community and youth well\-being\.
