The UBC Midwifery Program’s Admissions Interviews will take place on Thursday, April 22nd and Saturday, April 24th, 2021\.  Applicants can expect to hear about their interview status near the end of March/early April – whether they have been selected for interviews and, if selected, which time slot/date they have been scheduled\.  All interviews will take place virtually this year so candidates will not be expected to travel to participate\.  More information will follow as it becomes available\.  Stay tuned\!
