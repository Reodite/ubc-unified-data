**Student Services Manager &amp; Advisor**

Email: [aisha\.sallad@ubc\.ca](mailto:aisha.sallad@ubc.ca)

Phone: 604 875 2424 ext\.4046

Pronouns: she/her/hers

Role

Open Close

The Midwifery Student Manager is responsible for overseeing all aspects of student recruitment, advising, educational program planning, and student retention initiatives for two UBC Midwifery programs – the 4\-year undergraduate Midwifery program and the Internationally Educated Midwives Bridging Program \(IEMBP\)\.

Biography

Open Close

Aisha joins UBC Midwifery after working with UBC Go Global in various roles, most recently as an Associate Advisor\. She has provided high\-level support for various Go Global programs, including Inbound and Outbound Exchange, Global Seminars, and the Visiting International Research Students \(VIRS\) program\.

Aisha was an executive member of the UBC Black Caucus in 2023\. The UBC Black Caucus promotes community, progress, and support for Black students, staff, and faculty at UBC\. In this role, she advocated for the safety, well\-being, academic and professional development of Black individuals and supports recruitment, mentorship, retention, and advancement opportunities for Black students at UBC\.
