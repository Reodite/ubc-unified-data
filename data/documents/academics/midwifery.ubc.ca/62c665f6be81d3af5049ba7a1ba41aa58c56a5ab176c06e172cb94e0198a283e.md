Dear UBC Midwifery Program Students, Staff &amp; Faculty,

Please join us for our inaugural Midwifery Student Association \(MSA\) event, our Social Soiree &amp; Annual General Meeting, on Tuesday September 5th at 4pm\. We will have a cocktail &amp; appetizer soiree from 4\-6pm, which all students, staff, and faculty are welcome to attend\. Following this, we will be holding a student\-only Annual General Meeting from 6\-8pm, which we hope all midwifery students can attend\!

For midwifery students, tickets for the event will be $10 at the door, and will include your MSA membership fees for the 2017\-2018 year, plus 2 drink tickets and a plethora of appetizers\. If the fee causes any financial hardship, please email the MSA \(ubc\.msa@gmail\.com\) to discuss, as we hope for all students to be able to attend this event\. For program staff and faculty, we ask you to join us for mingling, drinks and appetizers from 4\-6pm, and will have $5 tickets for drinks and food by donation\.

For those needing childcare, we are able to provide child minding services if necessary\. When RSVP\-ing, please send an email to ubc\.msa@gmail\.com that includes your need for child care, how many children, and their ages\. Though adorable kids are allowed, we ask for this to be a midwifery program\-only event and for you to leave your \(equally adorable\) partners at home 🙂  
For more details and to RSVP, please follow this link: http://evite\.me/BzGrgYF86C

– The Midwivery Students’ Association Executive Board
