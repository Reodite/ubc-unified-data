Photos of Elaine and Ken Carty during her induction into the Canadian Medical Hall of Fame last night in Halifax\. She is the only midwife honored with this fellowship\.

See the CMHF’s amazing tribute to Elaine, describing her many contributions to the professionalization of midwifery in BC, along with a timeline of her career and contributions here: [https://www\.cdnmedhall\.ca/laureates/elainecarty](https://www.cdnmedhall.ca/laureates/elainecarty)\.
