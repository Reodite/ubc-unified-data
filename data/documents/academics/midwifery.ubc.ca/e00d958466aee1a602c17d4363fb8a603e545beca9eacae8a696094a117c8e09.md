Bio

Open Close

Maryam Dehnadi \(she, her\) is a Projects Coordinator, Research at the Birth Place Lab, where she helps bring people, projects, and communication together\. She holds a Master of Public Health and has a background in research, evaluation, and community engagement\. Maryam enjoys bringing structure to complex work, connecting people and ideas, and helping projects move forward\.

Having worked across different health and community settings in Canada and internationally, Maryam has developed a strong interest in health equity and in how research and evaluation can help create health services that are more responsive to the people and communities they serve\. She is especially drawn to collaborative, people\-centred approaches that bring different perspectives and lived experiences into the work\.

At the Birth Place Lab, Maryam is excited to bring her experience and curiosity to research in perinatal care and to contribute to work that can support meaningful change in care, services, and systems\.
