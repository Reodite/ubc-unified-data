Biography

Open Close

Nazanin is a Research Assistant with the Perinatal Wellbeing Research Team within the Midwifery Program in the Department of Family Practice at the University of British Columbia \(UBC\)\. She is currently a fifth\-year UBC Behavioural Neuroscience student\. Nazanin’s research interests lie in mood disorders and mental health, with a particular emphasis on understanding their development, neurobiological mechanisms, and potential therapies\. In her spare time, Nazanin enjoys reading, volunteering, and spending time with friends and family\.
