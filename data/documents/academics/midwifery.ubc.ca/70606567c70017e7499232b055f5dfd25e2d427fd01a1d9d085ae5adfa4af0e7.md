20 new undergraduate students met each other, faculty and staff on September 3rd\. They opened the day by writing a word or symbol on a rock that expressed their hopes and wishes for their midwifery studies and future midwifery practice\. The words included patience, optimism, compassion, faith, resilience, balance and growth\. We wish these all and so much more for the class of 2023\!
