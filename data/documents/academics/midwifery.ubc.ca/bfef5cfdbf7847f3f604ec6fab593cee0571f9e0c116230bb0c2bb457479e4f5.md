## Alumni Birth Art Show\!

**UBC Midwifery Alumni**, please consider loaning us your birth art \(from any of the past 20 years\) and submit your *Birth and Its Meaning\(s\)* artwork for the Birth Art Show, which will be held at this event\! If you have any kid’s art you’d like to showcase in our ‘children’s corner,’ please include that too\!

Submissions will be collected on the Island and in Vancouver\.

**TO SUBMIT:**

Complete the submission form here: [https://form\.jotform\.com/222425419559259](https://form.jotform.com/222425419559259)\. You will need to submit:

1\) a photograph of your piece \(to assist our planning\)

2\) a short artist’s statement to accompany your work at the event \(1\-2 paragraphs\)

You can also email [allison\.campbell@ubc\.ca](mailto:allison.campbell@ubc.ca) if you need an alternate way to submit\.

submission deadline: November 1, 2023

  
We are also seeking photos from your time as a student, midwife, or preceptor \(including with clients and babies\) to contribute to a slideshow at the event\. Please send yours to [tracey\.mason@ubc\.ca](mailto:tracey.mason@ubc.ca)\.  
  
*Please ensure you have received image consent from all subjects in your photos before submitting them to us\.*
