Biography

Open Close

My name is Terry Lyn Evans\. My passion for babies began with me pursuing my degree in nursing at UBC\- I specialized in maternity and began my career at BCWH\-which I loved, leading me to pursue midwifery\- My dream of becoming a midwife was fulfilled in 2002\. Since that time I have had the privilege of practicing at Pacific Midwifery where I am the practice owner\. For many years I was involved with the College of Midwives and past president, where I worked toward ensuring the best care for childbearing families in British Columbia\.  I am also a Clinical associate Professor in the Facility of Medicine/ Department of Midwifery at UBC\.

I continue to be involved in supporting the profession as a Member of UBC Faculty of medicine clinical faculty appointments review committee , the BCWH perinatal review committee and the department of midwifery executive committee\.
