Bio

**Prisha Vaidya** \(she/her\) is a Graduate Research Assistant with the Birth Place Lab and serves as Project Coordinator for the Justice and Equity in Perinatal Services Hub\. She is completing a Master’s degree examining integrative reproductive care in India, with a focus on how healthcare providers navigate institutional, cultural, and social contexts to deliver reproductive care within complex systems\.

Her interests include equitable, accessible, and person\-centred approaches to care, supported by experience in qualitative research and knowledge translation\. Through her work with the Birth Place Lab and the Justice and Equity in Perinatal Services Hub, Prisha contributes to research and initiatives that advance justice, inclusion, and innovation in perinatal health while helping translate evidence for academic, clinical, and community audiences\.
