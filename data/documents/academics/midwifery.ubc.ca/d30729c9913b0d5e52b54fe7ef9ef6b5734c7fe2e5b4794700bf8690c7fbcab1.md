## Explore Your Future in Midwifery

Passionate about maternal and newborn care? Curious about what a career in midwifery could offer? Join us at one of the UBC Midwifery Information Sessions to explore the possibilities and learn how to become a part of this dynamic profession\.

Our information sessions are designed to provide you with a comprehensive understanding of the UBC Midwifery program, admission requirements, and the enriching opportunities that await\.

**Program Overview**: Get an in\-depth look at our Bachelor of Midwifery program, including its curriculum and clinical placements\.

**Admission Insights:** Understand the application process, admission criteria, and key deadlines to help you prepare a strong application\.

**Q&amp;A Session:** Facilitated question\-and\-answer session to address any queries or concerns from the audience\. This will provide clarity and help potential participants make informed decisions\.
