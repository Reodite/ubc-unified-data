**Join us for the Department of Family Practice Residents Research Day – Friday, June 21, 2013**

We invite all members of the Department of Family Practice, including Clinician Scholars, community\-based researchers, midwives, clinical and academic faculty, to this year’s **Residents Research Day** on Friday**, June 21, 2013**\.  Residents from across the province and midwifery students will be presenting their scholarly work on this day\.

Location:  Regent College, 5800 University Boulevard \(intersection: Wesbrook Mall\)

Time:  08:45 – 16:45 \(followed by a reception from 16:45 – 17:45\)

Attached is a draft agenda for Resident Research Day\.

**Faculty and Staff:  Please RSVP through online registration:** [https://docs\.google\.com/spreadsheet/viewform?formkey\=dEF3LUVqQXlhS0EwNEhtTWRiR1hKa0E6MA\#gid\=0](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheet/viewform?formkey=dEF3LUVqQXlhS0EwNEhtTWRiR1hKa0E6MA#gid=0)

**The deadline is June 14\.**
