Student Practice Education, referred to as clinical placement, in British Columbia occurs in multiple health authorities\. Before placement can begin in any health authority, preparation, orientation, and requirements must be fulfilled\.

There is a Health Authority Preparedness guide available to students in CANVAS\. The [BC Student Practice Education website](https://spe.healthcarebc.ca/Pages/Orientation-and-Preparation.aspx) also lists current orientation and preparation items\. 

The Ministry of Health works with BC’s Health Authorities to provide quality, appropriate, and timely health services to British Columbians\.

The Ministry sets province\-wide goals, standards, and performance agreements for health service delivery through the Health Authorities\. Midwifery students are not always mentioned in the student practice guidelines and on health authority materials, but their requirements are applied to all students entering clinical placement\.

-   [Island Health \(Vancouver Island\)](https://www.islandhealth.ca/careers/students/professional-practice/student-practice)
-   [Vancouver Coastal Health](https://www.vch.ca/en/staff-hub/education-development/student-practice-education)
-   [Fraser Health Authority](https://careers.fraserhealth.ca/student-practice-education/)
-   [Interior Health](https://www.interiorhealth.ca/careers/students/student-placement)
-   [Northern Health](https://www.northernhealth.ca/for-health-professionals/student-practice-education)

Separate health organizations within British Columbia’s healthcare system:

-   [Provincial Health Services Authority \(PHSA\)](http://www.phsa.ca/health-professionals/education-development/student-practice-education)
-   [Providence Health Care \(PHC\)](https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/students-internships)
