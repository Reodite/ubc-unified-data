UBC Midwifery relies on clinical faculty preceptors in clinical placement and for clinical skills teaching throughout the year\. Clinical skills teaching provides the opportunity for students to bridge the gap between theory and clinical practice\.

Clinical skills instructors’ substantial service in supporting the education and preparation of the UBC Midwifery student is valued and esteemed\. Thank you for your commitment to teaching future midwives of BC\.

## JaneApp

UBC Midwifery uses JaneApp to track and schedule our clinical skills and OSCEs\. With our program expansion, we are now offering clinical skills teaching at three sites: Vancouver \(BC Women’s Hospital\), Victoria \(Victoria General Hospital/Royal Jubilee Hospital\), and Surrey \(Surrey Memorial Hospital\)\. **Please ensure you click on the correct location when signing up to teach\.** 

Jennifer Nguyen, the Clinical Skills and Simulation Lead, has provided an instructional video below on how to apply using the JaneApp for additional support\.

The Jane App does not now allow you to use the Calendar view intuitively, so **please see the [clinical skills training calendar](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about/clinical-faculty/clinical-skills-teaching/clinical-skills-training-calendar/) for the next schedule** to see which sessions are available each day for sign\-up\.

## Payment Information

Payments for clinical instruction are processed on a Net 30 basis\. Instructors can expect to receive payment approximately 4\-6 weeks after providing instruction\. 

Invoices are not provided for clinical instruction as a payment agreement is established\.  

Payment is via mailed cheques or an electronic funds transfer \(EFT\)\. EFTs are set up with UBC and require a banking institution stamp or signature\.  

As of July 2024, the current teaching rate is $106\.03 per hour\. For the most up\-to\-date rates, please refer to the Faculty of Medicine’s [Appointment Policy and Compensation Terms](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/appointment-policies/) page \(see Compensation Terms\)\.  

Please get in touch with [Marisa\.Ortiz@ubc\.ca](mailto:Marisa.Ortiz@ubc.ca) regarding any other payment or invoicing queries\.
