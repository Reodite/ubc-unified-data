Tuition and fees for the Midwifery Program are approximately $8,000\+ per year\.

The 2025/2026 tuition fees:

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PROGRAM | PER\-CREDIT AMOUNT | FIRST\-YEAR FULL COURSE LOAD | FULL COURSE LOAD TUITION |
| Midwifery | $202\.64 | 32 | $6,484\.48 |

The [2025/2026 student fees](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/fees/student-fees) are approximately $1,200\.00\.

To estimate the current year’s tuition and fees, refer to the UBC cost calculator below\.

[UBC COST CALCULATOR](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/cost/#cost-calculator)

Please visit [UBC Student Services](https://students.ubc.ca/)  for information about finances and financial support\. Here you will find information on:

-   BC Student Loans: types available, qualifications, grant funding via StudentAid BC
-   Workday: tutorials for paying tuition and student fees, course registration, degree planning and more\.
-   Financial Support Options: scholarships, bursaries, grants, financial emergencies,
-   Awards: scholarships, bursaries

[UBC STUDENT SERVICES/FINANCES](https://students.ubc.ca/finances/)

### Grant funding

Some students who apply for student loans will be eligible for grant funding, which does not have to be repaid\. Your eligibility is based on your application\.  
  
Refer to StudentAid BC to view a [list of available programs](https://studentaidbc.ca/explore/grants-scholarships)  to support students with the cost of post\-secondary education\.
