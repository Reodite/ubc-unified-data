## MSN, CNM, DSc, DLitt

**Professor Emerita**

Email: [elaine\.carty@mac\.com](mailto:elaine.carty@mac.com)

Pronouns: she/her/hers

Biography

Open Close

Over the years, Elaine Carty’s research and scholarship work has focused on changing the healthcare system to meet women’s needs\. She has been active in the efforts towards the regulation of midwifery for 40 years, studied the integration of midwives into the BC health care system and was the founding Director of the Midwifery Program at the University of British Columbia\.

Other research focused on early postpartum discharge from the hospital, women’s experiences of long\-term antenatal hospitalization, resources for women with disabilities and the impact of drug advertising on women’s health\.

In recognition of her major contributions to health care for women in Canada she was inducted into the Order of Canada, and into the Order of British Columbia, in 2017\.

In June 2023, Elaine was inducted into the Canadian Medical Hall of Fame \(CMHF\) to recognize her outstanding contributions to and leadership in medicine and health sciences that have led to extraordinary improvements in human health\. The CMHF’s tribute to Elaine, describing her many contributions to the professionalization of midwifery in BC, along with a timeline of her career and contributions are at: [https://www\.cdnmedhall\.ca/laureates/elainecarty](https://www.cdnmedhall.ca/laureates/elainecarty)\.
