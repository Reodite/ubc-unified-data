UBC Midwifery invites our preceptors and faculty to share their voices in celebration of this year’s International Day of the Midwife\.

**Theme:** *Midwives: Critical in Every Crisis*  
We’re gathering short, two\-question micro\-interviews to spotlight the essential role of midwives during times of crisis\. These stories will be featured on our social media, website, and community channels\.

**Deadline to submit:** May 5, 5:00 PM  
**Submit your story here:** [https://bit\.ly/IDM2025CrisisStories](https://bit.ly/IDM2025CrisisStories)

Let’s uplift the voices of those who provide care when it matters most\.
