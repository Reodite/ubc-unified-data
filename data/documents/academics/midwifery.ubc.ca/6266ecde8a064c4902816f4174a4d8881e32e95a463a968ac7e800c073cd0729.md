April is *Caesarean Awareness Month*—a time to raise awareness, share knowledge, and honour the experiences of birthing people who have had caesarean births\.

In this video, we highlight the role of midwives in supporting informed choice, reducing unnecessary caesarean births, and improving outcomes for families across British Columbia\. Grounded in evidence\-based care and respectful collaboration, midwives work alongside clients and health care teams to promote safe, person\-centred birth experiences\.

Learn how midwifery care makes a difference—and why it matters\.
