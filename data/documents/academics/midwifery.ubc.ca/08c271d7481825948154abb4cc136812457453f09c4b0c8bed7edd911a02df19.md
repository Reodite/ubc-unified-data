[Graphic Text International Womens Day 2024](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/IWD-Web-Banner-940x412.png)

This year, as part of the worldwide celebrations of women’s achievements, there are two major themes\. The IWD organization and UBC’s theme is **\#InspireInclusion** to encourage us to imagine a gender\-equal, diverse, equitable and inclusive world\. The Government of Canada’s and United Nation’s themes are **Invest in Women: Accelerate progress**\. Inclusion and progress go so well together\.

UBC’s Faculty of Medicine \(FoM\) highlighting its talent and thought leaders asked FoM women to share their perspectives on the 2024 them **\#InspireInclusion**\. For International Women’s Day, the nominated outstanding women – learners, faculty and staff members who are transforming health for everyone, share their perspectives on the 2024 theme\.

Our director, Kellie Thiessen, nominated UBC Midwifery Professor [Dr\. Saraswathi Vedam](https://midwifery.ubc.ca/2014/02/18/saraswathi-vedam/) to be recognized on International Women’s Day\.

It’s a call to action and a reminder that gender equality is one of the most effective ways to build healthier, prosperous, and inclusive communities\. We all have a role in building a future where everyone can reach their full potential\. It is time to act – because women’s success is everyone’s\. Read on to learn what Faculty of Medicine community members chose to share\. **Web page**: [https://www\.med\.ubc\.ca/news/international\-womens\-day\-2024/](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/international-womens-day-2024/)
