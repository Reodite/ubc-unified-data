Announcement: Kiley Ketchum, registered dietician and third\-year midwifery student and Professor Cecilia M\. Jevitt have recently co\-authored a paper titled “**Evidence\-Based Eating Patterns and Behavior Changes to Limit Excessive Gestational Weight Gain: A Scoping Review**,” published in the International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health \(IJERPH\)\. IJERPH is an open\-access journal published monthly online by MDPI, covering various disciplines such as Global Health, Healthcare Sciences, Behavioral and Mental Health, Infectious Diseases, Chronic Diseases, Disease Prevention, Exercise, Health\-Related Quality of Life, Environmental Health, and Environmental Sciences\.

[Read or download the publication\.](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/01/2023-Ketchum-Jevitt-ijerph-eating-behaviors-to-limit-gestational-weight-gain.pdf)
