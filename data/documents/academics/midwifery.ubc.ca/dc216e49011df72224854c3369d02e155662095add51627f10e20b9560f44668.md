IEMBP Assistant Lead

Email: [ann\.lovold@ubc\.ca](mailto:ann.lovold@ubc.ca)

Pronouns: she/her/hers

Biography

Open Close

*Bio Coming*

With a special interest in international health and the training of health workers, Ann focuses on postpartum hemorrhage prevention and emergency skills training\. Her work has been published in the *International Journal of Obstetrics and Gynaecology*\.

**Education**

McGill University BA in international development and anthropology  
McMaster University BHSc Midwifery  
Johns Hopkins University MPH Reproductive Health and International Health

Role

Open Close

Ann Lovold supports UBC’s Midwifery Undergraduate Program and works closely with internationally educated bridging midwives\. She is also an instructor for the Midwifery Emergency Skills Program\.
