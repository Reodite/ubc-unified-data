Announcement: Professor Cecilia M\. Jevitt and student Kiley Ketchum have co\-authored a paper titled “**Pairing Evidence\-Based Strategies With Motivational Interviewing to Support Optimal Nutrition and Weight Gain in Pregnancy**,” published in The Journal of Perinatal and Neonatal Nursing \(JPNN\)\. Known as the “Pink Journal,” JPNN is a peer\-reviewed publication focusing on labour and delivery, intrapartum services, and perinatal services at large\. The journal is dedicated to advancing evidence\-based practices in perinatal and neonatal nursing\.
