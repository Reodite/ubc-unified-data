Are you passionate about supporting birthing individuals and families through their pregnancy journey? Don’t miss your chance to join the UBC Midwifery Program\! The deadline to submit your UBC application is fast approaching on December 5th

Here’s what you need to know:

-   **Choose Midwifery as your first choice:** Applicants must select UBC Midwifery as their top program choice to access the supplemental application\.
-   **Prerequisites:** Ensure you’re registered in or have completed BIOL 155 \(or equivalent\) and WRDS 150\.
-   **Details matter:** Gather transcripts, highlight your accomplishments, and showcase your suitability for the program in the supplemental application, due January 15th\.

Ready to start your journey? Visit [our How to Apply page](https://midwifery.ubc.ca/program/applying-to-the-program-2/) and take the first step toward making a difference in maternal health\.
