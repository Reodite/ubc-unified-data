**Big news:** as of **January 1, 2026**, midwives join **Canada’s Student Loan Forgiveness Program, unlocking** up to **$30,000 in federal loan forgiveness** for eligible service in rural and smaller communities\. 

This marks a major milestone for the profession and reflects years of advocacy to recognize the essential role midwives play in improving access to care across Canada\. 

## **What’s changed?** 

The federal government has expanded the Student Loan Forgiveness Program, which previously applied only to doctors and nurses, to include **midwives**\. 

Under the updated program, midwives may be eligible for loan forgiveness if they: 

-   Have worked as a midwife for **12 consecutive months** 
-   Have provided at least **400 consecutive hours of service** 
-   Are working in an **eligible community**, defined as: 
-   A rural area, or 
-   A population centre with **30,000 people or fewer** 

Qualified applicants may receive **up to $30,000** in federal loan forgiveness over a maximum of five years\. 

## **Why this matters** 

This change is the result of sustained advocacy by the **Canadian Association of Midwives \(CAM\)** and the **National Council of Indigenous Midwives \(NCIM\)**, who have long called for midwives to be recognized alongside other health professionals in federal retention and recruitment initiatives\. 

In **April 2024**, the federal budget committed to this expansion through legislative changes\. After 18 months, midwives are now included\. 

For students and new graduates, this gives more than financial relief\. It also confirms midwifery as a vital part of Canada’s healthcare workforce, especially in rural, remote, and underserved communities\. 

## **How much forgiveness is available?** 

Eligible midwives can receive loan forgiveness over time, up to a maximum of **$30,000**: 

-   **Year 1:** $4,000 
-   **Year 2:** $5,000 
-   **Year 3:** $6,000 
-   **Year 4:** $7,000 
-   **Year 5:** $8,000 

**Note:** This program applies **only to federal student loans**\. It does not cover provincial or territorial portions, though some provinces and territories may offer their own forgiveness programs\. 

## **Applying for the program** 

Midwives can apply for loan forgiveness after completing **one year of eligible service**\. Applications must be submitted within **90 days** of completing that service period\. 

Because midwives are newly included in the program: 

-   Applications submitted between **January 1, and March 2026** must be completed **by mail\.** 
-   After **March 2026**, applications will be available **online** through the **National Student Loans Service Centre \(NSLSC\)** 

To check eligibility and learn how to apply, visit:   
[**Canada’s Student Loan Forgiveness Program**](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/education/student-aid/grants-loans/repay/forgiveness.html)   
 

To confirm whether your workplace qualifies as an eligible community, use the government’s [**postal code lookup tool\.**](https://tools.canlearn.ca/cslgs-scpse/cln-cln/lfnd-erpm/1-eng.do) 

## **A meaningful step forward** 

For those serving in rural or small communities, this brings **financial relief** and **recognition**\. 

UBC Midwifery celebrates this milestone with our partners\. We thank the organizations that made it possible and encourage midwives and students to explore this new opportunity\.
