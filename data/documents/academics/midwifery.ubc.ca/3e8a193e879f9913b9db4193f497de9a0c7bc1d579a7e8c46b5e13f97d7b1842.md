Three midwifery researchers have been awarded a total of $30,000 in the 2019 Stollery Midwifery Research Awards\. Read details under the Recipients tab about their research in water birth, vaginal birth after cesarean at home and the practice environment for midwives\.

If you’re a Midwife or Midwifery researcher looking for partner\-funds or a jumpstart to your research project, UBC’s Midwifery Program is launching a research fund for Midwifery\-led and Midwifery\-related projects\!

There are going to be about five projects funded each year, with a preference for projects in the range of $5,000\-$15,000\. You can read all about this opportunity on [UBC Midwifery’s Stollery webpage](https://midwifery.ubc.ca/research/stollery/)\. Applications are due November 2nd\. Best of luck to all applicants\!
