There are new voices in our faculty work groups\! Laura Mercer has been promoted to Clinical Assistant Professor \(MIDW\) to represent Midwifery in the Department of Family Practice Clinical Faculty Appointment and Promotions Committee \(DCFAPC\)\.  

The DCFAPC meets two or three times a year, depending on the number of applications received\. The members review applications for promotion to assistant professor\. The members meet via Zoom and are compensated under the CF Compensation Terms Designated Committees\.
