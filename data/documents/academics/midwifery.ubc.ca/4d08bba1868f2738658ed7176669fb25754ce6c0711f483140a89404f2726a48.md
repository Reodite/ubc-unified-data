The Assessment Team is completing the last reviews of all supplemental applications\. The Midwifery Program will be compiling the scores in combination with GPA assessments completed by Enrolment Services, and will be contacting those eligible for Interviews by the end of February/early March\.

Interviews for the 20 seats offered for the Fall of 2014 will take place on one of the following two dates:

**Friday, March 28\[th\], 2014**

**Saturday, March 29\[th\], 2014**

The Midwifery Program thanks everyone for their interest and will be in touch via email soon with the results of this year’s applications\.
