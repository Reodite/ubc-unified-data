The UBC Midwifery Program is hiring clinical faculty to support several courses in the 2025 academic year\. Positions are available across Term 1 and Term 2, with both online and in\-person teaching opportunities\. 

Applicants must be midwives with active or recent clinical practice\. A Master’s degree and teaching experience are strongly preferred\. 

Courses range from introductory theory and applied health sciences to advanced midwifery care\. Tutors are responsible for attending class, supporting students, assisting with grading, and facilitating tutorials, where applicable\. Compensation is $5,333 per 3\-credit course\. 

If you’re interested, please email [allison\.campbell@ubc\.ca](mailto:allison.campbell@ubc.ca) with a brief expression of interest, your relevant experience, and a CV **by July 31****, 2025**\. Positions will be filled as soon as possible\. 

Detailed [course descriptions](https://med-midwifery-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/prospective-students/program-overview/) are available on our website\. Year\-one courses start with a 1, year\-two with a 2, and so on\. 

## **Term 1 \(Sep 2 – Dec 5\)** 

#### **MIDW\_V 103: Applied Health Sciences in Midwifery** 

-   Tuesdays, 1–4 pm \| Online \+ In\-person \(Vancouver, Victoria, or Surrey\) 
-   Course Lead: Cele Jevitt 
-   Responsibilities: Attend class, support students, assist with grading 

#### **MIDW\_V 210: Evidence\-Informed Midwifery** 

-   Thursdays, 9–12 pm \| Online
-   Course Lead: Hamideh Bayrampour 
-   Responsibilities: Attend class, support students, assist with grading 

#### **MIDW\_V 405: Theory for Advanced Midwifery Care** 

-   Thursdays, 9–12 pm \| Online \+ 5\-day intensive in Vancouver 
-   Course Lead: Kim Campbell 
-   Responsibilities: Facilitate tutorial group, support grading 

## **Term 2 \(Jan 5 – Apr 10\)** 

#### **MIDW\_V 108: Theoretical Foundations for Midwifery Practice** 

-   Tuesdays \(online\) &amp; Thursdays \(1–4 pm, in\-person @ BCWH\) 
-   Course Lead: Natalie Johnson 
-   Responsibilities: Attend Thursday sessions in person, support grading
