Students may enter Engineering Physics from first year Science, Science One, or Applied Science at UBC or one of the local transfer colleges\. Typically, students apply for and enroll in first year Applied Science directly from Grade 12, and apply to the Engineering Physics Program during the first year of studies at UBC\.

Students in first year Science or Science One may wish to consult with [the director](https://www.engphys.ubc.ca/contact-us/) to ensure a smooth transition into Applied Science\.

Please direct inquiries concerning placement into engineering programs to Engineering Academic Services, Faculty of Applied Science, Vancouver Campus\.  Phone 604 822\-6556\.

Admission to this Program is based on the student’s prior academic performance combined with broad\-based evaluation which has been in the form of an in\-person interview\.

In Engineering Physics we strongly believe that marks and written statements alone do not provide us with the full picture of whether a student is suited for our program, or whether the Program is the best one to achieve a particular student’s goals\.

Applicants will be required to submit a 5 minute video and attend an in person interview\.  In the meeting, we assess the applicant’s motivation, preparation, and need for entry into our program, as well as try to answer any questions the applicant may have to ensure they are well informed with respect to the challenges associated with Engineering Physics\.

More information and an application are posted on the home page of this web site under “News and Events” early in the new year\.  Applicants will be required to register for a time and day for their interview\.  Interviews are normally conducted in April of each year\.

Admission to UBC is handled centrally by Enrolment Services\.
