A compulsory interview is a mandatory component of the Engineering Physics application process\.  Students spend approximately 30 minutes meeting with Engineering Physics staff and students, in multiple 5 minute meetings\.  There is no need to prepare\.

Applying for Engineering Physics also requires submission of a 5 min video version of your personal statement at the same time that you sign up for an in\-person interview\. Interviews for the September entering class are held in April\.\.

**We may only interview students who have received an email invitation from us or whose first year average over all Math and Phys courses is forecast to be &gt; 80%\.**  

Detailed instructions on applying to Engineering Physics are posted on the Home Page of this web site under “News and Events / Links” early in the new year\.
