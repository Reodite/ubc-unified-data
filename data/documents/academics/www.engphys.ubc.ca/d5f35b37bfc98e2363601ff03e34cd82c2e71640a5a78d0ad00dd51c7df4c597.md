## [2016 Info Session for high school students was held January 24th](https://www.engphys.ubc.ca/2016/01/06/engphys-high-school-info-session-sun-january-24th-2016/)

Over 200 senior high school students and parents came to UBC to hear about Engineering Physics from the program director, Andre Marziali, director of the project lab, Jon Nakane, Bernhard Zender, and students in the program\. Attendance at this popular event increases every year\. Check back in early 2017 for details and sign up\.
