We are committed to providing the highest quality training in radiology to undergraduate medical students and enhancing learning in radiology by employing unique and innovative teaching methods\.

## Teaching Materials

Explore a variety of free and public teaching materials\.

[UBC Radiology App](https://med-fom-radiology-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/spp.jpg)

**UBC Radiology App**

Our innovative medical app includes 500\+ anatomy questions featuring x\-ray, CT, MRI and ultrasound images\. There are also 60\+ fully\-worked clinical cases, 3 comprehensive approaches for reading images and a quiz for learning which tests to order\.

[Learn more](http://www.ubcradiologyapp.ca/)

[Dr\. Bruce Forster with Sectra table](https://med-fom-radiology-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/sectra.jpg)

**Sectra Anatomy Table**

The Sectra table is a unique teaching tool for anatomy and radiology already in use at the University of British Columbia Medical School\.

[Learn more](https://med-fom-radiology-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/education/undergraduate/undergraduate-training-resources/sectra-table/)

[UBC Clock Tower](https://med-fom-radiology-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/06/clock-tower-ubc.jpg)

**Electives**

The UBC Department of Radiology offers a variety of exciting elective rotations for medical students and residents at its various teaching sites in British Columbia\.

[Learn more](https://med-fom-radiology-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/education/undergraduate/undergraduate-electives/)
