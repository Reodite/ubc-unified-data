Thank you for your interest in the Radiology Fellowship programs at the University of British Columbia\.

The table below provides you with the update on the application and selection process for the UBC Radiology Fellowship Programs, along with the application deadlines\. This table is frequently updated, so please check back regularly for the latest updates\.

Please note:

1.  **Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted\.**
2.  The review and selection process begins after the application deadline\.
3.  The shortlisted candidates are usually contacted within 4\-6 weeks after application deadline\.
4.  Applicants are responsible for ensuring that all required documents, including reference letters, are submitted by the deadline\. Due to the high volume of applications, we are unable to follow up with applicants individually\.
5.  For any technical issues, please contact our office at UBCRAD\.Fellowship@ubc\.ca

We appreciate your interest and effort in applying to the UBC Fellowship Programs\.
