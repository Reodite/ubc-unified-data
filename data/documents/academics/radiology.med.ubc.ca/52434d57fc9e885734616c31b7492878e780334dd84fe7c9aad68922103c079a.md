> > #### Site\(s\): – BC Cancer Vancouver

The British Columbia Oncologic Body Imaging  Fellowship Program at British Columbia Cancer Agency \(BCCA\) – Vancouver provides a comprehensive curriculum in Oncologic Body Imaging including Head, Neck, Chest, Abdomen and Musculoskeletal Imaging including procedural skills\.

BC Cancer Agency \(BCCA\) is a world\-renowned cancer center, affiliated with the University of British Columbia \(UBC\) that provides high\-standard subspecialty care for adults with oncologic diseases\.

## **Our Mission**

Our mission is to help interested fellow radiologists around the world to keep abreast of cancer imaging in the era of modern oncologic therapies, becoming experts in imaging interpretation and reporting tumor response criteria in patients undergoing cancer treatment utilizing cancer genomics, checkpoint inhibitors, molecular targeted therapies, and clinical trials\.

**Our Vision**

BC Cancer Agency is one of the largest cancer centers in Canada\. In recent years, rapid advancements in targeted molecular therapy and high throughput genome sequencing methods, resulted in development of modern therapies and imaging response criteria\. Our vision is to provide a comprehensive program to educate and train our current and future radiologists to excel in their roles as an integral part of diagnosis, treatment decisions and cancer management, and provide robust resources to support the next generation of oncologic radiologists\.

**Unique Clinical Experience in Our Site**

BC Cancer Vancouver is home to Canada’s first photon counting computed tomography \(PCCT\) machine\. Installed in April 2025, NAEOTOM Alpha CT scanner using quantum technology offers high\-resolution images with spectral information\. This groundbreaking technology provides oncologic body imaging fellows with unique educational, clinical and research experience by enhancing image resolution, post processing capabilities and improving pathology detection\.

Gaining hands\-on experience with high\-quality imaging while benefiting from enhanced biopsy mode and monoenergetic reconstructions, allow the radiology fellows to refine their skills in performing procedures and interpreting complex cases\.

Fellows can engage in innovative research opportunities and participate in projects that explore PCCT’s roly in oncology\. Gaining training at the forefront of oncologic imaging at BC Cancer, will empower candidates to elevate their future career and gain transformative experience with us\.

The BC Oncologic Body Imaging Fellowship Program provides a broad spectrum of clinical exposure in cancer imaging\. Our Clinical Fellow’s training time is equally divided between head, neck, thoracic and abdominal cancer imaging throughout the year\. Clinical fellows have exposure to a wide array of interventional oncologic procedures\.

**Education and Training Experiences**

Fellows receive one\-on\-one, onsite training from faculty members with various subspecialties and skills\. All of the attending radiologists at BC Cancer Vancouver are fellowship trained in one or more areas including Head and Neck, Neuroradiology, Chest, Abdominal and Musculoskelal imaging\.

We serve a large population sector of British Columbia\-Vancouver, with providing large array of services including:

**MRI**: Approximately 4000 MRI exams are performed each year\. The MRI curriculum focuses on advanced oncologic imaging including head and neck/oropharyngeal and nasopharyngeal cancers, primary and metastatic brain tumors, mesenchymal cancers, GI &amp; GU cancers including hepatobiliary and colorectal cancers, prostate cancer, rectal cancer, female pelvis, whole Body MRI screening in high\-risk settings, and MSK cancers including sarcoma\.

**CT Scan**: Approximately 12000 CT exams are performed per year at BC Cancer Vancouver\. The CT training includes cancer staging and post treatment oncologic follow\-up for head and neck, chest, abdomen and pelvis to monitor treatment response, detect treatment complications and provide tumor response interpretation and follow\-up plan\.

**Ultrasound** : Over 1500 ultrasound studies are performed each year at BCCA\. Ultrasound studies include neck, thyroid, abdominal, male and female pelvic/genitourinary, and superficial soft tissue exams\.

**Procedures**: Over 800 ultrasound and CT guided percutaneous biopsies and drainage procedures are performed over the course of the year with a wide variety of procedures including fiducial markers placement, head and neck lesion biopsy, thyroid, lymph node, lung, mediastinum and chest wall biopsy, abdominal, pelvic, prostate and retroperitoneal biopsies in addition to thoracentesis, paracentesis, lumbar puncture, and chest tube placement\.

**X\-Ray**: Interpreting wide variety of radiographs and performing fluoroscopic imaging including speech language pathology imaging makes BC Oncologic Body Imaging Fellowship Program a comprehensive program\.

The BCCA diagnostic imaging PACS is integrated with BCCA nuclear medicine/ molecular imaging\. Although reporting nuclear medicine exams in not included, the CT PET scans are available to review simultaneously with diagnostic CT and MRI which adds a wealth of knowledge and insight into their training\.

**Additional Rotations:**

1.  Added clinical rotations can also be offered to interested clinical fellows including 1\-week block of clinical rotation in Medical Oncology unit and 1\-week block of clinical rotation in Radiation Oncology unit at the bedside\. This provides a unique opportunity to work directly with the treatment team and offers tremendous benefits in understanding treatment strategies and response management\.

-   Additionally, 2\-weeks block of PET/Molecular imaging at BCCA can be offered electively to interested clinical fellows upon request\.

**Fellow trainee\-Supervisor Interaction:**

Our Fellows at CB Cancer Oncologic Body Imaging Program have the opportunity to work closely with their preceptors on one\-on\-one basis\. Each study is dictated by the clinical fellow,  and subsequently is being read out and reviewed with the assigned attending radiologist before getting finalized\. This gives exceptional opportunity for our fellows to receive guidance and support at the point of care, ensuring high standard of care\.

**Point of Care Evaluation and Feedback**

Ongoing educational feedback is provided by the preceptor after reviewing the case at the point of care, and the educational feedback is accessible to the Fellows through Fluency M\-Modal software for learning and retrospective review\. Using scoring tool in the software allows for providing more objective feedback as well\.

**Other Educational Resources**

Formal radiology lectures are offered as Radiology Grand Rounds, Medical Oncology and Radiation Oncology Grand Rounds and Radiology Academic Half Days\.

**Program Features and Duration**

Our program provides the following opportunities for interested fellow applicants:

1.  Oncologic cross sectional imaging fellowship Program: 1\- year duration, extendable to 2 years, upon successful completion of the first year
2.  Oncologic interventional procedure fellowship program maybe offered after the successful completion of first year of cross sectional imaging for 6 months as part of second year training\.

**Leadership &amp; Teaching Experiences**

There are various leadership experiences available at the BC Oncologic Body Imaging Fellowship Program at BCCA including: attending and presenting in various provincial conferences and multidisciplinary tumour boards, grand rounds and consultation activities\. Interested clinical fellows are also welcome to hold teaching sessions for medical students and teaching rounds for residents\.

**Research Experience**

Our cutting\-edge photon counting computed tomography \(PCCT\) machine at BC Cancer marks a new era for oncological research\. This innovative technology empowers researchers and radiology fellows to conduct advanced imaging studies and participate in groundbreaking research projects\. BC Cancer is poised to lead the way in pioneering research that shapes the future of cancer diagnosis and treatment\.

Various opportunities for oncological research and quality improvement projects are available at BCCA\. Principal investigators and research mentors are available to guide and support interested fellows to engage in multiple research projects and excel their skills in designing a proposal, obtaining REB approval, conducting the research, writing the manuscript, submitting and presenting  projects in world class seminars and conferences\.

Presenting in seminars and conferences including Canadian Association of Radiologists \(CAR\) and Radiological Society of North America \(RSNA\) is supported by offering 5 days leave to attend the conferences\. 

Research opportunities are also available in the field of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning\. Clinical fellows are encouraged to participate and engage in this developing and dynamic field\.

**Clinical Responsibilities**

-   Clinical coverage responsibilities include providing imaging services on weekdays, evenings, holidays and weekends at the BCCA\.
-   Clinical Fellows cover contrast injections, clinical consultations, image interpretation and procedural activities\.
-   Participating and presenting in provincial and regional multidisciplinary tumor boards are encouraged\.

**Multidisciplinary Tumour** **Conferences**

BCCA\-Vancouver is a home for comprehensive Tumor Boards\. Each month we are presenting in at least 44 Tumor Board Conferences which is one of the most educational and informative activities\.

Our skilled radiologists provide medical imaging expertise at numerous provincial and regional multidisciplinary tumour conferences each year\. The conferences are held regularly at BCCA with tumor specific multidisciplinary team with participation of radiation oncologists, surgical oncologists, medical oncologists, oncologic imaging radiologists and pathologists\. Complex cases are reviewed to optimize treatment strategy and therapeutic plan\. Multidisciplinary weekly conferences include head and neck tumor board, stereotactic brain conference, nervous system tumor board, gastrointestinal tumor board, genitourinary tumor board, lung cancer conference, lymphoma tumor board, melanoma tumor board and spine tumor board, stereotactic cancer panel etc\.

 **Clinical Trials**

BCCA\- Vancouver is a tertiary care cancer center participating in a wide range of multicenter clinical trials\.  Radiologists and clinical fellows play an important part in staging assessment, tumor measurement, and response evaluation, which provide an excellent learning opportunity for the clinical fellows\.

**Personalised Onco\-Genomics \(POG\) Program**

BC Cancer’s Personalized Onco\-Genomics \(POG\) program is a clinical research initiative with the goal of integrating the information from genomic sequencing into diagnostic and treatment planning for patients with cancer\. Interested Fellows are welcome to explore this area if interested\.

**Progress Evaluation and Feedback Process**

The point of care assessment and feedback is provided through Fluency M\-Modal software integrated into our reporting system\. Scoring evaluation is also available for quality and quantity assessment\.

The curriculum and schedules are provided to our clinical fellows at the start of the fellowship with the list of clinical responsibilities\.

 The Fellow trainees progress is evaluated on a weekly basis for the first three months by providing formal feedback to the trainees\.

After the first 3 months, the evaluation and feedback will be offered every month to the fellow\. The areas of improvement will be identified, and suggestions for improvement will be offered in person\. Upon successful completion of the fellowship program,  certificate of completion will be provided to the Fellows\.

**Probation Period**

At the end of 3 months, the clinical fellows who have successfully met the clinical and professional expectations will be offered to continue the fellowship program for the rest of the period\. In case their performance is above expectations there is an opportunity for offering an additional clinical fellowship in next year\. 

**Eligibility &amp; Requirements**

Applicants should meet the criteria for applying in clinical radiology fellowship program at the UBC including:

-   English Proficiency Certificate
-   Source Verified Medical Diploma and Radiology Specialist Certificate
-   Eligible to work in Canada
-   Good standing from practicing jurisdiction
-   3 Recommendation letters
