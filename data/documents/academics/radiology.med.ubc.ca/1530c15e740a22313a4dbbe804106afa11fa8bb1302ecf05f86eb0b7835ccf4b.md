Medical students wishing to participate in radiology\-related research are encouraged to reach out to one of the following faculty, depending on their interests\.

If projects can be completed remotely, there is no requirement for the supervising radiologist to be at the same site as the student\.

Radiologists also supervise UBC FLEX projects \(MEDD 419, MEDD 429, MEDD 449\)\. Students should follow their program instructions\.

Radiologists are also available to supervise students participating in the [Summer Student Research Program \(SSRP\)](https://www.med.ubc.ca/current-learners/summer-student-research-program/)\.

Undergraduate education is supported by the Undergraduate Radiology Education Committee \(UREC\)\. The mandate of this committee is to oversee the development, deployment and administration of the radiology curriculum in the MDUP\. This committee is comprised of faculty from across the province as well as residents\. 

Faculty interested in teaching or becoming more involved in the program are encouraged to send us an email\.
