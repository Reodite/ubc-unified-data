## **Message from Department Head**

We are a world class university radiology department with a collaborative, equitable and diverse community that is recognized for excellence in clinical care, education, research, and innovation\. A place where everyone feels valued and proud to work\.

*We are blessed with a diverse UBC Radiology department\. Our faculty is diverse in so many ways including areas of focus and interest with all making unique and important contributions to our department and helping us fulfill our mission\.*

*We are extremely grateful for our large and strong clinical faculty team – in addition to their health professional role, the clinical faculty members are committed to teaching and inspiring learners, supporting translational and clinical research, pursuing professional development, and providing administrative leadership\.*

*We acknowledge the significant contributions our clinical faculty have made to the Department – your support and contributions are fundamentally important to our radiology community and university\!* *We’ll work together with each of you, and I am here, committed to your growth as clinical faculty, and to help ensure you achieve your academic and professional goals\.*  
  
**Jonathon Leipsic, M\.D\., F\.R\.C\.P\.C\., M\.S\.C\.C\.T,**  
**Professor and Head, Department of Radiology**

## Our Mission

At the University of British Columbia Department of Radiology, we strive for excellence in radiology education and research, collaborating with our department members to improve the lives of patients both locally and globally, and are committed to improving equity, diversity, and inclusion in all aspects of what we do\.

## Our Vision

We are a world class university radiology department with a collaborative, equitable and diverse community that is recognized for excellence in clinical care, education, research, and innovation\. A place where everyone feels valued and proud to work\.
