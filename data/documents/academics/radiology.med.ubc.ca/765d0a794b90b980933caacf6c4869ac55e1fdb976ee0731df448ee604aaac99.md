We aim to offer educational medical imaging resources to developing and underserved areas of the world, while serving as a hub for exchanging information about outreach opportunities and maintaining partnerships with other global health organizations, sharing resources and facilitating on\-site projects where appropriate\.

## **Mission**

Technology and the internet have made our world smaller\. News is shared within seconds, and disasters impacting human health, frequently unknown in the past, have become an everyday reality\.  Due to technological advances, the global medical community is consistently growing, and the need for global cooperation is urgent\.

The University of British Columbia \(UBC\) Department of Radiology strives to strengthen the global medical community by providing training and improving access to Diagnostic Imaging\. While UBC has an established role of bringing in radiologists across the globe for fellowship training, there is a growing need for outreach initiatives in the low\-resource communities both domestically and internationally\.

## **Goals**

### Collaboration

-   Create a hub for exchange of outreach experiences and opportunities among UBC radiologists, trainees and supporting personnel
-   Promote and support clinical outreach programs and initiatives within UBC Faculty of Medicine Departments beyond Radiology
-   Seek and maintain collaboration with other institutional chapters of the Canadian Radiology Global Outreach Program \(CRGOP\), as well as similar national and international global health organizations, such as Medicine sans Frontiers \(MSF\), RAD AID, and Radiology across Borders \(RAB\)

### Education

-   Provide lectures and other education resources in medical imaging to radiologists and technologists in the low\-resource communities in Canada and internationally
-   Participate in conferences aimed to improve global health
-   Involve medical students, residents and fellows to foster a passion for outreach and teaching

### On\-site projects

-   Seek and share opportunities for the on\-site domestic and international Radiology projects, including diagnostic/skills workshops and clinical work\.
-   Navigate funding options and grants to support trainee participation in on\-site visits

### Vietnam Visit 2026

### Pacific Symposium

The Pacific Symposium, held annually in Fiji, is a joint venture between UBC RGOP and Radiology Across Borders \(RAB\), in collaboration with our Fijian radiology colleagues\. The event brings together clinicians, radiologists, technologists, and trainees for interactive medical imaging lectures and hands\-on workshops held over multiple days\. **The next Pacific Symposium will be in October 2026 on the topic of Emergency Radiology\!**

### Fiji Case Review Rounds Project

This is a joint project between our Fijian radiology colleagues and the RGOP radiology residents born from the Pacific Symposium\. The objective is to share lectures and interesting cases between the two groups, to strengthen both program’s residency training, and provide a safe resident\-led space to practice taking cases, learn from mistakes, and to serve as “consultants” for each other\. Beyond the immediate learning potential,  the project strengthens the longitudinal relationship between our two countries and serves as a launchpad for future collaboration and resident\-led initiatives\.

### ICRF Physics Cases

Have you encountered physics\-related artifacts in your imaging studies while reporting? Please consider submitting cases and contributing to the International Certificate in Radiology Fundamentals \(ICRF\) initiatives\. 

The ICRF is an innovative, one\-year online certificate program created by Radiology Across Borders \(RAB\) in collaboration with the University of British Columbia Radiology Global Outreach Program \(RGOP\)\. The course is tailored for clinicians, radiographers, and sonographers alike internationally by teaching the core principles of radiology to improve healthcare\. 

As part of the course design/delivery team, we are looking to build a teaching archive of real\-world radiology artifacts for the physics module of the ICRF program\. This module covers the fundamentals necessary to understand how medical imaging equipment operates, including key topics of radiation physics &amp; properties, interaction with matter, different imaging modalities, and safety &amp; protection\. 

Please consider submitting any **ANONYMIZED CASES** that:

-   Clearly demonstrate a specific physics principle from any of the different imaging modalities
-   Include representative images with brief clinical context
-   Optional: highlight a clear teaching “take\-home” point

Cases do not need to be rare or complex\. Our emphasis is on clarity and educational value\. Some examples: beam hardening artifact, motion artifact, metal artifact, ring artifact, partial volume artifact, and so on\! 

Please submit cases to the following link: [https://forms\.gle/ryyT6tJXMAcsno1d9](https://forms.gle/ryyT6tJXMAcsno1d9) 

Thank you for your time and your contribution to our educational and humanitarian initiatives to improve healthcare internationally\.
