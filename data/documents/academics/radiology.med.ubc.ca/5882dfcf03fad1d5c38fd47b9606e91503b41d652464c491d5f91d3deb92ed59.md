Ahead of the new academic year, [the UBC Resident Wellness Office \(RWO\)](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/) has put together a helpful guide containing information on the various wellness resources available to you to support you throughout your residency\.

Please see the **[2022\-23 RWO Wellness Resource Guide](https://med-fom-radiology-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/06/RWO-Wellness-Resources-PDF-2022-2023.pdf)** for more information about the RWO\. You can also find the guide linked on the RWO website [here](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/resources/resident-wellness-resources-guidebook-pdf/)\.

Thank you and if you have any questions, please feel free to reach out to me or the RWO directly: [resident\.wellness@ubc\.ca](mailto:resident.wellness@ubc.ca)
