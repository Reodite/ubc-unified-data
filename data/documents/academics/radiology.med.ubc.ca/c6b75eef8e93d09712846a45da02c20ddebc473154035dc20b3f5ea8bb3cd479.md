#### Site: Royal Columbian Hospital \(RCH\)

## Overview

The RCH Breast &amp; Body Imaging Fellowship is a dedicated 12\-month subspecialty program within the UBC Department of Radiology, delivering training at one of British Columbia’s highest\-volume tertiary centers\. The fellowship integrates full\-spectrum clinical training across all breast &amp; body imaging modalities and interventional procedures, multidisciplinary tumor board participation, and teaching opportunities\. The fellow will divide the year equally between breast and body radiology, with the opportunity to spend elective time in other subspecialties, if desired\.

Fellows will develop expertise across all breast imaging modalities — including mammography, breast ultrasound, and breast MRI\. Fellows will also develop skills across the spectrum of body imaging, including plain films, ultrasound, CT, dual\-energy CT and MRI \(including hepatobiliary, pancreatic, adrenal, renal, bowel, rectal, pelvic, and soft tissue pathology\)\.

Fellows acquire competence in the full range of image\-guided breast and body procedures, including ultrasound\-guided biopsy/FNA, fluoroscopic procedures, stereotactic/tomosynthesis\-guided biopsy, MRI\-guided biopsy, wire and wireless/seed localization, specimen radiography, and radiologic–pathologic concordance assessment\.

Multidisciplinary breast and tumor rounds participation offer the opportunity for fellows to present complex cases and collaborate with other subspecialties\. The call commitment includes 1 weekend of daytime call per month, with no overnight work\. Fellows receive up to 20 days of vacation per year\. Opportunities for moonlighting will be available, pending credentialing/licensing approval\.

The program places a strong emphasis on building a broad range of skills in breast and body imaging in a high\-volume, high\-complexity centre\. The program offers a unique opportunity to become a confident, versatile radiologist and a strong asset to future groups\.

## **Fellowship**

Fellowship candidates must have:

  
■ a Medical Degree and Specialty Diploma in Radiology OR

  
■ will be completing an Accredited Residency Program in Diagnostic Radiology and will be eligible for licensure by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia

  
All qualified applicants are encouraged to apply, however, Canadian and permanent residents of Canada will be given priority\.  
Important note: Medical Council of Canada Evaluating Examination \(MCCEE\) is not required\.
