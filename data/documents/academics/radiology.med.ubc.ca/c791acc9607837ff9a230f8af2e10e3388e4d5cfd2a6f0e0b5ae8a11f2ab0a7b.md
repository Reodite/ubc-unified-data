### **Please read carefully before you start the application**\.

\(At bottom of the form there is a ‘save and continue later’ option if you need more time to gather information\)\.

1.  Applicants are only allowed to apply for a **maximum** of **2 fellowship programs** for each academic year\. Please note that multiple applications will be deleted\.
2.  All uploaded files must be in **PDF\.**
3.  The email address provided will be used for all communications before, during and after your fellowship\. Please provide us with a **permanent** email address\.
4.  For the **International Applicants**: Please note that although a passing ELP \(English Language Proficiency\) score is not required at the time of application, priority will be given to the applicants who have a valid [English Proficiency Certificate](https://www.cpsbc.ca/files/pdf/REG-ELP.pdf)\. \(Please note a score of 7 has to be achieved in all categories\)\. Note: Your English Language Proficiency Certificate is valid for 2 years from the date of issue\.
5.  Due to the high volume of applications, unfortunately, the incomplete applications will not be reviewed\. Please make sure you fill in required fields and upload all the required documents\. As an applicant, it is your responsibility to ensure that the application is completed correctly\. This office will NOT be contacting applicants regarding any incomplete information or documents\.

NOTE: following up with your referees to email the Letters of Recommendation \(LOR\) directly to the provided email address is also your responsibility as an applicant\. Ask your referees to make sure:

-   **The LORs are sent before the indicated deadline\.**
-   **Your name and last name are in the subject line of the email\.**
-   **The letter is informal letterhead \(letter in a plain word document won’t be accepted\)**

#### Due to the high volume of applications **ONLY** **short\-listed** applicants will be contacted and unfortunately this office won’t be able to communicate with the rest of applicants\.
