Science One is an immersive 28\-credit program that combines different subjects—Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Physics—in a unified, integrated format\. Science One is built around community, a place where you can immerse yourself in science\. Being a small, cohort based program, Science One is a place to meet people who want more out of their science education\. 

### [What is Science One?](https://scienceone.ubc.ca/what)

Science One is an innovative program that focuses on the connections among core science disciplines\. It presents both foundational science content and the tools and approaches of modern scientific research – within a single course\.

[Learn more about Science One](https://scienceone.ubc.ca/what)

### [Why Science One?](https://scienceone.ubc.ca/why)

Science One is a tight\-knit community of learners, a place where you can connect with like\-minded peers who are supported by a team of dedicated instructors\. Its enriched curriculum includes dedicated classes on science literacy and computer programming, mentored research projects, guest speakers, student conferences, and field trips\.

[Learn more about what makes Science One different](https://scienceone.ubc.ca/why)

### [Apply to Science One](https://scienceone.ubc.ca/apply)

The Science One class is limited to only 75 students, facilitating peer\-to\-peer learning and support\. Admission to UBC Faculty of Science \(Vancouver Campus\) is a requirement for admission to Science One\.

[Learn how to apply to Science One](https://scienceone.ubc.ca/apply)
