## End of First Term Scholarships

### [Abdul Ladha Scholarship](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/award-search/vancouver/faculty-science/science-one-program/4619?destination=enrolment/finances/award-search/result%3Fcampus%3DVancouver%26faculty%3DSCIE%26dept%3DSCIG%26level%3DUNGR%26type%3DAll%26name%3D%26id%3D)

-   Current value: Please refer to the awards search page
-   Award \#: 04619

Offered to a student entering the Science One Program from a B\.C\. secondary school, with preference given to a student from King George Secondary School in Vancouver and then to a student from any secondary school in the Burnaby School District\. An additional scholarship of may be offered in some years, without geographic restriction, to any student entering Science One from secondary school\. The awards are made on the recommendation of the Director of Science One\.

## End of Academic Year Scholarships

### [Michael Rust\-Smith Science One Memorial Scholarship](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/award-search/vancouver/faculty-science/science-one-program/4444?destination=enrolment/finances/award-search/result%3Fcampus%3DVancouver%26faculty%3DSCIE%26dept%3DGeneral%26level%3DUNGR%26type%3DAll%26name%3DMichael%2520Rust-Smith%26id%3D04444)

-   Current value: Please refer to the awards search page 
-   Award \#: 04444

Offered to an outstanding student who has completed the Science One Program\. Preference is given to a student who has embraced the philosophy of Science One, where intellectual growth comes through the expression of creativity and an inquisitive mind; and who has demonstrated a passion for the natural sciences\. High academic standing commensurate with a scholarship is expected, but is not the primary criterion for this award\. The award is made on the recommendation of the Science One teaching team in consultation with the Director of the Science One Program\. A $1,000 scholarship has been endowed by the family and friends of Michael Rust\-Smith, a Science One student who lived a full and joyous life, and who was fascinated by science and the natural world\.

### [Larry Roberts Science One Memorial Scholarship](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/finances/award-search/vancouver/faculty-science/science-one-program/4415?destination=enrolment/finances/award-search/result%3Fcampus%3DVancouver%26faculty%3DSCIE%26dept%3DAll%26level%3DUNGR%26type%3DAll%26name%3DLarry%2520Roberts%2520Science%2520One%2520Memorial%2520Scholarship%26id%3D04415)

-   Current value: Please refer to the awards search page 
-   Award \#: 04415

Made on the recommendation of the Science One teaching team in consultation with the Director of the Science One Program\. Preference is given to candidates who have achieved high academic standing and who have embraced the philosophy of the Science One Program, where intellectual growth comes through the expression of creativity and an inquisitive mind\. Endowed by family and friends in memory of Larry Roberts for outstanding students who have completed the Science One Program\.
