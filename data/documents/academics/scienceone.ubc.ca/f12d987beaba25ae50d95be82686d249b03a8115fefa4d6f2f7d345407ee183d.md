```text
Science One Sessional Lecturer Position
Job Posting Date: August 7, 2026
Application Deadline: August 20, 2026

The Faculty of Science invites applications for a part-time Sessional Lecturer position to teach the
Scientific Thinking and Literacy component of Science One in 2026 Winter Term 1 and Term 2.

Term 1 runs September 8 – December 22, 2026; Term 2 runs January 5 – April 27, 2027.

Course Description:

The core of Scientiﬁc Thinking & Literacy (STL) is a set of 12-15 one-hour lessons integrated into
Science One, an intensive multidisciplinary first-year program. These lessons were developed by
previous instructors and deal with the soft aspects of science that are often missed in ﬁrst year,
including the types of literature, scientiﬁc writing, ethics, presenting at conferences, etc.
STL lessons support two capstone projects in which students explore a scientific discovery and
design and conduct an original experiment, both culminating in student-led conference-style
presentations.

Job Description:

Responsibilities include teaching STL lessons at flexible times within the Science One schedule;
providing written and occasionally oral feedback on student work; holding regular office hours;
mentoring and evaluating approximately 12–15 students per term on their capstone projects;
collaborating with the instructional team on course schedule and other STL-related matters;
coordinating STL-related activities including capstone projects, project presentations, and guest
speakers; attending weekly instructional team meetings.

The workload is equivalent to approximately 1.5 credit per term.

Qualifications:

Candidates should have a Master or PhD in any field of science, and relevant teaching experience at
the post-secondary level, ideally with experience teaching writing or communication. Experience with
the Canvas learning management system is an asset.

How to apply:

Applications should include an up-to-date curriculum vitae and cover letter. Names of references
will be requested from short-listed candidates. Applications should be submitted to
science1@mail.ubc.ca. For any questions, please email our program administrator at
science1@mail.ubc.ca.
```
