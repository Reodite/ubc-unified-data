#### Thanks to the generosity of external donors and UBC, the Biology Program is able to offer a number of awards to our undergraduate students\. The Biology Program is responsible for nominating its students for these awards each Spring and Fall\.

#### **General Student Eligibility:** A student must be registered in a full course load of 24 credits for the session of the award, attain a minimum of a 75% average, and have no failed courses\.  Please read the [Regulations Governing University Awards](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/student-awards) for the full list of eligibility requirements for undergraduate awards\.

#### To view award descriptions, please visit the [UBC Award Search](https://tools.students.ubc.ca/award-search/)\.

Fall Award Applications open until **Sept 20th 2026\.** To apply, please visit [our Qualtrics form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_bPmfv0hFW0txczQ)\.
