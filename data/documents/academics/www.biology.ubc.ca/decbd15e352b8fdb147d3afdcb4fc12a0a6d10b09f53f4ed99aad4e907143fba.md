Read the UBC Calendar policy on Academic Concessions if you have experienced unanticipated events or circumstances that interfere with your ability to accomplish academic work\. [This self\-declaration form](https://sandbox-biologyprogram.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/03/Academic-Concession-Form-2021W-v2.pdf) may be used to request academic concession outside of those requiring traditional  
documentation\. It is student’s responsibility to fill out the Academic Concession Form and send it to their course instructor to request an Academic Concession\.

[Read More](https://biologyprogram-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/academic-resources/)

[For academic questions, advisors are available in](https://sandbox-biologyprogram.sites.olt.ubc.ca/learning-and-wellbeing-resources/academic-resources/#) [Biology](http://www.biology.ubc.ca/), [Science](https://science.ubc.ca/students/advising), and [other UBC Faculties](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-advising)\.  
  
For finance and student life questions, try contacting your [Enrolment Services Professional \(ESP\)](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors) or an [International Student Advisor\.](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/international-student-advising)
