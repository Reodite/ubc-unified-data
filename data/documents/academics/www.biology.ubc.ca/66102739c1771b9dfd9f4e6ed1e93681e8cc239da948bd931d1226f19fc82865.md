## Prospective Biology Students

**Once accepted into UBC Science \([click here for information on Applying to UBC](https://www.ubc.ca/prospective-students/)\), students apply to be placed in the Major of their choice at the end of Year One\. Science Advising admits students into Year 2 Biology\. Biology accepts new students from other programs into Year 3 directly\. More information on Admission to 2nd Year, 3rd Year Biology, Transfer students, Minor in Biology below\.**

### Admission to 2nd Year Biology

***UBC Science runs a coordinated admissions process for second\-year specializations\. All eligible students must take part in this application process\.***  
  
All Majors, Combined Majors, and a few second\-year declarable Honours and Combined Honours specializations can be applied to via this application\.

[Read More](https://science.ubc.ca/students/degree/apply)

### Admission to 3rd Year Biology &amp; Honours

Biology has two application periods for admission into the 3rd Year Biology program – May and August\.   
**May admission** is open to students who have all their requirements for Year 3 in Biology completed by April\.  
**August admission** is open to students who complete all their requirements for Year 3 in Biology completed by August\. More information below\.

[Read More](https://www.biology.ubc.ca/admissions/3rd)

### Transfer Students

Qualified students attending a post\-secondary institution are welcome to apply for transfer to UBC\.

### Biology Minor

A Biology minor consists of 18 upper\-level BIOL credits \(i\.e\. 3xx or 4xx level courses\) and the lower\-level prerequisites required to take the upper\-level courses\.

[Read More](https://www.biology.ubc.ca/admissions/biology-minor)
