## Student Opportunities

### Biological Student Society \(BIOSOC\)

BIOSOC is the undergraduate student society for Biology students\. Join in on their events and activities\!

[Read More](https://www.zoology.ubc.ca/biosoc/blog/)

### Undergraduate Research Opportunities

There are many ways to gain research experience as an undergraduate Biology student\. Read more about it\!

[Read More](https://biologyprogram-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/undergraduate-research-opportunities/)

### Science Co\-op

A Co\-op placement is a great way to gain work experience during your studies\.

[Read More](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/)

### Biology Career Exploration

Wondering what you can do with a career in Biology? Check out this resources page with Student Services\.

Our UBC Biology Alumni have a broad range of career paths and use their transferable skills to contribute to a various roles\. Check out some stories in the blog below\.

[Read More](https://blogs.ubc.ca/biologyalumni/)

[Read More](https://bamfieldmsc.com/education)
