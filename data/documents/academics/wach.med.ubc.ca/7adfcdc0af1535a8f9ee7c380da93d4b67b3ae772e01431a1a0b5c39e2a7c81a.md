Endometriosis affects nearly 1 in 10 women in Canada and an unmeasured number of gender diverse people\.

Dr\. Paul Yong is helping ignite change for thousands of people faced with this painful disorder\. Dr\. Yong is a supervisor in the WACH program, and works as a gynaecologist offering surgery and interdisciplinary care at BC Women’s Centre for Pelvic Pain and Endometriosis\.

Learn more about the work of Dr\. Yong’s team, including WACH graduate students, from the this [feature story](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/endometriosis-towards-better-diagnosis-and-treatment) by Faculty of Medicine Communications\.
