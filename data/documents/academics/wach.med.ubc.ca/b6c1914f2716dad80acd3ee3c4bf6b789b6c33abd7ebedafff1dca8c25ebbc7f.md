[https://med\-fom\-wach\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/congratulations\-to\-claire\-lawrence\-on\-her\-successful\-msc\-defense/lawrence\-claire\-2/](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/congratulations-to-claire-lawrence-on-her-successful-msc-defense/lawrence-claire-2/)[Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/07/Lawrence-Claire-150x150.jpg)Congratulations to Claire, who completed her MSc defense last week\!

Claire is the first WACH student to defend their thesis\.

Claire’s thesis project was: “Evaluation of the effectiveness of a web\-enabled psychoeducational intervention for postpartum mental health for women in British Columbia\.” She was co\-supervised by Dr\. Lori Brotto and Dr\. Genevieve Breau\.

We asked Claire what her top 5 experiences were during the MSc program:

> 1\. Having the opportunity to conduct behavioural research that impacted real people in their daily lives\.  
> 2\. Taking classes that specifically related to my intersecting interests in women’s health and mental health \(e\.g\., WACH 512: Women’s Mental Health across their Lifecycle, WACH 503/SPPH 537: Perinatal Epidemiology\)  
> 3\. Earning a [Canada Graduate Scholarship – Master’s](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/canada-graduate-scholarships-masters-cgs-m-program) award for my thesis project proposal\.  
> 4\. Being a part of the exceptional [UBC Sexual Health Lab](https://brottolab.med.ubc.ca/) and providing mentorship to research assistants\.  
> 5\. Getting to present my research at the Canadian National Perinatal Research Meeting in 2022 and 2023\.
