Zeba Khan, with PhD supervisor Dr\. Paul Yong

Congratulations to Zeba Khan, WACH PhD student, on being awarded one of the inaugural WHRI Graduate Student Achievement Awards\!

This award recognizes Zeba’s academic excellence in completing her Master’s research project, Contraception Options and Reproductive Access \(CORA\) for Newcomer Youth in Canada: A Qualitative Knowledge Translation Investigation, in 2023\.

Zeba received the award at the [WHRI Annual Women’s Health Research Symposium](https://whri.org/news-events/annual-womens-health-research-symposium/) on March 8\.

*To learn more about the WHRI Graduate Student Achievement Award, please visit: [https://whri\.org/our\-services/whri\-graduate\-student\-achievement\-award](https://whri.org/our-services/whri-graduate-student-achievement-award)\. Please note that in order to apply, trainees must be a [WHRI student/trainee member](https://whri.org/become-a-member/)\.*
