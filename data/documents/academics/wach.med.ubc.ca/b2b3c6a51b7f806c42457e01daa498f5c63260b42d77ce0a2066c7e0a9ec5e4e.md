Congratulations to Erica Qureshi, one of the 2024\-25 recipients of the [IODE War Memorial Scholarship](https://www.iode.ca/express-news/war-memorial-scholarship-winners5584851)\!

[https://med\-fom\-wach\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/?attachment\_id\=2000](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?attachment_id=2000)[Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/Qureshi-300x300.jpg)

Erica is a student in the MD\-PhD program, where her doctoral research focuses on the pediatric emergency department\. By designing and evaluating programs to better connect pediatric emergency medicine and community healthcare, Erica aims to improve patient outcomes while optimizing emergency department resources\. Erica is supervised by Dr\. Quynh Doan\.

Founded in 1900, IODE Canada is a national women’s charitable organization dedicated to enhancing the quality of life for individuals through education support, community services and citizenship programs\. Recipients of the IODE War Memorial Scholarships are selected for academic achievement, potential, and volunteerism\.

*The IODE War Memorial Scholarship competition is currently open\. Applications are due October 1\. View detailed eligibility and application procedures [here](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/iode-war-memorial-scholarship), and stayed tuned for updates and reminders from [@ubcWACH](https://twitter.com/ubcWACH)\.*
