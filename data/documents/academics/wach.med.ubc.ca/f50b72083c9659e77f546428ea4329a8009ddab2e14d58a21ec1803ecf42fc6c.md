Congratulations to Taylor Ricci and Dwayne Tucker, who were awarded Affiliated Fellowships for 2022\-23\!

**[Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/07/Photo-Ricci-150x150.jpg)Taylor Ricci  
**MSc student

Research project: Mechanism of Endothelial Dysfunction at Diabetes DiagnosisSupervisor: Angela Devlin

**[Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/07/Photo-Tucker-150x150.jpeg)Dwayne Tucker  
**PhD candidate

Research project: Clinical and Molecular Predictors of Pain\-related Outcomes in EndometriosisSupervisors: Paul Yong, Aline Talhouk

*The Affiliated Fellowships \([Master’s](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/affiliated-fellowships-masters-program) and [Doctoral](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/affiliated-fellowships-doctoral-program)\) are awarded to meritorious students for full\-time study and/or research leading to a graduate degree\. Students either apply through the Canada Graduate Scholarship competitions, if eligible, or directly to the graduate program\. Application deadlines will be communicated by the WACH program\. You can also stay tuned for updates and reminders from [@ubcWACH](https://twitter.com/ubcWACH)\.*
