The WACH Student Organization is a student\-run organization that coordinates social events, wellness initiatives, and more\.

Check out some of the events they ran in 2023\-24\!

You can also follow **ubcwachso** on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ubcwachso/)  [Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/03/Instagram-Icon-150x150.png) or reach them at [wach\.social@ubc\.ca](mailto:wach.social@ubc.ca)\.

**WACH Us Move Challenge[https://med\-fom\-wach\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/?attachment\_id\=1750](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?attachment_id=1750)[Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/WACHSO-2024-1-150x150.png)**

A fun, week\-long challenge to promote student wellness through daily activities\. Students could participate daily for a chance to win exciting prizes and we received lots of submission via Instagram\! There was a special in\-person event as well for students to be active together\!

**WACH Journal Club[https://med\-fom\-wach\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/?attachment\_id\=1754](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?attachment_id=1754)[Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/WACHSO-2024-5-150x150.png)**

Discussed the paper, “Willingness to Self\-Collect a Sample for HPV\-Based Cervical Cancer Screening in a Well\-Screened Cohort: HPV Focal Survey Results\.” It was an in\-person casual hangout after work at Cactus Club\. Really great turn out and amazing discussions\!

**Paint Night Social[https://med\-fom\-wach\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/?attachment\_id\=1753](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?attachment_id=1753)[Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/WACHSO-2024-4-150x150.png)**

Painted, enjoyed dinner, and competed for a prize with spring\-themed art\!

**Charity Spin Class[https://med\-fom\-wach\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/?attachment\_id\=1751](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?attachment_id=1751)[Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/WACHSO-2024-2-150x150.png)**

A free spin class for WACH students and all proceeds were donated to the Better life Foundation\!

**WACH Journal Club**

Discussed the paper, “Sulforaphane induced NRF2 activation in obese pregnancy attenuated developmental redox imbalance and improves early\-life cardiovascular function in offspring\.”

**Bunny Therapy[https://med\-fom\-wach\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/?attachment\_id\=1755](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?attachment_id=1755)[Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/WACHSO-2024-6-150x150.png)[https://med\-fom\-wach\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/?attachment\_id\=1752](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?attachment_id=1752)[Image](https://med-fom-wach.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/04/WACHSO-2024-3-150x150.png)**

Popular event amongst students and definitely a favourite\!
