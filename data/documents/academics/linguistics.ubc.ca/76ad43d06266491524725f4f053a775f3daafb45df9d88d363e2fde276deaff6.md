You can receive **regular email notifications** about colloquia by subscribing to the colloquium mailing list\. To do this, send an email to [this address](mailto:listserv@lists.ubc.ca) with no subject and a body consisting of only `subscribe ling-colloq firstname lastname`, replacing `firstname` and `lastname`\. You will receive an email asking you to confirm your subscription\. Alternatively, you can use [this sign\-up form](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/news-events/join-the-colloquium-mailing-list/), but that will take some time to be processed\. \(Linguistics grad students and faculty receive colloquium emails automatically\.\)

You can ensure that **your calendar app syncs automatically** with this event calendar by subscribing to it by clicking here\. If this doesn’t work, you will need to copy and paste the link \(`webcal://linguistics.ubc.ca/feed/eo-events/`\) into your app manually\.
