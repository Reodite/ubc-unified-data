> Many departments in the Faculty of Arts offer courses outside linguistics that complement our Honours, Major, and Minor programs in Linguistics and Speech Sciences\.

Note that depending on your program \(e\.g\. major in linguistics, Honours in Speech Science, etc\.\) you may be allowed to take a certain number of non\-LING credits as part of your program; the following is a list of recommended courses that complement our programs\.

You will still need to [contact an Undergrad Advisor](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/undergraduate/advising/) if you want to apply the course to your program\. \(It is *easiest* for us if you do this after having at least enrolled in **all** of the courses you may want to count toward this requirement, so that we don’t have to do a lot of manual overrides\. But we are willing to make the adjustment any time\.\) Any course not listed here has not been vetted by our advisors, and you should [contact an Undergrad Advisor](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/undergraduate/advising/) \*before\* taking the course to discuss whether it could be applied toward your program\.

Note: courses can count toward *both* your major *and* your [Ways of Knowing breadth requirements](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/degree-planning/degree-requirements/bachelor-of-arts/)\. We have noted below which ‘area of breadth’ the various courses belong in\. Please consult with an advisor if you have questions about how to distribute your courses\.
