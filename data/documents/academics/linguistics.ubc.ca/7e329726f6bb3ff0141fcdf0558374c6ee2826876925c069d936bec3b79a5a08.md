## Featured initiatives 

### The Edna Dharmaratne Award in Linguistics

Edna Dharmaratne served as the Administrator of the Department of Linguistics from 1997 to 2017\. She was the face and cornerstone of the Department, and is admired for her strength of character and generosity\. This award was established upon Edna’s retirement in 2017 in recognition of her legacy of compassion, competence, and integrity\.
