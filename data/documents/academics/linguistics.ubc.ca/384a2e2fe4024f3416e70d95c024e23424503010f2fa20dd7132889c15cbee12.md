## Speech &amp; Linguistics Student Association \(SALSA\)

SALSA represents students from the interdisciplinary fields of Speech Science \(Speech/Audiology\), First Nations Languages, Linguistics with members also specializing in Cognitive Systems, Psychology, French, Spanish, Anthropology, and many other disciplines\. We plan events, peer tutoring, study groups, and activities to keep the linguistics community engaged on campus\.
