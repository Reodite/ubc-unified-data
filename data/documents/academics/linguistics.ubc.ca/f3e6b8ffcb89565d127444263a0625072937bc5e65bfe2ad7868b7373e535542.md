### Dr\. Stanley Nam defends his dissertation

 

Stanley Nam successfully defended his doctoral dissertation on June 22\. Its title is “Learning a Phonologically Partitioned Lexicon\.” Stanley's joint dissertation supervisors were Kathleen Currie Hall and Gunnar Ólafur Hansson, with Miikka Silfverberg as the third committee member\.
