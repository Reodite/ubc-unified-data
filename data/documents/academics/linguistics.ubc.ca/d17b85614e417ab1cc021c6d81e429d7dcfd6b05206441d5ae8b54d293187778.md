> The department’s undergraduate advisors are here to help you\.

Please use this page to find the information you need or get more personalized help by either submitting a question, booking an online advising appointment, or finding a drop\-in advising session\. Please note that we can only help with questions that are specific to our courses and programs — we may refer you to your home faculty advising office for more general questions, such as faculty graduation requirements\.

## Ask a Question

If you have any questions that you’d like to ask an advisor or would like to book an appointment not using the calendar above, please fill out the form below\. You can generally expect to hear back within 1 week of submitting the form; we prioritize responding to urgent messages\.

If your question is about ASL course registration, please join the waitlist\. Due to high demand, we cannot guarantee a seat for all students\. We encourage you to contact your home advising office to discuss alternative course options\.
