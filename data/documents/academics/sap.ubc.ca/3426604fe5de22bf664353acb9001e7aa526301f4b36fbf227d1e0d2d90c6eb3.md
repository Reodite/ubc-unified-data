SaP is an initiative that provides grants to support UBC Vancouver undergraduate students working in partnerships with faculty to redesign UBCV undergraduate courses\. The following is a snapshot of the impact of the initiative to date\.

In my experience, engaging in a SaP project is a **great way for students to have a positive impact on the program they are involved in**\. I would encourage students to research the SaP model themselves, and propose their project ideas to faculty members\. Increased student involvement in SaP projects have the possibility to bring out the best of the SaP model, and can **open the door to new kinds of pedagogy, learning, and sustained student investment in academic work and academic communities**\.

— Student partner

### Call for Proposals

To date, there have been three calls for proposals \(February 2022, June 2022, February 2023\) with a final call scheduled for June 2023\. An adjudication committee consisting of UBC students, faculty, and staff provides feedback on each proposal and decides which proposals to fund\.

### SaP Project Support

Funded SaP projects receive the following support from CTLT:

-   **Learning Design support** – Support from an assigned Learning Designer who guides course design; provide feedback on proposed course design activities; and provides project management support​
-   **Evaluation support** – Support from the SaP Evaluation Specialist \(graduate student role\) who provides support on methods of evaluation and feedback on proposed evaluation activities
-   **Partnership support** – Support from the SaP Strategist \(staff role\) and SaP Coordinator \(undergraduate student role\) who connects projects to partnership resources; provides guidance on the process of working in partnership, relationship\-building​

### SaP Cohort Support and Workshops

The following workshops are provided to SaP project partners in their respective cohorts:

-   Cohort Kick\-Off
    -   This workshop provides opportunities for knowledge\-sharing and reflection between projects, and across student and faculty peer communities\.
-   Course Design Workshop
    -   This workshop introduces partners to course design principles for developing learner\-centred courses and explores partnership considerations in course design
-   SaP &amp; TLEF Evaluation Workshop
    -   Hands\-on workshop to help projects develop their evaluation framework\. This workshop introduces useful evaluation frameworks, identify strategies \(e\.g\., commonly used data collection methods\), and discuss common challenges of evaluation
-   Inclusive Teaching Workshop
    -   This workshop introduces participants to literature underpinning inclusive teaching and explores diversity amongst UBC’s student population
