## Graduate Programs

### Master of Science in Nursing \(MSN\)

A Master of Science in Nursing \(MSN\) prepares students to enact advanced roles in nursing practice, education and research\. The program equips you to actively contribute to improving health care delivery, advancing and applying nursing knowledge, and evolving health policy\.

[LEARN MORE](https://nursing.ok.ubc.ca/graduate/msn/)

### Doctor of Philosophy \(PhD\) in Nursing

Join our extensive community of scholars and work with your supervisor to develop a research training program that meets your goals\. Gain opportunities to access independent studies, practicums, and research and teaching opportunities\.

[LEARN MORE](https://nursing.ok.ubc.ca/graduate/phd/)

For anyone interested in the Doctorate of Nursing Program \(DN\), kindly subscribe to our mailing list here for more information: [Doctorate of Nursing – Prospective Students](https://e1.envoke.com/ext/pages/f2f4f75acd06fb27f8c6a283ce01d34a)
