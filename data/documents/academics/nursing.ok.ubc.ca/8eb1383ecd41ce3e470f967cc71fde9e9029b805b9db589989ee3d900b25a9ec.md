## [UBCO nursing students host annual Global Gala](https://nursing.ok.ubc.ca/2019/11/07/ubco-nursing-students-host-annual-global-gala/)

November 7, 2019 (Thursday, November 7th, 2019, 6:00 am)

UBC Okanagan nursing students are organizing a gala evening of food and entertainment as a fundraising initiative for the university’s Global Health Practicum program\.
