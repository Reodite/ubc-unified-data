### Faculty of Health and Social Development

The Schools of Nursing, Exercise Sciences, and Social Work join forces to transform and improve health care and well\-being for our local communities and beyond\. Our students are active global citizens who work with local and international communities to optimize human health: improving treatments, preventing disease, and meeting future health\-care challenges\. Innovative faculty produce cutting\-edge research and are committed to providing students with relevant hands\-on learning\. A passion for health and healing and a desire to create positive change transforms students into leaders who will define the future of quality health care\.

Consider how your support could help us improve public health practice\.
