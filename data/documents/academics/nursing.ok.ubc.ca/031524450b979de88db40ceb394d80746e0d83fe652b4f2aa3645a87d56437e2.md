At UBC Okanagan, we offer two distinct paths to help you advance your nursing career\. Whether you're seeking practical, skills\-based training to apply immediately in primary care settings or looking to deepen your theoretical knowledge and leadership capabilities, we have the right option for you\.

## Micro\-Credentials &amp; Post\-Baccalaureate Certificate

Our **Micro\-Credentials** provide flexible, career\-focused education that equips nurses with in\-demand skills, while our **Post\-Baccalaureate Certificate** offers a more comprehensive, theory\-based education for nurses aiming for leadership roles in primary care\. As the curricula of the micro\-credentials and certificate are complementary, you may also opt to do both\. Online flexible delivery of both programs is designed for working professionals\.

### [Hands typing on a laptop with a stethoscope nearby\.](https://nursing.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/120/2024/10/Post-Bac.jpg)

### Post\-Baccalaureate Primary Care Nursing Certificate

For Registered Nurses and Registered Psychiatric Nurses looking to expand their theoretical knowledge and step into leadership roles, our **Post\-Baccalaureate Certificate in Primary Care Nursing** is a comprehensive program to advancing your career\.

#### Courses include:

-   **Primary Care Nursing I:** Build a deep understanding of concepts, frameworks and competencies foundational to primary care nursing\.
-   **Advanced Primary Care Nursing II**: Build evidence\-informed knowledge of case management, care coordination, system navigation and quality improvement\.
-   **Primary Care Nursing Preceptorship**: 120\-hour preceptorship to apply knowledge and skills learned in Primary Care Nursing I and II\.

#### Why Choose the Post\-Baccalaureate Certificate?

-   Ideal for Registered Nurses or Registered Psychiatric Nurses aspiring to leadership or advanced practice roles in primary care\.
-   Comprehensive theoretical foundation plus preceptorship ready you for a broad scope of practice\.

[Learn more and apply](https://nursing.ok.ubc.ca/home/non-degree-programs/post-baccalaureate-primary-care-nursing-certificate/)

### Primary Care Practice Essentials Micro\-Credentials

Looking to enhance your nursing skills with practical, job\-ready training? Our **Primary Care Nursing Practice Essentials Micro\-Credentials** are designed to equip registered nurses with advanced competencies tailored for primary care settings\.

#### Micro\-Credentials include:

-   [**Health Assessment for Primary Care Nursing:**](https://cpe.ok.ubc.ca/programs/health-assessment-for-primary-care-nursing/) Master the skills to perform comprehensive health evaluations\.
-   [**Case Management &amp; Care Coordination:**](https://cpe.ok.ubc.ca/programs/case-management-and-care-coordination-for-primary-care-nursing/) Coordinate care effectively across interdisciplinary teams to improve patient outcomes\.
-   **[Caring for Select Populations:](https://cpe.ok.ubc.ca/programs/caring-for-select-populations-in-primary-care-nursing/)** Gain specialized knowledge to care for diverse and underserved populations\.
-   **[Health Promotion, Prevention &amp; Screening](https://cpe.ok.ubc.ca/programs/health-promotion-prevention-screening-in-primary-care-nursing/):** Learn how to promote health and prevent disease in diverse populations\.

***These programs are eligible for the StrongerBC future skills grant\. The grant provides up to $3,500 in funding for most BC residents aged 19 years or older***

#### Why choose Micro\-Credentials?

-   Immediate, practical skills to apply and advance your career\.
-   Pick and choose\. Each micro\-credential develops skills in a specific area of primary care\.
