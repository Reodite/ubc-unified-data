## [Province partners with Interior universities for COVID\-19 research solutions](https://nursing.ok.ubc.ca/2021/02/18/province-partners-with-interior-universities-for-covid-19-research-solutions/)

February 18, 2021 (Thursday, February 18th, 2021, 9:42 am)

The BC Ministry of Health is investing in BC Interior research universities to understand the harmful effects of COVID\-19 and mitigate its impact on communities across the province\.
