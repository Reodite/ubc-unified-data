Jane Philpott, Dean of the Faculty of Health Sciences and Director of the School of Medicine at Queen’s University and Dr\. Jason Pennington, Assistant Professor, General Surgery at the University of Toronto\.

## April 15 at 12:00 pm – 1:30 pm
