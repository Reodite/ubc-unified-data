## [Future of Health Forum focuses on research and innovation in cancer care](https://nursing.ok.ubc.ca/2019/09/20/future-of-health-forum-focuses-on-research-and-innovation-in-cancer-care/)

September 20, 2019 (Friday, September 20th, 2019, 8:53 am)
