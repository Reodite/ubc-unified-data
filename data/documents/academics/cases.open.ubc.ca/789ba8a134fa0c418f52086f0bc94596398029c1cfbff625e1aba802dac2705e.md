This page provides information about our process in setting up the Open Case Studies project\.

We held a two\-day sprint in May 2016, during which faculty and graduate students drafted the first case studies\. Undergraduate students also participated in that sprint\.

Please see below for information about what the case study authors and the undergraduate students did during the sprint, as well as information about a workshop we held before the sprint took place\.
