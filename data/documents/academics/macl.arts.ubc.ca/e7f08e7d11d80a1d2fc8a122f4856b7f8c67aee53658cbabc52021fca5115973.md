#### [University of British Columbia Website](http://www.ubc.ca/)

The UBC website is useful to learn more about the history and direction of UBC as an institution, and links to other UBC entities and services that provide student support\.

#### [UBC Student Services](http://students.ubc.ca/)

While geared primarily towards undergraduate students, Student Services provides a plethora of useful resources for students, regardless of how long they’ve attended UBC\.

#### [UBC Grad School](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/)

From professional development to health and wellbeing, UBC Grad school provides events and workshops to help you maximize your graduate student experience at UBC\.

#### [UBC iSchool](https://lais.air.arts.ubc.ca/students/)

The UBC iSchool has compiled a handy resource list for students for a variety of needs, such as course planning, international students, and technology\.

#### [Sustainability Scholars Program](https://sustain.ubc.ca/teaching-applied-learning/ubc-sustainability-scholars-program)

The Sustainability Scholars Program connects UBC graduate students with opportunities to work on funded sustainability projects for on\- and off\-campus partner organizations\.

#### [UBC Public Humanities Hub Resources](https://publichumanities.ubc.ca/resources/)

From Podcast, Exhibition, and Op\-Ed [toolkits](https://publichumanities.ubc.ca/resources/toolkits/) to a plethora of professional development, grad\-student support, and public scholarship events, the Public Humanities Hub is here to support graduate student learning and engagement\.
