## Course Registration

> Upon being accepted to the program, please speak with the [MACL program chair](https://macl.arts.ubc.ca/tess-prendergast/) about your interests and relevant courses to ensure the best course registration experience and to address any questions or concerns\.

**Course Registration Step\-by\-Step**

-   To log in, you must obtain a campus\-wide login \(CWL\) \([see instructions](https://activate.id.ubc.ca/iamweb)\)\.
-   To browse course offerings, log into your student account and choose “Course Schedule\.” Follow directions from there\. Graduate courses are all at the 500\-level\. 
-   We strongly recommend watching video registration tutorials on Workday\. More information can be found here – [https://students\.ubc\.ca/enrolment/registration](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration)
-   We will also try to have an updated course list on the MACL website before registration each year: [https://macl\.arts\.ubc\.ca/program/courses](https://macl.arts.ubc.ca/program/courses)
-   Because courses often fill quickly \(some almost immediately\), we strongly recommend students do the following before registration day: create a *Saved Schedule* in Workday, with the courses you want to register in\. Make a list of alternate courses in case the ones you want fill up\. Be on your computer the moment registration opens\!
-   Some departments allow you to add yourself to a waitlist for full classes and others do not\.
-   If you don’t get a course, please keep checking back or even reach out to the instructor\. We often find courses will fill initially, but students may switch and drop courses right into the first week of classes\. You can also directly contact the instructors to check availability\. 
-   For courses marked RESTRICTED, write to the program chair and course instructor for information about eligibility\. 
-   For more information, please review the following resources:[Registration](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/registration), [UBC Enrollment](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment) and [UBC Graduate Student Society – Newly Admitted](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/newly-admitted)
