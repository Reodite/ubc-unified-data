**MACL’s Nicola Kirk Presenting at The Child and The Book Conference 2025\!**

[Image](https://macl2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/04/Nicola-Kirk-225x300.jpg)Nicola Kirk will be presenting her work, “From Everyday to Exceptional: Representations of Syrian Child Refugees Navigating their Changing Worlds in Youth Literature” at The Child and The Book Conference at Tilburg University in the Netherlands\. The theme of this year’s conference is “Children Shaping The\(ir\) World: Between the Exceptional and the Everyday\.” View the entire program [here](https://www.diggitmagazine.com/sites/default/files/2025-04/CBC25_Programme.pdf) and more info about the conference [here](https://www.diggitmagazine.com/events/child-and-book-conference-2025)\. Congratulations Nicola\!
