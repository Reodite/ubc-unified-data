## The MACL community wants your news\!

The Master of Arts in Children's Literature \(MACL\) wants to connect with, encourage, and celebrate the accomplishments of alumni, faculty and current students\.

Fill out this form to share your news or opportunity with our community via social media, email lists, and the website\.

We are especially interested in children's literature awards, scholarships, calls for participation and submission, conferences, events, research, librarianship, teaching, and creative writing\.
