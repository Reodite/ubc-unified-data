> Our academic advisors are here to help you\.

There is dedicated support available to all students in the Interdisciplinary Studies \(IDST\) program in understanding requirements and planning for success in the program\.

In addition, the Arts Academic Advising team can also provide programs and services to help you make informed and thoughtful decisions about your academic path throughout your degree\.

---

## Interdisciplinary Studies \(IDST\) advising

#### Jazmin Ysmael

Jazmin Ysmael, our Student Program Coordinator, is available for appointments online\. She offers support with understanding program requirements, navigating policies and procedures, incorporating transfer credits into your program, and discussing program options and plans\.
