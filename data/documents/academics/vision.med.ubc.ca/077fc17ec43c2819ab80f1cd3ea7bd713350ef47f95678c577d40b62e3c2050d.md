1.  1.  Download the **[screensaver file for Windows](https://vision.med.ubc.ca/files/2021/08/UBCFOM.sCr)**\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Windows-Download.png)

1.  2.  ****Right click**** on the screensaver file, select ****Properties,****  check the ****Unblock**** box and press ****Apply**** as shown below\.
    

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/08/properties.png)  
[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/08/Unblock.png)

1.  3.  **Right click** on the screensaver file, and select **Copy**\.
    

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Right-Click-Copy.png)

1.  4.  Type C:\\ in the **File Explorer** and press **Enter** to browse to C:\\\.
    

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/File-Explorer.png)

1.  5.  Right click on **Windows** and select **Paste**\.
    

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Windows-Paste.png)

1.  6.  You will be prompted to provide administrator permission; click on **Continue** to enter the **administrator password**\.
    

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Admin-Password.png)

1.  7.  Once the file is copied, search for screensaver in the **Start Menu**\. Select **Change screen saver**\.
    

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Change-Screensaver.png)

1.  8.  In the screensaver dropdown list, select **UBC Faculty of Medicine**\.
    

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Screensaver-Settings-Dropdown.png)

1.  9.  Once UBC Faculty of Medicine is selected, click **Apply**\.
    

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Screensaver-Settings-Apply.png)
