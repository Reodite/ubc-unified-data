1.  Download the **[screensaver file for MacOS](https://vision.med.ubc.ca/files/2021/08/UBC_Faculty_of_Medicine_Screensaver_2021_new.app.zip)**\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/MacOS-Download.png)

3.  Double click on the zip file to extract the app file\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Zip-File.png)

5.  Double click on the app file\. *You may be prompted that the developer cannot be verified\.* Click **Cancel**\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/MacOS-Verification.png)

7.  Go to **System Preferences**\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/System-Preferences.png)

9.  Click on **Security &amp; Privacy**\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Security-Privacy.png)

11.  Under the **General** tab, you see the file that was blocked\. Click **Open Anyway**\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Open-Anyway.png)

13.  Click **Open** to open the file\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/MacOS-Open.png)

15.  The Faculty of Medicine screensaver installation window will open\. Click on **down arrow ▼ beside Install**\. Select **Install for Me**\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Install-for-Me.png)

17.  Wait for the installation to finish\. Once installation is completed, the **Desktop &amp; Screen Saver** window will open with **UBC Faculty of Medicine** selected\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Desktop-Screen-Saver.png)
