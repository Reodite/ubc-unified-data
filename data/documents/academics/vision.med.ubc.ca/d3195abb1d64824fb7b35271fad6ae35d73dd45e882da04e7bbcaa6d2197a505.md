1.  If you’re not on campus network, please make sure you’re connected to **UBC VPN**\.
2.  Launch the **Software Centre**\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Software-Centre-Launch.png)

4.  Find and click on **FOM – iScreensaver**\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Software-Centre.png)

6.  Click **Install**\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Install.png)

8.  Wait for the installation to finish; you’ll see the following screen when it’s completed\.

[Image](https://med-fom-vision-values.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/Installation-Complete.png)

10.  The screensaver is now installed\.
