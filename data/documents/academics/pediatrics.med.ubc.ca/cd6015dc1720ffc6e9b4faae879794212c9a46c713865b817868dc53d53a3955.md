Congratulations to Dr\. Sharon Dell on this special recognition of her significant contributions to advancing respiratory care through clinical practice, research, teaching, and mentorship of clinical trainees and early career clinicians\.

The CTS Honorary Lecture Award honours exemplary leadership in respiratory research and education in Canada and is presented as part of the honorary lecture series of the CHEST Conference\.
