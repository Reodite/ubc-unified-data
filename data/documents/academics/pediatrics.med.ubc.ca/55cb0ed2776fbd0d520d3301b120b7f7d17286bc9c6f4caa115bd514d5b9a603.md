*Vision 2028: Charting a Course for Health Equity*

Updated Mar 28 2025

In November 2024, the Health Justice and Health Equity Working Group comprised of eighteen child health experts convened to establish priorities for implementing the Vision 2028 Strategic Plan Pillar 3’s priority: Expand our impact for health justice and health equity in the province and beyond\. The group, which included Department of Pediatrics’ members and hospital administrators overseeing equity\-related mandates in their work units, engaged in productive discussions that yielded several key recommendations\.

The working group’s recommendations span four major areas: clinical, education, research and human resources\. In the clinical domain, priorities include developing a Provincial Child Health Strategic Plan, better recognition of social determinants in pediatric outcomes, enhancing child health advocacy skills and expanding support for complex care programs\. The group emphasizes the need for improved virtual care models and broader network expansion across the health authorities\.

Health equity educational initiatives emerged as a key priority\. It was recommended that the department prioritize creating an inclusive work environment by integrating equity metrics into curricula and providing fair compensation for equity\-related contributions\. The research recommendations highlighted the importance of fostering equitable child health research, encouraging innovative and non\-traditional studies, and advancing early childhood development research\. In terms of human resources, the group proposed evaluating recruitment processes through an equity lens, transitioning from physician\-centric healthcare models, and actively supporting employee well\-being\.

The next steps for this Working Group include gaining consensus on measurable and achievable initiatives, followed by appropriate resource allocation by the Department of Pediatrics’ leadership team\. While some of the issues that have been identified are beyond the Department of Pediatrics’ immediate scope, discussions at the next meeting will focus on identifying partners and establishing both short\-term achievements and longer\-term goals\.
