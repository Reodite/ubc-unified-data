Sarah Riedlinger is thrilled to announce her role at UBC as a Clinical Instructor in the Sunny Okanagan\.

After completing her Pediatrics training, and a fellowship in Endocrinology, at BC Children’s Hospital, she has set up private practice in Kelowna\. She looks forward to providing her services in diabetes, endocrinology, and general pediatrics, to children throughout the BC Interior region\. Dr\. Riedlinger is very excited to work and train with the next generation of physicians at UBC Medical School\.
