[BCCHF Miracle Weekend 2014](http://www.bcchf.ca/events/event-calendar/miracle-weekend/ "BCCHF Miracle Weekend 2014")[P](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/03/FINAL-MIRACLE-WEEKEND-25E8-360x102.jpg)

BC Children’s Hospital Foundation will continue to offer the ***Hospital Family Matching Gift Program for 2014***\. Members of the Department of Pediatrics at BCCH, Sunny Hill and CFRI, as members of the Hospital Family who personally donate **$10,000** or more to BC Children’s Hospital Foundation during the calendar year, will have their gift matched with **$10,000** from the Foundation\. Your donation and the matching gift will be assigned to your designated fund or endowment of choice at the hospital, Sunny Hill or CFRI\.

This is a great opportunity to support your area of care or research and direct donations to goals that are important to you\. A donation of any amount makes a difference to child health care\.

Note:  All donations and pledges collected by **May 28, 2014** will be reflected in our Department’s presentation cheque total during [Miracle Weekend](http://www.bcchf.ca/events/event-calendar/miracle-weekend "Miracle Weekend") on **Sunday, June 1\[st\.\]**

If you have not already done so, please take a moment to contribute via the [website](https://secure.bcchf.ca/donate/donation.cfm?Donation=Pediatrics "Matching Gift Program 2014 - Pledge") or pledge by contacting [Janice Williams](mailto:jwilliams@bcchf.ca "Janice Williams") at the Foundation at \(604\) 875\-2677\.  She is happy to answer any questions about the Hospital Family matching gift program, available funds or ways to donate\.
