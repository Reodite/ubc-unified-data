## [Timely preprint comparing RSV infections and hospitalizations in children before and during COVID\-19 pandemic](https://pediatrics.med.ubc.ca/2022/11/22/timely-preprint-comparing-rsv-infections-and-hospitalizations-in-children-before-and-during-covid-19-pandemic/)

Recent work by researchers from the Department of Pediatrics, BC Children’s Hospital Research Institute and the BC Centre for Disease Control show more RSV cases in children ages 2\-3 years old without increased severity even after the pandemic mitigation measures were lifted in 2021\-22\.
