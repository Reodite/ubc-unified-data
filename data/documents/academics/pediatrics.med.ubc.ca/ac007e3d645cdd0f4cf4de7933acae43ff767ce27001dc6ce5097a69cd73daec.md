You’re Invited\!

Please join us for the Re\-dedication Event for the Tree of Hippocrates at BC Children’s Hospital\.

The Working Green by Nature group at BCCHR will be hosting a celebration of the tree and the beautiful nature that we are privileged to have here at the C&amp;W campus\. Please join us for a mindful walk through our campus to the tree, followed by a short dedication and light refreshments\. Everyone is welcome\!

**Date:** Thursday, April 10, 2025

**Time:** 3:00 – 4:00 PM

**Location:** Meet at the Main Lobby of BCCHR\. Dr\. Dzung Vo will take us for a guided walk across campus to the tree, followed by a short ceremony, then light refreshments in Room C&amp;W D306 \(Shaughnessy Building\)
