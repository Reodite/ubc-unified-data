Dr\. Wingfield Rehmus and several residents and fellows co\-authored, *Pediatric Dermatology: A Primer by Dr\. Wingfield Rehmus, MD, MPH*, a valuable resource, which is free for download at [https://pressbooks\.bccampus\.ca/pedsdermprimer](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/pedsdermprimer)

The manual covers the basics of treating common skin conditions like Melanoma and Cancer in children, adolescents, and adults and it is meant to accompany rotation or a course in pediatric dermatology\.
