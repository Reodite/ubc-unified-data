Dr\. Natalie Chan, Clinical Assistant Professor

**Name:**  
Dr\. Natalie Chan

**Role:**  
Clinical Assistant Professor, UBC  
Medical Director of the Neonatal Follow\-up Clinic  
Neonatologist, BC Women’s Hospital

**Qualifications:**  
MD, MPH, FRCPC, FAAP

**Biography:**  
Dr\. Natalie Chan completed her Paediatrics Residency and Neonatal\-Perinatal Medicine Fellowship training at the University of Toronto\. More recently, she had completed additional training in Neonatal Neurology at University of California, San Francisco followed by an additional year in Neonatal\-Perinatal Medicine at UC Davis Medical Center\.  Natalie also holds a Master’s in Public Health \(MPH\) from Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health in Baltimore\.

Clinically, Dr\. Natalie Chan is interested in learning how we can protect the vulnerable developing brain and improve neurodevelopmental outcomes, especially in the most fragile babies\. She is also passionate about global neonatal health and scaling up innovative solutions to reduce neonatal mortality and help children everywhere live to their fullest potential\.
