[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/03/Capture-360x227.jpg)Ella Chan has been awarded “Best Oral Presentation” for her talk on *Compensatory Renal Growth Predicts Outcome in Children with a Solitary Functioning Kidney* at the 2020 Western Medical Research Conference\.

Based on judges’ scoring, her work was highlighted from over 350 high quality student and trainee presentations\.

Congratulations on this achievement\!
