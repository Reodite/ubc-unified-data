[Headshot of Dr\. Tim Oberlander](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/09/tim-oberlander-photo-360x240.jpeg)This prestigious recognition honours a senior scientist who has made outstanding contributions to advancing the health and well\-being of children and families while demonstrating exceptional leadership within the BC Children’s Hospital Research Institute\. Dr\. Oberlander’s pioneering research, unwavering commitment to mentorship, and dedication to improving outcomes for children and families have had a profound impact locally, nationally, and internationally\.

We are proud to celebrate this well\-deserved achievement and grateful for Dr\. Oberlander’s leadership, vision, and contributions to child health research\.

Congratulations, Tim, on this remarkable honour\. We look forward to celebrating this achievement with you\. We welcome everyone to attend this event\.

**Award Presentation**

Date: November 18, 2026

Time: 2:00 to 3:00 PM

Location: Chan Centre for Family Health Education, BCCHR

Be sure to check out Tim’s award photo, now proudly displayed outside the Bean in the BCCRI, a fitting location given his well\-known passion for coffee\.

Please join us in congratulating Tim on this well\-deserved honour\!
