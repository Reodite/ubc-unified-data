For National Pediatrician Day on Jan\. 28th, we honor some special individuals, nominated from within our community\.

Their hard work and dedication to excellence in pediatric care, research, and education, has provided the best potential for every child, youth, and caregiver in BC and beyond\.

Learn more about our **[2024 faculty spotlights:](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/01/2024-National-Pediatrician-Bios-DeptofPediatricsUBC.pdf)** Drs\. Mor Cohen Eilig, Pei\-Yoong Lam, Zaneta Lim, David Smith, Anita Malik and Paul Rogers\.

Let’s celebrate their work and we extend our thanks to all of those who serve in the profession of pediatric medicine\!
