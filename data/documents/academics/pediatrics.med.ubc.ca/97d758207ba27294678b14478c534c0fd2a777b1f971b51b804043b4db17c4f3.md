Congratulations to our valued faculty members for receiving new MSFHR funding\!

The [Convening &amp; Collaborating](https://www.msfhr.org/2019-convening-collaborating-award-recipients) \(C2\) Program and [Reach](https://www.msfhr.org/2019-reach-award-recipients) awards are designed to foster collaboration between researchers and research users, support the co\-development of research questions and the dissemination of research evidence\.

**Faculty of medicine C2 awardees and their projects are:**

-   [**Manish Sadarangani**](https://experts.news.ubc.ca/expert/manish-sadarangani/), department of pediatrics, *Development Of A Comprehensive Vaccine Research Agenda For BC Through Multi\-Faceted Collaboration*
-   [**Laura Sauvé**](https://www.bcchr.ca/lsauve), department of pediatrics,*‘Exploring Being Well Together’: Maternal &amp; Infant Health Equity In The Context Of HIV*

**Faculty of medicine Reach awardees and their projects are:**

-   [**Julie Bettinger**](https://experts.news.ubc.ca/expert/julie-bettinger/), department of pediatrics, *“Vaccine Booster Talk” short film for frontline healthcare professionals*
-   [**Stuart Turvey**](https://bcchr.ca/sturvey), department of pediatrics,*Asthma in the Age of Epigenetics &amp; Microbiomics: Research, Clinical Practice &amp; Policy*
