Dr\. Joseph Ting has been appointed to the position of Clinical Assistant Professor and is a Staff Neonatologist with the Division of Neonatology\. He completed his medical training and Pediatrics residency in Hong Kong in 2006\. He obtained his Master of Public Health in 2009\.

He arrived in Vancouver in 2010 to receive his fellowship training in Neonatal\-Perinatal Medicine\. He then spent six months at The Hospital for Sick Children, Toronto to receive further training in Neonatal Hemodynamics and Targeted Neonatal Echocardiography \(TnECHO\)\.

Married with two daughters, Joseph enjoys spending time with his family, reading and watching dramas\.
