**PHM Rounds:  Pediatric Hospital Medicine Rounds** \(formerly PrisM Rounds\)

[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/03/Prism-LOGO-337x300.jpg)

**Aim:**  A weekly focus for the Pediatric Hospital Medicine Section of our Division, open to all Pediatric Staff, learners and allied health\. An opportunity to promote collaborative learning, advance evidence\-based health care and ultimately improve clinical care for our patients\. Rolling topics will include M\+M meetings, Case Based Discussions, Journal Club, Research Updates, Quality Improvement and Health Equity Rounds\.

**When:** Thursdays 8:15am\-9am on Microsoft Teams \(Hybrid/In person, see weekly invite for details\)

**Please don’t forget to** **[complete the PHM Rounds evaluation](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_57lMkbauZRK6982)** **to receive MOC credits and improve PHM Rounds\.**
