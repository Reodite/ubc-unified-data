Just released, *Live 5\-2\-1\-0 at BC Children’s Hospital: Report on Achievements \(2016 – 2023\)* shares the heartfelt journey of the Live 5\-2\-1\-0 team with their supporters and partners in spreading the 5\-2\-1\-0 message at BC Children’s Hospital\.  
   
*“We would like to extend our heartfelt thank you to all of the patients, families and staff at BC Children’s Hospital who contributed to these projects over the past seven years – including the BC Children’s Hospital Foundation for  
their generous support in bringing the Live 5\-2\-1\-0 initiative to the hospital\.”*  
   
Shazhan Amed MSc, MD
