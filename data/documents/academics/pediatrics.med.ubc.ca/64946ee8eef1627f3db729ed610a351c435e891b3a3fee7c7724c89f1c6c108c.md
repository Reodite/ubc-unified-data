BC Children’s is also part of a new national training platform for child health researchers, [Empowering Next\-generation Researchers In perinatal and Child Health \(ENRICH\)](https://cchcsp.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/ENRICH-Announcement-v3.pdf), that will provide new training opportunities for early career researchers and trainees\. Dr\. Quynh Doan, Dr\. Pascal Lavoie and Dr\. Jill Zwicker are leading BC Children’s ENRICH team\.
