**Pediatric Chairs of Canada Academic Leadership Awards**

Since 2007, the Pediatric Chairs of Canada \(PCC\) has recognized and promoted excellence in academic pediatric leadership through its Pediatric Academic Leadership Awards\. These awards celebrate outstanding contributions across distinct academic career paths, reflecting PCC’s belief that the health and well\-being of children and youth depend on strong leadership in the areas of clinical practice, medical education, research, and academic administration\.

**Emerging Leader \(Large institution\) Award[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/JonathanRayment-300x300.webp)**

**Dr\. Jonathan H\. Rayment**

Dr\. Jonathan H\. Rayment has established himself as an outstanding clinician\-scientist and academic leader through his innovative work in pediatric respiratory medicine and cystic fibrosis research\. His research has advanced the development and application of novel pulmonary imaging and lung function technologies, improving both clinical care and scientific discovery for children with respiratory disease\. Since joining BC Children’s Hospital and the University of British Columbia, he has built a highly successful research program, secured significant research funding, and produced influential scholarship with national and international impact\. Through his leadership, mentorship, and collaborative approach, Dr\. Rayment represents the next generation of academic leadership in Canadian pediatrics\.
