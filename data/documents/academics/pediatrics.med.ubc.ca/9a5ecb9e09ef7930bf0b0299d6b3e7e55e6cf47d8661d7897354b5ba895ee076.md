[mattcarwana](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/08/mattcarwana-247x300.jpg)Matt presented two posters and one oral presentation at this year’s 2016 Canadian Paediatric Society Conference in beautiful PEI\. Matt ended up winning the Resident Research Award for Excellence\.

This is the second year in a row that UBC has won this prestigious award\. Last year, Rebecca Ronsley, a PGY\-3 won\.
