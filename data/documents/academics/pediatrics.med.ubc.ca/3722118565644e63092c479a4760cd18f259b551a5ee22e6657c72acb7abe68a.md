Pediatrics is a four\-year PGY1 entry program consisting of a core of three years and a fourth year which may be dedicated to General Pediatrics or the first year of a subspecialty program\. Some residents opt to remain in General Pediatrics for four years before progressing to a subspecialty; particularly as there is currently additional funding available, allowing them to do so\. Currently,we have 47 residents in the pediatrics program\.

In February 2013, our program was fully accredited by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada\.
