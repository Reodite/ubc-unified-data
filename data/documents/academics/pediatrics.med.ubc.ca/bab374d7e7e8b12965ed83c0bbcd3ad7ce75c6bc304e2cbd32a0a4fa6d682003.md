You are invited to attend World Kidney Day on Thursday, March 13, 2014 from 10:30 a\.m\. – 2:30 p\.m\. at the Shaughnessy Cafeteria, BCCH

March is Kidney Month and Thursday, March 13, 2014 is World Kidney Day\. Your kidneys are for life\! Learn about kidneys and how to protect them\.

Featured activities:

– Free blood pressure measurement by a nurse or resident

– Learn how to protect your kidneys

– Play the Kidney Quiz &amp; win prizes

Meet the people of the Nephrology Program on March 13th in the Shaughnessy Cafeteria at BC Children’s Hospital &amp; Women’s Hospital Site\.

If you would like to learn more or are unable to attend, please visit the following websites for additional information on World Kidney Day and kidney health information:

Kidney Smart [www\.kidneysmart\.com](http://www.kidneysmart.com/);
