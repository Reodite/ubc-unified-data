[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/02/BCCHR.jpg)The Departments of Obstetrics &amp; Gynaecology and Pediatrics are collaborating on the development of an umbrella program for graduate student training in Women\+ and Children’s Health Sciences \(WACH\)\. The term Women\+ embraces women, transgender, and non\-binary individuals\. [\[MORE\]](https://wach.med.ubc.ca/wach-program-launched/)

WACH learners choose one of 3 streams and complete courses and training relevant to their chosen stream:  
– Women\+ Health  
– Child Health  
– Reproductive and Developmental Sciences \(RDS\)

[Visit the WACH program page](https://wach.med.ubc.ca/)
