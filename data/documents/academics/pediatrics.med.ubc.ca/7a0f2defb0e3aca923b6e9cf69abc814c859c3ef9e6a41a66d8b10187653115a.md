The annual “Balding for Dollars” event will take place May 14th at the BC Children’s Hospital\. Visit the [Balding For Dollars website](http://www.baldingfordollars.com/) for information on how to participate in the event\.

BCCH’s own Dr Bjorn Baadjes will be giving up his beloved locks to raise funds to support Oncology, Hematology, and BMT\. You can help by pledging your donations to Dr Baadjes by [clicking here](http://bit.ly/1SumBzt)\.

We hope to set a new record for total donations this year, so please contribute what you can\.

Skip that afternoon cookie, dine\-in, or put that emergency $5 bill in your wallet to good use\! Every little bit helps the children of BC\!
