[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/11/ronsley-125x150.png)Dr\. Ronsley is 1 of 3 trainees awarded the Dr\. John Dupré Award\. The Resident Clinical Case/Research Project Poster Award includes a $500 honoraria and certificate awarded to trainees who have presented the highest ranked *oral or poster clinical research projects* or *resident poster clinical vignettes* at the CSEM \(Canadian Society of Endocrinology &amp; Metabolism\) Annual Professional Conference in Edmonton Nov 1\-4 2017\.

Rebecca was given the award for her poster entitled: Adherence to a Pediatric Diabetic Ketoacidosis Protocol in Children Presenting to a Tertiary Care Hospital\.

Congratulations to Rebecca\!
