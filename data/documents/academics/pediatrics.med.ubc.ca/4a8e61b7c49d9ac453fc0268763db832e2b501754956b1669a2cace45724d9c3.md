Within the Faculty of Medicine, the Department of Pediatrics has participated and provided leadership to one of the largest transformations of a medical school in Canada, if not in North America\. The UBC Pediatric Residency Program offers community rotations via a distributed teaching model during each of the four years of training\.  Residents having an interest in rural pediatrics have the opportunity to design their own elective experiences in smaller centres within the province of British Columbia\.
