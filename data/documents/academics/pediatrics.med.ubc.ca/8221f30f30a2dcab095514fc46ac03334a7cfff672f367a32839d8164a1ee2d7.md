Dr\. Tapas Kulkarni, Neonatologist

**Name:**  

Dr\. Tapas Kulkarni

**Role:**  

Neonatologist, BC Women’s Hospital

**Qualifications:**  

MB BCh BAO, FRCPC

**Biography:**         

Dr\. Tapas Kulkarni completed his medical school training at the University College Dublin in Ireland, and a paediatric residency program at the University of South Alabama in Mobile, Alabama\. Followed by a fellowship in neonatal\-perinatal medicine at the University of Toronto\.

His areas of interest include medical education, simulation education and techniques to improve ventilation strategies in neonates\.
