[On September 22nd, Dr\. Edmond Chan was awarded *The Donald Stark Award for Excellence in Leadership* from Canadian Allergy, Asthma and Immunology Foundation\.](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/10/CAAIF-Leadership-Award_auz-060-copy.jpg)

Dr\. Chan is the Head of the Division of Allergy and Immunology at the Department of Pediatrics and a Clinical Investigator at BC Children’s Hospital Research Institute\.

In 2020, Dr\. Edmond Chan was also awarded the The F\. Estelle R\. Simons Award for Research in recognition of his exemplary research in the field of Allergy, Asthma, and Immunology from Canadian Allergy, Asthma and Immunology Foundation\.
