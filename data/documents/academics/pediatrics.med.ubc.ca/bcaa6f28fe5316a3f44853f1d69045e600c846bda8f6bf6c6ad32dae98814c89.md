Dr\. Ruth Grunau

Dr\. Ruth Grunau has been awarded 2021 Aubrey J\. Tingle Prize\.

Dr\. Grunau, a professor in the faculty of medicine’s department of pediatrics, has been awarded the Michael Smith Foundation for Health Research \(MSFHR\) Aubrey J\. Tingle Prize\.

The award is one of several recently announced by Life Sciences BC which recognize the talented individuals and organizations who contribute to the vibrant life sciences ecosystem in B\.C\. [\[MORE\]](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/dr-ruth-grunau-awarded-2021-aubrey-j-tingle-prize/)

Congratulations Dr\. Grunau\!
