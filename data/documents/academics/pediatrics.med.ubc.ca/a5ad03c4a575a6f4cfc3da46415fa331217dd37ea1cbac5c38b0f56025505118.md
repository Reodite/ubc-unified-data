[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/07/khildebrand-150x150.jpg)The Royal College National Awards program honours the exceptional personal achievements of specialist physicians and surgeons, and other medical professionals across Canada and internationally\. These awards recognize contributions to the specialty medical field, the Royal College mission, and to the health care of citizens, both in Canada and globally\.

The selection of award recipients is based on a rigorous nomination and review process, overseen by the Royal College’s Awards Committee\. Dr\. Kyla Hildebrand was awarded the 2020 Royal College AMS Donald Richards Wilson Award for CanMEDS integration\.
