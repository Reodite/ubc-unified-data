Kristel Hilarius, she was born in the Netherlands, and studied Medicine at the University of Amsterdam\. She continued her medical training in both Pediatrics and Anesthesia and Intensive Care at the Karolinska University Hospital in Stockholm, Sweden\.

After working as an Anesthesiologist and Intensive Care specialist for 3 years in both the adult and pediatric department at Karolinska, she moved to Vancouver in 2019 for a Pediatric Critical Care Fellowship\. Since graduating from the program in 2021, she has now joined the department as a Clinical Instructor\.
