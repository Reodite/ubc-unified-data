The Pediatric Research Course is organized by The Research Curriculum Advisor and Formal Course Leader and facilitated by the Scholarly Activities Coordinator\.

***Date/ Time/and Location:***

-   The Research Course occurs one Tuesday a month from  12:30\-4:00 pm during Residents Academic Half\-Day \(AHD\) in protected time
-   Sessions are held in Room K0\-157 in the Ambulatory Care Centre Building on the BCCH site

***Attendance:***

-   Attendance is compulsory for residents and fellows enrolled in the course
-   Trainees are excused with valid reason \(e\.g\. vacation, post\-call, conferences, etc\)
-   Attendance is recorded, evaluated and summarized annually by the Scholarly Activities Coordinator
