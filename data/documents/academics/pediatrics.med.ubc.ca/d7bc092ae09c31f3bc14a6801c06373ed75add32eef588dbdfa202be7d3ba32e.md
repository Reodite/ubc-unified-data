We are pleased to announce the appointment of Dr\. Tram Nguyen to the Department of Pediatrics as Clinical Instructor, Community\-based\.

Dr\. Tram Nguyen is a pediatrician with special interests in the areas of pediatric dermatology, social pediatrics, and medical education\.  She completed her medical degree at the University of British Columbia and continued with Pediatric specialty training at McMaster Children’s Hospital, where she completed her training\. She then completed a fellowship in Community Pediatrics at the University of Toronto, at the Hospital for Sick Children with additional training in pediatric dermatology\.  

She currently practices in Richmond as a Consultant General Pediatrician\.
