The Department of Pediatrics, UBC &amp; BCCH; the Division of Nephrology; and, BC Children’s Hospital Research Institute are pleased to announce that Dr\. Susan Samuel, will be joining us on August 1, 2024\.

Dr\. Samuel is a Professor at the Department of Pediatrics, Cumming School of Medicine, at the University of Calgary, Alberta; she is a Pediatric Nephrologist, with Alberta Children’s Hospital; and she was appointed a Canada Research Chair Tier 1, Precision Medicine, and Data Science in Child Health in 2021\.

Since 2018, she has served as the Director for the ENRICH \(Empowering Next\-Generation Researchers in Perinatal and Child Health\) program\. ENRICH is a network of mentors and learners focused on perinatal and child health across Canada\.

She brings a wealth of experience in mentorship, research, advocacy, and education\. Welcome Dr\. Samuel\!⁠
