**Pediatric Chairs of Canada Academic Leadership Awards**

Since 2007, the Pediatric Chairs of Canada \(PCC\) has recognized and promoted excellence in academic pediatric leadership through its Pediatric Academic Leadership Awards\. These awards celebrate outstanding contributions across distinct academic career paths, reflecting PCC’s belief that the health and well\-being of children and youth depend on strong leadership in the areas of clinical practice, medical education, research, and academic administration\.

[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/ManishSadarangani-300x300.webp)**Clinician Investigator Award**

**Dr\. Manish Sadarangani**

Dr\. Manish Sadarangani is an internationally recognized clinician\-scientist whose pioneering work in translational vaccinology has significantly advanced pediatric infectious disease research and vaccine development\. As Director of the Vaccine Evaluation Center at BC Children’s Hospital, he has built one of Canada’s leading academic vaccine research programs, spanning laboratory science, clinical trials, epidemiology, and public health implementation\. His research has informed vaccine policy nationally and internationally, including pandemic response, maternal immunization, antimicrobial resistance, and vaccine effectiveness\. Through his exceptional scholarship, mentorship, and international collaboration, Dr\. Sadarangani has made enduring contributions to pediatric research and innovation\.
