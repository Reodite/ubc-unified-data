***ChildCare 2020: from vision to action***

Thursday\-Saturday, November 13\-15, 2014  
Fort Garry Hotel, Winnipeg, Manitoba  
[Register online](http://cts.vresp.com/c/?FirstCallBCChildandY/7e2a3db007/4652cef49b/5db82a10e2) to secure your spot\.  Space is limited\.  
Find more information at [childcare2020\.ca](http://cts.vresp.com/c/?FirstCallBCChildandY/7e2a3db007/4652cef49b/fc369739ad "ChildCare 2020")

   
Organized by the Canadian Child Care Federation, the Child Care Advocacy Association of Canada and the Childcare Resource and Research Unit, this conference is a rare opportunity to help create a vision for early childhood education and care in Canada for the next decade and beyond\. Join early childhood educators, researchers, policymakers, advocates and parents all working together for a better system\. Help put child care back on the public agenda and connect with others\. English/French translation will be provided for all the plenaries and other sessions\.
