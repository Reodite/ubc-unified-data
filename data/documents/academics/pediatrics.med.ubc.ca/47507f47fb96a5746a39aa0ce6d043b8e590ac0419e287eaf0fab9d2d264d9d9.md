## [Congratulations to Drs\. Tom McLaughlin and Dan Metzger\!](https://pediatrics.med.ubc.ca/2021/04/28/congratulations-to-drs-tom-mclaughlin-and-dan-metzger/)

  The American Academy of Pediatrics has awarded two AAP District VIII Special Achievement Awards to two Department of Pediatrics faculty members\. Dr\. Tom McLaughlin, won the Clinician/Leadership category and Dr\. Dan Metzger, was recognized in the Health Advocacy category\.
