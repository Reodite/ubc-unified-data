For the first time ever, Vancouver will host the International Pediatric Association \(IPA\) Congress from August 17\-22\. More than 2,400 delegates from around the world, including BC Children’s Hospital, will come together to discuss initiatives and research, listen to speakers and take part in a variety of workshops\.
