**New Position at BC Children’s Hospital \(0\.2 FTE\)**

A newly created Pediatrician \(General or Specialist\) position is available at BC Children’s Hospital, one\-day per week to serve in the role of ***Pediatric Subspecialists Electronic Medical Record Leader*** and our representative on the **CERNER Pediatric Leadership Council**\. In collaboration with at least two other CERNER\-supported Children’s Hospitals, the goal of this project is to develop electronic order\-sets and Power Plans for patients hospitalized at BC Children’s who have complex, rare and/or specialty\-specific disorders that will not be easily managed using the General Pediatric electronic order\-sets that are currently being developed by the provincial CST project\.

The position is available now\. Please contact Dr\. Allison Eddy if you are interested: [allison\.eddy@cw\.bc\.ca](mailto:allison.eddy@cw.bc.ca "Allison Eddy")
