Immunizations have saved more lives in Canada than any other health intervention in the last 50 years\. The National Immunization Awareness Week is a publicly funded initiative to strengthen immunization through public awareness\. Take part in this Canada\-wide campaign by visiting the Vaccine Evaluation Center’s information booth held at the cafeteria of the Shaughnessy building at [C&amp;W](http://www.bcchildrens.ca/AboutUs/MapsDirections) on:

**Tues April 29th, 10am\-2pm  [https://med\-fom\-pediatrics\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2014/03/Immunization\.jpg](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/03/Immunization.jpg)[Immunization](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/03/Immunization-360x291.jpg)**

**Thurs May 1st, 10am\-2pm**

You can now record and store your personalized vaccine information in the ImmunizeCA App, available for free on Android, Blackberry, and Apple devices\. For more information, visit [http://immunize\.ca/en/app\.aspx](http://immunize.ca/en/app.aspx)
