Congratulations to Dr\. Lena Xiao, Clinical Assistant Professor from the Division of Respiratory Medicine\.

She is the 2024 recipient of the American Thoracic Society’s \(ATS\) *Carole L\. Marcus Outstanding Achievement Award\.*

This award recognizes career accomplishments in pediatric sleep and respiratory neurobiology\.

ATS aims to improve global health by advancing research, patient care, and public health in pulmonary disease, critical illness, and sleep disorders\.

Congratulations Dr\. Xiao\!
