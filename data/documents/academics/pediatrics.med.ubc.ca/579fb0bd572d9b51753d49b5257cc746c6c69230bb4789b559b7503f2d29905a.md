The Department of Pediatrics would like to welcome Dr\. Erin Peebles to her new role as Undergraduate Program Director\.

[https://med\-fom\-pediatrics\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2022/11/epeeblesheadshot\.png](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/11/epeeblesheadshot.png)[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/11/epeeblesheadshot-150x150.png)​Dr\. Peebles completed her medical school at University of Ottawa, followed by residency at Memorial University, Newfoundland\. She has also completed her Masters in Health Professions Education through Maastrict University\. Dr\. Peebles has a strong interest in undergraduate education, having had numerous undergraduate leadership roles at Western University prior to her move to BC\. Since joining BCCH in 2020, she has been attending on the CTU and Complex Care, and has also been the undergraduate DSSL for BC Children’s Hospital, along with her role as the CTU Education Lead\. We are excited that Dr\. Peebles has accepted this new role and look forward to her contributions to Undergraduate Pediatric Education

[https://med\-fom\-pediatrics\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2022/11/callen\-sor\.jpg](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/11/callen-sor.jpg)[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/11/callen-sor-150x150.jpg)We would also like to acknowledge Dr\. Callen Sor for his 5 years’ leadership as the Pediatric Undergraduate Program Director, and thank him for all his work in this role\. He will be missed and we wish him the best in all of his future endeavours\.

– Mary Bennett, MD FRCPC  
Clinical Professor and Associate Head, Education  
Department of Pediatrics, UBC  
BC Children’s Hospital, Vancouver, BC
