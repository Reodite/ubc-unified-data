National Physicians’ Day is dedicated to recognizing and supporting those who serve in the profession of medicine\. It is celebrated on May 1st annually and honors the birthday of [Dr\. Emily Stowe](https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/emily-stowe), the first female physician to practice in Canada\.

Learn more about our 2023 faculty spotlights: Drs\. [Marie\-Noelle Trottier\-Boucher](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/05/Marie-Noelle-Trottier-Boucher-bio-1.pdf), [Anamaria Richardson](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/05/Anamaria-Richardson-Bio.pdf), and [Caron Strahlendorf](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/05/CStrahlendorf-bio.pdf)\. Let’s celebrate their work and dedication to improving children’s health across the province\!
