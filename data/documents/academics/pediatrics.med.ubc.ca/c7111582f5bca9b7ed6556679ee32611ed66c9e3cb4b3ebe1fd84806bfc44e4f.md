With more than 140 SARS\-CoV\-2 vaccines in development, the race is on for a successful candidate to help prevent COVID\-19\. But—according to a new paper published today in the *[Canadian Medical Association Journal](https://www.cmaj.ca/content/early/2020/07/09/cmaj.201237) \(CMAJ\)*—there are challenges in evaluating the efficacy of these vaccines during the pandemic\.
