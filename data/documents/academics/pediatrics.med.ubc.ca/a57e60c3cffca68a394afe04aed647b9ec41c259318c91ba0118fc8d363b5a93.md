The Pediatric Practice Plan project is a transformational initiative that will support our advancement as an academic health organization\. The current stage of the project will have multiple phases that focus on two primary goals: \(1\) to stabilize our funding model, and \(2\) increasing our ability to collect, interpret and apply key data regarding our core services\.
