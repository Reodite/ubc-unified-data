Please join us as we welcome Dr\. Melissa Paquette to the Department of Pediatrics\.

Dr\. Melissa Paquette is a new general Paediatrician in Kamloops\.  She completed her residency at the Alberta Children’s Hospital in 2013\.

She graduated from the University of Calgary’s Medical Class of 2009 and completed her undergraduate training at the University of Alberta\.  Melissa grew up in Westlock, a small town in Alberta, and always had plans to ultimately live in a smaller city to best facilitate her interest in both hospital and office based medicine\.

She is excited about the medical and recreational opportunities in Kamloops and is honoured to be working with UBC learners\.
