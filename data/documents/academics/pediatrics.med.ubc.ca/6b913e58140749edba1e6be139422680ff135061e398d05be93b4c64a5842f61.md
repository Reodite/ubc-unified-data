The BCCH ChildKind project team submitted the application for certification in July 2022 and received overwhelmingly positive feedback from the international governing body, which granted C&amp;W a site visit – the final hurdle to achieving certification\.

[https://med\-fom\-pediatrics\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2023/03/groupphoto\.png](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/03/groupphoto.png)[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/03/groupphoto-768x312.png) Certification committee members from ChildKind International visited C&amp;W on January 30, 2023\. During the visit, they wanted to understand how BCCH meets the five principles of the ChildKind framework, how we manage pain and promote comfort using the practices of the Comfort PACT, and how our pain care makes a positive impact for patients and families\.
