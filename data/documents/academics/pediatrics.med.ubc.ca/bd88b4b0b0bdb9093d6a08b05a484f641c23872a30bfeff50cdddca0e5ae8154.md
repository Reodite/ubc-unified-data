Dr\. Nancy Lanphear, Clinical Associate Professor, Developmental Pediatrics

Dear Colleagues,

I would like wish Dr\. Nancy Lanphear all the best in her next chapter as she travels abroad with her husband\.

Nancy is leaving the department as Head, Division of Developmental Pediatrics at UBC, a role she has held since July 1, 2008 and she is also stepping down as Senior Medical Director, Child Development and Rehabilitation at BCCH since 2009\. Her last day with us will be December 4, 2022\. [\[MORE\]](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/11/nancylanphearangieip-announcement-final.pdf)

With best wishes,  
Steven
