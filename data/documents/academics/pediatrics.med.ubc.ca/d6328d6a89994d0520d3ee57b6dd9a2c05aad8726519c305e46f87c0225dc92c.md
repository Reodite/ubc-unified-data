### Resident Research Funding Competition Application Form

### Resident Research Funding Competition Application Form

The deadline for 2024\-2025 Competition is after the winter break\. Results are announced before February 15\. To learn more, contact [pediatrics\.research@ubc\.ca](mailto:pediatrics.research@ubc.ca)

---

#### What you need to Know

Since 2009, the Department of Pediatrics is recognizing resident initiated research projects with a special ‘Resident Research Grant Competition’\.

A total of $3,000 is available for the annual grant competition, and applications can be up to this amount\. The Department of Pediatrics Research Committee will review and deliberate to determine grant approval\. Partial award may be offered\.

---

#### What Can Be Funded?

**Eligible expenses include all activities that cannot be completed by the resident within the context of the research project\.**

e\.g\. costs for mailing, software, stationery, project supplies, copyright, consultant fees, specific lab tests or covering local travel related to the project, chart review

---

#### What Cannot Be Funded?

**Activities that should be done by the resident in the context of the research project:**

e\.g\. hiring a research assistant to extract information from medical records or getting consents from parents or interviewing families

---

#### Costs related to scientific presentations:

e\.g\. fees associated travel and accommodation to a conference will not be considered acceptable\.  
**Statistical consultant fees**\-residents are provided free of charge  
**Items covered by your supervisor’s budget**

---

#### What to Include in the Application?

Completed and signed application form
