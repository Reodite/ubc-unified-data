## [Congratulations to Dr\. David Wensley, 2021 award recipient of the Dr\. Don Rix Award for Physician Leadership\!](https://pediatrics.med.ubc.ca/2022/06/07/congratulations-to-dr-david-wensley-2021-award-recipient-of-the-dr-don-rix-award-for-physician-leadership/)

Last month, Dr\. David Wensley was honoured by the Doctors of BC with a Lifetime Achievement Award\. “It is the highest honour that they bestow\.
