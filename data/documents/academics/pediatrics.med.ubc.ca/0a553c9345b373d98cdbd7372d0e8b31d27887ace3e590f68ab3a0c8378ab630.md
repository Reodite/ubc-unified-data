Dr\. Jonathan Wong, Clinical Assistant Professor, UBC

**Name:**  
Dr\. Jonathan Wong

**Role:**  
Clinical Assistant Professor, UBC  
Neonatologist, BC Women’s Hospital

**Qualifications:**  
BSc, MD, MScCH

**Biography:**  
Dr\. Wong completed medical school at the University of Toronto, pediatric residency training at Queen’s University, and sub\-speciality training in neonatal\-perinatal medicine at the University of Toronto\. His areas of interest include neonatal respiratory medicine, health informatics and technology, and medical education in which he has a master’s degree in Health Practitioner Teacher Education from the University of Toronto\.
