*Vision 2028: Providing World Class Education and Cultivating Scholarly Excellence in Pediatric Health Research*

Updated Mar 28 2025

Members from Pillar 2 recently held a productive Working Group meeting to advance the Vision 2028 Strategic Plan Pillar 2 initiative: Supporting robust education, compelling research and scholarly activity\.

Building on the foundation established by Dr\. Steven Miller and over 120 faculty and staff, the Working Group explored innovative and collaborative ways to strengthen the integration of research, education, clinical work and community engagement, highlighting the department’s commitment to both academic excellence and clinical care\.

Key themes emerged from the discussions, centered on creating a more cohesive and supportive academic environment\. The group identified the need for enhanced infrastructure, both in clearer job plans that allow for non\-patient facing activities, as well as to bridge the gap between clinical and research activities\. For the latter, there is potential to create a new leadership role, such as an Associate Head of Research and Innovation\. Participants emphasized the importance of building strong professional networks and fostering social capital through initiatives like departmental research days and mentorship programs\.

A significant focus was placed on redefining success metrics to better reflect the diverse nature of academic contributions, including teaching innovations, quality improvement work and community engagement\. The group proposed expanding beyond traditional productivity measures to incorporate qualitative aspects of professional fulfillment and clinical impact\. Discussions highlighted the value of “creative professional activities” – scholarly work encompassing education, quality improvement and community engagement – as essential components of academic achievement\.

Looking ahead, the department plans to implement several initiatives, including developing more comprehensive feedback mechanisms for clinical impact assessments, enhancing support for academic career development and creating opportunities for greater collaboration between the Department of Pediatrics and the BC Children’s Hospital Research Institute\. These efforts aim to cultivate an environment where both individual and collective achievements are valued, ultimately leading to improved pediatric care across British Columbia\.
