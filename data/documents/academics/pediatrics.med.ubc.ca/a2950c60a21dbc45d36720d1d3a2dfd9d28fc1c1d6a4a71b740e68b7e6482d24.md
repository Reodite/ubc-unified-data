Steven Paul Miller, MDCM FRCPC FRSC FCAHS Professor and Head, Dept Pediatrics, Faculty of Medicine, UBC, Chief Pediatric Medicine, BC Children’s Hospital

Updated May 20, 2026

Dr\. Steven Paul Miller has served as Head of the Department of Pediatrics in the Faculty of Medicine at UBC, and as Chief of Pediatric Medicine at BC Children’s Hospital, since April 2022\. His early interest in pediatric neurology led him to study the relationship between pain, the newborn brain, and congenital heart disease—work he continues today with the neonatal neurology research group at BCCHR\. Previously, he was Head of Neurology and Head of the Garry Hurvitz Centre for Brain &amp; Mental Health at SickKids Hospital and the University of Toronto\.

Under Dr\. Miller’s guidance and stewardship, the Department of Pediatrics is now halfway through a new course he is leading for our collective\. His vision supports a thriving community that drives excellence and integration across pediatric care, research, and education, enabling the best possible outcomes for every child, youth, and caregiver in BC and beyond\. Read more about our new plan in [Vision 2028\.](https://pediatrics.med.ubc.ca/about/our-strategic-direction/)
