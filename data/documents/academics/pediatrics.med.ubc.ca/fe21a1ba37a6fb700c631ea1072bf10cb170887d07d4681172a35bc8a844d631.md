Run to work every day, uphill both ways? Power walk like you’re on a mission? Whether you’re a seasoned

pro or run like you’re in slow\-mo, come out on April 17th and join the Department of Pediatrics Team at the 32nd Annual Vancouver Sun Run\. This is a great opportunity to enjoy the Spring weather and enjoy the company of your fellow colleagues\.

Don’t run? Don’t worry\! The team will be participating in a 10K walk and will leave no one behind\!

The Sun Run starts at 9:00am but the fun will continue afterwards at Hurricane Grill in Yaletown to replenish all those burned calories\!

To join the PEDS Sun Run team, click here to be taken to the [registration](http://www.vancouversun.com/sunrun/entry+fees+deadlines/5568848/story.html?__lsa=433f%E2%80%902bd1) site and follow these [instructions](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/02/Screen-Shot-2016-02-17-at-8.52.24-AM-e1455729144754.png)\.

For more information, contact Michelle Man at [michelle\.man@cw\.bc\.ca](mailto:michelle.man@cw.bc.ca)
