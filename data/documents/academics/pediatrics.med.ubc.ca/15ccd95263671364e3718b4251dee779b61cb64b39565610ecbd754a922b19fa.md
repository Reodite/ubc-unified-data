On May 31, 2023 at the University Golf Club, the UBC Faculty of Medicine celebrated the academic achievements and distinguished careers of faculty members\.

Recipients from across the faculty wore black gowns to mark their recent promotions to Professor, Clinical Professor or Professor of Teaching\.

**New Promotions:** Drs\. Mariana Brussoni and Pascal Lavoie were both promoted to the rank of Professor by Department Head, Dr\. Steven Miller\.

**Also honored were:**  
Dr\. Allison Eddy, Professor Emeritus  
Dr\. Sheila Pritchard, Clinical Associate Professor Emeritus  
Dr\. Richard Schreiber, Clinical Professor Emeritus  
Dr\. Sandra Whitehouse, Clinical Associate Professor Emeritus
