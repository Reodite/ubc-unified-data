The Spring 2016 edition of the Pharmacy Informer is hot off the press\!

The Pharmacy Informer is a quarterly newsletter that contains important updates and highlights from the Children’s &amp; Women’s Health Centre of BC, Department of Pharmacy\.

**The Spring edition includes:**

-   *Benzodiazepines for Seizure Rescue*
-   *Amoxicillin for Community Acquired Pneumonia*
-   *Ivacaftor/lumacaftor: A New CFTR Modulator*
-   *Corticosteroids for Acute Asthma*
-   *Intravenous Procedural Sedation &amp; Analgesia*
