Congratulations to Drs\. Manish Sadarangani, Mariana Brussoni, and Angela Devlin on their awards\!

**Dr\. Manish Sadarangani \| Overall Excellence — Mid\-Career**  
Recognizes outstanding contributions in the areas of research, education and service\.

**Dr\. Mariana Brussoni \| Service to the University and Community**  
Recognizes significant contributions to local, national or international communities aimed at improved health outcomes of the population\.

**Dr\. Angela Devlin \| Excellence in Education**  
Recognizes significant contributions to health education in the areas of leadership, innovation and/or scholarship of education\.

Kudos to our HR Dept for their support with the nomination applications\.

Congratulations to all of the 2024 recipients\!
