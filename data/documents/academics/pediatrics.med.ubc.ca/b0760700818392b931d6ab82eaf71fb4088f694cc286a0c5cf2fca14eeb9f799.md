[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/06/group-3.jpg)The UBC Faculty of Medicine’s strategic plan, *[Building the Future](http://stratplan.med.ubc.ca/)*, identifies 21 strategic objectives organized into four pillars: Education, Research, Organization, and Partnership\.

To enable investment in advancing the objectives, the Faculty created a Strategic Investment Fund of $2M per year\. This Fund was made possible through the Program Budgeting and Marginal Analysis \(PBMA\) exercise\. [\[MORE\]](https://mednet.med.ubc.ca/AboutUs/StrategicPlanning/Strategic-Investment-Fund/Pages/default.aspx)

Congratulations Drs\. Allison Eddy, Stuart Turvey, Laura Sly, Soren Gantt, and Shazhan Ahmed\!
