The Noni MacDonald Award recognizes an article, published in *[Paediatrics &amp; Child Health](http://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=0011kUh5ksdfm9uBGoRBbogE4ER5EOdcYCkeXl4FZInXJh3-BrkWVZigedbHQAlFX-Mr0F1ha5EYTYISg9g-H5CdLkMzgHD3SzFTKPkjB8csQsOHF1jRMluLityV0WOV_zsW9WkAoX4i9rFJTtYk-a7ZoEeOsj850S3fywSgrchlhagJZmLKkZOuQ==&amp;c=qlthCJzagPLpIPwIQACdC1tzi2AJtUyb7KqnnMdfxhgLOg6aSTAqmg==&amp;ch=ODyCbysZ0HniJ97OaNsjDyTtEoaJsvOKhpG4RFnUrxqM1M4fjEKIQw==)*, which has positively affected paediatric medicine\.

This year’s award goes to [***Canadian Pediatric Endocrine Group extension to WHO growth charts: Why bother?***](http://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=0011kUh5ksdfm9uBGoRBbogE4ER5EOdcYCkeXl4FZInXJh3-BrkWVZigcCh98Z7xCkHlQdTFfPHj5KJbcMbyQ4r-DzoO7s42N0zSkWZWwYRkh-eq_7cmXLv6TsQpae_reui2g-CtIeSREzK9ym6QcZrYKQ36sT-XnNn8EfTt7LLXN7W-F1rMNysMRvCFFY9bfQI7TksGE_Da0w_PmvJLCp-KZgH1s-fycZVozRhja8qbVK5sa1CC8gmeCnnlLKKuqfcl-ls76Dip5ZfCFoA_45312FCSVhCec980r_SCzDcNy9TO1HmZy1nV2KvOKVjqw3Q3T3psCHg6bksDUKOohxnPQ==&amp;c=qlthCJzagPLpIPwIQACdC1tzi2AJtUyb7KqnnMdfxhgLOg6aSTAqmg==&amp;ch=ODyCbysZ0HniJ97OaNsjDyTtEoaJsvOKhpG4RFnUrxqM1M4fjEKIQw==)***,*** which highlights complementary growth curves developed by the Canadian Pediatric Endocrine Group, based on the 2010 WHO Growth Charts for Canada: [https://pediatrics\.med\.ubc\.ca/cpeg\-growth\-charts/](https://pediatrics.med.ubc.ca/cpeg-growth-charts/ "CPEG Growth Charts")

Authors are Dr\. Sarah Lawrence, Dr\. Elizabeth Cummings, Dr\. Jean\-Pierre Chanoine, Dr\. Daniel Metzger, Dr\. Mark Palmert, Dr\. Atul Sharma and Dr\. Celia Rodd\.
