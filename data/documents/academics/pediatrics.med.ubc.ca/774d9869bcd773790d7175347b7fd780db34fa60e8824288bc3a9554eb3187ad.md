We are pleased to announce the appointment of Dr\. Sara Leo, Clinical Instructor, to the Division of Allergy &amp; Immunology, Department of Pediatrics, BC Children’s Hospital\. Dr\. Leo studied Molecular Biology and Biochemistry at Simon Fraser University and completed her medical degree at the University of Alberta\. She completed her residency program in Pediatrics and continued with fellowship training in Allergy &amp; Immunology at BC Children’s Hospital\. She is delighted to be joining the Department\!

Please join us as we welcome Dr\. Leo to the Department\!
