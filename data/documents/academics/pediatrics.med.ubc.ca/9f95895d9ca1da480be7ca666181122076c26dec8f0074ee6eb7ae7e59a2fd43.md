We are pleased to announce the appointment of [Dr\. Paul Rogers](http://cfri.ca/our-research/researchers/results/Details/111), Clinical Professor, Department of Pediatrics, Division of Hematology/Oncology, as Secretary\-General to the board of the [International Society of Pediatric Oncology \(SIOP\),](http://www.siop-online.org/ "SIOP") for the term 2013\-2016\.

SIOP was established in the late 1960’s and has grown into a dynamic and vibrant global organization with over 1000 members worldwide, including doctors, nurses, other health\-care professionals, scientists and other researchers\. SIOP members are dedicated to increasing knowledge about all aspects of childhood cancer; its mandate is to improve and optimize treatments throughout the world\. **SIOP’s vision is that no child should die of cancer****\.**
