*From Dr\. Tyler Black, Chief Medical Informatics Officer and Nancy Kotani, Vice\-President, Inter\-Hospital Services*

We are pleased to report that **PowerChart has officially replaced EVE** **as our primary place to view clinical results**\. Dictated reports have also begun populating Cerner\.

**To obtain access to Cerner PowerChart**

**1\.**  C&amp;W Manager, Coordinator, or UBC academic program assistant must submit Application Access IMITS eForm located at [http://imitsrequest](http://imitsrequest/)\.

Physicians can submit their own request\.

**2\.**  Clinicians and staff must complete the required eLearning\.

Please enroll on LearningHub for the **[Cerner \(eChart\): All Clinicians and Staff](https://learninghub.phsa.ca/Courses/6062/cerner-echart-all-staff-and-clinicians-required-elearning)** **eLearning Curriculum** and register for the courses that you need to complete\.  The **[Cerner PowerChart \(eChart\): Basics](https://learninghub.phsa.ca/Courses/5385/cerner-powerchart-echart-basics)** course is required for access\.

You will receive an email containing your Cerner account \(user name\) and log\-in instructions

**Call the eChart Command Centre x2632 if you require urgent Cerner PowerChart access to provide patient care\.**

**What does this mean to you?**

-   **All NEW** lab and medical imaging results and dictated reports are being fed to PowerChart *\(only\)\.*
-   EVE is now a historical viewer *\(see Available Data diagram below\)\.*
-   New Dictation phone numbers are active\.
-   Patient MRN numbers will remain up\-to\-date in EVE\.
-   For downtime information refer to: [eChart Downtime procedures](http://teamsites.phsa.ca/sites/eChart/Web%20Pages/Downtime.aspx) – \[[http://teamsites\.phsa\.ca/sites/eChart/Web%20Pages/Downtime\.aspx](http://teamsites.phsa.ca/sites/eChart/Web%20Pages/Downtime.aspx)\]\.
-   **Excelleris** will be used to distribute all signed transcribed reports via fax or electronically to your Excelleris Launch pad web portal or EMR\.

**Below you’ll find the Change Notice:**

**Changes to Transcribed Documents and Discontinuation of Lab feeds to EVE**

**On June 24** **th**, all dictating authors using Central Transcription Services will be impacted by the C&amp;W site\-wide deployment of the new **M\*Modal** dictation/Transcription system\. All transcribed reports will be viewed, edited, and signed in the **Cerner PowerChart Message Center**\.

[https://med\-fom\-pediatrics\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2014/06/Message\-Center\.png](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/Message-Center.png)[Message Center](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/Message-Center-360x119.png)

Ensure you have access to PowerChart \(eChart\) by completing the on\-line training course in the Learning Hub: **Cerner \(eChart\) – All Staff and Clinicians \(3 brief courses, including e\-Sign and Viewing Results\)\.**

Of particular importance is to complete the **Message Centre – Signing Transcribed Documents** course which reviews the **NEW WAY OF SIGNING YOUR REPORTS**\. Reports are only distributed once they are signed\. The time period for reports to be signed remains at 14 days\.

Transcribed reports in **EVE** will only be available for pre June 24th historical data\. **Reports dictated prior to June 24** **th** **will still need to be signed in EVE**\.

**Dictation ID** can be obtained M\-F 8:00 to 4:00 from **Transcriptions Services** 604\-806\-9696 or email [transcriptionalerts2@vch\.ca](mailto:transcriptionalerts2@vch.ca)

The following **Lab feeds to EVE are discontinued** **as of 12:00 25\[th\] June\.** The information from these systems will now be sent to **Cerner PowerChart \(eChart\)**:

-   **PHSA Sunquest Lab and CoPath Information Systems**
-   **Newborn Screening**
-   **Laboratory Genetics**
