[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/02/jaclyn-strauss-146x150.jpg)Dr\. Jaclyn Strauss, one of our current R3’s was awarded the International Abstract of Distinction for her resident research work entitled “Catheter Related Complication in Central Venous Catheters Locked with Taurolidine vs Ethanol in pediatric Patients with Intestinal Failure” at the American Society for Enteral and Parenteral Nutrition \(ASPEN\) in Las Vegas earlier this year\. ASPEN is one of the largest and most prestigious nutrition conferences in North America\.

Congratulations Jaclyn\!
