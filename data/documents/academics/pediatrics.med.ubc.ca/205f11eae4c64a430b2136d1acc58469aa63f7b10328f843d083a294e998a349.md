2021 Faculty of Medicine Distinguished Achievement Award Recipients have been announced and Drs\. Julie Bettinger &amp; Stuart Turvey’s contributions have been recognized\.

Each year, the Faculty of Medicine recognizes faculty members who exemplify exceptional contributions in the areas of education, research and/or service, and who are dedicated to advancing both the Faculty’s values and vision of transforming health for everyone\. [\[MORE\]](https://www.med.ubc.ca/news/2021-faculty-of-medicine-distinguished-achievement-award-recipients/)

[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/08/jbettinger.jpg)Dr\. Julie Bettinger has been recognized in the Excellence in Clinical or Applied Research category for her outstanding clinical or applied research and scholarly contributions\.  
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/08/sturvey.jpg)Dr\. Stuart Turvey has been recognized in the Overall Excellence – Senior Faculty category for his outstanding contributions in the areas of research, education, and service\.  
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
Congratulations both\!
