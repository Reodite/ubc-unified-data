[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/06/judge-227x300.png)Dr\. Judge is the Pediatric Emergency Medicine Practice’s \(PEMP\) 2016 “Issue of the Year” award winner for his article entitled, “Congenital Heart Disease in Pediatric Patients: Recognizing the Undiagnosed and Managing Complications in the Emergency Department\.” The award recognizes the efforts of authors who have expanded and enhanced the body of evidence\-based emergency medicine literature via EB Medicine’s premier journal\.

The article can be found at: [http://www\.ebmedicine\.net/topics\.php?paction\=showTopic&amp;topic\_id\=502](http://www.ebmedicine.net/topics.php?paction=showTopic&amp;topic_id=502)

Congratulations to Dr\. Judge\!
