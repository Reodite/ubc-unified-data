This National Pediatrician Day, we’re shining a spotlight on some of our pediatricians whose expertise and compassion transform lives every day\. For 2026, we are spotlighting the contributions of Drs\. Jill Houbé, Daniel Metzger, Mona Patel, Nicholas Avdimiretz, Margaret Choi and David Wensley\.

Through exceptional clinical care, groundbreaking research, and inspiring education, they ensure that every child in BC reaches their fullest potential\.

Join us in celebrating their remarkable contributions\.

To all those who dedicate their careers to children’s health — thank you\!
