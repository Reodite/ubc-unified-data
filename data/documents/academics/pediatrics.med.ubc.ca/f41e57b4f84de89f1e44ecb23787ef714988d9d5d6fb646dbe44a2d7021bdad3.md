[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/07/Shrewe-brett-150x150.jpg)Two UBC doctoral students, Stephanie Lake \(Faculty of Medicine\) and Brett Schrewe \(Faculty of Education\), have received the prestigious 2017 Pierre Elliott Trudeau Doctoral Scholarship\. They are the 22nd and 23rd recipients of this scholarship from UBC since its inception, and Brett is the first from educational studies\.

To learn more about Dr\. Schrewe and the 2017 Pierre Elliott Trudeau Doctoral Scholarship, please see: [https://www\.grad\.ubc\.ca/about\-us/news/two\-ubc\-doctoral\-students\-awarded\-2017\-pierre\-elliott\-trudeau\-doctoral\-scholarships](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/about-us/news/two-ubc-doctoral-students-awarded-2017-pierre-elliott-trudeau-doctoral-scholarships)

Dr\. Schrewe is a Clinical Assistant Professor with the Department of Pediatrics\.

Congratulations Dr\. Schrewe\!
