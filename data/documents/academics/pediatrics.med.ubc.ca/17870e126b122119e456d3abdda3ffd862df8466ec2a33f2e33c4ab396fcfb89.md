We are pleased to welcome Dr\. Anne Marie Jekyll to the Department of Pediatrics as Clinical Instructor, Division of General Pediatrics, Community\-based\. Dr\. Jekyll is a General Pediatrician working in the community\. She completed a Bachelor of Science with a major in Physiology at McGill University in 2004 and graduated from medical school at UBC in 2008\. She completed her pediatric residency at UBC at BC Children’s Hospital in Vancouver in 2012\. During her residency she acted as Associate Chief and Chief Resident\. Since completing her residency program she has been practicing in various communities including Vancouver, North Vancouver, Prince George and Penticton in community pediatrics\.  She has also been working part\-time in the Spinal Cord Clinic at BC Children’s Hospital\.
