Tatiana Sotjindo and Ms\. Sabrina Gill for their anti\-racism and inclusion work at BC Children’s Hospital and BC Women’s Hospital \+ Health Centre \(C&amp;W\)\.

The People of Colour Mentorship Program was developed to address diversity in leadership, and with seed funding from the HPIF, they began the process of creating a formalized mentorship program with C&amp;W’s BIPOC \(Black, Indigenous, and people of color\) staff\.
