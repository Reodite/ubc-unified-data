We are delighted to announce that Dr\. Quynh Doan has been appointed Associate Dean, Research, BC Children’s Hospital Research Institute \(BCCHR\), UBC Faculty of Medicine and the Senior Executive Director, BCCHR for a five\-year term effective to June 30, 2028\.

Dr\. Doan is an associate professor in the Department of Pediatrics’ Division of Emergency Medicine and a health services research scientist at UBC\. She is also the Director of Clinical Research at BCCHR, where she has served as interim Senior Executive Director since December 2021\.
