Dr\. David Smith is the 2020 recipient of the *Life Membership* at the Canadian Paediatric Society \(CPS\)\. The CPS Life Membership recognizes CPS members who have made exceptional contributions to the Society over a long period of time\. Dr\. Smith was on the CPS Board for 6 years as the BC representative and then President in 2000\-2001\. He has continued to do committee work for the organization and he is currently the English speaking representative on the Resolutions committee \.[\[MORE\]](https://www.cps.ca/en/awards-prix/life-membership)

Congratulations to Dr\. Smith\!
