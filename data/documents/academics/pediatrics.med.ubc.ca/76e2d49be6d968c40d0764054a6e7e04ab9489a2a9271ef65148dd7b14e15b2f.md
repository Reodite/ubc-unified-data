Each year, the Faculty of Medicine recognizes faculty members who have made exceptional contributions in the areas of education, research and/or service, and who are dedicated to advancing both the Faculty’s values and vision of transforming health for everyone\.

Congratulations to Dr\. Brett Schrewe, based at our Island Medical Program at the University of Victoria\!

For 2025, he was awarded the Excellence in Education Award which recognizes significant contributions to health education in the areas of leadership, innovation and/or scholarship of education\.
