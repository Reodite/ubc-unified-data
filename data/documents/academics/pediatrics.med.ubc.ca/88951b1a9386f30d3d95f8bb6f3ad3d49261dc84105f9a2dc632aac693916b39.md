In September’s issue of The Lancet Child &amp; Adolescent Health, Drs\. Anton Miller, Gurpreet \(Preety\) Salh and Nancy Lanphear’s article “Asking difficult questions about fetal alcohol spectrum disorder in the context of the child, the mother, and the systems in which they live” argue that the term fetal alcohol spectrum disorder has contributed to a culture of racism and discrimination for many who are diagnosed with it\.
