The Department of Pediatrics is pleased to have participated in the UBC Alumni event: [Healers of children: What is the latest thinking about kids’ health?,](http://www.alumni.ubc.ca/2014/webcasts/healers-of-children-what-is-the-latest-thinking-about-kids-health-2/ "Healers of children: What is the latest thinking about kids' health?") on February 19, 2014\.

[https://med\-fom\-pediatrics\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2014/03/HealersOfChildren\-757x422\-560x312\.jpg](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/03/HealersOfChildren-757x422-560x312.jpg)[HealersOfChildren\-757x422\-560x312](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/03/HealersOfChildren-757x422-560x312-360x200.jpg)

The expert panel featured [Dr\. Edmond Chan,](http://www.alumni.ubc.ca/2014/events/healers-of-children-what-is-the-latest-thinking-about-kids-health/#chan "Dr. Edmond Chan") [Dr\. Barbara Fitzgerald](http://www.alumni.ubc.ca/2014/events/healers-of-children-what-is-the-latest-thinking-about-kids-health/#fitzgerald "Dr. Barbara Fitzgerald"), [Dr\. Margaret Colbourne](http://www.alumni.ubc.ca/2014/events/healers-of-children-what-is-the-latest-thinking-about-kids-health/#colbourne "Dr. Margaret Colbourne"), and [Dr\. Kristin Houghton](http://www.alumni.ubc.ca/2014/events/healers-of-children-what-is-the-latest-thinking-about-kids-health/#houghton "Dr. Kristin Houghton"), our notable specialists in Allergy &amp; Immunology, Developmental Pediatric s, Emergency Medicine and Sports Medicine, respectively\. Our panel was moderated by Global TV’s Sophie Lui and distinguished truth from fiction, providing sage advice for parents\.
