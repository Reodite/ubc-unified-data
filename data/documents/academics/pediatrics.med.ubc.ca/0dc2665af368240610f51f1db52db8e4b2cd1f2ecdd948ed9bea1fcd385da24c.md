*[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/03/white-christine-150x150.png)Dr\. Christine White, MD, MAT,* is a graduate of the University of Missouri Columbia School of Medicine\. She completed her residency in pediatrics and chief residency at Cincinnati Children’s Hospital Medical Center\. Dr\. White’s interests and research focus on quality improvement\. She has led hospital wide efforts to increase medication reconciliation completion\. She is currently leading institute\-wide improvement projects on improving capacity management and the patient/family experience\. In 2010, she was the recipient of the Faculty Teacher of the Year Award\. And, in 2015 she was the recipient of the Cincinnati Children’s Hospital Medical Centre, Cincinnati Children’s Research Foundation “Excellence in Clinical Care” faculty award\.

The presentation will take place at the Chan Auditorium, Friday, May 19, 2017, 830\-8930 am\.
