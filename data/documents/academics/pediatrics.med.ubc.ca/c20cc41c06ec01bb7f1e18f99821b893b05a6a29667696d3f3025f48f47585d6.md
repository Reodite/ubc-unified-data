**Drop\-in Sessions starting again at the REB\! –** Do you have questions about your RISe application?  Are you unclear if you need REB review?  Confused about your provisos?

The C&amp;W UBC Research Ethics Board Administrative staff are holding drop\-in information sessions every Friday from 9:30\-11:00 am\.  Sessions will be held in the REB office located in the CFRI administration area of the CFRI Translational Research building \(A2\-140/141\)\.  We’ll be happy to answer any questions you may have regarding research ethics at C&amp;W\.

Please be aware that if that time doesn’t work for you – we are always here to answer your questions\.  Email us, call us or arrange an alternate time to come and meet with us \(we don’t bite\!\)\.  Our contact information can be found on our website at [http://cfri\.ca/research\-support/reb/contacts](http://cfri.ca/research-support/reb/contacts)\.
