**[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/11/schultz-kirk-126x150.jpg)**Dr\. Kirk Schultz is the recipient of the 2017 Dr\. Judith Hall Award from the BC Pediatric Society\. Dr\. Schultz is the Director, Michael Cuccione Childhood Cancer Research Program, Co\-Lead, Childhood Cancer &amp; Blood Research Group, and Professor, Division of Hematology and Oncology, Department of Pediatrics, University of British Columbia\.

The award comes with a $500 cheque which will be presented to the charity of Dr\. Schultz’s choice\.

Congratulations Dr\. Schultz\!
