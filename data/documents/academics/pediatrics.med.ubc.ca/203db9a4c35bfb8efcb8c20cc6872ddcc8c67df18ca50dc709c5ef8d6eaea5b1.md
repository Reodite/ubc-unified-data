On Friday, February 17th, 2023, Drs\. Sotindjo and Kissoon, will be discussing “Black lives, Black voices and Black identities in the Faculty of Medicine\.”

Together with other members on the panel, this talk will explore IBPOC experiences, knowledge, and challenges\.

This virtual event is part of the UBC FoM IBPOC Voices series and will be facilitated by Dr\. Neila Miled an Anti\-racism Advisor with REDI\.
