Did someone special help you with your research? Perhaps someone provided an inspirational spark? An annual competition offers residents an opportunity to show their appreciation for a mentor that supported their research learning\. The award is announced at the annual Banquet for the Department of Pediatrics\. The mentor will receive a personal plaque and be added to the Department of Pediatrics special “Resident Research Faculty Mentor of the Year” plaque\. They will also have bragging rights for years to come\.

---

**What to include in your submission letter?**  
The nomination letter should be up to 500 words\.

-   Title of the research project\(s\)
-   Duration of time working with the mentor\.
-   Brief description of the reasons for your nomination
-   Availability of the mentor
-   Mentorship quality
-   Support provided to you as a resident or fellow
-   Encouragement, empowerment
-   Mentor ability to promote your work, including presentations at conferences and meetings
-   How did the mentor enable you to write and submit manuscripts and publications
-   Current state of the project

Please submit your nomination letters to Sophia Li at [pediatrics\.research@ubc\.ca](mailto:pediatrics.research@ubc.ca)

**Call for nominations is announced in the Spring with deadline details\.**
