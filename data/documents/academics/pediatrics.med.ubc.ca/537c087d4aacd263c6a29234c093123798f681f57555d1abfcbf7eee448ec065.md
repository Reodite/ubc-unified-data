[krames](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/10/krames-169x300.jpg)Magnetic resonance imaging scientist Dr\. Christian Kames from [Dr\. Alexander Rauscher’s UBC MRI lab](http://mriscience.med.ubc.ca/) has won the 2016 International Quantitative Susceptibility Mapping \(QSM\) Reconstruction Challenge for creating the best method currently available\. QSM is a technique that turns an MRI scan into a high resolution map of magnetic properties of biological tissues\. Due to its high sensitivity, QSM promises to become a marker for tissue damage and repair in the central nervous system\.

Congratulations to Dr\. Kames\!
