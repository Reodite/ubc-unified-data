[metzger](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/10/metzger-219x300.png)The Canadian Society of Endocrinology and Metabolism \(CSEM\) has awarded Dr\. Daniel Metzger the DR\. HARVEY GUYDA EDUCATOR OF THE YEAR AWARD\.

CSEM will be recognizing Dr\. Metzger’s excellence in teaching and his significant contribution to the teaching of endocrinology on:  
October 29, 2016  
12:45PM – 1:30PM  
Westin Ottawa, Governor General III

Congratulations to Dr\. Metzger\!
