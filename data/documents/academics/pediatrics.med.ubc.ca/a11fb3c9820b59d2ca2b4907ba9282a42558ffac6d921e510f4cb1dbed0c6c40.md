Updated Sept 16 2025

The Department of Pediatrics, UBC &amp; BCCH, is a multidisciplinary team of physicians, scientists, allied health professionals, and administrative staff, all trained in pediatric and child health specialties\.

Our members include full\-time, part\-time, and community\-based pediatricians\. The Department is committed to leadership and excellence in clinical care, education, research, and child advocacy\.

We bring together the expertise, knowledge, and leadership needed to deliver specialized care to newborns, children, and youth across BC Children’s Hospital and BC Women’s Hospital, while fulfilling the academic mission of the UBC Faculty of Medicine in teaching, research, and service\.

As the only provincial clinical and academic Department of Pediatrics, we embrace both the unique challenges and the opportunities that come with this responsibility\.
