[quinn, stephany](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/08/quinn-stephany-259x300.jpg)Dr\. Quinn joined the department on August 1, 2016 as a Clinical Associate in the Division of Neonatology\.

Dr\. Quinn completed her undergraduate education at Walsh University, North Canton, Ohio on soccer scholarship and she completed medical school at McMaster University in 2012 and completed her paediatric residency at UBC in 2016\. Her long term plan in general paediatrics includes global health and child protection\.
