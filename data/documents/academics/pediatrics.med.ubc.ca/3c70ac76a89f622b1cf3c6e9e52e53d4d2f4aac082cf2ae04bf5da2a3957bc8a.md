As 2023 draws to a close, I would like to express sincere gratitude for our work together\. I am so impressed at how our community comes together with compassion to support one another, our patients, and their families\.

As we grow as a community, we have a lot to celebrate from 2023\. A few highlights include:

• We launched Vision 2028 with four strategic priorities  
• We welcomed 17 new Clinical and 5 Academic faculty, and 8 of our faculty were promoted  
• We received 31 FTE Workload Funding to recruit more clinicians  
• Our new Fraser Site launched, and the first two residents started in July  
• The Sunny Bear Clinic was created and will continue to support Emergency intake  
• Our research funding increased a full 10%  
• Late 2023 Fiscal year, BCCH Foundation increased support to hire 10 additional Clinical Fellows for next year  
• Our faculty was recognized in 2022 with 24 awards, the highest number in the past decade  
• And, we received funding for 12 Associate Physicians, the first department to use this new medical class of physicians licensed by CPSBC\.

As we look ahead to the holiday season, I wish you all peace, joy, and time with loved ones,

Steven Paul Miller MDCM FRCPC FRSC  
Hudson Family Hospital Chair in Pediatric Medicine, James &amp; Annabel McCreary Chair in Pediatrics  
Professor and Head, Pediatrics \| Faculty of Medicine \| University of British Columbia  
Chief, Pediatric Medicine \| BC Children’s Hospital
