Dr\. Anil Chacko, Clinical Assistant Professor, UBC

**Name:**  

Dr\. Anil Chacko

**Role:**    
Clinical Assistant Professor, UBC  
Medical Lead, Neonatal POCUS Program  
Neonatologist, BC Women’s Hospital

**Qualifications:**  

MBBS, MD, MRCPCH, CCT\(UK\), FRCPC

**Biography:**         

Dr\. Anil Chacko obtained medical degree and post graduate qualification from Bangalore University\. He completed specialist training from the United Kingdom, McMaster Children’s Hospital and SickKids Hospital Toronto\. Trained in the use of point\-of\-care ultrasound in newborn critical care, he leads the Neonatal POCUS Program at BCWH NICU\.
