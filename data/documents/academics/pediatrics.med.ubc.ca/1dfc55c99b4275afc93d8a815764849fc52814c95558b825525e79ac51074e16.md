## [Congratulations to recipients of the 2018 MSA Recognition and Achievement Awards\!](https://pediatrics.med.ubc.ca/2018/06/21/congratulations-to-recipients-of-the-2018-msa-recognition-and-achievement-awards/)

The Medical Staff Association of Children’s and Women’s Hospital \(MSA\) presented the Recognition and Achievement Awards to several Department of Pediatrics faculty\. These awards are held annually to spotlight medical staff at BC Children’s Hospital and BC Women’s Hospital \+ Health Centre for their contributions in delivering exceptional mentorship and care to colleagues and patients \[…\]
