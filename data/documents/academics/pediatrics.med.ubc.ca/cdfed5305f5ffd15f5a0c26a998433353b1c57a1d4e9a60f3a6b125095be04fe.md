Sexual and gender\-related violence \(SGRV\) against girls in sub\-Saharan Africa remains a major crisis, impacting mental health and well\-being\. Despite existing efforts, 1 in 3 women still experience gender\-based violence in their lifetime\. A powerful intervention in Uganda used music to raise awareness—celebrity artists created a song and video highlighting key issues, reaching millions through social media, concerts, and national broadcasts\. This approach shows promise for other countries to create localized versions that spark change and support survivors\. Read more about Dr\. Macnab’s work\.
