Dr\. Saleem Razack, Professor, Division of Critical Care, Department of Pediatrics, Faculty of Medicine, University of British Columbia

Dr\. Saleem Razack, MD, FRCPC, has been announced as the 2023 Professor\-in\-Residence by the Royal College\.

Dr\. Razack, whose research focuses on equity, diversity and inclusion, is a pediatric intensive care physician\. He recently joined the Department of Pediatrics at UBC located at BC Children’s Hospital after a 25\-year career as a pediatric intensivist and medical educator/education researcher at McGill University\. Currently a CanMEDS educator at the Royal College, his volunteer work has previously extended to exams and accreditation\.
