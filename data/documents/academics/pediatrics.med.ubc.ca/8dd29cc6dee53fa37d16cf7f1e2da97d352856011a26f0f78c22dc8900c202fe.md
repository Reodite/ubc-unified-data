The Division of Allergy &amp; Immunology is excited to announce two big awards from the Canadian Society of Allergy and Clinical Immunology:

[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/10/cbiggs-150x150.jpg)Dr\. Catherine Biggs was awarded *The CSACI Early Career Award* which is presented to a CSACI Fellow, who has been working post\-fellowship for five years or less, and has shown contribution to the CSACI\.

[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/10/echan-150x150.jpg)Dr\. Edmond Chan was awarded the *The F\. Estelle R\. Simons Award for Research* in recognition of his exemplary research in the field of Allergy, Asthma, and Immunology\. To even be considered for this award, the candidate’s research is to be viewed as significant and of benefit to the specialty\.

Congratulations All\!

Stuart Turvey MBBS DPhil FRCPC
