The Child &amp; Family Research Institute \(CFRI\) celebrates the researchers and trainees who have recently received awards and grants\.

**2013 CFRI Awards**

[**Dr\. Sandra Dunn**](http://www.cfri.ca/our-research/researchers/results/Details/34 "Dr. Sandra Dunn") and **Dr\. Chris Dunham**, Childhood Cancer &amp; Blood Research – Michael Cuccione Childhood Cancer Research Program Brain Tumour Grant – Project: “Personalizing the treatment of pediatric medulloblastoma\.” $200,000/2 years\.

[**Dr\. James Chinten Lim**,](http://www.cfri.ca/our-research/researchers/results/Details/360 "Dr. James Lim") Childhood Cancer &amp; Blood Research – Michael Cuccione Childhood Cancer Research Program Brain Tumour Grant, start\-up funding – Project: “Regulation of brain tumor chemo\-and radioresistance by integrin\-mediated cell adhesion\.”  $15,000/1 year\.

[**Dr\. Christopher Maxwell**,](http://www.cfri.ca/our-research/researchers/results/Details/389 "Dr. Chris Maxwell") Childhood Cancer &amp; Blood Research – Michael Cuccione Childhood Cancer Research Program Brain Tumour Grant – Project: “RHAMM is needed to balance the growth and differentiation of neuroepithelial progenitor cells\.” $50,000/1 year\.

[**Dr\. Rod Rassekh**](http://www.cfri.ca/our-research/researchers/results/Details/292 "Dr. Rod Rassekh"), Childhood Cancer &amp; Blood Research – Michael Cuccione Childhood Cancer Research Program Brain Tumour Grant, start\-up funding – Project: “Outcomes in brain tumors treated with a chemotherapy only approach\.” $15,000/1 year\.
