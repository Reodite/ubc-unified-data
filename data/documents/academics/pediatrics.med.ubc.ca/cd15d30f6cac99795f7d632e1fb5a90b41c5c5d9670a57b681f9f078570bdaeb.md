We are excited to share our year two progress updates of our strategic plan, *VISION 2028: Inspiring a Brighter Future of Pediatric Health Through Inclusion and Innovation* which was built with input from across all levels of our community\.

As a collective, we continue to aspire to be a thriving community driving excellence and integration in pediatric care, research, and education—enabling the best potential for every child, youth, and caregiver in BC and beyond\.

Through collaboration throughout our organization and across the province, we have defined our Department’s priorities within four strategic pillars\. This plan integrates our roles in clinical care, education, research, and child\-health leadership\.

Our strategic plan reflects several key aspirations and values that define our community: a deep commitment to health justice and equity; a vision to broaden collaborations with health authorities and strengthen our work with Child Health BC to ensure the highest quality of clinical care across the province; a pledge to pursue scholarly excellence; and above all, a dedication to foster a supportive work environment rooted in compassion and respect\.
