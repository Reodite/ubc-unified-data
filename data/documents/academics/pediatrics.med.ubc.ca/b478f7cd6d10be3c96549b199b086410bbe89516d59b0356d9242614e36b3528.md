You are invited[http://www\.opsei\.bc\.ca/](http://www.opsei.bc.ca/)[OPSEI](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/05/OPSEI.jpg) to the **10\[th\] Anniversary of the Office of Pediatric Surgical Evaluation and Innovation** on Wednesday, June 4, 2014\.

To help celebrate OPSEI’s anniversary, we have organized a [special programme](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/05/OPSEI-10th-Anniversary-and-Celebration-Programme.pdf "OPSEI 10th Anniversary Celebration") for this occasion and hope that you will be able to attend\.

All sessions will be held in the CFRI Auditorium with the exception of the Pediatric Surgery Team Appreciation Lunch which will be held in the Shaughnessy Auditorium\.   Please RSVP to Bindy Sahota at [bsahota@cw\.bc\.ca](mailto:bsahota@cw.bc.ca "Bindy Sahota")
