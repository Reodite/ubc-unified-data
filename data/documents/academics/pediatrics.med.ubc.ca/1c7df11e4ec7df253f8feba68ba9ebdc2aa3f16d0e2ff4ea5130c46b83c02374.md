## [Congratulations Ella Chan\!](https://pediatrics.med.ubc.ca/2020/03/11/congratulations-ella-chan/)

Ella Chan has been awarded “Best Oral Presentation” for her talk on Compensatory Renal Growth Predicts Outcome in Children with a Solitary Functioning Kidney at the 2020 Western Medical Research Conference\. Based on judges’ scoring, her work was highlighted from over 350 high quality student and trainee presentations\. Congratulations on this achievement\!
