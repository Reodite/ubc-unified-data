**[PowerChart](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/05/PowerChart-219x300.jpg)**

**Cerner PowerChart,** a component of our eChart \(Electronic Health Record\) project, will become the *primary place* to view patient lab results, radiology results and reports, and to edit and sign transcribed documents\. It is also where you can access the Provincial eHealth Viewer \(CareConnect\) for patient information from across BC\.

**EVE** will remain “view only” for historical lab and transcribed reports\.
