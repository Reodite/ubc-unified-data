The Research Project is a key component of the research curriculum that should culminate in conference presentations and publications\. By the September of their second year, residents must have a research faculty supervisor and an identified research project\. In May of their first year, residents review their research project progress with the Research Director and Scholarly Activities Coordinator to work towards identifying a faculty research supervisor and research project theme\. Procedure:

-   A Research Project Application Form, signed by the resident and supervisor is submitted to the Research Director for approval;
-   After year one, residents submit a Research Project Follow\-up Form to the Research Director;
-   describing past activity and activity plans  for the next 12 months;
-   Residents have the opportunity to apply for a research month elective involving their research project during their residency;
-   A goal for residents, sub\-specialty residents &amp; fellows is to present their research projects at Celebrate Pediatric Research Day held each March\.

In addition our trainees are offered opportunities and encouraged to present their research projects at conferences\.

-   Trainees are expected to obtain approval from their faculty research supervisor to submit their research abstracts to conferences and to address funding issues and travel expenses if their abstract is accepted;
-   Trainees must also secure appropriate permission from their program to be permitted time to present their research work;
-   The Residents’ Activities Fund supports residents attending and/or presenting at conferences within an annual budget allowance and limited funding related to materials for research presentations\.

Trainees are encouraged to publish their research results in medical journals for which they receive support from their research faculty supervisors\. Residents are invited to nominate their research faculty supervisor for the annual *Faculty Research Mentor Award,* initiated in 2012\.
