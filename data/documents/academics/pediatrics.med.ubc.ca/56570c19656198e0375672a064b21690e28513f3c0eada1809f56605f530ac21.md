The UBC Faculty of Medicine recently revised its [policy on clinical faculty appointments\.](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/appointment-policies/) These updates are in direct response to feedback from clinical faculty to clarify policies and processes for appointments, expand opportunities for promotion, and align rank criteria to more fully recognize the distinctive and significant contributions of clinical faculty\. To learn more about the changes, all clinical faculty are invited to [register](https://www.med.ubc.ca/clinical-faculty/appointment-policies/) for one of four information sessions taking place this fall\.

NOTE: This message only applies to Clinical Faculty \(not Academic Faculty or Partner Faculty\)\.
