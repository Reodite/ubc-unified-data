[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/10/victoria-cook-108x150.jpg)Victoria Cook, a pediatric clinical immunology and allergy fellow at the Faculty of Medicine, has been [selected](https://icreblog.royalcollege.ca/2017/09/06/congratulations-to-dr-victoria-e-cook-frcpc-dr-mark-e-lipson-frcsc-winners-of-the-2017-kristin-sivertz-resident-leadership-award/) as one of two recipients of the 2017 Kristin Sivertz Resident Leadership Award\. This award was established to recognize residents who exemplify leadership within Canadian specialty education and encourage the development of future leaders in medicine\.
