We are pleased to announce the appointment of Dr\. Kyla Hildebrand, Clinical Assistant Professor and Program Director, to the Division of Allergy &amp; Immunology, Department of Pediatrics, BC Children’s Hospital\.

Dr\. Hildebrand is a Pediatric Clinical Immunologist and Allergist\. She completed medical school at the University of Manitoba, Pediatrics Residency at the University of Alberta, and a fellowship in Clinical Immunology and Allergy at the University of Toronto\. While pursuing her specialty fellowship, Dr\. Hildebrand completed a Master of Science in Community Health \(Health Practitioner Teacher Education Program\) at the University of Toronto and was the 2010 recipient of the RCPSC Fellowship for Studies in Medical Education\.

Dr\. Hildebrand is newly recruited to UBC from Western University where she started her academic career\. Her research interests include medical education, primary immunodeficiency and asthma\.

Welcome, Dr\. Hildebrand to the Department of Pediatrics\!
