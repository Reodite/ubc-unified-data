[Image](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/06/msa-333x300.jpg)The Medical Staff Association of Children’s and Women’s Hospital \(MSA\) presented the Recognition and Achievement Awards to several Department of Pediatrics faculty\. These awards are held annually to spotlight medical staff at BC Children’s Hospital and BC Women’s Hospital \+ Health Centre for their contributions in delivering exceptional mentorship and care to colleagues and patients in the areas of quality patient care, safety, education, leadership, research and Indigenous outreach\.

*The MSA is comprised of the physicians, dentists, and midwives who hold a permit to practice medicine, dentistry, and midwifery in the facilities and programs operated by PHSA and who have been appointed to the medical staff\.* 

**QSVI Champion**  
Dr\. Ashley Roberts    

The Quality Safety and Value Initiatives champions improve the quality and safety of health care delivery at BC Children’s and BC Women’s\.

**Rookie of the Year**  
Dr\. Mia Remington

This award honours new medical staff members who have made outstanding contributions to the medical staff community and are in their first five years of practice\.

**Mentor/Educator of the Year**  
Dr\. Kyla Hildebrand

This award celebrates medical staff members recognized as outstanding teachers\.

**Leadership Recognition Award**  
Winners: Dr\. Garth Meckler

The Leadership Recognition award highlights medical staff members whose efforts in a leadership role have made a significant difference to our working lives\.

**Research Recognition Award**  
Dr\. Stuart Turvey

This award acknowledges medical staff members who dedicate most of their time to further the research goals of BC Children’s and BC Women’s and beyond\.

**President’s Award**  
Dr\. David Wensley

Recipients of the President’s Award have been nominated in any of the above categories and are recognized for their multi\-faceted outstanding contributions to BC Children’s and BC Women’s\.

Photos from the awards ceremony are available at [https://msacw\.ca/msaawards/](https://msacw.ca/msaawards/)
