Dr\. Synnes is the recipient of the *2024 Distinguished Neonatologist Award* from the Canadian Paediatric Society\.

She is a neonatologist, clinical professor, researcher, and leader in neonatal follow\-up\.

Her passion is improving outcomes for vulnerable children born sick or preterm\.

Dr\.Synnes was a steering committee member of the Canadian Neonatal Network from its earliest days, and the founding director of the Canadian Neonatal Follow\-Up Network\.

This award is granted to a neonatologist who has made exceptional contributions to the field of neonatology, particularly in Canada\.

Congratulations to Dr\. Synnes\!
