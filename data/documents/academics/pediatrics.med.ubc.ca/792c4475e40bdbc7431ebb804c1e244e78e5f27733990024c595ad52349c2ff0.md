[jasmineallaire](https://med-fom-pediatrics.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/08/jasmineallaire.jpg)Congratulations to Dr\. Jasmine Allaire recipient of the *2016 Faculty of Medicine Award for Innovation in CME/CPD*\. This award recognizes the outstanding innovation and creativity with original work in CPD program development and continuing medical education research\. The committee was very impressed by the innovation and impact of Dr\. Allaire’s *Pediatric Advanced Trauma Simulation* course\.

The Awards Reception will be held on Wednesday, October 5, 2016 at 5:30 pm at the University Golf Club\.

Dr\. Jasmine Allaire is a Pediatric Emergency Physician in the Division of Emergency Medicine\.
