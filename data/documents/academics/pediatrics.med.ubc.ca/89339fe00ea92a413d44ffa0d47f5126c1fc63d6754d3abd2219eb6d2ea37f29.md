## [Congratulations to Dr\. David Smith\!](https://pediatrics.med.ubc.ca/2020/05/04/congratulations-to-dr-david-smith/)

Dr\. David Smith is the 2020 recipient of the Life Membership at the Canadian Paediatric Society \(CPS\)\. The CPS Life Membership recognizes CPS members who have made exceptional contributions to the Society over a long period of time\. Dr\.
