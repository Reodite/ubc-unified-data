## Science Advising

Science Advising supports the academic success of students in the Faculty of Science\.

Neuroscience students can meet with Science Advising to receive help with general inquiries\. Topics include:

• applying for a minor  
• taking a term off  
• faculty level requirements  
• taking summer courses  
• determining if you’re eligible to graduate

Science Advising also provides support to students with extenuating circumstances impacting their academic life\.

[See Science Advising](https://science.ubc.ca/students/advising)

## Frequently Asked Questions

  

### How can I apply to join the Neuroscience program?

See our [applying to Neuroscience](https://neuro.ubc.ca/undergraduate/apply/) page for details on how to join the Neuroscience program\. The page includes details for how to apply if you're not yet at UBC, if you are in first year Science, or if you're seeking to switch specializations\.

### Is it possible to register in an NSCI course without being in the Neuroscience Program?

All Neuroscience courses are restricted to students in the Undergraduate Program in Neuroscience with the exception of NSCI 140, NSCI 301, NSCI 302, NSCI 303, NSCI 311, NSCI 401, and NSCI 487A\. NSCI 140 is a 100\-level course open to anyone at UBC\. It is not a requirement for students in the Undergraduate Program in Neuroscience\. Students with a specialization in Neuroscience will receive first priority when enrolling in NSCI 301, NSCI 302, NSCI 303, NSCI 311, NSCI 401, and NSCI 487A\. However, students not in the Undergraduate Program in Neuroscience may still register for waitlists for these Neuroscience courses through Workday\.

### Is there a minor available in Neuroscience?

There is no minor available in Neuroscience at this time\.
