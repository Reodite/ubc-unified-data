## NSCI 448

## 

Learn more about our course NSCI 448: Directed Studies in Neuroscience\.

### On This Page

-   [Course Information](https://arts-upn-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/undergraduate/courses/nsci-448/#course-info)
-   [Application Procedure](https://arts-upn-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/undergraduate/courses/nsci-448/#example)

## Course Information

A 3 or 6 credit directed investigation of a neuroscience research question requiring a report of the findings\. Students who wish to pursue a Directed Studies course can choose between two types of projects: empirical projects and review projects\. Students must have at least a 70% average in the preceding 30 credits, permission of the supervisor, and permission of the director of the neuroscience specialization\. This course can be taken in years 2 – 4 of a student’s degree\.

## Application Procedure

• Arrange supervision with a UBC faculty member doing neuroscience research\. The [Graduate Program in Neuroscience faculty list](https://neuroscience.ubc.ca/faculty/) is a good starting point for your search for a supervisor\.

• Submit a [NSCI 448 Directed Studies Application Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3mWnJr60zXsHoLc)\.

• Receive approval of your project from the Course Coordinator\.

• Register in NSCI 448\. This will be done by the program once your project is approved\.

NSCI 448 Directed Studies courses are either 3 credits or 6 credits\. Students are expected to work 9 or 18 hours per week, as follows:

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Credit Value** | **Term** | **Dates** | **Hours per week** |
| 3 credits | Winter Term 1 | Sept to Dec | 9 hours per week |
| 3 credits | Winter Term 2 | Jan to Apr | 9 hours per week |
| 3 credits | Summer | May to Aug | 9 hours per week |
| 6 credits | Winter Term 1\+2 | Sept to Apr | 9 hours per week |
| 6 credits | Summer | May to Aug | 18 hours per week |

For a summer Directed Studies, the application must be submitted by **May 15, 2026**

For a winter Directed Studies Term 1 start, the application must be submitted by **September 11, 2026**\. For a Term 2 start, the application must be submitted by **January 8, 2027**\.
