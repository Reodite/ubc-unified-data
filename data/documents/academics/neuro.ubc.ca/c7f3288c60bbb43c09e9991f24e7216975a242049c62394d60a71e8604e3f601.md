## Undergraduate

## 

The Undergraduate Program in Neuroscience is a 4\-year Bachelor of Science major offered by the UBC Vancouver Departments of Psychology, Cellular &amp; Physiological Sciences, and Zoology\.

## Undergraduate Program in Neuroscience

The [Undergraduate Program in Neuroscience](https://neuro.ubc.ca/undergraduate/program/) brings together research and teaching expertise spanning multiple faculties and departments to offer a unique interdisciplinary approach to neuroscience\.

The Undergraduate Program in Neuroscience leverages UBC’s strength as a centre for research in neuroscience to provide Bachelor of Science students with an excellent background in neuroscience that will position them to apply their unique training to tackle complex problems in healthcare, research, and policy\.
