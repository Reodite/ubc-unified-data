## Land One info session

First year at university can be daunting\! Land One is a program for first\-year students in the Faculty of Forestry \(FRST\) and the Faculty of Land and Food Systems \(LFS\) that offers a unique small\-group experience to help you transition from high school to university\. All Land One students attend lectures together and have a more personalized connection with instructors\. In general, students have access to a designated study space, creating a smaller social and learning community, and learn through group work, field trips and various hands\-on activities\. Land One instructors are working hard to create online activities that achieve all of these goals\.

If you want to learn more about this unique opportunity, attend this online info session where you will meet the program’s coordinator and former Land One students\.
