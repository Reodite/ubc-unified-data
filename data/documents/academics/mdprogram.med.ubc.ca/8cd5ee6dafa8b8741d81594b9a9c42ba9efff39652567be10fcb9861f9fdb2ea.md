On **June 5th,** the MD Admissions Office will be launching a **new version of the Online Application System \(OAS\)**\. As the new system is **not connected** to the current one, the transition will affect applicants differently depending on their application status:

#### **Current &amp; Previous Applicants \(2024\-25 Cycle and Earlier\)**

If you applied during the 2024\-25 MD Admissions cycle or in any prior cycle, you’ll need to access your application through a **legacy URL, which will be provided when the new system is launched\.**

Please ensure you **download and save** any application materials you wish to keep\. Access to the legacy system will be **time\-limited** and available only until the end of September 2025\.

#### **Recently Accepted or Waitlisted Applicants**

Applicants who have received an offer or are currently on the waitlist, starting **June 6\[th\], 2025**, should use a **legacy URL, which will be provided when the new system is launched\.**

**You will be able to:**

-   View and accept offers of admission
-   Access new student information
-   Communicate with the MD Admissions Office

As we prepare for this transition, the next **round of offers will resume on June 9\[th\]**

#### **Future Applicants \(2025\-26 Cycle and Beyond\)**

Planning to apply for the 2025/26 admissions cycle?

The new Online Application System will be available at ***oas\.med\.ubc\.ca*** starting **June 6\[th\], 2025**\. However, the application cycle will not open until **mid\-June**\. Please check our [Admissions website](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/) regularly for updates\.

#### **System Outage – June 5\[th\], 2025**

Please note that the Online Application System will be **temporarily unavailable on June 5\[th\]** as we transition to the new platform and move the current system to the legacy URL\.

For any questions or help navigating the transition, please contact us at [**admissions\.md@ubc\.ca**](mailto:admissions.md@ubc.ca)\.

We appreciate your patience as we move to a new application experience\!
