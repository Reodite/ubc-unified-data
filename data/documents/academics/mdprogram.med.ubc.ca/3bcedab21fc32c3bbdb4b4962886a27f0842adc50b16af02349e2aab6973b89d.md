[https://med\-fom\-ugrad\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2013/04/Town\-Hall\-pics\_Page\_1\.jpg](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/04/Town-Hall-pics_Page_1.jpg)[Town Hall pics\_Page\_1](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/04/Town-Hall-pics_Page_1-360x214.jpg)Dawn DeWitt, Associate Dean, Undergraduate Medical Education will be holding the final in a series of three Town Halls on the topic of Undergraduate Medical Curriculum Renewal on **Thursday, April 11th from 7:30am to 9:00am\.**

This session is one facet of our curriculum renewal communications plan, aimed at communicating in an open forum where we will:

-   provide you with information on the future direction of our MD Undergraduate curriculum renewal project and progress to date,
-   answer your questions, and
-   solicit your feedback\.

Faculty members  who have been closely involved in our curriculum renewal initiative will sit on a panel to address questions on the renewal of our undergraduate curriculum\.  The panel session will be approximately 60 minutes in duration\.  Here are the particulars:

**Venue:**  The Diamond Health Care Centre Lecture Theatre DHCC 1020, 2775 Laurel Street

**Date:**  Thursday, April 11\[,\] 2013

**Time:**  7:30am – 9:00 am

Presenters, Facilitators and Panelists are to be announced\.  

**Before** and **during** the town hall,  attendees are urged to submit any questions they would like answered about curriculum renewal or this townhall to the email address **[crtownhalls@ubc\.ca](mailto:crtownhalls@ubc.ca)**
