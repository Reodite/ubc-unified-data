The University of British Columbia’s \(UBC\) MD Undergraduate Program \(MDUP\) has been granted continued full accreditation through Spring 2033 by the [Committee on Accreditation of Canadian Medical Schools \(CACMS\)](https://cacms-cafmc.ca/)\.

Accreditation is a rigorous, peer\-reviewed process through which medical education programs leading to the M\.D\. degree demonstrate compliance with nationally accepted standards of educational quality and effectiveness\. Administered by CACMS, this process assures the public that a medical school provides the education, resources and institutional support that prepares graduates to become competent, ethical and compassionate physicians\.

## Accreditation Status Summary

-   **Current Status:** Full Accreditation through Spring 2033
-   **Next Major Submission:** Status Report due December 1st, 2027
-   **Visit Date:** Spring 2033

## Findings

Of the 96 CACMS Elements that were assessed:

-   92 Elements were rated “Satisfactory”
-   Of the 92 Elements, 11 Elements were rated “Satisfactory with a need for monitoring”
-   4 Elements were rated “Unsatisfactory” and require additional action and monitoring

These findings reflect the program’s strong foundation in educational excellence, as well as our commitment to ongoing quality improvement\. The Faculty of Medicine and the MDUP will continue to address identified areas for development to ensure the program meets and exceeds national accreditation standards\.
