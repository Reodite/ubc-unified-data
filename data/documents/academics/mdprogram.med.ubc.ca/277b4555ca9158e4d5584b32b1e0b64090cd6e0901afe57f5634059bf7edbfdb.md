There has been a server end related issue with our OAS website with intermittent access to the site throughout the night and into this morning\. We are currently investigating and will provide an update as soon as we can\.

**UPDATE 7:05 AM – Pacific Time:** Due to the unexpected website issues, we will not be adhering to the deadline today, we will post more information on this as soon as we can\. For now, we ask that all applicants refrain from trying to submit their applications or login to the website until we have resolved the issues\. We will inform you both through email and through this blog when the issues have been resolved\.

**UPDATE 3:05 PM – Pacific Time:** This issue has been resolved\. Given that this issue arose in the night prior to our application deadline, we are extending the application and transcript deadline to Wednesday, September 16, 2026 at 3:00 PM Pacific Time\. Thank you for your patience as we have worked to resolve this matter\.

**Some users may be experiencing login issues\.** If you see a prompt for a 2FA token, this token will be emailed to your email address\. If you do not get a 2FA prompt, you will have to reset your password using the Forgot Password feature\. If after trying these things you are unable to login, you may then contact us\.
