The Indigenous\* Student Initiatives Manager develops and manages support services and programs for current Indigenous medical students, works with MD students and post\-grad residents who have an interest in Indigenous health, and acts as a faculty advisor to the Aboriginal Health Initiative, a UBC medical student organization\.

*\*Indigenous refers to non\-status &amp; status Indians, Treaty, First Nations, Métis and Inuit peoples of Canada\.*
