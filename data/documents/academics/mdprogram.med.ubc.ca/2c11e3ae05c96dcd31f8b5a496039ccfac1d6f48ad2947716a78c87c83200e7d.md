## **General Program Overview**

The Fraser East MD Undergraduate Program \(MDUP\) Integrated Community Clerkship \(ICC\) site has been in place since 2004, making it the longest running integrated clerkship in Canada\. The clerkship has many enthusiastic teachers in participating hospitals and surrounding communities\. While the Fraser East ICC originated in Chilliwack, clinical experiences also occur in Abbotsford and, to a smaller extent, other surrounding communities\. All are a short commute by car\.  Family physicians serve as the primary care\-providers for several of the participating hospitals\. Opportunities for learning are available from several facets of medicine including nursing staff, paramedics, occupational and physiotherapists, and clinical counselors\.

This clerkship requires students to be highly motivated, flexible and self\-directed\. It is also necessary to be able to work as a functioning member of a very dynamic team\.

## **Specific Experiences**

Students are scheduled on a ‘mini\-block’ rotation, meaning students spend two or three weeks at a time with specialty preceptors and return to a given rotation one to four times in the academic year\. A typical rotation week consists of three days with a specialty preceptor, one half\-day with a family preceptor, academic half\-days and one flex day where students can pursue their own interests in medicine\.  
Rotation sites largely include hospitals and/or clinic offices in the cities of Chilliwack \(home site\) and Abbotsford\.  Depending on availability, some time may also be spent in Hope, Mission and other surrounding communities, all within a reasonable commute time by car\.

-   #### **Family Medicine**
    
-   Students in the Fraser East ICC are attached to a family medicine preceptor with whom they work one half day per week, typically Thursday mornings\.
-   Many of these preceptors have special interests such as geriatric medicine, addiction medicine and obstetrical care\.
-   Students are encouraged to develop their own roster of patients for which they take responsibility and assume many aspects of care, under the supervision of their primary preceptor\.
-   Preceptors and medical staff have a long history of medical teaching, from nursing students to medical students and Family Medicine residents\.
-   #### **Flex Weeks**
    
-   The Fraser East ICC site offers several weeks of flex where students can choose areas of interests\.
-   Special interest rotations are available in a wide variety of specialties\. Some of these include Urology in Chilliwack, and Oncology through the BC Cancer Agency in Abbotsford\.
-   #### **Academic Half Days \(AHD\)**
    
-   Academic half\-days are held Thursday afternoons and include:
-   Meeting with the Site Director\.
-   Medical case presentations by other students in the clerkship\.
-   Seminars, simulations and workshops hosted by local specialty preceptors and residents\.

#### **Family Medicine Residency Program**

-   This ICC location is also home to a Family Medicine Residency Program with eighteen residents per year; as part of training, medical students and residents learn together in a supportive and mutually beneficial environment\.
-   Students can work with residents in each department, including on overnight call in the hospital – a fantastic learning opportunity for Fraser East ICC students\.
-   Overnight call is considered an invaluable time for one\-on\-one learning with residents\.

## **Participating Hospital Sites \(all accessible via commute by car\)**

**Chilliwack General Hospital \(CGH\) – Chilliwack, BC**  
[Image](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/Chilliwack-General-Hospital-1-e1724450595426-360x241.jpg)

-   CGH is considered the Fraser East ICC’s “home site” where students experience core rotations and many specialty interests\.
-   CGH has 170 beds and serves the population of Chilliwack and surrounding area\.
-   Services include Emergency Medicine, Internal Medicine, Orthopedics, Urology, Obstetrics and Gynecology, Ophthalmology, Otolaryngology, Pediatrics, Radiology, Psychiatry, Anesthesia, Geriatrics, and Palliative Care\.

**Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre \(ARHCC\) – Abbotsford, BC**  
[Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/Abbotsford-Regional-Hospital-and-Cancer-e1724450449964.jpg)

-   ARHCC is the secondary site where students experience several rotations and specialty interests\.
-   ARHCC has approximately 300 beds and serves Abbotsford and much of the surrounding region in Fraser East\.
-   Services include Emergency Medicine, General Surgery, Internal Medicine, Medical Imaging \(including MRI, CT scanning and Cardiac Services\), Maternity and Pediatric Care \(including a neonatal intensive care unit\), Dialysis, and a full range of cancer services\.

**Mission Memorial Hospital \(MMH\) – Mission, BC**  
[Mission Memorial Hospital](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/Mission-Memorial-Hospital-1-e1724451712525-360x240.jpg)

-   MMH currently provides additional Emergency Medicine experiences\.
-   MMH has a 25\-bed medical inpatient unit and 20\-bed PATH unit \(Patient Assessment and Transition Home\)\. It is also home to a 10\-bed Christine Morrison Hospice\.
-   Services include Emergency Medicine, General Medicine and several ambulatory care services\.
-   MMH has a highly integrated network of community\-based programs and services\.

**Fraser Canyon Hospital \(FCH\) – Hope, BC**

[Fraser Canyon Hospital](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/08/Fraser-Canyon-Hospital-e1724451864323-360x240.jpg)

-   FCH is currently the most rural location in Fraser East’s ICC\.
-   FCH is a 10\-bed community hospital that provides additional Emergency Medicine exposure\. Field service to the community of Boston Bar’s Family Medicine Clinic may also be available\.
-   Services include Emergency Medicine and General Medicine\. There are also several ambulatory care services supported by an integrated network of community\-based programs, ranging from health promotion and prevention, to residential and hospice care\.
