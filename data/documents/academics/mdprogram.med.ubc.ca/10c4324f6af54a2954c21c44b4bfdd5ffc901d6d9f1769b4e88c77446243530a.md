Our online application system \(OAS\) is an essential part of how we run our admissions for the MD Undergraduate Program and how we will communicate with you during the admissions process\. The application system houses a copy of all the messages we send to applicants\. We recommend checking your messages for important updates from the Admissions Team about your application\. 

**Note:** We send both general messages and messages to specific applicants\. It is your responsibility as an applicant to check your email and/or review the Communications tab in the OAS for new messages\. 

## Useful Resources

We encourage you to review these resources prior to beginning your application\.

-   **Applicant Guide:** The MD Undergraduate Program application is long and requires very specific information\. The [Applicant Guide](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/when-you-apply/applicant-guide/) is here to help you\. Read it before you begin and refer to it as you fill in each section of the application\.
-   **Documentation Checklist:** A list of all required [supporting documents](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/when-you-apply/applicant-guide/) to include with your application\.

## Notice:

The MD Admissions Office  launched a **new Online Application System on** **June 6** **th****, 2025, application data from the 2024\-25 and earlier cycles is no longer available to be viewed online\.** If you are looking to access this information you will need to email the MD Admissions Office at [admissions\.md@ubc\.ca](mailto:admissions.md@ubc.ca) and provide us with the following information:

1.  Your full name at the time of application\.
2.  The year or cycle you last applied\.
3.  The email you used to apply\.
4.  If you have a UBC student number \(if applicable\)\.

We can only provide you with a copy of your application forms\.
