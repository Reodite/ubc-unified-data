As an important part of our mandate, UBC is committed to making our Doctor of Medicine program as accessible as possible\. We recognize that our applicants come from a wide range of backgrounds and we encourage and celebrate this diversity within the MD Undergraduate Program\.

We also recognize that the path to medicine can be a complex, time consuming, and often expensive process\. As part of our efforts to minimize existing barriers to application we are pleased to share that in collaboration with The Association of Faculties of Medicine of Canada \(AFMC\), UBC’s Medical School new Application Fee Waiver Program is now open for the 2026/27 application cycle\.

The Application Fee Waiver Program is open to all residents of British Columbia and is intended support applicants who are prevented from applying, or likely to undergo significant financial strain, due to the cost\. Fee waivers are limited in number, so those with the greatest demonstrated financial need will be prioritized\.

If you wish to apply for an application fee waiver, please carefully review and follow all the steps outlined in the [UBC Fee Waiver Program Application Guide](https://www.afmc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026_UofBC_Fee_Waiver_EN_FINAL.pdf)\. As part of this process you will also be asked to fill out a [UBC Fee Waiver Application Form](https://www.cognitoforms.com/AFMC3/UBC_Fee_Waiver_2026)\. Please visit the [AFMC’s website](https://www.afmc.ca/strategic-priorities/education/aspirants-applicants/) for a complete overview of the process\. Information specific to the UBC Application Fee Waiver Program can be found towards the bottom of the webpage\.

**The fee waiver application period is now open\. It will close on August 19, 2026 at 8:59 PM Pacific Time\.** Applicants will receive notification of fee waiver receipt by August 24, 2026 and will then have until the September 15\[th\] application deadline to complete their application\. Submitting a fee waiver application does not guarantee that a fee waiver will be issued\. Funding is limited and unfortunately, we may not be able to meet all requests\. Please do not assume you have been granted a waiver until you receive formal notification from the Admissions Office\.

All applicants can create an account in the [Online Application System](https://www.oas.med.ubc.ca/security/login.aspx?ReturnUrl=%2fdefault.aspx) before paying any application fees\. We ask that you do so if applying for an application fee waiver so we can most easily communicate with you\. If you have already paid any application fees, or do so in advance of the fee waiver notification date, we will refund the waived fees should a fee waiver be granted\. We recommend only paying UBC’s application fees in advance of fee waiver notification if you intend to apply regardless of the outcome\.

Please review the table below and ensure that you are aware of all deadlines\. You can [contact AFMC](mailto:info@afmc.ca) at any time with questions about the fee waiver application process\. All decisions will be adjudicated by the AFMC directly\. UBC will not have access to any personal documents or financial information provided to the AFMC and this information will not be used to determine any Admissions decisions outside of the application fee waiver process\.

We wish you all the best as you work on your application, and encourage you to message us through the Communications tab in your application profile if you have any questions\.
