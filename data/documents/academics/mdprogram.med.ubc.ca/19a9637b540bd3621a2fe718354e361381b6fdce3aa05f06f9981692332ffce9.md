In response to the ongoing COVID\-19 pandemic, the 2022 MMI will once again be taking place in a completely virtual format\. This decision has been made to ensure the continued safety of all our participants including both applicants and interviewers\. More information regarding the virtual format will be provided in December when interview notifications are released\.  

All virtual interviews will be completed over the first two weekends of February 2022 \(February 5, 6, 12 &amp; 13\)\.
