Shadan Ashrafi, UBC medical student

A group of UBC medical students will be showcasing their theatrical talents this week for MedPlay, an annual production performed and produced by learners in collaboration with a professional director from the community\.

Each year, students volunteer as cast members, crew and producers to create unique productions of noteworthy plays\.

[This year’s production](https://ubcmedplay.wordpress.com/2022-2/) will be a movie rendition of Without Rule of Law, by Canadian playwright Michaela Jeffery\.

“Audiences can expect a fun coming\-of\-age movie about teenage girls who take their futures into their own hands,” said Shadan Ashrafi, a fourth\-year medical student at UBC and one of the producers for MedPlay\.

All proceeds from this year’s play will be donated to Justice for Girls, a Canadian non\-profit organization that provides support and resources to young women in poverty in an effort to end violence\.
