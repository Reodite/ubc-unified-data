Your performance might be impacted by health/illness, personal situations, learning styles and learning disorders\. Please [contact Student Affairs](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/contact-student-affairs/) to discuss\.

Log in to [Elentra](https://ubc.elentra.cloud/community/studentaffairs:career_planning) for more resources\.

### Academic Advising

Student Affairs offers referrals to academic advising\. Students can also request academic advising support\.

Each site has a separate mechanism for referring students for tutoring, please contact your Student Affairs Assistant Dean\.

Student Academic Development Subcommittees tailor academic plans depending on situations and particular difficulties\.

### Resources

For more academic support resources, join a study group and test\-taking in the UGME, log in to [Elentra](https://entrada.med.ubc.ca/community/studentaffairs:academic_support)\.
