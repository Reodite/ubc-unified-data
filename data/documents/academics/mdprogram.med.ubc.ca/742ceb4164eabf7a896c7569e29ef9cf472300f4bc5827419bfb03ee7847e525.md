## [UBC Aboriginal MD program meets goal five years ahead of schedule](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/2015/05/15/ubc-aboriginal-md-program-meets-goal-five-years-ahead-of-schedule/)

May 15, 2015

In 2002, UBC’s Aboriginal MD Admissions program set an unofficial goal of graduating 50 more Aboriginal MDs by 2020\. Now, the ambitious goal has been reached five years early\.
