Have a passion for medicine and research? The MD/PhD Program is a 7\-year program offered jointly by the Faculty of Medicine and Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies and is designed for students who want to pursue a career as clinician\-scientists\. This program provides highly\-qualified students with the opportunity to combine their medical school experience with intensive scientific training\.
