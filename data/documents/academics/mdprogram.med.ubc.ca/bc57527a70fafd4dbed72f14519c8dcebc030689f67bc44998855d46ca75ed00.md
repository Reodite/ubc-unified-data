Student Affairs is part of the UBC Faculty of Medicine, Undergraduate Medical Education Program\.

**Student Affairs Mission and Vision**

**Vision**: To provide nonjudgmental, holistic support to enable the well\-being, safety, and success of all our medical students, so they are able to sustainably care for our diverse communities and work to transform health for everyone\.

**Mission**: Student Affairs functions independently from the curricular and assessment teams of the MD Undergraduate Program to support students in the areas of well\-being, career planning, financial aid and debt management, academic advising and in initiatives that enhance and embrace student diversity\.

Please contact Student Affairs for more information:

### General Contact

[md\.studentaffairs@ubc\.ca](mailto:md.studentaffairs@ubc.ca)

**Administrative Hours:**  
Monday to Friday, 8:30 a\.m\. – 4:30 p\.m\.

**Site Contacts:** To book an appointment with Student Affairs during office hours, contact your site coordinator \(see below\)\.

For more **urgent concerns**, email your Site Assistant Dean directly and they will do their best to get back to you within 2 business days\.  Please note Student Affairs Assistant Deans are not an emergency service\.
