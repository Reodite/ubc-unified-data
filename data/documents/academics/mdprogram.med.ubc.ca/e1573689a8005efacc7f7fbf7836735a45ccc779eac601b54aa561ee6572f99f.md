## [Building empathy through theatre: annual production enables medical students to walk in other shoes](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/2016/01/21/building-empathy-through-theatre-annual-production-enables-medical-students-to-walk-in-other-shoes/)

January 21, 2016

For over a decade, UBC students in both the Faculty of Medicine and Dentistry have been participating in MedPlay\. The annual production is a tradition amongst the students, who enjoy the creativity and time away from their studies\.
