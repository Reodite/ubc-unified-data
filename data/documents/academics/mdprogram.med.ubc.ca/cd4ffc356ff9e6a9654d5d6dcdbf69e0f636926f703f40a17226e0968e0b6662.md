## [Student research: skin cancer risk reduction in organ transplant recipients](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/2015/06/04/student-research-skin-cancer-risk-reduction-in-organ-transplant-recipients/)

June 4, 2015

Skin cancers comprise nearly 40% of malignancies in organ transplant recipients\. A UBC student research project looks at ways to reduce skin cancer risk through preventive education of patients and healthcare professionals\.
