#### Ellie Parton

Vancouver Fraser Medical Program student, Ellie Parton, acknowledges she wasn’t the greatest student growing up\. Upon becoming a young mother at the age of 16, Ellie wanted to become a role model to her daughter\. Ellie kept raising the academic bar higher and higher for herself, including being accepted into the UBC FOM MD Undergraduate Program\. Ellie’s journey to becoming a resident has made her not only a role model for her daughter, but also a role model to future Indigenous medical students throughout British Columbia\.

#### Adrienne Stedford

Northern Medical Program student, Adrienne Stedford, shares her unconventional path to being accepted into the UBC Faculty of Medicine MD Undergraduate Program\. Starting off as a dump truck driver and ending up a medical student, Adrienne’s story proves life is about the journey not the destination\.
