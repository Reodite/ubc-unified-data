UBC medical students have a dedicated Health &amp; Safety Team that develops and implements safety programs, investigates and follows up on incidents and accidents, and is a resource for any health and safety concerns, including mistreatment help\.

### Report an accident/incident

Any event involving injury to a person or damage to property, or with the potential to do so, must be reported to UBC within 24 hours\.

[UBC Centralized Accident/Incident Reporting System](https://www.cairs.ubc.ca/)
