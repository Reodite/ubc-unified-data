Sarah DeLeeuw is an Assistant professor at UBC’s Northern Medical Program and the Michael Smith scholar in creative arts and health\.  She was recently interviewed on CBC’s [Daybreak North](http://www.cbc.ca/nxnw/podcast/2013/03/16/podcast-saturday-march-16/), where she discussed how the creative arts can aid medical practitioners\.
