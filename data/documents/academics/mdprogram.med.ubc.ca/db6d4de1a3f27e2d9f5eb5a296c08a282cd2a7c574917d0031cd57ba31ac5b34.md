The Innovation Cafe, part of the 2014 MDUP Annual Retreat, will will be a moderated conversation including some of he key players who represent the diverse experiences and perspectives of distribution\.

**Panel A**

“From idea to reality: transforming medical education in British Columbia”

Panelists:  Dr\. John Cairns \(Dean, FOM, 1996\-2003\), Dr\. Joanna Bates \(Senior Associate Dean, MDUP 2000\-04\),  Chuck Jago \(UNBC President, 1998\-06, NHA Board\)

**Panel B**

“10 years of distributed medical education: impacts and outcomes”

Panelists:  Jody Anderson \(2010 IMP grad, Family MD/Palliative, Victoria\), Chuck Jago \(UNBC President 1998\-­06, NHA Board\) Sandra Jarvis\-­‐Selinger \(Director of Curriculum\)  
Chris Lovato \(Director, ESU\)

### Meet the panelists:

#### [Jody Anderson photo](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/Jody-Anderson-photo-e1401820901967-250x300.png)

#### Jody Anderson, MD CCFP, MBA

Dr\. Jody Anderson graduated from the UBC Island Medical Program in 2010, completed her family medicine residency at Queen’s University in Kingston, Ontario in 2012 and a year of added competency in palliative care at UBC in Victoria in 2013\. A bit unconventional as a medical student, she embarked on medicine as a second career after obtaining an MBA and working for 6 years in marketing and corporate social responsibility\. She is currently living in Victoria BC, working in palliative care and family medicine and was recently elected to the board of the Victoria Division of Family Practice\. When she’s not working, she can be found cooking, dancing, reading or playing with her husband &amp; crazy dog\.

#### **[https://med\-fom\-ugrad\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2014/06/Joanna\-Bates\-e1401821685729\.jpg](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/Joanna-Bates-e1401821685729.jpg)[Joanna Bates](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/Joanna-Bates-e1401821685729.jpg)Joanna Bates, MDCM, CCFP, FCFP**

Dr\. Bates is an academic family physician\. While in community\-based practice, she became a Certificant of the College of Family Physicians of Canada \(CCFP\) \(1983\) and a Fellow \(FCFP\) \(1994\)\. In 1997, she was first appointed to the Faculty of Medicine, was promoted to Associate Professor in 2002, and to Professor in 2008\. She has contributed to postgraduate education in Family Medicine both as postgraduate program director at UBC, and nationally through the CFPC\. Prior to becoming the founding Director of the UBC Centre for Health Education Scholarship \(CHES\), Dr\. Bates was Senior Associate Dean, Education and prior to that, Senior Associate Dean, UME, and Associate Dean Admissions\. During her tenure in these roles, Dr\. Bates led the doubling of the UBC Undergraduate program, and its distribution to two new campuses\. She has received many awards, including an honorary doctorate from L’Universite de Sherbrooke\.

####  **[https://med\-fom\-ugrad\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2014/06/John\-Cairns\-e1401822581476\.jpg](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/John-Cairns-e1401822581476.jpg)[John\-Cairns](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/John-Cairns-e1401822581476.jpg)**John Cairns, MD, FRCPC, FRCP \(Lond\), FCAHS, FACC

Dr\. John Cairns graduated from UBC Medical School in 1968, did his internship at McGill Medical School, and joined the medical school at McMaster in 1975, where he served for 21 years, earning the position of Chair of Medicine\. His early research in aspirin and acute coronary syndromes set the stage for one of the most important medical discoveries of this century\. He was named Dean of Medicine at UBC in 1996\-2003 where he worked closely with UBC and the provincial government to build the rationale and plans for expansion of the Medical School, now underway in partnership with the Universities of Victoria and Northern BC\.

#### [https://med\-fom\-ugrad\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2014/06/CharlesJago\_CHAIR\-e1401822948773\.jpg](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/CharlesJago_CHAIR-e1401822948773.jpg)[CharlesJago\_CHAIR](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/CharlesJago_CHAIR-e1401822948773.jpg)Charles J\. Jago C\.M\., O\.BC

Dr\. Jago completed his doctoral studies at Cambridge University and has held academic appointments in three Canadian universities over forty years\. As a university administrator, he was Principal of Huron University College at Western University from 1987\-95 and President of the University of Northern BC from 1995\-2006 and 2008\-2009\.

Currently Dr\. Jago is Chair of the Boards of the Northern Health Authority, and the Sinclar Group – Forest Products Ltd\. He also serves on the Boards of Canfor Pulp Products Inc\., Initiatives Prince George and the Canada West Foundation\.

Dr\. Jago received both the Queen’s Silver and Diamond Jubilee Medals and was appointed a Member of the Order of Canada in 2006 in recognition of his distinguished academic career and of his role in the creation of the Northern Medical Program in British Columbia\. In 2013 he was named to the Order of British Columbia\.

#### [https://med\-fom\-ugrad\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2014/06/SJS\-2014a\-e1401823060664\.jpg](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/SJS-2014a-e1401823060664.jpg)[SJS \(2014\)a](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/SJS-2014a-e1401823060664.jpg)Sandra Jarvis\-Selinger, PhD

Dr\. Jarvis\-Selinger is the Assistant Dean for Faculty Development, Director of Curriculum – MD Undergraduate Program, and an Associate Professor in the Department of Surgery\. She received a Michael Smith Foundation Career Investigator Award, the Department of Surgery’s Hjalmar Johnson Outstanding New Investigator Award and the Faculty of Medicine’s Distinguished Achievement Award – Excellence in Education\. She is also the educational faculty member for the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons’ course for Orthopaedic Educators\. She is a PhD\-trained developmental psychologist in the area of Human Learning, Development and Instruction\.

Her research focuses on educational innovation including: 1\) developing teaching excellence, 2\) curriculum design, development and evaluation, and 3\) using technology to support education\. Her work addresses positive curricular change, effective lifelong learning and professional identity formation by supporting critical educational transitions includingsecondary to post\-secondary education \(e\.g\., eMentoring\); throughout post\-secondary education \(e\.g\. Surgical Residents\-as\-Teachers\); and during professionals’ careers \(e\.g\. Orthopaedic Educators\)\.

#### [https://med\-fom\-ugrad\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2014/06/lovato\-e1401911824648\.jpg](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/lovato-e1401911824648.jpg)[lovato](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2014/06/lovato-e1401911824648.jpg)Chris Y\. Lovato, PhD

Dr\. Lovato is a Professor in the School of Population and Public Health, and Founding Director, Evaluation Studies Unit, Faculty of Medicine, UBC\. Her primary areas of expertise are in health promotion/population health and program evaluation\. Her research focuses on evaluating the impact of health programs and policies\. She is currently conducting research related to evaluating the impact of medical school initiatives implemented in response to nationally\-identified health resource shortages\. She teaches program planning and evaluation at UBC’s School of Population and Public Health\.  Chris has extensive experience in program evaluation and has provided consultation to numerous government and non\-governmental agencies evaluating health\-related programs\.
