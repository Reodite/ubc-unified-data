As a UBC medical undergraduate student you may have questions regarding financial assistance and how to financially prepare for your four years in the program\. Some of the areas Student Affairs advises on include: government student loans, scholarships, bursaries, tuition fees, credit lines, budgeting, and loan repayment\. Student Affairs is here to support to you with any and all of these matters\.

Current, accepted, or interviewed students are encouraged to [direct questions](mailto:fao.studentaffairs@ubc.ca) to or [make an advising appointment](https://calendly.com/fao-studentaffairs/30min) with the Student Financial Assistance Officer\. MDUP students also have access to UBC central [Enrolment Services Advisors \(ESAs\)](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/ "https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/enrolment-services-advisors/") for assistance navigating financial support options\.
