Student Affairs is part of the UBC Faculty of Medicine, Undergraduate Medical Education Program\. We are a confidential resource for current MD students seeking support, guidance, and advice on a range of issues, including: health and well\-being, financial planning, career planning, safety, academic support, mentorship, and any other topics students might wish to discuss\. See the left sidebar for further information on our offerings and services\.

Please contact Student Affairs for more information:

### General Contact

If you are unsure who to contact, please reach out to: [md\.studentaffairs@ubc\.ca](mailto:md.studentaffairs@ubc.ca)

### Emergencies

If this is an emergency, **call 911**\.

### Urgent matters after hours

Student Affairs Assistant Deans at all sites are readily accessible by email but are not an emergency service\. For more **urgent concerns**, email your Site Assistant Dean directly and they will do their best to get back to you within 24 to 48hrs\.

To book an appointment with Student Affairs during office hours, contact your site coordinator:
