Below is an overview of key dates and deadlines you need to keep in mind when applying for the MD Undergraduate Program\.

### Note

We encourage you to carefully review the timeline that applies to you as our deadlines will not change, and we may rescind offers if we do not receive your required documents by the dates outlined in the timeline\.

Please review both our [Admission Requirements](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/) and the [Applicant Guide](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/when-you-apply/applicant-guide/) for more detailed information on requirements at each of these stages\. Our [Admissions team](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/faq-contact/contact-resources/) is here for you if you have any questions or concerns about the admissions requirements\.

### 2025/2026 Cycle \(entering in August 2026, MED 2030\)

### View dates for 2025/2026 Cycle

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Late June/Early July, 2025** | Online application \(OAS\) opens\. A notice will be posted on the Online Application System and our blog page when the application opens\. |
| **September 13, 2025** | Last accepted MCAT test date\. **All scores must be released every year in time for the MCAT score deadline of October 15, 2025, by 11:59 p\.m\. Pacific Time\.** |
| **September 15, 2025 \- 12:00 p\.m\. \(noon\) Pacific Time** | Deadline for submission of applications including online application, all applications fees, all North American transcripts, IB/AP or another acceptable English exam score \(if applicable\), proof of BC Residency \(if required\), and disability accommodation forms to the Centre for Accessibility\. MCAT scores, international transcripts and WES/ICES evaluations are not required until October 15\. |
| **October 15, 2025 \- 11:59 p\.m\. Pacific Time** | Deadline for receipt of MCAT scores via the MCAT Score Reporting System\. **Valid test dates April 17, 2015** ****\- September 13, 2025, provided scores can be received by the MD Admissions Office on or before October 15, 2025 \- 11:59 p\.m\. Pacific Time\.****Deadline for receipt of all non\-North American transcripts and all required WES/ICES evaluations\. |
| **September \- Early December, 2025** | [Evaluation](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/evaluation-criteria/) of complete submitted applications\. |
| **Mid\-December, 2025** | Interview invitations will be sent to selected applicants via their OAS account\. All supplemental application requirements, forms, and instructions will be made available via the OAS account\. |
| **Mid\-December, 2025** | **Deadline to book an interview\.** |
| **January 31 \- February 1, 2026** | Multiple Mini\-Interview weekend \(Virtual MMI\)\. |
| **February 8, 2026** | Northern and Rural Virtual Panel Interviews \(Virtual\)\. |
| **February 2026** | **Supplemental Application deadline** Reference letters, site preferences, and graduate students must confirm that they will complete their graduate program by July 31, 2026\. |
| **April 30, 2026** | All applicants must complete a minimum of 90 credits and all English prerequisite courses\. |
| **May 2026** | **Applicants are notified of admissions decisions via their OAS account\.** |

| **July 31, 2026** | [Graduate students](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/) must provide proof of completion of their program\. |
