When thinking about medical school, prospective applicants should consider the expenses that will be incurred:

Educational Expenses: these are for the most part, set costs associated with one’s education\. They will include more than just tuition, books, and supplies, but also student fees, health &amp; dental fees, accident/disability insurance fees, and the annual CPR course\. Please see the chart below for estimated amounts of educational expenses for each medical year\.

First and second year expenses are very similar to undergraduate degree costs because students mainly attend classes at The Life Sciences Centre at UBC and then at The Diamond Health Care Centre\.

In third and fourth year there will be unanticipated costs &amp; expenses that are involved with rotations, clerkships, and CaRMS interviews\. Please contact the [Student Financial Assistance Officer](mailto:fao.studentaffairs@ubc.ca) for these details\.

*Please note that while we endeavour to keep the below fees as accurate as possible, there may be times when a fee has changed without notice\. Please notify the [Student Financial Assistance Officer](mailto:fao.studentaffairs@ubc.ca) if this is the case and we will update this page accordingly\.*

3.  Student fees are quoted for students remaining at the VFMP site for the 4 years of their education but can be considered an estimate of fees for those students in the IMP, NMP, or SMP\. Students in the IMP, NMP, and SMP programs will be charged the respective UVic, UNBC, and UBC\-O student fees and will either be exempt from or compensated for the UBC student fees that they will not benefit from\. IMP and NMP students will have affiliated student status at UVic, UNBC, and UBC\-O respectively, and will have access to student representation and services \(i\.e\. UVic/UNBC/UBC\-O student society, health services, athletics\) at their respective sites\. **All student fees will be collected by UBC\.**
4.  Although enrollment in the AMS extended health and dental plan \(or equivalent\) is not required past 2nd year it is strongly recommended that this coverage is continued\. Consider speaking with you Student Financial Assistance Officer for more details\.
5.  Travel costs can be substantially different depending on where electives and CaRMS interviews take place\.

**Living Expenses**

Are based on the StudentAid BC Moderate Standard of Living Allowances \(MSOL\); these may or may not reflect one’s actual level of spending; these numbers are based on living costs across the province, not just Vancouver\. Please see the chart below for BC living costs\.

There are numerous living expenses that a student can have depending on their situation\. Student Affairs encourages students to develop a budget to prepare for the 4 years of medical school\. Budgeting can be a complicated and cumbersome task\. Student Affairs provides advice and information on budgeting strategies; please contact the [Student Financial Assistance Officer](mailto:fao.studentaffairs@ubc.ca) to discuss these topics\.
