## General

### Can I meet with an advisor?

We do not provide any one\-on\-one advising sessions\. This includes scheduled advising appointments as well as informal drop\-in advising\. However, we do offer small\-group [information sessions](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/information-sessions/ "Information Sessions")\. They follow a question\-and\-answer format and are free on a first come, first served basis\.

If you have an admissions\-related question that cannot be answered by reviewing our website, we recommend [contacting the Admissions office by email](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/faq-contact/contact-resources/)\. If you are a current applicant, please email the office through the online application system\.

### When and where are your information sessions? Do I have to RSVP?

Please see the [Information Sessions](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/information-sessions/ "Information Sessions") page\.

### Are my marks strong enough to enter medical school?

Please view our [Statistics](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admissions-statistics/) to compare your academic standing with the averages of successful applicants and also review information on [Minimum Academic Standing](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/faq-contact/frequently-asked-questions/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/ "Admission Requirements")\.

### Does UBC have a pre\-med program?

UBC does not have a pre\-med program\. Please see our [Admissions Requirements](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/ "Admission Requirements") for information on all prerequisites necessary for application\.

### When are the statistics posted?

Interim statistics for the current application cycle are posted in mid\- to late fall\. Final statistics are posted in early fall\.

**For more information on this topic, please see the [Selection](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/after-you-apply/selection/ "Selection") and [Post\-Acceptance Conditions](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/after-you-apply/post-acceptance-conditions/ "Post-Acceptance Conditions") pages\.**

### How many years is the MD Undergraduate Program?

The MD Undergraduate program is a four\-year program\. This is followed by a residency in a chosen or assigned specialization, which may take between two and seven years to complete\.

### How much does it cost to attend the MD Undergraduate Program?

[More information about tuition, fees, and other expenses](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/financial-support/cost-of-an-md-student/)\.

### How many available seats are there in total?

In 2026/2027, 328 seats will be available – 208 seats for the Vancouver Fraser Medical Program \(UBC\-Vancouver\), which has three learning communities: Fraser Learning Community \(64 seats\), North Learning Community \(64 seats\), and South Learning Community \(80 seats\); 40 seats for the Island Medical Program \(University of Victoria\); 40 seats for the Northern Medical Program \(University of Northern British Columbia\); and 40 seats for the Southern Medical Program \(UBC\-Okanagan\)\.

### What are the Island, Northern and Southern Medical Programs?

In partnership with UBC\-Vancouver, the University of Victoria, University of Northern BC and UBC\-Okanagan are a part of a provincial program to increase the number of physicians in underserved areas of the province\. Please see the websites of the University of Victoria’s [Island Medical Program](http://imp.uvic.ca/ "Island Medical Program"), University of Northern BC’s [Northern Medical Program](http://www.unbc.ca/northern-medical-program "Northern Medical Program"), and UBC\-Okanagan’s [Southern Medical Program](http://smp.med.ubc.ca/welcome.html "Southern Medical Program") for more information on those sites\.

### How do I apply to the Northern Medical Program, the Island Medical Program, or the Southern Medical Program?

All sites are part of the UBC MD Undergraduate Program and there is only one application for the program\. During the interview stage, applicants invited to interview have the opportunity to learn about each site and are asked to indicate their preferred site\(s\)\.

### What are the Vancouver Fraser Medical Program \(VFMP\) learning communities?

Starting in 2025, the VFMP will adopt a learning communities model — an innovative approach designed to foster connection and continuity throughout the medical education journey\. The VFMP will have three learning communities: Fraser Learning Community \(64 students\), North Learning Community \(64 students\), and South Learning Community \(80 students\)\.

### How many seats are available for out\-of\-province applicants?

Up to 10% of seats \(33 total in 2026/2027\) are reserved for out\-of\-province applicants\.

### I am an international medical school student\. How do I apply for visiting clerkship opportunities?

Please see the [Visiting Student Elective Program](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/visiting-student-elective-program/?login) page for more information\.

For more information on this topic, please see the following pages: [Eligibility](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/eligibility/ "Who Should Apply"), [Graduate Students](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/), [Selection](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/after-you-apply/selection/ "Selection"), and [Post\-Acceptance Conditions](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/after-you-apply/post-acceptance-conditions/ "Post-Acceptance Conditions")\.

### Do you accept international applicants?

No\. Only permanent residents of Canada are eligible to apply, either as Canadian citizens or permanent residents\.

We will accept applications from persons with refugee status in Canada\. Admission will be contingent on attaining permanent residency in Canada and providing proof of this status to the MD Admissions Office two weeks prior to the start of classes\. A one year deferral can be requested if a refugee status applicant is unable to provide proof of permanent residency within the above stated time frame\. All deferrals are made at the discretion of the MD Admissions Office\.

On interview day, all applicants are required to submit official proof \(original government\-issued documentation\) of one of the following: citizenship, permanent residency, or refugee status\.

### Do you accept transfers from other medical schools?

*We are **not** currently accepting transfers into the UBC MD Undergraduate Program\.*

### Can I apply if I have attended a different medical school, or if I will be starting medical school at a different institution after I submit my application?

You can apply as long as you were/are enrolled in a foreign medical school and you did not withdraw from the other medical school due to academic reasons\. When you apply, please write a short paragraph explaining the details of your attendance in the appropriate box on the application\. Note that if accepted to our program, you will start in Year 1 with the rest of the entering class\. If you are currently attending or withdrew from a Canadian medical school, please contact the MD Admissions office before applying, as applications from students in these circumstances are not usually considered\.

### Is there preference given to Honours, Graduate, or Science students?

There is no preference given to applicants in Honours, Graduate, or Science programs\. Keep in mind graduate students must agree to [these terms](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/ "Graduate Students") at the time of application\.

### Are there any special requirements for applicants who are current graduate \(Master's or PhD\) students?

Yes\. Please see [Graduate Students](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/ "Graduate Students") eligibility information\.

### How do I apply as an Indigenous applicant?

Indigenous applicants are encouraged to review information on the [Indigenous Admissions Pathway](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/eligibility/indigenous-pathway/) and/or to contact the Indigenous Student Initiatives Manager for information about the Indigenous admissions process and specific requirements\. Please contact [indigenous\.md@ubc\.ca](mailto:indigenous.md@ubc.ca)\.

### Do I have to disclose if I have a disability?

No, you do not have to disclose this information to the Admissions Office\.

### Why do you ask if an applicant wants to be identified as having a disability?

You do not need to disclose, but we do want to be able to make any necessary accommodations during the application cycle\. The [Centre for Accessibility](https://students.ubc.ca/about-student-services/centre-for-accessibility) coordinates accommodations for disabilities, and can be reached at 604\-822\-5844\. For more information on requesting accommodations, please see the [Applicants with Disabilities](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/) section\.

If you have further questions about disclosing your disability you may also contact the Associate Dean, Equity\.

### I am not sure if something that happened in my past may affect my chances of obtaining an educational license and being accepted into the program\. What should I do?

If you have any questions that might affect your ability to study or practice medicine in BC, you should contact the College of Physicians &amp; Surgeons of BC \(see Educational Licensure under [Post\-Acceptance Conditions](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/after-you-apply/post-acceptance-conditions/ "Post-Acceptance Conditions")\)\. Applicants should do this before undertaking the lengthy and possibly expensive application and evaluation process, especially if travel is required to attend an interview\.

### Are there any age restrictions to apply?

There are no age limitations to apply or enter the program\.

### My university has a different credit system than UBC\. How do I know if I have 90 credits with grades?

Generally, half\-year courses are worth 3 credits, and full\-year courses are worth 6 credits\. "Credits with grades" refers to course credits that are assigned a percentage and/or letter grade on your transcript \(not courses that are graded as pass/fail, credit received, satisfactory, completed, etc\.\)\.

### Do pass/fail \(or CR/NCR\) courses count toward the minimum 90 credits required to enter the MD program? Do they count toward the OGPA or AGPA?

Pass/fail, CR/NCR and other courses without letter or percentage grades do not count toward the OGPA, AGPA, or the 90\-credit minimum requirement\.

### Will the credits I received for my AP or IB courses count towards the 90\-credit minimum requirement?

No, credits given for AP or IB courses do not count toward the 90\-credit minimum requirement\.

### By when must I complete 90 credits?

Our [Application Timeline](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/when-you-apply/application-timelines/ "Application Timelines") lists all important dates in the application cycle\. As noted, your 90 credits with grades must be completed by the April 30 deadline in the year of entry\.

### Do I need a degree to enter the program?

You do not need a degree to apply or enter the program\. However, we require that 90 university credits with grades be completed by April 30 of the year of entry into medical school\. For most people, this means you can apply in your third year of university as long as you will have completed at least 90 credits with grades by the end of the spring semester\.

### What do you consider a full course load?

We consider a full course load to be approximately 10 courses/30 credits taken over the fall and spring semesters\.

### Do I have to carry a full course load? What if I haven't taken a full course load each year?

A full course load is not necessary for application to the program, and we recognize that there are many reasons why  applicants may have taken less than a full course load\. However, applicants must display an ability to handle the demands of medical school and may be required to explain the reasons behind a part\-time course load\.

### Do CEGEP courses count as credit?

Graded pre\-university CEGEP courses are considered post\-secondary courses and count towards the 90\-credit minimum requirement\.

### Are diploma courses counted toward the 90 credit requirement and included in GPA calculations?

Generally, diploma courses do not count toward the 90 credit requirement and grades from diploma courses are not included in GPA calculations\. This includes diplomas in most trades and clinical health services\. However, there are some exceptions to this rule, so if you would like confirmation on whether your particular diploma courses will be accepted, please email the Admissions Office at [admissions\.md@ubc\.ca](mailto:admissions.md@ubc.ca)\.

### How do you treat grades from diploma programs when the diploma courses are granted block transfer credit as part of a Bachelor's degree? For example, diploma courses that are transferred to a Bachelor of Health Sciences degree from TRU\-OL\.

The diploma courses do not count toward the 90\-credit minimum requirement and are not included in GPA calculations\.

### Can my nursing degree or diploma be applied to the 90 required credits?

Applicants with a BSc in Nursing \(conferred or in progress\) will have their graded courses applied to their GPA and 90\-credit minimum requirement\. Nursing diplomas are generally not considered\.

### Are credits from colleges such as institutes of technology or other programs such as Chiropractic, Naturopathic, etc\. considered as part of the academic evaluation?

Credits from non\-university\-transferable courses cannot be counted towards the 90 minimum credits or included in the academic evaluation\. If you would like confirmation on your specific program, please contact the Admissions Office at [admissions\.md@ubc\.ca](mailto:admissions.md@ubc.ca)\.

For more information on this topic, please see the [Admission Requirements](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/ "Admission Requirements") page\.

### What are your prerequisite courses?

2026/2027: Either 6 credits of English, or 3 credits of English plus 3 credits of Indigenous Studies are required\.  
2027/2028: Either 6 credits of English, or 3 credits of English plus 3 credits of Indigenous Studies are required\.  
2028/2029: 3 credits of English and 3 credits of Indigenous Studies are required\.

Courses in general biology, general chemistry, organic chemistry, and biochemistry are strongly recommended but not required\.

Please see the Prerequisites section of the [Admission Requirements](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/) page for more information about prerequisites\.

### When do my prerequisite courses need to be completed?

2026/2027: By April 30, 2027  
2027/2028: By April 30, 2027 \(before applying\)  
2028/2029: By April 30, 2028 \(before applying\)

With the exception of the 2026/2027 application cycle, prerequisite courses must be completed by April 30 of the year of application\.

### Can you confirm which English courses at my university are accepted toward the English prerequisite requirement?

Please see the [English Courses Chart](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/English-Courses-Chart-2026-2027.pdf) for a list of some acceptable English courses\. More information about our criteria for acceptable English prerequisites can be found under the Prerequisites section of the [Admission Requirements](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/) page\. If you have a question about a course not listed on the chart, please email us at [admissions\.md@ubc\.ca](mailto:admissions.md@ubc.ca)\.

### Can International Baccalaureate \(IB\), Advanced Placement \(AP\) or other exam results satisfy the English prerequisite requirement?

Please see the AP, IB and Other Exam Results section of the [Admission Requirements](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/ "Admission Requirements") page\. English proficiency exams such as TOEFL or IELTS are not accepted\.

### Can I use my CEGEP courses to fill the English requirement?

Yes, four "603" CEGEP courses can be used to fill the English requirement\.

### Can you confirm which Indigenous Studies courses at my university are accepted toward the Indigenous Studies requirement?

Please see the [Indigenous Studies Courses Chart](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/Indigenous-Studies-Courses-Chart-2026-2027.pdf) for a list of some acceptable Indigenous Studies courses\. More information about our criteria for acceptable Indigenous Studies courses can be found under the Prerequisites section of the [Admission Requirements](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/) page\. If you have a question about a course not listed on the chart, please email us at [admissions\.md@ubc\.ca](mailto:admissions.md@ubc.ca)\.

### Can I take my prerequisite courses under the Credit/D/Fail option rather than for a letter/percentage grade? How will that be looked at?

English and Indigenous Studies prerequisite courses must be taken for a grade\. Courses taken as Credit/D/Fail, CR/NCR, or other pass/fail options will not satisfy the prerequisite requirement\.

Applicants considering taking the strongly recommended and recommended courses as Credit/D/Fail should be mindful that applicants are required to demonstrate their academic ability to determine their suitability for the rigorous MD curriculum\. Taking strongly recommended and recommended courses under the Credit/D/Fail option may not be a helpful indicator in the selection process, and could potentially jeopardize your application to the program\.

### My English and/or Indigenous Studies courses were taken several years ago\. Will you still accept them?

Yes, we will\. Courses do not have an 'expiration date\.'

### What is the difference between the English prerequisite requirement and the UBC English language competency requirement? Do I have to meet both?

English prerequisite requirement:

-   Specific to the MD program
-   Can be met only through English coursework or by achieving high enough scores on AP, IB, or other similar exams taken in high school

UBC's English language competency requirement:

-   General requirement for learning at UBC
-   Can be met in a [number of ways](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/UBC-English-Language-Competency-Requirement-MD-Admissions.pdf)\. Please note that English proficiency exams such as IELTS or TOEFL are not accepted toward the MD Admissions English prerequisite requirement, but they can be used to satisfy UBC's English language competency requirement\.

Students must meet both the English prerequisite requirement and the UBC English language competency requirement\.

For more information on this topic, please see the [Admission Requirements](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/ "Admission Requirements") page and the [Evaluation Criteria](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/evaluation-criteria/ "Evaluation Criteria") page\.

### What are your MCAT requirements?

Please see the [Admission Requirements](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/admission-requirements/ "Admission Requirements") page\.

### How do I send my MCAT scores to UBC?

It is the applicant’s responsibility to ensure that all MCAT results are sent to UBC and received by the Admissions Office\. MCAT scores must be released every application cycle after the application opens\.

UBC is not a part of the American Medical College Application Service \(AMCAS\)\. You must therefore specify that you want your test results sent to UBC\. This can only be done through the MCAT Score Reporting System, and only after your results have been released to you\. We encourage applicants to check the “Scores Sent to Institutions” screen on their AAMC accounts to confirm that results have been successfully released to UBC\.

### I have written more than one MCAT\. Which score is used?

You must release all results\. We will evaluate the exam with the highest score, as long as that exam 1\) meets our date requirements and 2\) has a total score of at least 496\.

### How is the MCAT used?

As long as minimum score and date requirements are met, the MCAT is not a factor in determining who is invited to interview\. MCAT scores are evaluated post\-interview, but further information on how they are evaluated is confidential\.

### Will students need to take additional classes to prepare for the MCAT?

Information provided by MCAT shows that the concepts tested in the four sections: \(Biological and Biochemical Foundations of Living Systems; Chemical and Physical Foundations of Biological Systems; Psychological, Social, and Biological Foundations of Behaviour; Critical Analysis and Reasoning\) are taught in the courses most applicants take at post\-secondary institutions\. However, examinees prepare in different ways\. Please refer to the AAMC website for information on the exam and suggestions on how to prepare\. The Admissions Office does not endorse and cannot provide advice on any third\-party MCAT study guides or prep courses\.

For more information on this topic, please see the [Evaluation Criteria](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/evaluation-criteria/ "Evaluation Criteria") page\.

### How do you calculate my grade point average?

The overall grade point average \(OGPA\) calculation includes all university credits with grades and all university\-transferable credits with grades completed at an accredited post\-secondary institution\. For applicants who meet the criteria, an adjusted grade point average \(AGPA\) is also calculated, in which up to 30 credits from the academic year with the lowest academic average are removed from the OGPA calculation\. Fails, duplicates and repeat courses are included in both calculations\. See the [Evaluation Criteria](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/evaluation-criteria/) page for more information on OGPA and AGPA calculations\.

### How do you calculate averages from non\-UBC transcripts?

When applicants enter their grades and credits \(as used by their institution\) in the online application system, each letter grade will convert to the same percentage grade as per the [appropriate table](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/files/2012/08/ADM_grade_conversion_tables.pdf "Grade Conversion Tables")\. Grades from schools already using percentage grades will not be converted\.

### How are these conversion tables determined?

They are based on research of the schemes used by schools across Canada and the US, as well as comparison with scales used by other institutions\.

### Are AP or IB courses included in the grade average calculations?

No, they are not\.

### Are summer courses counted for credit and calculated into my OGPA?

Summer courses from the year of application are not included in the academic evaluation\. Courses from previous summers are included in the applicable average calculations\.

### Are graduate courses included in the grade average calculations?

Graduate courses are included in the grade average calculations and are treated the same as undergraduate courses\.

### Should I repeat courses as an unclassified student to improve my academic grades?

Generally, we do not recommend repeating courses to improve your overall GPA\. Students who enroll in unclassified, non\-degree programs for the sole purpose of improving their academic qualifications are advised that only a small proportion of such candidates gain admission\.

### What if I have failed a course?

All courses, including failures, are included in the overall GPA calculation\.

### What if I withdrew from a course?

We do note course withdrawals, but in the context of an entire application withdrawal from one course is unlikely to have a significant negative impact\. Applicants who withdrew from multiple courses or who withdrew from their academic program should provide an explanation on the application\.

### Are my grades from the current academic year looked at?

We evaluate grades from courses taken during the current application cycle only for applicants who receive an offer to the program or who are placed on the waitlist\. For applicants who receive an offer, we recalculate the overall GPA or adjusted GPA \(if applicable\) with the new coursework added in\. If the resulting GPA has dropped 5% or more from the overall or adjusted GPA we calculated during our file evaluation in the fall, the Admissions Selection Committee reserves the right to revoke the offer of admission\.

### How important is the non\-academic portion of the application?

UBC’s MD Undergraduate Program prides itself on the diverse student body\. Academic scores are critical, but we believe that a strong non\-academic background is equally important to demonstrate an applicant’s suitability to be a doctor\. Applicants will be assessed based on the non\-academic experiences included in the application\.

### Should I volunteer specifically in the health industry?

There is not a specific volunteer model that enhances an applicant’s profile\. Successful applicants in the past have included not only medical volunteers, but athletes and artists\. Generally, those who demonstrate a commitment to their community and service ethic are valued; all of this must be documented in the non\-academic experiences section for evaluation\.

### Should I shadow a physician or other healthcare professional in order to improve my NAQ score or gain experience in the medical field?

In accordance with [the guidelines](https://www.cpsbc.ca/files/pdf/PSG-Job-Shadowing-Observing.pdf) put forward by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC, the MD Undergraduate Program discourages individuals not enrolled as students of health professions regulated by the *Health Professions Act* or *Emergency Health Services Act* from participating in physician shadowing\. Such activities pose significant concerns to patient privacy and confidentiality, and will not result in an increased NAQ score or improved chances of admission\.

For more information on this topic, please see the [Eligibility](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/eligibility/ "Who Should Apply") page\.

### What opportunities for rural training are there?

Students at every site will undergo some rural practice training\. In addition, the [Northern Medical Program](http://www.unbc.ca/northern-medical-program "Northern Medical Program"), [Southern Medical Program](http://smp.med.ubc.ca/welcome.html), and [Island Medical Program](https://imp.uvic.ca/) provide opportunities to complete undergraduate medical training in regional centres that serve rural, remote, and northern communities\.

### What is the RRSS?

The Rural and Remote Suitability Score \(RRSS\) is calculated to determine the suitability of an applicant for the Northern Medical Program and the rural positions in the Southern Medical Program and Island Medical Program\. An applicant’s experiences in rural/remote/northern/Indigenous settings, along with community ties and activities, factor into this assessment\.

### When do I submit my reference letters?

References are not required until the interview stage, and we do not accept unsolicited reference letters\. All reference providers must complete a specific online form\. More information about references is given to applicants who are invited to interview\.

### What kind of references do you require?

Reference criteria may change from year to year, but recently we have required one academic reference and one service/professional reference\. Applicants invited to interview are given further information about references during the interview stage\. All reference providers are required to submit a specific online form\.
