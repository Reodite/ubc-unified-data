UBC MD/PhD students are required to fulfill the PhD portion of the degree in a department which is affiliated with the Faculty of Medicine and listed in the [Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/faculties/faculty-medicine) at UBC\.  The research supervisor must be a full member of the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral  Studies, and is usually from a Department, Interdisciplinary Program or Institute within the Faculty of Medicine\. The research supervisor must hold a current CIHR research operating grant or from an equivalent peer\-reviewed, federal or provincial granting agency\. Students who have chosen a research supervisor outside the Faculty of Medicine are required to have a co\-supervisor within the Faculty\.

Information on “[tips for finding a research supervisor in the Faculty of Medicine](https://www.med.ubc.ca/current-learners/research/find-a-supervisor/)” is available on the Faculty of Medicine webpage\. Students can also visit the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies webpage for information on “[finding a supervisor](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/application-admission/finding-supervisor)“, “[who can be a supervisor](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/supervision-advising/research-supervisor)” and “[identify research experts](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/researcher-search)“\. Students interested in doing research at the [distributed sites](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/about/distributed-program-sites/) are welcome to visit the site webpages for information \([UBCO](https://gradstudies.ok.ubc.ca/programs-applying/find-a-program/), [UVic](https://imp.med.ubc.ca/?_gl=1%2A1h4b4lt%2A_ga%2AMTkwODQwNTc4MS4xNzUxOTk3MzU1%2A_ga_661615T9R4%2AczE3NTIwMTU1NzQkbzMkZzEkdDE3NTIwMTU3ODMkajYwJGwwJGgw%3Flogin), [UNBC](https://www.unbc.ca/apply/graduate/listing-potential-supervisor)\) \.
