The Faculty of Medicine accepts the following payment methods for application fees:

1.  **Online** by debit card \(preferred\) or credit card \(Visa or Mastercard\) when the application is submitted\.  
    If you pay online, the transaction will show UBC E\-Payment in the description field\. Please call 604\-822\-2008 with any questions or concerns about your transaction record\.
2.  **Mailed** to the Faculty of Medicine, [MD Undergraduate Admissions Office](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/faq-contact/contact-resources/)\.  
    Mailed payment must be a bank draft or money order made payable to “University of British Columbia”, referencing your full name in the memo field\.

The MD Undergraduate Admissions Office will not accept cash or personal cheques\.

## Application Fees 2026/2027

Application fees are **non\-refundable** and **not applicable to tuition**\. Application fees are in Canadian dollars\.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **BC Residents:** | $141\.75 |
| **Out\-of\-Province Applicants:** | $210\.50 |
| **Out\-of\-Province transcripts \(any number\):** | $39\.00 |

### Notes:

In addition, a fee of $50 is charged to start an application\. The $50 is credited to your application fee\.

For an application to be considered complete, the Admissions Office must have received all official transcripts, citizenship and residency documentation, your submitted online application and application fee\.

At UBC, one of our priorities is to ensure medical education is accessible to all individuals and this includes individuals who come from households with limited financial resources\. We offer an application fee waiver program in collaboration with the Association of Faculties of Medicine of Canada \(AFMC\)\. The fee waiver period for the 2026\-2027 application is currently open and will close August 19, 2026\. Please find more information at this website: [Aspirants &amp; Applicants – AFMC](https://www.afmc.ca/strategic-priorities/education/aspirants-applicants/ "https://www.afmc.ca/strategic-priorities/education/aspirants-applicants/")

### Tuition Fees Information

The [Office of Student Affairs](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/ "Student Resources") can provide information about [tuition fees](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/financial-support/cost-of-an-md-student/ "MD Student Costs") and [financial assistance](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/financial-support/ "Financial Support")\.
