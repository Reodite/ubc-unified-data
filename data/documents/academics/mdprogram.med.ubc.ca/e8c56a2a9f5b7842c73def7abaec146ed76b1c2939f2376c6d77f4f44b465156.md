We are excited to announce that the UBC MD Undergraduate Program will be returning to in\-person interviews this year for our multiple\-mini\-interview\. While we have enjoyed the flexibility of virtual interviewing, we find that there really is no replacement for meeting our future students in person\. The interviews will be hosted on the Point Grey Campus of UBC Vancouver\. More details will be available on our website in the coming weeks\.

For those of you applying to the MD program this year, we will be hosting a virtual information session about the interviews this fall\. We will send all applicants with submitted applications more information about this session after the application closes\.  We look forward to connecting with many of you soon\!
