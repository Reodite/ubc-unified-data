## [\#BCMD10: celebrating 10 years of our MD program’s expansion and distribution](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/2014/11/18/bcmd10-celebrating-10-years-of-our-md-programs-expansion-and-distribution/)

November 18, 2014

2014 marks the ten year anniversary of the launch of UBC’s distributed MD Undergraduate Program\. To mark this occasion, we have developed a microsite, which will take people through the history, challenges and successes of the program\.
