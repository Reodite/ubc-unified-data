Whether you are looking for more information on housing options, need support managing the financial and personal demands of academic life or need help launching a new student initiative, Student Affairs can help\.

#### [Contact Student Affairs](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/contact-student-affairs/)

Contact the Student Affairs office or the Student Affairs Coordinator located at your program site\.

#### [Health &amp; Wellbeing](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/health-wellbeing/)

Find resources, support services and information about leaves of absence\.

#### [Finances](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/financial-support/)

Understand program costs, available support services, funding opportunities and housing options\.

#### [Career Planning](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/career-planning/)

Access career resources and book career advising\.

#### [Academic Support](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/academic-support/)

Request academic advising support, access resources and join a study group\.

#### [Mentorship](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/mentorship/)

Join a mentor group for guidance and support throughout the program\.

#### [Black Students](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/black-students/)

Find program and community supports and resources, and contact the Black Student Initiatives Manager\.

#### [Indigenous Students](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/indigenous-students/)

Find career and support resources, and contact the Indigenous Student Initiatives Manager\.

#### [Safety &amp; Emergency Management](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/safety-emergency-management/)

What to do in an emergency and important clinical safety procedures\.

#### [Credentialing/Degree Verification](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-resources/credentialing-degree-verification/)

How to submit a request for MD credentialing\.
