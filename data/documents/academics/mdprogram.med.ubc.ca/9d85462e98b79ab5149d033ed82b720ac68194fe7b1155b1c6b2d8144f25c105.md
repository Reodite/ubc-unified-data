Student Affairs programs support student well\-being, including:

-   Career planning
-   Financial management
-   Health and safety

Well\-being must be a collaboration with students and is a lifelong learning process:

-   To prioritize time and energy between competing professional and personal priorities
-   To address current and future challenges
-   To develop professionally identity and create self\-sufficiency

### Student Wellness and Resilience

Log in to [Elentra](https://ubc.elentra.cloud/community/studentaffairs:well-being/student_initiatives) to access resources, such as Wellness Initiative Network \(WIN\), Mental Illness Network for Destigmatization \(MIND\) and much more\.

### Personal Counselling

UBC MDUP students have access to various providers of personal counselling\. All counselling services are confidential\. Two embedded counsellors are now available \(exclusively\) for MDUP student mental health support\.  Log in [Elentra](https://ubc.elentra.cloud/community/studentaffairs:well-being/personal_counselling) to book an appointment with an MDUP embedded counsellor\.

MDUP students also have access to the general [UBC Student Counselling Services](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services/), IMP students also have access to counselling through [UVic](https://www.uvic.ca/students/health-wellness/counselling/index.php) and NMP through [UNBC](https://www.unbc.ca/counselling)\.

[**Physician Health Program**](https://www.physicianhealth.com/) \(PHP\) provides confidential, discreet help for BC medical students licensed in BC\. PHP offers support, referrals and counselling for a wide range of problems including physician and mental health issues, substance use disorders, career and life transitions, relationship stress, financial issues, professional support and referrals, and concern for colleagues\.

Call the Physician Health Program **24\-hour helpline** [1\-800\-663\-6729](tel:+1-800-663-6729) or send them an email\.

[**UBC Student Counselling Services**](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services) offers variety of mental health resources from self\-directed programs and tools to workshops, group programs and individual therapy\.

[**Here2talk**](https://here2talk.ca/main) offers individual counselling and support as well as a number of other resources\.

### Health Services

[**UBC Student Health Service:**](https://students.ubc.ca/health/student-health-service) Visit a doctor, nurse, or other medical professional on campus\. Offers year\-round health care for registered UBC students\. Services are provided by family doctors, registered nurses, and other health specialists\.

### Information for Family and Friends

Medical education is complex and changes across time\. For family and friends who want to support you, we’ve created the [How Can You Support Your Medical Student?](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2022/08/Friends-and-Family-Brochure-2024.pdf)

### Medical and Personal Leaves of Absence

Please [contact Student Affairs](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/student-affairs/contact-student-affairs/) at your site to confidentially discuss and/or arrange a leave of absence\. A leave of absence can be granted for medical or personal reasons\.

All absences \(planned and unplanned\), follow the [Leave of Absence Policy](https://med-fom-mednet.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/05/002-Leave-of-Absence-and-Return-to-Studies-Following-a-Leave-of-Absence-Policy.pdf)\. For more information, log in to [Elentra](https://ubc.elentra.cloud/community/policiesandforms:attendance_absence_scheduling)\.
