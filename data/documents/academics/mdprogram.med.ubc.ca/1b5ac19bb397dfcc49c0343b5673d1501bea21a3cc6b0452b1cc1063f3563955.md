**Black Student Support**

Our Black Student Initiatives Manager, Student Affairs provides support regarding holistic wellness, mentorship, collaborative advocacy, and community engagement for Black students within the MD Undergraduate Program\. They promote the personal and academic success of our students through these initiatives and by connecting them to a diverse range of university and community resources\.
