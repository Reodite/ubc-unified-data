**MD/PhD Student Research Forum &amp; Open House**

This annual event includes faculty and student presentations by the currently enrolled MD/PhD students\. The event is intended to showcase the research by our students, and to recruit new students and interested faculty into our Program\. It is a very informal arrangement, mainly giving an opportunity for students and prospective applicants to ask questions about the clinician\-scientist career path\.  Prospective applicants are welcome to attend the full event or any session of the event\. Come and join us\. Registration is not required\.
