#### Are you a physician who is looking to volunteer with The UBC Undergraduate Medical Program?

Student Affairs welcomes physicians from different backgrounds and fields of medicine to share their experiences and knowledge with the current Undergraduate Medical Students through various powerful career exploration and mentorship opportunities\. Those connections will foster a supportive environment and help students make better\-informed decisions regarding their career paths\.

Current opportunities:

1.  Shadowing
2.  Career Planning Café
3.  1\-on\-1 Mentorship
4.  Group Mentorship
5.  Other opportunities \(e\.g\. CaRMS Interview Practice\)

Please complete [this form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2sn4Y9TTS5wO1Ce) to tell us a little bit about yourself and select the programs that interest you\. We welcome your questions or suggestions at [careers\.studentaffairs@ubc\.ca](mailto:careers.studentaffairs@ubc.ca)\.
