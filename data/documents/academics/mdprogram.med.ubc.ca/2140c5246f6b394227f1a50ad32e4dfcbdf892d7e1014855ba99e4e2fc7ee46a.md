### Government Student Loans

This section covers eligibility, how to apply, expected amounts, and information about Government Student Loan responsibility\. [Learn more\.\.\.](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/student-resources/financial-support/sources-of-funds/government-student-loans/)

### Bursary/Grant/Scholarship Funding

This section covers important dates to apply to the UBC Bursary Program, affiliation bursaries and scholarships, and types of government grant funding available\. [Learn more\.\.\.](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/student-resources/financial-support/sources-of-funds/awardbursarygrantscholarship-funding/)

### Other Types of Funding
