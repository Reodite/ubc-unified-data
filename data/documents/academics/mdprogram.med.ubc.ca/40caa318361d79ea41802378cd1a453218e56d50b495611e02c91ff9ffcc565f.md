*Please note that the Undergraduate Medical Education office **only verifies UBC MD degree** credentials\.*

*Residency and Fellowship verification requests can be directed to [UBC Faculty of Medicine Postgraduate Studies](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/)\.*

*For elective verification, please contact [ubc\.electives@ubc\.ca](mailto:ubc.electives@ubc.ca)\.*

*To obtain a copy of your Medical Degree or Academic Transcript, you will need to submit a request to [UBC Enrolment Services](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/)\.*

*For Postgraduate CaRMS applications, all UBC MD graduates should use the copy of their MSPR available in their CaRMS account\. The version submitted as part of the CaRMS application process is considered final, and no further revisions or updates will be made to the document\.*

*The Undergraduate Medical Education office and Faculty of Medicine Deans office **do not provide identity certification service**, please visit Notary Public for assistance\.*

### Submit a request

To submit a request for credentialing, please complete the MD Credentialing Request Form:

[MD Credentialing Request Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0x3f5JI63zJYUHX)

A separate form must be submitted for each document requested \(e\.g\., If you need a letter of degree verification and a credentialing form, you will need to submit a separate request for each\)\.

### Payment

The Undergraduate Medical Education office charges a fee of **$25 for credentialing, per document** \(except for current students or those who have graduated within 6 months of the date of request\)\. **MSPR Documents fee is $40**\.

Payment is accepted by Credit Card Only:

-   [Credentialing – $25](https://secure.touchnet.net/C20435_ustores/web/product_detail.jsp?PRODUCTID=1608&amp;SINGLESTORE=true)
-   [MSPR Documents – $40](https://secure.touchnet.net/C20435_ustores/web/product_detail.jsp?PRODUCTID=1610&amp;SINGLESTORE=true)

### Processing times

Please allow a **four\-week processing period** beginning from the date the Undergraduate Medical Education office receives both the payment and the MD Credentialing Request Form, complete with the required documents attached\. Note that our Office is not responsible for any delivery delays or lost mail\.

### Contact

Please contact [ugme\.office@ubc\.ca](mailto:ugme.office@ubc.ca "mailto:ugme.office@ubc.ca") if you have any issues with completing the form\.
