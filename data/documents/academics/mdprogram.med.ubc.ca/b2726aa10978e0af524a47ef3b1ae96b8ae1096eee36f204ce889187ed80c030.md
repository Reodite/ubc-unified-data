## Welcome to the UBC Faculty of Medicine MD Undergraduate Program Admissions

The Doctor of Medicine Undergraduate Program at UBC’s Faculty of Medicine prepares students to meet the health challenges of tomorrow\. Our learners receive the training necessary for a career founded on evidence\-based practice, critical reflection, lifelong learning, and an ability to deliver care in a professional manner\. We believe these skills will enable our graduates to transform the communities they serve and improve the health of the BC population and beyond\.

[Welcome from the Assistant Dean, Admissions](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/welcome/)

[Read our mission statement](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/admissions/before-you-apply/welcome/#mission)

### Contact Us

#### Hours of Operation

Monday to Friday, 8:30am – 4:30pm PDT Closed statutory holidays and weekends\.

#### Phone

Our phone inquiries line is open on Tuesdays from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM PT\. The phone line is closed on statutory holidays\. Our phone number is 604\-822 9911\.

Your phone inquiry is important to us\. Our phone lines are busy which may result in a delay with your call being answered\. Please contact us by email at [admissions\.md@ubc\.ca](mailto:admissions.md@ubc.ca)\. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and look forward to answering your questions in writing\.

#### Current Applicants

Send us a message through the Online Application System \(OAS\)\.

#### Prospective Applicants

Send an email to [admissions\.md@ubc\.ca](mailto:admissions.md@ubc.ca)\. This email address is reserved for prospective applicant inquiries\.

### Admissions Process at a Glance

[1](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/1.png) **Application** deadline September 15, 2026

[2](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/2.png) **Evaluation** File evaluation September to December\. MCAT deadline October 15, 2026

[3](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/3.png) **Interview** Invitations sent out early to mid\-December\. Interviews held early to mid\-February\. Supplemental application forms due mid\-February\.

[4](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/4.png) **Selection** Final selection process mid\-February to early May

[5](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/5.png) **Offer** Offers sent out mid\-May: $1,000 deposit required upon acceptance

[6](https://med-fom-ugrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/6.png) **Admission** Orientation week late August
