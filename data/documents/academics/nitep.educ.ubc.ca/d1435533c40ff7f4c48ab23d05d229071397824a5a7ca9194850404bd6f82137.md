NITEP has revised our program to meet student needs for flexible learning options\. Two courses, **EDUC 140: Introduction to Indigenous Studies** and **EDUC 240: Issues in Indigenous Education**, are now offered in a blended delivery mode\. Much of these courses will be online however, there will be opportunities throughout the year for NITEP students to connect with other students and their instructor either face\-to\-face or through video conferencing\.

Students at all NITEP field centres will now be able to take these courses together, forming a stronger connection and a larger support network for when they arrive on campus to complete their final year\(s\) together\.

Other Indigenous Education courses are currently being developed for online delivery in subsequent years\.
