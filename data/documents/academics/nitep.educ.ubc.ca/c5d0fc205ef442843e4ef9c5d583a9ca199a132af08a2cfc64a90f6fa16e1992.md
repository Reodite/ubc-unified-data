Distance learning does not only aim to maintain the normal provision of courses, digitally enhance the level of learning and inspire independent study, it also facilitates a wide choice of educational possibilities and approaches that are digitally accessible at all times\. Distance learning is about teaching for everyone, anywhere\. Do you want to redefine your meaning of distance learning? Looking to better utilize digital devices? Eager to maximize online time during these times? Remember the work teachers do changes everyone’s life\. Keep teaching\!

As unexpected factors prevent students from attending school in\-person, how might educators leverage powerful tools to extend learning to home environments? Using communication platforms that support video conferencing is a useful start\. Video conferences you help us connect with our classes from anywhere\. Through easy\-to\-join video calls, you can meet face to face without the travel\. Next, find some video communications resources for today’s collaborative classrooms:

-   -   [**Hangouts Meet**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12GSnqyXQAdkb_dxqhdvfgmVaBaL6HQtH/view) by [**Google for Education**](https://edu.google.com/?modal_active=none) allows educators to connect with students virtually through secure video calls and messaging inside the Google domain\. A new video meeting experience with one goal: make joining meetings effortless to keep the learning going outside the classroom as productive and valuable as face\-to\-face\.
