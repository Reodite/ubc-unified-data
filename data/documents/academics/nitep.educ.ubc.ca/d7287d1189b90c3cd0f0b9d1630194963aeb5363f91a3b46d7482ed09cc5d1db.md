### [https://nitep\-educ\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2019/11/NITEP\-Info\-Session\-copy\-scaled\.jpg](https://nitep-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/11/NITEP-Info-Session-copy-scaled.jpg)[Image](https://nitep-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/11/NITEP-Info-Session-copy-360x240.jpg)From November to December, NITEP will be hosting a number of webinars in order to provide information on the program, different pathways, as well as details regarding application information\.

The webinar will feature an online presentation from NITEP Assistant Director Jessica La Rochelle and Program Advisor, Alexis Okabe\. Other NITEP team members and students may join to provide additional information\.

##### If you are not able to attend the online sessions, they will be available as a recording with the links posted on the NITEP website:

-   [**Thursday, November 21 at 11:00 AM – 12:30 PM** – **NITEP General Info Session**](https://nitep-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/NITEPevent/nitep-webinar-general-information-session/), *Guest: Lyam, NITEP Year 1 &amp; Engagement Peer*
-   [**Monday, November 25 at 10:00 AM – 11:30 AM** – **Indigenous Education Specialization**](https://nitep-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/NITEPevent/nitep-webinar-indigenous-education-specialization/)
-   [**Thursday, November 28 at 11:30 AM to 12:30 PM** – **NITEP Cariboo Professional Year**](https://nitep-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/NITEPevent/nitep-webinar-cariboo-professional-year-in-the-community/), *Guest: Johanna Sam, NITEP Cariboo Coordinator*
-   [**Tuesday, December 3 at 10:00 AM – 11:30 AM** – **NITEP Potential Okanagan field centre**](https://nitep-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/NITEPevent/nitep-webinar-potential-okanagan-field-centre/)

Please complete the form below to register for one of the above webinars\.
