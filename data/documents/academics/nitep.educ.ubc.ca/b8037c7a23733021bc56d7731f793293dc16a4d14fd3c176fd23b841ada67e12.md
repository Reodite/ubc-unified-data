[**UBC Library**](http://www.library.ubc.ca/) is a globally influential research library that provides collections, both physical and digital\. With more than six million volumes, the Library is a vital support for research, learning and teaching excellence\. **[Xwi7xwa Library](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/)** is a centre for academic and community Indigenous scholarship\. Its collections and services reflect Indigenous approaches to teaching, learning, and research\. X̱wi7x̱wa is pronounced ‘whei\-wha\.’ X̱wi7x̱wa means “echo” in the Squamish language, Sḵwx̱wú7mesh Sníchim\. Chief Simon Baker of the Squamish Nation gave the name to the library at the First Nations House of Learning Opening Ceremonies on May 25, 1993\. The mandate of the **[Xwi7xwa Library](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/)** is to “echo” the voices and philosophies of Indigenous peoples through its services, collections, and programs\. In the video below learn about the library’s history from Gene Joseph, Xwi7xwa’s first Librarian\.

For specialized Indigenous digital resources, **[Xwi7xwa Library](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/)** offers [**Open Indigenous Content**](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/open-content/) from digitized archival materials to streaming webcasts\. **[Xwi7xwa Library](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/)** as a centre for academic and community Indigenous scholarship offers valuable, extensive and unique collections\. The [**Open Indigenous Content**](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/open-content/) page highlights digital Indigenous content that is freely available for anyone, useful resources that include:

-   -   Open Databases like the [**First Nations Periodical Index**](http://www.lights.ca/sifc/INTRO.HTM),

-   -   Verna Kirkness’s Speeches inside [**cIRcle**](https://open.library.ubc.ca/search?q=verna%2520kirkness%2520speeches&amp;p=0&amp;sort=0&amp;view=0&amp;circle=n&amp;dBegin=&amp;dEnd=&amp;c=1&amp;creator=Kirkness,%20Verna%20J.),

-   -   [**IKBLC Webcasts**](https://ikblc.ubc.ca/category/webcasts/) Portal,

-   -   [**MOACAT**](http://collection-online.moa.ubc.ca/home), MOA’s online object catalogue\.

-   -   [**Reciprocal Research Network**](https://www.rrncommunity.org/) an online research environment that lets you access cultural First Nations items held at 29 institutions, all from the same convenient interface\.

Created from materials across all branches, UBC Library’s [**Open Collections**](https://open.library.ubc.ca/) are publicly\-accessible digital resources that include photos, books, newspapers, maps, videos, electronic theses, and dissertations\. These collections are constantly growing and reflect the research interests of our community\. With the goal to provide more digital accessibility, [**UBC Library**](http://www.library.ubc.ca/) is a helpful ally collecting useful resources just at our fingertips\. UBC’s open\-access digital repository is called [**cIRcle**](https://circle.ubc.ca/), a digital repository for research and teaching materials created by the UBC community and its partners\. Materials in cIRcle are openly accessible to anyone on the web, and will be preserved for future generations\. Learn how to use it in the following video:

**[Xwi7xwa Library](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/)** also offers a wide range of carefully curated library [**Research Guides**](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/xwi7xwaresearchguide), covering everything from [**Aboriginal Filmmakers**](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/aboriginalfilmmakers) to [**Indigenous Children’s Literature**](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/Indigenouschildrensliterature)\. These guides are particularly helpful places to start when looking for information about a specific subject\. Xwi7xwa librarians continually evaluate books, article indexes, websites to find reliable, authoritative information on relevant topics\. [**Research Guides**](http://guides.library.ubc.ca/xwi7xwaresearchguide) include search strategies for finding resources that are relevant to the multidisciplinary study of Indigenous topics and materials written from Indigenous perspectives\. [**Indigenous Education K\-12**](https://guides.library.ubc.ca/indigenous_ed_k12) guide, for example, includes search strategies for finding books and articles as well as curriculum resources including lesson plans, recommended resources, bibliographies, and search tips\.

UBC’s [**Xwi7xwa Library**](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/) is developing its collection of music by Indigenous artists as well\. The library has been focusing efforts to acquire recordings made by current musicians and recordings of publicly available traditional materials\. Music is embedded in many Indigenous cultures as a way of transmitting language, history, and cultural traditions\. The collection provides immense value for scholarly research and it is meant for enjoyment that could even spark interest in language revitalization and preservation\. It allows library users to access album art and read liner notes, which often provide significant information about the music\. Looking to relax a bit more… Experience history at the tip of your pencil crayons\! UBC Library’s Digital Colouring Books: [**Colour our Collections**](https://about.library.ubc.ca/colour-our-collections/) aims to inspire creativity and spark curiosity through the art found in their collections\. Offering a unique way to engage with library content using themed digital colouring books like ‘[**Mythical Creatures**](https://about.library.ubc.ca/files/2019/02/ColourOurCollectionsUBC_MythicalCreatures_Vol2_FINAL.pdf)’ or ‘[**The Maps and Landscapes’**](https://about.library.ubc.ca/files/2019/09/ColorOurCollectionUBC_AndrewMcCormick_Vol3_FINAL.pdf) series\. It is a colourful way to de\-stress using material from UBC Library’s [**Open Collections**](https://open.library.ubc.ca/) including historic images from the [**Andrew McCormick Maps and Prints Collection**](https://about.library.ubc.ca/files/2019/09/ColorOurCollectionUBC_AndrewMcCormick_Vol3_FINAL.pdf), the [**Japanese Maps of the Tokugawa Era Collection**](https://about.library.ubc.ca/files/2019/09/ColorOurCollectionUBC_JapaneseMaps_Vol4_FINAL.pdf) and [**Chinese Rare Books**](https://about.library.ubc.ca/files/2019/10/ColourOurCollectionsUBC_ChineseRareBooks_Vol5_FINAL.pdf)\.
