The UBC Faculty of Education is currently recruiting Indigenous students who already have a degree and would like to complete a 12\-month UBC Bachelor of Education degree in the Cariboo area starting August 2020\. Students will join other Indigenous students completing their BEd through NITEP\. This is a one\-time opportunity\. The application period is now open and we welcome applications\.
