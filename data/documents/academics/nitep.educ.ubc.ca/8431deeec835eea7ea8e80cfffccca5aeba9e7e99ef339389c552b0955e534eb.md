[Image](https://nitep-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/02/UBC-NITEP-2017-112-YORK8818-360x240.jpg)From April to June, NITEP will be hosting a number of webinars in order to provide information on the program, as well as details regarding application information\. The webinar will feature an online presentation from NITEP Assistant Director Jessica La Rochelle and a scholarship and award workshop with Fraser Valley Field Centre Coordinator Jennifer Anaquod, followed by a Q &amp; A period\.

If you were not able to attend our previous sessions on April 17, May 16, or June 14, please click the link below to access the recording:

-   [April 17 – NITEP Information and How to Apply](https://ca.bbcollab.com/collab/ui/session/playback)
-   [May 16 – Scholarship and Award Session](https://ca.bbcollab.com/recording/ae94a35bdc4744cd81d47eea19752f6b)
-   [June 14 – Applications, Scholarship and Award Session](https://ca.bbcollab.com/collab/ui/session/playback)

If you are interested attending a future webinar, please email [nitep\.educ@ubc\.ca](mailto:nitep.educ@ubc.ca)\.

For more information please click [here](https://nitep.educ.ubc.ca/NITEPevent/nitep-information-session-webinar-may-16th-2018/) for the event page or email [nitep\.educ@ubc\.ca](mailto:nitep.educ@ubc.ca)\.
