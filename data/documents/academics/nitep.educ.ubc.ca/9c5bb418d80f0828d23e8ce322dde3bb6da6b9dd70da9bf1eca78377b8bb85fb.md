#### October 24, 2014

UBC First Nations Longhouse  
1985 West Mall  
Vancouver, BC V6T 1Z2  
8:00 a\.m\. – 3:30 p\.m\.

Co\-hosted by NITEP and the BCTF Aboriginal Education Association

*Empowering Teachers to Teach about Residential Schools*
