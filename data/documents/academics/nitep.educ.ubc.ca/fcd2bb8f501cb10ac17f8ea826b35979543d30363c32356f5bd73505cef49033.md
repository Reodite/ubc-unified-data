### Students at Field Centres Other Than UBC Vancouver:

Students at field centres \(other than UBC Vancouver\) must pay tuition and fees to their field centre’s partner post\-secondary institution\.

**University of the Fraser Valley** – [ufv\.ca/admissions/feeandpay](http://www.ufv.ca/admissions/feeandpay/)

**Thompson Rivers University – Williams Lake** – [tru\.ca/williamslake/registration/fees\.html](http://www.tru.ca/williamslake/registration/fees.html)
