[**Flipgrid**](https://info.flipgrid.com/) is a free online video platform that offers students a place where they can share and reflect on ideas with other students and with their teachers\. [**Flipgrid**](https://info.flipgrid.com/) provides the perfect space for students to take part in classroom discussions and to share their thoughts and to hear their classmates’ ideas\. It is easy to use for teachers and students\. Teachers post topics to initiate the conversation and students respond with short videos\. Students join [**Flipgrid**](https://info.flipgrid.com/) with a code provided by the teacher without the need for them to remember user names and passwords\. Projects can be password protected to comply with schools regulations and to deal with any privacy concerns\.

With this app, learners can reflect, discuss and showcase what they are learning by creating short video responses to a question, topic, or comment\. Educators create online meeting places, called Grids where students can join and post short video responses to topics set by the teacher\. Depending on the settings set by the teacher, students can like each other’s videos, reply video\-to\-video, and upload their own\. [**Flipgrid**](https://info.flipgrid.com/) has a disco library that contains fabulous pre\-made designs to help set everything up\. Educators even have the ability to invite parents and community to view or participate in the Grid\.

Empower learners with Immersive Reader in [**Flipgrid**](https://info.flipgrid.com/)\! All students have an equal voice, students need to share their thoughts, opinions, reflections, and ideas\. But for many learners, it is challenging to share their voice when they have trouble reading\. Enter Immersive Reader, now built into [**Flipgrid**](https://info.flipgrid.com/)\. It brings powerful, proven techniques to improve reading and writing for learners, regardless of their age or ability\. Whether a learner is dyslexic, an early or struggling reader, an ELL student, or simply needs a little assistance, it makes reading and understanding easier\.

Within [**Flipgrid**](https://info.flipgrid.com/) Topics, Immersive Reader can:

-   -   Read text aloud

-   -   Change text size, font, spacing, and color

-   -   Highlight parts of speech

-   -   Break words into syllables

-   -   Provide line focus

-   -   Improve word understanding with picture dictionary
