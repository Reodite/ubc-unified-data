Following the latest University and Faculty of Education directives regarding COVID\-19, the Indigenous Teacher Education Program Office is closed and NITEP staff and faculty are working remotely\. During this time, the NITEP admissions team are continuing to review applications submitted for the 2021/2022 Bachelor of Education program

The NITEP Office can be reached via email at [nitep\.educ@ubc\.ca](mailto:nitep.educ@ubc.ca)\.

We are still accepting applications for the UBC Vancouver, Okanagan and Gitwangak field centres\.
