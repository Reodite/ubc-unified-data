**Chan Centre for the Performing Arts  
Tuesday, September 3, 2013  
2:00 – 3:30 pm**

[https://nitep\-educ\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2013/10/IMG\_2872\.jpg](https://nitep-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/10/IMG_2872.jpg)[Chan Centre Orientation](https://nitep-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2013/10/IMG_2872-246x300.jpg)The Teacher Education Office welcomes new students starting the Bachelor of Education program for 2013/14\. Doors open at 1:45 pm\. Presentations will begin at 2 pm\.

This is a general orientation for all BEd program options\. The session will outline important information regarding expectations, assistance available to teacher candidates while in the program, and an introduction to the Education Students’ Association \(ESA\)\.

Attendance is mandatory and teacher candidates must plan to attend for the full day on the program start date, as indicated in each program option\.

For more information please contact the Teacher Education Office at [teacher\.ed@ubc\.ca](mailto:teacher.ed@ubc.ca) or 604 822 5242\.
