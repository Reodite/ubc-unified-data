[Flip](https://info.flip.com/en-us.html) \(former Flipgrid\) is a free online video platform that offers students a place where they can share and reflect on ideas with other students and with their teachers\. This week in the mentoring circle, we explore how [Flip](https://info.flip.com/en-us.html) provides the perfect space for students to take part in classroom discussions, share their thoughts, and hear their classmates’ ideas\. Teachers post topics to initiate conversations between students by responding with short videos\. It is very easy to use for teachers and students\. To begin, students join the digital platform with a code provided by the teacher without the need for them to remember user names and passwords\. Ready to meet [Flip](https://info.flip.com/en-us.html)\!

With [Flip](https://info.flip.com/en-us.html), learners can reflect, discuss and showcase their learning by creating short video responses to a question, topic, or comment\. Educators create online meeting places, called Grids where students can join and post short video responses to topics set by the teacher\. Depending on the settings set by the teacher, students can like each other’s videos, reply video\-to\-video, and upload their own\. [Flip](https://info.flip.com/en-us.html) has a disco library that contains fabulous pre\-made designs to help set everything up and fabulous lenses for a whole new way to express ourselves while responding\. Educators even can invite parents and the community to view or participate in the Grid\. These can be password\-protected to comply with school regulations and to deal with any privacy concerns\.

[Flip](https://info.flip.com/en-us.html) is a social learning application that engages and empowers student voices in the classroom\. Empower learners with Immersive Reader in [Flip](https://info.flip.com/en-us.html)\! All students have an equal voice, students need to share their thoughts, opinions, reflections, and ideas\. But for many learners, it is challenging to share their voice when they have trouble reading\. Enter Immersive Reader, now built into [Flip](https://info.flip.com/en-us.html)\. It brings powerful, proven techniques to improve reading and writing for learners, regardless of age or ability\. Whether a learner is dyslexic, an early or struggling reader, an ELL student, or simply needs a little assistance, it makes reading and understanding easier\. Here is a list of things Immersive Reader can do:

-   -   Read text aloud

-   -   Change text size, font, spacing, and color

-   -   Highlight parts of speech

-   -   Break words into syllables

-   -   Provide line focus

-   -   Improve word understanding with picture dictionary
