Once you are admitted to the UBC BEd program, you are able to [register as a NITEP student](http://teach.educ.ubc.ca/students/registration/) on the appropriate date for your Elementary or Secondary program option\.

There are [registration guides](http://teach.educ.ubc.ca/students/registration/winter-session/) available for all Program Years 1 to 5 to download from the UBC Teacher Education website\. Please note the date and time for your registration\.

If you have any questions about how to register, please [contact your NITEP Coordinator](https://nitep-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/contact/ "Contact Us")\.
