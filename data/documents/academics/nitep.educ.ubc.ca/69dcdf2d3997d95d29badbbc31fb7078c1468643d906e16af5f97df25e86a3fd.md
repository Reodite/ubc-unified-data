### **Here’s a handy checklist of tips:**

**Get your [UBC card](http://www.ubccard.ubc.ca/)\.**  
This is your ticket to getting your U\-Pass, borrowing books from the library, qualifying for student discounts, and so much more\.

---

**Pick up your [U\-Pass](http://www.upass.ubc.ca/) early\.**  
It gives you access to ride the local transit \(bus, Skytrain, SeaBus, community shuttle\) throughout the Greater Vancouver area — and it’s automatically included in your student fees\. Make sure you [opt out](http://www.upass.ubc.ca/exemptions-and-subsidies/) if you are attending a field centre\.

---

**If you’re looking to live on\-campus, send in your [UBC Housing application](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/)\.**  
Year\-round housing recommended\. Ensure that you [self\-identify as Aboriginal](http://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/applications/how-we-assign-rooms/first-year-guarantee/) in order to guarantee housing\.

---

**Planning to live off\-campus? Check out [UV Rentsline](http://www.uvrentsline.com/)\.**

---

**If you’ve moved in the last 3 months, you need to update your contact information in the [Student Service Centre \(SSC\)](https://ssc.adm.ubc.ca/sscportal/servlets/SRVSSCFramework)\.**

---

**Look up and [purchase your books](http://shop.bookstore.ubc.ca/)\.**  
Buy or rent textbooks, find UBC swag, and purchase computer equipment here\.  

> Note: books for all courses and/or sections may not be available before school starts\.

---

**Take a [bus ride to UBC](http://www.translink.ca/) and explore UBC campus\.**  
Check out the UBC Welcome Centre \(located in the Robert H\. Lee Alumni Centre\), Neville Scarfe building, the Chan Centre, AMS Student Nest, and Brock Hall\. We recommend you take a detour to the Rose Garden, Museum of Anthropology \(MOA\), Nitobe Memorial Garden, Irving K Barber Learning Centre, and the Beaty Biodiversity Museum\.

---

**[Opt out](http://www.ihaveaplan.ca/) of the [AMS Extended Health Plan](http://www.ams.ubc.ca/services/health-dental-plan/): if you are First Nations and covered by your band council\.**  
Review the site to make sure you are covered before you decide to opt out\.

---

**Get the full scoop on [Orientation Day](https://teach-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/students/orientation/ "Orientation")\.** *Note: Orientation Day is only for Year 4 students\.*
