[https://clln\-chem\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2015/10/electric\-pickle\.jpg](https://clln-chem.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/10/electric-pickle.jpg)[electric pickle](https://clln-chem.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2015/10/electric-pickle-360x243.jpg)Did you know that the UBC Chemistry Learning Lab Notebook hosts one of the world’s best\* collections of analytical chemistry demonstrations?

This past June, analytical chemistry faculty from across Canada and the US gathered in a [special session](http://abstracts.csc2015.ca/00001154.htm) at the 2015 CSC to share their favourite teaching demonstrations\.  They also produced a series of videos that provide detailed instructions for  anyone who would like to try these demonstrations themselves\.

You can find all the videos and more information on our [Analytical Chemistry: Teaching with Demonstrations](https://clln-chem.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resources/ubc-chemistry-learning-resources/analytical-chemistry-demos-csc-2015/) page in our [resources](https://learning.chem.ubc.ca/resources/ubc-chemistry-learning-resources/) section\.
