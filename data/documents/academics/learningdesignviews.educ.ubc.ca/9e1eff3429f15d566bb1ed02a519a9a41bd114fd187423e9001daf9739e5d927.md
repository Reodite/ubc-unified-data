Are you planning to use AI tools and wondering how to critically assess and evaluate their features and output? Are you in search of tools capable of generating multimodal content while considering potential ethical issues and limitations? We have curated a set of activities to facilitate and guide your exploration\. These hands\-on activities follow a three\-step process: Explore\-Reflect\-Extend, encouraging meaningful, critical and thoughtful exploration\.

These activities can be undertaken individually or in small groups, providing a flexible approach to enhance your understanding and assist in the selection of the most suitable tools for your need\.  
 

## Learning Objectives

-   Engage with AI tools​\.
-   Enhance critical AI literacy skills through evaluation and reflection\.
-   Extend understanding of the limitations, controversies and implications of AI within the context of teaching and learning​\.
