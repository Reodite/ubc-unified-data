## Learning Objectives

At the end of this activity, students will be able to:

-   Develop new understandings of themselves as critical, strategic writers for empowerment and self\-expression\.
-   Identify the composite writing skills that facilitate learning and understanding\.
-   Identify and clearly articulate how Artificial Intelligence impacts the writing process\.

## Writing is Thinking

“Writing is thinking” is a concept that emphasizes the close relationship between writing and cognitive processes\. Writing serves as a tool for clarifying and refining our thoughts\. When we write, we engage in a process of active reflection, organizing our ideas, exploring connections, and filling gaps in our understanding\. The act of writing forces us to articulate our thoughts in a coherent and structured manner, leading to a deeper comprehension of the subject matter\. As a result, writing becomes an avenue for critical thinking, problem\-solving, and knowledge construction\.

## Discussion Prompts

1.  Do you think that writing is a form of self\-expression and exploration?
2.  How does the act of writing affect your thought process?
3.  Do you ever find that you have a different understanding of a topic after you’ve written about it?
4.  How do you think the process of thinking and building understanding through writing changes when mediated with AI tools such as ChatGPT?
