**February 3, 2026**

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) can be a powerful tool for enhancing productivity at work\. But AI systems pose significant privacy risks when not used with care\. The following activity will help you understand what kind of personal information should not be shared with AI chatbots, in line with UBC policy and the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act \(FIPPA\)\.

## Activity: Evaluating Privacy Risk When Using AI for Email

### Scenario

An instructor at UBC is writing an email to colleagues about grade moderation\. The instructor isn’t happy with how their email is organized, and they want to make sure there aren’t any typos\. To address these issues, the instructor decides to run the email through ChatGPT\. However, the email includes sensitive personal information that should not be shared with an AI chatbot\.

### Activity

1.  **Click or tap to select the personal information in the body of the sample email below** that must be deleted or anonymized before the email can be safely run through ChatGPT\.
2.  This email also includes an additional violation of UBC’s privacy guidelines that the instructor needs to address before sending it\. Click or tap to select this additional violation\.
3.  After you complete the activity, reflect on the following questions:
    -   How can we modify our use of AI to better protect personal information and privacy — our own and that of our students?
    -   What responsibility do AI companies have to ensure that the personal information we share with them \(e\.g\., names, passwords, credit card information\) stays secure?
    -   What other problems could AI pose for privacy and security?

**Hint:** There are four potential privacy violations in the sample email below\. Can’t find them all? Read our [Guidelines for Communicating Through Email](https://lddi.educ.ubc.ca/guidelines-for-communicating-through-email/) and [UBC’s Privacy Fact Sheet: What Is Personal Information?](https://universitycounsel.ubc.ca/files/2023/12/Fact-Sheet-What-is-Personal-Information-updated-Dec-2023.pdf) for clues\.
