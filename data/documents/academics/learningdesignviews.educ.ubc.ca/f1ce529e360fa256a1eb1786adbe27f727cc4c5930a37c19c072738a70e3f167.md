**December 5, 2024**

[Image](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/12/lddi-station-map.jpeg)

A map of the six stations staff were invited to explore during the Artificial Intelligence in the Workplace: Part 2 professional development session\.

**Reading Time:** About 2 minutes

On November 26, 2024, LDDI hosted an in\-person professional development session for Faculty of Education \(FoE\) staff on Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) in the Workplace\. This session was a follow\-up to an earlier session which took place on April 23, 2024\.

During the session, LDDI presented six hands\-on activity stations to help FoE staff learn about and engage with AI tools\. These activities explore prompt design, creativity, multimedia, accessibility, wellbeing and AI coding games\. They follow a three\-step process — Explore\-Reflect\-Extend — encouraging meaningful, critical and thoughtful exploration of AI tools\. We invite you to [explore these activities](https://learningdesignviews.educ.ubc.ca/explore-reflect-extend-deeper-dive/) for yourself and also to try out the related [AI Knowledge Quest](https://learningdesignviews.educ.ubc.ca/ai-knowledge-quest/) quizzes\. You can find these [Critical AI Tool Activities](http://learningdesignviews.educ.ubc.ca/ai-corner/#ctools), along with the activities from the April 2024 session, in the Faculty of Education’s AI Corner\.

At the end of the session, we asked participants to share their key takeaways, what tools or techniques they will use in their workplaces and what they are still curious about\. Their responses and reflections are recorded below\. Responses that refer to a particular activity or tool are linked to that activity sheet\. Some responses have been lightly edited for clarity\.

## What are your takeaways from the session?

-   Always fact\-check
-   AI does not replace human creativity
-   AI is very fast
-   [AI Corner](https://learningdesignviews.educ.ubc.ca/ai-corner/) is a very helpful starting point
-   Be ready for changes/innovations
-   Be mindful of privacy
-   [The one\-by\-one question approach using Microsoft Copilot](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-and-Prompt-Design-1.pdf)
-   Experiment with [creating bespoke graphics](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-for-Visual-Content.pdf)
-   Concerns about security and confidential information
-   [Games generator](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/Coding-Game-Challenges-with-Claude.pdf) as a teaching tool
-   Scrutinizing the content produced
-   [Using AI for minutes, transcripts, translation, email](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-and-Prompt-Design-1.pdf)
-   There’s a lot more AI can do than we can imagine
-   Importance of privacy when using AI tools

## What will you use in your workplace?

-   [Prompt design with Copilot](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-and-Prompt-Design-1.pdf)
-   [Magic ToDo with Goblin Tools](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-and-Well-being.pdf)
-   [Image generation with AI](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-for-Visual-Content.pdf)
-   [Copilot’s email drafting tone adjustments](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-and-Prompt-Design-1.pdf)
-   [Microsoft Word’s “Transcribe” tool](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-and-Prompt-Design-1.pdf)
-   [Goblin Tools for breaking down tasks and time allocation](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-and-Well-being.pdf)
-   [AI to get meeting transcripts](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-and-Prompt-Design-1.pdf)
-   [AI for producing videos](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-in-Multimedia-Audio-and-Video.pdf)
-   [ChatGPT to prepare promotion emails](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-and-Prompt-Design-1.pdf)

## What are you still curious about?

-   How will AI continue to evolve?
-   How will my job look in five years?
-   When will AI\-generated videos be mainstream?
-   Voice\-generation tools — particularly for translating
-   Text\-based video editing
-   In general, the university’s direction with AI
-   Using AI to imitate a voice to teach
