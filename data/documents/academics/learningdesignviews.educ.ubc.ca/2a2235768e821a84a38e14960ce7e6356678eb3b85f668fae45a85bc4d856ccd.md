### Exploring imaginative ideas and creative inspirations for teaching and learning\.

LDDI’s 2025 theme, *Creativity*, aims to explore and showcase the intersection of technology and creativity\. Our goal is to harness the creative potential of digital design, tools, and platforms\. Partner with us to ignite your creative spark, drive innovation, and transform teaching and learning\.

---

## Events and Initiatives

[Image](https://ets-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/viewpoints_AI-and-human-creativity.jpg)

**Blending AI and Human Creativity in Learning**

**Tuesday, May 13, 2025 \| 3:00 pm – 4:30 pm**

This Viewpoints panel discussion delved into the dynamic intersection of generative AI and human creativity in shaping meaningful learning experiences\. Designed for faculty, staff, and students, this panel explored how AI can support educators and learners to innovate, personalize education, brainstorm ideas, and tackle challenges\. At the same time, it addressed critical concerns, including over\-reliance on AI, the potential impact on critical thinking, and ethical implications\.

[Summary &amp; Recording](https://learningdesignviews.educ.ubc.ca/blending-ai-and-human-creativity-in-learning/)

[Image](https://ets-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/Creativity-Play-and-Spontaneity-for-Educators.jpg)

**Creativity, Play &amp; Spontaneity for Educators**

**Tuesday, April 1, 2025 \| 2:00 pm – 3:30 pm**

In this interactive, participatory workshop, Erica Mohan and Jude Walker \(EDST\) showed how creativity, play and spontaneity is an attitude we can adopt in our teaching rather than something to add onto our already packed syllabi\. They shared their own experiences and introduced participants to activities they have used in their classes, from games to role\-plays to improv and artistic playful engagements, all of which support risk\-taking, make teaching and learning more fun and further the learning of both teachers and students\.

[Learn More](https://lddi.educ.ubc.ca/creativity-spontaneity-and-play-for-educators/)

[Image](https://ets-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/03/Imagining-Education.jpg)

**Creativity Challenge: Imagining Education**

**April 2025**

This challenge invited instructors, staff and students in the Faculty of Education to share their creative visions of education\. Participants created images reflecting on the future of education, diversity in education, educational spaces, or a topic of their choice related to education\. Participants were able to choose their preferred medium, including sketch, doodling, photography, digital design, generating an image using artificial intelligence \(AI\)\. The images will be posted on Instagram as a way to share them with the Faculty of Education community\.

[Learn More](https://lddi.educ.ubc.ca/creativity-challenge-imagining-education/)

[Image](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/02/Podcasting-as-Creative-Pedagogy-flyer.jpg)

**Podcasting as Creative Pedagogy**

**Tuesday, March 11, 2025 \| 3:00 pm – 4:00 pm**

This Viewpoints panel discussion showcased creative and emerging uses of podcasting for teaching and learning\. Designed for faculty, staff and students, this panel explored how to leverage podcasting to enhance multimodality, accessibility, student engagement and connection to land and place\. At the same time, it unveiled the creative and technical process of podcast production, including low\-tech recording, editing, and sharing strategies and opportunities for collaboration\.

[Summary &amp; Recording](https://learningdesignviews.educ.ubc.ca/podcasting-as-creative-pedagogy/)

[Image](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/Creative-Writing-in-a-Connected-World-flyer.jpg)

**Creative Writing in a Connected World**

**Thursday, November 28, 2024 \| 3:30 pm – 4:30 pm**

This Viewpoints panel discussion engaged with the evolving landscape of creative writing in the digital age\. Tailored for faculty, staff, and students, the event delved into how technology is reshaping the way we write, teach, and engage with creative content\. This panel offered valuable perspectives and practical advice on future of creative writing and incorporating more technology in your classroom\.

[Summary &amp; Recording](https://learningdesignviews.educ.ubc.ca/creative-writing-in-a-connected-world/)

[Image](https://ets-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/Lungs-of-the-Earth-flyer.jpg)

**Lungs of the Earth**

**November 14–16, 2024**

Lungs of the Earth was a spatialised sound installation inspired by field recordings from the Amazon rainforest, recorded by sound artist Felix Blume and mixed by composer Bernardo Alvarado Rojas\. Four elemental poems by Brazilian writers — “Water” by Patrizia Longhitano, “Fire” by Virna Teixeira, “Air” by Alyssa Martens, and “Earth” by Monika Radojevic — took audiences on a narrative journey, highlighting the call and response between animal and human voices\.

The installation served as a monument to the songs being lost to deforestation and climate change, and encouraged audiences to develop a personal relationship with the story of ‘the last forest’ through a multi\-sensory and interactive exhibition environment\.
