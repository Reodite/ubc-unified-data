### Supporting a sustainable and equitable future for all\.

LDDI’s Climate Justice and Sustainability Initiative aims to enhance awareness of and engagement with principles of sustainability in education to address multi\-dimensional environmental challenges\. Our aim is to develop skills and capacities to create and share resources and professional development opportunities to inspire impactful change in learning and course design\.

### A monthly campaign to ignite climate justice action\.

Our monthly campaign is designed to deepen our understanding of climate justice and engage in meaningful and collective action to lessen the causes and impacts of climate change\.

**  
February 2024: [UBC Furniture Reuse Program](https://facilities.ubc.ca/projects/ubc-furniture-reuse-program/#:~:text=The%20program%20accepts%20donations%20of,even%20more%20waste%20from%20disposal.)**

Donate gently used, surplus furniture from UBC Vancouver faculties and departments\.

**How?** Donate gently used, surplus furniture from UBC Vancouver faculties and departments  
**Why?** So far the program has:  
• Kept 1500 furniture items from disposal  
• Avoided 95 \(tonne CO2e\) of carbon emissions  
• Saved over $900,000 in cost\-avoidance

**The Intersection of Climate Justice and Digital Innovation: Raising Awareness of Technologies Environmental Footprint**

**Wednesday, February 28 \| 12pm – 1:30pm**

Join panelists, Dr\. Keri Ewart, Dr\. Sandra Scott, Dr\. Derek Gladwin, and Dr\. Kshmata Hunter for a conversation about the intersections and implications of climate justice and digital innovation\. Panelists will share their research and perspectives on the nexus between technology and the environment and discuss strategies and limitations across these intersections\.
