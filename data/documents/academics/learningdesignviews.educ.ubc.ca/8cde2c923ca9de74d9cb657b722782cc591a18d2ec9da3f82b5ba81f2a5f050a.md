### Reimagining digital innovation for teaching and learning through the ABCs of intention: Awareness, Betterment and Care\.

LDDI’s 2026 theme, **Intention**, aims to foster conscientious approaches to digital innovation and technology integration\. Our goal is to challenge the fast\-paced, unconscious, uncritical use of technology in education\. Through the ABCs framework, we identify three core principles of intentionality:

[Icon 1](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/icon_awareness.png)

### Awareness

Build knowledge and understanding of digital technologies through evidence\-based inquiry, critical thinking and mindfulness to improve pedagogy and practice\.

[Icon 1](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/icon_betterment.png)

### Betterment

Empower educators and students to make a positive impact and build a better future in education and beyond, for this generation and the next\.

[Icon 1](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/icon_care.png)

### Care

Take responsibility for the impact that our technology use has on people and the planet; be mindful of how we integrate digital tools and practices in teaching and learning\.

### Activities and challenges designed to inspire thoughtful and intentional engagement with technology\.

Our rotating campaign of activities invites educators and students to engage with technology through critical thinking, intentional action and thoughtful reflection\. Each activity is grounded in one of the ABCs of intention — starting with Awareness — and can be used to spark in\-class exploration of technology\-related topics\.

**  
AI and Email Privacy Quiz**

Do you know how to protect privacy — your own and your students’ — when using AI to write or edit emails? Test your knowledge with our interactive quiz\!

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) can be a powerful tool for enhancing productivity at work, but it can also pose significant privacy risks when not used with care\. This activity will help you understand what kind of personal information should not be shared with AI chatbots\.

[Take the AI and Email Privacy quiz](https://learningdesignviews.educ.ubc.ca/2026/02/04/ai-and-email-privacy/)

## Events and Initiatives

[Image](https://ets-educ.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/02/Practicing-Intention-flyer.jpg)

**Practicing Intention in the Age of AI**

**Thursday, March 26, 2026 \| 12:00 pm – 1:30 pm**

This Viewpoints panel discussion brought together emergent scholars working at the intersection of AI, pedagogy and critical practice, inviting a grounded conversation about what critical and caring use of AI can look like in real educational settings\. Designed for faculty, staff and students, this session sparked a rich discussion about how educators can encourage critical thinking in the age of AI, as well as an exploration of the reasons students might engage — or choose not to engage — with artificial intelligence tools\.

[Summary &amp; Recording](https://learningdesignviews.educ.ubc.ca/practicing-intention-in-the-age-of-ai/)

[Image](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/11/Cultivating-Empathy-flyer.jpg)

**Cultivating Empathy Through Digital Storytelling**

**Wednesday, October 29, 2025 \| 12:00 pm – 2:00 pm**

This Viewpoints panel discussion explored how digital storytelling can cultivate empathy, deepen understanding and inspire meaningful change across educational and community contexts\. Panelists shared insights from their research and practice in areas such as ecological storytelling, immersive audio experiences, multimodal literacies, and community\-based digital narratives\. Together, they addressed critical considerations including ethical storytelling, digital equity and authentic human connection, guided by the ABCs of Intention: Awareness, Betterment and Care\.

[Summary &amp; Recording](https://learningdesignviews.educ.ubc.ca/cultivating-empathy-through-digital-storytelling/)

[Image](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/Studio-B-podcast.jpg)

**Studio B Podcast**

**New episodes released throughout 2025–26**

Recorded in LDDI’s [Studio B](https://lddi.educ.ubc.ca/faculty-of-education-studios/studio-b/), this podcast explores the evolving role of technology in education, with a special focus on artificial intelligence \(AI\), ethics and innovative approaches to technology\. Hosted by members of the Learning Design &amp; Digital Innovation team, each episode sparks conversation around emerging tools and the future of teaching and learning\. Designed for the Faculty of Education community and beyond, Studio B invites listeners to reflect, experiment and learn about what’s possible in technology\-enhanced education\.
