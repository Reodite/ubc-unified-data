Curious about how generative AI can be applied in real\-world scenarios? Dive into activities that harness the power of AI in creative ways, applying it across diverse contexts while evaluating how effectively the results meet your goals\. These hands\-on activities follow a three\-step process: Explore\-Reflect\-Extend, encouraging meaningful, critical and thoughtful exploration\.

These activities can be undertaken individually or in small groups, providing a flexible approach to enhance your understanding and assist in the selection of the most suitable tools for your need\.

To get started, view the presentation below to explore how AI can be used in the workplace and other contexts with a focus on privacy, ethics and productivity\.

## Learning Objectives

-   Engage with AI tools​\.
-   Enhance critical AI literacy skills through evaluation and reflection\.
-   Extend understanding of the limitations, controversies and implications of AI within the context of teaching and learning​\.

## Activity Sheets

*Please note that unless otherwise specified, these tools are not Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act \(FIPPA\) compliant and have not undergone a UBC Privacy Impact Assessment \(PIA\)\. In the absence of a PIA, instructors should not mandate the use of a tool or service without presenting alternatives that do not necessitate the use of students’ personal information\.*

### Prompt Design with Copilot

Learn guiding principles and best practices for prompt design, including how to transcribe, summarize, analyze survey data, craft emails and create a poem with Microsoft Copilot\.  
[Download the Guiding Principles on Prompt Designing Activity Sheet \[PDF\]](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-and-Prompt-Design-1.pdf)

### Creativity and Visual Design

Generate visual content with Microsoft Copilot, Adobe Creative Cloud and Canva\. Discuss the implications for copyright and the environmental impact of using AI to generate visual content\.  
[Download the Generating Visual Content with GenAI Software Tools Activity Sheet \[PDF\]](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-for-Visual-Content.pdf)

### Video, Audio, and Podcasting

Discover a range of AI\-driven audio, video, and podcasting tools: Descript, ElevenLabs, and CamtasiaRev\. These tools offer features including transcription, voice cloning and captioning\. You will also discuss the ethical concerns and implications of these tools\.  
[Download the Multimedia: Audio, Video, &amp; Podcasting Activity Sheet \[PDF\]](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-in-Multimedia-Audio-and-Video.pdf)

### AI for Accessibility

Explore speech\-to\-text, alt\-text generation and HTML code creation features within Microsoft Copilot\.  
[Download the Improving Content Accessibility with Copilot Activity Sheet \[PDF\]](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-for-Accessibility.pdf)

### AI and Wellbeing

Leverage tools such as Microsoft Copilot, Goblin and ChatGPT to help improve wellbeing\. Explore AI\-driven task management and prompt design for mindfulness activities\.  
[Download the Crafting AI Solutions for Workplace Wellbeing Activity Sheet \[PDF\]](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/AI-and-Well-being.pdf)

### Coding Game Challenges with Claude

Play, explore, interact and review three games — Tic Tac Toe, Connect 4, and PacMan — created with Claude\. Then, take it a step further by designing your own exciting challenge\!  
[Download the Coding Game Challenges with Claude Activity Sheet \[PDF\]](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/11/Coding-Game-Challenges-with-Claude.pdf)

### AI and Misinformation

Learn how to determine if an image is AI\-generated and reflect on how AI tools have changed how we interact with visual media and visual evidence\.  
[Download the AI and Misinformation Activity Sheet \[PDF\]](https://educ-learningdesignviews-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/AI-and-Misinformation.pdf)
