The *Program in the Study of Religion* is an interdisciplinary undergraduate program that includes a [major](https://rgst.arts.ubc.ca/program/major-requirements/) and [minor](https://rgst.arts.ubc.ca/program/minor-requirements/)\. It offers students an opportunity to study a variety of religions from a number of disciplinary perspectives, incorporating different regions and historical periods, while allowing students to specialize in religious traditions of their choice\. [Click here for more information about the program](https://rgst.arts.ubc.ca/program/)\.
