We have created these resources with instructors in mind\. As such, they include complete lessons and workshops, timing guides and answer keys for associated student activities\.

Some of these materials are openly visible, but you will only be able to download the associated answer keys and complete lesson resources after contacting a site administrator with your details \(including a verifiable academic institution email address\)\. Once you have contacted us [here](https://sciencewritingresources2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/access-request-and-feedback-form/), you will receive a password granting you full access\.

Some of the resources were developed as part of our Writing Across the Curriculum\+ \(WAC\+\) program\.

Please visit our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCvynvmsn_NTlS9lc8cH-OFw) for videos aimed at helping students improve different elements of their science writing\. Among the featured topics are the active and passive voice, creating writing outlines, and sources\.
