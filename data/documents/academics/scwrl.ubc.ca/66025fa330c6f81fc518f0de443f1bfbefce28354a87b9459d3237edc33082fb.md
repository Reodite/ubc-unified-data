**1\)** Should the following piece of information, taken from a real source, be quoted directly or paraphrased and cited?

*‘Telomeres are specialized structures found at the natural ends of eukaryotic linear chromosomes\.’* \= **PARAPHRASED AND CITED \(it is not unique to the author – it is not an opinion, for example\)**

**2\)** For the sake of this question, suppose that you chose to paraphrase and cite the previous piece of information, and that it came from a research article written by Vicki Lundblad and Jack Szostak in 1989\. Which of the following is the correct style for citing this in your work when using the ‘Expanding Referencing’ style of citing?

**A: Lundblad and Szostak \(1989\) showed that… \= CORRECT**  
B: Lundblad et al\. \(1989\) showed that… **\[‘et al\.’ should only be used for more than two authors\]**  
C: Vicki Lundblad and Jack Szostak \(1989\) showed that… **\[first names should not be used in the in\-text citations\]**

**3\)** Should the following piece of information, taken from a real source, be quoted directly or paraphrased and cited?

*‘I’m so excited – this new discovery blows the old belief clean out of the water\.’* \= **QUOTED \[it is well phrased and unique\]**

**4\)** For the sake of this question, suppose that you chose to paraphrase and cite the previous piece of information, and that it came from a research article written by Mitchell Tonker in 2007\. Which of the following is the correct style for citing this in your work when using the ‘Expanding Referencing’ style of citing?

A: Mitchell Tonker \(2007\) said that… **\[first names should not be used in the text, even before a citation is given\]**  
**B: It was said that… \(Tonker 2007\) \= CORRECT**  
C: Tonker, 2007, said that… **\[the date would be better included in parentheses\]**

**5\)** Should the following piece of information, taken from a real source, be quoted directly or paraphrased and cited?

*‘Changes to the conformation of coding and non\-coding RNAs form the basis of elements of genetic regulation and provide an important source of complexity, which drives many of the fundamental processes of life\.’* \= **PARAPHRASED AND CITED \[it is not particularly well phrased – you could simplify and shorten it, and it is not unique to the author; it is not an opinion, for example\]**

**6\)** For the sake of this question, suppose that you chose to paraphrase and cite the previous piece of information, and that it came from a research article written by Elizabeth A Dethoff, Katja Petzold, Jeetender Chugh, Anette Casiano\-Negroni and Hasham M Al\-Hashimi in 2012\. Which of the following is the correct style for citing this in your work when using the ‘Expanding Referencing’ style of citing?

A: Dethoff et al\. recently suggested that…**\[no date is listed to check the citation in the reference list\]**  
B: Dethoff, Petzold, Chigh, Casiano\-Negroni, and As\-Hashimi \(2012\) suggested that… **\[‘et al\.’ should be used when more than two authors write an article\]**  
C: Dethoff et al\. \(2012\) suggested that… \= **CORRECT**

Do the following pieces of information need to be cited?

**7\. NO \[this is common knowledge within this specific field\]**  
**8\. YES \[these are facts taken from other sources that you are using as evidence\]**  
**9\. YES \[this is an authoritative/original idea that helped develop a field of research, and is not common knowledge within your audience\]**  
**10\. NO \[this is a readily verifiable fact\]**
