In this Quick Tips podcast, we listen to the golden nuggets of information provided by our science writing experts in other podcasts in this series\. These quick tips include the pick of the advice for implementing [peer review](https://sciencewritingresources2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/educator-resources/podcasts/peer-review/) in your classes, increasing efficiency in [grading and providing useful feedback](https://sciencewritingresources2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/educator-resources/podcasts/grading-and-providing-feedback/), encouraging students to access [writing\-specific tutoring services](https://sciencewritingresources2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/educator-resources/podcasts/tutoring/), and [designing effective writing assignments](https://sciencewritingresources2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/educator-resources/podcasts/designing-effective-writing-assignments/) that will engage your students\.

Please note that the four topics highlighted above are covered in much greater detail in their own dedicated podcasts, all of which can be accessed from the main [Podcasts](https://sciencewritingresources2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/educator-resources/podcasts/) page\. We have created complementary resources for each podcast \(such as lesson plans, activities, and student tip sheets\)\.

All of these can be downloaded from each specific podcast page, but you will need to contact a site administrator [here](https://sciencewritingresources2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/access-request-and-feedback-form/) and provide your details \(including a verifiable academic institution email address\) to receive the password that will enable you to download marking keys/rubrics\.
