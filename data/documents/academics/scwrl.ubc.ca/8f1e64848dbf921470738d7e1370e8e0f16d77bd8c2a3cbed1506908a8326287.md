All of our science writing resources are freely available \(under a Creative Commons license\), but they have been tailored to different audiences and some require a brief authentication check before being accessed and downloaded\.

[Student resources](https://scwrl.ubc.ca/stem-writing-resources/) can be viewed and downloaded by anyone, whereas the [educator resources](https://scwrl.ubc.ca/instructor-resources/) have been created with instructors in mind\. These resources include lesson plans and workshops, and incorporate timing guidelines and answer keys for student activities\. To gain access to any of these, simply contact a site administrator [here](https://sciencewritingresources2020.sites.olt.ubc.ca/access-request-and-feedback-form/) with your details \(including a verifiable academic institution email address\) and you will receive a password to download all the materials\.

We have incorporated quick quizzes at the end of most of our student resources, so that you can see whether you have mastered some of the key concepts discussed within these specific resources\.

We also have a dedicated [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCvynvmsn_NTlS9lc8cH-OFw), which houses all of our video resources\. These may be useful to students focusing on specific elements of science writing, as well as to instructors, who may wish to teach these elements in their classes\. Lastly, we have created a dedicated [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/scwrl) page that houses audio podcasts\. The majority of these have been created with instructors in mind, but students are also welcome to listen to them as they all contain tips that are useful to both demographics\.
