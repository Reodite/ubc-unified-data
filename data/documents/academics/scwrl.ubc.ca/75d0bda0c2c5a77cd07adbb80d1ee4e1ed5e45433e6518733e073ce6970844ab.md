We have designed these podcasts for instructors who are keen to implement specific elements of science writing into their classes\. Each podcast features general discussion and tailored tips that can be applied in different teaching scenarios\.

We chat to a number of different instructors, tutors and science writing specialists with personal experience in teaching science communication to bring a variety of viewpoints and expertise to the mix\.

All of our podcasts are housed on the [ScWRL SoundCloud Channel](https://soundcloud.com/scwrl), which we invite you to subscribe to\.

Additionally, we have designed complementary resources, such as PowerPoint presentations, student handouts, and tip sheets to accompany each podcast\. Links to these appear within each specific podcast page\.
