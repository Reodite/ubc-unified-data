Anyone can access these resources freely, and without registration\. We have primarily designed them for undergraduate students but they will also be useful for students at earlier and later stages of their education\.

You will be able to download many of these resources to keep as study guides, and can also complete [quick quizzes](https://scwrl.ubc.ca/student-resources/quick-quizzes/) online to test your conceptual development and application\. The quick quizzes have been designed to give you an idea of how well you have grasped the important concepts relating to the topics that they assess\. We encourage you to read the associated resources before attempting to complete the quizzes\.

We are also very keen to receive your feedback on the website, and on specific resources you have used, or would like to see us create in the future\. You can submit your feedback through our [feedback form](https://scwrl.ubc.ca/feedback-what-do-you-think/)\.
