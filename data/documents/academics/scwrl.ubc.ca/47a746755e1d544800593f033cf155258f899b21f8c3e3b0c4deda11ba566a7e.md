We have created our resources with the aim of inspiring students and educators across the globe to take an \(even bigger\) interest in science writing\. At the heart of that aim is the desire to see science communicated more widely, so that more and more people hear and read about science and learn how it has shaped our past and how it will continue to shape our future\.

In early 2016, we launched a guest blog which will regularly feature posts from science and communications experts, whose expertise and enthusiasm will open our eyes to tips and tricks for success in the growing field of science communication, and stimulate our brains by exposing us to some weird and wonderful science\.

If you are an educator, budding science journalist, or if you work in a related industry, and would like to contribute to this blog, please let us know by filling out the form below\!
