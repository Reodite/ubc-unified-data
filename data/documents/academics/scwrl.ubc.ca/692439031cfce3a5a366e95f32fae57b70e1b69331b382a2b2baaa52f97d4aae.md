## **About ScWRL…\.**

The **STEM Writing Resources for Learning \(ScWRL\)** provides freely available materials for teaching and learning in STEM communication\. Our team aims to provide students with the resources needed to communicate effectively, and to provide educators with the tools needed to support students as they develop their writing skills\. 

To read more about this project, click [here](https://scwrl.ubc.ca/about/)\. Students can access the STEM Writing Resources [here](https://scwrl.ubc.ca/stem-writing-resources/), while educators can access the Instructor Resources [here](https://scwrl.ubc.ca/instructor-resources/)\. Additional content for instructors can be accessed once they have completed the [access request and feedback form](https://scwrl.ubc.ca/access-request-and-feedback-form/)\.
