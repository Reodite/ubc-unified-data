The Faculty of Medicine supports Postgraduate Medical Education trainees, residency programs, faculty and staff in achieving educational success, including aligning training programs in a manner that fits societal needs, through leading innovative practices to enhance, integrate and extend programs; and strategically engaging our partners in the provincially distributed training model\.

The Faculty of Medicine’s support for this mission is reflected throughout our strategic plan, [Building the Future, 2021\-2026](https://stratplan.med.ubc.ca/), and is specifically articulated under ”Education” in the “Pillars” section\.
