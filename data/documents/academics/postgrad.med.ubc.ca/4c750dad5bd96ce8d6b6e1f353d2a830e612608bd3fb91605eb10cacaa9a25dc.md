Postgraduate Trainees are post\-MD trainees who are pursuing further clinical or research training in their own specialty, but who have not yet completed sufficient training that would allow them to be listed \(registered\) as a specialist in their home country\.

Click the tabs below to find more information on eligibility, application procedures, English language proficiency requirements, and more\.

#### Eligibility

International Postgraduate Trainee appointments at UBC are available to:

-   Post\-MD trainees who are pursuing further clinical or research training in their own specialty, but who have not yet completed sufficient training that would allow them to be listed \(registered\) as a specialist in their home country\.
-   Candidates who have met the time requirements of training but who have not succeeded in the examination requirements of their specialty\. This requirement will only be waived if the candidate is within a two\-year period of examination eligibility\.

**Note:** Training provided in this appointment will NOT be evaluated by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada\. This appointment will NOT be considered for positions as a paid physician assistant, nor postdoctoral research trainees, nor subspecialty residents\.

#### Application Process and Timeline

##### Application Process

1.  The [Application for Approval of Appointment of International Postgraduate Trainee Form](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ApplicationforApprovalofAppointment-PostgraduateTrainee_20260914.docx) must be completed and signed by the following individuals, in the following order:
    -   Postgraduate Trainee
    -   Training Supervisor
    -   UBC Department/Division Head
    -   Health Authority Department Head/Division Head/Chief Officer of training site\(s\)
2.  Once a completed application has been prepared, signatures obtained, and the fee\(s\) paid, the Department/Division is responsible for submitting the application to the Postgraduate Medical Education \(PGME\) Dean’s Office\. The Department/Division is then responsible for obtaining licensure from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia\.
3.  The applicant must pay a $500\.00 non\-refundable, non\-transferable administrative fee\. The administrative fee can be paid by Internal Service Delivery \(ISD\) or [online](https://secure.touchnet.net/C20435_ustores/web/store_main.jsp?STOREID=38&amp;SINGLESTORE=true)\. Each Postgraduate Trainee is eligible for a one\-time extension fee waiver for short extensions up to 3 months\.

##### Application Timeline

Complete applications must be submitted to PGME a minimum of 8 weeks in advance of the training start date\. Failure to meet this deadline will result in a returned application\.

Please provide sufficient time for the training program to review your application and prepare all the required documentation in advance of this 8 week deadline\. The program must confirm that all the required documents are in place, signatures have been obtained, and the fee has been paid\. Once everything is completed, the Department/Division is responsible for submitting the application to the Postgraduate Medical Education \(PGME\) Dean’s Office\.

The Department/Division is responsible for obtaining licensure from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia\.

#### Administrative Fees

Administrative fees can be paid [online](https://secure.touchnet.net/C20435_ustores/web/store_main.jsp?STOREID=38&amp;SINGLESTORE=true) or by Internal Service Delivery \(ISD\)\.

Postgraduate Trainee Fee is $500\.00

Note: For internal payment submission using ISD, programs are requested to provide program ID/Workday worktag to [pgme\.clinicalfellows@ubc\.ca](mailto:pgme.electives@ubc.ca)\.

#### Credentials Verification

The College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia \(CPSBC\) requires all physicians who have completed their medical education outside of Canada and who are now seeking medical licensure in B\.C\. submit copies of certain documents to the Medical Council of Canada’s [Physicians Apply](http://physiciansapply.ca/services/) for source verification\.

Check the CPSBC’s website for more information on [credentials verification requirements](https://www.cpsbc.ca/registrants/current-registrants/registration-and-licensing/student-and-graduates/postgraduate-trainee)\.

#### English Language Proficiency Requirements

Any candidate for licensure in British Columbia must have the ability to speak, read, and write English to the satisfaction of the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia’s Registration Committee\.

Further information about CPSBC English Language Proficiency requirements can be found [here](https://www.cpsbc.ca/files/pdf/REG-ELP.pdf "CPSBC English Language Requirements")\.

#### Registration Fees

Postgraduate Trainees are not registered as residents with the University and do not have to pay registration fees\. However, they will be required to pay an annual licensing fee to the College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC\.

#### Salary

Postgraduate Trainees are not under the umbrella of the Resident Doctors of BC\. Salary arrangements are the responsibility of the sponsoring agency or specialty program\. Self\-funded or unfunded applicants are not eligible for training\.

#### Insurance

Postgraduate Trainees are required to purchase personal medical malpractice liability insurance for their activities\.

#### Immigration / Work Permit Documentation

All immigration/work permit documentation is the responsibility of the sponsoring specialty program\. Please see UBC’s [Immigration Process for Foreign Clinical Fellows](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/faculty-relations/immigration/immigration-process-for-foreign-clinical-fellows/) for further information\.

For resources on housing and childcare services in British Columbia, please see our [Moving to BC page\.](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/moving-to-bc/)

#### Questions

Questions regarding the appointments of Postgraduate Trainees can be sent to [pgme\.clinicalfellows@ubc\.ca](mailto:pgme.clinicalfellows@ubc.ca)\. Please allow five business days for a response\.
