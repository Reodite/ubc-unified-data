Often residency training can feel daunting, overwhelming, and impossible\.

Running 100 miles can seem impossible too\- until it isn’t\. Watch Brendan Leonard and Jayson Sime, two normal guys, [train and conquer Run Rabbit Run](https://www.rei.com/blog/run/how-to-run-a-100-miles), a 100 mile endurance race in Colorado with over 20,000 feet of elevation gain\.

As narrator Brendan Leonard suggests, the mental capacity for determination can really help us do anything we want\. Courtney Dauwalter, a professional ultramarathoner whose accomplishments include an outright win by over 10 hours at the 2017 Moab 240 Mile Race, [shares how mental determination can help all of us accomplish things that we never thought were possible](https://uproxx.com/life/ultramarathon-runner-courtney-dauwalter-interview/)\.
