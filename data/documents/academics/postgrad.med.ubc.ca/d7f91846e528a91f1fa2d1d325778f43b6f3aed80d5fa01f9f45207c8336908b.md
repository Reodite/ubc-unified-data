The **4th Annual UBC Postgraduate Faculty and Staff Wellness Award** honours faculty and staff members in Postgraduate Medical Education who demonstrate leadership in resident wellness\. This award recognizes faculty and staff members located at any site across the province\.

Do you know a faculty and/or staff member whose leadership and advocacy has helped to create a healthy residency experience? Nominate someone for the Faculty and Staff Wellness Award today\.

**Criteria**

\-Displays a commitment to promoting and advocating for a healthy residency experience;

\-Considers all aspects of a resident’s well\-being

\-Leads by example: models wellness in daily life;

\-Takes a leadership role in influencing the culture of wellness in their department/ program\.

**Eligibility**

The recipient must be a current faculty or staff member in UBC postgraduate medical education\. This may include program directors, program administrators, clinical professors, program managers, site coordinators, and other roles\.

The Award This award will be presented at the [12th Annual Resident Talent Night, on Thurs\. Sept\. 26, 2019](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/resident-talent-night/)\. Please join us for this entertaining evening celebrating arts in medicine\!

**How to nominate**

Any current UBC resident can make a nomination by visiting the [Award Webpage](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/faculty-and-staff-wellness-award/)\.  **Deadline for submissions is September 4th, 2019\.**
