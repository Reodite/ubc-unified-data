The Clinical Education Fellowship Program is an opportunity for senior residents \(R3\-R5\) or established clinicians to develop experience and skills in health education scholarship\. The two\-year fellowship is designed for those interested in developing an academic career as a Clinician Educator\. Residents making an application must currently be in a Canadian training program, in a Canadian institution\.

Please visit the Centre for Health Education Scholarship website for program details, eligibility, and application deadlines\.

[Centre for Health Education Scholarship website](http://ches.med.ubc.ca/programs/clinical-educator-fellowship-program/%20)
