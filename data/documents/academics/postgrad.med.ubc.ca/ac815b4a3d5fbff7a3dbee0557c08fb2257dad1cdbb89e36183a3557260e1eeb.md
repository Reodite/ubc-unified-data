## [Orientation Icon](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/orientations.png)

## Occupational Health &amp; Safety Orientation

Residents attend one half\-day virtual session offered on the following days: June 12, AM or PM; June 26, AM or PM; or June 29, AM\. This session is part of the [Resident Doctors Occupational Health &amp; Safety and Violence Prevention Training](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/orientations-trainings/online-training/#health)\.

## IMG Orientation

June 15\-19, 22\-23

All International Medical Graduates \(IMGs\) who match to UBC postgraduate medical residency programs through the R1 CaRMS match are required to attend the IMG Orientation\.

## RDBC Orientation

Multiple dates and locations\. The details and registration link are provided on their [website](https://residentdoctorsbc.ca/about/news/r1-residents-orientation-2026)\. Please register by June 15\.

## CBME Elentra Resident Orientation

July 9 at 10:00\-11:30am\. [See poster for more details](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/ElentraOrientation_AGD_20260709_Rev0.pdf)\.

## PGME New Resident Orientation

July 14 \(Afternoon\)\.

## Faculty Feedback &amp; Support \(FFS\) Resident Orientation

New residents will attend a session on either July 30, 3\-4pm or Aug 11, 11\-12pm\. If you do not receive information on this, please reach out to your program administrator\.
