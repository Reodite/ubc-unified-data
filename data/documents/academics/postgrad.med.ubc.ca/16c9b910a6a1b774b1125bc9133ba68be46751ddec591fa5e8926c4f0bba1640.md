Many people [experience imposter syndrome during their residency training](https://thedo.osteopathic.org/2018/08/imposter-syndrome-preventing-it-overcoming-it-and-the-link-to-burnout/)\.

The way Imposter Syndrome is experienced can differ based on a number of different factors, including gender\. This gendered experience of Imposter Syndrome is not specific to medicine; [other communities such as female endurance athletes](https://www.trailsisters.net/2019/08/07/imposter-syndrome-keeps-women-from-dreaming-big-at-ultras/) experience Imposter Syndrome as a result of factors on both on an institutional level and internalized mindsets from external messaging\. [Suggests of helping to manage Imposter Syndrome](https://thedo.osteopathic.org/2018/08/imposter-syndrome-preventing-it-overcoming-it-and-the-link-to-burnout/) can be applicable across multiple environments, including ultrarunning and medicine\.
