## Physician Health Program and Other Supports

In addition to what is offered by the [Resident Counselling &amp; Peer Support Office](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/rcaps/), residents also have access to the [Physician Health Program](https://www.physicianhealth.com/) \(PHP\)\. PHP do not charge for any counselling, support, advisory, advocacy and referral services that they provide to resident doctors and their eligible dependents\. Residents are free to contact either RCAPS or PHP for counselling supports\. Both PHP &amp; RCAPS work closely together and will refer to one another if capacity limits are reached\.

PHP can provide assistance in [finding a family physician](https://www.physicianhealth.com/gp/d4d/)\. The BC College of Family Physicians also have the following resource to [help individuals find a Family Physician](https://bccfp.bc.ca/for-the-public/find-a-family-doctor/)\.

In addition to [PHP](https://www.physicianhealth.com/) and [RCAPS](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/rcaps/) you may also seek support via your health authority Employee and Family Assistance Program \(EFAP\) or Employee Wellness program\. As per the [Resident Collective Agreement](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2022-2025-RDBC-Collective-Agreement-FINAL.pdf) \(Article 15\.04\) residents have support available to see a registered clinical counsellor, psychologist or social worker via the resident extended health plan\.

#### How to access Electronic Pay Statements Online

The following guide has been created with the purpose of supporting UBC residents with self\-registration of their VCH employee ID with their health authority network accounts\. This will allow residents to access their electronic pay statements online, with any BC health authority network account\.

[How to Access Pay Statements Online](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/VCH-Residents-Self-Register-Network-ID-to-Access-Electronic-Pay-Statements-Online.pdf)

#### T4

Residents are able to access their T4 slips online through either:

1.  [Electronic Pay Statement](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/VCH-Residents-Self-Register-Network-ID-to-Access-Electronic-Pay-Statements-Online.pdf)
2.  [CRA account](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency.html)

If residents receive paper pay statements, they can expect to receive their T4 slips in the mail\. However, if they have access to their pay statements online, their T4 will also be available online\. This distinction depends on the Health Authority where they work and that Health Authority should be contacted if there is any confusion\. Individuals should expect to have received their T4 for the previous year in the mail by the end of February\.

For general T4 and pay inquiries, contact Payroll Services at [PayrollVCH@phsa\.ca](mailto:PayrollVCH@phsa.ca) or call 604\-297\-8683 \(select Option 2\) / 1\-866\-875\-5306 \(select Option 2\)\.

For technical issues accessing the Electronic Pay Statement site, please contact the Service Desk at [ServiceDesk@vch\.ca](mailto:ServiceDesk@vch.ca) or phone 604\-875\-4334/1\-88\-875\-4334\.

#### T2202

The T2202 – Tuition and Enrollment Certificate is a tax form used to certify a student’s eligibility for the education and textbook amounts\. Please note, that the T2202 form has **replaced** the T2202A and the TL11B forms\)\. This form is used to indicate the maximum student tax deductions for tuition, education, and textbook amounts\.

Residents can access their T2202 through [Workday](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/finances/downloading-your-tax-forms/)\. Questions about T2202A can be directed to UBC [AskMe](https://www.askme.ubc.ca/form/s/)\.

#### T2200

T2200 is only for expenses that are required as part of residents’ employment contract that are not directly reimbursed\. There are very few expenses that residents would be required to incur that are not directly reimbursed by UBC or the Health Authority\.

The T2200 form can be accessed through the [Canada Revenue Agency](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2200.html)\. Residents who want to file the T2200 with their tax return, should complete the form and then submit it for approval via email to [VCH Payroll](mailto:ResidentDoctors@vch.ca)\. If there are costs on the T2200 form that are specific to their department, residents should specify which program in their email to VCH\.
