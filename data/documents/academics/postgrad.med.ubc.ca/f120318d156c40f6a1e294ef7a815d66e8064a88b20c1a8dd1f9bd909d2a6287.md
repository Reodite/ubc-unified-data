Postgraduate Medical Education \(PGME\) registration for the 2026\-2027 academic year will begin on April 16, 2026 at 12PM and must be **completed by May 1, 2026**\. In order to register you will need to set up your [Campus\-Wide Login \(CWL\)](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/getting-started-at-ubc/set-up-your-ubc-cwl-account/), [Multi\-Factor Authentication](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/getting-started-at-ubc/set-up-your-ubc-cwl-account/#mfa) and [UBC email address](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/getting-started-at-ubc/email-address/)\.

Resident registration is mandatory; failure to complete registration in a timely manner will result in a delay of the commencement of your training program\.  

[Image](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/fillout-300x270.png)

## **To complete RMS registration you need to:** 

1.  **Login:** When registration begins, you will receive an email with a unique link and login instructions to the online [Resident Management System](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/resident-management-system-rms/) \(RMS\)\. Follow the link and login\. \(Note: for residents new to UBC, emails will be sent to the email address provided with your residency application\)\.  
2.  **Update Contact Information:** Both new and returning residents will be required to update their contact information annually in RMS during the registration period\. All UBC residents need to use a [UBC\-issued email address](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/getting-started-at-ubc/email-address/) when corresponding with the PGME Dean’s Office and Programs and while using the Resident Management System\. Make sure your UBC email address is listed in RMS\. 
3.  **Sign your letter of Offer:** Residents who are new to UBC, or beginning a new program will be prompted to sign a Letter of Offer through RMS during registration\. This must be completed before your training can begin\. 

## **Returning Residents** 

Returning residents must confirm their contact information annually in the [Resident Management System \(RMS\)](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/resident-management-system-rms/) during the registration period\. Please note that letters of offer are only issued and signed \(electronically\) through RMS at the start of a UBC residency training program\.
