This section is intended for Program Directors, Program Administrators, Faculty and other members of the postgraduate medical education community who are involved in the administration of postgraduate training programs at the University of British Columbia\. Click on any of the **section titles** to learn more, discover further resources, or sign up for events\.

#### [Faculty Appointments](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/faculty-appointments/)

Discover how to become Clinical Faculty at UBC and see PGME job descriptions and appointment policies\.

#### [Report new Faculty and Staff](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/report-new-staff/)

Use the form on this page to report new Program Administrators, Program Directors, or Site Directors\.

#### [Policies and Procedures](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/policies-procedures/)

View PGME policies and procedures, and download forms for expense reimbursement and prescription privileges\.

#### [Quality Improvement and Patient Safety \(QIPS\)](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/quality-improvement-and-patient-safety-qips/)

Learn more about the QIPS curriculum and how this curriculum can be integrated into each residency program\.

#### [**Learning Environment Modules**](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/learning-environment-modules/)

Resources for faulty and residents that aim to address specific adverse and interpersonal relationships that may arise among peers and colleagues\.

#### [**PGME Orientation for Program Administrators**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0rCyJy8FsP8jyKN)

If you’re new to PGME, or even if you’ve been involved for a while, we recommend attending a half\-day orientation\.

#### [Guide for Program Directors](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/guide-for-program-directors/)

This resource provides PDs with information specific to directing a residency program at UBC\.

#### [Competency Based Medical Education](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/competency-based-medical-education/)

Follow this link for more information on CBME at UBC, CBD modules and resources and Entrada user guides\.

#### [Resident Counselling &amp; Peer Support Office](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/rcaps/)

Find resources to help residents manage stress and connect with counselors\. Join workshops and community events like Talent Night\.
