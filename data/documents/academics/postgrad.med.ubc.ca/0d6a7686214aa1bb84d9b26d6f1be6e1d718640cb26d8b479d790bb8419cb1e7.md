The 12th Annual Resident Talent Night took place last week at the Medical Student and Alumni Centre\! A great time was had by all, enjoying some beautiful photos and paintings in the artwork display before watching a large display of talent from residents, alumni, and even the Executive Associate Dean of Education, Dr\. Roger Wong\. Stay tuned for a video showcasing highlights of the evening and details of the winners of the 4th Annual Faculty and Staff Wellness Awards\! Thanks to all who came out to perform, submit artwork, and watch\!
