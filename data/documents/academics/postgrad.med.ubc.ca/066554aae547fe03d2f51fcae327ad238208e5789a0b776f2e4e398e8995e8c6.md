Postgraduate Medical Education \(PGME\) registration for the 2026\-2027 academic year will begin on **April 16, 2026 at 12PM**\. At this time, you will receive an email from PGME with detailed instructions for how to complete registration\.

## New Residents

Resident registration is mandatory; failure to complete registration in a timely manner will result in a delay of the commencement of your training program\. New residents should follow the steps in the [New Residents Registration Guide](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/getting-started-at-ubc/rms-registration/)\.

## Returning Residents

Returning residents are required to update their contact information annually in the [Resident Management System \(RMS\)](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/resident-management-system-rms/) during the registration period\. Please note that letters of offer are term\-based, and are only issued \(and signed electronically\) through RMS at the start of a UBC residency training program\. You will not be required to sign a letter of offer for the 2026\-27 academic year if you signed your letter of offer electronically in RMS in a previous academic year, and are not starting a new program in 2026\-27\.

Returning residents must also remember to login to [Workday](http://myworkday.ubc.ca/) and pay the registration fee once it has been posted by enrollment services\.
