[A new resident getting ready for their training by doing their mandatory online courses\.](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/onlinetraining.jpg)

Make sure to do all your online trainings before your start date\!

Before you start your program you will need to complete a variety of online training courses\. There are some general courses that all residents must complete in addition to health authority and program specific training\. You will be notified of any program specific course requirements be your program\. Make sure to give yourself plenty of time to complete the online training requirements as courses vary in length\.

You will also be required to attend several different orientations in June and early July\. For details please see the pages linked below\.
