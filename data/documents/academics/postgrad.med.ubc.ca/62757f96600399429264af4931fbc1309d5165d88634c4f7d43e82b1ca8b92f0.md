### Welcome from the Associate Dean, Postgraduate Medical Education

On behalf of the Faculty of Medicine, congratulations and welcome to postgraduate training at the University of British Columbia\!

You are now part of a community that includes almost 2000 postgraduate trainees \(residents, fellows, and international trainees\)\. At UBC, we are committed to providing an environment of excellence in education, inclusion, innovation, and scholarship\. Faculty, Program Administrators, Program Directors, and Site Directors are committed to your training and are continuously striving to optimize your experience and medical education\. As part of our overall goal of learning and improvement, we will be seeking your input along the way – we encourage you to provide feedback on what’s working well, challenges and suggestions for change\. The PGME Office team strives to support our trainees, faculty, administrators, and community along the way\.

Read more

At UBC, our training is distributed throughout the province, providing exceptional experiences which range from tertiary and quaternary care, to rural, remote, and indigenous training settings\. Our Family Medicine program has 23 sites along with 76 Royal College residency programs, 15 Royal College accredited fellowship programs, as well as many clinical fellowships\.

Our PGME Team is here because of you and strives to support you and your program during your training\. Familiarize yourself with the resources and information available and don’t hesitate to reach out to connect\.

Sincerely,

**Sonia A\. Butterworth, MD, FRCPSC,**  
Associate Dean, Postgraduate Medical Education  
Clinical Professor, Department of Surgery  
Faculty of Medicine, University of British Columbia

### What to do Next

We know there’s a lot to do before your program begins\. We will do our best to keep this section up to date with tasks you can accomplish now to ensure a smooth transition into residency\. If you want to plan ahead you can also see our [full checklist](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/#checklist)\.

1.  Make sure you have completed your [licensing](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/licensing-medical-liability/educational-licensing/) with CPSBC\.
2.  Report your [immunization record](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/immunizations-health-safety/immunization-reporting/) and attend an [employee immunization clinic](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/immunizations-health-safety/immunization-clinics/)\.
3.  Complete [online training requirements\.](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/orientations-trainings/online-training/) 
4.  Get ready to [attend orientations](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/orientations-trainings/orientations/)\.
5.  Pay [UBC Registration Fee](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/getting-started-at-ubc/registration/#fee) online through Workday by **July 8**\.

#### New or returning to BC?

-   Check out our page with helpful information on [Moving to BC](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/moving-to-bc/)\.
-   Once you’ve arrived in BC, apply for [Medical Services Plan \(MSP\) coverage](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/payroll-benefits-reimbursements/msp-enrolment/)\.

**Note:** the Student Practice Education Core Orientation commonly known as **SPECO is no longer a requirement for new residents**\. The [Resident Doctors OHS/Violence Prevention Training](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/orientations-trainings/online-training/#health) as well as any other HA specific trainings are still required\.
