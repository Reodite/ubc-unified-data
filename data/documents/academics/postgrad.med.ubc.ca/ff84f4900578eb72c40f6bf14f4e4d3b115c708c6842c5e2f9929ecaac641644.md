Before gaining access to electronic health systems used in clinical settings across the province, health authorities in B\.C\. require all trainees complete online training\. It is the responsibility of the trainee to ensure they complete all necessary training requirements in a timely manner to avoid delayed access to health authority systems\. **Trainees only need to complete the training requirements for the Health Authorities they are placed in\.** The health authorities of B\.C\. will communicate with trainees by email\. It is imperative you check your email regularly for instructions and updates in preparation for your time spent training in each of the health authorities\.

**Note:** the Student Practice Education Core Orientation commonly known as **SPECO is no longer a requirement for new residents**\. The [Resident Doctors OHS/Violence Prevention Training](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/orientations-trainings/online-training/#health) as well as any other HA specific trainings are still required\.

#### First Nations Health Authority \(FNHA\)

Please see the [First Nations Health Authority](http://www.fnha.ca/) website\.

#### Fraser Health Authority \(FHA\)

Visit the [Fraser Health Authority \(FHA\)](https://careers.fraserhealth.ca/student-practice-education/resources-for-students/#placement-orientation) website for a step\-by\-step breakdown of FHA training requirements\. You must also complete their  [Privacy &amp; Confidentiality](https://learninghub.phsa.ca/courses/12680) training\.

#### Interior Health Authority \(IHA\)

Please see the [Interior Health](https://www.interiorhealth.ca/careers/students/student-placement) website for more details\. You should also fill out their [Confidentiality Undertaking for Student Placement](https://www.interiorhealth.ca/sites/default/files/PDFS/confidentiality-undertaking-for-student-placement.pdf) form\.

#### Island Health

All Island Health training requirements are organized by the UBC Regional Office\. If you are a new UBC resident, UBC visiting elective resident or out of province visiting elective, you will be contacted by this office\.

All resident doctors working within Island Health facilities are required to review and complete the outlined education on the [Island Health Resident Doctors Onboarding Webpage](https://medicalstaff.islandhealth.ca/onboarding/resident-doctors-onboarding)\.

#### Northern Health Authority \(NHA\)

Please see the [Northern Health Authority](https://www.northernhealth.ca/for-health-professionals/professional-practice) website for more details\. You should also fill out their [NHA Acknowledgement](https://www.northernhealth.ca/sites/northern_health/files/health-professionals/student-practice-education/documents/acknowledgement-of-confidentiality.pdf) form\.

#### Providence Health Care \(PHC\)

Visit the [Providence Health Care \(PHC\)](https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/students-internships/discipline-specific/medicine) website for a step\-by\-step breakdown of PHC training requirements\. You should also complete the [PHC Privacy and Confidentiality Undertaking for Student Placements](https://learninghub.phsa.ca/Courses/22654/phc-privacy-and-confidentiality-undertaking-for-student-placements-form) form\.

#### Provincial Health Services Authority \(PHSA\)

Visit the [Provincial Health Services Authority \(PHSA\)](http://www.phsa.ca/health-professionals/education-development/student-practice-education) website for a step\-by\-step breakdown of PHSA training requirements\. If you have already completed [Resident Doctors OHS/Violence Prevention Training](https://learninghub.phsa.ca/Courses/25906/resident-doctors-ohs-violence-prevention-training), start with [Prepare for Your Practicum](http://www.phsa.ca/health-professionals/education-development/student-practice-education/prepare-for-your-practicum)\.

You should also complete the [PHSA Privacy &amp; Confidentiality](https://learninghub.phsa.ca/Courses/3544/privacy-and-security-101) training\.

#### Vancouver Coastal Health \(VCH\)

Visit the [VCH Student Practice Education](https://www.vch.ca/en/staff-hub/education-development/student-practice-education) website for a step\-by\-step breakdown of VCH training requirements\. You will also need to complete the [VCH Privacy and Confidentiality](https://learninghub.phsa.ca/Courses/11990/vch-privacy-and-confidentiality) course\.

Trainees who have completed PCIS training in the past can contact [pcissecurityvarhs@vch\.ca](mailto:pcissecurityvarhs@vch.ca)\.
