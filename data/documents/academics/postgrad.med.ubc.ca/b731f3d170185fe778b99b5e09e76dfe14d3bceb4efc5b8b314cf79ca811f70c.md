## **Who Pays Me?** 

Residents are employees of the Health Authorities of British Columbia\. Payroll and Benefits are administered by the Provincial Health Services Authority \(PHSA\)\. Pay is shown as “BC Residents Paying Agency” on bank statements\. 

## **When do I Get Paid?** 

Residents are paid every two weeks by direct deposit to the bank account you provide to the PHSA in your new hire package\. For exact dates you can check the [2026 Payroll Calendar](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/payroll-Calendar-2026.pdf) \(pay dates are marked with a “P”\)\. 

## **How do I Access My Paystubs?** 

To access paystubs online, UBC residents must self\-register their VCH employee ID with their health authority network accounts\. Check out this guide for instructions on [how to access pay statements online](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/VCH-Residents-Self-Register-Network-ID-to-Access-Electronic-Pay-Statements-Online.pdf)\. 

## How do I Find My Employee ID?

You can find instructions on how to get your Employee ID on the [Resident Management System](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/resident-management-system-rms/) page under “Finding your Employee ID”\.

## **Where Can I Find More Details on My Pay?**  

Resident pay and benefit eligibility information can be found in the [Resident Collective Agreement](https://residentdoctorsbc.ca/contract-and-benefits/collective-agreement/current-contract) on the RDBC website\. 

## **Where Can I Find More Details on Benefits Available to Me?** 

For more information on the benefits available to UBC residents \(including life and disability insurance\) please either refer to your Pacific Blue Cross account or access the Benefits page on the [Resident Doctors of BC website\.](https://residentdoctorsbc.ca/contract-and-benefits/benefits) 

## **How do I Setup Payroll and Benefits?** 

Make sure you [fill out the new hire forms](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/payroll-benefits-reimbursements/employee-forms/) and submit them to PHSA by the deadline to ensure you get paid correctly and on time\. Also make sure to sign up for the [Medical Services Plan](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/payroll-benefits-reimbursements/msp-enrolment/) when eligible\.
