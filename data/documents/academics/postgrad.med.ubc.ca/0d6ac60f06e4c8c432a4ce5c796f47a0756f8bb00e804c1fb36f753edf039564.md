Postgraduate Medical Education at UBC is synonymous with being distributed\. With sites spread across four geographically distinct regions around the province, no two programs are the same\. This page will provide information on items relating to moving to British Columbia and the resources you may need to flourish\. Due to the distributed nature of PGME at UBC, most information will be general province\-wide\. However, we will update this page regularly as more site\-specific resources are discovered or become available\.

#### Disclaimer: The PGME Office does not endorse any of the below listed websites
