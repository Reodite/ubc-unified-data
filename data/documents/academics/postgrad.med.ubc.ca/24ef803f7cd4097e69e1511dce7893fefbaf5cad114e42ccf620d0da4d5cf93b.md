“Mindfulness is the simple act of recognizing what’s going on inside of ourselves and what’s going on around us, in the present moment and without judgement\. Mindfulness is a tool that helps us manage thoughts and emotions, notice more of what’s happening in a situation, and immerse ourselves in the present moment as it unfolds” \([please see a full description of mindfulness](https://www.heretohelp.bc.ca/wellness-module/wellness-module-11-mindfulness)\)\.

## Mindfulness\-Based Stress Reduction

The “[Mindfulness\-Based Stress Reduction Workbook for Anxiety](https://www.newharbinger.com/9781608829736/)” is available for loan from the [Resident Counselling &amp; Peer Support Library](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/rcaps/resources/lending-library/)\. In addition, you can find a collection of Mindfulness\-Based Stress Reduction Worksheets and audio\-recordings for guided meditation on [their website](https://www.newharbinger.com/9781608829736/) for free\.
