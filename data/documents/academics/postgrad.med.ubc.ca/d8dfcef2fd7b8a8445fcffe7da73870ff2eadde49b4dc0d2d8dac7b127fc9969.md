### Interested in Counselling?

[Book Now\!](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_cGZyUhYBmSI4ww6)

---

Medical training presents a unique set of stressors\. We believe all residents and fellows should have access to private, confidential, non\-judgmental support services to help them navigate through this critical and demanding point in their careers\. Whether you’re navigating the pressures of residency, adjusting to training in Canada, managing relationships, or simply looking for a space to reflect, our counsellors are here to support you\. Our counsellors regularly support physicians from a wide range of educational and international backgrounds, and recognize that each learner’s path into medicine is unique\.

## Individual Counselling

The most common service offered is 1:1 counselling support between a counsellor and a client, focusing on the client’s topics of choice, at their pace, over Zoom or in person\. Individual counselling is open to active residents \(including IMG\), clinical and AFC fellows, and visa trainees\.

### Couples Counselling

Relationship support is available to a resident and their partner and can focus on the challenges of having a relationship in medicine or focus on topics external to residency\. Partners do not need to be residents to access this support\.

### Indigenous Counselling

This support is offered by an Indigenous Elder, Jean Wasegijig, and focuses on supporting residents mentally, physically, spiritually and emotional using traditional tools and teachings through an Indigenous lens\. *All residents* are eligible to access supports with Elder Jean, though Indigenous residents will be prioritized\. [Click here to learn more\!](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/rcaps/indigenous-counselling/)

### Areas We Support:

-   Depression, anxiety, burnout, and stress
-   Adjusting to residency or fellowship
-   Relocating to a new city or country
-   Homesickness and being far from family or support systems
-   Navigating a new healthcare system or workplace culture
-   Cultural adjustment and identity
-   Communication challenges in clinical settings
-   Relationship Concerns \(partnerships, colleagues, family\)
-   Experiences of discrimination, racism, or exclusion at work
-   Grief, life transitions, and uncertainty
-   Imposter syndrome and confidence

### Eligibility

We provide counselling services to all UBC medical residents, subspecialty residents, clinical and AFC fellows, SEAP trainees, and visa trainees\. Please contact [pgme\.rcaps@ubc\.ca](mailto:pgme.rcaps@ubc.ca) if you would like to confirm your eligibility\.

*\*Please note: The RCAPS Office counsellors reserve the right to, at their discretion, discontinue counselling and refer medical learners to the Physician Health Program\. The RCAPS Office is also unable to provide advocacy letters on behalf of medical learners\.*

### Session Maximum

The maximum number of free counselling sessions available to residents is 10 sessions per academic year, starting July 1st and ending June 30th\. Sessions can be used for individual or couples counselling but cannot be used in tandem\. The 10 sessions will renew once the academic year resets\.

### Community Referral Network

[Image](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/rcaps-Community-Referral.png)It is common for residents to see a private counsellor without a core understanding about residency training\. After receiving feedback related to the frustrations of explaining residency, the RCAPS Office trained 25 counsellors across BC who understand the complexities of medical training\. The Community Network counsellors come from varied backgrounds and expertise areas which include; **Black, Indigenous and People of Colour \(BIPOC\) counsellors, neurodivergent, Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder \(ADHD\) /Autism specializations**, and counsellors from the **Two\-Spirit, Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Trans, Queer, Intersex, Asexual \(2SLGBTQIA\+\) community**\. For residents seeking Black counsellors specifically, [click here](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Black-Counsellor-Resources.pdf)\. Our Network has flexible evening and weekend availability to accommodate busy resident schedules\. Download our [Community Referral Network](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/rcaps-Community-Referral-Network-PDF.pdf)\.
