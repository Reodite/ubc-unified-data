Managing your professional income can be a daunting task, whether you are self\-employed or a salaried employee\.  These seminars support your business acumen\.

#### Telemedicine

Our session on virtual care and EMR use can be found here:

[Transition Into Practice Events](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/transition-into-practice/events/)

#### Billing

The following three modules were designed to provide you with an introduction to billing in BC:

[Billing in BC:  Basics for Family Medicine](https://ubccpd.ca/learn/learning-activities/course?eventtemplate=14)

[Billing in BC:  Basics for Specialty Medicine](https://ubccpd.ca/learn/learning-activities/course?eventtemplate=11)

[Billing in BC:  Advanced for Family Medicine](https://ubccpd.ca/learn/learning-activities/course?eventtemplate=9)

An essential resource is the MSP Fee Guide, which is updated regularly\.  The latest information can be found [here](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/msp/physicians/payment-schedules/msc-payment-schedule)\.

Visit the Events page [Transition Into Practice Events](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/transition-into-practice/events/)\.

#### Practice Management

The following module is available from UBC CPD to help you identify key organizations that offer practice management resources for physicians and how to access them\.

[Practice Management](https://ubccpd.ca/learn/learning-activities/course?eventtemplate=42)

In addition, we offer a number of sessions to help you feel confident running your own practice:

-   For help with setting up an office from scratch please join us at Business of Medicine 101\.  This session delves into specific costs and responsibilities\.
-   It can be daunting to negotiate a legal contract\.  For help with this, join us at Contracts 101\.  This session will describe the basics of contractual agreements and terms in medical practice\.

To register, please visit the Events page\.

[Transition Into Practice Events](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/transition-into-practice/events/)

#### Incorporation

Incorporate or not…that is the question\.  This session explains the benefits and difference between self employment and incorporation\.

To register, please visit the Events page\.

[Transition Into Practice Events](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/transition-into-practice/events/)

#### Non Fee\-for\-Service Alternative Payment Plans

The latest information on Non\-Fee for Service opportunities is available on the Doctors of BC website\.  You will need to log\-in as a resident member, once you follow this link to the ‘[New Contract Options](https://www.doctorsofbc.ca/managing-your-practice/compensation/contract-offerings)‘ page\.

The New to Practice Physician Contracts are rolling out across BC\.  They are designed to attract Family Medicine practitioners at this time\.  Our session provides detailed information about this opportunity\.

To register, please visit our Events page\.
