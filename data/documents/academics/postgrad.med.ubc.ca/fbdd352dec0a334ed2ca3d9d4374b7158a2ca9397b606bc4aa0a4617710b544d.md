Whether you’re interested in pursuing a career as a family physician, a child and adolescent psychiatrist or a geriatrician, our postgraduate medical education programs are designed to prepare you for practice in the 21st century\. As a postgraduate medical trainee at UBC, you will be equipped with the tools you need to offer patient\-centered care in inter\-professional settings\. You will also receive close mentorship from some of the top medical researchers and scholars in the world, be awarded opportunities to expand your knowledge and hone your skills, while building connections for life\.
