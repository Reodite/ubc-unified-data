What you need to know about immunizations and Occupational Health &amp; Safety

As you get closer to starting your training, you must self report your immunization status to the Provincial Workplace Health Contact Centre\.  Afterwards, you will be able to attend employee immunization clinics\. Finally, this section features necessary information on Occupational Health &amp; Safety including N95 fit testing, blood and body fluid exposures, exposure to communicable disease or incident, WorkSafe BC Regulations and more\.
