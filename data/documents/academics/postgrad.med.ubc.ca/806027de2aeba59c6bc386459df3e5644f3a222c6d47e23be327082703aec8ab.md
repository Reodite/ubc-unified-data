**[Doctor Icon](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Doctor.png)All residents must obtain a postgraduate educational license** from the [College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC](https://www.cpsbc.ca/registrants/current-registrants/registration-and-licensing/student-and-graduates/postgraduate-residents) \(CPSBC\) prior to the commencement of training\. Until you have received confirmation from CPSBC via email that your license has been issued, you will not be permitted to start your residency program\.

**CPSBC will contact most residents directly** via email regarding the application process when they open licensing applications for new residents on **April 15, 2026**\. However, residents who have already had a full license for independent practice with CPSBC will need to contact CPSBC themselves to request licensing for their residency program\.

If you have not held a full license previously and you have not received any correspondence from CPSBC by the end of April, check your spam/junk mail folders before you contact them via email at [educationalapplications@cpsbc\.ca](mailto:educationalapplications@cpsbc.ca)\. A response may take several days\. **CPSBC have requested that residents not send duplicate emails\.**

**Please complete your entire application process** \(i\.e complete the online application form, make payment of your fees and submit supplementary documentation, if required\) by the deadlines stated in the email from CPSBC\. This will ensure that you are licensed for your residency start date\.

Gathering all required documentation can take some time so we suggest you start as soon as you receive an invitation to apply from CPSBC\.

[License icon](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/License.png)

**For new residents, the CPSBC application deadline is June 4, 2026\.** Some residents may not have their medical diploma [verified](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/licensing-medical-liability/verify-medical-diploma/) in time to have their application submitted by the deadline\. In this case, please apply as soon as you have all the necessary documents\. If you are an IMG resident and do not anticipate that your MD will be available until June, please email [educationalapplications@cpsbc\.ca](mailto:educationalapplications@cpsbc.ca)\.

If you’d like a resident perspective see the [Resident Insider](https://www.ubcresidentinsider.ca/)\.

Address all licensing questions to [educationalapplications@cpsbc\.ca](mailto:educationalapplications@cpsbc.ca)\. A response may take several days\.

## Certificates of Professional Conduct

As part of your licensing application, you may be required to submit a Certificate of Professional Conduct \(CPC\)\.

Original Certificates of Professional Conduct must be submitted from:

-   all jurisdictions in which you have ever been registered or licensed to train in or practise medicine outside of British Columbia, even if you were not clinically active
-   all jurisdictions in which you have practised, or are currently practising, in another regulated profession \(nursing, pharmacy, etc\.\), including British Columbia

CPCs may take some time to obtain so we suggest you start this request process early\. However, please note that **CPCs must be dated within 60 days of your license start date**\.

-   If your program start date is July 1, 2026, the CPC should be dated no earlier than May 2, 2026\.
-   If you are completing IMG Orientation and will need a license starting June 15, 2026, your CPC should be dated no earlier than April 16, 2026\.

Please refer to CPSBC’s [CPC Instructions](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/CPC-instructions-2025-11-06.pdf) for more information\.
