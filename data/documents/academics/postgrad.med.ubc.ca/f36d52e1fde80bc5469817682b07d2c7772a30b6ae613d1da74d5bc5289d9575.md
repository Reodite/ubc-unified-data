UBC PGME is proud to support its residents in participating in research projects during residency training\. The emphasis on research is based on the CanMEDS role of Scholar, which can be demonstrated through the creation and dissemination of knowledge and practices applicable to health\. This page includes resources intended to help residents learn and understand scientific principles, research ethics and methodology, and scholarly writing\.

## [https://postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca/wp\-content/uploads/2026/07/SD\-2026\-Abstracts\.pdf](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/SD-2026-Abstracts.pdf)[Image](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ScholarDayAbstracts2026-1-231x300.png)PGME Scholar Day

PGME is excited to announce the 2nd annual PGME Scholar Day on Wednesday, **October 14, 2026,** an event that highlights the outstanding scholarly work of our postgraduate trainees\. **Open to residents across all PGME programs**, the event will feature resident\-led oral presentations in research, quality improvement \(QI\), and other scholarly activities, with community\-based research projects encouraged\. This is a great opportunity to showcase your work, engage with peers and faculty, and receive valuable feedback\.

[Learn more and submit your abstract\!](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/news/scholar-day-2026-abstracts/)

### Dr\. Ahsen Chaudhry

Dr\. Ahsen Chaudhry recently completed his sub\-specialty residency training in Endocrinology and is now a fellow in the Clinician Investigator Program\. His research focuses on islet transplantation and the emerging area of stem\-cell derived cell therapy for diabetes\. He is the 2024 recipient of the JDRF Canada Clinician Investigator Fellowship and CSEM Fernand Labrie Fellowship Research awards\.
