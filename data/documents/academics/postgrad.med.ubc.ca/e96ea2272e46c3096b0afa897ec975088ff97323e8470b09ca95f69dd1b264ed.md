After a night shift, workers are generally highly fatigued and have and can accumulated a huge amount of sleep dept\. Read ways that [you can help mitigate negative effects of working night shifts](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/319918.php) on your sleep patterns and [how to optimize your daily schedule](https://www.sleepio.com/articles/shiftwork/optimize-your-schedule-for-night-shifts/) to accommodate sleep deprivation from night shifts\.

This [information guide](https://shop.rcplondon.ac.uk/products/working-the-night-shift-preparation-survival-and-recovery?variant=6334287429), designed for junior doctors in the UK, provides helpful tips of action to take before, during, and after a night shift to mitigate the effects of fatigue and sleep dept\.
