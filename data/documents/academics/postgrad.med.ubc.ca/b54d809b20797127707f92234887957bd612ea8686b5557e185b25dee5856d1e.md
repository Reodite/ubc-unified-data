The RCAPS Office is pleased to lend out the books from our Lending Library, accessible to all UBC residents at all locations across the province\.  
To borrow a book:

-   Check the list below for book availability
-   Fill out the form to request a book
-   Book lending periods are four weeks\.

## Request a Book

*\*For Vancouver residents, a pickup time will be arranged\. For residents outside of Vancouver, a courier service will be provided*
