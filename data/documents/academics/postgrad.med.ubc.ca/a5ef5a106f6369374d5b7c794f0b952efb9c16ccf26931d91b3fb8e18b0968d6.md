The [12th Annual Resident Talent Night](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnas5i8-N0M) will take place on Thursday September 26th at the Medical Student and Alumni Centre\.

**Perform at Resident Talent Night**

We are calling all singers, dancers, musicians, poets, comedians, gymnasts, magicians, and more, to perform at Resident Talent Night in front of our warm and supportive audience\. 

Interested residents \(and faculty\!\) can sign up by visiting the Resident Talent Night website at: [https://postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca/resident\-wellness/events/resident\-talent\-night/](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/resident-talent-night/)\. Click on the ‘Sign up to Perform ’ link and submit your information **by Aug\. 23rd**\. The RWO will **confirm your performance by September 2nd\.**

Do you know someone who might love the chance to perform? Forward this email\!

**Showcase Your Art**  

Celebrating the many types of talent in the UBC PGME community, we invite all residents and faculty to submit their art for display at Resident Talent Night\. We are looking for:

\-       Paintings/drawings/illustrations/graphics 

\-       Photography 

\-       Poetry/short stories 

\-       Textiles/sculptures 

To showcase your artwork, visit the Resident Talent Night website at: [https://postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca/resident\-wellness/events/resident\-talent\-night/](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/resident-talent-night/)\. Click on the ‘Submit to the Art Exhibit’ link and submit your information **by Aug\. 23rd**\.  

There will be a physical exhibit and a digital exhibit for those who are unable to drop art off in advance\.
