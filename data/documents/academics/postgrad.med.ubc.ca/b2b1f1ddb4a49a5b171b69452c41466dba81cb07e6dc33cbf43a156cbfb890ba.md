Looking for ways to keep fit during residency? [Fitin30](https://med-fom-pgme.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/05/FitIn30_Corporate-Poster.pdf) is offering a discount on personal training to residents\! Have a look at [other Health and Lifestyle Discount](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/resources/health-lifestyle-corporate-discounts-for-residents/)s available to you as residents\.

Feel free to have a look at the [RWO Community Events page](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/community-events/), which outlines different community fitness activities available in locations around the province\.
