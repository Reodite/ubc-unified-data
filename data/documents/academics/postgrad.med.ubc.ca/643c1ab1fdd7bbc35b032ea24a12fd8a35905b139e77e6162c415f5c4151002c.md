## [Image](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/payroll-1-247x300.png)

In addition to regular salary, residents are paid for other items\. The following is a quick summary of additional payments and how they are processed\. 

## Call Stipends

In order to receive payment for call shifts, residents must submit a Call Stipend form\. Call stipend amounts are stipulated in the Resident Collective Agreement and are processed by Resident Doctors of BC \(RDBC\)\. Call stipend payments are added to your regular paycheque\. See [https://residentdoctorsbc\.ca/contract\-and\-benefits/submitting\-a\-call](https://residentdoctorsbc.ca/contract-and-benefits/submitting-a-call) 

## Statutory Holiday Pay

Residents who work on statutory holidays are compensated per the amounts in the Resident Collective Agreement\. Residents submit stat holiday pay requests through Resident Management System \(RMS\)\. Submissions are approved by the Program Administrators and submitted to VCH for pay via resident paycheques\. See [https://postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca/current\-trainees/resident\-management\-system\-rms/](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/resident-management-system-rms/)  

## Mandatory Training Expense Reimbursements

Residents are reimbursed for a number of training expenses including travel for mandatory distributed rotations, mandatory courses, and mandatory academic events\. Residents submit these reimbursement requests via the Resident Reimbursement System and they are approved by your program and PGME before being reimbursed by PHSA\. See [https://postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca/current\-trainees/resident\-reimbursements/](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/resident-reimbursements/)  

## Resident Activity/Educational Fund and Program Reimbursements

Residency programs reimburse residents for other educational expenses not considered mandatory training expenses\. These expenses are submitted by your Program Administrator through the UBC Workday system and are paid by UBC directly\. Please ensure that you set up your direct deposit in Workday\. See instructions at [https://workday\.students\.ubc\.ca/finances/setting\-up\-your\-direct\-deposit/](https://workday.students.ubc.ca/finances/setting-up-your-direct-deposit/)
