All medical residents are eligible to receive these discounts\. To receive your discount, please bring your current payslip with you as proof of employment as well as your UBC Card \(obtainable through the [UBC Card Office](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/)\)\.

Click below to learn more about each\.

### Chan Centre for Performing Arts

UBC residents receive a 10% discount on all Chan Centre Presents series performances\. Located at 6265 Crescent Road on the UBC Point Grey Campus, the Chan Centre for the Performing Arts has earned an international reputation for its striking design and stellar acoustics\. Artists, critics and patrons alike are unanimous in their praise of this multi\-faceted facility, winning it a place among North America’s premier performing arts centres\. To access this discount visit [www\.chancentre\.com/our\-season](https://chancentre.com/) and choose an event\. Click on Buy Tickets and enter the promotional code: ubcstaff\.

### Float House

Floating is a practice that has many applications such as stress management, chronic pain management, and meditation\. [Float House](http://floathouse.ca/) is pleased to extend a special 25% off their first float to all UBC staff and faculty members towards their service of flotation therapy in either of their 2 Vancouver locations\! To redeem this discount please print the redemption form [UBC Float House](http://www.hr.ubc.ca/health/files/UBC-Float-House.pdf) and follow the guidelines\.

### Richmond Olympic Oval

The Richmond Olympic Oval offers UBC residents one free day pass to their facilities upon presentation of their UBC ID or pay stub\. ### Vancouver Board of Parks &amp; Recreation

UBC residents are entitled to a 25% discount on Park Board flexipasses\. The Park Board flexipass is good at all city\-run swimming pools, ice rinks and 13 different fitness centres\. The Park Board Flexipass is good at Park Board Fitness Centres, but not Association\-run Fitness Centres\. There are 25 fitness facilities in located in Community Centres, but only 14 of these fitness centres are run by the Park Board\. Visit the [Vancouver Parks Board website](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/fitness-passes-and-discount-cards.aspx) to learn more\. In order to sign up for the corporate rate, you will need to show your UBC Card and a copy of your most recent paystub\.

### YWCA Health and Fitness Centre

All students, including postgraduates, have access to our student rate of $58/month\. UBC staff memberships are $61/month\. All members also pay a $50 joiner fee, unless we’re running a promotion\. All members except students also pay a $45 cancellation fee, except under medical circumstances\. Staff and students just need to show proof of employment/enrollment in order to qualify\.

For more information or a free day pass, contact [memberservices@ywcavan\.org](mailto:memberservices@ywcavan.org)\. [www\.ywcahealthandfitness\.com](http://www.ywcahealthandfitness.com/)\.
