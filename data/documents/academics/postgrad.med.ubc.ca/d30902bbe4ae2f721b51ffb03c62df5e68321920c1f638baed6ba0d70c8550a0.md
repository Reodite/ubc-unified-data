Residents are required to self\-report their immunization status to the Provincial Workplace Health Contact Centre \(PWHCC\)\. **You can self\-report starting June 1, 2026**\. Please do not self\-report prior, as your employment record will not have been created yet and you will be asked to self\-report later\.

Residents can self\-report their immunization history to the PWHCC  via one of the three methods below:

-   **Vaccination online self\-reporting tool [Health Care Worker Self\-Reporting \(healthcarebc\.ca\)](https://healthandsafety.healthcarebc.ca/)**
-   By phone – 1\-866\-922\-9464 select Option 1

**Note:** when reporting through the PWHCC Self\-Reporting Tool, all residents should select Vancouver Coastal Health as their Health Authority employer, regardless of training location\. Please review the [step\-by\-step guide](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Vaccination-status-self-reporting-tool-guide-for-employees.pdf) for completing the online self\-reporting tool\.

You MUST be physically located in British Columbia \(BC\) to call the PWHCC\. The phone number is toll free and usable in BC only \(1\-866\-922\-9464\)\. Please have all previous immunization records ready before talking to the nursing team at the PWHCC\.

If you have any questions about immunizations, please contact the PWHCC directly at [HH@WHCallCentre\.ca](mailto:HH@WHCallCentre.ca)\.

After you complete self\-reporting, your records will be reviewed by the nursing team at the PWHCC and uploaded into the VCH employee immunization record system\. This process can take a few days\.

Residents with outstanding immunizations will receive an email with information on how to access their Vaccine and Immunity Assessment Summary after completing self\-reporting of their immunization status, along with our VCH staff immunization clinic schedule\.

## Vaccine Documentation Required for PWHCC

Residents are required to self\-report the status of their current immunizations\. If you do not have all vaccines listed below, you will receive immunization recommendations from the PWHCC nursing team as needed\.

### **Measles/Mumps/Rubella**

All residents born on or after 1957 require 2 documented doses of a measles\-containing vaccine, or laboratory evidence of immunity\.

All residents born between 1957 and 1969 require 1 documented dose of a mumps\-containing vaccine\. Proof of 2 documented doses of a mumps\-containing vaccine is required for those born on or after 1970\.

All residents require 1 documented dose of a rubella containing vaccine, or laboratory evidence of immunity\.

### Hepatitis B

Completion of a documented 3\-dose series of hepatitis B vaccine and laboratory\-confirmed immunity are accepted as proof of immunity\.

### Varicella \(Chickenpox\)

Two documented doses of varicella vaccine or laboratory\-confirmed varicella, are accepted as proof of immunity\. A self\-report varicella is adequate only if the disease occurred before 2004\.

### Polio

An adult booster dose of the polio vaccine is recommended for health care workers who are at risk of exposure to feces\.

### Tetanus/Diphtheria

Booster doses are recommended every 10 years\. One adult dose of a pertussis\-containing vaccine \(Tdap\) is also recommended\.

### Historical TB Skin Testing records

Baselien TB skin testing on hire is not required\.

Previous TB skin test results and/or TB chest x\-ray reports are not required to be submitted\. Records will be stored if provided\.

#### Residents working at Island Health \(IH\) sites:

Baseline TB screening is required unless residents have previously completed TB skin testing in BC and meet all of the following criteria:

-   Negative test
-   Have not traveled to any TB endemic countries since their test
-   Have not been exposed to TB since there test
-   Currently do not have any active TB symptoms and have never been told that they have TB in their lifetime
-   Have documentation of their TB skin test and results

For clarification or questions, please contact the IH nursing team [EHN\-OHS@islandhealth\.ca](mailto:EHN-OHS@islandhealth.ca) If baseline TB skin testing is advised, the test can be completed either through the IH nursing team after arrival on the island, or at a VCH employee immunization clinic in Vancouver\.
