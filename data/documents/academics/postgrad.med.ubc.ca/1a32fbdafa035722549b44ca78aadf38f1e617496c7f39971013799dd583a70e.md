Get your forms done promptly to ensure a smooth onboarding\!

In this section we will give you information on setting up payroll and benefits\. In addition, you will also learn about reimbursements and where to find your tax forms\. 

First thing to know, residents are employees of the Health Authorities of British Columbia\. Payroll and Benefits are administered by Provincial Health Services Authority \(PHSA\) on behalf of Vancouver Coastal Health \(VCH\) who acts as the paymaster on behalf of all Health Authorities\. UBC Postgraduate Medical Education informs the resident employer of updates to residents’ records that affect pay and benefits\. All UBC residents employed by the Health Authorities are members of the Resident Doctors of BC \(RDBC\) professional association and are covered under the [Collective Agreement](https://residentdoctorsbc.ca/contract-and-benefits/collective-agreement/current-contract)\.  

For more in\-depth information on what you need to know and do before your program starts please explore the pages below\.
