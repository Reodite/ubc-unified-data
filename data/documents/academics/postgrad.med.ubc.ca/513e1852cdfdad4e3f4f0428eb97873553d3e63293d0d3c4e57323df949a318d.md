### Looking for Counselling?

[Book Now\!](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_cGZyUhYBmSI4ww6)

The **Resident Counselling and Peer Support \(RCAPS\) Office** \(formerly known as the Resident Wellness Office\) is committed to supporting all residents to achieve their best possible state of well\-being through private and confidential support services for Residents and their partners\. We know that innate systemic issues act as barriers to physicians being the best version of themselves, especially during their residency\. In acknowledging how the medical system makes it difficult for doctors to engage in self\-care, the RCAPS Office works to empower Residents to engage in taking care of their mental, emotional, spiritual and physical well\-being by facilitating a community of care amongst the Resident body\.
