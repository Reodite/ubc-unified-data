We are very pleased to announce the recipients of the 4th Annual [UBC Postgraduate Faculty and Staff Wellness Award](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/pgme-faculty-staff-wellness-award/) that was presented at the 12th Annual [Resident Talent Night](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/resident-talent-night/) that took place on September 26th, 2019\.

The *UBC Postgraduate Faculty and Staff Wellness Award* honours faculty and staff members in Postgraduate Medical Education who demonstrate leadership in resident wellness\. All residents across the province were invited to nominate a staff or faculty member for the award\. This award recognizes faculty and staff members at any site across the province who:

–          display commitment to promoting and advocating for a healthy residency experience;

–          consider all aspects of a resident’s well\-being;

–          lead by example and model wellness in daily life;

–          take a leadership role in influencing the culture of wellness in their department/program\.

Out of an overwhelming number of nominations, four winners were selected, and we are delighted to congratulate the individuals below for receiving this significant recognition\.

**Dr\. Claire Sheldon, Program Director Ophthamology**

**Dr\. Tracy Scott, Program Director General Surgery**

**Dr\. Ahmer Karimuddin, Program Director General Surgery**

**Aleah Ross, Senior Program Assistant, Victoria Psychiatry** 

To learn more about the Faculty and Staff Wellness Award and to read what residents had to say about these outstanding individuals, please **[click here](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/pgme-faculty-staff-wellness-award/)\.** 

Please join us in congratulating all who were nominated for this award and in celebrating those who continue to support resident wellness across the province\!
