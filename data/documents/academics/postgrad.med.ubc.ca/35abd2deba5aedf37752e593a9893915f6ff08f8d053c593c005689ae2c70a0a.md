**Tuesday Seminar Series**

-   Tuesday evenings from 6:00 – 7:00 PM \(unless otherwise noted\)\.
-   Covering a wide range of non\-medical topics\.
-   Residents of all levels and programs are welcome to attend via Zoom\.
-   Registration is required\. Repeat attendance is welcomed\.
-   Sessions are not recorded\.

**Academic Half Days**

-   TIP offers Academic Half Days each year in the Fall and Spring\.
-   The content includes the core curriculum and is refreshed annually\.
-   Next scheduled dates are May 7th and 8th, 2026\.
-   Residents should obtain their program approval to attend these\.

**September 1, 2026 6:00\-7:00 PM, PST**

[**Negotiated benefits for all BC Physicians**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_bJV9OFlwDxIMc6y)

An overview of BC government paid benefits available to all MSP billing physicians \(including service contract, sessional, and fee\-for\-service\)\.  Benefits include CRPSP, CME, CMPA, REAP, PDI\.  Discusses how to access or apply for these valuable benefits\.

**September 15, 2026 6:00\-7:00 PM, PST**

**[The LFP Payment Model 101: How to get started](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_78ylkPAF4neyBro)**

Learn about the LFP Payment Model as a compensation option for longitudinal family physicians and locums\.

**September 22, 2026 6:00\-7:00 PM, PST**

[**Money Management Foundations**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_806UeztXS8PFINM)

This session will cover the foundational money management basics\. Focus areas include:  Getting your financial house in order, how to keep more of what you earn, how to reduce taxes, debt management, budgeting, and managing cash flow\.

**September 29, 2026 6:00\-7:00 PM, PST**

[**Make PathwaysBC your go to resource for optimized referrals and so much more**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_ePeeAGc7ZHgZIXQ)

A guided tour to Pathways’ online directory\.  Learn how to streamline referrals, access specialist/specialty clinic info \(e\.g\., current wait times, sub\-expertise, pre\-consultation blood work, and imaging requirements\) and search patient and physician resources\.

**October 6, 2026 6:00\-7:00 PM, PST**

**[Insurance Planning](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_1zThcHVyAXYGS34)**

A general overview of personal insurance; how to prioritize personal insurance depending on practice type and/or family situation\.  Review of Disability Insurance in particular and its importance in a physician’s career\.

**October 20, 2026 6:00\-7:00 PM, PST**

[**Incorporate or not…that is the question**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_72JJsiftRPucRJc)

What it means to incorporate, what groundwork is required and what are the advantages\.  Review of a few examples with discussion of advantages of different structures\. Review the recent changes to medical corporation taxation in Canada\.

**October 27, 2026 6:00\-7:00 PM, PST**

[**HR success:  How to think like a boss\!**](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3t9d6JEZVwuhI3A)

Transitioning into a busy practice is often a major adjustment for many post graduates\.  In this session we will look at the following topics and HR best practices that will assist you with the next phase in your career\.

-   Appropriate workplace conduct and professional boundaries
-   Respectful workplaces/anti\-bullying and harassment
-   Managing staff; having difficult conversations
-   Dealing with performance related issues
-   Employment laws and how they impact the workplace

The objective of this session is to provide UBC post graduates with the fundamental tools to navigate within a practice where they will have responsibility and, in some cases the authority to manage and supervise others\.
