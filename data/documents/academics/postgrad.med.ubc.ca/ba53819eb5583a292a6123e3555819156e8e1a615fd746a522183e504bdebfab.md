The first issue of *The ITP Letter* is here\.

At RCAPS, we recognize that internationally trained physicians often navigate unique challenges alongside the demands of residency\. To help foster connection, reflection, and wellbeing, we’ve launched *The ITP Letter,* a newsletter designed specifically for IMG residents\.

In our first issue, *Home Is Where the Heart Is*, we explore homesickness, belonging, and the emotional realities of training far from familiar people, places, and routines\. We also highlight practical coping strategies and available supports for residents who may be struggling quietly\.

We invite you to take a few minutes to read, reflect, and remember that support is available at every stage of training\. ❤️

The first issue is available below\. Happy reading\! 📖
