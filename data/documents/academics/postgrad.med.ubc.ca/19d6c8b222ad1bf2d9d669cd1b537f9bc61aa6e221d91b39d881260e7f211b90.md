### Indigenous Counselling for All\!

Using traditional Indigenous tools and perspectives, Elder Jean provides a unique approach to medicine, focusing on counselling, support and the wisdom of Elder teachings\.

### Main Benefits:

[Image](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/ElderJean-photo3.jpg)

-   Cultural Connection &amp; Safety
-   Holistic Healing
-   Spiritual Support
-   Empowerment of Identity
-   Mental Clarity &amp; Coping Skills

In sessions, Elder Jean often incorporates the medicine wheel, a symbol of health and healing used by generations of Indigenous communities across Turtle Island\. This powerful tool aids both Indigenous and non\-Indigenous residents in exploring the physical, mental, spiritual and emotional side of wellness\. Elder Jean also offers group sessions known as Elder Circles\. These gatherings provide a safe space for residents to share, connect, and discuss group concerns or wellness topics, fostering a sense of community and mutual support\.

### Eligibility

*All residents* are eligible to access supports with Elder Jean, though Indigenous residents will be prioritized\. Elder Jean is available Mondays &amp; Fridays from 9am\-3pm at the Gordon &amp; Leslie Diamond Health Care Centre or on Zoom\.

### Curious About Indigenous Counselling?

Whether you’re interested in exploring your identity or seeking to enhance your professional development, our Indigenous Counselling service offers a unique experience\. Join Elder Jean to deepen your understanding of wellness and culture in the context of medicine through; traditional knowledge and practices, holistic approaches, patient\-centered care, mentorship and guidance and cultural competence\.
