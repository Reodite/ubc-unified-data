Once you have an employee ID and have [self\-reported your immunization status](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-resources/resident-registration-guide/immunizations-health-safety/immunization-reporting/) online or by phone to the Provincial Workplace Health Contact Centre \(PWHCC\), you will receive an email with information on how to access your Vaccine and Immunity Assessment Summary, along with the VCH Employee Immunization Clinic Schedule\.

## Appointment Registration

Residents within the lower mainland can book appointments for clinic visits through the [Jane App](https://vchpeoplesafety.janeapp.com/) starting June 8, 2026\. You can schedule your appointments on or after your start date in July\.

You can view the [VCH Employee Immunization Clinic Schedule](https://one.vch.ca/working-here/safety/communicable-disease-prevention/baseline-health-immunization-assessments) on the VCH intranet using your employee ID and a work email\.

If you are offsite, sign in to the VCH intranet using your userID\\email and password \(i\.e\., Domain\\Username: VCH\\JSmit h\)\. An employee ID is required before visiting VCH Immunization Clinics\.

Residents outside the lower mainland who are unable to attend any of the listed clinics may be eligible for reimbursement\. ***Pre\-approval is required\.***

For reimbursement inquiries, please contact the VCH Field Services Nursing team at [safetyandpreventionfsn@vch\.ca](mailto:safetyandpreventionfsn@vch.ca)\.
