Registration now open for this year’s **Practice Survival Skills Conference: What I Wish I Knew in My First Years of Practice taking place Saturday, June 12\.**  Join for practical tips, virtual exhibit &amp; job fair\! 

**[Practice Survival Skills Conference: What I Wish I Knew in My First Years of Practice](https://ubccpd.ca/course/practice-survival-skills-2021)**

**Jun 12** \(Sat\) \| 0900–1400 PDT  [\+Add to calendar](https://ubccpd.ca/ical/913/event.ics)

**Target Audience:** physicians, family practice residents, specialty residents, locums, IMGs, and physicians new to BC\.

**Up to 4\.5** Mainpro\+/MOC Section 1 credits\.

#### [**Course Webpage**](https://ubccpd.ca/course/practice-survival-skills-2021) **\|** [**Agenda**](https://ubccpd.ca/course/practice-survival-skills-2021#agenda) **\|** [**Registration Form**](https://ubccpd.arlo.co/register?sgid=8938b61a225842139e1b9ab6403b01f5)
