[Resident Talent Night](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/resident-talent-night/), the UBC Resident Wellness Office’s most anticipated event of the year, will be taking place on **Thurs\. Sept\. 26**\! Resident Talent Night celebrates the resident community’s many talents beyond medicine, including musicians, spoken word poets, singer\-songwriters, dancers, martial artists, magicians, acrobats, comedians, and more\. It’s also a chance to showcase resident photography, painting and drawing submissions through an art exhibit\. 

All residents across all programs are invited to attend this **free** event showcasing the incredible talents of your colleagues and peers\. It’s a great reason to plan a social with your colleagues, either by attending as a group or by tuning into the live webcast, and for those at distributed sites who will have academic activities in Vancouver scheduled around Sept\. 26, we encourage you to join us if you can\.  

**12** **\[th\]** **Annual Resident Talent Night**   
Thurs\. Sept\. 26, 2019, 6:3 0\-9:00pm  

MSAC Building, 2750 Heather St\., Vancouver 

FREE of cost   
Friends and family are welcome\!   
RSVP at: [https://postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca/resident\-wellness/events/resident\-talent\-night](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/resident-talent-night)/ *\(RSVP is optional and will help us with planning numbers\)* 

**Perform at Resident Talent Night**

We are calling all singers, dancers, musicians, poets, comedians, gymnasts, magicians, and more, to perform at Resident Talent Night in front of our warm and supportive audience\. 

Interested residents \(and faculty\!\) can sign u p by visiting the Resident Talent Night website at: [https://postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca/resident\-wellness/events/talent\-night/perform\-at\-resident\-talent\-night/](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/talent-night/perform-at-resident-talent-night/)\. Click on the ‘Sign up to Perform ’ link and submit your information **by Aug\. 23rd**\. The RWO will **confirm your performance by September 2nd\.**

Do you know someone who might love the chance to perform? Forward this email\!

**Showcas** **e Your Art**  

Celebrating the many types of talent in the UBC PGME community, we invite all residents and faculty to submit their art for display at Resident Talent Night\. We are looking for:

\-       Paintings/drawings/illustrations/graphics 

\-       Photography 

\-       Poetry/short stories 

\-       Textiles/sculptures 

To showcase your artwork, 

visit the Resident Talent Night website at: [https://postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca/resident\-wellness/events/resident\-talent\-night/](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/talent-night/)\. Click on the ‘Submit to the Art Exhibit’ link and submit your information **by Aug\. 23rd**\.  

There will be a physical exhibit and a digital exhibit 

for those who are unable to drop art off in advance\.    

**Distributed Sites**   

There are a number of ways to participate in Resident Talent Night no matter where you are located\! 

**1\.    Submit a video performance** 

Grab your phone or webcam and record a video of your talent\. We’ll showcase all video submissions at the event\! 

Email your video submission to [resident\.wellness@ubc\.ca](mailto:resident.wellness@ubc.ca) **by Aug\. 23rd**

**2\.    Submit your art digitally** 

We’ll have a digital art exhibit displaying artwork up on the big screen\. Fill out the ‘Artists’ form at [https://postgrad\.med\.ubc\.ca/resident\-wellness/events/talent\-night/submit\-artwork\-to\-display\-at\-resident\-talent\-night/](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/resident-wellness/events/talent-night/submit-artwork-to-display-at-resident-talent-night/) by **by Aug\. 23rd**   

**3\.    Watch the li** **ve webcast** 

Resident Talent Night will be webcast live from the MSAC, so you can watch it anywhere on any device \(no videoconference necessary\!\)\. A great excuse to get together with your colleagues and make a night of it – more details to come in September\. 

Check out this [one\-minute highlight video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnas5i8-N0M) to get a feel for what it’s all about\! 

Please email [resident\.wellness@ubc\.ca](mailto:resident.wellness@ubc.ca) with any questions, and we look forward to seeing you on Sept\. 26th\!
