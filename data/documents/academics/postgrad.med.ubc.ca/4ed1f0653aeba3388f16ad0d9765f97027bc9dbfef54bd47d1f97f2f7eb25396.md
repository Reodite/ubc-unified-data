[A verified medical diploma](https://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/verify-diploma.png)

The College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC requires that medical degrees be verified through [physiciansapply\.ca](https://physiciansapply.ca/)\. This process can take a significant amount of time, especially for IMGs\. IMGs should submit their medical diploma ASAP via [physiciansapply\.ca](https://physiciansapply.ca/) for [source verification](https://mcc.ca/credentials-and-services/services/verification-of-credentials/)\. Canadian medical schools will submit medical diplomas for CMGs automatically, but they should log in to [physiciansapply\.ca](https://physiciansapply.ca/) in May or June to confirm verification has been completed\.

If you’d like a resident perspective on this process see the [Resident Insider](https://www.ubcresidentinsider.ca/)\.
