## Biography

Dr\. Michael Davies served as a senior advisor for Teck Resources, Canada’s largest diversified mining company\. Michael joined Teck in early 2012 as Vice\-President, Environment and, in late 2017, transitioned as part of a phased retirement to a senior advisory role\. He retired from Teck in 2022\. During his time as a senior advisor, his responsibilities included assisting with Teck’s technical pursuits, providing internal review, leading Teck’s tailings governance program, guiding research and development related to mine waste, and supporting talent development\.

  
In addition to his corporate mining experience, prior to joining Teck, Michael had over 25 years of consulting experience\. Over his combined career, he worked on all stages of mining projects, from advanced exploration through decommissioning and reclamation\. He worked on more than 100 tailings facilities and contributed to and/or facilitated more than 30 multidisciplinary risk assessments for mining and other projects\. In addition to tailings projects, he has extensive experience in mine waste management, soil mechanics, geomechanics, environmental permitting and management, mine water management, and engineering geology\. He has also served on several review boards\.

  
In addition to his professional industry activities, Michael has always had a passion for talent development and education\. Starting with teaching assistant assignments in the 1980s, he has provided lectures at universities around the world, including many at UBC beginning in 1992\. He has also been an invited speaker at student events in many countries and has served as a review member for several master’s and doctoral thesis candidates in North America, Australia, and Chile\.
