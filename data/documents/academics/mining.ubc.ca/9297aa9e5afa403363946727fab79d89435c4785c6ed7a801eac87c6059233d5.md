Each year, we hire a limited number of Teaching Assistants \(TAs\) to support our undergraduate and graduate courses\. TAs play an essential role in helping us deliver high‑quality learning experiences and hands‑on education\.

## About TA Positions

All undergraduate and graduate students, including non‑research students, are welcome to apply\.

TAs at UBC are members of [CUPE 2278 \(Component 1\)](https://cupe2278.ca/), and all TA appointments follow the CUPE 2278–UBC Collective Agreement\. Union membership is a condition of employment, and salaries are set according to CUPE and UBC rates\.

We are committed to equity, diversity, and inclusion\. We strongly encourage applications from individuals who identify with groups underrepresented or marginalized under the B\.C\. Human Rights Code\.

## Application Timeline

-   **Applications open:** Tuesday, March 31, 2026
-   **Priority deadline:** April 30, 2026, at 11:59pm  
    Applications submitted after this date may be considered only if additional capacity remains\.

Current CUPE 2278 members \(Component 1\) should apply by this date to be considered preferred candidates\.

Apply using the link at the bottom of this page\.

## Eligibility Requirements

To apply, you must:

-   Be a UBC student \(undergraduate or graduate\) or a recent UBC graduate \(within 8 months\)\.
-   Be eligible to work in Canada and reside in British Columbia for the duration of the appointment\.
-   Be available for onsite duties, including labs, tutorials, and lectures\.
-   Meet workload limits and safety/training requirements\.
-   Complete the Faculty of Applied Science \(APSC\) TA training program\.

Full details are available in the accordions below\.

### Full Eligibility Requirements

### Registration

-   Current UBC students or recent graduates \(within 8 months\) are eligible\.
-   Applicants must be registered or unconditionally accepted into a UBC program for the term of the TA appointment\.
-   Students from other programs may be considered based on experience and suitability\.

### Workload Limits

Because Winter TA applications open before course schedules and final enrolments are confirmed, the number of hours assigned to each course can vary from year to year\. Some courses—especially low‑enrolment graduate courses—may receive fewer hours or may not receive a TA at all\.  
At the Norman B\. Keevil Institute of Mining Engineering, TA workload is typically:

-   \~64 hours per term for a full TA appointment
-   \~32 hours per term for marker‑only positions

*\(Actual hours may vary depending on course needs and final enrolment\.\)*

Under the CUPE 2278 Collective Agreement, the maximum allowable workload is:

-   Up to 192 hours per term
-   Up to 384 hours total in the Winter Session \(Sept–Apr\)
-   Up to 192 hours during the Summer term \(May–Aug\)

Students may hold more than one part‑time TA position across UBC, provided their combined total hours remain within these CUPE 2278 limits\.

### Work Eligibility &amp; Location

-   Must reside in British Columbia for the duration of the appointment\.
-   Must be legally eligible to work in Canada and provide documentation \(e\.g\., study permit \+ SIN\)\.

International students without a study permit before the term begins must decline the offer\.

### On‑Site Availability

-   Must be able to attend lectures, labs, tutorials, meetings, and on‑campus duties\.

If you will be away from BC for a significant portion of the term, please do not accept a TA assignment\.

### Conflicts &amp; Other Employment

-   Must disclose any other TA positions or responsibilities before accepting an offer\.
-   Must ensure combined hours do not exceed limits set in the Collective Agreement\.
-   Cannot hold a TA position while enrolled in the same course\. 

### Offer Acceptance &amp; Onboarding

-   Offers must be accepted or declined within 10 business days\.
-   All Workday HR onboarding tasks must be completed once the appointment is initiated\.
-   New TAs and returning TAs who have not participated in the Faculty of Applied Science \(APSC\) TA training program must complete the training, as it is designed to help ensure that TAs are successful in their roles\. If you receive a TA position, information on this training will be provided to you \(typically within the first few weeks of the term\)\.

### Employment Equity

Equity and diversity are essential to academic excellence\. An open and diverse community fosters the inclusion of voices that have been underrepresented or discouraged\. We encourage applications from members of groups that have been marginalized on any grounds enumerated under the B\.C\. Human Rights Code, including sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, racialization, disability, political belief, religion, marital or family status, age, and/or status as a First Nation, Metis, Inuit, or Indigenous person\.

### Safety

Safety is a core expectation in the Norman B\. Keevil Institute of Mining Engineering\. All Teaching Assistants must complete the department’s required safety training before participating in any teaching activities, including labs, demonstrations, or fieldwork\.

TA appointments are contingent on completing all required training\.

Required training may include:  
•    Mining Department Safety Orientation  
•    Laboratory‑specific safety instructions \(as applicable\)  
•    Any additional training identified by the course instructor or the Safety Officer

Safety requirements, listed under "Paid Student Workers including TAs and RAs" can be found on the MINE Safety Online Workshop through Canvas\.

Please contact the Safety team here: [safety@mining\.ubc\.ca](mailto:safety@mining.ubc.ca) to organize in\-person orientations\.

### Required Competencies

Teaching Assistant applicants will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

-   Demonstrate strong understanding and mastery of Mining Engineering course material
-   Support active student learning and explain concepts clearly and logically
-   Lead discussions, tutorials, or lab activities in ways that facilitate student engagement and understanding
-   Evaluate student work constructively, including assignments, presentations, and lab work
-   Apply and model strong academic and professional skills such as communication, teamwork, problem‑solving, critical thinking, numeracy, leadership, and planning
-   Adhere to all UBC, WorkSafeBC, and departmental safety policies and procedures
-   Communicate effectively through excellent written and oral English

### Supervisor Approval

Students in research‑based programs must obtain approval from their Research or Program Supervisor before accepting a TA position\.

Supervisors must confirm:

1.  That the student is permitted to accept employment alongside academic responsibilities
2.  That total hours \(including multiple TA positions, if applicable\) are appropriate

### Types of TA Duties

**Laboratory Assistance**

-   Setting up materials
-   Supervising experiments
-   Maintaining lab safety and equipment
-   Guiding students through lab activities

**Tutorial Assistance**

-   Leading or assisting in tutorials
-   Preparing and reviewing solutions
-   Supporting student understanding of course concepts

**Course Administration**

-   Attending lectures, labs, and tutorials
-   Managing learning resources in\-person and on Canvas
-   Responding to student questions
-   Holding office hours

**Curriculum Development \(Senior TAs only\)**

-   Assisting with creation or revision of course materials
-   Engaging in pedagogical improvement
-   Supporting or mentoring other TAs

**Marking**

-   Grading assignments, exams, quizzes, and lab reports
-   Entering grades accurately

**Invigilation \(Mandatory\)**

-   All TAs must be available for the entire exam period
-   You may be assigned to invigilate courses other than the course you TA

**Additional Training**

Completing any required instructor‑assigned training

### Pay Rates &amp; TA Classifications

All rates are set by the [CUPE 2278 Collective Agreement:](https://www.cupe2278.ca/collective-agreement-basics)

-   **Senior TA \(STA\):** $42\.95/hr
-   **Graduate TA 1 \(GTA1\):** $40\.16/hr
-   **Graduate TA 2 \(GTA2\):** $38\.65/hr
-   **Undergraduate TA 1 \(UTA1\):** $26\.40/hr
-   **Undergraduate TA 2 \(UTA2\):** $24\.44/hr
-   **Exam Invigilator:** $24\.06/hr
-   **Senior Exam Invigilator:** $28\.91/hr

Brief classification descriptions remain exactly as in your original document\.

*Please note these rates are subject to change\. The figures listed above were last confirmed on March 2, 2026\.*

### Understanding TA Preference \(CUPE 2278\)

Under the CUPE 2278 Collective Agreement:

-   Previously appointed TAs receive hiring preference for a fixed number of teaching years\.
-   New applicants are considered after all preferred candidates have been reviewed\.
-   Apply before the deadline to be considered in the preference review\.

Full agreement: [https://hr\.ubc\.ca/sites/default/files/documents/UBC\_CUPE\_2278\_Collective\_Agreement\_2022\-2025\.pdf](https://hr.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/UBC_CUPE_2278_Collective_Agreement_2022-2025.pdf)

Not all applicants will receive a position\.
