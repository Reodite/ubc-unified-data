Adjunct Professor, Sessional Lecturer \- MINE 470/559

## Biography

A mining and mineral exploration professional with over 25 years of experience and proven success in leading development of social and environmental license for resource projects\. An executive team member with experience in recruitment and development of project teams for mineral project advancement\. A Registered Professional Biologist focused on connecting the concerns and interests of potentially affected communities with the objectives of resource development proponents\. An excellent communicator with a self reliant and resourceful work style, capable of analyzing complex issues quickly and devising strategies for resolution and advancement of project objectives\. National and international experience in managing multi\-disciplinary teams to achieve regulatory and social licence objectives for project advancement\.

UBC is located on the traditional, ancestral and unceded territories of the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm people \(Musqueam; which means 'People of the River Grass'\) and Syilx Okanagan Nation\. The land has always been a place of learning for the Musqueam and Syilx peoples, who for millennia have passed on their culture, history and traditions from one generation to the next\.
