## Graduate Student Seminar

All graduate students are required to self\-enroll in the **Canvas Course MINE 598/698 Seminar\.**

[Enroll in MINE 598/698 Seminar Canvas course](https://canvas.ubc.ca/enroll/MNCLHP "Canvas Seminar for Graduate Students")

### WorkDay Enrolment

#### MEng Students

Seminar is not required for MEng Students **but** students should only register in the course MINE 598 when they plan to present\.

#### MASc Students

Students **must** maintain continuous registration in the graduate seminar through Workday by enrolling in MINE 598\.

#### PhD Students

Students **must** maintain continuous registration in the graduate seminar through Workday by enrolling in MINE 698\.
