## Biography 

Ali has a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from McGill University, Canada\. He also had been a postdoctoral fellow at Earth/Mine Energy Research Group \(EMERG\) of McGill University for 3 years\. Since 2001, he has been actively involved in the fields of mine\-mechanical systems, mine energy systems, thermo\-fluids, energy systems, computational fluid dynamics and heat transfer modeling as well as automation and control\. His main research interests are mine\-mechanical systems, mine energy systems, energy conservation, and renewable energies\.
