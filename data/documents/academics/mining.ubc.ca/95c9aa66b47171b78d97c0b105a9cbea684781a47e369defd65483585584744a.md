## Intellectually challenging, global in scope, and interdisciplinary in content\.

Image

[A man working in the lab\.](https://mining.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/original_image/public/2025-05/mine-graduate-studies-banner-960x330px.png.webp?itok=HCfO7SAM)

[**Catch up on our most recent information session from November 20, 2025\.**](https://mining.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/ubc-mining-grad-programs-nov-2025.pdf "Information session slides from November 20, 2025")

The Norman B\. Keevil Institute of Mining Engineering offers excellent courses and conducts cutting\-edge research across a spectrum of disciplines related to the mining industry\.

Our graduate students come from across the globe and exhibit diverse backgrounds; civil, electrical, and mechanical engineering, material sciences, as well as disciplines such as geology and commerce are all represented\.  
  
Graduate students at UBC can expect to work with leading scientists in state\-of\-the\-art facilities and at international field sites\.

[Mining engineer using laptop inside a vehicle\.](https://mining.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2025-05/graduate-meng-banner.png.webp?h=4c1fc98e&amp;itok=bp9MyeRW)

### [Master of Engineering Degree](https://mining.ubc.ca/graduate/master-of-engineering-meng-program)

A globally oriented program offering technical depth and professional development which akin to an MBA\.

[Maria Holuszko with a grad student\.](https://mining.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2025-05/graduate-card-banner-research_0.png.webp?h=4c1fc98e&amp;itok=TZig4gcA)

### [Master of Applied Science and Doctor of Philosophy Degrees](https://mining.ubc.ca/graduate/masc-and-phd-degrees)

An advanced research training across mining, processing, environment, and social impact\.

[A man monitors multiple control screens in an industrial control room\.](https://mining.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2025-05/graduate-card-banner-certificate.png.webp?h=4c1fc98e&amp;itok=oOM841sK)

### [Graduate Certificate in Global Mine Waste Management](https://mining.ubc.ca/graduate/graduate-certificate-in-global-mine-waste-management)

A focused program preparing professionals for the 21st century\.

[Students seated in a classroom using laptops and taking notes\.](https://mining.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/card_focal_point_sm/public/card-images/2025-05/54142475376_bd3f96625f_c.jpg.webp?h=c9f93661&amp;itok=UyacT8zB)

### [Graduate Financial Aid &amp; Scholarships](https://mining.ubc.ca/graduate/graduate-financial-aid-scholarships)

Funding options that recognize achievement and help meet your financial needs\.
