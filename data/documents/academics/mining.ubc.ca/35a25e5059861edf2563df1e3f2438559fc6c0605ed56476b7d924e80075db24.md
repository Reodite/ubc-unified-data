Adjunct Professor

## Biography

Engaged engineering expert in complex problem solving, critical thinking and creativity\. Executes with the mantra “if it can not be measured, it can not be improved” \(Lord Kelvin\)\. Passionate about harnessing the inventive creativity of the whole organisation to help drive process design and operational improvements that offer both economic and environmental benefits through stakeholder engagement, multi\-disciplinary teams and group design activities\.

Expertise in fields including: wear and its modelling and analysis, stirred and tumbling mill performance, digitization with a focus on sensor development, energy efficiency and recovery, innovation process evaluation and improvement, as well as technology vetting and evaluation\.

UBC is located on the traditional, ancestral and unceded territories of the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm people \(Musqueam; which means 'People of the River Grass'\) and Syilx Okanagan Nation\. The land has always been a place of learning for the Musqueam and Syilx peoples, who for millennia have passed on their culture, history and traditions from one generation to the next\.
