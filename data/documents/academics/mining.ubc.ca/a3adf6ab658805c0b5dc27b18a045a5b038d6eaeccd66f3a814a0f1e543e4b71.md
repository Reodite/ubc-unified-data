## Why Work With Us

Our department is at the forefront of mining education and research\. We are committed to sustainability, technological innovation, and global partnerships that drive positive change across the mining sector\. UBC is consistently ranked among the top universities worldwide, and our team benefits from:

-   Access to world\-class research facilities and industry networks
-   An inclusive, collaborative academic environment
-   A vibrant campus in one of the world’s most livable cities

## Student &amp; Research Roles

UBC Mining supports student hiring through co\-op placements, work\-learn positions, and research assistantships\. Interested students can:

-   Check the [UBC Work Learn Program](https://students.ubc.ca/career/ubc-experiences/work-learn-program/) for on\-campus roles
-   Explore [APSC Co\-op Opportunities](https://coop.apsc.ubc.ca/)
-   Contact our faculty directly for potential undergraduate or graduate research positions
