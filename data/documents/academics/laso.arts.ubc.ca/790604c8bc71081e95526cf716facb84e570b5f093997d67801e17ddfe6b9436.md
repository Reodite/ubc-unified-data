##### 1\. Are there perks for UBC alumni?

Yes\! Check out the list of UBC Alumni’s [**benefits and services**](http://www.alumni.ubc.ca/services/) including career resources and discounts on travel, events, products and attractions\.

These offers not only save you money and keep you connected, but part of the proceeds goes to support alumni and student programs\.

##### 2\. How do I find out more about department events?

Visit our [**Events Listing**](https://laso.arts.ubc.ca/events/) or email us at \[ insert [**placeholder\-name@ubc\.ca**](mailto:placeholder@ubc.ca) \] for more information\.

To learn about UBC\-wide events, check out the [***alumni UBC***](http://www.alumni.ubc.ca/events/calendar/) listing\.

##### 3\. How can I add my name to the department newsletter mailing list?

Contact \[insert name\] at \[insert phone number\] or by email at \[insert e\-mail address\] to join the department newsletter mailing list\.

##### 4\. How do I remove my name from an email or publication mailing list?

To update your UBC subscriber listings, please email UBC Alumni Records at [**alumni\.records@ubc\.ca**](mailto:alumni.records@ubc.ca)\. Within the body of your email, please state your first and last name, degree and what subscription you would like removed from your alumni profile\.

##### 5\. I’ve heard that alumni have access to a free UBC e\-mail forwarding service\. Where do I sign up?

You can sign up for our free email forwarding service [**here**](http://www.alumni.ubc.ca/services/email-forwarding/)\.

##### 6\. What is an Alumni A\-Card?

The Alumni A\-Card, which is free for all UBC alumni, gives you access to discounts on a host of attractions, services, events and much more\. [**Sign up for your Alumni A\-Card**](http://www.alumni.ubc.ca/services/acard/) today\!

Don’t see your question here? Visit the UBC Alumni Affairs website for [**more FAQs**](http://www.alumni.ubc.ca/about/faq/)\.
