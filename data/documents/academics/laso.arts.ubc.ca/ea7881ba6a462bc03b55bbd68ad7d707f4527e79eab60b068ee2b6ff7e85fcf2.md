In addition to the core course and other coursework in the Minor, students and faculty in the program will be invited to participate in the on\-going Law and Society speaker series\.  The speaker series, co\-chaired by Professor [Margot Young](http://www.allard.ubc.ca/faculty-staff/margot-young) \(Law\) and Dr\.[Renisa Mawani](http://www.soci.ubc.ca/index.php?id=11343), features speakers from UBC as well as prominent scholars in Law and Society from around the world\.  In 2007\-2008 the series featured 22 events\.   As appropriate and schedule permitting, guest speakers on campus will be invited to meet with students and/or speak with classes in the Minor program\.
