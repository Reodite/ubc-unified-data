An information session and networking event for LASO minors and prospective minors will be held on November 17th, 2021, at 5PM, in Michael Kingsmill Forum in the AMS Nest\.  The Law and Society Chats Club and the LASO Director will be there to answer questions and talk about Law and Society at UBC\.
