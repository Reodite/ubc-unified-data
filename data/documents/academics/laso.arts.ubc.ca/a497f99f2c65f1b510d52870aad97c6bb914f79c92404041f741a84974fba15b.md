The Law and Society Minor is a multidisciplinary program, situating the study of legal structures, rules and
institutions within their social, historical, economic, and political contexts. The Minor comprises LASO 204 and at
least 27 and no more than 39 credits from courses on the LASO planning worksheet or specifically approved by
the LASO advisor.
Students undertaking a Minor in Law & Society must consult with the Law and Society Advisor, complete the
advising form, and bring it to the Law and Society Advisor by the term in which they are registered in LASO 204. If
you declare the LASO Minor and have not been able to register in LASO 204 or are in third year, please
complete and return this form to the advisor as soon as possible.
