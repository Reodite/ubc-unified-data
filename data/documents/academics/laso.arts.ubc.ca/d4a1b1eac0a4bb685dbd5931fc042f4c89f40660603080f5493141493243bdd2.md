As part of the University’s **[*start an evolution*](https://startanevolution.ubc.ca/category/projects-by-faculty/faculty-of-arts/)** campaign, the Department of Sociology invites alumni and friends to support thinking that can change the world\.

At UBC, we generate ideas that improve and inspire\. You can help start an evolution through involvement and investment\. This can be as simple as **[volunteering your time](http://www.arts.ubc.ca/community-and-alumni/volunteer/)** or as generous as **[making a donation](https://www.donate.startanevolution.ca/ea-action/action?ea.client.id=1730&amp;ea.campaign.id=16736&amp;Direct%20my%20Gift%20to%20Module=S862)**\. You can have a lasting impact on generations of students and scholars\.

To learn about different **[ways to give](http://startanevolution.ubc.ca/ways-to-give/)** or how you can get involved, please contact:
