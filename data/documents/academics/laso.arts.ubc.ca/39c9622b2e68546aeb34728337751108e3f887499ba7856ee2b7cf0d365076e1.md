#### [About Law and Society](https://laso.arts.ubc.ca/why-is-there-a-law-and-society-minor/)

In recent years, scholars from many fields and academic institutions have grown increasingly interested in the place of law in social, political, economic and cultural life\. Scholars, teachers, and practitioners in law, sociology, political science, psychology, anthropology, economics, and history as well as in other related areas, have turned their attention to the study of socio\-legal phenomena\. [Read More](https://laso.arts.ubc.ca/why-is-there-a-law-and-society-minor/ "Read More")

#### [LASO 204: Introduction to Law and Society](https://laso.arts.ubc.ca/why-is-there-a-law-and-society-minor/law-and-society-core-course-laso-204/#new_tab)

All students in the Law and Society Minor must complete the 200\-level core course, Introduction to Law and Society \(LASO 204\)\. This course will be taught by current members of the Faculty of Arts from a variety of disciplines\. [Read More](https://laso.arts.ubc.ca/why-is-there-a-law-and-society-minor/law-and-society-core-course-laso-204/#new_tab "Read More")

#### [LASO at UBC](https://laso.arts.ubc.ca/examining-social-structure-and-social-change/)

By taking relevant courses in a wide range of disciplines, students study legal structures and institutions within their full social, historical, economic, and political contexts in British Columbia, Canada, and around the world\.   [Read More](https://laso.arts.ubc.ca/examining-social-structure-and-social-change/ "Read More")
