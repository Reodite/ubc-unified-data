# First Prof Talk of 2018\!

Come join us on March 8th at 5:30pm at Mahonys &amp; Sons  
with **Dr\. Arlene Sindelar\!** 

Not only will there be a stimulating discussion, but there will also  
be beer and nachos to go around\!

Professor Sindelar is the advisor for both the History and Medieval Studies Major and Minor programs, as well as the chair and advisor for the Law and Society minor program\.  
With over 15 conference presentations and a wide range of research interests spanning from the history of women and the family to medieval English common law and legal profession, this prof talk is sure to be one you won’t forget\!

UBC Law and Society Chats: ***Rule of Law or Rule by Law: What does it matter?***
