Dr\. Sally Choi is a vascular surgeon at Richmond Hospital\. She moved from South Korea to Vancouver as a child and completed her medical school and residency training at the University of British Columbia\. Dr\. Choi is highly trained in a wide range of core and complex open and endovascular procedures, with broad clinical interests spanning the full spectrum of arterial and venous disease\. 

She has been involved in multiple research projects, including work on management of thoracic and abdominal aortic pathology, surgical education, and rare case reports\.

Dr\. Choi loves living on the West Coast and is excited to continue her practice in the city and serve the local community\. Outside of work, she enjoys staying active, discovering new restaurants, and exploring new countries\.
