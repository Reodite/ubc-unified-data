Another year of studies is coming swiftly to a close, big congratulations on making it to the end\! Behind the scenes, we have been hard at work preparing for the next school year — and we’d love your feedback to write the next chapter of the Media Studies story\.

Please join us on April 2, 2026, at 12:30 – 1:30pm, in Buchanan B141, to contribute ideas of how the BMS can better cultivate its fabulous community, and lend your voice to the conversation\.

Come with your BMS community hopes and dreams, and leave with a fresh energy to carry you through the summer\.

Snacks and refreshments will be served\! We would love to see you all, but unfortunately this focus group facilitation has a capacity of 48\.

Please RSVP below quickly to claim your spot\.

##### Notice: The event will be photographed, please indicate if you are not comfortable with your image being used on a UBC website, social media, or promotional material\.
