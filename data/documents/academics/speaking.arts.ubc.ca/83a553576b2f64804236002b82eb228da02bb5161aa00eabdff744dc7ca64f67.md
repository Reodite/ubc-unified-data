On this page, we have collected further resources to help you develop your familiarity and comfort with scholarly speaking\. The links below share opportunities to locate more recorded presentations — and opportunities to orally present your own work, too\. [Contact us](https://speaking.arts.ubc.ca/contact/) if you know about other links we should add\!

### [Watch: M\-DEA Talk](https://mediastudies.arts.ubc.ca/news/m-dea-talk-2022-recap/)

Watch recorded presentations from “M\-DEA Talk: UBC Bachelor of Media Studies \[BMS\] Public Speaking Competition\.” On this page, you can see five BMS student speaker 2022 finalists present 4\-6 minute speeches on the topic of, ***“***What should Media mean to us?”

### [Present: MURC](https://students.ubc.ca/career/events-workshops/multidisciplinary-undergraduate-research-conference)

Interested in sharing your own research? Check out MURC, UBC’s Multidisciplinary Undergraduate Research Conference, and find out about opportunities to present your scholarly work live\.
