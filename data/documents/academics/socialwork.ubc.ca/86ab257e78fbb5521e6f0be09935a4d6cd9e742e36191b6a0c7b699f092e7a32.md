### Workshop Description:

In a context of increasing discrimination and violence against 2S/LGBTQ\+ communities—particularly in schools—this workshop invites field instructors to reflect on the roles of helping professionals in responding to homophobia and transantagonism\. What responsibilities do we hold, and how can we act in meaningful, supportive ways? Grounded in empowerment and anti\-oppressive theories, this session will explore how these frameworks can inform our practice and strengthen our capacity to foster inclusive, affirming environments for queer individuals\.

Our presenter, Antoine Coulombe, brings deep experience from his early work in Quebec, where he led initiatives to combat discrimination in schools, support queer youth, and shape progressive government policies\. Now at UBC, Antoine’s teaching and scholarship focus on how schools of social work can better prepare practitioners to work effectively and compassionately with 2S/LGBTQ\+ populations\.
