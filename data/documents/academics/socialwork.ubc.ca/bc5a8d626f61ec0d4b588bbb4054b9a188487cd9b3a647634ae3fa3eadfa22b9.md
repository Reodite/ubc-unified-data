**Project Summary**

The project was aimed to decolonize and Indigenize social work education within the UBC School of Social Work, specifically: 1\) the Bachelor of Social Work \(BSW\) undergraduate curriculum, and 2\) Field education\.

**Phase 1:**

**Information Gathering and Relationship Building**

-   Syllabus review and faculty survey
-   Environmental scan of other Schools of Social Work in Canada
-   Literature review of international models of decolonizing and Indigenizing Schools of Social Work
-   Field education team piloted “Seven Promising Principles for Decolonizing Field Education” framework

**Indigenous Community Engagement**

-   8 focus groups with 25 Indigenous participants including Elders and knowledge keepers, alumni, former Indigenous faculty, and field instructors
-   Hosted two Indigenous Social Service Agency Fairs to build relationships with community, and to provide the opportunity for students to learn about and network with Indigenous\-focused services

**Phase 2: Develop and Implement Curricular Resources and Field Sites**

-   Curated free and publicly accessible teaching and learning resources on an Open Educational Website [https://indigenous\-resurgence\.arts\.ubc\.ca/](https://indigenous-resurgence.arts.ubc.ca/)
    -   84 journal articles or book chapters
    -   51 Indigenous films 54 Indigenous textbooks
    -   29 Indigenous focused reports/legislation
    -   6 inquires
    -   19 memories/novels
-   Developed a Teaching Guide for faculty
-   First Elder co\-supervisor for field site

**Phase 3: Knowledge Mobilization and Evaluation**

-   Teaching and Learning presentation to UBC School of Social Work Faculty, April 2024
-   UBC Decolonizing and Indigenizing Health Professional Education Community of Practice Circle presentation, May 2024
-   CASWE National Social Work Conference presentation, June 2024
-   S’tenistolw International Indigenous Post Secondary Education Conference, August 2024

Evaluation included regular meetings with the Indigenous Advisory Circle and project team, and further impact evaluation is ongoing\.

**Results**

This Project was led by Indigenous Peoples throughout, including the School’s Indigenous Advisory Circle, Indigenous knowledge keepers, faculty, students and community members\. The following are some outputs of this project:

-   Circular environmental assessment
-   Compilation of curricular resources
-   Creation of Teaching Guide for faculty
-   Guidelines for implementing the Seven Promising Principles framework for Indigenizing and decolonizing field education
-   Increased network of Indigenous field instructors and practice sites
-   New policies supporting Indigenous learning in field

Aligned with ISP Goal \#1 \(1\), Goal \#2 \(8\), Goal \#4 \(15, 16\), Goal \#7 \(32\)

**Continuing Steps**

-   Annual Indigenous Social Service Agency Fair with wider reach
-   The MSW program renewal will be guided by lessons learned and will incorporate curated resources
-   Departmental Circle of Practice
-   Ongoing development of Guide for respectful engagement with Elders
