The time since the last *Bridge* has been full of fun research, teaching and service work\. I particularly enjoyed international field work and conference presentations with my wonderful research colleagues\. This year included data collection in Taiwan, keynotes or conference presentations in Chile, Taiwan, Sydney and Dunedin \(New Zealand\), as well as domestic presentations for the Canadian Association for Works and Labour Studies \(Hamilton\) and the Canadian Association of Gerontology Studies \(Montreal\)\. We are currently wrapping up a three\-country study on emancipatory dialogue between Indigenous and anti\-oppressive social work \(Baines, PI\), which is extending our knowledge and theory, and generating promising findings and innovative recommendations for strengthening practice and policy\.

I am also part of international research teams that have been looking at harm reduction and supportive housing for older people who use drugs, and poverty among older, undocumented Asian women \(Tamara Daly, PI\), as well as an additional research project on mental health social workers in the context of neoliberalism \(Catrina Brown, PI\)\. It is such a privilege to work with such extraordinary researchers and students\.
