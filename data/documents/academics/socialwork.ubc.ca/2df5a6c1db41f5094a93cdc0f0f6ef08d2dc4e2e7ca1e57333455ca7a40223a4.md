School of Social Work students at Shandong University

In July 2025, a UBC delegation participated in the International Summer University in Social Work \(ISUSW\), a two\-week academic program that brings together students and faculty from around the world to deepen knowledge, strengthen global partnerships, and learn from diverse social work contexts\.

Representing the UBC School of Social Work were Dr\. Barbara Lee and MSW student representatives Emma McIlroy and Kyle Turone, who joined participants from nine partner universities for an immersive experience hosted by Shandong University \(SDU\) in Jinan, China\. ISUSW is a long\-standing international consortium committed to advancing social work education through cross\-cultural and interdisciplinary engagement\. Each year, the program centres on a specific theme\. The 2025 edition focused on “Inter\-disciplinarity and Social Work,” exploring how social work collaborates across applied disciplines to address complex social issues\.

Dr\. Lee delivered a workshop titled “Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization Across Applied Disciplines: Highlighting Social Work’s Distinct Practices,” contributing to scholarly dialogue on the role of knowledge exchange in social work and across related fields\.

For MSW students Emma and Kyle, ISUSW offered a unique opportunity to engage with peers from across the globe, participate in interdisciplinary seminars, and gain insight into how social work is practiced and taught in different cultural and political contexts\. Their participation highlighted the leadership, curiosity, and global citizenship that UBC students bring to international academic spaces\.

The 2025 ISUSW cohort strengthened existing collaborations and sparked new partnerships among participating universities\. For UBC, the experience continues to build pathways for future academic exchanges, collaborative research, and enhanced global learning opportunities for students\. Kyle and Emma represented the School with professionalism, insight, and enthusiasm by contributing to discussions, engaging in intercultural learning, and reflecting the School’s commitment to preparing graduates who are globally informed and socially responsive\.

## About Shandong University

The host institution, Shandong University, is one of China’s oldest and most prestigious universities, recognized under the country’s Double First\-Class University Plan\. Shandong University is located in Jinan, the capital city of Shandong Province and a region known for its philosophical heritage as the birthplace of Confucius\.

Throughout the two\-week program, the UBC delegates had the opportunity to:

-   Participate in lectures and workshops led by international faculty\.
-   Engage in group projects with students from universities across Europe, Asia, Australia, and North America\.
-   Visit local community centres and neighbourhoods to learn about local social services delivery and provisions\.
-   Explore cultural and historical sites, including an overnight trip to Mount Tai, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and visits to Confucius’s birthplace and family memorial grounds
-   These experiences offered a deeper understanding of how local histories, cultural values, and social policies shape social work practices in China\.
