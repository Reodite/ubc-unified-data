**Dean of Arts Mentorship Award**

Image

This year, Dr\. Barbara Lee, received the UBC Dean of Arts Mentorship Award in recognition of her mentorship with undergraduate and graduate students\. This award reflects her ongoing dedication to creating supportive and inclusive learning environments that nurture student growth and scholarly development\.

**Perspectives Publication**

Dr\. Barbara Lee and KX Team: Michelle O’Kane, Cathy Jiu, Qian, Zhou, and Maddie Cathcart, co\-authored an article on knowledge exchange and mobilization in social work in the BCASW Perspectives magazine\. This work highlights social work’s disciplinary contributions to advocacy, social change, and UBC’s growing capacity for Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization \(KxM\)\.

Lee, B\., O’Kane, M\., Jiu, C\., Zhou, Q\., &amp; Cathcart, M\.\(2025\)\. *Knowledge Exchange and Mobilization in Social Work: A Strategy for Advocacy and Social Change*\. BCASW *Perspectives Newsmagazine*\.
