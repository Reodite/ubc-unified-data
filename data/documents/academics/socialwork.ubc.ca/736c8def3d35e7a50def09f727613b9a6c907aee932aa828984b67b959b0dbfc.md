### Protection of Privacy

Please note that the UBC School of Social Work adheres to Canadian Anti\-Spam Law \(CASL\), which requires us to obtain consent before sending Commercial Electronic Messages to our mailing list\.

By filling out this form, you are providing the School of Social Work at the University of British Columbia with your preferred valid email address, which will be used to sign you up to the School of Social Work’s mailing list\. Your email address will be provided to a third party, [Envoke](https://envoke.com/), which will send the newsletter to you on behalf of the School of Social Work\. Your personal information is collected under the authority of section 26\(c\) of the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act \(FIPPA\)\. Envoke will store your email address on its server, located in Canada\.

If you have questions about the collection, use, and disclosure of this information, please [contact us](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/about/contact/)\.
