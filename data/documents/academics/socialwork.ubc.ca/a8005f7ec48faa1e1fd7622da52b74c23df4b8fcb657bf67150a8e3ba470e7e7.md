> Build your existing knowledges through study and research, develop your skills in field education practicums, and make valuable connections with faculty and fellow students at the UBC School of Social Work in Vancouver, British Columbia\.

Building upon a foundation of social justice and an ethic of care, the Advanced Master of Social Work \(MSW\) and PhD in Social Work prepares you for teaching, research, and administration in social work theory and practice\.

## Advanced Master of Social Work \(MSW\)

The Advanced MSW offers an advanced\-year curriculum focusing on the fields of child and welfare, health and social inclusion, and international and social development\.

Discover our Advanced MSW program *arrow\_right\_alt*

[Source link](https://socialwork.ubc.ca/graduate/masters-program/)

## Advanced Master of Social Work \(MSW\)

The Advanced MSW offers an advanced\-year curriculum focusing on the fields of child and welfare, health and social inclusion, and international and social development\.

## Doctor of Philosophy \(PhD\) in Social Work

The PhD in Social Work provides students an opportunity for advanced scholarship and professional growth in the context of a research\-intensive program in social work, social policy, and social development\.
