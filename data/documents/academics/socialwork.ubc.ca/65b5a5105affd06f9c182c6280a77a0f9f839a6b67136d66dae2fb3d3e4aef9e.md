### Event Details:

The Field Education team at the UBC School of Social Work would like to invite you to save the date for our annual Agency Fair event designed for our current 3rd year students to help prepare them as they think ahead to their 4th year placements, which will happen for them in January 2027\. 

When:  **Monday March 30\[th\], 2026 from 2:30 pm to 4:00pm**  \(students will arrive at 3 pm\)

Where:  UBC Campus – **Jack Bell Building, 2080 West Mall** \(Come see our new building\!\)

Bring:  Pamphlets or other materials to display and hand out

We would like to invite you to bring pamphlets or any other materials, and we will set you up with a table so that students can approach you to ask questions about the agency services and practicum opportunities\.  This is an excellent opportunity for students to make connections and gather information about the places where social workers may work in the community\.

Light refreshments, coffee/tea and juice will be provided\. 

If you have any questions, please contact Josh at [FieldEd\.Assistant@ubc\.ca](mailto:FieldEd.Assistant@ubc.ca)\. Thank you, and thanks again for your support of our students\!
