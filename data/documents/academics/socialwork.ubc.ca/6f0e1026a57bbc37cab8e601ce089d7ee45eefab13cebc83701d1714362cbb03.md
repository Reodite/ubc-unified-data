### UBC Micro\-certificate in Mental Health and Substance Use

Dr\. Benoit\-Antoine Bacon, President and Vice\-Chancellor of UBC, shares a statement about the new UBC Micro\-certificate in Mental Health and Substance Use, a self\-paced online course for developing the skills required for working with individuals who struggle with mental health and substance use challenges\.

[Source link](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/programs-credentials/mental-health-substance-use-level-1-microcertificate)

> Based on a commitment to fundamental social work values and a focus on social justice and an ethic of care, the School of Social Work at the University of British Columbia prepares you for generalist and advanced professional practice at undergraduate and masters levels\. Our PhD program is a research degree that prepares students for academic positions and for research and leadership roles in government and non\-profit organizations\.
