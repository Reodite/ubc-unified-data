> Change the world through the power of the arts\.

Your vision of a healthier, more sustainable world is possible\. In the face of humanity’s most critical and complex issues, UBC Arts students, researchers and alumni are asking the hard questions\. They’re diving deep and sharing evidence\-based ideas and equitable solutions for a better future\.

What happens here can change everything\. Your support is the catalyst\. Join the School of Social Work in advancing healthy lives, a healthy planet and healthy societies\.

## Featured Initiatives

### School of Social Work Community Learning Fund

The School of Social Work Community Learning Fund was developed to expand access to education that strengthens care, builds capacity and uplifts our communities\. This new fund subsidizes tuition for our Continuing Education Programs, a direct response to address an identified need in the community\. **A featured cause for UBC Giving Day 2026, we raised $15,209\!**

School of Social Work Community Learning Fund

*launch*

[Source link](https://give.ubc.ca/social-work-community-learning)

### School of Social Work Bursary Fund

Consider supporting the development of trained professionals in Social Services\. Donations help students reduce financial barriers in obtaining their BSW or MSW credentials\.  

School of Social Work Bursary Fund

*launch*

[Source link](https://donate.support.ubc.ca/page/18682/donate/1?transaction.dirgift=School+of+Social+Work+Bursary+Fund%20C743)

### Richard Splane Fund for Aboriginal Social Work

This fund supports the School of Social Work to better prepare students to work with Aboriginal people by addressing the Truth and Reconciliation Commission’s recommendations on social work policies, practice, and education\. Funds donated to this initiative directly support the School of Social Work’s [Indigenous Resurgence Project](https://indigenous-resurgence.arts.ubc.ca/)\.  

Richard Splane Fund for Aboriginal Social Work

*launch*

[Source link](https://donate.support.ubc.ca/page/18682/donate/1?transaction.dirgift=Richard+Splane+Fund+for+Aboriginal+Social+Work+Programs%20G2165)

### Mary Hill Scholarship in Social Work

The estate of Professor Emerita Mary Hill, a pioneer in the field of social gerontology, has made an annual scholarship available to Social Work students studying in the area of gerontology\. Support this field by donating today\.  

Mary Hill Scholarship in Social Work
