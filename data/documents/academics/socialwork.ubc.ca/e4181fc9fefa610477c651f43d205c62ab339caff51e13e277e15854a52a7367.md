Hannah is currently finalizing knowledge mobilization activities associated primarily with one project she has been leading in recent years\. This project, which is funded by a SSHRC Partnership Engage Grant and is borne out of a collaboration with Egale Canada, has focused on examining the healthcare and social service experiences, issues, and needs of transgender and gender\-diverse \(TGD\) older adults in Canada\. After she and her team published a flagship scholarly manuscript to highlight the findings of this study in the [*Journal of Applied Gerontology,*](https://doi.org/10.1177/07334648241295570) she supported one of her students, K\.A\. Kenney, to lead a community\-facing webinar with Egale, which can be found [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OU6VqJKOKBw)\. Another scholarly manuscript, which draws on the findings of the study to conceptualize historically\-informed praxis with TGD older adults, is currently under review, and several presentations associated with this work are being planned, including one at the 2026 World Conference on Qualitative Research\.

Beyond the above project, over the last year, Hannah has led and supported a variety of projects and scholarly outputs addressing the dynamic – and often volatile – context of 2S/LGBTQ\+ health\.
