### Workshop Details:

**Session Overview:** This session for UBC Social Work Field Instructors will highlight current research and clinical trends related to compassion fatigue, burnout, and compassion satisfaction among social workers, and will explore practical strategies to mitigate fatigue and burnout while fostering compassion satisfaction within the  instructor\-student relationship\.

**Learning Outcomes:** By the end of this interactive session, participants should be able to:

1.  Describe and recognize compassion fatigue, burnout, and compassion satisfaction as they relate to social work practice
    
2.  Identify risks and protective factors associated with compassion fatigue and burnout among social workers
    
3.  Deepen recognition of early warning signs of compassion fatigue and burnout personally and among practicum students
    
4.  Apply supportive, relational, and ethical strategies to respond to identified concerns and promote student well\-being
    
5.  Reflect on individual and collective strategies for strengthening compassion satisfaction, professional resilience, and well\-being in the supervisory role
    

**Presenter:** Dr\. Rae Morris \(she/her\) is a Clinical Social Worker and Director of Rae Morris Counselling Group, a group practice offering neuro\-affirming counselling, consultation, and education for youth, adults, families, and community providers across Canada\. Rae has facilitated professional development workshops for diverse audiences on topics including neurodiversity, and burnout, compassion fatigue, and compassion satisfaction in caring roles\. In addition to her clinical roles, Rae is a post\-doctoral researcher with UBC\-Okanagan Canadian Institute for Inclusion and Citizenship\. [www\.raemorriscounselling\.com](http://www.raemorriscounselling.com/ "http://www.raemorriscounselling.com/")
