At the UBC School of Social Work, we are committed to ongoing learning and continued professional development, as well as to empowering practitioners with the knowledge and skills to improve and expand their practice\. To that end, we have created our new and growing continuing education program\.

On this site, you will find our current, upcoming, and past continuing education courses, as well as descriptions of the knowledgeable and experienced instructors that develop and deliver them\.

It is our intention that every continuing education course offers an opportunity for the development of applied practice skills, through engaging instruction and experiential learning\.

As we grow, we plan to offer a wide range of learning opportunities that will help you achieve your professional learning goals, so please make sure to check back regularly for new offerings\!
