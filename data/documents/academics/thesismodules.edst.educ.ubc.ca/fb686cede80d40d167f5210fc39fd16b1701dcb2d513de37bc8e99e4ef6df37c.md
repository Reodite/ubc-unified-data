**Module Instructors:** Dr\. Claudia Ruitenberg; Dr\. Tom Sork; Dr\. Anthony Pare; Dr\. Deirdre Kelly; Dr\. Shauna Butterwick

This module is designed to provide resources for various stages of the dissertation writing process\. Enrol in this module early in your degree and come back to it as you move to new stages of writing\. In this module you will find various faculty members discussing the proposal structure, literature review, writing processes and strategies, and ethics \(BREB\) amongst other tips and resources\.
