We will host a series of virtual \(zoom webinar\) seminars this term with guest speakers from around the world\. The focus is on how materials processing, manufacturing, and end of life can affect our ambitions for achieving “Net Zero” and what tools we can use to assess change\.

---

**Seminars January – April, 2022** – Seminars will be \~ 30\-40 min length with 10\-15 minutes for questions/discussion afterwords\. The link for the zoom webinars is given below
