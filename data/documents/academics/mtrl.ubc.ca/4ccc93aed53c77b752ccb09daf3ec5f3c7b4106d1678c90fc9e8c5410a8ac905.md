Have questions about our graduate programs? Visit our FAQs\!

Admissions are administered by the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies \(G\+PS\) at UBC\. Please visit [their website](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/application-admission) for detailed information on eligibility, admission requirements, and application procedures\.

Yes, absolutely – we welcome applicants from a wide range of disciplines\. Many of our current students hold degrees in related fields such as Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Physics, Chemistry, and others\. In fact, around 40% of our admission offers over the last four years have gone to students with degrees outside of Materials Engineering\.

To apply for a Master’s degree \(MASc/MSc\), you must have completed or expect to complete a Bachelor’s degree in a relevant discipline\. While both the MASc and MSc are the same program i\.e\., sharing the same admission and program requirements – applicants with an engineering background \(BASc holders\) should apply for the MASc, whereas those with a science/art background \(BSc holders\) should apply for the MSc\.

To apply for a PhD, you must hold a Master’s degree in a relevant discipline\.

In some exceptional cases, well qualified MASc and MSc candidates may apply for transfer to a PhD program after one year of residency at UBC and the completion of 9 credits of coursework \(at the 500\-level or above\) with an overall 85% average and clear evidence of research ability\.
