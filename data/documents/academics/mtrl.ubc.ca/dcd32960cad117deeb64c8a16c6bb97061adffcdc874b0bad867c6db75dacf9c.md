**SESSIONAL LECTURER FOR:**

**MTRL 497F 101**

Materials Engineering

**GENERAL INFORMATION**

The Faculty of Applied Science at the University of British Columbia, Vancouver campus invites applications for a part\-time sessional lecturer to teach MTRL 497F 101 Fibres, Textiles and Wearable Technologies fall term one \(September 1 to December 31, 2026\)\.  MTRL 497F 101 is a 3\-credit course offered within the Faculty of Applied Science by the Materials Engineering\. It offered as a Technical Elective for Engineering students\. This course in the Materials Engineering \(MTRL\) Program will focus mainly on the basic properties of fibers will be examined, including how they are made \(solution, melt and electro spinning\) and how they are structured \(weaving, knitting, braiding\) to create textiles used in clothing, aircraft, automobiles, and medical devices, as well as being integrated with electronics to create smart wearable devices\. The course is a combination of lectures, demonstrations, laboratories and industry presentations\.

**JOB DESCRIPTION**

The position involves preparing and giving a one\-hour lecture two times per week and a two\-hour tutorial each week during the first term for one section\. The course runs from September 8 to December 7, 2026\. The instructor will prepare tutorials, assignments, mid\-term exams, and final exam\. It is the responsibility of the instructor to maintain an interactive learning environment in which all students are involved\. This typically involves posing thought\-provoking questions to students as well as answering questions posed by students\. The instructor will need to be available to students outside of normal classroom hours to answer questions\. The instructor is responsible to maintain records of grades and to submit final grades in a timely fashion to the Department office\.

The salary is $2873\.68 per credit\.

**QUALIFICATIONS**

Applicants should have a degree in Engineering with experience in Materials Engineering and proven evidence of teaching effectiveness\. Experience in teaching is an asset\.

Salary will be commensurate with qualifications and experience
