### Research and Teaching Interests

Composite Materials and Manufacturing; Industry 4\.0/5\.0 and Smart Manufacturing; Sustainable and Human\-Centric Manufacturing Systems; Engineering Education and the Impact of Industry 5\.0 on Society\.

### Current Research Work

My work focuses on advanced manufacturing and production systems with an emphasis on bridging engineering research and industrial practice\. I am interested in composite materials and manufacturing, computational modeling and process simulation, sustainable and human\-centric manufacturing, and Industry 4\.0/5\.0–enabled production systems\. My current interests include manufacturing systems optimization, simulation\-driven engineering, strategic supply development and manufacturing integration, and improving resilience, sustainability, and decision\-making in modern manufacturing environments through data\-driven and systems\-level approaches\.

In both teaching and research, I am particularly interested in the role of engineering education in preparing future engineers for Industry 5\.0 and exploring the broader impact of engineering education on society, human\-centric innovation, sustainability, and industrial transformation\.
