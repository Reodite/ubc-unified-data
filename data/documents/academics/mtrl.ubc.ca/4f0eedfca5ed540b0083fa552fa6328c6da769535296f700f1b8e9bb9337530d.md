## MTRL Graduate Student Association \(GSA\)

The MTRL graduate student community extends beyond classrooms and laboratories\. The MTRL Graduate Student Association \(GSA\) is a student\-led organization committed to enriching your graduate experience\. Past events organized by the GSA include:

**Social Events**

-   Welcome orientation\.
-   Weekly coffee sessions\.
-   Outdoor excursions such as hiking,  
    skiing trips\.

**Professional Development**

-   Career workshops and seminars\.
-   Networking events with professionals\.

**Academic Support**

-   Peer mentorship programs\.
-   Study groups, collaborative projects\.

## Professional Development and Well\-Being

At UBC, we recognize that your success as a graduate student extends beyond academics\. We are committed to supporting your professional growth and personal well\-being throughout your journey\.

[Image](https://apsc-mtrl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/01/22709158259_7b9b807aed_o-940x625.jpg)

[Image](https://apsc-mtrl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/01/37965347504_b7c5d7afe1_o-940x628.jpg)

[Image](https://apsc-mtrl.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/01/32863655437_3a010336ee_o-940x628.jpg)

UBC offers a comprehensive range of professional development resources designed to help you acquire the skills and experience needed for your future career, whether in academia, industry, or beyond\.

-   **[Graduate Pathways to Success \(GPS\)](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/professional-development/graduate-pathways-success)**: A program offering workshops, seminars, and resources covering topics such as strategies to academic success, research skills, career building, academic communication, and leadership development\.
-   **[Mitacs Training](https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/training-students-postdocs/)**: Offers training programs in project management, entrepreneurship, communication, and more, enhancing your professional skill set\.
-   **[Teaching Development](https://ctlt.ubc.ca/programs/graduate-student-ta-programs/)**: The Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology \(CTLT\) provides opportunities to develop your teaching skills through workshops and certificate programs\.
-   **[Experiential Learning Hub](https://experience.apsc.ubc.ca/graduate-students):** Find opportunities such as mentoring program, TA training, and more\.

Your mental and physical well\-being are essential to your success\. UBC provides a variety of services and resources to support your health\. More information on this topic can be found on the  [G\+PS Health, Wellbeing, and Safety](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/health-wellbeing-safety) site as well as the [Graduate Student Society](https://gss.ubc.ca/wellness/) portal\.

-   **[Counseling Services](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services/)**: Confidential counseling for personal, academic, or career\-related concerns\.
-   **[UBC Wellness Centre](https://students.ubc.ca/health/wellness-centre/)**: Offers peer support and resources on topics like mental health, nutrition, and sexual health\.
-   **[Recreation Facilities](https://recreation.ubc.ca/)**: Access to gyms, fitness classes, intramural sports, and outdoor recreation programs to keep you active and engaged\.

[UBC Student Housing](https://vancouver.housing.ubc.ca/getting-started/graduate-students/) and [G\+PS Housing Portal](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/housing) provide information about graduate student housing on campus\. The Graduate \+ Postdoctoral Studies has also been working with several units on campus to address housing affordability for the graduate community at UBC\. Guided by the Campus 2050 vision, a [redevelopment](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024PSFS0036-001343) and expansion of a current housing site is expected to commence in Fall 2026 with a new facility expected to start in 2028\.

-   [**GSS Housing Aids**](https://gss.ubc.ca/housing-aids/): There are several financial aids available, through GSS or the provincial support program\.
