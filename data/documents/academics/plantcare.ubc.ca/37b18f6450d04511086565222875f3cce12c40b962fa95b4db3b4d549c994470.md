Orientation is provided to each researcher before the start of a project\. 

We cover the following topics:

-   Proper use of equipment
-   First aid locations
-   Safety practices and procedures specific to the location where the project is to take place\.

Upon request, Plant Care Services can offer workshops on:

-   Integrated pest management or knowing how to recognize the good guys\!
-   Mitigating disease pressure in protected culture
-   Hydroponics and plant nutrition
-   Grafting strategies
-   Steering plant growth for fruit production or getting the factory to produce your fruits\!
