## [JRNL 503B Newsroom Practices and Standards](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-503-b/)

This course is designed to help students think more broadly about journalism and who they are as journalists\.

## [WRDS 499 Directed Studies in Writing Studies](https://jwam.ubc.ca/courses/wrds-499/)

Individual project on a topic of interest in Writing Studies, completed under the guidance and supervision of a UBC faculty member with relevant expertise\.

[WRDS 498 Special Topics in Writing Studies](https://jwam.ubc.ca/courses/wrds-498/ "WRDS 498 Special Topics in Writing Studies")Several open laptops along with other accessories and food items like coffee, tea, nuts etc on a wooden table

## [WRDS 498 Special Topics in Writing Studies](https://jwam.ubc.ca/courses/wrds-498/)

In\-depth study of selected topics in Writing Studies\. Topics vary year\-by\-year\. Restricted to students with third year standing or higher\.

[JRNL 425 Journalism and Social Change Movements](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-425/ "JRNL 425 Journalism and Social Change Movements")TV news crew live broadcasting a street protest

## [JRNL 425 Journalism and Social Change Movements](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-425/)

Critique the role journalism and news coverage have played in major social movements in Canada and internationally\. Learn how journalism has catalyzed, promoted, misrepresented, or undermined social change through news reporting\.

[JRNL 420 Decoding Social Media](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-420/ "JRNL 420 Decoding Social Media")social media app icons on a mobile phone screen

## [JRNL 420 Decoding Social Media](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-420/)

Identify and understand impacts and implications of social media on journalism and society\. Apply social media tools and practices in storytelling and engagement with users\.

[JRNL 325 Fundamentals of Community Reporting](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-325/ "JRNL 325 Fundamentals of Community Reporting")Bradcast journalist covering a Pride Day demonstration

## [JRNL 325 Fundamentals of Community Reporting](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-325/)

Situates journalism practice in the context of social change and explores the unfair power dynamics inherent in deep reporting and storytelling\.

[JRNL 320 Multimedia Journalism](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-320/ "JRNL 320 Multimedia Journalism")Giant multimedia video and image wall

## [JRNL 320 Multimedia Journalism](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-320/)

Principles of multimedia storytelling with hands\-on journalistic methods in information gathering, fact\-checking, and ethical decision making\.

[JRNL 201 Introduction to News Audiences](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-201/ "JRNL 201 Introduction to News Audiences")Broadcast camera in the news audience

## [JRNL 201 Introduction to News Audiences](https://jwam.ubc.ca/jrnl-courses/jrnl-201/)

Introduction to the evolution of news audiences and their relationships with journalists\. Examines different approaches to audience engagement and various forms of such engagement\.

[WRDS 450 &lt;br&gt; Writing Practices for Publics &amp; Publications](https://jwam.ubc.ca/courses/wrds-450/ "WRDS 450 &lt;br&gt; Writing Practices for Publics &amp; Publications")WRDS 450 Writing Practices for Publics &amp; Publications

## [WRDS 450  
Writing Practices for Publics &amp; Publications](https://jwam.ubc.ca/courses/wrds-450/)

Writing processes and strategies conducive to developing larger, long\-term research projects into genres of writing intended to publicly engage with scholars, activists, and intellectuals\.

[WRDS 400 &lt;br&gt; Writing and Communication Capstone](https://jwam.ubc.ca/courses/wrds-400/ "WRDS 400 &lt;br&gt; Writing and Communication Capstone")man in white t\-shirt and grey pants standing on top of hill

## [WRDS 400  
Writing and Communication Capstone](https://jwam.ubc.ca/courses/wrds-400/)

Critical assessment of approaches to writing and communication\. Key principles of the field applied to public and professional domains relevant to students’ future work, studies, service, or work\.
