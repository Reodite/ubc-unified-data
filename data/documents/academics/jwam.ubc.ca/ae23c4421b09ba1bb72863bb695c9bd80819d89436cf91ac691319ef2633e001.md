## About

Uytae Lee is a columnist and filmmaker whose work covers the complex issues surrounding our cities\. He’s the creator of the CBC series ‘[Stories About Here](https://gem.cbc.ca/media/uytae-lees-stories-about-here/s01)’, where he explores topics such as underground streams, zoning reform, public washrooms, street\-food, and much much more\. In addition to his CBC series, Uytae also produces urban planning documentaries for his own YouTube channel [About Here](https://www.youtube.com/abouthere)\.
