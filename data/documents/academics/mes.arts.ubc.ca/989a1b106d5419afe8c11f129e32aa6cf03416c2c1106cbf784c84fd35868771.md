### How do I get to UBC using the Vancouver Transit System?

UBC is easily accessible by public transit\. There are numerous different bus routes that end directly at the Vancouver campus, including the **R4, 44, 84, 99, 33, 49, 14, 25**, and **4** buses\.

Use the Trip Planning feature on the [Translink website](https://tripplanning.translink.ca/) to find the most efficient routing from anywhere in the Metro Vancouver region\.

Check out Translink's [UBC Bus Exchange Guide](https://students.ubc.ca/sites/students.ubc.ca/files/Buses_from_UBC_Exchange.pdf) for a detailed map on where each bus stop location is\.

### What parking is available close to Allard Hall?

**Driving**   
To get to UBC from downtown Vancouver by car, cross the Burrard or Granville Street bridges, and then head west on 4th Avenue, 10th Avenue or 16th Avenue\. To get to UBC from the airport, cross the Arthur Laing bridge and then head west on SW Marine Drive\.

**Parking**  
There are many parking lots available at UBC\. The closest parkades to Allard Hall are:

-   Rose Garden Parkade \(6278 NW Marine Drive, Vancouver, BC V6T 1Z1\)
-   The North Parkade \(6115 Student Union Boulevard, V6T 1Z1\)

For the full list of parking accommodations available at UBC, check out [UBC's interactive parking map,](https://parking.ubc.ca/map) which includes pricing, and real time lot availability updates\.

### What accommodation options are available near UBC?

**On\-campus Accommodations  
**[UBC Suites](https://suitesatubc.com/accommodations/affordable-reliable-hostels/) There are a limited number of on\-campus UBC Suites available to book\.

Most suites accommodate up to 2 adults, and include amenities such as a fully equipped kitchen with appliances, a washroom, complimentary wireless internet, and LCD TV, depending on which option you choose\.

**Off\-campus Accommodations**  
Vancouver has a wide variety of accommodations to stay at during your trip\. To compare different options and pricing, check out [Trip Advisor's recommendations for hotels near UBC\.](https://www.tripadvisor.ca/HotelsNear-g154943-d591266-University_of_British_Columbia-Vancouver_British_Columbia.html)
