The conference will be held at Allard Hall at UBC’s Vancouver campus\. For a map and directions, please see our [Location, Transportation and Accommodation Guide](https://mes.arts.ubc.ca/location-transportation-accommodation-guide/)\.

**9:15\-9:55am: Registration \+ Coffee  
Location: Allard Lobby**

**10:00\-10:55am: Keynote Lecture by Dr\. Mark Muhannad Ayyash**

### **Keynote Lecture** *\(Allard 105\)*

**Location: Allard 105** 

[Dr\. Mark Muhannad Ayyash](https://mes.arts.ubc.ca/keynote-speaker-dr-mark-muhannad-ayyash/), Decolonial Sovereignties in Palestine: The Intifada's Popular Committees as a Guide Towards a Decolonial Life
