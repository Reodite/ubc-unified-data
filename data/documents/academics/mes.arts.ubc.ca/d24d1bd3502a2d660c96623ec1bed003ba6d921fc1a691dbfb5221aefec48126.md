### The Middle East Studies Student Association \(MESSA\)

The Middle East Studies Student Association is dedicated to bringing forth and broadening conversations on the region\. MESSA will organize academic and social events that offer the UBC community the opportunity to learn more about the Middle East \(broadly construed\) and other global contexts in which the Middle East has resonance\. Our focus is to create platforms for UBC students and faculty to engage more substantively with a highly relevant region and recognize its diverse and long\-standing histories, religions, and cultures\.  

Please contact ubcmessa@gmail\.com if you have any questions or would like to join the association\.
