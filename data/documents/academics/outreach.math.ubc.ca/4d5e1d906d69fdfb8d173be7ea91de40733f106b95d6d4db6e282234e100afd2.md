**Calculators\.** Calculators are not permitted\. Answers that are “calculator ready”, like 3\+ln\(7\)\+11, are fully acceptable\. Students do not have to simplify their answers unless a problem explicitly asks them to\.

**Formulas\.** A [trigonometric formula sheet](https://outreach.math.ubc.ca/calc_challenge.html/formulas.pdf) will be provided with the exam\. No other formulas or aids will be allowed\.

**Showing work\.** Candidates are expected to show all the work leading to the answers they present\. \(unless a particular question specifically says otherwise\)\. In some cases it is acceptable just to write down the answer—for example, when asked to compute something routine like the derivative of cos\(3x\[2\]\+x\)\. However, there are no part marks for incorrect answers obtained this way: A student who just writes down the incorrect \(6x\+1\)tan\(3x\[2\]\+x\) will likely score 0 \(out of 3\), while one who goes explicitly through the chain rule and finishes with the same expression may \(perhaps\) earn 1 point\.
