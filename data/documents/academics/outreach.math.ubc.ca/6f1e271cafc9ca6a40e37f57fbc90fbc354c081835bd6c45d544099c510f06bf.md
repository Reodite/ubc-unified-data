The Math department has expanded its outreach activities\. These include:

-   School workshops: UBC students and faculty run workshops at schools in the Metro Vancouver region\.

-   UBC Math Circle: Students from Metro Vancouver come once a week to UBC for lectures and math problem solving sessions with UBC faculty and students\.

-   Euclid Contest: UBC students and faculty, together with local teachers and alumni, mark the papers of the Euclid contest\. On Euclid day, top contestants have a chance to visit UBC and win UBC scholarships\.
