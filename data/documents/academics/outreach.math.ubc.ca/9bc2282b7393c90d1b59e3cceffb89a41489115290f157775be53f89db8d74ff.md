**U B C      M A T H E M A T I C S**

This is a school\-by\-school study based on final marks in December 1999 for the various \(new\) UBC first year calculus courses for students who graduated from BC schools in 1998/99:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Course** | **Description** |

**MATHEMATICS 100:** Differential Calculus with Applications to Physical Sciences and Engineering\.

**MATHEMATICS 102:** Differential Calculus with Applications to Life Sciences\.

**MATHEMATICS 104:** Differential Calculus with Applications to Commerce and Social Sciences\.

**MATHEMATICS 120:** Honours Differential Calculus\. \(Invitation based on excellence on the Euclid Contest or Principles of Mathematics 12 Scholarship result\.\)

**MATHEMATICS 121:** Honours Integral Calculus\. For students who obtained a mark of 5 in AP Calculus AB or at least 75% in the UBC\-SFU\-UVIC\-UNBC Calculus Challenge Examination \(administered by SFU in 2000; by UBC in 2001\)\. In the first term of 2000/01, MATHEMATICS 101, Integral Calculus with Applications to Physical Sciences and Engineering, will be offered instead of MATH 121\.

**Table of Results in Dec 1999** compares performances for each course\. For the students of each course the average Principles of Math 12 and UBC course grades are given\.

**Table A** compares schools by region and type\. B\.C\. schools identified by region are public schools\. Suburban Vancouver includes the Vancouver, Burnaby, New Westminster, Maple Ridge, Surrey, Coquitlam, Delta, North Vancouver, West Vancouver, and Richmond school districts\.

The **school\-by\-school Tables \(I, II, III\)** include all schools with at least 10 students in UBC calculus classes in the Fall of 1999\.

The "ranking" of a school is determined by equally weighting its results in the categories of % passing, % with A standing, and the change of its students’ average grades in Principles of Math 12 to UBC Math courses\.
