**[Table of Results in Dec 1995](https://outreach.math.ubc.ca/FirstYearCalculus/1995_school_study_conf.html#TABLE%20OF%20RESULTS%20IN%20DEC%201995)** compares performance of female and male students\. For the students of each course the average Math 12 grade and average course grade are given\.

**[Table A](https://outreach.math.ubc.ca/FirstYearCalculus/1995_school_study_conf.html#Table%20A)** compares schools by region and type\. B\.C\. schools identified by region are public schools\. Suburban Vancouver refers to public schools in Burnaby, Richmond, Coquitlam, Delta, New Westminster, North Vancouver, West Vancouver, Maple Ridge, and Langley\.

The **school\-by\-school Tables \([IA](https://outreach.math.ubc.ca/FirstYearCalculus/1995_school_study_conf.html#Table%20IA) Vancouver schools, [IIA](https://outreach.math.ubc.ca/FirstYearCalculus/1995_school_study_conf.html#Table%20IIA) Suburban Vancouver schools, [IIIA](https://outreach.math.ubc.ca/FirstYearCalculus/1995_school_study_conf.html#Table%20IIIA) B\.C\. schools outside metropolitan Vancouver\)** include all schools with at least 10 students in B C calculus classes in the Fall of 1995\. The continued improvement of students from **Earl Marriott** and **Burnaby South** should be noted\.

The "ranking" of a school is determined by equally weighting its results in the categories of % passing, % with A standing, and the change of its students’ average grades in Math 12 and UBC Math courses\.

**David Thompson** \(Vancouver east\) has ranked in the top third of all schools for **nine** consecutive years \(1987\-1995\)\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!

**Eric Hamber** \(Vancouver west\) has ranked in the top third for six consecutive years \(1990\-1995\)\!\!\!\!\!\!

**Killarney** \(Vancouver east\) has ranked in the top third for five consecutive years \(1991\-1995\)\!\!\!\!\!

**Handsworth** \(North Vancouver\) has ranked in the top third for four consecutive years \(1992\-1995\)\!\!\!\!

**Burnaby North** \(Burnaby\), **Lord Byng** \(Vancouver west\) and **Sir Winston Churchill** \(Vancouver west\) have ranked in the top third for three consecutive years\!\!\!

**Crofton House** \(Private, Vancouver\), **Prince of Wales** \(Vancouver west\), **Point Grey** \(Vancouver west\) and **Windermere** \(Vancouver east\) have ranked in the top third for two consecutive years \(1994\-1995\)\!\!

**Abbotsford** \(Abbotsford\), **Yale** \(Abbotsford\), **Sutherland** \(North Vancouver\), **Steveston** \(Richmond\), **Kitsilano** \(Vancouver west\), **Little Flower Academy** \(Catholic, Vancouver\) and **Templeton** \(Vancouver east\) ranked in the top third in 1995\!
