Abstract: 

Translational tiling is a covering of a space \(e\.g\., Euclidean space\) using translated copies of a building block, called the "tile'', without any positive measure overlaps\.  
Which are the sets that tile by translations? What can be said about the structure of the tilings? The study of these questions attracts many researchers and involves methods from various areas\. In a joint work with Terence Tao we disprove the long standing periodic tiling conjecture, which asserts that any translational tile admits a periodic tiling\. In the talk, I will survey the study of translational tilings and discuss our recent progress\.
