Abstract: 

Let p be a prime\. In the '90s, F\. Bogomolov asked the following question: if K is a field containing an algebraically closed field, then is the closure of the commutator subgroup of the Sylow pro\-p subgroup of the absolute Galois group G\_K of K, a free pro\-p group \(cf\. \[1\]\)? Later on, L\. Positselski generalized Bogomolov's question to the following: If K is a field containing a root of 1 of order p \- and \\sqrt\[p^\\infty\]\{K\} denotes the compositum of all radical extensions \\KK\(\\sqrt\[p^n\]\{a\}\), with n &gt;\=1 and a in K \- then the maximal pro\-p Galois group of \\sqrt\[p^\\infty\]\{K\} is a free pro\-p group \(cf\. \[3\]\)\. In a recent work \[4\], Thomas Weigel and myself translated Positselski's version of Bogomolov's conjecture into purely group theoretic language, and verified it for those fields whose maximal pro\-p Galois group is a pro\-p group of elementary type, as defined by I\. Efrat \(e\.g\., local fields, PAC fields, p\-rigid fields, algebraic extensions of global fields with finite K^\\times/\(K^\\times\)^p\.\.\.\)\. This group\-theoretic formulation is tightly related to a cohomological property enjoyed by maximal pro\-p Galois groups of fields, called \`\`Kummerian property'' \(introduced in \[2\], and related, in turn, to smooth profinite groups introduced by M\. Florence\), which may be used also to detect pro\-p groups that do not occur as absolute Galois groups \-\-\- if time allows I will show some significant examples\.

\[1\] F\. Bogomolov, On the structure of Galois groups of the fields of rational functions, K\-theory and algebraic geometry: connections with quadratic forms and division algebra\. In: Proceedings of Symposia on Pure Mathematics, 1992, Santa Barbara CA, vol\. 58 \(1995\)\.

\[2\] I\. Efrat and C\. Quadrelli, Efrat, The Kummerian property and maximal pro\-p Galois groups\. J\. Algebra 525 \(2019\)\.

\[3\] L\. Positselski, Koszul property and Bogomolov’s conjecture\. Int\. Math\. Res\. Not\. 31 \(2005\)\.

\[4\] C\. Quadrelli and Th\. Weigel, Oriented pro\-l groups with the Bogomolov\-Positselski property\. Res\. Number theory 8, no\. 2 \(2022\)\.
