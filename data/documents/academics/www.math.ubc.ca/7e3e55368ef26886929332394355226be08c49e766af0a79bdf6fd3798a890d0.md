Abstract: 

Title: Multilinear singular integrals associated with hypergraphs and an application to products of simplices

L^p estimates for multilinear singular integral forms with cubical structure have been a topic of several recent investigations and have found applications in ergodic theory and Euclidean Ramsey theory\. In the two\-dimensional case, their generalizations to the higher degrees of multilinearity are certain singular integral forms associated with graphs, first introduced by Kovač in the dyadic model\. In the higher\-dimensional case one obtains forms associated with hypegraphs, studied by Stipčić in the dyadic model\. In this talk we introduce a continuous variant of multilinear singular integral forms associated with hypegraphs and explain how certain cancellation estimates for these forms can be used to detect isometric copies of “many” dilates of products of given non\-degenerate simplices, in subsets of positive Lebesgue measure of the unit cube\. Based on joint work with M\. Stipčić\.
