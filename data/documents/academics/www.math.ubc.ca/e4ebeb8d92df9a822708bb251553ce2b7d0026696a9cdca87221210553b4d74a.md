We will be having a mathematics education talk on \*Friday July 22nd at 10 AM\* \(PDT/Vancouver time\) over Zoom\. Professor Mike Pawliuk \(University of Toronto Mississauga\) will be speaking about a recent "ungrading" experiment he tried in a third\-year combinatorics class\. Please see his abstract below\. 

[View All Events](https://www.math.ubc.ca/news-events/seminars-and-colloquia)

Abstract: 

Abstract: In the 2021/2022 school term I taught two third\-year combinatorics courses with 105 students each, without assignments, tests, or exams\. At the end of the terms students decided their own final grades \(with a small number of exceptions\), supported by portfolios of their work from the entire course\. I was surprised by how overwhelmingly positive the outcomes were for students\. I will share specific tools that helped the students succeed, and explore the challenges we faced\.

Length of talk: 45 minutes talk \+ 15 minutes questions, and Mike is happy to stay and chat afterwards\.
