    If you would like to learn calculus in an enriched, rigorous course, or if you are planning to study Honours Mathematics, you should consider taking [MATH 120](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/courseschedule?pname=subjarea&amp;tname=subj-course&amp;dept=MATH&amp;course=120)\. Note that while MATH 120 is more challenging than other first\-year Calculus courses, final grades in MATH 120 are generally adjusted upward based on elements of the final exam common to all first\-year Calculus courses\.
