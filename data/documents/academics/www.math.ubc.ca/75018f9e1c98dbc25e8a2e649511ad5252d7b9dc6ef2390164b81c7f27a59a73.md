Abstract: 

Anderson operator H on a closed Riemannian surface is the random perturbation of the Laplace operator by a space white noise random potential \- a distribution of regularity &lt;\-1\. The low regularity of the potential makes the construction of the operator as an unbounded operator on L2 a non\-trivial task, that can nonetheless be tackled with the help of the tools developed a few years ago for the study of the so\-called singular stochastic PDEs \- M\. Hairer was awarded the Fields medal in 2014 for his foundational contributions to this domain\. The operator H can then be defined as a closed self\-adjoint unbounded operator on L2 with discrete real spectrum bounded below\. This interesting operator has a rich behaviour, related to a number of other objects like the polymer measure\. We will see how to define its zeta determinant\. It will turns out to satisfy a Polyakov\-type anomaly formula\.
