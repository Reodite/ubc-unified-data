Abstract: 

If G is a finite group, we define a G\-symmetric knot \(K, alpha\) to consist of a faithful action alpha of G on S^3 and a G\-equivariant smooth embedding K of S^1 in S^3 \(for some action of G on S^1\)\. We define the type of a G\-symmetric knot \(K, alpha\) to consist of whatever can be determined from the action of G on the oriented smooth manifolds S^3 and on S^1, ignoring the specifics of the knot K\. Having made this definition, I will show that the analysis of types reduces largely to orthogonal representation theory of cyclic and dihedral groups\. From here, I will explain which types arise from G\-symmetric structures on prime knots\.
