Abstract: 

It is well known that Lebegues almost everywhere points are normal numbers\.  Firstly, we will give some background on this topic, and survey some recent results on normal numbers on fractals\. We will discuss how to use some powerful tools from probability theory, harmonic analysis, measure rigidity in ergodic theorem, to study the problems of normality on fractals\. These topics are accessible to a general mathematical audience\.

The second part is devoted to investigate the Fourier transform of fractals measure, including the Fourier decay estimate of some self\-similar measure with special algebraic contractive ratios, and of projected measures of product of Bernoulli convolutions under different directions parameters\.  We study various kinds of conditions which are imposed on fractal measures to guarantee that generic points are absolutely normal\. Our main goal is to show how we use Diophantine approximation tools, such as Thue\-Siegel\-Roth theorem and Linear forms in logarithms to deal with the Fourier analytic behaviors of some fractal measures\.

 Finally, we will discuss some work we are working on and several open problems we plan to go further\.
