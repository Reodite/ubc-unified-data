Abstract: 

In this talk we will discuss estimates for the minimal Euler characteristic of even dimensional manifolds with a given finite fundamental group and a highly connected universal cover\. In particular we strengthen the Hausmann\-Weinberger invariants and extend them to higher dimensions\. As an application we obtain new restrictions for non\-abelian finite groups arising as fundamental groups of rational homology 4–spheres\.

This is joint work with Ian Hambleton\.
