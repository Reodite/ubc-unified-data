Abstract: 

The incipient infinite cluster is a critical percolation cluster that is conditioned on being infinitely large\. Even though the condition has probability zero, Kesten demonstrated the first such construction for critical two\-dimensional percolation in 1986 through a suitable limiting scheme\. Similar constructions for critical high\-dimensional percolation have been carried out by van der Hofstad and Jaraí in 2004\. In this talk, I will present a construction of an incipient cluster with \*two\* disjoint connections to infinity, which we coin bi\-infinite incipient cluster\. Based on joint work with Manuel Cabezas, Alexander Fribergh, and Antal Jaraí\.
