Abstract: 

I will review some aspects of degeneration technique in curve counting problems via Gromov\-Witten and Donaldson\-Thomas theories\. This has been one of the major tools in developments in both theories and computing the invariants\. I will then talk about a progress on the foundation of the degeneration technique for some moduli spaces of stable sheaves that is a joint work with Dohoon Kim\.

*Amin defended his PhD thesis at the University of British Columbia in 2007, under the supervision of Jim Bryan, and is currently a professor at the University of Maryland\.*
