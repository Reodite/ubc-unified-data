Abstract: 

We confirm a conjecture of Fässler and Orponen about projections onto one\-dimensional families of lines and planes in \\mathbb\{R\}^3\. Let \\gamma: \[0,1\] \-&gt; S^2 be a non\-degenerate curve\. Let \\rho\_\\theta: ℝ^3 \-&gt; Span\(\\gamma\(\\theta\)\) and \\pi\_\\theta: ℝ^3 \-&gt; \\gamma\(\\theta\)^\\perp be the orthogonal projections\. We show that if A \\subset R^3 is a Borel set, then for almost all \\theta \\in \[0,1\], \\dim\_H \\rho\_\\theta A\=\\min\\\{1,\\dim\_HA\\\} and \\dim\_H \\pi\_\\theta A \= \\min\{2,\\dim\_H A\}\. The proof uses a Fourier decomposition which allows the application of decoupling for the cone\. The results about \\pi\_\\theta are joint with Gan, Guo, Guth, Harris, and Wang\. The results about \\rho\_\\theta are joint with Gan and Guth\. Our work about \\rho\_\\theta uses different techniques \(leading to different exceptional set estimates\) from the recent work of Pramanik, Yang, and Zahl, which also confirms Fässler and Orponen's conjecture about restricted projections to lines\.
