Abstract: 

Atiyah\-Patodi\-Singer came up with a signature formula for manifolds with boundary by introducing a correction term called eta invariant, extending Atiyah\-Singer signature formula for closed manifolds\. We deal with flat Hermitian bundles over surfaces with boundary to relate the signature to Toledo invariant by introducing rho invariant, which is a refined version of eta invariant and a class function over U\(p,q\)\. This is a joint work with X\. Wan and P\. Pansu\.
