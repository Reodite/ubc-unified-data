Abstract: 

In 1987, with an eye towards anticyclotomic Iwasawa theory of CM elliptic curves at inert primes, Rubin proposed a basic conjecture on the structure of Iwasawa module of local units over anticyclotomic extensions of the unramified quadratic extension of $\\mathbb\{Q\}\_p$\. The talk will report on recent proof of Rubin's conjecture and some of subsequent developments \(joint with S\. Kobayashi and K\. Ota\)\.
