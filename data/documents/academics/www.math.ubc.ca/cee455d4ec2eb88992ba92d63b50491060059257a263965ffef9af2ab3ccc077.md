Abstract: 

Radial projections have been a quickly developing topic in harmonic analysis\. The work of Orponen\-Shmerkin\-Wang connected this topic to a continuum Beck theorem for lines, finding a \(lower\) bound for the dimension of lines that contain at least two points in a given subset of $\\mathbb\{R\}^n$\. Since then, results of Ren and B\.\-Marshall, have studied similar Beck\-type theorems in higher dimensions\. The work thus far has made use of \(dual\) Furstenberg sets\. We discuss these results as a way to motivate dual Furstenberg\-type problems, including a new problem of this type that has come out of ongoing work of B\.\-Ortiz\-Zakharov\.
