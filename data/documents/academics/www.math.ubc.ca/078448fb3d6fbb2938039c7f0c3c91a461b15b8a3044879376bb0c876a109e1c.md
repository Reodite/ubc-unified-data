Abstract: 

We apply and extend the renormalisation group method developed by  
Bauerschmidt, Brydges and Slade to analyse near\-critical finite\-size  
scaling for the 4\-dimensional $n$\-component hierarchical $\|\\phi\|^4$  
model, for all $n\=1,2,3,\\ldots$  In particular, we compute the universal  
scaling profile of the susceptibility in a critical window around the  
infinite\-volume critical point\.  Our methods have the potential to  
extend from the hierarchical to the Euclidean model\. Implications for  
the self\-avoiding walk \(the case $n\=0$\) are also discussed\.  This is  
based on joint work in progress with Emmanuel Michta and Jiwoon Park\.
