Abstract: 

This talk will describe joint research with Brenda Danilowitz into the countercultural intellectual life of one of David Hilbert’s star students, Max Dehn\. Known primarily for his pioneering work in geometry and topology, Dehn also forged a distinctive approach to mathematics informed by its history and prehistory\.
