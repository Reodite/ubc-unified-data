**SPOTLIGHT: SARAH DIJOLS**

Sarah is a UBC Mathematics Postdoctoral Fellow who works in representation theory\. Having been an exchange student at UBC in 2010, Sarah chose to return to UBC to work alongside her postdoc mentor Dr\. Julia Gordon\.
