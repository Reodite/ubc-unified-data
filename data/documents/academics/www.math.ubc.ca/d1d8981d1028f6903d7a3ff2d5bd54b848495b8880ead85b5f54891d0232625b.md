Abstract: 

We discuss work to determine Sato\-Tate groups for higher genus curves\. In so doing, we detail an effective algorithm that computes the identity componenent of Sato\-Tate groups\. We will also discuss open problems related to this work and graduate students are encouraged to attend\. This is joint work with H\. Goodson and A\.Peyrot\.
