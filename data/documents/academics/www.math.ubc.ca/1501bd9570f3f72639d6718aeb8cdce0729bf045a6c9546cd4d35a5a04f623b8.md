Abstract: 

In this talk we discuss two backward uniqueness theorems for extrinsic geometric flow of possibly non\-compact hypersurfaces in general ambient complete Riemannian manifolds\. These are applicable to a wide range of extrinsic geometric flow, including the mean curvature flow, inverse mean curvature flow, Gauss curvature flow and so on\. This is a joint work with Dasong Li\.  

*John obtained his PhD in 2017 at UBC, under the guidance of Professor Jingyi Chen\. He is now an Assistant Professor in the Southern University of Science and Technology \(SUStech\)\.*
