#### Equity Communication Form

Please use this form to communicate your suggestions/ideas/concerns with the Equity Committee\. Example usages include: 

-   Concerns you would like the committee \(or select members of the committee\) to address;
-   Incidents you would like to report \(though see below for more appropriate resources to report incidents of harassment\);
-   Suggestions for changes you would like to see in the department;
-   Ideas you would like the committee to consider the committee implementing;
-   Feedback on events the committee has run\. 

[Equity Communication Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6Sz3PAhsDb6UP3g)
