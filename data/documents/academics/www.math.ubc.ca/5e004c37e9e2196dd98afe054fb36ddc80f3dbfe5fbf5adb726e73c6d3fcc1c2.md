Abstract: 

In this talk I will discuss some conjectures on which values of n we have that $\\binom\{2n\}\{n\}$ is not divisible by sets of primes $p\_1,p\_2,\.\.\.,p\_r$, including a conjecture of R\. L\. Graham\. We do not resolve these conjectures, but have made some progress on some of these conjectures\. This is joint with Hamed Mousavi and Maxie Schmidt\.
