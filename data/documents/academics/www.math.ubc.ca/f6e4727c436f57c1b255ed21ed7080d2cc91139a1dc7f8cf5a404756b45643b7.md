Abstract: 

We say that a finitely generated group algebraically fibers if it admits a homomorphism onto the integers with finitely generated kernel\. This is a group theoretic analogue of the manifold property of being a fibre bundle over a circle\. In this talk, I will give an overview of this and related notions in group theory and geometric topology, and discuss my joint work with Norin and Wise on algebraic fiberings of right\-angled Coxeter groups\.
