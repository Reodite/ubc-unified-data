Abstract: 

One of the main problems in fractal geometry is computing the dimension of self\-similar and self\-conformal \(cookie\-cutters\) when there are complicated overlaps\. The exact overlaps conjecture predicts that, at least in the analytic case, the dimension is the "expected one" \(the minimum between 1 and the zero of the Bowen pressure\) unless the semigroup generated by the maps in the IFS is not free \(there is an exact overlap\)\. I will survey some dramatic progress towards this conjecture in the linear and non\-linear setting by Hochman, Varju and Rapaport, among others\.
