Abstract: 

Equivariant Witt vector bundles, over a scheme of characteristic p&gt;0, were introduced in collaboration with Charles De Clercq and Giancarlo Lucchini\-Arteche\. Their purpose is to serve as a geometric tool, in proving mod p^2 liftability of mod p representations of smooth profinite groups\- comprising the case of Galois representations\. This is still work in progress\.

I will explain some liftability statements \(positive or negative\) achieved so far, and related techniques\. I will then discuss a "general lifting statement", which I believe is within reach\.
