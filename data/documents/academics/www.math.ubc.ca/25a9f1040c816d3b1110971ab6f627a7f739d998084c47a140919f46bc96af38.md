Abstract: 

We investigate the weak solvability and properties of weak solutions to the Dirichlet problem for a scalar elliptic equation $\\Delta u\+b\(\\alpha\)\\cdot\\nabla u\=f$ in a bounded domain $\\Omega\\subset \\Bbb R^2$containing the origin, where $f\\in W^\{\-1\}\_q\(\\Omega\)$ with $q &gt; 2$ and $b\(\\alpha\):\=b\-\\frac\{\\alpha x\}\{\|x\|^2\}$ , $b$ is a divergence\-free vector field and $\\alpha\\in\\Bbb R$ is a parameter\. Then we further improved this result to three dimensional case with a bounded domain $\\Omega \\subset\\Bbb R^3$ and drift $b\(\\alpha\):\=b\-\\frac\{\\alpha x'\}\{\|x'\|^2\}$, where $x'\=\(x\_1,x\_2,0\)$\.
