In addition to its own geometric interest, understanding the  
dimension of radial projections of fractal sets \(and, more generally, the  
distribution of fractal measures in tubes\) has connections and applications  
to several outstanding problems in geometric measure theory, including the  
dimension of distance sets and the discretized sum\-product problem\. I will  
survey some recent progress around radial projections, obtained in joint  
work with Tuomas Orponen and Hong Wang\.
