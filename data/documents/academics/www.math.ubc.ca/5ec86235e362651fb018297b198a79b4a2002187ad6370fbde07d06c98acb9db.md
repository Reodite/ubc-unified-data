Abstract: 

We prove a new signed elementary symmetric function expansion of the chromatic quasisymmetric function of any natural unit interval graph\. We then use sign\-reversing involutions to prove new combinatorial formulas for melting lollipops and for K\-chains, which are formed by joining cliques at single vertices\.
