Abstract: 

The Zakharov system is a coupled system of the Schrodinger  
equation and the acoustic wave equation to describe the Langmuir  
turbulence in plasma\. The goal in this talk is to classify the solutions  
depending on the initial data, in particular to distinguish dispersive  
waves, solitons and blow\-up, among all solutions with energy below or  
slightly above the ground states\. In the subsonic limit, the system is  
reduced to the nonlinear Schrodinger equation, but the analysis of the  
system is much more challenging than the latter, due to the derivative  
loss, the low degree of nonlinear interactions, and the loss of symmetry  
for scaling and momentum\. I will review the recent progress together with  
the ideas to resolve those obstructions\.
