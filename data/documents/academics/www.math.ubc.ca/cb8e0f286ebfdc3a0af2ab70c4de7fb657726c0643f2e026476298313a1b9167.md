Abstract: 

In this talk, I will discuss a conjecture made by  Fässler and Orponen on the restricted projections in R^3\. In the first part, I will introduce some background on the projection problems and its relation with Furstenberg set problem and Szemerédi \-Trotter  theorem\. In the second part, I will introduce the decoupling and high\-low method, and give a proof of the conjecture\.
