##  Undergraduate Students Sub\-Navigation

The Undergraduate Mathematics Society \(UMS\) is the official social club of the Mathematics Department at UBC Vancouver\. The UMS hosts social events, offers a reference library, chalkboards, board games, and a space to relax at their lounge in MATX 1119\. The UMS also sells exam packages for many 100\-300 level Math courses\.
