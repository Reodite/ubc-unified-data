Abstract: 

Given two sets X, Y in R^d, such that X isn't contained in a k\-plane, we show that there exists a point x in X such that the radial projection pi\_x \(Y\) has Hausdorff dimension &gt; min\(dim\(X\), dim\(Y\), k\) \- eps, for any given eps &gt; 0\. This improves a result of Orponen\-Shmerkin\-Wang by removing a lower bound assumption on dim\(Y\)\. The proof borrows heavily from the known d \= 2 case, but there are some obstacles\. In this talk, we explain the main idea to overcome these obstacles\. Then, we will present applications to the Falconer pinned distance set problem in R^d\.
