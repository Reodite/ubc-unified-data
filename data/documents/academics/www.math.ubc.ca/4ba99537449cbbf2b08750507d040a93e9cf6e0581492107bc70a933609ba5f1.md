Abstract: 

In this talk, we study the space of complete embedded rotationally symmetric self\-shrinking hypersurfaces\. First, we derive explicit upper bounds for the entropy of all such self\-shrinkers\. Second, as an application we prove a smooth compactness theorem on the space of all such shrinkers\. We also prove that there are only finitely many such self\-shrinkers with an extra reflection symmetry\. This is a joint work with Ali Muhammad and Niels Martin Møller\.
