Abstract: 

Fix an odd prime number $p$\. Let $K$ be a quadratic imaginary field where $p$ splits\. Let $A$ be an abelian variety defined over $K$ with good supersingular reduction at both primes above $p$\. In this talk, we investigate some aspect of the Iwasawa theory for $A$ over the $\\mathbb\{Z\}\_p^2$\-extension of $K$\. We begin by giving an overview of the relevant Selmer groups building on the work of B\{\\"u\}y\{\\"u\}kboduk and Lei\. We then show that the Mordeil\-Weil rank of A along this $\\mathbb\{Z\}\_p^2$\-extension grows like a function which is $O\(p^n\)$ \(joint with J\. Ray\)\.

Finally, using the recently developed theory of gamma\-systems, we prove an algebraic functional equation involving the Pontryagin dual of our Selmer groups\.
