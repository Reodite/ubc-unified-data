Abstract: 

The Weyl discriminant is a polynomial function on a Lie algebra \(whcih generalizes the discriminant of the characteristic polynomial of a matrix\), which arises in many questions in representation theory\. We compute the log canonical threshold of this polynomial, and from it, make a more precise qualitative statement about local integrability of Fourier transforms of orbital integrals that appear in Harish\-Chandra's work and are ubiquitous in harmonic analysis on reductive p\-adic groups\. This is joint work in progress with Itay Glazer and Yotam Hendel\.
