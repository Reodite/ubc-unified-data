Abstract: 

Braid groups are infinite discrete groups that can be defined in a variety of ways: combinatorially, algebraically, and topologically\. They arise as mapping class groups, in the study of configuration spaces, or via hyperplane arrangements\. Thus braid groups lie in the overlap of many themes in geometric group theory and topology, and are a central object of study\. In this talk, I will survey various perspectives and generalizations on braid groups that are of interest in geometric group theory\. I will also discuss some of my contributions to understanding them\.
