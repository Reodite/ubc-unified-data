Abstract: 

Generalized holomorphic bundles are the analogues of holomorphic vector bundles in the generalized geometry setting\. For some generalized complex structures, these bundles correspond to co\-Higgs bundles, flat bundles or Poisson modules\. I will give an overview of what is known about generalized holomorphic bundles, and describe their moduli spaces in some specific examples\.
