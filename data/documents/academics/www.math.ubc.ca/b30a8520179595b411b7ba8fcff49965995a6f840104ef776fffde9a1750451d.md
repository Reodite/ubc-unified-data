Abstract: 

This is a joint work with Alexander Braverman and David Kazhdan\. The goal is to understand Hecke operators on the Schwartz space of half\-densities over the stack of G\-bundles on a curve over a local non\-archimedean field\. Assuming that the curve has good reduction, we reduce the problem to studying functions on G\-bundles over the reductions of the curve modulo powers of the maximal ideal\. We introduce an analogue of the subspace of cuspidal functions and study it using tools from representation theory\. It contains the usual space of cuspidal functions for the reduction of the curve over the finite field\. We identify some subspaces of cuspidal functions, which show up modulo the square of the maximal ideal, with functions on certain Hitchin fibers\.
