## **Ergodic Theory Seminar** 

[View All Events](https://www.math.ubc.ca/news-events/seminars-and-colloquia)

Abstract: 

Abstract: Given a subshift, there exist certain words that can always be replaced by other words \(possibly of different size\)\. In this talk I will show how the measure of maximal entropy of these two words are related by an inequality\. Furthermore I will present some applications of these inequalities\. This is joint work with Ronnie Pavlov\.
