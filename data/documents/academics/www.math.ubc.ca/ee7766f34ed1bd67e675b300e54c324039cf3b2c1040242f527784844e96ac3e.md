Abstract: 

In this talk, I will discuss a long\-standing problem on type II singularity of Ricc flow\. First I recall some known results on long\-time behavior of Ricci flow, then I will present our solution to the above problem\. I may also discuss a related problem concerning clasification of Fano manifolds in the end\.
