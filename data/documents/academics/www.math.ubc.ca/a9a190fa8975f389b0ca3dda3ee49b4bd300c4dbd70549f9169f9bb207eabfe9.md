Abstract: 

The conjectural mod $p$ Langlands program relates continuous mod $p$ local Galois representations to smooth admissible representation of $p$\-adic groups \(with coefficient field of characteristic $p$\)\. Except for $GL\_1$ and $GL\_2\(\\mathbb\{Q\}\_p\)$, there is no known construction of mod $p$ Langlands correspondence\. However, it is possible to construct a candidate using the space of mod $p$ automorphic forms and the Talyor\-Wiles method\. It is not clear whether this candidate, constructed by taking non\-canonical choices of global data, is determined by the initial mod $p$ local Galois representation\.

In this talk, we discuss a question in reverse direction: can we recover the initial local Galois representation from the smooth admissible representation of $p$\-adic group constructed above? We will discuss the proof of this statement in the case of $GSp\_4\(\\mathbb\{Q\}\_p\)$ and local Galois representations that are upper\-triangular, maximally non\-split, and generic\. One of the main ingredients in the proof is explicit Jantzen filtration of lattices in a certain tame type\. This is joint work with John Enns\.
