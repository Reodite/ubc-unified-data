Abstract: 

Arithmetic function fields are one variable function fields over complete discretely valued fields\. They naturally admit several collections of overfields with respect to which one can study local\-global principles\. The talk will concern such local\-global principles for torsors under linear algebraic groups, as well as their obstructions\. \(Joint work with J\.L\.\-Colliot\-Thélène, D\. Harbater, D\. Krashen, R\. Parimala, and V\. Suresh\. \)
