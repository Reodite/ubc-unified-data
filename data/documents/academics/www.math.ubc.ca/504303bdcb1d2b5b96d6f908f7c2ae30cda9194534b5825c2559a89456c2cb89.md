Welcome back UBC Mathematics students\!

It's an exciting time on campus as we kick off a new academic year\. To our returning students, we hope you had a fantastic summer and are ready to dive back into your studies, reconnect with friends, and explore new opportunities\. We're thrilled to have you back on campus\.

To our new students, a huge, warm welcome\! We're so glad you've chosen to be a part of our community\. This is a big moment, and we know you have a lot to navigate\. Don't worry, you are not alone\. The UBC Mathematics department is full of people and resources dedicated to helping you succeed, from your professors to student services and countless clubs and organizations\. We encourage you to ask questions, explore new interests, and build connections that will last a lifetime\.

Whether you're starting your final year or your first day, this year is full of potential\. Let's make it a great one together\!
