Abstract: 

In this talk I will outline the geometric ingredients of smooth compactifications of the heterotic string\. This includes an SU\(3\) structure manifold \(for example, a Calabi\-Yau threefold\) and a holomorphic, Mumford poly\-stable vector bundle supported over it\. Much recent work has attempted to explore the moduli spaces of such objects and the way that their properties determine the effective 4\-dimensional physics\. I will describe some of this progress, focusing in particular on fibration structures in Calabi\-Yau manifolds and geometric transitions between topologically distinct Calabi\-Yau threefolds\.
