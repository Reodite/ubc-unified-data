Abstract: 

Intersection theory studies the Chow ring, an algebraic structure whose elements correspond to subvarieties of a variety X, with the ring product reflecting the intersection of subvarieties in X\. Thanks to the foundational work of Fulton and MacPherson, this theory is well\-understood when X is smooth\. In this talk, I will present a naive attempt to extend the definition of the Chow ring to singular schemes â€” and explain why it fails\.
