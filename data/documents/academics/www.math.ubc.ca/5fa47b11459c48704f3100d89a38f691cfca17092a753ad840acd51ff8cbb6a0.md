Abstract: 

*Joint work with Punya Satpathy*

For a reductive group G, Borel and Serre introduced a compactification of a large class of arithmetic quotients of the symmetric space attached to G\. After reviewing some aspects of their construction, we explain how to generalize it to the case when G is replaced by an infinite\-dimensional analogue LG, the loop group of G\. Along the way, we describe  a partition of an arithmetic quotient of LG, inspired by the work of P\.\-H\. Chaudouard for GL\_n and related to earlier constructions of Harder\-Narasimhan and Behrend\.
