Abstract: 

From the work on Bernoulli convolutions by P\. Erd\\H\{o\}s in 1939 and up to the  
recent advances on self\-similar measures by M\. Hochman, P\. Shmerkin, P\. Varj\\'u, and others, questions on absolute continuity and singularity remain to be a challenge\. They are at the intersection of several fields, including fractal geometry, ergodic theory, and harmonic analysis\.  After a general introduction, I will discuss recent work with Adam \\'Spiewak, where we obtain results on absolute continuity for \`\`typical'' self\-similar measures in the plane\.
