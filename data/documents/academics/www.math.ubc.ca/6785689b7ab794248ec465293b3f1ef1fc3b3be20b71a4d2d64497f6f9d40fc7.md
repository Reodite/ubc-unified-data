Abstract: 

A conjecture of Borel and Tits says that an abstract group homomorphism between the groups of rational points of algebraic groups \(over infinite fields\) should admit a “standard description,” which is a factorization as a composition of maps in which one of those maps is regular\. For existing results on split groups and groups of the form SL\_\{n,D\}, they key object in analyzing such a homomorphism is an algebraic ring structure on the Zariski closure of the image of a one dimensional \(unipotent\) root group\. We prove a case of the conjecture for a class of quasi\-split special unitary groups by extending this method to treat two\-dimensional root groups and their associated algebraic rings\.
