Abstract: 

We prove the existence, uniqueness and regularity of weak solutions to the Dirichlet problem for an elliptic equation with a drift b and related result for L^q gradient estimates for perturbed Stokes system\. We assume b belongs to weak Morrey class which includes in the 3D case, in particular, drifts having a singularity along the axis x\_3\. For perturbed Stokes system  we consider drift from critical L^n space\.  

*Misha Chernobai completed their PhD at UBC and specialist degree at Saint\-Petersburg state university\. Their main field of study are non\-linear PDE's related to fluid dynamics\.*
