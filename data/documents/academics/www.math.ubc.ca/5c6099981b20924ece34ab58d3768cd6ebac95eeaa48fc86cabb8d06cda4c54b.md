Abstract: 

What is the structure of the set of the last few points visited by a random walk on a graph? We show that on vertex\-transitive graphs of bounded degree, this set is decorrelated \(it is close to a product measure in total variation\) if and only if a simple geometric condition on the diameter of the graph holds\. In this case, the cover time has universal fluctuations: properly scaled, this time converges to a Gumbel distribution\. To prove this result we rely on recent progress in geometric group theory \(about quantitative versions of Gromov's Theorem for finite vertex\-transitive graphs\) and we prove refined quantitative estimates showing that the hitting time of a small set of vertices is typically approximately an exponential random variable\. This talk is based on joint work with Nathanaël Berestycki and Lucas Teyssier\.
