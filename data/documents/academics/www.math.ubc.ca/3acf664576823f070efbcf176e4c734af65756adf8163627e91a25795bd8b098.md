Abstract: 

Multiplication, relative to addition, is convex, and this is one of the explanations for the sum\-product phenomenon over the reals\. It turns out that convexity alone is really only a first order approximation to the truth, and higher order convexity \-\- a phenomenon less exploited by traditional geometric methods \-\- leads to better results about expanders\. I'll discuss this and how it allows us to deduce new geometric results concerning dot products from expansion estimates\.
