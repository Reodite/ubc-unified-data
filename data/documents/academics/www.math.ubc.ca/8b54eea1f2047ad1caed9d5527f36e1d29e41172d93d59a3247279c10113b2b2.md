Abstract: 

Asgarli, Ghioca, and Reichstein recently proved that if $K$ is a field with $\|K\|&gt;2$, then for any positive integers $d$ and $n$, and separable field extension $L/K$ with degree $m\=\\binom\{n\+d\}\{d\}$, there exists a point $P\\in \\mathbb\{P\}^n\(L\)$ which does not lie on any degree $d$ hypersurface defined over $K$\. They asked whether the result holds when $\|K\| \= 2$\. In this talk, I will discuss how to address this question\. More generally, I will show that for each positive integer $r$ and separable field extension $L/K$ with degree $r$, there exists a point $P \\in \\mathbb\{P\}^n\(L\)$ such that the vector space of degree $d$ forms over $K$ that vanish at $P$ has the expected dimension\. Joint work with Shamil Asgarli and Jonathan Love\.
