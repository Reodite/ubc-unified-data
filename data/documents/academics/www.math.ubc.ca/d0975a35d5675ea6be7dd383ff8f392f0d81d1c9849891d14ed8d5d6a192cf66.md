Abstract: 

The notions of G and H convergences of elliptic operators were introduced by De Giorgi \- Spagnol and Murat\-Tartar a half century ago\. With foundational contributions from many prominent mathematicians, the subject experienced a tremendous expansion in both theories and applications in the following decades\. The purpose of this lecture is to discuss how the weak convergence &amp; averaging properties of elliptic operators \(with wildly oscillating coefficients\) are surprisingly connected to the analytic properties of solutions of such operators\. In particular we discuss the validity of Calderon\-Zygmund type estimates, Liouville type theorem, strong unique continuation, 3 sphere theorem and large scale analyticity\.
