Abstract: 

 In this joint work with X\. Zhou, we exhibit the first examples of non\-generic metrics on the three sphere in which the varifold associated with a two\-parameter min\-max construction must be a multiplicity two minimal surface\. This is proved by a new area\-and\-separation estimates for certain minimal surfaces with Morse index two\.
