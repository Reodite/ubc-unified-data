##  Undergraduate Students Sub\-Navigation

### What is a Co\-op?

Co\-op students alternate between academic terms and challenging, practical, and paid work experience\. Co\-op Programs aim to offer work opportunities that will help students excel in both academic and professional capacities, in preparation for a future career\.

A Co\-op experience provides students with a platform to explore Mathematics career options, along with the opportunity to explore interests in other science or art\-related areas\. The Co\-op work experience will enable you to build your personal network connections and learn critical skills not taught in a classroom\. UBC works with a diverse range of community partners and sectors to provide transformative workplace learning for students\.
