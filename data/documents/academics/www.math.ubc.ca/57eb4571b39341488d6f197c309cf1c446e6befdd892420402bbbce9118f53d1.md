Abstract: 

We will explain how, to any quiver, one can associate a finite length abelian category which categorifies the corresponding K\-theoretic Hall algebra\. The simples in this category provide a \(dual\) canonical basis of the Hall algebra\. In particular, if the quiver is affine, this provides a basis for the positive half of the corresponding quantum toroidal algebra\. We also explain how this abelian category is naturally endowed with renormalized r\-matrices\.
