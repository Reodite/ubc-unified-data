Abstract: 

Let *E* be an elliptic curve over an imaginary quadratic number field *K* and let *p* be an odd prime number\. Greenberg introduced a topology for the family of linearly independent Z\_p extensions of *F* for any number field *F*\. A conjecture of Mazur predicts that the growth of rational points of *E* along a Z\_p extension of *K* is bounded unless the extension is the anticyclotomic extension, and the root number of *E* over K is \-1\. We study the growth of rational points of *E* in the family of Z\_p extensions and its implications for Mazur's conjecture\.
