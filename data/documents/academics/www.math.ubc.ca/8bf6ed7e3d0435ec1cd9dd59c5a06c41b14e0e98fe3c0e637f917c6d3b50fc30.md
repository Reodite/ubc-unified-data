Abstract: 

Given an abelian variety over a number field, its Sato\-Tate group is a compact Lie group, and it is conjectured to control the distribution of Euler factors of the L\-function of the abelian variety\. In this talk we will begin with a discussion on the Sato\-Tate conjecture for elliptic curves and discuss work that computes the Sato\-Tate groups of families of hyperelliptic curves of arbitrarily high genus and discuss some open problems in this area\. This work is joint with H\. Goodson and A\. Peyrot\.
