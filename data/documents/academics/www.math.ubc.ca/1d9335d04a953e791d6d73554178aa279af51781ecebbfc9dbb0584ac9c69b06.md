Abstract: 

Rost and Voevodsky proved the Bloch\-Kato conjecture relating Milnor k\-theory and Galois cohomology\. It implies that if a field F contains a primitive pth root of unity, then the Galois cohomology ring of F with coefficients in the trivial F\-module with p elements is generated by elements of degree one\. In this talk, I will discuss a systematic approach to studying this phenomenon in finite field extensions via decomposition fields\.

*This is joint work with Sunil Chebolu, Jan Minac, Cihan Okay, and Andrew Schultz\.*
