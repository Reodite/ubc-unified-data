Abstract: 

Given a finite group G and a faithful representation G \-&gt; GL\(V\) where V is a finite dimensional k\-vector space, the essential dimension of G over k is the minimal dimension dim\(Y\) of an irreducible faithful G\-variety Y with a dominant G\-equivariant rational map V \-&gt; Y\. The essential dimension of a finite group G can be viewed as the minimum number of independent objects needed to define roots of a "generic" polynomial with Galois group G\. It is believed that the essential dimension of the symmetric group Sn is n\-3 for n&gt;\=5, at least in characteristic 0\. We show that the essential dimension of Sn can be n\-4 in prime characteristic\.
