Abstract: 

We give a tour of several topics in control theory and machine learning, from data\-based models to a parameterization of stabilizing policies\. The crux of the talk is how to unify seemingly disjoint areas of control theory and make them play nicely with the reinforcement learning paradigm\. For a relaxing break from marking exams, we will do so with a minimal set of ingredients and a number of illustrations, including examples on a pilot\-scale system\.
