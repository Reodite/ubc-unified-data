Abstract: 

I will discuss recent joint work with Tom Bachmann and Mike Hopkins establishing an analog of the Freudenthal suspension theorem for \\\(\\mathbb\{P\}^1\\\)\-suspension in unstable motivic homotopy theory\. I will try to give a quick introduction to motivic homotopy theory, explaining some reasons why having such a result might be considered useful \(e\.g\., concrete applications\), and, time\-permitting, some ideas regarding proofs\.
