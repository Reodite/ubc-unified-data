Abstract: 

For positive integer $n$, Erdős's minimum overlap problem is to determine the minimum $M\(n\)$ over all equal sized partitions $A \\cup B \= \[2n\]$ of the maximum intersection of $A$ and a translation of $B$\. Many upper and lower estimates have been obtained in the past eight decades, and the state of the art is $0\.356 &lt; M\(n\)/n &lt; 0\.381$\. We obtain a substantially improved lower bound, namely $\\lim\_\{n \\to \\infty\} M\(n\)/n &gt; 0\.379$\. Our approach uses Fourier analysis to translate the problem to a convex optimization program\.
