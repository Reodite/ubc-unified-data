Abstract: 

 Vogan's geometric perspective on the local Langlands correspondence suggests that certain categories of smooth representations of p\-adic groups might be closely related to categories of equivariant perverse sheaves of moduli spaces of Langlands parameters, in a manner similar to what has been conjectured by Soergel for Lie groups\. In this talk I’ll introduce a precise conjecture about these categories which we call Local Langlands Duality, and present an example\. Joint work with James Steele and Kristaps Balodis\.
