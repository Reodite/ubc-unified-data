This talk is part of a special Number Theory Seminar which will feature talks by Haruzo Hida and Nike Vatsal\.

[View All Events](https://www.math.ubc.ca/news-events/seminars-and-colloquia)

Abstract: 

I will discuss work in progress on the extension of an Iwasawa\-style p\-adic L\-function to a certain kind of function on a perfectoid space\. This kind of extension is implicit in work of Colmez, but the applications seem not to have been studied yet\.
