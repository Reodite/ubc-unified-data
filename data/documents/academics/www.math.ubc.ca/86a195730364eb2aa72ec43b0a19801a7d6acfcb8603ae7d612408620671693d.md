Abstract: 

In this talk, I will first discuss some results on Ricci flow on compact complex manifolds with positive first Chern class\. Those manifolds are also referred as Fano manifolds\. Next I will discuss a Laplacian comparison for manifolds with Kato bound on curvature and its applications, in particular, we give an alternative proof of the Hamilton\-Tian conjecture\.
