Abstract: 

Conifold transitions are a mechanism in which a Calabi\-Yau 3\-fold is deformed into another by contracting curves and smoothing out the resulting conical singularities\. Reid's Fantasy conjectures that all Calabi\-Yau 3\-folds can be linked by a sequence of these transitions\. It is further conjectured that these spaces all admit geometries solving a system of PDEs from heterotic string theory\. In this talk, I will discuss some results related to these conjectures as well as touch on the 7\-dimensional analogue of this problem\. Based on joint works with Benjamin Friedman, Sébastien Picard, Henrique N\. Sá Earp, and Julieth Saavedra\.
