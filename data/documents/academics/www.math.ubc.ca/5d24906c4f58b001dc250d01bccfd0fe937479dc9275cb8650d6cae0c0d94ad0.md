Abstract: 

We will introduce various geometric and \(higher\) categorical constructions in a mostly informal manner\. The goal of this talk is to explain what **kinds** objects appear in geometric representation theory and \(the statement and proof of\) geometric Langlands, rather than what the objects actually are\.
