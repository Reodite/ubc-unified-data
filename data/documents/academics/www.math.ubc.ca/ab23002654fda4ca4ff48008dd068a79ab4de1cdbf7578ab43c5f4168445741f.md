Abstract: 

In this talk, I will discuss joint work with Malabika Pramanik on the problem of locating patterns in sets of high Hausdorff dimension\.  More specifically, suppose $\\Gamma$ is a smooth curve in Euclidean space that passes through the origin\.  Is it true that every set with sufficiently high Hausdorff dimension must contain two distinct points $x,y$ such that $x\-y \\in \\Gamma$?  We showed that if $\\Gamma$ is suitably curved then the answer is yes, while for certain flat curves the answer is no\.  This generalizes work of Kuca, Orponen, and Sahlsten, who answered this question affirmatively when $\\Gamma$ is the standard parabola in $\\mathbb\{R\}^2$\.
