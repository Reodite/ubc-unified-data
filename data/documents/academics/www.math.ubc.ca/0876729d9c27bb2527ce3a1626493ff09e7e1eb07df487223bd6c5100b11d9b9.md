Abstract: 

In this talk, I will describe three projects designed to support students’ development as mathematical thinkers, with a focus on their engagement with mathematics\. First, I will discuss the co\-creation of ‘Student Perspective’ course materials for Math 220 \(Mathematical Proof\) that address common misunderstandings and cultivate intuition\. Second, I will outline the creation of a summer program that emphasizes collaboration, communication, and creativity at an early stage in students’ mathematical development\. Finally, I will share approaches to welcoming non\-STEM students into mathematics, to change apprehension into curiosity, engagement, and even excitement\.
