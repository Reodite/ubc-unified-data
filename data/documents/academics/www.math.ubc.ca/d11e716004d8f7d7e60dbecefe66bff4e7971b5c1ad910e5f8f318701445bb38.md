Graduating in May 2026 \(or graduated in Nov 2025\)? Don't forget to book your graduation photography appointment to be included in the official 2026 graduating class composite\.

[BOOK YOUR APPOINTMENT](https://artona.com/schools/UBC-ALLFAC)

###### **Photo deadline:** March 30, 2026  
*You must be photographed by this date to appear on the 2026 graduating class composite\.*

If you are graduating later in 2026, you are still welcome to book your photo now\. Your photo will be included in the following year’s composite\.

#### Booking Instructions

When booking your appointment, please be sure to include:

-   Student number
-   Department: Math
-   Convocation date

This information is required to ensure your photo is placed in the correct department composite and graduation year\.

#### Book Early

Appointments are open to all graduating students, and evening and weekend time slots fill up quickly\. Early booking is strongly encouraged\.

#### Deposit Information

-   A $60 fully refundable deposit is required at the time of booking\.
-   The deposit will appear as a credit in your online account and can be applied to any photo order\.
-   If you choose not to place an order, the deposit can be refunded by returning the previews within 30 days of your photo session\.

#### Questions

Please contact Artona directly at [**csr@artona\.com**](mailto:csr@artona.com)\.
