Abstract: 

 I will discuss toric Fano surfaces in the complement of a smooth anticanonical divisor and their mirror Landau\-Ginzburg theories\. I will focus on relations between open Gromov\-Witten invariants of fibers of the Gross fibration, relative invariants, scattering diagrams and broken lines, tropical curves, superpotentials and wall crossing\. This joint work with Tim Graefnitz and Helge Ruddat builds on works of Chan, Lau, Leung, Tseng; the wall crossing takes its cue from the work Gross, Pandharipande, Siebert\.
