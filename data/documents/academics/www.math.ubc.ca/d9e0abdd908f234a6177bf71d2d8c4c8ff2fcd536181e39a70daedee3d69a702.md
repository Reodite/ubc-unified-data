Abstract: 

With Daniel Ahlberg and Brett Kolesnik, we consider a model of competing branching brownian motions \(BBM\)\. Different colours branch and move independently, and when two different colour particles meet they annihilate\. We study the posibililty and nature of coexistence\. No background knowledge of BBM will be assumed\.
