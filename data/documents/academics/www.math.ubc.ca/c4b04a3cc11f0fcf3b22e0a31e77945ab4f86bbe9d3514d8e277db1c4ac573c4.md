Abstract: 

Flows of multiphase systems, and in particular solid particles suspended in fluids, are prevalent in natural and industrial settings\. In this talk, we discuss the the numerical simulation of multiphase and multi\-scale flows using high\-fidelity models\. We focus on the application of computational methods, including spectral element, finite volume/immersed boundary and finite difference/point\-particle, to problems addressing sustainability, such as microplastics in the oceans and fibrous flows\. Finally, we explain how the statistical analysis of the results of the high\-fidelity models is used to establish reduced\-order models that provide physics\-based information for mitigation and efficiency improvement strategies\.
