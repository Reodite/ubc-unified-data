Abstract: 

\(Joint work with Yonatan Harpaz\.\)  Let k be a field and p be a prime\. According to a conjecture of Mináč and Tân, Massey products of n&gt;2 classes in H^1\(k,Z/pZ\) should vanish whenever they are defined\.  We establish this conjecture when k is a number field, for any n\.  This constraint on the absolute Galois group of k was previously known to hold when n\=3 and when n\=4, p\=2\.
