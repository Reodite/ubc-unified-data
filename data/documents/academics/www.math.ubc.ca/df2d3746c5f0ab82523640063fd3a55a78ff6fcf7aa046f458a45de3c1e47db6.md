Calling all math major/honours undergrads\!

We've got exciting news\! Our brand new undergrad lounge opens this fall, and we want **YOUR** artwork to be a part of its debut\! From August 2nd to 16th, submit your creations for a chance to see them displayed on our lounge walls\.

Four winners will be chosen and each will receive a:

-   UBC Mathematics tote bag 
-   UBC Mathematics Water bottle 
-   UBC Mathematics Hoodie 
-   More small UBC Math goodies\!

Artwork specifications:

-   Size: 24" x 36"
-   Resolution: 300dpi
-   **File name should include your first and last name \(ex\. Jane\_smith\.pdf\)**
-   Accepted formats: PDF, AI and EPS

Showcase your creativity and leave your mark on our vibrant new space\! Don't miss out on this opportunity to shine\. 

Submissions should be uploaded to the link below:

[UPLOAD YOUR ARTWORK](https://owncloud.math.ubc.ca/index.php/s/JSoetm47jowPiZH)

**Once you've submitted your artwork, please email us at [engage@math\.ubc\.ca](mailto:engage@math.ubc.ca) to let us know that you've submitted your work\. Please include your name, email address and the name of your artwork file\.**

**Artwork does not need to be math related; however, it would be great if it was\!**
