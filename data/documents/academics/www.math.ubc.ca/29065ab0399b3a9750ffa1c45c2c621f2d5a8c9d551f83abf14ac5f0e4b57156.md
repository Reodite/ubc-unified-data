Abstract: 

For a cuspidal automorphic representation $\\Pi$ of GSp\(4\) and a cuspidal automorphic representation $\\pi$ of GL\(2\), Furusawa's formula can be used to study the special values of the degree\-eight p\-adic L\-function $L\(s,\\Pi\\times\\pi\)$\. In this talk, I will explain a construction of the p\-adic L\-function for $\\Pi\\times\\pi$ by using Furusawa's formula and a family of Eisenstein series\. The construction includes choosing local test sections at p and computing the corresponding local zeta integrals\.
