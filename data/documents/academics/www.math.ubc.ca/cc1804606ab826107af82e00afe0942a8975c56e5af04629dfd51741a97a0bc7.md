Abstract: 

I will speak about a generalization of the Vafa\-Intriligator formula that  
expresses the Gromov\-Witten invariants of a Fano variety, for fixed markings and  
insertions of even degrees, in terms of eigenvalues of multiplication operators  
on the small quantum cohomology ring\. This generalizes earlier known formulas  
for cominuscule flag varieties, including Grassmannians of type A and  
Grassmannians of maximal isotropic subspaces\. In the Grassmannian cases, the  
relevant eigenvalues are explicitly known from the work of Rietsch and Cheong\.  
The eigenvalues can also be described explicitly for Fano complete  
intersections\. Applications include simple formulas for Tevelev degrees\. This is  
joint work with Rahul Pandharipande\.
