Abstract: 

Given an abelian variety G defined over a field of characteristic 0, the intersection between a subvariety X of G with another subvariety Y of G for which dim\(X\) \+ dim\(Y\) &lt; dim\(G\) is generally expected to be empty\. Rooted in this basic observation, Pink\-Zilber and Bombieri\-Masser\-Zannier formulated the following conjecture regarding unlikely intersections in arithmetic geometry \(over a field of characteristic 0\)\. So, let X be an irreducible subvariety of dimension d of the abelian variety G, and assume X is not contained in any proper algebraic subgroup of G\. Then the intersection of X with the union of all algebraic subgroups of G of codimension d\+1 is predicted not to be Zariski dense in X\. Motivated by this conjecture, we present a couple of related questions in arithmetic dynamics\.
