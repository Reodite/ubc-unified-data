Abstract: 

The last 10\-15 years have seen several works aimed at understanding the behaviour of scale\-invariant norms near a potential blow\-up time/finite maximal time of existence for a variety of PDE\. Most of these results and proofs are qualitative and provide no quantitative information\. In a very interesting recent work, Terence Tao provided the first quantitative blow\-up rates for globally defined scale\-invariant norms of the Navier\-Stokes equations\. In this talk, I will address what happens quantitatively in the local vicinity of any potential singularity\. I will also discuss some other results in this direction and further perspectives\. Part of this talk is joint work with Christophe Prange \(CNRS, Cergy Paris Université\)\.
