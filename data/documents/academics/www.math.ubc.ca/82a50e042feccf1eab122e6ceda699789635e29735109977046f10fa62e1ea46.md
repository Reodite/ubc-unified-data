Abstract: 

This talk will be an introduction to the field of symbolic dynamics, which concerns various classes of maps between spaces of infinite symbol sequences called subshifts, and in particular to the questions within symbolic dynamics that my dissertation addressed\. My goal is for the audience to leave with a reasonable feeling for the things named in the title of my dissertation, namely Gibbs measures \(on subshifts\) and factor codes \(between subshifts\), and a sense of what it is like to work with these things\. To the latter end, the talk will center on a series of pitches for four games I have developed \(one tabletop game, one movement/dance game, two that are a mix of both\), whose gameplay encapsulates the kinds of challenges and ideas I have found most central in my research, with sufficient explanation for the interested player to know where to look in my dissertation to learn more about what they are doing\.
