Abstract: 

We will discuss conjectures and results regarding the Hilbert Property, a generalization of Hilbert's irreducibility theorem to arbitrary algebraic varieties\. In particular, we will explain how to use conic fibrations to prove the Hilbert Property for the integral points on certain surfaces, such as affine cubic surfaces\.
