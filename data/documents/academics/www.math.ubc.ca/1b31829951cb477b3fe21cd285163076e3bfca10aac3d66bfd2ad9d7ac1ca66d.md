### MAY 6 \- 8, 2025

In 2025, the UBC Mathematics Building will mark its 100th anniversary, celebrating a century of mathematical discovery, learning, and innovation\. To commemorate this milestone, we are hosting a special three\-day\-long celebration filled with engaging talks, historical reflections, and exciting events\. The festivities will feature distinguished speakers from across the globe, highlighting the building’s rich legacy and the contributions of UBC’s mathematics community\. Alumni, students, and faculty will come together to honour the past while looking ahead to the future of mathematical research and education\. Join us for a week of celebration as we recognize this historic achievement\!
