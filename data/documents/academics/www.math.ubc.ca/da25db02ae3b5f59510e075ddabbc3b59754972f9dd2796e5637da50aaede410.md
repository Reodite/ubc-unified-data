Abstract: 

We consider the linear wave equation with the time\-dependent scale\-invariant damping\. \(We also treat the corresponding equation with the energy critical nonlinearity if time permits\.\) Our aim is to show that the solution scatters to a modified linear wave solution and to obtain its asymptotic order\.  This talk is based on joint works with Haruya Mizutani \(Osaka Univ\.\)\.
