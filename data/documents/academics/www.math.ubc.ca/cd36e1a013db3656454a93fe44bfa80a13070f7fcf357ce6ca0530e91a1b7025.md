Abstract: 

In this metamathematical talk I shall argue that we genuinely belong  
under one roof: that no matter how we self\-identify, in an important  
sense we all do the same thing\.  Recent developments have made the  
question "what is it that we do?" even more important\.

The mathematical vehicle carrying the metamathematics will be problems  
that start in mathematical physics, but reach numerical methods, number  
theory, and even mathematical logic\.

Most of the talk should be accessible to undergraduate students\.
