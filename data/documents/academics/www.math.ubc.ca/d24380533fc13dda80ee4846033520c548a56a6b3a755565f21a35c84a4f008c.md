Abstract: 

In this talk, I will discuss Toen's theory of affine stacks and their relation to unipotent group schemes\. I will also briefly talk about how this is applied in studying certain unipotent homotopy group schemes associated with algebraic varieties studied in joint work with Emanuel Reinecke\.
