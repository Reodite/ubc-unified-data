*Pam is currently an assistant professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Toronto\. She completed her Ph\.D\. at UBC in 2018 under the supervision of Ailana Fraser\. Before arriving at the University of Toronto, she worked as a lecturer at Yale University and as an assistant professor at York University\. Currently, Pam is primarily interested in the scholarship of teaching and learning\. In particular, she is interested in finding new ways of creating authentic active learning experiences in large\-scale classrooms and supporting graduate students in their role as educators\.*
