Abstract: 

The insulated conductivity problem is a question about the strength of the electric field between two almost\-touching insulators\.  Mathematically it is a question about harmonic functions which is extremely simple to state, yet the optimal bounds in higher dimensions are completely unknown\.   I will give some background on this problem, describe some known results, and then show how a maximum principle argument gives new bounds in dimensions 4 and higher\.
