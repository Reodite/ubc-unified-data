Abstract: 

Conformally invariant equations in $n\\geq3$ have played an important role in the study of $\\sigma\_k$\-Yamabe problem in geometric analysis\. In this talk, we will discuss a class of Mobius invariant equations in dimension two and then present a Liouville type theorem for such equations\. We will then discuss a \\sigma\_2\-Nirenberg problem on \\mathbb\{S\}^2\. This is a joint work with Yanyan Li and Siyuan Lu\.
