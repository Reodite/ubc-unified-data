Abstract: 

We'll talk about a piece of mathematics bringing together number theory \(Ivan Niven's subject\), geometry, and dynamical systems, that exemplify many of the things I love about math \- community, diversity, collaboration, beautiful images, and an ongoing story of discovery\. Lots of pictures\!
