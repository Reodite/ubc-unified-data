Abstract: 

In a celebrated paper Gromov computed the topological entropy of endomorphisms of the complex projective space\. In a recent joint work with Junyi Xie and Tuyen Trung Truong we extended Gromov's arguments to the non\-archimedean case\. We shall present the objects involved in this setting and discuss the arithmetic difficulties to compute this entropy in general\.
