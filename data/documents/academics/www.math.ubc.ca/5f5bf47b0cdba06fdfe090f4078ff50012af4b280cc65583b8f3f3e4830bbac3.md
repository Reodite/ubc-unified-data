Abstract: 

The even filtration, introduced by Hahn\-Raksit\-Wilson, is a canonical filtration attached to a commutative ring spectrum which measures its failure to be even\. Despite its simple definition, the even filtration recovers many arithmetically important constructions, such as the Adams\-Novikov filtration of the sphere or the Bhatt\-Morrow\-Scholze filtration on topological Hochschild homology\. I will describe a linear variant of the even filtration which is naturally defined on associative rings as well as joint work with Raksit on the resulting extension of prismatic cohomology\.
