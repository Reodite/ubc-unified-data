Abstract: 

In this survey split over two talks, I shall review how – over 50 years – quadratic forms, and in particular Pfister forms, have been used to produce more and more examples of rationally connected varieties over the complex field which are not stably rational\. We shall start with the Artin\-Mumford examples on conic bundles over the plane and end with the work of Schreieder on hypersurfaces of small slope\.
