Abstract: 

 I will use the remarkable Stirling’s formula for N\! to introduce the asymptotic analysis of Riemann\-Hilbert problems\.  Then I will describe the kinetic theory of soliton gases\.  To accomplish this, I will explain by example some integrable nonlinear pdes, multi\-soliton solutions, and what is the mathematical meaning of “the formation of a soliton gas”\.   There will be movies\.
