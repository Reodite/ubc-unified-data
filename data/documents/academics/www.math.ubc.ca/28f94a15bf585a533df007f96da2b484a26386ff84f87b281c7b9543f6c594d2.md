Abstract: 

The distance set of a set E consists of all non\-negative numbers  
that represent distances between pairs of points in E\. If E is quantifiably  
large, how large is its distance set? How does the structure of E influence  
the distribution of its distances? Questions of this nature play a  
fundamental role in geometric measure theory\. This talk will report on  
joint work with Senthil Raani, investigating various properties of  
distances generated by sparse \(less than full\-dimensional\) Euclidean sets\.
