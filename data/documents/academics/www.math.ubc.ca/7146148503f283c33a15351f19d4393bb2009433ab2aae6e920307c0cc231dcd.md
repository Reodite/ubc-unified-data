Abstract: 

I will discuss my implementation of active learning methods in the math majors sections of matrix algebra \(Math 221\) and multivariable calculus \(Math 200\), covering some examples of the course materials I used, the students' response to the learning methods, and outcomes from the semester\.  I will also set out my plans for future iterations of the materials and their potential for use in large sections of the courses\.
