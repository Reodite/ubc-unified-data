Abstract: 

We obtain upper bounds for the Steklov eigenvalues of warped products $\\Omega\\times\_h\\Sigma$, where $\\Omega$ is a compact Riemannian manifold with boundary and $\\Sigma$ is a closed Riemannian manifold, under assumptions on the $L^p$\-norm of the warping function $h$\. The upper bounds that we obtain are very different depending on the dimension $n$ of the fiber $\\Sigma$ and the value of $p$\. In some cases, we obtain optimal upper bounds and stability estimates\. This is joint work with Jade Brisson, Bruno Colbois and Katie Gittins\.
