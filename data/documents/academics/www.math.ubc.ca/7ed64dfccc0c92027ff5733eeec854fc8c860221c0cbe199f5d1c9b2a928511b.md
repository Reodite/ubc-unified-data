Abstract: 

Satellite knots are a class of knots constructed via certain cut\-and\-paste operations; their knot Floer homology groups have been studied extensively and yielded many topological applications\.  In this talk, I will introduce a new technique to study the knot Floer homology of satellite knots, which significantly simplifies the computation compared to previous approaches\. Our method is built on the immersed\-curve technique introduced by Hanselman\-Rasmussen\-Watson\. This talk is based on joint work in progress with Jonathan Hanselman\.
