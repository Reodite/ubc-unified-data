Abstract: 

In this session, we present a pilot qualitative analysis from a Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\) project examining the integration of culturally responsive practices in undergraduate mathematics teacher education\. The project focuses on a redesigned capstone assignment in MATH 335 \(Introduction to Mathematics\), offered within the Indigenous Teacher Education Program \(NITEP\), where teacher candidates design and present lessons aligned with the BC K\-7 curriculum while engaging with culturally responsive and Indigenous\-informed pedagogies\. We will share early insights into how assignment design supports teacher candidates' pedagogical agency and engagement with Indigenous ways of knowing\. As ethics approval for full data sharing is pending, the session emphasizes instructional design considerations, pilot findings, and implications for improving undergraduate mathematics teaching practices\.
