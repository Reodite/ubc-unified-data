Abstract: 

Given a prime power $q \\equiv 1 \\pmod 4$, the Paley graph of order $q$ is the graph defined over $\\mathbb\{F\}\_q$ \(the finite field with $q$ elements\), such that two vertices are adjacent if and only if their difference is a square in $\\mathbb\{F\}\_q$\. A clique in the Paley graph over $\\mathbb\{F\}\_q$ is a subset of $\\mathbb\{F\}\_q$ that induces a complete subgraph, equivalently, a subset of $\\mathbb\{F\}\_q$ such that all pairwise differences are squares in $\\mathbb\{F\}\_q$\.

In this talk, I will report some new results related to cliques in Paley graphs, Paley\-like graphs, and their generalizations\. I will also discuss some interesting connections between Paley graphs and number theory\. Joint work with Shamil Asgarli, Andries E\. Brouwer, Sergey Goryainov, Seoyoung Kim, Greg Martin, Leonid Shalaginov, and Semin Yoo\.
