Abstract: 

Introduced in conjectural form 35 years ago and put on a solid foundation only 10 years ago, Arthur packets now play an inportant role in the Langlands program\. In this talk I will describe some fundamental properties of local Arthur packets, review a conjectural generalization due to Adams, Barbash and Vogan, made 30 years ago, and explain the current status of this conjecture\.
