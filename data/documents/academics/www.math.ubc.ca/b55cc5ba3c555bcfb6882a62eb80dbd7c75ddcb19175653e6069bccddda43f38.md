Abdul Ladha Science Student Centre

2nd Floor

Canada

FUSE \(FoS x SUS UBC Science Exchange\) is a research showcase celebrating the diverse research taking place across the UBC Faculty of Science\. Undergraduate students who conducted research over the summer with the Faculty of Science are invited to present their work through a research poster or e\-poster, with presentations adjudicated by Faculty of Science professors\.

Top presentations will receive a certificate signed by the adjudicators in recognition of their work\. Whether presenting or attending, FUSE is a great opportunity to share research, discover projects from across the Faculty, receive valuable feedback from faculty members, and connect with fellow student researchers and professors\.

**Eligibility:** Open to all undergraduate students who completed research under the UBC Faculty of Science during Summer 2026 \(funded or volunteer\)\.  
**Registration Deadline:** Sunday, September 13, 2026 at 11:59 PM
