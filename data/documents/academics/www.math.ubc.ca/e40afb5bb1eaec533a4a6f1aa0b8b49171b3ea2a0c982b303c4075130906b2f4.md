##  Undergraduate Students Sub\-Navigation

The Mathematics Department offers a large selection of courses in many areas of pure and applied mathematics which require various levels of mathematical sophistication\.

If you have just completed your first year in UBC Science, or if you are transferring to the Faculty of Science from another UBC Faculty, do not use the forms below\. You must apply using the [centralized process administered by the Faculty of Science](https://science.ubc.ca/students/degree/apply)\.

If you are in the Faculty of Science and have already declared a specialization, or if you are in the Faculty of Arts, you must submit an application directly to the department using the appropriate form below\. All applications will be considered in May\. The application forms below are open between May 1 and May 20\. Full details of admission requirements can be found in the [UBC Calendar](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/vancouver/index.cfm?tree=12,197,282,93)\.

Applying for a B\.A\. Mathematics Majors/Honours Program

The Department offers Bachelor of Arts \(B\.A\.\) majors and honours programs in Mathematics and in Mathematics combined with another subject, such as Economics, English, Linguistics, Music, or Philosophy\.

##### Admission Requirements:

B\.A\. students applying for admission need to complete at least 27 credits by the time of application\. B\.A\. students therefore typically apply at the end of their second year\. They must also meet the following requirements:

-   6 credits of first\-year calculus equivalent to MATH 100 and 101;
-   One of MATH 111 or 131;
-   One of MATH 200, 217, or 226;
-   MATH 220 \(students who complete MATH 131 or MATH 226 are exempt from MATH 220\);
-   3 credits that satisfy the Writing Component of the Research and Writing Requirement;
-   3 credits of the Computing Requirement chosen from CPSC 110 or CPSC 103\+107, or MATH 210\. Note that students who have only completed CPSC 103 will be considered\.

[BA Application Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3BG0fyWHvv6O6jA)

Note that students wishing to apply for programs that combine Mathematics and Economics need to apply through the [Vancouver School of Economics](https://economics.ubc.ca/study-here/)\.

Selection is based on the entire transcript, with a focus on the courses listed above\.

Applying for a B\.Sc\. Mathematics Majors/Honours Program

The Department offers Bachelor of Science \(B\.Sc\.\) Majors and Honours programs in Mathematics and in Mathematics combined with another subject, such as Economics, Physics, or Computer Science\.

##### Admission Requirements:

Students applying for admission by transfer from year 2 of the B\.Sc\. need to meet the following requirements:

-   6 credits of first\-year calculus equivalent to MATH 100 and 101;
-   One of MATH 111 or 131;
-   One of MATH 200, 217, or 226;
-   MATH 220 \(students who complete MATH 131 or MATH 226 are exempt from MATH 220\);
-   6 credits that satisfy the B\.Sc\. Communication Requirement;
-   3 credits of the Computing Requirement chosen from CPSC 110 or CPSC 103\+107, or MATH 210\. Note that students who have only completed CPSC 103 will be considered\.

Students currently in year 2 or beyond wishing to transfer to a B\.Sc\. Majors or Honours program in Mathematics or in Mathematics combined with another subject need to apply for admission\. Applications from students who have transferred to UBC after year 2 may be considered after their first term at UBC\.

[BSc Application Form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3BG0fyWHvv6O6jA)

Note that students wishing to apply for programs that combine Mathematics and another subject will also need to apply for the other subject, separately, through the appropriate department\.

Selection is based on the entire transcript, with a focus on the courses listed above\.

Students who have questions about Mathematics programs should consult one of the Mathematics Department advisors using the Advising Contact Form\.

[Advising Contact Form](https://www.math.ubc.ca/php/MathNet/UG-Form/)
