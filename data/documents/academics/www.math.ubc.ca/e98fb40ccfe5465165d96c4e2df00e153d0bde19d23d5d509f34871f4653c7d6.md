Abstract: 

Knot Floer homology is a powerful link invariant\. In its most general version, it is a bigraded chain complex over a polynomial ring F\[U,V\]\. In this talk, I will describe the structure theorem of such objects \- they are a direct sum of snake complexes and local systems \- and explain what information each summand carries about the knot\.
