Abstract: 

The directions determined by a subset $U \\subset AG\(2,p\)$ is the set of slopes formed by pairs of points from $U$\. For $U \= A \\times B$, a Cartesian product, we give a new lower bound on the number of directions determined by $U$\. Combining this result with estimates on exponential sums, we make progress on the Paley graph conjecture \(a double character sum estimate\)\.

When $A\=B$ is an arithmetic progression, we give an asymptotic formula for the number of directions\. Our method involves computing an asymptotic formula for the number of solutions to the Diophantine equation $ad\+bc \= p$\.

Joint work with Daniel Di Benedetto, Greg Martin, and J\\'ozsef Solymosi\.
