Abstract: 

 Let p be an odd prime and let $e\_p$ be its irregularity index\. If $$4e\_p\+8\\leq \\frac\{p\-1\}\{2\},$$ we construct a Galois representation with image in the diagonal torus of that lifts to a characteristic 0 representation unramified at all primes $\\ell\\neq p$ with image containing a finite index subgroup of $\\operatorname\{GSp\}\_4\(\\mathbb\{Z\}\_p\)$\.
