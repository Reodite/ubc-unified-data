In the community of mixing times, a famously wrong conjecture asserts that a sequence of ergodic Markov chains exhibits cutoff as soon as the product of their Poincare constant and mixing time tends to infinity\. In this talk, I will prove that this conjecture is actually valid, provided one replaces the Poincare constant with its natural non\-equilibrium version\. This is based on a \(very recent\) joint work with Francesco Pedrotti\.
