Abstract: 

The algebraic form of Hilbert's 13th Problem asks for the resolvent degree $\{\\rm rd\}\(n\)$ of a general polynomial $f\(x\) \= x^n \+ a\_1 x^\{n\-1\} \+ \\ldots \+ a\_n$ of degree $n$, where the coefficients $a\_1, \.\.\., a\_n$ are independent variables\. Here $\{\\rm rd\}\(n\)$ is the minimal integer $d$ such that every root of $f\(x\)$ can be obtained in a finite number of steps, starting with $\\mathbb C\(a\_1, \.\.\., a\_n\)$ and adjoining an algebraic function in $\\leqslant d$ variables at each step\. Here $\\mathbb C$ is the field of complex numbers\. It is known that $\{\\rm rd\}\(n\) \= 1$ for every $n \\leq 5$\. It is not known whether or not $\{\\rm rd\}\(n\)$ is bounded as $n$ tends to infinity; it is not even known whether or not $\{\\rm rd\}\(n\) &gt; 1$ for any $n$\. Recently Farb and Wolfson defined the resolvent degree $\{\\rm rd\}\_k\(G\)$, where $G$ is a finite group and $k$ is a field of characteristic $0$\. In this setting $\{\\rm rd\}\(n\) \= \{\\rm rd\}\_\{\\mathbb C\}\(S\_n\)$, where $S\_n$ is the symmetric group on n letters\. In this talk I will define $\{\\rm rd\}\_k\(G\)$ for any field $k$ and any algebraic group $G$ over $k$\. Surprisingly, Hilbert's 13th Problem simplifies when $G$ is connected\. My main result is that $\{\\rm rd\}\_k\(G\) \\leq 5$ for an arbitrary connected algebraic group $G$ defined over an arbitrary field $k$\.
