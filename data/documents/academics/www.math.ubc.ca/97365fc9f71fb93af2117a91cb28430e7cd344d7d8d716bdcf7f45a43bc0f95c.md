Abstract: 

Let E be an elliptic curve over a number field F\. The group of F\-rational is a finitely generated abelian group and the study of this "Mordell–Weil" group is among the centrepieces in the study of the arithmetic of elliptic curves\. In this talk we will explain the role played by Iwasawa theory in studying the Mordell\-Weil groups along certain infinite Galois extensions of the number field\. We will also explain some of the recent results on the variation of certain invariants along families of Z\_p\-extensions of the number field\.
