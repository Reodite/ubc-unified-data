Abstract: 

*An overview of normal numbers from an analytic aspect*

In this talk, I will review the notion of normal numbers, and show its connection with uniform distribution modulo one and the strong law of large numbers\. Moreover, I will also present some analytical work which has been done on this subject\.

Please contact Caleb Marshall \([cmarshall@math\.ubc\.ca](mailto:cmarshall@math.ubc.ca)\) for Zoom information\.
