Abstract: 

Mathematical models are often used to describe, analyze, and understand scientific problems from other disciplines\. Occasionally, such interdisciplinary investigations also lead to challenges that provide interesting mathematical questions\. In this retrospective talk, I will describe some of the applications of mathematics to problems in cell biology, and mention a few of the mathematical questions that arose\. I will concentrate on models for the behaviour of living cells \(from their inner workings, to their motion, to the interactions of many cells to form a cluster\)\. Some of the models for cells can be connected to similar approaches to macroscopic biological systems, including swarms and flocks\. I wish to thank the many trainees that have contributed to this research over the years\.
