Abstract: 

Nonlocal diffusion equations are evolution equations which resemble the heat equation except that, instead of describing an ensemble of a large number of particles undergoing Brownian motion, the particles are instead governed by a jump process\. In this talk, I will explain a variational perspective on nonlocal equations, which is suggested by optimal transport as well as the second law of thermodynamics\. Time permitting, I will outline possible future applications to sampling algorithms for non\-log\-Sobolev distributions\.
