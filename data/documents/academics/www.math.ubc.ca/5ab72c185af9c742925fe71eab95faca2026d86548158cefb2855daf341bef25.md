##  Graduate Students Sub\-Navigation

Every year the Department of Mathematics celebrates excellence by presenting Graduate Research Awards to deserving Doctoral students for research done whilst at UBC\. If suitable nominees exist, a minimum of three awards are given, with at least one female winner, one winner in Applied Mathematics, and one winner in Pure Mathematics\.

#### **Award**

The award consists of an invitation to speak in the Department Colloquium, Applied Mathematics Colloquium, or equivalent venue; a certificate to be presented at the Department graduation reception; and a cheque\.

#### **Eligibility**

All Ph\.D\. students in Mathematics who have passed the candidacy exam, and have not already won the award\.

#### **Nominations**

A nomination is made by a research supervisor of the eligible student\. A nomination will consist of a

1.  Description of Research Contribution \(maximum 2 pages\) written by the nominee;
2.  a resume or CV;
3.  an unofficial UBC transcript of grades;
4.  two letters of reference including one from a research supervisor\.

**Deadline for nominations**: October 15th

**Announcement of award**: November 1st
