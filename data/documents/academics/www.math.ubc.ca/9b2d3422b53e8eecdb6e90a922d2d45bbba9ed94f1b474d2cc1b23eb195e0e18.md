Abstract: 

A Kazhdan\-Lusztig atlas on a stratified variety is a structure that models the stratification of the variety on that of Schubert varieties\. We will describe a classification of Kazhdan\-Lusztig atlases on toric surfaces\. We will touch on topics such as pizzas, nutrition, toppings and braids\.
