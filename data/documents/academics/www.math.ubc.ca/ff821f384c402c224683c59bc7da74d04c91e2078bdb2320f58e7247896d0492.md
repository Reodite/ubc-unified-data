Abstract: 

We will discuss the metatheorem that if \\\(G\\\) is a locally compact and Hausdorff topological group that admits some kind of hierarchical decomposability then the Farrell–Tate cohomology \\\(\\widehat\{\\mathrm\{Ext\}\}\_G\(A,B\)\\\) vanishes whenever \\\(A\\\) and \\\(B\\\) are \\\(G\\\) \-modules such that \\\(A\\\) is \\\(FP\_\\infty\\\) and \\\(B\\\) is projective on restriction to the level zero subgroups of the hierarchy\. We’ll look at two special cases: first when \\\(G\\\) is solvable or linear and discrete, and secondly when \\\(G\\\) is a pro\-\\\(p\\\) group that is solvable of type \\\(FP\_\\infty\\\)\. The strategy in the non\-discrete case uses the new Clausen–Scholze condensed mathematics in which topological spaces are replaced by sheaves over the pro\-étale site of a point\.
