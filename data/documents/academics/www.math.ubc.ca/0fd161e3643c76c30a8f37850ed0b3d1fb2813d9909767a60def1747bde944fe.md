Abstract: 

Sub\-Gaussian random matrices are often studied and used in applications\. In many cases, their performance is captured by how close they are to an isometry \(i\.e\., preserves 2\-norm\) on the dataset\. Liaw et al\. showed a remarkable uniform concentration inequality for such deviations in 2017\. However, their result had a sub\-optimal dependence on the sub\-Gaussian parameter\. In this talk, I will present the above inequality in a more general form and with the optimal parameter dependence\. I will also discuss a new Bernstein type inequality, a new Hanson\-Wright inequality and a few applications of our results\.
