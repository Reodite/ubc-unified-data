Question: 

How do I submit an academic concession?

Answer: 

Academic concessions for final assignments and exams are handled through your Faculty Advising Office\. For in\-term concessions, fill in and submit the form [here](https://owncloud.math.ubc.ca/index.php/s/mumsWsljdjR1idJ) to your instructor\.
