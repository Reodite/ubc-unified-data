##  Undergraduate Students Sub\-Navigation

See the UBC Math Course Map for a helpful visualization of program pathways\.

[UBC Math Course Map](https://ubcmath.github.io/coursemap/)

Certain courses work especially well together\. There are course streams listed on the UBC Math Course Map\. If you are a Math Major, you are strongly recommended to select all the courses in one of the five listed streams, and then to fill out your remaining options as you choose\.
