Abstract: 

In this talk, I will first describe how the classical Dieudonne module of finite flat group schemes and p\-divisible groups can be recovered from crystalline cohomology of classifying stacks\. Then I will explain how to classify finite flat group schemes over a fairly general base by using classifying stacks and the formalism of prismatic F\-gauges due to Drinfeld and Bhatt\-\-Lurie\.
