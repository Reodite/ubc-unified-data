Abstract: 

In this talk, we consider the asymmetric simple exclusion process with open boundaries \(open ASEP\)\. We give an overview on recent results on mixing times for the open ASEP\. In particular, we discuss mixing times for the open ASEP at the triple point\. This talk is based on joint work with Patrik Ferrari\.
