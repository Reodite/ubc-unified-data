##### Harmonic Analysis/Fractals Hybrid Seminar

Abstract: 

*Two\-point patterns determined by curves*

Abstract: Given a smooth function $\\Phi \\colon \\mathbb\{R\} \\rightarrow \\mathbb\{R\}^d$, we ask whether every Borel subset of $\\mathbb\{R\}^d$ with sufficiently high Hausdorff dimension contains a pair of the form $\\\{x, x\+\\Phi\(t\)\\\}$ with $\\Phi\(t\) \\neq 0$\. We prove that a large class of \`\`curved” functions have this property while certain \`\`flat” functions do not\. This is joint work with Malabika Pramanik\.
