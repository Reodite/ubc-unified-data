Abstract: 

The SL\_2 Kakeya problem is a special case of the three\-dimensional Kakeya conjecture, and it is surprisingly connected to the restricted projection problem in geometric measure theory\. In this talk, I will first discuss a proof of SL\_2 Kakeya problem using regulus strips\. Then I will discuss the connection between SL\_2 Kakeya and the restricted projection theorem, and why regulus strip is the correct object to study\. Finally, I will discuss a related problem that helps to rule out the SL\_2 enemy scenario in Katz\-Zahl's improvement in three\-dimensional Kakeya conjecture\. This talk is based on joint work with Nets Katz and Joshua Zahl\.
