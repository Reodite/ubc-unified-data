Abstract: 

Classical enumerative geometry often involves identities between coherent and topological \(or motivic\) invariants\. For instance, the Deligne\-Milnor formula expresses the Euler characteristic of the vanishing cycles at an isolated hypersurface singularity in terms of the Jacobi algebra of the singularity\. In quadratic enumerative geometry, numerical invariants are refined into classes in the Grothendieck\-Witt ring of the base field\. On the coherent side, this refinement process involves Grothendieck duality, while on the motivic side, it involves stable motivic homotopy theory\. Both sides of the Deligne\-Milnor formula admit such a natural quadratic refinement\. In a joint work with Marc Levine and Vasudevan Srinivas, we compute both sides in a class of simple examples of singularities and show that the two sides do not match up: correction terms appear, whose origin is still mysterious\.
