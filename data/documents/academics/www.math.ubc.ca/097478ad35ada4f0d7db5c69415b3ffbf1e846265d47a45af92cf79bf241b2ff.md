Abstract: 

I will recall the notion in the title and will  
explain examples of relevance in the Langlands  
program\. It turns out that for mod p smooth  
representations of p\-adic groups this center  
contains little information \(in contrast to the case  
of complex coefficients studied by Bernstein\)\.  
Then I will discuss recent joint work with  
K\. Ardakov on a sheafified version of the center\.
