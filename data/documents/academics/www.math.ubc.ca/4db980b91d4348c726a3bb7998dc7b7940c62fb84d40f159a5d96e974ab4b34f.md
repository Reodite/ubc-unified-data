Abstract: 

I will report on recent progress in the study of a quasilinear equation involving the n\-Laplacian and an exponential nonlinearity in R^n \. In particular quantization issues for blow\-up masses in the non\-compact situation will be discussed and their relationship with Harnack inequalities of sup \+ inf type\. I will also present some new achievements concerning sharp sup \+ inf inequalities\.
