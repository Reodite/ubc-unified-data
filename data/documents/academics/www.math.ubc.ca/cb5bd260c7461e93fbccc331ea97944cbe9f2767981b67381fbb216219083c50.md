Abstract: 

Computational thinking is the process of formulating problems and their solutions in ways that computers can implement, and computational teaching is the set of instructional practices designed for computational thinking\. We will talk about a systematic approach for computational teaching across the undergraduate mathematics program at UBC including online computational environments, autograding software, open educational resources, and instructor training\. The organizational framework relies heavily on the Python and Jupyter ecosystem of software tools such as SciPy, JupyterLab, JupyterBook and nbgrader\.
