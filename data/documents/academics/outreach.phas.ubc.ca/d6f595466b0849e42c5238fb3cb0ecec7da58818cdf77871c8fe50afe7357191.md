Below are listed the exams \(with solutions\) from previous years\. Click on the year of your choice to view an exam or solution set\. Passwords are required to view the solutions: the intent is for you to work through the problems first\.

#### **To obtain password for viewing solutions**

\*In order to obtain password for accessing CAP High School Prize Exams from 2023 and older, you must request them by e\-mail from **[capexam@physics\.ubc\.ca](mailto:capexam@physics.ubc.ca)**\.  Please include the word “**password**” in the subject line\.  Please **do not send any other emails** to this account, as it will not be read by anyone\.  This account is intended to respond to requests for passwords only\.  Please note that **there is an intentional 24 hour delay** in the response so that there is some time to work through the problems before the solutions are available, so don’t fret if you don’t receive a response immediately\.

\*To access exams from 2024 –, the CAP Exam and solutions are available in one document, without password protection\.
