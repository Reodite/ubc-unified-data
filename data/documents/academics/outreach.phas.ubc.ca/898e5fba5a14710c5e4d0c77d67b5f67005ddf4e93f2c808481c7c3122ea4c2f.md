## Parking

There is NO parking adjacent to the Hennings Building, but there are \(pay\) parkades nearby\.

As of now, the closest parking lot is located at the old bus loop \(\~$3 an hour on weekdays\)

Alternatively, there is the B1 parking lot on Wesbrook Mall $4\.25 all day\. \(\~12 minute walk away\)

Click [here](https://parking.ubc.ca/) for the latest information on Visitor Parking from [UBC Parking Services](https://parking.ubc.ca/)\.
