**Welcome to our Science discovery lecture series page, “*How the Universe Works\!*“**

The [UBC Department of Physics &amp; Astronomy](https://phas.ubc.ca/) has partnered with the [Vancouver Public Library \(VPL\)](https://www.vpl.ca/) and the UBC Department of Earth, Ocean &amp; Atmospheric Sciences \(EOAS\) to bring you a fun and accessible science series for adults who are curious about science and want to know more about cutting\-edge research and new discoveries that affect our lives\. Presentations are by UBC Faculty of Science researchers and instructors, as well as local guest speakers, who come from a variety of science departments\.

We hope you enjoy this learning space that brings *you* to the science conversation\. 

**All lectures are held in\-person at the [Vancouver Public Library Central Branch](https://www.vpl.ca/hours-locations), in the Alice Mackay room \(downstairs\)\.**
