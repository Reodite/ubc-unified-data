We are excited to use this blog to share our upcoming events, fun &amp; educational hands\-on activities, “behind\-the\-scene” stories, and profiles of physicists and astronomers in the department\. Make sure to watch this space for more to come – and stay in the loop by subscribing to our [Outreach Program newsletter](http://eepurl.com/g3ZQ5)\.

Today we want to give you a very short preview of what you will see at our booth at the upcoming [Vancouver Mini Maker Faire](http://makerfaire.ca/) on June 6/7 at the PNE Forum\. Hosted by the [Vancouver Maker Foundation](http://www.vancouvermakerfoundation.org/), VMMF is an annual event, probably the most awesome show and tell in the city\. Local Makers gather and showcase the things they made – from electronics, crafts, to 3D\-printed anything\. We are excited to be part of the VMMF this year together with the UBC Engineering Physics Program\.
