The Outreach Program has physics and astronomy challenges that students can do at home\. These activities are meant to be fully self\-guided by students above grade 8, but could also be completed by younger children with the some assistance from parents and teachers\. Each activity consists of a general introduction, summaries of physics concepts, videos, and online experiment simulations to support students’ learning experience\. All ages are welcome to join\!

**Question? Do feel free to message us via the comments section of the page, through DM on social media, or email [outreach@phas\.ubc\.ca](mailto:outreach@phas.ubc.ca)\. UBC physicists and astronomers will be available to answer questions from participants, guardians, and educators\. You will also be able to submit project designs or videos, which will be featured on our website and on social media\!** Check out the [@ubcphasoutreach](https://www.instagram.com/ubcphasoutreach/) on instagram to see other participants projects\. Follow us or check back here for updates and new challenges\.
