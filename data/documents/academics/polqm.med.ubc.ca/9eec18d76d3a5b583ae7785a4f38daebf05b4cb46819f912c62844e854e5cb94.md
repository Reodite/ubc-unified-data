Ensuring Quality in a Changing Diagnostic Landscape

On June 2023 we \(the UBC Program Office for Laboratory Quality Management or POLQM\) hosted a conference on medical laboratory quality management\.

This year we had focused on the Changing Diagnostic Landscape with particular reference to the experiences gained from the Covid\-19 pandemic and diagnostic testing being done outside the clinical laboratory\.

Some of the topics covered included *Considering risk, safety and laboratory utilization when ordering diagnostics*\. and *Lab\-community POCT interface\- the role of labs in supporting quality of community\-based testing\.*

We would like to thank all our participants, speakers, and contributors for making this event a success\.
