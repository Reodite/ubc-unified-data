### The Laboratory Quality Management program is an on\-line 20\-week course designed to provide knowledge, discussion, and expertise for those interested in quality management for clinical and research laboratories\.  
The course is highly valuable for those who are or intend to be laboratory quality professionals, and for those that work with quality management issues\.  
It is appropriate for technologists, physicians, administrators, and other laboratory professionals\.

## Course Format

Asynchronous, completely online via Canvas LMS, accommodating an audience based across many time zones, globally\. The program features readings, written assignments, online discussion/learning among the participants and course facilitators, quizzes, and a final examination\.

**Program Structure**

The full program consists of two 10\-week courses: LQM 101 and LQM 102\. Please note that the 10\-week duration covers instructional time only and excludes the additional week dedicated to writing the final exam\.

**Course Length**

Two 10 weeks courses \(LQM 101 course running from January to March; LQM 102 course running from April to June\), excludes 1 week for writing final exam\.

**Time Commitment** The course will require a time commitment of approximately 10 hours per week \(for reading, discussions, and assignments\.\)

Learning objectives will be logically ordered into modules and supported by multimedia content to deliver principles and reinforce applied practices in the laboratory setting\.

## Assessment

Assessment is based on discussion participation, assignments, quizzes and a final examination \(all conducted on\-line\.\)  
Minimum pass mark is 60%\.

## Requirements

It is strongly recommended that all course participants should have at least one of the following credentials \(BSc, BMLSc, MD, PhD, RT, MT, Diploma in Medical Laboratory Science technology\) and at least 2 years of work experience in a medical or research laboratory setting\.  
This course will be limited to 75 participants\.

## Refund Policy

Students can withdraw from the course within 14 days of the course start date\. Course fees will be refunded \(less a $300 e\-course materials and administration fee\)\. If cancellation occurs prior to the start of the program, only the administration fee \($50\) will be retained\.
