The Laboratory Quality Management Conference \(LQM Conference\) is now Lab Q Con\! Join us at the Paetzold Education Centre in Vancouver General Hospital on June 16th and 17th, 2025\. This year’s theme is Tools and Best Practices to Promote Diagnostic Quality\. Healthcare leaders from Canada and around the world will present their perspectives and learnings on improving diagnostic quality\.[Lab Q Con 2025](https://med-fom-polqm.sites.olt.ubc.ca/polqm-quality-conference/)
