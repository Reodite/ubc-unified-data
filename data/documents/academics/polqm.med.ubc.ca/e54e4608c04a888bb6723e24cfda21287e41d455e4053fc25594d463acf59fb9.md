### **[Save the date\! June 2025 – Laboratory Quality Conference – Vancouver, BC](https://polqm.med.ubc.ca/polqm-quality-conference/)** 

The UBC Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine’s Program Office for Laboratory Quality Management \(POLQM\) has hosted 9 conferences on medical laboratory quality management since 2008\. Themes and topics covered included

-   **Ensuring Quality in a Changing Diagnostic Landscape**
-   **Knowing your Customers**
-   **Meeting the Needs**
-   **Laboratory Quality in Challenging Times**
-   **Customer Satisfaction and the Medical Laboratory**
-   **Culture of Quality**
-   **Cost of Quality**
-   **Quality Management for Medical Laboratories**

POLQM conferences have provided opportunities for health professionals to interact and exchange ideas more readily and this was something many participants expressed\.

We would like to thank all our participants, speakers, and contributors for making these events a success\.

Please check the presentations available\.

The next POLQM conference will be held in June, 2025 in Vancouver, BC, Canada\.

See you next June\!
