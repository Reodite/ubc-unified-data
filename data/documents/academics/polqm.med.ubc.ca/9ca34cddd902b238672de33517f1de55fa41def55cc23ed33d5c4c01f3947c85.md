The first Certificate course on Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing \(AST\) and Interpretation started on January 15, 2024\. This course is a completely online, 12 week, asynchronous, mentored, discussion based course to provide students with an understanding of the core principles of AST and its interpretation  
Each week 1\-2 thought provoking questions will be posted on the learning management system discussion board which will be monitored and guided by a lead Subject Matter Expert \(SME\) who is currently practising in the field of microbiology\. https://polqm\.med\.ubc\.ca/ast/  
Students will interact with a different faculty lead/subject matter expert for each module of the course, and work through the curriculum together on a weekly basis\.

Visit our website for more details:  
[https://polqm\.med\.ubc\.ca/ast/](https://polqm.med.ubc.ca/ast/)
