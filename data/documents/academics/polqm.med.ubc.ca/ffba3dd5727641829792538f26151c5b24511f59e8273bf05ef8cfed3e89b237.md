The Laboratory Quality Management Conference \(LQM Conference\) is now **Lab Q Con\!** Join us at the Paetzold Education Centre in Vancouver General Hospital on **June 16\[th\] and 17\[th\], 2025**\. This year’s theme is Tools and Best Practices to Promote Diagnostic Quality\. Healthcare leaders from Canada and around the world will present their perspectives and learnings on improving diagnostic quality\.

**Registration closed\!** If you are unable to make it to the conference, Zoom registration is also available\.

Scheduled talks at Lab Q Con are available on the [Schedule](https://polqm.med.ubc.ca/lab-q-con-2025-schedule/) page\.

**Conference address:**Room JPPS 1891 LT, [Paetzold Health Education Centre\_\(MAP AND DIRECTIONS\)](https://med-fom-polqm.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/06/Paetzold_Education_room_MAP-AND-DIRECTIONS.pdf) at Jim Pattison Pavilion, in the Vancouver General Hospital, 899 W 12th Ave, Vancouver, BC V5Z 1M9\.
