Canadian Society for Medical Laboratory Science\(CSMLS\) will grant Continuing Education Credits to their members who successfully complete the Laboratory Quality Conference \(LABQCON\) 2025 and receive proof of Certification\.

The Laboratory Quality Conference  2025 was approved for 14\.3 PEP hours\. CSMLS file number is 5367\-25\.

This assessment has been completed in compliance with the process described on the CSMLS website:
