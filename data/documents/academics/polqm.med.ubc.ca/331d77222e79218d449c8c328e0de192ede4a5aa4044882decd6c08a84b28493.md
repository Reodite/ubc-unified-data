#### Requirements

Students should have a foundational knowledge of microbiology before considering enrollment\.

This course is self\-paced\. Students can work on your own pace and complete this course within 365 days\.

#### Tuition 

**Program fee**: $750 per course \(AST 101 or AST 102\); $1500 for the program\.

Course fee includes required electronic course materials, standards, and examinations\.  
Upon successful completion of the course, an electronic certificate will be provided\. If you require a hard copy certificate, a CAD $30 shipping fee will be charged for Canada; outside Canada shipments will be charged a CAD $60 shipping fee\.

#### Schedule

**Course starts:** On\-demand \(Leraners can join anytime\)  
**Duration: This program allows 365 days of unlimited access\. The program consists of two core courses—AST 101 and AST 102—with each course structured to take approximately six weeks to complete\. While the standard track allows you to move through the full program in about 12 weeks, you also have the freedom to learn entirely at your own pace\. If you need more time, you can take up to one year from your initial enrollment to complete both courses, ensuring you can balance your studies with your other commitments\.**

-   You will receive course website access invitation one week after you register\.
-   You must finish the course within 365 days\.
-   This course is fully self\-directed\.

#### Registration and Payment

You will be directed to UBC’s e\-payment site for proceeding the online payment by VISA, MasterCard or domestic Debit Card\.

**[Registration is now open\.](https://pathology-payment.med.ubc.ca/antimicrobial-susceptibility-testing-and-interpretation-course-101/)**

Your registration information will be collected while you process the payment online\. The registration will be finished once the payment is received\. Within one week of completing registration, you will receive the course website access invitation email from UBC\.  
**Payment due at time of registration\.**

#### Cancellation

For those participants who withdraw from the course up to 14 days after the course commencement, tuition fee will be refunded, less the cost of course materials \(CAD$100\) and administration fee \(CAD$100\)\.

No refunds after 14 days of course commencement\.
