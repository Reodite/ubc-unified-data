### The Laboratory Quality Management \(LQM\) 2026 program started on January 14, 2026 and ended on June 2nd\. Registration for the next LQM course starts in fall\. We are updating the course\. Please check back this August\.

**Contact: Maggie Ma, Program Administrator**

UBC Program Office for Laboratory Quality Management

Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine,

University of British Columbia

G401, 2211 Wesbrook Mall,  
Vancouver BC Canada V6T2B5

E\-mail: [lqm@pathology\.ubc\.ca](mailto:lqm@pathology.ubc.ca)

#### Requirements

It is recommended that all course participants should have at least one of the following credentials \(BSc, BMLSc, MD, PhD, RT, MT, Diploma in Medical Laboratory Science technology\) and at least 2 years of work experience in a medical or research laboratory setting\.

Students must be able to commit approximately 10 hours per week \(reading, discussion, and assignments\) to successfully complete this 20\-week course that runs January to June\.

#### Tuition 2027

**Course fee**: CAD 3300

We are updating the LQM course\. Registration for the next LQM program is open in mid\-August 2026\.

Registrations after December 28th will be considered if space is still available and a rush processing fee of CAD $100 will be added to the regular course fee\.  
Course fee includes required electronic course materials, standards, and examinations\. Course materials are non\-refundable once the course starts\.  
Upon successful completion of the course, an electronic certificate will be provided\. If you require a hard copy certificate, a CAD $30 shipping fee will be charged for Canada; outside Canada shipments will be charged a CAD $60 shipping fee\.

#### Schedule

**Course starts:** January, 2027

**Course schedule**

LQM 2027 course schedule will be available in September, 2026\.

#### Registration and Payment

Register is open in mid\-August\. You will be directed to UBC’s e\-payment site for proceeding the online payment by VISA, MasterCard or domestic Debit Card\.

Your registration information will be collected while you process the payment online\. The registration will be finished once the payment is received\.  
**Payment due at time of registration\.**  
If you want to pay the course fee by cheque or money order, please fill in the [Application Form](https://med-fom-polqm.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/07/UBC-LQM-2026-program-Application-Form.pdf) \(pdf\) then mail it with the check or money order to:

**Vancouver BC, Canada**  
**V6T2B5**

Please make the cheque or money order payable to **UBC POLQM**

#### Registration deadline

**Dec 28, 2026\. After December 28 and before January 7, 2027, registrations will be considered if space is still available and a rush processing fee of CAD $100 will be added to the regular course fee\.**

Enrolment is limited to the first 75 participants\.

#### Cancellation

For those participants who withdraw from the course within 14 days of course commencement, funds will be returned, less the cost of course materials \(CAD$300\) and administration fee \(CAD$300\.00\)\.  
If cancellation occurs prior to January 13, 2027, only the administration fee will be retained\.
