Interview with Dr\. Noble in the CSA In Health Newsletter Issue 02 Winter 06\.

This [pdf\-format of a PowerPoint presention](https://med-fom-polqm.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/11/noble17043june2009.pdf) was given at a workshop \(June 2009 – Leuven Belgium\) of the EuGeneTest, the EU\-funded network of excellence for European genetics testing laboratories and PT providers\.  
The topic was to look at standards for quality and competence for proficiency testing providers\.  
The subject was the new creation of an ISO standard 17043 and where it would fit as a measure that EuGenTest providers could consider\.
