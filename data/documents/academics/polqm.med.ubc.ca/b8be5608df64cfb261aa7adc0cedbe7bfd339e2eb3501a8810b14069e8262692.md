Laboratory Quality Management Conference

On October 28\-30, 2015 we \(the UBC Program Office for Laboratory Quality Management or POLQM\) hosted the 2015 POLQM Laboratory Quality Management Conference in Vancouver, BC, Canada\.

Topics covered included:

-   **Health – Quality – Law**
-   **Costs of Quality**
-   **Managing Risk**
-   **Conflict Resolution positive impact on Culture of Quality**
-   **Opportunities in Quality Activities – Local and Global**
-   **Modern Tools for the Modern Quality Manager**
-   **International Activities in Laboratory Quality**
-   **Laboratory Safety is a Quality Imperative**
-   **Communicating Quality to your Staff**
-   **Preparing for a new Accreditation**
-   **Celebrating Quality**

and many more\. Please check the Presentations on this page’s sidebar\.
