## Call for Submissions

Would you like to give a talk at Lab Q Con 2025? We are now accepting submissions for talks surrounding our theme, Tools and Best Practices to Promote Diagnostic Quality\. We would greatly appreciate your valuable information and insight\.

Talks can be given in person at Vancouver General Hospital or over Zoom\.

Please include the following in your submission:

-   Title of your talk
-   A brief summary of the topic \(we recommend the SAR format – describe the **S**ituation, then discuss the **A**ctions you and your team took or research performed, and finally show the **R**esults of those actions\)
-    Links to any associated publications

Submissions can be sent to lqmconference@pathology\.ubc\.ca until the deadline, **February 28, 2025**\.
