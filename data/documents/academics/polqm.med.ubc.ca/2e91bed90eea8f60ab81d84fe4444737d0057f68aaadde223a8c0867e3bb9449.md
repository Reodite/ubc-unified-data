On November 2019 we \(the UBC Program Office for Laboratory Quality Management or POLQM\) hosted a conference on medical laboratory quality management\.

This year we had focused on “Meeting the Needs” with particular reference to Crosby and his definition of Quality as Meeting Requirements and the Measurement of Quality as the Price of Nonconformance, which we modified to the ***Costs and Consequences of Poor Quality\.***
