## What we do

At the Program Office for Laboratory Quality Management \(POLQM\), our mission is to address the growing global need for skilled laboratory quality professionals in alignment with ISO 15189:2022 standards for medical laboratory quality and competence\. To support healthcare and laboratory systems across Canada and internationally, we provide a comprehensive suite of educational programs, training, and professional services\.

Beyond instruction, POLQM actively advances the field through academic research, scholarly publications, advanced education, and expert quality consultations to help organizations achieve and sustain laboratory excellence\.

## Our team

Founded in 2002 within the Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine at the University of British Columbia, the Program Office for Laboratory Quality Management \(POLQM\) was created to meet the rising demand for skilled laboratory quality professionals ahead of international standard requirements\. Over two decades later, as global interest in accreditation under ISO 15189:2022 and related standards continues to expand, POLQM remains at the forefront of quality education and consultation\.

The dedicated team delivers a comprehensive suite of offerings, including the signature UBC Certificate Course in Laboratory Quality Management, self\-paced courses like Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing and Interpretation, international workshops, and the Vancouver\-based Laboratory Quality Management conference\. Through continuous research, academic publications, advanced training, and expert consultations, the POLQM team empowers laboratory professionals worldwide to achieve and sustain excellence in quality management\.

**Dr\. Lucy Perrone**

Dr\. Lucy A\. Perrone is a diplomatic and value\-centric leader and has more than 20 years’ experience at the international level strengthening health and laboratory systems in more than 30 countries\.

Dr\. Dr\. Perrone currently holds the Donald B\. Rix Professorship in Laboratory Quality, and is an Associate Professor in the Faculty of Medicine in the Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine at the University of British Columbia in Vancouver, Canada\. Dr\. Perrone is the Chair of the Canadian Microbiology Proficiency Testing Program \(ISO 17043:2022 accredited\), and the Director of the Program Office for Laboratory Quality Management\. Dr\. Perrone is a member of the Canadian Standards Association and the ISO working group TC2\-12 which shapes the ISO 15189:2022 standard\.

## Ambassadors and Partners

At the heart of POLQM’s global impact is a dynamic network of international partners, healthcare organizations, and dedicated regional ambassadors\. By collaborating with leading academic institutions, accreditation bodies, standards organizations, and public health agencies, POLQM bridges the gap between international quality standards and real\-world laboratory practices\.

Our regional ambassadors act as vital champions on the ground, advocating for ISO 15189 compliance, facilitating local training, and guiding healthcare professionals toward quality excellence within their respective regions\. Together with our global development and clinical partners, these ambassadors help extend POLQM’s educational reach, foster international knowledge\-sharing, and empower medical laboratories around the world to build sustainable cultures of quality and safety\.
