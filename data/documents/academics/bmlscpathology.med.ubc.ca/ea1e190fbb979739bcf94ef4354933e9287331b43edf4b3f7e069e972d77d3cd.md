Prospective Students

Students enter at Year 3 and complete the degree over two years, taken as a **Standard Timetable**\. Graduates finish having fulfilled most prerequisites for medical, dental, or graduate school\. For full course descriptions, see [UBC’s Academic Calendar](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/course-descriptions/subject/pathv)\.

Year 3 — 31–32 cr

Year 4 — 30 cr

[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/home-page/prospective-students/curriculum/) Fixed Standard Timetable — no electives to plan

Courses are grouped below by subject area\. Year 3 is **31 or 32 credits**: students who haven’t completed `CHEM_V 211` before entering take it in third year \(a 4\-credit course\), which brings the year to the higher total\. Course codes link to a syllabus where one is published\.

PATH\_V 438 · 6 credits

## Directed Studies — do real research

PATH\_V 438 gives students hands\-on research experience\. Projects run in the laboratory of a Faculty member of the Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine, who supervises\. You help design the project, perform the experimental work, and analyze the data\.

[Learn more about PATH 438 Directed Studies →](https://pathology.ubc.ca/pathology-438/)
