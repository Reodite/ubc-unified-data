Prospective Students

# International *applicants*

Applications from qualified international students are accepted\. Here’s what you’ll need to meet, and where to get support before and after you arrive\.

What you need to meet

## Three sets of requirements

International applicants must meet UBC’s language and general admission requirements **in addition to** the requirements for the BMLSc Program\.
