# Entrance *requirements*

Applicants must meet UBC’s [general admission requirements](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/) and complete every prerequisite from one of the two entry routes below \(or be completing them at the time of application\)\. Students enter the BMLSc at Year 3 and complete two years of study\. Meeting the criteria doesn’t imply or guarantee admission\.

[https://sciencecoop\.ubc\.ca/prospective/apply/bmlsc](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/bmlsc)[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/home-page/prospective-students/entrance-requirements/) Co\-op option available

Route 2 / Certified MLT

## For certified MLTs

Applicants on this route must hold all of the following:

1.  Completion of the **Medical Laboratory Technologist \(general\) diploma** of the Canadian Society of Medical Laboratory Science \(CSMLS\), or an equivalent international diploma\.
    
2.  Completion of UBC `CHEM 233` \+ `CHEM 235`, **or** `CHEM 203` \+ `CHEM 213` \+ `CHEM 245` — Organic Chemistry \(or equivalent from another institution\)\.
    
3.  `CHEM 211` is not required but is **strongly recommended** for admission\. Note this course is a degree requirement\.
    

For more on medical laboratory technologists and existing MLT training programs, see the [FAQ section](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/home-page/faqs/)\. Course equivalents at B\.C\. colleges and universities are listed in the [BC Transfer Guide](http://bctransferguide.ca/)\.
