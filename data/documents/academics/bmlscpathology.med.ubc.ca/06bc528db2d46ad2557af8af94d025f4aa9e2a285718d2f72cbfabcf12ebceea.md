# BMLSc *Co\-op*

Alternate your studies with paid work terms in academic, clinical, and industrial laboratories — supervised by practising professionals, in partnership with UBC Science Co\-op\.

[Apply via Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/bmlsc) [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/co-op-program/) [Read Vivian’s story](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/co-op-program/#story)

What is BMLSc Co\-op?

## Real lab work, woven into your degree

Minimum average B− *68%* Cumulative, good standing

Eligible Year 3 Once you’ve completed the Year 3 Standard Timetable

Work terms start May · Sept · Jan Flexible placement timing

In partnership with [Science Co\-op](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/employers/why) UBC Vancouver

The BMLSc Co\-op program prepares qualified students for careers in medical laboratory science through work placements supervised by practising professionals\. Developed in partnership with the [UBC Science Co\-op Program](https://sciencecoop.ubc.ca/prospective/apply/bmlsc), it’s open to BMLSc students who have completed the Year 3 Standard Time Table \(STT\)\.

Admission is based on academic performance and employment suitability, usually determined through an application and interview\. You might work as a research assistant or technical support staff in an academic, clinical, or industrial lab\. Being admitted to Co\-op doesn’t guarantee a placement in every work term\.

## Vivian’s story

Co\-op · Summer [Vivian, a 4th\-year BMLSc Co\-op student](https://med-fom-bmlscpathology.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/07/Vivian.png)

**Vivian** 4th\-year BMLSc · Co\-op at ALS Global \(Geochemistry\)

Co\-op has helped me grow both personally and professionally, giving me a clearer sense of direction in my career\.

What do you do in your current Co\-op placement?

At ALS Geochemistry, a laboratory group that provides analytical testing services for the mining industry, I work as a laboratory assistant, assisting with various tasks related to the analysis of mineral samples\. This includes sample preparation followed by different digestion procedures such as acid leaching and fluoride fusions\. I also operate instruments for elemental determination within the digested samples while evaluating QC checks and calibration standards\.

How is Co\-op helping your personal growth?

Co\-op has significantly enhanced my social and communication skills through day\-to\-day interactions with my mentors and supervisors\. My Co\-op experiences have also strengthened my confidence in both technical and soft skills as I became more familiar with the professional working environment\.
