## Preparing Future Leaders in Laboratory Science

The **Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Science \(BMLSc\)** at UBC offers an immersive, hands\-on learning experience in one of North America’s leading laboratory science programs\. Students train in laboratories within the [Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine](https://pathology.ubc.ca/), gaining the practical skills and scientific expertise needed to succeed in a rapidly evolving field\.

Our graduates pursue careers in **research**, **biotechnology**, **healthcare**, and **industry**, while many continue on to graduate studies, medicine, and other professional programs\. With small cohort sizes, personalized mentorship, and intensive laboratory training, the BMLSc Program provides a strong foundation for a wide range of opportunities in the life sciences\.

## Hands\-on training in well\-equipped, expert\-guided environments

Students learn in laboratories within UBC’s [Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine](https://pathology.ubc.ca/), in a program that emphasizes practical skills alongside theoretical knowledge\.

-   Master advanced laboratory techniques and scientific methods through extensive hands\-on training
-   Develop skills to analyze data, solve problems, and make evidence\-based decisions
-   Learn to design experiments, interpret results, and communicate scientific findings with confidence
-   Work closely with faculty mentors in a collaborative and supportive learning environment
-   Build professional, ethical, and teamwork skills that prepare you for research, healthcare, and biotechnology careers

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Program Overview** **Admissions &amp; Applying** **Medical Laboratory Technology**

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) What is Medical Laboratory Science?**

It’s the study of the scientific principles behind the work of hospital and medical research labs — spanning pathology, histology, medical microbiology, clinical chemistry, hematology, toxicology, and immunopathology\. The BMLSc focuses on science from a human\-health perspective and bridges these fields so you learn to work across disciplines\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) How is the program taught?**

Through lectures, seminars, small\-group problem\-based tutorials, medical case analysis, and hospital\-lab site visits\. Many courses pair an hour of lecture with an hour of hands\-on lab; others have a dedicated lab period for techniques like sectioning, embedding, and staining tissues\. With just 24 students a year, you work closely with classmates and instructors\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) What skills will I gain?**

Over two years: medical microbiology \(e\.g\. bacterial ID\), tissue culture, molecular biology \(PCR, gel electrophoresis, Western blotting\), chromatography including FPLC, light and electron photomicrography, immunohistochemistry, and lab safety — plus oral presentation, critical appraisal of papers, and professional scientific communication\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) Is the program difficult?**

It’s challenging, but students with a genuine interest in human health and disease tend to thrive — on average, students raise their GPA by about a letter grade between entry and graduation\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) Where are classes held, and who teaches them?**

In the Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine at UBC Hospital on the Vancouver campus, taught by department faculty and other UBC experts — many of them practising pathologists engaged in current research\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) What career options do graduates have?**

Since 1980 the program has graduated over 700 students who’ve gone on to careers in healthcare, research, and biotechnology, and to further study in medicine, dentistry, and graduate programs\. [Explore career opportunities](https://file.pathology.ubc.ca/Career.html)\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) Can you evaluate my credentials or prerequisites before I apply?**

No\. Credentials, equivalencies, and transfer credits are assessed only once an application is submitted — that evaluation is part of the process\. It’s each applicant’s responsibility to confirm they meet the [minimum eligibility requirements](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/home-page/prospective-students/entrance-requirements/) beforehand\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) Do I need to complete all prerequisites before entry?**

Yes — all courses listed as entrance requirements must be finished before you enter the program\. If you have questions about a missing prerequisite, reach out through the Contact Us link\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) I am missing CHEM 211\. Can I complete it in Year 3?**

Yes\. If you haven’t completed CHEM 211 before entering, you’ll take it in term one of Year 3, as it’s a prerequisite for the CHEM 315 lab in term two\. Because of seating limits in CHEM 211, you’re strongly encouraged to finish it before entering the program\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) Can I take BMLSc PATH courses while completing prerequisites?**

No — BMLSc Pathology \(PATH\) courses are restricted to students enrolled in the program, and they aren’t offered during the Summer session\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) Can I take electives in the program?**

The Year 3 curriculum follows a standard timetable with no room for electives\. In Year 4, you may take additional courses that fit the standard timetable\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) Can I apply as a 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year student?**

Yes — provided you’ve completed all first\- and second\-year prerequisites, you can apply from your second, third, or fourth year\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) Can I apply if I already have a degree or I’m an international student?**

You’re eligible if you’ve completed the prerequisites, but enrolment is limited and qualified applicants usually exceed the seats\. Priority goes to qualified applicants who don’t yet hold an undergraduate degree, and to Canadian citizens and permanent residents\. Degree holders and international applicants are considered only if seats remain\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) How many students apply, and how many are admitted?**

Applications range from about 50 to 125 each year, and facility limits cap admission at a maximum of 24 students\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) Is there a minimum GPA?**

No\. There’s no minimum GPA requirement — each applicant is assessed individually on their academic record\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) What was the GPA cut\-off last year?**

The minimum accepted GPA shifts each year with the applicant pool\. Previous years’ figures aren’t published, since they don’t indicate what may be accepted in the year you apply\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) When will I hear back about my application?**

Applicants are contacted in early to mid\-May\. You can check your status any time through the Applicant Service Centre \(ASC\)\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) What is a Certified Medical Laboratory Technologist \(MLT\)?**

An MLT is a member of the healthcare team who performs laboratory investigations used to diagnose, treat, and prevent disease — analysing specimens such as blood, urine, and tissue\. They’re the people who take your sample at a lab when you have medical tests\.

**[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/) Is the BMLSc the same as an MLT program?**

No\. Completing an MLT diploma lets you write the Canadian Society for Medical Laboratory Science \(CSMLS\) certification exam\. UBC’s BMLSc does not award the MLT diploma and does not prepare you to write that exam — MLT diploma programs are offered separately at institutions across BC\.
