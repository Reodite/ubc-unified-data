# Contact *us*

Questions about the BMLSc — admissions, prerequisites, or the program itself? The program office is happy to help\.

Three ways to reach us

[Email bmlsc@pathology\.ubc\.ca The best route for most questions\. We aim to reply within a few working days\.](mailto:bmlsc@pathology.ubc.ca) [Phone 604 822 7108 Monday to Friday during UBC office hours\.](tel:+16048227108)

Visit UBC Hospital, Koerner Pavilion G110 – 2211 Wesbrook Mall  
Vancouver, BC V6T 2B5

Check the FAQs first — you may get a faster answer

Most questions we receive are already answered on the [FAQs page](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/home-page/faqs/) — including minimum GPA, missing prerequisites, transfer credit, and when applicants hear back\. Note that the program can’t pre\-assess credentials or transfer credit before an application is submitted\.
