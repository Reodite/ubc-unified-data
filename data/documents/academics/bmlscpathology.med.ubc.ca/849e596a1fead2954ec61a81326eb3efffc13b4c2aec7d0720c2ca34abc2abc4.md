# Research *opportunities*

Two ways to do real research in the Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine — one over the summer, one built into Year 4 of the BMLSc\.

Summer

## Summer Student Fellowship Program

Research or clinical experience in laboratory medicine, supervised and mentored by the Department’s faculty\.

Open to undergraduate &amp; medical students

Year 4 · Optional course

## PATH\_V 438 Directed Studies

A full research project of your own, carried out under a faculty supervisor — from research design through to data analysis\.

BMLSc students · 6 credits

Over the summer

## Summer Student Fellowship Program

The Summer Student Fellowship Program provides undergraduate and medical students with research or clinical experiences in laboratory medicine, under the supervision and mentorship of the Department’s faculty\.

[Program details &amp; how to apply](https://pathology.ubc.ca/education-resource/summer-student-fellowship-program/) [Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://bmlscpathology.med.ubc.ca/home-page/current-students/research-opportunities/)

In Year 4

## PATH\_V 438 Directed Studies

In Year 4 of the BMLSc, you can enrol in an optional directed studies course\. Under the guidance of a faculty supervisor, you carry out and participate in **all aspects** of a research project — not just the bench work\.

### What “all aspects” means

Design → Experiment → Analyse

01

Research design

You take part in shaping the project from the start — the question being asked, and how it will be tested\.

02

Experimentation

You carry out the experimental work yourself, in a faculty member’s laboratory\.

03

Data analysis

You analyse the results and work out what they show — the part that turns experiments into findings\.

In practice the three overlap and repeat: analysis usually shapes the design of the next experiment\.
