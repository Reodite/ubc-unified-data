Clinical Assistant Professor 

Clinic:  [Stroke](https://www.vancouverstrokeprogram.com/) @ Vancouver General Hospital

Biography &amp; Research Interest:

Dr\. Murphy received her MD from UBC in 1998\. She completed her neurology residency, certification in clinical neurophysiology and certification by the American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology\. She was appointed to the UBC Department of Medicine and VGH Division of Neurology and she joined the Vancouver Stroke Program in 2003\. Appointed in 2009 to the RCPSC Examination Board in Neurology, she has an interest in resident education and served as Regional Representative, as well as Vice Chair, for the RCPSC Specialty Committee\. She is a Clinical Assistant Professor at UBC and a fellow of the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada \(RCPSC\)\.
