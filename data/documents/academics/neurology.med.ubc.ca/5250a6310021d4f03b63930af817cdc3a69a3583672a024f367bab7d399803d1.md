Clinical Associate Professor 

Clinic:  Stroke @ Vancouver General Hospital

Biography &amp; Research Interest:

Dr\. Samuel Yip received his PhD and MD degree from UBC\. He completed his neurology residency at UBC, followed by fellowship training in Stroke and Cerebrovascular Disease at University of California, San Diego\. His clinical and research interests are quality assurance of stroke care\.
