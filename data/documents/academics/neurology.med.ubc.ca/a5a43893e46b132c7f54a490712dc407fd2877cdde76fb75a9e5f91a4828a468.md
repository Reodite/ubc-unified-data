The UBC Neurology Academic Enhancement Fund \(AEF\) supports vital activities in research and education to improve the neurological health of individuals locally and globally\. By expanding academic capacity within the Division of Neurology, the AEF will prepare future physician leaders and accelerate patient care through groundbreaking discoveries and knowledge translation of diseases of the nervous system\. Support from our Faculty members and donors is critical to sustain this important work\.

#### **SUPPORT THE NEUROLOGY ACADEMIC ENHANCEMENT FUND**

Individual donations are eligible to receive charitable tax receipts and contributions made through corporations are eligible to receive a business acknowledgment letter\. If you wish to receive a business acknowledgment letter, please do not make contributions through your corporation online\.

To make donation or contribution through your corporation by cheque, please make it payable to the **University of British Columbia, Faculty of Medicine with a memo: Neurology AEF**\. If you wish to receive a business acknowledgment letter, include a note with attention to Alana Schultz stating the name of your corporation and request for the letter\. Mail the cheque to:
