Dr\. Jon Stoessl is the recipient of Graydon S\. Meneilly Award for Excellence in Mentoring 2020\. This award recognizes a faculty member for their exemplary leadership as a mentor, fostering excellence in others \(faculty, residents, students or staff\) and/or advancing the equity agenda of the Department\. Dr\. Jon Stoessl is also the recipient of 2020 FOM Distinguished Achievement Award for Service to the University and Community\. https://www\.med\.ubc\.ca/news/2020\-faculty\-of\-medicine\-award\-recipients/
