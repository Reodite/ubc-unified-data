Dr\. Dvir Dori MD, MSc  
Specialty: General Neurology, Neuromuscular  
Clinic: Complex Neuromuscular Clinic, Victoria General Hospital  
  

Biography and Research Interest:

Dr\. Dvir Dori, MD, MSc, is a neuromuscular neurologist at Island Health and Head of the Complex Neuromuscular Clinic at Victoria General Hospital, Canada\. He is a Clinical Assistant Professor at the University of British Columbia and a researcher at Baycrest Health Sciences\. Dr\. Dori earned his MD and MSc in Neurobiology, completed residency training in Neurology, and subsequently completed three fellowship programs in Neuromuscular Medicine, Neurogenetics, and Cognitive and Behavioral Neurology at McGill University and the University of Toronto\. He is also a graduate of advanced studies in sports medicine\. His clinical and research interests focus on neuromuscular disorders, neurodegeneration, and brain–muscle interactions\. He is certified in electromyography by the Canadian Society of Clinical Neurophysiologists \(CSCN\) and the American Board of Electrodiagnostic Medicine \(ABEM\), and has successfully completed the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada examinations in Adult Neurology\.
