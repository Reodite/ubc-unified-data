### Biography:

Jan D\. Lünemann is Professor of Neurology, the UBC Chair in Multiple Sclerosis \(MS\) and Autoimmune Neurology Research, and Director of the MS and Neuromyelitis Optica Program at the University of British Columbia in Vancouver, Canada\. Before joining UBC, he was Professor of Neurology and Director of the MS and Neuroimmunology Outpatient Clinic and its associated Clinical Trial Centre at the University Hospital Münster, Germany\.

Dr\. Lünemann received his medical degree from Humboldt University in Berlin and completed his neurology residency at Charité – University Hospital Berlin\. He subsequently conducted postdoctoral research in cellular immunology at the U\.S\. National Institutes of Health \(NIH\) and Rockefeller University in New York\. Prior to his appointment in Münster, he served as Assistant Professor and Head of the Department of Neuroinflammation at the University of Zürich and as Senior Consultant in Neurology at the University Hospitals of Zürich and Basel, Switzerland\.

His research focuses on the immunological mechanisms underlying MS and other autoimmune neurological diseases, with the goal of translating discoveries into innovative immune\-based therapies\. Dr\. Lünemann is a Fellow of the European Academy of Neurology \(FEAN\) and the American Neurological Association \(FANA\), serves on the European Academy of Neurology Scientific Panels on MS and Neuroimmunology, and has received several international awards for his contributions to MS and neuroimmunology research\.\.
