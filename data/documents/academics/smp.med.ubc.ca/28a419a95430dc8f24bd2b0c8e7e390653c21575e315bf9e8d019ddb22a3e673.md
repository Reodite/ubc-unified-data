## [Dr\. Gregory Costello appointed new Clinical Skills, Site Director](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/2025/08/18/dr-gregory-costello-appointed-clinical-skills-site-director/)

August 18, 2025 (Monday, August 18th, 2025, 9:48 am)

Dr\. Gregory Costello has been appointed the new Clinical Skills, Site Director for the Southern Medical Program \(SMP\)\. Dr\. Costello is a family physician, Clinical Assistant Professor with the UBC Department of Family Practice, and SMP alumnus \(Class of 2016\)\.

Posted in [SMP News](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/category/news/)
