The Southern Medical Program is one of four regional sites for the UBC MD Undergraduate Program\. The distributed education model provides excellent training opportunities in communities across the BC Interior\.

[Image](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/93/2018/11/Admissions-Feature-1-768x307.jpg)

### MD Undergraduate Program

The MD Undergraduate Program is based on a distributed model of education\. Each year, the Faculty of Medicine selects 308 students to train across the province\.

[Learn more](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/admissions/md-undergraduate-program/)

[Image](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/93/2018/11/Admissions-Feature-2-768x307.jpg)

### Distributed Education Sites

Our program provides education opportunities in 30 different communities and in\-depth exposure to the amazing work\-life balance the region has to offer\.

[Learn more](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/admissions/education-sites/)
