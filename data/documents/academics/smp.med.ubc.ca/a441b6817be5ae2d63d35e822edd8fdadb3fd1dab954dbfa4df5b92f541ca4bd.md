**Join the Southern Medical Program \(SMP\) students for the 4th annual SMP Student Golf Tournament at Sunset Ranch – Sunday, September 20, 2015\.**

***Message from Parm Brar and Kevin Janz, Class of SMP 2018***

We would like to formally invite you to the 4th annual SMP Student Golf Tournament\. This year, we are hoping to grow the numbers of golfers to 80\.

The tournament will once again be held at the [Sunset Ranch Golf and Country Club](http://sunsetranchbc.com/)\. It will be a fun event for all skill levels with a golf scramble format\. It’s an amazing opportunity for everyone to connect and have fun including students, faculty, staff, and their families\. Golf includes warm\-up balls at the range, a shared golf cart and 18 holes of golf\. Golf will start at 12:30 pm sharp\.

Golf will be followed by a premium buffet and awards dinner\. There will also be a silent auction with the proceeds going to [Kidsport Kelowna](http://www.kidsportcanada.ca/british-columbia/kelowna/)\. Kidsport is a registered charity that provides funding for kids wanting to participate in organized sport\. As tournament organizers, we hope you can join us in celebrating the importance of sport in the lives of our community’s children\.

We would like to thank our tournament sponsors Scotiabank and Meyers Norris Penny for supporting our event\.

**Registration details below:**

**Register by August 8th \(Golf &amp; Dinner\) :** Students – $50; Faculty/Staff – $130

**After August 8th \(Golf &amp; Dinner\) :** Students – $70; Faculty/Staff – $150

**No Early Bird \(Dinner only\)**: Students/Faculty/Staff – $30\.
