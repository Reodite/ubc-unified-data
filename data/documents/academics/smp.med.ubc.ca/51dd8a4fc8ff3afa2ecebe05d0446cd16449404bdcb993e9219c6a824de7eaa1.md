The Healthcare Travelling Roadshow takes a multidisciplinary group of health care students to rural communities across BC to showcase career opportunities to high school students\.

Developed in 2010 as a grassroots initiative, the provincial initiative has connected with more than 16,500 high school students during 102 community visits throughout northern and rural BC\.

The Healthcare Travelling Roadshow is delivered in partnership with the University of Northern British Columbia, UBC Faculty of Medicine, Northern Medical Programs Trust, Interior Health, Island Health, and Rural Education Action Plan\. To learn more about the provincial program, [visit the Roadshow homepage](https://www2.unbc.ca/healthcare-travelling-roadshow)\.

### **2026 Roadshow Recruitment**

The Healthcare Travelling Roadshow is recruiting health profession students and recent graduates to participate in our 2026 roadshows:

-   Thompson Okanagan Roadshow \(Logan Lake, Salmon Arm, Vernon\): May 3 – 9, 2026
-   Bulkley\-Nechako Roadshow \(Smithers, Houston, Burns Lake, Fraser Lake\): May 3 – 9, 2026
-   Cariboo Roadshow \(Bella Coola, 100 Mile House, Quesnel, Williams Lake\): May 24 – 30, 2026
-   Island Roadshow \(Port McNeil, Campbell River, Comox Valley\): May 3 – 9, 2026

**Application deadline is February 27, 2026**\. All travel, accommodation, and meal expenses are covered\. **Please note**: **UBC Medicine** students can only participate in the roadshow as part of the FLEX \(Flexible and Enhanced Learning\) course\. **UBC Physical Therapy, UBC Occupational Therapy, and UBC Dentistry** students must obtain approval from their clinical placement coordinator prior to applying\. For more information, contact [warren\.brock@ubc\.ca](mailto:warren.brock@ubc.ca)\.
