## [New guideline decreases breast cancer re\-operation rates](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/2019/04/10/new-guideline-decreases-breast-cancer-re-operation-rates/)

April 10, 2019 (Wednesday, April 10th, 2019, 9:31 am)

A UBC medical student has determined that a new surgical guideline is making a difference for breast cancer patients\. Alex Monaghan, a second\-year Southern Medical Program \(SMP\) student at UBC Okanagan, recently completed a study using patient data from BC Cancer\-Kelowna\.
