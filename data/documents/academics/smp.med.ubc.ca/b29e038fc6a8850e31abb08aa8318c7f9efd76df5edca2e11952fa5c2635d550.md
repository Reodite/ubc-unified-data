## [New Clerkship Site Director, Kelowna General Hospital](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/2026/08/24/new-clerkship-site-director-kelowna-general-hospital/)

August 24, 2026 (Monday, August 24th, 2026, 8:15 am)

Dr\. Heather White has been appointed Clerkship Site Director, Kelowna General Hospital \(KGH\) for the Southern Medical Program \(SMP\) effective September 1, 2026\. Dr\. White is an emergency medicine physician at KGH and a Clinical Instructor with the UBC Department of Emergency Medicine\.
