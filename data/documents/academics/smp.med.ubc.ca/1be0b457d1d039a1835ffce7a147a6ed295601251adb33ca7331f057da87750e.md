## [Excellence in Community Practice Teaching: Dr\. Scot Mountain](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/2014/10/03/excellence-in-community-practice-teaching-dr-scot-mountain/)

October 3, 2014 (Friday, October 3rd, 2014, 1:07 pm)

Dr\. Scot Mountain has been recognized with the UBC Faculty of Medicine’s Award for Excellence in Community Practice Teaching\. The award acknowledges faculty members who have demonstrated exceptional teaching skills while making an educational impact in their community\. Dr\.
