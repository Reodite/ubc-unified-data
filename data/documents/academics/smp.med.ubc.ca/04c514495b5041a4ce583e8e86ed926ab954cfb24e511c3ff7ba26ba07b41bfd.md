## [BC College of Family Physicians Honours Dr\. Marjorie Docherty](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/2017/12/08/bc-college-of-family-physicians-honours-dr-marjorie-docherty/)

Dr\. Marjorie Docherty was recently honoured as the inaugural recipient of the BC College of Family \(BCCFP\) Physicians Coin\. The new award created by the BCCFP acknowledges the tremendous work of family physicians across British Columbia\. Dr\.
