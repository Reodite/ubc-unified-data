## [Student Golf Tournament Supports Mental Health](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/2018/01/31/student-golf-tournament-supports-mental-health/)

January 31, 2018 (Wednesday, January 31st, 2018, 11:43 am)

The Southern Medical Program \(SMP\) Class of 2020 and the local medical community traded their stethoscopes for golf clubs for a fun afternoon on the links in support of the Canadian Mental Health Association \(CMHA\) Kelowna\. Over 40 medical students, physicians, and guests enjoyed eighteen holes of golf and a delicious dinner at the Harvest \[…\]
