## [SMP 2019 Graduation](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/2019/05/27/smp-2019-graduation/)

May 27, 2019 (Monday, May 27th, 2019, 11:10 am)

The SMP Class of 2019 took part in their official hooding and graduation ceremonies took place in Vancouver on May 21 and 22, 2019\. We wish them the best of luck for their residency and hope to see them back in the Interior in the not so distant future\. Read the SMP Class of 2019 Graduation Newsletter\.
