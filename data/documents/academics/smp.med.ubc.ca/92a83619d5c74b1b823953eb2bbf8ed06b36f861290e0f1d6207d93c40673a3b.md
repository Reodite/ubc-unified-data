## [Dr\. Chris West reappointed Assistant Dean, Research](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/2025/09/23/dr-chris-west-reappointed-assistant-dean-research/)

September 23, 2025 (Tuesday, September 23rd, 2025, 4:38 pm)

Dr\. Christopher West has been reappointed as the Assistant Dean, Research, Southern Medical Program \(SMP\) for a three\-year term\. Dr\. West is an Investigator with the Centre for Chronic Disease Prevention and Management and an Associate Professor with the UBC Faculty of Medicine’s Department of Cellular and Physiological Sciences\.
