## [Student Golf Tournament Swings Big for Rural Health](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/2020/02/05/student-golf-tournament-swings-big-for-rural-health/)

February 5, 2020 (Wednesday, February 5th, 2020, 9:43 am)

Nearly 100 golfers and guests participated in the eighth annual Southern Medical Program \(SMP\) Student Golf Tournament in support of rural health\. In partnership with the event’s title sponsor Scotiabank, the students raised over $20,000 for Hope Air to help patients\.
