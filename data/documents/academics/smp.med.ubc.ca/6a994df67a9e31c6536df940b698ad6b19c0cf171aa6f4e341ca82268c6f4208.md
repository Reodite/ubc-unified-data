[Image](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/93/2026/08/Heather-White-web-300x300.jpg)The Southern Medical Program is pleased to announce the appointment of Dr\. Heather White as Clerkship Site Director, Kelowna General Hospital \(KGH\) for the Southern Medical Program \(SMP\) effective September 1, 2026\. Dr\. White is an emergency medicine physician at KGH and a Clinical Instructor with the UBC Department of Emergency Medicine\.

Dr\. White completed her medical degree and residency training at Queen’s University\. Over the past six years, she has served as a tremendous advocate for medical education at KGH\. As the Discipline Site Specific Lead for Emergency Medicine, Dr\. White has helped enhance training opportunities for third\-year medical students and foster an outstanding learning environment\. Most recently, she has served as Chair of the UBC Interior Emergency Medicine Residency Core Curriculum Committee and as a member of the Royal College Emergency Medicine Examination Board\.

As the new Clerkship Site Director, Dr\. White will lead the Year 3 rotational clerkship at KGH\. She will work in close collaboration with the Year 3 Site Directors across UBC’s distributed sites, Year 3 and 4 Associate Director of Curriculum, UGME Director of Curriculum, and the SMP administrative team to support program delivery\.
