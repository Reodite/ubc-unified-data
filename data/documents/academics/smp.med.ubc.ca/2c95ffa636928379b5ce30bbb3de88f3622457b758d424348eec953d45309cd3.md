The Southern Medical Program is training the next generation of doctors, making new discoveries, and creating pathways to better health for communities across the Interior Health region\.

[Image](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/93/2018/08/MD-Undergraduate-Program-Feature-Area-1.jpg)

## MD Undergraduate Program

Meet the healthcare challenges of tomorrow with a career based on evidence\-based practice, critical reflection, and lifelong learning\. Learn about the MD admissions pathways and how to apply\.

[Doctor of Medicine \(MD\) Program](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/admissions/md-undergraduate-program/)

[Image](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/93/2018/08/Clinical-Faculty-Feature-Area-2.jpg)

## Clinical Faculty

Teach and inspire future doctors as they prepare for the challenges of serving the needs of their patients\. Join a network of over 1,500 health professionals teaching students across the BC Interior\.

[Clinical Faculty](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/faculty/)

[Image](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/93/2018/08/Research-Feature-Area-3.jpg)

## Research

Discover how our researchers foster new discoveries and knowledge translation in the prevention and management of chronic diseases\. Explore research priorities and collaboration opportunities\.

[Research](http://smp.med.ubc.ca/research/)
