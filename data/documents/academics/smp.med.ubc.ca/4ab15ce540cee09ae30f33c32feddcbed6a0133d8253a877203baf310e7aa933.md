## [Southern Medical Program hosts diabetes awareness event for the community](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/2024/12/16/smp-diabetes-awareness-event/)

December 16, 2024 (Monday, December 16th, 2024, 2:05 pm)

The SMP recently launched a community\-engaged learning initiative to help students gain real\-world learning experiences\. The first event, held in November, featured a Diabetes Awareness outreach activity in partnership with the UBC Small Steps for Big Changes program and the Orchard Park Shopping Centre\.
