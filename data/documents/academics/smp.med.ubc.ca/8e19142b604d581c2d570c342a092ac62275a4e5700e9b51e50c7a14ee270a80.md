Volunteer Patients allow medical students to gain confidence and competence by practicing communication and clinical skills in a realistic setting\. Volunteer Patients participate as themselves and represent their authentic medical history, whether healthy or living with an illness or chronic condition\.

### Who can be a Volunteer Patient?

People of all ages, genders, physical conditions and backgrounds can participate\. No previous experience is necessary\. A Volunteer Patient is a teaching model for a small group of medical students who perform repeated specific physical examinations and gather relevant information\. Many clinical skills sessions require people in generally good health, while other sessions require specific symptoms or conditions\.

### What can I expect as a Volunteer Patient?

Clinical skills sessions typically include small groups of medical students and a qualified clinician who guides the session\. For some sessions, we may ask you to change into a hospital gown to allow students to examine different areas of the body \(such as knees, feet, shoulders, upper abdomen, chest\)\. Sensitive areas are excluded from examinations\.

Depending on the type of session, students may ask questions regarding health history, daily routine, psychosocial aspects of your life or family background\. There is no obligation to share\. SMP faculty and students are bound by the health professional’s Code of Ethics, therefore any information shared is held in strict confidence\.

### What is the time commitment?

Typically, clinical skills sessions last two to three hours and occur on weekday afternoons between 12:30\-5:00pm\.

For new Volunteer Patients, we provide an orientation interview and facilities tour\. We will ask you to complete health information and consent forms\. The process usually takes about an hour\.

During the academic year \(September to April\), we carefully match Volunteer Patients with the learning objectives and course requirements\. Volunteer Patients generally participate in 2\-8 sessions per academic year; we cater to your availability and the best suited sessions\. We provide a small honorarium in appreciation of your time\.

### Where are the clinical skills sessions held?

Most sessions take place at the Clinical Academic Campus at Kelowna General Hospital\. A small number of sessions take place on the UBC Okanagan campus\. Parking will be reimbursed\.

### What are the specific findings or conditions for some Volunteer Patients?

At times, clinical skills sessions require Volunteer Patients with specific findings or symptoms\. If you are living with any of the following conditions and would like to volunteer, please contact us at [smp\.patient@ubc\.ca](mailto:smp.patient@ubc.ca) or 250\-980\-1329\.

**Cardiology**: heart murmurs, enlarged heart, arrhythmia, congestive heart failure, congenital heart abnormalities, previous cardiac by\-pass, previous heart valve surgery, peripheral vascular disease, previous aneurysm \(abdominal aortic or peripheral\), carotid stenosis\.

**Dermatology**: chronic skin conditions \(psoriasis, eczema\), vasculitis, ulcers, skin cancer, alopecia, rosacea, vitiligo lupus, congenital skin abnormalities, neurofibromatosis \(NF\)\.

**Gastrointestinal**: chronic liver disease \(Hepatitis C or cirrhosis\), hernia, polycystic disease, hemochromatosis, Wilson’s disease, jaundice\.

**Neurology**: changes in strength, movement, sensation or speech as a result of: stroke, Multiple Sclerosis, A\.L\.S\., head injury, Huntington’s disease, ALS, spinal cord injury, Parkinson’s disease\.

**Pulmonary/Respiratory**: Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease \(COPD\) /Emphysema/ chronic bronchitis, lung fibrosis, pleural effusion, Cystic Fibrosis, asthma, Occupational Lung Disease\.

### Do I need to be an actor?

No\. A Volunteer Patient participates as themselves and does not need to rehearse or audition\. If you have acting experience, you may also consider the role of [Standardized](http://smp.med.ubc.ca/community/standardized-patients/) [Patient](http://smp.med.ubc.ca/community/standardized-patients/)\.

### Will I receive medical advice?

Participation as a Volunteer Patient does not substitute for medical care, advice or treatment\. You will not receive prescriptions nor participate in invasive or diagnostic procedures\.

### HOW CAN I APPLY \(MUST BE BASED IN THE OKANAGAN\)?

These opportunities are for those based in the Okanagan\. Any other questions, please contact Patient Programs at [smp\.patient@ubc\.ca](mailto:smp.patient@ubc.ca) or 250\.980\.1329\.
