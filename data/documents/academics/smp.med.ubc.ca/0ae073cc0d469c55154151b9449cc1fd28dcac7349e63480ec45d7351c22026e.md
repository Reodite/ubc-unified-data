## [Dr\. Aleks Tkach jointly\-appointed as new Clinical Scientific Director](https://smp.med.ubc.ca/2026/05/13/dr-aleks-tkach-jointly-appointed-as-new-clinical-scientific-director/)

May 13, 2026 (Wednesday, May 13th, 2026, 2:41 pm)
