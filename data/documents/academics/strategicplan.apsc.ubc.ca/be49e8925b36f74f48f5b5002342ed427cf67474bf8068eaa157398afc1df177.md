UBC Applied Science’s core purpose is to discover, design, innovate, provide unwavering top\-tier education and champion a community of responsible professionals devoted to serving a thriving, sustainable, and healthy society\.

## Mission

### We shape the leaders and professions that shape the world\.

**We do this by:**  
Creating the leaders for tomorrow

Impacting our professions through our research and practice

## Vision

### Thriving people, places and planet\.

**Thriving reflects:**  
Social equity and well\-being  
Physical and mental health

Sustainable and diverse environment

> “Our graduates are instruments of change, they need to leave here with a mission to be leaders in their communities”
> 
> Staff Member, UBC Applied Science
