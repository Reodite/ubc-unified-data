The six priority areas which emerged from the identified drivers of change directly align with our Faculty’s vision and mission\.

The first three describe how we achieve our mission: how we design the university as the bedrock for continuous innovation, prepare society and our professions for an evolving workplace, and educate future societal professional leaders\. The last three directly support our vision of thriving people, places and planet\.
