At UBC Applied Science, we are committed to creating lasting change by discovering and applying knowledge\.

Though like everyone else, we need a strong foundation before enacting our strategic plan\. That is why we collaborated with the Applied Science community to shape the building blocks of our plan\. Learn more about our process below\.

  

## Our journey

Discover how we found our direction by collaborating with members of the APSC community\. Through these engagement sessions, we formed 6 priorities, 17 strategies and 5 commitments to address key drivers that have the power to affect our future\.

[Learn more](https://strategicplan.apsc.ubc.ca/our-journey/)

## Future\-proofing the plan

Our strategic plan is tested against the most important drivers of uncertainty to UBC Applied Science\.

[Learn more](https://strategicplan.apsc.ubc.ca/future-proofing-the-plan/)
