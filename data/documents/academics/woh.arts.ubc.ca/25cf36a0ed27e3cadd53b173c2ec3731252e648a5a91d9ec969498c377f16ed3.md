H5P is an application that makes it possible to create and integrate HTML5 interactive content into desktop and web publishing platforms\.

#### Notable Features

-   Plugs in directly to WordPress and Drupal as a plugin\.

#### Limitations

-   Not possible to integrate existing work on platforms such as PowerPoint
-   Lacks a preview and survey tool
