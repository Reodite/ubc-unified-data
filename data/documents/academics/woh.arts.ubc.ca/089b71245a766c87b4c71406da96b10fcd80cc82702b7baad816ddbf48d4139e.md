## [Venngage](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/venngage/)

Venngage is a web\-based graphic design tool that offers ease of access for creating professional high\-quality infographics\.

## [Infogram](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/infogram/)

Infogram is a data visualization and infographics platform for making and sharing digital charts, infographics and maps\.

## [Inkscape](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/inkscape/)

Inkscape is a vector graphics editor similar to Adobe Illustrator\.

## [GIMP](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/gimp/)

GIMP is a cross\-platform image editor available in different operating systems\.

## [Tinkercad](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/tinkercad/)

Tinkercad is an application for 3D design, electronics, and coding\.

## [Crello](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/crello/)

Crello is a visual editor that creates social media images, ad banners, posters, email headers, and other popular formats\.

## [Vectr](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/vectr/)

Vectr is a free graphic design software used to create vector graphics using mathematical equations to draw a design\.

## [Renderforest](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/renderforest/)

Renderforest is an online tool used to create logos, intros, animations, slideshows, and other media components for branding\.

## [Canva](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/canva/)

Canva is a web\-based graphic design tool that offers ease of access for creating professional high\-quality graphics\.

## [Adobe Express](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/adobe-spark/)

Adobe Spark is a free online graphic design and video creation application, that is also offered in a paid version with greater benefits\.
