[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://player.vimeo.com/video/143407878)TimelineJS is a free web\-based storytelling tool that highlights the time period of a series of events through a timeline, and an accompanying popup slideshow\.

#### Notable Features

-   Can pull media from a variety of sources \(YouTube, Wikipedia, Twitter, etec\.\)
-   Works off a simple Google spreadsheet

#### Limitations

-   Is not appropriate for non\-linear timelines, or timelines that necessitate jumping around events
-   Limited tutorials available
