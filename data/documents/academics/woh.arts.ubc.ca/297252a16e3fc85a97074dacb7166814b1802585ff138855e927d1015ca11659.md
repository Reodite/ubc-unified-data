#### Notable Features

-   Offers a non\-linear approach to presentations that can be more effective for conceptual illustrations
-   is very distinct in aesthetic from the standard use of PowerPoint

#### Limitations

-   Offers limited design options in the free version
-   Often overuses the zoom function as a transition
