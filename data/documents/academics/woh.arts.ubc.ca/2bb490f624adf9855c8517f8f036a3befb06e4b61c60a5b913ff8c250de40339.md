[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/u30fTyXkOdo)VideoScribe is a desktop based video creation platform specific to creating storyboarding/mind map type animated videos, and is predominantly used in advertising and eLearning contexts\.

#### Notable Features

-   Simple user interface
-   High ease of importing materials
-   Large library of free assets

#### Limitations

-   Vector objects often have a jagged edge to them
-   Search function capabilities are not organized effectively
-   Tricky refund policy
