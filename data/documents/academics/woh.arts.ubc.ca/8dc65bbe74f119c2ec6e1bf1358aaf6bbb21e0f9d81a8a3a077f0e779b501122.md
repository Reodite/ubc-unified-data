## [Tableau Public](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/tableau-public/)

Tableau Public creates and shares interactive charts, graphs, maps, and live dashboards that are publicly publishable\.

## [TimelineJS](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/timelinejs/)

TimelineJS is a free web\-based storytelling tool that highlights the time period of a series of events through a timeline\.

## [StoryMapJS](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/storymapjs/)

StoryMapJS is a free web\-based storytelling tool that highlights the locations of a series of events through a map\.

## [Second Life](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/second-life/)

Second Life is a platform that allows users to create virtual realities that are accessible to other users globally\.

## [PlayPosit](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/playposit/)

PlayPosit is a interactive video and assessment tool that lets users add interactive elements to existing streaming content on sites\.

## [Omeka](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/omeka/)

Omeka is a free, open source web publishing system for online digital archives\.
