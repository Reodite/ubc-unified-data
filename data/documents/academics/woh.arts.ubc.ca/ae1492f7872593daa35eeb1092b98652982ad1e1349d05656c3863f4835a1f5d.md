[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/S346o8SQu14)Moovly is a cloud\-based platform that generates multimedia content including animated videos, presentations, and infographics\. Moovly is particularly relevant to creating pedagogical video content\.

#### Notable Features

-   Offers a large stock media library and over 500,000 royalty\-free templates
-   Very user\-friendly

#### Limitations

-   Cloud interface is a bit clunky
-   Purchase is expensive and entails a monthly membership
-   Content created in the trial version is watermarked
