[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/0LC-HO0ljN0)Second Life is a web\-based and desktop based virtual reality creation and exploration platform that allows users to create virtual realities that are accessible to users globally, and also view virtual realities created by other users of the application\.

#### Notable Features

-   Offers alternative learning methodologies
-   Has effective user\-user communication channels \(including voice calling\)
-   Makes real\-time access to faculty possible
-   Users can join groups based on their own interests

#### Limitations

-   Requires a strong processor and high\-speed internet to effectively process 3D images
-   Can be particularly challenging to get familiarized with
-   Contains adult content since it operates as a social network
-   Requires high instructor engagement and technical ability
