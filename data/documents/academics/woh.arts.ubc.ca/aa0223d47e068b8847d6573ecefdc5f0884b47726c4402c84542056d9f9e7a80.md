[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/yAr8CS-Vaj8)Adobe Lightroom is a desktop based Photo Editing software\.

#### Notable Features

-   Ideal for editing raw files
-   Arguably easier to use than Adobe Photoshop
-   Photo edits can be synced to multiple images
-   Settings can be saved as common templates to apply to other images

#### Limitations

-   Lacks layers
-   Minimal graphic design elements
-   Only free if used as part of the trial period
