[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/IPR36uraNwc)Drupal is one of many free, online website building applications that operates in a dashboard format\. The user is offered the ability to edit websites through an accessible user interface, instead of coding, though the latter option is also offered to the interested user\.

#### Notable Features

-   Offers a fairly accessible platform for building complex websites
-   Much more secure than most open\-source website building platforms

#### Limitations

-   May require significantly more pre\-existing technical knowledge than other platforms
