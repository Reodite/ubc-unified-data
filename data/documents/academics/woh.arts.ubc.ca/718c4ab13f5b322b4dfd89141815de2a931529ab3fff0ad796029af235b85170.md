#### Notable Features

-   Allows users to interact with data within the graphs and charts
-   Uses tools that are effective in communicating information in a visually pleasing manner
-   Accesses and connects with stored data \(eg\. Google Sheets\)

#### Limitations

-   Data used is public and not secure
