PlayPosit \(formerly known as EduCanon\) is a web and Google Chrome based interactive video and assessment tool\. It allows users to add interactive elements such as questions and pauses to existing streaming content on sites such as YouTube and Vimeo\. Commonly used by educators\.

#### Notable Features

-   Fairly accessible user interface
-   Integrates very well with learning management systems such as Canvas

#### Limitations

-   Basic free version is limited, and content may be very similar
