[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/g_rLw2FoRbg)Animaker is a cloud\-based video animation software\. It allows users to create animated videos using pre\-built characters and templates\.

#### Notable Features

-   Lets user construct an animation directly on the Animaker website
-   All assets, including the user’s animation, are stored in and always accessible from the cloud

#### Limitations

-   Does not provide offline presenting
-   Limited amount of customization
