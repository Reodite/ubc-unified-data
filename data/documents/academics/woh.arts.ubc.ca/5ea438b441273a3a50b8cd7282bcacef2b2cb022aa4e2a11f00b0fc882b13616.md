[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/ZWEVOghjkaw)Adobe Express is a free online graphic design and video creation application, that is also offered in a paid version with greater benefits\.

#### Notable Features

-   Very accessible user interface
-   Has 24 video templates available
-   Previews can be easily accessed, large music library\.

#### Limitations

-   Can be glitchy with Google Chrome though is better with Firefox
-   Adobe logo cannot be removed without upgrading to a paid account
-   Text size cannot be adjusted, slide transitions are specific to the chosen template\.
