OpenShot is an open source desktop\-based video editor\.

#### Notable Features

-   Is free and open\-source, unlike most video editors
-   Includes most traditional video editing elements, as well as video effects
-   Supports more than 70 languages

#### Limitations

-   Slow video rendering speed
-   Program can sometimes crash
-   Due to the open source nature of the application, robust support options are lacking, though there is a large community of users and forums
