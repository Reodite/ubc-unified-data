[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/Lqqsp8soXTo)PressBooks is an online publishing tool for creating books that operates off the WordPress blogging platform\.

#### Notable Features

-   Operates off WordPress, which significantly improves accessibility
-   Has a strong blog\-to\-book import feature \(for publishing a series of blogs, for example\)

#### Limitations

-   Books are watermarked by default for the free version, and removing the watermark costs between $20\-$100
