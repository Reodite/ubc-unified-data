[Embedded iframe \(view source\)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/PNACvjldl3I)Wix is one of many free, online website building applications that operates in a dashboard format, and builds websites through an accessible user interface, instead of through from scratch web development\.

#### Notable Features

-   Offers a professional aesthetic in an accessible manner
-   Takes a classification approach to limiting options to the user’s needs
-   Offers a variety of website templates arranged by type of website \(education, wellness, etc\.\)
-   Wix offers significant free options, with 500 MB storage space and an in\-house support blog with a variety of tutorials and new content for users

#### Limitations

-   Search Engine Optimization is limited in Wix, since image names are automatically changed upon uploading to the platform
