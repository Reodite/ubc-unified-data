WordPress was one of the first free, online website building applications offered which operated in a dashboard format\. The user is offered the ability to edit websites through either an accessible user interface or through coding\.

\*UBC offers its own installation of WordPress called UBC Blogs at [blogs\.ubc\.ca](http://blogs.ubc.ca/)\. It’s free to use for all faculty, staff and students\.

#### Notable Features

-   The open source nature of WordPress offers continual access to new templates
-   Offers the most storage space for its free account \(3 GB\)
-   Compatible with other tools such as H5P \(which has a plug\-in specific to WordPress\)

#### Limitations

-   Limited in its approach to aesthetics \(for example, the platform offers a choice of only 4\-5 fonts for free users\)
