MS PowerPoint is the industry standard for creating presentations, and is part of the MS Office Suite of applications\.

#### Notable Features

-   Is a very easily accessible presentation tool available on most computers
-   Provides users with a lot of customization opportunities

#### Limitations

-   Operates in a linear format, i\.e\. slide 1 is followed by slide 2, with limited options to return to previous slides without going through the whole presentation
