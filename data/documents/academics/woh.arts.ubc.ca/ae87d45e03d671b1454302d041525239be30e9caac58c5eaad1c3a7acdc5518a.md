## [Powtoon](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/powtoon/)

Powtoon helps create videos and presentations, and enhances videos with animation, stock footage, images, soundtracks, etc\.

## [Vectr](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/vectr/)

Vectr is a free graphic design software used to create vector graphics using mathematical equations to draw a design\.

## [TimelineJS](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/timelinejs/)

TimelineJS is a free web\-based storytelling tool that highlights the time period of a series of events through a timeline\.

## [StoryMapJS](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/storymapjs/)

StoryMapJS is a free web\-based storytelling tool that highlights the locations of a series of events through a map\.

## [Renderforest](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/renderforest/)

Renderforest is an online tool used to create logos, intros, animations, slideshows, and other media components for branding\.

## [Prezi](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/prezi/)

Prezi is an online based tool for creating engaging presentations\.

## [PowerPoint](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/powerpoint/)

MS PowerPoint is the industry standard for creating presentations, and is part of the MS Office Suite of applications\.

## [Moovly](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/moovly/)

Moovly is a cloud\-based platform that generates multimedia content including animated videos, presentations, and infographics\.

## [Camtasia](https://woh.arts.ubc.ca/camtasia/)

Camtasia allows the user to create video presentations by capturing content on\-screen and editing through the built\-in editing tool\.
