Audacity is a free and open source desktop based audio editor and recording application\.

#### Notable Features

-   Has a good selection of editing and effect plug\-ins
-   Users can download additional audio through online databases\.

#### Limitations

-   Outdated aesthetic of the user interface can inhibit effective engagement with the program
-   Requires downloading a LAME encoder to import MP3 files
