We’re thrilled to welcome **Cory Doctorow** to the UBC Master of Educational Technology Speaker Series for a conversation about AI, power, and the technological futures we are being thrust into, in many ways against our consent\.

Doctorow is an internationally acclaimed science\-fiction author, journalist, activist, and critic of Big Tech whose work has given us some of the most useful language for understanding the contemporary digital landscape—including the now\-ubiquitous idea of “enshittification\.”

In this conversation, Doctorow will draw from his substantial body of work including, ***The Reverse Centaur’s Guide to Life After AI: How to Think About Artificial Intelligence Before It’s Too Late***, to ask what AI actually is, whose interests it serves, and how we might think more clearly about its place in our lives, institutions, and futures\.

For those of us working in education, these questions are especially urgent\. What happens when AI systems become embedded in the infrastructures of teaching, learning, knowledge, and creative work? How do we resist both technological hype and technological fatalism? And what choices might still be available to us?
