Dear Students,

Join us on **September 23 from 4:00–5:00 PM on Zoom** for the first MET Speaker Series event of the new school year: **Meet the MET Faculty**\.

For this session, we’re bringing together members of the core MET faculty team to share a little about the professional and research journeys that led them to the shores of the MET program\.

Come hear from **Dr\. Jen Jenson, Dr\. Leah Macfadyen, Dr\. Sam McCready, and Dr\. Rachel Horst** as they share their research interests, current work, and some of the paths that brought them to MET\.

We’ll leave plenty of time for questions and conversation, so this is a great opportunity to meet some of the people behind the program and learn more about the work happening across MET\.

**Date: September 23**  
**Time: 4:00–5:00 PM \(PST\)**  
**Location: Zoom**  
**Topic: Meet the MET Faculty**

Sign up to receive the Zoom link and calendar event download [here](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_8kNenQbLPsvJq3Y)\.

Everyone is welcome\!
