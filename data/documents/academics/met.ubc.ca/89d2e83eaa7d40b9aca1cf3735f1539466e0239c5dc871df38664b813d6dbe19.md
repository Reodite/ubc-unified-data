# [Image](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/08/MET-Book-Club-Poster-1-scaled.png)

Join the MET community for our **second book club gathering ahead of Cory Doctorow’s visit this November**\. Together, we’ll explore *The Reverse Centaur’s Guide to Life After AI: How to Think About Artificial Intelligence—Before It’s Too Late**\.* In the book he argues that the central question about AI is not simply what the technology can do, but who it works for—and who is made to work for it\.

### About the Book Club

We’ll meet online for a guided discussion of *The Reverse Centaur*, considering how this technology increases human agency, or reorganizes people around the needs of the machine and the institutions that own it\.

Find at your local bookstore, library, or purchase here: [https://shop\.craphound\.com/](https://shop.craphound.com/)
