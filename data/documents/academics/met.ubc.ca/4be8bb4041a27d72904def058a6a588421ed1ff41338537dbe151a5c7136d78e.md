 [Image](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/1780935590536-200x300.jpeg)The 2026 MET Conference &amp; Hackathon brought together students, alumni, faculty, and community partners for a weekend of creativity, critical inquiry, and collaborative design at the intersection of education and technology\.

### Conference Highlights \| Saturday, June 6

The conference showcased an impressive range of student and alumni work, spanning AI‑native storytelling, immersive interactive narratives, public pedagogy, and design‑driven educational interventions\. Presenters shared projects that were thoughtful, imaginative, and often deeply personal, reflecting the diverse ways MET learners are engaging with emerging technologies and contemporary educational challenges\.

Across the day, participants explored themes such as:

-   AI‑supported creative production
-   Interactive and multimodal narrative design
-   Equity, representation, and public pedagogy
-   Community‑centred approaches to educational technology

The conference underscored the depth of innovation happening within the MET community and the value of creating space for unfinished ideas, experimentation, and critical dialogue\.

### Hackathon Highlights \| Sunday, June 7

This year’s hackathon invited participants to respond to the prompt:

> When Help Becomes Harm: Ethical AI in Education\.

[Image](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/06/1780935587063.jpeg)

Teams worked intensively throughout the day to design tools, policies, practices, and prototypes that grappled with urgent questions around AI, learner agency, trust, and responsible design\. The atmosphere was collaborative, energetic, and deeply thoughtful, with participants approaching the challenge through multiple disciplinary and ethical lenses\.

**Prize‑Winning Projects**

-   First Prize: Undependent\.AI
-   Second Prize: Trustlens
-   Third Prize: AI Grader
