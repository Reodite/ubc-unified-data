Dr\. Jennifer Jenson

As announced by Dr\. Jan Hare, Dean, [Dr\. Jennifer Jenson](https://met.ubc.ca/people/jen-jenson/) will be leading the Master of Educational Technology \(MET\) program as the new Director\.

Dr\. Jenson is a Professor in the Department of Language and Literacy Education \(LLED\)\. Her research spans over three decades, publishing widely on education, technology, gender, design and development of digital games, and the technology policies and policy practices in K\-12 schooling\. She currently holds a SSRHC Insight Grant, studying the ways in which making digital games can support the acquisition of computational literacies\. In the MET program, she has co\-created and taught: New Foundations of Educational Technology \([ETEC 511](https://met.ubc.ca/courses/etec-511/)\), METatheory: New Materialism Meets the History of Educational Media \([ETEC 534](https://met.ubc.ca/courses/etec-534/)\), Digital Games and Learning \([ETEC 544](https://met.ubc.ca/courses/etec-544/)\), and three MET Summer Institutes\.

Prior to UBC, Dr\. Jenson spent 18 years at York University in Toronto, Canada\. She was the Director of the Institute for Research on Digital Learning, the co\-editor of Loading: The Journal of the Canadian Game Studies Association, and a past, long\-serving President of the Canadian Game Studies Association\. With a firm belief that play is central to learning, she developed a series of educational video games with Professor Suzanne de Castell \(SFU\) and a team of students in her CFI\-funded Play:CES \(Play in Computer Environments\) lab\. These included: “Contagion”, “Epidemic: Self\-Care for Crisis”, a Baroque music game, and an iPad game for early readers, “Compareware”\.

Please join us in welcoming Dr\. Jenson to the MET leadership team\!
