Vancouver’s long\-running weekly newspaper, [The Georgia Straight](https://www.straight.com/), sought out [MET Director Dr\. Teresa Dobson](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/people/teresa-dobson/)‘s insights into MET’s contribution to online learning development\.

In [this August 13th 2020 article](https://www.straight.com/education/ubcs-master-of-educational-technology-program-offers-grads-deep-insights-into-online), Dr\. Dobson describes how many MET students and alumni have been leading efforts to accelerate the ‘leap to online’  and lead instructional design in their workplaces across Canada and internationally\.

*“Many of our students lead online instructional design in their workplaces,” Dobson stated\. “When global lockdowns commenced in view of COVID\-19, our students were in high demand\. Some were called upon to lead their respective workplaces—schools, universities, businesses, et cetera—rapidly through a pivot from face\-to\-face to online forms of education\. Others were hired into new positions as leaders in developing online education options to supplement or replace existing face\-to\-face programs,” she continued\. “MET students continue to be in high demand and will be for the foreseeable future\.”*
