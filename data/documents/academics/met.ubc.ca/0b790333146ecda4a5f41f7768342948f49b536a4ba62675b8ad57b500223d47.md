### Interested in developing a deeper understanding of anti\-racism education approaches?

---

-   Dates: July 12\-16, 2021 \(some advance reading expected and assignment deadlines will extend beyond the live institute\)\.
-   Instructors: [Dr\. Kisha McPherson](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/people/kisha-mcpherson/)
-   Location: *Virtual \(mostly synchronous\)*

---

[Image](https://educ-met-site.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/02/hands-329x300.jpg)This summer institute is about responsibility of educators\. Focusing on addressing anti\-Black and anti\-Indigenous racism in education, the questions that surround the reflection and learning in this course centre on educators’ individual responsibility to facilitate and support anti\-racist approaches and strategies throughout their practice\.

Anti\-racism teaching approaches benefit all students\. This course is an opportunity to read, reflect, and develop a more in\-depth understanding of racism, how it operates, and how it impacts all students in educative spaces\. Ultimately, you will be  challenged to apply this learning to create and enhance transformative approaches to anti\-racism in your online and technological practices\.
