In the summer of 2018, MET students had the opportunity to enroll in an in person summer course entitled *Virtual Reality: Viewpoints on a New Medium* offered experimentally by the Faculty of Education as undergraduate course EDUC 490V\. This 3 credit, one week intensive course had students working in groups to produce their own 360º video projects\. The results, shared at a Friday evening gala reception were impressive\. If you are interested in viewing the projects and learning more about this courese, watch instructor, Kyle Stooshnov shared his reflections in the  [August 13\[th\] edition of ‘This Week in Education’](http://educ.ubc.ca/virtual-reality-viewpoints-summer-institute/)\.

Watch this video playlist to see the different stories that course teams created, shot and edited in 360º video, each of them exploring and expressing a point of view into virtual reality\.
