Join the MET community for our **first book club gathering ahead of Cory Doctorow’s visit this November**\. Together, we’ll explore *Enshittification: Why Everything Suddenly Got Worse and What to Do About It*—a sharp, timely look at technology, power, and the platforms shaping our digital lives\.

### About the Book Club

We’ll meet online for a guided discussion of *Enshittification*, considering the questions Doctorow raises and the implications for educators, technologists, and learners\. Participants who choose to read along will receive biweekly reflections, prompts, and guiding questions as we move through the book together\.

### Event Details

-   **Date:** Saturday, August 29
-   **Time:** 10:00–11:00 am \(PST\)
-   **Where:** Online via Zoom
-   **Who:** MET students, alumni, and faculty
-   **Capacity:** Limited to 50 participants
