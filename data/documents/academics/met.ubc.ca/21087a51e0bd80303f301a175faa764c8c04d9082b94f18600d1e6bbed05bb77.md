Dr\. Rachel Horst

[Dr\. Rachel Horst](https://met.ubc.ca/people/rachel-horst/) is a digital literacy scholar and educator\. She joined the MET community in September for a one\-year lectureship and will also be the new Academic Advisor\!

Dr\. Horst teaches: Curriculum Issues in Cultural and New Media Studies \([ETEC 531](https://met.ubc.ca/courses/etec-531/)\), Artificial Intelligence in Education – Using AI to Enhance Learning \([ETEC 565H](https://met.ubc.ca/courses/etec-565h/)\), Constructivist Strategies for E\-Learning \([ETEC 530](https://met.ubc.ca/courses/etec-530/)\), and Ableism, Equity, and Educational Technology \([ETEC 565C](https://met.ubc.ca/courses/etec-565c/)\)\. She holds a PhD in Language and Literacy Education from UBC and is a graduate of the MET program herself\.

Dr\. Horst’s research examines how digital arts\-based methodologies, futures literacies pedagogy, and critical \(post\)digital literacies contribute to meaning\-making in an increasingly digitally saturated world\. She’s particularly interested in exploring how technology such as generative AI may deepen creative practices, enable collaborative knowledge creation, and shape new methods of communication\. Dr\. Horst is committed to equitable, decolonizing, and relational approaches that critically engage digital tools and technologies\. It is her wish that she will empower her learners to engage and co\-create in a more equitable and inclusive digital future as well\.

Before pursuing her doctoral degree, Dr\. Horst was a secondary school teacher working in Bella Bella and the Sunshine Coast\. Her experience working with students outside mainstream contexts continues to inform her teaching practices to this day\.

Please join us in welcoming Dr\. Rachel Horst as the new Academic Advisor of the MET program\!
