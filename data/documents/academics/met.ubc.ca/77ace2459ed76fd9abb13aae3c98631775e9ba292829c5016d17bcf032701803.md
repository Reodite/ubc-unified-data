Join us on May 19 from 5:00–6:00 PM on Zoom for 1 Hour: 3 Tools, a quick student\-led MET workshop featuring practical presentations on Notion, Obsidian, and UBC’s home\-grown HelpMe chatbot\.

Our guest presenters will be **Sooyoung Yang**, **Melissa Chung**, and **Sean Jeon**, who will each share a tool, how they use it, and why it might be useful for learning, teaching, research, or staying semi\-organized\.

We’ll also leave time for Q&amp;A and a group tool share, so bring a favourite tool, workflow, or tiny digital survival trick if you have one\.

**Date: May 19**  
**Time: 5:00 – 6:00 PM \(PST\)**  
**Location: Zoom \(link below\)**  
**Topic: Notion, Obsidian, and UBC’s HelpMe chatbot**

Everyone is welcome\!
