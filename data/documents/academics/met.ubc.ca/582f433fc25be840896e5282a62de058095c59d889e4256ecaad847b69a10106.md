The Master of Educational Technology program offers a comprehensive curriculum exploring topics relevant to learning technologies and eLearning\.

The application deadline for the May 2026 intake is ***January 25, 2026***\.

## RSVP for the Info Session\!

Join us for an open Q&amp;A session designed for anyone considering the UBC Master of Educational Technology or Graduate Certificate program, including educators, professionals from diverse fields, and applicants exploring next steps\. This session will cover key questions about:

-   admissions requirements
-   transitioning from the Graduate Certificate to the Master’s
-   how MET’s asynchronous but not self\-paced courses actually work
-   what to expect in terms of weekly structure and the rare synchronous components
-   typical program timelines, financial aid considerations
-   where MET graduates go after completing the program

Use this informal opportunity to get clarity, understand the learning experience, and ask any questions that will help you decide whether the MET program is the right fit for you\.

**Date:** December 10, 2025  
**Time:** 4:30 – 5:30pm PT  
**Format:** Online via Zoom – *Zoom link will be made available upon RSVP*
