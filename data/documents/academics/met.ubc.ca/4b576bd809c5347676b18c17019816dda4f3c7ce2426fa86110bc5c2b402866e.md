Congratulations to **Dr\. David Vogt** and **Dr\. Leah Macfadyen**, who have been selected as **2026 UBC Open Educational Resources \(OER\) Champion Award** recipients at the AMS UBC Teaching and Learning Champions Reception\.

This event celebrates faculty, staff, and administrators who have demonstrated an outstanding commitment to excellence in teaching and learning at UBC\. The award is presented annually by the Alma Mater Society \(AMS\) in collaboration with the UBC Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology and the UBC Library\.

This year’s celebration highlights faculty who champion equitable, inclusive, and innovative education, including those advancing affordable access through Open Educational Resources \(OERs\) and fostering inclusive learning through Universal Design for Learning \(UDL\)\.

At a time when affordability and accessibility in education are pressing concerns, faculty, staff, and administrators play a crucial role in removing barriers to learning\. Their dedication to OERs ensures that all students, regardless of financial background, have access to the materials they need to succeed\. Likewise, those who embrace UDL create inclusive learning environments that empower all students to fully engage in their education\. Through their leadership, OER and UDL Champions are transforming teaching and learning at UBC and reinforcing the University’s mission of academic excellence, innovation, and accessibility\.

UBC MET is proud to see both Dr\. Vogt and Dr\. Macfadyen recognized for their contributions\. Their work continues to shape learning environments that empower all students and strengthen UBC’s commitment to academic excellence and innovation\.

###### The above was initially sent by *Zarifa Nawar \(**Vice\-President, Academic and University Affairs,* *Alma Mater Society\) with* Dr\. David Vogt and Dr\. Leah Macfadyen\.

**OER Contributions from the Award Recipients**

Dr\. Leah Macfadyen is recognized for her work developing openly accessible scholarly resources that explore the intersections of culture, communication, and digital technologies\. Her recent OER initiatives include the interactive research collection **[DigitalCultures\.ca](https://digitalcultures.ca/)** and the open‑access Pressbook [**Culture and Communication in Digital Worlds**](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/ccdw/)\. Both resources are integrated into ETEC 542, where students actively use and contribute to the growing collection\.

Dr\. David Vogt is honoured for his long‑standing commitment to a “regenerative curriculum” model in ETEC 522 and ETEC 523, where students collaboratively research, author, and publish OERs for a global audience\. These student‑generated resources are published through an open WordPress environment \([https://blogs\.ubc\.ca/etec522/](https://blogs.ubc.ca/etec522/?utm_source=copilot.com)\), forming a continually evolving repository that serves as core learning material for future cohorts\.
