## MET Speaker Series: Lucas Wright

**What happens to assessment when AI enters the room?**

Join us for the next MET Speaker Series event featuring **Lucas Wright**, Senior Education Consultant at UBC’s Centre for Teaching and Learning Technology \(CTLT\)\. Lucas will dig into how educators can get creative with assessment by designing tasks that spark genuine thinking, play to what makes us human, and actually work in a world where AI is part of the landscape\.

This isn’t a sit\-back\-and\-listen kind of session\. Expect hands\-on activities, lively conversation, and a Q&amp;A where no question is off limits\. Bring your curiosity \(and maybe your favorite AI tool\)\.

### Event Details

**Date:** April 15, 5–6 pm PST

**Format:** Online via Zoom

**Open to:** The MET community and other Education units at UBC

Hope to see you there\!
