## **Five takeaways:**

**1\) AI literacy as entangled dimensions  
**It is helpful to understand AI through an entangled dimensions framework \(data, algorithmic, epistemic, relational, creative, political, ecological, etc\.\), rather than only treating it as technical skills or an ethics add\-on\.

*Check out [this resource](https://rshorst.github.io/Entangled-Dimensions-of-AI-Literacy/)*\.

**2\) AI mediation can be collaborative and iterative, supporting cycles of prompting, reflection, revision, and discovery\.**Educators and learners can actively shape AI outputs toward their own learning goals, values, and contexts — as starting points for exploration, not endpoints\.

*Feel free to adapt [this activity](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1bzHg-2yuT7mXuTtnyi47xI4NMzknHG_0leu_ZxR9sOc/edit?slide=id.g3ad32107199_1_133#slide=id.g3ad32107199_1_133) for your context*\.

**3\) The** [**mediation orchard**](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/orchard-interface-feedback-mediations-assessment-practice-horst-gsypc) **frames learning and expression as a landscape shaped by different mediations\.**AI adds new fruits to the orchard of learning and expression\. Attentiveness to mediation helps educators guide deeper learning rather than police use\.

**4\) Computational sense\-making  
**Computational sense\-making offers a framework for helping students orient to the computational nature of AI outputs\. This practice helps students understand that these outputs are the result of computational processes rather than human\-like understanding or a point of view\.  
[https://rshorst\.github\.io/computational\-sense\-making/](https://rshorst.github.io/computational-sense-making/)

**5\) Critical AI literacy requires radical difference and *your* participation\.  
**We are actively building the futures of AI through critical engagement, resistance, and pedagogy\. The more diversity and creativity we bring to this work, the more resilient and just those futures can become\. Radical difference insists that the futures of AI be shaped by plural ways of knowing, designing, and relating, not by passive adaptation to existing technological systems\.
