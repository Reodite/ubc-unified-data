1.  When designing assessments that integrate AI, it is important to recognize that the ways students use AI are complex and to consider how they need to learn and think with AI\.
    
2.  AI is now ubiquitous, and the growing use of AI wearables, such as glasses, is increasing its availability for students while also creating new academic integrity challenges\.
    
3.  Clear communication about AI use is necessary but not sufficient for addressing academic integrity\. In addition to communication, instructors need to intentionally structure how AI is used, either to support or to limit its use, depending on the learning goals\.
    
4.  Currently, many instructors are re\-envisioning their assessments to both mitigate and integrate AI\. Some of these changes may be temporary, and it is important to consider longer\-term, sustainable approaches to assessment\.
    
5.  Process focused assessment as well as assessments that promote choice and agency are possible future approaches to assessment\.
