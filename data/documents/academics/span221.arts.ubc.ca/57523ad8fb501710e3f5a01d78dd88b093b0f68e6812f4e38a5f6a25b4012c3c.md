Today’s class reminded me a little of the German dramatist Bertolt Brecht’s famous poem [“Die Lösung”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_L%C3%B6sung)\.

Anyhow, the upshot is that the [proposed assessment adjustment](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TS9vx2RYitLxic1L3qb4QPqDGlEQ4rnsDqACv-tsLmw/) *passed* unanimously\.

As such:

You now have a choice:

EITHER Instead of writing the final essay \(currently worth 15% of final grade\), more weight will be given to both the group project \(which will be worth 30%\) and the final exam \(25%\) for your individual grade\.

OR You can write the final essay, and so keep to the syllabus as it is if you want\.
