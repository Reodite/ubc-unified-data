### Why is my role as a teacher so important?

Clinical Faculty members are crucial to the Faculty of Medicine’s and the Department of Family Practice’s vision\. You teach and inspire our learners and are the role models for the next generation of health care professionals, and importantly future family physicians\. Many students rate the program as one of their most important educational experiences\.

## Year 3 — Clerkship

-   85\-90% of students complete their four\-week family practice clerkship in a rural or semi\-rural location
-   10\-15% of students complete their four\-week family practice clerkship in an urban or suburban location
-   Provides students an understanding of the total health care of individuals and their communities, including the philosophy of medical care that looks after patients from birth to death and from home to hospital and back
-   Focus is on students participating in the active delivery of care in a clinic \(vs\. a hospital\)
-   Commitment: Minimum 3\.5 days per week over a four week rotation within the year
    -   Commitment can be shared with local colleagues

## Year 4 — Electives

-   During their fourth year, two\- and four\-week electives are in very high demand as competition for family practice residents has increased
-   Students are interested in general \(urban and rural\) and focused electives, including working with family physicians in anesthesia, emergency medicine, opioid substitution, geriatric medicine, oncology, dermatology, Indigenous care, sport medicine, obstetrics, hospitalist and palliative care
-   Commitment: Minimum 3\.5 days per week over a two or four week rotation within the year
    -   Commitment can be shared with local colleagues

## What are the benefits of teaching?

-   Faculty appointment \(includes a UBC library card providing access to many medical journals; discounts on travel and other goods and services, free entry to the Museum of Anthropology, etc\)\.
-   The opportunity to teach enthusiastic learners who can invigorate and sustain your clinical and academic interests\.
-   Association and collaboration with others in the Department of Family Practice and the Faculty of Medicine\.
-   The opportunity to influence the next generation of health care professionals\.  
    

## Is there renumeration?

Yes\. $106\.03 per half day \(one learner\) and $129\.59 per half day \(two or more learners\), if eligible\.
