Sciences Po is a distinguished research\-intensive and internationally\-focused university specializing in the social sciences and humanities\. Science Po is highly regarded for its education of new leaders in many fields, including politics\. An innovator in international BA dual degree programs, Science Po’s dual degree with UBC is its only dual degree program in Canada\.  
Sciences Po aims to offer education firmly rooted in five social science disciplines: economics, law, history, sociology and political science, with an overarching mission to educate future leaders in the public and private sectors\.

The [University of British Columbia \(UBC\)](http://www.ubc.ca/) is one of Canada’s leading research universities and is consistently ranked among the top 40 in the world\. The university attracts 62,000 students from across Canada and 140 countries around the world to its two campuses in Vancouver and in the Okanagan\.  
UBC’s Faculty of Arts is a research\-intensive faculty, encompassing disciplines in the social sciences, humanities, the creative and performing arts, and professional schools\. The Faculty of Arts is UBC’s largest faculty, and educates more than 14,000 students in 25 departments, schools, institutes, and programs\.

[Learn more about UBC’s Faculty of Arts](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/)
