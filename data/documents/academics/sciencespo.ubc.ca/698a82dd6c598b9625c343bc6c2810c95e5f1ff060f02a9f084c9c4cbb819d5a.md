### Prospective student advising appointments

Book a virtual appointment with Tim Flanagan, program coordinator \(and alumnus\) to discuss the Dual Degree and application process\. \(To arrange an in\-person visit on the UBC campus, please email [arts\.dualba@ubc\.ca](mailto:arts.dualba@ubc.ca) directly to discuss\.\)

*Appointments available Wednesdays, 10:00–12:00 and Thursdays, 13:00–15:00 Pacific time; please reach out to discuss an alternative if those times don’t work for you\.*
