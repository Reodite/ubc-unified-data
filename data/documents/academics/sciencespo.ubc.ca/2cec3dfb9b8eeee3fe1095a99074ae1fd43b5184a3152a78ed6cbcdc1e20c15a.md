### Contact Form

We would love to hear from you\! Please fill out this form and we will get in touch with you shortly\.

#### To contact us directly by email:

If you would prefer to contact us directly via email, email us at [arts\.dualBA@ubc\.ca](mailto:Arts.DualBA@ubc.ca)\. Be sure to include your UBC student number in the subject line of your email if you are a prospective student applying to the program, and you have already begun your UBC application\.

#### In person:

Please email us to schedule an appointment\.

*Note that no transcripts or other documents should be submitted to the program directly, outside of the supplemental application; UBC Admissions performs all document verification and will reach out when action is required of a candidate\.*

I have a question about **program\-related matters**, such as housing, healthcare, ASTU seminars, or general program inquiries: [arts\.dualba@ubc\.ca](mailto:arts.dualba@ubc.ca)\.

I have a question about **academic advising and course planning at UBC**: [dualba\.advising@ubc\.ca](mailto:dualba.advising@ubc.ca)\.

I am a current Year 3 or 4 student and would like to arrange an **advising appointment** with a Dual Degree advisor: dualba\.advising@ubc\.ca\.
