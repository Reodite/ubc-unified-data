### UBC SCPO Dual Degree Year 3 \- Program Advising Form

Due MAY 16th \(11:59PM\)\. This is a mandatory form for all incoming Year 3 DD UBC/SCPO students to indicate their choice of Program and answer other questions about their academic path\. After you submit your Program choice\(s\) to the Dual BA Advising team, you will be provided with a master plan for course registration outlining the number of credits and which specific courses must be taken to graduate\. \*\*If you are applying to a competitive major or Honours program, please make sure you do so alongside this form by the deadlines specified by the corresponding Department or School\.
