You may submit your supplemental application over the weekend before January 19\. **If you are affected by these issues, you may submit your supplemental application with a placeholder student number 00000000 before submitting your UBC application\.** Contact the program office at [arts\.dualba@ubc\.ca](mailto:arts.dualba@ubc.ca) after submitting your supplemental application to notify us of your situation if this is the case\.

**Requests to submit the supplemental application after 07:00 Vancouver time / 16h Paris time January 19 must be sent to arts\.dualba@ubc\.ca before the application closes and be accompanied by a substantive rationale\.** EduPlannerBC issues do not constitute grounds for an extension\. The application portal will otherwise close at 07:00 Vancouver time Monday, January 19\.

**We recommend starting the application no later than 06:15 / 15h15 to ensure you have enough time to complete it**\. Supplemental applications submitted after 07:00 / 16h will not be included in the list shared with the evaluation committees\.

Students in the UBC–Sciences Po Dual Degree program are bright and enthusiastic individuals who have a keen interest in global affairs and perspectives\. The application to the UBC Sciences–Po Dual Degree program is a two\-step process\. Applicants must submit:

1.  An online general application to UBC at [you\.ubc\.ca](http://you.ubc.ca/), with the Vancouver Bachelor of Arts \(BA\) program as your first choice; and
2.  A [supplemental application](https://sciencespo.ubc.ca/admissions/supplemental-application/) to the UBC–Sciences Po Dual Degree program\.

**The extended deadline for both components is January 15, 2026\.**

### Step 1: Submit your application to UBC

*Applicants experiencing issues with the EduPlannerBC platform may skip to Step 2 to submit their supplemental application before the portal closes\.*

Complete your application through the provincial portal, following the instructions on [you\.ubc\.ca](http://you.ubc.ca/), and **choose the UBC Vancouver BA as your first choice of program**\. Candidates with a first choice other than the Vancouver Bachelor of Arts will not be considered\. This Dual Degree program is exclusive to the Vancouver Faculty of Arts, and is not available for students interested in attending the Okanagan campus or in pursuing a credential other than a Bachelor of Arts \(BA\)\. Dual Degree students are still eligible for admission to [the B\+MM program](https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/programs/dual-degrees/bachelor-and-master-management) to complete a Master of Management upon completion of their UBC–BA coursework\.

Candidates should be attentive to all fields in the UBC application, as only those who receive an offer of admission to the UBC BA are eligible for an offer from the Dual Degree specifically\. In addition to admission to the Dual Degree program, you will be considered automatically for UBC’s regular BA program\. You must submit a separate application to [Sciences Po](http://www.sciencespo.fr/admissions/en) if you wish to be considered for the Sciences Po BA program, or to any of their other Dual Degree partnerships; there is no restriction on applying to multiple Sciences Po programs\.

*Domestic* applicants \(who do not require a student visa\) are encouraged to submit their **UBC online application before December 1** to be considered for entrance awards, including the [UBC Major Entrance Scholarships](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-canadian-students/)\.

*International* applicants \(who require a student visa\) may be considered for [international student awards](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-international-students/) regardless of when they submit their application\. *\(The December 1 awards deadline only applies to Domestic students\.\)*

Applicants must submit both the online UBC application and the supplemental application by **January 15, 2026**\.

### **Step 2: Submit your supplemental application to the Dual Degree**

*Applicants experiencing issues with the EduPlannerBC platform may complete this step using the placeholder student number 00000000\.* 

Once you have completed Step 1 and submitted your UBC online application, you will then need to submit a supplemental application online at sciencespo\.ubc\.ca\. The written responses in the supplemental application are the primary criteria when selecting candidates for the interview shortlist\.

The [supplemental application](https://sciencespo.ubc.ca/admissions/supplemental-application/) includes submitting personal information, a written response, CV, and academic transcripts from the last two years\. You will also be asked to indicate the regional Sciences Po campus that you would like to study at, and your preferred interview dates and location\. Note that the two processes are entirely discrete: those who review your Supplemental Application submission will not have access to your EduPlanner application, and vice versa\.

To begin your supplemental application,  click the “Apply Now” button below\. Applicants must have an 8\-digit UBC student number in order to proceed \(generated after submission of the EduPlanner application\)\. Applicants who do not submit a supplemental application will not be considered for admission to the Dual BA program\.

Applicants must submit both the online UBC application and supplemental application by **January 15, 2026**\. Limited extensions based on extenuating circumstances may be provided if requested before the deadline\.

### **Step 3: Submit your required documents to UBC Admissions**

In order to complete the admission process, all UBC applicants must [submit their required documents to UBC](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/applied/submit-documents/) by the designated deadlines\. Applicants to the UBC–Sciences Po Dual Degree program must send required documents directly to UBC Admissions\. You will be asked to send copies of your transcripts to both UBC Admissions and to the UBC–Sciences Po Dual Degree separately\. This allows us to consider you simultaneously for both the regular BA at UBC and the Dual Degree\.

### **I’ve submitted my application to UBC and the Dual Degree supplemental application\. Now what?**

Once both parts of your application have been received, you will be assessed simultaneously for the regular UBC BA and the Dual Degree with Sciences Po\. Applicants are responsible for submitting required documents to UBC Admissions to ensure they will continue to be assessed for both degree programs\.

Successful applicants will be invited for an interview conducted online with the UBC–Sciences Po Dual Degree Admissions committee\. All applicants will be notified by mid\-February if they have been invited for an online interview\.
