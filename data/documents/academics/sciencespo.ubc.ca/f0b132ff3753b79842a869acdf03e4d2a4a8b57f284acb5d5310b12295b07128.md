> The UBC–Sciences Po Dual Degree program attracts ambitious, curious, and globally\-minded students interested in the study of global politics, international relations, and critical perspectives\.

The unique combination of the in\-depth, comprehensive social sciences curriculum at Sciences Po and UBC’s research\-intensive culture across hundreds of disciplines prepares students to become leaders in law, business, international affairs, politics, and more\.

This unique program offers you the opportunity to earn two Bachelor of Arts degrees over four years of intensive study: a BA from Sciences Po, and a BA from UBC\. You’ll spend your first two years at one of Sciences Po’s regional campuses, and your third and fourth years at the UBC Vancouver campus\.

The Dual Degree admits students through a specialized process overseen by the Joint Admissions Committee\. Review the pages in this section to learn about each component\.

### Note: We are aware of issues impacting the EduPlannerBC platform\.

You may submit your supplemental application over the weekend before January 19\. **If you are affected by these issues, you may submit your supplemental application with a placeholder student number 00000000 before submitting your UBC application\.** Contact the program office at [arts\.dualba@ubc\.ca](mailto:arts.dualba@ubc.ca) after submitting your supplemental application to notify us of your situation if this is the case\.

**Requests to submit the supplemental application after 07:00 Vancouver time / 16h Paris time January 19 must be sent to arts\.dualba@ubc\.ca before the application closes and be accompanied by a substantive rationale\.** EduPlannerBC issues do not constitute grounds for an extension\. The application portal will otherwise close at 07:00 Vancouver time Monday, January 19\.

**We recommend starting the application no later than 06:15 / 15h15 to ensure you have enough time to complete it**\. Supplemental applications submitted after 07:00 / 16h will not be included in the list shared with the evaluation committees\.
