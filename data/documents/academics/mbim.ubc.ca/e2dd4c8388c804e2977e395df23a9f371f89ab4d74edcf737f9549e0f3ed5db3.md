To adapt to nutrient limitation and other forms of environmental stress, the infectious bacterium *Staphylococcus aureus* synthesizes a nucleotide second messenger compound known as \(p\)ppGpp to initiate cellular reprogramming\. In this study, the authors report that disrupting this pathway, known as the stringent stress response, substantially influences gene expression in *S\. aureus* and alters the RNA cargo inside extracellular vesicles produced by the bacterium\. This work reveals that targeting the stringent response impairs both internal gene regulation and external signalling in *S\. aureus* and represents a promising strategy to boost efficacy against drug\-resistant infections\.
