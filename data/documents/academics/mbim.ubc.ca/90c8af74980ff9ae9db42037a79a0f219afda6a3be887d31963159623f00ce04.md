Disease transmission is highly heterogeneous, yet is often classified based on a single host trait\. In this study, the authors investigated interactions between *Caenorhabditis* nematodes and a wild bacterial parasite to understand the effects of different components of the infection cycle on disease transmission\. They found that parasite shedding alone did not predict downstream infection success, indicating that transmission is shaped by multiple ecological bottlenecks\.
