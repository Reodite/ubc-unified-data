###### **Students who earn an undergraduate degree from the Department of Microbiology and Immunology are equipped with the knowledge and skills needed to work or pursue further education in a range of relevant industries\. These include:**    
 

Science

-   Agricultural scientist 

-   Bioinformatician 

-   Environmental scientist 

-   Epidemiologist 

-   Microbiologist 

-   Mycologist 

-   Research professor 

-   Teacher/lecturer 

-   Toxicologist 

-   Virologist  

Medicine

-   Allergist 

-   Clinical immunologist 

-   Cytologist 

-   Dentist 

-   Geneticist 

-   Occupational therapist 

-   Pathologist 

-   Pharmacologist 

-   Physical therapist 

-   Veterinarian 

Beyond

-   Bioanimator/filmmaker 

-   Biological/medical illustrator 

-   Patent agent/attorney 

-   Science communication specialist 

-   Science consultant 

-   Science policy analyst 

-   Technical/medical writer
