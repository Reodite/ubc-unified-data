## Associate Professor of Teaching

[Marcia Graves](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/square_400/public/profile-images/2025-12/Marcia%20Graves.jpg.webp?itok=CD-5MWaK)

[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://mbim.ubc.ca/people/marcia-graves)

I take a learner\-centered and inclusive approach to teaching\. In developing curriculum, I focus on creating learning environments that promote metacognitive awareness and intrinsic motivation, so that students have agency in their learning process\. I have a special interest in helping students build scientific process skills and a sense of belonging in science\. I am involved in scholarly educational research on how students engage with and evaluate the primary literature, the role of instructor feedback on developing scientific writing skills, and in designing course\-based undergraduate research experiences to increase equitable opportunities for students to authentically “do science\.”

**Awards and Distinctions:**

-   2021, UBC Alma Mater Society Open Education Champion Award
-   2018/2019, UBC Killam Teaching Prize
