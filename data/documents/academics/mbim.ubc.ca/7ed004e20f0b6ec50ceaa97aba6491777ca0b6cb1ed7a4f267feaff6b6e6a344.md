## Science Education Specialist

[Kirsty McIntyre](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/square_400/public/profile-images/2025-12/Screenshot%202025-12-03%20at%2011.42.54%E2%80%AFAM.png.webp?itok=a56iovIC)

[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://mbim.ubc.ca/people/kirsty-mcintyre)

I am an educational developer partnering with faculty to create engaging learning experiences and to assess the impact of educational innovations through research\. 

**Research interests:**

Lab accessibility, open educational resources to support equitable student learning, public and patient involvement in research\. 

**Bio:**

-   BSc \(Honours\) Biomedical Science – Edinburgh Napier University 
    
-   International Student Exchange Program – University of Vermont
-   Research and Teaching Assistant – Murdoch University
-   PhD Medicine \(Human Development\) – University of Manchester
-   Postgraduate Diploma Academic Practice – University of Glasgow
-   Lecturer \(Assistant Professor\) – University of Glasgow, Undergraduate Medical School 

**Honours include:** 

-   Senior Fellow of the Higher Education Academy \(2022\)
-   University of Glasgow Teaching Excellence Award \(2021\)
-   Royal Society of Biology Outreach and Engagement Award \(2018\)
