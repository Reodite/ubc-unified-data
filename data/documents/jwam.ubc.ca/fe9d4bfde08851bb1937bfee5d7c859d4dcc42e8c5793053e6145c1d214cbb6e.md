The University of British Columbia’s Graduate School of Journalism invites all alumni, students, faculty and staff of the school to join the [UBC Graduate School of Journalism Alumni group on LinkedIn](http://www.linkedin.com/groups?about=&amp;gid=4070634)\.

[Image](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/connect-with-ubc-journalism-alum-on-linkedin/)The group is a great way to stay professionally connected with the UBC j\-school community\.

Use it to alert fellow alum to job opportunities\. Start, follow and participate in discussions on topics that are of interest to you\.

And stay up\-to\-date on what’s happening at the school via the group’s newsfeed, which is synced with the Graduate School of Journalism’s own website\.

Our alums have a broad range of experience, having worked at national and international news organizations such as CBC, Globe and Mail, BBC and Al Jazeera\.

The UBC Graduate School of Journalism Alumni group on LinkedIn is just the latest place for current and future graduates of the j\-school to connect\. You can also visit us at the [UBC Journalism page on Facebook](http://www.facebook.com/ubcjournalism) or follow us on Twitter at [@UBCJournalism](http://twitter.com/#!/ubcjournalism)\.
