### Registration in WRDS 150 or WRDS 151

**Due to limited availability, students are permitted to register into one section of WRDS 150/151 for either Term 1 or Term 2 of the Winter Session\.**

-   Students in other faculties may register into a section of **WRDS 150**\.
-   Students in the Faculty of Arts may register into a section of **WRDS 151\.**
-   When sections start to fill up and begin to close, students can choose to place themselves into a WRDS course\-wide waitlist for their preferred term\.
-   ***Please scroll down for waitlist and section preference form information\.***

### Placement from a Waitlist into a WRDS Section

Registration priority from a waitlist into **all WRDS courses** will be given to students, who:

-   **Make available** \- space on one's course timetable to avoid conflict with other courses\.
-   **Have enough credits \-** to take the course\.
-   **Have no active** registration holds \- \(i\.e\. finance\)
-   ***Due to time constraints****,** the WRDS Office is unable to process swap requests\.****  
    Students, who are registered in a section of the course, are welcome to swap themselves into any available open section that becomes available, up until the Add/Drop Date\. Students who are already in a WRDS section will be removed from the waitlists and section preference forms without further notice\.*

### Waitlist Section ID's &amp; Opening / Closing Dates

When waitlists open, usually during the registration period, please register yourself into one of the WRDS course\-wide waitlists below\.

## Term 2 waitlist section IDs:

-   WRDS\_V **150\-WL2** \(non\-Arts\)
-   WRDS\_V **151\-WL2** \(Arts\)
-   ***Term 2 Waitlists will** **close on** **December 17, or earlier** \- if the seating capacity has been reached before the closing date\.*

#### Please contact [wrds\.advisor@ubc\.ca](mailto:wrds.advisor@ubc.ca) if the waitlists fill\-up and close before the “waitlist closing dates”\.
