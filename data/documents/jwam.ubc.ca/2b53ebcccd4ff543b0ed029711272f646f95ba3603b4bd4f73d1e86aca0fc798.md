The School of Journalism, Writing, and Media \(JWAM\) is inviting applications for the WRDS Communications and Events Coordinator for the 2026S Summer Session\.

The WRDS Communications and Events Coordinator will assist with projects relating to the programs offered by the School specifically in Writing, Research, and Discourse Studies \(WRDS\)\. The Communications and Events Coordinator will be responsible for a range of duties, which will result in valuable learning and professional development opportunities\.

**Some Duties of the position can be found below with the complete list and qualifications being found on Careers Online:**

-   helping plan and coordinate WRDS public and educational events;
-   writing and disseminating WRDS event announcements via multiple communication channels \(with a focus on web\-based, digital, and social media channels\);
-   assisting with the implementation of WRDS web/digital communication strategies;
-   supporting the upkeep of web pages relating to WRDS programs, courses, events, achievements, and initiatives;
-   assisting with the research, writing, and preparation of web, digital, and social media content relating to WRDS programs, courses, events, achievements, and initiatives
-   carrying out copy assignments that support the WRDS web/digital communication strategy, which involves working with faculty and students to create consistent, high impact material for digital media
-   writing and disseminating feature stories relevant to WRDS programs, courses, events, achievements, and initiatives via multiple communication channels \(with a focus on web\-based, digital, and social media channels\);
-   creating high\-impact banners and posters to promote WRDS events, programs, and courses on campus and at student information sessions \(print &amp; digital signage\);
-   creating and maintaining procedural guides for creating WRDS communications and events materials, for succession planning \(e\.g\., social media templates, guidelines for sourcing images, guidelines for making communications/marketing requests via UBC channels\)\.

**Applications will be accepted through** **[Careers Online](https://ubc-csm.symplicity.com/?simplify_interface=1)** **until March 25th, 2026,**

JWAM is committed to research and pedagogy that responds to and addresses historic inequities and therefore welcomes applicants who would contribute to our ongoing efforts to create an open and diverse community, one that fosters the inclusion of voices that have been underrepresented or discouraged in the fields of journalism, writing, and academia in general\.

Equity and diversity are essential to academic excellence\.  An open and diverse community fosters the inclusion of voices that have been underrepresented or discouraged\.  We encourage applications from members of groups that have been marginalized on any grounds enumerated under the B\.C\. Human Rights Code, including sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, racialization, disability, political belief, religion, marital or family status, age, and/or status as a First Nation, Métis, Inuit, or Indigenous person\. We encourage all qualified persons to apply\.
