## Frequently Asked Questions

WRDS 150 and WRDS 151 are designed in a series of steps where each assignment prepares you for the next\. As instructors introduce key foundational material early in the course, we strongly encourage students to finalize their registration in WRDS 150 or WRDS 151 by the **end of the first week of term** at the latest\. Please keep in mind that instructors are not obligated to reteach any material that has been missed, so it is recommended that you begin attending at the start of the course \(or as early as possible afterwards\) to give yourself the best opportunity for success\.

### When should I take WRDS 150 or WRDS 151?

Since WRDS 150 and WRDS 151 are designed to prepare you for university\-level scholarship,  these courses should be taken early in your time at UBC, ideally in your first or second year\. **Students in the Faculty of Arts must attempt three credits in a writing\-focused course within their first 30 credits and complete them within 54 credits\.** 

**Students in Faculties outside of Arts should confirm deadlines with their individual programs\.**

### What do I need to know about waiting lists?

If WRDS 150/151 classes fill, we will be opening up course\-wide waiting list seats\. Please waitlist yourself on the waiting list for the term in which you plan to take the course\.  Space permitting, you will be registered in WRDS 150 or WRDS 151 when a seat becomes available\.

### Can I repeat WRDS 150 or WRDS 151?

If you fail WRDS 150/151, you may repeat the course once\. If you pass the course but want to improve your grade \(perhaps in order to gain access to a particular program of study\), you may also repeat it once\.

### How do I register in a full course or switch sections?

You are welcome to swap yourself into any open section on Workday Student\.  Openings in sections fluctuate up until the add/withdrawal date\. Please check Workday Student regularly to see if a space becomes available in the section of your choice\. There are often seats that come open at the beginning of term as students adjust their schedules\.

Please note that we do not use add/drop forms for WRDS classes\. Instructors do not control registration for their sections and cannot add your name to their class lists\.

### What is the difference between WRDS 151, and CAP and Arts One?

While they all meet the writing component of the Writing and Research Requirement for completing the BA degree in the Faculty of Arts, WRDS 151 is a single term course\. CAP is an 18 credit standard timetable made up of a cohort of courses\. Arts One is an 18 credit course which includes the disciplines of English, History and Philosophy\. In CAP and Arts One you take the majority of your courses with the same group of students and the subjects you study make up a pre\-arranged theme, which changes year by year\. WRDS 151, in contrast, is a single course in your overall program of study\.

Please note: Students should only take **ONE** of WRDS 151, ENGL 100, CAP 100 or 101 \(through CAP\) or Arts 001\-A \(through Arts One\) to meet the Faculty of Arts Writing Requirement\.

### When should I take WRDS 151?

Students in the Faculty of Arts must attempt three credits in a writing\-focused course within their first 30 credits and complete them within 54 credits\.

### Should I take WRDS 151 or WRDS 350?

You should take WRDS 151 if you:

-   haven’t taken a college\-level writing or research methods course\.
-   are concerned about your proficiency in standard written English\.

You should take WRDS 350 if you are:

-   in third or fourth year standing at UBC\.
-   transferring to UBC from another post\-secondary institution and have previously taken a college\-level writing, discourse studies, or research methods course\.

If you are in second year and would like to take an advanced writing elective or transfer credits for a course you have taken elsewhere, please contact Arts Academic Advising\.

### I took a writing course at another university; why do I still need to take WRDS 151?

If you believe that a writing course you took at some other school satisfies the course objectives of WRDS 151, you may request that the writing requirement be waived\. For further information, consult [this site](https://www.arts.ubc.ca/writing-research-requirement-faqs/)\. Note that AP and IB English are not accepted as the equivalent of WRDS 151\.
