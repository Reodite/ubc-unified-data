The School of Journalism, Writing, and Media is excited to announce that two new courses will be taught in the upcoming Winter 2026\-27 academic term\. Learn more about WRDS 390: Writing Disability with Dr\. Jennifer Gagnon and WRDS 450: Writing Practices for Publics and Publication with Dr\. Alexis McGee at the links below, and save your spot today\.

WRDS 390: Writing Disability

Join Dr\. Jennifer Gagnon for a course that course centres learning from Disabled writers and centering the intersectional multiple identities they hold\.

[Source link](https://jwam.ubc.ca/courses/level/300/writing-disability-with-dr-jennifer-gagnon-wrds-390/)

WRDS 450: Writing Practices for Publics and Publication

Join Dr\. Alexis McGee to hone your writing and prepare for your next phase of life, school, or career\.

[Source link](https://jwam.ubc.ca/courses/level/400/writing-practices-for-publics-and-publication-with-dr-alexis-mcgee-wrds-450/)

Students completing the WRDS Minor program will transform their knowledge into a capstone project that is led by Dr\. Katja Thieme in WRDS 400: Writing and Communication Capstone\.  More details are at the link below\.

WRDS 400: Writing and Communication Capstone

Dr\. Katja Thieme takes WRDS Minor students through the culmination of their writing and communications journey at the school\.

[Source link](https://jwam.ubc.ca/courses/level/400/writing-and-communication-capstone-with-dr-katja-thieme-wrds-400/)
