Amy Juschka \(‘09\) has won the Community Newspaper Award for her journalism on Vancouver’s Downtown Eastside\. The award honors the best community newspaper journalists in British Columbia\.

[https://soj\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2009/06/Amys\_Photo\_Web\.jpg](https://soj.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2009/06/Amys_Photo_Web.jpg)Amy Juschka

Amy Juschka

Amy holds an undergraduate degree in Political Science and International Development from Wilfrid Laurier University and is currently a Master of Journalism candidate at the University of British Columbia’s School of Journalism\. Her passion for social justice brought her to Megaphone Magazine, a non\-profit magazine sold by homeless and low\-income people, where she writes on social and political issues affecting people in the Downtown Eastside\.

She hopes to pursue a career in freelance feature writing upon graduation\.
