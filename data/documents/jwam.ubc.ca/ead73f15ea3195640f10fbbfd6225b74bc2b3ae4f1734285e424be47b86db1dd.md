The School of Journalism, Writing, and Media in the Faculty of Arts at the University of British Columbia, Vancouver, invites applications for **two full\-time, tenure\-track positions at the rank of Assistant Professor**, with the possibility that an exceptional candidate be appointed at the rank of Associate Professor, beginning July 1, 2021\. Click [here](https://asrw.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2020/09/JWAM_AsstProfs_V2_WritingandDiscourseStudies-9-11-20-1.pdf) for information about the position\. Click [here](https://jrnl.air.arts.ubc.ca/writing-studies-application-for-assistant-professors-tenure-track/) to apply\.
