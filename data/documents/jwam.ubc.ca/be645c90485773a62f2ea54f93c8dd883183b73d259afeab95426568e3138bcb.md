Journalism professor Candis Callison was one of the panelists on a University of British Columbia forum to discuss the issues raised by the recent Maclean’s article, “Too Asian?”

The forum on November 25, 2010 followed a strong expression of interest from students in a space for dialogue on the issue\.

The other panelists included:

-   Dr\. Kerry Jang, Professor, UBC Psychiatry and Vancouver City Councillor
-   Dr\. Henry Yu, Associate Professor, Dept\. of History, UBC and Principal pro tem, St\. John’s College
-   Elysa Hogg, Undergraduate Student, Faculty of Arts
-   William Tao, Undergraduate Student, Faculty of Arts

The forum opened with comments from Brian Sullivan, UBC Vice\-President Students, and Ekaterina Dovjenko, Vice\-President Administration, AMS Student Society of UBC Vancouver\.
