Learn the essential writing and communication skills you need to thrive in academic, professional, and public contexts\. Gain confidence writing and communicating and gain the perspective you need to think critically about the complexity and diversity of writing and communication practices at UBC and beyond\. The Writing, Research, and Discourse Studies \(WRDS\) Unit offers a new interdisciplinary Minor in Writing and Communication to students from across campus, as well as undergraduate courses that help students fulfill their UBC writing requirements\.
