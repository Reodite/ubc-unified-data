[image](https://jwam.ubc.ca/news/grad-01-appointed-business-in-vancouver-online-news-editor/)Andrew Petrozzi \(‘01\) is now the online news editor for Business in Vancouver \(BIV\) newspaper, a weekly business publication\.

He remains a senior reporter for Business in Vancouver as well as editor of the Employment Paper, a weekly employment publication also produced by Glacier Media’s BIV Media Group\.

Petrozzi worked as a city reporter for the Victoria Times Colonist and the Vancouver Sun as well as a sports and entertainment reporter for the Province\.

He was also the editor of Cariboo Press’ Arrow Lakes News, a weekly publication based in the West Kootenay\.
