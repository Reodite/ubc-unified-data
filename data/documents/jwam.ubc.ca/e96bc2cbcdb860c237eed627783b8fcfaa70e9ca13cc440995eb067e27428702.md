> Change the world through the power of the arts\.

Your vision of a healthier, more sustainable world is possible\. In the face of humanity’s most critical and complex issues, UBC Arts students, researchers and alumni are asking the hard questions\. They’re diving deep and sharing evidence\-based ideas and equitable solutions for a better future\.

Since our beginnings more than 20 years ago as the School of Journalism, we have been empowering young journalists to investigate the most pressing global issues of our time\. Our commitment to outstanding education, research, and teaching wouldn’t be possible without the support of our alumni, friends, and partners\. At a time when trust in media has eroded, it’s more important than ever to support independent, high\-quality journalism that is essential to a vibrant democracy\.

What happens here can change everything\. Your support is the catalyst\. Join the School of Journalism, Writing, and Media, in advancing healthy lives, a healthy planet and healthy societies\.

Support the future of journalism\. Give today\.

## Featured initiatives

### Journalism Student Fund

Support the next generation of journalists in Canada and around the world with the Journalism Student Fund\. The fund helps domestic and international applicants to the Master of Journalism degree with financial support through entrance scholarships and various student awards during their two years in the program\.

### Global Reporting Centre

[The Global Reporting Centre](https://globalreportingcentre.org/about/) is an editorially independent and not\-for\-profit newsroom based at the University of British Columbia\. We are leaders in reimagining global reporting\.

### The Brian Truscott Memorial Award in Journalism

[The Brian Truscott Memorial Award in Business Journalism](https://give.ubc.ca/memorial/brian-truscott/) is an annually\-funded $1,000 award that will be offered to an outstanding student enrolled in the UBC Graduate School of Journalism with an interest in business journalism\.
