> Faculty and students in the School of Journalism, Writing, and Media, are actively involved in numerous research projects\. Browse and discover some of their work\.

## Learning Research, Learning Reciprocity

The small TLEF awarded project, led by [Evan Mauro](https://cap.arts.ubc.ca/profile/evan-mauro/) \(CAP\) and [Kirby Manià](https://jwam.ubc.ca/profile/kirby-mania/) \(WRDS\), puts undergraduate work at the centre of a multidirectional knowledge exchange, involving Downtown Eastside \(DTES\) communities, community\-engaged researchers, librarians, and instructors in the Faculty of Arts\.

Learn more about this project

*arrow\_right\_alt*

[Source link](https://jwam.ubc.ca/projects/learning-research-learning-reciprocity/)

## Planting Seeds Across the Curriculum

The project is funded by the Teaching and Learning Enhancement Fund \(TLEF\), and led by Drs\. [Laila Ferreira](https://jwam.ubc.ca/profile/laila-ferreira/) \(principal investigator\), [Katja Thieme](https://jwam.ubc.ca/profile/katja-thieme/), and [Rebecca Carruthers Den Hoed](https://jwam.ubc.ca/profile/rebecca-carruthers-den-hoed/) from the Writing, Research, and Discourse Studies \(WRDS\) unit in JWAM, and Vantage College’s [Jennifer Walsh Marr\.](https://vantagecollege.ubc.ca/jennifer-walsh-marr) It is aimed at helping faculties across UBC create inclusive writing experiences in their courses and classrooms\.

Learn more about Planting Seeds

*arrow\_right\_alt*

[Source link](https://jwam.ubc.ca/projects/planting-seeds/)
