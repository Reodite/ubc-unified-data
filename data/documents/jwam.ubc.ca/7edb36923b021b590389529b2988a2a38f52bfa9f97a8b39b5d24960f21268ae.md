Professors Alfred Hermida and Kathryn Gretsinger from the School of Journalism are working with students from the [Faculty of Land and Food Systems](http://www.landfood.ubc.ca/ "Faculty of Land and Food Systems") on a podcasting project\.  Students taking Prof [Andrew Riseman’s](http://www.landfood.ubc.ca/research/faculty_webpages/riseman.htm "Andrew Riseman's") course in applied agroecology are learning to tell stories in a podcast\.

Watch the interview with Prof\. Riseman in which he explains why he is using podcasting as an educational tool\.
