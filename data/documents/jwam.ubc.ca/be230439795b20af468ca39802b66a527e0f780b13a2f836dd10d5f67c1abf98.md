In celebration of 25 years of excellence in journalism education at UBC, the School of Journalism, Writing, and Media \(JWAM\) and the School of Public Policy and Global Affairs \(SPPGA\) welcome you to join three\-time Emmy Award\-winning director and producer, and Professor at JWAM, **Peter Klein,** as he presents [**Bribe, Inc\.**](https://bribeinc.com/), his latest investigative documentary at the Chan Centre for the Performing Arts, Royal Bank Cinema on Friday, March 14 \[5:30 PM – 7:45 PM\]\.

Following the screening, join us for a 30\-45 minute Q&amp;A and discussion with Peter Klein, moderated by JWAM Professor and Director Kamal Al\-Solaylee\.\.
