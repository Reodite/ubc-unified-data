The deadline for admissions to the UBC Graduate School of Journalism is fast approaching\. The deadline is January 15, 2009 for our September, 2009 intake\.

Once you have reviewed the information, please don’t hesitate to contact Barbara Wallin, journal@interchange\.ubc\.ca, for answers to your specific questions\.
