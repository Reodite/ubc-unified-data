Emerge Media Award Nominees Ian Holliday, Pauline Holdsworth, Nicole Gibillini and Ian Wood

Four UBC journalism students have been nominated for the inaugural [Emerge Media Awards](http://www.emergemediaawards.ca/)\.

The awards, which are administered by the University of Guelph\-Humber, honour student work in journalism, communications and media studies across Canada\.

Out of five nominations in the videography category, two are from the UBC j\-school\.

Nicole Gibillini and Ian Wood are nominated for their video, [SRO SOS\.](http://www.vancourier.com/news/video-downtown-eastside-residents-seek-safe-secure-housing-1.1659534) It examines the poor conditions in single\-room occupancy hotels in Vancouver’s Downtown Eastside\.

The video, which was initially a final project for the advanced video class, was published last December in the Vancouver Courier\.

The second nomination in the video category is Ian Holliday and Pauline Holdsworth’s video, [From Pacific to plate: The tale of a sustainable coho salmon\.](http://thethunderbird.ca/2014/02/13/from-pacific-to-plate-the-tale-of-a-sustainable-coho-salmon/) It illustrates how fish make it from the catch to the table\.

The story ran in February 2014, on the school’s student publication, [The Thunderbird](http://thethunderbird.ca/)\.

Holliday was also nominated in the audio storytelling and written word categories\.

Both stories were produced as assignments during Holliday’s first year at the j\-school\.

His Thunderbird piece, [Vancouver experiment in affordable condos fails to live up to hype,](http://thethunderbird.ca/2014/03/26/vancouver-experiment-in-affordable-condos-fails-to-live-up-to-hype/) was nominated for writing\. And a class assignment, [Vancouver Aquarium’s Ling Cod Egg Mass Survey 2014,](https://soundcloud.com/ihollid1/vancouver-aquariums-ling-cod) was nominated in the audio category\.

The panel of judges includes 31 professionals including Paul Berton the editor in chief for the *Hamilton Spectator*, Rose Pereira the art director for *Chatelaine* and Dianne Rinehart a writer for the *Toronto Star*\.

Winners will be announced at an awards ceremony on April 20\.
