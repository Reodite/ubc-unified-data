October 13, 2023

### Situation:

In alignment with [Strategy 11](http://strategicplan.ubc.ca/strategy-11-education-renewal/) of UBC's Strategic Plan \- [Shaping UBC's Next Century](https://strategicplan.ubc.ca/) \- and funded by the UBC’s Academic Excellence Fund, a dedicated fund of $1\.5 million over two years was allocated to mobilize this strategy\. This fund aimed to propel projects within three focus areas: enhancing teaching effectiveness, restructuring programs with a focus on developing program\-level outcomes, and advancing graduate programs\. Strategy &amp; Decision Support initiated the project with a specific focus on the comprehensive process of running a special call for proposals, encompassing planning, selection, and implementation\.

### Our Actions:

-   Collaborated with the Centre for Teaching, Learning, and Technology \(CTLT\) to develop comprehensive guidelines and processes for proposal submissions and selection
-   Strategically identified internal capacities, collaborated with the Learning Technology Hub \(LT Hub\) to implement a seamless online application process\. This step aimed to automate and streamline the application submission and review procedures
-   Worked with the UBC Communications team to ensure the call for proposals was supported by a robust communication strategy

### Results:

-   A total of 21 projects were funded, effectively utilizing $1\.3 million from the allocated fund
-   The funded projects spanned across different educational levels, benefiting both undergraduate and graduate students
-   The projects demonstrated a strategic focus on the identified priority areas, contributing to the overarching goals outlined in UBC's Strategic Plan
-   In addition to these tangible results, we extended our impact by compiling a comprehensive toolkit\. This toolkit encapsulates insights from the planning, selection, and implementation processes, offering valuable resources for future call administrators\. This knowledge\-sharing initiative aims to facilitate seamless replication and adaptation of successful strategies in funding calls at UBC
