**Joseph Ferguson, Senior Strategy Analyst** 

Joseph brings 13 years of UBC experience to SDS, specializing in business intelligence, complex data analysis \+ visualization, senior level financial modeling, and decision\-making support\. Prior to joining SDS, Joseph worked at UBC’s Okanagan campus as a Financial Analyst, supporting both the Planning &amp; Institutional Research and Finance Strategies teams, in addition to roles with the offices of the Principal &amp; Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Provost &amp; Vice\-President Academic\. Joseph is most passionate about connecting decision makers with the information and insights they need to make strategic decisions\. Joseph has a Bachelor of Management \(BMgt, with Honours, 2009\) from UBC\. Joseph's role is a joint position with both SDS and the Planning &amp; Institutional Research Team \(PAIR\) team\.
