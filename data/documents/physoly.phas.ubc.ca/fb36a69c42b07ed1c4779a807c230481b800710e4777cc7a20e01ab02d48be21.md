## **Information for the 2026 UBC Physics Olympics**

Hello schools\!

The 2026 Physics Olympics will be held in\-person on **Saturday, February 28th, 2026** on UBC campus\. We look forward to seeing you\!

This in\-person Physics Olympics will consist of 6 events: two Pre\-builds, two labs, Quizzics\(\!\), and Fermi Questions\.

Pre\-build rules will be available by January\. The general rules will be similar to those from previous years, and will be uploaded on our website by the end of January\. Meanwhile, we recommend that teams look over past Rule books and get started with preparing for Quizzics and Fermi Questions\. The examples of previous rules include [https://physoly\.phas\.ubc\.ca/rulebooks/2021\-general\-rules/](https://physoly.phas.ubc.ca/rulebooks/2021-general-rules/) and [https://physoly\.phas\.ubc\.ca/rulebooks/2019\-general\-rules/](https://physoly.phas.ubc.ca/rulebooks/2019-general-rules/)\.

We will give registration priority to schools/programs that are registered with the Province of BC \([http://www\.bced\.gov\.bc\.ca/apps/imcl/imclWeb/Home\.do](http://www.bced.gov.bc.ca/apps/imcl/imclWeb/Home.do)\)\. Homeschool groups are also welcome\. All other registrations will be placed on a waitlist until the cancellation deadline, after which we will contact the waitlist groups if spots are available\. Registration is on a first\-come, first\-serve basis\.

For more information, visit our web site: [http://physoly\.phas\.ubc\.ca/](http://physoly.phas.ubc.ca/) or contact Dr\. Marina Milner\-Bolotin \([marina\.milner\-bolotin@ubc\.ca](mailto:marina.milner-bolotin@ubc.ca)\)\.

## **Registration and Payment**

**December 5, 2025**: **We have reached capacity for teams and are now reviewing our registration\. Questions? Please contact us at outreach@phas\.ubc\.ca for registration queries\.**

**October, 2025: Due to space restrictions, there is a set capacity for the number of Physics Olympics teams we can host\. We will open registration for school teams until at capacity, then we will add further registration requests to a waitlist\. If you have any questions or issues with registration****, please contact us directly at outreach@phas\.ubc\.ca for registration assistance\.** 

To register, please click on the registration button, below\. This will lead you to a registration form, which when completed, will automatically go to a secure UBC payment information and payment processing page\. Once payment is complete, you will receive a confirmation of your registration and a payment receipt via email\. 

If you would like to register a second team from your school, please fill out an additional registration form and select “this registration is for a second team from my school”\. We will invite schools’ second teams to register once we’ve confirmed all 1st team spots and second team available spots\.

This year, registration/team is $80, which includes 1 t\-shirt\. To order more t\-shirts, please fill out the size/number on the registration form\. T\-shirts are $15 each\.

If you have any issues with registration, please contact Dr\. Marina Milner\-Bolotin \([marina\.milner\-bolotin@ubc\.ca](mailto:marina.milner-bolotin@ubc.ca)\)\.
