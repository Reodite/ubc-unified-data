| **Project Title** | Enhancement and Customization of an accessible Communication Strategies Textbook |
| **Principal Investigator** | Neil Leveridge |
| **Faculty** | Forestry |
| **Funding Year** | 2021 |
| **Project Summary** | This project aims to create a Communication Strategies open textbook that is more easily accessible for both local and international students\. The open textbook will be developed from existing materials used in WOOD 225 including weekly modules with readings, videos, student workshops, quizzes\. The textbook will be developed on Pressbooks and the first module introduces the foundations of communication while the following modules address various applications of communication strategies\. |
