    The “Collective Bibliography Beyond the Classroom” project aims to incorporate students’ cooperative work \(currently on the courses’ Canvas platform\) into the existing course blogs and to adapt and create this list into UBC\-Wiki resource pages\. As active course contributors from diverse cultural backgrounds, the “Collective  
    Bibliography” project seeks to cultivate inclusive, accessible, and equitable student\-centred teaching, learning, and research materials\. Aligned with decolonizing, anti\-racist, intersectional practices, this project aims to build engagement beyond the classroom, transforming and adapting our collaborative project into teaching and learning social justice open materials\.
