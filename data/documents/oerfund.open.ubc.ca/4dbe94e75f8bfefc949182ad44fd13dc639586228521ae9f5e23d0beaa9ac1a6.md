  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The Center for the Integration of Research, Teaching, &amp; Learning \(CIRTL\) at UBC provides graduate students and postdoctoral fellows with professional development opportunities focused on teaching and learning\. Our goal is to better prepare future faculty for both teaching in the future and in their current role, often as teaching assistants or sessional instructors\. We currently provide multiple opportunities for training that are synchronous with limited spaces available\. One of our popular training sessions is on Justice, Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion \(JEDI\)\.  
      
    Although popular, the prior knowledge of participants is widely varied, which has implications on the topics covered and depths of discussions during the training\. As an alternative to in\-person synchronous training, we plan to develop self\-paced asynchronous modules that are open resources, specifically surrounding JEDI and teaching\. The design of an introductory, basic module will be aimed at graduate students and postdoctoral fellows assuming no prior knowledge on JEDI\. Through this self\- paced, asynchronous module, participants will be able to learn about what JEDI is, why it is important to teaching, and what role they may play in supporting JEDI at the university both in their current role and beyond\. With the creation of this resource, more graduate students and postdoctoral fellows will have the opportunity to participate, as many of our sessions on these topics are frequently waitlisted\. They will also have the opportunity to learn and reflect with greater privacy and at a pace that is comfortable to them, hopefully reducing potentially high levels of resistance and discomfort that are common when learning about JEDI\. Lastly, the module could potentially be used as a prerequisite for a synchronous training session that is discussion\-based\.
