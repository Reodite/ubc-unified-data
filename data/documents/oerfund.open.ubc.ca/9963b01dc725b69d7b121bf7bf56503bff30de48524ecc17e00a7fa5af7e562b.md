    **Project Summary**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    This project will develop an open\-access digital textbook designed to demystify the complex landscape of classical film theory for undergraduate students\. Currently, students in Cinema Studies face a financial barrier in the form of expensive, $180\+ anthologies that are frequently incomplete or lack sufficient explanatory context\. This project creates a standalone explanatory resource that bridges the gap between primary theoretical texts and student comprehension without being tied to specific, copyright\-restricted files\.  
      
    The Guide will offer clear, accessible, yet academically rigorous explanations of major theoretical movements – ranging from early pioneers to semiotics and feminist film theory\. Designed for maximum flexibility, it functions as a pedagogical backbone that can be used alongside any set of primary readings\. It is specifically designed to support not only students but also precariously employed sessional instructors and graduate students who may be teaching these difficult concepts for the first time with little guidance\. This model reduces student costs from $182 to $0 while ensuring the resource remains useful across different universities and platforms\.
