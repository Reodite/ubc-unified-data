    The project’s goal is to effectively communicate the key terminology and concepts relevant to all aspects of entrepreneurship to the broader UBC student communities\. The objectives of the UBC Entrepreneurship Primer OER is to expand the content from COMM 280 and introduce and engage all UBCV students in learning about the common traits, behaviours, and motivations of entrepreneurs\. The open\-access interactive tutorial will also offer foundational principles of innovation with Canadian and global examples to prepare students for entrepreneurship courses, programs and student competitions, while also improving their entrepreneurship and innovation literacy\.
