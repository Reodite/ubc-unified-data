UBC is committed to making education more affordable and accessible by supporting the integration of open educational resources \(OER\) into credit courses\. This year, over $220,000 in OER Affordability Grants were awarded to support 10 projects across six Faculties, with the goal of lowering the cost of learning materials and improving access for UBC students\. \[…\]
