  - **Cell 2:**
  
    An antiracist framework for French as a second or additional language will be a text\-based professional learning book created to educate current and future French teachers on implementing antiracist pedagogy in Canadian French language programs\. The framework will be available in English and French via UBC Pressbooks, with the option of a downloadable PDF or eBook\. Our book is being co\-authored by nine French educators from British Columbia, Ontario, Manitoba, and Quebec\. There are five sections: 1\) Critical reflection; 2\) Teaching and learning French in a settler colonial state; 3\) Antiracism 101; 4\) Examples of antiracist French activities; 5\) Addressing common concerns\. The framework includes a glossary that summarizes key concepts in accessible language, additional resources related to antiracism, decolonization, inclusive language practices, and academic references for further reading\.   
      
    Our framework is designed to be the main text for LLED352: Teaching and Learning French in Multilingual Contexts \(Elementary\), a mandatory course for all elementary\-stream Bachelor of Education students at UBC\. It can also be used in the Bachelor of Education French specialisation courses \(LLED325, 371, 372\) and in UBCO French Pathways\. Beyond UBC, the framework can be used in \(bilingual\) teacher education programs and ongoing professional learning across the country
