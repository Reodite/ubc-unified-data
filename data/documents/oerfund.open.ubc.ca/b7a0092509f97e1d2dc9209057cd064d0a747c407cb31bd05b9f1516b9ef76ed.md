| **Project Summary** | CPSC\_V 210 \(Software Construction\) is a core course that all computer science students, and those in the 12\+ combined majors programs, must take\. Approximately 1,000 students take this course each year\. Computer Science is currently embarking on a curriculum renewal process, and the first course that will be revitalized is CPSC 210\. A cornerstone of the curriculum renewal process for CPSC 210 will be integrating an online reader for the course, consisting of a free textbook custom designed for 210 learners\. This reader will have 1:1 mapping to each 210 lecture, and will carefully transition students both into the course from its predecessor, CPSC 110 but also out of the course so they are best prepared for its follow\-up course, CPSC 310\. By creating a free online textbook, we will avoid assigning a textbook which could save students \~$90,000 / year\. These readings would be accessible to students on any device without cost or registration\. It would also enable the course resources to be easily updated and extended by course staff and students would be encouraged to submit errata for improvement as well\. |
