    **Project Summary**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    Korean language programs across North America, including UBC, are experiencing rapid growth in student enrollment alongside persistent instructional staffing shortages\. This context increases the need for flexible, high\- quality learning resources that support students both inside and outside the classroom\. And today’s learners, largely Gen Z students, bring diverse learning profiles, strong digital literacy, and expectations for interactive, technology\-enhanced learning\. Commercial print textbooks and workbooks, which are often expensive and inflexible, can create financial barriers and limit equitable access to learning materials\.  
    This project will develop an Open Educational Resource \(OER\) textbook for Korean language courses at UBC\.  
      
    The OER will integrate online textbook content with guided self\-study materials, interactive practice activities, and clearly structured learning pathways aligned with learning outcomes\. Beyond foundational language skills, the materials will emphasize real\-world communication tasks across interpretive, interpersonal, and presentational modes\.  
      
    By replacing commercial textbooks with OER materials, this project will generate direct student cost savings and ensure immediate, free, and ongoing access to core course resources\. Designed with accessibility and inclusivity in mind, the OER will be adaptable for diverse learners and instructional contexts\. Through contemporary language use and embedded cultural content, the project will enhance student engagement, intercultural awareness, and long\-term sustainability\.
