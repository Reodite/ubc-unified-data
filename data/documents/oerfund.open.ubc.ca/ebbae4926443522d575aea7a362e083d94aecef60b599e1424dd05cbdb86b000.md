    **Project Summary**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    SPPH 381H \(Selected Topics\) is a new, interdisciplinary course launching in September 2026 under an existing  Senate\-approved shell\. Adopting a “Zero to AI Co\-Pilot” pedagogy, the course guides students from basic literacy to advanced AI\-augmented workflows\. Currently, replicating this curriculum would require purchasing multiple commercial textbooks for R, Python, and Visualization \(totaling \~$350\-700\), creating a significant financial barrier\. This project proposes a “Born Open” strategy: converting the course’s raw curriculum \(developed in 2024/2025 during the Principal\-Applicant’s teaching release\) into a comprehensive, open\-access textbook and interactive problem bank\. The OER will uniquely integrate the core pillars of health data science: \(1\) navigating complex health data sources; \(2\) applying data privacy, security, and Indigenous Data Sovereignty principles; \(3\) performing side\-by\-side “polyglot” analyses \(R and Python\) via AI\-assisted workflows; \(4\) auditing AI\-generated code for bias and accuracy; and \(5\) mastering “Last Mile” knowledge translation skills, from dynamic reporting to deploying  interactive dashboards for policymakers\.   
      
    By converting these materials into OER, the project will ensure SPPH 381H is zero\-cost while arming students with essential  workforce\-ready skills\. Furthermore, this resource will immediately serve as a technical companion for five  established SPPH courses \(SPPH 500, 521, 533, 604, 610\), scaling impact and affordability to over 250 students annually\.
