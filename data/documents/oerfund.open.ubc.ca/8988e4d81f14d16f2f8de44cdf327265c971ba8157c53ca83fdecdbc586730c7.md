    **Project Summary**
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    The project “Open Access Decolonized Resources in French Courses at the University and Pre\-University Level” is about publishing and sharing existing resources previously created by our team under the Students as Partners Fund \[2022/23\] and CCEL Global Fund \[2024\]\. This next step intends to create a website to host Teaching and Learning activities aligned with Equity, Diversity, Inclusion, and Decolonization approach to the French language\.  
      
    The resources published on our proposed website will aim to educate website users on how to use the non\-binary French pronoun “iel” in a classroom and how to develop and expose students to the role of being an ally to non\-binary people\. The website will be accessible to all French instructors, who may use it in their own teaching, with the goal of supporting the development of their EDID learning activities, thereby having the option to make it available to all their students, pending instructors’ interest\. The website will also be accessible to the general public, so anyone who wishes to learn/teach French outside of UBC can engage with our EDID resources\. Further, we will contact BC SOGI123 \[Sexual Orientation Gender Identities\] to include our resource within their list of materials intended to support parents of students in BC schools\. In addition, resources from our website will be shared with the following local organizations upon their interest\.
