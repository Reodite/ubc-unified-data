**Vancouver, BC, March 10th, 2024**

[Image](https://kpark.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/03/Paid-Parking.png)

Dr\. Park was recently featured in a [Vancouver Sun article](https://vancouversun.com/news/local-news/pay-parking-at-metro-vancouvers-regional-parks-raises-hackles) by Gordon McIntyre\. The article discusses the introduction of paid parking at three parks in West Vancouver and the public backlash it has sparked\.

Residents and visitors express dissatisfaction, arguing that charging for parking on public land restricts access to nature and imposes financial burdens, particularly for seniors and low\-income individuals\. Concerns are raised about the lack of alternatives for transportation, especially for those with disabilities or limited access to public transit\. Critics suggest that while managing parking congestion is important, it shouldn’t impede access to parks\.

Dr\. Park emphasizes the importance of a balanced approach to park management incorporating a better access strategy\. The article also delves into the broader implications of park ownership, environmental justice, and the need for balanced approaches to managing urban green spaces\.
