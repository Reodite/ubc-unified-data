Dr\. Geoff Wasteneys has been the Academic Director of the Bioimaging Facility since 2007 and has been responsible for writing the grants that have acquired most of the infrastructure located in BIF\. Besides grant writing and garnering support for the Bioimaging Facility, Dr\. Wasteneys has lead international workshops on imaging and is a vocal advocate for ethical data collection\.
