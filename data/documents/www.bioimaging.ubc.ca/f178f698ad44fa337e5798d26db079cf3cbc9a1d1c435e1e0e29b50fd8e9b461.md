The FOSI seminar series has returned for the academic year\! On September 26th at 3:30pm in the LSI room 1416, we will feature a seminar, “Introduction to imaging facilities at UBC: From confocal to super resolution to electron microscopy\.” Dr\. Guang Gao from LSI Imaging will speak about confocal microscopy and STED, while Dr\. Miki Fujita from the Bioimaging Facility will present on multiphoton imaging and electron microscopy\.

We encourage all those interested in learning more about available microscopy platforms, especially those who are new to UBC, to attend this seminar\. This is a great opportunity to learn what systems are available for your research and speak with the managers of imaging facilities around campus\.

As always, you can expect snacks to be provided\! [Image](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2019/09/FOSI-September-2019.png)
