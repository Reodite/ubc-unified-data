## **Capabilities**:

– Imaging and targeting of thin layers of the cell surface \(TIRF and variable\-angle near TIRF mode\)

– Single\-molecule analysis

– Fluorescence Recovery After Photobleaching \(FRAP\) on TIRF

– High\-sensitivity, high\-speed imaging with sCMOS camera

## **Cost**:

BIF members: $24/h, Academic users: $40/h

## **Specifications:**

#### Microscope:

Zeiss Observer Z1 inverted microscope

TempModuleS: Temperature\-control stage and objective lens heater

CO\[subscript: 2\]/O\[subscript: 2\]ModuleS: CO\[subscript: 2\] and O\[subscript: 2\] chamber

#### Light Source:

Solid\-laser lines for 405, 488, 561 and 633 nm

COLIBRI 2 LED lines for 365, 470, and 590 nm

63x/NA 1\.46/Oil Korr TIRF
