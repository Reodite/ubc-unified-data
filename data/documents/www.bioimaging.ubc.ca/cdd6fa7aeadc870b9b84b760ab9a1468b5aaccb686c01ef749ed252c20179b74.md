## [\(Untitled\)](https://www.bioimaging.ubc.ca/2023/06/19/3858/)

Madhureeta Das Gupta, a PhD student in MacLachlan lab \(UBC Chemistry\), uses the FEI Tecnai Spirit TEM to visualize nanofibers that were formed through the co\-assembly of Ni and Cu complexes\. By changing the ratio of Ni/Cu complex, the length of the nanofibers can be controlled\.
