Immunogold TEM is a technique used to examine the localization of proteins/molecules of interest labelled with colloidal gold\-conjugated antibodies\.

#### Equipment:

FEI Tecnai Spirit TEM  
FEI Tecnai G2 Twin TEM  
Leica EM IGL Immunogold labelling system

#### Sample preparation:

1\. Fixation: Cryo\-fixation or Chemical fixation using aldehyde fixatives  
2\. Dehydration  
3\. Infiltration/embedding in Hydrophilic LR White or HM20 resin  
4\. Ultramicrotome sectioning  
5\. Antibody incubation for Immunolabelling  
6\. Grid Staining with uranyl acetate and lead citrate  
7\. TEM imaging
