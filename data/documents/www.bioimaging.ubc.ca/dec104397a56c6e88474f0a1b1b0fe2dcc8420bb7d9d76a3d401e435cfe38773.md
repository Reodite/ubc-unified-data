The [Vitrobot](http://www.fei.com/products/sample-prep/vitrobot.aspx) is a vitrification device for plunge freezing of colloidal suspensions for cryoTEM imaging\.  The touch screen is easy to use and its temperature and humidity controlled chamber make for custom sample preparation\.

**Funded by CFI/BCKDF**
