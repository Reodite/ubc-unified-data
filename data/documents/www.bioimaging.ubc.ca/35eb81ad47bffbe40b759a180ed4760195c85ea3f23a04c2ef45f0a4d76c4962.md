The BIF has two transmission electron microscopes \(TEMs\) that are used for somewhat different reasons\. Your specific needs will determine which TEM you will be trained to operate\. For routine TEM imaging, you will most likely be trained on the Hitachi H7600 TEM\. For cryoTEM or TEM tomography, you will be trained on the FEI Tecnai TEM\.
