Focused On Scientific Imaging \(FOSI\) seminar is back for the month of March, and will take place on March 27th \(Monday\) at 1 pm in Michael Smith Laboratory \(MSL\) Auditorium 102 or Zoom \(Hybrid\)\. Claire Atkinson, Facility manager at UBC High Resolution Macromolecular Cryo\-Electron Microscopy, will be giving a talk titled “Cryo\-EM at HRMEM”, Guillermo Caballero Silva, a PhD candidate in Strynadka lab \(UBC Biochemistry\) will give a talk titled “Characterization of EspB, a T7SS virulent effector from Mycobacterium tuberculosis”\.

Prior to the talk \(12:45\-1 pm\), Snacks and Coffee will be provided for those attending in person outside the MSL auditorium\. Please arrive before the seminar starts as food and drinks are not allowed in the auditorium\.

For those who don’t know the FOSI seminar, FOSI seminar series was created to provide a space for everyone to exchange ideas about imaging techniques and to connect with the people that use the imaging techniques that you might need for your own research\.  
We hope that you can join us at this month’s FOSI seminar\! For more information, see our home page at http://fosiseminar\.wixsite\.com/fosiseminarseries\.
