## **Capabilities:**

– High\-speed multi\-color image acquisition with spinning disk

– High resolution multi\-color image with 6 laser lines \(405, 440, 488, 514, 561 and 640 nm\)

– Fast and high\-resolution image acquisition with EMCCD camera \(30\.6 frame/s, 1000 x 1000 pixels\)

## **Cost:**

Academic users: $40/h, Industry users: $60/h

[Image](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/05/PE-SDCM-scaled.jpg)

## **Specifications:**

#### Microscope:

Leica DMi8 inverted microscope

Prior motorized stage

Yokogawa CSU\-X1 spinning disk scan head

#### Illumination and Detection:

Solid\-laser lines for 405, 440, 488, 514, 561 and 640 nm

Available for DAPI, CFP, GFP, Alexa488, FITC, YFP, RFP, mCherry, Propidium iodide in switchable filter wheel

Alexa405/DAPI 435\-485

CFP 460\-500

YFP 525\-555

Alexa 488/GFP/FITC 507\-543

RFP/PI 570\-620

Alexa 635/Cy5 612\-688

#### Objectives:

10x/NA 0\.3/Air \(WD 11\.13mm\)

20x/NA 0\.7/Glycerol \(WD 2\.0mm\)

40x/NA 1\.25/Oil \(WD 0\.1mm\)

63x/NA 1\.3/Glycerol \(WD 0\.28mm\)

100x/NA 1\.4 /Oil \(WD 0\.09mm\)

#### Camera:

Hamamatsu 9100\-02 CCD camera \(30\.6 frame/s, 1000 x 1000 pixels\) for all objectives

Hamamatsu 9100\-23B CCD camera \(70\.4 frame/s, 512 x 512 pixels\) for 100x/Oil objective

#### Computer:

Improvision Volocity software operated by Windows 7
