The Bioimaging Facility is equipped with three transmission electron microscopes \(FEI Tecnai G2, Hitachi H7600 and Tecnai Spirit\) and 2 scanning electron microscopes \(Hitachi S2600 and Zeiss XB350\)\.

- **Row 1**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [**Hitachi SU8700 SEM**](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?p=4871/)  
      
    \- Standard SEM imaging with a field emission gun  
    \- [SEM imaging on non\-coated samples](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/sem-techniques/)  
    \- Energy Dispersive X\-ray analysis \(EDX\)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    [Image](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/10/Hitachi-SU8700-rotated.jpg)

- **Row 2**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [**Zeiss XB350 FIB\-SEM**](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/?p=3752/)  
    \- Standard SEM imaging with a field emission gun  
    \- CryoSEM  
    \- Focused ion beam \(FIB\)\-SEM  
    \- CryoFIB\-SEM  
    \- Energy Dispersive X\-ray analysis \(EDX\)
  
  - **Cell 2:**
  
    [Image](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/04/XB350.jpg)

- **Row 3**

  - **Cell 1:**
  
    [**FEI Tecnai G2 Twin 200kV TEM**](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/equipment/electron-microscopes/fei-tecnai-g2-tem/)  
      
    \- Maximum Resolution: 1\.4 Å with 200kV LaB6 filament  
    \- Conventional TEM imaging  
    \- [Cryo\-TEM](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/tem-techniques/)  
    \- [3D electron tomography](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/tem-techniques/)  
    \- [Correlative light and electron microscopy \(CLEM\) with iCorr system](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/tem-techniques/)
