[https://bioimaging\-2018\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2016/07/Leica\-UC7\-ultramicrotome\.jpg](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/07/Leica-UC7-ultramicrotome.jpg)[Leica UC7 ultramicrotome](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/07/Leica-UC7-ultramicrotome-150x150.jpg)The newest generation of ultramicrotome available from Leica Microsystems, the UC7 is equipped with a digital interface for custom control\. This ultramicrotome is designed for cutting ultra\-thin sections \(&lt;100nm\) of resin embedded material for TEM\. A colour digital camera and large display makes this ultramicrotome very useful for instruction\. When coupled to the Leica FC7, the newest generation of ultramicrotome cryo\-chamber, the UC7/FC7 becomes a cryo\-ultramicrotome capable of maintaining temperatures as low as \-160 C for cutting frozen samples for the Tokuyasu technique for immunolabeling as well as high\-pressure frozen samples for CEMOVIS\.
