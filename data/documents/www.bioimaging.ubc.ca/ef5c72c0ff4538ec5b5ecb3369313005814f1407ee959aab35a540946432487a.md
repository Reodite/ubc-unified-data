Intrinsic fluorescence of *Humulus lupulus* \(hops\) glandular trichomes through development, using the Olympus Multi\-photon FV1000 microscope\. Image courtesy of Sam Livingston \(PhD student\) and Dr\. Teagen Quilichini in Dr\. Lacey Samuels lab \(UBC Department of Botany\)\. Operating in two\-photon excitation mode \(720nm in wavelength\), autofluorescence was captured in two emission channels, 420\-460 nm \(false\-colour teal\) and 485\-540 nm \(false\-colour red\)\.
