[https://bioimaging\-2018\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2016/07/sputter\-coater\.jpg](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/07/sputter-coater.jpg)[Cressington sputter coater](https://bioimaging-2018.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2016/07/sputter-coater-150x150.jpg)The [Cressington 208HR](http://cressington.com/product_208hr.html) is a turbo pumped high vacuum coater set up to sputter coat a very thin, very fine layer of gold, gold/palladium, platinum or chromium\.  The coater is equipped with a tilting, rotating stage as well as a thickness monitor to ensure the coating is as even as possible\.
