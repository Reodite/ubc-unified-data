The system that we are setting up has the below specifications and features\.

-   VS200 6\-slide slide scanning system with brightfield, darkfield, fluorescence, polarized light, and Speckle Illumination \(confocal like\) fluorescence\.
-   Dual cameras, \(1\) high resolution colour and \(2\) Hamamatsu ORCA Fusion for high resolution and high sensitivity fluorescence\.
-   2, 4, 10, 20, 40, and 60xOil lenses with automated oil dispersion\.
-   Ability to image batch scan up to six \(6\) slides at a time
-   Fluorescence excitation including DAPI, AF488/GFP, Cy3, mCherry/AF568, AF594\.Texas Red, AF647/Cy5, and AF750/Cy7
