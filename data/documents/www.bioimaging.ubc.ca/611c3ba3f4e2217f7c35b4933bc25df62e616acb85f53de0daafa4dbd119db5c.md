If you are interested in learning more about porosity analysis, you may want to register for a free Dragonfly webinar on March 27, 2025\.

You will need to register to secure your spot\.  
https://dragonfly\.comet\.tech/en/events/webinar\-dragonfly\-for\-industry\-focus\-on\-porosity

You will learn:  
Manual and automated thresholding methods for porosity quantification  
Extracting pore size distributions and exporting statistical data  
High\-quality imaging and animations of porosity results  
Open vs closed porosity differentiation  
AI\-driven segmentation for complex datasets  
Real\-world applications on practical samples

After the webinar, you can use Dragonfly software in one of our image\-processing computers at the BIF\.  
They also offer non\-commercial licenses for students and academic researchers at no cost\.

If you want to know more about Dragonfly software, please ask BIF staff, we are happy to help\!
