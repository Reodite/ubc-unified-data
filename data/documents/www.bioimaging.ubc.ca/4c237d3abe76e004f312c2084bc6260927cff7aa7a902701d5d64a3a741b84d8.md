    **TEM Technician**  
      
    Valerie Pringle is a transmission electron microscope technician who started in this position in May 2023\. Prior to this, Valerie worked at BIF in a work\-learn position from May of 2022 to the end of April 2023\. Valerie recently obtained a B\.Sc\. from UBC \(class of '23\) where she majored in Integrated Sciences\. Her major gave her the opportunity to create a personalized degree that focuses on the presence and effect of genetic variation in the human endocannabinoid system; this unique program integrates topics from biological chemistry, genetics, and \(neuro\)physiology\. In the future, Valerie hopes to pursue post\-graduate studies that will enable her to contribute to the safe prescription and use of medical cannabinoids as a natural alternative to substances such as opioids\. In her spare time, Valerie enjoys reading, painting, and keeping active\.
