Dr\. Jingqian Chen, a postdoctoral fellow in Dr\. Orlando Rojas’ lab \(UBC Chemical and Biological Engineering\), uses a Zeiss Crossbeam350 FIB\-SEM to visualize monodispersed lignin particles \(left, Dr\. Jingqian Chen’s sample\)\. She also used a Tecnai Spirit TEM to show branched silica particles with bacteria cellulose fibers \(center, Dr\. Dong Wang’s sample\)\.  Finally, Dr\. Chen identified twisted particles assembled from biomolecules extracted from wood bark by using a FEI Tecnai G2 Twin TEM \(right, Xun Niu’s sample\)\.
