Image courtesy of Duo Cheng in Dr\. Vanessa Auld lab, Dept of Zoology\.

Immunofluorescence labelling of cellular proliferation within the brain lobe of 3\[rd\] instar Drosophila melanogaster larvae\.

Green fluorescence represent glial cell membrane, red fluorescence represents the phosphohistone 3, marking cellular proliferation and blue is cell nuclei\. The image was acquired by the Olympus FV1000 confocal microscope\.
