    **TEM Technician**  
      
    Jesus joined the UBC Bioimaging Facility in 2025, bringing a multidisciplinary background rooted in biomedical and biotechnological sciences, complemented with business and management education\. He has worked across both academic and industry laboratories, supporting research in advanced sample processing, ultra\-resolution microscopy, and cellular biology\. Before joining the Facility, Jesus contributed to cardiac cell research and high\-resolution imaging at Dr\. Ed Moore Lab in the Dept\. of Cellular and Physiological Sciences at UBC, and previously held supervisory and research roles focused on microbiology, protein expression, and immunoproteomics\. Outside the lab, Jesus enjoys an active lifestyle, and he’s passionate about exploring B\.C\.’s hiking trails\.
