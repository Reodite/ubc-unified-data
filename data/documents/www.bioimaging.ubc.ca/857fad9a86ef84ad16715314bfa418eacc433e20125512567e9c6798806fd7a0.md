The confocal microscopes located in the BIF are available for your project\. The PerkinElmer spinning disk with FRAP is an inverted system designed to capture dynamic events in living cells and tissues while the Olympus FV1000 MPE is an upright system capable of spectral detection and/or deep tissue imaging with its pulsed MP laser\. Contact us \(click TRAINING and fill out the form\) for more information to ensure training on the appropriate system\.
