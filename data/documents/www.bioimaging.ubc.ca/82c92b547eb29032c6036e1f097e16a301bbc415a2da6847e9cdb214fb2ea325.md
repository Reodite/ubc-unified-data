CellProfiler [https://cellprofiler\.org](https://cellprofiler.org/) is the free, open source image editing software, and the free interactive virtual workshop will be held on June 24th, 2021 from 1\-5pm \(UTC\) \(6am to 10am in Vancouver time\)\.  
The workshop is ideal for image analysis beginners \(undergraduate students and early graduated students\) who would like to learn quantitative image analyses\.  
If you are interested in attending the workshop, Please visit the link below to indicate your interest in participating by May 15th, 2021\.
