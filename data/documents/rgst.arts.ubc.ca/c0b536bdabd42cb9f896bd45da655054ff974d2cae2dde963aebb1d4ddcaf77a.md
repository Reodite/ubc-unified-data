Islamic Law: A Conversation with Professor Rumee Ahmed

Join us for an informal conversation about studying Islam and Islamic law with Professor Rumee Ahmed \(Asian Studies\)\. We will hear about his current and past research—including how to “hack” Islamic law—and what studying Islam looks like in Canada today\. Come to listen or to ask questions\!

*Light refreshments will be provided\.*

[Image](https://rgst.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/01/RA-for-AS-scaled-1-200x150.jpg) **Rumee Ahmed** holds a Canada Research Chair in Theology &amp; Ethics, studying and teaching Islamic law and theology at UBC\. His research spans religion, law, theology, philosophy, and hermeneutics\. His books include [*Sharia Compliant: A User’s Guide to Hacking Islamic Law*](https://www.sup.org/books/religious-studies/sharia-compliant) \(2018\), [*Islamic Theology in 36 Dialogues \(and 60 Lessons\): A Cultural Translation of Abu al\-Yusr al\-Pazdawi’s* Usul al\-Din](https://utppublishing.com/doi/10.3138/9781487575618) \(2025\), and [*The Oxford Handbook on Islamic Law*](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28121) \(2019\)\.
