### Applying For Your Emeritus &amp; Retired Staff UBCcard

Please ensure that you have the following before filling out your application online:

-   Your seven digit UBC employee number
-   Your CWL username and password
-   A colour digital photo meeting [UBCcard program specifications](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/obtaining-a-ubccard/photo-specifications)

[Emeritus &amp; Retired staff online application form](https://services.ubccard.ubc.ca/)

**Picking up your UBCcard:**

We care about the health and safety of our staff and the UBC Community and have developed a safe and quick pick up process\! Once your card is ready, an email will be sent with pick up location\.

***\*Individuals who request a physical UBC card with their application and fail to pick it up \(past 1 year\) will have their card returned to de\-active status and subsequently destroyed\. If a replacement card is needed under these circumstances, a replacement fee will apply\.***
