### I lost my UBCcard / my UBCcard was stolen\. What do I do?

Lost or stolen UBCcards must be reported immediately\. Stolen UBCcards can be used against you in the form of identity theft\. As such, it is important to notify us immediately in the event your card is stolen\. UBC departments \(including the Bookstore, Library, Food Services, Housing, Athletics and others\) assume no responsibility for fraudulent use of your UBCcard before the Lost or Stolen Card Notification Form is submitted and processed by UBC\.  
[Report your lost or stolen UBCcards online\.](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/card-renewals-lost-stolen-cards/loststolen-cards)

### I have to renew my UBCcard?

UBCcards are re\-issued at no cost within 2 months of the expiration date printed on the back of the card\. Renewal eligibility is for students who are enrolled in at least one class in the following term\. Submit a Renewal application [online](https://services.ubccard.ubc.ca/)\.

Cardholders are required to update their digital photos at this time\.

### Do I need my UBCcard to obtain my UPass?

You must  have a valid UBCcard to obtain your U\-Pass\. Please note that once a a renewal is submitted online, your expired UBCcard will no longer allow access to obtain your U\-Pass\.

### I use my UBCcard for keyless access\. How will renewing my card affect this?

It takes one hour for your renewed UBCcard to be updated into the system\. Please continue to use your expired card until your renewed card allows entry into the department/building which you have access to\.\.

[Renew your UBCcard](https://services.ubccard.ubc.ca/)
