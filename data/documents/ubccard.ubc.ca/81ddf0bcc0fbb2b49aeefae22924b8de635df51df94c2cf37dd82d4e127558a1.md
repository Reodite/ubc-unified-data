#### [https://ubccard\.ubc\.ca/slider/1449](https://ubccard.ubc.ca/slider/1449)

Ubcard applications are accepted on\-line\. Once ready, cards can be picked up from the Access Desk located inside the UBC Bookstore at 6200 University Blvd\. For our hours of operation visit www\.parking\.ubc\.ca 

**Students enrolled in courses can apply for their UBCcard up to 2 months before classes begin\. An email notification will be sent once the card is ready for pick up\.  
Students moving into UBC Student Housing or participating in Jump Start will receive their UBCcard in their welcome package on their first day\.**
