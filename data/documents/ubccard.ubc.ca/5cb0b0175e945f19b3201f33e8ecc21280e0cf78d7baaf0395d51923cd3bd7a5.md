If you have lost your UBCcard or it has been stolen, there is a two\-step process for obtaining a new UBCcard\.

Step1: If your card is used for access, inform your supervisor\!

### **Step 1: If your card is used for access, inform your supervisor\!**

### **[Step 2: Request a new card \(link\)](https://services.ubccard.ubc.ca/)**

### **[Step 3: Deactivate your meal plan dollars here](http://secure.housing.ubc.ca/)** 

**\*Once a UBCcard has been reported lost or stolen, it cannot be reactivated\.** 

**The replacement fee for a new UBCcard is $15 plus tax\. This fee may be waived if the card was stolen and you provide a valid police file number\. Please note that fees are subject to change without notice\.**
