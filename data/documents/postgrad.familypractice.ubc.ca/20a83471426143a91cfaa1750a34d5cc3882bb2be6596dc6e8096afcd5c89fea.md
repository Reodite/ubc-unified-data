### Second Iteration Application Process

Please refer to [CaRMS Eligibility Criteria](https://www.carms.ca/match/r-1-main-residency-match/eligibility-criteria/#tab-1569521374022-4-2) for details\.

#### Selection Process

Applicants will be shortlisted based on their clinical experience, past performance, exam scores and suitability for the site\(s\) with available position\.  The overall selection process is based on File review and Interview scores\. Please refer to the Second iteration Program Description on Carms\.ca

#### Interviews

Interviews for second iteration are offered by invitation only\. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted by the program\. Notification for interview will be made by email\.
