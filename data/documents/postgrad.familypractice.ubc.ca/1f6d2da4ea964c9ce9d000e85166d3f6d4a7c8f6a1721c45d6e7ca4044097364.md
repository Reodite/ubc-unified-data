### Scheduling Glossary

Sites may use the same term for schedules that could differ in timing, sequencing and combination of experiences\. The terms below describe the overall or predominant schedule structure rather than a standardized model\.

-   **Block:** Training is organized into dedicated periods, usually several weeks, focused on one discipline or clinical setting at a time\.
-   **Longitudinal:** Residents return regularly to the same discipline or clinical setting over an extended period while completing other training experiences\.
-   **Partial Longitudinal:** Some experiences continue regularly over an extended period, while the remainder of the curriculum is organized in blocks or another format\.
-   **Partial Horizontal:** Some disciplines or clinical experiences recur alongside other rotations rather than being completed as separate, dedicated blocks\.
-   **Partial Integrated:** Some disciplines or clinical experiences are combined within the ongoing schedule, while others remain separate\.
-   **Integrated Longitudinal/Block:** The schedule combines longitudinal learning experiences with dedicated block rotations\.
-   **Integrated:** Multiple disciplines and clinical experiences are intentionally combined within an ongoing schedule rather than organized as separate rotations\.
-   **Block, Longitudinal FP**: Most training is organized in blocks, with Family Practice continuing regularly throughout the year\.
-   **Longitudinal FP:** Family Practice is scheduled regularly over an extended period, supporting continuity with patients, preceptors and the clinical setting\.
