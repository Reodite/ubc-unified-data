## MedNet

**Reimbursements for:** mandatory rotations, rural rotations, mandatory events, extended commuting/off\-site callback, leadership retreat or standing committee meetings

##### Step 1:  
Resident

Resident submits reimbursable amount to the PGME MedNet site

##### Step 2:  
Site Admin

Shown as “Program Approval”

Site administrator reviews the claim and approves/sends back for edits

##### Step 3: Central Program

Shown as “Department Approval”

Central program reviews the claim and approves/sends back for edits

##### Step 4: PGME

PGME reviews the claim and approves/sends back for edits

## Workday

**Reimbursements for:** conference registration, site\-specific resident retreat, resident education fund expenses, scholarship project

##### Step 1:  
Resident

Resident submits receipts for reimbursable expenses to the site administrator

##### Step 2:  
Site Admin

Site administrator submits and expense report in Workday on the resident’s behalf

##### Step 3:  
Central Program

Central program reviews the expense report and approves/sends back for edits
