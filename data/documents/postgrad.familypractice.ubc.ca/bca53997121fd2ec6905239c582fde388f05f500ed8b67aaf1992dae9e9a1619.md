Burns Lake

**Number of Residents:** 1CMG  
**Location:** Small Town/ Rural &amp; Remote, BC  
**Community:** 10,000  
**Hospital:** Lakes District Hospital and Health Centre

**Curriculum Type:** Longitudinal FP  
**R2 Elective Time:** Resident driven

## The Town

You won’t ﬁnd any rush hour traﬃc here in Burns Lake\. Just charming small\-town energy and easy walking distance to all the services and amenities you need, including great independent coﬀee shops, a growing arts scene, vibrant Indigenous heritage, locally owned specialty stores and lively events\. 

Burns Lake is a rural village in the north\-western\-central interior of British Columbia\. The village has a population of 1,659\. There are multiple lakes in town and surrounding area to enjoy\. The surrounding community is growing as well, with exciting new development projects underway, including a new golf course and disc golf course\. These additions complement Burns Lake’s longstanding cross\-country ski trails, world\-class mountain biking, and endless opportunities for ﬁshing, hunting, and camping\. Located just 2\.5 hours from the northern hub of Prince George and 1\.5 hours from Smithers it has rivers, mountains, and a ski hill\. Burns Lake oﬀers an exceptional balance of rural lifestyle, outdoor adventure, and access to regional amenities\.

## The Clinical Experience 

Burns Lake’s primary medical clinic is growing, with three recent UBC Residency graduates  
and two new PRA physicians joining the established team of two part\-time physicians and  
two nurse practitioners\. Eager to teach, collaborate, and build a strong rural\-focused  
community, the team offers an excellent environment for a rural resident seeking  
meaningful hands\-on learning, supportive mentorship, and the opportunity to become an  
integral part of a welcoming northern practice\.

  
The resident would also have the opportunity to work with the medical team at the Carrier  
Sekani Family Services \(CSFS\) medical clinic providing family medicine care in town as well  
as doing community outreach to the surrounding First Nation communities\. There are 6  
First Nation communities that practitioners have the privilege ofserving in and around the  
Burns Lake area: Burns Lake Band, Lake Babine Nation, Wet’suwet’en First  
Nation, Cheslatta Carrier Nation, Nee Tahi Buhn First Nation and Skin Tyee First Nation\.  
There are numerous opportunities to grow as a physician and gain a better understanding  
of the complexities of rural care, the Social Determinants of Health and health equity\.

  
Burns Lake is a small, tight knit community that offers exposure to prenatal care, chronic  
disease management, adolescent medicine, addictions medicine, emergency medicine and  
Indigenous health\. The catchment area serves close to 10,000 people\. There are no  
additional specialists in community, leading the on\-call physicians to manage complex  
cases while being able to consult healthcare members in adjacent communities when  
needed\. Physicians on call are responsible for any patients admitted in the ER and have the  
opportunity to provide inpatient care as well\. The hospital in town is called the Lakes  
District Hospital and houses one trauma bay, a procedure room, a gyne room, 4 other ER  
exam rooms, a palliative care room, 13 acute care beds\. There is also a community clinic on  
the Southside that requires a \(free\) ferry to get to and a clinic at Wetʼsuwetʼen First Nation  
which residents would also have the opportunity to work at\.

  
Burns Lake is also home to the new Foundry clinic and as such also allows for the keen  
learner to become involved in youth mental health, additions and adolescent  
medicine\.

## The Community

Burns Lake is a community that loves to get together and celebrate with events such as Canada Day, Halloween parties for the kids, parades, community hall dinners, and a festival for the performing arts, and the Big Pig Bike Festival\!

Burns Lake is a great community for families or couples to enjoy small\-town living or country living on acreage\.

Enjoy the local culture, no commute, and the wonder of nature\.
