## Site Director Message

The Okanagan South UBC Family Practice Residency Program is based in Penticton/*snpink’tn* and serves a population of 90,000 in the immediate and surrounding area\.  We are blessed to live, work, and play on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the Okanagan Syilx people\. 

Established in 2016, our site expanded to 6 residents in 2024 \(5 CMG, 1 IMG\)\.  We are a *rural* program, providing a total of five months of rural placements, as well as opportunities to practice in nearby underserved communities\.  Primary preceptors in Penticton, Summerland, and Oliver host residents in their home offices over the duration of residency\.  Residents also spend plenty of time spent with GPs with focused areas of practice \(e\.g\. addictions, hospitalist, maternity\)\.  We have close relationships with our specialist colleagues, and residents spend time with the full spectrum of specialties, often as the only learner\.  Out of office training is primarily at Penticton Regional Hospital and South Okanagan General Hospital \(Oliver\)\.  Our site is unique in having a Critical Care Skills lead faculty to ensure residents feel prepared to work in rural and high acuity settings by coordinating acute and emergency skills sessions, SIM learning and POCUS training\.  Recently built spaces for learners \(call rooms, lounge, library, conference rooms\) provide comfort and convenience\. 

Penticton is a great place to live, play, work, and raise a family, during residency and beyond\.  Recreational opportunities abound, and we strive to support our residents to maintain a healthy work\-life balance\.  Please see our 2\-min video of residents \(and former Site Director Dr\. Michelle Linekin\) having fun at work and at play\. 

We hope to see you as an applicant and future colleague\! 

Dr\. Robin Masson and Dr\. Jeff Hoekstra, Interim Co\-Site Directors

## Lead Resident Message

Penticton is a stunning city of 33,000 nestled between Okanagan and Skaha Lakes\. With 200\+ vineyards, mountain biking, climbing, ski hills, and endless water sports, it’s no wonder the region attracts athletes, vacationers, and physicians alike\. 

   
 Our program is known for its collegial, knowledgeable, and welcoming community\. From the moment you join, local physicians and residents embrace you, ensuring a smooth transition through social and administrative support\. Our program administrator, Kristen Hart, has earned multiple awards for her dedication to learners\.  

**Learner Opportunities**    
 Now in its tenth year, our program offers a unique, learner\-centered model\. With only family medicine residents on site, you’re first in line for procedures, deliveries, and surgical assists—often with a one\-to\-one teacher ratio\. We emphasize education over service, supported by monthly SIM sessions, a strong POCUS curriculum, and experiences at the award\-winning Martin Street Outreach Clinic\. Flexibility allows you to pursue specific areas of interest\.  

**Continuity of Care**    
 Spending your first year and much of your second in Penticton allows you to build meaningful relationships with patients and physicians\. Weekly family practice clinics ensure continuity of care and the chance to refine your long\-term management skills\.  

**Neighbouring** **Opportunities**    
 Residents gain valuable experience in nearby communities such as Princeton, Oliver, and Keremeos, as well as in larger centres like Kelowna and Vernon\. Training also includes work with marginalized populations at the Penticton Indian Band, Foundry Centre for youth, and Martin Street Outreach\.  

**New Hospital Facilities**    
 Our $312M hospital, completed in 2019, is equipped with MRI, CT, 3D Cardiac ECHO, and more\. Dedicated UBC space offers call rooms, lockers, showers, a resident lounge, SIM lab, and on\-site library support—ensuring both comfort and growth throughout training\.
