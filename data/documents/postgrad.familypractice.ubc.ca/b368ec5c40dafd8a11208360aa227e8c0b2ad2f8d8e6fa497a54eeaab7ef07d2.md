### Method and Written Report Guidelines

An essay is a way of presenting your research and knowledge about a topic in a succinct and coherent way so that others may learn from your work\.  An essay usually presents your idea \(or thesis\) in an objective way with a critique of available evidence\.

Therefore, an essay must include a review and critical analysis of the existing situation and relevant literature\.  It should have a traditional structure with introduction, body, conclusion, and annotated references\.  The introduction should include a brief orientation to the topic, your thesis, and a brief summary of the main points\.

Word count: min 2000, max 3000\.

Your essay must be written in English and must be defended orally at Resident Research Day

Some essay topic examples:

-   [**clinical** **controversy** **or ethics**](https://postgrad.familymed.ubc.ca/resident-resources/scholarship/resident-scholar-project/types-of-projects/analytic-essay/ethics/) \(Eg\.  “the right to die”\)
-   [**connecting basic science to clinical** **practice**](https://postgrad.familymed.ubc.ca/resident-resources/scholarship/resident-scholar-project/types-of-projects/analytic-essay/basic-science/) \(Eg\.  hypothesis that genome research will achieve a cure for type 1 diabetes\)
-   [**advocacy** **initiative**](https://postgrad.familymed.ubc.ca/resident-resources/scholarship/resident-scholar-project/types-of-projects/analytic-essay/health-advocacy/) \(Eg\. refugee health services\)
-   **[innovation](https://postgrad.familymed.ubc.ca/resident-resources/scholarship/resident-scholar-project/types-of-projects/analytic-essay/innovation/)**
-   [**new** **program**](https://postgrad.familymed.ubc.ca/resident-resources/scholarship/resident-scholar-project/types-of-projects/analytic-essay/program-description/) \(Eg\. safe injection sites\)
-   [**health** **policy**](https://postgrad.familymed.ubc.ca/resident-resources/scholarship/resident-scholar-project/types-of-projects/analytic-essay/health-policy/) \(Eg\. aboriginal children in foster care\)
