If you or someone you are with is in immediate danger, please call 911\.

The Faculty of Medicine policy on the Health and Safety of Postgraduate Medical Trainees can be found in the [Policies and Procedures Manual\.](http://postgrad.med.ubc.ca/current-trainees/policies-procedures/)

Program specific policies on safety can be found under [Resident Safety](https://fom-familypractice-2025.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resident-resources/policies-guidelines/general-policies/health-safety/)\.

Site specific policies on safety can be found within the [site\-specific](https://med-fom-fmprpostgrad.sites.olt.ubc.ca/resident-resources/site-specific-information/) portion of the website\.

If you have been mistreated or have witnessed mistreatment, please [click here](https://mistreatmenthelp.med.ubc.ca/?login) for supports\.
