# **Family Practice Postgraduate Program**

*We are the UBC Family Practice Postgraduate Program—the largest family medicine residency program in Canada\. Our distributed training is delivered at 23 sites across the province, ranging from large urban centres to rural regions of BC\. Over the course of two years, residents gain direct experience working in hospital and community\-based settings and engage in a wide variety of scholarly activities\.*
