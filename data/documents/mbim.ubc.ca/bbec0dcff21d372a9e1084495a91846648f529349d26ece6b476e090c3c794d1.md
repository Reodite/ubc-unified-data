##### Our graduate program coursework provides instruction in advanced topics, research techniques, and professional skills in microbiology and immunology\. Course offerings are updated each year but have previously included:  
 

-   Microbiological and Immunological Research: Development of core skillset needed to conduct research with a focus on written and oral communication skills, peer review skills, scientific ethics, and the ability to critically evaluate papers in the literature  
     
-   Flow cytometry: Introduction to flow cytometry with an emphasis on experimental design, antibody panel generation, data acquisition, and analysis  
     
-   Teaching and Learning in Microbiology and Immunology: Introduction to the latest teaching principles and best practices to enhance teaching and learning in science  
     
-   Data Science Research in Microbiology and Immunology: Hands\-on experience with computational tools used to process, visualize, and analyze datasets from microbiology and immunology research  
     
-   Advanced Immunogenetics: Examination of current and emerging concepts of immunology and immunogenetics in health and disease  
     
-   **Ecological and Synthetic Microbiology**: Exploration of new disciplinary interfaces between microbial ecology and biological engineering  
     
-   Select topics in microbiology, immunology, and virology
