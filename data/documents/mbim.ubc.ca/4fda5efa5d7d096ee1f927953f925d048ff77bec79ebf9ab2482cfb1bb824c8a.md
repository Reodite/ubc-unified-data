**Ecological and evolutionary consequences of carbon preferences in bacteria**

Bacteria utilize different carbon sources preferentially\. In this talk, I will argue that phylogenetic and metabolic constraints shape trait distributions of heterotrophic bacteria, and that these traits have important consequences for the ecology and evolution of bacterial populations\.
