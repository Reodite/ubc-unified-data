## Assistant Professor of Teaching

[Evelyn Sun](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/square_400/public/profile-images/2025-12/Evelyn%20Sun.png.webp?itok=AxfOUpre)

[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://mbim.ubc.ca/people/evelyn-sun)

I am broadly interested in how we can implement ways to promote science literacy in the classroom\. The focus of my post\-doctoral work which has continued to this day is to expand the number of course\-based undergraduate research experiences \(CUREs\) in the department\. I am interested in adapting a long\-running CURE model in our department to include research in various areas of interest\. I have recently been involved in the development of data science CURE that focuses on microbial ecology\.

**Awards and Distinctions:**

-   Killam Teaching Assistant Award \(2018\)
-   John Richard Turner Graduate Student Scholarship \(2018\)
-   CIRTL Teaching as Research Award \(2017\)
