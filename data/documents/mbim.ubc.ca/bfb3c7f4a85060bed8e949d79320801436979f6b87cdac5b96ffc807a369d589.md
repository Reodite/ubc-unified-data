##### The Department of Microbiology and Immunology offers several programs that lead to a graduate degree\. These include: 

##### **Master’s \(MSc\)** **Degree**

The MSc program is a research\-based program consisting of coursework, a teaching assistantship, and completion of a written thesis document and oral thesis defence\. MSc students will hone their observational, technological, and critical thinking skills and gain valuable research experience that will advance their scientific interests, depth of knowledge, and scope of career prospects\.  

L earn more about the program and the application and admission process:

[More information](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/graduate-degree-programs/master-of-science-microbiology-immunology)

##### **Doctor of Philosophy \(PhD\)** **Degree**

The PhD program is a research\-based program consisting of coursework, a teaching assistantship, publication of original research, presentation of an exit seminar, and completion of a written thesis document and oral thesis defence\. PhD students will become experts in their field of research and grow into independent, critical, and creative thinkers with original contributions to research\. 

L earn more about the program and the application and admission process:

[More information](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/graduate-degree-programs/phd-microbiology-immunology)

##### **Doctor of Medicine/Doctor of Philosophy \(MD/PhD\) Degree**

The combined MD/PhD program is a seven\-year program offered jointly by the Faculty of Medicine and Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies\. The program provides the opportunity to combine medical school experience with intensive scientific training for students pursuing careers as clinician\-scientists\.  

L earn more about the program and the application and admission process:

[More information](https://mdprogram.med.ubc.ca/mdphd/)

**Please note: In order to be admitted to a graduate program, applicants must secure a research supervisor\. Applicants are not required to have found a supervisor prior to applying; however, acceptance is contingent upon securing a supervisor\. We encourage applicants to contact the individual research faculty members they are interested in working with via email\.**
