## Lecturer

[Sreeparna Vappala](https://mbim.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/styles/square_400/public/profile-images/2025-12/20250708_104031.jpeg.webp?itok=MkFplDxw)

[Embedded svg \(view source\)](https://mbim.ubc.ca/people/sreeparna-vappala)

My teaching interests focus on helping students develop scientific thinking and effective science communication skills\. I have taught and coordinated large enrollment courses in cell biology, advanced genetics, developmental biology, and biochemistry at the University of Manitoba and the University of British Columbia\. At the University of British Columbia, I am part of the teaching team for SCIE 113 \(First\-Year Seminar in Science\) and MICB 323 \(a third year immunology and virology lab\), among others\.

In my teaching, I have worked on course redesigns incorporating flipped classrooms, two\-stage exams, peer evaluation tools like Peerceptiv, blended learning strategies, and hands\-on modeling activities, even in large classrooms\. These practices promote active learning, engagement, and meaningful feedback\.

Beyond the classroom, I am interested in supporting faculty development\. For example, I have been involved in peer observation initiatives and recently co\-organized the 2025 Association for Biology Laboratory Education conference in Winnipeg\.

As a lecturer, I am particularly interested in integrating undergraduate research experiences into microbiology labs to help students develop critical thinking and take ownership of their learning\. I am also interested in designing training programs for graduate teaching assistants to lead undergraduate research projects, supporting their growth as educators, mentors, and researchers\.
