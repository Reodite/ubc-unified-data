**Advancing tuberculosis drug discovery by new chemical scaffolds and harnessing the concept of target protein degradation** 

Tuberculosis \(TB\), caused by *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* \(*Mtb*\)*,* is the leading global killer among infectious diseases\. Treatment is prolonged and complex, and rising multi drug resistance \(MDR\) bacteria worsen outcomes, underscoring the need for better and more effective TB drugs\. My research focuses on the discovery of new compounds active against *Mtb* and their structural optimization to generate novel lead candidates\. In my talk, I will discuss the identification and medicinal chemistry\-based optimization of a new chemical scaffold with a potential to be advanced to preclinical studies for targeting drug\-resistant TB\. In addition, I will present a complementary research program aimed at developing a novel host\-directed therapy \(HDT\) for TB\. This work applies the emerging concept of targeted protein degradation \(TPD\), a rapidly advancing drug\-development approach that enables modulation of disease\-relevant targets that have been historically challenging to address using conventional small\-molecule therapies\.
