The Microbiology and Immunology Undergraduate Research Symposium provides students an opportunity to share their research findings, develop communication skills, and network with UBC faculty and peers\. The symposium will feature research our undergraduate students have conducted through working in research laboratories and participating in our experiential learning courses\. This year, the symposium will consist of 10\- and 3\-minute oral presentations, posters, and a keynote lecture on regulating human immunity from Professor Megan Levings\. Visit the [Undergraduate Research Symposium website](https://blogs.ubc.ca/mbimurs/) for more information\.
