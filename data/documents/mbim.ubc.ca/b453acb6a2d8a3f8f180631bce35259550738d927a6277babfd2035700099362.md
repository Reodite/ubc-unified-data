Postdoctoral Research Fellow

At UBC, we believe that attracting and sustaining a diverse workforce is key to the successful pursuit of excellence
in research, innovation, and learning for all faculty, staff, and students. Our commitment to employment equity
helps achieve inclusion and fairness, brings rich diversity to UBC as a workplace, and creates the necessary
conditions for a rewarding career.

The expected pay range for this position is $60,000 - $65,000 per annum.

POSTING JOB TITLE: Postdoctoral Research Fellow – Immunology (Domeier Lab)
VP/FACULTY: Faculty of Science
DEPARTMENT: Microbiology & Immunology
EMPLOYMENT TERM: Three years
EXPECTED START DATE: November 1, 2026




 Job Summary
 Fully funded positions are available for highly motivated candidates interested in investigating vaccine-
 generated immune memory and mechanisms of protective memory B cell recall in the Domeier laboratory at
 the University of British Columbia (UBC). We are looking for a motivated candidate with good organizational
 skills who enjoys working collaboratively to advance scientific knowledge.
    The candidate is expected to lead a defined immunology project using a combination of spectral flow
 cytometry, fluorescent microscopy and transcriptomic approaches to study immune memory in human
 organoid cultures and knockout/transgenic mouse models. The candidate will also contribute to other ongoing
 projects of the laboratory, assisting other experiments whenever necessary and training other members. The
 candidate will be expected to present their work both internally and externally and contribute to the
 development of manuscripts.
    The preferred candidate will have a Ph.D. in immunology, biochemistry, cell biology, microbiology, virology,
 or a related field. Further experience in flow cytometry, fluorescent microscopy, molecular biology, standard
 immunological assays, in vivo experiments using mouse models, and CL2 work with virus is also preferred.
 Postdoctoral Fellow Training at UBC: The Domeier laboratory is affiliated with several research clusters,
 including the Preparing for Pandemics through Rapid Research in Evolution (PrePARE) and Autoimmune
 Biomedical Collaborative (ABC) research, providing access to extensive opportunities for interdisciplinary
 collaboration in immunology and infectious disease research. The Domeier lab is also actively involved in
 several research training programs funded by NSERC, including the ImmunoEngineering (ImmunoE) research
 cluster and the Biodevices for Human-Based Model Systems (BioHuMS).
