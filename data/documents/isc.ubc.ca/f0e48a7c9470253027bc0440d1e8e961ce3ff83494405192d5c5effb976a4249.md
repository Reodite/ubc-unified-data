The Integrated Service Centre \(ISC\) leadership team sets the priorities and goals for the ISC, including our purpose and mission, and aligns our work to the [UBC Strategic Plan](https://strategicplan.ubc.ca/)\. They facilitate [collaboration](https://isc.ubc.ca/continuous-improvement/collaboration "Collaboration") with departments and faculties across UBC, and support the continued development of Workday and its role in UBC administration, operations, and systems\. 

Learn more about our leaders below and see [Contact Us](https://isc.ubc.ca/contact "Contact Us") for other key contacts\. Explore our website to learn more about [what the ISC does](https://isc.ubc.ca/about "About") and [Workday at UBC](https://isc.ubc.ca/about/workday "About Workday")\.

**Harjot Guram** [**\#harjot**](https://isc.ubc.ca/about/leadership#)

Senior Director, Integrated Service Centre \(ISC\)

[harjot\.guram@ubc\.ca](mailto:harjot.guram@ubc.ca)

As the Senior Director, Integrated Service Centre \(ISC\), Harjot works in partnership with the Director, Enterprise Automation and Solution Delivery to deliver integrated services to the UBC community\. A key part of this role is to partner across UBC to deliver on service requirements for the UBC community through a collaborative, inclusive approach\.

The Senior Director co\-leads strategy implementation in a “two in a box” model with the Director, Enterprise Automation and Solution Delivery\. Both Directors are accountable for the planning, development, implementation, control, review and delivery of strategy, including ensuring an excellent customer service experience for faculty, staff and student employees\. 

The Senior Director leads, mentors and inspires the team to deliver service excellence, including working with and inspiring their peers and key stakeholders on continuous improvement activities to deliver on UBC goals\. This critical work also includes collaboratively creating and sustaining a culture with and for the UBC community that supports UBC’s vision, purpose and values\.

Harjot joined UBC in January of 2018 as Director, Payroll and Benefits with the Financial Operations department\. He brings with him a wealth of experience in payroll, accounts payable, and procurement services from Simon Fraser University, where he was Associate Director, Financial Operations for five years\. He has also worked at ADP as the Manager, Implementation where he provided excellent customer service and business solutions to his clients across Canada\.

Harjot’s qualifications include a Master of Business Administration, an undergraduate degree in Business, and over 10 years of experience in a public sector, multi\-union environment\.

In his spare time, Harjot enjoys playing recreational hockey and going skiing\. He volunteers on the executive at both Burnaby Minor Hockey and Metro Ball Hockey and enjoys coaching children in both recreational and competitive ball hockey\.

**Laleh Mosadegh** [**\#laleh**](https://isc.ubc.ca/about/leadership#)

Director, Enterprise Automation and Solution Delivery \(OCIO\)

[laleh\.mosadegh@ubc\.ca](mailto:laleh.mosadegh@ubc.ca)

Laleh splits her time between the ISC and the Office of the Chief Information Officer\. Her role at the ISC is to lead the technical aspects of Workday, including product management and application sustainment\.

With over 10 years at UBC, Laleh has had the opportunity to work on many interesting projects and initiatives\. These include the implementation of a new recruitment and admission system for international and domestic undergraduate students, the establishment of Integration Enablement Center to allow UBC systems to exchange data in real time, and the Integrated Renewal Project\. She enjoys making connections, both between different tools and systems and with people across the university\.

Laleh began her IT career with Accenture, where she did project management, vendor management and help desk management\. She first joined UBC as a Client Services Manager with IT, a role that allowed her to meet with many departments and faculties\.

She has lived in several countries, but is very happy to call the city of Vancouver home\. Making things better is her hobby, whether it is designing a home, creating art, or investing time to nurture the growth and success of her team\.

Senior Product Manager

As the Senior Product Manager, Henrik is responsible for ensuring the products and services delivered by the ISC continually meet the needs of the UBC community by managing and overseeing the strategic development and implementation of product and service improvements over time\.

Henrik has been with UBC since 2019 as a Senior Project Manager, UBC IT leading the integration of the RISe and Blackbaud applications with Workday\. In her role as Senior Manager, Change Management, Learning and Enablement, Rakhee oversees all aspects of change management for the ISC, including communications, knowledge management and training\. Her team supports UBC faculty, staff, and student employees in using Workday effectively\.

Rakhee is a knowledgeable change management practitioner with experience working in complex organizations and on major digital transformation projects\. She is a certified Prosci Change Management Practitioner, Project Management Professional \(PMP\) and Certified Scrum Master \(CSM\)\.

Her interests include travel, sustainable fashion, hiking, camping, low waste living, and home organization\.
