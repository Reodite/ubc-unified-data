To see what’s new or updated in Workday, visit [Workday This Week](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article&amp;sysparm_article=KB0017265) \(CWL required\)\. Workday as a system is constantly changing, and Workday This Week is where you can find summaries of important changes, as well as links to related resources in the Workday Knowledge Base and training courses\.

[Read Workday This Week](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article&amp;sysparm_article=KB0017265)

While Workday This Week is accessible to all faculty, staff and student employees, it’s especially recommended for those with an administrative role, such as managers of teams, people who oversee budgets, or who perform specific functions or tasks in Workday\.

Workday This Week focuses on changes in Workday as a system\. For news related to UBC policy, procedures, and best practices, visit the [HR](https://hr.ubc.ca/working-ubc/news), [Finance](https://finance.ubc.ca/announcements) and other UBC websites\.  

To stay up to date on the most recent updates, check Workday This Week regularly\. Workday This Week posts are also often featured in relevant newsletters such as the [HR Network News](https://hr.ubc.ca/news-and-events/news/hr-network-news) and [Finance Update](https://communications.vpfo.ubc.ca/publish/internal-channels-vpfo/vpfo-newsletters/#:~:text=Community%20Newsletters)\. 

For information about current known issues \(i\.e\. situations where Workday is not working as expected\), visit the [Workday Knowledge Base](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_view4&amp;kb_knowledge_base=af5ffe361b8ad4105edd43b4bd4bcb09) \(CWL required\)\. System\-wide Workday outages or performance issues will be posted on the [IT Status Page](https://status.it.ubc.ca/)\.
