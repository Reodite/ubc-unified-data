May 26, 2026

*Rakhee Raj, Senior Manager, Change Management and Communications*, *and Veekay Padhi, Senior Manager, Service Centre, on how the ISC is improving resources and support for faculty and staff\.* 

The Integrated Service Centre is continuously working to improve Workday and related systems, and we are equally committed to improving how we support the faculty and staff who use Workday\. With this goal in mind, the ISC recently completed two initiatives to give faculty and staff new ways to find information and resources as well as stay up to date with any system changes\. Read more about these updates below, or [watch our video overview](https://isc.ubc.ca/news/may-05-2026/continuing-grow-and-improve-workday-resources-and-support#video)\.

#### Workday Knowledge Base Redesign and New Features 

When faculty and staff visit the [Workday Knowledge Base homepage](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_view4&amp;kb_knowledge_base=af5ffe361b8ad4105edd43b4bd4bcb09), they will see a redesigned space with improved navigation, dynamic content, more targeted search, and new features and functionality\. Changes include:

-   Updated categories and quick access to subcategories to make it easier to browse by topic\.
-   Central search bar that only shows results from the Workday Knowledge Base \(not IT, etc\.\)
-   Dynamic cards that show which resources are new, recently updated, and most popular\.
-   Quick access to useful links, such as Workday support and relevant UBC websites\. 

A common piece of feedback from the community was that it can be challenging to keep track of what’s changed in Workday, as well as what’s changed in terms of UBC practices\. The new ‘Subscribe’ feature will address this as faculty and staff can now choose to receive an email notification when a knowledge base resource is updated with important changes\. Additionally, when there is a significant update to a task or process, a new Recent Updates section on individual resources will show what changed, and when, for easy reference\. 

From the new knowledge base homepage, faculty and staff can find their personalized list of Favorites and Subscriptions, quickly see when new resources have been added, and browse or search by topic more easily\. 

We hope these updates will make our knowledge base more useful and valuable for the community\. 

#### ISC Chatbot 

Our new [ISC chatbot](https://isc.ubc.ca/resources-support/isc-chatbot "ISC Chatbot"), found on the knowledge base homepage, will give faculty and staff a new way to get quick answers to their Workday questions and to find information and resources\. 

Sometimes, people just need a quick reminder on how to do something or have a specific question\. The ISC chatbot can help by sharing relevant information from the knowledge base or UBC websites directly in the chat, as well as links where they can learn more\. The chatbot is available 24/7 and can help answer straightforward questions, allowing our support team to spend more time on complex inquires\. 

The chatbot will also learn over time through community feedback, as it is powered by GenAI\. Currently, the chatbot can only help with general, HR, Finance, and Payroll topics, but we hope to be able to expand it to include Workday Student topics for faculty and staff in the future\. 

While we are excited to provide this new tool to the UBC community, we want to emphasize that this will not replace our dedicated support team, who will continue to provide personalized support by phone, email, and through Workday support requests\. 

#### Learn More and Contact Us 

You can learn more about these initiatives and find relevant resources on [Workday This Week](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=kb_article&amp;sysparm_article=KB0020033#kb_redesign), or watch the video below for a short overview\. If you have questions or feedback to share about these changes, or Workday resources and support in general, please [contact us](https://isc.ubc.ca/contact "Contact Us") or submit a [Workday support request](https://ubc.service-now.com/selfservice?id=sc_category&amp;sys_id=48e309381bcb54908f672fc02a4bcb5a)\.
