Part of the ISC’s mission is to support the continuous improvement of Workday at UBC\. This means making updates and changes to Workday so that it better meets the needs of the UBC employees who use Workday, as well as the university as a whole\. We do this by partnering with others at UBC, as well as working directly with [Workday](https://isc.ubc.ca/about/workday "About Workday") \(the company\) and other post\-secondary institutions\.
