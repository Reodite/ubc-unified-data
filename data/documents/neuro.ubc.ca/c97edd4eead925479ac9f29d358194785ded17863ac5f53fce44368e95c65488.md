## About Us

The UBC Neuroscience Association \(UNA\) is the UPN’s social community\. They work to provide social and academic events to student members in order to foster a community within the undergraduate neuroscience community\. Some of their biggest events have included the Neuroscience Undergraduate Research Conference \(NURC\), the Gala, drinks with profs, the murder mystery night, and journal clubs\. While many members are UPN students, the UNA welcomes any student at UBC\.

### General Membership

**Oct 30th – Halloween Cookie Decorating:** Join the UBC Neuroscience Association for a fun\-filled evening of cookie decorating and a Halloween movie night\!  Whether you’re an expert decorator or just want to enjoy some sweet treats and spooky vibes, this event is perfect to get into the Halloween spirit and socialize with your fellow neuroscience enthusiasts\! 

[Image](https://arts-upn-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/10/Cierna-Annie.jpg)

**Nov 8th \(1\-2pm\) – Ciernia Lab Tour:** The Ciernia laboratory combines experimental and computational approaches to understand how epigenetic mechanisms regulate gene expression across our lifespan\. The lab specifically focuses on mechanisms of epigenetic regulation in multiple brain cell populations across normal brain and immune system development and in rodent models of neurodevelopmental disorders\. If you’re interested in the Ciernia lab’s research, RSVP to attend\!

[Image](https://arts-upn-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/10/siyun-wang.jpg)

**Nov 13th – Wang Lab Tour:** We are using molecular approaches to understand the microorganisms that post major threats to food safety, security and public health\. If you’re interested in the Wang lab’s research, RSVP to attend\!

[Image](https://arts-upn-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/10/Brett-Hilton.jpg)

**Nov 20th – Hilton Lab Tour:** We are a multidisciplinary neuroscience laboratory in Vancouver, BC, Canada\. Our research is dedicated to understanding the fundamental processes that prevent regeneration and restrict functional recovery following spinal cord injury\. If you’re interested in the Hilton lab’s research, RSVP to attend\!

[Image](https://arts-upn-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/10/image-4-e1730264250491.png)

**Nov 22nd – Drinks With A Prof:** Join us for our annual DRINKS WITH A PROF event for an amazing night of connecting with professors from a wide range of disciplines: Neuroscience, Psychology, Biology, and related fields\! We would be doing all this and over drinks and food, so this is an event you do not want to miss\.
