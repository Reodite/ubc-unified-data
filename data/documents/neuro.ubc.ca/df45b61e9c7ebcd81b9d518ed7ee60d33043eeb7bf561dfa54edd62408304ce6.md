**Second Year Awards**

**1\.** **Outstanding Second\-Year Student Leadership in Neuroscience:** A service/leadership\-based award designed to recognize an outstanding student leader in our undergraduate neuroscience program\. 

[Image](https://arts-upn-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/mehar_mago_2025_second_year_leadership_award-677x960.jpg)

**2025 Winner: Mehar Mago**

Mehar is a Neuroscience student at the UBC with a deep commitment to health equity, mental health, and inclusivity\. She actively contributes to interdisciplinary research projects spanning UBC, York, NOSM, and the University of Waterloo, focusing on emergency care systems, digital health, mental health disparities, and human resources for health in underserved settings\. As a member of the NSCI EDI\-D Committee, Mehar supports initiatives that advance equity, diversity, inclusion, and decolonization in neuroscience education\. Beyond academia, she engages in advocacy for neurodiversity and women’s health through leadership roles in various campus\-based organizations and initiatives\.

[Image](https://arts-upn-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/08/abby_sun_2025_second_year_leadership_runner-720x960.jpg)

**2025 Runner\-Up: Abby Sun**

Abby is a third\-year Neuroscience student in the Cellular &amp; Molecular stream with a passion for mentorship, leadership, and community building\. She has served as Internal Committee Co\-Chair for the Science Undergraduate Society \(SUS\), Jump Start Orientation Leader, and as a Teaching Assistant for UBC’s Computer Science Department\. Currently, she contributes to the Student Alumni Council as an Internal Project Coordinator, striving to foster mentorship and professional development opportunities\. Abby’s research interests lie in vision and aging processes, which she had the opportunity to explore this past summer as an NSERC awardee at the Moritz Lab\. In her free time, Abby enjoys baking, running, and exploring new cafés around Vancouver\.

  
**2\. Outstanding Second\-Year Research Contributions:** An award recognizing the student showing the greatest engagement within a neuroscience research lab at UBC\.  

[Image](https://arts-upn-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/leo_chung_second_year_research_award_2-720x960.jpg)

**2025 Winner: Leo Chung**

Leo is an undergraduate researcher in the Ciernia Lab entering his junior year\. His work explores how Inflammatory bowel disease contributes to neuropsychiatric disorders through novel microbial\-transplant mouse models, with a focus on variations in microglial morphology and gene expression\. This fall, he will join the Simons Foundation as an undergraduate Research Fellow, studying autism\-associated chromatin\-remodelling variants in neurodevelopment\. Leo’s work has been fortunate to receive funding totalling $35,000 as of the summer\. Outside of neuroscience, Leo is an avid fan of any sport with a ball, and enjoys burning his pay checks by discovering new foods around Vancouver\.

Jessica is going into 3rd year Behavioural/Cognitive Neuroscience\. She began her journey into research in Grade 10, volunteering at UBC’s Child and Teen Studies Lab\. Throughout undergrad, her research interest has shifted from developmental psychology towards neuroscience, and she is currently a WorkLearn research assistant in the Rankin Lab characterizing Parkinson’s disease risk genes using c\. elegans\. She is involved in the Haas Lab as well, studying how seizures during early brain development affect neural circuit formation and long\-term brain function using a Xenopus laevis tadpole model\. Outside of research, she enjoys painting, motorcycle rides, and exploring bookstores\.

## Featured Award Winner: Thomas van der Sloot

[Image](https://arts-upn-2021.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2026/04/Tom_van_der_Sloot_photo-940x627.jpg)

**2025 National Co\-operative Education and Work\-Integrated Learning \(CEWIL\) Canada’s Student of the Year award**

Thomas van der Sloot is a fourth\-year neuroscience student who has been spending this co\-op year as lab manager and researcher in the Cembrowski Lab, studying Alzheimer’s disease pathology in a mouse model\. He also completed a four\-month co\-op placement working on strategy for Satori Health, a healthcare network in Alberta\. In March 2026, Tom was named the CEWIL Canada &amp; Emery\-Dufault University Co\-op Student of the Year – Canada’s highest recognition for university co\-op achievement, awarded annually to one student from across 190\+ post\-secondary institutions nationally\. He is the first UBC Science student, and third UBC student overall to receive the award in its 32\-year history\. He is also the first neuroscience student to be selected as the UBC Science Co\-op Student of the Year\. Read the official CEWIL press\-release here: [https://www\.cewilcanada\.ca/CEWIL/Updates\-and\-Events/Updates\-and\-News/2026/SOTYA\_Winner\_2025\_Thomas\_Van\_der\_Sloot\_UBC\.aspx](https://www.cewilcanada.ca/CEWIL/Updates-and-Events/Updates-and-News/2026/SOTYA_Winner_2025_Thomas_Van_der_Sloot_UBC.aspx)\.
