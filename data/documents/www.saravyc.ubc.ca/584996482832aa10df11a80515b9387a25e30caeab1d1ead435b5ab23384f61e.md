## Eli G\. Godwin EdM, MPH

Eli G\. \(he/him\) has admired SARAVYC’s work from south of the border for several years, so he was thrilled to join SARAVYC in summer 2021 as a practicum student while attending the Harvard T\.H\. Chan School of Public Health\. He has since graduated with his MPH, and continues to work on the RCY project, studying the experiences of gender minority youth in British Columbia\. Eli is also a part\-time research specialist at Boston Children’s Hospital, a member of the Harvard SOGIE Health Equity Research Collaborative, and a member of the Institutional Review Board at The Fenway Institute\. Eli’s research interests lie at the intersections of gender, adolescent health, educational environments, measurement, and political/legal epidemiology\. In his non\-academic time, he enjoys curling, skiing, and just about any other endeavour on frozen water\.
