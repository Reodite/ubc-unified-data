## Carla Hilario PhD

### Assistant Professor, School of Nursing, Faculty of Health and Social Development, University of British Columbia \(Okanagan\)

Dr\. Carla Hilario is an Assistant Professor in the School of Nursing at the University of British Columbia \(Okanagan\)\. Over the past 12 years, she has conducted qualitative, quantitative, and mixed\-methods research with youth, including immigrants, refugees, racialized young people, and Indigenous youth\. Her current research focuses on improving youth mental health\.
