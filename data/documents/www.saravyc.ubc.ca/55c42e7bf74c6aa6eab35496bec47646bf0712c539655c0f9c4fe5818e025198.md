## Jessy Dame RN\(c\), MSN

### Indigenous Two Spirit Advisory, SARAVYC

Jessy Dame is a very proud Two\-Spirit, Métis, Certified Registered Nurse\. Jessy’s family is from Treaty 1 and 2 territories, which is known today as Winnipeg and St Rose\-du\-lac\. Jessy has worked within Neonatal/Postnatal health\. He currently works casually within a queer sexual health clinic in downtown Vancouver and as the Two\-Spirit Program Manager at the Community\-Based Research Centre\. Within this position, Jessy is able to work with and for the Two\-Spirit community to create resources and advocate for services\.
