On May 31st, we released a new report on the health of gender\-diverse youth in BC, in partnership with the McCreary Centre Society\. The report, *Gender\-Diverse: A spotlight on the health of trans and non\-binary young people in BC* shares data from the 2018 BC Adolescent Health Survey \(BC AHS\)\.

Webinars were scheduled for June 9th and 10th to share key findings from the report\.

The report is available to download [here](https://www.saravyc.ubc.ca/2021/05/31/gender-diverse-a-spotlight-on-the-health-of-trans-and-non-binary-young-people-in-b-c/)\.
