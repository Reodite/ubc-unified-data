Ava \(she/her\) is a Research Assistant at SARAVYC and a PhD student in Nursing at the University of British Columbia\. She holds a BSc in Nursing and an MSc in Public Health Nursing and has experience in nursing research and teaching\. She has conducted quantitative, qualitative, and systematic review research\. Her research interests include HIV and infectious diseases, evidence\-based innovation, AI and health, and youth and women health\. Outside of work, she enjoys hiking, camping, running, and paddleboarding, as well as exploring restaurants and trying foods from different cuisines\.
