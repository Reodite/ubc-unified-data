## Shunfu Hu, MA

### Former Research Assistant

Shunfu is a PhD candidate in Measurement, Evaluation, and Research Methodology at UBC\. His research connects machine learning and psychometrics to optimize assessments\. Shunfu has worked on a systematic review of population\-based estimates of sexual minorities internationally for SARAVYC\.
