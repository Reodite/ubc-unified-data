## Shams Al\-anzi

Shams Al\-anzi \(she/her/hers\) holds a Bachelors in Nursing degree from Jordan and a Master of Science in Nursing from the University of British Columbia\. She is currently a Ph\.D\. student at UBC\. She is a registered nurse, nurse specialist, nurse educator, and nursing instructor in the Middle East\. She has contributed to the advancement of nursing for years, both in clinical\-nursing and undergraduate nursing programs\. She is a member of Sigma Theta Tau International, Honor Society of Nursing\.

Her research focusses on youth living with stigma, stigma\-related victimization, \(i\.e\., bullying, discrimination\), and stigma\-related mental health challenges\. She is also interested in highlighting the health needs among youth immigrants during their journey from the Middle East to Canada\.
