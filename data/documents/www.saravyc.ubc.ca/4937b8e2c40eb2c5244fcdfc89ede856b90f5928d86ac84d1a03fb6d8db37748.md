Marie Louise Umwangange \(She, her, hers\) is a Research Assistant at SARAVYC and joined the team in December 2023\. She is currently pursuing a PhD in Nursing at the University of British Columbia\. She held the Warren George Povey Award in Global Health and the Gunter and Cordula Paetzold Fellowship\. Her research interests focus on improving the health of school\-aged children and adolescents, particularly those experiencing health disparities\. She holds a Master’s degree in Pediatric Nursing from the University of Rwanda, a Master’s degree in Public Health from Universidad Central de Nicaragua \(in partnership with Texila American University\), and a Bachelor’s degree in Nursing \(Honors\)\. Previously, she worked for the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention \(CDC\) in Rwanda as a Public Health Specialist, coordinating HIV clinical services, and as a Research and Evaluation Assistant\.
