## Ace Chan PhD Candidate

### Postdoctoral Fellow

Ace is a postdoctoral fellow in the School of Population and Public Health and the School of Nursing at The University of British Columbia\. Ace’s research interests include sexual and gender minority \(SGM\) health, creating inclusive and accessible health care spaces for 2SLGBTQ\+ communities, and Ace strives towards providing scientific evidence to achieve health equity in marginalized populations\. Ace’s passion for accessible and inclusive services for 2SLGBTQ\+ people extends beyond academia and into the community as well\.
