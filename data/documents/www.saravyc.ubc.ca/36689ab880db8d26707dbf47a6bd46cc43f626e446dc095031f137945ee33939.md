## Lorraine Grieves

### Provincial Program Director, TransCare BC

  
change@lorrainegrieves\.com

Lorraine Grieves, MA, R\.C\.C\., is the Provincial Program Director of Trans Care BC at the Provincial Health Services Authority\. Her current work focuses on improving access to gender\-affirming care and supports through a community co\-designed, multipronged approach\.  Her general research and publication interests include: peer\-based approaches to building the capacity of communities who face marginalization, community\-based research partnerships to better inform the health care system about needs and gaps, participatory program planning approaches and amplifying the voices and agency of sexual and gender minority youth and young adults\.  She is also a clinical counsellor with a specialization in mental health and substance use, addictions and gender diversity
