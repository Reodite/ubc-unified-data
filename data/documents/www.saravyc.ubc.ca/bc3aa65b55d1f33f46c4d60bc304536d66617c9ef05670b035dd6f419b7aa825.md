## Michele Ybarra, PhD, MPH

### President and Research Director, Center for Innovative Public Health Research  
Associate Professor – Adjunct, Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health

Dr\. Ybarra is an internationally recognized researcher in the areas of adolescent health, including sexual health, LGBT\+ health, and cyberbullying\. She has led over 15 federally funded research grants and has published over 100 peer\-reviewed articles and 5 book chapters\. She is the President and Research Director of the Center for Innovative Public Health Research \(C*i*PHR\)\.
