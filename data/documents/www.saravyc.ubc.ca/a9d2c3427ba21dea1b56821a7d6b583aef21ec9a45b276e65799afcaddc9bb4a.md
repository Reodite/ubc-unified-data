### Resources in English

## **National**

#### Trans Lifeline

*This line is primarily for transgender people experiencing a crisis\. This includes people who may be struggling with their gender identity and are not sure that they are transgender\. While our goal is to prevent self\-harm, we welcome the call of any transgender person in need\. We will do our very best to connect them with services that can help them meet that need\.*

 Website: [http://www\.translifeline\.org/](http://www.translifeline.org/)

In Canada Call: \(877\) 330\-6366

In USA Call: \(877\) 565\-8860

#### PFLAG Canada

*24/7 support\. Provides support, information and resources to gay, lesbian, bisexual, transgender or questioning people, as well as to their families and friends\.  
For support on gender identity:*

Website: [http://pflagcanada\.ca/](http://pflagcanada.ca/)

Call toll\-free: 1\-888\-530\-6777

Email: [operations@pflagcanada\.ca](mailto:operations@pflagcanada.ca)

#### Kids Help Phone

*24/7 counseling help from trained professionals on the phone for youth up to age 20\. Text support is also available 24/7\. Text CONNECT to 686868\.* 

Website: [https://kidshelpphone\.ca/](https://kidshelpphone.ca/)

Hotline: 1\-800\-668\-6868
