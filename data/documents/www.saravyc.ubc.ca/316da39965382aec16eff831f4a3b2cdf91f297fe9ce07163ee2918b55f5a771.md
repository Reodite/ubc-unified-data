## Linnea Ritland, BFA

### Video Production and Illustration

Linnea Ritland is a half\-Asian &amp; queer video person based in Vancouver, BC\. She is a settler on unceded xʷməθkʷəy̓əm \(Musqueam\), Sḵwx̱wú7mesh \(Squamish\), and  səlilwətaɬ \(Tsleil\-Waltuth\) first nations lands, aka Vancouver, BC\. They hold a BFA in film production from the University of British Columbia \(UBC\)\. They currently work a freelance editor, with clients &amp; collaborators including Salish Legends media, Jon Chiang, LifeLearn \(Zoetis, Boehringer Ingelheim\), and the CBC\. She has experience writing &amp; directing for film and video, including: award\-winning queer romcom, “Violet and June”; years of crafting educational content for UBC; [video essays](https://www.youtube.com/c/artisanalcheese/videos) exploring topics ranging from human head transplants, Epicureanism \(feat\. Drake\), and internet porn bans\.
