## Hilary A\. Rose PhD, CFLE

### Associate Professor, Concordia University  

Dr\. Rose is an Associate Professor in the Department of Applied Human Sciences \(and the Minor in Interdisciplinary Studies in Human Sexuality\) at Concordia University in Montreal\. Her research interests lie in parent/child relations and child development–primarily in gender and sexual identity development\.  She studies risk and resilience in sexual\-minority youth, including factors such as victimization, suicide, and parental support\.
