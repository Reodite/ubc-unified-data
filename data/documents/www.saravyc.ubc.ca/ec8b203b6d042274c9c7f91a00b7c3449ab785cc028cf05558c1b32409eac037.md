## LEARNING LUNCH with the SARAVYC Bunch

### Helen Uche Okoye presents: Trends in Racism among African\-Canadian Adolescents in British Columbia

Join the SARAVYC team for a lunch &amp; learn on March 4 presented by Helen Uche Okoye\. Bring your own refreshments and note\-taking tools, and we’ll provide the pizza\! Helen will be presenting about the trends in racism among African\-Canadian Adolescents in British Columbia\.

**Date:** Wednesday, March 4, 2020  
**Time:** 12 pm to 1 pm  
**Place:** UBC Hospital, Room T187\-T188

There’s limited space, and we need to know how much pizza to order, so please RSVP to [saravyc@nursing\.ubc\.ca](mailto:saravyc@nursing.ubc.ca) by 8 am on March 4\.
