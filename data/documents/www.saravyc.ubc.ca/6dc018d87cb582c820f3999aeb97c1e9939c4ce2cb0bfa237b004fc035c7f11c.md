## Cormac O’Dwyer

### Former Research Assistant

  

Cormac is a first\-year medical student at UBC and comes to SARAVYC with a background in community services and program development\. He has also been active in advocacy, working to improve access to healthcare for gender\-diverse youth in BC\. Cormac currently provides support on several projects at SARAVYC including the Canadian Trans &amp; Non\-binary Youth Health Survey and a global systematic review looking at sexual minority youth populations\.
