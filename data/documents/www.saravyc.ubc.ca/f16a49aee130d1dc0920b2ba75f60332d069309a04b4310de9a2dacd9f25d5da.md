# Taking Pride: 2SLGBTQIA\+ Youth Healthy Relationship Workshop &amp; Training Resources

[Image](https://apsc-saravyc.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/02/Taking-4.png)

Taking Pride is a healthy relationship workshop series\. These resources are **designed by 2SLGBTQIA\+ youth, for 2SLGBTQIA\+ youth**, ensuring that conversations about relationships, boundaries, and consent reflect their lived experiences\.

As part of our **Taking Pride** curriculum—developed in collaboration with the **McCreary Centre Society** and funded by the **Public Health Agency of Canada**—we created **training materials and discussion guides** to help educators, caregivers, and service providers support 2SLGBTQIA\+ youth in building healthy relationships\.

While these **English and French resources** are available for **free download**, they can also be **facilitated upon request**\. If you’d like to arrange a session, please contact us at **saravyc@nursing\.ubc\.ca**\.
