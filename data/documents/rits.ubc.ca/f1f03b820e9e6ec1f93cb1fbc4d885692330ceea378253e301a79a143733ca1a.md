The UBC\-Ritsumeikan University collaboration was initiated in 1991\.

The Academic Exchange Program is a cohort program that includes custom\-designed courses in both fall and winter terms structured to support students’ English language proficiency\.

The customized courses are in ARTS \(Faculty of Arts\) and LLED \(Department of Language and Literacy Education\)\.
