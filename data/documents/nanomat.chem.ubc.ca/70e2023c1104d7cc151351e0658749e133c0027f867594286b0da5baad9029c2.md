#### Please complete the following steps if you would like to be part of the NanoMat program:

**1\. Look over the research interests of the NanoMat Principal Investigators to identify a potential supervisor\.**

**2\. Contact a potential supervisor from the list of NanoMat Principal Investigators\.**

**3\. Apply to UBC in the graduate program of your potential supervisor\.**
