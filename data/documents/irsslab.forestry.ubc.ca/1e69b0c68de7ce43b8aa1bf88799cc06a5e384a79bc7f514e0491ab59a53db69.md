In 2007 the IRSS lab took part in a meeting with members of the Canadian Forest Service and the Spatial Pattern Analysis and Research lab from the University of Victoria\. The meeting was held over a weekend at the Malcolm Knapp Research Forest’s newly renovated Loon Lake Camp\.
