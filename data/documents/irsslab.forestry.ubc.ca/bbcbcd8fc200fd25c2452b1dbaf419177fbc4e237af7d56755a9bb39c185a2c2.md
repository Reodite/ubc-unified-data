**Nicholas Coops**

[nicholas\.coops@ubc\.ca](mailto:nicholas.coops@ubc.ca)

[https://irss\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2021/06/Nicholas3\-scaled\.jpg](https://irss.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/Nicholas3-scaled.jpg)[Image](https://irss.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/Nicholas3-1024x681.jpg)Dr\. Coops is a faculty member in the Department of Forest Resources Management at UBC, and holds a Canada Research Chair \(CRC\) in remote sensing\.

Nicholas obtained his PhD from the Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology in Melbourne Australia, and then worked at CSIRO, the Australian government research labs, for 10 year prior to coming to UBC in 2004\.

Nicholas’ research focuses on the application of remote sensing imagery to the monitoring and mapping of forest and vegetation health and productivity\.
