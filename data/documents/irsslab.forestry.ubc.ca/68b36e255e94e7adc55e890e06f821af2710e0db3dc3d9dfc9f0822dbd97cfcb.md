Chris Bater’ field work was completed over two years on Vancouver Island near the towns of Ucluelet and Tofino, he and a team of willing lab members measured ground verification data among some of the BC’s coastal giant Douglas\-firs\. Members of the UBC Faculty of Forestry Landscape Ecology lab helped Chris with fieldwork\.
