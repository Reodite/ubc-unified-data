We recognize the systemic bias affecting members of the indigenous, racialized and LGBTQ2S\+ communities\. In our recruitment, we value individual potential over past training that is often driven by privilege\. If you feel that you have been held back in the past we encourage you to outline in the cover letter how you feel we could best support you in reaching your full potential\.

### Postdoctoral fellows

We are currently recruiting highly skilled and passionate Postdoctoral Fellows for multiple projects in the Canadian Pediatric Cancer Consortium ACCESS\. You will develop and lead a research project focusing on spatial and single cell proteomics in high\-grade childhood brain tumors, sarcomas or infant leukemias\. Other possible areas of research focus include reshaping the neuroblastoma tumor microenvironment during CAR\-T treatment, plasma and extracellular vesicle proteome and immunopeptidome analysis with the goal to establish liquid biopsy testing for select pediatric malignancies\. Re\-think precision oncology with us and work with us to bring proteomics to routine clinical application\.

In addition to our fully funded positions, we are always open to excellent candidates who would like to develop a fellowship application together with us\. Candidates from Germany interested in joining as Feodor Lynen Fellows supported by the Humboldt Foundation \(https://www\.humboldt\-foundation\.de/bewerben/foerderprogramme/feodor\-lynen\-forschungsstipendium\) are particularly encouraged to reach out\.
