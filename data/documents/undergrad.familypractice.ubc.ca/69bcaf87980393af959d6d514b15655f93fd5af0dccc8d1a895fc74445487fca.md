## Yes, I want to teach\!

**Family Medicine Office Visits:** Three hours per office visit on flexible days and times

**Locum and Special Interest Visits:** We also send students to clinics hosted by locums and focused practice \(i\.e\., hospitalist, care for the elderly, obstetrics, sport medicine, etc\.\)

**Seminars:** Eight\-10 students per group\. Held at UBC or VGH\. Tutor manuals provided\. Topics cover:

-   Menopause
-   Fatigue
-   Physical activity
-   Office procedures

-   Diabetes
-   Hypertension
-   Palliative care
-   Headache

-   Headache
-   Reflections
-   Vital signs
-   Basic suturing

-   Contraception
-   Obesity

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there remuneration?**

You will be paid $106\.03 per session for teaching 1 student, or $129\.59 per session of 2 students, if eligible\. In addition, you will receive a UBC faculty appointment which includes many benefits such as free access to most leading medical journals online\.

**What will the students be doing in my office?**  
  
Students learn either through in person visits or via tele\-health\. For example, students can take a history for 20 minutes while you see two patients, and then you can review with them and observe or demonstrate part of a physical exam\!

**I do not know if I have time to take medical students\!** **What can I do?**  
  
Preceptor time commitment is flexible and manageable\. The time requirement is only 10\-12 half days \(2\-3 hours each\) or 5\-6 full days \(4\-6 hours each\) per term\. Times are flexible to suit YOUR schedule\. It is possible to incorporate medical students into your busy practice, and we’re here to help\!
