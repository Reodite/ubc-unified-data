Hi everyone,

We are very excited to announce that Lianne Dale from Leica will give us a talk about their Stellaris confocal microscopy system on October 23rd \(Monday\) from 11 am to 12 pm at the Michael Smith Laboratories \(MSL\) auditorium and run the workshop from November 6th to 10th at the UBC Bioimaging facility \(BIF\)\.  
During the workshop, you can bring your samples \(live, fixed, biological and materials samples\) to try out their system \(inverted microscope, resonant scanner, tunable white light and 405 lasers, HyD detectors, FRAP and more\)\. BIF staff will be there to discuss your experiments and find out your imaging needs\.

Everyone is welcome to the seminar\.  
If you want to try out their confocal microscope during the workshop \(Nov 6\-10\), please register from the link below\. Registration is required\.  
[https://www\.leica\-microsystems\.com/c/am/lsr\-c/stellaris\-workshop/?nlc\=20220404\-SFDC\-014638](https://www.leica-microsystems.com/c/am/lsr-c/stellaris-workshop/?nlc=20220404-SFDC-014638) &lt;[https://www\.leica\-microsystems\.com/c/am/lsr\-c/stellaris\-workshop/?nlc\=20220404\-SFDC\-014638](https://www.leica-microsystems.com/c/am/lsr-c/stellaris-workshop/?nlc=20220404-SFDC-014638)&gt;

Hope that you can join the seminar and hands\-on microscopy workshop\!

Please feel free to contact Miki \(bif\.manager@ubc\.ca\) if you have any questions\.  
BIF team
