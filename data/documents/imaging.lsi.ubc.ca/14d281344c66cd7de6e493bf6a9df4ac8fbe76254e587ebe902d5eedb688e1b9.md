# Public Education and Outreach: **Seeing is believing**\.

[Image](https://lsiimaging.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/09/Outreach.jpg)

Imaging is one of the most powerful technologies in life sciences research, allowing us to visualize the unseen and uncover the mysteries of biology\. At our imaging facility, we are excited to share the cutting\-edge science happening in our research environment\. We welcome school students of all ages and members of the public interested in life sciences research to visit our facility, explore advanced imaging technologies, and see how imaging drives discovery and advances human health\.
