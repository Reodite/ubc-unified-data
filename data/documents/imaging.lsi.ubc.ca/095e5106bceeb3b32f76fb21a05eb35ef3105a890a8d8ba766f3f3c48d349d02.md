LSI IMAGING is hosting a demo on Leica THUNDER Imager\. Some sample images and features of the system could be found here [https://www\.leica\-microsystems\.com/products/thunder\-imaging\-systems/p/thunder\-imager\-3d\-live\-cell/](https://www.leica-microsystems.com/products/thunder-imaging-systems/p/thunder-imager-3d-live-cell/)

THUNDER imagers are a class of computational clearing high\-resolution wide\-field microscopes from Leica Microsystems\. These imagers can acquire sharp data and high\-quality images by instant removal out\-of\-focus blur or haze inherent to thick 3D samples that are generally acquired using widefield microscopy\. These imagers also significantly shorten the imaging workflow from image acquisition to data analysis with the new LAS\-X navigator software\. The samples could be cells, organoids, tissue\.
