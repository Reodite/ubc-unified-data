## Study Here

### Top\-ranked University

UBC Okanagan fosters a collaborative environment between students and faculty to inspire and influence change\. From groundbreaking research to locally driven, globally minded undergraduate and graduate programs, the Okanagan campus in Kelowna bolsters the strength of UBC as one of the world’s top public universities\.

Whether it’s close\-knit classrooms, internationally renowned research opportunities or experiential education programs that take learning beyond the classroom, you can explore your passions at the University of British Columbia’s Okanagan campus\.

### Why choose UBC Okanagan?

UBCO’s vibrant population of domestic and international students has helped turn Kelowna into one of Canada’s fastest\-growing cities\. Find out why so many graduates choose to stay within the region, to join a number of great industries thriving in the Okanagan\.

Come study within the stunning natural beauty of BC’s Okanagan Valley—a four season playground that’s accessible to the world\. [Learn more about campus life and student supports at UBCO](https://ok.ubc.ca/student-life/)\.
