## Learning Resources

Explore resources and services available to support your educational journey—whether you’re learning more about courses or important deadlines\.

### [Academic calendar](http://www.calendar.ubc.ca/okanagan/)

A comprehensive guide to all programs, courses, services and records for many of the academic policies and procedures at UBC Okanagan\.

### [Find a course](https://courses.students.ubc.ca/cs/main?campuscd=UBCO)

Browse by courses offered in specific sessions \(Fall, Winter, Summer\), save them to a worklist, view them in a timetable and even register\.

### [Dates and deadlines](https://okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca/dates-and-deadlines)

When do classes start? When are fees due? What’s the deadline to drop a course? Check out important dates throughout the academic year\.

### [Academic support](http://students.ok.ubc.ca/advising/)

Need advice about your program? Want support? Reach out to access study strategies, tutor assistance or help from our academic advisors\.

### [UBC Canvas](https://canvas.ubc.ca/)

Login to Canvas to engage with course materials, complete learning activities, collaborate with classmates, preview grades and more\.

### [Academic leadership](https://provost.ok.ubc.ca/)

The Provost and Vice\-Principal Academic executes UBC’s bold vision on the Okanagan campus while providing academic leadership and administration\.
