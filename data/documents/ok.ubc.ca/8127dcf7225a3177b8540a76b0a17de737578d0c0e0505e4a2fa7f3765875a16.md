## September 2024

[Syilx Okanagan Nation staff in front of a UBC Okanagan podium\.](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/10/trc-committments-870x550-1.jpg)

Five years ago, UBC Okanagan declared its commitments to the Truth and Reconciliation Commission’s Calls to Action\. In September, UBCO acknowledged the steps that have been taken and commits to continue advancing this important work\. [See the journey so far](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pSW2SdbdxP4&amp;t=89s)\.
