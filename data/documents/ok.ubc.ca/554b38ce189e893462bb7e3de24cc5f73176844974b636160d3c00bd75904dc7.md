UBC’s Okanagan campus has nine Faculties and Schools, each offering a range of academic programs\.

### Arts and Social Sciences, Irving K\. Barber Faculty of

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/cultural-studies-class-humanities.jpg)The Irving K\. Barber Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences drives economic, social and cultural change through learning and research\.

#### [fass\.ok\.ubc\.ca](https://fass.ok.ubc.ca/)

Departments:

-   [Community, Culture and Global Studies](https://ccgs.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   [Economics, Philosophy and Political Science](https://epp.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   [History and Sociology](https://hs.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   [Psychology](https://psych.ok.ubc.ca/)

### Creative and Critical Studies, Faculty of

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/professor-students-fina-gallery-art-fccs-faculties-and-schools-300x180.jpg)Home to UBC Okanagan’s fine arts programming, FCCS provides an exciting intellectual environment for critically examining communication, cultural forms and visual arts\.

#### [fccs\.ok\.ubc\.ca](http://fccs.ok.ubc.ca/)

### Education, Okanagan School of

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/maker-day-teacher-school-children-education-faculties-and-schools-300x180.jpg)The Okanagan School of Education applies a learner\-focused approach that inspires prospective and current educators from multiple careers and disciplines to approach educative practices more wisely, creatively, and responsively\.

#### [education\.ok\.ubc\.ca](http://education.ok.ubc.ca/)

### Engineering, School of

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/researcher-sintering-machine-engineering-faculties-and-schools-300x180.jpg)Part of UBC’s [Faculty of Applied Science](https://apsc.ubc.ca/), the School of Engineering is a close\-knit, multidisciplinary environment for finding creative engineering solutions that positively impact the community and the world\.

#### [engineering\.ok\.ubc\.ca](http://engineering.ok.ubc.ca/)

### Health and Social Development, Faculty of

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/nursing-students-lab-health-social-development-faculites-and-schools-300x180.jpg)FHSD focuses on some of the most challenging health and social issues facing Canada and the world through collaborative programs of research and scholarship\.

#### [fhsd\.ok\.ubc\.ca](http://fhsd.ok.ubc.ca/)

Schools:

-   [Health and Exercise Sciences](https://hes.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   [Nursing](https://nursing.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   [Social Work](https://socialwork.ok.ubc.ca/)

### Management, Faculty of

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/management-students-in-class-faculty-of-management-faculties-and-schools-300x180.jpg)Researchers and leaders in business education deliver practical, hands\-on learning that pushes students to improve business practices and produce organizational solutions\.

#### [management\.ok\.ubc\.ca](http://management.ok.ubc.ca/)

### Science, Irving K\. Barber Faculty of

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/students-scanner-kgh-medical-physics-barber-school-faculties-and-schools-300x180.jpg)The Irving K\. Barber Faculty of Science offers flexible, student\-focused learning and research and innovation in scientific disciplines\.

#### [science\.ok\.ubc\.ca](https://science.ok.ubc.ca/)

Departments:

-   [Biology](https://biology.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   [Chemistry](https://chemistry.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   [Computer Science](https://cs.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   [Mathematics, Physics and Statistics](https://mps.ok.ubc.ca/)
-   [Earth and Environmental Sciences](https://eesc.ok.ubc.ca/)

### Southern Medical Program, Faculty of Medicine

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/medical-students-baby-simulator-souther-medical-program-medicine-faculties-and-schools-300x180.jpg)SMP provides distributed medical education, allowing students to complete their four\-year MD undergraduate training in rural and underserved communities in British Columbia’s interior\.

#### [smp\.med\.ubc\.ca](http://smp.med.ubc.ca/)

### Graduate Studies, College of

[Image](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/graduate-student-professor-in-library-graduate-studies-faculties-and-schools-300x180.jpg)The College of Graduate Studies empowers graduate students and postdoctoral fellows to explore research interests, challenge ideas, create new knowledge and innovate\.

#### [gradstudies\.ok\.ubc\.ca](http://gradstudies.ok.ubc.ca/)
