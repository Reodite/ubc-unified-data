**September 23, 2021 – 10:15 am PDT**

Thank you to our campus community for your patience and cooperation this morning\.

Members of the UBCO community seeking mental health supports have access to the following resources:

Students : [Student counselling and mental health resources](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/health-wellness/counselling-mental-health/) available through campus Health and Wellness\.

Faculty and Staff: [Mental health resources](https://hr.ubc.ca/health-and-wellbeing/mental-health/faculty-and-staff-mental-health-resources) available through Human Resources

**September 23, 2021 – 10:05 am PDT**

The area of John Hindle Drive and Academy Way has been cleared and regular traffic has resumed\. Campus remains open and classes are continuing as normal\.

**September 23, 2021 – 9:58 am PDT**

UBCO is open, classes are continuing at this time\. The campus community is asked to avoid the area of John Hindle Drive and Academy Way until instructed otherwise by RCMP\.

**September 23, 2021 – 9:40 am PDT**

UBC Okanagan is aware of an unfolding police incident adjacent to campus\. Please avoid the area of Academy Way and John Hindle Drive until directed otherwise by RCMP\. Updates will be available on on this page, as appropriate\.
