## September 2006

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/fipke-6-million-donation-2006-history.jpg)

Kelowna resident Charles Fipke—a UBC alumnus and the discoverer of diamonds in Canada’s Far North—donates $6 million through his foundation to UBC’s Okanagan campus to build the Charles E\. Fipke Centre for Innovative Research\.
