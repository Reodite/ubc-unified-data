## January–March 2013

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/eme-building-new-buildings-doors-open-2013-history.jpg)

The landscape of campus changed and the student learning environment took a giant leap forward when two major buildings opened\. In January, the 17,200\-square\-metre Engineering Management Education \(EME\) building officially opened\. At $68 million, the EME building was the largest single project undertaken as part of the campus master plan\. Two months later, the four\-storey, 7,800\-square\-metre Arts and Sciences Centre opened, featuring new classrooms, research and teaching labs, offices, student commons, and two large lecture theatres\.
