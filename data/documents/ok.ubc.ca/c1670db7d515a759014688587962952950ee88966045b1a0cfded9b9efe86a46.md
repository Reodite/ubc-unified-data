**Jan\. 27, 2025, 2:55 pm**

The incident has now been cleared and no other disruptions to campus are expected\.

**Jan\. 27, 2025, 8:15 am**

Due to an incident on the William R\. Bennett Bridge, which is blocking traffic in both directions, members of the UBC Okanagan community may experience delays until traffic resumes\.

Campus remains open and is expected to operate as normal\.

Commuters are advised to plan ahead and consider alternate routes\. For information on current conditions, visit [www\.drivebc\.ca](https://www.drivebc.ca/)\.

If students are unable to attend campus, please inform your instructor for further guidance\.

Faculty and Staff are advised to speak with their supervisors if they experience significant delays\.
