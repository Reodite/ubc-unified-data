## September 2019

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2019/11/TRC-signing-2019-history.jpg)

Elders, chiefs and community members from the Syilx Okanagan Nation joined with students, faculty and staff at UBC Okanagan on September 24 to witness the historic signing of UBCO’s commitments toward reconciliation with Indigenous peoples\.
