## June 2020

[UBCO establishes downtown presence](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2021/05/milestones-downtown-site.jpg)

UBC Okanagan and the City of Kelowna announce plans to establish a university presence in downtown Kelowna\. The mixed\-use development, at 550 Doyle Avenue, will feature community\-facing academic space, office space and university rental housing\. The site is expected to drive innovation, attract and grow the region’s talent pool, and help diversity the economy\.
