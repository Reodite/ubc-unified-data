## December 2006

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/dss-chris-hadfield-2006-history.jpg)

Novelist and historian Ronald Wright, is the first speaker featured in UBC Okanagan’s new Distinguished Speaker Series public lecture\. The series has welcomed several high\-profile presenters, including former Commander of the International Space Station Chris Hadfield; renowned autism spokesperson and animal behaviour expert Temple Grandin; iconic science broadcaster Jay Ingram; social activist and best\-selling author Naomi Klein; public intellectual and economist Richard Florida; and former National Chief of the Assembly of First Nations Phil Fontaine\.
