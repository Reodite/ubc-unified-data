## NOVEMBER 2022

[Alumn walking to the fall graduation ceremony\.](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/02/history-milestone-fall-graduation-ceremony2.jpg)

After completing their studies during the COVID\-19 pandemic, more than 600 alumni from the 2020 and 2021 graduating classes returned to campus to celebrate their accomplishments\. While virtual ceremonies were held during the pandemic, hosting a special fall ceremony gave alumni the opportunity to experience this iconic moment in person\.
