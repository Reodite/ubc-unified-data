### Enjoy social activities on campus

**Build lasting relationships**: Community is everything at UBCO\. Meet new people through our one\-of\-a\-kind student orientation\. Join student clubs and make friends who share your interests\. Meet other students at one of the hundreds of sports and campus events we host each year\.

## Dedicated supports for international students

Even for excellent students, studying abroad can be difficult\. Overcome your challenges with exclusive funding and advisor support\. Let us help—so you can focus on gaining skills, knowledge and lifelong friendships\.

### [Students discussing work while sitting on a couch\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/05/int.prospective-students-learning-hub-1000x600-1.jpg)

### Receive personalized support from an award\-winning team of advisors

Access an international advising team, recognized as one of the top teams in Canada \(BCCIE 2024 Outstanding Program in International Education Award\)\. Advisors are trained to provide guidance on topics such as:

-   Immigration
-   Permanent residency advising
-   Settlement \(e\.g\., cultural acclimatization\)
-   Health insurance
-   Working in Canada
-   Career transitions

[Learn more at students\.ok\.ubc\.ca/international](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/international/)

## Learn new skills from a community of professionals

UBC Okanagan has deep ties to local industry\. Leverage community partnerships to combine work, learning and relationship\-building\.

Co\-op grads earn 15% higher starting salaries than non\-co\-op grads\. They are more likely to land more prestigious jobs than their non co\-op peers\.

### [Get an internship, co\-op or job placement](https://ee.ok.ubc.ca/)

**Co\-op opportunities:**  
Gain skills in the community\. Receive industry\-standard wages while gaining work experience\. Get career\-ready through application reviews, interview preparation and ongoing support\.

**Work Study:**  
Gain experience while studying\. Work Study offers on\-campus, part\-time jobs during the school year\.

### [Plan your career](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/advising-options/academic-advising/)

**Career support:**  
Access advisors and resources to navigate the world of work\.

**Global Pathways:**  
Get a tailored career and immigration plan for success after graduation\. The Global Pathways office is here to guide you every step—even beyond graduation\.

## Become part of the community

### Gain language fluency

**English language resources:**   
Attend regular workshops and get writing help\. [The Student Learning Hub \(SLH\)](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/learning-hub/) and [library](https://library.ok.ubc.ca/) provide support\. Post\-grads can also get support through [the Centre for Scholarly Communication](https://csc.ok.ubc.ca/)\.

**English Foundations Program:**   
Develop your language skills while working toward your degree\. [The EFP](https://education.ok.ubc.ca/degrees-programs/english-foundation-program/) helps students meet minimums\. Courses can be taken alongside first year studies\.

### Find transportation

**City\-wide transit:**  
Tuition includes a [Universal Bus Pass \(U‑PASS\)](https://www.suo.ca/u-pass/) for affordable city‑wide transit\. Additional on\-campus options include bike lockers and electric scooters\.

### Get guaranteed housing

**First\-year housing guarantee:** Undergraduates get [guaranteed on\-campus housing](https://okanagan.housing.ubc.ca/) in their first year \(Winter Session\)\. Some conditions apply\.

### [Athletics and recreation](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/)

UBCO invests in student health\. Our campus has a professional\-sized turf field, gymnasium and fitness centre\. On average, our recreation programs receive over 13,000 student registrations\.

Your safety is our top priority\. UBCO offers a range of services designed to help you feel secure\.

-   24/7 SafeWalk program

All international students auto\-enroll in temporary health insurance \(IMED Health Insurance\)\.

## Where is the Okanagan campus?

UBCO is just 20 minutes from Kelowna’s vibrant city centre, one of Canada’s fastest\-growing and most entrepreneurial cities\. Kelowna is a mid\-sized city in the Okanagan, found in the Southern Interior of British Columbia, Canada\. Connect with nature, community and a diverse economy\.

### Access the world—from Kelowna

Kelowna is a globally loved travel destination\. Its airport \(YLW\) is a one\-hour flight away from Vancouver, Calgary and Seattle\. YLW is Canada’s 9th busiest airport, despite Kelowna being Canada’s 37th most populated city\. YLW serves over two million passengers every year—more than Victoria, Saskatoon, or even Quebec City\.
