## July 2005

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/key-ceremony-2005-history.jpg)

On July 4, 2005, UBC ‘received the keys’ to Okanagan University College’s 105 hectare \(260 acre\) North Kelowna campus and Okanagan College was officially established as a separate entity with its own network of campuses in Kelowna, Vernon, Penticton, and other regional communities\.
