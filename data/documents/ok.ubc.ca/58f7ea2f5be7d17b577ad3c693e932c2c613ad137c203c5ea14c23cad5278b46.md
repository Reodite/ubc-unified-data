### More study options

Looking for additional ways to pursue your learning? UBC Okanagan offers a variety of courses and programs to support your education\.

#### [Continuing and Professional Education](http://cpe.ok.ubc.ca/)

Gain knowledge and proficiency in skills that will advance your career through micro\-credentials and flexible courses and programs\.

#### [English Foundation Program](http://students.ok.ubc.ca/efp/)

Meet all the admission requirements except UBC’s English language standards? Explore the pathway English Foundation Program\.

#### [Okanagan Global Summer Program](https://global.ok.ubc.ca/international-okanagan-summer-program/)

Study a short\-term program while enjoying the benefits of a top ranked university, meeting new people and experiencing the Okanagan\.

#### [UBC Extended Learning Programs](https://extendedlearning.ubc.ca/)

Access an engaging, learner\-centred experience and a community of motivated individuals with UBC’s online or Vancouver\-based courses\.
