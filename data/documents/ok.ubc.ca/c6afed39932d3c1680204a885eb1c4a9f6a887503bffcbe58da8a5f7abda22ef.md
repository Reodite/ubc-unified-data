## October 2018

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2019/11/first-homecoming-2018-history.jpg)

Alumni and community members are welcomed back to the UBC Okanagan campus for its first\-ever Homecoming\. The two\-day event celebrated the growth and diversity of campus through numerous activities including a scavenger race led by Olympic champion and Amazing Race Canada host Jon Montgomery\.
