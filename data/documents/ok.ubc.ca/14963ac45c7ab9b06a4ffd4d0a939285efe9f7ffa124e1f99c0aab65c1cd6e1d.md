## March 2017

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/engineering-equipment-celebrate-research-2017-history.jpg)

Research excellence is showcased during UBC Okanagan’s annual Celebrate Research Week\.  
For the first time, the Researcher of the Year award for faculty is replaced by three disciplinary  
Researcher of the Year awards in each Tri\-Council category: Health, Social Sciences and  
Humanities, and Natural Sciences and Engineering\.
