## OCTOBER 2022

[Dr\. Deborah Buszard](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2023/02/history-milestone-deborah-buszard-870x550-1.jpg)

Dr\. Deborah Buszard was appointed UBC’s Interim President and Vice\-Chancellor as the Board of Governors announced a search for the university’s next president\. Dr\. Buszard made her mark in the Okanagan as UBC Okanagan’s Deputy Vice\-Chancellor and Principal between 2012 and 2020\.
