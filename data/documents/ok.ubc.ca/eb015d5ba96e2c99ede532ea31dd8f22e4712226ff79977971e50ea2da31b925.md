Learning that combines hand\-on experiences with critical reflection to equip you with the skills and insights needed to succeed in your career and navigate life after university\.

[A co\-op student helping wine makers process grapes at a vineyard\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/10/ee-vineyard-1000x600-1.jpg)

Experiential education offers opportunity to learn through experience and understand how classroom concepts and ideas are used in practice\. Students will collaborate with a range of partners beyond UBC Okanagan, gain practical insights and see firsthand how concepts from their courses come to life in professional settings\.

UBCO offers a wide variety of experiential education opportunities so students can choose experiences that suit their interests, support degree completion and career aspirations\.

[Learn more about experiential education at UBCO](https://ee.ok.ubc.ca/)
