## Why Study at UBC’s Okanagan Campus

Consistently ranked among the top universities globally, UBC’s excellence reaches into the stunning Okanagan region, where students benefit from a close\-knit campus environment and receive a world\-class education\.

Nestled in the interior of British Columbia, UBC Okanagan sits next to a growing international airport—giving researchers and learners a unique advantage in making global connections\.

[An aerial view of UBC Okanagan's campus\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/04/campus-aerial-1000x600-1.jpg)

### A naturally beautiful campus and region

See what makes Kelowna a four\-season playground—hike the mountains, ski the slopes, lounge lakeside on the beach or go for a swim\. UBCO’s campus is green, picturesque and ready for adventure\.

[UBC Okanagan students in lab coats talking and walking down a hall\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/07/all-in-plant-research-1000x600-1.jpg)

### Move learning beyond the classroom

Get real\-world experience and prepare for your career with experiential education at UBCO\. With opportunities like co\-op, go global, work study and student research, you can graduate ready for the workforce\.

[An aerial view of downtown Kelowna, BC, Canada\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/07/aerial-downtown-kelowna-1000x600-1.jpg)

### One of Canada’s fastest growing tech hubs

Digital animation and gaming\. AI\-powered aerospace\. Technologically\-fuelled manufacturing and agriculture\. Kelowna’s thriving downtown tech centre is contributing to one of the fastest growing job markets in BC\.
