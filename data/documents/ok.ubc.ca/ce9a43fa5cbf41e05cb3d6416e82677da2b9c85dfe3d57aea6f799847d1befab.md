## September\-december 2021

[Skeena residence](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2022/05/milestones-sustainability-skeena3.jpg)

A leader in campus sustainability, UBC Okanagan expands upon green initiatives with the launch of the Bachelor of Sustainability program in September, the opening of the uber energy\-efficient Skeena Residence in October, and the December release of the UBC Climate Action Plan 2030\.
