## Six tips for a successful school year

### [An icon of a volleyball\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/10/RECATH-volleyball-FINAL-150x150.png)  
1\. Take care of your mind and body

Not feeling yourself? [Talk to someone at Student Wellness](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/health-wellness/)\. It can be hard to ask for help, but it gets easier with support\.

Stay active\. Keep up with your fitness regimen or even start a new one at [UBC Recreation](https://recreation.ok.ubc.ca/)\.

### [An icon of a folded map with two connected locations indicated\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/10/Satellite-and-partner-locations-FINAL-150x150.png)  
2\. Find food on campus

[Explore where to eat on campus](https://food.ok.ubc.ca/feed-me/)—whether it’s a coffee pick\-me\-up or an end\-of\-day meal\. And keep an eye out for food trucks parked along University Way\.

[View the campus map](https://ok.ubc.ca/about/maps-directions-tours/) to find directions\.

### [A graphic of a parasol on the beach\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/09/Beaches-FINAL-150x150.png)  
3\. Relieve stress

Discover a relaxed environment\. Break for lunch, do schoolwork and connect with others at a [UBCO Collegia space](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/campus-life/collegia-spaces/)\.

Unwind from your studies by snuggling a puppy at a [B\.A\.R\.K\. drop\-in session](https://bark.ok.ubc.ca/)\.

### [An icon of an hourglass with a cursor\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/11/Deadline-to-apply-150x150.png)  
4\. Manage your courses and important deadlines

If you’re dropping or withdrawing from a class, be sure to [change your registration](https://okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/records-and-registration/change-registration) before the deadline\. For dates and important deadlines, view the [2025/26 Academic Calendar](https://okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca/dates-and-deadlines)\.

Need support? [Contact an advisor\.](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/advising-options/academic-advising/)

### [An icon of a pile of books with an apple on top\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/10/Teaching-and-learning-FINAL-150x150.png)  
5\. Get academic support

Explore first\-year planning guides in [Academic and Career Advising](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/advising-options/academic-advising/)\. Advisors are also available to help you with your questions\.

For free and flexible learning supports and resources, navigate to the [Student Learning Hub](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/academic-success/learning-hub/)\.

### [Graphic of some people together on a crest\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2024/09/Student-Clubs-FINAL-1-150x150.png)  
6\. Learn from current students

Get advice from other students—everything from morning playlists to tips to recover from a busy exam period\. [Explore the UBCO Life Blog](https://students.ok.ubc.ca/campus-life/blog/)\.
