## **July–October 2012**

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/prof-deborah-buszard-news-social-media.jpg)

Campus experienced several changes in its senior leadership team\. Prof\. Deborah Buszard is appointed Deputy Vice Chancellor and Principal, succeeding Doug Owram; global education scholar Wisdom Tettey is appointed dean of the Faculty of Creative and Critical Studies; human kinetics scholar Gordon Binsted is appointed dean of the Faculty of Health and Social Development; and international business scholar Roger Sugden is appointed dean of the Faculty of Management\.
