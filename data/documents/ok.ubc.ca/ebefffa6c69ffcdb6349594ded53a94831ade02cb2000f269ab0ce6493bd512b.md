## June 2006

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/first-graduation-ceremony-bill-bowering-2006-historyt.jpg)

On June 6, 470 members of UBC Okanagan’s very first graduating class crossed the stage to receive their degrees in two ceremonies\. Honorary degrees were conferred upon Dr\. William Bowering, a former Okanagan University College president who expanded post\-secondary education in the Okanagan, and upon Jeannette C\. Armstrong, then\-director of the Okanagan Nation’s En’owkin Centre in Penticton, whose 1985 work *Slash* is considered the first novel by an Indigenous woman in Canada\.
