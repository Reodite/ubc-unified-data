[A graphic illustration of UBC Okanagan's UNC and Nechako buildings covered in colourful snow and light\.](https://okmain.cms.ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2025/12/1920x780-Holiday-Illustration.png)

Most units on campus will be operating within regular hours until December 25, 2025\.

From **December 25, 2025 to January 1, 2026**, various services on campus will operate with adjusted hours and some offices will be closed\.

**Note:** For units/departments not listed, please visit their respective websites for complete details: [Faculties and Schools](https://ok.ubc.ca/academics/faculties-and-schools/) \| [Campus Services](https://ok.ubc.ca/about/campus-services/)\.

---

### [Campus Security](http://security.ok.ubc.ca/)

Campus Security patrols and dispatch will operate through the holiday period\. Dispatch is available 24/7\.

Non\-emergency contact: 250 807 9236 \| Emergency contact: 250 807 8111

Security dispatch office: ADM 104A

**Office closure**

-   December 24: Closed at 4 pm
-   December 25 – January 1: Closed
-   January 2: Return to regular hours, 8 am – 4 pm
