## April 2011

[Image](https://ok.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2018/02/live-case-challenge-faculty-management-2011-history.jpg)

April 2011 marked the first\-ever Live Case Challenge, a hands\-on class assignment in which third\-year management students tackle a real\-world, business\-related challenge involving a local organization\. Over the years, students have competed to solve business challenges for Disney Club Penguin, HuStream \(Accelerate Okanagan\), the South Okanagan\-Similkameen Healthy Living Coalition, Totalamber, the City of Kelowna and SunRype\.
