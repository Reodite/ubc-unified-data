## Got a question? Give us a shout\.

Student Services is standing by to help you with any inquiry you might have about Land One\. Drop by the Student Services office in your home faculty so we can get to know you\! Alternatively, you can call or send an email:

Email us at [Land\.One@ubc\.ca](mailto:Land.One@ubc.ca)\.

If you’re in Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship, call [604\-822\-1834](tel:604-822-1834) or email Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship Undergraduate Student Services, \([forestry\.undergrad@ubc\.ca](mailto:forestry.undergrad@ubc.ca)\)\.

If you’re in Land and Food Systems, call [604\-822\-2620](tel:604-822-2620) or email LFS Student Services \([lfs\.advising@ubc\.ca](mailto:lfs.advising@ubc.ca)\)\.
