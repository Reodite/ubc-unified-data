    ### MISSION
    
    UBC Library advances research, learning and teaching excellence by connecting communities, within and beyond the University, to the world’s knowledge\.
    
    ### VISION
    
    We are a globally influential research library, inspiring knowledge creation, exploration and discovery\.
    
    ### VALUES
    
    The UBC Library values:
    
    -   service excellence
        
    -   intellectual freedom and the pursuit of knowledge
        
    -   collaboration with campus and community partners
        
    -   stewardship of collections and institutional resources
        
    -   innovation, creativity and risk\-taking
        
    -   an open, inclusive, respectful work environment
        
    -   opportunities for leadership and individual growth throughout the organization

    During fall 2009, the UBC Library’s Strategic Planning Steering Committee hosted a series of planning forums and interviews with library staff and campus and community stakeholders, to assess current services and future opportunities\. These discussions have resulted in the development of our five\-year strategic directions and goals detailed in the UBC Library Strategic Plan 2010\-2015\.Our plan is informed by *Place and Promise: The UBC Plan*, and the views and needs of the diverse communities with whom we share resources and expertise\. We invite you to engage with the Library as we work towards realizing the goals and commitments required to remain a top North American research library in the 21st century\.We welcome your comments and questions as implementation proceeds\. Please contact us at [lib\-plansc@interchange\.ubc\.ca](mailto:lib-plansc@interchange.ubc.ca)\.
