To share the results of a previous BC CRC, it must have been completed within the past 5 years to be valid\. The CRC which you are sharing must have also been for the same type of check **\(Type: *Schedule B*,** **Works With:** ***Children and vulnerable adults*****\)**, otherwise you will need to submit a new BC CRC request\.

-   **Sharing Online CRC Link**: [https://justice\.gov\.bc\.ca/criminalrecordcheck](https://justice.gov.bc.ca/criminalrecordcheck)
-   **Organization ID \#**: ZTRPVY3M2Q \(this ID \# must be used to ensure the results are delivered directly to the School of Social Work\.

Every year there are applicants who, when completing the CRC application process online, receive an error message stating they are not eligible to submit the online application\. By contacting the BC Ministry of Public Safety and Solicitor General for assistance when this error message is received, they are able to complete the process online\. The online process offers the greatest advantages to students, practicum agencies, and the School of Social Work; if at all possible, it is the preferred method for submitting your CRC\.

*\*Applicants who have a criminal record or are seeking a pardon are advised that this can be a lengthy process, and they should initiate the criminal record check procedure at least 6 months before classes commence\.*
