UBC Okanagan School of Social Work Assistant Professor Mary Clare Kennedy was recently recognized by the Kelowna Chamber of Commerce as a “Top 40 Under 40” for 2026\. The annual program recognizes notable leaders in the community who inspire, lead, build and contribute to the diverse landscape and community personally and professionally\.

Read more [here](https://www.kelownachamber.org/events-programs/top40/2026-top-40-honourees/)

Congratulations to Mary Clare\!

Read more about Mary Clare’s work around harm reduction, public policy and health service evaluation [here](https://socialwork.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/mary-clare-kennedy/)\.
