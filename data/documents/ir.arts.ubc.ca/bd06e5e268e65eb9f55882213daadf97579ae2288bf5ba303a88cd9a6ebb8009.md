Calling all prospective IR major and minor students\! We are very excited to announce the first of two events we will be hosting for you this term\.

Our annual Prospective Evening is an opportunity for those who are looking to declare a major/minor in IR to learn more about our fantastic program\! You will get the chance to meet with members of the UBC IR community where you can ask questions about everything from requirements to extra\-curricular opportunities\.

We will also provide a full overview of the IR major’s admissions process and answer any questions you may have\. The event will take place in the C\.K\. Choi Building conference room and light refreshments will be served

Hosts: IR Program Manager, Adam Jelley, and Program Co\-Chairs, Dr\. Michael Lanthier, and Dr\. Allen Sens\.

Please submit an RSVP below if you would like to attend\.

Don’t forget – the application window for the IR Major opens on Friday January 31st\!
