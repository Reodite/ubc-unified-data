> The UBC International Relations Program, in its current format, was founded in 1991\.
> 
> As an interdisciplinary program within the Faculty of Arts, we work closely with the Department of Political Science, the Vancouver School of Economics, and the Department of History to best serve our students\.

Located on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm \(Musqueam\), we are a community of faculty members, practitioners, students, research and support staff, all working together to advance the study and understanding of International Relations\.
