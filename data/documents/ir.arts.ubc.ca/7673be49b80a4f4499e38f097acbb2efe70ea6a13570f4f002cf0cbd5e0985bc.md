> The International Relations Program is an interdisciplinary undergraduate community in the Faculty of Arts at UBC\.
> 
> We are committed to fostering innovative interdisciplinary education, reflecting UBC’s commitment to excellence in global scholarship\.

The program is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the international system and its complexities\. Our intention is to enable students to develop an in\-depth understanding of IR by combining the insights and perspectives of different disciplines such as Economics, History, and Political Science, along with language study\.

UBC’s IR Program stands as a vibrant intellectual hub for the study of global affairs in Canada, offering students unparalleled opportunities for academic and professional growth in the field of international relations\.
