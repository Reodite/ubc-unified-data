The primary accommodations for NAISA 2017 will be on campus, primarily in the Gage Towers, which are only a few moments’ walk from all conference venues\. The accommodation website is now active [here](https://reserve.ubcconferences.com/vancouver/availability.asp?hotelCode=%2A&amp;startDate=06%2F19%2F2017&amp;endDate=06%2F27%2F2017&amp;adults=1&amp;children=&amp;rooms=1&amp;requesttype=invBlockCode&amp;code=G170619A%20#%3Chttps://reserve.ubcconferences.com/vancouver/availability.asp?hotelCode=%2A&amp;startDate=06%2F19%2F2017&amp;endDate=06%2F27%2F2017&amp;adults=1&amp;children=&amp;rooms=1&amp;requesttype=invBlockCode&amp;code=G170619A%20%3E)\.

Recommendations and reserved blocks for additional accommodations off\-campus will be available in early 2017\. If you would like to support an Indigenous\-owned hotel, try [Skwachàys Lodge](http://www.skwachays.com/)\. Please note that downtown is some distance from the UBC campus and will require travel by taxi or bus\. Uber and Lyft do not operate in Vancouver\.

Before choosing an Airbnb option in Vancouver, please be aware that Airbnb has had a significant negative impact on affordable housing in the city, similar to what has happened in places like San Francisco\.
