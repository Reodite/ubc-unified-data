In August 2025, Nigeria banned the export of raw shea nuts to promote domestic processing and increase value addition within the country’s shea sector\. The research examines how this policy is affecting women involved in shea collection and processing\. Focusing on women collectors, crackers, and processors, the study explores how changes in market opportunities influence livelihoods, economic empowerment, and perceptions of shea parkland conservation\. By examining the links between trade policy, gender, and natural resource governance, the research aims to contribute evidence to discussions on the future of Nigeria’s shea sector and the sustainable management of shea parklands\.

Researchers: [Kudirat Oyiza Abdulkareem](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kudirat-oyiza-abdulkareem/),  [Terry Sunderland](https://ca.linkedin.com/in/terry-sunderland-3b5a93149)
