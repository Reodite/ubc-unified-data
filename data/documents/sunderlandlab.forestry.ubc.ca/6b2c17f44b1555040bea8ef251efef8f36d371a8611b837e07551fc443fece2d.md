We had the pleasure to host another guest speaker, [Linda Yuliani](https://www.cifor.org/research-staff/836/linda-yuliani)  from CIFOR Indonesia\. Linda has conducted extensive research related to the mechanics of oil palm expansion in Kalimantan in relation to local community management issues\. On Monday, she presented her work on *‘**Forest or oil palm? Local people’s perspectives and implications to land use decisions and forest cover’* to the Sunderland Lab\. For more details on her work, please visit her Google Scholar link: [https://scholar\.google\.com/citations?user\=jl7IdqIAAAAJ&amp;hl\=en](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=jl7IdqIAAAAJ&amp;hl=en)
