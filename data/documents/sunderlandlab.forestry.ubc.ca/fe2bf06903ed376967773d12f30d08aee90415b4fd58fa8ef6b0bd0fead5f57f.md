“UBC’s Public Scholars Initiative \(PSI\) was launched in 2015 to support UBC doctoral students whose research extended beyond the academy, and beyond traditional disciplinary approaches, to have a tangible impact for the public good through collaborative, action\-oriented, and/or creative forms of scholarship in their dissertation work\. ”

A sincere congratulations to Winy on this award\! See more information about the Public Scholars Initiative [here](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/psi)\.
