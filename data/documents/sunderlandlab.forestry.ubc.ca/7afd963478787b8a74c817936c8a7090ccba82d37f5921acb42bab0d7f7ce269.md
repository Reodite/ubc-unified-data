Sima recently presented two symposiums on “Landscape Approaches for Nature Conservation, Development and Sustainability” and “Landscape Ecology for Future of SDGs: A Paradigm Shift to Regenerative Development Goals” at IALE 2023 in Nairobi, Kenya\. She also took part in a panel discussion on “Toward Equitable Publishing in Landscape Ecology: identifying barriers and solutions”\.

Sima received the IALE 2023 Foreign Scholar Travel Award\. See more about the IALE conference [here](https://iale2023.org/sites/default/files/2023-07/IALE-BOOK-OF-ABSTRACT-2023-FINAL.pdf)\.
