Terry Sunderland was recently featured on the Current with Matt Galloway show on CBC Radio to discuss why tackling deforestation is key to avoid future pandemics\. Terry emphasized on being proactive instead of reactive as a society and looking at what we can do to avoid these situations in the future\. We need to reflect on our diets, and whether we really need items that contribute to deforestation, he said\.
