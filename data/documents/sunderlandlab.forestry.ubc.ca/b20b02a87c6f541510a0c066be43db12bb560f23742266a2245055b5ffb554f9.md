Dr\. Sima Fakheran has joined the Sunderland lab from Isfahan University of Technology in Iran\. Her research interests include Monitoring Landscape Pattern Change and its Impacts on Biodiversity, Ecological Impacts of Road Networks, Corridor Design, Climate change, and Conservation Planning\. She will be contributing to the [COLANDS](https://www2.cifor.org/colands/) project while at UBC\.

Read more about Dr\. Fakheran [here](https://sunderlandlab.forestry.ubc.ca/people/)\!
