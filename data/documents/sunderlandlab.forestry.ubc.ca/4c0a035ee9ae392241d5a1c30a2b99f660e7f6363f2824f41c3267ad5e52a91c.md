The Carbon for Good \(C4G\) project aims to enhance climate resilience and promote sustainable forest management\. It is funded by the Government of Canada, with technical support from the Center for International Forestry Research and World Agroforestry \(CIFOR\-ICRAF\), the University of British Columbia \(UBC\), and Flinders University of Australia\.

On March 26, the Department of Agriculture and Environment of Can Tho City, Viet Nam, in collaboration with CARE Vietnam, held the launching ceremony for the project *“Technical support towards building capacity to implement climate change mitigation plans and mobilize sustainable financing for forest protection and development, including a high\-quality forest carbon market for Can Tho City,”* abbreviated as the Carbon for Good \(C4G\) project\.

C4G will be implemented from the date of approval through March 2028, with a total budget of 896,667 Canadian dollars \(equivalent to more than 16\.4 billion VND\)\. Project activities will take place in Vinh Phuoc and Vinh Chau wards, as well as the communes of Cu Lao Dung, Lai Hoa, and Vinh Hai\.
