## Welcome to the Sunderland Lab at UBC\!

[https://sunderlandlab\.forestry\.ubc\.ca/files/2020/04/1500x500\-1\.jpg](https://sunderlandlab.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2020/04/1500x500-1.jpg)[Image](https://sunderlandlab.forestry.ubc.ca/files/2020/04/1500x500-1.jpg)

Sunderland lab is based in the [Faculty of Forestry](https://forestry.ubc.ca/) at the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/) in Vancouver, Canada\. The lab is led by [Dr\. Terry Sunderland](https://profiles.forestry.ubc.ca/person/terry-sunderland/), Professor in the Department of [Forest and Conservation Sciences](https://fcs.forestry.ubc.ca/)\. Our research is focused on applied research, biodiversity, conservation, sustainable resource management, and linkages to levering policy influence\. For more details, please visit the ‘[Research](https://sunderlandlab.forestry.ubc.ca/research/)‘ page\. For recent updates on our work, please follow [Sunderland lab](https://ca.linkedin.com/company/sunderlandlab?trk=public_post_feed-actor-name) and [Dr\. Terry Sunderland](https://ca.linkedin.com/in/terry-sunderland-3b5a93149) on LinkedIn\!
