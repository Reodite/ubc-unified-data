It was great to welcome UBC President Dr\. Benoit\-Antoine Bacon to our research lab earlier this morning during his visit to the Faculty of Forestry\. Members of our lab each shared a summary of their research taking place across the Tropics including Colombia, Ghana, Namibia, South Africa, Tanzania, Vietnam, and Zambia\. We enjoyed the engaging questions posed by the President and his encouragement for the lab to continue the great work\.
