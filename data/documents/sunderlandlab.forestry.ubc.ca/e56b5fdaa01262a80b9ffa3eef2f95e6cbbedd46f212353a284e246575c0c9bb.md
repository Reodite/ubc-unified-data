We had the pleasure to host another guest speaker, [Michael Balinga](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michael-balinga-1541a610/), who is the Biodiversity Conservation Specialist for the [U\.S\. Agency for International Development \(USAID\)\-funded\) and implemented by Tetra Tech Biodiversity and Climate Change Project \(WA BiCC\)](http://www.tetratech.com/en/projects/west-africa-biodiversity-and-climate-change-project)\. Michael has over fifteen years of experience in community forestry and tropical and coastal forest management and he spoke to us about the ongoing work in building Regional Capacity to Address Wildlife Crime in West Africa\.

The recording of the talk is available [here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1FFyVcf32GvriXtdMg-AvS2qtw4IfZF3h)\.
