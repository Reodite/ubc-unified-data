We are excited to announce that **Sarah** is the first recipient of the **Aisha S\. Uduman Memorial Award for Graduate Students in Forestry**\. This prestigious award was established in memory of Aisha S\. Uduman, a cherished member of the Forestry community, whose passion and dedication continue to inspire us all\. In honor of Aisha’s life and contributions, her family created this memorial fund at UBC\. We congratulate Sarah on this well\-deserved recognition and are proud to see her continue the legacy of Aisha’s work and commitment to the field of forestry\.
