# Palm oil industry leaves Indonesian village struggling with loss and regret

In 2002, Rukam villagers sold their land to a palm oil company\. Since then, they’ve seen their peatlands, forests and fish stocks vanish\. Now they’re weighing up the environmental cost of their decision and future hopes\. Free, prior, &amp; informed consent is critical in industry/community partnerships\. A case study from Indonesia featuring Terry Sunderland\.
