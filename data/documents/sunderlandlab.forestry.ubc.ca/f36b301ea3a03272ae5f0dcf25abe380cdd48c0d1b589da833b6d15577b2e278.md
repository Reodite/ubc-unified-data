September 27, 2023

Congratulations to PhD Candidate Samuel Adeyanju for receiving the prestigious Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship\. Sam’s work investigates the effectiveness of livelihood interventions in Northern Ghana’s Community Resource Management Areas \(CREMAs\)\. Read more about the award [here](https://twitter.com/sunderland_lab/status/1696640722823454736), and more about Sam’s work at UBC [here](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/campus-community/meet-our-students/adeyanju-samuel-2018)\!
