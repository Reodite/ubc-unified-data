Despite the challenges of conducting research in the face of Covid\-19, we’ve remained busy here at the Vision Lab\.

Ray MacNeil presented his work on tracking closed\-eye movements with electrooculography at the 2021 Virtual VSS Meeting back in late May\. Check out his work [here](https://visionlab-psych.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/MacNeil-Raymond-VSS-May-2021.pdf)\.

Manlu Liu also recently presented her research on objective state of mind reduces sensitivity to other’s emotional expressions at the 2021 CSBBCS meeting\. Check out Manlu’s poster [here](https://visionlab-psych.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/CSBBCS-poster.pdf)\.

We’re also delighted to welcome aboard our newest lab member, undergraduate honours student: Stephanie Yu\.
