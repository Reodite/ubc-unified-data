Help us to assess the impact of MCF on UBC’s instructors and students\!

Through surveys about experiences with Mid\-Course Feedback at UBC, CTLT and the AMS are continuously assessing how these evaluations impact the courses in which they are conducted\. These will be used to continue refining best practices for collecting MCF at UBC\. Reports containing the results of past assessment can be viewed on our [About](https://teaching-eval.sites.olt.ubc.ca/about/ "About") page\.
