Masters and PhD students as well as Post\-Doctoral Fellows enrolled at a Canadian university can apply for a four month research\-based internship, valued at $15,000\. This program provides internship opportunities for graduate students and post docs pursuing collaborative research with an industrial partner\. While the MITACS Accelerate program accepts applications at any time, there is a December 2010 deadline for IRAP subsidized internships\. Applicants for internships with January /February start date, should be apply in early December\.  
**Deadline:** December 15, 2010 for IRAP subsidized interships – other applications accepted at any time  
**Value:** $15,000  
**Link:** [http://www\.grad\.ubc\.ca/awards/mitacs\-accelerate\-program](http://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/mitacs-accelerate-program)
