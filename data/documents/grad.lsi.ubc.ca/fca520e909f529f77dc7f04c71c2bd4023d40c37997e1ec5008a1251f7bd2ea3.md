### CSI@LSI \- November 29th

Students and teachers from the Greater Vancouver area are invited to participate in hands\-on workshops in the research labs at the Life Sciences Centre at UBC on Nov 29th\.  
  
Grade 10 or 11 science teachers are encouraged to apply for this unique science outreach event\.  
  
Life Sciences Centre  
University of British Columbia  
2350 Health Sciences Mall  
Vancouver BC V6T 1Z3  
  
**Application Deadline is Oct 18, 2013\.**  
For more information please contact: lsi\.grad@ubc\.ca
