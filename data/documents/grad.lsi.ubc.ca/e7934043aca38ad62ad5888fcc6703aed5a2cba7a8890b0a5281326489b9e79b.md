Registration is now open\. Please forward this announcement to the graduate students and postdocs in your network\.

**What’s Mitacs Step?** Program provides graduate students and postdocs with opportunities to build on their professional and transferrable skill sets\. Opportunities are open to all past, present and prospective graduate students and postdocs\.

**Why should you attend?** Strengthen your working relationships, increase your ability to connect with industry, improve research results and impact\. Access to industry recognized expertise\.

\*Please note:

-   A valid credit card is REQUIRED to complete and secure your registration
-   The workshops are free to attend however there is a $50\.00 charge for no\-shows and late cancellations
-   You have 5 calendar days prior to the scheduled workshop to modify or cancel your registration at no penalty
-   Certificates of participation are issued to those who complete the full workshop, please ensure you can stay for the full session when organizing transport to and from the workshop venue

You MUST first register, then request travel funding, only those registered for a workshop are eligible for travel funding\!
