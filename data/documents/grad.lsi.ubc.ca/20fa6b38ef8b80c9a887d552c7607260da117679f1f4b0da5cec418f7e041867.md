– the online application is not yet available  
– call for nominations will be issued by Grad Awards when updated information is available

Monday 15 Oct 2012**

**CIHR Doctoral Scholarships**

**Affiliated Fellowships**  
– key change from previous competitions:  international students who hold 4YF funding are **not**  
required to apply to the Affiliated Fellowships competition \(they may choose to apply, but are not  
required to do so\)  
– the application form is not yet available  
– call for nominations will be issued by Grad Awards after the CIHR, NSERC, and SSHRC application forms have all been released  
– deadline for applications to be submitted by students to their graduate program:  to be set by the graduate program  
– deadline for nominations to be submitted by graduate programs to Grad Studies: **  
Monday 15 October 2012**
