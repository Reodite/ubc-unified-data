Starting this week, the Faculty of Graduate Studies will be hosting an advisor from Student Financial Assistance and Awards office, who will be available to provide confidential one\-on\-one financial advising for graduate students at the Grad Studies office **on Wednesday afternoons**\.  Linda Hallam, Senior Financial Support Advisor, will be in the Faculty of Graduate Studies office on Wednesday afternoons from 1:00\-4:00pm\.  On Wednesday 6 October, Linda will be available to meet with students who stop by; for subsequent weeks students can call 604\.822\.5111 to make an appointment, identifying themselves as a grad student and indicating the purpose for their appointment\.

Please note that graduate students continue to be able to make an appointment to meet with a Student Financial Assistance and Awards advisor in Brock Hall at any time by calling 604\.822\.5111\.
