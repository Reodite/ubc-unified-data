CSI@LSI is the equivalent of combining your favorite CSI TV show and a murder mystery all in one\.  High school students are invited to explore science through this annual outreach activity designed by the UBC Life Sciences Institute\-Graduate Student Association \(LSI\-GSA\) and UBC Post\-Doctoral Association \(UBC\-PDA\)\.  Grade 10, 11, and 12 science teachers located in the GVRD area are encouraged to apply for this unique science outreach event\.

CSI@LSI will be an all day event held on October 25th, 2012\.  The application deadline is September 21st, 2012\.  The school chosen to participate will be notified by email on September 28th, 2012\.  To find out more information about CSI@LSI, please click [here](https://grad.lsi.ubc.ca/lsigsa/csi/ "CSI@LSI page with application link")\.

To submit an application, click on the link below\.[https://grad\.lsi\.ubc\.ca/lsigsa/csi/csi\_lsi\-apply/](https://grad.lsi.ubc.ca/lsigsa/csi/csi_lsi-apply/ "CSI@LSI Oct 2012 Application Forum")
