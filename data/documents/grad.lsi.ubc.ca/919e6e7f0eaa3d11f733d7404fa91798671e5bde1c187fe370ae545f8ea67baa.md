[https://gradlsi1\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2011/06/CrimeScene\-300x450\.jpg](https://gradlsi1.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/06/CrimeScene-300x450.jpg)[CSI@LSI Crime Scene](https://gradlsi1.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/06/CrimeScene-300x450-200x300.jpg)In March 2011 the LSI\-GSA was awarded a outreach grant to expand their current CSI@LSI program to an out of town school\. On May 27&amp;28th the LSI\-GSA welcomed Bella Bella Community School to the LSI to solve the investigation of Eve Hill\. The LSI\-GSA reviewed several applications from dedicated teachers in various remote communities in BC\. After evaluating all the applications the LSI\-GSA made the tough decision to invite 19 students from Bella Bella Community School to the LSI\. \(more photos viewable on our facebook page [here](https://www.facebook.com/media/set/?set=a.208782942493068.55735.125967727441257&amp;l=79612d42a2)\.\)

CSI@LSI is an engaging outreach activity where students are presented a case to be solved using real scientific experiments and equipment\. Based on the television show Crime Scene Investigation \(CSI\), highly trained graduate students, post\-docs, and staff guide students through experiments to help them collect evidence\. Once students have worked their way through various stations they use deductive reason to identify the guilty party\.
