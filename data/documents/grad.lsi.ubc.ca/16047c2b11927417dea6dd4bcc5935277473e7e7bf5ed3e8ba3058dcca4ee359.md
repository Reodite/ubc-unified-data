[https://gradlsi1\.sites\.olt\.ubc\.ca/files/2011/01/IMG\_4376\.jpg](https://gradlsi1.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/01/IMG_4376.jpg)[IMG\_4376](https://gradlsi1.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2011/01/IMG_4376-300x225.jpg)Annually, the LSI\-GSA coordinates a research day in the LSI as part of UBC Celebrate Research Week\. LSI\-GSA Research Day is an opportunity to present LSI research with other members of the LSI, UBC students, staff and faculty, as well as the public\.

Talks are given by trainees and invited speakers\. International and local speakers are chosen via vote by LSI graduate students to give talks and support for speakers is graciously provided by LSI Research Groups\. Graduate students interested in joining the Graduate Student Mailing List please contact the LSI\-GSA at lsigsa@zoology\.ubc\.ca
