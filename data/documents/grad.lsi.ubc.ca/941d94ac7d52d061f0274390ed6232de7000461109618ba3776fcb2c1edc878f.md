The Student Biotechnology Network and Genome BC are pleased to invite BC’s scientific community to participate in the annual Research Exchange &amp; Poster Competition\. Students from BC’s academic institutions will have an opportunity to present their research to our province’s scientific community\. Cash prizes will be given for the top three posters\. Students will have the opportunity to talk with researchers from academia and industry, as well as meet company representatives\. Please join us for this great opportunity to showcase your research\!

The deadline to register for the competition and submit your abstract is April 30th 2013\. Please visit [http://www\.thesbn\.ca/Events/Events%202012\-2013/poster\-competition\-2013](http://www.thesbn.ca/Events/Events%202012-2013/poster-competition-2013) for more information about the poster competition\.
