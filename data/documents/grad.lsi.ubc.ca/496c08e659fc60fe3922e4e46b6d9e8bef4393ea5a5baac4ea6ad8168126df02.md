Annually, the LSI\-GSA hosts a Research Day as part of UBC Celebrate Research Week\.

**Research Day 2014 is Friday, March 14, 2014**

The keynote speakers are being sponsored by the **Bacterial Adaptation and Response Networks \(BARN\)** and the **Neuroscience** Research Groups\. Each research group nominates a list of speakers and LSI members vote of their top choice from each group\.

This years potential speakers are:

## BARN:

**1\. Dr\. E\. Peter Greenberg** – focused on the emerging field of sociomicrobiology  
University of Washington  
[http://depts\.washington\.edu/micro/faculty/greenberg\.htm](http://depts.washington.edu/micro/faculty/greenberg.htm)

**2\. Dr\. Suzanne Noble** – Candida albicans is unique among \~1,000,000 species of fungi on this planet, as it is both a normal component of the mammalian microbiome and the most common fungal pathogen of humans\. The core research objective of our laboratory is to define the molecular basis of host\-microbe interactions that foster both lifestyles  
UC San Francisco  
[http://bms\.ucsf\.edu/directory/faculty/suzanne\-noble\-md\-phd](http://bms.ucsf.edu/directory/faculty/suzanne-noble-md-phd)

**3\. Dr\. Alan Grossman** – We use a combination of genetic, molecular, physiological, biochemical, cell\-biological, and genomic approaches to study how cells of Bacillus subtilis sense internal and external conditions and control basic cellular processes in response to these conditions\.  
MIT  
[https://biology\.mit\.edu/people/alan\_grossman](https://biology.mit.edu/people/alan_grossman)

**4\. Dr\. John Mekalanos** – engaged in the analysis of bacterial virulence and functional genomics\. The goal of these studies is to understand how pathogenic bacteria grow, evade the host immune system and ultimately cause disease\.  
Harvard  
[http://mekalanoslab\.med\.harvard\.edu/research\.html](http://mekalanoslab.med.harvard.edu/research.html)

**5\. Dr\. Roberto Kolter** – pursue research projects that span areas of biofilm physiology, interspecies interactions, small molecule natural products, and microbial ecology and evolution\.  
Harvard  
[http://gasp\.med\.harvard\.edu/research/research\.html](http://gasp.med.harvard.edu/research/research.html)

## Neuroscience:

**1\. Dr\. Michael Meaney** – Early environmental regulation of gene expression and brain development: how does early experience exert a sustained influence on neuronal function?  
McGill  
[http://neurology\.mcgill\.ca/meaney\_m\.html](http://neurology.mcgill.ca/meaney_m.html)

**2\. Dr\. Sheena Josslyn** – dedicated to understanding the neural basis of cognitive function and dysfunction\.  
University of Toronto  
[http://www\.youtube\.com/watch?v\=UjEnmFIHFAU](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UjEnmFIHFAU)

**3\. Dr\. Alan Basbaum** – Science of Pain  
UC San Fransisco  
[http://bms\.ucsf\.edu/directory/faculty/allan\-basbaum\-phd](http://bms.ucsf.edu/directory/faculty/allan-basbaum-phd)

**4\. Dr\. Alcino Silva** – Learning and Memory  
UCLA  
[http://www\.silvalab\.org/](http://www.silvalab.org/)

**5\. Dr\. David Anderson** – focuses on understanding how emotional behavior is encoded in the brain, at the level of specific neuronal circuits, and the specific neuronal subtypes that comprise them\.  
Cal Tech  
[http://www\.youtube\.com/watch?v\=D9xJl4S6NsM](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9xJl4S6NsM)

**6\. Dr\. Dennis O’Leary** – studies development and plasticity of the vertebrate nervous system  
Salk Institute  
[http://www\.salk\.edu/faculty/oleary\.html](http://www.salk.edu/faculty/oleary.html)

**All LSI members are encouraged to vote\. Please vote for one speaker from each research group\.**
