This is to let you know that St\. John’s College is still welcoming applications for residential membership for September 2011\.

If you know of any graduate students – or visiting scholars or post\-doctoral fellows – who are looking for on\-campus housing and who might be interested in becoming a part of our vibrant international community, please forward the attached information to them and invite them to apply\. Information packages and application forms can be downloaded from our website, stjohns\.ubc\.ca, and the applications submitted on\-line; alternatively, packages are also available in our Administration office at 2111 Lower Mall\.

Potential applicants are welcome to contact our Membership Assistant, Andrea Paterson, at 604\-822\-8790 or sjc\.membership@ubc\.ca for further information\.
