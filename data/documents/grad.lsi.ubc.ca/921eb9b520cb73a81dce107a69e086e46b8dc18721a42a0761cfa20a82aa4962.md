## LSI\-GSA Research Day 2013 was March 8th, 2013\.

#### keynote speakers

This year we had two keynote speakers\.  Our first speaker was **Dr\. John Stamatoyannopoulos** from the University of Washington, who presented his work on *Gene Regulation and Common Disease*\.  Our second speaker was **Dr\. Mark Davis** from Standford University, who spoke about *Immunology Taught by Humans\.*
