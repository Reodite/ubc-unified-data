The Department of Zoology at UBC is internationally renowned for its research in a variety of modern biological sciences, including ecology, evolution, physiology, neurobiology, cell biology and development\.

From DNA to proteins to cells, researchers use model organisms to study fundamental principles in cell and developmental biology\.  Opportunities to study in Zoology labs at the LSI at the masters and PhD level\.
