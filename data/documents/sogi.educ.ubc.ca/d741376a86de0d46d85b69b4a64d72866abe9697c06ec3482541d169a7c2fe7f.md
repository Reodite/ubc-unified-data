This year, we are highlighting graduate student work focused on gender and sexuality in education\. These presentations were a wonderful opportunity for graduate students to share their research and to receive feedback from faculty and students in the SOGI community of practice\. We look forward to hosting more in the fall of 2026\. 

**lavender cloud:** *“Educators Practicing Solidarity with Trans Children: Ideas, Hopes and Imaginations for My Research”*

Wednesday, October 22, 5:00 – 7:00 pm, SCRF 310

**Jody Billingsby:** *“Considering Tails &amp; Tales:  Pre\-service Teachers’ Professional Identity Development on Practicum, an Intersectional Approach\.”*

Wednesday, November 19, 11:30 – 1:00 pm, SCRF 310 

**Makayla Freeman:** *“Queer and dear: The visibility of queer families in perinatal research\.”*

Wednesday, January 21, 11:30 – 1:00 pm, PCN 1001
