## Creating Safer Schools for Everyone

Looking to learn more about gender pronouns? Looking for resources how to create anti\-oppressive environments for all students? Want to learn about policies and practices that can help protect 2SLGBTQ\+ students? We offer a variety of resources to support you in creating safer and more welcoming schools\.

Check out the resources in the sections below for specific websites, guides and tools related to SOGI inclusiveness\. Whether you’re a student, an educator, or an ally, we all have a part in nurturing anti\-oppressive, SOGI inclusive learning environments\. We have organized this list of resources through loose categories\. However, many of these categories blend together, so we encourage you to use the full list to find what you are looking for\.

If you have any questions about the resources or would like to send new resources to SOGI UBC, please contact us at [sogi@educ\.ubc\.ca](mailto:sogi@educ.ubc.ca)\.
