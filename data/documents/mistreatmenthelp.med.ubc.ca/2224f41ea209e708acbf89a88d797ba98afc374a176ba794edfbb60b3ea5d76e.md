#### We are a hub for education, coaching, referrals, and support to help you access the resources you need and navigate the complex campus environment related to mistreatment\.

-   We strive to engage in deep listening and compassionately understand diverse perspectives\.
-   We are champions of fairness, committed to treating all our partners with care, dignity, and respect\.
-   We use a trauma\-informed, consent\-based approach to guide all parties through the process\.
-   We believe feedback is a valuable gift that helps us build a more excellent and inclusive Faculty\. We also acknowledge that the fear of retaliation can prevent people from raising concerns\. Therefore, we work strategically to create meaningful change while mitigating fears of retaliation\.
