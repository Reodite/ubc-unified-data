**Team:** Beth Turner, PhD; Tahia Devisscher, PhD; Christian Messier, PhD

**Timeline:** 2022\-2026

**Partners:** Community of Manawan of the Atikamekw First Nation

Indigenous\-led Nature\-based Solutions \(NbS\) are emerging as a critical option to uphold indigenous rights and wellbeing while providing local pathways for adaptation and resilience in the face of today’s global challenges\. However, visions from Indigenous Peoples as to what such NbS look like have received little attention\. Likewise, research is also lacking with regards to how these NbS may support social and ecological values in the long term to enable resilience\. It is critical that such research itself be Indigenous\-led, and centered on their knowledge systems, worldview, and voices\.

Through partnership with the community of Manawan of the Atikamekw First Nation, this research explores these issues, looking specifically at a proposed Indigenous Protected and Conserved Area\. First, visioning exercises were held with diverse actors in the community to build conceptualizations of what such a protected area looks like to them\. Second, participatory modelling was carried out to explore pathways as to how this protected area may support the community’s social and ecological values under scenarios of global change\. Such research is advancing the community’s own process of establishing a protected area on their territory while contributing to knowledge\-sharing on the broader adaptation outcomes of indigenous\-led NbS\.
