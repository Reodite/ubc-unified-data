Opportunities

**At the Faculty of Forestry, University of British Columbia, we offer** [**Ph\.D\. in Forestry**](https://forestry.ubc.ca/future-students/graduate/thesis-based-degrees/doctor-of-philosophy/) **or** [**Master of Science in Forestry**](https://forestry.ubc.ca/future-students/graduate/thesis-based-degrees/master-of-science-master-of-applied-science/) **programs\.  If interested in joining the RISE lab, please email me at** [tahia\.devisscher@ubc\.ca](mailto:tahia.devisscher@ubc.ca)  with the following \(general inquiries are also welcome\):

-   A brief statement of interest \(1\-2 paragraphs\) describing your research ideas, and your motivation for joining the RISE Lab
-   Your updated CV
-   Writing samples that you are most proud of
-   For funding opportunities see examples [**here**](https://forestry.ubc.ca/students/future-graduate-students/financial-support/)
