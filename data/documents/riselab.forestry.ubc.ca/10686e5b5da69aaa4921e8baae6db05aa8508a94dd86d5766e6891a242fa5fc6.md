**Team:** Roberta Gonzalez, MSc Student; Tahia Devisscher, PhD; Matthew Mitchell, PhD

**Timeline:** May 2024 – May 2026

**Committee:** Cole Burton, PhD; Barbara Frei, PhD

[Image](https://riselab.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/12/birds4-768x1024.jpg)

The composition of urban forests on private land is largely unknown, with very few studies aimed at understanding the role these forests play in providing ecosystem services to the city and local environment\. The objective of this project is to understand the role these forests play in providing habitat for urban birds in Vancouver, for both residents and migratory birds\.

This will be done by collecting a full tree inventory of the sampled private areas, focusing on tree functional traits \(such as canopy structure, fruit production\) and placing audio recorders to detect bird diversity\. This will take place during the summer of 2025\.

Findings directly benefit birds facing habitat loss and have special conservation concern or are endangered including Olive\-Sided Flycatcher, Barn Swallow, Rufous hummingbird, and Western screech Owl\.

The recordings will be processed using software that identifies bird species and manually checked for accuracy, as well as filtering out any sounds\. The results of this study can be used to inform urban planning and conservation strategies, highlighting the importance of private urban forests in supporting biodiversity\.
