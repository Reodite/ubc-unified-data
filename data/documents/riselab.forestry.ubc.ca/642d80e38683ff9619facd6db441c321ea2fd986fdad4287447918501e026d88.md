[Webinar: Voices for Action – Growing the Mini Forest Movement in Canada](https://youtu.be/3yGqnrzU0_w?si=o7fg6E7bwkYlLYYj)

Green Communities Canada hosts voices for Action, and its first session of 2025 session, **“Growing the Mini Forest Movement in Canada,”** focused on the critical role of green infrastructure and nature\-based solutions in mitigating and adapting to climate change in Canada\.

The speakers: Kristen Bill, Project Manager, Forestry Health, [City Of Hamilton](https://www.hamilton.ca/home-neighbourhood/environmental-stewardship/community-environmental-initiatives/community-tree); Professor [Tahia Devisscher](https://forestry.ubc.ca/faculty-profile/tahia-devisscher/), Assistant Professor, Faculty of Forestry, University of British Columbia; Sharon McGougan, President, [Garden City Conservation Society](https://gardencityconservation.ca/)\.
