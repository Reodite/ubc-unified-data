**Team:** Aubrey Benson, MSc; Finn Koepf, MSc student; Jeff Burton, MSc; Tahia Devisscher, PhD

**Timeline:** September 2024 – May 2025

**Partners:** UBC SEEDS

[Image](https://riselab.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/12/Urban-Forest-Assessment2-Aubrey-Benson-768x1024.jpg)

This project analyzes UBC’s campus tree inventory data for the very first time, as part of a collaboration between the RISE Lab and the UBC SEEDS Sustainability Program\. We use data collected over the past five years by UFOR220 students to understand the diversity, structure, and benefits of the urban forest in the academic campus\. Key aspects, such as abundance of tree species, their size and location, are analyzed to assess the current state of UBC’s urban forest\. The evaluation also estimates the ecosystem services provided by the urban forest, including carbon storage and stormwater management, to emphasize its role in promoting community resilience\.

The results will be shared with the UBC community to inform long\-term monitoring and research, the Campus Vision 2050, and the development of UBC’s urban forest strategy\. This work supports efforts to protect and manage campus trees, ensuring they continue to provide vital benefits for years to come\.
