## [Dr\. Scott Ramsay receives IMPaCT Award](https://ics.nursing.ubc.ca/2026/04/30/dr-scott-ramsay-receives-impact-award/)

April 30, 2026

Dr\. Scott Ramsay an assistant professor at the UBC School of Nursing has been awarded $70,000 through the IMPaCT Training Award and Michael Smith Health Research BC to support a pilot randomized controlled trial examining telehealth as a tool for pediatric concussion follow\-up care\.
