## [In Memoriam: Madeleine Kétéskwēw Dion Stout](https://ics.nursing.ubc.ca/2026/01/05/in-memoriam-madeleine-keteskwew-dion-stout/)

January 5, 2026

Madeleine Kétéskwēw Dion Stout was a nurse, Cree speaker, and preeminent scholar\. She was a member of the Kehewin Cree Nation in Alberta, and was former student of the Blue Quills Residential School near St\. Paul, Alberta\.
