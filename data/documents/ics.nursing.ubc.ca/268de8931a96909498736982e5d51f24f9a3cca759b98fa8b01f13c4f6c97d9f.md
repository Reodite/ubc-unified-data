This past May, three Indigenous nurses made history at the UBC School of Nursing, Crystal Point, Loa Page, and Shannon Fitton became the first graduates of the Indigenous Graduate Education in Nursing \(IGEN\) pathway within the Master of Science in Nursing program\. The celebration was a landmark moment: the realization of a long\-held vision for Indigenous\-led graduate nursing education in British Columbia\.
