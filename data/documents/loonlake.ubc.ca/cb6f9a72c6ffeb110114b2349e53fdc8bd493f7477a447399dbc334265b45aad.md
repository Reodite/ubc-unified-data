We offer groups a wide assortment of nature\-based activities including interpretive trails, swimming, canoeing, battle archery, bubble ball, plus rappelling and a climbing wall\. We also offer outdoor learning and wilderness skill\-building programs\. Relax and unwind with a session in out lakeside premium sauna\.  
  
Together with our partners, [Wild &amp; Immersive](https://wildlearnings.ca/), [Pinnacle Pursuits](http://www.pinnaclepursuits.com/about-pinnacle-pursuits)  and [Stellar Play](https://www.stellarplay.ca/), we are proud to offer you award\-winning team\-building events, leadership training experiences, and reasons to both learn, and have fun, in the great outdoors\.
