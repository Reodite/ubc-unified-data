Surrounded by forest and calm waters, we will immerse ourselves in nature, engage in heartfelt sharing circles, and practice forest bathing\.  
  
Accommodations: Single room or shared rooms available, travel with 2 or more people and save\. \(no bunkbeds\)\.  
  
Meals: All meals are included in your weekend retreat\. The chefs prepare heart\-warming comfort food with a flair for the extraordinary\.  
  
Forest Bathing: Be introduced to the healing practice of Forest bathing\. We will quiet the chatter in our minds, connect to our intuition, and practice stillness as we engage in mindful observation\.  
  
Healing Sound Bath: We will have a beautiful afternoon sound bath and meditation\.  
  
Creativity: You will be led through an intuitive art practice, and create something beautiful to take home with you\.  
  
Stress Reduction Workshop: Included in the retreat is a stress management workshop where you will learn techniques to calm your nervous system, manage your stress and learn how to harness the healing power of your vagus nerve\.
