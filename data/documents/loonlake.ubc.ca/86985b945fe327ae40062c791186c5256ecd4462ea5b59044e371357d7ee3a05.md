Join Christine Price Clark at Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre for a half\-day of nature and inspiration, and a 1 hr class\. You'll get the chance to visit the Lower Mainland's best kept secret \- a year\-round lakeside destination less than an hour from Downtown Vancouver, and open to your own retreat bookings\. 

This is exclusive to yoga &amp; wellness instructors only\. Round\-trip transportation will be provided from Waterfront Station departing at 8:45am, and returning at approximately 2pm\. A delicious vegetarian lunch will also be provided\.

About Christine: 

Christine is the lead teacher and program director for the [Vancouver School of Yoga's 300\-Hour Advanced Teacher Training](https://vsoha.com/programs/yoga-teacher-training/) as well as contributor within their foundational programs\. She was named in Vancouver Magazine’s 'Best of Vancouver' and is known throughout the city and beyond as an empowering mentor and a teacher's teacher\.
