Loon Lake is about 1\.6kms long, and on average about 300m wide\.  It covers approximately 56 hectares \(140 acres\), and is fed by a number of small streams that drain down throughout its watershed\. The private lake is designated as a non\-fishing lake within the BC Provincial Fishing Regulations\. 

This regulation was enacted so that all of our lakes could be studied long term and compared to un\-fished lake populations – something that is quite rare in Southern British Columbia\.  Additionally, there is a prohibition on motorized vessels \- in turn helping to keep our lake pristine in every sense of the word\.
