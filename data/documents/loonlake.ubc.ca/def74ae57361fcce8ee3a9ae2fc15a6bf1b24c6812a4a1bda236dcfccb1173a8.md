**About Us**

Located in scenic Maple Ridge, B\.C\., approximately one hour east of Downtown Vancouver, Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre is a year\-round meeting, conference, and retreat destination nestled within the University of British Columbia's Malcolm Knapp Research Forest, part of UBC's Faculty of Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship\.

MKRF\-1949\-00\-P02340\.jpg

**History**

Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre has a long tradition of providing environmental education and outdoor recreation\. Originally built in 1949 to house the University of British Columbia's Faculty of Forestry field camps, it continues to be used for this purpose every spring\. Today many other educational groups take advantage of our idyllic setting for their own outdoor education needs\. In addition, a wide range of adult and youth groups rent the year round facility for retreats, workshops and other events seeking our private wilderness setting\.

In 2003, UBC signed an agreement with the Canadian Cancer Society, designating Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre as the permanent home of Camp Goodtimes\. Camp Goodtimes is an annual summer event for kids aged 6 to ­16, that are battling cancer\. In consideration of their use of the facility, a substantial contribution was donated for enhancements that include upgrades to the Student Centre, as well as the construction of the Walter C\. Koerner Forestry Centre\.

**Team**

What draws guests to the Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre is our convenience and our tranquility\. Yet it’s Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre’s genuine hospitality that brings our guests back year after year\. From the time you enquire, you'll be treated as friends\. We will work with you to design the best retreat possible for your unique needs and interests\.  You'll be provided a range of options, from single and shared guest rooms, a private cabin, and dormitory\-style accommodation within a multipurpose facility\.

  
Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre prides itself in the food we provide our guests\. Personalized for each group, the culinary team works hard to offer a menu that serves the tastes for each of our visitors\. Whether it’s a hearty comfort food or a unique vegetarian meal, the kitchen at Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre takes pride in being creative and accommodating\.
