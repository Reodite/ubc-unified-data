**Experience the Transformative Power of Sauna at Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre\.**

Join us for the ultimate wellness experience at Loon Lake’s public sauna sessions—where nature, relaxation, and revitalization come together in a peaceful lakeside setting\.

**What to Expect:**

-   Lakeside sauna with scenic views
    

-   Optional cold plunge for invigorating contrast therapy
    
-   Natural forest setting for ultimate tranquility
    
-   Coffee, tea, and fruit provided
    

**Reserve your spot and reconnect with yourself and the natural world\.**

Spaces are limited—book early to secure your preferred date and time\. Each session is for a total of 2 hours\.

**Why Join the Sauna Experience?**

Immerse yourself in the healing benefits of contrast therapy with our premium sauna and optional cold plunge in Loon Lake\. The hot\-cold cycle supports:

-   Improved circulation
    
-   Detoxification
    
-   Stress reduction
    
-   Immune system support
    
-   Faster recovery and mental clarity
