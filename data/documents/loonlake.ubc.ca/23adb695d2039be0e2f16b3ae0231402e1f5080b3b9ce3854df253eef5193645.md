Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre

**West Coast retreats that connect\.**

[Contact Us](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/contact-us)

Located less than an hour from Vancouver, Maple Ridge’s Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre provides a nature\-based retreat setting for a variety of group bookings including corporate retreats, wellness weekends, and youth and school groups\.

As a proud part of the University of British Columbia’s [Faculty of Forestry &amp; Environmental Stewardship](https://forestry.ubc.ca/), we create spaces that inspire learning, support personal growth, and celebrate our shared natural environment\.

At Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre, we connect the mind, the body and the soul\.

[Image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/569ebff4c647ad650f36d23c/5146eca8-ffdc-4134-b3ef-d7e88877aaa2/jk_panabode_14.jpg)

### Corporate Retreats

Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre is a natural venue for fresh starts, re\-alignment and team building\. We work with every group to provide an experience that is designed around their unique needs and interests\. Boardrooms, classrooms, break\-out rooms and private dining rooms, we have everything you need for your next company retreat\.

[Image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/569ebff4c647ad650f36d23c/19657180-4ce3-44ba-ae86-2e8a791ae5d3/jk_yoga_9816.jpg)

### Wellness Retreats

Our serene lakeside setting is ideal for yoga retreats and fireside wellness seminars\. We offer heated floors, health\-conscious meal planning, and scalable accommodation options to provide practitioners with budgetary choice, convenience, planning assistance, and a mindful classroom setting\.

[Image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/569ebff4c647ad650f36d23c/c17e8cef-e1cf-4482-8075-33adf3fb9781/youthful-special-interest.jpg)

### Youth &amp; Special interest

We invite you to bring your next overnight school, youth, or special interest group to Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre\. Take advantage of our popular outdoor adventure options, or add our innovative and instructed, Wild &amp; Immersive curriculum that blends fun around key deliverables, and priced at a remarkably low day rate of $15/youth\. **Our Setting**

The UBC Malcolm Knapp Research Forest is located on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the q̓ic̓əy̓ \(Katzie\) First Nation\.

Often described as the Lower Mainland’s best\-kept secret, Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre is nestled on the Vancouver side of Golden Ears Provincial Park\. It is a place for quiet reflection, where fresh air, clean water, and towering fir trees frame views of the majestic Garibaldi Mountain Range\.

We offer guests profound natural connections, inspired learning, and shared personal moments\. For 5 weeks during the summer, we are proud to be home to the Canadian Cancer Society’s Camp Goodtimes, a summer program for remarkable kids fighting cancer\.

**Our Accommodations**

At full capacity, Loon Lake Lodge &amp; Retreat Centre can accommodate up to 195 individuals\. Facilities range from corporate\-ready Whistler\-style accommodations, to well\-maintained dormitories that are ideal for students, large families, or those working within a budget\.

**Our Services**

In addition to helping design your group’s lodging, conference, and dining needs, we offer exceptional team\-building, learning, and leadership programs by way of our partner, [Pinnacle Pursuits](http://www.pinnaclepursuits.com/)\. We offer outdoor educational programs through [UBC’s Wild &amp; Immersive program](https://wildandimmersive.ubc.ca/), and outrageously fun &amp; active events, like forest laser tag and bubble archery through [Stellar Play](https://stellarplay.ca/)\.  
  
In addition, our property features a modern gymnasium, a network of scenic nature trails, personal watercraft, a ropes course, a climbing wall, and opportunities for both rock climbing and rappelling\. We also offer a [lakeside sauna](https://loonlake.ubc.ca/sauna), providing a relaxing way to unwind after your adventure\.
