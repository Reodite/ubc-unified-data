*We’ve created a weekend that will leave you feeling like you just had a relaxing weekend with the girls planned just for you\.***Peaceful, rejuvenating, and connected\.***You’ve felt the power of climbing the career ladder while raising a family, and taking care of everything and everyone else\.  
  
And maybe you’ve been doing it all because you think you should\.  
  
There’s a new power calling to you now\.  
  
One that you get to say yes to, not because you think you should,  
  
but because you deeply desire to say YES\!****  
  
To you  
For you  
From you  
  
****If you’ve never experienced something like this before…  
  
…this weekend is going to feel like an exhale when you didn’t know you were holding your breath\.  
  
There is a part of you that’s been guiding you towards this moment, to read these words, and feel a stirring deep inside you\.*

*This weekend is a chance for you to experience what it feels like to be seen, not as the one who has it all under control, but as the real, raw and sometimes messy human you are\.  
  
This weekend is a chance for you to experience what it feels like to be heard, not just your words, but the truth that lies hidden underneath them\.  
  
This weekend is a chance for you to experience what it feels like to be held, not just physically, but emotionally and spiritually by a group of gifted facilitators who know how hard it can be to allow yourself to be held when you’re the one who’s used to holding everyone else\.***What to expect:***Nestled amongst tall trees and calming waters is an oasis where you’ll be fed the most mouth\-watering and nutritious meals, while having honest and heartfelt conversations with new women who quickly feel like old friends during the day\. And at night, you’ll feel your whole body exhale as you fall into a bed you have all to yourself in your own private room that feels like a sanctuary\.*
