## Movement Breaks, Outdoor Recess &amp; Team Building Activities

The underlying principles of taking your whole crew on an outdoor retreat are to build up collaborative morale, ignite creativity, cultivate gratitude, and inspire growth\. Much of this is emphasized through time spent outside the conference room via team\-building activities and forest recesses\.

### Movement Breaks

Place yourself a few decades back, right into the heart of your preschool and kindergarten classrooms\. Remember when your teacher would suddenly stop the education, turn on some jovial music, and invite you to get out of your chair for a quick wiggle? Every little spirit in that room would leap into the air, bursting into smiles and giggles\. The energy would instantly shift and all hints of fatigue and frustration would disappear\. There was a reason for that, and it benefits adults as much as kids\.

## How Movement Benefits Productivity

Periodically getting up from a sitting position and stimulating just a few moments of movement sends a surge of blood flow to the brain\. This is known to immediately enhance key factors of concentration and productivity by spiking the brain’s ability to focus and stay alert\. Plus, it’s a natural antidote for stress and anxiety\.

Give your team the power to unleash their inner child with scheduled movement breaks and outdoor recesses\. All that’s required is a few minutes of total presence, and stepping out on the deck to immerse in 3\-5 minutes of forest bathing\. Take deep breaths of the pristine air, notice the scent of cedar and soil, feel the breeze or the sunlight, listen for the sounds of nature\.

### Activity Breaks Aka Recess

In addition to quick movement breaks, children and adults alike, benefit from periodic recesses, usually about 30\-60 minutes several times a day\. And while this might not be possible in your typical corporate setting, it’s certainly encouraged while on an outdoor corporate retreat\.
